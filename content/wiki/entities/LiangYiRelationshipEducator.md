# 梁毅 / Liang Yi

## Overview
梁毅 / Liang Yi appears in [[shi-di-fu-shuo-465-liangyi-cong-zigong-zouxiang-xianshi-lhLhohpUHBpofcWXtzOuxDIvRber]] as a relationship and nonviolent-communication educator returning to [[ShiDiFuShuo|史蒂夫说]] to discuss [[SteveShiDiFuShuoHost|Steve]]'s new fatherhood.

## Current Profile
The current source presents Liang Yi as a conversational educator who uses intimate relationship, nonviolent communication, and developmental language to help Steve examine fatherhood without turning it into a simple pro-childbirth argument. His role is to notice Steve's unusually energized fatherhood, test the meaning of that experience, and repeatedly redirect parenting from blame, speed, or control toward feeling exchange, regulation, and slow growth.

## Key Characteristics
- Works from relationship education and nonviolent-communication frames rather than from a clinical diagnostic role in this source.
- Treats fatherhood as a gradual relationship-building process that can change adult identity and attention.
- Reframes fear of marriage or childbearing as a signal with meaning, not merely resistance that must be overcome.
- Uses multi-perspective feeling and needs analysis to move parenting conflict beyond culprit-finding.
- Emphasizes regulation over fixed speed, arguing that mismatch can be noticed and adjusted.
- Connects parenting with broader human development, including adult learning, recovery, and the ability to witness small changes.
