# Sarah Hardy

Sarah Hardy is the [[Airbnb]] colleague whom [[LauraModi]] chose as [[Bobbie]]'s co-founder and COO in [[bobbie-laura-modi-how-a-baby-formula-startup-took-market-share-from-two-industry-giants-4488842b-74d3-40ed-8776-5831c5934e69]]. Investors had suggested finding a scientist as co-founder, but Modi chose Hardy because she believed culture and operating trust had to be present from the beginning.

In the wiki, Hardy anchors the episode's culture-first founder-team lesson. Infant formula still required scientific and regulatory expertise, but the source argues that a high-stress, regulated, inventory-constrained company also needed a partner who could build an organization able to survive recalls, fundraising pressure, and supply shortages.
