# 虞卿 / Yu Qing

虞卿 / Yu Qing enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-1-loxyybm3njrtnudydafwzu9jp9jz]] as the [[ZhaoState|赵国]] adviser who gives the clearest diplomatic warning before the [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] command crisis. When [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] asks how to respond to Qin pressure, Yu Qing rejects direct peace-seeking with [[QinState|秦国]] because Qin already holds battlefield initiative and has little reason to grant a real settlement.

His alternative is to rebuild leverage first. Yu Qing urges Zhao to send important figures and gifts to [[ChuState|楚国]] and [[QiState|齐国]], renew a [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] posture, and only then negotiate with Qin from a position that is not isolated.

The episode validates his forecast through [[ZhengZhu|郑朱]]'s mission. Qin receives the envoy visibly, letting other lords conclude that Zhao is already seeking peace, but it does not conclude peace. Yu Qing therefore becomes the source's strategic-clear-sight figure: he understands that a diplomatic gesture can become information that weakens the sender.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-2-lutsvwvnwzsezoorhe-vu7yjthyx]] returns Yu Qing after the battlefield disaster, now advising against executing the six-city cession to [[QinState|秦国]]. He argues that Qin withdrew because it was exhausted, so giving Qin land would help the enemy recover. His alternative is to give the same cities to [[QiState|齐国]], converting a humiliating cession into a visible [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] signal, though the episode cautions that Qi's willingness is doubtful.
