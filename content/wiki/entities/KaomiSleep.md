# Kaomi Sleep

## Overview
Kaomi Sleep is the sleep-product company founded by [[CaitlinKale]] in [[advice-line-with-kip-tindell-of-the-container-store-93508940-11b5-4b29-ba7b-eb077edee062]]. Its flagship product, the Sherpa Nest, is a fitted bedsheet with soft supportive borders sewn into the perimeter.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Kaomi Sleep as a fast-moving viral product company that must become a durable brand. Caitlin reports large organic social reach, pre-order growth without outside capital, $635,000 in eight-month revenue, a provisional patent, a pending trademark, and many scam or infringement accounts using product imagery.

## Key Characteristics
- Personal-origin sleep product rooted in Caitlin's teenage anxiety and pillow-fort sleeping routine.
- Viral consumer-product case that used organic views and pre-orders to scale without outside capital.
- IP and impersonation-risk case because copycats and scam accounts appeared quickly.
- Brand-expansion candidate around the feeling of security, comfort, sleep health, fabrics, patterns, pillows, and related sleep products.
