# 民政职业大学 / Minzheng Vocational University

Minzheng Vocational University / 民政职业大学 is the elder-care vocational-education institution in [[ep241-xiaoqi-hezuo-shi-xinyidai-de-tiefanwan-ma-lnmpsbvoqbexeardxyx88hywufig]]. [[LiXiaojie|李小杰]] uses the school to explain why [[ElderCareWorkforce|养老照护人才培养]] needs real elder-care scenes, not only theory and classroom simulation.

The source says the school has become a vocational undergraduate institution and that students increasingly consider civil-service exams, compiled-position exams, and other routes rather than automatically entering frontline elder care. That makes the school a key example for [[VocationalDegreeProgression|职业教育升学通道]] and the tension between professional training and credential-oriented mobility.
