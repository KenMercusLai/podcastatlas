# 刘如意 / Liu Ruyi

刘如意 / Liu Ruyi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-178-maodun-weihe-yao-fangguo-liubang-zhe-cai-shi-zhenxiang-2-loluh53iyrzo4ucf3ttzmn3-otkv|Hanji 178 part 2]] as [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s young son whom Liu Bang makes king of [[DaiKingdomEarlyHan|代国]] after [[LiuXiDaiKing|刘喜]] flees the Xiongnu-linked attack.

The source does not yet develop Liu Ruyi's later succession politics. Its immediate point is territorial: a vulnerable northern kingdom remains a Liu-family appointment, but the recipient changes from Liu Bang's elder brother to a son after Liu Xi's failure at the frontier.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-180-xihan-diyi-yinghan-guangao-de-gushi-1-li5u-dmsmb1lp4ny3rk02kaiyu4k|Hanji 180]] moves Liu Ruyi again, from Dai to the Zhao kingship after [[ZhangAo|张敖]] is released but stripped of the title. The source uses the move as part of Liu Bang's broader reduction of exposed or suspect non-Liu royal autonomy after the Guan Gao case.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-181-jiemi-liubang-feili-taizi-de-zhenshi-yuanyin-lgnbvnnfwxe4weupqgz-rh9cbp2s|Hanji 181]] turns Liu Ruyi from a replacement Zhao king into [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s favored alternative heir. The episode says Liu Bang keeps him in Chang'an, sees him as more like himself than [[LiuYing|刘盈]], and considers replacing the crown prince with him. The source's central point is that Liu Ruyi has neither a front-court bloc nor durable protection: [[QiJi|戚姬 / 戚夫人]] can plead for him, and [[ZhouChang|周昌]] is later sent to Zhao, but this still leaves him exposed under [[AlternativeHeirProtectionFailure|备位继承人保护失灵]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-189-liubang-siqian-zuihou-yibo-lllydj2xyk4cr7b9d2qx0hvfw-dz|Hanji 189]] closes Liu Ruyi's active replacement opportunity in this branch. Liu Bang still wants to make him crown prince, but [[ZhangLiang|张良]], [[ShusunTong|叔孙通]], and [[ShangshanSiHao|商山四皓]] make the existing heir's support too visible to overturn. Liu Ruyi remains the favored son, but the source shows no independent power base that could overcome Liu Ying's established position.
