# Gaius Marius Younger / 小马略

## Overview

Gaius Marius Younger / 小马略 is the young Marian figurehead whose accelerated consulship, temple-treasure seizure, and defeat at Praeneste show that anti-Sullan politics was also breaking republican restraint.

## Current Profile

Episode 704 introduces Marius Younger as the son and political heir of [[GaiusMarius]]. After [[LuciusCorneliusCinna]]'s death, he becomes a symbolic leader for the Marian side against [[LuciusCorneliusSulla]], even though he is only 26 and has held no major magistracy.

His career in the episode is short and violent. When the Temple of Jupiter burns, he takes the treasure stored there rather than rescuing sacred objects, then uses that wealth to pay private legions and bribe voters. His consulship in 82 BC is presented as an unprecedented breach of age and office norms. After Sulla defeats him in Campania, he retreats to Praeneste, remains trapped while the [[Samnites]] make their final move on Rome, and commits suicide before Sulla arrives.

## Key Characteristics

- He inherits Marian factional symbolism after the elder Marius has died.
- He becomes consul at 26 without the normal magistracy path.
- He uses sacred treasure for military and electoral purposes.
- He raises and funds private legions in the civil-war environment.
- His defeat at Praeneste removes the Marian family line as a political force.
