# Squishy Dumplings

Squishy Dumplings are the viral toy product at the center of [[what-makes-a-toy-go-viral]]. The episode describes them as rubbery, stretchy, gel-filled dumpling-shaped toys sold in small plastic steamer baskets, with versions that vary by color, scent, theme, and rarity.

Their wiki relevance is that they convert a simple sensory object into a platform-shaped product. [[ReallyMagicalStuff|RMS]] made the reveal part of the experience through [[MysteryToyPackaging]], then used [[CollectibleDropScarcity]] and [[IntermittentReinforcement]] to encourage repeat purchases. [[TikTok]] unboxing videos turned the opening moment into distribution, while [[FiveBelow]] made the price and channel fit low-friction impulse buying.

The product also illustrates the operating side of a viral fad. [[ZachBarber]] says RMS benefited from surprise demand because it had a [[ViralToySupplyChain]] built around short orders, fast factory approvals, direct retailer shipment, and limited U.S. inventory exposure.
