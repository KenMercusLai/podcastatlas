# Alibaba Cloud / 阿里云

[[jia-yangqing-wo-suo-jingli-de-rengongzhineng-yisi-dao-ai-dianfu-shijie-de-shunian-jubian-chuantai-shengdongjixi-s10e24-a3884ade-4669-4d5c-ab2e-f98aa580f429]] adds [[JiaYangqing|Jia Yangqing]]'s operator view of Alibaba Cloud as a place where compute, data warehousing, big-data systems, and AI support had to become customer-facing platform capability. The source links Alibaba Cloud to his later [[NeoCloud]] and [[LeptonAI|Lepton AI]] thesis about AI infrastructure as a product.

Alibaba Cloud / 阿里云 is the cloud-infrastructure arm of [[Alibaba]] behind [[AliyunBailian]] and the source's Alibaba supernode context. In [[1-yi-token-julebu-jibaole-ai-de-ranliao-bugoule-duitan-yu-wenyuan-aliyun-bailian-jishu-fuzeren-ltn5k9jd9e04i5mfdkdo-ycoslsm]], Alibaba Cloud appears through model serving, GPU scheduling, confidential inference, and the conversion of compute into tokens.

[[guochan-ai-suanli-neng-ping-chaojiedian-wandao-chaoche-ma-waic-shendu-guancha-s10e23-a6c6ab3e-72b2-470b-aefd-04b19679d37f]] adds Alibaba Cloud to the domestic [[AIAcceleratorSupernode|supernode]] landscape. The episode names Alibaba among the vendors with supernode-related displays or protocols, making the cloud unit part of [[ProprietaryAIInterconnectFragmentation]] and [[DomesticAIChipOrderValidation]] rather than only MaaS serving.

[[ai-chongji-qiye-ruanjian-jutou-yu-sap-yuanxin-liao-damoxing-to-b-de-dianfu-yu-bianjie-1-174-1]] adds Alibaba Cloud as [[SAP]]'s China infrastructure partner in the source. [[YuanXin]] says SAP products in China first land on Alibaba Cloud infrastructure, while [[Qwen]] is embedded into the model-service layer and both sides explore FDE, post-training, and customer scenarios.
