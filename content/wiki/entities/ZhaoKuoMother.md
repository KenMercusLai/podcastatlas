# 赵母 / Zhao Kuo's Mother

赵母 / Zhao Kuo's Mother enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-1-loxyybm3njrtnudydafwzu9jp9jz]] as the family witness who argues that [[ZhaoKuo|赵括]] should not command at [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]]. Her warning comes after [[LinXiangru|蔺相如]] has already objected and after [[ZhaoShe|赵奢]]'s earlier private judgment of his son is recalled.

Her argument is concrete rather than abstract. She contrasts Zhao She, who shared rewards with subordinate officers, cared for soldiers, and put state affairs before household affairs once commissioned, with Zhao Kuo, who accepts the appointment with visible arrogance, takes rewards home, and buys fields and houses. The episode uses her as the sharpest source for command character: a general must be able to share hardship, distribute credit, and stay focused on the war rather than private accumulation.

When [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] still appoints Zhao Kuo, she asks that the family not be punished if the campaign fails. The request links her warning to [[CollateralPunishmentAndGuiltByAssociation|guilt-by-association]] risk and shows that she expects failure strongly enough to seek protection before the battle outcome.
