# Hongmeng Zhixing / 鸿蒙智行

Hongmeng Zhixing / 鸿蒙智行 is the Huawei-led intelligent-vehicle alliance described in [[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]]. The source says the brand system grew from Huawei's earlier "smart selection car" cooperation and was upgraded in 2023 into a broader intelligent automotive technology ecosystem.

The entity matters because it makes [[HuaweiNoCarManufacturingBoundary|Huawei's no-car-manufacturing boundary]] visible in organizational form. [[Huawei]] does not hold the whole-vehicle qualification in the source's account; partner automakers own manufacturing, vehicle revenue, and the formal car brands, while Huawei shapes product definition, intelligent cockpit, assisted driving, electric-drive systems, launch events, retail channels, service standards, and user operations.

The portfolio is segmented by partner and price band. [[AITO|问界]] with [[Seres]] is the first breakthrough case, [[Luxeed|智界]] with [[Chery]] adds an export-capable manufacturing partner, [[Stelato|享界]] with [[BAICGroup|北汽]] attacks high-end sedans, [[Shangjie|尚界]] with [[SAICMotor|上汽]] targets lower price bands, and [[Maextro|尊界]] with [[JACGroup|江淮]] attempts ultra-luxury positioning.

## Source Position
- The source presents 鸿蒙智行 as a technology, product, brand, channel, and service ecosystem rather than a single automaker.
- Its claimed scale gives Huawei a possible [[AutonomousDrivingDataFlywheel|data and deployment flywheel]] in assisted driving and cockpit systems.
- The five brands are treated as differentiated cooperation cases, not one repeated template.
- The alliance also creates governance tension: strong Huawei involvement can raise product coherence, but partner automakers still carry manufacturing, delivery, and quality responsibility.
