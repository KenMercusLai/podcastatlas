# Hongmeng Zhixing / 鸿蒙智行

## Overview

Hongmeng Zhixing / 鸿蒙智行 is the Huawei-led intelligent-vehicle alliance tracked through [[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] and [[ep279-dang-fangxiangpan-manman-songkai-women-ruhe-yu-che-xiangchu-lq2elbnjy6xrhixxqjs-y7erhlnv]]. The current wiki treats it as a technology, product, brand, channel, service, and user-operation ecosystem rather than a conventional Huawei-owned automaker.

## Current Profile

The alliance makes [[HuaweiNoCarManufacturingBoundary|Huawei's no-car-manufacturing boundary]] visible in practice. Partner automakers keep vehicle qualification, factories, manufacturing, vehicle ownership, and delivery responsibility, while Huawei shapes product definition, intelligent cockpit, assisted driving, electric-drive systems, launches, retail channels, service standards, and feedback loops.

EP279 extends the profile from organization design into user experience. Its owners and product speaker show how the alliance sells not only vehicle brands but also assisted-driving trust, cabin living space, outdoor scenarios, and feedback-responsive product iteration.

## Key Characteristics

- Organizes multiple automaker partnerships under a Huawei-led product, technology, channel, and service system.
- Maintains a formal boundary where Huawei is not the whole-vehicle manufacturer while still exercising strong customer-facing product influence.
- Segments brands by partner and price or use case, including [[AITO|问界]], [[Luxeed|智界]], [[Stelato|享界]], [[Shangjie|尚界]], [[Maextro|尊界]], and the G9 case.
- Uses assisted driving, intelligent cockpit, channel explanation, and owner feedback as adoption mechanisms.
- EP279 adds a lifestyle branch where vehicles become offices, meeting rooms, rest spaces, camping infrastructure, and family interaction spaces.

- **Partner segmentation:** [[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] maps [[AITO|问界]] to [[Seres]], [[Luxeed|智界]] to [[Chery]], [[Stelato|享界]] to [[BAICGroup|北汽]], [[Shangjie|尚界]] to [[SAICMotor|上汽]], and [[Maextro|尊界]] to [[JACGroup|江淮]].
- **Trust and driving adoption:** [[ep279-dang-fangxiangpan-manman-songkai-women-ruhe-yu-che-xiangchu-lq2elbnjy6xrhixxqjs-y7erhlnv]] has owners describe moving from distrust to routine assisted-driving use through concrete road and parking experiences.
- **Living-space branch:** [[ep279-dang-fangxiangpan-manman-songkai-women-ruhe-yu-che-xiangchu-lq2elbnjy6xrhixxqjs-y7erhlnv]] adds work, meeting, recording, rest, oxygen, family, and camping uses around M9, R7, and G9.

## Qualifications

- The alliance's formal structure still leaves manufacturing, delivery, and quality obligations with partner automakers.
- EP279 is a brand-and-owner conversation, so its positive assisted-driving and lifestyle evidence should be read beside independent testing and regulation sources.
- Delivery-scale and feedback-process claims remain source-scoped unless corroborated by separate sources.
