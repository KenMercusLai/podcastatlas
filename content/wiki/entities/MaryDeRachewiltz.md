# Mary de Rachewiltz

Mary de Rachewiltz appears in [[i-robot-ai-and-consciousness-6a880840b0d5e24ea40c7015]] through an obituary-style segment about her childhood, translation work, and relationship to [[EzraPound]]. The source says she was born to Pound and Olga, was raised by foster parents in South Tyrol, and later defended Pound's lucidity while continuing to translate [[TheCantos]].

The episode frames Mary as a figure of real affection and moral complication. Her 1985 dual-language English-Italian version of [[TheCantos]] becomes both a literary achievement and an act of loyalty to a father whose [[ItalianFascism|fascist]] commitments and wartime conduct cannot be ignored.
