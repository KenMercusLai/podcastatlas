# 刘肥 / Liu Fei (Qi king)

刘肥 / Liu Fei enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-174-liubang-weihe-dafeng-zijide-chouren-1-lnzzvpieqtgatmvgogz3msyhs60u|Hanji 174]] as [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s son from before the formal imperial household order. Liu Bang grants him the early Han [[QiKingdomEarlyHan|齐国]], described by the source as the broad region where people speak the Qi language.

This page is intentionally named `LiuFeiQiKing` rather than `LiuFei`: the wiki already has [[LiuFei|刘飞]], a modern podcast host with a different Chinese character and subject. The semantic suffix keeps the public route distinct while preserving display links as 刘肥 where appropriate.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-188-gudai-zhongguo-de-tianxia-wushui-xian-lnoib0bvz8w0ihvhxmtx19tr4jdz|Hanji 188]] gives Liu Fei's Qi kingdom a military-support role. When [[LiuBang|刘邦]] waits behind fortifications against [[YingBu|英布 / 黥布]], Liu Fei and [[CaoShen|曹参]] arrive with a large Qi force; only then does Liu Bang order the multi-sided attack that breaks Ying Bu.
