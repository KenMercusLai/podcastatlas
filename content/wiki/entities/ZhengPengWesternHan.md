## Overview

郑朋 / Zheng Peng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-478-chushi-zhihui-zhezhong-ren-tai-jiduan-yudao-qing-yuanli-lq3hhg58lohdi-tbpmcuhwajjo7h|Hanji 478]] as a Western Han court actor whose failed attachment to [[XiaoWangzhiWesternHan|萧望之]] turns into resentment and then accusation through [[ShiGaoWesternHan|史高]]'s outer-relative side.

## Current Profile

The episode gives Zheng Peng a compact but sharp profile: he had connections to [[ZhouKanWesternHan|周堪]], tried to attach himself to Xiao Wangzhi, and was rejected when Xiao judged him corrupt or untrustworthy. Zheng then carries private resentment into factional politics, moving toward Shi Gao and supplying accusations against Xiao Wangzhi, Zhou Kan, and Liu Gengsheng.

Zheng Peng therefore matters less as an independent policy figure than as a mechanism of political injury. The source uses him to show how a private slight can become public accusation when an ambitious person finds a hostile faction willing to use his grievance.

## Key Characteristics

- Former friend or associate of Zhou Kan in the episode's account.
- Attempts to attach himself to Xiao Wangzhi but is rejected as untrustworthy.
- Turns resentment against Xiao Wangzhi into factional alignment with Shi Gao's side.
- Supplies accusations used against Xiao Wangzhi, Zhou Kan, and Liu Gengsheng.
- Illustrates how private grievance can become political weaponry.
