## Overview

郑朋 / Zheng Peng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-478-chushi-zhihui-zhezhong-ren-tai-jiduan-yudao-qing-yuanli-lq3hhg58lohdi-tbpmcuhwajjo7h|Hanji 478]] as a Western Han court actor whose failed attachment to [[XiaoWangzhiWesternHan|萧望之]] turns into resentment and then accusation through [[ShiGaoWesternHan|史高]]'s outer-relative side. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-469-weishenme-junzi-dou-buguo-xiaoren-kankan-xiaowangzhi-de-xiachang-lj8iji-c3scj6vznntavm6awolho|Hanji 469]] supplies the fuller prequel: Zheng first enters Xiao and [[ZhouKanWesternHan|周堪]]'s circle, learns enough to become dangerous, and then defects after Xiao cuts him off.

## Current Profile

The source set gives Zheng Peng a compact but sharp profile: he has connections to Zhou Kan, uses the rivalry between Xiao's side and Shi Gao's side to enter Xiao's circle, and is rejected when Xiao judges him corrupt or untrustworthy. Zheng then carries private resentment into factional politics, moving toward Shi Gao and [[XuZhangWesternHan|许章]] and supplying accusations against Xiao Wangzhi, Zhou Kan, and Liu Gengsheng.

Zheng Peng therefore matters less as an independent policy figure than as a mechanism of political injury. The source uses him to show how a private slight can become public accusation when an ambitious person finds a hostile faction willing to use his grievance.

## Key Characteristics

- Former friend or associate of Zhou Kan in the episode's account.
- Attempts to attach himself to Xiao Wangzhi, enters the circle long enough to gather information, but is rejected as untrustworthy.
- Turns resentment against Xiao Wangzhi into factional alignment with Shi Gao's side.
- Supplies accusations used against Xiao Wangzhi, Zhou Kan, and Liu Gengsheng.
- Helps show how a weak boundary around an ambitious attaché can become a later procedural weapon.
- Illustrates how private grievance can become political weaponry.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 478 and Hanji 469. It does not settle Zheng Peng's full offices, exact relationship to Zhou Kan, the content of his accusations, his later fate, or the broader legal record around Xiao Wangzhi's fall.
