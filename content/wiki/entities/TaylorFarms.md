# Taylor Farms

Taylor Farms is the large U.S. salad processor at the center of [[big-lettuce-meets-small-intestine]]. The [[PlanetMoney]] episode presents the company as the leading American salad supplier and as a key example of how [[BaggedSaladIndustrialization]] turned lettuce into a standardized, value-added product.

In the source, Taylor Farms buys lettuce mostly from [[California]], [[Arizona]], and increasingly [[Mexico]], grows some of its own, and processes produce through more than two dozen facilities. Its customer list includes [[TacoBell]], [[McDonalds|McDonald's]], [[Costco]], [[WholeFoodsMarket|Whole Foods]], Aldi, and Safeway.

The company matters to the wiki because its scale illustrates [[ProduceContaminationAmplification]]. A processor can make fresh produce more convenient and consistent, but central washing, chopping, mixing, and national distribution can spread a small contamination event much farther than a local farm stand would.

## Key Claims
- The episode says Taylor Farms was founded in 1995 by [[BruceTaylor]] and became the largest U.S. salad processor.
- Taylor Farms is treated as a processor and middleman rather than only a farm.
- The source says Taylor Farms issued a 27-state recall for lettuce that could have been contaminated during the July 2026 [[Cyclospora]] outbreak.
- Its model depends on consistent year-round supply and consumer expectations that lettuce products can be available every week.
