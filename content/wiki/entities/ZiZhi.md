# 子之 / Zi Zhi

子之 / Zi Zhi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-52-chenzhuang-panqin-duanqi-wan-chifan-fangxia-wan-ma-niang-ls13ixmbhflepjgpelxgu6idjqa2]] as the negative talent-judgment example in [[YanState|燕国]]. The episode says [[YanWangKuai|燕王哙]] treated Zi Zhi as a worthy person, but that judgment helped lead to Yan's collapse and Qi's intervention.

The source uses Zi Zhi mainly as contrast. [[YanZhaowang|燕昭王]] later treats [[LeYi|乐毅]] and [[JuXin|剧辛]] as the real talent base for Yan's recovery, showing that valuing talent is not enough if the ruler cannot judge who is actually useful.
