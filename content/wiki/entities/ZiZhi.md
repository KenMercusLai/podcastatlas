# 子之 / Zi Zhi

子之 / Zi Zhi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-52-chenzhuang-panqin-duanqi-wan-chifan-fangxia-wan-ma-niang-ls13ixmbhflepjgpelxgu6idjqa2]] as the negative talent-judgment example in [[YanState|燕国]]. The episode says [[YanWangKuai|燕王哙]] treated Zi Zhi as a worthy person, but that judgment helped lead to Yan's collapse and Qi's intervention.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-47-yanwangkuai-de-lixiangzhuyi-lhgl6rqkv9nonzxc9xqquzzlz5pr]] gives the mechanism behind that judgment. Zi Zhi is linked to [[SuDai|苏代]] by marriage, and Su Dai first gets Yan Wang Kuai to treat trust in Zi Zhi as the difference between a hegemonic ruler and an ineffective one. [[LuMaoshou|鹿毛寿]] then turns that trust into abdication language and finally into real appointment authority.

The source presents Zi Zhi's public refusal of the state as tactically useful rather than proof of worthiness. Once Yan Wang Kuai hands over the seals for senior officials, people and finances follow Zi Zhi, making him the practical ruler and the beneficiary of [[RuleBreakingAbdication|破坏规则式禅让]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-48-junwang-weisha-buneng-chuanwei-zaixiang-lnwhljnn888mzlj7let2ptn1pcdm]] shows the practical rule collapsing. After three years of Zi Zhi's control, [[YanState|燕国]] is disordered enough that [[ShiBei|市被]] and the crown prince attack him, then the failed internal move opens the way for [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]] and [[ZhangZiQiGeneral|张子]]. Qi captures Zi Zhi and kills him brutally, turning his rise from appointment power into a negative case for [[LiAsPoliticalOrder|礼制政治秩序]].

The source uses Zi Zhi mainly as contrast. [[YanZhaowang|燕昭王]] later treats [[LeYi|乐毅]] and [[JuXin|剧辛]] as the real talent base for Yan's recovery, showing that valuing talent is not enough if the ruler cannot judge who is actually useful.
