# Ctrip / Trip.com Group

## Overview
Ctrip / Trip.com Group is represented in the wiki as China's major online-travel platform: a company built from hotel booking, call centers, ticketing operations, supplier systems, customer service, mobile recovery, capital consolidation, hotel-channel dependence, global expansion, antitrust pressure, and now AI travel-planning ambitions. The current synthesis holds both sides together: Ctrip created real booking convenience and service infrastructure, but the same infrastructure can become pricing, ranking, inventory, and merchant-control power.

## Current Profile
Ctrip's platform power comes from both capital history and operating infrastructure. [[kunzai-xitong-li-de-jiudian-ni-buzhidao-de-xiecheng-longduan-lianchengshi-keji-luandun]] uses [[LiangJianzhang]], [[JiQi]], [[ShenNanpeng]], and [[FanMin]] for the founding mix; [[WuHai]], [[Shangzhixing]], [[ModernExpressTravel]], [[WangShengli]], and [[CoastAirService]] for early booking and fulfillment capacity; and later ties to [[Qunar]], [[Elong]], and [[TongchengTravel]] for the broader platform system.

Hotel groups respond to Ctrip as a demand channel they cannot ignore but may partly route around. [[140-jiudian-jituan-weishenme-dou-rezhongyu-dazao-huiyuan-he-jifen-tixi-chuantai-yuanxingzhe-yu-suibingjiang-lj-xa3wxzjog6-8zm9nfakh6ulew]] grounds that response in [[HotelLoyaltyPrograms]] and [[HotelDirectBookingChannels]], while also noting that Chinese OTAs have made their own membership benefits more valuable after the pandemic.

The penalty-centered governance layer turns Ctrip from a concentration story into a conduct story. [[ep91-dingfang-dingpiao-dingjiangshan-xiecheng-51-yi-wei-aoman-maidan-lovhfkz4rklv1ik-uqyeswrdf3uw]] says a [[StateAdministrationForMarketRegulation]] action centered on merchant-side [[TravelPlatformMerchantExclusivity]] and [[TravelPriceParityEnforcement]], while cancellation, price disclosure, and hidden-fee complaints remained part of the broader trust problem.

The founder-side operating account clarifies why Ctrip's power is not just traffic. [[liangjianzhang-luoyonghao-xiecheng-renkou-ai-ljpurcsyivjkwjyak-3kt3zly-fp]] has Liang Jianzhang emphasize service process detail, mobile transition, hybrid-work experiments, pandemic live-streaming, internationalization, employee fertility support, and travel-domain AI; those same operating assets also explain why governance has to inspect data, user support, live inventory, and transaction closure.

The earnings layer is now explicit. [[tulasi-pingguo-cheng-jinnian-aimeijiang-de-zuida-yingjia-xiecheng-erjidu-youying-zhuankui-1015756412]] reports second-quarter 2026 revenue growth of about 6% alongside a loss caused by the antitrust penalty, about RMB 2.7 billion of net profit excluding that penalty, slightly lower transport-ticketing revenue, and international platform revenue growth above 50% led by inbound travel and Asia-Pacific. The same source carries an [[HSBC]] view that soft summer travel demand, antitrust rectification, international expansion, and AI investment may keep margins pressured for two to three quarters, which makes the penalty a continuing operating constraint rather than a closed event.

## Key Characteristics
- Chinese [[OnlineTravelAgency]] built from hotel booking, call centers, ticketing, offline fulfillment, and later mobile app habits.
- Platform whose hotel inventory, traffic, invoices, business-travel convenience, and supplier systems reinforce [[OTAPlatformConcentration]].
- Company whose e-booking and PMS-adjacent tools can create both efficiency and [[HotelPlatformPricingPower]] concerns.
- Demand channel that hotel groups try to offset through loyalty programs and direct booking.
- Platform-governance case where merchant exclusivity, full-network-lowest-price enforcement, repricing tools, and hidden fees are central trust issues, and where the resulting penalty was large enough to turn a quarter into a loss.
- Internationalizing travel company tied to the Trip.com brand, [[MakeMyTrip]], [[Skyscanner]], and Asian and global travel markets.
- Travel-AI operator whose potential advantage comes from live inventory, prices, support, user preference, and booking data rather than generic chatbot fluency alone.

- Platform concentration: [[kunzai-xitong-li-de-jiudian-ni-buzhidao-de-xiecheng-longduan-lianchengshi-keji-luandun]] links Ctrip's inventory, PMS-adjacent systems, traffic, business-travel habits, and consolidation with [[Qunar]], [[Elong]], and [[TongchengTravel]] to [[OTAPlatformConcentration]].
- Hotel-channel response: [[140-jiudian-jituan-weishenme-dou-rezhongyu-dazao-huiyuan-he-jifen-tixi-chuantai-yuanxingzhe-yu-suibingjiang-lj-xa3wxzjog6-8zm9nfakh6ulew]] shows hotel groups using direct channels, loyalty, brand portfolios, and service data to reduce OTA dependence.
- Antitrust and trust: [[ep91-dingfang-dingpiao-dingjiangshan-xiecheng-51-yi-wei-aoman-maidan-lovhfkz4rklv1ik-uqyeswrdf3uw]] makes the alleged abuse mechanics concrete through traffic status, full-network-lowest-price promises, cross-platform price monitoring, automatic repricing, and order-reserve controls.
- Mobile transition: [[liangjianzhang-luoyonghao-xiecheng-renkou-ai-ljpurcsyivjkwjyak-3kt3zly-fp]] adds the founder-side claim that the 2012-era mobile transition required "all in mobile," app-first discussion, and organizational change.
- Internationalization: [[kunzai-xitong-li-de-jiudian-ni-buzhidao-de-xiecheng-longduan-lianchengshi-keji-luandun]], [[ep91-dingfang-dingpiao-dingjiangshan-xiecheng-51-yi-wei-aoman-maidan-lovhfkz4rklv1ik-uqyeswrdf3uw]], and [[liangjianzhang-luoyonghao-xiecheng-renkou-ai-ljpurcsyivjkwjyak-3kt3zly-fp]] all connect Ctrip to global travel through Trip.com, [[MakeMyTrip]], [[Skyscanner]], and overseas market growth.
- Travel AI: [[ep91-dingfang-dingpiao-dingjiangshan-xiecheng-51-yi-wei-aoman-maidan-lovhfkz4rklv1ik-uqyeswrdf3uw]] frames [[CtripWendao]] as a trust-recovery asset, while [[liangjianzhang-luoyonghao-xiecheng-renkou-ai-ljpurcsyivjkwjyak-3kt3zly-fp]] adds Liang's argument that reliable travel AI requires real-time locations, prices, inventory, preferences, and transaction data.
- Penalty-driven earnings: [[tulasi-pingguo-cheng-jinnian-aimeijiang-de-zuida-yingjia-xiecheng-erjidu-youying-zhuankui-1015756412]] gives the second-quarter 2026 revenue growth, the loss caused by a penalty close to RMB 5.2 billion, about RMB 2.7 billion of net profit excluding it, a roughly 1% decline in transport ticketing, and more than 50% growth in international platform revenue with inbound travel and Asia-Pacific leading.
- Margin outlook: [[tulasi-pingguo-cheng-jinnian-aimeijiang-de-zuida-yingjia-xiecheng-erjidu-youying-zhuankui-1015756412]] cites HSBC that weak summer travel demand plus antitrust rectification, international expansion, and AI investment may keep margins under pressure for two to three quarters.

## Qualifications
The evidence mixes critical platform-governance sources with founder-side retrospective claims. Ctrip's service and data assets can create real consumer value, but those same assets can strengthen pricing power, merchant dependence, and ranking opacity. Claims about internal experiment results, live-streaming contribution, international revenue mix, and AI product reliability remain source-reported rather than independently verified in the wiki.
