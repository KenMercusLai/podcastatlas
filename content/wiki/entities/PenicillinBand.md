# 盘尼西林乐队 / Penicillin

## Overview
盘尼西林 is the rock band in which spine surgeon [[MaHaoning|马浩宁]] is presented as a keyboard and accordion player.

## Current Profile
The source says the band already existed around 2012–2013 and that Ma joined near the end of 2015 after meeting members through Beijing's live-music network. He later participated in *The Big Band*, a tour, and selected larger performances, while remaining unable to take on the band's full-time schedule.

The name predates Ma's involvement. According to his account, band member 小乐 chose “盘尼西林” because the four Chinese characters sounded striking; the later presence of a doctor in a band named after penicillin is presented as coincidence rather than founding logic.

## Key Characteristics
- Chinese rock band reported to have existed before Ma Haoning joined.
- Uses the Chinese name for penicillin as its band name without a medical founding story in this source.
- Includes Ma as a keyboard and accordion contributor for selected performances.
- Appeared with Ma in the 2019 season of *The Big Band* and in later live-performance contexts.
