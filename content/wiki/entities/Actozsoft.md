# Actozsoft

Actozsoft is one of the Korean companies tied to [[LegendOfMir|《传奇》]] in [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]]. The episode describes Legend's copyright and operational rights as complex from the beginning because Actozsoft and [[WeMade]] were both attached to the underlying IP while [[Shanda|盛大]] held the China operating opportunity.

Shanda's later purchase of a large Actozsoft stake is presented as a classic attempt to convert an agency vulnerability into ownership leverage. It helped Shanda manage one side of the dispute, but it did not eliminate all [[OnlineGameIPRightsDisputes]] around Legend's later mobile, private-server, and licensed-product afterlife.
