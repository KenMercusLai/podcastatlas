# 美嘉 / Mijia

美嘉 appears in [[137-qinli-riben-shengyu-butie-guojia-bang-wo-yangwa-guilai-reng-shi-tunjinshou-lowze7haomxbmqrrajpl51dvvxe7]] as a [[NihongDianbo|霓虹电波]] guest and parent who has raised a child across China-Japan contexts. Her experience adds a maternal and household-administration view of [[JapaneseFertilitySupportSystem|Japanese fertility support]]: birth-related benefits, municipal paperwork, child allowance deposits, child medical coverage, and later education spending.

Her examples complicate a simple "Japan pays people to have children" story. The child was born in [[China]], but the family could still receive Japanese-related childbirth and child benefits because the child's father was employed and socially insured in [[Japan]]. She also emphasizes that child medical support feels most valuable when compared with routine pediatric expenses after returning to China.
