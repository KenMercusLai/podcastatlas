## Overview

Hollywood Haunted Hayride is a Los Angeles Halloween attraction operated by [[ThirteenthFloorEntertainment|13th Floor Entertainment]] and used in the source as a case of coordinated live and technical scare design.

## Current Profile

The attraction combines performers, audio, lighting, animatronic props, motion sensors, and small show controllers. In the episode's account, these systems are not independent spectacles: atmosphere prepares the guest, sensors detect movement, controllers trigger timed effects, and live performers deliver the interpersonal surprise that automation cannot fully substitute.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses lighting and audio to establish atmosphere before individual scares.
- Uses motion sensing and distributed controllers to trigger effects at selected moments.
- Combines automated props with live performers rather than treating them as substitutes.
- Belongs to a temporary-production model requiring setup and teardown.
- Presents engineering as hidden infrastructure for story, timing, and audience response.
