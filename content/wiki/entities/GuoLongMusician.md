# 郭龙 / Guo Long

## Overview
郭龙 / Guo Long is represented as [[ZhangWeiweiMusician|张玮玮]]'s Baiyin childhood acquaintance, longtime friend, and musical collaborator whose relationship with Zhang runs from adolescent conflict through Guangzhou busking, Beijing scene-building, joint performance, rupture, and later reconciliation.

## Current Profile
Guo's importance in the bounded source is relational rather than a complete solo biography. He first appears within adolescent peer hierarchy, later joins Zhang in Guangzhou and Beijing survival, becomes the first bartender at the [[WildChildrenBand|野孩子]]-associated 合酒吧, and remains part of Zhang's performing and creative life across decades. Their repeated separations and repairs make collaboration look less like permanent harmony than a durable capacity to resume honest exchange.

## Key Characteristics
- Childhood peer whose early relationship with Zhang included intimidation and later friendship.
- Survival and music partner across Guangzhou street performance and Beijing independent-music life.
- Participant in the social and practical infrastructure around 合酒吧.
- Long-term collaborator whose bond with Zhang survives repeated artistic and personal rupture.
