## Overview

吕禄 / Lü Lu is presented as the Lü-family general who held the Northern Army in Chang'an after [[LuZhi|吕雉]]'s death, then surrendered its seal and command under pressure from his friend [[LiJiWesternHan|郦寄]], opening the way for [[ZhouBo|周勃]] to take the capital's central force.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-205-nvhuangdi-lvzhi-jingran-yin-gou-er-si-2-ltgwfdhn9cdwphudy0blyh36fge1|Hanji 205 part 2]] says Lü Lu followed Lu Zhi's deathbed instruction to remain with the northern army rather than attend her funeral. That arrangement helped preserve immediate order but also made control of the capital's troops the decisive object of the emerging anti-Lü coalition.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-205-nvhuangdi-lvzhi-jingran-yin-gou-er-si-1-loyaahdx4svudlgwwq5ww_fcj8f9|Hanji 205 part 1]] supplies the instruction before the crisis. Lu Zhi reportedly names Lü Lu commander of the Northern Army, warns that ministers oppose Lü-family kingship, and orders him to remain in camp and protect the palace rather than attend her funeral. The source establishes the episode's security logic but not Lü Lu's own commitment, legal authority, or later coup intent.

The same source makes Lü Lu's daughter the wife of [[LiuZhangChengyangKingWesternHan|刘章]] and the reported intelligence channel through which Liu Zhang learned of an alleged Lü-family coup plan. Her loyalty, the plan, and Lü Lu's own intent remain claims inside the episode's transmitted narrative.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-207-liubang-xiaoyizi-lvxu-bei-gunsha-1-lsrs42rb8-qref1eevqdnhe3mufv|Hanji 207 part 1]] supplies the outcome: [[LiJiWesternHan|郦寄]] reportedly helps separate Lü Lu from Northern Army control, after which the victorious coalition kills Lü Lu and his household during the wider Lü-family purge. The source's Ban Gu defense centers on alleged coercion of Li Ji's father and does not settle Lü Lu's full intent or legal culpability.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-206-zhu-lv-an-liu-shishang-diyi-miemen-an-2-lpsj8exoquanifh7njkrhqszrhkh0|Hanji 206 part 2]] reconstructs the transfer itself. A proposal presents surrender of office as a way to remove Qi's pretext and protect the Lü household; Lü Lu hesitates amid divided family advice, trusts Li Ji, hands over the general's seal, and leaves the camp. Zhou Bo then enters and tests the troops' allegiance. The conduct is source-supported within the episode, but Lü Lu's private reasoning and whether voluntary retreat could actually have secured safety remain uncertain.

## Key Characteristics

- Lü-family military figure holding the northern army after Lu Zhi's death.
- Executor of a deathbed security arrangement designed around anticipated ministerial resistance and uninterrupted command in Chang'an.
- Father-in-law of Liu Zhang through an unnamed daughter.
- Political target whose military position helps trigger inside-outside anti-Lü coordination.
- Friend of Li Ji whose trust enables the reported handover of the general's seal and Northern Army command.
- Defeated factional figure whose attempted retreat fails to prevent household liquidation.

## Qualifications

This remains a source-bounded profile. The deathbed wording, alleged coup plan, the daughter's information and motives, Lü Lu's intent, coercion of Li Shang, exact chain of command, proposal, dialogue, seals, tally procedure, arm-baring response, death list, and legal basis of household punishment require comparison with transmitted histories. The promise that surrender would protect the Lü household is a proposal inside the narrative, not proof of a viable or enforceable settlement.

## What Changed

- Added the pre-death assignment, ministerial-threat assessment, and explicit order to maintain uninterrupted Northern Army command.
