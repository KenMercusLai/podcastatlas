# Airbus

Airbus appears in [[139-cong-zuo-ri-de-shi-jie-dao-ru-jin-de-ou-zhou-mei-hao-nian-dai-belle-epoque-ta-hai-neng-hui-lai-ma-ltvsgov8ht9-l7yp9qamdgjmyrf]] as the episode's clearest example of European industrial integration. The source describes final assembly in Toulouse, work in Hamburg, Spanish tail and rear-fuselage responsibilities, and British wing-related participation as evidence that production can be deliberately distributed across European countries.

The episode's interpretation is political as much as industrial. Airbus represents [[EuropeanIntegrationWarPrevention]]: supply chains and industrial roles can make European economies mutually embedded, reducing incentives for internal conflict. The source also qualifies the idea by noting that economic dependence outside the European common project did not prevent the Russia-Ukraine war.
