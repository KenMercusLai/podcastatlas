## Overview

甘忠可 / Gan Zhongke enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-581-han-aidi-zai-guishen-zhidao-you-duo-yuchun-ltok7kbzmuf-os84spseww0qjbrm|Hanji 581]] as a Western Han fangshi-style figure from Qi who uses [[Taipingjing|《太平经》]] and renewed-mandate language to approach [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] during the emperor's no-heir anxiety.

## Current Profile

The source presents Gan Zhongke as a specialist who turns an actual imperial vulnerability into a cosmic diagnosis. He claims Chengdi's lack of heirs shows that Liu-family mandate power has declined and that the emperor must receive Heaven's command anew. [[LiuXiang|刘向]] rejects this as spirit-based deception, Chengdi accepts Liu Xiang's judgment, and Gan dies in prison after arrest.

Gan's significance is larger than his failed Chengdi approach because [[XiaHeliangWesternHan|夏贺良]] later preserves the method and reintroduces it under [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]. The source therefore treats Gan as the origin point of a technique that can survive punishment and become newly persuasive when court disorder, illness, and omen anxiety intensify.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses no-heir anxiety as the opening for a heavenly-mandate diagnosis.
- Links [[Taipingjing|《太平经》]] to Liu-family sacred legitimacy rather than to ordinary private cult practice.
- Fails because Chengdi consults Liu Xiang and accepts skeptical counsel.
- Becomes politically important through transmission to Xia Heliang after his own death.

## Qualifications

This page reflects the episode's account rather than a full intellectual history of Gan Zhongke or the Taipingjing tradition. His authorship, textual details, follower network, and inner motive remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Created from Hanji 581 as the Chengdi-era origin point for the renewed-mandate fangshi method later used under Han Aidi.

## Relationships

- [[Taipingjing|《太平经》]] - text Gan uses to package mandate renewal and social peace.

- [[XiaHeliangWesternHan|夏贺良]] - disciple who preserves and later reactivates Gan's method.
