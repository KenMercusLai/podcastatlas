## Overview

《僮约》 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-433-xihan-wangbao-de-shengzhu-de-xianchen-song-shuo-le-sha-lmaah4tjg_gsewzb6izhojtommlx|Hanji 433]] as [[WangBaoWesternHan|王褒]]'s other important work. The episode contrasts it with [[ShengzhuDeXianchenSong|《圣主得贤臣颂》]]: the ode wins court praise, while 《僮约》 is presented as more valuable to historians because it preserves concrete traces of Han-era Sichuan daily life.

## Current Profile

The current profile is source-scoped and evidentiary. Hanji 433 says 《僮约》 records things Wang Bao encountered in Sichuan and is important for studying social conditions there. The episode especially foregrounds its tea references, describing it as an early record for drinking, buying, and planting tea.

## Key Characteristics

- Work associated with [[WangBaoWesternHan|王褒]] in the source's account.
- Valued less as court praise than as social-history evidence.
- Presented as a rare window into Han-era Sichuan daily practices.
- Treated by the episode as significant for early tea-culture history.
