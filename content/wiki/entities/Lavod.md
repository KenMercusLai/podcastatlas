# Lavod

Lavod is one of [[Evoken]]'s main products discussed by [[ChenMian]] in [[duihua-liblib-chenmian-guanyu-huoxialai-yiji-suoyou-jiejin-siwang-de-shike-1-175-1]]. The source does not give a full product teardown, but it uses Lavod to show how overseas AI application competition can force hard pricing, subsidy, and survival decisions.

Chen says Lavod went through roughly two months of aggressive subsidies after larger overseas platforms subsidized first, and that the later model-cost and compute environment made that strategy unsustainable. In the interview, Lavod becomes part of [[AIStartupUnitEconomics]] and [[AIApplicationSurvivalStrategy]] rather than only a product label.

## Key Points
- The source presents Lavod as an overseas growth and competition front inside [[Evoken]].
- It is described as a product that came out of a painful environment, suggesting that pressure can create product experimentation but also organizational drag.
- Chen still sees overseas room for Lavod, while acknowledging that company attention had to return to product, engineering, and organization problems.
