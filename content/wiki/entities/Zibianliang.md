# 自变量 / Zibianliang

## Overview
自变量 is an embodied-intelligence company represented in [[dang-jushen-zhineng-zoudao-shizilukou-duitan-sudu-mayi-lingbo-zibianliang-poke-sizhong-yixian-panduan-ls6w3mrjjrecuqg33yc3lbdmdzpf]] by [[WangQianZibianliang]]. The source positions the company through a data-and-infrastructure view of robot intelligence: model capability depends on the data, validation, hardware, simulation, and real-world evaluation loops needed to make physical action work.

## Current Profile
In this source, Zibianliang is less a body-first robotics company than a participant in the robot foundation-model and deployment debate. Wang Qian argues that large general models entering robotics would still need to build embodied data pipelines, simulators, robot hardware, and real-world validation. That makes the competitive boundary a system problem rather than a simple question of who has the largest language model.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats embodied intelligence as a data-and-validation problem, not only a model-architecture contest.
- Sees general foundation-model companies as potential entrants that would still have to become robotics system builders.
- Uses customer payment and expanding recurring value as the cleanest commercial test.
- Frames logistics and other productive scenarios through ROI rather than demo visibility alone.
- Connects robotics to long-run physical production and the missing material layer in AI self-improvement stories.
