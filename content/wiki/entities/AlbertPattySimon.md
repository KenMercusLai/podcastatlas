# Albert "Patty" Simon

[[AlbertPattySimon]] is the Barbudan historical voice in [[sand-heists-and-property-rights-in-the-caribbean-summer-school]]. He explains how [[Barbuda]] moved from colonial leasehold under Christopher Codrington, through slavery and abolition, into a land system where formerly enslaved people remained on the island without adopting ordinary private title.

The source uses Simon's account to ground [[PathDependence]]. Barbuda's current land conflict is not treated as a fresh policy choice in a vacuum; it inherits the institutional absence, neglect, and community practice that later became [[CommunalLandTenure]] and then the [[BarbudaLandAct]].
