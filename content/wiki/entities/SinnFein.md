# Sinn Fein

Sinn Fein appears in [[699-the-troubles-return-of-the-ira-part-3-glt9519811642]] through the republican split that followed the December 1969 division inside the [[IrishRepublicanArmy|IRA]]. The episode says a similar split occurred in Sinn Fein after dissident IRA members formed the [[ProvisionalIRA]], connecting armed strategy to the wider republican political vehicle.

The source uses [[GerryAdams]] as the later disputed personnel link between Sinn Fein, the Provisional IRA, and the politics that eventually moved toward settlement. The page is therefore source-scoped to the episode's 1969-1971 republican split rather than a full party history.
