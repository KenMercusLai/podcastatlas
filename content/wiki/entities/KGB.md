# KGB / 克格勃

[[KGB|KGB / 克格勃]] enters the wiki through [[80-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-shang-691866649]] as the [[SovietUnion|Soviet]] state-security and foreign-intelligence institution that formed [[OlegGordievsky|Oleg Gordievsky]]. The episode links it backward to the NKVD family background and forward to the Cold War intelligence station culture that sent Gordievsky to [[Denmark]].

The source presents the KGB as both bureaucracy and threat environment. Its career incentives included family control, marriage expectations, foreign-posting competition, surveillance of officers, ideological conformity, and fear of internal betrayal. That makes the institution central to [[TotalitarianDoubleLife|totalitarian double life]]: officers could perform loyalty while privately distrusting the system.

The episode also gives the KGB a historical mirror through [[KimPhilby|Kim Philby]], whose success as a Soviet source inside British intelligence became part of KGB institutional mythology before Gordievsky later moved in the opposite direction.
