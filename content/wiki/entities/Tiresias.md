# Tiresias

## Overview

Tiresias is the dead prophet whose underworld warning tells [[Odysseus]] how the return can still fail.

## Current Profile

In [[687-the-odyssey-hero-of-the-trojan-horse-part-1-glt2393125423]], Odysseus must travel to Hades and let the dead drink sacrificial blood before Tiresias can speak. Tiresias warns him not to harm the cattle of [[Helios]] on Thrinacia and foretells that disobedience will leave Odysseus returning late, miserable, without his comrades, and facing suitors in his house.

## Key Characteristics

- Tiresias makes the homecoming conditional rather than guaranteed.
- His warning repeats and sharpens [[Poseidon]]'s curse through a specific taboo around Helios' cattle.
- He shifts the story into explicit mortality because Odysseus must seek guidance from the dead.
- His prophecy turns later crew hunger into a known violation rather than an accidental mistake.

- Cattle warning: [[687-the-odyssey-hero-of-the-trojan-horse-part-1-glt2393125423]] says Tiresias warns Odysseus not to kill or eat Helios' cattle.
- Return prophecy: [[687-the-odyssey-hero-of-the-trojan-horse-part-1-glt2393125423]] says Tiresias foretells a late, miserable, companionless return and trouble from suitors.
