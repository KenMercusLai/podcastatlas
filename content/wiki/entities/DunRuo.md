# 顿弱 / Dun Ruo

顿弱 / Dun Ruo enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-113-limu-ruhe-yong-yiji-zhili-zu-qinguo-tongyi-luymqpcolsvyz5miodit3wdlx0ob]] as a [[QinState|秦国]] intelligence actor operating against [[ZhaoState|赵国]] during Zhao's disaster and famine crisis. The source says he uses heavy money to buy Zhao people into spreading rumors and helps create an ominous sign involving white hair or white growth appearing from the ground.

The episode's role for Dun Ruo is strategic, not biographical. He shows Qin using [[DisasterExploitingConquestTiming|disaster-exploiting timing]] and [[CounterintelligenceRumorWedge|information warfare]] before the 229 BCE military attack. The same source then describes a Qin bribery operation through [[GuoKai|郭开]] against [[LiMu|李牧]] and [[SimaShang|司马尚]], keeping Dun Ruo inside the broader Qin pattern of weakening Zhao's morale and command trust without treating every later spy action as personally attributable to him.
