# Tokyo Stock Exchange

The Tokyo Stock Exchange appears in [[wake-up-haul-an-ozempic-moment-for-the-brain-6a85798d1ad8716bb6078744]] through reform of Japan's growth market. The source says that from 2030, companies unable to maintain a 10 billion yen market capitalization after five years will be delisted.

In the episode, the reform is meant to push IPOs from a premature cash-out endpoint toward a growth milestone. [[YamajiHiromi]] says the aim is to make IPOs a starting point rather than the end goal, which directly extends [[JapaneseStartupExitConstraint]].
