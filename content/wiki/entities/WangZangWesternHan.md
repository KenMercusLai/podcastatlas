## Overview

王臧 / Wang Zang is an early-Wudi Confucian-aligned official whose work is stopped and whose life ends when [[EmpressDowagerDouWesternHan|窦太后]] defeats the attempt to reduce Eastern Palace influence over major policy.

## Current Profile

Hanji 249 supplies Wang Zang's programmatic role before the collapse. [[DouYingWesternHan|窦婴]] and [[TianFenWesternHan|田蚡]] recommend him as 郎中令; with [[ZhaoWanWesternHan|赵绾]], he plans a Mingtang and asks [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] to summon their teacher [[ShenGongWesternHan|申公]].

Hanji 251 then groups Wang with Zhao as officials advancing the early Confucian line. Zhao's proposal to stop reporting major affairs to the Eastern Palace triggers Dowager Dou's reaction, but Wang is included in the investigation and in the collapse of the Mingtang-related program.

Wudi then yields: he stops the work for which the two officials are responsible and turns them over for judgment. Both reportedly die by suicide. Wang's individual authorship and conduct are less developed than Zhao's, so the current profile treats him as a co-target of the failed institutional challenge rather than assigning Zhao's proposal to him.

## Key Characteristics

- Confucian-aligned official associated with Wudi's early reform program.
- Recommended by Dou Ying and Tian Fen as 郎中令 in Hanji 249.
- Student of Shen Gong and participant in the Mingtang plan.
- Partner of Zhao Wan in the political reversal triggered by the Eastern Palace proposal.
- Target of Dowager Dou's ordered investigation.
- Loses his work and dies after Wudi retreats in the episode's account.

## Qualifications

Hanji 249 identifies Wang Zang as 郎中令, but the sources give him no separate policy statement and do not establish the recommendation process, division of labor in the Mingtang project, authorship, defense, charges, prison experience, or the voluntariness of his death. Hanji 251's claim that both men acted as Wudi's trial balloons is plausible within its narrative but remains an inference. This page therefore does not merge Wang's role with Zhao Wan's more specific proposal.
