## Overview

王臧 / Wang Zang is an early-Wudi Confucian-aligned official whose work is stopped and whose life ends when [[EmpressDowagerDouWesternHan|窦太后]] defeats the attempt to reduce Eastern Palace influence over major policy.

## Current Profile

The episode groups Wang Zang with [[ZhaoWanWesternHan|赵绾]] as officials advancing the early Confucian line around [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]. Zhao's proposal to stop reporting major affairs to the Eastern Palace triggers Dowager Dou's reaction, but Wang is included in the investigation and in the collapse of the Mingtang-related program.

Wudi then yields: he stops the work for which the two officials are responsible and turns them over for judgment. Both reportedly die by suicide. Wang's individual authorship and conduct are less developed than Zhao's, so the current profile treats him as a co-target of the failed institutional challenge rather than assigning Zhao's proposal to him.

## Key Characteristics

- Confucian-aligned official associated with Wudi's early reform program.
- Partner of Zhao Wan in the political reversal triggered by the Eastern Palace proposal.
- Target of Dowager Dou's ordered investigation.
- Loses his work when Wudi stops the Mingtang project and retreats.
- Dies after being surrendered for judgment in the episode's account.

## Qualifications

The source gives Wang Zang no separate policy statement and does not establish his precise office, authorship, defense, charges, prison experience, or the voluntariness of his death. The episode's claim that both men acted as Wudi's trial balloons is plausible within its narrative but remains an inference. This page therefore does not merge Wang's role with Zhao Wan's more specific proposal.
