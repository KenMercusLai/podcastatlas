## Overview

蒋诩 / Jiang Xu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-644-shui-gan-he-huangdi-shizhe-taojiahuanjia-lkgn0idimbu6twz-symr5012f8sn|Hanji 644]] as a former Yanzhou inspector who leaves office after [[WangMang|王莽]] takes imperial authority. The episode pairs him with [[GuoQinXinOfficial|郭钦]] as a clean official whose refusal takes the form of illness-based retirement and lifelong seclusion.

## Current Profile

Jiang Xu is not developed as a full biographical subject in the episode. His durable wiki role is as part of the Xin-era refusal spectrum: a reputable official sees Wang Mang's political order as unserviceable, claims illness, returns home, and closes the gate until death.

This makes him a quieter counterpart to [[GongShengXinScholar|龚胜]]. The episode values the shared nonparticipation while distinguishing tactics: death, allusive reclusion, and low-visibility withdrawal are different ways to avoid serving the new regime.

## Key Characteristics

- Former Yanzhou inspector associated with clean and upright conduct.
- Withdraws from office after Wang Mang's takeover.
- Uses illness as a defensible exit form.
- Remains privately secluded until death.
