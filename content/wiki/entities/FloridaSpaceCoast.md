# Florida Space Coast

The Florida Space Coast appears in [[tech-20260818-tech-pod-128-tech-20260818-tech-pod-128]] as the busiest U.S. launch hub and the concrete place where [[SpaceLaunchCapacityBottleneck]] becomes visible. The episode says more than 100 orbital launches occurred there last year, setting a record while leaving launch pads crowded and in need of upgrades.

The source treats the Space Coast as a high-value but capacity-constrained part of [[SpaceEconomyInfrastructure]]. Its relationship to [[CapeCanaveral]], ocean access, population separation, and launch-pad modernization shows why more rockets and satellites do not automatically translate into enough launch throughput.
