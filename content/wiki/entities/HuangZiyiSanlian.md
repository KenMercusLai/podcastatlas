# 黄子逸 / Huang Ziyi

黄子逸 / Huang Ziyi is the [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] reporter in [[ep240-ba-zong-tian-chong-zai-haiwai-duanju-chuhai-de-chanye-mima-ltvxvfj5ko6djhsjatiphdy-dzek]] who explains the main industry arc behind [[ShortDramaOverseasExpansion|短剧出海]]. He connects the 2023 domestic miniprogram paid-unlock boom, the later free-platform pressure from [[Hongguo]], and the move by short-drama companies toward overseas markets.

His contribution is to frame short drama as an exportable operating system rather than only a story format. In the episode, that system includes [[ShortDramaEconomics]], [[ShortDramaPaidTrafficDistribution]], [[ChineseWebFictionMethodology]], low-cost production, social-media buying, and a product-like feedback loop around hooks and ROI.
