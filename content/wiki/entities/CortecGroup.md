# Cortec Group

Cortec Group is the private-equity firm discussed in [[yeti-ron-and-ryan-seiders-how-two-brothers-turned-a-400-cooler-into-a-2-billion-brand-d7f8e0e8-51a5-4444-8c1d-f5c43c353285]] as buying a significant stake in [[YETI]] in 2012. The episode says the reported transaction was roughly $70 million for about two-thirds of the company, while [[RoySeiders]] and [[RyanSeiders]] each retained minority ownership.

## Key Points
- Cortec enters the episode after YETI had proven premium cooler demand, specialty retail growth, and a more resilient manufacturing base.
- The investment changed the brothers' ownership but did not immediately remove them from the company.
- The later [[YETIRambler]] expansion made the brand much larger after the private-equity transaction.
- The episode connects Cortec to the broader [[FounderRoleTransition]] question because Roy and Ryan later stepped down from CEO and president roles.
