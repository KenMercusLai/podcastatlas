# 袁振侠 / Yuan Zhenxia

## Overview

袁振侠 is [[NiKuang|倪匡]]'s doctor-investigator, represented in the bounded episode through his role in [[BaoHuNiKuangNovel|《宝狐》]].

## Current Profile

In 《宝狐》, Yuan enters after a coffin is opened and the preserved woman inside disappears. Curiosity draws him into [[LengZiquan|冷自泉]]'s account, but the episode distinguishes his function from [[WesleyNiKuang|卫斯理]]: Yuan has a defined medical profession, a gentler manner, and a continuing emotional life across the series, while Wesley is framed as the more forceful curiosity-driven adventurer.

Yuan's role in this story is primarily investigative and mediating. He reconstructs a decades-long romance, considers the possibility that [[BaoHuCharacter|宝狐]] exists as signal rather than stable matter, and helps bring Leng to the final disclosure. The ending's indirect conversation with a gentleman and his wife is read by the hosts as a Wesley–白素 crossover, but that identification remains source-scoped.

## Key Characteristics

- Combines medical identity with investigation of extraordinary events.
- Enters mysteries through curiosity without being characterized as forcefully as Wesley.
- Functions as an observer and mediator of emotionally charged cases.
- Uses speculative inference to connect apparent supernatural events with nonhuman life.
- Belongs to a series with more continuous relationships than the episode attributes to the Wesley novels.
