# 陈墩汉墓 / Chendun Han Tomb

## Overview

陈墩汉墓 / Chendun Han Tomb is an elite Western Han burial within the [[SanzhuangHanTombComplex|三庄汉墓群]], presented in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-345-2-qiannian-wangling-de-guiyi-xianxiang-daodi-zhenxiang-ruhe-ljkkhfbumlsgf7ppz96qvys_ok3d|Hanji 345-2]] as the smaller tomb examined before [[DaqingdunHanTomb|大青墩汉墓]].

## Current Profile

The episode describes a relatively well-preserved pit-and-wooden-chamber tomb with one coffin, one outer chamber, and one side compartment. Bronze, ceramic, lacquer, jade, cosmetic-container, and musical-instrument finds indicate wealth and status, but they do not independently supply a royal title.

The skeleton and seal create a layered identity problem. The body is reported as a roughly thirty-year-old woman classified through physical anthropology as Xiongnu, while a copper seal is read as “张廷意.” Neither observation explains whether the seal belonged to the body, another person represented in the burial, or a portable object placed there for some other reason. Chendun is therefore evidence for diversity within a royal-cemetery landscape, not evidence against the landscape's broader attribution.

## Key Characteristics

- Early Western Han pit-and-wooden-chamber construction with one coffin, one outer chamber, and one side compartment.
- Elite grave goods and a stringed instrument, with disputed material and dimensions in secondary retellings.
- Well-preserved skeleton reported as an adult woman after specialist reassessment.
- Xiongnu biological or geographic classification reported by the episode but not independently established here.
- Copper “张廷意” seal whose owner, function, and relationship to the body remain unresolved.
- Smaller scale and different evidentiary role from nearby Daqingdun.

## Evidence

- Form and status: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-345-2-qiannian-wangling-de-guiyi-xianxiang-daodi-zhenxiang-ruhe-ljkkhfbumlsgf7ppz96qvys_ok3d|Hanji 345-2]] describes the chamber arrangement and elite bronze, ceramic, lacquer, and musical grave goods.
- Body assessment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-345-2-qiannian-wangling-de-guiyi-xianxiang-daodi-zhenxiang-ruhe-ljkkhfbumlsgf7ppz96qvys_ok3d|Hanji 345-2]] reports the change from an on-site male impression to a specialist identification of a roughly thirty-year-old woman classified as Xiongnu.
- Named object: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-345-2-qiannian-wangling-de-guiyi-xianxiang-daodi-zhenxiang-ruhe-ljkkhfbumlsgf7ppz96qvys_ok3d|Hanji 345-2]] reports a copper seal normalized as “张廷意,” without resolving whose name it bears in relation to the burial.

## Qualifications

The supplied note is not the technical excavation report. Its excavation year, mound area, instrument material and length, “largest” claim, sex-assessment sequence, age estimate, ancestry method, seal metal, reading, findspot, and inference of rank require direct publication comparison. Skeletal morphology and a portable seal answer different questions and should not be fused into a complete biography.

## What Changed

- Added the tomb as a canonical site distinct from the wider cemetery and Daqingdun.
- Preserved the reported body and seal evidence without forcing them into one personal identity.
- Flagged the instrument description and excavation chronology as conflicting secondary details.
