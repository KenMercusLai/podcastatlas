# Miguel Gomez

## Overview
Miguel Gomez is the Cornell specialty-crop economist used by [[PlanetMoney]] to frame branded produce as a race for differentiation.

## Current Profile
Gomez supplies the episode's economic language. He explains why growers and supply chains try to escape commodity competition through distinct crop varieties, packaging, brand identity, or other traits consumers will recognize. He also adds a qualification: too many named options can raise search costs, and the apple market may already show signs of saturation.

## Key Characteristics
- Specialty-crop economist cited for the "race for differentiation" frame.
- Connects genetics, supply chains, marketing, and consumers into one produce-market system.
- Treats branded produce as a response to price-only commodity competition.
- Warns that differentiation can run into consumer choice overload or search-cost limits.
