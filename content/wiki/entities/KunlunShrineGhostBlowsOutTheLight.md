# 《昆仑神宫》 / Kunlun Shrine

## Overview

《昆仑神宫》 is the fourth book in the first phase of [[GhostBlowsOutTheLight|《鬼吹灯》]]. The current page covers its ending through a [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] episode focused on the sacrifice puzzle, the removal of the protagonists' curse, their escape, and their decision to leave tomb raiding.

## Current Profile

The ending first appears to require a living member of the group to die, then reveals that the ritual requires a pair of eyes transformed by the ghost cave. The ghost mother's crystal eyes provide a substitute after a final struggle, allowing the group to use them with the 雮尘珠 and complete the ritual. The hosts see this as a mixed solution: it preserves the group's loyalty and avoids arbitrary mutilation, but the timely substitute lowers the cost promised by the setup.

The novel then prolongs danger through collapse, corpses, snakes, 阿香's dissociation, and escape. Character closure works better for the hosts than the climax's pacing. 胡八一 accepts his bond with Shirley 杨, recognizes what adventure gave him and 胖子, rejects continued risk for grave goods, and removes the 摸金符 as a public exit from that life.

## Key Characteristics

- Resolves the first four books' curse through a ritual substitution involving the ghost mother's eyes and the 雮尘珠.
- Tests loyalty through a false death lottery that 胡八一 uses to delay and control 明叔 rather than to select an actual victim.
- Combines a central sacrificial dilemma with an extended physical escape sequence after the ritual is complete.
- Uses the Bremen Town Musicians story to value what is gained during a journey over arrival at the nominal destination.
- Ends with romance, restitution to fallen comrades' families, plans for migration, and removal of the 摸金符.
