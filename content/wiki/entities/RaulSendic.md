# Raul Sendic

## Overview

Raul Sendic is presented in [[261-uruguay-the-tupamaros-glt1539968794]] as the socialist labor activist who founded the Movimiento de Liberacion Nacional, better known as the [[Tupamaros]].

## Current Profile

The episode traces Sendic from a rural family background and legal studies into Socialist Party activism among agricultural and sugar-cane workers. Violent repression of labor protest and the regional example of the Cuban Revolution pushed his politics toward armed organization. His significance here is therefore both biographical and causal: he connects rural labor grievance to an urban guerrilla strategy whose later escalation helped transform Uruguay's political order.

## Key Characteristics

- Came from outside Montevideo and studied law.
- Joined socialist youth and party politics.
- Organized around agricultural and cane-worker conditions.
- Radicalized after violent repression of protest.
- Founded the Tupamaros in 1963 in the episode's chronology.
- Was imprisoned after the movement's defeat and died in Paris in 1989.

## Evidence

- Political formation: [[261-uruguay-the-tupamaros-glt1539968794]] links Sendic's family background, legal study, Socialist Party work, and rural labor campaigning.
- Turn to armed organization: [[261-uruguay-the-tupamaros-glt1539968794]] connects protest repression and post-Cuban-Revolution radicalization to the Tupamaros' founding.
- Later fate: [[261-uruguay-the-tupamaros-glt1539968794]] identifies imprisonment, later residence in Paris, and death in 1989.

## Qualifications

This is not a complete biography or intellectual history. The episode compresses the causal move from labor repression to guerrilla organization and does not establish Sendic's responsibility for each later tactic, the movement's internal decision process, or whether armed escalation followed inevitably from his founding strategy.
