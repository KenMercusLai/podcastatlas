# Derry / Londonderry

Derry/Londonderry appears in [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] as the symbolically charged city where civil-rights protest and loyalist-unionist historical memory collided. The episode treats the 5 October 1968 banned march as a decisive publicity moment because [[RoyalUlsterConstabulary|RUC]] violence was filmed and broadcast beyond [[NorthernIreland]].

The city later becomes the setting for the [[BattleOfTheBogside]], the Apprentice Boys parade flashpoint, [[DerryCitizensDefenceAssociation|local Catholic defense preparations]], and the first [[BritishArmy]] deployment in August 1969.
