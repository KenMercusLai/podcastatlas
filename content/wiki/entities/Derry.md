# Derry / Londonderry

Derry/Londonderry appears in [[697-the-troubles-murder-in-belfast-part-1-glt4877520736]] as the local emblem of [[InstitutionalSectarianDiscrimination]] in [[NorthernIreland]]. The episode says the city had a Catholic majority but Protestant control through ward boundary manipulation, while Protestant housing access contrasted with a large Catholic waiting list.

In [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]], Derry becomes the symbolically charged city where civil-rights protest and loyalist-unionist historical memory collided. The episode treats the 5 October 1968 banned march as a decisive publicity moment because [[RoyalUlsterConstabulary|RUC]] violence was filmed and broadcast beyond Northern Ireland. The city later becomes the setting for the [[BattleOfTheBogside]], the Apprentice Boys parade flashpoint, [[DerryCitizensDefenceAssociation|local Catholic defense preparations]], and the first [[BritishArmy]] deployment in August 1969.
