# Derry / Londonderry

## Overview
Derry/Londonderry is the Troubles sequence's sharpest local case of how housing, ward boundaries, historical memory, civil-rights protest, state authority, and military action converged inside [[NorthernIreland]].

## Current Profile
The current profile is Derry as a city where local administration becomes national legitimacy crisis. Part 1 uses it as the emblem of Catholic-majority grievance under Protestant-controlled local power. Part 2 turns it into the site of filmed police violence, the [[BattleOfTheBogside]], and first British troop deployment. Part 4 then returns to Derry as the location of [[FreeDerry]] and [[BloodySunday]], where British Army attempts to reassert authority over a no-go area end in civilian killings.

## Key Characteristics
- Catholic-majority city presented as controlled through boundary design and Protestant political advantage.
- Civil-rights publicity site where police violence in 1968 travels beyond Northern Ireland.
- Parade and street-war setting for the [[BattleOfTheBogside]] in August 1969.
- Location of [[FreeDerry]], the barricaded no-go area that challenged Stormont and British authority.
- Site of Bloody Sunday, making local legitimacy collapse internationally visible.
