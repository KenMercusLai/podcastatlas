# John Gottman / 约翰·哥特曼

## Overview
[[JohnGottman|John Gottman / 约翰·哥特曼]] enters the wiki through the Culture Limited Vol.349 discussion of [[GottmanMethod]] and [[RelationshipFourHorsemen]].

## Current Profile
The sources present Gottman as a research figure behind a data-informed approach to couple interaction. His relevance here is not a full biography, but the way the episodes use his relationship framework to show that intimacy is built through detailed mutual knowledge, positive regard, repair, influence, problem-solving, and shared meaning, while criticism, contempt, defensiveness, and stonewalling warn of breakdown.

The Huberman episode names [[JulieGottman]] alongside him and places the four patterns inside a wider account of attachment, empathy, autonomic coordination, and partner interpretation. It reinforces contempt as the most serious warning in that source while leaving the underlying prediction methods and effect sizes unreviewed.

## Key Characteristics
- He is associated in the source with long-running couple observation and interaction research.
- His work grounds the episode's practical language for love maps and relationship maintenance.
- His framework turns relationship quality into observable patterns rather than vague compatibility alone.
- His four-horsemen vocabulary gives the episode a warning system for destructive communication.
- His work is jointly attributed with [[JulieGottman]] in the Huberman episode.

## Evidence
- Research association: [[vol-349-yexu-ni-gai-zhao-ge-ren-liaoliao-shuochu-tongku-huode-yongqi-gkwrimaoywpmaycmmatldgrv]] says Gottman tracked couples from the 1970s and used interaction data to model relationship outcomes.
- Love maps and maintenance: [[vol-349-yexu-ni-gai-zhao-ge-ren-liaoliao-shuochu-tongku-huode-yongqi-gkwrimaoywpmaycmmatldgrv]] summarizes relationship layers including love maps, fondness and admiration, turning toward, accepting influence, solving solvable problems, moving through gridlock, and shared meaning.
- Observable patterns: [[vol-349-yexu-ni-gai-zhao-ge-ren-liaoliao-shuochu-tongku-huode-yongqi-gkwrimaoywpmaycmmatldgrv]] frames the method as data-supported rather than only intuitive advice.
- Four-horsemen vocabulary: [[vol-349-yexu-ni-gai-zhao-ge-ren-liaoliao-shuochu-tongku-huode-yongqi-gkwrimaoywpmaycmmatldgrv]] identifies criticism, contempt, defensiveness, and stonewalling as major relationship-breakdown signals.
- Joint attribution and contempt emphasis: [[the-science-of-love-desire-and-attachment-scim1112390541]] names John and Julie Gottman while describing contempt as the strongest predictor among the four patterns.

## Qualifications
The page records Gottman only as introduced by two public episode summaries. It does not independently assess the prediction studies, empirical strength, effect sizes, limits, or later debates around the method. The framework should not flatten coercion, abuse, or danger into symmetrical couple communication.
