## Overview

王圣 / Wang Sheng appears first in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-799-donghan-mingchen-yangzhen-ruhe-jujue-zou-houmen-lmufb7lvb-kzsvjtn6iu-zrpiptf|Hanji 799]] as [[EmperorAnOfHan|Emperor An]]'s wet nurse, enfeoffed as Yewang Jun and given a costly mansion that [[YangZhenLateHan|杨震]] criticizes. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-802-andi-zhisi-donghan-wangchao-quanli-da-xipai-lgvne92e0jjzjnznzfkz7nvztnbj|Hanji 802]] later places her in the old An-era power field removed after [[EmpressDowagerYanLateHan|阎太后]] and [[YanXianLateHan|阎显]] install Beixiang Hou.

## Current Profile

Hanji 799 gives Wang Sheng's active status before the Shun succession crisis. Emperor An enfeoffs her as Yewang Jun because of her wet-nurse relationship, then orders envoys to build a large mansion for her. Yang Zhen objects that the state is suffering disasters, poverty, frontier wars, and an empty treasury, so using public resources for a wet nurse's mansion is improper.

The same source makes Wang Sheng a key example of private favor entering public title and expenditure. The page does not claim that she personally controlled the wider appointment abuses Yang describes, but her mansion sits in the same memorial as Zhou Guang, Xie Yun, Fan Feng, and other favored actors who use proximity to solicit offices and accept bribes.

Hanji 802 then shows the later reversal. After Beixiang Hou's accession, Wang Sheng is grouped with [[GengBaoGrandGeneralLateHan|Geng Bao]], [[FanFengEunuchLateHan|Fan Feng]], Xie Yun, Zhou Guang, and others accused of factional misconduct, abuse, and grave wrongdoing. Fan Feng, Xie Yun, and Zhou Guang are executed, while Wang Sheng and her son are exiled.

## Key Characteristics

- Emperor An's wet nurse whose private palace relationship becomes a public title and construction claim.
- Yewang Jun recipient whose treatment draws Yang Zhen's fiscal and moral criticism.
- Example of wet-nurse favor entering the same field as appointment and bribery abuse.
- Palace-linked old-network actor targeted after the Yan-backed accession.
- Exiled with her son rather than executed in the source's compressed account.
- Identity-disambiguation case kept separate from unrelated Wang Shengli.
