## Overview

王圣 / Wang Sheng appears in Hanji 802 as a palace-linked member of the old [[EmperorAnOfHan|Emperor An]]-era power field removed after [[EmpressDowagerYanLateHan|阎太后]] and [[YanXianLateHan|阎显]] install Beixiang Hou. The page keeps her source-bounded because this episode does not reconstruct her full palace status or earlier career.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-802-andi-zhisi-donghan-wangchao-quanli-da-xipai-lgvne92e0jjzjnznzfkz7nvztnbj|Hanji 802]] introduces Wang Sheng only through the Yan Xian cleanup after Beixiang Hou's accession. She is grouped with [[GengBaoGrandGeneralLateHan|耿宝]], [[FanFengEunuchLateHan|樊丰]], Xie Yun, Zhou Guang, and others accused of factional misconduct, abuse, and grave wrongdoing.

The source says Fan Feng, Xie Yun, and Zhou Guang are executed, Geng Bao is demoted and kills himself, and Wang Sheng and her son are exiled. Her role therefore helps show that the purge reaches not only formal military or ministerial actors but also palace-linked relationships attached to the old An-era power structure.

## Key Characteristics

- Palace-linked old-network actor in the post-An succession crisis.
- Purge target grouped with Grand General, eunuch, and other court figures.
- Exiled with her son rather than executed in the source's compressed account.
- Evidence that Yan Xian's cleanup reaches household and palace-relationship channels.
- Identity-disambiguation case kept separate from unrelated Wang Shengli.
