## Overview

王圣 / Wang Sheng now appears as [[EmperorAnOfHan|汉安帝]]'s wet nurse whose household becomes a recurring case of private favor entering public title, construction, appointment, and purge politics. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-796-donghan-mingchen-vs-diwang-shenbianren-shui-neng-ying-lkyzp2v-cjysd65syoyuldlbdb5p|Hanji 796]] shows her enfeoffment and household tie to [[BoRongLateHan|伯荣]] and [[LiuGuiLateHan|刘瑰]]; Hanji 799 shows her mansion as [[YangZhenLateHan|杨震]]'s fiscal target; Hanji 802 shows her later exile during Yan Xian's cleanup.

## Current Profile

Hanji 796 supplies Wang Sheng's earliest current layer. In 120 CE, An enfeoffs her as Yewang Jun because of her wet-nurse relationship. Her daughter Bo Rong is presented as a favored figure near An, and Bo Rong's marriage to Liu Gui helps make Liu Gui's shizhong office and Shaoyang marquisate succession politically controversial. Yang Zhen's memorial therefore treats Wang Sheng's household as a title-order problem, not just a private family story.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-799-donghan-mingchen-yangzhen-ruhe-jujue-zou-houmen-lmufb7lvb-kzsvjtn6iu-zrpiptf|Hanji 799]] gives the later mansion layer. An orders envoys to build a large mansion for Wang Sheng. Yang objects that disasters, poverty, frontier wars, and an empty treasury make such public expenditure improper. The same memorial then attacks favored actors' solicitation, bribery, and corrupt appointment placement, placing Wang Sheng's privilege inside a broader [[PrivateFavorAppointmentErosion|私恩任官侵蚀]] field.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-802-andi-zhisi-donghan-wangchao-quanli-da-xipai-lgvne92e0jjzjnznzfkz7nvztnbj|Hanji 802]] records the later reversal. After the Yan-backed accession of Beixiang Hou, Wang Sheng is grouped with Geng Bao, Fan Feng, Xie Yun, Zhou Guang, and others accused of factional misconduct, abuse, and grave wrongdoing. Fan Feng, Xie Yun, and Zhou Guang are executed, while Wang Sheng and her son are exiled.

## Key Characteristics

- Emperor An's wet nurse whose private palace relationship becomes a public title claim.
- Yewang Jun recipient whose household is tied to Bo Rong and Liu Gui's disputed title succession.
- Mansion beneficiary whose treatment draws Yang Zhen's fiscal and moral criticism.
- Example of wet-nurse favor entering the same field as appointment, bribery, and palace-household privilege.
- Old-network palace actor targeted after the Yan-backed accession.
- Exiled with her son rather than executed in the source's compressed purge account.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 802, Hanji 799, and Hanji 796. It does not settle Wang Sheng's full office status, son identity, household structure, personal decision-making, or the legal truth of the later accusations. The route uses `WangShengPalaceActorLateHan` to avoid collision with [[WangShengli|王胜利]], and the body normalizes the transcript's 王胜 to 王圣 for the late-Han context.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 796 as Wang Sheng's earlier Yewang Jun and Bo Rong/Liu Gui household layer.
- Reframed Hanji 799's mansion as a later expression of a favorite-household problem already visible in 120 CE.
- Preserved Hanji 802's exile as the cleanup of an old An-era palace network.
