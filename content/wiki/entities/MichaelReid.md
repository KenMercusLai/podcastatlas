# Michael Reid

Michael Reid enters the wiki through [[taken-littorally-spains-sudden-crisis-in-ceuta-6a70712c034f16a52ebfaed7]] as the [[TheIntelligence]] participant explaining the political backstory to the [[Ceuta]] crisis. He links the immediate crossing surge to a Spanish court ruling, Moroccan social-media interpretation, the 2021 Ceuta precedent, [[WesternSahara]], [[Algeria]], and [[PedroSanchez]]'s immigration policy.

Reid's wiki role is geopolitical synthesis. He treats the episode as more than a sudden humanitarian emergency: the crossing becomes evidence that [[Morocco]] can pressure [[Spain]] and the [[EuropeanUnion]] by letting migration flows become visible.
