## Overview

赵破奴 / Zhao Ponu is presented by [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-376-2-cong-liulanger-nixi-cheng-jiangjun-jiemi-hanjiang-zhaoponu-lszmnylwk8zxpjhelkd1gd6jsfsd|Hanji 376-2]] as a Western Han general whose movement from youthful wandering in Xiongnu territory to military rise, Western Regions achievement, battlefield capture, escape, and household extermination makes his life a compressed image of [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s expansion and late political catastrophe.

## Current Profile

The source gives Zhao Ponu a cross-frontier background rather than a simple court-to-army career. It says he wandered among Xiongnu when young, later returned to Han, and rose in the victorious era associated with [[HuoQubing|霍去病]]. His knowledge of both sides and his military status help explain why Xiongnu keeps him alive after capture rather than killing him in the episode's interpretation.

His career combines achievement and command failure. The episode credits him with earlier merit and a seven-hundred-cavalry victory over Loulan, but says that in 103 BCE he leaves a force of twenty thousand cavalry at night to seek water, is detected and captured, and later escapes with his son Zhao Anguo. Return therefore follows battlefield error and captivity rather than erasing them.

The final branch remains deliberately unresolved. The terse record says Zhao Ponu was later implicated in the [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]] and his clan exterminated. The host suggests he may have supported Crown Prince Liu Ju because of his Huo Qubing affiliation, but also states that the historical record does not reveal his actual alignment or the reason for implication. His life can support an era-trajectory interpretation without supplying a settled verdict on his final politics.

## Key Characteristics

- Cross-frontier figure who is said to have wandered in Xiongnu territory before returning to Han service.
- General whose rise and early merit are located in Huo Qubing's victorious military era.
- Western Regions commander credited by the episode with defeating Loulan using seven hundred cavalry.
- Army leader captured after leaving his force at night during the 103 BCE campaign against the Xiongnu Left Wise King.
- Captive who later escapes with his son and returns to Han.
- Wugu casualty whose household destruction is recorded more clearly than his alleged political alignment.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to one short podcast source rather than a full primary-source biography. The 103 BCE date, troop number, night search for water, capture mechanics, Loulan force size, sequence of returns to Han, and motives for Xiongnu leniency remain source-scoped. The proposed Liu Ju alignment is explicitly speculative and is not adopted as fact; association with Huo Qubing does not independently establish Zhao Ponu's Wugu position.
