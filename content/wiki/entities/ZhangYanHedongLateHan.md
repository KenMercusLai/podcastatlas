# 张衍 / Zhang Yan (Hedong-Hongnong actor)

## Overview
张衍 / Zhang Yan (Hedong-Hongnong actor) enters the wiki through Hanji 1070 as the Hongnong-area actor who rises in response to [[ZhangShengLateHan|张盛]]. This page is intentionally disambiguated from [[ZhangYanLateHan|张燕]] of the Black Mountain Army and [[ZhangYanTaiweiLateHan|张延]] the late-Han taiwei.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1070 gives Zhang Yan a compact role in the western disturbance chain. After Zhang Sheng gathers forces around Xiaoshan and Mianchi, Zhang Yan rises in Hongnong in response. The episode's larger point is that Cao Cao's control problem extends beyond Hebei: disturbances around Guanzhong-facing routes and Hedong make the appointment of [[DuJiLateHan|杜畿]] strategically urgent.

## Key Characteristics
- Hongnong-area actor in the post-Ye western disturbance chain.
- Responds to Zhang Sheng's armed activity.
- Distinct from the better-known Black Mountain Zhang Yan.
- Helps explain why Hedong control matters to Cao Cao after northern victories.
