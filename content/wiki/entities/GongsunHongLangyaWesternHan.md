## Overview

公孙洪 / Gongsun Hong is the Langya administrator in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-625-jiemi-renxing-anmian-weihe-pangren-chenggong-zong-ling-women-xinsheng-jihen-li64ngkv8nujwegbix6iororjmbo|Hanji 625]] who is killed after refusing to join the 5 CE praise chorus around [[WangMang|王莽]] and discussing disasters in his commandery office. He is distinct from [[GongsunHong|公孙弘]], the Han Wudi-era minister already present in the wiki.

## Current Profile

The current source uses Gongsun Hong as Ban Zhi's fatal contrast. Both officials fail to conform to the court's praise and auspicious-reporting expectations, but Gongsun Hong's case is framed as more exposed because he is accused of fabricating or spreading disaster talk, not merely failing to publicize auspicious signs.

Once [[ZhenFengXinOfficial|甄丰]] encourages local accusation channels, Gongsun Hong's speech can be rewritten as hostility to Wang Mang's sacred governance. [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]'s protective distinction helps Ban Zhi but leaves Gongsun Hong isolated; the court arrests and kills him. The episode therefore treats him as the costliest case of nonconformity under forced political praise.

## Key Characteristics

- Langya administrator who does not join praise-making under Wang Mang's 5 CE court.
- Disaster-talk case whose words become evidence in a political accusation.
- Execution victim who contrasts with Ban Zhi's protected retreat.
- Identity-distinct 公孙洪, not the better-known 公孙弘 of Han Wudi's court.

- Fatal outcome: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-625-jiemi-renxing-anmian-weihe-pangren-chenggong-zong-ling-women-xinsheng-jihen-li64ngkv8nujwegbix6iororjmbo|Hanji 625]] says Gongsun Hong is arrested separately and killed after Wang Zhengjun distinguishes Ban Zhi's case.

## Qualifications

This page is source-bounded to Hanji 625. It does not settle Gongsun Hong's full biography, the factual status of the disaster reports, the legal procedure behind the execution, or his private attitude toward Wang Mang.
