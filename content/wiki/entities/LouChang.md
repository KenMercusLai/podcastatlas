# 娄昌 / Lou Chang

娄昌 / Lou Chang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-1-loxyybm3njrtnudydafwzu9jp9jz]] as the [[ZhaoState|赵国]] adviser who recommends sending an important envoy to [[QinState|秦国]] to seek peace during the [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] prelude. His advice is the option [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] effectively follows when he sends [[ZhengZhu|郑朱]] to [[Xianyang|咸阳]].

The episode uses Lou Chang mainly as a foil for [[YuQing|虞卿]]. Lou Chang's direct-peace approach treats negotiation as the immediate escape from battlefield pressure, while Yu Qing argues that negotiation without restored alliance leverage only broadcasts weakness.
