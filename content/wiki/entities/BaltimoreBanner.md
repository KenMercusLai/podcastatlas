# Baltimore Banner

## Overview
Baltimore Banner is the local newsroom whose journalists investigated the Baltimore row-home portfolio at the center of [[the-loan-at-the-heart-of-a-new-foreclosure-crisis]].

## Current Profile
In this source, the Banner supplies the local investigative layer that makes a national mortgage-product story legible. Its reporters moved from a tip to property records, spreadsheets, field reporting in Baltimore, a trip to Spring Valley, New York, and published stories that preceded an FBI investigation and private-lender blacklisting.

## Key Characteristics
- The Banner reporting begins with a tip about major investor foreclosures in Baltimore.
- Its journalists traced public records into a portfolio of more than 700 homes and about $100 million in borrowing.
- The newsroom connected local property deterioration to [[DSCRLoans|DSCR loan]] mechanics and private lending.
- Its reporters separated confirmed facts from a suspected inflated-transaction theory.
- The reporting helped move the case from local rumor into public accountability and investigation.
