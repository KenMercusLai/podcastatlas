# Curry Barker

## Overview

Curry Barker is the YouTube-origin horror filmmaker discussed as the director of [[ObsessionCurryBarkerFilm|Obsession / 《痴迷》]].

## Current Profile

The episode uses Barker to illustrate [[InternetNativeFilmmakingPipeline]]. Online shorts and audience data are described as practical training in whether jokes or scares land, while the feature's small initial financing, festival exposure, competitive acquisition, and later marketing support show how digital proof can connect to conventional production and distribution institutions.

His cited creative strength is conceptual clarity: the film turns a wish for love into a consent violation and uses performance, voice, possession, and body horror to expose control inside romantic idealization.

## Key Characteristics

- YouTube-origin filmmaker who moved from online shorts toward feature horror.
- Uses direct audience response to study timing and the legibility of scares.
- Director associated with a low-budget-to-festival-to-acquisition production path.
- Interested in sound and vocal performance as components of horror effect.
- Treats contemporary audiences as genre-literate rather than easily manipulated.
