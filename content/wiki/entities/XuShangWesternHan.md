## Overview

徐商 / Xu Shang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-507-ta-zhansha-xiongnu-chanyu-weihe-fanbei-xiayu-lhfkej7pqtikx5st73sls-kwo_ze|Hanji 507]] as the boshi sent to inspect the flood-control issue raised by [[FengQuWesternHan|冯屈]]. The episode says Xu Shang concludes that state funds are insufficient and that the Tunshi River need not be dredged.

## Current Profile

Xu Shang's role is narrow but important because he marks the court's conversion of a warning into a deferred maintenance decision. Feng Qu frames the blocked Tunshi River as a lower-Yellow-River risk. Xu Shang's review produces a cost-based non-action recommendation. The later breach makes him part of the episode's short-term-savings critique.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han boshi assigned to inspect the Tunshi River flood-control proposal.
- Represents the court-review layer between local warning and central decision.
- Recommends non-dredging on the grounds of insufficient state funds.
- Becomes a source-scoped example of how preventive infrastructure can be downgraded as optional expense.
