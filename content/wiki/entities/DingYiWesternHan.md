## Overview

丁义 / Ding Yi is presented in Hanji 344-2 as the 乐成侯 who recommended [[LuanDaWesternHan|栾大]] to [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] and was later executed after Luan Da's supernatural claims and false report collapsed.

## Current Profile

The current source gives Ding Yi only an intermediary-and-liability profile. His recommendation helps Luan Da enter the imperial court, but the episode does not describe his reasons, what he knew, or whether he participated in any claimed technique. When Luan Da is executed, Ding Yi is publicly beheaded, showing that a coercive patron can extend responsibility from a failed specialist to the broker who vouched for him.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han marquis identified by the title 乐成侯.
- Court intermediary who recommends Luan Da to Han Wudi.
- Recommender whose own knowledge and motive are not supplied.
- Subject of derivative accountability after Luan Da's claims are exposed.

- **Punishment:** [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-344-2-hanwudi-dangnian-weihe-yaozhan-qinnvxu-llmavvgeaaxwcsz_pxirfulbfpaa|Hanji 344-2]] says Ding Yi was publicly beheaded after Luan Da's execution.

## Qualifications

One short podcast summary is the complete current evidence inventory. It does not establish Ding Yi's full biography, the recommendation procedure, his relationship with Luan Da, knowledge of fabrication, statutory liability, trial, or exact execution sequence. His punishment demonstrates attributed recommender liability in the episode; it does not prove guilt by modern evidentiary standards.
