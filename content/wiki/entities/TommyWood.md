# Tommy Wood

## Overview
Tommy Wood is the medical doctor, neuroscience researcher, Better Brain chief science officer, and human-performance guest interviewed by [[AndrewHuberman]] in [[accelerate-learning-increase-cognitive-capacity-dr-tommy-wood-scim4201272551]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Wood as a synthesis-oriented brain-health and performance explainer. He connects adult learning, exercise physiology, nutritional sufficiency, dementia prevention, concussion recovery, and his own natural strongman training into one practical frame: the brain adapts when the person keeps supplying meaningful input, tolerable difficulty, arousal, recovery, and safety-bounded intervention choices.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains [[Neuroplasticity]] through connection strengthening, weakening, and pruning rather than simple adult neuron growth.
- Prefers broad, embodied, socially and cognitively demanding activities over narrow or passive stimulation for adult learning.
- Separates performance states from learning states by treating flow as useful but not required for skill acquisition.
- Keeps nutrition and supplementation focused on adequacy, testing, interaction effects, and safety rather than maximal dosing.
- Treats concussion and dementia claims with clinical caution, prevention emphasis, and staged recovery logic.
- Uses natural strongman competition as a lived example of difficult adult athletic challenge.

## Evidence
- Neuroplasticity explainer - [[accelerate-learning-increase-cognitive-capacity-dr-tommy-wood-scim4201272551]] has Wood define plasticity as the brain adapting to inputs through strengthened, weakened, and pruned connections.
- Learning-practice stance - [[accelerate-learning-increase-cognitive-capacity-dr-tommy-wood-scim4201272551]] has Wood recommend dance, sports, language, music, martial arts, creative arts, mistakes, and shame tolerance as adult learning stimuli.
- Exercise and brain-health stance - [[accelerate-learning-increase-cognitive-capacity-dr-tommy-wood-scim4201272551]] has Wood distinguish acute exercise, HIIT, aerobic training, and resistance training across cognitive and structural outcomes.
- Nutrition and supplement stance - [[accelerate-learning-increase-cognitive-capacity-dr-tommy-wood-scim4201272551]] has Wood focus on energy balance, nutrient sufficiency, lab testing, multivitamin evidence, creatine, and caution about peptides.
- Concussion and aging stance - [[accelerate-learning-increase-cognitive-capacity-dr-tommy-wood-scim4201272551]] has Wood discuss dementia risk modification, serious illness, vaccination signals, and active staged concussion recovery.
