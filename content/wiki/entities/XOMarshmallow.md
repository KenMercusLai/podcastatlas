## Overview
XO Marshmallow is a gourmet, better-for-you marshmallow company led by Lindsey Shanks and advised on grocery expansion in [[advice-line-with-tara-bosch-of-smartsweets-8aab80a1-b0c5-4103-b875-f5d4823d07ce]].

## Current Profile
The company is a profitable, bootstrapped, mostly DTC brand attempting to add grocery without eroding its online business. Its opportunity depends on giving impulse buyers immediate access while preserving indulgent taste, extending shelf life, choosing retailer-compatible formulations, and expanding self-manufacturing reliably enough to support retail velocity.

## Key Characteristics
- Reports just under $5 million in annual revenue with roughly 90% of sales coming from DTC.
- Self-manufactures near Chicago and is raising outside capital for manufacturing expansion.
- Is removing corn and extending shelf life from about four months to 18 months for grocery readiness.
- Positions itself as better-for-you indulgence rather than a protein- or fiber-delivery product.
- Is testing lower-sugar alternatives while recognizing that retailer ingredient rules may require separate SKUs or channels.

## Evidence
### Existing demand and channel gap
- [[advice-line-with-tara-bosch-of-smartsweets-8aab80a1-b0c5-4103-b875-f5d4823d07ce]] reports material DTC revenue alongside customer complaints about shipping cost and delay, supporting a distinct accessibility case for grocery.

### Product and production readiness
- [[advice-line-with-tara-bosch-of-smartsweets-8aab80a1-b0c5-4103-b875-f5d4823d07ce]] records corn removal, shelf-life work, allulose testing, retailer constraints, capital raising, and the addition of a former Smashmallow manufacturing executive as an adviser.

## Qualifications
Revenue, profitability, channel share, shelf-life results, and formulation work are company-reported. Grocery incrementality, sell-through, margins, and production reliability remain unproven in the source.

## What Changed
- Established XO Marshmallow as a DTC-to-grocery case where product, channel, and manufacturing gates must be solved together.
