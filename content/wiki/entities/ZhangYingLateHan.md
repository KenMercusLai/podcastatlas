# 张英 / Zhang Ying (late Han)

张英 / Zhang Ying (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-983-zhi-hui-pianren-de-laoban-yongyuan-bushi-hao-laoban-lknr2zydwjytjo7od-rt3zi-4k-s|Hanji 983]] as the [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]] officer assigned to guard [[DanglikouLateHan|当利口]]. His deployment runs alongside [[FanNengLateHan|樊能]] and [[YuMiLateHan|于糜]] at [[HengjiangLateHan|横江]].

The source makes Zhang Ying part of the first defensive system against [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s Jiangdong pressure. It does not develop him as a standalone actor, but his placement at Danglikou clarifies the geography that [[SunCe|孙策]] will later have to break through or bypass.
