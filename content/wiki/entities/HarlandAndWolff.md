# Harland & Wolff

## Overview

Harland & Wolff was the Belfast shipbuilder that designed and constructed [[RMSTitanic|RMS Titanic]] within a long relationship with [[WhiteStarLine]].

## Current Profile

[[428-titanic-kings-of-the-world-part-2-glt7773063166]] presents the yard as an industrial system joining apprenticeship, departmental knowledge, naval architecture, large-scale fabrication, fitting out, and sea trials. [[ThomasAndrews]] advanced through that system before specializing in large, comfortable, steady, and safety-conscious liners. Titanic's double bottom and compartment design represented serious contemporary engineering, while the unbuilt provision for more lifeboats shows how regulatory requirements, deck use, and prevailing evacuation assumptions constrained the safety package actually delivered.

The yard was also embedded in [[Belfast]]'s political order. Construction and launch overlapped with Home Rule conflict, Lord Pirrie's pro-Home Rule position, and reported anti-Home Rule graffiti on Titanic's keel, making the ship both an industrial achievement and a contested local symbol.

## Key Characteristics

- Belfast shipbuilder responsible for Titanic's design, construction, launch, fitting out, and trials.
- Long-term industrial partner of White Star Line.
- Apprenticeship and cross-department training environment in which Thomas Andrews developed.
- Builder of large liners emphasizing comfort, steadiness, and contemporary safety engineering.
- Organization whose broader lifeboat plans were not adopted under the rules and assumptions of the period.
- Industrial institution embedded in Belfast's sectarian and Home Rule politics.

## Evidence

- Design organization: [[428-titanic-kings-of-the-world-part-2-glt7773063166]] follows Andrews from teenage apprentice through several departments into drafting and ship design.
- Construction and safety: [[428-titanic-kings-of-the-world-part-2-glt7773063166]] describes Titanic's double bottom, sixteen compartments, survivable flooding assumptions, and bulkhead-height limit.
- Lifeboat decision boundary: [[428-titanic-kings-of-the-world-part-2-glt7773063166]] says plans for substantially more boats existed but were not required or selected.
- Political setting: [[428-titanic-kings-of-the-world-part-2-glt7773063166]] places the yard and launch inside Belfast's Home Rule crisis.
