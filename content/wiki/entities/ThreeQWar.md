# 3Q War / 3Q 大战

## Overview
The 3Q War / 3Q 大战 is the [[Qihoo360|Qihoo 360]] versus [[Tencent]] conflict summarized in [[no-216-shi-bu-sha-yi-ren-hongyi-dapao-zhouhongyi-de-sanshinian-quanmian-zhanzhengshi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-27-1008693437]]. The episode treats it as a public crisis where security software, instant messaging, platform power, and user choice were turned into weapons.

## Current Profile
The event is the clearest case in this source for [[ChineseInternetPlatformWars]]: competitors with privileged access to user desktops could escalate product conflict into deletion, blocking, public accusation, and "choose one" pressure. The source also frames the aftermath as strategically consequential, pushing Tencent toward more openness and investment while pushing 360 to experiment with browser/platform opening.

## Key Characteristics
- Platform conflict between a security-software/browser company and a dominant social/IM platform.
- User-hostage moment in which software compatibility and deletion choices became public pressure.
- Government-mediated settlement rather than a purely market-resolved fight.
- Strategic turning point in the episode's account of Tencent openness and 360 platformization.
