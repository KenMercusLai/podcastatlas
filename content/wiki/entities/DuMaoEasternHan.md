## Overview

杜茂 / Du Mao is currently synthesized as an Eastern Han frontier commander whose removal marks a command-accountability point in Jianwu 15 / 39 CE. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-717-13-ge-zi-yinfa-de-diwang-zhinu-liz3jsbvw3snfapzsrqw917fqv7v|Hanji 717]] says he is dismissed after one of his subordinate commanders kills someone, after which [[MaChengEasternHan|Ma Cheng]] replaces him and [[ZhangKanEasternHan|Zhang Kan]] later uses his former troops at Gaoliu.

## Current Profile

Du Mao's page is intentionally narrow. The current source does not develop him as a full founding general biography; it records the accountability event and the reuse of his force structure. His importance is that Guangwu-era frontier correction reaches command rank rather than stopping at local disorder.

The reassignment branch also matters. Du Mao's removal does not make his troops useless: Liu Xiu sends Zhang Kan with Du Mao's troops, and Zhang Kan defeats Xiongnu at Gaoliu. Du Mao therefore connects punishment, personnel replacement, and continued operational use of frontier manpower.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Han frontier commander removed because misconduct by a subordinate commander is attributed upward.
- Accountability case showing that command responsibility could reach senior military office.
- Personnel bridge between Ma Cheng's replacement command and Zhang Kan's later use of former troops.
- Source-bounded figure currently known through one 39 CE frontier notice.

## Qualifications

The current source does not establish Du Mao's full earlier service, rank history beyond the frontier notice, or the legal details of the subordinate's killing. The page should not infer personal culpability beyond the source's removal-and-replacement sequence.
