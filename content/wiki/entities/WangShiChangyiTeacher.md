## Overview

王式 / Wang Shi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-409-hanxuandi-liubingyi-cong-yuzhong-zouchulai-de-chuanqi-huangdi-ljyxm9nd5rvheusna29wkxuraqux|Hanji 409]] as [[LiuHeHaihunHou|刘贺]]'s teacher, whose survival after the Changyi entourage punishment turns on whether teaching classics can count as remonstrance.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Wang Shi as a narrow but important boundary case. He is not shown stopping Liu He, but he can answer the charge that he failed to admonish because his daily [[Shijing|《诗经》]] instruction repeatedly highlighted loyal ministers, filial sons, and failed rulers. His tears and repeated explanation make teaching more than curriculum delivery: it becomes a record of attempted moral correction.

That profile distinguishes him from ordinary followers who merely accompanied Liu He into danger. Wang Shi survives because his role leaves a legible account of responsibility, even though the ruler-student did not change.

## Key Characteristics

- Changyi teacher attached to Liu He before or during the failed accession.
- Classics instructor whose Shi jing teaching is presented as repeated moral warning.
- Failed adviser whose unsuccessful instruction still becomes legally and politically meaningful.
- Survivor of the Changyi entourage punishment because he can document fulfilled role duty.
