# Planet Labs

## Overview
Planet Labs is a public Earth-observation company discussed in the All-In liquidity panel through co-founder [[WillMarshall|Will Marshall]]. In the source, the company operates a large satellite constellation that images Earth daily and sells time-series geospatial data into agriculture, energy, civil government, defense, intelligence, disaster response, and security use cases.

## Current Profile
The source frames Planet Labs as both a public-market transition case and a space-data infrastructure case. Going public gave early shareholders liquidity, added cash, and increased credibility with large customers that need assurance of supplier continuity. Technologically, Planet's profile centers on turning frequent satellite imaging into a current, analyzable data layer for real-world conditions rather than a static map product.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses a many-satellite constellation to make Earth observation more current and repeatable.
- Sells data into both commercial and government markets, with the source saying government customers are about 60% of revenue.
- Treats public-company status as customer credibility as well as investor liquidity.
- Connects cheaper launch and satellite miniaturization to expanded space applications.
- Serves as a concrete input source for [[EarthObservationAI]] and large Earth model claims.
