# Nemesis Drone Unit

## Overview
Nemesis is a Ukrainian drone battalion presented in [[drone-truth-russia-has-the-upper-hand-6aad03f98a1508074de8337e]] as specializing in long-range operations behind the front.

## Current Profile
The unit combines intelligence, target planning, and quadcopter operations in a tightly organized control-room workflow. Its namesake aircraft are described as flying long distances and dropping bombs on targets selected in advance.

## Key Characteristics
- Conducts long-range missions behind the front line.
- Integrates intelligence, planning, and aircraft operation.
- Uses large quadcopters to drop bombs on preselected targets.
- Operates through a focused control-room organization.
