# Clocked Out

## Overview
Clocked Out is a Marketplace reporting series about Americans who have left the workforce because they wanted to or because circumstances required it.

## Current Profile
The series enters the wiki through a short [[MarketplaceTech]] episode about [[ChrisBell]]. Its initial value is a case-based approach to labor-force exit: broad participation trends are made concrete through management pressure, household decisions, financial uncertainty, and life after formal employment.

## Key Characteristics
- Covers voluntary and involuntary exits from the American workforce.
- Uses individual stories to connect labor statistics with lived career and household decisions.
- Includes technology work as one branch rather than defining workforce exit as a technology-only phenomenon.
- Extends across Marketplace's morning and afternoon programming according to the launch episode.

## Evidence
- Series scope - [[tech-20260907-0907-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260907-0907-mp-tech-pod-128]] introduces Clocked Out as reporting on Americans who have left jobs voluntarily or involuntarily.
- First technology case - [[tech-20260907-0907-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260907-0907-mp-tech-pod-128]] follows Chris Bell's early retirement after a technology design and management career.
- Distribution - [[tech-20260907-0907-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260907-0907-mp-tech-pod-128]] directs listeners to further stories on Marketplace's morning and afternoon shows.

## Qualifications
Only the launch episode is represented here. Its broad claim that labor-force participation had fallen sharply is not accompanied in the supplied note by the underlying measure, comparison window, or demographic decomposition.
