# Sacred Paws Soap Company

## Overview
Sacred Paws Soap Company is the self-care kit business founded by [[AngelRaymer]] in [[advice-line-with-michelle-wahler-of-beyond-yoga-c2626aae-0d71-4025-842f-3b854ccc3ca6]], built around soap with a gemstone inside and an affirmation matched to that stone.

## Current Profile
Each kit contains a soap with an embedded stone, such as rose quartz for love, amethyst for calm, or tiger's eye for luck, plus an affirmation and a bag that collects the stone once the soap has dissolved. Raymer says many of the designs come from specific moments in her own life, including using tiger's eye for courage when she started the business. The kits sell for roughly $35 to $38, work strongly in person at fairs and festivals, and are pitched in the episode as plausible for a retail channel such as [[WholeFoodsMarket]].

## Key Characteristics
- Gifting-oriented self-care product with a ritual mechanic: the soap dissolves and leaves a kept stone.
- Story-backed design where the founder's own life stages shape the product line.
- Strong live-event selling and weak online translation of the same emotional response.
- Website gap: attractive product pages, empty About page, repeated "self care kit" language, and stone meanings hidden behind clicks.
- Price point near $35-38 a kit, with a retail-channel possibility raised by the advisor.
