# Lucas (Startup Embassy Resident)

## Overview
Lucas is the Seville-born Startup Embassy resident used in [[KenjiYamamoto]]'s account to illustrate entrepreneurial persistence.

## Current Profile
Lucas' wiki role is a source-scoped founder example rather than a verified company profile. The episode presents him as spending about a decade on pre-AI self-coding software while trying to sell a startup, making his story an example of long-duration founder commitment under family and health strain.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder from Seville, Spain, living in the hacker-house environment.
- Worked on pre-AI self-coding software for creating apps without another coding application.
- Tried to sell his startup to a large company, though the deal did not work out at the time described.
- Continued the effort while his wife was ill and his four young children remained in Spain.
