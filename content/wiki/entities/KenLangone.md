# Ken Langone

## Overview

Ken Langone is the investment banker, financier, and cofounder figure in [[home-depot-1]] who recognized [[BernieMarcus]] and [[ArthurBlank]]'s value before the market did and helped turn the post-Handy Dan firing into [[HomeDepot|Home Depot]].

## Current Profile

Langone's profile combines capital formation, founder belief, governance influence, and long-term ownership. The episode treats him as both the outside sponsor who could raise the first money and the investor who held Home Depot through severe drawdowns.

## Key Characteristics
- Outside believer who identified Handy Dan's operating strength.
- Capital organizer after the Ross Perot funding attempt failed.
- Board and succession actor involved in recruiting [[BobNardelli]].
- Long-term shareholder used as the episode's holding-discipline example.

## Evidence
- [[home-depot-1]] says Langone reviewed Handy Dan's financials, met Marcus, and concluded the business quality was real.
- [[home-depot-1]] says Langone told Marcus after the firing that they would start the company Marcus had described.
- [[home-depot-1]] says Langone raised money from roughly 40 investors after the Ross Perot deal collapsed.
- [[home-depot-1]] says Langone had not sold a Home Depot share and held a multibillion-dollar stake despite major drawdowns.

## Qualifications

Langone is a central narrator and source contact for the episode, so some founder and investor judgments remain source-scoped to the episode's perspective.
