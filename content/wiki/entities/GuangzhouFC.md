## Overview
广州队 / Guangzhou FC is the former Evergrande-era Guangzhou club whose memory anchors the episode's discussion of football legacy. The source presents the club as both a peak-achievement case in Chinese professional football and a damaged institution whose disappearance left academy players, supporters, slogans, and city identity looking for new carriers.

## Current Profile
The source treats Guangzhou FC as more than a defunct club shell. Its eight Chinese top-flight titles, two Asian championships, strong South China supporter base, and link to [[EvergrandeFootballSchool]] made it a regional identity. At the same time, the club is part of the critique of salary escalation, contract symbolism, and governance boundary blur during the Evergrande era.

Post-collapse, Guangzhou FC's identity survives unevenly. Some supporters chant "广州队" for [[GuangdongChenxingJuli]], while many resist treating [[GuangzhouLeopard]] as a simple replacement because they see the original club's shutdown and the new team's creation as institutionally disconnected.

## Key Characteristics
- Evergrande-era club remembered for eight domestic league titles and two Asian championships in the source.
- Regional football identity that reached beyond Guangzhou into Guangdong and South China.
- Academy-linked institution whose player pipeline now continues partly outside the original club.
- Contested legacy case because sporting success coexisted with salary inflation, commercial-contract controversy, and blurred state/club goals.
- Defunct or discontinued identity whose supporters distinguish between city football, team name, and concrete institutional lineage.
