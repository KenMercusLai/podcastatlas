## Overview

郭圣通 / Guo Shengtong enters the wiki as the deposed Guangwu empress whose lost status reshapes the Eastern Han succession field. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-720-donghan-kaiguo-taizi-weihe-fanfu-da-cizhi-baogao-lslizspo8rywnf3cebyhincdhvz0|Hanji 720]] makes her deposition the direct background to [[LiuQiangDonghaiKing|刘强]]'s repeated request to give up the crown-prince position; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-722-rang-liuxiu-xiansi-de-toutongbing-zenme-huishi-logwccdsca7vm2zzssu8ubm8rwra|Hanji 722]] shows later compensation to the Guo branch; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-732-donghan-guangwudi-liuxiu-jiabeng-bingsi-de-lnsu1le2x-cj75cjslysvbndeuvr|Hanji 732]] shows the same grievance still usable at [[EmperorMingOfHan|Mingdi]]'s accession.

## Current Profile

This page is source-bounded to Guo Shengtong's political afterlife rather than a full biography. Hanji 720 supplies the decisive succession layer: once Guo is deposed, Liu Qiang no longer has the maternal status that made his crown-prince position secure. Zhi Yun's advice to Liu Qiang treats the changed mother-son field as both a danger and a filial problem, so Liu Qiang's withdrawal becomes a way to protect himself and serve his mother from a safer position.

Hanji 722 gives the next Guo-branch layer. After Empress Guo's displacement, Liu Xiu changes [[LiuFuPeiKingEasternHan|Liu Fu]] from Zhongshan king to Pei king and rewards Guo Kuang with office, visits, and large gifts. The episode presents this as compensation: Guo's loss of empress status does not erase her branch, but it shifts their security from succession centrality to managed honor.

Hanji 732 shows why the deposition remains politically live. After [[LiuXiu|Guangwu]] dies, [[LiuJingGuanglingKingEasternHan|Liu Jing]] sends Liu Qiang an anonymous letter arguing that Liu Qiang had been wrongly displaced and that Queen Guo had been wrongly removed. The letter tries to turn Guo's earlier status loss into accession grievance, but Liu Qiang reports it and Mingdi handles the matter quietly.

## Key Characteristics

- Deposed Guangwu empress whose lost status changes her son's crown-prince security.
- Maternal-line node rather than an independently developed biography in the current wiki evidence.
- Her displacement helps explain Liu Qiang's voluntary withdrawal from heirship in 43 CE.
- Her branch receives compensatory honor through Liu Fu's re-enfeoffment and Guo Kuang's rewards.
- Her removal remains a dangerous grievance frame at Mingdi's accession.

## Qualifications

The current page does not reconstruct Guo Shengtong's full life, marriage history, original empress appointment, deposition procedure, death, or natal-family genealogy. It tracks only the bounded evidence that affects succession risk, branch compensation, and later accession grievance. Guo Kuang remains source-scoped until a separate source makes his profile durable.
