# Bowuzhi / 《博物志》

[[Bowuzhi|《博物志》]] is the strange-knowledge text associated with [[ZhangHua|张华]] in [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-jie-shangji-704051550]]. The episode presents it as a source of unusual stories, omens, transformations, and object lore rather than as a narrowly modern encyclopedia.

Its role in the wiki is archival. Like [[SouShenJi|《搜神记》]] and [[YouyangZazu|《酉阳杂俎》]], it stores odd reports that can reveal how people connected natural signs, artifacts, politics, and supernatural explanation.

## Key Claims
- A miscellany-like text can preserve cultural reasoning even when claims are not treated as verified fact.
- The episode uses Bowuzhi to connect strange narration with star and object symbolism.
- Its value is best kept source-scoped, not converted into a factual proof archive.
