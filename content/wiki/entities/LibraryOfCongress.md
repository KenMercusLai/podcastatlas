# Library of Congress

The Library of Congress appears in [[tech-20260421-0421-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260421-0421-mp-tech-pod-128]] as an alternative web-archiving institution. [[AndrewDeck]] says it has a smaller but substantial internet archiving project, making it relevant to the question of whether [[PublicWebArchiving]] could rely more on state-supported institutions.

The episode immediately adds a governance caution. State-funded archiving may provide scale and durability, but governments can have incentives to change, hide, or narrow the historical record. That tension links the Library of Congress example to [[InternetHistoryFragility]] rather than making it a simple replacement for the [[InternetArchive]] or [[WaybackMachine|Wayback Machine]].
