# 萧何 / Xiao He

萧何 / Xiao He enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-6-dingji-tianshilun-touziren-lvgong-lhqqja27zxdiypgf6wuv1abgkw3j|Qinji 128-6]] as a [[PeiCounty|沛县]] official already close enough to [[LiuBang|刘邦]] to manage his public excess. At [[LuGongLiuBangFatherInLaw|吕公]]'s banquet, Xiao He is responsible for sorting guests by gift amount and seating status.

When Liu Bang arrives without money and loudly claims "贺钱一万," Xiao He tries to defuse the scene by saying Liu Ji talks big and achieves little. The episode treats this not as mere mockery, but as evidence that Xiao He understands Liu Bang's style and can manage the social risk around him.

The source later groups Xiao He with Lu Gong and [[ZhangLiang|张良]] as people who can recognize Liu Bang's unusual potential. Xiao He therefore belongs to [[GrassrootsOfficialNetwork|基层官吏网络政治资本]]: the lower county-office circle that Liu Bang builds before he has a serious army and that later feeds the [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]] founding group.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-7-liubang-he-xiangyu-youhe-butong-llwcqatndck4zita2piufwhjpxp1|Qinji 128-7]] makes Xiao He's recognition operational during the Pei County uprising. After the [[DazexiangUprising|大泽乡起义]] spreads, Xiao He and [[CaoShen|曹参]] advise the county magistrate to recall [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s fugitive group because it can help control the local situation. When the magistrate panics and plans to kill them, they escape over the wall and join Liu Bang.

Xiao He then helps turn Liu Bang's outlaw force into a county army. After Liu Bang's letter persuades Pei County elders to kill the magistrate and open the gates, Xiao He and Cao Shen recruit in and around [[PeiCounty|沛县]], gathering more than three thousand men. This makes Xiao He central to [[PeiCountyUprisingMobilization|沛县起义动员]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-1-jiemi-wuguang-bei-miekou-de-lishi-zhenxiang-lk1bkhqkwf9upbbj08pjkn8cstyp|Qinji 129-1]] moves Xiao He from Pei County mobilization into Liu Bang's early battlefield politics. After [[LiuBang|刘邦]] defeats the Sishui commandery supervisor Ping, Xiao He and [[XiahouYing|夏侯婴]] persuade Ping to surrender Huling County. The episode uses this as part of Liu Bang's local-gain line while [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]] suffers larger command failures.
