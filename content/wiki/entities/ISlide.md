# iSlide

## Overview
iSlide is [[JustinKittredge]]'s company producing customized slide sandals, slippers, and socks.

## Current Profile
iSlide serves professional leagues, colleges, local teams, companies, and individual buyers through direct e-commerce, wholesale, bulk orders, and partner dropshipping. Its multi-channel structure supplied resilience when pandemic conditions shifted demand, but the next revenue stage may require asymmetrical resource allocation rather than equal attention everywhere. The proposed approach keeps secondary channels under named owners while leadership concentrates on larger, repeatable partnerships or other leading opportunities.

## Key Characteristics
- Has operated for thirteen years.
- Reports about $5.5 million in annual revenue and ten employees.
- Targets $10 million in annual revenue.
- Combines direct, wholesale, bulk, and dropshipping channels.
- Explores AI-assisted outreach and communication for its sales team.
