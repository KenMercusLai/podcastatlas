# Julius Kurfgen

## Overview
Julius Kurfgen is the [[Uplane]] co-founder interviewed by [[OmerKhan]] in [[selling-before-building-1m-arr-in-six-months]]. The episode presents him as a marketing-operator founder whose prior work building thousands of ads by hand shaped Uplane's AI marketing automation thesis.

## Current Profile
Julius's strongest contribution to the wiki is tactical: he argues that founders should sell before building. His method is to run discovery calls, listen for concrete operational pain, schedule a near-term demo, and build a real prototype around the customer's stated problem before the next conversation.

The episode also gives him an operating profile. He treats fast responsiveness as a startup advantage, accepts that early Uplane delivery includes service work, uses performance-linked pricing to align incentives, and sees AI value in better marketing decisions rather than indiscriminate content volume.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses founder-market fit from prior marketing work as the basis for startup insight.
- Advocates [[DiscoveryLedDemoSprint|discovery-led demo sprints]] and paid pilots before substantial product buildout.
- Treats founder responsiveness and fast customer communication as part of the early product experience.
- Accepts a [[ManagedServiceAutomationLayer]] where human operators deliver outcomes while automation matures.
- Emphasizes relevance, attribution, and [[AtomicContentGuardrails|guardrails]] over raw AI-generated marketing volume.

## Evidence
- Founder-market fit: [[selling-before-building-1m-arr-in-six-months]] says Julius previously worked in marketing, built around 10,000 ads by hand, and experienced the tracking and iteration pain Uplane addresses.
- Sell-first method: [[selling-before-building-1m-arr-in-six-months]] records his advice to run discovery calls, book a demo about one week later, and build a working solution for the identified pain.
- Responsiveness: [[selling-before-building-1m-arr-in-six-months]] says Uplane has a rule that client questions should be answered within 120 seconds.
- AI marketing judgment: [[selling-before-building-1m-arr-in-six-months]] says Julius criticizes low-quality AI spam and wants AI to use performance data, audience context, and approved content to improve relevance.

## Qualifications
The page reflects Julius's self-reported account in one interview. It does not independently verify Uplane's customer results, exact founding timeline, prior work history, or the long-term scalability of his recommended operating model.
