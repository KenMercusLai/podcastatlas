# Google Ventures

[[GoogleVentures|Google Ventures]] is the venture fund [[BillMaris|Bill Maris]] says he built inside [[Google]] with [[RichMiner|Rich Miner]] in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bill-maris-how-google-could-crush-ai-competitors-why-small-funds-win-and-ais-atari-stage-41586010]]. The source treats the fund as a case of [[VentureComputerScienceEdge]] because Maris says the team used machine learning, simulations, and backtesting to reason about portfolio construction and fund size.

The source also makes Google Ventures part of Google's broader moonshot and startup interface. Maris says he helped incubate projects such as [[Waymo]], [[GoogleX|Google X]], and [[Calico]], while the hosts mention Climate Corp's $1 billion sale to Monsanto as one investment case.

## Source Position
- Google Ventures was created when Google saw a need for venture exposure but did not yet have a coherent strategy or budget.
- [[RichMiner|Rich Miner]] is presented as Maris's partner in the early buildout.
- The source says Google would not let them call the work AI at the time, so they called it machine learning.
- Maris estimates Google Ventures' publicly inferable return at about 4.1x and says the investments he led performed better than that broad figure.
