# Sebastian (The Residency)

Sebastian is the [[TheResidency]] operator/interviewee in [[tanfang-hacker-house-guigu-nianqingren-zhengzai-banjin-ai-chuangye-sushe-s10e10-f0870843-fd16-49f5-8f24-80281f9fff77]]. The source uses him to explain the hacker-house taxonomy: informal friend houses, branded but mainly rent-driven houses, and equity-driven houses that exchange founder support for company ownership.

Sebastian also supplies several of the episode's core operating claims. He says The Residency founders' companies have produced roughly $3 billion in combined valuation over recent years, describes non-local founder entry into the Bay Area as [[FounderNetworkArbitrage]], and estimates the San Francisco operating cost of The Residency at roughly $2 million to $2.5 million per year.
