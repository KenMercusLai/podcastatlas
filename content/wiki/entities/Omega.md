# Omega

Omega is a watch brand used in [[rolex-1]] as both a historical competitor and a strategic contrast to [[Rolex]]. The episode associates Omega with aviation and space legitimacy through the NASA Moonwatch story, while arguing that Omega reacted poorly during the quartz era by expanding models, leaning into quartz, and diluting the brand.

For the wiki, Omega helps make [[MechanicalWatchRepositioning]] concrete. Rolex's relative discipline matters because another strong Swiss brand had technical legitimacy but, in the hosts' reading, did not protect category meaning as cleanly during the same shock.
