# Assam

Assam appears in [[settler-bet-a-west-bank-flashpoint-6a842bb539e73ea8f3c13a2d]] as one of the [[BharatiyaJanataParty|BJP]]-ruled Indian states that has passed a [[UniformCivilCodeIndia|uniform civil-code]] version. The episode groups it with [[Gujarat]] and [[MadhyaPradesh]] after the initial [[Uttarakhand]] case.

The source does not develop Assam's legal text in detail. Its role is comparative: state-level adoption makes the civil-code issue a spreading governance pattern rather than a single-state experiment affecting [[IndianMuslims]] and [[MuslimPersonalLawIndia]].
