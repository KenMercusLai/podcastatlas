# Robin Tauquedet

## Overview
Robin Tauquedet is the founder of [[Taximetry]], represented by one [[TomorrowToday]] interview as an engineer and advertising-technology operator building AI-assisted creative workflows.

## Current Profile
The source presents Tauquedet as pursuing scalable ad production without treating generation as the whole system. He separates research, strategy, asset creation, quality review, and campaign feedback; supports different degrees of AI involvement; and argues that brand assets, verifier agents, human reviewers, regulation, education, and cybersecurity provide complementary controls. His commercial case remains prospective because the episode supplies no audited performance comparison.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames [[CreativeFatigue]] as an asset-supply and experimentation problem for advertisers.
- Orchestrates specialized image, video, voice, reasoning, and storytelling systems rather than relying on one model.
- Supports selectable automation, including AI selection and recombination of human-produced brand libraries.
- Retains human review for public brand representation while acknowledging that oversight becomes harder at high volume.
- Separates campaign learning from canonical brand identity through [[BrandIdentityFeedbackIsolation]].
- Treats technical verification as necessary but insufficient against deliberate synthetic-media fraud.
