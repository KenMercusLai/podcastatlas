# First Southwest Bank

## Overview
First Southwest Bank is the Colorado bank used in the Marketplace Tech episode as the lending-workflow example for AI-enabled document review and borrower-meeting preparation.

## Current Profile
The source presents First Southwest Bank as using AI to reduce administrative burden around loan applications while preserving a human decision boundary. Chief credit officer [[ChristyEscobel]] says the bank wants staff to spend more time on borrower relationships and less time on paperwork, but is not using AI to make loan decisions.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses AI in loan-document review and staff preparation rather than final approval or denial.
- Frames speed as a way to redirect human attention toward borrower relationships.
- Treats bias, privacy, accuracy, and vendor accountability as core constraints.
- Supplies the source's clearest example of [[HumanInTheLoopCreditDecisioning]] inside small-bank AI adoption.
