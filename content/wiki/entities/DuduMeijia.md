# 嘟嘟美甲 / Dudu Meijia

## Overview
嘟嘟美甲 / Dudu Meijia is the home-manicure O2O company tied to [[LiuFei|刘飞]]'s first-person founder memory in this episode.

## Current Profile
The source uses Dudu Meijia to add an inside-operator perspective to the O2O story. Founded in Shanghai in 2014 by seven technical-background founders, including Liu Fei, it tried to standardize home manicure through training, uniforms, fixed pricing, style labels, tools, group-buying scenarios, and promotional events before later being sold to 58 Daojia.

## Key Characteristics
- It is the episode's main first-person O2O operator case.
- It attempted to standardize a skilled beauty service rather than only run an open artisan marketplace.
- Its operating details make the abstract standardization problem visible through tools, training, styles, pricing, promotion, and enterprise orders.
- Its later sale to 58 Daojia shows how independent local-service O2O projects often exited into broader service platforms.
