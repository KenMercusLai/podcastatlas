## Overview

明德马皇后 / Empress Ma enters the wiki through Hanji 752 as the lost patron whose death changes the political exposure of the Ma household under [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Emperor Zhang]]. The source does not reconstruct her full reign; it uses her absence to explain why the formerly protected [[MaFamilyZhangEraEasternHan|Ma family]] has to survive without the court shelter that had made wealth, guests, and access less immediately dangerous.

## Current Profile

Hanji 752 presents Empress Ma indirectly. While alive, she is the Ma family's strongest protection channel and a marker of legitimate closeness to the imperial household. Once she dies, Ma Liao, Ma Fang, Ma Guang, and the wider household remain high-status relatives, but their visible luxury and guest network no longer enjoy the same political cover.

This makes her page a patronage-boundary node. The important point is not that Empress Ma herself orders later Ma behavior, but that a family can keep acting as if old protection still exists after the person who made that behavior tolerable has gone. The episode contrasts this with the rising Dou family: one in-law house is losing cover just as another is gaining access.

## Key Characteristics

- Posthumous protection figure for the Zhang-era Ma family.
- Imperial consort whose death alters the risk surface around Ma household wealth, guests, and younger relatives.
- Background for Ma Liao's failure to convert inherited prestige into disciplined self-protection.
- Contrast to Empress Dou's rising natal-family influence in the same episode.
- Source-bounded figure whose independent biography is not developed in this wiki page.

## Evidence

- Lost-patronage setup: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-752-shiqu-kaoshan-de-ren-gai-ruhe-chushi-lt0qb7alyh0ipf6mk8hvz92cs297|Hanji 752]] says that after Empress Ma's death, the Ma family becomes a powerful household that has lost its main court backing.
- Ma household exposure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-752-shiqu-kaoshan-de-ren-gai-ruhe-chushi-lt0qb7alyh0ipf6mk8hvz92cs297|Hanji 752]] links her absence to Ma Fang and Ma Guang's luxury, guests, horses, and exactions becoming visible to Zhangdi.
- Contrast with Dou ascent: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-752-shiqu-kaoshan-de-ren-gai-ruhe-chushi-lt0qb7alyh0ipf6mk8hvz92cs297|Hanji 752]] places Ma decline beside Empress Dou's brothers gaining palace access and reward.

## Qualifications

The current bounded evidence is one episode and uses Empress Ma chiefly as a lost protection channel. The page should not be treated as a full biography, a complete assessment of her political role, or a full account of earlier Ma-family privilege. The source's practical lesson about "靠山" is the host's synthesis from the Ma household aftermath.
