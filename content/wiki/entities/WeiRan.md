# 魏冉 / Wei Ran

魏冉 / Wei Ran appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-59-yichao-junwang-yichaochen-ljq-qkueix0rnzfekzuadicce1bh]] at the end of the episode's Qin succession notice. After [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] dies without a son, his brothers struggle for the throne, and Wei Ran uses forceful measures to make his nephew Ying Ji succeed as [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-60-zhongguo-di-yi-wei-bachi-chaozheng-de-taihou-jingshi-ta-ltazis9qo0dwvwmzxikk8mmpvosc]] supplies those details. Wei Ran is made general and guards [[Xianyang|咸阳]], then suppresses [[YingZhuang|嬴壮]]'s revolt by killing or eliminating rebel princes, officials, and followers. The purge also reaches [[QinHuiwenhou|惠文后]] and Qin Wuwang's wife, making Wei Ran the coercive arm of [[XuanTaihou|芈八子 / 宣太后]]'s early court.

The episode also gives him a structural role: under a young Qin Zhaoxiang Wang, Xuan Taihou hands major power to Wei Ran, who becomes feared throughout Qin. He therefore anchors [[ForeignRelativeCourtPolitics|楚系外戚政治]] as more than maternal influence; the kin bloc has armed force, office, and purge capacity.
