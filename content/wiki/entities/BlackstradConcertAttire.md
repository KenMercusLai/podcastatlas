## Overview
Blackstrad Concert Attire is an apparel company founded by orchestral flutist [[MercedesSmith]] to make formal, comfortable clothing that meets performance dress codes.

## Current Profile
The source presents Blackstrad as an early company with meaningful revenue and strong founder-domain credibility, but with product iteration, returns, restocking, and founder overload constraining scale. The proposed path is to concentrate on a hero SKU, use professional networks for feedback, delegate operations, and test institutional ensemble orders.

## Key Characteristics
- Emerged from a performer's long-running difficulty finding suitable concert clothing.
- Combines formal appearance, comfort, and dress-code compliance as its product promise.
- Reportedly generated about $40,000 in monthly revenue less than two years after launch.
- Faces tension between repeated product refinement and launch or restock speed.
- Depends heavily on a founder who also holds a full-time symphony position.
