# Hakeem Soleimani

## Overview
Hakeem Soleimani is the Yemeni American founder of [[YaffaCafe]], a Brooklyn specialty-coffee business opened in 2019 near his father [[TarekSoleimani]]'s deli.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Soleimani as a second-generation entrepreneur who transformed rather than rejected his family's retail inheritance. After working in the deli and pursuing freelance photography, he combined an unmet need for neighborhood cafe workspace, specialty-coffee knowledge, Yemeni origin identity, and his family's local and overseas relationships. The cafe later became profitable and added a second location.

## Key Characteristics
- Grew up learning customer service and small-business operations through the family bodega.
- Identified a neighborhood demand for a place to work and gather rather than simply inheriting the deli format.
- Combined modern specialty drinks with Yemen's coffee history and direct sourcing.
- Relied on family relationships for a lease, local credibility, farm contacts, inspection, and difficult import logistics.
- Adapted through severe transport and payment constraints created by war and banking disruption.
