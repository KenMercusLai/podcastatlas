# 惠施 / Hui Shi

惠施 / Hui Shi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-27-weiguan-weihuiwang-he-qiweiwang-shi-za-shangye-hupeng-lminlxhrm5bethub9kyy-8blimrb]] as the [[WeiState|魏国]] adviser who talks [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王 / 梁惠王]] out of an emotional revenge campaign against [[QiState|齐国]] after [[MalingBattle|马陵之战]]. The episode presents him as a strategic realist: Wei has already suffered from conflict with Zhao and Qi, so another all-out attack would risk more than it could gain.

His proposed move is [[LowPostureStatecraft|low-posture statecraft]] as a tactic. Hui Shi suggests that Wei defer publicly to [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]], then use the news to irritate [[ChuState|楚国]] and create space for Qi-Chu conflict. In the episode, this advice becomes one half of [[XuzhouMutualKingship|徐州相王]]: Wei can acknowledge Qi's kingly title without simply accepting permanent humiliation, and Qi can return the recognition to preserve Wei's face.

This page is source-scoped. It records Hui Shi's role in the Xuzhou diplomacy episode without trying to summarize his wider intellectual biography.
