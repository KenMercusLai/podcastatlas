## Overview
Tara Bosch is the founder of [[SmartSweets]] and the founder of [[Snackish]], appearing as the guest adviser in [[advice-line-with-tara-bosch-of-smartsweets-8aab80a1-b0c5-4103-b875-f5d4823d07ce]].

## Current Profile
Bosch's advice combines packaged-food operating experience with a customer-learning discipline. She treats taste as the threshold for functional snacks, retail as a channel with a distinct purchase occasion rather than an automatic substitute for DTC, manufacturing as a scale gate, customer language and referral behavior as evidence, and a concrete five-year vision as a decision filter.

## Key Characteristics
- Builds consumer products around health improvements that preserve familiar pleasure and emotional appeal.
- Uses direct customer conversations to find positioning language and reasons for retention.
- Distinguishes product-market-fit evidence from early enthusiasm by looking for completion, recommendation, and conversion behavior.
- Treats channel choice, production capacity, and retailer requirements as connected operating decisions.
- Reports greater comfort with uncertainty in her second venture without suggesting that prior success removes startup intensity.
