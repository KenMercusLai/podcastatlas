# 刘先 / Liu Xian (late Han)

## Overview
刘先 / Liu Xian (late Han) is a Jingzhou adviser who appears with [[HanSongLateHan|韩嵩]] in Hanji 1036, urging [[LiuBiao|刘表]] to stop drifting between [[YuanShao|袁绍]] and [[CaoCao|曹操]].

## Current Profile
The current source treats Liu Xian as a concise but important voice inside Liu Biao's advisory circle. Alongside Han Song, he argues that Liu Biao must either pursue a larger independent strategy after the Yuan-Cao struggle weakens both sides or quickly aid one camp. His advice matters because it names the cost of Liu Biao's apparent neutrality: a powerful regional ruler cannot hold troops, refuse aid, avoid alliance, and still expect both stronger sides to tolerate him indefinitely.

## Key Characteristics
- Advises Liu Biao during the 199 CE Yuan Shao-Cao Cao confrontation.
- Shares Han Song's assessment that Liu Biao's wait-and-see posture is becoming strategically dangerous.
- Treats Cao Cao as the more likely winner because of military ability, strategy, and talent attraction.
- Frames Liu Biao's troop strength as a source of exposure, not only a source of safety.
- Functions as an internal warning voice rather than as an independent powerholder in the current source.
