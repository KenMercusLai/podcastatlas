# 刘先 / Liu Xian (late Han)

## Overview
刘先 / Liu Xian (late Han) is a Jingzhou adviser who warns [[LiuBiao|刘表]] against prolonged indecision and later receives office under [[CaoCao|曹操]].

## Current Profile
Hanji 1036 places Liu Xian beside [[HanSongLateHan|韩嵩]] in arguing that Liu Biao cannot keep troops, refuse aid, avoid alliance, and expect both [[YuanShao|袁绍]] and Cao Cao to tolerate him. He also judges Cao Cao the likelier winner because of military ability, strategy, and talent attraction.

Hanji 1095 adds the post-conquest result. After Jingzhou submits, Cao Cao appoints Liu Xian master of writing, converting a former internal warning voice into a court official.

## Key Characteristics
- Advises Liu Biao during the Yuan-Cao confrontation.
- Treats neutrality as strategic exposure rather than safety.
- Judges Cao Cao the stronger likely winner.
- Functions as an internal warning voice rather than an independent powerholder.
- Receives a Cao-side court appointment after Jingzhou's surrender.
