# 赵葱 / Zhao Cong

赵葱 / Zhao Cong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-113-limu-ruhe-yong-yiji-zhili-zu-qinguo-tongyi-luymqpcolsvyz5miodit3wdlx0ob]] as one of the replacement commanders [[ZhaoYoumiuWang|赵迁 / 赵幽缪王]] appoints after removing [[LiMu|李牧]] and [[SimaShang|司马尚]]. The source does not develop his tactics; his importance is structural.

In the episode, Zhao Cong's appointment marks the moment when a court decision solves a ruler's suspicion while damaging the army. The host stresses that even before evaluating Zhao Cong and [[YanJuZhao|颜聚]] as commanders, changing generals in front of an invading Qin force undermines morale and continuity.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-114-1-jintian-liaoliao-yingzheng-dou-you-naxie-canren-shouduan-lmqky6g3qi9nklpdnye4lk5rrbz6]] gives the battlefield result of that appointment. In the source's 228 BCE continuation, [[WangJianQin|王翦]] defeats Zhao, Zhao Cong is killed, Yan Ju flees, and [[Handan|邯郸]] falls.
