# Zhao Yufei / 赵雨飞

## Overview
Zhao Yufei / 赵雨飞 is presented by [[307-weishenme-guonei-henduo-xueshu-fanyi-zhiliang-zheme-cha-lkbjmojuqcokbee64h9hqvrzaf4r]] as a Chinese-trained political-philosophy reader and translator who discusses standards for reliable scholarly translation.

## Current Profile
The episode draws on Zhao's undergraduate reading experience at Peking University's Yuanpei College and on collaborative translation work involving [[JeanJacquesRousseau]]. He argues that intellectual translation requires more than bilingual fluency: stable terminology, knowledge of a thinker's whole system, investigation of context and correspondence, and explicit reasons for revising established translations.

His position distinguishes rigorous scholarly editions from more relaxed popular translation without treating accessibility as irrelevant. The central boundary is that readability and elegance cannot license silent changes to argument, ambiguity, or conceptual relations.

## Key Characteristics
- Reader and translator with formal Chinese academic training as described by the source.
- Uses difficult reading experiences to show how unreliable translations can misdirect reader self-assessment.
- Prioritizes terminology consistency and whole-author understanding in intellectual translation.
- Treats annotation and textual research as substantive scholarly labor rather than optional ornament.
- Accepts different publication audiences while demanding explicit justification for changing established terms.
