## Overview

新丰 / Xinfeng is the Western Han settlement that [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-328-1-zhongguo-zuqiu-shijie-diyi-juran-shi-2000nian-lvm7t6lvc2neidswaddufxgdw8w_|Hanji 328-1]] says [[LiuBang|刘邦]] built east of Chang'an in imitation of Pei County so that [[LiuTaigong|刘太公]] could recover his familiar social environment and play [[HanDynastyCuju|蹴鞠]] with old companions.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Xinfeng as a family-welfare project executed through urban and population policy. Material abundance in Chang'an does not restore Liu Taigong's well-being because the move has separated him from friends, routines, and recreation. Liu Bang therefore reproduces the layout of [[PeiCounty|沛县]] and moves former neighbors near the capital, turning place-making into the repair of a disrupted social network.

The source associates the settlement with today's Xinfeng town in Lintong, Shaanxi. Because it does not provide a geographical or textual apparatus, exact distance, construction scale, relocation mechanics, and modern-site identification remain episode-attributed.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han settlement represented as a deliberate imitation of Pei County near Chang'an.
- Family-support project intended to restore Liu Taigong's familiar community and daily life.
- Cuju setting that connects urban relocation, friendship, recreation, and elder well-being.
- Source-scoped ancient place associated by the episode with modern Xinfeng in Lintong.

## Qualifications

This page currently rests on one compact podcast retelling rather than the underlying *Xijing Zaji* passage, archaeological evidence, or a historical-geography study. The episode's "league" language is a modern analogy, and the claim that the settlement copied Pei County at full scale should not be expanded beyond the source without further evidence.
