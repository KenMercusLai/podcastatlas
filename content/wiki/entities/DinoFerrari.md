# Dino Ferrari

Dino Ferrari is [[EnzoFerrari]]'s son whose early death is treated in [[ferrari-1]] as part of Ferrari's emotional history. The source says Enzo stopped attending races after Dino died, while Ferrari's wider mythology continued to bind speed, danger, grief, and vitality together.

For the wiki, Dino is source-scoped as a family and myth node rather than a general biography. He matters because the episode uses his death to explain why [[Ferrari]]'s brand meaning cannot be separated from Enzo's personal losses.
