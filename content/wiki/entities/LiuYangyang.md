# 刘洋洋 / Liu Yangyang

刘洋洋 / Liu Yangyang appears in [[22-zuqiu-jingjixue-duzhe-bubi-shi-qiumi-570874427]] as the translator of the Chinese edition of [[Soccernomics|《足球经济学》 / Soccernomics]]. The episode ends by stressing that the translation was unusually difficult because the book requires both football literacy and the ability to carry economic argument, historical detail, and cultural references.

Her page is source-scoped: the episode does not provide a full translator biography. Its wiki value is to preserve the publishing labor behind a book whose Chinese release was delayed by requested deletions and author refusal to accept those cuts.
