# Leonardo Lins

Leonardo Lins appears in [[pump-and-circumstance-is-china-the-new-opec-6a7c37287cf3c58886773a66]] as the Brazilian comedian whose hate-speech and racist-speech conviction is used to test the limits of Brazil's tougher speech-law regime. The source says a Sao Paulo court sentenced him to eight years and three months in a high-security prison and imposed fines of more than $300,000, before a higher court later overturned the conviction.

The episode uses Lins less as a biography than as a boundary case for [[HateSpeechCriminalizationTradeoff]]: harsh punishment of racist expression may signal social seriousness, but it can also create free-speech and judicial-discretion concerns.
