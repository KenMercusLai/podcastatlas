# Honda / 本田

[[wake-up-haul-an-ozempic-moment-for-the-brain-6a85798d1ad8716bb6078744]] adds Honda as a postwar [[Japan]] startup-success example in [[EthanWu]]'s argument. The source uses [[Sony]], Honda, and [[SoftBank]] to separate historical entrepreneurial capacity from the present [[JapaneseStartupExitConstraint]] around late-stage capital, founder status, and early IPO pressure.

Honda / 本田 appears in [[yushu-shangshi-baozhang-dan-renxing-jiqiren-de-qian-daodi-cong-nali-zhuan-s10e26-4a50d4a3-a6ff-4c89-b754-367b73ce924b]] as an early humanoid-robot reference. The episode places Honda's humanoid work inside a "new mobility" logic: because human spaces are built around human bodies, robots that help people might need a roughly human form.

The source does not treat Honda's argument as settled proof. Instead, Honda supplies the historical reason humanoid form remains attractive, while [[RobotFormFactorPragmatism]] keeps open whether a humanoid is better than quadruped, wheeled, industrial, service, or entertainment-specific forms in real commercial scenes.
