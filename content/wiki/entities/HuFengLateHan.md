# 胡封 / Hu Feng (late Han)

胡封 / Hu Feng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-984-liangzhouxi-junfa-neidou-ta-weihe-di-yige-daoxia-lurb2qfzsa7e-owh-dl-m-sccze5|Hanji 984]] as the figure named in the Hou Hanshu variant of [[FanChouLateHan|樊稠]]'s death. The page is deliberately narrow because the source uses Hu Feng only inside that variant.

In the variant, [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] is jealous of Fan Chou's military prestige and army support. He invites Fan Chou to a banquet, gets him drunk, and has Hu Feng kill him. The main sequence in the same source instead places Fan Chou's death after [[LiLiLateHan|李利]] reports Fan Chou's friendly contact with [[HanSui|韩遂]] and Fan Chou later asks for troops.

The wiki therefore treats Hu Feng as part of a source-variant death mechanism rather than as proof that the banquet version has displaced the meeting version.
