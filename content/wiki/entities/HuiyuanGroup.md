# Huiyuan Group / 汇源集团

## Overview
Huiyuan Group / 汇源集团 is the beverage operating group described in the source as retaining distributor and industrial-chain resources while no longer being entitled to sell juice under the Huiyuan trademark.

## Current Profile
The episode places Huiyuan Group inside the aftermath of debt problems and Beijing Huiyuan's bankruptcy restructuring. After a first-instance infringement ruling, the group announced a break with the Huiyuan brand and shifted marketing and distributor recruitment toward [[ZanjiaGuoyuan|咱家果园]]. Its current profile is therefore an operating network attempting to transfer commercial capability into a new consumer identity.

## Key Characteristics
- Beverage group with production, distribution, and industrial-chain resources described by the episode.
- Party to a trademark and restructuring conflict involving the Huiyuan mark.
- Operator found at first instance to have infringed through Huiyuan-branded production and sales.
- Company attempting a national rebrand and distributor recruitment through 咱家果园.
