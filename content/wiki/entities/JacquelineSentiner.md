# Jacqueline Sentiner

Jacqueline Sentiner is the [[HourglassClimate]] speaker in [[tech-20260728-0728-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260728-0728-mp-tech-pod-128]] explaining why olivine matters for [[OceanAlkalinityEnhancement]]. She describes olivine as a natural silicate mineral and says that when it dissolves in seawater it can convert CO2 into bicarbonate.

Her role in the source is to supply the accessible chemistry behind [[OlivineCarbonRemoval]]. The episode then pairs that explanation with [[AdamSubhas]]'s caution that the field still has unresolved scale, speed, tradeoff, and marine-ecosystem questions.
