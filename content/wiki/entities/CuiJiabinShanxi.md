# 崔嘉斌 / Cui Jiabin (Shanxi psychiatrist)

## Overview
崔嘉斌 / Cui Jiabin is identified in [[vol-189-lunuzheng-zhenxiang-ni-renzhule-shenghuo-de-qingxu-que-zai-kaiche-shi-shikong-lrhaufg_zvbv4wn86-vjh3q3n4ws]] as a psychiatry attending physician affiliated with Shanxi Children's Hospital and Shanxi Maternal and Child Health Hospital.

## Current Profile
In the episode, Cui Jiabin helps distinguish normal anger from [[RoadRageAndDrivingEmotion|road-rage behavior]] that becomes persistent, functionally impairing, or dangerous. The profile is practical and source-scoped: anger can protect a sense of rights or fairness, but the decisive safety boundary is whether arousal overrides judgment and becomes action.

Cui also links driving arousal to bodily regulation, everyday wellbeing, traffic design, and the emotional environment outside the car. The episode's neuroscience explanations are useful public metaphors but are not presented with study citations or enough detail for clinical inference.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses functional impairment and driving danger rather than a standalone diagnostic label to bound road rage.
- Separates the presence of angry fantasy from acting on an unsafe impulse.
- Explains slow breathing as an accessible route into bodily down-regulation.
- Places driving emotion inside work, relationships, exercise, outdoor activity, and healthy emotional outlets.
- Treats road design, signs, lights, glare, rules, and enforcement as part of prevention.

## Evidence
- Diagnostic boundary - [[vol-189-lunuzheng-zhenxiang-ni-renzhule-shenghuo-de-qingxu-que-zai-kaiche-shi-shikong-lrhaufg_zvbv4wn86-vjh3q3n4ws]] has Cui state that road rage is not a DSM-5 or ICD-10/11 entry and distinguish ordinary anger from sustained, impairing, or unsafe loss of control.
- Impulse and judgment - [[vol-189-lunuzheng-zhenxiang-ni-renzhule-shenghuo-de-qingxu-que-zai-kaiche-shi-shikong-lrhaufg_zvbv4wn86-vjh3q3n4ws]] uses the difference between imagining and acting out to mark the shift into danger.
- Body and environment - [[vol-189-lunuzheng-zhenxiang-ni-renzhule-shenghuo-de-qingxu-que-zai-kaiche-shi-shikong-lrhaufg_zvbv4wn86-vjh3q3n4ws]] connects breathing, daily recovery, online hostility, road color, width, lighting, and glare to driving emotion.

## Qualifications
The wiki has no independent biographical evidence beyond this episode note. The source's name is 崔嘉斌, which is kept distinct from [[CuiJiabin|崔嘉宾]], an existing same-pinyin psychiatric guest. The neuroscience and regulation discussion is public education, not individualized psychiatric, neurological, or driving-safety advice.
