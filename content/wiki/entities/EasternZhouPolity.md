# 东周国 / Eastern Zhou Polity

东周国 / Eastern Zhou polity appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-16-sunbin-pangjuan-enyuan-jiuge-guiling-zhi-zhan-lryhzkc1-xsvfjhvicu8yxa9d2jr]] as the target of a [[HanState|韩国]] attack in 353 BCE. The episode distinguishes this small polity from the broader Eastern Zhou historical period by explaining that the weakened [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]] realm had split into eastern and western Zhou governments.

The page is source-scoped. Its role is to keep the episode's "韩攻东周" notice from being flattened into dynasty-period language. It belongs with [[WesternZhouPolity|西周国]] and [[EasternWesternZhouSplit|东周西周分裂]] as evidence of the royal house's territorial and political contraction.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-76-shui-geile-chuqingxiangwang-de-zixin-lsut4cfic7xbbrmbmimrruf7n6y0]] returns to the naming issue while explaining why [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]] can send [[DongzhouWugong|东周武公]] and yet be associated with [[WesternZhouPolity|西周国]]. The episode stresses that this late use of East/West Zhou is a split-polity question inside the old royal domain, not the same thing as the broad Eastern Zhou era.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-102-1-lvbuwei-shi-jiangziya-houren-lveg-wsmggroaxjsnxopaevexqod]] gives Eastern Zhou its terminal source branch. The episode says the Eastern Zhou ruler secretly plots with other lords against [[QinState|秦国]], after which [[QinZhuangxiangwang|秦庄襄王]] sends [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]] to destroy the polity and move its ruler to Yangren. The source treats this as the point where the [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]]'s royal sacrifices finally end.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-102-2-kongzi-zai-luguo-de-shenshi-zhimi-lphy12exg4u7f-30qkir96gv634r]] references the same terminal field but with a chronology tension. It places Qin's destruction of Eastern Zhou and the movement of the [[Jiuding|九鼎]] to Qin in a Qin Zhaoxiang Wang fifty-second-year frame while explaining why [[LuState|鲁国]] is destroyed by [[ChuState|楚国]]. The wiki keeps this as a source-scoped mismatch with the prior Qinji 102-1 Zhuangxiang-year framing.
