# Dustin Allen

## Overview
Dustin Allen is presented in the bounded source as the founder of [[Trinitite]], a privacy and AI-governance company. His account connects probabilistic agent behavior to test-driven controls, private deployment, selective human escalation, and user-defined safety boundaries.

## Current Profile
Allen argues that autonomous-agent safety cannot be reduced to static command rules because agents may vary across runs, compose errors across steps, and find alternate technical paths toward a goal. His proposed response is [[TestDrivenAgentGovernance]]: specify expected outcomes, test edge cases under more repeatable inference, validate outputs, and distill narrow corrections into adapters or smaller models. He also frames safety as enabling [[PersonalizedAgentSafety]], with users retaining control of data and deciding which actions fit their risk tolerance.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats probabilistic execution and workflow composition as central agent-governance problems.
- Advocates expected-outcome testing and specialized enforcement at the model and workflow layers.
- Concentrates human review on consequential exceptions rather than routine decisions.
- Favors on-device or private-cloud processing where sensitive data need not be collected centrally.
- Resists blanket claims that either open or closed models are inherently safer.
