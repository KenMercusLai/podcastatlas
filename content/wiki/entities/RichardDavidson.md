# Richard Davidson

## Overview
Richard Davidson is a neuroscientist represented in the wiki through a [[HubermanLab]] interview on meditation, neuroplasticity, attention, emotion regulation, and human flourishing.

## Current Profile
Davidson presents meditation as mental training rather than mind-clearing or guaranteed calm. His account separates focused attention from open monitoring, treats distraction and discomfort as observable material, and uses [[MeditationStateTraitTraining]] to explain how repeated short practices might change baseline tendencies over time.

His practical emphasis is low-friction consistency: choose the smallest daily dose that can actually be sustained, attach it to ordinary cues, and cultivate [[MetaAwarenessResponseSpace]] in formal sitting or everyday activity. He organizes broader wellbeing around [[FourPillarsFlourishing]]—awareness, connection, insight, and purpose—while expressing more caution about psychedelics for general flourishing than for selected clinical research contexts.

## Key Characteristics
- Neuroscientist studying meditation, brain activity, plasticity, emotion, and wellbeing.
- Distinguishes temporary mental states from more durable traits that repeated training may shape.
- Treats meta-awareness as noticing what the mind is doing before automatic reaction.
- Prioritizes sustainable consistency over an intense but fragile practice dose.
- Frames flourishing through awareness, connection, insight, and purpose.
- Combines optimism about trainability with caution around psychedelic integration and preliminary neuromodulation findings.
