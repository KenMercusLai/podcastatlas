# 于红 / Yu Hong

## Overview
于红 / Yu Hong is a former investor and the founder of [[TuDongdong|兔咚咚]], introduced in this source through her move from roughly fifteen years in investing toward children's [[SocialEmotionalLearning|social-emotional learning]].

## Current Profile
The bounded source presents Yu Hong as a practitioner linking education, investment judgment, parenting, and personal choice. She describes education as a long-held mission, with investing at 华兴、GGV纪源资本 and 美团龙珠 serving as an intermediate path for capability and influence before she shifted toward education entrepreneurship in 2023–2024.

Her framework is not that AI makes conventional subjects useless. It is that lower information and execution costs make time allocation more important, so children and adults need stronger self-knowledge, emotional literacy, openness, relationship skill, and reflective judgment. Her own career changes and investment mistakes are used as cases of learning by action and honest review.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats education as a long-horizon mission rather than a sudden AI-market pivot.
- Connects children's SEL practice with adult work, investing, relationships, and decision-making.
- Uses emotional experience as evidence for self-understanding without treating emotion as an automatic command.
- Frames choice as experimentation, future direction, value ranking, and review rather than option maximization.
- Values openness and evidence updating alongside intelligence, including the language of testable hypotheses.
