## Overview

王匡 / Wang Kuang (Xin general) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-660-2-yi-ji-kan-dong-chimei-jun-de-dansheng-lgqk1gkvh-0cwder6g5-wryi6mce|Hanji 660-2]] as the Taishi sent by [[WangMang|王莽]] with [[LianDanXinGeneral|廉丹]] against [[ChimeiRebellion|赤眉]] and other eastern rebels. This route is deliberately disambiguated from [[WangKuangXinPrince|Wang Kuang / 王匡 (Xin prince)]] and [[WangKuangLateHan|王匡 / Wang Kuang (late Han)]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 660-2 makes Wang Kuang a negative official-command case. He is sent with major forces, but the campaign loses civilian legitimacy because the army is not restrained. The popular saying preserved in the source distinguishes him from Lian Dan: people would rather meet Chimei than the Taishi, and even the Taishi is preferable to the Gengshi general who kills them. The exact wording is source-scoped, but the political effect is clear: official anti-rebel violence helps the rebels by making state troops look worse than disorder.

## Key Characteristics

- Xin Taishi and anti-rebel commander in the 22 CE eastern field.
- Paired with Lian Dan in a large official deployment against Chimei.
- Associated with failure to restrain troops and resulting civilian fear.
- Disambiguated from Wang Mang's private son Wang Kuang and the later late-Han Wang Kuang.
