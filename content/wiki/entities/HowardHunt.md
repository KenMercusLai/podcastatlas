# E. Howard Hunt

## Overview

E. Howard Hunt appears as a former [[CIA]] officer and [[BayOfPigsInvasion|Bay of Pigs]] veteran who moved from the [[WhiteHousePlumbers]] into the campaign operations that produced the Watergate burglary.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Hunt to show continuity between Cold War covert-action experience, White House leak control, and domestic political dirty tricks. Working with [[GordonLiddy]], he developed [[OperationGemstone]] proposals and helped organize the team that entered Democratic National Committee offices. An address-book reference to Hunt then helped investigators trace the arrested burglars toward the White House.

## Key Characteristics

- Former CIA officer associated with the Bay of Pigs operation.
- Member of the White House plumbers' leak-control effort.
- Collaborator with Liddy on Gemstone and campaign dirty tricks.
- Linked to Cuban exile operatives used in the Watergate break-in.
- Became an evidentiary path from the burglars toward the administration.
