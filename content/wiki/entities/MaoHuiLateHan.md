# 毛晖 / Mao Hui (late Han)

毛晖 / Mao Hui (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1026-jiemi-sanguo-di-yi-mengjiang-lvbu-de-luomu-lhjf-agujuliglqchumtff3tftxg1|Hanji 1026]] as a former [[CaoCao|曹操]] subordinate who had betrayed him during the [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] crisis and later fled with [[XuXiLateHan|徐翕]] to [[ZhangBaLateHan|张霸]]. Cao Cao initially demands their heads after defeating [[LyuBu|吕布]] at [[Xiapi|下邳]].

The page's main value is the reversal after Zhang Ba refuses. Cao Cao spares and employs Mao Hui, making him part of the post-Xiapi signal that surrender can be more valuable than revenge when the goal is to settle local networks before the [[YuanShao|袁绍]] confrontation.
