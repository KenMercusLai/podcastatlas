# NBCUniversal

NBCUniversal enters the wiki through [[tech-20260731-0731-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260731-0731-mp-tech-pod-128]], where [[AnitaRamaswamy]] discusses a multi-year deal to put [[PeacockStreaming|Peacock]] content inside [[YouTubePremium|YouTube Premium]]. The source treats the deal as more than a discount bundle because Peacock programming can be watched inside the [[YouTube]] platform.

The source also names [[Comcast]] as NBCUniversal's parent and says Comcast had announced plans to spin off NBC a few weeks earlier. In that context, the YouTube deal is framed as potentially helping NBCUniversal or NBC succeed more independently by expanding distribution and making its content easier to reach through a dominant video platform.
