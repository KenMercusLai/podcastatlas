# When We Were Young Festival

When We Were Young Festival appears in [[working-memory-the-surprising-decline-of-dementia-6a6b0eeca585d3be78cd9d1b]] as evidence that emo's revival is broader than [[MyChemicalRomance]]. The episode says the Las Vegas festival launched in 2022 and gathered many major emo acts.

In the source, the festival helps explain [[EmoRevivalIdentityCulture]] as a live-market and belonging pattern. It packages a generational sound, old fan memory, and younger discovery into a concentrated event.
