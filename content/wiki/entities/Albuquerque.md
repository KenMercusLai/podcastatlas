# Albuquerque

[[kyiv-brawl-ukraines-top-general-fired-6a609aec4fac21e67f1be165]] uses Albuquerque as the main case in [[DanielKnowles]]'s report on U.S. police killings. The city is presented as one of the country's highest-rate police-killing places and as a test of whether formal reform can change police behavior.

The source centers [[JoseArmas]]'s May 26 death after his family called for help during a suicide threat and expected social workers rather than police. It also says Albuquerque police have shot and killed 116 people since 2013, placing the city inside [[PoliceKillingRateDivergence]], [[MentalHealthCrisisPoliceResponse]], and [[PoliceConsentDecreeCultureGap]].
