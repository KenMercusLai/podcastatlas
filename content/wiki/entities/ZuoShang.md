## Overview

左殇 is a central [[PhantomBladeZero|《影之刃零》]] figure used to explore strategic brilliance, grand purpose, and the danger of forcing the world into a total plan.

## Current Profile

The source presents Zuo Shang as the inverse of [[SoulPhantomBlade|魂]]. He begins from an ambition to save the world and uses broad planning to pursue it, yet that very scale and certainty may produce destruction. His ornate golden weapon language expresses confidence, status, and an almost absolute belief in his own judgment.

## Key Characteristics

- Acts from a large world-saving purpose.
- Prefers global planning and strategic control.
- Represents the pragmatic, ambitious, and externally successful side of the creator's internal duality.
- Risks turning moral purpose into delusion or destructive imposition.
- Uses luxurious gold design to express pride and certainty.
