## Overview

脂习 / Zhi Xi is Kong Rong's late-Han friend whose repeated warnings and public mourning make him a source-scoped figure of loyal counsel and funerary courage.

## Current Profile

The current evidence gives Zhi Xi a two-stage role around [[KongRong|孔融]]. Before the arrest, he repeatedly warns that Kong Rong's uncompromising speech will bring disaster. After Kong Rong and his household are executed and the bodies are left exposed, Zhi Xi approaches and mourns when others are afraid to do so. [[CaoCao|曹操]] spares him, which the episode interprets as evidence that conspicuous loyalty did not automatically become lethal when it lacked organized political force.

## Key Characteristics

- Old friend who recognizes the danger in Kong Rong's direct speech.
- Unsuccessful counsellor whose warning is remembered only after the predicted disaster.
- Public mourner who accepts visible risk around a politically condemned body.
- Symbolic dissenter spared because the episode sees grief as less threatening than political capacity.
