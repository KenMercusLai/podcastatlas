## Overview

复株累若鞮单于 / Fuzhulei Ruodi Chanyu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-504-nanren-haose-zenmeban-kexi-guren-zhezhao-ni-xuebuhui-lsevoh__1vxdtzqhlzq6_an3xdq6|Hanji 504]] as Diaotao Mogao, the son chosen by [[HuhanyeChanyuWesternHan|呼韩邪单于]] to succeed him immediately.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Diaotao Mogao as the stability candidate in a contested Xiongnu household. Although Huhanye loves Qiemo Che, Zhuanqu Yanzhi argues that Diaotao Mogao is better suited for immediate rule. After Huhanye's death, Diaotao Mogao becomes Fuzhulei Ruodi Chanyu, appoints brothers or half-brothers to major Xiongnu offices, and marries [[WangZhaojun|王昭君]] according to Xiongnu custom.

## Key Characteristics

- Xiongnu successor chosen for near-term political stability.
- Installed through a compromise that anticipates future transfer to Qiemo Che.
- Uses office appointments to place Qiemo Che, Nangzhiyasi, and Qiemixu within the new order.
- Marries Wang Zhaojun after Huhanye's death according to Xiongnu custom.
