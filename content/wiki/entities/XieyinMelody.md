## Current Profile
The source presents Melody as a demanding long-form creator whose work is built around research, extended writing, and strong format judgment. Her account of [[ZongHengSiHai|纵横四海]] emphasizes preparation intensity: one script can take more than fifty hours before reading, recording, and editing are counted. She treats long podcasts as valuable because they can accompany walking, running, travel, and other continuous listening contexts without forcing constant selection.

Melody also supplies the episode's professional-media comparison. She values reported podcasts that combine long coverage of a company, interviews, documentation, evidence, narrative structure, and a clear point of view. At the same time, she is candid about creator fatigue, multi-account confusion, and the difficulty of carrying long-content judgment into short-video platforms where title and topic selection operate differently.

## Key Characteristics
- She works in high-preparation long-form podcasting, with extensive scripting and research behind individual episodes.
- She treats long audio as a practical listening form for continuous activities and a durable archive rather than a short traffic object.
- She evaluates professional podcasts through reporting depth, evidence, narrative structure, and explicit judgment.
- She sees media workers as well suited to podcast transition because they already understand interviews, sources, editing, rhythm, and production standards.
- She experiences multi-show and multi-account fatigue when long-form creator logic meets platform distribution pressure.
