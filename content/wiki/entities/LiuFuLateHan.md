# 刘馥 / Liu Fu (late Han)

## Overview
Liu Fu is presented in Hanji 1051 as the Cao-side Yangzhou inspector who turns a nearly empty [[HefeiLateHan|Hefei]] into an administrative, settlement, and defensive base. The source spells his name as 劉富/刘富, but the Yangzhou-inspector/Hefei-building profile is normalized here as 刘馥 / Liu Fu.

## Current Profile
The current evidence makes Liu Fu a weak-position institution builder rather than a battlefield commander. [[CaoCao|Cao Cao]] recommends him when [[YangzhouLateHan|Yangzhou]] is fragmented, Lujiang is unstable, and the only usable foothold is around [[JiujiangCommandery|Jiujiang]]. Liu Fu responds by anchoring his office at Hefei, using formal legitimacy to reassure local armed groups, asking the court to regularize some leaders with official posts, repairing livelihoods, building schools, and preparing defenses.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses formal appointment as a practical tool for creating order where direct control is initially thin.
- Avoids early coercive confrontation and instead converts hostile or unattached local forces into semi-official dependents.
- Links submission to material benefit through water management, agriculture, crafts, and population return.
- Treats education and administrative presence as part of frontier consolidation, not as separate civil adornments.
- Builds defensive walls and stores after political absorption, making the pacification durable rather than merely conciliatory.

## Evidence
**Formal legitimacy under weak control**
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1051-ta-weihe-bei-chengzuo-shi-zui-you-nengli-de-yangzhou-cishi-ltquwyzzq-sqibj33bpbu3qyvz2h|Hanji 1051]] describes Cao Cao recommending Liu Fu to the Yangzhou inspector post despite the province being mostly outside Cao's direct control.

**Conversion of hostile forces**
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1051-ta-weihe-bei-chengzuo-shi-zui-you-nengli-de-yangzhou-cishi-ltquwyzzq-sqibj33bpbu3qyvz2h|Hanji 1051]] emphasizes reassurance, partial autonomy, office grants, tribute, and nominal obedience as Liu Fu's way to turn local armed groups into manageable insiders.

**Livelihood and defense**
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1051-ta-weihe-bei-chengzuo-shi-zui-you-nengli-de-yangzhou-cishi-ltquwyzzq-sqibj33bpbu3qyvz2h|Hanji 1051]] credits Liu Fu with water works, agriculture, crafts, schools, walls, and stored defensive materials that make Hefei a Jiang-Huai base.

## Qualifications
The page currently rests on one episode and should not be treated as a complete biography. The transcript's 劉富/刘富 spelling is retained as a source-specific note, while the canonical page follows the Liu Fu/刘馥 identification because the office and Hefei-building branch match the received historical profile.

## What Changed
- Created Liu Fu as a distinct late-Han entity tied to Yangzhou, Hefei, and Cao-side Jiang-Huai consolidation.
- Normalized the source's 劉富/刘富 spelling to Liu Fu/刘馥 while preserving the evidence caveat.
