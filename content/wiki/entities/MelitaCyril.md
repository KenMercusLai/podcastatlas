# Melita Cyril

## Overview
Melita Cyril is the founder of natural-fiber apparel company [[QForQuinn]] and the first caller in the Advice Line episode.

## Current Profile
Cyril is presented as a profitable, bootstrapped founder deciding whether strategic capital would help Q for Quinn move faster without weakening its natural-material mission. The company supports her family, her husband has joined full-time, and she sees athleisure as the next category after early sports-bra traction.

## Key Characteristics
- Founded Q for Quinn after seeking organic-cotton socks for a son with eczema.
- Built a profitable, family-supporting company without outside capital.
- Weighs faster market capture against control and mission integrity.
- Pursues natural-fiber athleisure as a logical adjacency.
