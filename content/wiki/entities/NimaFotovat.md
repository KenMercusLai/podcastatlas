# Nima Fotovat

Nima Fotovat is the [[MadeGood]] and [[RiversideNaturalFoods]] founder/operator featured in [[madegood-salma-and-nima-fotovat-lost-their-first-business-they-grew-their-next-one-into-a-snack-giant-cf5715bf-e9e0-45e0-a8a9-1256a7a4ed0d]]. The episode follows him from childhood in [[Iran]], immigration to [[Canada]], and broad operating work in the family food business to identifying the school-safe snack opportunity behind MadeGood.

## Key Points
- Nima joined the family business after leaving university and learned across reception, purchasing, payroll, maintenance, sales, and customer development.
- In 2013 he noticed that school allergy rules created a snack problem for parents, schools, and children, which became the MadeGood opportunity.
- He pushed for owned manufacturing because the family understood production and wanted credible allergen-free control.
- His early growth playbook emphasized a concentrated local market, store support, demos, and hands-on merchandising.
- He often agreed to difficult retailer requests before the operating details were fully solved, making him central to [[RetailerResponsiveManufacturing]].
- The episode presents him as aware of focus risk after [[GoodToGo]] and more data-driven as MadeGood matured.
