# KimWolf Botnet

KimWolf is the botnet named by [[BrianKrebs]] in [[tech-20260415-0415-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260415-0415-mp-tech-pod-128]]. The episode says it compromised more than three million devices and highlights TV streaming boxes as a major affected category.

The source uses KimWolf to show why [[IoTBotnetRisk]] is not limited to visibly broken computers. A device sold for cheap pirated entertainment can become part of [[MaliciousProxyNetworks]], call back to [[CommandAndControlInfrastructure]], or contribute bandwidth to [[DDoSAttackAmplification]] while the buyer experiences the box as a working media device.

## Key Claims
- The episode describes KimWolf as compromising more than three million devices.
- The affected devices are framed mainly around TV boxes rather than only routers.
- The risky devices often attract buyers by promising free paid-streaming access after a one-time fee.
- The botnet case links consumer piracy, malware installation, proxy relaying, and denial-of-service capacity.
