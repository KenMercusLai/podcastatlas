# 赵豹 / 平阳君 / Zhao Bao

赵豹 / 平阳君 / Zhao Bao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-93-zai-mengli-cangzhe-toutian-gaiming-1-ljshdryvf9qgabtn-siontthzsok]] as one of [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]]'s senior royal relatives consulted over [[FengTing|冯亭]]'s offer of [[Shangdang|上党]] to [[ZhaoState|赵国]].

His advice is to refuse the offer. Zhao Bao's core argument is "圣人甚祸无故之利": a wise person treats a large, unearned benefit as a source of disaster, because the benefit may carry another actor's hidden cost. In this case, accepting Shangdang means inheriting [[QinState|秦国]]'s attack.

The episode uses Zhao Bao as the voice of strategic caution against [[WindfallDecisionTrap|无故之利 / 利令智昏]]. His warning is not that territory has no value; it is that value detached from cause, cost, and retaliation must be priced as risk before it is accepted.
