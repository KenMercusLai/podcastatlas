# Blaise Agüera y Arcas

## Overview
Blaise Agüera y Arcas is presented as the founder of Google's Paradigms of Intelligence program and the evolutionary-biology voice in the Long Now conversation on AI and interdependence.

## Current Profile
In the bounded source, Agüera y Arcas uses [[Symbiogenesis]], collective intelligence, and nested levels of competition and cooperation to reject simple boundaries between organism and network, human and nature, or biology and technology. He treats minds as relational systems and argues that technological loss must be weighed against changes in individual and collective capability, while keeping harmful cooperation and political turbulence visible.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats merger, partnership, and division of labor as major drivers of evolutionary complexity.
- Uses the [[CartesianWound]] to criticize false separations that hide mutual dependence.
- Understands intelligence as collective and embedded in accumulated social and technical infrastructure.
- Rejects single-objective optimizer models as inadequate descriptions of living and social systems.
- Evaluates cooperation across nested levels rather than assuming it is always beneficial.
