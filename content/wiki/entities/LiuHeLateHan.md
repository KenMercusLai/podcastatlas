# 刘和 / Liu He (Late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-954-renxingbenshan-haishi-renxingbene-lkurz9dbb25jrjyl5c-rrahda39h|Hanji 954]] adds Liu He's earlier hostage-like position before his later use as an anti-[[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] banner. The source says Liu He is serving as shizhong in Chang'an after following [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] west, but the host reads that service as practical control by [[DongZhuo|董卓]].

This matters for [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]]'s refusal of the imperial title. If Liu Yu accepted the title offered by [[YuanShao|袁绍]] and [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]], Liu He and the family could become exposed to Dong Zhuo's retaliation. Hanji 954 therefore makes Liu He part of Liu Yu's title-risk calculus before Hanji 998 makes him a posthumous legitimacy banner.

刘和 / Liu He (Late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-998-baima-jiangjun-gongsunzan-cong-sanqian-baima-dao-kunshou-gucheng-lp-1l2gvbsbfnyznutdghxwwvqna|Hanji 998]] as the son of [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]]. After Liu Yu's death, [[Wuhuan|乌桓]], [[Xianbei|鲜卑]], [[XianyuFu|鲜于辅]], and [[YuanShao|袁绍]]-aligned forces welcome Liu He as a banner for their campaign against [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]].

The source does not develop Liu He's independent actions. His function is political: Liu Yu's surviving line gives the anti-Gongsun coalition a name around which revenge, local legitimacy, and external military support can gather.
