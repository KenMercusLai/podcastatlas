# 刘和 / Liu He (Late Han)

刘和 / Liu He (Late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-998-baima-jiangjun-gongsunzan-cong-sanqian-baima-dao-kunshou-gucheng-lp-1l2gvbsbfnyznutdghxwwvqna|Hanji 998]] as the son of [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]]. After Liu Yu's death, [[Wuhuan|乌桓]], [[Xianbei|鲜卑]], [[XianyuFu|鲜于辅]], and [[YuanShao|袁绍]]-aligned forces welcome Liu He as a banner for their campaign against [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]].

The source does not develop Liu He's independent actions. His function is political: Liu Yu's surviving line gives the anti-Gongsun coalition a name around which revenge, local legitimacy, and external military support can gather.
