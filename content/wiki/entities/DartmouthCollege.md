# Dartmouth College / 达特茅斯学院

## Overview
Dartmouth College is the New England university setting for the 1956 Dartmouth AI workshop discussed in the source.

## Current Profile
In this wiki branch, Dartmouth matters less as a full institutional profile and more as a spatial condition in the episode's theory of innovation. Yang Bin argues that its distance from stronger AI, cybernetics, and computing power centers helped reduce authority pressure and gave the workshop room to define artificial intelligence outside existing mainstream channels.

The source therefore uses Dartmouth as a campus-place example: sometimes the productive setting for a new field is not the highest-status center, but a quieter edge where participants can gather, wander, and improvise.

## Key Characteristics
- New England college setting for the 1956 AI workshop.
- Described as outside the strongest focal institutions such as MIT, Harvard, or Stanford.
- Provides the source's geographic basis for a peripheral innovation field.
- Connected to McCarthy's assistant-professor role in mathematics and astronomy.

- Workshop setting: [[401-bianyu-manju-manzou-women-haineng-yinglai-lingwai-yige-datemao-si-de-xiatian-ma-f7495e05]] places the 1956 workshop at Dartmouth College in New England.
- Peripheral role: [[401-bianyu-manju-manzou-women-haineng-yinglai-lingwai-yige-datemao-si-de-xiatian-ma-f7495e05]] explicitly contrasts Dartmouth with stronger focal institutions and treats that edge position as useful.
- McCarthy connection: [[401-bianyu-manju-manzou-women-haineng-yinglai-lingwai-yige-datemao-si-de-xiatian-ma-f7495e05]] identifies John McCarthy as a young assistant professor at Dartmouth when the proposal was written.
