# Gary Burrell

Gary Burrell is one of the two [[Garmin]] founders named in [[kafeidou-liangci-zaoyu-pingguo-chongji-yundong-shoubiao-jiaming-weihe-hai-neng-zengzhang-1006272684]]. The episode emphasizes his navigation background and says Garmin's name comes from combining the first-name fragments of Gary Burrell and [[MinKao|Min Kao / 高明环]].

Within this wiki, Burrell's role is mainly to anchor Garmin's origin in professional [[GlobalPositioningSystem|GPS]] equipment rather than consumer lifestyle electronics. That origin matters because Garmin later survived phone and watch platform pressure by returning to specialized navigation, sport, and outdoor use cases.
