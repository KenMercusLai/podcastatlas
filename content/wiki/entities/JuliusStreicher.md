# Julius Streicher

## Overview

Julius Streicher was the Nazi publisher of the violently antisemitic newspaper *Der Stürmer* whom [[UnityMitford|Unity Mitford]] cultivated as a route into Nazi political networks and public notoriety.

## Current Profile

The episode describes Streicher as monstrous even by Nazi standards and uses Unity's relationship with him to reject an innocent-socialite account of her conduct. She did not merely meet senior Nazis: she deliberately associated with an extreme antisemitic propagandist, wrote to *Der Stürmer* using explicit antisemitic language, and asked that her full name be printed.

That publicity served both ideology and access. It strengthened Unity's credibility with [[AdolfHitler]], made her more newsworthy, and supported her role as a broker between Nazi figures and British fascists.

## Key Characteristics

- Senior Nazi propagandist identified with extreme public antisemitism.
- Publisher of *Der Stürmer*.
- Social and political contact cultivated by Unity Mitford.
- Gatekeeper whose platform helped convert a British aristocrat's provocation into explicit public commitment.
- Node connecting propaganda, celebrity, and transnational fascist networking.
