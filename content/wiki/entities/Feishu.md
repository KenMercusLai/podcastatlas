# Feishu / 飞书

Feishu / 飞书 is referenced in [[ai-chongji-qiye-ruanjian-jutou-yu-sap-yuanxin-liao-damoxing-to-b-de-dianfu-yu-bianjie-1-174-1]] as an office-collaboration surface alongside [[DingTalk]]. The episode contrasts these tools with [[EnterpriseResourcePlanning|ERP]], HCM, finance, procurement, and manufacturing systems: collaboration products carry visible work communication, while backend enterprise systems carry operational state, permissions, and audit-critical processes.

[[270-da-chang-yazhu-ai-bangong-feishu-he-dingding-que-xian-chengle-peijue-lmb4dgcgov3mr4cn7cikbghpfro4]] adds Feishu as both disputed AI-office protagonist and enterprise-data substrate. [[EricFeishu|Eric]] says Feishu and Volcano Engine can face the same customers and budgets, while [[DoubaoEnterpriseEdition|Doubao enterprise edition]] often makes Feishu the carrier for AI bought by enterprises. The source treats Feishu's documents, meetings, org charts, permissions, approvals, and chat records as possible [[EnterpriseOperationalMemory]] for [[AIOfficeAgent|AI office agents]], while also noting that mature customer expectations made the 2023 AI redesign and intelligent-partner push more conservative.
