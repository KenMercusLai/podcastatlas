# Feishu / 飞书

## Overview

Feishu / 飞书 is ByteDance's office-collaboration suite in the wiki's enterprise-software and AI-office branch. The bounded sources use it both as a collaboration surface distinct from backend systems and as a workplace context substrate for [[AIOfficeAgent|AI office agents]] such as [[DoubaoWork|Doubao Work]].

## Current Profile

Feishu's strategic importance comes from the work context it already captures: documents, meetings, org charts, permissions, approvals, chat records, enterprise Q&A, members, and web documents. Episode 270 frames that context as [[EnterpriseOperationalMemory]] for future AI-office agents, while the enterprise-software source contrasts collaboration products with backend ERP, HCM, finance, procurement, and manufacturing systems.

Episode 272 adds that Feishu is now part of a tighter Doubao Work story. [[DoubaoWorkPartner]] is described as the renamed Feishu AI partner, and Doubao Work's strongest source-scoped advantage is smoother first-party access to Feishu context. The same evidence keeps Feishu under product-transition pressure: mature enterprise customers, old collaboration workflows, model-choice differences, token-cost packaging, and third-party connector friction complicate a clean AI-native redesign.

## Key Characteristics

- Office-collaboration surface carrying documents, meetings, chats, approvals, permissions, org context, and knowledge records.
- ByteDance enterprise-work substrate for [[DoubaoWork]] and [[DoubaoWorkPartner]].
- Distinct from backend systems such as [[EnterpriseResourcePlanning|ERP]], even when AI may blur collaboration and execution boundaries.
- Creates a first-party context advantage for Doubao Work relative to third-party connectors.
- Carries mature-product constraints because enterprise users expect stability and existing workflows to keep working.

- **Commercial overlap:** [[270-da-chang-yazhu-ai-bangong-feishu-he-dingding-que-xian-chengle-peijue-lmb4dgcgov3mr4cn7cikbghpfro4]] has Eric explain overlap between Feishu and Volcano Engine customer budgets; [[272-cong-feishu-jizuo-dao-agent-youxian-doubao-gongzuo-all-in-one-jinzhui-workbuddy-lqmfcnfkkhoxt440qy26vwpnvswp]] extends that overlap into product packaging and model-cost questions.

## Qualifications

- Feishu's exact relationship to Doubao Work, Doubao Work Partner, and Doubao enterprise packaging is source-scoped rather than fully normalized.
- Collaboration context is valuable only when customer work is actually digitized and permissioned enough for agents to use.
- Product tests and model-choice observations in episode 272 may change with future releases.
