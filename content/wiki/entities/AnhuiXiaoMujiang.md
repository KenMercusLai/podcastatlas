# 安徽小木匠 / Anhui Xiao Mujiang

## Overview
《安徽小木匠》 is [[XiaoGuoGege|小果哥哥]]'s breakout AI comic drama, adapted from [[FanqieNovel|番茄小说]] IP and used across two sources as a case of low cash production cost, intensive creator labor, paid amplification, and nonlinear platform returns.

## Current Profile
The first source reports production cost below RMB 3,000, 350 million plays on one platform, and roughly RMB 500,000 paid to the creator by recording time. Its reach depended on more than generation: IP authorization, [[Doubao|豆包]] adaptation, repeated video generation, salvage and editing, submission, distributor amplification, review, and delayed settlement shaped the result.

The follow-up says 《安徽小木匠》 remained the creator's highest-play and highest-income work after ten completed dramas and more than 1.6 billion aggregate plays. That persistence strengthens its role as an outlier rather than a baseline return. The rural craft setting and regional familiarity may have helped audience resonance, but neither source establishes a single causal explanation.

## Key Characteristics
- Very low reported cash production cost paired with substantial manual labor.
- Fanqie IP adaptation distributed through the Hongguo/Douyin ecosystem.
- Breakout whose apparent virality included distributor and paid-traffic amplification.
- Rural carpenter story with craft, countryside, and regional-memory appeal.
- Creator's strongest work even after a larger six-month portfolio.

## Evidence
- Production, reach, and settlement: [[267-3000-kuai-chengben-3-5-yi-ci-bofang-ai-duanju-zenme-zai-douyin-zhengqian-lrvmi-ruxz81afnqxdfk-dldixhx]] reports sub-RMB-3,000 cost, 350 million plays, roughly RMB 500,000 creator payout, and delayed revenue sharing.
- Workflow and distribution: [[267-3000-kuai-chengben-3-5-yi-ci-bofang-ai-duanju-zenme-zai-douyin-zhengqian-lrvmi-ruxz81afnqxdfk-dldixhx]] traces rights, scripts, prompts, generation, editing, review, distributor amplification, and the later closure of that specific adaptation channel.
- Portfolio persistence: [[lhhrwpwmn7e-unjt7wpxdixllxgs-lhhrwpwmn7e-unjt7wpxdixllxgs]] says the work still led the creator's ten-drama portfolio in plays and income six months later.

## Qualifications
Cost, playback, payout, audience-resonance, and portfolio-ranking claims are creator-reported and not independently audited. The project used a rights route later described as closed, and its distribution, timing, platform review, and subject fit limit replication.
