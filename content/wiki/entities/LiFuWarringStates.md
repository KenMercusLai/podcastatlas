# 栗腹 / Li Fu (Warring States)

栗腹 / Li Fu is the [[YanState|燕国]] envoy and policy advocate in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-100-2-liuguo-jingran-tongshi-wei-ta-diaosang-ln6-5v-b-ybqmf5ucepusdo3lf68]]. [[YanWangXi|燕王喜]] sends him to [[ZhaoState|赵国]] under the pretext of making an alliance, and he gives [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] five hundred gold for wine expenses.

On return, Li Fu reports that Zhao's adult males died in [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] and that the children left behind are not yet grown, so Yan should attack. The episode treats his advice as the central diagnostic mistake: he converts visible demographic trauma into a conclusion that Zhao's military capacity has disappeared.

In the [[HaodaiBattle|鄗代之战]], Li Fu commands the Yan attack toward Hao and is defeated by [[LianPo|廉颇]]. His page therefore anchors [[PostDisasterPredationMisread|post-disaster predation misread]]: the effort to exploit a neighbor's wound can fail if the attacker ignores surviving institutions, commanders, and fighting culture.
