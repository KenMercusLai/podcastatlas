# Pininfarina

Pininfarina is the Italian automotive design partner that [[ferrari-1]] treats as central to [[Ferrari]]'s road-car beauty and luxury meaning. The hosts compare the relationship to the [[Apple]] design pairing of [[SteveJobs]] and [[JonyIve]].

For the wiki, Pininfarina marks how Ferrari converted racing machinery into objects of desire. It is part of the bridge from engineering credibility to [[ProductLedWillingnessToPay]], where buyers pay for form, heritage, and identity as well as performance.
