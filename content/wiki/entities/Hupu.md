# 虎扑 / Hupu

Hupu / 虎扑 is the sports-forum community case in [[no-212-cengjing-de-jingshen-jiaoluo-douban-zhihu-tieba-hupu-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-25-1003510980]]. The episode traces it back to [[ChengHang|程杭]]'s HoopChina, which began from NBA news translation and basketball-fan demand before becoming a broader sports portal and forum.

The source presents Hupu's key cultural asset as the Pedestrian Street and JR identity. Rather than accepting only the label of "straight male culture," Cheng Hang describes it as "barbecue-stall culture": users gather around games when games exist, and around jokes, ratings, gendered debates, the Goddess Competition, "green literature", and everyday arguments when they do not.

Commercially, Hupu illustrates [[OnlineCommunityCommercializationFriction]]. Sports marketing, basketball parks, games, esports, lotteries, live broadcasts, streetball, and IPO attempts all appeared, but durable high-margin growth was difficult. The strongest derivative path in the source came from equipment-community demand: [[YangBing|杨冰]] developed ShiHuo and Du App, which later became [[Dewu|得物]] and separated from Hupu.
