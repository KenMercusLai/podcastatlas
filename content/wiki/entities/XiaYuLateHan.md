# 夏育 / Xia Yu (late Han)

## Overview

Xia Yu is a late-Han frontier officer whose current wiki evidence pairs a 169 CE role in Duan Jiong's anti-Qiang campaign with a later failed 177 CE expedition against Xianbei.

## Current Profile

In Hanji 891, Xia Yu appears as one of [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]]'s field officers, paired with [[TianYanLateHan|田晏]] in a cavalry surprise and later a silent night ascent to the western mountain. In Hanji 909, he becomes the offensive advocate whose memorial frames repeated [[Xianbei|鲜卑]] raids as a reason for a deep punitive campaign. That later campaign fails badly after [[TanshihuaiXianbei|檀石槐]] concentrates Xianbei forces.

The current profile therefore separates tactical usefulness from strategic judgment. Xia Yu can function inside Duan's bounded Qiang operation, but his later confidence about Xianbei becomes part of an [[OverextendedFrontierExpedition|overextended frontier expedition]] once state capacity, enemy strength, and internal conditions are ignored.

## Key Characteristics

- Cavalry officer under Duan Jiong in the 169 CE Qiang campaign.
- Participant in the silent-movement western force before the Shehu Valley attack.
- Later hu Wuhuan xiaoyu / protector of Wuhuan who argues for a deep attack on Xianbei.
- Example of how a real frontier threat can still produce an over-scaled military response.

## Evidence

- Qiang-campaign role: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-891-gao-duanwei-de-muqin-shi-jia-de-dinghai-shenzhen-lmqkzsnhtiwc9wzcee869kuu57m5|Hanji 891]] says Duan Jiong sends Xia Yu with Tian Yan and five thousand elite riders to surprise the Qiang camp.
- Silent movement: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-891-gao-duanwei-de-muqin-shi-jia-de-dinghai-shenzhen-lmqkzsnhtiwc9wzcee869kuu57m5|Hanji 891]] later has Xia Yu and Tian Yan each lead seven thousand picked troops with "衔枚" silence toward the western mountain.
- Offensive memorial: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-909-2025-ruhe-yangcheng-yi-shen-guiqi-lomndmphrybunjbbbbn9ebafcsj|Hanji 909]] presents Xia Yu as the Wuhuan-protector figure who asks for a deep cross-frontier campaign after repeated Xianbei raids.
- Defeat and punishment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-909-2025-ruhe-yangcheng-yi-shen-guiqi-lomndmphrybunjbbbbn9ebafcsj|Hanji 909]] says Xia Yu leads one of the three Han columns, is crushed by Tanshihuai, returns with only a few dozen riders, is imprisoned, and redeems punishment through money and connections.

## Qualifications

This page treats the 169 夏育 and the 177 夏育 as the same canonical late-Han route because the current wiki evidence offers no reason to split them. Hanji 909's failure judgment should not be projected backward onto every earlier tactical role.
