# Habibay (Startup Embassy Resident)

## Overview
Habibay is the Istanbul-born scientist and Startup Embassy resident highlighted in [[KenjiYamamoto]]'s hacker-house account.

## Current Profile
Habibay is represented as an inventor-founder example where personal tragedy, technical work, and business learning converge. In the episode, her cellphone-sized fire extinguisher responds to a fatal dormitory fire in Istanbul, while her repeated prototyping shows the exacting side of startup and invention culture.

## Key Characteristics
- Young scientist from Istanbul living inside the hacker-house/startup-dreamer environment.
- Invented a small fire extinguisher after a dormitory fire killed 16 young women.
- Used by Yamamoto as an example of becoming both a scientist and a businesswoman.
- Represents perfectionist iteration through the reported 122 versions of her invention.
