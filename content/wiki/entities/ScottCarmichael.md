# Scott Carmichael

## Overview

Scott Carmichael is the [[DefenseIntelligenceAgency]] counterintelligence officer whose initial inquiry into [[AnaBelenMontes]] and later records search anchor [[the-queen-of-cuba-the-queen-of-cuba|The Queen of Cuba]]'s distinction between ambiguous behavior and an objective investigative trigger.

## Current Profile

After [[RegBrown]] presented circumstantial concerns, Carmichael interviewed Montes, checked parts of her explanations, and found no financial anomaly or personnel-history evidence strong enough to continue. He noticed unusual reactions and suspected she might be withholding something, but generated innocent or personal explanations that preserved the working assumption that she was not a Cuban agent.

Years later, clues derived from decoded Cuban communications identified a highly placed agent who used a particular DIA messaging system and visited Guantanamo during a narrow period. Carmichael's search of travel and clearance records returned Montes, transforming diffuse suspicion into a specific, independently testable lead.

## Key Characteristics

- DIA counterintelligence officer responsible for the first inquiry into Montes.
- Corroborated parts of her account and initially closed the case for lack of sufficient evidence.
- Noticed behavioral anomalies without treating them as reliable proof of espionage.
- Later searched institutional records against decoded communications clues.
- Serves in the episode as a case of both truth-default vulnerability and evidence-based correction.
