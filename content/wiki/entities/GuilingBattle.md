# 桂陵之战 / Battle of Guiling

桂陵之战 / Battle of Guiling enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-16-sunbin-pangjuan-enyuan-jiuge-guiling-zhi-zhan-lryhzkc1-xsvfjhvicu8yxa9d2jr]] as the campaign outcome of [[EncirclingWeiToRescueZhao|围魏救赵]]. [[WeiState|魏国]] is attacking [[ZhaoState|赵国]] and besieging [[Handan|邯郸]], so [[QiState|齐国]] under [[TianJi|田忌]] and [[SunBin|孙膑]] does not head straight to Zhao. It threatens [[Daliang|大梁]] instead.

The battle matters because it turns an idiom into an operational pattern. By attacking what Wei cannot ignore, Qi changes the enemy army's direction, makes it abandon the original siege, and then defeats it by ambush at Guiling. In the source's account, the victory is less about rushing to the endangered ally than about forcing the aggressor to fight on terrain and timing chosen by the rescuer.
