# Colette Kress

## Overview
Colette Kress is [[Nvidia]]'s chief financial officer and, according to the source, its interim chief executive after [[JensenHuang|Jensen Huang]]'s reported resignation.

## Current Profile
The sources place Kress at the center of a continuity bridge rather than presenting her as the confirmed permanent successor. Her finance leadership may support near-term stability while Nvidia's board searches for a long-term strategic leader. Her reported skepticism toward some of Huang's investments and guarantees sharpens the transition into a capital-allocation test: tighter discipline may reduce Nvidia's contingent risk while exposing dependent customers.

The latest sources add internal and external legitimacy tests. Strong inherited-quarter earnings, fewer publicly reported departures, and accelerated pay adjustments do not settle long-run retention or trust. Kress's reported statement that the board would not restore Huang and the episode's claims of tighter financing for new cloud providers and halted H200 sales to China make her interim tenure consequential, but do not establish that those policies originated with her or will persist.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as Nvidia's chief financial officer.
- Was appointed interim CEO in the episode's account of Huang's departure.
- Represents immediate internal continuity during a board-led succession search.
- Is not identified by the source as the selected permanent chief executive.
- Is reported to have challenged some of Huang's aggressive investment and guarantee choices and is associated with a more conservative financing and China-sales posture, though decision ownership remains unclear.
- Has a finance role and reported risk preference that do not by themselves establish either strength or weakness in product and engineering leadership.
- Faces employee pressure over stock-linked compensation and disclosure while trying to preserve operating continuity.
