# 三川 / Sanchuan (Warring States)

三川 / Sanchuan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-55-zhangyi-tongshi-guiren-qinhuiwang-lsclfbwn06fgzooipf5up9sdkedy]] as part of [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]'s proposed opportunity for [[QinState|秦国]] after [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] dies. Zhang Yi tells [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] that if he is sent to [[WeiState|魏国]], [[QiState|齐国]] will attack Wei because it hates him, giving Qin an opening to attack [[HanState|韩国]] and advance into Sanchuan.

The source does not pause to define Sanchuan geographically, so this page should remain source-scoped. Its wiki function is to mark the royal-domain pressure implied in Zhang Yi's pitch: entering Sanchuan sits beside the stronger claim that Qin could pressure the [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]] and seize the maps and registers associated with political authority.
