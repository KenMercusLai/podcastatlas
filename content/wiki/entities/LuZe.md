# 吕泽 / Lu Ze

吕泽 / Lu Ze enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-152-xiangyu-3wanren-ruhe-wansheng-liubang-56wan-dajun-2-lq9ecpjb4r-ij0-e-iu-ezfavowj|Hanji 152 part 2]] as [[LuZhi|吕雉]]'s brother and a [[LiuBang|刘邦]]-side military actor after the [[PengchengBattle|彭城之战]]. The episode says Lu Ze is leading troops at [[Xiayi|下邑]] when Liu Bang reaches him after the rout.

The source role is a recovery point rather than a full biography. Liu Bang's main coalition has just collapsed, his family has been captured, and many lords are shifting toward [[XiangYu|项羽]], but Lu Ze's force gives Liu Bang a place to gather scattered soldiers and continue the campaign.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-153-xiayi-huace-canbai-hou-liubang-ruhe-qisi-huisheng-1-lgvjuxuskyyfmyeeqm9y8aeln-hu|Hanji 153]] keeps Lu Ze in the background as the Xiayi recovery context while shifting the focus to [[ZhangLiang|张良]]'s [[XiayiPlan|下邑画策]]. Lu Ze's troops make the pause possible; the episode then asks how Liu Bang can turn that pause into renewed anti-Chu strategy.

Lu Ze therefore extends the Lu-family branch beyond marriage and later regency. In this source, the Lu family is simultaneously exposed through Lu Zhi's capture and useful through Lu Ze's field force.
