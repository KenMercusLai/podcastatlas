# 刘虞 / Liu Yu (Late Han)

刘虞 / Liu Yu (Late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-998-baima-jiangjun-gongsunzan-cong-sanqian-baima-dao-kunshou-gucheng-lp-1l2gvbsbfnyznutdghxwwvqna|Hanji 998]] as the Youzhou authority whom [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] has already killed, along with his family. The episode uses Liu Yu mostly through aftermath: his death creates a revenge and legitimacy center that Gongsun Zan's coercive rule cannot extinguish.

Liu Yu's old staff and supporters, especially [[XianyuFu|鲜于辅]], gather forces to avenge him. His son [[LiuHeLateHan|刘和]] becomes a banner around which [[Wuhuan|乌桓]], [[Xianbei|鲜卑]], and [[YuanShao|袁绍]]-aligned forces can coordinate against Gongsun Zan.
