# Democritus / 德谟克利特

Democritus appears in [[ep256-ai-shidai-ziyou-yizhi-hai-cunzai-ma-lk9aci8oqnwerk26jy683nbdddcx]] as the ancient atomist example behind one early pressure on [[FreeWill|free will]]. [[TuMotuo|土摩托]] uses the atomist picture to explain why a world made of basic units obeying fixed laws can look like a fully determined world.

In this source, Democritus is not profiled as a full philosopher. He is the entry point into [[CausalDeterminism]], where human choice can appear to dissolve into earlier material causes.

## Connections
- [[FreeWill]] - problem his atomism helps stage in the episode.
- [[CausalDeterminism]] - concept linked to atomistic lawfulness.
- [[Chrysippus]] and [[LaplacesDemon]] - later causal-chain and predictability examples in the same source.
