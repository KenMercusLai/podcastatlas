# Pierre Plantard

## Overview

Pierre Plantard was a twentieth-century French political and esoteric self-mythologizer presented by the source as the central fabricator behind the medieval history later attributed to the [[PrioryOfSion|Priory of Sion]].

## Current Profile

[[301-the-real-da-vinci-code-glt5654192229]] describes Plantard as repeatedly seeking status through secret societies, political intervention, titles, prophecy, and invented lineage. His actual Priory began in 1956 as a local association near Annemasse, but in the early 1960s he recast it as an esoteric chivalric fellowship, connected himself to monarchic destiny, and helped create genealogies and leadership lists that were deposited in the Bibliothèque nationale.

The later sacred-bloodline theory exceeded Plantard's own reported claims. Gérard de Sède and Henry Lincoln carried the planted evidence into wider circulation, while *The Holy Blood and the Holy Grail* and [[TheDaVinciCode|The Da Vinci Code]] added religious stakes and achieved far greater commercial reach.

## Key Characteristics

- Pursued status and authority through political, chivalric, esoteric, and genealogical self-presentation.
- Founded the documented Priory of Sion in 1956 rather than inheriting an ancient medieval order.
- Recast a mundane local association as an esoteric fellowship connected to French monarchic destiny.
- Helped manufacture an archival-looking documentary trail through forged genealogies and leadership lists.
- Lost narrative control as later writers added the Jesus-Mary Magdalene bloodline and popularized the story globally.

## Evidence

- Political and status formation: [[301-the-real-da-vinci-code-glt5654192229]] reports Plantard's far-right interwar activity, approach to Pétain, Alpha Galates, later Freemasonry, appeals to de Gaulle, and clairvoyant claims.
- Organization and reinvention: [[301-the-real-da-vinci-code-glt5654192229]] dates his Priory to 1956 and its esoteric reboot to the early 1960s.
- Fabricated record: [[301-the-real-da-vinci-code-glt5654192229]] attributes the forged genealogies, grand-master lists, and planted archive materials to Plantard and an associate.
- Narrative afterlife: [[301-the-real-da-vinci-code-glt5654192229]] traces the materials through de Sède and Lincoln into later sacred-bloodline publishing.
