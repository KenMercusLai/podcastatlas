# McDonald's

## Overview

McDonald's appears in the wiki as a global fast-food company whose cases span behavioral economics, U.S. traffic pressure, China control structure, and African market-entry constraints.

## Current Profile

The current profile is not a single growth story. McDonald's can function as a familiar everyday brand and pricing example, but recent sources use it to show multiple limits on scale: U.S. demand can weaken under price and traffic pressure, China growth can require local control, and sub-Saharan African expansion can be blocked by product identity and beef supply-chain requirements. The African comparison sharpens the point that a global brand does not automatically carry the same operating fit across proteins, markets, and supplier systems.

This page covers the global company and is separate from [[McDonaldsNetherlands]], a country-unit case in an AI-generated advertising episode.

## Key Characteristics

- Everyday fast-food brand used to explain pricing, refills, and marginal-consumption logic.
- U.S. demand-pressure case where traffic weakness, price sensitivity, and operating burden matter.
- China local-control template through the 2017 transaction involving [[CITICCapital]] and [[CarlyleGroup]].
- Sub-Saharan African laggard relative to [[KFC]] in the source's regional comparison.
- Burger-centered brand whose beef supply-chain standards can constrain market expansion.

## Evidence

- Behavioral-economics example: [[155-meimao-neng-dang-fan-chi-ma-xiang-zhuanqian-gai-zuo-dian-sha-jieju-shi-ying-bimian-shenme-xingwei-jingjixue-siwei-you-shenme-yong-920167210]] uses McDonald's with [[CocaCola|Coca-Cola]] refills to explain [[MarginalAnalysis]], [[PriceElasticity]], and targeted marketing.
- U.S. traffic and price pressure: [[fuzhuang-pinpai-a-f-xunzhao-zhongguo-hezuo-huoban-fufei-tiqian-kan-telangpu-tiewen-fuwu-shangxian-1004810677]] reports weak same-store sales growth, falling traffic, uneven franchisee pricing compliance, and new-product operating burden; [[caracas-under-pressure-democracy-in-venezuela-6a7d8bbe7d85cbdf96e00822]] links weak fast-food traffic to price, low-income pressure, food-safety fears, and health concerns.
- China local control: [[145-gaijia-zhongzi-de-canyin-yangpinpai-feng-tou-quan-1-145-1]] presents McDonald's China as the early [[ForeignRestaurantBrandLocalControl]] template through the 2017 CITIC/Carlyle transaction and later ownership reshuffling.
- African footprint limit: [[zai-feizhou-mai-kuaican-weishenme-kendeji-bi-maidanglao-geng-chidekai-1010601062]] says McDonald's is present only in [[SouthAfrica]] within [[SubSaharanAfrica]] and has far fewer South African stores than KFC.
- Beef and brand constraint: [[zai-feizhou-mai-kuaican-weishenme-kendeji-bi-maidanglao-geng-chidekai-1010601062]] says McDonald's beef patties require specific cuts and proportions, while the brand remains associated with beef burgers even after adding chicken items in South Africa.

## Qualifications

Current sales, traffic, share-price, store-count, and expansion-plan figures are source-scoped. The African episode compares KFC and McDonald's in sub-Saharan Africa and should not be read as a full global ranking or a complete account of McDonald's performance.

The China-control sources show one market structure, not a universal rule that McDonald's must use the same ownership model everywhere.
