# 中国邮政

## Overview
中国邮政 is the universal-service postal system and former commercial-regulatory center against which China's private express industry first developed.

## Current Profile
The source presents postal exclusivity as a real public-service funding argument and a major barrier to private document delivery. Before the 2006 separation of postal administration and business and the 2009 revised Postal Law, private carriers faced fines, confiscation, required handoffs, and prolonged disputes over which letters could be carried outside the postal system.

## Key Characteristics
- Universal-service provider with nationwide service obligations.
- Historical holder of reserved letter-delivery rights.
- Early combination of EMS operations and industry regulation.
- Continuing high-service and rural-logistics role after private-carrier legalization.

## Evidence
- Public-service rationale: [[no-219-kuaidi-jianghu-sanshinian-tonglubang-taobao-pinduoduo-yu-jiage-zhan-1015471748]] says postal exclusivity was defended as cross-subsidy for low-density regions.
- Regulatory conflict: [[no-219-kuaidi-jianghu-sanshinian-tonglubang-taobao-pinduoduo-yu-jiage-zhan-1015471748]] describes fines, letter quotas, the 2002 “64号文,” and the seven-year weight-threshold dispute.
- Later role: [[no-219-kuaidi-jianghu-sanshinian-tonglubang-taobao-pinduoduo-yu-jiage-zhan-1015471748]] reports high 72-hour delivery performance and shared village service stations involving postal, private, transport, and retail operators.
