# Vision Pro

[[qishui-yinyue-chongji-tengxun-wangyi-tesila-deng-cheqi-zai-guonei-kaizhan-daguimo-zhaohui-1008396395]] adds a cost-and-usage update. The source says Apple cut about 100 Vision Pro-related roles, with immersive-video and game teams affected, because the device's active-user base remained limited while immersive video could cost millions of dollars per episode to produce.

Vision Pro appears in [[tech-20260424-0424-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260424-0424-mp-tech-pod-128]] as one of [[Apple]]'s attempts to move beyond the mature [[IPhone|iPhone]] era. [[AnitaRamaswamy]] notes mixed reaction to the product while arguing that Apple still needs to find the next computing form factor people want to buy.

In the wiki, Vision Pro belongs to the [[AIPlusTerminals]] and [[SmartphoneAIHub]] branch as a cautionary hardware case: even a major platform company can ship a technically ambitious device without proving that it is the post-phone interface users will adopt at scale.
