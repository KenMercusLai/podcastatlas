# 雍正 / Yongzheng Emperor

雍正 / Yongzheng Emperor is the Qing emperor whose response to the [[ZengJing|曾静]] case drives [[11-yongzheng-wangchao-zhi-dayi-juemi-shijingqian-bi-xia-de-qiangu-qi-an-547265513]]. The episode presents him as a ruler who treated a rural anti-Qing letter not only as a criminal matter but as a challenge to his personal reputation, succession legitimacy, and the Manchu-Han order of the Qing state.

The source emphasizes Yongzheng's unusual willingness to argue. After [[YueZhongqi|岳钟琪]] reported [[ZhangXi|张熙]] by [[SecretMemorialPoliticalIntelligence|secret memorial]], Yongzheng personally refuted claims about greed, lust, fratricide, patricide, succession, and Manchu oppression. He then tried to convert the case into [[DaiYiJueMiLu|《大义觉迷录》]], sparing Zeng Jing while making [[LuLiuliang|吕留良]] and Zhejiang literati the deeper ideological enemy.

That approach makes Yongzheng a source case for [[ImperialPublicRebuttal]] and [[PoliticalPropagandaBackfire]]. The episode reads his rebuttal as politically sincere and strategically risky: by publishing the accusations, he made them memorable, while later suppression under [[QianlongEmperor|乾隆]] reinforced suspicion rather than eliminating rumor.
