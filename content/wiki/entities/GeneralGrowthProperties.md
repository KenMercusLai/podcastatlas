# General Growth Properties

## Overview
General Growth Properties is the bankrupt real-estate company [[BillAckman]] cites in the All-In interview as [[PershingSquare]]'s most successful equity investment and as the origin of [[HowardHughesHoldings]].

## Current Profile
In the bounded source, General Growth matters as a restructuring case. Ackman says Pershing bought a large stake during bankruptcy, helped restructure the company, and later received Howard Hughes as a spin-out of assets analysts disliked.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as the bankruptcy-investing case Ackman uses to explain Pershing's historic equity upside.
- Provides the corporate origin story for Howard Hughes Holdings in the source.
- Illustrates Ackman's claim that disliked assets can become a new compounding platform under different capital allocation.
