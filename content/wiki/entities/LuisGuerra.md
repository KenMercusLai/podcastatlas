# Luis Guerra

## Overview

Luis Guerra is the composer for [[RevisionistHistory]], presented as a longtime professional musician who designs music around narration, mood, story structure, and performance flexibility.

## Current Profile

In [[return-to-the-404-return-to-the-404|Return to the 404]], Guerra describes a musical path beginning in childhood and developing professionally after his move to Austin in the early 1990s. His scoring method starts from the show's interest in overlooked or misunderstood subjects: familiar musical clichés can be inverted, recombined, or made slightly strange so the score shares the program's revisionist posture.

Guerra treats transparency as a craft constraint. The score should support the story without dominating it, yet still develop enough to reward close listening and work in live performance. He also composes in relation to [[MalcolmGladwell]]'s speaking voice, selecting compatible tonal centers or deliberately contrasting keys when the story needs tension.

## Key Characteristics

- Multi-instrumentalist and professional musician whose career developed in Austin.
- Podcast composer who reworks familiar musical language to fit revisionist storytelling.
- Voice-sensitive arranger using tonality and contrast to support narration and tension.
- Production collaborator balancing unobtrusiveness, musical development, and live adaptability.
