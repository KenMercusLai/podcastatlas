# Archive of Our Own / AO3

## Overview
Archive of Our Own / AO3 is the fan-work archive whose loss of access in China is the central institutional consequence discussed in EP113.

## Current Profile
The episode presents AO3 as a shared creative resource affected by conflict around a fan work involving [[XiaoZhan|肖战]]. Because the archive served users beyond the original dispute, its reported inaccessibility transformed a narrow offense claim into a conflict over community infrastructure, speech, reporting, and collective punishment. The source does not independently prove the technical or legal chain from fan reports to the access change.

## Key Characteristics
- Archive and community infrastructure for transformative and fan-created work.
- Shared resource whose disruption affected people outside the originating fandom conflict.
- Case in which platform access, content rules, and organized reporting became entangled.
- Symbol around which counter-mobilization expanded toward an idol, fans, and commercial brands.
