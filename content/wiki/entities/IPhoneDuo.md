# iPhone Duo

## Overview
iPhone Duo is the foldable iPhone product discussed across the wiki as Apple's first major foldable phone. The current sources treat it as a new product category between [[IPhone|iPhone]] and iPad rather than just another Pro-tier slab phone.

## Current Profile
The bounded sources frame iPhone Duo as a polished Apple entry into a category Android manufacturers established years earlier, but not as a compromise-free Pro replacement. Its inner and outer displays, folding postures, camera-preview possibilities, iPad-derived layouts, and potential Apple Pencil use create a plausible phone-tablet bridge. A machine test strengthens the mechanical case because more than 32,000 folds produced no significant hinge change, while first-week owners show that primary-device readiness is a wider system test: both retained their old iPhones because eSIM switching, weight, camera quality, heat, durability anxiety, public-screen exposure, accessories, and uneven apps outweighed the large-screen benefit. Reading and video are already persuasive; work and all-day phone substitution are not yet settled.

## Key Characteristics
- Duo is positioned as Apple's largest recent iPhone form-factor change and a test of whether [[FoldablePhoneProductivity]] can justify premium tradeoffs.
- The source describes it as technically ambitious, with a 7.6-inch inner display, outer display, A20 Pro chip, under-display inner camera, and high-refresh screens.
- Its software value depends on folding posture, inner/outer-screen continuity, large-screen layouts, iPad-derived interaction experience, and whether apps avoid black-bar or enlarged-phone compatibility modes.
- Its China-market purchase decision is constrained by [[RegionalESIMConstraint]] and national-app adaptation because eSIM support, WeChat, Alipay, and other daily apps can matter as much as Apple's own platform integration.
- It is not treated as a simple Pro replacement: early owners kept their old iPhones because camera quality, weight, eSIM friction, public-screen privacy, heat, and app behavior still matter in ordinary use.
- Liu Fei's source reads Duo as Apple's mature-company entry into a validated premium foldable niche, not as proof that foldables will replace slab iPhones.
- Stern's machine test provides encouraging early hinge evidence after more than 32,000 folds, while owner behavior shows that failure reports and durability anxiety can still suppress opening even without a verified unit failure.

## Evidence
- Form-factor evidence: [[vol-174-iphone-duo-mai-bu-mai-pingguo-26-qiujifabuhui-1-6695-1]] says the product opens from a phone into a larger inner-screen device and places it between phone and iPad.
- Specification evidence: [[vol-174-iphone-duo-mai-bu-mai-pingguo-26-qiujifabuhui-1-6695-1]] records a 7.6-inch inner display, 2670x1878 resolution, roughly 430 PPI, A20 Pro chip, under-display inner camera, and 0-120Hz displays.
- Purchase-friction evidence: [[vol-174-iphone-duo-mai-bu-mai-pingguo-26-qiujifabuhui-1-6695-1]] gives source-scoped China prices of 15999 yuan, 17999 yuan, and 21499 yuan across storage tiers and calls 254g heavy.
- Connectivity evidence: [[vol-174-iphone-duo-mai-bu-mai-pingguo-26-qiujifabuhui-1-6695-1]] says global eSIM-only design makes mainland, Hong Kong, and U.S. versions difficult to choose among.
- Developer evidence: [[vol-174-iphone-duo-mai-bu-mai-pingguo-26-qiujifabuhui-1-6695-1]] says app layouts may need rework for Duo's wide screen and tab-bar changes.
- Strategy evidence: [[no-233-dankou-shouji-weishenme-yaozhedie-apple-weishenme-yao-genfeng-gkwriaioeekuaj5mwgtv2l0u]] says Apple is entering a proven premium foldable segment with supply-chain, product-taste, software, and interaction advantages rather than inventing the category from zero.
- Ecosystem evidence: [[no-233-dankou-shouji-weishenme-yaozhedie-apple-weishenme-yao-genfeng-gkwriaioeekuaj5mwgtv2l0u]] argues that iPad software experience and system-level reachability design may be Duo's core edge over Android foldables.
- China-market evidence: [[no-233-dankou-shouji-weishenme-yaozhedie-apple-weishenme-yao-genfeng-gkwriaioeekuaj5mwgtv2l0u]] qualifies the iPad-ecosystem advantage by noting that WeChat, Alipay, and other national apps still determine much of the everyday unfolded-screen experience in China.
- Durability evidence: [[tech-20261023-1023-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261023-1023-mp-tech-pod-128]] reports no significant hinge-operation change after more than 32,000 test folds, while a groove remained visible under some conditions.
- Early-utility evidence: [[tech-20261023-1023-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261023-1023-mp-tech-pod-128]] finds the larger display useful for reading and video but not yet a strong work-oriented iPad replacement.
- First-generation compromise evidence: [[tech-20261023-1023-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261023-1023-mp-tech-pod-128]] places the price just under $2,000 and notes two rear cameras and no Face ID.
- First-week substitution evidence: [[podcast176-v1-podcast176-v1]] says both hosts declined to activate eSIM and retained their previous iPhones because the Duo did not satisfy their full daily-device needs.
- Everyday-use evidence: [[podcast176-v1-podcast176-v1]] finds strong reading, video, image, and tablet-layout value but records 254g weight, weaker cameras, heat, privacy concerns, accessory friction, and uneven apps.
- Developer evidence: [[podcast176-v1-podcast176-v1]] reports that real hardware exposed unfolding, rotation, and camera-preview failures absent from simulator testing and that Apple review pressure may accelerate adaptation.

## Qualifications
This page is grounded in launch material, market commentary, one controlled fold-cycle test, and several days of ownership rather than longitudinal evidence. The machine test does not establish fleet failure rates, lifetime reliability, repairability, resistance to drops or debris, or consistency across production units; isolated failure reports do not establish those rates either. Prices, benchmark scores, regional eSIM rules, app behavior, review policy, future software support, reservation counts, sales, battery life, and opening-frequency claims remain source-scoped.
