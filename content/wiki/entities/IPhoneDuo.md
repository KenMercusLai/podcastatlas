# iPhone Duo

## Overview
iPhone Duo is the foldable iPhone product introduced in the Fengyan Fengyu discussion of Apple's 2026 fall event. In this source it is treated as a new product category between [[IPhone|iPhone]] and iPad rather than just another Pro-tier slab phone.

## Current Profile
The episode frames iPhone Duo as a high-completion Apple version of ideas already explored by Android foldables: inner and outer displays, hinge-centered postures, camera-preview tricks, under-display inner camera, large-screen software layouts, and possible Apple Pencil use. Its appeal comes from integration, animation, screen quality, chip performance, and new use cases; its purchase friction comes from price, weight, eSIM restrictions, app adaptation, and uncertainty around first-generation durability.

## Key Characteristics
- Duo is positioned as Apple's largest recent iPhone form-factor change and a test of whether [[FoldablePhoneProductivity]] can justify premium tradeoffs.
- The source describes it as technically ambitious, with a 7.6-inch inner display, outer display, A20 Pro chip, under-display inner camera, and high-refresh screens.
- Its software value depends on folding posture, inner/outer-screen continuity, large-screen layouts, and whether apps avoid black-bar or enlarged-phone compatibility modes.
- Its China-market purchase decision is constrained by [[RegionalESIMConstraint]] because the source says all versions are eSIM-only while mainland and overseas eSIM support are mutually awkward.
- It is not treated as a simple Pro replacement: the hosts separate Duo from iPhone Pro and speculate that a future Duo Pro could add stronger camera and Pro features.
- The source's buyer judgment is enthusiastic but qualified: the hosts want to buy or test it, while warning ordinary users to weigh communication, carrying, storage, and price costs.

## Evidence
- Form-factor evidence: [[vol-174-iphone-duo-mai-bu-mai-pingguo-26-qiujifabuhui-1-6695-1]] says the product opens from a phone into a larger inner-screen device and places it between phone and iPad.
- Specification evidence: [[vol-174-iphone-duo-mai-bu-mai-pingguo-26-qiujifabuhui-1-6695-1]] records a 7.6-inch inner display, 2670x1878 resolution, roughly 430 PPI, A20 Pro chip, under-display inner camera, and 0-120Hz displays.
- Purchase-friction evidence: [[vol-174-iphone-duo-mai-bu-mai-pingguo-26-qiujifabuhui-1-6695-1]] gives source-scoped China prices of 15999 yuan, 17999 yuan, and 21499 yuan across storage tiers and calls 254g heavy.
- Connectivity evidence: [[vol-174-iphone-duo-mai-bu-mai-pingguo-26-qiujifabuhui-1-6695-1]] says global eSIM-only design makes mainland, Hong Kong, and U.S. versions difficult to choose among.
- Developer evidence: [[vol-174-iphone-duo-mai-bu-mai-pingguo-26-qiujifabuhui-1-6695-1]] says app layouts may need rework for Duo's wide screen and tab-bar changes.

## Qualifications
This page is grounded in one podcast source based on launch material, hands-on reports, and host judgment rather than long-term ownership or independent durability testing. Prices, benchmark scores, regional eSIM rules, app behavior, and future software support should remain source-scoped until additional evidence is ingested.
