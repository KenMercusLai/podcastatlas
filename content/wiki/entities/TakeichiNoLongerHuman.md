# 竹一 / Takeichi

竹一 / Takeichi is the classmate in [[NoLongerHuman|《人间失格》]] who sees through [[ObaYozo|大庭叶藏]]'s performed clowning in [[33-renjian-shige-danxiaogui-de-zhuanglie-xianji-599924624]]. His sentence that Yozo is doing it on purpose terrifies Yozo because it pierces the [[ComicMaskSurvival|comic mask]] that had kept him socially hidden.

The source treats Takeichi as more than a threat. After Yozo tries to monitor and befriend him, Takeichi becomes a strange witness who predicts that Yozo will be loved by women and become a great painter. His description of Van Gogh's self-portrait as a "monster picture" gives Yozo a glimpse of another mode of expression: showing the frightening self directly instead of disguising it with jokes.

## Key Claims
- Takeichi makes Yozo visible at the exact point where invisibility had been Yozo's safety.
- His recognition is destabilizing but also briefly liberating because it suggests that ugly or uncanny self-knowledge can become art.
- In the source's reading, Takeichi is one of the few people who can meet Yozo outside ordinary normality demands.
