# Bayer

## Overview
Bayer appears in this source as the owner of [[Monsanto]] and the corporate party exposed to large [[Roundup]] litigation risk.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Bayer through litigation and regulatory-preemption risk. [[DavidFriedberg]] says Bayer has paid and reserved large sums for Roundup lawsuits and still faces many outstanding claims. The business issue is whether federal pesticide labeling authority through the [[EnvironmentalProtectionAgency]] can preempt state failure-to-warn suits.

## Key Characteristics
- Parent company connected to Monsanto and Roundup litigation.
- Exposed to large product-liability settlement and reserve claims in the source.
- Dependent on federal-versus-state legal interpretation around pesticide labels.
- Used as a business-law case where regulatory compliance may not end state-law liability.
