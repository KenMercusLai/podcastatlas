# 卫怀君 / Lord Huai of Wey

卫怀君 / Lord Huai of Wey appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-100-1-huangdi-zhong-de-chaochang-daiji-wang-guoshi-le-lt75scavlm8qjn6ulpd-b2kwzwjf]] as the [[WeyState|卫国]] ruler who goes to see [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]] and is killed by him. The episode links this to Wey's degraded position under [[WeiState|魏国]] and to the broader late-Warring-States order in which even a pressured Wei can still dominate a smaller polity.

His death is therefore used less as a biography than as an instance of [[WeakOverWeakPredation|弱者向更弱者转嫁压迫]]. [[WeiState|魏国]] suffers repeated losses and humiliation before [[QinState|秦国]], but Wei Anxi Wang still exercises lethal authority downward against Wey and then installs Huaijun's brother [[WeyYuanjun|卫元君]].
