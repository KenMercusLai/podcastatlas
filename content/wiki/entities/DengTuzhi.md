# 邓图直 / Deng Tuzhi

邓图直 / Deng Tuzhi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-42-gei-mengchangjun-ti-yijian-de-houguo-lr1jp0tpiaboaca3j5-yvyhyn4vf]] as the person assigned to transport the ivory bed that the Chu king gives to [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]].

The episode uses him to make the gift concrete. The ivory bed is valuable, bulky, and hard to move; Deng Tuzhi fears that any damage during transport would leave him unable to pay compensation. He therefore asks Mengchang Jun's retainer [[GongsunXu|公孙须]] to get the gift refused and offers an ancestral sword as repayment.

For the wiki, Deng Tuzhi matters less as a full biography than as the logistical pressure behind a successful remonstrance. His private problem creates a useful public argument: accepting extravagant gifts can damage political reputation and future diplomatic protocol.
