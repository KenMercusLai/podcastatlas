# Pierre-Simon Laplace / 拉普拉斯

Pierre-Simon Laplace appears in [[ep256-ai-shidai-ziyou-yizhi-hai-cunzai-ma-lk9aci8oqnwerk26jy683nbdddcx]] through [[LaplacesDemon|Laplace's demon]]. The episode uses this image of complete causal predictability as one of modern science's classic challenges to [[FreeWill|free will]].

His role in the source is conceptual rather than biographical. Laplace represents the extension of [[IsaacNewton|Newtonian]] lawfulness into the thought that, if an intelligence knew every particle state and law, the future might be calculable in principle.
