# 郭昊天 / Guo Haotian

## Overview
郭昊天 / Guo Haotian enters the wiki as the returning [[WushichaYanjiusuo|无时差研究所]] guest who discusses [[GLP1Agonists|GLP-1]] weight-loss drugs from personal-use, biotech, and public medical-literacy angles.

## Current Profile
In the EP384 source, Guo functions as an industry-adjacent explainer rather than as a clinician. He uses his own tirzepatide experience, mechanism explanations, drug-development logic, and market observations to separate regulated metabolic medicine from casual appearance optimization. His strongest contribution is boundary-setting: GLP-1 drugs can be effective for selected people, but personal response, side effects, pregnancy uncertainty, rebound, muscle loss, and unverified peptide injection all need caution.

## Key Characteristics
- Returning podcast guest associated in the episode with earlier synthetic-biology discussion.
- Personal-use narrator whose tirzepatide response differed from the expected appetite-suppression pattern.
- Mechanism explainer connecting GLP-1, GIP, insulin, gastric emptying, satiety, and energy expenditure.
- Biotech-industry interpreter linking patents, approvals, production capacity, pricing, and multi-target drug development.
- Cautionary voice against unverified self-injection and research-use peptides.
