# 卫元君 / Lord Yuan of Wey

卫元君 / Lord Yuan of Wey enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-100-1-huangdi-zhong-de-chaochang-daiji-wang-guoshi-le-lt75scavlm8qjn6ulpd-b2kwzwjf]] as the brother of [[WeyHuaijun|卫怀君]]. After [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]] kills Wey Huaijun, he installs Yuanjun as the new ruler of [[WeyState|卫国]].

The episode uses the replacement to show how little autonomy Wey retains. Succession is not decided internally by Wey's own political order, but by the stronger [[WeiState|魏国]] ruler who has just used lethal pressure against the prior ruler.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-107-zuihou-yici-hezong-faqin-fa-le-ge-jimo-lrzq1qks7z-oromd-5f-dgqjwjhw]] revisits the late Wey branch in a source-scoped way. The transcript associates [[WeyYuanjun|卫元君]] with the remnant Wey line before [[WeyJunJiao|卫君角]], while also using Wei-like phrasing around Qin's seizure of Chaoge and Puyang. This page preserves the connection to [[WeyState|卫国]] and marks the ambiguity through [[TextualVariantPoliticalStakes|史书异文政治重量]].
