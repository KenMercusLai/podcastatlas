## Overview

夏贺良 / Xia Heliang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-581-han-aidi-zai-guishen-zhidao-you-duo-yuchun-ltok7kbzmuf-os84spseww0qjbrm|Hanji 581]] as [[GanZhongkeWesternHan|甘忠可]]'s disciple whose renewed-mandate method succeeds temporarily under [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] after failing under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]].

## Current Profile

Xia Heliang survives the earlier punishment of Gan Zhongke's circle and keeps studying the [[Taipingjing|《太平经》]]. Under Aidi, he reaches court through [[LiXunWesternHan|李寻]] and [[JieGuangWesternHan|解光]], then tells the emperor that illness and disasters are Heaven's warnings. His solution is a public ritual-symbolic reset: change the reign title, adopt a new imperial style, alter the water-clock scale, and promise health, heirs, and disaster relief.

The source makes Xia a case of fangshi authority crossing into personnel politics. When the promised effects fail to appear, Xia's group argues that senior ministers do not understand Heaven's plan and that Li Xun and Jie Guang should guide government. Aidi reverses most of the edict, arrests Xia's group, and Xia is executed.

## Key Characteristics

- Preserves Gan Zhongke's renewed-mandate method after the first court failure.
- Gains access under Aidi because illness, disaster anxiety, and disorder make the claim newly attractive.
- Converts cosmic warning into concrete symbolic policy: reign title, imperial style, amnesty, and timekeeping change.
- Moves from ritual cure to proposed personnel replacement after the initial promise fails.
- Becomes the clearest Hanji 581 example of fangshi delivery risk and punishment.

## Qualifications

The page does not settle whether Xia believed his own claims, how much of the proposal came from Xia alone, or the full legal wording behind his execution. The source presents the episode as a political-legitimacy case, not a full religious biography.
