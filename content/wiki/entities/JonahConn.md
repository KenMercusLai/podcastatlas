# Jonah Conn

Jonah Conn appears in [[you-bet-your-life-insurance]] as a former Coventry employee who later helps start a brokerage representing sellers rather than buyers. His role in the source is to explain [[LifeSettlementPricingOpacity|pricing opacity]]: many policyholders do not know what a policy is worth in the [[LifeInsuranceSecondaryMarket|secondary market]], so they may accept much less than a competitive auction would produce.

Conn's perspective turns the market from a simple buyer-seller exchange into an information problem. A life settlement may unlock value for a policyholder, but price discovery can be weak if the seller lacks medical valuation, policy valuation, and multiple bids.

## Key Claims
- Conn says many sellers do not know the market value of the policies they are selling.
- His broker-side role is presented as a response to buyer-side informational advantage.
- The episode uses his view to frame brokerage as potentially protective, while still subject to commission costs and incentives.
