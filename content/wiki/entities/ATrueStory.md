# A True Story / 《真实的故事》

## Overview

*A True Story* is [[LucianOfSamosata|Lucian of Samosata]]'s openly invented satirical voyage, represented here through lunar travel, interplanetary colonial war, male reproduction, cultivated people, detachable organs, and a paradoxical contract about truth and fiction.

## Current Profile

The work begins by telling readers that its events never happened and could not happen. It then narrates impossible adventures with the measurements, enumerations, customs, and assured tone associated with factual travel and history. The comedy depends on readers recognizing both the invention and the authority performance.

The lunar material functions as a layered thought experiment. Earth becomes visible from elsewhere; Moon and Sun kingdoms fight over colonization using fantastic animals and engineering; a male-only society reorganizes reproduction without abolishing marriage roles or rank; and detachable organs make bodies technically revisable while preserving wealth differences. Physical possibility expands, but political and social judgment remains unresolved.

## Key Characteristics

- Declared fiction that parodies deceptive travel and historical narration.
- Voyage that relocates the observer from Earth to the Moon.
- Interplanetary conflict structured by colonization, siege, treaty, tribute, and alliance.
- Reproductive inversion involving male pregnancy and cultivated new people.
- Replaceable eyes and sexual organs that blur body–tool boundaries.
- Social order in which monarchy, military organization, age roles, and wealth survive radical bodily change.
- Early speculative work retrospectively associated with science fiction.

## Qualifications

The episode discusses only selected adventures and does not provide a complete textual analysis. “First science-fiction novel,” “cyborg,” reproductive-technology, feminist, and technology-ethics readings are retrospective interpretive frames; they do not establish modern genre membership, authorial prediction, or Lucian's endorsement of a male-only society.

## What Changed

- Established the work as declared fiction whose truth value lies in satire rather than factual report.
- Added the joint analysis of cosmic politics, reproductive inversion, and class-marked bodily modification.
