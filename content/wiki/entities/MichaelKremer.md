# Michael Kremer

Michael Kremer is the development economist in [[piles-of-cash-and-a-town-of-solutions-in-kenya-nigeria-summer-school]] who helped make [[BusiaKenya|Busia]] a landmark site for [[RandomizedControlledTrials]] in economics. The source says he had previously taught high school near Busia and later designed a randomized textbook study for a nonprofit working with schools.

The textbook result is important because it was negative on average. In the episode's account, average test scores did not improve even though high-scoring students benefited, pushing development economists toward [[RemedialEducationTargeting]] and a more empirical approach to what education spending actually does.
