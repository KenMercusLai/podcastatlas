## Overview

贾延 / Jia Yan is synthesized as a Western Han Yushi Dafu whose current wiki role now begins before the Wang Jia prison aftermath. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-586-weile-aiqing-gudai-huangdi-you-duo-chongdong-lizfxc8a4do88e6vyrcupyet3vs|Hanji 586]], he joins [[WangJiaWesternHan|王嘉]] in warning [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] not to privately enfeoff [[DongXian|董贤]] without public examination of the original case record. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-599-gan-dui-qianguize-shuo-bu-chengxiang-wangjia-you-duo-yong-lpptx-fzls9xbakw52b8n1n6xow0|Hanji 599]], his later removal creates the office vacancy through which Aidi restores [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]] after Wang Jia's prison death.

## Current Profile

The current evidence is narrow but now has two layers. Hanji 586 presents Jia Yan as Wang Jia's partner in a confidential objection to Dong Xian's reward. Their memorial does not only oppose the outcome; it asks Aidi to make the old memorial public and let court officials and scholars test whether Dong Xian's merit claim can stand.

Hanji 599 says Jia Yan is later removed from the Yushi Dafu post just as Wang Jia's final words reach Aidi. Aidi then appoints Kong Guang as Yushi Dafu, later promotes him to chancellor, and appoints [[HeWuWesternHan|何武]] to the Yushi Dafu role.

Jia Yan is therefore a transition marker in the episode's personnel sequence, not yet a developed actor.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Yushi Dafu in the immediate Wang Jia aftermath.
- Joins Wang Jia in a procedural objection to Dong Xian's formal enfeoffment.
- Removed from office before Kong Guang's appointment to the same post.
- Functions as an office-vacancy marker in Aidi's partial correction.
