# Edward Carson

Edward Carson appears in [[697-the-troubles-murder-in-belfast-part-1-glt4877520736]] as the unionist leader whose 1912 anti-home-rule covenant and volunteer mobilization supplied a lasting armed precedent for Ulster unionism. The episode says nearly half a million unionists signed the covenant and around 100,000 organized as volunteers to resist home rule.

For the source, Carson matters less as a biographical subject than as a memory ancestor. Later loyalist groups could present armed organization as continuity with respected unionist defense rather than as ordinary criminal violence, feeding [[HistoricalMemoryContest]] and [[UnionistSiegeMentality]].
