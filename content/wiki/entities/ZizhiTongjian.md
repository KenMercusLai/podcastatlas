# 《资治通鉴》 / Zizhi Tongjian

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-04-yurang-wei-zhiyao-fuchou-ln34tydha6iqwlrgmw7iugqfgfik]] adds an earlier Zhou-annals sequence around [[ZhiYao|智瑶]], [[YuRang|豫让]], [[ZhaoXiangzi|赵襄子]], and the formal recognition of the three Jin houses. The episode treats the work's opening lessons on rank and [[TalentVirtueDistinction|才德之分]] as programmatic for later political reading.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-05-guangcai-duomu-de-weiwenhou-1-lmkxzbmwhw3nhurape-eebydqcdl]] adds the next Wei Wenhou leadership branch. The episode reads the chronicle's selection of respect, promise-keeping, and Han-Zhao diplomacy stories as evidence that [[SimaGuang|司马光]] wanted rulers to see [[RenhePoliticalPower|人和]] as practical statecraft.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-1-lk-6ejgmjhvukqgtlz2lowu8qx-v]] adds the chancellor-selection branch behind the episode title. The source reads [[LiKe|李克]]'s advice to [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]] as practical "资治" material: judge people across circumstances, compare the level of talent they draw into government, and decide when a flawed military figure such as [[WuQi|吴起]] can still be used.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-2-lmfzo4sl9nd5kz3mrxdl1dl9efei]] reads the Zhou-annals section around early [[WarringStatesPeriod|战国时期]] events. The episode does not survey the whole work; it uses one short stretch to explain military leadership, succession notices, posthumous names, and politically ambiguous assassination language.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-1-lvkp-ju7pf5z5cz3thojv3s2fkyx]] adds another Zhou-annals segment, this time around [[ZhouAnWang|周安王]]'s first four years. The source shows how the chronicle's terse notices invite supplemental reconstruction through [[QinghuaJianXinian|清华简《系年》]] and later omen interpretation through [[WangYinglin|王应麟]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-2-lhngst3xsnyjrmzh1cvheocvvsqm]] adds King An's fifth to eighth years. The episode shows how the chronicle can move quickly from eclipse notice to assassination, succession, blank year, and city defection, making [[ChronicleSourceGaps]] and source variants such as [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] part of the reading problem.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-1-lq5r3xe-3y7al9nkbc8kyhntipll]] adds a court-politics reading around [[WuQi|吴起]] and [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]]. The episode uses the West River dialogue for [[VirtueOverNaturalBarriers]] and the Tian Wen chancellor dispute for [[TransitionFitOverMerit]], making the chronicle a source for organizational judgment as well as chronology.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-2-lvaqlgyccrugfo0l-wuwf1mt1kcm]] continues the same Wu Qi arc after his flight to [[ChuState|楚国]], using his reforms under [[ChuDaoWang|楚悼王]] for [[WarringStatesReformBacklash]]. It also shows the chronicle's annalistic function through clustered Qin/Zhao/Han succession notices and the formal recognition of [[TianHe|田和]] by the [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-3-ltca-ikuzo9ej7a0lfrgwv2-wulb]] completes that arc through King An's later years. The episode reads the chronicle's sparse notices on [[QinXianGong|秦献公]]'s coup, Tian-family Qi succession, [[WeiState|魏国]]'s defeat of [[ZhaoState|赵国]], and a solar eclipse alongside the fuller death scene where [[WuQi|吴起]] is killed against [[ChuDaoWang|楚悼王]]'s corpse.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl]] continues the [[ZhouAnWang|周安王]] years into 380-376 BCE. The episode uses terse war, succession, and extinction notices to frame [[EarlyWarringStatesInterstateWar]], the endings of [[QiKangGong|齐康公]]'s Jiang-line Qi and [[JinJinggong|晋静公]]'s Jin, and [[Zisi|子思]]'s governance counsel as material selected for practical rule.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] opens [[ZhouLiewang|周烈王]]'s reign. It uses the chronicle's 375 BCE notice for [[HanState|韩国]] destroying [[ZhengState|郑国]], the blank 374 BCE year as a [[ChronicleSourceGaps|source-gap]] case supplemented by [[Shiji|《史记》]], and the 373-372 BCE notices for war, succession, and territorial-scale explanation.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-2-lmnqfjmydygqnfec6xhzpixralvi]] adds a more explicit error-checking layer. The episode accepts the chronicle as the working frame for [[ZhouLiewang|周烈王]]'s fifth and sixth years, but flags [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]]'s visit and [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]]'s succession disorder as likely cases of [[ChronicleChronologyDrift|chronology drift]] inherited from thin early Warring States records.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-3-lmhj4le2mwu1yhhl-qmuoqkfmhjf]] continues into Zhou Lie Wang's seventh year and death, then follows the Wei succession conflict into Han-Zhao intervention. The chronicle frame here supports a statecraft reading: a civil succession conflict can become an interstate settlement problem once neighboring states win the battle but disagree about what should replace the defeated order.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-4-lhjz-m12pedkfszxyzgqtjnpsn3p]] steps outside the base chronicle to ask what its selectivity leaves out. The episode says [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] omits the story of [[ZhaoCangtang|赵苍唐]] using [[Shijing|《诗经》]] allusions and status etiquette to help the future [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]] return from [[ZhongshanState|中山国]], while [[ZizhiTongjianBu|《资治通鉴补》]] later restores that material. The source therefore extends this page's role from compact chronology and chronology drift into deliberate omission and later supplementation.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-11-shijie-fengqi-yunyong-1-lt60b2gddiosf5kade35uopwohi5]] opens the second Zhou-annals volume under [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]]. The episode reads the chronicle's short notices on Qi attacking Wei, Zhao attacking Qi, Wei-Han coordination against Qin, Wei attacking Song, Qin's [[ShimenBattle|石门之战]] victory, and blank years as evidence of accelerating [[EarlyWarringStatesInterstateWar]], [[QinEastwardPressure]], and continuing [[ChronicleSourceGaps]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-11-shijie-fengqi-yunyong-2-llzzalowv5vmbvgd6l-jujltkbyf]] continues the same annalistic pattern in 362 BCE: battle notices and succession notices sit beside each other. The source reads Wei's defeat of Han-Zhao, Qin's victory at [[Shaoliang|少梁]], and Wey/Yan/Qin successions as a compressed setup for [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]] and the later [[ShangYangReforms|商鞅变法]] branch.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-12-gongsunyang-qiuzhi-lu-shang-de-la-guanxi-zou-houmen-lvv4-bz8sm9scezci-o-msapkvil]] develops Zhou Xian Wang's eighth year by expanding Qin Xiao Gong's recruitment edict and Shang Yang's entry path. The episode reads the chronicle's compact setup through [[Shiji|《史记》]]-style context: [[QinMugong|秦穆公]] memory, Qin's lost Hexi shame, [[GongshuWeiChancellor|公叔痤]]'s failed advice to [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王 / 梁惠王]], and [[JingJian|景监]] as the channel that brings Shang Yang before Qin's ruler.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-13-gongsunyang-keke-bianfa-re-shashen-1-lgmjapc2eecefbdshnpenzfcv6ga]] continues Zhou Xian Wang's chronology into 360-359 BCE. The episode marks another blank year, then uses [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]]'s debate with [[GanLong|甘龙]] and [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]]'s backing to show how the chronicle can compress a major institutional turn into a reform notice with long-term consequences.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-13-gongsunyang-keke-bianfa-re-shashen-2-lk-d9uibhneerfol6z2rrle-6ypm]] supplies the enforcement and commentary sequel. The prince-lawbreaking case becomes a [[HighStatusLawEnforcementSignal|high-status law-enforcement signal]] through punishments tied to [[GongziQian|公子虔]] and [[GongsunJia|公孙贾]], while [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s commentary turns [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]]'s "信" into a governing-credit lesson beside [[QiHuanGong|齐桓公]], [[JinWenGong|晋文公]], and [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-14-qiweiwang-buming-zeyi-yiming-jingren-lrs6rbifyiqrojxg0mccpg7sf9rv]] adds the 358-356 BCE cluster and makes source criticism central again. The episode records interstate war, defense, marriage, and meetings, then argues that Qi Wei Wang's received placement is a [[QiChronologyDispute|chronology dispute]] inherited through thin Qi material before turning to the "一鸣惊人" governance story.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-15-jiu-bu-chun-yinfa-de-shijie-dazhan-li-iojxthq8komhlhyumho3yvtem]] adds the 355-354 BCE cluster. The episode reads the work's terse notices through two broader interpretive frames: [[TalentAsStateTreasure|以贤臣为宝]] in [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]]'s exchange with [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王]], and [[LuWineHandanWarCascade|鲁酒薄而邯郸围]] in the chain from [[LuState|鲁国]] wine, [[ChuState|楚国]] anger, and [[WeiState|魏国]]'s siege of [[Handan|邯郸]]. It also flags a small textual-variant issue around [[LuKanggong|鲁康公]]'s personal name in [[Shiji|《史记》]] versus the chronicle.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-16-sunbin-pangjuan-enyuan-jiuge-guiling-zhi-zhan-lryhzkc1-xsvfjhvicu8yxa9d2jr]] adds the 353 BCE cluster: Qi's rescue of Zhao through [[EncirclingWeiToRescueZhao|围魏救赵]], Han's attack on [[EasternZhouPolity|东周国]], and Chu's appointment of [[ZhaoXixu|昭奚恤]]. The episode shows the chronicle moving between battlefield strategy, royal-domain shrinkage, and chancellor-centered court politics in the same annalistic year.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-17-hanzhaohou-he-shenbuhai-lghjmjhq9qs4se96c5ofgz6caqof]] adds the 352-350 BCE cluster. The episode follows the chronicle through Qin attacks on Wei, Wei-Zhao reconciliation, [[ShenBuhai|申不害]]'s service to [[HanZhaohou|韩昭侯]], [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]]'s second reform round, the Qin-Wei meeting at [[TongWarringStates|同地]], and Zhao succession disorder after [[ZhaoChenghou|赵成侯]]'s death.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-18-zhongguo-lishishang-di-yi-li-rentoushui-lgqnequk0rxejqgihwdqxqyqnsvq]] adds the 349-343 BCE cluster. The episode preserves blank years, reads [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]]'s tax reform as likely [[PollTax|poll tax]], uses [[ZhaoFan|赵范]]'s "袭" notice for [[MilitaryActionTerminology|word-level war reading]], and treats "诸侯会于京师" and "王致伯于秦" as compact evidence for [[AudienceVsMeetingDiplomacy|royal-audience decline]] and [[ZhouHegemonRecognition|Qin status elevation]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-19-maling-zhi-zhan-lhbapt09x2ptdqx0uopkp8apbada]] adds the 342-341 BCE transition. The episode keeps the blank 342 BCE year visible, then reads [[MalingBattle|马陵之战]] through rescue timing, Wei-Han pressure, Qi's attack toward [[Daliang|大梁]], [[PrinceShenWei|太子申]]'s command, and [[SunziBingfa|《孙子兵法》]]'s warning about pursuit and forced marches.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-20-pangjuan-si-ci-shu-xia-ls-8-3jhz5q1c5ca11mhdzmz4wg4]] completes the same Maling passage through the final ambush. The episode reads the chronicle's story as a sequence from [[DecreasingStovesDeception|reduced stoves]] to rapid pursuit, night ambush, [[PangJuan|庞涓]]'s suicide, Wei disorder, Qi victory, and [[PrinceShenWei|太子申]]'s capture, while still noting that the famous tree inscription carries some dramatic shaping.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-21-zouji-xia-yinzhao-tianji-guowai-binan-lkvmjoxolgfgw-ig7ir-ngvjifij]] moves from battlefield outcome to political aftershock. The episode uses the chronicle's "欲行事" wording, contrasts it with [[Shiji|《史记》]] and [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]], and reads [[ZouJi|成侯邹忌]]'s planted divination accusation against [[TianJi|田忌]] as a case where a compact phrase can encode treason suspicion and ruler-minister distrust.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-22-gongsunyang-zha-wei-gongzi-ang-lss1l7lojfwgwdtcje7m5ijodpxi]] continues into Zhou Xian Wang's twenty-ninth year, 340 BCE. The episode notes the brief Qi-Zhao and Chu succession entries but centers the chronicle's fuller [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]] passage: Qin's strategic case against [[WeiState|魏国]], Shang Yang's personal command, and the capture of [[GongziAng|公子卬 / 公子昂]] through false peace.
