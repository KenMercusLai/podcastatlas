# Sarah Edwards

## Overview
Sarah Edwards is the media-studies professor interviewed by [[MarketplaceTech]] about influencer intrusion into remote communities, engagement incentives, moderation limits, and user responses to harmful content.

## Current Profile
The bounded source presents Edwards as an analyst of the gap between ethical judgment and platform measurement. She argues that systems registering clicks, comments, views, and reaction do not necessarily distinguish approval from condemnation, allowing critical attention to widen the reach of [[ExploitativeInfluencerContent]]. Her practical recommendation is to report or block clear violations and otherwise avoid interaction that rewards the post.

## Key Characteristics
- Interprets influencer exploitation through platform incentives rather than individual misconduct alone.
- Connects "poverty porn" and performed generosity to unequal relationships between creators and filmed communities.
- Emphasizes that engagement metrics compress ethically different audience responses into similar activity signals.
- Treats both human and automated moderation as limited by speed, scale, and missing context.
- Recommends deliberate non-engagement, reporting, and blocking as user-side responses.
