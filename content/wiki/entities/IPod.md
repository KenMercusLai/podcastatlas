# iPod

iPod appears in [[we-almost-had-a-smartphone-in-the-90s-why-did-it-fail]] as [[Apple]]'s disciplined contrast to [[GeneralMagic]]. The episode says [[TonyFadell]] brought the lessons of General Magic into the portable MP3-player project after [[SteveJobs]] greenlit it in March 2001 while Apple was financially constrained.

The source frames iPod around a clear customer desire: carrying digital music everywhere. Instead of building every part from scratch, Fadell looked for existing processors, software, batteries, screens, hard drives, and interface ideas, making iPod a case for [[BuildVsBorrowProductStrategy]].

iPod also extends [[ProductLaunchUnderConstraint]]. The team worked toward a Christmas deadline, with smaller milestones and competitive pressure from [[Sony]], then iterated through many later models before the path widened toward [[IPhone]].
