## Overview

田肯 / Tian Ken is an early [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]] adviser whose recorded appearance in this wiki links [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s capture of [[HanXin|韩信]] to a geographic and kinship-based argument about who should rule [[QiState|齐国]].

## Current Profile

Tian Ken congratulates Liu Bang on capturing Han Xin and on holding [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]], whose passes and river-mountain defenses give the new center strategic depth. He then describes Qi as the eastern counterpart to Qin: wealthy, extensive, defensible, and capable of large mobilization. His policy conclusion is that a territory of this weight should not be entrusted to an unrelated king; only the emperor's son or brother should rule it.

The episode treats this intervention as an early rationale for [[SameSurnameKingEnfeoffment|同姓王分封]]. It also speculates that Tian Ken may descend from the Qi Tian lineage because of his surname, local knowledge, and concern for Qi's disposition. That ancestry remains unverified within the bounded source.

## Key Characteristics

- Frames political appointments through strategic geography rather than reward alone.
- Treats Qi as an eastern power base comparable in importance to Qin's western heartland.
- Recommends close imperial kinship as the eligibility boundary for ruling a high-risk kingdom.
- Appears briefly but receives a five-hundred-jin gold reward for advice Liu Bang accepts.
- Has a possible Qi Tian-lineage background that remains source-scoped speculation.

## Qualifications

The current profile rests on one podcast source and one brief historical appearance. It does not independently establish Tian Ken's ancestry, official career, later life, exact influence on subsequent grants, or sole authorship of a durable Han rule. His intervention is best treated as an early articulation of an emerging settlement logic.
