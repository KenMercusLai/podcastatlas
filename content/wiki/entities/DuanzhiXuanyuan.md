# 断指轩辕 / Duanzhi Xuanyuan

## Overview

断指轩辕 is [[LiDazui|李大嘴]]'s mother and a legendary gambling expert in [[WulinWaizhuan|《武林外传》]]. The bounded source focuses on how she uses that expertise to teach [[BaiZhantang|白展堂]] and her son not to gamble.

## Current Profile

[[110-wulin-waizhuan-xiaodu-yiqing-zuihaode-jiedu-xuanchuanpian-lor4w6cimesyf4dlwgr5ywfgwgxb]] presents her as both protective parent and strict moral authority. She refuses to let Li Dazui escape punishment as a witness because she wants him to bear responsibility. When punishment leaves Bai's reasoning intact, she changes method: she wins back the transferred goods, offers repeated stopping points, and lets Bai reveal that victory rather than amusement governs his play.

Her final lesson uses overwhelming skill, fear, and mercy in sequence. After allowing the wager to rise to Bai's life, she stages the threatened loss of his hand, extracts recognition that the first bet created the entire chain, and then cancels every debt. The late revelation that she is the famous “断指轩辕,” with an original sixth finger accounting for the name, also completes the story's compact misdirection.

## Key Characteristics

- Combines maternal care with insistence that her son accept punishment.
- Uses gambling expertise against gambling rather than presenting skill as a route to safe participation.
- Restores goods first, then tests whether Bai can stop once accounts are even.
- Repeatedly states the value of the wages and labor Bai is risking.
- Uses a staged severe consequence to convert abstract warning into personal recognition.
- Cancels the debts after Bai commits to abstinence, revealing education rather than enrichment as her aim.
- Supplies the final identity and six-finger reversal that pays off the “断指” legend.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to one story and a podcast's reconstruction of it. Her ability to target one player, split dice through internal force, and produce nineteen points are theatrical devices, not realistic gambling instruction. Whether fear is an effective or appropriate intervention outside the comedy is not established by the source.

## What Changed

- Created a bounded profile of her combined parental, punitive, and educational authority.
- Identified debt cancellation as evidence that her gambling performance serves correction rather than gain.
- Connected the anti-gambling lesson to the story's final identity and bodily-assumption reversal.
