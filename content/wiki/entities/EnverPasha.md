# Enver Pasha

## Overview

Enver Pasha enters the wiki through [[675-the-first-world-war-slaughter-at-gallipoli-part-5-glt4394132715]] as an Ottoman leader whose German alignment and Caucasus offensive form part of the prehistory of the [[GallipoliCampaign|Gallipoli campaign]].

## Current Profile

The episode uses Enver to connect Ottoman strategy to Allied decision-making. His attraction to Germany helped orient the [[OttomanEmpire|Ottoman Empire]] toward the Central Powers, and his disastrous Sarikamish campaign against Russia created the context for Russia's request that Britain make a diversion against the Ottomans.

The source also links the defeat's aftermath to the Young Turk leadership blaming Armenians, placing Enver in a dark prelude to the Armenian genocide. That claim remains source-scoped here because the page is not yet a full treatment of late Ottoman politics or genocide.

## Key Characteristics

- Enver Pasha appears as a leading Ottoman figure drawn toward Germany in 1914.
- His alignment mattered because Ottoman fear of Russia and German influence shaped entry into the war.
- He led a large Caucasus offensive against Russia that ended in catastrophic losses.
- The Sarikamish disaster helped trigger Russia's request for a British diversion against the Ottomans.
- The episode connects the defeat's blame politics to the prehistory of the Armenian genocide.

## Evidence

- German alignment: [[675-the-first-world-war-slaughter-at-gallipoli-part-5-glt4394132715]] says Enver was especially drawn to Germany as Ottoman leaders considered their options in 1914.
- Sarikamish disaster: [[675-the-first-world-war-slaughter-at-gallipoli-part-5-glt4394132715]] says Enver led around 100,000 men against Russia and that cold, disease, and poor conditions devastated the Ottoman army.
- Gallipoli precondition: [[675-the-first-world-war-slaughter-at-gallipoli-part-5-glt4394132715]] says Russia's request for a diversion after the Caucasus crisis gave Churchill an opening for the Dardanelles plan.
