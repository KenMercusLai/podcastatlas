# Wendy Whitman Cobb

Wendy Whitman Cobb appears in [[tech-20260818-tech-pod-128-tech-20260818-tech-pod-128]] as a space-policy scholar emphasizing the scarcity of viable U.S. launch geography. After [[DanielAckerman]] lays out the criteria for launch sites, Cobb says very few areas in the [[UnitedStates]] still meet those combined requirements.

Her contribution is to make [[SpaceportSitingConstraint]] a structural bottleneck rather than an ordinary permitting delay. Even if the [[FederalAviationAdministration|FAA]] reduces regulatory burdens, [[SpaceLaunchCapacityBottleneck|launch capacity]] remains bounded by geography, population exposure, ports, airspace, and environmental sensitivity.
