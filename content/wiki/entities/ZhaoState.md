# 赵国 / Zhao State

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-04-yurang-wei-zhiyao-fuchou-ln34tydha6iqwlrgmw7iugqfgfik]] adds Zhao's pre-recognition branch through [[ZhaoXiangzi|赵襄子]]. Zhao helps end [[ZhiYao|智瑶]]'s power after Jinyang, absorbs [[DaiState|代国]] through kinship deception, survives [[YuRang|豫让]]'s revenge attempts, and then moves through a contested succession involving [[ZhaoBolu|赵伯鲁]]'s line, [[ZhaoHuanzi|赵桓子]], and [[ZhaoXianzi|赵献子]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-05-guangcai-duomu-de-weiwenhou-1-lmkxzbmwhw3nhurape-eebydqcdl]] adds Zhao to [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]]'s diplomacy case. Zhao asks [[WeiState|魏国]] to join an attack on [[HanState|韩国]], but Wei refuses on brother-state grounds; when Zhao learns Wei had also refused Han's anti-Zhao request, the symmetry turns anger into trust.

赵国 / Zhao State appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-1-lvkp-ju7pf5z5cz3thojv3s2fkyx]] through two early Warring States notices. First, Zhao joins [[HanState|韩国]] and [[WeiState|魏国]] in a joint attack on [[ChuState|楚国]]. Second, [[ZhaoLiehou|赵烈侯]] dies and Zhao people install his younger brother [[ZhaoWuhou|赵武侯]].

The page is source-scoped. Zhao's wiki role here is to anchor the episode's claim that the first generation of post-Jin powers was already moving through interstate war and succession pressure at the same time.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-2-lvaqlgyccrugfo0l-wuwf1mt1kcm]] adds a second Zhao succession: Zhao Wuhou dies in 387 BCE, and Zhao people install [[ZhaoLiehou|赵烈侯]]'s son [[ZhaoJinghou|赵靖侯]]. The episode also notes a Zhao prince's later revolt, flight to [[WeiState|魏国]], and failed attack on Zhao's newly moved capital, keeping Zhao tied to succession instability.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-3-ltca-ikuzo9ej7a0lfrgwv2-wulb]] adds a terse 383 BCE war notice: [[WeiState|魏国]] attacks Zhao and defeats Zhao at Tutai. The episode does not force a motive, but uses the notice to show that Three Jin solidarity has already become unstable interstate rivalry.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl]] makes Zhao a main actor in [[EarlyWarringStatesInterstateWar]]. Zhao first joins Han and Wei against Qi, then attacks [[WeiState|魏国]], is pressed by Wei and Qi together, seeks help from [[ChuState|楚国]], and later joins Han and Wei in ending [[JinState|晋国]]. Because the transcript's ruler name is unstable in the war narration, this page keeps the added role state-level.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] adds [[ZhaoJinghou|赵靖侯]]'s death in 375 BCE and [[ZhaoChenghou|赵成侯]]'s accession. It then shows Zhao acting aggressively early in the new reign by attacking [[WeiState|魏国]] and taking seventy-three "du-bi" settlements before Wei wins at Beiling.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-2-lmnqfjmydygqnfec6xhzpixralvi]] continues Zhao's aggressive role in the next year cluster. The source says Zhao attacks [[QiState|齐国]] as far as Juan, while [[WeiState|魏国]] defeats Zhao at Huai.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-3-lmhj4le2mwu1yhhl-qmuoqkfmhjf]] makes Zhao a direct outside patron in Wei's succession fight. [[GongzhongHuan|公中缓]] seeks Zhao help, and [[ZhaoChenghou|赵成侯]] joins [[HanYihou|韩懿侯]] to defeat Wei at [[ZhuozeBattle|浊泽之战]]. Zhao's preferred settlement would install Gongzhong Huan and take land, but Han's partition plan prevents agreement.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-11-shijie-fengqi-yunyong-1-lt60b2gddiosf5kade35uopwohi5]] adds Zhao's opportunistic and anti-Qin roles under [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]]. Zhao attacks [[QiState|齐国]] in 368 BCE and takes a Qi-built wall or fortification line; later, when [[QinState|秦国]] attacks [[WeiState|魏国]] at Shaoliang in 363 BCE, Zhao sends troops to help Wei and appears to stop Qin from taking the place.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-14-qiweiwang-buming-zeyi-yiming-jingren-lrs6rbifyiqrojxg0mccpg7sf9rv]] adds Zhao to the 356 BCE meeting cluster. Zhao meets [[YanState|燕国]] at Ajuan, then joins [[QiState|齐国]] and [[SongState|宋国]] at Pinglu. The source uses these notices as part of a dense [[EarlyWarringStatesInterstateWar|early Warring States]] diplomatic field rather than as settled alliance structure.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-16-sunbin-pangjuan-enyuan-jiuge-guiling-zhi-zhan-lryhzkc1-xsvfjhvicu8yxa9d2jr]] adds Zhao as the endangered state in the idiom source. [[WeiState|魏国]] besieges [[Handan|邯郸]], Zhao seeks help from [[QiState|齐国]], and [[SunBin|孙膑]]'s indirect rescue plan turns Zhao's crisis into [[GuilingBattle|桂陵之战]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-17-hanzhaohou-he-shenbuhai-lghjmjhq9qs4se96c5ofgz6caqof]] adds Zhao's post-Handan repair and next succession problem. [[WeiState|魏国]] returns [[Handan|邯郸]] and joins Zhao in the [[ZhangshuiAlliance|漳水之盟]] because [[QinState|秦国]] has become the stronger shared threat. The source then records [[ZhaoChenghou|赵成侯]]'s death, [[GongziXie|公子泄]]'s failed challenge, and the crown prince's accession as [[ZhaoSuhou|赵肃侯]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-18-zhongguo-lishishang-di-yi-li-rentoushui-lgqnequk0rxejqgihwdqxqyqnsvq]] adds the next aftershock of Zhao's succession disorder. In 347 BCE [[ZhaoFan|赵范]] attacks [[Handan|邯郸]] by surprise, fails, and is killed. The source treats the "袭" wording as [[MilitaryActionTerminology|evidence of a surprise attack]] aimed at the newly established [[ZhaoSuhou|赵肃侯]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-30-suqin-de-kaigua-rensheng-lkmn-fruukdxgkddx7zb4lc3o9bw]] adds Zhao as both an initial failure and the next diplomatic target in [[SuQin|苏秦]]'s biography. A Zhao chief minister first refuses him, but after [[YanWengong|燕文公]] funds Su Qin, Zhao becomes the state Yan needs to court because it blocks [[QinState|秦国]] from reaching [[YanState|燕国]]. The episode uses that mission to state the [[CommonEnemyAlliance|common-enemy alliance]] principle.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-31-hezong-lianheng-weiou-qin-lncpgmxrojc0y1meg9a3ul-o9nih]] makes Zhao the staging state for the broader [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] plan. Su Qin persuades [[ZhaoSuhou|赵肃侯]] that Zhao's strength will not save it if Qin first defeats Han and Wei, and Zhao Suhou gives him the mandate to organize the six-state anti-Qin alliance.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-35-lishishang-diyi-qibing-jiaofu-shi-ta-lnxgnu1czkl-ftfb3gq9kzxwnxdk]] adds Zhao's next succession turn. [[ZhaoSuhou|赵肃侯]] dies in 326 BCE, and [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]] succeeds. The new ruler creates advisory and fault-correction posts, honors [[FeiYi|肥义]], and thereby makes Zhao a case of [[NewRulerReformSignaling|new-ruler reform signaling]] before a later military reform branch.
