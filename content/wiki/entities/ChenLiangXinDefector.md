## Overview

陈良 / Chen Liang (Xin defector) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-640-qipa-wangmang-ruhe-chedi-bifan-xiongnu-lhxbx6keibmfe8z6bcsmlpnr6eht|Hanji 640]] as a Western Regions subordinate who joins [[ZhongDaiXinDefector|终带]] and others in killing Wuyi Colonel Diao Hu and defecting to [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] during the early [[XinDynasty|新朝]] frontier crisis.

## Current Profile

The source presents Chen Liang as a fear-driven frontier defector rather than an independent rebel founder. In a setting where Xiongnu pressure and Western Regions instability make Xin authority look fragile, Chen Liang, Zhong Dai, Han Xuan, Ren Shang, and others decide that killing their commander and fleeing to Xiongnu offers survival.

The mutiny is politically amplified after the fact. The group takes more than two thousand officials, families, and dependents toward Xiongnu, and the chanyu gives Chen Liang and Zhong Dai titles. [[SunJianXinGeneral|孙建]] then uses the case as evidence for stronger anti-Han symbolic measures inside the Xin court.

## Key Characteristics

- Xin frontier subordinate whose current role is a mutiny and defection case.
- Acts with Zhong Dai and other officials rather than alone.
- Participates in killing Wuyi Colonel Diao Hu and members of Diao Hu's family.
- Helps move a large mixed group of officials, staff, relatives, and dependents into Xiongnu territory.
- Becomes evidence in Sun Jian's argument for anti-Han symbolic purge.

## Qualifications

The source does not give Chen Liang's full career, personal motives, or later fate. It frames the defection as part of frontier command trust breakdown under Xiongnu pressure, but the exact balance between fear, opportunism, and ideology remains source-scoped.
