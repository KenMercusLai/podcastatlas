# 鹧鸪哨 / Zhegu Shao

## Overview

鹧鸪哨 is a 扎格拉玛 descendant, the last major 搬山道人 of his period, and Shirley 杨's maternal grandfather in [[GhostBlowsOutTheLight|《鬼吹灯》]]. The bounded episode presents him as a capable but unsuccessful seeker of the [[MuchenPearl|雮尘珠]] whose losses and preserved clues pass the family mission to the next generation.

## Current Profile

His competence combines firearms, agility, ventriloquism, tomb knowledge and improvisation, yet his defining quality in the initiation story is not invincibility. During 了尘长老's South Song tomb test, several constraints fail at once; he protects the candle, prevents a corpse transformation, redirects a cat and retrieves the required garment near cockcrow. He then reports the timing honestly even though concealment might secure recognition.

His pursuit remains tragic. Learning 摸金 methods expands his ability to locate tombs, but the Black Water City expedition yields no 雮尘珠. A toxic black swarm costs him an arm, 了尘长老 dies, and 金算盘 cannot be found. Migration to the United States delays the curse's effect without curing the family, so survival and failure coexist in his legacy.

## Key Characteristics

- Treats tomb exploration as a collective survival mission rather than ordinary treasure seeking.
- Combines 搬山 force and craft with learned 摸金 location methods.
- Solves layered constraints through observation and improvisation.
- Gains trust by reporting an ambiguous rule outcome honestly.
- Pays lasting bodily and relational costs without completing his quest.
