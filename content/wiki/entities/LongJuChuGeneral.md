# 龙且 / Long Ju

龙且 / Long Ju enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-159-1-liubang-xijiao-de-yuren-zhishu-lga3puexunjzeu3s8pvt8saaa8n3|Hanji 159-1]] as a [[XiangYu|项羽]]-side general whose counterattack limits the immediate payoff from [[YingBu|英布]]'s defection. After Ying Bu flees to [[LiuBang|刘邦]], Long Ju defeats the remaining Jiujiang force and restores Chu control over [[JiujiangChuHan|九江国]].

The episode then moves Long Ju north toward the central front. His support strengthens Xiang Yu around the [[XingyangStandoff|荥阳相持]], where Chu captures [[Aocang|敖仓]], controls Yellow River grain transport, and repeatedly attacks Han's supply corridor. Long Ju's role therefore links the Jiujiang defection branch back to the material pressure on Liu Bang at Xingyang and Chenggao.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-160-chenping-gongxinji-bi-zhenhuanchuan-jingcai-1-lujcothn8aunydmznta1tkglqt-q|Hanji 160 part 1]] names Long Ju as one of the few upright and reliable Chu-side figures [[ChenPing|陈平]] believes still matter around [[XiangYu|项羽]]. The episode does not give Long Ju a new action, but it makes his trusted-core status part of the target field for Chen Ping's [[CounterintelligenceRumorWedge|反间流言楔入]].
