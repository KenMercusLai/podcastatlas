# Bernadine Healy

## Overview

Bernadine Healy was a physician, medical leader, and the first woman to direct the [[NationalInstitutesOfHealth|National Institutes of Health]]. The episode presents her as a direct institutional reformer who joined women's health and research access to a defense of fairness for scientists accused of misconduct.

## Current Profile

Healy's profile combines personal resistance to professional sexism with procedural reform. After advancing through medicine when women faced quotas and presumptions of lesser commitment, she confronted a sexually degrading all-male club at Johns Hopkins despite warnings that speaking up would damage her career. At NIH, she pressed for women in clinical trials, attention to minority participation, broader access to science, and budget growth.

Her defining role in the episode is to stop misconduct enforcement from treating accusation as adjudication. After recognizing [[RameshSharma]]'s case as part of a wider pattern, she withstood [[JohnDingell]]'s 1991 hearing, established appeal rights, and informed Sharma that the disputed protein name had been judged a typographical error.
