# 冯道 / Feng Dao

Feng Dao / 冯道 is the literati official used in [[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]] to discuss service across multiple regimes in [[FiveDynastiesTenKingdoms|五代十国]]. The episode treats his career as normal within a world where upper-level regime turnover often did not mean every official could or should die for a fallen ruler.

Feng Dao becomes controversial because [[OuyangXiu|欧阳修]] later judges him through a stricter Song loyalty ethic in [[NewFiveDynastiesHistory|《新五代史》]]. The source uses that contrast to separate Five Dynasties survival logic from later moral memory.
