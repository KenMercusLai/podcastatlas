# 金子才 / Jin Zicai

Jin Zicai appears in [[175-gongmu-jijin-erjibao-jizhi-de-baotuan-yu-gelie-zhihou-lv20uprxrcw0tde5ugohu29tmlg6]] as the Caitong Fund manager whose asset scale rose sharply during the 2026 technology-growth cycle. The source uses him as a main example of a new active-fund star formed by AI, compute, and semiconductor holdings.

His quoted framework is more verification-oriented than pure theme enthusiasm. The source says he read North American hyperscaler capex, stronger reasoning models, AI Coding adoption, and model-company ARR as evidence that AI moved from theme investing into fundamental validation, while also warning that upstream tight links can become more volatile.

## Key Claims
- Jin Zicai is linked to the new-generation manager rotation inside [[ActiveFundCrowding|active fund crowding]].
- His framework emphasizes stepwise confirmation, moving from capex and model capability to application demand and then upstream material shortages.
- The source says he reduced exposure after [[DeepSeek]] disrupted overseas compute-capex expectations, then became more positive again after AI Coding and ARR growth.
