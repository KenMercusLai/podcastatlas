# Josef Hoyos

## Overview

Josef Hoyos is the count who arrives at Mayerling before the deaths and later carries the news back toward the Hofburg.

## Current Profile

Hoyos matters as a discovery-and-notification figure. He sees [[CrownPrinceRudolf]] alive during the hunting-party setup, leaves before the fatal night, then rushes back to [[Vienna]] after the bodies are found but cannot directly tell [[FranzJosephI]].

## Key Characteristics

- Hoyos appears in the episode as part of the hunting-cover setting at Mayerling.
- His arrival helps establish that Rudolf is publicly maintaining ordinary plans just before the deaths.
- His later trip to Vienna begins the court's news-management sequence.
- His inability to tell Franz Joseph directly moves [[EmpressSisi]] into the notification role.

## Evidence

- Hunting setup: [[690-a-murderous-affair-death-at-mayerling-part-2-glt7203783068]] says Hoyos and [[PhilipOfCoburg]] arrive at Mayerling for hunting and find Rudolf alive.
- Public routine: [[690-a-murderous-affair-death-at-mayerling-part-2-glt7203783068]] has Rudolf use illness and a telegram to manage expectations while Mary remains hidden.
- News relay: [[690-a-murderous-affair-death-at-mayerling-part-2-glt7203783068]] says Hoyos rushes back to Vienna and cannot bring himself to tell the emperor directly.

## Qualifications

The source does not make Hoyos responsible for the deaths or the suppression strategy. His role is bounded to observation and notification.
