# Shanda Box / 盛大盒子

Shanda Box / 盛大盒子 is the home-entertainment device and strategy discussed in [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]]. [[ChenTianqiao]] and [[Shanda|盛大]] positioned it as an interactive entertainment center, hoping to connect games, video, literature, music, and other digital content through the living-room screen.

The episode treats the box as a clean example of [[FounderVisionTimingMismatch]]. The strategic premise anticipated later smart-TV, IPTV, and content-platform competition, but the product faced high prices, weak broadband, unclear user demand, copyright and broadcast-license constraints, and a dependence on partnerships that the market was not ready to absorb.
