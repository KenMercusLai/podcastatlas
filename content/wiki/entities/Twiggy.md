# Twiggy

## Overview

Twiggy, born Leslie Hornby, is the working-class London teenager whose 1966 modeling breakthrough made her a defining face of Swinging London.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Twiggy as a concentration of class mobility, teenage purchasing culture, media manufacture, and the period's waif-like feminine ideal. Raised in Neasden, she spent much of her Saturday hairdressing wages on clothes; encouragement from Justin de Villeneuve and a Daily Express feature on 23 February 1966 then helped turn her into “the face of 66.” Her success demonstrates youth agency and mobility while also exposing the narrow body and age ideal celebrated by the fashion system.

## Key Characteristics

- Born Leslie Hornby and raised in a respectable working-class Neasden household.
- Participated in youth consumer culture through music, television, dancing, and clothing purchases.
- Entered modeling with encouragement from Justin de Villeneuve.
- Achieved national visibility through the Daily Express's “face of 66” feature.
- Embodied both class mobility and an extremely youthful, thin fashion ideal.
