# Recursor

Recursor is the earlier company/product direction behind [[FeatherlessAI|Featherless AI]] in [[featherless-ai-when-your-weekend-experiment-makes-more-than-your-startup]]. The source says Recursor worked around the [[RWKV]] open-source project and let users fine-tune RWKV models with their own data.

Recursor matters in the wiki as the pre-pivot case. The team built [[GPUHotSwapping]] because customers were creating many fine-tuned models and the company could not afford dedicated [[GPU]] capacity for every model. That internal resource constraint later became reusable infrastructure for serving [[Llama]] and [[MistralAI|Mistral]] models.
