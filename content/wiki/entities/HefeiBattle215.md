# 合肥之战 / Battle of Hefei (215)

## Overview
合肥之战 / Battle of Hefei (215) is the source's campaign in which [[ZhangLiao|张辽]] and [[LiDian|李典]] use a small preemptive assault to disrupt [[SunQuan|孙权]]'s much larger force before a failed siege of [[HefeiLateHan|合肥]], then exploit a vulnerable withdrawal near Xiaoyao Ford.

## Current Profile
While [[CaoCao|曹操]] campaigns against Zhang Lu in 215 CE, Sun Quan attacks Hefei. Cao Cao's sealed instructions divide the garrison's roles: Zhang Liao and Li Dian attack, [[LeJinLateHan|乐进]] holds the city, and Xue Ti stays out of combat. Zhang Liao reads the relief delay and enemy assembly window as reasons to attack immediately rather than absorb the first blow behind the walls.

Zhang Liao and Li Dian reportedly lead eight hundred selected troops into the Wu camp. The action does not destroy the besieging army, but it changes expectations: Wu momentum weakens while the defenders gain confidence. Sun Quan later withdraws after unsuccessful assaults and epidemic pressure; Zhang Liao pursues through the resulting gap, nearly trapping the ruler before [[GuLiLateHan|谷利]] helps him cross a damaged bridge and [[HeQiLateHan|贺齐]] receives him.

## Key Characteristics
- Asymmetric defense built around timing and morale rather than matching force totals.
- Delayed contingency order that assigns attack, defense, and nonparticipation roles before the crisis.
- Cooperation between Zhang Liao and Li Dian despite reported personal friction.
- Initial shock attack followed by fortress defense rather than continuous open battle.
- Withdrawal vulnerability that turns an unsuccessful siege into a near-capture of the attacking ruler.
