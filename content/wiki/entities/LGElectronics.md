# LG Electronics

## Overview
LG Electronics is the hardware partner the source says is working with [[Apple]] on smart-home devices designed to operate with Apple's forthcoming home hub.

## Current Profile
The source describes a division of labor in which doorbells, thermostats, and related products would carry LG branding, while LG would handle manufacturing and after-sales service and Apple would participate in design and development oversight. The arrangement is presented as an ecosystem-extension report, not a launched product line.

## Key Characteristics
- Consumer-electronics manufacturer used as Apple's reported smart-home hardware partner.
- Expected to carry manufacturing and service responsibility.
- Reported devices retain LG branding while receiving Apple design input.
