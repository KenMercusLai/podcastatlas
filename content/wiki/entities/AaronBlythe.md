# Aaron Blythe

Aaron Blythe is the [[DataScienceWithSam]] guest in [[ep-7-data-science-mlops]]. The source identifies him as a [[GoogleCloud|Google Cloud]] customer engineer with a background in healthcare application development, DevOps, DevOps community organizing, and machine-learning interest.

Aaron's main contribution to the wiki is a practical map of how [[MLOps]], [[MachineLearningEngineering]], and [[DataEngineeringForDataScience]] fit around data science. He argues that data scientists need curiosity, math, programming, research-reading habits, and enough [[DataScientistMLOpsFluency]] to work well with data engineers and ML engineers.

The episode uses his DevOps background to connect [[DevOpsCALMS]], [[MLCICD]], [[ProductionMLFeedbackLoops]], and [[IntegratedMLTeams]]. Aaron's stance is not that every data scientist should become an operations specialist; it is that production ML needs shared language, automation, measurement, and feedback loops so models can keep improving after deployment.
