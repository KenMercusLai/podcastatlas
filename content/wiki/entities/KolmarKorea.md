# Kolmar Korea / 韩国科玛

## Overview

Kolmar Korea is presented as a Korean beauty research and manufacturing company that supports brands from product development through production and overseas-market compliance.

## Key Characteristics

- Integrated OEM/ODM and broader development capability for beauty brands.
- Reusable technical libraries that can accelerate samples and launches.
- Research into local preferences, regulations, stability, and transport conditions.
- Experience with technically complex makeup and skincare formats.
- Upstream position whose capabilities can persist across changing consumer brands.
