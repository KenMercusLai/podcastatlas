# Fabrizio Benedetti

## Overview
Fabrizio Benedetti is the placebo researcher whose book and Parkinson's disease work provide a major evidence frame for [[how-placebo-effects-work-to-change-our-biology-psychology-scim2208611274]].

## Current Profile
In the current source, Benedetti represents a mechanistic approach to [[PlaceboNoceboExpectationEffects]]: placebo responses are studied through symptoms and measurable neural chemistry rather than treated as mere mistaken reporting. The episode uses Parkinson's research to argue that expectation can influence dopamine release while remaining weaker and less reliable than active treatment.

## Key Characteristics
- Studies placebo and nocebo effects as biological and clinical phenomena.
- Provides the episode's main scholarly frame through a cited book on placebo effects.
- Is associated here with Parkinson's studies linking inert treatment to symptom change and measured dopamine release.
- Supports a bounded interpretation in which expectation can recruit real pathways without duplicating every drug effect.
