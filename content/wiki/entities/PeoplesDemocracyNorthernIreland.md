# People's Democracy (Northern Ireland)

People's Democracy appears in [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] as the radical student civil-rights group that launched a Belfast-to-Derry march on New Year's Day 1969. The episode names [[BernadetteDevlin]] among its leading figures and says [[TerenceONeill|Terence O'Neill]] and other civil-rights figures warned that the march would worsen the crisis.

At [[BurntolletBridgeAttack|Burntollet]], loyalists, including off-duty [[BSpecials|B Specials]], attacked the marchers while the [[RoyalUlsterConstabulary|RUC]] stood by. The source treats the march and the attack as paired examples of [[ProvocationPublicityPolitics]] and sectarian escalation.
