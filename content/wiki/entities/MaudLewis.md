# Maud Lewis / 莫娣

Maud Lewis appears in [[ep265-dang-qing3xiu13-chongshang-resou-women-ruhe-gei-rensheng-fang-yige-changjia-lsloczlnkmcrrfpn9coh8rrqo-wb]] as one of the cultural entry points into [[Canada]]. [[LeiTao|雷涛 / 无二旅人]] describes seeing her living space and artwork in [[Halifax]] and recommends the film *Maudie* as a way to approach the artist.

Her wiki role in this source is not full art history. She represents the episode's claim that travel becomes richer when a place is entered through a person, room, artwork, and life story rather than only through landscape.
