# Stephen Westaby / 史蒂芬·维斯塔比

Stephen Westaby / 史蒂芬·维斯塔比 is the cardiac surgeon at the center of [[86-dakai-yike-xin-na-meihao-de-zhang-wo-yijing-daguo-le-702323437]] and [[DakayiKexin|《打开一颗心》 / Open Heart]]. The episode presents him as a working-class child born in 1948 who became fixated on heart surgery after seeing heart-lung-machine surgery on television and after watching illness take close family members.

The source's Westaby is not only a technically gifted surgeon. His career is built from repeated encounters with death: a failed mitral-valve operation he watched as a student, a first lead surgery complicated by unexpected bleeding, burned patients who needed custom airway work, the desert-infant case, early artificial-heart patients, Julie's AB180 rescue, and Kirsty's near-hopeless infant operation.

His strongest wiki role is to connect [[HighRiskSurgicalInnovation]] with [[MedicalRiskManagement]]. The episode frames his rule-bending and institutional anger as dangerous but not casual: he works at the boundary where doing nothing, waiting for permission, or optimizing public statistics can also harm patients.
