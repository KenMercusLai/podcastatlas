# 刘宠 / Liu Chong (late Han minister)

## Overview

Liu Chong is the ministerial figure in Hanji 891 who moves from situ to taiwei during the 169 CE Three Excellencies reshuffle.

## Current Profile

This page exists to keep the source's "司徒刘宠" distinct from [[LiuChongChenKing|陈王刘宠]]. Hanji 891 gives only the office movement: Liu Chong is serving as situ and is appointed taiwei in June 169 after Wenren Xi's removal. The page should remain narrow until later evidence connects this official to a broader career.

## Key Characteristics

- Late-Han high official named as situ before the June 169 reshuffle.
- Appointed taiwei after the removal of Wenren Xi.
- Part of the same office-turnover field that elevates Xu Xun and Liu Xiao.
- Disambiguated from the later Chen king Liu Chong because the current source gives a ministerial office context.
