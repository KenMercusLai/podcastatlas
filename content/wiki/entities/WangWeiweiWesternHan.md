## Overview

王卫尉 / Wang is the Western Han palace-security official who persuades [[LiuBang|刘邦]] to release [[XiaoHe|萧何]] after the chancellor is imprisoned over a proposal to let people cultivate unused imperial-park land.

## Current Profile

The episode identifies him only by surname and office. His intervention joins constitutional role, past conduct, and historical warning: a chancellor should request policies that benefit the people; Xiao He had much stronger opportunities for betrayal while controlling [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] during wartime; and [[LiSi|李斯]]'s management of praise and blame did not save Qin.

The remonstrance succeeds in the narrow institutional sense that Liu Bang releases Xiao He that day. It does not erase the hierarchy that produced the imprisonment, because Xiao He still returns barefoot to apologize and the ruler reframes the reversal without directly admitting error.

## Key Characteristics

- Identified by the source as a Wang-surnamed 卫尉 rather than by a secure personal name.
- Questions the legal and political basis for suddenly imprisoning the chancellor.
- Defines advocacy for public benefit as part of a true chancellor's office.
- Uses Xiao He's long wartime control of Guanzhong to test the plausibility of late, petty corruption.
- Rejects Li Si's blame-management model by connecting it to Qin's refusal to hear its own faults.

## Evidence

Official-duty argument:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-190-xiaohe-ruyu-lggvu7f-6lodqnb1eqnmdmq5c3gm|Hanji 190]] attributes to Wang the claim that a chancellor ought to request what benefits the people.

Conduct-based defense:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-190-xiaohe-ruyu-lggvu7f-6lodqnb1eqnmdmq5c3gm|Hanji 190]] says Wang points to Xiao He's Guanzhong authority during Liu Bang's campaigns as evidence against the alleged motive.

Successful correction:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-190-xiaohe-ruyu-lggvu7f-6lodqnb1eqnmdmq5c3gm|Hanji 190]] says Liu Bang feels shame and orders Xiao He's release that day.

## Qualifications

The source supplies no secure given name, detailed career, or independent account of Wang's later life. His exact title translation, the wording of the exchange, and Liu Bang's internal reaction remain episode-attributed.
