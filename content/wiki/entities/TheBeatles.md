# The Beatles / 披头士

## Overview
The Beatles / 披头士 enter the wiki through [[BecauseSong|"Because"]], which [[RenNing]] reads beside [[LuoDayou|罗大佑]]'s [[SuoyiSong|《所以》]] as a song that uses a logical connective poetically rather than argumentatively.

## Current Profile
The source treats the band as a canonical musical reference point rather than a full biographical subject. "Because" supplies the earlier half of the episode's because/so comparison: a 1969 song where world, wind, sky, and feeling are connected by "because" without becoming strict causal proof.

The episode also refuses a simplistic old-world/new-world contrast. It notes that 1969 was not innocent: the Vietnam War, the Cold War, and the band's own breakup pressures remain in the background. The contrast is instead about mediation density: "Because" gives a sparse relation between self and world, while 《所以》 puts many technological and systemic mediators between human beings and the world.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents the earlier musical text in the episode's cross-era song comparison.
- Supplies "because" as a poetic rather than logical connector.
- Shows a relatively sparse lyrical world where sky, wind, world, and feeling are directly adjacent.
- Remains historically non-innocent despite the source's contrast with the denser technological world of 《所以》.
- Functions as a classic that later creators can hear differently from a new historical position.
