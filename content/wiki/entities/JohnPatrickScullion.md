# John Patrick Scullion

John Patrick Scullion appears in [[697-the-troubles-murder-in-belfast-part-1-glt4877520736]] as a Catholic man attacked by UVF men on 27 May 1966 after they failed to find a Republican activist. The episode says Scullion later died in hospital.

Scullion's death is part of the source's early loyalist-violence sequence between [[MatildaGould]] and [[PeterWard]]. It shows how anti-IRA rhetoric and preemptive loyalist violence could turn into attacks on civilians chosen because the intended target was unavailable.
