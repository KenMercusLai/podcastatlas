# Varun Sharma

## Overview
Varun Sharma is an Indian actor presented in the current source through his path from Jalandhar, media study, theatre, and casting assistance to screen roles including Chucha in *Fukrey*, Sexa in *Chhichhore*, and Katanni in *Roohi*.

## Current Profile
The bounded account presents Sharma as an actor whose career formation joined craft, information gathering, relationship building, repeated auditions, and family support. His nine-month *Fukrey* process is the clearest example: prior casting-room observation and repeated variations helped him convert an opportunity despite having no earlier comedy experience, but selection remained controlled by a wider production process.

He treats comedy and AI through the same human-centered lens. Comedy emerges from coordinated performers and production departments, while AI can accelerate visualization or language work without replacing lived experience, consent, judgment, or human reaction.
