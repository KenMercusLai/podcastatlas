[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-432-kai-jinian-gongchen-zhi-xianhe-jiemi-qilinge-shiyi-gongchen-lj65dvmawrppq2iomuzarg1ah9ak|Hanji 432]] supplies the title-refusal backstory behind that reputation: Wei resists taking his father's marquisate, even feigning madness, before Xuan forces succession and uses him in office.

## Current Profile

Wei Xuancheng's role remains source-scoped rather than biographical. Hanji 432 explains why later sources can use him as a moral signal: after Wei Xian's death, his family wants the second son Wei Xuancheng to inherit because the elder son has been jailed, but Wei judges that arrangement false to his father's intent and tries to refuse by pretending madness. Xuan rejects impeachment, compels him to inherit, and then appoints him Henan taishou because his high-minded refusal is useful to the court rather than simply private eccentricity.

Hanji 459 then uses that reputation for yielding his own title as a signal: sending him to Huaiyang is read as Xuan's way of teaching Liu Qin to accept his place rather than compete for succession. Hanji 476 later places Wei in a formal personnel sequence as the new Yushi Dafu after disaster-era resignations. Hanji 488 then places him as an incumbent target: Jing Fang's complaint groups him with [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]] and [[WuluChongzongWesternHan|五鹿充宗]] as part of a court environment resistant to [[KaogongfaReformBacklash|kaogong]] assessment.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han senior official named in Jing Fang's criticism.
- Builds an early reputation through refusing or resisting a disputed title succession.
- Appointed Henan taishou after Xuan compels him to inherit the family title.
- Appointed Huaiyang zhongwei in Hanji 459 as a restraint-and-instruction signal to Liu Qin.
- Appointed Yushi Dafu after Xue Guangde resigns in the Yongguang 1 disaster sequence.
- Represents the incumbent-performance side of Hanji 488's reform conflict.

## Qualifications

This page is limited to Hanji 432, Hanji 459, Hanji 476, and Hanji 488 and does not assess Wei Xuancheng's whole career, policy record, or historical reputation. Hanji 432 supplies the title-refusal story through the podcast's retelling; the page does not independently settle Wei Xian's full family situation, the elder brother's case, or the original legal procedure.
