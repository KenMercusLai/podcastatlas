## Overview

Yuan Bi / 元璧 is the palace attendant in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-658-erzi-queshi-buyinggai-wangmang-nusha-taizi-ln4tu6ly8eie6jspvrurzn2rjjp|Hanji 658]] whose relationships with [[WangMang|王莽]] and [[WangLinXinCrownPrince|王林]] create the scandal that ends with Wang Lin's suicide. Her confession under interrogation turns private palace conduct into a dynastic-succession crisis.

## Current Profile

The source identifies Yuan Bi as an attendant of [[EmpressXiaomuXin|孝睦皇后]] and as someone Wang Mang privately favored without publicly making her a consort. Wang Lin's contact with her becomes dangerous because it violates the ruler-father's sexual and household hierarchy.

After Empress Xiaomu dies, Wang Mang arrests Yuan Bi and others for interrogation. Yuan Bi cannot withstand torture and confesses the murder plot, making her the evidentiary hinge through which Wang Mang turns household scandal into Wang Lin's forced death and official disappearance.

## Key Characteristics

- Palace attendant whose position gives her access to the Xin imperial household.
- Woman privately connected to Wang Mang but not publicly acknowledged as a consort.
- Scandal hinge linking Wang Lin's fear, patricide planning, and torture-based confession.
- Source-scoped actor whose agency is hard to separate from coercion and court power.
