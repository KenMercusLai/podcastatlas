# Henry Royce

Henry Royce appears in [[vol-268-liang-ge-lao-si-lai-si-1003563933]] as the engineering side of the early Rolls-Royce partnership. The episode pairs him with [[CharlesRolls]], who brought elite customers and flying enthusiasm, and [[ClaudeJohnson]], who helped turn the partnership into an operating business.

The source uses Royce to connect product quality with brand formation. The early Silver Ghost story is presented as a proof of smoothness and quietness, making engineering reliability part of the luxury claim before Rolls-Royce became a major aircraft-engine supplier.
