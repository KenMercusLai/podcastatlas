# 魁头 / Kuitou (Xianbei)

魁头 / Kuitou enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-920-mingwu-zhege-daoli-caineng-zhuan-daqian-lqmabg3bpyz6ct2v4arx-iun57ws|Hanji 920]] as the nephew-line successor in the [[Xianbei|鲜卑]] leadership branch after [[HeLianXianbei|和连]] dies. The source transcript renders him as "葵头"; this page normalizes him to Kuitou because the succession sequence matches the late-Han Xianbei account.

Kuitou matters because the source turns succession into a frontier-stability issue. He takes leadership while He Lian's son is still young, but when that son later grows up and contests territory with him, the Xianbei following disperses into internal struggle.
