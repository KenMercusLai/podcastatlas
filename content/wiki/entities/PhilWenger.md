# Phil Wenger

## Overview

Phil Wenger is [[ChesterWenger]]'s gay son, whose removal from Mennonite employment and congregational life, later Episcopal belonging, and marriage to Steve supply the personal stakes of [[generous-orthodoxy-generous-orthodoxy|Generous Orthodoxy]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Phil's coming out as a test of whether a community centered on faith, shared life, and reconciliation could include him. After he publicly identified as gay, he lost his church job, was told to confess, and was removed from his congregation without prior consultation. His faith largely collapsed and he expected to remain outside traditional church life.

Phil later formed a lasting relationship with Steve and joined the Episcopal Church after Gene Robinson became its first openly gay bishop. When Pennsylvania legalized same-sex marriage in 2014, Phil and Steve asked Chester to officiate officially, turning familial acceptance into an action that exposed Chester to church sanction.

## Key Characteristics

- Gay son raised within a tightly connected Mennonite family and religious community.
- Church employee and congregant who experienced coming out as vocational, social, and spiritual exclusion.
- Person whose loss of faith shows that doctrinal enforcement can disrupt more than formal membership.
- Long-term partner of Steve and later participant in an affirming Episcopal community.
- Son who asked his father to make family recognition public and legally official through marriage.
