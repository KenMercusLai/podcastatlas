# Trae

## Overview

Trae is the ByteDance AI coding/developer product discussed in [[270-da-chang-yazhu-ai-bangong-feishu-he-dingding-que-xian-chengle-peijue-lmb4dgcgov3mr4cn7cikbghpfro4]] and [[272-cong-feishu-jizuo-dao-agent-youxian-doubao-gongzuo-all-in-one-jinzhui-workbuddy-lqmfcnfkkhoxt440qy26vwpnvswp]] as part of ByteDance's movement from developer-oriented AI tools toward broader work-agent products.

## Current Profile

The bounded sources place Trae at the coding-to-office boundary. Episode 270 mentions Trae as one of the parallel ByteDance AI lines whose existing business inertia complicates a sharp office-agent push. Episode 272 gives the stronger integration story: Eric says the former Stone system, including Trae and [[Coze|扣子]], merged into the Flow system, while Zhong Tai says Trae and Coze were initially more developer-focused before office-agent pressure pushed them closer to work scenarios.

In this source set, Trae matters less as a standalone IDE feature inventory than as a bridge between [[AIProgrammingEngineShift|AI coding]] and [[AIOfficeAgent|AI office agents]]. Trae Work is treated as source-scoped: the episode says Feishu-using teams may choose it or Doubao Work because the surrounding workplace context is already in Feishu.

## Key Characteristics

- ByteDance AI product associated with developer and coding-agent use.
- Part of the Stone-to-Flow integration story in the source.
- Represents the coding-to-office path where developer tools become work-agent infrastructure.
- Has a source-scoped Trae Work branch tied to Feishu-team adoption and model openness.
