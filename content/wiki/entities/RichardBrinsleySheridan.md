# Richard Brinsley Sheridan / 谢里丹

Richard Brinsley Sheridan appears in [[78-xifang-juedou-shi-chujian-ba-weile-rongyu-687731000]] through the episode's story of two duels around a published insult against the singer Linley. The hosts use the case to show how defending another person's reputation could move from correction, withdrawal, and public notice into renewed insult and severe injury.

Sheridan's example is important because it makes [[ProceduralizedViolence]] unstable. The first duel seems to restore reputation without death, but the public handling of the apology becomes a new humiliation and triggers a second duel.
