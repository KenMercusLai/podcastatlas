# Whistler / 惠斯勒

Whistler is the mountain-town extension from [[Vancouver]] in [[ep265-dang-qing3xiu13-chongshang-resou-women-ruhe-gei-rensheng-fang-yige-changjia-lsloczlnkmcrrfpn9coh8rrqo-wb]]. [[LeiTao|雷涛 / 无二旅人]] recommends a first Canada route that enters through Vancouver and follows the Sea to Sky corridor toward Whistler and farther north or east.

In the source, Whistler matters because it makes [[NatureCityProximity|nature-city proximity]] usable. A traveler can leave a major city and quickly enter mountain, hot-spring, lake, waterfall, and national-park routes without treating outdoor travel as a separate expedition category.
