# 杜若 / Du Ruo

杜若 is the author of [[GuwenDeLiliang|《古文的力量》]] and [[QinZong|秦总]]'s former high-school Chinese teacher in [[164-guwen-de-liliang-qing-shou-xia-zhe-fen-qiannian-wei-bian-de-shenqing-dongjian-he-yongqi-940236576]]. QinZong presents her as the teacher who made her fall in love with Chinese and classical prose, then later as the author whose weekly drafts she helped shape as an editor.

The source makes Du Ruo's authority pedagogical rather than purely academic. Her book is described as explaining familiar prose pieces through life situations, feeling, and value judgment instead of only through grammar points, making her central to the episode's [[ClassicalProseReading]] method.
