## Overview

Emperor Yuan of Han / 汉元帝 enters this wiki branch through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-592-handai-chengxiang-shi-ruhe-pingjia-qian-boss-de-lncdt3l6lk1hfxupuxti_dsjh9u5|Hanji 592]] as a precedent inside [[WangJiaWesternHan|王嘉]]'s confidential memorial to [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]].

## Current Profile

The source's use of Emperor Yuan is narrow and rhetorical. Wang Jia praises Yuan as gentle, modest, without strong private greed, and fiscally restrained enough that palace treasuries and state reserves could support disaster relief and military expense during hardship.

The page therefore does not synthesize Yuan's reign as a whole. It records how Wang Jia uses Yuan's remembered frugality to create comparative pressure before a later warning about late-Aidi private favor.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han emperor used as a favorable precedent in Wang Jia's memorial.
- Associated in this source with thrift, restraint, and avoidance of visible private favoritism.
- Presented as leaving enough fiscal reserve to absorb disaster and frontier pressure.
- Functions as a rhetorical benchmark rather than as the source's main subject.

- Reserve capacity: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-592-handai-chengxiang-shi-ruhe-pingjia-qian-boss-de-lncdt3l6lk1hfxupuxti_dsjh9u5|Hanji 592]] says accumulated funds helped cover disaster relief and Xiqiang-related military expense.
- Favor restraint: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-592-handai-chengxiang-shi-ruhe-pingjia-qian-boss-de-lncdt3l6lk1hfxupuxti_dsjh9u5|Hanji 592]] says Wang Jia uses Yuan's reward handling around favored palace women to stress fairness and avoidance of public resentment.

## Qualifications

This page is not a full reign profile for Emperor Yuan. The source is focused on Wang Jia's political rhetoric, so Yuan's complete policies, fiscal numbers, consort politics, and responsibility for Western Han decline remain outside the current bounded evidence.
