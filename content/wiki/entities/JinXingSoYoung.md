# Jin Xing (So-Young) / 金星（新氧）

## Overview

Jin Xing / 金星 is the founder and CEO of [[SoYoung|新氧]] interviewed in [[ep153-ep153]] about moving the company from medical-aesthetic lead generation into directly operated clinics.

## Current Profile

The episode describes Jin as an internet operator whose career included Tom.com, Mop, Renren, Tencent, and two failed startups before entering medical aesthetics in 2013. His current strategic argument is that a platform should enter supply directly when the main failure lies in service incentives and delivery rather than missing information alone.

His proposed solution is deliberately capital-intensive: headquarters control, a narrow treatment menu, centralized procurement, clinician training and assessment, visible workflows, patient feedback, and long-term retention metrics. He does not present it as a universal playbook; smaller operators are advised to choose a narrow position and serve a stable customer base instead of copying a market leader's chain infrastructure.

## Key Characteristics

- Internet-platform background followed by medical-aesthetic entrepreneurship.
- Willingness to replace a lighter traffic model with direct operational responsibility after growth and trust limits became visible.
- Definition of a real chain through headquarters decisions and shared operating infrastructure rather than common branding alone.
- Preference for repeat purchase, customer lifetime value, and unit-model validation over one-time sales.
- Capital conservatism expressed through a stated minimum cash balance despite aggressive long-term expansion goals.
- Conditional strategy that separates leader, challenger, flank, and niche approaches by market position and resources.
- Interest in AI-assisted clinics while retaining a multi-year, regulation-dependent boundary around physician replacement.
