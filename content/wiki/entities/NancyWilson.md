# Nancy Wilson

## Overview

Nancy Wilson is a musician and member of Heart represented as [[CameronCrowe]]'s co-creator of the ten-song cycle for [[BlueSeattle]].

## Current Profile

During her 1986 honeymoon with Crowe, Wilson joined him and her sister [[AnnWilson]] at a small cabin in Cannon Beach, Oregon. The three turned Crowe's interest in [[ElvisPresley]] films into an intensive four- or five-day writing and recording session.

The episode treats the recordings as more than development material. They preserve the energy of a new marriage and a compact creative partnership even though the screenplay was never pitched and the marriage later ended. Wilson's contribution is therefore central to the project's [[UnrealizedCreativeWorkValue]], not incidental background to Crowe's authorship.

## Key Characteristics

- Guitarist and songwriter participating in rapid, close-quarters collaboration.
- Co-created genre-faithful material rather than approaching the Elvis form only as parody.
- Links the project's surviving songs to a specific marriage, place, and creative moment.
- Shares authorship of the realized recordings inside an unrealized film project.

## Qualifications

The source is Crowe and Gladwell's retrospective account, not session logs, multitracks, complete credits, or Wilson's independent account. It establishes collaborative participation but does not precisely allocate composition, arrangement, performance, or production decisions among the three creators.
