# Greg (Scott Page's partner)

Greg appears in [[you-bet-your-life-insurance]] as [[ScottPage]]'s partner and the first case through which the episode explains the human origin of [[ViaticalSettlement|viatical settlements]]. The source says Greg had AIDS, a $100,000 life insurance policy, and a premium the couple could not afford while his health was declining.

Greg's case matters because it starts as care financing rather than investment design. A wealthy backer pays premiums and living expenses so the policy stays active, then is repaid from the death benefit after Greg dies in January 1993.

## Key Claims
- Greg's policy was valuable only if the couple could keep paying premiums.
- The support arrangement turned a future death benefit into present survival and hospice resources.
- The later market abstraction should be read against this first case of illness, household cash pressure, and insufficient outside support.

## Connections
- [[ScottPage]] - partner who turned the first arrangement into a broader model.
- [[ViaticalSettlement]] - transaction structure that grew from similar AIDS-crisis cases.
- [[LifeSettlement]] - later broader market descended from the same policy-sale logic.
- [[InsuranceRiskTransfer]] - existing insurance concept that Greg's case extends into beneficiary transfer and premium rescue.
