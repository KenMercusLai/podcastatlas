# Salma Fotovat

Salma Fotovat is one of the sibling operators behind [[MadeGood]] in [[madegood-salma-and-nima-fotovat-lost-their-first-business-they-grew-their-next-one-into-a-snack-giant-cf5715bf-e9e0-45e0-a8a9-1256a7a4ed0d]]. The episode gives her perspective on the family's move from [[Iran]] to [[Canada]], the sudden loss of [[TasteOfNature]], and the supply-chain and values work behind [[RiversideNaturalFoods]].

## Key Points
- Salma's early exposure to the family business included hands-on factory work such as stickering bars with her sister.
- She was at the office with [[SabaFotovat]] when the 2012 buyout took effect and unfamiliar men arrived to take control.
- In the MadeGood phase, she handled end-to-end supply chain and rebuilt vendor relationships from the previous business.
- The episode presents vendor trust as one of the family's most valuable assets after the loss of [[TasteOfNature]].
- Salma frames the 2024 recall as a values test: the company's broad response showed what safety and trust meant in practice.
