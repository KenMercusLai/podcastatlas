# 考芬 / Kaofen

## Overview
考芬 / Kaofen is presented as a New York University film-studies doctoral student and a host of the podcast “越考越糊,” invited by [[WushichaYanjiusuo|无时差研究所]] to discuss film representations of illness, aging, death, disability, and care.

## Current Profile
In the available source, Kaofen reads cinema through form and social relation together. The discussion connects unreliable narration, broken space and time, sound, music, genre, and endings to dementia subjectivity, migrant-family distance, disclosure ethics, disability dignity, care labor, race, and class. Her approach values what a film helps viewers feel while retaining a boundary between cinematic resolution and real-world care evidence.

## Key Characteristics
- Film-studies commentator joining close formal analysis to social and ethical questions.
- Podcast participant attentive to illness, migration, disability, gendered care, race, and class.
- Interpreter who distinguishes a film's emotional truth from a universal practical prescription.
