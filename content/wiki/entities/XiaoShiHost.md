# 小时

小时 is one of the four women speakers in [[chuantai-ni-chi-xiangcai-ma-nvshengban-ni-yuanyi-he-xingzhuan-zhihou-de-ziji-zai-yiqi-ma-555771723]]. She initially says she would be willing to date a gender-swapped self, not because the idea is narcissistic, but because a highly similar person seems likely to share her values and understand sudden thoughts that previous partners dismissed.

Her strongest relationship criterion is [[SincerityAsRelationshipTrust]]: she names truthfulness, kindness, and courage as qualities she wants in a partner and believes she also has. When asked to argue the opposite side, she gives the episode's clearest risk argument against sameness: two people with the same emotional instability or impulsiveness may collapse or rush into danger together rather than balancing one another.
