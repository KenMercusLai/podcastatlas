## Overview

司马徽 / Sima Hui is a Jingzhou talent evaluator whose recommendation makes [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] and [[PangTong|庞统]] legible to [[LiuBei|刘备]] as unusually valuable advisers.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1083-1084 present Sima Hui as a transmission point between local reputation and political opportunity. Hanji 1083 adds the evaluator-side observation: prolonged conversation with the initially underestimated [[PangTong|庞统]] changes or confirms Sima Hui's judgment before the memorable appraisal circulates. Hanji 1084 then attributes to him the “卧龙、凤雏” recommendation, showing how a trusted evaluator can move talent from a limited scholar circle into a ruler's decision field.

## Key Characteristics

- Holds recognizable authority within the Jingzhou scholar network.
- Evaluates talent for an outsider seeking capable advisers.
- Links Zhuge Liang and Pang Tong through a memorable paired label.
- Converts local reputation into access to political patrons.
- Functions as a recommender, not as proof that later outcomes were guaranteed.
- Revises public underestimation through direct, extended evaluation in the Pang Tong account.

- Added direct evaluation of an initially underestimated Pang Tong before wider reputation transmission.
