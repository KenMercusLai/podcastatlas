# Barbara Stuckey

## Overview

Barbara Stuckey is a food strategist represented in the wiki through her effort to prevent cultivated animal-cell products from inheriting the fear-laden communication problems associated with genetically modified food.

## Current Profile

In [[frankenfood-the-mistakes-series-frankenfood-the-mistakes-series|Frankenfood]], Stuckey recognizes that “lab-grown meat,” “cell-based meat,” and Petri-dish imagery can define a product through its least appetizing production association. Drawing on the earlier GMO backlash, she and her team test roughly one hundred alternatives and select “cultivated meat” as a term intended to remain honest about difference without making the laboratory the consumer's first mental image.

Her contribution is not simply euphemism. The episode presents naming as a balance among accuracy, novelty, appetite, and trust, then connects the selected term to tasting and broader consumer education.

## Key Characteristics

- Food strategist focused on how consumers interpret unfamiliar technologies.
- Uses the GMO tomato backlash as a cautionary case for cultivated meat.
- Treats names and visual presentation as part of product adoption rather than cosmetic promotion.
- Supports “cultivated meat” as a differentiating but less alienating category term.
- Connects language testing with firsthand tasting and consumer experience.
