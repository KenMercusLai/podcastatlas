## Overview

何武 / He Wu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-604-xihan-wangmang-duoquan-hou-ruhe-qingxi-quanli-geju-ltwscyyiobc1ocvgti3fd-deajru|Hanji 604]] as the former general who tries with [[GongsunLuWesternHan|公孙禄]] to block another outer-relative monopoly after [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]'s death. His role is narrow but significant because the proposal is politically rational and still fails once [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] and most ministers favor [[WangMang|王莽]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 604 presents He Wu as an early critic of concentrated outer-relative command. He and Gongsun Lu cite the Lu, Huo, and Shangguan precedents as warnings that powerful consort kin can endanger the Liu house. Their answer is office balancing: He Wu recommends Gongsun Lu for Da Sima, while Gongsun Lu recommends He Wu.

The source does not portray the plan as foolish. It fails because the final decision belongs to Wang Zhengjun and because most ministers treat Wang Mang's prior reputation and Wang-family tie as decisive. The failed recommendation then becomes a record that can later expose He Wu to Wang Mang's cleanup politics.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han official who diagnoses outer-relative command concentration as a succession danger.
- Acts jointly with Gongsun Lu in a mutual-recommendation plan.
- Loses the Da Sima office contest to Wang Mang.
- Becomes exposed because failed opposition is remembered by the new power center.
- Currently source-scoped to the first post-Aidi transition layer.
