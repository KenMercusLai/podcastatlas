## Overview

何武 / He Wu is synthesized as a Western Han senior official who is first restored after [[WangJiaWesternHan|王嘉]]'s death, then later appears in the failed effort to block another outer-relative monopoly after [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] dies. His role is narrow but significant because he is treated as worthy by Wang Jia and briefly restored by Aidi, yet his later balancing proposal with [[GongsunLuWesternHan|公孙禄]] fails once [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] and most ministers favor [[WangMang|王莽]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 599 supplies the earlier restoration layer. Wang Jia names He Wu as a worthy former high official whom he failed to recommend strongly enough, and after Wang Jia's prison death Aidi appoints He Wu Yushi Dafu after moving Kong Guang up to chancellor. Aidi soon transfers He Wu to Qian Jiangjun and appoints [[PengXuanWesternHan|彭宣]] Yushi Dafu.

Hanji 604 then presents He Wu as an early critic of concentrated outer-relative command. He and Gongsun Lu cite the Lu, Huo, and Shangguan precedents as warnings that powerful consort kin can endanger the Liu house. Their answer is office balancing: He Wu recommends Gongsun Lu for Da Sima, while Gongsun Lu recommends He Wu.

The source does not portray the plan as foolish. It fails because the final decision belongs to Wang Zhengjun and because most ministers treat Wang Mang's prior reputation and Wang-family tie as decisive. The failed recommendation then becomes a record that can later expose He Wu to Wang Mang's cleanup politics.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han senior official whom Wang Jia identifies as worthy before dying.
- Restored by Aidi as Yushi Dafu, then transferred to Qian Jiangjun.
- Official who diagnoses outer-relative command concentration as a succession danger.
- Acts jointly with Gongsun Lu in a mutual-recommendation plan.
- Loses the Da Sima office contest to Wang Mang.
- Becomes exposed because failed opposition is remembered by the new power center.
- Current evidence spans Wang Jia's death aftermath and the first post-Aidi transition layer.

## Qualifications

This page is not a full He Wu biography. Hanji 599 supports his restoration and transfer after Wang Jia's death, while Hanji 604 supports his office-contest role and anti-outer-relative reasoning. His full career, exact legal exposure, private motive, and later fate remain source-scoped.
