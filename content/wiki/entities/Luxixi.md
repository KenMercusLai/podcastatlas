# 鲁西西 / Luxixi

鲁西西 / Luxixi is a [[ZhengYuanjie|郑渊洁]] child character connected in the wiki to [[TonghuaDawang|《童话大王》]] and the show's earlier memory of girl-centered childhood reading. In [[23-feima-pai-qiche-anquan-shi-di-yi-wei-de-572699943]], she appears through [[XunTuJi|《训兔记》]], where the episode says she quickly becomes part of the rabbit-training track.

The current source uses that detail to make obedience look less innocent: a "good" child can be absorbed by a system that values docility over judgment. This extends [[AntiAuthoritarianEducation]] while preserving the earlier [[FemaleSelfPossession]] branch from the childhood-reading episode, where Luxixi's goodness can also include courage, secrecy, and care.
