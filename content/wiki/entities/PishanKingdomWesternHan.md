## Overview

皮山国 / Pishan Kingdom enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-517-gudai-waijiao-zhongguo-weisha-ai-da-zhonglian-chong-pangzi-liemyvmwxw5_yd-trusbuqqhsbng|Hanji 517]] as the Western Regions polity chosen as the endpoint of a limited Han escort for a mission returning toward [[JibinKingdomWesternHan|罽宾]].

## Current Profile

The source defines Pishan by its decision-boundary role rather than by a full political history. [[DuQinWesternHan|杜钦]] says the route south beyond Pishan crosses several polities outside Han control and exposes escorts to robbery, provisioning failure, illness, narrow mountain paths, and fatal falls. Stopping at Pishan therefore preserves some diplomatic ceremony while refusing to make Han responsible for the most dangerous and least controllable segment.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Regions polity used as a practical escort endpoint.
- Boundary between a limited Han commitment and a route described as beyond reliable Han control.
- Geographic device in a compromise between diplomatic reception and operational risk.
