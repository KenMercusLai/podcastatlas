# Shanda / 盛大

Shanda / 盛大 is the company at the center of [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]]. [[ChenTianqiao]] founded it in 1999 with early help from [[ChenDanian]] and [[LuoQianqian]], first exploring a graphical virtual community before its decisive shift into [[LegendOfMir|《传奇》]] operations.

The episode treats Shanda as an early Chinese internet giant because it assembled a full operating stack for games: agency rights, servers, bandwidth, [[InternetCafePointCardDistribution]], customer service, [[OnlineGameSocialOrganization]], capital-market financing, and later IP control. Its post-Legend story is more mixed: [[ShandaBox|盛大盒子]], [[ShandaLiterature|盛大文学]], [[Haofang|浩方]], [[Bianfeng|边锋]], [[Ku6|酷六]], [[ShandaGames|盛大游戏]], and [[ShandaInnovationInstitute|盛大创新院]] show a broad entertainment-platform ambition that often ran ahead of market readiness or organizational focus.
