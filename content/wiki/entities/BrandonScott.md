# Brandon Scott

## Overview

Brandon Scott is the Baltimore mayor presented across the bounded sources as the political leader who made comprehensive violence reduction a governing priority after growing up amid both persistent violence and aggressive policing.

## Current Profile

The episodes tie Scott's policy commitment to lived experience. He contrasts broad stop-and-arrest practices that did not make his neighborhood feel safer with a coordinated strategy directed at the people and networks most exposed to serious violence. Part One adds his public target of reducing homicides by 15 percent each year and reports steep declines beginning in 2023 after two years without improvement; Part Two presents his role as requiring agencies and service providers to work as one prevention system.

The Lehrer interview compresses this account into a contrast with punishment-led policy. It presents Baltimore's decline as consistent with intensive, individualized work on emotional regulation, retaliation, family relationships, and material support, while still not isolating Scott's program from other causes.

## Key Characteristics

- Brings personal experience of violence and repeated police detention to public-safety policy.
- Rejects arrest volume as a sufficient proxy for safety or precise violence prevention.
- Prioritizes comprehensive intervention that combines support, prevention, community participation, and enforcement.
- Uses mayoral authority to demand cooperation across institutions with different professional identities and incentives.
- Accepts public accountability through a numerical homicide-reduction target despite early non-improvement.
- Sponsors individualized intervention that treats emotional and relational conditions as operational public-safety concerns.

## Evidence

### Lived policy context

- [[wolfgang-s-law-part-two]] says Scott grew up in Baltimore, experienced aggressive policing, and was detained and handcuffed without cause seven times between adolescence and early adulthood.
- [[wolfgang-s-law-part-one]] connects his Park Heights upbringing to shootings, overdoses, vacant housing, lost civic facilities, and repeated detention.

### Governing approach

- [[wolfgang-s-law-part-two]] contrasts his comprehensive violence-reduction priority with earlier mass-arrest policy and an incomplete punishment-heavy version of focused deterrence.
- [[wolfgang-s-law-part-one]] reports his annual reduction target and Baltimore's homicide declines from 2023 through 2025 while showing weekly case review in the Western District.
- [[brian-lehrer-and-malcolm-gladwell-talk-policing-and-guns]] presents Baltimore as a move away from mass arrests toward focused support for a small at-risk population.

## Qualifications

This profile is limited to three episode accounts and does not independently evaluate Scott's full administration, Baltimore crime data, program budgets, implementation record, or competing interpretations of the city's violence decline. The reported time series does not isolate the effect of any one policy.

## What Changed

- Created a source-bounded profile of Scott as the political sponsor of Baltimore's comprehensive violence-reduction approach.
- Added his numerical accountability target, the initial lack of progress, and the later episode-reported decline.
- Clarified the individualized emotional, relational, and material content attributed to Baltimore's strategy.
