# The Blind Assassin / 《盲刺客》

## Overview
*The Blind Assassin* is a novel by [[MargaretAtwood|Margaret Atwood]] in which [[IrisChase|Iris Chase]]'s late-life family account contains newspaper notices, a clandestine romance published under [[LauraChase|Laura Chase]]'s name, and a further science-fiction story about blinded child assassins and a sacrificed girl.

## Current Profile
The represented novel makes structure part of its theory of truth. Public notices preserve respectable appearances; Iris's memoir supplies private history; the attributed novel carries desire and concealment; and the planetary fable translates domination, sacrifice, blindness, and voicelessness into allegory. Each layer creates suspense, changes pace, and limits what can be known from any one record.

The eventual disclosure that Iris was both Alex's lover and the author of the book attributed to Laura reorders the reader's picture of her. The apparently passive wife also possessed desire, secrecy, and limited resistance. Yet the revelation does not repair Laura's abuse or death: the final manuscript remains an act of testimony, responsibility, and self-confirmation after remedy and direct forgiveness are no longer possible.

## Key Characteristics
- Nested memoir, newspaper, romance, and science-fiction narratives.
- Multiple suspense lines whose solutions require comparison across textual layers.
- Persistent contrast between public reputation and domestic violence.
- Female silence, desire, sacrifice, and resistance encoded through attributed and embedded fiction.
- Repeated objects and images that plant cross-layer evidence before the central reveal.
- Late-life narration concerned with responsibility and self-definition rather than simple mystery-solving.
- Family history shaped by war, economic crisis, marriage exchange, and intergenerational loss.
