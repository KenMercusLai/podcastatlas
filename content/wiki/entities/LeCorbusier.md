# 柯布西耶 / Le Corbusier

## Overview
柯布西耶 appears in the episode as the modernist architect whose Paris-period relationship with [[LaiAnArchitect|赉安 / 赖安]] helps explain Lai An's theoretical and modernist background.

## Current Profile
The source says Lai An and Le Corbusier were close during Lai An's Paris period, that Le Corbusier wrote a recommendation letter after Lai An obtained his architect qualification in 1919, and that Lai An joined the international modernist architecture group organized by Le Corbusier in 1928. The episode uses Le Corbusier's collective-housing ideas as a comparison point for Lai An's different Shanghai route.

## Key Characteristics
- Le Corbusier functions as a marker of Lai An's modernist network.
- His collective-housing model is used to explain one branch of modernist architecture.
- His relationship with Lai An remains partly interpretive in the source.

- Concept evidence: [[498-xunzhao-laian-wei-jindai-shanghai-wutongqu-chuangzao-fengmao-de-faguo-jianzhushi-lskf8bdkhs71lbwjomt3gu1ko8be]] uses Le Corbusier's Marseille apartment model to contrast collective modernist planning with Lai An's Shanghai path.

## Qualifications
The claim that Lai An may have taught Le Corbusier architectural knowledge, and the counterfactual that they might have competed had Lai An stayed in Paris, remain the source's interpretive claims.
