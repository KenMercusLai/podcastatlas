# Arthur Smith Woodward

## Overview

Arthur Smith Woodward was keeper of geology at the Natural History Museum and [[CharlesDawson|Charles Dawson]]'s principal scientific collaborator on [[PiltdownMan|Piltdown Man]].

## Current Profile

Woodward supplied the institutional authority, anatomical reconstruction, and scientific naming that transformed Dawson's fragments into *Eoanthropus dawsoni*. [[483-the-mysterious-case-of-the-ape-man-glt8701811415]] treats him primarily as a respected expert who was deceived, not as the hoax's proven co-author. His role demonstrates how a forger can borrow authority by controlling provenance while encouraging specialists to complete the interpretation.

## Key Characteristics

- He held a senior geological role at the Natural History Museum.
- His reputation made Dawson's amateur discovery scientifically credible.
- He participated in Piltdown excavation and reconstructed the jaw and skull as one individual.
- He named the proposed species *Eoanthropus dawsoni*.
- The episode lists him among historical suspects but centers the case against Dawson.

## Evidence

- Institutional validation: [[483-the-mysterious-case-of-the-ape-man-glt8701811415]] identifies Woodward's museum position and reputation as central to early acceptance.
- Reconstruction and naming: [[483-the-mysterious-case-of-the-ape-man-glt8701811415]] describes his excavation partnership and naming of Dawson's Dawn Man.
- Attribution boundary: [[483-the-mysterious-case-of-the-ape-man-glt8701811415]] surveys Woodward as a suspect but argues that Dawson alone fits every stage.

## Qualifications

This profile is limited to Woodward's Piltdown role and is not a general account of his scientific career. Being wrong and institutionally influential does not establish complicity; the episode does not present decisive evidence that he knowingly participated in fabrication.
