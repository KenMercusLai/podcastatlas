# 《污名陷阱》

[[161-zuanzhe-wenping-shiye-wo-zenme-pa-chu-zhege-xianjing-933696202]] introduces 《污名陷阱》 as the central book for a [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] episode on [[HighlyEducatedUnemployment|highly educated unemployment]]. The source says the book is based on 139 interviews with highly educated unemployed people and uses those cases to show how joblessness becomes a [[UnemploymentStigma|stigma]] rather than only an income problem.

In the episode's use of the book, the "trap" is social and psychological. Employers may suspect long gaps, acquaintances may become cautious after learning someone is unemployed, families may shift support into blame, and job seekers may begin reading market rejection as proof that they are failures.

## Key Claims
- The book is used to shift unemployment from individual blame toward a wider interaction among hiring systems, social judgment, and self-concept.
- The episode treats the book's U.S. interview material as useful but not fully portable; [[QinZong|秦总]] repeatedly adapts the discussion to Chinese job-search and family-pressure conditions.
- Its most durable contribution to the wiki is the link between [[OpaqueHiringMarket]], [[LongTermUnemploymentPenalty]], [[StatusDescentPressure]], and [[ShameBasedSelfConcept]].
