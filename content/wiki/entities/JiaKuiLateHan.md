# 贾逵 / Jia Kui (late Han)

## Overview
贾逵 / Jia Kui (late Han) enters the wiki through Hanji 1056 as the Jiang County defender in [[HedongCommandery|河东郡]] during [[GuoYuanLateHan|郭援 / 郭元]]'s Yuan-family western campaign. The episode uses him as a local-office and reputation case: even after military defeat, his standing with local elders, officials, and common people limits what the occupying commander can safely do to him.

## Current Profile
When Guo Yuan attacks Hedong, Jia Kui defends the Jiang County position. The local elders eventually agree to surrender, but only on the condition that Jia Kui not be harmed. Guo Yuan initially agrees, then becomes angry when Jia Kui refuses to accept service under him and attempts to punish or kill him. Officials and people protect Jia Kui, and Guo Yuan ultimately confines him at Huguan in a pit or cellar rather than executing him immediately. [[ZhuGongdaoLateHan|祝公道]] secretly rescues Jia Kui at night, turning a defeated local defense into a survival story grounded in public protection.

## Key Characteristics
- Hedong/Jiang County defender whose profile is currently bounded to Hanji 1056.
- Local official whose authority survives military defeat through community protection.
- Refuses to serve Guo Yuan after surrender, making political loyalty visible under coercion.
- Protected by local elders, officials, and common people as a people-based security case.
- Rescued from confinement by Zhu Gongdao, making his survival depend on local networks rather than battlefield strength.
