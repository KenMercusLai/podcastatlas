# Kenya

Kenya enters the wiki through [[piles-of-cash-and-a-town-of-solutions-in-kenya-nigeria-summer-school]] as a major [[DevelopmentEconomics]] and [[RandomizedControlledTrials]] case. [[PlanetMoney]] uses [[BusiaKenya|Busia]], near the Uganda border, to explain how school, health, and poverty interventions became testable policy questions.

The episode also uses Kenya as a civic-participation contrast. [[TavneetSuri]] says voter turnout and willingness to stand in long lines signal a desire to participate, tying civic energy to the entrepreneurial spirit discussed in the Nigeria segment.
