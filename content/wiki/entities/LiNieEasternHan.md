## Overview
Li Nie is the Guanghan scholar in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-713-liuxiu-nu-le-mingjiang-wuhan-jing-xuexi-chengdu-lv6555l99it33gw34uydsz4tkbje|Hanji 713]] who refuses Gongsun Shu's summoned office and drinks poison rather than serve under coercion.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Li Nie as a learned Guanghan figure whom Gongsun Shu tries to summon as boshi. Li Nie first refuses by claiming illness. Gongsun Shu then sends Yin Rong with an edict, a title, and poison, making the choice office or death.

Li Nie drinks the poison without consulting his family. Gongsun Shu later sends condolences and silk, but Li Nie's son Li Hui flees and refuses the regime's compensation. After Shu is pacified, Liu Xiu marks Li Nie's door with a stone inscription praising his integrity, converting refusal into post-conquest moral capital.

## Key Characteristics
- Guanghan scholar targeted by Gongsun Shu's coercive summons.
- Office refuser who treats service under forced legitimacy as worse than death.
- Family-linked moral case whose son also refuses Gongsun Shu's compensatory gesture.
- Posthumous beneficiary of Liu Xiu's symbolic recognition after the conquest of Shu.
- Evidence point for poison-backed recruitment turning scholar prestige into regime liability.
