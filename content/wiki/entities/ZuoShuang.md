# 左爽 / Zuo Shuang

左爽 / Zuo Shuang appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-39-lishishang-zhangyi-shi-zenyang-nudui-qiangdi-de-lv2gc2wtcxuhugfgixk7rbbhpa6x]] as the adviser who gives [[ChenZhenWarringStates|陈枕]] a route out of a blocked Wei job search. After [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] has already told [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王 / 梁惠王]] that Chen Zhen serves [[ChuState|楚国]] interests, Zuo Shuang warns that Chen Zhen is unlikely to win in [[WeiState|魏国]] if Zhang Yi keeps obstructing him.

His advice is to work with the hostile situation rather than deny it directly. By letting Zhang Yi's private words become known to Chu people, Chen Zhen can turn the accusation of Chu alignment into a path back to [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]]'s favor. The episode uses this as a compact example of [[SituationalPoliticalPivot|因事立道式政治转身]].
