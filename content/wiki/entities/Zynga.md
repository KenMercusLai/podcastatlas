# Zynga

Zynga appears in [[tsr-s5-spenserskates-v2audio-tsr-s5-spenserskates-v2audio]] as the prior workplace context for people who already understood high-resolution behavioral analytics in social and mobile games. [[SpenserSkates]] says many early [[Amplitude]] customers and investors came through an ex-Zynga network because those operators understood how product data could shape retention and monetization.

The source uses Zynga as market-education infrastructure. It helps explain why [[TwelveGigs|12gigs]], a mobile slots casino gaming company, saw value in Amplitude quickly while many other prospects only expressed polite interest.

[[qiquan-zhe-zhang-bing-weishenme-yuelaiyue-nanchi-le-1]] adds a compensation-governance example. The hosts cite Zynga as a pre-IPO case where some employees were reportedly pressured to give back part of their options or leave, making the company a source-scoped example of [[OptionPoolRecaptureIncentive]] rather than only analytics culture.
