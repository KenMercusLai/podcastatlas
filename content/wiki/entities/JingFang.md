# Jing Fang / 京房

Jing Fang / 京房 is the Han figure used in [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-jie-shangji-704051550]] to anchor [[ChineseAstrologicalPolitics|star-omen politics]]. The episode presents him as an important influence on later astrology because he read celestial changes as signs of political gain, loss, and ministerial failure.

His role is also political. The source says Jing Fang connected Heaven's signs to court criticism and died after offending powerful actors, making astrology more than neutral prediction or calendar technique.

## Key Claims
- Star interpretation can become a language for criticizing government.
- Han astrology made celestial irregularity legible as political evidence.
- Jing Fang connects [[ChenweiPolitics|谶纬政治]] with calculative and astronomical technique.
