# 冷建国 / Leng Jianguo

冷建国 / Leng Jianguo is a [[RandomFluctuations|随机波动]] speaker in [[ep258-women-ruhe-chongshi-shuiqian-dushu-lobyzxqk2oaov0v51cm355u8noix]]. Her role is to keep reading practical and unsentimental: if a book cannot be read now, the reader can put it down, switch fields, and return later when the book answers a live question.

The episode also uses her examples to connect reading with private pleasure and long attention. She describes childhood fairy-tale rereading, interest in history and graphic novels, sleep-adjacent fiction work while illustrating Aimee novels, and a resistance to chasing every new media or AI wave just because it is currently loud.
