# 伊阙之战 / Battle of Yique

伊阙之战 / Battle of Yique enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-67-zhanguo-mieba-baiqi-de-junshi-caineng-jiujing-ruhe-ljw0lltowxd4qz75v-wcnlkwqskv]] as the 293 BCE battle that brings [[BaiQi|白起]] onto the Warring States stage. [[HanState|韩国]] and [[WeiState|魏国]] form a coalition against [[QinState|秦国]], while [[WeiRan|魏冉]] recommends Bai Qi as Qin commander.

The episode's military point is that the coalition's formal size is less important than its internal weakness. Bai Qi judges that Han and Wei are not genuinely coordinated, routes Qin forces toward the coalition rear, breaks separated elements, and then surrounds the main force at Yique. The battle is therefore filed under [[CoalitionFractureExploitation|联盟裂缝利用]] rather than only under raw Qin strength.

The source reports the outcome as 240,000 Han-Wei soldiers killed and [[GongsunXiWarringStates|公孙喜]] captured. Afterward Bai Qi is promoted, and Qin Zhaoxiang Wang's broader strategy of attacking the Three Jin and seeking dominance is accelerated.
