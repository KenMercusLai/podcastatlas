# 张超 / Zhang Chao (Late Han)

张超 / Zhang Chao (Late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-997-zanghong-zhisi-shi-yibaoyuntian-haishi-qingjie-nandu-lln2liblq-kyjrsvitnbh7cspbax|Hanji 997]] as [[ZangHong|臧洪]]'s old superior and the absent figure whose fate drives the episode's loyalty conflict. The source says Zhang Chao's family is destroyed while [[YuanShao|袁绍]] does not come to the rescue.

Zhang Chao matters less as an independently narrated actor in this episode than as the moral claim Zang Hong refuses to release. Zang Hong argues that because Yuan Shao had called [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] a brother, Zhang Miao's brother Zhang Chao should also have received rescue. The host accepts the emotional force of that claim while questioning whether it can override Zang Hong's current service to Yuan Shao and Yuan Shao's alliance with [[CaoCao|曹操]].
