# 张超 / Zhang Chao (Late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-974-caocao-xiang-ta-tuo-qi-xian-zi-weihe-zhuanshen-ai-daozi-lhfabab7xv2vfsgnhr9ozwqhmhd|Hanji 974]] adds Zhang Chao to the original Yanzhou betrayal planning group. The source names him with [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]], [[XuSiLateHan|许汜]], and [[WangKaiLateHan|王楷]] as they secretly discuss turning against [[CaoCao|曹操]] while Cao Cao is away in [[Xuzhou|徐州]], then push [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] toward inviting [[LyuBu|吕布]] into [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-987-dingtao-zhizhan-caocao-he-lvbu-dianfeng-duijue-lofq0pq8n9rrfvfttunahisy0ave|Hanji 987]] backfills the immediate pre-siege position. After [[CaoCao|曹操]] defeats [[LyuBu|吕布]] at [[DingtaoBattleLateHan|定陶之战]], Zhang Chao is described as leading the Zhang family group into [[YongqiuLateHan|雍丘]] while [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] continues with Lü Bu. This makes the later Yongqiu catastrophe a direct continuation of the lost [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] struggle.

张超 / Zhang Chao (Late Han) enters the wiki through the Zang Hong branch in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-996-luanshi-zanghong-jiangyiqi-de-ren-xiachang-zui-biequ-lgpa-m6h6bvhyintnuj6zztopui76|Hanji 996]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-997-zanghong-zhisi-shi-yibaoyuntian-haishi-qingjie-nandu-lln2liblq-kyjrsvitnbh7cspbax|Hanji 997]] as [[ZangHong|臧洪]]'s old superior and the absent figure whose fate drives the loyalty conflict. Hanji 996 says Zhang Chao once employed Zang Hong in [[GuanglingCommandery|广陵]], later defends [[YongqiuLateHan|雍丘]] against [[CaoCao|曹操]], and expects Zang Hong to remember that old恩.

Zhang Chao matters less as an independently narrated actor than as the moral claim Zang Hong refuses to release. When [[YuanShao|袁绍]] refuses rescue and Cao Cao captures Yongqiu, Zhang Chao and his family are killed. Zang Hong later argues that because Yuan Shao had called [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] a brother, Zhang Miao's brother Zhang Chao should also have received rescue. The host accepts the emotional force of that claim while questioning whether it can override Zang Hong's current service to Yuan Shao and Yuan Shao's alliance with Cao Cao.
