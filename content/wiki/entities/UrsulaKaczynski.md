# Ursula Kaczynski / Agent Sonya

## Overview

Ursula Kaczynski, known as Agent Sonya, was a professional Soviet military-intelligence officer whose domestic appearance helped conceal her work in Britain.

## Current Profile

[[37-spies-with-ben-macintyre-glt4382644548]] describes Kaczynski as a colonel in Soviet military intelligence rather than an amateur hiding behind family life. Her case shows how investigators' gendered expectations could become an operational advantage: the episode says MI5 detected radio activity and interviewed her but discounted the possibility that a mother occupied with domestic responsibilities could be a serious agent.

## Key Characteristics

- Served professionally in Soviet military intelligence.
- Rose to the rank of colonel in the episode's account.
- Used an ordinary domestic identity as effective cover.
- Benefited from investigators' gendered assumptions about competence and risk.
- Represents the possibility that the most successful spies remain historically obscure because they evade exposure.
