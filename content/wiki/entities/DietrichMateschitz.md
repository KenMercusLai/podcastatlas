# Dietrich Mateschitz

Dietrich Mateschitz appears in [[hard-cedar-lebanon-seeks-trumps-help-6a5f41e97bddc0bea5542de5]] as the Austrian entrepreneur who founded [[RedBull]] in 1984. The episode says he discovered a Thai energy tonic on a business trip, then adapted it for European tastes by making it carbonated and less sweet.

The source presents Mateschitz's real innovation as brand construction. Slogans, stunts, extreme sports, media production, and events turned Red Bull into an [[AssetLightBrandMachine]], with outsourced production sitting behind a highly visible cultural surface.
