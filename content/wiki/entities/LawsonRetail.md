# Lawson (convenience-store chain)

## Overview

Lawson is a Japanese convenience-store chain appearing in the bounded source as a prospective entrant to India after 7-Eleven's exit.

## Current Profile

The source reports that Lawson plans to begin with five Mumbai stores in 2027, expand to 100 Indian stores by 2030, and pursue a long-term target of 10,000 by 2050. The plan signals continued belief in Indian convenience retail, but it does not establish that Lawson has solved competition from [[KiranaRetail|kirana stores]] and [[InstantRetail]], regional assortment complexity, or the density threshold that constrained [[SevenEleven|7-Eleven]].

## Key Characteristics

- Japanese convenience-store operator considering staged Indian market entry.
- Reported plan begins with a small Mumbai test before larger expansion.
- Long-range targets depend on localization, repeat demand, supply execution, and store density.
