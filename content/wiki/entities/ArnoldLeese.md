# Arnold Leese

## Overview

Arnold Leese was a veterinarian, specialist in one-humped camels, intense antisemite, and founder of the [[ImperialFascistLeague]] in 1928.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Leese to show that Britain's early fascist fringe included explicit racial conspiracy rather than anti-Bolshevism alone. He believed Jewish marriages were corrupting the British aristocracy and organized the Imperial Fascist League around an intensely antisemitic politics.

His professional background as a veterinarian and camel specialist makes the biography appear eccentric, but the episode does not treat eccentricity as exculpatory. The organization remained politically marginal while its ideology was still sinister and harmful.

## Key Characteristics

- Founder of the Imperial Fascist League in 1928.
- Veterinarian by profession.
- Recognized in the source as an expert on one-humped camels.
- Intense antisemite advancing conspiracy claims about the aristocracy.
- Leader of a uniformed but politically marginal fascist organization.
