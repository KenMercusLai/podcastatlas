# 《龙岭迷窟》 / Longling Maze

## Overview

《龙岭迷窟》 is the second book in [[GhostBlowsOutTheLight|《鬼吹灯》]]. The current bounded [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] discussion concentrates on its latter movement: the eye-shaped marks lead from the 精绝 ghost cave to Shirley 杨's family history, [[ZheguShao|鹧鸪哨]]'s search for the [[MuchenPearl|雮尘珠]], and the map that opens the route into [[YunnanWormValleyGhostBlowsOutTheLight|《云南虫谷》]].

## Current Profile

The book functions as a genealogical and causal hinge. What first appears as a mark on 胡八一 and 王胖子 becomes a shared inherited danger whose history runs through the 扎格拉玛 people, their false jade eye, the origin of the 搬山道人 and Shirley 杨's family. 鹧鸪哨's failed Black Water City expedition does not resolve the curse, but it preserves a bone inscription and transfers the mission across generations.

Its adventure logic is most legible in 鹧鸪哨's South Song tomb test. Candle position, cockcrow, a corpse at risk of transformation, an intruding cat and the requirement to retrieve burial clothing combine into a timed constraint system. Skill matters because he works within interacting rules and reports the ambiguous result honestly, not because force alone removes the danger.

The ending converts backstory into forward motion. 孙教授 links the eye sign to phoenix language without locating the artifact; 陈瞎子 supplies an account and map of 献王墓; and an eye-like jade beast redirects the party toward Yunnan. The hosts treat the episode's physiological, geomantic and folkloric explanations as story construction rather than external evidence.

## Key Characteristics

- Reclassifies the eye-shaped mark as an inherited, bodily and historical problem within the fiction.
- Uses 鹧鸪哨's backstory to explain why 搬山道人 search tombs and why Shirley 杨 inherits that task.
- Builds adventure around interacting rules, limited time and improvised compliance.
- Connects the failed Black Water City search to a new Yunnan clue rather than resolving the artifact quest locally.
- Uses supporting figures such as 了尘长老、金算盘、孙教授 and 陈瞎子 as links to other periods and expandable stories.
