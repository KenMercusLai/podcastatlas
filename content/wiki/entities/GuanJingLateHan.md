# Guan Jing (Late Han)

## Overview
关静 / Guan Jing is Gongsun Zan's late-Han adviser in the Yijing crisis. His wiki significance lies in the gap between good crisis reasoning and irreversible trust collapse: he identifies that Gongsun Zan's departure could make the garrison feel abandoned, but after the fortress falls he concludes there may no longer have been a good path and dies in loyalist combat.

## Current Profile
Guan Jing first appears as the adviser who blocks Gongsun Zan's proposal to leave Yijing and seek outside allies, warning that the remaining troops will collapse if the leader appears to abandon them. In the final siege source, he revisits that advice after Gongsun Zan's defeat, says the earlier blocked sortie might have left some chance, refuses to save himself, and charges into Yuan Shao's army. Yuan Shao later sends Guan Jing's head with Gongsun Zan's to Xudu.

## Key Characteristics
- Adviser focused on the morale and abandonment signal created by leadership movement.
- Witness to the command-trust damage produced by Gongsun Zan's earlier refusal to rescue subordinates.
- Loyalist endpoint figure who chooses death with the fallen regime rather than personal escape.
- Interpretive bridge between rescue reciprocity, fortress security, and battlefield morale.

## Evidence
- Abandonment-risk warning: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1030-shouwo-hao-pai-gongsunzan-weihe-da-de-xilan-lpkntyov4viqyzgxgcujn36qhv8|Hanji 1030]] records Guan Jing arguing that if Gongsun Zan leaves Yijing, the remaining defenders may believe they have been abandoned and disperse.
- Loyalist death and retrospective doubt: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1031-gongsunzan-zhisi-cong-bianjiang-zhanshen-dao-kunshou-zifen-lmqoechccfwmfpmbdx8-tjmnm3jz|Hanji 1031]] reports Guan Jing's post-fall reflection, his refusal to flee, his charge into Yuan Shao's forces, and the sending of his head to Xudu.

## Qualifications
The final source does not reduce Guan Jing's earlier advice to a simple error. Hanji 1030 presents the abandonment problem as real; Hanji 1031 adds retrospective tragedy by showing that staying also leaves Gongsun Zan vulnerable to deception and siege engineering.

## What Changed
- Hanji 1031 completes Guan Jing's arc from adviser to loyalist casualty.
- The page now records his retrospective doubt after Gongsun Zan's defeat.
- His role is now tied to both the blocked sortie and the failed final sortie.
