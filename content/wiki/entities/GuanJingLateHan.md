# 关静 / Guan Jing (Late Han)

关静 / Guan Jing (Late Han) appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1030-shouwo-hao-pai-gongsunzan-weihe-da-de-xilan-lpkntyov4viqyzgxgcujn36qhv8|Hanji 1030]] as [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]]'s adviser inside [[YijingFortress|易京]]. When Gongsun Zan considers leaving the fortress with elite cavalry, joining forces connected to the [[HeishanArmy|黑山军]], and counterattacking [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]], Guan Jing argues that the move will cause the remaining troops to think they have been abandoned.

His advice turns the episode from a simple siege story into a study of command confidence. The soldiers still hold because their families are inside Yijing and because Gongsun Zan remains visibly with them; if he departs with the strongest cavalry, the fortress may still exist physically but lose its remaining cohesion. Guan Jing therefore exposes the practical limit of [[FortressSecurityIllusion|筑城避世安全幻觉]] and the earlier damage caused by failed [[RescueReciprocityCommandTrust|救援互惠式统帅信任]].
