# Dan Driver

## Overview
Dan Driver appears in [[ep-40-governance-first-the-architecture-framework-that-makes-ai-auditable-defensible-and-99-cheaper]] as the founder of [[DriverAIAgency]] and architect of [[CaseReadyIntakeAI]].

## Current Profile
Driver's wiki role is the governance-first legal AI builder in this source. He says he is not an attorney and not a developer by trade, but draws on regulated-industry, Six Sigma, ISO, sales, revenue, and problem-solving experience. His product thesis comes from navigating two employment discrimination claims pro se and wanting a tool that could organize narrative, timeline, and evidence without crossing into legal advice.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats AI governance as an operating architecture rather than a static policy document.
- Uses lived legal-process experience to define a narrow legal-intake product boundary.
- Emphasizes documented decisions, testable controls, and auditable launch criteria.
- Separates deterministic legal-risk checks from LLM generation.
- Keeps unauthorized-practice-of-law exposure central to product design.
