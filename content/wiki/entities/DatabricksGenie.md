# Databricks Genie

## Overview
Databricks Genie is an AI analytics product described by [[AliGhodsi]] as operating over a continuously updated organizational ontology that connects people, reporting lines, projects, communications, documents, metrics, and decisions.

## Current Profile
In the episode, Genie is less a generic question-answer interface than a company-context analysis layer. Databricks uses it to test management hypotheses, segment employee feedback, inspect reporting depth and engineering practices, summarize customer meetings, aggregate feature requests, identify approval delays, and compare behavior with company principles.

## Key Characteristics
- Reuses an ontology of organizational entities and relationships rather than reconstructing context for every query.
- Connects quantitative metrics with authors, teams, documents, meetings, and operating history.
- Supports management analysis across feedback, organization design, engineering, customers, decisions, and culture.
- Can surface counterexamples to proposed causal stories instead of only confirming a manager's initial hypothesis.
- Concentrates substantial interpretive and monitoring power that requires privacy, validation, and governance boundaries.

## Evidence
- Context model: [[audio-99-alighodsi-databricks-audio-99-alighodsi-databricks]] describes Genie's graph of people, projects, communications, metrics, and decisions.
- Management applications: [[audio-99-alighodsi-databricks-audio-99-alighodsi-databricks]] reports survey, hierarchy, engineering, meeting, customer-request, blocker, and culture analyses.
- Counterexample use: [[audio-99-alighodsi-databricks-audio-99-alighodsi-databricks]] says Genie challenged a proposed relationship between work-life balance and perceived management action.

## Qualifications
The source does not provide product documentation, independent accuracy tests, privacy controls, consent procedures, causal-validation methods, or evidence that inferred cultural and productivity judgments are fair across roles. The examples are company-selected internal demonstrations and may not generalize to other organizations.

## What Changed
- Established Genie as an organizational-context and management-analysis system rather than only a conversational analytics interface.
- Made employee-monitoring, classification validity, and governance explicit boundaries on the product claims.
