# 陈县 / Chen County (Chu-Han)

陈县 / Chen County (Chu-Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-172-liubang-yangyou-yunmengze-zhiqin-hanxin-lrpkye8y6ibqdqs9pz7artk-6chv|Hanji 172]] as the meeting place [[ChenPing|陈平]] recommends for [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s trap against [[HanXin|韩信]]. The source says Chen County falls within Han Xin's Chu jurisdiction, which makes the summons look less like an invasion and more like an imperial tour moving through a vassal king's territory.

The place matters because it converts geography into access control. If Liu Bang openly attacked Chu, Han Xin's superior command ability could make the result dangerous; if Liu Bang announces a tour to [[Yunmengze|云梦泽]] and gathers the vassal kings at Chen County, Han Xin is pushed into ritual compliance before he can safely mobilize.

This page uses the Chu-Han suffix to avoid confusion with [[ChenxianChangsha|郴县 / Chen County (Changsha)]], the different place tied to [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]]'s exile and death.
