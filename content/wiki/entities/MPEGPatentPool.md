# MPEG Patent Pool

The MPEG patent pool is [[the-invention-invention]]'s modern example of a legally structured [[PatentPool]]. The source says the pool was designed around patents essential to the [[MPEG]] video standard, with [[KenRubenstein]] acting as an "umpire" over what counted as necessary rather than merely useful or competing.

Its importance comes from the 1997 [[USDepartmentOfJustice|U.S. Department of Justice]] approval described by the episode. By approaching the DOJ directly and promising not to exclude competitors or impose coercive contracts, the MPEG team created a template for later standard-related pools in [[Bluetooth]], [[DVD]], [[ThreeGMobilePhones|3G]], and [[FiveG|5G]].
