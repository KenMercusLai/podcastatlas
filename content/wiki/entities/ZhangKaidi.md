# 张开地 / Zhang Kaidi

张开地 / Zhang Kaidi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-26-mengzi-laile-ln4twwp2frmr-xpumu0rqg-2gpyn]] as the [[HanState|韩国]] chief minister who succeeds [[ShenBuhai|申不害]] after Shen's death in 337 BCE. The episode uses him less for a standalone biography than for the continuity of one Han-serving family across several rulers.

His importance is retrospective. Because Zhang Kaidi is presented as [[ZhangLiang|张良]]'s grandfather and [[ZhangPingHan|张平]]'s father, the source reads Zhang Liang's later anti-Qin and pro-Han action as rooted in family-political memory rather than only personal grievance.
