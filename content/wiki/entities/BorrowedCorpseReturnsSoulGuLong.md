# 《借尸还魂》 / Borrowed Corpse Returns the Soul

## Overview

《借尸还魂》 is a [[GuLong|古龙]] novel in the [[ChuLiuxiang|楚留香]] sequence. The bounded source treats it as a linked supernatural-seeming identity puzzle, arranged-marriage escape, professional-assassin investigation, and study of how unequal fighters redefine victory.

## Current Profile

The title mystery begins when 左明珠 is certified dead, awakens claiming to be 施茵, knows private details of the 施 household, and can perform its 小鹰爪功 while 施茵 appears to lie dead elsewhere. The accumulating proofs make possession look harder to dismiss than any single eerie event. The later solution reverses that frame: the two young women and their lovers coordinate a false death, substituted corpse, exchanged knowledge, learned technique, and medical lie to evade family-controlled marriages.

A second plot follows 楚留香's investigation of a professional assassin organization. 薛笑人 hides ambition and swordsmanship beneath performed madness, while his brother [[XueYirenGuLong|薛衣人]] supplies the novel's strongest conflict between mastery, family duty, and moral responsibility. The two mysteries are joined through the 薛 family and 楚留香's investigative movement, although the episode finds the character depth and duel stronger than every step of the title puzzle.

## Key Characteristics

- Makes an apparent possession plausible through several independent-looking forms of proof.
- Converts supernatural expectation into a coordinated social deception aimed at escaping arranged marriages.
- Runs a romantic identity puzzle beside an assassin-organization investigation.
- Uses 薛笑人的 performed madness as both concealment and retrospective clue.
- Gives 薛衣人 more moral and psychological complexity than most of the lovers driving the title scheme.
- Reframes an unequal duel through evasion, verbal pressure, and an altered definition of victory.
- Ends a romantic episode with female-initiated departure while leaving its gender politics open to two readings.
