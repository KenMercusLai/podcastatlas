# University of North Texas

University of North Texas appears in [[ep-6-data-science-ai-talk]] as [[PaulinaNemkova|Paulina Nemkova]]'s PhD institution. The source uses it as a source-scoped academic setting for AI and machine-learning research rather than as a broad institutional profile.

In the episode, UNT is where Paulina works on [[EEGBrainReading]], explores AI subfields through coursework and projects, and starts a student club intended to connect computer science with the business school.
