# 吕范 / Lu Fan

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-983-zhi-hui-pianren-de-laoban-yongyuan-bushi-hao-laoban-lknr2zydwjytjo7od-rt3zi-4k-s|Hanji 983]] first names Lu Fan in Sun Ce's early following. After [[YuanShu|袁术]] sends [[SunCe|孙策]] to recruit through [[WuJingLateHan|吴景]] and [[SunBenLateHan|孙贲]], Sun Ce travels back toward [[QuALateHan|曲阿]] with Lu Fan and [[SunHeLateHan|孙河]]. This source gives Lu Fan a pre-management role as part of the small early group around Sun Ce.

吕范 / Lu Fan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-994-genzhe-dongwu-zhongchen-lvfan-xuexi-zhichang-zhihui-lgqp2vqmh1mss96gcwmflp-ccpfu|Hanji 994]] as a [[SunCe|孙策]] follower whose value lies in accepting an unglamorous organizational role. As Sun Ce's camp expands, Lu Fan asks to serve as wartime `都督` for discipline and enforcement, even though Sun Ce initially thinks the office is beneath someone of Lu Fan's status and record.

The episode emphasizes Lu Fan's "same boat" framing. He says that leaving home to follow Sun Ce means sharing the organization's fate, so repairing a military-discipline gap is not merely a favor to the leader but a way to keep everyone aboard from being endangered. Once Sun Ce grants formal credentials, Lu Fan's enforcement makes the camp more serious, orderly, and harmonious.

This makes Lu Fan a source case for [[OperationalGapOwnership|主动补组织短板]]: a capable subordinate sees a low-status but high-importance execution gap and takes responsibility before the leader has to force the issue. The page should stay distinct from later high-command uses of `都督`, because Hanji 994 explicitly contrasts this early disciplinary office with later grand-commander titles associated with figures such as [[LuXunDongWu|陆逊]].
