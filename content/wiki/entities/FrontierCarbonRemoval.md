# Frontier Carbon Removal

Frontier Carbon Removal appears in [[peter-tsr-v3-audio-converted-peter-tsr-v3-audio-converted]] as the [[Stripe]]-organized [[AdvancedMarketCommitment|advanced market commitment]] for carbon removal. [[PeterReinhardt]] says Frontier pooled about $1 billion of purchasing power and that [[CharmIndustrial|Charm Industrial]] was the first company it signed a purchase agreement with.

The source uses Frontier as early demand infrastructure. A large purchase commitment does not by itself prove low-cost, permanent removal, but it can help companies survive the period before ordinary [[CarbonRemoval]] markets exist. In the wiki, Frontier therefore connects [[ProductLedWillingnessToPay]] to [[ClimateStartupCommercializationGap]]: buyers can pay for learning and scale-up when the social need is clear but the cost curve is still immature.
