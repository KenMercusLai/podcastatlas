# 面包新语 / BreadTalk

BreadTalk, known in Chinese as 面包新语, appears in [[vol-270-da-shi-dai-liu-zai-le-ta-de-shi-dai-1006063835]] as the bakery brand [[GuoMingzhong|郭明忠]] founded in 2000 after building [[FoodJunction]] and [[FoodRepublic|大食代 / Food Republic]]. The episode uses BreadTalk to show the same design-led food-retail logic applied to a bakery: bright stores, product refresh, visible novelty, and early hit products such as meat-floss bread.

The source also says the BreadTalk group later operated or represented [[DinTaiFung|鼎泰丰]] in several markets. In the wiki, the brand's role is to connect food-court operation with a broader consumer-chain habit: standardize ordinary food, make the store itself legible, and turn small daily consumption into a branded format.
