# 毛哲 / Mao Zhe (TCM Guest)

## Overview
Mao Zhe is the traditional-Chinese-medicine guest in [[vol-154-chuntian-yang-dao-naoxin-poju-guomin-jie-de-shengcun-zhinan-lnxaxys733qzi9rkcwvgqt6uzfur]], discussing spring allergy, rhinitis, urticaria, diet, tea, household exposure, and escalation to medical care.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Mao Zhe as a TCM explainer who interprets allergy through season, place, constitution, wind, heat, cold, dampness, and organ-system relationships. His most clinically cautious contributions are that allergy is not simply low immunity, testing is incomplete, acute medication can be useful, breathing difficulty is dangerous, and self-directed allergen exposure is unpredictable. His specific dietary, tea, herbal, acupressure, aloe, tap-water-rinsing, blood-flow, and constitutional-treatment claims remain source-scoped and are not independently validated by the transcript.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses “因时、因地、因人” to individualize seasonal and regional interpretations.
- Classifies allergic symptoms through TCM patterns such as wind-heat, wind-cold, damp accumulation, and deficiency.
- Combines food, tea, environmental, herbal, and acupressure suggestions with conventional antihistamine and medical-escalation advice.
- Explicitly treats breathing difficulty as an urgent risk and cautions that deliberate allergen exposure can be painful and unpredictable.
