# Is a River Alive? / 《活水》

## Overview
Is a River Alive? is the [[RobertMacfarlane|Robert Macfarlane]] book discussed in [[episode-225-yidai-yilu-8212959555-438866|Episode 225: 一袋一录]] as a nature-writing entry into whether rivers can be understood as living beings.

## Current Profile
In the source, the book becomes the bridge from literary carrier-bag theory to river personhood, rights-of-nature law, and more-than-human imagination. The host uses it to show that a river is not just scenery or infrastructure; it gathers sediment, waves, organisms, microorganisms, boats, migration memory, legends, place names, cities, and political claims.

## Key Characteristics
- Centers the question of whether a river is alive.
- Connects writing form to ecological and legal imagination.
- Exemplifies bag-like nature writing that moves through maps, navigation, geography, and layered details.
- Helps the host defend grand scale when it arises from relation rather than narrative monopoly.
