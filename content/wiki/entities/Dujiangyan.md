# 都江堰 / Dujiangyan

都江堰 / Dujiangyan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-52-chenzhuang-panqin-duanqi-wan-chifan-fangxia-wan-ma-niang-ls13ixmbhflepjgpelxgu6idjqa2]] as the later irrigation project the episode uses to explain why conquered [[ShuStateWarringStates|蜀国]] becomes so valuable to [[QinState|秦国]]. The source attributes the project to [[LiBing|李冰]] and his son.

The episode's use of Dujiangyan is strategic rather than engineering-focused. It marks the shift from hard-to-enter territory to a productive [[BaShu|巴蜀]] grain base that can feed Qin's larger expansion.
