# Koch Fertilizer

Koch Fertilizer appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-charles-chase-koch-on-how-they-quietly-built-a-150b-empire-41270895]] as a [[KochIndustries|Koch Industries]] business used to explain [[ChaseKoch|Chase Koch]]'s personal application of [[ComparativeAdvantage]]. Chase says he fired himself from running the fertilizer business after concluding another leader had stronger comparative advantage for the operating role.

The source treats this as a values-and-capability test. Instead of keeping a role because of family status, Chase frames the decision as finding the person most likely to create value for customers, employees, and the company. The move then helped lead him toward [[KochDisruptiveTechnologies|Koch Disruptive Technologies]].
