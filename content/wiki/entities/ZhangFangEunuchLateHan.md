## Overview
张防 / Zhang Fang is a Shun-era eunuch powerholder whose current wiki profile now combines a detailed 126 CE abuse-and-retaliation case with a later intimidation marker. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-806-xiangyao-chengshi-zhaodui-ren-hen-guanjian-luswh-p-oyutysxue6shweqcwqxs|Hanji 806]] shows [[YuXuEasternHan|虞诩]] accusing him of bribery and power abuse before Zhang Fang's palace access turns the case back against Yu Xu; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-818-guanchang-weishenme-yao-duo-jiaoyou-neng-jiuming-liyziudpezaoa4l6pbsq0wk2qdjdr|Hanji 818]] later shows [[WangGongTaiweiLateHan|王龚]] abandoning an impeachment plan against him.

## Current Profile
Hanji 806 supplies the fuller case. Zhang Fang, identified as zhongchangshi, is accused by Yu Xu of bribery and power abuse. Yu Xu's memorials are initially shelved, and the reversal comes through Zhang Fang's access to Emperor Shun: after Zhang Fang complains, Yu Xu is sent to Tingwei prison, punished at left-school labor, and tortured through repeated interrogations.

The same source also shows Zhang Fang's protection failing once the case becomes public and reaches Shun through alternative channels. [[SunChengLateHan|孙程]] confronts Shun and asks that Zhang Fang be arrested, Yu Xu's family and students petition, and [[GaoFanLateHan|高梵]] reports the public pressure. Shun then banishes Zhang Fang, punishes Jia Lang and others who had defended the case against Yu Xu, and releases Yu Xu.

Hanji 818 supplies the later danger marker. Wang Gong wants to impeach Zhang Fang and ask for execution, but relatives cite [[YangZhenLateHan|杨震]]'s fate as a warning and Wang Gong gives up. Zhang Fang therefore remains a node where eunuch power can be punished in one publicized case while still intimidating later anti-eunuch action.

## Key Characteristics
- Zhongchangshi accused of bribery and power abuse in the 126 CE Yu Xu case.
- Palace-access actor able to reverse accusations onto a censorial opponent before alternative channels reopen the case.
- Retaliatory powerholder whose danger is visible in Yu Xu's imprisonment, labor punishment, and interrogation.
- Punished but not erased: Hanji 806 records banishment, while Hanji 818 still treats him as a dangerous impeachment target.
- Evidence for the limits of anti-eunuch correction under Emperor Shun.

## Qualifications
This page should not be read as a complete Zhang Fang biography. Hanji 806 gives a concrete 126 CE accusation, retaliation, and banishment sequence; Hanji 818 later uses Zhang Fang as an impeachment-danger target but does not explain how his post-banishment status developed. Hanji 818's transcript writes 张坊; this page normalizes the durable route to 张防 while keeping exact source phrasing source-scoped.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 806 as the fuller Zhang Fang layer: Yu Xu's accusation, Zhang Fang's retaliation, Jia Lang's defense, and banishment after outside pressure reaches Shun.
- Reframed Hanji 818 as later evidence of lingering intimidation rather than the sole Zhang Fang case.
