## Overview
张防 / Zhang Fang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-818-guanchang-weishenme-yao-duo-jiaoyou-neng-jiuming-liyziudpezaoa4l6pbsq0wk2qdjdr|Hanji 818]] as a eunuch powerholder whom [[WangGongTaiweiLateHan|王龚]] considers impeaching under [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]]. His current significance is less a narrated personal biography than a survival-pressure node in Shun-era anti-eunuch politics.

## Current Profile
Zhang Fang's current profile is bounded to one source note. The episode first establishes the danger of anti-eunuch remonstrance through Wang Gong's earlier accusation and rescue by [[LiGuLateHan|李固]] and [[LiangShangLateHan|梁商]]. It later says Wang Gong wants to impeach Zhang Fang and ask for execution.

The plan does not proceed. Wang Gong's relatives cite [[YangZhenLateHan|杨震]]'s fate as a warning, and Wang Gong gives up the impeachment. Zhang Fang therefore functions in the wiki as a marker for the intimidation effect of eunuch power: even an official who has survived one accusation may retreat when the target is too dangerous.
