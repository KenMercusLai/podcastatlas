# 毛公 / Mao Gong

毛公 / Mao Gong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-97-2-jiemi-xinlingjun-ping-shenme-shi-si-gongzi-zhi-shou-lodqntthbil9o8ezppt66lywzd-f]] as one of the Zhao worthies whom [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] seeks after the relief of [[Handan|邯郸]]. The episode says Mao Gong mixes among gamblers, making him a low-status or socially irregular target for an aristocratic patron.

His importance is not a developed biography but the test he creates for Xinlingjun's reputation. Xinlingjun cannot recruit him through ordinary display or rank; he has to walk into Mao Gong's own social setting and associate with him on terms that high nobles could find improper. Mao Gong therefore extends [[TalentAttractionThroughRespect|礼贤下士式人才吸附]] from the earlier [[HouYing|侯嬴]] scene into Xinlingjun's new life in [[ZhaoState|赵国]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-103-1-simaqian-kanzhong-de-xinlingjun-suan-shi-xiaoren-ma-lvty1pdnibvqpk0m16kkxzw4t-ck]] gives Mao Gong's advice a state-saving payoff. When [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]] summons Xinlingjun back from Zhao, Mao Gong and [[XueGong|薛公]] argue that the respect Xinlingjun receives from the lords exists because he still has [[WeiState|魏国]] behind him. If Qin destroys [[Daliang|大梁]] and the Wei ancestral temple, Xinlingjun's personal honor cannot stand apart from that loss.
