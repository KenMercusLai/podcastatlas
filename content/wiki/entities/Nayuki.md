# Nayuki / 奈雪的茶

## Overview

Nayuki / 奈雪的茶 is a Chinese freshly made tea-drink chain used by the source to show how store-born beverage brands are testing bottled products and supermarket distribution.

## Current Profile

The bounded evidence presents Nayuki as an early bottled sparkling-water entrant around 2020 and as a brand willing to modify pack size and price for supermarket exposure. That makes the company a channel-adaptation case rather than proof of a successful second growth curve: retail reach can increase awareness and trial, but the source provides no sales, margin, repeat-purchase, or inventory-turn evidence.

## Key Characteristics

- A store-born tea-drink brand extending into packaged retail formats.
- An early sparkling-water experimenter whose first attempt did not establish durable differentiation.
- Willing to adjust volume and price to fit supermarket-channel requirements.
- Uses retail exposure as a complement to its own store network.

- **Early packaged trial:** [[kafeidou-xicha-ruixing-baishang-shangchao-huojia-xianzhiyinpin-qijia-de-pinpaimen-weishenme-douzai-fali-pingzhuangyinliao-gkwrijiowiswahe8wgt2vl4q]] places Nayuki among the tea brands that launched bottled sparkling water from 2020.

## Qualifications

The episode does not provide audited packaged-beverage revenue, profitability, sell-through, repeat purchase, or channel inventory. Willingness to adapt a pack for a retailer shows commercial flexibility, not that the business has achieved scale or attractive unit economics.
