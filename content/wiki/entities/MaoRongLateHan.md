## Overview

茅容 / Mao Rong enters the wiki through Hanji 860 as a late-Han figure whose ordinary conduct becomes evidence of character. The episode first places him in the [[HuangQiongLateHan|黄琼]] funeral aftermath with [[XuZhiLateHan|徐稚]], then uses his rain-shelter posture and filial household meal to show why [[GuoTaiLateHan|郭泰]] sees hidden virtue before public achievement.

## Current Profile

Hanji 860 gives Mao Rong two connected roles. In the funeral scene, he catches up with Xu Zhi and reports that Xu refuses to discuss court affairs but speaks at length about farming and harvests. Guo Tai reads this not as failure by Mao Rong or Xu Zhi, but as evidence that Xu Zhi knows Mao Rong has worth while still choosing topics suited to circumstance.

The second role is more developed. Guo Tai later sees Mao Rong sheltering from rain in the fields. While others sit casually, Mao Rong remains upright and respectful, prompting Guo Tai to stay at his home. The next morning Mao Rong kills a chicken, gives the good food to his mother, stores the rest for her, and serves Guo Tai simple fare. Guo Tai bows to him and urges him to study, after which the source says Mao Rong becomes a person of notable virtue.

## Key Characteristics

- Late-Han Chenliu figure whose current evidence is source-scoped to Hanji 860.
- Interlocutor whose exchange with Xu Zhi helps reveal selective speech and audience judgment.
- Everyday virtue case whose posture in bad weather signals self-command before status.
- Filial household actor who prioritizes his mother over guest display.
- Talent-recognition recipient whom Guo Tai encourages toward study and later moral achievement.
