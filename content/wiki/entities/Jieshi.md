# 碣石 / Jieshi

碣石 / Jieshi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-123-1-shen-zhi-yuyan-wangqin-zhe-hu-jing-shi-zhi-lg7m3vfdbcweb4joxe4jvhxjx6y8]] as a northern coastal stop in [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]]'s 215 BCE eastern tour. The episode says this third post-unification tour is directed toward former [[YanState|燕国]] territory and the search for ancient immortals.

In the source, Jieshi is both a ritual-inscription site and a hinge into policy. Qin Shi Huang has a stele carved there after sending [[LuSheng|卢生]] to seek immortal traces; the later return of "亡秦者胡" turns this sea-facing search route into the prelude to [[ProphecyTriggeredPolicy|预言触发政策]] and the northern campaign against [[Xiongnu|匈奴]].

The episode also places the post-Jieshi order to remove large city walls and levees near this route sequence, making Jieshi part of the transition from [[ImperialTourPoliticalTheater|帝国巡游政治剧场]] into [[StrongTrunkWeakBranchCentralization|强干弱枝式集权]].
