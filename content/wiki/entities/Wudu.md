# 《五蠹》 / Wu Du

《五蠹》 / Wu Du enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-112-2-qinshihuang-yeshi-tade-shumi-llkoy-zvhjjr6af9tflcw1xvogwz]] as one of [[HanFei|韩非]]'s major writings. The episode places it in the same cluster as [[Gufen|《孤愤》]], 《内外储说》, 《说林》, and 《说难》, all produced after Han Fei's reform advice fails to move [[HanAn|韩王安 / Han An]].

In this source, 《五蠹》 matters less as a separately analyzed chapter than as a text powerful enough to cross state boundaries. [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]] reads it with 《孤愤》, becomes Han Fei's admirer, and wants to meet him. That turns a Legalist text into a political bridge from weak Han into the dangerous Qin court.
