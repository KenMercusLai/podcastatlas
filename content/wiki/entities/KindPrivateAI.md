# Kind Private AI

[[KindPrivateAI]] is the desktop AI product described by [[JonathanSchaeffer]] in [[ep-47-the-ai-pioneer-who-decided-privacy-matters-more-than-hype]]. The episode says it launched in February 2026, runs locally, has no internet access during use, downloads an open-source model with the executable, and lets users build collections from personal files, videos, pictures, documents, and other private material.

The product is framed as a [[LocalPrivateAI]] answer to [[AIQueryPrivacyRisk]] and [[AIProfessionalDataSecurity]]. Its architecture combines a proprietary local database, [[RetrievalAugmentedGeneration]], guardrails, citations, and an explicit refusal or "does not know" answer when the local data does not support a response.

## Key Claims
- Kind is presented as useful for material the user would not put into a public chatbot, including [[PersonalHealthData]], family archives, and proprietary work.
- Its local design shifts the trust boundary from a remote AI service to the user's own machine, which also connects it to [[LocalAIWorkstation]].
- The episode treats product quality and privacy as linked: a private AI assistant still needs grounding, citations, and [[AIVerification]] rather than merely local execution.
