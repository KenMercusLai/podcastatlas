# 焦触 / Jiao Chu (late Han)

## Overview
焦触 / Jiao Chu (late Han) enters the wiki through Hanji 1070 as the Yuan-side subordinate who helps push [[YuanXiLateHan|袁熙]] and [[YuanShang|袁尚]] out of Youzhou and then organizes a coerced blood-oath surrender to [[CaoCao|曹操]]. His page is the main actor record for the episode's coercive surrender ritual.

## Current Profile
After Yuan Xi and Yuan Shang fall back toward Youzhou, Jiao Chu and [[ZhangNanLateHan|张南]] reverse against Yuan Xi. Jiao Chu then claims the Youzhou inspector position, gathers many local officials and soldiers, kills a white horse, smears blood in covenant ritual, and orders the assembled officials to abandon the Yuan family for Cao Cao on pain of death.

The striking limit comes through [[HanHengLateHan|韩恒]]. Han Heng refuses to swear, saying he has received deep Yuan-family favor and cannot surrender again without losing moral integrity. Jiao Chu does not execute him; he lets him go and then submits to Cao Cao. Cao Cao rewards Jiao Chu and other surrendering actors with marquis status, making Jiao Chu a case where coercive betrayal still becomes useful under the new hierarchy.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuan-side subordinate turned anti-Yuan actor after Cao Cao's northern advantage becomes clear.
- Self-claimed Youzhou inspector who uses public oath ritual to bind local officials to Cao Cao.
- Coercive surrender organizer willing to threaten dissenters with execution.
- Pragmatic enough to spare Han Heng's loyal refusal instead of making him a martyr.
- Incorporated and rewarded by Cao Cao despite the betrayal of his previous patrons.
