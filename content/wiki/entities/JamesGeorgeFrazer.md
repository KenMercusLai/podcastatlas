# James George Frazer / 詹姆斯·乔治·弗雷泽

[[JamesGeorgeFrazer|James George Frazer / 詹姆斯·乔治·弗雷泽]] appears in [[29-qianmian-yingxiong-edipusi-zhengzai-xiaban-ji-ditie-590112891]] through [[TheGoldenBough|《金枝》 / The Golden Bough]]. The episode uses Frazer to explain sacrificial kingship, nature worship, taboo, ritual death, renewal, and the idea that a ruler can be treated as a vessel of collective life force.

The source is careful about Frazer's status. His comparisons are treated as imaginatively and historically influential, but many examples are said to be unreliable or hard to defend by present research standards. Frazer therefore strengthens the wiki's [[MythAsHistoricalEvidence]] branch precisely because he shows both the attraction and danger of reading ritual and myth as direct social evidence.
