# 胡兰 / Hu Lan (late Han)

## Overview
Hu Lan is the Guiyang bandit leader in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-867-wugu-bei-po-zangshui-ta-xuanze-baqi-huiji-lqenvoy7x8ic-qn3-bf93qj8tjvk|Hanji 867]] whose alliance with [[ZhuGaiLateHan|Zhu Gai]] expands the 165 CE Jingzhou rebellion. His force pressures [[GuiyangCommanderyLateHan|Guiyang]] and [[LinglingCommanderyLateHan|Lingling]], then his remnants become the hinge of the later [[ZhangPanLateHan|Zhang Pan]] false-accusation case.

## Current Profile
Hu Lan functions as the connective rebel actor between local banditry, regional siege warfare, and inter-provincial blame. At Lingling, his side attempts to use diverted water against the city, but [[ChenQiuLateHan|Chen Qiu]] drains the water out and turns the flood against the attackers. After [[DuShangLateHan|Du Shang]] defeats the main rebel force, Hu Lan's remnants flee to [[CangwuCommanderyLateHan|Cangwu]], where Zhang Pan suppresses them.

## Key Characteristics
- Guiyang bandit leader whose force combines with a Jingzhou soldier rebellion.
- Siege opponent defeated by Chen Qiu's improvisational Lingling defense.
- Residual-security actor whose remnants create the opening for Du Shang's false report against Zhang Pan.
