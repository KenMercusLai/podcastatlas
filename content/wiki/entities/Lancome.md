# Lancôme / 兰蔻

Lancôme / 兰蔻 appears in [[lvdian-xiaoyang-yao-tuiguang-tihuanxin-de-ba-meizhuang-chongzuo-yibian-1006757937]] as a [[LOreal|L'Oreal / 欧莱雅]] prestige-beauty brand used to make [[RefillableBeautyPackaging]] concrete. The source uses Lancôme cream and the "小黑瓶" refill example to show why a high-design, high-material package can be worth keeping across repeat purchases.

The brand's source-specific role is not a full Lancôme strategy account. It is the case where refill adoption depends on making a premium container durable, lowering material use, making the replacement step smooth, and showing the refill option clearly beside the full product in counters and online search.
