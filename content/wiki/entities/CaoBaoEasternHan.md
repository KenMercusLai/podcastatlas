## Overview

曹褒 / Cao Bao was an Eastern Han ritual specialist whose Han-rites project now has both a creation layer and a later abandonment layer. Hanji 758 says he completes a broad ritual code under [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Emperor Zhang]] and has it accepted without further review. Hanji 768 then shows the same project failing to survive organized court resistance under [[EmperorHeOfHan|Emperor He]]. The wiki keeps him distinct from [[CaoBaoLateHan|曹豹 / Cao Bao]], a different late-Han person.

## Current Profile

Hanji 758 supplies Cao Bao's constructive role. He bases the project on older statutes, adds Confucian Five Classics material and chenwei records, and produces 150 sections covering rites from emperor to commoner, including capping, marriage, sacrifice, funeral, and calamity rituals. Emperor Zhang accepts the work directly. In the episode's "分寸感" frame, Cao Bao's rites are not decorative detail; they make rank, occasion, and proper distance legible as institutional order.

Hanji 768 supplies the later political test. Emperor Zhang had assigned Cao Bao to create Han institutional rites, and Emperor He used those rites during his capping ceremony. When [[ZhangPuEasternHan|Zhang Pu]] and Zhang Min repeatedly attacked the work from a Taixue-orthodoxy position, Emperor He recognized some political self-interest in the opposition but still stopped using the rites.

Cao Bao's profile therefore joins ritual production to enforcement risk. A court can commission and accept formal order, but the order remains fragile if later rulers will not defend it against organized criticism.

## Key Characteristics

- Ritual specialist attached to an imperial project of defining Han rites.
- Compiler of a large code that links household, court, sacrifice, mourning, and disaster rites into one hierarchy of conduct.
- Dependent on imperial sponsorship rather than an independent political base.
- Target of orthodox institutional resistance from Taixue-linked officials after Zhangdi's death.
- Exposes Emperor He's weakness: the ruler could know opposition was self-interested yet still abandon the work.
- Should not be conflated with the later figure 曹豹 represented by [[CaoBaoLateHan]].

## Qualifications

Cao Bao's page remains source-bounded. Hanji 758 gives the general scope and acceptance of the ritual code but not its detailed clauses. Hanji 768 gives the later abandonment conflict but filters it through a leadership lesson, not a complete ritual-history dossier. The page therefore treats Cao Bao as a ritual-order and court-enforcement figure rather than as a fully reconstructed scholar-official biography.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 758 as the creation and acceptance layer for Cao Bao's ritual code.
- Reframed the Hanji 768 abandonment as the later enforcement failure of a project that Hanji 758 shows had already been accepted under Zhangdi.
