# Moon

The Moon appears in [[tech-20260211-0211-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260211-0211-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the destination where renewed human spaceflight, resource interest, and international rule-making converge. The episode says [[NASA]]'s [[Artemis2]] could send humans around the Moon for the first time in more than five decades, while [[China]] aims to land humans there by 2030.

The source frames the Moon as a governance and infrastructure test, not only an exploration target. Because states cannot claim lunar territory but may exploit resources, the Moon becomes a practical case for [[LunarResourceGovernance]], [[SpaceResourceExtraction]], safety zones, and the stable energy systems behind [[LunarNuclearPower]].

[[e239-spacex-yao-rang-taikong-suanli-cong-kehuan-zouxiang-xianshi-dan-ta-huasuan-ma-259291f5-2715-4dde-bcfe-b5beb4df5793]] adds the Moon as the pragmatic pole in [[MoonMarsStrategySplit]]. [[LiuBinyan]] argues that low gravity and proximity could make the Moon useful for power stations, orbital data centers, deep-space logistics, and resource development, while [[LouisHong]] treats it as a politically driven but useful test field on the way to [[Mars]].

[[tech-20260807-0807-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260807-0807-mp-tech-pod-128]] adds a debris-and-manufacturing update through [[SpaceX]]. The episode says SpaceX discussed using rockets to build manufacturing on the Moon, while also noting a discarded rocket collision with the lunar surface as a concrete trigger for [[OrbitalDebrisGovernance]].
