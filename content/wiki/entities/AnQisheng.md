## Overview

An Qisheng / 安期生 is the legendary immortal whom [[LiShaojunWesternHan|李少君]] claims to have met at sea in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-259-hanwudi-wo-ba-ni-dang-dashi-ni-ba-wo-dang-shazi-1-lqczbezelhdjrtxdrgsfcnmpoklx|Hanji 259 part 1]]. The current wiki treats him as a mythic authority inside a court-fangshi claim, not as a verified historical encounter.

## Current Profile

The episode associates An Qisheng with [[Penglai|蓬莱]], selective visibility, and giant jujubes said to grant or signify long life. Li Shaojun's claimed encounter supplies personal authorization for his wider program, while the rule that only destined people can see the immortal protects the claim from ordinary failed observation. After Li dies, Wudi sends [[KuanShuWesternHan|宽舒]] to seek both Penglai and An Qisheng, converting a named legendary figure into a target of state-supported maritime search.

## Key Characteristics

- Legendary immortal associated with Penglai and maritime encounter.
- Selectively visible figure whose accessibility depends on the seeker's supposed destiny.
- Claimed source of giant longevity-associated jujubes and Li Shaojun's personal authority.
- Named objective of continued imperial search after Li Shaojun's death.

## Evidence

- **Claimed encounter:** [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-259-hanwudi-wo-ba-ni-dang-dashi-ni-ba-wo-dang-shazi-1-lqczbezelhdjrtxdrgsfcnmpoklx|Hanji 259 part 1]] says Li Shaojun claims to have met An Qisheng at sea and eaten unusually large jujubes.
- **Selective access:** [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-259-hanwudi-wo-ba-ni-dang-dashi-ni-ba-wo-dang-shazi-1-lqczbezelhdjrtxdrgsfcnmpoklx|Hanji 259 part 1]] presents An Qisheng as visible to the destined and invisible to others.
- **Imperial search target:** [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-259-hanwudi-wo-ba-ni-dang-dashi-ni-ba-wo-dang-shazi-1-lqczbezelhdjrtxdrgsfcnmpoklx|Hanji 259 part 1]] says Wudi sends Kuan Shu to seek An Qisheng and Penglai after Li's death.

## Qualifications

The episode establishes An Qisheng's narrative and political role, not his historical existence, location, selective visibility, food, or immortality. Li Shaojun is the claimant, and no independently verified meeting or successful search appears in the supplied source.

## What Changed

- Created a source-bounded profile for An Qisheng as a named mythic authority and search target.
- Distinguished narrative influence from verified biography or supernatural existence.
