# 《太平年》

《太平年》 is the historical TV drama that gives [[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]] its public entry point into [[FiveDynastiesTenKingdoms|五代十国]]. The episode does not mainly recap the plot; it uses the drama's popularity to explain the period's political categories, regional regimes, and social life.

The source highlights the drama's unusual vantage from [[Wuyue|吴越]] toward the central plains. It also notes that some scenes, including a cannibalism episode, are dramatized or displaced from other late-Tang evidence, making the show useful as a prompt but not as a complete historical account.
