# Lionel Trilling / 莱昂内尔·特里林

## Overview
Lionel Trilling / 莱昂内尔·特里林 appears in the wiki through Episode 226 of [[ChizaoGengxin|迟早更新]], where his work on sincerity helps frame modern discomfort with ritual speech.

## Current Profile
The episode uses Trilling as a historical caution against treating sincerity as the only measure of language's value. In the source's account, sincerity becomes a modern virtue with a history rather than an eternal standard that can automatically invalidate ritual, apology, or gratitude when they feel awkward.

This role matters because the episode's proposed "谢谢，抱歉" ritual after birdsong playback is easy to dismiss as artificial. Trilling helps the source ask whether that dismissal depends too heavily on the modern demand that speech transparently reveal inner feeling.

## Key Characteristics
- Functions as the episode's literary-critical reference for sincerity as a historically situated virtue.
- Helps explain why modern listeners may distrust ritualized apology as fake or self-comforting.
- Supports a distinction between inner-state authenticity and language's social or behavioral force.
- Connects the episode's birdwatching ethics to broader modern self-expression history.
