# 徐晃 / Xu Huang (Cao general)

## Overview
徐晃 / Xu Huang is introduced here as a [[CaoCao|曹操]]-side general in the [[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]], distinct from [[XuHuangLateHan|徐璜]], the Huan-era eunuch with the same romanization. The current bounded profile now includes both the interception of [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s grain convoy under [[HanMengLateHan|韩猛]] and Hanji 1061's surrender-reward advice during Cao Cao's Ye campaign.

## Current Profile
The present evidence is compact. After [[XunYou|荀攸]] advises that Han Meng is brave but careless, Cao Cao sends Xu Huang with [[ShiHuanLateHan|史涣]] to strike the convoy. The operation defeats Han Meng and burns Yuan Shao's baggage.

Xu Huang therefore enters the wiki not through a full military biography, but through a specific Guandu logistics action. The convoy strike matters because it precedes [[XuYouLateHan|许攸]]'s defection and the larger [[WuchaoRaidLateHan|乌巢]] operation, showing Cao's camp already looking for the supply route rather than only holding the front.

Hanji 1061 adds a non-battlefield judgment. When [[HanFanLateHan|韩范]] and [[LiangQiLateHan|梁岐]] surrender county cities while the Yuan brothers remain undefeated, Xu Huang advises Cao Cao to reward them because other counties are watching. Cao accepts the advice and grants them Guannei Hou titles, turning surrender treatment into campaign psychology.

## Key Characteristics
- Cao-side general in the Guandu logistics struggle.
- Paired with Shi Huan in the attack on Han Meng's convoy.
- Shows both tactical execution and political-military judgment in the current bounded evidence.
- Treats visible rewards for surrender as a way to influence undecided counties.
- Disambiguation route for 徐晃, kept separate from the unrelated eunuch 徐璜.

## Qualifications
The current page is not a complete Xu Huang biography. It exists to prevent a route collision with the established [[XuHuangLateHan|徐璜]] eunuch page and to preserve source-bounded Cao-side actions. Hanji 1061 broadens him beyond execution, but only within one surrender-policy episode.
