# 徐晃 / Xu Huang (Cao general)

## Overview
徐晃 / Xu Huang is a [[CaoCao|曹操]]-side general whose current bounded profile joins logistics attack, surrender-policy judgment, corridor defense, and the decisive landward relief of [[FanchengBattle219LateHan|Fancheng]]. He remains distinct from [[XuHuangLateHan|徐璜]], the Huan-era eunuch with the same romanization.

## Current Profile
The present evidence is compact. After [[XunYou|荀攸]] advises that Han Meng is brave but careless, Cao Cao sends Xu Huang with [[ShiHuanLateHan|史涣]] to strike the convoy. The operation defeats Han Meng and burns Yuan Shao's baggage.

Xu Huang therefore enters the wiki not through a full military biography, but through a specific Guandu logistics action. The convoy strike matters because it precedes [[XuYouLateHan|许攸]]'s defection and the larger [[WuchaoRaidLateHan|乌巢]] operation, showing Cao's camp already looking for the supply route rather than only holding the front.

Hanji 1061 adds a non-battlefield judgment. When [[HanFanLateHan|韩范]] and [[LiangQiLateHan|梁岐]] surrender county cities while the Yuan brothers remain undefeated, Xu Huang advises Cao Cao to reward them because other counties are watching. Cao accepts the advice and grants them Guannei Hou titles, turning surrender treatment into campaign psychology.

Hanji 1107 moves Xu Huang into post-[[RedCliffsBattle|赤壁之战]] damage control. After Cao Cao's retreat, Xu Huang is stationed at 樊城 while [[CaoRen|曹仁]] holds [[JianglingLateHan|江陵]], making him part of the defensive line intended to slow further Sun-Liu expansion.

Hanji 1170 makes him the relief commander in the same corridor eleven years later. Reinforced while Cao Cao remains at Mobei, Xu Huang reportedly feints toward Weitou, shifts against Sizhong, defeats [[GuanYu|关羽]]'s returning cavalry, and pursues through successive field defenses until the landward encirclement of Fancheng breaks. The episode's claim that Guan Yu calls him “大兄” adds personal familiarity, but the operational profile is professional separation: prior respect does not prevent deception, attack, and sustained pursuit.

## Key Characteristics
- Cao-side general in the Guandu logistics struggle.
- Paired with Shi Huan in the attack on Han Meng's convoy.
- Shows both tactical execution and political-military judgment in the current bounded evidence.
- Treats visible rewards for surrender as a way to influence undecided counties.
- Holds Fancheng as part of the post-Red-Cliffs defensive line.
- Uses a false target, rapid redirection, and follow-through to break Guan Yu's Fancheng siege works.
- Disambiguation route for 徐晃, kept separate from the unrelated eunuch 徐璜.

## Qualifications
The current page is not a complete Xu Huang biography. It exists to prevent a route collision with the established [[XuHuangLateHan|徐璜]] eunuch page and to preserve source-bounded Cao-side actions. Hanji 1061 broadens him beyond execution, and Hanji 1107 supplies a deployment marker rather than continuous service at Fancheng. Hanji 1170's friendship language, camp count, place-name readings, troop total, feint sequence, fortification details, and pursuit depth require comparison with underlying chronicles.

## What Changed
- Added the 219 feint, counterattack, and siege-breaking relief of Fancheng.
- Connected the earlier corridor deployment to a later detailed field action without implying continuous command.
- Preserved uncertainty around friendship language, numbers, place names, and tactical detail.
