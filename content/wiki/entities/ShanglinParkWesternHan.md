## Overview

上林苑 / Shanglin Park enters the current wiki as [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s imperial hunting and recreation landscape whose proposed enlargement turns court pleasure into a dispute over fertile land, common access to forests and water, tax revenue, displacement, ancestral graves, and ruler safety.

## Current Profile

Hanji 253 says Wudi assigns Wuqiu Shouwang to survey and price a large zone around Acheng, Zhouzhi, and Yichun for incorporation into the park. The court also seeks reports of wasteland that could compensate residents of Hu and Du counties. The planning therefore has an administrative and compensation layer, but the source's [[DongfangShuo|东方朔]] branch argues that nominal replacement land does not preserve the same livelihoods, local resources, homes, graves, or fiscal value.

The park is consequently more than a leisure venue in this evidence. Its boundary determines whether productive land and shared resource access remain in ordinary use or become reserved imperial space. Wudi's reported decision to proceed after rewarding the critic makes Shanglin a concrete case of [[RewardedUnadoptedRemonstrance|赏谏不纳]].

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han imperial park associated here with hunting and court recreation.
- Proposed enlargement is preceded by land survey, valuation, and a compensation inventory.
- Boundary expansion would convert fertile and resource-bearing land into reserved imperial space.
- Dongfang Shuo's objection joins livelihood, revenue, displacement, historical-precedent, and ruler-safety concerns.
- The episode says the enlargement proceeds despite the remonstrance.

## Qualifications

The current profile rests on one popular-history summary rather than a full institutional or geographical history of Shanglin Park. The exact boundaries, land values, survey method, compensation quality, number of displaced households, access regime, construction sequence, and relationship between this enlargement and other phases of the park require primary-source and historical-geography comparison.

## Relationships

- [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] - ruler who commissions the survey and reportedly approves enlargement.
- [[DongfangShuo|东方朔]] - remonstrant who identifies the enlargement's livelihood, fiscal, displacement, and safety costs.
- [[RewardedUnadoptedRemonstrance|赏谏不纳]] - governance pattern illustrated when the critic is rewarded but the expansion proceeds.
- [[ImperialPrivateDesireGovernanceRisk|帝王私欲治理风险]] - broader relationship because recreation reserves public resources and shifts costs onto residents.
