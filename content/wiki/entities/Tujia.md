# 途家 / Tujia

## Overview
途家 appears in [[vol-272-2026-nian-le-zenme-hai-you-ren-yao-zuo-zhongguo-ban-airbnb-1008880863]] as an early Chinese Airbnb-referencing homestay platform whose practical path moved closer to vacation-rental and managed lodging supply.

## Current Profile
In this episode, Tujia's wiki role is comparative rather than comprehensive. It marks one reason China did not simply replicate [[Airbnb]]'s original spare-room model: easier-to-standardize vacation rentals and commercial supply fit platform operations better than ordinary homeowners hosting strangers during a rising property cycle.

## Key Characteristics
- Chinese homestay platform used as a contrast to ordinary spare-bedroom sharing.
- Represents a move toward vacation-rental supply rather than pure "rent my own room" hosting.
- Helps define why [[YijianCiwo|一间次卧]] is positioned as a different timing and supply bet.
