## Overview
Geng Kuang / 耿况 is presented as [[GengYanEasternHan|耿弇]]'s father and the northern military-family base that helps [[LiuXiu|刘秀 / 光武帝]] gain Youzhou cavalry. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-685-donghan-dachen-songhong-weihe-buken-zuo-guangwudi-jiefu-ljafg8giyhg5wmumv-g-09lywcu2|Hanji 685]] gives the direct rebellion-pressure scene, where [[PengChongEasternHan|彭宠]] repeatedly asks Geng Kuang to join and Geng Kuang refuses by killing the envoys. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-733-2-gengyan-youzhizhe-shijingcheng-jiangmen-huzi-yongguan-sanjun-lnlfumtdzrigby9ox4v6pzbeogeh|Hanji 733-2]] then uses the same pressure field to frame the Geng family as a local force that can either stabilize or endanger Liu Xiu's founding coalition.

## Current Profile
The sources place Geng Kuang in a command environment where household prestige, horses, and local troops are decisive political assets. Geng Yan's ability to help Liu Xiu depends on persuading this paternal power base to support Liu Xiu rather than drift with the Gengshi regime or local rebels.

Geng Kuang becomes especially important during the Peng Chong rebellion. Hanji 685 says Peng Chong believes their contributions are comparable and repeatedly sends messages asking him to join the revolt; Geng Kuang rejects the appeal and kills the messengers. Hanji 733-2 broadens the political reading: because Peng Chong has prior ties with the Geng family and expects sympathy, refusal becomes evidence that Liu Xiu can continue to trust the family. The episode further frames Geng Kuang's dispatch of [[GengGuoEasternHan|耿国]] to Luoyang as a deliberate signal that the family accepts central authority.

## Key Characteristics
- Northern military-family patriarch whose resources give Geng Yan practical leverage.
- Youzhou power broker whose alignment helps Liu Xiu convert local cavalry into founding capacity.
- Refuses Peng Chong's rebellion directly, even though Peng tries to argue from comparable merit and old connection.
- Loyalty signaler who refuses Peng Chong's pull despite prior personal connection.
- Political risk manager who sends Geng Guo to court to reduce Liu Xiu's suspicion.

## Qualifications
The page is based on sources whose main subjects are Song Hong/Peng Chong and Geng Yan rather than Geng Kuang alone. Hanji 685 strengthens the direct refusal evidence but does not reconstruct Geng Kuang's independent command biography. The exact titles, command geography, messenger procedure, and chronology around Shanggu/Youzhou should remain source-scoped until cross-checked with the transmitted histories.

## What Changed
