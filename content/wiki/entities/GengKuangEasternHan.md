## Overview
Geng Kuang / 耿况 is presented in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-733-2-gengyan-youzhizhe-shijingcheng-jiangmen-huzi-yongguan-sanjun-lnlfumtdzrigby9ox4v6pzbeogeh|Hanji 733-2]] as [[GengYanEasternHan|耿弇]]'s father and the northern military-family base that helps [[LiuXiu|刘秀 / 光武帝]] gain Youzhou cavalry. His profile matters less as an independent campaign biography than as the family-power node that can either stabilize or endanger Liu Xiu's founding coalition.

## Current Profile
The episode places Geng Kuang in a command environment where household prestige, horses, and local troops are decisive political assets. Geng Yan's ability to help Liu Xiu depends on persuading this paternal power base to support Liu Xiu rather than drift with the Gengshi regime or local rebels.

Geng Kuang becomes especially important during the [[PengChongEasternHan|彭宠]] rebellion. Because Peng Chong has prior ties with the Geng family and expects sympathy, Geng Kuang's refusal to join him becomes evidence that Liu Xiu can continue to trust the family. The episode further frames his dispatch of [[GengGuoEasternHan|耿国]] to Luoyang as a deliberate signal that the family accepts central authority.

## Key Characteristics
- Northern military-family patriarch whose resources give Geng Yan practical leverage.
- Youzhou power broker whose alignment helps Liu Xiu convert local cavalry into founding capacity.
- Loyalty signaler who refuses Peng Chong's pull despite prior personal connection.
- Political risk manager who sends Geng Guo to court to reduce Liu Xiu's suspicion.
