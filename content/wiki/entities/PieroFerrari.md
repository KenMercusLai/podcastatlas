# Piero Ferrari

Piero Ferrari is [[EnzoFerrari]]'s son and the family continuity figure in [[ferrari-1]]. The episode says the [[Fiat]] transaction preserved a 10% stake for Piero while securing a broader ownership path for [[Ferrari]].

In the public-company era, the source treats Piero as part of the voting-control structure that keeps Ferrari from behaving like an ordinary public automaker. Alongside [[Exor]], his continued influence supports [[PublicMarketLuxuryGrowthTension]] by balancing public shareholders against long-term scarcity discipline.
