## Overview

陈宠 / Chen Chong appears in the current wiki as the Emperor-He-era official whose appointment as tingwei makes judicial temperament part of court governance: a personnel choice can soften the punitive style of the center.

## Current Profile

Hanji 769 records Emperor He appointing Chen Chong, formerly dasinong, as tingwei. The source emphasizes Chen Chong's humane, composed character and says he repeatedly handled difficult cases by citing Confucian classics and following a forgiving approach.

The episode treats that appointment as more than a biographical notice. Because tingwei sits close to legal judgment and penal policy, Chen Chong's selection is read as a way to moderate a previously harsh governing atmosphere.

## Key Characteristics

- Emperor-He-era official currently defined by appointment from dasinong to tingwei.
- Associated with humane bearing, seriousness, and lenient handling of hard legal cases.
- Uses Confucian classical reasoning as an interpretive resource in judicial work.
- Shows how appointment choice can shape the moral and procedural feel of court governance.
