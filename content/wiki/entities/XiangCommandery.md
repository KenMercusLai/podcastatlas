# 象郡 / Xiang Commandery

象郡 / Xiang Commandery enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-123-2-gudai-shiqi-de-daochamen-daodi-you-duo-weixian-lgcknwubwm3uzqmzgbgdlxlvcwxy]] as the broadest of the three southern commanderies the episode names after Qin's 214 BCE campaign against [[Baiyue|百越]]. The host describes it as including today's southern Guangxi, southwestern Guangdong, and central Vietnam.

Its role in the wiki is to show how far the source's Lingnan incorporation claim reaches. Compared with [[KuaijiCommandery|会稽郡]], which marks the earlier Jiangnan/Wu-Yue administrative branch, Xiang Commandery belongs to a deeper post-unification frontier expansion paired with large-scale convict migration.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-184-lishi-kongbupian-liubang-de-pengshi-roujiang-2-liebzrdrvo1okonpdjvrjatuwx9i|Hanji 184]] later names Xiang alongside [[NanhaiCommandery|南海郡]] and [[GuilinCommandery|桂林郡]] as part of the southern field tied to [[ZhaoTuo|赵佗]]'s [[Nanyue|南越]]. In that episode, the commandery matters as evidence of the scale behind Liu Bang's decision to recognize Zhao Tuo before fighting.
