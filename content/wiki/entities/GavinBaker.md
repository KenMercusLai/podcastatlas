# Gavin Baker

Gavin Baker appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-inside-the-private-stock-market-boom-spacex-anthropic-openai-the-rise-of-secondaries-41564170]] as the [[AtreidesManagement|Atreides Management]] investor voice arguing that AI ROI has been empirically positive and that public markets can provide useful discipline. He is skeptical that there is a strong reason for companies to stay private longer when public investors can pressure-test strategy and capital allocation.

His main contribution to the episode is [[PublicPrivateMarketDiscipline]]. Baker contrasts public investors who can buy or sell with private investors who may avoid hard questions because they want continued access to management teams. He also warns that venture returns may become more concentrated around a few trillion-dollar-plus companies, making exposure to [[SpaceX]], [[Anthropic]], [[OpenAI]], or similarly large winners a fund-level franchise question.
