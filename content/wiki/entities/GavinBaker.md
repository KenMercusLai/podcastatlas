# Gavin Baker

## Overview
Gavin Baker is tracked as an Atreides Management investor voice whose wiki role connects public-market discipline, AI infrastructure economics, open-model competition, private-market liquidity, and AI IPO valuation.

## Current Profile
Baker's current profile is a public-market investor applying infrastructure and market-structure discipline to AI narratives. He is bullish that AI demand can expand even if a specific company loses share, skeptical that private valuation targets should be overread without public disclosure, and repeatedly focused on where physical bottlenecks or market mechanics determine outcomes. The new All-In episode adds his highest-signal infrastructure claims: HBM and DRAM are binding AI bottlenecks, Micron's quarter reflects memory scarcity, large AI IPOs can be absorbed if float and price are handled carefully, and Chinese open-weight models can be composed into enterprise routing systems.

## Key Characteristics
- Public-market investor voice tied to [[AtreidesManagement|Atreides Management]].
- Uses public-market disclosure and sell discipline as a check on private-company narratives.
- Treats AI infrastructure as a stack of physical bottlenecks, especially memory capacity and bandwidth.
- Separates broad AI demand from company-specific share, execution, and valuation risk.
- Applies market microstructure and IPO pricing logic to Anthropic, SpaceX, Cerebras, and other AI-adjacent listings.
- Adds source-scoped political commentary on DSA voter composition and nonprofit/governance failure in New York.
