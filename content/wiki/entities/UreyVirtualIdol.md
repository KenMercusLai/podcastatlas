# Yuri / 尤栗 / 优瑞

## Overview
Yuri / 尤栗 / 优瑞 is the AI-native virtual idol and digital singer created by [[HanqingUrey|汗青]] in the [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] source.

## Current Profile
The source presents Yuri as an original AI character rather than a celebrity imitation. Hanqing says the project became plausible after [[OmniHuman]] improved expressive singing performance, then required a multi-model workflow across music, voice, image, lip sync, and video generation.

Yuri's current profile is an IP system: personality, visual identity, model assets, voice, songs, clothing, props, and future narrative are meant to compound over time. The source's key boundary is that Yuri should not merely pretend to be human. Her AI identity is part of the character, and real-time consumer chat is constrained because a virtual idol can still damage trust by saying the wrong thing.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuri is framed as an original AI-native virtual idol, not a public-figure recreation.
- The source dates the launch to 2025-06-03 and treats [[OmniHuman]] as an important enabling model window.
- The production workflow combines multiple models rather than depending on one end-to-end system.
- The core asset is personality and long-term narrative, not only a song, video, or model checkpoint.
- The visual identity includes East Asian facial features, blue-highlight hair, and non-standardized beauty in the source account.
- Consumer-facing dialogue remains limited because live AI interaction can create trust and brand risk.
