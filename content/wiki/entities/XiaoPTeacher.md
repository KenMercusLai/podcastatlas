# 小P老师 / Xiao P Teacher

小P老师 is the biotech early-research guest in [[vol-117-shengwu-yiyao-de-2025-chaodi-zhongguo-yanfa-jiaolv-he-xinwang-jiwei-lmhral0rmq6tohiqdwsgmfapnyn7]]. The episode introduces him as a scientist at a leading domestic innovation-drug company, and uses his research-side view to compare platform enthusiasm with clinical, BD, and company-execution reality.

His main contribution to the wiki is a sober 2025 biotech frame. He treats [[PD1MarketSaturation|PD-1]], mRNA, and [[CARTCellTherapy|CAR-T]] as important but no longer magical, then watches [[AIClinicalValidationInDrugDiscovery|AI drug]], [[DualPayloadADC|dual-payload ADCs]], and [[InVivoMRNACART|in vivo mRNA CAR-T]] as combination-innovation directions that still need clinical proof.
