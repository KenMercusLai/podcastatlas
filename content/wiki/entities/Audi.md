# Audi

Audi appears in [[zhongguo-xiaofeizhe-daidong-lafu-laolun-zengzhang-donghang-youhua-jipiao-tuigaiqian-zhengce-1005631805]] as one of the German luxury automakers facing weaker China demand. The source groups Audi with [[MercedesBenz|Mercedes-Benz]] and [[BMW]], saying all three saw first-half automotive-business margins below 4% and China sales down by roughly 20%.

In the wiki, Audi's role in this source is a comparative one. The episode uses the brand to show that [[GermanLuxuryCarChinaPressure]] is not isolated to one company: a broader premium-fuel-car value proposition is being challenged by local new-energy vehicles, discount discipline, and changing expectations in the RMB 300,000-500,000 range.
