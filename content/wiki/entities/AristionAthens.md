# Aristion of Athens / 雅典的阿里斯提昂

## Overview

Aristion of Athens / 雅典的阿里斯提昂 is the philosopher-politician who leads [[Athens]] into revolt against pro-Roman oligarchs and alliance with [[MithridatesVI]] during Sulla's eastern campaign.

## Current Profile

Episode 703 presents Aristion as a naturalized Athenian philosopher who turns resentment of wealthy pro-Roman collaborators into revolution. He leads the people against the plutocrats, sends envoys to Mithridates, and receives Pontic support. Once [[LuciusCorneliusSulla]] reaches Athens, Aristion has installed himself as tyrant and mocks the Roman commander from the walls while the city resists.

His profile is ultimately bound to the siege's failure. Athens starves while promised reinforcements do not arrive, Sulla rejects diplomatic appeals to ancient Athenian glory, and Roman troops sack the city in March 86 BC. Aristion flees to the Acropolis, is captured at Athena's altar, and is forced to drink poison.

## Key Characteristics

- Aristion converts anti-Roman and anti-oligarchic resentment into a revolutionary regime in Athens.
- His legitimacy depends on Mithridates's claim to be liberating Greek cities from Roman domination.
- He becomes a tyrant rather than a stable democratic restorer.
- His public defiance of Sulla contrasts with Athens's worsening starvation and strategic isolation.
- His death marks the failure of Athens's Mithridatic revolt and the end of remaining meaningful independence in the source's reading.

## Qualifications

The source gives Aristion through the Roman Civil War and Mithridatic War frame. It does not settle his philosophical school, earlier career, exact constitutional claims, or the full social composition of his supporters.
