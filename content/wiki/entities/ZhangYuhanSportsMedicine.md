## Current Profile
The source presents 张雨涵 as working with joint problems, especially shoulder and knee conditions. Her role is clinical translation: she explains meniscal shape and injury, cruciate and collateral ligaments, joint stability, postoperative quadriceps atrophy, support devices, and why ordinary patients and professional athletes may pursue different recovery endpoints.

Her contribution complements [[ZhouHaoRehabilitationEngineer|周浩]]'s engineering perspective. She turns proposed modeling capabilities into clinical questions about what should be measured, how rehabilitation changes over time, and when performance demands alter treatment choices.
