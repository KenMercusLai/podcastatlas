# Sebastian Galiani

Sebastian Galiani is the economist in [[currency-chaos-in-argentina-summer-school]] who explains Argentina's volatility, capital controls, fiscal deficit problem, and post-Milei stabilization attempt. The episode uses his comments to connect daily stories about dollars, jobs, and prices to [[CapitalControls]], [[MenuCosts]], [[Austerity]], and [[ExchangeRateFlexibility]].

Galiani argues that Argentina's alternating political factions often undo one another's reforms, creating volatility in currency values, debt levels, and the business climate. He also compares Argentina with [[Chile]] by noting that predictable mortgage markets depend on a stability that many Argentines lack.
