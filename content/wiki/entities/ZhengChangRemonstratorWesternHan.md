## Overview

郑昌 / Zheng Chang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-441-gaikuanrao-bei-hanxuandi-bisi-beihou-de-zhichang-zhenxiang-tai-fengci-lm7iqjl6m638ulcpvir1jv7gxho0|Hanji 441]] as a Western Han remonstrance official who defends [[GaiKuanraoWesternHan|盖宽饶]] after Gai's memorial is interpreted as treasonous under [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]].

## Current Profile

Zheng's profile is defined by one intervention. He argues that Gai is loyal and worried for the state, not a rebel, and that a censorial official with no powerful family backing will naturally have many enemies. His memorial also states his own office obligation: as a remonstrance official, he must speak when he believes a loyal official is being trapped by hostile interpretation.

The intervention fails. Xuan does not accept Zheng's defense, and Gai is handed to judicial process before killing himself. Zheng therefore functions as a witness to both the ethical value and the limited protective power of correct advice inside a hostile court setting.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han remonstrance official whose current wiki profile is bounded to the Gai Kuanrao case.
- Defender of loyal but politically exposed criticism.
- Analyst of why supervisory officials accumulate enemies when they lack kinship or elite backing.
- Example of failed protective remonstrance under a ruler already angered by the target.
