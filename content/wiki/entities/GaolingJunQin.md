# 高陵君 / Gaoling Jun (Qin)

高陵君 / Gaoling Jun appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-89-qinwang-jing-bei-ta-dui-mabaonan-ljugedx8qxhtuzd1ja-xiwxgse19]] as one of the four Qin nobles [[FanJu|范雎]] names when pressing [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] to confront internal power fragmentation.

The episode does not develop Gaoling Jun beyond the four-nobles list. His wiki role is therefore structural: he helps define [[QinFourNoblesCourtPower|秦国四贵权力结构]] as more than [[WeiRan|魏冉]] alone, showing that Qin's authority problem includes royal-brother and kin networks under the shadow of [[XuanTaihou|宣太后]].
