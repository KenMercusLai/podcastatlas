# 朱治 / Zhu Zhi (Late Han)

朱治 / Zhu Zhi (Late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-992-luanshi-you-duo-kepa-jiemi-changan-de-xuese-mengyan-ltuxzrb-1x5y2ctwo4drggxn5stz|Hanji 992]] as the former [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] officer who tells [[SunCe|孙策]] not to remain under [[YuanShu|袁术]]. He advises Sun Ce to move back toward Jiangdong, where family ties and local military openings can become a base. The transcript renders the name as "朱智"; this page keeps the existing canonical Zhu Zhi form.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-996-luanshi-zanghong-jiangyiqi-de-ren-xiachang-zui-biequ-lgpa-m6h6bvhyintnuj6zztopui76|Hanji 996]] later uses Zhu Zhi as the Danyang commandery officer who drives out the Wu commandery administrator and occupies Wu. That short Jiangdong branch shows local authority changing hands quickly in the same late-Han disorder that frames [[ZangHong|臧洪]]'s later loyalty crisis.

The source does not develop Zhu Zhi's broader career. His function here is to show that formal offices no longer guarantee control: one official can push out another, and the displaced administrator then looks for protection from [[YanBaihu|严白虎]], a local armed strongman.
