# 朱治 / Zhu Zhi (Late Han)

朱治 / Zhu Zhi (Late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-996-luanshi-zanghong-jiangyiqi-de-ren-xiachang-zui-biequ-lgpa-m6h6bvhyintnuj6zztopui76|Hanji 996]] as the Danyang commandery officer who drives out the Wu commandery administrator and occupies Wu. The episode uses this short Jiangdong branch as evidence that local authority is changing hands quickly in the same late-Han disorder that frames [[ZangHong|臧洪]]'s later loyalty crisis.

The source does not develop Zhu Zhi's broader career. His function here is to show that formal offices no longer guarantee control: one official can push out another, and the displaced administrator then looks for protection from [[YanBaihu|严白虎]], a local armed strongman.
