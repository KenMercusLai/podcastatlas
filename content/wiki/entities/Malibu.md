# Malibu

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-adam-carolla-on-californias-collapse-fires-failed-leadership-and-gyno-fascism-39710360]] adds [[Malibu]] as [[AdamCarolla|Adam Carolla]]'s home and a concrete example in his argument about [[CaliforniaPostFireRebuildingDelay]]. He says he was evacuated during the fire and argues that permitting, the [[CaliforniaCoastalCommission|California Coastal Commission]], and rebuilding rules make recovery hard enough that some owners may leave rather than rebuild.

[[tech-20260420-0420-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260420-0420-mp-tech-pod-128]] previously added [[MalibuHighSchool|Malibu High School]] as a project example for [[EmbodiedCarbonBuildingCode]], making Malibu relevant to the wiki's construction-policy branch even outside the Carolla episode.
