# Friends of Friendless Churches

## Overview

Friends of Friendless Churches is represented as a church-preservation organization working in England and Wales whose sites and expertise support public engagement with otherwise vulnerable historic churches.

## Current Profile

[[96-the-uks-best-churches-glt7919970372]] says the organization was founded in 1957 by Ivor Bulmer Thomas and a wider group including [[TSEliot|T. S. Eliot]], John Betjeman, John Piper, Lady Mander, and Roy Jenkins. It protects churches no longer used for worship in England and Wales; the episode says that 60 churches were then in its care and that it operated with a small grant.

Both this survey and [[133-christmas-churches-glt8843719433]] present the organization through [[RachelMorley]], who turns church objects, fabric, and stories into accessible cultural history. St Brothen's at Llanfrothen and St Michael and All Angels at Llanfihangel Rogiet are directly identified as connected sites. Its role is therefore both custodial and interpretive: preservation keeps buildings publicly accessible, while explanation reconnects them to politics, war, charity, literature, art, music, folklore, and community memory.

## Key Characteristics

- Organization founded in 1957 to preserve historic churches no longer used for worship.
- Works across England and Wales according to the earlier episode.
- Represented in both sources by Rachel Morley.
- Connects building care with interpretation of material and social history.
- Uses overlooked local sites to widen public understanding of church heritage.

## Evidence

- Foundation and remit - [[96-the-uks-best-churches-glt7919970372]] gives the 1957 founding, named founder group, redundant-church mission, and England-and-Wales scope.
- Custodial examples - [[96-the-uks-best-churches-glt7919970372]] identifies St Brothen's at Llanfrothen as a church in its care; [[133-christmas-churches-glt8843719433]] identifies Llanfihangel Rogiet as another connected site.
- Public interpretation - [[96-the-uks-best-churches-glt7919970372]] and [[133-christmas-churches-glt8843719433]] build broad historical surveys from church fabric, furnishings, art, graves, ruins, and stories through Morley's expertise.
- Public-support case - [[96-the-uks-best-churches-glt7919970372]] argues for accessible public inheritance, while [[133-christmas-churches-glt8843719433]] closes with an explicit appeal for support.

## Qualifications

The sources do not provide a full legal structure, current property inventory, conservation policy, funding history, or account of how churches enter its care. The stated 60-church portfolio and small grant describe the organization as presented in a September 2021 episode rather than a current institutional total, and the founder list may not be exhaustive.
