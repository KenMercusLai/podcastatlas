# Friends of Friendless Churches

## Overview

Friends of Friendless Churches is represented as a British church-preservation organization whose sites and expertise support public engagement with otherwise vulnerable historic churches.

## Current Profile

[[133-christmas-churches-glt8843719433]] presents the organization through [[RachelMorley]], a returning guest who turns church objects and fabric into accessible cultural history. One directly connected site is St Michael and All Angels at Llanfihangel Rogiet, where self-raising-flour inventor Henry Jones married; the episode also ends with an appeal for listeners to support the organization.

Its role in the source is therefore both custodial and interpretive. Preservation keeps buildings and their material traces available, while public explanation reconnects them to charity, food, literature, art, music, folklore, and community memory.

## Key Characteristics

- British organization associated with preserving historic churches.
- Represented in the episode by Rachel Morley.
- Connects building care with interpretation of material and social history.
- Uses overlooked local sites to widen public understanding of church heritage.
- Supported through an explicit listener appeal at the episode's close.
