# Coffee Processing Technology Boundary / 咖啡处理技术边界

## Definition
Coffee processing technology boundary is the distinction between legitimate processing or fermentation methods that shape coffee quality and the mistaken assumption that all strong processed flavor is artificial flavoring.

## Current Synthesis
The source treats processing as an important but bounded layer in Yunnan coffee. Fermentation, equipment, temperature control, humidity monitoring, sugar monitoring, color sorting, drying equipment, sheds, and raised beds can make coffee cleaner and more repeatable, but Yu Wenjie ranks variety first, microclimate second, and processing third for Yunnan flavor formation.

## Key Claims
- Fermentation and flavor additives should not be collapsed into the same category.
- Processing can make quality more scientific, uniform, clean, and repeatable.
- Processing cannot fully compensate for unsuitable variety or site conditions.
- Consumer understanding of processing may lag behind rapid specialty-coffee experimentation.
- Infrastructure such as color sorting, drying equipment, sheds, and raised beds supports quality at the farm or cooperative level.

## Evidence
- Fermentation boundary: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] records Yu Wenjie's claim that inoculated fermentation and added flavor are not the same.
- Bounded hierarchy: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] says variety comes first, special microclimate second, and processing third in Yunnan flavor factors.
- Repeatability: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] says earlier Yunnan experiments aimed to build more scientific, homogeneous, repeatable processing.
- Infrastructure: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] mentions investments in color sorting, drying, sheds, raised beds, temperature, humidity, and sugar monitoring.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not evaluate every fermentation method or prove which methods create better cups. It argues only for a more precise boundary between food-science processing and flavor-additive suspicion.
