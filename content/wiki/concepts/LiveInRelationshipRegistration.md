# Live-In Relationship Registration

Live-in relationship registration is the cohabitation-reporting provision described in [[settler-bet-a-west-bank-flashpoint-6a842bb539e73ea8f3c13a2d]]. In [[Uttarakhand]], the source says couples must register a live-in relationship within 30 days of moving in together or risk jail time.

The concept extends [[UniformCivilCodeIndia]] beyond marriage, divorce, and inheritance. [[KiraHuyu|Kira Huju]] frames the provision as a significant expansion of state surveillance over personal life, while the episode notes limited early uptake, with fewer than 100 registrations as of July.

## Key Claims
- A family-law reform can reach beyond formal marriage into cohabitation monitoring.
- Criminal penalties make registration more than a neutral data-collection exercise.
- Low registration numbers may signal weak implementation, social avoidance, or reluctance to disclose private relationships.
- The provision helps explain why the source treats the civil-code project as [[MajoritarianLegalUniformity]] plus surveillance rather than only equal-law reform.
