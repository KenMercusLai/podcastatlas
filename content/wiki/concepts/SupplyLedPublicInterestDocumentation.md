# Supply-Led Public-Interest Documentation / 供给创造需求的公共记录

## Definition

Supply-led public-interest documentation is the practice of recording overlooked people, places, and histories before a measurable audience demand or search query exists, allowing the work itself to reveal why the subject matters.

## Current Synthesis

The source calls this “抢救式记录”: ordinary lives, old shops, neighborhood memory, migration history, or aging witnesses may disappear before anyone knows what to ask. Demand-led content systems are poorly equipped to value such work because current clicks cannot represent future historical need or interest that only a finished story can awaken.

The concept is not a claim that editors or creators should ignore audiences. It marks a public-interest exception to using demonstrated demand as the primary production test. Investigation, interviewing, and preservation can create value for the creator, the subject, and a later audience even when immediate distribution remains small.

## Key Claims

- Some socially valuable subjects are invisible until documentation makes them discoverable.
- Rapid urban and generational change creates irreversible loss when memory is not recorded in time.
- Current demand and traffic are weak measures of future archive or historical value.
- Creator curiosity and editorial judgment can originate public demand rather than merely respond to it.
- Low immediate response does not establish that the work lacks public value.

## Evidence

- Rescue documentation: [[184-weile-zai-suanfa-shidai-bei-tingjian-women-gaibian-le-duoshao-ziji-duitan-shengdongjixi-zhangjing-lpotw0uqivlj8m6otnoxh0kahee5]] discusses ordinary people whose experiences are unknown until someone records them.
- Urban-memory evidence: [[184-weile-zai-suanfa-shidai-bei-tingjian-women-gaibian-le-duoshao-ziji-duitan-shengdongjixi-zhangjing-lpotw0uqivlj8m6otnoxh0kahee5]] points to disappearing restaurants, shops, residents, and aging intellectual or public figures in fast-changing Chinese cities and New York.
- Delayed-value evidence: [[184-weile-zai-suanfa-shidai-bei-tingjian-women-gaibian-le-duoshao-ziji-duitan-shengdongjixi-zhangjing-lpotw0uqivlj8m6otnoxh0kahee5]] uses Zhang Jing's World Trade Center reconstruction feature to illustrate intensive reporting whose learning and archive value exceeded its immediate response.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Creator conviction does not guarantee public importance, accuracy, consent, or responsible representation. Scarce editorial resources still require selection, and claims about future value are hard to validate at publication time. The source offers practitioner examples rather than a general method for ranking undocumented subjects.
