# Red Head Ships / 红头船

Red head ships are the Chaoshan maritime vessels discussed in [[no-205-chaoshan-wangshi-qiaopi-jingji-tequ-he-meiyou-denglaide-shenzhen-qiji-989721046]]. The episode ties the name to Qing-era coastal vessel color rules, then treats the ships as part of a trade system linking [[Chaoshan]], [[ZhanglinPort]], Siam rice, and Southeast Asia migration.

Their importance is practical and symbolic. Practically, they helped move goods and people across the South China Sea when local land and grain supply were constrained. Symbolically, they belong to [[ChaoshanMaritimeCommercialCulture]]: a vessel type became shorthand for a regional habit of solving scarcity through sea routes, trust, and overseas connections.

## Key Claims
- Shipping technology and state rules can become part of regional commercial identity.
- Grain trade with [[Thailand]] helped make overseas routes economically durable.
- Red head ships sit between commodity trade, migrant movement, and later [[QiaopiRemittanceNetworks]].
