# Career Cognition Education / 职业认知教育

Career cognition education / 职业认知教育 is the part of schooling that helps students understand an occupation before choosing or rejecting it. [[ep241-xiaoqi-hezuo-shi-xinyidai-de-tiefanwan-ma-lnmpsbvoqbexeardxyx88hywufig]] makes it a gap in [[VocationalEducation|职业教育]]: many students need more than technical courses, because they are deciding among further study, exams, company jobs, flexible work, and leaving the industry.

The source contrasts short-term income from ride-hailing or delivery with slower skill accumulation in technical or care roles. Its point is not that flexible work is always wrong, but that students need to see income curves, promotion routes, skill transfer, working conditions, and industry cycles before deciding what stability really means.

## Key Claims
- Career cognition links skill training to realistic knowledge of pay, promotion, occupational dignity, and work culture.
- Enterprises can provide information that many teachers lack if those teachers have limited recent workplace experience.
- The concept is especially important when [[SchoolEnterpriseCooperation|校企合作]] is marketed as stability: students need to know what the path requires and where it can fail.
- Good career cognition can reduce both blind credential chasing and blind entry into low-progression jobs.
