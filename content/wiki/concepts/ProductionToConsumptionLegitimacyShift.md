# Production-to-Consumption Legitimacy Shift / 从生产正当性到消费正当性

## Definition
Production-to-consumption legitimacy shift is the institutional and moral transition required when expanding output is no longer the obvious social priority and household demand, leisure, care, and consumption become necessary parts of economic coordination.

## Current Synthesis
The episode argues that scarcity made diligence, thrift, investment, property accumulation, and larger production morally intuitive. Persistent overcapacity changes that premise without automatically changing institutions or public judgment. When tax revenue, official evaluation, credit, and local competition still reward production more directly than consumption, governments can rationally attract similar factories and subsidize output even as households remain cautious and price competition intensifies.

The shift is not a command to consume indiscriminately. It is a demand to separate useful production, household welfare, demand formation, and moral worth. Consumption can circulate income, sustain services, support chosen brands and culture, and express preference, while still remaining bounded by affordability, environmental cost, manipulation, and unequal access.

## Key Claims
- The moral prestige of production was historically reinforced by genuine material scarcity.
- Overcapacity weakens the assumption that every additional unit of output creates proportional social value.
- Production-linked fiscal and evaluation incentives can sustain duplicate investment and price wars even when broader demand is weak.
- Consumption policy is constrained when the production location captures more fiscal benefit than the place bearing the subsidy or service cost.
- A consumption-oriented transition requires income, time, security, public services, and institutional incentives, not only exhortation or temporary discounts.
- Revaluing consumption does not make waste, debt, coercive marketing, or status competition automatically desirable.

### Consumption as participation
- [[e255-cong-dajieguo-dao-dahongguo-ni-hai-xiangxin-yue-nuli-yue-xingyun-ma-lmrfbkmfdmun7-cjfvaeogeo6fqi]] treats buying, supporting a favored brand, and other demand-side choices as possible votes for a preferred world rather than merely private indulgence.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source offers a macroeconomic interpretation, not a measured causal decomposition. It does not establish the scale of national overcapacity, the exact distribution of taxes, the net welfare effect of subsidies, or a complete reform design. Production remains necessary, technological upgrading can raise welfare, exports can support income, and consumption can create debt, waste, or environmental harm. The claim is that legitimacy and incentives must become more balanced, not that production has ceased to matter.
