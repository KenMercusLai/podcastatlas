## Definition
Women's football visibility / 女足主体性可见度 is the condition in which women footballers and women's teams are watched, discussed, supported, and criticized as their own sporting subjects rather than mainly as symbols used to praise or shame another group.

## Current Synthesis
The Zhao Lina source treats visibility as necessary but incomplete. Public attention after China women's football wins can bring luck and opportunity, but it can also erase subjectivity when women's success is used mainly to criticize men's football. The source points toward a more durable form of visibility: local fans, girls who choose football, live matches, and media stories that let women athletes be understood through their own pressure, tactics, personality, and transition.

## Key Claims
- Attention after victory can expand recognition while still making women athletes dependent on result spikes.
- Comparison with men's football can be flattering on the surface but subjectivity-erasing when women become a tool for another debate.
- Local fan communities make women's football more durable because they watch the team itself rather than only viral national moments.
- Girls' football participation needs visible women football scenes that present football as an available choice.
- Athlete-controlled or athlete-centered media can help women athletes narrate their own complexity beyond beauty, toughness, or national pride labels.

## Evidence
- Comparison problem: [[zhao-lina-zaichang-bi-ying-zhongyao-menjiang-de-xinxin-zhijue-yu-reai-229fde7d-d39d-4f7c-a33d-607f5370fc60]] says Chinese women's football is often pushed into visibility after wins and used in comparison with men's football, which Zhao connects to lack of collective female subjectivity.
- Local fan development: [[zhao-lina-zaichang-bi-ying-zhongyao-menjiang-de-xinxin-zhijue-yu-reai-229fde7d-d39d-4f7c-a33d-607f5370fc60]] mentions Shanghai Jinshan local women's football fan growth as evidence that women football spectatorship can develop directly.
- Girls' choice and athlete narration: [[zhao-lina-zaichang-bi-ying-zhongyao-menjiang-de-xinxin-zhijue-yu-reai-229fde7d-d39d-4f7c-a33d-607f5370fc60]] links football atmosphere, girls' voluntary participation, post-retirement podcasting, and multiple labels around Zhao's public role.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Comparison with men's football can still bring attention, funding pressure, or broad awareness; the source's caution is that attention is not the same as subjectivity. The current page is China- and Zhao-source scoped until more women's football sources are ingested.
