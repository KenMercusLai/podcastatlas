# Warring States Retainer Patronage / 战国养士

Warring States retainer patronage / 战国养士 is the system [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-41-mengchangjun-wo-shi-ruhe-dabai-zhe-40-duo-wei-gege-de-ltxgq6oxo1zlkkc6xqjo8-5htns4]] builds from [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]] after he inherits [[XueWarringStates|薛地]]. The episode presents养士 as privately funded political infrastructure: a powerful lord uses wealth, housing, food, courtesy, and attention to retainers' families to gather mobile士人 and fugitives around himself.

The concept has both a demand side and a supply side. On the patron side, Mengchang Jun's inherited wealth and guest-facing household office let him turn spending into reputation. On the士人 side, the episode says many wandering figures have good birth or education but insufficient family support; before men such as [[WuQi|吴起]], [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]], [[SuQin|苏秦]], and [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] become famous, they belong to this wider labor market of men looking for a patron who can recognize and use them.

This differs from simple generosity. The episode stresses礼数 and self-respect: giving works because it preserves the receiver's dignity. That makes the system adjacent to [[RetainerReciprocityEthic|士为知己者死]] and [[AristocraticHonorOverLife|贵族名誉高于生命]], but also unstable. The same dignity sensitivity that makes recognition powerful can make small slights feel intolerable, as in the banquet story where a guest mistakes lighting for unequal treatment and kills himself after the misunderstanding is corrected.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-42-gei-mengchangjun-ti-yijian-de-houguo-lr1jp0tpiaboaca3j5-yvyhyn4vf]] adds [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s ethical test for the same system. For Sima Guang,养士 is legitimate when it supports worthy people so they can serve the state and the people; it becomes corrupt when a lord indiscriminately gathers capable and incapable, good and bad, into a private force. The episode therefore turns this page from a mechanism description into a two-sided concept: patronage can be talent infrastructure, but it can also be private power built from public wealth.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-43-mengchangjun-qizi-touqing-bei-faxian-ta-zha-chuli-lr2idcktgfxtdkik1biaj9tiz8ix]] adds the governance cost of that private force. Mengchang Jun's retainers can retaliate against public mockery in [[ZhaoState|赵国]] with mass killing, and a retainer's affair with Mengchang Jun's wife can threaten household order. The same episode also shows why patrons kept using such people: under [[RetainerRiskConversion|门客风险转化]], a dangerous retainer sent to [[WeyState|卫国]] can later protect [[QiState|齐国]] diplomatically.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-63-degao-wangzhong-mengchangjun-shi-weijunzi-lmd0owe3rq-faponbwzhkaigprin]] adds a sharper quality critique through [[WangAnshi|王安石]]. [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]]'s retainers help him escape [[QinState|秦国]], but the episode argues that this does not prove he knows how to find great talent. It creates [[JimingGoudaoTalentCritique|鸡鸣狗盗式人才批评]] as the negative side of patronage: if a lord's门下 is dominated by low-level tricks and mixed-quality people, real statecraft talent may choose not to enter that network at all.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-64-gudai-gangjing-gongsunlong-yangchengji-lkrchesllfi9ipgfapft7av2mnh5]] extends the concept from Mengchangjun to [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]]. Pingyuanjun's circle includes [[GongsunLong|公孙龙]], whose value is intellectual and rhetorical rather than military. The episode therefore adds a new patronage test: a great household can attract dazzling specialists, but the patron still needs enough judgment to tell [[MingjiaDisputation|名辩]] apart from [[TruthSeekingDebateNorm|truth-seeking debate]].

## Key Claims
- Patronage is infrastructure when money, lodging, food, social rank, kin outreach, and public reputation are organized into a durable talent network.
- Warring States mobility creates a market for patrons because educated or high-born but unsupported men need a place where ability can turn into office, protection, or fame.
- Respect is not ornamental. The episode treats dignity protection as the mechanism that makes material support feel like recognition rather than charity.
- Large-scale养士 can magnify a lord's reputation, but it also imports volatile honor codes into political life.
- The system sits between [[TalentAttractionThroughRespect|礼贤下士式人才吸附]] and [[WarringStatesCareeristMobility|乱世职业经理人式流动]]: it is both a patron's recruitment design and a mobile professional class's survival strategy.
- Sima Guang's critique adds a public-purpose gate: without selection for virtue and public service, retainer patronage can become private clique-building.
- The system creates control problems as well as talent options: retainers can become violent honor enforcers or household risks, but can sometimes be redeployed into external leverage.
- Zhouji 63 adds a quality-signaling failure: indiscriminate openness can make a patron famous while making truly capable people doubt the network.
- Zhouji 64 adds an intellectual-quality failure: rhetorical brilliance inside a patronage circle may still need outside critics to test whether it serves truth.
