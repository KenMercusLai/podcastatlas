# Local Battery Industrial Clusters

Local Battery Industrial Clusters is the city-level industrial-policy pattern in [[e229-cong-shougong-zuofang-dao-quanqiu-diyi-zhongguo-dongli-dianchi-nixi-shi]]. The episode uses [[Changzhou]] and [[Yibin]] to show how local governments combine funds, land, suppliers, talent pipelines, electricity, universities, and anchor firms to create battery-production capacity.

The concept is not just "subsidy." Changzhou shows supplier density and a four-hour industrial circle, while Yibin shows how a later city can use green power, hydropower, a university city, and [[CATL]] to join an existing national chain.

[[ep241-xiaoqi-hezuo-shi-xinyidai-de-tiefanwan-ma-lnmpsbvoqbexeardxyx88hywufig]] adds the talent-pipeline layer. [[Changzhou|常州]]'s new-energy-vehicle cluster helps explain why [[ChangzhouIndustrialVocationalTechnicalCollege|常州工业职业技术学院]] and [[LiAuto|理想汽车]] can build an [[IndustrialCollege|产业学院]] around nearby industry demand rather than only generic classroom training.

[[chengshi-jiushi-zheyang-21-shiyan-yizuo-yin-qiche-erqi-you-bugan-bei-qiche-paoxia-de-chengshi-1001770066]] adds a legacy-city variant through [[Shiyan]]. Unlike Changzhou or Yibin in the earlier battery-industrialization branch, Shiyan starts from [[SecondAutomobileWorks|二汽]], [[DongfengMotor|东风汽车]], and a [[CommercialVehicleIndustrialBase|commercial-vehicle industrial base]], then tries to connect into batteries through [[EnvisionAESC]] and [[WanrunNewEnergy]] as part of diversification.
