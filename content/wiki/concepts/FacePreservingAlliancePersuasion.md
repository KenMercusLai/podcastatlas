# Face-Preserving Alliance Persuasion / 保全体面的结盟说服

## Definition
Face-preserving alliance persuasion is a weak-side coalition method that makes an exposed actor's necessary dependence acceptable by describing it as reciprocal partnership, demonstrating the counterparty's value, and providing a trusted personal bridge.

## Current Synthesis
Hanji 1099 presents [[LuSu|鲁肃]]'s approach to [[LiuBei|刘备]] after Changban as a three-part persuasion sequence. He contrasts [[SunQuan|孙权]]'s Jiangdong capacity with [[WuJuLateHan|吴巨]]'s weak refuge, calls the proposed relationship cooperation in a shared enterprise rather than submission, and invokes his friendship with [[ZhugeJin|诸葛瑾]] to connect the offer to [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]].

The method does not remove power asymmetry. Its practical effect is to let a damaged actor choose the stronger alliance without publicly accepting the identity of a dependent refugee. The episode further argues that changing the sequence—from first taking Jingzhou to first building the alliance—can reduce exposure when territorial acquisition would otherwise provoke both neighboring powers.

## Key Claims
- Partner-value contrast can turn a retreat from a weak fallback into an apparent strategic upgrade.
- Equalizing language helps preserve the target's status while still moving them toward necessary dependence.
- A personal relationship bridge reduces uncertainty between camps that do not yet share durable institutions.
- Alliance sequencing can matter as much as alliance choice when acquiring contested territory too early would create a two-front threat.

## Evidence
- Value contrast: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1099-liubei-kuai-pochan-shi-ruhe-nixi-panshang-sunquan-llnev7pzbraogd00u0h7i7ba65eq|Hanji 1099]] has Lu Su praise Jiangdong's territory, troops, supplies, and talent while dismissing Wu Ju's refuge as vulnerable.
- Status-preserving language: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1099-liubei-kuai-pochan-shi-ruhe-nixi-panshang-sunquan-llnev7pzbraogd00u0h7i7ba65eq|Hanji 1099]] emphasizes Lu Su's framing of the move as common enterprise rather than living under another ruler.
- Trust bridge and sequence: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1099-liubei-kuai-pochan-shi-ruhe-nixi-panshang-sunquan-llnev7pzbraogd00u0h7i7ba65eq|Hanji 1099]] links Lu Su to Zhuge Jin and interprets alliance-before-Jingzhou as a survival advantage.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is a popular-history narration that attributes precise intent and emotional effect to the participants. It does not establish that Liu Bei saw himself as an equal, that Zhuge Jin's relationship materially secured the alliance, or that alliance-first sequencing was costless. The concept is narrower than [[DignityBasedPersuasion|尊严激将式说服]], which often uses shame or status provocation; here dignity is protected to make cooperation acceptable.

## What Changed
- Created the concept from Lu Su's post-Changban alliance pitch in Hanji 1099.
