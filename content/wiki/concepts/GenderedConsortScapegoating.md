## Definition

红颜祸水式替罪叙事 / gendered consort scapegoating is the pattern where dynastic failure, ruler weakness, succession crisis, or court violence is condensed into blame against a favored woman, especially when the accusation offers a vivid moral story and an easy political target.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 573 creates the concept through [[ZhaoFeiyanWesternHan|赵飞燕]] and [[ZhaoHedeWesternHan|赵合德]]. Hanji 571-572 now supply the severe accusation material that makes the pattern hard to flatten into simple exoneration: [[CaoGongWesternHan|曹宫]] accuses the Zhao sisters before dying, a Zhao Zhaoyi-linked messenger appears in the chain around her son's disappearance, and Zhao Hede is reported as pressuring Chengdi after [[XuMeirenWesternHan|徐美人]] gives birth. Hanji 573 then argues that the sisters' received image as causes of [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]'s no-heir disaster may still hide a more complicated evidence and power problem: Chengdi's children had died young before the Zhao sisters' involvement, the Liu imperial line shows broader fragility in the source's reading, and the later investigation is politically useful to [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] and anti-Zhao actors.

The concept is not a claim that favored women are never politically active or never dangerous. It is a warning about narrative compression. A palace scandal can become easier to remember and retell when structural succession weakness, ruler health, factional rivalry, and evidentiary uncertainty are personified as a beautiful woman's destructive power.

## Key Claims

- Gendered blame can turn succession and institutional problems into a simpler morality tale about beauty destroying rule.
- Scapegoating is especially likely when a sudden death or heir failure demands a culprit.
- Literary repetition can stabilize a gendered image even when the underlying case remains evidentially uncertain.
- The pattern should be distinguished from exoneration: a consort may remain suspicious while still being overused as an explanation.
- Direct accusation against a consort network can coexist with caution against making favored women the whole causal explanation.
- Emotional-consort pressure in a source can be historically important without becoming a complete account of dynastic heir failure.
- Reading against scapegoating requires checking evidence, political incentives, and non-consort causal possibilities.

## Evidence

- Severe accusation layer: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-571-hanchengdi-qinshou-shazi-shi-zhen-shi-jia-lhjrwpg-t6izp__tshwzem1t0dyzh|Hanji 571]] says Cao Gong accused the Zhao sisters before dying and links a Zhao Zhaoyi messenger to the pressure around the infant.
- Xu Meiren pressure layer: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-572-lishishang-zhe-nanren-zhen-gou-hen-lkd0wugllcr6ewmmz6lyximyzl6c|Hanji 572]] says Zhao Hede challenged Chengdi over Xu Meiren's child, with fear of Xu-family revival mixed into the emotional accusation.
- Broader palace-control allegation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-572-lishishang-zhe-nanren-zhen-gou-hen-lkd0wugllcr6ewmmz6lyximyzl6c|Hanji 572]] says Wu Qiuzun claimed Yeting officials were Zhao Hede's people and that women who bore imperial children had been killed.
- Pre-Zhao child loss: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-573-zhaofeiyan-jiemei-shi-hongyanhuoshui-haishi-tizuigaoyang-lkacfndvi8t1opep67xmquyzr_ww|Hanji 573]] says Chengdi had children by Xu family women and Ban Jieyu who died young before the Zhao sisters explain the problem.
- Political target formation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-573-zhaofeiyan-jiemei-shi-hongyanhuoshui-haishi-tizuigaoyang-lkacfndvi8t1opep67xmquyzr_ww|Hanji 573]] says Aidi had reason to attack Zhao outer-relatives and that many people resented the Zhao sisters.
- Evidence uncertainty: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-573-zhaofeiyan-jiemei-shi-hongyanhuoshui-haishi-tizuigaoyang-lkacfndvi8t1opep67xmquyzr_ww|Hanji 573]] says the investigation lacks expected physical evidence.
- Literary afterlife: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-573-zhaofeiyan-jiemei-shi-hongyanhuoshui-haishi-tizuigaoyang-lkacfndvi8t1opep67xmquyzr_ww|Hanji 573]] says Zhao Feiyan stories remain durable partly because later writers repeatedly adapt them.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source set does not erase the received suspicion around Zhao Hede and Zhao Feiyan. Hanji 571-572 record damaging accusation chains, and `Han Shu` and `Zizhi Tongjian` still matter as the tradition being challenged. The concept should therefore be used where the issue is narrative overconcentration of blame, not as a blanket defense of all court favorites.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 572's Xu Meiren and Yeting-control accusation layers while preserving Hanji 573's warning against narrative overconcentration of blame.
