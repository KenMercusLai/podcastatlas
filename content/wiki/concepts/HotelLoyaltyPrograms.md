# Hotel Loyalty Programs

Hotel loyalty programs are the points, elite tiers, benefits, direct-booking incentives, and guest-data systems that hotel groups use to turn lodging from a one-time commodity comparison into a repeat relationship. In [[140-jiudian-jituan-weishenme-dou-rezhongyu-dazao-huiyuan-he-jifen-tixi-chuantai-yuanxingzhe-yu-suibingjiang-lj-xa3wxzjog6-8zm9nfakh6ulew]], [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]], [[MaocaoYuanxingzhe|猫草]], and [[CindyYuanxingzhe|Cindy]] argue that hotels need this machinery because their supply is fragmented, substitutes are easy to compare, and brand trust has to be rebuilt on every stay.

The source distinguishes hotel loyalty from simple "points hacking." A loyalty program can raise [[CustomerLifetimeValue]], push users into [[HotelDirectBookingChannels]], make [[HotelBrandPortfolio|multi-brand portfolios]] legible, and give staff data that supports better service. Its risk is that benefits become purely financial or gamified, losing the hospitality purpose that makes guests return for recognition, not only for rebates.

## Key Claims
- Hotel loyalty works by expanding the time horizon of a booking decision across future stays.
- Tier thresholds, lifetime status, free breakfast, upgrades, and points change user choice by making the next stay feel connected to the current stay.
- Loyalty programs can counter [[OnlineTravelAgency]] power when direct channels are cheaper or more valuable than OTA booking.
- Loyalty is stronger when benefits reinforce reliable service standards rather than hide inconsistent property quality.
- The hotel version is more service-heavy than [[AirlineLoyaltyProgramMoat]] because the lodging product varies by property, staff, location, and trip purpose.
