# Submission Through Status Elevation / 尊号式投降方案

Submission through status elevation / 尊号式投降方案 is the pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-2-ltlobdujy-hdipimfdupvwod2ech]] builds from [[XinyuanYan|新垣衍]]'s proposal during the [[Handan|邯郸]] siege. With [[JinBi|晋鄙]] halted at [[YeWarringStates|邺城]] and [[ChuState|楚国]] unable to easily reach [[ZhaoState|赵国]] without [[WeiState|魏国]] transit, Xin Yuan Yan suggests that Wei and Zhao honor [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] as western emperor.

The concept names a surrender-like move that operates through status rather than immediate land transfer. The episode argues that calling Qin's ruler emperor would not merely flatter him; it would acknowledge Qin's superior order and ask for withdrawal by accepting a lower position. That makes the proposal different from ordinary alliance ritual and closer to political submission.

The case sits opposite [[DignityBasedPersuasion|尊严激将式说服]]. [[MaoSui|毛遂]] persuades [[MiWan|楚考烈王]] to resist Qin by making humiliation intolerable, while Xin Yuan Yan tries to make a humiliating status concession look like the remaining practical exit from annihilation.

## Key Claims
- Status concessions can function as diplomatic capitulation even without immediate territorial cession.
- A defeated or besieged state may consider symbolic submission when military rescue, transit access, and coalition reliability all fail.
- The political cost is high because recognizing the enemy's emperor title also rewrites the hierarchy among states.
- The source frames the western-emperor plan as crisis appeasement, not as a neutral honorific.
