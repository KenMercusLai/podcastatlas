## Definition

Chinese astrological politics / 中国星占政治 is the use of celestial phenomena, planetary movement, stellar regions, and sky-earth correspondences as evidence about rulers, ministers, war, legitimacy, and state conduct. It tracks interpretation and political deployment, not modern astronomical validity.

## Current Synthesis

The current sources show astrological politics as a technical language that could be folded into court decision-making. Fangshu materials explain the background: sky observation, twenty-eight mansions, five visible planets, Jupiter-year counting, fenye correspondences, yin-yang/five-phases classification, and specialist interpretation made celestial signs available for political use.

The political uses split in two directions. One path reads the sky as warning or diagnosis, such as Qin's "Mars guarding Heart" crisis and late-Han court predictions. Another path turns a favorable configuration into action language, as in Hanji 689-3's five-planet eastern alignment formula. The [[FiveStarsOutOfTheEastBrocade]] is important because it materializes astrological politics in textile form, carrying a sky sign into tomb, campaign memory, and possible Western Regions diplomacy.

The strongest synthesis is that celestial language had political force when it connected a sky event to a jurisdiction, person, policy, or enemy. Without that interpretive step, a planet or star was just a phenomenon; with it, the same phenomenon could become evidence for responsibility, purge, legitimacy, campaign timing, or victory memory.

## Key Claims

- Astrological politics depends on translating sky phenomena into earthly jurisdictions, persons, offices, or military outcomes.
- Specialist systems made celestial evidence reusable across dynastic legitimacy, court warning, and frontier war.
- Negative signs could diagnose ruler danger or justify coercive action against ministers.
- Favorable alignments could be turned into victory language and diplomatic-political messaging.
- Material objects can preserve and circulate astrological political claims outside the court record.
- Modern astronomical comparison can clarify the kind of event being discussed but does not prove ancient political meaning.

## Evidence

- Technical infrastructure: [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-wei-wan-daixu-704051997|Fangshu history part 1]] describes early sky observation, twenty-eight mansions, planetary tracking, Jupiter-year counting, fenye mappings, and yin-yang/five-phases frames as the background for political astrology.
- System consolidation: [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-jie-shangji-704051550|Fangshu history part 2]] traces Jing Fang star-omen methods, chenwei, Wang Mang's Dipper-linked legitimacy, and the later systematization of fangshu and Daoist techniques.
- Imperial warning signs: [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-126-qinshihuang-siyin-zhimi-1-lgtlhau6ixqglk1ict1fs0jexjwe|Qinji 126 part 1]] explains "Mars guarding Heart" as an astrological warning attached to the ruler's fate during Qin Shi Huang's late omen crisis.
- Coercive court use: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] shows Dong Zhuo turning an astrologer's minister-death prediction into justification for killing Zhang Wen.
- Five-planet victory language: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-689-3-wuxing-chu-dongfang-li-zhongguo-yige-gulao-yuyan-de-xiandai-jiedu-lqccxzbvg-jziurfxm5tr5k4jlvq|Hanji 689-3]] connects five visible planets appearing in the east to an auspicious formula, the Niya brocade, Zhao Chongguo's Southern Qiang campaign, and possible Western Regions diplomatic messaging.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

These sources do not ask the wiki to endorse celestial causation. Qinji 126 treats "Mars guarding Heart" as evidence of political pressure and narrative framing, not proof that Qin Shi Huang's death was star-caused. Hanji 957 shows interpretation can be manipulated by coercive rulers. Hanji 689-3 uses a modern 2040-09-09 comparison to explain five-planet gathering, but the episode's own artifact gift, warning, and talisman readings remain bounded interpretations.
