## Definition
朝廷盟友保护网络 / Court Ally Protection Network is the pattern in which a politically exposed official survives accusation or punishment because credible allies with rank, ruler access, public pressure, and usable procedural language move a defense through the court before a hostile channel hardens.

## Current Synthesis
The current bounded cases show protection as a channel problem, not simple popularity. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-806-xiangyao-chengshi-zhaodui-ren-hen-guanjian-luswh-p-oyutysxue6shweqcwqxs|Hanji 806]], [[YuXuEasternHan|虞诩]]'s accusation against [[ZhangFangEunuchLateHan|张防]] is buried and then reversed through Zhang Fang's access to [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]]. Yu Xu survives only when friends bring in [[SunChengLateHan|孙程]], family and students make the case publicly visible, and [[GaoFanLateHan|高梵]] carries that pressure back into the palace.

In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-818-guanchang-weishenme-yao-duo-jiaoyou-neng-jiuming-liyziudpezaoa4l6pbsq0wk2qdjdr|Hanji 818]], [[WangGongTaiweiLateHan|王龚]] faces a different version of the same danger after anti-eunuch speech. [[LiGuLateHan|李固]] converts Wang Gong's danger into an institutional argument: a Three Excellency should not be hauled before lower judicial offices unless the charge is grave enough to justify that humiliation. [[LiangShangLateHan|梁商]] then carries that argument to Shun, whose cancellation of the inquiry turns status, relationship, and access into survival.

The concept is intentionally bounded. The same Hanji 806 sequence later shows Sun Cheng's own accession-merit group becoming politically containable, while Hanji 818 shows Wang Gong abandoning an impeachment plan against Zhang Fang after relatives invoke [[YangZhenLateHan|杨震]]. Ally protection can keep one case alive long enough to be judged, but it does not dissolve palace retaliation or make contributors permanently secure.

## Key Claims
- Court friendships become protection only when allies have credibility, access, and willingness to act quickly.
- Public pressure can make a buried case harder to ignore when ordinary memorial and prison channels are captured.
- A procedural or status argument can slow a retaliatory accusation without requiring the accused official to win the whole factional conflict.
- Effective protection combines relationship capital with institutional language that the ruler can accept.
- The network protects a vulnerable decision point, not the official's long-term freedom from political danger.
- The cases qualify heroic remonstrance: courage needs channels, and channels can still be too risky against entrenched palace power.

## Evidence
- Captured initial channel: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-806-xiangyao-chengshi-zhaodui-ren-hen-guanjian-luswh-p-oyutysxue6shweqcwqxs|Hanji 806]] says Yu Xu's memorials against Zhang Fang are not acted on and Zhang Fang's complaint turns punishment back onto Yu Xu.
- Public pressure plus palace report: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-806-xiangyao-chengshi-zhaodui-ren-hen-guanjian-luswh-p-oyutysxue6shweqcwqxs|Hanji 806]] says Yu Xu's family and students petition Gao Fan, who reports the matter to Shun.
- Access ally intervention: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-806-xiangyao-chengshi-zhaodui-ren-hen-guanjian-luswh-p-oyutysxue6shweqcwqxs|Hanji 806]] says Sun Cheng confronts Shun and asks that Zhang Fang be arrested, helping force the case into judgment.
- Immediate exposure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-818-guanchang-weishenme-yao-duo-jiaoyou-neng-jiuming-liyziudpezaoa4l6pbsq0wk2qdjdr|Hanji 818]] says Wang Gong's anti-eunuch memorial is followed by an accusation from eunuch-linked clients and Shun's order to appear.
- Li Gu and Liang Shang's conversion chain: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-818-guanchang-weishenme-yao-duo-jiaoyou-neng-jiuming-liyziudpezaoa4l6pbsq0wk2qdjdr|Hanji 818]] says Li Gu writes to Liang Shang, frames Wang Gong's treatment as improper humiliation of a Three Excellency, and Liang Shang presents the argument to Shun.
- Bounded protection: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-806-xiangyao-chengshi-zhaodui-ren-hen-guanjian-luswh-p-oyutysxue6shweqcwqxs|Hanji 806]] later contains Sun Cheng's merit backlash, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-818-guanchang-weishenme-yao-duo-jiaoyou-neng-jiuming-liyziudpezaoa4l6pbsq0wk2qdjdr|Hanji 818]] later has Wang Gong stop short of impeaching Zhang Fang after relatives cite Yang Zhen.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The current evidence is two Shun-era court cases. It does not prove that broad networking reliably saves officials, and it should not be reduced to "having friends" as a generic cure. Yu Xu needs public witness and palace access after ordinary channels fail; Wang Gong needs Li Gu's procedural argument and Liang Shang's access; both cases remain bounded by later retaliation risk, contributor containment, and Zhang Fang's continuing intimidation value.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 806's Yu Xu rescue chain as the second bounded case.
- Reframed the concept around channel access, public pressure, and procedural conversion rather than elite friendship alone.
- Preserved Hanji 818's Wang Gong case as a status-and-access version of the same protection pattern.
- Added stronger limits from Sun Cheng's merit backlash and Wang Gong's later retreat from Zhang Fang.
