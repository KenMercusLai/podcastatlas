# Resource Abundance Coordination Burden

## Definition

Resource abundance coordination burden is the loss of execution quality that can occur when additional money, people, technologies, objectives, and stakeholder obligations increase integration demands faster than an organization can establish priorities and a coherent operating system.

## Current Synthesis

[[RobertFalconScott]]'s Antarctic expedition is the grounding case. Its substantial funding enabled 65 crew members, motor sledges, ponies, dogs, scientific programs, and national ambition, but the portfolio produced physical overload, incompatible transport assumptions, missing repair capability, delayed movement, and competing definitions of success. Abundance became a burden through interaction, not through quantity alone.

The contrast with [[RoaldAmundsen]] is therefore conditional. His smaller expedition concentrated resources on a dog-and-ski system and one symbolic objective, but that does not prove that poverty or narrow missions are inherently superior. Resources remain advantages when they are tested, integrated, repairable, governed by clear priorities, and protected from stakeholder-driven goal multiplication.

## Key Claims

- Additional resources create value only when coordination capacity grows with them.
- Multiple unintegrated technologies can amplify failure modes instead of providing useful redundancy.
- Funding and patronage can add objectives and decision constraints as well as material capacity.
- A clear primary goal helps resolve tradeoffs among cargo, methods, timing, and personnel.
- Scientific or public value outside the primary goal should be counted rather than dismissed as distraction by definition.
- Scarcity can enforce focus, but underfunding can also remove safety margins, expertise, and resilience.

## Evidence

- Physical overload - [[tim-harford-cools-us-down-this-summer-tim-harford-cools-us-down-this-summer]] says Scott's ship nearly sank under personnel, fuel, animals, and machinery.
- Technology portfolio - [[tim-harford-cools-us-down-this-summer-tim-harford-cools-us-down-this-summer]] reports a lost motor sledge, two later breakdowns without repair expertise, unsuitable ponies, dogs, and eventual human hauling.
- Goal multiplication - [[tim-harford-cools-us-down-this-summer-tim-harford-cools-us-down-this-summer]] joins the pole race to magnetic, biological, and other scientific objectives.
- Stakeholder burden - [[tim-harford-cools-us-down-this-summer-tim-harford-cools-us-down-this-summer]] links funding and [[ClementsMarkham]]'s influence to obligations that constrained Scott's focus.
- Focused contrast - [[tim-harford-cools-us-down-this-summer-tim-harford-cools-us-down-this-summer]] presents Amundsen's smaller dog-based expedition as a coherent system organized around one goal.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The episode supplies a vivid comparative case, not a general causal study. Scott's scientific objectives had independent value, weather and geography matter, and diversified methods can improve resilience when operators can test, maintain, and coordinate them. Amundsen's focus also depended on adequate resources, learned expertise, and ethically troubling deception. The concept should diagnose a mismatch between resource variety and integration capacity, not justify austerity or dismiss plural missions.

## What Changed

- Created the concept by separating the coordination cost of abundance from a simplistic claim that fewer resources are always better.
