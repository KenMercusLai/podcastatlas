# Mental-Health Symptom Escalation / 心理症状就医升级边界

## Definition
Mental-health symptom escalation is the public-education rule that professional assessment becomes more important as emotional, cognitive, behavioral, sleep, or bodily symptoms grow severe, persist, impair ordinary functioning, or depart substantially from the person's usual baseline.

## Current Synthesis
The sources resist three shortcuts: dismissing distress as “just personality,” converting one symptom into a remote diagnosis, and treating everyday “depressed” language as proof of a depressive disorder. Anxiety may appear through worry, fear, sleep problems, gastrointestinal symptoms, breathing difficulty, chest tightness, headache, weakness, numbness, or other bodily experiences, while low mood may include sadness, disappointment, low energy, or loss of interest. Clinical meaning depends on the whole pattern and context.

The practical screen combines symptom form, severity, duration, functional impairment, change from baseline, and safety. VOL.57 gives depressive symptoms persisting for roughly two weeks with substantial life impact as a source-scoped example, while VOL.72 warns against turning one duration threshold into a universal rule. A comprehensive psychiatric interview and appropriate supporting assessment carry more weight than an MBTI result, everyday vocabulary, or one isolated checklist. Suicidal thinking, self-harm, psychosis-linked danger, or inability to remain safe bypasses routine watchful waiting and enters [[MentalHealthCrisisInterventionBoundary|urgent escalation]].

## Key Claims
- Emotional distress can present through thoughts, feelings, behavior, sleep, and bodily symptoms.
- Severity, persistence, functional impairment, and change from personal baseline matter together when considering escalation.
- One symptom, personality label, or self-test cannot establish a psychiatric diagnosis.
- Depressed mood and a depressive disorder overlap but are not interchangeable; symptom pattern, duration, severity, function, and risk must be assessed together.
- Comprehensive clinician interviewing is central because symptom meaning depends on history, context, pattern, and differential assessment.
- Acute self-harm, suicide, psychosis-related, or safety risk requires a faster crisis pathway than routine symptom monitoring.

## Evidence
- Multidomain symptoms - [[vol-72-jingshenke-bie-tai-na-xingge-ceshi-dangshi-sanjia-jingshenke-yisheng-jiao-ni-he-zhichang-zuo-jianya-qiege-li6jofbvucknthuqw1flm-r0m4kw]] describes worry, fear, sleep disturbance, diarrhea, breathing difficulty, chest tightness, headache, weakness, numbness, and related bodily experiences as possible parts of anxiety presentations.
- Escalation dimensions - [[vol-72-jingshenke-bie-tai-na-xingge-ceshi-dangshi-sanjia-jingshenke-yisheng-jiao-ni-he-zhichang-zuo-jianya-qiege-li6jofbvucknthuqw1flm-r0m4kw]] emphasizes symptom severity, duration, functional impact, and comparison with the person's prior baseline.
- Assessment boundary - [[vol-72-jingshenke-bie-tai-na-xingge-ceshi-dangshi-sanjia-jingshenke-yisheng-jiao-ni-he-zhichang-zuo-jianya-qiege-li6jofbvucknthuqw1flm-r0m4kw]] places diagnosis in comprehensive interviewing with supporting psychological assessment rather than a personality test alone.
- Help-seeking and medication boundary - [[vol-72-jingshenke-bie-tai-na-xingge-ceshi-dangshi-sanjia-jingshenke-yisheng-jiao-ni-he-zhichang-zuo-jianya-qiege-li6jofbvucknthuqw1flm-r0m4kw]] advises prioritizing health over generalized insurance fear and keeping medication changes under clinician guidance and follow-up.
- Mood-versus-disorder boundary - [[vol-57-jingshenke-xinli-jibing-daozhi-de-qingsheng-ke-yufang-jiyu-peiban-shi-zuihao-de-anwei-renshi-yiyu-he-yiyu-qingxu-lhgqmsvos2ybk74qjnrt3bxs0l7d|VOL.57]] distinguishes ordinary low mood from depressive illness through a symptom cluster, persistence, severity, and disruption of work, study, relationships, or daily life.
- Safety override - [[vol-57-jingshenke-xinli-jibing-daozhi-de-qingsheng-ke-yufang-jiyu-peiban-shi-zuihao-de-anwei-renshi-yiyu-he-yiyu-qingxu-lhgqmsvos2ybk74qjnrt3bxs0l7d|VOL.57]] treats suicidal signals and dangerous hallucination- or delusion-linked behavior as requiring attention beyond ordinary self-care.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The page is a triage concept, not a diagnostic rule. VOL.72's six-month anxiety reference and VOL.57's two-week depression reference should not be generalized across all disorders or presentations. Severe chest pain, breathing difficulty, neurological symptoms, self-harm risk, psychosis-linked danger, inability to function, or other acute concerns may require urgent medical or crisis evaluation regardless of duration. The sources do not supply a complete differential diagnosis or validated risk tool.

## What Changed
- Added the distinction between transient depressed mood and depressive disorder.
- Added safety as an override to routine duration and function monitoring.
- Preserved disorder-specific time thresholds as source-scoped examples rather than universal rules.
