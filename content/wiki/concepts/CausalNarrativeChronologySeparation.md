## Definition

因果叙事与事件编年分离 is the source-critical practice of distinguishing where an event appears in a historical narrative from when the event occurred, especially when an annalistic compiler groups material to preserve an actor, theme, or causal chain.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 1155 argues that the *Zizhi Tongjian* places actions attributed to [[HuangQuanLateHan|黄权]], the [[ZhangFei|张飞]]-[[ZhangHeLateHan|张郃]] conflict, and [[ZhaoYanLateHan|赵俨]] beside the 215 Hanzhong aftermath even though the episode assigns them to later years. On that reading, narrative adjacency explains why the events matter together but cannot by itself establish simultaneity.

The practical method is comparative. Readers should reconstruct dates from explicit year markers, biographies, parallel histories, and commentarial evidence, then separately ask what the compiler gains by grouping the material. This preserves the explanatory value of causal composition without mistaking composition for a transparent timeline.

## Key Claims

- Narrative position and event date are different kinds of evidence.
- Annalistic writing can still group material across years when an actor or causal sequence would otherwise fragment.
- Biographical histories may clarify dates obscured by thematic or causal grouping, but they also require source criticism rather than automatic preference.
- A chronology correction should identify the textual basis for both the received placement and the proposed date.
- Explaining an apparent displacement as deliberate composition is an interpretation, not a default excuse for every inconsistency.
- Historical synthesis should preserve both the reconstructed timeline and the compiler's narrative logic.

## Evidence

- Proposed displacements: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1155-caocao-weishenme-jujue-simayi-de-mieshu-jianyi-lsurimkb646qwumyxv0yxiorda_p|Hanji 1155]] assigns the Huang Quan, Zhang Fei-Zhang He, and Zhao Yan cases to 217, 218, and 219 while saying the *Zizhi Tongjian* groups them with 215 material.
- Compiler-purpose interpretation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1155-caocao-weishenme-jujue-simayi-de-mieshu-jianyi-lsurimkb646qwumyxv0yxiorda_p|Hanji 1155]] attributes the grouping to [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s effort to make causes, consequences, and governance lessons legible.
- Comparative method: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1155-caocao-weishenme-jujue-simayi-de-mieshu-jianyi-lsurimkb646qwumyxv0yxiorda_p|Hanji 1155]] recommends checking the annalistic account against biographical material in the *Sanguozhi*.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The proposed dates and intentional-grouping explanation come from one structured episode summary and have not been independently established here from critical editions. Apparent chronological displacement can also arise from uncertain dating, source disagreement, later interpolation, abridgment, or the modern speaker's own reconstruction. Biographical arrangement is not inherently more accurate than annalistic arrangement, so comparison must evaluate explicit evidence rather than genre labels alone.
