# Educational Tracking By Testing / 测试驱动的教育分流

[[ep262-gaokao-guohou-zhishi-guiling-women-xuyao-zenyang-de-kexue-jiaoyu-ls8ecqcdpe9d5ioytyca3jqdf-3r]] adds a science-education version. The episode argues that once primary-school science becomes a higher-status subject, schools can respond by making it another test-and-ranking object, producing [[ExamDrivenScienceEducation|exam-driven science education]] instead of inquiry, experiment, and public scientific literacy.

Educational tracking by testing / 测试驱动的教育分流 is the pattern where children are placed into different schools, curricula, resources, or life expectations by test result. [[37-zhishang-ceshi-qingwen-ni-shi-zhili-poluomen-ma-608020679]] develops the pattern through [[AlfredBinet|Binet]] and [[TheodoreSimon|Simon]]'s child-testing origins, American school sorting, and Britain's 11+ exam and grammar-school system.

The episode's criticism is not that children all learn identically. It is that early test sorting can become class reproduction when middle-class families can prepare for "neutral" tests and selected schools receive better resources. This extends the wiki's [[AchievementPressureMentalHealth]] and [[AntiAuthoritarianEducation]] branches by showing how measurement can turn childhood into a gatekeeping process.

## Key Claims
- Educational tracking can begin as support for struggling students and later become a hierarchy of opportunity.
- Test-based selection is not socially neutral when family resources affect preparation and interpretation.
- Early sorting can convert a child's current development into an assumed destiny.
- The episode links British 11+ anxiety to broader Chinese education pressure and "衡水式" competition.
