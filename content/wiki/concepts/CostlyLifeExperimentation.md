## Definition
Costly life experimentation / 有成本的持续折腾 is repeated entry into new projects, roles, skills, or environments without treating effort as a guarantee of success and without hiding the money, health, attention, time, or relationship costs of trying.

## Current Synthesis
The source separates continued action from success mythology. A food stall can fail despite good ingredients, music or film desire can exceed current ability, a technology company can consume enormous capital, and production work can be cold, repetitive, delayed, or physically exhausting. “折腾” is therefore not evidence that a person will win, nor is every abandoned project retroactively valuable.

Its defensible value lies in preserved agency and revisability. Repeated attempts can reveal constraints, create relationships, supply comic or creative material, and keep a person engaged with changing tools and opportunities. [[LuoYonghao|罗永浩]]'s preference for another technology attempt over retirement and [[SunShuhengStandup|孙舒恒]]'s small business, music, and relocation stories exemplify that stance, but remain personal value judgments rather than a universal duty to stay busy.

## Key Claims
- Continued experimentation does not guarantee commercial, artistic, or personal success.
- Failure can reveal constraints and redirect later choices without needing to be reframed as a hidden victory.
- The value of trying may include agency, learning, relationships, renewed energy, or new material rather than only outcome.
- Experimentation carries real financial, bodily, attentional, and relational costs that must remain visible.
- Choosing continued activity over retirement is a source-scoped preference, not a rule for health, worth, or longevity.

- Agency and continued engagement: [[bukaixiaowan-luoyonghao-de-x-zilukou-zheteng-weibi-hui-ying-dan-bu-zheteng-yiding-hui-houhui-lga0l5q48bht8ms9aotmbz-wh7ot]] has Luo prefer another technology-company attempt and a future stand-up tour to passive retirement.
- Cost visibility: [[bukaixiaowan-luoyonghao-de-x-zilukou-zheteng-weibi-hui-ying-dan-bu-zheteng-yiding-hui-houhui-lga0l5q48bht8ms9aotmbz-wh7ot]] foregrounds capital loss, preparation breakdown, bodily strain, waiting, and production labor.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
One live comedy conversation cannot show that repeated experimentation generally improves well-being, skill, health, income, or longevity. Novelty can become avoidance, compulsion, financial overreach, unpaid labor, or refusal to consolidate learning. Illness, disability, caregiving, economic constraint, rest, and chosen stability can all make non-participation rational. The concept supports bounded attempts with visible downside, not permanent hustle or moral judgment of retirement.
