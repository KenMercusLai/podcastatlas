# China-EU Hybrid Vehicle Trade Constraint / 中欧混动车贸易约束

## Definition
The China-EU hybrid-vehicle trade constraint is the source-reported bilateral understanding expected to reduce exports of Chinese conventional and plug-in hybrids to the European Union after those categories grew outside the extra tariff treatment applied to some other electrified vehicles.

## Current Synthesis
The case illustrates category migration under trade policy. When battery EVs and range extenders receive additional duties but conventional and plug-in hybrids do not, exporters can shift product mix toward the less-burdened categories. A later negotiated constraint can close that channel, but without disclosed measures the mechanism could involve price, volume, licensing, undertakings, or other controls and should not be treated as settled.

## Key Claims
- Product definitions can redirect trade before total automotive demand changes.
- Rapid growth in untariffed hybrid categories can trigger a second round of negotiation after an EV-focused measure.
- Expected volume reduction depends on implementation details that were not disclosed in the source.
- Strong model sales by [[BYD]] and [[Chery]] make hybrid policy commercially material rather than merely classificatory.

## Evidence
- Policy gap: [[gaojiesi-weixin-shangxian-mianfei-duanju-xiaochengxu-zhongguo-dui-oumeng-de-hundongche-chukou-yuji-jianshao-1023591374]] says the 2024 extra anti-subsidy duties covered Chinese battery EVs and range extenders but not conventional or plug-in hybrids.
- Growth and negotiation: [[gaojiesi-weixin-shangxian-mianfei-duanju-xiaochengxu-zhongguo-dui-oumeng-de-hundongche-chukou-yuji-jianshao-1023591374]] reports more than 100% year-over-year export growth for both hybrid categories in the cited first eight months and a later understanding expected to cut projected exports by more than half over four years.
- Market relevance: [[gaojiesi-weixin-shangxian-mianfei-duanju-xiaochengxu-zhongguo-dui-oumeng-de-hundongche-chukou-yuji-jianshao-1023591374]] says two BYD models and one Chery model occupied the top three European plug-in-hybrid sales positions in the cited first half.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The understanding's text, legal form, baseline forecast, category definitions, country coverage, compliance mechanism, and effective dates are absent. Reported projected reductions are not observed outcomes, and export growth rates do not by themselves establish tariff avoidance, consumer preference, or long-term competitiveness.

- Preserved the announced export effect as a projection pending disclosed implementation.
