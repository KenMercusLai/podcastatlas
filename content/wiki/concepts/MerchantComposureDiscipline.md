# 商人定气功夫 / Merchant Composure Discipline

Merchant composure discipline is the business frame in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-927-zuo-shengyi-de-zuigao-jingjie-shi-shenme-lngfbjn6xui9k-9lp-ihdqkhpbpr|Hanji 927]]. The host says doing business at a high level requires both gentlemanly credibility and hard-headed decisiveness: one cannot be only kind, only emotional, or only calculating.

The phrase remains source-scoped. It is not a concrete pricing, sales, or finance method. Its function in the episode is to frame judgment under pressure: use relationships without being ruled by them, use money to create more money, stay calm enough to decide when to be kind and when to act decisively, and keep success or failure from breaking one's posture.

The concept connects to [[TrustAsBusinessAsset|trust as business asset]] because credibility is part of the host's business logic, but it also points toward [[RootCausePacification|查因安抚式治理]] because both parts of the episode value composure before action. A person who cannot settle judgment will treat symptoms, flattery, anger, or attachment as the decision rule.
