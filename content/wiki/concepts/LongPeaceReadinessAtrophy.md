# Long-Peace Readiness Atrophy / 长期和平战备退化

## Definition
Long-peace readiness atrophy / 长期和平战备退化 is the loss of usable military capacity and strategic responsiveness when prolonged peace is not paired with preparation, practice, threat reassessment, or credible mobilization.

## Current Synthesis
The late-Qi case separates peace's civilian benefit from its capacity cost. More than forty years without major war protect [[QiState|齐国]]'s population, but they also leave its forces without the accumulated experience of Qin's commanders and soldiers. Because [[HouSheng|后胜]]'s advice treats continued friendship as a reason not to prepare, the state discovers the gap only after its buffers have disappeared. At that point, [[JimoDafuLateQi|即墨大夫（齐末）]] can articulate an ambitious restoration plan, but rhetorical troop totals cannot substitute for practiced capability.

## Key Claims
- Peace does not itself cause weakness; unmaintained readiness during peace does.
- Combat-experienced forces can widen the gap even if the peaceful state retains nominal territory, population, or troop counts.
- Threat misreading turns readiness maintenance from a prudent hedge into something leaders perceive as unnecessary.
- Late emergency plans may confuse theoretical manpower with deployable, coordinated military power.
- Avoided wartime casualties remain a genuine benefit even when readiness has deteriorated.

## Evidence
- **Asymmetric experience:** [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-4-shishang-neng-cheng-juying-de-junwang-qiwangjian-livcg5uz60ne-6oje-qzjfpghhph]] contrasts nearly a century of Qin warfare and battle-tested troops with Qi's more than forty years without war.
- **Preparation suppressed by reassurance:** [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-4-shishang-neng-cheng-juying-de-junwang-qiwangjian-livcg5uz60ne-6oje-qzjfpghhph]] says Hou Sheng recommends continued Qin friendship and sees no need for stronger war preparation.
- **Nominal strength versus usable capacity:** [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-4-shishang-neng-cheng-juying-de-junwang-qiwangjian-livcg5uz60ne-6oje-qzjfpghhph]] presents the late-Qi Jimo Dafu's enormous territory and troop claims as motivational or textually inflated rather than reliable proof of combat power.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not quantify Qi's actual forces, training, logistics, or equipment, and the host's readiness diagnosis is interpretive. Long peace can preserve wealth, population, and social stability; military activity is not automatically beneficial. By the terminal moment, avoiding battle may still have been more humane than attempting an unrealistic restoration war.

## What Changed
- This concept is newly created from the episode's distinction between nominal resources and practiced military capacity.
