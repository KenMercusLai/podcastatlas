# Social Darwinism / 社会达尔文主义

## Definition

Social Darwinism / 社会达尔文主义 is the ideological transfer of evolutionary language into claims that social hierarchy, racial domination, competition, and coercive policy are natural or biologically necessary.

## Current Synthesis

The intelligence-testing source distinguishes [[CharlesDarwin|Charles Darwin / 查尔斯·达尔文]]'s scientific work and caution from later social uses of evolution. In the [[FrancisGalton|Francis Galton / 弗朗西斯·高尔顿]] and testing branch, evolutionary prestige made class, race, ability, and reproductive ranking appear like biological order rather than institutional judgment.

The Nazi source shows the move radicalized into a total moral and political framework. Propaganda described all life as struggle and compassion as weakness, while racial pseudoscience represented domination, exclusion, sterilization, and killing as service to nature. The common error is not evolutionary science itself but the conversion of descriptive biological claims into prescriptive social hierarchy and state violence.

## Key Claims

- Social Darwinism borrows scientific prestige to naturalize social ranking and domination.
- It confuses descriptions of biological processes with moral and political permission.
- In eugenic policy, it turns measured difference into claims about group worth or reproductive fitness.
- Nazi ideology radicalized struggle language into racial law, collective morality, and lethal persecution.
- Scientific caution and institutional humility are safeguards against this ideological conversion.

## Evidence

- Testing and hierarchy: [[37-zhishang-ceshi-qingwen-ni-shi-zhili-poluomen-ma-608020679]] connects Galton, heredity, intelligence measurement, and eugenic ranking while separating Darwin from later applications.
- Nazi struggle propaganda: [[408-the-nazis-in-power-hitlers-dream-part-5-glt6100029744]] describes propaganda presenting all life as competition and compassion as weakness.
- Policy consequences: [[408-the-nazis-in-power-hitlers-dream-part-5-glt6100029744]] links racial biology to antisemitism, reproductive duty, forced sterilization, and involuntary killing.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Evolutionary biology does not entail social Darwinism, racial hierarchy, or eugenic policy. The label covers related historical appropriations rather than one uniform doctrine. The Nazi source's account of intellectual lineage is interpretive and does not make Darwin responsible for later political misuse.

## What Changed

- Extended the concept from intelligence ranking into Nazi racial struggle, propaganda, and lethal policy.
- Clarified the descriptive-to-prescriptive error at the center of the ideology.
