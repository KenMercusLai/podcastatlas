# Social Darwinism / 社会达尔文主义

Social Darwinism / 社会达尔文主义 is the ideology branch named in [[37-zhishang-ceshi-qingwen-ni-shi-zhili-poluomen-ma-608020679]] when the hosts explain how evolutionary language was applied to social hierarchy. The episode ties the pattern to [[FrancisGalton|Francis Galton / 弗朗西斯·高尔顿]] after [[CharlesDarwin|Charles Darwin / 查尔斯·达尔文]]'s evolutionary theory changed his view of nature, heredity, and human capacity.

The source is careful about attribution. It distinguishes Darwin's own caution from later social use of evolutionary ideas, then treats social Darwinism as a way to naturalize class, race, ability, and reproductive hierarchy. In this episode, social Darwinism feeds [[EugenicGovernance|eugenic governance]] by making inequality look like biological order rather than institutional advantage.

## Key Claims
- Social Darwinism borrows evolutionary prestige to justify social ranking.
- The source treats the move from natural selection to social policy as ethically and logically dangerous.
- In the intelligence-testing story, social Darwinism supports the belief that test differences prove group worth or reproductive fitness.
- The episode's counterframe is [[RationalHumility]]: science should not become a master key for contempt or domination.
