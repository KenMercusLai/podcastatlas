# Founder Health Stop Sign

Founder health stop sign is the episode's frame for bodily or mental-health crisis as a forced interruption in founder ambition. In [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]], [[ChenTianqiao]] describes illness as a "Stop sign" that made him reconsider where he was going after the high-speed [[Shanda|盛大]] years.

The concept matters because the source does not treat stopping as simple retreat. Chen's panic disorder, cancer, move to Singapore, Buddhist turn, investment work, neuroscience philanthropy, and later [[Apodex]]/[[DiscoveryModel]] interests become a second life arc rather than a clean founder-exit endpoint.
