# Gendered Patronage Inversion

Gendered patronage inversion names the central power reversal the hosts identify in the title story of [[LaoJiChao|《老妓抄》]]. In [[08-laojichao-laoji-busi-shenzhi-buzeng-diaoling-541092994]], an old retired geisha supports the young repairman Ugi's dream of becoming an inventor: she gives him food, lodging, money, leisure, and attention without asking for a conventional sexual or marital exchange.

The reversal matters because it does not become simple empowerment fantasy. Ugi's free time exposes the weakness of his dream, while the old geisha's generosity carries control, maternal projection, curiosity, and possible self-testing. The episode reads their relation as a way to make dependency, economic independence, gendered self-respect, and failed ambition visible.

## Key Claims
- Reversing a gendered support structure does not automatically make the relation free of power.
- Economic dependence can reveal whether a person's dream had substance or was sustained by the excuse of lacking time and money.
- The patron can also be dependent on the dependent person as a symbolic answer to her own unfinished life.
- The source connects gender power to money and work before it turns into romance or psychology.
