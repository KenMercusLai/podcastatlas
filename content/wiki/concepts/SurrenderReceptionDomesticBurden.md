## Definition

归降接纳的内外成本冲突 / surrender reception domestic burden is the governance tension that arises when ceremony, security display, and rewards make surrender credible to outsiders while the money, transport, labor, status inequality, legal exceptions, or punishments used for reception damage legitimacy among the state's own population.

## Current Synthesis

The two episodes make the reception's benefit legible before exposing its cost. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-313-2-ta-touxiang-xihan-shoufeng-wanhuhou-lu7zpyyfemjcntnubgsfr7bu9_tg|Hanji 313-2]] places [[HunyeKingWesternHan|浑邪王]] inside a military and architectural display: Northern Army formations, Chang'an, and [[WeiyangPalace|未央宫]] present welcome and deterrence together, while a reported ten-thousand-household marquisate converts surrender into recognized status. Smaller fiefs for associated leaders and horse-keeping service for the Xiutu King's sons also show that incorporation sorts people into unequal positions rather than simply admitting them as one bloc.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-314-1-xiongnu-wang-guijiang-wudi-weihe-nusha-500-hanren-ljb-ocshiauhvyra88aykkqlxkak|Hanji 314-1]] then places both sides of the conflict in the same reception. [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] has a strategic reason to display sincerity: inadequate transport or an ungenerous welcome could make surrender appear unsafe and damage state prestige. But the required vehicles, borrowed horses, rewards, and civilian service arrive after costly war and a strained treasury, so households experience the same display as another extraction.

The merchant case shows that the conflict is legal as well as fiscal. A frontier rule meant to restrict strategic exports is applied to local trade with surrendered Xiongnu already in Chang'an. [[JiAnWesternHan|汲黯]] argues that subjects could not reasonably recognize this as prohibited export and that killing them protects the rule's “leaf” by injuring the state's own “branch.” Credible external incorporation therefore cannot be judged only by how the surrendering group is treated; it also depends on whether domestic burdens remain intelligible and proportionate.

## Key Claims

- Generous reception can make surrender credible and signal that submission will be honored.
- Military formation, monumental space, and court ritual can combine welcome with deterrence and make the receiving state's hierarchy visible.
- Enfeoffment converts elite submission into recognized status, but graded rewards and coerced service also preserve sharp inequality within the incorporated group.
- Reception costs become politically dangerous when they are shifted onto a war-exhausted population without consent or visible compensation.
- Diplomatic prestige and domestic legitimacy can diverge even when both are framed as state interests.
- Rules designed for hostile frontier exchange can become unjust when applied without attention to the changed status and location of surrendered people.

- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-313-2-ta-touxiang-xihan-shoufeng-wanhuhou-lu7zpyyfemjcntnubgsfr7bu9_tg|Hanji 313-2]] presents military formation, Chang'an, and Weiyang Palace as the visible experience of Han power surrounding the welcome.

- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-313-2-ta-touxiang-xihan-shoufeng-wanhuhou-lu7zpyyfemjcntnubgsfr7bu9_tg|Hanji 313-2]] reports a ten-thousand-household marquisate for Hunye King, smaller fiefs for associated leaders, and palace horse-keeping service for the Xiutu King's sons.

- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-314-1-xiongnu-wang-guijiang-wudi-weihe-nusha-500-hanren-ljb-ocshiauhvyra88aykkqlxkak|Hanji 314-1]] says the treasury is strained, households hide horses, and civilians are compelled to serve the surrendered group.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The current concept rests on two compact podcast summaries and does not establish the optimal scale of reception, the actual strategic effect of generosity, the fiscal capacity available to Wudi, or whether less costly alternatives existed. Hanji 313-2's submission tableau is explicitly imaginative, and its military, architectural, title, name, fief, and institutional details require comparison with primary texts. Unequal outcomes inside the surrendered group do not by themselves establish one consistent court policy or the voluntariness of any assignment. Hanji 314-1 also does not show that every frontier restriction was unreasonable: weapons, money, and ordinary goods may create different security risks. It most clearly establishes death sentences, not that all sentences were carried out, and its account of legal meaning requires comparison with primary statutes and transmitted histories.

## What Changed

- Added military display, monumental space, and court ritual as mechanisms that make surrender credible and hierarchy visible.
- Added graded enfeoffment and coerced service as unequal forms of status conversion within an incorporated group.
- Reframed later fiscal and legal criticism as the domestic cost of a reception whose external political purpose is now more concrete.
