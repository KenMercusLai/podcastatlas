# Ethnographic Everyday Friction / 民族志日常摩擦

Ethnographic everyday friction is the source's reminder that fieldwork knowledge is shaped by mundane obstacles. In [[35-tianzhende-renleixuejia-zuo-tianye-hui-shangyin-de-603418624]], [[NigelBarley|Nigel Barley]]'s research among the [[DowayoPeople|Dowayo people]] is inseparable from visas, airports, illness, dental damage, dirty injections, money, housing, alcohol etiquette, lawsuits, language learning, and transport.

The point is not that these details are comic relief. They determine who can stay, who can ask questions, who becomes dependent, which conversations happen, and which parts of social life remain invisible.

## Key Claims
- Logistics are part of method because they shape access and dependence.
- Bodily vulnerability can change field relations as much as intellectual preparation does.
- A fieldworker's failures, misunderstandings, and humiliations can be evidence about the social system and the observer's position.
- Formal ethnography becomes misleading if it hides the practical conditions that produced its clean claims.
