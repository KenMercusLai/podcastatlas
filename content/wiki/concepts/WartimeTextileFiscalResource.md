# 战时绵绢财政资源 / Wartime Textile Fiscal Resource

## Definition
战时绵绢财政资源 / wartime textile fiscal resource is Hanji 1046's frame for cotton-silk goods that can function as tax object, store of value, reward material, diplomatic gift, and military supply when coinage and long-distance grain logistics are unreliable.

## Current Synthesis
The episode explains why [[CaoCao|曹操]] cares about cotton-silk collection in [[YangAnLateHan|阳安]] even while the levy risks unrest. War damages currency confidence and makes grain movement expensive; high-value textiles are easier to transport and preserve. Cotton-silk therefore sits between money, logistics, and state reward: it can move across regions, compensate soldiers and officers, support diplomatic gifts to frontier groups, and become raw material for military equipment.

## Key Claims
- Textile goods can become fiscal infrastructure when currency loses reliability during war.
- Portability and durability matter because grain is bulky, perishable, and consumed by its own transport chain.
- Cotton-silk's use in reward, diplomacy, and military supply makes collection strategically valuable.
- The same fiscal value increases the danger of overcollection in exposed localities, tying the resource to taxation-stability tradeoffs.

- Currency substitute: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1046-ta-ping-shenme-bei-chengzuo-sanguo-yincang-zui-shen-de-niuren-limiqxjqzbk4fttl8d6rviqwwe3b|Hanji 1046]] says warfare weakens coin reliability while cotton-silk remains portable, storable, and stable in value.
- Reward and diplomacy: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1046-ta-ping-shenme-bei-chengzuo-sanguo-yincang-zui-shen-de-niuren-limiqxjqzbk4fttl8d6rviqwwe3b|Hanji 1046]] presents cotton-silk as usable for rewarding generals and gifting groups such as the Xiongnu and Wuhuan.
- Military material: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1046-ta-ping-shenme-bei-chengzuo-sanguo-yincang-zui-shen-de-niuren-limiqxjqzbk4fttl8d6rviqwwe3b|Hanji 1046]] says textiles can become tents, uniforms, and flags, reducing procurement friction.
- Institutionalization: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1046-ta-ping-shenme-bei-chengzuo-sanguo-yincang-zui-shen-de-niuren-limiqxjqzbk4fttl8d6rviqwwe3b|Hanji 1046]] links the case to Cao Cao's later grain-plus-textile tax order in Jian'an 9 / 204 CE.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The page captures the episode's fiscal interpretation and does not independently establish the full late-Han monetary history of cotton-silk. It also does not override the source's own Yang'an case: a valuable resource can still be badly timed as a levy.
