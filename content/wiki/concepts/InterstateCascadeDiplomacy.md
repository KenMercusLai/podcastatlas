# Interstate Cascade Diplomacy / 多国连锁外交

Interstate cascade diplomacy / 多国连锁外交 is the pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-57-gudai-fa-dushi-haiyou-zhe-zuoyong-lj6wi61osaytowgmdp8dtkrwb-si]] extracts from [[Zigong|子贡 / 端木赐]] saving [[LuState|鲁国]]. Instead of asking [[QiState|齐国]] not to attack Lu on moral grounds alone, Zigong changes the incentive map across several states.

The cascade begins when Zigong persuades [[TianChang|田常]] to redirect Qi away from weak Lu toward [[WuState|吴国]]. He then persuades [[Fuchai|吴王夫差]] to attack Qi, reassures Wu by getting [[Goujian|越王勾践]] to appear submissive, and lets Wu's later conflicts with Qi and [[JinState|晋国]] create the opening for [[YueState|越国]] to destroy Wu.

The concept differs from a single persuasive speech. Its effect comes from chaining multiple actors' fears, ambitions, and timing constraints until a local threat becomes a changed regional order.

## Key Claims
- A weak state can sometimes be saved by changing the whole threat map rather than by resisting directly.
- The same diplomatic intervention can have consequences far beyond the original rescue target.
- The persuader's skill lies in fitting each message to a different state's incentives.
- This source makes [[MotiveBasedTalentEvaluation|motive-based talent evaluation]] concrete: Zigong's craft resembles later [[ZonghengjiaDiplomacy|纵横家外交]], but its stated purpose is rescuing Lu.
