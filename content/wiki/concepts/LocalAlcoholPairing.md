# Local Alcohol Pairing / 地方酒饮餐配

Local alcohol pairing / 地方酒饮餐配 is the episode's frame for pairing food with drinks that carry the same regional materials or sensory world. In [[ep245-yinian-yidu-xunwei-zhilv-jinnian-qu-chi-chuangyi-cai-li9wtavemokcckialbbj7unpmqsz]], this includes Guizhou rice wine, low-filter drinks using 木姜子, cocktails that translate cured meat or smoked duck memory, and nonalcoholic tea drinks built from butter, mushrooms, low temperature, freezing, and cold-brew black tea.

The point is broader than alcohol strength or pairing rules. Drinks can become another channel of [[RegionalFlavorTransfer|地方风味迁移]] when they express grain, microbes, smoke, fat, spice, acid, tea, or local dish memory. That makes the concept a drink-side extension of [[ChineseBistroLocalization|中式小酒馆本土化]] and [[FlavorAsSelfKnowledge|flavor as self-knowledge]].

## Key Claims
- Local pairing works best when the drink shares the dish's sensory logic, not only its geographic label.
- Rice wine can become terroir-like when grain variety, starter culture, local microbes, and region-specific fermentation all matter.
- Cocktails and nonalcoholic drinks can translate a dish's aroma or memory without copying its ingredients literally.
- Pairing systems can help a regional cuisine become more legible by giving diners more than one sensory entry point.
