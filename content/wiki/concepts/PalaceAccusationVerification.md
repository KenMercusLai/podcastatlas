## Definition

Palace Accusation Verification / 宫廷指控核验 is the governance pattern where a ruler or regent slows down a high-risk palace accusation, checks behavior and evidentiary fit, and avoids broad coercive interrogation when the likely cost is implicating innocent people.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 777 defines the current pattern through two early [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Empress Dowager Deng]] cases. In the jewel theft case, Deng does not begin with dragnet interrogation even though the court is in mourning and a large box of valuables is missing. She worries that broad questioning would create innocent victims, personally inspects the relevant workers, and narrows the case until the thief confesses.

The Ji Cheng case adds the higher-risk version because the charge is wugu. The source says the testimony and evidence look clear, but Deng doubts the fit: Ji Cheng had been close to [[EmperorHeOfHan|Emperor He]] and was normally quiet, making the collective accusation suspicious. Repeated verification exposes the case as a frame-up by other attendants. The concept therefore captures legal restraint inside palace politics: fact-finding protects both the accused and the regime from accusation spirals.

## Key Claims

- Palace accusations are dangerous because proximity, rivalry, mourning, and invisible-harm claims can turn weak evidence into punishment quickly.
- Verification includes behavioral fit and motive plausibility, not only surface confession or testimony.
- Avoiding dragnet interrogation can be an active justice strategy when broad coercion is likely to produce innocent victims.
- Wugu accusations require special caution because hidden harm can make accusation hard to disprove and easy to weaponize.
- The pattern strengthens regency legitimacy when restraint produces a visible correction rather than a wider case.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

This concept currently rests on one source note and should remain a narrow Deng-regency pattern. The source praises Deng's judgment, but it does not give a full legal procedure, independent court records, or a general rule that personal observation is sufficient evidence. The stronger current claim is restraint before coercive escalation, especially when palace rivalry and wugu language make accusation unusually contagious.

## What Changed

- Created the concept from Hanji 777's jewel-theft and Ji Cheng wugu-accusation cases.
- Defined the pattern as verification and anti-dragnet restraint rather than general mercy or skepticism.
