# Counterfeit Supply-Chain Modularization / 仿冒供应链模块化

## Definition

Counterfeit supply-chain modularization is the separation of unauthorized production, storage, aggregation, wholesale, buyer guidance, inspection, online acquisition, private selling, and logistics into specialized roles and locations.

## Current Synthesis

In [[no-225-putian-xie-wangshi-gkwrijeowkkqa6hffgt1ih2w]], the counterfeit shoe trade is not a simple factory-to-consumer line. Factories or workshops make goods; “放货人” aggregate supply; [[AnfuMarket|安福市场]] stalls and hidden warehouses coordinate wholesale; guides help outsiders find sellers; inspection teams clean and check products; agents and content accounts acquire customers; and logistics services obscure origin or imitate international movement.

This modularity reduces the visibility of the whole system and lets participants enter with limited assets, but it also produces information loss. Each handoff can add price, weaken quality control, and support false “factory direct” or provenance claims.

## Key Claims

- Specialized intermediaries allow production, inventory, customer acquisition, and fulfillment to operate without one integrated firm.
- Geographic and functional separation can reduce exposure to enforcement and make responsibility harder to trace.
- Low entry costs at one module can coexist with deep cluster capability elsewhere in the chain.
- Repeated handoffs increase markup, quality variance, and provenance opacity.
- Content marketing, private-domain selling, and manipulated logistics make the digital acquisition layer part of the counterfeit supply chain.

## Evidence

- **Production and distribution:** [[no-225-putian-xie-wangshi-gkwrijeowkkqa6hffgt1ih2w]] separates small workshops, factories, stock organizers, stalls, agents, and end sellers.
- **Services around trade:** [[no-225-putian-xie-wangshi-gkwrijeowkkqa6hffgt1ih2w]] describes guides, sourcing instruction, product checking, cleaning, minor finishing, and one-piece fulfillment.
- **Origin concealment:** [[no-225-putian-xie-wangshi-gkwrijeowkkqa6hffgt1ih2w]] reports cross-city forwarding and fabricated tracking histories that made goods appear to originate elsewhere.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

- The episode combines investigations from different years; it does not establish that every described role remains equally important now.
- Modular organization is not unique to illegal trade. The relevant distinction is the use of specialization to conceal infringement, origin, responsibility, or truthful product identity.
- Accounts of workshop costs, output, margins, and enforcement avoidance remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Added a role-by-role model of counterfeit trade beyond the visible factory or storefront.
- Connected physical distribution to content acquisition, private selling, and logistics-data manipulation.
