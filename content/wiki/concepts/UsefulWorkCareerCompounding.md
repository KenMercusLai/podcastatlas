# Useful Work Career Compounding

Useful work career compounding is the career strategy highlighted in [[brave-new-whirl-turkeys-opposition-overhaul-6a69c7766043035f89eb097f]] through [[BenjaminTodd]] and [[EightyThousandHours|80,000 Hours]]. The core advice is to get very good at something that helps others, then let passion develop from competence, contribution, and feedback.

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-charles-chase-koch-on-how-they-quietly-built-a-150b-empire-41270895]] adds the Koch-family version through [[CharlesKoch|Charles Koch]] and [[ChaseKoch|Chase Koch]]. Charles frames flourishing as discovering one's gifts and using them to help others succeed, while Chase's feed-yard and [[KochFertilizer|Koch Fertilizer]] stories make contribution and role fit more important than status, credentials, or inherited position.

The concept is a practical answer to [[PassionTrapCareerAdvice]]. It does not require a young graduate to already know a life mission. Instead, it asks for skill accumulation, real usefulness, and patience while work identity forms over time.

## Key Claims
- Competence can create motivation by making contribution visible.
- Helpful work provides external feedback that pure introspection cannot.
- Career patience matters because many people take time to find their life's work.
- The frame can coexist with impact ambition, but it avoids making every decision a moral optimization problem.
- The Koch source adds that contribution can require leaving a prestigious role when another person has clearer comparative advantage.
