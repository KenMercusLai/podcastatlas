# Legitimacy Window Seizure / 合法性窗口抢占

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-968-jiemi-sanguo-zui-lihai-de-yangmou-lg4qfkdgw-stixrebhjdoshil6yj|Hanji 968]] adds the earliest Cao-side formulation through [[MaoJie|毛玠]]: "奉天子以令不臣" is paired with farming and military stockpiling before [[CaoCao|曹操]] has actually secured the emperor. The source therefore shows the legitimacy window first as a strategy to be prepared, then as a route problem through [[ZhangYangLateHan|张杨]], [[DongZhaoLateHan|董昭]], [[HeneiCommandery|河内郡]], and [[ZhongYao|钟繇]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-976-sanguo-moushi-dapandian-shui-shi-ni-xinzhong-de-shen-ljgpbosq-9xiowpgaeyossmgst6x|Hanji 976]] adds a compressed retrospective formula through [[XunYu|荀彧]]: "奉天子以令不臣" is treated as the legitimacy package that lets [[CaoCao|曹操]] turn the Han emperor's recognized name into order, commands, and talent attraction. The source is thematic and later than the underlying narrative, so the detailed timing remains grounded in Hanji 1002 and the emperor-flight branch.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-989-ningwei-taipingquan-buzuo-luanshi-ren-lkoycygmjnltuvpq-6wjv-ls61s6|Hanji 989]] adds the title-without-command precondition. The court can still recognize [[ZhangJiLateHan|张济]], [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]], [[YangDingLateHan|杨定]], [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]], and [[DongChengLateHan|董承]], but no one yet turns [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]'s recognized name into stable order. This is the exposed phase before a stronger actor such as [[CaoCao|曹操]] can later operationalize the title.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-990-lijue-guosi-shengsi-yuanjia-weihe-turan-lianshou-lmop5mcrsjuq-ixuv0sfho5mvc-f|Hanji 990]] adds an even earlier pre-window condition. [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] cannot force [[YangDingLateHan|杨定]], [[DongChengLateHan|董承]], and [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]] to stop attacking [[DuanWeiLateHan|段煨]], yet his person is still valuable enough that [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]] reunite to recover him. The later "welcome the emperor" opportunity rests on this paradox: the title is operationally weak but strategically scarce.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-991-zhengduo-xiandi-zhizhan-shui-neng-zuizhong-qusheng-lkbsl5uzqxouzm6mlewrnuns0x6p|Hanji 991]] adds the dangerous precondition for the later legitimacy window. [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] is still recognized enough that [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]], [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]], [[ZhangJiLateHan|张济]], [[DongChengLateHan|董承]], [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]], and their reinforcements fight over his route and body, but no actor has yet converted that possession into durable order. The window later seen by [[JuShou|沮授]] and [[XunYu|荀彧]] exists because this source first shows the emperor as valuable, exposed, and not securely operationalized.

Legitimacy window seizure / 合法性窗口抢占 is first backfilled by [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-992-luanshi-you-duo-kepa-jiemi-changan-de-xuese-mengyan-ltuxzrb-1x5y2ctwo4drggxn5stz|Hanji 992]] through [[JuShou|沮授]]'s advice to [[YuanShao|袁绍]]. [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] is displaced, the Han court is materially broken, and rival lords are acting in the name of public order; Ju Shou argues that welcoming the emperor would let Yuan Shao command others through the recognized sovereign before someone else captures that advantage.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1002-yuanshu-chengdi-hou-sunce-chengwei-zui-da-ying-llya3c2-pefujwxdxefvqxwxjizjo|Hanji 1002]] then draws the positive Cao Cao version from [[XunYu|荀彧]] persuading [[CaoCao|曹操]] to welcome Emperor Xian. The emperor has returned to [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]], the old capital is ruined, and the court is vulnerable; that weakness makes action risky, but it also creates a short window in which supporting the recognized sovereign can become public order, strategic leverage, and talent attraction.

The source's three-part formula is "大顺、大略、大德." "大顺" is settling public feeling around the still-recognized Han ruler, "大略" is gaining the ability to issue commands through the emperor's name, and "大德" is making Cao Cao's camp a credible destination for capable people. The window must be seized because another strong actor could act first and make the same legitimacy harder for Cao Cao to claim later.

This concept overlaps with [[MingqiLegitimacy|名器合法性]] and [[StrategicCapitalRelocation|战略性迁都]], but it is narrower than either. Mingqi legitimacy explains why titles, seals, offices, and sovereign names still matter. Strategic capital relocation explains the later move from Luoyang to [[Xudu|许都]]. Legitimacy window seizure names the earlier timing problem: when an exposed but recognized sovereign is available, delay can transfer the legitimacy advantage to a rival.

The concept also contrasts with [[AntiUsurpationCoalitionPressure|讨逆同盟压力]]. In the Yuan Shu branch, legitimacy forces rivals to respond to an illegitimate claimant. In the Cao Cao branch, legitimacy rewards the actor who first protects and operationalizes the still-recognized ruler.

## Key Claims
- Hanji 968 adds the pre-execution layer: Mao Jie names the legitimacy strategy and Cao Cao must still open the Henei-Chang'an court route before title leverage can be used.
- Hanji 976 adds the retrospective "奉天子" slogan version, while the operational timing remains grounded in Hanji 989-1002.
- Hanji 989 adds that the legitimacy window begins before Cao Cao's decision room: the title is recognized, but route control still belongs to shifting armed escorts.
- Hanji 990 adds that a legitimacy window can begin while the sovereign is still too weak to enforce an order against nearby soldiers.
- Hanji 991 adds the pre-window condition: a recognized sovereign can be fought over by armed escorts and pursuers before anyone turns that control into a stable legitimacy platform.
- Hanji 992 adds the missed-window version: a stronger actor can lose legitimacy advantage by treating the vulnerable sovereign mainly as a constraint.
- Weak sovereigns can still create strong strategic windows when public recognition of the title remains alive.
- The value of acting first is partly reputational: the same move looks different when framed as rescue rather than later opportunism.
- Legitimacy can attract talent as well as justify orders, because capable people need a credible political platform.
- A legitimacy window is not automatically safe; the court's vulnerability is exactly what makes the opportunity time-sensitive.
- This mechanism precedes the material mechanics of relocation, supply, and armed control developed in the follow-on episodes.
