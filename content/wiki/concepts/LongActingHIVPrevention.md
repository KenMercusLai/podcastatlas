# Long-Acting HIV Prevention

Long-acting HIV prevention is the [[GileadSciences|Gilead]] branch in [[vol-117-shengwu-yiyao-de-2025-chaodi-zhongguo-yanfa-jiaolv-he-xinwang-jiwei-lmhral0rmq6tohiqdwsgmfapnyn7]]. The episode says a twice-yearly injectable HIV-prevention drug showed complete prevention in a women's clinical trial, making HIV feel closer to fully preventable and controllable.

The source stresses that the product is not a vaccine in mechanism, even if the long dosing interval and prevention effect can feel vaccine-like. Its wiki role is to contrast Gilead's strong antiviral franchise with the same company's more troubled oncology investment record.

## Key Claims
- Long dosing intervals can change prevention practicality, not only pharmacology.
- The source distinguishes antiviral/prevention success from vaccine mechanism.
- Gilead's HIV result is used as a counterweight to its CD47, TIGIT, and ADC oncology disappointments.
- The concept belongs to medical-product strategy, not personal medical advice.
