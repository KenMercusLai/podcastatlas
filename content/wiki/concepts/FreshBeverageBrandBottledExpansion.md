# Fresh-Beverage Brand Bottled Expansion / 现制饮品品牌瓶装化

## Definition

Fresh-beverage brand bottled expansion is the move by store-born tea and coffee chains into packaged ready-to-drink products to reach occasions, retailers, and geographies beyond their own stores.

## Current Synthesis

The source frames bottled expansion as a response to slower store-led growth, but the move is not simply a product-format change. Brands can reuse familiar flavors, customer recognition, ingredients, and sometimes cold-chain infrastructure; manufacturers can lower the asset threshold; and retailers can provide rapid exposure or scale. Each advantage is conditional: a store bestseller may not survive processing or shelf-life requirements, outsourced products can converge, and retailer access transfers specification, price, inventory, and channel obligations onto the brand.

The strongest differentiation comes from a capability that survives the format change. Guming's refrigerated high-pressure-processed juice is the clearest example because its existing cold chain supports a product ordinary shelf-stable entrants cannot copy as easily. Even there, refrigeration narrows distribution and raises execution demands. Bottled products are therefore better treated as a long-cycle capability test than an automatic second growth curve.

## Key Claims

- Slower growth in freshly made drinks increases interest in packaged products beyond continued store openings.
- Existing flavors and customer recognition can reduce launch friction without proving packaged-product demand.
- Ingredient, sourcing, or cold-chain capabilities can create more defensible transfer than branding alone.
- Contract manufacturing lowers fixed investment and speeds experimentation.
- Growth retailers can substitute for a self-built national distributor network during early expansion.
- The strategy succeeds only if the brand develops packaged-product differentiation and channel operations outside its store system.

## Evidence

- **Growth and occasion extension:** [[kafeidou-xicha-ruixing-baishang-shangchao-huojia-xianzhiyinpin-qijia-de-pinpaimen-weishenme-douzai-fali-pingzhuangyinliao-gkwrijiowiswahe8wgt2vl4q]] links slower fresh-tea growth to portable products that reach consumers and markets outside brand stores.
- **Transferable brand and product knowledge:** [[kafeidou-xicha-ruixing-baishang-shangchao-huojia-xianzhiyinpin-qijia-de-pinpaimen-weishenme-douzai-fali-pingzhuangyinliao-gkwrijiowiswahe8wgt2vl4q]] says brands often translate store-tested flavors into bottled juice tea, milk tea, coffee, or juice.
- **Capability-based differentiation:** [[kafeidou-xicha-ruixing-baishang-shangchao-huojia-xianzhiyinpin-qijia-de-pinpaimen-weishenme-douzai-fali-pingzhuangyinliao-gkwrijiowiswahe8wgt2vl4q]] uses Guming's refrigerated high-pressure-processed juice and existing cold chain as the strongest operating example.
- **External production and distribution:** [[kafeidou-xicha-ruixing-baishang-shangchao-huojia-xianzhiyinpin-qijia-de-pinpaimen-weishenme-douzai-fali-pingzhuangyinliao-gkwrijiowiswahe8wgt2vl4q]] describes contract manufacturers and growing retailers as lowering factory and network entry barriers.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Early sparkling-water attempts by HeyTea and Nayuki did not establish durable differentiation, and the source reports a costly HeyTea recall. Shared factories and similar claims can recreate price competition. Cold-chain differentiation also restricts reach, while lower-tier markets and traditional small stores may not accept the same brands or price points. The market-growth estimate, launches, costs, and executive judgments are episode-reported; no comparative sell-through, profitability, or repeat-purchase dataset is supplied.

## What Changed

- Established bottled expansion as a multi-capability move rather than a simple packaging extension.
- Distinguished transferable supply-chain capability from flavor or brand recognition alone.
- Treated retailer access and contract manufacturing as accelerants that leave channel and inventory risk with the brand.
