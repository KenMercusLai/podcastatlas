# Research Impact Beyond Acceptance / 论文录用之外的研究影响力

## Definition
Research impact beyond acceptance is the evaluation of work through the value and rigor of its question, accessible artifacts, sustained maintenance, real adoption, and useful feedback rather than treating conference or journal acceptance as the final reward.

## Current Synthesis
When paper volume overwhelms reviewers and readers, acceptance becomes a noisy but still relevant filter rather than a complete measure of contribution. Code, data, maintenance, tools, downloads, user response, and public explanation can reveal whether knowledge travels and solves a problem, but each is also vulnerable to gaming. Impact therefore requires a portfolio of evidence anchored by sound claims, not replacement of one leaderboard with another.

## Key Claims
- A paper is primarily a vehicle for sharing and testing knowledge, not the terminal purpose of research.
- Problem importance and evidentiary rigor remain upstream of venue acceptance.
- Open code, data, reproducibility, maintenance, and real use can add evidence that a contribution matters.
- Academic social media can help valuable work cross information overload and attract faster criticism or collaborators.
- Publication count, downloads, followers, and attention can all be manipulated and should not be used alone.
- Flooding venues with model-generated submissions externalizes costs onto a scarce peer-review system.

## Evidence
- Publication-system evidence: [[ai-shidai-phd-lushang-de-10-ge-guanjian-wenti-duitan-lilei-gangda-phd-xiaomi-mimo-yanjiuyuan-lpbsxeoxbcgtv0qckx8lctpdwviv]] describes reviewer overload, decision randomness, submission limits, senior-reviewer responsibility, AI assistance, and discussion-based decisions.
- Artifact and adoption evidence: [[ai-shidai-phd-lushang-de-10-ge-guanjian-wenti-duitan-lilei-gangda-phd-xiaomi-mimo-yanjiuyuan-lpbsxeoxbcgtv0qckx8lctpdwviv]] says candidate evaluation can inspect code, data, project maintenance, downloads, and real use in addition to papers.
- Distribution evidence: [[ai-shidai-phd-lushang-de-10-ge-guanjian-wenti-duitan-lilei-gangda-phd-xiaomi-mimo-yanjiuyuan-lpbsxeoxbcgtv0qckx8lctpdwviv]] presents social media as a faster feedback and discovery layer in a field with thousands of papers.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Acceptance standards, open-source norms, and hiring signals vary by discipline and institution. Downloads and followers may reward marketing, existing status, or platform effects; maintenance favors projects with continuing resources; confidential, theoretical, negative, or long-horizon work may have large value without public adoption. AI-assisted review may reduce workload while introducing its own reliability and accountability failures.

## What Changed
- Established a multi-signal impact model without declaring publication obsolete.
- Added maintenance and adoption as harder-to-fake but still gameable evidence.
- Positioned academic social media as distribution and feedback infrastructure, not a substitute for research quality.
