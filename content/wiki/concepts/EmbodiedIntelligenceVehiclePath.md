# Embodied Intelligence Vehicle Path

## Definition
Embodied intelligence vehicle path is the strategy that treats intelligent vehicles and autonomous driving as the first large deployed scene for physical-world AI before broader robots and home services mature.

## Current Synthesis
The bounded sources converge on a car-to-robotics route from different company positions. [[LiAuto|理想汽车]] defines embodied intelligence functionally: models, perception, a brain, and physical action matter more than humanoid form. [[HorizonRobotics|地平线]] adds an industrial sequencing argument: automotive supply chains, annual volume, installed compute, field data, cash flow, and chip-production discipline make vehicles a practical first scaled physical-AI market before broader robots mature.

The route transfers platform engineering, chip design, perception, decision, control, and data-loop methods, but it does not erase the embodiment gap. Household and industrial robots add many joints, balance, manipulation, tactile and force feedback, different latency budgets, and unsettled product economics.

## Key Claims
- Form factor should follow task efficiency; embodied intelligence does not require a humanoid body.
- Autonomous driving can be an embodied-intelligence path because it combines perception, planning, control, and physical action at scale.
- Household robots and car users may overlap because both are private-life service contexts.
- L3/L4 readiness depends on physical-world models, control systems, regulation, compute, and user trust rather than one feature launch.
- Vehicle scale can finance long-cycle chips and create production and data-loop experience before robot markets support equivalent volume.
- Transfer is partial: manipulation and multi-joint control need body-specific hardware, data, and validation.

## Evidence
- Definition by function: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]] says vehicles, humanoids, and planes can all be robots in the Transformers sense if they act intelligently in the physical world.
- Stage framing: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]] explicitly calls autonomous driving the first half of embodied intelligence and humanoids the second half.
- Technical gates: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]] links L4 to 3D VIT, physical-world pretraining, faster control, and near-ten-thousand-TOPS compute.
- Industrial sequencing: [[tefan-cong-hui-kaiche-dao-hui-zuoshi-ai-ruhe-jinru-zhenshi-shijie-gkwrijeopcilaoo4hwtn2dns]] says Horizon concentrated on cars because their mature supply chain and large annual volume could support chip shipments, engineering feedback, cash flow, and later robot expansion.
- Transfer boundary: [[tefan-cong-hui-kaiche-dao-hui-zuoshi-ai-ruhe-jinru-zhenshi-shijie-gkwrijeopcilaoo4hwtn2dns]] treats chip and data-loop experience as reusable while identifying multi-joint balance, manipulation, and robot-data acquisition as additional problems.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The path is strategic, not proven. Strong vehicle autonomy does not automatically transfer to household manipulation, and car data, regulatory regimes, robot form factors, and service economics may diverge. Both sources are company-strategy accounts; neither proves that vehicle incumbency creates durable robot-platform leadership.

## What Changed
- Added Horizon's supply-chain, shipment, cash-flow, and chip-cycle rationale for vehicle-first sequencing.
- Made the transfer boundary explicit: platform method can travel farther than action data or body control.
