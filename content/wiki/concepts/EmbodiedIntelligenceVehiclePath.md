# Embodied Intelligence Vehicle Path

## Definition
Embodied intelligence vehicle path is the strategy that treats intelligent vehicles and autonomous driving as the first large deployed scene for physical-world AI before broader robots and home services mature.

## Current Synthesis
The current synthesis is [[LiAuto|理想汽车]]'s car-to-robotics route. [[LiXiangLiAuto|李想]] defines embodied intelligence functionally in [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]]: a machine with models, perception, a brain, and physical action can be embodied intelligence whether it is car-shaped, humanoid, or another form. In that frame, autonomous driving is the first half, humanoid robots are the second half, and cars supply real product, sensor, compute, control, safety, and customer scenarios.

## Key Claims
- Form factor should follow task efficiency; embodied intelligence does not require a humanoid body.
- Autonomous driving can be an embodied-intelligence path because it combines perception, planning, control, and physical action at scale.
- Household robots and car users may overlap because both are private-life service contexts.
- L3/L4 readiness depends on physical-world models, control systems, regulation, compute, and user trust rather than one feature launch.

## Evidence
- Definition by function: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]] says vehicles, humanoids, and planes can all be robots in the Transformers sense if they act intelligently in the physical world.
- Stage framing: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]] explicitly calls autonomous driving the first half of embodied intelligence and humanoids the second half.
- Technical gates: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]] links L4 to 3D VIT, physical-world pretraining, faster control, and near-ten-thousand-TOPS compute.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The path is strategic, not proven. Strong vehicle autonomy does not automatically transfer to household manipulation, and car data, regulatory regimes, robot form factors, and service economics may diverge.
