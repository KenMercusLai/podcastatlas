# Elite Relocation Control / 豪杰迁徙控制

Elite relocation control / 豪杰迁徙控制 is the post-conquest governance method [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-120-4-bing-liuguo-hou-qinshihuang-jiao-ni-ruhe-shou-jiangshan-lgwsd0c3wkl-cphma8sm3ftkowwr]] identifies in [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]]'s forced movement of 120,000 households of powerful local people to [[Xianyang|咸阳]]. The episode presents the policy as more than population transfer: Qin enriches the capital, watches potential opposition more closely, and uproots local elites from the social soil that made them powerful.

The concept is a control answer to the usual pacification problem after conquest. A softer approach would rely on respected local figures to calm conquered populations. Qin instead weakens those figures by moving them into unfamiliar surroundings and placing mixed populations under direct imperial management.

This makes elite relocation an implementation layer for [[CommanderyCountyAntiEnfeoffment|郡县制反分封定局]]. Rejecting hereditary fiefs gives Qin formal direct rule, but relocated elites reduce the ability of old regional networks to obstruct commandery-county administration from below.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-125-1-qinshihuang-xiujian-shishang-zuida-lanweilou-afanggong-lhqc6jw4jzoyrg7jzc2mbxdebj4]] adds a second-order consequence: relocation also swells [[Xianyang|咸阳]] itself. The source estimates that 120,000 relocated households plus dependents and servants could make the capital population surge, helping explain why Qin Shi Huang moves offices south of the Wei River and begins planning [[AfangPalace|阿房宫]]. Elite relocation therefore feeds not only surveillance, but also capital expansion and [[ImperialConstructionOverload|帝国工程过载]].

## Key Claims
- Forced relocation is aimed at people and networks, not only at land.
- Moving elites to the capital lets the ruler combine surveillance with capital prosperity.
- Uprooting local豪杰 weakens their ability to mobilize familiar followers and kinship-place ties.
- The policy reverses a common conquered-region strategy of governing through respected local intermediaries.
- The episode treats relocation as a social counterpart to [[PostConquestDisarmament|post-conquest disarmament]] and [[QinAdministrativeStandardization|administrative standardization]].
- Qinji 125-1 adds that relocation can create capital-planning pressure: surveillance concentration also produces crowding, office relocation, and new palace construction.
