# 虚假无援诱降 / False Relief-Denial Surrender

## Definition

虚假无援诱降 / false relief-denial surrender is a deception pattern in which an attacker induces surrender by making a defender believe that friendly relief is unavailable, too distant, or incapable of changing the outcome.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1152-sunquan-weishenme-tongma-liubei-shi-pianzi-lvyngihgs-obrqasnub4_lptryop|Hanji 1152]] defines the pattern through [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]] and [[HaoPuLateHan|郝普]] at [[LinglingCommanderyLateHan|零陵郡]]. Hao Pu initially resists. Lyu Meng then recruits Hao Pu's friend Deng Xuanzhi to say that [[LiuBei|刘备]] and [[GuanYu|关羽]] cannot rescue the city. Hao Pu accepts surrender because the message changes the expected value of waiting.

The mechanism differs from merely hiding one's own troops. It targets the defender's model of future support. The trusted intermediary makes the claim credible; operational urgency limits verification; and surrender becomes rational under the false picture even though the picture is engineered. After the city is yielded, the concealed recall order reveals that Liu Bei and Guan Yu were much closer than represented.

## Key Claims

- A defended position's willingness to hold depends partly on expected relief, not only present walls, troops, or supplies.
- False denial of relief can shorten a siege by changing the defender's estimate of future options.
- A trusted personal intermediary can carry deceptive operational information more credibly than an enemy demand alone.
- The tactic exploits verification delay: the decision may become irreversible before the defender can test the claim.
- Successful deception does not prove that continued defense would certainly have succeeded; it proves that the surrender decision was made under a manipulated information set.

## Evidence

- Continued defense: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1152-sunquan-weishenme-tongma-liubei-shi-pianzi-lvyngihgs-obrqasnub4_lptryop|Hanji 1152]] says Hao Pu holds Lingling after Changsha and Guiyang submit.
- Trusted delivery: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1152-sunquan-weishenme-tongma-liubei-shi-pianzi-lvyngihgs-obrqasnub4_lptryop|Hanji 1152]] has Lyu Meng use Hao Pu's friend Deng Xuanzhi to carry the false no-relief claim.
- Post-surrender revelation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1152-sunquan-weishenme-tongma-liubei-shi-pianzi-lvyngihgs-obrqasnub4_lptryop|Hanji 1152]] says Hao Pu learns only after leaving the city that Liu Bei is at Gong'an and Guan Yu near Yiyang.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The evidence currently consists of one episode's compressed account. The precise dialogue, force positions, travel times, city condition, trust relationship, and Hao Pu's alternatives need primary-text comparison. The source presents the deception as decisive, but other military pressures may also have influenced surrender. The concept should not be generalized into a claim that all negotiated surrenders rest on false information.

## What Changed

- Created from Hanji 1152 to distinguish manipulation of relief expectations from broader feints, false surrender, and hidden-force deception.
