# Fresh Start Effect

## Definition

The fresh start effect is a change in motivation around a temporal or situational landmark that separates a hoped-for new period from an earlier identity or performance record.

## Current Synthesis

[[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835|You, But Better]] reports increased gym attendance, diet searches, and goal setting around new weeks, months, years, birthdays, and holidays associated with beginnings. A landmark can create psychological distance from past failure and interrupt routine long enough for reflection. The same reset can be harmful when it erases evidence of success: the episode reports improved performance after cross-league trades for struggling baseball players but decline among players who had been doing well.

## Key Claims

- Temporal landmarks can make a new attempt feel categorically distinct from earlier failure.
- Routine interruption can prompt reflection on goals that daily momentum had obscured.
- Fresh-start motivation is strongest as an opening, not proof of durable follow-through.
- Resetting a negative record can help persistence, while resetting a positive record can remove useful momentum.
- The value of a fresh start therefore depends on the trajectory being interrupted.

## Evidence

- Temporal-landmark behavior - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835]] reports changes in gym attendance, diet searches, and goal setting around beginnings.
- Performance boundary - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835]] uses cross-league baseball trades to contrast struggling and successful players after statistical resets.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The supplied summary does not provide study methods, samples, effect sizes, or replication evidence. A new date or environment cannot remove structural constraints, clinical barriers, accumulated consequences, or the need for concrete planning. People already succeeding may be better served by continuity than symbolic reset.

## What Changed

- Established a two-sided account in which temporal separation can renew or disrupt motivation.
