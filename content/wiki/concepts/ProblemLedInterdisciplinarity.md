## Definition
Problem-led interdisciplinarity is research that crosses disciplinary boundaries because the object of inquiry demands multiple forms of explanation, not because interdisciplinarity is itself a branding goal.

## Current Synthesis
The three sources now connect principle, individual practice, and intellectual movement. Border history shows why geography, ecology, law, political economy, oral history, and everyday life must meet when a changing social relation exceeds one department. [[XuZhuoyun|Xu Zhuoyun]] combines texts, quantitative evidence, sociology, archaeology, comparison, and rural observation to model agricultural society rather than display disciplinary range. The [[AnnalesSchool|Annales tradition]] supplies a wider methodological lineage: official archives, political chronology, and elite biography remain useful but become insufficient when the question concerns collective mentality, material circulation, ordinary experience, or long-lived social structures. Across the cases, the problem selects methods, while evidence standards and explicit limits keep crossing accountable.

## Key Claims
- Disciplinary boundaries provide shared vocabulary and standards but should not determine a question's final shape.
- A real object of inquiry supplies the organizing logic for selecting fields, scales, and evidence.
- Varied sources become explanatory only when they are connected by a defined problem rather than accumulated for breadth.
- Lived observation can generate hypotheses and categories but cannot substitute for systematic evidence and corroboration.
- Interdisciplinary quality depends on explanatory need, source criticism, and integration, not the number of disciplines named.
- A research tradition can institutionalize boundary crossing without turning it into one fixed doctrine.

## Evidence
- Boundary principle: [[405-cong-dili-bianjie-dao-siwei-bianjie-women-ruhe-bei-huajie-suzao-16a80abc]] argues that border life requires geography, ecology, law, political economy, oral history, archives, identity, and everyday practice because the object exceeds one department.
- Institutional constraint: [[405-cong-dili-bianjie-dao-siwei-bianjie-women-ruhe-bei-huajie-suzao-16a80abc]] contrasts real-question inquiry with research organized mainly by a field's production mechanisms.
- Individual historical practice: [[503-wuxi-taibei-pizibao-fengjunwen-huiyi-shidai-jubian-zhong-de-lishixuejia-xuzhuoyun-llhgtepdszpyv65xkizxvaquicu]] describes Xu's study across archaeology, history, sociology, statistics, religion, geography, and the Annales tradition.
- Integrated model evidence: [[503-wuxi-taibei-pizibao-fengjunwen-huiyi-shidai-jubian-zhong-de-lishixuejia-xuzhuoyun-llhgtepdszpyv65xkizxvaquicu]] presents *Han Agriculture* as textual, quantitative, sociological, and observed rural evidence organized around an open, circulating smallholder society.
- Movement-level method: [[516-cong-make-buluohe-ru-si-xianxianci-kan-nianjian-xuepai-yu-dangdai-faguo-shehui-wenhua-sichao-lhu6nyredc2pu2nxvqmrhxw1r6n]] shows Annales historians using economic, medical, literary, legal, geographic, psychological, and material evidence to answer questions official texts could not settle alone.
- Problem integration: [[516-cong-make-buluohe-ru-si-xianxianci-kan-nianjian-xuepai-yu-dangdai-faguo-shehui-wenhua-sichao-lhu6nyredc2pu2nxvqmrhxw1r6n]] uses Bloch's study of royal healing to connect ritual, belief, political legitimacy, church-state competition, and collective mentality.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Crossing fields can produce shallow analogy when specialist standards are ignored, and personal observation can be vivid without being representative. The Annales example also shows that an interdisciplinary tradition does not eliminate methodological disagreement or replace every event-centered and archival approach. These sources establish a research discipline, several historical cases, and an institutional lineage rather than a universal guarantee of rigor.

## What Changed
- Added the Annales tradition as a movement-level case of problem-led evidence expansion.
- Distinguished integrated total history from unbounded source accumulation.
- Added institutionalized interdisciplinarity without assuming doctrinal unity.
