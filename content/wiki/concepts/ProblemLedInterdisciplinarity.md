## Definition
Problem-led interdisciplinarity is research that crosses disciplinary boundaries because the object of inquiry demands multiple forms of explanation, not because interdisciplinarity is itself a branding goal.

## Current Synthesis
The border-history source states the principle: disciplines provide useful entry structures but become restrictive when internal production rules define the question in advance. The Xu Zhuoyun source adds a historical practice case. Xu used texts, rural observation, sociology, statistics, archaeology, geography, environmental material, European history, and civilizational comparison to answer questions about agricultural society and Chinese historical formation. In both cases, the question selects the methods and keeps boundary crossing accountable.

## Key Claims
- Disciplinary boundaries can supply shared vocabulary, standards, and an initial route into a problem.
- The same boundaries become obstacles when the object involves processes they cannot explain alone.
- A genuine problem supplies the organizing logic for selective, accountable boundary crossing.
- Lived observation can generate hypotheses and categories, but it must be tested and extended through disciplined evidence.
- Interdisciplinarity should be judged by explanatory need and evidence quality, not by the number of fields named.

## Evidence
- Boundary principle: [[405-cong-dili-bianjie-dao-siwei-bianjie-women-ruhe-bei-huajie-suzao-16a80abc]] argues that borders require geography, ecology, law, political economy, oral history, archives, identity, and everyday life because the object exceeds one department.
- Institutional constraint: [[405-cong-dili-bianjie-dao-siwei-bianjie-women-ruhe-bei-huajie-suzao-16a80abc]] contrasts real-question inquiry with research organized mainly by a field's production mechanisms.
- Historical practice: [[503-wuxi-taibei-pizibao-fengjunwen-huiyi-shidai-jubian-zhong-de-lishixuejia-xuzhuoyun-llhgtepdszpyv65xkizxvaquicu]] describes Xu's Chicago study across history, sociology, statistics, Weber, religion, and the Annales school.
- Integrated model evidence: [[503-wuxi-taibei-pizibao-fengjunwen-huiyi-shidai-jubian-zhong-de-lishixuejia-xuzhuoyun-llhgtepdszpyv65xkizxvaquicu]] presents *Han Agriculture* as a combination of textual records, quantitative method, and observed rural production used to model an open, circulating smallholder society.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Crossing fields can produce shallow analogy when specialist standards are ignored, and personal observation can be vivid without being representative. The sources offer a research principle and one retrospective practice case, not proof that every interdisciplinary synthesis is rigorous.

## What Changed
- Added Xu Zhuoyun's historical research as a concrete practice case.
- Added the boundary that lived observation should generate and inform inquiry without substituting for systematic evidence.
