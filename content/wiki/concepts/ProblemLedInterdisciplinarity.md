## Definition
Problem-led interdisciplinarity is research that crosses disciplinary boundaries because the object of inquiry demands multiple forms of explanation, not because interdisciplinarity is itself a branding goal.

## Current Synthesis
[[405-cong-dili-bianjie-dao-siwei-bianjie-women-ruhe-bei-huajie-suzao-16a80abc]] treats disciplines as useful entry structures that become restrictive when their internal production rules define the question in advance. Borders illustrate the problem: understanding one line may require geography, ecology, law, political economy, oral history, state archives, identity, and everyday life.

The source's alternative is not undisciplined synthesis. A real question organizes which methods are needed and provides a reason for crossing boundaries. This aligns with [[HistoricalQuestionConsciousness]], where the question acts as a selection mechanism for otherwise unmanageable evidence.

## Key Claims
- Disciplinary boundaries can help researchers enter a shared vocabulary and method.
- The same boundaries become obstacles when inquiry reaches processes they cannot explain alone.
- Institutional incentives can make research reproduce departmental categories rather than pursue the underlying problem.
- A genuine problem supplies the organizing logic for selective, accountable boundary crossing.
- Interdisciplinarity should be judged by explanatory need and evidence, not by the number of fields named.

## Evidence
- Entry value: [[405-cong-dili-bianjie-dao-siwei-bianjie-women-ruhe-bei-huajie-suzao-16a80abc]] says disciplinary framing helps people enter a discussion context.
- Constraint: [[405-cong-dili-bianjie-dao-siwei-bianjie-women-ruhe-bei-huajie-suzao-16a80abc]] says deeper inquiry can turn the same frame into a restriction.
- Institutional mechanism: [[405-cong-dili-bianjie-dao-siwei-bianjie-women-ruhe-bei-huajie-suzao-16a80abc]] contrasts research led by real questions with research led by production mechanisms inside a field.
- Border case: [[405-cong-dili-bianjie-dao-siwei-bianjie-women-ruhe-bei-huajie-suzao-16a80abc]] itself moves from local memory and ecology through archives, state policy, colonialism, economics, and law.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Crossing fields can produce shallow analogy if researchers ignore specialist methods or evidence standards. The source offers a research principle, not a guarantee that every interdisciplinary project is rigorous.
