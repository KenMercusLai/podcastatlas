# AI Director-Core Workflow

## Definition
AI director-core workflow is an AI-video production pattern where teams organize around director intent, scripts, assets, storyboards, shot requirements, performance judgment, and client/audience translation rather than around undifferentiated prompt labor.

## Current Synthesis
The current synthesis is grounded in [[Chouxiangzai|抽象仔 / 抽象宅]], [[Taitai]], [[JiaoshouYiXiaoxing|叫兽易小星]], and [[LiChenFenmo|李晨]]'s practitioner accounts. The earlier What's Next source describes a shift from "抽卡师" execution toward directors who can tell models what scene, movement, emotion, and shot they want. The Chouxiangzai 三五环 source adds internet-expression judgment, client communication, and business translation: a team must know which ideas can become viral public work, which shots a model can execute, and how to reframe client demands without losing the intended expression. The No.227 source adds a film-short collaboration pattern in which one creator supplies theme and script while another turns the script into storyboard, model, shot, and polishing decisions.

The workflow begins before generation, but it need not follow one fixed order. Strong IP or topic choice, audience psychology, scripts, character and scene assets, style constraints, and storyboards can set the odds before a model call; [[ShanYinAIDirector|山音]]'s edit-centered practice shows that useful generated forms can also feed back into text and story. Better [[VideoModels]] such as [[Seedance]]/C-DANCE 2.0 and [[KlingAI|可灵]] reduce some low-level draw labor, but they increase the value of people who can define the scene, judge outputs, preserve continuity, and decide whether a generated result carries the intended emotion.

The new evidence also concentrates accountability. Traditional directors can attribute failures across budget, actors, cinematography, production, or coordination; a small AI team gives the director more direct control and therefore fewer places to displace responsibility. The core skill is not merely accepting a technically successful generation but exercising what Shanyin calls the “眉头一皱的能力”: recognizing what is clichéd, emotionally false, or wrong for the work.

## Key Claims
- AI short-drama production does not become automatic when video models improve; the bottleneck moves upstream to script, IP, assets, storyboards, and directing language.
- The director's ability to "speak to AI" becomes a production capability: the prompt is closer to giving performance direction than to typing a tool command.
- Multi-reference generation can reduce the need for low-level draw labor, but it makes continuity, performance selection, and shot diagnosis more important.
- Director-core AI video includes client communication: rigid literal requests may need to be translated into achievable staging, angles, or props.
- Viral AI-video work adds a public-expression layer: topic choice, cultural contrast, meme literacy, and sincere emotion shape whether generated media travels.
- AI short-film work can split concept/script from visual execution or let both co-evolve, but either pattern still depends on shared taste and clear intent.
- Human review remains central because repeated generations create options, not final creative judgment, and smaller teams concentrate final responsibility in the director.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept is based on practitioner evidence from AI-video, short-drama, advertising, and AI short-film teams. It does not prove that every AI-video workflow should be director-led, that every director must personally execute all departments, or that model improvement will never absorb more continuity and shot-control work. Contest results, festival recognition, production times, income viability, viral-performance claims, ROI claims, and model comparisons remain source-scoped.

## What Changed
- Broadened the workflow from a mostly storyboard-first sequence to include edit-centered co-evolution.
- Added concentrated director accountability as a consequence of smaller teams and greater control.
- Defined rejection of plausible but wrong output as a positive directing capability.
