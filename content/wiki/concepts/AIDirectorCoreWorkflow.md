# AI Director-Core Workflow

## Definition
AI director-core workflow is an AI-video production pattern where teams organize around director intent, scripts, assets, storyboards, shot requirements, performance judgment, and client/audience translation rather than around undifferentiated prompt labor.

## Current Synthesis
The current synthesis is grounded in [[Chouxiangzai|抽象仔 / 抽象宅]] and [[Taitai]]'s practitioner accounts. The earlier What's Next source describes a shift from "抽卡师" execution toward directors who can tell models what scene, movement, emotion, and shot they want. The newer 三五环 source adds that the same workflow also requires internet-expression judgment, client communication, and business translation: a team must know which ideas can become viral public work, which shots a model can execute, and how to reframe client demands without losing the intended expression.

The workflow begins before generation. Strong IP or topic choice, audience psychology, scripts, character and scene assets, and storyboards set the odds before a model call. Production then runs through model-specific generation, editing, performance selection, and client or platform feedback. Better [[VideoModels]] such as [[Seedance]]/C-DANCE 2.0 reduce some low-level draw labor, but they increase the value of people who can define the scene, judge outputs, preserve continuity, and decide whether a generated result carries the intended emotion.

## Key Claims
- AI short-drama production does not become automatic when video models improve; the bottleneck moves upstream to script, IP, assets, storyboards, and directing language.
- The director's ability to "speak to AI" becomes a production capability: the prompt is closer to giving performance direction than to typing a tool command.
- Multi-reference generation can reduce the need for low-level draw labor, but it makes continuity, performance selection, and shot diagnosis more important.
- Director-core AI video includes client communication: rigid literal requests may need to be translated into achievable staging, angles, or props.
- Viral AI-video work adds a public-expression layer: topic choice, cultural contrast, meme literacy, and sincere emotion shape whether generated media travels.
- Human review remains central because repeated generations create options, not final creative judgment.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept is based on practitioner evidence from AI-video and short-drama teams. It does not prove that every AI-video workflow should be director-led, or that model improvement will never absorb more continuity and shot-control work. The sources also keep viral-performance and ROI claims source-scoped.
