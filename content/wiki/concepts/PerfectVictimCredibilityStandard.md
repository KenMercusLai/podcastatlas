# Perfect Victim Credibility Standard / 完美受害者可信度标准

## Definition

The perfect victim credibility standard is a public judgment pattern in which reported harm receives belief only when the harmed person appears exceptionally accomplished, calm, consistent, industrious, and free of unrelated personal faults.

## Current Synthesis

[[ep389-wo-jia-na-guinu-zhang-jiaqi-dai-luwa-dongya-qinzi-guanxi-weihe-zong-chongman-shangtong-he-yihan-gkwridoopvw0aeyu2wtodgpk]] develops the contrast through [[ZhangJiaqiDiver|张家齐]] and [[DaiLuwa|戴露娃]]. Zhang's visible athletic labor, elite results, long public history, confident factual replies, and relatively calm confrontation make her easy for audiences to recognize as wronged. Dai's lower school marks, early relationship, rebellious behavior, acting profession, and incomplete contextualization are instead used to reduce sympathy.

The correct response is not to equalize the two stories or ignore evidential problems. Credibility should track the specific claim, corroboration, context, and uncertainty. A person's unrelated imperfections may explain conflict or motivate further checking, but they do not make hitting, dismissal, or other concrete harms unreal.

## Key Claims

- Public belief often attaches to achievement and composure rather than only to evidence about the reported harm.
- Familiar, verifiable effort gives some victims a credibility reserve unavailable to ordinary people.
- Unrelated faults can be misused to cancel specific harm claims.
- Narrative omission should trigger qualification and further inquiry, not automatic reversal into disbelief.
- Different cases can warrant different judgments without requiring either person to be flawless.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept does not require equal belief in every allegation or prohibit scrutiny of inconsistency, omission, motive, or corroboration. Calmness can help communication, and verifiable records legitimately affect confidence. The error is allowing academic performance, occupation, romance, rebellion, wealth, or likability to substitute for evidence about a specific event. The source describes host impressions of online reaction rather than a representative audience study.

## What Changed

- Established a claim-specific credibility standard that separates evidential qualification from moral demands for personal perfection.
