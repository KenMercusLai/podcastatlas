## Definition
Advertiser pressure and editorial independence / 广告压力下的编辑独立 is the capacity of a publication to preserve criticism and factual standards when a major advertiser can impose substantial financial cost.

## Current Synthesis
Episode 504 grounds the concept in a reported 1987 dispute between the [[HongKongEconomicJournal|《信报》]] and [[LiKaShing|李嘉诚]]. When criticism prompted a threat to withdraw advertising worth millions of Hong Kong dollars, [[LinXingzhi|林行止]] did not retract on that basis. The source argues that the refusal strengthened the paper's public reputation.

The episode pairs resistance with correction. Lin reportedly apologized promptly when a different article's criticism of Wu Jinglian proved factually inaccurate. Editorial independence therefore does not mean defending every published claim; it means distinguishing external leverage from evidence-based correction.

## Key Claims
- Advertising concentration can turn ordinary revenue dependence into editorial leverage.
- Independence becomes visible when a publication accepts material cost rather than suppressing grounded criticism.
- Credibility requires separating pressure-driven retreat from evidence-driven correction.
- Operational resilience and editorial principle are linked because a financially fragile publication has less room to refuse pressure.
- A single confrontation can shape public reputation, but durable independence requires repeated institutional practice.

## Evidence
- Commercial pressure: [[504-lin-xingzhi-xinbao-yu-xianggang-jingji-huangjin-niandai-lsqh0b2fi11uc5udp330ibwuyih]] recounts the threat to remove substantial advertising after criticism of Li Ka-shing.
- Editorial response: [[504-lin-xingzhi-xinbao-yu-xianggang-jingji-huangjin-niandai-lsqh0b2fi11uc5udp330ibwuyih]] says Lin refused to retreat in response to that threat.
- Correction boundary: [[504-lin-xingzhi-xinbao-yu-xianggang-jingji-huangjin-niandai-lsqh0b2fi11uc5udp330ibwuyih]] contrasts the dispute with Lin's apology over inaccurate criticism of Wu Jinglian.
- Organizational basis: [[504-lin-xingzhi-xinbao-yu-xianggang-jingji-huangjin-niandai-lsqh0b2fi11uc5udp330ibwuyih]] shows that early newspaper survival also depended on Luo Youmei's management and financing work.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The advertising value, exact communications, and causal effect on later trust rankings are source-scoped. One successful refusal does not prove complete newsroom independence across all topics or ownership periods.
