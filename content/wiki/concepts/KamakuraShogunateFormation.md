# Kamakura Shogunate Formation

## Definition
Kamakura shogunate formation is the process by which [[MinamotoNoYoritomo]] turns victory in the [[GenpeiWar]] into a military government based in Kamakura while preserving imperial forms in Kyoto.

## Current Synthesis
In [[661-dawn-of-the-samurai-the-shogun-triumphant-part-4-glt7837412375]], the formation of the shogunate is the episode's governing answer to the samurai civil war. [[MinamotoNoYoshitsune]] wins the battles, but Yoritomo controls legitimacy, eliminates rivals, and builds the political structure that survives his own death.

The settlement is not presented as abolishing the emperor. Instead, it resembles a military autocracy dressed in older constitutional language: Kyoto remains the ritual and imperial center, while Kamakura becomes the real governing center.

## Key Claims
- Yoritomo's political achievement is separating real power from direct residence in Kyoto.
- Imperial sanction remains useful enough that samurai rulers need it even after military victory.
- Rival elimination is part of state formation: Kiso and Yoshitsune cannot remain alternative loyalty centers.
- The 1192 shogunal appointment formalizes a shift already visible in Kamakura's practical control.
- The resulting bakufu makes samurai rule durable beyond Yoritomo's lifetime.

## Evidence
- Legitimacy and control: [[661-dawn-of-the-samurai-the-shogun-triumphant-part-4-glt7837412375]] describes the capture and use of [[GoShirakawa]] as constitutional leverage.
- Rival suppression: [[661-dawn-of-the-samurai-the-shogun-triumphant-part-4-glt7837412375]] follows Yoritomo's destruction of [[KisoYoshinaka]] and later persecution of Yoshitsune.
- Institutional result: [[661-dawn-of-the-samurai-the-shogun-triumphant-part-4-glt7837412375]] dates Yoritomo's formal shogun appointment to August 21, 1192, and describes bakufu government from Kamakura.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode uses broad institutional language and comparison to Augustus; administrative details of the early Kamakura order remain outside this source.
