# Joint Supplement and Treatment Boundary / 关节补剂与治疗边界

## Definition
Joint supplement and treatment boundary / 关节补剂与治疗边界 distinguishes nutrients and health products that may support intake or symptoms from interventions that diagnose, repair, or treat joint, cartilage, bone, and pain conditions.

## Current Synthesis
[[vol-90-yundong-yixue-pengyou-ni-huaxue-ma-bugai-yao-chenzao-kemu-san-jinji-renqun-lvfameb1ztoxm5yx5e-ylsrekbsq]] applies this boundary to glucosamine, calcium, and bone broth. Glucosamine is discussed as a cartilage-matrix-related supplement that may offer limited symptom support in some contexts, not as a drug that regenerates cartilage cells or reverses established structural damage. Calcium advice is framed around gradual age-related loss rather than guaranteed restoration, while bone broth is rejected as a direct equivalent of bone or calcium replacement.

The reusable judgment is not whether a product sounds biologically related to a tissue. It is whether there is an actual nutritional need, what outcome is plausible, how strong the evidence is, and whether pain or functional change requires assessment and established treatment instead of consumer self-care.

## Key Claims
- A nutrient being involved in cartilage or bone biology does not prove that oral supplementation rebuilds damaged tissue.
- Supplements and foods should not be presented as substitutes for diagnosis, indicated medication, rehabilitation, or surgery.
- Benefit, if any, can depend on baseline need, disease stage, symptom target, dose, and individual context.
- Pain that affects activity or daily life should be clinically assessed rather than masked through indefinite reliance on health products.
- Food resemblance is not treatment evidence: bone-derived food is not automatically a meaningful calcium or bone therapy.

- Glucosamine boundary: [[vol-90-yundong-yixue-pengyou-ni-huaxue-ma-bugai-yao-chenzao-kemu-san-jinji-renqun-lvfameb1ztoxm5yx5e-ylsrekbsq]] describes glucosamine as a supplement related to cartilage matrix while rejecting it as a fundamental repair for cartilage cells.
- Treatment escalation: [[vol-90-yundong-yixue-pengyou-ni-huaxue-ma-bugai-yao-chenzao-kemu-san-jinji-renqun-lvfameb1ztoxm5yx5e-ylsrekbsq]] says pain affecting life should prompt care and established treatment rather than reliance on glucosamine alone.
- Calcium and food claims: [[vol-90-yundong-yixue-pengyou-ni-huaxue-ma-bugai-yao-chenzao-kemu-san-jinji-renqun-lvfameb1ztoxm5yx5e-ylsrekbsq]] frames calcium loss as gradual and says bone broth mainly supplies fat rather than functioning as direct bone replacement.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode does not review trials, product formulation, guideline recommendations, deficiency testing, dietary intake, kidney risk, medication interactions, or comparative treatment outcomes. Its age thresholds and efficacy language should therefore remain source-scoped; supplement decisions can require individualized nutritional and medical review.
