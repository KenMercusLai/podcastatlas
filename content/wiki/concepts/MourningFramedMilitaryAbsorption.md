# 丧礼化收编 / Mourning-Framed Military Absorption

丧礼化收编 / mourning-framed military absorption is the political mechanism [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1010-sanguo-liubiao-jiujing-shibushi-piaoliang-de-caobao-lo58hzfo-qv0soauogtgfrkourrd|Hanji 1010]] extracts from [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s handling of [[ZhangJiLateHan|张济]]'s death at [[Rangcheng|穰城]]. Liu Biao refuses to treat the death as a victory, says he receives mourning rather than congratulations, and sends people to reassure Zhang Ji's remaining soldiers.

The mechanism works because it gives the defeated or leaderless force a dignified path into submission. Liu Biao does not advertise conquest; he reframes the clash as an unfortunate failure of hospitality, which reduces revenge pressure and lets the remnants enter [[Jingzhou|荆州]]'s order without feeling publicly humiliated.

The concept is close to [[PoliticalSurrenderCascade|招降示范级联]] and [[OccupationPacificationStrategy|占领区怀柔治理]], but it is narrower. Those concepts emphasize surrender policy and pacification after conquest; this source emphasizes moral language, mourning, and face-saving absorption after a disorder-era armed collision.

## Key Claims
- Mourning language can soften the optics of victory and make absorption easier.
- A ruler can gain force without presenting the event as open conquest.
- The defeated side's face and future safety are part of the strategic terrain.
- Liu Biao's move shows political skill even if the broader episode still treats him as a ruler with a strategic ceiling.
