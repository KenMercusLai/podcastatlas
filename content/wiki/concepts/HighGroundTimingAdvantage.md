# High-Ground Timing Advantage / 制高点时机优势

High-Ground Timing Advantage / 制高点时机优势 is the tactical pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-87-xialu-xiangfeng-yongzhe-sheng-zhongyu-zhaodao-chuchu-le-loppr83sdq5hhesmqihckwmhpl2h]] draws from [[XuLiWarringStates|许历 / 许立]]'s advice during [[EyuBattle|阏与 / 燕宇之战]]. Xu Li tells [[ZhaoShe|赵奢]] that the side which first reaches the northern mountain will dominate the battle.

The concept is about timing as much as terrain. High ground matters because both sides can see its value, but only one side can occupy it first. Zhao She's rapid forced march creates the possibility, and Xu Li's advice turns that possibility into a concrete deployment order for ten thousand elite troops.

## Key Claims
- Terrain advantage can be time-sensitive; value is lost if the enemy occupies the point first.
- Tactical advice is most useful when it converts a general principle into immediate deployment.
- At Eyu, high-ground timing completes the earlier deception sequence: Qin misreads Zhao's delay, while Zhao arrives soon enough to seize the decisive feature.
