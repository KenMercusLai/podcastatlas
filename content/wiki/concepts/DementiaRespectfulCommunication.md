# Dementia Respectful Communication / 认知症尊重式沟通

## Definition
Dementia respectful communication is a person-centered approach that reduces memory tests, correction, abstraction, and overload while using introductions, simple language, visible choices, invitation, tone, expression, and body posture to preserve understanding and agency.

## Current Synthesis
The episode's core correction is that communication should not repeatedly expose a person's deficit. Asking “do you remember who I am?” can turn contact into a test; saying “Mom, I'm your son” supplies the missing context and lets the relationship continue.

Because language comprehension and expression may weaken unevenly, useful communication combines one idea at a time with nonverbal warmth and concrete objects. Respect also means speaking to the person rather than discussing them as if absent, offering bounded choices, and inviting participation so remaining ability is used rather than erased.

The household-care episode adds an emotional and longitudinal layer. Patience, encouragement, familiar routines, visible cues, and manageable contribution can reduce confrontation and the feeling of being useless or rejected. This is not agreement with every unsafe action; it is a way to preserve relationship and remaining capability while adapting support as cognition changes.

## Key Claims
- Memory testing during ordinary interaction can create shame or distress without adding useful information.
- Introductions and contextual cues can support connection more effectively than demanding recall.
- One-step language reduces cognitive and linguistic load.
- Tone, facial expression, gesture, posture, and objects can carry meaning when verbal processing is difficult.
- Concrete, bounded choices preserve agency without creating excessive complexity.
- Speaking about a person in their presence as though they are absent undermines dignity.
- Invitation and useful participation can preserve belonging and remaining capability.

## Evidence
- Recall-test alternative - [[vol-133-nin-haijide-wo-shi-shui-ma-qing-tingzhi-zheyang-de-tiwen-nineng-zuo-de-haiyou-henduo-lmnn6rq16uvpkpmnimxfd5xqbr4a]] replaces “do you remember who I am?” with an active introduction.
- Cognitive-load reduction - [[vol-133-nin-haijide-wo-shi-shui-ma-qing-tingzhi-zheyang-de-tiwen-nineng-zuo-de-haiyou-henduo-lmnn6rq16uvpkpmnimxfd5xqbr4a]] recommends simple communication and one task or idea at a time.
- Choice scaffolding - [[vol-133-nin-haijide-wo-shi-shui-ma-qing-tingzhi-zheyang-de-tiwen-nineng-zuo-de-haiyou-henduo-lmnn6rq16uvpkpmnimxfd5xqbr4a]] uses holding up an apple and an orange as a concrete choice.
- Nonverbal sensitivity - [[vol-133-nin-haijide-wo-shi-shui-ma-qing-tingzhi-zheyang-de-tiwen-nineng-zuo-de-haiyou-henduo-lmnn6rq16uvpkpmnimxfd5xqbr4a]] says facial expression, voice, and posture remain emotionally important.
- Participation - [[vol-133-nin-haijide-wo-shi-shui-ma-qing-tingzhi-zheyang-de-tiwen-nineng-zuo-de-haiyou-henduo-lmnn6rq16uvpkpmnimxfd5xqbr4a]] recommends invitations that communicate respect and usefulness.
- Long-term emotional support - [[vol-99-ba-xueke-lianhe-chunjie-jiankang-dai-huijia-gei-zan-bu-tingquan-de-bama-zhenjing-jiazuqun-de-yangsheng-ganhuo-yinshi-huli-dahan-xingeng-zuzhong-xiaohua-deng-lrvlx1wbukf4g2pbsem4cbzqbuqz]] connects patient encouragement, stable habits, useful participation, and avoidance of confrontational correction with dignity during progressive cognitive change.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The advice is a public-care framework, not a universal script. Communication needs vary with dementia type and stage, hearing, vision, language, culture, distress, pain, and environment. Respectful support does not remove the need to assess sudden or severe change clinically.

## What Changed
- Added encouragement, continuity, and useful participation as protections against shame and perceived rejection.
