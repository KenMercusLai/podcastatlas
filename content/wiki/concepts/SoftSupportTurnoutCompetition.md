# 软支持者投票率竞争 / Soft-Support Turnout Competition

## Definition
Soft-support turnout competition is an electoral contest over whether weakly attached favorable voters actually participate, not only over which candidate they name in a preference question.

## Current Synthesis
The Taipei case in [[b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26-b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26mp3aid6af3219e1f5126ac85b049f0chid66b07190af99592]] centers voters who previously supported [[HuangShanshan|黄珊珊]]. They reportedly view [[ShenBoyang|沈伯洋]] more favorably but lack stable attachment to his party. Shen's task is therefore to make the choice important and asymmetric enough to generate turnout; [[JiangWanan|蒋万安]] can improve his relative position by reducing their confidence or enthusiasm even if they never switch to him.

## Key Claims
- Favorability, stated vote choice, and actual turnout are distinct electoral states.
- Weak party attachment makes support more sensitive to motivation and candidate image.
- A candidate can benefit from demobilizing an opponent's soft supporters without converting them.
- Positive candidate definition can increase both preference and willingness to participate.
- Turnout gaps across different election types should not be read as direct evidence of issue support or opposition.

## Evidence
- Pivotal bloc: [[b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26-b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26mp3aid6af3219e1f5126ac85b049f0chid66b07190af99592]] identifies former Huang voters as decisive independents with greater current affinity for Shen.
- Weak attachment: [[b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26-b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26mp3aid6af3219e1f5126ac85b049f0chid66b07190af99592]] says their lack of stable party identification makes willingness to vote softer.
- Asymmetric strategy: [[b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26-b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26mp3aid6af3219e1f5126ac85b049f0chid66b07190af99592]] says Shen needs to strengthen participation while Chiang can benefit merely by reducing enthusiasm.
- Turnout interpretation: [[b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26-b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26mp3aid6af3219e1f5126ac85b049f0chid66b07190af99592]] notes lower recall turnout than recent mayoral turnout and rejects classifying nonparticipants as a unified pro- or anti-recall bloc.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source supplies aggregate turnout comparisons and selected polls, not individual-level evidence linking former Huang voters to current preference and future participation. Election type, timing, campaign intensity, and sampling assumptions can all affect turnout estimates.

## What Changed
- Established a three-part distinction among favorability, preference, and participation.
- Added demobilization without conversion as an explicit campaign pathway.
