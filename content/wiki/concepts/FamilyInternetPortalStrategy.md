# Family Internet Portal Strategy

Family internet portal strategy is [[ChenTianqiao]] and [[Shanda|盛大]]'s attempt to turn online entertainment assets into a living-room entry point. [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]] adds the concept through [[ShandaBox|盛大盒子]], acquisitions in games and content, and the idea that a company could become a networked entertainment platform rather than only a game operator.

The source shows both the appeal and weakness of the strategy. [[ShandaLiterature|盛大文学]], [[Qidian|起点中文网]], [[Haofang|浩方]], [[Bianfeng|边锋]], [[Ku6|酷六]], and the [[Sina|新浪]] stake purchase all make sense as pieces of a content-and-entry-point puzzle, but bandwidth, hardware cost, regulation, user habits, and partner dependence kept the system from closing.
