# Informal Jianghu Order

## Definition
Informal Jianghu order is the non-state rule system that lets mobile, low-trust, or semi-legal Jianghu actors coordinate work, resolve conflict, protect trade knowledge, and survive without stable formal protection.

## Current Synthesis
The strongest institutional detail comes through [[JiangHuCongTan|《江湖丛谈》]]: 老何, 拜相/拜码头, 常春会, 生意下处, trade-spacing rules, taboo speech, thief-guard negotiation, and respected insiders all help [[StreetJianghuSociety]] maintain working order outside formal law [[102-jianghu-congtan-pianshu-heihua-he-minjian-daoyi-736516488]].

A practical-skill layer appears in the "以江为眼，以湖为口" formula: one must see clearly, read people, improvise, and use口才 to keep moving [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-483-2-lishi-jiemi-hun-zhichang-de-jianghu-guiju-ltukp3q2cg7p5egxqte7nuseenaw]]. The same skills can support performance, mediation, persuasion, or deception, so the concept remains morally mixed rather than celebratory.

## Key Claims
- Informal order can exist without formal legality, but it remains vulnerable to coercion, exclusion, and predation.
- The episode's "义气" is often instrumental: people keep rules because everyone needs enough predictability to continue earning.
- Local brokers such as 老何 help strangers interpret a market, avoid conflict, and know when to move elsewhere.
- Lodging rules and trade-spacing customs protect secrets, tools, income, and status boundaries.
- Thief-guard negotiation shows a non-state conflict-management logic, but it also exposes how much protection depends on belonging to the network.
- Observation and adaptive speech are practical infrastructure: they let people identify risk, perform competence, negotiate belonging, or mislead others.

## Evidence
### Rule And Mediation Mechanisms
- [[102-jianghu-congtan-pianshu-heihua-he-minjian-daoyi-736516488]] details brokers, initiation gestures, meeting associations, lodging sites, trade spacing, taboo speech, and insider mediation as working order outside formal law.

### Instrumental Morality
- [[102-jianghu-congtan-pianshu-heihua-he-minjian-daoyi-736516488]] treats Jianghu morality as practical rather than pure: people preserve enough order because everyone still needs to earn.

### Observation And Speech
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-483-2-lishi-jiemi-hun-zhichang-de-jianghu-guiju-ltukp3q2cg7p5egxqte7nuseenaw]] frames "眼亮" and口才 as core Jianghu skills, including quick adaptation and, at the predatory edge, seamless lying.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Informal order is not the same as justice. The same mechanisms that reduce uncertainty can gatekeep outsiders, shelter coercion, or enable fraud. The Hanji 483-2 skill formula is thematic commentary, while the [[JiangHuCongTan|《江湖丛谈》]] source provides more concrete street-practice evidence.
