# Creator-Parasite Moral Frame / 创造者-寄生者框架

Creator-Parasite Moral Frame / 创造者-寄生者框架 is the episode's way of reading [[AynRand|Ayn Rand / 安·兰德]]'s fiction as symbolic moral sorting rather than psychological realism. In [[09-atelasi-songsongjian-anlande-zhishi-shuangwen-zuojia-ma-543277898]], the frame links [[TheFountainhead|The Fountainhead / 《源泉》]] and [[AtlasShrugged|Atlas Shrugged / 《阿特拉斯耸耸肩》]]: creators produce buildings, railways, metal, energy, knowledge, or value directly, while parasites live through imitation, flattery, regulation, shame, moral blackmail, or institutional capture.

The source treats this as both powerful and dangerous. It can give readers a vocabulary for resisting social pressure, false humility, and compulsory sacrifice. But it can also become a sorting weapon when readers assume they are creators and others are parasites, which is where the concept touches [[PureRationalityTrap]] and [[IdeologicalTotalizationRisk]].

## Key Claims
- The frame works best as an allegorical reading tool, not as a reliable diagnostic for real people.
- In 《源泉》, the contrast is most visible between independent making, social-climbing imitation, and moralized power over taste.
- In 《阿特拉斯耸耸肩》, the contrast expands from art and architecture to production, infrastructure, science, entrepreneurship, and state-backed extraction.
- The source's caution is that mythic clarity can become contempt if separated from ordinary human complexity.
