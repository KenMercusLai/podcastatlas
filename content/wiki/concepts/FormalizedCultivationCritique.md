# 形式化修行批判 / Formalized Cultivation Critique

## Definition

Formalized cultivation critique is the claim that outward signs of practice can become evasive or self-flattering when they do not correspond to inner non-attachment, compassion, or honest self-knowledge.

## Current Synthesis

In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-5-xiuxingren-shi-ruhe-kandai-xiyou-de-lurr8m74eut6qcemzruhn3ia62oq|Hanji 456-5]], the critique appears through [[ChenXuanzangConqueringDemons|陈玄奘]] and his master in [[JourneyToTheWestConqueringDemons|《西游降魔篇》]]. Chen can speak of universal love and maintain surface purity, but the episode says he is still caught in distinctions around gender, desire, food, and moral status. The master's goose-leg lesson makes the point sharply: eating or not eating matters less than whether the attachment remains in the heart.

## Key Claims

- External abstinence can hide attachment when the practitioner still desires what is publicly refused.
- Moral language can become a way to avoid admitting ordinary love, desire, fear, or weakness.
- Real cultivation requires facing inner desire directly rather than preserving a clean self-image.
- Criticizing others' visible practice can become self-elevation rather than discernment.
- The concept remains source-scoped because it is developed through a film interpretation, not a full doctrinal account.

## Evidence

- External abstinence versus inner attachment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-5-xiuxingren-shi-ruhe-kandai-xiyou-de-lurr8m74eut6qcemzruhn3ia62oq]] uses the master's goose-leg teaching to say that having no goose leg in the heart matters more than outwardly refusing meat.
- Moral language as evasion: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-5-xiuxingren-shi-ruhe-kandai-xiyou-de-lurr8m74eut6qcemzruhn3ia62oq]] reads Chen's "great love" language as partly covering his unresolved attraction and attachment to [[DuanMissConqueringDemons|段小姐]].
- Directly facing desire: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-5-xiuxingren-shi-ruhe-kandai-xiyou-de-lurr8m74eut6qcemzruhn3ia62oq]] says desire must be faced rather than merely seen through or avoided, using [[ZhuBajie|猪八戒]] / 朱刚烈 as the symbolic case.
- Self-elevating practice critique: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-5-xiuxingren-shi-ruhe-kandai-xiyou-de-lurr8m74eut6qcemzruhn3ia62oq]] criticizes people who use surface markers such as not eating meat to judge others and raise themselves.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

This concept should not be flattened into a claim that forms, vows, abstinence, or discipline are useless. The source's narrower point is that form without inner transformation can become another attachment.
