# Talent Referral Quality / 荐才层级质量

Talent referral quality / 荐才层级质量 is the chancellor-selection lesson in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-1-lk-6ejgmjhvukqgtlz2lowu8qx-v]]. [[DiHuang|翟黄]] argues from the number and usefulness of his recommendations, while [[LiKe|李克]] explains why [[WeiCheng|魏成]] can still be superior.

The source's distinction is level and effect. Di Huang's referrals, including [[WuQi|吴起]], [[XimenBao|西门豹]], [[LeYang|乐羊]], Li Ke himself, and [[QuHoufu|屈侯鲋]], are useful ministers, generals, officials, or teachers. Wei Cheng's network brings in [[ZiXia|子夏]], [[TianZifang|田子方]], and [[DuanGanmu|段干木]], figures [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]] treats as his own teachers. That changes not only staffing, but the ruler's learning environment and the state's reputation for honoring worth.

The concept does not dismiss operational talent. It says chancellor-level evaluation has to ask what kind of people a candidate draws toward the center of power, what relationship those people have to the ruler, and whether their presence raises the political order rather than merely filling posts.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-12-gongsunyang-qiuzhi-lu-shang-de-la-guanxi-zou-houmen-lvv4-bz8sm9scezci-o-msapkvil]] adds a darker version through [[GongshuWeiChancellor|公叔痤]] and [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]]. Gongshu's recommendation has high predictive quality: the later Qin career proves he was right about Shang Yang's capacity. But recommendation quality is not enough if [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王 / 梁惠王]] lacks prior trust in the candidate; Qin converts the opportunity only when [[JingJian|景监]] makes Shang Yang's access to [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]] actionable.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-75-2-qiongshe-jichi-de-zushiye-guanzhong-lo8uei2u1qwj9tszftel1b4lswoa]] adds the former-enemy version through [[BaoShuya|鲍叔牙]]. Bao Shuya recommends [[GuanZhong|管仲]] to [[QiHuanGong|齐桓公]] even though Guan Zhong served [[GongziJiu|公子纠]] and shot at Jiang Xiaobai during the succession race. The quality of the recommendation lies not just in recognizing talent, but in persuading a new ruler that state need outranks personal revenge.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-99-2-mingming-yilian-baixiang-de-caize-ping-shenme-baixiang-lhfyb6hzcqkpbzdrwsxajb7hhmhe]] adds a more ambiguous recommendation through [[FanJu|范雎]] and [[CaiZe|蔡泽]]. Cai Ze is talented enough to impress [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]], but the host also reads Fan Ju's recommendation as a possible way to hand off political danger after [[ZhengAnping|郑安平]] and [[WangJiQin|王稽 / 王继]] have made Fan Ju vulnerable. Referral quality therefore has to ask whether a recommendation serves the ruler's needs, the candidate's fit, or the recommender's exit problem.

## Key Claims
- Referral quantity is weaker evidence than the level, fit, and effects of referred talent.
- A candidate's network can show what kind of political culture they will build around the ruler.
- Ministerial capability and teacher-level influence are different forms of contribution.
- The concept extends [[TalentAttractionThroughRespect]] by showing that a minister's own resource use can help attract respected people.
- A good recommendation can still fail when the ruler lacks enough trust in either the recommender's judgment or the candidate's status.
- In the Bao Shuya case, recommendation quality includes making an enemy's talent politically acceptable to the injured ruler.
- A recommendation can be partly self-protective; talent recognition and risk transfer may coexist.
