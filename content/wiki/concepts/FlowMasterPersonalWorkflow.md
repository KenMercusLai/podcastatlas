# Flow Master Personal Workflow

## Definition
Flow Master personal workflow is the self-management role that observes the work system, identifies bottlenecks and friction, adjusts process design, and protects the executor from converting every delay into self-blame.

## Current Synthesis
The episode's Flow Master idea is the personal counterpart to a Scrum Master or operations manager. The worker is not only an executor who must push harder; they also need a process owner who asks why the flow is blocked, what WIP should be closed, which bottleneck needs help, whether slack is adequate, and what can be changed in the next sprint.

This separation matters psychologically. When execution slips, the Flow Master frame moves the response from shame to diagnosis. The question becomes how to redesign the system so finished work can flow with lower loss.

## Key Claims
- Self-management improves when execution and process diagnosis are treated as separate roles.
- The Flow Master watches bottlenecks, WIP, queueing slack, critical paths, and recurring waste.
- Workflow problems should trigger redesign before self-blame.
- Weekly review gives the Flow Master evidence about the system rather than vague mood data.
- Learning and recovery belong inside the system because they affect long-term throughput.

## Evidence
- Role-splitting evidence: [[ep86-dianfu-gongzuoliu-xiaolu-tisheng-3000-gkwrimaoad7vbjtrsqtotc4k]] says personal task management can split into executor and Flow Master.
- Diagnosis evidence: [[ep86-dianfu-gongzuoliu-xiaolu-tisheng-3000-gkwrimaoad7vbjtrsqtotc4k]] has the Flow Master solve process problems rather than blame the executor.
- Sprint evidence: [[ep86-dianfu-gongzuoliu-xiaolu-tisheng-3000-gkwrimaoad7vbjtrsqtotc4k]] ties Flow Master work to weekly sprint planning and retrospective review.
- Learning-system evidence: [[ep86-dianfu-gongzuoliu-xiaolu-tisheng-3000-gkwrimaoad7vbjtrsqtotc4k]] treats learning as part of the long-term content-production and personal-output system.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The Flow Master role is the host's personal management construct, not a standardized operations term. It is most useful as a reflective stance for knowledge work, and it should not replace accountability, collaboration, or medical care where those are the actual constraints.
