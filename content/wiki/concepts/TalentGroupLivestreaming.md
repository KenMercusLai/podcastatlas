# Talent Group Livestreaming / 才艺团播

Talent group livestreaming / 才艺团播 is the source's term for a staged live-performance format where multiple anchors dance, sing, interact, switch camera focus, and respond to viewers inside a guild-managed production room. In [[baiwan-ge-jingguan-shehui-tingshuo-ni-ye-xiang-dang-zhubo-lgd0yapclwvsessg7xnhaarpyiwy]], [[TianFeng|田峰]] and his students show that this is not simply a group of attractive performers but a live system involving hosts, operators, cameras, lights, makeup, costume, data, platform rules, and review.

The concept extends [[LivestreamPersonaLabor]] from individual persona management into ensemble coordination. A member has to know when to face which camera, how to respond to a fan or comment while dancing, when to hold a moment for the host, and how to maintain a differentiated role inside the group.

## Key Claims
- Talent group livestreaming combines stage performance, short-video pacing, live chat, and platform monetization.
- Its production quality depends on backstage labor: host judgment, operator monitoring, camera movement, lighting, styling, makeup, rehearsal, and review.
- The group format distributes expression opportunities, because giving attention to only one member can damage teamwork and audience variety.
- The format can absorb people from art schools, dance training, trainee systems, variety shows, theaters, TV stations, short video, and other performance paths.
- It is a high-turnover labor market: professional support does not eliminate stamina pressure, uncertain upside, or intense competition.
