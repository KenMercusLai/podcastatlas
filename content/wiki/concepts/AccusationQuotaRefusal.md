## Definition

Accusation quota refusal / 诬陷指标拒绝 is the official-integrity pattern where an administrator refuses to manufacture named offenders merely because a superior campaign expects each jurisdiction to produce cases.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 877 creates the concept through [[ShiBiLateHan|史弼]]. During the Party Prohibition reporting campaign, other Qingzhou jurisdictions submit lists of party figures, and court pressure treats Pingyuan's zero report as suspicious. Shi Bi refuses to convert that pressure into names, arguing that local conditions differ and that if he were willing to frame innocent people, every household in Pingyuan could be made into a party case.

The concept sits between bureaucratic compliance and moral courage. Shi Bi is not rejecting all state authority; he is rejecting the premise that a campaign expectation can substitute for evidence. Because the names would feed [[RegistryBasedPoliticalExclusion|登记造册式政治禁锢]] and broader Party Prohibition liability, false compliance would create real future harm.

## Key Claims

- Campaign pressure can behave like a quota even when no explicit number is named.
- Cross-jurisdiction comparison is weak evidence: other places producing cases does not prove every place must have cases.
- A truthful zero report can look noncompliant inside a politicized enforcement campaign.
- Fabricating names under pressure converts administrative obedience into harm against innocent households.
- Refusal is meaningful before the official knows whether amnesty or punishment will follow.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept does not claim that every zero report is truthful. It applies where the source frames the zero report as evidence-bound and the superior demand as pressure to fabricate. Hanji 877's evidence is source-scoped to Shi Bi's Pingyuan case.

## What Changed

- Hanji 877 creates the concept from Shi Bi's refusal to invent Party Prohibition names under Qingzhou and court pressure.
