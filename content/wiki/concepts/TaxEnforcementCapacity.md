# Tax Enforcement Capacity

## Definition

Tax enforcement capacity is the practical ability of a state to identify, investigate, contest, adjudicate, and collect tax obligations from actors with incentives and resources to resist.

## Current Synthesis

The complete source set shows that rules do not enforce themselves. In ancient [[ZhaoState|赵国]], capacity means authority to make an elite household comply and political skill to show that fiscal privilege weakens the state protecting that household's wealth. In the modern [[MaltaTaxLoophole]] case, it means expert staff, treaty interpretation, summonses, legal doctrine, disclosure requirements, and continuity across political turnover.

The multinational-litigation branch adds scale and duration. The [[InternalRevenueService|IRS]] may identify a disputed [[TransferPricing]] method and win at trial, yet still need years of specialized legal and accounting work to defend the result on appeal against a multinational firm. Capacity therefore includes endurance, not only detection or statutory authority.

## Key Claims

- Legal rules require staff, expertise, evidence, authority, and political backing to have practical force.
- Powerful households and multinational firms are especially demanding enforcement targets.
- Warnings are weaker than disclosure, investigation, case-specific action, and durable adjudication.
- Delay, turnover, and resource asymmetry can shape the effective tax boundary.
- Enforcement capacity protects revenue, equal treatment, and taxpayers who may otherwise buy risky advice.
- Litigation endurance becomes more important when intangible assets make valuation complex.

## Evidence

- Elite-compliance authority: [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-86-lishishang-kao-shouzu-de-jiangjun-jingran-shi-ta-lmmmfz4ii-hx7trbxv2rcpkllcq]] presents [[ZhaoShe|赵奢]] enforcing tax law against [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]]'s household and linking compliance to state survival.
- Administrative toolkit: [[the-leaked-tapes-that-show-how-the-rich-avoid-taxes]] describes IRS warnings, treaty clarification, summonses, economic-substance arguments, and proposed shelter disclosure.
- Political continuity: [[the-leaked-tapes-that-show-how-the-rich-avoid-taxes]] links stalled rulemaking and enforcement uncertainty to staff reductions, political turnover, and conflicts around senior appointments.
- Litigation endurance: [[islamism-in-britain-what-is-the-threat-6aa9164ffca3d5c77b4b383a]] describes a Coca-Cola case lasting more than a decade, with accumulated interest and repeated use of the disputed formula increasing the stakes.
- Resource asymmetry: [[islamism-in-britain-what-is-the-threat-6aa9164ffca3d5c77b4b383a]] contrasts reduced IRS resources with multinational access to tax lawyers and accountants.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Greater capacity does not prove that every assessment is correct or that aggressive enforcement is fair. The Malta strategy and Coca-Cola allocation remain legally contested, while the Zhao She account belongs to a very different institutional order. Comparable valuation, due process, taxpayer service, and limits on agency power remain necessary alongside enforcement strength.
