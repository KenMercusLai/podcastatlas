# Satellite Heat-Signature Conflict Monitoring

## Definition
Satellite heat-signature conflict monitoring repurposes orbital fire-detection data to use intense thermal events as a near-real-time proxy for explosions and combat activity.

## Current Synthesis
The episode describes *The Economist* using a [[NASA]] system built for forest-fire detection to compare activity along the Russia–Ukraine front. The method adds a timely, consistently observed signal to territorial maps: it indicates an unusually active three-month period in which Ukraine reportedly made modest net gains. It does not by itself identify the weapon, responsible force, casualties, or strategic value of each detected event.

## Key Claims
- Civilian Earth-observation systems can be repurposed for conflict measurement.
- Heat detections provide a comparative activity signal rather than a complete battle record.
- Activity intensity and territorial direction should be analyzed separately.
- Small net gains can be meaningful on a largely static front without establishing overall strategic advantage.
- Method interpretation requires conventional mapping, operational context, and explicit uncertainty.

## Evidence
- **Repurposed sensor:** The source says a NASA forest-fire system was used to detect thermal signatures from explosions. [[all-eyes-on-eisenkot-israels-could-be-prime-minister-6af19d18e7c6d309d1116743]]
- **Comparative intensity:** The resulting series reportedly showed more frontline activity during the preceding three months than in any comparable period of the war. [[all-eyes-on-eisenkot-israels-could-be-prime-minister-6af19d18e7c6d309d1116743]]
- **Territorial interpretation:** Ukraine’s reported net gain of 73 square kilometres and [[OperationVivaldi]] provide map and operational context for the heat signal. [[all-eyes-on-eisenkot-israels-could-be-prime-minister-6af19d18e7c6d309d1116743]]

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Heat can indicate an explosion without reliably classifying its cause, side, target, casualty effect, or military importance. Cloud, sensor coverage, detection thresholds, repeated strikes, and the baseline chosen for comparison may affect interpretation. The source provides conclusions but not enough methodological detail to reproduce the analysis, and intense activity can coexist with only minor territorial movement.
