# Democratic Faction Map

## Definition
Democratic faction map is Silver's source-specific division of the Democratic Party into the left, abundance liberals, and resistance liberals.

## Current Synthesis
The concept gives the wiki a compact way to distinguish different Democratic responses to the same anti-status-quo environment. The left emphasizes ideological and anti-establishment contrast; abundance liberals are more pro-market and critical of blue-state governance constraints; resistance liberals organize around opposition to Trump and blue-team defense. The map matters because 2026 and 2028 strategy can look different depending on which faction defines the party's offer.

## Key Claims
- Silver places AOC, Bernie Sanders, and Zohran Mamdani in the Democratic left faction.
- Abundance liberals are presented as more pro-market and more critical of blue-state governance failure.
- Gavin Newsom's base is described as closer to resistance liberalism and blue-team fighter politics.
- Factional strength matters more in change elections because voters may reject continuity even inside their own party.
- The map helps explain why the same Democratic environment can favor the party in 2026 but make particular 2028 candidates vulnerable.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This is Silver's source-specific taxonomy, not a complete political-science ontology. It also compresses many politicians and voters into broad buckets; later sources may split abundance, left-populist, labor, identity, and institutional Democratic factions more finely.
