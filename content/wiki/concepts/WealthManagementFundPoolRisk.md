# Wealth-Management Fund Pool Risk / 银行理财资金池风险

## Definition

Wealth-management fund-pool risk arises when money from multiple products or periods is operated collectively while the apparent product maturity, valuation, or repayment promise differs from the liquidity and credit cycle of the underlying assets.

## Current Synthesis

The bank-wealth source describes the historical mechanism: rolling short-duration products funded longer loans, trust-beneficiary rights, non-standard debt, or other credit-like assets; pooled operation and amortized-cost accounting could smooth reported returns while new issuance or other product income absorbed losses. This made products feel deposit-like and supported implicit guarantees.

Episode 187 extends the mechanism beyond formal bank wealth products. A wealth manager or quasi-financial institution can combine fund pooling with self-financing or related-party borrowers, leaving the same controller to pursue industrial expansion and judge financial risk. The investor-facing product may look stable until liquidity or confidence breaks, so smooth repayment history and moderate yield are not evidence that assets and liabilities are matched.

## Key Claims

- **Apparent product maturity can understate asset duration.** Short issuance may fund long, illiquid, or non-standard credit.
- **Pooling can weaken product-level traceability.** Investors may not know which asset, borrower, or cash flow supports their claim.
- **Accounting smoothness is not economic safety.** Amortized cost or discretionary valuation can delay visible recognition of credit and exit risk.
- **Self-financing compounds the conflict.** A related industrial group may control both capital demand and risk approval.
- **Rollover dependence can transform local losses into system-wide fragility.** New money or other products may temporarily conceal impaired assets.
- **Regulation reduces but does not abolish the need for investor verification.** Legal issuer, underlying assets, related parties, liquidity, and valuation still matter after net-value reform.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Pooling, amortized-cost accounting, long-duration assets, or related-party transactions do not individually establish fraud. Some are lawful under defined mandates and controls. Risk rises when traceability, independent underwriting, liquidity matching, valuation discipline, and disclosure are weak. Episode 187's unnamed cases remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Extended the concept from historical bank wealth products to quasi-financial institutions and private wealth managers.
- Added self-financing and common-control conflicts between industrial expansion and financial underwriting.
- Clarified that moderate yield and smooth prior payments do not validate asset-liability matching.
