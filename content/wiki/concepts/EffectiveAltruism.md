# Effective Altruism

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-anthropics-2t-ipo-zucks-ai-manifesto-nvidias-500b-ai-bet-groks-comeback-42442555]] adds a sharply critical AI-governance use of effective altruism. [[GavinBaker|Gavin Baker]] characterizes the effective-altruist position around [[Anthropic]] as believing AI is too dangerous to distribute, while he contrasts it with [[DecentralizedAIControl]] and the view that concentrated AI power can itself be dangerous.

Effective altruism appears in [[brave-new-whirl-turkeys-opposition-overhaul-6a69c7766043035f89eb097f]] as the influence behind [[BenjaminTodd]]'s impact-oriented career advice in [[EightyThousandHours|80,000 Hours]]. The source uses it as a way to compare career options by their likely contribution to global good.

In this episode, effective altruism is not explored as a full movement history. It functions as the intellectual background for [[ImpactCareerChoice]], including interest in fields such as pandemic prevention and AI regulation. The reviewer admires the ambition but warns that impact maximization can become demoralizing if it overwhelms ordinary career exploration.
