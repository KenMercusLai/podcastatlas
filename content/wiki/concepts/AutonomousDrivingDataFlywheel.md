# Autonomous Driving Data Flywheel

Autonomous driving data flywheel is [[CaoXudong]]'s strategic trunk for [[Momenta]] in [[momenta-ipo-hou-zai-fang-cao-xu-dong-jiu-shi-xiang-zuo-mei-you-jin-tou-de-ai-1-172-1]]. The claim is that autonomous driving cannot be solved only by clever module design: the algorithm architecture has to be data-driven, and the company has to obtain huge physical-world driving data through mass-production deployment.

In Cao's telling, this produced Momenta's "two legs" strategy. One leg is mass-produced assisted driving, which creates customer pressure, revenue, and real-world data. The other leg is more fully driverless Robot applications such as Robo One, Robotruck, and Robotaxi. The flywheel therefore links product delivery, data collection, training, safety improvement, and commercial expansion rather than treating them as separate roadmaps.

The source also makes the concept a bridge to robotics. Cao argues that [[WorldModels]] and physical-world data from driving can support a shared model base for different robot businesses, although the source does not prove that home robots already have the same data scale as cars.

[[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] adds the Huawei scale version through [[HongmengZhixing|鸿蒙智行]] and [[ShenzhenYinwang|引望]]. The episode says Huawei's intelligent-driving installation base is moving toward multi-million-vehicle scale, suggesting that product deployment, assisted-driving data, engineering amortization, and supplier bargaining power could reinforce one another if legal and safety boundaries are handled well.

## Key Claims
- Autonomous-driving progress depends on data scale, data quality, and architecture that can keep absorbing fleet experience.
- Mass production is not only revenue; it is the mechanism that makes the data loop real.
- Supplier concentration can follow from the flywheel because early scale, data, customer programs, and R&D spending reinforce one another.
- A broad automaker supplier platform such as [[ShenzhenYinwang|引望]] can strengthen a flywheel if it expands deployment beyond one brand alliance, but the source keeps installation forecasts source-scoped.
- The flywheel complements [[PhysicalWorldDataFlywheel]] but is narrower: cars can create repeated, sensor-rich, regulated physical-world data before general robots have comparable household deployment.
- Robotaxi, Robotruck, and delivery robots are downstream business routes when the same perception, prediction, planning, validation, and safety infrastructure can be reused.
