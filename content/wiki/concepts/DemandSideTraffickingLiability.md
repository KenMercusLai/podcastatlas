# 拐卖需求端责任 / Demand-Side Trafficking Liability

## Definition
Demand-side trafficking liability is the idea that purchasers and receiving households are part of the trafficking system and should not be treated as morally or legally separate from abductors.

## Current Synthesis
The episode derives this concept from its description of Han law: knowing buyers are punished as participants, and even buyers who claim not to know the victim was abducted still face penalties. The result is a market analysis of trafficking in which legal pressure must reach the demand side, because abduction is sustained by people willing to buy human beings into labor, marriage, lineage, or household roles.

## Key Claims
- Trafficking markets depend on buyers as well as abductors.
- Knowing purchase converts demand into active participation in the crime.
- Claimed ignorance can still be punishable when law expects buyers to bear responsibility.
- Buyer liability is linked to family harm because demand converts missing persons into hidden household arrangements.
- The source frames buyer punishment as deterrence against social complicity.

## Evidence
- Knowing buyers: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-527-2-choujinbapi-gudai-de-renfanzi-zhen-huogai-ljfol4zmcqwewv5h4z4rby5vfw5p]] says people who knowingly bought trafficked persons were treated as similarly culpable.
- Claimed ignorance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-527-2-choujinbapi-gudai-de-renfanzi-zhen-huogai-ljfol4zmcqwewv5h4z4rby5vfw5p]] says buyers claiming ignorance still faced tattooing, hard labor, penal labor, or related punishment.
- Modern bridge: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-527-2-choujinbapi-gudai-de-renfanzi-zhen-huogai-ljfol4zmcqwewv5h4z4rby5vfw5p]] uses [[BlindMountainFilm|《盲山》]] to show forced marriage as a buyer-side endpoint of trafficking.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not compare ancient buyer liability with modern Chinese criminal law or international anti-trafficking standards. Its claim is limited to the episode's moral and historical reading of Han law and modern trafficking narratives.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to capture the source's emphasis on purchasers as part of the trafficking chain.
