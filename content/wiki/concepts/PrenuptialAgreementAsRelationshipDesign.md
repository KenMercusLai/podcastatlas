# Prenuptial Agreement As Relationship Design

## Definition
Prenuptial agreement as relationship design is the source's reframing of prenup negotiation as a structured way to clarify purpose, expectations, assets, liabilities, caregiving sacrifice, and conflict rules before a marriage crisis.

## Current Synthesis
The concept begins from [[MarriageDefaultContract]]: couples already enter a legal regime, so deliberate negotiation can make implicit expectations discussable. In Sexton's account, the useful part is not only the eventual document but the conversation about why the partners are marrying, what each contributes, what "yours, mine, and ours" should mean, and how safety can be protected without making financial dependence the reason someone stays. Separate counsel, disclosure, voluntariness, and jurisdiction-specific enforceability remain essential boundaries.

## Key Claims
- Prenup discussion can surface expectations before fear, resentment, or litigation makes them harder to examine.
- The agreement can distinguish separate property, shared property, assets, liabilities, support, and couple-specific concerns.
- Caregiving and reduced earning power are relationship contributions that may need explicit financial protection.
- Separate lawyers matter because affection does not eliminate potentially divergent legal interests.
- A partner's response to a difficult request provides information about how the couple handles fear and disagreement.
- The conversation may indicate communication skill, but it does not by itself cause marriage durability.

## Evidence
- Purpose-first design: [[contracts-of-love-money-that-make-or-break-relationships-james-sexton-scim2050136218]] says Sexton's first prenup question is why the couple is getting married.
- Structure and scope: [[contracts-of-love-money-that-make-or-break-relationships-james-sexton-scim2050136218]] describes a "yours, mine, and ours" structure and mentions assets, liabilities, alimony, infidelity clauses, pets, and couple-specific terms.
- Safety and caregiving: [[contracts-of-love-money-that-make-or-break-relationships-james-sexton-scim2050136218]] uses reduced earning power and generous exit provision to show how a prenup can reduce fear-based dependence.
- Communication signal: [[contracts-of-love-money-that-make-or-break-relationships-james-sexton-scim2050136218]] connects difficult negotiation with a couple's capacity to disagree before crisis.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Prenups can be coercive, strategically timed, under-disclosed, unaffordable, or unenforceable, and power imbalances do not disappear merely because both parties sign. The source's low divorce count among Sexton's prenup clients may reflect selection rather than causation. Legal validity and appropriate terms require jurisdiction-specific independent advice.
