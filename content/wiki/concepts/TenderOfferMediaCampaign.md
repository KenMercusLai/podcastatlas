# Tender Offer Media Campaign / 收购要约媒体战

Tender offer media campaign / 收购要约媒体战 is the mechanism [[vol-269-xiao-lishi-bu-yao-pa-shi-jishu-xing-tiaozheng-1004793119]] extracts from the [[HongkongLandDairyFarmTakeover|置地饮牛奶]] case. The source says [[HongkongLand|香港置地]] repeatedly used [[SouthChinaMorningPost|南华早报]] advertising to explain its offer, attack [[DairyFarmInternational|牛奶公司]]'s defense, display property assets, and tell shareholders how to accept.

The concept is narrower than ordinary public relations. In a tender battle with paper share certificates and dispersed retail holders, media becomes operational infrastructure: it tells investors what the deal means, where to go, which management team to trust, and how quickly to act.

## Key Claims
- Advertisement frequency, placement, charts, photographs, and procedural instructions can change a takeover's outcome when retail shareholders must act individually.
- A media campaign can frame a share-exchange offer as both financial upside and managerial modernization.
- The side with stronger preparation and communication capacity may win before the target can produce an equally legible counter-story.
