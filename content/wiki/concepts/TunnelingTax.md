# Tunneling Tax / 管窥税

## Definition
Tunneling tax / 管窥税 is the hidden cost paid when scarcity or urgency narrows attention so strongly that immediate relief becomes visible while delayed costs, external risks, and alternative actions disappear from view.

## Current Synthesis
EP87 presents tunneling as the dark side of useful focus. A deadline, hunger, loneliness, money shortage, or unfinished task can pull attention into a narrow channel. Inside that channel, the immediate benefit of scrolling, borrowing, staying up late, buying, accepting another commitment, or skipping a safety step feels unusually large.

The tax appears later. Sleep debt, future workload, loan fees, damaged relationships, missed safety checks, or lost planning time were present all along, but the tunnel made them psychologically unavailable. The episode's strongest warning is that tunneling is not only a small productivity problem: in safety-critical settings, it can become fatal.

## Key Claims
- Tunneling makes the scarce target more salient while suppressing information outside the tunnel.
- Immediate rewards feel larger inside the tunnel because attention amplifies perceived importance.
- The tax is delayed: future time, health, money, safety, and relationship costs become visible after the decision.
- Tunneling can explain self-sabotaging relief behaviors such as late-night scrolling, overwork, impulsive spending, and procrastination.
- Safety-critical systems should design around tunneling instead of relying on individual alertness under urgency.

## Evidence
- Attention narrowing evidence: [[ep87-xique-ruhe-sheji-yige-rang-ziji-bian-congming-de-xitong-gkwrirwoeedlbme1xqtv2orv]] connects tunneling with task-positive focus, small-screen immersion, and narrowed brainstorming associations.
- Immediate-relief evidence: [[ep87-xique-ruhe-sheji-yige-rang-ziji-bian-congming-de-xitong-gkwrirwoeedlbme1xqtv2orv]] uses late-night phone use and "borrowing time from the future" to show how the tunnel foregrounds present relief.
- Attention-amplification evidence: [[ep87-xique-ruhe-sheji-yige-rang-ziji-bian-congming-de-xitong-gkwrirwoeedlbme1xqtv2orv]] uses coin-size perception and personal salience examples to argue that attention enlarges felt importance.
- Safety evidence: [[ep87-xique-ruhe-sheji-yige-rang-ziji-bian-congming-de-xitong-gkwrirwoeedlbme1xqtv2orv]] uses the firefighter seatbelt case to show how urgent mission planning can crowd out a basic safety behavior.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Tunneling is not identical to deep work or concentration. Focus becomes a tax when it blinds a person or system to material costs outside the current target. The firefighter account is a source example, not a complete accident investigation.
