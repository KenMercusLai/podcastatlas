# Mobile Community Fragmentation Risk / 移动社区入口碎片化风险

Mobile Community Fragmentation Risk is the product-strategy problem the source attaches most clearly to [[Douban|豆瓣]]. In [[no-212-cengjing-de-jingshen-jiaoluo-douban-zhihu-tieba-hupu-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-25-1003510980]], Douban entered mobile by splitting functions into many separate apps before launching a strong main app called Douban.

The source treats this as a historically understandable but costly decision. During the early mobile transition, it was not obvious whether the future belonged to super apps or specialized apps. In hindsight, however, fragmentation weakened daily habit formation and made it harder for Douban's PC-era identity to survive the shift into phone-based use.

## Key Claims
- A PC community can lose user routine when mobile entry points divide identity, notifications, and use cases.
- Specialized apps may make sense for functions, but a community also needs a recognizable home.
- Fragmentation is especially risky when the platform's value comes from cross-domain taste and relationship memory rather than one narrow task.
- The concept links product architecture to [[OnlineCommunityCommercializationFriction]] because scattered attention reduces the base from which restrained monetization could work.
