# Post-Defeat Force Reconstitution / 败后兵力重组

## Definition
Post-defeat force reconstitution / 败后兵力重组 is the process of turning a routed or separated organization back into usable capacity through route revision, rear-guard protection, reunion of detached forces, allied reinforcement, and selection of a defensible operating base.

## Current Synthesis
Hanji 1094 defines the pattern through [[LiuBei|刘备]] after [[ChangbanBattle|长坂坡之战]]. The recovery does not erase the defeat: civilians, baggage, family members, and troops are lost. It works because different survival tasks remain connected. [[ZhangFei|张飞]] buys time, [[ZhaoYun|赵云]] preserves family continuity, the route shifts away from blocked Jiangling toward Hanjin, [[GuanYu|关羽]] redirects a separate force, and [[LiuQiLateHan|刘琦]] adds Jiangxia support before the group uses Xiakou as a new base.

The concept is therefore narrower than generic resilience. Belief alone does not reconstitute force; surviving command relationships, geographic alternatives, allied capacity, and a place from which to organize must be assembled into a new operational whole.

## Key Claims
- Defeat recovery begins by preserving time and decision space, not by immediately reversing the battlefield result.
- Route change can be more important than persistence toward an objective the enemy has already blocked.
- Separated commanders and forces become assets only when rendezvous points and movement choices reconnect them.
- Political relationships can supply military mass when the original organization has been scattered.
- A defensible base converts mere survival into a platform for subsequent alliance and operations.

## Evidence
- Time and continuity: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1094-changbanpo-zhizhan-zhangfei-he-zhaoyun-ruhe-zai-zhanchang-fengshen-ltymdohev05pmxhwcdme9d2jymzk|Hanji 1094]] links Zhang Fei's rear guard and Zhao Yun's rescue to preservation during the rout.
- Route and reunion: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1094-changbanpo-zhizhan-zhangfei-he-zhaoyun-ruhe-zai-zhanchang-fengshen-ltymdohev05pmxhwcdme9d2jymzk|Hanji 1094]] says Liu Bei abandons the Jiangling route, moves toward Hanjin, and reconnects with Guan Yu.
- Allied reinforcement and base: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1094-changbanpo-zhizhan-zhangfei-he-zhaoyun-ruhe-zai-zhanchang-fengshen-ltymdohev05pmxhwcdme9d2jymzk|Hanji 1094]] places Liu Qi's Jiangxia force and the Xiakou base inside the renewed capacity before Red Cliffs.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept currently rests on one popular-history source and should not be generalized into a formula. The source's troop totals, distances, route details, command chronology, and causal compression require primary-source checking. Reconstitution also depends on assets that may not survive every defeat: trusted subcommanders, open geography, allies with usable forces, and an unoccupied base.

## What Changed
