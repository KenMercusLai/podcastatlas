## Definition
Story novelty and audience connection is the principle that a culturally important story does more than execute familiar craft well: it makes an emerging social feeling, relationship, fear, or aspiration newly visible to its audience.

## Current Synthesis
[[SongFangjin|宋方金]] argues that audiences first need “new stories,” not merely polished good stories. Newness here is not random premise invention. It comes from noticing changes that have already entered ordinary life but have not yet been given a convincing narrative form.

This criterion explains why production scale alone cannot guarantee relevance and why an aesthetically disputed work may still matter as evidence of a new mood. It also requires method: creators who are not naturally synchronized with their time can study social facts, technologies, relationships, and audience responses without reducing storytelling to trend copying.

## Key Claims
- Narrative novelty can consist of recognizing a new social feeling rather than inventing an unprecedented plot.
- Technical polish and budget cannot replace contact with current life.
- Audience response can reveal an unarticulated need without making popularity a complete quality judgment.
- Creators can build methods for observing change instead of relying only on personal instinct.
- A story's novelty decays when creators repeat past formulas after their social conditions change.

## Evidence
- New-story criterion: [[lt24pcobvqnyxjeven2dwhuhvmfu-lt24pcobvqnyxjeven2dwhuhvmfu]] records Song's statement that people first need new stories and his argument that creators must discover stories already latent in their time.
- Technology example: [[lt24pcobvqnyxjeven2dwhuhvmfu-lt24pcobvqnyxjeven2dwhuhvmfu]] uses Liu Zhenyun's recognition of communication change in 《手机》 as a model of story discovery.
- Method and audience: [[lt24pcobvqnyxjeven2dwhuhvmfu-lt24pcobvqnyxjeven2dwhuhvmfu]] has Song describe deliberate observation of social facts and an internet-connected audience able to expose weak assumptions rapidly.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Novelty is not sufficient for coherence, ethics, craft, or lasting value. Audience success can arise from publicity, distribution, stars, controversy, or timing, and a creator's claim to represent “the times” may be selective. The concept is a discovery criterion, not a license to equate trend visibility with truth.
