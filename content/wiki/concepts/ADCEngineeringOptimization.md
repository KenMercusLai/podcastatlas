# ADC Engineering Optimization

ADC engineering optimization is the episode's frame for antibody-drug conjugates as a combinatorial engineering field. In [[vol-117-shengwu-yiyao-de-2025-chaodi-zhongguo-yanfa-jiaolv-he-xinwang-jiwei-lmhral0rmq6tohiqdwsgmfapnyn7]], [[XiaoPTeacher|小P老师]] breaks ADCs into antibody, payload, and linker, then argues that many combinations can change pharmacology, efficacy, safety, manufacturability, and deal value.

The source says this kind of iterative optimization may fit Chinese biotech strengths in engineering and execution. It does not treat ADCs as a guaranteed revolution: assets can be licensed for large sums and still be stopped later if data, safety, or strategic fit disappoint.

## Key Claims
- ADC value depends on the interaction among antibody targeting, linker behavior, and payload effect.
- China's strength in engineering optimization may help it produce second-wave ADC assets.
- Large licensing deals do not guarantee later success or approval.
- [[DualPayloadADC]] is a more complex extension of the same optimization logic.
