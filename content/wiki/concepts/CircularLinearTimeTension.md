# Circular And Linear Time Tension / 环形时间与线性时间张力

Circular and linear time tension / 环形时间与线性时间张力 is [[shan-shang-shan-xia-jie-renjian-wushi-xiaoyao-ji-shenxian-lhjiguq74flhatdxnbrnzhjg-acp]]'s contrast between agricultural seasonal time and modern industrial progress time. [[LiChuang|李闯]] describes circular time as returning with seasons and work rhythms, while linear time asks today to exceed yesterday and tomorrow to exceed today.

The source treats both forms ambivalently. Circular time can help a person return to the present and bodily rhythm, but it can also bind people to ancestral values and inherited pressure. Linear time can motivate exploration, study, and change, but it also feeds anxiety when life becomes endless comparison with past failure or future improvement.

## Key Claims
- Time structure is a social pressure, not only a private feeling.
- The episode's Daoist and medical frame values returning to daily rhythm, sleep, meals, and recovery without making tradition automatically benign.
- Linear progress narratives can generate anxiety when a person cannot inhabit the present except as a deficit before a future target.
