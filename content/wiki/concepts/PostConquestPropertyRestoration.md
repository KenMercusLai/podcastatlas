# Post-Conquest Property Restoration / 战后民产归还

## Definition
Post-conquest property restoration is the choice to return civilian fields and housing rather than distribute them as rewards to the victorious coalition, treating livelihood recovery as infrastructure for governability.

## Current Synthesis
Hanji 1147 supplies a single case through [[ZhaoYun|赵云]]'s reported advice after [[LiuBei|刘备]] takes Chengdu. The argument joins a moral limit on victory spoils to a practical sequence: civilians who recover homes and fields can settle, support the new order, produce taxable output, and supply recruits. The concept therefore links restraint toward civilians to state capacity without assuming that property restoration alone produces legitimate or durable rule.

## Key Claims
- Victory does not automatically convert civilian land and housing into legitimate rewards for the conquerors.
- Returning productive assets can help war-affected households resume settled life.
- Civilian settlement, political support, taxation, and recruitment form a connected post-conquest system.
- Remonstrance matters because coalition members may have immediate material incentives to distribute captured property.
- Restoration is a governability mechanism as well as a moral claim, but its actual effects require evidence beyond the advice itself.

## Evidence
- Property boundary: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1147-zhongyong-wushuang-hunshen-shidan-zhaozilong-zhuan-lpesotvhdf7zmkqtuv90rdny1kgz|Hanji 1147]] says Zhao Yun opposes distributing Chengdu fields and houses to Liu Bei's followers.
- Livelihood recovery: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1147-zhongyong-wushuang-hunshen-shidan-zhaozilong-zhuan-lpesotvhdf7zmkqtuv90rdny1kgz|Hanji 1147]] frames return of property as a condition for restoring civilian stability after war.
- State-capacity chain: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1147-zhongyong-wushuang-hunshen-shidan-zhaozilong-zhuan-lpesotvhdf7zmkqtuv90rdny1kgz|Hanji 1147]] explicitly connects popular support and settlement to later recruitment and taxation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This is a single source-scoped case from a structured podcast summary. The exact proposal, quotation, legal ownership, scale of contemplated distribution, implementation of restoration, civilian response, fiscal effect, and recruitment effect require verification. Returning property can coexist with coercive conquest, new taxes, elite displacement, or other harms; the concept does not establish a complete post-conquest settlement model.

## What Changed
- Established the concept from the Chengdu property-distribution dispute.
- Connected civilian restitution to a bounded settlement-support-revenue-recruitment mechanism.
