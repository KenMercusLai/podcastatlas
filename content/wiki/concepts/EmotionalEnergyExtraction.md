# Emotional Energy Extraction / 情绪能量掠夺

Emotional energy extraction / 情绪能量掠夺 is [[18-ruhe-shibie-taoli-bing-bimian-chengwei-jingshen-xixuegui-563629000]]'s nonliteral language for the way some relationships consume attention, emotion, and self-trust. The source explicitly rejects treating "energy" as a mysterious electrical substance; it means the psychological resources a person needs for work, happiness, calm, and self-direction.

In the episode, extraction can look like constant complaint, volatile reaction, cold withdrawal after another person's joy, attention capture in public space, repeated crisis, or a conversation that always returns to the extractor's needs and grievances. The result is not just annoyance but a drain that can leave the other person anxious, blank, guilty, or unable to focus.

## Key Claims
- Attention is treated as a limited resource that can be captured or exhausted.
- Healthy relationships involve emotional exchange; extraction is one-sided and recurrent.
- Negative emotion can still feed the pattern because anger, sadness, and desperation keep the victim engaged.
- The source's practical response is to reduce contact, avoid feeding dramatic conflict, and keep attention tied to one's own reality and outside relationships.
