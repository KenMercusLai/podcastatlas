# Allostatic Body Budget

## Definition
Allostatic body budget is [[LisaFeldmanBarrett]]'s practical metaphor for the brain's predictive regulation of bodily resources and for affect as a coarse summary of whether current and anticipated demands are affordable.

## Current Synthesis
The episode describes allostasis as regulation in advance: the brain predicts bodily needs and adjusts energy use, circulation, breathing, water, salt, glucose, oxygen, and other resources before and during action. Affect provides a low-resolution sense of pleasantness, unpleasantness, activation, fatigue, or calm, while an emotion category supplies a more specific account of what those signals mean in context and what to do next.

This distinction creates a practical ordering rule. Before treating a bad feeling as conclusive evidence about the world, check whether sleep loss, hunger, illness, stiffness, inactivity, alcohol, stimulants, drugs, uncertainty, or relational strain may be shaping the substrate. Bodily repair can change affect, but it does not invalidate social reality or replace medical and psychological assessment.

## Key Claims
- Allostasis regulates anticipated bodily needs rather than only correcting deviations after they occur.
- Affect is a coarse summary of bodily state and is not identical to a specific emotion.
- Sleep, food, movement, substances, illness, uncertainty, and relationships can alter affective experience.
- Emotion construction interprets affect through context, concepts, past experience, and action needs.
- Trust, synchrony, kindness, and reliable social support can reduce uncertainty and regulatory cost.
- Basic bodily care can improve regulation without constituting a complete mental-health treatment.

## Evidence
- Predictive regulation - [[how-to-understand-emotions-dr-lisa-feldman-barrett-scim3919749252]] defines allostasis as the brain's anticipatory management of bodily resources.
- Affect distinction - [[how-to-understand-emotions-dr-lisa-feldman-barrett-scim3919749252]] separates broad pleasantness, unpleasantness, arousal, fatigue, and calm from emotion categories.
- Practical foundations - [[how-to-understand-emotions-dr-lisa-feldman-barrett-scim3919749252]] connects affect to sleep, real food, exercise, movement, sunlight, substances, illness, and social connection.
- Social regulation - [[how-to-understand-emotions-dr-lisa-feldman-barrett-scim3919749252]] describes trust, reciprocal help, and interpersonal synchrony as possible body-budget benefits.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
"Body budget" is an explanatory metaphor, not a measurable personal bank balance or a complete causal model. Barrett's depression-as-metabolic-illness claim, medication remarks, synchrony research, and specific bodily examples remain source-scoped. Bodily foundations should not be used to dismiss danger, abuse, grief, psychiatric illness, or persistent symptoms, and they do not replace qualified care.
