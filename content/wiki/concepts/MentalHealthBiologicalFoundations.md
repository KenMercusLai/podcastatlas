# Mental-Health Biological Foundations

## Definition
Mental-health biological foundations are the episode's six-part base of sleep, light and darkness, movement, nutrition, social connection, and stress regulation that supports mood stability without replacing deeper psychological or clinical care.

## Current Synthesis
The source treats the six foundations as a coordinated platform rather than six isolated hacks. Regular sleep and wake timing, brighter days and darker nights, cardiovascular and resistance exercise with recovery, sufficient minimally processed food, attention to regulating or draining relationships, and both real-time and longer-horizon stress skills all influence the biological conditions in which thought and emotion occur.

The key boundary is structural: these practices are necessary but insufficient. A steadier brain-body budget may make emotional states more predictable and create capacity for [[EmotionalGranularity]], reflection, therapy, medication, or trauma work, but routine optimization does not diagnose or cure serious psychiatric illness.

## Key Claims
- Sleep, light-dark exposure, movement, nutrition, social connection, and stress regulation work as an interacting foundation rather than independent cures.
- Regularity and adequate recovery matter alongside total sleep, exercise volume, or food composition.
- Social interactions can consume, preserve, or restore regulatory capacity and therefore belong inside a biological mental-health model.
- Real-time calming and gradual stress-capacity training solve different problems.
- A stronger foundation can increase predictability and capacity for deeper psychological work.
- Foundational practices do not replace qualified care for serious symptoms, trauma, or psychiatric disorders.

## Evidence
- Six-part model - [[mental-health-toolkit-tools-to-bolster-your-mood-mental-health-scim1991900083]] names sleep, light and darkness, movement, nutrition, social connection, and stress control as the core daily supports.
- Interaction and regularity - [[mental-health-toolkit-tools-to-bolster-your-mood-mental-health-scim1991900083]] connects schedule consistency, day-night contrast, exercise plus rest, adequate food, relationship effects, and stress tools to a more predictable neurochemical and autonomic environment.
- Sufficiency boundary - [[mental-health-toolkit-tools-to-bolster-your-mood-mental-health-scim1991900083]] repeatedly distinguishes foundational self-care from psychotherapy, medication, clinically guided psychedelic research, and trauma treatment.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is a broad public-education synthesis, not a comparative trial of the six-part package. Exact sleep duration, sunlight timing, exercise dose, diet, cold exposure, and social needs vary, and several mechanisms and numerical prescriptions are summarized without full methods. Severe depression, mania, psychosis, trauma symptoms, suicidality, medication decisions, and disabling anxiety require qualified assessment rather than lifestyle-only treatment.
