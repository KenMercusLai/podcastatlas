## Definition
Mandatory AI incident investigation is the proposal that serious AI failures should trigger independent access to relevant logs, systems, and decision records rather than leaving scope and disclosure to the company involved.

## Current Synthesis
The concept enters through a reported OpenAI-Hugging Face testing incident and the argument that frontier AI incidents can become public safety events. The first Marketplace Tech source states the accountability demand directly through [[NateSoares]]: outside parties should be able to inspect relevant logs rather than rely on a company-defined review. The follow-up episode with [[MariaCurie|Maria Curi]] shows the same idea moving into mainstream policy attention, where independent review helps turn abstract AI-doom warnings into something lawmakers can discuss.

## Key Claims
- Serious AI incidents require evidence access beyond company-controlled summaries.
- Investigation scope should include relevant logs and adjacent incidents, not only the publicized event.
- Independent review is meant to turn AI safety from voluntary reputation management into accountable public-risk assessment.
- The airplane-crash analogy frames some AI failures as public safety events with broader learning value.
- Independent-review findings can change political salience by giving lawmakers a concrete incident rather than only forecasts or company messaging.

## Evidence
- Scope and logs: [[tech-20260910-tech-pod-128-tech-20260910-tech-pod-128]] records [[NateSoares]] criticizing a narrow investigation and arguing that third parties should be able to examine all relevant logs.
- Public-safety analogy: [[tech-20260910-tech-pod-128-tech-20260910-tech-pod-128]] says Soares compares AI incident review to airplane crash investigations.
- Voluntary-review weakness: [[tech-20260910-tech-pod-128-tech-20260910-tech-pod-128]] links limited third-party scope to the claim that voluntary oversight is inadequate.
- Political salience: [[tech-20260911-tech-pod-128-tech-20260911-tech-pod-128]] says independent auditors found cause for concern and that the review helped create more Washington motivation to act.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The sources do not specify the legal standard, subpoena power, data-protection process, trade-secret boundary, or technical body that would run such investigations. They also summarize concern about the incident without giving the full independent technical record or a detailed company response.
