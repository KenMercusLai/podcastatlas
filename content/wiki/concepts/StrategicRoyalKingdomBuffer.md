## Definition

宗室封国战略缓冲 is the use of a comparatively trusted royal kingdom's territory and military position to constrain stronger or less controllable princely states.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 221 attributes this logic to [[JiaYi|贾谊]] after [[LiuYiLiangKingWesternHan|梁怀王刘揖]] dies without an heir. Rather than abolish the vacant kingdom, Jia Yi proposes a paired geography in which [[LiangKingdomWesternHan|Liang]] checks Qi and Zhao while Huaiyang checks Wu and Chu. The mechanism differs from simple decentralization and from later automatic fragmentation: the center retains selected royal domains because their location can make them instruments of balance. That benefit remains conditional because same-surname kingdoms can themselves accumulate autonomous power.

## Key Claims

- A royal kingdom can be retained for strategic location even when a succession vacancy permits abolition.
- Relative trust and geography matter together; kinship alone does not create a useful buffer.
- The design uses one layer of princely power to contain another rather than immediately replacing all kingdoms with direct administration.
- Present calm does not remove the need for intergenerational risk planning.
- Later battlefield performance can support the plausibility of the design but cannot prove a single-cause policy success.

## Evidence

Paired containment geography:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-221-qiangu-tan-jiasheng-shenme-zaocheng-le-jiayi-de-beiju-ll-7nmh4sxxkc1kvtgrw4xcovsei|Hanji 221]] says Jia Yi assigns Liang a Qi-Zhao containment role and Huaiyang a Wu-Chu containment role.

Implementation and retrospective test:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-221-qiangu-tan-jiasheng-shenme-zaocheng-le-jiayi-de-beiju-ll-7nmh4sxxkc1kvtgrw4xcovsei|Hanji 221]] says Liu Heng moves Liu Wu to Liang and uses Liang's later resistance in the Seven States rebellion as retrospective support.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept currently rests on one episode's rendering of Jia Yi's memorial and later history. A buffer kingdom can become a new center of autonomy, and later Liang success may depend on leadership, resources, and circumstances absent from the original design. The source does not establish the exact borders or military capacity necessary for the proposed balance.

## What Changed

- Created the concept to distinguish geographic buffering from gradual territorial fragmentation.
- Added a source-scoped Liang-Huaiyang pairing and its later retrospective test.
