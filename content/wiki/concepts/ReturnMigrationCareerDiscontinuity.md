# Return-Migration Career Discontinuity

## Definition
Return-migration career discontinuity is the break between experience accumulated abroad and the roles, hiring signals, compensation, and working conditions available after returning to a home labor market.

## Current Synthesis
In [[ep174-yiqing-xia-de-kuaguo-zhilu-ckwrimaeijawabaaaacyyipn|EP174]], [[XiaoyuanReturnee|小元]] expected the removal of U.S. visa and specialization constraints to increase career freedom. Instead, Chinese lateral hiring emphasized experience that could be used immediately, while her model-risk specialization had few direct equivalents. She anticipated lower pay and longer hours, yet the concrete offers and repeated rejection of attempted pivots still produced a larger discontinuity than expected.

The concept distinguishes possessing valuable skills from having those skills recognized as portable. Return can expand geographic, family, or identity fit while reducing occupational continuity. A later role outside risk work may be an intentional reset, but it does not erase the cost of lost seniority, local networks, market vocabulary, or employer legibility.

## Key Claims
- Overseas expertise is not automatically portable when the home market organizes roles differently.
- Lateral hiring can penalize exploratory pivots because employers seek immediately deployable local experience.
- Removing visa constraints may increase formal freedom without increasing the number of credible job matches.
- Pay, working time, occupational identity, family proximity, and cultural fit can move in different directions.
- A career reset can be purposeful even when it follows rejection and involves loss of accumulated position.

## Evidence
- Specialization mismatch: [[ep174-yiqing-xia-de-kuaguo-zhilu-ckwrimaeijawabaaaacyyipn|EP174]] says Xiaoyuan's U.S. model-risk work lacked a direct domestic counterpart.
- Hiring and compensation: [[ep174-yiqing-xia-de-kuaguo-zhilu-ckwrimaeijawabaaaacyyipn|EP174]] records emphasis on ready-to-use experience, unsuccessful transition attempts, lower salary expectations, and longer-hours concerns.
- Reset rather than continuity: [[ep174-yiqing-xia-de-kuaguo-zhilu-ckwrimaeijawabaaaacyyipn|EP174]] describes her eventual choice of a role outside risk work as a new beginning under uncertainty.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The evidence is one guest's account during an unusual pandemic labor market. The source does not identify employers, offers, role definitions, compensation bands, or later outcomes. It therefore supports a mechanism and a case, not a claim that returning professionals generally lose career value.

## What Changed
- Initial synthesis created to separate geographic return from occupational continuity.
