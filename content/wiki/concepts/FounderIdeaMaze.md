## Definition
The founder idea maze is the detailed internal map of a problem space, including plausible routes, dead ends, dependencies, competitors, technical constraints, and decisions that a deeply engaged founder can explain before reality forces further revision.

## Current Synthesis
The idea maze combines preparation with adaptability. Deep detail can reveal years of engagement and reduce avoidable mistakes, but entrepreneurship still unfolds under uncertainty, so a credible map must support pivots rather than become a story for defending the initial plan. It is evidence of domain learning, not proof of truth, integrity, or eventual success.

## Key Claims
- Increasingly detailed questions can expose whether a founder has explored the problem beyond status-level imitation.
- Strong founders pre-plan multiple paths while expecting real customers, technology, markets, and competitors to invalidate parts of the map.
- Course correction is compatible with conviction when the underlying problem remains important and new evidence changes the route.
- Intrinsic motivation helps sustain maze-building before external rewards or consensus appear.
- Detailed narratives remain vulnerable to confidence, hindsight, and storytelling bias and therefore require external tests.

## Evidence
- Depth as a signal: [[how-risk-taking-innovation-artificial-intelligence-transform-human-experience-marc-andreessen-scim7464698870]] records Andreessen's practice of probing founders until either accumulated detail or imitation becomes visible.
- Adaptation under uncertainty: [[how-risk-taking-innovation-artificial-intelligence-transform-human-experience-marc-andreessen-scim7464698870]] compares entrepreneurship to fog of war and argues that extensive preparation must coexist with continual correction.
- Motivation: [[how-risk-taking-innovation-artificial-intelligence-transform-human-experience-marc-andreessen-scim7464698870]] connects the work to intrinsic reward and the idea that the journey itself supplies reinforcement.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Verbal fluency and encyclopedic detail can be mistaken for customer evidence, technical ability, ethical judgment, or execution. Outsiders may have a better route precisely because they have not internalized incumbent assumptions. The source offers an experienced investor's selection heuristic, not a validated interview instrument or guarantee against deception.

## What Changed
- Created the concept as a preparation-and-adaptation model rather than a synonym for founder conviction.
- Added explicit boundaries around narrative confidence and the need for external testing.
