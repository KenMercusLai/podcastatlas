## Definition

奢侈品身份信号效率 / Luxury status signal efficiency is the idea that luxury objects can function as fast social signals, helping observers infer class position, resources, taste, seriousness, or expected cooperation value before deeper interaction.

## Current Synthesis

The source develops this concept after the diamond and romance discussion. [[KuaigeStandup|快哥]] says luxury goods and famous watches have little use in his stand-up club context because performers may not recognize or reward those signals. [[LuoYonghao|罗永浩]] separates practical product quality from luxury signaling by noting that a travel suitcase should still be evaluated by handle rigidity and wheel smoothness.

The panel nevertheless acknowledges that luxury symbols can have communication efficiency in some business settings. A recognizable watch, bag, or other object can make a person easier to classify and therefore easier to treat according to expected status or purchasing power. The concept is morally mixed: signaling can reduce friction, but it also reinforces [[SnobberyAsValueRanking|snobbery]] and [[SocialComparisonPressure|social comparison]] by making hierarchy legible through objects.

## Key Claims

- Luxury goods can work as quick classification devices in status-sensitive settings.
- Signal value depends on audience literacy; a famous watch does little if the relevant audience does not recognize it.
- Practical quality and status signaling should be separated because a costly object may be useful, symbolic, both, or neither.
- Business-status signals can improve interaction efficiency while also reproducing unequal treatment.
- The same object may be vanity, identity, memory, hobby, practical tool, or communication shortcut depending on context.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source does not claim luxury signals work everywhere or for everyone. It repeatedly qualifies signal value by scene, audience knowledge, and practical product function.
