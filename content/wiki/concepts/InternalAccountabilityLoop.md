# Internal Accountability Loop

## Definition
Internal accountability loop is a self-directed action cycle in which a person records a specific commitment, protects attention, completes a bounded task, marks it done, and treats fulfilled effort as evidence and reward.

## Current Synthesis
The AMA treats motivation less as a feeling to await than as a loop that can be made visible. Writing the goal, using a checkmark or signed self-contract, and defining a concrete action reduce ambiguity. Removing the phone or placing it in another room protects the action from easier rewards. Completion then becomes the feedback signal: the person did what they promised and can use that result to support the next commitment.

External support can still matter, especially when it creates real accountability. The qualification is that public announcement or praise can sometimes simulate progress without producing action. The useful distinction is therefore not internal good, external bad; it is whether the accountability arrangement reliably closes the loop between promise and behavior.

## Key Claims
- Written commitments reduce ambiguity and make completion inspectable.
- A small bounded action is more useful than waiting for motivation to feel sufficient.
- Phone removal and distraction control protect the commitment at the moment of action.
- Marking completion creates feedback and evidence of self-trust.
- Effort itself can become rewarding instead of depending only on praise or outcome.
- External accountability helps when it changes follow-through, but public announcement alone can substitute for action.

## Evidence

- Attention protection: [[ama-18-cold-therapy-advice-skin-health-tips-motivation-learning-strategies-more-scim1100391235]] repeatedly recommends turning off or physically removing the phone during difficult work.
- Closed-loop reward: [[ama-18-cold-therapy-advice-skin-health-tips-motivation-learning-strategies-more-scim1100391235]] treats promised action, completion, sign-off, and the work itself as the reinforcing sequence.
- Social qualification: [[ama-18-cold-therapy-advice-skin-health-tips-motivation-learning-strategies-more-scim1100391235]] distinguishes genuine accountability from support or announcement that permits indefinite delay.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source offers a practical motivational heuristic, not a tested treatment for depression, ADHD, addiction, severe fatigue, trauma, or neurological apathy. Self-contracts can become punitive when tasks are unrealistic or health constraints are ignored. External support, treatment, accommodations, and collaborative accountability may be essential rather than optional.

## What Changed
- Created a closed-loop model joining written commitment, distraction control, completion, and self-reward.
- Preserved a role for external accountability while separating it from announcement-only motivation.
