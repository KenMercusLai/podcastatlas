# Shield-State Rescue Failure / 屏障国救援失灵

Shield-state rescue failure / 屏障国救援失灵 is the late-stage alliance failure [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-2-lvqctrazn8ddkcnp-lhspzzmzmns]] builds from [[QiState|齐国]]'s refusal to lend grain to [[ZhaoState|赵国]] during [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]]. [[ZhouziQiMinister|周子]] warns that Zhao is a shield for Qi and [[ChuState|楚国]], and that if Zhao falls the danger will soon reach them. The warning fails.

The concept is distinct from [[DelayedRescueStatecraft|晚救权谋]]. In delayed rescue, Qi privately commits to help later and uses timing to improve leverage. In shield-state rescue failure, the threatened state receives neither timely intervention nor grain relief, even though its survival protects others from the common enemy.

It extends [[CoalitionSelfPreservationFailure|联盟自保失灵]] beyond a coalition army already in the field. Here the failure happens while no effective rescue coalition forms: Han and Wei are constrained by Qin, Chu is cautious after earlier damage, Yan is focused on Qi, and Qi's peace-oriented court refuses the food request that could keep Zhao's army alive.

## Key Claims
- A state can be strategically valuable as a buffer even when it is also a rival.
- Refusing small relief, such as grain, can be as consequential as refusing troops if the endangered army is already encircled.
- Short-term avoidance can make sense locally while damaging the larger anti-Qin balance.
- Alliance failure can be passive: states need not betray a treaty openly to let a shield state collapse.
- Moral and strategic arguments can align, as in Zhouzi's warning, and still lose to fear, resentment, or near-term calculation.
