# Antisemitism Israel Criticism Boundary

## Definition
The antisemitism-Israel criticism boundary is the distinction between legitimate criticism of Israeli government policy and identity-based blame or dual-loyalty claims directed at Jews.

## Current Synthesis
In the source, Shapiro insists that antisemitism and Israel policy should be treated as related but separate conversations. He criticizes Netanyahu and supports a two-state direction while warning that holding Jews collectively responsible for Israeli government decisions invokes an antisemitic dual-loyalty trope. The concept protects two claims at once: people must be free to protest and criticize governments, and bigotry should be called out consistently across left and right.

## Key Claims
- Criticism of Israel, Netanyahu, Gaza policy, Iran policy, or U.S. policy is not automatically antisemitism.
- Blaming Jews collectively for Israeli state action crosses into antisemitic dual-loyalty logic.
- Antisemitism appears on both the left and the right and should be called out consistently.
- Support for Israel's existence can coexist with criticism of Netanyahu's government.
- A two-state direction is presented as compatible with opposing Hamas rule over a Palestinian state.
