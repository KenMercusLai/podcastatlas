# Parental Sacrifice Return Claim / 父母付出回报索取

## Definition

Parental sacrifice return claim is the conversion of real or claimed investment in a child into an open-ended entitlement to the adult child's obedience, money, time, emotional reassurance, or gratitude.

## Current Synthesis

[[ep389-wo-jia-na-guinu-zhang-jiaqi-dai-luwa-dongya-qinzi-guanxi-weihe-zong-chongman-shangtong-he-yihan-gkwridoopvw0aeyu2wtodgpk]] separates parental effort from a right of collection. [[DaiLuwa|戴露娃]]'s mother can have performed exhausting care work and financed acting-school preparation while Dai's reports of hitting and emotional dismissal remain real. [[ZhangJiaqiDiver|张家齐]] can recognize that her mother brought her to activities without accepting the stronger claim that her athletic career, earnings, or adult decisions belong to the parent.

The asymmetry of child-rearing matters: a parent may give everything available, yet the child cannot promise an equal future return. Gratitude can be meaningful when freely felt and specifically expressed. It becomes control when sacrifice is repeatedly invoked to cancel boundaries, transfer ownership of success, or demand repayment through compliance.

Hanji 1149 adds an emotional-repayment form: a parent gives up work, place, or personal direction and makes the child's achievement the meaning of that sacrifice. The child may then feel unable to fail or enjoy life freely because both outcomes appear to waste or betray the parent's investment. This extends the return claim beyond money and obedience into the child's emotional permission to live independently.

## Key Claims

- Genuine parental sacrifice and genuine harm can coexist.
- Raising a child is structurally asymmetric and does not create a mathematically equal future debt.
- A return claim becomes controlling when it overrides the adult child's financial, relational, or emotional boundaries.
- Narrating a child's success as the parent's investment return can erase the child's labor and agency.
- Gratitude remains ethically distinct from compulsory obedience or unlimited access.
- A child can experience ordinary failure or happiness as forbidden when parental sacrifice becomes a total-life debt.

## Evidence

### Material support without moral cancellation

- [[ep389-wo-jia-na-guinu-zhang-jiaqi-dai-luwa-dongya-qinzi-guanxi-weihe-zong-chongman-shangtong-he-yihan-gkwridoopvw0aeyu2wtodgpk]] holds Dai Luwa's mother's demanding paid work and education spending together with Dai's reports of physical and emotional harm.

### Success, money, and adult autonomy

- [[ep389-wo-jia-na-guinu-zhang-jiaqi-dai-luwa-dongya-qinzi-guanxi-weihe-zong-chongman-shangtong-he-yihan-gkwridoopvw0aeyu2wtodgpk]] reports Zhang Jiaqi disputing her mother's ownership-like account of the diving path and describes parental access to her bank information and resistance to her financial management.

### Unequal return capacity

- [[ep389-wo-jia-na-guinu-zhang-jiaqi-dai-luwa-dongya-qinzi-guanxi-weihe-zong-chongman-shangtong-he-yihan-gkwridoopvw0aeyu2wtodgpk]] argues that total parental effort does not imply the child can or must provide an equal return, and that expected repayment can become a reason to reconsider parenthood.

### Emotional repayment and permission to live

- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1149-jiemi-sanguo-dingji-moushi-xunyou-de-guanchang-shengcunshu-ljv1fwephe86-qczfy0o1n2yh7um|Hanji 1149]] reports an anecdotal student who feels unable to fail or be happy because his mother's decade of sacrifice has been placed on his success.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept does not deny ordinary reciprocity, negotiated family care, legal duties, gratitude, or the reality that parents may bear severe material costs. It identifies the shift from bounded responsibility to indefinite entitlement. EP389 does not establish the full financial arrangements in either family, and allegations that Zhang Jiaqi's earnings were depleted remain unverified. Hanji 1149's student story is anecdotal and does not establish a clinical cause of depression. A parent's request is not automatically coercive; behavior, alternatives, power, and consequences for refusal matter.

## What Changed

- Established a distinction between honoring parental sacrifice and treating it as ownership of an adult child's life or resources.
- Extended the return claim from obedience and resources into emotional permission to fail, enjoy life, and choose independently.
