# Written Succession Credential / 书面继承凭证

Written succession credential / 书面继承凭证 is the concept [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-97-4-shishang-wei-shiye-song-laopo-diyi-ren-lo8omg01gkwlojkpiflaep99x-vz]] draws from the jade tally in [[QinZhuangxiangwang|异人 / 子楚]]'s adoption by [[HuayangFuren|华阳夫人]]. The episode argues that the tally is not a decorative detail: it turns a court-family arrangement into a recorded, materialized claim that Huayang Furen can use to protect her future position.

The mechanism connects family politics to legitimacy. [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]]'s strategy depends on [[MaternalStatusSuccessionLeverage|子以母贵式继承杠杆]], but adoption alone might remain vulnerable if later denied or reframed. A written credential gives the arrangement public and legal weight, so Yiren's future authority becomes tied to acknowledging Huayang Furen as the maternal source of his legitimate succession status.

This concept is narrower than generic [[MandateOfHeavenLegitimacy|天命合法性]]. It is not about Heaven approving a ruler; it is about documentation binding a future ruler to the path by which he became legitimate. In the source's reading, ordinary people around a kingly system have limited ways to constrain a future ruler, but written, witnessed credentials can make betrayal politically costly.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-127-2-lisi-weisha-bu-bang-yingming-de-fusu-lq-vny5duwwzfubpeijr21oazxbg]] adds the inverse case. After [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]] dies on tour, [[ZhaoGao|赵高]] tells [[LiSi|李斯]] that the letter to [[FuSu|扶苏]] and the imperial seals are in [[QinErshi|胡亥]]'s hands, so the small inner circle can decide who will be named heir. Here the credential no longer binds a legitimate prior arrangement; it becomes the physical surface that enables a [[ForgedEdictSuccessionCoup|伪诏继位政变]].

## Key Claims
- Succession politics can depend on material proof, not only favor, affection, or oral promise.
- A credential can protect the patron as much as the heir by recording the exchange that creates legitimacy.
- Written proof matters most when a future ruler may later want to erase the dependency that elevated him.
- The concept belongs near command credentials such as [[TigerTallyCommandSystem|虎符调兵制度]], but its function is legitimacy binding rather than military authentication.
- A credential can also become dangerous when the issuing ruler is dead and a small group controls the document, seal, and announcement timing.
