## Definition
Character-based romantic compatibility is the assessment of a relationship through demonstrated values, recurring conduct, life direction, money and stability preferences, appetite for adventure, humor, and capacity for continued growth rather than attraction or surface similarity alone.

## Current Synthesis
[[RobertGreene]] separates admiring a trait from being able to build a life with its owner. Shared taste can support connection, and physical attraction remains relevant, but durable compatibility becomes visible in choices: how each person treats animals and other people, handles money and comfort, approaches career and risk, and behaves when the social audience changes.

The framework also resists a static checklist. Mystery, humor, rebelliousness, music, and ongoing evolution matter because a long relationship is lived with a changing person. Compatibility is therefore neither pure sameness nor perfect prediction; it is a revisable judgment about whether two patterns of character and direction can sustain mutual life.

Compatibility can also be tested under scarcity. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-286-canbei-wujie-qiannian-zhumaichen-xiuqi-de-fanzhuan-zhenxiang-1-lpvzvzomh6u6zbr1ispaiycenl3f|Hanji 286 part 1]], [[CuiShiZhuMaichenWife|崔氏]] has endured poverty with [[ZhuMaichenWesternHan|朱买臣]], but they assign opposite meanings to his reading and recitation while gathering firewood: he treats learning and future advancement as credible direction, while she sees inadequate provision and humiliating impracticality. The source's modern interpretation is that prolonged endurance does not settle whether a couple can continue sharing work, responsibility, and a believable future.

The [[XiakeXingNovel|《侠客行》]] episode adds a direct challenge to additive ranking. [[MeiFanggu|梅芳姑]] may surpass [[MinRou|闵柔]] across beauty, martial ability, literature, domestic skill, and cooking, yet [[ShiQingXiakeXing|石清]] consistently chooses Min Rou. The case does not deny accomplishment; it shows that no trait total creates entitlement to another person's love. Temperament, boundaries, shared history, core values, and the experience of enduring grief together are relationship evidence that a comparison table misses.

## Key Claims
- Attraction and admiration do not by themselves establish compatibility.
- Values become more credible when they appear in repeated behavior across contexts.
- Money, comfort, stability, adventure, career, animals, and treatment of others can expose consequential differences.
- Surface preferences and physical attraction can support rapport without resolving deeper conflicts in character or direction.
- Continuing growth and some mystery can help a relationship remain alive rather than frozen around an initial image.
- Material pressure can reveal incompatibility in how partners interpret work, responsibility, dignity, and future promises.
- Comparative excellence cannot compel reciprocal love or substitute for temperament, boundaries, value alignment, and lived ease.

## Evidence
- Value domains: [[a-process-for-finding-achieving-your-unique-purpose-robert-greene-scim1811421687]] names animals, money, comfort, stability, adventure, and career as revealing compatibility tests.
- Contextual conduct: [[a-process-for-finding-achieving-your-unique-purpose-robert-greene-scim1811421687]] recommends observing a partner around other people because a managed mask may weaken across settings.
- Attraction boundary: [[a-process-for-finding-achieving-your-unique-purpose-robert-greene-scim1811421687]] retains physical attraction while placing lasting relationship quality on deeper convergence.
- Dynamic character: [[a-process-for-finding-achieving-your-unique-purpose-robert-greene-scim1811421687]] adds mystery, evolution, music, rebelliousness, and humor as clues to character and continued interest.
- Scarcity and divergent futures: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-286-canbei-wujie-qiannian-zhumaichen-xiuqi-de-fanzhuan-zhenxiang-1-lpvzvzomh6u6zbr1ispaiycenl3f|Hanji 286 part 1]] interprets Zhu and Cui's separation through opposed judgments about labor, learning, provision, dignity, and promised advancement.
- Ranked accomplishment and reciprocal choice: [[lovpnvggvmrtfzpn9gvezhrg6xfo]] contrasts Mei Fanggu's many visible advantages with Shi Qing's consistent boundary and his shared temperament, history, and values with Min Rou.
- Compatibility under grief: [[lovpnvggvmrtfzpn9gvezhrg6xfo]] treats Shi Qing and Min Rou's refusal to blame each other after child loss as evidence of durable partnership rather than passive sameness.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
These episodes do not establish a universal compatibility test or prove which traits predict relationship durability. Apparent value convergence can change over time, and observing behavior does not eliminate projection, power imbalance, attachment dynamics, or self-presentation. The Zhu-Cui case is a modern interpretation of an incomplete historical retelling, while the Shi-Mei-Min case is a conversational reading of fiction; neither can recover private motive or make one pairing universally correct. Relationship safety, consent, direct conversation, and responsiveness to conflict remain more important than romanticizing mystery, suffering, or future promises.

## What Changed
- Added scarcity, household labor, dignity, and confidence in a shared future as compatibility tests.
- Added a boundary between prolonged endurance and continuing fit.
- Added reciprocal choice and the failure of comparative excellence to create romantic entitlement.
- Added shared grief response as evidence about compatibility under pressure.
