## Definition

人生定位与适配 / career positioning through fit is the judgment pattern where effort becomes more effective when personal desire, capacity, temperament, inherited resources, and institutional role are aligned before major life or career commitments are made.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 757 creates this concept through the host's reading of [[GuoGongEasternHanJurist|Guo Gong]]. Guo Gong's legal career is not presented as accidental ambition. His father worked in law, he learned legal judgment from childhood, and later descendants continued the family expertise. The source uses that pattern to argue that some paths become easier and more productive when existing resources and personal disposition already point in that direction.

The concept is also a warning against misdirected effort. The episode says wrong positioning consumes time and energy in places where the person may lack fit, causing confusion and internal competition without useful accumulation. Its phrase "尺有所短，寸有所长" makes fit relational: strength depends on matching the task, not on ranking people in the abstract.

## Key Claims

- Effort depends on direction; more effort in a badly matched role can increase waste rather than output.
- Family resources and inherited training can be legitimate advantages when they match temperament and actual ability.
- Professional expertise is cumulative, so early exposure and practice can make a specialized role more realistic.
- Fit is not superiority; it is matching strengths and limits to a specific role and situation.
- The source's career lesson is interpretive life advice, not a deterministic rule that everyone should inherit a family path.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

This concept is bounded to Hanji 757's interpretive frame. The source does not prove that inherited paths are always best, and it does not analyze cases where family expectation suppresses a better individual fit. Its stronger claim is narrower: existing resources help only when they align with capacity, interest, temperament, and role demand.
