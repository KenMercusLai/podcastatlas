# Housing Stigma Discount

Housing stigma discount is the price reduction attached to homes that buyers or tenants avoid for social, emotional, ritual, or reputational reasons. In [[kyiv-brawl-ukraines-top-general-fired-6a609aec4fac21e67f1be165]], [[JikobukkenIncidentProperty|jiko bukken]] in [[Japan]] can rent or sell for 10% to 50% below normal value depending on the perceived severity of the previous death.

The concept extends [[HousingAsConsumptionGood]] by adding non-physical utility and discomfort. A unit can have the same location, structure, and rent level as comparable housing, while its story changes who will live there and at what price.

## Key Claims
- Stigma can function like a market discount even when the physical property is unchanged.
- The discount varies by narrative severity: natural deaths are treated differently from suicide or murder in the source.
- Reassurance businesses such as [[Kachimodo]] can try to narrow the discount by making fear auditable.
- Disclosure can protect tenants and buyers, but it can also make stigma durable and searchable.
- Housing-cost pressure can convert taboo into affordability tradeoff.
