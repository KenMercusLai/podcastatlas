# Retail Format Substitution Pressure

## Definition

Retail format substitution pressure occurs when different operating systems satisfy the same customer job closely enough that a new format must prove incremental value rather than merely reproduce access to products.

## Current Synthesis

The 7-Eleven India case shows a convenience chain squeezed between two substitutes. [[KiranaRetail|Kirana stores]] offer nearby, low-cost, relationship-based access, while [[InstantRetail]] offers broad selection and delivery without a store visit. Each format defines convenience differently, but both reduce the white space available to an imported convenience-store chain.

The competitive test is therefore job-level differentiation. Fresh food, private label, reliability, experience, price, or a distinctive occasion must create a reason to switch or add visits. Opening an online channel does not solve the problem if the underlying assortment and economics remain undifferentiated.

## Key Claims

- Formats compete through customer jobs, not only through formal category labels.
- A low-cost local incumbent and a technology platform can pressure the same entrant from different directions.
- Product differentiation must be strong enough to change repeat behavior, not only attract trial.
- Adding delivery cannot compensate automatically for weak assortment or store economics.
- Existing local density can matter as much as the entrant's global scale.

## Evidence

- Local substitute: [[gkwridoovnyyahvaqqtzrpda-gkwridoovnyyahvaqqtzrpda]] describes millions of kirana stores serving frequent neighborhood purchases.
- Digital substitute: [[gkwridoovnyyahvaqqtzrpda-gkwridoovnyyahvaqqtzrpda]] reports a multibillion-dollar Indian quick-commerce market plus fast-delivery efforts by large ecommerce firms.
- Entrant outcome: [[gkwridoovnyyahvaqqtzrpda-gkwridoovnyyahvaqqtzrpda]] says 7-Eleven's localized products did not generate sufficient demand and that the chain closed all Indian stores.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source does not isolate how much of the closure came from substitution versus site choice, partner decisions, capital allocation, assortment, execution, or macroeconomic conditions. Formats can also complement one another, and the source notes that traditional stores themselves are adopting digital ordering and delivery.
