# Public–Private Record Gap / 公共记录与私人经验落差

## Definition
The public–private record gap is the divergence between institutionally visible accounts of a person or event and experiences known within intimate, domestic, or otherwise concealed settings.

## Current Synthesis
In [[TheBlindAssassinNovel|《盲刺客》]], newspaper notices grant Richard the legibility of a successful businessman and political figure while failing to register control, sexual abuse, and fear inside his household. The reports need not be fabricated in every detail to be radically incomplete. Their authority comes from public visibility, conventional relevance, and access to reputable speakers, all of which can exclude the people most harmed.

Private memory is not automatically complete or neutral either. [[IrisChase|Iris]] understands Laura belatedly, narrates after irreversible loss, and has her own secrecy and responsibility. The concept therefore does not replace a false public account with an infallible private one. It requires comparison among records, positions, silences, incentives, and the conditions under which an experience could be stated.

## Key Claims
- Public documentation can be factually selective while appearing objective and complete.
- Reputation and institutional status influence whose version becomes recordable.
- Domestic violence may remain invisible when harmed people lack safety, access, or credibility.
- Private testimony can recover excluded experience without becoming automatically exhaustive.
- Disagreement among records should prompt source-position analysis rather than reflexive trust in either publicity or intimacy.
- Fiction can stage the gap by placing documents and recollection in direct structural tension.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This concept comes from a fictional case interpreted in a podcast. It should not imply that private allegations require no corroboration or that every public record is primarily reputational concealment. Evidence standards, safety, privacy, and legal process remain domain-specific, and later memory can also be selective or mistaken.

## What Changed
- Created a record-comparison framework that preserves both public incompleteness and private-testimony fallibility.
