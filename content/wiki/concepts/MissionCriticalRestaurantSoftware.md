# Mission-Critical Restaurant Software

Mission-critical restaurant software is software whose failure immediately disrupts live service, payments, kitchen execution, or customer trust. [[toast-aman-narang-how-a-long-wait-for-the-dinner-check-launched-a-2-billion-business-6eca81bc-b0e1-47e2-a87d-f3b243e76a97]] adds the concept through [[Toast]]'s first customer install at [[DwellTime]].

The source says the system went down within about 20 minutes of taking the first few orders. [[AmanNarang]] describes the team falling back to paper orders and written card numbers, which makes the concept more severe than ordinary SaaS downtime: a broken restaurant system interrupts a physical, time-sensitive service operation.

## Key Claims
- Restaurant software reliability has to be judged against dinner-rush conditions, not only normal application uptime.
- Support, hardware handling, training, and fallback procedures are part of the product because restaurant staff cannot pause the service window.
- Switching vendors requires trust because a bad install can damage the restaurant's revenue and customer experience immediately.
- Founder-led hustle can rescue early failures but cannot substitute for scalable reliability and support systems.
- The concept connects restaurant technology to [[TrustHeavyInfrastructureSales]] even when the buyer is a small business rather than a large enterprise.
