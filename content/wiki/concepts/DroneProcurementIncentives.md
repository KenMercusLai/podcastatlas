# Drone Procurement Incentives

Drone procurement incentives are the military-adaptation mechanism attributed to [[MykhailoFedorov]] in [[kyiv-brawl-ukraines-top-general-fired-6a609aec4fac21e67f1be165]]. The episode says Fedorov changed procurement so effective brigades could receive more drones and ammunition, connecting battlefield performance to resource allocation.

The concept extends [[LowCostDroneWarfare]] from hardware economics into organizational design. Cheap drones matter less if procurement remains centralized, slow, or insensitive to which units actually use them well.

## Key Claims
- Drone scale-up requires supply, ammunition, operators, and authority to reward effective use.
- Incentive design can let innovative brigades compound advantage instead of being starved by rigid allocation rules.
- Procurement reform can threaten older command systems because it shifts power toward units that demonstrate battlefield effectiveness.
- A drone-heavy war effort needs rapid feedback between frontline performance and resupply.
- The concept is limited by air-defense and missile constraints that drone procurement alone cannot solve.
