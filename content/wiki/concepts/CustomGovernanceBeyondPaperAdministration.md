## Definition

风俗治理与文书行政 / custom governance beyond paper administration is the source-scoped distinction between processing documents and cases after problems appear and shaping the shared role expectations, kinship duties, and moral judgments that influence conduct beforehand.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-218-jiayi-weihe-bei-chengwei-qiangu-diyi-zhengzhi-qicai-2-lgeecnyc_wqgig49ftcsnztsnwag|Hanji 218 part 2]] attributes the distinction to [[JiaYi|贾谊]]'s [[ZhianCe|《治安策》]]. Officials are criticized for treating document handling as the whole of government while accepting deteriorating custom, profit-only judgment, and violence within families as ordinary social facts.

The positive program is institutional rather than merely exhortative. Ruler and official, superior and subordinate, parent and child, siblings, spouses, and other kin are to have legible duties and relative positions. Repetition across generations is expected to make those duties a stable background for conduct, much as [[RitualPreventionAndLegalResponse|ritual prevention]] is meant to act before legal judgment becomes necessary.

The distinction does not imply that paperwork, adjudication, or formal rules are useless. It says administrative throughput is an incomplete measure of state capacity when the conditions producing cases remain untreated. The episode's sinking-boat analogy makes timing central: institutions built only after social trust and role order fail may arrive too late for the crisis they are supposed to manage.

## Key Claims

- Administrative throughput is not equivalent to successful governance when custom and ordinary conduct are deteriorating.
- Officials have an upstream responsibility to notice and address the social conditions that repeatedly generate cases.
- Role clarity can function as political infrastructure by making duties and expectations legible before enforcement.
- Family and political order are linked in the source because both rely on patterned reciprocal obligations.
- Profit-centered judgment can weaken nonmarket duties when advancement becomes the dominant measure of action.
- Preventive institution building must occur before crisis; calm conditions do not demonstrate that an unbuilt system is resilient.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The current evidence inventory contains one compact structured episode summary rather than a primary-text comparison, administrative dataset, or observed policy evaluation. “Custom” can conceal hierarchy, suppress dissent, or place unequal duties on family members as well as support cooperation; this source does not test those costs. Document processing can also protect consistency, reviewability, and legal rights, so the concept should not be read as an argument against records or procedure. The causal claim that Shang Yang's material incentives produced Qin's family customs and short rule remains episode-attributed and cannot isolate alternative causes.

## What Changed

- Created the concept from Hanji 218 part 2's explicit contrast between document handling and transformation of custom.
- Preserved formal administration as necessary but insufficient rather than treating it as inherently harmful.
- Made role hierarchy and family order explicit as benefits claimed by the source and as sites of untested coercive risk.
