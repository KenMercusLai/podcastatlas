# Spring-Autumn to Warring States Political Violence / 春秋战国政治暴力转型

Spring-Autumn to Warring States political violence / 春秋战国政治暴力转型 is the episode's frame for a change in elite conflict. In [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-1-lvkp-ju7pf5z5cz3thojv3s2fkyx]], the host contrasts older aristocratic politics, where defeated heirs might be exiled or allowed descendants, with a harsher Warring States mode where factions kill rulers, ministers, and rival lineages to seize power.

The main source case is [[ZhengState|郑国]]: the ruler kills [[ZiyangZheng|子阳]], Ziyang's faction later kills the ruler, and [[ZhengKangGong|郑康公]] is installed. The concept extends [[SpringAutumnWarfareRitual]] from interstate combat norms into court politics: rules and status codes still exist, but they are losing force.

## Key Claims
- The breakdown of ritual constraint changes internal politics as much as interstate war.
- Exile, lineage preservation, and negotiated displacement become less reliable once factions learn that total elimination can work.
- Assassination can operate as state-capture technique, not only as revenge or moral protest.
- The concept is historical-institutional; it complements but is distinct from [[PoliticalAssassinationEthics]], which asks when political killing can seem morally intelligible.
