## Definition

Speculative rule consistency / 幻想设定的规则一致性 is the standard that asks whether an unreal premise remains governed by intelligible constraints and consequences, rather than whether the premise could exist in ordinary reality.

## Current Synthesis

The episode separates accepting Titans as a starting premise from accepting every later ability or survival device. A transformation triggered by injury can work if it becomes part of stable dramatic grammar; Founder control, memory alteration, consciousness transfer, or survival after decapitation invite stronger scrutiny when their scope appears late, solves an immediate plot problem, or weakens previously important political mechanisms.

Consistency is not literal realism. Rules may expand, earlier clues may be reinterpreted, and revelation can legitimately change the audience's model. The question is whether new information is prepared, bounded, and paid for through consequences. Early Wall Titans and the eventual Rumbling support long-range planning, while some late biological and Founder mechanics feel to the hosts like compressed repair rather than earned disclosure.

The standard also remains viewer-relative. One audience member may value scientific plausibility, another character expression or visual force. That difference should not become a hierarchy of intelligence. Criticism becomes stronger when it identifies which rule, prior expectation, consequence, or thematic function is affected instead of treating mere surprise as incoherence.

## Key Claims

- Unreal premises do not need ordinary-world plausibility, but they do need sufficiently stable internal constraints.
- A later revelation is stronger when earlier details support it and its scope remains bounded.
- A power that solves immediate plot problems can weaken political or thematic stakes if it bypasses prior mechanisms.
- Planned destination and compressed execution can coexist in the same work.
- Viewer disagreement may reflect different weighting of realism, spectacle, character, and theme rather than unequal competence.
- Criticism should name the violated expectation and consequence instead of equating surprise with contradiction.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The supplied document summarizes a conversational recollection rather than checking every rule against the complete manga and anime. Apparent inconsistency may result from omitted explanation, adaptation differences, translation, memory, or deliberate uncertainty. Conversely, foreshadowing a destination does not establish that every mechanism used to reach it is coherent. This concept assesses narrative construction, not scientific possibility.

## What Changed

- Created a standard separating speculative premise acceptance from internal rule evaluation.
- Distinguished planned destination from the coherence and pacing of late mechanisms.
- Added viewer-priority differences as a qualification on claims of obvious irrationality.
