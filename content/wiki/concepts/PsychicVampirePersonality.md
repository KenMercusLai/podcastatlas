# Psychic Vampire Personality / 精神吸血鬼人格

Psychic vampire personality / 精神吸血鬼人格 is the relationship pattern named by [[18-ruhe-shibie-taoli-bing-bimian-chengwei-jingshen-xixuegui-563629000]] through [[JingshenXixueguiBook|《精神吸血鬼》]]. The source uses it as a metaphor for people who seem unable to sustain themselves emotionally without pulling attention, reassurance, labor, guilt, or self-doubt from others.

The episode keeps the label broader than intimate abuse but narrower than "any difficult person." Psychic vampires can appear in friendship, family, work, and romantic relationships; they may be paranoid, manipulative, guilt-making, dependent, or apparently fragile. The shared sign is the relational effect: the other person becomes tired, anxious, confused, isolated, self-blaming, and less able to trust their own perception.

## Key Claims
- The pattern is diagnosed through repeated relationship effects, not a single unpleasant behavior.
- Charm can be part of the pattern because early intensity and apparent understanding make later exhaustion harder to name.
- Lack of visible malice does not remove harm; the source separates psychic vampires from sadists while still centering the victim's real depletion.
- The safest response is usually recognition, distance, evidence, outside connection, and firm boundaries rather than attempted rescue.
- The concept should not become a universal insult; the episode explicitly says not every harmful act or conflict should be reduced to this label.
