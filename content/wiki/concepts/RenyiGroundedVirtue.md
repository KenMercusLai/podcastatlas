# Renyi-Grounded Virtue / 仁义为本的信勇

Renyi-grounded virtue / 仁义为本的信勇 is the standard extracted from [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-1-chaijie-jingke-ciqin-bi-bai-de-mimi-lnonhw9tlsu5dudu8750zvgeaz1p]]. The episode follows [[SimaGuang|司马光]] in denying that outward displays automatically count as virtue: crouching and submission are not necessarily true respect, keeping a promise is not necessarily true trust, lavish giving is not necessarily true beneficence, and self-harm or death is not necessarily true courage.

The gate is 仁义 plus public judgment. A quality becomes admirable only when it serves a morally sound aim through proportionate and politically responsible action. Without that grounding, the same quality can become destructive: loyalty can serve a private faction, bravery can serve assassination, and self-sacrifice can mask shallow strategy.

The concept sharpens the wiki's existing assassination branch. [[JingKe|荆轲]] may be memorable as a侠士, and his willingness to die can be read through [[RetainerReciprocityEthic|士为知己者死]], but Qinji 119-1 says that recognition and courage do not make the assassination义. [[YanTaiziDan|燕太子丹]]'s weak-state plan fails because it substitutes personal grievance and a dagger for the statecraft that Sima Guang thinks a ruler owes his people.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-2-zanmen-dou-yuanwang-le-beifu-2000-duonian-heiguo-de-yegong-haolong-lh-h-gezpqtvnyq40zlqac8s-5e]] turns the comparison into a full case. [[YeGong|叶公]] warns [[ZiXiChu|子西]] that [[BaiGongSheng|白公胜]]'s apparent信 and勇 cannot be trusted because they are not ordered by仁 and义. Bai's later murder of Zi Xi, seizure of [[ChuHuiwang|楚惠王]], and collapse in the [[BaiGongShengRebellion|白公胜之乱]] make the standard a practical personnel rule, not only a moral aphorism.

## Key Claims
- Virtue terms need moral direction; visible conduct alone is not enough.
- 信 and 勇 become dangerous when detached from 仁义, strategy, and public responsibility.
- Self-sacrifice can intensify a bad plan rather than redeem it.
- The standard connects personnel judgment and political ethics: a ruler should not confuse fierce commitment with worthy service.
- Qinji 119-1 and 119-2 use [[BaiGongSheng|白公胜]] and [[YeGong|叶公]] to generalize beyond Jing Ke: revenge background and courage still need a renyi gate.
- Violent speech can be a warning sign that outward courage is already detached from benevolent intention.
