# Evidence-Bound Folklore Inquiry

## Definition

Evidence-bound folklore inquiry studies a transmitted story's variants, material traces, testimony, and social uses without treating every vivid retelling as a record of the original event or a proof of supernatural causation.

## Current Synthesis

Different evidence types answer different questions. Early local records may support a remembered loss without confirming a later supernatural plot; recurring motifs and friend-of-a-friend attribution explain circulation without proving occurrence. Sustained witness records may document sincere and socially consequential experience without establishing its cause. Rival explanations should therefore be compared against dates, places, media, class position, and context rather than forced into one clever solution.

The method applies across idiom etymology, medieval legend, urban rumor, literary yokai research, and ghost history. Refuting an attractive story does not prove a replacement, while preserving unresolved residue does not license an extraordinary conclusion.

Autobiographical strange tales add another evidence layer. A remembered episode may document what a narrator experienced, feared, or later made creatively significant, while still leaving perception, memory, species identity, medical state, and causation open. Naturalistic alternatives deserve comparison, but an incomplete ordinary explanation is not itself proof of a supernatural one.

## Key Claims

- Date and genre separate early traces from later narrative additions.
- Several hypotheses can explain parts of a legend while leaving its original event unresolved.
- Transmission patterns and communal anxieties are researchable even when the narrated incident lacks corroboration.
- Testimony can be historically or creatively valuable without settling metaphysical or causal questions.
- Witness credibility must be assessed without reproducing class-based exclusion.
- Attractive etymologies, images, puns, photographs, and instruments require contextual tests before they count as evidence.
- Fraud in one layer should be identified precisely rather than used to explain every surrounding report automatically.

## Evidence

- Early record versus later motif - [[111-huayi-modishou-kuailai-he-lishixuejia-yiqi-tuili-tonghua-zhenxiang-751190766]] distinguishes [[PiedPiperOfHamelin]]'s early child-loss evidence from later rat-catching and broken-contract versions and compares several incomplete historical hypotheses.
- Transmission versus event - [[95-dushi-chuanshuo-lieqi-gushi-he-women-neixin-shenchu-de-jiaolv-723831611]] examines friend-of-a-friend attribution, changing motifs, infrastructure, medical fear, brands, and conspiracy without treating recurrence as proof; its Hamelin material is part of the earlier story chain rather than independent corroboration.
- Origin-story test - [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-46-ma-ni-shi-ge-erbaiwu-de-lishi-laiyuan-lkz5hiamaojtvanllbunecy3g3ff]] rejects the popular [[SuQin]] reward-splitting derivation of “二百五” on economic and social grounds without claiming to establish the true origin.
- Interpretive restraint - [[115-jingji-xiayan-jinxi-xu-baigui-bu-yanjiu-yaoguai-zenme-haohao-zuoren-760601309]] uses fictional yokai research to show that endangered local testimony may be worth collecting while images, puns, and a researcher's first elegant theory remain insufficient proof.
- Testimony, class, and instrument - [[66-ghosts-glt2309926019]] distinguishes the sustained [[HintonAmpner]] record, the [[CockLaneGhost]] hoax, and the publicity-heavy [[BorleyRectory]] case; it also notes historical discounting of servants and the evidentiary ambiguity of technical ghost investigation.
- Autobiographical strange tales - [[073-nikuang-chuanqi-di-erdan-nikuang-qiyi-wangshi-da-gongbu-lomvpjz7gvqycnafmlajvyge-lkv]] preserves [[NiKuang|倪匡]]'s moving-newspaper, underground-eel, buried-skeleton, and apparent-possession stories while separating narrative detail from corroboration and comparing sleep, acoustic, psychiatric, physiological, linguistic, and memory-based alternatives.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Hamelin's disputed loss is not resolved by any one migration, epidemic, battle, famine, or accident theory. The repeated Hamelin account is not a second attestation. The yokai example is fiction, so its research method is analogy rather than historical witness. Rejecting the “二百五” tale does not establish another etymology. In ghost cases, sincere testimony and unexplained incidents do not prove the supernatural, while documented fraud does not automatically explain every claim attached to a site. Ni Kuang's stories come through retrospective memoir and podcast retelling; the absence of a complete ordinary explanation neither verifies the reported details nor establishes paranormal causation.

## What Changed

- Extended the method from transmitted legend and investigated hauntings to autobiographical strange tales.
- Separated remembered experience and creative influence from historical, medical, scientific, or supernatural verification.
- Added competing sleep, acoustic, psychiatric, physiological, linguistic, and memory-based explanations without forcing closure.
