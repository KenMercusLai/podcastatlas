# Metabolic Mental-Health Biomarkers

## Definition
Metabolic mental-health biomarkers are emerging multi-measure panels intended to identify metabolic or mitochondrial patterns associated with severe psychiatric states and eventually support more individualized assessment.

## Current Synthesis
The episode says there is no single available blood test that directly captures mitochondrial health. It instead points to research using panels: one described set contains 20 biomarkers, while another sex-specific five-biomarker panel reportedly distinguished severe suicidal depression from controls with high sensitivity and specificity.

These reports define a research direction, not a clinical test recommendation. A panel that separates selected cases from controls still needs replication, external validation, threshold calibration, and evidence that its use improves decisions and outcomes across diverse populations.

## Key Claims
- No single blood test is presented as an available measure of overall mitochondrial health.
- Multi-biomarker panels may capture distributed metabolic signals better than one marker.
- Early discrimination performance does not by itself establish clinical usefulness.
- Sex-specific panels may reflect meaningful biology or study-specific model design and require validation.
- The intended long-term value is earlier detection and more personalized psychiatric care.

## Evidence
- Measurement gap: [[transform-your-mental-health-with-diet-lifestyle-dr-chris-palmer-scim2561346508]] says no single available blood test measures mitochondrial health.
- Panel research: [[transform-your-mental-health-with-diet-lifestyle-dr-chris-palmer-scim2561346508]] describes a 20-biomarker effort and a separate five-biomarker male/female panel.
- Reported performance: [[transform-your-mental-health-with-diet-lifestyle-dr-chris-palmer-scim2561346508]] reports over 90% sensitivity and specificity for distinguishing severe suicidal depression from controls in the discussed research.
- Clinical goal: [[transform-your-mental-health-with-diet-lifestyle-dr-chris-palmer-scim2561346508]] links biomarker development with earlier metabolic detection and personalized treatment.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source note does not identify the studies, cohorts, validation design, confidence intervals, decision thresholds, or current regulatory and clinical status. Reported case-control performance should not be interpreted as a diagnostic test, suicide-risk screen, or substitute for clinical assessment.
