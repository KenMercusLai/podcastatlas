# Personal-Enterprise Memory Ownership

## Definition
Personal-enterprise memory ownership is the unresolved boundary over who may retain, reuse, transfer, delete, or monetize AI-extracted knowledge when a worker's prior experience and workplace learning are combined in persistent memory or reusable skills.

## Current Synthesis
The episode identifies three possible memory domains: personal, shared, and enterprise. The classification is easy at the extremes—a private life archive differs from a company process—but difficult when an employee applies pre-existing expertise, learns through work, and then has an AI system compress that mixture into a reusable skill.

The bounded conclusion is that technical access controls alone cannot settle ownership. Provenance, role, contract, labor rights, trade-secret duties, portability, and handoff rules all affect whether a digital employee's memory can stay with a company or move with a person. The source raises the issue but does not provide a legal rule or tested governance model.

## Key Claims
- AI can make tacit work knowledge more explicit, transferable, and economically valuable.
- A learned skill may combine personal experience, employer data, coworker contributions, and company-specific process.
- Enterprise continuity can justify retaining role and process knowledge after an employee leaves, but not automatically every personal trace used to produce it.
- Memory systems need provenance and access boundaries that distinguish personal, shared, and enterprise context.
- Replicating worker capability can create productivity gains alongside new labor, consent, attribution, and bargaining conflicts.

## Evidence
### Mixed-origin skills
- [[lsh8sdro6i9mkt5nug4zkibv95tn]] says an employee's skill can derive partly from prior personal experience and partly from learning acquired inside the company.

### Enterprise continuity
- [[lsh8sdro6i9mkt5nug4zkibv95tn]] describes digital-worker job knowledge and operating logic remaining inside the enterprise for transfer after a human employee leaves.

### Layered governance
- [[lsh8sdro6i9mkt5nug4zkibv95tn]] proposes future memory platforms that distinguish personal memory, shared memory, and enterprise memory.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- The episode offers no jurisdiction-specific law, contract analysis, labor agreement, or adjudicated example.
- Ownership may not be a single transferable property right; privacy, copyright, trade secret, confidentiality, employment, and data-protection rules can apply differently.
- Some knowledge is general professional capability that should remain portable, while company records or confidential procedures may properly remain restricted.
