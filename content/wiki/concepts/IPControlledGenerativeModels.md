# IP-Controlled Generative Models

IP-controlled generative models are the rights-holder deployment pattern discussed in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-open-source-wins-agi-is-here-and-scorseses-ai-toolkit-with-ceos-of-cerebras-black-forest-labs-42029880]]. [[RobinRombach|Robin Rombach]] says [[BlackForestLabs|Black Forest Labs]]' public tools prevent generation of certain IP, while the company works with some IP holders to develop models under controlled terms.

The source frames the opportunity as licensed creativity rather than unrestricted imitation. A studio or franchise owner could allow fans, creators, or internal teams to generate stories, imagery, or films with protected characters only inside approved software, policy, and payment boundaries.

## Key Claims
- Public blocking and partner enablement can coexist: a provider can refuse protected IP in open tools while supporting licensed models for rights holders.
- Fan creation creates demand that IP owners may want to monetize rather than suppress, but only if brand, likeness, attribution, and revenue rules are explicit.
- The model itself becomes part of the licensing surface because access, prompts, outputs, filters, and distribution can be governed.
- IP-controlled models extend licensing beyond training-data deals into user-facing creation rights.
