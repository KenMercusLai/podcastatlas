# Workplace Informal Talk / 职场非正式闲聊

Workplace informal talk / 职场非正式闲聊 is the episode's management-side claim that employees need spaces for casual conversation, shared meals, and low-stakes gossip. [[83-shumao-bagua-ji-yuyan-de-jinhua-renji-guanxi-dengbashu-zan-bu-fu-bu-xing-698132861]] interprets tea rooms and communal tables through [[GossipAsSocialGrooming]]: they help coworkers maintain trust, notice each other's state, and coordinate beyond formal tasks.

The concept qualifies, rather than cancels, [[WorkplaceCommunicationRisk]]. Informal talk can create glue, but careless jokes, private facts, or repeated ambiguous favors can still create rumor and reputational harm. The useful management lesson is to preserve humane social space while still respecting boundaries.

## Key Claims
- Informal talk is not automatically wasted time; it can maintain trust and make cooperation easier.
- Removing shared spaces can weaken the everyday relationship layer that formal processes assume.
- Casual talk can surface weak signals about morale, confusion, and friction before they become formal problems.
- The social benefit depends on boundaries: gossip that humiliates, excludes, or exposes private information can damage trust.
- Workplace design should account for human social-maintenance needs instead of treating employees as pure task executors.
