# Political Trial Theatre

Political trial theatre is the use of legal procedure, space, ritual, and visible hierarchy to make a contested judgment appear authoritative. In [[696-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-killing-the-queen-of-scots-part-6-glt2334035496]], [[FotheringhayCastle]] becomes a stage for trying [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]] under the [[ActForTheQueensSafety|Act for the Queen's Safety]].

The episode emphasizes the empty throne of [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]], the hostile commissioners, Mary's lack of counsel, and the use of decrypted correspondence from [[ThomasPhillips]] and [[FrancisWalsingham]]. The trial is legally consequential, but it also performs state power by turning Mary's status from queenly claimant into convicted traitor.
