# Integrated Zone 2 Movement

## Definition
Integrated zone 2 movement is the practice of accumulating conversational-intensity aerobic work through ordinary activities rather than requiring every minute to occur in a dedicated cardio session.

## Current Synthesis
The source reframes zone 2 as a weekly movement target that can include brisk walking, errands, walking meetings or calls, and active play. Its practical test is elevated heart rate and breathing with conversation or nasal breathing still possible. This makes aerobic-base work a scheduling and adherence tool within [[FlexibleWeeklyFitnessScaffold]], not only a gym modality.

The episode proposes 200 minutes or more per week and says this work does not impair strength, hypertrophy, speed, or other cardio. Those quantities and the non-interference claim remain source-scoped because the supplied summary does not identify training status, intensity measurement, studies, or recovery conditions.

## Key Claims
- Ordinary movement can contribute meaningful low-intensity aerobic volume when its effort reaches a conversational zone.
- Walking errands, meetings, phone calls, and active play can reduce the scheduling barrier created by cardio-only sessions.
- Conversation or nasal-breathing capacity is a practical intensity proxy, not a precise physiological measurement.
- Weekly accumulation matters more than whether every minute occurs in formal exercise clothing or equipment.
- The source's 200-plus-minute target and non-interference claim require individual training and recovery context.

## Evidence
- Daily integration - [[science-supported-tools-to-accelerate-your-fitness-goals-scim7037527631]] counts brisk walking, errands, walking meetings, calls, and play toward zone 2 movement.
- Intensity proxy - [[science-supported-tools-to-accelerate-your-fitness-goals-scim7037527631]] defines the effort as elevated heart rate and breathing while conversation or nasal breathing remains possible.
- Weekly target - [[science-supported-tools-to-accelerate-your-fitness-goals-scim7037527631]] recommends roughly 200 minutes or more and attributes a non-interference claim to Andy Galpin.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The talk test varies with fitness, terrain, temperature, altitude, illness, medication, and speaking pattern, and it is less precise than measured thresholds. The source note does not provide study methods for the weekly dose or non-interference claim. Cardiovascular disease, symptoms, pregnancy, disability, injury, heat, and rapid progression can require a different intensity or clinical guidance.
