# Kitsch As Waste Denial / 媚俗作为排泄否认

[[KitschAsWasteDenial|Kitsch as waste denial]] is the Kundera aesthetic concept [[12-man-dang-milan-kundela-yi-ju-zhengjing-meiyou-549335675]] brings into the wiki through [[SlowKundera|《慢》]] and [[TheUnbearableLightnessOfBeing|《不可承受的生命之轻》]]. In the source's summary, Kundera's anti-kitsch stance rejects a sanitized image of life that denies excrement, dirt, shame, and bodily absurdity.

The concept explains why vulgarity in [[SlowKundera|《慢》]] is not only provocation. The body, sexual failure, poolside embarrassment, and crude images interrupt the impulse to turn desire, charity, literature, or politics into clean, sentimental form. That makes [[AntiLyricism]] a craft choice as much as a taste.

## Key Claims
- Kitsch is not only bad taste; it is a moral-aesthetic cleaning operation that refuses waste and impurity.
- Bodily comedy can be anti-sentimental because it returns characters to what public rhetoric tries to hide.
- The concept links Kundera's famous and less-famous works by making apparent unseriousness part of the same critique.
