# Yin-Yang Five-Phases Political Theory / 阴阳五行政治理论

Yin-yang five-phases political theory / 阴阳五行政治理论 is the source's frame for how natural classification becomes political explanation. In [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-wei-wan-daixu-704051997]], yin and yang move from light/shade and environmental contrast toward broader paired categories, while five-phase reasoning develops through mixture, overcoming, generation, and dynastic virtue claims.

The concept is anchored by [[ZouYan|邹衍]]. The episode presents him as a key figure in joining cosmology to historical cycles, making later dynastic legitimacy and fangshu claims easier to formulate.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] adds a source-scoped political use of the number five. The host suggests that [[TaishiDan|太史旦]]'s five-hundred-year language to [[QinXianGong|秦献公]] reflects a cultural environment where five-virtue and five-phases reasoning made "five" especially resonant.

## Key Claims
- Yin-yang and five-phase reasoning should not be treated as one finished theory from the beginning.
- Political use grows when natural categories become historical and dynastic categories.
- Five-virtue claims let rulers describe regime change as cosmic sequence.
- Later fangshu gains authority by borrowing this explanatory grammar.
- Number symbolism can strengthen political persuasion even when the speaker is also making a practical diplomatic appeal.
