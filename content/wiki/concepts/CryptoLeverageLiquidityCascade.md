# Crypto Leverage-Liquidity Cascade

Crypto leverage-liquidity cascade is the source's explanation for why a tariff headline could produce a rapid flash crash in crypto. [[bu-xi-deng-e02-bi-quan-shan-beng-mei-guo-zheng-fu-guan-men-first-brands-po-chan-wa-ha-ha-feng-bo-lt-ghzgmiroo9a-odaih4xkhsulu]] says [[DonaldTrump]]'s sudden 100% China-tariff message was the visible trigger, but the deeper setup was months of gains, high leverage, fragile market-making, and thin real demand in smaller coins.

The mechanism extends [[CryptocurrencyMarketStructure]]. In a stress event, market makers may conserve capital and quotes for [[Bitcoin]] and other large assets, while smaller tokens lose liquidity. Prices then fall through thin order books because there is no deep natural bid, producing liquidations that create more forced selling.

## Key Claims
- A political headline can trigger a crash without being the full cause of the crash.
- Leverage converts price moves into forced selling, which can amplify rather than absorb shocks.
- Market-maker retreat can make small-token prices move discontinuously because quoted liquidity was doing much of the apparent price support.
- The concept separates crypto technology belief from tradable-market fragility.
