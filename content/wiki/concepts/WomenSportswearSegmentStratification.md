# Women's Sportswear Segment Stratification / 女性运动服饰细分分层

## Definition
Women's sportswear segment stratification is the division of one broad apparel category into finer price, style, occasion, and identity segments, so that new labels can enter through a specific position rather than through general performance claims.

## Current Synthesis
The wiki already treats sportswear identity as something built through venues, classes, and community, and treats multi-brand portfolios as a way to address different consumers. The current source adds market structure: it reports an industry view that Chinese women's sportswear consumption keeps stratifying, with room in narrower segments, and that [[NikeSkims]] can draw young fashion-oriented consumers through overseas trend heat while product localization, channel layout, and marketing determine longevity. Read together with [[SportswearExperienceSpace]], the picture is a category where brands compete less on a single performance ladder and more on which occasion, body story, and style identity they own.

## Key Claims
- Category growth increasingly happens in segments rather than in one mass women's sportswear market.
- A new label can enter on borrowed trend heat, but that attention is not the same as a durable position.
- Fit, sizing, and body-story positioning function as differentiation alongside technical performance.
- Fashion and performance codes are converging, so a bodywear brand and a performance brand can combine rather than compete.
- Channel and occasion design, including whether the brand has a physical or experience presence, shapes conversion.
- Localization quality decides whether an imported position survives contact with domestic price and style competition.

## Evidence
- Segment view - [[tulasi-pingguo-cheng-jinnian-aimeijiang-de-zuida-yingjia-xiecheng-erjidu-youying-zhuankui-1015756412]] reports an industry view that Chinese women's sportswear consumption keeps stratifying with meaningful space in narrower segments.
- Entry logic - [[tulasi-pingguo-cheng-jinnian-aimeijiang-de-zuida-yingjia-xiecheng-erjidu-youying-zhuankui-1015756412]] says NikeSkims carries overseas trend heat that can attract fashion-oriented young consumers.
- Durability condition - [[tulasi-pingguo-cheng-jinnian-aimeijiang-de-zuida-yingjia-xiecheng-erjidu-youying-zhuankui-1015756412]] says long-term success depends on product localization, channel layout, and marketing and operations capability.
- Identity construction - [[tulasi-pingguo-cheng-jinnian-aimeijiang-de-zuida-yingjia-xiecheng-erjidu-youying-zhuankui-1015756412]] ties the brand's position to a partner identity built on shapewear, full-size range, and everyday wear rather than only athletic use.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The stratification claim is attributed industry opinion inside a short news segment, not market research, and the episode gives no share, price-band, or demographic data. Fashion-led entry can also prove shallow when trend attention decays faster than product and channel adaptation.
