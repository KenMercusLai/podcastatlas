# Price-Signal Resource Steering / 价格信号调控物流

Price-signal resource steering / 价格信号调控物流 is the trade-policy mechanism [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-75-4-weishenme-shuo-guanzhong-caishi-jingji-zhan-de-bizu-lrdleqozazh4-xdwas4karzgbmza]] extracts from [[GuanZhong|管仲]]'s line "天下之所下者，我高之；天下之所倾者，我重之." In the episode's reading, a state can use favorable prices to pull scarce or desired goods inward and make it less attractive for critical goods to flow outward.

This concept sits between ordinary trade policy and [[InterstateEconomicWarfare|interstate economic warfare]]. In constructive form, price steering supports [[TradeHubStatecraft|trade-hub statecraft]] by making [[QiState|齐国]] attractive to merchants and goods. In hostile form, it becomes visible in [[HengshanEconomicWarfare|恒山之谋]], where high prices move weapons and grain in ways that weaken the target.

## Key Claims
- Prices are not only market outcomes; the episode treats them as policy signals.
- A state can influence material flows without formally confiscating goods.
- High prices can attract foreign goods, preserve domestic supply, or lure a rival into exposure depending on intent.
- Price policy has to be read with grain reserves, border control, and trade openness; the signal alone is not the whole strategy.
