## Definition

德位不配风险 / Reward fitness risk is Hanji 430's warning that honor, wealth, or office can become dangerous when it exceeds the recipient's merit, virtue, or capacity to carry it.

## Current Synthesis

The concept is grounded in [[ZhangAnshiWesternHan|张安世]]'s repeated effort to reduce rewards attached to [[ZhangHeWesternHan|张贺]] and [[ZhangPengzuWesternHan|张彭祖]]. The host reads Zhang Anshi's caution through the formula "德不配位，必有灾殃": excessive reward may create backlash, envy, exposure, or moral imbalance.

The idea complements [[YindeRewardCausality|阴德福报因果]]. Hidden virtue deserves eventual repayment, but the recipient should still avoid grasping at rewards that exceed the actual basis for honor.

## Key Claims

- Reward is not only a benefit; it creates exposure and burden.
- Zhang Anshi's refusal is presented as prudence rather than ingratitude.
- The problem is proportionality between merit, virtue, status, and visible gain.
- The episode turns an imperial reward scene into a broader warning about taking money or honor one has not earned.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

This concept records the episode's moral interpretation. It does not prove that every disproportionate reward causes disaster, nor does it settle the full historical motives behind Zhang Anshi's refusal.
