# Civilian Infrastructure Air Campaign

## Definition
Civilian infrastructure air campaign is the sustained use of drones and missiles against energy, transport, logistics, retail, water, heat, industry, and population centers to weaken a country’s economic capacity and social endurance behind a relatively stable front.

## Current Synthesis
The bounded sources apply the pattern to [[Russia]]’s attacks across [[Ukraine]]. The strategic effect is cumulative: damaged services and supply chains can increase frustration and emigration, burden soldiers’ families, distract troops, and interact with political distrust even without a decisive territorial breakthrough. Sabotage, assassination plots, and disinformation can amplify uncertainty around ordinary routines, while decentralized networks and prior preparation limit the chance of total shutdown.

The available activity measures sharpen the strategic contrast. Russian strikes at least 100 kilometres beyond the front or border reportedly tripled from June to September, with 290 civilian-infrastructure targets and at least 62 civilian deaths in the preceding month and more than 500 trains hit since the invasion began. These attacks accelerated while Ukraine made modest frontline gains, reinforcing the distinction between territorial momentum and the wider campaign against state, economic, and social capacity.

## Key Claims
- Long-range strike advantage can matter even when the front line barely moves or shifts against the attacker locally.
- Civilian, commercial, transport, industrial, and utility targets connect military pressure to wartime economic endurance.
- Family hardship and routine disruption behind the front can affect morale, attention, and political trust.
- Sabotage, assassination threats, and disinformation can magnify the social effect of physical strikes.
- The campaign is asymmetric in scale without implying that Ukraine conducts no long-range attacks of its own.
- The effect is conditional, not automatic; battlefield balance and social resilience can persist despite severe attacks.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Damage estimates, target classification, casualty figures, and predictions of desertion or political spirals are source-scoped assessments rather than independently verified outcomes. Russia has often overestimated its capabilities, and severe disruption need not produce systemic collapse or the political result its planners seek. Ukraine also conducts long-range attacks, so asymmetry should be established through scale, target pattern, and effect rather than by denying reciprocity. The sources do not show that disinformation created the real governance grievances it can exploit.

## What Changed
- Added reported strike, target, train, and civilian-death counts to quantify deep-campaign escalation.
- Made coexistence between modest Ukrainian territorial gains and expanding Russian infrastructure pressure explicit.
- Added calibrated asymmetry: Ukrainian long-range attacks continue, but the reported Russian acceleration is much larger.
