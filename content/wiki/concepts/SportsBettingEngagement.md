# Sports Betting Engagement

Sports betting engagement is the attention effect described in [[the-nfl-1]] where legalized gambling increases the number of reasons fans follow games, players, injuries, lines, fantasy rosters, and broadcasts. The episode updates NFL betting numbers from 46 million Americans betting on the league to an estimated 76 million and cites gambling-related sponsorship and indirect annual benefit estimates.

The concept is adjacent to prediction-market integrity issues but has a different center of gravity. In the NFL source, betting is mainly a demand and monetization accelerator for the [[SportsEntertainmentFlywheel]], while still carrying integrity, regulatory, and trust risk.

## Key Claims
- Betting can deepen engagement even for games a fan's own team is not playing.
- Sponsorship revenue understates total benefit if betting increases viewing, fantasy activity, and social attention.
- The same engagement mechanism can create integrity and trust exposure if leagues, books, and regulators fail to manage conflicts.
