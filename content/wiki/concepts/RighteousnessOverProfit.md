# Righteousness Over Profit / 义利之辨

Righteousness over profit / 义利之辨 is [[30-mengzi-zhanguo-fenqing-de-chizi-zhixin-592494138]]'s frame for [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]]'s answer to [[LiangHuiWang|梁惠王]]: a state cannot be stabilized if everyone from ruler to ministers to commoners treats private advantage as the first question. The episode reads "何必曰利？亦有仁义而已矣" as a political diagnosis rather than as anti-practical moralism.

The concept becomes plausible only inside the [[WarringStatesPeriod|战国时期]] setting. When states compete for people, resources, and legitimacy, renyi is not merely personal virtue; it is a way to prevent predatory logic from dissolving the bonds that keep a polity viable.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-26-mengzi-laile-ln4twwp2frmr-xpumu0rqg-2gpyn]] refines the concept through [[RenyiBenefitOrdering|仁义优先的利益秩序]]. The episode stresses that Mencius is not against benefit itself; through [[Zisi|子思]] and [[SimaGuang|司马光]], it distinguishes public benefit produced by renyi from a ruler's profit-first language.

## Key Claims
- Asking first about profit teaches every rank to imitate self-interest downward.
- Renyi is presented as a practical foundation for order, not as refusal to govern.
- The source contrasts Mencius's position with the broader Warring States atmosphere of usefulness, gain, and rivalry.
- The concept supports but does not exhaust [[MencianBenevolentGovernment]].
- Zhouji 26 adds that benefit can be a legitimate outcome of renyi when it is not treated as the first governing aim.
