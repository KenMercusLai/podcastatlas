# AI-Native Narrative Worlds

## Definition
AI-native narrative worlds are content products where AI is the base layer for world state, character behavior, story progression, and user participation, rather than a feature added to a preexisting comic, game, video, or chatbot format.

## Current Synthesis
The current synthesis is source-scoped to [[LIVO]]. [[ChenAnni]] argues that AI should produce a new storytelling carrier comparable to how electricity helped make film possible: users should enter living worlds, build relationships with agents, and influence unfolding fate. The concept overlaps with [[AISimulationContent]] and [[AIInteractiveEntertainment]], but this source adds a stronger premium-content and IP requirement: a world is not valuable merely because it runs; it needs authored drama, emotional payoff, and shared-versus-personal narrative balance.

## Key Claims
- AI-native narrative worlds differ from "old content plus AI" because the story world, characters, and interaction loops are built around model-driven behavior from the start.
- The user is positioned as a participant inside the story rather than only an observer, reader, player of fixed levels, or chatbot conversational partner.
- The product still needs authored worldviews, themes, IP quality, and emotional arcs; generative openness is not a substitute for content judgment.
- Personalization and common culture must be balanced: users can experience different relationships and fate paths while still sharing a world background and public discussion object.
- Persistent worlds raise AI-cost and quality thresholds because token consumption continues beyond a single prompt or static media output.

- Participatory storytelling: [[ta-beipian-guo-beijiu-guo-beipeng-shang-shentan-guo-beitengtong-jidao-guo-jueding-zai-ai-shidai-jixu-maoxian-duitan-chen-anni-lomvn15fklk80bhkreied1iotuof]] contrasts LIVO with Stanford-town-style observation by emphasizing the user's role inside the world and story.
- Authored content requirement: [[ta-beipian-guo-beijiu-guo-beipeng-shang-shentan-guo-beitengtong-jidao-guo-jueding-zai-ai-shidai-jixu-maoxian-duitan-chen-anni-lomvn15fklk80bhkreied1iotuof]] says LIVO worlds need layered, premium IP that produces happiness and emotional movement.
- Shared/personal balance: [[ta-beipian-guo-beijiu-guo-beipeng-shang-shentan-guo-beitengtong-jidao-guo-jueding-zai-ai-shidai-jixu-maoxian-duitan-chen-anni-lomvn15fklk80bhkreied1iotuof]] answers the one-user-one-story problem by separating common world tone and storyline from personalized relationship depth.
- Cost threshold: [[ta-beipian-guo-beijiu-guo-beipeng-shang-shentan-guo-beitengtong-jidao-guo-jueding-zai-ai-shidai-jixu-maoxian-duitan-chen-anni-lomvn15fklk80bhkreied1iotuof]] notes token cost and argues high-quality content is necessary because running AI worlds is expensive.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept is still a product hypothesis. The source does not demonstrate that users will retain, pay, or build shared culture around LIVO-style worlds; it only explains the founder's intended product logic.

## What Changed
- Added a narrower concept for LIVO-style AI-native storytelling that sits between simulation content, interactive entertainment, and content IP.
