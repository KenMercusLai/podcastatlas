# Planned-Community New Build

## Definition

Planned-community new build is a housing-production model in which a developer controls the subdivision, construction sequence, available plans, and upgrade menu while the buyer selects among bounded design, structural, finish, electrical, and lot options.

## Current Synthesis

[[ep160-wo-zai-meiguo-ziji-jian-fangzi-meiguo-ye-you-xuequ-fang-ckwriueecxq5abaaaacki6jm|EP160]] places the model between buying a finished resale home and commissioning a fully custom house. Standardization makes price, scheduling, inspections, crews, and product choice more manageable, but it also turns customization into a controlled sales system whose convenience can carry substantial markups.

## Key Claims
- Buyer choice is real but bounded by builder plans, available lots, preset options, neighboring-facade rules, zoning, and code.
- Standardization can coordinate construction and inspection while reducing the buyer's architectural freedom.
- Structural, electrical, and finish decisions have different reversibility, making early infrastructure choices more valuable than some cosmetic upgrades.
- Builder-installed upgrades exchange convenience and immediate readiness for higher prices; post-handover work exchanges money savings for time, disruption, and coordination risk.
- A new-build transaction remains incomplete until inspection, appraisal, mortgage closing, and key transfer align.

## Evidence
- Bounded choice and standardization: [[ep160-wo-zai-meiguo-ziji-jian-fangzi-meiguo-ye-you-xuequ-fang-ckwriueecxq5abaaaacki6jm|EP160]] describes a fixed set of floor plans, facade-spacing rules, option tiers, and zoning constraints.
- Inspection and handover: [[ep160-wo-zai-meiguo-ziji-jian-fangzi-meiguo-ye-you-xuequ-fang-ckwriueecxq5abaaaacki6jm|EP160]] reports staged city inspections, correction of failed work, later appraisal, closing, and key transfer.
- Upgrade economics: [[ep160-wo-zai-meiguo-ziji-jian-fangzi-meiguo-ye-you-xuequ-fang-ckwriueecxq5abaaaacki6jm|EP160]] contrasts expensive coordinated builder options with cheaper but disruptive owner-arranged changes.
- Infrastructure timing: [[ep160-wo-zai-meiguo-ziji-jian-fangzi-meiguo-ye-you-xuequ-fang-ckwriueecxq5abaaaacki6jm|EP160]] shows preinstalled networking and electrical choices supporting later Wi-Fi, cameras, controls, and appliances.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept comes from one large-builder project in the Phoenix area and should not be generalized to every U.S. jurisdiction, builder, custom-home contract, condominium, or manufactured home. The source reports a smooth project and early delivery, but does not compare defect rates, warranty enforcement, builder margins, or inspection quality across firms.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to distinguish builder-bounded customization from both resale purchase and fully custom construction.
