# Tacit Management Knowledge / 默会知识

Tacit management knowledge / 默会知识 is [[FanXinyu|范鑫宇]]'s term in [[ep255-chang-erdai-de-jicheng-zhi-zhan-nianqingren-jieban-hui-chongsu-qiye-ma-likvzmpgwxnetpaezxclsx4s-hkw]] for the judgment that cannot be learned only from school, books, or business frameworks. In the episode, this is the central missing layer for many [[FactorySecondGeneration|厂二代]] successors.

The concept extends the wiki's [[ProcessKnowledge]] page from technical manufacturing into management. A successor may understand finance, English, strategy, or digital tools, but still need repeated exposure to workers, dealers, suppliers, customers, family relations, and crisis decisions before they develop the "hand feel" that founders acquired through long practice.

## Key Claims
- Tacit management knowledge includes judging people, reading a situation, knowing when to intervene, and understanding how formal decisions play out in informal relationships.
- Founders may seek business school to systematize what they already learned by doing; successors often have formal education but lack practical accumulation.
- Working from after-sales, assistant roles, factory-floor learning, and marketing execution can be ways to build this knowledge.
- Personal IP and digital commerce are insufficient if successors never understand the company's full operating chain.
