# Local Vehicle Assembly Tax Strategy / 汽车本地组装税务策略

## Definition
Local vehicle assembly tax strategy is the choice to assemble imported parts through a local plant or partner so a vehicle qualifies for tax treatment, pricing, configuration, or market-access advantages unavailable to fully imported units.

## Current Synthesis
The BYD Malaysia report shows localization as a spectrum rather than a factory-or-import binary. When a wholly owned plant was reportedly paused after government negotiations failed, partner assembly offered a faster route to local tax treatment after exemptions for imported complete electric vehicles expired. The model can improve price competitiveness while giving BYD less direct control than its own factory.

## Key Claims
- Tax classification can determine whether local assembly is commercially necessary.
- Contract assembly can provide speed and lower commitment when greenfield negotiations stall.
- Local partners contribute facilities and market access but introduce coordination and control tradeoffs.
- Assembly localization is shallower than full local supply, manufacturing ownership, or R&D.
- Price and configuration benefits remain contingent on realized tax treatment and execution.

## Evidence
- Route change: [[qianyu-jia-yingyuan-zhibo-tiyu-saishi-shangbannian-guonei-jinshi-xiaofeiliang-xiadie-sancheng-gkwridooqlkafaplwtreagx]] says BYD paused its own Malaysia factory and moved to assembly at a distributor-affiliated plant.
- Negotiation constraint: [[qianyu-jia-yingyuan-zhibo-tiyu-saishi-shangbannian-guonei-jinshi-xiaofeiliang-xiadie-sancheng-gkwridooqlkafaplwtreagx]] says BYD and the Malaysian government did not agree on factory conditions.
- Tax mechanism: [[qianyu-jia-yingyuan-zhibo-tiyu-saishi-shangbannian-guonei-jinshi-xiaofeiliang-xiadie-sancheng-gkwridooqlkafaplwtreagx]] says imported-EV exemptions expired while locally assembled vehicles could avoid import, excise, and sales taxes.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not provide contract terms, local-content requirements, capacity, start date, models, tax rulings, labor effects, or comparative cost. A partner plant can reduce commitment while limiting process learning and supply-chain control.

## What Changed
- Created a localization concept centered on tax classification and assembly depth.
