## Definition
中国足球财务纪律 / Chinese football financial discipline is the source's proposed governance layer for keeping club expansion and league recovery from becoming another cycle of unpaid wages, unpaid taxes, shareholder-loan debt, and unstable access rules.

## Current Synthesis
The source treats financial discipline as the non-negotiable partner of [[ChineseFootballLeagueExpansion]]. It does not call primarily for another salary cut; instead it emphasizes strict treatment of new tax arrears, wage arrears, and debt disguised as shareholder support. The broader idea is that expansion creates a policy window to harden entry and monitoring rules while the league market is recovering.

## Key Claims
- Expansion should not proceed as a stand-alone measure; finance rules must be tightened at the same time.
- New tax arrears are treated as especially unacceptable and potentially deserving relegation-level punishment.
- Historical tax arrears may need installment treatment, but the source distinguishes legacy cleanup from new violations.
- Shareholder support should not accumulate indefinitely as club debt through loans.
- Better monitoring, including bank-flow authorization and wage-arrears checks, is discussed as a possible but not yet implemented system.

- Expansion precondition: [[vol-226-zhongchao-kuojun-yu-kuanianzhi-liansai-xiayibu-gai-zenme-zou-ft-chenyong-gkwrimaoef2yadmp9qtvpacu]] says expansion cannot be isolated from support policies.
- Tax and wage rules: [[vol-226-zhongchao-kuojun-yu-kuanianzhi-liansai-xiayibu-gai-zenme-zou-ft-chenyong-gkwrimaoef2yadmp9qtvpacu]] calls for stricter treatment of new unpaid taxes and wage arrears.
- Debt structure: [[vol-226-zhongchao-kuojun-yu-kuanianzhi-liansai-xiayibu-gai-zenme-zou-ft-chenyong-gkwrimaoef2yadmp9qtvpacu]] criticizes long-term shareholder loans becoming hidden club debt.
- Monitoring gap: [[vol-226-zhongchao-kuojun-yu-kuanianzhi-liansai-xiayibu-gai-zenme-zou-ft-chenyong-gkwrimaoef2yadmp9qtvpacu]] mentions professional-league research into bank-flow and real-time arrears monitoring but says it had not been put in place.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not provide audited balance sheets or official enforcement texts. It treats [[HenanFC]] and [[ShandongTaishan]] as examples of financial strain and cost changes, but those examples remain source-scoped rather than complete club-finance assessments.

## What Changed
- Added financial discipline as the governance precondition for Chinese football expansion.
