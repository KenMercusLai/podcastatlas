# 五代义子假子制度 / Five Dynasties Adoptive-Son System

Five Dynasties adoptive-son system names [[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]]'s discussion of义子 and假子 ties in late Tang and Five Dynasties military groups. The source treats adoption, name changes, and pseudo-kinship as tools for expanding armed capacity and binding loyalty when formal institutions were weak.

Figures such as [[LiKeyong|李克用]], [[LiCunxu|李存勖]], and [[LiSiyuan|李嗣源]] make the system concrete. The episode also notes that after regime change, names could be changed again to cut old bonds or signal a new affiliation.
