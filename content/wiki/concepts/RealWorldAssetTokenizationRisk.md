# Real World Asset Tokenization Risk

Real world asset tokenization risk is the source's caution about putting private credit and similar off-chain assets onto blockchain rails. [[bu-xi-deng-e02-bi-quan-shan-beng-mei-guo-zheng-fu-guan-men-first-brands-po-chan-wa-ha-ha-feng-bo-lt-ghzgmiroo9a-odaih4xkhsulu]] explains RWA as "real world asset" tokenization, then notes that private credit is one of the more active RWA categories because it lacks public-market liquidity.

The risk is that tokenization can change the wrapper faster than it changes the asset. If the underlying loans, receivables, marks, collateral, or cash controls are opaque, an on-chain format may create a liquidity story without solving [[PrivateCreditReceivablesOpacity]] or [[PrivateCreditTailRisk]].

## Key Claims
- Tokenization can improve distribution and transfer mechanics while leaving underwriting and collateral risk intact.
- Private credit is especially tempting for RWA because it already lacks mature public trading.
- An RWA label should not be mistaken for transparency if the underlying asset remains hard to value or audit.
- The concept connects crypto-market infrastructure to old credit-cycle risks rather than treating it as a purely new asset class.
