# 等级场景中的进言包装 / Hierarchy-Aware Advice Framing

## Definition
等级场景中的进言包装 / Hierarchy-Aware Advice Framing is the pattern where accurate counsel needs a form the superior can accept without feeling publicly diminished. It does not mean diluting substance into flattery; it means preserving the decision value of advice by fitting wording, timing, and face-saving structure to the hierarchy.

## Current Synthesis
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] supplies the underlying [[YuanShao|袁绍]] council scene: [[JuShou|沮授]] gives a strong recovery-first plan after the [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] war, but frames the campaign against [[CaoCao|曹操]] as a dangerous move against the court-backed side and distinguishes righteous troops from arrogant troops. [[GuoTu|郭图]] and [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]] answer with a more ego-compatible frame, stressing Yuan Shao's large army, available grain, and the possibility of making the attack look righteous.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] develops the communication lesson explicitly. Ju Shou's strategic content remains strong, but his blunt legitimacy language gives Yuan Shao less room to accept without losing face. Flattering advisers can then win the meeting with weaker strategy because their wording better matches the leader's desired self-image. The concept therefore sits between content and reception: in a hierarchy, the delivery channel is part of the advice's operational design.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1042-congming-haishi-wangu-tianfeng-yin-dingzhuang-yuanshao-ruyu-lk5x6if2rouarmgichtynzhl3cgs|Hanji 1042]] shows the harsher failure mode through [[TianFengLateHan|田丰]]. Tian Feng's defensive, agricultural, and harassment plan is more concrete than a vague objection, but he presses it just when Yuan Shao wants to move against [[CaoCao|曹操]]. Yuan Shao interprets the persistence as shaking military morale and imprisons him, so the case extends advice framing from "not accepted" to "treated as a threat."

## Key Claims
- Advice quality includes both strategic content and the acceptance path created by wording, timing, and status management.
- Blunt correctness can fail in hierarchy when it publicly lowers the decision-maker's legitimacy or competence.
- Flattery can beat better analysis when it matches the leader's desired self-image and lets the leader avoid painful constraint.
- The remedy is not dishonest praise but decision-oriented framing that gives the superior a face-saving route to accept correction.
- Failed advice framing can have organizational effects when a leader responds by weakening the warning adviser's command authority.
- Repeated blunt correction before mobilization can be criminalized as morale damage even when the proposal is strategically coherent.

## Evidence
- Correct content, painful frame: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] has Ju Shou recommend rest, agriculture, court-facing victory reporting, gradual positioning, and harassment while warning that attacking Cao Cao risks looking like opposition to the emperor.
- Ego-compatible counterframe: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] shows Guo Tu and Shen Pei praising Yuan Shao's strength and arguing that the campaign can be cast as righteous.
- Communication diagnosis: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] treats Ju Shou's proposal as strategically strong but poorly packaged for Yuan Shao's status psychology.
- Organizational consequence: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] says Yuan Shao weakens Ju Shou by dividing his forces among Ju Shou, Guo Tu, and [[ChunyuQiong|淳于琼]].
- Punitive recoding: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1042-congming-haishi-wangu-tianfeng-yin-dingzhuang-yuanshao-ruyu-lk5x6if2rouarmgichtynzhl3cgs|Hanji 1042]] says Tian Feng keeps pressing a hold-and-harass strategy before Yuan Shao's campaign, after which Yuan Shao shackles and imprisons him as a danger to morale.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept should not be used to blame advisers for every ignored warning. Hanji 1033, Hanji 1034, and Hanji 1042 also identify Yuan Shao's preferences, visible force advantage, factional infighting, Cao-side organization, and pre-battle morale anxiety as causes. It also does not recommend false praise; the useful boundary is preserving truth while designing a viable route for the truth to be heard.

## What Changed
- Added Tian Feng's imprisonment as the punitive endpoint of failed hierarchy-aware framing.
- The concept now ties advice framing to concrete organizational consequences such as command-splitting and morale-punishment.
