# 等级场景中的进言包装 / Hierarchy-Aware Advice Framing

## Definition
等级场景中的进言包装 / Hierarchy-Aware Advice Framing is the source-scoped pattern where accurate counsel needs a form the superior can accept without feeling publicly diminished. It does not mean diluting substance into flattery; it means preserving the decision value of advice by fitting wording, timing, and face-saving structure to the hierarchy.

## Current Synthesis
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] develops this concept through [[JuShou|沮授]]'s advice to [[YuanShao|袁绍]] before [[GuanduCampaign|官渡]]. Ju Shou's strategic content is strong: recover from the long war against [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]], repair agriculture, harass [[CaoCao|曹操]], and wait for a better decisive window. The host's qualification is that Ju Shou phrases the point in a way that wounds Yuan Shao's status by treating Cao's side as more legitimate and Yuan's campaign as lacking just cause. That gives flattering advisers such as [[GuoTu|郭图]] and [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]] room to win the meeting with weaker strategy but better emotional fit.

The concept therefore sits between content and reception. Correct advice still matters, but in a hierarchy the delivery channel is part of the advice's operational design. When language triggers self-defense, the leader may choose praise over truth; when the adviser preserves dignity while stating constraints, hard counsel has a better chance of becoming action.

## Key Claims
- Advice quality includes both strategic content and the acceptance path created by wording, timing, and status management.
- Blunt correctness can fail in hierarchy when it publicly lowers the decision-maker's legitimacy or competence.
- Flattery can beat better analysis when it matches the leader's desired self-image and lets the leader avoid painful constraint.
- The remedy is not dishonest praise but decision-oriented framing that gives the superior a face-saving route to accept correction.
- Hanji 1034 makes communication style a causal mechanism inside Yuan Shao's broader adviser-system failure.

## Evidence
- Correct content, weak acceptance path: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] treats Ju Shou's rest, agriculture, harassment, and delay proposal as strategically strong but poorly packaged for Yuan Shao.
- Flattery outcompeting analysis: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] shows Guo Tu and Shen Pei praising Yuan Shao's martial greatness and Hebei's strength, which the source says better fits Yuan Shao's psychology.
- Organizational consequence: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] contrasts Yuan Shao's factional advice meeting with Cao Cao and Xun Yu's more precise shared assessment of the opposing camp.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept should not be used to blame advisers for every ignored warning. Hanji 1034 also identifies Yuan Shao's preferences, factional infighting, adviser incompatibility, and Cao-side organization as causes. It also does not recommend false praise: the useful boundary is preserving truth while designing a viable route for the truth to be heard.

## What Changed
- Created this concept from Hanji 1034's distinction between Ju Shou's correct strategy and his status-wounding delivery.
