# Tariff Consumer Price Pass-Through

## Definition

Tariff consumer price pass-through is the process by which import duties paid at the border affect household prices, product availability, or purchasing conditions downstream.

## Current Synthesis

Pass-through is partial, delayed, and category-sensitive. Inventory timing, substitutes, seasonality, regulation, routing, margins, and competitor responses affect when households see the burden. Domestic producers may also gain room to raise prices when imported alternatives become more expensive, while exemptions and intermediaries can redistribute costs without eliminating them.

The consumer-refund case adds an observability boundary. A separately itemized tariff line makes the passed-through amount legible and potentially refundable. When the burden is embedded in a broader price change, inflation, supply costs, bundles, and normal repricing make both incidence and later repayment harder to reconstruct.

## Key Claims

- Tariff costs can reach households with a delay rather than immediately at the policy announcement.
- Pass-through is uneven because product categories differ in inventory, substitutes, seasonality, and compliance needs.
- Exemptions and gray routing can mute shelves going empty while still raising total system cost.
- Domestic substitute prices can also rise when tariffed imports become more expensive.
- Consumer price effects can become a political bargaining channel across borders.
- Itemized charges are easier to attribute and refund than tariff costs embedded in a changing retail price.

## Evidence

- **Category and routing differences:** [[vol-128-guanshui-zhan-xiayibu-zouxiang-hefang-meiguo-renmin-ruhe-kandai-telangpu-bairi-xinzheng-kuangxi-bokejie-duihua-zhongshu-talich-ltivoqhue1t-q2ejjlzlenormkhb]] uses toys, baby gear, fast fashion, ecommerce goods, exemptions, and gray routing to show uneven transmission.
- **Lag and substitute response:** [[146-meiguo-jingji-zheme-cha-meigu-hai-neng-jixu-zhang-ma-chuantai-meilun-meihuan-lrshkxgofprel-e-7lkiqu-en8gu]] attributes delayed pass-through to inventories and pricing adjustment and includes domestic substitutes in the channel.
- **Political pressure:** [[elbows-up-again-a-us-canada-trade-flare-up-6a8c0fa3a89d26398ee7a5db]] makes higher American consumer prices part of Canada's waiting and bargaining strategy.
- **Visible versus embedded incidence:** [[should-we-all-be-getting-tariff-refunds-right-now]] contrasts [[PanicSoftware|Panic]]'s separate charge with console price increases that could not be matched cleanly to customer refunds.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The bounded sources do not establish a single economy-wide pass-through rate. Their inflation estimates, price examples, tariff schedules, and bargaining effects are source- and date-specific. A price increase during a tariff period is not proof that the tariff caused the entire increase, and itemization improves traceability without proving that the amount perfectly measures economic incidence.
