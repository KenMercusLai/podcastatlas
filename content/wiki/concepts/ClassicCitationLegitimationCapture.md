## Definition

Classic citation legitimation capture / 引经据典式欲望包装 is the court-rhetoric pattern where authoritative classical precedent is used to legitimize a ruler's desire or status claim while the precedent's limiting conditions are narrowed, ignored, or redirected.

## Current Synthesis

The current evidence shows two uses of learned precedent. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-919-xiang-huode-hao-xian-tikai-shanliang-de-ziji-lrqxfzir4bqxt0v3ccvfef8faade|Hanji 919]], attendants cite the [[MenciusText|Mencius]] contrast between [[ZhouWenwang|King Wen of Zhou]] and [[QiXuanWang|King Xuan of Qi]] to make [[EmperorLingOfHan|Emperor Ling]]'s garden project sound compatible with shared public pleasure after [[YangCiLateHan|Yang Ci]] warns about farmland and displacement. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-607-jiemi-wangmang-de-quanli-boyi-lgnxg-zlf5gowjbirazpijvyks78|Hanji 607]], [[WangMang|王莽]] uses the white-pheasant story around [[ZhouGong|周公 / Duke of Zhou]] to make a staged distant tribute look like the repetition of sage-regent history.

The pattern is not anti-classical. The danger is that cultural prestige can outrun the governance test that made the old story meaningful. A court can turn a citation into decoration for private pleasure, or into an omen-backed route from practical power to higher title.

## Key Claims

- Citation becomes manipulation when it supplies prestige while discarding the conditions of the precedent.
- Learned flattery is more dangerous than blunt flattery because it can sound principled.
- Classical rhetoric needs an external governance test, such as public access, livelihood protection, source credibility, or institutional restraint.
- Precedent can authorize not only projects but also personal status expansion when a current actor is cast as the return of an admired ancient model.

## Evidence

- Garden-project capture: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-919-xiang-huode-hao-xian-tikai-shanliang-de-ziji-lrqxfzir4bqxt0v3ccvfef8faade|Hanji 919]] says Emperor Ling hesitates after Yang Ci's warning, but attendants use Mencian shared-pleasure rhetoric to rehabilitate the garden plan.
- Lost public-use condition: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-919-xiang-huode-hao-xian-tikai-shanliang-de-ziji-lrqxfzir4bqxt0v3ccvfef8faade|Hanji 919]] treats the Wenwang/Qi Xuan comparison as captured because the attendants keep the prestige of the precedent while dropping its public-access test.
- White-pheasant precedent capture: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-607-jiemi-wangmang-de-quanli-boyi-lgnxg-zlf5gowjbirazpijvyks78|Hanji 607]] says Wang Mang stages Yuechang tribute and uses the Duke of Zhou white-pheasant story to imply that a present sage has appeared.
- Status result: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-607-jiemi-wangmang-de-quanli-boyi-lgnxg-zlf5gowjbirazpijvyks78|Hanji 607]] says ministers respond to the staged sign by proposing Anhan Gong status and expanded fief households for Wang Mang.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept does not mean every classical citation is manipulation. [[AllusiveRemonstrance]] shows that allusion can also protect criticism or encode principled warning. Hanji 919 supports the rhetoric pattern but leaves the attendants' exact motives and Emperor Ling's inner response source-scoped. Hanji 607 supports Wang Mang's use of the white-pheasant precedent, but Yuechang geography, the precise local instruction chain, ministerial coordination, and Wang Mang's private motive remain source-scoped.
