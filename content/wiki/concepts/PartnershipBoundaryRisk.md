## Definition

合伙关系边界风险 / partnership boundary risk is the management problem where closeness, brotherhood language, or present alignment makes collaborators underprice future conflicts over interest, envy, control, information, and changed circumstances.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-493-xiongdi-shi-hehuo-weisha-douhui-chouren-shi-sanhuo-lishi-gaosu-ni-llquknhihdms-6jyjbtfdfhvwv4g|Hanji 493]] builds this concept through analogy. The historical core is [[HouYinWesternHan|侯胤]] arguing that [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] should not remove border defenses merely because [[HuhanyeChanyuWesternHan|呼韩邪单于]] is currently submissive and offers protection. The host then maps the border-defense logic onto modern cooperation: trust and closeness are not substitutes for structure.

The current concept is therefore not anti-trust. It says that cooperation becomes safer when each side preserves rules, privacy, rhythm, and fallback capacity. A relationship can be warm and still need boundaries because future power, resources, jealousy, and interpretation may differ from the moment when the partnership began.

## Key Claims

- Early intimacy or shared hardship should not be treated as proof that incentives will remain aligned.
- Cooperation needs explicit boundaries around information, authority, money, responsibility, and rhythm.
- A partner's current dependence or gratitude can change if their leverage, status, or alternatives improve.
- Giving a collaborator control over a critical defense line can turn trust into ongoing bargaining dependency.
- Boundary maintenance is compatible with cooperation; it protects the relationship from preventable ambiguity and escalation.

## Evidence

- Historical analogy: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-493-xiongdi-shi-hehuo-weisha-douhui-chouren-shi-sanhuo-lishi-gaosu-ni-llquknhihdms-6jyjbtfdfhvwv4g|Hanji 493]] says Hou Yin warns that current Xiongnu humility comes from weakness and Han help, not permanent character.
- Critical-line delegation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-493-xiongdi-shi-hehuo-weisha-douhui-chouren-shi-sanhuo-lishi-gaosu-ni-llquknhihdms-6jyjbtfdfhvwv4g|Hanji 493]] says letting Huhanye guard the border could later become a reward-demanding claim.
- Modern cooperation frame: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-493-xiongdi-shi-hehuo-weisha-douhui-chouren-shi-sanhuo-lishi-gaosu-ni-llquknhihdms-6jyjbtfdfhvwv4g|Hanji 493]] explicitly applies the lesson to partners who begin like brothers but later become enemies.
- Practical boundary frame: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-493-xiongdi-shi-hehuo-weisha-douhui-chouren-shi-sanhuo-lishi-gaosu-ni-llquknhihdms-6jyjbtfdfhvwv4g|Hanji 493]] recommends keeping boundaries, privacy, one's own rhythm, and necessary caution.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept should not flatten all partnership into suspicion. The source's historical analogy is a podcast application, not a legal partnership manual, and its extreme poisoning example is not sufficiently detailed for independent fact treatment. The durable claim is narrower: close cooperation should retain safeguards because affection, dependence, and gratitude are variable, especially when money, authority, and future leverage are involved.
