# Battlefield Information Control / 战场信息控制

Battlefield Information Control / 战场信息控制 is the command pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-87-xialu-xiangfeng-yongzhe-sheng-zhongyu-zhaodao-chuchu-le-loppr83sdq5hhesmqihckwmhpl2h]] extracts from [[ZhaoShe|赵奢]]'s conduct before [[EyuBattle|阏与 / 燕宇之战]]. Zhao She camps close to [[Handan|邯郸]], strengthens defenses, forbids discussion of military affairs, and kills an officer who presses him to advance, creating an outward appearance of caution or delay.

The key move is that the appearance is allowed to travel. A [[QinState|秦国]] spy sees the fortified inaction, receives generous treatment, and returns with a report that Zhao will not rescue the besieged city quickly. Zhao She then immediately force-marches toward the battlefield, turning the enemy's intelligence channel into a deception channel.

The concept links discipline and deception. In this source, command secrecy is not merely silence; it is coordinated behavior that shapes what the opponent thinks is happening, while preserving the commander's freedom to move at the chosen time.

## Key Claims
- Information control can include what an enemy spy is permitted to see, not only what one's own soldiers are told.
- Strict speech discipline can be part of deception, especially before a rapid movement.
- Zhao She's case pairs information control with later tactical openness: he suppresses premature advice near Handan but accepts [[XuLiWarringStates|许历 / 许立]]'s grounded battlefield counsel near Eyu.
