# Ideal-to-Practice Conversion / 理想的实践转化

## Definition
Ideal-to-practice conversion / 理想的实践转化 is the movement from dissatisfaction, identity aspiration, or desire for recognition toward a specific activity that can be repeated, learned, and valued before public success arrives.

## Current Synthesis
The episode distinguishes wanting a richer life from knowing what one wants to keep doing. [[LiuXiaoyang|刘晓漾]] is portrayed as attracted to books, music, beauty, understanding, and “the outside” without one stable occupational or creative anchor. [[YuXiuhua|余秀华]], [[FanYusu|范雨素]], and [[XuYuanchongTranslator|许渊冲]] are used—imperfectly and source-scopingly—to show the different psychological order created when writing or translation precedes recognition. The concept does not require monetization or fame; it requires a practice concrete enough to survive modest beginnings, feedback, and delay.

## Key Claims
- Dissatisfaction can initiate change without specifying a workable destination.
- A durable ideal needs a concrete practice, learning loop, and tolerable next step.
- Recognition before practice can create an expectation debt in which each beginner setback feels like public failure.
- Fame and income may support a practice, but they are unstable substitutes for intrinsic or craft-centered motivation.
- Safety nets, time, domestic labor, teaching, feedback, and material access shape whether practice can continue.

## Evidence
- Vague-direction problem: [[ep179-womende-neixin-dou-zhuzhe-yige-liuxiaoyang-zuoziji-shi-yao-fuchu-juda-daijia-ckwriaset1ykabaaaadc008h]] distinguishes Liu’s powerful dissatisfaction from a clearly specified activity or destination.
- Practice-before-recognition comparison: [[ep179-womende-neixin-dou-zhuzhe-yige-liuxiaoyang-zuoziji-shi-yao-fuchu-juda-daijia-ckwriaset1ykabaaaadc008h]] contrasts Liu with Yu Xiuhua and Fan Yusu as writers whose practice preceded wider attention.
- Work-as-goal model: [[ep179-womende-neixin-dou-zhuzhe-yige-liuxiaoyang-zuoziji-shi-yao-fuchu-juda-daijia-ckwriaset1ykabaaaadc008h]] invokes Xu Yuanchong’s daily translation as a life organized around the activity itself.
- Recognition burden: [[ep179-womende-neixin-dou-zhuzhe-yige-liuxiaoyang-zuoziji-shi-yao-fuchu-juda-daijia-ckwriaset1ykabaaaadc008h]] links early fame and public hope to Liu’s fear of disappointing others and reduced room to begin modestly.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode’s comparisons compress different lives, resources, genres, bodies, and historical circumstances. Concrete practice does not guarantee recognition, livelihood, wellbeing, or freedom, and exploratory periods without a named craft can still be meaningful. The framework should not turn creative life into another demand for productivity or blame people whose time and resources are constrained.

## What Changed
- Established public recognition before craft development as a possible burden rather than an unqualified advantage.
- Separated concrete repeatable practice from both fame-seeking and productivity pressure.
