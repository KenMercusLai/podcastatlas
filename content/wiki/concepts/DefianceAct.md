# Defiance Act

The Defiance Act is the U.S. legislative remedy discussed in [[tech-20260109-0109-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260109-0109-mp-tech-pod-128]] as potentially more applicable to AI-enabled intimate-image abuse than a publication-only rule. The episode says it covers creation and solicitation and would allow individuals to sue.

In the source, the act fills a gap left by [[TakeItDownAct|Take It Down Act]] enforcement. When a user prompts a model to generate a non-consensual image, the core harm may begin before publication, so civil action around creation or solicitation can matter.

## Key Claims
- Creation and solicitation are legally important in AI image-abuse cases because the generated output may never have existed before the prompt.
- Private rights of action can give victims a route beyond waiting for criminal enforcement.
- The act is connected to [[ChatbotGeneratedContentLiability]] because a platform-integrated model can make creation and distribution part of one workflow.
- The source treats the act as a possible remedy to watch, not as a fully settled answer to all platform-AI abuse.
