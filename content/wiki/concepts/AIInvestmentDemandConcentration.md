# AI Investment Demand Concentration

## Definition
AI investment demand concentration is the risk that a small set of capital-intensive, externally financed buyers account for most model, compute, and infrastructure spending, making headline growth a weak proxy for broad profitable adoption.

## Current Synthesis
The source separates technological importance from the quality of present demand. A transformative technology can attract bubble-like investment, and scarce infrastructure can remain useful even when revenue is concentrated or financed by investors who expect future rather than current profits. The durable test is whether AI spending diffuses into mature organizations that can tie recurring use to productivity, revenue, or margin after compute, supervision, and financing costs.

## Key Claims
- A small buyer population can create rapid supplier growth without establishing broad end-customer demand.
- Venture funding can circulate through startups into model and compute purchases, then return as revenue evidence used in later financing.
- Useful technology, infrastructure scarcity, company solvency, and investor returns are separate questions.
- Durable diffusion requires customers with sustainable cash flow and measured economic outcomes.

## Evidence
### Concentration and capital circulation
- [[allin-e294-ch-allin-e294-ch]] reports the hosts' internal estimate that about 1% of firms account for 90% of AI spending and argues that many leading buyers remain venture-funded and unprofitable.

### Diffusion test
- [[allin-e294-ch-allin-e294-ch]] contrasts startup spending with adoption by mature profitable enterprises and treats that transition as the stronger evidence of lasting demand.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not disclose the dataset, definitions, sample, time window, or method behind the 1% and 90% figures. Concentration may be normal during infrastructure buildout, and venture finance can fund real experimentation before profits appear. The concept does not establish a bubble, forecast a crash, or show that current AI revenue is circular in an accounting sense.

## What Changed
- Established a distinction between rapid AI supplier revenue and broad, outcome-backed demand.
