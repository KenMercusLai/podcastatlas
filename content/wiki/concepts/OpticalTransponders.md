# Optical Transponders

Optical transponders are the devices highlighted in [[tech-20260126-0126-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260126-0126-mp-tech-pod-128]] as part of [[AmazonWebServices|AWS]]'s AI networking hardware stack. [[SatishVangala]] explains that they convert electrical signals, or data, into light waves that travel over fiber optic cable.

In the wiki's infrastructure branch, transponders are useful because they make [[AIClusterNetworking]] visibly physical. AI data movement is not only a routing or software issue; it depends on components that translate data into optical signals, move it through fiber, and keep large clusters communicating reliably.

## Key Claims
- Optical transponders convert electrical data signals into light waves for fiber transmission.
- They sit in the component layer between compute hardware and fiber network capacity.
- Their deployment and reliability affect whether AI cluster networks can scale without becoming a bottleneck.
- The Marketplace Tech source treats transponders as one of many small hardware innovations behind large AI infrastructure investment.
