# Inspirational Narrative Commercialization / 励志叙事商业化

Inspirational narrative commercialization / 励志叙事商业化 is the episode's warning about what happens when [[SuQin|苏秦]]'s biography becomes a reusable "knowledge changes fate" story. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-29-suqin-lizhi-shuangwen-zhishi-gaibian-mingyun-lrf5cqvoriyep2lkbozju-lyjaxv]] says that because many people want to challenge fate, Su Qin can become a life mentor figure, and surrounding symbols acquire commercial value.

The concept does not reject inspiration. It marks the gap between a complex historical story and the packaged promise that study, humiliation, and eventual status reversal can be turned into a simple product or script. [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s poem to [[WuZhicai|吾之才]] shows that this reuse is old, while modern success-culture language makes it easier to commodify.

## Key Claims
- A historical life can become a motivational template once later readers recognize their own frustration in it.
- Commercialization tends to compress uncertainty, chronology, failure, and social pressure into a cleaner ascent arc.
- Reception history matters because a story's later uses can become part of its practical cultural meaning.
