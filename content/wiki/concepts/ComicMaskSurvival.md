# 搞笑面具式生存 / Comic Mask Survival

Comic mask survival is the source's pattern where joking, clowning, and self-humiliation become a defensive social technology. In [[33-renjian-shige-danxiaogui-de-zhuanglie-xianji-599924624]], [[ObaYozo|大庭叶藏]] uses "搞笑" to keep family members, servants, classmates, and teachers from seeing his fear or punishing his mismatch with ordinary expectations.

The episode makes the mask tragic because it works. Yozo can calm rooms, satisfy his father, entertain classmates, and avoid confrontation, but every successful performance teaches him that unmasked existence is impossible. [[TakeichiNoLongerHuman|竹一]]'s sentence that the performance is intentional becomes terrifying because it exposes the one tactic that had let Yozo survive.

## Key Claims
- Humor can be a survival mask when direct need, anger, refusal, or sadness feels unsafe.
- The mask can protect social contact while deepening self-erasure.
- Being seen through is not automatically relief; for someone dependent on performance, recognition can feel like danger.
- The pattern links individual shame to [[CoercedNormality|normality coercion]]: the more the environment punishes awkwardness or refusal, the more the mask becomes necessary.
