## Definition

Zero-sum conflict hesitation risk / 死斗局优柔寡断风险 is the timing failure that appears when an actor still seeks procedural, indirect, or reversible solutions after the other side has begun treating the conflict as existential.

## Current Synthesis

The current synthesis has two late-Han anti-eunuch cases. In 168 CE, [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-885-shengsi-guantou-ta-qingjia-xiuxi-luydnh9oqdyyfpbeztooi2e7lvca|Hanji 885]] shows [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] losing tempo before the plan formally leaks: [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦妙 / 窦皇太后]] hesitates, [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] urges open confrontation, [[LiuYuAstrologerLateHan|刘瑜]] warns of palace danger, [[ZhengSaLateHan|郑飒 / 郑萨]] is interrogated, and Dou still leaves the palace on September 7. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-886-shenchu-zhichang-zuoge-chengfu-henshen-de-ren-lo6dgcdbshjgto-qsfcouwjersdi|Hanji 886]] then shows Dou losing the initiative before open battle: his anti-eunuch plan leaks, he is away from the palace, and the eunuch side moves faster to control [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]], Dou Miao, gates, seals, tallies, and edict production. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-887-dang-zhuduizhang-pengshang-zhuduiyou-jieguo-taican-lvyh9a-kvsdqaokmylgg139bvhfk|Hanji 887]] presents Dou's remaining military response as too late: Chen Fan is killed, [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]] controls accusation language, and Zhang Huan's uninformed troops help reverse Dou's army.

In 189 CE, the [[HeJin|何进]] sequence shows the same risk at larger scale. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-944-xiang-zai-luanshi-zhong-cunhuo-xian-guan-zhu-zui-lp7omiak9mpcigrj4f1nummfwntr|Hanji 944]] shows an exposed anti-eunuch plan before execution, [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-945-zhinian-tai-shen-de-ren-zhihui-yuelaiyue-tongku-lm6xaiy2pcqunke04yzqwwxsxxl|Hanji 945]] shows He Jin trying to solve the palace problem through outside pressure and dowager persuasion, and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-946-zhichang-douzheng-shi-yourouguaduan-you-duo-zhiming-lpyyweaajonzg7kxuhwm9z7ae7p5|Hanji 946]] shows [[ZhangRangLateHan|张让]] and [[DuanGuiLateHan|段珪]] exploiting palace access, forged orders, and faster violence.

The concept is therefore not a general praise of aggression. It names the point at which delay, leaked intent, soft handling, and indirect escalation create danger because the target still has enough access, information, symbols, and incentives to strike first.

## Key Claims

- Delay is dangerous when it exposes existential intent without removing the opponent's ability to act.
- Procedure can become hesitation when evidence-gathering, memorial routing, and leave-taking continue after the conflict has become a live palace-security struggle.
- Formal authority and visible troops do not offset poor execution if the opponent controls rooms, summonses, information, symbols, and accusation language.
- Indirect escalation can be a form of hesitation: it widens the crisis while postponing the decisive move.
- Once a conflict has become lethal, boundary-setting and procedural persuasion come too late if they leave the target faction intact.
- The 168 Dou Wu case and the 189 He Jin case differ in scale, but both attach hesitation to plan leakage, palace access, and faster counteraction.
- The concept remains source-scoped; the sources diagnose timing failure without proving that speed alone would have guaranteed success.

## Evidence

- Procedural delay before the leak: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-885-shengsi-guantou-ta-qingjia-xiuxi-luydnh9oqdyyfpbeztooi2e7lvca|Hanji 885]] says Dou Wu persists with interrogation and memorial procedure after Zheng Sa's arrest, while Chen Fan argues for immediate killing and Liu Yu's omen warning frames the palace as already dangerous.
- Initiative lost before open battle: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-886-shenchu-zhichang-zuoge-chengfu-henshen-de-ren-lo6dgcdbshjgto-qsfcouwjersdi|Hanji 886]] says Dou Wu's plan is known to the eunuch side before he has secured the emperor, dowager, seals, gates, or edict channel.
- Exposed intent before execution: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-887-dang-zhuduizhang-pengshang-zhuduiyou-jieguo-taican-lvyh9a-kvsdqaokmylgg139bvhfk|Hanji 887]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-944-xiang-zai-luanshi-zhong-cunhuo-xian-guan-zhu-zui-lp7omiak9mpcigrj4f1nummfwntr|Hanji 944]] both treat anti-eunuch intent as dangerous once the target faction has warning but remains operational.
- Incomplete or indirect action: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-887-dang-zhuduizhang-pengshang-zhuduiyou-jieguo-taican-lvyh9a-kvsdqaokmylgg139bvhfk|Hanji 887]] frames Dou Wu's delay as fatal, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-945-zhinian-tai-shen-de-ren-zhihui-yuelaiyue-tongku-lm6xaiy2pcqunke04yzqwwxsxxl|Hanji 945]] frames He Jin's outside-force summons and dowager pressure as avoidance of direct resolution.
- Faster palace counteraction: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-886-shenchu-zhichang-zuoge-chengfu-henshen-de-ren-lo6dgcdbshjgto-qsfcouwjersdi|Hanji 886]] shows Cao Jie and Wang Fu seizing the palace-symbol field before Dou rallies troops, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-946-zhichang-douzheng-shi-yourouguaduan-you-duo-zhiming-lpyyweaajonzg7kxuhwm9z7ae7p5|Hanji 946]] shows Zhang Rang and Duan Gui luring and killing He Jin.
- Access and information advantage: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-944-xiang-zai-luanshi-zhong-cunhuo-xian-guan-zhu-zui-lp7omiak9mpcigrj4f1nummfwntr|Hanji 944]], [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-945-zhinian-tai-shen-de-ren-zhihui-yuelaiyue-tongku-lm6xaiy2pcqunke04yzqwwxsxxl|Hanji 945]], and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-946-zhichang-douzheng-shi-yourouguaduan-you-duo-zhiming-lpyyweaajonzg7kxuhwm9z7ae7p5|Hanji 946]] show eunuch-side family channels, palace familiarity, and forged orders outpacing formal office.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The sources do not prove that immediate killing or purging would have solved either crisis. They show that delay became dangerous after the conflict had already been made lethal and the target faction still possessed access and information channels. Hanji 885's criticism of Dou Wu's procedure and leave-taking remains a source interpretation of crisis tempo, not a general rule against process. Hanji 946's modern "打直球" analogy should stay lower-stakes and source-scoped; this concept is about survival conflicts, not ordinary disagreement.

## What Changed

- Hanji 885 adds the pre-leak delay phase to the Dou Wu case before the 886 leakage and 887 troop reversal.
- The synthesis now treats hesitation as a cluster of delay, exposed intent, partial action, symbolic-control loss, and indirect escalation.
- The page distinguishes crisis-tempo diagnosis from any blanket endorsement of impulsive violence.
