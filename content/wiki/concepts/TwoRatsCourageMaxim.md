# Two Rats Courage Maxim / 两鼠斗穴勇者胜

Two Rats Courage Maxim / 两鼠斗穴勇者胜 is the phrase [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-87-xialu-xiangfeng-yongzhe-sheng-zhongyu-zhaodao-chuchu-le-loppr83sdq5hhesmqihckwmhpl2h]] uses to explain [[ZhaoShe|赵奢]]'s decision to rescue [[EyuBattle|阏与 / 燕宇之战]]. When [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] asks whether the distant, narrow route can be crossed against [[QinState|秦国]], Zhao She compares the situation to two rats fighting in a hole: the braver general wins.

The episode treats this as the source-layer behind the later idiom "狭路相逢勇者胜." The concept should not be flattened into raw aggression. In this source, bravery becomes effective only because Zhao She pairs it with deception, timing, rapid movement, fortified encampment, and willingness to seize the decisive terrain before Qin.

## Key Claims
- Narrow terrain can reduce the advantage of the stronger or better-positioned side.
- Courage matters most when retreat, maneuver, or avoidance is constrained.
- In this episode, courage is operationalized through preparation and timing, not detached from them.
