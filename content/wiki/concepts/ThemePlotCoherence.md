# Theme-Plot Coherence

## Definition

Theme-plot coherence is the degree to which a story's causal events, character decisions, and revelations adequately support the meaning its ending asks the audience to accept.

## Current Synthesis

[[121-quhua-qizhongwuqi-zhi-kongqueling-gulong-xiegei-chengnianrende-xinxin-yuyan-lixp1oc4wx0wiksvzo4zehqj45b4]] supplies a useful split judgment. The hosts find [[PeacockFeatherGuLong|《孔雀翎》]]'s central claim—that confidence, rather than an invincible object, enables victory—clear and affecting. They nevertheless question why 高立 must borrow the manor's defining secret, how an expert killer loses it without noticing, why 秋凤梧 reveals the deeper truth, and why secrecy requires the apparent murder of a friend already trusted with so much.

The resulting concept avoids two flattenings: a plot gap need not erase a story's thematic insight, and an attractive theme does not automatically repair weak motivation. Reader theories can propose missing causes, but they should remain speculation unless the text supplies setup.

## Key Claims

- Thematic clarity and causal plausibility are separate dimensions of narrative success.
- A revelation is strongest when earlier action makes both its surprise and its consequences credible.
- Character decisions require motivation proportionate to the moral and practical stakes.
- Coincidence becomes more costly when it carries the entire thematic turn or tragic ending.
- Reader repair should not be promoted to textual fact without prior setup or corroboration.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Narrative plausibility depends partly on genre convention, pacing, edition, and reader tolerance for allegory. The source is a conversational response rather than a systematic comparison with the complete 《七种武器》 sequence. The claim that 高立 dies is based on converging implication rather than explicit narration, although the hosts regard the implication as strong.
