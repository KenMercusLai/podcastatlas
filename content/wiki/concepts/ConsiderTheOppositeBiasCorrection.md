# Consider-The-Opposite Bias Correction / 反向设想偏见校正

## Definition
Consider-the-opposite bias correction is a structured practice of generating reasons, evidence, or a causal account for a competing position before deciding how strongly the initial interpretation is supported.

## Current Synthesis
[[123-renxing-shiyan-dangxin-ni-ziji-779479569|Episode 123]] distinguishes this practice from the weak instruction to “be objective.” A general aspiration leaves the preferred interpretation intact, while constructing the opposing case forces the same evidence to be viewed from another standpoint. The method targets partisan evaluation, overconfidence, and the tendency to experience motivated judgment as neutral.

[[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-d08500083dda91b814b053f30bd7b557-87666361-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-d08500083dda91b814b053f30bd7b557-87666361|The Story of Stories]] extends the mechanism from evidence weighting to explanation. People do not spontaneously search for ways their preferred story might be wrong, so constructing an alternative explanation can expose gaps, exceptions, and hidden assumptions. The strongest use pairs rival-account generation with detailed mechanism, source checking, and a stated condition for revision.

## Key Claims
- Abstract reminders to be objective are often too vague to change judgment.
- Bias correction becomes concrete when a person generates reasons the opposing side or rival explanation could fit the evidence.
- The method is especially useful when a claim confirms political identity, stereotype, or a satisfying causal story.
- Rival generation can expose knowledge gaps and unsupported assumptions without requiring emotional neutrality.
- Correction remains incomplete unless alternatives are checked against evidence and the initial view is genuinely revisable.

## Evidence
- Partisan evaluation - [[123-renxing-shiyan-dangxin-ni-ziji-779479569]] reports that “be objective” did not meaningfully reduce bias in the discussed capital-punishment study, while consider-the-opposite instruction performed better.
- Explanatory alternatives - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-d08500083dda91b814b053f30bd7b557-87666361-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-d08500083dda91b814b053f30bd7b557-87666361]] recommends generating arguments for competing positions because people rarely search spontaneously for ways they may be wrong.
- Knowledge calibration - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-d08500083dda91b814b053f30bd7b557-87666361-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-d08500083dda91b814b053f30bd7b557-87666361]] pairs alternative explanation with detailed self-explanation and asking how a belief is known.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Generating an opposite does not make it equally plausible, guarantee debiasing, or replace domain knowledge. A person can construct a weak straw alternative, rationalize the original view more skillfully, or become falsely balanced when evidence is asymmetric. The supplied notes summarize studies without full methods, effect sizes, or boundary conditions, so effectiveness remains context-dependent.
