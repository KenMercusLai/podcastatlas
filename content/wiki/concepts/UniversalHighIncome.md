# Universal High Income

Universal high income is [[ElonMusk]]'s distribution answer in [[an-interview-with-elon-musk-6a6212214fac21e67f9b8c8c]] for a future where AI and robots produce more goods and services than people can consume. It is a stronger version of universal basic income: Musk argues that if output grows faster than the money supply, the state could issue checks without the usual inflation problem becoming binding.

The concept sits inside [[AIAbundanceNarrative]]. It treats taxes, wages, and redistribution as transitional structures on the way to a post-scarcity economy, but the interview leaves unresolved how societies would cross the transition period when jobs, ownership, political legitimacy, and claims on production are still unequal.

## Key Claims
- Universal high income depends on rapid physical-world productivity, not only better software.
- The claim assumes deflationary abundance from [[PhysicalAI]], [[HumanoidRobotCommercialization]], and automation at scale.
- The transition problem remains political: people lose income before abundance may be broadly available.
- The idea shifts the policy question from minimum subsistence to who controls and distributes AI-produced surplus.
