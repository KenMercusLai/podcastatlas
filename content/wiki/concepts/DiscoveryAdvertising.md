# Discovery Advertising

## Definition
Discovery advertising is advertising designed to introduce a product or experience before the consumer has formed explicit purchase intent, in contrast with search advertising that responds to a known need or query.

## Current Synthesis
The AppLovin source treats discovery advertising as recommendation-led demand creation. Mobile-game users already exchange attention for in-game rewards, and better models may use that moment to recommend shopping experiences rather than only other games. The strategic claim is that this can create incremental transactions, but the boundary between useful discovery and manufactured desire depends on relevance, transparency, targeting inputs, user agency, and credible incrementality measurement.

## Key Claims
- Search advertising usually captures articulated intent, while discovery advertising tries to create or surface a desire that was not yet expressed.
- Recommendation quality can make an ad feel closer to useful content, especially when the format is interactive or entertaining.
- Mobile-game rewarded inventory may become a commerce surface if product recommendations convert beyond game-to-game acquisition.
- Discovery advertising can be economically additive only if it produces transactions that would not otherwise have occurred.
- Relevance can improve the attention exchange, but it does not by itself resolve privacy, manipulation, or attribution concerns.

## Evidence
- Intent distinction: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-adam-foroughi-applovin-ceo-surviving-a-92-drawdown-ads-as-ml-1-0-the-50b-game-ad-market-42967913]] contrasts Google-style search intent with AppLovin- and Meta-style product discovery.
- Inventory expansion: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-adam-foroughi-applovin-ceo-surviving-a-92-drawdown-ads-as-ml-1-0-the-50b-game-ad-market-42967913]] says AppLovin historically recommended one game from another and now wants its models to recommend broader shopping experiences.
- Ad-as-content claim: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-adam-foroughi-applovin-ceo-surviving-a-92-drawdown-ads-as-ml-1-0-the-50b-game-ad-market-42967913]] points to Instagram shopping influence and playable game ads as examples where relevant ads can function like engaging recommendations.
- Incrementality claim: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-adam-foroughi-applovin-ceo-surviving-a-92-drawdown-ads-as-ml-1-0-the-50b-game-ad-market-42967913]] argues that discovery is more additive than intent capture when it creates a purchase that otherwise would not happen.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode does not provide independent incrementality studies, user research, or evidence that mobile-game attention transfers reliably into non-game commerce. A purchase attributed to discovery may have occurred through another channel, while a highly relevant recommendation can also narrow consumer choice or exploit personal data. The economic claim therefore depends on causal measurement, not only conversion correlation.

## What Changed
- Created the concept from AppLovin's contrast between intent capture and demand creation.
- Added the mobile-game-to-commerce expansion hypothesis.
- Preserved privacy, agency, and attribution as unresolved limits.
