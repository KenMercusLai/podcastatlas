# Short Drama Platform Aggregation / 短剧平台聚合

## Definition
Short-drama platform aggregation is the strategy of placing independently supplied short dramas behind a first-party discovery entrance while the platform coordinates traffic, viewing, advertising, and links across its ecosystem.

## Current Synthesis
Aggregation can turn many disconnected mini-program audiences into a more legible market without requiring the platform to produce the dramas itself. The platform gains control over discovery and monetization, providers gain reach, and users gain a free entrance, but the model's durability still depends on retention, ad economics, content quality, revenue sharing, and governance.

## Key Claims
- Distribution control can matter independently of content ownership.
- Free viewing supported by inter-episode ads lowers the consumer payment barrier while making attention and ad yield central to unit economics.
- First-party aggregation may increase the platform's bargaining power relative to both small providers and standalone short-drama applications.
- Cross-surface links can convert existing social or video traffic into serialized viewing.

## Evidence
- Aggregation without production: [[gaojiesi-weixin-shangxian-mianfei-duanju-xiaochengxu-zhongguo-dui-oumeng-de-hundongche-chukou-yuji-jianshao-1023591374]] describes [[GreenBubbleDrama|绿泡泡短剧]] as an official pool supplied by external mini-programs.
- Free-and-ad-supported distribution: [[gaojiesi-weixin-shangxian-mianfei-duanju-xiaochengxu-zhongguo-dui-oumeng-de-hundongche-chukou-yuji-jianshao-1023591374]] says viewers watch free while advertising appears between episodes.
- Audience consolidation: [[gaojiesi-weixin-shangxian-mianfei-duanju-xiaochengxu-zhongguo-dui-oumeng-de-hundongche-chukou-yuji-jianshao-1023591374]] reports 427 million monthly active users across WeChat short-drama mini-programs and presents an official entrance as a response to fragmentation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not establish unique viewers, retention, provider concentration, ad pricing, revenue shares, or whether aggregation improves total ecosystem profitability. Centralized discovery can help small suppliers, but it can also deepen dependency on platform ranking and monetization rules.

## What Changed
- Added WeChat's official mini-program pool as a focused platform-aggregation case.
- Distinguished distribution ownership from content production.
