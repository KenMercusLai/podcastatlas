# 工具化宗教权力 / Instrumentalized Religious Power

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-923-chengnianrende-dingji-henjin-guzi-lide-juejue-lifzh-gohpqnmdznnfv72tndqf8h|Hanji 923]] adds the [[TaipingDao|太平道]] origin layer that Hanji 995 had only used as a comparator. In this source, [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]]'s healing work during epidemic and poverty pressure becomes [[ReliefBasedReligiousMobilization|救济型宗教动员]]: practical help and sacred authority create trust, then the thirty-six `fang`, "甲子" marks, and [[MaYuanyiLateHan|马元义]]'s palace-contact plan turn the movement into rebellion infrastructure.

工具化宗教权力 / instrumentalized religious power is the pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-995-ta-zuishang-cibei-weihe-duoci-cansha-enren-llicukoezccd5iycin4uq7qun51k|Hanji 995]] extracts from [[ZeRong|笮融]]. Ze Rong uses the grain office entrusted by [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] to build Buddhist temples at [[Xiapi|下邳]], order sutra recitation, grant exemptions to Buddhist households, and hold mass bathing-Buddha feasts tied to [[ShakyamuniBuddha|释迦牟尼佛]].

The concept does not claim that religion itself is false or violent. It names the danger that appears when religious symbols, charity, ritual, and belonging become an organizational shell for coercion, plunder, or private following. Ze Rong's later murders of [[ZhaoYuLateHan|赵昱]], [[XueLiLateHan|薛礼]], and [[ZhuHaoLateHan|朱皓]] turn his pious surface into a diagnostic contrast: the visible doctrine does not constrain his actual action.

Hanji 995 uses [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]]'s [[TaipingDao|太平道]] and [[ZhangLuFivePecks|张鲁]]'s [[WudoumiDao|五斗米道]] as late-Han comparators for religious mobilization. They are not treated as equivalent to Ze Rong; their role is to show that religion can become an organizing medium in a collapsing order, while Ze Rong shows a more predatory form of that medium.

## Key Claims
- Hanji 923 adds that religious power can grow first through practical relief or cure claims in a disaster environment, before it becomes openly military or political.
- Religious ritual can become political infrastructure when it organizes people, food, exemption, and loyalty.
- A moral or compassionate vocabulary is not reliable evidence of moral constraint.
- Critiquing instrumentalized religion is different from rejecting all religious meaning.
- Comparator cases should be bounded: [[TaipingDao|太平道]] and [[WudoumiDao|五斗米道]] contextualize late-Han mobilization without making every movement Ze Rong-like.
- The pattern connects to [[InstrumentalBeliefCritique]] when doctrine becomes usable cover rather than binding commitment.
