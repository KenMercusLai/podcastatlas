# Population-Level Health Advice

Population-level health advice is guidance derived from large groups that should inform but not mechanically dictate individual behavior. [[the-case-of-the-missing-totem-aung-san-suu-kyi-6a560fc4f96471dde4821d2f]] adds it through sleep-duration research, where the source gives recommended ranges while repeatedly warning against exact personal optimization.

The episode's key discipline is translation. [[UKBiobank]] and [[OrganSpecificAgingClock|organ-specific aging clocks]] can estimate a [[SleepDurationUShape]], but [[JunhaoWen]] warns that cause and effect are difficult to separate and [[MichaelGrandner]] stresses that different body systems may have different needs.

## Key Claims
- Large studies can identify useful ranges and risks while still being weak for individual prescription.
- Associations can reflect reverse causality or third variables, especially in health behavior.
- Advice should reduce harmful extremes without creating unnecessary anxiety around normal variation.
- Practical guidance should include subjective body signals and behavior context, not only cohort averages.
