# Investment Behavior Coaching / 投资行为陪伴

## Definition
Investment behavior coaching is ongoing support that helps an investor connect goals and suitability to a holdable process, then keep decisions aligned with that process through temptation, uncertainty, and drawdowns.

## Current Synthesis
The bounded evidence now spans formal fund advisory, portfolio-product communication, self-coaching, and individualized one-to-one work. Across those settings, coaching is not primarily a better prediction service. It begins before a crisis by clarifying what the money is for, what loss path is acceptable, what the product or strategy actually does, and which decisions should be slowed, recorded, or reviewed on a schedule.

E250 adds the strongest autonomy boundary. A coach can supply questions, history, emotional support, and process friction, but should not replace the investor's judgment or responsibility. The endpoint is not permanent dependence on reassurance; it is a person who can explain and execute a suitable system independently, including the choice to simplify participation or decline advanced investing.

## Key Claims
- Coaching creates value when it improves actual holding behavior, not merely when it supplies a portfolio or more information.
- Goals, life stage, capital obligations, product mechanics, and tolerable drawdown must be credible before encouragement to hold can be responsible.
- Trust is most useful when built before volatility, but trust in a person must eventually become understanding of the plan rather than substitute for it.
- Effective coaching can include market explanation, written reasons, cooldown periods, scheduled review, environmental friction, and emotional support during drawdowns.
- Individualized coaching can legitimately conclude that simpler investing, basic financial management, or nonparticipation is the better fit.
- The investor retains final decisions, losses, and responsibility; coaching is not a guarantee of return or a license to defend an unsuitable product.

## Evidence
### Suitability before holding support
- [[145-jijin-tougu-zhide-xinren-ma-lrckug0zjqolcczni8ajikb0k5mi]] argues that goal-based profiling and conflict disclosure must precede post-purchase accompaniment.
- [[suoyou-jingzhi-quxian-beihou-doushi-ren-zhengtai-fenbu-de-putongren-lsnrkqhtktjlmrscow2-mt2df0yq]] treats drawdown budget, entry date, and client psychological floor as product-design inputs rather than after-the-fact communication problems.

### Process and self-coaching
- [[166-putongren-neng-cong-jigou-touzizhe-shenshang-xuedao-shenme-chuantai-touzi-abc-lpg8zl95kbvjvxsjr8cpb8ogkeg6]] recommends lower decision frequency, cooldown periods, written reasons for large weights, and scheduled review.
- [[ep239-he-daka-liaoliao-jinjia-you-shuang-ruo-zhuo-biao-le-putongren-hai-neng-shangche-ma-lgcy2trgzt7expvr5bcybybrummq]] frames gold-product support around recognition, entry, and holding difficulty while retaining sizing and suitability boundaries.

### Autonomy and one-to-one accompaniment
- [[e250-weishenme-xuele-zheme-duo-zhishi-que-haishi-zuobuhao-touzi-lqb8zb64t2lk_xh-kdazq8hnlghq]] describes long-term individualized dialogue that begins with life purpose, can recommend doing less, and aims for independent action rather than delegated control.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Behavior support can become sales pressure, personality dependence, or a way to keep clients in unsuitable products. The sources mix product managers, advisers, podcast educators, and a self-described coach; their legal duties, incentives, evidence, and authority are not interchangeable. None establishes that reassurance improves risk-adjusted returns, and E250's client outcomes are anecdotal. A coach's confidence cannot validate a strategy or override fiduciary, licensing, disclosure, and suitability requirements.

## What Changed
- Reframed coaching as a path from relational trust toward investor autonomy, not permanent reliance on reassurance.
- Added the legitimate endpoint of simpler participation or no advanced investing.
- Added one-to-one questioning, complete-cycle accompaniment, and explicit retention of investor responsibility.
