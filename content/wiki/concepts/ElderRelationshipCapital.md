# Elder Relationship Capital / 老年关系资本

Elder relationship capital is the episode's idea that trusted friends, relatives, neighbors, and community ties are late-life assets built through years of attention. In [[vol-118-danshen-renshi-yanglao-zhinan-bushi-gudu-de-daimingci-shi-gengzao-zhangwo-rensheng-de-zhudongquan-lhvkuveznxd4citlwf5marzaj-wt]], the speakers compare relationships to accounts that need deposits before they can support old age.

The concept is not a claim that friends should become unpaid carers. Its point is that [[ActiveSoloLiving]] still requires people who notice absence, share information, provide emotional recognition, understand preferences, and can be trusted around decisions that family default roles no longer cover.

## Key Claims
- Deep relationships do not appear automatically at retirement; they require time, reciprocity, and maintenance.
- Friendship, relatives, neighbors, and community ties can partly replace the social functions once assumed to come from spouse and children.
- Work relationships may fade after retirement, so relying only on professional networks creates late-life fragility.
- The episode treats male friendship as a special risk when it remains activity-based but avoids vulnerability and practical dependence.
- Relationship capital reduces the danger that living alone becomes social isolation.
