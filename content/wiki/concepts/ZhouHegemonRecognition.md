# Zhou Hegemon Recognition / 周室霸权承认

Zhou hegemon recognition / 周室霸权承认 is the episode's reading of "王致伯于秦" in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-18-zhongguo-lishishang-di-yi-li-rentoushui-lgqnequk0rxejqgihwdqxqyqnsvq]]. The host explains the phrase as the [[ZhouRoyalHouse|Zhou king]] conferring on [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]] the status of senior lord or hegemon among the vassals.

The concept extends earlier Zhou-Qin legitimacy patterns. [[KinshipLegitimacyDiplomacy]] shows Zhou trying to bind Qin through old origin language, while [[RitualRecognitionOfMilitaryPower]] shows Zhou recognizing Qin's battlefield prestige through material rank. Here, Zhou recognition becomes more explicit: [[QinState|秦国]] is elevated within the interstate order even though substantive power has already shifted away from the royal center.

## Key Claims
- A weak ritual center can still upgrade a rising power's public status.
- Recognition is not the same as control; Zhou can name hierarchy without commanding the battlefield.
- Qin Xiao Gong's absence from the ceremony does not erase the recognition, because [[GongziShaoguan|公子少官]] represents Qin with troops at [[Fengze|逢泽]].
- The episode reads the event as a major status signal before the next year's [[MalingBattle|马陵之战]] reshapes the interstate map again.
