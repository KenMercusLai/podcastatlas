# Love As Containing Frame / 爱作为包裹性框架

## Definition
Love as containing frame / 爱作为包裹性框架 is [[WuZhenyu|吴镇宇]]'s metaphor that love is not one more emotion used to fill a box, but the relation that can wrap the box containing desire, fear, joy, grief, anger, evil, and pain.

## Current Synthesis
In [[ep281-duihua-wuzhenyu-kun-zai-chuangshang-li-de-women-jueding-zhongxin-zhaohui-ziji-lvvreapoykpj5xtlryaaupu1xo7r]], the episode's emotion-card structure moves through desire, fear, joy, grief, anger, evil, and love. Wu's closing move is to refuse treating love as a simple positive feeling that replaces the darker ones. Instead, he frames the other emotions as tools for recognizing love, and love as something one can return to and receive in two directions.

For the wiki, the concept helps connect [[ChanDrama|《蝉》]]'s trauma, aging, temporary family, and self-search threads. Love does not erase Yu Songfan's suicidal despair, Ding Ning's childhood shadow, or the drama's moral grayness. It makes a relation possible in which those states can be held without becoming the only truth about the person.

## Key Claims
- Love is not simply the opposite of fear, grief, anger, or evil; it can contain and reframe them.
- The other emotions can become ways of discovering what love is protecting, seeking, or failing to reach.
- Love has a return structure: it can be given and received rather than only felt internally.
- In trauma stories, love is reparative only when it holds pain without pretending the pain has disappeared.
- Naming love too loudly may be less important than making it ordinary, ambient, and trustworthy.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept is a reflective metaphor from one interview, not a psychological model or moral rule. It should not be used to excuse harm, demand reconciliation, or treat love as sufficient where safety, treatment, boundaries, or accountability are needed.
