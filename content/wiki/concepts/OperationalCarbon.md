# Operational Carbon

Operational carbon is the building-emissions category contrasted with [[EmbodiedCarbonBuildingCode]] in [[tech-20260420-0420-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260420-0420-mp-tech-pod-128]]. The episode describes it as emissions from operating a building, such as air conditioning and lighting, which have historically received more attention in green-building work.

The concept matters because it explains why embodied carbon feels like a new policy frontier. A building can use cleaner electricity, solar panels, or efficient systems and still carry a large construction-carbon footprint from steel, concrete, insulation, paint, and other materials.

## Key Claims
- Operational carbon comes from energy use while a building is in service.
- It differs from embodied carbon, which comes from construction and materials.
- Green-building work historically focused more on operational emissions than on material emissions.
- Building climate policy needs both categories because efficiency after occupancy cannot erase every construction choice.
