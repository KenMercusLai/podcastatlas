# Ocean Heat Monitoring

Ocean heat monitoring is the measurement layer in [[taking-the-shine-off-albedo-and-global-temperatures-6a730fdaca067b295bab9760]] that verifies [[EarthEnergyImbalance]] outside satellite calibration alone. [[OliverMorton]] says most absorbed energy goes into the oceans, so ocean temperature, salinity, and depth measurements can confirm whether the planet is retaining more heat.

The source names [[ArgoFloats]] as the key post-2000 system. Their data tracks closely with [[NASA]] satellite measurements, making ocean heat a practical check on [[AlbedoFeedback]] and falling reflectivity.

## Key Claims
- Ocean heat is a better whole-system signal than air temperature alone because oceans store most excess heat.
- Independent ocean measurements help validate satellite estimates of energy imbalance.
- The Argo-era record makes recent ocean heat less dependent on isolated ship or station observations.
