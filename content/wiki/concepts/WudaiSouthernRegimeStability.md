# 五代南方政权相对稳定 / Southern Regime Stability in the Five Dynasties

Southern regime stability in the Five Dynasties names [[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]]'s contrast between heavily disrupted northern phases and comparatively stable southern states. The source does not romanticize the south, but it argues that lower war intensity there mattered historically.

This stability helped make [[EconomicCulturalCenterSouthwardShift]] possible. Regimes such as [[Wuyue|吴越]], [[FormerShu|前蜀]], [[LaterShu|后蜀]], [[SouthernTang|南唐]], and [[SouthernHan|南汉]] could preserve local administration, wealth, artifacts, and culture while central-plain regimes rose and fell quickly.
