# Unverified Territorial Promise Trap / 未验地先断交陷阱

Unverified territorial promise trap / 未验地先断交陷阱 is the diplomatic failure pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-50-zhangyi-shi-zenyang-ba-chuhuaiwang-huyou-que-de-loxg3ylhmskvdm-axtco5y-acege]] extracts from [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]'s deception of [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]]. Zhang Yi promises [[ChuState|楚国]] six hundred li of [[Shangyu|商於]] if Chu cuts ties with [[QiState|齐国]], but the promised benefit is not verified before Chu destroys the alliance relationship that made Qin willing to bargain.

The trap has two stages. First, a huge prospective gain makes the target act before delivery; second, the target's own follow-up actions make reversal harder. In this source, Chu Huaiwang does not merely announce a break with Qi: after Zhang Yi delays, Chu sends Song Yi to insult [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]], pushing Qi toward [[QinState|秦国]] and making the original alliance much harder to repair.

[[ChenZhenWarringStates|陈枕]] names the correct countermeasure inside the episode. He does not say Chu can never bargain with Qin; he says Chu should preserve a hidden Qi tie and send an envoy with Zhang Yi until the land is actually transferred. The concept therefore belongs as much to risk sequencing as to deception: irreversible concessions should wait for verifiable delivery.

The concept differs from [[StrategicConcessionDiplomacy|欲擒故纵式让利外交]]. In the earlier Zhang Yi cases, Qin gives back a real smaller asset from a position of strength to extract a larger concession. Here the promised asset is the bait, and the target pays first by weakening its own alliance position.

## Key Claims
- The dangerous move is not considering a Qin-Chu bargain; it is sacrificing the Qi-Chu alliance before Qin delivers the promised land.
- Very large promised upside can make verification feel like obstruction rather than prudence.
- The target's attempts to prove sincerity can deepen the trap after the deceiver delays.
- A hedge can preserve optionality: outward posture, private alliance maintenance, and delivery verification can be separated.
- Once the target has offended both sides, post-error decisions become damage control rather than ordinary strategy.
- The trap shows how [[LianhengStrategy|连横]] can work by breaking a single anti-Qin link instead of persuading every eastern state at once.
