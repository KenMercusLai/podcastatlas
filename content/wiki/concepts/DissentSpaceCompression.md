# Dissent Space Compression

Dissent space compression is the pattern where public criticism remains formally possible but becomes costlier through police action, patriotic loyalty tests, internet restrictions, and stigma around being anti-national. [[cockroaches-1-modi-0-indias-remarkable-protests-6a6729a33e2f0b6f92d26ab8]] develops the concept through [[India]]'s [[CockroachMovement]].

The episode says protesters carried copies of the Constitution partly to protect themselves against the [[BharatiyaJanataParty|BJP]] habit of branding critics as anti-national. It then describes police beatings, tear gas, pellet guns, internet shutdowns, and metro closures in [[Delhi]], making the protest a case of shrinking dissent space as well as education-policy anger.

The source's twist is that jokes and memes can push back against that compression. Gen Z protesters mocked [[NarendraModi]]'s apology video and used the cockroach image to make fear and ridicule work in the other direction.
