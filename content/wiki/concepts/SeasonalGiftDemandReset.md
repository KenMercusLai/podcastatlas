# Seasonal Gift Demand Reset

## Definition
A seasonal gift demand reset is a sustained decline in a ritual product category when institutional gifting weakens and consumer preferences no longer support the category's traditional product, price, or health proposition.

## Current Synthesis
The source presents China's mooncake market as entering a second consecutive down year. It reports year-over-year sales value and volume declines above 40% at monitored major stores before Mid-Autumn Festival and attributes much of the pressure to reduced corporate group buying, employee benefits, and relationship visits.

Health preference adds a product-level reset. Traditional high-oil, high-sugar mooncakes conflict with current nutrition concerns, so low-sugar and low-GI claims become prominent. Yet reformulation and labeling do not automatically restore trust, because a product can avoid added sucrose or lower glycemic response while remaining energy-dense or high in fat and fructose.

## Key Claims
- Seasonal categories can depend more on corporate and social gifting than household consumption alone suggests.
- Reduced group purchase, employee-benefit, and relationship-visit demand can lower both volume and willingness to pay.
- Discounting may reveal excess inventory and weaker ritual demand rather than only normal holiday promotion.
- Health preferences pressure traditional high-oil, high-sugar formulations.
- Low-sugar and low-GI claims can refresh positioning but cannot substitute for whole-product nutrition and credible labeling.

## Evidence
- Market decline: [[yuebing-shichang-chixu-jiangwen-doubao-suojian-duihua-yewu-tuandui-1017879242]] reports monitored mooncake sales value and volume down more than 40% year over year.
- Gifting channel: [[yuebing-shichang-chixu-jiangwen-doubao-suojian-duihua-yewu-tuandui-1017879242]] cites reduced corporate group purchase, benefits, and visits as a primary explanation.
- Product preference: [[yuebing-shichang-chixu-jiangwen-doubao-suojian-duihua-yewu-tuandui-1017879242]] links high oil and sugar to current health concerns and notes the rise of low-sugar and low-GI selling points.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode does not identify the monitoring sample, measurement method, geographic coverage, average price, channel mix, or inventory timing. One pre-holiday period cannot establish a permanent category decline, and household, hotel, ecommerce, and corporate channels may move differently.

- Kept the reported mooncake decline bounded by missing measurement details.
