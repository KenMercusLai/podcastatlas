# Post-Disaster Predation Misread / 灾后趁虚误判

Post-disaster predation misread / 灾后趁虚误判 names the error of treating another actor's recent catastrophe as proof that it can no longer resist. In [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-100-2-liuguo-jingran-tongshi-wei-ta-diaosang-ln6-5v-b-ybqmf5ucepusdo3lf68]], [[YanWangXi|燕王喜]] and [[LiFuWarringStates|栗腹]] see [[ZhaoState|赵国]] after [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] and conclude that Zhao's adult male losses make it a ready target.

The source's correction is the [[HaodaiBattle|鄗代之战]]. [[LeJian|乐间]] had warned that Zhao is a fighting state with soldiers and wartime habits; [[JiangQu|将渠]] had warned that the attack violates recent alliance etiquette. When [[LianPo|廉颇]] defeats Yan and pushes back to the Yan capital region, the episode shows that trauma and weakness do not erase command quality, defensive motivation, institutional memory, or accumulated anger.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-106-jingren-faxian-xinlingjun-de-feizhengchang-siwang-lsj3kd2tb4tqtuvhwvsdwkh-h5el]] adds a second Yan-Zhao version through [[JuXin|剧辛]] and [[PangNuan|庞煖]]. Yan Wang Xi again attacks Zhao, this time after Ju Xin says Pang Nuan is easy to handle based on old acquaintance from Zhao. Pang Nuan defeats Yan, kills Ju Xin, and captures 20,000 soldiers, showing that personal familiarity can be as misleading as demographic arithmetic when pricing a wounded opponent.

The concept is adjacent to [[WeakOverWeakPredation|弱者向更弱者转嫁压迫]], but the emphasis is different. Weak-over-weak predation describes the pressure chain; post-disaster predation misread describes the attacker's faulty assessment of the target's remaining capacity.

## Key Claims
- A visible wound is not the same thing as strategic helplessness.
- Demographic or material loss must be weighed against morale, terrain, institutional habits, and surviving commanders.
- Opportunistic war is especially dangerous when the attacker ignores advisers who price the target's residual capacity.
- Recent alliance etiquette can turn a military gamble into a diplomatic credibility loss.
- Personal familiarity with a commander can still misprice residual military capacity when the attacker wants the target to be weak.
