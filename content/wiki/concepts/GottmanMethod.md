# Gottman Method / 哥特曼方法

## Definition
Gottman Method is the relationship-therapy framework the source presents as a data-informed way to understand couple connection, repair, conflict, and shared meaning.

## Current Synthesis
The Culture Limited episode introduces [[GottmanMethod]] while explaining [[LoriGottlieb|Lori Gottlieb]]'s clinical background. In the source, the method matters because it gives intimate relationships a practical structure: partners need to know each other's inner worlds, preserve fondness and admiration, turn toward bids for connection, accept influence, handle solvable problems, work with gridlock, and build shared meaning.

The method's risk side is equally important. [[RelationshipFourHorsemen]] makes criticism, contempt, defensiveness, and stonewalling visible as repeated communication habits, not isolated bad moods. The source especially emphasizes contempt because it erodes respect and appreciation, the conditions under which repair remains possible.

## Key Claims
- Relationship quality depends on detailed knowledge of a partner's inner world, not only attraction or compatibility.
- Repair is built through ordinary turning-toward behavior as much as through dramatic conversations.
- Some problems are solvable, while others require work with gridlock and meaning rather than immediate elimination.
- Shared meaning is a relationship achievement, not a background assumption.
- The method pairs positive maintenance practices with negative warning signs.
- Contempt is the strongest danger in the source's summary because it attacks respect.

- Maintenance layers: [[vol-349-yexu-ni-gai-zhao-ge-ren-liaoliao-shuochu-tongku-huode-yongqi-gkwrimaoywpmaycmmatldgrv]] lists fondness and admiration, turning toward, influence, solvable problems, gridlock work, and shared meaning.
- Data-informed frame: [[vol-349-yexu-ni-gai-zhao-ge-ren-liaoliao-shuochu-tongku-huode-yongqi-gkwrimaoywpmaycmmatldgrv]] says the method comes from tracked couple interaction and physiological data.
- Warning signs: [[vol-349-yexu-ni-gai-zhao-ge-ren-liaoliao-shuochu-tongku-huode-yongqi-gkwrimaoywpmaycmmatldgrv]] introduces the four horsemen as signals of relationship breakdown.
- Contempt emphasis: [[vol-349-yexu-ni-gai-zhao-ge-ren-liaoliao-shuochu-tongku-huode-yongqi-gkwrimaoywpmaycmmatldgrv]] calls contempt especially serious because it destroys respect and appreciation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source summarizes the method for a general audience and does not test its empirical validity. The page should not be read as couple-therapy instruction or as a replacement for qualified care in abusive, unsafe, or coercive relationships.
