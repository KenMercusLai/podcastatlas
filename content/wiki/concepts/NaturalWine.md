# Natural Wine

Natural wine is the low-intervention wine style discussed in [[sanlian-chuantai-shen-ci-gei-ren-putao-shijie-weixun-le-585125156]]. [[Heimai|黑麦]] describes it as a loose category rather than a single guaranteed taste: less artificial intervention can make the wine feel closer to recently fermented grape juice, but the results may be simple, variable, acidic, sweet, or strange.

The source's main claim is social as much as technical. Natural wine helped younger Chinese drinkers approach [[Wine|wine / 葡萄酒]] with less ceremony around 2017-2019, after earlier wine culture had often been shaped by prestige brands, gifts, and restaurant status. It therefore becomes a case where [[WineAsAgriculturalCulture]] and [[TasteTraining]] can become more accessible, while still requiring honest attention to flavor variation.

## Key Claims
- Natural wine foregrounds farming, fermentation, and intervention level, but the source does not treat it as automatically better.
- Its appeal partly comes from lowering the emotional barrier around wine: the drink can feel casual, fresh, and less attached to luxury codes.
- The same openness creates variability; sourness, residual sugar, cloudiness, or unusual aromas may be part of the experience.
- Natural wine is useful in the wiki as a consumer-culture shift, not only a winemaking definition.
