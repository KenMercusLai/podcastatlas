# Parental Projection Control / 父母投射式控制

## Definition
Parental projection control is a caregiving pattern where a parent treats the child, teacher, or educational path as an extension of the parent's own beliefs, ambitions, fear, or identity rather than as a relationship with independent people.

## Current Synthesis
VOL.214 develops the concept through "monster parent" scenes in [[TrueEducationKDrama|《铁拳教育》/《真教育》]]. A parent can say they act for the child while actually defending a fixed image of what the child should become and who counts as an obstacle.

The episode's critical point is not that parental care is false. It is that care becomes controlling when the parent cannot distinguish the child's needs from the parent's plan, and when teachers or institutions are forced into the parent's control system through repeated demands, harassment, or status pressure.

## Key Claims
- "For your good" language can hide objectification when the child is treated as property, tool, or extension of the parent.
- Projection becomes more intense when the child's actual desire, ability, or teacher relationship threatens a parental education plan.
- Teachers can become targets when they are seen as blocking the parent's preferred child trajectory.
- The pattern can coexist with real love; the problem is love organized around control rather than the child's independent subjectivity.
- The source links this family pattern to broader [[StructuralPowerImbalance|structural power imbalance]] because schools and teachers may be pressured by parent demands they cannot symmetrically answer.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not provide a general parenting study or a diagnostic category. It identifies a controlling pattern through drama scenes, psychiatric interpretation, and education examples. Concerned parental planning, teacher complaint, or academic ambition should not automatically be treated as abusive without looking at behavior, power, evidence, and the child's independent interests.
