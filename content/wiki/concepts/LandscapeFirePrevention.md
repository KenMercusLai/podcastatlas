# Landscape Fire Prevention

## Definition

Landscape fire prevention is the sustained reduction of ignition, fuel, and spread risk before a fire becomes an extreme emergency, combined with detection and response while it is still containable.

## Current Synthesis

The technical ingredients are mostly known: vegetation management, behavior controls, fire breaks, detection, modeling, aircraft, drones, and practice-sharing. The difficulty is institutional commitment across seasons, budgets, landholders, and jurisdictions. [[burning-questions-a-more-fire-prone-world-6a687ea64207b2a60119df8b]]

The Indonesian peatland case shows why incentives and landscape condition belong inside prevention. Where burning is cheap, peat is combustible, and enforcement is weak, emergency tools such as firefighters, water bombing, and cloud seeding treat the active fire without changing the underlying risk. Peat rewetting, corporate responsibility, land-clearing alternatives, and early use of [[ElNino|El Nino]] forecasts must begin before the emergency. [[dunes-and-gloom-mbss-annus-horribilis-0a44ecf1bba6689b3b1e02af9ca13b2c]]

## Key Claims

- Prevention depends on long-term maintenance and incentives, not only emergency suppression.
- Rapid detection matters only when information connects to capable crews and decision authority.
- Landscape condition, including dry or drained peat, can turn small ignitions into persistent fires.
- Rules must change the economics of risky burning as well as punish harm after smoke spreads.
- Restoration, fire breaks, forecasting, and suppression cover different failure points and should be layered.
- Cross-border smoke makes domestic prevention a regional governance issue.

## Evidence

- **Known prevention toolkit:** [[burning-questions-a-more-fire-prone-world-6a687ea64207b2a60119df8b]] names landscape management, behavior controls, fire breaks, detection, modeling, aircraft, drones, and best-practice sharing.
- **Commitment constraint:** [[burning-questions-a-more-fire-prone-world-6a687ea64207b2a60119df8b]] says worsening fire seasons make sustained prevention and readiness harder.
- **Peat and incentive mechanism:** [[dunes-and-gloom-mbss-annus-horribilis-0a44ecf1bba6689b3b1e02af9ca13b2c]] links deliberate land clearing, combustible peat, and El Nino drying.
- **Layered response:** [[dunes-and-gloom-mbss-annus-horribilis-0a44ecf1bba6689b3b1e02af9ca13b2c]] describes suppression, corporate cases, and peatland restoration while reporting continued criticism of enforcement.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The sources do not compare the cost-effectiveness of individual tools or establish how much each would reduce burned area. Fire attribution, land tenure, poverty, plantation contracting, ecological fire use, and enforcement capacity can complicate blanket bans. Cloud seeding and water bombing are episode-reported responses, not evidence of durable prevention success.
