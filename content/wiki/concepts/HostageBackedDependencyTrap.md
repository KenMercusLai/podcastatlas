# Hostage-Backed Dependency Trap / 家属质押式依附陷阱

Hostage-backed dependency trap / 家属质押式依附陷阱 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-981-sanguo-zui-gang-moushi-chengyu-yi-ju-hua-rang-caocao-tanfu-lhlhlrmf2gvspwyvjkxg6kz44fth|Hanji 981]], where [[YuanShao|袁绍]] asks [[CaoCao|曹操]] to send his family to Ye while Cao Cao is short of grain and weakened in [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]]. The episode treats the offer as more than hospitality: a family move would convert relief into leverage and make independent withdrawal harder.

The concept names a dependency form where protection, refuge, or alliance is secured by placing one's household under another power's control. It is not identical to ordinary hostage diplomacy because the immediate danger is loss of strategic independence: the weaker actor may survive materially while becoming subordinate in practice.

[[ChengYu|程昱]]'s advice defines the exit from the trap. He does not deny Cao Cao's danger; he argues that Cao Cao still has enough cities, soldiers, advisers, and ambition to avoid becoming Yuan Shao's subordinate. The decision therefore turns on realistic capacity assessment rather than pride alone.

## Key Claims
- Relief offered by a stronger patron can become control when family members or dependents become collateral.
- A powerholder under pressure may over-discount remaining capacity and accept subordination too early.
- Anti-dependency advice works only if it identifies both the stronger patron's limits and the weaker actor's remaining assets.
- The concept differs from ordinary alliance caution because the household pledge changes future bargaining power before any formal surrender is announced.
