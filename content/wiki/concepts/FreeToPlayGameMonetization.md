# Free-to-Play Game Monetization

Free-to-play game monetization is the shift from charging for play time or access toward making the game free and charging for items, privileges, convenience, cosmetics, or other in-game value. [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]] adds the concept through [[ShandaGames|盛大游戏]]'s decision to make several games free in 2005.

In the source, Shanda's move is both adaptive and destabilizing. It responds to changing user expectations and competition, but it also helps move the market away from the older [[InternetCafePointCardDistribution]] model that made [[LegendOfMir|《传奇》]] dominant. Later competitors could use free-to-play mechanics more aggressively, leaving Shanda caught between old IP, newer item-charging games, and higher-production MMORPGs.

[[ep242-duli-youxi-shi-yitiao-gaoqian-hao-saidao-ma-lrqbzrceumbuggzuero5qb4jv3zu]] adds a contrast case. [[WuJunIndieGameCreator|巫君]] distinguishes mobile-game in-app purchase, draw-card, and advertising models from [[Steam]] independent games, which in his case are closer to buyout products with discounts and long-tail sales.
