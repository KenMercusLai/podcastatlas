# Book Price War Signal Trap / 图书价格战信号陷阱

Book price war signal trap is the source's publishing example for [[PrisonersDilemma]] and [[SignalDesign]]. [[79-gewei-lingdao-danfan-zan-xuedian-boyilun-690004262]] argues that publishers and platforms discount partly to avoid looking weak or unsellable, but collective discounting can damage industry margins and train readers to wait for unsustainable prices.

The episode is especially sharp about book publishing because books are not treated as ordinary commodities. The source argues that channels, bookstores, editorial labor, and reader habits can be weakened when price becomes the only visible signal.

## Key Claims
- A price cut can signal competitiveness in the short term while damaging the category's long-term value.
- If publishers are assessed by gross sales value rather than profit, internal metrics can deepen the discount race.
- Book channels need ecological protection because retail habit, bookstore survival, and publishing quality are linked.
- The trap resembles a prisoner's dilemma: each actor fears losing alone, but collective discounting can make everyone worse off.
