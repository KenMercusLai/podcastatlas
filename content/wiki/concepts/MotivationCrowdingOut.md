# Motivation Crowding Out / 动机挤出

Motivation crowding out is [[79-gewei-lingdao-danfan-zan-xuedian-boyilun-690004262]]'s explanation for reward systems that reduce the very behavior they try to buy. The episode uses blood donation through [[RichardTitmuss|Richard Titmuss]] and a Swiss nuclear-waste siting example to distinguish intrinsic motivation from external reward or punishment.

The source's management lesson is that incentives have meaning. A cash payment can signal respect, but a small or badly framed payment can turn a civic, moral, identity-based, or meaningful act into a cheap transaction.

## Key Claims
- Intrinsic motivation includes honor, justice, love, public spirit, and meaning.
- External motivation includes money, punishments, and explicit rewards.
- External rewards can damage intrinsic motives when they change the perceived meaning of the act.
- A manager may need either enough money to be taken seriously or enough meaning and dignity to preserve non-monetary motivation.
