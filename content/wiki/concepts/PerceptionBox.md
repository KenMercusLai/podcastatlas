# Perception Box

## Definition
A perception box is the source's metaphor for the constrained experienced reality produced by sensory receptors, prior assumptions, memory, culture, embodiment, bodily state, and personal history.

## Current Synthesis
The model explains why people can encounter the same stimulus or event yet inhabit meaningfully different realities. Bayesian priors organize ambiguous input; body form and receptors limit what can be sensed; memory, emotion, and culture influence what is expected and noticed. Optical illusions offer a narrow demonstration, while virtual reality, empathy, therapy, meditation, and altered states are presented as stronger attempts to expose or revise those assumptions.

The useful version of the concept is neither total relativism nor unrestricted self-reinvention. No sentient observer fully escapes a constructed perspective, and changed belief does not erase external facts, trauma, biology, or social conditions. The practical aim is to notice rigid priors, encounter disconfirming experience, and make a perception box more flexible without treating subjective conviction as proof.

## Key Claims
- Perception is an inference shaped by prior expectations as well as incoming signals.
- Bodies, receptors, memories, culture, and internal state make experienced reality partly bespoke.
- Direct experience can sometimes revise assumptions more strongly than abstract information.
- Empathy approximates another viewpoint without granting complete access to another mind.
- Curiosity and agency may support revision, while cynicism and rigid priors can close it down.
- A changed perception box can alter interpretation and action without rewriting external reality.

## Evidence
- Ambiguous perception - [[scim8616450810-scim8616450810]] uses the dress illusion and Bayesian priors to show different interpretations of shared input.
- Perspective-taking - [[scim8616450810-scim8616450810]] describes a virtual-reality race embodiment and Oliver Sacks's empathic attention to coherent realities produced by different brains.
- Psychological change - [[scim8616450810-scim8616450810]] connects therapy, memory reconstrual, community, meditation, belief, and curiosity to revising entrenched interpretations.
- Limits - [[scim8616450810-scim8616450810]] says a sentient creature cannot step wholly outside constructed perception.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source uses a broad organizing metaphor rather than a fully specified scientific theory. Perspective-taking simulations may evoke insight without reproducing another person's life, and placebo or expectation effects do not show that belief can overcome every disorder or constraint. The model should not turn structural harm, illness, trauma, or disagreement into a claim that reality is merely chosen.
