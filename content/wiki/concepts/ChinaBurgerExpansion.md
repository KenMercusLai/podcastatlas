# China Burger Expansion / 中国汉堡开店潮

China burger expansion is the restaurant-market pattern described in [[zhongguo-yinglai-hanbao-kaidianchao-guochan-huangningmeng-jiage-huiluo-1006920543]], where foreign burger chains and adjacent Chinese restaurant brands all move into the burger occasion. The source names [[FiveGuys]]' Beijing opening, burger stores from [[Haidilao]] and [[MStand]], and [[PizzaHutChina]]'s store-within-store burger rollout.

The episode's demand explanation is pragmatic. Burgers still have a limited share of China's fast-food market, but they can be read as a filling meal at a relatively controlled price when consumers are cautious. That makes the category attractive to chains that already have traffic, kitchens, supply routines, or brand attention.

## Key Claims
- Burger expansion is not only a Western-chain story; hot-pot, coffee, and pizza operators are also testing the category.
- The source-cited growth projection of 8.7% annualized market growth through 2035 is kept source-scoped.
- Existing restaurant infrastructure can lower the cost of entry when a chain can use [[StoreWithinStoreFoodservice]].
- The category's appeal depends on price, fullness, meal occasion, and operational repeatability.
- Novelty queues can create launch attention, but repeat demand still depends on [[ProductLedWillingnessToPay]] and local execution.
