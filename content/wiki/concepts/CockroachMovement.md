# Cockroach Movement

The Cockroach Movement is the youth-led protest wave described in [[cockroaches-1-modi-0-indias-remarkable-protests-6a6729a33e2f0b6f92d26ab8]]. The source says it began after jobless youth were seemingly compared to cockroaches, then grew through [[AbhijitTipke]]'s meme-making, AI cockroach protest imagery, Hindi rap, and large online followings.

Its political force came from grievance concentration. [[NationalEligibilityCumEntranceTest|NEET]] leaks, student suicides, unemployment, public education underfunding, and the demand for [[DharmendraPradhan]]'s resignation made the cockroach symbol a way to turn humiliation into collective pressure.

The episode treats the movement as a rare challenge to [[NarendraModi]]'s political invulnerability. It won a resignation, but the source also keeps the future uncertain because the [[BharatiyaJanataParty|BJP]] remains strong and the opposition is described as weak.
