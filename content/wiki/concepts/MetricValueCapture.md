# Metric Value Capture

## Definition
Metric value capture is the replacement of a rich, contextual, and revisable value by a simple quantified proxy until the proxy becomes what a person or institution actually treats as valuable.

## Current Synthesis
[[CThiNguyen]] argues in [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-6abee3c124a8134e46fbc03d3d8edac7-63678825-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-6abee3c124a8134e46fbc03d3d8edac7-63678825]] that metrics solve a real coordination problem: qualitative judgment carries context but travels poorly, whereas grades, page views, citations, engagement hours, ratings, steps, or health outcomes can be aggregated across large institutions. The same portability deletes intensity, meaning, duration, beauty, community, and forms of expertise that resist mechanical recognition.

Capture occurs when this partial representation stops being one input and becomes the operative value. This differs from merely gaming a target under [[GoodhartsLaw]]: a person may strategically respond to a metric while still knowing the real goal, whereas value capture narrows attention until what lies outside the measure is no longer experienced as relevant. Its institutional extreme is value collapse, where people who optimize exclusively gain resources and authority and then reinforce the same measurement regime.

## Key Claims
- Quantitative portability is useful precisely because it removes context, creating both scale and loss.
- Every measurement system embeds classification choices that foreground some qualities and omit others.
- Capture is psychological and normative, not only behavioral: the proxy becomes the value rather than merely distorting incentives.
- Metrics are especially persuasive when they resemble the underlying value closely enough to hide the substitution.
- Clear measures can systematically defeat subtle values because they are easier to communicate, audit, and enforce.
- Optimization systems inherit the incompleteness of their measurable targets, including in education, social media, universities, public health, and AI.
- A defensible response retains valid measurements while refusing to treat them as complete or automatically overriding.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept does not imply that qualitative judgment is unbiased, scalable, or sufficient, nor that every response to incentives changes a person's underlying values. The source's practical safeguards are strongest at the personal and local level and less developed for institutions that must coordinate across distance. Claims about university cuts, classroom incentives, AI-art objectives, and value collapse remain philosophical and episode-scoped rather than causal estimates from the underlying systems.

## What Changed
- Established a distinction between strategic metric gaming and internal replacement of the underlying value.
- Added portability, attention, and institutional selection as mechanisms through which partial measures become dominant.
- Preserved measurement as useful but incomplete rather than treating quantification itself as the failure.
