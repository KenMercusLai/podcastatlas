# Symmetric Interstate Credibility / 对称外交信用

Symmetric interstate credibility / 对称外交信用 is the diplomatic pattern in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-05-guangcai-duomu-de-weiwenhou-1-lmkxzbmwhw3nhurape-eebydqcdl]]. [[HanState|韩国]] asks [[WeiState|魏国]] to join an attack on [[ZhaoState|赵国]], and Zhao asks Wei to join an attack on Han; [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]] refuses both requests with the same brother-state rationale.

The credibility comes from symmetry that the other parties can later verify. The source stresses that Wei Wenhou does not rush to advertise his refusal to either side. By letting information circulate, he makes Han and Zhao discover the consistency for themselves, which converts restraint into trust and hierarchy among the Three Jin.

## Key Claims
- A neutral-looking choice becomes credible when it is applied consistently to rival parties.
- A state gains more trust when other actors can verify its consistency without depending only on self-praise.
- Diplomatic restraint can create leadership status when surrounding states are tempted by short-term attacks.
- The episode makes this interstate version of [[SmallTrustBuildsLargeTrust|trust-building]] part of [[RenhePoliticalPower|人和政治力量]].
