# Mythic Parent Archetypes / 神话中的父母原型

[[MythicParentArchetypes|Mythic parent archetypes / 神话中的父母原型]] is the episode's pattern for how hero stories stage mother, goddess, father, and authority figures. In [[29-qianmian-yingxiong-edipusi-zhengzai-xiaban-ji-ditie-590112891]], the goddess can be mother, sister, lover, safety, beauty, temptation, threat, womb, and tomb; the father can be order, command, violence, test, law, and reconciliation.

The concept extends the wiki's [[MotherComplex]] and [[JungianArchetypalReading]] material beyond one literary case. The source treats these figures as symbolic stages inside [[HeroJourneyNarrative]], not as fixed gender rules. Their value is that they show how growth stories often require the hero to face both nurturing and devouring safety, both oppressive and integrating authority.

## Key Claims
- Goddess and mother images in myth can be loving and terrifying at once.
- Father images can be cruel or orderly, but the hero's task is often to integrate or survive that authority rather than simply defeat it.
- Androgynous or whole-god images in the episode mark a hoped-for integration after mother/father conflicts.
- The source keeps the pattern archetypal and literary, not clinical.
