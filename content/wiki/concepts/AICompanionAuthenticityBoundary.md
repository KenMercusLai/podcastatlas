## Definition
AI companion authenticity boundary is the problem of evaluating an emotionally powerful AI relationship when the represented person, voice, or partner is synthetic while the user's experience and response may be real.

## Current Synthesis
Authenticity in AI companionship is layered rather than binary. A system may be fabricated as a person or relationship while still producing real comfort, grief, attachment, or perceived understanding. The evidence spans a satirical deceased-grandfather simulation, perfect-partner thought experiments, everyday descriptions of AI as friend or confidant, a philosophical account of human reciprocity and mortality, and a supervised generative-ghost study.

The newest evidence clarifies that perceived authenticity is relational as well as factual. Participants often tolerated invented biographical details but reacted to message length, emoji use, and the communication medium associated with the deceased. That makes style fidelity powerful but not self-justifying: reproducing familiar habits can deepen meaning while also strengthening illusion, dependence, or manipulation. The boundary therefore requires transparency about synthesis, consent and provenance, preservation of agency, and recognition that smooth responsiveness is not the same as reciprocal human vulnerability.

## Key Claims
- Synthetic representation and genuine emotional response can coexist without making the representation literally authentic.
- Perceived authenticity depends on relational habits, style, memory, and interaction framing as well as factual accuracy.
- Knowledge that a partner is nonhuman can remain a decisive species or reciprocity boundary even under ideal subjective fit.
- AI can feel like friend, teacher, confidant, or memorial presence before users treat it as a formal companion product.
- Human intimacy retains differences grounded in embodiment, vulnerability, mortality, independent agency, and the cost of freely given attention.
- Style fidelity and active memory can increase comfort while also increasing attention capture, dependency, and manipulation risk.
- AI companionship may be legitimate for some users without becoming equivalent to human intimacy or safe in every context.

## Evidence
- Synthetic representation and real feeling: [[suanli-kuangxiangqu-wo-zai-ai-gongchang-de-qiyu-lorijulltfhttspka22jnn4qjf-i]] asks whether a fake grandfather voice cancels the reality of the listener's tears.
- Romantic species and obligation boundary: [[ln6gnhmsx1xo3p2n8z2powciqnur-ln6gnhmsx1xo3p2n8z2powciqnur]] contrasts panelists open to perfect subjective experience with those blocked by knowing the partner is nonhuman, while distinguishing hypothetical choice from abandoning an existing partner.
- Everyday companion attachment: [[wuliaozhai-luoyonghao-de-x-zilukou-xijugongzuozhe-zai-ai-shidai-keyi-duo-huo-ji-nian-luag5vkvmerd4sl805m1gdehyngs]] describes AI as friend, teacher, confidant, and memory-bearing assistant, alongside unease about dependence.
- Reciprocity and life-world difference: [[zai-keji-shidai-zhongxin-lijie-womende-ai-gongzuo-yu-shenghuo-ljjrm6-ovwduouaecvymkbeyuvuqu]] distinguishes smooth AI recognition from human co-presence, bodily vulnerability, death, effort, and attention another person could have withheld.
- Relational style: [[tech-20261012-1012-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261012-1012-mp-tech-pod-128]] reports that participants often overlooked invented details while mismatched length, emojis, and texting-versus-email habits disrupted the experience.
- Supervision boundary: [[tech-20261012-1012-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261012-1012-mp-tech-pod-128]] reports mostly positive brief experiences under human mediation, not direct long-term commercial use.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The evidence combines satire, live comedy thought experiments, a philosophical public interview, and one brief supervised participant study. It does not establish therapeutic benefit, safe long-term attachment, or the real-world existence of a perfect AI partner. Style sensitivity may show relational recognition, but it may also show how easily surface cues sustain an illusion. The concept is an evaluation boundary, not a verdict that AI companionship is inherently beneficial or harmful.

## What Changed
- Added empirical support for distinguishing factual fidelity from relational-style fidelity.
- Added representation versus reincarnation as an interaction frame that changes the system's identity claim.
- Narrowed the positive generative-ghost finding to brief, supervised use with a human intermediary.
- Made explicit that the same style fidelity that supports meaning can amplify illusion and dependence.
