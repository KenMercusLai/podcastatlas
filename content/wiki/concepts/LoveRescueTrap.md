# Love Rescue Trap / 以爱拯救陷阱

Love rescue trap / 以爱拯救陷阱 is the relationship pattern where a person treats loving, forgiving, waiting, or enduring as the way to save a harmful partner. In [[84-binglu-kuanghua-hao-guniang-ni-zhishi-wangle-yonggan-699443676]], [[JoyWiebe|Joy "Joey" Wiebe / Joey]] names the pattern from her own history and warns [[AmyButcher|Amy Butcher / 艾米]] not to spend her life trying to love a dangerous man into safety.

The source distinguishes this trap from ordinary care. Love may be real, but real love does not cancel danger, violence, addiction, humiliation, or chronic fear. The episode's bear story makes the point bluntly: something can be loved and still require a protective, final decision.

This concept overlaps with [[TraumaticAttachment]] and [[FawnResponse]], but it names the moral fantasy that keeps the person invested: if she loves correctly, patiently, or sacrificially enough, the man may become safe. The source rejects that fantasy without mocking the person who was caught in it.

## Key Claims
- Love can be genuine and still be an unsafe reason to stay.
- Rescue fantasies often convert a partner's violence, addiction, or volatility into the victim's responsibility.
- Women may be praised for endurance and care in ways that make exit feel selfish or cruel.
- Naming the trap helps separate compassion from self-erasure.
