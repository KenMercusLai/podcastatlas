# Participatory Portrait Co-Creation / 参与式约拍共创

## Definition
Participatory portrait co-creation is an appointment-photography experience in which photographer, subject, and collaborators jointly transform a theme into a performed and shareable image artifact.

## Current Synthesis
EP388 locates much of appointment photography's value outside the final photograph. Participants choose or negotiate a theme, prepare costume and role, enter a temporary shared situation, respond to direction and one another, wait for the edit, and later circulate the result. The photographer still carries unequal responsibility for research, feasibility, direction, technical quality, and selection, so co-creation means structured participation rather than equal control over every decision.

## Key Claims
- The experience begins before the shoot through idea selection, preparation, references, costume, and anticipation.
- Role names, scene discussion, actions, and interaction help participants temporarily inhabit another identity.
- Small teams can translate historical, literary, or cinematic material into a jointly produced artifact without entering a mature film industry.
- Enjoyment continues through waiting for, receiving, and sharing the finished work.
- Co-creation depends on consent and prior agreement, distinguishing it from uninvited street capture.
- Unequal expertise remains productive when the photographer uses it to guide rather than erase participant agency.

## Evidence
- Shared preparation: [[ep388-wangmama-huamulan-yiri-tiyanka-liaoliao-yuepai-zhege-chuquan-de-shengyi-gkwrijioif27ag4hdwtbxhjb]] describes customers bringing ideas, photographers proposing styles, and teams discussing scenes and movement before shooting.
- Temporary identity: [[ep388-wangmama-huamulan-yiri-tiyanka-liaoliao-yuepai-zhege-chuquan-de-shengyi-gkwrijioif27ag4hdwtbxhjb]] says participants are addressed as Xiaoqing, Fahai, or Mulan and gradually enter the collectively built situation.
- Extended experience: [[ep388-wangmama-huamulan-yiri-tiyanka-liaoliao-yuepai-zhege-chuquan-de-shengyi-gkwrijioif27ag4hdwtbxhjb]] explicitly distributes pleasure across preparation, the shoot, waiting for finished material, and publication.
- Achievable scale: [[ep388-wangmama-huamulan-yiri-tiyanka-liaoliao-yuepai-zhege-chuquan-de-shengyi-gkwrijioif27ag4hdwtbxhjb]] closes by arguing that creation can begin with a realizable one- or two-day idea rather than a large professional production.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Participation does not remove service asymmetry, production labor, or conflict over the result. When the subject is a paying client, appearance and delivery expectations may legitimately override the photographer's preferred expression; larger story projects can also expand beyond the supposedly accessible one- or two-day scale.

## What Changed
- Established appointment portraiture as an extended co-creation experience rather than a single transaction.
- Preserved photographer responsibility and client asymmetry inside the participation frame.
