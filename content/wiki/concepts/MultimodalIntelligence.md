# Multimodal Intelligence

Multimodal intelligence is the source's route from language-only systems toward visual, spatial, continuous, and eventually predictive world understanding. In [[133-dui-xie-saining-de-7-xiaoshi-ma-la-song-fangtan-shijie-moxing-taochu-guigu-ami-labs-liangci-jujue-ilya-yang-likun-li-feifei-he-42]], [[XieSaining]] describes stages from pure language models, to show-and-tell image QA, to continuous visual streams and spatial understanding.

[[claire-isabel-webb-nina-miolane-the-geometry-of-consciousness-iulbwkinatk]] adds a computational-neuroscience example of spatial intelligence. [[NinaMiolane]] shows that both biological navigation circuits and artificial networks trained on position prediction can organize around [[NeuralGeometry]] such as the [[SpatialNavigationTorus]], suggesting that spatial representation may have task-driven mathematical structure.

[[youhua-shenglv-erfei-peilv-ba-yi-jian-shi-zuodao-lilun-shang-gaiyou-de-yangzi-duitan-lianxu-chuangyezhe-albert-lu0vamaawctwva3qblnsf99esar2]] adds [[Albert]]'s product-founder phrasing: he reserves "multimodal" more for understanding than for image or video generation alone. His question of what happens when the "eyes have a brain" links stronger visual understanding to new interaction containers and [[ModelCapabilityPackaging]].

## Key Claims
- Language is a powerful interface, but not the whole world and not the only form of thought or decision.
- Visual and spatial intelligence need representations that can process continuous perceptual streams, not just isolated images.
- Spatial intelligence can be studied through population-level geometry rather than only through input/output behavior.
- A final route should move toward predictive [[WorldModels]] that explain the observed world and support planning.
- Over-tokenizing visual streams for LLMs may hide the physical structure that models need to learn.
- In product terms, stronger multimodal understanding matters when it changes what a user can ask, inspect, control, or automate, not only when it produces more media.
