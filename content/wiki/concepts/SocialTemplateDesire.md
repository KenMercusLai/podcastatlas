# Social-Template Desire / 社会模板欲望

Social-template desire is the episode's frame for wanting that is borrowed from available social scripts rather than discovered through direct self-knowledge. In [[132-dang-guodu-sikao-de-dagongren-yu-shang-di-yuwang-de-shidai-luaqswjqezbakxdrcshifxi0ermo]], the speakers name good student, good employee, marriage, promotion, property, goods, travel, and other visible milestones as templates that can make people believe they want something before they have examined why.

The source does not claim that every template is false. Templates can carry real information about security, care, status, or belonging. The problem appears when template-following becomes automatic, when failure to match the template becomes shame, or when low desire is just the collapse of a borrowed desire rather than the absence of all desire.

The concept's practical question is not "what would I want in total isolation?" It is "which of these inherited or algorithmically amplified desires still fits my body, constraints, temperament, relationships, and future?"

## Key Claims
- Social life supplies desire scripts before a person can name their own preferences.
- Achievement pressure can make templates feel morally mandatory rather than optional.
- Algorithmic feeds amplify templates by making other people's milestones continuously visible.
- Low desire can indicate that a social template has lost credibility, not that the person has no desires.
- Self-knowledge requires checking whether a desire still fits one's actual life conditions.
