# Coup-to-State-Terror Escalation

## Definition
Coup-to-state-terror escalation is the movement from forcibly removing a government to building recurring institutions and practices of detention, torture, killing, disappearance, and political intimidation.

## Current Synthesis
[[371-the-1973-chilean-coup-general-pinochet-seizes-power-part-2-glt3740766328]] separates the completion of the September 11 coup from the choices that followed it. Mass roundups and stadium detention began immediately; the Caravan of Death then extended punitive killing into the provinces; DINA made repression organizationally durable; and [[AugustoPinochet]] converted temporary junta primacy into personal rule. The sequence supports the episode's judgment that perceived emergency did not mechanically require continuing terror.

## Key Claims
- A successful coup does not explain or necessitate later organized repression.
- Mass detention infrastructure can rapidly turn political classification into torture and killing.
- Mobile punitive campaigns can impose terror beyond the capital and discipline local authorities.
- Intelligence bodies can institutionalize practices first visible in improvised post-coup detention.
- Personal consolidation and personality cult can preserve emergency violence after immediate resistance has ended.

## Evidence
- Immediate detention: [[371-the-1973-chilean-coup-general-pinochet-seizes-power-part-2-glt3740766328]] reports roughly 13,000 to 14,000 early detainees and stadium-based interrogation and torture.
- Targeted killing: [[371-the-1973-chilean-coup-general-pinochet-seizes-power-part-2-glt3740766328]] includes [[VictorJara]] and American detainees among victims of the early apparatus.
- Geographic and institutional extension: [[371-the-1973-chilean-coup-general-pinochet-seizes-power-part-2-glt3740766328]] presents the Caravan of Death as a precursor to DINA.
- Leadership choice: [[371-the-1973-chilean-coup-general-pinochet-seizes-power-part-2-glt3740766328]] argues Pinochet did not have to continue killing or retain the machinery of repression.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source provides estimates rather than a complete victim registry and does not reconstruct every chain of command. Cold War fears and armed conflict claims may explain how perpetrators justified coercion, but explanation does not establish necessity, proportionality, or legitimacy.

## What Changed
- Created a staged model linking immediate roundup, mobile killing, intelligence organization, and personal dictatorship.
- Preserved the episode's distinction between contextual explanation and individual choice.
