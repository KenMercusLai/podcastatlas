# Midterm Backlash Forecasting

## Definition
Midterm backlash forecasting is the practice of estimating congressional control by combining the president's approval, economic mood, special-election evidence, map structure, and candidate quality.

## Current Synthesis
Silver's source-dated forecast separates the House and Senate. He treats the House as strongly favorable for Democrats in 2026 because backlash against the president's party, Trump unpopularity, economic anxiety, and special-election strength all point in the same direction. The Senate remains harder because control depends on Democrats winning seats in red states, so a favorable national mood does not translate mechanically into chamber control.

## Key Claims
- House control can lean strongly toward the opposition party when presidential approval, economic anxiety, and special-election signals align.
- Senate control can remain difficult even in a good national environment because the seat map may force wins in red states.
- Gas prices matter because voters experience them as visible, repeated price signals.
- Foreign-policy or security shocks, including Iran in this source, can shift the electoral environment.
- A Democratic Senate win would likely imply an even stronger Democratic year in the House and governorships.
