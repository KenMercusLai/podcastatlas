# Donju Market Class

Donju market class is the source's term for North Korean entrepreneurs, translated in [[getting-entrepreneurial-in-korea-summer-school]] as "lords of money." The episode uses [[JessieNorthKorea]] to show that entrepreneurial instincts can appear even in a highly restrictive system, and it says people running shops, factories, software companies, and startup ideas attended business classes during [[KimJongUn]]'s limited opening.

The concept is politically bounded. Donju activity is tolerated when it serves the regime, but ordinary entrepreneurs lack secure [[PropertyRightsAsInvestmentIncentive]], courts, or reliable ownership protection. The source therefore treats the donju as evidence of market energy without treating North Korea as a normal market economy.
