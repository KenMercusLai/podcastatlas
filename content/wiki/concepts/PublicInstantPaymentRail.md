# Public Instant Payment Rail

Public instant payment rail is the infrastructure pattern shown by [[Pix]] in [[working-memory-the-surprising-decline-of-dementia-6a6b0eeca585d3be78cd9d1b]]. The episode presents Pix as a central-bank-created system that lets people transfer money instantly, 24 hours a day, usually free of charge, through phones and QR codes.

The concept differs from a private wallet or card network because state authority and mandatory bank participation can make the rail broadly available. In the source, that design helps Pix compete most directly with cash and bring poorer users into formal finance.

## Key Claims
- Public instant rails can make digital payments feel as normal as cash.
- Financial inclusion depends on cost, always-on availability, phone access, and institutional trust.
- A central bank may be able to coordinate banks in ways a private network cannot.
- The same public role creates governance questions around data concentration, competition, and operator control.
