## Definition

西海郡流放和法令增殖 / Xihai exile administrative burden is the episode's pattern where creating a frontier commandery and adding a small set of legal articles expands into mass exile, bureaucratic workload, and popular resentment.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 623 and Hanji 624 ground the concept in the 4 CE establishment of Xihai Commandery under [[WangMang|王莽]]'s late-Western-Han influence. Hanji 623 supplies the legitimating prelude: envoys present Qiang land-offering as the result of [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] and Wang Mang's virtue, fill the report with auspicious signs, and let Wang Mang argue that a western commandery completes the east-south-north-west map. Hanji 624 then shows the administrative burden after that acceptance, as the court adds fifty legal articles and sends offenders to Xihai.

The episode places this legal move beside broader administrative rearrangement: [[LiuLiWesternHan|Liu Li]] is demoted and exiled, the Chang'an capital region is redrawn, official grades are altered, boundaries between state and commandery populations shift, and commandery or kingdom jurisdictions are repeatedly cancelled, added, or changed. The host reads the cluster through "庸人自扰": government activity meant to order the realm instead makes officials and people carry extra burden.

## Key Claims

- A small formal increase in law can produce large coercive effects when tied to exile administration.
- Frontier commandery creation can become a punishment pipeline rather than only territorial organization.
- A frontier commandery can be legitimized first through praise, auspicious reporting, and symbolic geography before its legal burdens become visible.
- Administrative redrawing imposes real workload on officials even when described as technical ordering.
- Popular resentment in the source comes from cumulative burden: law, exile, status punishment, and jurisdictional churn interact.
- The episode uses "庸人自扰" as a source-scoped moral frame for overactive governance.

- Scale and resentment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-624-du-lishi-wu-xingfu-ni-shi-rensheng-yingjia-haishi-yongren-zirao-daibiao-lpavicltc7nducbxiqgpr6t7iczd|Hanji 624]] says tens of thousands are eventually exiled and commoners complain.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The page is grounded in two adjacent episode summaries. It does not settle the full statutory content of the fifty articles, exact exile totals, Xihai geography, Qiang-side motives, or whether the policy had security or frontier-colonization aims not developed by the sources. The "庸人自扰" diagnosis is the host's interpretive frame from Hanji 624.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 623 as the praise-and-auspicious-reporting prelude to Xihai Commandery.
- Preserved Hanji 624 as the legal, exile, resentment, and administrative-churn consequence layer.
