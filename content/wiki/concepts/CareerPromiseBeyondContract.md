# Career Promise Beyond Contract / 超越退出条款的职业承诺

## Definition
Career promise beyond contract is the choice to treat an originally stated term of service as morally binding even when the written agreement provides a lawful, priced exit and leaving would improve the worker's position.

## Current Synthesis
[[daoyan-dapeng-luoyonghao-wo-zhe-liang-beizi-liytv2s219yn3ox5dhr4z4mrovyt]] develops the concept through a direct disagreement. [[DapengDirector|大鹏]] says he remained with Sohu because a promised five- or ten-year term should be completed; [[LuoYonghao|罗永浩]] argues that paying the agreed penalty would itself honor the contract. The difference is therefore not compliance versus breach, but contractual option versus relational promise.

The same case prevents simple moral ranking. Staying can express gratitude, identity, reciprocity, and credible commitment, but can also preserve undercompensation and make one party absorb costs the formal agreement permits them to escape. A strong promise ethic needs a boundary against turning loyalty into unilateral extraction.

## Key Claims
- A priced exit clause can make departure contractually compliant while leaving a separate promise-based moral question.
- Workers may value platform, mentorship, opportunity, and shared history beyond immediate compensation.
- Promise loyalty can build trust and coherent identity, but it can also weaken bargaining and normalize unequal reward.
- Gratitude for opportunity does not by itself settle whether compensation and follow-through were fair.
- The relevant disagreement should remain explicit rather than redefining either contractualism or loyalty as obviously immoral.

## Evidence
- Staying despite exit advice - [[daoyan-dapeng-luoyonghao-wo-zhe-liang-beizi-liytv2s219yn3ox5dhr4z4mrovyt]] records Dapeng declining advice to pay the penalty and leave for higher income.
- Contractual counterargument - [[daoyan-dapeng-luoyonghao-wo-zhe-liang-beizi-liytv2s219yn3ox5dhr4z4mrovyt]] records Luo's view that using the agreed exit mechanism can preserve rather than violate contract spirit.
- Reciprocity tension - [[daoyan-dapeng-luoyonghao-wo-zhe-liang-beizi-liytv2s219yn3ox5dhr4z4mrovyt]] places Dapeng's gratitude for platform and colleagues beside an episode-attributed unfulfilled sharing promise after [[JianbingManFilm|《煎饼侠》]].

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not provide the contracts or both parties' complete accounts, so it cannot determine legal rights, actual breach, or fair compensation. Promise ethics should not be generalized into advice to remain in unsafe, coercive, discriminatory, or exploitative work.

## What Changed
- Established the distinction between formal exit compliance and a stricter personal promise ethic.
- Added undercompensation and bargaining asymmetry as necessary limits on celebrating loyalty.
