# GLP-1 Body Composition Stack

## Definition
GLP-1 body composition stack is the episode's term-adjacent frame for combining GLP-1 drugs, growth-hormone or GHRH-related modulation, and androgen therapies to pursue rapid fat loss and muscle gain.

## Current Synthesis
The sources treat the stack as a visible optimization trend, not a settled medical protocol. Bakri says some people combine [[GLP1Agonists|GLP-1s]], growth-hormone or GHRH modulation, and androgen therapies to change body composition quickly. The All-In retatrutide segment adds a more mainstream-looking version of the same desire: a regulated next-generation incretin candidate is discussed not only for obesity and diabetes, but also for possible fat-biased weight loss, muscle preservation, and short-term fitness cuts.

The unresolved part is long-term health. Each component has its own indication, contraindication, dose, monitoring, and adverse-effect profile, and stacked use makes attribution harder. Koniver's clinic account adds a slower-loss rationale: lower compounded GLP-1 doses, adequate protein, and resistance training are used in an effort to reduce lean-mass loss, sometimes alongside sermorelin. That remains a practice model rather than evidence that a combined stack outperforms appropriately dosed single therapy. The strongest wiki claim is therefore cautionary: rapid visible recomposition does not establish that the combined intervention is durable, safe, medically indicated, or separable from training, nutrition, protein intake, muscle preservation, and clinical monitoring.

## Key Claims
- The stack combines distinct intervention classes rather than one coherent approved protocol.
- Rapid fat loss and muscle gain claims are source-described as culturally visible but medically unresolved.
- GLP-1 dosing, rebound, appetite changes, nutrition, and maintenance remain central even before adding other hormones.
- Growth-hormone and androgen-adjacent components add monitoring and risk layers.
- Long-term outcomes cannot be inferred from short-term appearance changes.
- Retatrutide may intensify body-composition narratives even if its legitimate use remains indication-, approval-, and clinician-bounded.
- Slower weight loss, protein intake, and resistance training are muscle-preservation principles, not proof that adding a secretagogue improves outcomes.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not provide controlled outcome data for the stack. It should not be treated as a recommendation for combining GLP-1s, growth-hormone secretagogues, or androgen therapies.

The retatrutide source reports trial and mechanism claims but does not establish off-label fitness use as safe, approved, or medically appropriate. Koniver's lower-dose and combination approach has no controlled comparator in the source, and compounded dosing should not be treated as inherently safer or more effective.
