# Ningbo Board Game Supply Chain / 宁波桌游供应链

## Definition

Ningbo board game supply chain / 宁波桌游供应链 is the local cluster of printing, molding, injection, packaging, and related suppliers that supports board-game, card, puzzle, and toy manufacturing in Ningbo.

## Current Synthesis

[[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]] uses the board-game supply chain to explain why [[Mideer|迷鹿]] can connect original design with actual manufacturing. The city has long produced for European and American customers, so local factories understand overseas household and adult board-game demand as well as the physical production steps.

The concept is a design-manufacturing bridge. Original illustration and children's-product positioning need suppliers who can execute printing, cutting, material selection, safety requirements, and packaging at consistent quality.

## Key Claims

- Board-game and toy supply chains depend on many local process steps, not only final assembly.
- Export OEM experience can teach manufacturers about mature overseas demand patterns.
- Design-led brands benefit when manufacturing, packaging, and quality-control suppliers are nearby.
- A local supply chain can support both OEM production and original consumer brands.

## Evidence

- **Cluster scope:** [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]] says Ningbo has one of China's fuller board-game industry chains and hundreds of local board-game/card suppliers.
- **Production steps:** [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]] names printing, mold opening, injection molding, and packaging as local capabilities.
- **Market learning:** [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]] ties long-term Western OEM experience to understanding overseas family and adult board-game demand.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

- The source does not list all Ningbo board-game manufacturers or verify the top-20 manufacturer count independently.
- A capable supply chain does not automatically create an original brand; Mideer still required design, channel, and safety execution.

## What Changed

- Added Ningbo board-game supply chain as the local toy-manufacturing base behind Mideer's design-led route.
