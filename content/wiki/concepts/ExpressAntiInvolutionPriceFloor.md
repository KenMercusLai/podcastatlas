# 快递反内卷与区域成本底线

## Definition
Express anti-involution price floors are regulatory efforts to stop persistently below-cost parcel pricing by estimating regional cost baselines and sanctioning carriers that undercut them.

## Current Synthesis
The source links the recent campaign to earlier 2021 action in Yiwu. After national policy language shifted from preventing to comprehensively addressing destructive involution, postal regulators again used regional cost floors, interviews, fines, and operating restrictions. Reported 2026 revenue growth then exceeded volume growth, suggesting pricing relief, but not proving a durable solution to franchise or labor incentives.

## Key Claims
- Regional cost floors target competition below sustainable operating cost.
- Enforcement can quickly change industry pricing and profit conditions.
- Higher revenue per parcel may relieve but does not automatically repair last-mile pay.
- Concentrated markets can become more profitable after regulatory coordination.

## Evidence
- Enforcement lineage: [[no-219-kuaidi-jianghu-sanshinian-tonglubang-taobao-pinduoduo-yu-jiage-zhan-1015471748]] connects 2021 Yiwu action with renewed 2025 anti-involution meetings and cost baselines.
- Early outcome: [[no-219-kuaidi-jianghu-sanshinian-tonglubang-taobao-pinduoduo-yu-jiage-zhan-1015471748]] reports 2026 first-half revenue growth above parcel-volume growth.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The evidence is early and source-scoped. Revenue growth can reflect mix, geography, or other changes, and a price floor may protect firms without guaranteeing courier income, service quality, or innovation.
