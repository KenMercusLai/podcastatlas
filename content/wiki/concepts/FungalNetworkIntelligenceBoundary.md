# Fungal Network Intelligence Boundary

## Definition
The fungal network intelligence boundary separates demonstrated fungal sensing, growth, chemical exchange, and electrical activity from stronger claims about language, cognition, consciousness, or communications infrastructure.

## Current Synthesis
Mycelium is a distributed living network that detects conditions, allocates growth, digests material externally, exchanges nutrients, and interacts with other organisms. Patterned electrical activity may provide measurable signals related to those processes. These properties make fungi valuable systems for studying distributed biological coordination.

They do not, by themselves, establish a vocabulary, semantic content, subjective experience, human-like reasoning, or a landscape-scale communications system. “Natural intelligence” is most useful here as a clearly marked metaphor for adaptive decentralized behavior; stronger claims require experiments that link reproducible signal patterns to specific states, interventions, predictions, and functions.

## Key Claims
- Fungal networks exhibit real distributed sensing, resource exchange, growth responses, and electrical activity.
- Recurrent signal patterns are not automatically words or messages with semantic content.
- Adaptive behavior can be described without assuming consciousness or human-like cognition.
- A communication claim needs controlled sender, signal, receiver, and response evidence beyond correlation.
- Conductive or computational applications remain speculative until they outperform simpler physical and biological explanations.

## Evidence
- Ecological network behavior - [[scim1074623919-scim1074623919]] describes nutrient recycling and interaction among fungi, plants, microbes, insects, and animals.
- Electrical patterns - [[scim1074623919-scim1074623919]] reports recurring packets of electrical activity in fungal systems.
- Explicit speculation - [[scim1074623919-scim1074623919]] presents fungal language, conductive landscape networks, and cosmic analogies as hypotheses rather than settled findings.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source supplies no signal dataset, decoding method, intervention study, falsifiable language model, or consciousness measure. Neural analogies can guide questions but may obscure major differences in structure and function. Rejecting premature cognitive labels does not deny fungal complexity or the possibility of future discoveries.

## What Changed
- Created a boundary that preserves fungal signaling evidence without converting it into proof of language or consciousness.
