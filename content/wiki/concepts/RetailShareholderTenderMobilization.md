# Retail Shareholder Tender Mobilization / 散户要约动员

Retail shareholder tender mobilization / 散户要约动员 is the operational layer in [[vol-269-xiao-lishi-bu-yao-pa-shi-jishu-xing-tiaozheng-1004793119]] where [[HongkongLand|香港置地]] had to turn dispersed [[DairyFarmInternational|牛奶公司]] shareholders into tendering participants. The source emphasizes that shares were still paper certificates, so acceptance required concrete instructions and physical handling.

The concept connects corporate control to retail mechanics. If a target company lacks a controlling block, an acquirer can bypass management by making the offer legible and actionable to thousands of holders. In the episode, that meant advertising not only the financial case but also the process.

## Key Claims
- Dispersed shareholders can be a takeover vulnerability when they are reachable through public media and direct procedural instructions.
- Paper-share systems make logistics part of corporate-control strategy.
- Retail mobilization can spill into broader [[RetailInvestorCrowding]] when the takeover becomes a visible market success story.
