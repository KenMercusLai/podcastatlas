# Short Squeeze

## Definition
Short squeeze is a positive-feedback price move in which rising prices or tightening risk constraints force short sellers to buy the security back, and that new demand pushes the price higher enough to pressure additional shorts.

## Current Synthesis
The GameStop case shows why squeeze potential depends on position structure as well as company fundamentals. A short seller may expect a retailer to decline over years, yet still face immediate losses when concentrated short interest, limited tradable shares, social attention, option demand, and risk limits collide. Forced covering can then dominate valuation temporarily. The episode also warns that identifying a squeeze does not identify its peak or guarantee that late buyers can exit profitably.

## Key Claims
- Short covering creates buy demand rather than merely removing a bearish position.
- Rising prices can turn a deliberately small short into a portfolio-level risk.
- Risk limits, margin needs, and investor redemptions can force covering before the fundamental thesis resolves.
- Concentrated short interest and constrained share supply make feedback more fragile.
- A short squeeze can interact with, but is distinct from, dealer hedging in a [[GammaSqueeze]].

## Evidence
- **Forced-covering loop:** [[ep159-gamestop-gupiao-touzi-yulehua-weishenme-jiazhi-touzi-bu-chixiangle-ckwrirwd-dwbabaaaachv6pg]] describes GameStop shorts buying shares as losses grew, creating further upward pressure.
- **Asymmetric exposure:** [[ep159-gamestop-gupiao-touzi-yulehua-weishenme-jiazhi-touzi-bu-chixiangle-ckwrirwd-dwbabaaaachv6pg]] contrasts limited maximum profit with theoretically unbounded loss and gives examples of hedge positions expanding into large portfolio damage.
- **Mixed participants:** [[ep159-gamestop-gupiao-touzi-yulehua-weishenme-jiazhi-touzi-bu-chixiangle-ckwrirwd-dwbabaaaachv6pg]] argues that retail traders may have helped initiate attention while institutions also traded against vulnerable shorts.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
High short interest alone does not guarantee a squeeze, and a squeeze does not validate the issuer's long-term business. The source does not reconstruct every GameStop trade or quantify retail, institutional, covering, and dealer-hedging flows separately. Short-interest and stock-loan data can also be delayed or difficult to interpret.

## What Changed
- Created a distinct forced-short-covering mechanism rather than folding it into gamma hedging.
