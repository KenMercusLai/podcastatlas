# Power Didactic Communication / 权力式说教沟通

## Definition
Power didactic communication is speech that presents instruction as an obligation imposed from above: the speaker treats their view as the listener's required understanding rather than as a claim open to explanation, challenge, or mutual adjustment.

## Current Synthesis
The episode frames "说教" as more than a disliked tone. In unequal relationships, the speaker can stand on parental, teacherly, managerial, elder, male, or success-based authority and convert communication into a command. The core shift is from reason-giving to position-taking: the speaker no longer needs to ask whether the listener's experience, evidence, or refusal matters.

The concept also contains a warning about critique. Naming "爹味" or hierarchy can be accurate when speech reproduces domination, but the label can become a mirror image of didactic shutdown when it is used to end every difficult exchange before hearing what the other person is actually saying.

## Key Claims
- Didactic speech becomes power speech when it defines the listener's proper understanding from above.
- Hierarchy can corrupt language by making reasons, explanations, and responses unnecessary for the person with more authority.
- "For your good" and "there is no room to discuss this" are typical formulas of power-backed instruction.
- The critique of preaching remains valid only if it preserves distinctions between coercive hierarchy, clumsy advice, and ordinary disagreement.
- Anti-preaching language can copy the same hierarchy when it uses labels to terminate conversation rather than reopen agency.

## Evidence
- Hierarchical wording: [[347-daodi-weisha-youxie-shadan-zhihui-yi-shuojiao-de-fangshi-goutong-lhhiozceulxpeyczobgjaolyymmt]] contrasts "you should understand it this way" with a more situated "this is how I understand it."
- Authority positions: [[347-daodi-weisha-youxie-shadan-zhihui-yi-shuojiao-de-fangshi-goutong-lhhiozceulxpeyczobgjaolyymmt]] lists parent-child, teacher-student, boss-subordinate, elder-younger, male-female, and success-failure asymmetries as common settings for preaching.
- Language corruption: [[347-daodi-weisha-youxie-shadan-zhihui-yi-shuojiao-de-fangshi-goutong-lhhiozceulxpeyczobgjaolyymmt]] adapts Acton's power-corruption formula into a claim that power especially corrupts language.
- Mirror risk: [[347-daodi-weisha-youxie-shadan-zhihui-yi-shuojiao-de-fangshi-goutong-lhhiozceulxpeyczobgjaolyymmt]] says anti-preaching can itself become a preaching mirror when "爹味" becomes a blanket conversation-ending verdict.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept does not say that every piece of advice, correction, or uncomfortable view is domination. The episode explicitly separates power-backed speech from clumsy, poor, or fearful communication, and it treats the listener's discomfort as evidence to examine rather than automatic proof of oppression.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to capture the episode's distinction between reasoned speech and authority-backed instruction.
