# Succession as Continuous Entrepreneurship / 接班即持续创业

## Definition

Succession as continuous entrepreneurship / 接班即持续创业 is the view that inheriting a family firm transfers an operating platform, not a finished answer: the successor must continue discovering products, markets, capabilities, relationships, and governance suitable for the next era.

## Current Synthesis

[[ep255-chang-erdai-de-jicheng-zhi-zhan-nianqingren-jieban-hui-chongsu-qiye-ma-likvzmpgwxnetpaezxclsx4s-hkw|EP255]] establishes the concept through factory-second-generation cases. A successor may receive assets, workers, brands, or dealer relationships, but those resources can be stranded when competition shifts toward branding, e-commerce, design, IP, foreign trade, or new consumer trust. A real operating role lets the successor discover where inherited capability still works and where new capability must be built.

The later-stage boundary comes from [[155-he-kewosi-qiancheng-liao-fu-yu-zi-qi-erdai-jieban-de-yangben-yu-jili-jiqiren-he-wo-neixin-de-zhuixun-ltpy6k_plbv7sfr1d4rzchfhwzii|episode 155]]: appointment as CEO does not complete succession. [[QianChengEcovacs|钱程]] argues that the meaningful outcome is whether [[EcovacsRobotics|Ecovacs]] can cross into a new era, retain useful cultural roots, rebuild organization, and keep creating user value. The successor is therefore neither merely a custodian nor an unconstrained founder starting from zero.

## Key Claims

- A family firm can be an inherited platform while still requiring entrepreneurial discovery.
- Proving grounds such as new business, digital commerce, or overseas markets can create successor legitimacy and practical judgment.
- Title transfer does not automatically transfer founder relationships, tacit knowledge, or organizational authority.
- The successor must distinguish cultural roots worth preserving from operating mechanisms that need rebuilding.
- Continuous entrepreneurship is bounded by inherited people, systems, obligations, and capabilities; it is not a greenfield reset.
- Succession is successful when the company renews its ability to create value, not when a formal handoff is declared complete.

## Evidence

- **Inherited platform, new discovery:** [[ep255-chang-erdai-de-jicheng-zhi-zhan-nianqingren-jieban-hui-chongsu-qiye-ma-likvzmpgwxnetpaezxclsx4s-hkw|EP255]] uses [[WoteShoes|沃特鞋企]], [[Fadior|法迪奥]], and other factory-successor cases to show inherited manufacturing meeting new brand, channel, and market work.
- **Operating-role legitimacy:** [[ep255-chang-erdai-de-jicheng-zhi-zhan-nianqingren-jieban-hui-chongsu-qiye-ma-likvzmpgwxnetpaezxclsx4s-hkw|EP255]] identifies new business, digitalization, and overseas trade as comparatively open entry routes.
- **CEO title boundary:** [[155-he-kewosi-qiancheng-liao-fu-yu-zi-qi-erdai-jieban-de-yangben-yu-jili-jiqiren-he-wo-neixin-de-zhuixun-ltpy6k_plbv7sfr1d4rzchfhwzii|episode 155]] says becoming CEO exposed rather than completed Qian Cheng's understanding of the inherited enterprise.
- **Renewal standard:** [[155-he-kewosi-qiancheng-liao-fu-yu-zi-qi-erdai-jieban-de-yangben-yu-jili-jiqiren-he-wo-neixin-de-zhuixun-ltpy6k_plbv7sfr1d4rzchfhwzii|episode 155]] defines succession as a transition whose value depends on the company's continued improvement and adaptation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept can romanticize inheritance by understating ownership privilege, employee risk, family conflict, or the option of professional management and sale. Successors are not necessarily the best entrepreneurs, and novelty is not automatically better than continuity. The two sources are journalistic and interview-based; they do not compare successor-led firms systematically or prove that the highlighted routes improve long-run performance.
