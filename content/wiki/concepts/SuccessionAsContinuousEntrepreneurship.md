# Succession as Continuous Entrepreneurship / 接班即持续创业

Succession as continuous entrepreneurship / 接班即持续创业 is the episode's reframing of [[FactorySecondGeneration|厂二代]] inheritance in [[ep255-chang-erdai-de-jicheng-zhi-zhan-nianqingren-jieban-hui-chongsu-qiye-ma-likvzmpgwxnetpaezxclsx4s-hkw]]. The host cites a Wenzhou successor's formulation that taking over a family firm can feel less like inheriting a finished business and more like rebuilding or continuously starting a company.

The concept resists a passive inheritance frame. A successor may receive assets, factories, workers, brands, or dealer relationships, but those resources can be stranded if the market has shifted from production to branding, e-commerce, design, IP, foreign trade, or new consumer trust.

## Key Claims
- A family firm can be an inherited platform while still requiring entrepreneurial discovery.
- The successor's willingness to stay often depends on finding a real operating role rather than only obeying family duty.
- Rebuilding may happen inside the old category, as with [[WoteShoes|沃特鞋企]], or through category and market education, as with [[Fadior|法迪奥]].
- Succession can also mean using family resources to open a different business direction rather than preserving the old line unchanged.
- The next generation may not be forced into the same path, which makes continuity less absolute and more choice-based.
