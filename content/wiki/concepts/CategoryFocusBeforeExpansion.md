# Category Focus Before Expansion

Category focus before expansion is the startup discipline of going deep enough in one category, geography, or use case before broadening the surface area of the company. In [[tsr-s3-eddylu-v2-tsr-s3-eddylu-v2]], [[EddyLu]] says [[GOAT]] learned focus from [[GrubWithUs]] expanding into many cities before making one city work.

The source applies the lesson to sneakers and apparel. GOAT stayed deep in sneakers before expanding into apparel, while Grub With Us's earlier city expansion made its unresolved marketplace-friction problem harder to fix. The concept is adjacent to [[ScalingBrokenProduct]]: growth can multiply weaknesses when the core loop is not strong yet.

[[advice-line-with-bobbi-brown-of-jones-road-beauty-september-2025-427590c2-11bc-43fa-9c92-d1eccf05842a]] adds a CPG positioning version through [[TannenOralCare]]. The advice is not to remain coffee-only forever, but to broaden only after the company can explain why the product is useful beyond coffee and test that claim in a specific tribe such as a gym, dentist office, or wellness community. [[AbbyRose]] adds a channel version: live social selling may expand through new faces before the company jumps into wholesale.

## Key Claims
- Expansion can hide unresolved product-market fit problems by spreading attention across markets or categories.
- A focused category can let a marketplace build stronger trust, supply depth, culture knowledge, and operating standards.
- New categories should inherit a working operating model, not become a substitute for fixing the first one.
- Category focus can coexist with ambition; the question is sequencing, not smallness.
- Repositioning from a niche origin should still choose a first expansion context where the broader promise can be learned and trusted.
