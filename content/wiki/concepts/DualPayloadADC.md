# Dual-Payload ADC

Dual-payload ADC is the later-stage ADC direction discussed in [[vol-117-shengwu-yiyao-de-2025-chaodi-zhongguo-yanfa-jiaolv-he-xinwang-jiwei-lmhral0rmq6tohiqdwsgmfapnyn7]]. The episode describes ADCs moving from one antibody plus one payload toward bispecific ADCs and then toward bispecific, dual-payload formats.

The source's promise is resistance management and differentiated effect; its warning is manufacturing complexity. Adding another payload can create harder CMC, quality-control, and cost problems, so the episode expects any major BD wave to depend on convincing preclinical or clinical evidence rather than format novelty alone.

## Key Claims
- Dual payloads may help address tumor heterogeneity or resistance if the payloads are chosen well.
- The format adds production and quality-control difficulty.
- [[XiaoPTeacher|小P老师]] treats 2026 or 2027 as a more plausible heavier BD window than immediate 2025 explosion, if data supports the category.
- It is an example of combination innovation rather than an entirely new platform.
