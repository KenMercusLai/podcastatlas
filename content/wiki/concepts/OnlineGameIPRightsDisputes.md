# Online Game IP Rights Disputes

Online game IP rights disputes are the legal and commercial conflicts that arise when a game's development rights, operation rights, derivative products, regional licenses, and revenue shares are split among multiple companies. [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]] adds the concept through [[LegendOfMir|《传奇》]], [[Actozsoft]], [[WeMade]], [[Shanda|盛大]], [[KaiyingNetwork]], and later operators.

The episode shows why early loose contracts become dangerous after explosive growth. A short agency agreement, delayed payments, foreign-exchange explanations, technical-support complaints, and later mobile-game licenses all became part of a dispute field that lasted well beyond the first boom. The same conflict also reveals [[GameIPLongTail]]: parties keep fighting because the IP remains valuable.
