# Cognitive Light Cone

## Definition
A cognitive light cone is the spatial and temporal scale of the largest goals a being can represent and work toward.

## Current Synthesis
The framework compares minds by the reach of their concern rather than by familiar material, body plan, or species membership. A narrow light cone may organize a local physiological state; a wider one may encompass distant people, future generations, or planetary consequences. Because goals can exist at unfamiliar sizes and speeds, observers may fail to recognize agency in organs, cellular collectives, hybrids, or machines.

## Key Claims
- Goal scale offers one dimension for comparing heterogeneous minds.
- Bodies contain overlapping agents with different problem spaces.
- Biological material alone does not determine moral or cognitive reach.
- Compassion can extend a cognitive horizon beyond immediate self-interest.
- Unfamiliar embodiment creates systematic risk of mind blindness.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The framework does not by itself measure consciousness, moral status, intelligence quality, or benevolence. Large goals can be harmful, and observers may infer goals where only mechanistic regularity exists. Operational tests remain outside the supplied summary.
