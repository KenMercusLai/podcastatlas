## Definition

Infanticide custom correction / 杀婴风俗纠偏 is the governance pattern where an official confronts a normalized harmful local practice by treating it as punishable homicide and using a morally salient case to reset communal expectations.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-874-zhenjing-muzi-xiangcan-tianli-nanrong-lpr3eglbnwbve1clrttapw6k5zdq|Hanji 874]] creates this concept through [[JiaBiaoLateHan|贾彪]]'s service as Xinxi magistrate. The source says local custom tolerated killing infants, but Jia Biao forbids it and treats the killing of children as homicide. When a bandit-killing case and a mother-child killing case arrive together, he prioritizes the latter because a mother killing her own child is presented as a deeper breach of heavenly and human order.

The key synthesis is that law and moral signal work together. Jia Biao does not merely condemn the custom; he makes enforcement visible enough that local expectations change. The episode says bandits surrender and thousands of children later grow up under the changed custom, remembered as "Jia father's sons." The page therefore treats the case as a source-bounded example of local governance using law to reverse a normalized practice without reducing order to fear alone.

## Key Claims

- A harmful custom can become politically urgent when normalization hides the victim or makes violence look ordinary.
- Treating infant-killing as homicide converts moral shock into administrable law rather than leaving it as private lament.
- Enforcement priority can signal which violation attacks the basic moral order most directly.
- Publicly legible correction can change expectations beyond the individual case.
- The pattern remains qualified because moral salience can guide enforcement well, but it can also overrun due process if detached from evidence.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept is source-bounded to the episode's account of Jia Biao's county governance. It does not quantify infant-killing, prove demographic scale outside the source, or claim that every morally salient enforcement choice is legitimate. The useful pattern is correction of normalized harm through law, priority, and public meaning; the risk is that moral shock can become arbitrary punishment if evidence and procedure are absent.

## What Changed

- Hanji 874 creates the concept from Jia Biao's anti-infanticide enforcement in Xinxi.
- The current judgment separates moral-order enforcement from later Party Prohibition rescue, while keeping both within Jia Biao's broader timing-and-responsibility profile.
