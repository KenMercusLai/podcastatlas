# Niche Origin Story Repositioning

Niche origin story repositioning is the move from a very specific founding use case into a broader category promise while keeping the original story as proof and memory. In [[advice-line-with-bobbi-brown-of-jones-road-beauty-september-2025-427590c2-11bc-43fa-9c92-d1eccf05842a]], [[TannenOralCare]] begins as a post-coffee mouth rinse, but [[BobbiBrown]] and [[GuyRaz]] argue that coffee-only packaging may limit a product that could also serve wine, tea, stomach issues, general freshness, and wellness use cases.

The concept is a boundary condition for [[StoryLedConsumerBranding]] and [[CategoryFocusBeforeExpansion]]. The origin story should not disappear, because it explains why the founder noticed the problem. But if the story becomes the whole category definition too early, customers who have the broader need may assume the product is not for them.

## Key Claims
- A narrow origin can make a product memorable, but it can also overconstrain who thinks the product is relevant.
- Repositioning should preserve the credibility of the origin story while clarifying the broader functional benefit.
- Ingredient, taste, packaging, and usage language have to change together; a broader promise cannot live only in founder explanation.
- The first expansion should still choose a tribe or channel where the broader promise can be tested credibly.
