# Textile Knowledge Transfer

Textile knowledge transfer is the source's concrete version of development learning in [[getting-entrepreneurial-in-korea-summer-school]]. The episode follows [[AbdulMajidChowdhury]] and [[Daewoo]] as 128 Bangladeshis train in Korea for six months before returning to open [[DeshGarments]] in [[Bangladesh]].

The concept emphasizes that manufacturing capability travels through people, routines, factory organization, and cross-cultural adjustment. It also remains qualified by institutional conditions: the later garment boom created jobs and export scale, while [[RanaPlaza]] shows how weak safety and public capacity can turn rapid industrialization into disaster risk.
