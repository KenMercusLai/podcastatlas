# 人单合一 / RenDanHeYi

人单合一 / RenDanHeYi is the [[HaierGroup|Haier]] management model discussed in [[79-gewei-lingdao-danfan-zan-xuedian-boyilun-690004262]]. The episode describes it as an attempt to turn internal colleagues into customer-like relationships and make employees compete more directly around user orders or market signals.

The source does not treat the model as universally bad; it treats it as a limit case. Market mechanisms can raise initiative when an organization is too planned or rigid, but [[ContractTheory]] asks why the firm exists at all if every internal relation should become a market transaction.

## Key Claims
- Internal marketization can increase competitive pressure and make some contribution signals more visible.
- The model becomes risky when applied to tasks that depend on trust, proprietary knowledge, incomplete contracts, or long-run collaboration.
- A firm can be more efficient than a market when it reduces repeated bargaining, monitoring, and transaction costs.
