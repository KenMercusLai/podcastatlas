# 五代继承危机 / Five Dynasties Succession Crisis

Five Dynasties succession crisis names the "王二代" problem in [[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]]. The source argues that founders who rose through war often struggled to transmit stable rule to heirs raised under different conditions.

The episode gives several mechanisms: comfort after hardship, selection and palace politics that eliminated better candidates, and the difficult shift from martial founding to civil administration. [[ChaiRong|柴荣]] is presented as a rare powerful second-generation ruler, which makes his early death especially consequential.
