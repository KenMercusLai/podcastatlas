# Founder Agreement Documentation

## Definition

Founder agreement documentation is the discipline of converting expectations about equity, roles, authority, cash, leave, return, crisis decisions, and company formation into written commitments before memory and incentives diverge.

## Current Synthesis

The [[StarlingBank]] account shows the ownership and formation version: [[TomBlomfield]] says informal equity assurances and personal cash support left him exposed when his relationship with [[AnneBoden]] broke down. The [[SeventhGeneration]] account shows the operating-role version: [[AlanNewman]] and [[JeffreyHollender]] never documented what a six-month sabbatical meant for Alan's return, position, or responsibilities. Written agreements do not eliminate betrayal, power conflict, or changing circumstances, but they narrow the space in which incompatible memories can masquerade as a shared plan.

## Key Claims

- Verbal founder promises become fragile when financing, firing, exhaustion, or power changes enter the relationship.
- Equity, personal cash, employment, creditor status, and founder authority should not be allowed to blur together.
- Leave and sabbatical agreements should specify duration, communication, decision rights, return conditions, and expected role.
- Crisis procedures matter because the moment of maximum ambiguity is often the worst time to negotiate first principles.
- Documentation supports trust by making expectations inspectable; it cannot substitute for continuing communication or good faith.

## Evidence

- Ownership and formation ambiguity: [[tsr-s5-tomblomfield-v2-audio-tsr-s5-tomblomfield-v2-audio]] records Tom's account of informal Starling equity expectations, personal funding of engineers, firings, and the eventual regrouping that formed [[Monzo]].
- Leave and return ambiguity: [[seventh-generation-alan-newman-and-jeffrey-hollender-a-partnership-that-flourished-until-it-failed-2021-cf1c5327-05ed-44de-b54e-e2d81fb740fa]] records that Alan and Jeffrey had no written agreement about Alan's sabbatical return, role, or responsibilities before Jeffrey ended his time at the company.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Both cases are founder-side retrospective accounts of disputed relationships rather than adjudicated legal histories. A document can still be incomplete, unenforceable, coercive, or overtaken by changed circumstances. The practical claim is not that paperwork prevents conflict, but that material expectations should be explicit enough to discuss, revise, and enforce.

## What Changed

- Extended the concept from equity and company formation into sabbatical, return, and operating-role agreements.
- Added communication cadence and crisis procedure as documentation subjects.
- Clarified that documents narrow ambiguity but do not replace relationship maintenance.
