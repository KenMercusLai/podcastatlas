# Depression Mood-System Frame

## Definition
Depression mood-system frame interprets low mood through an interacting system of biology, environment, relationships, temperament, behavior, and perceived threats or blocked goals while distinguishing potentially informative low mood from severe destructive illness.

## Current Synthesis
In [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-d08500083dda91b814b053f30bd7b557-87666361-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-d08500083dda91b814b053f30bd7b557-87666361|The Story of Stories]], [[JonathanRottenberg]] offers the frame as an alternative to treating depression only as an internal defect or simple chemical imbalance. Low mood may sometimes slow effort, direct attention toward an unresolved problem, or register adverse social and structural conditions. Reframing can reduce shame and help ask what is changeable.

The qualification is part of the model. Severe depression can be chronic, incapacitating, reality-distorting, physically harmful, and fatal; it need not carry a lesson or silver lining. Medication, psychotherapy, activity, social support, new pursuits, reflection, and time may each help some people, and medication decisions remain with the individual and treating professional. Contextual meaning complements rather than replaces assessment, crisis support, and treatment.

## Key Claims
- Mood reflects interacting bodily, psychological, relational, behavioral, and environmental conditions rather than one universal defect.
- Low mood can sometimes support pausing or reevaluation without making every episode adaptive.
- Shame and self-criticism can add a second burden to the primary mood state.
- Economic, political, social, and environmental conditions can contribute to distress.
- Severe clinical depression may destroy function and contact with reality and can require urgent treatment.
- Care is pluralistic; no single interpretation, behavior, therapy, or medication is universally sufficient.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode does not establish that clinical depression evolved for a current benefit, that every episode contains actionable information, or that environmental explanation excludes biological treatment. Listener stories cannot establish treatment effects, and creativity associations should not be generalized. Persistent, severe, suicidal, psychotic, bipolar-spectrum, or otherwise dangerous symptoms require qualified assessment, and no medication should be changed on this frame alone.

## What Changed
- Established a contextual mood-system interpretation with severe-illness, no-silver-lining, and treatment-plurality boundaries.
