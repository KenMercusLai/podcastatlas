# Jingpiao Creative Ceramic Ecology / 景漂创意陶瓷生态

Jingpiao Creative Ceramic Ecology / 景漂创意陶瓷生态 is the contemporary creator ecosystem in [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]]. After the decline of the [[StateOwnedCeramicFactorySystem]], the city still had materials, makers, kiln access, workshops, schools, sales channels, and factory spaces that lowered entry barriers for young ceramic creators.

The source uses [[LottePotteryWorkshop|乐天陶社]], Saturday markets, old factory reuse, and [[Taoxichuan|陶溪川]] to show how a manufacturing city can become a creative-city platform. The same ecology also creates pressure around copied designs, homogeneity, master titles, tourist demand, and [[CeramicLivestreamCommerceRisk]].
