# Rodent-Borne Infection Prevention

## Definition
Rodent-borne infection prevention is the practical prevention frame in the hantavirus episode: reduce rodent access and nesting, avoid contact with wild rodents and their excreta, disinfect before disturbing dust, and clean in ways that do not aerosolize contaminated material.

## Current Synthesis
The source treats prevention as environmental control plus careful cleanup. [[ZihanDoctor|子涵医生]] links hantavirus risk to wild rodents, rodent urine, saliva, feces, contaminated dust, food or water, bites, scratches, and broken-skin or mucosal exposure. Prevention therefore centers on sealing rodent entry points, removing food and shelter, disinfecting surfaces before sweeping or handling debris, using gloves and masks in old rooms or storage spaces, washing hands, and disposing of contaminated material without secondary exposure.

## Key Claims
- The main prevention target is the rodent reservoir and contaminated environment, not ordinary human contact.
- City high-rise homes, rural houses, storage rooms, campsites, and long-vacant spaces have different practical exposure points but share the same control logic.
- Wet disinfection before cleaning is safer than immediately sweeping or vacuuming potentially contaminated dust.
- Protective behavior is simple but sequence-sensitive: mask and gloves, disinfect, wipe gently, bag waste, remove protection correctly, and wash hands.
- Pet risk is low when animals come from controlled environments, but contact between pets and wild rodents should be avoided.

## Evidence
- Rodent reservoir: [[vol-205-xiangbi-hantanbingdu-ni-genggai-liaojie-hantan-hancheng-yaxing-chuxian-zhege-zhengzhuang-yiding-like-qu-yiyuan-lhze9i64h8ifso_on-1cmnskxtng]] describes rodents as the natural host and humans as accidental victims exposed to rodent excreta.
- Household and rural control: [[vol-205-xiangbi-hantanbingdu-ni-genggai-liaojie-hantan-hancheng-yaxing-chuxian-zhege-zhengzhuang-yiding-like-qu-yiyuan-lhze9i64h8ifso_on-1cmnskxtng]] summarizes prevention as cutting off rodent food, clearing nests, and sealing routes.
- Cleaning SOP: [[vol-205-xiangbi-hantanbingdu-ni-genggai-liaojie-hantan-hancheng-yaxing-chuxian-zhege-zhengzhuang-yiding-like-qu-yiyuan-lhze9i64h8ifso_on-1cmnskxtng]] recommends masks, gloves, disinfectant spraying or wiping, avoiding dry sweeping and vacuuming before disinfection, and handwashing after cleanup.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This concept is limited to source-scoped public education. Actual cleanup of heavy infestation, occupational exposure, travel exposure, suspected infection, or high-risk medical contexts may require local public-health, pest-control, occupational-safety, or clinical guidance.
