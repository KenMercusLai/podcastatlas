# Art As Revenge / 艺术作为复仇

Art as revenge is the source-scoped reading that a work of art can preserve traumatic evidence and return it against the power that caused the trauma. [[17-diyubian-zhongsheng-jie-ku-yi-nian-ru-mo-561780565]] adds the concept through [[HellScreen|《地狱变》]]: Yoshihide's completed screen can be read not only as artistic obsession, but as a record of Horikawa's cruelty and a visual accusation against a whole lordly world.

The concept is ethically unstable by design. The episode does not excuse Yoshihide's cruelty to apprentices or his failure to save his daughter, and it does not claim that art redeems the death. The harder claim is that in a world where power can kill openly and still be praised, art may be the only surviving form of counter-witness.

## Key Claims
- Art can become revenge when it fixes an atrocity in a form power cannot easily absorb as praise.
- This does not make the artist innocent; revenge art can still be built from harm.
- The value of the artwork depends partly on whether it exposes power or merely consumes suffering.
- The viewer's moral discomfort is part of the work's pressure rather than an error to resolve too quickly.
