# Intelligence Devaluation

## Definition

Intelligence devaluation is the reduction in scarcity, bargaining power, or status attached to routine cognitive capability when AI makes knowledge, coding, drafting, analysis, or other intellectual execution much cheaper to obtain.

## Current Synthesis

[[zhili-bianzhi-de-chunjie-jianwenlu-yu-nachang-zhengzai-yunniang-de-youdai-weiji-1]] frames AI as repricing education, coding, professional knowledge, and the income stability built around them, while retaining domain observation, deployment, trust, and customer understanding as scarcer complements. [[136-quanqiu-da-moxing-jibao-di-9-ji-he-guang-miliao-coding-shi-agi-di-er-mu-guigu-yusanjia-zhenxiang-moxing-zheng-chengwei-xin-yidai-os-lh-cqyoss-dztmyb5kmbjapa6w9v]] intensifies the mechanism through coding agents that turn digital work into model capability, compute, and tokens. [[suanli-kuangxiangqu-wo-zai-ai-gongchang-de-qiyu-lorijulltfhttspka22jnn4qjf-i]] adds the normative boundary: human value cannot be reduced to speed or output volume without delegating life itself. [[lhhxptqoanrsmgih0htfelq-znbg-lhhxptqoanrsmgih0htfelq-znbg]] adds a distribution warning: cheap intelligence may concentrate wealth and decision power, and basic transfers may preserve consumption without preserving autonomy.

## Key Claims

- AI can commoditize some cognitive execution without making intelligence useless.
- Coding is an early visible case, while deployment, verification, product judgment, and responsibility remain scarce.
- Lower capability cost can expand access and reduce credential premiums at the same time.
- Labor repricing can propagate into household credit, consumption, and institutional stability.
- Redistribution addresses resources more directly than voice, autonomy, or control over life choices.
- Human value remains broader than efficient output and includes relationships, situated judgment, care, and chosen participation.

## Evidence

### Labor and credential repricing
- [[zhili-bianzhi-de-chunjie-jianwenlu-yu-nachang-zhengzai-yunniang-de-youdai-weiji-1]] links cheaper cognition to coding, education, professional work, product homogeneity, and prime-borrower risk.
- [[136-quanqiu-da-moxing-jibao-di-9-ji-he-guang-miliao-coding-shi-agi-di-er-mu-guigu-yusanjia-zhenxiang-moxing-zheng-chengwei-xin-yidai-os-lh-cqyoss-dztmyb5kmbjapa6w9v]] links coding agents and model platforms to junior, consulting, SaaS, and outsourcing pressure.

### Human meaning and distribution
- [[suanli-kuangxiangqu-wo-zai-ai-gongchang-de-qiyu-lorijulltfhttspka22jnn4qjf-i]] uses satire to distinguish useful delegation from allowing agents to perform life on a person's behalf.
- [[lhhxptqoanrsmgih0htfelq-znbg-lhhxptqoanrsmgih0htfelq-znbg]] connects cheap intelligence to wealth concentration, UBI skepticism, personal relationships, and retained responsibility.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

- The sources offer scenarios and practitioner judgments rather than causal labor-market estimates or distribution models.
- AI can complement workers, create new demand, and raise output as well as substitute for tasks; effects vary by occupation and institution.
- UBI is discussed as a concern rather than evaluated through a specific policy design, funding mechanism, or empirical program.
- Satirical and personal examples clarify values but do not measure economy-wide outcomes.

## What Changed

- Added wealth and decision-power concentration as distinct from wage pressure.
- Added the qualification that material transfers alone may not preserve autonomy or civic voice.
