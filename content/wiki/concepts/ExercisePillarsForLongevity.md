# Exercise Pillars For Longevity

## Definition
Exercise pillars for longevity are the source's four-part physical-healthspan framework: strength, stability, aerobic efficiency, and aerobic peak output, with added attention to power, landing, and fall resilience.

## Current Synthesis
The episode treats exercise as protection against decline rather than only fitness or appearance. Attia emphasizes that after age 65, hip or femur fractures can carry high one-year mortality, and that aging especially erodes type 2 fast-twitch fibers, reducing speed, power, and reactive capacity before ordinary strength is fully gone.

The practical synthesis combines measurable fitness and real-world movement competence. Strength, stability, zone 2 aerobic base, and VO2 max-style peak output are all needed, while jumping, landing, eccentric control, step-ups, and step-downs preserve the ability to brake, react, and avoid falls. This is adjacent to muscle infrastructure but more explicitly organized around the four exercise pillars and anti-fall skill.

## Key Claims
- Physical healthspan requires more than muscle size or gym-machine strength.
- Strength and stability support daily function and fall resistance.
- Zone 2 training is framed as the width of the aerobic base.
- VO2 max is framed as the height of aerobic peak output.
- Aging-related fast-twitch fiber loss makes power and speed important, not optional.
- Jumping, landing, eccentric control, and slow step-ups or step-downs train braking and movement skill.
- Stability is hard to define and train but central for aging well.

## Evidence
- Fall consequence: [[improve-vitality-emotional-physical-health-lifespan-dr-peter-attia-scim5907126956]] says hip or femur fracture after age 65 is associated with roughly 15-30% mortality within 12 months, depending on the study.
- Fiber loss: [[improve-vitality-emotional-physical-health-lifespan-dr-peter-attia-scim5907126956]] says aging particularly affects type 2 fast-twitch fibers, often reducing speed before strength or size.
- Training examples: [[improve-vitality-emotional-physical-health-lifespan-dr-peter-attia-scim5907126956]] recommends strength, reactivity, explosive work, jumping, landing, eccentric control, and slow step-ups or step-downs.
- Four pillars: [[improve-vitality-emotional-physical-health-lifespan-dr-peter-attia-scim5907126956]] lists strength, stability, aerobic efficiency, and aerobic peak output.
- Real-life competence: [[improve-vitality-emotional-physical-health-lifespan-dr-peter-attia-scim5907126956]] notes that gym machines can help resistance training but do not fully train real-life stability.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode summary is not an individualized exercise prescription. Injury history, cardiovascular disease, osteoporosis, frailty, disability, neurological conditions, medications, pregnancy, sport goals, and access to coaching can change appropriate strength, impact, aerobic, or stability work.
