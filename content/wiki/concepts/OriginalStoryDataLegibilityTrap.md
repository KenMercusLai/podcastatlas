## Definition
The original-story data legibility trap is the tendency for investors and platforms to prefer adaptations, established IP, stars, or measured audience signals because they are easier to value and control than an original screenplay with no prior behavioral data.

## Current Synthesis
The trap is circular. Original work lacks historical metrics because it is new; decision-makers then treat that absence as evidence of excess risk and allocate resources to already measurable material. Repeated preference for legible IP can further shrink the production record from which original ideas might prove themselves.

[[SongFangjin|宋方金]] links this structure to a shift from making works, through making products, toward making financial products. His periodization is source-scoped, but the durable mechanism is that financial comparability can displace story judgment and turn creators into controlled execution layers.

## Key Claims
- Prior audience data can make existing IP easier to finance without making it creatively stronger.
- Originality and ex ante measurability are structurally in tension.
- Data can inform judgment but becomes dangerous when it replaces judgment or can be gamed.
- Control preference can be mislabeled as a shortage of talented, cooperative writers.
- A healthier portfolio needs room for original work, adaptation, and selective remake rather than one dominant category.

## Evidence
- Data gate: [[lt24pcobvqnyxjeven2dwhuhvmfu-lt24pcobvqnyxjeven2dwhuhvmfu]] records Song's claim that platforms often demand existing data that an original script cannot possess.
- IP control: [[lt24pcobvqnyxjeven2dwhuhvmfu-lt24pcobvqnyxjeven2dwhuhvmfu]] says large IP became attractive because capital could evaluate and control it while reducing writers, directors, and actors to execution roles.
- Judgment failure: [[lt24pcobvqnyxjeven2dwhuhvmfu-lt24pcobvqnyxjeven2dwhuhvmfu]] uses the phrase “听话的好编剧” and argues that many investors cannot reliably identify an unusual good script.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not establish the relative commercial performance of original and adapted projects, nor does it show that all platform data are false or creatively harmful. Adaptation can carry craft, originality, and audience value; original projects can fail for reasons unrelated to financing. The concept targets overreliance on legibility, not measurement itself.
