## Definition
Three-refusals integrity is a public-office discipline in which an official protects judgment by refusing attachment to wine, sexual entanglement, and money. In the current wiki it is anchored in Yang Bing's late-Han profile and functions as a practical counter-model to eunuch privilege, bribery, and palace-mediated corruption.

## Current Synthesis
The concept is not a generic moral slogan. It describes a concrete boundary system for officials operating inside captured institutions: do not let appetite, intimacy, or money become leverage points that others can use to bend public judgment.

In the 165 CE layer, this discipline matters because ordinary enforcement is repeatedly reversed by eunuch access. Yang Bing's refusal of wine, sexual entanglement, and money shows one way an official preserves the credibility and independence needed to punish corruption and recommend remonstrants.

## Key Claims
- Integrity functions as a boundary system, not only as private reputation.
- Refusing money blocks the most direct route from office to personal extraction.
- Refusing wine and sexual entanglement reduces the social and intimate channels through which power can capture judgment.
- The concept gains political meaning only when paired with office action, such as punishing corrupt officials or recommending critics.
- Three-refusals discipline contrasts with eunuch-mediated protection, where proximity and favors turn law against enforcement.

- Office-action test: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-866-zhenzheng-huomingbai-de-ren-dongde-sanbuzhan-lhhmmyjfo6mkf-qs6bhhzu8hhln6|Hanji 866]] pairs the integrity profile with Yang Bing and Zhou Jing's punishment of corrupt officials.

- Contrast environment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-866-zhenzheng-huomingbai-de-ren-dongde-sanbuzhan-lhhmmyjfo6mkf-qs6bhhzu8hhln6|Hanji 866]] sets Yang Bing's clean discipline beside cases where bribery and eunuch access protect Yang Yuanqun, Su Kang, and Guan Ba.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept does not claim that personal restraint alone repairs a captured court. Hanji 866 places Yang Bing's integrity beside persistent enforcement reversal, showing that clean officials need institutional backing and ruler responsiveness to convert virtue into durable correction.

## What Changed
- Created the concept from Yang Bing's "three nots" profile.
- Defined the three refusals as a public-office control system rather than a lifestyle maxim.
- Connected personal restraint to anti-corruption enforcement and remonstrance credibility.
