## Definition
Three-refusals integrity is a public-office discipline in which an official protects judgment by refusing attachment to wine, sexual entanglement, and money. In the current wiki it is anchored in Yang Bing's late-Han profile and functions as a practical counter-model to gift capture, eunuch privilege, bribery, and palace-mediated corruption.

## Current Synthesis
The concept is not a generic moral slogan. It describes a concrete boundary system for officials operating inside captured institutions: do not let appetite, intimacy, or money become leverage points that others can use to bend public judgment.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-836-xiongdi-fanmu-beihou-ruzhe-he-jianchen-de-jiaofeng-lq9wjn5c8mwt-onrtslum4fw53eq|Hanji 836]] backfills the practice as long-term formation rather than late-life branding. Yang Bing is shown taking only enough official pay for basic life, blocking extra gifts and family bribery, remaining unmoved by wine, sexual entanglement, and money, and refusing a former subordinate's large cash gift even when poverty leaves him eating every other day. The key test is scarcity: the refusal matters because the gift would solve an immediate private need at the cost of public independence.

In the 165 CE layer, this discipline matters because ordinary enforcement is repeatedly reversed by eunuch access. Yang Bing's refusal of wine, sexual entanglement, and money shows one way an official preserves the credibility and independence needed to punish corruption and recommend remonstrants.

## Key Claims
- Integrity functions as a boundary system, not only as private reputation.
- The discipline becomes stronger evidence when it holds under scarcity and under gift offers from subordinates.
- Refusing money blocks the most direct route from office to personal extraction.
- Refusing wine and sexual entanglement reduces the social and intimate channels through which power can capture judgment.
- The concept gains political meaning only when paired with office action, such as punishing corrupt officials or recommending critics.
- Three-refusals discipline contrasts with eunuch-mediated protection, where proximity and favors turn law against enforcement.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept does not claim that personal restraint alone repairs a captured court. Hanji 836 shows Yang Bing's warning can be ignored even when his integrity is strong. Hanji 866 places Yang Bing's integrity beside persistent enforcement reversal, showing that clean officials need institutional backing and ruler responsiveness to convert virtue into durable correction.

## What Changed
- Hanji 836 backfills the "three nots" as an earlier life practice tied to clean pay, anti-bribery family boundaries, and gift refusal under poverty.
- The concept now treats scarcity-tested refusal as central evidence, not only a reputation formula.
- Hanji 866 remains the office-action layer that connects personal restraint to anti-corruption enforcement and remonstrance credibility.
