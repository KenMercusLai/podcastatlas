# Low-Barrier Collaborative Production

## Definition
Low-barrier collaborative production is a design in which participants can make small, immediately useful contributions to a shared artifact without first completing a credentialing process or producing a finished work alone.

## Current Synthesis
The [[Nupedia]]-to-[[Wikipedia]] transition separates openness from absence of standards. Nupedia placed quality control before participation through credentials and seven review stages; Wikipedia let a correct stub or edit enter immediately, then exposed it to expansion, correction, sourcing, and community rules. The advantage was not that crowds are automatically right, but that lowering the unit of contribution increased both production and the number of people able to inspect errors.

## Key Claims
- Contribution systems scale more easily when useful work can be smaller than a finished publication.
- High entry friction can suppress qualified participation without guaranteeing correctness.
- Shared editable artifacts reduce version conflict and let contributions compound rather than arrive as incompatible documents.
- Post-publication correction can widen scrutiny, but it still requires norms, provenance, and governance.
- Rapid output is not sufficient evidence of quality; the design works only when errors remain visible and corrigible.

## Evidence
- Review-first limit - [[vmp5702291377-vmp5702291377]] reports that Nupedia produced 21 articles in its first year despite a seven-stage expert process and still published plagiarism.
- Small-unit participation - [[vmp5702291377-vmp5702291377]] says Wikipedia contributors could start with a minimal accurate statement that others expanded.
- Shared artifact - [[vmp5702291377-vmp5702291377]] contrasts wiki editing with emailed Word documents that produced incompatible revisions.
- Scale result - [[vmp5702291377-vmp5702291377]] says Wikipedia surpassed Nupedia's article count in roughly two weeks.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The case does not prove that every domain should replace expert review with immediate public editing. Safety-critical, private, adversarial, or legally constrained work may require stronger gates. Wikipedia's output figures and one Nupedia plagiarism case do not independently measure comparative accuracy; low barriers create value only when correction, sourcing, dispute resolution, and abuse controls also work.

## What Changed
- Established the concept from the Nupedia-Wikipedia comparison while separating participation scale from automatic reliability.
