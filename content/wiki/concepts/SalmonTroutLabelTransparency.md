# Salmon-Trout Label Transparency / 鲑鳟商品名透明

Salmon-trout label transparency is [[ep260-yikou-feimei-zhifang-cangzhe-zhongguo-yangzhi-de-xin-gushi-linjq2fkzhqvzjcgrmhudq12vs4j]]'s consumer-trust frame for the Chinese "三文鱼" dispute. The episode explains that "三文鱼" began as a Cantonese transliteration of salmon, but in Chinese commerce it can be used narrowly for Atlantic salmon or more broadly for salmonid fish, including [[RainbowTrout|虹鳟]].

The concept treats disclosure as more important than winning a naming fight. A package that says "三文鱼（虹鳟）" gives the consumer more usable information than a restaurant menu that says only "三文鱼" while hiding origin, species, or production method.

## Key Claims
- Taxonomic ambiguity becomes a consumer problem when menus and packaging hide the actual species or origin.
- A broad industry definition can be defensible only if it is paired with visible disclosure.
- Restaurant menus are the weak point because consumers often cannot inspect origin, species, safety testing, or farming method.
- Label transparency also protects domestic producers: disclosed [[RainbowTrout|rainbow trout]] can compete on freshness without relying on imported-salmon confusion.
