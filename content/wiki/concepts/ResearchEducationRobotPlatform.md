# Research Education Robot Platform

## Definition
Research education robot platform is a robotics commercialization wedge where schools, labs, developers, and early startups buy or use robot bodies to run experiments, teach, publish, and build software before the robot is mature enough for broad home or industrial deployment.

## Current Synthesis
In [[22-sui-de-jushen-ceo-5-lun-rongzi-guo-yi-meiyuan-buzhi-tian-you-duo-gao-yinian-chile-shi-nian-de-ku-duitan-huangyi-luobo-paidui-chuangshiren-ceo-ljwdmocryiblf0k3rkz8eonkqox]], [[LuoboPaidui]] uses [[RPORobot|RPO]] and [[RP1Robot|RP1]] to pursue this wedge. [[HuangYiRobotics|Huang Yi]] argues that research and education customers are especially valuable because they understand the product, generate technical feedback, help each other, request platform-level interfaces, and can create papers or ecosystem reuse that compounds beyond a single sale.

## Key Claims
- Research and education customers can be more valuable than early consumer attention because they supply precise technical feedback and credibility.
- A robot platform for this market must expose enough interfaces, synchronization, and data support for serious experimentation.
- Education/research demand can validate manufacturing and support needs before broader home, entertainment, or industrial commercialization.
- The wedge still requires productization: customers need safe, stable, shippable hardware rather than a fragile demo platform.
- Customer composition matters because research, factory, and entertainment users pull the product toward different feature sets.

## Evidence
- Technical feedback and credibility: [[22-sui-de-jushen-ceo-5-lun-rongzi-guo-yi-meiyuan-buzhi-tian-you-duo-gao-yinian-chile-shi-nian-de-ku-duitan-huangyi-luobo-paidui-chuangshiren-ceo-ljwdmocryiblf0k3rkz8eonkqox]] says RPO customers include schools and startups, and that research users can generate higher compound value.
- Interfaces and experimentation: [[22-sui-de-jushen-ceo-5-lun-rongzi-guo-yi-meiyuan-buzhi-tian-you-duo-gao-yinian-chile-shi-nian-de-ku-duitan-huangyi-luobo-paidui-chuangshiren-ceo-ljwdmocryiblf0k3rkz8eonkqox]] lists requests around hands, EtherCAT, hard synchronization, head design, and egocentric data.
- Productization and customer composition: [[22-sui-de-jushen-ceo-5-lun-rongzi-guo-yi-meiyuan-buzhi-tian-you-duo-gao-yinian-chile-shi-nian-de-ku-duitan-huangyi-luobo-paidui-chuangshiren-ceo-ljwdmocryiblf0k3rkz8eonkqox]] describes RP1's fixes, RPO delivery constraints, and Huang's split of research-education, factory, and entertainment/customer categories.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This wedge can create platform learning without proving mass-market demand. The source itself says Luobo Paidui has temporarily deprioritized pretraining, home, and entertainment scenarios, and that industrial needs such as 24-hour work require different optimization.

## What Changed
- Created this concept to capture research and education as a robotics platform wedge.
- Added RPO/RP1 as an example where early customer learning sits between prototype proof and wider humanoid commercialization.
