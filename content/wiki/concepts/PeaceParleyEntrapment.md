# Peace-Parley Entrapment / 和谈诱捕

Peace-parley entrapment / 和谈诱捕 is the deception pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-22-gongsunyang-zha-wei-gongzi-ang-lss1l7lojfwgwdtcje7m5ijodpxi]] uses to explain [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]]'s capture of [[GongziAng|公子卬 / 公子昂]]. Instead of faking battlefield weakness, Shang Yang turns a diplomatic ritual into a trap: old acquaintance, alliance oath, shared drink, and mutual withdrawal become the cover for hidden armed men.

The tactic works because the false signal is moral and relational, not logistical. Gongzi Ang wants to believe that an old contact can separate public duty from private friendship and end the campaign without bloodshed. The episode treats that as [[MotivatedBias|动机偏差]]: he notices the attractive peace story but not the opposing incentive, namely that [[QinState|秦国]] would be retreating from an invasion with no gain.

The concept sits beside [[DecreasingStovesDeception|减灶诱敌]] as another Warring States deception form. Maling uses controlled physical evidence to activate [[PangJuan|庞涓]]'s contempt for Qi; Shang Yang's parley trap uses controlled social evidence to activate Gongzi Ang's trust in status, old ties, and peace rhetoric.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-23-weihuiwang-hui-buyong-gongshu-zhi-yan-lveaqklo7oncmqudgmdk7l03qhsf]] adds the tactic's payoff and cost. The capture lets Qin break Wei and win Hexi, but the episode also treats the trap as a case of [[TacticalCredibilityCost|战术信用成本]]: because the deception spends personal trust, it later helps explain why [[WeiState|魏国]] refuses to receive Shang Yang when he flees.

## Key Claims
- A parley, oath, or banquet can become a military instrument when one side controls the setting.
- Deception can operate through friendship and moral language as well as through battlefield signals.
- A peace proposal should be tested against incentives: if the proposer bears the cost, the offer may be a lure.
- The target's preferred outcome can make warning signs feel irrelevant.
- In this source, the trap converts commander capture into potential bargaining leverage.
- In the sequel source, the trap produces major territorial gain but damages the deceiver's future refuge options.
