# Work-Rest Boundary / 工作休假边界

Work-rest boundary is [[ep265-dang-qing3xiu13-chongshang-resou-women-ruhe-gei-rensheng-fang-yige-changjia-lsloczlnkmcrrfpn9coh8rrqo-wb]]'s practical condition for a holiday to become rest rather than remote work in a nicer setting. [[LeiTao|雷涛 / 无二旅人]] says the vacationer's mindset matters, but coworkers and bosses must also refrain from interrupting a person who is on leave.

The concept extends [[WorkSelfWorthBoundary]] and [[WorkplacePacing]] from daily work into sanctioned time off. A person can decide to put the phone away, but if the organization treats leave as standby time, the boundary is structurally weak. Conversely, coworkers who absorb or defer work can make rest a shared practice rather than a private act of will.

## Key Claims
- Leave requires a communication boundary, not only a travel booking.
- Boss and coworker behavior determines whether rest is socially safe.
- Pre-vacation overwork can sabotage rest by making the person enter the holiday already depleted.
- Vacation planning should include handoff, response expectations, and what truly counts as emergency contact.
- Time zones and travel distance can help, but they do not replace explicit workplace norms.
