# Work-Rest Boundary / 工作休假边界

## Definition
Work-rest boundary is the personal and organizational separation that lets evenings, commutes, breaks, and leave become recovery time rather than continued work in another location.

## Current Synthesis
The vacation source establishes the structural side of the boundary. A worker can put away a phone or travel far away, but rest remains weak when bosses and coworkers treat leave as standby time. Handoffs, response expectations, duration, and social permission therefore matter alongside the individual's mindset.

VOL.72 extends the same rule into the ordinary workday. Commute space, short sensory pauses, breathing, movement, and deliberate de-roling can help attention cross from work into home life. These practices are useful as cues, not cures: a personal ritual cannot neutralize chronic overload, coercive management, or severe mental-health symptoms.

EP196 adds a sharper test: a boundary has failed when formal quitting time, sick leave, or vacation does not give the worker practical authority to stop. Always-on messaging, individual project ownership, unchanged workload, and performance anxiety can keep work active even from a hospital bed. Respectful norms—including clear urgency, handoff, and “节后看” messages—reduce the psychological demand for immediate response.

## Key Claims
- Rest requires a communication and availability boundary, not only free time on a calendar.
- Boss and coworker behavior helps determine whether disengagement is socially safe.
- Short transitions and pauses can support daily recovery before strain accumulates into uninterrupted role spillover.
- Travel distance, time zones, commutes, and rituals can reinforce a boundary but do not replace explicit work norms.
- The boundary should include handoff, response expectations, genuine emergencies, and the right to discard techniques that do not help.
- Formal hours, benefits, and leave labels are weak evidence of a boundary unless workload and stopping authority support them.
- Personal recovery practices should not be used to excuse structural overwork or delay care for significant symptoms.

## Evidence
- Organizational permission - [[ep265-dang-qing3xiu13-chongshang-resou-women-ruhe-gei-rensheng-fang-yige-changjia-lsloczlnkmcrrfpn9coh8rrqo-wb]] shows that leave fails as rest when managers and coworkers continue to treat the worker as reachable.
- Vacation design - [[ep265-dang-qing3xiu13-chongshang-resou-women-ruhe-gei-rensheng-fang-yige-changjia-lsloczlnkmcrrfpn9coh8rrqo-wb]] connects duration, changed environment, phone boundaries, nature, and local-life contact to recovery from work pressure.
- Daily transition - [[vol-72-jingshenke-bie-tai-na-xingge-ceshi-dangshi-sanjia-jingshenke-yisheng-jiao-ni-he-zhichang-zuo-jianya-qiege-li6jofbvucknthuqw1flm-r0m4kw]] uses commuting, listening, sensory attention, breathing, movement, and de-roling as possible buffers between work and home.
- Fit and escalation - [[vol-72-jingshenke-bie-tai-na-xingge-ceshi-dangshi-sanjia-jingshenke-yisheng-jiao-ni-he-zhichang-zuo-jianya-qiege-li6jofbvucknthuqw1flm-r0m4kw]] says to keep a ritual only if useful and separately directs severe, persistent, or impairing distress toward professional assessment.
- Nominal-versus-real boundary - [[ep196-renlei-gaoxiao-gongzuo-yanjiu-baogao-ckwrijifxc-uabaaaad057ge]] contrasts a formal 10-to-7 schedule with unchanged task ownership and describes work continuing during illness and leave.
- Response-pressure design - [[ep196-renlei-gaoxiao-gongzuo-yanjiu-baogao-ckwrijifxc-uabaaaad057ge]] uses a colleague's “节后看” label to show how urgency cues can protect time even when a message is sent.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The sources do not establish one ideal vacation length, commute duration, breathing protocol, or universally effective transition ritual. Work-rest boundaries may remain structurally impossible in some jobs, emergencies, care roles, or precarious employment. EP196's company-policy and health examples are experiential rather than independently verified. Individual coping and ergonomic equipment should not be presented as substitutes for staffing, workload reform, labor protections, medical assessment, or psychiatric care.

## What Changed
- Made stopping authority and unchanged workload explicit tests of whether formal hours are real.
- Added illness and hospital-bed work as severe boundary-failure cases.
- Added urgency labeling as a low-cost organizational protection for delayed response.
