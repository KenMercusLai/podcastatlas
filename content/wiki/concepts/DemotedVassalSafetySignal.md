# Demoted Vassal Safety Signal / 降爵诸侯安全信号

Demoted vassal safety signal / 降爵诸侯安全信号 is the consolidation logic [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-183-hanxin-zhisi-zhiyu-le-liubang-de-jingshen-neihao-lrbucdibto5zezrhl7ue0y9vhb-l|Hanji 183]] draws from [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s treatment of [[HanXin|韩信]] after the [[Yunmengze|云梦泽]] arrest and reduction to Huaiyin marquis. In the host's source-scoped reading, keeping Han Xin alive and wealthy was not only mercy or hesitation; it made demotion legible as a survivable outcome for other exposed kings.

The signal matters because early Western Han was already rolling back autonomous powerholders under [[DifferentSurnameKingSuspicion|异姓诸侯王猜忌]]. If the most famous military commander and former king could lose title yet keep status and comfort in [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]], other kings might believe that yielding did not automatically mean death. If Han Xin were killed immediately, by contrast, the center would teach them that surrender, demotion, or loss of kingship merely delayed execution.

This concept is the positive mirror of [[PunitiveSurrenderDeterrence|杀降纳降反噬]]. Punishing surrender can harden enemies; preserving a demoted powerholder can lower the expected cost of submission. Hanji 183 argues that Han Xin's death damaged that reassurance even if it solved a capital-security problem for [[LuZhi|吕雉]]'s side.

## Key Claims
- A living demoted vassal can be a policy signal, not only a defeated person.
- The signal works only if wealth, status, and bodily safety remain visible after title loss.
- The stronger and more famous the preserved figure is, the more credible the safety signal can become for others.
- Killing the exemplar can reverse the signal and make remaining autonomous actors prefer rebellion.
- The mechanism does not require the center to trust the demoted actor fully; it requires the center to find his visible survival useful.
- Hanji 183 keeps this argument source-scoped because it depends on the host's inference that Liu Bang saw more value in preserving Han Xin than killing him.
