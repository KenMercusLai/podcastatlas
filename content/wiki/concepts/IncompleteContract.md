# Incomplete Contract / 不完全契约

Incomplete contract is the source's frame for agreements that cannot specify, verify, or enforce every future condition. [[79-gewei-lingdao-danfan-zan-xuedian-boyilun-690004262]] introduces the idea through [[PrisonersDilemma]], workplace promises, marriage, employment, and third-party proof problems.

The concept matters because many real relationships look voluntary but are constrained by sunk cost, asymmetric information, switching cost, non-competitive alternatives, and unverifiable behavior. A handshake or slogan may be too thin to solve the strategic problem.

## Key Claims
- Incomplete contracts make information, reputation, fallback options, and later enforcement central.
- When future behavior cannot be fully written down, institutions need monitoring, incentives, repeated interaction, or credible signals.
- Treating an incomplete contract as if it were complete creates false confidence and often blame-shifts failure to individual character.
