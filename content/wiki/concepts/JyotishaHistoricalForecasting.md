# Jyotisha Historical Forecasting

## Definition
Jyotisha historical forecasting is [[AbhigyanAnand]]'s source-scoped practice of combining detailed planetary configurations, timing systems, geographic mapping, and selected historical parallels to forecast collective events.

## Current Synthesis
The method is more structured than simplified sun-sign astrology: it uses exact positions, divisional charts, multiple timing systems, geographic correspondences, and comparisons with prior crises. Anand also states a useful evaluation norm—predictions should be specific, public, recorded before events, and judged against misses. The current source, however, provides selected retrospective matches, broad regions and event classes, unfinished forecasts, and no complete archive or comparison baseline. Its 2026–2040 claims therefore remain scenarios internal to the astrological framework rather than validated forecasts.

## Key Claims
- Methodological detail can make a forecast more inspectable without proving its causal mechanism or predictive accuracy.
- Prospective publication and explicit time windows are stronger than vague retrospective matching, but still require complete scoring of successes, failures, and base rates.
- Historical analogy can generate scenarios while also inviting selection bias when non-matches and alternative explanations are omitted.
- Intuition or spiritual attribution may guide investigation but cannot substitute for replicable evidence.
- High-stakes geopolitical, financial, agricultural, or safety decisions require independent domain evidence.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The supplied file is a structured episode summary, not a forecast corpus or independent audit. Broad event categories, multiple regions, flexible causal stories, retrospective selection, and incomplete accounting of misses can inflate apparent accuracy. The method's historical relationship to astronomy does not transfer modern empirical validity. Practical resilience advice can be sensible without validating the astrological timing used to motivate it.

- Preserved the method's demand for prospective specificity while locating it inside the existing evidence boundary.
