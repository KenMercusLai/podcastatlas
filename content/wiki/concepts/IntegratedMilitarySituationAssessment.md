## Definition

综合军情判断 / integrated military situation assessment is a decision method that combines fighting quality, force ratio, attack and defense conditions, movement and mobilization time, local political cohesion, communication delay, and experienced pattern recognition before choosing whether intervention is still useful.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-508-dahan-mingjiang-chentang-shoubashou-jiao-ni-yunchou-weiwo-lmd26sxabxte3ilfzfxdv96vcdu|Hanji 508]] grounds the concept in [[ChenTangWesternHan|陈汤]]'s response to [[DuanHuizongWesternHan|段会宗]] being surrounded by [[Wusun|乌孙]] forces. Chen Tang does not infer danger from the word "siege" alone. He discounts nominal numbers by combat quality, checks whether the attackers meet an attack-to-defense threshold, estimates whether relief troops can cover the distance before the crisis resolves, and judges whether the surrounding coalition can sustain pressure. His five-day forecast is therefore presented as compressed expertise rather than divination.

The decision value lies partly in recognizing when action is already too late. A relief force that arrives only after defeat can punish or avenge, but it cannot perform the rescue for which it was raised. Good judgment therefore separates visible activity from timely effect and permits a decision not to mobilize when intervention cannot alter the immediate outcome.

## Key Claims

- Nominal troop counts should be adjusted for weapons, training, cohesion, and real fighting quality.
- Attack and defense impose different force requirements, so encirclement alone does not establish likely defeat.
- Mobilization and travel time must be compared with the crisis window rather than treated as background logistics.
- Local political cohesion affects endurance; a loose coalition may surround a target without being able to sustain an assault.
- Experience can compress several explicit variables into a time-bounded forecast, but it should remain inspectable rather than mystical.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

This concept is derived from one compact podcast retelling rather than an audited operational record. The stated ratios, march rates, and forecast do not establish universal military constants, and the later message confirms the outcome without independently proving every step in Chen Tang's reasoning. Expert intuition can also hide unsupported assumptions; the concept therefore treats experience as one input to an explicit model, not as authority immune from review.
