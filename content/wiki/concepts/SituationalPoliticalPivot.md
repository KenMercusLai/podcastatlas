# 因事立道式政治转身 / Situational Political Pivot

因事立道式政治转身 / situational political pivot is [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-39-lishishang-zhangyi-shi-zenyang-nudui-qiangdi-de-lv2gc2wtcxuhugfgixk7rbbhpa6x]]'s practical lesson from [[ZuoShuang|左爽]]'s advice to [[ChenZhenWarringStates|陈枕]]. Chen Zhen's original plan is to seek office in [[WeiState|魏国]], but [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] has already framed him as someone who serves [[ChuState|楚国]].

The pivot is to stop treating the hostile frame as a problem to refute inside Wei and instead use it as a route back to Chu. By passing Zhang Yi's private words through Chu-facing channels, Chen Zhen rebuilds standing with [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]]. The source turns this into a rule for unstable political settings: when the field changes, survival may depend on changing the goal and converting an accusation into proof of usefulness somewhere else.

## Key Claims
- A hostile narrative can sometimes be repurposed instead of defeated directly.
- The relevant question is not only "How do I win here?" but "Which court or audience can use what this situation now proves?"
- Pivoting requires reading the power field correctly: Zuo Shuang judges that Zhang Yi can keep blocking Chen Zhen in Wei.
- The mechanism belongs to [[WarringStatesCareeristMobility|乱世职业经理人式流动]] because mobile actors can change courts when one path closes.
