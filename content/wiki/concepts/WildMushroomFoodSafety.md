# Wild Mushroom Food Safety

## Definition
Wild mushroom food safety is the prevention and risk-interpretation framework for mushrooms whose species, sourcing, storage, preparation, portion, cross-contamination, or diner response may be uncertain.

## Current Synthesis
The current sources join cultural caution to clinical escalation. [[150-chi-jun-he-acuo-liao-yunnan-de-jizong-songrong-jianshouqing-ca-koushui-897563165|episode 150]] shows that pleasure, local knowledge, refusal rules, careful handling, and ecological restraint coexist inside [[YunnanWildMushroomCulture]]. [[vol-124-gu-du-huan-zhe-bing-bu-gu-du-zhong-xi-xin-li-7-zhen-shi-9-yisheng-yi-yi-fenxi-lnqswz3nim_8767094yiysgmef1g|VOL.124]] shows why these boundaries matter: people sharing a dish may become ill at different doses and times, symptom relief may precede delayed organ injury, and persistent neurologic or visual complaints may require cross-specialty follow-up.

The resulting rule is deliberately conservative. Local experience and restaurant controls can reduce some errors, but neither familiarity, cooking confidence, a small serving, nor an unaffected companion proves safety. Prevention should not become a preparation manual, and post-exposure response belongs under [[WildMushroomPoisoningTriage]].

## Key Claims
- Uncertain identity, source, storage, preparation, utensil contact, or reheating makes wild-mushroom consumption a high-risk practice.
- Local expertise and controlled restaurant processes may reduce risk but cannot eliminate biological variability or guarantee a non-toxic meal.
- Different diners can have different symptoms despite sharing a dish, so one person's mild or absent reaction is not reassurance for another.
- Online hallucination humor distorts risk by hiding gastrointestinal, neurologic, hepatic, renal, visual, and fatal outcomes.
- Temporary symptom improvement does not reliably exclude delayed severe poisoning.
- The safest boundary for an uncertain mushroom is non-consumption; suspected symptomatic exposure calls for medical escalation, not online identification or folk treatment.

## Evidence
- Household and cultural risk discipline - [[150-chi-jun-he-acuo-liao-yunnan-de-jizong-songrong-jianshouqing-ca-koushui-897563165]] describes sorting, separating, refusing unknown mixtures, and resisting casual outsider handling.
- Variable dose and response - [[vol-124-gu-du-huan-zhe-bing-bu-gu-du-zhong-xi-xin-li-7-zhen-shi-9-yisheng-yi-yi-fenxi-lnqswz3nim_8767094yiysgmef1g]] reports differing symptoms among diners and illness after a small reported amount.
- Process controls - [[vol-124-gu-du-huan-zhe-bing-bu-gu-du-zhong-xi-xin-li-7-zhen-shi-9-yisheng-yi-yi-fenxi-lnqswz3nim_8767094yiysgmef1g]] describes timed cooking, utensil replacement, and retained samples as restaurant safeguards, while keeping them short of a safety guarantee.
- Anti-romanticization - both [[150-chi-jun-he-acuo-liao-yunnan-de-jizong-songrong-jianshouqing-ca-koushui-897563165]] and [[vol-124-gu-du-huan-zhe-bing-bu-gu-du-zhong-xi-xin-li-7-zhen-shi-9-yisheng-yi-yi-fenxi-lnqswz3nim_8767094yiysgmef1g]] reject “seeing little people” as an adequate account of poisoning.
- Delayed danger - [[vol-124-gu-du-huan-zhe-bing-bu-gu-du-zhong-xi-xin-li-7-zhen-shi-9-yisheng-yi-yi-fenxi-lnqswz3nim_8767094yiysgmef1g]] explains delayed liver and kidney syndromes and the possibility of an apparent recovery period.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The sources do not establish that every wild mushroom is poisonous or that every symptom after a meal was caused by mushroom toxin. “见手青” is a broad vernacular category, and the clinical episode does not identify a specimen or final toxin. The restaurant practices described are examples, not validated universal protocols. This page is public safety synthesis and cannot replace poison-center, emergency, toxicology, or local public-health guidance.
