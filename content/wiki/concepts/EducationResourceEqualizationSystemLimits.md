# Education Resource Equalization System Limits / 教育资源均衡的系统边界

## Definition
Education resource equalization system limits are the constraints that prevent teacher reassignment alone from equalizing schools when staffing by subject, student composition, family resources, materials, routines, incentives, transport, and long-term institutional capacity remain uneven.

## Current Synthesis
[[ep188-jiaoshijie-duihua-zuotijia-yangchengshi-dangdai-zuotijie-xianzhuang-dayi-ckwriasfcgevabaaaadf6hi3|EP188]] supports the fairness goal of teacher and principal rotation but treats teaching quality as institutional rather than portable in one person. A rotating teacher enters an established timetable, preparation system, curriculum archive, peer group, student body, and family environment while bearing commute and adaptation costs. The same systems view explains how overall hiring competition can coexist with severe geography-teacher shortages after subject-choice reform: supply can be abundant in aggregate and mismatched by discipline, place, and timing.

## Key Claims
- Teacher quality matters, but it is embedded in school routines, materials, teams, student composition, and family context.
- Rotation can spread experience while also imposing commute, adaptation, and continuity costs.
- Incentives, professional advancement, and time horizons shape whether rotating staff invest in long-term local capacity.
- Aggregate teacher competition can coexist with subject-specific or regional scarcity.
- Policy changes in student demand require matching workforce planning rather than only higher recruitment standards.
- Equity reform should track institutional capacity and student opportunity, not only personnel movement.

## Evidence
- Rotation boundary: [[ep188-jiaoshijie-duihua-zuotijia-yangchengshi-dangdai-zuotijie-xianzhuang-dayi-ckwriasfcgevabaaaadf6hi3|EP188]] links school quality to student intake, family investment, teaching systems, materials, and accumulated practice, while noting commute and adaptation costs.
- Incentive and continuity: [[ep188-jiaoshijie-duihua-zuotijia-yangchengshi-dangdai-zuotijie-xianzhuang-dayi-ckwriasfcgevabaaaadf6hi3|EP188]] asks whether short-term rotating teachers will invest in local resources without title, compensation, and continuity support.
- Subject mismatch: [[ep188-jiaoshijie-duihua-zuotijia-yangchengshi-dangdai-zuotijie-xianzhuang-dayi-ckwriasfcgevabaaaadf6hi3|EP188]] describes increased geography selection producing overloaded teachers and early recruitment even as other teaching posts attract elite-degree applicants.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not evaluate a completed rotation program or provide comparative outcome data, and its staffing examples come from one teacher's environment. Rotation may still improve access, professional exchange, and perceived fairness. The concept sets an implementation test: personnel movement should be evaluated together with incentives, school capacity, subject demand, continuity, and student outcomes.

## What Changed
- Created a systems-level qualification to teacher rotation and aggregate staffing claims.
