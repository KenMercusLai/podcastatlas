# Demonstrable Understanding Under AI

## Definition
Demonstrable understanding under AI is the requirement that a person explain, defend, adapt, and evaluate an artifact when fluent written production no longer reliably shows who understands or originated it.

## Current Synthesis
The episode applies this problem to mathematics. A deep, correct paper once supplied strong evidence that its writer understood the relevant ideas; generative systems weaken that inference by enabling people to submit sophisticated work they may not be able to explain. Assessment therefore shifts toward oral examination, discussion, lecture, live defense, and questions that expose reasoning, while preserving written work as evidence rather than treating it as self-authenticating proof of mastery.

This is not an anti-AI rule. AI-assisted output can be useful and correct, but institutions still need to distinguish artifact quality, personal understanding, contribution, and responsibility. Those are related judgments, not one score.

## Key Claims
- Polished or technically correct output is no longer sufficient evidence of personal mastery.
- Oral defense and live explanation can test whether a person can give reasons, answer challenges, and transfer an idea to a new case.
- Artifact quality, authorship, originality, understanding, and responsibility should be evaluated separately.
- Hiring and graduate education need transparent norms for acceptable AI assistance and evidence of contribution.
- Human understanding remains necessary for judging importance, usefulness, and explanatory value even when formal verification is available.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Oral performance can be affected by language, disability, anxiety, status, and evaluator bias, and it does not by itself prove originality or exclude concealed assistance. Written work remains valuable for careful reasoning, revision, and durable communication. A credible assessment system should combine declared tool use, process evidence, artifact review, live explanation, and task-appropriate accommodations rather than replacing one single signal with another.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to separate output quality from personal understanding, authorship, and responsibility.
- Added oral defense as one useful but incomplete evidence channel.
