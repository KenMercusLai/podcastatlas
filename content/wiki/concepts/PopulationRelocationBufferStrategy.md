## Definition

迁民式战略缓冲 / population-relocation buffer strategy is the use of organized civilian relocation to reduce settlement, food, labor, intelligence, or transport support in a contested corridor and thereby increase the logistical cost of an opponent's future advance.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1164-jingti-zhichang-tai-nenggan-de-xianjing-lrw8byuza9fzasd0ewwszkoj3mu|Hanji 1164]] attributes a post-Hanzhong proposal to [[ZhangJiCaoOfficialLateHan|张既]]. After [[CaoCao|曹操]] abandons Hanzhong but remains concerned about movement toward Guanzhong, Zhang Ji reportedly recommends moving residents eastward toward the Fufeng–Tianshui area. A less populated intervening corridor would deny an advancing force easy access to residents and supplies.

The strategy treats civilian geography as military infrastructure. That makes its logistical logic inseparable from governance and human cost: a claim of voluntary movement requires evidence about choice, inducement, authority, property, destination support, and the consequences for people who move or remain.

## Key Claims

- Territorial withdrawal can be followed by reshaping the supply geography behind the abandoned position.
- Population, food, labor, transport, and local knowledge are part of an army's usable operational environment.
- A thinly settled corridor can add friction without requiring continuous forward occupation.
- Strategic evaluation must include consent, displacement cost, livelihood reconstruction, and burdens shifted onto civilians.

## Evidence

Logistical buffer proposal:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1164-jingti-zhichang-tai-nenggan-de-xianjing-lrw8byuza9fzasd0ewwszkoj3mu|Hanji 1164]] says Zhang Ji proposes eastward relocation to make the Hanzhong–Guanzhong approach less able to support a Liu-side advance.

Implementation boundary:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1164-jingti-zhichang-tai-nenggan-de-xianjing-lrw8byuza9fzasd0ewwszkoj3mu|Hanji 1164]] says the movement should avoid direct force and offer practical assistance, but supplies no independent evidence sufficient to establish free consent or outcomes.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The current concept rests on one structured episode summary. The source does not verify the number of residents, exact corridor, legal authority, property arrangements, inducements, resistance, destination capacity, survival, or military effect. “Voluntary” movement under wartime power can still involve constrained choice, and depopulation can damage the relocating state as well as the opponent. The concept describes a strategic mechanism without endorsing it ethically or treating the reported implementation as benign.

## What Changed

- Created a bounded concept linking relocation, supply denial, border depth, and civilian cost.
