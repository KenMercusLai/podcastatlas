# Village Self-Governance / 村庄自主治理

Village self-governance is the collective decision pattern described in [[144-nvxia-duan-sihe-he-tade-jiaxiang-mapingguan-women-ceng-yiwei-you-dian-you-lu-zhishi-meng-linmjqt-n-sa5sarpqgdxgtoeejt]] through [[Mapingguan|马坪关]]. The source says village meetings could be noisy, long, and conflict-heavy, but still oriented toward solving shared problems rather than merely airing grievances.

The episode's distinction is that external facilitators did not invent village capacity. [[HuangYunwu|黄云武]] and others helped make meeting rules clearer: one speaker is heard, opinions are gathered, disagreement is integrated, and proposals can proceed after a sufficient share of support.

## Key Claims
- Self-governance can look disorderly before it becomes legible; an argument-like meeting may still be a real deliberation process.
- Outside facilitators can improve method without replacing the villagers as decision-makers.
- Village self-governance is practical when it reaches toilets, roads, matsutake mountains, school advocacy, visitor reception, and public funds rather than remaining a slogan.
- [[MamaHui|妈妈会]] and other local groups matter because governance capacity is distributed across formal and informal organizations.
- The source's "自主制" language should be kept source-scoped because the episode does not provide formal bylaws or legal structure.
