# Administrative Information Conformity / 行政信息圆美化

## Definition
Administrative information conformity is the distortion that occurs when official communication is rewarded for safety, polish, and alignment more than for exposing operational problems.

## Current Synthesis
The Qing memorial system in the source illustrates a real tradeoff. Direct and secret channels can reduce public factional performance, yet restricted speaking rights, fear of private-motive accusations, and concentrated review can make officials avoid responsibility. “圆美” then becomes an information pathology: smooth documents circulate while difficult facts fail to travel upward.

## Key Claims
- Channel secrecy can reduce one political distortion while creating another.
- The right to report is also a responsibility structure; narrow access makes intermediaries more cautious.
- Blame-sensitive review encourages agreement, rejection without ownership, or polished ambiguity.
- Decision quality declines when local implementation facts cannot safely reach the center.

## Evidence
- Channel evidence: [[501-cong-hongliangji-gongzizhen-dao-weiyuan-qingzhongye-de-ren-ruhe-kandai-ziji-guojia-weilai-lgu0h9kaxmfwzoe1byxeyq7png9o]] compares public Ming memorial practice with Qing direct and secret memorials.
- Language evidence: [[501-cong-hongliangji-gongzizhen-dao-weiyuan-qingzhongye-de-ren-ruhe-kandai-ziji-guojia-weilai-lgu0h9kaxmfwzoe1byxeyq7png9o]] defines “圆” as avoiding edges and injury and “美” as polished appearance rather than problem engagement.
- Decision evidence: [[501-cong-hongliangji-gongzizhen-dao-weiyuan-qingzhongye-de-ren-ruhe-kandai-ziji-guojia-weilai-lgu0h9kaxmfwzoe1byxeyq7png9o]] describes senior advisers either broadly rejecting proposals or following established approval to avoid responsibility.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not show that publicity is always better: open memorials could become reputational performance and factional weapons. The concept is therefore a channel-design tradeoff, not a simple argument for full openness.

## What Changed
- Added a bounded model of how secrecy, restricted access, blame, and polished prose can degrade administrative truthfulness.
