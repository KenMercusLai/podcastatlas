# Local Governance As Care / 地方治理作为照护

[[150-zhongguo-ruhe-tiaochu-pinkun-xianjing-xiandaihua-zhi-lu-you-hui-zouxiang-hefang-lgpvp04rngagkpubte2ac6i5ji3g]] adds a modernization-stage version through [[ServiceOrientedLocalGovernment]]. The episode argues that [[China]]'s local governments were strong at investment attraction and growth mobilization, but later modernization needs service, welfare, care, business-environment, and citizen-feedback capacity that is harder to measure than GDP or fiscal revenue.

[[144-nvxia-duan-sihe-he-tade-jiaxiang-mapingguan-women-ceng-yiwei-you-dian-you-lu-zhishi-meng-linmjqt-n-sa5sarpqgdxgtoeejt]] adds a village rather than scholar-official version through [[Mapingguan|马坪关]]. Roads, electricity, toilet renovation, school life-administrator advocacy, opera-stage and bridge restoration, and matsutake reserve funds show care distributed among villagers, outside facilitators, and government support rather than embodied in one official.

Local governance as care is the episode's frame for [[SuShi|苏轼]]'s practical service outside the court center. In [[105-su-dongpo-yiqiang-haomai-zhiqi-yi-dupi-buhe-shiyi-741072645]], the hosts repeatedly resist a merely leisurely Su Dongpo image by pointing to water works, flood defense, relief, medicine, infant rescue, burials, bridges, and schooling.

The concept makes governance visible at the scale of bodies and households. [[Hangzhou|杭州]] is not only West Lake scenery; it includes grain storage, medical facilities, medicine distribution, and the [[SuCauseway|苏堤]]. [[Xuzhou|徐州]] is not only a post; it is flood protection. [[Huizhou|惠州]] and [[Danzhou|儋州]] show that even exile can contain care work.

## Key Claims
- Episode 144 adds that local governance as care can be collective and village-based: meetings, women's groups, commons revenue, and school advocacy can become care infrastructure.
- Good local governance often appears as concrete maintenance rather than ideology.
- Infrastructure, medicine, relief, burial, and childcare all belong to public duty when poverty and disaster threaten bodies.
- Cultural memory can preserve a scenic object while forgetting the administrative care that produced it.
- Exile or demotion does not fully remove public responsibility if an official still has presence, relationships, and practical means.
- Episode 150 adds that modern local governance must move from mobilizing growth to delivering services and feedback-sensitive daily support.
