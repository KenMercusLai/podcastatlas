# Family-Business Work-Life Boundary / 家族企业工作生活边界

Family-business work-life boundary / 家族企业工作生活边界 is the generational difference in [[ep255-chang-erdai-de-jicheng-zhi-zhan-nianqingren-jieban-hui-chongsu-qiye-ma-likvzmpgwxnetpaezxclsx4s-hkw]] between founders who treat the company as life itself and successors who want responsibility to coexist with personal choice, family time, and remote or systematized management.

The source makes this visible through [[Fadior|法迪奥]] and [[WoteShoes|沃特鞋企]]. Founders may work seven days a week and treat the company as a child; successors may still accept duty, but hope that better organizational structure, management systems, and technology will let them run the firm without always being physically present.

## Key Claims
- Work-life boundary is not necessarily refusal of responsibility; it can be a demand for a company that is governable beyond founder endurance.
- The boundary becomes sharper when successors see the firm as a job, platform, or self-realization vehicle rather than as their whole identity.
- Succession can soften the obligation imposed on the third generation if successors decide not to force their own children to take over.
- The concept extends [[FounderWorkBoundaries]] from startup-founder self-care into family-firm inheritance and intergenerational obligation.
