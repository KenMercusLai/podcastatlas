# 虚构援军欺敌 / False Reinforcement Deception

## Definition
虚构援军欺敌 is the creation of a credible but false relief force so that a besieger changes course before the advertised manpower actually appears.

## Current Synthesis
The Hefei case in Hanji 1112 depends on evidence design, not merely a large lie. [[JiangJiLateHan|蒋济]] reportedly uses several messages and allows the opposing side to intercept some, so the claim of forty thousand arriving troops looks like discovered operational coordination. The deception is effective because [[SunQuan|孙权]] already faces the risks of a long siege and possible attack from both inside and outside [[HefeiLateHan|合肥]].

## Key Claims
- A false reinforcement claim becomes stronger when the enemy believes it has independently discovered the evidence.
- Siege fatigue and fear of an inner-outer attack create the decision context in which the claim matters.
- The mechanism changes estimated risk; it does not create the advertised combat power.

## Evidence
- Credibility construction: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1112-bei-digu-de-caowei-mingjiang-zhanchang-shang-you-duo-meng-lkqecaphucolkdcsutsvjyxounln|Hanji 1112]] attributes multiple letters and deliberate interception to Jiang Ji's plan.
- Behavioral result: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1112-bei-digu-de-caowei-mingjiang-zhanchang-shang-you-duo-meng-lkqecaphucolkdcsutsvjyxounln|Hanji 1112]] says Sun Quan burns siege equipment and withdraws after accepting the report.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
One popular-history episode does not establish a universal deception rule or isolate the lie from weather, siege duration, logistics, and Sun Quan's other information. The real relief force, message count, claimed total, interception design, and complete withdrawal decision remain source-scoped.

## What Changed
- Established the concept from the Jian'an 14 Hefei relief case.
