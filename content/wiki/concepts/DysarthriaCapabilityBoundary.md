# Dysarthria Capability Boundary

## Definition
Dysarthria capability boundary is the episode's distinction between unclear articulation and a person's broader cognition, creativity, humor, writing, or communicative potential.

## Current Synthesis
The source treats dysarthria as a speech-production problem rather than a global measure of ability. [[LihuaSpeechTherapist|梨花医生]] agrees with the host's plain-language explanation that dysarthria mainly means speech is unclear, while other development or capability may be preserved. The episode uses this distinction to push back against social misrecognition: unclear speech should not be equated with low intelligence or lack of expressive value.

The rehabilitation branch stays functional. Training may include syllables, oral-motor control, words, sentences, stories, songs, and relaxed interaction, but the communication goal is not merely pronunciation perfection. The episode repeatedly values joking, sharing, emotional connection, and real dialogue over sterile vocabulary accumulation.

## Key Claims
- Dysarthria is framed as unclear articulation or speech production, not as proof of global cognitive limitation.
- Listeners should not infer intelligence, writing ability, humor, or performance capacity from speech clarity alone.
- Speech training can move through sounds, articulation, sentences, stories, and songs depending on the person's needs.
- Communication value includes joking, sharing, emotional connection, and participation, not only correct word production.
- Dysarthria-related rehabilitation remains individualized and professional, especially when brain injury, motor control, or developmental factors are involved.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is a public rehabilitation conversation rather than a diagnostic guide to dysarthria types, causes, prognosis, or treatment. Individual motor-speech disorders can coexist with cognitive, language, neurological, emotional, or developmental issues, so the boundary prevents assumption but does not rule out clinical complexity.
