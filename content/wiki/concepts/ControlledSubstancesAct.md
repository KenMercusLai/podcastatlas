# Controlled Substances Act

The Controlled Substances Act is the federal drug-law frame in [[a-trip-to-the-magic-mushroom-megachurch]]. The episode uses it to explain why [[PsilocybinMushrooms]], [[DMT]], and other substances used by [[PsychedelicChurches]] remain legally risky even when a group calls them religious sacrament.

The source's practical point is that local tolerance and religious sincerity do not by themselves erase federal exposure. [[ZydorChurch]] can operate in [[OaklandCalifornia]] under a more permissive local climate, but [[JohnRapp]] still warns that churches need [[ReligiousControlledSubstanceExemption]] or remain vulnerable to enforcement and court action.

## Key Claims
- The statute turns psychedelic church practice into a federal-risk issue rather than only a local policing issue.
- Religious vocabulary can support an exemption argument, but it does not automatically override federal drug law.
- The risk is organizational as well as personal: churches can face raids, seizures, prosecution threats, and security demands.
- Local decriminalization lowers some practical enforcement pressure without changing the federal baseline.
