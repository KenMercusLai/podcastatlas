## Definition

信任型宦官权力形成 / trusted eunuch power formation is the process by which eunuch influence begins through crisis usefulness, personal trust, reward restraint, and routine access to the ruler before it becomes an openly dominant political structure.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-766-lishi-jiaogei-ni-weishenme-yao-guyi-fa-piqi-lrs-mguyfmnxe970hdwjvmevl8oc|Hanji 766]] now supplies the crisis-usefulness layer before the reward layer. [[EmperorHeOfHan|Emperor He]] cannot safely consult outer ministers under [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]]'s dominance, so he secretly plans with [[ZhengZhongEasternHan|Zheng Zhong]] and uses [[LiuQingQingheKing|Liu Qing]] to relay a request for historical precedents about punishing powerful maternal uncles.

Hanji 767 then grounds the post-victory trust consolidation. Emperor He rewards allies after removing the Dou family and promotes Zheng to da changqiu. Zheng refuses rewards and presents humility, which makes the emperor trust him and discuss government with him.

The source set treats this as the beginning of eunuch power. The important point is sequence: Zheng is not portrayed here as seizing control by force. His influence forms because a ruler emerging from outer-relative danger values a close, apparently self-restraining palace servant as a safer consultation channel.

## Key Claims

- Eunuch power can begin as trusted crisis service before it hardens into structural monopoly.
- Reward refusal may increase influence when the ruler interprets it as virtue and low threat.
- Inner-court proximity matters because repeated consultation can become a governing channel.
- Post-outer-relative consolidation can make palace servants look safer than natal-family or ministerial blocs.
- The same crisis access that solves an immediate blockage can create the next structural access problem.
- The formation layer should be distinguished from later mature eunuch dominance.

- Precedent search: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-766-lishi-jiaogei-ni-weishenme-yao-guyi-fa-piqi-lrs-mguyfmnxe970hdwjvmevl8oc|Hanji 766]] says Liu Qing relays to Zheng the request to gather cases of emperors killing powerful maternal uncles.
- Post-Dou reward field: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-767-congming-ren-dou-shanchang-yanxi-lq3ol7y-jdh7dnnfkmtfdg4ibryq|Hanji 767]] says Emperor He rewards allies after removing the Dou family.
- Promotion: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-767-congming-ren-dou-shanchang-yanxi-lq3ol7y-jdh7dnnfkmtfdg4ibryq|Hanji 767]] says Zheng Zhong is promoted to da changqiu.
- Humility and refusal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-767-congming-ren-dou-shanchang-yanxi-lq3ol7y-jdh7dnnfkmtfdg4ibryq|Hanji 767]] says Zheng refuses rewards and appears modest.
- Trust and consultation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-767-congming-ren-dou-shanchang-yanxi-lq3ol7y-jdh7dnnfkmtfdg4ibryq|Hanji 767]] says Emperor He sees him as worthy and often discusses government with him.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The page records the source's interpretation that eunuch power begins here, but it does not by itself prove a continuous institutional chain from Zheng Zhong to every later eunuch-dominance episode. The concept should be used for early formation through crisis trust and access, not as a synonym for all eunuch politics.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 766 as the secret-planning layer before Hanji 767's reward-refusal layer.
- Redefined the opening stage of eunuch power as crisis usefulness plus trusted access after outer-relative danger.
