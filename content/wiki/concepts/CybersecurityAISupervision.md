# Cybersecurity AI Supervision

Cybersecurity AI supervision is the work pattern where humans direct, inspect, prioritize, and constrain AI agents that perform security tasks such as vulnerability discovery. [[tech-20260410-0410-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260410-0410-mp-tech-pod-128]] adds the idea through [[JoannaStern]]'s comparison between cybersecurity and coding: as AI systems become more capable, professionals may spend more time managing agents and less time manually performing every task.

The concept connects [[AICyberDefenseUtility]] to ordinary work design. Defensive cyber AI may be valuable, but the episode's risk framing implies that humans still need to choose targets, verify findings, coordinate remediation, and prevent capability from becoming an offensive automation tool.

## Key Claims
- AI can change cybersecurity labor by accelerating vulnerability search, triage, and analysis.
- Human judgment remains necessary because vulnerability discovery has disclosure, prioritization, false-positive, and abuse-risk consequences.
- Strong cyber agents make access control and supervision part of the security system, not an optional wrapper around the model.
- The work pattern resembles [[AIManagingAI]] and [[AgenticWorkflow]], but with higher stakes because the output can expose exploitable weaknesses.
