# Responsibility Against Romantic Escape / 以责任抵抗浪漫逃避

[[ep243-jiang-qiming-x-li-xueqin-x-shuang-xuetao-xiang-zai-dongbei-shangtian-zhe-shi-er-haishi-tai-fuza-le-lkqbo0zg5y7p1fo4x90n8yq394t6]] adds a film-family version through [[FeixingjiaFilm|《飞行家》]]. Here responsibility does not simply defeat the wish to fly; it gives the wish its ethical shape through [[FamilySupportedAspiration]], parents, partners, friends, work, and the ordinary obligations around Li Mingqi.

[[ResponsibilityAgainstRomanticEscape|Responsibility against romantic escape / 以责任抵抗浪漫逃避]] is the maturity path proposed by [[07-yong-rongge-lilun-fenxi-xiaowangzi-yongheng-shaonian-ji-qita-539091165]]. After reading [[TheLittlePrince|《小王子》]] as a [[PuerAeternus|puer aeternus / 永恒少年]] story, the episode argues that the cure is not to lose imagination, sensitivity, or childlike feeling. The cure is to let those qualities survive contact with work, suffering, commitment, and responsibility for other people.

The source repeatedly contrasts fantasy with grounded effort. The eternal youth says "总有一天" about future greatness, perfect love, or a meaningful life, but avoids the dull tasks that would make any of them real. The episode's repair path is deliberately unglamorous: administrative work, practical obligations, writing under constraint, and staying with relationships count as maturity training rather than betrayal of art.

## Key Claims
- Maturity does not mean surrendering to a dead adult world; it means preserving imagination while becoming accountable.
- Romantic careers, dramatic gestures, and beautiful death can hide refusal to live in ordinary time.
- Work that feels boring can be psychologically necessary because it forces fantasy into contact with reality.
- Responsibility includes relational reciprocity: being changed by the fox or rose is not enough if the other party is simply left behind.
- This concept extends [[GrowthAsProtectorRole]] from fairy-tale growth into adult psychological growth.
