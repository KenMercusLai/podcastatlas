# 可交换功臣 / Minister as Exchangeable Asset

Minister as exchangeable asset / 可交换功臣 is the power calculation [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-53-qinhuiwang-yong-zhangyi-huan-dipi-lvmo2cwbafcg2gnxliuo18fewimk]] attaches to [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] and [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]. The source says Qin Huiwen Wang values Zhang Yi, but not enough to make Zhang Yi's life categorically higher than a major territorial opportunity around [[Qianzhong|黔中]].

The concept has two sides. If [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] uses Zhang Yi or trades him back, [[QinState|秦国]] may gain territory. If Chu kills him, Qin may gain a justified war pretext because interstate practice treats envoy-killing as a serious violation. In this reading, a minister's value is not only his service record; it is the range of state gains his survival or death can produce.

This differs from [[WarringStatesCareeristMobility|乱世职业经理人式流动]]. Careerist mobility emphasizes the strategist's own agency across courts. Minister as exchangeable asset emphasizes the ruler's willingness to place that mobile professional inside a bargain where state advantage outranks personal protection.

## Key Claims
- A high-value minister can still be subordinated to territorial bargaining when the ruler sees multiple useful outcomes.
- The most dangerous version of the bargain is asymmetric: the ruler can gain from the subordinate's success, transfer, or death.
- Public diplomatic norms can turn harm to a minister into state advantage when the harmed person is also an envoy.
- The concept sharpens Zhang Yi's tragedy in the episode: his past service to Qin does not prevent Qin from exposing him to Chu's revenge.
