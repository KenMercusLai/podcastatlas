# Self-Commodification Through Work / 劳动中的自我商品化

## Definition
Self-commodification through work is the extension of market exchange from time and skill into personality, popularity, relationships, visibility, and the felt right to exist, so that declining demand for one's work is experienced as declining value of the person.

## Current Synthesis
The concept begins with [[ArthurMiller|Arthur Miller]]'s reported answer that [[WillyLoman|Willy Loman]] sells “himself.” In [[DeathOfASalesman|《推销员之死》]], charm, contacts, being remembered, and being liked are not supplements to the job; they are treated as the product and as proof of worth.

The episode extends this beyond a historical salesman. Workers exchange time, credentials, capacities, and social presentation for livelihood, while platform culture adds exposure, persona maintenance, and external feedback. The danger is not exchange itself but allowing market attention to become the only available evidence that a person matters.

## Key Claims
- Some jobs make personality and relationships part of what is sold, evaluated, and replaced.
- When the product is experienced as the self, rejection is harder to limit to a task or market judgment.
- Visibility and popularity can become labor assets without becoming valid measures of personhood.
- Provider identity can turn economic contribution into the condition for belonging inside a family.
- The mechanism extends to personal branding and platform attention but should not erase differences among occupations and institutions.
- Durable protection requires non-market sources of relation, judgment, and value.

## Evidence
- Selling the self: [[vol-355-tuixiaoyuan-zhisi-naxie-shabusi-wode-weishenme-yizhi-zai-dawo-gkwriw4ols9xawr6vqtifmya]] uses Miller's formulation to interpret Willy's personality and contacts as his commodity.
- Employer rejection: [[vol-355-tuixiaoyuan-zhisi-naxie-shabusi-wode-weishenme-yizhi-zai-dawo-gkwriw4ols9xawr6vqtifmya]] connects loss of company need with Willy's inability to confirm that he retains value.
- Provider identity: [[vol-355-tuixiaoyuan-zhisi-naxie-shabusi-wode-weishenme-yizhi-zai-dawo-gkwriw4ols9xawr6vqtifmya]] reads life insurance as a final conversion of death into family utility.
- Contemporary extension: [[vol-355-tuixiaoyuan-zhisi-naxie-shabusi-wode-weishenme-yizhi-zai-dawo-gkwriw4ols9xawr6vqtifmya]] compares Willy's popularity belief with personal branding, social visibility, and fear of not being seen.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
All paid work involves exchange, and relationship skill, reputation, or public visibility can be legitimate parts of competence. The concept does not imply that self-presentation is inherently false or that market feedback is useless. It marks the collapse of boundaries when a bounded work judgment becomes a total judgment of personhood.

## What Changed
- Created the concept by joining Miller's “selling himself” formulation to the episode's provider-role and social-media comparison.
