# Urological Symptom Triage / 泌尿症状分层判断

## Definition
Urological symptom triage is the practice of interpreting urinary symptoms, urine color, and urine-test context by pattern, duration, volume, associated symptoms, contamination risk, and likely organ system rather than by one isolated sign.

## Current Synthesis
[[vol-31-miniao-waike-guanyu-niaopin-niaoye-huang-jieshi-nanxing-qianliexian-de-yaoyan-ji-wuqu-lpn3fhozej2v-3oijpakvkdx-r-y]] uses urinary frequency, urgency, and yellow urine to show why common symptoms need contextual judgment. Frequency and urgency often signal bladder or urinary-tract irritation, but the source keeps infection, urethritis, prostatitis, and rarer causes such as tuberculosis in view rather than assigning one organ by default. Yellow urine is often concentrated urine after heat, sweating, fever, or overnight water restriction, but persistent discoloration that does not improve with hydration can point outside urology, such as hepatobiliary obstruction.

The episode's practical rule is to compare symptoms with hydration, total urine volume, weather, infection signs, pain, persistence, and test quality. Midstream urine collection matters because early urine can carry perineal secretions into the sample, so the collection method can affect interpretation before the lab result is even read.

## Key Claims
- Urinary frequency and urgency are lower-urinary-tract irritation signals, not proof that the bladder, kidney, or prostate alone is responsible.
- Infection, urethritis, prostatitis, and less common tuberculosis-related disease can produce overlapping urinary symptoms.
- Cold-weather urinary frequency can be physiological, especially when total water balance and sweating differ from hot weather.
- Severe kidney dysfunction may reduce urine rather than simply make urine more frequent, so "more urine means kidney failure" is an unreliable folk rule.
- Yellow urine is commonly explained by concentration from fever, sweating, fluid loss, or overnight non-drinking, but persistent yellow urine despite hydration deserves broader clinical consideration.
- Midstream urine collection reduces sample contamination from perineal secretions and bacteria; it is a test-quality practice, not a urine-color rule.
- Persistent, painful, obstructive, infectious, or unexplained symptoms should move from self-interpretation to qualified care.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This concept does not diagnose urinary frequency, urgency, urine color, infection, kidney function, liver or biliary disease, or tuberculosis. It captures the episode's public-education triage logic and should not replace urinalysis, imaging, physical examination, or clinician-guided follow-up.
