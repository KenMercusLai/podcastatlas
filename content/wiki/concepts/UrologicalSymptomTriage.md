# Urological Symptom Triage / 泌尿症状分层判断

## Definition
Urological symptom triage is the practice of interpreting voiding difficulty, urinary frequency or urgency, urine appearance, leakage, urine-test context, and pelvic or abdominal pain by pattern, duration, volume, associated symptoms, life stage, contamination risk, and likely organ system rather than by one isolated sign.

## Current Synthesis
[[vol-29-miniao-waike-renshi-miniao-xitong-he-rongyi-gua-cuo-hao-de-miniao-waike-llxb263cooyirukwcezzhysm57va]] establishes the entry question: urology is not a men-only specialty, and department choice should begin with organs and symptom patterns. It places voiding delay, incomplete emptying, increasing nocturia, hematuria, dysuria, urgency or incontinence, and persistent foamy urine inside clinical assessment while acknowledging overlap with gynecology and other specialties.

[[vol-31-miniao-waike-guanyu-niaopin-niaoye-huang-jieshi-nanxing-qianliexian-de-yaoyan-ji-wuqu-lpn3fhozej2v-3oijpakvkdx-r-y]] uses urinary frequency, urgency, and yellow urine to show why common symptoms need contextual judgment. Frequency and urgency often signal bladder or urinary-tract irritation, but the source keeps infection, urethritis, prostatitis, and rarer causes such as tuberculosis in view rather than assigning one organ by default. Yellow urine is often concentrated urine after heat, sweating, fever, or overnight water restriction, but persistent discoloration that does not improve with hydration can point outside urology, such as hepatobiliary obstruction.

[[vol-30-miniao-waike-nvxing-miniao-xitong-de-changjian-wenti-yaoyan-ji-wuqu-lmeg-qk-dio77mj7retw0qoyoop-o]] broadens the same triage logic into female urinary health. It adds department confusion between urology and gynecology, leakage triggered by abdominal pressure, pregnancy urine markers, infection behavior, red urine, terminal perineal pain, stone-like restless pain, menstrual irregularity, post-sex abdominal pain, and acute or recurrent abdominal pain as sorting problems rather than self-diagnoses.

[[vol-147-daodi-yitian-yao-he-duoshao-shui-weisha-dongtian-shang-cesuo-geng-pinfan-huyu-gonggong-changsuo-duo-jian-nvce-lj9q7zljd68jmbtcd4klgrbj1vaz]] sharpens three distinctions. Frequency should be interpreted alongside voided volume; hesitation means delayed initiation, whereas retention means a full bladder cannot empty; and urine color or odor can reflect concentration, diet, metabolism, or disease without diagnosing the cause. It adds anxiety, sleep disruption, cold exposure, outlet obstruction, inflammation, stones, neurological or metabolic disease, split stream, residual urine, weight change, and painful bladder distension as contextual inputs.

The shared practical rule is to compare symptoms with hydration, total and per-void volume, weather, infection signs, pain, pregnancy status, leakage trigger, emptying function, persistence, test quality, medications, metabolic or neurological context, and functional impact. Midstream urine collection matters because early urine can carry perineal secretions into the sample, while pregnancy urinalysis needs marker-specific interpretation before a lab result becomes a treatment conclusion.

## Key Claims
- Urinary symptoms are pattern-and-context signals, not proof that one organ, specialty, or diagnosis is responsible.
- Frequency, urgency, discoloration, infection signs, leakage, and pelvic or abdominal pain can overlap across bladder, urethra, prostate, kidney, gynecological, and non-urological causes.
- Sex-specific and life-stage context matters, including female urethral and vaginal proximity, pregnancy urine testing, and male prostate-related symptoms.
- Voiding delay, inability to empty a full bladder, incomplete emptying, increasing nocturia, hematuria, dysuria, persistent foamy urine, split stream, and loss of urinary control warrant assessment according to persistence, severity, volume, pain, and associated findings.
- Test interpretation depends on collection quality and marker type, including midstream urine collection and pregnancy markers such as white cells, red cells, nitrites, glucose, and protein.
- Incontinence triage starts with trigger pattern: leakage during coughing, sneezing, laughing, or abdominal pressure points toward stress urinary incontinence rather than urge alone.
- Persistent, painful, obstructive, infectious, pregnancy-associated, bloody, severe, recurrent, or unexplained symptoms should move from self-interpretation to qualified care.

- Physiological and behavior context: [[vol-31-miniao-waike-guanyu-niaopin-niaoye-huang-jieshi-nanxing-qianliexian-de-yaoyan-ji-wuqu-lpn3fhozej2v-3oijpakvkdx-r-y]] describes cold-weather frequency, hot-weather sweating, urine concentration, first-morning urine color, and hydration as interpretation variables; [[vol-30-miniao-waike-nvxing-miniao-xitong-de-changjian-wenti-yaoyan-ji-wuqu-lmeg-qk-dio77mj7retw0qoyoop-o]] adds bathing, sex, hygiene, clothing, tampon, and hydration behavior boundaries.

- Female-specific pattern recognition: [[vol-30-miniao-waike-nvxing-miniao-xitong-de-changjian-wenti-yaoyan-ji-wuqu-lmeg-qk-dio77mj7retw0qoyoop-o]] distinguishes stress urinary incontinence by leakage during abdominal-pressure events and frames abdominal pain by acute, chronic, urinary, menstrual, post-sex, and stone-like patterns.

- Appearance, odor, and stream: [[vol-147-daodi-yitian-yao-he-duoshao-shui-weisha-dongtian-shang-cesuo-geng-pinfan-huyu-gonggong-changsuo-duo-jian-nvce-lj9q7zljd68jmbtcd4klgrbj1vaz]] treats color, odor, split stream, weight change, and intake-output mismatch as assessment clues whose meaning depends on hydration, diet, inflammation, diabetes, prostate, kidney, and other context.
- Care escalation: [[vol-29-miniao-waike-renshi-miniao-xitong-he-rongyi-gua-cuo-hao-de-miniao-waike-llxb263cooyirukwcezzhysm57va]], [[vol-30-miniao-waike-nvxing-miniao-xitong-de-changjian-wenti-yaoyan-ji-wuqu-lmeg-qk-dio77mj7retw0qoyoop-o]], [[vol-31-miniao-waike-guanyu-niaopin-niaoye-huang-jieshi-nanxing-qianliexian-de-yaoyan-ji-wuqu-lpn3fhozej2v-3oijpakvkdx-r-y]], and [[vol-147-daodi-yitian-yao-he-duoshao-shui-weisha-dongtian-shang-cesuo-geng-pinfan-huyu-gonggong-changsuo-duo-jian-nvce-lj9q7zljd68jmbtcd4klgrbj1vaz]] keep persistent, severe, obstructive, infectious, bloody, painful, unexplained, or embarrassing symptoms inside regular or urgent clinical care rather than self-diagnosis.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This concept does not diagnose urinary retention, incomplete emptying, urinary frequency, urgency, hematuria, foamy urine, urine color or odor, split stream, leakage, infection, kidney function, liver or biliary disease, gynecological disease, pregnancy complications, stones, appendicitis, ectopic pregnancy, tuberculosis, diabetes, neurological disease, or prostate disease. Reported voiding counts, bladder volumes, timing suggestions, and intake-output or weight heuristics are source-scoped and not universal diagnostic thresholds. The concept captures public-education triage logic and should not replace urinalysis, imaging, residual-volume measurement, physical examination, obstetric or gynecological evaluation, urology care, or clinician-guided follow-up.
