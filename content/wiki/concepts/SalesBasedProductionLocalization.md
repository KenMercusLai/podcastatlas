# 销地产模式 / Sales-Based Production Localization

## Definition
销地产模式 is a production-location strategy where a company builds factories close to regional sales markets so goods can be produced and sold locally.

## Current Synthesis
The Wahaha source presents 销地产 as a second infrastructure layer alongside [[LianxiaotiDistribution|联销体模式]]. For bottled beverages and shelf-stable foods, proximity to demand lowers logistics cost, improves regional responsiveness, and can align expansion with local employment and government support. The model works best when a company already has enough volume and channel discipline to justify multi-region production bases.

## Key Claims
- Localized production can turn national distribution into a regional manufacturing network.
- Beverage logistics make distance costly, so production near demand can protect margin and freshness expectations.
- Local factories can create government and employment support that strengthens market entry.
- The model depends on sufficient sales certainty; premature localization would add fixed assets before demand is proven.
- Production localization complements but does not replace product renewal and digital-channel adaptation.

## Evidence
- Origin case: [[fengwei-chihe-wahaha-zongqinghou-de-sanci-duiju-gkwrijiouqosa60omatfv8yx]] says Zong Qinghou formed the idea after Wahaha's Fuling plant succeeded while supporting the Three Gorges reservoir region.
- Operating logic: [[fengwei-chihe-wahaha-zongqinghou-de-sanci-duiju-gkwrijiouqosa60omatfv8yx]] defines the model as producing and selling near the consumer market.
- Scale: [[fengwei-chihe-wahaha-zongqinghou-de-sanci-duiju-gkwrijiouqosa60omatfv8yx]] gives source-scoped 2024 figures for production bases, subsidiaries, and employees across China.
- Boundary: [[fengwei-chihe-wahaha-zongqinghou-de-sanci-duiju-gkwrijiouqosa60omatfv8yx]] later shows that localized production did not solve brand aging, diversification misfit, or e-commerce delay by itself.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept is grounded in a high-volume beverage company. It should not be generalized to asset-light software, niche CPG, or low-volume luxury products without separate evidence.

## What Changed
- Created a concept for Wahaha's regional factory strategy.
