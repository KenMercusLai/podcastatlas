# Technology Ambivalence in Fire and Signal / 火与信号的技术双重性

## Definition
Technology ambivalence in fire and signal / 火与信号的技术双重性 is the episode's reading of Piggy's glasses and fire in [[LordOfTheFlies|《蝇王》]] as tools that can rescue, organize, damage, and destroy.

## Current Synthesis
The concept captures the episode's claim that civilization's technical power is never morally single-use. Piggy's glasses make fire possible, and fire first stands for rescue and rule discipline. But fire also burns beyond control and later becomes Jack's weapon for hunting Ralph. The rescue at the end is therefore bitterly ambivalent: destructive fire gets noticed where disciplined signal fire failed.

## Key Claims
- A technical object can concentrate knowledge, vulnerability, and power at once.
- Signal fire ties technology to collective discipline, not only to possession of a tool.
- The same fire that promises rescue can become accidental disaster or deliberate weapon.
- The ending makes salvation depend on destruction, sharpening the novel's pessimism.
- The concept is a literary technology-risk frame, not a general theory of all tools.

- Concentrated object: [[vol-351-yingwang-21shiji-de-huangdao-gushi-haineng-zenme-kai-naodong-1983-nuojiang-dezhu-gkwriueodap1a3taaattkgol]] says Piggy's glasses are used to make fire and become a contested object.
- Discipline test: [[vol-351-yingwang-21shiji-de-huangdao-gushi-haineng-zenme-kai-naodong-1983-nuojiang-dezhu-gkwriueodap1a3taaattkgol]] says signal fire is a rescue hope and an organizational test.
- Accidental damage: [[vol-351-yingwang-21shiji-de-huangdao-gushi-haineng-zenme-kai-naodong-1983-nuojiang-dezhu-gkwriueodap1a3taaattkgol]] notes the first fire nearly causes wider destruction.
- Weaponization: [[vol-351-yingwang-21shiji-de-huangdao-gushi-haineng-zenme-kai-naodong-1983-nuojiang-dezhu-gkwriueodap1a3taaattkgol]] says the final fire is set to smoke Ralph out.
- Bitter rescue: [[vol-351-yingwang-21shiji-de-huangdao-gushi-haineng-zenme-kai-naodong-1983-nuojiang-dezhu-gkwriueodap1a3taaattkgol]] emphasizes that the destructive fire brings naval rescue.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not treat technology itself as evil. The danger comes from weak social control, fear, rivalry, and ignorance of power; fire is ambivalent because it amplifies those conditions.
