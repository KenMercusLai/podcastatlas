## Definition

Wuzhu purchasing power frame / 五铢钱购买力框架 is the episode's interpretive method for making “八千万” legible by translating it into five-zhu coins and rough grain-buying capacity.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 410-2 treats “八千万” as eighty million five-zhu coins. The host then uses a grain-price example to give listeners a felt scale for the phrase. This is useful because the inscription otherwise risks becoming only a lucky number or empty exaggeration.

The concept is deliberately narrow. It records a pedagogical frame, not a settled price index. The episode itself uses the conversion to compare ancient aspiration with modern “small target” wealth language, while acknowledging through its explanatory style that the calculation is approximate and illustrative.

## Key Claims

- The inscription's number is interpreted through five-zhu coins rather than abstract currency.
- Grain purchasing power is used to make the scale concrete for modern listeners.
- The conversion functions as historical imagination more than precise price reconstruction.
- Five-zhu money appears as a durable monetary reference point in the source's Han economic story.
- The frame connects material inscription, currency history, and wealth aspiration.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source does not establish a rigorous long-run price series, regional price spread, or wage comparison. Its five-zhu-to-grain comparison should therefore be used as an explanatory scale marker, not as a precise economic-history conversion.
