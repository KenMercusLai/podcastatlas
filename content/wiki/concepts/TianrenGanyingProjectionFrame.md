## Definition

Tianren ganying projection frame / 天人感应投射框架 is the source-scoped view that the outer world mirrors or responds to inner cognition, attention, speech, conduct, love, resentment, and repeated thought patterns.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-161-caiyun-he-renji-guanxi-de-liangzi-jiuchan-3-ll74kq2hg2vp-zx-cqxkadxahamd|Hanji 161 part 3]] links older Chinese天人感应 language with a holographic or crystal-like world, projection, the attraction law, karma, and current cognition when explaining wealth, hardship, family harmony, and repeated bad events. The concept extends [[RelationalFateNetwork|关系命运网络]] from social ties into a broader cosmic-feedback claim: a person is not only affected by parents, partners, friends, and speech; the world itself is imagined as responsive to thought, love, resentment, and awareness.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-369-2-gaiming-bikan-yinji-faze-yyds-foitbkrmhmc1xgeu0tk4btyvmi98|Hanji 369-2]] gives the same worldview a compact name, “印记法则.” Impressions of other people are said to return like a mirror, while thoughts are seeds whose later conditions magnify their results. The episode applies this to [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] by interpreting a depleted talent pool as a reflection of earlier harshness, then turns the lesson inward: maturity means observing one's thoughts and accepting responsibility for the conduct they generate.

The wiki keeps both formulations source-scoped and separates their practical and metaphysical layers. Repeated attention, language, expectations, and conduct can affect relationships, decisions, and opportunities. The stronger claim that inner state directly manifests wealth, hardship, illness, victimization, or external events is not stored as settled physics, psychology, economics, history, or moral responsibility.

## Key Claims
- Ancient天人感应 is used as a bridge between religious cosmology and the episode's "quantum" language.
- The world is compared to a holographic or crystal-like field where inner ideas project outward.
- Wealth and hardship are explained through multiple layers: present cognition, present behavior, present relationships, and karma.
- Family love, acceptance of parents, and compassion are treated as ways to change the projected field.
- The frame is broader than peer influence because it makes cosmos, society, and inner state mutually responsive.
- Hanji 369-2's “印记法则” uses mirror and seed metaphors to connect impressions and thoughts with later experience.
- Thought observation is the practical bridge: noticing a mental pattern can interrupt the conduct through which it otherwise shapes relationships and decisions.

## Evidence

- Projection field: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-161-caiyun-he-renji-guanxi-de-liangzi-jiuchan-3-ll74kq2hg2vp-zx-cqxkadxahamd|Hanji 161 part 3]] links天人感应, holographic-world language, attraction, karma, cognition, family love, wealth, and hardship.
- Reflective practice: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-161-caiyun-he-renji-guanxi-de-liangzi-jiuchan-3-ll74kq2hg2vp-zx-cqxkadxahamd|Hanji 161 part 3]] says repeated relational and material problems should prompt examination of thought, speech, action, resentment, and love.
- Mirror and seed formulation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-369-2-gaiming-bikan-yinji-faze-yyds-foitbkrmhmc1xgeu0tk4btyvmi98|Hanji 369-2]] says impressions return like a mirror and thoughts grow like seeds when later conditions support them.
- Historical application: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-369-2-gaiming-bikan-yinji-faze-yyds-foitbkrmhmc1xgeu0tk4btyvmi98|Hanji 369-2]] interprets Han Wudi's recruitment crisis as feedback from earlier severity, while the source note preserves that link as the host's moral reading rather than established causation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Both sources blend spiritual, ethical, and practical claims. Quantum, holographic, attraction-law, karmic, and direct manifestation language is not treated as established science. Self-observation can support agency without making a person the sole cause of every event; deception, violence, illness, job loss, institutional power, economic structure, and chance remain external conditions. The Han Wudi application likewise cannot prove that harsh punishment alone caused the loss of senior officials.
