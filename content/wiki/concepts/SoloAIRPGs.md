# Solo AI RPGs

## Definition
Solo AI RPGs are AI-mediated interactive stories where a user becomes the protagonist and the system turns a short story, book, or scenario into an open-ended role-playing experience.

## Current Synthesis
[[MarkStiltner]] describes [[SoloAIRPGs]] in [[ep-39-why-the-future-of-ai-belongs-to-divergent-thinkers]] as a creative product format emerging from his tabletop role-playing and AI publishing work. The format resembles choose-your-own-adventure fiction, but the AI is meant to preserve story structure, characters, and setting while allowing more open user action.

The concept belongs to the wiki's AI creativity branch because it turns generative AI into an interactive narrative engine rather than a one-shot drafting tool. In this source, it also functions as a hands-on AI learning project: Mark uses creative publishing to understand AI capabilities and apply those lessons back to work.

## Key Claims
- Solo AI RPGs turn fixed stories or scenarios into interactive narratives where the user plays the protagonist.
- The format is compared with choose-your-own-adventure books but aims for a more open world.
- AI must preserve story structure, characters, and setting while responding to user choices.
- The current source describes the format as text-based, with graphics and more immersive experiences left as future possibilities.
- For Mark, the format is both a creative outlet and a practical AI learning lab.
