# Behavior Change Obstacle Matching

## Definition

Behavior change obstacle matching is the practice of identifying the specific force blocking a desired action and selecting an intervention designed for that barrier.

## Current Synthesis

[[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835|You, But Better]] rejects a single willpower explanation for failed goals. Low enjoyment may call for fun or [[TemptationBundling]]; intimidating scale for smaller units; invisible progress for immediate feedback; stale motivation for a [[FreshStartEffect|fresh start]]; forgetfulness for reminders; procrastination for intermediate deadlines; friction for a helpful default; and [[PresentBias]] for automation or precommitment.

The framework treats human limitations as design inputs rather than moral verdicts. Its discipline is causal humility: a tool is useful only if it targets the operative barrier and does not introduce greater cost, coercion, or risk.

## Key Claims

- Similar failures can arise from different obstacles and therefore require different interventions.
- Enjoyment, scale, feedback, memory, timing, friction, procrastination, and present bias are separable diagnostic candidates.
- Environmental and temporal design can complement motivation without assuming people lack knowledge or values.
- An intervention should be evaluated by sustained behavior and side effects, not by how ingenious or demanding it appears.
- Structural and clinical barriers must not be relabeled as personal design failures.

## Evidence

- Diagnostic range - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835]] distinguishes distraction, forgetfulness, impulsiveness, low enjoyment, procrastination, and present bias.
- Matched toolkit - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835]] links fun, bundling, small steps, feedback, fresh starts, reminders, deadlines, defaults, automation, and commitment devices to different barriers.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source offers a practical framework rather than a validated diagnostic taxonomy. Barriers interact, self-diagnosis can be wrong, and an intervention that helps initiation may not sustain performance. Poverty, unsafe work, discrimination, disability, caregiving, illness, and other structural conditions can dominate individual-level design.
