## Definition

Absent-presence character design constructs a figure who is physically dead, missing, or outside the main timeline yet continues to cause present action through memory, objects, skills, obligations, relationships, and delayed revelation.

## Current Synthesis

[[XiaXueyi|夏雪宜]] dies before the main action of [[BixueJian|《碧血剑》]], but his revenge, love for [[WenYiBixueJian|温仪]], exploitation of [[HeHongyao|何红药]], treasure, weapons, martial knowledge, and daughter [[WenQingqing|温青青]] remain active causes. The novel can therefore disclose him gradually through effects and witnesses rather than treating his death as completed backstory.

The method creates suspense and moral layering. New information does not merely describe a fixed predecessor; it revises how present characters understand inheritance, abandonment, blame, and motive. Absence becomes narratively productive when the figure's residues change choices in the current plot.

## Key Claims

- Physical absence does not equal causal absence when a character leaves active material and relational residues.
- Gradual reconstruction lets different witnesses disclose incompatible or partial versions of the same person.
- Objects and techniques can carry character history when later users inherit both capability and obligation.
- Posthumous revelation can alter living characters' judgments about abandonment, guilt, identity, and duty.
- The device is strongest when present choices change, not when backstory merely adds lore.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept currently rests on one podcast reading and one principal case, with 胡一刀 mentioned only as an analogy. A dead character with extensive exposition is not automatically an absent presence; the defining test is whether the remaining traces continue to organize present causality and judgment.

## What Changed

- Created a narrative framework linking posthumous influence to objects, relationships, revelation, and present choice.
