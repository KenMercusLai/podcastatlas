# BJD Doll Makeup Commission / BJD妆面约稿

BJD doll makeup commission is the craft-service example in [[ep242-duli-youxi-shi-yitiao-gaoqian-hao-saidao-ma-lrqbzrceumbuggzuero5qb4jv3zu]] where participants pay makeup artists to paint and customize ball-jointed doll heads. [[WangYiranSanlian|王依然]] uses it to show that [[OriginalCharacterCommissionEconomy|设圈]] income is not limited to digital illustration.

The source's price examples are modest but revealing: ordinary head makeup can be low-priced, while complex work that must accurately reproduce a character setting can cost more. The economic unit is therefore a mix of manual skill, fandom literacy, faithful interpretation, and client trust.
