## Definition

Omen-coded remonstrance / 天象灾异包装式进谏 is the practice of using celestial signs, disasters, strange natural events, and sacred doctrine as a protected vocabulary for political criticism. In Hanji 873, [[XiangKaiLateHan|襄楷]] uses this method to accuse [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] without framing the memorial as ordinary personal attack.

## Current Synthesis

The concept sits between omen interpretation and advice under danger. [[CelestialOmenPoliticalResponsibility|天象政治责任]] and [[NaturalDisasterPoliticalOmen|自然灾害政治征兆]] make political failure legible through signs; omen-coded remonstrance turns those signs into a speaking strategy when direct accusation is risky.

Hanji 873 shows the strategy moving through layers. Xiang Kai begins from stars and mansions, adds cold, hail, plague, and Yellow River anomalies, then attaches those signs to eunuch favor, harsh punishment, wrongful killing, and Huan's lack of an heir. His second memorial adds a further turn: because Huan worships Laozi and the Buddha, those religious teachings can be used to expose contradiction between ritual display and conduct.

The method does not make the speaker safe. The same coding that lets the memorial reach court also lets Huan treat the content as astronomy rather than open rebellion, but the political attack remains clear enough that Xiang Kai is sentenced to two years. The concept therefore records a partial bypass of [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|君臣反馈失灵]], not a stable remedy for it.

## Key Claims

- Omen language can carry direct political criticism while preserving a formally acceptable cosmic-warning frame.
- The method can combine sky signs, terrestrial disasters, hydrological anomalies, succession pressure, and moral doctrine in one argument.
- Ambiguous signs are rhetorically usable because the speaker can read them against the surrounding political field rather than in isolation.
- Religious authority can become a mirror against rulers who honor a teaching ritually while violating its ethical demands.
- Omen coding can lower immediate execution risk without removing punishment risk.

## Evidence

- Layered omen vocabulary: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-873-ta-ruhe-liyong-tianxiang-tongma-diwang-lqebkodcbcrsvyf80qwxnobydbhc|Hanji 873]] has Xiang Kai move from planets and mansions to cold, frost, hail, thunder, plague, and Yellow River clarity.
- Political target: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-873-ta-ruhe-liyong-tianxiang-tongma-diwang-lqebkodcbcrsvyf80qwxnobydbhc|Hanji 873]] links the signs to eunuch favor, excessive punishment, wrongful killing, official injustice, and Huan's childlessness.
- Ambiguous auspiciousness: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-873-ta-ruhe-liyong-tianxiang-tongma-diwang-lqebkodcbcrsvyf80qwxnobydbhc|Hanji 873]] says the Yellow River turning clear is suspicious under a field of anomalies, even though omen tradition can read river clarity favorably.
- Religious reversal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-873-ta-ruhe-liyong-tianxiang-tongma-diwang-lqebkodcbcrsvyf80qwxnobydbhc|Hanji 873]] says Xiang Kai uses Laozi and Buddhist restraint, quietude, non-attachment, and non-killing ideals against Huan's desire and punishment.
- Safety limit: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-873-ta-ruhe-liyong-tianxiang-tongma-diwang-lqebkodcbcrsvyf80qwxnobydbhc|Hanji 873]] says Huan does not kill Xiang Kai but still sends him to a two-year sentence.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept is source-scoped to Hanji 873 for now. It should not absorb all indirect criticism or all omen politics: [[AllusiveRemonstrance|隐语进谏]] can work through metaphor without astrology, and ordinary [[ChineseAstrologicalPolitics|中国星占政治]] can explain signs without becoming a memorial strategy. The source also records the host's interpretation of Huan's Buddhist favor; it does not independently settle the full chronology of Buddhism in China.

## What Changed

- Created the concept from Hanji 873's two-memorial account of Xiang Kai's star, disaster, river, succession, and religious criticism.
