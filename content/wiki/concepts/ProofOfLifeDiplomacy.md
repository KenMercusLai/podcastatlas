# Proof of Life Diplomacy

Proof of life diplomacy is the use of verification access as a diplomatic demand when a detained figure's condition is uncertain. [[the-case-of-the-missing-totem-aung-san-suu-kyi-6a560fc4f96471dde4821d2f]] adds the concept through [[AungSanSuuKyi]], whose last confirmed sighting in the episode was December 30, 2022.

The concept sits between humanitarian verification and political bargaining. [[KimEris]] wants foreign leaders to demand proof that his mother is alive and well, while the [[MyanmarMilitaryJunta]] may see access as costly because it could revive her [[NonviolentSymbolicPower]]. At the same time, allowing diplomats to see her could help the junta in [[ASEAN]] and [[UnitedNations]] legitimacy contests.

## Key Claims
- Proof of life is different from release: it asks first for credible access or verification, not necessarily political settlement.
- Unverifiable photos, claimed meetings, and leaked records do not resolve the diplomatic problem if outside observers cannot confirm timing and conditions.
- The demand can create leverage because regimes that want normalization may need to show humane treatment.
- Verification can also over-personalize a national crisis if it becomes a substitute for broader accountability.
