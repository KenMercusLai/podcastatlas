# Creative Focus by Subtraction / 减法式创作聚焦

## Definition
Creative focus by subtraction is the deliberate removal of projects, appearances, obligations, and low-value concerns so finite attention and energy can deepen a smaller set of intrinsically important practices.

## Current Synthesis
[[LiRonghao|李荣浩]] describes this shift through a menu metaphor: rather than continuing to order six dishes because two might be interesting, he now keeps the four he actually wants and expects the list may narrow further. In practice, fewer television and gala appearances leave more time for albums, mixing, skill practice, concerts, and solitary interests.

The subtraction is not passive withdrawal. It protects repeated technical work that can absorb many hours and acknowledges that age changes recovery, memory, and the opportunity cost of saying yes. The related decision rule is to accept that choosing one path excludes another, rather than demand the benefits of incompatible choices and call the loss regret.

## Key Claims
- Focus requires explicit refusal because adding priorities without removing commitments does not create time.
- Deep craft can justify a narrower portfolio when iteration, practice, and recovery have high attention costs.
- Age and changing capacity can improve selection even while reducing stamina or memory.
- Owning a choice includes accepting the opportunity cost of the path not taken.
- Subtraction should follow genuine value and capacity rather than fear, status avoidance, or blanket isolation.

## Evidence
- Menu metaphor - [[vol-57-duitan-lironghao-gai-fangxia-jiu-fangxia-gai-buyao-jiu-buyao-lujrbxdhqr5-hrqmyj746w6uap5]] records the move from six partly exploratory choices toward four genuinely wanted ones.
- Work reallocation - [[vol-57-duitan-lironghao-gai-fangxia-jiu-fangxia-gai-buyao-jiu-buyao-lujrbxdhqr5-hrqmyj746w6uap5]] links reduced program and gala participation to concentrated album, mixing, and concert work.
- Choice cost - [[vol-57-duitan-lironghao-gai-fangxia-jiu-fangxia-gai-buyao-jiu-buyao-lujrbxdhqr5-hrqmyj746w6uap5]] has Li reject regret framed as wanting both mutually exclusive outcomes after choosing one.
- Capacity context - [[vol-57-duitan-lironghao-gai-fangxia-jiu-fangxia-gai-buyao-jiu-buyao-lujrbxdhqr5-hrqmyj746w6uap5]] connects slower recovery and selective forgetting after forty to the broader “该不要就不要” attitude.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
One artist's preference does not establish an ideal number of projects or prove that public appearances, collaboration, exploration, or broad portfolios reduce creativity. Narrowing can protect mastery, but it can also shrink opportunity, feedback, income, relationships, and novelty. Capacity changes and memory concerns should not be romanticized when they cause impairment or distress.
