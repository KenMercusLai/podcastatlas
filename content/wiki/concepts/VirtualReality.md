# Virtual Reality / 虚拟现实

## Definition
Virtual reality is an immersive interface mode where a head-mounted device primarily places the user inside a virtual environment rather than keeping the physical world as the main interface.

## Current Synthesis
The RayNeo source uses VR as a contrast class for AI glasses. [[LiHongwei|李鸿伟]] treats VR/MR headsets as more like game consoles or low-frequency content devices, valuable for immersive stories and large-space entertainment but less suited to all-day daily wear.

That does not make VR useless. The episode describes large-space VR content as meaningful entertainment and treats [[VisionPro|Vision Pro]] as useful foundation hardware for interaction and content experiments. The qualification is that VR's content-driven model differs from ordinary smart glasses' high-frequency real-world augmentation path.

## Key Claims
- VR is more immersive and virtual-world-centered than ordinary smart glasses.
- VR/MR headsets may be stronger for content, games, and special venues than for all-day assistant use.
- Content availability and experience quality are central to VR adoption.
- VR can still provide valuable interaction and display experiments for broader spatial computing.
- The source distinguishes VR's product rhythm from AI glasses rather than ranking one category absolutely above the other.
