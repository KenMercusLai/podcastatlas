# Islamist Democratic Trust Problem

The Islamist democratic trust problem is the suspicion described in [[spring-then-fall-a-weakened-muslim-brotherhood-6a7aef1b616564bf00d9b354]] that a movement such as the [[MuslimBrotherhood]] may participate in elections only until it can impose an Islamist governing model. The episode presents this as one reason governments and non-Brotherhood actors see the movement as subversive or untrustworthy.

The concept matters because it turns democratic participation into an unstable signal. Winning an election does not settle legitimacy if opponents believe the winner will later narrow pluralism, while repression can then be justified by the same actors as pre-emptive defense against future authoritarianism.
