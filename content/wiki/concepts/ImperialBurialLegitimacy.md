# Imperial Burial Legitimacy / 帝后合葬名分

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-900-zhichang-quanshu-dou-budong-gai-zenyang-shengcun-lmhjwosy4eswyhfppdghfsmfycqr|Hanji 900]] supplies the pre-meeting layer for imperial burial legitimacy / 帝后合葬名分. After [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦皇太后]] dies, her initial poor treatment and the attempt by [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] and [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]] to give her guiren-level rites show that burial status is already a factional decision before formal deliberation begins.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-901-jixingzi-de-ren-hui-shi-shenme-xiachang-lh1yfyjzqptxvtnv9taiqms9yjud|Hanji 901]] then develops the open debate. The source treats the question of whether Dou should be buried with [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] as a live political judgment, not a ceremonial detail.

The dispute turns burial into public recognition. [[ChenQiuLateHan|陈球 / 陈求]] and [[LiXianTaiweiLateHan|李贤]] argue that Dou's empress-dowager status, support for [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s accession, and separation from the Dou family's political failure should preserve her place beside Huan. [[ZhaoZhongLateHan|赵忠]], [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]], and [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]] resist because restoring Dou's status would also reopen the grievance memory around [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] and [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]].

The concept overlaps with [[MingqiLegitimacy|名器合法性]], [[LiAsPoliticalOrder|礼制政治秩序]], and [[PosthumousNamePolitics|谥号政治]], but it is narrower. It names cases where tomb placement, co-sacrifice, posthumous title, and spouse/dowager recognition decide whether a deceased political actor remains inside or outside the official ritual order.

## Key Claims

- Hanji 900 adds that posthumous demotion can begin through transport, temporary neglect, and funeral-rank framing before the court reaches the formal burial question.
- Burial placement and sacrificial pairing can recognize or erase a political role after death.
- Family-guilt logic can be extended into posthumous status, not only into living punishment.
- A court may fight over burial because burial fixes public memory and factional blame.
- Hanji 901 adds a successful but narrow repair: Emperor Ling accepts Li Xian's memorial and grants Dou the Huan Si Empress title, while the larger eunuch influence problem remains.
