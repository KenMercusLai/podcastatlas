# Slow Rural Tourism / 慢速乡村旅游

Slow rural tourism is the development stance described in [[144-nvxia-duan-sihe-he-tade-jiaxiang-mapingguan-women-ceng-yiwei-you-dian-you-lu-zhishi-meng-linmjqt-n-sa5sarpqgdxgtoeejt]] through [[Mapingguan|马坪关]] and [[DuanSihe|段四合]]'s homestay. The village wants income, learning, and outside contact, but the source repeatedly warns against entering tourism or online promotion faster than residents can absorb.

The concept differs from anti-tourism. It treats tourism as legitimate only when guest flow, hosting capacity, public rules, ecology, and local rhythm can be managed together.

## Key Claims
- Tourism can damage a village by arriving too fast, even when demand would bring money.
- Slow opening can be rational if residents still need language practice, reception routines, meeting rules, and confidence with outsiders.
- [[HuangYunwu|黄云武]]'s warning that the village may lack absorptive readiness rather than resources makes capacity, not publicity, the bottleneck.
- Slow tourism connects with [[MatsutakeCommonsManagement]] because ecological resources and village trust are the assets tourism must not consume too quickly.
- The future museum idea can become [[HeritageTourismCommercialization]] only if it remains grounded in local memory rather than becoming a thin display for visitors.
