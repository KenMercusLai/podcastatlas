## Definition

公开辞让式升格 / Public refusal status inflation is the pattern where a powerful actor's visible refusal of reward does not end the reward cycle, but instead increases public pressure, moral credit, and justification for a higher grant.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 608, Hanji 614, Hanji 620, and Hanji 624 define the pattern through [[WangMang|王莽]] before his formal usurpation. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-608-jiemi-xihan-aosika-zuijia-yanyuan-wangmang-llwfchwpkmuesz0nmdr48qpuwcjk|Hanji 608]] supplies the earliest clean version: Wang Mang asks that [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]], [[WangShunXinTaishi|王顺]], [[ZhenFengXinOfficial|甄丰]], and [[ZhenHanXinOfficial|甄邯]] be rewarded while he is left out; after repeated refusal and illness claims, ministers press [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] to recognize him, and he receives Taifu, Anhan Gong, Four Assistant authority, and larger nominal grant while continuing to refuse added households. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-614-liaoliao-wangmang-gaiming-he-jianv-de-guaidan-gushi-lhz58yc4bqn2pqfa1f21hbsiteql|Hanji 614]] supplies a misfired version: Wang Mang's self-deprecating language about his daughter is taken by Wang Zhengjun as a reason to exclude Wang-family women from empress selection, after which petition pressure repairs the outcome and makes the daughter of Anhan Gong look indispensable. In Hanji 620, Wang Shun and more than 8,000 officials and commoners petition for Wang Mang's reward; Wang Zhengjun personally confers the "宰衡" status package; Wang Mang refuses his sons' rewards and claims illness; and Kong Guang and other ministers turn that refusal into a reason to reject further退让 memorials and summon him back to government. Hanji 624 gives the later, larger version: officials and commoners petition because Wang Mang refuses the Xinye grant, Wang Mang asks that the petitions be ignored and speaks of finishing ritual institutions before retiring, and the performance gives Wang Zhengjun a reason to prepare [[JiuXiRitualPromotion|Nine Bestowments]].

The important synthesis is that refusal can be politically productive when the audience, messenger channel, and superior endorsement are already aligned. The episode does not prove Wang Mang's private intent, but it shows the public mechanism: refusal creates humility credit, humility credit creates dependence, and dependence justifies greater status.

## Key Claims

- Public refusal can amplify rather than reduce status when others are ready to convert it into virtue.
- Refusal language can also misfire when the superior accepts it literally, requiring a second audience-pressure step to recover the intended result.
- Petition campaigns make reward appear externally demanded rather than self-requested.
- Ministerial and trusted reporting channels can turn refusal language into policy input for a higher authority.
- Superior endorsement converts humility rhetoric into formal promotion.
- The pattern is especially potent when refusal is paired with public-welfare language or unfinished ritual institutions that frame the refuser as indispensable.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept is currently source-grounded in Wang Mang's case. It should not be flattened into a general claim that every refusal is manipulative; the sources leave Wang Mang's inner motive inferred rather than directly proven. Hanji 608 supports the reward mechanics and public-welfare language, but exact Four Assistant authority, full grant lists, and Wang Mang's private intent remain source-scoped. Hanji 614 is a caution that refusal scripts require interpretation and aligned intermediaries: Wang Zhengjun's literal reading initially defeats the tactic. Hanji 620's "全民大选" framing is an analogy for mass petition pressure, not evidence of election procedure. The pattern also differs from ordinary [[RitualRenunciationPoliticalCapital|ritual renunciation as political capital]] because the status jump here is court-wide, ceremonial, and tied to a usurpation trajectory.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 608 as the earliest clean refusal-to-promotion loop: other officials rewarded first, illness-based absence, ministerial pressure, Taifu/Anhan Gong elevation, and continued material refusal.
- Preserved Hanji 614 as a misfired refusal-and-petition-repair case around Wang Mang's daughter.
- Preserved Hanji 620 as a later "宰衡" petition-refusal-summons version of the same status-inflation mechanism.
- Preserved Hanji 624 as the later Xinye and Nine Bestowments version.
