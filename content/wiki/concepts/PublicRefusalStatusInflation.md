## Definition

公开辞让式升格 / Public refusal status inflation is the pattern where a powerful actor's visible refusal of reward does not end the reward cycle, but instead increases public pressure, moral credit, and justification for a higher grant.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 620 and Hanji 624 define the pattern through [[WangMang|王莽]] before his formal usurpation. In Hanji 620, [[WangShunXinTaishi|王顺]] and more than 8,000 officials and commoners petition for Wang Mang's reward; [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] personally confers the "宰衡" status package; Wang Mang refuses his sons' rewards and claims illness; and [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]] and other ministers turn that refusal into a reason to reject further退让 memorials and summon him back to government. Hanji 624 gives the later, larger version: officials and commoners petition because Wang Mang refuses the Xinye grant, Wang Mang asks that the petitions be ignored and speaks of finishing ritual institutions before retiring, and the performance gives Wang Zhengjun a reason to prepare [[JiuXiRitualPromotion|Nine Bestowments]].

The important synthesis is that refusal can be politically productive when the audience, messenger channel, and superior endorsement are already aligned. The episode does not prove Wang Mang's private intent, but it shows the public mechanism: refusal creates humility credit, humility credit creates dependence, and dependence justifies greater status.

## Key Claims

- Public refusal can amplify rather than reduce status when others are ready to convert it into virtue.
- Petition campaigns make reward appear externally demanded rather than self-requested.
- Ministerial and trusted reporting channels can turn refusal language into policy input for a higher authority.
- Superior endorsement converts humility rhetoric into formal promotion.
- The pattern is especially potent when ritual institutions are unfinished and the refuser is framed as indispensable.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept is currently source-grounded in Wang Mang's case. It should not be flattened into a general claim that every refusal is manipulative; the sources leave Wang Mang's inner motive inferred rather than directly proven. Hanji 620's "全民大选" framing is an analogy for mass petition pressure, not evidence of election procedure. The pattern also differs from ordinary [[RitualRenunciationPoliticalCapital|ritual renunciation as political capital]] because the status jump here is court-wide, ceremonial, and tied to a usurpation trajectory.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 620 as an earlier "宰衡" petition-refusal-summons version of the same status-inflation mechanism.
- Preserved Hanji 624 as the later Xinye and Nine Bestowments version.
