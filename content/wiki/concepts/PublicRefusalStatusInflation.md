## Definition

公开辞让式升格 / Public refusal status inflation is the pattern where a powerful actor's visible refusal of reward does not end the reward cycle, but instead increases public pressure, moral credit, and justification for a higher grant.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 624 defines the pattern through [[WangMang|王莽]] before his formal usurpation. Officials and commoners petition because Wang Mang refuses the Xinye grant. Wang Mang then says the petitions should be ignored and claims he wants only to complete ritual institutions before retiring. Rather than shrinking his power, that performance gives [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] a reason to insist that great affairs still need Wang Mang and that the court should prepare [[JiuXiRitualPromotion|Nine Bestowments]].

The important synthesis is that refusal can be politically productive when the audience, messenger channel, and superior endorsement are already aligned. The episode does not prove Wang Mang's private intent, but it shows the public mechanism: refusal creates humility credit, humility credit creates dependence, and dependence justifies greater status.

## Key Claims

- Public refusal can amplify rather than reduce status when others are ready to convert it into virtue.
- Petition campaigns make reward appear externally demanded rather than self-requested.
- A trusted reporting channel can turn refusal language into policy input for a higher authority.
- Superior endorsement converts humility rhetoric into formal promotion.
- The pattern is especially potent when ritual institutions are unfinished and the refuser is framed as indispensable.

- Petition base: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-624-du-lishi-wu-xingfu-ni-shi-rensheng-yingjia-haishi-yongren-zirao-daibiao-lpavicltc7nducbxiqgpr6t7iczd|Hanji 624]] says mass petitions ask that Wang Mang be rewarded after he refuses Xinye land.
- Refusal language: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-624-du-lishi-wu-xingfu-ni-shi-rensheng-yingjia-haishi-yongren-zirao-daibiao-lpavicltc7nducbxiqgpr6t7iczd|Hanji 624]] says Wang Mang asks the court to shelve the petitions and speaks of finishing rituals before retirement.
- Conversion to promotion: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-624-du-lishi-wu-xingfu-ni-shi-rensheng-yingjia-haishi-yongren-zirao-daibiao-lpavicltc7nducbxiqgpr6t7iczd|Hanji 624]] says Zeng Han reports the statement and Wang Zhengjun orders Nine Bestowments ritual prepared.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept is currently source-grounded in Wang Mang's case. It should not be flattened into a general claim that every refusal is manipulative; the source leaves Wang Mang's inner motive inferred rather than directly proven. It also differs from ordinary [[RitualRenunciationPoliticalCapital|ritual renunciation as political capital]] because the status jump here is court-wide, ceremonial, and tied to a usurpation trajectory.
