# Rural Parent-Child Reading / 乡村亲子阅读

Rural parent-child reading is the education and confidence practice added by [[144-nvxia-duan-sihe-he-tade-jiaxiang-mapingguan-women-ceng-yiwei-you-dian-you-lu-zhishi-meng-linmjqt-n-sa5sarpqgdxgtoeejt]] through [[AiWawaReadingGroup|爱娃娃亲子阅读小组]]. The source presents reading as a response to [[Mapingguan|马坪关]] children's school-consolidation stress, Mandarin gaps, lack of preschool experience, and shame about mountain identity.

The concept links books to social contact. Picture books and activities gather parents and children, while outside volunteers and city children create repeated exposure that helps village children become less afraid of strangers.

## Key Claims
- Reading can be a social-ease and identity-support practice before it becomes test preparation.
- Parent participation matters because school consolidation can make family and village support feel farther away.
- The source extends [[FamilyReadingEcology]] by showing reading as a village project rather than only a household atmosphere.
- Rural parent-child reading also supports [[RuralWomenPublicParticipation]] because mothers and caregivers organizing children gain public practice themselves.
