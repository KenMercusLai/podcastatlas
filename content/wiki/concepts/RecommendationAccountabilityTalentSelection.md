## Definition

举荐问责式选才 / recommendation-accountability talent selection is a recruitment design in which senior officials nominate candidates and later receive reward or punishment according to the nominees' demonstrated fitness, making sponsorship a monitored judgment rather than a costless favor.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-248-dongzhongshu-ruhe-gaibian-zhonghua-minzu-de-mingyun-2-lrjy7ozds7peyu6x3iwufety2zvt|Hanji 248(2)]] begins from three alleged distortions in Han office access: family background, purchase, and promotion by accumulated tenure. Its answer combines decentralized search with centralized or formal evaluation. Marquises, commandery governors, and high-ranking officials are asked to find capable people in the jurisdictions they know, while examination tests ability and conduct informs placement.

The distinctive mechanism is retrospective responsibility. A recommender benefits when a nominee proves capable and is penalized when the nominee proves unfit. This can make senior officials invest more care in discovery and screening, but it also creates incentives to nominate safe, conforming, or easily monitored people and may punish reasonable judgments because of later outcomes outside the sponsor's control.

## Key Claims

- Local and senior officials can possess candidate knowledge that a central ruler or examination process lacks.
- Reward and penalty turn recommendation from ceremonial endorsement into an accountable administrative judgment.
- Examination of ability and assessment of conduct answer different parts of appointment fitness.
- Seniority alone is weak evidence of talent because time served can coexist with poor judgment or low capacity.
- Outcome-linked sponsor liability may improve screening while also encouraging risk avoidance, conformity, and patronage protection.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept currently rests on one compact popular-history summary and does not independently establish the proposal's transmitted wording, nomination quota, salary threshold, administrative adoption, or relationship to later 察举 and 孝廉 practice. Rewarding or punishing recommenders can sharpen incentives, but the source does not show how responsibility was assigned when performance reflected training, office conditions, factional conflict, or later policy choices rather than initial candidate quality.

## What Changed

- Established the concept from the episode's combination of local nomination, examination, conduct review, and retrospective sponsor liability.
- Added incentive-side qualifications separating careful screening from risk-averse or conformity-biased nomination.
