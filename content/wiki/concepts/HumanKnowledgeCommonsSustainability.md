# Human Knowledge Commons Sustainability

## Definition
Human knowledge commons sustainability is the ability of an openly useful knowledge ecosystem to keep attracting current human participation when platforms, search products, and AI systems can extract or summarize its value without reproducing its access, recognition, traffic, or contributor incentives.

## Current Synthesis
The [[Reddit]] case joins three dependencies that are often discussed separately. Communities such as [[AskHistorians]] need experts who see public value in contributing; platforms need visits and revenue to operate; and AI systems need fresh human material to remain current and diverse. Licensing can transfer money to the platform, but it does not by itself preserve open access, contributor motivation, community legitimacy, or the flow of new human knowledge.

## Key Claims
- Human knowledge production is a renewable social process, not a fixed corpus that can be extracted indefinitely.
- Answer layers can create public convenience while weakening visits, recognition, and participation at the source.
- Platform compensation does not automatically compensate contributors or preserve their reasons for participating.
- Access walls may protect platform value in the short run while reducing public learning and the future supply of contributions.
- AI companies share an ecosystem interest in sustaining current, diverse, human-generated knowledge rather than relying on recursively generated material.

## Evidence
- Contribution mission - [[tech-20261111-1111-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261111-1111-mp-tech-pod-128]] uses AskHistorians to show that experts may contribute for public education and that restricted access can weaken that purpose.
- Platform-AI dependency - [[tech-20261111-1111-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261111-1111-mp-tech-pod-128]] connects Reddit data licensing and AI summaries to reduced source visits while arguing that AI still needs daily human discussion.
- Recursive-data warning - [[tech-20261111-1111-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261111-1111-mp-tech-pod-128]] cites model degradation after repeated synthetic-data training as a reason to preserve fresh human production.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not measure how many contributors will stop posting, whether login users contribute more, whether licensing revenue can subsidize community health, or which compensation and access designs work best. Login requirements can also reduce scraping or abuse, and filtered synthetic data can remain useful; the concept therefore identifies a sustainability constraint rather than proving that every access wall or AI summary causes net harm.

## What Changed
- Established a concept linking contributor motivation, open access, platform viability, and AI's dependence on current human knowledge.
