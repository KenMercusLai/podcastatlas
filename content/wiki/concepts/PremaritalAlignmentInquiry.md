# Premarital Alignment Inquiry / 婚前共识核对

## Definition
Premarital alignment inquiry is the source's practice of testing marriage readiness through observed daily conduct and explicit discussion of children, work, money, family obligations, living arrangements, responsibility, and life direction rather than through affection or formal eligibility alone.

## Current Synthesis
The episode treats readiness as evidence gathering under irreducible uncertainty. Cohabitation, where acceptable and safe, can reveal habits and patterns hidden by episodic dating, while discussion can expose incompatible assumptions about childbearing, careers, relocation, parents, household work, and the meaning of commitment. Similar background may lower some coordination costs, but genuine affection, character, and the capacity to build a new household matter more than a demographic checklist.

Inquiry cannot simulate every future crisis or secure a guarantee against illness, abandonment, infidelity, or personal change. Its value is narrower: replace avoidable ambiguity with present evidence, surface consequential differences before legal and practical entanglement deepens, and distinguish ordinary fear of uncertainty from a known conflict that the couple is declining to address.

## Key Claims
- Marriage readiness depends on demonstrated conduct and shared-life fit, not only attraction or socially approved conditions.
- Cohabitation can reveal daily habits and conflict patterns that intermittent dating may conceal.
- Children, work, money, location, care for parents, and household responsibility should be discussed before marriage.
- Similar family background can reduce some friction but is neither necessary nor sufficient for compatibility.
- No inquiry can guarantee how a partner will behave in every future crisis.
- The goal is informed commitment under uncertainty, not perfect prediction.

## Evidence
- Daily-life observation: [[ep132-lingzheng-santianqian-wo-konghunle-cmcoosadbrdubrkxrgbjepiy]] presents premarital cohabitation as one way to expose ordinary habits and interaction patterns.
- Consequential questions: [[ep132-lingzheng-santianqian-wo-konghunle-cmcoosadbrdubrkxrgbjepiy]] asks partners to discuss childbearing, family background, responsibility, and the ability to form a new household.
- Uncertainty boundary: [[ep132-lingzheng-santianqian-wo-konghunle-cmcoosadbrdubrkxrgbjepiy]] rejects hypothetical questions as guarantees about future illness or crisis and returns judgment to present character and shared experience.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This is a conversational framework from several urban participants, not a validated readiness instrument or evidence that cohabitation causes marital success. Cohabitation may be unavailable, unsafe, culturally unacceptable, legally consequential, or financially impractical. Direct discussion can still be distorted by self-presentation, unequal power, coercion, or changed circumstances, and background similarity can coexist with serious incompatibility or harm.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to separate useful premarital evidence gathering from impossible demands for certainty.
