# Distant-State Appeasement Trap / 远国安抚误读

Distant-state appeasement trap / 远国安抚误读 is the Qin-Qi pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-3-lishishang-di-yi-wei-zhenzheng-yiyishang-chuilian-tingzheng-taihou-liq9d3itmbar00ij43zzed-mulul]] draws from [[JunWangHou|君王后]]'s long peace policy. A distant state can read its quiet border and favored treatment as diplomatic success while the expansionist state is simply postponing that target under [[YuanjiaoJingongStrategy|远交近攻]].

The concept differs from ordinary [[AppeasementLogic|appeasement logic]]. In Qi's case, the policy is not empty: neutrality, trustworthiness toward other lords, and caution toward Qin help preserve [[QiState|齐国]] for more than forty years. The trap is that the same success can hide the sequencing problem. Qin's friendliness toward Qi is not proof that Qin has accepted Qi's independence as durable; it can be a way to keep the distant state quiet until closer states are removed.

It also differs from [[LowPostureStatecraft|low-posture statecraft]]. Low posture can be a temporary tactic. In this source, low posture hardens into a whole late-Qi orientation, making [[QiWangJian|齐王建 / 田建]] vulnerable to reading continued Qin access, [[HouSheng|后胜]]'s advice, and personal travel to Qin as normal diplomacy rather than terminal exposure.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-120-1-shui-shi-huangdi-zhong-de-mabaonan-lhw8vsj6ta6rjdkugcvfohaqqxc0]] gives the trap its endpoint. Once [[WangBenQin|王贲]] can enter [[QiState|齐国]] from the north and reach [[Linzi|临淄]], the long peace no longer means safety; it means Qi has arrived at the end of Qin's conquest sequence. The episode still complicates the judgment by saying the same policy helped create [[CasualtySparingSurrender|保全生灵式投降]], so the trap is strategic rather than a flat denial that the peace had value.

## Key Claims
- A distant state may be safer in the short term precisely because the aggressor has nearer targets.
- Short-term peace can confirm the weaker state's policy in its own eyes even when the stronger state is only sequencing conquest.
- Appeasement is not purely cowardice in this pattern; it can buy real time, prosperity, and social calm.
- The strategic danger arrives when the policy destroys the actor's ability to recognize when the sequencing has reached it.
- Diplomatic dignity gestures, such as the jade linked-rings story, can preserve honor without solving the structural threat.
- The endpoint can be low-casualty surrender rather than annihilating battle, which means the concept must separate state survival from population survival.
