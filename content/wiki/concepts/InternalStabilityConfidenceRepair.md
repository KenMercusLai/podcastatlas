# Internal Stability Confidence Repair

Internal stability confidence repair is the source's cross-system synthesis in [[vol-111-guanyu-2025-nian-de-si-ge-caixiang-lgaira5qw1fhic4qwihrimed6q9g]]. The host's phrase "殊途同归" links two very different policy settings: [[China]] trying to break a confidence and local-fiscal loop through [[ChinaLocalDebtResolution]], and [[DonaldTrump]] trying to consolidate domestic governing control through [[TrumpismInstitutionalization]], [[AdministrativeStateDismantling]], and [[DepartmentOfGovernmentEfficiency]].

The concept does not claim the two systems are morally or institutionally the same. It records the source's narrower argument that both large powers may spend 2025 trying to restore internal order, belief, and operating room before external strategy can become stable.

## Key Claims
- Confidence is treated as a political-economic object, not only a market sentiment index.
- China-side confidence repair runs through local governments, company cash flow, household expectations, demand, and anti-involution policy.
- U.S.-side confidence repair runs through anger at bureaucracy, medical-system distrust, immigration and tariff promises, and presidential control over the administrative state.
- The concept helps connect macro policy to social trust: fiscal repair or bureaucratic reform can be framed as an attempt to stop citizens and firms from assuming the system is working against them.
