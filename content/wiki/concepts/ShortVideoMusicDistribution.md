# Short-Video Music Distribution

Short-video music distribution is the pattern where music discovery, listening intent, and recommendation data flow from short-video platforms into dedicated music products. In [[qishui-yinyue-chongji-tengxun-wangyi-tesila-deng-cheqi-zai-guonei-kaizhan-daguimo-zhaohui-1008396395]], [[QishuiMusic|汽水音乐]] is described as using [[Douyin]] content and traffic advantages to pressure [[TencentMusic|Tencent Music]] and [[NetEaseCloudMusic|NetEase Cloud Music]] in a mature online-music market.

The concept extends [[RecommendationDistributionAdvantage]] into music. A short-video platform can observe which clips, background tracks, challenges, creators, and user interests make songs travel, then route that signal into a listening app with lower onboarding and higher personalization.

## Key Claims
- In a mature online-music market, user entry points can matter as much as catalog history or incumbent memberships.
- Short-video BGM and viral-song behavior create music-demand signals before users deliberately open a music app.
- The source-scoped 139 million monthly-active-user claim for Qishui is best treated as a distribution-pressure signal, not as settled proof of durable monetization.
- The pattern does not eliminate [[DigitalMusicLicensing]] or paid-membership economics; it changes where discovery and habit formation begin.
