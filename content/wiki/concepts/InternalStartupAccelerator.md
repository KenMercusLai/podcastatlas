# Internal Startup Accelerator / 企业内部加速器

## Definition
An internal startup accelerator is a temporary, goal-bounded operating mode in which selected employees co-locate, receive shared life support, and use a fixed review cadence to solve one company problem.

## Current Synthesis
[[ACEStudio|ACE Studio]] adapts the external [[HF0]] residency for a remote company. Rather than making co-location permanent, it selects a project, gathers the relevant team for about three months, uses a retreat and recurring demos or weekly updates, and removes some food and living friction. Employees rotate through the mode rather than participating continuously.

The concept therefore differs from a general return-to-office rule. Its rationale is temporary coordination and environmental immersion around an unusually clear cross-functional problem, followed by return to the ordinary remote system.

## Key Claims
- Temporary co-location can be designed as a project mode rather than a permanent office policy.
- A bounded problem, explicit target, and recurring review cadence are more central than the location itself.
- Daily-life support can reduce coordination and attention switching during an intensive period.
- Rotation can limit continuous exposure to residential intensity while preserving access to concentrated collaboration.
- The mechanism is most plausible for clear, interdependent work and less obviously suited to routine or individually paced work.

## Evidence
- Operating design - [[e258-liaoliao-guigu-chuangye-xiudaoyuan-hf0-yu-openrouter-zaoqi-gushi-f6618818-b288-4798-bab8-d60d77c70aff]] describes retreats, Demo Dinners, weekly updates, living support, and roughly three-month projects.
- Remote-company boundary - [[e258-liaoliao-guigu-chuangye-xiudaoyuan-hf0-yu-openrouter-zaoqi-gushi-f6618818-b288-4798-bab8-d60d77c70aff]] says ACE Studio closed its Beijing office and kept a remote operating system outside accelerator periods.
- Project range - [[e258-liaoliao-guigu-chuangye-xiudaoyuan-hf0-yu-openrouter-zaoqi-gushi-f6618818-b288-4798-bab8-d60d77c70aff]] reports algorithm, development, and growth accelerators in several locations.
- Output attribution - [[e258-liaoliao-guigu-chuangye-xiudaoyuan-hf0-yu-openrouter-zaoqi-gushi-f6618818-b288-4798-bab8-d60d77c70aff]] links one accelerator to a first internal foundation model and another to ACE Studio 2.0 integration.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not compare accelerator periods with matched remote projects, so the claimed productivity and inspiration effects are retrospective. Residential intensity can strain privacy, family life, health, inclusion, and psychological safety. Clear exit conditions, voluntary participation, reasonable hours, and accommodation needs remain essential even though the source does not specify them.

## What Changed
- Created the concept from ACE Studio's repeated adaptation of HF0 inside a remote company.
- Distinguished temporary project co-location from a universal return-to-office mandate.
