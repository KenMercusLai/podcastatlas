# China Research Visibility at ASCO

## Definition
China research visibility at ASCO is the episode's use of growing Chinese oral and late-breaking oncology presentations as evidence that Chinese researchers and drug developers have become harder for the global field to ignore.

## Current Synthesis
[[vol-172-tanboshi-dabainiu-xiaolong-guochan-chuangxinyao-daodi-shi-zhenqushi-haishi-xuhuo-asco-dahui-yishou-xianchang-zhiji-lmypzc0k-w1lxfkn5q25rystwuev]] treats conference visibility as a trend signal, not a championship table. More presentations can reflect better assets, trials, investigators, recruitment, and reporting, but presentation share alone does not establish global leadership, durable patient benefit, regulatory success, or commercial reach.

## Key Claims
- Chinese oncology research visibility at ASCO has increased across several years in the guests' account.
- Late-breaking and oral presentations signal that international reviewers consider some Chinese work timely and consequential.
- Visibility depends on clinical investigators, patient recruitment, trial execution, data quality, and competitive therapies working together.
- Conference presence is weaker evidence than replicated outcomes, approval, adoption, survival benefit, and real-world access.

## Evidence
- Visibility trend - [[vol-172-tanboshi-dabainiu-xiaolong-guochan-chuangxinyao-daodi-shi-zhenqushi-haishi-xuhuo-asco-dahui-yishou-xianchang-zhiji-lmypzc0k-w1lxfkn5q25rystwuev]] reports a rising Chinese share of ASCO oral presentations from 2022 through 2025.
- Research examples - [[vol-172-tanboshi-dabainiu-xiaolong-guochan-chuangxinyao-daodi-shi-zhenqushi-haishi-xuhuo-asco-dahui-yishou-xianchang-zhiji-lmypzc0k-w1lxfkn5q25rystwuev]] points to multiple late-breaking TKI studies and a positive solid-tumor cell-therapy result as examples of higher-profile Chinese work.
- Capability base - [[vol-172-tanboshi-dabainiu-xiaolong-guochan-chuangxinyao-daodi-shi-zhenqushi-haishi-xuhuo-asco-dahui-yishou-xianchang-zhiji-lmypzc0k-w1lxfkn5q25rystwuev]] connects visibility with physicians, patients, recruitment speed, regulation, and company execution rather than with conference marketing alone.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The supplied summary does not identify the presentation dataset, denominator, molecule, company, trial, or indication behind several numerical and clinical examples. Conference acceptance is selective evidence and cannot by itself establish comparative national leadership or patient benefit.

## What Changed
- Established a qualified distinction between rising conference visibility and demonstrated global oncology leadership.
