# Revolutionary Virtue Politics / 革命美德政治

## Definition

Revolutionary virtue politics is a moralized political language in which sincerity, patriotic sacrifice, public feeling, and fidelity to the people's will become tests of legitimacy, making ordinary interests or disagreement vulnerable to recoding as corruption and betrayal.

## Current Synthesis

The sources place this politics before and during the radical [[FrenchRevolution|French Revolution]]. Before 1789, sensibility, authenticity, patriotism, sacrifice, general will, and traitor language served incompatible programs and did not yet constitute terror. The radical milieu around [[MaximilienRobespierre|Robespierre]], [[JeanPaulMarat|Marat]], and the [[JacobinClub|Jacobins]] sharpened the division between virtuous people and concealed enemies. Robespierre's incorruptibility became transferable political authority: leaders claiming access to the people's true will could suspend constitutional rule and present terror as virtue armed for defence. By 1794, terror had become severe justice for building virtue, while the [[CultOfSupremeBeing|Cult of the Supreme Being]] attempted to move enforcement inside citizens through belief in an all-seeing moral guarantor. This intended exit from coercion remained unstable because dissent could still signify vice, law could punish vague hostility, and Robespierre's ceremonial centrality made shared morality look like personal rule.

## Key Claims

- Virtue becomes politically dangerous when one faction claims direct access to the people's moral will.
- Sensibility and authenticity can make visible feeling seem like evidence of political truth.
- Patriotic sacrifice language can turn compromise or plural interest into suspected selfishness.
- Suspicion of hidden interests can make normal disagreement appear conspiratorial or treasonous.
- Virtue language does not automatically produce terror, but it can authorize coercion as both emergency defence and permanent moral construction.
- Personal integrity can legitimate institutional coercion when followers infer that virtuous leaders make emergency power trustworthy.
- Attempts to internalize virtue through sacred observation can reduce dependence on force in theory while deepening conformity and leader-centered suspicion in practice.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

- The same vocabulary could support elite privilege defence, constitutional reform, equal citizenship, or radical coercion; shared words do not prove identical programs.
- Virtue, patriotism, or public emotion do not inherently cause terror, and institutional breakdown, war, scarcity, faction, and actual betrayal also mattered.
- The sources provide interpretive narrative rather than a complete history of Rousseau's influence or all revolutionary moral traditions.
- Claims that Vizille's language already contained a totalitarian logic and that particular actors were sincere or cynical remain source-scoped.
- Robespierre's personal sincerity and integrity do not validate his diagnosis, while psychological and Rousseauian causal claims remain interpretive.
- The democratic and egalitarian content of the Constitution of 1793 was real at the level of text and promise even though emergency rule prevented ordinary implementation.
- Opposition to de-Christianization shows that moralized revolutionary politics contained competing accounts of virtue rather than one undifferentiated radical program.
- Sincerity can explain the internal logic of coercion without validating its factual diagnoses or legal outcomes.
- Belief in a Supreme Being was one proposed moral foundation, not proof that atheists lacked civic ethics or that religious observation could reliably replace legal constraint.
- The festival's reception and Robespierre's hoped-for transition away from terror remain interpretive and source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Added the Supreme Being as an attempt to internalize virtue and supply a non-coercive endpoint to emergency government.
- Added the paradox that sacred moral surveillance and leader-centered ritual could intensify conformity and political fear.
- Connected virtue judgment to the Law of 22 Prairial's vague enemy categories and compressed defence.
