# Animal Spirits Opportunity Activation / 动物精神与机会激活

Animal spirits opportunity activation / 动物精神与机会激活 is [[jifa-dongwu-jingshen-chuangzao-gengduo-jihui-lndyfdmtgo-9l5cr8oy82t1xnlfe]]'s interpretation of macro confidence. [[ZhouLuohua|周洛华]] treats animal spirits as the willingness to consume, invest, build, and take risk after people see believable future opportunity.

The source uses [[JohnMaynardKeynes|Keynes]] to argue that government spending should restart the economic engine, not replace it forever. It also uses China's reform-era memory, especially the feeling of new possibility after Deng Xiaoping's southern tour, as an example where material conditions did not change instantly but opportunity perception did.

## Key Claims
- Animal spirits are activated by believable opportunity, not only by cheaper money.
- Demand repair is incomplete if households and entrepreneurs still see no future path.
- Government spending can be useful as ignition, but the source rejects permanent debt-financed operation as the end goal.
- The source connects deflation, debt pressure, youth caution, and low entrepreneurial investment to opportunity disappearance.
- Creating more opportunity is presented as a more important long-run answer than merely numbing the pain of stagnation.
