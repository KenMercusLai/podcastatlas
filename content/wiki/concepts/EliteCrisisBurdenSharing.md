## Definition

Elite crisis burden sharing / 贵族危机共担动员 is the conversion of protected elite wealth, household capacity, personal service, or inherited privilege into visible support during a state emergency. It concerns whether elites materially share the danger for which rulers ask ordinary people and soldiers to sacrifice.

## Current Synthesis

The bounded evidence now contains a successful voluntary case and a failed-example-to-sanction case. During the [[Handan|邯郸]] siege, [[LiTan|李谈 / 李同]] tells [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] that household privilege is strategically irrational to preserve if [[ZhaoState|赵国]] falls. Pingyuanjun distributes wealth, turns metal into weapons, sends household members into the army, and helps recruit three thousand dare-death fighters. Visible contribution improves the credibility of collective defense.

Hanji 323-2 and 353-2 supply overlapping versions of the Western Han contrast. [[BuShiWesternHan|卜式]] offers himself and his son for the [[Nanyue|南越]] war and is publicly rewarded; Hanji 353-2 additionally names experienced Qi sailors. No broad official or marquis response follows. The subsequent [[ZhoujinForfeiture|酎金夺爵]] removes 106 titles for deficient ritual gold in Hanji 353-2, while Hanji 323-2 gives the looser count of more than one hundred. The sequence supports movement from exemplary voluntary mobilization to coercive elite discipline, although the claim that nonresponse caused the forfeitures remains interpretive.

## Key Claims

- In an existential or expansionary campaign, visible elite exemption can undermine the credibility of demands placed on commoners and soldiers.
- Burden sharing becomes politically meaningful when it changes material arrangements or personal exposure, not only when elites offer supportive words.
- Elite contribution can be induced positively through example and reward, as in Bu Shi's public commendation.
- A positive example may fail to mobilize peers, leaving the ruler to rely on legal or ritual sanctions instead.
- Pingyuanjun's case shows that belated voluntary conversion of privilege can still improve recruitment and morale.
- The Nanyue case broadens the mechanism from city defense to imperial military mobilization but does not prove that every later punishment was caused by nonparticipation.

## Evidence

Material and household conversion:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-4-lqs3pj2-rebp0uh6q7ayecpju3hk|Zhouji 96 part 4]] says Pingyuanjun distributes household wealth, converts metal to weapons, and sends household members into the defense after Li Tan's rebuke.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-4-lqs3pj2-rebp0uh6q7ayecpju3hk|Zhouji 96 part 4]] connects the action to the recruitment of three thousand dare-death fighters.

Exemplary service and failed diffusion:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-323-2-fangyangren-chushen-de-ta-ruhe-caogen-nixi-lq4cwhskuiffrrw84lop3t6qrzzk|Hanji 323-2]] independently says Bu Shi offers himself and his son, receives public reward, and fails to inspire broader official participation before more than one hundred marquises lose title.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-353-2-hanwudi-zhoujin-duojue-beihou-de-yinmou-llzksgz2rjcpyjtp1fxjdtmh8q3p|Hanji 353-2]] says Bu Shi volunteers himself, his son, and Qi sailors and receives a public reward.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-353-2-hanwudi-zhoujin-duojue-beihou-de-yinmou-llzksgz2rjcpyjtp1fxjdtmh8q3p|Hanji 353-2]] says other officials and marquises do not respond before 106 title forfeitures.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The cases differ sharply. Handan is under siege, while Nanyue is an imperial expedition; Pingyuanjun's household contribution is narrated as a direct morale intervention, whereas the link between nonvolunteering and ritual-gold punishment is the host's inference. Hanji 323-2 and 353-2 are overlapping podcast treatments rather than fully independent documentary traditions, and the first calls the contribution “税金” while the more specific existing account identifies 酎金. The concept does not imply that hereditary elites must personally fight in every campaign, that all protected resources are available for emergency conversion, or that coercive title removal is normatively equivalent to voluntary sacrifice.
