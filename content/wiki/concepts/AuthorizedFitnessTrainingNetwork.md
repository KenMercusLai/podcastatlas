# Authorized Fitness Training Network

Authorized fitness training network is the business layer where gyms, coaches, courses, community events, and participant access are tied to an official race brand. [[shangye-xiaoyang-47-jianshen-zuotijia-de-xinhuan-hyrox-daodi-you-shenme-huchenghe-1008179335]] adds the concept through [[HYROX]], whose authorized gyms can receive official programming, community-event support, and priority ticket access.

The source says the gym-authorization business accounts for about 10% of HYROX revenue and cites a $1,500 authorization fee per gym. Its strategic value may be larger than the fee itself: priority race access and official training status turn [[TrademarkBasedEventProtection]] into a practical choice for gyms, while local classes and communities keep participants preparing between events.

## Key Claims
- Authorization gives gyms a reason to affiliate with the official event instead of running an unlicensed copy of the same workout.
- Priority event access can be more compelling than training content when race slots are scarce.
- The network connects local training frequency to global event scarcity, creating year-round demand around periodic competitions.
- Licensing works best when paired with standardized courses, participant data, and a recognized brand.
