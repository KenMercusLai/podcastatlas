# Daoist Ritual Technology / 道教仪式技术

Daoist ritual technology / 道教仪式技术 names the episode's cluster of pacing the Dipper, talismans, spells, swords, mirrors, invisibility arts, command phrases, and apotropaic objects. In [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-jie-shangji-704051550]], these are techniques that make cosmic order actionable through body movement, written signs, implements, and formulaic language.

The source treats ritual technology as layered. Some elements come from older fangshi and folk practices, some from bureaucratic command language such as "急急如律令", and some later spell culture is affected by Buddhist mantra transmission.

## Key Claims
- Ritual power can be expressed through movement, writing, objects, and administrative formulae.
- Pacing the Dipper translates celestial order into embodied sequence.
- Talismans and spells become stronger as they absorb script, secrecy, sound, and bureaucratic command.
- The source keeps specific magical claims as historical-cultural material rather than verified efficacy.
