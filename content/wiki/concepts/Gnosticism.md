# Gnosticism / 诺斯替主义

[[Gnosticism|Gnosticism / 诺斯替主义]] enters the wiki through [[20-demian-gaiyin-de-etou-shang-na-shenmi-de-yinji-567302734]] as one of the religious backgrounds for [[HermannHesse|Hesse]]'s [[DemianNovel|《德米安》]]. The episode explains it less as an institutional church history and more as a way of seeking special, experiential "true knowledge" rather than relying only on received doctrine.

In the source, Gnosticism matters because it makes [[Abraxas|Abraxas / 阿布拉克萨斯]] legible: a symbol that is not simply good or evil, but difficult, whole, and resistant to ordinary moral simplification. The hosts connect this to Hesse's interest in Eastern philosophy and to the novel's pressure against inherited religious categories.

## Key Claims
- Gnosticism is stored here as a source-scoped literary and religious influence, not as a full theology page.
- Its role in the episode is to make inner knowledge, hidden wholeness, and anti-orthodox symbolism intelligible.
- The concept links [[CainMarkReinterpretation]] and [[AbraxasWholeGodSymbolism]] into the same problem: received stories may need re-reading before they become personally true.
