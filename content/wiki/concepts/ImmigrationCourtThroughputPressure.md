# Immigration Court Throughput Pressure

## Definition
Immigration court throughput pressure is the procedural condition where high case volume, accelerated hearings, weak notice, and lack of lawyers make removal orders easier to generate than individualized legal defense.

## Current Synthesis
The episode adds this concept through [[AaronBraun]]'s Los Angeles court observations. The immigration courts are described as Department of Justice courts rather than independent courts, and "mega master hearings" with dozens of cases in a short window become part of how deportation policy scales after arrest.

## Key Claims
- Court volume can become an enforcement tool when the state increases arrests faster than adjudicative capacity or legal representation.
- Mega master hearings compress time for understanding rights, finding counsel, and presenting facts.
- Changing hearing times and weak notice can convert confusion into in absentia removal orders.
- Throughput pressure links field enforcement to procedural outcomes, making removal totals rise without every case receiving meaningful contestation.
- The source treats the courts as structurally dependent on the executive branch, not as independent courts.

## Evidence
- Mega hearings - [[ire-and-ice-the-toll-of-americas-deportations-6a993426809ae7c2f4293df9]] reports Braun observing 50 to 100 cases in a three-hour setting and a judge trying to process 96 cases in one morning.
- Notice and lawyers - [[ire-and-ice-the-toll-of-americas-deportations-6a993426809ae7c2f4293df9]] says hearing changes, weak notice, absent lawyers, and nonappearance can produce in absentia removal orders.
- Enforcement pipeline - [[ire-and-ice-the-toll-of-americas-deportations-6a993426809ae7c2f4293df9]] links court throughput to the administration's effort to increase removal orders.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is a reported observation of particular proceedings, not a full statistical analysis of every immigration court. The page should preserve the distinction between procedural-risk evidence and a settled claim about every judge or hearing.

## What Changed
- Created a concept for the procedural court-throughput branch of the deportation campaign.
