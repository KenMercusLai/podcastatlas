# Irreversible Defection Binding / 断路式策反绑定

Irreversible defection binding / 断路式策反绑定 is the diplomatic mechanism [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-158-koucai-hao-de-tianhuaban-yiren-ding-baiwan-dajun-lnup-vmw-ff-nqmuh6-x8si0k8zu|Hanji 158]] shows through [[SuiHe|随何]]'s mission to [[YingBu|英布]]. The persuader does not stop after winning a private promise. He creates a public act that makes returning to the old camp politically or militarily impossible.

The source case has two stages. First, Sui He uses [[ChuaimoTechnique|揣摩]] and [[InterestReframingPersuasion|利益重构式说服]] to get Ying Bu to admit that his apparent loyalty to [[XiangYu|项羽]] is hollow and that siding with [[LiuBang|刘邦]] is safer over time. Ying Bu privately agrees, but tells Sui He to keep the matter secret because he still fears Chu.

Second, when Xiang Yu's envoy arrives to demand troops, Sui He publicly sits above the envoy and says Jiujiang has already submitted to Han. That announcement converts Ying Bu's hidden option into open evidence. Sui He then urges Ying Bu to kill the envoy, crossing the [[EnvoyKillingTaboo|不斩来使]] boundary so Xiang Yu will treat Ying Bu as an enemy rather than as a wavering subordinate.

The concept is therefore harsher than ordinary persuasion. It combines diagnosis, argument, exposure, and exit closure. Its success is measured not by whether the target feels convinced in the moment, but by whether the target is forced to act consistently with the new camp after the persuader leaves.

## Key Claims
- A private defection promise remains fragile when the target still fears the old patron's retaliation.
- Public exposure can turn ambiguous intent into a political fact that other actors must respond to.
- The commitment device may be morally costly: in this source, killing the Chu envoy is useful precisely because it destroys Ying Bu's route back to Xiang Yu.
- The tactic depends on timing. Sui He waits until Ying Bu has privately agreed, then exploits the Chu envoy's sudden arrival before Ying Bu can retreat into ambiguity.
- Irreversibility can create strategic value beyond the local court by forcing the enemy to divide troops, attention, and trust.
