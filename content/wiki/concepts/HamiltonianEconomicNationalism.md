# Hamiltonian Economic Nationalism

[[179-xian-jiu-riyuan-zai-jiu-changzhai-jiuhuoduizhang-beisente-zai-zou-yi-tiao-zenyang-de-gangsi-lqjc6vz-1ungnkpdw9d5kbsa-ouo]] uses a Hamiltonian label to describe [[ScottBessent|Scott Bessent / 贝森特]]'s claimed mix of production security, fiscal capacity, finance, and trade policy. The source says this framing wraps tax cuts, deregulation, and energy expansion in a state-capacity argument about rebuilding U.S. manufacturing and negotiating the external order.

## Key Claims
- The frame is more state-directed than a simple free-market slogan, even when it uses Republican tax and deregulation tools.
- It links production capacity, debt capacity, dollar dominance, and trade policy into one national-power package.
- The source treats it as the ideological background for [[BessentImpossibleTriangle]]: remaking globalization while relying on dollar and Treasury privileges from the old order.
