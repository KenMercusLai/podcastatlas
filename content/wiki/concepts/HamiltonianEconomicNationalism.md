# Hamiltonian Economic Nationalism

## Definition

Hamiltonian economic nationalism is an economic-policy frame that links central state capacity, public finance, banking, manufacturing, trade posture, and national power.

## Current Synthesis

The Hamilton episode supplies the historical core: [[AlexanderHamilton|Alexander Hamilton]] is presented as wanting a strong central government, national bank, manufacturing support, and closer relations with Britain against Jeffersonian agrarian suspicion. The Bessent episode supplies the modern reuse: [[ScottBessent|Scott Bessent / 贝森特]] is read as wrapping tax cuts, deregulation, energy expansion, trade bargaining, and Treasury-market management inside a production-security and dollar-finance argument. The current concept therefore tracks a reusable policy grammar rather than a single program: economic sovereignty is pursued through finance and state capacity as much as through factories.

## Key Claims

- Hamiltonian economic nationalism is more state-capacity oriented than a simple free-market slogan.
- The historical Hamilton version links central authority, banking, manufacturing, and foreign alignment.
- The modern Bessent use links production security, debt capacity, dollar dominance, trade policy, and Treasury-market absorption.
- The frame can combine market-facing tools with state-directed goals, creating ambiguity between liberalization and intervention.
- The Bessent source treats the frame as background for [[BessentImpossibleTriangle]]: remaking trade order while relying on dollar and Treasury privileges from the old order.

## Evidence

- Historical program: [[685-hamilton-duel-to-the-death-part-3-glt9231431046]] says Hamilton wanted strong central government, a national bank, support for manufacturing, and closer relations with Britain.
- Political contrast: [[685-hamilton-duel-to-the-death-part-3-glt9231431046]] contrasts Hamilton's program with Jeffersonian suspicion of centralization, finance, and British-style politics.
- Modern label: [[179-xian-jiu-riyuan-zai-jiu-changzhai-jiuhuoduizhang-beisente-zai-zou-yi-tiao-zenyang-de-gangsi-lqjc6vz-1ungnkpdw9d5kbsa-ouo]] uses a Hamiltonian label for Bessent's claimed mix of production security, fiscal capacity, finance, and trade policy.
- Macro contradiction: [[179-xian-jiu-riyuan-zai-jiu-changzhai-jiuhuoduizhang-beisente-zai-zou-yi-tiao-zenyang-de-gangsi-lqjc6vz-1ungnkpdw9d5kbsa-ouo]] treats the frame as ideological background for a conflict among trade-deficit reduction, dollar dominance, and foreign Treasury demand.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The sources do not establish that Bessent's modern program is a faithful continuation of Hamilton's full historical thought. The concept is a wiki synthesis label for recurring state-capacity, finance, and production-security arguments, with the Bessent application remaining source-scoped and contingent.
