# Benign Gynecological Finding Triage / 妇科良性发现分诊

## Definition
Benign gynecological finding triage is a framework for interpreting common cysts, endometrial measurements, pelvic fluid, polyps, fibroids, and cervical appearances through timing, persistence, change, location, symptoms, fertility context, and concerning features before choosing observation or intervention.

## Current Synthesis
An alarming report word is not a treatment indication by itself. Ovarian structures can reflect normal follicular or luteal physiology, endometrial thickness changes across the cycle, small-volume pelvic fluid can follow ovulation or ordinary peritoneal lubrication, and cervical cysts or an ectropion-like appearance may be benign. Repeat assessment at an appropriate cycle time can therefore be more informative than reacting to a single snapshot.

Reassurance is conditional rather than absolute. Rapid growth, persistence outside the expected cycle phase, sudden or progressive pain, abnormal bleeding, infertility, pressure symptoms, or suspicious imaging features change the decision. The framework also prevents the opposite error: dismissing worsening symptoms merely because the broad category is usually benign.

## Key Claims
- A report label does not establish severity, cause, or need for treatment.
- Menstrual phase and repeat observation can distinguish transient physiology from persistent abnormality.
- Growth, persistence, location, symptoms, fertility relevance, and suspicious features matter more than one isolated measurement.
- Sudden severe pain or rapidly changing findings require escalation rather than routine reassurance.
- Observation is an active plan only when timing, follow-up, and warning signs are clear.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is a broad podcast summary, not a validated rule or current clinical guideline. Imaging appearance, pregnancy possibility, age, examination, laboratory results, symptom severity, personal and family history, and local guidance can change interpretation. A benign category can still cause serious symptoms, and a reassuring population-level frequency does not diagnose an individual finding.

## What Changed
- Established a cross-condition framework for interpreting common gynecological report findings without either automatic panic or automatic dismissal.
