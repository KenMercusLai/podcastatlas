# Family-Enterprise Labor Boundary / 家庭经营中的亲属劳动边界

## Definition
A family-enterprise labor boundary distinguishes reciprocal household help from employment by making role, hours, pay, rest, health, refusal, and authority explicit when relatives work in the same business.

## Current Synthesis
Kinship can supply trust, flexibility, patient capital, and rapid help, especially in a debt-financed migrant business. Those strengths also make labor easy to hide. When owners describe all available family time as a shared obligation, the worker may be held to employee output without employee wages, hours, sick leave, or exit rights.

Xiaorui's case shows the boundary problem rather than proving that all family help is exploitation. She worked for Yingjie for pay while still being expected to return to her parents' shop, sometimes for days extending beyond twelve hours. A defensible arrangement asks whether help is occasional and reciprocal or scheduled and revenue-producing, and whether the relative can know the terms, keep earnings, report illness, rest, and refuse without family retaliation.

## Key Claims
- Family trust and shared capital can reduce transaction costs while obscuring employment obligations.
- Regular revenue-producing work should not become unpaid merely because worker and owner are relatives.
- Debt pressure explains the demand for family labor but does not settle its fairness.
- Working for another employer does not make a relative continuously available to the family shop.
- Health complaints require credible response rather than moral interpretation as laziness.
- Clear hours, pay, duties, rest, and refusal rights protect both the relationship and the enterprise.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not provide contracts, payroll, ownership shares, complete schedules, or the parents' accounting. Family enterprises vary: relatives may share profits or voluntarily exchange short-term help. The concept therefore evaluates boundary conditions rather than classifying every informal contribution as wage theft.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to separate family reciprocity from recurring employment in a debt-financed shop.
