# Augmented Reality / 增强现实

## Definition
Augmented reality is an interface mode that overlays digital information onto the physical world rather than replacing the user's surroundings with a fully virtual environment.

## Current Synthesis
The RayNeo source uses AR to distinguish ordinary-glasses-like smart glasses from VR headsets. [[LiHongwei|李鸿伟]] frames AI glasses as devices that primarily face the real world and add recognition, translation, background information, navigation, prompts, and other digital layers when useful.

In this source, AR is valuable when it lowers real-world interaction friction. The strongest examples are street-sign translation, museum or city guidance, person or object context, and light visual feedback that appears while the user keeps attention on the surrounding world.

## Key Claims
- AR adds information to the user's physical surroundings rather than replacing them.
- AI glasses make AR more useful when overlays are grounded in what the wearer is currently seeing.
- Early AR use cases may be modest text, translation, prompts, and guide information rather than immersive spectacle.
- AR product fit depends on optical-display comfort and social tolerance as much as software capability.
- The current source treats AR as part of AI-glasses product design, not as a complete history of the field.

## Evidence
- Definition-by-contrast - [[no-221-leiniao-ceo-xinjishu-yuelaiyueduo-women-weishenme-hai-xuyao-yifu-zhineng-yanjing-gkwridon6uwmaoosszqset9vd]] contrasts VR-style headsets with glasses that augment reality through AI and AR information.
- Translation and guide cases - [[no-221-leiniao-ceo-xinjishu-yuelaiyueduo-women-weishenme-hai-xuyao-yifu-zhineng-yanjing-gkwridon6uwmaoosszqset9vd]] names street-sign translation, AI guide, museum explanation, and surrounding-context understanding as AR-adjacent use cases.
- Display constraint - [[no-221-leiniao-ceo-xinjishu-yuelaiyueduo-women-weishenme-hai-xuyao-yifu-zhineng-yanjing-gkwridon6uwmaoosszqset9vd]] ties AR adoption to transparent, low-friction optical display.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not survey industrial AR, enterprise AR, gaming AR, mobile AR, or headset history. It is a consumer smart-glasses account.
