# High-Salary Exit Scam Trap / 高薪出境骗局

## Definition
A high-salary exit scam trap is a recruitment scam in which unusually attractive work, income, or opportunity claims persuade someone to cross a border or leave ordinary protections before coercion becomes visible.

## Current Synthesis
The episode uses northern Myanmar to turn an anti-fraud warning into a movement-risk warning. A suspicious job offer is not only a bad contract or fake platform; once it involves travel to a weakly governed border area, the victim's ability to refuse, verify, or leave can collapse. The trap works because the offer arrives wrapped in desire: high pay, better prospects, a friend's or relative's invitation, small favors, money, or sexual temptation.

The concept is therefore related to [[SocialEngineeringFraud]], but its center is not persuasion alone. The decisive threshold is exit: the victim moves out of a familiar jurisdiction or support network and becomes reachable by intermediaries, guards, or compound operators. The source's practical advice is blunt but useful: slow down and independently verify before travel, especially when the reward is disproportionate and the destination is opaque.

## Key Claims
- High pay can function as bait when the offer requires rapid travel to an unfamiliar or weakly governed destination.
- Recruitment by acquaintances can feel safer while still serving a scam pipeline.
- Small benefits and emotional or sexual inducements can make victims discount the travel risk.
- The danger escalates sharply after crossing a border because ordinary exit options, family help, and law-enforcement access may weaken.
- The trap is a labor, fraud, and personal-safety problem at the same time.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not provide a full typology of labor trafficking, recruitment networks, or rescue procedures. The concept should be used as a warning pattern, not as a claim that every overseas job offer or Myanmar-related opportunity is fraudulent.
