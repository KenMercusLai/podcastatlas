# OPHIS Research Workflow

OPHIS is [[LiuZiming|Liu Ziming]]'s proposed structure for making research legible enough to train AI systems on it. In [[149-qinli-zhongmei-new-labs-ziben-kuangchao-he-qinghua-liuziming-liao-ai-for-ai-jizhi-kejieshixing-he-max-tegmark-lm33q4n6w8tzcd2fxdbuk9unc2xv]], the acronym stands for Observation, Problem, Hypothesis, Intervention, and Speed up.

The source's key move is to treat research as a language. If papers and lab work can be marked up as observations, problems, hypotheses, interventions, and acceleration moves, then [[AutoResearch]] can train on more than final papers. The structure is also meant to capture the missing reasoning between failed experiments, intuitive leaps, and next actions.

## Key Claims
- Research papers hide much of the thinking that produced them, especially false starts and local judgment.
- OPHIS creates a schema for turning research work into training data.
- A structured research language may let models learn when to propose hypotheses, run interventions, or speed up an experiment.
- The workflow is a bridge between human [[ResearchTaste]] and automated research systems.
- Liu treats OPHIS as an early requirement for [[AIForAI]], not a finished research autopilot.
