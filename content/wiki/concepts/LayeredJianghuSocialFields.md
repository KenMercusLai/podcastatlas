# Layered Jianghu Social Fields

## Definition
Layered Jianghu social fields is the source's map of Jianghu as a social condition that appears differently in the court, among literati, in commerce, and among marginal people trying to survive.

## Current Synthesis
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-483-2-lishi-jiemi-hun-zhichang-de-jianghu-guiju-ltukp3q2cg7p5egxqte7nuseenaw]] argues that Jianghu is not limited to martial romance. Wherever people form grudges, obligations, livelihoods, ambitions, and dependency, there is a version of Jianghu. The episode then sorts that field by social position: court Jianghu is power entanglement, literati Jianghu is attachment and release, merchant Jianghu is timing and exit, and street Jianghu is hunger-shaped survival.

The concept is useful because it prevents Jianghu from becoming a single moral label. The same word can name elite power struggle, spiritual withdrawal, commercial prudence, or marginal predation, but each layer has different evidence and different survival skills.

## Key Claims
- Jianghu is a layered social field, not only a martial or criminal image.
- Court Jianghu centers on power proximity, old status attachment, and experienced insiders.
- Literati Jianghu centers on loosening attachment, especially through [[Zhuangzi|庄子]]'s "相忘于江湖" frame.
- Merchant Jianghu centers on timing, profit, identity change, and exit, with [[FanLi|范蠡]] as the source's ideal example.
- Street Jianghu centers on livelihood instability, hunger, skill, fraud, and possible violence.
- The workplace lesson is less a checklist than a situational craft: read people clearly, speak adaptively, and know when attachment or continued presence becomes dangerous.

## Evidence
### Court And Power
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-483-2-lishi-jiemi-hun-zhichang-de-jianghu-guiju-ltukp3q2cg7p5egxqte7nuseenaw]] treats the court as a Jianghu of experienced insiders, danger, and lingering attachment after leaving the power center.

### Literati And Release
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-483-2-lishi-jiemi-hun-zhichang-de-jianghu-guiju-ltukp3q2cg7p5egxqte7nuseenaw]] uses [[Zhuangzi|庄子]] to frame Jianghu as a place where clinging to old mutual dependence can be inferior to releasing attachment and returning to a broader world.

### Commerce And Exit
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-483-2-lishi-jiemi-hun-zhichang-de-jianghu-guiju-ltukp3q2cg7p5egxqte7nuseenaw]] uses [[FanLi|范蠡]] as the model of entering commerce, accumulating wealth, changing name, and withdrawing after success.

### Marginality And Survival
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-483-2-lishi-jiemi-hun-zhichang-de-jianghu-guiju-ltukp3q2cg7p5egxqte7nuseenaw]] describes marginal people without stable livelihood or social incorporation as vulnerable to hunger, deception, intimidation, and violence.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept comes from one short interpretive episode, so it should not be treated as a full taxonomy of Chinese social history. Its value is comparative and synthetic: it shows how one cultural term stretches across social layers, but each layer still needs separate historical grounding.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to preserve Hanji 483-2's four-layer Jianghu map and its workplace-survival reading.
