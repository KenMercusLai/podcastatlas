# Jianghu Scam Craft

## Definition
Jianghu scam craft is the face-to-face fraud repertoire that turns observation, language, props, collaborators, fear, greed, and social pressure into staged authority or opportunity.

## Current Synthesis
The core repertoire includes fortune-telling, physiognomy, fake medicine, mole removal, railway-station setups, insurance fraud, and staged family-recognition plots [[102-jianghu-congtan-pianshu-heihua-he-minjian-daoyi-736516488]]. The target is not merely lied to; they are placed inside a role as the recipient of secret knowledge, rescuer, bargain-hunter, filial actor, disaster avoider, or person confirming an existing fear.

The same pattern can scale toward [[FangshiFraudAndAuthority|fangshi authority]], where similar techniques gather around rulers, illness, grief, death anxiety, and supernatural certainty [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-wei-wan-daixu-704051997]]. A compact skill formula from Hanji 483-2 says Jianghu survival requires bright eyes and a flexible mouth [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-483-2-lishi-jiemi-hun-zhichang-de-jianghu-guiju-ltukp3q2cg7p5egxqte7nuseenaw]]. Observation and口才 are therefore morally ambivalent tools, useful for legitimate performance and also for seamless lying.

## Key Claims
- Scam craft often begins with observation: clothing, accent, age, emotion, travel route, gender role, and local occupational patterns become clues.
- A "free" first interaction can be a diagnostic tool, not generosity.
- Collaborators create false independence and make staged information look supernatural or accidental.
- Ambiguous pricing and second-step charges let the scam grow after the victim is already committed.
- Pseudoscientific, religious, medical, or folk-theory packaging can make ordinary inference look like authority.
- Jianghu "eyes" and "mouth" mark the skill base beneath both performance and fraud: read the situation, then speak persuasively enough to move the interaction.
- The fangshu source adds that the same observation, staging, and authority mechanics can scale upward when powerful patrons want supernatural certainty.

## Evidence
### Street Fraud Repertoire
- [[102-jianghu-congtan-pianshu-heihua-he-minjian-daoyi-736516488]] details fortune-telling, physiognomy, fake medicine, mole removal, railway-station scams, collaborators, free hooks, and role assignment.

### Authority Scaling
- [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-wei-wan-daixu-704051997]] connects street technique to fangshi promises of certainty, healing, immortality, and supernatural knowledge near political power.

### Observation And Speech
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-483-2-lishi-jiemi-hun-zhichang-de-jianghu-guiju-ltukp3q2cg7p5egxqte7nuseenaw]] identifies "眼亮" and口才 as Jianghu survival skills, explicitly including adaptive speech and the capacity for convincing lies.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Observation and speech are not inherently fraudulent. Street performance, mediation, sales, and ordinary survival can use the same capabilities without predation. The concept becomes "scam craft" only when role assignment, false authority, staged evidence, or coercive escalation is used to extract from the target.
