## Definition

Historical possibility fiction / 历史可能性小说 preserves major documented events and outcomes while inventing actors, motives, mechanisms, relationships, and encounters that could occupy gaps in the record or legendary space around it.

## Current Synthesis

The formula attributed to [[MaBoyong|马伯庸]] is “大事不虚，小事不拘.” In [[QinErshiMustDie|《秦二世必须死》]], principal historical figures do not escape their known endpoints, but fictional action can change how those endpoints are reached or experienced. Suspense therefore depends less on whether Qin Er Shi dies than on who acts, why, at what moral cost, and how intervention can remain invisible inside the known result.

[[BookAndSword|《书剑恩仇录》]] extends the framework from documentary gaps into wuxia legend. Qianlong, Haining, frontier settings, anti-Qing restoration, and inherited stories give the Jianghu a recognizable historical world, while the survival of Qing rule makes the restoration cause unable to succeed at the level it promises. The result is not only reduced surprise: foreknowledge becomes tragic pressure. A reader may not know every local event, but knows that heroism cannot overturn the public outcome.

Possibility becomes historically meaningful when institutions, geography, collective memory, or political limits constrain action rather than merely decorate it. In the Qin case, legitimacy systems, commandery-county rule, offices, and transition shape choices. In the wuxia case, dynasty, ethnic-political cause, regional legend, and the boundary between Jianghu action and imperial history do similar work. The method therefore trades unrestricted counterfactual freedom for credibility, irony, pressure, and inquiry into agency within limits.

## Key Claims

- Major documented outcomes remain binding while gaps and legendary space allow bounded invention.
- Suspense can move from “what happens” to agency, mechanism, motive, relationship, and moral cost.
- Historical institutions, geography, and political limits must constrain action if the setting is to be causally specific.
- Minor or fictional actors can redirect the reader's view without replacing the public record.
- Foreknown failure can create tragic irony and pressure rather than only weaken surprise.
- Historical and local legend can provide narrative credibility without becoming documentary fact.
- Material research alone does not make a plot historically specific if its central action could occur unchanged in another era.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

“Possible” is not the same as documented, probable, or ethically neutral. Detailed institutions, real persons, or local legend can coexist with distorted causation and invented psychology. Reader knowledge changes suspense, so fixed outcomes do not produce one reception effect; a reader may know the dynasty-scale result while remaining uncertain about every character. The concept combines one author's reported label with podcast interpretation of two very different novels and is not a universal definition of historical fiction or proof of either author's intention.

## What Changed

- Extended the method from archival gaps to wuxia built from history and local legend.
- Added foreknown failure as a source of tragic irony rather than only a loss of suspense.
- Distinguished fixed public outcome from continuing uncertainty about local action and personal fate.
