# AI Existential Meaning Anxiety

AI existential meaning anxiety is the source's youth-facing version of AI anxiety in [[151-17sui-bei-2026-nian-icml-shoulu-lunwen-de-xiao-shaonian-wo-bet-kaixin-kaixin-kaixin-lgs-qedm2hdrxrfkgnsphg4i5h5u]]. [[SuTinghao|苏廷昊]] worries that smarter AI may make human careers, contribution narratives, and younger people's motivation feel unstable, especially for children who grow up knowing from the start that AI can think and act faster than they can.

The concept overlaps with [[AIJobSecurityAnxiety]] but goes deeper than employment. The source asks what learning, ambition, and success mean if AI can do the work better, and then answers pragmatically through [[HumanAgencyUnderAI]], [[HumanConnectionUnderAI]], present-life attention, and making oneself and loved people happier.

## Key Claims
- AI can threaten mission as well as jobs: the person may ask why to study or strive if models can perform the same work.
- The anxiety can hit especially early when students must choose long training paths while AI capability keeps changing.
- Catastrophic-risk and concentration-of-power narratives can create mood effects even before they become ordinary policy problems.
- The source's answer is not denial or withdrawal; it is to keep using AI while preserving human-selected goals, relationships, and happiness.
- For AI-native youth, meaning anxiety can coexist with strong technical enthusiasm and genuine research achievement.
