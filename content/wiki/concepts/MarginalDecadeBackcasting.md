# Marginal Decade Backcasting

## Definition
Marginal decade backcasting is [[PeterAttia]]'s source-described method of defining the activities and independence desired in the final decade of life, translating them into measurable physical requirements, and training enough reserve earlier to tolerate expected decline.

## Current Synthesis
The method changes longevity from an abstract wish into a planning problem. A person first describes what they want daily life to include near the end of life: walking, carrying, climbing, getting off the floor, traveling, or other personally meaningful tasks. Those tasks are then decomposed into requirements for strength, stability, aerobic efficiency, and peak aerobic capacity.

Backcasting adds time and decline to the calculation. If a future activity requires a given VO2 max, load-carrying ability, or movement skill, the present target must be higher because capacity tends to decrease with age. The durable insight is not one universal benchmark; it is the direction of reasoning from desired function to current preparation.

## Key Claims
- Longevity goals become more actionable when they specify desired late-life activities rather than only age or disease avoidance.
- Functional tasks can be decomposed into strength, stability, aerobic, power, and movement requirements.
- Present training targets should include reserve for age-related decline rather than merely meeting today's demands.
- Backcasting makes exercise selection answerable to personally meaningful function.
- Reassessment is necessary because health, disability, preferences, and available support can change the desired or feasible plan.

## Evidence
- Final-decade prompt: [[exercise-nutrition-hormones-for-vitality-longevity-dr-peter-attia-scim2767035219]] has Attia ask patients to describe the final decade they want in concrete detail.
- Capacity translation: [[exercise-nutrition-hormones-for-vitality-longevity-dr-peter-attia-scim2767035219]] turns those activities into measurable strength, stability, and cardiorespiratory requirements.
- Decline allowance: [[exercise-nutrition-hormones-for-vitality-longevity-dr-peter-attia-scim2767035219]] uses VO2 max and an estimated per-decade decline to illustrate why current capacity needs a buffer.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source provides a clinical planning heuristic, not a validated universal prediction model. Rates of decline vary, and disability, illness, injury, caregiving, resources, environmental accessibility, and changing values can alter both the goal and the appropriate training path. Numeric targets and exercises require individual medical and coaching context.
