# Transfer Pricing

## Definition

Transfer pricing is the pricing of assets, goods, services, or intellectual property moved between entities inside the same multinational group. It is operationally necessary, but disputed prices can relocate taxable profit across jurisdictions.

## Current Synthesis

[[islamism-in-britain-what-is-the-threat-6aa9164ffca3d5c77b4b383a]] explains the mechanism through the [[CocaCola|Coca-Cola]] dispute with the [[InternalRevenueService|IRS]]. A group can sell or license valuable assets to a subsidiary in a lower-tax jurisdiction and then record more profit there, while the tax authority may argue that the internal allocation understates the value created in the United States.

The case shows why intangible assets make the boundary difficult. Marketing investment, brands, algorithms, and intellectual property do not have a simple external-market price, so both companies and tax authorities can defend different allocation models. Enforcement therefore depends on comparable valuation, documentation, litigation resources, and durable agency capacity.

## Key Claims

- Multinationals need internal prices even when no tax-minimization strategy is intended.
- Contested internal prices can move profit toward low-tax subsidiaries.
- Intangible assets make arm's-length comparison and value attribution especially difficult.
- Long litigation and interest can make eventual exposure much larger than the original assessment.
- A precedent-setting case can change enforcement credibility beyond the company directly involved.

## Evidence

- Basic mechanism: [[islamism-in-britain-what-is-the-threat-6aa9164ffca3d5c77b4b383a]] explains how assets can be sold cheaply within a group and profit recorded where tax is lower.
- Corporate defense: [[islamism-in-britain-what-is-the-threat-6aa9164ffca3d5c77b4b383a]] reports Coca-Cola's argument that foreign subsidiaries made valuable marketing investments and used a formula previously accepted by the IRS.
- Enforcement stakes: [[islamism-in-britain-what-is-the-threat-6aa9164ffca3d5c77b4b383a]] describes a decade-long case whose interest and repeated use of the disputed formula could multiply the amount at risk.
- Intangible-asset trend: [[islamism-in-britain-what-is-the-threat-6aa9164ffca3d5c77b4b383a]] links the issue to the growing share of corporate value held in intellectual property, algorithms, and AI models.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Transfer pricing is not inherently tax evasion or even tax avoidance. The Coca-Cola numbers, procedural status, and likely outcome are reported claims from an active dispute. A victory for either party would resolve particular legal and valuation questions, not eliminate the need for internal pricing or settle every intangible-asset allocation.
