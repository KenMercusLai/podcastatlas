# Opaque Hiring Market / 不透明招聘市场

Opaque hiring market is the episode's nameable pattern for job search under limited information. [[161-zuanzhe-wenping-shiye-wo-zenme-pa-chu-zhege-xianjing-933696202]] describes employers, HR screeners, referrers, and applicants all guessing from incomplete signals: a resume may be scanned quickly, a rejection rarely explains the real reason, and a candidate cannot know which part of their background triggered concern.

The concept extends [[WorkplaceHiddenRules]] to the hiring side. Hidden rules here include how much of the resume should be shown, whether a senior candidate looks overqualified, whether a pay cut looks sincere, how a gap is interpreted, and whether a person changing fields can make a credible narrative.

## Key Claims
- Hiring decisions are not transparent evidence of personal worth.
- More credentials or experience can create suspicion when they do not match the open role's expected profile.
- Applicants often cannot improve efficiently because rejection feedback is sparse or formulaic.
- [[LongTermUnemploymentPenalty]] is partly produced by opacity: because no one knows why a person remains unemployed, people fill the gap with suspicion.
- Honest self-presentation can still matter, but it has to be understood as negotiation under uncertainty rather than naive disclosure.
