## Definition

Talent-Virtue Distinction / 才德之分 is the personnel-judgment frame that treats ability as an amplifier of moral direction rather than as an independent good: the more power a role controls, the more dangerous talented bad judgment or bad character becomes.

## Current Synthesis

Zhouji 04 supplies the core Sima Guang rule through [[ZhiYao|Zhi Yao]]: a ruler should prefer virtue over talent when both cannot be found, because talent gives harmful intent more tools. The fourfold division between saint, fool, gentleman, and lesser person is therefore not abstract moralism; it is a theory of appointment risk.

The later Warring States sources qualify that rule. Zhouji 06 shows [[LiKe|Li Ke]] recommending that [[WuQi|Wu Qi]] can still be used for war because role fit and military scarcity matter. Zhouji 47 and Zhouji 52 show the opposite failure: [[YanWangKuai|Yan Wang Kuai]] mistakes [[ZiZhi|Zi Zhi]] for a worthy person and damages Yan, while [[YanZhaowang|Yan Zhaowang]] rebuilds by attracting better people. Zhouji 56 adds motive through [[YangXiong|Yang Xiong]]'s critique of [[ZhangYiStrategist|Zhang Yi]] and [[SuQin|Su Qin]], and Zhouji 63 adds level through [[WangAnshi|Wang Anshi]]'s critique of [[Mengchangjun|Mengchangjun]]'s chicken-crow and dog-theft retainers.

Hanji 154 then adds a crisis-use boundary through [[WeiWuzhi|Wei Wuzhi]] defending [[ChenPing|Chen Ping]]: a wartime ruler may need useful talent even when personal virtue concerns remain unresolved. Hanji 779 reasserts the severe side in an Eastern Han setting. [[RenShangLateHan|Ren Shang]] has military reputation, but his temperament and judgment are misfit for Western Regions governance after [[BanChaoEasternHan|Ban Chao]]; [[FanZhunLateHan|Fan Zhun]] and [[LuGongLateHan|Lu Gong]] provide the positive contrast, where the right person repairs education and judicial procedure.

## Key Claims

- Talent is an amplifier: it can serve public order or make harmful action more effective.
- Virtue is not only private morality; it includes direction, restraint, and judgment under authority.
- Role fit can qualify the severe rule when a flawed person has scarce, bounded usefulness in a specific crisis.
- Rulers fail the distinction through false positives, especially when reputation, persuasion, or useful tricks are mistaken for governing-level worth.
- Motive and talent level matter because skill used for private gain or low-level rescue is not automatically statecraft.
- Hanji 779 adds that military reputation can fail the talent-virtue test when the job requires local legitimacy, patience, and indirect governance.

## Evidence

- Core amplifier rule: [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-04-yurang-wei-zhiyao-fuchou-ln34tydha6iqwlrgmw7iugqfgfik|Zhouji 04]] uses Zhi Yao to state that talent without virtue is more dangerous than incapacity.
- Role-fit qualification: [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-1-lk-6ejgmjhvukqgtlz2lowu8qx-v|Zhouji 06]] says Li Ke can still recommend Wu Qi for military use despite moral doubts; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-154-diangu-chenping-daosao-lingyou-yinqing-li-c6ezwfpejcvbrpzojg-xv2ge8|Hanji 154]] similarly has Wei Wuzhi defend Chen Ping by asking whether his strategy helps Liu Bang win.
- False-positive judgment: [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-47-yanwangkuai-de-lixiangzhuyi-lhgl6rqkv9nonzxc9xqquzzlz5pr|Zhouji 47]] shows Yan Wang Kuai transferring real authority to Zi Zhi under moralized abdication rhetoric; [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-52-chenzhuang-panqin-duanqi-wan-chifan-fangxia-wan-ma-niang-ls13ixmbhflepjgpelxgu6idjqa2|Zhouji 52]] contrasts that with Yan Zhaowang's recovery through Guo Wei, Le Yi, and Ju Xin.
- Motive and level tests: [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-56-qiaoqiao-liangqian-duo-nianqian-sha-shi-da-zhangfu-ltokbutqgsnrno-y29xmzwa3da41|Zhouji 56]] distinguishes persuasive effectiveness from morally acceptable purpose; [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-63-degao-wangzhong-mengchangjun-shi-weijunzi-lmd0owe3rq-faponbwzhkaigprin|Zhouji 63]] distinguishes emergency tricks from true statecraft-level talent.
- Eastern Han appointment warning: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-779-lishi-gaosu-ni-yongcuo-ren-jing-hui-wangguo-miejia-liwe-syvny7vfmn4xpy24mgvd-z6|Hanji 779]] restates the fourfold frame and shows Ren Shang's capable but harsh Western Regions governance failing after he ignores Ban Chao's advice.
- Positive repair contrast: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-779-lishi-gaosu-ni-yongcuo-ren-jing-hui-wangguo-miejia-liwe-syvny7vfmn4xpy24mgvd-z6|Hanji 779]] pairs the Ren Shang warning with Fan Zhun's education memorial and Lu Gong's judicial-calendar reform.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept is normative and source-framed, not a neutral modern hiring model. Several sources deliberately qualify the strictest reading: Wu Qi and Chen Ping show that flawed people may be useful when the role is bounded, the crisis is acute, and the decision-maker can still manage risk. Hanji 779 also does not prove that Ren Shang lacked every virtue or ability; it shows that his known strengths were mismatched to Western Regions governance.
