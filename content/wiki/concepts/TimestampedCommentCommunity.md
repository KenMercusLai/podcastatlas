# Timestamped Comment Community / 时间戳评论共同体

Timestamped Comment Community describes the source's argument that time-linked comments can add a community layer to audio. In [[no-214-xunzhao-tonglei-xiaohongshu-bilibili-yiji-wuhuabamen-de-naxie-shequ-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-26-1005977305]], [[Xiaoyuzhou|小宇宙]]'s timestamped comments are compared to [[BulletCommentCommunity|bullet comments]] for video.

The concept matters because podcasting is usually asynchronous and private. A timestamped comment lets listeners see other reactions at the exact sentence, joke, claim, or music moment they are hearing, creating a lightweight shared-presence layer without turning the whole medium into a social feed.

## Key Claims
- Time-linked comments can turn passive listening into a shared archive of reactions.
- The feature changes creator feedback because comments become attached to specific moments rather than only the whole episode.
- It can deepen [[PodcastIntimacy]] while still preserving podcasting's asynchronous character.
- The same mechanism needs [[PlatformCommunityGovernance]] because comments can shape show atmosphere and listener expectations.
