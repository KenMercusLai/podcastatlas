# Filial Piety Story Afterlife

Filial piety story afterlife is the reception problem named by [[06-soushenji-ershisi-xiao-zhe-kou-guo-ganbao-bu-bei-536821044]]. The episode argues that some extreme filial-piety tales associated with [[TwentyFourFilialExemplars|《二十四孝》]] may come from or pass through [[SouShenJi|《搜神记》]], but their later use as didactic exempla should not be retroactively treated as the whole meaning of [[GanBao|干宝]]'s collection.

The concept matters because it separates source recording from later moral packaging. A story can be preserved as a marvel, warning, local anecdote, or strange event, then later be excerpted into an instructional tradition. The source's title claim, "干宝不背," means that selected filial stories should be read inside [[StrangeTaleMoralPluralism]] rather than as proof that the entire book commands self-erasure before family authority.

## Key Claims
- Reception can narrow an old story by making one later moral use look original or total.
- Extreme filial tales need to be read with attention to source context, genre, and neighboring stories.
- A collection that includes filial wonders can also include resistance, revenge, remarriage after abuse, and sympathy for nonhuman beings.
- The problem is adjacent to modern debates over filial duty, but it is not the same as [[FilialPietyLaws]], which concerns state-enforced elder-care obligations.
- Moral afterlife should be logged as a transformation, not treated as automatic authorial intent.
