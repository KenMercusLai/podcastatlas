## Definition

Legitimate profit boundary / 正当逐利边界 is the episode's rule for distinguishing socially acceptable wealth-seeking from harmful greed: earn through labor and intelligence, create material value, take reasonable profit, and avoid disrupting government or harming ordinary people.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 410-2 does not treat the desire for profit as the problem. Its boundary is method and social effect. The host frames [[SimaQian|Sima Qian]]'s “道” as work, intelligence, and productive wealth creation, then summarizes the acceptable limit as not乱政 and not扰民.

That makes the concept a commercial-ethics complement to [[RighteousnessOverProfit|义利之辨]]. Where righteousness-over-profit warns against making private gain the first political language, legitimate profit boundary asks how private gain can be pursued without destroying public order. The concept remains source-scoped because the episode gives an ethical interpretation, not a full legal code or theory of markets.

## Key Claims

- Profit-seeking is not condemned by itself.
- The rightful path to profit depends on labor, intelligence, value creation, and reasonable return.
- Social harm, political disorder, and disturbance of common people mark the boundary of illegitimate pursuit.
- Creating material wealth can scale from family enrichment to state enrichment.
- The boundary helps reconcile wealth desire with ethical and public-order concerns.

## Evidence

- Method boundary: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-410-2-rilibaqianwan-gudai-shangren-zhuanqian-you-duoganxiang-lskrjfgimcqzdztlo42pnp6vgh5|Hanji 410-2]] says the “道” of wealth is using labor and wisdom to earn.
- Public-order boundary: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-410-2-rilibaqianwan-gudai-shangren-zhuanqian-you-duoganxiang-lskrjfgimcqzdztlo42pnp6vgh5|Hanji 410-2]] summarizes the limit as making money openly and reasonably while neither disturbing politics nor the people.
- Positive social effect: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-410-2-rilibaqianwan-gudai-shangren-zhuanqian-you-duoganxiang-lskrjfgimcqzdztlo42pnp6vgh5|Hanji 410-2]] says wealth creation can enrich households and the state when people “任其能，竭其力.”

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept does not answer every hard case of monopoly, speculation, crisis profit, or state collusion. It captures the source's ethical line, which still needs other wiki concepts when evaluating concrete institutions or exploitative practices.
