# 大厂 ROI 思维的创作外溢 / ROI-Mindset Creative Spillover

## Definition
大厂 ROI 思维的创作外溢 is the transfer of organizational habits around measurable return, growth, predictability, review, and bad-case prevention into personal creative work, where those habits can reject uncertain experiments before practice generates the evidence needed to judge them.

## Current Synthesis
[[no-218-mengyang-dachang-xunlian-ni-xian-suan-shouyi-er-ziyou-zhiye-jiao-ni-yongbao-suiji-gkwrijenemmyatrz5grxxlq6]] does not argue that ROI, planning, or risk review are inherently wrong. Inside large organizations they coordinate budgets, protect systems, and make decisions accountable. The problem is domain transfer: [[MengYangStandup|梦阳 / 孟阳]] found herself screening every short video for hit potential and challenging every freelance choice for failure modes, even though creative fit and audience response could only emerge through repeated action.

The corrective is not blind risk. It is staged exposure to uncertainty: publish, perform, observe direct feedback, and let concrete problems replace global life anxiety. [[LiuFei|刘飞]] adds that podcast returns and platform windows were partly unforeseeable, so analysis can improve an experiment without fully predicting its downstream opportunity.

## Key Claims
- ROI and bad-case reasoning are context-sensitive tools rather than universal decision rules.
- Creative discovery often requires action before reliable return estimates exist.
- Corporate-scale growth expectations can make ordinary progress look like failure.
- Self-challenge becomes paralyzing when every idea must survive all objections before a first test.
- Direct craft feedback can reveal fit more accurately than title, scale, prestige, or retrospective career narrative.
- Repeated low-cost output can narrow uncertainty without pretending to eliminate it.

## Evidence
- Premature hit prediction - [[no-218-mengyang-dachang-xunlian-ni-xian-suan-shouyi-er-ziyou-zhiye-jiao-ni-yongbao-suiji-gkwrijenemmyatrz5grxxlq6]] says Mengyang began judging whether each short video could become a hit before publishing it.
- Risk-review spillover - [[no-218-mengyang-dachang-xunlian-ni-xian-suan-shouyi-er-ziyou-zhiye-jiao-ni-yongbao-suiji-gkwrijenemmyatrz5grxxlq6]] describes her corporate strength in avoiding bad cases and challenging plans becoming a habit of finding problems with anything she might do independently.
- Unpredictable return - [[no-218-mengyang-dachang-xunlian-ni-xian-suan-shouyi-er-ziyou-zhiye-jiao-ni-yongbao-suiji-gkwrijenemmyatrz5grxxlq6]] has Liu say that starting podcasts and later creating 半拿铁 produced returns that were not fully knowable in advance.
- Action-generated information - [[no-218-mengyang-dachang-xunlian-ni-xian-suan-shouyi-er-ziyou-zhiye-jiao-ni-yongbao-suiji-gkwrijenemmyatrz5grxxlq6]] links open mics, repeated publishing, and trying unsuccessful directions with learning what is exciting, viable, and worth continuing.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is autobiographical and does not prove that planning reduces creative success. Freelancers still need cash-flow, safety, contract, health, and household risk management, and large bets may not be reversible. The narrower claim is that return analysis becomes misleading when the decision has too little evidence and the proposed test is bounded enough to learn from safely.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to distinguish useful organizational ROI discipline from its premature application to creative discovery and career experimentation.
