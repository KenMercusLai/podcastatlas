# Interview As Embodied Reporting / 采访作为具身报道

Interview as embodied reporting / 采访作为具身报道 is the AI-era journalism boundary in [[ep244-jizhe-yanzhong-de-hao-gongzuo-shenme-yang-lk8iz9cllzx2jou5-sw9yt2ogpyb]]. [[WeiQianSanlian|魏茜]] argues that AI can write prose, but it cannot replace the human interaction of interviewing: two bodies in a setting, with emotion, hesitation, environment, judgment, and responsibility entering the final account.

The concept extends the wiki's [[AIWrittenJournalism]] branch by separating article production from reporting production. In this source, the byline matters because a named journalist is accountable for what they saw, felt, selected, judged, and wrote.

## Key Claims
- Interviewing is not only information extraction; it is interaction, observation, context, and situated judgment.
- AI-generated prose can simulate article form without carrying the reporter's embodied evidence trail.
- A journalist's signature implies responsibility for perception and judgment, not only authorship of sentences.
- The harder automation boundary is therefore upstream of writing, in access, presence, trust, and interpretation.
