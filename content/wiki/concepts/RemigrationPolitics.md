# Remigration Politics

## Definition

Remigration politics is the far-right policy language that recasts immigration restriction as a project of removing migrants or people with migration backgrounds from the country.

## Current Synthesis

The source introduces remigration through [[AlternativeForGermany|AfD]]'s Saxony-Anhalt campaign and program. It appears alongside demands to end asylum rights, anti-immigration politics, pro-Russian foreign policy, and attacks on elite or "cartel" parties.

In this episode, remigration is not treated as a normal border-management term. It marks a harder nationalist position that affects coalition acceptability, public fear, civil-society preparation, and the question of what a state government could actually implement under German federal constraints.

## Key Claims

- Remigration politics intensifies immigration backlash by shifting from entry control to removal-oriented national identity politics.
- The term can help make a party unacceptable to mainstream coalition partners even when it attracts electoral support.
- State-level remigration promises can exceed the formal powers available to a regional government.
- The language can produce concrete local costs when residents, workers, or organizations fear they would be targeted.

## Evidence

- AfD program: [[right-in-front-afd-could-win-german-state-6a97f0a5a4a22c581728b85f]] says AfD promotes remigration while opposing immigration and asylum rights.
- Coalition boundary: [[right-in-front-afd-could-win-german-state-6a97f0a5a4a22c581728b85f]] places those positions inside the broader explanation for the Brandmauer.
- State-power limit: [[right-in-front-afd-could-win-german-state-6a97f0a5a4a22c581728b85f]] notes that state government cannot directly control many national AfD positions.
- Social fallout: [[right-in-front-afd-could-win-german-state-6a97f0a5a4a22c581728b85f]] reports that some people in Saxony-Anhalt say they or people they know would consider leaving under AfD rule.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source does not provide a full legal definition of remigration, a complete AfD policy history, or evidence of exactly how such proposals would be implemented. The concept should remain tied to far-right nationalist removal politics rather than ordinary migration administration.
