# Mosquito Bite Prevention

## Definition
Mosquito bite prevention is the practical prevention frame for reducing bites through effective repellent ingredients, correct application, reapplication, physical barriers, and household source reduction.

## Current Synthesis
The current source treats prevention as an evidence-and-use problem. A repellent is not judged mainly by whether it smells natural, comes as a wearable object, or feels technologically novel; it is judged by active ingredient, concentration, expected duration, body coverage, and whether the user reapplies it after sweat or water exposure.

The prevention frame also includes mosquito ecology and household behavior. Listeners are told to treat standing water, exposed skin, dark clothing, sweat, carbon dioxide, and smoke-producing mosquito coils as part of the prevention field rather than relying on a single product.

## Key Claims
- Active ingredients and concentration matter more than product form, scent, or "natural" branding.
- Repellents need even skin application and reapplication when sweat, water, or exposure time reduces protection.
- Mosquito attraction is multifactorial, involving carbon dioxide, sweat or odor, clothing, pregnancy, drinking, body state, and genetics rather than a settled blood-type rule.
- Household prevention combines standing-water removal, long sleeves, mosquito nets, and caution with burning coils or smoke sources.

- Ingredient and concentration gate: [[vol-213-wenzi-weishenme-pianai-yao-ni-pifuke-yisheng-chaichuan-quwen-zhishangshui-fu-kuaisu-zhiyang-xuanyao-zhinan-lifkugb27d79ut30lwfmu5vojegec]] compares DEET, picaridin or icaridin, IR3535, lemon eucalyptus extract, and citronella oil by use duration, texture, price, availability, and context.
- Application and reapplication: [[vol-213-wenzi-weishenme-pianai-yao-ni-pifuke-yisheng-chaichuan-quwen-zhishangshui-fu-kuaisu-zhiyang-xuanyao-zhinan-lifkugb27d79ut30lwfmu5vojegec]] says effective repellents must be applied to exposed body areas and reapplied after water or sweat.
- Attraction factors: [[vol-213-wenzi-weishenme-pianai-yao-ni-pifuke-yisheng-chaichuan-quwen-zhishangshui-fu-kuaisu-zhiyang-xuanyao-zhinan-lifkugb27d79ut30lwfmu5vojegec]] rejects blood-type certainty while listing carbon dioxide, sweat, body odor, alcohol, pregnancy, body size or exertion, clothing color, and genetics as more plausible factors.
- Household source control: [[vol-213-wenzi-weishenme-pianai-yao-ni-pifuke-yisheng-chaichuan-quwen-zhishangshui-fu-kuaisu-zhiyang-xuanyao-zhinan-lifkugb27d79ut30lwfmu5vojegec]] ends with standing-water cleanup, physical barriers, and fire or respiratory caution around burning mosquito coils and indoor smoke.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This is a public medical-literacy frame, not individualized advice for children, pregnancy, allergy history, pets, product labels, or local vector-control rules. The episode's concentration-duration claims and product comparisons remain source-scoped, and users still need to follow product instructions and qualified medical guidance for sensitive situations.

## What Changed
- VOL.213 adds a mosquito-specific prevention concept that joins repellent ingredients, correct use, exposure context, and household source control.
