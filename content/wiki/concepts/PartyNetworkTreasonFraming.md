## Definition

Party network treason framing / 朋党网络谋反化 is the pattern where a reputation, recommendation, teaching, association, or follower network is explained to a ruler as disorder, factional danger, or rebellion risk.

## Current Synthesis

The current concept now starts before formal prosecution. Hanji 871 gives the social-substrate layer through [[ZhouFuLateHan|周福]] and [[FangZhiLateHan|房植]] at Ganling: two reputation poles attract guests and followers, mutual ridicule hardens into north-south divisions, and the episode treats that local split as an early beginning of party talk. The same source also shows Taixue public evaluation through [[GuoTaiLateHan|郭泰]] and [[JiaBiaoLateHan|贾彪]], where praise-and-blame pressures officials before any court accusation is filed.

The first prosecution case in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-874-zhenjing-muzi-xiangcan-tianli-nanrong-lpr3eglbnwbve1clrttapw6k5zdq|Hanji 874]] runs through [[LaoXiuLateHan|劳修]]. After [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]] executes [[ZhangChengAstrologerLateHan|张成]] father and son despite an amnesty, Zhang Cheng's eunuch connections back retaliation. Lao Xiu accuses Li Ying and other士人 of praising Taixue游士, receiving students, mutual praise, party formation, slandering the court, and disordering customs. This version does not yet require an explicit rebellion explanation; it is enough to make association look like a public-order threat before [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-893-rongyu-bang-ruhe-biancheng-siwang-mingdan-lmxzqoauf79-swwnk-0cevav0gq|Hanji 893]] gives the later and sharper source case through [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]]. When officials report that party figures are mutually implicated, the fourteen-year-old [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] asks what the wording means. Cao Jie explains mutual recommendation and faction formation, then escalates the explanation into possible overthrow of the court and emperor.

The framing is powerful because it translates social visibility into ruler fear. A士人 network may begin as teacherly favor, local followers, Taixue praise, student reception, or reputation politics, but once an inner-court interpreter controls the explanation, those ties can be made to look like coordinated danger. Hanji 871 shows the pre-accusation network and public-opinion forms; Hanji 874 shows the public-order version; Hanji 893 shows the full rebellion version.

## Key Claims

- Party framing starts from visible social organization before it becomes formal prosecution.
- Reputation and teaching ties become punishable when an accuser can define them as factional disorder.
- The early framing can work through public-order language before it becomes explicit treason language.
- Ambiguous "party" wording becomes more lethal when a court insider defines it for the ruler.
- Mutual recommendation and reputation ties can be recoded as preparation rather than social esteem.
- The treason frame does not need to prove actual rebellion if it succeeds in activating ruler self-protection.
- Palace access matters because the person explaining the accusation can decide whether the ruler sees honor, faction, disorder, or rebellion.

## Evidence

- Local follower split: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-871-cheng-e-yang-shan-weihe-fanbei-wuxian-ruyu-lujkihgvh-cafkh9bfak6-vttpwt|Hanji 871]] says Zhou Fu and Fang Zhi's Ganling supporters mock one another, build opposing follower groups, and produce north-south local divisions.
- Public-opinion substrate: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-871-cheng-e-yang-shan-weihe-fanbei-wuxian-ruyu-lujkihgvh-cafkh9bfak6-vttpwt|Hanji 871]] says Guo Tai, Jia Biao, Li Ying, Chen Fan, and Wang Chang form a Taixue-linked praise field that officials fear.
- Early accusation channel: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-874-zhenjing-muzi-xiangcan-tianli-nanrong-lpr3eglbnwbve1clrttapw6k5zdq|Hanji 874]] says Lao Xiu, backed by Zhang Cheng's eunuch connections, accuses Li Ying and others after Zhang Cheng's execution.
- Association-to-disorder move: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-874-zhenjing-muzi-xiangcan-tianli-nanrong-lpr3eglbnwbve1clrttapw6k5zdq|Hanji 874]] says the accusation includes Taixue游士, student reception, mutual praise, party formation, court slander, and disordering customs.
- Arrest effect before Huan: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-874-zhenjing-muzi-xiangcan-tianli-nanrong-lpr3eglbnwbve1clrttapw6k5zdq|Hanji 874]] says Emperor Huan orders arrests after this accusation while Chen Fan resists the arrest document.
- Interpreter role: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-893-rongyu-bang-ruhe-biancheng-siwang-mingdan-lmxzqoauf79-swwnk-0cevav0gq|Hanji 893]] says Emperor Ling asks Cao Jie what the party accusation means.
- Rebellion escalation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-893-rongyu-bang-ruhe-biancheng-siwang-mingdan-lmxzqoauf79-swwnk-0cevav0gq|Hanji 893]] says Cao Jie explains mutual recommendation and party formation as preparation for improper action, then tells the emperor the party figures may overthrow the court and emperor.
- Later arrest authorization: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-893-rongyu-bang-ruhe-biancheng-siwang-mingdan-lmxzqoauf79-swwnk-0cevav0gq|Hanji 893]] says Emperor Ling soon approves the report after hearing Cao Jie's explanation.
- Local-to-court bridge: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-893-rongyu-bang-ruhe-biancheng-siwang-mingdan-lmxzqoauf79-swwnk-0cevav0gq|Hanji 893]] also shows Zhu Bi's accusation against Zhang Jian and others becoming part of the court arrest sequence.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept names the framing mechanism, not proof that every named party figure was innocent of all political ambition. Hanji 871 supports a source-scoped judgment about visible local and Taixue networks before prosecution; Hanji 874 supports reputation networks being framed as disorder before Emperor Huan; Hanji 893 supports the later and stronger judgment that Cao Jie converts association into rebellion fear for a young emperor. The concept should not collapse every social network into factional politics; it tracks the hostile interpretive move.

## What Changed

- The current synthesis now starts with Hanji 871's pre-accusation local and Taixue network layer.
- The concept distinguishes social substrate, disorder framing, and rebellion framing as three escalation stages.
- The definition now includes follower networks alongside teaching, recommendation, and reputation ties.
