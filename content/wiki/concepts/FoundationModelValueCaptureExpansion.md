# Foundation Model Value-Capture Expansion

## Definition

Foundation model value-capture expansion is the move from selling model access or tokens toward participating in workflows, products, transactions, or measurable business outcomes created with the model.

## Current Synthesis

The episode argues that frontier labs may become commercial co-creators rather than only infrastructure suppliers. In medicine, finance, enterprise services, and digital production, a lab could combine model capability with domain partners, workflow integration, verification, and outcome measurement, then charge for more than API consumption.

This can increase the addressable value pool and reduce exposure to token commoditization, but it also moves the provider into delivery risk. Outcome attribution, domain liability, human review, customer data, implementation labor, and bargaining over who created the value become part of the business model. Expansion is therefore not automatically higher-margin or defensible.

## Key Claims

- Token and API revenue need not be the endpoint when the model contributes directly to accepted business outcomes.
- Moving upward requires domain workflow, integration, verification, and accountability rather than model capability alone.
- Outcome-linked participation can defend value capture when generic inference prices fall.
- The larger value pool comes with delivery, attribution, regulatory, and liability exposure.
- Vertical partners and customers may retain bargaining power because they own data, distribution, licenses, and final acceptance.

## Evidence

- **Co-creation thesis:** [[03e51cc1-020d-4c30-8c07-8f8614b46e7f-03e51cc1-020d-4c30-8c07-8f8614b46e7f]] describes a shift from selling model access toward jointly solving industry problems and sharing the resulting value.
- **Vertical examples:** [[03e51cc1-020d-4c30-8c07-8f8614b46e7f-03e51cc1-020d-4c30-8c07-8f8614b46e7f]] names drug development, rare-disease products, finance, investment banking, and enterprise workflows as possible settings for deeper participation.
- **Coding boundary:** [[03e51cc1-020d-4c30-8c07-8f8614b46e7f-03e51cc1-020d-4c30-8c07-8f8614b46e7f]] treats coding as creation across the digital world, connecting model capability to products and automated processes rather than only developer assistance.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source presents a strategic possibility, not audited evidence of completed outcome-sharing contracts or durable margins. Model providers may lack domain authority, customer access, proprietary data, implementation capacity, or willingness to assume outcome risk. Customers and application companies can also route among models and keep most of the value above the model layer.

## What Changed

- Created a model-layer strategy frame that distinguishes API growth from participation in downstream value.
- Added delivery risk and customer or vertical-partner bargaining power as limits on value-capture expansion.
