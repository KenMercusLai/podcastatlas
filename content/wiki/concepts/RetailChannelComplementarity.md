## Definition
Retail channel complementarity is the condition in which physical retail and direct sales serve meaningfully different purchase, trial, education, assortment, or relationship needs so that one channel can add value rather than merely transfer revenue from the other.

## Current Synthesis
[[XOMarshmallow]] supplies an occasion hypothesis: immediate low-ticket indulgence in a store differs from planned online baskets with shipping. [[DrSquatch]] supplies a stage transition in which DTC demand proof preceded majority-retail scale while e-commerce remained meaningful. [[Rothys|Rothy's]] supplies a tactile unfamiliar-product case: stores let customers try on a shoe whose recycled-plastic description might otherwise imply discomfort, while online sales retained convenience and reach. Together the cases show that complementarity can arise from immediacy, trial, explanation, assortment, and company stage. None independently proves incrementality; channel cohorts, margins, attribution, and cannibalization still require measurement.

## Key Claims
- Channel choice should begin with the customer's purchase job, timing, trial need, and information gap rather than a generic DTC-versus-retail preference.
- Physical retail can add value where immediate possession, fit, touch, taste, demonstration, or explanation matters.
- DTC can retain assortment depth, bundles, planned replenishment, gifting, customer data, story, and direct service.
- Retail expansion should follow measured tests of sell-through, production reliability, unit economics, and customer overlap.
- A high-performing pilot location can justify learning but does not prove that performance will generalize across formats or geographies.
- Broad retail reach can require manufacturing, replenishment, merchandising, and working-capital capabilities that an online brand has not yet built.
- Complementarity is an empirical claim: incremental customers and contribution margin must be separated from channel substitution.

## Evidence
### Distinct purchase occasions
- [[advice-line-with-tara-bosch-of-smartsweets-8aab80a1-b0c5-4103-b875-f5d4823d07ce]] records XO Marshmallow customers objecting to shipping cost and delay and frames grocery as an immediate occasion distinct from broader online purchasing.

### DTC proof before retail scale
- [[dr-squatch-jack-halldrup-from-a-closet-full-of-soap-to-a-1-5-billion-brand-vmp1750831437]] says Dr. Squatch moved from an overwhelmingly e-commerce model to majority-retail sales while preserving significant online revenue and building owned output capacity.

### Tactile education and fit
- [[rothy-s-roth-martin-the-art-dealer-who-built-a-1b-shoe-brand-from-plastic-bottles-vmp9564565828]] says Rothy's stores helped customers understand and try recycled-polyester shoes, while farmers-market selling supplied direct observation of fit and questions.

### Staged channel commitment
- [[advice-line-with-tara-bosch-of-smartsweets-8aab80a1-b0c5-4103-b875-f5d4823d07ce]] recommends natural or regional retail before mass expansion, and [[rothy-s-roth-martin-the-art-dealer-who-built-a-1b-shoe-brand-from-plastic-bottles-vmp9564565828]] reports testing a small store before wider rollout.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- Retail can cannibalize higher-margin direct sales, add wholesale discounts, trade spending, returns, chargebacks, inventory fragmentation, and weaker attribution.
- XO Marshmallow's complementarity remains advice and hypothesis rather than measured channel evidence.
- Dr. Squatch's account does not disclose comparable channel margins, cohort overlap, retailer terms, or a no-retail counterfactual.
- Rothy's reported store productivity comes from one founder account and may reflect location, brand maturity, novelty, or local customer concentration rather than a replicable format.
- Physical availability does not guarantee sell-through; product, price, merchandising, service, fit, repeat use, and replenishment remain decisive.

## What Changed
- Added tactile trial and unfamiliar-material education through Rothy's.
- Strengthened the distinction between a successful pilot store and generalizable retail incrementality.
- Preserved DTC as a relationship and assortment channel rather than treating retail transition as replacement.
