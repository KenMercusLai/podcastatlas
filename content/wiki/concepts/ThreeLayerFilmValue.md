## Definition
Three-layer film value is [[SongFangjin|宋方金]]'s source-scoped framework distinguishing immediate emotional activation (情绪), sustained relationship feeling (情感), and wider personal, biographical, or cultural attachment (情怀).

## Current Synthesis
The framework helps explain why a film can generate momentary excitement without building durable feeling, and why very large audience response may combine several kinds of attachment. Song reads 《你好，李焕英》 through all three layers: comic and time-travel activation, a mother-daughter bond, and the creator's biographical connection to her mother.

The categories are analytic rather than objective scores. A film may blur all three, and stronger box office does not prove that every layer is present. Their best use is diagnostic: asking what kind of response a work is organizing and what disappears when production chases immediate affect alone.

## Key Claims
- Immediate arousal and durable relational feeling are not identical audience effects.
- Personal or cultural attachment can enlarge a story beyond plot mechanics.
- Large-scale resonance may emerge when several value layers reinforce one another.
- Optimizing only immediate emotion can flatten relationship and meaning.
- The framework describes audience response but does not fully measure artistic quality.

## Evidence
- Three-part framework: [[lt24pcobvqnyxjeven2dwhuhvmfu-lt24pcobvqnyxjeven2dwhuhvmfu]] records Song's explicit distinction among 情绪价值、情感价值、情怀价值.
- Film example: [[lt24pcobvqnyxjeven2dwhuhvmfu-lt24pcobvqnyxjeven2dwhuhvmfu]] applies the distinction to 《你好，李焕英》 through time-travel affect, mother-daughter relationship, and biographical attachment.
- Industry critique: [[lt24pcobvqnyxjeven2dwhuhvmfu-lt24pcobvqnyxjeven2dwhuhvmfu]] argues that contemporary production can over-pursue immediate emotional value at the expense of the other layers.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The framework comes from one interview and is not a validated audience model. Chinese terms such as 情绪、情感、情怀 overlap, and reception differs by viewer. Box office can also reflect distribution, release timing, competition, price, publicity, and social events rather than layered value alone.

## What Changed
- Created the concept as a bounded film-analysis framework rather than a general law of box office.
