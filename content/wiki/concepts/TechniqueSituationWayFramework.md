# Technique–Situation–Way Action Framework / 术—势—道行动框架

## Definition

The technique–situation–way action framework / 术—势—道行动框架 separates concrete execution, the reading and shaping of political circumstances, and the purpose or replacement order that makes an intervention more than a successful maneuver.

## Current Synthesis

In the bounded source's reading of [[QinErshiMustDie|《秦二世必须死》]], “术” covers investigation, escape, assassination craft, deception, and survival; “势” covers judging, creating, and redirecting the conditions under which action can spread; and “道” asks why Qin should be opposed and what order can legitimately follow it. The hierarchy explains why killing an emperor may injure a regime without solving the political problem that produced it.

The novel distributes emphases among [[ZhangCang|张苍]], [[XuFu|徐福]], and [[ZhangLiang|张良]], but the mapping is not an exhaustive personality taxonomy. Zhang Liang's early desire to restore Han and the six states is the central qualification: thinking at the level of ultimate purpose does not guarantee that one's proposed purpose is adequate. Nor must real uprisings begin from a finished theory; the hosts explicitly suggest that action, planning, and public persuasion can develop together.

## Key Claims

- Technique concerns operational capability and can succeed without creating durable political change.
- Situation concerns timing, leverage, expectations, coalition, and the direction of a changing field.
- Way concerns justification, ends, and the institutional order intended to replace what is opposed.
- Higher-level framing does not eliminate the need for lower-level execution or situational judgment.
- Asking the right level of question does not guarantee a correct answer.
- Political action can refine its theory while events unfold rather than following a complete prior blueprint.

### Character emphases

- [[114-zai-shiliao-zhong-wajue-mixin-maboyong-zuixin-zuopin-qinershi-bixusi-lkgah7ss4dd7-vs6o5tdeuiq-fjl]] associates Zhang Cang with execution and survival, Xu Fu with the management of circumstances, and Zhang Liang with the search for an anti-Qin political direction.

### Theory under revision

- [[114-zai-shiliao-zhong-wajue-mixin-maboyong-zuixin-zuopin-qinershi-bixusi-lkgah7ss4dd7-vs6o5tdeuiq-fjl]] qualifies the hierarchy through Zhang Liang's restorationism and the hosts' doubt that historical rebels first constructed a complete doctrine before acting.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The framework comes through a novel and an informal podcast interpretation, not a demonstrated general theory of strategy or a reconstruction of how actual anti-Qin movements planned. “势” can also carry meanings broader than “situation,” and “道” should not be treated as proof of moral truth merely because it is the highest term. Roles overlap, plans emerge through action, and operational success, popular mobilization, and legitimate replacement can fail independently.

## What Changed

- Established the three-level framework and its central warning that assassination is not equivalent to political replacement.
