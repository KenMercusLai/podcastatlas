# 王安石变法 / Wang Anshi Reforms

## Definition

王安石变法 / Wang Anshi Reforms is the Northern Song reform field where fiscal strain, security demands, state economic intervention, factional conflict, and later historical reception collide. The wiki treats it as a policy problem with real motives and real harms, not as a simple morality tale.

## Current Synthesis

The existing Su Shi source frames the reforms as a serious response to [[NorthernSong|北宋]] fiscal and security pressure, while stressing that implementation could become coercive, extractive, and painful for ordinary people. [[SuShi|苏轼]] and [[SuZhe|苏辙]] oppose damaging execution, but Su Shi's later criticism of old-party retaliation keeps the page from reducing the debate to one virtuous faction against one villainous faction.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-396-4-wanganshi-bianfa-zenme-daozhi-beisong-miewang-de-lslupw7c76poanj-zzmh59z1iofr|Hanji 396-4]] sharpens the fiscal-market critique. It presents the market-exchange law and Green Sprouts law as [[StateCommercialMonopolyExtraction|官营商业垄断式汲取]]: the court uses fairness language, official commerce, and state-backed credit to capture merchant profit and lending interest. In that source, the reforms can make the state richer while weakening market vitality and public livelihood.

The Zhouji reception sources show that Wang Anshi's reform politics also affected how later listeners read ancient material. [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s wording around [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]] could sound like a Song-era argument over reform backlash, while [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]] reception connects Wang Anshi to the relation between renyi, benefit, and legitimate state action.

## Key Claims

- Reform can answer genuine fiscal and security problems while still harming society through coercive implementation.
- The charge of "与民争利" marks the blurred line between state capacity and state extraction.
- Direct official participation in commerce or lending can turn policy into market suppression when state authority competes with private actors.
- Opposition to reform can be grounded in public suffering rather than mere factional conservatism.
- Reversal of reform can also become unjust if it turns into old-party retaliation.
- Ancient reform and classical-benefit debates became proxies for Song reform politics in later historiographical and intellectual reception.
- Hanji 396-4 adds a stronger dynasty-collapse interpretation but leaves other causes of Northern Song failure outside its short argument.

## Evidence

- Motive and harm: [[105-su-dongpo-yiqiang-haomai-zhiqi-yi-dupi-buhe-shiyi-741072645|Su Dongpo 105]] acknowledges fiscal and security motives while stressing harsh implementation and ordinary people's suffering.
- Conscience beyond faction: [[105-su-dongpo-yiqiang-haomai-zhiqi-yi-dupi-buhe-shiyi-741072645|Su Dongpo 105]] shows Su Shi criticizing damaging new-law practice and later old-party retaliation.
- State commercial extraction: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-396-4-wanganshi-bianfa-zenme-daozhi-beisong-miewang-de-lslupw7c76poanj-zzmh59z1iofr|Hanji 396-4]] describes the market-exchange law, official commercial coercion, Green Sprouts credit monopoly, and treasury income from interest and market profit.
- Late-Northern-Song extension: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-396-4-wanganshi-bianfa-zenme-daozhi-beisong-miewang-de-lslupw7c76poanj-zzmh59z1iofr|Hanji 396-4]] links [[CaiJing|蔡京]] to a later continuation of extractive new-party logic.
- Ancient-reform reception: [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-24-shangyang-yue-wo-za-lie-le-lt4yd6pwlezqia5yljg-gankyhut|Zhouji 24]] says Sima Guang's framing of Shang Yang resentment could resonate with Song debates over Wang Anshi.
- Mencius reception: [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-26-mengzi-laile-ln4twwp2frmr-xpumu0rqg-2gpyn|Zhouji 26]] says Wang Anshi helped raise Mencius's status, tying reform politics to renyi-benefit language.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The sources do not provide a complete institutional history of the reforms. The Su Shi episode is more balanced about motive and factional reversal, while Hanji 396-4 is deliberately sharper and more critical. The wiki therefore records the extraction and monopoly critique without treating "Northern Song亡于王安石变法" as a fully settled causal explanation.
