# 小爱与大爱整合 / Love-Compassion Integration

## Definition

Love-compassion integration is the claim that intimate, particular love can be part of broader compassion rather than merely an obstacle to it.

## Current Synthesis

The Hanji 456-4 film reading makes this concept the pivot of [[ChenXuanzangConqueringDemons|陈玄奘]]'s arc. The problem is not that worldly love must be rejected in order to reach universal compassion. The problem is that Chen uses "great love" as a way to deny "small love." Only after he admits love for [[DuanMissConqueringDemons|段小姐]], loses her, and feels pain directly can compassion stop being an abstract slogan.

## Key Claims

- Universal compassion becomes hollow when it is used to avoid concrete attachment and pain.
- Romantic love can disclose vulnerability, grief, and empathy rather than only desire.
- Letting go is different from never acknowledging the bond.
- Pain and loss can make another person's suffering intelligible.
- The concept remains source-scoped because it is a film interpretation, not a complete Buddhist doctrine.

- Romantic love as vulnerability: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-4-zhoumo-ruiqi-pei-ni-lingyi-shijiao-zai-kan-yibian-xiyou-xiangmo-lolxgqbkq2owmzzay91dy4fanwn3]] centers Duan Miss's demand for present love and Chen's eventual confession.
- Letting go after acknowledgment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-4-zhoumo-ruiqi-pei-ni-lingyi-shijiao-zai-kan-yibian-xiyou-xiangmo-lolxgqbkq2owmzzay91dy4fanwn3]] distinguishes the final farewell to worldly attachment from Chen's earlier refusal.
- Pain as compassion ground: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-4-zhoumo-ruiqi-pei-ni-lingyi-shijiao-zai-kan-yibian-xiyou-xiangmo-lolxgqbkq2owmzzay91dy4fanwn3]] says one knows sentient beings' pain only after pain and knows release only after attachment.
- Source-scoped doctrine: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-4-zhoumo-ruiqi-pei-ni-lingyi-shijiao-zai-kan-yibian-xiyou-xiangmo-lolxgqbkq2owmzzay91dy4fanwn3]] grounds the claim in the film's plot and symbols rather than in a systematic Buddhist argument.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept should not be generalized into a claim that all attachment is spiritually necessary or that suffering is automatically ennobling. The source's bounded claim is narrower: in this film reading, denied romantic love must be acknowledged before it can be transformed.
