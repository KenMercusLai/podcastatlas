# Parental Defense Conflict / 父母辩护冲突

Parental defense conflict / 父母辩护冲突 is the ethical and legal clash that appears when protecting one's child may require betraying evidence, victims, public duty, or future safety. [[24-hanwei-yagebu-xiangxin-ni-shi-wo-weiyi-de-zhinian-576218441]] names the pattern through [[AndyBarber|Andy Barber]] and [[LaurieBarber|Laurie Barber]] in [[DefendingJacob|《捍卫雅各布》 / Defending Jacob]].

Andy embodies one version: he believes [[JacobBarber|Jacob]] must be innocent and uses his legal knowledge to shield him. Laurie embodies another: she fears Jacob may be guilty or dangerous and moves toward a terrible act framed as prevention. The source treats both as parental love under impossible pressure, but it refuses to turn love into automatic justification.

## Key Claims
- Parental loyalty can be morally understandable while still legally corrupting.
- Professional roles intensify the conflict when the parent has duties to the justice system.
- Protecting one child may create risk for other children, victims' families, or the public.
- The conflict becomes harder when [[LegalTruthEvidenceGap]] prevents certainty.
- The concept sits near [[LawMoralityBoundary]] because emotion may matter, but it cannot become unconstrained permission.
