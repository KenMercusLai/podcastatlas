# Narcissistic Relational Control / 自恋式关系控制

Narcissistic relational control / 自恋式关系控制 is [[ep273-npc-haishi-npd-dou-bie-xiang-nazou-women-rensheng-de-yaokongqi-lqqnd-e29xvhx2mtf6uuzx13wwvf]]'s relationship-level account of how an extremely self-centered person can pull attention, obedience, and value confirmation from others. The episode does not reduce the pattern to loud arrogance; [[GongXueping|龚学平]] describes it as a fragile need to be centered because the person lacks stable inner worth.

The control can appear in small scenes. A partner may frame cycling as too dangerous, dismiss a friend's achievement, erase another person's happiness or pain, or require unspoken understanding while offering little reciprocal care. The shared effect is that the other person's world narrows: outside admiration, friendships, hobbies, bodily choices, and ordinary self-confidence are pulled back toward the controller.

## Key Claims
- Charm and attraction can precede control; being drawn to charisma, competence, beauty, style, or status is not itself shameful.
- The damaging shift happens when the admired person becomes the button that confirms one's own worth.
- Control often hides in ordinary details rather than only in dramatic violence.
- Safety, expertise, taste, and superiority can all become tools for making another person distrust their own judgment.
- The pattern overlaps with abuse concepts but should still be observed through repeated relational effects, not through one disliked behavior.
