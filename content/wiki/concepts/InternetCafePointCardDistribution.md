# Internet-Cafe Point-Card Distribution

Internet-cafe point-card distribution is the offline-to-online channel system highlighted in [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]]. In the episode, [[Shanda|盛大]] could not rely on modern downloads, app stores, or mobile payment, so it built promotion and sales around internet cafes, prepaid cards, local dealers, and customer service.

The concept explains why [[LegendOfMir|《传奇》]] scaled so quickly in early-2000s China. Shanda's channel work turned internet cafes into acquisition, installation, payment, and support nodes, making [[ChineseOnlineGameCommercialization]] possible before consumer internet infrastructure was mature.
