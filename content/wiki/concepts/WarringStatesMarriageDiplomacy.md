# Warring States Marriage Diplomacy / 战国联姻外交

Warring States marriage diplomacy / 战国联姻外交 is the kinship-alliance pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-14-qiweiwang-buming-zeyi-yiming-jingren-lrs6rbifyiqrojxg0mccpg7sf9rv]] draws from [[YouyinHei|右尹黑]] going from [[ChuState|楚国]] to [[QinState|秦国]] to receive a bride. The episode treats the bride as probably intended for [[ChuXuanwang|楚宣王]], making the marriage a ruler-level diplomatic act rather than a private official's wedding.

The source's important limit is that marriage does not dissolve interest conflict. Qin and Chu can maintain a relationship through marriage and still later fight when state interests diverge.

## Key Claims
- Marriage can operate as interstate diplomacy through ruling-house kinship.
- Protocol details, such as who receives the bride, can reveal political rank and purpose.
- A marriage tie signals relationship-building but does not override military and territorial incentives.
- The concept sits beside [[KinshipLegitimacyDiplomacy]] but focuses on alliance maintenance rather than origin legitimation.
