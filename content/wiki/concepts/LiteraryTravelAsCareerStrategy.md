# 文人漫游作为仕途策略 / Literary Travel As Career Strategy

Literary travel as career strategy is the episode's caution against reading Tang poet travel as pure leisure or pure reclusion. [[ep264-tafang-tangshi-zhilu-shengtang-qixiang-shaonianxin-loo2dfuxffizpbl4si5nwcixnngi]] shows that roaming could include scenic experience, cultural pilgrimage, seeking Daoist or elite recommendation, gaining reputation, exchanging poems, and building an image that might later help official entry.

The source uses [[MengHaoran|孟浩然]] and the [[ZhedongTangPoetryRoad|浙东唐诗之路]] for the southern version, while [[CenShen|岑参]] shows the frontier-office version after limited Chang'an success. The point is not cynicism about poetry; it is that poems, careers, and social institutions formed each other.

## Key Claims
- Tang literati mobility could be aesthetic, spiritual, social, and career-oriented at the same time.
- Reclusion could be sincere, staged, or strategically legible to recommendation networks.
- Frontier service offered another route for writers when exams, recommendation, or capital networking stalled.
- Literary geography should include institutions and incentives as well as scenery.
