# Self-Permission / 自我许可

Self-permission is the central frame added by [[ep254-you-xiaoying-x-wenqi-x-du-sujuan-nvxing-zhe-yisheng-dou-zai-fanfu-xuexi-xuke-ziji-lm-w7ryj4zay6vldsrkadohj997d]] through [[WoXukeFilm|《我许可》]], [[YouXiaoying|游晓颖]], [[WenQi|文淇]], and [[DuSujuan|杜素娟]]. It names the practice of treating one's body, feeling, need, ambition, and refusal as legitimate before external permission fully arrives.

The concept extends [[FemaleSelfPossession]] but is slightly more processual. The episode suggests that women do not become self-permitted once and remain that way; they repeatedly relearn it at menarche, in clinics, in cars, at work, in friendships, in front of mirrors, and while looking back at mothers' sacrificed possibilities.

## Key Claims
- Self-permission begins with [[SelfFeelingTrust]]: discomfort, fear, tiredness, desire, and pride deserve attention before they are dismissed as shameful or troublesome.
- Body knowledge matters because [[MenstrualStigma]], [[FemaleBodyFreedom]], and [[GynecologicalExamDignity]] determine whether a woman can treat ordinary bodily experience as legitimate.
- Permission is not only internal confidence. It often requires [[CommunicationBoundarySetting]]: asking for the car to stop, saying no to harassment, declining disrespectful medical observation, or refusing a bad professional rule.
- Women-to-women recognition makes permission easier to learn. [[FemaleFriendshipRecognition]] and online comments can turn private embarrassment into shared vocabulary.
- Mother-daughter history gives self-permission a future-facing test: [[MotherDaughterIntergenerationalMirror]] asks whether a daughter will someday regret the self her mother did not live.
