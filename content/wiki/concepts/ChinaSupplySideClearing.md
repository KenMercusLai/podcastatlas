# China Supply-Side Clearing

[[153-quanqiu-hongguan-he-ziben-shichang-2026-zhanwang-da-nian-zhihou-reng-shi-da-nian-lupeqjdszon-wp5zdq06w3ustw2d]] adds a demand-side qualification through [[ChinaDeflationDemandRepair]]. The source treats anti-involution and capacity clearing as useful but insufficient: without demand repair, price and profit improvement may not be strong enough to validate the equity rally.

China supply-side clearing is [[MouYiling|牟一凌]]'s frame in [[vol-112-yici-feigongshi-de-2024-fansi-he-2025-zhanwang-duihua-lanxiaokang-x-mouyiling-lkasjtodqgmnbpyambqoxl-fcsyv]] for why strong production can coexist with weak prices and profits. The episode notes that since 2022 or 2023, electricity use and PPI have diverged: output can keep rising while price competition and overcapacity pressure compress margins.

The policy implication is that demand stimulus alone may not repair listed-company earnings. If production volume and industry "involution" keep pushing prices down, the supply ecology has to change so weak firms exit and surviving firms gain pricing, cash-flow, or market-share durability.

[[vol-111-guanyu-2025-nian-de-si-ge-caixiang-lgaira5qw1fhic4qwihrimed6q9g]] adds the policy-signal version through [[ChinaCorporateAntiInvolution]]. The episode says medical procurement, new-energy vehicles, batteries, and photovoltaics had become visible enough by late 2024 that central policy language turned toward preventing "内卷式恶性竞争" and regulating both local governments and enterprise behavior.

[[vol-115-quanqiu-hongguan-he-ziben-shichang-2025-zhanwang-duanqi-wenti-bu-jiejue-jiu-meiyou-zhongqi-he-changqi-le-lrfl2e5nuf7-ubq-shybqytjhuhx]] adds a sequencing caveat: supply-side clearing and [[NewQualityProductiveForces]] need [[ShortTermDemandBeforeLongTermReform|short-term demand repair]] to become investable at the market level. The source's A/H view therefore watches whether policy support can move from valuation repair to PPI and earnings recovery.

## Key Claims
- High production volume is not automatically good for shareholders when price and profit per unit fall.
- A supply-driven economy can generate activity, capacity, and flow while weakening company earnings.
- Policy may work by changing supply structure and forcing tail firms out, not only by boosting demand.
- The concept connects macro data to [[NewOrderAssetPricing]] because investors must ask who actually captures value after supply clears.
- The vol.111 source adds that supply-side clearing can become a political and diplomatic issue once price wars meet trade backlash and strategic-industry anxiety abroad.
- Vol.115 adds that supply repair is only one side of the 2025 equity thesis; without demand, price, and profit confirmation, policy optimism can remain a valuation trade.
