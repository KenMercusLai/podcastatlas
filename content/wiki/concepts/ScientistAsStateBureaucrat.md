# Scientist As State Bureaucrat

Scientist as state bureaucrat is the source's frame for [[IsaacNewton|Isaac Newton / 牛顿]] at the [[RoyalMint|Royal Mint]]. In [[26-niudun-yu-weibi-zhizaozhe-keneng-shi-sineipu-de-yuanxing-580065317]], Newton does not simply apply abstract intelligence to policy; he studies horses, machines, workshops, wages, procurement, accounts, worker quality, dies, and production rhythm during the [[GreatRecoinage]].

The concept matters because it joins scientific habits to administrative work without romanticizing bureaucracy. Measurement, calculation, and system inspection can improve production, but state service also pulls Newton into coercion, informants, courts, and reputation warfare against [[WilliamChaloner|William Chaloner]].
