## Definition

实力支撑的冲突降温 / strength-backed de-escalation is the movement from confrontation toward an acceptable exit after demonstrated capacity establishes boundaries and direct communication makes the cost and timing of continued escalation legible.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1133-sunquan-rensheng-zui-xiaosa-de-zhanyi-ruxukou-zhi-zhan-lrkz2p2fvh2fpw5owjdj7xg_dc8g|Hanji 1133]] grounds the concept in the [[RuxukouCampaign213LateHan|213 CE Ruxukou campaign]]. [[SunQuan|孙权]] does not ask for peace from an undefended position: Jiangdong first sustains resistance through the river line and naval readiness. His reported letter then combines useful warning about rising water with an explicit statement that [[CaoCao|曹操]] remains an enemy.

Cao Cao's withdrawal completes the mechanism in the episode's interpretation. The signal works not because candor automatically creates trust, but because observable conditions support it: continued attack is becoming less favorable, Sun Quan has shown he can resist, and leaving preserves Cao Cao's force. De-escalation is therefore neither appeasement nor reconciliation. It is a bounded decision to stop increasing losses while the underlying rivalry remains unresolved.

## Key Claims

- De-escalation becomes more credible when boundaries are supported by demonstrated capacity rather than reassurance alone.
- Direct adversarial language can coexist with useful information and a practical exit path.
- An opponent can be trustworthy about a shared constraint without becoming a friend or abandoning hostile interests.
- Changing terrain, time, and expected return should be reassessed instead of subordinated to face or sunk cost.
- Withdrawal can preserve long-term capability even when it concedes the immediate objective.
- A temporary stop does not resolve the conflict's underlying cause or guarantee durable peace.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept rests on one episode's interpretation of a transmitted historical scene. A threat can harden conflict instead of lowering it; information can be deceptive; and withdrawal may reflect logistics, command limits, politics, or unrelated constraints rather than the opponent's message. The source does not independently establish the exact letter, the actors' private reasoning, or a causal sequence from candor to trust to withdrawal. Modern workplace and business analogies should therefore be treated as bounded heuristics, not a rule that force or blunt language reliably produces settlement.

## What Changed

- Created the concept from Hanji 1133's combination of credible resistance, direct warning, and condition-sensitive withdrawal.
