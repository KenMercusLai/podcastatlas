# Tian Ji Horse Racing / 田忌赛马

Tian Ji horse racing / 田忌赛马 is the resource-allocation pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-16-sunbin-pangjuan-enyuan-jiuge-guiling-zhi-zhan-lryhzkc1-xsvfjhvicu8yxa9d2jr]] uses to introduce [[SunBin|孙膑]]'s strategic mind. [[TianJi|田忌]] races horses against Qi nobles; Sun Bin observes that both sides have upper, middle, and lower grades of horses, then arranges Tian Ji's lower horse against the opponent's upper, upper against middle, and middle against lower.

The point is not clever gambling alone. The source reads the story as a general method: classify unequal resources, stop trying to win every local exchange, and sequence losses and wins so the total match is favorable. That same logic later scales into [[EncirclingWeiToRescueZhao|围魏救赵]], where Qi does not meet Wei at the obvious point of strength.

## Key Claims
- A local loss can be rational when it buys two higher-value wins.
- Strategy begins with comparing relative tiers rather than treating all resources as interchangeable.
- The method is useful only when the decision-maker can define the real contest as the series, not the single matchup.
- The episode uses the story as a miniature version of Sun Bin's later battlefield thinking.
