# Host City Supporter Sponsorship

Host City Supporter sponsorship is the 2026 [[FIFAWorldCup|FIFA World Cup]] local-sponsor layer described in [[vol-267-ruhe-xianchang-kan-shijiebei-bing-da-kui-yi-bi-feat-yingyan-shijian-1002986145]]. Unlike [[FIFA]] global sponsorship, it is sold city by city and helps local host committees fund [[WorldCupHostCityEconomics]].

The source says each host city can generally sign up to ten local sponsors, with New York approved for a larger set. These sponsors cannot compete with FIFA's global sponsors and do not receive stadium exposure, so their value lies mainly in local event presence, hospitality, ticket packages, suites, VIP experiences, fan-zone interaction, and association with the city's World Cup project.

## Key Claims
- Local sponsorship partly compensates for the fact that cities bear costs while FIFA holds the biggest rights.
- Category restrictions protect global sponsors, limiting how much local sponsor inventory cities can sell.
- Sponsor value is closer to [[CorporateHospitalityPlatform]] and civic relationship building than pure mass advertising.
- The local sponsor layer turns the tournament into a more city-specific commercial project, not only a national or FIFA-controlled event.
