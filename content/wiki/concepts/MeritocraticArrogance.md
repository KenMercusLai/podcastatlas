# Meritocratic Arrogance / 绩优主义傲慢

Meritocratic arrogance / 绩优主义傲慢 is the present-day status posture criticized in [[37-zhishang-ceshi-qingwen-ni-shi-zhili-poluomen-ma-608020679]]. The episode connects "智力婆罗门" jokes, school labels, elite credentials, specialized vocabulary, and high-IQ self-image to the older history of [[PsychometricSocialSorting|psychometric social sorting]].

The source does not treat ability or achievement as imaginary. Its objection is moral: when a person uses score, degree, job, reading, or jargon to feel above others, ability becomes contempt. The hosts' closing "check your privilege" argument connects the concept to [[RationalHumility]] and [[SocialComparisonPressure]] rather than to anti-effort cynicism.

## Key Claims
- Achievement can become a status weapon even when it began as study, discipline, or talent.
- Intellectual advantage should increase responsibility to explain, listen, and remain corrigible, not license contempt.
- "智力种姓" is dangerous because it makes a measured or inferred ability difference look like a stable human rank.
- The repair path is not pretending differences do not exist; it is refusing to convert difference into dignity hierarchy.
