# Internal Marketization Limit / 企业内部市场化边界

Internal marketization limit is [[79-gewei-lingdao-danfan-zan-xuedian-boyilun-690004262]]'s boundary on importing market competition into firm interiors. The episode uses [[HaierGroup|Haier]]'s [[RenDanHeYi|人单合一]] as the concrete case and asks why firms would exist if market-like relations were always more efficient.

The concept's core claim is transaction-cost logic. Firms can coordinate tasks, protect secrets, manage incomplete contracts, and avoid repeated bargaining in ways that external markets cannot always do cheaply.

## Key Claims
- Market mechanisms are useful in some rigid internal systems, especially when they restore initiative and accountability.
- Over-marketization can damage cooperation when teammates become transactional counterparties rather than shared-goal collaborators.
- R&D, design, contract management, and customer relationships often depend on knowledge that is hard to price or outsource cleanly.
- The boundary depends on where hierarchy lowers transaction costs more than it suppresses useful market feedback.
