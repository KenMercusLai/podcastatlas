# Teacher AI Augmentation

## Definition
Teacher AI augmentation is the use of AI to support teachers with planning, explanation, differentiation, feedback, and classroom preparation while keeping human teaching relationships, judgment, motivation, and responsibility central.

## Current Synthesis
The concept began as a practical K-12 workflow frame: AI can draft lesson-plan materials, differentiated examples, and alternative explanations, but a teacher still decides what fits students. The Fei-Fei Li episode broadens the concept into a public-policy boundary. Teachers should not be forgotten while technologists, policymakers, and investors debate AI; they need hands-on literacy, support for cheating concerns, and tools designed to preserve student agency rather than offload the core work of learning.

## Key Claims
- AI can reduce teacher preparation burden by drafting materials, examples, explanations, and differentiated language.
- Teacher judgment remains necessary because students need context, motivation, care, and relationship.
- Teacher AI literacy is a prerequisite for useful augmentation, especially when students already use AI outside formal school rules.
- Cheating concerns are real but incomplete; support and redesign are more durable than denial or panic.
- AI should help teachers make students more active learners, not remove the productive struggle that builds understanding.

## Evidence
- Workflow evidence: [[ep-9-chatgpt-and-education-systems]] says [[ChatGPT]] can help with lesson plans, objectives, tone shifts, differentiated paragraphs, math explanations, and teacher preparation.
- Human element evidence: [[ep-9-chatgpt-and-education-systems]] argues that AI should assist teachers rather than replace the human element of teaching.
- Literacy evidence: [[ep-9-chatgpt-and-education-systems]] frames AI literacy as important for all teachers because students already live around tools such as Google, Siri, Alexa, and ChatGPT.
- Policy support evidence: [[using-ai-to-increase-your-intelligence-enrich-humanity-dr-fei-fei-li-scim5701398040]] says teachers should be shown the technology, supported around cheating concerns, and treated as central to society.
- Student-agency evidence: [[using-ai-to-increase-your-intelligence-enrich-humanity-dr-fei-fei-li-scim5701398040]] warns that AI tools should not take away young people's agency or motivation to learn.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The sources do not support replacing teachers with AI systems. Augmentation can become harmful when it increases shortcut behavior, hides errors, weakens classroom relationships, or leaves teachers without time and institutional support to evaluate outputs.
