## Definition

太岁与犯太岁信仰 / Tai Sui belief is the Chinese folk-belief pattern that links year, zodiac, direction, deity or stellar force, and personal fortune-risk. In this wiki it is currently grounded by [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-602-tanxun-guoxue-zhihui-pojie-2024nian-fantaisui-zhi-mi-lvrwnpanicgjpf43cbmveigtxqj|Hanji 602]], which moves from a Han court lodging decision to modern 2024 "犯太岁" advice.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 602 makes Tai Sui useful to the wiki because it bridges elite historical ritual and contemporary folk practice. The historical scene is not a household superstition anecdote: [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]'s court arranges a Xiongnu chanyu's lodging around the phrase "太岁在申，厌伏南方," then hides that rationale under diplomatic respect language. The guest's displeasure after learning the reason shows that astrological avoidance could conflict with interstate dignity.

The modern half of the episode treats Tai Sui as a layered practice rather than a single doctrine. It includes zodiac animals, 2024's claimed southeast direction, household and office placement, cleanliness, red clothing, jewelry, deity images, and auspicious objects. The host does not fully endorse these as predictive technology; the stable contribution is a bounded cultural account plus a practical turn toward environmental clearing and gentle conduct.

The strongest synthesis is therefore behavioral, not magical. The episode treats "犯太岁" as a high-risk-year frame that can be converted into vigilance, low profile, fewer disputes, compassion, tolerance, and time-for-space handling of crises. The wiki keeps the metaphysical details source-scoped while preserving the cultural fact that the belief shapes decisions, emotions, and ritualized risk management.

## Key Claims

- Tai Sui belief can shape political and diplomatic procedure, not only private household custom.
- The belief combines zodiac, direction, deity or stellar force, fortune-risk, and ritual remedy into one layered practice.
- Modern remedy lists in this source are culturally meaningful but not validated as causal claims.
- The episode's durable advice converts Tai Sui anxiety into self-command: low profile, cleanliness, gentleness, and conflict avoidance.
- Tai Sui practice belongs near Chinese folk-religion layering because it tolerates mixed ritual, symbolic, spatial, and ethical explanations.

## Evidence

- Political-diplomatic procedure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-602-tanxun-guoxue-zhihui-pojie-2024nian-fantaisui-zhi-mi-lvrwnpanicgjpf43cbmveigtxqj|Hanji 602]] says Han Aidi lodged the Xiongnu chanyu in Shanglinyuan's Putao Palace because "太岁在申，厌伏南方," while publicly explaining it as respect.
- Layered practice: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-602-tanxun-guoxue-zhihui-pojie-2024nian-fantaisui-zhi-mi-lvrwnpanicgjpf43cbmveigtxqj|Hanji 602]] links Tai Sui to zodiac, birth timing, auspicious and inauspicious spirits, year direction, furniture placement, cleaning, red clothing, transfer beads, deity images, pixiu, and vehicle or home ornaments.
- Source-scoped modern claims: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-602-tanxun-guoxue-zhihui-pojie-2024nian-fantaisui-zhi-mi-lvrwnpanicgjpf43cbmveigtxqj|Hanji 602]] names 2024 as a southeast-direction Tai Sui year and lists dragon, dog, rabbit, and ox as offending zodiac animals, but the source note treats these as traditional or networked claims rather than verified rules.
- Behavioral conversion: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-602-tanxun-guoxue-zhihui-pojie-2024nian-fantaisui-zhi-mi-lvrwnpanicgjpf43cbmveigtxqj|Hanji 602]] emphasizes cleaning, low profile, compassion, tolerance, gentle speech, reduced disputes, and delaying unresolvable crises.
- Folk-religion fit: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-602-tanxun-guoxue-zhihui-pojie-2024nian-fantaisui-zhi-mi-lvrwnpanicgjpf43cbmveigtxqj|Hanji 602]] shows ritual objects, space, zodiac, morality, and mental state coexisting without a single systematic theology.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The wiki does not treat Tai Sui direction, zodiac-offense lists, ornaments, red clothing, deity images, or directional adjustments as empirically verified. Hanji 602 is a popular history-and-life-advice episode, not a specialist study of Chinese astrology or religion. The exact classical development of Tai Sui, the relationship between Tai Sui deity cult and astronomical Jupiter/Sui cycles, and regional ritual variations remain outside the current evidence.
