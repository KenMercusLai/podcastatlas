# Mindset Effects on Physiology

## Definition
Mindset effects on physiology are changes in biological or health-related outcomes associated with the interpretive frame through which a person understands the same food, activity, or intervention.

## Current Synthesis
The episode distinguishes mindset effects from a narrow inert-placebo model by emphasizing specific information about what an experience means. In the studies attributed to [[LeahCrum]], identical milkshakes labeled as indulgent or sensible were associated with different ghrelin and satiety responses, while hotel workers told that their physical labor counted as exercise later showed changes in several health metrics. The current evidence supports interaction between meaning, behavior, and physiology, not the claim that framing erases material inputs.

## Key Claims
- Informational framing can change the expected meaning of an otherwise similar physical input.
- Food beliefs may interact with hunger-related hormonal and satiety responses.
- Recognizing occupational activity as exercise may alter health behavior, appraisal, or measured outcomes.
- Mindset is richer than a simple positive thought because it organizes prior knowledge, context, and expectation.
- Material nutrition, activity, disease, and treatment remain causally important even when mindset contributes.

## Evidence
### Milkshake framing
- [[how-placebo-effects-work-to-change-our-biology-psychology-scim2208611274]] reports steeper ghrelin reduction and different satiety after an identical drink was described as indulgent rather than sensible.

### Occupational exercise framing
- [[how-placebo-effects-work-to-change-our-biology-psychology-scim2208611274]] reports reduced blood pressure, resting heart rate, and body weight after hotel workers were told their daily work qualified as exercise.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not resolve whether attention, subsequent behavior, reporting, statistical variation, or other mediators explain each outcome. Full protocols, effect sizes, replications, and durability are absent. These findings do not justify misleading labels or treating belief as a substitute for nutrition, exercise, or medical care.

## What Changed
- Created a bounded synthesis linking meaning and expectation to food- and activity-related outcomes.
