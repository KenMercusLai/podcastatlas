# Mindset Effects on Physiology

## Definition
Mindset effects on physiology are changes in experience, behavior, biological response, or health-related outcomes associated with the interpretive frame through which a person understands food, activity, stress, feedback, symptoms, treatment, or time.

## Current Synthesis
Across the bounded evidence, mindset is a domain-specific assumption that organizes expectation, attention, explanation, and action rather than generic optimism. Identical-shake labeling is associated with different ghrelin responses, while hotel workers informed that their labor met exercise guidelines later showed changes in health measures. Repeated coverage of those studies broadens provenance but is not independent replication.

The Crum interview extends the framework to false sleep feedback, workplace stress, and accurate framing during active allergy treatment. The Langer interview independently retells the occupational-activity example and adds reports that functioning followed perceived sleep, vision changed under altered eye-chart expectations, and wound healing tracked manipulated clock time. These additions broaden the candidate outcomes and make perceived temporal context especially salient, but they do not establish one mechanism or show that interpretation overrides material inputs. Nutrition, activity, sleep, disease, treatment, social conditions, measurement, and behavior remain joint causes.

## Key Claims
- Mindsets are domain-specific assumptions that organize expectation, attention, explanation, motivation, and behavior.
- Informational framing can change responses to similar food, activity, treatment, or feedback in the reported studies.
- Repeated hotel-worker and milkshake accounts are overlapping provenance, not multiple independent replications.
- Sleep, vision, symptoms, and elapsed-time expectations are plausible extensions of the framework but currently remain source-scoped here.
- Stress and treatment framing can change anxiety, symptom interpretation, engagement, and possibly physiological pathways without making adversity or side effects harmless.
- Material conditions and active care remain causally important; mindset is neither an unrestricted mechanism nor a substitute treatment.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source notes do not supply enough protocol, sample-size, effect-size, replication, mediator, or durability detail to unify these examples under one mechanism. Attention, subsequent behavior, demand effects, reporting, anxiety, learned prediction, statistical variation, and physiological pathways may contribute differently. Symptom relief or changed performance need not mean correction of underlying disease. These findings do not justify deceptive labeling, withholding information, patient blame, unsafe exposure, or replacing nutrition, exercise, sleep, public-health measures, or medical care with belief.

## What Changed
- Added perceived time, vision expectation, and symptom variability as source-scoped extensions beyond food, activity, stress, and treatment framing.
- Strengthened the judgment that the examples may involve different mechanisms and cannot yet support unrestricted mind-over-body claims.
