# PTSD Homecoming Narrative / 创伤版回家叙事

## Definition

PTSD homecoming narrative is the pattern where a war return story turns from physical arrival into a question of whether the survivor can live with memory, guilt, violence, and damaged worthiness.

## Current Synthesis

The episode reads Nolan's [[TheOdyssey]] as a veteran-homecoming film rather than only an adventure epic. In this version, [[Odysseus]]' difficulty is not just reaching [[Ithaca]]; he has to face the [[TrojanHorse]], dead comrades, survivor guilt, and the feeling that he no longer deserves [[Penelope]] or household restoration. Nostos becomes moral and psychological before it becomes spatial.

## Key Claims

- Homecoming after war can fail internally even when the road home is found.
- Survivor guilt turns remembered adventures into self-judgment rather than only heroic trial.
- A veteran protagonist may experience safety, intimacy, and family as undeserved or unreachable.
- Mythic episodes can represent trauma responses when memory is fragmented, delayed, or dreamlike.
- The frame is interpretive, not diagnostic: ancient epic and modern PTSD vocabulary should remain distinct.

## Evidence

- Worthiness problem: [[dang-xila-lishi-meiguo-zhengzhi-nuolan-meixue-zai-aodesai-li-da-qilai-gkwrijiov2xkbtdknqthj-ue]] says the film's Odysseus feels he may not deserve to return home.
- Survivor guilt: [[dang-xila-lishi-meiguo-zhengzhi-nuolan-meixue-zai-aodesai-li-da-qilai-gkwrijiov2xkbtdknqthj-ue]] reads Scylla, the whirlpool, Helios' cattle, and crew losses as repeated self-judgment.
- Trojan Horse as original wound: [[dang-xila-lishi-meiguo-zhengzhi-nuolan-meixue-zai-aodesai-li-da-qilai-gkwrijiov2xkbtdknqthj-ue]] treats the Trojan Horse as Odysseus' central guilt and compares it to modern destructive technology.
- Diagnostic boundary: [[dang-xila-lishi-meiguo-zhengzhi-nuolan-meixue-zai-aodesai-li-da-qilai-gkwrijiov2xkbtdknqthj-ue]] explicitly notes that modern PTSD is not an ancient Greek category even though war injury is cross-temporal.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept should not diagnose Homeric characters or turn every difficult return into PTSD. It names a modern adaptation and film-reading strategy, and its claims remain bounded to this episode's interpretation of Nolan's film.

## What Changed

- Created the concept to distinguish Nolan's trauma-centered homecoming from the existing Homeric nostos and recognition pages.
