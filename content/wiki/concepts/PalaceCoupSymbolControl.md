## Definition

Palace Coup Symbol Control is the pattern where a court coup succeeds by capturing the practical signs and channels of authority: palace entry, shangshu communication, gates, armed guards, imperial tallies, seals, and the target's office insignia.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 848 gives the concept a clear late-Han case. [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] does not defeat [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] by open battlefield superiority; he wins by acting first inside the palace, neutralizing Liang's monitor, convening the shangshu channel, guarding the palace offices, controlling tallies and seals, and sending troops before Liang can turn his apparent network into command.

The concept's main claim is that symbols are not merely decorative. In this coup, whoever holds access to the palace, the documents, the seals, the tallies, and the guards can make authority operational. Liang's status as general-in-chief collapses when the palace-centered command chain makes that title unusable and then physically removes his seal.

## Key Claims

- A palace coup is decided by timing and channel control before formal titles can be mobilized.
- Arresting the target's palace monitor prevents the target from reading or disrupting the operation early.
- Controlling shangshu offices, palace gates, seals, and tallies turns imperial will into executable command.
- Removing the target's office seal converts political defeat into visible loss of legitimate authority.
- Armed force matters most when it is synchronized with symbolic control, so the target cannot contest which commands are valid.
- Public settlement after the coup uses confiscation, tax remission, title demotion, and new rewards to make the victory legible.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

This concept does not claim that all coups are purely symbolic or that military force is secondary in every context. Hanji 848 shows a specific palace-centered pattern: armed pressure works because the emperor's side controls the channels that make force legitimate and coordinated. The source also does not fully reconstruct Liang Ji's possible counterforce; it records how quickly his network fails once the palace sequence begins.

## What Changed
