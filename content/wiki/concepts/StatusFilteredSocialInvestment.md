## Definition

身份过滤式社交投资 / Status-filtered social investment is the pattern where people allocate attention, warmth, help, invitations, or patience according to another person's current or expected future status.

## Current Synthesis

The source develops the concept through stand-up comedy. [[KuaigeStandup|快哥]] describes a small early industry where he could help many lower-status performers, then a later scene where sudden warmth from people who had previously excluded him felt false after he became more visible. The episode calls this both upward networking and downward betting: a person may court established status while also supporting newcomers who might later matter.

The concept is not limited to cynical opportunism. Early help can be generous, and noticing talent before status arrives can be socially valuable. The problem appears when the filter becomes too obvious, retroactively humiliates the person who was ignored, or converts relationship into pure expected return.

## Key Claims

- Social investment often follows current status, but can also follow guesses about future status.
- Helping low-status newcomers is morally different from treating them only as speculative assets, though the behaviors can look similar from outside.
- Sudden warmth after someone gains fame can feel insulting because it exposes earlier exclusion.
- Industry growth makes equal attention harder, turning personal generosity into a resource-allocation problem.
- The same behavior can be interpreted as mentorship, networking, opportunism, or snobbery depending on memory, timing, and power asymmetry.

- Upward and downward pattern: [[luoyonghao-de-x-zilukou-women-neng-huodao-jintian-duokui-le-zuchuan-de-shiliyan-lhkqsz8pkcblnmaebsz1m50tysos]] explicitly discusses "向上社交" and "向下押宝" in the stand-up industry.
- Early help: [[luoyonghao-de-x-zilukou-women-neng-huodao-jintian-duokui-le-zuchuan-de-shiliyan-lhkqsz8pkcblnmaebsz1m50tysos]] has Kuaige recall helping or feeding less successful performers during earlier low-income conditions.
- Post-fame warmth: [[luoyonghao-de-x-zilukou-women-neng-huodao-jintian-duokui-le-zuchuan-de-shiliyan-lhkqsz8pkcblnmaebsz1m50tysos]] has Kuaige describe a previously excluding club boss suddenly using warmer titles after he became popular.
- Low-sensitivity contrast: [[luoyonghao-de-x-zilukou-women-neng-huodao-jintian-duokui-le-zuchuan-de-shiliyan-lhkqsz8pkcblnmaebsz1m50tysos]] has [[HulanStandup|呼兰]] say he is less inclined to spend energy interpreting these shifts.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The evidence is source-bounded to the comedy-industry stories in one live panel. It should not be treated as a complete account of Chinese stand-up career mobility, club hiring, or comedy mentorship.
