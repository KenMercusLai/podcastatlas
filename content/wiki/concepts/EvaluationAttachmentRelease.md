# Evaluation Attachment Release / 放下评价执念

## Definition
Evaluation attachment release / 放下评价执念 is the practice of not building self-worth from praise, criticism, perceived neglect, friendship ranking, or another person's failure to meet an imagined script.

## Current Synthesis
The 天真不天真 episode closes by making evaluation lighter from several directions. The host describes once treating a friend's absence from a birthday event as evidence of disrespect serious enough to justify blocking the friend for two and a half years. Later, she apologizes and reads that apology partly as a repair toward herself. Near the end, she also says that if someone says she is bad, perhaps they are right, and if someone says she is good, she does not need to take that too seriously either.

This concept gathers the relationship, self-worth, and public-evaluation material without recommending passivity. The first source separates honest need expression from control, apology from self-abasement, and criticism from identity collapse.

Vol.58 adds a behavioral route when detachment is not yet available. A small-business owner can distinguish evaluators with customer, expertise, or opportunity relevance from familiar observers whose imagined opinion has no operational role; begin through a psychologically easier channel; describe the customer, problem, and solution; and run a 30-day action experiment. The practical shift is not to wait for immunity from shame, but to collect real consequences while judging the action and business separately from personal worth.

## Key Claims
- Praise and criticism are too unstable to carry identity.
- Hurt often intensifies when the self turns another person's behavior into a global judgment of one's own importance.
- Blocking, punishment, and withdrawal can feel like self-protection while actually preserving attachment to the injury.
- Apology can repair a relationship and also revise the story one has been telling about oneself.
- Letting go of evaluation does not require suppressing needs; it requires expressing them without forcing a scripted response.
- Public criticism becomes less controlling when it is treated as partial information rather than final truth.
- Evaluation loses force when its practical relevance is tested and visible action supplies evidence stronger than imagined reaction.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The sources do not erase legitimate harm, accountability, compatibility judgment, or market feedback. Some criticism deserves correction, some relationship failures justify distance, and repeated customer nonresponse can require a changed offer or strategy. Lower-friction promotion can enable learning, but permanent avoidance can also keep the feared evaluation untested. The concept is about loosening identity-level attachment, not ignoring evidence or tolerating repeated disrespect.

## What Changed
- Extended release from a reflective stance into an action-and-evidence practice.
- Added evaluator relevance, lower-friction channels, and time-bounded promotion experiments.
- Distinguished an unsuccessful action or offer from a global verdict on personal worth.
