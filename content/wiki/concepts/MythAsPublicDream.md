# Myth As Public Dream / 神话作为公共的梦

[[MythAsPublicDream|Myth as public dream / 神话作为公共的梦]] is the episode's shorthand for [[JosephCampbell|Joseph Campbell / 约瑟夫·坎贝尔]]'s psychologically inflected myth reading. In [[29-qianmian-yingxiong-edipusi-zhengzai-xiaban-ji-ditie-590112891]], myths are treated as outward, shared dream material, while private dreams can be read as miniature myths.

The concept connects Campbell to [[CarlJung|Jung]], [[CollectiveUnconscious|collective unconscious]], and [[SigmundFreud|Freud]]. It helps explain why mothers, fathers, forbidden desire, threshold spaces, monsters, death, rebirth, and return recur with strong emotional force. The source also marks the risk: dream reading can illuminate story feeling, but it becomes [[InterpretationAndOverinterpretation]] when every similarity is promoted into proof.

## Key Claims
- Myth and dream are read as related symbolic forms, not as identical evidence.
- The frame makes [[Monomyth|单一神话]] psychologically plausible without proving it historically.
- The episode uses the idea as a literary and cultural heuristic rather than a scientific demonstration.
