# Evolutionary Misrecognition / 进化式误认

## Definition
Evolutionary misrecognition is E46's synthesis that the mind's survival-oriented capacities also create systematic ways of not seeing reality or other people directly. Valuation, wanting, simulation, mentalizing, and language are adaptive breakthroughs, but each adds a layer of possible misperception.

## Current Synthesis
Meng Yan rereads [[ABriefHistoryOfIntelligence]] as a natural history of "discriminating mind" and "ignorance". The claim is not that evolution made cognition defective in a simple sense. The same mechanisms that let organisms move, learn, plan, cooperate, and transmit culture also make them attach positive or negative signs, chase wanting beyond liking, replay imagined futures, fill others with self-derived models, and compress complexity into names. Human suffering and interpersonal misrecognition arise because those mechanisms are working as designed for survival rather than for transparent seeing.

## Key Claims
- "Good" and "bad" begin as organism-relative action signals, not intrinsic properties of the world.
- Reinforcement learning can produce wanting without durable satisfaction.
- Simulation enables planning and regret but also anxiety over futures that have not occurred.
- Mentalizing supports social life while making others vulnerable to projection.
- Language supports civilization while compressing people and events into rigid labels.
- The Buddhist vocabulary of ignorance becomes an overlay of these cognitive layers rather than only a moral accusation.

## Evidence
- Five-layer frame: [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] explicitly maps intelligence breakthroughs from movement through language and treats each as both gift and suffering.
- Film application: [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] applies the layers to Hyun-tae's failure to see In-chul in [[GoodFriendsFilm|《好朋友们》]].
- Buddhist interpretation: [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] connects the sequence to 分别心, 空, 渴爱, and 无明 while keeping the explanation grounded in evolved cognition.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is an interpretive synthesis, not a settled neuroscience proof of Buddhist doctrine. It uses evolutionary cognition to explain why misrecognition is structurally likely, but it does not claim that every mistake is biologically determined or impossible to revise.
