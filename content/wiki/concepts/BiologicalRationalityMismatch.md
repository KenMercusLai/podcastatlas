# Biological Rationality Mismatch

## Definition
Biological rationality mismatch is the gap between the environments and constraints for which decision systems were shaped and the demands imposed by modern information, market, food, and social environments.

## Current Synthesis
Bounded rationality asks what limited brains and energy budgets can compute. Biological rationality adds a second question: whether a decision rule fits the ecology in which it evolved. The episode argues that mechanisms suited to small, face-to-face groups, slower change, physical activity, natural food, and lower inequality can misfire amid global markets, mass social exposure, rapid technological change, inactivity, and processed abundance.

## Key Claims
- Rationality must be judged under information-processing and energy constraints.
- A decision rule can be adaptive in one ecology and error-prone in another.
- Modern environments expose brains to scale, speed, abstraction, and abundance outside ancestral ranges.
- Social sensitivity remains useful but can produce tribalism, status capture, or market imitation at mass scale.
- Environmental redesign can sometimes outperform demands for individual willpower.

## Evidence
- Conceptual distinction - [[how-to-make-better-decisions-dr-michael-platt-scim9606820714]] separates bounded rationality from biological rationality.
- Environmental contrast - [[how-to-make-better-decisions-dr-michael-platt-scim9606820714]] contrasts small face-to-face groups and slower change with money, markets, mass exposure, inactivity, and processed food.
- Applied mechanisms - [[how-to-make-better-decisions-dr-michael-platt-scim9606820714]] connects mismatch to attention switching, group identity, brand attachment, and copying in markets.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Evolutionary mismatch is an organizing hypothesis, not evidence that every modern preference is maladaptive or that ancestral conditions were uniform, healthy, peaceful, or desirable. Cultural learning, institutions, and deliberate tools can extend rather than merely distort biological capacities.

- Created an ecological-fit framework joining bounded cognition with modern-environment mismatch.
