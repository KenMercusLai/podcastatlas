# Resume Gap Stigma / 简历空白污名

Resume gap stigma is the hiring suspicion attached to visible time away from ordinary employment. In [[ep257-bu-shangban-yi-chang-taoli-yu-xunzhao-de-fuza-maoxian-li-wflyd48dmxvgersphxfp-gnng]], [[MiaFreelancer|Mia]] describes a friend who spent two years in serious medical treatment but felt unable to tell HR the real reason, instead packaging the gap as entrepreneurship.

The concept extends [[UnemploymentStigma]] and [[LongTermUnemploymentPenalty]]. A gap can come from illness, recovery, care work, burnout, study, exploration, layoffs, failed job search, or deliberate rest, but [[OpaqueHiringMarket|opaque hiring]] lets employers read the blank period as hidden defect, instability, low motivation, or poor fit. The result is a pressure to create a respectable transition story even when the true story is reasonable and human.

## Key Claims
- Resume gaps become risky because employers often treat missing continuity as evidence rather than as an information gap.
- Health treatment and burnout recovery are especially hard to narrate because honesty may trigger doubts about future reliability.
- Entrepreneurship can become a socially acceptable cover story, but that cover also reinforces the idea that rest, treatment, or unemployment need justification.
- The stigma makes "gap year" culturally uneven: some institutions and families can frame a pause as growth, while hiring screens may still read it as risk.
- Gap stigma connects personal shame to market mechanics; the individual is managing both self-worth and recruiter inference.
