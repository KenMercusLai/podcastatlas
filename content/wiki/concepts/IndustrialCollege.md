# Industrial College / 产业学院

Industrial college / 产业学院 is the campus-based enterprise cooperation form discussed in [[ep241-xiaoqi-hezuo-shi-xinyidai-de-tiefanwan-ma-lnmpsbvoqbexeardxyx88hywufig]]. The episode's main example is [[LiAuto|理想汽车]]'s industrial college inside [[ChangzhouIndustrialVocationalTechnicalCollege|常州工业职业技术学院]], where vehicles, training facilities, company training, and student learning share a site.

The concept sits between a light partnership and a full [[DualSystemVocationalEducation|双元制]] program. It can anchor students in a visible industry and give companies earlier access to talent, but its depth depends on whether the enterprise influences curriculum, evaluation, teacher training, equipment updates, and real practice rather than only providing branding.

## Key Claims
- Industrial colleges work best when they are tied to a real local industry cluster rather than an abstract major name.
- Campus presence can make enterprise culture and work pathways visible before students reach final-year internships.
- The model can support [[AIProgramAdjustment|program adjustment]] when companies quickly add short courses or micro-specializations around new skills.
- It can become shallow if the company withdraws, the school changes strategy, or the industry cycle weakens.
