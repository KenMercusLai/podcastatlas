# Mencian Benevolent Government / 孟子仁政

Mencian benevolent government / 孟子仁政 is [[30-mengzi-zhanguo-fenqing-de-chizi-zhixin-592494138]]'s nameable political program behind [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]]'s exchanges with [[LiangHuiWang|梁惠王]] and [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]]. It includes reducing harshness, caring for livelihood, preventing starvation, extending compassion, and building a basis on which ordinary people can live steadily enough to act morally.

The source treats renzheng as demanding rather than sentimental. Liang Hui Wang's relief efforts are not dismissed, but Mencius says they remain "fifty steps" rather than full reform. Qi Xuan Wang's compassion for a sacrificial ox is accepted as real, but the point is to extend that "不忍之心" to the people instead of leaving it as isolated feeling.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-45-yi-shi-wei-jian-junwang-daodi-gai-bu-gai-sharen-chengxing-lo3cok7cfwzrxesp-i8miz1fc1db]] adds a unification-facing version of renzheng. In Mencius's answer to [[WeiXiangwang|魏襄王]], benevolent rule is not merely local welfare; it is what makes people under violent regimes willing to return or submit without terror. The episode therefore links renzheng to [[NonKillingUnification|不爱杀人者能一之]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-49-mengzi-yue-wo-jiao-ni-zhiguo-ni-que-ba-wo-dang-huaping-lhy14pbf9thcfdiaulcmf8dwcef8]] adds a post-conquest operational version. Mencius tells [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]] that if Qi wants to justify taking [[YanState|燕国]], it must stop plunder, release vulnerable captives, consult Yan's people, install a ruler, and withdraw. In this source, renzheng is not an attitude toward the people but a concrete occupation-governance test.

## Key Claims
- Benevolent government is not just emergency charity; it must reshape ordinary conditions.
- A ruler's partial kindness can be morally real while still politically insufficient.
- Compassion becomes political only when it is generalized beyond the visible case.
- The source links renzheng to [[ConstantPropertyConstantHeart|stable livelihood]] and [[WangdaoVsBadao|kingly rule]].
- Benevolent conquest requires immediate administrative repair; otherwise "rescue" becomes occupation and extraction.
