# Carrier-Bag Narrative / 载物袋叙事

## Definition
Carrier-bag narrative / 载物袋叙事 is a story form that gathers people, objects, memories, feelings, places, and knowledge into meaningful relation instead of organizing the work only around a hero, enemy, weapon, conflict, and final victory.

## Current Synthesis
The concept comes from the episode's reading of [[TheCarrierBagTheoryOfFiction]]. It does not mean dumping unrelated fragments into a work. A successful carrier-bag narrative behaves more like an ecosystem: its contents coexist, but they also exchange meaning, alter one another's significance, and let care, preservation, memory, and connection organize the reader's experience.

## Key Claims
- Carrier-bag narrative is an alternative to spear-like, arrow-like, or hero-victory plot structure.
- Conflict can appear inside a carrier-bag work, but conflict does not have to be the work's organizing principle.
- The form depends on relation; loose accumulation without reciprocal meaning is not enough.
- Chinese literary traditions supply strong examples of gathered, parallel, anecdotal, or atmosphere-heavy composition.
- The form can move beyond fiction into feminist theory, environmental politics, and nature writing.

## Evidence
Le Guin frame:
- [[episode-225-yidai-yilu-8212959555-438866|Episode 225: 一袋一录]] summarizes [[UrsulaKLeGuin|Le Guin]] as replacing spear/arrow metaphors with a bag that gathers characters, memories, emotions, and details into relation.

Chinese literary examples:
- [[episode-225-yidai-yilu-8212959555-438866|Episode 225: 一袋一录]] reads [[Shijing|《芣苢》]], anecdotal Chinese prose, [[RulinWaishi|《儒林外史》]], [[HongLouMeng|《红楼梦》]], 《呼兰河传》, and Wang Zengqi as evidence that weak plot can still support high literary structure.

Ecological extension:
- [[episode-225-yidai-yilu-8212959555-438866|Episode 225: 一袋一录]] says a true carrier bag is like an ecosystem, then extends the form through [[DonnaHaraway]], Colombian Mochila bags, rivers, and [[RobertMacfarlane]].

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept should not be used as a license for shapelessness. The source explicitly rejects treating carrier bag as a vague suitcase word and requires internal relations among gathered elements.

## What Changed
- Added carrier-bag narrative as a canonical narrative-theory concept.
- Added its distinction from hero/conflict structure.
- Added the source's ecological criterion for distinguishing meaningful relation from loose fragments.
