# Travel Agency Itinerary Operations

## Definition
Travel agency itinerary operations are the product, supplier, route, leader, and backup systems that turn a destination idea into a repeatable guided-trip experience.

## Current Synthesis
The source uses [[DaocaorenTravel|稻草人旅行]] to show that small-group travel work is not only selecting attractions. Route products require destination research, supplier negotiation, local experience testing, difficulty labeling, leader matching, timing adjustments, and emergency coverage.

The concept is especially visible during holidays. When National Day demand is high, capacity can be limited by trained leaders, restaurant reservations, hotel resources, and disruption response, not only by consumer willingness to pay.

## Key Claims
- Guided-trip quality depends on invisible preparation before the traveler sees the itinerary.
- Product research includes suppliers, local experiences, field scouting, route sequencing, and practical details.
- Leader preparation has to compensate for unfamiliar destinations through intensive reading, media review, point notes, and logistical rehearsal.
- New-route launches can require dual support from product staff and leaders.
- Capacity during peak holidays can be constrained by trained leader supply.
- Backup systems matter because holiday disruptions include illness, missed leaders, road closures, traffic, and accidents.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source describes one operator's practices and one leader's perspective. It does not prove that all travel agencies operate this way, nor does it compare Daocaoren's system with competing travel companies. Specific operational claims should remain source-scoped unless corroborated by additional sources.
