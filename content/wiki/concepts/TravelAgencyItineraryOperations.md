# Travel Agency Itinerary Operations

## Definition
Travel agency itinerary operations are the product, supplier, route, leader, and backup systems that turn a destination idea into a repeatable guided-trip experience.

## Current Synthesis
The sources show that small-group travel work is not only selecting attractions. [[DaocaorenTravel|稻草人旅行]] supplies the operating system: destination research, supplier negotiation, local experience testing, difficulty labeling, leader matching, timing adjustments, and emergency coverage. The [[NittanWuyu|日谈物语]] excerpt supplies a compact product-positioning example: a late-autumn Japan route avoids the conventional Kyoto foliage frame and instead organizes the experience around highlands, mountains, changing elevation, color progression, and a six-to-ten-person group.

The concept is especially visible during holidays. When National Day demand is high, capacity can be limited by trained leaders, restaurant reservations, hotel resources, and disruption response, not only by consumer willingness to pay.

## Key Claims
- Guided-trip quality depends on invisible preparation before the traveler sees the itinerary.
- Product research includes suppliers, local experiences, field scouting, route sequencing, and practical details.
- Leader preparation has to compensate for unfamiliar destinations through intensive reading, media review, point notes, and logistical rehearsal.
- New-route launches can require dual support from product staff and leaders.
- Capacity during peak holidays can be constrained by trained leader supply.
- Backup systems matter because holiday disruptions include illness, missed leaders, road closures, traffic, and accidents.
- A seasonal route can be differentiated by a legible natural pattern, such as following autumn color across elevation bands, rather than by adding more famous attractions.

- Leader preparation: [[ep386-shiyi-chuxing-bu-xiang-dang-dayuanzhong-zanmen-lai-tingting-zishen-lingdui-de-xiaoqiaosi-gkwrirwodgrhaeuitgtugmxp]] says 老宝 prepared nearly three weeks for Sicily using route material, stories, media, point details, and restroom information.

- Leader capacity: [[ep386-shiyi-chuxing-bu-xiang-dang-dayuanzhong-zanmen-lai-tingting-zishen-lingdui-de-xiaoqiaosi-gkwrirwodgrhaeuitgtugmxp]] says holiday group volume can be limited by how many leaders are available.
- Backup response: [[ep386-shiyi-chuxing-bu-xiang-dang-dayuanzhong-zanmen-lai-tingting-zishen-lingdui-de-xiaoqiaosi-gkwrirwodgrhaeuitgtugmxp]] describes senior leaders held in reserve during National Day to cover sudden problems.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The main source describes one operator's practices and one leader's perspective. The 日谈物语 excerpt is only a promotional announcement and does not identify its operator, destinations, suppliers, price, safety plan, or whether the route was delivered. Neither source proves that all travel agencies operate this way, and specific operational claims should remain source-scoped unless corroborated.

## What Changed
- Added elevation-driven foliage progression as a compact route-differentiation pattern.
