# Public AED Access Readiness / 公共AED可及性准备

## Definition
Public AED access readiness / 公共AED可及性准备 is the combination of device placement, visibility, reliable access, mapping, maintenance, retrieval time, and public familiarity that turns a nominally nearby automated external defibrillator into usable emergency infrastructure.

## Current Synthesis
[[vol-122-ni-zhidao-li-ni-zuijin-de-aed-zai-na-ma-buneng-zhishi-tongding-sitong-ranhou-buliaoliaozhi-han-xinfei-fusu-liucheng-aed-shiyong-jiangjie-loltrw1svirwsfkt_skd40mxq7d8]] separates owning or mapping AEDs from making them operationally available. A device can appear close yet remain slow to retrieve because it is indoors, on an unknown floor, behind access control, unavailable after hours, poorly signed, or unfamiliar to people nearby.

Readiness therefore begins before anyone collapses. People, venues, employers, schools, communities, and dispatch systems can learn exact locations, make routes and cabinets obvious, keep access dependable, and practice role assignment. During an arrest, this preparation joins rather than replaces [[CPRAEDResponseBoundary|CPR]] and emergency calling.

## Key Claims
- AED coverage should be evaluated by usable round-trip retrieval time, not device count or map distance alone.
- Indoor position, floor, signage, access control, opening hours, and cabinet visibility can make a nominally nearby AED unavailable in practice.
- AED maps are most valuable as preparedness tools used before an emergency, although dispatch integration can also help during a call.
- Public placement should favor locations that are easy to describe, find, and access across the venue's operating hours.
- Communities and organizations need both devices and people able to assign retrieval, calling, compression, and crowd-management roles.
- AED access complements continuous CPR when multiple responders are available; retrieval choices for a lone rescuer remain protocol- and situation-sensitive.

## Evidence
- Operational proximity: [[vol-122-ni-zhidao-li-ni-zuijin-de-aed-zai-na-ma-buneng-zhishi-tongding-sitong-ranhou-buliaoliaozhi-han-xinfei-fusu-liucheng-aed-shiyong-jiangjie-loltrw1svirwsfkt_skd40mxq7d8]] presents a three-minute retrieval aspiration and notes that floor, indoor positioning, doors, and opening times can lengthen the actual trip.
- Mapping and dispatch: [[vol-122-ni-zhidao-li-ni-zuijin-de-aed-zai-na-ma-buneng-zhishi-tongding-sitong-ranhou-buliaoliaozhi-han-xinfei-fusu-liucheng-aed-shiyong-jiangjie-loltrw1svirwsfkt_skd40mxq7d8]] describes WeChat AED maps and a Hangzhou 120 feature that can surface devices within a stated radius, while arguing that people should learn locations in advance.
- Placement mismatch: [[vol-122-ni-zhidao-li-ni-zuijin-de-aed-zai-na-ma-buneng-zhishi-tongding-sitong-ranhou-buliaoliaozhi-han-xinfei-fusu-liucheng-aed-shiyong-jiangjie-loltrw1svirwsfkt_skd40mxq7d8]] contrasts public-site deployment with the episode's claim that many arrests occur at home, identifying a coverage and access gap rather than a simple equipment shortage.
- Team readiness: [[vol-122-ni-zhidao-li-ni-zuijin-de-aed-zai-na-ma-buneng-zhishi-tongding-sitong-ranhou-buliaoliaozhi-han-xinfei-fusu-liucheng-aed-shiyong-jiangjie-loltrw1svirwsfkt_skd40mxq7d8]] links AED retrieval to explicit bystander roles and alternating compression providers.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not provide a complete deployment study, maintenance audit, geospatial access model, cost-effectiveness analysis, or current national inventory. Its three-minute retrieval aspiration, city examples, household-arrest proportion, suggested placement, and lone-rescuer sequence are source-scoped. Local emergency services, current resuscitation protocols, building access, device maintenance, dispatcher guidance, and certified training should govern implementation and use.

## What Changed
- Public AED availability is defined as operational access rather than map presence or equipment count.
- Advance familiarity, indoor wayfinding, access reliability, and role assignment become part of emergency preparedness.
- The home-versus-public-placement mismatch is visible as an infrastructure limitation rather than evidence that public AEDs lack value.
