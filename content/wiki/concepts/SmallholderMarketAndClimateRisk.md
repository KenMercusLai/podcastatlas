# Smallholder Market And Climate Risk / 小农的市场与气候风险

## Definition

Smallholder Market And Climate Risk is the combined exposure of household farmers to volatile prices, demand-driven planting, weak bargaining power, biological timing, and natural disaster.

## Current Synthesis

Hengzhou jasmine illustrates a coupled risk system. Daily flowering imposes a perishable labor deadline, market prices can shift within minutes, high prices and consumer demand encourage expansion, and later supply or inventory pressure can depress returns after costs are committed. Flooding can then destroy plants, homes, and lives regardless of downstream standardization. Modernized supply chains therefore do not eliminate risk at the production frontier and may distribute it unevenly.

## Key Claims

- Perishability forces farmers to sell or harvest on biological rather than financially optimal timelines.
- Demand booms can induce expansion before future supply and price conditions are known.
- Smallholders commit labor and inputs before discovering the price available at sale.
- Standardization downstream does not remove weather exposure upstream.
- Market and climate shocks can compound, making a profitable prior year a poor guide to the next one.

## Evidence

- Price and timing: [[98b809db-0025-4671-9ca4-195f6e0c722f-98b809db-0025-4671-9ca4-195f6e0c722f]] reports sharp year-to-year jasmine price changes, daily picking needs, and minute-scale market movement.
- Expansion and bargaining: [[98b809db-0025-4671-9ca4-195f6e0c722f-98b809db-0025-4671-9ca4-195f6e0c722f]] links beverage demand and high prices to expanded planting while attributing later weakness partly to supply and factory inventory.
- Disaster exposure: [[98b809db-0025-4671-9ca4-195f6e0c722f-98b809db-0025-4671-9ca4-195f6e0c722f]] describes 2026 flood damage and argues that agricultural production remains exposed even as intermediated processing becomes more industrialized.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source supplies field observations and reported prices, not a representative farm panel, verified market series, crop-budget study, disaster assessment, or causal supply model. Larger growers and companies can also face loss, and bargaining power varies by contracts, cooperatives, insurance, storage, crop, and region.

## What Changed

- Established a coupled market-and-climate account of smallholder exposure from the jasmine case.
