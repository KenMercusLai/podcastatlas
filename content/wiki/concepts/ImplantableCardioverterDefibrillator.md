# Implantable Cardioverter-Defibrillator / 植入式心脏复律除颤器

## Definition
An implantable cardioverter-defibrillator (ICD) / 植入式心脏复律除颤器 is an implanted cardiac electronic device that monitors rhythm and can deliver programmed pacing, antitachycardia therapy, or an electrical shock when it detects selected dangerous ventricular arrhythmias.

## Current Synthesis
[[vol-152-nazha-wo-ming-you-wo-bu-you-tian-xianshi-zhong-de-gaiming-shenqi-ni-zhidao-ma-zhen-neng-yufang-cusi-ma-lvt24r7x6bppx7e5gqhas1yn-jru]] explains the ICD as continuous protection for clinically selected high-risk patients, not a device every healthy person should implant. Its central case is a patient with dilated cardiomyopathy, severe heart failure, and a reported ejection fraction around 20% whose device later recorded and treated ventricular fibrillation during a home collapse.

The episode's useful boundary is that an ICD is neither merely a pacemaker nor literally an AED placed inside the body. It can include bradycardia pacing and defibrillation functions, but selection, leads, programming, resynchronization needs, inappropriate-therapy risk, follow-up, and generator replacement make it an individualized clinical system.

## Key Claims
- ICDs continuously sense cardiac electrical activity and apply programmed therapies when detection criteria are met.
- Their defining role is treatment of selected life-threatening ventricular tachyarrhythmias, while ordinary pacemaker function principally prevents pathologically slow rates.
- ICD candidacy depends on [[SuddenCardiacDeathRiskStratification]], not on a general desire for reassurance.
- ICDs complement rather than replace [[CPRAEDResponseBoundary|public CPR/AED response]], because most people at risk of arrest do not already have an implanted device.
- Daily activity may remain broad after recovery, but security procedures, magnetic or electromagnetic exposure, driving, sport, wound care, and follow-up require device- and patient-specific guidance.
- Battery depletion generally requires generator replacement rather than treating the implant as permanent and maintenance-free.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is a public podcast summary, not an implantation guideline. Its device-life estimates, detection delay, material claims, travel examples, and electromagnetic-interference reassurance are source-scoped; actual performance and restrictions vary by manufacturer, programming, lead system, health condition, and current clinical guidance. The reported rescue is illustrative evidence, not proof that every candidate will receive effective therapy or avoid complications.

## What Changed
- Created an ICD concept centered on selective indication, continuous rhythm response, and life-cycle management.
