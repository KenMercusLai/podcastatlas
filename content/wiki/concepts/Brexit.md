# Brexit

## Definition
Brexit is the [[UnitedKingdom]]'s departure from the [[EuropeanUnion]], treated in the wiki as a political rupture whose consequences combine trade friction, immigration promises, regulatory sovereignty, and national-identity reconstruction.

## Current Synthesis
The current Brexit synthesis is skeptical but not one-dimensional. The policy record shows cumulative [[BrexitEconomicFriction]], disappointed [[PostBrexitImmigrationPolitics]], and only limited [[BrexitRegulatoryDividend]]. At the same time, both sources ask what Britain can still do with selective freedom in finance, AI, farming, defence, education, life sciences, and creative industries.

The newer 声东击西 episode adds that Brexit cannot be explained only as a cost-benefit policy error. It also concentrated an older [[PostImperialIdentityGap]]: Britain still had imperial symbols, royal ritual, common-law prestige, English-language reach, and global rule-setting habits, but weaker growth and regional decline made those symbols harder to connect to daily prosperity.

## Key Claims
- Brexit began politically with [[DavidCameron]]'s referendum promise and became electorally possible through Leave's emotional clarity and [[BorisJohnson]]'s support.
- Its economic cost is framed as many smaller barriers compounding across goods, services, finance, professional recognition, investment, and regional confidence.
- Sovereignty was easier to campaign on than to turn into growth, deregulation, or lower migration.
- The 2016 referendum turned older Europe-versus-Britain discomfort into a sharper identity vote than the economically framed 1975 referendum.
- The practical post-Brexit problem is no longer only whether the vote was right, but what role the United Kingdom can credibly play now.

## Evidence
- Referendum and leadership mechanics: [[keep-qualms-and-carry-on-a-decade-after-brexit-6a3a53028a3c8822ab345d4b]] ties Brexit to Cameron's referendum gamble, Johnson's Leave support, and a 52-48 result.
- Cumulative economic cost: [[keep-qualms-and-carry-on-a-decade-after-brexit-6a3a53028a3c8822ab345d4b]] and [[396-tuoyou-shinian-dang-jingji-chengbuqi-shenfen-xushi-yingguo-haisheng-shenme-92f097c7-3ae4-4a2b-87d0-e23b518b590a]] both frame damage as cumulative drag rather than instant collapse.
- Identity turn: [[396-tuoyou-shinian-dang-jingji-chengbuqi-shenfen-xushi-yingguo-haisheng-shenme-92f097c7-3ae4-4a2b-87d0-e23b518b590a]] contrasts the 1975 referendum's food, money, and jobs language with the 2016 emphasis on borders, law, security, finance, and protection.
- Sovereignty-to-capacity gap: [[keep-qualms-and-carry-on-a-decade-after-brexit-6a3a53028a3c8822ab345d4b]] stresses limited regulatory dividend, while [[396-tuoyou-shinian-dang-jingji-chengbuqi-shenfen-xushi-yingguo-haisheng-shenme-92f097c7-3ae4-4a2b-87d0-e23b518b590a]] stresses the gap between imperial-style self-image and material capacity.
- Future-role problem: both [[keep-qualms-and-carry-on-a-decade-after-brexit-6a3a53028a3c8822ab345d4b]] and [[396-tuoyou-shinian-dang-jingji-chengbuqi-shenfen-xushi-yingguo-haisheng-shenme-92f097c7-3ae4-4a2b-87d0-e23b518b590a]] identify post-Brexit role construction as the remaining strategic question.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The sources do not claim that every British problem began with Brexit. The 声东击西 episode explicitly points to 2008-era stagnation, regional decline, inherited class signals, institutional authority loss, and global nationalist mood as additional causes. The Intelligence episode also preserves possible narrower policy openings in AI, farming, finance, and defence.
