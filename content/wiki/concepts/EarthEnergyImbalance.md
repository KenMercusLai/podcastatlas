# Earth Energy Imbalance

Earth energy imbalance is the source's measure of how much more energy Earth absorbs from the sun than it emits as infrared radiation. In [[taking-the-shine-off-albedo-and-global-temperatures-6a730fdaca067b295bab9760]], [[OliverMorton]] says the imbalance has more than doubled in the past 20 years.

The concept matters because it looks at the whole heat budget rather than only air temperature. The episode says the imbalance is mainly measured by [[NASA]] satellite instruments and independently checked through [[OceanHeatMonitoring]], because most excess energy enters the oceans.

## Key Claims
- The imbalance rises when less sunlight is reflected and more energy is retained by the Earth system.
- It is presented as a more fundamental view of warming than surface air temperature alone.
- Satellite data and ocean heat data reinforce one another despite calibration challenges.
