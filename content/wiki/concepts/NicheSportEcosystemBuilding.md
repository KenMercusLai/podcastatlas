# Niche Sport Ecosystem Building / 小众运动生态建设

## Definition
Niche sport ecosystem building is the coordinated use of equipment, athletes, education, communities, competitions, media, and repeatable participation formats to make a small sport understandable and sustainable.

## Current Synthesis
[[vol-274-chengwei-saluomeng-1013268300]] presents [[Salomon]]'s trail-running work as more than endorsement marketing. Shoes and hydration packs solve activity-specific problems; [[KilianJornet|athlete performance]] supplies proof; [[GoldenTrailWorldSeries|GTWS]] creates recurring competition and media; and [[GravelRunning|Gravel]] experiments create a lower-friction urban entry point. Together these layers can expand the sport and the commercial category, although the brand-supported source does not separate public participation gains from Salomon's private return.

## Key Claims
- Equipment adoption grows faster when athletes demonstrate its value under demanding conditions.
- A recurring race series gives a niche sport shared rules, stories, rankings, destinations, and media surfaces.
- Community cultivation and technical education reduce vocabulary and confidence barriers for newcomers.
- New formats can connect specialist terrain with ordinary city life and broaden participation.
- Category-building creates value for the sport and the sponsor, but those interests are not always identical.

## Evidence
- **Product and athlete layer:** [[vol-274-chengwei-saluomeng-1013268300]] links Kilian Jornet's 2008 UTMB performance to hydration-vest visibility.
- **Competition and media layer:** [[vol-274-chengwei-saluomeng-1013268300]] presents GTWS as a Salomon-founded race series visible in European sports media.
- **Participation-format layer:** [[vol-274-chengwei-saluomeng-1013268300]] describes route-free Gravel events in Shanghai and Chengdu.
- **Integrated system:** [[vol-274-chengwei-saluomeng-1013268300]] explicitly groups product development, athlete sponsorship, community cultivation, technical education, and event operation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- The source does not quantify whether the ecosystem grows total participation, shifts share toward one brand, or both.
- Brand-controlled events can privilege sponsor products and narratives and may not represent the whole sport.
- A successful event IP still depends on safety, local permissions, volunteers, media reach, and repeat participant value.

## What Changed
- Created the concept from Salomon's trail-running and Gravel category-building cases.
