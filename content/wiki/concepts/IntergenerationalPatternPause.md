# Intergenerational Pattern Pause / 代际模式暂停键

Intergenerational pattern pause / 代际模式暂停键 is [[ep273-npc-haishi-npd-dou-bie-xiang-nazou-women-rensheng-de-yaokongqi-lqqnd-e29xvhx2mtf6uuzx13wwvf]]'s description of what psychotherapy can do inside a family lineage. [[GongXueping|龚学平]] says people who enter therapy may be pressing pause on inherited patterns and saying that life should not keep going in the same direction.

The concept does not imply that family history is destiny. The episode insists that original-family experience leaves traces, but today's person still has choices. Its practical form is a shift from explaining the past to acting differently in the present: noticing what was unbearable, refusing to pass it forward, and building new neural or behavioral pathways through repeated action.

## Key Claims
- Family trauma can repeat through care styles, control patterns, emotional numbing, and the belief that suffering is normal.
- Recognition of a painful past may be difficult because it removes the old story that the harm was necessary or deserved.
- A person cannot obtain a better past, but they can create a different future through new behavior.
- [[TherapyRelationshipAndBoundaries|Therapy]] supports the pause by pacing pain, restoring emotional perception, and helping the person test new actions.
