## Definition
Breakfast locality is the persistence of regionally specific morning foods, combinations, service rhythms, and social uses despite wider restaurant standardization.

## Current Synthesis
The source attributes breakfast's locality to two reinforcing conditions: residents have stable morning tastes, and low margins leave substantial room for small shops and family operators rather than only national chains. Locality is not just a dish list. Shenyang's pancake-and-egg-custard pairing, Taiyuan's beef-head soup, and Wuzhong's offal, breads, beef noodles, and tea are embedded in different shop routines, customer groups, ingredient systems, and lengths of stay.

## Key Claims
- A local breakfast is a combination of food, timing, service, memory, and social occasion rather than a single signature dish.
- Stable morning preferences can resist nationwide menu convergence.
- Low margins may preserve independent operators, though they can also limit labor and investment capacity.
- Breakfast boundaries vary locally: some Shenyang residents treat rice, stir-fries, or pickled-cabbage stew as acceptable morning food.
- Regional distinctiveness persists through preparation knowledge and ingredient supply as well as consumer taste.

## Evidence
- Shenyang combinations: [[ep287-yiqi-guang-zaoshi-xunzhao-beifang-zui-xianhuo-de-zaochen-lpuox4b-b02k-awt-hsjuph-froi]] describes diaolubing with savory egg custard, chicken-broth hand-pulled noodles, and seasoned chicken frames, alongside shop accommodations for children and older readers.
- Taiyuan repertoire: [[ep287-yiqi-guang-zaoshi-xunzhao-beifang-zui-xianhuo-de-zaochen-lpuox4b-b02k-awt-hsjuph-froi]] names beef-head soup, ma ye with old tofu, meatball soup, flatbread, and liang da tang with steamed bread.
- Wuzhong occasions: [[ep287-yiqi-guang-zaoshi-xunzhao-beifang-zui-xianhuo-de-zaochen-lpuox4b-b02k-awt-hsjuph-froi]] distinguishes an extended beef-noodle-derived morning tea from the more ordinary lamb-offal-and-white-bread breakfast.
- Economic explanation: [[ep287-yiqi-guang-zaoshi-xunzhao-beifang-zui-xianhuo-de-zaochen-lpuox4b-b02k-awt-hsjuph-froi]] explicitly links local diversity to stable tastes and breakfast's comparatively low-profit independent shops.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode offers an editorial explanation rather than comparative market data on chain penetration, margins, or dietary change. Tourism and short-video promotion can preserve visibility while standardizing the most photogenic foods. Stable taste does not mean a frozen tradition: Wuzhong morning tea itself developed through migration, urbanization, commerce, and recent public naming.

## What Changed
- Established a model connecting dishes to shop economics, service routines, and social occasions.
- Added three contrasting northern city cases without treating northern breakfast as uniform.
- Qualified preservation with adaptation, migration, and tourism pressure.
