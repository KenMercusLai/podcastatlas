# Will Exposure Practice

## Definition
Will exposure practice is Portal's frame that will becomes visible when a person meets resistance, stimulus pull, or reluctance and returns to chosen action without relying only on motivation, discipline, force, or self-punishment.

## Current Synthesis
The Portal episode separates will from several neighboring supports. Motivation can help, discipline can scaffold, and playfulness can keep difficulty from hardening into threat, but none of these is identical to will. Will is researched in the moment when a person encounters something they do not want to do.

The practical method is not to crush resistance. Portal recommends choosing tasks that sometimes produce reluctance, noticing rigidity, softening, and lowering the task if the body hardens too much. The same structure applies to phone pull or algorithmic stimulus: early environmental removal may be necessary, but later practice asks the person to feel the pull, relax, and return.

## Key Claims
- Will appears most clearly when a person meets a task, sensation, or stimulus they do not want.
- Motivation, discipline, and playfulness are useful scaffolds but should not be confused with will itself.
- Forcing through rigidity can miss the practice target because the body may only harden around resistance.
- Lowering difficulty can make resistance workable without abandoning the chosen direction.
- Softening around stimulus pull can train choice more precisely than only removing every trigger.
- Repeated small exposures may support change better than rare extreme tests.

## Evidence
- Will distinction: [[movement-practice-to-strengthen-your-mind-body-connection-ido-portal-scim2496765743]] records Portal saying motivation, discipline, and playfulness can all serve as scaffolding, while will is exposed when unwanted action is encountered.
- Difficulty adjustment: [[movement-practice-to-strengthen-your-mind-body-connection-ido-portal-scim2496765743]] has Portal recommend choosing tasks one only sometimes resists and lowering the task when the body rigidifies.
- Stimulus pull: [[movement-practice-to-strengthen-your-mind-body-connection-ido-portal-scim2496765743]] contrasts deleting an app as early scaffolding with later practice of noticing the pull, relaxing, and returning to the chosen task.
- Gentle repetition: [[movement-practice-to-strengthen-your-mind-body-connection-ido-portal-scim2496765743]] favors repeated gentle practices over single extreme events when working with deep change.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept should not be used to moralize depression, neurological apathy, addiction, injury, unsafe training, sleep deprivation, or coercive environments. Portal's claims are source-scoped practice philosophy, not a clinical exposure protocol.

- Created the concept to preserve Portal's will-versus-discipline distinction and its practical softness around resistance.
