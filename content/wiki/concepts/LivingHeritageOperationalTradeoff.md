# Living Heritage Operational Tradeoff / 活态遗产运行权衡

## Definition

The living heritage operational tradeoff arises when a historically valuable site remains embedded in transport, education, residence, commerce, or everyday public use, making conservation a continuing negotiation rather than simple enclosure.

## Current Synthesis

Shouhuang Hall and Wanning Bridge show two sides of the problem. Returning an old building from youth-palace use to restored display strengthens monument protection while narrowing a form of everyday civic access. Keeping buses on an ancient bridge preserves urban operation but requires vehicle and visitor-management compromises.

The synthesis is not that use defeats preservation or that restoration is inherently exclusionary. It is that the public value of living heritage has several dimensions—fabric, memory, access, movement, and continuing use—and interventions should state which dimensions they protect, limit, or replace.

## Key Claims

- Material conservation and everyday public use are related but distinct heritage values.
- Complete closure can protect fabric while ending socially meaningful use.
- Continued operation can preserve living continuity while adding wear and safety risks.
- Compromise measures should match the actual threat rather than assume only closure or unrestricted use.
- Tourism can increase protection incentives and visitor conflict at the same time.

## Evidence

- Adaptive-use reversal: [[406-cong-jingshan-qi-dao-sanlitun-zai-jietou-xunzhao-beijing-de-shinian-henji-shizhounian-tebie-jiemu-2f9cb979]] describes Shouhuang Hall's move from youth-palace use to restoration and heritage display.
- Operational compromise: the source describes Wanning Bridge as both a heritage point and an active transport crossing, with temporary visitor controls and shorter buses used instead of full rerouting. [[406-cong-jingshan-qi-dao-sanlitun-zai-jietou-xunzhao-beijing-de-shinian-henji-shizhounian-tebie-jiemu-2f9cb979]]
- Layered memory: the route connects river burial, former market use, surviving place names, and current tourism around the bridge area. [[406-cong-jingshan-qi-dao-sanlitun-zai-jietou-xunzhao-beijing-de-shinian-henji-shizhounian-tebie-jiemu-2f9cb979]]

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The episode does not provide structural assessments, visitor counts, conservation budgets, or the full positions of residents and authorities. Some sites may require closure when damage risk is high; others can sustain intensive use. The framework identifies the tradeoff but does not predetermine the correct balance.

## What Changed

- Created a framework separating material protection, civic access, transport operation, and living memory.
