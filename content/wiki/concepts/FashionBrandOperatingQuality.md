# Fashion Brand Operating Quality / 时尚品牌经营质量

## Definition

Fashion brand operating quality is the extent to which reported demand becomes retained, full-price, repeatable, new-product-led, and inventory-efficient business rather than payment volume produced by promotion, channel shift, or high-return impulse purchases.

## Current Synthesis

[[ep151-ep151]] proposes a measurement stack beyond GMV: active brand search, full-price conversion, repeat purchase, high-quality customer share, new-product contribution, gross margin, marketing expense, and inventory turnover. These measures connect brand mindshare to realized economics. They also help distinguish a [[ChineseFashionBrandPremiumization|premium brand]] from a seller temporarily lifted by platform traffic or discounting.

No single metric is decisive. Repurchase can reflect discounts or an unusually defined cohort; high gross margin can coexist with excessive marketing; payment GMV can disappear through returns; and full-price demand can still create unhealthy inventory. The framework therefore depends on a balanced view across customer, product, price, channel, and working capital.

## Key Claims

- Payment GMV should be reconciled with returns and retained revenue.
- Active brand search is a useful signal of intentional demand rather than feed-induced impulse.
- Full-price conversion and new-product contribution test whether brand preference survives without clearance.
- Repeat purchase tests whether first purchase becomes a relationship, but definitions and cohorts matter.
- Gross margin must be read together with marketing expense and inventory turnover.
- Long inventory cycles can weaken apparently strong sales and margins.
- Unified channel inventory and prices can improve coherence but do not remove execution risk.

## Evidence

- Customer-demand metrics - [[ep151-ep151]] names active search, high-quality customers, and repeat purchase as brand-quality indicators.
- Price and product metrics - [[ep151-ep151]] emphasizes full-price sales and new-product contribution.
- Financial and working-capital metrics - [[ep151-ep151]] links gross margin, marketing expense, and inventory turnover and calls 300-360 inventory days unhealthy.
- Return adjustment - [[ep151-ep151]] uses very high reported apparel livestream returns to show the gap between payment GMV and final sales.
- Brand cases - [[ep151-ep151]] cites source-scoped repurchase figures for Shanxia Yousong and ICICLE while leaving their methodologies undefined.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source gives a practitioner framework rather than a standardized accounting model. Definitions can vary by company, platform, cohort, and period, and cross-brand comparison may be invalid without consistent return windows, customer identity resolution, inventory accounting, and channel coverage. Healthy ranges also differ by product seasonality and business model.
