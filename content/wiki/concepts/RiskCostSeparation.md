# Risk-Cost Separation / 风险与成本分离

Risk-cost separation / 风险与成本分离 is [[jifa-dongwu-jingshen-chuangzao-gengduo-jihui-lndyfdmtgo-9l5cr8oy82t1xnlfe]]'s frame for why policy, investment, health care, and industrial development should not treat lower cost as automatically better. [[ZhouLuohua|周洛华]] argues that low cost paired with high risk can make entrepreneurs, doctors, investors, or households unwilling to act, while a higher-cost but lower-risk environment can create room for technical substitution and innovation.

The concept turns debt and development analysis toward the asset side. A debt problem is not solved only by shrinking liabilities; it changes when factories, assets, businesses, and young people have enough future opportunity to earn returns. That connects the page to [[AnimalSpiritsOpportunityActivation]], [[InvestmentRiskManagement]], and [[MedicalRiskManagement]].

## Key Claims
- Cost reduction and risk reduction are different policy levers.
- Lowering nominal cost can fail if legal, operational, market, or personal risk remains high.
- High cost can motivate innovation when the risk of attempting improvement is low enough.
- Debt becomes more bearable when opportunity and return on the asset side improve.
- Medical and legal systems should price risk in ways that protect useful professionals without leaving victims unprotected.
