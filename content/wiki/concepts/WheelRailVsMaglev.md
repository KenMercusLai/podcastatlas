# Wheel-Rail vs Maglev

Wheel-rail vs maglev is the technology-route dispute in [[no-201-zhongguo-gaotie-jianshi-976314772]]. The episode frames it as a conflict between adopting mature wheel-rail systems such as Japan's Shinkansen, France's TGV, and Germany's ICE, or leapfrogging into magnetic levitation with higher theoretical speed and less wheel-rail wear.

The source's practical test is network fit. [[ShanghaiMaglev]] proved that maglev could run fast, but it also showed high cost, high fares, operating losses, and weak interoperability with the existing rail network. Wheel-rail looked less futuristic, but it had commercial precedents, supplier ecosystems, and compatibility advantages that made [[ChineseHighSpeedRail]] scalable.

## Key Claims
- Technology routes should be judged by deployment ecosystem, not only headline speed.
- Isolated demonstration lines can consume opportunity cost if they do not connect into a larger network.
- Mature technology can be the more ambitious choice when the goal is national-scale rollout.
