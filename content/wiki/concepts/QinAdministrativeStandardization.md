# Qin Administrative Standardization / 秦国行政标准化

Qin administrative standardization / 秦国行政标准化 is the second-reform package [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-17-hanzhaohou-he-shenbuhai-lghjmjhq9qs4se96c5ofgz6caqof]] attributes to [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]] in [[QinState|秦国]]. The source groups several measures together: villages are consolidated into counties with county magistrates and deputies, Qin reaches thirty-one counties, older field boundaries are abolished or opened, land is redistributed, and measures such as dou, hu, zhang, and chi are standardized.

The concept captures the administrative side of [[ShangYangReforms|商鞅变法]]. Instead of treating reform only as harsh law or military rank, this source shows Qin making territory, land, office, and measurement easier for the state to see and govern. It sits beside the move to [[Xianyang|咸阳]] and [[HouseholdSeparationLaw|分居令]] as part of a deeper conversion of social space into state capacity.

## Key Claims
- County organization gives Qin a more direct administrative grid.
- Land-boundary reform changes the relationship between fields, households, and state allocation.
- Standard measures make taxation, storage, exchange, and command more legible.
- The package links geographic centralization, household control, and administrative measurement into one reform logic.
