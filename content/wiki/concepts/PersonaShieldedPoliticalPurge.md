## Definition

Persona-shielded political purge / 人设护盾式政治清除 is the mechanism where a dominant actor removes a rival or obstacle through other people's accusations, public-interest language, and staged restraint so that the elimination protects rather than damages the actor's moral image.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-606-duan-diao-qin-shushu-wangmang-de-xinji-you-duo-shen-lswummzfg74d-_dm_k4vc7fg8eut|Hanji 606]] creates the source case through [[WangMang|王莽]]'s removal of [[WangLiWesternHan|王立]]. Because Wang Li is Wang Mang's uncle and [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]'s brother, Wang Mang cannot openly attack him without damaging the persona of upright kinship, humility, and public-minded rectitude that supports his power.

The solution is procedural indirection. [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]] supplies the formal impeachment, using old accusations and an heir-proposal danger. When Wang Zhengjun resists, Wang Mang reframes the issue as a fragile succession problem and warns that dowager private feeling could corrupt ministers. The personal threat is thus converted into a public-order question.

The source extends the pattern beyond Wang Li. Wang Mang's allies can hint, memorialize, and praise while he performs surprise, tears, and refusal. This makes the concept stronger than simple hypocrisy: the persona is not only cover after the fact, but a working control system that determines who must speak, who appears reluctant, and how punishment can look like principle.

## Key Claims

- A moral persona can constrain direct action while making indirect action more valuable.
- Procedural proxies let the principal actor avoid appearing personally vindictive.
- Public-order language can convert kinship threat or private rivalry into a state problem.
- Staged grief, refusal, or ignorance can make orchestrated outcomes appear reluctant.
- The method depends on a reliable operator network that can turn hints into formal memorials.
- Persona-shielded purges create risk for proxy actors, who may later discover they are tools rather than partners.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept does not prove Wang Mang's private psychology beyond source-backed actions and the host's interpretation. It also does not say every indirect impeachment is a purge; the pattern requires a mismatch between the public procedure and the private power interest being protected. Wang Li's full culpability, Kong Guang's degree of consent, and Wang Zhengjun's inner judgment remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Created the concept from Hanji 606's Wang Li removal case and Wang Mang's broader proxy-performance control method.
