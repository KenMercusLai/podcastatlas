# Toyota Production System Personal Waste

## Definition
Toyota Production System personal waste is the application of TPS ideas such as waste elimination, Just in Time, pull, Kanban, one-piece flow, takt time, Jidoka, Andon, and continuous improvement to personal and knowledge-work systems.

## Current Synthesis
The episode treats the Toyota Production System as a mature operations model that can discipline personal work. Its roof is eliminating waste; its pillars include Just in Time and Jidoka. The host maps the seven wastes onto everyday behavior: over-reading, over-planning, waiting for replies, unnecessary movement, over-processing, inventory of notes, and rework caused by weak upfront planning.

TPS also supplies a cultural claim. An Andon cord only helps if stopping the line is treated as contribution rather than blame. Applied to personal work, this means visible defects, weekly review, and workflow redesign are better than hiding failure and compensating with more effort.

## Key Claims
- Waste elimination applies to knowledge work when overproduction, waiting, over-processing, inventory, motion, and defects become task friction.
- Just in Time shifts work from push accumulation toward pull based on real need.
- Jidoka and Andon make defect exposure part of the operating system rather than a personal failure.
- Continuous improvement matters more than one-time productivity redesign.
- TPS needs culture as well as mechanism because people hide problems when defects are punished.

## Evidence
- TPS history evidence: [[ep86-dianfu-gongzuoliu-xiaolu-tisheng-3000-gkwrimaoad7vbjtrsqtotc4k]] traces Toyota's production logic from automatic stop ideas and Just in Time into the Toyota Production System.
- Waste evidence: [[ep86-dianfu-gongzuoliu-xiaolu-tisheng-3000-gkwrimaoad7vbjtrsqtotc4k]] maps seven wastes to over-reading, over-noting, over-planning, waiting, perfectionism, and rework.
- Pull-system evidence: [[ep86-dianfu-gongzuoliu-xiaolu-tisheng-3000-gkwrimaoad7vbjtrsqtotc4k]] describes Kanban, pull, one-piece flow, and takt time under Just in Time.
- Culture evidence: [[ep86-dianfu-gongzuoliu-xiaolu-tisheng-3000-gkwrimaoad7vbjtrsqtotc4k]] says Andon-like problem surfacing requires a culture that does not punish exposed defects.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Personal knowledge work is less repeatable than automobile production, so TPS terms should be adapted cautiously. The episode's value is in identifying waste and feedback loops, not pretending personal creativity is a factory line.
