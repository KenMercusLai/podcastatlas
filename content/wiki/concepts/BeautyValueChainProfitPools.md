# Beauty Value-Chain Profit Pools / 美妆价值链利润池

## Definition

Beauty value-chain profit pools are the comparatively durable sources of value at the upstream research/manufacturing and downstream distribution/retail ends of a beauty system when individual consumer brands face rapid imitation, traffic dependence, and short life cycles.

## Current Synthesis

[[e261-biaoyan-youxiao-zutuan-chuhai-hanguo-meizhuang-ruhe-qiangzhan-meiguo-shichang-9753c5f7-03dd-4cfa-8d4a-40531f7c4b79]] argues that [[Cosmax]] and [[KolmarKorea|Kolmar Korea]] can reuse technical capability across many customers, while [[Silicon2]] and [[OliveYoung|OLIVE YOUNG]] aggregate brands, logistics, data, compliance, discovery, and consumer access. These positions may remain valuable even as the winning skincare label changes.

The thesis is conditional rather than universal. Brands can still own pricing power, loyalty, intellectual property, customer data, and enduring identity. The episode's contribution is to ask whether a market optimized for repeated viral launches shifts bargaining power toward the shared systems every launch needs.

## Key Claims

- Reusable upstream capability can earn across many brand cycles.
- Downstream aggregation can control access, comparison, data, compliance, and consumer education.
- Brand-level traffic dependence increases the relative durability of shared infrastructure.
- Homogeneous product supply can weaken individual brand bargaining power.
- A viral brand acquisition may capture a temporary growth dividend without transferring a repeatable creation capability.
- Profit-pool location must be tested with margins, concentration, switching costs, and cash flow rather than inferred from visibility.

## Evidence

- Upstream examples: [[e261-biaoyan-youxiao-zutuan-chuhai-hanguo-meizhuang-ruhe-qiangzhan-meiguo-shichang-9753c5f7-03dd-4cfa-8d4a-40531f7c4b79]] presents COSMAX and Kolmar Korea as cross-brand technical infrastructure.
- Downstream examples: [[e261-biaoyan-youxiao-zutuan-chuhai-hanguo-meizhuang-ruhe-qiangzhan-meiguo-shichang-9753c5f7-03dd-4cfa-8d4a-40531f7c4b79]] presents Silicon2 and OLIVE YOUNG as distribution, compliance, discovery, and sales infrastructure.
- Brand qualification: [[e261-biaoyan-youxiao-zutuan-chuhai-hanguo-meizhuang-ruhe-qiangzhan-meiguo-shichang-9753c5f7-03dd-4cfa-8d4a-40531f7c4b79]] uses acquisition and homogeneity examples to argue that traffic-era brand value can decay quickly.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source provides no segment-margin decomposition, return on invested capital, customer concentration, contract terms, retailer take rates, bargaining-power data, or longitudinal brand-survival analysis. Manufacturers and retailers can face commoditization, inventory risk, regulation, and customer concentration, while exceptional brands can remain the strongest profit pool.

## What Changed

- Created a conditional framework for comparing brand economics with shared beauty infrastructure.
