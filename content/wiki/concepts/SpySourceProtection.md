# Spy Source Protection / 间谍线人保护

Spy source protection / 间谍线人保护 is the intelligence tradeoff introduced by [[80-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-shang-691866649]]. Once [[OlegGordievsky|Oleg Gordievsky]] begins supplying information to [[MI6]], the service must decide when acting on intelligence would reveal that a [[KGB]] insider exists.

The source frames protection as both moral and strategic. A source who takes mortal risk should not be burned for a small tactical gain, and a source who survives can provide more valuable intelligence over time. [[OperationPimlico]] extends the same logic from information use into physical extraction.

[[81-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-xia-693711209]] adds two harder versions of the same problem. First, [[MI6]] must share enough [[OperationRYAN]] intelligence with the [[UnitedStates]] to reduce nuclear misunderstanding while hiding that Gordievsky is the source. Second, when [[AldrichAmes|Aldrich Ames]] exposes him, protection becomes [[ExtractionPromiseEthics]]: the service has to risk diplomacy and personnel to honor the emergency plan.

## Key Claims
- Intelligence has value only if the collection path can survive repeated use.
- Acting too directly on a correct report can expose the source who supplied it.
- Source protection requires restraint, not only clever signals or escape routes.
- Trust depends on visible preparation for the source's worst-case risk.
- Source protection must account for allied curiosity and insider compromise, not only hostile counterintelligence.
