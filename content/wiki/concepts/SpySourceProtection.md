# Spy Source Protection / 间谍线人保护

## Definition

Spy source protection is the operational and ethical discipline of preserving a human source's identity, access, trust, physical safety, and viable escape options while still using the intelligence supplied.

## Current Synthesis

The Gordievsky sources establish protection as restraint and preparation. [[80-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-shang-691866649]] shows [[MI6]] limiting direct action that might reveal [[OlegGordievsky|Oleg Gordievsky]] and building [[OperationPimlico]] before a crisis. [[81-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-xia-693711209]] adds sanitized allied sharing, the exposure risk created by [[AldrichAmes|Aldrich Ames]], and the obligation to honor extraction promises.

The [[AdolfTolkachev|Adolf Tolkachev]] case establishes two harder boundaries. First, technical intelligence becomes more useful as it circulates, but wider knowledge of its domain can help an insider identify the source without knowing a name. Second, a source's request for suicide means may express rational fear and desire for control while creating severe ethical, concealment, accident, and misjudgment risks for handlers. [[506-yiwan-meiyuan-jiandie-tuokaqiefu-yu-lengzhan-wanqi-meisu-qingbao-daan-lvqmgp0k-6r7o0whwbakbuct8kvn]] therefore extends protection across information architecture, operational practice, family consequences, emergency extraction, and disputed last-resort autonomy.

## Key Claims

- Intelligence use must be calibrated so action or dissemination does not reveal the collection path.
- Source protection begins during recruitment and routine handling, not only after compromise.
- Compartmentation must protect combinations of descriptors, access domain, timing, and methods as well as names.
- Allied sharing and internal analytical use can be necessary while still enlarging exposure risk.
- Trust depends on credible contingency planning, including signals, routes, and extraction capacity where feasible.
- A source's family and workplace behavior can become part of the protection problem even when relatives are not operational participants.
- Requests for suicide means create a conflict among source autonomy, handler duty, accidental harm, and institutional responsibility.

## Evidence

### Restraint and prepared extraction

- [[80-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-shang-691866649]] connects cautious intelligence use to the early design of Operation Pimlico.

### Liaison exposure and rescue obligation

- [[81-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-xia-693711209]] shows sanitized sharing failing against insider access and frames Gordievsky's extraction as keeping faith with a source.

### Descriptor leakage and last-resort autonomy

- [[506-yiwan-meiyuan-jiandie-tuokaqiefu-yu-lengzhan-wanqi-meisu-qingbao-daan-lvqmgp0k-6r7o0whwbakbuct8kvn]] links Howard's knowledge of domain and method to Tolkachev's identification and recounts CIA debate before providing a cyanide pen.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

No protection system can remove the source's voluntary risk, hostile surveillance, random discovery, or betrayal by a trusted insider. Extraction may be impossible, and promises can endanger diplomats, families, and third parties. The sources narrate exceptional exposed agents and do not provide a representative success rate. The cyanide decision is described, not endorsed as a general practice; the account lacks full clinical, legal, and policy context.
