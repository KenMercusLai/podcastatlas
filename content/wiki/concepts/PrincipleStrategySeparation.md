# Principle-Strategy Separation

## Definition

Principle-strategy separation is the practice of maintaining commitment to a cause, value, or desired end while treating the current plan for advancing it as a revisable hypothesis.

## Current Synthesis

[[AdamGrant]] uses this distinction to answer the concern that habitual rethinking dissolves loyalty and conviction. A person can remain committed to humane warfare, a company, a community, or another cause while changing methods when evidence shows that the current route is ineffective or harmful.

The distinction is useful only when “principle” is not defined so broadly that every preferred tactic becomes morally untouchable. Revision therefore requires explicit ends, observable strategy claims, and willingness to examine who bears the costs of persistence.

## Key Claims

- Stable values do not require fixed plans.
- Strategies should make testable claims about how they advance the governing principle.
- Failed methods can be abandoned without treating revision as betrayal.
- Persistence is valuable when directed toward ends, not insulated tactics.
- Cost-bearing stakeholders matter when deciding whether a plan still serves its principle.

## Evidence

- Clarifying rethinking - [[malcolm-gladwell-and-adam-grant-malcolm-gladwell-and-adam-grant]] records Grant preserving passions, causes, principles, and core values while asking for flexibility about plans.
- Scientific strategy - [[malcolm-gladwell-and-adam-grant-malcolm-gladwell-and-adam-grant]] recommends approaching methods as hypotheses rather than defending them as identity.
- Persistence counterexamples - [[malcolm-gladwell-and-adam-grant-malcolm-gladwell-and-adam-grant]] uses BlackBerry, employee effects, regional spillovers, sports loyalty, and *Don Quixote* to test when steadfastness is admirable or costly.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Principles themselves can be mistaken, conflicting, or too vague to guide action. Strategy results may be delayed or ambiguous, and short-term failure does not always justify abandonment. The framework structures revision but does not determine the correct moral end or evidentiary threshold.

## What Changed

- Established a durable-values, revisable-methods framework with stakeholder costs as a boundary on persistence.
