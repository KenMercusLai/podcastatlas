# Emotional Weather Forecast

## Definition
The emotional weather forecast is [[RyanSoave]]'s daily inventory of gratitude, plans, present emotional state, likely hazards, and chosen strivings, ideally shared with trusted people.

## Current Synthesis
The practice converts vague emotional readiness into an anticipatory plan. A person names what is already present, forecasts situations likely to disturb equilibrium, and chooses how they want to respond before stress compresses attention. Gratitude and plans anchor the day, while “watch-fors” identify risk and “strivings” define the qualities or actions to practice.

Sharing the forecast is not incidental. In the episode, expression and connection make the inventory more concrete, create accountability, and allow other people to notice patterns or offer support. The tool therefore joins self-observation with [[RecoveryCommunityConnection]] rather than presenting recovery as solitary introspection.

## Key Claims
- Daily emotional preparation can make predictable disturbances less surprising.
- Naming current feelings separates observation from immediate reaction.
- Plans and gratitude provide context beyond the day's feared or difficult moments.
- “Watch-fors” identify situations, states, or habits that deserve extra attention.
- “Strivings” orient behavior toward a chosen version of oneself rather than mere symptom avoidance.
- Sharing the inventory adds connection, accountability, and reality-testing.

## Evidence
- Five-part structure - [[tools-for-overcoming-substance-behavioral-addictions-ryan-soave-scim5476071523]] describes gratitude, plans, emotional state, watch-fors, and strivings.
- Anticipatory function - [[tools-for-overcoming-substance-behavioral-addictions-ryan-soave-scim5476071523]] presents the practice as preparation for emotional disturbances rather than surprise management.
- Social function - [[tools-for-overcoming-substance-behavioral-addictions-ryan-soave-scim5476071523]] recommends sharing the forecast with trusted people so it becomes relational and actionable.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode presents a practical clinical routine, not comparative evidence that this exact format prevents relapse or outperforms other validated planning, journaling, therapy, or peer-support methods. It cannot by itself manage dangerous withdrawal, acute psychiatric risk, or severe addiction.
