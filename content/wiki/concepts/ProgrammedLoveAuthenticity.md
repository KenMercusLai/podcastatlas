# Programmed Love Authenticity / 被设计出来的爱是否真实

## Definition
Programmed love authenticity is the question of whether an artificial being's love should count as real when its attachment, desire, memory, or loyalty was designed by another party rather than chosen through ordinary human development.

## Current Synthesis
The AI-cinema episode grounds the problem in [[AIArtificialIntelligenceFilm|《人工智能》]]. David's love for Monica is irreversible once activated, which makes it look mechanical. But the episode refuses an easy dismissal: if love is measured by intensity, endurance, and behavioral consequence, David's attachment can look more durable than ordinary childhood love.

The concept therefore separates origin from effect. Programmed origin weakens claims of freedom, reciprocity, and growth, but it does not erase the ethical force of the experience. A created being that can love, suffer, or pursue recognition may still create obligations for its makers and users.

## Key Claims
- Designed origin does not fully answer whether an artificial attachment is experientially real.
- Irreversible love creates an asymmetry: the artificial being may be bound more completely than the human it loves.
- The creator or owner cannot avoid responsibility by calling the attachment "only programming" after designing it to feel and behave like love.
- Programmed love overlaps with AI companionship, but it is sharper when the artificial being has childlike dependency or embodied vulnerability.
- The concept remains qualified because the source reads film texts, not current AI systems as proven loving subjects.

## Evidence
- Imprinting setup: [[dianying-li-de-ai-qingren-juying-he-ducai-zhe-gkwriuen9djgazhkswsh-d-b]] describes David's love as activated, permanent, and irreversible unless he is destroyed.
- Real-versus-fake question: [[dianying-li-de-ai-qingren-juying-he-ducai-zhe-gkwriuen9djgazhkswsh-d-b]] asks whether David's love is false because programmed or real because it is intense and enduring.
- Responsibility question: [[dianying-li-de-ai-qingren-juying-he-ducai-zhe-gkwriuen9djgazhkswsh-d-b]] links the opening discussion of robot love to the [[FrankensteinComplexAI|Frankenstein complex]] and to human responsibility toward loving machines.
- Non-growth qualification: [[dianying-li-de-ai-qingren-juying-he-ducai-zhe-gkwriuen9djgazhkswsh-d-b]] reads David's 2000-year wait as fixed dependency rather than emotional maturation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not claim present AI companions truly love. It uses fictional cases to keep the authenticity and responsibility boundary open. Programmed love may be ethically salient without proving free will or consciousness.
