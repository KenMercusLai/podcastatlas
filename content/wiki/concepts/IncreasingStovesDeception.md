## Definition

Increasing Stoves Deception / 增灶示强 is the battlefield-information tactic of increasing visible camp stoves or similar logistics traces so an enemy reads the force as growing stronger, reinforced, or harder to attack.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 788 grounds the concept in [[YuXuEasternHan|虞诩]]'s move toward [[WuduCommanderyLateHan|武都郡]]. Yu Xu first tells the Qiang that he lacks troops and must wait for reinforcements. When the enemy disperses, he force-marches, but he also has the troops make extra stoves and double the visible stove count each day. The signal makes Qiang pursuers think Han reinforcements have arrived, slowing their willingness to close.

The episode's analytic value comes from its explicit comparison with [[DecreasingStovesDeception|decreasing stoves deception]]. Sun Bin reduces stoves to show false weakness and invite pursuit; Yu Xu increases stoves to show false strength and delay pursuit. The same kind of physical evidence can therefore push enemy tempo in opposite directions depending on the commander's objective.

## Key Claims

- Camp remains and logistics traces can be turned into deliberate intelligence signals.
- False strength and false weakness are different tools; the useful direction depends on whether the commander wants pursuit, hesitation, dispersal, or distance.
- A stove signal works best when it fits a prepared story, in this case Yu Xu's prior claim that reinforcements were expected.
- The tactic is operational rather than decorative: Yu Xu uses it to buy movement time before the Chiting defense.
- Physical deception needs a follow-on plan; the stove count alone does not defeat the enemy without forced march, crossbow timing, false reinforcements, and ambush.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

This is a one-source concept, so the page should not treat increasing stoves as a universal recipe. It depends on enemy access to visible traces, a believable reinforcement story, and enough movement discipline to use the hesitation. If an enemy scouts better or ignores the signal, the trick alone does not solve the commander's force disadvantage.
