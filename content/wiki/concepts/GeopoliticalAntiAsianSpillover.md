# Geopolitical Anti-Asian Spillover / 地缘政治反亚裔外溢

## Definition
Geopolitical anti-Asian spillover occurs when hostility toward an Asian government, wartime opponent, economic competitor, or political ideology is transferred to Asian-looking people who may have no relevant nationality, allegiance, or political position.

## Current Synthesis
International rivalry does not stay at the level of states. War narratives, anti-communism, espionage suspicion, trade competition, and pandemic blame can activate the [[PerpetualForeignerStereotype]], making domestic Asian populations appear to embody an external threat. Because racial recognition is coarse, hostility aimed at China or Japan can reach Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Southeast Asian, and other people without regard to citizenship or self-identification.

## Key Claims
- State-directed hostility can alter the domestic social meaning of Asian appearance.
- Attackers often fail or refuse to distinguish nationality, citizenship, generation, and political belief.
- Espionage and loyalty discourse can spread from particular investigations to generalized ethnic suspicion.
- Anti-communist narratives can recruit migrants as political symbols while also making other co-ethnics suspect.
- Pandemic blame can combine with preexisting state rivalry and racial stereotypes.
- Domestic anti-racism and adversarial foreign policy can remain in tension when official language racializes the opponent.

## Evidence
- China-facing tension: [[ep169-meiguo-shehui-ruhe-zhizao-mofan-shaoshu-he-beihou-de-yinxing-qishi-ckwriaiewlfyabaaaacimxkl]] connects COVID-19 and worsening U.S.-China relations to hostility directed at Asian Americans.
- Category collapse: [[ep169-meiguo-shehui-ruhe-zhizao-mofan-shaoshu-he-beihou-de-yinxing-qishi-ckwriaiewlfyabaaaacimxkl]] notes that non-Chinese victims may be told to return to China and that [[VincentChin]] was mistaken for Japanese.
- Loyalty suspicion: [[ep169-meiguo-shehui-ruhe-zhizao-mofan-shaoshu-he-beihou-de-yinxing-qishi-ckwriaiewlfyabaaaacimxkl]] links claims about Chinese scholars, espionage, and untrustworthiness to wider perceptions of Asian people in the United States.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Criticism of a government, ideology, war, or security risk is not inherently racial prejudice. Spillover occurs when evidence about a state or individual is generalized through ancestry or appearance. The episode supplies mechanisms and examples, not incidence estimates or a method for attributing every attack or institutional decision to geopolitics.

## What Changed
- Established racial category collapse as the bridge between foreign-policy hostility and domestic anti-Asian harm.
- Distinguished legitimate state criticism from ancestry-based suspicion and collective blame.
