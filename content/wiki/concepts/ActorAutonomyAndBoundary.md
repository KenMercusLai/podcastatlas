# Actor Autonomy and Boundary / 演员职业自主与边界

## Definition
Actor autonomy and boundary is the practical ability to retain meaningful choice over roles, working conditions, publicity, health, private life, and self-definition inside a career that depends on producers, platforms, audiences, and uncertain opportunities.

## Current Synthesis
[[103-duihua-yanyuan-guoxiaoting-ta-zai-guochanju-li-zhangda-ye-kanzhe-guochanju-bianhua-gkwrimaoeesma84v2atvmtsf]] presents autonomy as a negotiated condition rather than complete independence. [[GuoXiaoting|郭晓婷]] may accept less financial advantage, decline high-visibility formats, state a fandom boundary, or resist strategic socializing in order to keep decision rights she values. Those choices carry opportunity costs, but she describes coerced participation in choices she judges wrong as more damaging.

The boundary remains adaptive. Guo tries unfamiliar but not intrinsically wrong activities, learns short-video publicity, and appreciates fan investment while separating character fantasy from private life. Autonomy therefore means controlled permeability: outside systems can inform practice without becoming the sole authority over craft, body, or identity.

## Key Claims
- Career autonomy is partly control over tradeoffs, not freedom from every dependency or consequence.
- Saying no can protect health, judgment, and identity even when it narrows visibility or income.
- A workable boundary distinguishes experimentation from compelled participation in conduct one judges harmful or wrong.
- Audience appreciation and fandom can be valued without granting unlimited access to an actor's private life.
- Treating acting as work can preserve meaning by preventing fame, metrics, or career status from becoming the whole self.

## Evidence
- Decision rights and tradeoffs: [[103-duihua-yanyuan-guoxiaoting-ta-zai-guochanju-li-zhangda-ye-kanzhe-guochanju-bianhua-gkwrimaoeesma84v2atvmtsf]] says Guo was willing to yield benefits in exchange for greater freedom to choose and decide.
- Health and refusal: [[103-duihua-yanyuan-guoxiaoting-ta-zai-guochanju-li-zhangda-ye-kanzhe-guochanju-bianhua-gkwrimaoeesma84v2atvmtsf]] connects declining a demanding variety format with singing-dancing fit and aversion to severe sleep disruption.
- Fandom boundary: [[103-duihua-yanyuan-guoxiaoting-ta-zai-guochanju-li-zhangda-ye-kanzhe-guochanju-bianhua-gkwrimaoeesma84v2atvmtsf]] describes gratitude for CP fans followed by direct clarification when fictional projection entered family life.
- Work-self separation: [[103-duihua-yanyuan-guoxiaoting-ta-zai-guochanju-li-zhangda-ye-kanzhe-guochanju-bianhua-gkwrimaoeesma84v2atvmtsf]] uses “上班” to keep work as a route to value and exploration rather than an ultimate purpose.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept comes from one actor's retrospective and does not establish which tradeoffs are available to less established workers. Refusal can be easier when a person has family support, accumulated credits, financial room, or alternative opportunities. The source does not quantify income sacrificed, contractual power, health outcomes, or the career effects of particular refusals.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to join role choice, health, publicity, fandom, and self-definition under one decision-rights framework.
