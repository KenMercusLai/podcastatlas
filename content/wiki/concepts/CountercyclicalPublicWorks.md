# Countercyclical Public Works / 逆周期公共工程

Countercyclical public works / 逆周期公共工程 is the employment-stabilization idea [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-75-4-weishenme-shuo-guanzhong-caishi-jingji-zhan-de-bizu-lrdleqozazh4-xdwas4karzgbmza]] attributes to [[GuanZhong|管仲]]. The episode says that when the economy is weak and people lack work, the state can build palaces or other large projects not mainly for enjoyment, but to create jobs and keep income moving.

The source compares this to much later Keynesian thinking. The wiki keeps that comparison source-scoped: the important stored claim is not direct lineage to modern macroeconomics, but the episode's reading that public construction can be justified as labor demand and social stabilization rather than elite luxury alone.

## Key Claims
- Public construction can be framed as employment policy during downturns.
- The same physical project can be read differently depending on economic context: indulgence in ordinary times, labor absorption in weak times.
- The episode connects public works to Guan Zhong's broader belief that livelihood and circulation support political order.
- The modern Keynes comparison is treated as analogy, not as historical dependency.
