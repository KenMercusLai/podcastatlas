# Medical Dogma Trust Repair

## Definition
Medical dogma trust repair is the wiki's source-scoped frame for rebuilding confidence after public-health institutions overstate certainty, resist updating guidance, or treat legitimate questions as illegitimate dissent.

## Current Synthesis
The concept now has health-agency, science-policy, dissent-protection, and institutional-admission versions. Makary focuses on COVID-era school closures, vaccine mandates, natural immunity, toddler masking, nutrition guidance, and FDA/CDC transparency. Kratsios extends the pattern through masking, restrictions, and anti-science labeling. Weinstein protects inquiry without predetermined guilt or exoneration. Bhattacharya adds a direct repair rule: state what evidence established, separate risk groups and policy judgments, permit open disagreement, investigate injury claims, and admit error rather than using unified messaging to simulate certainty.

## Key Claims
- Public-health authority depends on humility, update discipline, and evidence strength as much as credentials.
- Trust is damaged when recommendations are presented as equally certain after evidence changes or when risk groups differ.
- COVID-era disputes function in the wiki as trust-repair evidence, not as settled adjudication of every intervention.
- The Kratsios source broadens the concept by arguing that people who asked questions were too readily labeled anti-science.
- The Weinstein source adds that trust repair also requires protecting inquiry into sensitive origin, funding, and institutional-responsibility questions.
- Repair requires distinguishing empirical claims, policy tradeoffs, political values, and uncertainty.
- Treating the public as partners requires accessible evidence and correction, not merely stronger institutional messaging.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The bounded sources are critical of COVID-era institutions and sympathetic to Trump administration health or science reformers. The concept therefore stores a trust mechanism, not a final verdict on pandemic policy, vaccine policy, Sweden, masks, lockdowns, myocarditis thresholds, censorship, COVID origins, the Great Barrington Declaration, or Fauci's overall record. Listening to reported injury and dissent is compatible with requiring causal evidence and does not itself establish every claim.

## What Changed
- Added Bhattacharya's first-person account of evidence-bounded communication, open debate, and institutional admission of error.
- Added patient listening and subgroup distinctions without treating reports or policy criticism as automatic causal proof.
- Preserved the one-sided source composition and contested empirical claims as explicit qualifications.
