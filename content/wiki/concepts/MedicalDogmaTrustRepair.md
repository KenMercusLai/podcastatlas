# Medical Dogma Trust Repair

Medical dogma trust repair is the episode's frame for rebuilding confidence after institutions overstate certainty or resist updating guidance. In [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-supercharging-a-new-fda-marty-makary-on-science-power-patients-39750050]], [[MartyMakary|Marty Makary]] criticizes COVID-era policies on toddler masking, vaccine mandates for low-risk young people, boosters for healthy children, natural immunity, and prolonged school closures.

The wiki stores this as a source-scoped governance concept, not as independent adjudication of every COVID claim. Its broader contribution is that scientific institutions can lose trust when they treat dissent, parental questions, or changed evidence as illegitimate rather than as part of public reasoning.

## Key Claims
- Public-health authority depends on humility as well as credentials.
- Trust can be damaged when policy certainty persists after evidence changes.
- Repair requires distinguishing evidence strength, risk groups, and uncertainty instead of presenting every recommendation as equally settled.
- The concept connects to [[FDAReviewModernization]] because Makary presents transparency and public reasoning as part of regulatory speed.
