# Slow Gathering Improvisation / 慢聚慢奏

## Definition
Slow gathering improvisation is Yang Bin's phrase for an innovation mode where people gather loosely, think slowly, and interact like jazz improvisers before a problem, field, or route is fully defined.

## Current Synthesis
The source uses the Dartmouth AI workshop as the central case. "Slow" does not mean laziness; it means protecting time for wandering, romance, and unresolved questions. "Improvisation" means participants respond to one another in real time rather than follow a fixed agenda.

The concept is useful for the wiki because it distinguishes original innovation from ordinary execution. When goals are undefined and evaluation metrics are immature, rigid planning can suppress the very variation that later becomes important.

## Key Claims
- Slow gathering protects unclear questions from being narrowed too early.
- Improvisational exchange lets participants build on partial ideas without requiring immediate consensus.
- Small, shifting groups can be productive when the work is discovery rather than execution.
- The mode depends on enough psychological safety for people to risk incomplete or non-mainstream ideas.
- Slow gathering is fragile under efficiency-first, non-negotiable execution pressure.

## Evidence
- Dartmouth definition: [[401-bianyu-manju-manzou-women-haineng-yinglai-lingwai-yige-datemao-si-de-xiatian-ma-f7495e05]] says Yang coined "慢聚慢奏" to describe people slowly gathering and exchanging like jazz musicians.
- Loose structure: [[401-bianyu-manju-manzou-women-haineng-yinglai-lingwai-yige-datemao-si-de-xiatian-ma-f7495e05]] says the Dartmouth workshop abandoned its initial work plan and moved into freer discussions with shifting attendance.
- Possibility preservation: [[401-bianyu-manju-manzou-women-haineng-yinglai-lingwai-yige-datemao-si-de-xiatian-ma-f7495e05]] says Dartmouth's value was showing many AI possibilities rather than settling one route.
- Fragility under efficiency: [[401-bianyu-manju-manzou-women-haineng-yinglai-lingwai-yige-datemao-si-de-xiatian-ma-f7495e05]] says this mode becomes hard when efficiency dominates or an organization must win a defined battle.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not claim slow gathering fits every problem. It is presented for original innovation under uncertainty, not for urgent execution, safety-critical operations, or mature delivery work where planning and accountability may be necessary.

- Created the concept from Yang Bin's term and tied it to Dartmouth-style original innovation.
