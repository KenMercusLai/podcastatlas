# Subjectivity Through Failure / 失败中的主体性

Subjectivity through failure is [[164-guwen-de-liliang-qing-shou-xia-zhe-fen-qiannian-wei-bian-de-shenqing-dongjian-he-yongqi-940236576]]'s reading of how a person can retain agency when outcomes are bad or public evaluation is hostile. The episode uses [[WangAnshi|王安石]] and [[YouBaochanShanJi|《游褒禅山记》]] to argue that "尽吾志" is not empty self-comfort if the person really pursued the ideal with available strength.

The concept is not a claim that failure proves righteousness. Wang Anshi's reform limits, imperial power, and policy consequences still matter. The point is narrower: life judgment cannot be reduced to external success, reputation, or whether history later calls the project a failure.

## Key Claims
- Subjectivity is a practiced relation to one's own ideal, not only a modern slogan.
- Failure can still reveal agency if it includes informed effort and responsibility.
- The idea needs [[GoodIntentionsPoliticalLimits]] so it does not excuse harm caused by failed projects.
