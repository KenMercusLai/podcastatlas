# Synthetic Younger-Self Reflection

## Definition
Synthetic younger-self reflection is an autobiographical AI practice in which earlier personal records are used to model how a past version of someone might speak, value choices, or respond to the present self.

## Current Synthesis
In [[MichelleWong]]'s case, childhood diary entries anchored a synthetic version of herself at age 14. The value did not come from claiming literal access to that child, but from creating a constrained conversational surface for revisiting earlier language and priorities. Questions such as whether freedom or love mattered more turned archived writing into a prompt for present-day reflection.

This practice is best treated as interpretive reconstruction. It can surface discontinuities and continuities across a life, but the model's answers remain generated approximations shaped by the selected archive, system design, and present prompt. The exercise therefore needs privacy care, uncertainty, and a clear distinction from therapy, recovered memory, or communication with an independently existing person.

## Key Claims
- Personal diaries can ground a model more specifically than generic internet-derived material.
- Dialogue can make archived writing easier to interrogate than passive rereading alone.
- The synthetic voice is an interpretation of a selected record, not the historical younger person.
- Present-day prompt choices and model defaults influence what the earlier self appears to say.
- Reflective value does not establish clinical efficacy and should not displace human support.

## Evidence
- Diary corpus - [[tech-20261026-1026-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261026-1026-mp-tech-pod-128]] reports that [[MichelleWong]] supplied childhood diary entries to an AI system and conversed with a modeled 14-year-old self.
- Reflective questioning - [[tech-20261026-1026-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261026-1026-mp-tech-pod-128]] describes questions about freedom, love, and how her younger self might have answered.
- Personal assessment - [[tech-20261026-1026-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261026-1026-mp-tech-pod-128]] says Wong found the exercise therapeutic because the responses were weighted toward her diaries.
- Relationship boundary - [[tech-20261026-1026-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261026-1026-mp-tech-pod-128]] warns against making AI the only outlet for personal problems.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode does not describe the diary sample, model, retrieval method, privacy safeguards, failure cases, or any clinical evaluation. A generated younger self can sound authoritative while inventing language or values absent from the archive. Selective diaries may preserve only particular moods and contexts, and present-day users may project desired answers onto ambiguous output. The method should not be used to establish factual memory or replace professional mental-health care.

## What Changed
- Created the concept from Wong's diary-grounded younger-self experiment.
- Defined the output as interpretive reconstruction rather than recovered identity.
