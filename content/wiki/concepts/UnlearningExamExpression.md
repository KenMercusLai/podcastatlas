# Unlearning Exam Expression / 去除应试表达

## Definition
Unlearning exam expression / 去除应试表达 is EP280's name for undoing the habits that make students translate perception too quickly into approved themes, moral summaries, polished PPTs, or exam-trained images. It is a prerequisite for fieldwork that starts from what is actually seen.

## Current Synthesis
The concept applies to both cultural-subject schooling and art-school training. Students may learn to guess what teachers want, raise small topics into safe grand themes, and use concepts before experience. Art students face a parallel problem from pre-college studio training: drawing and making can become exam behavior rather than personal perception. EP280 treats "unlearn" as slow work because students need permission and practice to record awkward details before claiming a finished meaning.

## Key Claims
- Schooling can teach students to summarize and moralize before they have observed enough.
- Art training can leave visible technique while weakening the student's ability to locate an inner motive or question.
- PPT and theme-first assignments can hide thin perception behind fluent concepts.
- Field notes, documentary patience, and repeated teacher questioning can interrupt formulaic expression.
- Unlearning is not anti-knowledge; it clears room for knowledge to be rebuilt from concrete encounters.

- Art-training residue: [[ep280-guangzhou-meiyuan-fangwenji-dang-meishusheng-kaishi-zuo-tianye-diaocha-lr4-jmpdo-oo7w8sdmcgcw-chqc9]] compares art-school entrance training to exam drilling that can turn inner drawing impulse into test behavior.
- Note-taking alternative: [[ep280-guangzhou-meiyuan-fangwenji-dang-meishusheng-kaishi-zuo-tianye-diaocha-lr4-jmpdo-oo7w8sdmcgcw-chqc9]] describes teachers replacing PPT display with field notes and sustained discussion around what students saw.
- Rebuilt knowledge: [[ep280-guangzhou-meiyuan-fangwenji-dang-meishusheng-kaishi-zuo-tianye-diaocha-lr4-jmpdo-oo7w8sdmcgcw-chqc9]] has Qin Si frame the hardest part as "unlearn" before students can find a fresh angle outside inherited concepts.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not reject professional technique, concepts, or final projects. It argues that those tools become stronger when they come after observation and reflection rather than substituting for them.
