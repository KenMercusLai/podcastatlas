# Corporate Values as Labor Control / 企业价值观劳动控制

## Definition

Corporate values become labor control when mission, gratitude, dreams, family belonging, or personal growth are tied to evaluation and used to recast employer-imposed sacrifice as the worker's freely chosen moral development.

## Current Synthesis

Values are not inherently coercive. The boundary is institutional: whether employees can disagree, decline, rest, or report harm without losing standing, and whether practical training, staffing, pay, and safety support the declared ideals. In the Watami case, dream-centered founder study occupied substantial evaluation space while frontline training was minimal; volunteering and reports consumed recovery time; and social-purpose claims coexisted with ignored warnings about severe fatigue.

The mechanism is especially effective when the mission contains genuine good. Real agricultural or public-interest projects can attract conscientious workers and make endurance feel like service to something larger than a company. That sincerity increases the need to distinguish employee commitment from the employer's duty to design safe work.

## Key Claims

- Values become coercive through assessment and refusal penalties, not merely through inspirational language.
- Founder-centered learning can displace job preparation while strengthening personal loyalty.
- Genuine social-purpose work does not offset unsafe staffing, hours, training, or recovery conditions.
- “Family” and gratitude rhetoric can make requests for relief feel like personal or moral failure.
- A credible people-first culture must respond to recorded distress, not only collect reports about commitment.

## Evidence

- **Assessment architecture:** [[2026-qiuji-pian-fanwai-jinru-dachang-lianggeyue-ta-xuanzele-zisha-lmtfkbce-sjbl05lcdcvs8vhtnmc]] says founder philosophy and related study represented a large share of evaluation while kitchen instruction was brief.
- **Mission attraction:** [[2026-qiuji-pian-fanwai-jinru-dachang-lianggeyue-ta-xuanzele-zisha-lmtfkbce-sjbl05lcdcvs8vhtnmc]] says Mina was drawn by agriculture and changing society rather than by restaurant work alone.
- **Declared versus operational care:** [[2026-qiuji-pian-fanwai-jinru-dachang-lianggeyue-ta-xuanzele-zisha-lmtfkbce-sjbl05lcdcvs8vhtnmc]] contrasts people-as-family rhetoric with ignored fatigue reports and severe sleep loss.
- **External responsibility boundary:** [[2026-qiuji-pian-fanwai-jinru-dachang-lianggeyue-ta-xuanzele-zisha-lmtfkbce-sjbl05lcdcvs8vhtnmc]] argues that charity and environmental projects cannot substitute for basic responsibility to employees.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Shared values can coordinate judgment, motivate meaningful work, and support ethical standards. The concept applies when values are coupled to asymmetric control and material sacrifice without real refusal or safety protections. The source does not establish how every Watami employee experienced its philosophy or whether later reforms changed the system.

## What Changed

- Established the distinction between genuine social mission and its use as an evaluation-linked labor-control mechanism.
