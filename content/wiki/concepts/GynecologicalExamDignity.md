# Gynecological Exam Dignity / 妇科检查尊严

Gynecological exam dignity is the medical-care concept added by [[ep254-you-xiaoying-x-wenqi-x-du-sujuan-nvxing-zhe-yisheng-dou-zai-fanfu-xuexi-xuke-ziji-lm-w7ryj4zay6vldsrkadohj997d]]. [[YouXiaoying|游晓颖]] describes postpartum follow-up as feeling like punishment, while [[DuSujuan|杜素娟]] recalls refusing a row of trainee doctors being brought in to observe her exam.

The concept qualifies [[GenderedMedicalization]]. The issue is not that gynecological care is unnecessary; the problem is when necessary care is delivered as exposure, dismissal, or contempt. Fear, pain, embarrassment, and refusal remain valid patient data, especially when a scene involves intimate examination or teaching observation.

## Key Claims
- Medical need does not cancel dignity. Explanation, consent, privacy, and the right to refuse observers still matter.
- Dismissing fear as "矫情" can reproduce [[MenstrualStigma]] and [[FemaleBodyFreedom]] constraints inside a clinical setting.
- Teaching hospitals and routine exams need [[SubjectLedCare]] and [[DoctorPatientCommunication]] principles, not only technical correctness.
- The concept sits inside [[MedicalRiskManagement]] because humiliation and poor communication can make patients avoid needed exams later.
