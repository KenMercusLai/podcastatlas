# Treasure Hunt Retail

Treasure hunt retail is the [[costco-1]] pattern where a retailer rotates a meaningful share of merchandise so customers keep visiting to discover unusual or temporary deals. [[Costco]] uses treasure-hunt SKUs alongside everyday staples, making repeat visits feel useful without relying on ordinary sale gimmicks.

The concept complements [[LimitedSKUOperatingModel]]. Costco can keep the total SKU count low while still creating novelty because the changing items are curated and constrained rather than an unlimited marketplace assortment.

## Key Claims
- Scarce or rotating items can create repeat visits without raising the whole store's complexity too far.
- Treasure hunt works only if the base trust in price and quality is already strong.
- The mechanism adds emotional interest to a warehouse format that might otherwise feel purely utilitarian.
