# Work-Related Suicide Recognition / 过劳自杀工伤认定

## Definition

Work-related suicide recognition is the legal and institutional attribution of suicide or self-harm to mental illness and psychological burden materially caused by work, rather than excluding the event automatically as a private voluntary act.

## Current Synthesis

The Mori case shows that recognition depends on reconstructing actual control and cumulative burden rather than accepting payroll categories or employer labels. Scheduled shifts, early arrival, delayed departure, required learning, assessment-linked volunteering, reporting, transport constraints, sleep loss, visible decline, and ignored internal warnings formed one evidentiary system. The family's records and collective support moved the dispute from individual psychology toward employer safety responsibility.

Recognition is not the claim that every suicide has a single cause. It is a route for testing whether work materially produced or intensified mental illness and danger, including through chronic long hours rather than only a discrete accident, assault, or episode of harassment.

## Key Claims

- Suicide should not be categorically separated from occupational injury when work materially causes mental illness or loss of safety.
- Actual burden matters more than whether the employer called an activity voluntary or outside work.
- Chronic hours, sleep deprivation, role overload, and failed response to warnings can combine into foreseeable psychological danger.
- Personal vulnerability does not erase an employer's contribution or duty of care.
- Records, unions, media, administrative review, and litigation can make hidden labor and causation contestable.
- Recognition should remain causally careful because suicide is often multifactorial.

- **Failed prevention:** [[2026-qiuji-pian-fanwai-jinru-dachang-lianggeyue-ta-xuanzele-zisha-lmtfkbce-sjbl05lcdcvs8vhtnmc]] says colleagues and managers saw deterioration and headquarters received fatigue warnings without reducing the burden.
- **Recognition path:** [[2026-qiuji-pian-fanwai-jinru-dachang-lianggeyue-ta-xuanzele-zisha-lmtfkbce-sjbl05lcdcvs8vhtnmc]] traces initial denial, review, 2012 recognition, union support, litigation, and the 2015 settlement.
- **Doctrinal significance:** [[2026-qiuji-pian-fanwai-jinru-dachang-lianggeyue-ta-xuanzele-zisha-lmtfkbce-sjbl05lcdcvs8vhtnmc]] presents the case as helping make chronic overwork and work-caused mental illness legible within occupational safety.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The page is an institutional concept, not a clinical or legal test for an individual case. The episode's comparison with Chinese law and its account of Japanese thresholds and doctrinal change require primary legal verification. Work-related recognition can coexist with multiple contributing factors and should not be inferred from hours alone without evidence about illness, timing, control, and causation.

## What Changed

- Established a work-related-suicide framework integrating chronic burden, mental illness, actual control, warnings, and employer safety duty.
