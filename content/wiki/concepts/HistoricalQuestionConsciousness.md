## Definition
Historical question consciousness is the historian's practiced ability to decide what problem a source can answer, what context it needs, and which path through abundant material is worth following.

## Current Synthesis
The episode makes question consciousness both an archive method and an AI-era education method. Li Ji's work with hundreds of missionary letters required metadata tables and repeated source reading, but the decisive layer was the question that selected what mattered: where was 三台子, who were the girls, why could they write, and what does this show about ordinary people inside large history? In the classroom, AI makes event lookup easier, so humanistic teaching shifts toward question formation, conversation, experience, and judgment.

## Key Claims
- Archive abundance creates confusion unless a research question selects relevant patterns and returns the historian to the original source.
- Good questions usually come from historical context, not from isolated curiosity alone.
- AI can reduce extraction labor but cannot decide what is worth asking.
- Teaching history under AI should emphasize inquiry, judgment, and experience rather than only event recitation.
- Question consciousness connects method to ethics because the chosen question determines whose life becomes visible.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Question consciousness is not a license to force sources into a predetermined argument. The episode's own method starts from source surprises and fieldwork correction, so the question must remain revisable.
