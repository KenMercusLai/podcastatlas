# Attention Capacity Selection

## Definition
Attention capacity selection is the episode's frame for attention as a limited-capacity system that selects among external stimuli, internal thoughts, goals, and distractions.

## Current Synthesis
[[how-to-improve-motivation-overcome-procrastination-dr-masud-husain-scim3386045656]] treats attention as a control problem rather than a pure willpower trait. Bottom-up signals such as sudden threats, top-down goals such as searching for a red object, and internal thoughts can all compete for limited processing capacity. Motivation matters because intrinsically rewarding targets are easier to sustain, but the episode keeps the practical advice modest: reduce distractions, protect working memory load, and expect attention training to generalize less than people often hope.

## Key Claims
- Attention exists because the brain cannot process all sensory and internal information at full resolution.
- Bottom-up salience, top-down goals, and internal thought streams compete for selection.
- Working memory has quality limits: holding more items can reduce the fidelity of each item.
- Motivation and attention interact because rewarding or meaningful targets are easier to sustain.
- Environmental design, such as reducing notifications, email exposure, and noise, can protect attention.
- Attention training often improves trained tasks without reliably transferring into broad focus gains.

## Evidence
- Capacity definition - [[how-to-improve-motivation-overcome-procrastination-dr-masud-husain-scim3386045656]] defines attention as selecting important information because full sensory processing is impossible.
- Selection modes - [[how-to-improve-motivation-overcome-procrastination-dr-masud-husain-scim3386045656]] distinguishes bottom-up attention, top-down goal-directed attention, and internal thought competition.
- Working-memory load - [[how-to-improve-motivation-overcome-procrastination-dr-masud-husain-scim3386045656]] says Husain's work suggests that holding more items reduces the quality of each remembered item.
- Practical boundary - [[how-to-improve-motivation-overcome-procrastination-dr-masud-husain-scim3386045656]] recommends reducing distractions while noting that training gains often stay task-specific.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode does not claim a general attention-training protocol with broad transfer. It also keeps ADHD diagnosis difficult because attention varies continuously and impairment is partly judged by functional context.
