# Brandmauer Cordon Sanitaire

## Definition

Brandmauer cordon sanitaire is the German party-system norm under which mainstream parties refuse to form coalitions with [[AlternativeForGermany|AfD]], treating it as beyond the acceptable governing boundary.

## Current Synthesis

The source makes Brandmauer a coalition barrier, not an all-purpose electoral barrier. It can exclude AfD from ordinary coalition bargaining, but it becomes weaker if AfD can win enough seats to govern alone in [[SaxonyAnhalt|Saxony-Anhalt]]. The episode therefore uses Brandmauer to distinguish social and institutional refusal from the harder problem of outright electoral victory.

The concept also sits between democratic legitimacy and constitutional defense. Supporters of exclusion can view the firewall as protection against extremist rule; AfD supporters can frame exclusion as cartel behavior. The Saxony-Anhalt case turns that dispute from symbolism into a practical question of judges, security briefings, civil-society planning, fiscal authority, and public-service implementation.

## Key Claims

- A cordon sanitaire can block coalition entry without preventing a party from winning direct governing power.
- The norm depends on mainstream-party discipline and therefore becomes vulnerable when an excluded party can plausibly win a majority.
- Exclusion politics can be reframed by the excluded party as evidence that rival parties form an illegitimate cartel.
- Institutional containment can move from coalition refusal into rule design, security-information access, and civil-society preparation.
- The Saxony-Anhalt case makes the firewall a governance problem rather than only a campaign slogan.

## Evidence

- Coalition norm: [[right-in-front-afd-could-win-german-state-6a97f0a5a4a22c581728b85f]] describes the Brandmauer as the reason mainstream parties have long excluded AfD from power.
- Majority vulnerability: [[right-in-front-afd-could-win-german-state-6a97f0a5a4a22c581728b85f]] says the Saxony-Anhalt election is different because AfD may be able to win a majority on its own.
- Cartel framing: [[right-in-front-afd-could-win-german-state-6a97f0a5a4a22c581728b85f]] says AfD portrays rival parties as cartel parties.
- Containment measures: [[right-in-front-afd-could-win-german-state-6a97f0a5a4a22c581728b85f]] reports parliamentary rule changes and discussion of limiting sensitive security access for a possible AfD interior minister.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source does not prove whether Brandmauer will hold nationally, how voters interpret it, or whether institutional containment measures would survive court or political challenge. The claim is narrower: a coalition firewall is structurally different from a direct-majority barrier.
