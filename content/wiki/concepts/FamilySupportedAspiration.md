# Family-Supported Aspiration / 家庭承托的愿望

[[FamilySupportedAspiration|Family-supported aspiration]] is the pattern where a person's dream is made possible, constrained, judged, and given meaning by family and close relationships rather than by solitary will alone. [[ep243-jiang-qiming-x-li-xueqin-x-shuang-xuetao-xiang-zai-dongbei-shangtian-zhe-shi-er-haishi-tai-fuza-le-lkqbo0zg5y7p1fo4x90n8yq394t6]] adds the concept through [[FeixingjiaFilm|《飞行家》]].

The source explicitly resists a simple dream-versus-family frame. [[LiXueqin|李雪琴]] notes that many pursuit stories make relatives, lovers, and friends into obstacles, while this film surrounds Li Mingqi with people who support him. [[ShuangXuetao|双雪涛]] frames the final leap as personal pursuit, family responsibility, and ethical realization at once.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-161-caiyun-he-renji-guanxi-de-liangzi-jiuchan-1-lhkqrhbrejnhuxoouem8x8-1aujj|Hanji 161]] adds a more spiritualized cousin concept through [[FamilyBlessingOutcomeFrame|家人祝福与成事框架]]. The source says consequential actions such as marriage, entrepreneurship, or retreat practice are easier to carry when family members offer support, blessing, and understanding. This does not replace the practical aspiration frame; it shows the same dependency expressed through 因缘 and fate language.

## Key Claims
- A dream can be relational without becoming less real; support, questioning, obligation, and practical help all shape what the dream means.
- Family may provide love and resources while also carrying education pressure, role hierarchy, intergenerational projection, and guilt.
- Ordinary aspiration differs from heroic individualism because it keeps the costs and dependencies visible.
- This concept complements [[ResponsibilityAgainstRomanticEscape]]: responsibility can discipline fantasy without requiring the wish to disappear.
- Hanji 161 adds that family support may be experienced not only as resources or permission but as blessing and relational alignment.
