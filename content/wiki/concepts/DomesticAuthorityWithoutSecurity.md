# Domestic Authority Without Security / 有权理家而无保障

## Definition

Domestic authority without security is the condition in which a person exercises substantial operational control over a household but lacks durable protection over marital fidelity, status, property, succession, bodily safety, or institutional survival.

## Current Synthesis

[[120-honglou-jinfen-jinling-shierchai-zhi-wangxifeng-lixu8fhy-rjn0fkf01kmqswxny3]] grounds the concept in [[WangXifeng|王熙凤]]. She can allocate work, enforce discipline, manage accounts, represent the [[JiaFamilyHousehold|贾府]], and earn elders' trust, but this authority is delegated inside a patriarchal and declining family. It does not give her reciprocal control over [[JiaLian|贾琏]]'s sexual conduct, guarantee her place in marriage, or protect her when household power reverses.

The distinction clarifies why visible power and underlying vulnerability can coexist. It helps explain Wang Xifeng's investment in control, face, and exclusivity, but explanation does not excuse retaliatory violence. Her treatment of 尤二姐 shows how a constrained actor can still dominate people with less protection and remain responsible for doing so.

## Key Claims

- Operational household command is not the same as secure ownership, legal independence, marital reciprocity, or protection from expulsion.
- Delegated authority remains vulnerable to the family members and institutions that authorize it.
- A person can be powerful downward in a hierarchy while remaining insecure upward or within marriage.
- Structural vulnerability can intensify control-seeking without determining or absolving harmful choices.
- Institutional decline can consume managerial labor while making both authority and personal safety progressively less reliable.

## Evidence

### Operational command

- [[120-honglou-jinfen-jinling-shierchai-zhi-wangxifeng-lixu8fhy-rjn0fkf01kmqswxny3]] describes Wang Xifeng's work allocation, accountability, ritual fluency, social judgment, and ability to coordinate a large elite household.

### Marital asymmetry

- [[120-honglou-jinfen-jinling-shierchai-zhi-wangxifeng-lixu8fhy-rjn0fkf01kmqswxny3]] contrasts her desire for exclusivity with Jia Lian's repeated affairs, threatened violence, and greater latitude under the gender order.

### Downward power under upward insecurity

- [[120-honglou-jinfen-jinling-shierchai-zhi-wangxifeng-lixu8fhy-rjn0fkf01kmqswxny3]] uses the 尤二姐 case to show Wang Xifeng exercising severe control over a more vulnerable woman while her own marital position feels threatened.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept is derived from one literary discussion and should not be treated as a complete legal history of Qing marriage or household property. Wang Xifeng's authority is real, and emphasizing insecurity can wrongly minimize the suffering she causes. The episode itself leaves some acts and motives contested, including the pregnancy episode and her memory of 尤二姐's death anniversary.

## What Changed

- Created the concept to separate practical household command from durable personal security.
- Added the two-directional power claim: vulnerability to superiors can coexist with domination of less protected people.
- Preserved moral responsibility against explanations that make structure a total defense.
