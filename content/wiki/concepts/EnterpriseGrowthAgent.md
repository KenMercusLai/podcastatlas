# Enterprise Growth Agent / 企业级增长 Agent

Enterprise growth agent is [[Lingyang|瓴羊]] CEO [[PengXinyu|彭新宇]]'s frame in [[e248-yi-ge-cui-fahuo-ai-yao-paotong-260-bu-he-ali-lingyang-pengxinyu-liaoliao-zhongguoshi-fde-9e923c4c-1c87-499b-90a4-9a21cc83e4b1]] for agents that improve business growth and circulation rather than only automating isolated tasks. The source organizes the field around marketing, sales, customer service, and operations, with agents evaluated by customer-service throughput, marketing ROI, sales productivity, conversion, or other measurable business effects.

The concept sits between [[DigitalEmployees]] and [[BusinessLedAITransformation]]. It treats agents as role-bearing workers, but the reason to deploy them is not novelty or headcount theater; it is whether they can act inside real workflows where the company spends the most people, money, or time.

## Key Claims
- Growth agents should begin from outcome-rich workflows such as投放,客服,销售拜访,复购服务,投诉处理, and cross-system operational queries.
- The "4+X" pattern starts with preset roles for marketing, sales, customer service, and operations, then adds the enterprise's own data, contexts, approvals, and authority boundaries.
- The agent should learn from best performers, such as top投手,首席客服, or销冠, rather than only from generic model knowledge.
- The commercial model can be labor-substitution pricing for support work or uplift/effect-linked pricing for growth work.
- Enterprise growth agents require [[EnterpriseDataActivation]] and [[EnterpriseOperationalMemory]] because they need customer, order, platform, workflow, and exception context to act.
