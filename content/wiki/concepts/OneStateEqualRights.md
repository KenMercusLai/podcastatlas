# One-State Equal Rights

## Definition
One-state equal rights is a proposed political framework in which Jews and Palestinians live within one state under equal citizenship and political rights, without expelling either people.

## Current Synthesis
The episode presents this as a Palestinian-liberation alternative to a state organized around permanent Jewish demographic predominance. It joins equal residence and citizenship to refugee return while acknowledging that the physical return of every refugee is unlikely. The proposal remains normative and under-specified rather than an implementation plan.

## Key Claims
- Equal rights, not expulsion of Jews, is presented as the proposal's core.
- The model challenges ethnonational demographic privilege rather than Jewish residence.
- Refugee return or remedy remains part of political equality.
- Security, institutions, consent, and transition arrangements are unresolved.

## Evidence
- Normative proposal: [[ep177-ba-yi-chongtu-women-gai-ruhe-lijie-yaoyuan-de-kusheng-ckwriaiepeo1abaaaac8efxz]] says a mainstream Palestinian liberation direction can be framed as equal Jewish and Palestinian rights in one state.
- Return qualification: [[ep177-ba-yi-chongtu-women-gai-ruhe-lijie-yaoyuan-de-kusheng-ckwriaiepeo1abaaaac8efxz]] retains return as a claim while conceding universal physical return is unrealistic.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not explain constitutional design, security integration, minority protection, collective self-determination, transitional justice, resource allocation, or how consent would be built. Existing wiki material also records a two-state prescription, so no consensus model is established.

- Created the concept as the episode's preferred but explicitly incomplete political horizon.
