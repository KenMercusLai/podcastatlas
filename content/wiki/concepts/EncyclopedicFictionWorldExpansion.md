# Encyclopedic Fiction World Expansion / 百科式小说世界扩展

## Definition
Encyclopedic fiction world expansion is a literary method in which technical explanation, classification, documents, history, and multiple genres enlarge a plot object into a model of its surrounding material, social, and philosophical world.

## Current Synthesis
[[MobyDick|《白鲸》]] supplies the founding case. The hunt gives readers a route through the book, but chapters on whale anatomy, processing, equipment, work, etymology, scripture, and industrial history are not merely delays. Together with sermons, essays, poetry, drama, quotation, humor, and viewpoint shifts, they ask how many forms of attention are needed to approach an object that resists complete understanding.

The method changes the criterion of relevance. A passage can matter without advancing the chase when it reveals the labor system, energy economy, social order, religious imagination, or human scale carried inside the voyage. Reading therefore becomes exploratory rather than only destination-driven.

## Key Claims
- Plot can function as a navigational spine without being the work's complete source of value.
- Technical and documentary detail can build social and philosophical scale when integrated with voice and form.
- Genre plurality allows a work to approach one object from incompatible or incomplete perspectives.
- Apparent digression can preserve labor, industry, and ordinary life excluded from heroic summary.
- Encyclopedic expansion invites curiosity but can also produce real reader fatigue.

## Evidence
- Knowledge expansion - [[vol-356-baijing-yongchang-de-shenghuo-yishougou-qing-dahai-daizou-wode-aichou-gkwriw4oms-ma1lixgtjj3um]] traces movement from whales to whaling technology, energy, commerce, society, religion, nature, and existence.
- Formal plurality - [[vol-356-baijing-yongchang-de-shenghuo-yishougou-qing-dahai-daizou-wode-aichou-gkwriw4oms-ma1lixgtjj3um]] identifies etymology, extracts, classification, essay, poetry, drama, humor, and changing narrative person.
- Industrial archive - [[vol-356-baijing-yongchang-de-shenghuo-yishougou-qing-dahai-daizou-wode-aichou-gkwriw4oms-ma1lixgtjj3um]] argues that detailed whaling passages preserve work and production practices from a declining industry.
- Reading friction - [[vol-356-baijing-yongchang-de-shenghuo-yishougou-qing-dahai-daizou-wode-aichou-gkwriw4oms-ma1lixgtjj3um]] records impatience with informational chapters and proposes an adventure-game “main quest plus compendium” frame rather than denying the difficulty.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Not every accumulation of facts creates meaningful literary scale; detail can remain inert, inaccurate, or structurally excessive. The founding evidence is one podcast's reading of one novel, and historical assertions require direct verification. The concept describes a possible function of digression, not a rule that readers must admire every digression.
