# Land Reform And Equality In Development

Land reform and equality in development is the early-condition frame in [[getting-entrepreneurial-in-korea-summer-school]]. [[OliverKim]] says land reform is one policy shared by [[SouthKorea|South Korea]] and [[Taiwan]], and the episode explains that land redistribution may raise food production, create markets, and generate demand for factory goods.

The source keeps the claim qualified. It says evidence on land reform is mixed, while treating relatively equal income distribution as important because more people had a stake in long-term national development. In the wiki, the concept connects institutional starting conditions to later [[ExportDiscipline]] and [[IndustrialLadder]] movement rather than replacing them.
