## Definition

Festival demand signaling is the process by which sellers and buyers use screenings, audience response, reviews, test data, awards, advocacy, scarcity, and competitive interest to infer whether a film has a reachable audience.

## Current Synthesis

The signals described in [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-20c3269bc63dde599e9e1898d7da833b-33908266-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-20c3269bc63dde599e9e1898d7da833b-33908266|Anatomy of a movie market]] do not reveal demand directly; they create partial, socially amplified evidence under time pressure. Strong reactions can attract buyers, a targeted test screening can answer an audience objection, and awards or credible advocates can reduce perceived risk. Yet publicity, room composition, expectation management, and staged scarcity also shape what market participants observe.

## Key Claims

- A screening is both an audience event and a market-information event.
- Expectations alter interpretation: too much hype can turn positive response into perceived disappointment.
- Targeted test screenings can challenge a buyer's assumptions about who will embrace a film.
- Awards and trusted advocates can validate demand when buyers remain hesitant.
- Scarce experiences and visible competition can intensify attention without proving durable demand.
- Decentralized online reviews make early narratives faster and harder for sellers to control.

## Evidence

- Expectation calibration: [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-20c3269bc63dde599e9e1898d7da833b-33908266-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-20c3269bc63dde599e9e1898d7da833b-33908266|Anatomy of a movie market]] uses *Napoleon Dynamite* to argue that restrained hype can preserve discovery value.
- Audience evidence: [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-20c3269bc63dde599e9e1898d7da833b-33908266-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-20c3269bc63dde599e9e1898d7da833b-33908266|Anatomy of a movie market]] presents the Harlem test screening of *Precious* as evidence against distributors' audience doubts.
- External validation: [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-20c3269bc63dde599e9e1898d7da833b-33908266-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-20c3269bc63dde599e9e1898d7da833b-33908266|Anatomy of a movie market]] links awards and advocacy from Tyler Perry and Oprah Winfrey to reduced perceived risk.
- Staged attention: [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-20c3269bc63dde599e9e1898d7da833b-33908266-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-20c3269bc63dde599e9e1898d7da833b-33908266|Anatomy of a movie market]] describes an exclusive Wu-Tang listening event as “Sundance theater.”

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Festival audiences are selected, socially visible, and exposed to publicity, so their reactions may not generalize. Test screenings, awards, advocates, and scarcity can supply useful evidence while also reflecting access or strategic promotion. The source does not quantify predictive accuracy.

## What Changed

- Established a unified account of audience, critic, award, advocacy, scarcity, and competition signals.
- Made signal construction and predictive limits explicit rather than treating enthusiasm as direct demand.
