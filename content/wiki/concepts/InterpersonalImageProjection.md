# Interpersonal Image Projection / 人际意象投射

## Definition
Interpersonal image projection is the pattern where a person sees another person through a constructed image made from observed behavior, memory, fear, desire, labels, and disowned parts of the self. In E46, this is the mechanism behind the claim that "the person in my eyes may partly be myself."

## Current Synthesis
The concept joins the film reading of [[GoodFriendsFilm|《好朋友们》]] with [[JidduKrishnamurti]]'s image/register vocabulary. Hyun-tae's belief that In-chul abandoned his friends survives for seventeen years because it is not just a factual hypothesis; it is a stable model that filters later evidence. Krishnamurti's frame then generalizes the problem: in close relationships, two people may be interacting through accumulated images rather than meeting each other directly.

[[DuanYu|段誉]] and [[WangYuyan|王语嫣]] add a literary romantic case. Wang Yuyan's appearance activates Duan Yu's prior jade-statue “神仙姐姐” image, and his repeated use of that title suggests that intense, sacrificial feeling can remain directed partly toward an internal ideal. The revised ending can be read as release from that image, but the source preserves disagreement over whether this thematic gain outweighs the lost romantic payoff.

## Key Claims
- Familiarity can strengthen projection because old memories and labels make the image feel evidential.
- A judgment about another person may contain real observations, personal fear, and hidden self-knowledge at the same time.
- Projection can become self-confirming when later actions are interpreted through the existing image.
- Communication can thicken the image if it only adds more charged registrations to an old model.
- Seeing the image as image is the first loosening of the projection, even when no repeatable method is available.
- Sincerity and sacrifice measure intensity of attachment, not necessarily accuracy of perception or knowledge of the beloved.

## Evidence
- Film case: [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] reads Hyun-tae's false belief about In-chul's childhood betrayal as a projection shaped by fear and by Hyun-tae's own behavior.
- Image/register frame: [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] uses Krishnamurti to define the image as accumulated memory, judgment, naming, and emotional registration.
- Relationship generalization: [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] argues that the loved one, parent, child, coworker, or friend in front of us is often seen through a mental construction rather than directly.
- Romantic literary case: [[058-wuren-buyuan-youqing-jienie-duanyu-caishi-tianlongbabu-diyi-zhujue-lmwnsc-ey8o7ysczh0qynwypqyqp]] links Duan Yu's attraction to Wang Yuyan with her resemblance to the jade statue and his repeated “神仙姐姐” address.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept should not be flattened into "all perceptions are false." The sources say images include real appearance or conduct by the other person; the problem is that observation, projection, and self-material become hard to separate once the model feels complete. The Duan Yu reading is a podcast interpretation rather than proof that he feels nothing for Wang Yuyan herself, and the revised ending remains contested rather than a uniquely correct cure for projection.

## What Changed
- Added romantic idealization as a literary case in which sincere sacrifice coexists with incomplete perception.
- Made version-sensitive release from projection a possible reading rather than a settled prescription.
