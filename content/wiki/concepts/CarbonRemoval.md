# Carbon Removal

[[taking-the-shine-off-albedo-and-global-temperatures-6a730fdaca067b295bab9760]] adds carbon removal as one response to rising [[EarthEnergyImbalance]]. [[OliverMorton]] says reducing greenhouse-gas warming would require not only stopping carbon dioxide emissions but also removing carbon dioxide already in the atmosphere, while [[SolarGeoengineering]] is presented as a separate, controversial reflectivity intervention.

Carbon removal is the climate-mitigation category of taking already-emitted CO2 out of the atmosphere rather than only reducing future emissions. [[tech-20260713-tech-pod-128-tech-20260713-tech-pod-128]] adds the concept through [[DirectAirCapture]], with [[HeirloomCarbonTechnologies|Heirloom Carbon Technologies]] as the concrete company example.

The episode frames carbon removal as necessary but contested. It says climate mitigation likely needs both emissions cuts and removal of existing atmospheric CO2, yet critics worry that removal can consume large amounts of energy, remain expensive, and become a license for continued fossil-fuel production if used as a substitute for emissions reduction.

[[peter-tsr-v3-audio-converted-peter-tsr-v3-audio-converted]] adds a biomass and storage route through [[CharmIndustrial|Charm Industrial]]. [[PeterReinhardt]] describes [[BioOilCarbonRemoval]] as converting biomass such as wildfire residues into bio-oil and injecting it underground. The source also adds a market-formation layer through [[FrontierCarbonRemoval|Frontier]] and [[AdvancedMarketCommitment|advanced market commitments]], while making [[CarbonRemovalPermitting]] a central scale constraint.

[[tech-20260728-0728-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260728-0728-mp-tech-pod-128]] adds an ocean-chemistry route through [[MarineCarbonRemoval]]. [[Vesta]]'s field demonstration near Duck, North Carolina, uses olivine in an [[OceanAlkalinityEnhancement]] experiment, shifting the carbon-removal question toward bicarbonate formation, seawater monitoring, marine-life effects, and whether distributed ocean storage can be verified.

## Key Claims
- Carbon removal addresses existing atmospheric CO2, while emissions reduction prevents additional pollution.
- Technical removal does not automatically settle climate politics because cost, energy use, storage permanence, and fossil-fuel involvement all matter.
- Corporate buyers can help create demand, but demand does not remove the need for credible [[CarbonStoragePermanence]] and lower [[CarbonRemovalCostCurve|removal costs]].
- The category is politically unusual because some projects retain bipartisan support even amid broader climate-program cuts.
- Carbon removal includes different physical pathways; bio-oil storage has different feedstock, logistics, permitting, and verification problems from direct air capture.
- Marine pathways add another distinct verification problem: the project may be chemically plausible, but claimed storage has to be measured through ocean chemistry and lifecycle accounting rather than a contained capture-and-injection chain.
- The albedo source separates carbon removal from [[SolarGeoengineering]]: both respond to warming, but one changes greenhouse-gas stock while the other changes planetary reflectivity.
