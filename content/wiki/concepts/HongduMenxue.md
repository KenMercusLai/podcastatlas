# 鸿都门学 / Hongdu Menxue

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-914-xinren-de-ren-weihe-zong-shang-ni-zui-shen-lvwit-rzrkscn5a3h6wblntxppso|Hanji 914]] introduces 鸿都门学 / Hongdu Menxue as a late-Han court-sponsored literary and artistic channel favored by [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]. The episode says Ling has Hongdu literary figures painted and praised, nominally to encourage later students.

The host's reading makes Hongdu Menxue political rather than merely educational. Because Ling resents the士人 field after the [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] and [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] anti-eunuch crisis, he can use Hongdu writers and artists as a rival prestige group against the famous士人 classifications admired by court society.

[[YangQiuLateHan|阳球]]'s memorial gives the concept its institutional edge. He accuses the favored Hongdu figures of shallow learning, dependence on powerful relatives and elites, delegated writing, and rapid appointment through favor. The warning is ignored, so Hongdu Menxue becomes an early appointment-channel problem before the same episode moves to open Xidi office sale.

The concept differs from ordinary education policy. Its wiki role is to mark how cultural recognition can become [[CourtOfficeCapture|朝廷开府任官失序]] when the ruler uses literary prestige to bypass or counter an established personnel field, and how the resulting critique can still fail through [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|君臣反馈失灵]].

## Key Claims
- Hongdu Menxue is framed as a favored court cultural channel, not just a school.
- The source reads the channel as part of Emperor Ling's anti士人 politics after older anti-eunuch conflicts.
- Yang Qiu's opposition turns Hongdu Menxue into a personnel-selection issue because literary favor is tied to office advancement.
- The ignored memorial shows that accurate criticism can fail when it attacks a ruler's chosen political counterweight.
- Hanji 914 places Hongdu Menxue immediately before Xidi office sale, making both part of a broader appointment-order breakdown.
