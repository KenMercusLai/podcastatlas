# 鸿都门学 / Hongdu Menxue

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-907-suoyou-de-bing-doushi-ni-de-xinbing-ltqzyohcauduwuanlvqph9l81d79|Hanji 907]] adds an early literary-favor layer before Cai Yong's Hanji 908 warning and the later Hongdu establishment explanations. [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] draws fu-writing students and later edict-writing, bird-seal, and calligraphy talent toward the Hongdu gate; the episode says figures such as Yue Song and Jia Hu use that channel to recommend flatterers and poorly regarded people. Hongdu therefore appears first as ruler-favored cultural access that can turn amusement, writing, and court proximity into appointment advantage.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-908-nijing-chu-qiangzhe-xialu-chu-yongzhe-gudu-chu-zhizhe-lqih1dradhzi0mipxsaiedd52dlj|Hanji 908]] adds a pre-Hongdu warning layer from [[CaiYong|蔡邕]]'s 177 CE memorial. Before [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] formalizes [[HongduMenxue|鸿都门学]] as a favored channel, Cai already criticizes the court for giving appointment weight to writing, calligraphy, painting, fu composition, and other "small ways" when they displace deeper office responsibility and classical-ritual grounding.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-910-shehui-shengcun-wo-quan-ni-duodian-chengfu-lp2fvuyb8ampzoeyr7znztnr0fgk|Hanji 910]] backfills Hongdu Menxue at its establishment and political-motive layer. The episode reads [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s support source-scopedly as an attempt to route appointment power around [[Taixue|太学]], great-clan control, and the察举 recommendation field.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-911-gudai-gongwuyuan-ye-neng-bao-fenpei-ls3pxu9xlpuulsdailx8tcoylv9e|Hanji 911]] then adds the curriculum and appointment-channel layer. The episode presents Hongdu as a court-sponsored literary and artistic channel teaching fu writing, fiction-like narrative, letters, calligraphy, and painting rather than the classical curriculum associated with Taixue.

The new source makes the school political before later remonstrance scenes. Hongdu students receive a faster appointment path, so Taixue students and士大夫 experience the channel as [[OfficialSelectionChannelCompetition|选官通道竞争]]: a finite office market is being redistributed toward a ruler-favored and eunuch-linked group. [[ShiYiguanLateHan|师宜官]] and [[LiangHuLateHan|梁鹄]] also keep the artistic-talent branch visible, so the page should not reduce Hongdu only to a personnel scandal.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-912-mimi-daodi-gai-bugai-shuo-chukou-lhsgipxwc17pahc-r6jl7xdyrs2a|Hanji 912]] adds the omen-remonstrance prelude to the Hongdu Menxue page. Asked about palace anomalies, [[YangCiLateHan|杨赐]] criticizes Hongdu-favored flatterers rising while capable people remain buried, and [[CaiYong|蔡邕]] connects personnel selection to ornamental writing, private request, and rapid promotion.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-914-xinren-de-ren-weihe-zong-shang-ni-zui-shen-lvwit-rzrkscn5a3h6wblntxppso|Hanji 914]] introduces 鸿都门学 / Hongdu Menxue as a late-Han court-sponsored literary and artistic channel favored by [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]. The episode says Ling has Hongdu literary figures painted and praised, nominally to encourage later students.

The host's reading makes Hongdu Menxue political rather than merely educational. Because Ling resents the士人 field after the [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] and [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] anti-eunuch crisis, he can use Hongdu writers and artists as a rival prestige group against the famous士人 classifications admired by court society.

[[YangQiuLateHan|阳球]]'s memorial gives the concept its institutional edge. He accuses the favored Hongdu figures of shallow learning, dependence on powerful relatives and elites, delegated writing, and rapid appointment through favor. The warning is ignored, so Hongdu Menxue becomes an early appointment-channel problem before the same episode moves to open Xidi office sale.

The concept differs from ordinary education policy. Its wiki role is to mark how cultural recognition can become [[CourtOfficeCapture|朝廷开府任官失序]] when the ruler uses literary prestige to bypass or counter an established personnel field, and how the resulting critique can still fail through [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|君臣反馈失灵]].

## Key Claims
- Hanji 907 adds the early court-access layer: literary and calligraphic talent around Hongdu can become a path for flatterers before later remonstrants name the selection problem.
- Hanji 910 adds the establishment/motive layer: Hongdu Menxue can be read as Emperor Ling's attempt to weaken Taixue and great-clan control over appointments.
- Hanji 911 adds the establishment/curriculum layer: Hongdu Menxue teaches literary and artistic skills and competes with Taixue as an office-access path.
- Hanji 912 adds that Hongdu appears first as part of omen-remonstrance criticism, before Hanji 914 develops the channel as an appointment-order problem.
- Hongdu Menxue is framed as a favored court cultural channel, not just a school.
- The source reads the channel as part of Emperor Ling's anti士人 politics after older anti-eunuch conflicts.
- Yang Qiu's opposition turns Hongdu Menxue into a personnel-selection issue because literary favor is tied to office advancement.
- The ignored memorial shows that accurate criticism can fail when it attacks a ruler's chosen political counterweight.
- Hanji 914 places Hongdu Menxue immediately before Xidi office sale, making both part of a broader appointment-order breakdown.
