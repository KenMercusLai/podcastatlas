# PD-1/VEGF Bispecific

PD-1/VEGF bispecific is the [[Akeso|康方生物]]-anchored mechanism in [[vol-117-shengwu-yiyao-de-2025-chaodi-zhongguo-yanfa-jiaolv-he-xinwang-jiwei-lmhral0rmq6tohiqdwsgmfapnyn7]]. The episode describes a Chinese phase-3 readout where a PD-1/VEGF bispecific showed strong progression-free-survival data against [[Keytruda|K药]] in the source's account, making the asset difficult for global pharma to ignore.

The source is careful about uncertainty. [[XiaoPTeacher|小P老师]] says the mechanism is not fully explained, with possible explanations including same-molecule synergy or tumor-local concentration. [[Merck|默沙东]] is described as publicly asking for overall-survival evidence while still acting defensively through later Chinese asset buying.

## Key Claims
- PD-1/VEGF bispecifics try to combine immune-checkpoint and angiogenesis-related intervention in one molecule.
- The episode treats Akeso's data as a major China-biotech signal, not as settled proof that the mechanism will always beat PD-1 monotherapy.
- Mechanistic uncertainty and final survival data remain important.
- The asset category is commercially strategic because it sits near [[PD1MarketSaturation]] and [[MultinationalPharmaPatentCliffAnxiety]].
