# PD-1/VEGF Bispecific

## Definition
PD-1/VEGF bispecifics are large-molecule therapies designed to combine immune-checkpoint intervention with inhibition of tumor angiogenesis in one molecule.

## Current Synthesis
The sources use [[Akeso|康方生物]] and [[Ivonescimab|伊沃西]] to show why this mechanism became globally important: reported Chinese phase-3 progression-free-survival results against [[Keytruda|K药]] forced multinational attention and supported cross-border development. The same evidence also sets a boundary. Same-molecule synergy and tumor-local concentration remain proposed explanations, strong PFS does not settle OS, and multinational approval still requires population- and regulator-relevant trials.

## Key Claims
- The modality tries to join immune release and anti-angiogenesis in one molecular design.
- Reported comparative data made the category a meaningful clinical and strategic challenge to PD-1 monotherapy.
- Mechanistic rationale remains less certain than headline clinical differentiation may imply.
- PFS strength must be separated from overall-survival, safety, quality-of-life, and later-treatment effects.
- Global market entry requires multinational clinical execution rather than automatic transfer of one country's result.

## Evidence
- Comparative signal - [[vol-117-shengwu-yiyao-de-2025-chaodi-zhongguo-yanfa-jiaolv-he-xinwang-jiwei-lmhral0rmq6tohiqdwsgmfapnyn7]] describes strong Chinese phase-3 PFS data against Keytruda, while [[lkgutywsgyzvtv9zy7ct8ouoj_u-lkgutywsgyzvtv9zy7ct8ouoj_u]] uses ivonescimab's advanced-lung-cancer result as the episode's central example.
- Mechanism uncertainty - [[vol-117-shengwu-yiyao-de-2025-chaodi-zhongguo-yanfa-jiaolv-he-xinwang-jiwei-lmhral0rmq6tohiqdwsgmfapnyn7]] presents same-molecule synergy and tumor-local concentration as hypotheses rather than settled biology.
- Endpoint discipline - [[156-shengwu-yiyao-de-2026-dang-shichang-bu-zai-wei-bd-zaodong-zhongguo-yaoqi-de-xingchen-dahai-cai-ganggang-zhankai-lil-ugrzq8uvzviq3f8i-wm9ilup]] says VEGF-related tumor shrinkage can strengthen PFS while OS remains harder to interpret because of resistance, subsequent therapy, and patient condition.
- Global evidence - [[lkgutywsgyzvtv9zy7ct8ouoj_u-lkgutywsgyzvtv9zy7ct8ouoj_u]] describes trials across Europe, the United States, Australia, and Japan as part of seeking local approvals.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
All three inputs are structured podcast summaries rather than primary trial publications, protocols, labels, or regulatory decisions. A favorable result in one indication, population, endpoint, or treatment line does not establish class-wide superiority. Safety, OS, quality of life, manufacturing, reimbursement, and regulator-specific evidence remain separate gates.

## What Changed
- Added ivonescimab as the named drug case and multinational development as a distinct evidence-portability gate.
- Preserved the prior distinction between strong PFS and unsettled OS or mechanistic proof.
