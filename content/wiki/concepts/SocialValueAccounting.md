# Social Value Accounting

## Definition
Social value accounting is the source's model of how brains assign value to status, mating, attention, reciprocity, protection, and other relationship-relevant information while tracking exchanges across time.

## Current Synthesis
Macaque experiments in the episode suggest that social images can carry measurable economic value and that grooming relationships involve a running record of giving and receiving. The broader human analogy is not a literal one-to-one ledger: power differences and different needs can create exchange rates in which attention, protection, affiliation, status, or care have unequal value to each participant.

## Key Claims
- Social information can compete directly with material reward in valuation tasks.
- Status and mating cues can acquire positive value, while low-value social images may require compensation to view.
- Reciprocity can be tracked across minutes or weeks rather than settled immediately.
- Hierarchy changes the exchange rate between acts such as grooming and protection.
- Human relationship balance may depend on subjective value, not equal counts of messages, favors, or time.

## Evidence
- Pay-per-view valuation - [[how-to-make-better-decisions-dr-michael-platt-scim9606820714]] reports monkeys giving up juice to see sexually or status-relevant images.
- Grooming ledgers - [[how-to-make-better-decisions-dr-michael-platt-scim9606820714]] describes computer-vision tracking and neural signals associated with who owed grooming to whom.
- Power-sensitive exchange - [[how-to-make-better-decisions-dr-michael-platt-scim9606820714]] uses grooming exchanged for later protection to show that reciprocity need not be like-for-like.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The human relationship examples are analogies from primate and laboratory evidence, not proof that affection, altruism, texting, or partnership can be reduced to conscious transactions. Economic value in a task does not exhaust moral, cultural, or subjective meaning.

## What Changed
- Created a hierarchy-sensitive synthesis of social information value and delayed reciprocity.
