## Definition

执行细节的态度归因 / Attitude Attribution from Execution Details is the interpretation of a concrete action, omission, or preparation failure as evidence about an actor's hidden respect, loyalty, care, or competence. It distinguishes the observable operational defect from the larger interpersonal meaning assigned to it.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-334-1-shenwei-kuli-de-ta-ruhe-yibubu-lunwei-yehu-lje_l2eoefz2pw2-yj9qve4on94w|Hanji 334-1]] supplies a power-unequal example, and overlapping [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-334-2-wei-shenme-laoban-geng-xihuan-zuo-biaomian-wenzhang-de-ren-lp6r9orqzbzy8ffkq1fkneds3xzf|Hanji 334-2]] makes its workplace generalization explicit. An unrepaired road to [[GanquanPalace|甘泉宫]] produces a bad journey, but [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] interprets it as evidence that [[YiZongWesternHan|义纵]] did not expect his recovery or did not take the ruler seriously. The practical failure and the symbolic inference are related but not identical: road condition is observable, while contempt, disloyalty, and enemy orchestration are interpretations requiring additional evidence.

The concept therefore captures both a real management risk and an inference boundary. Repeated travel and known responsibility may make neglect informative about anticipation or control, especially when the neglected party holds power. Hanji 334-2 adds a snapshot problem: a superior may see one phone check or one unfinished visible task and infer a stable pattern of idleness or disregard. Yet the same detail can arise from limited capacity, divided responsibility, incomplete information, timing, or manipulation by third parties. The sources' workplace and family analogies are useful explanatory extensions, not proof that every omission reveals attitude.

## Key Claims

- Concrete execution details can operate simultaneously as practical outcomes and social signals.
- Power asymmetry raises the stakes because the person assigning meaning may also control punishment.
- Context such as known priorities, repeated use, and clear responsibility can strengthen—but does not prove—an attitude inference.
- A visible snapshot can dominate evaluation even when it is not representative of an actor's longer pattern.
- Alternative causes must remain visible before an omission is treated as evidence of contempt or disloyalty.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The current concept rests on two adjacent, substantially overlapping podcast episodes, not independent comparative evidence across organizations or relationships. The historical account does not prove Yi Zong's intent, the exact administrative chain for road repair, a plan to change allegiance, a conspiracy by enemies, or that the road incident caused his execution. The hosts' Starship, workplace, phone, praise, and partner-family examples illustrate the proposed pattern but do not establish its frequency, predictive accuracy, or universal validity.

## What Changed

- Clarified that the second episode is overlapping provenance and does not independently confirm the historical or workplace claims.
