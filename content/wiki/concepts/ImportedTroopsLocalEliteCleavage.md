# Imported Troops Local Elite Cleavage / 外来武装与本土豪族裂缝

Imported troops local elite cleavage / 外来武装与本土豪族裂缝 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-981-sanguo-zui-gang-moushi-chengyu-yi-ju-hua-rang-caocao-tanfu-lhlhlrmf2gvspwyvjkxg6kz44fth|Hanji 981]], where [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]'s [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] order is described as divided between Dongzhou troops and older local elite families. The Dongzhou side is made from Nanyang and Sanfu migrant military resources; the local side holds older regional status and interests.

The concept names an integration failure that appears after an outsider-backed or migrant-backed ruler gains a local base. Imported troops can secure the regime, but if they become a privileged extraction group, local elite cooperation weakens and capable local people may exit or rebel.

[[LiuZhang|刘璋]] supplies the source case after Liu Yan's death. He inherits the military-local split, defeats [[GanNing|甘宁]]'s rebellion through Dongzhou force, but loses Gan Ning eastward. In the episode's reading, suppressing a challenge is not the same as converting local talent into durable capacity.

## Key Claims
- A ruler can hold a province militarily while failing to integrate the province politically.
- Migrant or imported troops may solve initial security needs but deepen local resentment if resource distribution feels externalized.
- Local elite rebellion or talent exit can be a sign that the regime lacks a shared order, not only that individual actors are disloyal.
- The concept differs from [[LocalEliteProxySelection|地方豪强代理人选择]]: that concept asks which proxy local elites choose; this one asks what happens when a ruling bloc cannot fuse imported force and local standing.
