## Definition

Alchemy-chemistry continuity / 炼金术与化学连续性 is the historical claim that alchemy, chemistry, metallurgy, pharmacy, medicine, theology, and life-making were once entangled, while also recognizing that modern chemistry required methodological changes that make it more than renamed alchemy.

## Current Synthesis

The current sources support continuity in people, apparatus, practices, and questions. [[Frankenstein|《弗兰肯斯坦》]] inherits a world in which Albertus Magnus, Agrippa, [[Paracelsus]], electricity, anatomy, animation, and chemistry can still belong to one imagination of life and matter. [[IsaacNewton|Newton]] combines mechanics and optics with alchemy and theology, while the Chinese branch joins cinnabar, mercury, sulfur, furnaces, coded texts, immortality, and cosmology through [[WeiBoyang|魏伯阳]], [[GeHong|葛洪]], and earlier Han technical compilation.

Continuity is not identity. [[WesternAlchemy|Western alchemy]] and [[DaoistAlchemy|Daoist alchemy]] preserved or developed metallurgy, distillation, pharmacy, furnace design, repeated material manipulation, and observation, yet their transmutation, immortality, universal-cure, and artificial-life claims remained embedded in symbolic or sacred systems. [[RobertBoyle|Robert Boyle]] marks a stronger verification regime: quantities, conditions, results, public records, and reproducibility matter, and a failed premise must be rejectable. Modern chemistry therefore emerges through inheritance plus methodological differentiation, not simple triumph or total rupture.

## Key Claims

- Alchemy and chemistry did not begin as sharply separated intellectual or occupational domains.
- Alchemical traditions accumulated real apparatus, material procedures, pharmaceutical practice, and observations without thereby validating their cosmologies or promised outcomes.
- Early-modern scientists such as Newton could pursue mathematical physics, experiment, theology, and alchemy without experiencing them as later disciplinary categories.
- Chinese and Mediterranean-European traditions show comparable transformation ambitions but should not be collapsed into one institution or assumed transmission chain.
- Chemistry differentiates through quantitative measurement, recorded conditions, reproducibility, public scrutiny, and the ability to abandon failed premises.
- The strongest historical model is continuity plus rupture: inherited techniques and questions survive while standards of proof change.

## Evidence

- **Life-making inheritance:** [[141-zhizao-fulankensitan-kehuan-zhimu-he-tade-shenqi-shidai-857923931]] places Victor Frankenstein's alchemical reading inside a wider history of chemistry, electricity, anatomy, vitalism, and experimental creation.
- **Early-modern overlap:** [[26-niudun-yu-weibi-zhizaozhe-keneng-shi-sineipu-de-yuanxing-580065317]] presents Newton's alchemy as a search for hidden divine activity in matter while situating him in experimental and institutional science.
- **Chinese compilation and systematization:** [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-wei-wan-daixu-704051997]] connects Han technical compilation to astronomy, devices, and recipes; [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-jie-shangji-704051550]] adds coded mercury-sulfur practice, furnace alchemy, and Daoist immortality theory.
- **Technique and methodological differentiation:** [[46-guren-ruhe-changsheng-bulao-dongxifang-de-lianjinshu-liandanshu-yu-changshengyao-liporj2ydsu1pwehpgflapgm6zpx]] credits metallurgy, distillation, pharmacy, and repeated material work while using Boyle's measurement and documentation norms to explain chemistry's separation.
- **Internal criticism:** [[46-guren-ruhe-changsheng-bulao-dongxifang-de-lianjinshu-liandanshu-yu-changshengyao-liporj2ydsu1pwehpgflapgm6zpx]] presents [[IbnSinaAvicenna|Ibn Sina]] as distinguishing visible alteration from genuine transmutation, showing that criticism existed within the broader tradition before modern disciplinary chemistry.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Continuity must not become a progress myth in which every alchemist is treated as an early chemist or every symbolic recipe as disguised modern theory. Shared materials such as mercury, sulfur, lead, and cinnabar do not prove direct transmission among Chinese, Indian, Islamic, and European traditions. Podcast summaries cannot settle attribution, priority, textual dating, or exact experimental practice. The current evidence does not verify metal transmutation, immortality, universal medicine, homunculi, or revival after poisoning. Boyle is a methodological landmark in these sources, not a sole founder or a clean date at which alchemy ended.
