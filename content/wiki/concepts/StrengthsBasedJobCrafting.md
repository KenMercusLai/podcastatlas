# Strengths-Based Job Crafting

## Definition
Strengths-based job crafting is the practice of bringing valued personal capacities—such as humor, kindness, creativity, fairness, social intelligence, bravery, or love of learning—into the tasks and relationships of an existing role.

## Current Synthesis
[[LaurieSantos]] presents job crafting as a route to meaning that does not require every worker to find a perfectly matched calling. Ordinary roles can change experientially when people gain room to use characteristic strengths in service of coworkers, customers, patients, or the quality of the work itself.

The claim is conditional. Personal reframing and initiative cannot repair unsafe work, poverty wages, discrimination, impossible workload, surveillance, or absent autonomy. Crafting is most credible where the organization permits discretion and where strengths use improves rather than displaces the role's responsibilities.

## Key Claims
- Meaning at work can come partly from how a role is enacted, not only from occupation or status.
- Identifying characteristic strengths can reveal additional ways to contribute within ordinary tasks.
- Humor, care, creativity, learning, fairness, and social intelligence can alter relational and service quality.
- Crafting requires enough autonomy and must remain compatible with safety, competence, and role obligations.
- Individual initiative supplements rather than excuses fair pay, humane conditions, and organizational responsibility.

## Evidence
- Strength identification: [[how-to-achieve-true-happiness-using-science-based-protocols-dr-laurie-santos-scim5061134579]] discusses character strengths including bravery, humor, love of learning, fairness, and social intelligence.
- Ordinary-work application: [[how-to-achieve-true-happiness-using-science-based-protocols-dr-laurie-santos-scim5061134579]] uses hospital janitorial workers to illustrate how humor, creativity, or social intelligence can add purpose to routine work.
- Meaning frame: [[how-to-achieve-true-happiness-using-science-based-protocols-dr-laurie-santos-scim5061134579]] places strengths use within a wider account of sensory experience, personal story, values, and larger meaning.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The supplied summary does not provide study design, workplace comparison groups, durability, or effect sizes. Job crafting may be unavailable or penalized in tightly controlled, hazardous, precarious, discriminatory, or understaffed settings. It must not be used to romanticize exploitation or transfer responsibility for bad work design entirely to workers.

## What Changed
- Created a bounded concept for using personal strengths within an existing role while preserving organizational responsibility.
