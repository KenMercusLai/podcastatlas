# Halo Product Strategy

Halo product strategy is the use of rare, high-status products to lift the meaning, margins, and customer aspiration around the rest of a brand. In [[ferrari-1]], [[FerrariF40]], limited supercars, and [[FerrariIcona]] cars play this role for [[Ferrari]].

The strategy works because the halo product is not simply more expensive. It condenses heritage, scarcity, danger, design, and access into a product that makes the broader range feel part of a larger world.

## Key Claims
- Halo products can contribute disproportionate profit when buyers are highly price insensitive.
- The broader brand benefits when rare products validate taste and ambition without becoming common.
- The risk is dilution: if everything is called special, scarcity stops organizing customer desire.
