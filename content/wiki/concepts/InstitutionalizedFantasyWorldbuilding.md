# Institutionalized Fantasy Worldbuilding / 制度化幻想世界构建

## Definition

Institutionalized fantasy worldbuilding makes inherited or extraordinary motifs feel like a lived society by embedding them in recurring organizations, procedures, material routines, and imperfect historical systems.

## Current Synthesis

The bounded Harry Potter discussion distinguishes invention of magical ingredients from organization of them. Witches, broomsticks, potions, alchemy, magic wands, and chosen artifacts predate the series, but Hogwarts converts them into courses, textbooks, admissions, assessment, and school supplies. Diagon Alley, Gringotts, wizard currency, rail travel, and shopping then connect that education system to commerce, transport, family resources, and ordinary preparation.

Credibility comes partly from friction. A list of robes, cauldrons, bottles, books, and pets resembles a real child's beginning of school while remaining strange; irregular currency ratios look historically inherited rather than designed for reader convenience. The method does not require every economic calculation to survive scrutiny. Its effect is to make magic repeatedly usable, teachable, purchasable, and socially administered instead of leaving it as isolated spectacle.

## Key Claims

- Familiar mythic materials become a distinctive fictional society through recombination and institutional placement.
- Schools make powers legible by dividing them into subjects, materials, levels, teachers, practice, and rules.
- Admissions, shopping, banking, transport, and money connect spectacular magic to repeatable daily life.
- Concrete objects let readers enter an unfamiliar system through recognizable preparation routines.
- Irregular or inconvenient details can imply historical accumulation and increase perceived realism.
- Institutional coherence can create credibility even when individual economic or technical claims remain underexplained.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept rests on one conversational reading of one story and does not establish Rowling's documented design process. Institutional detail can suggest depth without resolving contradictions, and irregularity alone is not proof of coherent history. Exchange rates, coin materials, ancestry, and other setting explanations in the episode remain source-scoped unless independently grounded.
