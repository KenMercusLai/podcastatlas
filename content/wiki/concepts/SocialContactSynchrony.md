# Social Contact Synchrony

## Definition
Social contact synchrony is the source's hypothesis that reciprocal, mutually attended, real-time interaction may satisfy social need more effectively than passive, broadcast, anonymous, or asynchronous contact.

## Current Synthesis
Contact cannot be measured by message count alone. The episode places voice calls above text for real-time mutual coordination and contrasts direct attention with social-media broadcasts that invest little in any one viewer. Expectations, identity, relationship history, and context can change how the same gesture is received, so synchrony is one candidate dimension rather than a complete metric. Social media may activate comparison or observation without reliably producing the shared presence that reduces loneliness.

## Key Claims
- Quantity of contact and perceived social nourishment are not interchangeable.
- Real-time voice interaction provides more mutual timing information than asynchronous text.
- Direct communication can contain more reciprocal investment than a general broadcast.
- Passive feeds may intensify exclusion or comparison without satisfying the need to participate.
- Relationship history and expectation shape whether the same contact feels supportive, neutral, or threatening.

## Evidence
- Modality comparison - [[the-biology-of-social-interactions-and-emotions-dr-kay-tye-scim4105255786]] has Tye contrast voice-call synchrony with text messaging.
- Mutual investment - [[the-biology-of-social-interactions-and-emotions-dr-kay-tye-scim4105255786]] contrasts one-to-one attention with a vacation post broadcast to everyone.
- Context dependence - [[the-biology-of-social-interactions-and-emotions-dr-kay-tye-scim4105255786]] emphasizes identity, expectations, relationship history, and setting in social interpretation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Tye explicitly says she had not performed the relevant social-media study. Claims about loneliness relief, oxytocin, exclusion, and inter-brain synchrony are hypotheses or broad interpretations, not universal rankings of media or proof that text-based relationships are unreal.

## What Changed
- Added synchrony and mutual investment as qualified dimensions of socially nourishing contact.
