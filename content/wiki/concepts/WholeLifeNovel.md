## Definition

A whole-life novel follows a character beyond formation into later work, relationships, decline, retrospective reinterpretation, aging, and mortality, allowing early events to change meaning across an entire lifespan.

## Current Synthesis

[[LessonsNovel|《钢琴课》 / Lessons]] supplies a case in which no single event becomes the final key to [[RolandBaines|Roland Baines / 罗兰·贝恩斯]]. Abuse, abandonment, fatherhood, failed ambitions, family secrets, historical crises, confrontations, and grandchildren form a changing accumulation. The form differs from a coming-of-age arc that closes when personality appears established: later decades can revise what youth meant without erasing what happened.

## Key Claims

- Formation is one phase of a life rather than its narrative endpoint.
- Aging and retrospective reinterpretation can alter the meaning assigned to earlier events.
- Several partial causes can remain active without one becoming the complete explanation.
- Public history, intimate life, and ordinary duration can share one narrative scale.
- Decline, care, grandchildren, and mortality are structural material rather than epilogues.

## Evidence

### Life-course reach

- [[vol-358-gangqinke-wo-yijing-xiangkai-le-xiwang-ni-ye-xiangkai-yidian-gkwrijeotl1aaype0qtvn81u]] describes a novel extending from boarding school and adolescent abuse through marriage, single fatherhood, aging, and grandparenthood.

### Revised meaning

- [[vol-358-gangqinke-wo-yijing-xiangkai-le-xiwang-ni-ye-xiangkai-yidian-gkwrijeotl1aaype0qtvn81u]] shows Roland reclassifying a once-prized sexual experience under later legal and ethical knowledge.

### Multiple causation

- [[vol-358-gangqinke-wo-yijing-xiangkai-le-xiwang-ni-ye-xiangkai-yidian-gkwrijeotl1aaype0qtvn81u]] reads Roland's life as a collage of desire, family, accident, and history rather than one decisive turn.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The category is supplied by one episode's literary discussion and is not a settled genre taxonomy. Long chronology alone does not guarantee psychological depth, historical explanation, or truth about real lives, and a compressed novel can explore a life course without narrating every stage.

## What Changed

- Created the concept from *Lessons* as a life-course form continuing beyond maturation into decline and reinterpretation.
