# Research Funding Additionality

## Definition
Research funding additionality is the principle that public research money should fund work markets are unlikely to fund naturally, especially basic, early-stage, pre-competitive discovery science.

## Current Synthesis
The Kratsios and Weinstein interviews use additionality to redraw the boundary of federal research funding from opposite angles. Kratsios argues that government should fund early-stage, pre-competitive discovery where private capital is not already abundant. Weinstein argues more broadly that deep basic science is often inexhaustible, hard to exclude, and too specialized for markets or philanthropy to price reliably. The latest source extends the test beyond discovery into translation: public money can also be additional when it builds shared tools or coordinated engineering that academic publication and private product incentives both undersupply. Together the sources make funding defensible when it fills a real discovery or institutional gap rather than protecting a legacy channel.

## Key Claims
- Public research funding should emphasize basic and pre-competitive discovery rather than duplicating private AI or commercial product investment.
- Additionality depends on asking what other actors are not incentivized to do, not only which institution historically received money.
- Quantum computing as a scientific instrument and AI-for-science as researcher support illustrate missions distinct from ordinary commercial product competition.
- Additionality gives a reform-friendly rationale for funding focused research organizations, independent scientists, and universities when they are the right venue.
- Private philanthropy can help but cannot reliably cover all specialized domains because adjacent fields may be difficult even for brilliant funders to evaluate.
- Additionality becomes stronger when the knowledge base is a public good whose downstream applications cannot be predicted at the time of funding.
- Additionality can apply to translation infrastructure when shared tools are too engineering-heavy for papers and too nonexclusive or immature for firms.

## Evidence
- Boundary setting: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-michael-kratsios-trumps-science-agenda-anti-science-claims-faucis-damage-dei-china-42543770]] says government-funded research should refocus on basic, early-stage, pre-competitive discovery science.
- Market-gap test: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-michael-kratsios-trumps-science-agenda-anti-science-claims-faucis-damage-dei-china-42543770]] says the postwar model is less relevant when the R&D mix has shifted toward private funding.
- Mission examples: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-michael-kratsios-trumps-science-agenda-anti-science-claims-faucis-damage-dei-china-42543770]] distinguishes quantum as a research instrument and AI-for-science as researcher support rather than a private-model race.
- Basic-science public-good argument: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-eric-weinstein-the-state-of-american-science-breakthrough-coverups-and-the-danger-of-physics-42568645]] says basic science is inexhaustible and difficult to exclude, using homotopy theory as an example of important knowledge without an obvious industrial endpoint.
- Philanthropy limit: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-eric-weinstein-the-state-of-american-science-breakthrough-coverups-and-the-danger-of-physics-42568645]] says even brilliant private funders cannot cover the breadth of mathematics, physics, astrophysics, and cosmology because modern specializations are hard to evaluate from the outside.
- Translation and infrastructure gap: [[cash-test-can-europe-support-ukraine-alone-60e0fa97fc3b0dcfbac470b2da9e20ba]] presents [[FocusedResearchOrganizations]] and [[Cultivarium]] as ways to produce practical, public research resources outside ordinary paper and product incentives.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Additionality is easier to state than to administer. The sources do not supply a neutral test for when private markets are sufficient, when universities remain the best venue, when philanthropy can substitute for public funding, or when a claimed gap is actually a politically preferred priority. Temporary mission organizations also create maintenance and continuity questions after their initial funding window ends.

## What Changed
- Extended additionality from underfunded discovery to shared translation infrastructure and research tools.
- Added continuity and maintenance as tests for time-bounded institutional funding.
