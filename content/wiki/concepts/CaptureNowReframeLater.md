# Capture Now, Reframe Later / 先记录后取景

## Definition
Capture now, reframe later is an imaging workflow that records a broad or full surrounding scene first and moves viewpoint selection, composition, and camera movement into later software editing.

## Current Synthesis
[[nk78yhgkzrza-pj5ex42hwz7n352-nk78yhgkzrza-pj5ex42hwz7n352]] presents this workflow as the bridge that moved panoramic cameras beyond dependence on VR viewing. A creator can remain present in an event, preserve reactions outside a conventional frame, and later export a normal video with selected viewpoints. The tradeoff is displaced labor: reduced capture-time composition can create more editing, storage, image-processing, and software-dependence after capture.

## Key Claims
- Broad capture reduces the risk of missing an unanticipated subject or reaction outside the original frame.
- Deferred framing can lower the skill and attention required while an event is happening.
- Software reframing makes panoramic footage compatible with conventional flat-video sharing.
- The workflow shifts rather than eliminates creative work, moving some composition and camera movement into post-production.
- Its value depends on the entire hardware-software path, including stitching, stabilization, editing, export quality, and ease of use.

## Evidence
- Workflow transformation: [[nk78yhgkzrza-pj5ex42hwz7n352-nk78yhgkzrza-pj5ex42hwz7n352]] says 360-degree content initially lacked conventional shot control, while later editing and reframing enabled ordinary video outputs.
- User-value evidence: [[nk78yhgkzrza-pj5ex42hwz7n352-nk78yhgkzrza-pj5ex42hwz7n352]] emphasizes personal memories, surrounding reactions, sports, travel, and moments where active framing competes with participation.
- Product-system evidence: [[nk78yhgkzrza-pj5ex42hwz7n352-nk78yhgkzrza-pj5ex42hwz7n352]] links the camera's value to software editing rather than optical capture alone.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Capturing everything does not automatically produce a compelling story; creators still need selection and timing. Panoramic recording can increase editing burden, storage demand, processing complexity, stitching artifacts, and dependence on proprietary tools. The source is a founder account and does not independently compare output quality or total workflow time across device categories.

## What Changed
- Established deferred framing as a distinct creator workflow rather than only a camera specification.
- Made the capture-time freedom versus post-production burden explicit.
