# Youth Criminal Responsibility

Youth criminal responsibility is the legal and moral question in [[the-elon-game-musks-vision-of-the-future-6a633594d19896314260e5c4]] about when children should be treated as criminally responsible for serious offenses. [[MeghnaNanu]] uses [[Sweden]]'s new child prison units to compare countries lowering or debating the age threshold.

The episode frames the problem as double-sided. Children can commit serious harm, but they can also be exploited by gangs that recruit minors precisely because legal thresholds are lower; critics also argue that adolescents lack adult-level impulse control, reasoning, and judgment.

## Key Claims
- Sweden planned child prison units for children aged 15 or older convicted of serious crimes.
- The source says Sweden considered lowering criminal responsibility to 13 and then planned to return with a 14-year-old threshold.
- The episode says [[Argentina]] lowered its age from 16 to 14, while politicians in the [[Philippines]] and [[SouthKorea|South Korea]] were discussing similar changes.
- The source says the UN recommends 14 as the minimum age.
- Lowering the age may not deter gangs; in [[UnitedKingdom|Britain]], gangs still use children younger than the criminal-responsibility threshold.
- Early criminalization can worsen reoffending and lock children into a criminal path rather than breaking gang recruitment.
