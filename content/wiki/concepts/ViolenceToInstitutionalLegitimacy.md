# 暴力资本的体制化转换 / Violence-to-Institutional-Legitimacy Conversion

## Definition

暴力资本的体制化转换 / Violence-to-Institutional-Legitimacy Conversion describes the movement of personal fighting ability, coercive reputation, and follower organization from an irregular setting into an institution that supplies command, logistics, rank, and political authorization.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1086-sunquan-beihao-zhuang-rentou-de-muhe-weihe-pianpian-fangguo-sufei-lt7xfjunv42n9532eqog-8k64odf|Hanji 1086]] interprets [[GanNing|甘宁]]'s career as such a conversion. The capacities associated with the “锦帆贼” - force, courage, organization, intimidation, and loyalty networks - are unstable when privately exercised but become more durable after [[SunQuan|孙权]] gives them a military platform, supplies, official protection, and a recognized purpose.

Institutional entry does not erase the old violence. Gan Ning's killing of [[LingCaoLateHan|凌操]] follows him into the new organization through [[LingTong|凌统]]'s revenge claim, so Sun Quan must actively govern the social liabilities attached to the absorbed capacity.

## Key Claims

- Irregular violence and official command can draw on overlapping skills while differing in authorization, supply, and durability.
- Institutional sponsorship can make personal coercive capacity scalable by adding troops, logistics, rank, and protection.
- Absorption does not automatically remove grievances or habits created in the pre-institutional period.
- The receiving ruler's contribution is both opportunity creation and containment of the imported risk.

## Evidence

- Capacity continuity: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1086-sunquan-beihao-zhuang-rentou-de-muhe-weihe-pianpian-fangguo-sufei-lt7xfjunv42n9532eqog-8k64odf|Hanji 1086]] links Gan Ning's earlier force, courage, organization, and loyalty code to his later military usefulness.
- Institutional gains: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1086-sunquan-beihao-zhuang-rentou-de-muhe-weihe-pianpian-fangguo-sufei-lt7xfjunv42n9532eqog-8k64odf|Hanji 1086]] names army, supplies, rank, legitimacy, and political protection as advantages of office.
- Residual liability: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1086-sunquan-beihao-zhuang-rentou-de-muhe-weihe-pianpian-fangguo-sufei-lt7xfjunv42n9532eqog-8k64odf|Hanji 1086]] shows Ling Cao's death continuing as an internal feud after Gan Ning changes patrons.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

This is the host's modern analytical vocabulary, not a demonstrated self-description by Gan Ning. The source does not prove that he pursued office only as calculated “capital conversion,” and it should not turn all irregular violence into latent merit or erase victims, coercion, and political contingency.
