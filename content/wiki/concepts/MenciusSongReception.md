# Mencius Song Reception / 宋代孟子接受史

Mencius Song reception / 宋代孟子接受史 is [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-26-mengzi-laile-ln4twwp2frmr-xpumu0rqg-2gpyn]]'s frame for why [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s treatment of [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]] cannot be read only as neutral classical commentary. The episode says Sima Guang reconciles the renyi-benefit passage but personally disliked Mencius and wrote [[YiMeng|《疑孟》]].

The concept also brings in [[WangAnshi|王安石]], who is described as loving Mencius and helping raise Mencius's status. That makes Mencius reception part of the same Song political-intellectual world as [[WangAnshiReforms|王安石变法]] and [[SongFactionalPolitics|宋代党争]]: debates about renyi, benefit, reform, and textual authority could echo current policy conflicts.

## Key Claims
- A commentator can preserve and reconcile a passage while remaining skeptical of its speaker.
- Mencius's status was historically contested in Song discourse rather than simply fixed by later canon.
- Wang Anshi's elevation of Mencius makes classical reception politically resonant.
- The ancient renyi-benefit debate became relevant to Song arguments about reform, public benefit, and state pressure.
