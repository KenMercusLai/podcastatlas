## Definition

赴死式直谏 / death-defying remonstrance is a high-risk feedback pattern in which an adviser makes willingness to die part of a direct political argument, attempting to overcome intimidation, silence, or captured access to the ruler.

## Current Synthesis

The pattern works only when bodily risk is joined to a political reason for correction. [[MaoJiao|茅焦]] turns threatened execution into a warning that violence against kin and remonstrants will dissolve public support for Qin; [[LuanBu|栾布]] turns his own imminent punishment into a warning that killing [[PengYue|彭越]] will frighten other merit-holders. Both persuade the ruler by moving the issue from private anger to state consequence.

Late-Han cases show a wider outcome range. [[LiXianTaiweiLateHan|李贤]] helps secure [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦皇太后]]'s burial recognition; [[SimaZhiLateHan|司马直]] dies but produces a temporary pause in a palace-construction fee; [[ShenZhongLateHan|沈忠]] stakes body and family on a memorial that is shelved; and [[LiuTaoLateHan|刘陶 / 刘桃]] preserves moral testimony without repairing a court whose information channels are already captured.

Hanji 331-3 adds that Li Xian's prepared poison was said to be crushed pepper. That detail intensifies the material image of his resolve but does not change the concept's judgment: method of threatened death is secondary to whether the intervention restores usable feedback, and the poison identification itself remains source-scoped.

## Key Claims

- Willingness to die can make intimidation ineffective and break a silence that ordinary advice cannot penetrate.
- Personal sacrifice persuades only when translated into a consequence the ruler has reason to prevent.
- Outcomes range from pardon or policy correction to temporary relief, shelving, imprisonment, or death without repair.
- Successful intervention can still reveal institutional failure because feedback depends on extraordinary personal risk.
- Death-facing resolve strengthens moral witness but does not guarantee factual accuracy or political effectiveness.
- The threatened method of death may shape a narrative's symbolism without determining whether the remonstrance succeeds.

## Evidence

- Public-consequence persuasion: [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-108-1-lishi-gan-dangmian-dingzhuang-qinshihuang-diyi-ren-lg0dssyicp387foiraohkv1x5aij|Qinji 108-1]] has Mao Jiao connect Qin Shi Huang's violence to loss of public orientation, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-184-lishi-kongbupian-liubang-de-pengshi-roujiang-2-liebzrdrvo1okonpdjvrjatuwx9i|Hanji 184]] has Luan Bu connect Peng Yue's punishment to merit-holder fear.
- Successful burial intervention: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-901-jixingzi-de-ren-hui-shi-shenme-xiachang-lh1yfyjzqptxvtnv9taiqms9yjud|Hanji 901]] presents Li Xian carrying poison and securing a favorable decision, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-331-3-guren-jingran-yong-huajiao-zisha-lohtg2tifzlc63cxkcjkekoyk9w3|Hanji 331-3]] identifies the prepared substance as crushed pepper.
- Narrow policy breakthrough: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-932-weishenme-ren-yue-hao-yue-nan-dang-daguan-lvwzel782wcxs1lpt3rg2vmkdy29|Hanji 932]] presents Sima Zhi's death memorial as producing a temporary halt to the palace-construction fee without dismantling the wider extraction system.
- Failed or blocked feedback: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-916-ming-hao-he-yun-hao-nage-geng-zhongyao-lmzudjq5zfkfsukmhdeiumesm9qu|Hanji 916]] shows Shen Zhong's death-pledged accusation being shelved, and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-935-ren-huo-yi-kou-qi-nan-cheng-de-shi-ziji-lmy8tyrttv-gjvke7sdi6dx-oopw|Hanji 935]] shows Liu Tao's warning ending in prison death without institutional repair.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

These sources are podcast syntheses of historical narratives, not a comparative primary-source study. Reported speeches, motives, punishments, causal effects, and ruler reactions remain source-scoped. The pattern should not romanticize suicide, self-harm, or coercive threats: extreme risk may signal captured institutions, may fail to improve the decision, and does not make the speaker correct. Hanji 331-3's pepper detail is compatible with Hanji 901's generic poison but is not independent confirmation of the substance or its lethality.

## What Changed

- Added crushed pepper as the source-attributed material form of Li Xian's threatened death.
- Clarified that method symbolism is secondary to feedback outcome and political argument.
- Reorganized six earlier cases into successful, narrow, shelved, and failed feedback outcomes.
- Migrated the concept to the synthesis-first schema while preserving the complete source order.
