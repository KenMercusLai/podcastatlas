# Bureaucratic Absurdity Escalation

Bureaucratic absurdity escalation is the pattern where an institution responds to a simple blocked problem by adding procedures, departments, representatives, devices, and reporting layers until the response becomes larger than the original problem. [[23-feima-pai-qiche-anquan-shi-di-yi-wei-de-572699943]] adds the concept through [[FeimaPaiQiche|《飞马牌汽车》]], where a bus door that should be opened instead generates meetings, a traffic-jam bureau, door guards, remote services, media events, research products, fees, and promotions.

The escalation remains locally coherent. Each step can be explained as risk control, coordination, public communication, hygiene, logistics, or fairness. The absurdity lies in the whole sequence: the system grows more capable of managing stuckness than of ending it.

## Key Claims
- Bureaucratic escalation can look energetic while avoiding the central decision.
- Each new layer creates its own interests, metrics, and defensible language.
- A temporary emergency can become a durable institution once people, budgets, vendors, and reputations attach to it.
- The pattern links literary satire to real organizational analysis because both depend on incentives and accountability paths.
