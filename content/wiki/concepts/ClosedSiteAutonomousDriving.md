# Closed-Site Autonomous Driving

Closed-site autonomous driving is the source's scene-specific autonomy pattern for bounded industrial environments such as mines and ports. [[chengshi-jiushi-zheyang-21-shiyan-yizuo-yin-qiche-erqi-you-bugan-bei-qiche-paoxia-de-chengshi-1001770066]] adds it through [[Shiyan]] companies extending commercial-vehicle and special-vehicle capability into smart mining trucks.

The source distinguishes this from open urban robotaxi deployment. Mine roads and port environments can still be difficult, but routes, access control, speed ranges, operating goals, and vehicle types are more constrained than ordinary city streets, making them a plausible place for [[CommercialVehicleIndustrialBase|commercial-vehicle]] firms to upgrade.

For Shiyan, closed-site autonomy matters because it offers a technology path that fits legacy truck and special-vehicle know-how. It does not require the city to win the whole consumer passenger-EV race; it can compete around bounded work scenes where vehicle durability, payload, and industrial operations are central.
