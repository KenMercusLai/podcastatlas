## Definition

Idealized love deconstruction is a literary reading that refuses to define love through one purified signal such as exclusivity, sacrifice, beauty, passion, or marriage, and instead tests relationships across desire, mutual knowledge, agency, care, responsibility, boundaries, power, and shared life.

## Current Synthesis

The bounded [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] episode applies this reading to [[LuDingJi|《鹿鼎记》]]. [[WeiXiaobao|韦小宝]] does not reproduce the single, sacred, life-and-death romance associated with several earlier [[JinYong|金庸]] couples. His seven marriages distribute relational goods and failures unevenly: [[MuJianping|沐剑屏]] offers youthful affection, [[FangYi|方怡]] exposes coercion and damaged trust, [[Shuanger|双儿]] supplies durable companionship with limits, [[ZengRou|曾柔]] begins in hero worship, [[PrincessJianning|建宁公主]] brings bodily and temperamental compatibility, [[SuQuan|苏荃]] brings strategic partnership, and [[AkeLuDingJi|阿珂]] concentrates beauty, projection, and possession.

The result is not the claim that love disappears. It is that no single criterion settles the question. Wei's objectifying language and acquisitive pride matter, but so do understanding, protection, non-abandonment, respect for some boundaries, and the labor of maintaining a household. The comparison with [[DuanZhengchun|段正淳]] makes the split explicit: willingness to die and romantic eloquence may coexist with long absence, while continuous care may exist without idealized exclusivity or sacrificial language.

## Key Claims

- Passion, beauty, sacrifice, exclusivity, marriage, and duration are evidence about love, but none is sufficient by itself.
- Objectification and practical care can coexist in one relationship and must both remain visible in judgment.
- Agency and respected boundaries distinguish durable companionship from simple obedience or possession.
- Coercion, survival pressure, pregnancy, status, and household structure are constitutive conditions rather than external noise around romance.
- Compatibility and shared labor can produce relational value without matching a sacred or singular love ideal.
- “Anti-love” literature can challenge purification of love without claiming that affection, loyalty, or responsibility are unreal.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept derives from one multi-host reading of a structured episode summary, not a universal theory of love or a direct collation of Jin Yong editions. The seven marriages occur within gendered, coercive, and unequal conditions that responsibility alone cannot repair. Nonexclusivity should neither be rejected automatically nor treated as consensual merely because it persists. Claims about authorial intention, future happiness, and each character's inner state remain interpretive.

## What Changed

- Established a multi-criterion framework for reading love without reducing it to passion, sacrifice, exclusivity, or marital outcome.
- Added the coexistence of objectification and practical care as a central unresolved tension.
- Added agency, boundaries, coercion, and household labor to the assessment of romantic claims.
