# Porcelain Stone-Clay Binary Formula / 瓷石瓷土二元配方

Porcelain Stone-Clay Binary Formula / 瓷石瓷土二元配方 is the material recipe in [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]]. The episode explains that early Jingdezhen production relied heavily on porcelain stone, but adding porcelain clay such as [[Kaolin]] reduced deformation at high firing temperatures.

The formula matters because it turned local material knowledge into product range. More stable bodies made larger and more technically demanding wares possible, helping [[Jingdezhen]] move from early small wares toward [[YuanBlueAndWhitePorcelain|large blue-and-white vessels]] and later imperial production.
