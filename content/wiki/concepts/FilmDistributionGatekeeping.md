## Definition

Film distribution gatekeeping is the concentrated control exercised by distributors, streaming platforms, acquisition executives, and internal approvers over which completed films receive financing for release and access to broad audiences.

## Current Synthesis

The episode makes the streaming menu the endpoint of a narrow decision chain. A film can clear production and festival selection yet still depend on a small number of commercially relevant buyers, each constrained by budgets, competing acquisitions, release strategy, and corporate approval. Sales agents can improve access and evidence, but they do not remove the gate; they help filmmakers navigate it.

## Key Claims

- Production completion and festival selection do not guarantee public availability.
- A small viable buyer pool concentrates attention and bargaining power.
- Internal corporate approval can remain decisive after an acquisition executive shows interest.
- Competing films consume limited budgets and organizational attention.
- Streaming competition can widen seller leverage, while mergers and spending cuts can narrow it.
- Gatekeeping shapes cultural visibility without proving that selected films are artistically superior.

- Budget competition: [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-20c3269bc63dde599e9e1898d7da833b-33908266-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-20c3269bc63dde599e9e1898d7da833b-33908266|Anatomy of a movie market]] says bidding on one film can absorb buyers' budgets and attention.
- Structural change: [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-20c3269bc63dde599e9e1898d7da833b-33908266-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-20c3269bc63dde599e9e1898d7da833b-33908266|Anatomy of a movie market]] connects the softer market to post-COVID theatrical weakness, mergers, and reduced spending.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The approximate buyer count applies to the documentary segment and commercial level described by Cinetic, not to all films or distribution routes. Direct digital release, niche platforms, self-distribution, community exhibition, and later catalogue acquisition can provide alternatives, though the source does not evaluate their scale or economics.

## What Changed

- Established a distribution-specific model spanning buyer concentration, budget competition, and internal approval.
- Qualified the model by distinguishing mass commercial acquisition from alternative release paths.
