# Consumer Product Value Legibility / 消费产品价值可理解性

## Definition
Consumer product value legibility is the degree to which a buyer can understand, experience, and remember why a product is better without needing insider engineering knowledge or unusually effortful configuration.

## Current Synthesis
The Laifen interview supplies both a communication and a usage test. Its first hair dryer reportedly remained commercially weak until explanatory content translated motor speed, airflow, noise, price, and experience into a credible story. Its toothbrush then shows the opposite side: advanced sweep-and-vibration control and app customization may be technically real, yet fewer than one in ten users reportedly enter the app and most live with the default. Value becomes legible when claims are explainable before purchase and benefits remain perceptible in ordinary default use afterward.

## Key Claims
- Unexplained technical advantage can be commercially equivalent to no advantage when customers cannot evaluate it.
- Credible demonstration and detailed explanation can reduce uncertainty for a new hardware brand.
- Optional sophistication has limited reach when most users never configure it.
- Default experience deserves priority because it is where the majority of value realization may occur.
- Legibility does not require simplifying away evidence; it requires translating specifications into user-relevant outcomes and boundaries.

## Evidence
- Pre-purchase explanation: [[ll4ekwtvjnpxit2gdkvcfphf0eli-ll4ekwtvjnpxit2gdkvcfphf0eli]] contrasts minimal early hair-dryer sales with growth after technical videos explained the product.
- Launch content: [[ll4ekwtvjnpxit2gdkvcfphf0eli-ll4ekwtvjnpxit2gdkvcfphf0eli]] describes a low-budget launch recording whose direct, complete technical explanation reportedly reached a large audience.
- Default-use evidence: [[ll4ekwtvjnpxit2gdkvcfphf0eli-ll4ekwtvjnpxit2gdkvcfphf0eli]] reports that under ten percent of toothbrush users opened the app and most used default settings.
- Premium boundary: [[ll4ekwtvjnpxit2gdkvcfphf0eli-ll4ekwtvjnpxit2gdkvcfphf0eli]] notes that higher internal component standards and cost do not automatically produce matching willingness to pay.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not isolate content effects from price, distribution, timing, founder publicity, or broader social proof. App-open rates do not prove users failed to value the default product, and low configuration can indicate either good defaults or irrelevant complexity. Legible claims can also become misleading if demonstrations omit reliability, safety, comparison conditions, or long-term performance.

## What Changed
- Created the concept by combining the hair-dryer explanation case with the toothbrush default-use case.
