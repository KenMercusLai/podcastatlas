# Tech-Government Accommodation

Tech-government accommodation is the pattern where technology leaders adapt their public posture, policy asks, or relationship management to maintain access to a powerful administration. [[tech-20251223-1223-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251223-1223-mp-tech-pod-128]] adds the concept through [[SuyashPasi]]'s account of major technology leaders aligning with the [[DonaldTrump]] [[WhiteHouse]] during 2025.

The source does not treat all alignment as ideological conversion. Some actors may be part of a committed [[TechnologyRightPowerNetwork|technology right]], while others may be seeking deregulation, visa access, crypto-friendly policy, defense business, or reversals of specific cuts. That makes accommodation a practical access strategy as well as a political signal.

## Key Claims
- Technology leaders can become more publicly agreeable to the federal government when regulation, procurement, immigration, and infrastructure approvals depend on political access.
- AI companies may gain from accommodation through [[FederalAIPreemption]] and infrastructure support.
- Crypto's renewed prominence gives some technology and finance actors a second policy surface where deregulation and legitimacy matter.
- Accommodation can coexist with factional conflict: tech leaders may align with the administration on AI or defense while clashing with protectionist [[MAGA]] actors on H-1B visas.
- The 2026 midterms are framed by the source as a test of whether less ideological technology leaders continue tacit support for the administration.
