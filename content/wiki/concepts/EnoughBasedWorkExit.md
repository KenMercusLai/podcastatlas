# Enough-Based Work Exit

## Definition
Enough-based work exit is a household strategy that makes departure from paid employment possible by defining a sufficient material standard and treating additional consumption as a tradeoff against control of time.

## Current Synthesis
[[DrewHaven]] and [[LindsayHaven]] do not describe themselves as free from financial concern. Their arrangement combines existing resources with lower spending, limited transportation, deliberate housing and vehicle choices, and recurring comparison between a purchase and continued job freedom. The concept therefore differs from a claim of unlimited financial independence: it is a conditional practice of keeping needs and wants within the resources that support [[EarlyRetirementWorkAutonomy]].

## Key Claims
- A household can move the work-exit threshold by revising its definition of enough rather than only accumulating toward a fixed conventional retirement target.
- Lower consumption can buy time autonomy, but it also produces practical constraints and coordination costs.
- Purchase decisions become decisions about future labor when higher recurring costs could require a return to paid employment.
- The strategy depends on resources already available and should not be mistaken for a universal option created by frugality alone.
- Sustainability requires continuing household agreement about spending, care, transport, health, and what forms of inconvenience are acceptable.

## Evidence
- Sufficiency threshold - [[tech-20261109-1109-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261109-1109-mp-tech-pod-128]] reports Drew Haven's conclusion that the household could make existing resources enough rather than wait for a predetermined retirement figure.
- Consumption-time tradeoff - [[tech-20261109-1109-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261109-1109-mp-tech-pod-128]] describes his recurring comparison between purchases and the value of remaining job-free.
- Practical constraints - [[tech-20261109-1109-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261109-1109-mp-tech-pod-128]] identifies simple living and one car shared by three adults as deliberate but sometimes conflict-producing choices.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source supplies no balance sheet, income stream, housing cost, health-insurance arrangement, or long-run cash-flow model. Defining enough cannot substitute for minimum resources, and the couple's example does not show that workers with low income, debt, dependents, unstable housing, or major health costs can reproduce the strategy. Lower consumption also shifts logistical and emotional costs into household coordination.

## What Changed
- Created a sufficiency-based mechanism for work exit that distinguishes time autonomy from unlimited financial independence.
