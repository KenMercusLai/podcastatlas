# Partition of Jin / 三家分晋

Partition of Jin / 三家分晋 is the wiki's frame for the staggered transfer of power from [[JinState|晋国]] to Han, Zhao, and Wei. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-04-yurang-wei-zhiyao-fuchou-ln34tydha6iqwlrgmw7iugqfgfik]] emphasizes the 453 BCE threshold: after the Battle of Jinyang, [[HanState|韩国]], [[ZhaoState|赵国]], and [[WeiState|魏国]] divide [[ZhiYao|智瑶]]'s territory, and the source treats this as the practical beginning of the [[WarringStatesPeriod|战国时期]].

The existing Zhouji branch adds later thresholds. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-04-yurang-wei-zhiyao-fuchou-ln34tydha6iqwlrgmw7iugqfgfik]] also records [[ZhouWeilieWang|周威烈王]] formally recognizing [[WeiWenhou|魏斯 / 魏文侯]], [[ZhaoLiehou|赵籍 / 赵烈侯]], and [[HanJinghou|韩虔 / 韩景侯]] as vassals, which is the de jure threshold captured by [[ThreeJinVassalRecognition|三晋受封]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl]] records the later 376 BCE endpoint when the three houses depose [[JinJinggong|晋静公]] and divide Jin's remaining land.

The concept therefore avoids forcing one start date. The partition has at least three layers: military balance after Zhi's destruction, title recognition by the Zhou royal house, and final disposal of residual Jin territory.

## Key Claims
- "The beginning of the Warring States" can refer to different political thresholds rather than one universally settled event.
- 453 BCE marks de facto power rearrangement after Zhi's fall in this source.
- 403 BCE marks formal Zhou recognition of the three Jin houses in the Zizhi Tongjian opening frame.
- 376 BCE marks the final extinction of residual Jin authority in the later Zhouji 09 source.
