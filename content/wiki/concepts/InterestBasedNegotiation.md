# Interest-Based Negotiation / 利益导向谈判

## Definition
Interest-based negotiation is the practice of discovering the need, fear, value, identity, or operational constraint beneath a stated demand before trying to resolve the visible position.

## Current Synthesis
Listening and asking why can turn rigid positions into tradable issues. The delivery-penalty example in [[106-bie-zai-haipa-chongtu-kuai-lai-xuexi-tanpan-shu-742539645]] shows how downstream delay risk and emergency-transport cost become visible only after both sides explain their interests. The same discipline enters identity-sensitive persuasion in [[132-zhenglun-yu-shuofu-women-weishenme-chaojia-zenme-cai-suan-ying-807583318]], where rapport, permission, confidence, and reflection matter more than accusation or fact dumping.

Hypothesis testing through [[TacticalEmpathy]] and [[CalibratedNegotiationQuestions]] broadens "ask why" into a less interrogative method. In [[how-to-succeed-at-hard-conversations-chris-voss-scim5142864264]], the negotiator offers a best guess, lets correction reveal the real concern, and uses response quality to distinguish thought from reflex.

## Key Claims
- Positions often remain rigid because the underlying interests have not been stated.
- Listening and provisional interpretation can reveal interests that direct argument conceals.
- Asking why, how, or what can turn a single-issue dispute into a solvable multi-issue problem.
- Information exchange reduces speculative readings of intent but does not eliminate strategic deception.
- Identity-sensitive persuasion requires ethical purpose, permission, and rapport as well as factual accuracy.

## Evidence
- Commercial interests - [[106-bie-zai-haipa-chongtu-kuai-lai-xuexi-tanpan-shu-742539645]] uses delivery penalties and transport cost to show how hidden constraints create new bargaining issues.
- Identity and belief - [[132-zhenglun-yu-shuofu-women-weishenme-chaojia-zenme-cai-suan-ying-807583318]] uses deep canvassing to connect questions and reflection to fear, confidence, identity, and value.
- Hypothesis testing - [[how-to-succeed-at-hard-conversations-chris-voss-scim5142864264]] uses labels, mirrors, and corrigible guesses to help the other person disclose what the negotiator has missed.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Not every disclosed interest is honest, compatible, or safe to accommodate. Questions can feel invasive when rapport or consent is absent, and abusive or coercive settings may require boundaries, exit, or outside support rather than deeper exploration. The three sources describe practical methods rather than a guarantee of agreement.
