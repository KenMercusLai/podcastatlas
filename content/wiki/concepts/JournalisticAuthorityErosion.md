# Journalistic Authority Erosion / 记者权威感消失

Journalistic authority erosion / 记者权威感消失 is [[ep244-jizhe-yanzhong-de-hao-gongzuo-shenme-yang-lk8iz9cllzx2jou5-sw9yt2ogpyb]]'s media-trust diagnosis. A middle-aged media worker recalls an era when reporters felt like a "fourth power"; the younger journalists describe a later world where officials, subjects, and online audiences often treat reporters as intrusive, self-interested, or traffic-seeking.

The concept is not only about public misunderstanding. The episode also points to media-industry irregularity, high-pressure management, performance systems, and unprofessional practices as internal reasons why external trust damage spreads to everyone in the industry.

## Key Claims
- Reporter authority can erode from outside through obstruction, public suspicion, and platform-era distrust.
- It can also erode from inside when media institutions rely on pressure, weak labor norms, or traffic-oriented practices.
- Authority loss changes the reporter's self-image: the job becomes less a moral mission and more a negotiated occupation.
- Trust damage is collective; one outlet or practice can lower the standing of other reporters in the same field.
