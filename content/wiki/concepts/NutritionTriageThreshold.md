# Nutrition Triage Threshold / 营养不良分诊门槛

## Definition
Nutrition triage threshold / 营养不良分诊门槛 is the use of a measurable clinical cutoff to decide who receives scarce therapeutic or fortified nutrition support in a humanitarian setting.

## Current Synthesis
In the South Sudan source, WFP staff use a colored upper-arm measurement band to screen children. Fortified nutrition food is targeted to children under five and pregnant or postpartum women, with severe child malnutrition tied to an upper-arm circumference below 11.5 centimeters.

The concept's key tension is that objective thresholds make scarce aid more accountable, but they can also create painful edge cases. Wang Hongrui observes children who are visibly hungry yet do not receive nutrition supplies because the program is reserved for those who meet acute criteria.

## Key Claims
- Measurement thresholds make triage legible and repeatable under scarcity.
- Severe-malnutrition cutoffs can leave visibly hungry children outside specialized support.
- Target groups reflect biological risk, especially young children and pregnant or postpartum women.
- When food is scarce enough, children may perceive being more malnourished as the path to receiving food.

## Evidence
- Screening method: [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] describes staff measuring child upper-arm circumference with a colored band.
- Eligibility threshold: [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] says children below the severe-malnutrition cutoff can receive nutrition food.
- Edge-case hunger: [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] describes a child saying he is hungry while not receiving nutrition supplies.
- Scarcity psychology: [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] records Wang's observation that children may envy those thin enough to qualify.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode does not evaluate the medical validity of the threshold or compare it with other nutrition protocols. Its contribution is ethical and operational: how threshold-based rationing feels at the field edge.
