# Colocation Data Center

Colocation data center is the infrastructure model highlighted in [[tech-20260127-0127-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260127-0127-mp-tech-pod-128]] through [[Equinix]]'s Palo Alto facility. Instead of every network or company building a fully separate facility, customers lease space where servers, cloud providers, internet service providers, and enterprise networks can sit near one another and exchange data.

The episode treats colocation as a physical business model behind seemingly virtual services. The same building moved from [[PacificTelephoneAndTelegraph]] telephone switchboards to [[AltaVista]] and [[PaloAltoInternetExchange|PAIX]], then to cloud and AI traffic, showing that [[StrategicAIInfrastructureDependence]] includes real estate, cages, cables, and interconnection as well as chips and power.

## Key Claims
- Colocation makes network proximity a leased service rather than a private facility problem for every participant.
- The model becomes more valuable when many unrelated networks need a neutral place to exchange traffic.
- AI infrastructure discussions that focus only on GPUs, power, or capital can miss the interconnection layer that lets compute capacity become usable service capacity.
