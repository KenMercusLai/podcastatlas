# RULER Framework

## Definition
RULER Framework is [[MarcBrackett]]'s source-scoped emotional-intelligence model for recognizing, understanding, labeling, expressing, and regulating emotions.

## Current Synthesis
Across the two Brackett sources, RULER gives emotional intelligence an ordered skill vocabulary. Recognition makes emotion visible, understanding interprets causes and meaning, labeling makes the state precise, expression asks whether and how to communicate it, and regulation chooses a context-sensitive strategy.

The framework matters because the episodes reject both suppression and uncontrolled expression. It treats emotion skills as teachable in families, classrooms, and organizations only when people share language and examine whether strategies improve relationships, performance, decisions, and well-being. The earlier interview adds [[MoodMeterEmotionalCheckIn]], behavior-reading limits, and the claim that different emotional states can support different tasks rather than every unpleasant state requiring repair.

## Key Claims
- Emotional intelligence can be broken into recognizable component skills.
- Labeling is central because vague labels make needs and strategies harder to identify.
- Expression is a skill separate from feeling; not every accepted emotion should be expressed in every context.
- Regulation depends on goals, person, emotion, and situation rather than a universal tactic.
- Shared emotional language lets schools, families, and teams practice regulation systemically.
- Behavior should trigger inquiry rather than confident emotional inference, because the same outward action can arise from different underlying states.

## Evidence
- Skill sequence - [[how-to-better-regulate-your-emotions-dr-marc-brackett-scim6338474733]] has Brackett summarize emotional intelligence as recognizing, understanding, labeling, expressing, and regulating emotions.
- Labeling evidence - [[how-to-better-regulate-your-emotions-dr-marc-brackett-scim6338474733]] distinguishes anxiety, stress, pressure, and fear to show why precise labels matter.
- Expression boundary - [[how-to-better-regulate-your-emotions-dr-marc-brackett-scim6338474733]] separates accepting all emotions from expressing all emotions in every context.
- Systemic school use - [[how-to-better-regulate-your-emotions-dr-marc-brackett-scim6338474733]] says emotional-intelligence work needs leaders, teachers, students, and parents using the same language.
- Cause and behavior boundary - [[how-to-increase-your-emotional-intelligence-dr-marc-brackett-scim6059113217]] uses disappointment-versus-anger and a child's stomping to show why understanding must precede advice or discipline.
- Check-in and task fit - [[how-to-increase-your-emotional-intelligence-dr-marc-brackett-scim6059113217]] maps pleasantness and energy while arguing that different states can support brainstorming, consensus, or detailed work.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The sources present RULER through public podcast conversations rather than a full program manual or research review. Claims that the skills are measurable, predictive, or effective at school remain Brackett's source claims unless supported by later evidence.
