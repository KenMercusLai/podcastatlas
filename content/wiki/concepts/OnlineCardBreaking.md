# Online Card Breaking

Online card breaking is the live-commerce format where a host opens sealed boxes or packs of trading cards on stream after buyers have purchased shares assigned by team, player, or other rules. [[buai-zhibo-daihuo-de-oumei-xiaofeizhe-weihe-zai-zhejia-pingtai-shang-daba-huaqian-1007104138]] adds the concept through [[Whatnot]]'s sports-card marketplace activity.

The source frames card breaking as both a discovery ritual and a risk point. Buyers are purchasing into an uncertain reveal, and that uncertainty can feel entertaining and social; it can also resemble gambling when the expected value is unclear, valuable cards are rare, and the stream makes repeated participation easy.

## Key Claims
- Online card breaking turns sealed-card uncertainty into a shared live event.
- The format depends on clear allocation rules, seller trust, and buyer understanding of what share they are purchasing.
- It strengthens [[IntermittentReinforcement]] because rare pulls can justify repeated participation even when many outcomes are low value.
- Legal and ethical concerns remain source-scoped: the episode cites lawsuits alleging gambling-like structure, but the wiki should not treat that as a settled ruling across all card breaks.
