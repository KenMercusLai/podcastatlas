## Definition

Direct remonstrance criminalization / 直谏入罪 is the court-politics pattern in which a ruler treats open corrective speech as personal disrespect or political offense, then extends punishment from the original remonstrant to solidarity speakers and clemency advocates.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 850 defines the pattern through [[LiYunLateHan|李云]] and [[DuZhongLateHan|杜仲]] under [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]]. Li Yun's memorial is not anonymous or indirect: it is unsealed, copied to the Three Excellencies, and moves from overreward criticism into a challenge to the emperor's willingness to hear truth. Huan answers by arresting him, interrogating him, and turning the warning into a capital case.

The pattern widens because later speech is also criminalized. Du Zhong's shared-fate memorial, [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]]'s precedent-based clemency plea, other officials' petitions, and [[GuanBaLateHan|管霸]]'s final mitigation attempt all fail to reopen the advice channel. Huan's punishment of intercessors and execution of Li Yun and Du Zhong show feedback collapse becoming exemplary terror: the court learns that loyal criticism and rescue can both become offenses.

An earlier Emperor An case in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-800-yangzhen-zhisi-yi-chang-jianchen-dui-qingguan-de-jiaosha-lk9qcfs4xl-rjd-zpwdtsblybxa8|Hanji 800]] shows the lethal edge before the Liu Bao deposition crisis. [[ZhaoTengLateHan|赵腾]] submits a blunt memorial criticizing court gains and losses; An converts it into deception and grave disloyalty, imprisons him, rejects [[YangZhenLateHan|杨震]]'s mitigation plea, and has Zhao Teng executed. Yang Zhen's own disaster-remonstrance and evidence-gathering against [[FanFengEunuchLateHan|樊丰]], [[ZhouGuangLateHan|周广]], and [[XieYunLateHan|谢恽]] then become punishable as resentment after Fan Feng's side activates [[GengBaoGrandGeneralLateHan|耿宝]] as formal accuser. Yang is stripped of the taiwei seal, sent home, and pushed into suicide, while his funeral is humiliated.

A second, less lethal Emperor An variant appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-801-han-andi-weishenme-henxin-fei-taizi-lgnhbsqnp6vdigsmqfkxiqmx2vdy|Hanji 801]]. [[LaiLiLateHan|来历]], [[HuanYanLateHan|桓焉]], [[ZhangHaoRemonstratorLateHan|张皓]], [[ShiYanLateHan|施延]], [[ZhuChangLateHan|朱伥]], and other officials defend ten-year-old [[EmperorShunOfHan|刘保]] against deposition. An's edict recodes the Hongdu Gate remonstrance as noisy hidden motive and threatens punishment; after Lai Li persists, An dismisses Lai Li and his brothers, cuts his fief revenue, and degrades and excludes his mother from palace remonstrance. The An-era sequence therefore ranges from execution and forced suicide to threat, office removal, fief reduction, and family pressure.

## Key Claims

- Direct remonstrance becomes most dangerous when it shifts from policy correction to the ruler's self-image and hearing capacity.
- Public or evidence-backed form can amplify risk by making criticism hard to absorb privately or easy for threatened favorites to answer with counteraccusation.
- A captured court can recode corrective speech as resentment, deception, or factional disloyalty when the target is favored insiders.
- Criminalization can widen from the original speaker to defenders, solidarity actors, and clemency advocates.
- Historical precedent and loyalty framing can fail when the ruler reads them as pressure or comparison with tyrants.
- Criminalization ranges from execution and forced suicide to edicts, dismissal, fief reduction, and family pressure.
- The outcome strengthens the captured court environment by teaching officials that truthful correction can end in punishment or prison death.

## Evidence

- Ruler-focused criticism: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-850-huandi-weihe-zhiyi-sha-zhongchen-lokxtceq7vv-giec6iufwwk9fwk7|Hanji 850]] says Li Yun criticizes overlarge rewards and then questions whether Huan wants to hear truth and review right and wrong.
- Public memorial form: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-850-huandi-weihe-zhiyi-sha-zhongchen-lokxtceq7vv-giec6iufwwk9fwk7|Hanji 850]] says Li Yun submits an unsealed memorial and sends copies to the Three Excellencies.
- Solidarity widening: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-850-huandi-weihe-zhiyi-sha-zhongchen-lokxtceq7vv-giec6iufwwk9fwk7|Hanji 850]] says Du Zhong asks to live and die with Li Yun, after which Huan sends him into the same criminal process.
- Failed clemency framing: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-850-huandi-weihe-zhiyi-sha-zhongchen-lokxtceq7vv-giec6iufwwk9fwk7|Hanji 850]] says Chen Fan invokes Liu Bang, Emperor Cheng, Zhou Chang, Zhu Yun, and Bigan to argue against killing Li Yun, but Huan punishes intercessors.
- Failed mitigation and final terror: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-850-huandi-weihe-zhiyi-sha-zhongchen-lokxtceq7vv-giec6iufwwk9fwk7|Hanji 850]] says Guan Ba tries to minimize the case as ignorant speech, Huan rejects release, and Li Yun and Du Zhong die in prison.
- Zhao Teng's execution: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-800-yangzhen-zhisi-yi-chang-jianchen-dui-qingguan-de-jiaosha-lk9qcfs4xl-rjd-zpwdtsblybxa8|Hanji 800]] says Zhao Teng criticizes court gains and losses, is treated as deceiving the ruler and committing grave disloyalty, and is executed despite Yang Zhen's plea for mitigation.
- Yang Zhen as resentful critic: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-800-yangzhen-zhisi-yi-chang-jianchen-dui-qingguan-de-jiaosha-lk9qcfs4xl-rjd-zpwdtsblybxa8|Hanji 800]] says Yang Zhen investigates forged orders by Fan Feng's group, after which the group uses a star report and Geng Bao's accusation to portray him as resentful and unrepentant.
- Office removal and forced suicide: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-800-yangzhen-zhisi-yi-chang-jianchen-dui-qingguan-de-jiaosha-lk9qcfs4xl-rjd-zpwdtsblybxa8|Hanji 800]] says An strips Yang Zhen of the taiwei seal, sends him home, and Yang kills himself at Xiyang Pavilion after saying he failed to punish corrupt favorites.
- Hongdu Gate threat: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-801-han-andi-weishenme-henxin-fei-taizi-lgnhbsqnp6vdigsmqfkxiqmx2vdy|Hanji 801]] says Emperor An's edict accuses Lai Li and other remonstrants of hidden motives and threatens punishment if they persist.
- Administrative and family punishment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-801-han-andi-weishenme-henxin-fei-taizi-lgnhbsqnp6vdigsmqfkxiqmx2vdy|Hanji 801]] says Lai Li persists after others retreat, after which An dismisses Lai Li and his brothers, reduces fief revenue, and degrades Wu'an Princess.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept is grounded primarily in Hanji 850, Hanji 800, and Hanji 801 and should not be generalized to every direct remonstrance. Nearby Huan-era episodes qualify the pattern: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-852-dang-meinv-ruyun-shi-fu-haishi-huo-luml30ytbc-awpvk-7-23ehzbqpx|Hanji 852]] shows partial acceptance of Chen Fan's harem-cost warning and initial tolerance of Yuan Hong's blunt middle-ruler diagnosis, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-873-ta-ruhe-liyong-tianxiang-tongma-diwang-lqebkodcbcrsvyf80qwxnobydbhc|Hanji 873]] shows Xiang Kai punished but not killed. Hanji 800 also mixes categories: Zhao Teng is executed for a blunt memorial, while Yang Zhen is not formally executed but is removed and driven to suicide after exposing forged-edict corruption. Directness is therefore a risk amplifier under certain authority, face, evidence, and faction conditions, not a sufficient cause by itself.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 800 as the earlier Emperor An death layer: Zhao Teng's execution, Yang Zhen's failed mitigation plea, and Yang's removal and suicide after investigating Fan Feng's forged-edict group.
- Reframed Hanji 801 as the second An-era variant, focused on Hongdu Gate threat, Lai Li's persistence, and office/fief/family punishment.
- Created the concept from Hanji 850's Li Yun, Du Zhong, Chen Fan, and Guan Ba sequence.
