# Treasury Safe-Asset Fracture / 美债安全资产裂纹

## Definition

Treasury safe-asset fracture is the weakening of long-duration U.S. Treasuries' financing convenience, crisis hedge behavior, or perceived institutional reliability before an outright default or disorderly market break occurs.

## Current Synthesis

The episode distinguishes orderly repricing from collapse. Investors can continue buying Treasuries while demanding more term premium for inflation, fiscal uncertainty, issuance, and buyer risk. Foreign surplus countries and the Federal Reserve are described as successive marginal buyers whose reliability has weakened under inflation and balance-sheet reduction, leaving the long end more exposed to market absorption.

Debt sustainability depends not only on the interest-rate-versus-nominal-growth comparison but also on primary deficits and institutional response. A safe asset should finance cheaply in normal times and provide negative beta in crises; episodes of simultaneous stock-and-long-bond losses weaken the second function. Short bills can retain stronger liquidity and dollar-infrastructure support even while long bonds lose some hedge quality.

## Key Claims

- Safe-asset status can erode gradually through risk premium and correlation before default risk becomes dominant.
- The marginal-buyer structure matters when issuance is large and central-bank purchases are constrained.
- Debt dynamics depend on rates, nominal growth, deficits, rollover, and institutional credibility together.
- Fiscal dominance risk rises if monetary policy must accommodate government financing or inflation erodes debt value.
- Short- and long-duration Treasuries can retain different safety properties.

## Evidence

- **Buyer and term-premium channel:** [[lq3ilhp6uzpmrwqprbuh28j5ljdt-lq3ilhp6uzpmrwqprbuh28j5ljdt]] describes changing marginal buyers and orderly demands for higher compensation.
- **Debt-dynamics channel:** [[lq3ilhp6uzpmrwqprbuh28j5ljdt-lq3ilhp6uzpmrwqprbuh28j5ljdt]] combines r-versus-g with persistent deficits and rigid spending.
- **Hedge-function channel:** [[lq3ilhp6uzpmrwqprbuh28j5ljdt-lq3ilhp6uzpmrwqprbuh28j5ljdt]] contrasts long-bond stock-bond co-movement with short-bill dollar liquidity.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source explicitly does not claim imminent U.S. default or current disorder. Treasury depth, taxation capacity, dollar invoicing, collateral use, Federal Reserve tools, and global liquidity demand can preserve safety functions. Stock-bond correlation varies by inflation regime, and a positive correlation episode does not by itself end reserve-asset status.

## What Changed

- Added a middle category between unquestioned risk-free treatment and imminent-collapse narratives.
- Separated long-bond crisis hedging from short-bill liquidity infrastructure.
