# Localized Product Definition / 本土场景产品定义

## Definition

Localized product definition / 本土场景产品定义 is the strategy of redesigning a product around local usage conditions, pain points, habits, and service expectations rather than copying a foreign template or competing only on production cost.

## Current Synthesis

In [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]], [[FangtaiGroup|方太]] is the core case. [[MaoZhongqun|毛中群]] argues that copied European and American range hoods failed Chinese kitchens because Chinese cooking produced heavier oil smoke and exposed pain points around oil leakage, suction, noise, appearance, cleaning, and after-sales service.

The concept explains why a manufacturing firm may need brand, R&D, sales channels, and service. If the main capability is defining and solving local use problems, the factory is only one part of the moat.

## Key Claims

- Localized definition begins with use-context mismatch, not simply national branding.
- It can turn a familiar product category into a premium product when local pain points are real.
- It requires front-end market research and product judgment before manufacturing optimization.
- Channel and service matter because local product fit includes buying, installation, repair, and trust.
- The concept is different from OEM depth: it moves the firm closer to consumer problem ownership.

## Evidence

- **Fangtai range hoods:** [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]] describes the mismatch between copied foreign products and Chinese kitchen oil-smoke conditions.
- **Founder decision:** [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]] says Mao Zhongqun chose range hoods after research while Mao Lixiang initially preferred microwaves.
- **Brand route:** [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]] connects Fangtai's R&D, sales channel, after-sales, and brand ownership to the shift beyond lighter exports.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

- Localized product definition does not imply every successful manufacturer must build a consumer brand; Shenzhou's OEM route remains a counterexample inside the same source.
- The source gives Fangtai as a strong case, but it does not prove the strategy works across all appliance categories.

## What Changed
