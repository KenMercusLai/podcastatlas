# OpenClaw For Teams

OpenClaw for Teams is the product-form question developed in [[women-shi-ruhe-dingyi-openclaw-for-teams-xin-chanpin-xingtai-de-duitan-kuse-junior-lianchuang-jian-cto-yuhao-lkp1a0todflxoyycyo3zhrap3ebv]] through [[Kuse]] and [[Junior]]. The source argues that a team version of [[OpenClaw]] or [[OpenCloud]] cannot simply attach a personal assistant to a shared workspace; it has to behave like company labor.

The key distinction is orientation. Personal agents may organize memory around an owner, while team agents need [[EnterpriseAgentMemory]] around company goals, projects, roles, permissions, sensitive data, and coworker relationships. They also need [[AgentIdentityAndAuthentication]] because they may use email, phone numbers, payment accounts, browsers, and external services under company authority.

The product form therefore joins several layers: [[DigitalEmployees]] as the buyer metaphor, [[AgentEvaluationBenchmarks]] as the quality loop, [[EnterpriseAgentGovernance]] as the security and audit layer, and [[OutcomeBasedAIPricing]] or salary-like pricing as the commercial frame.

## Key Claims
- Team agents should be designed as role-bearing workers, not only as shared assistants.
- Company-first memory is different from personal memory because the agent must understand projects, reporting lines, privacy boundaries, and what should not be shared.
- Useful authority expands risk: an agent that can register services, pay, email customers, or access company systems also needs phishing, prompt-injection, and disclosure tests.
- The team product can target labor budgets rather than only SaaS budgets, but that requires stronger reliability, security, auditability, and customer trust.
