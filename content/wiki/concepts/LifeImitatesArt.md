# Life Imitates Art / 生活模仿艺术

Life imitates art is the [[OscarWilde|Wilde]] aesthetic stance emphasized in [[10-wang-erde-qiyi-gushiji-yong-zui-hui-sahuang-de-zuichun-qinwen-tianshi-545332195]]. The source retells Wilde's story about a hunter who invents mythic visions until he actually sees them, then refuses to admit it; the point is that imagination can teach perception what to notice before reality appears.

For the wiki, the concept names the source's link between lying, fiction, social performance, and aesthetic perception. Wilde's "lying" is not stored here as ordinary fraud; it is the artistic capacity to make a world vivid enough that life begins to copy or recognize it. That connects the concept to [[ArtAgainstMoralInstruction]], because the story's value lies in crafted unreality rather than moral reportage.

## Key Claims
- Fiction can precede recognition: art may shape what later counts as experience.
- Aesthetic lying differs from deception when it creates perceptual or emotional possibility rather than merely hiding facts.
- Social persona, oral storytelling, aphorism, and written fiction can belong to one aesthetic practice.
- The concept is risky when inflated into permission for false evidence; [[FictionalEvidenceSeduction]] names that darker edge.
