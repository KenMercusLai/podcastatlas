## Definition

Hetu-Luoshu / 河图洛书 is the mythic sign complex summarized by "河出图，洛出书": diagrams or writings associated with river-borne sacred animals and later linked to Fuxi, Bagua, yin-yang, the Yijing tradition, and cosmic order.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-651-hetu-luoshu-zhi-mi-ni-neng-pojie-ma-lvkgovzsk0yqjb-vbvfxl0ikx2u9|Hanji 651]] uses Hetu-Luoshu less as a technical diagram than as a legitimacy language. The episode opens with dragon-horse and turtle legends to explain why a court could treat water movement, earth, and symbolic pattern as politically meaningful.

The source's main historical wiki use is interpretive. It shows how a culturally prestigious and authenticity-contested sign tradition could be repurposed by officials around [[WangMang|王莽]]: a riverbank collapse becomes evidence that "earth controls water," and that reading is made to predict Xiongnu defeat. Hetu-Luoshu therefore belongs beside [[AuspiciousOmenPolitics|祥瑞政治]] and [[MandateOfHeavenLegitimacy|天命合法性]] as a repertoire for making events feel cosmically legible.

The [[KunlunShrineGhostBlowsOutTheLight|《昆仑神宫》]] discussion adds a literary-decision use. Its summary assigns one and six to water and north, two and seven to fire and south, three and eight to wood and east, four and nine to metal and west, and five and ten to earth and the center; 胡八一 uses “天一生水” to justify a northward escape. This demonstrates cultural portability, not predictive validity or a settled reconstruction of the diagrams' textual history.

## Key Claims

- Hetu-Luoshu matters politically because its cultural prestige lets later actors translate physical anomalies into meaningful signs.
- The tradition links myth, diagrams, cosmology, and classics rather than only one fixed object.
- Its authenticity can remain disputed while its political and cultural effects remain historically important.
- In Hanji 651, the sign system is activated by officials rather than by a neutral observation: interpretation converts a river event into a military omen.
- The concept should be used for sign-language and legitimacy analysis, not as proof that the underlying myths or diagrams caused events.
- Later fiction and popular explanation can turn the number-direction correspondences into a compact navigation or decision device.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The page does not settle the authenticity of Hetu-Luoshu or reconstruct its full textual history. Hanji 651 explicitly treats the tradition as influential despite dispute, and the wiki preserves that boundary. The concrete military judgment in 16 CE is evidence of court interpretation and flattery, not evidence that the river event predicted the campaign result. The 《昆仑神宫》 episode is literary conversation, not specialist verification of the diagrams, their chronology, or their efficacy.
