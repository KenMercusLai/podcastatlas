# UAP Compartmentalization

## Definition
UAP compartmentalization is the claim that unidentified aerial phenomena may remain unresolved because special access programs, national-security secrecy, and fragmented testimony prevent observers from assembling the full technical or organizational picture.

## Current Synthesis
The Weinstein interview makes UAPs a secrecy and epistemology problem rather than a simple alien-visitation claim. Weinstein says there is a real unresolved signal, but explicitly keeps several explanations open: Chinese technology, U.S. technology, a fabricated narrative, or a non-human contact scenario if general relativity is not the final boundary. The wiki stores the concept beside [[ScientificSkepticism]] because the source relies heavily on testimony from people Weinstein finds credible.

## Key Claims
- UAP claims can be serious without immediately proving alien visitation.
- National-security compartmentalization can prevent even informed people from knowing whether sightings are domestic, adversarial, misleading, or anomalous.
- Weinstein weights repeated accounts from smart, sober people more than videos, which makes the claim dependent on testimony quality.
- The interpretation changes with the physics assumption: if general relativity is final, the source leans toward nation-state systems; if not, contact remains possible in Weinstein's view.
- The concept overlaps with UFO conspiracy culture but is narrower because it focuses on information architecture, special access, and technical uncertainty.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not present primary evidence for a specific UAP case. Because the claim depends on testimony, secrecy, and inference from physics uncertainty, the wiki keeps it source-scoped and linked to skepticism rather than treating it as a resolved technical or extraterrestrial conclusion.
