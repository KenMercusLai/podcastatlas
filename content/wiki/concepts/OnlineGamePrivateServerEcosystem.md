# Online Game Private-Server Ecosystem

Online game private-server ecosystem names the gray-market field that forms when leaked code, unofficial servers, internet cafes, small teams, or later licensees operate variants of an online game outside the original official service. [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]] adds the concept through [[LegendOfMir|《传奇》]].

The episode treats private servers as both harm and extension. They competed with [[Shanda|盛大]] and complicated [[OnlineGameIPRightsDisputes]], but they also generated gameplay variants, player memory, and market signals that later fed [[GameIPLongTail]] and more regularized licensing.
