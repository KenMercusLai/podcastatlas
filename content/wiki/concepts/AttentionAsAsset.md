# Attention as Asset / 注意力资产

## Definition

Attention as asset is the episode's proposal to treat sustained and changing public interest in topics, industries, accounts, and content brands as a measurable resource that can influence distribution, commercial value, and market narratives.

## Current Synthesis

[[xie-yi-fen-jilv-md-daiti-wo-rushi-touzi-lnb4eenlg5uvx2btqppqa87eieuf]] distinguishes two sides of attention. A person's own attention is finite operating capacity consumed by development, expression, investing, and context switching; attention received from others can become reach, advertising income, customer discovery, or narrative momentum. The proposed product therefore emphasizes rate of change, competitive strength, topic composition, and content cadence rather than follower totals alone. This is a product hypothesis, not established evidence that attention predicts price or cash flow.

## Key Claims

- Stock measures such as total followers can miss whether an account or narrative is accelerating or fading.
- Relative attention trends may help creators compare competitors, choose topics, and discuss advertising prices.
- Price movement can create narratives and public attention, while narrative diffusion may amplify later price acceleration.
- Personal attention and audience attention are economically asymmetric: the first is a limited cost, while the second can become distribution and revenue.
- Metrics can change creator behavior, so a useful attention measure may also increase ranking pressure and short-term optimization.

## Evidence

- Measurement proposal - [[xie-yi-fen-jilv-md-daiti-wo-rushi-touzi-lnb4eenlg5uvx2btqppqa87eieuf]] proposes tracking marginal attention change, topic labels, publishing cadence, content quality signals, and account strength.
- Commercial use - [[xie-yi-fen-jilv-md-daiti-wo-rushi-touzi-lnb4eenlg5uvx2btqppqa87eieuf]] names advertising pricing, growth-account discovery, industry observation, and creator topic selection as intended uses.
- Market linkage - [[xie-yi-fen-jilv-md-daiti-wo-rushi-touzi-lnb4eenlg5uvx2btqppqa87eieuf]] treats attention mostly as lagging but suggests that acceleration in discussion may sometimes precede broader narrative breakout.
- Capacity contrast - [[xie-yi-fen-jilv-md-daiti-wo-rushi-touzi-lnb4eenlg5uvx2btqppqa87eieuf]] links the product idea to finite daily energy and the difficulty of switching between deep development and public expression.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The feature was still being developed, and the source supplies no validation set, metric definition, causal test, or audited commercial outcomes. Attention can follow price rather than predict it, and engagement can be manipulated or disconnected from trust, purchasing power, and durable cash flow. Ranking systems may reward controversy, cadence, or superficial reach while degrading creative quality and user well-being.

## What Changed

- Created a concept distinguishing marginal attention measurement from follower counts and from individual cognitive attention.
