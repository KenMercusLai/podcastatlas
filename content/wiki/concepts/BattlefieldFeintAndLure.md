## Definition

Battlefield feint and lure / 声东击西与诱敌 is the use of false targets, controlled exits, visible weakness, feigned retreat, scarcity bait, identity confusion, or misleading display to move an enemy into a weaker position before the decisive blow.

## Current Synthesis

The current synthesis treats battlefield deception as control over enemy expectation, not merely hidden movement. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-733-2-gengyan-youzhizhe-shijingcheng-jiangmen-huzi-yongguan-sanjun-lnlfumtdzrigby9ox4v6pzbeogeh|Hanji 733-2]] develops this through [[GengYanEasternHan|耿弇]]'s Qinghe and Qi-region campaigns: he leaves an exit to avoid desperate resistance, announces one target while attacking another, refuses to let imperial arrival set tempo, feigns retreat, and prepares ambushes on retreat paths.

The pre-Linzi and Linzi Geng Yan cases broaden that pattern into information travel and prestige manipulation. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-695-ta-xinku-da-xia-qianli-jiangshan-weihe-gongshou-songren-lttlnoazrrg5dtdvfqmngcbfeody|Hanji 695]] has panic and false timing travel through released or escaping enemies, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-696-bi-hanxin-geng-qiang-zhe-wei-donghan-mingjiang-zhen-de-meng-lkomaa6qhwwqwmb48vxyabay0skt|Hanji 696]] has a false Xi'an announcement, rapid Linzi capture, no-plunder provocation, visible weakness, fortified defense, and retreat-path ambush against [[ZhangBuEasternHan|张步]].

Hanji 688 adds two older and rougher Chimei layers. The [[ChimeiRebellion|赤眉]] use scarcity bait by putting beans over earth in abandoned baggage, exploiting hungry Han troops who stop to scramble for food and become vulnerable. [[FengYiEasternHan|冯异]] later reverses the deception problem: he selects troops, dresses them like Chimei soldiers, hides them, and attacks after fatigue and confusion make identity unreliable. The same source also links failed susceptibility to [[StatusDrivenCommandFailure|status-driven command failure]] because [[DengYuEasternHan|邓禹]] fights hungry soldiers into a trap-prone condition.

The Chengdu campaign in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-712-liuxiu-weihe-bei-yuwei-zui-hui-da-zhang-de-huangdi-lt7rx7tblodjfngqfdq33ikrcmyn|Hanji 712]] shows deception used by both sides after earlier command risk. [[WuHanEasternHan|Wu Han]] uses flags, fires, and secret night movement to escape encirclement and rejoin [[LiuShangEasternHan|Liu Shang]]. [[YanCenEasternHan|Yan Cen]] later uses a visible bridge/market challenge to fix Wu Han's attention while cavalry circles behind him. [[ZhangKanEasternHan|Zhang Kan]] then recommends showing weakness rather than retreating, treating enemy exhaustion as a condition for a lure.

Hanji 697 reinforces the Geng Yan pattern as a broader tactical repertoire: psychological warfare, strongpoint attacks, encirclement-relief, false-target strikes, avoid-strong/strike-weak choices, and calm morale maintenance after a thigh wound. It also ties deception to finish-work because Zhang Bu's surrender is followed by troop and wagon dispersal rather than left as a battlefield result alone.

## Key Claims

- Feints work by changing enemy movement and information circulation, not only by hiding one's own movement.
- Leaving an apparent exit can be more effective than total encirclement when panic resistance would be costly.
- Public statements and provocations become tactical instruments when they make the enemy treat an exposed move as safe, urgent, or prestigious.
- Scarcity bait can turn an army's hunger into battlefield vulnerability before direct combat begins.
- Identity confusion works when clothing, fatigue, and concealment make enemy recognition unreliable.
- Feigned weakness or retreat is useful only if the pursuer's route and timing have already been prepared.
- Tactical deception becomes strategically durable only when followed by surrender handling, force dispersal, or territorial consolidation.

## Evidence

- Controlled exit in encirclement: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-733-2-gengyan-youzhizhe-shijingcheng-jiangmen-huzi-yongguan-sanjun-lnlfumtdzrigby9ox4v6pzbeogeh|Hanji 733-2]] describes leaving an escape route rather than closing a desperate enemy in completely.
- Fear transmission and false timing: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-695-ta-xinku-da-xia-qianli-jiangshan-weihe-gongshou-songren-lttlnoazrrg5dtdvfqmngcbfeody|Hanji 695]] says Geng Yan leaves an escape gap at Zhu'a so panic reaches Zhongcheng, then releases captives to tell Fei Yi a false attack date for Juli.
- High-ground lure and follow-through: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-695-ta-xinku-da-xia-qianli-jiangshan-weihe-gongshou-songren-lttlnoazrrg5dtdvfqmngcbfeody|Hanji 695]] says Geng Yan treats the Juli siege equipment as bait for Fei Yi, occupies high ground, kills Fei Yi, displays the head, and then sweeps Jinan camps.
- Announced target and real target: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-696-bi-hanxin-geng-qiang-zhe-wei-donghan-mingjiang-zhen-de-meng-lkomaa6qhwwqwmb48vxyabay0skt|Hanji 696]] says Geng Yan announces Xi'an while striking Linzi, and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-733-2-gengyan-youzhizhe-shijingcheng-jiangmen-huzi-yongguan-sanjun-lnlfumtdzrigby9ox4v6pzbeogeh|Hanji 733-2]] summarizes the same false-target pattern.
- Provocation and prepared defense: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-696-bi-hanxin-geng-qiang-zhe-wei-donghan-mingjiang-zhen-de-meng-lkomaa6qhwwqwmb48vxyabay0skt|Hanji 696]] says Geng Yan's no-plunder message angers Zhang Bu, whose march from Ju makes him tired and exposed against fortified camp defenses.
- Lure and ambush: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-696-bi-hanxin-geng-qiang-zhe-wei-donghan-mingjiang-zhen-de-meng-lkomaa6qhwwqwmb48vxyabay0skt|Hanji 696]] says Geng Yan shows weakness before the main attack and sets ambushes along Zhang Bu's retreat route, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-733-2-gengyan-youzhizhe-shijingcheng-jiangmen-huzi-yongguan-sanjun-lnlfumtdzrigby9ox4v6pzbeogeh|Hanji 733-2]] presents feigned retreat, provoked pursuit, and retreat-path ambush as recurring moves in the Zhang Bu-linked campaign.
- Scarcity bait: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-688-cong-yongbing-baiwan-dao-kuibuchengjun-dengyu-jingli-le-shenme-lpabdvtmhtrf37puwvgi4c9ajp60|Hanji 688]] says Chimei leaves bean-covered baggage that draws hungry Han soldiers into disorder over what proves to be earth underneath.
- Identity-confusion ambush: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-688-cong-yongbing-baiwan-dao-kuibuchengjun-dengyu-jingli-le-shenme-lpabdvtmhtrf37puwvgi4c9ajp60|Hanji 688]] says Feng Yi dresses selected troops like Chimei soldiers, hides them, and attacks after Chimei fatigue and confusion set in.
- Tactical repertoire and command composure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-697-yi-kou-qi-kan-dong-donghan-mingjiang-gengyan-de-chuanqi-rensheng-lpbhbmedezq7pjuz5l3q-s1unh7c|Hanji 697]] summarizes Geng Yan's use of psychological warfare, attack on strongholds, encirclement-relief, false-target strikes, avoid-strong/strike-weak tactics, and calm encouragement after being wounded in the thigh.
- Surrender follow-through: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-697-yi-kou-qi-kan-dong-donghan-mingjiang-gengyan-de-chuanqi-rensheng-lpbhbmedezq7pjuz5l3q-s1unh7c|Hanji 697]] says Zhang Bu's surrender is followed by Geng Yan dispersing the remaining troops and wagons and completing Qi pacification.
- Concealed re-concentration: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-712-liuxiu-weihe-bei-yuwei-zui-hui-da-zhang-de-huangdi-lt7rx7tblodjfngqfdq33ikrcmyn|Hanji 712]] says Wu Han uses flags and fires while secretly moving at night to rejoin Liu Shang after being surrounded.
- Frontal display and rear cavalry: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-712-liuxiu-weihe-bei-yuwei-zui-hui-da-zhang-de-huangdi-lt7rx7tblodjfngqfdq33ikrcmyn|Hanji 712]] says Yan Cen beats drums and displays flags at the bridge/market front while cavalry circles behind Wu Han and defeats him.
- Show-weakness lure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-712-liuxiu-weihe-bei-yuwei-zui-hui-da-zhang-de-huangdi-lt7rx7tblodjfngqfdq33ikrcmyn|Hanji 712]] says Zhang Kan advises Wu Han to show weakness and lure Gongsun Shu's side instead of retreating under food pressure.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The page currently synthesizes six source notes and should not be treated as a complete theory of ancient Chinese deception tactics. Hanji 688 adds Chimei and Feng Yi cases, but exact Xiaodi/Mianchi place readings, Deng Hong's full role, and force counts remain source-scoped. Hanji 695 adds a strong fear-and-false-timing case, but Fei Yi, Fei Gan, Zhu'a, Zhongcheng, Juli, and exact camp counts remain source-scoped; its Fei Yi is not the existing Warring States [[FeiYi|肥义]] page. Hanji 712 ends before the final outcome of Zhang Kan's show-weakness advice, so that item supports intended tactical logic rather than a completed result in this source. Hanji 696 and Hanji 697's "比韩信更强", "战神", "兵仙", and "every battle victorious" language is treated as source-scoped evaluation, not as independent tactical proof.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 688 as the scarcity-bait and identity-confusion layer in the Chimei/Feng Yi campaign.
- Connected Deng Yu's susceptibility to deception with status-driven command failure and hungry-troop conditions.
- Preserved Hanji 695 and Hanji 696 as paired Geng Yan layers around fear transmission, false targets, provocation, and retreat-path ambush.
- Preserved Hanji 712's Chengdu examples and Hanji 697's connection between tactics and surrendered-force dispersal.
