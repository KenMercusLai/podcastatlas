# MacGuffin / 麦格芬

MacGuffin / 麦格芬 is the suspense device explained in [[27-xiqukeke-yizai-nin-zhuoxia-anzhi-zhadan-582370829]] as a thing, organization, secret, or question that pushes characters into motion and makes the audience curious without necessarily being the story's final meaning.

The episode's main case is [[PsychoFilm|《惊魂记》 / Psycho]]. The stolen forty thousand dollars appears to define the plot, but after the motel murder it loses centrality; the film's real pressure shifts toward identity, desire, violence, and the hidden mother/persona problem. The point is not that the money was useless, but that it worked as a lure that handed the audience from one story track to another.

## Key Claims
- A MacGuffin creates pursuit, risk, curiosity, and expectation, but it does not have to remain important after it has moved the plot.
- The device can be an object, group, clue, absent person, target location, or unresolved question.
- A successful MacGuffin can disappear without making the opening meaningless, because its job was to control attention and action.
- The device is closely related to [[NarrativeMisdirection]] because it can make the audience invest in a provisional center of the story.
