# Mobile Agent Capability Convergence

## Definition
Mobile agent capability convergence is the tendency of leading smartphone vendors to promise similar multi-step assistants because they control comparable device sensors, applications, system tools, and everyday user contexts.

## Current Synthesis
OPPO, vivo, and Honor are reported to be emphasizing agents in new operating systems, with differentiation expressed through step counts, callable skills, reminders, schedule recognition, and post-call tasks. The common direction supports the phone as a [[SmartphoneAIHub]], but headline task breadth does not establish reliability, permission safety, interoperability, or a durable lead.

## Key Claims
- Operating-system tool access is a core mobile-agent asset.
- Multi-step execution is becoming a shared flagship claim across handset brands.
- Similar scenarios make reliable completion and permission design more important differentiators than feature lists.
- Advertised skill or step counts are not directly comparable without common evaluation.

## Evidence
- Honor case: [[jinghu-gaotie-zhongqiujie-qian-chuxian-jiangjia-xinbailun-qisu-dikanong-qinquan-1017386449]] reports an assistant capable of more than 100 steps plus schedule, parcel, and post-call task handling.
- vivo case: [[jinghu-gaotie-zhongqiujie-qian-chuxian-jiangjia-xinbailun-qisu-dikanong-qinquan-1017386449]] reports more than 6,000 basic capabilities exposed as agent tools for over 10,000 tasks.
- Market convergence: [[jinghu-gaotie-zhongqiujie-qian-chuxian-jiangjia-xinbailun-qisu-dikanong-qinquan-1017386449]] says leading domestic vendors' capabilities and imagined scenarios were close, without a clear leader.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode provides vendor claims and an industry assessment, not common benchmark results. Task counts, tool counts, autonomy, device coverage, privacy, failure recovery, and comparison with Apple remain source-scoped.

## What Changed
- Created the concept from the reported OPPO, vivo, and Honor operating-system launches.
