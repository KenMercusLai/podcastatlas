# Chinese Woodblock Printing Economics / 中国雕版印刷经济性

## Definition
Chinese woodblock printing economics is the source's frame for why woodblock printing could remain practically dominant in China: production cost, labor process, typeface, edition scale, paper, and reuse mattered more than a simple technology-progress story.

## Current Synthesis
The episode uses [[YiweiHangzhiExhibition|“意味杭之”]] to challenge a common movable-type-centered misunderstanding. It argues that Chinese printing history should be read through practical production incentives: woodblocks could be efficient for actual publishing conditions, Song-style typefaces made carving faster, and cheaper but fragile bamboo paper signaled commercial scale as well as preservation risk.

## Key Claims
- Movable type was not automatically more economical or suitable than woodblock printing in Chinese publishing practice.
- Typeface design affected labor cost because straighter Song-style forms were easier for artisans to carve quickly.
- Material choices such as bamboo paper can reveal commercialization as well as later preservation fragility.
- Printing technology history should be explained through workflow and cost, not only invention priority.

## Evidence
- Woodblock dominance - [[246-hangzhou-bowuguan-yiwei-hangzhi-haojiu-mei-kan-guo-zheme-tuijian-de-zhanlan-le-8217911423-973793]] says the hosts emphasize China's long reliance on woodblock printing because it remained cost- and efficiency-competitive.
- Typeface and labor - [[246-hangzhou-bowuguan-yiwei-hangzhi-haojiu-mei-kan-guo-zheme-tuijian-de-zhanlan-le-8217911423-973793]] links Song typeface forms to faster carving and lower cost.
- Bamboo paper - [[246-hangzhou-bowuguan-yiwei-hangzhi-haojiu-mei-kan-guo-zheme-tuijian-de-zhanlan-le-8217911423-973793]] treats late-Ming bamboo paper as both fragile and evidence of publishing commercialization.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source offers a podcast-level synthesis rather than a full economic model. It supports a corrective against simplistic movable-type triumphalism, but exact cost comparisons should remain source-scoped.
