# Overseas Public Relations / 企业出海公关

## Definition
Overseas public relations is the long-term capability by which a company explains itself and responds to scrutiny in a target market through locally relevant stories, accessible spokespeople, media and government relationships, social and news monitoring, crisis readiness, and evidence of local contribution.

## Current Synthesis
[[ep137-cong-tiktok-shijian-liaoliao-qiye-chuhai-gongguan-you-duo-nan-wkg5jv9ddtotvckwbgkes6bhu2q322]] rejects the idea that overseas PR is paid press-release distribution, translation, or landmark advertising. The operative unit is a local public: reporters need a story their readers can use; residents care about employment, products, housing, prices, safety, and community effects; officials care about law and public interest; and creators protect their own audience trust. A company-centered recital of achievements does not answer those needs.

The capability is organizational as much as rhetorical. Local hires, English-capable spokespeople, live interviews, timely approvals, public affairs, monitoring, and headquarters-overseas coordination determine whether a company can participate in a news cycle. Earned coverage depends on relevance and relationship rather than a payment expectation, especially where outlets enforce strict independence rules.

Trust accumulates before the crisis. Frequent, accurate, and accessible communication gives journalists firsthand material; silence leaves the narrative to competitors and third parties. Yet local hiring or a prestigious executive does not automatically create legitimacy, and employment claims do not answer every community concern. Public relations must connect to real conduct and cannot substitute for product fit, governance, legal compliance, or political settlement.

## Key Claims
- Overseas PR is an operating capability spanning media, social channels, content, monitoring, crisis work, government relations, and public affairs.
- Local relevance is the story-selection gate: communication must explain consequences for users, workers, communities, and institutions in the target market.
- Earned media relies on credible information and durable reporter relationships rather than payment or hospitality expectations.
- Spokesperson language ability, response speed, interview access, and internal approval design materially affect whether the company is heard.
- Local teams reduce cultural distance but still require authority and effective coordination with headquarters.
- Trust is cumulative and conduct-backed; it cannot be manufactured only after a crisis begins.
- Public relations can improve understanding and credibility but cannot by itself remove legal, geopolitical, or business-model constraints.

## Evidence
- Scope and organization: [[ep137-cong-tiktok-shijian-liaoliao-qiye-chuhai-gongguan-you-duo-nan-wkg5jv9ddtotvckwbgkes6bhu2q322]] distinguishes full media, social, monitoring, crisis, government, and public-affairs work from paid distribution.
- Local relevance and independence: [[ep137-cong-tiktok-shijian-liaoliao-qiye-chuhai-gongguan-you-duo-nan-wkg5jv9ddtotvckwbgkes6bhu2q322]] uses an unavailable Chinese dating app and a sponsored Shenzhen invitation to show why company goals or payment do not create a U.S. news story.
- Access and timeliness: [[ep137-cong-tiktok-shijian-liaoliao-qiye-chuhai-gongguan-you-duo-nan-wkg5jv9ddtotvckwbgkes6bhu2q322]] connects English-speaking interview capacity and rapid response to deadlines, relationship formation, and message accuracy.
- Local public value: [[ep137-cong-tiktok-shijian-liaoliao-qiye-chuhai-gongguan-you-duo-nan-wkg5jv9ddtotvckwbgkes6bhu2q322]] uses employment, products, Haier's inaccessibility, and opposition to Amazon's New York project to show that contribution claims must answer the audience's actual concern.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The framework comes from one 2020 practitioner conversation, not a comparative study of national media systems or campaign outcomes. “Chinese” and “American” PR contain diverse outlets, firms, industries, and professional standards. Reporter practice, sponsorship rules, platform dynamics, and regulation also change over time. The cases support a disciplined operating model, not a universal claim that earned media is unpaid in every setting or that locally framed communication guarantees favorable coverage.

## What Changed
- Created a framework separating locally grounded relationship work from translated domestic messaging and paid visibility.
- Added spokesperson access, approval speed, public affairs, and headquarters-local coordination as core operational capabilities.
- Established an explicit boundary between communication improvements and structural geopolitical resolution.
