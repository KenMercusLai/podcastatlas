# Modular Customization / 模块化定制

Modular customization / 模块化定制 is the strategy of decomposing varied customer requirements into standardized modules and recombining those modules into many customer-specific products. In [[ep271-tanfang-rouxing-zhizao-gongchang-shi-shui-rang-ni-xiangyao-jiu-neng-maidao-lqyrioptggnbb8hl-llej0-5ow0v]], [[HaierGroup|海尔]] uses this approach for commercial central air-conditioning, where building area, machine-room size, humidity, temperature, and energy targets make pure standardization impractical.

The concept is a middle path between rigid mass production and one-off engineering. It helps [[FlexibleManufacturing|flexible manufacturing]] scale because the factory can reuse components, data, tests, suppliers, and process knowledge while still configuring solutions for different sites. In the Haier case, modular customization is paired with [[COSMOPlat|卡奥斯]], customer selection systems, [[ManufacturingDigitalTwin|digital twins]], and [[ManufacturingDigitalThread|digital-thread coordination]].

## Key Claims
- Customization becomes scalable when differences are captured as reusable modules rather than rebuilt from scratch.
- The product architecture has to decide which variations are standardized and which remain project-specific.
- Modular customization can improve sales because customers can get an initial scheme through a selection system before deep engineering work starts.
- The model works best when production, design, ordering, and quality systems share the same module logic.
- Poor modularization can create complexity without true flexibility if modules do not map to real customer needs.
