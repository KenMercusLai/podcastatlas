# Albedo Feedback

Albedo feedback is the climate loop in [[taking-the-shine-off-albedo-and-global-temperatures-6a730fdaca067b295bab9760]] where a less reflective Earth absorbs more sunlight and therefore warms further. [[OliverMorton]] says warming contributes to fewer clouds and scarcer ice caps, while reduced sulphur pollution also means less sunlight is reflected back into space.

The concept extends the wiki's climate branch beyond [[WildfireClimateFeedback]] and [[ClimateAdaptation]]. It treats reflectivity as both a consequence of warming and a driver of additional warming, making [[EarthEnergyImbalance]] a more direct measure than air temperature alone.

## Key Claims
- Less cloud, less ice, and fewer reflective aerosols all lower planetary reflectivity.
- Reduced reflectivity means more solar energy is absorbed rather than reflected back into space.
- The feedback makes warming partly self-reinforcing because warming changes the surfaces and clouds that govern sunlight reflection.
