# Female Journalist Fieldwork / 女记者现场方法

Female journalist fieldwork / 女记者现场方法 is the source's practical answer to the "all women reporters" label in [[xianchang-xianchang-2025-nianzhong-teji-sanlian-nvjizhe-jiemi-xinwen-beihou-de-ku-yu-guang-loz-sggelhx166n2me8v6d8k9a3c]]. The episode treats gender as a reporting condition that can shape access, risk, anger, empathy, and source trust, without reducing women reporters to soft topics or claiming they cannot handle hard news.

[[QinSi|秦思]] explains that a less aggressive appearance can sometimes lower defensiveness during covert or difficult interviews, but she also insists that women reporters still do criminal cases, disasters, war-related stories, and confrontational investigation. [[WeiZhaoyang|魏昭阳]] adds the source-trust side: as a young woman reporter, she could live closely with a woman source and hear long, vulnerable accounts that might not have opened the same way to every reporter.

## Key Claims
- Gender can affect field entry and trust, but it is not a complete theory of reporting skill.
- Women's anger at gendered violence can become reporting attention, provided it is disciplined by evidence, context, and accountability.
- The source rejects the equation of hard reporting with masculine style.
- [[InterviewAsEmbodiedReporting|Embodied interviewing]] includes how the reporter's age, gender, appearance, and manner are read by sources.
