# Human-Scale AI Use / 人作为 AI 的尺度

Human-scale AI use is the standard in [[ep275-token-tongzhang-shidai-shui-hai-neng-bu-ke-tidai-renzai-zhongliu-tebie-cehua-01-lgnagvell4jyvv7eejrcgno2xjsr]] that asks whether AI improves the user's work quality, life quality, value creation, and agency. [[ChenMingxia|陈明霞]] condenses it as the idea that the person, not the tool, token budget, KPI, or capital narrative, should be the scale by which AI use is judged.

The concept complements [[AIUsePacing]] and [[HumanAgencyUnderAI]]. Pacing asks when to slow down; agency asks what deserves delegation. Human-scale use supplies the evaluative test: a workflow is good only if it serves the person and the real context, rather than turning the person into the tool's operator, supervisor, or justification.

## Key Claims
- AI use should be evaluated through real value, quality, relief, learning, or life improvement, not through tool adoption alone.
- Token budgets and subscription access can become false signals if they encourage consumption without accepted output or better work.
- Human-scale use can mean using AI intensively, using it narrowly, or choosing not to use it when the process itself is valuable.
- The test applies to organizations too: executives should ask whether AI investment follows mission and value rather than anxiety or fashion.
- Human-scale use turns "what can AI do?" into "what should this person or organization do with AI in this situation?"
