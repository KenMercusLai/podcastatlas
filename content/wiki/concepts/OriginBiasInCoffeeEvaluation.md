# Origin Bias in Coffee Evaluation / 咖啡产区偏见

## Definition
Origin bias in coffee evaluation is the tendency for drinkers or buyers to let a producing region's reputation, stereotype, or written description shape perceived cup quality before tasting is independently tested.

## Current Synthesis
The Yunnan coffee episode treats origin bias as a practical sensory problem. Earlier descriptions of Yunnan coffee as earthy, astringent, or carrying a "devil aftertaste" became shared expectations; [[YuWenjie|余文杰]] argues that blind cupping and current-origin knowledge are needed because the region's beans and processing have changed.

## Key Claims
- Written or secondhand origin descriptions can become authority and then alter later sensory judgment.
- Yunnan coffee's older negative labels are not reliable enough to describe current production.
- Blind cupping is a practical countermeasure because it tests the cup before the label.
- Bias can also work through prestige comparison when Yunnan is judged mainly against Ethiopia or Panama.
- Better evaluation requires an origin-specific coordinate system that includes local variety, terroir, and processing.

## Evidence
- Label carryover: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] says early Yunnan descriptions such as earthiness and "devil aftertaste" shaped later expectations.
- Current production change: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] says Yunnan coffee is now much more diverse than five years earlier.
- Blind cupping countermeasure: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] says Yu Wenjie often blind cups in product selection.
- Prestige comparison: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] records his objection to asking only why a buyer would not choose Ethiopia at the same price.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not deny that some older Yunnan coffees may have had defects or distinctive aftertastes. Its claim is that inherited labels should not govern current evaluation without cup evidence.

## What Changed
- Added a sensory-bias frame for coffee-origin evaluation.
