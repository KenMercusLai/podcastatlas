# Packaged Ice-Cream Demand Pressure / 包装雪糕需求压力

## Definition
Packaged ice-cream demand pressure is the decline or squeeze faced by factory-packaged ice cream and popsicles when health concerns, beverage substitutes, fresh-made desserts, and price-band polarization divert peak-season demand.

## Current Synthesis
The source says China's traditional packaged ice-cream and popsicle category fell again during the May-August peak season. The pressure is multi-sided: consumers see high-sugar ice cream less as guilt-free pleasure, tea and coffee brands compete for the same refreshment occasions, fresh-made ice cream takes more of the high-end market, and mass packaged sales concentrate around lower price bands.

## Key Claims
- Health awareness can turn an impulse dessert from reward into guilt.
- Tea and coffee chains can substitute for packaged ice cream in hot-weather refreshment occasions.
- Fresh-made ice cream can capture high-end demand that packaged products once served.
- Packaged ice cream may remain strong in lower price bands even as premium packaged formats weaken.

## Evidence
- Sales evidence - [[tulasi-zishengtang-qisu-pingjia-shangchao-aoleqi-alo-qianyue-wangyibo-1014854463]] cites May-August sales falling by nearly 13% year over year.
- Health evidence - [[tulasi-zishengtang-qisu-pingjia-shangchao-aoleqi-alo-qianyue-wangyibo-1014854463]] says health awareness made high-sugar, high-calorie ice cream feel more guilt-inducing.
- Substitution evidence - [[tulasi-zishengtang-qisu-pingjia-shangchao-aoleqi-alo-qianyue-wangyibo-1014854463]] says tea drinks and coffee brands are taking some packaged ice-cream customers.
- Format evidence - [[tulasi-zishengtang-qisu-pingjia-shangchao-aoleqi-alo-qianyue-wangyibo-1014854463]] says fresh-made ice cream stores accounted for more than 60% of the RMB30-plus high-end market during June-August.
- Price-band evidence - [[tulasi-zishengtang-qisu-pingjia-shangchao-aoleqi-alo-qianyue-wangyibo-1014854463]] says packaged sales concentrate in the RMB3-6 band where [[Mengniu|蒙牛]] and local brands are strong, while brands such as [[Nestle]] and Magnum target mid-to-high-end positions.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source reports category and channel signals, not audited company-by-company sales. It also does not show that packaged ice cream is disappearing; lower-price packaged products may remain durable even while premium packaged formats lose share.
