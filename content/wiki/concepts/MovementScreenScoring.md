# Movement Screen Scoring / 动作筛查评分

## Definition
Movement screen scoring is the episode's do-it-yourself technique assessment: go joint by joint through shoulder, elbow, low back, hip, knee, and ankle with representative pressing and pulling movements, record frontal and side views, and score each pattern 0, 1, or 3.

## Current Synthesis
The screen is a gate rather than a scoreboard. A 0 means do not do the exercise because of high acute risk, a 1 means a minor flaw that requires caution with load and volume, and a 3 means the movement is close enough to good to proceed on a reasonable protocol.

Galpin's four things to watch are symmetry, stability, awareness, and full range of motion. He notes that many apparent technique problems are awareness problems rather than structural limits, which is why the screen produces a corrected attempt rather than a permanent verdict. The screen sits before strength and power testing in [[FitnessAssessmentTestingWeek]], because maximal effort is only safe once the pattern has cleared that gate.

## Key Claims
- The screen covers the whole body joint by joint through upper- and lower-body press and pull patterns rather than one athletic movement.
- Scoring is deliberately coarse: 0 forbids the exercise, 1 permits it with load and volume caution, and 3 permits a reasonable protocol.
- Symmetry, stability, awareness, and full range of motion are the four things the recording is checked for.
- Many technique faults are awareness deficits that resolve immediately once the person is told the cue, so a single screen is not a diagnosis.
- The screen functions as a gate that should be cleared before heavy or maximal strength testing.

## Evidence
- Screen structure: [[essentials-how-to-assess-improve-all-aspects-of-your-fitness-dr-andy-galpin-scim5641095924]] goes joint by joint through shoulder, elbow, low back, hip, knee, and ankle using an upper-body press, upper-body pull, lower-body press, and lower-body pull such as a push-up, pull-up or bent row, squat, and deadlift.
- Recording method: [[essentials-how-to-assess-improve-all-aspects-of-your-fitness-dr-andy-galpin-scim5641095924]] records a frontal and a side view with three to 10 slow controlled reps per angle.
- Scoring rule: [[essentials-how-to-assess-improve-all-aspects-of-your-fitness-dr-andy-galpin-scim5641095924]] scores 0, 1, or 3, with 0 meaning don't do the exercise, 1 meaning a minor flaw needing caution with load and volume, and 3 meaning close enough to proceed.
- Awareness finding: [[essentials-how-to-assess-improve-all-aspects-of-your-fitness-dr-andy-galpin-scim5641095924]] cites students who corrected squat technique immediately once told their heels should stay on the ground.
- Gold standard: [[essentials-how-to-assess-improve-all-aspects-of-your-fitness-dr-andy-galpin-scim5641095924]] names a highly qualified physical therapist or movement specialist as the professional version of the same screen.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Self-screening is not a clinical examination and cannot identify structural pathology, neurological deficits, or the cause of pain. The scoring scale is the episode's own practical heuristic rather than a validated instrument, and the source notes that apparent technique problems can reflect coaching or awareness rather than physical limitation, so a low score is information to act on rather than a diagnosis to accept.
