# Advance News Trading Experiment

The advance news trading experiment is the markets case described in [[cockroaches-1-modi-0-indias-remarkable-protests-6a6729a33e2f0b6f92d26ab8]], where participants saw historical next-day newspaper front pages and then traded stocks and bonds. The source says ordinary participants, and even AI models, struggled to turn advance news into strong returns.

The experiment matters because it separates information from investable edge. Participants often misread the [[MarketExpectationGap]] - what was new relative to what markets already expected - and then damaged results through poor [[PositionSizing]], leverage, and insufficient abstention.

Expert macro traders did better in the source because they adjusted size sharply with confidence and sometimes chose not to bet. That makes the experiment an applied case for [[InvestmentRiskManagement]], [[InvestmentEdge]], and [[KellyCriterion]] rather than only a puzzle about prediction.
