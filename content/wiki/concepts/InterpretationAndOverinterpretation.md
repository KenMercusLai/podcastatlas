# Interpretation And Overinterpretation

## Definition
Interpretation asks what a sign means in its genre and context; overinterpretation treats an attractive pattern as a proven cause, genealogy or authorial code without adequate independent evidence. Literary pleasure and historical proof are different claims.

## Current Synthesis
Across literary, historical, folkloric and palaeographic examples, the failure is not looking for meaning but promoting resemblance into certainty. Appropriate checks differ: compare textual variants and direct quotations for a novel, dates and routes for a transmitted tale, excavated forms for a character, and provenance for an archival claim. A counter-reading can itself overreach; skepticism of received stories does not license the next elegant conspiracy.

## Key Claims
- Layered analogy and symbolic recurrence can illuminate a work without proving universal origin or a one-to-one historical key.
- A fictional detective's coherent pattern can fail when evidence, access and actors' contingent actions are reconstructed.
- Textual allusion, authorship and political allegory require graded evidence, not a total decoding system.
- Concrete institutions and competing versions often explain a story better than a single dynastic or biographical cipher.
- Early records, chronology and material traces constrain folklore, historical revision and character etymology.
- A forged document or visually satisfying clue may preserve a theory's appeal even after its evidentiary basis collapses.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
These twenty notes are from one book-discussion program, not independent academic replications. The comparison between [[StoryMotifTransmission|motif survival]] and demonstrated genealogy must not be collapsed. The hosts' preference for legal/family readings over total-cipher Redology does not establish Cao Xueqin's private intent. Literary inference is allowed at lower confidence without masquerading as a dated historical finding.

## What Changed
- Grouped twenty episodes by evidence failure and corresponding checks, rather than source arrival.
- Kept the Red Chamber and Eco episodes as linked interpretive debates, not independent confirmation.
