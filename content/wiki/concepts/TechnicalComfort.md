## Definition
Technical comfort is [[HeatherHasson]]'s product principle for joining the performance requirements of professional clothing with softness, mobility, fit, functional storage, and deliberate visual design.

## Current Synthesis
In the [[FIGS]] case, comfort is not merely softer fabric or looser clothing. It is a system built around the tasks and identity of healthcare work: stretch supports movement, pockets and tool storage support workflow, fit and layering support different bodies and conditions, and color and silhouette communicate that clinicians deserve thoughtfully designed apparel. The concept converts user respect into testable product attributes rather than leaving it as brand language alone.

## Key Claims
- Technical comfort integrates physical feel, movement, task function, fit, and visual design.
- Direct observation and use-context feedback are necessary because professional requirements are easy to miss in generic apparel design.
- Comfort and technical performance can reinforce rather than oppose willingness to pay when the product is mandatory and frequently used.
- A design principle becomes commercially meaningful only when manufacturing quality reproduces it consistently.

## Evidence
- **Use-context discovery:** [[figs-trina-spear-and-heather-hasson-the-2-5-billion-scrubs-brand-that-investors-said-would-never-work-vmp6448511514]] describes the founders asking clinicians about fit, pockets, tools, comfort, and existing-uniform failures outside emergency rooms.
- **Product expression:** [[figs-trina-spear-and-heather-hasson-the-2-5-billion-scrubs-brand-that-investors-said-would-never-work-vmp6448511514]] links the principle to proprietary fabric work, stretch, softness, colors, gender-specific cuts, slim pants, sleeveless tops, and underscrubs.
- **Manufacturing dependence:** [[figs-trina-spear-and-heather-hasson-the-2-5-billion-scrubs-brand-that-investors-said-would-never-work-vmp6448511514]] says the founders moved production among Los Angeles, China, and Vietnam while refining quality and fit.
- **Economic expression:** [[figs-trina-spear-and-heather-hasson-the-2-5-billion-scrubs-brand-that-investors-said-would-never-work-vmp6448511514]] reports higher production spending supported by direct distribution and a smaller relative price premium.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The phrase is company-originated and the source supplies no comparative wear test, clinical outcome evidence, return data, or independent material analysis. Which attributes improve comfort or performance will vary by role, body, climate, infection-control requirement, and workplace policy. Design appeal should not be treated as proof of technical superiority.

## What Changed
- Added technical comfort as a product-design system grounded in healthcare-worker tasks, bodies, and identity.
- Qualified the founder framing against the absence of independent performance evidence.
