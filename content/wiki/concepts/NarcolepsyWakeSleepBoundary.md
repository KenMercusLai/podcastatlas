# Narcolepsy Wake-Sleep Boundary

Narcolepsy wake-sleep boundary is the source's explanation of narcolepsy as a disorder where the boundary between sleep and waking is blurred. In [[wake-up-haul-an-ozempic-moment-for-the-brain-6a85798d1ad8716bb6078744]], the condition can involve sudden sleep and, in severe forms, cataplexy.

The concept matters because it gives [[OrexinAgonists]] their first grounded medical target. The episode says people with narcolepsy lack enough orexin molecules in the brain, and it uses [[EmmanuelMignot]]'s dog research to explain how orexin became central to the disease story.

## Key Claims
- Narcolepsy is presented as rare, affecting roughly one in 1,000 to one in 2,000 people worldwide in the source's account.
- Orexin biology turns narcolepsy from a symptom description into a drug-target story.
- [[Ovoperextone]] and company work by [[TakedaPharmaceutical]], [[Alkermes]], and [[EliLilly]] make the condition the first commercial test for the broader orexin category.
- Broader neuropsychiatric uses remain less grounded than narcolepsy in this source.
