# 前倨后恭 / Qianju Hougong Reversal

前倨后恭 / qianju hougong reversal is the status-reversal pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-33-lishi-diyi-shuangwen-nanzhujiao-suqin-lvebjyhvzctjgaevwwoddpvg9nr0]] draws from [[SuQin|苏秦]]'s homecoming. The same family that neglected him when he returned poor later treats him with fearful deference after he has rank, wealth, and public recognition from [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]] at [[Luoyi|洛阳 / 洛邑]].

The concept is not merely "people are hypocritical." The episode stresses that the sister-in-law's answer is bluntly structural: she changes because Su Qin is now high-status and rich. Kinship, in this reading, is affected by external rank and money, so family warmth cannot be treated as independent of social power.

[[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] supplies the earlier humiliation in detail, while [[Shiji|《史记》]] supplies the triumphant return. Together they turn Su Qin's biography into a reusable social-mobility scene: failure, intimate shame, self-rebuilding, public recognition, and the formerly contemptuous household forced to submit.

## Key Claims
- The same person can be socially reclassified when wealth and rank become visible.
- Close-family contempt can become the emotional fuel for [[FailureDrivenSelfStudy|failure-driven self-study]] because it is harder to dismiss than ordinary outside criticism.
- The reversal is pleasurable as narrative, but the episode treats it as a cold lesson about how status and money alter human relations.
- As an idiom source, the story is part of [[InspirationalNarrativeCommercialization|inspirational narrative commercialization]]: later readers can package the reversal as triumph while losing the source's unease about human feeling.
