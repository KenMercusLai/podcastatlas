# Prosperity Investing Trap / 景气度投资陷阱

## Definition
Prosperity Investing Trap is the failure mode where investors treat strong earnings growth, accelerating fundamentals, or positive surprises as sufficient, while the market is actually revising the company's terminal space, durability, or discount rate downward.

## Current Synthesis
The source uses "prosperity investing" to describe a focus on growth rate, marginal growth, and beats versus expectations. Its warning is that these signals can work only when they are aligned with a stable or improving terminal narrative.

This concept qualifies the wiki's growth-investing and earnings-acceleration branches. Growth is not rejected; it is made conditional on whether current price already embeds the good news and whether the terminal ceiling remains credible.

## Key Claims
- Earnings growth is not enough if the market lowers its view of the company's terminal profit or cash-flow ceiling.
- Marginal acceleration can be misleading when the base, cycle, or competitive environment is deteriorating.
- Positive earnings surprises help only if they change priced expectations more than they were already anticipated.
- Relative valuation can compress despite good current results when the terminal narrative weakens.
- The trap is avoided by comparing current price to implied narrative, not by reading growth in isolation.

## Evidence
- **Growth versus terminal ceiling:** [[caibao-de-gen-guzhi-de-jing-xushi-de-guoshi-lolxytqwzzxq9kzjbtkmfolafavb]] argues that faster near-term profit growth can still produce poor returns when terminal space is revised downward.
- **Three prosperity signals:** [[caibao-de-gen-guzhi-de-jing-xushi-de-guoshi-lolxytqwzzxq9kzjbtkmfolafavb]] identifies growth rate, marginal growth, and earnings surprise as the common prosperity-investing focus.
- **Valuation compression:** [[caibao-de-gen-guzhi-de-jing-xushi-de-guoshi-lolxytqwzzxq9kzjbtkmfolafavb]] links stock performance to changes in narrative rather than reported strength alone.
- **Reverse-expectation discipline:** [[caibao-de-gen-guzhi-de-jing-xushi-de-guoshi-lolxytqwzzxq9kzjbtkmfolafavb]] uses implied valuation thinking to ask whether good current numbers are already priced.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- Momentum and prosperity signals can matter when they reveal genuine terminal-space expansion or a durable expectation gap.
- The source does not reject growth investing; it rejects reading growth without price, expectation, and terminal-state context.
- Some cyclical industries may need separate cycle-specific treatment before terminal-value conclusions are drawn.
- The concept is a diagnosis of a failure mode, not a standalone screening model.

## What Changed
- Added a valuation-based qualification to growth and earnings-acceleration concepts.
- Separated current earnings strength from terminal-space narrative.
- Linked earnings surprises to priced expectations rather than treating them as inherently positive.
