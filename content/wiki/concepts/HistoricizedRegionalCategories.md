## Definition
Historicized regional categories treats names such as “East Asia,” “China,” center, and periphery as useful but historically produced classifications whose boundaries and internal meanings must remain open to inquiry.

## Current Synthesis
The episode does not propose abandoning regional terms. It asks how European history, colonial knowledge, language, religion, race, state administration, and later scholarship made particular groupings appear natural. “East Asia” may conventionally join China, Japan, and Korea while variably including Vietnam or Mongolia; that variation is evidence that geography alone does not settle the category.

Historicization also changes the meaning of China. A multilingual and multiethnic historical community cannot be reduced to Han language, Confucianism, or Jiangnan elite culture. The political implication is anti-centerist rather than a reversal of hierarchy: displacing Europe only to enthrone China would preserve the same explanatory structure.

## Key Claims
- Regional categories are historical tools rather than timeless natural containers.
- Category boundaries should be examined through the institutions and power relations that stabilized them.
- A useful category can be retained while its meaning is expanded and contested.
- China and Northeast China contain linguistic, ethnic, ecological, religious, and livelihood plurality.
- Decentering Europe should produce plural narratives rather than a replacement civilizational center.

## Evidence
- East Asia formation: [[cong-dongbei-dao-dongya-lishi-shi-yige-guocheng-ren-yeshi-lgolueqq4aiwj4bg5jk-im4qhzc5]] describes the region as a historical and colonial knowledge construction with variable membership.
- China plurality: [[cong-dongbei-dao-dongya-lishi-shi-yige-guocheng-ren-yeshi-lgolueqq4aiwj4bg5jk-im4qhzc5]] points to multiple Qing written languages and Northeast ecological and cultural diversity.
- Anti-centerism: [[cong-dongbei-dao-dongya-lishi-shi-yige-guocheng-ren-yeshi-lgolueqq4aiwj4bg5jk-im4qhzc5]] explicitly distinguishes opposing centerism from replacing Europe with China.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Historically made does not mean arbitrary or useless: categories can correspond to durable institutions, networks, languages, and political realities. Internal plurality also should not erase unequal power, conflict, coercion, or the practical need for bounded comparison.
