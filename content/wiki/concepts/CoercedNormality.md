# 正常性强制 / Coerced Normality

Coerced normality is the social pressure to appear useful, positive, orderly, and compatible with accepted life routes even when a person's inner life cannot honestly do so. [[33-renjian-shige-danxiaogui-de-zhuanglie-xianji-599924624]] builds the concept through [[ObaYozo|大庭叶藏]] in [[NoLongerHuman|《人间失格》]]: family meals, paternal expectations, school life, official ambition, collective living, and later domestic roles all feel less like neutral norms than demands to perform humanness.

The episode extends this from family to society. Its closing critique targets a culture that permits only "positive," "useful," and uplifting people, especially under larger political or historical narratives. In that environment, the weak or frightened person is not only unhappy; they are told that their unhappiness disqualifies them.

## Key Claims
- Normality can become coercive when it denies space for fear, awkwardness, sadness, refusal, or non-usefulness.
- The demand to appear positive can make suffering less speakable and therefore more isolating.
- Coerced normality explains why [[ComicMaskSurvival|comic masking]] can feel rational: the mask is a way to pass inside a social order that cannot receive the real self.
- The concept should not be used to romanticize self-destruction; it identifies one pressure that can make repair harder.
