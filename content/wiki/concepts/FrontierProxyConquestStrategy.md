## Definition

Frontier proxy conquest strategy is the use of key-node diagnosis, hostage princes, local royal legitimacy, rival local states, allied steppe or oasis powers, local supplies, and small Han detachments to defeat or replace frontier opponents without relying primarily on a large central expedition.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 743 supplies the operational precedent. [[BanChaoEasternHan|Ban Chao]] sees that Duti, the Qiuci-backed ruler installed over [[ShuleKingdom|Shule]], is not locally rooted. He arrests Duti through Tian Lu, gathers Shule officials, denounces [[QiuciKingdom|Qiuci]] interference, and installs Zhong from the previous Shule royal line. The key move is not direct annexation; it is a regime-restoration act that makes Han authority work through local legitimacy. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-705-3-gudai-tezhongbing-banchao-yige-ganyu-danqiangpima-shenru-dibei-de-chuanqi-lgmwbhpwxqqniawf-n6xuctqgqkc|Hanji 705-3]] later reinforces this Shule layer by emphasizing local resentment against Duti and Ban Chao's decision to spare him for later Qiuci diplomacy.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-750-jiemi-donghan-wanmei-nvxing-beihou-manshi-xisheng-lu-96lbdh7r0tsmiopbqqrj0q1gt|Hanji 750]] then states the strategy explicitly through Ban Chao's memorial. Ban Chao wants to restore Qiuci hostage prince Ba Ba as king, draw Western Regions states into the operation, use Shache and Shule's local resources for supply, exploit the weak local legitimacy of Qiuci-installed rulers in Gumo and Wensu, and coordinate with Wusun's cavalry strength. His explicit formula is "以夷攻夷."

Hanji 705-4 turns that formula into a broader strategic arc. It says Ban Chao identifies [[QiuciKingdom|Qiuci]] as the main Western Regions contradiction and [[YanqiKingdomLateHan|Yanqi]] as the other key problem, then connects local supply, allied troops, Wusun contact, the Shache campaign, the Yuezhi/Kushan supply-line defense, and Qiuci's surrender after Northern Xiongnu defeat. The source also shows a boundary of proxy conquest: victory over a distant power can require diplomatic repair rather than permanent escalation.

The concept is narrower than general frontier diplomacy. It focuses on coercive replacement and military leverage through local actors. Han power still matters, but in the sources it appears as authorization, arrest power, hostage kingship, envoy contact, and the limited force sent with [[XuGanEasternHan|Xu Gan]], not as a huge army that carries the whole campaign by itself.

## Key Claims

- Key-node diagnosis matters because local proxy strategy works best when it targets the polity whose alliance network holds the resistance together.
- Proxy strategy lowers central costs by turning local legitimacy and local supplies into operational assets.
- Locally rooted kingship can make outside intervention appear as restoration rather than annexation.
- Hostage princes can become instruments for replacing hostile frontier regimes.
- Rival oasis states and allied powers can be assembled into a campaign ecology.
- Small Han reinforcements can stabilize local operations when paired with capable frontier agents.
- Proxy conquest carries uncertainty because local states may defect, rebel, follow the stronger regional power, or require post-victory accommodation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The strategy is still risky. Hanji 743 shows a successful local-line installation, and Hanji 705-3 reinforces that event as a compact recap, but later Shule material records defection and renewed coercion around Wang Zhong. Hanji 750 is partly a proposal and early implementation layer. Hanji 705-4 compresses later success into a capstone story, but its Wusun and Kushan details remain source-scoped, and later Ren Shang material shows that Ban Chao's personal judgment did not automatically transfer to successors.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 743 as the operational precedent for local-legitimacy proxy replacement before Ban Chao's explicit Hanji 750 formula.
- Expanded the definition beyond hostage princes to include local royal-line restoration and legitimacy diagnosis.
- Added Hanji 705-4 as a capstone showing key-node targeting, local supply, allied forces, Shache deception, Yuezhi/Kushan accommodation, and Qiuci collapse as one strategy.
- Added Hanji 705-3 as corroboration for the Shule local-resentment and Duti-sparing layer.
