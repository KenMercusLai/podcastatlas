## Definition

Frontier proxy conquest strategy is the use of hostage princes, local royal legitimacy, rival local states, allied steppe or oasis powers, local supplies, and small Han detachments to defeat or replace frontier opponents without relying primarily on a large central expedition.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 743 supplies the operational precedent. [[BanChaoEasternHan|Ban Chao]] sees that Duti, the Qiuci-backed ruler installed over [[ShuleKingdom|Shule]], is not locally rooted. He arrests Duti through Tian Lu, gathers Shule officials, denounces [[QiuciKingdom|Qiuci]] interference, and installs Zhong from the previous Shule royal line. The key move is not direct annexation; it is a regime-restoration act that makes Han authority work through local legitimacy.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-750-jiemi-donghan-wanmei-nvxing-beihou-manshi-xisheng-lu-96lbdh7r0tsmiopbqqrj0q1gt|Hanji 750]] then states the strategy explicitly through Ban Chao's memorial. Ban Chao wants to restore Qiuci hostage prince Ba Ba as king, draw Western Regions states into the operation, use Shache and Shule's local resources for supply, exploit the weak local legitimacy of Qiuci-installed rulers in Gumo and Wensu, and coordinate with Wusun's cavalry strength. His explicit formula is "以夷攻夷."

The concept is narrower than general frontier diplomacy. It focuses on coercive replacement and military leverage through local actors. Han power still matters, but in the sources it appears as authorization, arrest power, hostage kingship, envoy contact, and the limited force sent with [[XuGanEasternHan|Xu Gan]], not as a huge army that carries the whole campaign by itself.

## Key Claims

- Proxy strategy lowers central costs by turning local legitimacy and local supplies into operational assets.
- Locally rooted kingship can make outside intervention appear as restoration rather than annexation.
- Hostage princes can become instruments for replacing hostile frontier regimes.
- Rival oasis states and allied powers can be assembled into a campaign ecology.
- Small Han reinforcements can stabilize local operations when paired with capable frontier agents.
- Proxy conquest carries uncertainty because local states may defect, rebel, or follow the stronger regional power.

## Evidence

- Local-line replacement: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-743-chuo-ren-xinwo-de-lishi-shunjian-mingdi-liuzhuang-de-wenqing-meng-lgwtuqtug5bh90qohwiskccx77hy|Hanji 743]] says Ban Chao removes Qiuci-backed Duti and installs Zhong from the previous Shule king's brother's line.
- Legitimacy diagnosis: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-743-chuo-ren-xinwo-de-lishi-shunjian-mingdi-liuzhuang-de-wenqing-meng-lgwtuqtug5bh90qohwiskccx77hy|Hanji 743]] says Duti is not from Shule, making local origin a decisive weakness.
- Authority without direct annexation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-743-chuo-ren-xinwo-de-lishi-shunjian-mingdi-liuzhuang-de-wenqing-meng-lgwtuqtug5bh90qohwiskccx77hy|Hanji 743]] has Ban Chao gather Shule officials, denounce Qiuci, and release Duti to signal Han grace and authority after restoring local kingship.
- Hostage kingship: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-750-jiemi-donghan-wanmei-nvxing-beihou-manshi-xisheng-lu-96lbdh7r0tsmiopbqqrj0q1gt|Hanji 750]] has Ban Chao propose making Qiuci hostage prince Ba Ba king of Qiuci.
- Local supply and legitimacy: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-750-jiemi-donghan-wanmei-nvxing-beihou-manshi-xisheng-lu-96lbdh7r0tsmiopbqqrj0q1gt|Hanji 750]] says Shache and Shule are fertile and can supply operations, while Qiuci-appointed Gumo and Wensu rulers lack local support.
- Limited Han reinforcement: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-750-jiemi-donghan-wanmei-nvxing-beihou-manshi-xisheng-lu-96lbdh7r0tsmiopbqqrj0q1gt|Hanji 750]] sends [[XuGanEasternHan|Xu Gan]] with about one thousand released prisoners and volunteer soldiers rather than a large expedition.
- Alliance extension: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-750-jiemi-donghan-wanmei-nvxing-beihou-manshi-xisheng-lu-96lbdh7r0tsmiopbqqrj0q1gt|Hanji 750]] says Ban Chao suggests using Wusun and that Zhangdi sends envoys to comfort Wusun.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The strategy is still risky. Hanji 743 shows a successful local-line installation, but later Shule material records defection and renewed coercion around Wang Zhong. Hanji 750 is partly a proposal and early implementation layer, so later success, failure, and costs must be checked against subsequent Western Regions episodes before the wiki treats proxy conquest as a completed or universally reliable model.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 743 as the operational precedent for local-legitimacy proxy replacement before Ban Chao's explicit Hanji 750 formula.
- Expanded the definition beyond hostage princes to include local royal-line restoration and legitimacy diagnosis.
