# Overextended Frontier Expedition / 边境远征过度

Overextended frontier expedition / 边境远征过度 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-909-2025-ruhe-yangcheng-yi-shen-guiqi-lomndmphrybunjbbbbn9ebafcsj|Hanji 909]]. The episode shows a court converting repeated [[Xianbei|鲜卑]] raids into a deep punitive campaign even though [[CaiYong|蔡邕]] warns that state capacity, border control, enemy strength, and internal order do not support the offensive.

The concept is not simple pacifism. It identifies a specific mismatch: a frontier threat is real, but the proposed campaign asks a weakened polity to project force far beyond its support conditions. In the source, [[XiaYuLateHan|夏育]], [[TianYanLateHan|田晏]], and [[ZangMinLateHan|臧旻]] each lead more than 10,000 cavalry more than 2,000 li into enemy territory, where [[TanshihuaiXianbei|檀石槐]] concentrates Xianbei forces and destroys the expedition.

## Key Claims

- A real raid problem can still be answered by the wrong scale of war if the state mistakes anger and face for capacity.
- Deep frontier expeditions require enemy assessment, supply, border discipline, intelligence control, and domestic stability; failure in any of these makes battlefield courage expensive.
- Patronage-driven command selection increases expedition risk when it bypasses sober military assessment.
- Strategic restraint is not identical to weakness; in this source it is the attempt to preserve resources until conditions support action.
- A failed punitive campaign can worsen the very frontier and fiscal pressure it meant to solve.
