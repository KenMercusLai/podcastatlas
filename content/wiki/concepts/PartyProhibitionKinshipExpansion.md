## Definition

Party prohibition kinship expansion / 党锢亲属牵连扩大 is the late-Han party-prohibition pattern where punishment moves from named party figures into kin, students, old subordinates, hosts, and other social ties.

## Current Synthesis

The current evidence now shows a pre-party-codification layer plus the later formal party-prohibition layers. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-887-dang-zhuduizhang-pengshang-zhuduiyou-jieguo-taican-lvyh9a-kvsdqaokmylgg139bvhfk|Hanji 887]] places the mechanism immediately after the 168 CE defeat of [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] and [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]]: punishment expands from coup actors into Dou-family kin, retainers, guests, close associates, Chen/Dou-linked students, followers, old subordinates, and recommended officials. This is not yet the whole 169 CE party-prohibition disaster, but it shows the same move from named political actors into relationship fields.

The later sources show formalization and intensification. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-893-rongyu-bang-ruhe-biancheng-siwang-mingdan-lmxzqoauf79-swwnk-0cevav0gq|Hanji 893]] gives the target-list layer: a士人 "贤达榜" and teacher-student reputation networks make people such as [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]] and [[FanPangLateHan|范滂]] visible to hostile power, while Li Ying's students and former subordinates are prohibited from office after his death. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-894-yi-chang-taowang-yinfa-de-da-tusha-lhulp9hqu07uayyfl3eg-xwrtkeu|Hanji 894]] gives the 169 CE terror layer: party accusation kills more than one hundred people, exiles wives and children, removes or punishes hundreds more, and lets private enemies use the party label against people with weak or no real connection to the accused. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-907-suoyou-de-bing-doushi-ni-de-xinbing-ltqzyohcauduwuanlvqph9l81d79|Hanji 907]] gives the later office-exclusion layer: after [[CaoLuanLateHan|曹鸾]] memorializes for party figures, [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] punishes Cao Luan and expands liability to students, followers, old subordinates, fathers, sons, brothers, and eventually relatives within five degrees of mourning.

The concept's institutional point is that political punishment moves from named actors into social networks. Family, study, service, patronage, and shelter ties become administratively legible evidence, so the policy blocks士人 advancement and converts ordinary relation into a liability field. It is a late-Han party-prohibition form of [[CollateralPunishmentAndGuiltByAssociation|collateral punishment and guilt by association]], though its mechanisms range from killing and exile in Hanji 894 to office exclusion in Hanji 907.

## Key Claims

- Political repression can expand by turning studentship, patronage, and kinship into disqualifying evidence.
- The 168 CE post-coup purge shows the relationship-expansion mechanism before the 169 CE party-prohibition disaster names it more broadly.
- Public reputation lists make repression easier when they preselect visible targets.
- The party label can become a tool for private vengeance once authorities reward accusation and local offices follow the purge logic.
- Kinship categories make repression administratively legible, but they also punish people who are not accused of the original act.
- The episode frame ties widened party prohibition to stronger士人 resentment before later anti-eunuch retaliation.
- Later narrowing or amnesty should be read against this earlier expansion rather than as isolated mercy.

## Evidence

- Post-coup relationship punishment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-887-dang-zhuduizhang-pengshang-zhuduiyou-jieguo-taican-lvyh9a-kvsdqaokmylgg139bvhfk|Hanji 887]] says punishment after Dou Wu and Chen Fan's defeat expands to Dou-family kin, retainers, guests, close associates, and officials, students, followers, and old subordinates attached to the two men.
- Target-list prelude: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-893-rongyu-bang-ruhe-biancheng-siwang-mingdan-lmxzqoauf79-swwnk-0cevav0gq|Hanji 893]] says a士人 honor list becomes a death list once eunuch power treats public reputation as a threat.
- Student and subordinate liability: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-893-rongyu-bang-ruhe-biancheng-siwang-mingdan-lmxzqoauf79-swwnk-0cevav0gq|Hanji 893]] says Li Ying's students and former subordinates are prohibited from office after his imprisonment and death.
- 169 CE terror layer: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-894-yi-chang-taowang-yinfa-de-da-tusha-lhulp9hqu07uayyfl3eg-xwrtkeu|Hanji 894]] says the party case kills more than one hundred people, exiles wives and children, and leaves six or seven hundred people executed, exiled, or removed.
- Accusation spillover: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-894-yi-chang-taowang-yinfa-de-da-tusha-lhulp9hqu07uayyfl3eg-xwrtkeu|Hanji 894]] says eunuchs treat respected scholars and moral public figures as party people, while private enemies compete to frame others through old grudges.
- Later formal expansion: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-907-suoyou-de-bing-doushi-ni-de-xinbing-ltqzyohcauduwuanlvqph9l81d79|Hanji 907]] says Cao Luan's corrective memorial leads to his death and the expansion of prohibition to students, old subordinates, close kin, and five-degree relatives.
- Repair contrast: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-907-suoyou-de-bing-doushi-ni-de-xinbing-ltqzyohcauduwuanlvqph9l81d79|Hanji 907]] points forward to later narrowing and amnesty concepts, showing why partial repair has to be read against the widened liability field.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

This concept covers the expansion mechanism, not every event in the party-prohibition disasters. Hanji 887 is a post-coup purge precursor, Hanji 893 emphasizes reputation-list and student-network exposure, Hanji 894 includes execution, exile, dismissal, household punishment, and local opportunism, and Hanji 907 focuses more narrowly on office exclusion through kinship and social relations. Later narrowing through [[PartyProhibitionKinshipNarrowing|党锢亲属牵连缩限]] does not equal full rehabilitation or safety.

## What Changed

- Hanji 887 adds a 168 CE post-coup precursor to the later party-prohibition expansion.
- The current synthesis now separates post-coup relationship punishment, target-list creation, terror punishment, and later formal kinship expansion.
