# 党锢亲属牵连扩大 / Party Prohibition Kinship Expansion

Party prohibition kinship expansion / 党锢亲属牵连扩大 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-907-suoyou-de-bing-doushi-ni-de-xinbing-ltqzyohcauduwuanlvqph9l81d79|Hanji 907]]. After [[CaoLuanLateHan|曹鸾]] memorializes for party figures, [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] not only punishes Cao Luan but orders a wider review of party figures' students, followers, old subordinates, fathers, sons, and brothers. The episode then says the prohibition expands again to relatives within five degrees of mourning.

The concept's institutional point is that political punishment moves from named actors into social networks. Students, old service relationships, and kin become career-disqualifying ties, so the policy blocks士人 advancement and converts family structure into a liability field. It is a late-Han party-prohibition form of [[CollateralPunishmentAndGuiltByAssociation|collateral punishment and guilt by association]], but narrower in mechanism: the penalty here is exclusion from office and political life rather than clan execution.

This concept is the negative counterpart to [[PartyProhibitionKinshipNarrowing|党锢亲属牵连缩限]]. Hanji 907 supplies the widening blast radius; Hanji 917 later shows a limited repair when [[HeHaiLateHan|何海]] persuades Emperor Ling to reduce liability for more distant kin. Neither step equals full [[PartyBanCrisisAmnesty|党锢危机赦免]] or later [[PartyProhibitionRehabilitation|党人翻案合法性]].

## Key Claims

- Political repression can expand by turning studentship, patronage, and kinship into disqualifying evidence.
- Kinship categories make repression administratively legible, but they also punish people who are not accused of the original act.
- The episode frames the widening party prohibition as a cause of stronger士人 resentment before later anti-eunuch retaliation.
- Later narrowing or amnesty should be read against this earlier expansion rather than as isolated mercy.
