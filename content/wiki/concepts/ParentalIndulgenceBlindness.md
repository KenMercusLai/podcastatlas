## Definition

溺爱不明 / parental indulgence blindness is the pattern where love for a child weakens judgment about the child's faults and turns protection into exemption from discipline, consequences, or social limits.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-392-1-hanwudi-shoule-meimei-de-qian-weihe-reng-yao-shasi-qinwaisheng-lhey4utgx3njqea-suiqohjybrpa|Hanji 392-1]] builds the concept from [[LongluePrincessWesternHan|隆虑公主]] and [[ZhaoPingjunWesternHan|赵平君]]. The episode opens with the Great Learning line that people may not know their own child's faults and Zhu Xi's gloss "溺爱者不明." It then reads Longlü Princess's payment for future pardon as protective love that may also have taught Zhao Pingjun that status and maternal planning could absorb consequences.

The source's educational point is not that parents should abandon children. It argues that children need boundaries, self-care capacity, and clear knowledge of non-negotiable limits if they are to live independently in society. The Zhao Pingjun case is extreme because the missing boundary becomes a capital crime and a public-law crisis.

## Key Claims

- Love can distort judgment when a parent cannot see or name a child's wrongdoing clearly.
- Protection becomes dangerous when it tries to buy exemption before wrongdoing has even occurred.
- Children need explicit limits, not only material shelter and emotional defense.
- The pattern can produce political consequences when the child belongs to a powerful family whose protection pressures public law.
- Family education and governance meet when private exemption makes public punishment necessary.

- Protective exemption: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-392-1-hanwudi-shoule-meimei-de-qian-weihe-reng-yao-shasi-qinwaisheng-lhey4utgx3njqea-suiqohjybrpa|Hanji 392-1]] says Longlü Princess pays money and asks Han Wudi to spare Zhao Pingjun if he later commits a capital offense.
- Boundary failure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-392-1-hanwudi-shoule-meimei-de-qian-weihe-reng-yao-shasi-qinwaisheng-lhey4utgx3njqea-suiqohjybrpa|Hanji 392-1]] links Zhao Pingjun's later arrogance and killing of an attendant to deficient upbringing and lack of constraint.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept is based on one short popular-history episode, not a developmental-psychology study. It should not be used to blame every parental effort to protect a child; the source criticizes protection that erases responsibility and fails to teach limits. The historical case is unusually severe because it involves royal privilege, homicide, and capital punishment.
