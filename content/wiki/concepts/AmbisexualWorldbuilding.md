# Ambisexual Worldbuilding / 双性世界建构

Ambisexual worldbuilding / 双性世界建构 is the episode's main gender thought experiment in [[36-heian-de-zuoshou-liufang-yu-ai-de-weilai-wangshi-605254522]]. Through [[UrsulaKLeGuin|Ursula K. Le Guin / 厄休拉·勒古恩]]'s [[TheLeftHandOfDarkness|《黑暗的左手》]], [[Gethen|格森星 / Gethen]] becomes a society where people do not live as permanently male or female, and where sexual role appears cyclically rather than as a fixed social baseline.

The concept's value is not novelty alone. The episode uses it to unsettle ordinary social defaults: who is assumed to protect, who is assumed to be weak, how family and childcare are imagined, how desire is categorized, and how language trains perception. [[GenlyAi|金利·艾 / Genly Ai]] cannot easily explain what "woman" means because the category has been stripped of its home-world assumptions.

## Key Claims
- Removing fixed male/female sorting makes many hidden social scripts visible.
- A science-fiction gender premise becomes analytically useful when it changes institutions, intimacy, language, and power rather than only character design.
- The Gethen case does not automatically solve gender injustice; it lets readers see which parts of gender are biological, social, political, or imagined.
- The concept connects speculative estrangement to modern questions about who carries bodily risk, caregiving cost, protection expectations, and public authority.
