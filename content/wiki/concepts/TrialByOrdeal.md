# Trial By Ordeal / 神裁法

Trial by ordeal is the religious-legal background for [[JudicialDuel|judicial duel]] in [[78-xifang-juedou-shi-chujian-ba-weile-rongyu-687731000]]. The episode describes ordeals such as hot iron, boiling water, and water tests as procedures built on the belief that God would reveal guilt, innocence, or truth through bodily danger.

The concept is useful because it shows how evidence, law, and morality can be organized by a cosmology rather than by modern proof standards. In this setting, a violent or painful test does not appear only as punishment; it appears as a way to make hidden truth visible.

## Key Claims
- Ordeal procedures make sense only inside a belief system where divine judgment is expected to intervene.
- The body becomes an evidence surface: injury, survival, victory, or failure is interpreted as truth.
- Judicial duel is more active than some ordeals because the parties can fight, but it remains bound to the same divine-judgment logic.
- The decline of ordeal logic marks a shift toward human evidence standards, court procedure, and state responsibility for judgment.
