# AI Shopping Demand Discovery / AI 购物需求发现

## Definition
AI shopping demand discovery is the use of recommendation, dialogue, virtual try-on, and merchant tools to surface or clarify purchase needs that a consumer has not translated into a conventional search query.

## Current Synthesis
The episode places AI inside a Double 11 attention problem: always-on discounts have weakened the distinctiveness of the annual shopping festival, so platforms are trying to create new discovery and conversion surfaces. Tmall's AI tools and AI-token category expand both consumer assistance and AI-product merchandising, while JD.com and Douyin emphasize merchant operations. The current limit is intent understanding: complex, professional, or underspecified purchases still favor search and human comparison.

## Key Claims
- AI can lower the effort of moving from an uncertain need to candidate products.
- Virtual try-on and recommendations address different stages of confidence and conversion.
- Merchant-side AI can matter even when consumers continue to use traditional search.
- Shopping-festival AI is partly an attention and demand-generation strategy, not only a service improvement.
- Complex or specialist purchases remain a weak point for current systems.

## Evidence
- Platform features: [[qianyu-jia-yingyuan-zhibo-tiyu-saishi-shangbannian-guonei-jinshi-xiaofeiliang-xiadie-sancheng-gkwridooqlkafaplwtreagx]] reports Tmall AI recommendations, virtual try-on, an assistant, and an AI-token category.
- Merchant operations: [[qianyu-jia-yingyuan-zhibo-tiyu-saishi-shangbannian-guonei-jinshi-xiaofeiliang-xiadie-sancheng-gkwridooqlkafaplwtreagx]] says JD.com and Douyin also highlighted AI support for routine merchant work.
- Adoption limit: [[qianyu-jia-yingyuan-zhibo-tiyu-saishi-shangbannian-guonei-jinshi-xiaofeiliang-xiadie-sancheng-gkwridooqlkafaplwtreagx]] says industry observers still find weak understanding of complex needs and specialist categories, while most consumers retain search habits.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode provides no controlled evidence of conversion, satisfaction, return rates, incremental demand, or merchant productivity. Festival novelty may produce trial without durable behavior, and recommendation incentives can diverge from user fit.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to distinguish demand discovery from transaction execution in agentic commerce.
