# Ceramic Livestream Commerce Risk / 陶瓷直播电商风险

Ceramic Livestream Commerce Risk / 陶瓷直播电商风险 is the trust problem opened by Jingdezhen's move into short-video and live-commerce channels. In [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]], livestreaming helped ceramic products reach larger markets after 2020, but it also made fake certificates, vague origin stories, exaggerated master claims, and mixed-quality goods easier to sell.

The concept matters because handmade ceramics are hard for buyers to evaluate remotely. The source connects this risk to [[JingpiaoCreativeCeramicEcology]], copyright enforcement, local market governance, and the broader need for [[DigitalCommerceLiteracy]] when craft, story, and platform traffic all shape perceived value.
