# Range-Extended EV / 增程式电动车

## Definition

Range-extended EV / 增程式电动车 is a vehicle architecture in which electric motors drive the wheels while an onboard combustion system generates electricity, allowing battery-led daily use with liquid-fuel backup for longer or less predictable travel.

## Current Synthesis

Across the sources, range extension is best understood as a product and infrastructure response rather than an immutable brand identity. [[LiXiangLiAuto|李想]] says [[LiAuto|理想汽车]] chose it when household travel, sparse charging, battery cost, and replenishment anxiety made pure EV ownership difficult; the goal was to preserve electric driving while preventing every family trip from revolving around a charger. Xiaomi and Huawei-backed examples show the same architecture later serving portfolio expansion, demand broadening, or repair.

The route is therefore contingent. Better batteries and charging can shrink the situations in which gasoline backup matters, while slowing category growth and copied positioning can reduce differentiation. Li's own framing reinforces this boundary: Li Auto's durable position is the family user, not range extension itself, and both range-extended and pure-electric vehicles belong to the electrification phase.

## Key Claims

- Range extension can preserve electric drive for ordinary use while adding fuel-backed resilience for long trips or charging gaps.
- The product value depends on household use, battery cost, charger density, route coverage, and price rather than on architecture alone.
- [[LiAuto|Li Auto]] used the route as part of a wider six-seat family-vehicle solution, not as the sole reason for purchase.
- Xiaomi and Huawei-backed brands show that range extension can also broaden a product portfolio or repair demand after pure-EV positioning.
- Infrastructure improvement and category competition can reduce the route's relative advantage, so timing and differentiation remain material.

## Evidence

- Family-use origin: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-sixiaoshi-malason-fangtan-lixiang-shoudu-gongkai-jiangshu-25-nian-chuangye-zhi-lu-lqzfpstn-stz-j4ysiqhokq6s228]] has Li connect range extension to sparse charging, battery and vehicle cost, long family trips, and the product definition of [[LiAutoONE|理想 ONE]].
- Portfolio expansion: [[mai-xiangzao-qijia-de-bainian-huawang-weishenme-chengle-bandaoti-zhizao-de-guanjiang-qiye-1004213131]] describes Xiaomi's large-battery route as pure-EV-like for many use cases with fuel backup at the edge, while noting weaker category growth in early 2026.
- Alliance and demand cases: [[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] describes the early [[AITO|问界]] M5 and a later [[Stelato|享界]] variant as two Huawei-backed uses of range extension.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The sources do not provide lifecycle emissions, fuel-consumption, battery-sizing, reliability, cost-of-ownership, or standardized comparative test data. Li's account explains Li Auto's choice retrospectively and does not independently establish that range extension was technically or commercially optimal. Reported category decline and later product plans are time-sensitive and source-scoped.
