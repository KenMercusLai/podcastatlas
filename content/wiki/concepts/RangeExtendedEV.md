# Range-Extended EV / 增程式电动车

Range-extended EV / 增程式电动车 appears in [[mai-xiangzao-qijia-de-bainian-huawang-weishenme-chengle-bandaoti-zhizao-de-guanjiang-qiye-1004213131]] through the side item on [[Xiaomi]]'s Pengcheng-series vehicles. The episode says [[LeiJun]] described Xiaomi's route as large-battery range extension, where many use cases can feel like pure EV driving while gasoline provides backup in edge cases.

The source treats the route as a product and demand-management choice. It says the Pengcheng series was initiated in early 2023, when Li Auto had opened market space with range-extended vehicles, but also notes that 2026 first-half wholesale volume for range-extended models was down year over year.

## Key Claims
- Range extension is positioned as a compromise between pure-EV experience and long-trip or edge-case anxiety.
- For Xiaomi, the episode frames the route partly as a way to broaden delivery and sales options rather than only as a technical preference.
- The category's attractiveness can change as market growth slows, making timing and product differentiation important.
