# Range-Extended EV / 增程式电动车

## Definition

Range-extended EV / 增程式电动车 is a vehicle architecture in which electric motors drive the wheels while an onboard combustion system generates electricity, allowing battery-led daily use with liquid-fuel backup for longer or less predictable travel.

## Current Synthesis

Across the sources, range extension is best understood as a product and infrastructure response rather than an immutable brand identity. The 2020 source frames it as a bridge for dense cities and households without fixed parking or dependable private charging. [[LiXiangLiAuto|李想]] later says [[LiAuto|理想汽车]] chose it when family travel, sparse charging, battery cost, and replenishment anxiety made pure EV ownership harder; the goal was to preserve electric drive while preventing every trip from revolving around a charger. Xiaomi and Huawei-backed examples show the same architecture later serving portfolio expansion, demand broadening, or repair.

The route is contingent. Fuel backup can preserve mobility when charging fails, but charge-depleted operation, added components, fuel use, cost, and maintenance can weaken the experience. Better batteries and [[EVChargingInfrastructure|charging]] can shrink the situations in which backup matters, while copied positioning can reduce differentiation. Li's own framing reinforces this boundary: the durable position is the user problem, not range extension itself.

## Key Claims

- Range extension can preserve electric drive for ordinary use while adding fuel-backed resilience for long trips or charging gaps.
- The product value depends on household use, parking, charger access, battery cost, route coverage, and price rather than architecture alone.
- The route can serve as a transition where private charging is structurally unavailable, not only where public fast charging is sparse.
- [[LiAuto|Li Auto]] used the route inside a wider six-seat family-vehicle solution rather than as the sole reason for purchase.
- Xiaomi and Huawei-backed brands show that range extension can broaden a portfolio or repair demand after pure-EV positioning.
- Infrastructure improvement and category competition can reduce the route's relative advantage, while depleted-battery performance and added complexity remain tradeoffs.

## Evidence

- Early transition frame: [[ep142-shizhijinri-ni-hai-kanbuqi-guochan-xinnengyuanche-ma-cmcoosidyoetcpz-zabeibq]] connects range extension to dense urban living, missing fixed parking, private-charger constraints, and concern about depleted-battery experience.
- Family-use origin: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-sixiaoshi-malason-fangtan-lixiang-shoudu-gongkai-jiangshu-25-nian-chuangye-zhi-lu-lqzfpstn-stz-j4ysiqhokq6s228]] has Li connect range extension to sparse charging, battery and vehicle cost, long family trips, and the product definition of [[LiAutoONE|理想 ONE]].
- Portfolio expansion: [[mai-xiangzao-qijia-de-bainian-huawang-weishenme-chengle-bandaoti-zhizao-de-guanjiang-qiye-1004213131]] describes Xiaomi's large-battery route as pure-EV-like for many uses with fuel backup at the edge, while noting weaker category growth in early 2026.
- Alliance and demand cases: [[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] describes the early [[AITO|问界]] M5 and a later [[Stelato|享界]] variant as Huawei-backed uses of range extension.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The sources do not provide lifecycle emissions, charge-depleted fuel consumption, reliability, maintenance, cost-of-ownership, battery-sizing, or standardized comparative test data. The 2020 “transition product” label does not prove the route will disappear on a fixed schedule. Li's account explains Li Auto's choice retrospectively and does not independently establish that it was technically or commercially optimal. Reported category decline and product plans are time-sensitive and source-scoped.
