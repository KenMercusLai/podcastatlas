# Money As Flow / 货币是流量

Money as flow / 货币是流量 is the core concept added by [[129-huobi-de-benzhi-yiji-huangjin-de-zhenzheng-jiazhi-chuantai-shifen-xiyin-lsjbfttqxf58uk-a4g8-srretkwb]]. [[ShiLei|时雷]] and [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] argue that money should be understood less as a stored object and more as a process of payment, clearing, credit creation, debt settlement, and network acceptance.

The source uses cowries, feiqian, jiaozi, goldsmith receipts, central-bank money, commercial-bank deposits, and cross-border payment rails to make the same point at different scales. A shell, gold coin, banknote, deposit balance, dollar, RMB, or Bitcoin only becomes money when others can use it to price goods, settle obligations, and trust that the network will continue accepting it.

## Key Claims
- Money's value comes from circulation through an economic and payment network, not only from material content, legal declaration, or scarcity.
- A single monetary object has little meaning outside the counterparties, goods, debts, and clearing paths that accept it.
- The flow view links [[EndogenousMoneyCreation]] to [[PaymentClearingNetwork]]: money appears and disappears through borrowing, payment, settlement, and repayment.
- Monetary confidence can break when users doubt either the issuer, the payment network, or the goods and balance sheets behind the currency.
- For investors, the flow view turns [[CurrencyRisk]] into a base-layer risk because the unit used to measure wealth is itself a changing network claim.
