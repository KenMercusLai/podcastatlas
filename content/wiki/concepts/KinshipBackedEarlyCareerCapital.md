## Definition
Kinship-backed early-career capital is the conversion of family support into education, peer access, reputation, and later outside finance before a person possesses office, territory, or an independent resource base.

## Current Synthesis
Hanji 1097 uses [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s youth to reject a purely solitary “grass-roots rise” story. After paternal death and household poverty, his mother's persistence and clan support make study under [[LuZhiLateHan|卢植]] possible; that school connects him to [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]], while sustained help from [[LiuYuanqiLateHan|刘元起]] keeps the path open. Later money from [[ZhangShipingLateHan|张世平]] and [[SuShuangLateHan|苏双]] helps turn social capacity into an initial following.

The mechanism is cumulative. Family resources do not directly create Liu Bei's later regime, and the source does not erase his own judgment or endurance. They lower entry barriers at successive stages: survival, schooling, peer formation, credibility, and mobilization. Merchant backing then extends a kinship-supported platform into a wider political network.

## Key Claims
- Apparent self-made mobility can rest on hidden family transfers made before public success.
- Education matters partly because it supplies teacher authority and durable peer relationships, not only formal learning.
- Long-duration small or repeated support can be more consequential than a single dramatic gift.
- Early merchant finance becomes usable because family, school, and reputation networks already make the recipient legible.
- The concept describes enabling conditions, not a deterministic guarantee of later political success.

- Sustained clan backing: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1097-cong-fanlu-shaonian-dao-liuhuangshu-ta-de-nixi-bi-ni-xiang-de-geng-hen-llhlpnoymxxf7y7_rkhtjb1jkkeg|Hanji 1097]] presents [[LiuYuanqiLateHan|刘元起]] as supporting Liu Bei from youth into adulthood.
- Network conversion: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1097-cong-fanlu-shaonian-dao-liuhuangshu-ta-de-nixi-bi-ni-xiang-de-geng-hen-llhlpnoymxxf7y7_rkhtjb1jkkeg|Hanji 1097]] links Lu Zhi's school to [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] and later merchant money from [[ZhangShipingLateHan|张世平]] and [[SuShuangLateHan|苏双]] to follower formation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The current evidence is a single podcast reconstruction. It does not quantify the support, prove that Liu Yuanqi's aid continued in an unchanged form, or establish that schooling caused Liu Bei's later success. The host's “angel investor” and “3F” language is a modern analogy that usefully identifies early risk-bearing but can blur differences between clan obligation, friendship, commerce, and formal investment.
