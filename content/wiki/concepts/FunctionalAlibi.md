# Functional Alibi

Functional alibi is the [[ferrari-1]] idea that engineering, heritage, craftsmanship, and performance can justify a purchase whose deeper demand is emotional, social, or identity-based. A [[Ferrari]] buyer may talk about power, handling, racing history, or materials while also buying passion, status, community, and the feeling of being alive.

The concept does not mean the functional claims are fake. In the Ferrari case, racing proof, [[Pininfarina]] design, [[MakeToOrderLuxuryManufacturing]], and [[FerrariClassiche]] service make the emotional purchase more credible because the object has real technical and historical substance.

## Key Claims
- Luxury willingness to pay is stronger when symbolic meaning is backed by material proof.
- The alibi must remain credible: poor product quality or hollow heritage can expose the purchase as pure status signaling.
- Functional alibis help explain why obsolete or nonessential categories can still command high prices when they carry trust, craft, and identity.
