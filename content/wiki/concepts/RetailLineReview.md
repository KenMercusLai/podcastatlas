# Retail Line Review

Retail line review is the big-box buying process described in [[who-decides-what-big-box-sells-our-game-got-us-answers]], where retailers decide what products stay, leave, or get added for a coming season. The source says the process happens twice a year and places [[SellMeASasquatch]] in a compressed pitch environment where [[ErikaBeras]] may have only a few minutes to make the case.

The concept is a channel gate, not a normal customer interview. In the episode, [[ExplodingKittens]] prepares multiple prototype games, the retailer's name is withheld as sensitive, and the buyer response focuses on audience, player count, two-player fit, price point, and the support [[PlanetMoney]] can bring after launch.

## Key Claims
- Line review turns product strategy into a short buyer-facing argument about audience, price, support, and shelf performance.
- Getting a meeting is not the same as getting distribution; [[MattAdelman]] describes a funnel where only a small share of submissions reach shelves.
- The buyer's job is comparative: a new product must justify displacing another product inside [[PlanogramGatekeeping]].
- Line review links [[RetailShelfAppeal]] to [[SalesVelocity]] because the product must first be accepted by buyers and later prove itself with shoppers.
