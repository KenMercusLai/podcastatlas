# Structure Over Prediction / 结构优先于预测

Structure over prediction is [[163-dang-guzhuyizhi-de-nianqingren-yushang-zhege-caotaibanzi-de-shijie-chuantai-qingdao-kuaima-lvxfvtz-wgwdl8mmtzyehwgwtbta]]'s core strategic answer to randomness: when the future cannot be forecast cleanly, the decision should be judged by whether its structure can survive multiple futures. [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] contrasts prediction-based decisions, such as a single interest-rate or market-direction call, with structure-based decisions that build cash, risk assets, optionality, and behavioral resilience into the same system.

The frame translates [[AssetAllocation]] into a broader life method. It does not deny macro, AI, class, or labor-market uncertainty; it asks whether the person has enough stable base, upside contact, liquidity, skill, relationships, and initiative to keep acting after the first forecast fails.

## Key Claims
- Randomness weakens prediction, but it does not make strategy meaningless.
- A structure can be good even if one component fails, because the point is survivability across scenarios rather than one correct call.
- [[BarbellStrategy]] is one structure-over-prediction implementation: preserve a safe side while exposing a small side to high upside or adaptation.
- [[BoundedSpeculativeOptionality]] turns a rescue fantasy into a priced and sized option-like sleeve.
- [[PortfolioSuitability]] decides whether the structure fits the holder's income, obligations, temperament, and human-capital risk.
- The life-design version asks whether a person can stay in the game through skills, relationships, health, cash, habits, and low-cost experiments.
- This frame resists both helpless determinism and all-in heroics.
