# Cognition As Fortune Root / 认知通达决定福禄

Cognition as fortune root / 认知通达决定福禄 is [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-161-caiyun-he-renji-guanxi-de-liangzi-jiuchan-4-lml7lpd3u9l7esryknmhoampgx6v|Hanji 161 part 4]]'s claim that wealth, poverty, health, and ease are shaped most deeply by cognition. The host explicitly downranks feng shui, astrology, cosmetic change, fortune-telling, and lucky objects as decisive explanations, arguing instead that通达的认知 is the root layer.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-920-mingwu-zhege-daoli-caineng-zhuan-daqian-lqmabg3bpyz6ct2v4arx-iun57ws|Hanji 920]] adds a narrower money-focused branch through [[WealthThroughDaoFrame|求财先明道]]. The source says money is visible while Dao is hidden, and that earning durable wealth requires seeing rules, trends, human feeling, value, humility, learning, and moral capacity. [[FanLi|范蠡]] is used as the source's positive example because he reads pattern and human nature before rebuilding wealth.

This concept is the epistemic counterpart to [[FateChangeThroughVirtuePractice|积善修心式改命]]. Fate can change only if the person can perceive differently, abandon old thought constraints, and act from a clearer model of causes, relationships, and conduct. In the source's vocabulary, cognition is not only information; it includes worldview, moral judgment, and whether a person hears a teaching and practices it.

The wiki keeps the stronger fortune claim source-scoped. The practical version is that cognition shapes decisions, relationships, risk reading, and opportunities; it should not be used as a total explanation for poverty, illness, or structural constraint.

## Key Claims
- External techniques and symbolic objects are not treated as the root of fortune.
- 通达的认知 means seeing causes, conduct, and consequences more clearly.
- The source links cognition to wealth but also to health, relationships, and personal peace.
- Old thought patterns can constrain fate when they prevent different action.
- Hearing a principle without practicing it is treated as incomplete cognition.
- Hanji 920 adds a narrower wealth-through-Dao formulation where clear cognition includes reading rules, trends, human nature, value, and moral limits.
