# Commercial Vehicle Industrial Base

Commercial vehicle industrial base is the truck, special-vehicle, component, worker, and supplier system that can remain after passenger-car and headquarters functions move elsewhere. [[chengshi-jiushi-zheyang-21-shiyan-yizuo-yin-qiche-erqi-you-bugan-bei-qiche-paoxia-de-chengshi-1001770066]] adds the concept through [[Shiyan]] after [[DongfengMotor|Dongfeng]] shifted its headquarters toward [[Wuhan]].

The source says Shiyan retained trucks, commercial vehicles, special vehicles, many auto workers, and thousands of suppliers. This base is valuable because it holds process knowledge and supply-chain density, but it is exposed to a different demand cycle from consumer cars: logistics, infrastructure, mining, construction, and real-estate activity all affect truck purchases.

The concept also sets up a technology distinction. Passenger EVs are not the only future of the auto industry; commercial vehicles may upgrade through [[CommercialVehicleEnergyTransition|new-energy commercial vehicles]], smart mining trucks, and [[ClosedSiteAutonomousDriving|closed-site autonomous driving]] before or alongside long-haul heavy-truck electrification.
