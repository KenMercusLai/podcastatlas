# Creator Fact-Checking Responsibility / 创作者事实核查责任

## Definition

Creator fact-checking responsibility is the duty of independent media creators to verify consequential claims, interpret sources in context, disclose uncertainty, and correct errors even when they lack a traditional newsroom's editors, legal review, and institutional standards.

## Current Synthesis

The current evidence spans financial and healthcare commentary, AI-assisted historical reporting, public correction, and live interviewing. [[141-jiageng-yinwei-boke-wo-shouyao-qu-gelunbiya-daxue-zuo-fangwenxuezhe-le-lluk9kruhveokkbmfo0adrj7etpj]] shows how persuasive but weak sourcing in a Japanese healthcare episode survived until [[QiongQiong|琼琼]] challenged it. [[147-zaitan-riben-yiliao-yu-zhaohu-hangye-zhi-wo-ceng-zai-beihaidao-de-yiyuan-dang-hushi-lgokb-l-nvnqsstfzlqwhtnkqzhp]] turns that correction into a public knowledge resource grounded in trained experience, system detail, and explicit limits.

[[ep264-tafang-tangshi-zhilu-shengtang-qixiang-shaonianxin-loo2dfuxffizpbl4si5nwcixnngi]] extends the responsibility to AI-assisted cultural reporting. Models can organize routes and materials but may invent books, scholars, phrasing, chronology, or geography, so primary texts, archaeology, field visits, expert work, and uncertainty remain necessary.

The live-interview boundary appears in [[the-joe-rogan-intervention-the-joe-rogan-intervention|The Joe Rogan Intervention]]. Rogan's team finds a relevant paper, but the episode argues that host and guest misread secondary bacterial pneumonia as evidence against influenza. Verification therefore includes semantic and causal interpretation: displaying a source is not enough if its conclusion is reversed or detached from the guest's claim.

## Key Claims

- The absence of newsroom infrastructure increases rather than removes a creator's personal verification burden.
- Persuasive delivery, platform scale, and real-time source retrieval do not establish that a claim is supported.
- Verification includes original-source context, terminology, chronology, data fit, causal structure, and whether the cited material entails the conclusion.
- Duties rise with the stakes of healthcare, politics, markets, public institutions, and historical reinterpretation.
- Listener or expert correction can become public knowledge when creators investigate it openly and revise the record.
- AI can assist orientation and organization but cannot inherit responsibility for hallucinations, source choice, or unresolved interpretation.
- Strong correction moves beyond admitting error toward better evidence, professional experience, and clearer boundaries.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

These sources establish responsibilities and failure modes rather than one universal newsroom procedure. Verification depth depends on stakes, format, time, access, and whether the work is reporting, commentary, conversation, or art. Professional experience can correct abstraction but remains partial evidence; field observation does not replace population data; and a public correction cannot erase every downstream effect. The Gladwell episode is openly adversarial and its interpretation should still be checked against the paper, interviews, and wider evidence.
