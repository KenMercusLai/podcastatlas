# Fieldnote To Public Writing / 田野笔记到公共写作

Fieldnote to public writing / 田野笔记到公共写作 names [[LiChuang|李闯]]'s record method in [[shan-shang-shan-xia-jie-renjian-wushi-xiaoyao-ji-shenxian-lhjiguq74flhatdxnbrnzhjg-acp]]. The episode says he first records events, people, speech, expression, and environment, then records his own response and judgment before reorganizing the material into readable stories.

The concept extends [[FieldworkAsKnowledgeMethod]] by emphasizing the conversion step. Raw proximity is not enough; a fieldworker or nonfiction writer has to preserve details, distinguish observation from interpretation, and later decide which fragments can become public without erasing their source conditions. This also connects to [[PersonalArchiveAsHistory]] because diaries, hospital notes, and Wudang records become evidence only when kept and sorted over time.

## Key Claims
- Fieldnotes should preserve both observed detail and the observer's later interpretation, while keeping the difference visible.
- Writing is not separate from fieldwork; it is where field contact becomes shareable knowledge.
- Repeated daily notes can turn ordinary settings such as a shop, mountain, or hospital into an archive.
