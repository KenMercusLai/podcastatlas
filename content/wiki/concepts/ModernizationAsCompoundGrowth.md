# Modernization As Compound Growth / 复合增长式现代化

Modernization as compound growth is [[150-zhongguo-ruhe-tiaochu-pinkun-xianjing-xiandaihua-zhi-lu-you-hui-zouxiang-hefang-lgpvp04rngagkpubte2ac6i5ji3g]]'s definition of modernity through [[LiLu|李璐]]: a society escapes the [[MalthusianTrap]] when productivity, market exchange, technology, and organization enter a self-reinforcing growth path. The source contrasts this with agricultural civilization, where improvements tend to be eaten by resource and population pressure.

The concept is not only a GDP claim. The episode presents compound growth as a civilizational regime created by the [[FreeMarketScientificLoop]]: free markets create demand and allocation, science and technology create new productive possibility, and industrial systems turn knowledge into scalable goods.

## Key Claims
- Modernization means sustained compounding, not only higher output in one period.
- The source links economic growth to science, market institutions, and industrial translation.
- The same frame makes long-term investing partly a judgment about whether a country and its firms remain inside a compounding modernization path.
