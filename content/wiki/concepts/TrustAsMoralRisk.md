# 信赖作为道德风险 / Trust As Moral Risk

Trust as moral risk is the episode's nameable problem around [[YoshikoNoLongerHuman|豪子]] in [[NoLongerHuman|《人间失格》]]. In [[33-renjian-shige-danxiaogui-de-zhuanglie-xianji-599924624]], [[ObaYozo|大庭叶藏]] is moved by Yoshiko's unguarded trust, but after the ambiguous scene with the visiting merchant he asks whether trust itself is a sin.

The concept does not mean that the source blames the trusting person. The episode leans toward reading Yoshiko as harmed and Yozo as unable to protect or even ask clearly. The risk is moral and relational: if trust can be violated so easily, Yozo can no longer tell whether innocence is beautiful, dangerous, foolish, or complicit in its own injury.

## Key Claims
- Trust is not only a private feeling; it is a condition that lets people live without constant suspicion.
- A violation of trust can damage bystanders and relationships, not only the direct victim.
- Ambiguity can intensify harm when a person lacks the courage to ask, protect, or stay.
- The concept should be read with [[MoralSuspensionInArtReading]] so that attention to Yozo's collapse does not turn into blame toward Yoshiko.
