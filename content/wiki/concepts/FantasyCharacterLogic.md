## Definition

Fantasy character logic requires choices to remain credible relative to established knowledge, values, relationships, and opportunities even when physical rules are invented.

## Current Synthesis

The bounded [[AChineseGhostStory1987|《倩女幽魂》(1987)]] discussion distinguishes “物理设定” from “人情设定.” A fantasy can add rules explaining ghosts, sword magic, underworld travel, or daylight limits, but a new rule cannot alone explain why a demon hunter knowingly coexists with repeated predation or why protagonists suddenly gain unprepared capabilities.

Character logic and world rules interact but should not be collapsed. Yan Chixia's history, constraints, or incomplete knowledge could justify non-intervention, but the film must dramatize enough for that behavior to fit his hatred of evil. A climax likewise needs prior capability, tactical leverage, cost, or an explicit rule change.

## Key Claims

- Invented physics does not suspend credible motive and decision.
- Behavioral coherence depends on what a character knows, values, can do, and has tolerated.
- Explanations added after a problem appears are weaker when not dramatized.
- Power escalation becomes a causal problem when it changes prior capability without preparation.
- Emotional success can coexist with weak character logic and should be judged separately.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
