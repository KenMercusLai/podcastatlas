# Impact-Driven Work

## Definition
Impact-driven work treats durable satisfaction as a possible consequence of solving consequential problems for other people rather than as the direct pursuit of comfort, status, or consumption.

## Current Synthesis
In [[scim3646778859-scim3646778859]], [[DanielEk|Daniel Ek]] describes happiness as a trailing indicator of impact. His early financial success gave him consumption and freedom but not a durable reason to act; building became meaningful when it joined difficult problem-solving to service. This is a personal motivation model, not a requirement that everyone maximize scale or sacrifice rest. The same episode treats self-knowledge, energy, relationships, and recovery as necessary to selecting and sustaining the right game.

## Key Claims
- Money and status can remove constraints without supplying a durable purpose.
- A consequential unsolved problem can provide motivation when the builder sees whom the solution serves.
- Impact is broader than company size and can exist wherever work solves a real problem for someone.
- Mission can guide ownership decisions when a founder judges whether another steward could advance the work better.
- Durable contribution requires a game compatible with the person's motives, energy, and abilities.

## Evidence
- Post-exit emptiness - [[scim3646778859-scim3646778859]] records Ek describing depression and passivity after reaching his teenage wealth target early.
- Problem and service - [[scim3646778859-scim3646778859]] defines impact through solving a problem that matters to people or society.
- Mission stewardship - [[scim3646778859-scim3646778859]] says Spotify acquisition interest was evaluated by whether a buyer could carry the mission, not only by price.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This account is autobiographical and does not establish that impact causes happiness for everyone. “Impact” can rationalize overwork, founder grandiosity, or neglect of close relationships, and difficult social problems require evidence that intended benefits actually occur. Comfort, ordinary work, caregiving, and bounded contribution need not be treated as lesser lives.

## What Changed
- Created the concept from Ek's distinction among wealth, consumption, consequential problems, and durable satisfaction.
