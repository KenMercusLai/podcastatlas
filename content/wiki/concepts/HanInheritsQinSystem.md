# Han Inherits Qin System / 汉承秦制

Han inherits Qin system / 汉承秦制 enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-148-1-hanxin-yupan-le-zhanghan-de-yupan-lodhpuxpossjno87gorjkfrtitbe|Hanji 148-1]] as the episode's frame for [[HanXin|韩信]]'s "申军法" after he becomes [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s chief commander. The source keeps the point military and organizational: Liu Bang cannot reopen [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] only by having a clever route, because the army first needs discipline, incentives, and a stronger command order.

The episode links that reform to Qin's prior state-building. [[ShangYangReforms|商鞅变法]] and the [[MilitaryMeritRankSystem|军功爵制]] had made Qin armies unusually effective by tying reward, punishment, status, demobilization, office access, property, and identity to battlefield performance. In this source, Han Xin's value is partly that he can take those useful Qin military rules and apply them inside the Han force rather than treating Qin's institutions as something to discard wholesale after Qin's political collapse.

The concept is source-scoped. It does not claim that all Western Han institutions are already settled in this campaign; it records the episode's narrower claim that the Han victory begins when Liu Bang's side selectively inherits Qin's military-organizational advantages.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-176-shusuntong-ruhe-wei-dahan-zhiding-liyi-zhidu-loeoqccrfwbmtr634ukyau9xykqy|Hanji 176]] broadens the pattern from military organization into court ritual and official knowledge. The episode says [[ShusunTong|叔孙通]]'s Han ritual draws heavily on Qin-era titles, palace vocabulary, and procedures, and that ritual/law books later become difficult official knowledge. The concept therefore now names selective inheritance across army discipline, court ceremony, and administrative learning rather than a simple pro- or anti-Qin stance.

## Key Claims
- Anti-Qin politics does not require rejecting every Qin institution; useful command and incentive systems can be inherited by the successor regime.
- Han Xin's contribution is organizational as well as tactical: "申军法" makes the later route operation credible.
- [[MilitaryMeritRankSystem|军功爵制]] matters here as transferable military technology, not only as Qin domestic reform history.
- Institutional inheritance can begin in wartime practice before it becomes a formal imperial settlement.
- Hanji 176 adds that Han inheritance of Qin also operates through court ritual, titles, law/ritual books, and restricted official expertise.
