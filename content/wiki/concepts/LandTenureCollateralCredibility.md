# Land-Tenure Collateral Credibility

## Definition

Land-tenure collateral credibility is the degree to which lenders believe a land claim is clear, enforceable, transferable, and valuable enough to secure long-term credit.

## Current Synthesis

The Zimbabwe case separates issuing a deed from making it bankable. New documents may identify current holders, but lenders can still doubt claim priority and resale value while former owners' compensation remains unresolved. The problem affects black farmers on redistributed or public land, returning white operators, and joint ventures: control sufficient to farm today may still be too uncertain to support a loan repaid over many years.

Collateral credibility also depends on contract duration and trust. A farmer working under a five-to-seven-year joint venture may rationally avoid avocado, blueberry, or other investments whose returns mature slowly, especially when the source expects many partnerships to end early.

## Key Claims

- Formal title and lender-accepted collateral are related but not identical.
- Unresolved prior claims can weaken the priority, transferability, and resale value of new deeds.
- Unpaid compensation can remain a current credit constraint rather than only a historical grievance.
- Short or fragile operating agreements discourage investments with long payback periods.
- Alternative crop finance can bypass collateral for a season without repairing long-term tenure.

## Evidence

- Deed-compensation gap: [[disorder-of-magnitude-frances-riots-454c603849c78e6a57bfa1c8bf33de7c]] says banks remain concerned about former-owner compensation despite a new title program.
- Payment gap: [[disorder-of-magnitude-frances-riots-454c603849c78e6a57bfa1c8bf33de7c]] reports that most of a nominal $3.5 billion compensation agreement remains unpaid.
- Investment horizon: [[disorder-of-magnitude-frances-riots-454c603849c78e6a57bfa1c8bf33de7c]] links mistrust and finite joint ventures to reluctance to plant long-maturing crops.
- Workaround boundary: [[disorder-of-magnitude-frances-riots-454c603849c78e6a57bfa1c8bf33de7c]] shows tobacco contract finance operating where conventional long-term lending remains scarce.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source does not analyze the deed instruments, claim-priority rules, foreclosure law, bank underwriting, land markets, compensation schedule, or realized loan decisions. Lender caution may reflect macroeconomic, currency, governance, crop-price, or enforcement risk as well as title. Clear title is therefore neither the only condition nor a guarantee of productive investment.

## What Changed

- Established the distinction between nominal deed issuance and lender-accepted collateral.
- Connected historical compensation, current claim priority, agreement duration, and long-horizon investment.
