## Definition

五均六管 / Five Equalizations and Six Controls is the [[XinDynasty|新朝]] economic-control program that [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-638-xuemai-qinqing-keyi-you-duo-wuqing-lv-gtknrhlipsmrtt3c4d7gcr-pc|Hanji 638]] introduces through 10 CE 五均 and 钱府 price-credit policy, and that [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-653-ba-shehuizhuyi-daihui-gudai-wangmang-youduo-shen-lo7fqi-zlujpd3cymg7uxsujy4yk|Hanji 653]] later presents as [[WangMang|王莽]]'s fuller 17 CE attempt to regulate prices, provide official credit, and put key resources and commodities under state control.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 638 now gives the concept an earlier 10 CE setup. It describes officials in Chang'an, Luoyang, Handan, Linzi, Wan, and Chengdu setting graded prices, buying unsold goods at cost after verification, selling reserves when prices rise, allowing free exchange when prices fall, and lending through 钱府 at a stated interest rate. The source's policy warning is already institutional: price-setting, purchase verification, reserve sale, and lending put officials in a position to both regulate and participate in the market.

Hanji 653 then treats 五均六管 as the clearest fiscal and market-control layer inside [[WangMangReforms|王莽改制]]. Its language is explicitly retro-classical: Liu Xin names the program through ancient texts, and Wang Mang's court wraps economic intervention in the authority of earlier ritual and administrative models.

The five equalizations are presented as price regulation for mountains-and-marshes resources, salt, iron, money, and cloth. The state sets official prices, intervenes when market prices rise above them, allows private exchange below the official level, and offers loans to people with transaction difficulties. The six controls extend the same logic into state management of mountains-and-marshes resources, salt, iron, money, cloth, and alcohol.

The source's synthesis is not that state price control or anti-usury policy is inherently impossible. Its claim is narrower and more institutional: a system meant to restrain wealthy merchants and protect farmers is executed largely through wealthy merchants and local officials, creating direct capture risk. Once operators collude, falsify accounts, and extract private gains, the reform fails fiscally and socially at the same time.

The episode therefore makes 五均六管 a bridge between policy design and revolt. The policy's proclaimed livelihood aims collide with harsh prohibitions, slave taxes, corvee, disaster, legal delay, and property seizure; later armed groups become intelligible not as isolated criminality but as survival responses inside overbearing and predatory governance.

## Key Claims

- 五均六管 joins price regulation, official credit, resource monopoly, commodity monopoly, and tax control into one state-led economic program.
- Its legitimacy depends on [[TuoguReformPolitics|托古改制]]: ancient textual naming makes a new intervention look like restoration of proper order.
- The stated goal is livelihood protection against merchant extraction and high-interest lending.
- Implementation is captured because wealthy merchants and local officials administer the system they are supposed to restrain.
- Fiscal failure and social failure reinforce each other: the treasury is not strengthened, while commoners face more predation, legal risk, taxes, and survival pressure.
- The program extends [[IdeologicalOvercontrol|意识形态过度控制]] into markets because morally attractive order is imposed without matching feedback, monitoring, or administrative capacity.
- Hanji 638 shows the capture risk already at the 五均/钱府 design stage: the same officials can set prices, certify costs, buy, sell, and lend.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The sources do not provide a technical institutional history of every office, rate, product category, or regional implementation channel. Hanji 638 introduces 五均/钱府 design and the host's regulator-participant warning, while Hanji 653 supplies the fuller 五均六管 capture-and-breakdown layer; the wiki keeps the exact relationship between the 10 CE setup and later 17 CE presentation source-scoped. The sources also do not prove that all later rebellion derived only from 五均六管; later wiki pages add private-coinage punishment, famine relief corruption, official-army abuse, and command failure as separate layers. The "socialism" framing remains a modern analogy used by the Hanji 653 title, not the wiki's analytical category.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 638 as the earlier 10 CE 五均/钱府 setup before Hanji 653's fuller 五均六管 account.
- Sharpened the implementation-capture claim: even the design stage gives officials combined pricing, purchasing, selling, verification, and lending authority.
- Preserved Hanji 653 as the main evidence for merchant-official capture and downstream social breakdown.
