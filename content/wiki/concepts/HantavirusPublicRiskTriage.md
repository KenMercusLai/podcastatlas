# Hantavirus Public Risk Triage

## Definition
Hantavirus public risk triage is the source's framework for judging hantavirus news by outbreak setting, viral subtype, rodent reservoir, transmission route, geography, clinical syndrome, and information quality rather than by treating every cluster as a COVID-like mass-transmission threat.

## Current Synthesis
The VOL.205 [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode uses a cruise-ship cluster to separate serious local risk from generalized panic. [[ZihanDoctor|子涵医生]] explains that hantaviruses have long been studied, usually depend on rodent reservoirs and contaminated environments, and differ by subtype. Americas-associated disease is framed through [[HantavirusPulmonarySyndromeEscalation]], while China and Eurasia are linked more strongly to [[HemorrhagicFeverRenalSyndromeEscalation]]. The concept therefore turns "is this scary?" into "what subtype, exposure, route, syndrome, and source of information are we dealing with?"

## Key Claims
- Hantavirus risk is real for exposed people in contaminated or enclosed environments, but that does not automatically imply broad population spread.
- Hantavirus is a studied virus family rather than a newly appearing pathogen, so existing knowledge about reservoirs, routes, symptoms, and treatment support matters.
- Subtype and region change the public-health message: pulmonary syndrome and renal hemorrhagic fever have different warning signs and care concerns.
- Rodent exposure, contaminated dust, and cleanup behavior are more central to ordinary risk than casual contact with people.
- Public risk triage should combine prevention behavior, symptom escalation, and official information checking.

## Evidence
- Localized outbreak versus global threat: [[vol-205-xiangbi-hantanbingdu-ni-genggai-liaojie-hantan-hancheng-yaxing-chuxian-zhege-zhengzhuang-yiding-like-qu-yiyuan-lhze9i64h8ifso_on-1cmnskxtng]] describes the cruise-ship event as a serious cluster for people in a specific environment but low overall global risk.
- Subtype and syndrome distinction: [[vol-205-xiangbi-hantanbingdu-ni-genggai-liaojie-hantan-hancheng-yaxing-chuxian-zhege-zhengzhuang-yiding-like-qu-yiyuan-lhze9i64h8ifso_on-1cmnskxtng]] separates Americas-associated pulmonary syndrome from China-relevant hemorrhagic fever with renal syndrome.
- Reservoir and exposure pathway: [[vol-205-xiangbi-hantanbingdu-ni-genggai-liaojie-hantan-hancheng-yaxing-chuxian-zhege-zhengzhuang-yiding-like-qu-yiyuan-lhze9i64h8ifso_on-1cmnskxtng]] explains rodents as the main host and humans as accidental victims through contaminated dust, food, water, bites, scratches, or mucosal/skin contact.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The outbreak counts and WHO-event details are source-scoped to recording time and should be updated from official public-health sources if used operationally. The episode is public education, not individualized infectious-disease, travel, cleaning, vaccination, emergency, pulmonary, renal, or ICU guidance.
