# Opposition Party Hollowing

Opposition party hollowing is the pattern where a formal opposition party remains alive on paper while losing leadership credibility, voter support, and organizational substance. [[brave-new-whirl-turkeys-opposition-overhaul-6a69c7766043035f89eb097f]] uses the [[RepublicanPeoplesParty|CHP]] as the case after [[OzgurOzel]] is removed and many MPs defect to the [[NewPartyTurkey|New Party]].

The source's sharpest image is the remaining CHP as a "political zombie." Polling falls into single digits, the restored leadership is portrayed as easier for [[RecepTayyipErdogan]] to manage, and a new-member ceremony reportedly uses paid extras. Hollowing therefore works both institutionally and symbolically: the party still exists, but its visible life no longer proves electoral vitality.

## Key Claims
- A ruling party does not have to ban an opposition party if it can leave behind a weakened shell.
- Leadership reversal can split real supporters away from the formal organization.
- Symbolic staging can expose rather than repair a party's loss of organic support.
- Hollowing can trigger realignment when voters and MPs seek a new vehicle.
