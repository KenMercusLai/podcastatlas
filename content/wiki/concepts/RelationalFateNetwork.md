# Relational Fate Network / 关系命运网络

Relational fate network / 关系命运网络 is the Hanji 161 claim that personal fate is shaped inside a web of people rather than by a solitary will. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-161-caiyun-he-renji-guanxi-de-liangzi-jiuchan-1-lhkqrhbrejnhuxoouem8x8-1aujj]] says a person is entangled from birth with parents, partners, children, friends, enemies, and social circles; love, hurt, language, behavior, and repeated contact can all affect life direction.

The concept differs from ordinary networking advice. It is not only that useful contacts create opportunities. The source turns relationship into fate language: who gathers around someone, who blesses or resists them, who shares meals and habits, and who resonates emotionally are all treated as part of the conditions under which wealth, action, accident, and direction appear.

The wiki keeps the frame source-scoped because the episode's evidence is experiential and metaphorical. It is still useful as a relationship model: a person's closest circle can reflect and reinforce values, risk tolerance, mood, ambition, and practical opportunity.

## Key Claims
- A person's fate is treated as relationally distributed rather than privately owned.
- Close companions are diagnostic and causal in the episode: the people around someone reveal who they are and help shape who they become.
- Speech, attention, care, injury, and shared habit become fate-relevant actions, not merely emotional background.
- The frame connects spiritual vocabulary such as 因缘 and 共业 with social-science-adjacent ideas about peer effects and support networks.
