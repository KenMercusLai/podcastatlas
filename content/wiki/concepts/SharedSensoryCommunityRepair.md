# Shared Sensory Community Repair

## Definition
Shared sensory community repair is the use of a common meal, artwork, performance, or embodied ritual to reopen contact inside a divided group before agreement is achieved in words.

## Current Synthesis
[[BabettesFeast|《巴贝特之宴》]] supplies the episode's main case. A religious community initially treats taste and pleasure as spiritual threats, but Babette's meal gradually loosens suspicion and remembered grievance. The repair comes through shared presence and artistic generosity rather than argument. The source does not claim that food resolves structural conflict; it shows how an embodied experience can make reconciliation imaginable again.

## Key Claims
- Shared sensory experience can interrupt rehearsed hostility and restraint.
- Art and pleasure need not be enemies of spiritual seriousness.
- Repair can begin through coordinated presence before explicit agreement.
- Gift-giving may restore relation when it is received as participation rather than control.

## Evidence
### Babette's feast
- [[vol-353-qiuri-teji-pingguo-de-xiangwei-zhong-wo-jianjian-hunhun-yushui-gkwriaiohmv_a7ndcatastow]] describes a French meal gradually reconnecting a divided, ascetic religious community.

### Art beyond consumption
- [[vol-353-qiuri-teji-pingguo-de-xiangwei-zhong-wo-jianjian-hunhun-yushui-gkwriaiohmv_a7ndcatastow]] reads the feast as artistic, spiritual, and therapeutic rather than merely luxurious eating.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- A moving event does not by itself resolve material inequality, coercion, or repeated harm.
- Shared ritual can also enforce conformity; its effect depends on consent and power.
- The concept is currently grounded in one fictional case discussed by one episode.

## What Changed
- Created a bounded account of communal repair through shared sensory and artistic experience.
