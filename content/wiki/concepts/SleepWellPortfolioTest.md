# Sleep-Well Portfolio Test / 睡眠理论

The sleep-well portfolio test, or 睡眠理论, is [[vol-124-xinxi-guozai-hou-ruhe-baochi-lengjing-touzi-zhang-fupan-ltpmll0jmcw-dl0-32qesddwem4l]]'s practical risk-tolerance rule: hold only the amount of risky assets that still allows normal sleep, daily life, and judgment. If a portfolio makes the investor unable to sleep, the source says the investor should consider reducing exposure until they can sleep again.

The concept turns [[DrawdownPsychology]] into an allocation input. Risk tolerance is not only a questionnaire answer, expected-volatility number, or heroic claim during calm markets; it is revealed by whether a real person can live through the position's price path without losing decision quality.

[[135-hongguan-dashi-pinfa-qi-ruhe-baochi-dingli-touzi-zhang-2025-banniandu-fupan-lism1-w05rt4jdwnun3l8wscymlq]] adds a screen-time and action-frequency version. After a turbulent but profitable first half, [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] says the second-half plan is to reduce operations, check markets less often, and let the allocation anchor work, showing that sleep-well discipline can mean less attention rather than only lower nominal risk.

## Key Claims
- A profitable thesis can still be too large if it damages sleep and judgment.
- The suitable sleep level changes with experience, asset size, market cycles, and life stage.
- A portfolio built for life should not make investment the center of life.
- Sleep quality is a behavioral stop sign, not a prediction about whether the market will rebound.
- Episode 135 adds that market-checking frequency and urge-to-trade are part of whether the portfolio is actually livable.
