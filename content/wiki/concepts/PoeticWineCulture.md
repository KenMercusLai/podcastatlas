# Poetic Wine Culture / 诗酒文化

Poetic wine culture / 诗酒文化 is [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-131-3-liqingzhao-zhongnian-fuqi-libie-hejiu-jie-xiangsi-lnzxhrj2iulahccy2-pkm-wphdei|Qinji 131-3]]'s constructive branch of the wiki's alcohol material. Instead of treating drinking only as a source of disorder, the episode reads it as a medium for friendship, inspiration, self-disclosure, poetic occasion, longing, and grief.

The concept begins with social warmth and literary creation. [[LiBai|李白]] is used for the idea that drinking can ignite poetic force, while [[DuFu|杜甫]]'s 《饮中八仙歌》 shows alcohol becoming a technique for drawing social portraits in Chang'an. [[LiQingzhao|李清照]] then shifts the concept from Tang literati sociability into Song lyric subjectivity: wine becomes a way to write leisure sorrow, marital separation, widowhood, and national loss.

This concept stays tied to [[TemperateDrinkingSelfControl|节制饮酒与自我控制]]. The source does not say alcohol is automatically ennobling. It says wine can carry culture and emotion when the drinker does not lose clarity, judgment, or responsibility.

## Key Claims
- Wine and alcohol can function as literary occasion, emotional intensifier, and social medium.
- Poetic drinking is not only personal indulgence; it can make temperament and relationships visible.
- In Li Qingzhao's case, wine links life stages to changing emotional registers, from young leisure to late desolation.
- The constructive value of drinking depends on restraint; once control is lost, the same medium returns to the destructive patterns tracked by [[AlcoholAsPowerCatalyst|酒作为权力催化剂]].
