# Newtonian Alchemy Theology

Newtonian alchemy theology is the source's interpretation of why [[IsaacNewton|Isaac Newton / 牛顿]] secretly pursued alchemy. [[26-niudun-yu-weibi-zhizaozhe-keneng-shi-sineipu-de-yuanxing-580065317]] does not treat the alchemy thread mainly as greed or stupidity; it reads it as part of Newton's attempt to find a hidden divine vitality inside matter after mechanical philosophy seemed to explain more of the world through reason.

The concept extends [[AlchemyChemistryContinuity]] by adding an early-modern religious motive. Alchemy, chemistry, coinage, and law overlap in the source because making gold or silver was not only a speculative practice but also potentially close to illegal value creation and counterfeiting.
