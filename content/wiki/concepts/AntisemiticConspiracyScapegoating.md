# Antisemitic Conspiracy Scapegoating

## Definition

Antisemitic conspiracy scapegoating is the false attribution of diffuse social, economic, political, medical, or cultural harms to coordinated Jewish agency, turning complex systems and uncertainty into an identifiable human enemy.

## Current Synthesis

[[12-conspiracy-theories-glt7580472793]] traces a changing but connected repertoire from medieval blood libel and plague accusation through the *Protocols of the Elders of Zion*, the Dreyfus Affair, Nazi ideology, and modern claims involving finance, media, COVID-19, or George Soros. The targets and idioms change, but the mechanism is stable enough to compare: impersonal institutions or frightening events are personalized as secret Jewish control.

The episode emphasizes a historical shift in conspiratorial imagination from Jews as excluded outsiders to Jews as supposed ultimate insiders controlling money, information, and power. That contradiction is functional rather than evidentiary. The target can be described as alien, covertly assimilated, weak, or omnipotent according to the anxiety that needs explanation. Once political power adopts the story, false belief can become administrative classification, exclusion, dispossession, and mass violence.

## Key Claims

- Antisemitic conspiracy claims personalize complex systems by assigning them a coordinated Jewish controller.
- The tradition is adaptable enough to recast Jews as both alien outsiders and secretly dominant insiders.
- Blood libel shows how devotional, financial, and institutional incentives can stabilize a false accusation.
- Modern versions attach inherited motifs to finance, media, disease, technology, and named public figures.
- Nazi rule demonstrates that a conspiracy worldview can acquire state capacity and weaponize its false premises.
- Historical continuity does not make every form identical; religious, political, and racialized antisemitism require contextual distinction.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

This concept describes false identity-based collective blame; it does not prohibit evidence-bound criticism of a specific person, institution, financial practice, media organization, government, or policy. The episode is a compressed conversational survey and does not independently establish every claimed transmission route, institutional motive, or historical detail. [[RacialAntisemitism]] is a related but narrower modern pseudobiological recasting and should not be projected unchanged onto medieval religious accusations.
