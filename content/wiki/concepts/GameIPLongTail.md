# Game IP Long Tail

Game IP long tail is the capacity of a game brand, ruleset, memory, and rights bundle to keep generating revenue after the original product cycle has passed. [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]] adds the concept through [[LegendOfMir|《传奇》]], whose later mobile games, licensed variants, private-server descendants, and legal disputes outlived [[Shanda|盛大]]'s own period of dominance.

The concept is not pure nostalgia. The source shows that IP longevity depends on legal control, user memory, gameplay recognizability, distribution channels, and the ability to regularize or monetize derivative ecosystems. That is why [[Actozsoft]], [[WeMade]], [[ShandaGames|盛大游戏]], [[KaiyingNetwork]], and [[CenturyHuatong|世纪华通]] remain relevant long after the 2001-2004 breakout.
