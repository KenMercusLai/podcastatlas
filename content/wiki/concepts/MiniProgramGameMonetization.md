# Mini-Program Game Monetization / 小程序游戏变现

Mini-program game monetization is the revenue pattern where lightweight platform-embedded games convert traffic and casual play into advertising revenue, in-app purchases, or both. [[zhizhuxia-xinpian-naxia-jinban-guonei-piaofang-ai-moxing-baofa-jiagezhan-1004403588]] adds the concept through the source's claim that mini-program mobile games continued to lead growth while in-app purchase revenue approached 70% of the category.

The key shift is from ad inventory toward payment depth. When total game users barely grow, revenue growth depends more on monetization efficiency, repeat spending, item design, social loops, and whether casual mini-program users can be converted into paying players without losing the low-friction entry that made the format grow.

## Key Claims
- Mini-program games can grow inside large host platforms without requiring a full app-store download loop.
- In-app purchase growth suggests the format is no longer only an ad-supported casual-game channel.
- A stagnant user base makes ARPU, conversion, retention, and payer segmentation more important.
- The source links female-user share in WeChat mini-program games to a broader audience mix than some older mobile-game stereotypes.
- The model sits between casual discovery and [[FreeToPlayGameMonetization]], where free entry and paid depth have to coexist.
