# Podcast Production Workflow

Podcast production workflow is the practical system by which a show turns research, scripts, hosts, recording, editing, ads, and release cadence into a finished episode. [[fanwai-14-gen-lidan-liaoliao-boke-chuangzuo-ai-yu-zhongnian-972693552]] adds [[Banlatte|半拿铁]]'s version: the host leading an episode researches and writes the main transcript, while the other host may enter the recording without knowing the content and respond live.

This workflow makes a produced show feel conversational without requiring equal preparation from every speaker. [[LiDan|李诞]] reads the structure as lowering collaboration cost, because the prepared host carries the explanation and the other host supplies real-time reception, questions, and additions. Editing and re-recording then let the show keep both scripted clarity and conversational rhythm.

## Key Claims
- Heavy research and transcript preparation can coexist with live response.
- A single-lead workflow reduces cohost coordination cost when topics are research-heavy.
- The unprepared cohost can approximate listener curiosity and provide a natural comprehension check.
- Editing can preserve conversational texture while fixing timing, missing jokes, or later-realized additions.
- Low commercial "efficiency" may still be rational when a show treats deep story episodes as book-like creator work.
