# Embodied Identity Boundary / 身体身份边界

Embodied identity boundary is the source's question of where the self stops when body, prosthesis, machine interface, memory, place, and feeling no longer line up cleanly. [[166-youyou-changfeng-wenrou-de-huanxiang-zhi-ge-fuping-jiaolv-de-gouhe-948043059]] develops it through [[AStretchOfHighwayTwoLanesWide|《一段双车道公路》]], where Andy's new prosthetic arm also feels like a road in Colorado.

The episode's point is not only that Andy has phantom pain or a technological malfunction. Andy first mourns the discarded flesh arm, then mourns the removed chip that connected him to the road. The body boundary therefore shifts twice: from biological limb to prosthetic control, then from prosthetic control to an impossible relation with landscape and information.

This extends [[EmbodiedPersonhood]] by making embodiment technologically and geographically unstable. Personhood remains bodily, but the body is no longer only skin and muscle; it can include interface, sensation, hallucinated place, and a memory that the character chooses to mark permanently through a tattoo.

## Key Claims
- A prosthetic or interface can change identity by changing what a person can feel, control, and miss.
- The boundary between symptom and experience can be ethically important; removing the malfunction may also remove something the person values.
- Attachment does not always fit ordinary categories such as romance, friendship, kinship, or property.
- Embodiment can become distributed across device, nervous system, and imagined or real place.
