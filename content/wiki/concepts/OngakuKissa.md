# Ongaku Kissa

## Definition
Ongaku kissa are Japanese music cafes where drinking tea or coffee is paired with deliberate listening, usually through carefully maintained audio equipment and curated recorded music.

## Current Synthesis
The episode treats ongaku kissa as the historical source for contemporary listening bars. The form began in Japan in the 1920s with large gramophones, then became especially important after the Second World War when imported records and high-quality audio equipment were expensive. Its current significance in the wiki is as a model for making listening the main activity rather than background ambience.

## Key Claims
- Ongaku kissa turn recorded music into a primary venue experience instead of a secondary atmosphere.
- The model historically provided access to records and equipment that many individuals could not privately afford.
- Silence or restrained conversation can be part of the listening format because attention is treated as the scarce good.
- The concept helps explain why modern listening bars focus on speakers, amps, turntables, styluses, vinyl, and curation.
- As an export, ongaku kissa carry Japanese cultural cachet but are adapted differently across local nightlife scenes.

## Evidence
- Origin point: [[after-the-flood-nepals-ongoing-rescue-6a969d560cefab2730527078]] says the first ongaku kissa opened in Japan in the 1920s, with visitors drinking tea or coffee while listening to music on large gramophones.
- Postwar access function: [[after-the-flood-nepals-ongoing-rescue-6a969d560cefab2730527078]] says jazz cafes became popular after the Second World War because imported records and audio equipment were expensive.
- Listening discipline: [[after-the-flood-nepals-ongoing-rescue-6a969d560cefab2730527078]] says many Japanese music cafes ask visitors to remain silent.
- Equipment focus: [[after-the-flood-nepals-ongoing-rescue-6a969d560cefab2730527078]] describes close attention to speakers, amps, turntables, styluses, and other audio equipment.
- Export role: [[after-the-flood-nepals-ongoing-rescue-6a969d560cefab2730527078]] presents ongaku kissa as the inspiration behind listening bars spreading outside Japan.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source provides a concise cultural history rather than a full taxonomy of Japanese music cafes. It does not compare regional Japanese variants, ownership models, or customer testimony, so claims about atmosphere and silence should remain source-scoped.
