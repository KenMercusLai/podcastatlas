# Political Image Maintenance

Political image maintenance is the work of preserving public legitimacy through origin stories, visible role performance, media attention, and controlled rivalry. [[for-bucks-sake-the-rise-of-self-made-billionaires-6a61e076562e3ad886d54c95]] develops the idea comically through [[LarryTheCat]], whose life at [[Number10DowningStreet|10 Downing Street]] becomes a guide to political survival.

The source is not a literal theory of governing. Its useful contribution is the pattern: public figures can survive longer when they have a memorable backstory, a simple role that supporters can repeat, enough media presence to define themselves, and a willingness to contain challengers before rival symbols take over.

## Key Claims
- A backstory can make a public figure legible before performance is evaluated.
- Perception management can compensate for ambiguous or hard-to-measure job performance.
- Media attention can turn a marginal role into a durable public symbol.
- Rival symbols matter because attention and affection are scarce political resources.
- The comic form makes the concept lighter than [[PoliticalBrandLicensing]] or [[PoliticalInfluenceMonetization]], but it still belongs in the wiki's branch on public legitimacy and symbolic power.
