# OCD-Coded Order Pressure / 强迫症式秩序压力

## Definition
OCD-coded order pressure / 强迫症式秩序压力 is the source's comedy-panel frame for how intense order, cleanliness, symmetry, exact-number, and object-responsibility habits can organize daily life while also creating pressure for people nearby.

## Current Synthesis
[[lir8w53xewcmvbqrb1x1osairafg-lir8w53xewcmvbqrb1x1osairafg]] uses [[ShiLifen|史礼芬]] to show a nonclinical but vivid order system. He describes knowing where household objects belong, detecting moved shoes or chopsticks, using different clocks for different family timing needs, avoiding random debris, sorting puzzle pieces, deleting photos to a round number, preferring hard editions that can stand neatly, and feeling responsible for objects bearing his name.

The episode's useful contribution is relational rather than diagnostic. A person can do most of the cleaning and still change the atmosphere for a spouse, child, guest, or partner who feels watched, indebted, or constrained. [[LinJianxinStandup|林简欣]] supplies that counterpoint by saying a messier person may feel pressure even when the cleaner says nothing.

## Key Claims
- Order pressure in the source is broader than cleanliness; it includes symmetry, time, numbers, object placement, storage, and responsibility for things.
- The source treats these habits as internally coherent for Shi Lifen rather than as random quirks.
- Helpful cleaning can still create relational pressure when others feel they must avoid creating work or disorder.
- Public-touch avoidance can escalate as people search for less-contaminated contact points, turning hygiene into a moving strategy.
- The episode keeps the pattern in comedy and self-report, so it should not be used to diagnose clinical OCD.

## Evidence
- Order system - [[lir8w53xewcmvbqrb1x1osairafg-lir8w53xewcmvbqrb1x1osairafg]] has Shi Lifen describe exact household storage, object-angle detection, clock offsets, and round-number preferences.
- Relational pressure - [[lir8w53xewcmvbqrb1x1osairafg-lir8w53xewcmvbqrb1x1osairafg]] has Luo Yonghao and Lin Jianxin ask whether silent cleaning makes family members tense or indebted.
- Public-space strategy - [[lir8w53xewcmvbqrb1x1osairafg-lir8w53xewcmvbqrb1x1osairafg]] discusses door handles, subway or airport handholds, and the escalation of touching supposedly cleaner locations.
- Object responsibility - [[lir8w53xewcmvbqrb1x1osairafg-lir8w53xewcmvbqrb1x1osairafg]] has Shi Lifen describe keeping named place cards because he feels responsible for objects connected to him.
- Diagnostic boundary - [[lir8w53xewcmvbqrb1x1osairafg-lir8w53xewcmvbqrb1x1osairafg]] is a comedy discussion and does not provide clinical assessment, impairment evaluation, or treatment guidance.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This concept is deliberately source-scoped and should not replace [[ObsessiveCompulsiveDisorder]]. The source uses "强迫症" in a public conversation about habits and relationships, while the clinical wiki concept requires intrusive obsessions, compulsions, distress, impairment, assessment, and treatment boundaries.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to keep the episode's order-and-relationship material separate from clinical OCD claims.
