# Perioperative Circadian Recovery

## Definition
Perioperative circadian recovery is the episode's frame for supporting surgical preparation and recovery through baseline fitness, sleep, circadian regularity, daylight, nighttime darkness, and gradual rebuilding.

## Current Synthesis
The Q&A episode answers a kidney-donation question by treating recovery as partly dependent on the state a person brings into surgery and the sensory environment around hospitalization. The advice is not to overtrain before the operation; it is to arrive rested, fit, metabolically stable, and on a normal rhythm, then protect day-night cues during recovery as much as the hospital setting allows.

The concept extends the wiki's sleep and muscle branches into surgical context. Prior strength and muscle size are framed as easier to regain than to build from scratch, while daylight, darkness, an eye mask, earplugs, and a window are practical supports for circadian continuity.

## Key Claims
- Surgical preparation should emphasize fitness, rest, and metabolic health without exhausting the patient.
- Cardiovascular training, strength training, adequate sleep, and normal circadian rhythm are presented as useful before and after surgery.
- Hospital recovery can be helped by daylight exposure and darkness at night when feasible.
- Eye masks, earplugs, and access to a window are practical ways to reduce hospital circadian disruption.
- Prior strength and muscle size may be easier to regain after surgery because of muscle memory.

## Evidence
- Preparation state - [[your-top-health-questions-answered-scim2882548864]] advises going into kidney-donation surgery fit, rested, and metabolically healthy without overtraining.
- Training and sleep - [[your-top-health-questions-answered-scim2882548864]] recommends cardiovascular training, strength training, adequate sleep, and maintaining a normal circadian rhythm around surgery.
- Hospital light environment - [[your-top-health-questions-answered-scim2882548864]] says daylight exposure, nighttime darkness, a window, an eye mask, and earplugs can help hospital recovery conditions.
- Rebuilding capacity - [[your-top-health-questions-answered-scim2882548864]] says prior strength and muscle size are usually easier to regain than first-time muscle building because of muscle memory.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is not a surgical clearance protocol. Organ donation, anesthesia, medication, wound healing, complications, and return-to-training timelines must be guided by the surgical team and the patient's medical context.
