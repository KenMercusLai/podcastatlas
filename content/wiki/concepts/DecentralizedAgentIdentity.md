# Decentralized Agent Identity / 去中心化智能体身份

Decentralized agent identity / 去中心化智能体身份 is [[jifa-dongwu-jingshen-chuangzao-gengduo-jihui-lndyfdmtgo-9l5cr8oy82t1xnlfe]]'s speculative AI-and-Bitcoin branch. [[ZhouLuohua|周洛华]] argues that future robots or intelligent agents may need identity, accounts, and reward functions, and that Bitcoin-like decentralized infrastructure could help prevent one central super-agent from controlling all others.

The claim is source-scoped and forward-looking. It does not settle whether [[Bitcoin]] can become everyday money or whether current language models possess real motivation. Instead, it links [[AgentPaymentInfrastructure]], identity, asset ownership, and AI motivation to the older monetary question of whether identity and property claims are unified or separated.

## Key Claims
- Current large language models are described as probability and imitation systems rather than fully motivated agents.
- More action-capable AI would need identity, reward, payment, and conflict-management layers.
- Bitcoin is imagined as a possible identity/account substrate for agents, not only a human investment asset.
- A decentralized identity layer is presented as a way to reduce conflicts among agents and prevent central control.
- The source's stronger Bitcoin projection remains a tension with existing cautious pages on [[DigitalGold]], [[BitcoinSafeHavenBehavior]], and [[CryptoPaymentPracticalityGap]].
