# Public Values Under Cost / 有代价时的公开价值检验

## Definition

Public values under cost is the principle that an institution's declared moral identity becomes credible only when it guides action despite political, bodily, reputational, or operational cost.

## Current Synthesis

The Baoxiang Kingdom discussion treats “救苦救难” as more than the pilgrimage project's public slogan. Guanyin argues that ignoring an abducted woman when rescue threatens relations with the star bureaucracy would empty that identity of meaning. Xuanzang's willingness to be transformed into a tiger and Li Changgeng's willingness to offend protected officials convert the claim into accountable action. The case also broadens the mission: real suffering outside the planned route can matter more than a formally completed project.

## Key Claims

- A value claim is weak evidence of institutional character until it constrains action under pressure.
- Unplanned cases test mission more sharply than staged or administratively convenient demonstrations.
- Costly action does not require purity of motive; personal debt, professional duty, empathy, and institutional interest can converge on the same justified intervention.
- A partial rescue can vindicate a value without proving that the surrounding institution is just.
- Encountering suffering outside a project's scope can expose a gap between successful delivery and the mission's deeper purpose.

## Evidence

### From public identity to costly action

- [[108-quhua-maboyong-taibai-jinxing-youdianfan-p4-shijian-duoshao-baihuaxiu-lt0tfpkc85hf4dpii7ntrevjtb9n]] presents Guanyin's refusal to claim “救苦救难” without rescuing Baihuaxiu, Xuanzang's bodily risk, and Li Changgeng's acceptance of institutional conflict.

### Mission beyond the planned route

- [[108-quhua-maboyong-taibai-jinxing-youdianfan-p4-shijian-duoshao-baihuaxiu-lt0tfpkc85hf4dpii7ntrevjtb9n]] ends with Xuanzang asking how many sufferers remain outside the pilgrimage itinerary and whether formal attainment can displace direct aid.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept does not make every costly act morally correct; cost can signal commitment while the goal or method remains harmful. Nor does one successful intervention prove durable institutional reform. In the source, Baihuaxiu is rescued, but the light punishment and dependence on hierarchy show that the system's value ordering remains compromised.

## What Changed

- Established a framework connecting public moral identity to action when compliance creates real cost.
