# Album as Integrated Work

## Definition
Album as integrated work is the view that a set of songs gains artistic meaning through sequence, contrast, accumulated structure, production coherence, and team control beyond the value of its most popular track.

## Current Synthesis
[[CuiJian|崔健]] argues that single-track circulation changes consumption without exhausting the album's artistic function. His mountain metaphor makes the hit or peak dependent on a broader base: an album demonstrates sustained seriousness and coordinated control even when listeners encounter it song by song.

## Key Claims
- Distribution format and artistic form are related but not identical.
- A peak song can depend on the contrast and support created by surrounding tracks.
- Album-scale work demonstrates range, sequencing, team effort, and production control.
- Preserving albums does not require denying the convenience or discovery value of singles.

## Evidence
- Mountain metaphor: [[cuijian-luoyonghao-qu-tiantang-he-qu-diyu-dou-yao-daishang-yishujia-ljzslbjyhppwf11pkwo009u2hxii]] presents the album as a mountain whose peak needs a base.
- Production seriousness: [[cuijian-luoyonghao-qu-tiantang-he-qu-diyu-dou-yao-daishang-yishujia-ljzslbjyhppwf11pkwo009u2hxii]] links albums to team work, mixing, monitoring, and comprehensive artistic control.
- Distribution qualification: [[cuijian-luoyonghao-qu-tiantang-he-qu-diyu-dou-yao-daishang-yishujia-ljzslbjyhppwf11pkwo009u2hxii]] accepts single-song circulation while rejecting the inference that it makes albums meaningless.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not establish that every album is coherent, that listeners must consume works sequentially, or that album production is superior to singles, EPs, playlists, live sets, or other forms. The claim is about retained expressive capacity, not mandatory format.

## What Changed
- Separated the decline of album-centered distribution from the continuing value of album-scale form.
- Added teamwork and production control to the account of why integrated works matter.
