# Album as Integrated Work

## Definition
Album as integrated work is the view that a set of songs gains artistic meaning through sequence, contrast, accumulated structure, production coherence, and team control beyond the value of its most popular track.

## Current Synthesis
[[CuiJian|崔健]] argues that single-track circulation changes consumption without exhausting the album's artistic function. His mountain metaphor makes the hit or peak dependent on a broader base: an album demonstrates sustained seriousness and coordinated control even when listeners encounter it song by song.

[[ZhengXingMusician|郑兴]] extends integration beyond track sequence. Movement joins songs written on trains, cars, and planes to the temporary-resident viewpoint of his first album, a train-carriage concert, landscape revealed outside the windows, and later tour concepts joining music, visuals, stage design, and set order. A work can therefore be integrated across sound, narrative position, place, and performance. Collaboration does not negate coherence: an artist may receive songs and production choices from others while using selection, interpretation, and touring to form a new whole.

## Key Claims
- Distribution format and artistic form are related but not identical.
- A peak song can depend on the contrast and support created by surrounding tracks.
- Album-scale work demonstrates range, sequencing, team effort, and production control.
- Preserving albums does not require denying the convenience or discovery value of singles.
- A unifying concept can extend from recorded tracks into venue, movement, visual design, set order, and audience position.
- Collaborative authorship can remain compatible with integration when selection, production, interpretation, and performance establish a coherent arc.

## Evidence
- Mountain metaphor: [[cuijian-luoyonghao-qu-tiantang-he-qu-diyu-dou-yao-daishang-yishujia-ljzslbjyhppwf11pkwo009u2hxii]] presents the album as a mountain whose peak needs a base.
- Production seriousness: [[cuijian-luoyonghao-qu-tiantang-he-qu-diyu-dou-yao-daishang-yishujia-ljzslbjyhppwf11pkwo009u2hxii]] links albums to team work, mixing, monitoring, and comprehensive artistic control.
- Distribution qualification: [[cuijian-luoyonghao-qu-tiantang-he-qu-diyu-dou-yao-daishang-yishujia-ljzslbjyhppwf11pkwo009u2hxii]] accepts single-song circulation while rejecting the inference that it makes albums meaningless.
- Movement across form: [[5f912c9a3957c933351ee56baa580685-5f912c9a3957c933351ee56baa580685]] connects a first album's travel perspective to songs written in motion and a Taipei-to-Yilan train concert.
- Album-to-tour coherence: [[5f912c9a3957c933351ee56baa580685-5f912c9a3957c933351ee56baa580685]] describes the “来这个星球一阵子” tour as an integrated design spanning music, visuals, staging, and set order.
- Collaborative integration: [[5f912c9a3957c933351ee56baa580685-5f912c9a3957c933351ee56baa580685]] describes an album partly written and produced by others that became more fully inhabited through touring.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The sources do not establish that every album is coherent, that listeners must consume works sequentially, or that album production is superior to singles, EPs, playlists, live sets, or other forms. Concept-led staging can also become decorative, and collaborative variety can resist integration rather than automatically produce it. The claim is about retained expressive capacity, not mandatory format.

## What Changed
- Extended album integration from sequencing and production into movement, venue, visual design, and touring.
- Added collaborative authorship as compatible with coherence when later interpretation forms a convincing whole.
