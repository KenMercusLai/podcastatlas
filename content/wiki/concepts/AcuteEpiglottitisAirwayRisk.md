# 急性会厌炎气道风险 / Acute Epiglottitis Airway Risk

## Definition
Acute epiglottitis airway risk is the possibility that inflammatory or allergic swelling around the epiglottis will narrow the upper airway and impair inhalation, sometimes requiring closely observed or emergency airway management.

## Current Synthesis
The epiglottis helps protect the airway during swallowing and lifts for breathing. Because its tissues can swell, inflammation above the vocal opening can become an airway problem rather than merely a painful-throat problem. The episode therefore treats severe pain around the larynx, pronounced foreign-body sensation, muffled or unclear speech, and breathing difficulty as reasons for prompt otolaryngology or emergency evaluation.

The source resists simple trigger stories. Spicy food, cold drinks, sleep loss, fatigue, allergy, and mucosal injury may contribute in particular contexts, but none establishes a universal causal chain. Once significant swelling is present, clinicians may use observation, oxygen, medication, temporary access such as cricothyroid puncture, or a surgical airway according to severity; those interventions are clinical decisions, not self-care instructions.

## Key Claims
- Epiglottic swelling can obstruct airflow near the vocal opening and may worsen rapidly.
- Laryngeal pain, muffled speech, strong foreign-body sensation, or breathing difficulty deserves prompt examination.
- Ordinary mouth or throat inspection may not adequately visualize the epiglottis.
- A food combination or a night of poor sleep should not be treated as a proven single cause.
- Serious cases may require monitored airway support or emergency airway access.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Most sore throats are not acute epiglottitis, and the episode does not establish the cause of the fatal news case it discusses. Its medication examples, observation periods, airway procedures, and individual outcomes are source-scoped. A person with breathing difficulty, cyanosis, inability to handle secretions, altered consciousness, or rapid deterioration needs emergency services rather than remote diagnosis.
