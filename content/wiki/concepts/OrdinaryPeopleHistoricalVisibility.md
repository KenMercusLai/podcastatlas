## Definition
Ordinary people historical visibility is the problem of how people without official rank, fame, or durable public archives become legible to later historians.

## Current Synthesis
The Fuyou Tiandi case makes visibility a source ecology problem. The [[DuFamilyGirls|杜氏女孩]] become visible because church literacy produced letters, the [[ParisForeignMissionsSociety|巴黎外方传教会]] preserved them, [[LiJiHistorian|李纪]] asked where 三台子 was and who the girls were, and later fieldwork connected archives to descendants, genealogy, and oral memory. Visibility here is not full recovery of inner life; it is a disciplined widening of what can be known from small traces.

## Key Claims
- Ordinary people often enter history through accidental or institutionally mediated traces rather than records made for their own self-representation.
- Visibility improves when historians combine letters, maps, family memory, genealogy, oral accounts, and field sites instead of relying on one artifact.
- Women's visibility can be especially fragile because naming, literacy, marriage, and family hierarchy filter what survives.
- A small source can be historically serious when it connects ordinary life to migration, religion, gender, and global institutions.
- The method must preserve uncertainty: making someone visible is not the same as pretending to know everything about them.

- Multi-source reconstruction claim: [[100-nian-qian-dongbei-nvhai-xie-gei-bali-de-xin-duitan-lishixuezhe-liji-putongren-ruhe-chuanguo-da-lishi-3d36f181-94ad-4883-8db3-5f040b5ed45b]] connects archives, field visits, family genealogy, descendants, and oral recollection.
- Gender fragility claim: [[100-nian-qian-dongbei-nvhai-xie-gei-bali-de-xin-duitan-lishixuezhe-liji-putongren-ruhe-chuanguo-da-lishi-3d36f181-94ad-4883-8db3-5f040b5ed45b]] says even the girls' father would have been hard to see in ordinary history, while daughters were still less likely to leave names.
- Scope-discipline claim: [[100-nian-qian-dongbei-nvhai-xie-gei-bali-de-xin-duitan-lishixuezhe-liji-putongren-ruhe-chuanguo-da-lishi-3d36f181-94ad-4883-8db3-5f040b5ed45b]] warns against romanticizing the letters and instead reads them through religious, gendered, and social context.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not claim that archives can fully recover the girls' inner lives. It also shows that visibility can depend on unequal institutions such as mission archives, so the surviving trace is never neutral.
