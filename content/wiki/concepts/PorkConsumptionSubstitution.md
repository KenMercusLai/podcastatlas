# Pork Consumption Substitution / 猪肉消费替代

## Definition
Pork consumption substitution is the shift of meat demand away from pork toward poultry, beef, seafood, aquatic products, or other foods.

## Current Synthesis
The source presents pork consumption substitution as a demand-side reason low pork prices have not automatically produced a sharp price rebound. High prices during African swine fever encouraged some consumers to try substitutes, while rising incomes and health preferences widened the acceptable protein basket. Pork remains important, but its share of meat consumption is no longer treated as fixed.

## Key Claims
- Pork's share in Chinese meat consumption has declined from the level seen around 2018.
- Household per-capita pork consumption has recently fallen for consecutive years in the source account.
- Poultry, beef, seafood, and aquatic products are no longer only occasional substitutes; they can become habitual alternatives.
- Health and diet preferences make some consumers less responsive to pork-price declines.
- Weaker demand growth makes [[PorkCycle|猪周期]] rebounds less forceful.

## Evidence
Consumption-share shift:
- [[zhurou-jiage-jingli-man-chang-xiadie-weishenme-zhuzhouqi-buzai-zunxun-laojiezou-1012895922]] says pork's share of meat consumption has fallen from roughly the low-sixties percentage range in 2018 to a lower share in the latest cited data.

Behavioral substitution:
- [[zhurou-jiage-jingli-man-chang-xiadie-weishenme-zhuzhouqi-buzai-zunxun-laojiezou-1012895922]] argues that high pork prices during African swine fever helped poultry and beef substitution become more durable.

Price-cycle effect:
- [[zhurou-jiage-jingli-man-chang-xiadie-weishenme-zhuzhouqi-buzai-zunxun-laojiezou-1012895922]] uses demand substitution to explain why lower supply alone may be insufficient to restore older rebound patterns.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not claim that pork has lost its central role in Chinese diets. It supports a relative-share and marginal-demand argument, not a replacement thesis.

## What Changed
- Created a demand-side concept for changing meat-consumption patterns in China.
- Connected food-category substitution to the altered pork-price cycle.
