## Definition
Blockchain consensus is the coordination mechanism by which people or machines that do not fully trust one another maintain a shared ledger.

## Current Synthesis
The current wiki evidence uses [[ChangpengZhao|CZ]]'s explanation to separate a proven base layer from slower application adoption. For CZ, the clearest consensus already achieved is not that every early blockchain use case arrived, but that [[Bitcoin]] did not disappear and is broadly treated as valuable and useful. Payments, property certification, and other imagined applications remain more uneven because technical performance, upgrade speed, existing payment quality, and implementation costs still matter.

## Key Claims
- Blockchain consensus is a trust-minimizing accounting arrangement, not simply a payment app or investment story.
- The persistence and valuation of [[Bitcoin]] are treated as a realized consensus even when broader applications lag.
- Slow protocol upgrades can preserve stability while also making capability improvement harder.
- Payment adoption and asset-certification use cases still depend on practical demand, existing alternatives, performance, and institutions outside the chain.

## Evidence
- Basic definition and Bitcoin durability: [[kangya-yingbian-yu-zhongsheng-dongli-cz-de-renshi-he-tianming-1736950d-420c-4021-9a56-a180810612d7]] has CZ define blockchain consensus as untrusted participants maintaining a ledger and identify Bitcoin's survival and value as the major realized agreement.
- Payment and certification limits: [[kangya-yingbian-yu-zhongsheng-dongli-cz-de-renshi-he-tianming-1736950d-420c-4021-9a56-a180810612d7]] says payment adoption has not reached early hopes partly because many domestic payment systems already work and most people do not often need cross-border payments.
- Performance and upgrade tradeoff: [[kangya-yingbian-yu-zhongsheng-dongli-cz-de-renshi-he-tianming-1736950d-420c-4021-9a56-a180810612d7]] notes that blockchain performance improvement remains slow and that Bitcoin's slow upgrade path is both a stability advantage and an implementation constraint.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The page currently rests on one explanatory interview. It captures CZ's framing of consensus and adoption rather than a technical consensus-protocol taxonomy or independent measure of blockchain utility.
