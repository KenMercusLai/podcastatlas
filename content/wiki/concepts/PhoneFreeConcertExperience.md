# Phone-Free Concert Experience

## Definition
Phone-free concert experience is a live-event design that restricts or discourages device use so performers and audiences share attention without routine filming, posting, or screen barriers.

## Current Synthesis
The source presents two enforcement levels. [[PhoebeBridgers]] and other artists use sealed [[Yondr]] pouches, while Harry Styles is described as making a softer request and supplying disposable cameras. Both approaches treat recording as a tradeoff: digital capture preserves and distributes the event, but can reduce direct attention, performer-audience intimacy, and the ephemerality that gives a live performance distinctive value.

The case extends [[ScreenTimeFriction]] from personal routines and retreats into collective event governance. Because the episode mostly voices artist preferences, the current synthesis remains qualified: phone restrictions may improve shared presence for some attendees while creating accessibility, emergency, coordination, enforcement, and consent costs for others.

## Key Claims
- Screens can become a visible barrier between performers and audiences.
- Restricting capture can make a performance feel more scarce and ephemeral.
- Physical pouches provide stronger enforcement than a verbal request.
- Disposable cameras preserve limited documentation without continuous smartphone access.
- Phone-free policy governs a collective norm, not only an individual's habit.
- Accessibility, safety, emergency communication, and audience preference remain underexamined tradeoffs.

## Evidence
- Enforced restriction: [[brazil-rut-lula-v-bolsonaro-again-6abb8653ed4e198024b38eb5]] says [[PhoebeBridgers]] uses sealed [[Yondr]] pouches on current and prior tours.
- Broader adoption: [[brazil-rut-lula-v-bolsonaro-again-6abb8653ed4e198024b38eb5]] names several other major artists using phone-free shows and contrasts them with a softer request-and-camera approach.
- Rationale: [[brazil-rut-lula-v-bolsonaro-again-6abb8653ed4e198024b38eb5]] reports artist concerns about screens, distance, reposting, and the loss of fleeting live-event value.
- Scope limit: [[brazil-rut-lula-v-bolsonaro-again-6abb8653ed4e198024b38eb5]] gives little attention to audience objections or practical disadvantages.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not compare attention, enjoyment, safety, accessibility, bootlegging, or satisfaction across phone-free and ordinary shows. Audience filming can support memory, disability needs, coordination, documentation, and cultural circulation. A ban may relocate distraction into pouch logistics or conflict rather than eliminate it.

## What Changed
- Created the concept as a collective live-event form of screen-time friction with explicit access and safety qualifications.
