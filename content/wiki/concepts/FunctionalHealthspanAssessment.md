# Functional Healthspan Assessment

## Definition
Functional healthspan assessment is the use of body-composition, bone, cardiorespiratory, strength, stability, and lived-function measures alongside biomarkers to evaluate whether a person is building the capacity required for later life.

## Current Synthesis
The source treats blood work as useful for silent disease risk but incomplete for healthspan. ApoB and other biomarkers can inform cardiovascular or metabolic prevention, yet they do not directly show whether a person has adequate bone density, lean mass, aerobic capacity, stability, or the ability to perform desired tasks.

The assessment layer therefore combines multiple kinds of evidence. DEXA can estimate bone mineral density, visceral fat, appendicular lean mass, and fat-free mass; CPET or field estimates can characterize VO2 max; strength and stability tests reveal performance; and emotional health still requires context that a laboratory panel cannot supply. Measurements are useful when they change a decision, establish a baseline, or evaluate an intervention—not simply because more testing feels more precise.

## Key Claims
- Biomarkers and functional measures answer different health questions and should not be treated as substitutes.
- DEXA is most useful here for bone, visceral-fat, and lean-mass context rather than one body-fat percentage.
- VO2 max, strength, and stability help quantify physical reserve that routine blood work cannot show.
- Bone and muscle assessment should connect to progressive loading and whole-body function rather than passive tracking alone.
- Testing frequency should follow the rate of change and whether a result will alter management.
- Emotional health and lived capability remain partly outside laboratory measurement.

## Evidence
- Biomarker boundary: [[exercise-nutrition-hormones-for-vitality-longevity-dr-peter-attia-scim2767035219]] says blood markers can inform lifespan risk but are less complete for physical and emotional healthspan.
- Functional battery: [[exercise-nutrition-hormones-for-vitality-longevity-dr-peter-attia-scim2767035219]] discusses DEXA, CPET, VO2 max, zone-two lactate, fat oxidation, strength, and stability as complementary measures.
- DEXA interpretation: [[exercise-nutrition-hormones-for-vitality-longevity-dr-peter-attia-scim2767035219]] prioritizes bone mineral density, visceral fat, appendicular lean mass, and fat-free mass over body-fat percentage alone.
- Testing purpose: [[exercise-nutrition-hormones-for-vitality-longevity-dr-peter-attia-scim2767035219]] ties repeated blood work to changing variables and monitored interventions rather than testing for its own sake.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not establish one required testing battery, interval, or cutoff for everyone. Radiation exposure, cost, access, measurement error, body size, modality, medications, pregnancy, disability, illness, training status, and clinical history affect interpretation. DEXA estimates are not direct measures of muscle quality, and fitness associations do not establish that every benchmark is a causal treatment target.

## What Changed
- Created a measurement framework that joins biomarkers to physical capability without collapsing healthspan into either one.
