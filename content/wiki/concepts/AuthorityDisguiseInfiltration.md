# 官威伪装渗透 / Authority Disguise Infiltration

## Definition
官威伪装渗透 / authority disguise infiltration is the deception pattern in which an operative passes through a hostile security perimeter by performing the role, confidence, and coercive habits of a recognized command authority.

## Current Synthesis
Hanji 1062 defines the current case through [[LiFuLateHan|李孚]] at the siege of [[YeLateHan|邺城]]. Because [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s moat and water isolation make ordinary communication impossible, [[YuanShang|袁尚]] needs someone to reach [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]] inside the city. Li Fu does not rely only on darkness or concealment. He makes Cao's guards believe he belongs to the command system by wearing the right headgear, staging inspection behavior, yelling at soldiers, and moving as if verification would be insolent.

The tactic exploits hierarchy. Guards who might question an unknown rider hesitate when the rider appears to hold authority over them, especially at dusk and amid camp routine. Li Fu's exit then shows the limitation of the method: once the entry identity is burned, he needs a different cover, so he escapes among old and weak people sent out under white flags.

## Key Claims
- Authority disguise works by making the target fear the cost of challenging a superior more than the risk of letting a stranger pass.
- Costume, props, timing, voice, and aggressive behavior reinforce one another; the deception is social performance, not only visual disguise.
- The method is strongest where routines are hierarchical and verification norms are weak under pressure.
- A successful entry can destroy the possibility of reusing the same identity for exit.
- The pattern can break information isolation without changing the underlying military balance.

## Evidence
- Siege isolation need: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1062-shanzei-de-nixi-daozei-chushen-de-ta-shi-ruhe-zhandui-de-lvkhmezd68key-p-kup55zypbjet|Hanji 1062]] says Yuan Shang cannot communicate normally with Ye after Cao Cao's moat and water barrier.
- Command-role performance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1062-shanzei-de-nixi-daozei-chushen-de-ta-shi-ruhe-zhandui-de-lvkhmezd68key-p-kup55zypbjet|Hanji 1062]] describes Li Fu posing as a Cao-side `dudu`, scolding and striking guards while riding through the camp.
- Wall access: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1062-shanzei-de-nixi-daozei-chushen-de-ta-shi-ruhe-zhandui-de-lvkhmezd68key-p-kup55zypbjet|Hanji 1062]] has Li Fu reach the city wall and reveal himself to the defenders only once he is outside immediate camp control.
- Different exit cover: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1062-shanzei-de-nixi-daozei-chushen-de-ta-shi-ruhe-zhandui-de-lvkhmezd68key-p-kup55zypbjet|Hanji 1062]] says Li Fu escapes by blending into surrendering old and weak people rather than repeating the command disguise.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This is currently a single-source concept. The source's costume details and guard reactions come from an automated podcast text and should not be treated as a general operational manual. The concept also does not imply that camp discipline is absent; it identifies a temporary verification failure under hierarchy, darkness, and performance pressure.

## What Changed
- Created the concept from Li Fu's Ye infiltration in Hanji 1062.
- Distinguished authority-performance infiltration from ordinary stealth and from false surrender.
