# 宗庙仪式反制政变 / Ritual Ambush Countercoup

宗庙仪式反制政变 / Ritual ambush countercoup is the pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-138-wudu-qiannian-de-lishi-zhenxiang-zhaogao-bushi-taijian-2-lvtievjrclb1lxxxxr6k-q7kryto|Qinji 138 part 2]] builds from [[ZiyingQin|子婴]]'s killing of [[ZhaoGao|赵高]]. A ritual sequence that should legitimate a new ruler is read as a trap, then reversed by feigned illness, controlled access, and an ambush in the fasting chamber.

After [[ImperialTitleRetrenchment|帝号退回王号]], Zhao Gao tells Ziying to bathe, fast, sacrifice in the royal ancestral temple, and receive the Qin king's seal. Ziying tells his sons that Zhao Gao has killed two emperors and may be preparing another move; he therefore refuses the scheduled temple appearance, forces Zhao Gao to come personally, and has him killed by hidden men.

## Key Claims
- Ritual can be a danger zone when the actor controlling accession also controls timing and access.
- Ziying's countercoup works because he treats the ceremony as political terrain rather than as neutral legitimacy.
- The episode creates a narrative inversion: Zhao Gao's palace coup kills Hu Hai, but Ziying uses palace/ritual access to kill Zhao Gao.
