# Feminist Fairy-Tale Rewriting / 女性主义童话改写

Feminist fairy-tale rewriting is the episode 38 frame for changing who gets desire, danger, voice, and decisive action inside inherited tales. In [[38-anjila-kate-jingguai-gushiji-o-mirende-hanfu-609607139]], [[AngelaCarter|Angela Carter / 安吉拉·卡特]] does this both by editing [[JingguaiGushiji|《精怪故事集》]] and by writing works such as [[TheBloodyChamber|《染血之室》]].

The source stresses that this is not only a gender-swap trick. Carter's method keeps folklore's dirt, sex, danger, humor, and violence while shifting agency away from the tame princess or male rescuer pattern. Rewriting therefore becomes a way to expose what earlier retellings made invisible: mothers, grandmothers, lust, household labor, hunger, female anger, and female comic intelligence.

## Key Claims
- Rewriting can alter narrative power without making the new text morally simple.
- Carter's Bluebeard example matters because rescue comes through the heroine's mother rather than through brothers or a male savior.
- The method depends on [[FairyTaleSanitization]] being reversible: cleaned tales can be reopened to their rougher social and bodily material.
- Feminist rewriting overlaps with [[AdaptationOriginalTextConfusion]] when a later mass adaptation makes the cleaned version feel original.
