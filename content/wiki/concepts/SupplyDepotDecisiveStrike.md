# Supply Depot Decisive Strike / 粮草命门打击

## Definition
Supply Depot Decisive Strike describes a campaign pattern in which a concentrated attack on an enemy's grain or supply center changes the war faster than frontal attrition. The strike matters because it attacks material endurance, morale, command confidence, and alliance cohesion at once.

## Current Synthesis
The concept is grounded in the [[WuchaoRaidLateHan|Wuchao Raid]] sequence. The newer pre-raid evidence shows the target becoming visible before the fire: [[CaoCao|Cao Cao]] is nearly out of grain, [[JuShou|Ju Shou]] warns [[YuanShao|Yuan Shao]] to strengthen convoy protection, and [[XuYouLateHan|Xu You]] defects with information that Yuan's supplies around Gushi and Wuchao are lightly guarded. Hanji 1049 then completes the mechanism when Cao turns that information into a strike against the point that keeps Yuan's larger army functional.

The concept is distinct from ordinary logistics pressure because the attack is concentrated, time-sensitive, and politically contagious. Once Wuchao burns, Yuan Shao loses grain, [[ChunyuQiong|Chunyu Qiong]]'s defeat damages confidence, [[GuoTu|Guo Tu]] shifts blame, [[ZhangHeLateHan|Zhang He]] and [[GaoLanLateHan|Gao Lan]] surrender, and the larger force loses the coherence that numerical superiority alone cannot restore.

Hanji 1143 adds a preventive variant at [[WanChengLujiang|皖城]]. The target is not yet a mature depot: [[ZhuGuangLateHan|朱光]] is cultivating rice and building water works that could support a larger Cao-side force. [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]] therefore argues for striking before harvest, making future logistical capacity—not only stored grain—a legitimate campaign object when the growth trajectory, timing window, and access conditions are visible.

## Key Claims
- A decisive or preventive supply-node strike can reverse numerical imbalance or remove future force capacity when endurance depends on a vulnerable depot or emerging production base.
- Intelligence from a defector or insider can turn a hidden logistical weakness into a targetable battlefield objective.
- Ignored protection warnings can make a depot or convoy vulnerability strategically decisive.
- Concentration matters: Cao's refusal to divide his small force keeps pressure on the decisive target until Wuchao falls.
- The effect is material and psychological at the same time; grain loss becomes morale collapse once troops see the supply base destroyed.
- The strike can trigger political secondary effects, including blame shifting, elite defection, and leadership flight.
- The pattern depends on timing and risk tolerance, because failed penetration behind enemy lines could destroy the weaker army.

## Evidence
- Target discovery and protection failure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1048-xuyou-pan-yuan-xianji-guandu-zhanju-de-jingtian-bianshu-lt3sewxvtpytrufrex8b0s0p0hju|Hanji 1048]] links Cao Cao's grain crisis, Ju Shou's rejected convoy-protection warning, Xu You's defection, and the identification of lightly guarded supplies at Gushi and Wuchao.
- Vulnerable supply node: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1049-guandu-zhizhan-jieju-jiemu-caocao-zhongyu-yi-ruo-sheng-qiang-lvtutwyhrix4rhiypa9dknz3da1m]] presents Wuchao as Yuan Shao's grain depot and therefore as the point where a raid can break a much larger army.
- Intelligence and risk: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1049-guandu-zhizhan-jieju-jiemu-caocao-zhongyu-yi-ruo-sheng-qiang-lvtutwyhrix4rhiypa9dknz3da1m]] says Cao acts on Xu You's information even though the information might be false and the target lies behind Yuan lines.
- Concentrated attack: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1049-guandu-zhizhan-jieju-jiemu-caocao-zhongyu-yi-ruo-sheng-qiang-lvtutwyhrix4rhiypa9dknz3da1m]] highlights Cao's choice to keep attacking Wuchao rather than splitting forces when Yuan reinforcements approach.
- Secondary collapse: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1049-guandu-zhizhan-jieju-jiemu-caocao-zhongyu-yi-ruo-sheng-qiang-lvtutwyhrix4rhiypa9dknz3da1m]] links Wuchao's burning to Chunyu Qiong's execution, Guo Tu's slander, Zhang He and Gao Lan's surrender, Yuan Shao's flight, and Ju Shou's capture.
- Preventive supply-base strike: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1143-guren-lihun-neng-you-duo-qipa-lutfgjyzwfwwh4ovzuevene8xupy|Hanji 1143]] says Lyu Meng urges attack on Wan City before Zhu Guang's rice harvest can sustain a larger forward force.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept rests on a tight Guandu cluster plus one later preventive case and should be extended cautiously. A depot or supply-base strike is not automatically decisive: it requires accurate intelligence, a reachable node, enough force concentration, and an enemy unable to compensate quickly. Wuchao should be treated as the Guandu hinge rather than its only cause; Wan City's agricultural scale, maturity, and counterfactual future force capacity remain episode-level claims.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 1048's pre-raid evidence: ignored convoy protection, Xu You's defection, and the Gushi-Wuchao target disclosure.
- Reframed the strike as a sequence from vulnerability recognition to concentrated attack rather than only the burning itself.
- Extended the concept from destruction of stored supplies to preventive removal of an emerging production-and-supply base.
