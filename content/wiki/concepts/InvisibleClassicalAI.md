# Invisible Classical AI

Invisible classical AI is [[ChristopherMims]]' reminder in [[tech-20260203-0203-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260203-0203-mp-tech-pod-128]] that generative chatbots are not the whole AI story. The episode points to older, pre-generative systems embedded in Google Maps routing, insurance-claim apps, search results, and social-media feeds.

The concept helps separate public AI attention from operational AI influence. Many consequential AI encounters are not framed as talking to a model; they appear as a route, ranking, recommendation, claims decision, or feed order inside an ordinary product.

## Key Claims
- Generative AI can dominate public attention while older AI systems continue shaping daily choices in less visible ways.
- Invisible AI often works through ranking, routing, classification, prediction, and recommendation rather than natural-language generation.
- User agency is harder to evaluate when the AI decision is hidden inside a normal product surface.
- The concept is compatible with [[AIConsumerDecisionShaping]] and [[FeedCuration]] because the main issue is how unseen systems order options before users choose.
