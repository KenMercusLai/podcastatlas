# 联销体模式 / Lianxiaoti Distribution

## Definition
联销体模式 is the Wahaha channel system described by 大小电波: a distributor alliance built around deposits, advance payment, territorial discipline, tiered wholesalers, and reciprocal interest-like returns.

## Current Synthesis
In the source, 联销体 is less a sales tactic than a working-capital and control design. It turns distributors into a semi-locked channel community, reduces receivables risk, creates predictable cash flow, and lets products such as [[FutureCola|非常可乐]] reach county, township, and rural stores. Its strength is also its boundary: when consumer discovery shifts toward e-commerce and platform traffic, a deeply offline distributor system can become slower to adapt.

## Key Claims
- Deposits and advance payment can convert distributor relationships into a cash-flow engine.
- Territorial rules and tiered wholesaling can reduce channel disorder when retail infrastructure is fragmented.
- Distributor loyalty becomes more durable when the manufacturer shares some financial return and product upside.
- A powerful offline channel can create lower-tier-market advantage even against stronger global brands.
- The same channel depth can become a constraint when new consumption shifts toward platform retail, live commerce, and direct online demand.

## Evidence
- Cash-flow design: [[fengwei-chihe-wahaha-zongqinghou-de-sanci-duiju-gkwrijiouqosa60omatfv8yx]] says Wahaha built the model after major receivables problems, requiring distributor deposits and advance payment.
- Channel discipline: [[fengwei-chihe-wahaha-zongqinghou-de-sanci-duiju-gkwrijiouqosa60omatfv8yx]] describes a three-level wholesale system and anti-cross-region rules.
- Lower-tier reach: [[fengwei-chihe-wahaha-zongqinghou-de-sanci-duiju-gkwrijiouqosa60omatfv8yx]] credits the network with helping 非常可乐 enter county, township, rural, and small-store markets.
- Adaptation limit: [[fengwei-chihe-wahaha-zongqinghou-de-sanci-duiju-gkwrijiouqosa60omatfv8yx]] contrasts Zong Qinghou's defense of offline channels with peers' faster movement onto Tmall, JD, and other online channels.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is favorable toward the model's historical effectiveness but also shows its era dependence. It does not prove that deposit-heavy distribution is universally transferable across categories, regulation regimes, or later online retail conditions.
