# Love as Conquest Logic / 爱情的征服逻辑

## Definition

Love as conquest logic is a relational frame in which being desired, obeyed, or able to destroy another person proves one's value, while desiring them in return feels like surrendering control and risking defeat.

## Current Synthesis

The bounded case is [[LinXianer|林仙儿]] in [[SentimentalSwordsmanRuthlessSword|《多情剑客无情剑》]]. Her influence over admirers is not interpreted primarily as a search for money or physical pleasure. Submission supplies evidence of beauty and worth, while breaking an already powerful man makes the victory feel complete.

阿飞 disrupts that structure because his attachment is sincere rather than merely captivated, and she may care for him in return. The source interprets her refusal of reciprocal vulnerability and continued secret activity as attempts to restore control. By the time she is ready to accept love, lost status and his changed perception make the original relation unavailable.

## Key Claims

- Attraction becomes a status test when another person's desire is used chiefly as evidence of one's worth.
- Control can require escalation from admiration to obedience, dependency, humiliation, or destruction.
- Reciprocal attachment threatens conquest logic because each person becomes vulnerable to refusal and change.
- A person can seek repeated confirmation without being able to trust the confirmation once obtained.
- Insecurity may help explain control without making deception or cruelty harmless.
- Late willingness to love cannot guarantee restoration of a person whose agency and perception have changed.

### Destruction beyond acquisition

- [[045-xiaoli-feidao-cheng-juexiang-gulong-duoqing-jianke-wuqing-jian-zhong-de-aihen-jiufen-lnmv8ezqdjjgeyd4ezi-b-isxibp]] uses 吕凤先's collapse to distinguish proving control from merely acquiring wealth, access, or pleasure.

### Reciprocal vulnerability and timing

- [[045-xiaoli-feidao-cheng-juexiang-gulong-duoqing-jianke-wuqing-jian-zhong-de-aihen-jiufen-lnmv8ezqdjjgeyd4ezi-b-isxibp]] interprets Lin Xianer's treatment of 阿飞 and eventual rejection as the cost of understanding love through winning and losing.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept is drawn from an informal interpretation of fictional conduct. It is not a diagnosis, and the source does not establish Lin Xianer's childhood, attachment style, or inner motives independently. Beauty, sexuality, ambition, or non-monogamy are not themselves evidence of conquest logic; the relevant pattern is instrumental control and deliberate damage used to stabilize self-worth.

## What Changed

- Created the concept from Lin Xianer's contrast between securing desire and accepting reciprocal vulnerability.
