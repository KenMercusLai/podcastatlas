## Definition

颂圣不合群风险 / coerced praise conformity risk is the pattern where a political environment makes praise, auspicious reporting, or public agreement so expected that silence, skepticism, or disaster reporting can be reclassified as hostility.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 625 grounds the concept in [[WangMang|王莽]]'s 5 CE court. Envoys return with reports that customs are unified, songs praise the regime, markets are orderly, litigation has disappeared, and even offenders are morally transformed. In that setting, praise is not decoration; it is a loyalty signal backed by rewards.

The danger appears through [[BanZhiWesternHan|班至]] and [[GongsunHongLangyaWesternHan|公孙洪]]. Ban Zhi's failure to report auspicious signs and Gongsun Hong's discussion of disasters are turned into accusatory material once [[ZhenFengXinOfficial|甄丰]] activates local petition channels. Ban Zhi survives through [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]'s protection and visible demotion, while Gongsun Hong is killed. The mechanism is therefore about forced alignment: when praise becomes compulsory, nonparticipation becomes legible as opposition.

## Key Claims

- Coerced praise systems convert positive reporting into a loyalty test.
- Rewards for praise intensify the risk because they make nonparticipation stand out as refusal.
- Disaster reporting becomes especially dangerous when the dominant faction is claiming universal order.
- Political attacks can be laundered through local petitions or subordinate accusations rather than stated directly by the central powerholder.
- Survival may require visible retreat or lowered office even when the original conduct was only silence or skepticism.

## Evidence

- Rewarded praise environment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-625-jiemi-renxing-anmian-weihe-pangren-chenggong-zong-ling-women-xinsheng-jihen-li64ngkv8nujwegbix6iororjmbo|Hanji 625]] says envoys report harmonized customs and tens of thousands of songs, then receive marquisates with other builders of moral-political infrastructure.
- Nonparticipation as exposure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-625-jiemi-renxing-anmian-weihe-pangren-chenggong-zong-ling-women-xinsheng-jihen-li64ngkv8nujwegbix6iororjmbo|Hanji 625]] says Ban Zhi never reports auspicious signs and Gongsun Hong talks about disasters while the court praises Wang Mang's governance.
- Accusation laundering: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-625-jiemi-renxing-anmian-weihe-pangren-chenggong-zong-ling-women-xinsheng-jihen-li64ngkv8nujwegbix6iororjmbo|Hanji 625]] says Zhen Feng sends subordinates to induce local officials and people to accuse the two men.
- Divergent outcomes: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-625-jiemi-renxing-anmian-weihe-pangren-chenggong-zong-ling-women-xinsheng-jihen-li64ngkv8nujwegbix6iororjmbo|Hanji 625]] says Gongsun Hong is killed while Ban Zhi survives after dowager protection and voluntary demotion.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept currently rests on one source and should not be generalized to every court praise scene. Hanji 625 does not prove that all reported songs or auspicious signs were fabricated, nor does it settle Ban Zhi's or Gongsun Hong's private motives. It supports the narrower claim that under Wang Mang's 5 CE court environment, failure to join praise could become politically actionable.

## What Changed

- Created from Hanji 625's Ban Zhi and Gongsun Hong contrast under Wang Mang's praise and auspicious-reporting pressure.
