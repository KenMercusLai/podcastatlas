# AI Cognitive Gym / 把 AI 当健身房

AI cognitive gym is [[WangTianfan]]'s advice in [[reai-yige-hangye-15-nian-de-liyou-shi-shenme-duitan-wang-tianfan-woyao-tou-zhenzheng-de-kuaile-tou-zui-chun-de-yuanjing-tou-renxing-de-guanghui-gonglu-boke-lu98aa1byafbbljyjrn8oquiezk]]: use AI as a coach and training environment rather than as a place to outsource all thinking. The point is to increase the frequency and intensity of thinking with AI while keeping the user's own brain active.

The concept is a constructive counterpart to [[CognitiveDebt]]. Wang accepts heavy AI use, but argues that users should be proactive, explicit, and context-rich in prompts so the model can help them analyze tradeoffs rather than simply produce a quick answer.

## Key Claims
- AI use can train thinking when users bring context, uncertainty, concerns, and goals into the prompt.
- Long prompts are not just verbosity; they force the user to articulate what they want and why.
- The risk is not AI use itself, but letting model output replace attention, reflection, and personal judgment.
- A good AI product should help the user preserve agency, not erode capability through disuse.
