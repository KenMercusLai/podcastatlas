# Gender Barrier Spaceflight

Gender barrier spaceflight is the institutional pattern where women can meet or exceed technical and physical thresholds for spaceflight but still be excluded from official selection. [[omission-accomplished-why-the-iran-war-cycle-spins-on-6a6c7475aa1642a30f17f1cb]] adds the concept through [[WallyFunk]], who took the same tests as men in [[WilliamRandolphLovelace]]'s program but did not become an astronaut in the early space age.

The concept matters because the barrier is not framed as a capability gap. [[AnnWroe]]'s profile emphasizes that Funk kept flying, instructed pilots, inspected airplanes, investigated crashes, and continued space training before finally reaching space through [[BlueOrigin]] at 82.

## Key Claims
- Testing access does not equal institutional selection if the decision-making body excludes a category of candidate.
- Later recognition can be meaningful without undoing the career time and public role denied earlier.
- A biography can expose an institution's selection logic more clearly than abstract policy language.
