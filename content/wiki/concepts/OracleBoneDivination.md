# Oracle-Bone Divination / 甲骨占卜

Oracle-bone divination / 甲骨占卜 is the earliest technique layer foregrounded in [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-wei-wan-daixu-704051997]]. The episode explains "卜" through burned cracks on turtle shells or animal bones, then treats inscribed oracle bones as both ritual objects and records of Shang political and social life.

The concept matters because the source does not reduce oracle bones to prediction. They also preserve questions, dates, diviners, topics, outcomes, and social priorities, making divination a record of how power asked the world for permission or warning.

## Key Claims
- Divination can be both a ritual technology and an archive of ordinary state concerns.
- Repeated positive and negative questioning gave rulers and diviners room to seek usable answers.
- Recorded verification can strengthen authority even when interpretation remains controlled by specialists.
- Oracle-bone material connects early writing, ritual, governance, and uncertainty management.
