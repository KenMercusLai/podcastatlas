# Oracle-Bone Divination / 甲骨占卜

## Definition

Oracle-bone divination is the Shang ritual practice of preparing turtle plastrons or animal shoulder bones, applying heat to create cracks, interpreting those cracks, and sometimes inscribing the question, diviner, prediction, and later outcome.

## Current Synthesis

Across the current sources, oracle bones are neither ordinary Shang writing material nor mere prediction devices. They are costly ritual objects through which royal authority manages uncertainty about ancestors, weather, agriculture, war, hunting, illness, travel, and disaster. Their inscriptions also create an archive of what the court asked, who divined, what was predicted, and occasionally what happened afterward.

Material workflow and later historical reuse deepen that synthesis. Bones required cleaning, flattening, and drilled hollows before burning; complete texts may contain preface, charge, prognostication, and verification; and modern recovery allowed [[WangGuowei|王国维]] to compare inscribed names with transmitted Shang genealogy. That evidentiary success remains bounded and should not be generalized to periods without equivalent support.

## Key Claims

- Divination can be both a ritual technology and an archive of state concerns.
- High preparation cost and specialist procedure contribute to the object's sacred and political authority.
- Recorded questions and later verification join uncertainty management to retrospective validation.
- Oracle-bone evidence connects early writing, ritual, governance, and modern historical reconstruction.
- Excavated and transmitted sources can test one another, but conclusions must remain within the supported period and material.

## Evidence

- State concerns - [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-wei-wan-daixu-704051997]] lists ancestors, weather, agriculture, war, hunting, feasting, and escaped slaves among preserved questions.
- Interpretive control - [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-wei-wan-daixu-704051997]] describes repeated positive and negative questioning and specialist authority over usable answers.
- Material workflow - [[244-shuxie-gei-guoqu-weilai-de-ren-yu-shenming-zhongguo-wenming-zhong-de-shuxie-chuantong-yu-changqi-zhuyi-8225426843-437457]] explains cleaning, leveling, thinning hollows, heating, cracking, writing, and carving.
- Inscription structure - [[244-shuxie-gei-guoqu-weilai-de-ren-yu-shenming-zhongguo-wenming-zhong-de-shuxie-chuantong-yu-changqi-zhuyi-8225426843-437457]] presents preface, charge, prognostication, and verification through eclipse and accident examples.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The surviving corpus is a specialized selection rather than a representative sample of all Shang writing. Details of whether signs were written before or after carving remain disputed in the source, and later loss of practical divination knowledge limits confident reconstruction. Verification records show a mechanism of retrospective recording but do not establish supernatural prediction.
