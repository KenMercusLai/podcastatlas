# Coffee Laxative Myth

## Definition
Coffee laxative myth is the episode's boundary around treating iced Americano or coffee as a reliable constipation solution.

## Current Synthesis
The source allows that moderate coffee may have regulatory effects for some people, including effects on gut function or microbiome context, but rejects the everyday shortcut that one iced Americano reliably "solves" constipation. Constipation can involve motility, sigmoid-colon anatomy, rectal structure, functional issues, or organic disease, so persistent or serious constipation belongs in clinical assessment rather than beverage folklore.

## Key Claims
- Coffee can be experienced as bowel-stimulating by some people, but the episode does not treat it as a dependable treatment.
- Constipation is a symptom category with multiple possible functional and structural causes.
- Beverage-based self-management can delay evaluation when constipation is persistent, severe, or associated with other warning signs.
- The useful public-health move is to separate harmless habit from therapeutic claim.
