# User Feedback Vehicle Definition / 用户反馈驱动车辆定义

## Definition

User feedback vehicle definition is the product process of turning owner complaints, dealership or store reports, questionnaires, NPS signals, city interviews, and observed real-use scenes into vehicle features, controls, settings, or product priorities.

## Current Synthesis

EP279 presents feedback as part of Hongmeng Zhixing's product-definition loop. The source does not show a complete requirements system, but it gives several concrete signals: monthly nationwide store feedback, questionnaires, NPS tracking, city owner interviews, young product teams using their own pain points, and a live owner complaint about overly sensitive approach unlocking.

The current judgment is that feedback matters most when it becomes scenario-specific design. G9 physical buttons are not described as nostalgia for old cars; they are justified by outdoor power, locks, doors, and other operations where physical controls can be easier than screen-first interaction.

## Key Claims

- Owner feedback becomes useful when it is translated into concrete scenes and controls rather than treated as generic satisfaction data.
- Store and service networks can expose recurring problems that a central product team may not see from specifications alone.
- NPS and questionnaires can support product learning, but they need qualitative owner interviews to explain why users react as they do.
- Physical controls can be feedback-driven when the task is urgent, outdoor, gloved, repetitive, or difficult to perform through screens.
- Feedback loops can improve product fit while still being source-scoped unless the process, sample, and implementation outcomes are visible.

## Evidence

- **Feedback channels:** [[ep279-dang-fangxiangpan-manman-songkai-women-ruhe-yu-che-xiangchu-lq2elbnjy6xrhixxqjs-y7erhlnv]] says Peng Lei describes monthly nationwide store feedback collection, questionnaires, NPS metrics, and city owner interviews.
- **Live complaint:** [[ep279-dang-fangxiangpan-manman-songkai-women-ruhe-yu-che-xiangchu-lq2elbnjy6xrhixxqjs-y7erhlnv]] records an owner complaint that approach-based automatic unlocking can be too sensitive, with Peng Lei responding that more unlocking choices could be considered.
- **Scenario controls:** [[ep279-dang-fangxiangpan-manman-songkai-women-ruhe-yu-che-xiangchu-lq2elbnjy6xrhixxqjs-y7erhlnv]] explains G9 physical buttons through outdoor, power, differential-lock, and door-control scenes.
- **Team pain points:** [[ep279-dang-fangxiangpan-manman-songkai-women-ruhe-yu-che-xiangchu-lq2elbnjy6xrhixxqjs-y7erhlnv]] says the product team is young and can bring its own and nearby users' pain points into product definition.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

- The source does not provide response rates, NPS methodology, prioritization rules, or evidence that a specific complaint shipped as a feature.
- Feedback can bias toward vocal owners if sampling is not disciplined.
- Product teams still need safety, compliance, cost, manufacturability, and brand-positioning filters; user requests do not automatically become good design.

## What Changed
