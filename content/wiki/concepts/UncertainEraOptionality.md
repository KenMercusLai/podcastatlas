# Uncertain-Era Optionality / 乱纪元选择权

Uncertain-era optionality is [[176-niuyue-yinian-yige-beiguanzhuyizhe-de-huofa-725-shalong-shilu-lte-k8lw8e1txxw3nfxvo3wal8ah]]'s life-design answer to "乱纪元": an environment where market shocks, geopolitics, borders, pandemics, identity, and social disorder can suddenly enter ordinary life. [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] does not present pessimism as a reason to withdraw. He treats it as a reason to preserve choices.

The concept extends [[StructureOverPrediction]] and [[LifeAntifragility]]. It includes asset diversification, but it is not limited to portfolios. The same posture applies to geographic mobility, currency exposure, skills, content work, family plans, and the ability to move one's body when conditions change.

## Key Claims
- Optionality is a response to unpredictable systems, not a claim that every bad scenario can be forecast.
- All-in choices become more dangerous when risk can arrive through multiple channels at once.
- Asset allocation, base-currency decisions, and physical mobility are parts of one broader survival-and-choice structure.
- The concept avoids nihilism by pairing pessimism with present-tense work, relationships, and learning rather than endless scenario anxiety.
- Good structure should preserve future action even when the next shock does not match the forecast.
