# Transport Corridor Severance / 交通线切割

Transport corridor severance / 交通线切割 is the strategy pattern highlighted in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-92-lishishang-di-yi-wei-zhuming-tiangou-shi-shui-1-lmwf0q0-3hxb6d4hro8fdmc3zdxp]]. [[FanJu|范雎]]'s advice to [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] is not framed as simple conquest. It targets roads, passes, and geographic seams that make [[HanState|韩国]] governable as one unit.

In the episode's reconstruction, Qin pressure around Xingyang and Chenggao, the taking of [[HanNanyangWarringStates|韩国南阳]], and the attack on [[TaihangRoad|太行道]] would split Han into separated pieces. The immediate effect is that [[Shangdang|上党]] loses its route to Xinzheng, making local defection or surrender decisions more likely to drive the next war.

The concept extends [[YuanjiaoJingongStrategy|远交近攻]] by explaining how "near attack" can work below the level of state destruction. Qin can weaken a neighbor by severing communication and support lines first, then absorb or pressure the isolated pieces in sequence.

## Key Claims
- Route control can matter as much as city capture because it changes whether a state can reinforce, communicate with, or command an exposed region.
- Cutting corridors turns one enemy state into multiple isolated operational problems.
- A severed region may become politically unstable before it is formally conquered.
- The mechanism links geography to diplomacy: once [[Shangdang|上党]] is isolated, its commander can seek [[ZhaoState|赵国]] protection and widen the war.
