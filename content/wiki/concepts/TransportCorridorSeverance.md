# Transport Corridor Severance / 交通线切割

Transport corridor severance / 交通线切割 is the strategy pattern highlighted in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-92-lishishang-di-yi-wei-zhuming-tiangou-shi-shui-1-lmwf0q0-3hxb6d4hro8fdmc3zdxp]]. [[FanJu|范雎]]'s advice to [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] is not framed as simple conquest. It targets roads, passes, and geographic seams that make [[HanState|韩国]] governable as one unit.

In the episode's reconstruction, Qin pressure around Xingyang and Chenggao, the taking of [[HanNanyangWarringStates|韩国南阳]], and the attack on [[TaihangRoad|太行道]] would split Han into separated pieces. The immediate effect is that [[Shangdang|上党]] loses its route to Xinzheng, making local defection or surrender decisions more likely to drive the next war.

The concept extends [[YuanjiaoJingongStrategy|远交近攻]] by explaining how "near attack" can work below the level of state destruction. Qin can weaken a neighbor by severing communication and support lines first, then absorb or pressure the isolated pieces in sequence.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-93-zai-mengli-cangzhe-toutian-gaiming-1-ljshdryvf9qgabtn-siontthzsok]] supplies the immediate political aftereffect. Once [[Shangdang|上党]] is cut off, [[FengTing|冯亭]] tries to avoid Qin surrender by offering the territory to [[ZhaoState|赵国]]. The severed corridor therefore creates not just administrative failure for [[HanState|韩国]], but a [[WindfallDecisionTrap|windfall decision trap]] for the neighboring state asked to receive the isolated region.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-93-zai-mengli-cangzhe-toutian-gaiming-2-lrlulw0b6v0fb2xpr2i5ifem1h4n]] completes the mechanism: the isolated region is actually received by Zhao. [[ZhaoBao|赵豹 / 平阳君]] recognizes the transfer as "嫁祸," but [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] supports acceptance and [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] formalizes the transfer through generous rewards. Corridor severance therefore becomes a cause chain from geography to diplomatic bait to the [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] prelude.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-2-lvqctrazn8ddkcnp-lhspzzmzmns]] adds a battle-scale analogue. Once Zhao Kuo attacks, Bai Qi cuts the Zhao army away from its camp and food route, while Qin Zhaoxiang Wang mobilizes Henei to prevent relief. This does not replace the earlier regional corridor concept; it links it to [[BattlefieldEncirclementLogistics|战场围困与断粮]], where the severed route belongs to a trapped field army rather than an isolated territory.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-134-ni-buzhidao-de-pofu-chenzhou-banben-ltnry1fva2hzplw4ft8nrfpoiolw|Qinji 134]] adds a siege-relief version at [[JuluBattle|巨鹿之战]]. Qin's protected yongdao carries food from [[Aocang|敖仓]] toward [[WangLiQin|王离]]'s army at [[Julu|巨鹿]], while [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] guards the broader supply system. [[YingBu|英布]] and [[PuGeneral|蒲将军]] break that corridor before Xiang Yu's main attack, so severance now works against the besieger rather than against an exposed province or field army.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-156-bingxian-hanxin-de-fengshen-zhizhan-1-ltkz7bwqgkcqessdfbn9unf5e05l|Hanji 156 part 1]] adds a campaign-passage version. At [[JingxingPass|井陉口]], [[LiZuoChe|李左车]] reads [[HanXin|韩信]] and [[ZhangEr|张耳]]'s force as vulnerable because food trails behind a long army moving through a narrow route. His proposed side-path attack would sever the transport line before Han can choose a normal battle.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-159-1-liubang-xijiao-de-yuren-zhishu-lga3puexunjzeu3s8pvt8saaa8n3|Hanji 159-1]] adds the central-front version against Han. Chu captures [[Aocang|敖仓]], controls Yellow River transport, and repeatedly attacks the Han food corridor around [[XingyangStandoff|荥阳相持]]. Here severance is not only a proposed tactic or pre-battle setup; it becomes the pressure that makes [[LiuBang|刘邦]] receptive to a bad political plan.

## Key Claims
- Route control can matter as much as city capture because it changes whether a state can reinforce, communicate with, or command an exposed region.
- Cutting corridors turns one enemy state into multiple isolated operational problems.
- A severed region may become politically unstable before it is formally conquered.
- The mechanism links geography to diplomacy: once [[Shangdang|上党]] is isolated, its commander can seek [[ZhaoState|赵国]] protection and widen the war.
- Zhouji 93 adds that corridor severance can transfer risk outward: the isolated region may be offered as an apparent gift that imports the original attacker's retaliation.
- Zhouji 93 part 2 adds that once the recipient ceremonially accepts the severed region, the imported conflict becomes harder to unwind.
- Zhouji 94 part 2 adds that the same logic can shrink to battle scale: cutting the army's food route turns maneuver into starvation.
- Qinji 134 adds that corridor severance can support siege relief: cutting the besieger's protected food route can make direct battle winnable.
- Hanji 156 adds that a narrow pass can make a moving army's supply tail the corridor to cut, even before formal encirclement begins.
- Hanji 159-1 adds that food-corridor pressure can distort political judgment, pushing a ruler toward risky legitimacy schemes when the front is starving.
