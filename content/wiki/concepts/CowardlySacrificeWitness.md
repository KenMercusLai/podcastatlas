# 胆小鬼的献祭 / Cowardly Sacrifice As Witness

Cowardly sacrifice as witness is the episode's source-scoped reading of [[NoLongerHuman|《人间失格》]]. In [[33-renjian-shige-danxiaogui-de-zhuanglie-xianji-599924624]], the hosts connect the closing description of [[ObaYozo|大庭叶藏]] as a godlike good child to a broader claim: a failed, frightened, and socially despised person can still function as a witness for those who cannot meet the standards of ordinary human success.

This is not redemption in the clean sense. The source does not make Yozo lovable or innocent, and it does not say that self-destruction saves others. The "sacrifice" is literary exposure: [[DazaiOsamu|太宰治]] writes weakness, cowardice, dependence, addiction, and shame in a form that readers may reject, but that can also give language to hidden failure.

The [[YukioMishima|三岛由纪夫]] contrast helps define the concept. If public strength can also be a performance, then admitting fear may require another kind of severity. The episode's claim is that confessed cowardice can resist [[CoercedNormality|coerced normality]] more honestly than heroic posture.

## Key Claims
- A literary witness for failure is not the same as a moral model.
- The concept depends on [[MoralSuspensionInArtReading]]: readers must understand the exposure before deciding what judgment should remain.
- The "sacrifice" reading is source-scoped and interpretive, not a settled doctrine about Dazai's life.
- Its value lies in making shame and unfitness speakable for readers who otherwise only receive commands to improve, perform, or disappear.
