# Corporate Memphis

Corporate Memphis is the flat, colorful design style named in [[tech-20260813-0813-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260813-0813-mp-tech-pod-128]] as an example of aesthetic exhaustion. The episode describes it through simplified figures with small heads and noodle-like arms: a style that could once feel fresh, then become a signal of low effort after enough corporate repetition.

In the wiki, Corporate Memphis is a compact case for how taste depends on timing and saturation. It connects [[EmbodiedTaste]] and [[AlgorithmicCulturalFlattening]] to [[AIContentDevaluation]]: mass-producing a once-legible style can make the style read as generic even if its surface features remain recognizable.

## Key Claims
- A design style can move from fresh to stale through repeated use.
- Taste is partly historical: the same visual language can mean different things after cultural saturation.
- Low-effort mass production can make an aesthetic feel less tasteful even when the formal style is coherent.
- Generative AI may accelerate this exhaustion by making imitable styles cheaper to reproduce.
