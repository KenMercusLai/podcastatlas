# Jikobukken Incident Property

Jikobukken incident property is [[kyiv-brawl-ukraines-top-general-fired-6a609aec4fac21e67f1be165]]'s Japan housing concept: homes associated with tragic, brutal, or socially unsettling deaths. [[MoekaIida]] uses an apartment where an isolated elderly woman died and was not discovered for days to show how death, memory, and market value can attach to a dwelling.

The concept matters because the stigma is not only supernatural belief. The episode links it to ancestral worship, Buddhist mourning rituals, abnormal death, psychological discomfort, disclosure, and the fear that a home carries bad luck or lingering presence.

## Key Claims
- Death-associated housing can become a visible category in the real-estate market.
- The stigma is strongest when death is seen as abnormal, violent, lonely, or ritually unsettled.
- Buyers and tenants may demand a discount even if they do not fully believe in supernatural effects.
- Maps, memoirs, YouTube visits, cleansing rituals, and inspection certificates can all become part of the market around the stigma.
- Rising housing costs can make some tenants more willing to accept a stigmatized home.
