# Late-Blooming Talent / 大器晚成式人才显现

Late-blooming talent / 大器晚成式人才显现 is the life-course pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-102-1-lvbuwei-shi-jiangziya-houren-lveg-wsmggroaxjsnxopaevexqod]] draws from [[JiangTaigong|姜太公 / 姜子牙]]. The source presents Jiang Taigong as poor, dependent, and publicly unaccomplished until very late in life, but still studying astronomy, geography, military strategy, and governance.

The concept is not that age by itself creates ability. The episode's stronger pattern is that latent preparation, patience, and timing must meet recognition. Jiang Taigong's old age matters because [[ZhouWenwang|周文王]] recognizes him and gives him a political stage; without that recognition, capacity would remain private.

For the wiki, this sits beside talent-recognition and fit concepts rather than replacing them. [[TalentReferralQuality|荐才层级质量]] asks who can identify ability, [[TransitionFitOverMerit]] asks whether prior merit fits a new environment, and Late-Blooming Talent asks how long-hidden capacity becomes visible only when circumstance and patronage align.
