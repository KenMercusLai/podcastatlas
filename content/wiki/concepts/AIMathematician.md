# AI Mathematician

## Definition
An AI mathematician is a system that can prove, conjecture, formalize, use mathematical knowledge, and eventually help expand mathematical research frontiers.

## Current Synthesis
The current synthesis distinguishes proof production from mathematical understanding. [[HongLetong]] treats [[AxiomProver]] as one step toward a broader [[AIForMath]] system that can formalize literature, generate conjectures, and support human research taste. The [[OpenAI]] Navier-Stokes controversy adds a second test: even if an AI system produces a correct proof, mathematicians still need explanations, route visibility, priority judgment, and insight extraction before the work functions like ordinary mathematics. [[AlexKontorovich]] adds an institutional consequence: AI can point toward progress like a compass, but education and hiring must separately test whether a person understands, can defend, and can extend the result.

## Key Claims
- Proof is necessary but incomplete: mathematical creativity also involves asking natural questions, finding useful definitions, and generating conjectures.
- Human mathematicians may shift toward higher abstraction, taste, direction-setting, and benchmark design as AI handles more formal proof labor.
- Machine proof can be correct without being elegant; the system may first become useful through brute-force formal reasoning before matching human intuition.
- A true AI mathematician needs [[AutoFormalization]] so it can read and convert ordinary mathematical literature, not only solve already-formalized statements.
- The source connects the idea to [[MathematicalAbundance]], where math supply expands dramatically and mathematicians allocate attention and compute toward the best problems.
- Major-problem claims make [[AIGeneratedProofGovernance]] part of the AI mathematician target, because discovery must be reviewable and attributable.
- Human-AI collaboration still requires trained experts who can interpret, teach, prioritize, and integrate generated mathematics.

- Human role - [[137-dui-hong-letong-de-4-xiaoshi-fangtan-ai-for-math-ba-shuxue-biancheng-lean-shuxue-tianshu-zhong-de-zhengming-zhijue-bei-chuangzao-yu-bei-faxian-de-lha-faiwxtget0qmbcosts3cb5vb]] keeps human abstraction, benchmark design, and research taste central.
- Governance pressure - [[out-numbered-ais-contentious-maths-milestone-6aa3ce622e8bb8424c920cfb]] says the claimed OpenAI result raises questions about the purpose of mathematics, credit, explanation, and the insights generated along the way.
- Collaborative role and mastery - [[tech-20261102-1102-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261102-1102-mp-tech-pod-128]] treats AI as a compass rather than a complete substitute and separates sophisticated written output from demonstrated understanding.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The Navier-Stokes claim and the newer episode's approximate progress counts remain unvalidated in this wiki. These sources do not prove that a full AI mathematician has arrived or that demand for human mathematicians will rise; they show the capability, acceptance, interpretation, and assessment burdens created by increasingly strong systems.

## What Changed
- Added human interpretation and demonstrable understanding as requirements around AI-generated mathematics.
- Added the compass model of collaborative research while keeping labor effects prospective.
