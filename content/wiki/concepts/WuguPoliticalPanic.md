# Wugu Political Panic / 巫蛊政治恐慌

Wugu political panic / 巫蛊政治恐慌 is the source's nameable pattern for the final section of [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-wei-wan-daixu-704051997]]. The episode defines wugu as curse practice using figures, needles, burial, and hidden ritual action, then follows how belief in such hidden harm enters the [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]] court.

The concept matters because it resembles a witch-hunt mechanism inside imperial politics. Once illness and misfortune are attributed to invisible curse action, digging, torture, accusation, and denunciation can produce "evidence" that keeps expanding.

## Key Claims
- Wugu panic depends on institutions treating hidden ritual action as politically actionable evidence.
- Palace rivalry and succession anxiety make accusations useful even when truth is unclear.
- Torture and denunciation can expand a case far beyond the original claim.
- The source's strongest warning is that superstition becomes most dangerous when joined to state violence.
