## Definition

强势君主下的丞相双重约束 / strong-ruler chancellorship double bind is the role conflict in which a chief minister must remain personally deferential and avoid appearing independently dominant while still exercising enough judgment and administrative capacity to manage the ruler's demanding state agenda.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-332-1-gudai-zuigaowei-zhiye-zhiyi-juran-you-ta-lk6luhbh37m9xnachwjp9zbdmcnu|Hanji 332-1]] frames the Western Han chancellorship under [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] through two simultaneous demands. A chancellor who is too individual, forceful, or resistant may not retain the confidence of a highly assertive ruler. Yet the same office must handle expansion, military strategy against the [[Xiongnu|匈奴]], fiscal mobilization, resource supply, and coordination within the court. Passivity protects neither the office nor the state because the workload itself requires competence and initiative.

The tension helps explain why personal pliability cannot be equated with an easy or purely ceremonial post. [[LiCaiWesternHan|李蔡]] is the episode's opening case, while [[GongsunHong|公孙弘]] serves as the exceptional predecessor who dies naturally in office and [[ZhuangQingdiWesternHan|庄青翟]] and [[ZhaoZhouWesternHan|赵周]] extend the adverse-outcome sequence. The concept is an interpretive risk model, not proof that every chancellor fell for the same reason or that the double bind directly caused any one death.

## Key Claims

- Strong personal rule can make visible ministerial independence politically dangerous even when the office formally carries broad responsibility.
- Large war, finance, logistics, strategy, and coordination burdens still require a chancellor with real administrative competence.
- Deference and capability are not opposites, but satisfying both becomes difficult when initiative can be read as rivalry or disobedience.
- High turnover and adverse outcomes can reflect multiple mechanisms inside the same risk environment rather than one uniform purge policy.
- A surviving or successful chancellor is therefore not evidence that the structural tension disappears; it may instead indicate unusually effective political adaptation.

## Evidence

Compliance demand:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-332-1-gudai-zuigaowei-zhiye-zhiyi-juran-you-ta-lk6luhbh37m9xnachwjp9zbdmcnu|Hanji 332-1]] characterizes Han Wudi as highly forceful and says an overly individual chancellor would struggle to keep the post.

Capacity demand:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-332-1-gudai-zuigaowei-zhiye-zhiyi-juran-you-ta-lk6luhbh37m9xnachwjp9zbdmcnu|Hanji 332-1]] assigns the chancellor work spanning territorial expansion, fiscal and supply mobilization, assessment of the Xiongnu war, and internal court coordination.

Outcome pattern:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-332-1-gudai-zuigaowei-zhiye-zhiyi-juran-you-ta-lk6luhbh37m9xnachwjp9zbdmcnu|Hanji 332-1]] reports thirteen chancellors during Wudi's reign, emphasizes dismissals and suicides, and contrasts Gongsun Hong's death in office with the later outcomes of Li Cai, Zhuang Qingdi, and Zhao Zhou.

Bounded case:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-332-1-gudai-zuigaowei-zhiye-zhiyi-juran-you-ta-lk6luhbh37m9xnachwjp9zbdmcnu|Hanji 332-1]] uses Li Cai's career and suicide to introduce the risk pattern, but does not supply the land-case details or prove that the double bind caused his death.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept currently rests on one short popular-history episode. Its chancellor count, outcome categories, reign length, office sequence, and characterization of Han Wudi have not been independently reconstructed here. The episode itself says the chancellors did not all meet bad ends for the same reason, which argues against a single-cause theory. Formal jurisdiction, factional conflict, criminal allegations, disaster responsibility, health, succession politics, and individual decisions can each alter an officeholder's risk. Gongsun Hong's natural death in office is an important counterexample to inevitability, while Li Cai's stated terminal charge in Hanji 332-2 concerns a land transaction rather than independent policy resistance.

## What Changed

- Created the concept to separate the office's simultaneous compliance and competence demands from any single chancellor's terminal case.
