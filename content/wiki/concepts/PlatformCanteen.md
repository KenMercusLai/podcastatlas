# Platform Canteen / 平台食堂

Platform canteen is the foodservice pattern extracted from [[vol-270-da-shi-dai-liu-zai-le-ta-de-shi-dai-1006063835]] where local-services or ecommerce platforms organize branded meals inside canteen-like spaces. The source names [[MeituanHuanxiongShitang|美团浣熊食堂]] and [[JDCom|京东]]-linked efforts as examples of platforms moving into spaces that can substitute for office-park food courts or traditional food cities.

The model differs from the old [[FoodCourtSecondLandlord|food-court second landlord]] because the platform can bring app traffic, merchant data, ordering interfaces, and brand-selection rules. The source also flags a cost side: platform standards for renovation, environment, system integration, and fees can make a struggling food-city operator's payback harder even if the platform improves perceived quality.

## Key Claims
- Platform foodservice can turn delivery traffic and brand data into offline tenant selection.
- The space can resemble a canteen, food court, or ghost-kitchen frontage depending on customer and landlord needs.
- Platform entry may discipline weak food courts, but it can also shift cost and compliance pressure onto operators.
- The model competes with office-park food cities because it promises more branded, app-connected, standardized choices.
