# AI Amplifies Professionals

## Definition
AI amplifies professionals is the claim that AI does not simply make expertise obsolete; it magnifies the output of people who already understand the domain, constraints, and deployment standards.

## Current Synthesis
The current synthesis is a corporate talent version of AI leverage. [[LiXiangLiAuto|李想]] argues in [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]] that ordinary users may create unusable code with AI, while professionals can turn AI into much larger leverage because they know architecture, quality, deployment, and business constraints. The more interesting organizational problem is identifying workers whose thinking is strong but whose old-era communication, expression, or resource-grabbing ability hid their value.

## Key Claims
- AI leverage depends on domain judgment and deployment standards, not only prompt access.
- Old talent-evaluation systems can miss people who become unusually effective with agent tools.
- Companies should be careful about cutting people according to pre-AI productivity metrics.
- The "super individual" still needs organizational data, collaboration, and evaluation support.

## Evidence
- Professional leverage: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]] says professional workers using AI can move from being two or three times stronger to ten or one hundred times stronger.
- Non-expert limit: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]] says ordinary users' AI-generated code can be poor and dangerous at scale.
- Hidden talent: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]] says high-token internal users were often very smart but previously weaker at expression or resource competition.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This is a founder interpretation rather than a measured labor study. It may hold more strongly in technical or operational environments with good data and tools than in work where task definition, review, or accountability remains weak.
