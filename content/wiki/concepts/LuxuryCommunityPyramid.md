# Luxury Community Pyramid

Luxury community pyramid is the layered demand structure in [[ferrari-1]] where a brand has broad emotional participation at the base and tightly managed ownership at the top. [[Ferrari]]'s base is the [[Tifosi]] and global racing fandom; the top includes repeat collectors, special-series buyers, and clients who can access former F1 cars and extreme Ferrari programs.

The pyramid lets the brand be public without making the product common. Fans supply cultural energy, owners receive status and belonging, and the company uses events, clubs, track days, allocation, and service to turn ownership into a continuing relationship.

## Key Claims
- A large fan base can strengthen a luxury brand when fandom has a legitimate outlet separate from ownership.
- Top-client programs deepen loyalty because the scarce product becomes membership in a world.
- Community is costly to operate, but it can protect brand meaning better than passive advertising alone.
