# Labor Search Frictions

Labor search frictions are the practical costs of finding, applying for, quitting, and starting another job. [[the-real-horror-of-alien-and-how-it-explains-why-were-not-paid-enough]] uses [[ArinDube|Arin Dube]]'s explanation to show why workers do not instantly leave when pay or conditions are slightly worse than a competitive-market ideal.

The episode broadens [[LaborMonopsony]] beyond a single-company town. Commutes, coworkers, exhaustion, application costs, relocation, local market concentration, and [[NonCompeteAgreements]] can all make jobs sticky enough for employers to exercise wage and condition discretion.
