# Modern Chinese Academic Institution Building / 中国现代学术制度建设

Modern Chinese academic institution building is the source's claim that research greatness often appears as training, funding, rules, laboratories, surveys, publications, and organizational survival. In [[19-huangye-shang-de-dashi-wo-bei-yu-luanshi-qiu-yanjiu-ben-wei-niliu-zhi-wangju-565412437]], [[ZhangQuan|张泉]] treats the four institutional peaks in [[HuangyeShangDeDashi|《荒野上的大师》]] as achievements alongside individual scholarship.

The concept is especially clear in [[DingWenjiang|丁文江]] and [[FuSinian|傅斯年]]. Both are described as people whose administrative responsibilities advanced disciplines and protected younger scholars while consuming their own research completion. The episode therefore treats institution building as intellectual work with a cost, not as a lesser task outside scholarship.

## Key Claims
- Institutions make methods durable by training people, funding work, preserving evidence, and coordinating publication.
- The founder generation often had to build the road before walking far enough on it themselves.
- Administrative labor can be tragic when it advances a field at the expense of a scholar's own writings.
- [[AcademicFreedom]] and [[ScholarshipInWartime]] define whether institutions can protect serious inquiry under pressure.
