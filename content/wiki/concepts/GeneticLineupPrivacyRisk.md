# Genetic Lineup Privacy Risk

## Definition
Genetic lineup privacy risk is the civil-liberties problem created when a person's DNA profile remains searchable in a policing database and can also expose biological relatives.

## Current Synthesis
The episode uses Gleiberson's "perpetual genetic lineup" phrase to separate DNA databases from ordinary identity records. A retained CODIS profile can make a person repeatedly searchable in future criminal investigations, and because genetic information is shared across biological relatives, the exposure can extend beyond the person who was detained or sampled.

## Key Claims
- DNA database inclusion can create continuing investigation exposure rather than a one-time identity check.
- Biological relatives may be affected even if they were never detained, arrested, or sampled.
- The risk is amplified when removal review is unclear and profiles appear to be kept indefinitely.
- Genetic searchability makes immigration DNA collection a family and civil-liberties issue, not only an individual privacy issue.

## Evidence
- Lineup framing - [[tech-20260831-0831-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260831-0831-mp-tech-pod-128]] quotes Gleiberson describing DHS-sampled people and their relatives as placed in a perpetual genetic lineup.
- Retention issue - [[tech-20260831-0831-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260831-0831-mp-tech-pod-128]] says Georgetown's center has not found a regular process for reviewing and removing profiles.
- Searchability - [[tech-20260831-0831-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260831-0831-mp-tech-pod-128]] says CODIS is searched by policing agencies from federal to local levels.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not specify exactly how familial searching is configured across CODIS categories, nor does it include a government explanation of safeguards. The family-exposure claim should stay framed as a privacy-risk implication of searchable genetic data.

## What Changed
- Initial synthesis creates a concept for the family-exposure and continuing-search consequences of retained detainee DNA profiles.
