# Platform-Level Biotech BD

Platform-level biotech BD is the source's description of a 2025 deal-structure change in Chinese innovation drugs. In [[156-shengwu-yiyao-de-2026-dang-shichang-bu-zai-wei-bd-zaodong-zhongguo-yaoqi-de-xingchen-dahai-cai-ganggang-zhankai-lil-ugrzq8uvzviq3f8i-wm9ilup]], [[XiaoPTeacher|小P老师]] says earlier China-outbound BD often licensed one or two molecules, while 2025 saw more package, platform, and company-level cooperation.

The concept marks a shift from "project supplier" to "capability supplier." If an agreement covers many molecules or a technology platform, the buyer is not only purchasing a near-term asset; it is expressing belief that the partner can repeatedly generate useful drug candidates.

## Key Claims
- Platform-level BD suggests MNCs are evaluating Chinese R&D systems, not only isolated molecules.
- Larger and broader agreements can signal higher trust, but they also increase follow-through complexity.
- The model connects naturally to [[PlatformPipelineBiotechStrategy]]: owning a platform and owning assets create different value-capture choices.
- Platform BD feeds into [[BiotechBDDataValidationPhase]] because the broader the promise, the more future data and execution have to validate it.
