# Non-Sleep Deep Rest Recovery

## Definition
Non-sleep deep rest recovery is the use of guided relaxation or yoga nidra as a possible stress-regulation and restoration aid without treating it as a replacement for sleep or adequate training recovery.

## Current Synthesis
The AMA presents NSDR as a low-cost practice that may help replenish subjective mental and physical vigor during travel or after exercise. That makes it an optional recovery tool rather than a required protocol.

Its most useful diagnostic role may be indirect: if every workout or work bout creates a need for rescue, the underlying intensity, duration, sleep, stress, or schedule may be unsustainable. Rest practice can support a sound load; it should not normalize chronic overload.

## Key Claims
- NSDR or yoga nidra is presented as a possible way to downshift stress and restore subjective vigor.
- The practice can be used during travel or after exercise without requiring specialized equipment.
- NSDR is not established as a biological substitute for sufficient sleep.
- Repeated dependence on post-workout NSDR may signal excessive exercise intensity or duration.
- Sustainable training sometimes leaves capacity in reserve rather than pursuing maximum effort every session.

## Evidence
- Travel use - [[ama-16-sleep-vertigo-tbi-ocd-tips-for-travelers-gut-brain-axis-more-scim8512997965]] describes daily NSDR during travel for stress management and subjective recovery.
- Post-exercise use - [[ama-16-sleep-vertigo-tbi-ocd-tips-for-travelers-gut-brain-axis-more-scim8512997965]] presents NSDR after training as a possible recovery aid.
- Load signal - [[ama-16-sleep-vertigo-tbi-ocd-tips-for-travelers-gut-brain-axis-more-scim8512997965]] says needing it after every workout can indicate an overly demanding training dose.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source supplies no controlled effect sizes, physiological endpoints, optimal duration, or comparison with sleep, passive rest, meditation, or clinical treatment. Severe fatigue, exercise intolerance, sleep disruption, faintness, pain, or persistent decline requires assessment rather than additional relaxation audio.

## What Changed
- Created a bounded NSDR recovery concept that separates optional restoration from compensation for chronic overload.
