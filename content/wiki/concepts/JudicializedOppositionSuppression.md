# Judicialized Opposition Suppression

Judicialized opposition suppression is the use of courts, prosecutors, police, and legal procedure to weaken political challengers while preserving the surface language of legality. [[brave-new-whirl-turkeys-opposition-overhaul-6a69c7766043035f89eb097f]] presents [[Turkey]] as the case through the court removal of [[OzgurOzel]], the imprisonment of [[EkremImamoglu]], and the arrests of opposition mayors and officials.

The concept matters because it differs from a simple election ban. The source shows a layered approach: disqualify or imprison likely candidates, return a tame leader to the old party, storm party headquarters after supporters reject a ruling, and keep backup figures such as [[MansurYavas]] under threat. That makes [[AutocraticSuccession]] and electoral competition dependent on who remains legally able to run.

## Key Claims
- Legal process can reshape party leadership before voters choose between parties.
- Candidate removal can be more decisive than campaign persuasion if the strongest challenger is kept off the ballot.
- Courts and police can hollow out an opposition party while avoiding the optics of formally abolishing it.
- Rumors of future legal action can discipline backup candidates even before charges are filed.
