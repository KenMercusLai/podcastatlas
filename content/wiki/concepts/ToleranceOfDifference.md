# Tolerance of Difference / 容差异

## Definition
Tolerance of difference is the organizational and educational norm of protecting divergent people, ideas, and routes before they can be classified as right, wrong, useful, or wasteful.

## Current Synthesis
The source uses "容差异" as a sharper phrase than "tolerating failure." Yang Bin argues that failure already assumes judgment has occurred, while the harder and more creative task is to hold judgment long enough for difference to show what it might become.

The concept extends the wiki's constructive-dissent branch. It is not only about arguing against a decision; it is about sustaining heterogeneity before the decision frame itself is settled.

## Key Claims
- Difference needs protection before success or failure can be known.
- Premature judgment can kill routes that later become important.
- Psychological safety is concrete only when people can express non-mainstream ideas without status punishment.
- Education should let students develop distinct profiles rather than measure everyone by a single ruler.
- Tolerance of difference supports innovation but still needs shared direction, care, and serious engagement.

## Evidence
- Difference before error: [[401-bianyu-manju-manzou-women-haineng-yinglai-lingwai-yige-datemao-si-de-xiatian-ma-f7495e05]] has Yang criticize "容错" and argue that the harder thing to tolerate is difference.
- Delayed judgment: [[401-bianyu-manju-manzou-women-haineng-yinglai-lingwai-yige-datemao-si-de-xiatian-ma-f7495e05]] says people should hold judgment and avoid rushing to choose winners.
- Psychological safety: [[401-bianyu-manju-manzou-women-haineng-yinglai-lingwai-yige-datemao-si-de-xiatian-ma-f7495e05]] summarizes the Dartmouth atmosphere as free from excessive hierarchy, correctness, and face concerns.
- Education extension: [[401-bianyu-manju-manzou-women-haineng-yinglai-lingwai-yige-datemao-si-de-xiatian-ma-f7495e05]] says teachers should help different students show their own forms rather than evaluate by one scale.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not argue for unlimited indulgence or absence of standards. It preserves the need for deep thinking, shared purpose, and later evaluation; the point is timing and openness before early variation is erased.
