# Strategic Recognition Alignment / 谋略知己式归附

Strategic recognition alignment / 谋略知己式归附 is the pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-4-jiuquangui-zhangliang-ruhe-zai-liubang-shouxia-anshen-lp6qwg3ma5hdy15g4box7pe23jav|Qinji 129-4]] draws from [[ZhangLiang|张良]]'s first attachment to [[LiuBang|刘邦]]. Zhang Liang is originally going to join [[JingJu|景居 / 景驹]], but after meeting Liu Bang he changes direction and joins Liu Bang instead.

The source explains the choice through use-value and recognition rather than immediate power. Zhang Liang repeatedly speaks about [[TaigongBingfa|《太公兵法》]], and Liu Bang can understand the arguments and adopt them. Zhang Liang had tried to tell others the same things without being understood, so Liu Bang's comprehension becomes a decisive form of recognition.

This concept is adjacent to [[RetainerReciprocityEthic|士为知己者死]], but it is narrower and less death-centered. Its focus is the moment when a strategist selects a patron because the patron can process the advice, not only because the patron honors him, feeds him, or gives him rank.

It also differs from [[TalentAttractionThroughRespect|礼贤下士式人才吸附]]. Respect may draw talent to a ruler's door; strategic recognition keeps the talent there because counsel can become action.

## Key Claims
- A strategist's loyalty can begin with being understood before it becomes formal office or material reward.
- Recognition is practical: the patron must be able to grasp, adopt, and act on difficult counsel.
- A weaker patron may outcompete stronger claimants if the match between advice and decision-making is better.
- The source frames Liu Bang's ability to understand Zhang Liang as an early seed of their later ruler-strategist partnership.
