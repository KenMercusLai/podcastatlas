# Yoga Nidra and Sankalpa Belief Change

## Definition
Yoga nidra and sankalpa belief change is [[AlokKanojia]]'s source-scoped proposal that a deeply relaxed, focused state may make entrenched self-beliefs more open to a carefully formed resolve than ordinary surface-level affirmation does.

## Current Synthesis
The episode describes yoga nidra as a hypno-yogic state combining body-awareness rotation, parasympathetic downshifting, and one-pointed attention. Sankalpa is the resolve introduced within that state. Kanojia argues that the pairing may reach patterns that ordinary repetition does not, while hypnagogic and hypnopompic states offer related but harder-to-use windows.

The synthesis separates three levels of confidence. Relaxation and a subjective state shift can be immediate practical outcomes. Improved learning or belief revision is a stronger empirical claim requiring controlled comparison. Editing the unconscious, mystical experience, and deep identity transformation remain interpretive or personally meaningful claims rather than established consequences of one practice.

## Key Claims
- Yoga nidra combines guided body awareness with deep relaxation and narrowed attention.
- Sankalpa is a resolve embedded in that state rather than a surface affirmation alone.
- Focused attention and strong emotion can both increase the salience of learning.
- Immediate downshifting is a narrower claim than durable belief or identity change.
- Hypnagogic and hypnopompic states may be psychologically interesting but are difficult to use deliberately.
- Mystical meaning and scientific evidence should remain distinct even when both matter to a practitioner.

## Evidence
- Practice structure - [[unlearn-negative-thoughts-behaviors-patterns-dr-alok-kanojia-scim4778698454]] describes rotation of body awareness, parasympathetic activation, and a trance-like state.
- Resolve mechanism - [[unlearn-negative-thoughts-behaviors-patterns-dr-alok-kanojia-scim4778698454]] distinguishes sankalpa placed within yoga nidra from repeated affirmation at ordinary waking awareness.
- Learning proposal - [[unlearn-negative-thoughts-behaviors-patterns-dr-alok-kanojia-scim4778698454]] connects one-pointed attention to learning and compares traumatic learning with intense emotional focus.
- Evidence boundary - [[unlearn-negative-thoughts-behaviors-patterns-dr-alok-kanojia-scim4778698454]] explicitly leaves some mystical claims personally meaningful but scientifically uncertain.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The supplied note does not identify trials comparing sankalpa with ordinary affirmations, hypnosis, psychotherapy, meditation, passive rest, or sleep. Trauma formation does not prove that a relaxed practice can safely reverse trauma, and suggestibility can introduce error as well as change. Yoga nidra is not a substitute for sleep or clinical care; people with trauma, dissociation, psychosis risk, or destabilizing meditation responses may need qualified guidance.
