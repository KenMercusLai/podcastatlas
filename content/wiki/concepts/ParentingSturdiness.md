# Parenting Sturdiness

## Definition
Parenting sturdiness is the capacity to remain connected to one's own values, needs, and adult responsibilities while also staying warm and connected to a child whose feelings or preferences differ.

## Current Synthesis
[[BeckyKennedy]] presents sturdiness as the alternative to both harsh control and anxious permissiveness. The adult does not need agreement before acting and does not treat a child's distress as proof that the decision is wrong. At the same time, authority does not require denial, ridicule, fear, or emotional abandonment. The parent holds the decision and the relationship together.

Sturdiness is especially visible under emotional pressure. An adult can stop unsafe or unwanted behavior, tolerate protest, avoid retaliating against hurtful words, and repair after losing control. With highly reactive children or separating adolescents, this communicates that strong emotion is survivable and that the relationship remains available without making the child responsible for adult stability.

## Key Claims
- Healthy parental authority requires simultaneous self-connection and child-connection.
- A child's distress can be real without becoming evidence that an adult limit is mistaken.
- Firmness without warmth risks fear and disconnection; warmth without adult responsibility risks permissiveness and child-led family decisions.
- The adult's regulated action carries more authority than repeated demands for compliance.
- Sturdiness includes repair after adult failure; it does not require emotional perfection.
- Safety duties can require action even when an adolescent strongly rejects the parent.

## Evidence
- Core definition: [[protocols-for-excellent-parenting-improving-relationships-of-all-kinds-dr-becky-kennedy-scim7905596498]] defines sturdiness as connection to self and other at the same time.
- Emotion tolerance: [[protocols-for-excellent-parenting-improving-relationships-of-all-kinds-dr-becky-kennedy-scim7905596498]] recommends communicating “I am not scared of your feelings” to children overwhelmed by their own intensity.
- Decision responsibility: [[protocols-for-excellent-parenting-improving-relationships-of-all-kinds-dr-becky-kennedy-scim7905596498]] lets parents validate reluctance while retaining responsibility for family plans and safety.
- Imperfection and repair: [[protocols-for-excellent-parenting-improving-relationships-of-all-kinds-dr-becky-kennedy-scim7905596498]] treats apology and changed intention as sturdy conduct after yelling.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Sturdiness is a practical relational framework, not a validated diagnostic construct or a guarantee of child outcomes. Regulation capacity is constrained by exhaustion, poverty, disability, trauma, violence, and unsafe co-parenting. Warm firmness does not substitute for professional assessment when a child or teen is self-harming, violent, severely withdrawn, using substances dangerously, or showing major functional decline.
