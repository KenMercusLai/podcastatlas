# Supermarket Canteen / 超市食堂

Supermarket canteen is the retail-foodservice pattern described in [[vol-270-da-shi-dai-liu-zai-le-ta-de-shi-dai-1006063835]] through [[Hema|盒马]], Yonghui, Wumart, and similar supermarket meal counters. The source says supermarkets use seafood steaming, low-priced boxed meals, staple foods, and in-store dining to attract traffic, fill midday valleys, and increase customer dwell time.

The episode's key distinction is that supermarket meals do not have to behave like standalone restaurants. A grocer already has ingredients, cold chain, and clearance pressure; foodservice can convert near-expiry or discounted products into ready meals while also making the whole store more useful.

## Key Claims
- The supermarket can subsidize or justify foodservice through total store traffic, not only dish-level margin.
- Existing grocery supply chains give supermarkets an input advantage over many food-court operators.
- In-store meals can turn idle time periods into reasons to visit.
- The model competes with food courts by offering convenience and price without requiring a separate second-landlord layer.
