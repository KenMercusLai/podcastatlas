## Definition

Role attachment and power exit / 权力角色依附与退场 names the problem of treating a temporary office, rank, or attention position as one's permanent identity after the role has shifted or ended.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-558-ai-zhuang-caihuiying-wangmang-you-duo-hui-yinren-llvrlct7laohoo54s3lhzgwvsfc9|Hanji 558]] introduces the concept through the episode's "人生如戏" frame. [[LiuXiang|刘向]] is sincere and morally serious, but his confidence in imperial trust, his defense of the Liu house, and his criticism of the Wang family do not overcome the actual promotion field controlled by Wang-family, chancellor, and censor support. The episode then generalizes: high office and management authority are roles people pass through, and refusing to release the role after leaving the center of attention produces frustration and social misreading.

A harsher negative version appears through [[ChunyuZhangWesternHan|淳于长]]. After [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] initially only dismisses him and sends him to his fief, Chunyu Zhang cannot accept the loss of court access and tries to buy restoration through [[WangLiWesternHan|王立]]. That effort makes the case suspicious, triggers deeper investigation, and turns a survivable exit into prison death.

## Key Claims

- Political and managerial authority are role positions, not stable personal essence.
- Sincere moral speech can still fail when the surrounding support structure has changed.
- The danger is not only losing power, but continuing to act as if the old audience, authority, or backing remains.
- Role exit requires self-location: understanding when one is no longer at the center can prevent needless collision.
- A failed exit can reopen a case that was politically survivable when the first punishment was limited.

## Evidence

Temporary role versus durable identity:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-558-ai-zhuang-caihuiying-wangmang-you-duo-hui-yinren-llvrlct7laohoo54s3lhzgwvsfc9|Hanji 558]] uses acting and management analogies to argue that a person should not remain mentally trapped in a role after the scene has changed.

Support-structure failure:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-558-ai-zhuang-caihuiying-wangmang-you-duo-hui-yinren-llvrlct7laohoo54s3lhzgwvsfc9|Hanji 558]] says Liu Xiang's promotion repeatedly fails despite Chengdi's interest because Wang-family, chancellor, and censor support are absent.

Survivable demotion turned fatal:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-557-wangmang-pk-chunyuchang-nanrenjian-de-jingxin-jiaoliang-lnvmnizx0mhrln-g1p1a8u_9kgnx|Hanji 557]] says Chunyu Zhang could have survived dismissal by returning to his fief, but his gift-backed effort to regain office through Wang Li leads to renewed investigation and death.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept is source-scoped to two episode frames. It should not be read as a claim that Liu Xiang's remonstrance was merely vanity, that moral persistence is always a failure to exit a role, or that every demoted official can safely retreat; the sharper point is about role awareness under changed power conditions.
