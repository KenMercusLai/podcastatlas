# Algorithmic Media Literacy / 算法媒介素养

Algorithmic media literacy is the practical ability to notice how platforms shape what one sees, feels, reacts to, and feeds back into the system. [[164-suanfa-de-tuzi-dong-weishenme-ni-zong-zai-kanwan-xinwen-hou-huaxiang-yule-duitan-huangshengchun-jiaoshou-lo6ik8cj2njvu-cy2jdj-kl1jpuq]] frames this as the ordinary user's response to recommendation systems: do not reduce algorithms to good or bad, but recognize when a feed is training attention, emotion, and behavior.

The concept turns media critique into practice. If a user clicks, watches, comments, likes, searches, or keeps scrolling, they are giving signals to [[AlgorithmicPredictionLoop]] and [[PlatformFeedbackLoop]]. Literacy therefore includes deciding when not to react, when to cross-check sources, and when to curate inputs before the feed becomes the default picture of reality.

[[167-bailatu-lusuo-hayeke-alunte-sida-zhexuejia-hui-ruhe-jieshi-suanfa-shidai-chuantai-dushu-buchenglin-lmbsdbfeqqfz5vm3g67b-wjgdjfja]] adds a philosophical version of the same practice: the user may not leave the algorithmic cave, but can recognize that the shadows are projected, ask how selection works, and compare filters before treating a feed as the world itself.

[[271-tangchao-douyao-bucunzai-le-weishenme-weishilun-hui-zai-jinnian-da-baofa-lg7-wdrdw1b9h7lwa4vwkvmyb4un]] adds a pseudohistory case. The episode's "editor/search/algorithm worlds" frame makes literacy a source-checking practice: when a feed repeatedly surfaces [[PseudoHistoryConspiracy|伪史论]], the user needs to ask what professional community, archive, or cross-checking route exists outside the recommendation stream.

## Key Claims
- Algorithmic literacy begins with awareness of one's own feedback behavior.
- Not commenting on rage bait can be an active intervention rather than passivity.
- Users should compare platforms because different [[PlatformAffordance|affordances]] and ranking incentives reveal different blind spots.
- Literacy includes civic judgment: ask whether public information is being displaced by [[AlgorithmicEntertainmentRedirect]] or distorted by [[AlgorithmicAmplification]].
- Episode 167 adds that literacy includes knowing that one is inside a cave-like media structure and resisting the temptation to mistake ranking for reality or judgment.
- Episode 271 adds that literacy includes knowing when a feed has turned fringe historical reversal into apparent public common sense.
