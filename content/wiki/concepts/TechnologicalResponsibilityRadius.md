# Technological Responsibility Radius / 技术责任半径

## Definition
Technological responsibility radius is the episode's claim that when human capacity expands through tools, war, capital, satellites, AI, data, chips, or other systems, the field of consequences for which humans must answer expands too.

## Current Synthesis
[[episode-228-suoyi-aodesai-8211241331-711602]] builds this concept by moving from [[MetisHeroicCunning]] to modern technology. Odysseus' ability to build a raft or invent the [[TrojanHorse]] shows the power of "having a way," but the source insists that successful means do not end the story. The wooden horse opens Troy, and after that come slaughter, guilt, return, and the impossibility of leaving the scene.

The same logic carries into the source's reading of [[SuoyiSong|《所以》]]. GPT, AI, satellites, data, chips, climate pressure, capital, and war are not treated as external fate. They are consequences of enlarged human action, and they require delayed recognition: people often need distance before they can see what a new capacity has made.

## Key Claims
- A capability is ethically incomplete until its downstream consequences are faced.
- Cleverness and technical success can widen responsibility instead of settling the problem.
- Responsibility often appears after delay because people need distance to see what a capacity has produced.
- Irreversibility is the condition of ethics: if everything could simply be undone, technical correction would replace moral responsibility.
- AI-era and data-era problems should not be described only as backlash from neutral tools; they are part of the human field of action.
- Staying with the scene after harm is a responsibility demand even when the harmful actor is collective or hard to name.

## Evidence
- Odyssey case: [[episode-228-suoyi-aodesai-8211241331-711602]] links Odysseus' metis, raft-making, Trojan Horse, and postwar return to consequences that cannot be escaped.
- Song case: [[episode-228-suoyi-aodesai-8211241331-711602]] reads "行凶后别逃离现场" as an open command addressed to humans, technology companies, capital, creators, listeners, or all of them at once.
- AI/system density: [[episode-228-suoyi-aodesai-8211241331-711602]] treats GPT, AI, satellites, data, chips, climate, and war as signs of expanded human action rather than merely scenery.
- Irreversibility: [[episode-228-suoyi-aodesai-8211241331-711602]] argues that if every action could be revoked, the world would need only technology rather than ethics.
- Distance and hindsight: [[episode-228-suoyi-aodesai-8211241331-711602]] uses [[EarthrisePhoto|Earthrise]], [[WholeEarthCatalog]], and the phrase "future's past" to show that responsibility becomes clearer from a changed vantage point.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is a cultural essay, not a policy framework. It does not specify how to allocate liability among individuals, firms, states, markets, or technical systems, and its "radius" language should not be read as a precise legal doctrine.

## What Changed
- Created the concept for the episode's claim that expanded technical capacity creates an expanded ethical field.
