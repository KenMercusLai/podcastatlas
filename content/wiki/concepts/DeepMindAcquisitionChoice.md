# DeepMind Acquisition Choice

DeepMind Acquisition Choice is the episode's interpretation of why [[DemisHassabis]] chose [[Google]] over [[Facebook]] when selling [[DeepMind]]. In [[e226-liaoliao-deepmind-chuangshiren-hasabisi-yige-kexuejia-yu-shikong-de-ai-jingsai-7abda28b-99c6-4ebc-8c0d-37bcc77f6a73]], [[MarkZuckerberg]] is described as making an attractive offer but treating AI as one technology among VR, AR, and 3D printing, while [[LarryPage]] is described as making Google feel like a better home for the AGI mission.

The concept captures an acquisition as more than price. The episode says DeepMind needed compute, resources, scientific culture, independence, and AI safety commitments. It also says outside paths through [[ElonMusk]], [[ZhouKaixuan]], [[PeterThiel]], and [[FoundersFund]] mattered because they made Google notice the company and made the AGI project financable before acquisition.

## Key Claims
- DeepMind's sale is framed as a mission-and-resource decision, not only a founder liquidity event.
- The source treats Google's scientific culture and compute as decisive advantages over Facebook's broader technology portfolio framing.
- Acquisition made AlphaGo and AlphaFold possible at scale, but also created the later independence and commercialization tensions around [[GoogleDeepMind]].
- The estimated sale price is presented as cheap in hindsight because [[AlphaGo]], [[AlphaFold]], [[Gemini]], and related scientific prestige later made DeepMind far more strategically important.
