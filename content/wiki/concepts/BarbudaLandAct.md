# Barbuda Land Act

[[BarbudaLandAct]] is the 2007 law that [[sand-heists-and-property-rights-in-the-caribbean-summer-school]] says formalized Barbudans' collective land ownership. It turns [[CommunalLandTenure]] from only historical practice into a legal institution.

The episode places the act inside later conflict. [[GastonBrowne]] argues that Barbudans' communal ownership belief is a myth and that the law violated the constitution, while Barbudan residents rely on the same legal history to defend collective authority over development.

## Key Claims
- A law can formalize a commons without eliminating future constitutional or development conflict.
- The act matters because it gives Barbudans a legal vocabulary for collective ownership, not only a customary one.
- Post-disaster title reform can reopen apparently settled institutional arrangements when capital access, reconstruction, and foreign investment become urgent.
