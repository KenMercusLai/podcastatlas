## Definition

见几而作式风险退出 / incipient-risk exit is a judgment pattern in which a small behavioral change is interpreted as evidence that the underlying power relationship has deteriorated, prompting withdrawal before danger becomes explicit.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-232-wuwang-liubi-pingshenme-gan-faqi-qiguo-zhiluan-lqtwfjr6oknd9pgdtvnnz61rs1f1|Hanji 232]] creates the concept through [[MuXianshengWesternHan|穆先生]]. The relevant evidence is not the missing sweet wine alone but its position inside a repeated practice of rulerly accommodation inherited from Liu Jiao. When [[LiuWuChuKingWesternHan|刘戊]] stops maintaining it, Mu infers that the ethical basis of service is weakening.

The concept joins perception to timing. Mu leaves before an explicit threat, while Shen and Bai remain and are later humiliated after remonstrating. That sequence makes delay costly inside the episode, but hindsight also creates a qualification: later violence cannot turn every minor lapse into a dependable warning. A defensible use of the pattern therefore asks whether the signal belongs to a broader change in respect, constraint, and tolerance for dissent.

## Key Claims

- Small deviations matter when they reveal a change in a stable relational pattern, not merely because they are unusual.
- Risk judgment can require action before proof is complete, because waiting for overt coercion may remove the ability to exit safely.
- Retrospective confirmation strengthens a narrative case but can also hide false positives and encourage overgeneralization.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept currently rests on one retrospective historical anecdote. The later outcome validates Mu inside that telling but does not provide a comparison set of harmless lapses, alternative explanations, or failed predictions. It should therefore be used as a structured prompt to inspect pattern change, power asymmetry, and exit costs, not as a rule that every slight forecasts persecution.

## What Changed

- Established a distinction between an isolated slight and a deviation from a stable relationship pattern.
- Added timing and reversibility of exit as part of risk judgment.
- Made hindsight bias and false positives explicit limits on the lesson.
