# Chinese Online Game Commercialization

Chinese online game commercialization is the business-system pattern made concrete by [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]]. Through [[Shanda|盛大]] and [[LegendOfMir|《传奇》]], the episode shows that a hit game needed an operating stack: agency rights, servers, bandwidth, internet-cafe installation, prepaid cards, customer service, anti-cheat work, community management, and capital to scale with demand.

The concept matters because the early Chinese online-game market was not ready-made. [[ChenTianqiao]]'s contribution was to turn a product from [[Actozsoft]] and [[WeMade]] into a China-specific commercial machine, then to use that machine as a launchpad for listing, content expansion, and later [[FreeToPlayGameMonetization]] experiments.
