## Definition

Military Reward Eunuch Gatekeeping / 宦官门槛下的军功赏罚 is the late-Han pattern in which frontier merit does not map cleanly to title or reward because palace-access actors and eunuch favor shape the reward channel.

## Current Synthesis

The earlier Huangfu Gui case is the more coercive form. After [[HuangfuGuiLateHan|皇甫规]]'s frontier pacification and recall, he should be in line for marquisate-level recognition, but [[XuHuangLateHan|徐璜]] and [[ZuoGuanLateHan|左悺]] repeatedly send intermediaries around his merit review to imply a demand for gifts. Huangfu refuses to pay or apologize, after which accusation and punishment pressure revive. Here the gatekeeping mechanism is not only reduced reward; it is solicitation backed by the threat that old charges can be made politically effective. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-857-xuehui-gongying-cai-shi-zhenzheng-de-yingjia-lh3zx6pw-4ib7owiz8c68bv-9htn|Hanji 857]]

The later Zhang Huan case shows reward reduction after victory. [[ZhangHuanLateHan|张奂]] defeats [[XianlingQiang|先零羌]] forces attacking the Sanfu region and should receive marquisate-level reward, but the source says he receives only money and one family appointment because he refuses to flatter eunuchs. Zhang then refuses the reduced package and asks to move his household registration to Hongnong. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-878-bu-shou-yueshu-de-quanli-hairen-buqian-lvevnahcxshgbz5zyqbaf36pa7nk|Hanji 878]]

Together the cases clarify a specific failure mode inside late-Han military governance. Military performance remains real, and reward is not always absent, but the translation from frontier merit to durable honor is filtered through court access. That filter can demand side payments, revive accusation, reduce title, or substitute lesser compensation, making the reward system less legible as justice and more legible as loyalty to the palace-access channel.

## Key Claims

- Battlefield success can become politically discounted when the commander lacks eunuch-side access or refuses flattery.
- Gatekeeping can operate before reward as gift solicitation and accusation pressure, not only after reward as reduced compensation.
- Gatekeeping does not always erase reward; it can reduce, reformat, delay, or redirect reward away from title and honor.
- A commander's refusal of solicitation or reduced reward can become a reputation statement against the distorted system while increasing personal risk.
- The pattern helps connect frontier policy to inner-court access politics rather than treating border campaigns as separate from palace power.

## Evidence

- Pre-reward solicitation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-857-xuehui-gongying-cai-shi-zhenzheng-de-yingjia-lh3zx6pw-4ib7owiz8c68bv-9htn|Hanji 857]] says Xu Huang and Zuo Guan repeatedly send guests around Huangfu Gui's merit review, which the source reads as implied gift extraction.
- Accusation pressure after refusal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-857-xuehui-gongying-cai-shi-zhenzheng-de-yingjia-lh3zx6pw-4ib7owiz8c68bv-9htn|Hanji 857]] says Huangfu refuses to pay or apologize, after which old accusations revive and he is jailed until petitions and amnesty release him.
- Reward distortion: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-878-bu-shou-yueshu-de-quanli-hairen-buqian-lvevnahcxshgbz5zyqbaf36pa7nk|Hanji 878]] says Zhang Huan defeats the Qiang attackers but does not receive the expected marquisate because he does not flatter eunuchs.
- Reduced package: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-878-bu-shou-yueshu-de-quanli-hairen-buqian-lvevnahcxshgbz5zyqbaf36pa7nk|Hanji 878]] says Zhang receives money and one family appointment instead of the expected higher reward.
- Refusal and workaround: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-878-bu-shou-yueshu-de-quanli-hairen-buqian-lvevnahcxshgbz5zyqbaf36pa7nk|Hanji 878]] says Zhang refuses the package and asks for household registration relocation to Hongnong.
- Cross-source reward channel: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-857-xuehui-gongying-cai-shi-zhenzheng-de-yingjia-lh3zx6pw-4ib7owiz8c68bv-9htn|Hanji 857]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-878-bu-shou-yueshu-de-quanli-hairen-buqian-lvevnahcxshgbz5zyqbaf36pa7nk|Hanji 878]] together show the same late-Han failure mode through solicitation before recognition and downgraded recognition after victory.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The two cases should not be collapsed into a single proven procedure. Hanji 857 names eunuch actors and implied gift pressure, but the source's exact account figures and intermediary steps remain episode-bounded; Hanji 878 does not name the exact eunuch actor or procedural step that blocks Zhang Huan's marquisate. Zhang also receives some compensation and a special registration exception, while Huangfu's subordinates temporarily calm the matter through payment and apology. The stable claim is filtered, coercive, or reduced reward, not total exclusion from every form of court recognition.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 857's Huangfu Gui case as a pre-reward solicitation and retaliation variant.
- Distinguished gift-extraction pressure before reward from reduced/reformatted reward after victory.
- Reframed the concept around several gatekeeping outputs: side payments, accusation revival, title reduction, and lesser compensation.
