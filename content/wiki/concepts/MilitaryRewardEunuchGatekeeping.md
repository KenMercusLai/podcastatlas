## Definition

Military Reward Eunuch Gatekeeping / 宦官门槛下的军功赏罚 is the late-Han pattern in which frontier merit does not map cleanly to title or reward because palace-access actors and eunuch favor shape the reward channel.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-878-bu-shou-yueshu-de-quanli-hairen-buqian-lvevnahcxshgbz5zyqbaf36pa7nk|Hanji 878]] grounds the concept in [[ZhangHuanLateHan|张奂]]'s 167 CE victory over [[XianlingQiang|先零羌]] forces attacking the Sanfu region. The source says Zhang should have received marquisate-level reward but was reduced to money and one family appointment because he refused to flatter eunuchs. Zhang then refused the reduced package and asked to move his household registration to Hongnong.

The concept clarifies a specific failure mode inside late-Han military governance. Military performance remains real, and reward is not absent, but the translation from battlefield merit to durable honor is filtered through court access. That filter makes the reward system less legible as justice and more legible as loyalty to the palace-access channel.

## Key Claims

- Battlefield success can become politically discounted when the commander lacks eunuch-side access or refuses flattery.
- Gatekeeping does not always erase reward; it can reduce, reformat, or redirect reward away from title and honor.
- A commander's refusal of the reduced reward can become a reputation statement against the distorted system.
- The pattern helps connect frontier policy to inner-court access politics rather than treating border campaigns as separate from palace power.

## Evidence

- Reward distortion: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-878-bu-shou-yueshu-de-quanli-hairen-buqian-lvevnahcxshgbz5zyqbaf36pa7nk|Hanji 878]] says Zhang Huan defeats the Qiang attackers but does not receive the expected marquisate because he does not flatter eunuchs.
- Reduced package: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-878-bu-shou-yueshu-de-quanli-hairen-buqian-lvevnahcxshgbz5zyqbaf36pa7nk|Hanji 878]] says Zhang receives money and one family appointment instead of the expected higher reward.
- Refusal and workaround: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-878-bu-shou-yueshu-de-quanli-hairen-buqian-lvevnahcxshgbz5zyqbaf36pa7nk|Hanji 878]] says Zhang refuses the package and asks for household registration relocation to Hongnong.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source does not name the exact eunuch actor or procedural step that blocks the marquisate. Zhang also receives some compensation and a special registration exception, so the stable claim is filtered and reduced reward, not total exclusion from court recognition.

## What Changed

- Created this concept from Hanji 878's Zhang Huan reward case.
- Distinguished battlefield merit from the inner-court gatekeeping that translates merit into title.
