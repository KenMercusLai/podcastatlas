# 妈妈会 / Mama Hui

妈妈会 is the village women's organization described in [[144-nvxia-duan-sihe-he-tade-jiaxiang-mapingguan-women-ceng-yiwei-you-dian-you-lu-zhishi-meng-linmjqt-n-sa5sarpqgdxgtoeejt]]. [[DuanSihe|段四合]] presents it as common in [[BaiPeople|白族]] villages and as a group with strong mobilizing power around ritual, prayer, needs expression, and women's participation in public affairs.

In the source, 妈妈会 matters because it gives [[Mapingguan|马坪关]] women an existing social form through which they can gather, speak, organize, and later connect to activities such as dance, performance, Mandarin practice, and child-related work.

## Key Claims
- 妈妈会 is presented as a local institution, not merely a private mothers' chat group.
- Its ritual and social functions can become governance-adjacent when women use it to express needs and coordinate action.
- The source connects 妈妈会 to [[RuralWomenPublicParticipation]] because public speech often starts inside familiar village forms.
- The page is source-scoped because the episode does not provide a general history of 妈妈会 across Bai communities.
