# Automated Performance Marketing

Automated performance marketing is the shift from human media buyers manually tuning campaigns toward systems that encode bidding, budget, placement, campaign structure, and optimization rules. In [[quanmian-yazhi-buliu-kongdang-zijie-tiaodong-ruhe-zuo-zengzhang-zijie-tiaodong-di-7-ji-lqszvmur6jv8b9xt8rjgmwfdqtec]], [[XuHongliang]] says [[ByteDance]] was already doing this in 2019-2020, using terms such as bot or automation before today's AI language became common.

The concept explains how growth knowledge becomes infrastructure. When a machine learns or codifies投手 behavior, a small team can manage more markets, media channels, campaign variants, and spend than manual work would allow.

[[tsr-ycoffsite-kasishgupta-v1-audioonly-tsr-ycoffsite-kasishgupta-v1-audioonly]] adds an adjacent enterprise-marketing branch through [[Hightouch]]. [[KashishGupta]] describes [[AIMarketingDecisioning]] that uses customer data and reinforcement learning to choose better messages for individual customers, aiming to reduce message volume while improving relevance.

[[ep240-ba-zong-tian-chong-zai-haiwai-duanju-chuhai-de-chanye-mima-ltvxvfj5ko6djhsjatiphdy-dzek]] adds a short-drama overseas case. A marketing company in the source uses AI and algorithms to identify which creative clips fit which regions and age groups, then recommends stopping or increasing spend based on performance metrics. The source presents this as a way to reduce the burden on human optimization workers while preserving the need for judgment over [[CreativeMaterialIndustrialization|creative material]].

## Key Claims
- Automation can reduce dependence on individual投手, but it may create resistance when frontline staff feel their know-how is being extracted.
- Automated buying requires clean links among media APIs, campaign hierarchy, creative material, budget rules, and ROI attribution.
- It is most useful when the product has enough spend, conversion data, and repeatable optimization targets for the system to learn from.
- Automation does not remove the need for [[CreativeMaterialIndustrialization]] or [[GrowthRiskControl]]; it can amplify both good and bad inputs faster.
- Customer-data-driven decisioning extends marketing automation from media buying and budgets into message selection, lifecycle timing, and governed customer-data use.
- In overseas short drama, automated performance marketing can turn campaign monitoring into a regional segmentation and stop-loss system, but only if ad-platform feedback is timely and reliable.
