# Portable Divination Systems / 便携占算系统

## Definition

Portable divination systems / 便携占算系统 are procedures that compress a larger cosmology into repeatable prompts, layouts, counts, directions, or judgments usable in a concrete moment of uncertainty.

## Current Synthesis

The historical overview groups 六壬 hand-joint counting, 奇门遁甲, 文王课, coin divination, chess-piece lots, and signed lots as interfaces to larger structures of yin-yang, stems and branches, trigrams, palaces, seasons, and judgment phrases. Portability does not necessarily mean simplicity of theory; it means that a user can obtain or interpret a bounded output without reconstructing the whole cosmology each time.

The [[KunlunShrineGhostBlowsOutTheLight|《昆仑神宫》]] discussion adds a popular-literary account of 奇门遁甲. It distinguishes direction-oriented layers such as the eight gates from other plates and repeats “急从神，缓从门” as a rule for urgent versus slower decisions. The episode also warns prospective learners to investigate historical transmission and distrust expensive courses or unverifiable lineage claims. Both sources show social usability, not predictive validation.

## Key Claims

- Divination systems can scale by becoming portable and procedural.
- Simplification increases use while preserving the authority of a larger cosmology.
- Ambiguous judgments create room for interpretation and for specialists who explain them.
- The source links divination to both technical learning cost and everyday decision pressure.
- Popular literature and media can teach selected components as situational rules while leaving the full system opaque.
- Claims of lineage or instructional authority require scrutiny because procedural accessibility also creates a market for unverifiable expertise.

### Procedural compression

- [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-jie-shangji-704051550]] groups hand-joint calculation, coins, lots, 文王课, 六壬, and 奇门遁甲 as smaller interfaces to extensive cosmological systems.

### Situational rules and popular transmission

- [[113-zhai-fu-gui-chuideng-zhi-kunlun-shengong-dajieju-lgfedg0shv4w7ugzbdqfhzdxnrqw]] presents the eight gates as an audience-legible layer, uses the southeast/杜门 association to discuss the “东南角点烛” motif, and cites “急从神，缓从门” as a conditional decision rule.

### Authority and market risk

- [[113-zhai-fu-gui-chuideng-zhi-kunlun-shengong-dajieju-lgfedg0shv4w7ugzbdqfhzdxnrqw]] advises learners to study historical transmission and be wary of costly courses and disputed lineages.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Neither source demonstrates predictive efficacy. The historical overview is broad and synthetic, while the 《昆仑神宫》 episode uses occult systems mainly as literary and cultural context. The southeast-candle explanation and “急从神，缓从门” formulation remain source-scoped rather than established origins or universally accepted rules.
