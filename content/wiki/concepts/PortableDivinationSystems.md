# Portable Divination Systems / 便携占算系统

Portable divination systems / 便携占算系统 names the source's interest in how complex cosmologies become quick decision devices. In [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-jie-shangji-704051550]], 六壬 hand-joint counting, 奇门遁甲, 文王课, coin divination, chess-piece lots, and later signed lots compress yin-yang, stems and branches, trigrams, palaces, seasons, and judgment phrases into usable procedures.

The concept is not a claim that the systems work predictively. Its wiki value is social and epistemic: divination becomes popular when it gives users a repeatable interface for uncertainty, interpretation, and delegated judgment.

## Key Claims
- Divination systems can scale by becoming portable and procedural.
- Simplification increases use while preserving the authority of a larger cosmology.
- Ambiguous judgments create room for interpretation and for specialists who explain them.
- The source links divination to both technical learning cost and everyday decision pressure.
