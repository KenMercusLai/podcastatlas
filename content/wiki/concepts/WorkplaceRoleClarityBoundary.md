## Definition

Workplace role clarity boundary / 职场角色清醒边界 is the practice of knowing one's role, value, authority, limits, and core target inside an organization so personal approval, dislike, intimacy, or emotional dependence does not replace work judgment.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 534 creates the concept from the host's deliberately provocative "渣男心态" phrase. The episode explicitly says the phrase is not meant as moral praise for cruelty or irresponsibility. It is a shorthand for role clarity in a hierarchy: workers should not make a boss's liking the whole measure of whether to stay, and leaders should not let private affection, spouse involvement, or kinship proximity rewrite institutional rules.

The historical case gives the governance warning. [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]'s desire to elevate [[ZhaoFeiyanWesternHan|赵飞燕]] requires status engineering through Zhao Lin's enfeoffment and [[ChunyuZhangWesternHan|淳于长]]'s mediation with [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]. When [[LiuFuWesternHan|刘辅]] criticizes the private-desire problem, Chengdi's secret punishment shows what happens when personal face overpowers office-role feedback. The modern company anecdote converts that into enterprise language: intimate partners entering management and finance without clear roles can intensify shareholder and executive conflict.

## Key Claims

- Role clarity separates work value from whether a superior personally likes the worker.
- Emotional detachment in this concept means goal-directed boundaries, not contempt or irresponsibility.
- Leaders create governance risk when spouse, kin, favorite, or private affection changes authority and reward without transparent role design.
- Workers and executives need to know their contribution, bottom line, and decision scope before reacting to emotional signals.
- KPI or performance language is useful only when it clarifies responsibility rather than erasing ethics, evidence, and institutional limits.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source's phrase is intentionally attention-grabbing and should not be generalized into cynical conduct. Role clarity still permits care, loyalty, trust, and relationship building when they are subordinate to explicit responsibility and correctable process. The modern enterprise example is anecdotal and source-scoped; the wiki uses it as an illustration of boundary failure, not as a verified business case study.
