# Solo Aging Planning / 单身养老规划

Solo aging planning is the core frame of [[vol-118-danshen-renshi-yanglao-zhinan-bushi-gudu-de-daimingci-shi-gengzao-zhangwo-rensheng-de-zhudongquan-lhvkuveznxd4citlwf5marzaj-wt]]. The episode argues that single or solo later life should be planned as a full life system, not reduced to whether a person has a spouse, children, or a large savings balance.

The frame has five practical dimensions: wealth, residence, health, relationships, and emotions. The episode then adds a sixth implicit dimension, death preparation, because a solo elder may need clearer arrangements for emergency discovery, legal mandates, medical wishes, and the social shame attached to "孤独死".

## Key Claims
- Single aging is relevant even for married people, because children often live separately and one partner in a couple usually outlives the other.
- Planning should start before late old age; the episode points to people beginning retirement-life design around age 40.
- [[RetirementCashFlowSecurity]] addresses the fear of outliving money, but money alone cannot replace housing, care, relationships, or emotional readiness.
- [[AgingInPlaceSupportSystem]] and [[AgeFriendlyInfrastructure]] decide whether "staying at home" is realistic rather than merely romantic.
- [[ElderRelationshipCapital]] reframes friendship, relatives, neighbors, and community as long-term assets.
- [[CognitiveDeclineAdvancePlanning]] and [[SoloDeathPreparedness]] make late-life autonomy concrete before capacity is lost.
