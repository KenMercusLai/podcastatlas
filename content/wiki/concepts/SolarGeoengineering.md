# Solar Geoengineering

Solar geoengineering is the deliberate attempt to make Earth reflect slightly more sunlight. In [[taking-the-shine-off-albedo-and-global-temperatures-6a730fdaca067b295bab9760]], [[OliverMorton]] introduces it after explaining [[AlbedoFeedback]], [[AerosolCoolingMasking]], and the rising [[EarthEnergyImbalance]].

The source's specific reference is [[PaulCrutzen]]'s 2006 proposal to place reflective sulphate particles higher in the atmosphere, trying to preserve some cooling effect while avoiding the direct health burden of low-level sulphur pollution. Morton says he is not a fan of geoengineering and frames it as controversial, not a substitute for emissions cuts or [[CarbonRemoval]].

## Key Claims
- Solar geoengineering responds to warming by changing reflectivity rather than by directly removing greenhouse gases.
- The sulphate-aerosol version borrows from the observed cooling effect of pollution while trying to change where the particles sit.
- The source treats the idea as a high-stakes governance question because aerosol masking will fade as polluted places clean their air.
