## Definition

The Tanglong Coup is the 710 coup in the current source base led by [[LiLongjiTang|李隆基]] against the [[EmpressWeiTang|韦后]] faction, during which [[ShangguanWaner|上官婉儿]] is executed despite presenting an edict meant to prove Li-Tang alignment.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-536-3-jiemi-shangguan-waner-he-ta-beihou-de-nanrenmen-lqj7rpwwfllogrxrbav-xqwwnnnm|Hanji 536-3]] treats the Tanglong Coup as the endpoint of Shangguan Wan'er's factional balancing. After [[TangZhongzong|唐中宗]] dies, Empress Wei appears ascendant; Shangguan Wan'er doubts the faction's durability and tries to preserve a fallback by placing [[LiDanTang|李旦]] in the power core. When Li Longji moves militarily, that paper trail is not enough: the episode reads her death as the defeat of multi-sided survival by a winner demanding absolute dynastic loyalty.

## Key Claims

- The coup follows Zhongzong's death and Empress Wei's attempt to control the post-death order.
- Li Longji leads the military action that clears the Wei faction.
- Shangguan Wan'er tries to use an edict associated with Li Dan and Princess Taiping as proof of her position.
- The edict fails because Li Longji reads her record through factional loyalty rather than technical document wording alone.
- The coup exposes the limit of survival strategies based on keeping several exits open.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

This page does not settle the full military, legal, or historiographical account of the Tanglong Coup. It records the episode's interpretation of the coup as the fatal test of Shangguan Wan'er's political hedging.
