# Class Mobility Performance / 阶级上升表演

Class mobility performance is the pattern in which a person must perform the manners, beliefs, humility, credentials, or institutional loyalty expected by a higher-status world in order to move upward. [[21-hong-yu-hei-ta-si-yu-zhencheng-569042001]] makes this visible through [[JulienSorel|Julien Sorel]] in [[Stendhal|Stendhal]]'s [[RedAndBlackNovel|《红与黑》]].

Julien does not become a seminarian because the source presents him as deeply religious; he uses religion because [[FrenchRestoration|Restoration France]] has narrowed the heroic military path associated with [[NapoleonBonaparte|Napoleon]]. His black robe, memorized Latin, lowered posture, and Paris manners are therefore social tools as well as moral compromises.

The concept is not an excuse for manipulation. It names the structural pressure that makes falseness rational while also tracking the cost: a person trained to perform endlessly may lose access to ordinary sincerity, or may erupt when an intimate bond seems to expose the performance as pure fraud.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-33-lishi-diyi-shuangwen-nanzhujiao-suqin-lvebjyhvzctjgaevwwoddpvg9nr0]] adds an adjacent pre-Qin case through [[SuQin|苏秦]], though the mechanism is different. Su Qin's [[QianjuHougongReversal|前倨后恭]] homecoming is not about learning elite manners; it is about how public rank, money, and royal reception cause others to reclassify the same person almost instantly.

## Key Claims
- Mobility can require adopting the codes of a world one inwardly despises.
- Performance becomes morally corrosive when it forces a person to hide contempt, desire, faithlessness, or wounded pride all the time.
- A class performance can succeed externally while failing internally if the performer still needs recognition as a real person.
- The pattern helps explain why [[SincerityAgainstSocialHypocrisy]] becomes explosive in the source.
- Status recognition can also arrive from outside the performer: Su Qin's family changes behavior after visible office and wealth, not after discovering a new inner self.
