# Generic Drug Trust Rebuilding / 仿制药再信任

Generic drug trust rebuilding is the episode's explanation for why low price alone did not make Japanese patients accept generics. In [[vol-120-riben-yiliao-tixi-de-bengkui-yu-zhongsheng-yiyuan-yaopin-yibao-sanjiao-kunju-de-yizhong-jiefa-lp-p5p1cybbldphe0qajteggsitt]], the first low-price cycle damaged quality and public confidence, so later substitution required better quality, different pricing, pharmacy involvement, and industry clearing.

The source treats generic substitution as a social and professional trust problem. Patients need to believe that a cheaper drug is not inferior, and that belief is easier to build when [[PharmacistAsSecondDoctor|pharmacists]] can explain medication choices and monitor ongoing use.

## Key Claims
- Generic-drug uptake depends on quality credibility, not only insurance incentives.
- Excessive low-price competition can create adverse selection among manufacturers.
- Trust rebuilding requires a visible professional interface between patient, doctor, pharmacy, and insurer.
