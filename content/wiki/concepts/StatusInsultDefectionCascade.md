# Status Insult Defection Cascade / 名分羞辱触发叛变

Status insult defection cascade / 名分羞辱触发叛变 is the pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-2-lishishang-hejiu-neng-wu-duo-da-shi-zhuan-gei-jiugui-laogong-kan-zhe-ji-lvzxpoxclwdrr66bwejd-bga8dp|Qinji 129-2]] draws from [[LiLiangZhaoGeneral|李良]]'s betrayal of [[WuChen|武臣]]. The source does not make the defection a simple "drinking caused disaster" story. It shows a preloaded situation: [[WangLiQin|王离]] has already offered Li Liang a path back to Qin, Li Liang is blocked from taking Taiyuan, and restored Zhao is still unstable.

The insult supplies the trigger. [[ZhaoWuchenSister|武臣姐姐]] is drunk and fails to return the ritual respect Li Liang expects after he bows by the roadside. Li Liang's followers then broaden the slight into a future-status argument: if Wu Chen's sister already treats him as beneath her, Li Liang and his men will have even less standing once Zhao is secure.

The cascade is therefore social and strategic at the same time. A local face injury activates a military choice, a Qin recruitment channel, and a restored-state legitimacy problem. The result is not only revenge against the sister, but an attack on [[Handan|邯郸]] and the killing of Wu Chen.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1012-haose-caocao-luanshi-zhong-de-dute-qingshi-llvrucihuv2buvp1-lisz4usbprh|Hanji 1012]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1013-dianwei-zhisi-jingshi-caocao-haose-haileta-lhe6jrpjp3cfbdhvjtdiezijfnoh|Hanji 1013]] add a late-Han Wancheng variant through [[ZhangXiu|张绣]]. Cao Cao's taking of [[LadyZouWancheng|邹氏]], the widow of [[ZhangJiLateHan|张济]] and Zhang Xiu's aunt by marriage, insults Zhang Xiu immediately after surrender. Hanji 1013 then adds Cao Cao's reward to [[HuCheEr|胡车儿]] and leaked plan to kill Zhang Xiu, giving the insult a direct survival edge. The result is not only resentment; it becomes a pre-emptive military break planned with [[JiaXu|贾诩]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1025-bai-yu-caocao-lvbu-cuo-zai-jinjiuling-ljjt1t2mk77si6re-wew-hpl8qz|Hanji 1025]] adds a Xiapi suspicion variant through [[HouChengLateHan|侯成]]. Hou Cheng's wine-meat offering should affirm hierarchy by giving [[LyuBu|吕布]] the first portion, but Lü Bu's alcohol ban and distrust recode it as a plot. The insult here is not a public rank slight like Li Liang's or Zhang Xiu's; it is being treated as a likely traitor inside a besieged command, which pushes Hou Cheng toward [[SongXianLateHan|宋宪]], [[WeiXuLateHan|魏续]], and surrender to [[CaoCao|曹操]].

## Key Claims
- Hanji 1012 and Hanji 1013 add that status insult becomes more volatile when it is paired with a fresh surrender and a credible threat to the insulted actor's command network or life.
- Hanji 1025 adds that suspicion can create a status insult inside command hierarchy when a loyalty gesture is treated as conspiracy.
- Status injuries become politically dangerous when the injured actor already has another coalition option.
- Ritual mistakes matter more under unstable hierarchy because they can be read as forecasts of future exclusion.
- Subordinates can escalate an insult by converting it from personal embarrassment into group survival reasoning.
- Alcohol is an amplifier in the source's story, not the only cause; the deeper mechanism is honor, uncertainty, and defection opportunity.
