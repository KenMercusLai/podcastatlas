# Education Accountability Protest

Education accountability protest is the pattern in [[cockroaches-1-modi-0-indias-remarkable-protests-6a6729a33e2f0b6f92d26ab8]] where exam integrity, public education quality, job scarcity, and youth precarity become direct political accountability demands. The episode uses [[NationalEligibilityCumEntranceTest|NEET]] leaks and [[DharmendraPradhan]]'s resignation to show how a technical education failure can become a government-responsibility claim.

The concept is useful because the protest is not only about one leaked paper. The source links the leak to too few good colleges, too few jobs after study, reported student suicides, repeated exam leaks over a decade, and public education spending below the stated goal.
