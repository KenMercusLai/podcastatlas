# Relational Isolation Control / 关系孤立控制

Relational isolation control / 关系孤立控制 is the mechanism in [[18-ruhe-shibie-taoli-bing-bimian-chengwei-jingshen-xixuegui-563629000]] where a harmful person weakens the victim's outside relationships so the victim loses witnesses, reality checks, and practical exit support. The episode says psychic vampires often speak badly of friends and relatives, or create narratives that make the victim withdraw from anyone who might notice the pattern.

The host's counter-image is "daylight": keep facts public, keep social links alive, preserve evidence, and stop protecting secrets that only make the manipulative relationship darker and more enclosed.

## Key Claims
- Isolation makes self-doubt stronger because the victim has fewer external checks on the harmful person's story.
- The pattern can appear in intimate relationships, family dependency, and workplaces with opaque power.
- Secrecy often protects the controlling person more than it protects the relationship.
- Rebuilding outside contact is part of safety, not a betrayal of the relationship.
