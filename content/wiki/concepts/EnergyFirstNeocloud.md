# Energy-First Neocloud

Energy-first neocloud is the [[Crusoe]] thesis in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-the-future-of-everything-what-ceos-of-circle-crowdstrike-more-see-coming-in-2026-39870920]]: AI cloud capacity should be located and financed around abundant energy, not only around traditional network hubs. The source places Crusoe in the [[NeoCloud]] category with [[CoreWeave]], but makes power development, substations, gas plants, renewables, batteries, and future SMRs part of the product strategy.

The concept extends [[TokenFactoryAIInfrastructure]] and [[DataCenterPowerBottleneck]]. A neocloud can compete on GPU operations and model-serving specialization, but an energy-first neocloud treats power generation, rack density, construction labor, gas-turbine supply, project debt, and customer leases as equally central to whether tokens can be produced at scale.

## Key Claims
- AI cloud specialization can move down the stack into energy development and construction execution.
- Power-rich locations can become AI infrastructure hubs even when they are not traditional cloud-network centers.
- Customer leases, project debt, onsite power, and equipment supply decide whether a proposed AI factory is financeable.
- Batteries, hydro, geothermal, and SMRs are treated as strategic compute inputs when dense GPU clusters behave like large electrical loads.
