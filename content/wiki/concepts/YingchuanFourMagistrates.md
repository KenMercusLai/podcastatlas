## Definition

颍川四长 / Yingchuan Four Magistrates is the source label for four late-Han Yingchuan-associated county heads, [[HanShaoLateHan|韩韶]], [[ZhongHaoYingchuanLateHan|钟皓]], [[XunShuLateHan|荀淑]], and [[ChenShiLateHan|陈寔]], remembered for virtuous local governance and benefit to the people.

## Current Synthesis

The concept currently rests on Hanji 842 and should be read as a reputation pattern rather than a complete collective biography. The episode first gives Han Shao's concrete case: during rebellion and refugee hunger, his reputation deters bandits and his granary decision saves displaced households. It then names Zhong Hao, Xun Shu, and Chen Shi with Han Shao as the Four Magistrates, moving the judgment from one official's emergency choice to a county-level ideal.

The important synthesis is that late-Han local office can become a moral and practical prestige site. County heads are not treated merely as low-level administrators. In crisis, the right magistrate can lower violence, shelter displaced people, and make public stores serve survival before paperwork. The source does not prove that all four acted the same way; it shows that they were remembered together under a shared local-governance standard.

## Key Claims

- The Four Magistrates label turns county-level service into a public moral reputation field.
- Han Shao's granary relief supplies the cohort's clearest current example of practical people-benefiting governance.
- Yingchuan functions as a shared origin or reputation marker linking otherwise separate local officials.
- The concept joins routine 德政 and emergency responsibility rather than treating good governance as only ordinary kindness.
- The current evidence supports cohort membership but not a detailed comparison among all four officials.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source note provides a strong label but uneven detail. Han Shao receives a full crisis anecdote, while Zhong Hao, Xun Shu, and Chen Shi are named as cohort members without equivalent source-specific examples in this episode. The concept should therefore not rank the four or infer identical governing methods.
