# Short-Lived Digital Employment Empowerment / 短期数字就业赋能

## Definition
[[ShortLivedDigitalEmploymentEmpowerment]] describes technology projects that produce real short-term gains in income, working conditions, or access but do not build the skills, credentials, institutions, or protections needed to sustain those gains after demand changes.

## Current Synthesis
The Yonghe annotation base was neither simply empowerment nor simply exploitation. It offered mothers rare office work, regular schedules, and peak earnings far above local services, while also imposing production pressure, health costs, and hazardous review.

Its developmental limit became visible after basic orders declined. The women had helped create AI capability but generally did not leave with credentials or specialist skills that matched the next wave of annotation demand. A credible empowerment claim therefore requires a transition test: what remains when the project, subsidy, customer, or technology changes?

## Key Claims
- Temporary material improvement should be recognized rather than erased by later decline.
- Job counts and peak wages are insufficient measures of durable empowerment.
- Portable skills, credentials, bargaining power, safety, and transition support determine whether gains persist.
- Care-constrained workers face greater downside when local projects disappear.
- Philanthropic or poverty-relief branding should not obscure reciprocal value extracted from workers.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not evaluate every sponsoring program, participant outcome, training curriculum, subsidy, or later career. Some workers may retain useful digital familiarity or household gains not captured here. “Short-lived” describes the reported county-wide employment window, not a proof that every individual benefit vanished.

## What Changed
- Added a durability test separating immediate employment access from long-term empowerment.
- Added reciprocal labor value and post-project transition as checks on one-sided aid narratives.
