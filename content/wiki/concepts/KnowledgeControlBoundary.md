## Definition

Knowledge-control boundary is the distinction between understanding or predicting an event and possessing the power to prevent, produce, or govern its outcome.

## Current Synthesis

[[Prometheus]] embodies the boundary because foresight does not let him avoid punishment, escape his chain, or cancel necessity. His practical arts still matter: medicine, navigation, writing, number, and craft can improve response and enlarge agency without making every known process controllable.

The same distinction applies to contemporary prediction. A model can estimate disease, default, or economic risk from historical data, but a probability is neither a guaranteed event nor a complete intervention plan. More precise output can increase the temptation to confuse measurement with destiny.

## Key Claims

- Prediction and control are different capabilities.
- Knowledge can improve preparation and action without guaranteeing results.
- Probability describes uncertainty; it does not convert a possible future into necessary fate.
- Recognizing limits need not produce fatalism, because bounded knowledge can still support resistance and choice.
- Technical power cannot by itself settle which outcomes deserve pursuit.

- Tragic case: [[370-jishu-geile-renlei-mangmu-de-xiwang-ma-beifude-puluomixiusi-lveku27utltloytqi8ycq-nvurhd]] presents Prometheus as foresighted yet unable to avoid or immediately end his punishment.
- Practical arts: [[370-jishu-geile-renlei-mangmu-de-xiwang-ma-beifude-puluomixiusi-lveku27utltloytqi8ycq-nvurhd]] treats human arts as real improvements that remain weaker than necessity.
- Predictive systems: [[370-jishu-geile-renlei-mangmu-de-xiwang-ma-beifude-puluomixiusi-lveku27utltloytqi8ycq-nvurhd]] distinguishes machine-learning probability from inevitability.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The boundary should not be read as saying knowledge lacks causal power. Forecasting can change behavior, enable prevention, or create self-fulfilling and self-defeating feedback. The source offers a philosophical distinction, not a quantitative account of when prediction improves control.

## What Changed

- Created a reusable distinction among foresight, preparation, intervention, and mastery.
- Added probability-versus-destiny as the contemporary predictive-system boundary.
