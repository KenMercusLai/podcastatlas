# Science Communication Trust Repair

## Definition
Science communication trust repair is the source's frame for rebuilding public trust by having scientists communicate as humans, explain uncertainty, acknowledge failure, and make evidence accessible without flattening nuance.

## Current Synthesis
[[MaxKrummel]] connects his public writing to concern about the relationship between science and society. The episode's trust problem is practical: vaccines, pharma incentives, mandates, adverse experiences, and scientific uncertainty can become social flashpoints when institutions communicate as if questions are illegitimate or harms are unheard.

The repair route is not abandoning evidence. It is explaining how scientists know what they know, what remains uncertain, why failures happen, and why many scientists and medical workers are still trying to do careful work inside imperfect institutions.

## Key Claims
- Trust improves when scientists communicate uncertainty and failure rather than only success.
- Accessible analogies can help public understanding when they preserve complexity.
- People harmed or worried by medical decisions need to feel heard before evidence claims can land.
- Pharma incentives can damage trust even when individual scientists or employees are doing good work.
- Public-health communication should distinguish evidence, values, mandates, and personal risk questions.
- Science communication must avoid both overconfidence and anti-scientific cynicism.

## Evidence
- Public writing motive: [[how-your-immune-system-works-how-to-improve-it-dr-max-krummel-scim7009523546]] says Krummel's public writing grew from concern about science and society.
- Vaccine and pharma trust: [[how-your-immune-system-works-how-to-improve-it-dr-max-krummel-scim7009523546]] discusses mandates, poor communication, corporate incentives, and families who feel unheard as distrust sources.
- Communication method: [[how-your-immune-system-works-how-to-improve-it-dr-max-krummel-scim7009523546]] argues that scientists should communicate as humans, use accessible formats and analogies, and explain uncertainty and failure.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept does not claim that every public objection is scientifically valid. It says trust repair requires evidence-based communication that still takes questions, tradeoffs, and adverse experiences seriously.
