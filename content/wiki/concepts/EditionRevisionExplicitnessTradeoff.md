## Definition

The edition revision explicitness–motive tradeoff describes how textual revision can clarify desire, continuity, or immediate causation while also reducing ambiguity, creating downstream inconsistency, or removing the motive that once powered later action.

## Current Synthesis

The [[Azi|阿紫]] case shows revision as selective causal repair. Added harassment makes flight from 丁春秋 more understandable, and consolidating her fixation on [[XiaoFeng|萧峰]] creates a steadier emotional line. Yet the history remains layered because [[NiKuang|倪匡]]'s substitute-serialization blindness plot survives inside Jin Yong's later reworking, and earlier attractions do not belong equally to every edition.

[[BixueJian|《碧血剑》]] shows two opposite costs. Making [[YuanChengzhi|袁承志]] and [[AjiuBixueJian|阿九]] embrace, kiss, commit, wait, and remember one another strengthens visibility but makes Yuan's final nonselection harder to explain. Removing [[HeTieshou|何铁手]]'s mistaken love for [[WenQingqing|温青青]] changes representation and simplifies the relationship field, yet apprenticeship alone supplies a thinner motive for breaking with the Five Poisons Cult and repeatedly following Yuan.

Revision should therefore be evaluated across the whole causal chain. A locally clearer scene may improve immediate intelligibility while weakening restraint, later choice, or action continuity elsewhere.

## Key Claims

- Revision can strengthen immediate motive without producing global narrative coherence.
- Greater romantic explicitness can reduce productive ambiguity and increase the burden on a later nonselection or separation.
- Deleting a troubling or unwanted relationship line may also remove the causal energy behind institutional exit and later pursuit.
- Retained material from serialization or coauthorship can be repurposed rather than fully erased.
- Edition-specific claims should not be merged into a composite character psychology without qualification.
- The relevant unit of evaluation is the full action-and-consequence chain, not only the revised scene.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The evidence comes from conversational recollection rather than line-by-line collation of all serialized, collected, and revised texts. Increased explicitness is not inherently inferior, and deleting a motive may serve ethical, tonal, structural, or audience goals not captured by causal economy alone. Claims about authorial intention remain source-scoped unless supported by direct documentation.

## What Changed

- Created a cross-novel framework for evaluating revision across local clarity, ambiguity, motive, continuity, and later consequence.
- Added a boundary against merging incompatible editions into one stable character psychology.
