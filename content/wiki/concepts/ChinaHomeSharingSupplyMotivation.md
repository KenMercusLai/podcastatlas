# China Home-Sharing Supply Motivation / 中国家庭共享住宿供给动机

## Definition
China home-sharing supply motivation is the episode's thesis that ordinary homeowners become willing to rent spare bedrooms only when household cash-flow pressure, housing expectations, and trust infrastructure make hosting strangers feel worth the social and operational cost.

## Current Synthesis
The concept explains why an Airbnb-like product can fail in one macro period and become more plausible in another. In the source's account, China's earlier rising home-price and income expectations weakened spare-room supply, while falling property values, mortgage burdens, unemployment risk, and short-term local mobility now make extra monthly income more valuable.

## Key Claims
- Spare-bedroom supply is a household cash-flow response before it is a platform feature.
- Rising property and income expectations can suppress hosting motivation because the income from a bedroom feels too small relative to perceived wealth growth.
- Falling housing expectations, mortgage pressure, and unemployment risk can reopen the boundary between private home and income-generating asset.
- The relevant market is not limited to first-tier cities; provincial capitals and lower-tier cities may matter because cash-flow pressure is geographically broad.
- Supply motivation still needs trust, payment, compliance, and guest-fit mechanisms before it can become durable marketplace inventory.

## Evidence
- Macro timing: [[vol-272-2026-nian-le-zenme-hai-you-ren-yao-zuo-zhongguo-ban-airbnb-1008880863]] contrasts the U.S. 2008 crisis origin of [[Airbnb]] with China's 2012-2020 rising property and income expectations.
- Household pressure: [[vol-272-2026-nian-le-zenme-hai-you-ren-yao-zuo-zhongguo-ban-airbnb-1008880863]] says some homeowners now face falling asset values, mortgage burdens, reduced income expectations, and family unemployment, making RMB 2,000-level extra income meaningful.
- Regional scope: [[vol-272-2026-nian-le-zenme-hai-you-ren-yao-zuo-zhongguo-ban-airbnb-1008880863]] frames [[YijianCiwo|一间次卧]]'s core market as cities such as Hangzhou, Chengdu, Changsha, and Wuhan rather than only Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, or Shenzhen.
- Marketplace boundary: [[vol-272-2026-nian-le-zenme-hai-you-ren-yao-zuo-zhongguo-ban-airbnb-1008880863]] links supply motivation to payment custody, room verification, host feedback, and guest safety rather than assuming homeowners automatically become reliable hosts.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source's macro statistics and host composition claims are episode-reported and not independently verified here. The concept also does not imply that distress alone creates a good marketplace: regulation, property rights, landlord consent, neighborhood tolerance, safety, and platform operations can still block supply.
