# 电商平台与快递网络互相依赖

## Definition
Platform-express interdependence is the two-sided relationship in which ecommerce platforms supply concentrated orders and data while parcel carriers supply cheap, dense, nationwide fulfillment.

## Current Synthesis
[[Taobao]] changed carrier economics by aggregating price-sensitive sellers and dense delivery destinations; [[Pinduoduo]] and livestream commerce later added enormous volumes of low-value parcels. Carriers gained utilization and scale, but became exposed to platform order entry, merchant price comparison, and data control. Platforms gained fulfillment reach but could not fully control timeliness or service quality, prompting deeper logistics coordination and investment.

## Key Claims
- Dense ecommerce volume raises route and courier productivity.
- Platform merchants turn small price differences into carrier switching pressure.
- Data interfaces improve routing while increasing dependency.
- Equity links do not remove platform-carrier conflicts over control and service.

## Evidence
- Volume and pricing: [[no-219-kuaidi-jianghu-sanshinian-tonglubang-taobao-pinduoduo-yu-jiage-zhan-1015471748]] follows Taobao's discounted carrier access and Pinduoduo's parcel growth.
- Data and ownership: [[no-219-kuaidi-jianghu-sanshinian-tonglubang-taobao-pinduoduo-yu-jiage-zhan-1015471748]] describes [[Cainiao|菜鸟]], electronic waybills, and Alibaba investments in major carriers.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The relationship is not exclusive: carriers serve multiple platforms and other logistics segments, while platforms can build warehouses or diversify partners. Reported order shares and investment amounts remain source-scoped.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to capture reciprocal value and bargaining dependence.
