## Definition
Technology philosophy / 技术哲学 is the inquiry into how technologies mediate human beings, life-worlds, knowledge, agency, relationships, values, and social institutions rather than remaining neutral external tools.

## Current Synthesis
The episode introduces technology philosophy through [[WangXiaowei|王小伟]]'s account of science and technology philosophy, then narrows to the relation between technology, people, and everyday life. In this frame, AI is not judged only by whether it gives useful answers. It matters because it enters companionship, self-analysis, work rhythms, education, model values, governance, and moral advice.

The synthesis is that technology philosophy is a life-world method for AI. It asks what a tool changes in the user: attention, patience, vulnerability, judgment, pace, relationship expectations, and the background sense of what counts as normal life. That makes it a bridge between technical AI debates and concepts such as [[AICompanionAuthenticityBoundary]], [[AIJudgmentInfrastructure]], [[LifeTimeVsMachineTime]], and [[HumanAgencyUnderAI]].

## Key Claims
- Technology should not be treated as neutral once it reorganizes human agency, relationships, judgment, and self-understanding.
- AI is a philosophical issue because it mediates intimacy, work, education, emotion, and governance, not only because it automates tasks.
- Life-world effects include smoothness, optimization, dependence, bodily absence, time compression, and changed expectations of being understood.
- The value of human friction, waiting, vulnerability, and embodied context becomes clearer when technology tries to remove them.
- Technology ethics can draw on older philosophical traditions, including Confucian and virtue-ethical vocabularies, without treating those traditions as decorative add-ons.

- Work-time case - [[zai-keji-shidai-zhongxin-lijie-womende-ai-gongzuo-yu-shenghuo-ljjrm6-ovwduouaecvymkbeyuvuqu]] contrasts machine-paced efficiency with life rhythms that contain coffee, chatting, walking, and pauses.
- Governance case - [[zai-keji-shidai-zhongxin-lijie-womende-ai-gongzuo-yu-shenghuo-ljjrm6-ovwduouaecvymkbeyuvuqu]] links AI governance to safety, law, ethics, consciousness, moral status, privacy, copyright, and minors' use.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept is introduced through a public podcast interview and should not stand in for a full disciplinary history of philosophy of technology. The source is strongest as an applied AI-era life-world frame.
