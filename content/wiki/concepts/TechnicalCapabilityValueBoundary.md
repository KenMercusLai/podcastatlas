## Definition

Technical capability-value boundary is the distinction between knowing how to make or optimize something and judging whether its purpose, deployment, distribution, or consequences are worth accepting.

## Current Synthesis

[[Hephaestus]] can forge an effective chain but does not thereby gain authority to decide whether [[Prometheus]] should be bound. The episode uses this scene as a compact model of technical work under power: a system can function accurately and still serve surveillance, coercion, or another contested objective.

The boundary also limits broad promises about technology. Extending life does not decide what makes life worth living; improving production does not decide how gains should be distributed; optimizing a task does not establish that the task deserves completion. These remain questions of judgment, institutions, consent, and responsibility.

Pandora adds the stronger case of a system that works precisely as commissioned. Multiple divine specialists give her form, skills, attraction, and persuasive capacity, and [[Epimetheus]] accepts her; functional success is therefore compatible with a purpose designed to harm the recipient. Evaluation must reach upstream to objective setting and downstream to consequences, not stop at performance.

A user-side purpose failure appears when a person acquires unusual power without acquiring more worthy ends, then spends it on spectacle, status, humiliation, and distraction. [[FaustusMarlowe|Faustus]] makes the boundary applicable even when a capability is not imposed by an external commander: desire satisfaction, efficiency, and expanded options cannot independently establish what a life or project should serve.

Goethe's Faust adds a collective-planning case. Land reclamation and efficient reconfiguration promise a freer future, yet the project treats an existing home, memories, and ways of living as obstacles. The resulting deaths are not redeemed by Faust's claim that he requested removal rather than violence. The boundary therefore reaches both specification and objective choice: improving instruction-following can reduce divergence between command and action, but cannot establish that growth, efficiency, or total planning is a worthy command.

## Key Claims

- Effectiveness is not proof of ethical legitimacy.
- Makers may possess implementation power without authority over goals.
- Technical neutrality is weakened when design choices make a contested purpose easier to execute.
- Prediction of consequences does not equal control of consequences.
- Value judgment must address purpose, consent, distribution, and acceptable risk.
- A system can meet its specification while making the specification itself the central ethical failure, especially when the objective excludes concrete lives or nonquantified goods.
- Capability can magnify trivial or harmful desire when the user has not examined the purpose being served.

## Evidence

- Maker under command: [[370-jishu-geile-renlei-mangmu-de-xiwang-ma-beifude-puluomixiusi-lveku27utltloytqi8ycq-nvurhd]] uses Hephaestus's reluctant chain-making to separate skill from purpose.
- Surveillance analogy: [[370-jishu-geile-renlei-mangmu-de-xiwang-ma-beifude-puluomixiusi-lveku27utltloytqi8ycq-nvurhd]] notes that accurate recognition and tracking do not establish the acceptability of comprehensive monitoring.
- Life and distribution: [[370-jishu-geile-renlei-mangmu-de-xiwang-ma-beifude-puluomixiusi-lveku27utltloytqi8ycq-nvurhd]] distinguishes longer life and higher efficiency from good life and just allocation.
- Successful harmful design: [[369-panduola-shi-renlei-zuizao-de-keji-emeng-ma-hesie-de-gongzuo-yu-shiri-lkr0ibzoden-ip-es5xpnhnbvz7]] presents Pandora's attractiveness and effectiveness as serving Zeus's retaliatory objective rather than legitimizing it.
- User-side purpose failure: [[372-dang-keji-nenggou-manzu-yiqie-yuanwang-women-hai-zhidao-ziji-xiangyao-shenme-ma-maluo-fushide-boshi-de-beiju-ltehzo-lehi9vpgy5ukexbcrtcpr]] shows Faustus moving from grand plans to pranks and spectacle after acquiring extraordinary means.
- AI and biotechnology analogy: [[372-dang-keji-nenggou-manzu-yiqie-yuanwang-women-hai-zhidao-ziji-xiangyao-shenme-ma-maluo-fushide-boshi-de-beiju-ltehzo-lehi9vpgy5ukexbcrtcpr]] separates efficient work, longer life, bodily modification, and predictive precision from judgments about good work, good bodies, or good lives.
- Aligned execution of a bad goal: [[378-gede-ruhe-kandai-tongyong-rengong-zhineng-lp2zydrd_nxvglwfojhf443i_wwa]] uses Faust's land-reclamation project to show that efficiency and obedience can destroy homes and lives when the objective cannot value attachment, memory, plurality, or refusal.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The distinction does not imply that technical facts are irrelevant to ethics or that values can ignore feasibility. Design, error rates, reversibility, access, security, and operational constraints materially change ethical evaluation. Mythic and dramatic analogies frame the boundary but do not supply a complete governance procedure or establish facts about modern AI systems. The Faust case also depends on one interpretation of a literary project and does not show that planning or economic growth is inherently destructive. Desire and purpose can improve through new capability; the claim is that improvement is not automatic.

## What Changed

- Added Goethe's Faust as the case where progress and efficient planning erase concrete lives and nonquantified goods.
- Distinguished correcting execution from questioning the human objective itself.
