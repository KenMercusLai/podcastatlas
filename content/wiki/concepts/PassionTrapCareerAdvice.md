# Passion Trap Career Advice

Passion trap career advice is the source's critique of telling young people to simply follow their dreams or passions. [[brave-new-whirl-turkeys-opposition-overhaul-6a69c7766043035f89eb097f]] presents this as a weak guide for Gen Z graduates because many do not yet know what they want, and many passions do not map to enough paid work.

The episode uses [[BenjaminTodd]] and [[EightyThousandHours|80,000 Hours]] as the alternative. Rather than treating passion as the input, the reviewer favors building competence at useful work, helping others, and allowing passion to develop later.

## Key Claims
- Passion can be real without being a viable labor-market strategy.
- Young graduates may need experiments and skill-building before they can know what they want.
- Advice to follow passion can obscure job availability and the difference between hobbies and work.
- Passion is more durable when it follows mastery, usefulness, and contribution.
