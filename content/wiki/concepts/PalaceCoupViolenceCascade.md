# Palace Coup Violence Cascade / 宫廷政变暴力级联

Palace coup violence cascade / 宫廷政变暴力级联 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-946-zhichang-douzheng-shi-yourouguaduan-you-duo-zhiming-lpyyweaajonzg7kxuhwm9z7ae7p5|Hanji 946]] as the chain that follows [[HeJin|何进]]'s killing. One targeted palace assassination triggers forged appointments, gate assault, arson, false edicts, targeted killings, indiscriminate eunuch slaughter, He-family revenge, imperial abduction, and exposed flight.

The pattern is not simply "violence causes more violence." The source shows several transmission channels. [[ZhangRangLateHan|张让]] and [[DuanGuiLateHan|段珪]] use palace access and claimed orders; [[YuanShu|袁术]] and [[WuKuangLateHan|吴匡]] answer blocked gates with force and fire; [[YuanShao|袁绍]] and [[YuanWeiLateHan|袁隗]] use a false edict to kill [[FanLingLateHan|樊陵]] and [[XuXiangLateHan|许相]]; Wu Kuang redirects blame toward [[HeMiaoLateHan|何苗]]; and the emperor's flight exposes the weakness of [[MingqiLegitimacy|名器合法性]] when seals, guards, and officials are scattered.

The concept helps connect Hanji 946 to Hanji 947. By the time [[DongZhuo|董卓]] arrives, the palace has not been stabilized by anti-eunuch victory. It has been emptied of ordinary authority, creating a field where an outside army can appear as rescue while becoming control.

## Key Claims
- Palace violence can cascade through access control, forged documents, blocked gates, rumor, revenge, and uncertainty about legitimate command.
- Once the first killing destroys the expected decision channel, later actors may treat false orders and physical force as normal tools.
- Retaliation can overshoot its stated target, as Yuan Shao's anti-eunuch killing includes misidentified non-eunuchs.
- The imperial body can survive the cascade while imperial instruments, escort, and command capacity collapse.
- A successful purge of one faction does not necessarily restore order; it can produce a power vacuum for a third actor.
