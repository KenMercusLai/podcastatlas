# Strategic Luxury Sabotage / 奢侈诱导式削弱

Strategic luxury sabotage / 奢侈诱导式削弱 is the pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-37-suqin-liuguo-jituan-ceo-de-bukan-qingshi-fj2kdqlaxzyy2nqtcutktuwrq5fo]] extracts from [[SuQin|苏秦]]'s entry into [[QiState|齐国]]. After [[YanYiwang|燕易王]] learns of Su Qin's affair with [[YanWengong|燕文公]]'s wife, Su Qin proposes leaving [[YanState|燕国]] for Qi in a way that can still serve Yan.

The episode says [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]] appoints Su Qin as guest minister, and Su Qin urges him to enlarge palaces and gardens. The point is not ordinary flattery. In the episode's reading, Su Qin is trying to weaken Qi's economy by encouraging conspicuous court consumption, thereby reducing pressure on Yan.

This concept sits beside [[DignityBasedPersuasion|尊严激将式说服]] and [[ChuaimoTechnique|揣摩]] as another zonghengjia method: the persuader does not always ask a ruler to do the strategically correct thing. Sometimes he steers a target into self-costly behavior that helps a third party.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-75-3-gudai-de-jingji-zhanzheng-neng-you-duo-kepa-liybgoz8lspypax6dhuke2js0pxf]] adds an adjacent but broader hostile-demand pattern. Guan Zhong's textile and deer cases are not luxury sabotage in the narrow palace-consumption sense; they are [[DemandInducedAgrarianDisruption|demand-induced agrarian disruption]], where a rival is lured away from farming by profitable trade before food security is weaponized.

## Key Claims
- Induced luxury can be used as interstate strategy when a foreign adviser gains court access.
- The method depends on misaligned agency: the ruler hears prestige advice, while the persuader serves another state's interest.
- It turns palace consumption and garden building into instruments of geopolitical weakening.
- The case also shows why private scandal and public diplomacy are not separable in the Su Qin branch: flight from Yan becomes covert service to Yan.
- The Guan Zhong economic-war branch shows a related logic outside court luxury: the attacker can use demand itself to redirect enemy labor.
