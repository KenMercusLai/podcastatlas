# Adult Fever Medication Safety / 成人发热用药安全

## Definition
Adult fever medication safety is a patient-context framework for deciding whether and how symptom relief belongs in fever care without treating one temperature, product, or another person's response as a universal rule.

## Current Synthesis
[[vol-17-jizhen-weizhongbingke-yangkang-de-jizhen-yisheng-liao-xinguan-zhi-yongyao-wuqu-dui-azifuding-he-paxlovid-de-jinshen-taidu-lqdru5slonvj7tu2rmhjlf0vlnzx|VOL.17]] makes fever relief a medication-history and risk-management problem. Pregnancy or lactation, older age, allergy history, liver or kidney function, gastrointestinal risk, underlying disease, current medicines, and prior reactions can all change what is appropriate. A drug that worked for a clinician or friend is therefore evidence about one experience, not a transferable prescription.

The source also connects treatment to diagnostic uncertainty and safety-netting. Temperature can guide observation, but cause, duration, hydration, breathing, neurological state, associated symptoms, vulnerability, and deterioration determine whether self-care remains reasonable. Ingredient review is essential because familiar cold-medicine brands can contain several active drugs.

## Key Claims
- Adult fever treatment should account for pregnancy or lactation, age, allergy history, organ function, comorbidity, current medicines, and prior reactions.
- A temperature threshold can support observation but cannot by itself establish severity, diagnosis, or medication suitability.
- Brand familiarity does not reveal active ingredients, duplication, contraindications, or cumulative dose.
- Personal or professional anecdotes should not be generalized into a public medication recommendation.
- Persistent, worsening, or systemically concerning illness should move from repeated self-treatment to qualified assessment.

## Evidence
- Patient-specific selection: [[vol-17-jizhen-weizhongbingke-yangkang-de-jizhen-yisheng-liao-xinguan-zhi-yongyao-wuqu-dui-azifuding-he-paxlovid-de-jinshen-taidu-lqdru5slonvj7tu2rmhjlf0vlnzx]] discusses pregnancy, lactation, older age, allergy, liver risk, gastric risk, and clinician or pharmacist input.
- Threshold and trajectory: [[vol-17-jizhen-weizhongbingke-yangkang-de-jizhen-yisheng-liao-xinguan-zhi-yongyao-wuqu-dui-azifuding-he-paxlovid-de-jinshen-taidu-lqdru5slonvj7tu2rmhjlf0vlnzx]] uses temperature in its home-care discussion while also moving prolonged or complicated illness toward testing, imaging, and medical review.
- Ingredient literacy: [[vol-17-jizhen-weizhongbingke-yangkang-de-jizhen-yisheng-liao-xinguan-zhi-yongyao-wuqu-dui-azifuding-he-paxlovid-de-jinshen-taidu-lqdru5slonvj7tu2rmhjlf0vlnzx]] identifies multiple ingredients hidden inside familiar combination-product and brand names.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is a January 2023 podcast summary, not a current fever guideline or individualized medication review. Its 38.5°C threshold, cooling locations, pregnancy-week statement, and named-drug experiences remain source-scoped. Fever can be clinically important below that threshold and benign above it depending on the whole presentation. Current product labels, local guidance, clinicians, pharmacists, and urgent-care pathways take priority.

## What Changed
- Established an adult-specific fever medication frame based on patient context, ingredient literacy, trajectory, and escalation rather than one drug or threshold.
