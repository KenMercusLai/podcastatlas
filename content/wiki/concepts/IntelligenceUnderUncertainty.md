# Intelligence Under Uncertainty

## Definition
Intelligence under uncertainty is the capacity to sense, remember, coordinate, act, learn, and revise without complete knowledge of a changing system.

## Current Synthesis
The episode treats uncertainty not merely as noise to remove but as a condition that makes intelligence necessary. Cells, organisms, people, and institutions operate from partial views; responsible action therefore combines provisional commitment with attention to consequences and revision. Systems that require predicted outcomes before supporting inquiry can suppress the very discovery they seek.

## Key Claims
- Complete information is not a prerequisite for intelligent action.
- Memory and coordination turn partial signals into revisable choices.
- Inquiry may transform its original question rather than simply answer it.
- Immediate answers can reduce productive exploration and surprise.
- Responsibility requires action, consequence tracking, and willingness to change.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Uncertainty does not justify arbitrary action or indefinite openness. Some decisions require thresholds, safeguards, and deadlines, while confident answers can be useful when evidence is mature. The source offers a general stance rather than operational rules for allocating risk.
