# Bicycle Industrial Spillovers

Bicycle industrial spillovers are the source's claim in [[no-199-zixingche-200-nian-973489465]] that the bicycle industry left useful industrial assets after its boom and bust. Lightweight tubes, ball bearings, tires, chains, precision parts, repair skills, and vehicle-design habits helped create a mechanical base for later cars and aircraft.

The source makes this concrete through [[Peugeot]] and the [[WrightBrothers]]. Peugeot's move from bicycles toward motor vehicles and the Wright brothers' bicycle-shop-funded flight experiments make the bicycle a bridge technology rather than a finished consumer-goods story.

## Key Claims
- A consumer-product boom can build supplier, material, and skill bases for later industries.
- Spillovers work through people and shop practice as much as through formal patents.
- Lightweight precision manufacturing became strategic before cars and aircraft fully absorbed it.
