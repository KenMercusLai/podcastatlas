# High-Dimensional Consciousness Entanglement / 高维意识纠缠

High-dimensional consciousness entanglement / 高维意识纠缠 is [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-161-caiyun-he-renji-guanxi-de-liangzi-jiuchan-3-ll74kq2hg2vp-zx-cqxkadxahamd|Hanji 161 part 3]]'s final synthesis of Daoist, Buddhist, and frequency language. The host says consciousness can resonate with the universe's original life-consciousness, enter a high-dimensional parallel universe, and reach天人合一; she then folds this into a definition of quantum entanglement as unified consciousness energy whose three-dimensional appearances are projections.

This concept extends [[QuantumEntanglementFateMetaphor|量子纠缠命运隐喻]] to its strongest spiritual form. Earlier Hanji 161 installments used quantum language for relational fate, reaction choice, and attraction. Part 3 expands it into cosmic unity, worlds beyond ordinary perception, and the claim that all things have spirit.

The wiki keeps this as the source's metaphysical synthesis. It is not stored as a physics account of quantum mechanics, a verified account of parallel worlds, or a checked quotation history for [[NikolaTesla|尼古拉·特斯拉]]. Its value is to show how the episode's self-cultivation language becomes a full cosmology.

## Key Claims
- Consciousness frequency is treated as something that can resonate with a higher cosmic consciousness.
- 天人合一 is interpreted as entering a higher-dimensional or parallel-world state.
- Buddhist出三界 language and Daoist transcendence language are combined.
- 万物有灵 is used to make even tiny quantum entities part of a living consciousness field.
- The source defines quantum entanglement's essence as high-dimensional unified consciousness energy projected into three-dimensional forms.
