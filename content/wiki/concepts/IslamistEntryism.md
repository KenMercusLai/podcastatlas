# Islamist Entryism

## Definition

Islamist entryism is the source's term for a non-violent strategy in which a small ideological network seeks disproportionate influence by entering institutions, obscuring its harder commitments, and presenting its representatives as legitimate community voices.

## Current Synthesis

[[islamism-in-britain-what-is-the-threat-6aa9164ffca3d5c77b4b383a]] distinguishes entryism from terrorism. Its mechanisms include lobbying, protest, school and mosque influence, local political relationships, and attempts to shape which organizations police or government consult. The risk is not mere participation by Muslims or religious groups; it is hidden coordination, misrepresented beliefs, coercive pressure, or an unearned claim to represent a diverse population.

The concept also explains why security tools built around imminent violence may miss political influence that remains lawful or ambiguous. The response therefore requires evidence, representative pluralism, transparent consultation, protection against threats, and a refusal to infer guilt from religious identity alone.

## Key Claims

- Entryism is an influence strategy, not a synonym for violent extremism.
- Small organized groups can gain leverage when institutions mistake organization for representativeness.
- Schools, local councils, mosques, policing relationships, protests, and elections can become influence sites.
- Hidden commitments and intimidation distinguish the risk from ordinary democratic participation.
- Countering entryism without collective suspicion requires evidence and plural community representation.

## Evidence

- Strategic definition: [[islamism-in-britain-what-is-the-threat-6aa9164ffca3d5c77b4b383a]] describes non-violent Islamists seeking positions and influence rather than attacks.
- Institutional sites: [[islamism-in-britain-what-is-the-threat-6aa9164ffca3d5c77b4b383a]] names schools, mosques, police consultation, local councils, protests, and election campaigns.
- Representation mechanism: [[islamism-in-britain-what-is-the-threat-6aa9164ffca3d5c77b4b383a]] reports concern that organized groups can be treated as speaking for the Muslim community at large.
- Coalition pathway: [[islamism-in-britain-what-is-the-threat-6aa9164ffca3d5c77b4b383a]] describes Gaza as a flashpoint for tactical alignment between Islamists and parts of the far left.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source offers examples and expert estimates, not a quantified map of entryist networks. Muslim candidacy, protest, mosque consultation, conservative belief, or criticism of PREVENT does not by itself establish entryism. Claims about concealed beliefs and organizational influence require case-specific evidence.

## What Changed
