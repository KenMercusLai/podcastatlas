# Command Discipline Authority / 军法威严

Command Discipline Authority / 军法威严 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-936-luanshi-xian-sha-shengmu-xin-zhehua-dui-ma-lml-kuyc7yys2ijh2kt46pwnugar|Hanji 936]], where [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] urges [[ZhangWenLateHan|张温]] to execute [[DongZhuo|董卓]] under military law after Dong Zhuo returns without decisive victory, delays a summons, and behaves arrogantly toward his commander.

The concept is not blanket cruelty. The source explicitly makes Zhang Wen's hesitation understandable because Dong Zhuo has western military prestige and does not present a single uncontestable capital offense. The command problem is that visible contempt by a powerful subordinate can make the army see formal authority as optional.

The episode's phrase "士卒可下而不可骄" captures the boundary. A commander can lower himself to share hardship with soldiers, but cannot let soldiers or subordinate commanders become arrogant toward law and hierarchy. Mercy, legal caution, and procedural restraint can therefore conflict with the need to preserve command credibility in a disordering army.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1025-bai-yu-caocao-lvbu-cuo-zai-jinjiuling-ljjt1t2mk77si6re-wew-hpl8qz|Hanji 1025]] adds a negative endpoint through [[ZhangYangLateHan|张杨]]. The host's "慈不带兵" formula describes a commander who is generous and kind but cannot impose authority strongly enough to prevent subordinates from redirecting the force. When [[YangChouLateHan|杨丑]] kills Zhang Yang during the attempted relief of [[LyuBu|吕布]], and [[SuiGuLateHan|眭固]] then kills Yang Chou and turns toward [[YuanShao|袁绍]], command kindness has stopped functioning as military control.

## Key Claims

- Command authority can be damaged before battle if public contempt goes visibly unpunished.
- Punishment can function as a signal of order, not only as retaliation for a completed tactical failure.
- Legal caution remains a real constraint; the source does not erase Zhang Wen's reasons for hesitating.
- The late-Han problem is sharper because Dong Zhuo already has troops, prestige, and regional standing outside Zhang Wen's direct moral control.
- The concept sits between [[GoodIntentionsPoliticalLimits]] and [[BenevolentCommandExecutionGap|仁厚指挥执行缺口]]: good intent and humane restraint need executable authority.
- Hanji 1025 adds that benevolent command can fail internally when subordinates can kill the commander and redirect military attachment.
