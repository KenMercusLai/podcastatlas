# Life Definition Spectrum

Life definition spectrum is the idea that living and non-living are not always clean binary categories in biology labs. [[its-not-easy-being-green-zack-polanski-6a82d1a0ff328abd84724cf0]] introduces it through [[OliverMorton]]'s discussion of [[SyntheticSpudCells]].

The episode starts with a simple cell definition: a membrane-bound molecular unit that can grow, reproduce, and evolve. Synthetic spud cells satisfy some of that description better than earlier lab-built systems because they can reproduce for a few generations, but they remain unlike ordinary cells because reproduction is imperfect and metabolism is minimal.

## Key Claims
- A cell can be a practical definition of life, but engineered cell-like systems make the boundary harder.
- Reproduction alone does not settle whether a system is alive if metabolism, robustness, and evolutionary continuity are weak.
- Lab-built systems can be scientifically important even when they remain liminal rather than fully alive.
- Synthetic biology can clarify definitions by forcing researchers to specify which behaviours count and why.
