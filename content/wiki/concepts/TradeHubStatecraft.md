# Trade-Hub Statecraft / 商贸枢纽型治国

Trade-hub statecraft / 商贸枢纽型治国 is the open-commerce branch [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-75-4-weishenme-shuo-guanzhong-caishi-jingji-zhan-de-bizu-lrdleqozazh4-xdwas4karzgbmza]] attributes to [[GuanZhong|管仲]]. The episode says Guan Zhong opens [[QiState|齐国]] to merchants, lowers entry taxes for empty carts and small carriers, and has [[QiHuanGong|齐桓公]] build guest houses for foreign traders.

The point is not generic friendliness to commerce. The source presents merchant attraction as statecraft: lower transaction costs and better hospitality pull goods, information, and commercial traffic into Qi, giving the state more circulation and a larger fiscal base. This constructive openness coexists with the episode's coercive price and border strategies against rival states.

## Key Claims
- A state can strengthen itself by becoming easier for merchants to enter and use.
- Tax restraint at the border can produce more value through volume, settlement, and circulation.
- Hospitality infrastructure is economic policy when it makes foreign merchants stay and trade.
- Open trade and economic warfare are not opposites in the source; both depend on understanding how goods and people move.
