# Prepared Fallback Stronghold / 预置退路型堡垒

## Definition
A prepared fallback stronghold is a defensible base intentionally governed before crisis as a future refuge, with immediate extraction restrained so social loyalty, stores, and fortifications remain usable under pressure.

## Current Synthesis
The source defines the pattern through [[ZhaoJianzi|Zhao Jianzi]] and [[YinDuo|Yin Duo]] at [[Jinyang|Jinyang]]. When asked whether the city should yield wealth or serve as refuge, Zhao Jianzi chooses refuge; Yin Duo reduces burdens and repairs defenses; and Zhao Jianzi tells [[ZhaoXiangzi|Zhao Wuxu]] to go there if the polity breaks down. The later [[BattleOfJinyang|Battle of Jinyang]] gives the policy operational meaning: a base is resilient not only because of walls, but because its population has not already been exhausted by its own rulers.

## Key Claims
- Fallback capacity is built before the crisis and before the exact threat is known.
- Revenue maximization and refuge readiness can conflict when extraction erodes loyalty or reserves.
- Governance quality is a defensive resource alongside terrain, walls, and weapons.
- A fallback becomes actionable only when successors know when and where to use it.
- Preserved capacity buys options but does not eliminate civilian cost once siege begins.

## Evidence
- Mandate choice: Zhao Jianzi tells Yin Duo to treat Jinyang as a refuge rather than a source of extraction. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-02-jiangyu-baizhi-bi-gufuzhi-lglk2_eu1uvegdac8sj6bfuwv1dw|Zhouji 02]]
- Implementation and succession: Yin Duo lowers burdens and strengthens defenses, and Zhao Jianzi instructs Zhao Wuxu to seek Jinyang in a future crisis. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-02-jiangyu-baizhi-bi-gufuzhi-lglk2_eu1uvegdac8sj6bfuwv1dw|Zhouji 02]]

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept currently rests on one podcast's transmitted narrative, and exact policies and dialogue are not independently verified here. A low-burden city is not automatically defensible, and a fortress can still transfer catastrophic suffering to civilians. The concept differs from [[StrategicRetreatBaseBuilding|strategic retreat base-building]], which turns an imposed retreat into a new base after displacement; Jinyang is deliberately preserved in advance.

## What Changed
- Created to distinguish pre-crisis refuge preparation from post-retreat base construction.
