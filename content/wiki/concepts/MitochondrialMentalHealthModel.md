# Mitochondrial Mental Health Model

## Definition
The mitochondrial mental health model is Palmer's source-scoped proposal that mitochondrial function can connect metabolism, neurotransmitters, hormones, inflammation, stress response, gene expression, and psychiatric symptoms.

## Current Synthesis
The model is useful in this wiki because it explains why a diet intervention could plausibly affect mental health through more than calories or weight loss. Palmer describes mitochondria as resource-allocation and signaling systems involved in neurotransmitter release, steroid-hormone synthesis, stress biology, inflammatory regulation, mitophagy, and mitochondrial biogenesis. The judgment remains qualified: this is a unifying mechanism claim in the episode, not settled proof that ketogenic diet treats psychiatric disorders.

## Key Claims
- Mitochondria are presented as more than cellular power sources; they help allocate resources and regulate cellular function.
- The source connects mitochondria to serotonin, dopamine, glutamate, acetylcholine, cortisol, adrenaline, noradrenaline, estrogen, testosterone, progesterone, and inflammation.
- Ketogenic diet is said to stimulate mitophagy and mitochondrial biogenesis, which Palmer believes may matter for epilepsy and chronic mental disorders.
- The model tries to unify several mental-health research variables that are often discussed separately.
- The mechanism remains source-scoped because large psychiatric randomized trials are not yet available.

## Evidence
- Neurotransmitter and hormone roles - [[essentials-diet-nutrition-for-mental-health-dr-chris-palmer-scim2465421786]] says mitochondria participate in neurotransmitter production, release, and regulation, and contain enzymes for steroid-hormone synthesis.
- Stress and inflammation - [[essentials-diet-nutrition-for-mental-health-dr-chris-palmer-scim2465421786]] links mitochondria to cortisol, adrenaline, noradrenaline, hippocampal gene expression, inflammatory switching, reactive oxygen species signaling, and macrophage healing phases.
- Ketogenic mechanism - [[essentials-diet-nutrition-for-mental-health-dr-chris-palmer-scim2465421786]] says ketogenic diet stimulates mitophagy and mitochondrial biogenesis over time.
- Evidence boundary - [[essentials-diet-nutrition-for-mental-health-dr-chris-palmer-scim2465421786]] says the mitochondrial explanation is Palmer's model rather than settled psychiatric trial proof.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source's mitochondrial account is mechanistic reasoning. It connects known biological systems in a coherent way, but the episode does not provide completed clinical trials that isolate mitochondria as the causal pathway for psychiatric improvement.
