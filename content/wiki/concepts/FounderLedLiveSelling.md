# Founder-Led Live Selling

Founder-led live selling is the social-commerce pattern where customers buy partly because they can watch the founder or maker sell, customize, react, and perform the product in real time. In [[advice-line-with-bobbi-brown-of-jones-road-beauty-september-2025-427590c2-11bc-43fa-9c92-d1eccf05842a]], [[AbbyRose]] depends on [[AbbyRosalier]]'s live TikTok presence because customers want to see orders made live and interact with the brand.

The concept is powerful because live selling can combine entertainment, production transparency, community, and conversion. It also creates a bottleneck when the founder is the only trusted face. [[BobbiBrown]] and [[GuyRaz]] suggest experiments with family members, friends, guests, user-generated design contests, or commission-based representatives so the format becomes repeatable without losing the intimacy that made it work.

## Key Claims
- Live selling can turn the making process into proof that the product is personal and fun.
- The founder's presence may be a growth asset before it becomes an operating constraint.
- Scaling the format should begin with controlled experiments in new faces and segments, not immediate wholesale or paid-ad expansion.
- Community participation can deepen the loop when customers help design, choose, or witness products being made.
