# 红楼梦结构 / Red Chamber Dream Structure

Red Chamber dream structure is the source's way of reading "dream" as more than a title metaphor in [[HongLouMeng|《红楼梦》]]. [[ritan-chuantai-hongloumeng-haishi-keyi-du-yidu-de-587167638]] distinguishes a small dream, a middle dream, and a large dream: [[JiaBaoyu|贾宝玉]]'s fifth-chapter dream, the several-year [[GrandViewGarden|大观园]] youth interval, and the [[JiaFamilyHousehold|贾府]]'s broader rise-and-fall dream.

The episode's "dream is worth doing" stance matters because it refuses nihilism. The beauty, friendship, poetry, and [[TruthfulTemperamentInRedChamber|truthfulness]] inside the dream are doomed, but the source does not conclude that the experience was worthless; the tragedy is that the social world cannot preserve what the dream briefly makes possible.

## Key Claims

- "Dream" operates at multiple scales: episode, garden, and household history.
- Dream structure allows the novel to hold beauty and loss together without reducing either one.
- The fifth-chapter verdict material functions as early foreshadowing while keeping later events partly open.
- The dream is not invalid because it ends; its ending is part of the novel's moral pressure.
