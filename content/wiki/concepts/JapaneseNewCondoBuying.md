# Japanese New-Build Condo Buying / 日本新建公寓购房

Japanese new-build condo buying is the owner-facing process mapped by [[131-wo-zai-riben-mai-le-yi-tao-zizhu-fang-llofkgaj-hyajksrpkoylakuocxz]]. The source is narrow by design: [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] buys one new-build self-use condo in [[Yokohama]], so the page should not be generalized to all Japanese resale homes, detached houses, investment condos, or short-term rental property.

The useful wiki frame is systemic. A purchase is not only selecting a unit; it connects location choice, developer reputation, railway access, presale rules, area calculation, balconies as common evacuation space, management fees, repair reserves, disclosure documents, mortgage review, loan-purpose rules, insurance, tax, and long-term resale value.

## Key Claims
- The strongest source evidence applies to a new-build mansion-style condo, not to every Japanese property category.
- Self-use fit comes before pure investment return: commute, density, natural environment, local services, and the buyer's tolerance for paperwork shape the decision.
- New-build purchase reduces some information and coordination burdens because the developer, bank, judicial scrivener, contractors, and option providers are already organized around one process.
- The same system can feel rigid and expensive because convenience, standardization, compliance, insurance, and maintenance all carry explicit costs.
- The purchase turns the wiki's broader [[Japan]] branch from macro assessment into lived institutional contact with housing, banking, urban planning, and property management.
