# Creator Obituary Memory / 创作者讣告记忆

## Definition
Creator obituary memory is the public work of turning a creator's death into a structured account of what their work made possible for viewers, readers, friends, and later cultural memory.

## Current Synthesis
The 展开讲讲 episode opens from recent creator deaths but does not treat them as bare news. [[AhnPanSeok|安盼熙]] becomes part of the hosts' own early podcast history, [[HigashinoKeigo|东野圭吾]] becomes a generation's weekend reading and moral-imagination memory, and [[NansunShi|施南生]] becomes an example of memorial-site design and affectionate testimony. The concept therefore sits between obituary, media reception, and ordinary health reflection.

## Key Claims
- A creator obituary can record how a work shaped the audience's own language, habits, and life period.
- Memorial writing is useful when it organizes a life beyond a career list or breaking-news death notice.
- Public creator death often prompts private time awareness: viewers want creators, relatives, and themselves to live long enough for more work and shared memory.
- The format can connect professional achievement, audience reception, and personal gratitude without needing full biography.
- Health reminders can appear naturally in creator memorial talk because death news makes bodily maintenance feel less abstract.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This concept should not replace factual biography. The source's creator-death sections are memorial readings and audience-memory reflections, so career dates, award claims, and death notices remain source-scoped unless corroborated elsewhere.
