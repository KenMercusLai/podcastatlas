# Finance Industry Role Segmentation / 金融行业岗位分层

Finance industry role segmentation is [[169-ruguo-ni-18-sui-zheng-kaolu-weilai-ba-jinrong-dang-zhiye-gaokaoji-tebie-cehua-lnasw3osck-kaixbhshjkpmscl9c]]'s warning that "finance" is too broad to support useful career advice. [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] separates the industry by institution type, business line, and front/middle/back-office role rather than treating investment banking, banks, funds, insurance, trusts, accounting firms, regulators, and financial infrastructure as one career.

The episode's practical map combines heterogeneity and homogeneity. Across subindustries, the work differs sharply: credit, investment banking, asset management, investment advisory, sales, audit/consulting, risk/compliance, operations, and technology are different jobs. Inside the same subindustry, however, platforms may look surprisingly similar, so career movement can depend less on abstract "finance skill" and more on the specific business, title, client surface, and platform credibility a person has accumulated.

## Key Claims

- "Finance" as a parent label hides major differences among banks, brokerages, funds, insurers, trusts, leasing firms, accounting firms, wealth managers, venture investors, regulators, and market infrastructure.
- Institution type alone is still insufficient in China because mixed operations make business line and role more explanatory than the legal entity name.
- Front office means revenue-facing or service-facing work, not only sales; investment managers, traders, investment bankers, advisors, and relationship managers can all sit near revenue.
- Middle office roles such as compliance and risk control can have real veto power because they translate regulatory boundaries into business feasibility.
- Back-office operations and technology are often treated as cost centers, but they are central to clearing, system reliability, data handling, and AI-era infrastructure.
- Status hierarchies inside finance often reflect revenue visibility, scarcity, platform prestige, client control, and compensation rather than social value or workload.
- Career advice must be role-specific: a student targeting credit, quant research, sell-side research, fund investment, bank technology, or compliance is not preparing for one identical market.
