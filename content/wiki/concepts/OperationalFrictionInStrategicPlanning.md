# Operational Friction in Strategic Planning

## Definition

Operational friction in strategic planning is the accumulation of physical, human, logistical, informational, and emotional constraints that changes whether and how an abstract objective can be executed.

## Current Synthesis

[[a-serious-game-a-serious-game|A Serious Game]] develops the concept through a simulated effort to secure North Korean nuclear material after state collapse. “Secure the sites” expands into crossing a fortified border, negotiating with factions, controlling perimeters, entering defended or contaminated facilities, locating weapons and records, finding scientists, distinguishing civilians, moving thousands of troops, and repeating the process across many possible nuclear, chemical, and biological locations.

The road network becomes part of the strategy. Refugees, evacuating foreign nationals, civilian traffic, military movement, damaged vehicles, protective equipment, casualties, cold, fear, and a possible nuclear detonation at a choke point all compete for attention and capacity. The concept therefore rejects a clean separation between military mission and humanitarian environment: civilian movement, bodily limits, and emotional pressure can determine the mission's feasible tempo and scale.

## Key Claims

- Strategic objectives conceal chains of site-level tasks, dependencies, and resource demands.
- Civilian movement and humanitarian conditions can become central operational constraints.
- Protective equipment and contamination change visibility, mobility, speed, and endurance.
- Small, portable hazardous materials make nominal site control different from complete accounting.
- Repetition across many locations can turn a mission imagined in weeks into one requiring years.
- Emotion, casualties, ambiguity, and command pressure remain part of execution rather than external noise.

## Evidence

### Site security and accounting

- [[a-serious-game-a-serious-game|A Serious Game]] describes thousands of soldiers needed for one installation, numerous possible sites, defended facilities, records and scientists, and a nuclear component small enough to be removed in a backpack.

### Movement, contamination, and human load

- [[a-serious-game-a-serious-game|A Serious Game]] connects refugee and evacuation traffic, military congestion, full protective equipment, destroyed vehicles, casualties, freezing civilians, emotional strain, and a possible nuclear strike at a geographic choke point.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source reports one prepared game and does not provide force models, site inventories, road-capacity data, chemical-protection performance, refugee estimates, or sensitivity analysis. Its operational details are useful for exposing omitted categories, not for certifying exact troop or time requirements. Better logistics, diplomacy, local cooperation, intelligence, infrastructure, or changed objectives could alter the scenario, while other constraints may remain missing.

## What Changed

- Established operational friction as the bridge between an abstract security objective and its physical, civilian, and emotional requirements.
- Added road congestion and population movement as strategic rather than merely background conditions.
