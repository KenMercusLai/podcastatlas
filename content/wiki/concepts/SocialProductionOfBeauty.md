# Social Production Of Beauty / 美的社会生产

Social production of beauty / 美的社会生产 is [[TianFeng|田峰]]'s frame in [[baiwan-ge-jingguan-shehui-tingshuo-ni-ye-xiang-dang-zhubo-lgd0yapclwvsessg7xnhaarpyiwy]] for how livestream beauty is made. The source treats beauty less as a fixed attribute of an anchor and more as an outcome of makeup, styling, camera distance, lighting, choreography, platform review, audience reaction, and data feedback.

The concept connects aesthetic labor to [[PlatformFeedbackLoop]]. A livestream member's look is tested through makeup trials, half-face comparison, set photos, comments from operators and stylists, camera effects, and observed audience response. Beauty becomes a production workflow and a feedback regime.

## Key Claims
- Livestream beauty is co-produced by bodies, makeup, costumes, cameras, lighting, platform rules, and audience attention.
- Differentiation matters: group livestreaming cannot rely only on making all members look alike.
- Data and feedback make beauty iterative, because the team can test which look or performance moment draws response.
- Beauty production can empower skilled presentation while also intensifying [[FemaleBeautySelfSurveillance|beauty self-surveillance]].
- The source turns an aesthetic category into a labor and organization category.
