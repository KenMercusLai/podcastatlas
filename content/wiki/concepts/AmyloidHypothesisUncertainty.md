# Amyloid Hypothesis Uncertainty

Amyloid hypothesis uncertainty is the mechanism caution in [[working-memory-the-surprising-decline-of-dementia-6a6b0eeca585d3be78cd9d1b]]. The episode mentions protein tangles in the brain and says researchers remain unsure whether these are a cause of dementia, a symptom, or part of a more complicated process.

The concept helps explain why the source spends more time on prevention than on a single biochemical cure. If the dominant mechanism remains unsettled, age-adjusted decline and modifiable risk factors become especially important evidence.

## Key Claims
- The source does not reject amyloid research, but treats the causal story as unresolved.
- Mechanism uncertainty makes epidemiology, public health, and natural experiments more valuable.
- Drug-development disappointment can coexist with real prevention progress.
