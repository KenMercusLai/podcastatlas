## Definition

Weiqi cultural promotion is the movement of go beyond specialist play through popular narrative, screen adaptation, public figures, tournaments, honorific recognition, and material support.

## Current Synthesis

The bounded [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] preview presents [[JinYong|金庸]] and [[NieWeiping|聂卫平]] as complementary cultural intermediaries. Jin Yong's novels and adaptations can make go imagery familiar to audiences who did not enter through formal instruction, while Nie's sporting authority and the pair's reported event work connect that familiarity to organized public activity. The reported publishing-income donation adds a material-support route.

This is a plausible mechanism, not a measured effect. The excerpt names no readers who became players, gives no participation data, does not identify specific novel scenes, and stops before explaining the 炎黄杯 or honorary amateur rank. Narrative visibility, event promotion, and funding should therefore remain distinct contributions rather than being collapsed into proof of mass recruitment.

## Key Claims

- Popular fiction and adaptation can provide low-barrier cultural exposure to a specialist game.
- Prominent writers and elite players can connect narrative familiarity to public events and institutional attention.
- Honorary ranks can recognize cultural contribution without demonstrating professional playing strength.
- Donations and event support are materially different from symbolic representation, even when both serve promotion.
- Exposure, interest, participation, skill development, and durable institutional growth are separate outcomes requiring separate evidence.

### Material support

- [[092-jinyong-xiaoshuo-yu-weiqi-wenhua-nieweiping-yu-jinyong-fvyifftyret8uzjeqlax31kmtq9u]] connects authorized mainland publication income from 《书剑恩仇录》 to cultural institutions and go activity.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source is only a paid episode's three-minute opening and contains no audience study, participation figures, tournament documentation, donation record, or close reading of the promised literary examples. The supplied note overstates the royalty destination relative to a People’s Daily account and includes clear biographical transcription errors about Nie Weiping. It therefore supports a map of promotional channels, not a causal estimate of Jin Yong's or Nie's effect on go participation.

## What Changed

- Added a bounded multi-channel model joining fiction, adaptation, public figures, events, recognition, and financial support.
- Separated cultural exposure from measured recruitment, skill, and institutional growth.
- Preserved the excerpt's severe evidence limit and corrected its overstrong royalty formulation.
