# Particularized Moral Discernment

## Definition

Particularized moral discernment is the practice of suspending prior ideological attachment long enough to examine a case's facts, motives, relationships, institutional context, lived consequences, and suffering before applying or revising a moral category.

## Current Synthesis

[[descend-into-the-particular-descend-into-the-particular|Descend into the Particular]] presents the method through Jesuit casuistry and a scale balanced at zero. A “disordered attachment” is a commitment or aversion that prevents a person from hearing inconvenient evidence and human experience. Ferguson demonstrates that findings at different levels can coexist: insufficient evidence to prosecute one officer does not negate evidence of predatory, racially harmful municipal policing. [[AngelNavarro]]'s death demonstrates that video, family memory, inferred intent, tactical danger, and differently situated suffering can matter without becoming equivalent.

The technology-and-doctrine test in [[dr-rock-s-taxonomy-dr-rock-s-taxonomy|Dr. Rock’s Taxonomy]] follows [[JohnRock]] from poor women's reproductive burdens to the question of whether the pill belongs with prohibited barriers or permitted rhythm. The speculative mTOR example similarly forces a choice between “contraception” and “fertility preservation.” Particularity therefore does more than complicate a verdict after the fact: it can reveal which features of a new case should control its moral classification.

Together the episodes defend neither permanent neutrality nor exemption from rules. They argue for disciplined attention before conclusion, explicit comparison among relevant cases, and willingness to recognize when an inherited category hides important differences. The method remains vulnerable to rationalization and false equivalence, so evidence, power, consequences, and responsibility must constrain analogy.

## Key Claims

- Ideological allegiance can select supportive facts while making complicating facts or lived consequences psychologically inadmissible.
- Concrete attention can hold findings at different levels—individual liability and institutional legitimacy, present pregnancy prevention and future fertility preservation—without treating them as contradictions.
- Evidence constrains interpretation but does not make motive, moral meaning, or categorical fit self-evident.
- Listening to lived suffering and practical circumstances is part of moral judgment, not sentiment added after abstract analysis.
- Casuistic analogy can revise a taxonomy when a new case shares morally relevant features with a permitted category.
- Suspending attachment is temporary discipline for better judgment, not permanent indecision or permission to ignore principles.
- Particularity should complicate categories without erasing asymmetries of evidence, power, agency, loss, and responsibility.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The episodes offer a moral and interpretive method, not a validated decision procedure. “Both sides” attention can become false equivalence if it ignores unequal evidence, institutional power, causal responsibility, or preventable harm. Case-specific classification can also become motivated hair-splitting when a preferred conclusion determines the analogy. Principles remain necessary for consistency and protection against biased exceptions. Both accounts rely on structured podcast summaries rather than the complete reports, records, theological arguments, or clinical literature relevant to their cases.

## What Changed

- Extended particularized discernment from interpreting a morally complex event to classifying a new reproductive technology.
- Added mechanism, purpose, duration, and lived consequence as features that can determine a morally relevant analogy.
- Made rationalization and motivated taxonomy explicit limits on casuistic reasoning.
