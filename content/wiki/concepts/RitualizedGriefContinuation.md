# Ritualized Grief Continuation / 仪式化延续悲伤

## Definition
Ritualized grief continuation is the use of personally meaningful acts, objects, promises, or symbolic completions to carry a relationship with the dead while still returning to life.

## Current Synthesis
The concept comes from [[CaiKangyong|蔡康永]]'s grief advice in [[caikangyong-luoyonghao-zai-canku-de-shijie-xunzhao-zizai-yu-hejie-baochi-cibei-yu-zhenshi-lqbw_yofwhs8rpt3w0xse_g9ajj1]]. A ritual is not powerful because society labels it solemn; it works when the mourner genuinely creates an act that gives the dead person a continuing place without freezing the mourner inside loss.

## Key Claims
- Ritual should be made by the grieving person or relationship, not merely borrowed as a formal gesture.
- Completing a symbolic wish can honor the dead while helping the living person move.
- The goal is not forgetting; it is placing the relationship somewhere stable enough that life can continue.
- Ritual can be light, private, or idiosyncratic rather than grand or socially legible.
- The practice depends on sincerity and fit, so a generic ritual can fail even if it looks respectful.

## Evidence
- Self-created ritual: [[caikangyong-luoyonghao-zai-canku-de-shijie-xunzhao-zizai-yu-hejie-baochi-cibei-yu-zhenshi-lqbw_yofwhs8rpt3w0xse_g9ajj1]] includes Cai's advice that real ritual should be created by oneself and come from the heart.
- Symbolic wish: [[caikangyong-luoyonghao-zai-canku-de-shijie-xunzhao-zizai-yu-hejie-baochi-cibei-yu-zhenshi-lqbw_yofwhs8rpt3w0xse_g9ajj1]] has him recommend completing a symbolic wish for a dead lover as part of honoring the relationship.
- Continuing life: [[caikangyong-luoyonghao-zai-canku-de-shijie-xunzhao-zizai-yu-hejie-baochi-cibei-yu-zhenshi-lqbw_yofwhs8rpt3w0xse_g9ajj1]] frames the act as putting the love in an important place and then living well.
- Idiosyncratic fit: [[caikangyong-luoyonghao-zai-canku-de-shijie-xunzhao-zizai-yu-hejie-baochi-cibei-yu-zhenshi-lqbw_yofwhs8rpt3w0xse_g9ajj1]] repeatedly treats funerals, tombstones, and farewell practices as personal rather than fixed by social pressure.
- Sincerity boundary: [[caikangyong-luoyonghao-zai-canku-de-shijie-xunzhao-zizai-yu-hejie-baochi-cibei-yu-zhenshi-lqbw_yofwhs8rpt3w0xse_g9ajj1]] contrasts real ritual with hollow obligation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This is not a clinical grief protocol. Severe grief, depression, suicidality, traumatic loss, or complicated family conflict may require professional support; a personally meaningful ritual can help but should not be treated as sufficient for every bereavement.

## What Changed
- Created from Cai Kangyong's advice to a listener who remained stuck after a partner's death.
