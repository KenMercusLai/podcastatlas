# Accessible Entry Point Brand Extension

Accessible entry point brand extension is a consumer-brand pattern where a company with an expensive core product adds a lower-price product that lets more customers participate without abandoning the brand's promise. In [[yeti-ron-and-ryan-seiders-how-two-brothers-turned-a-400-cooler-into-a-2-billion-brand-d7f8e0e8-51a5-4444-8c1d-f5c43c353285]], [[YETIRambler]] plays this role for [[YETI]] by translating rugged cooler credibility into everyday drinkware.

## Key Claims
- The extension works when the cheaper product borrows trust from the premium product rather than feeling like unrelated merchandising.
- YETI's Rambler cups lowered the entry price from hundreds of dollars for a hard cooler to roughly tens of dollars for drinkware.
- A lower-price entry point can broaden demand quickly while preserving premium brand meaning if quality and design remain consistent.
- The pattern extends [[ProductLedBrandLongevity]] because the brand survives by adding products customers can use more often, not only by stretching the logo.
