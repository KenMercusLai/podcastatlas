# High-Dose Psychedelic Practice

High-dose psychedelic practice is [[DaveHodges]]' emphasis in [[a-trip-to-the-magic-mushroom-megachurch]] that [[ZydorChurch]] centers spiritual practice around doses of mushrooms much higher than ordinary recreational use. The source says the church's high-dose orientation became part of its identity after Dave's 2019 mushroom vision.

The concept sharpens the sincerity and safety problem. High doses may support Dave's claim that [[PsilocybinMushrooms]] are being used as sacrament rather than as casual entertainment, but they also raise [[MedicalRiskManagement]] and liability questions that internal critics of [[PsychedelicChurches]] worry about.

## Key Claims
- Dose can be part of religious meaning when it distinguishes sacramental practice from casual use.
- High-dose access can also heighten medical, psychological, legal, and public-perception risk.
- The practice makes screening, setting, ceremony, and supervision more important than in low-dose retail-like access.
- If the church's barrier to entry is low, high-dose practice intensifies outsider skepticism about safety and sincerity.
