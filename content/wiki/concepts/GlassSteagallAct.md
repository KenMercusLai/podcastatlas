# Glass-Steagall Act / 格拉斯-斯蒂格尔法案

The Glass-Steagall Act appears in [[170-1929-de-paomo-zhixia-sange-daibiao-renwu-he-tamen-zai-dangxia-zhouqi-de-yingzi-lo7uv7umozr0jrqfjzyhdbzicx1j]] as the regulatory response associated with separating commercial banking from investment banking after the 1929-era securities-distribution failures. The episode connects it most directly to [[CharlesEMitchell]] and [[NationalCityBank]].

In this wiki branch, the Act matters less as a complete legal history than as a boundary-making answer to [[FinancialInstitutionTrustTransmission]]. If banks can use deposit-like trust and retail reach to sell securities, then regulation may need to decide which activities may share the same brand, balance sheet, and customer relationship.
