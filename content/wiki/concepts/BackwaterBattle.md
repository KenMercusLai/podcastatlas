# 背水一战 / Backwater Battle

背水一战 / Backwater Battle enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-156-bingxian-hanxin-de-fengshen-zhizhan-2-li2c9d04jpepy4t0mquwjt-bli4|Hanji 156 part 2]] as [[HanXin|韩信]]'s completed [[JingxingPass|井陉口]] battle against [[ChenYu|陈馀]] and [[ZhaoXie|赵歇]]. The source treats it as more than the idiom "fight with one's back to the water." It is a full tactical chain joining prior intelligence, enemy misjudgment, terrain, false defeat, camp seizure, flag replacement, and morale collapse.

The sequence begins before the river-side formation. Han Xin first uses spies to confirm that Chen Yu has rejected [[LiZuoChe|李左车]]'s supply-line interdiction plan. That matters because the backwater array would be much less useful if Zhao were still fortifying and cutting Han's transport route. Once Han Xin knows Zhao is committed to direct battle, he can choose the timing and psychology of the encounter.

Han Xin then sends two thousand elite cavalry ahead with red flags, hiding them near the eastern mouth of Jingxing. A forward force crosses the Mianman water, identified in the source with the Ye River near Jingxing, and forms with water at its back. Zhao commanders read this as a beginner's error because it leaves no ordinary retreat route.

The battle's decisive movement combines morale pressure and deception. Han Xin and [[ZhangEr|张耳]] display flags and drums, fight for a long time, feign defeat, abandon flags and drums, and retreat toward the river-side Han camp. Zhao troops pursue and attack the Han camp; with the Zhao camp emptied, the hidden cavalry enters, removes Zhao flags, and raises Han red flags.

The red flags turn Zhao's rear into an information shock. Returning Zhao troops think their camp and possibly their king have already been taken. Their formation breaks despite officers trying to kill deserters, and Han uses the panic to defeat Zhao, kill Chen Yu, and capture Zhao Xie.

## Key Claims
- The backwater formation is not a standalone morale trick; it works inside a larger setup that first removes Li Zuoche's more dangerous supply-line alternative.
- "No retreat" matters, but the source's battle chain also depends on inducing Zhao to leave camp and then making Zhao believe the rear has collapsed.
- Han Xin's victory uses [[BattlefieldInformationControl|战场信息控制]] at multiple layers: spies, hidden cavalry, visible flags and drums, false defeat, and flag replacement inside the enemy camp.
- The source makes Zhao's morale collapse a social-information event: soldiers panic because the camp and king appear lost, not merely because they lose a normal field engagement.
- The concept is adjacent to [[BreakingCauldronsAndSinkingBoats|破釜沉舟]], but Han Xin's case relies more on enemy interpretation and camp seizure than on destroying his own army's fallback infrastructure.
- The episode leaves the deeper theoretical explanation of why the formation works to a later installment, so this page records the completed sequence rather than a final doctrine of all backwater tactics.
