# Yuan Blue-and-White Porcelain / 元青花

Yuan Blue-and-White Porcelain / 元青花 is the underglaze cobalt porcelain branch in [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]]. The episode describes the technique as painting cobalt pigment on a white body, covering it with transparent glaze, and firing it so the blue decoration becomes durable.

The source uses Yuan blue-and-white to connect [[Jingdezhen]] to overseas markets. Large plates, bowls, jars, and bottles fit demand from West Asian and Central Asian eating and display habits, while the [[DavidVases|David Vases]] later gave scholars a dated benchmark for recognizing the category.
