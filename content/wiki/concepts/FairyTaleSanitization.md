# Fairy-Tale Sanitization / 童话净化

Fairy-tale sanitization is the episode 38 frame for how rough folk material can be cleaned into safe children's literature, family morals, or aesthetic prettiness. In [[38-anjila-kate-jingguai-gushiji-o-mirende-hanfu-609607139]], the hosts say many nineteenth-century and middle-class retellings removed sexual, crude, violent, or otherwise embarrassing material, making later readers forget how strange folk stories could be.

The source treats [[AngelaCarter|Angela Carter / 安吉拉·卡特]] as working against that cleaning process. [[JingguaiGushiji|《精怪故事集》]] preserves absurd sex jokes, gruesome punishments, animal spouses, grotesque bodies, and moral non-resolution because those elements are part of the story tradition rather than accidental dirt.

## Key Claims
- Sanitization can make a tale easier to sell as children's literature while narrowing its adult, comic, erotic, and violent registers.
- Clean versions often hide female desire and domestic conflict, not only gore.
- The source's critique includes but is wider than [[TheWaltDisneyCompany|Disney]]-style adaptation memory.
- Desanitizing a tale does not automatically recover an "original"; it makes variants and editorial choices visible.
