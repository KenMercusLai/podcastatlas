# Total Attention Practice / 全然注意力

## Definition
Total attention practice is E46's Krishnamurti-derived frame for seeing without the old image doing the seeing. It is not egoic concentration on a target, but whole-body presence in which senses, body, and mind are active enough that automatic memory, judgment, and labeling lose force.

## Current Synthesis
The concept is the episode's practical endpoint after the film, cognitive-evolution, internet, and speech-harm sections. [[MengYan]] does not present total attention as a simple technique. He says there may be no method that reliably dissolves six hundred million years of survival-oriented mechanisms. Still, the moment of noticing that an image is operating already differs from being captured by the image. In that sense, total attention is less an instruction manual than a name for non-automatic seeing.

## Key Claims
- Total attention differs from ordinary focus because it is not centered on an ego trying to achieve a result.
- Whole-body sensory presence can interrupt automatic simulation, labeling, and registration.
- Old interpersonal images lose power when they are seen as images rather than treated as the person.
- New registrations may stop forming when insult, fear, or conflict are not automatically stored as identity-confirming injury.
- The practice is difficult and cannot be reduced to a quick method.

## Evidence
- Krishnamurti frame: [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] introduces total attention through [[JidduKrishnamurti]] after explaining image and register.
- Sensory description: [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] describes eyes, ears, nose, skin, body, and heart as present rather than the mental simulator doing the seeing.
- No-method qualification: [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] says the episode cannot offer a simple "术", only the possibility of seeing the image and the registration process.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not claim total attention is easy, permanent, or measurable. It also warns that the very terms "image", "register", and "total attention" can become new labels if the listener treats them as concepts rather than seeing their operation directly.
