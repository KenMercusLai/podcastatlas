# 选官驱动的德行表演 / Selection-Driven Virtue Signaling

## Definition

选官驱动的德行表演 describes a response to personnel incentives in which officials make the traits favored by selectors publicly visible—such as frugality, modesty, or plain appearance—because advancement and safety depend on appearing to satisfy the standard.

## Current Synthesis

The late-Han case develops from incentive creation to internal correction. [[CuiYanLateHan|崔琰]] and [[MaoJie|毛玠]] are said to reject flashy reputation, falseness, and faction-building while rewarding substance and humility; elite conduct then shifts toward rough carts, unadorned appearance, and walking to court. The earlier account calls this “表演性节俭”: the behavior may improve public norms, yet the visible act cannot by itself distinguish sincere virtue from strategic compliance. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1087-simayi-zhuangbing-ju-caocao-yi-tingshuo-yao-bei-zhua-miao-song-likur-xfjqbk3kr-ew6ftvtwixq|Hanji 1087]] grounds the selection mechanism.

Once the same conduct becomes legible enough to function as a moral credential, [[HeQiaLateHan|和洽]] supplies the corrective judgment. He accepts moderate frugality but warns that pushing it to an extreme burdens officials and lets eccentric or false display substitute for integrity and usefulness. That qualification is grounded in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1114-guanyuan-jiti-zhuangqiong-heqia-quanjian-neng-jiaoxing-caocao-ma-lgqmpyz2zm1avw5e-rdexmo6kswm|Hanji 1114]].

The concept therefore separates behavioral effect, signal quality, and moral truth. A selection rule can successfully make conduct more austere and socially imitable even when actors follow it because luxury has become risky. As the proxy becomes easier to perform, however, the system can select for conspicuous compliance rather than the intended quality. The same mechanism can also produce exaggeration and enforcement vulnerability when a ruler values obedience more than the announced virtue.

## Key Claims

- Personnel criteria act as incentives before any formal punishment is applied.
- Visible frugality can be both socially consequential and privately strategic.
- A collective behavioral shift does not prove collective moral conversion.
- Extreme or cheap-to-copy signals can crowd out harder evaluation of integrity and capacity.
- Rule-setters can govern indirectly by making selectors, promotion, and reputational risk reinforce one another.
- Virtue standards become politically dangerous when their application depends on discretionary loyalty tests.

## Evidence

- Incentive transmission: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1087-simayi-zhuangbing-ju-caocao-yi-tingshuo-yao-bei-zhua-miao-song-likur-xfjqbk3kr-ew6ftvtwixq|Hanji 1087]] links Cui Yan and Mao Jie's selection preferences to widespread adoption of rough carts, plain appearance, and walking.
- Behavioral ambiguity: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1087-simayi-zhuangbing-ju-caocao-yi-tingshuo-yao-bei-zhua-miao-song-likur-xfjqbk3kr-ew6ftvtwixq|Hanji 1087]] explicitly interprets the pattern as performative because frugality becomes a safety and promotion asset.
- Proxy failure and correction: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1114-guanyuan-jiti-zhuangqiong-heqia-quanjian-neng-jiaoxing-caocao-ma-lgqmpyz2zm1avw5e-rdexmo6kswm|Hanji 1114]] has He Qia distinguish moderate frugality from extreme display and warn that the latter can be exhausting and false.
- Power boundary: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1087-simayi-zhuangbing-ju-caocao-yi-tingshuo-yao-bei-zhua-miao-song-likur-xfjqbk3kr-ew6ftvtwixq|Hanji 1087]] uses Cui Yan's death and Mao Jie's imprisonment to argue that moral criteria do not independently bind concentrated power.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept currently rests on two related interpretive podcast episodes rather than an independent measurement of official motives. Plain conduct may reflect sincere frugality, imitation, fear, career calculation, or real norm internalization; neither source measures their relative weight. Their claims about a broad social transformation, its causal attribution to the selectors, and the scale of “pretended poverty” require corroboration from primary texts and wider scholarship.

## What Changed

- Added He Qia's correction that moderate frugality can become a cheap, extreme, and counterproductive proxy for integrity.
- Reframed the concept around behavioral effect, signal quality, and moral truth rather than performance alone.
