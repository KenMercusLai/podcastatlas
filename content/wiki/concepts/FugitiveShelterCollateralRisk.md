# Fugitive Shelter Collateral Risk / 收留逃亡者的牵连风险

## Definition

Fugitive shelter collateral risk is the danger that receiving, aiding, or being visibly connected to a politically pursued person transfers punishment from the fugitive onto hosts, family, friends, and other helpers.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 894 and Hanji 895 make this risk morally double-sided. [[ZhangJianLateHan|张俭]]'s flight is understandable because [[HouLanLateHan|侯览]]'s accusation places him in lethal danger, and the people who receive him act from reputation, friendship, and仁义. Yet the same sheltering chain turns hosts into punishment targets: [[LiDuLateHan|李笃]] and [[MaoQinLateHan|毛钦]] keep one route open, [[XiziRanLateHan|戏子然]] marks another refuge stop, and Hanji 894 says many helpers later die or are implicated.

Hanji 895 concentrates the same mechanism in the Kong household. [[KongRong|孔融]]'s decision to hide Zhang Jian is treated as courageous rescue, and the later attempt by Kong Rong, [[KongBaoLateHan|孔褒]], and [[KongRongMother|孔融母]] to absorb the charge gives the story its emotional force. At the same time, [[XiaFuLateHan|夏馥]] and [[DuGenLateHan|杜根]] show that refusing help, hiding without old contacts, or lowering exposure can also be ethical when accepting aid would drag others into lethal danger.

## Key Claims

- Political pursuit turns hospitality and friendship into possible evidence of complicity.
- A fugitive's moral reputation can attract help while also distributing danger across more households.
- The host's courage should not erase the fugitive's responsibility to consider who may be punished for sheltering him.
- Family responsibility can become a substitute battlefield when formal law cannot or will not distinguish initiative, invitation, and household authority.
- Avoiding friends while in danger can be a protective act rather than mere distrust or cowardice.
- Later idiom memory can preserve the fugitive's desperation while compressing the hosts' risk.

## Evidence

- Multi-household flight chain: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-894-yi-chang-taowang-yinfa-de-da-tusha-lhulp9hqu07uayyfl3eg-xwrtkeu|Hanji 894]] says Zhang Jian is sheltered by several people after Hou Lan's accusation, including Li Du and Xizi Ran, and that many helpers or relatives are later punished.
- Official discretion at a shelter point: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-894-yi-chang-taowang-yinfa-de-da-tusha-lhulp9hqu07uayyfl3eg-xwrtkeu|Hanji 894]] shows Mao Qin leaving Li Du's house without arresting Zhang Jian after a coded exchange about仁义.
- Shelter and punishment exposure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-895-xuexing-tudao-xia-de-gurou-qingshen-llsplyyrjxhqkpi2aiuqzulgm6w3|Hanji 895]] says Zhang Jian is hidden by Kong Rong in Kong Bao's household, then Kong Bao and Kong Rong are imprisoned after Zhang escapes.
- Family responsibility contest: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-895-xuexing-tudao-xia-de-gurou-qingshen-llsplyyrjxhqkpi2aiuqzulgm6w3|Hanji 895]] has Kong Rong, Kong Bao, and Kong Rong's mother each try to claim the guilt.
- Non-implication countermodel: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-895-xuexing-tudao-xia-de-gurou-qingshen-llsplyyrjxhqkpi2aiuqzulgm6w3|Hanji 895]] uses Xia Fu and Du Gen to argue that a fugitive or endangered person may protect others by not using their shelter.
- Idiom compression: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-895-xuexing-tudao-xia-de-gurou-qingshen-llsplyyrjxhqkpi2aiuqzulgm6w3|Hanji 895]] connects Zhang Jian's flight to [[WangmenTouzhi|望门投止]], highlighting desperate refuge-seeking under lethal pursuit.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

This concept is grounded in two adjacent short source notes. It should not be generalized into a claim that fugitives must never seek help or that hosts are wrong to provide refuge. Hanji 894 and Hanji 895 preserve both sides: Li Du, Mao Qin, and the Kong family's courage are admirable in the episode frame, while the later punishment of helpers and Xia Fu/Du Gen's counterexamples keep the collateral cost visible.

## What Changed

- Hanji 894 adds the earlier route and broad punishment chain behind Zhang Jian's flight, including Li Du, Mao Qin, and Xizi Ran.
- The current synthesis now separates the multi-household escape route from Hanji 895's concentrated Kong-family responsibility scene.
