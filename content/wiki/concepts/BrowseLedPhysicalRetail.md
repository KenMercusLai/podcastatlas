# 逛店驱动的实体零售 / Browse-Led Physical Retail

## Definition
Browse-led physical retail is a store strategy in which discovery, touch, display, movement, service, and unplanned desire are central reasons to visit rather than incidental costs around inventory fulfillment.

## Current Synthesis
The interview argues that online versus offline is not the deepest retail distinction. Standardized known goods may migrate easily toward e-commerce, while products that benefit from seeing, touching, comparison, social context, assistance, or impulse retain physical value. A successful store therefore has to be “好逛”: it must create useful discovery without relying only on a pre-existing shopping list.

[[Miniso|名创优品]] supplies the principal case. Rapid assortment change, thousands of SKUs, character displays, large formats, affordable impulse items, and differentiated store types turn browsing into demand formation. The mechanism still depends on product quality, price, replenishment, staff execution, site economics, and repeat visits; visual novelty alone is not a durable advantage.

## Key Claims
- Physical retail is strongest when the visit changes what customers notice, understand, feel, or decide to buy.
- Touch, display, comparison, service, and social atmosphere can make non-standard or emotional goods less substitutable by pure fulfillment.
- Assortment breadth and turnover can create discovery, but also increase inventory, forecasting, and operational risk.
- Affordable impulse items lower the cost of acting on in-store discovery.
- Store format should match the role of the visit; ordinary convenience stores and destination IP stores need not carry the same mix.
- Repeat purchase and unit economics, not foot traffic or check-ins alone, determine whether browsing creates durable value.

## Evidence
- Channel boundary: [[ltdplf6zr1agmhhbopqptutvr-ng-ltdplf6zr1agmhhbopqptutvr-ng]] records Luo and Ye distinguishing standardized e-commerce-friendly goods from purchases shaped by touch, browsing, impulse, and service.
- Miniso mechanism: [[ltdplf6zr1agmhhbopqptutvr-ng-ltdplf6zr1agmhhbopqptutvr-ng]] describes high SKU counts, rapid annual elimination, low ordinary-store basket size, larger destination formats, and IP-heavy displays.
- Management practice: [[ltdplf6zr1agmhhbopqptutvr-ng-ltdplf6zr1agmhhbopqptutvr-ng]] connects browse quality to weekly selection meetings, store visits, customer feedback, product standardization, and simple frontline routines.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept comes from a founder interview and does not isolate browsing from location, novelty, price, brand recognition, or broader mall traffic. Large stores and dense assortments can raise rent, inventory, labor, and markdown costs. Conversion, basket, SKU, and payback figures remain source-scoped, and strong traffic does not by itself prove repeat demand or profitability.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to separate physical discovery value from the broader category of experiential retail.
