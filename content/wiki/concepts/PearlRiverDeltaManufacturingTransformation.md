# Pearl River Delta Manufacturing Transformation / 珠三角制造业变迁

Pearl River Delta manufacturing transformation is the long industrial arc in [[ruci-dagong-30-nian-liushuixian-shang-de-miankong-yu-shijian-lodsuktwtcfxnhmzwum5iesbzbme]]. Through [[Dongguan|东莞]] and [[ChanganTown|长安镇]], the source moves from reform-era capital and export orders into large assembly lines, global demand shocks, [[FlexibleManufacturing|flexible manufacturing]], automation, domestic substitution, and [[ManufacturingMicroFragmentation|small-factory fragmentation]].

The concept adds a worker-side history to the wiki's manufacturing branch. The source does not treat the Pearl River Delta only as an export machine; it shows how [[TayloristFactoryTime|factory time]], dormitory life, overtime, remittances, layoffs, social-insurance gaps, and self-learning made global production locally felt.

## Key Claims
- Manufacturing transformation has to be read across capital, orders, technology, labor discipline, local services, and worker households.
- Global demand shocks such as SARS, the 2008 financial crisis, and pandemic supply-chain disruption become real for workers through orders, layoffs, and factory survival.
- [[FlexibleManufacturing|柔性制造]] and automation are later stages in a longer history of line discipline, cost control, and shifting order structure.
- Worker archives can reveal industrial transformation at a texture that firm-level and macro-level sources miss.
