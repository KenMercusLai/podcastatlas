# Pregnancy Urinalysis Triage / 孕期尿常规分层判断

## Definition
Pregnancy urinalysis triage is the context-aware interpretation of urine-test abnormalities during pregnancy, distinguishing possible urinary infection, gestational diabetes evaluation, kidney-related concern, and observation thresholds.

## Current Synthesis
[[vol-30-miniao-waike-nvxing-miniao-xitong-de-changjian-wenti-yaoyan-ji-wuqu-lmeg-qk-dio77mj7retw0qoyoop-o]] treats pregnancy urinalysis as a shared boundary between obstetric care and urology. White cells, red cells, and nitrites can suggest urinary infection, but the episode still asks whether there are urinary frequency, urgency, pain, or other symptoms before deciding whether the result is a clinical problem or a milder observation situation.

The source separates urine markers by implication. Glucose in urine points toward gestational-diabetes evaluation in the obstetric branch, protein in urine raises kidney-related concern, and suspected urinary infection should involve urology input while pregnancy care remains coordinated by obstetrics. The practical point is not to read a urine strip as a diagnosis without symptoms, pregnancy context, and the right clinical department.

## Key Claims
- White cells, red cells, and nitrites in pregnancy urine testing can indicate possible urinary infection, especially when frequency, urgency, pain, or other urinary symptoms are present.
- Abnormal markers without urinary symptoms may sometimes be handled with hydration, rest, lifestyle attention, and follow-up rather than immediate panic, depending on clinical context.
- Urine glucose during pregnancy is treated as a gestational-diabetes evaluation signal rather than a urology-only finding.
- Urine protein during pregnancy is treated as a kidney-related warning signal that requires broader clinical judgment.
- Suspected urinary infection in pregnancy belongs inside coordinated care, with urology consultation alongside obstetric management when needed.

- Observation boundary: [[vol-30-miniao-waike-nvxing-miniao-xitong-de-changjian-wenti-yaoyan-ji-wuqu-lmeg-qk-dio77mj7retw0qoyoop-o]] says asymptomatic findings may be approached with drinking water, rest, and lifestyle attention in some situations.

- Department boundary: [[vol-30-miniao-waike-nvxing-miniao-xitong-de-changjian-wenti-yaoyan-ji-wuqu-lmeg-qk-dio77mj7retw0qoyoop-o]] keeps urinary infection inside urology consultation while other pregnancy-linked findings remain in obstetric evaluation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This concept does not diagnose urinary infection, gestational diabetes, acute kidney injury, pregnancy complications, or fetal risk. The source is public education and does not define complete pregnancy-care thresholds, antibiotic decisions, repeat-test timing, or emergency criteria. Any abnormal pregnancy urinalysis should be interpreted by qualified clinicians with the patient's full pregnancy and symptom context.
