# Working Memory as Active Control

## Definition
Working memory as active control is the temporary maintenance and manipulation of information that is no longer directly present so it can guide reading, reasoning, planning, problem solving, and action.

## Current Synthesis
The episode distinguishes working memory from both passive short-term storage and long-term memory. In [[how-to-optimize-cognitive-function-brain-health-dr-mark-desposito-scim4205449835]], [[MarkDEsposito]] describes persistent frontal activity as one mechanism for keeping task-relevant information online, while [[ExecutiveFunction]] determines how that information is selected, transformed, and used.

Dopamine helps stabilize relevant frontal activity, but the relationship is not linear. The bromocriptine example supports [[DopamineInvertedU]]: a manipulation can improve performance among people with lower baseline capacity yet worsen it among those starting higher. Behavioral capacity, genetics, and other physiological measures may help characterize baseline state, but none is presented as a simple clinical dopamine test.

## Key Claims
- Working memory keeps unavailable information online and manipulates it for current goals.
- It supports comprehension, planning, reasoning, and problem solving rather than serving as passive storage alone.
- Persistent frontal activity is presented as one mechanism for maintaining task-relevant representations.
- Dopamine can stabilize working-memory representations, but too little and too much may both impair performance.
- Baseline capacity changes how a person may respond to a dopamine-related intervention.
- A working-memory score is an incomplete behavioral proxy, not a direct measure of brain dopamine.

## Evidence
- Active-maintenance definition - [[how-to-optimize-cognitive-function-brain-health-dr-mark-desposito-scim4205449835]] distinguishes holding and manipulating information from long-term memory.
- Frontal persistence - [[how-to-optimize-cognitive-function-brain-health-dr-mark-desposito-scim4205449835]] links dopamine-supported persistent frontal activity to keeping information online.
- Baseline-dependent effect - [[how-to-optimize-cognitive-function-brain-health-dr-mark-desposito-scim4205449835]] reports opposite bromocriptine effects in lower- and higher-baseline working-memory groups.
- Measurement boundary - [[how-to-optimize-cognitive-function-brain-health-dr-mark-desposito-scim4205449835]] says blood tests do not directly measure brain dopamine and presents capacity and COMT variation only as partial indicators.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The page does not imply that one task captures a person's general intelligence or executive capacity, that a behavioral score directly measures dopamine, or that dopamine agonists should be used for cognitive enhancement. The episode's drug, genetic, and biomarker examples remain source-scoped and require clinical and study-specific interpretation.
