# Yellow Lemon Price Cycle / 黄柠檬价格周期

Yellow lemon price cycle is the supply-demand pattern described in [[zhongguo-yinglai-hanbao-kaidianchao-guochan-huangningmeng-jiage-huiluo-1006920543]], where domestic yellow-lemon farmgate prices fell even after entering an August consumption peak. The source anchors the case in [[AnyueLemonProductionArea|四川安岳]], China's largest lemon production area.

The episode explains the decline as a three-sided squeeze. Supply rose after weather normalized and fruiting improved; Middle East conflict reduced exports and pushed export goods back into domestic inventory; and B-end buyers such as tea-drink brands and supermarkets reduced purchasing after last year's high yellow-lemon prices made many mid-tier tea brands cut related products.

## Key Claims
- Better growing conditions can turn last season's shortage into this season's inventory pressure.
- Export disruption can push externally intended fruit back into the domestic market.
- Tea-drink product decisions matter because the source says more than half of domestic fresh lemon demand comes from B-end buyers.
- A crop price can fall during a nominal consumption peak when supply, inventory, and procurement plans move against it.
- The concept connects agricultural volatility to beverage-chain menu and cost decisions.
