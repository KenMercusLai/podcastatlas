# Core Competence Strategy / 核心竞争力战略

## Definition
Core competence strategy is the discipline of identifying the durable capability that creates repeatable advantage, then choosing goals, markets, and resource allocation where that capability can matter.

## Current Synthesis
The episode separates core competence from visible assets. Brand, patents, user base, loyalty, and past achievements may be outputs, but the strategic question is what underlying skill, operating system, supply chain, insight, or digital capability keeps producing those outputs. For individuals, the same logic asks what capability produced a credential, role, viral product, or reputation, then uses that answer to choose where to compete and where to stop forcing a mismatch.

## Key Claims
- Core competence is closer to durable capability or [[BusinessMoat]] than to a static asset.
- Strategy should direct companies and people toward arenas where their competence is relevant.
- The "ask why five times" method helps move from visible outcomes to the underlying ability that produced them.
- Capability-fit can reduce wasted effort: failing outside one's competence range may be a strategy problem, not only an effort problem.
- Core business should receive disproportionate resources because it is where competence converts into value.

## Evidence
- Capability versus asset distinction: [[ep85-yingxiao-guanli-gkwrimaoomq-bvejtas8o50a]] says brands, patents, users, and loyalty can be results rather than the competence itself.
- Company cases: [[ep85-yingxiao-guanli-gkwrimaoomq-bvejtas8o50a]] frames [[SamsClub]] through supply chain, bargaining power, membership loyalty, and purchase-volume flywheel, and [[LuckinCoffee]] through flexible digital operations.
- Personal diagnosis: [[ep85-yingxiao-guanli-gkwrimaoomq-bvejtas8o50a]] recommends repeatedly asking why a result happened until the underlying ability becomes visible.
- Resource focus: [[ep85-yingxiao-guanli-gkwrimaoomq-bvejtas8o50a]] uses core-business analysis and divestment logic to argue for concentrating time, capital, and attention where competence works.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode's competence analysis is qualitative. It does not prove that named companies' advantages are sufficient or durable, and it does not turn self-acceptance into an excuse to avoid deliberate skill-building.
