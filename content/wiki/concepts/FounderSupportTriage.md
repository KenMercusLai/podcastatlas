# Founder Support Triage

Founder support triage is the YC operating distinction in [[tsr-s5-pg-v6final-audio-tsr-s5-pg-v6final-audio]] between startups that might still be saved through concentrated intervention and startups that mostly need humane support while failure becomes likely. [[PaulGraham]] describes "inflection point office hours" for promising founders in terrible shape, and "palliative care mode" for startups that seemed doomed.

The concept extends [[FounderFriendlyInvestorSupport]] by adding a choice about tone and intensity. If a startup has a plausible path, tough feedback and multiple partners may be justified. If the startup is unlikely to recover, the useful support may be gentler encouragement, emotional steadiness, and helping founders leave the experience with dignity.

## Key Claims
- Founder support is not one mode; the same institution may need rescue, hard truth, coaching, or emotional care depending on the company's state.
- "Inflection point" help depends on believing the founders or situation can still bend upward.
- "Palliative" support treats failed startups as human events, not only bad investment outcomes.
- Direct criticism can be care when it is tied to a plausible recovery path and real commitment to the founder.
- Triage reduces the risk of applying tough love to founders who are already past the point where pressure helps.
