# Impermanence And No-Self

## Definition
Impermanence and no-self is the wiki's practice frame for loosening the assumption that there is a stable inner controller who can own outcomes, experiences, relationships, or the future.

## Current Synthesis
Across the current sources, no-self is not a denial that persons, choices, or events appear. It is a challenge to the felt owner behind them. [[e43-zhang-xiaoyu-mengyan-duihua-xuzhe-meiyou-genghaode-shenghuo-lrsfby01kuournly5mlkkzi-ayls]] connects market uncertainty to the fact that outcomes do not submit to a controlling self. Effort still matters, but "因上努力，果上随缘" places effort on the cause side while leaving results contingent.

The everyday, relational branch comes through [[ritan-chuantai-jiemei-liaotianhui-chongqi-rensheng-yeshi-wo-zui-yaojiao-616116425]], where [[YinJie|殷姐]] models welcoming people and moments fully without requiring permanence. The direct meditation branch comes through [[essentials-using-meditation-to-focus-view-consciousness-expand-your-mind-dr-sam-harris-scim7293799235]], where [[SamHarris]] says the inner observer cannot be found when consciousness is examined directly. Together, the sources make no-self both a life-practice response to uncontrollable outcomes and a first-person inquiry into the apparent subject of experience.

## Key Claims
- Market uncertainty becomes a concrete case of impermanence rather than only a financial problem.
- No-self is presented as the loosening of a controlling observer, not as denial that persons or events appear.
- Effort belongs on the cause side; outcomes remain contingent.
- Non-attachment can be practiced through hospitality, friendship, and daily departures, not only through formal philosophy or market uncertainty.
- Harris adds a direct meditation version: when one looks for the thinker or observer behind experience, the separate entity is not found.
- Practice is distorted when it becomes another project for finally securing a better state.

## Evidence
- Uncertainty and effort: [[e43-zhang-xiaoyu-mengyan-duihua-xuzhe-meiyou-genghaode-shenghuo-lrsfby01kuournly5mlkkzi-ayls]] uses investing, options, fat-tail outcomes, and "因上努力，果上随缘" to separate effort from control over results.
- Non-attachment in ordinary life: [[ritan-chuantai-jiemei-liaotianhui-chongqi-rensheng-yeshi-wo-zui-yaojiao-616116425]] uses Yin Jie's Diamond Sutra and guesthouse examples to show welcome without possession or permanence.
- Direct no-self inquiry: [[essentials-using-meditation-to-focus-view-consciousness-expand-your-mind-dr-sam-harris-scim7293799235]] says the felt thinker, meditator, or observer cannot be found apart from the experience in which thoughts arise.
- Better-life attachment: [[e43-zhang-xiaoyu-mengyan-duihua-xuzhe-meiyou-genghaode-shenghuo-lrsfby01kuournly5mlkkzi-ayls]] and [[essentials-using-meditation-to-focus-view-consciousness-expand-your-mind-dr-sam-harris-scim7293799235]] both warn that practice or achievement can become another future-dependent happiness project.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The sources do not argue for fatalism, passivity, or the disappearance of ordinary identity. They preserve agency, hospitality, investing discipline, and practical decision-making while challenging the fantasy of full control. Harris's contribution is experiential and philosophical rather than a localized brain proof.
