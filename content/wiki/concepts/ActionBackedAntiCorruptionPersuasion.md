## Definition

行动托底式反腐劝诫 / action-backed anti-corruption persuasion is the integrity pattern where a warning against corrupt gain gains force because the adviser personally removes excuses, supplies an alternative, or bears a cost that makes the advice practical.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 754 supplies the concept through [[ZhengJunEasternHan|Zheng Jun]]. His brother has taken bribes in county office, so Zheng leaves home, works as hired labor, returns money and goods to his brother, and then warns that money can be earned again but official corruption can produce lifelong regret.

The persuasion works because it is not merely verbal blame. Zheng Jun's labor-backed repayment changes the brother's room for action and makes clean conduct materially imaginable. The episode pairs this with [[MaoYiEasternHan|Mao Yi]] to present integrity as conduct that can be misread at first but becomes legible through sacrifice and refusal.

## Key Claims

- Anti-corruption advice is stronger when the adviser reduces the material excuse for corrupt income.
- Zheng Jun's labor and repayment make the brother's change possible without pretending that household need is irrelevant.
- The warning reframes bribery as a life-damaging official crime rather than only a moral blemish.
- The concept connects family persuasion with public-office discipline because the corruption occurs in county service.
- Emperor Zhang's later recognition shows that private integrity anecdotes can become public moral capital.

- Labor-backed repayment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-754-gudai-ban-qieting-fengyun-jieju-shuangwen-ltvymh-2r-ic5diqfh-2ic9cudq2|Hanji 754]] says Zheng leaves home, works for wages, and returns money and goods to his brother.
- Practical warning: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-754-gudai-ban-qieting-fengyun-jieju-shuangwen-ltvymh-2r-ic5diqfh-2ic9cudq2|Hanji 754]] says Zheng warns that ordinary money can be earned again while corrupt official crime leaves lasting regret.
- Changed conduct: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-754-gudai-ban-qieting-fengyun-jieju-shuangwen-ltvymh-2r-ic5diqfh-2ic9cudq2|Hanji 754]] says the brother stops taking bribes and becomes a clean official.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The evidence is an anecdotal integrity case, not a full anti-corruption institution. It does not show audit design, law enforcement, salary policy, or repeatable bureaucratic controls. The modern disciplinary comparison in the episode is useful as commentary but remains source-scoped and should not be treated as historical equivalence.
