# China-Changnan Etymology Myth / China源于昌南说

China-Changnan Etymology Myth / China源于昌南说 is the local-origin story discussed and rejected in [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]]. The episode says Jingdezhen locals often connect "China" to the old name Changnan, but the timing is weak because related terms appear earlier through ancient India, Central Asia, and Persian transmission.

The concept matters as an evidence caution inside [[MaterialHistoryNarrative]]. A place can be globally important without every memorable etymology being true; the source contrasts the weak Changnan claim with the stronger [[Kaolin]] derivation from [[GaolingVillage|高岭村]].
