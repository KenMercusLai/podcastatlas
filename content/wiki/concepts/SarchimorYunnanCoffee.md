# Sarchimor Yunnan Coffee / 萨奇姆云南咖啡

## Definition
Sarchimor Yunnan coffee is the episode's strategic variety frame for a locally developed Yunnan coffee route that may balance flavor, disease resistance, yield, local identity, and farmer income.

## Current Synthesis
In [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]], Sarchimor matters because it is not only a specialty-cup story. Yu Wenjie presents it as a more scalable Yunnan answer than small Geisha plots: it can be locally bred, productive, disease-resistant, and expressive enough to create a recognizable flavor vocabulary, including acidity compared in the episode to tamarind.

## Key Claims
- Sarchimor is prioritized over Geisha in the source because it better fits Yunnan's scale and smallholder realities.
- The variety is presented as locally bred or locally adapted, giving it identity value beyond international fashion.
- Disease resistance, larger beans, and higher yield make it a farmer-income and commercial-quality lever.
- Its flavor vocabulary may need local descriptors that do not fully fit older specialty-coffee language.
- The strategic promise is broad production improvement, not only rare high-scoring lots.

## Evidence
- Priority over Geisha: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] says Yu Wenjie pays more attention to Sarchimor than Geisha.
- Local identity: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] describes Sarchimor as a Yunnan-bred or locally rooted variety, with named lines such as Del 396, Del 399, and Del 402.
- Production economics: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] contrasts small Geisha yields with much higher Sarchimor experimental-plot yields, while keeping exact figures source-scoped.
- Flavor vocabulary: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] says Sarchimor often has a tamarind-like acidity that older specialty descriptions may not capture well.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The page does not establish Sarchimor as universally superior in cup quality. The source also says Yunnan can produce good Geisha, but the comparison is about fit among yield, risk, price, and local expression.
