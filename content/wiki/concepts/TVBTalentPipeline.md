# TVB Talent Pipeline / TVB 人才输送机制

## Definition
TVB talent pipeline / TVB 人才输送机制 is the process by which Hong Kong television production trained writers, actors, comedy workers, and production staff who later fed the film industry.

## Current Synthesis
王晶's account makes the pipeline concrete through his own career. He entered [[TelevisionBroadcastsLimited|TVB]], learned comedy writing under pressure, earned by accepted material, worked around senior practitioners, and later carried those skills into film directing and fast commercial production.

The source also treats the pipeline as an industry-level advantage. TVB supplied actors and production people at a time when Hong Kong film could absorb them into star vehicles and genre films. When Wang says later Hong Kong and mainland actors lacked the older training depth, he is pointing to the disappearance or weakening of a practical apprenticeship ecology.

## Key Claims
- TVB trained speed, comedy rhythm, and reliability through production pressure.
- Learning was apprenticeship-heavy: senior workers and live production mattered more than formal curriculum.
- The pipeline helped film companies by supplying already-tested performers and writers.
- Strong actor cohorts depended on repeated television work before film stardom.
- Weakening long-term training can create actor and craft gaps even when the market is larger.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This concept is based on Wang Jing's practitioner memory. It should be checked against other TVB, actor-training, and Hong Kong film histories before becoming a comprehensive institutional account.
