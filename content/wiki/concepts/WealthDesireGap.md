# Wealth Desire Gap / 财富欲望差

Wealth desire gap is [[162-caifu-de-benzhi-yiji-ziyou-de-zhenzheng-hanyi-chuantai-shifen-xiyin-lofyxfhiprud7zvekufexsjhtust]]'s freedom formula: wealth equals what one has minus what one wants. The episode uses the phrase to shift wealth freedom from a fixed asset threshold toward the gap between resources, capabilities, time, and desires.

The concept is not an argument for suppressing desire. The source treats desire and ambition as a wider kind of liability when they grow faster than assets, skills, relationships, and time control. The practical point is to let wanted life and owned capacity grow in a livable relation, so the person keeps choice space instead of becoming more dependent on money, status, or persona.

## Key Claims
- Increasing wealth can mean increasing assets, income, skills, and relationships, or slowing the growth of socially borrowed wants.
- [[SocialComparisonPressure]] inflates wants by replacing "enough for use" with "enough to outrank others."
- [[LowDesireDefensiveContraction]] can protect people from status games, but it becomes unhealthy if it erases curiosity, relationships, health, or future optionality.
- Wealth freedom is close to time control, refusal power, and the ability to choose work and life conditions.
- The source treats money as a bridge toward final values, not as a final dwelling place.
