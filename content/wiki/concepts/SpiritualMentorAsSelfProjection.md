# Spiritual Mentor As Self Projection / 精神导师作为自我投射

[[SpiritualMentorAsSelfProjection|Spiritual Mentor As Self Projection / 精神导师作为自我投射]] is the psychological reading pattern the episode applies to [[DemianNovel|《德米安》]]. In [[20-demian-gaiyin-de-etou-shang-na-shenmi-de-yinji-567302734]], the hosts say a second reading can make [[MaxDemian|德米安]], [[FrauEva|夏娃夫人]], [[FranzKromer|克罗默]], and [[PistoriusDemian|皮斯托留斯]] feel less like separate plot agents and more like parts of [[EmilSinclair|辛克莱尔]]'s own psyche.

The pattern extends [[JungianArchetypalReading]] without requiring a literal identity twist. A figure may be socially real inside the story while also functioning as courage, shadow, anima, desire, inherited doctrine, or self-knowledge made visible.

## Key Claims
- Mentor figures can be necessary even when their final function is to return authority to the protagonist.
- Projection reading is strongest when it explains repeated symbolic pressure, not when it deletes the story's interpersonal scenes.
- The source uses this pattern to explain why Demian and Frau Eva can feel intimate, uncanny, and archetypal at the same time.
