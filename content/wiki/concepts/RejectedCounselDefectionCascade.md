# Rejected Counsel Defection Cascade / 进言拒绝叛逃级联

## Definition
Rejected Counsel Defection Cascade / 进言拒绝叛逃级联 is the failure pattern in which a leader repeatedly ignores actionable advice, while internal factional pressure makes the ignored adviser unsafe enough to leave or defect. The damage is larger than one unused proposal because the adversary may gain both the person and the rejected information.

## Current Synthesis
The Guandu source gives the pattern through [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s camp. [[JuShou|沮授]] warns that [[ChunyuQiong|淳于琼]]'s convoy needs an outer patrol under [[JiangQiLateHan|蒋奇]], and [[XuYouLateHan|许攸]] proposes a light night attack on [[Xudu|许都]], but Yuan Shao accepts neither. Those rejected proposals matter because they identify two exploitable surfaces: the supply convoy and Cao Cao's political center.

The cascade becomes dangerous when [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]] arrests Xu You's family at [[YeLateHan|邺城]]. Xu You's grievance does not remain an internal personnel issue; he crosses to [[CaoCao|曹操]] and reveals the Gushi-Wuchao supply vulnerability. Yuan Shao therefore loses advice twice: first by not using it, then by allowing factional conflict to deliver adviser knowledge to the enemy.

## Key Claims
- Rejected advice is still information; if the adviser defects, the enemy may receive the rejected model of the system's vulnerabilities.
- The cascade depends on more than disagreement: factional punishment, family pressure, or status threat must make continued service unsafe or intolerable.
- A large talent pool can worsen the failure when rival advisers convert disagreement into internal policing.
- The immediate loss may be a person, but the decisive loss is often operational knowledge.
- The Guandu case shows the pattern moving from ignored protection and raid proposals into Wuchao target disclosure.

- Factional trigger: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1048-xuyou-pan-yuan-xianji-guandu-zhanju-de-jingtian-bianshu-lt3sewxvtpytrufrex8b0s0p0hju|Hanji 1048]] says Shen Pei arrests Xu You's family at Ye, angering Xu You into going over to Cao Cao.
- Enemy gain: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1048-xuyou-pan-yuan-xianji-guandu-zhanju-de-jingtian-bianshu-lt3sewxvtpytrufrex8b0s0p0hju|Hanji 1048]] says Xu You tells Cao Cao about vulnerable Yuan supplies at Gushi and Wuchao and recommends burning them.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The pattern should not imply that every rejected adviser will defect or that every ignored proposal is correct. The source also keeps Xu You's family offense and personal anger source-scoped, so the concept is about the organizational mechanism: rejected counsel becomes strategically costly when factional coercion converts an adviser into an enemy intelligence channel.

## What Changed
- Created the concept from Xu You's Guandu defection and Yuan Shao's ignored adviser warnings.
- Defined the cascade as advice loss plus adviser safety failure plus enemy intelligence gain.
