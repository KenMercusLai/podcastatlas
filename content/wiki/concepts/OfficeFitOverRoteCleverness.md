# Office Fit Over Rote Cleverness / 任职才具重于应对小慧

## Definition

Office fit over rote cleverness is the personnel principle that high office requires the capacity suited to its actual burdens, not merely seniority, legal familiarity, or quick verbal response. In Hanji 753, [[WeiBiaoEasternHan|Wei Biao]] applies this principle to appointments generally and to the [[ShangshuTaiImperialAccess|Shangshu]] pipeline specifically.

## Current Synthesis

Wei Biao's argument has two layers. First, he treats good two-thousand-stone officials as the upstream condition for good recommendations, because the center depends on them to identify talent outside immediate court view.

Second, he criticizes a staffing habit that draws Shangshu personnel from langguan because they know laws and can answer quickly. The tiger-enclosure steward and Zhou Bo comparison makes the point that technical familiarity and high-affair judgment are different kinds of ability.

## Key Claims

- Appointment quality depends on matching capacity to office, not just finding people with general moral reputation.
- Seniority can become a substitute for judgment when selection avoids hard evaluation.
- Legal familiarity is useful but insufficient for confidential high office.
- Quick replies may signal procedural fluency while hiding weakness in large-affair judgment.
- Recommendation channels matter because poor high-level selectors distort the entire talent pipeline.

## Evidence

- Upstream selection channel: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-753-banchao-beigao-heizhuang-lt-sksxrz30sawit81eqbeonhzv|Hanji 753]] has Wei Biao argue that strong two-thousand-stone officials are needed because they recommend capable people to the center.
- Seniority critique: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-753-banchao-beigao-heizhuang-lt-sksxrz30sawit81eqbeonhzv|Hanji 753]] warns that talent and virtue are rare and that selecting by age order alone misreads capability.
- Shangshu critique: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-753-banchao-beigao-heizhuang-lt-sksxrz30sawit81eqbeonhzv|Hanji 753]] criticizes appointing Shangshu from langguan merely because they know statutes and answer rapidly.
- Capacity mismatch analogy: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-753-banchao-beigao-heizhuang-lt-sksxrz30sawit81eqbeonhzv|Hanji 753]] uses the tiger-enclosure steward and Zhou Bo comparison to separate narrow expertise from broad governing capacity.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

- The concept does not reject technical knowledge; it rejects treating technical knowledge as a complete proxy for office fit.
- The current evidence is prescriptive advice accepted by Zhangdi, not a measured personnel-reform outcome.
- Moral character remains part of the selection problem; the concept narrows only the role-fit and false-proxy issue.
