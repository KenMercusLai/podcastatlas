# AI Training-Labor Displacement / AI训练劳动替代

## Definition
[[AITrainingLaborDisplacement]] is the feedback mechanism in which workers produce labels, examples, checks, or corrections that improve systems capable of performing part of that same work, reducing or transforming later labor demand.

## Current Synthesis
The Yonghe episode provides a direct worker account of this mechanism: models began doing the first pass on simple annotation, leaving people to check results, while unit prices for basic 2D boxes reportedly fell sharply. The same market then demanded richer descriptions, specialist domains, and 3D point-cloud interpretation.

Displacement here is not proof that the entire market shrank. Aggregate demand reportedly grew while the task mix, customer structure, qualifications, and geography changed. The relevant question is who can move with the market and who bears the cost when accumulated production experience is not recognized as a portable credential.

## Key Claims
- Human training labor can create capabilities that automate parts of later training labor.
- Substitution often begins with a first automated pass and retained human verification.
- Falling unit prices can precede complete job elimination.
- New specialized roles do not automatically compensate workers displaced from basic tasks.
- Aggregate market growth is compatible with local decline and individual downward mobility.

## Evidence

- Price and volume pressure: [[fd1dff2221c6059b14dbae1c9b96e032-fd1dff2221c6059b14dbae1c9b96e032mp3aid6b06b256ea5e837f8fe77554chid66b07190af99592282]] describes falling 2D-box prices and periods with no afternoon work.
- Uneven upgrading: [[fd1dff2221c6059b14dbae1c9b96e032-fd1dff2221c6059b14dbae1c9b96e032mp3aid6b06b256ea5e837f8fe77554chid66b07190af99592282]] reports demand for graduates, specialists, and 3D skills that the local base could not assemble.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not isolate automation from customer insourcing, price competition, macroeconomic change, recruitment limits, or firm-specific commercial performance. Market forecasts and historical unit prices are episode-reported. Higher-skill annotation may create employment elsewhere, so the concept describes distribution and transition rather than inevitable net market contraction.

## What Changed
- Added a feedback loop between training work and automation of training work.
- Added the distinction between market expansion and worker or place-level retention.
