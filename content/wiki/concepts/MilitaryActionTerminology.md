# Military Action Terminology / 伐侵袭用语

Military action terminology / 伐侵袭用语 is the episode's distinction among three classical war words in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-18-zhongguo-lishishang-di-yi-li-rentoushui-lgqnequk0rxejqgihwdqxqyqnsvq]]. The host says "伐" carries the most righteous or publicly justified tone, "侵" describes an unannounced attack, and "袭" describes a light-force surprise attack against an unprepared target.

The concept is attached to [[ZhaoFan|赵范]]'s failed attack on [[Handan|邯郸]]. Because the notice says he "袭" Handan, the source reads his move as an attempt to catch [[ZhaoSuhou|赵肃侯]] before the new ruler's position stabilizes.

## Key Claims
- Chronicle verbs are part of the evidence, not neutral synonyms.
- "伐" implies public justification and formal military display more strongly than the other terms.
- "袭" helps characterize Zhao Fan's move as surprise attack rather than open campaign.
- The source uses terminology to connect word choice with political motive and tactical form.
