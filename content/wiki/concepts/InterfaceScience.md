# Interface Science / 界面科学

Interface science / 界面科学 is the technical bridge emphasized in [[mai-xiangzao-qijia-de-bainian-huawang-weishenme-chengle-bandaoti-zhizao-de-guanjiang-qiye-1004213131]]. The episode defines the relevant capability as controlling interactions among liquids, contaminants, and solid surfaces.

For [[Kao|花王]], this means the same family of problems appears across skin care, laundry, household cleaning, electronics, and [[SemiconductorPrecisionCleaning|semiconductor precision cleaning]]. A facial cleanser must remove excess oil without damaging skin; detergent must remove dirt without damaging fibers or colors; wafer cleaning must remove particles and residues without damaging chip structures.

The concept matters because it explains why a consumer-products company can have a credible path into semiconductor materials. The company is not exporting the consumer brand into fabs; it is exporting the underlying control of wetting, dispersion, adhesion, and residue behavior.

## Key Claims
- Interface control lets cleaning chemistry act selectively rather than only aggressively.
- The same technical grammar can move from daily life to industrial manufacturing when tolerance and validation become stricter.
- In semiconductors, interface science becomes part of [[ChemicalControlAsManufacturingCapability|chemical control as manufacturing capability]] because formulation choices affect yield and process stability.
