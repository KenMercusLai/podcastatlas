# Undefined War Objectives

## Definition
Undefined war objectives are the strategic failure that arises when a government initiates military action without clear goals, success criteria, rationale, or exit strategy.

## Current Synthesis
The source uses Trump's Iran war as the case. Shapiro calls it a war of choice and argues that the administration never defined what the United States was trying to accomplish. The critique is strategic as much as partisan: if leaders cannot state whether the mission is nuclear delay, regime change, deterrence, support for Israel, or something else, then the country cannot judge success, costs, or the path out.

## Key Claims
- A war of choice requires especially clear public objectives.
- Undefined goals make exit strategy and success measurement impossible.
- Inconsistent administration explanations can signal that policy is being improvised after escalation.
- Military action should be judged by American security and economic interests, not by another country's preferences.
- The source ties strategic ambiguity to material costs such as gas-price pressure and service-member deaths.
