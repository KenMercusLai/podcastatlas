# Street Art Game Layer

Street art game layer is the pattern in [[cockroaches-1-modi-0-indias-remarkable-protests-6a6729a33e2f0b6f92d26ab8]] where public art, mobile scanning, points, and fan competition create a playable overlay on the city. The source develops it through [[Invader]]'s mosaics and the [[FlashInvaders]] app.

The concept is distinct from ordinary phone distraction. The phone records and scores the encounter, but the app's practical effect is to make players walk, look up, compare walls, and notice small tile mosaics in physical streets.

In the wiki, this links culture, urban attention, and interface design. It is adjacent to [[CityCommercialObservation]] because both turn city details into evidence, but this case uses game mechanics and public art rather than business anomalies.
