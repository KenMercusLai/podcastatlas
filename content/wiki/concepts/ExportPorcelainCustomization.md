# Export Porcelain Customization / 外销瓷定制

Export Porcelain Customization / 外销瓷定制 is the source's frame for porcelain made to fit overseas demand. In [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]], [[YuanBlueAndWhitePorcelain|Yuan blue-and-white]] already shows Jingdezhen adapting forms and decoration to West Asian and Central Asian demand.

The episode later extends the pattern to 17th- and 18th-century European demand, including armorial porcelain where family, company, or institutional insignia were added to vessels. Some painting shifted toward Guangzhou because port proximity made buyer communication faster and reduced design misunderstandings.
