# Crisis Persistence Counsel / 危局坚守进言

## Definition
Crisis Persistence Counsel / 危局坚守进言 is advice that persuades a leader not to retreat at the lowest point when withdrawal would concede the strategic decision. It is not blind stubbornness; the adviser must show why the painful hold preserves a better future option than immediate safety.

## Current Synthesis
The concept enters through [[XunYu|荀彧]]'s reply to [[CaoCao|曹操]] during the [[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]]. Cao Cao is low on grain, troops are tired, and the surrounding burden has pushed people toward [[YuanShao|袁绍]], so retreat to [[Xudu|许都]] looks like a rational safety move.

Xun Yu reframes the moment as a contest over strategic standing. Because Yuan Shao has come to Guandu for a decision, the first side to retreat yields initiative and legitimacy of momentum. The advice works because it connects morale, enemy intent, historical analogy, and Cao's emperor-backed position into a reason to keep holding until a tactical opening appears.

## Key Claims
- Persistence counsel is strongest when it distinguishes temporary exhaustion from irreversible strategic concession.
- The adviser must explain why retreat changes the opponent's options, not just urge courage.
- Historical analogy can stabilize judgment when a leader's immediate pressure makes the present look uniquely hopeless.
- The counsel remains conditional: holding is justified only if there is a plausible route to a later opening.
- In the Guandu case, the later supply-line attacks make persistence operational rather than merely emotional.

- Strategic reframing: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1048-xuyou-pan-yuan-xianji-guandu-zhanju-de-jingtian-bianshu-lt3sewxvtpytrufrex8b0s0p0hju|Hanji 1048]] has Xun Yu argue that Guandu is a decisive moment over the future order and that the first retreating side loses position.
- Legitimacy and opponent diagnosis: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1048-xuyou-pan-yuan-xianji-guandu-zhanju-de-jingtian-bianshu-lt3sewxvtpytrufrex8b0s0p0hju|Hanji 1048]] contrasts Cao Cao's emperor-backed name and adviser use with Yuan Shao's inability to use talent.
- Operational payoff: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1048-xuyou-pan-yuan-xianji-guandu-zhanju-de-jingtian-bianshu-lt3sewxvtpytrufrex8b0s0p0hju|Hanji 1048]] follows the decision to hold with convoy interception and Xu You's Wuchao intelligence.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept should not be read as a rule against retreat. If the leader lacks supplies, defensible position, follower cohesion, or a plausible way to create a later opening, persistence can become wasteful overextension. In this source, Xun Yu's advice is validated only because subsequent events expose Yuan Shao's logistics and adviser-system weaknesses.

## What Changed
- Created the concept from Xun Yu's Guandu counsel to Cao Cao.
- Defined the boundary between disciplined persistence and blind refusal to retreat.
