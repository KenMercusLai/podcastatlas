# Sincerity As Relationship Trust / 真诚作为亲密关系信任

Sincerity as relationship trust is [[XiaoShiHost|小时]]'s most explicit partner criterion in [[chuantai-ni-chi-xiangcai-ma-nvshengban-ni-yuanyi-he-xingzhuan-zhihou-de-ziji-zai-yiqi-ma-555771723]]. She says she values truthfulness, kindness, and courage, and she links romantic exhaustion to moments when a partner withholds a simple truth for convenience.

The episode's age-story makes the point concrete: the harm is not the small age difference itself, but the fact that the other person lets a false impression persist until a side conversation exposes it. In this source, sincerity is less a grand moral ideal than a working condition for intimacy: if someone offers truth directly, they need the other person to receive and reciprocate it.

## Key Claims

- Small lies can damage trust when they make the other person manage unnecessary uncertainty.
- Sincerity is relational: it requires both truthful speech and a partner who can receive it without punishment or dismissal.
- A desire for a similar partner may partly be a desire for someone who understands why truth matters.
- Sincerity differs from oversharing; it still depends on [[CommunicationBoundarySetting]] and timing.
- This concept sits near [[SincerityAgainstSocialHypocrisy]] but is narrower and practical: the issue is everyday romantic trust rather than literary resistance to a false social order.
