# Labor Evidence Preservation / 劳动争议证据保全

## Definition
Labor evidence preservation is the practice of lawfully retaining contemporaneous records that can show working time, employer direction, performance expectations, transfer, access restriction, resignation pressure, or dismissal when an employment dispute later turns on proof.

## Current Synthesis
[[ep157-kun-zai-pinduoduo-de-xitong-li-weida-de-gongsi-bu-yinggai-rang-ren-gandao-haipa-ckwriued61wwabaaaacbzp2q|EP157]] argues that knowing one worked long hours is different from proving that the employer required, knew about, or benefited from those hours. The gap grows when demands remain oral, time is not formally recorded, and forced-exit pressure is expressed through low-value assignments, inconvenient transfers, or revoked access rather than a direct termination order.

The concept therefore emphasizes early, lawful preservation of schedules, messages, email, recordings where permitted, access changes, performance communication, and transfer or dismissal discussions. It is an evidence-readiness principle, not a prediction of legal outcome: admissibility, burden of proof, privacy, recording, overtime, handbook, termination, and non-compete rules depend on jurisdiction and facts.

## Key Claims
- A true event and a provable employment claim are not the same thing.
- Oral or indirect pressure can make contemporaneous records especially important.
- Evidence should be preserved before access is revoked or the relationship ends.
- Records need context showing time, speaker, task, expectation, and consequence rather than isolated screenshots alone.
- Preservation must remain lawful and proportionate; it does not justify unauthorized access, privacy violations, or removal of protected information.

## Evidence
- Overtime proof gap - [[ep157-kun-zai-pinduoduo-de-xitong-li-weida-de-gongsi-bu-yinggai-rang-ren-gandao-haipa-ckwriued61wwabaaaacbzp2q]] says workers may know they regularly worked late yet lack evidence that the company requested or recognized the work.
- Dispute preparation - [[ep157-kun-zai-pinduoduo-de-xitong-li-weida-de-gongsi-bu-yinggai-rang-ren-gandao-haipa-ckwriued61wwabaaaacbzp2q]] recommends retaining work-time and employment communication through recordings, email, and chat where lawful and relevant.
- Indirect exit pressure - [[ep157-kun-zai-pinduoduo-de-xitong-li-weida-de-gongsi-bu-yinggai-rang-ren-gandao-haipa-ckwriued61wwabaaaacbzp2q]] describes low-value work, relocation, access removal, and prolonged inactivity as conduct whose meaning may depend on a documented sequence rather than one explicit instruction.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is a podcast discussion, not a jurisdiction-specific legal guide. The existence of records does not establish admissibility, authenticity, employer knowledge, compensable time, unlawful dismissal, or entitlement to a remedy. Workers should not breach confidentiality, data-security rules, or applicable law in the name of preservation, and high-stakes disputes require qualified local advice.

## What Changed
- Established the distinction between lived overtime or exit pressure and evidence capable of supporting a later claim.
