# Animal Intelligence Modes

## Definition
Animal intelligence modes is the wiki's frame for comparing how animals solve problems through body, environment, social attention, causal inference, human signals, and species-specific needs rather than treating intelligence as one simple ladder.

## Current Synthesis
The concept began with a wolf-dog-human contrast from the Rowlands/Brenin source: wolves are framed as more mechanically and causally attentive, dogs as more socially embedded with humans, and humans as social modelers whose intelligence can become cooperation, deception, hierarchy, and excuse-making. The Cesar Millan episode adds the dog-training version of the same distinction. Dogs are presented as highly responsive to human energy, body language, intention, movement, and timing before words.

The current synthesis therefore treats dog intelligence as relational without making it merely sentimental. A dog can read and use the human, but that makes the human more responsible, not less. Training and care work best when the person observes the animal's state, meets movement and safety needs, and avoids projecting human stories onto behavior too quickly.

## Key Claims
- Intelligence is not one ladder with humans at the top; different animals solve different environmental and social problems.
- Wolf intelligence is source-scoped as more mechanical and causally attentive in the Brenin discussion.
- Dog intelligence is framed as socially embedded in human life, using human attention, action, and signals as part of the problem-solving environment.
- Human intelligence includes mind-reading, cooperation, deception, hierarchy, and rationalization, so it can damage as well as deepen human-animal relations.
- Dog handling requires observation of state, energy, body language, and context before interpretation or verbal instruction.
- Embodied and embedded cognition matter because thinking happens through body, movement, environment, tools, and other beings.

## Evidence
- Wolf, dog, and human contrast - [[39-zhexuejia-yu-lang-zai-langgeduoke-yongheng-de-xiatian-611233065]] uses door-opening examples and Brenin's behavior to distinguish wolf mechanical problem-solving, dog social intelligence, and human social calculation.
- Human civilization and deception - [[39-zhexuejia-yu-lang-zai-langgeduoke-yongheng-de-xiatian-611233065]] connects human intelligence to conspiracy, status exchange, lying, detecting lies, and responsibility failure around weaker beings.
- Dog social reading - [[raising-a-dog-mastering-calm-assertive-energy-cesar-millan-scim6380580289]] has Millan argue that dogs respond to energy, silence, body language, intention, leash handling, and action before sound.
- Observation before projection - [[raising-a-dog-mastering-calm-assertive-energy-cesar-millan-scim6380580289]] asks owners to understand dogs as spirit, animal, species, breed, and name, while [[39-zhexuejia-yu-lang-zai-langgeduoke-yongheng-de-xiatian-611233065]] keeps companion loyalty and present-tense life distinct from human contract logic.
- Embodied routine - [[raising-a-dog-mastering-calm-assertive-energy-cesar-millan-scim6380580289]] treats walks, greeting rituals, feeding, exercise, challenge, rest, and sleep as part of how dog behavior and human communication become legible.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The wolf-dog-human contrast is interpretive and source-scoped, not a full ethological taxonomy. Millan's claims about energy, pack positions, breed secondary status, and calm surrender should be stored as practical dog-handling philosophy rather than universal science. Human observers can easily overread animal behavior through affection, guilt, dominance scripts, or clever stories, so the concept needs [[ObservationBeforeInference]] as a guardrail.
