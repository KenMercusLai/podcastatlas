# Animal Intelligence Modes

## Definition
Animal intelligence modes is the wiki's frame for comparing how animals solve problems through body, environment, sensory specialization, social attention, causal inference, human signals, and species-specific needs rather than arranging minds on one simple ladder.

## Current Synthesis
The Rowlands/Brenin source contrasts wolves' mechanical and causal attention, dogs' social embeddedness with humans, and humans' capacity for cooperation, deception, hierarchy, and excuse-making. The Cesar Millan episode adds a practical claim that dogs attend to human state, movement, body language, intention, and timing before verbal explanation. The Westlund episode broadens the frame beyond dog handling: cognition is expressed through a species' sensory world, ecological problems, play rules, lateralized signals, attachment, food seeking, and threat assessment.

The resulting synthesis is relational and comparative. Dogs can use human attention as part of their environment; cats organize hunting, scent, and territory differently; horses' vigilance reflects prey and herd history; social play requires participants to read meta-signals and modulate strength. Shared emotional processes can be real without proving identical motives. Good interpretation therefore lies between anthropomorphism and anthropodenial.

## Key Claims
- Intelligence is plural and ecological rather than one ladder with humans at the top.
- Dog cognition is unusually embedded in human signals, action, and relationship history.
- Sensory ecology and predator-prey status shape what information an animal prioritizes.
- Play can require meta-communication, role reversal, activity shifts, and self-handicapping.
- Human intelligence can deepen care but also support projection, deception, hierarchy, and rationalization.
- Interpretation should recognize plausible cross-species continuities while preserving species-specific differences.

- Wolf, dog, and human contrast - [[39-zhexuejia-yu-lang-zai-langgeduoke-yongheng-de-xiatian-611233065]] uses problem-solving and Brenin's behavior to distinguish mechanical, social, and human strategic intelligence.
- Dog social reading - [[raising-a-dog-mastering-calm-assertive-energy-cesar-millan-scim6380580289]] emphasizes human energy, silence, body language, intention, leash handling, and action as signals dogs use.
- Sensory and ecological specialization - [[what-pets-actually-want-need-dr-karolina-westlund-scim2878719314]] compares horses, dogs, cats, parrots, primates, and zoo animals through predation, vigilance, foraging, scent, lateralization, and social organization.
- Social play and emotion reading - [[what-pets-actually-want-need-dr-karolina-westlund-scim2878719314]] uses play bows, role reversal, self-handicapping, fairness examples, and group co-regulation to describe socially responsive cognition.
- Observation before projection - [[39-zhexuejia-yu-lang-zai-langgeduoke-yongheng-de-xiatian-611233065]], [[raising-a-dog-mastering-calm-assertive-energy-cesar-millan-scim6380580289]], and [[what-pets-actually-want-need-dr-karolina-westlund-scim2878719314]] warn, in different language, that human affection, moral storytelling, or training ideology can obscure the animal's actual state and needs.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The wolf-dog-human contrast and Millan's energy language remain source-scoped rather than a complete cognitive taxonomy. Westlund explicitly marks some interpretations, including cat chattering and broad empathy claims, as tentative. Tail lateralization, fairness demonstrations, and play markers are context-sensitive findings, not universal mind-reading tools. The anthropomorphism-anthropodenial balance requires both openness and evidentiary restraint.
