# Animal Intelligence Modes

## Definition
Animal intelligence modes is the wiki's frame for comparing how animals solve problems through body, environment, sensory specialization, social attention, causal inference, human signals, and species-specific needs rather than arranging minds on one simple ladder.

## Current Synthesis
The Rowlands/Brenin source contrasts wolves' mechanical and causal attention, dogs' social embeddedness with humans, and humans' capacity for cooperation, deception, hierarchy, and excuse-making. The Cesar Millan episode adds a practical claim that dogs attend to human state, movement, body language, intention, and timing before verbal explanation. The Westlund episode broadens the frame beyond dog handling: cognition is expressed through a species' sensory world, ecological problems, play rules, lateralized signals, attachment, food seeking, and threat assessment.

The resulting synthesis is relational and comparative. Dogs can use human attention as part of their environment; cats organize hunting, scent, and territory differently; horses' vigilance reflects prey and herd history; social play requires participants to read meta-signals and modulate strength. The wolf-yawning study in [[08cf7dbf07d967235ec2004c6e8b93f5-08cf7dbf07d967235ec2004c6e8b93f5mp3aid6af5967413c30781a320173dchidd556eb54-6160-4]] adds that domestication may redistribute social responsiveness: dog ancestry was associated with less wolf-to-wolf yawn contagion, while broadly connected wolves triggered more copying than dominant or closely related animals.

Task design and model selection further qualify the comparison. Raccoons can defeat urban barriers through tactile, action-first persistence while performing poorly on some standard cognition measures; rats can learn directional vehicle controls while their tractability helps make them psychology's default. Intelligence claims therefore depend on what a task rewards, which behaviors a laboratory permits, and whether ecological effectiveness is mistaken for—or excluded from—a general cognitive ranking. Shared processes can be real without proving identical motives, empathy, general intelligence, or human equivalence.

## Key Claims
- Intelligence is plural and ecological rather than one ladder with humans at the top.
- Dog cognition is unusually embedded in human signals, action, and relationship history.
- Sensory ecology and predator-prey status shape what information an animal prioritizes.
- Play can require meta-communication, role reversal, activity shifts, and self-handicapping.
- Human intelligence can deepen care but also support projection, deception, hierarchy, and rationalization.
- Task performance reflects the interaction among species-typical behavior, sensory ecology, motivation, and experimental design rather than intelligence alone.
- Domestication can alter the direction and structure of social attention, while group-wide social centrality may predict behavioral contagion differently from hierarchy, kinship, or intimate bonds.

## Evidence
- Wolf, dog, and human contrast - [[39-zhexuejia-yu-lang-zai-langgeduoke-yongheng-de-xiatian-611233065]] uses problem-solving and Brenin's behavior to distinguish mechanical, social, and human strategic intelligence.
- Dog social reading - [[raising-a-dog-mastering-calm-assertive-energy-cesar-millan-scim6380580289]] emphasizes human energy, silence, body language, intention, leash handling, and action as signals dogs use.
- Sensory and ecological specialization - [[what-pets-actually-want-need-dr-karolina-westlund-scim2878719314]] compares horses, dogs, cats, parrots, primates, and zoo animals through predation, vigilance, foraging, scent, lateralization, and social organization.
- Social play and emotion reading - [[what-pets-actually-want-need-dr-karolina-westlund-scim2878719314]] uses play bows, role reversal, self-handicapping, fairness examples, and group co-regulation to describe socially responsive cognition.
- Observation before projection - [[39-zhexuejia-yu-lang-zai-langgeduoke-yongheng-de-xiatian-611233065]], [[raising-a-dog-mastering-calm-assertive-energy-cesar-millan-scim6380580289]], and [[what-pets-actually-want-need-dr-karolina-westlund-scim2878719314]] warn, in different language, that human affection, moral storytelling, or training ideology can obscure the animal's actual state and needs.
- Domestication and network position - [[08cf7dbf07d967235ec2004c6e8b93f5-08cf7dbf07d967235ec2004c6e8b93f5mp3aid6af5967413c30781a320173dchidd556eb54-6160-4]] reports lower yawn copying among captive wolves with dog ancestry and greater yawn spread from wolves connected broadly across the pack.
- Task and ecological fit - [[rat-vs-raccoon-rat-vs-raccoon|Rat vs. Raccoon]] contrasts raccoons' weak performance on selected standard measures with their persistent urban problem-solving and contrasts rat learning with laboratory tractability.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The wolf-dog-human contrast and Millan's energy language remain source-scoped rather than a complete cognitive taxonomy. Westlund explicitly marks some interpretations, including cat chattering and broad empathy claims, as tentative. The yawn study uses 33 captive wolves and does not establish mechanism, empathy, wild-population effects, or conservation consequences. The raccoon source does not provide the methods needed to compare Cole's puzzle boxes, MacDonald's tests, urban bin opening, or Lambert's rat driving on one scale. Tail lateralization, fairness demonstrations, play markers, contagious yawning, and task success are context-sensitive findings, not universal mind-reading tools. The anthropomorphism-anthropodenial balance requires both openness and evidentiary restraint.

## What Changed
- Added dog ancestry and pack-network position as possible influences on wolf social-response contagion.
- Distinguished an observable behavioral marker from empathy, genetic mechanism, and ecological consequence.
- Added task design and experimental tractability as determinants of which animal abilities become visible.
- Separated urban effectiveness and persistent exploration from a claim of exceptional general intelligence.
