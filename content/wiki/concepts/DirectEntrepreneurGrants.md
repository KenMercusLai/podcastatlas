# Direct Entrepreneur Grants

Direct entrepreneur grants are the policy tool tested through the [[YouWinProgram|YouWin Program]] in [[piles-of-cash-and-a-town-of-solutions-in-kenya-nigeria-summer-school]]. Instead of asking banks to lend to tiny firms without reliable records, the program gave screened entrepreneurs large grants that did not need to be repaid.

The source treats grants as a workaround for [[SmallBusinessFinancingGap]], not a full development recipe. [[LariatAlhassan]] used her grant to hire staff and rent a showroom for [[LarkluxPaint]], but she still later struggled to access more finance, keeping the broader [[MissingMiddle]] problem unresolved.

## Key Claims
- Grants can relax capital constraints when promising firms cannot prove themselves to banks.
- Screening and training can improve the pool before money is allocated.
- Randomization made the YouWin grant effect measurable through [[RandomizedControlledTrials]].
- Program continuity, later financing, and legal-financial infrastructure still matter after the first grant.
