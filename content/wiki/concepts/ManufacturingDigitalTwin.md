# Manufacturing Digital Twin / 制造数字孪生

Manufacturing digital twin / 制造数字孪生 is the factory-simulation layer described in [[ep271-tanfang-rouxing-zhizao-gongchang-shi-shui-rang-ni-xiangyao-jiu-neng-maidao-lqyrioptggnbb8hl-llej0-5ow0v]] through [[HaierGroup|海尔]]'s commercial central-air-conditioning factory. The source says Haier builds a digital system corresponding to the physical factory, then runs order and process logic digitally before physical production so problems can be discovered before they become rework.

This concept is distinct from [[WorkplaceDigitalTwins|workplace digital twins]], which represent a person through documents and meetings. A manufacturing digital twin represents equipment, layout, workflows, process dependencies, and production state. In EP271, the digital twin works with [[COSMOPlat|卡奥斯]], [[ManufacturingDigitalThread|the digital thread]], and [[ModularCustomization|module combinations]] to make large customized equipment more manageable.

## Key Claims
- Digital twins are most useful when physical iteration is costly, slow, or disruptive.
- In large-equipment manufacturing, simulation can reveal layout, sequence, process, and dependency problems before workers move heavy parts.
- Running orders digitally can reduce rework only if the model reflects real machines, materials, and process constraints.
- Digital twins do not replace manufacturing judgment; they make hidden coordination problems visible earlier.
- The concept becomes more valuable when paired with modular product architecture and live production data.
