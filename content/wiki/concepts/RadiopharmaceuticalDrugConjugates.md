# Radiopharmaceutical Drug Conjugates / RDC

Radiopharmaceutical drug conjugates, or RDCs, are introduced in [[156-shengwu-yiyao-de-2026-dang-shichang-bu-zai-wei-bd-zaodong-zhongguo-yaoqi-de-xingchen-dahai-cai-ganggang-zhankai-lil-ugrzq8uvzviq3f8i-wm9ilup]] as a radiation version of [[ADCEngineeringOptimization|ADC-like targeted delivery]]. The source's analogy is that ADCs deliver chemical payloads, while RDCs deliver radioactive payloads to target tissue.

The episode emphasizes operational constraints. Radioactive isotopes have half-lives and regulatory handling requirements, so RDC value depends on manufacturing location, hospital proximity, logistics, radiation-control procedures, and clinical workflow as much as molecular targeting.

## Key Claims
- RDCs can be understood as targeted radiotherapy rather than targeted chemotherapy.
- Smaller carriers such as peptides may fit some RDC uses better than antibodies because tissue penetration and timing matter.
- The source says current cancer use concentrates in areas such as prostate cancer and neuroendocrine tumors.
- Supply-chain and regulatory execution are core capability tests, not afterthoughts.
