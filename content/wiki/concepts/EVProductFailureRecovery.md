# EV Product Failure Recovery / 电动车产品失败后的恢复

## Definition
EV product failure recovery is the process by which an automaker learns from a poorly defined or poorly received vehicle, preserves enough capital and organizational confidence to continue, and then uses later models to regain market fit, volume, and investor credibility.

## Current Synthesis
The Leapmotor case shows recovery as a sequence rather than a single pivot. S01 exposed product-definition and 2C marketing gaps. T03 created enough volume improvement to escape the worst stagnation. C11 then became a higher-stakes turning point because the company continued the project while funds were tight and used configuration-to-price contrast to attract buyers. The pattern depends on capital endurance as much as product insight.

## Key Claims
- A failed first EV can be survivable if it reveals specific product-definition errors rather than only producing blame.
- Recovery requires a product that fits a clearer demand segment before the company runs out of capital.
- Capital support from founders, local government, or investors can decide whether the improved product ever reaches market.
- A later turning-point model needs a clear value proposition that buyers can understand before delivery.
- Product recovery remains incomplete until service, warranty, quality, and profitability prove durable beyond launch momentum.

## Evidence
- Failure diagnosis: [[lingpao-qiche-zhujiangming-luoyonghao-lingpao-shinian-buhui-jiang-gushi-de-ren-ruhe-maicheng-diyi-lhei72y0mlqrh2xxbetnqdp-0stb]] says S01's two-door coupe positioning, weak 2C marketing, and cautious spending caused a poor launch.
- Recovery product evidence: [[lingpao-qiche-zhujiangming-luoyonghao-lingpao-shinian-buhui-jiang-gushi-de-ren-ruhe-maicheng-diyi-lhei72y0mlqrh2xxbetnqdp-0stb]] says T03 grew from hundreds to thousands of monthly units and sold more than 40,000 units in 2021.
- Capital endurance evidence: [[lingpao-qiche-zhujiangming-luoyonghao-lingpao-shinian-buhui-jiang-gushi-de-ren-ruhe-maicheng-diyi-lhei72y0mlqrh2xxbetnqdp-0stb]] says Zhu and a deputy kept injecting funds for several months around mid-2020 before Hefei investment and renewed EV-market financing helped.
- Turning-point evidence: [[lingpao-qiche-zhujiangming-luoyonghao-lingpao-shinian-buhui-jiang-gushi-de-ren-ruhe-maicheng-diyi-lhei72y0mlqrh2xxbetnqdp-0stb]] says C11 was continued despite funding doubts and later exceeded expectations.
- Durability boundary: [[lingpao-qiche-zhujiangming-luoyonghao-lingpao-shinian-buhui-jiang-gushi-de-ren-ruhe-maicheng-diyi-lhei72y0mlqrh2xxbetnqdp-0stb]] later shifts to service speed, net promoter score, lifetime warranty, financial health, and profitability as unresolved proof points beyond product launch.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is a retrospective founder account. It may understate external factors such as policy cycles, competitor missteps, financing windows, distribution changes, and consumer sentiment. It also does not independently verify the sales or profitability attached to the recovery products.
