## Definition
Football club legacy transfer is the contested process by which supporters, players, slogans, colors, academy systems, venues, and city identity move or fail to move from a defunct club to a new institution.

## Current Synthesis
The Guangzhou case shows that legacy transfer is not automatic. A city can regain top-flight football through [[GuangzhouLeopard]], but former [[GuangzhouFC]] supporters may still resist if they see no direct institutional rescue. A lower-tier team such as [[GuangdongChenxingJuli]] can carry more emotional continuity if it has academy players, old chants, and visible lineage, even without the highest league status.

## Key Claims
- Supporters distinguish city representation from concrete club lineage.
- Academy players and chants can carry legacy more persuasively than a new team's formal city name.
- A new club's legitimacy depends on explanation, performance, time, and whether old supporters feel the original club was abandoned.
- Different football cities can treat succession differently; the source contrasts Guangzhou with more city-name-centered cases such as Dalian and Shaanxi.
- Legacy transfer can split between sporting level, emotional continuity, youth-development pipeline, and local government narratives.

## Evidence
- Chants and identity: [[vol-225-hengda-de-zuqiu-yichan-ft-panweili-gkwrijeozrpkafcpbatn-ivb]] says Chenxing Juli supporters kept chanting "广州队" despite changes in registration, venue, and name.
- Leopard resistance: [[vol-225-hengda-de-zuqiu-yichan-ft-panweili-gkwrijeozrpkafcpbatn-ivb]] says many old supporters resist Guangzhou Leopard because they see it as a new team after the old one was not rescued.
- City comparison: [[vol-225-hengda-de-zuqiu-yichan-ft-panweili-gkwrijeozrpkafcpbatn-ivb]] contrasts Guangzhou with Dalian and Shaanxi, where supporters are described as more willing to rally around a city name.
- Time-and-results pathway: [[vol-225-hengda-de-zuqiu-yichan-ft-panweili-gkwrijeozrpkafcpbatn-ivb]] says Guangzhou Leopard might gain partial support if it stays in the top league and competes well over a long period.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source's support-share estimate is speculative, and supporter identity can change as new generations attend matches. This concept should not imply that only direct legal continuity matters.
