## Definition

赏谏不纳 / rewarded but unadopted remonstrance is the court-feedback pattern in which a ruler praises, promotes, or materially rewards a critic while leaving the criticized policy or behavior substantially unchanged.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 253 supplies two paired cases under [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]. [[DongfangShuo|东方朔]] connects enlargement of [[ShanglinParkWesternHan|上林苑]] to lost livelihoods, reduced revenue, forced displacement, historical warnings, and danger during hunting. Wudi reportedly promotes and rewards him but continues the enlargement. [[SimaXiangru|司马相如]] separately argues that imperial responsibility makes personal pursuit of dangerous animals an unjustifiable irreversible risk; Wudi praises and rewards him but continues hunting.

The pattern matters because speaker treatment and decision correction are different variables. Reward may keep a talented adviser near court, acknowledge rhetorical skill, display tolerance, or reduce the social cost of refusal. None of those effects by itself demonstrates [[ReceptiveRemonstranceGovernance|纳谏转政策式治理]], whose threshold is a change in policy, enforcement, burden, or conduct.

## Key Claims

- Permission to speak, personal safety, reward, and policy adoption are distinct stages of a functioning advice channel.
- Reward can recognize the adviser while insulating the ruler's preferred decision from the advice's practical consequence.
- Repeated reward without adoption may preserve court talent but can also teach advisers that criticism is ceremonial rather than operational.
- The clearest diagnosis requires evidence both of favorable treatment toward the speaker and of continuity in the criticized action.
- A ruler may adapt around a symptom, as with safer lodging, while retaining the underlying preference that generates the wider risk.

Irreversible ruler-safety warning:

Symptom adaptation without preference reversal:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-253-hanwudi-nimen-shuo-de-dou-dui-dan-wo-jiushi-bu-ting-lhvsfcppigvfd0j2xdhqorxqvwb_|Hanji 253]] says Wudi establishes concealed lodging stations after an inn confrontation but does not abandon incognito recreational travel.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Reward without immediate adoption is not always bad faith. A ruler can protect a sincere adviser, preserve disagreement, or delay action while gathering evidence. This source does not establish Wudi's private agreement, the advisers' later influence, or the long-run implementation history of every proposal. The concept applies narrowly where favorable treatment and continued criticized conduct are both reported; it does not infer hypocrisy solely from a reward.
