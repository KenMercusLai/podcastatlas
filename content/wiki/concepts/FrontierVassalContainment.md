# 边境迁封式牵制 / Frontier Vassal Containment

边境迁封式牵制 / frontier vassal containment enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-174-liubang-weihe-dafeng-zijide-chouren-1-lnzzvpieqtgatmvgogz3msyhs60u|Hanji 174]] as [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s relocation of [[HanWangXin|韩王信]]. The source says Han Wang Xin is talented and that his old Han territory lies too close to strategic interior centers such as Luoyang and Huaiyang, so Liu Bang moves him to a new northern Han kingdom in the Taiyuan area.

The containment works by making danger useful. Han Wang Xin is not simply stripped of title; he is placed against [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] pressure, first at [[Jinyang|晋阳]] and then, after his request, at [[Mayi|马邑]]. His military ability becomes frontier defense, while his distance from the interior reduces his immediate threat to the imperial center.

This differs from [[HostileTerritoryRoyalContainment|敌意封国安置]] for Han Xin. Han Xin is moved into a locally difficult former Chu region after victory; Han Wang Xin is moved outward to a border where external threat and political restraint coincide.

## Key Claims
- A capable vassal can be contained by relocation rather than direct removal.
- Frontier assignment can turn a potential interior threat into external-defense capacity.
- The tactic is ambiguous because it may increase the vassal's military exposure and local autonomy while reducing central proximity.
- Capital placement inside the frontier kingdom matters: Mayi makes the assignment more militarily responsive and more politically exposed.
