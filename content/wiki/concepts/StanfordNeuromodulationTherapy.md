# Stanford Neuromodulation Therapy

## Definition
Stanford Neuromodulation Therapy is the accelerated, high-dose TMS treatment protocol Williams describes as a targeted stimulation approach for high-acuity depression.

## Current Synthesis
In the source, [[NolanWilliams]] says SAINT originally stood for Stanford Accelerated Intelligent Neuromodulation Therapy and is now called Stanford Neuromodulation Therapy. He distinguishes the TMS device from the treatment protocol: the therapy is the use of stimulation at a chosen region, timing, spacing, and dose for a specific psychiatric condition. Its design goal is high-acuity depression, where slower or less intensive options may not fit the clinical urgency.

The key synthesis is compression plus learning-like timing. Traditional TMS is described as once daily across six weeks, while SNT compresses a larger dose into five days with hourly sessions. Williams frames the signal as asking the prefrontal cortex to turn on and remain on. Reported remission rates and durability are promising in the episode, but they remain source-scoped and context-dependent.

## Key Claims
- SNT is a protocol, not merely the presence of a TMS machine.
- The protocol targets a specific brain region for a specific high-acuity psychiatric use case.
- It reorganizes stimulation around spaced learning theory and repeated hourly sessions.
- The source contrasts six-week daily TMS with a five-day, high-dose schedule.
- Williams reports rapid remission in substantial fractions of treated patients, depending on trial or open-label context.
- Durability varies, with some patients maintaining remission much longer than others.

## Evidence
- Naming and scope - [[essentials-psychedelics-neurostimulation-for-brain-rewiring-dr-nolan-williams-scim5296180263]] explains SAINT/SNT and distinguishes device from protocol.
- Dose compression - [[essentials-psychedelics-neurostimulation-for-brain-rewiring-dr-nolan-williams-scim5296180263]] describes ten hourly treatment blocks per day across five days.
- Outcome claims - [[essentials-psychedelics-neurostimulation-for-brain-rewiring-dr-nolan-williams-scim5296180263]] reports 60% to 90% remission in different contexts and variable durability.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode does not provide a patient-selection protocol, contraindication list, operational manual, or independent systematic review. SNT should remain a clinically delivered treatment concept, not a generic productivity or mood-reset technique.
