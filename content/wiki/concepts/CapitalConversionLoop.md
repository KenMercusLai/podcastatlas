# Capital Conversion Loop / 资本转换循环

Capital conversion loop is [[162-caifu-de-benzhi-yiji-ziyou-de-zhenzheng-hanyi-chuantai-shifen-xiyin-lofyxfhiprud7zvekufexsjhtust]]'s pattern for how economic, cultural, social, and symbolic capital can transform into one another. The episode uses [[BourdieuCapitalForms]] to argue that wealth moves not only through market prices, but also through reputation, credentials, networks, credibility, and stories that make resources easier or harder to mobilize.

The strongest negative case is [[AnnaSorokin|Anna Sorokin]]. The source says she fabricated economic capital and used fashion, hotels, art, parties, elite social scenes, and an art-foundation story to create cultural, social, and symbolic capital. The attempted bank loan was meant to close the loop back into economic capital, but verification, unpaid bills, and failed checks broke the structure.

## Key Claims
- Capital conversion can be legitimate when ability, trust, credentials, and reputation help a person or institution mobilize resources.
- Conversion becomes fragile when symbolic or social capital is not supported by cash flow, rights, competence, or enforceable claims.
- [[SocialComparisonPressure]] makes conversion more powerful because people watch the visible signs of capital and infer hidden resources from them.
- Social media can turn even failed status performance into monetizable symbolic capital if attention, controversy, and story rights become saleable.
- Investors should watch conversion loops because market narratives, analyst labels, policy recognition, and reputation can change asset pricing before fundamentals fully show up.
