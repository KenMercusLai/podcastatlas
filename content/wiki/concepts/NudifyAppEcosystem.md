# Nudify App Ecosystem

Nudify app ecosystem is the wiki's frame for the commercial and distribution stack around AI tools that generate sexualized images or videos from real people's photos. [[tech-20260727-0727-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260727-0727-mp-tech-pod-128]] grounds the concept through [[SinaLaubenstein]] and the [[InstituteForStrategicDialogue]], whose report found 181 websites offering these services.

The concept extends [[AINonConsensualIntimateImageAbuse]] beyond the moment an image is generated. In this source, the ecosystem includes social discovery through [[YouTube]] and [[Twitter|X]], paid or repeated promotion on [[Facebook]] and [[Instagram]], app-store search and removal decisions, legal pressure under the [[TakeItDownAct|Take It Down Act]] and [[California]] law, and [[AbusePaymentInfrastructure]] that can keep abusive services profitable.

## Key Claims
- The harm surface includes discovery, promotion, installation, payment, and legal response, not only generated output.
- Plain searches and direct ad language can expose nudify services without sophisticated keyword evasion.
- App-store removal and search-setting changes can reduce visibility, but do not prove the services have been eliminated.
- Payment and crypto routing matter because abusive services need profitable checkout paths, not only traffic.
- Enforcement is strongest when platform, app-store, payment, banking, and legal mechanisms are treated as one intervention map.
