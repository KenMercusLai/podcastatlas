# Stir-Fry Revival / 爆炒与小炒回潮

Stir-fry revival / 爆炒与小炒回潮 is the episode's closing trend for diners returning to hotter, rougher, fresher, and more daily-feeling restaurant formats. [[ep245-yinian-yidu-xunwei-zhilv-jinnian-qu-chi-chuangyi-cai-li9wtavemokcckialbbj7unpmqsz]] contrasts this with over-polished dining experiences: young diners may seek wok heat, speed, fresh offal, and small-shop directness after becoming tired of refinement.

The Guangxi examples make the concept concrete. 爆炒生料 depends on fresh pork, beef, offal, and precise textural control; Yulin, Liuzhou, Guilin, and Nanning versions differ in original flavor, acidity, heat, mixed pork-beef textures, crispness, tenderness, glutinousness, and sweetness. That ties the trend to [[MarketFreshnessCulture|market freshness culture]] and [[FoodTextureAesthetics|口感]] as much as to nostalgia.

## Key Claims
- The return of stir-fry is not only retro taste; it answers a desire for heat, texture, freshness, and visible cooking force.
- Small shops can feel newly attractive when refinement starts to look over-designed.
- The format can surface regional differences in raw material, acid, heat, offal handling, and texture.
- The trend belongs beside [[CreativeChineseCuisine|创意中餐]] because everyday forms can be creatively revalued without becoming fine dining.
