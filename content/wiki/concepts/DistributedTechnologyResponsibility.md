# Distributed Technology Responsibility / 分布式技术责任

## Definition

Distributed technology responsibility is the allocation of ethical and political responsibility across those who set a system's goals, design and build it, authorize deployment, accept or use it, and bear its direct or externalized consequences.

## Current Synthesis

The Pandora story contains a responsibility chain rather than a single culprit. [[Zeus]] sets a harmful objective, [[Hephaestus]] and other gods contribute specialist capabilities, [[Epimetheus]] accepts the gift, [[Pandora]] opens the jar, and humanity bears the consequences. Blaming only the immediate actor hides the authority and design choices that made the outcome likely.

Modern technical systems intensify this separation because researchers, firms, deployers, users, and affected non-users may have different knowledge, interests, power, and exit options. Responsibility should follow causal contribution, decision authority, foreseeability, benefit, and capacity to prevent or repair harm without erasing individual agency.

## Key Claims

- Responsibility does not end with the person who performs the last visible action.
- Goal-setting and deployment authority carry special weight because they organize others' contributions.
- Specialist makers remain relevant when their design choices make a harmful objective effective.
- Consent or use does not absorb harms imposed on less powerful users or non-users.
- Responsibility can be shared without becoming equal or disappearing into complexity.
- Evaluation should include benefit distribution, externalities, prevention capacity, and repair duties.

- Mythic chain: [[369-panduola-shi-renlei-zuizao-de-keji-emeng-ma-hesie-de-gongzuo-yu-shiri-lkr0ibzoden-ip-es5xpnhnbvz7]] separates Zeus's plan, divine manufacture, Epimetheus's acceptance, Pandora's act, and humanity's burden.
- Blame concentration: [[369-panduola-shi-renlei-zuizao-de-keji-emeng-ma-hesie-de-gongzuo-yu-shiri-lkr0ibzoden-ip-es5xpnhnbvz7]] argues that curiosity-centered blame obscures the designer's decisive role.
- Modern systems: [[369-panduola-shi-renlei-zuizao-de-keji-emeng-ma-hesie-de-gongzuo-yu-shiri-lkr0ibzoden-ip-es5xpnhnbvz7]] distinguishes researchers, institutions, users, and affected outsiders whose interests need not align.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Shared responsibility should not become an accountability sink in which everyone is involved and nobody is answerable. Allocation requires case-specific evidence about authority, knowledge, coercion, foreseeability, benefit, and feasible alternatives. Mythic roles illuminate the structure but do not determine liability in modern law or engineering practice.

## What Changed

- Created a responsibility-allocation framework spanning goals, design, deployment, use, and external effects.
