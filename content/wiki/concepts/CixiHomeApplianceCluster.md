# Cixi Home Appliance Cluster / 慈溪家电产业链

## Definition

Cixi home appliance cluster / 慈溪家电产业链 is the local appliance manufacturing base around Ningbo's Cixi area, combining household workshops, parts suppliers, finished-product firms, and channel-oriented appliance companies.

## Current Synthesis

[[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]] uses Cixi to explain why [[FangtaiGroup|方太]] and [[AUXGroup|奥克斯]] could grow inside a local appliance ecosystem while choosing different routes. Fangtai moved toward higher-end product definition and brand ownership; AUX is used as the scale, cost, and channel-efficiency comparison.

The cluster matters because regional manufacturing capacity is cumulative. Parts, workshops, managers, suppliers, and export routines make it easier for new appliance companies to assemble capabilities, but they do not dictate one strategy.

## Key Claims

- A cluster can support multiple strategies inside the same industry.
- Supplier density lowers the cost of moving from parts and workshops toward finished appliance firms.
- Local manufacturing depth does not remove the need for product, channel, or cost strategy.
- Cixi's appliance base helps explain Fangtai's launch context but does not fully explain Fangtai's premium route.

## Evidence

- **Cluster scale:** [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]] reports thousands of appliance firms and nearly ten thousand supporting firms in Cixi.
- **Global output role:** [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]] says Cixi produces a large share of global small appliances.
- **Route comparison:** [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]] contrasts Fangtai's product route with AUX's scale/cost/channel route.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

- The source provides scale figures but does not map all Cixi appliance subsectors.
- Cluster presence is enabling infrastructure, not a deterministic cause of firm success.

## What Changed

- Added Cixi's home-appliance cluster as the local industrial base behind Fangtai and AUX.
