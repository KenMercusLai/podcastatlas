# Mao Sui Self-Recommendation / 毛遂自荐

Mao Sui self-recommendation / 毛遂自荐 is the idiom and talent-visibility pattern introduced in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-1-lvqbmaabsvbdz2zibgmseidx5phe]]. During the [[Handan|邯郸]] emergency, [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] needs twenty capable retainers for a [[ChuState|楚国]] aid mission but finds only nineteen; [[MaoSui|毛遂]] then volunteers himself.

The episode links the phrase "毛遂自荐于平原君" to the idiom of self-recommendation. It also links Mao Sui's awl-in-a-bag answer to "脱颖而出": talent may not show because it has not been placed in the right occasion, not because it does not exist.

The concept extends [[WarringStatesRetainerPatronage|战国养士]] by adding a selection failure mode. A large patronage household can contain hidden ability, but the patron's normal reputation filters may still miss it; crisis lets the overlooked retainer force the evaluation problem into the open.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-2-ltlobdujy-hdipimfdupvwod2ech]] turns the idiom from self-nomination into tested usefulness. Mao Sui does not merely gain a place in [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]]'s delegation; he intervenes when Pingyuanjun's negotiation with [[MiWan|楚考烈王]] has stalled for a full day. The source therefore adds a second condition to the concept: self-recommendation only becomes vindicated when the hidden retainer can produce results under crisis pressure.

The same episode also narrows the kind of talent Mao Sui displays. His value is not routine administration but high-risk diplomatic disruption: he uses physical nerve, humiliation memory, [[DignityBasedPersuasion|dignity-based persuasion]], and immediate oath-making to convert [[ChuState|楚国]]'s hesitation into a [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] commitment.

## Key Claims
- Self-recommendation is not only self-promotion; in this source it is a response to a failed talent-discovery process.
- Crisis can expose hidden talent because ordinary household reputation may lag behind actual capability.
- The awl-in-a-bag image makes opportunity part of talent assessment: ability needs placement before it can be seen.
- Zhouji 96 part 2 shows that the self-recommended retainer's claim is tested by performance, not by confidence alone.
- Mao Sui's success depends on matching the occasion: a stalled royal negotiation rewards bold intervention that would be reckless in ordinary settings.
