## Definition

把总授权式治理 / direction-setting delegated governance is a leadership pattern in which the responsible official selects capable subordinates, gives them usable authority, holds overall priorities and accountability, and avoids unnecessary intervention in minor execution details.

## Current Synthesis

The first case comes from [[JiAnWesternHan|汲黯]]'s Donghai administration. The episode links his Huang-Lao preference for quiet government to a concrete management design: choose capable officials, authorize them to handle administration and litigation, and retain control of the overall direction without examining every small matter. Ji is frequently ill and rarely appears in the hall, yet the account credits the commandery with becoming orderly after a little more than a year.

Vol.58 extends the pattern into founder-led organizations. Delegation is incomplete when a founder transfers tasks but retains the thinking and every material decision. The episode specifies four operating conditions: define the result, move decision rights with responsibility, review at planned stages, and state the error or loss boundary in advance. It also separates founder-owned strategic and high-risk work, checkpointed new or major work, and mature routine work that can be fully transferred.

This is not leader absence as a virtue by itself. The claimed value depends on personnel judgment, usable authority, clear outcomes, information at the right moments, and continued responsibility for direction. The pattern differs from both micromanagement and abdication: execution is decentralized, while priorities, risk boundaries, and final accountability remain visible.

## Key Claims

- Delegation begins with selecting capable people rather than merely reducing the leader's workload.
- Subordinates need usable authority if responsibility is to move with execution.
- A leader can add value by setting direction and boundaries without repeatedly reopening minor decisions.
- Low-intervention administration is not the same as no administration; personnel design and accountability remain active functions.
- The model can reduce dependence on the leader's constant physical presence, but its transferability depends on subordinate quality and information flow.
- Review points and error budgets make learning possible without turning every mistake into immediate authority withdrawal.
- A satisfactory lower-resolution result can be rational when extra perfection would consume the leader's scarcer, higher-value capacity.

## Evidence

Personnel selection and authority:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-257-handai-dagangjing-jiujing-you-duogang-1-lp-pohns6ymvfpytkih9xkhwcoaw|Hanji 257 part 1]] says Ji An selects capable officials in Donghai and grants them authority over administrative and judicial work.

Direction rather than micromanagement:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-257-handai-dagangjing-jiujing-you-duogang-1-lp-pohns6ymvfpytkih9xkhwcoaw|Hanji 257 part 1]] says he focuses on the main direction rather than minor details and is nevertheless credited with restoring order despite frequent illness.

Outcome, authority, and review design:
- [[vol-58-youwenbida-cong-1-dao-0-ruhe-fangqi-que-bu-fouding-ziji-ljf7qgb2ckoql1xo5bkcqq6pgcin|vol.58]] requires agreed result standards, decision rights matched to responsibility, staged checkpoints, and predefined error or loss limits.

Risk-tiered transfer and opportunity cost:
- [[vol-58-youwenbida-cong-1-dao-0-ruhe-fangqi-que-bu-fouding-ziji-ljf7qgb2ckoql1xo5bkcqq6pgcin|vol.58]] keeps strategy and high-risk choices with the founder, uses checkpoints for major or novel work, fully transfers mature routine work, and asks whether improving a viable 70-point result to 90 is the founder's best use of time.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The historical evidence is compressed and does not identify the delegated officials, decision rights, reporting routines, measures of order, or causal contribution of Ji An's method relative to local conditions. The founder case is advisory and does not establish universal 70-point thresholds, checkpoint timing, or safe error budgets. Illness, absence, or withdrawal is not proof of good delegation, and decentralized authority can hide error, abuse, or strategic drift when selection, information, incentives, or review are weak.

## What Changed

- Extended the concept from historical light-touch administration into explicit founder delegation design.
- Added matched authority and responsibility, staged review, error budgets, and risk-tiered transfer.
- Added leader opportunity cost and tolerance for temporarily lower subordinate performance.
