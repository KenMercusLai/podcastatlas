# Deceptive Surrender Lull / 诈降麻痹

Deceptive surrender lull / 诈降麻痹 is the setup [[TianDan|田单]] uses in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-79-shishang-yong-gengniu-da-shengzhang-diyi-ren-lsrl0posblloqwugox160xsicp2p]] before the [[FireOxNightRaid|fire-ox night raid]]. After raising morale inside [[JimoWarringStates|即墨]], he makes the city look exhausted: weaker people appear on the walls, hidden fighters wait below, and envoys agree on a surrender timetable with the besieging [[YanState|燕国]] army.

The bribe completes the signal. Rich households send gold to Yan officers and ask that their families be spared after surrender, making the performance look like ordinary self-preservation rather than a military trap. That credibility lowers Yan vigilance at the exact moment Tian Dan needs surprise.

This differs from a peace-parley capture such as [[PeaceParleyEntrapment|和谈诱捕]]. The target is not a single commander at a meeting; the goal is to make an entire besieging army behave as if the campaign is already over.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-117-2-xuezhedian-qinchao-wujiang-jingran-ruci-gaoqingshang-lsyw3eitcnzfexl1l6vfh2gbtaoy]] adds a field-campaign version through [[XiangYan|项燕]]. The signal is peace rather than formal city surrender: Xiang Yan's posture makes [[LiXinQin|李信]] believe [[ChuState|楚国]] is intimidated, while a smaller Chu attack on [[NanCommandery|南郡]] gives Li Xin an urgent reason to move. The lull therefore operates before the decisive encounter, lowering Qin's suspicion while Chu positions the main force.

## Key Claims
- False surrender works by matching the enemy's expectation of exhaustion.
- Bribery can function as evidence inside a deception, not only as payment.
- The tactic requires internal morale first; otherwise the false signal may become real collapse.
- The lull is valuable because it creates the last timing window for a shock attack.
- The tactic can also work outside a siege when peace signals and a diversion make a mobile army misread the opponent's tempo.
