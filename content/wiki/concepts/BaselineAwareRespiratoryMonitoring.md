# Baseline-Aware Respiratory Monitoring / 基线化呼吸监测

## Definition
Baseline-aware respiratory monitoring interprets pulse oximetry, breathing, exertional capacity, intake, and ordinary function as changes from a person's usual state rather than treating one measurement as a complete diagnosis or universal care threshold.

## Current Synthesis
[[vol-16-jizhen-weizhongbingke-yangkang-de-jizhen-yisheng-liao-xinguan-zhi-jiezhen-qinshen-ganshou-loayers_pa8js_kaeuaqf1nn41om|VOL.16]] joins an objective signal with a functional one. Pulse oximetry can help detect deterioration that a resting person does not feel, but the reading must be checked for reliability and compared with the person's established baseline, especially when chronic lung or heart disease already changes usual values. Activity tolerance adds a real-world stress test: a new inability to walk a familiar distance, climb usual stairs, eat, or complete ordinary self-care can reveal decline not obvious at rest.

This is a safety-netting frame, not a home diagnostic algorithm. Trend, symptoms, respiratory effort, cognition, circulation, comorbidity, duration, device limitations, and access to care all influence urgency. Home monitoring can support a decision to seek care; it cannot establish the cause or safely override serious deterioration.

## Key Claims
- A pulse-oximeter value is more informative when interpreted beside device reliability, personal baseline, symptoms, and trend.
- Chronic cardiopulmonary disease can change usual oxygenation, so both absolute severity and meaningful deviation from baseline matter.
- New loss of walking, stair-climbing, eating, or self-care capacity can expose cardiopulmonary decline that is muted at rest.
- Older adults may underreport breathlessness because winter activity is low or symptoms are atypical; caregiver observation and recorded baseline can improve recognition.
- Serious oxygen decline, breathing difficulty, altered consciousness, rapid functional loss, or sustained deterioration requires qualified assessment rather than repeated home measurement alone.
- Hospital crowding and exposure risk affect routing but do not cancel the need for urgent care when warning signs are present.

## Evidence
- Oxygenation and baseline: [[vol-16-jizhen-weizhongbingke-yangkang-de-jizhen-yisheng-liao-xinguan-zhi-jiezhen-qinshen-ganshou-loayers_pa8js_kaeuaqf1nn41om]] emphasizes interpreting finger pulse oximetry against usual values, particularly in chronic lung disease, and treating substantial decline as clinically meaningful.
- Functional comparison: [[vol-16-jizhen-weizhongbingke-yangkang-de-jizhen-yisheng-liao-xinguan-zhi-jiezhen-qinshen-ganshou-loayers_pa8js_kaeuaqf1nn41om]] uses familiar walking distance, stair climbing, food intake, and daily activity to identify reduced cardiopulmonary reserve.
- Caregiver history and escalation: [[vol-16-jizhen-weizhongbingke-yangkang-de-jizhen-yisheng-liao-xinguan-zhi-jiezhen-qinshen-ganshou-loayers_pa8js_kaeuaqf1nn41om]] contrasts vague handoffs with concrete reports of appetite, fever, shaking, oxygenation, and functional change, while preserving urgent care for serious deterioration.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The page is based on one edited January 2023 podcast note, not a current clinical guideline. Consumer oximeters can be affected by fit, movement, circulation, nail products, device quality, and other factors; activity testing can itself be unsafe in a person who is severely ill, unstable, or already breathless. The episode's exact saturation values, percentage change, fever duration, and symptom timelines are source-scoped examples rather than universal instructions. Severe or rapidly worsening symptoms require local emergency guidance and qualified assessment.

## What Changed
- Established a combined oxygen-trend and functional-change framework for home respiratory monitoring.
- Made personal baseline and caregiver-observed change central without allowing baseline comparison to excuse severe absolute abnormalities.
- Preserved hospital-exposure risk as a routing consideration rather than a reason to delay necessary care.
