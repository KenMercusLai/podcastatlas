# Human Experience Content Premium / 真人体验内容溢价

## Definition
Human experience content premium is the added value of content that clearly comes from real use, personal judgment, lived friction, and accountable creators when cheap generated content makes generic explanations abundant.

## Current Synthesis
The No.229 source frames this as [[Sspai|少数派]]'s AI-era editorial defense. [[LaoMaiSspai|老麦 / Michael]] argues that AI makes content easier to produce and therefore easier to dilute; in that environment, real human use, deep experience, and traceable creator relationships become more scarce.

The concept does not reject AI tools. It says that when output becomes cheap, the differentiator moves to whether a person actually tried the product, understood the workflow, made a judgment, and can be trusted by a community. For product, app, and digital-life media, that experience layer may be the part that AI cannot fully substitute.

## Key Claims
- AI abundance can reduce the perceived value of generic content even when the content is fluent.
- Real usage experience becomes more valuable when audiences need to distinguish tested judgment from generated summary.
- Community trust can certify experience because authors have reputations and relationships inside the platform.
- The premium is strongest in practical domains where details, edge cases, and lived friction matter.
- AI can still help production, but the final value depends on human selection, testing, taste, and accountability.

- Real experience - [[no-229-duihua-shaoshupai-laomai-cong-palm-webos-dao-ai-yingjian-yiran-buxiang-chengwei-duoshupai-gkwriw4otoppacbriatd2fni]] frames 少数派's continuing value as real people outputting real experience and deep content.
- Practical media fit - [[no-229-duihua-shaoshupai-laomai-cong-palm-webos-dao-ai-yingjian-yiran-buxiang-chengwei-duoshupai-gkwriw4otoppacbriatd2fni]] traces 少数派's earlier app and hardware content through hands-on use, workflows, and digital-life problem solving.
- Community certification - [[no-229-duihua-shaoshupai-laomai-cong-palm-webos-dao-ai-yingjian-yiran-buxiang-chengwei-duoshupai-gkwriw4otoppacbriatd2fni]] connects author systems, long-active contributors, and creative users to the platform's content trust.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source states a strategic belief rather than proving audience willingness to pay for human-authored depth under AI abundance. The concept should be tested against future sources about reader behavior, disclosure, content quality, and monetization.
