# Bonus Share and Stock Split Misreading / 拆股送股误读

Bonus share and stock split misreading / 拆股送股误读 is the investor-education failure highlighted by [[vol-269-xiao-lishi-bu-yao-pa-shi-jishu-xing-tiaozheng-1004793119]]. During the [[HongkongLandDairyFarmTakeover|置地饮牛奶]] fight, [[DairyFarmInternational|牛奶公司]] proposed a one-for-one bonus issue followed by a five-for-one split, while [[HongkongLand|香港置地]] later promised a one-for-five bonus issue.

The source's point is not that splits and bonus shares create value by themselves. In an ex-rights adjustment, total share count and per-share price change together unless the underlying company value changes. The danger is that less experienced investors may read more shares, lower nominal prices, or higher headline dividends as free wealth, feeding [[RetailBullMarketPsychology]] and panic when adjusted prices look strange.

## Key Claims
- Share-count changes can make paper wealth feel larger even when economic ownership is unchanged.
- Ex-rights mechanics need explicit [[InvestorEducation]] because nominal prices can fall mechanically after splits or bonus issues.
- In a takeover contest, bonus-share promises can become persuasive weapons even when the value effect is mostly psychological and liquidity-related.
