# Sales Engineer Model

The sales engineer model is the use of technically credible salespeople as long-term customer advisors rather than only quota-driven closers. [[sweetwater-chuck-surack-how-a-customer-service-strategy-built-a-billion-dollar-online-pro-audio-and-music-company-50c2b5e3-3bc3-4039-a92d-26228b8ac908]] adds the concept through [[Sweetwater]], where customers are assigned dedicated sales engineers who are trained to understand gear, setups, goals, and support needs.

The source presents [[SweetwaterUniversity]] as the operating system behind the model. [[ChuckSurack]] describes sales training, role play, technical education, and a conversation sequence where fit and trust come before price. This makes the role adjacent to [[RelationshipLedSales]], but with more explicit product knowledge, troubleshooting ability, and ongoing account memory.

The model differs from [[CustomerSuccessEngineer]] because it sits before and during purchase as much as after deployment. In Sweetwater's version, the sales engineer lowers buyer risk in a technical consumer category and turns a retailer into a trusted advisor.

## Key Claims
- Sales engineering works when buyers need diagnosis, compatibility judgment, and confidence before committing.
- Dedicated account memory can make retail feel relational even at national online scale.
- Training and authority matter: the role loses credibility if salespeople cannot solve problems or escalate responsibly.
- The model can preserve margin when advice, fit, and support are valuable enough to offset price-only comparison.
