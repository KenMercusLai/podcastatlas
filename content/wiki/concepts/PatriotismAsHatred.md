# Patriotism As Hatred / 以仇恨运作的爱国主义

Patriotism as hatred / 以仇恨运作的爱国主义 is the political warning [[36-heian-de-zuoshou-liufang-yu-ai-de-weilai-wangshi-605254522]] draws from [[TheLeftHandOfDarkness|《黑暗的左手》]]. The episode says Tayber-style nationalism in [[Karhide|卡海德 / Karhide]] is called love of country, but practically works by stirring hatred of [[Orgoreyn|欧格瑞恩 / Orgoreyn]] and by turning [[Estraven|伊斯特拉凡 / Estraven]]'s peace-seeking border policy into treason.

The concept differs from particular love, care, or civic responsibility. Its mechanism is enemy-making: a political actor intensifies border conflict, radio propaganda, suspicion, and wounded reputation so that compromise becomes shameful and national feeling becomes easier to govern.

## Key Claims
- National language can conceal whether a movement is organized by care for a community or hatred of an enemy.
- Border conflict can make moderation look like betrayal when public honor has been attached to territory.
- Propaganda can reshape a decentralized polity by making common enemies feel more real than local obligations.
- The concept does not deny legitimate attachment to place; it asks what emotion and action the attachment actually produces.
