# Marketing Channel–Category Fit / 营销渠道与品类适配

## Definition
Marketing channel–category fit is the alignment among a product's economics, purchase risk, demonstration needs, target audience, use scene, and the discovery or transaction behavior a channel actually supports.

## Current Synthesis
Reach is not fit. A channel can generate entertainment, likes, visual inspiration, local intent, price comparison, detailed explanation, or reliable checkout, but these jobs do not automatically substitute for one another. Strong channel selection starts with the customer's decision problem and the category's operating constraints, then chooses the smallest useful set of surfaces for awareness, evaluation, purchase, and repeat business.

The source also makes fit dynamic. Large brands may enter a new channel to occupy attention before its transaction model is proven, and platform boundaries can change as users, subsidies, tools, and product categories expand. Experimentation remains rational, but fashionable tactics should not override unit economics, brand position, or evidence of actual demand.

## Key Claims
- Audience size, likes, or novelty do not by themselves establish conversion value.
- Different channels specialize in price, fulfillment, visual seeding, entertainment, local discovery, explanation, or transaction.
- High-ticket, local-service, beauty, food, and commodity products require different evidence and purchase paths.
- A brand should test emerging channels without treating every trend as a universal primary channel.
- Durable fit joins acquisition to product quality, margin, service capacity, and repeat purchase.

## Evidence
- Platform-role separation - [[ep145-yingxiao-taolu-shen-si-hai-women-shi-ruhe-yici-ci-diaoru-shuangshiyi-xiaofei-zhuyi-xianjing-de-cmcoor4dfoj5br3mlabkw9dw]] distinguishes Pinduoduo price, JD reliability, Douyin attention, Xiaohongshu beauty/fashion seeding, and Dianping local discovery.
- Reach-conversion gap - [[ep145-yingxiao-taolu-shen-si-hai-women-shi-ruhe-yici-ci-diaoru-shuangshiyi-xiaofei-zhuyi-xianjing-de-cmcoor4dfoj5br3mlabkw9dw]] says high likes and staged restaurant content may fail to produce sales or merchant approval.
- Category and brand constraints - [[ep145-yingxiao-taolu-shen-si-hai-women-shi-ruhe-yici-ci-diaoru-shuangshiyi-xiaofei-zhuyi-xianjing-de-cmcoor4dfoj5br3mlabkw9dw]] uses cars, property, restaurants, consumer goods, and culturally themed products to show that channel and content must match purchase logic.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The channel judgments are 2020 practitioner observations, not timeless platform classifications. Platforms can add commerce, local-service, search, logistics, and creator tools, while brand execution can change results inside the same channel. Fit must therefore be retested rather than inherited as a fixed platform stereotype.

## What Changed
- Created a channel-selection framework separating reach from category-specific discovery, evaluation, transaction, and repeat value.
