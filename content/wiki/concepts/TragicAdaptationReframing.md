## Definition

Tragic adaptation reframing converts a source's reunion, continuation, or domestic resolution into irreversible loss to reorganize emotional intensity and moral expectations.

## Current Synthesis

[[AChineseGhostStory1987|《倩女幽魂》(1987)]] supplies the bounded case. The 《聂小倩》 tale lets Xiaoqian join Ning Caichen's household, acquire human status, marry, and have children within a structure already including his wife and later concubinage. The film removes that future and gives the lovers mutual devotion followed by reincarnation and a missed farewell.

The change narrows the relationship into modern exclusive love, makes sacrifice and release the proof of care, and turns “人鬼殊途” into the final law. Tragedy gains force because the lovers succeed morally—Xiaoqian is freed—while failing romantically.

## Key Claims

- A changed ending can move a story's moral center even when much plot remains recognizable.
- Irreversible separation can intensify love by making rescue incompatible with possession.
- Modern romantic exclusivity can motivate removal of marriage, wife, concubinage, and household continuation.
- A tragic ending should be evaluated by its causal and ethical structure, not sadness alone.

## Evidence

### Domestic continuation versus separation

- [[054-qian-nv-youhun-chongying-yiqu-renguilian-daojin-jianghuqing-ltgcllmyiaxrx_kkvgsrrmcxxj2t]] contrasts the tale's marriage and children with reincarnation and the missed dawn farewell.

### Rescue without possession

- [[054-qian-nv-youhun-chongying-yiqu-renguilian-daojin-jianghuqing-ltgcllmyiaxrx_kkvgsrrmcxxj2t]] treats helping Xiaoqian move on, rather than retaining her as a ghost, as the final act of love.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept rests on one adaptation discussion. “Modern” love is not uniform, and the earlier domestic ending needs more historical context than the podcast supplies. Tragic intensity does not repair unrelated causal weaknesses.

## What Changed

- Established the concept from the replacement of household reunion with successful rescue and failed romantic union.
