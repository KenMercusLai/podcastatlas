# Correct Question Epistemology / 正确提问的认识论

Correct question epistemology / 正确提问的认识论 is the episode's nameable reasoning lesson from [[TheLeftHandOfDarkness|《黑暗的左手》]]. In [[36-heian-de-zuoshou-liufang-yu-ai-de-weilai-wangshi-605254522]], [[GenlyAi|金利·艾 / Genly Ai]]'s encounter with the Handdara foretellers makes the hosts stress that asking a true-answer machine the wrong question does not produce understanding.

The source gives two linked cases. Genly eventually asks whether [[Gethen|格森星 / Gethen]] will join the [[Ekumen|艾库曼 / Ekumen]] within five years, and the answer matters because the question has been disciplined. By contrast, the king's question about why people become traitors already traps [[Estraven|伊斯特拉凡 / Estraven]] inside a false frame; the answer cannot repair the bad premise.

## Key Claims
- Good questions require framing discipline before answer-seeking begins.
- A true answer can be useless when the question assumes the wrong moral or political category.
- The concept pairs reasoning humility with practical action: uncertainty does not excuse paralysis, but it should change what one asks.
- Fiction can dramatize epistemology by showing how characters live with the consequences of bad questions.
