# 武装部众整合成本 / Armed Follower Integration Cost

## Definition
Armed follower integration cost is the governance and logistics burden created when a ruler or adviser absorbs an armed group whose members need food, discipline, pay, and command structure.

## Current Synthesis
Hanji 1037 uses [[LiuYeLateHan|刘晔]] to separate military headcount from usable capacity. After killing [[ZhengBaoLateHan|郑宝]], Liu Ye can take thousands of followers, but he refuses because they lack discipline and he lacks the economic base to feed and control them. [[LiuXunLateHan|刘勋]] then accepts the group, and the added mouths combine with [[YuanYinLateHan|袁胤]]'s arrival to create grain pressure. The source therefore treats "more troops" as an asset only when supply and discipline can keep up.

## Key Claims
- Armed followers are not free strength; they bring food, pay, discipline, and command costs.
- A mature armed group can be more dangerous than useful if its habits include looting or local predation.
- Refusing command can be rational when the actor lacks the economic base to integrate followers.
- Passing followers to another actor shifts the burden rather than eliminating it.
- Food pressure from absorbed groups can push later strategic mistakes.

## Evidence
- Refusal logic: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1037-zhongyu-caocao-or-liubiao-jiemi-hansong-de-zhongyi-jueze-lomrbxffcu6euantozh2fjvrykva|Hanji 1037]] has Liu Ye explain that Zheng Bao's followers lack discipline, rob people, and cannot be supplied by him.
- Burden transfer: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1037-zhongyu-caocao-or-liubiao-jiemi-hansong-de-zhongyi-jueze-lomrbxffcu6euantozh2fjvrykva|Hanji 1037]] says Liu Ye transfers the group to Liu Xun, who later faces food pressure.
- Compounded consumption: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1037-zhongyu-caocao-or-liubiao-jiemi-hansong-de-zhongyi-jueze-lomrbxffcu6euantozh2fjvrykva|Hanji 1037]] links Yuan Yin's dependents and remnants to Liu Xun's grain depletion.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept does not deny that absorbing defeated or leaderless groups can build power. It marks the condition: the receiving actor needs grain, command discipline, and political capacity. Cao Cao's Qingzhou conversion branch in the wiki remains a positive comparison, while Liu Xun's case is a warning about unsupported absorption.

## What Changed
- Created the concept from Hanji 1037's Liu Ye, Zheng Bao, and Liu Xun branch.
