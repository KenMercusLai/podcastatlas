# 联盟不确定性下的退路预留 / Alliance Contingency Hedging

## Definition
联盟不确定性下的退路预留 / alliance contingency hedging is the practice of pursuing cooperation while preserving an explicit fallback if the prospective partner fails, defects, or loses the capacity to help.

## Current Synthesis
Hanji 1096 defines the pattern through [[SunQuan|孙权]] and [[LuSu|鲁肃]] before the Sun-Liu coalition hardens. Lu Su's mourning mission is authorized to investigate Jingzhou, contact [[LiuBei|刘备]] and Liu Biao's divided heirs, and seek joint resistance to [[CaoCao|曹操]]. Yet the episode also attributes to Sun Quan a fallback order: if Liu Bei collapses, Lu Su should submit a memorial at Xu. Cooperation and exit planning therefore coexist from the start.

The concept does not mean that the alliance is unreal. It means that early cooperation is conditional on partner survival and on Jiangdong's ability to resist. A contingency can reduce the cost of exploration, but it can also cap trust if every partner expects the other to leave when conditions worsen.

## Key Claims
- Exploratory diplomacy can carry both an alliance objective and a fallback instruction.
- A coalition can be strategically useful without being treated as irreversible.
- Partner survival is itself a condition of alliance value.
- Hedging reduces catastrophic downside while potentially limiting mutual trust and commitment.
- The strongest evidence is an explicit conditional instruction, not a retrospective claim that all alliances are cynical.

## Evidence
- Alliance exploration: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1096-chibi-zhizhan-qian-gefang-shili-ruhe-anzhong-boyi-licxjg9iqlwbxa23luxbopaqeqyn|Hanji 1096]] has Lu Su seek information and anti-Cao cooperation among Liu Bei and Liu Biao's divided succession field.
- Conditional fallback: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1096-chibi-zhizhan-qian-gefang-shili-ruhe-anzhong-boyi-licxjg9iqlwbxa23luxbopaqeqyn|Hanji 1096]] attributes to Sun Quan an instruction to submit a memorial at Xu if Liu Bei disintegrates.
- Strategic objective: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1096-chibi-zhizhan-qian-gefang-shili-ruhe-anzhong-boyi-licxjg9iqlwbxa23luxbopaqeqyn|Hanji 1096]] says Jiangdong still seeks leverage over Jingzhou, so resistance, territorial ambition, and self-preservation operate together.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The exact wording and force of the fallback instruction require verification against the underlying historical text. “献表” does not automatically establish unconditional surrender, and one contingency order cannot prove the host's broader maxim that alliances contain no loyalty. Later episodes show real mobilization and costly coordination, so hedging should be understood as an early-stage risk structure rather than proof that the alliance was empty.

## What Changed
- Created from Hanji 1096 to distinguish conditional alliance exploration from both full commitment and simple betrayal.
