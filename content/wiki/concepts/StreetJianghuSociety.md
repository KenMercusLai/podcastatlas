# Street Jianghu Society

## Definition
Street Jianghu society is Jianghu as an urban, mobile, low-status livelihood field where work, performance, secrecy, fraud, reputation, mutual aid, and violence overlap.

## Current Synthesis
The concept is grounded in [[LianKuoru|连阔如]]'s [[JiangHuCongTan|《江湖丛谈》]], where Jianghu appears as itinerant trade, street performance, fortune-telling, medicine selling, scams, lodging houses, beggar groups, guards, thieves, and quyi labor [[102-jianghu-congtan-pianshu-heihua-he-minjian-daoyi-736516488]]. This keeps romantic Jianghu imagery under pressure: mobility can mean freedom, but it can also mean unstable income, predation, and dependence on insiders.

An explanatory pressure point is hunger: people outside ordinary economic life, kinship support, or stable social order may enter Jianghu because they need to eat [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-483-2-lishi-jiemi-hun-zhichang-de-jianghu-guiju-ltukp3q2cg7p5egxqte7nuseenaw]]. Hunger and insecurity help explain why the same field can produce practical craft, adaptive speech, fraud, intimidation, and social damage.

## Key Claims
- Jianghu can be a social infrastructure for informal work, not only a genre setting.
- Street livelihood often needs language barriers, local brokers, lodging rules, reputation, and performance technique.
- The same system can support craft, mutual aid, and exploitation at once.
- The source's Jianghu is urban, itinerant, commercial, and marginal rather than courtly or heroic.
- Survival pressure helps explain both the creativity and destructiveness of marginal Jianghu life.
- Reading Jianghu this way explains why fiction could romanticize it: real practices already contained secrecy, codes, risk, reputation, and dramatic role-play.

## Evidence
### Informal Livelihood Field
- [[102-jianghu-congtan-pianshu-heihua-he-minjian-daoyi-736516488]] describes street Jianghu through trade, performance, medicine selling, scams, guards, thieves, beggar groups, lodging, and quyi labor.

### Craft And Predation Together
- [[102-jianghu-congtan-pianshu-heihua-he-minjian-daoyi-736516488]] refuses to reduce Jianghu to fraud because performers and workers may have real technique, but it also details staged deception, bodily fear, and insider enforcement.

### Survival Pressure
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-483-2-lishi-jiemi-hun-zhichang-de-jianghu-guiju-ltukp3q2cg7p5egxqte7nuseenaw]] says marginal people without stable work or social incorporation may be driven by hunger into craft, deception, intimidation, or violence.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This concept is not a general history of all lower-class life. Its strongest evidence comes from an episode on [[JiangHuCongTan|《江湖丛谈》]] and a short Hanji commentary, so it should preserve the distinction between documented street techniques, interpretive survival claims, and later literary Jianghu romance.
