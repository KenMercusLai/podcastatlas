# Labor Market Counterforces

Labor market counterforces are institutions that limit employer power over wages and working conditions. In [[the-real-horror-of-alien-and-how-it-explains-why-were-not-paid-enough]], [[ArinDube|Arin Dube]] names minimum-wage law, antitrust enforcement, and labor unions as ways to push back against [[LaborMonopsony]].

The episode makes the idea vivid by rewriting [[AlienFilm|Alien]] with a strong sectoral union, grievance procedure, and collective bargaining agreement. The joke works because it shows that the horror plot depends on the absence of countervailing worker power: if workers can challenge a dangerous order before losing pay, the company's hidden mission cannot proceed so easily.
