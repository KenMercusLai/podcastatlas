# Sacrament Donation Model

The sacrament donation model is [[ZydorChurch]]'s way of framing member payments in [[a-trip-to-the-magic-mushroom-megachurch]]. Members make cash donations and receive substances such as [[PsilocybinMushrooms]] or [[DMT]] from providers behind a counter, while [[DaveHodges]] emphasizes that the church does not call the exchange a sale.

The model is central to the episode's economic tension. Zydor charges initiation and monthly membership fees, has ATMs on site, and operates in cash because federally illegal substances keep it outside normal banking. Those facts support religious community operations, but they also invite skepticism that the church is functioning as a business-like workaround for drug laws.

## Key Claims
- Donation language can help distinguish sacrament from retail, but the practical exchange may still look commercial.
- Cash dependence follows from federal illegality but increases robbery, seizure, accounting, and legitimacy risk.
- Membership fees and high visit volume make revenue questions unavoidable even when the source cannot verify Dave's personal finances.
- A donation model does not settle whether a psychedelic church is sincere, exploitative, harm-reducing, or all of those in tension.
