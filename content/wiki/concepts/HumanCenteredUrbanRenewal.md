# Human-Centered Urban Renewal / 以人为本的城市更新

## Definition

Human-centered urban renewal judges intervention by whether it improves safety, infrastructure, public services, access, and daily life while preserving valuable buildings, relationships, affordability, and local identity where possible.

## Current Synthesis

The episode rejects both automatic clearance and preservation without modernization. Hutongs, lilong, parks, streets, and older residential areas may need water, sanitation, safety, cultural, medical, educational, or recreational upgrades. What is preserved or rebuilt should follow heritage value and resident needs rather than a visual preference for either uniform newness or untouched age.

This approach also distinguishes efficient standardization from mechanical copying. Reusable technical solutions can improve performance, but façade uniformity, imported tourist formats, or copied plans become harmful when they erase local geography, culture, livelihoods, and actual use.

## Key Claims

- Renewal should begin with residents' fundamental needs rather than surface tidiness.
- Heritage protection and modern infrastructure can be combined.
- Demolition, adaptive reuse, and in-place upgrading require case-specific evidence.
- Public-service gaps can justify functional change even when land use remains broadly residential.
- Standard methods are useful only when adapted to local conditions and lived use.

## Evidence

- Infrastructure plus heritage - [[ep128-shiwunianyigao-de-chengshi-guihua-jiujing-zai-guihua-shenme-cmcocqgc36nfb4cpjgba98wr|EP128]] describes updating water, sanitation, and interiors in hutong and lilong districts while retaining valuable form.
- Service completion - the source frames renewal as adding schools, hospitals, exercise, culture, reading, community services, and green space where existing districts lack them. [[ep128-shiwunianyigao-de-chengshi-guihua-jiujing-zai-guihua-shenme-cmcocqgc36nfb4cpjgba98wr]]
- Copying boundary - the episode distinguishes efficient spatial organization from mechanically importing another city's image or repeating generic residential plans. [[ep128-shiwunianyigao-de-chengshi-guihua-jiujing-zai-guihua-shenme-cmcocqgc36nfb4cpjgba98wr]]

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

“Human-centered” does not identify which residents prevail when renters, owners, visitors, merchants, and governments have conflicting interests. The source offers principles and examples but no participation protocol, compensation rule, budget model, or outcome evaluation.

## What Changed

- Created a renewal standard combining infrastructure, public services, heritage judgment, affordability, and local use.
