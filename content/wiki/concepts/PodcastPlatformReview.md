# Podcast Platform Review

Podcast platform review is the distribution constraint created when the same audio episode can pass review on one platform and fail review on another. In [[yi-tiao-jiemu-shuoming-xiwang-gewei-tingzhong-nenggou-tingwan-578621335]], [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] turns that constraint into a listener-facing notice: the host says the week's prepared episode was blocked on some platforms, directs listeners elsewhere, apologizes for confusion, and says future missed Wednesday updates may signal another review failure.

The concept sits between [[PodcastReleaseCadence]] and [[PodcastAsAsynchronousMedia]]. Podcast listening is asynchronous and multi-platform, but a review decision can still interrupt the expected feed on a specific platform. A show can preserve some continuity by explaining the blockage, routing listeners to approved surfaces, and keeping feedback channels open.

## Key Claims
- Platform review can make a podcast episode available in one listening environment but absent in another.
- Review failure turns distribution into a listener-communication problem, not only an internal production problem.
- A missing scheduled update can have multiple causes; in this case, the host teaches listeners to treat review failure as a likely explanation.
- Alternate-platform routing preserves access but fragments comments, ratings, and listener feedback.
- Hosts may respond by becoming more cautious about topic selection or phrasing, even when they do not identify the exact reviewed content.
