# Angelic Intelligence

## Definition
Angelic intelligence is [[ShekharNatarajan]]'s source-scoped term for technology that acts like a guiding force, amplifies human beauty, supports compassionate action, and nudges people toward better choices rather than merely optimizing productivity.

## Current Synthesis
Across the bounded sources, angelic intelligence is presented as a human-centered answer to AI alignment, efficiency hype, and meaning scarcity. In [[how-modern-brands-build-loyalty-purpose-and-identity-andy-stalman-shekhar-natarajan-podcast]], Shekhar frames it as guidance, purpose, and alignment, while [[AndyStalman]] treats the name as a way to understand an unseen helper through turbulent times. In [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-97450677c47f502770248abb02f35793-163628914-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-97450677c47f502770248abb02f35793-163628914]], Shekhar links it to moral coherence, and [[ShyalpaRinpoche]] recasts the frame through "absolute intelligence," compassion, and AI serving human development.

## Key Claims
- AI alignment is framed as a major problem that cannot be solved by efficiency alone.
- Technology should amplify human beauty and guide people toward better direction.
- The concept deliberately puts the human layer before the technology layer.
- Productivity, automation, and efficiency are current hype cycles, but the source argues that love, beauty, and human meaning remain the more durable center.
- The term is aspirational and source-scoped; it is a framing concept rather than an implemented technical system in the source.
- The newer source adds a compassion and meaning layer: AI should help deliver meaningful, compassionate explanations and serve humans rather than making humans dependent on it.
- Moral coherence becomes part of the alignment problem when Shekhar asks how AI can distinguish context and choose a compassionate action over time.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The sources do not define a technical architecture, product requirements, benchmark, or governance mechanism for angelic intelligence. The newer Buddhist framing adds normative depth but still leaves implementation, evaluation, and governance unresolved.

## What Changed
- Extended the concept beyond brand and human-beauty language into meaning scarcity, compassion, absolute intelligence, and moral coherence.
