# Angelic Intelligence

## Definition
Angelic intelligence is [[ShekharNatarajan]]'s source-scoped term for technology that acts like a guiding force, amplifies human beauty, and nudges people toward better choices rather than merely optimizing productivity.

## Current Synthesis
In [[how-modern-brands-build-loyalty-purpose-and-identity-andy-stalman-shekhar-natarajan-podcast]], angelic intelligence is presented as a human-centered answer to AI alignment and efficiency hype. Shekhar frames the concept as guidance, purpose, and alignment; [[AndyStalman]] responds that the name gives people a way to understand an unseen helper through turbulent times, while warning that the hard work is reconnecting people with essence, love, beauty, and human beings.

## Key Claims
- AI alignment is framed as a major problem that cannot be solved by efficiency alone.
- Technology should amplify human beauty and guide people toward better direction.
- The concept deliberately puts the human layer before the technology layer.
- Productivity, automation, and efficiency are current hype cycles, but the source argues that love, beauty, and human meaning remain the more durable center.
- The term is aspirational and source-scoped; it is a framing concept rather than an implemented technical system in the source.

## Evidence
- Concept statement: [[how-modern-brands-build-loyalty-purpose-and-identity-andy-stalman-shekhar-natarajan-podcast]] has Shekhar explain angelic intelligence as technology acting like a guiding force that amplifies human beauty.
- Alignment link: [[how-modern-brands-build-loyalty-purpose-and-identity-andy-stalman-shekhar-natarajan-podcast]] connects the concept to AI alignment and purpose.
- Human-layer response: [[how-modern-brands-build-loyalty-purpose-and-identity-andy-stalman-shekhar-natarajan-podcast]] has Stalman say the approach brings a human layer through technology.
- Meaning boundary: [[how-modern-brands-build-loyalty-purpose-and-identity-andy-stalman-shekhar-natarajan-podcast]] contrasts love, beauty, and human beings with AI productivity and automation hype.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not define a technical architecture, product requirements, benchmark, or governance mechanism for angelic intelligence. It should be treated as a normative and design-language concept until other sources give it implementation detail.
