# Imperial Kiln System / 御窑体系

Imperial Kiln System / 御窑体系 is the court-production framework in [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]]. The episode grounds it in the [[JingdezhenImperialKilnFactory]], where palace standards shaped workshops, materials, vessel forms, pigments, firing, and rejection rules.

The system was official but not isolated. It drew on [[DistributedCeramicProduction]], local labor, outsourced work, and the broader [[JingdezhenPorcelainProductionSystem]], while managers such as [[TangYing]] learned from artisans and turned practice into records and experiments.
