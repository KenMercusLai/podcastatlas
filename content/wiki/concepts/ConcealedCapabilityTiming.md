# Concealed Capability Timing / 藏器待时

## Definition
Concealed capability timing / 藏器待时 is the late-Han survival pattern where an actor with real ability preserves skill, reputation, and freedom of movement until the political timing and patron field make exposure useful.

## Current Synthesis
The concept begins in Hanji 959 as a general survival rule: a capable person should build capacity, reputation, and judgment without displaying sharpness before the surrounding power field can absorb it. [[GongsunDuLateHan|公孙度]] is the first case because his family relocation, chance patronage, recommendation, and later appointment become useful only when preserved ability meets an opening.

Hanji 1022 adds a group-refuge version through [[DuXiLateHan|杜袭]], [[ZhaoYanLateHan|赵俨]], and [[FanQinLateHan|繁钦]]. Their stay with [[LiuBiao|刘表]] in [[Jingzhou|荆州]] is not final service but a holding pattern: Du Xi warns against over-displaying talent before a host they do not judge capable of restoration, while Zhao Yan later moves to [[CaoCao|曹操]] only after [[Xudu|许都]] makes Cao's court-backed position legible.

## Key Claims
- Ability in disorder should be cultivated before it is displayed.
- Concealment is strategic only when paired with timing judgment, route choice, and patron assessment.
- Hospitality is not the same as allegiance; a refuge can preserve agency before final commitment.
- Low-profile conduct is not the same as strategic preparation; it is the safer fallback for people without enough capacity to compete.
- The concept sits inside [[PoliticalSurvivalFirst|安全第一政治生存]] but names a narrower problem: when capacity should become visible.

## Evidence
- Capability before display: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-959-ruguo-ni-chuanyue-dao-sanguo-gai-ruhe-cunhuo-lroaihmpnrelmfcugym9zx2h2dhq|Hanji 959]] uses "藏器于身，待时而动" to distinguish preserved ability from premature exposure.
- Survival fallback: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-959-ruguo-ni-chuanyue-dao-sanguo-gai-ruhe-cunhuo-lroaihmpnrelmfcugym9zx2h2dhq|Hanji 959]] contrasts capable preparation with the ordinary person's safer low-exposure path.
- Refuge and restraint: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1022-luanshi-sanguo-sanwei-mingshi-de-shengcun-jueze-lgmbekomxwiu7hjnpxkxggyhvwaf|Hanji 1022]] has Du Xi warn Fan Qin not to treat Liu Biao's hospitality as permission for full talent display.
- Timing of allegiance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1022-luanshi-sanguo-sanwei-mingshi-de-shengcun-jueze-lgmbekomxwiu7hjnpxkxggyhvwaf|Hanji 1022]] makes Zhao Yan's later move north depend on Cao Cao's post-Xudu position.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Concealed capability is not passive hiding. It becomes strategic only when it preserves future action; if concealment cuts off route choice, patron assessment, and accumulated capacity, it collapses into ordinary withdrawal.
