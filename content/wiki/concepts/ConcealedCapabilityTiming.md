# Concealed Capability Timing / 藏器待时

Concealed capability timing / 藏器待时 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-959-ruguo-ni-chuanyue-dao-sanguo-gai-ruhe-cunhuo-lroaihmpnrelmfcugym9zx2h2dhq|Hanji 959]] as the episode's answer to "how would you survive if you crossed into the Three Kingdoms?" The source borrows the phrase "君子藏器于身，待时而动" to describe the path for people with real ability.

The concept is not passive hiding. In the source's reading, a capable actor must accumulate skill, reputation, and judgment without exposing himself too early. [[GongsunDuLateHan|公孙度]] is the source hook: family relocation, chance patronage, official recommendation, and later appointment become usable only because opportunity eventually meets preserved capacity.

The episode pairs this with a humbler counter-rule. If one does not have unusual ability, survival comes from lowering exposure: stay modest, avoid fame and profit traps, do not show sharp edges, and if possible choose a safer place such as Liaodong under a stable local order.

## Key Claims

- Ability in disorder should be cultivated before it is displayed.
- Timing matters because early exposure can attract danger before an actor has leverage.
- Low-profile conduct is not the same as strategic preparation; it is the safer fallback for people without enough capacity to compete.
- The concept sits inside [[PoliticalSurvivalFirst|安全第一政治生存]] but names a narrower problem: when to reveal capacity.
