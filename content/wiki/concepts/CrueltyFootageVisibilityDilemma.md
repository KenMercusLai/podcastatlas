# Cruelty Footage Visibility Dilemma / 残忍影像可见性两难

## Definition
[[CrueltyFootageVisibilityDilemma]] is the advocacy problem where graphic cruelty footage can mobilize evidence, outrage, and public memory while also traumatizing viewers, harming minors, and possibly encouraging imitation.

## Current Synthesis
The 旺旺 episode treats the controversy over removing abuse footage as a real dilemma rather than a simple pro- or anti-censorship question. [[HongKongSPCA|香港爱护动物协会]] asked platforms to remove videos because of youth distress and imitation risk, but critics argued that removal looked like cooperation with mainland suppression when the case was already being made harder to discuss. The concept is strongest when evidence visibility and harm reduction conflict under unequal information conditions.

## Key Claims
- Graphic footage can be the trigger that makes hidden cruelty publicly undeniable.
- The same footage can produce secondary trauma, especially for children or viewers already emotionally affected.
- Removing footage can reduce imitation risk but may also weaken evidence, accountability, and memory.
- The dilemma becomes sharper when the original public sphere is already filtered or controlled.
- Institutions need to explain takedown requests carefully because harm-reduction language can be read as silence-making.

## Evidence
- Institutional takedown dispute: [[liulangquan-wangwang-zhisi-he-yichang-kuajing-dongbao-shengyuan]] reports [[HongKongSPCA|香港爱护动物协会]]'s request to remove videos and the resulting online criticism.
- Harm and visibility tradeoff: [[liulangquan-wangwang-zhisi-he-yichang-kuajing-dongbao-shengyuan]] frames the issue as balancing trauma and imitation risk against public evidence in a suppressed case.
- Mobilization role: [[liulangquan-wangwang-zhisi-he-yichang-kuajing-dongbao-shengyuan]] reports that many people learned about and reacted to the case after seeing circulated footage.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not establish whether keeping or removing the footage was the better decision in this case. It records competing risks and the public meaning attached to each choice.
