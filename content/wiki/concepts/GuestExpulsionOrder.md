# Guest Expulsion Order / 逐客令

Guest Expulsion Order / 逐客令 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-110-1-lisi-micang-laoshu-he-xiashuidao-laoshu-ni-zenme-xuan-lmv-fw-kgsuokonssq8bkbfvdsb]], where Qin royal-clan ministers persuade [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]] to expel foreign guest officials from [[QinState|秦国]]. The order follows [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]]'s removal and appears in a court atmosphere shaped by foreign-born power brokers, retainers, [[LaoAi|嫪毐]], and the exposed [[ZhengGuoHydraulicEngineer|郑国]] canal mission.

The concept is not just xenophobia in the abstract. The episode reads it as a merger of security panic and office politics: outsiders can be described as agents of their original rulers, while native Qin nobles can also use that suspicion to reclaim positions occupied by imported talent. [[LiSi|李斯]] matters because he is one of the expelled outsiders and will answer the order through [[JianZhuKeShu|《谏逐客书》]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-110-2-qinshihuang-de-cp-weihe-bei-quzhu-chuguo-lj0222vw8ijbvj25iwfzh-qhvzr9]] adds the reversal. Li Si argues that Qin's strongest rulers historically benefited from outside talent and that expulsion would give those people and their capacities to enemy states. The order is therefore defeated not by denying the Zheng Guo risk, but by showing that a sweeping category purge would damage Qin's larger unification strategy.

## Key Claims
- The order turns Qin's dependence on foreign talent into a vulnerability once foreign-origin scandals and espionage fears become politically salient.
- It uses loyalty suspicion as a category: guest officials may be accused of serving their home rulers even while holding Qin office.
- The order also reflects internal distributional conflict because Qin royal-clan ministers resent outside officials occupying powerful posts.
- [[ZhengGuoHydraulicEngineer|郑国]] provides the concrete "foreign agent" example that makes a sweeping order sound plausible.
- [[LiSi|李斯]] turns the policy from a mass expulsion into a personal and textual crisis through [[JianZhuKeShu|《谏逐客书》]].
- Qinji 110-2 adds that a security panic can be defeated by reframing talent as state capacity: the key question becomes not whether some foreigners are dangerous, but whether categorical expulsion helps Qin or its enemies.
