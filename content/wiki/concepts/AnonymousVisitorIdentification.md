# Anonymous Visitor Identification

Anonymous visitor identification is the B2B SaaS workflow of inferring which companies are visiting a website before a visitor fills out a form. In [[stuck-at-50k-arr-for-5-years-now-1-5m-with-ai-agents]], [[GeorgeGeorgiadis]] says [[Happierleads]] began with reverse IP lookup and a purchased data source, then added UI, filters, qualification, and campaign workflows around that data.

The value is not only identifying a company name. Happierleads uses AI to qualify whether the company fits the target customer, then can add promising leads to email campaigns. That connects the category to [[OutboundEmailGrowthEngine]], [[AIMarketingDecisioning]], and [[ProductLedWillingnessToPay]] because the buyer pays for a more actionable sales workflow, not raw analytics alone.

## Key Claims
- Website traffic can contain B2B demand signals before form-fill conversion makes a lead explicit.
- Reverse IP lookup or external company data can be a starting layer, but the durable product needs filtering, qualification, workflow integration, and trust.
- AI qualification is useful when it helps decide which visitors are worth follow-up rather than merely adding more names to a list.
- The category becomes more valuable when connected to sales action, such as re-engagement campaigns or partner outreach.
