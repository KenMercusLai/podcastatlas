# Surrogacy Policy Hypocrisy

Surrogacy policy hypocrisy is the scandal mechanism in [[brave-new-whirl-turkeys-opposition-overhaul-6a69c7766043035f89eb097f]]: [[JensSpahn]] defends [[Germany]]'s surrogacy ban and signs a resolution supporting a full ban, then reveals that he and his husband used a surrogate abroad.

The source treats the political damage as a contradiction between public rule-making and private exception-seeking. The issue is not only surrogacy itself, since the episode says German public opinion mostly supports the ban and similar bans exist in France, Spain, and Italy. The scandal comes from a politician benefiting from a path he helped deny domestically.

## Key Claims
- A policy ban becomes more explosive when a defender of the ban personally circumvents it abroad.
- Public anger can focus on rule asymmetry rather than the private family arrangement alone.
- Cross-border access can expose class and power gaps in morally charged domestic policy.
- A scandal can become a coalition-management problem when the politician is already unpopular or tied to earlier controversies.
