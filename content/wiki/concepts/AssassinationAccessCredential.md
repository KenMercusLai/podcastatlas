## Definition

Assassination access credential / 刺杀接近凭证 is the operational pattern in which gifts, trophies, maps, surrender posture, appointments, escort protocol, court meetings, or confidential-message language create the physical proximity required for a close-range political killing.

## Current Synthesis

The concept begins from the [[JingKe|Jing Ke]] mission. [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-114-3-jingke-ciqin-weihe-jiujiu-bu-xingdong-zai-deng-shui-liihe-kgwigu3bggxmyofgvotged|Qinji 114-3]] shows that courage and weapon preparation are not enough: Jing Ke first needs [[FanWuji|Fan Wuji]]'s head and the [[DuKangMap|Du Kang map]] to make an envoy audience plausible. [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-115-1-jingke-ciqin-sihu-you-dianer-caoshuai-le-lgz1adsn1t8jjiaobmtfedpr-ixn|Qinji 115-1]] activates those credentials through Meng Jia's court brokerage and Yan's surrender script, while [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-115-2-qinwang-danan-busi-zhihou-ganle-jian-ni-buzhidao-de-shi-lidfjb6-nggubw8vpz5fvnfacrqu|Qinji 115-2]] shows the boundary: the map wins proximity but cannot guarantee a successful strike once team execution breaks down.

The Western Han frontier-diplomacy variant appears through [[FuJieziWesternHan|Fu Jiezi]] in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-403-2-dujian-zhan-loulan-bugai-bei-yiwang-de-chaoji-waijiaoguan-fujiezi-lssxm3z-fkdblz8nfoy1pojnetja|Hanji 403-2]]. Fu's valuables draw [[LoulanKingAnguiWesternHan|Angui]] back into reception, and the claim of a confidential imperial message separates him from guards and nobles. Here the credential is not a surrendered map or fugitive head; it is the lure of imperial gifts plus secret-audience etiquette.

Late-Han cases broaden the pattern beyond interstate envoy audiences. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-963-shishang-bei-diantian-deng-de-diyiren-shi-ta-lkpkhlpkv3xpw-hur4b7sh-3druk|Hanji 963]] uses [[EmperorXianOfHan|Emperor Xian]]'s court meeting, guard placement, and an edict to give [[WangYunLateHan|Wang Yun]] and [[LyuBu|Lü Bu]] access to [[DongZhuo|Dong Zhuo]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-970-yuanshao-ansha-lvbu-weihe-hui-shibai-ln2pnvit-qgpnhn-pne7o-p1irrs|Hanji 970]] shows [[YuanShao|Yuan Shao]] turning appointment and escort courtesy into an attempted killing route against Lü Bu. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1019-jiemi-chenminwang-liuchong-de-chuanqi-yisheng-ll2fma7wupp5gczudysfett5lhio|Hanji 1019]] adds false surrender as the credential [[ZhangKaiAssassin|Zhang Kai]] uses to approach [[LiuChongChenKing|Liu Chong]] and [[LuoJun|Luo Jun]].

Across the sources, access credentials work by making suspicion costly or unnecessary for the target. They exploit established channels of diplomacy, submission, reward, court command, or elite courtesy. Their moral cost varies: Fan Wuji's head requires sacrificing a protected guest, Fu's valuables spend diplomatic credibility, and false surrender weaponizes a channel that normally makes defection and pacification possible.

## Key Claims

- Close-range assassination is partly an access-design problem, not only a bravery or weapon problem.
- Diplomatic gifts, surrender objects, confidential messages, court rituals, appointments, escorts, and false surrender can all become proximity tools.
- A credential must be paired with a plausible story and often with a broker or authority channel.
- Access success and assassination success are separate thresholds; proximity can still fail through team, target, or setting breakdown.
- Strong credentials often carry moral or political cost because they exploit trust-bearing institutions.
- Host-side courtesy and openness to surrender can become attack surfaces.
- Frontier-diplomacy credentials can overlap with [[CoerciveFrontierDeterrence|coercive frontier deterrence]] when the killing is meant as a public warning.

- [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-115-1-jingke-ciqin-sihu-you-dianer-caoshuai-le-lgz1adsn1t8jjiaobmtfedpr-ixn|Qinji 115-1]] says Meng Jia turns the head and map into a surrender narrative that wins a grand Qin audience.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-115-2-qinwang-danan-busi-zhihou-ganle-jian-ni-buzhidao-de-shi-lidfjb6-nggubw8vpz5fvnfacrqu|Qinji 115-2]] says the map reveals the dagger only after proximity has been achieved, but Jing Ke still fails to kill the Qin king.

Frontier valuables and confidential audience:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-403-2-dujian-zhan-loulan-bugai-bei-yiwang-de-chaoji-waijiaoguan-fujiezi-lssxm3z-fkdblz8nfoy1pojnetja|Hanji 403-2]] says Fu Jiezi displays gold, silver, jewels, and silk to draw Angui back into reception.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-403-2-dujian-zhan-loulan-bugai-bei-yiwang-de-chaoji-waijiaoguan-fujiezi-lssxm3z-fkdblz8nfoy1pojnetja|Hanji 403-2]] says Fu claims an imperial secret message to separate Angui from attendants before two Han warriors stab him.

Court meeting and imperial command:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-963-shishang-bei-diantian-deng-de-diyiren-shi-ta-lkpkhlpkv3xpw-hur4b7sh-3druk|Hanji 963]] says Emperor Xian's court meeting, controlled guards, Shi Sunrui's edict, and Li Su's placement create the access window for killing Dong Zhuo.

Courtesy and escort:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-970-yuanshao-ansha-lvbu-weihe-hui-shibai-ln2pnvit-qgpnhn-pne7o-p1irrs|Hanji 970]] says Yuan Shao uses appointment and armed escort language to create an assassination opening against Lü Bu, who survives by detecting the escort threat and escaping.

False surrender:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1019-jiemi-chenminwang-liuchong-de-chuanqi-yisheng-ll2fma7wupp5gczudysfett5lhio|Hanji 1019]] says Yuan Shu sends Zhang Kai under a surrender pretext to kill Liu Chong and Luo Jun.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

This concept is operational, not an all-purpose moral label for assassination. Qinji 115-2 shows that valid access does not guarantee mission success. Hanji 970 shows that a target who reads the credential as suspicious can defeat the attempt. Hanji 403-2 also adds Sima Guang's credibility objection: using gifts and private-message etiquette to kill a frontier ruler may win immediate shock while making later submission less trustworthy. The concept should therefore track both access effectiveness and institutional damage.
