# Communication as Ritual Participation / 传播作为仪式参与

## Definition

Communication as ritual participation is the view that media use does more than transmit information: listening, reading, commenting, and gathering can enact shared attention, reaffirm a worldview, and maintain relationships or community.

## Current Synthesis

The episode contrasts a transmission view, where value is measured by how efficiently information reaches a receiver, with a ritual view, where value also lies in participating in a common cultural event. This helps explain why an AI summary and a full conversation are not interchangeable. A summary may preserve main propositions while omitting the duration, voice, uncertainty, surprise, and social signals through which listeners feel involved.

The concept also connects digital and physical participation. Bullet comments, shared highlights, podcast affiliation, and offline gatherings can all tell a person that others are attending to the same object, though none guarantees deep community or mutual understanding.

## Key Claims

- Communication can maintain common culture and relationships even when it adds little new information.
- Full-form participation and efficient extraction are different media goals.
- Shared annotations and synchronized reactions can create a sense of co-presence across time and distance.
- A media preference can operate as a social signal that enables trust among strangers.
- Face-to-face events can restore embodied encounter and chance connection that personalized feeds often reduce.

- Transmission-versus-ritual frame: [[184-weile-zai-suanfa-shidai-bei-tingjian-women-gaibian-le-duoshao-ziji-duitan-shengdongjixi-zhangjing-lpotw0uqivlj8m6otnoxh0kahee5]] uses the distinction to explain why acquiring a summary is not the same as joining a conversation.
- Mediated co-presence: [[184-weile-zai-suanfa-shidai-bei-tingjian-women-gaibian-le-duoshao-ziji-duitan-shengdongjixi-zhangjing-lpotw0uqivlj8m6otnoxh0kahee5]] cites bullet comments and shared reading highlights as participation cues rather than mere knowledge tools.
- Offline and identity evidence: [[184-weile-zai-suanfa-shidai-bei-tingjian-women-gaibian-le-duoshao-ziji-duitan-shengdongjixi-zhangjing-lpotw0uqivlj8m6otnoxh0kahee5]] describes events and common podcast listening as ways strangers find unpredictability, initial trust, and a reason to talk.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Participation can become conformity, performance, or shallow affiliation; sharing a medium does not ensure agreement, care, or durable community. AI summaries may be entirely appropriate when the user's goal is quick information retrieval. The source applies a communication-theory distinction interpretively and does not establish the psychological effect of each feature.
