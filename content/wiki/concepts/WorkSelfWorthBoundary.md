# Work Self-Worth Boundary / 工作与自我价值边界

## Definition
Work self-worth boundary is the practice of separating task outcomes, employer demands, boss or customer judgment, market pressure, and technological usefulness from the worth of the person doing the work.

## Current Synthesis
The concept began as a response to job exit, burnout, and work-role overidentification: leaving a job does not automatically remove the habit of treating income, project success, customer approval, or constant usefulness as a verdict on the self. It then expanded through vacation, factory discipline, and AI-era worker anxiety into a broader rule: people need embodied reminders, rest, learning, and non-work perception before work becomes the only available measure of value.

VOL.214 adds the coercive workplace version. When a superior humiliates, overloads, excludes, or evaluates a worker inside a [[StructuralPowerImbalance|structural power imbalance]], the boundary is no longer only about healthy life design. It becomes immediate protection against converting bullying into self-blame, and it connects directly to [[PsychologicalBoundaryProtection|psychological boundary protection]] through object separation, evidence, expression, and outside support.

## Key Claims
- Work results are information about a task, process, market, organization, or power relationship; they should not automatically become a verdict on personhood.
- Leaving employment, taking leave, or using AI tools does not solve self-worth outsourcing if the person still measures worth by availability, output, income, or usefulness.
- Small pauses, rest boundaries, family contact, sensory perception, learning, and personal archives help keep non-work life emotionally available.
- Workers under automation or AI pressure need to preserve perception and learning without reducing human value to replaceability, token throughput, or tool fluency.
- Boss judgment becomes especially dangerous when it arrives through bullying, humiliation, surveillance, or coercive workload rather than ordinary performance feedback.
- The boundary is not denial of responsibility; it separates legitimate correction from total self-condemnation so the worker can decide, document, seek help, or change conditions.

## Evidence
- Job-exit and burnout boundary: [[ep257-bu-shangban-yi-chang-taoli-yu-xunzhao-de-fuza-maoxian-li-wflyd48dmxvgersphxfp-gnng]] frames "not being destroyed by work" as broader than quitting, because self-worth pressure can survive outside formal employment.
- Vacation boundary: [[ep265-dang-qing3xiu13-chongshang-resou-women-ruhe-gei-rensheng-fang-yige-changjia-lsloczlnkmcrrfpn9coh8rrqo-wb]] shows that official leave does not become rest if availability and guilt still define whether the worker feels valuable.
- Factory-discipline boundary: [[ruci-dagong-30-nian-liushuixian-shang-de-miankong-yu-shijian-lodsuktwtcfxnhmzwum5iesbzbme]] ties self-worth preservation to perception, learning, and self-construction inside long-term production discipline and automation.
- AI-era boundary: [[ep275-token-tongzhang-shidai-shui-hai-neng-bu-ke-tidai-renzai-zhongliu-tebie-cehua-01-lgnagvell4jyvv7eejrcgno2xjsr]] warns against treating AI proficiency, token spending, productivity ratchets, or corporate replacement anxiety as the measure of human value.
- Bullying boundary: [[vol-214-weishenme-yue-youxiu-de-ren-yue-rongyi-zai-zhichang-bei-pua-dao-huaiyi-ziji-cong-tiequan-jiaoyu-liaodao-tiequan-zhichang-lmx4ewrw7kvcqpyh-q3bur35ybgz]] adds that workplace PUA and humiliation can make excellent workers doubt themselves, so object separation and evidence-backed support become protective rather than merely reflective.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Self-worth boundaries should not be used to ignore real skill gaps, missed duties, customer harm, or safety-critical feedback. The concept is strongest when it separates responsibility from total self-condemnation; it is weaker if it turns every negative evaluation into abuse or every pressure into a reason to disengage.
