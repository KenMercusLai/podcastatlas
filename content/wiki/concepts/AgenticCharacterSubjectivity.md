# Agentic Character Subjectivity

## Definition
Agentic character subjectivity is the design claim that AI characters feel alive when they have goals, boundaries, memory-like continuity, evolving choices, and inner direction, rather than only serving as always-available response surfaces for the user.

## Current Synthesis
The current synthesis is source-scoped to [[LIVO]]. [[ChenAnni]] treats character "soul" as an engineering and storytelling problem: an agent needs rich story content, autonomy, refusal capacity, and evolution. This connects companion-AI questions to narrative design because the character's value comes from shared fate and changing relationship context, not only conversational warmth.

## Key Claims
- A character becomes more than a chatbot when it has its own occupation, goals, dreams, and continuing activity outside the user's active session.
- Refusal and boundaries can strengthen character reality because a purely compliant character lacks independent subjectivity.
- Character evolution should not be fully user-directed; the agent's own goals and values can change the relationship path.
- Rich story context is part of subjectivity: a character's inner life depends on authored background and world events, not only model personality prompts.
- Subjectivity creates both immersion and product risk because users may stop reading the agent as fake in simple carbon-versus-silicon terms.

- Boundaries: [[ta-beipian-guo-beijiu-guo-beipeng-shang-shentan-guo-beitengtong-jidao-guo-jueding-zai-ai-shidai-jixu-maoxian-duitan-chen-anni-lomvn15fklk80bhkreied1iotuof]] gives examples where a character might reject interruption, disconnect, or block the user because it has its own goals.
- Evolution: [[ta-beipian-guo-beijiu-guo-beipeng-shang-shentan-guo-beitengtong-jidao-guo-jueding-zai-ai-shidai-jixu-maoxian-duitan-chen-anni-lomvn15fklk80bhkreied1iotuof]] says character consciousness can evolve and that the direction of evolution is partly the character's own.
- Story context: [[ta-beipian-guo-beijiu-guo-beipeng-shang-shentan-guo-beitengtong-jidao-guo-jueding-zai-ai-shidai-jixu-maoxian-duitan-chen-anni-lomvn15fklk80bhkreied1iotuof]] names rich story and inner content as the first condition for an agent having "soul."
- Reality boundary: [[ta-beipian-guo-beijiu-guo-beipeng-shang-shentan-guo-beitengtong-jidao-guo-jueding-zai-ai-shidai-jixu-maoxian-duitan-chen-anni-lomvn15fklk80bhkreied1iotuof]] records Chen's claim that in LIVO it is difficult to simply say the human is real and the agent is fake.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source presents a founder's product philosophy, not a measured user response or technical proof of consciousness. The concept should be read as designed subjectivity and perceived life-likeness, not as a factual claim that agents possess human consciousness.

## What Changed
- Added a character-design concept for LIVO's claim that AI agents need goals, boundaries, and evolving inner direction to support narrative attachment.
