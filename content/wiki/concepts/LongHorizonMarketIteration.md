# Long-Horizon Market Iteration / 长周期市场迭代

## Definition

Long-horizon market iteration / 长周期市场迭代 is the operating pattern of choosing a consequential field that can sustain long commitment while using frequent market, user, and operational feedback to revise products and organizations inside that commitment.

## Current Synthesis

[[LiXiangLiAuto|李想]] summarizes strong entrepreneurship as “选得准、选得长,” but the source does not equate longevity with stubbornness. His examples move from PC media to automotive information, from automotive media to manufacturing, from an internal PHEV preference to range extension, and from product and crisis failures to revised components, communication, team process, and infrastructure.

The concept therefore joins persistence at one level with correction at another. A company can keep a long field or user problem while changing technology, configuration, organization, messaging, or execution. Market feedback is valuable only when the team can perceive it, argue about it, distinguish product weakness from noise, and act before commitment becomes inertia.

## Key Claims

- Long commitment is most useful when the chosen field has enough scale and change to reward accumulated learning.
- High-frequency feedback does not require abandoning the strategic field whenever a product or launch fails.
- Product, organization, financing, quality, and public response all generate market-relevant correction signals.
- Internal disagreement improves iteration when it exposes reasoning among trusted, complementary decision-makers.
- Persistence becomes dangerous when founder narrative converts contradictory evidence into mere hostility or temporary noise.

## Evidence

- Field selection: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-sixiaoshi-malason-fangtan-lixiang-shoudu-gongkai-jiangshu-25-nian-chuangye-zhi-lu-lqzfpstn-stz-j4ysiqhokq6s228]] follows Li from a slowing PC category into automotive media and then into the harder, longer vehicle industry.
- Product correction: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-sixiaoshi-malason-fangtan-lixiang-shoudu-gongkai-jiangshu-25-nian-chuangye-zhi-lu-lqzfpstn-stz-j4ysiqhokq6s228]] connects family-use research, range-extension testing, ONE quality remedies, MEGA changes, configuration reflection, and charging expansion.
- Organization feedback: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-sixiaoshi-malason-fangtan-lixiang-shoudu-gongkai-jiangshu-25-nian-chuangye-zhi-lu-lqzfpstn-stz-j4ysiqhokq6s228]] treats internal argument, senior hiring, and crisis disclosure as mechanisms for combining knowledge and action.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source is a successful founder's retrospective and is vulnerable to survivorship bias, narrative compression, and hindsight. It does not establish how to know in advance that a field is “accurate,” how frequently iteration should occur, or when persistence should yield to exit. Market response can also reward short-term positioning without proving durable value.
