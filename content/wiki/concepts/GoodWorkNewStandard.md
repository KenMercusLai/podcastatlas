# Good Work New Standard / 好工作的新标准

Good work new standard / 好工作的新标准 is the umbrella question in [[ep244-jizhe-yanzhong-de-hao-gongzuo-shenme-yang-lk8iz9cllzx2jou5-sw9yt2ogpyb]]. The episode treats a good job as a changing bundle of income, stability, dignity, basic protection, growth, interest, meaning, and fit with life stage rather than as one universal formula.

The concept is deliberately unstable. For some speakers, income still defines good work because money buys future security; for others, a good job must avoid dignity loss, pain, and identity capture. The episode's most important move is that "good" often collapses into [[NormalWorkBaseline|normal work]] when basic labor protections cannot be assumed.

## Key Claims
- Good work has to be judged across era, industry, age, family responsibility, body, and risk tolerance.
- High pay can make a job feel successful, but it does not erase questions of respect, control, leave, growth, or meaning.
- The standard changes when work no longer monopolizes identity, social recognition, and life purpose.
- A labor market with weak baseline protection makes ordinary rights sound like special benefits.
- In media work, "good" also depends on whether public value, learning, autonomy, and exhaustion remain in tolerable balance.
