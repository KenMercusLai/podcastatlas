# Gold Portfolio Products / 黄金家组合产品

Gold portfolio products / 黄金家组合产品 are [[ep239-he-daka-liaoliao-jinjia-you-shuang-ruo-zhuo-biao-le-putongren-hai-neng-shangche-ma-lgcy2trgzt7expvr5bcybybrummq]]'s product-design answer to ordinary investors who want gold exposure but struggle with recognition, entry timing, and holding discipline. The source describes "黄金家" as professionally managed combinations that include a meaningful gold sleeve, often at least 5%, while also holding fixed income, equities, or other assets depending on the product's risk profile.

The concept is not a single standardized product name in the source. It is better read as a category of multi-asset products where gold is supposed to be a [[StrategicGoldAllocation|strategic bottom position]] rather than a temporary add-on. [[WangLixin|王立新]] says such products may appear in FOF, bank wealth-management, or private-fund formats, and should be judged by provider quality, manager record, underlying assets, gold allocation rule, and investor risk preference.

## Key Claims
- The product's claimed value is behavioral as much as technical: it can reduce recognition difficulty, entry difficulty, and holding difficulty by embedding gold inside a managed allocation process.
- A valid "gold portfolio" should keep gold as a strategic sleeve, not simply add gold after a rally and remove it after the narrative cools.
- Conservative versions may combine fixed income and gold, while richer versions can include some equity exposure; the equity share changes the whole product's risk path.
- The investor still has to check channel, manager, fees, redemption terms, and whether the product's drawdown path fits their household need.
- Professional management can add [[InvestmentBehaviorCoaching|behavior support]], but it does not remove [[PortfolioSuitability]] or [[InvestmentRiskManagement]].
