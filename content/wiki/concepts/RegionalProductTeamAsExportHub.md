# Regional Product Team as Export Hub / 区域产品团队外溢

## Definition
A regional product team becomes an export hub when local autonomy, design capacity, and market learning develop far enough that the team creates products not only for its home market but also for neighboring or overseas markets.

## Current Synthesis
The Adidas case adds a stronger form of localization than adapting headquarters products. The source says the China team can develop local products without another management approval layer, that Shanghai designs most China-market products, that some already sell overseas, and that the team is beginning to develop ranges for Japan and South Korea. Local execution is therefore becoming reusable regional product capability.

## Key Claims
- Local decision rights can shorten the distance between consumer signals and product release.
- A mature regional team can produce transferable design knowledge rather than only execute headquarters plans.
- Overseas adoption is a stronger test than the share of products labeled local because another market must accept the output.
- Export-hub status requires talent, product authority, shared standards, and channels that can carry regional work across borders.

## Evidence
- **Authority and capacity:** [[gaojiesi-doubao-zengjia-shuidian-jiaofei-gongneng-ruixing-yinru-naicha-pinlei-gkwrirwopt2saflztgtozfrl]] reports nearly 200 designers and creative staff in China and local development without an additional management approval layer.
- **Cross-market output:** [[gaojiesi-doubao-zengjia-shuidian-jiaofei-gongneng-ruixing-yinru-naicha-pinlei-gkwrirwopt2saflztgtozfrl]] says about 65% of China-market products are Shanghai-designed, one quarter of those products sell overseas, and the team is developing for Japan and South Korea.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- The episode does not define the denominator, time period, revenue contribution, profitability, or product-level sales behind its percentages.
- Local autonomy can accelerate fit while creating duplication, brand inconsistency, portfolio complexity, or unclear accountability.
- One company's reported organization design does not prove that all regional teams should become export centers.

## What Changed
- Established the distinction between local adaptation and a regional team exporting product capability.
