# AI Cloud-Channel Dependence

## Definition

AI cloud-channel dependence is the reliance of a model provider on cloud platforms that simultaneously supply compute, financing, distribution, enterprise trust, integration support, security, and access to customer relationships.

## Current Synthesis

Cloud revenue share is not only a distribution toll. In an early market, it can buy scarce infrastructure, enterprise procurement access, implementation help, security assurances, and a party willing to carry operational responsibility. That bundle can accelerate scale even when the percentage paid appears high.

Dependence becomes strategically costly when the provider cannot distinguish its own customer relationship from the platform's, cannot compare gross and net economics, or lacks a credible route to direct sales. The right test is therefore the value and substitutability of each cloud function, not the fee percentage alone.

## Key Claims

- Cloud partners can be suppliers, financiers, channels, supporters, customers, and competitors at the same time.
- A high channel share can be economically rational when it bundles infrastructure and enterprise adoption support.
- Gross revenue presentation can obscure comparisons with providers reporting net of channel fees.
- Enterprise buyers may prefer cloud or data-platform procurement because integration, security, and accountability are bundled.
- Direct enterprise sales can reduce dependence only if the model provider builds comparable trust, support, and customer ownership.

## Evidence

- **Bundled channel role:** [[16de99b4-9940-4d7e-bd08-9ff0d4423df0-16de99b4-9940-4d7e-bd08-9ff0d4423df0]] says cloud providers contribute compute, customers, capital, technical support, security, and responsibility-bearing.
- **Reported economics:** [[16de99b4-9940-4d7e-bd08-9ff0d4423df0-16de99b4-9940-4d7e-bd08-9ff0d4423df0]] estimates cloud channel share at roughly 37% of revenue and says Anthropic uses gross revenue while OpenAI reportedly uses a net presentation.
- **Direct-sales limit:** [[16de99b4-9940-4d7e-bd08-9ff0d4423df0-16de99b4-9940-4d7e-bd08-9ff0d4423df0]] says Anthropic is building enterprise service capability but cannot quickly replace the platforms' customer access and implementation role.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source provides no channel contracts, fee definitions, partner-by-partner economics, customer-acquisition cost, support cost, gross-margin reconciliation, or proof of the reported accounting comparison. A lower direct-sales fee may be offset by higher sales, support, compliance, credit, and infrastructure costs. Multi-cloud distribution can diversify platform exposure while increasing integration complexity.

## What Changed

- Established a bundled-value interpretation of cloud channel share.
- Added customer ownership and gross-versus-net accounting as distinct dependence tests.
