# 连横 / Lianheng Strategy

连横 / lianheng strategy is the east-west, Qin-facing diplomatic path contrasted with [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-31-hezong-lianheng-weiou-qin-lncpgmxrojc0y1meg9a3ul-o9nih]]. The episode presents it mainly through [[SuQin|苏秦]]'s polemic: lianheng advocates tell eastern rulers to fear [[QinState|秦国]], pay Qin with land, and seek bilateral accommodation instead of joining a collective anti-Qin front.

The concept is not yet developed as [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]'s mature policy program in this source. Instead, the episode shows the precondition for that later contrast: Su Qin deliberately drives Zhang Yi toward Qin, secretly funds him, and lets him win [[YingSi|秦惠文王]]'s trust. Zhang Yi is therefore introduced as the coming lianheng counterpart while temporarily agreeing not to disrupt Su Qin's hezong work while Su Qin lives.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-34-qinguo-zongli-ruhe-wanzhuan-liuguo-lsenzwp0kr5wscb0btvuzmkibriq]] begins to show Zhang Yi's Qin-side method in action. Rather than asking all eastern states to submit at once, Zhang Yi works bilaterally on [[WeiState|魏国]]: after [[QinState|秦国]] takes [[PuyangWarringStates|蒲阳]], he returns it, sends a hostage, and extracts [[Shangjun|上郡]]'s fifteen counties. This makes lianheng more than a slogan against [[HezongAlliance|合纵]]; it is a practical art of separating one state into a costly bargain with Qin.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-35-lishishang-diyi-qibing-jiaofu-shi-ta-lnxgnu1czkl-ftfb3gq9kzxwnxdk]] adds the style behind that practice. Zhang Yi's advantage is not only clever wording but mobility: the episode says he does not belong fully to Qin and has not made private enemies of other states, so he can keep using bilateral pressure and reassurance without carrying Shang Yang's personal credibility costs. Qin's return of [[Jiaoyi|焦邑]] and [[QuwoWarringStates|曲沃]] to Wei is therefore another lianheng move: it separates Wei into a manageable bargain while Qin prepares a stronger status claim.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-37-suqin-liuguo-jituan-ceo-de-bukan-qingshi-fj2kdqlaxzyy2nqtcutktuwrq5fo]] keeps lianheng in motion through Zhang Yi's mobility. Zhang Yi takes [[ShanchengWarringStates|陕城]] from [[WeiState|魏国]] for Qin, meets Qi and Chu ministers at [[Niesang|啮桑]], and then leaves Qin to become Wei's chief minister. The episode therefore shows lianheng not only as Qin-centered persuasion but as a portable strategist's method inside a shifting multi-court contest with [[GongsunYan|犀首公孙衍]].

## Key Claims
- Lianheng is defined against hezong: where hezong tries to bind the eastern states together against Qin, lianheng turns Qin pressure into separate bargains.
- Su Qin's critique treats lianheng persuaders as misaligned agents because the land they recommend ceding to Qin belongs to rulers and states, not to the persuaders themselves.
- The strategy exploits fear of Qin's strength and the eastern states' difficulty maintaining trust with each other.
- In this episode, lianheng is both an opposing diplomatic logic and a career path for Zhang Yi inside Qin.
- Zhouji 34 starts turning that career path into policy practice through bilateral concession pressure against Wei.
- Zhouji 35 emphasizes Zhang Yi's portable diplomatic identity: lianheng works through flexible interest calculation rather than local loyalty alone.
- Zhouji 37 extends that portability: Zhang Yi can pressure Wei for Qin, meet Qi and Chu, and then move into Wei government as the anti-Qin field reorganizes.
