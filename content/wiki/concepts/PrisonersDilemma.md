# Prisoner's Dilemma / 囚徒困境

Prisoner's dilemma is the [[GameTheory]] situation in [[79-gewei-lingdao-danfan-zan-xuedian-boyilun-690004262]] where individual incentives can push participants toward a collectively worse result. The episode opens from a team-game anecdote and then uses the dilemma to interpret workplace conflict, price wars, and cooperation failure.

The source's important move is not cynicism. It argues that a prisoner's dilemma can sometimes be broken through better contract design, repeated interaction, credible signals, or a decision to leave a low-value game rather than keep proving one's virtue inside a bad structure.

## Key Claims
- Bad collective outcomes can emerge even when each actor is reacting rationally to uncertainty and risk.
- Moral insistence alone may fail if the other side has an incentive to defect and the agreement is not enforceable.
- Repeated play and reciprocal response can make cooperation more stable than one-shot trust.
