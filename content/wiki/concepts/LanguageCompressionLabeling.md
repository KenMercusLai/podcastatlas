# Language Compression Labeling / 语言压缩与标签化

## Definition
Language compression labeling is E46's claim that language lets people transmit models across minds and generations, but does so by compressing complex people, events, and relationships into words, labels, and short sentences that can harden into substitutes for perception.

## Current Synthesis
The concept is the fifth layer of [[EvolutionaryMisrecognition]]. Language creates civilization because one person's internal simulation can enter another person's mind and survive beyond direct experience. The cost is compression. A person such as In-chul in [[GoodFriendsFilm|《好朋友们》]] can become "the kind of person who abandons friends," and that sentence can filter seventeen years of later evidence. On the internet, the same compression becomes platform fuel: emotionally efficient labels beat complexity when feeds reward quick reaction.

## Key Claims
- Language allows knowledge to move between people, groups, and generations.
- Transmission requires compression, so words cannot carry the whole living complexity of a person or event.
- Labels can stabilize useful recognition, but they can also freeze another person into a past judgment.
- Algorithmic distribution rewards labels that carry fast emotion and identity signals.
- False or malicious speech matters because it installs distorted models inside other minds.

## Evidence
- Civilization function: [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] describes language as the breakthrough that lets simulations move between brains and accumulate across generations.
- Relationship cost: [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] says Hyun-tae's sentence about In-chul fixes a complex person into a betrayal label.
- Internet extension: [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] argues that social media and AI amplify language compression rather than inventing a wholly new problem.
- Speech harm: [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] connects harmful speech to the pollution of other people's internal worlds.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not reject language. It treats language as both civilization infrastructure and a source of distortion, so the practical issue is whether speakers and platforms preserve enough context, accuracy, and humility around labels.
