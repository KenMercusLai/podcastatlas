# Dairy Category Premiumization / 乳制品品类升级

## Definition
Dairy category premiumization is the shift from undifferentiated milk-volume competition toward higher-value dairy products, functional nutrition claims, fresh formats, cheese, and ice cream occasions.

## Current Synthesis
The source presents China's dairy industry as moving out of a down cycle caused by raw-milk oversupply and price competition. [[Mengniu|蒙牛]]'s first-half revenue and profit growth is treated as a recovery signal, while low-temperature fresh milk, cheese, nutrition-function products, and ice cream appear as higher-value growth points.

The concept sits between consumer health and food pleasure. High-protein and nutrition-oriented products connect dairy to functional demand, but ice cream, tea drinks, baking, and Western-food occasions expand use cases through taste and social consumption. The durable question is whether premiumization reflects stable demand or only a rebound from prior price pressure.

## Key Claims
- Dairy recovery can come from category mix, not only total milk volume.
- Low-temperature fresh milk, cheese, and nutrition-function products create higher-value occasions than commodity milk.
- Health and functional demand can support products such as high-protein dairy, but claims still need evidence and label clarity.
- Tea drinks, baking, Western-food occasions, and ice cream broaden dairy demand beyond household drinking milk.
- A price-cycle recovery does not guarantee durable margins if oversupply and competition return.

## Evidence
- Recovery signal: [[kaile-shi-zai-ouzhou-kaidian-guanggao-shouci-chengwei-b-zhan-diyi-da-yewu-1010144081]] says Mengniu reported first-half revenue and profit growth after a raw-milk oversupply and price-competition cycle.
- Mix-shift evidence: [[kaile-shi-zai-ouzhou-kaidian-guanggao-shouci-chengwei-b-zhan-diyi-da-yewu-1010144081]] names low-temperature fresh milk, cheese, and nutrition-function products as new dairy growth points.
- Occasion expansion: [[kaile-shi-zai-ouzhou-kaidian-guanggao-shouci-chengwei-b-zhan-diyi-da-yewu-1010144081]] says tea drinks, baking, Western food, and ice cream expanded dairy consumption scenes.
- Ice-cream branch: [[kaile-shi-zai-ouzhou-kaidian-guanggao-shouci-chengwei-b-zhan-diyi-da-yewu-1010144081]] reports that Mengniu's ice-cream unit grew and that Southeast Asian ice-cream brands were gaining share.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source gives directional recovery evidence, not a full industry cycle analysis. Functional dairy demand should not be treated as a clinical claim without product-specific evidence, and ice-cream growth may depend on weather, channel, pricing, and regional execution.

## What Changed
- Added a dairy-category concept linking recovery to fresh milk, cheese, functional products, ice cream, and expanded consumption scenes.
