# Flatulence Performance History / 放屁表演史

Flatulence performance history is the episode's entertainment branch: farting can move from accidental embarrassment into staged skill, paid spectacle, contest, sound effect, or novelty music. [[162-pishu-xianliao-renjian-zhi-qi-yitian-jici-zui-jiankang-938004270]] gives examples from medieval European "farter" performers through modern competitions, recorded sound effects, and talent-show acts.

The concept matters because it shows how taboo can become economic and artistic material. A body function that polite society suppresses can be packaged as timing, volume, duration, mimicry, musicality, or shock value when the setting declares it performance.

## Key Claims
- Performance context can convert shame into skill by making audience expectation explicit.
- The comic value depends on the same social taboo that makes accidental public farting embarrassing.
- Fart performance often mixes bodily control with clowning, sonic novelty, and status reversal.
- The source treats many anecdotes as entertaining cultural material rather than as rigorously verified history.
