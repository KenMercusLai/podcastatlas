# Construction Carbon Compliance

Construction carbon compliance is the practical process described in [[tech-20260420-0420-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260420-0420-mp-tech-pod-128]] for meeting [[California]]'s [[EmbodiedCarbonBuildingCode]]. The episode presents compliance as a documentation and review hurdle that builders have to plan for, but not as a requirement that automatically raises costs or extends schedules.

The source gives three routes: reuse at least 45% of an existing building, show that a project emits 10% less carbon, or use materials that meet lower global-warming thresholds. Those routes make the policy operational for consultants such as [[VerticalGroup]] and contractors such as [[BNBuilders]].

## Key Claims
- Compliance can be achieved through building reuse, whole-project carbon reduction, or material-specific thresholds.
- Documentation is part of the burden; builders must show that materials or projects meet the standard.
- Clear and actionable goals can make the requirement manageable for contractors.
- Compliance links policy to supplier behavior because builders need findable [[LowCarbonConstructionMaterials]].
