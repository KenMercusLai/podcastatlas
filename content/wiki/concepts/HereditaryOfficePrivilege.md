## Definition

任子式官职特权 / hereditary office privilege is the Hanji 434 personnel problem in which sons or younger relatives of officials enter office through family sponsorship rather than demonstrated ability, learning, or public usefulness.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 434 introduces the pattern through [[WangJiWesternHan|王吉]]'s Shenjue 1 memorial to [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]]. Wang Ji argues that a dynasty seeking durable order cannot rely on court favors, amnesties, or tax relief alone while its personnel pipeline still lets official households convert status into appointment.

The concept is not simply anti-family or anti-elite. Its boundary is public-office fit: Wang Ji's criticism is that many sponsored official sons are arrogant, historically ignorant, and untrained for governance. He therefore pairs abolition of "任子令" with open selection of talent and with the separate warning that people close to the ruler may receive gifts but should not receive important posts merely because they are intimate.

## Key Claims

- Family sponsorship can turn office into inherited privilege rather than public responsibility.
- The risk is capability and character mismatch: status-backed entrants may lack learning, historical judgment, and humility.
- Abolishing hereditary privilege belongs to a broader root-cause reform package, not only to moral scolding.
- The concept overlaps with ruler-intimate appointment risk because both substitute relationship for demonstrated fitness.

- Reform proposal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-434-yidai-nengli-dangan-zhize-huangdi-jiemi-hanchao-qiren-zhangchang-ligbhsgfymvjlhpvieqhpbkpvqk4|Hanji 434]] says Wang Ji advocates public selection of talent and abolition of the "任子令."
- Intimacy boundary: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-434-yidai-nengli-dangan-zhize-huangdi-jiemi-hanchao-qiren-zhangchang-ligbhsgfymvjlhpvieqhpbkpvqk4|Hanji 434]] says Wang Ji allows generous gifts to the ruler's intimates but rejects giving them major court offices.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

This page is currently grounded in one source's summary of Wang Ji's memorial. It does not reconstruct the full legal history of 任子令, the social composition of Han officeholding, or whether all family-sponsored officials were incompetent.

## What Changed

- Created the concept from Hanji 434's Wang Ji memorial against family-sponsored officeholding.
