# Compatibility as an Interaction Dynamic

## Definition

Compatibility as an interaction dynamic is the view that relational fit is discovered and developed through what two people create together across situations, not reliably predicted from isolated traits, preferences, surface similarity, or a single first impression.

## Current Synthesis

Profiles can describe individuals without capturing the distinctive pattern produced when they interact. The episode reports that extensive personality and preference questionnaires failed to predict speed-date connection, then argues that initial similarity may attract while durable fit depends more on qualities people discover, develop, and perceive together.

Compatibility therefore needs situational sampling. Emotional disclosure, play, physical effort, cooperation, disagreement, and problem solving reveal different dimensions of a possible relationship. This does not eliminate selection or deal breakers; it narrows what profile matching can promise and treats fit as partly path-dependent.

The Finkel episode adds a time-and-effort dimension by contrasting destiny and growth mindsets. Under a destiny view, conflict is evidence that fit was absent; under a growth view, some conflict can become information and practice through which partners understand one another and build compatibility. This extends interaction-developed fit beyond dating situations into established relationships without claiming that effort can repair every pairing.

## Key Claims

- Individual traits do not mechanically determine the dynamic formed by a particular pair.
- Stated partner preferences may fail to predict whom a person connects with in live interaction.
- Felt or developed similarity can matter differently from objective similarity measured in advance.
- A first impression from one setting under-samples behavior relevant to partnership or collaboration.
- Varied shared situations reveal more than repeated profile comparison alone.
- A growth mindset can treat some conflict as an opportunity to develop fit rather than automatic proof of incompatibility.
- Built compatibility still operates within values, consent, power, and deal-breaker constraints.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The supplied summaries do not identify the speed-dating or mindset studies, samples, measures, effect sizes, or replication records, so the empirical scope remains source-scoped. Interaction and effort cannot make every pair compatible, repeated exposure can reveal danger rather than fit, and office settings introduce power and breakup risks even when they offer varied observation. Growth framing must not turn coercion, abuse, chronic danger, or repeated refusal of accountability into a compatibility exercise.

## What Changed
