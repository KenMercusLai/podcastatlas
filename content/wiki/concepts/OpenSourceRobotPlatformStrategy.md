# Open Source Robot Platform Strategy

## Definition
Open source robot platform strategy is the use of a shared robot body, drawings, software, or development interface to lower adoption friction and turn outside researchers, schools, developers, and model companies into an ecosystem around a company's hardware platform.

## Current Synthesis
In [[22-sui-de-jushen-ceo-5-lun-rongzi-guo-yi-meiyuan-buzhi-tian-you-duo-gao-yinian-chile-shi-nian-de-ku-duitan-huangyi-luobo-paidui-chuangshiren-ceo-ljwdmocryiblf0k3rkz8eonkqox]], [[HuangYiRobotics|Huang Yi]] argues that open source is commercially useful only when the product itself is good enough to invite real reuse. For [[LuoboPaidui]], open source is meant to reduce duplicated robot-building work, make collaboration with model companies easier, generate research and education adoption, and let papers, users, and support communities feed back into the platform.

## Key Claims
- Openness lowers cooperation resistance when robot users and model companies need a body platform they can inspect and modify.
- The strategy depends on product quality; open-sourcing an unusable robot does not create meaningful ecosystem value.
- Research and education users are attractive early adopters because they can adapt the platform, publish work, and surface detailed technical needs.
- Open source can coexist with hardware commercialization when the company captures value through the platform, supply chain, productization, service, and follow-on products.
- The platform must keep improving through customer pain points; openness alone does not solve manufacturing, support, or reliability.

## Evidence
- Cooperation resistance and product quality: [[22-sui-de-jushen-ceo-5-lun-rongzi-guo-yi-meiyuan-buzhi-tian-you-duo-gao-yinian-chile-shi-nian-de-ku-duitan-huangyi-luobo-paidui-chuangshiren-ceo-ljwdmocryiblf0k3rkz8eonkqox]] records Huang's claim that good open-source products lower cooperation friction, while poor open-source products do not matter.
- Research and education adoption: [[22-sui-de-jushen-ceo-5-lun-rongzi-guo-yi-meiyuan-buzhi-tian-you-duo-gao-yinian-chile-shi-nian-de-ku-duitan-huangyi-luobo-paidui-chuangshiren-ceo-ljwdmocryiblf0k3rkz8eonkqox]] ties RPO and RP1 to schools, labs, startup users, paper production, and technical requests.
- Commercial capture and continuous improvement: [[22-sui-de-jushen-ceo-5-lun-rongzi-guo-yi-meiyuan-buzhi-tian-you-duo-gao-yinian-chile-shi-nian-de-ku-duitan-huangyi-luobo-paidui-chuangshiren-ceo-ljwdmocryiblf0k3rkz8eonkqox]] connects open source to Luobo Paidui's body-platform role between model companies and robot hardware.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is a founder interview, so it does not independently prove that open-source adoption will become a durable moat. It also shows execution risk: supply chain, delivery, support, and productization constraints still limited RPO even after open-source interest appeared.

## What Changed
- Created this concept to distinguish open-source robot platforms from general [[OpenSourceCommunityCommercialization]] and open-source AI infrastructure.
- Added Luobo Paidui's RPO/RP1 route as a robotics-specific case where openness is used to seed body-platform adoption.
