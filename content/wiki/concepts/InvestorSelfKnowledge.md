# Investor Self-Knowledge / 投资者自知

## Definition

Investor self-knowledge is the discipline of matching market participation to one's motives, temperament, competence, capital, bodily response, tacit experience, and beliefs about how returns are earned.

## Current Synthesis

The sources treat the investor as a concrete person rather than a defective rational optimizer. Rational knowledge matters, but action also carries history, preference, family context, habit, embodied response, tacit skill, and a foundational commitment to a particular game. Self-knowledge therefore begins before security selection: why enter markets at all, what kind of life and freedom are being sought, which adverse path and workload remain holdable, and whether active investing should be simplified, indexed, delegated, or declined.

This is not permission to call every impulse authentic. E250 makes the test more operational: write forecasts and feelings before outcomes, compare them with later results, experience at least one meaningful cycle, and distinguish a correct outcome from a repeatable decision process. A method becomes personally coherent only when its return source, work demands, time horizon, risk price, and execution behavior match the person who must live through it.

## Key Claims

- Securities investing is optional; nonparticipation, index exposure, or delegation can be mature choices when active investing lacks durable fit.
- Personal fit includes “心、技、钱”: psychological disposition, professional capability, and the properties and obligations of the capital.
- Investment purpose should be specific enough to shape required return, horizon, liquidity, and acceptable loss rather than defaulting to “more money.”
- Tacit and bodily knowledge can inform judgment, but they need training, context, and review rather than automatic trust.
- A strategy's adverse regime is part of its identity; self-knowledge includes knowing which drawdown, boredom, workload, and uncertainty one can actually bear.
- Freedom should be judged by recovered choice and responsibility, not only by an asset number or relative return.

## Evidence

- **Participation and freedom:** [[touzizhe-de-diren-wo-yu-wo-zhouxuan-jiu-ljhveb4f95emokkbltnvlmbxvbmd]] argues that “存在先于市场，自由先于收益” and explicitly permits opting out, indexing, or professional delegation.
- **Purpose and life fit:** [[e250-weishenme-xuele-zheme-duo-zhishi-que-haishi-zuobuhao-touzi-lqb8zb64t2lk_xh-kdazq8hnlghq]] begins coaching with why the person invests, how much is enough, and which life the money is meant to support.
- **Knowledge and action:** [[touzizhe-de-diren-wo-yu-wo-zhouxuan-jiu-ljhveb4f95emokkbltnvlmbxvbmd]] separates rational knowledge, tacit experience, bodily intelligence, and belief while insisting that none alone guarantees good action.
- **Strategy fit:** [[touzizhe-de-diren-wo-yu-wo-zhouxuan-jiu-ljhveb4f95emokkbltnvlmbxvbmd]] tests a method through required endowment, daily process, return source, favorable and adverse regimes, and risk cost; [[e250-weishenme-xuele-zheme-duo-zhishi-que-haishi-zuobuhao-touzi-lqb8zb64t2lk_xh-kdazq8hnlghq]] contrasts the time and attention demands of short-term trading, ETF systems, and individual-stock research.
- **Self-calibration:** [[e250-weishenme-xuele-zheme-duo-zhishi-que-haishi-zuobuhao-touzi-lqb8zb64t2lk_xh-kdazq8hnlghq]] uses contemporaneous logs and repeated market cycles to expose unreliable prediction, hindsight, fear, and confidence.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Self-knowledge is revisable and can be distorted by recent outcomes, identity protection, deference to a trusted coach, or rationalization. Bodily comfort is not proof of positive expected return, and confidence in a chosen “axis” does not validate the axis. One completed cycle also does not prove robustness across future regimes. Both sources are practical conversations rather than controlled studies of investor outcomes.

## What Changed

- Added life purpose, family context, workload, and a concrete definition of enough to the fit test.
- Added contemporaneous records and full-cycle experience as checks against self-description and hindsight.
