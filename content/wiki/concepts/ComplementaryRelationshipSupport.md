# Complementary Relationship Support / 互补式关系支持

Complementary relationship support is the source's argument that an intimate partner may be valuable precisely because they are not identical to oneself. In [[chuantai-ni-chi-xiangcai-ma-nvshengban-ni-yuanyi-he-xingzhuan-zhihou-de-ziji-zai-yiqi-ma-555771723]], [[ZhiZaoLaoYe|纸造/老爷]] and [[QinZong|秦总]] initially prefer difference because unfamiliarity, new experience, and opposite strengths can help a person grow or stabilize.

The concept is not a simple praise of opposites. The episode still assumes some baseline compatibility; shallow differences in music, food, schedule, or hobbies are easier to accept than fundamental value conflict. Complementarity becomes useful when it adds perspective, courage, steadiness, or practical capacity without turning into nonrecognition or contempt.

## Key Claims

- Mystery and gradual discovery can be part of romantic attraction.
- Different strengths can cover each person's weakest points, especially around anxiety, emotional collapse, and practical action.
- Complementarity fails when the other person's calmness becomes invalidation or when different values make support impossible.
- A partner need not match every interest if [[RelationshipNeedDiversification]] lets friends and communities absorb some needs.
- Good complementarity requires [[EmpathyBoundaries]]: understanding the other person without disappearing into them.
