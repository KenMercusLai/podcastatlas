# Focused Ultrasound Neuromodulation

## Definition
Focused ultrasound neuromodulation is the source's branch for using MRI-guided ultrasound energy to ablate, modulate, open delivery pathways, or otherwise influence brain targets without implanting electrodes.

## Current Synthesis
[[essentials-compulsive-behaviors-deep-brain-stimulation-dr-casey-halpern-scim9347675622]] presents focused ultrasound as a powerful non-invasive neurosurgical tool whose psychiatric promise depends on target knowledge. [[CaseyHalpern]] describes MRI-guided focused ultrasound as FDA approved for tremor ablation and routinely used for Parkinson's tremor or essential tremor, often on the dominant or most affected side. The source also points beyond ablation toward possible modulation, blood-brain-barrier opening, and targeted drug delivery.

The key synthesis is that tool precision is not enough. For tremor, the therapeutic target is comparatively established; for obesity, eating disorders, OCD, depression, or addiction, Halpern says the bottleneck is knowing where to intervene. That makes focused ultrasound dependent on [[IntracranialCircuitMapping]] and broader [[CircuitBasedPsychiatry]] rather than a stand-alone psychiatric fix.

## Key Claims
- MRI-guided focused ultrasound is presented as FDA approved for tremor treatment.
- The current established use in the source is ablation for Parkinson's tremor or essential tremor.
- Future directions may include reversible modulation, blood-brain-barrier opening, and targeted drug delivery.
- Psychiatric or behavioral use is limited less by the device than by uncertainty about the right brain target.
- The tool's non-invasive appeal does not remove the need for careful diagnosis, targeting, and clinical oversight.

## Evidence
- Established tremor use - [[essentials-compulsive-behaviors-deep-brain-stimulation-dr-casey-halpern-scim9347675622]] says MRI-guided focused ultrasound is FDA approved for tremor and used clinically by Halpern.
- Future applications - [[essentials-compulsive-behaviors-deep-brain-stimulation-dr-casey-halpern-scim9347675622]] mentions modulation, blood-brain-barrier opening, and targeted drug delivery as explored possibilities.
- Targeting bottleneck - [[essentials-compulsive-behaviors-deep-brain-stimulation-dr-casey-halpern-scim9347675622]] says psychiatric disease, obesity, and eating-disorder applications depend on knowing where to target.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not establish focused ultrasound as an approved or validated treatment for OCD, addiction, obesity, depression, or binge eating. It presents a promising tool whose psychiatric relevance remains bounded by target discovery and clinical evidence.
