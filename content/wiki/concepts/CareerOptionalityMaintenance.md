# Career Optionality Maintenance / 职业选择权维护

## Definition
Career optionality maintenance is the practice of preserving the credible ability to change roles by testing external demand, updating skills, diversifying experience, and evaluating openings before personal or industry conditions narrow the available window.

## Current Synthesis
EP393 frames job search as information gathering even for someone with a stable role. Recruiter and interview feedback can reveal market value and skill gaps, while an actual move may diversify a résumé and reduce dependence on one institution. But portability is incomplete: project history and industry relationships can travel, whereas accumulated trust, reputation inside a hierarchy, and permission to exercise judgment must be rebuilt.

Optionality is therefore not constant movement. A decision should compare the value of a current trust network against the learning, resilience, and future paths created by a new role, then stress-test both against the next downturn rather than the current hiring mood alone.

## Key Claims
- External search can produce useful market feedback without obligating immediate departure.
- Long tenure creates both valuable trust and concentration risk in one organizational environment.
- Past results and portable relationships help entry but do not automatically transfer internal authority.
- A cyclical hiring window can justify action while still requiring downside analysis.
- Compensation, growth, stability, résumé breadth, travel, family timing, and regret are joint decision variables.

- Trust and portability: [[ep393-30-zhaogongzuo-tiaocao-women-zai-danyou-shenme-yiqi-duguo-rensheng-de-aodesai-shike-gkwrirwoyp8hael-1gt5tggj]] separates reusable industry relationships from institution-specific trust that must be earned again.
- Multi-objective choice: [[ep393-30-zhaogongzuo-tiaocao-women-zai-danyou-shenme-yiqi-duguo-rensheng-de-aodesai-shike-gkwrirwoyp8hael-1gt5tggj]] explains why the selected offer did not maximize pay or exposure but balanced several constraints.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Continuous market testing can consume time, damage trust, or encourage reactive comparison. A hiring rebound, interview invitation, or offer does not prove durable demand, and staying can be rational when institutional trust, health, caregiving, or downside protection outweigh diversification. The source is one investor's account, not comparative evidence that moving after a given tenure improves outcomes.

## What Changed
- Established a career-optionality frame that treats organizational trust as both capital and switching cost.
- Added next-downturn resilience and life timing to the evaluation of a current hiring window.
