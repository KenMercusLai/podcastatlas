## Definition
香港足球职业化 / Hong Kong football professionalization is the source's account of Hong Kong league football moving from nominal amateur status to permitted professional players in 1968 after the amateur-eligibility fiction became unsustainable.

## Current Synthesis
The episode frames professionalization as a reactive institutional settlement. Before 1968, Hong Kong football was formally amateur because Olympic and Asian Games eligibility depended on amateur status, but top players were already receiving signing fees, wages, bonuses, or job-linked compensation. When the Olympic amateur-status dispute made the contradiction explicit, [[HongKongFootballAssociation|the football association]] changed its rules to allow professional players.

The result was not full modern professionalism. The source repeatedly shows semi-professional residue: corporate teams could attract players by arranging stable jobs, ordinary wages remained far from modern star salaries, and club organization mixed sports associations, companies, patrons, and governing-body rules. Hong Kong became early in Asian professional football, but the episode's point is that early status did not guarantee a durable planned industry.

## Key Claims
- The 1968 professional turn was triggered by amateur-status conflict rather than a comprehensive performance-reform strategy.
- Paid football labor existed before formal professionalization through wages, signing fees, bonuses, and informal compensation.
- Early professional Hong Kong football still relied on semi-professional arrangements, including employer-linked jobs.
- Professionalization made Hong Kong an early Asian professional-football case, but not a clean model of league industrialization.
- The later ownership and foreign-player debates grew from this mixed institutional base.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not supply a complete legal or statistical history of every Hong Kong football competition. Calling the league an early Asian professional case should therefore remain source-scoped, and the exact "first" status is less important here than the institutional mechanism the episode explains.
