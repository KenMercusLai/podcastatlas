# Sovereign Oil Fund Governance

Sovereign oil fund governance is the fiscal discipline mechanism illustrated by the [[NorwegianOilFund]] in [[how-to-beat-the-resource-curse-in-norway-summer-school]]. The episode treats the fund as a way to convert temporary oil revenue into long-horizon public wealth instead of a permission slip for immediate spending.

The fund is also a political commitment device. By separating oil money from ordinary budget pressure, [[Norway]] can spend some public wealth while preserving future claims and reducing the boom-bust risks associated with [[OilRevenueDependence]].

## Key Claims
- A sovereign oil fund can turn exhaustible-resource revenue into diversified public financial wealth.
- The fund's value depends on rules and restraint, because a large balance can intensify pressure for current spending.
- Sovereign savings reduce resource risk only when paired with taxation, institutions, and public trust.
