# Eugenic Governance / 优生学治理

## Definition

Eugenic governance / 优生学治理 is the use of heredity claims, diagnostic or intelligence labels, and state power to control reproduction, migration, schooling, institutionalization, or life itself in the name of biological improvement.

## Current Synthesis

The intelligence-testing source traces the pattern from [[FrancisGalton|Francis Galton / 弗朗西斯·高尔顿]] through measurement, immigration and education sorting, and the forced sterilization of [[CarrieBuck|Carrie Buck / 卡丽·巴克]]. It shows how class, race, gender, disability, sexuality, and institutional interest can be hidden inside supposedly neutral categories.

The Nazi source extends the pattern from reproductive control into lethal state policy. Hereditary-health law and medical courts made compulsory sterilization appear scientific and nonpunitive; the [[NaziInvoluntaryEuthanasiaProgram|involuntary-euthanasia program]] then treated people classified as genetically diseased as threats to racial health. Across both sources, benevolent, legal, or scientific language does not neutralize coercion.

## Key Claims

- Eugenic governance converts contested human differences into state authority over rights, reproduction, movement, and life.
- Measurement can amplify prejudice when institutions choose biased categories and attach coercive consequences to them.
- Courts, schools, medicine, administration, and family or social shame can reinforce one another.
- The policy sequence can escalate from classification and segregation to sterilization and killing.
- Scientific and welfare language can conceal rather than prevent bodily violence.

## Evidence

- Measurement and sorting: [[37-zhishang-ceshi-qingwen-ni-shi-zhili-poluomen-ma-608020679]] traces hereditarian testing into education, immigration, institutionalization, and social ranking.
- Legal bodily coercion: [[37-zhishang-ceshi-qingwen-ni-shi-zhili-poluomen-ma-608020679]] uses Carrie Buck to show gendered blame, institutional interest, courts, and diagnosis converging on forced sterilization.
- Nazi hereditary-health administration: [[408-the-nazis-in-power-hitlers-dream-part-5-glt6100029744]] describes compulsory sterilization through broad medical categories and health courts.
- Lethal extension: [[408-the-nazis-in-power-hitlers-dream-part-5-glt6100029744]] connects racial-health reasoning and professional participation to involuntary killing.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The existence of measurable differences or useful clinical assessment does not itself imply eugenics. The concept applies when institutions convert hereditarian classifications into coercive allocation of dignity, rights, reproduction, or life. The sources are narrative podcast episodes, so exact totals, causal pathways, and individual motives remain bounded by their notes.

## What Changed

- Extended the concept from measurement, sorting, and sterilization into Nazi medical courts and involuntary killing.
- Added the role of legal and professional form in disguising coercion as health policy.
