# Biological Agency / 生物能动性

Biological agency is [[ep256-ai-shidai-ziyou-yizhi-hai-cunzai-ma-lk9aci8oqnwerk26jy683nbdddcx]]'s claim that living beings differ from passive matter because they initiate action toward survival, food, reproduction, avoidance, and continuation. [[TuMotuo|土摩托]] uses the contrast between a rolling stone and an ant seeking food to make the point: agency is tied to life, not only to abstract intelligence.

The concept gives the episode a middle path between hard [[CausalDeterminism|determinism]] and ungrounded spiritualism. Life is still physical and causal, but it organizes uncertainty, perception, action, and [[MeaningAsEvolvedFunction|meaning]] in ways that make "what the organism is doing" a real explanatory level.

## Key Claims
- Agency begins before human verbal self-awareness; organisms act in ways that are oriented toward continuation.
- Biological agency makes meaning practical: signals matter because they guide action for a living system.
- The source treats free will as more plausible when linked to life and organism-level action than when reduced to random choice.
- AI becomes philosophically risky only if it develops comparable goal, meaning, and action structures rather than merely generating text.
