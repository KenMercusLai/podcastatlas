# Female Breadwinner Norms

Female breadwinner norms are the source's account of why wives out-earning husbands remains less common and more strained than egalitarian attitudes would suggest. In [[taking-the-shine-off-albedo-and-global-temperatures-6a730fdaca067b295bab9760]], [[DougDawson]] says wives out-earn husbands in only about 30% of American dual-income couples.

The episode uses [[TaylorSwift]] and [[TravisKelce]] as a celebrity hook, then moves to the broader [[UnitedStates]] pattern. The concept links occupation, hours, pay, couple preference, [[ReportedIncomeGenderNorms]], and [[DomesticLaborDoubleBurden]] into one relationship norm rather than treating earnings as a purely individual labor-market outcome.

## Key Claims
- Direct survey answers can sound more egalitarian than couple behavior.
- The male-provider norm can survive even when women have higher education and earnings potential.
- Income imbalance is not the only stressor; domestic labor allocation can make high-earning wives carry a double load.
