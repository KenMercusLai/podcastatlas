# Patent Cross-Licensing / 专利交叉许可

## Definition
Patent cross-licensing is an agreement in which two parties grant each other rights to use defined patent portfolios, reducing bilateral infringement risk while preserving ownership of the underlying intellectual property.

## Current Synthesis
The source presents [[Huawei]] and [[Qualcomm]] as using broad cross-licensing across 5G, computing, and AI, alongside a reported sale of some Huawei U.S. patents to Qualcomm. The arrangement shows that reciprocal access and outright asset transfer can coexist: cross-licenses reduce operating friction, while selected patent purchases change ownership.

## Key Claims
- Cross-licensing can reduce bilateral patent conflict across overlapping technology stacks.
- Portfolio scope matters because a broad agreement may span standards, computing, and AI.
- A cross-license does not necessarily transfer ownership of the licensed patents.
- Separate patent sales can accompany reciprocal licensing and should not be conflated with it.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
