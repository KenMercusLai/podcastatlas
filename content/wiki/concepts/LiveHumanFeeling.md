# 活人感 / Live Human Feeling

## Definition
活人感 / live human feeling is the public-expression quality by which a person, voice, performance, or creator presence feels like bounded living personhood rather than a fully managed persona or polished content machine.

## Current Synthesis
Across the current sources, 活人感 is not identical to raw confession or absolute authenticity. It usually appears through roughness, hesitation, ordinary detail, embodied limitation, audible voice, self-correction, and the sense that judgment remains attached to a real person. [[149-baiwu-teji-he-liangwei-laomeitiren-mantan-boke-meijie-yiji-shengyin-shengtai-de-weilai-lihbq8vujsqxvb7h2-pz2xm-eqga]] applies this to Chinese podcasting: listeners may trust a personal voice because it carries duration, hesitation, and judgment outside institutional polish.

The concept also has a boundary. [[xianliao-xiang-song-zhao-lusi-yi-ben-duan-ge-ji-bianji-tuishu-wu-suo-bu-yong-qi-ji-905512770]] warns that public vulnerability can become material for [[InternetMoralTrial|internet moral trial]], while [[140-haikeyi-de-jin-nvshi-suoyi-ren-weishenme-yao-nuli-a-854173655]] shows that "being oneself" can harden into another performance script when coolness, slackness, or refusal becomes a new social template.

AI-era sources sharpen the stakes. [[e234-weilai-shipai-dianying-hai-cunzai-ma-yu-daoyan-luchuan-liaoliao-ai-gei-yingshiren-de-kongju-yu-ziyou-b2be7093-3366-4ee2-8a7a-625f06206ae5]] links live feeling to voice, breath, imperfect timing, and unrepeatable film discovery; [[ep275-token-tongzhang-shidai-shui-hai-neng-bu-ke-tidai-renzai-zhongliu-tebie-cehua-01-lgnagvell4jyvv7eejrcgno2xjsr]] treats it as part of the human residue that can disappear when work chases tool fluency and polished output.

The Luo Yonghao flaw-themed panel adds a comedy-publicness version. [[lir8w53xewcmvbqrb1x1osairafg-lir8w53xewcmvbqrb1x1osairafg]] argues through examples rather than theory: baldness, ADHD, compulsive order, late preparation, review sensitivity, and public embarrassment can make people more memorable and relatable. The same source keeps the boundary visible by asking when flaws harm delivery, family life, platform trust, or safety.

## Key Claims
- 活人感 is usually perceived through imperfect but bounded signs of personhood: voice, hesitation, bodily limits, rough emotion, ordinary detail, and self-correction.
- Public performance is not automatically false; the key question is whether real judgment, responsibility, and boundaries remain present.
- Vulnerability and flaws can create empathy or creative value, but they can also become targets for voyeurism, moral trial, or harassment.
- AI-era polish makes embodied, irregular, and socially situated human presence more salient in voice, film, work, and conversation.
- "Being oneself" can itself become a script, so live feeling depends on self-feeling and judgment rather than simply adopting a looser style.
- The Luo Yonghao panel adds that visible or behavioral flaws can reduce distance between public people and audiences, but only when harm and responsibility are still recognized.

## Evidence
- Voice and podcast presence - [[149-baiwu-teji-he-liangwei-laomeitiren-mantan-boke-meijie-yiji-shengyin-shengtai-de-weilai-lihbq8vujsqxvb7h2-pz2xm-eqga]] says Chinese podcasts create trust through personal voice, duration, hesitation, and bounded judgment rather than institutional authority alone.
- Public vulnerability boundary - [[xianliao-xiang-song-zhao-lusi-yi-ben-duan-ge-ji-bianji-tuishu-wu-suo-bu-yong-qi-ji-905512770]] uses Zhao Lusi, Takuboku, and host self-disclosure to connect rough expression with empathy while warning against whole-person condemnation.
- Self-performance caution - [[140-haikeyi-de-jin-nvshi-suoyi-ren-weishenme-yao-nuli-a-854173655]] shows that coolness, looseness, or "doing myself" can become another respectability code unless grounded in [[SelfFeelingTrust|self-feeling trust]].
- AI-era embodied performance - [[e234-weilai-shipai-dianying-hai-cunzai-ma-yu-daoyan-luchuan-liaoliao-ai-gei-yingshiren-de-kongju-yu-ziyou-b2be7093-3366-4ee2-8a7a-625f06206ae5]] treats broken sound, breath, timing, natural light, real location, and live discovery as signs of human presence that AI should not automatically smooth away.
- AI-workplace human residue - [[ep275-token-tongzhang-shidai-shui-hai-neng-bu-ke-tidai-renzai-zhongliu-tebie-cehua-01-lgnagvell4jyvv7eejrcgno2xjsr]] links live feeling to non-algorithmic capabilities, human connection, embodied experience, and self-definition under token and output pressure.
- Flaws as public closeness - [[lir8w53xewcmvbqrb1x1osairafg-lir8w53xewcmvbqrb1x1osairafg]] uses baldness, ADHD, compulsive order, reviews, and live-show preparation to argue that flaws can create recognizability and closeness while still requiring boundaries around harm.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The sources do not claim that every flaw is valuable, every polished work is dead, or every vulnerable public figure is morally correct. 活人感 is audience-perceived and unstable: it can support empathy, but it can also invite surveillance, overidentification, harassment, or demands that public people perform roughness on command.
