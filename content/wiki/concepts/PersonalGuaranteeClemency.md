# 私人担保式赦免 / Personal-Guarantee Clemency

## Definition

私人担保式赦免 / Personal-Guarantee Clemency is a punishment exception in which a trusted intermediary converts personal standing into enforceable responsibility for the person seeking mercy.

## Current Synthesis

In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1086-sunquan-beihao-zhuang-rentou-de-muhe-weihe-pianpian-fangguo-sufei-lt7xfjunv42n9532eqog-8k64odf|Hanji 1086]], [[GanNing|甘宁]] does more than ask [[SunQuan|孙权]] to spare [[SuFeiLateHan|苏飞]]. He explains a reciprocal debt, predicts that clemency will bind Su Fei, and offers his own head if Su Fei escapes. The plea works because mercy is paired with a guarantor whose loyalty and usefulness Sun Quan already values.

The mechanism is personal and exceptional, not a general legal rule. It can preserve talent or repay prior aid, but it also makes access to a powerful intermediary decisive and leaves the guarantor exposed if the beneficiary defects.

## Key Claims

- Intercession becomes more credible when the intermediary accepts a concrete downside for failure.
- A ruler can grant mercy without treating the beneficiary as independently trustworthy by shifting part of the risk to a trusted guarantor.
- Reciprocal obligation can survive regime change when the beneficiary of an old favor gains standing in the new order.
- The mechanism remains unequal because people without a credible intermediary may not receive the same exception.

## Evidence

- Life-backed guarantee: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1086-sunquan-beihao-zhuang-rentou-de-muhe-weihe-pianpian-fangguo-sufei-lt7xfjunv42n9532eqog-8k64odf|Hanji 1086]] says Gan Ning offers his own head if Su Fei flees after pardon.
- Reciprocal debt: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1086-sunquan-beihao-zhuang-rentou-de-muhe-weihe-pianpian-fangguo-sufei-lt7xfjunv42n9532eqog-8k64odf|Hanji 1086]] makes Su Fei's earlier aid the moral ground for Gan Ning's intervention.
- Ruler acceptance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1086-sunquan-beihao-zhuang-rentou-de-muhe-weihe-pianpian-fangguo-sufei-lt7xfjunv42n9532eqog-8k64odf|Hanji 1086]] says Sun Quan changes the intended execution after hearing the guarantee.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source does not establish the formal legal status of the pardon, whether Gan Ning's promise was enforceable, or whether Su Fei later remained loyal. The exact scene and wording require textual verification, and a single elite case cannot establish a general clemency institution.

## What Changed

- Created the concept from Gan Ning's guarantee for Su Fei.
