# Daoist Temple Everyday Labor / 道观日常劳动

Daoist temple everyday labor / 道观日常劳动 is [[shan-shang-shan-xia-jie-renjian-wushi-xiaoyao-ji-shenxian-lhjiguq74flhatdxnbrnzhjg-acp]]'s main correction to "仙气飘飘" images of mountain practice. In [[LiChuang|李闯]]'s account, [[WudangMountain|武当山]] life includes lessons, sweeping, altar duty, incense, tea, fire and theft prevention, night shifts, damp rooms, cold weather, insects, animals, tourists, and human relationships.

The concept does not deny religious seriousness. It says the religious form is carried by ordinary work: reciting, cleaning, guarding, scheduling, responding to pilgrims, and adjusting between [[QuanzhenDaoism|全真道教]] time and scenic-area time. It therefore extends [[RitualTourismAuthenticityTension]] from public performance into the backstage labor that makes ritual continuity possible.

## Key Claims
- Sacred atmosphere depends on practical maintenance, not only belief or scenery.
- Temple time can be split between religious calendars and tourism operations.
- Daoist practitioners are shown as ordinary people with leisure, games, learning, preferences, and work obligations.
- Demystification strengthens rather than weakens the source's account of cultivation because practice has to survive weather, bodies, and schedules.
