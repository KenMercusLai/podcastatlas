## Definition
Royal Usurpation Speech Taboo is the rule-like political sensitivity around royal kin speaking, praying, or being flattered as future emperors, even when the source does not show an open rebellion.

## Current Synthesis
Hanji 768 supplies the current case through [[LiuChangLiangKing|Liang King Liu Chang]]. Bian Ji's statement that Liu Chang should become emperor moved the event from private ritual speech into the realm of punishable succession threat. [[EmperorHeOfHan|Emperor He]] chose limited territorial punishment instead of imprisonment, showing that the taboo could trigger sanction without automatically requiring extermination.

## Key Claims
- Royal-house members occupy a speech environment where emperor-making language is politically hazardous.
- Retainer flattery can implicate a prince because followers' words may look like factional ambition.
- Punishment can be calibrated: a taboo breach does not always mean full rebellion has been proven.
- Apology and voluntary concession can help convert a succession scare into a contained disciplinary case.
- The taboo links ritual language, legitimacy imagination, and security governance.

## Evidence
- Taboo utterance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-768-lishi-gaosu-ni-jidu-shi-shaxin-lgk8qoxvs5ax8oikgecrxqvispna|Hanji 768]] reports Bian Ji's statement that Liu Chang should become emperor.
- Calibrated sanction: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-768-lishi-gaosu-ni-jidu-shi-shaxin-lgk8qoxvs5ax8oikgecrxqvispna|Hanji 768]] says officials sought imprisonment, while Emperor He instead cut two counties from Liu Chang's kingdom.
- De-escalation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-768-lishi-gaosu-ni-jidu-shi-shaxin-lgk8qoxvs5ax8oikgecrxqvispna|Hanji 768]] says Liu Chang apologized and offered further reductions, which Emperor He declined.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not prove that Liu Chang had a concrete rebellion plan, so the concept should track speech and symbolic legitimacy risk rather than assume operational revolt. Emperor He's leniency also suggests the court distinguished between dangerous words and an active military challenge.

## What Changed
- Created the concept from Liu Chang's Hanji 768 case.
- Added a calibrated category between harmless flattery and proven rebellion.
