# Decision Guardrail Bypass

## Definition

Decision guardrail bypass occurs when a leader makes an exceptional commitment outside an established review process, removing the independent functions, delay, and dissent designed to test different dimensions of risk.

## Current Synthesis

[[the-sony-hack-the-mistakes-series-the-sony-hack-the-mistakes-series]] distinguishes a project's apparent merit from the quality of the process used to approve it. [[MichaelLynton]] found [[TheInterviewFilm|The Interview]] funny and financially plausible after an enthusiastic table read, but immediate approval removed finance, legal, marketing, public-policy, and parent-company review. A standard meeting might still have accepted the commercial case while rejecting the geopolitical exposure.

The guardrail is therefore not a claim that committees always make better decisions. Its value lies in separating emotional momentum from commitment and forcing materially different risk owners to state objections before an irreversible promise. The more unusual the exception and the larger the downside, the stronger the need to preserve review or explicitly document why it is being waived.

## Key Claims

- A decision can be commercially plausible while still failing legal, political, reputational, or security review.
- Cross-functional process creates value by exposing different risk categories, not merely by slowing approval.
- Enthusiasm and immediate social feedback are weak substitutes for independent assessment.
- A promise of an instant answer can itself remove the cooling-off period needed for disciplined judgment.
- Rare exceptions deserve more scrutiny because routine competence does not protect against an untested departure from routine.
- Process failure does not prove that the bypass caused every downstream harm, but it removes a chance to detect and bound exposure.

## Evidence

### Cross-functional review removed

- [[the-sony-hack-the-mistakes-series-the-sony-hack-the-mistakes-series]] reports that Sony's normal greenlight meeting included finance, legal, marketing, public policy, and other functions, but the film bypassed that meeting.

### Distinct commercial and geopolitical judgments

- [[the-sony-hack-the-mistakes-series-the-sony-hack-the-mistakes-series]] reports Lynton's view that the film might have passed financial review while legal, policy, or Japanese parent-company review would likely have stopped it.

### Emotional momentum

- [[the-sony-hack-the-mistakes-series-the-sony-hack-the-mistakes-series]] connects the immediate approval to a highly successful table read and Lynton's desire to join the admired creative group.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Formal processes can become slow, political, or falsely reassuring, and the source does not prove that a greenlight meeting would certainly have rejected the film or prevented an attack. The counterfactual depends on Lynton's retrospective judgment. The concept therefore supports deliberate waiver and multi-domain review, not a universal rule that every decision needs maximal bureaucracy.

## What Changed

- Created a process concept separating project quality from approval quality.
- Added cross-functional disagreement and cooling-off time as distinct safeguards.
