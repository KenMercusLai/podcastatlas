## Definition

Capacity-backed diplomatic hierarchy is the pattern where inherited ritual language, seals, titles, and status memories cannot restore a hierarchy unless the regime has enough military, fiscal, and political capacity to make counterparties accept the rank order.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-674-ruoguo-wuwaijiao-gengshi-huangdi-liuxuan-you-duo-biequ-lu59astedsljoefnl6yr9d4ilirh|Hanji 674]] grounds the pattern through [[LiuXuanGengshiEmperor|更始帝刘玄]]'s Xiongnu mission. Liu Xuan sends Liu Sa and Chen Zun to return old Han-style seals and detained kin, hoping to restore the earlier Han-Xiongnu order after [[WangMang|王莽]]'s disruptions. The Xiongnu chanyu refuses the frame and says Han should instead honor Xiongnu because Xiongnu helped overthrow Wang Mang.

The diplomatic object is therefore not missing. The problem is enforcement credibility. Liu Xuan can perform restoration through seals, relatives, and old formulas, but he cannot make the chanyu accept lower status while Gengshi authority is visibly fragmented by Chimei pressure, Gongsun Shu's Shu claim, Liu Xiu's independent consolidation, and eastern regional breakaways.

## Key Claims

- Diplomatic hierarchy depends on actual regime capacity as well as inherited ritual precedent.
- Restoring old seals or kinship arrangements can fail when counterparties read the center as militarily weak.
- A counterpart can reverse the moral debt narrative if it can credibly claim that the weakened center needed its help.
- The same episode links frontier humiliation to internal fragmentation, making foreign hierarchy a symptom of domestic capacity.
- The pattern is related to recognition politics but focuses on failed hierarchy restoration rather than successful incorporation.

## Evidence

- Restoration attempt: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-674-ruoguo-wuwaijiao-gengshi-huangdi-liuxuan-you-duo-biequ-lu59astedsljoefnl6yr9d4ilirh|Hanji 674]] says Liu Xuan sends Liu Sa and Chen Zun with old-style seals and returned chanyu kin to restore the Han-Xiongnu order.
- Status reversal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-674-ruoguo-wuwaijiao-gengshi-huangdi-liuxuan-you-duo-biequ-lu59astedsljoefnl6yr9d4ilirh|Hanji 674]] says the chanyu refuses and argues that Han should honor Xiongnu because Xiongnu helped overthrow Wang Mang.
- Internal capacity backdrop: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-674-ruoguo-wuwaijiao-gengshi-huangdi-liuxuan-you-duo-biequ-lu59astedsljoefnl6yr9d4ilirh|Hanji 674]] simultaneously shows Gongsun Shu defeating Liu Xuan's expedition, Chimei turning west, Liu Xiu building independent capacity, and eastern rivals rising.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

This is currently a one-source concept. It should not be generalized into a universal rule that diplomacy is only force or that ritual never matters. Hanji 674 specifically shows old forms failing under weak central capacity. Other pages such as [[RecognitionBackedFrontierDiplomacy|Recognition-Backed Frontier Diplomacy]] and [[HanXiongnuHeqinPolicy|Han-Xiongnu Heqin Policy]] cover cases where recognition, gifts, kinship, or negotiated hierarchy can still matter when backed by credible incentives or constraints.

## What Changed

- Created the concept from Hanji 674's Xiongnu mission and the title frame "弱国无外交."
- Defined the failure as a mismatch between inherited diplomatic forms and the Gengshi court's visible internal weakness.
