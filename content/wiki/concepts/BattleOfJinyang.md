# Battle of Jinyang / 晋阳之战

## Definition
The Battle of Jinyang is the siege in which Zhi Yao's coalition nearly destroys Zhao, then collapses when Wei and Han join Zhao, reverse the flood, and destroy the Zhi house.

## Current Synthesis
The source presents Jinyang as a coupled political-military system. Zhao Wuxu's base choice turns earlier lenient rule into endurance; Zhi Yao's hydraulic innovation brings the city close to collapse but reveals how the same technique could threaten his allies; Chi Ci sees the incentive fracture but cannot make counsel effective; and Zhang Mengtan converts shared fear into coordinated defection. Victory comes from the interaction of social resilience, engineering, intelligence, secrecy, and alliance reversal rather than from one heroic act.

## Key Claims
- Defensive capacity begins before the siege because local burdens and prior treatment shape whether people will hold.
- Hydraulic attack can reduce assault costs while creating an example that frightens exposed allies.
- Coalition labels conceal sequential-elimination fears when one member is much stronger than the others.
- Accurate counsel has no corrective value if the principal dismisses it and exposes the warning.
- Zhang Mengtan's persuasion works because it names an existing common interest and provides a secret route to act on it.
- Reversing the dike turns the attacker's infrastructure into the physical mechanism of defeat.

## Evidence
- Social base and endurance: Zhao Wuxu rejects Changzi and Handan, chooses Jinyang for its accumulated public loyalty, and holds through flood and famine. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-03-weiju-hanhu-wujiandao-lkiwf0zdnylmaqjgsix-dktxea0l|Zhouji 03]]
- Engineering and threat perception: Zhi Yao floods the city, then makes Wei and Han recognize that Anyi and Pingyang are similarly exposed. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-03-weiju-hanhu-wujiandao-lkiwf0zdnylmaqjgsix-dktxea0l|Zhouji 03]]
- Intelligence and failed feedback: Chi Ci predicts defection from the allies' behavior, but Zhi Yao rejects and leaks the warning. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-03-weiju-hanhu-wujiandao-lkiwf0zdnylmaqjgsix-dktxea0l|Zhouji 03]]
- Coalition reversal and battle outcome: Zhang Mengtan secures agreement, Zhao reverses the water, and Wei-Han flank attacks destroy Zhi's position. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-03-weiju-hanhu-wujiandao-lkiwf0zdnylmaqjgsix-dktxea0l|Zhouji 03]]

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept currently rests on one podcast summary of transmitted sources. The episode itself distinguishes an unspecified siege duration in the Zizhi Tongjian account from a three-year duration in the Zhanguo Ce tradition. Exact dialogue, water height, famine details, personal gestures, motives, and the total destruction or escape details of the Zhi clan remain source-scoped. Zhao's endurance also carried catastrophic civilian costs, so survival is not a complete moral vindication.
