# Preverbal Relational Attunement / 前语言关系调谐

## Definition
Preverbal relational attunement is the practice of treating crying, facial expression, touch, movement, and bodily state as meaningful communication from a whole person before language or argument can carry the relationship.

## Current Synthesis
[[shi-di-fu-shuo-475-tongchenjie-fanyiru-geren-chenggong-he-guanxi-gangwan-yuan-ni-erzhe-jiande-llOihxUTWgcZk-xLyVEK5wwRuaP4|史蒂夫说475期]] develops the concept through Steve's relationship with his infant daughter. Because the baby cannot be persuaded logically, the caregiver has to observe signals, tolerate uncertainty, and ask what need the crying expresses instead of treating the sound as a defect to eliminate.

The episode extends the lesson beyond parenting. Steve says the experience changes how he approaches counseling clients: a person is not merely a problem awaiting explanation or correction. Attunement therefore joins close observation with restraint, preserving the other person's subjecthood when language is absent, incomplete, overwhelmed, or unequal.

## Key Claims
- Communication begins before propositional language and includes body, affect, rhythm, expression, and touch.
- Crying can communicate need even when the caregiver cannot immediately identify or satisfy that need.
- Treating distress only as a problem to stop can erase the person whose state is being expressed.
- Attunement requires uncertainty tolerance because observation does not guarantee perfect interpretation.
- The parenting lesson can inform counseling and adult care without equating adults with infants.

## Evidence
- Infant communication - [[shi-di-fu-shuo-475-tongchenjie-fanyiru-geren-chenggong-he-guanxi-gangwan-yuan-ni-erzhe-jiande-llOihxUTWgcZk-xLyVEK5wwRuaP4]] says Steve and his partner must read their infant's crying, expression, touch, and bodily signals because language and logic are not yet available.
- Problem/person distinction - [[shi-di-fu-shuo-475-tongchenjie-fanyiru-geren-chenggong-he-guanxi-gangwan-yuan-ni-erzhe-jiande-llOihxUTWgcZk-xLyVEK5wwRuaP4]] records the correction from trying to eliminate crying toward understanding the need being communicated by a complete person.
- Counseling transfer - [[shi-di-fu-shuo-475-tongchenjie-fanyiru-geren-chenggong-he-guanxi-gangwan-yuan-ni-erzhe-jiande-llOihxUTWgcZk-xLyVEK5wwRuaP4]] says fatherhood influenced Steve's way of meeting counseling clients.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is a reflective parenting and counseling conversation, not developmental research or a clinical protocol. Caregivers can misread nonverbal signals, and infant distress may require medical assessment or practical intervention rather than interpretation alone. Applying the frame to adults should preserve adult agency, language, consent, and asymmetries of responsibility rather than infantilizing them.
