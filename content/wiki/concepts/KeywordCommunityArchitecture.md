# Keyword Community Architecture / 关键词社区架构

Keyword Community Architecture is the product pattern represented by [[BaiduTieba|百度贴吧]] in [[no-212-cengjing-de-jingshen-jiaoluo-douban-zhihu-tieba-hupu-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-25-1003510980]]. Instead of asking users to join a predesigned taxonomy, Tieba turned search keywords into community containers.

The architecture made discovery cheap. A person searching for a celebrity, school, novel, game, sports team, place, or niche interest could find others through the same query. This created a mixture of openness and separation: each bar was easy to enter, but the keyword boundary still gave the group a name and local culture.

## Key Claims
- Search demand can reveal latent communities before platform managers know how to categorize them.
- Keyword containers support long-tail interests and identity clusters with low creation cost.
- The same openness creates [[PlatformCommunityGovernance]] burden because low barriers also admit spam, raids, scams, and harmful content.
- Keyword-based communities can preserve subculture variety better than a single ranked feed, but they are vulnerable when the owner monetizes or intervenes without community legitimacy.
