## Definition

Bureaucratic impeachment review is the procedural pattern in which a damaging official accusation is not accepted only as office-backed truth, but is tested against the accused official's prior conduct, the accuser's possible private resentment, and the responsibility of anyone who manufactured charges.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 511 creates the current compact case through [[WangZunWesternHan|王尊]]. [[ZhangZhongWesternHan|张忠]] impeaches Wang Zun as cruel and arrogant, and Wang Zun loses office. [[GongchengXingWesternHan|公乘兴]] then reframes the case: Wang Zun had difficult capital-region achievements, while [[YangFuWesternHan|杨辅]] allegedly had private resentment and used office procedure to build accusations.

The concept's key move is the demand for public and symmetrical review. Gongcheng Xing does not ask that Wang Zun be believed automatically because he has merit. He asks that ministers and scholars examine Wang Zun's ordinary conduct, punish Wang Zun if the charges are true, and punish the accusers if the charges are false. Chengdi's appointment of Wang Zun as Xuzhou inspector suggests at least partial restoration, but the source does not show a full transparent verdict.

## Key Claims

- A formal impeachment can carry private grievance when charge material comes from an official with personal resentment.
- Prior administrative achievement should be weighed, but it should not make the accused immune from review.
- The fairest memorial strategy can be symmetrical: punish the accused if the accusation is true and punish the accuser if it is false.
- Public review by multiple court roles can separate black from white better than single-channel accusation.
- Partial rehabilitation does not equal complete vindication when the record does not preserve the full adjudication.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source does not give the full adjudication file, Zhang Zhong's reply, Yang Fu's defense, or a final written finding. The concept therefore records Gongcheng Xing's procedural ideal and Chengdi's partial restoration, not a settled proof that every charge against Wang Zun was false.
