## Definition

边疆代理承诺绑定 / frontier proxy commitment binding is a frontier strategy in which a central commander uses local kingship, coalition action, and irreversible participation against an outside power to make a dependent polity's future alignment harder to reverse.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 807 defines the pattern through [[BanYongLateHan|Ban Yong]]'s handling of [[CheshiHouKingdomLateHan|车师后王国]]. Ban Yong does not only send Han troops. He changes local rulership by installing Jiatenu, then organizes Western Regions forces against [[Xiongnu|Northern Xiongnu]]. After a captured Xiongnu royal kinsman falls into Han hands, Ban Yong has Jiatenu kill him personally.

That act matters because it turns Cheshi from a passive beneficiary of Han protection into an exposed participant in anti-Xiongnu violence. The source's interpretation is that personal execution creates enmity and reduces the chance that Cheshi and Northern Xiongnu can easily reconcile later. This makes proxy alignment a commitment problem, not only a military-force problem.

## Key Claims

- Frontier control can work through reshaping local kingship rather than direct occupation alone.
- A dependent polity's military participation becomes stronger commitment when it creates real cost with the opposing power.
- The central commander can use symbolic or personal acts of violence to make future neutrality harder.
- The tactic can stabilize alignment but also exposes the dependent polity to retaliation.
- The concept is narrower than recognition-backed diplomacy because it emphasizes coerced or engineered commitment, not only title and submission.

## Evidence

- Local kingship: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-807-banchao-zhizi-weihe-hui-hanyuan-ruyu-liiwgi77g2on1z2vnwaf1-wvph2d|Hanji 807]] says Ban Yong installs Jiatenu as king of Cheshi Hou and replaces another local ruler with a same-clan alternative.
- Coalition action: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-807-banchao-zhizi-weihe-hui-hanyuan-ruyu-liiwgi77g2on1z2vnwaf1-wvph2d|Hanji 807]] says Ban Yong organizes Western Regions forces to attack Northern Xiongnu.
- Irreversible participation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-807-banchao-zhizi-weihe-hui-hanyuan-ruyu-liiwgi77g2on1z2vnwaf1-wvph2d|Hanji 807]] says Ban Yong has Jiatenu execute a captured Xiongnu royal kinsman, and the host reads this as making reconciliation harder.
- Retaliation exposure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-807-banchao-zhizi-weihe-hui-hanyuan-ruyu-liiwgi77g2on1z2vnwaf1-wvph2d|Hanji 807]] says Northern Xiongnu later attacks Cheshi Hou before Han assistance drives the pressure back.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept is currently source-bounded. Hanji 807 supports the strategic reading through the host's interpretation, but it does not prove Cheshi's internal willingness, Jiatenu's own political motives, or the long-term settlement. The tactic should also not be romanticized: binding a proxy through violence can create security alignment while transferring retaliation risk onto the local polity.
