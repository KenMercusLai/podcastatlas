# Packaged Water Price War

## Definition
Packaged water price war is the margin and growth pressure created when bottled-water brands compete through low price in a category with limited functional differentiation.

## Current Synthesis
In [[waimai-chengwei-haidilao-zengzhang-zuikuai-de-yewu-legao-biaoshi-buhui-yong-ai-lai-sheji-chanpin-1008870590]], packaged water is the pressured branch of [[NongfuSpring|农夫山泉]]'s portfolio. The source says tea drinks grew strongly while packaged-water revenue grew slowly, making water a case where a mature category can remain large but lose relative importance when price competition and weather reduce upside.

## Key Claims
- Packaged water can remain a large revenue base while contributing less to incremental growth.
- Price competition matters more when the product category has limited room for visible differentiation.
- Weather can compound weak beverage demand when rainy conditions reduce consumption occasions.
- Portfolio companies can offset water pressure through higher-growth adjacent categories such as unsweetened tea.

## Evidence
- Slow water growth: [[waimai-chengwei-haidilao-zengzhang-zuikuai-de-yewu-legao-biaoshi-buhui-yong-ai-lai-sheji-chanpin-1008870590]] says Nongfu Spring's packaged-water revenue grew only modestly while its share of company revenue declined.
- Price pressure: [[waimai-chengwei-haidilao-zengzhang-zuikuai-de-yewu-legao-biaoshi-buhui-yong-ai-lai-sheji-chanpin-1008870590]] describes ongoing packaged-water price competition.
- Portfolio offset: [[waimai-chengwei-haidilao-zengzhang-zuikuai-de-yewu-legao-biaoshi-buhui-yong-ai-lai-sheji-chanpin-1008870590]] contrasts water pressure with strong tea-drink growth through [[OrientalLeaf|东方树叶]].

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not compare all Chinese packaged-water companies or prove that price competition alone caused the slower growth; rainy weather and broader beverage demand also matter.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to capture the pressured bottled-water branch in Nongfu Spring's portfolio.
