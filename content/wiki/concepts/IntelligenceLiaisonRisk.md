# Intelligence Liaison Risk / 情报盟友协作风险

Intelligence liaison risk / 情报盟友协作风险 is the problem that allied intelligence cooperation can expose the source it is meant to use. In [[81-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-xia-693711209]], [[MI6]] shares [[OperationRYAN]]-related warnings with the [[CIA]] and [[UnitedStates]] while trying to hide that [[OlegGordievsky|Oleg Gordievsky]] is the [[KGB]] source behind them.

The episode shows that sanitization can reduce but not eliminate risk. American analysts' desire to identify the British source, a Danish official's loose comment, and [[AldrichAmes|Aldrich Ames]]'s later betrayal turned the allied channel itself into part of the exposure chain. The concept extends [[SpySourceProtection]] from handling the enemy to managing friends.

## Key Claims
- Sharing intelligence can be strategically necessary while still increasing source-identification risk.
- Allies may cooperate and compete at the same time, especially over unusually valuable sources.
- Sanitized reporting can still leak patterns through timing, quality, geography, and institutional access.
- A compromised official inside an allied service can defeat the original handler's source-protection discipline.
