# Surfactant Technology / 表面活性剂技术

Surfactant technology / 表面活性剂技术 is the cleaning-chemistry capability highlighted in [[mai-xiangzao-qijia-de-bainian-huawang-weishenme-chengle-bandaoti-zhizao-de-guanjiang-qiye-1004213131]]. The source explains surfactants as tools that help detach contaminants from a surface and disperse them into water so they can be carried away.

In [[Kao|花王]]'s consumer products, this capability appears in soap, detergent, facial cleanser, and household cleaning. In [[SemiconductorPrecisionCleaning|semiconductor precision cleaning]], the same family of tools is pushed to higher precision: particles and residues must be removed from wafer or packaging surfaces without redepositing or damaging the substrate.

The source's useful abstraction is that surfactant technology is not only a product ingredient. When paired with [[InterfaceScience|interface science]], customer testing, and fab validation, it becomes part of [[ChemicalControlAsManufacturingCapability|chemical control as manufacturing capability]].
