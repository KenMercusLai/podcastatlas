# Robotaxi Internationalization

## Definition
Robotaxi internationalization is the transfer of autonomous vehicles and driving systems into a foreign market through local testing, regulatory approval, fleet operations, passenger service, and commercial partnerships.

## Current Synthesis
The London case separates roles: [[ApolloGo|萝卜快跑]] provides vehicles and autonomy, while Lyft and FreeNow supply local passenger and fleet interfaces. This can lower market-entry friction, but safety-driver testing remains far from proof of driverless approval or sustainable unit economics.

## Key Claims
- Technology providers can enter foreign markets without owning every customer and fleet operation.
- Local ride-hailing platforms contribute demand, operating knowledge, and regulatory interfaces.
- Safety-driver testing is a validation stage, not evidence of full autonomy.
- Competition and policy conditions can redirect international expansion toward markets with clearer testing paths.

## Evidence

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- The proposed next-year driverless launch is forward-looking and subject to technical, regulatory, insurance, labor, and public-acceptance constraints.
- Entry into a competitive test market does not establish commercial advantage.

## What Changed
- Added partnership-led foreign market entry as a distinct robotaxi commercialization problem.
