# Matsutake Commons Management / 松茸山公共资源治理

Matsutake commons management is the resource-governance pattern added by [[144-nvxia-duan-sihe-he-tade-jiaxiang-mapingguan-women-ceng-yiwei-you-dian-you-lu-zhishi-meng-linmjqt-n-sa5sarpqgdxgtoeejt]] through [[Mapingguan|马坪关]]'s matsutake mountains. It extends the wiki's existing [[Matsutake|松茸]] and [[ForagingEthics]] branch from individual harvesting boundaries into village-level rules, finance, and enforcement.

The source says villagers responded to destructive collection around 2008 by managing access through groups, draw lots, entrance guarding, rules against digging and tree cutting, village-only contracts, per-capita revenue distribution, and a public/emergency reserve.

## Key Claims
- Commons management can use local membership rather than open outside auction when the goal is to preserve resource control and village legitimacy.
- Ecological rules and fiscal rules reinforce each other: harvesting limits protect future mushrooms, while shared revenue makes protection politically valuable.
- The episode complicates a simple [[TragedyOfTheCommons]] story by showing collective rules that prevent depletion without fully privatizing the mountain to outsiders.
- Source-scoped revenue claims, including the reported prior-year total of more than 1.3 million RMB across eight mountains, should be treated as oral figures unless later corroborated.
- The concept connects [[YunnanWildMushroomCulture]] to public finance: matsutake is not only food, taste, or export price, but also a local common asset.
