# MOSFET / MOS Transistor

MOSFET / MOS transistor is the semiconductor device route discussed in [[zhenzheng-gaibian-shijie-de-jishu-weishenme-yikaishi-dou-bu-bei-kanhao-s10e16-8c95b3dc-d75a-4bdd-84d4-2c06fd2d85b1]]. The source says [[MuhammadAtalla]] and [[DawonKahng]] developed the technology at [[BellLabs]], but it was not initially favored because it was less stable and slower than [[BipolarJunctionTransistor|BJT]] devices.

The source's central interpretation is that MOS later became important because its weaknesses were not the whole story. Lower cost, simpler structure, and better fit with integration became decisive as companies such as [[FairchildSemiconductor]] and [[Intel]] improved stability and as [[MooreLaw]] made density and scale more important.

## Key Claims
- MOS was initially unattractive by the incumbent speed and stability metrics.
- Its structural simplicity and integration fit made it a stronger long-run candidate as chips became denser.
- The MOS story is the episode's clearest semiconductor example of [[DisruptiveInnovation]].
