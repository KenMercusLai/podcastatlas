## Definition

Suspicion-triggered command mutiny / 猜疑触发的部下倒戈 is a collapse pattern in which external pressure and internal distrust make subordinates read continued loyalty, dissent, or summons as personally dangerous. Once the commander treats ordinary contact as conspiracy, subordinates seek safety through seizure, defection, or open armed rupture.

## Current Synthesis

The pattern first entered the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1025-bai-yu-caocao-lvbu-cuo-zai-jinjiuling-ljjt1t2mk77si6re-wew-hpl8qz|Hanji 1025]], where [[LyuBu|吕布]]'s besieged [[Xiapi|下邳]] command collapses after a loyalty gesture is interpreted as conspiracy. [[HouChengLateHan|侯成]] sends wine and meat to Lü Bu after recovering a valuable horse, but Lü Bu's alcohol ban and suspicion turn the offering into an accusation. The episode's causal chain is already loaded by failed alliance management, blocked relief from [[YuanShu|袁术]], the collapse of [[ZhangYangLateHan|张杨]]'s aid channel, and [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s continuing siege pressure; suspicion is the final internal trigger.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-677-liuxuandi-dangnian-zhen-shi-yishou-haopai-da-xilan-lu8hizigjkqeps6ulqfqw9gfxnkv|Hanji 677]] extends the pattern backward into Gengshi Chang'an. [[LiuXuanGengshiEmperor|更始帝刘玄]] faces [[ChimeiRebellion|赤眉]] pressure and commander disagreement, rejects Zhang Ang's plunder-and-retreat proposal, then summons suspected dissenting commanders into the palace intending to kill them. [[WeiXiaoEasternHan|隗嚣]] avoids the summons, arms himself, and exits toward Tianshui; Zhang Ang, Liao Zhan, and Hu Yin flee; Shentu Jian is killed; rebels burn the palace gate; and Liu Xuan's authority collapses into further purges. The broader synthesis is that suspicion converts a strategic disagreement into a safety crisis for the commander's own officers.

## Key Claims

- Suspicion-triggered mutiny is not caused by one order or one insult alone; it needs a pressure field where subordinates can plausibly fear punishment and see an exit.
- A loyalty performance, dissenting strategy, or summons can become dangerous when a commander recodes it as conspiracy.
- External pressure makes internal trust failures more explosive because subordinates can shift toward the besieger, a regional base, or armed self-defense.
- The pattern differs from pure status humiliation: rank, face, or insult may matter, but the decisive mechanism is fear that the commander will punish or kill loyal-but-suspect subordinates.
- The result is command-base collapse: senior officers seize advisers, flee, join rivals, burn gates, or otherwise make the commander's formal authority unusable.

## Evidence

- Xiapi loyalty gesture becomes threat: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1025-bai-yu-caocao-lvbu-cuo-zai-jinjiuling-ljjt1t2mk77si6re-wew-hpl8qz|Hanji 1025]] says Hou Cheng's wine-and-meat offering after recovering a horse is interpreted by Lü Bu through suspicion and alcohol-ban discipline.
- Xiapi mutiny completes siege collapse: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1025-bai-yu-caocao-lvbu-cuo-zai-jinjiuling-ljjt1t2mk77si6re-wew-hpl8qz|Hanji 1025]] says Hou Cheng, [[SongXianLateHan|宋宪]], and [[WeiXuLateHan|魏续]] organize defection, capture [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] and [[GaoShun|高顺]], and leave Lü Bu without a usable command base.
- Chang'an strategic disagreement becomes safety crisis: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-677-liuxuandi-dangnian-zhen-shi-yishou-haopai-da-xilan-lu8hizigjkqeps6ulqfqw9gfxnkv|Hanji 677]] says Liu Xuan rejects the plunder-and-retreat proposal, then summons suspected commanders into the palace intending to kill them.
- Chang'an rupture becomes armed exit: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-677-liuxuandi-dangnian-zhen-shi-yishou-haopai-da-xilan-lu8hizigjkqeps6ulqfqw9gfxnkv|Hanji 677]] says Wei Xiao avoids the summons and arms himself, Zhang Ang's group flees or attacks, Shentu Jian is killed, and rebels burn the palace gate.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

This concept should not flatten every defection into suspicion. Hanji 1025 is a siege-collapse case where alliance failure, blocked relief, water pressure, and factional imbalance already matter before Lü Bu's suspicion triggers the final break. Hanji 677 is a palace-and-capital rupture under Chimei pressure rather than a besieged city surrender, and the exact motives of Zhang Ang, Liao Zhan, Hu Yin, Shentu Jian, Wei Xiao, Wang Zun, and Zhou Zong remain source-scoped. The concept therefore tracks a recurring mechanism, not a claim that Liu Xuan and Lü Bu faced identical political situations.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 677 as a second bounded case, extending the concept from Xiapi siege mutiny to Gengshi Chang'an palace rupture.
- Reframed the current judgment around suspicion turning ordinary contact, dissent, or summons into a subordinate safety crisis.
- Preserved Hanji 1025 as the origin case and kept the wine/offering episode as trigger rather than root cause.
