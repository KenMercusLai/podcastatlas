# Suspicion-Triggered Command Mutiny / 猜疑触发的部下倒戈

Suspicion-triggered command mutiny / 猜疑触发的部下倒戈 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1025-bai-yu-caocao-lvbu-cuo-zai-jinjiuling-ljjt1t2mk77si6re-wew-hpl8qz|Hanji 1025]], where [[LyuBu|吕布]]'s besieged [[Xiapi|下邳]] command collapses after a loyalty gesture is interpreted as conspiracy. [[HouChengLateHan|侯成]] sends wine and meat to Lü Bu after recovering a valuable horse, but Lü Bu's alcohol ban and suspicion turn the offering into an accusation.

The mechanism is not "wine causes defeat." The source's causal chain is already loaded by failed alliance management, blocked relief from [[YuanShu|袁术]], the collapse of [[ZhangYangLateHan|张杨]]'s aid channel, and [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s continuing siege pressure. Suspicion supplies the final internal trigger: Hou Cheng, [[SongXianLateHan|宋宪]], and [[WeiXuLateHan|魏续]] organize a defection, capture [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] and [[GaoShun|高顺]], and leave Lü Bu without a usable command base.

This differs from [[StatusInsultDefectionCascade|名分羞辱触发叛变]] by emphasizing command suspicion and punishment fear inside an active siege. Status humiliation still matters, but the decisive move is that the commander's distrust makes a subordinate read continued loyalty as personally dangerous.

## Key Claims
- A loyalty performance can become dangerous if a suspicious commander recodes it as conspiracy.
- Siege pressure makes internal trust failures more explosive because subordinates can immediately choose the besieger as an exit.
- A ban or order can be the trigger without being the root cause; prior isolation and command distrust make the order volatile.
- The mutiny completes an earlier [[LyuBuFactionalBalance|吕布集团派系平衡]] problem: Lü Bu needs advisers, loyal military specialists, and ordinary officers, but cannot keep their trust aligned.
