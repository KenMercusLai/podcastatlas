# Generative Biology

Generative biology is [[AmyWebb]]'s biology-and-chemistry branch of [[AIConvergence]] in [[tech-20260106-0106-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260106-0106-mp-tech-pod-128]]. She compares it to generative AI: data goes in, and a new molecule or genome can come out.

The episode names [[EVO2]] as a research project in this direction and compares the emerging workflow to [[VibeCoding]] in biology. The source's wiki value is as a bridge between the existing [[AIForScience]] and [[AIDrugDiscoveryPlatform]] pages and a broader claim that biological design may become more programmable.

## Key Claims
- Generative biology treats molecules, genomes, or related biological artifacts as outputs that models can help design.
- The source frames the field as an extension of generative AI into biology and chemistry, not as a proven replacement for wet-lab validation.
- The concept depends on verification: generated biological candidates still need experimental evidence, safety review, and usable deployment pathways.
- The analogy to vibe coding is useful as an interface claim, but it should not erase the higher stakes and slower feedback loops of biological systems.
