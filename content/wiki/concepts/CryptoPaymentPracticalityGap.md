## Definition
Crypto payment practicality gap is the difference between visible cryptocurrency acceptance and the ability or need to rely on crypto across ordinary payment life.

## Current Synthesis
The bounded sources separate crypto-payment possibility from payment-system substitution. Lugano shows that dense local acceptance can make short Bitcoin-only living possible across groceries, deliveries, medical places, municipal services, parking fines, and taxes, while still leaving transport, fuel, bills, dentists, and some medical services uncovered. CZ's interview adds the demand-side explanation: many countries already have adequate everyday payment systems, and most people do not often need cross-border payments. Crypto payments can therefore be real in niches without becoming a complete daily-money replacement.

## Key Claims
- Payment adoption should be measured by coverage of recurring life needs, not only merchant count or symbolic acceptance.
- Local acceptance can grow through municipal services and payment terminals while still falling short of complete substitutability.
- Existing domestic payment systems reduce the urgency of crypto payment adoption where ordinary payments already work well.
- Cross-border transfer needs may be more compelling than routine domestic retail payments, but they are not daily needs for most users.
- Bitcoin's persistence and value do not by themselves prove that crypto has become practical everyday money.

## Evidence
- Local acceptance and remaining gaps: [[tech-20260114-0114-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260114-0114-mp-tech-pod-128]] documents Lugano's Bitcoin payments across many shops and municipal services, while noting unresolved gaps in transport, fuel, bills, dentists, and some medical care.
- Volatility and symbolic resistance: [[tech-20260114-0114-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260114-0114-mp-tech-pod-128]] uses a McDonald's coffee purchase, changing Bitcoin amounts, resident skepticism, and the vandalized Satoshi statue to show why payment infrastructure does not automatically become public legitimacy.
- Demand-side constraint: [[kangya-yingbian-yu-zhongsheng-dongli-cz-de-renshi-he-tianming-1736950d-420c-4021-9a56-a180810612d7]] has CZ say payment adoption is slower than early hopes partly because most countries' existing payment systems are already adequate and most people do not do cross-border payments often.
- Consensus versus payment use: [[kangya-yingbian-yu-zhongsheng-dongli-cz-de-renshi-he-tianming-1736950d-420c-4021-9a56-a180810612d7]] treats Bitcoin's survival and value as achieved blockchain consensus while keeping payments as a less complete adoption branch.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept does not claim crypto payments are unused or impossible. Lugano shows meaningful local use, and CZ leaves room for cross-border value. The gap is about completeness, repeated usefulness, pricing volatility, and whether existing payment alternatives make crypto adoption urgent enough for ordinary users.
