# Mermaid Freediving / 美人鱼潜水

Mermaid freediving / 美人鱼潜水 is [[WuShubin|吴淑彬]]'s main water-sport case in [[ep238-2025-nian-ni-wan-guo-zenyang-de-xiaozhong-yundong-lpaw-kela5vk6avl-s11h-yexwlg]]. The episode treats it as a freediving branch: participants do not use scuba tanks, must hold their breath, wear a fish-tail-like monofin costume, and complete movement sequences underwater.

The concept sits between sport, performance, and image production. Training systems, basic certificates, instructor and judge credentials, underwater pool sets, fish-tail customization, and photo-friendly choreography all help turn it into [[SportsLifestyleConsumption]] rather than only athletic training. Its niche character comes from the combined need for water comfort, breath control, aesthetic performance, equipment, and a venue willing to stage the underwater experience.

## Key Claims
- The sport's competition logic is closer to completing specified movements than simply staying underwater longest or diving deepest.
- Commercial growth depends on pools, courses, certificates, underwater scenery, photography, and fish-tail equipment.
- The episode's Beijing pool examples show how an inland city can still host a water-oriented niche sport through specialized indoor venues.
- Mermaid freediving makes "出片" part of the value proposition, which distinguishes it from purely performance-metric sports.
- Gendered imagery is present, but the source also notes male fish-tail designs and male instructors, keeping the sport from being only a feminine costume niche.
