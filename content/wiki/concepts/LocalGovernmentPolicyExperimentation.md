# Local Government Policy Experimentation / 地方政府政策实验

Local government policy experimentation is the source's concrete institutional mechanism for China's early reform development. In [[150-zhongguo-ruhe-tiaochu-pinkun-xianjing-xiandaihua-zhi-lu-you-hui-zouxiang-hefang-lgpvp04rngagkpubte2ac6i5ji3g]], local governments do not simply wait for perfect rules or private markets. They attract investment, interpret central language, coordinate departments, and adjust rules as firms appear.

The episode treats local investment attraction as especially important. Officials' personal relationships, cross-department mobilization, fiscal incentives, and hometown networks helped reduce investor uncertainty, but also created risks of favoritism and corruption. The same mechanism later needs to turn toward [[ServiceOrientedLocalGovernment]] rather than only growth and fiscal revenue.

## Key Claims
- Local governments can become market builders when private markets are not yet self-sustaining.
- Policy experiment boundaries matter because open-ended experimentation can produce corruption or local protection.
- Regional variation is real: [[Fujian]], [[Zhejiang]], and [[Hubei]] enter development at different moments and through different resources.
- Later-stage development requires more service, welfare, and evaluation capacity than early招商引资.
