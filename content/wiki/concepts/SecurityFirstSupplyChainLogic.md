# Security-First Supply Chain Logic / 安全优先供应链逻辑

Security-first supply-chain logic is [[165-nianbaoji-zhong-de-zhenshi-zhongguo-2026-lpredevu-gakn92dwutmulytmslo|episode 165]]'s formulation of the new operating order described in company annual reports. [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] highlights language from [[ZijinMining|紫金矿业]] and [[SFHolding|顺丰控股]] suggesting that global resource and supply-chain decisions increasingly rank safety first, resilience second, and efficiency third.

The concept extends [[SupplyChainSovereignty]] from a policy objective into a company operating rule. Under this logic, firms may choose costlier local production, nearshoring, friendshoring, redundant suppliers, compliance buffers, or upstream resource control because an efficient chain that can be cut by tariffs, sanctions, war, or local politics is no longer the lowest-risk chain.

## Key Claims
- The older efficiency-first globalization logic is weakening under geopolitics, tariffs, regional protection, and supply-chain security demands.
- Security-first logic does not remove efficiency concerns; it demotes them below continuity and political reliability.
- Exporters such as [[HaierSmartHome]] and [[ShenzhouInternational]] show that cost, tariff, and logistics pressure now appear together.
- Logistics operators such as [[SFHolding]] become strategic infrastructure when cross-border consumer brands need compliance, customs, warehousing, and replenishment in many markets.
- The logic pushes Chinese firms from simple exporting toward [[LocalizedGlobalCompany|localized global company]] design.
