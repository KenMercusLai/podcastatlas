# Agent Entry Point

## Definition
An agent entry point is the product layer that lets users discover, create, call, compare, and trust agents, analogous to how browsers and portals made the early web usable.

## Current Synthesis
The episode frames the agent entry point as the largest near-term opportunity after AI infrastructure becomes broadly available. Zeng's analogy is direct: the 1990s web needed browsers and portals before ordinary users could publish and navigate information; the AI agent phase may need a comparable layer before users can publish and invoke capabilities. The opportunity is not a thin directory alone: the source expects durable value around standards, complex-task agents, user trust, and workflow fit.

## Key Claims
- Building agents may become the capability-sharing equivalent of building websites.
- A useful entry point must lower both creation and invocation friction for non-expert users.
- Browser, portal, directory, and standard-setting functions may combine because agent use requires discovery, permissions, and task fit.
- Complex-task agents are more defensible than simple agents because simple tasks may be absorbed by base-model improvements.
- The strongest entry point may become a platform only after enough valuable agents and repeated task flows exist.

- Missing-layer evidence: [[153-he-zengming-liao-chanye-shiguan-canku-de-zhenxiang-hui-xiaowang-de-gongsi-youxiu-budengyu-zhuoyue-oai-anth-dagailv-bushi-yuansheng-shidai-da-yingjia-luzvhptdkotbkvr-shfrkvvuim2p]] says the AI era has not yet clearly produced its Netscape or Yahoo equivalent.
- Task-complexity evidence: [[153-he-zengming-liao-chanye-shiguan-canku-de-zhenxiang-hui-xiaowang-de-gongsi-youxiu-budengyu-zhuoyue-oai-anth-dagailv-bushi-yuansheng-shidai-da-yingjia-luzvhptdkotbkvr-shfrkvvuim2p]] argues that agents solving complex tasks matter more because simple tasks can be overtaken by improving foundation models.
- Opportunity-scope evidence: [[153-he-zengming-liao-chanye-shiguan-canku-de-zhenxiang-hui-xiaowang-de-gongsi-youxiu-budengyu-zhuoyue-oai-anth-dagailv-bushi-yuansheng-shidai-da-yingjia-luzvhptdkotbkvr-shfrkvvuim2p]] names education, health, organizational brain, CEO agent teams, and emotional companionship as possible exploration directions without settling winners.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept remains speculative because the source has not seen a clear AI-era Netscape or Yahoo. Model providers, operating systems, browsers, office suites, and vertical applications could each capture parts of the entry point; the market may not collapse into one portal.
