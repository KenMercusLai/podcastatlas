# Shrouded Job Attributes

Shrouded job attributes are hidden or poorly understood job conditions that workers do not fully price before accepting work. In [[the-real-horror-of-alien-and-how-it-explains-why-were-not-paid-enough]], the hidden transmission clause in [[AlienFilm|Alien]] becomes the inciting labor-economics example: the crew learns that refusal means forfeiting their pay only after the risk becomes immediate.

The concept extends [[InformationAsymmetryInContracts]] and [[IncompleteContract]] into job design. A contract can look voluntary while still burying risks, obligations, or penalties that workers cannot evaluate or negotiate well, especially when [[LaborMonopsony]] and [[LaborSearchFrictions]] limit exit.
