# Manufacturing Sales Shift / 制造业销售重心转移

Manufacturing sales shift / 制造业销售重心转移 is [[ep255-chang-erdai-de-jicheng-zhi-zhan-nianqingren-jieban-hui-chongsu-qiye-ma-likvzmpgwxnetpaezxclsx4s-hkw]]'s diagnosis that many manufacturers have moved from a world where the hard part was producing more goods to one where the hard part is getting products sold, recognized, and trusted. [[WeiQianSanlian|魏茜]] frames this as a move from factory and technical competition toward online, brand, channel, and narrative competition.

The concept is important for [[FactorySecondGeneration|厂二代]] because it explains why successors can enter through marketing, live-streaming, design, IP, social accounts, overseas sales, or dealer management. These are not ornamental jobs if consumer attention, inventory turnover, and channel trust are now core operating constraints.

## Key Claims
- Production competence remains necessary, but it may no longer be sufficient for growth or survival.
- Short video, live-streaming, personal accounts, exhibitions, e-commerce, and overseas platforms are treated as practical sales surfaces.
- Light-asset brands can compete with old factories by outsourcing production while winning attention, IP, design, and channel.
- The shift increases the value of [[StoryLedConsumerBranding]], [[ConsumerBrandMoat]], and [[DigitalCommerceLiteracy]] inside manufacturing firms.
- Sales and brand work can look "lighter" than factory work while becoming strategically central.
