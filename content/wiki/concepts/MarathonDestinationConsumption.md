# Marathon Destination Consumption / 马拉松目的地消费

## Definition
Marathon destination consumption is the spending generated when runners combine race participation with travel, lodging, food, local transport, equipment, recovery, and short leisure stays in a host city.

## Current Synthesis
The episode treats the marathon as both a scarce participation product and a city-weekend demand engine. Oversubscribed races can pull spending beyond registration and shoes into transport and accommodation, particularly when runners turn an away race into a short trip. The cited aggregate and per-runner figures signal scale, but the source does not separate incremental host-city spending from purchases that would have occurred elsewhere.

## Key Claims
- Oversubscribed races can create travel demand even when participant capacity is fixed.
- Runner expenditure spans recurring equipment and event-specific destination costs.
- Away races bundle sport participation with short-trip consumption.
- Host-city benefit depends on visitor share, length of stay, local capture, and event cost.

## Evidence
- Demand intensity: [[qianyu-jia-yingyuan-zhibo-tiyu-saishi-shangbannian-guonei-jinshi-xiaofeiliang-xiadie-sancheng-gkwridooqlkafaplwtreagx]] reports 32,000 Beijing Marathon entrants from more than 265,000 applicants.
- Spending scale: [[qianyu-jia-yingyuan-zhibo-tiyu-saishi-shangbannian-guonei-jinshi-xiaofeiliang-xiadie-sancheng-gkwridooqlkafaplwtreagx]] reports more than RMB 20 billion in consumption associated with certified full and half marathons in the prior year.
- Consumption bundle: [[qianyu-jia-yingyuan-zhibo-tiyu-saishi-shangbannian-guonei-jinshi-xiaofeiliang-xiadie-sancheng-gkwridooqlkafaplwtreagx]] names equipment, supplies, registration, travel, lodging, and food around away races.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Application volume is not visitor count, and gross associated spending is not net local economic impact. The source does not account for event subsidy, policing, road closure, substitution from other weekends, resident displacement, or differences among cities.

## What Changed
- Created a destination-spending concept that keeps gross participation figures separate from net urban benefit.
