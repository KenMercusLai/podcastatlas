# Tuntian System / 屯田制

## Definition

屯田制 / the tuntian system is state-directed agricultural logistics that organizes land, displaced or surrendered population, offices, production, storage, and military supply under a repeatable administrative structure. In the Cao-side source set, it is not simply soldiers farming; it is a conversion mechanism from wartime population disorder into durable provisioning capacity.

## Current Synthesis

The evidence separates strategic prehistory from formal implementation. Hanji 968 connects the 192 CE Qingzhou surrender to [[DefeatedPopulationConversion|降众资源化]]: selected fighters become [[QingzhouSoldiers|青州兵]], while other people are directed toward farming and supply. [[MaoJie|毛玠]] then joins imperial legitimacy to deep farming and stockpiling. Hanji 979 adds [[ZaoZhi|枣祗]]'s earlier emergency organization at [[DongALateHan|东阿]], showing an administrative orientation before a dedicated屯田 apparatus exists.

Hanji 1008 provides the clearest formal implementation in 196 CE. Zao Zhi proposes the policy, [[CaoCao|曹操]] appoints him屯田都尉 and [[RenJun|任峻]]典农中郎将, and production around [[Xudu|许都]] becomes a repeatable grain base. Hanji 976 retrospectively places this logistics layer inside the broader Cao-side adviser and state architecture.

Hanji 1104 strengthens the strategic judgment but compresses the chronology, describing屯田 as beginning after the 192 CE Qingzhou surrender and turning a large displaced population into production and soldiers. The current synthesis treats that as the population and strategy phase, while retaining 196 CE as the source set's specific institutional implementation point. Hanji 974's claim that earlier land clearance and屯田 pressure contributed to Yanzhou elite resistance remains a source-scoped causal hypothesis.

## Key Claims

- Tuntian converts insecure people, abandoned land, and military demand into organized production rather than relying only on requisition or plunder.
- The Qingzhou surrender supplies a population base and strategic prehistory, while the Xudu appointments provide the clearest formal administrative start.
- Offices and named administrators matter because durable food security requires repeatable authority, storage, and distribution.
- Grain production creates strategic freedom by reducing immediate campaign dependence on seized supplies.
- The system belongs to a wider architecture of legitimacy, advisers, local defense, and population incorporation.
- Agricultural state capacity can provoke distributional conflict when land clearance or control threatens established local interests.
- Large population and harvest numbers in the podcast sources are explanatory estimates, not independently verified measurements.

- Elite-resistance hypothesis: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-974-caocao-xiang-ta-tuo-qi-xian-zi-weihe-zhuanshen-ai-daozi-lhfabab7xv2vfsgnhr9ozwqhmhd|Hanji 974]] links land pressure to the Bian Rang and Yanzhou crisis, but explicitly as an interpretive prehistory.
- Adviser-system context: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-976-sanguo-moushi-dapandian-shui-shi-ni-xinzhong-de-shen-ljgpbosq-9xiowpgaeyossmgst6x|Hanji 976]] treats屯田 as one component of Cao Cao's durable organizational capacity.
- Administrative precursor: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-979-zaoyu-tongliao-beici-ta-ruhe-wei-caocao-liwan-kuanglan-luu58a2ww11g-radh-5g4l7fcis|Hanji 979]] shows Zao Zhi organizing Dong'e's officials, people, and defense before formal implementation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source set does not support a single unqualified start date. Hanji 1104's 192 CE formulation is best read as strategic and demographic prehistory; Hanji 1008 gives the narrower 196 CE formal implementation. Hanji 974's elite-resistance claim may describe land-control conflict without proving a mature屯田 bureaucracy at that earlier point. The reported million斛 harvest, modern weight conversion, supported-population estimate, and “million refugees” language remain source-scoped. The system's coercive effects on displaced people and landholders are underdescribed by sources that emphasize Cao Cao's success.

## What Changed

- Distinguished the 192 CE Qingzhou population-conversion phase from the 196 CE formal Xudu implementation.
- Added Hanji 1104's stronger claim that agricultural logistics, not initial wealth alone, created durable northern-war capacity.
- Migrated the page to synthesis-v1 and made land-conflict and numerical limits explicit.
