# Enterprise Resource Planning

Enterprise Resource Planning, or ERP, is the enterprise software layer for planning and coordinating core business resources. In [[ai-chongji-qiye-ruanjian-jutou-yu-sap-yuanxin-liao-damoxing-to-b-de-dianfu-yu-bianjie-1-174-1]], [[YuanXin]] explains ERP through [[SAP]] as a system for people, finance, materials, procurement, suppliers, orders, revenue, payment, and cross-functional process linkage.

The source distinguishes ERP from collaboration software such as [[Feishu]] and [[DingTalk]]. Collaboration tools carry visible office interaction, while ERP and adjacent HCM, finance, procurement, and manufacturing systems carry backend operational state, permission boundaries, external reporting, and audit-critical data.

## Key Claims
- ERP is a business-process system, not only a collection of modules or screens.
- Its early financial core expands into end-to-end processes such as lead-to-cash and procure-to-pay.
- ERP complexity comes from identity, security, integration, payroll, tax, e-invoicing, APIs, suppliers, banks, and local compliance.
- AI may change ERP's interaction layer through [[LanguageUserInterface]] and [[AgenticWorkflow]], but the underlying process and data substrate still matters.
- The episode makes ERP central to [[ERPTrustMoat]] because regulated, multi-country, audited operations require trust beyond generated interfaces.
