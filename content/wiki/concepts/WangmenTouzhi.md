# 望门投止 / Wangmen Touzhi

## Definition

Wangmen touzhi / 望门投止 is the idiom Hanji 895 ties to Zhang Jian's flight: a desperate fugitive sees a household gate and seeks immediate shelter.

## Current Synthesis

The idiom preserves the pressure of political pursuit, but Hanji 895 refuses to leave it as only a survival phrase. [[ZhangJianLateHan|张俭]]'s need to live is real, yet the sheltering chain exposes [[KongRong|孔融]], [[KongBaoLateHan|孔褒]], and [[KongRongMother|孔融母]] to lethal punishment. The phrase therefore belongs with [[FugitiveShelterCollateralRisk|收留逃亡者的牵连风险]] as much as with courage or refuge.

## Key Claims

- The idiom foregrounds urgency: survival pressure can make any available household look like a temporary refuge.
- Its Hanji 895 use keeps the host side visible because Zhang Jian's refuge implicates the Kong family after he escapes.
- The phrase can romanticize desperate trust if separated from Xia Fu's criticism of collateral harm.
- Tan Sitong's later allusion keeps the idiom available for broader political-persecution memory.
