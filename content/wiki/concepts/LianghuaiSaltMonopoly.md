# Lianghuai Salt Monopoly / 两淮盐业专卖

Lianghuai Salt Monopoly / 两淮盐业专卖 is the state-controlled salt business that [[no-211-huishang-wangshi-cong-qianshi-buxiu-dao-zuiniu-fengtou-1002275189]] treats as the core profit engine behind [[HuizhouMerchants]]. The source explains salt as both a daily necessity and a government-licensed trade: merchants needed permits to draw salt from assigned production areas and sell it in assigned regions.

The episode shows how rules changed competitive advantage. Early grain-for-permit systems favored merchants who could provision northern frontiers; later silver-for-permit rules favored capital, proximity to the Lianghuai salt fields, and the ability to organize finance. Huizhou merchants were therefore advantaged by [[LineageCommercialCapital]] as well as geography.

The same system carried fragility. Overissued permits, private salt, total-merchant responsibilities, informal levies, and the Yu Li Yin fund made the salt business a site where private wealth, state revenue, bureaucratic opacity, and retrospective liability overlapped.
