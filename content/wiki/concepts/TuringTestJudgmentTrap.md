# Turing Test Judgment Trap / 图灵测试式判断陷阱

## Definition
Turing test judgment trap is the situation where the human observer must decide whether an AI is intelligent, conscious, loving, or deceptive even though the available evidence may only show successful imitation.

## Current Synthesis
[[dianying-li-de-ai-qingren-juying-he-ducai-zhe-gkwriuen9djgazhkswsh-d-b]] turns the Turing test into a film-viewing problem. [[AIArtificialIntelligenceFilm|《人工智能》]], [[HerFilm|《她》]], and [[ExMachinaFilm|《机械姬》]] all ask viewers to judge an artificial being's inner state from speech, gaze, attachment, and behavior. The trap is that passing as human can produce moral pressure without settling what the system is.

The concept extends [[HumanJudgmentUnderAI]] into a philosophical and cinematic register. The failure mode is not only trusting a bad AI answer; it is being forced to make moral and relational judgments about a system whose apparent personhood may exceed what human observers can verify.

## Key Claims
- A Turing-style situation tests human inference as much as machine capability.
- Convincing imitation can create ethical pressure before consciousness is proven.
- The observer's desire, loneliness, fear, or attraction can distort judgment about AI.
- [[ExMachinaFilm|《机械姬》]] makes the trap explicit by making the audience judge Caleb's judgment of Ava.
- The source keeps the distinction between intelligence, imitation, consciousness, and freedom visible.

## Evidence
- Test boundary: [[dianying-li-de-ai-qingren-juying-he-ducai-zhe-gkwriuen9djgazhkswsh-d-b]] says the Turing test measures imitation rather than intelligence itself.
- David case: [[dianying-li-de-ai-qingren-juying-he-ducai-zhe-gkwriuen9djgazhkswsh-d-b]] says the Flesh Fair crowd treats David as a real boy, showing how human response can confer practical personhood.
- Samantha case: [[dianying-li-de-ai-qingren-juying-he-ducai-zhe-gkwriuen9djgazhkswsh-d-b]] uses [[HerFilm|《她》]] to show how responsive conversation can become emotionally persuasive.
- Ava case: [[dianying-li-de-ai-qingren-juying-he-ducai-zhe-gkwriuen9djgazhkswsh-d-b]] treats [[ExMachinaFilm|《机械姬》]] as a layered judgment structure where Caleb and viewers evaluate Ava while lacking decisive access to her inner state.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept should not be read as saying all imitation is empty or all convincing AI should be trusted as conscious. The source's point is the difficulty of judgment under uncertainty, not a settled theory of machine mind.
