# Perceived Similarity In Relationships

## Definition
Perceived similarity in relationships is the felt sense that two people have much in common, even when measured demographic, personality, or value similarity does not strongly predict whether they will actually click.

## Current Synthesis
The source separates perceived similarity from actual measured similarity. Eastwick argues that people can attach the feeling of similarity to whatever matters after they like someone, which makes similarity partly interpretive rather than only a preexisting match score.

This qualifies older self-similarity arguments in the wiki. Similarity can still feel safe and meaningful, but the relationship-research claim is narrower: what predicts attraction may be the pair's developing perception of fit more than an abstract inventory of matched traits.

## Key Claims
- Perceived similarity is a global felt judgment, not just a count of matching traits.
- People can use motivated reasoning to define what similarity means once attraction or fondness is already present.
- Actual measured similarity is generally weak at predicting whether two people will click in the source's account.
- Deal-breakers and shared values can still matter, but they should not be confused with a complete compatibility formula.
- The concept reframes [[SelfSimilarityInIntimacy]] from "same traits are enough" toward "felt recognition can emerge inside the relationship."

## Evidence
- Definition and distinction: [[science-of-attraction-compatibility-romance-dr-paul-eastwick-scim9984287085]] defines perceived similarity as a general sense that two people have a lot in common.
- Motivated reasoning: [[science-of-attraction-compatibility-romance-dr-paul-eastwick-scim9984287085]] says people can attach perceived similarity to whatever matters to them when they like someone.
- Weak actual-similarity prediction: [[science-of-attraction-compatibility-romance-dr-paul-eastwick-scim9984287085]] says actual measured similarity is generally poor at predicting whether people click.
- Filter qualification: [[science-of-attraction-compatibility-romance-dr-paul-eastwick-scim9984287085]] says filters can be useful for deal-breakers such as wanting children even though abstract preferences often fail after meeting.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept should not be read as saying shared values, life goals, culture, religion, children preferences, or socioeconomic realities never matter. The source note summarizes claims without full measures, and it distinguishes long-term relationship pools from immediate attraction contexts. Perceived similarity can also rationalize a poor fit if attraction makes people ignore serious conflicts.
