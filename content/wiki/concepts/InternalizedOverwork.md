# Internalized Overwork / 内化型过劳

## Definition
Internalized overwork is the process by which responsibility, perfectionism, loyalty, or fear of letting others down turns organizational demands into self-imposed labor beyond what was explicitly required.

## Current Synthesis
[[ep196-renlei-gaoxiao-gongzuo-yanjiu-baogao-ckwrijifxc-uabaaaad057ge|EP196]] argues that workplace pressure does not operate only through direct orders. A conscientious worker may repeatedly repair the product, protect the brand, improve a document, or close every gap because leaving work merely “good enough” feels irresponsible. The organization can then receive near-perfect effort without issuing a corresponding instruction or recognizing its health cost.

The useful boundary is not indifference. It is calibrated responsibility: distinguish consequential defects from marginal polish, make tradeoffs visible, and treat health and non-work life as real constraints. Moving from 99 to 100 may have little organizational value while carrying a large personal cost.

## Key Claims
- External work pressure can become self-sustaining when employees identify responsibility with unlimited completion.
- Perfectionism can add unrequested labor even when managers have not explicitly demanded it.
- Loyalty to a company, product, or brand can make workers absorb missing coordination and staffing.
- “Good enough” is a risk-calibrated stopping rule, not a license for careless or unsafe work.
- Health, sleep, and life outside work are constraints on responsible performance rather than residual rewards.

## Evidence
- Voluntary escalation - [[ep196-renlei-gaoxiao-gongzuo-yanjiu-baogao-ckwrijifxc-uabaaaad057ge]] says some additional labor comes from workers who cannot tolerate defects and keep filling organizational gaps.
- Marginal-quality tradeoff - [[ep196-renlei-gaoxiao-gongzuo-yanjiu-baogao-ckwrijifxc-uabaaaad057ge]] contrasts the small company benefit of moving from 99 to 100 with the potentially large cost to the worker’s health.
- Embodied cost - [[ep196-renlei-gaoxiao-gongzuo-yanjiu-baogao-ckwrijifxc-uabaaaad057ge]] connects prolonged desk work, late nights, anxiety, sleep loss, pain, numbness, and emotional exhaustion to the need for a stopping boundary.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is experiential and does not establish the prevalence or causal weight of this mechanism. Some work has clinical, legal, safety, fiduciary, or engineering thresholds where additional checking is necessary. Internalized overwork should not be used to dismiss real duties; it identifies cases where marginal polish, role ambiguity, or chronic gap-filling is mistaken for unlimited personal responsibility.

## What Changed
- Established self-imposed escalation as a distinct bridge between organizational pressure, perfectionism, and health cost.
