## Definition
益州宽惠安抚 / Yizhou pacification by leniency is the late-Han pattern in which a provincial authority stabilizes rebellion-prone Yizhou by combining force, surrender treatment, local trust, and correction of official abuse rather than relying on punishment alone.

## Current Synthesis
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-790-lishi-gaosu-ni-ruhe-he-lingdao-da-jiaodao-lulf0r1sdkod3luzwru0-kkrijxk|Hanji 790]] supplies the earliest current example. [[ZhangQiaoLateHan|Zhang Qiao]] sends [[YangSongLateHan|Yang Song]] against Feng Li; Yang Song wins militarily, treats Feng Li generously after surrender, receives the submission of thirty-six tribal groups, and reports more than ninety abusive local officials for punishment. Leniency here is not softness before coercion; it is what converts victory into settlement and addresses the administrative conduct that helped make rebellion durable.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-940-de-minxin-zhe-de-tianxia-ni-xin-bu-xin-loipf7b0p5a-yh0rj2njnsml8a1j|Hanji 940]] shows the same concept in a later and larger base-building form. After [[JiaLongLateHan|Jia Long]] suppresses [[MaXiangLateHan|Ma Xiang]] and [[ZhaoZhiLateHan|Zhao Zhi]], [[LiuYanLateHan|Liu Yan]] moves the provincial seat to [[MianzhuLateHan|Mianzhu]] and uses lenient treatment to calm former rebels and unstable local people. The source makes leniency a state-building entry strategy after [[XiJianLateHan|Xi Jian]]'s extraction and Ma Xiang's self-proclaimed kingship make both office and rebellion dangerous to the population.

Together, the sources define Yizhou leniency as practical political security. It works when force has changed the immediate balance, but durable attachment requires making provincial authority safer than abusive local officials and rebel volatility. That is why the concept sits between [[PeopleBasedPoliticalSecurity|people-based political security]] and [[ProvincialShepherdAutonomy|provincial shepherd autonomy]].

## Key Claims
- Lenient pacification can convert post-revolt fear into provisional attachment to provincial authority.
- The pattern is strongest when paired with credible coercion and visible punishment of abusive officials.
- People-support works as practical security when a governor inherits populations harmed by both official extraction and rebel disorder.
- The same policy can be morally framed as care for people and strategically framed as base-building.
- Hanji 790 makes official-abuse correction part of the pacification mechanism; Hanji 940 makes social repair the opening layer of Liu Yan's later Yizhou autonomy.

## Evidence
- Force before settlement: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-790-lishi-gaosu-ni-ruhe-he-lingdao-da-jiaodao-lulf0r1sdkod3luzwru0-kkrijxk|Hanji 790]] says Yang Song defeats Feng Li before accepting surrender and treating him generously.
- Tribal submission: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-790-lishi-gaosu-ni-ruhe-he-lingdao-da-jiaodao-lulf0r1sdkod3luzwru0-kkrijxk|Hanji 790]] reports the submission of thirty-six tribal groups after Yang Song's settlement.
- Administrative correction: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-790-lishi-gaosu-ni-ruhe-he-lingdao-da-jiaodao-lulf0r1sdkod3luzwru0-kkrijxk|Hanji 790]] says Yang Song reports more than ninety abusive local officials, who are sentenced.
- Post-revolt repair: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-940-de-minxin-zhe-de-tianxia-ni-xin-bu-xin-loipf7b0p5a-yh0rj2njnsml8a1j|Hanji 940]] says Liu Yan uses lenient treatment after Jia Long suppresses Ma Xiang and Zhao Zhi.
- Base-building setting: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-940-de-minxin-zhe-de-tianxia-ni-xin-bu-xin-loipf7b0p5a-yh0rj2njnsml8a1j|Hanji 940]] links Liu Yan's leniency to moving the provincial seat to Mianzhu and to stabilizing people alienated by Xi Jian and rebel overreach.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept should not be reduced to benevolence. Hanji 790 begins with military defeat of Feng Li, and Hanji 940 occurs after Jia Long has already suppressed Ma Xiang and Zhao Zhi. The shared mechanism is sequenced pacification: coercion changes the immediate threat, leniency reduces fear, and administrative repair makes renewed submission politically plausible.
