# 益州宽惠安抚 / Yizhou Pacification by Leniency

益州宽惠安抚 / Yizhou pacification by leniency enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-940-de-minxin-zhe-de-tianxia-ni-xin-bu-xin-loipf7b0p5a-yh0rj2njnsml8a1j|Hanji 940]] as the policy response [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]] adopts after [[JiaLongLateHan|贾龙]] suppresses [[MaXiangLateHan|马相]] and [[ZhaoZhiLateHan|赵祗]] in [[YizhouLateHan|益州]]. The source says Liu Yan moves the provincial seat to [[MianzhuLateHan|绵竹]] and uses lenient treatment to calm former rebels and unstable local people.

The concept is not simple kindness. In the source's reading, leniency is a state-building entry strategy: after [[XiJianLateHan|郗俭]] loses the population through extraction and Ma Xiang overreaches into self-proclaimed kingship, Liu Yan's practical path is to make the new provincial authority feel safer than both abusive office and rebel volatility.

This concept therefore sits between [[PeopleBasedPoliticalSecurity|民心型政治安全]] and [[ProvincialShepherdAutonomy|州牧集权与地方割据]]. It shows the people-support side of Liu Yan's later autonomy before Hanji 959 develops the coercive and route-control side.

## Key Claims

- Lenient pacification can convert post-revolt fear into provisional attachment to a new provincial authority.
- People-support works as practical security when a ruler or governor inherits a population that has just seen both official abuse and rebel disorder.
- The same policy can be morally framed as care for people and strategically framed as base-building.
- Hanji 940 makes Liu Yan's later Yizhou autonomy begin with social repair, not only with military control and road blockage.
