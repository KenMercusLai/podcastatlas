# Official History Legitimacy Repair

Official history legitimacy repair is the process by which records, court-sponsored histories, ritual claims, and selective detail make a contested power transfer look lawful after the fact. [[170-songtaizu-zhisi-zhuying-fusheng-yinmoulun-zhong-de-renxing-mituan-xia-958158115]] develops the concept through [[ZhaoGuangyi|赵光义 / 宋太宗]], [[CandleShadowAxeSound|烛影斧声]], and [[GoldenCabinetPledge|金匮之盟]].

The episode's point is not that official history is simply false. It argues that an official record can preserve real traces while also serving the winner's legitimacy. Terse death notices, missing original texts, later versions of court records, and stories that make succession look pre-authorized all need to be read through motive and chronology.

The concept extends [[SongFoundingLegitimacyPerformance|宋初建国合法性表演]] from the founding coup into the next generation. A dynasty may first stage its creation as reluctant and orderly, then later repair a disputed succession by invoking family obligation, archival discovery, and controlled修史.

## Key Claims
- Legitimacy repair often works by adding lawful form to an already completed transfer of power.
- Source suspicion should begin from institutional motive, not from automatic rejection of official records.
- Missing originals and later revisions matter most when they align with clear political benefit.
- Official narratives can reduce uncertainty for governance while increasing historical doubt.
- Repairing one succession claim may endanger other claimants implied by the same logic.
