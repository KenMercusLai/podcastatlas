## Definition

Court Intimacy as Political Resource / 宫廷亲密关系作为政治资源 is the pattern in which sexual, romantic, companionate, household, or favorite access becomes a channel for patronage, information, rank, wealth, protection, replacement, or removal inside a court.

## Current Synthesis

The current evidence joins court configurations across gender and dynasty without assuming that every favorite relationship had the same sexual content. Royal power can allocate intimate access to [[ShanyinPrincessLiuChuyu|刘楚玉]]; favorites can gain and lose position around [[WuZetian|武则天]]; and [[PrincessTaiping|太平公主]] is presented as introducing a replacement favorite to redirect attachment and settle a grievance. Western Han cases show the other direction of access: [[DengTong|邓通]] receives minting wealth from [[LiuHeng|汉文帝]], [[HanYanWesternHan|韩嫣]] receives office, rewards, and daily proximity from [[HanWudi|汉武帝]], and [[LiYannianWesternHan|李延年]] converts performance skill, kinship, and personal proximity into rank. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-plus-pan-dian-shi-shang-de-hao-se-nv-ren-men-lgsf8_asfrictdmsptsyfizykpon|Hanji 616 plus]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-372-4-xihan-huangdi-duo-shi-shuangxinglian-de-yuanyin-lqinxoxbu6dhvmdiiowocjaahkwr|Hanji 372-4]] therefore support a common mechanism without making the relationships identical.

Access becomes materially consequential when it changes office, salary, household advantage, or the ruler's attention, but those gains remain conditional. Wealth can be confiscated after succession, a favorite can be killed by stronger palace-family authority, and performer mobility remains dependent on imperial attention.

The concept does not reduce intimacy to strategy or treat closeness as proof of control. It also does not translate favorite status, co-sleeping, or shared daily life automatically into modern sexual identity. Its narrower claim is institutional: unequal court access changes who receives attention, resources, office, protection, and exposure.

## Key Claims

- Intimate or favorite access can become patronage, rank, wealth, information, and political visibility without conferring formal sovereignty.
- Royal power can allocate partners or attendants through command, making coercion and hierarchy part of the access structure.
- Skill, beauty, performance, kinship, or childhood companionship can become scarce capabilities that intensify personal proximity.
- Favorites can extend access to relatives and allies or be strategically introduced as replacements, turning intimacy into a network effect.
- Favorite-derived power remains fragile when affection shifts, succession occurs, or a stronger palace actor intervenes.
- Gender changes the moral language applied to court intimacy but does not remove its political function.
- Historical sexuality claims require source criticism because favorite terminology and shared space do not map cleanly onto modern orientation categories.

## Evidence

Access converted into resources and office:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-372-4-xihan-huangdi-duo-shi-shuangxinglian-de-yuanyin-lqinxoxbu6dhvmdiiowocjaahkwr|Hanji 372-4]] says Deng Tong receives minting wealth, Han Yan receives the shang dafu office and lavish rewards, and Li Yannian receives the xielü duwei office and a two-thousand-shi salary.

Allocated and networked intimacy:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-plus-pan-dian-shi-shang-de-hao-se-nv-ren-men-lgsf8_asfrictdmsptsyfizykpon|Hanji 616 plus]] says Liu Ziye grants Liu Chuyu thirty male attendants, Princess Taiping introduces Zhang Yizhi, and Zhang Yizhi brings Zhang Changzong into the palace.

Skill and household brokerage:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-372-4-xihan-huangdi-duo-shi-shuangxinglian-de-yuanyin-lqinxoxbu6dhvmdiiowocjaahkwr|Hanji 372-4]] links Han Yan's martial skill and Li Yannian's performance skill plus Lady Li's favor to their court ascent.

Replacement and protection limits:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-plus-pan-dian-shi-shang-de-hao-se-nv-ren-men-lgsf8_asfrictdmsptsyfizykpon|Hanji 616 plus]] links Xue Huaiyi's loss of favor to retaliation and violent death.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-372-4-xihan-huangdi-duo-shi-shuangxinglian-de-yuanyin-lqinxoxbu6dhvmdiiowocjaahkwr|Hanji 372-4]] says Deng Tong loses wealth after succession and Han Yan is killed despite Wudi's protection.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Both sources are compressed popular history, and their most vivid private motives and sexual claims are not independently verified here. Favorite status does not prove that every reward or political act was sexually motivated, while access is not equivalent to control. Hanji 616 plus contains hostile or sensational traditions around elite women; Hanji 372-4 translates favorite relationships into “boyfriend” and “lover” language and offers an unsupported genetic explanation. The synthesis therefore uses observable access, office, reward, kinship, replacement, and punishment while keeping sexual identity, private motive, and scandal narratives source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Expanded the concept from female royal access and Wu-Zhou favorites to Western Han male favorites across friendship, performance, household, and imperial-patronage routes.
- Made the distinction between historically observable intimate access and modern sexual orientation labels explicit.
- Added succession reversal and stronger palace-family intervention as limits on favorite-derived power.
