## Definition

Court Intimacy as Political Resource / 宫廷亲密关系作为政治资源 is the pattern in which sexual or romantic access, favorite status, household placement, and partner selection function as channels for patronage, information, rank, protection, revenge, and removal inside a court.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-plus-pan-dian-shi-shang-de-hao-se-nv-ren-men-lgsf8_asfrictdmsptsyfizykpon|Hanji 616 plus]] joins three different configurations. [[ShanyinPrincessLiuChuyu|刘楚玉]]'s thirty male attendants show imperial authority allocating intimate access to a princess. [[WuZetian|武则天]]'s favorites show access flowing toward a woman ruler, with [[XueHuaiyi|薛怀义]], [[ZhangYizhi|张易之]], and [[ZhangChangzong|张昌宗]] gaining or losing position as preference changes. [[PrincessTaiping|太平公主]] is then presented as trying to use the introduction of a favorite to redirect Wu Zetian's attachment and settle a grievance.

The concept does not reduce every relationship to cynical strategy. Its narrower claim is that intimacy at court cannot be separated from institutional asymmetry: rulers and royal households can grant access, office, protection, or violence, while favorites remain vulnerable when affection shifts or factions change.

## Key Claims

- Intimate access can operate as patronage and a route into political influence.
- Royal rank can allocate partners or attendants through command rather than ordinary courtship.
- Favorites can broker access for relatives and allies, multiplying intimate power into a network.
- Replacement risk makes favorite-derived authority fragile.
- Gender changes the moral language applied to court intimacy but does not remove its political function.
- Sensational court stories require source criticism because hostile memory often fuses sex, scandal, and regime condemnation.

- Strategic introduction: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-plus-pan-dian-shi-shang-de-hao-se-nv-ren-men-lgsf8_asfrictdmsptsyfizykpon|Hanji 616 plus]] says Princess Taiping introduced Zhang Yizhi to redirect Wu Zetian's attention away from Xue Huaiyi.
- Network extension: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-plus-pan-dian-shi-shang-de-hao-se-nv-ren-men-lgsf8_asfrictdmsptsyfizykpon|Hanji 616 plus]] says Zhang Yizhi then brought Zhang Changzong into the palace.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source is compressed popular history, and its most vivid causal details are not independently verified. Favorite status does not prove that all actions were sexually motivated, while allegations of female promiscuity or male-favorite manipulation may reflect hostile historiography. The concept therefore retains political structure while keeping individual motives and scandal narratives source-scoped.
