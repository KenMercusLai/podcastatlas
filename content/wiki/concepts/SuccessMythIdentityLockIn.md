# Success Myth Identity Lock-In / 成功神话身份锁定

## Definition
Success myth identity lock-in is the condition in which a belief about how success works becomes so central to self-respect, sacrifice, and family authority that revising the belief feels like invalidating one's entire life.

## Current Synthesis
[[DeathOfASalesman|《推销员之死》]] supplies the founding case. [[WillyLoman|Willy Loman]] believes popularity, connections, attractiveness, and confidence should produce material success, and he teaches that belief to his sons. When experience repeatedly contradicts it, he protects the belief because it explains who he is and why his sacrifices mattered.

The concept distinguishes lack of options from inability to recognize options without identity collapse. Offered work, family help, lower-status life, or admitted error can be materially viable while remaining psychologically intolerable. [[BiffLoman|Biff]] begins to loosen the lock by accepting ordinariness and testing preference against inherited aspiration.

## Key Claims
- A success belief becomes identity-locking when it justifies past sacrifice and present authority at once.
- Contradictory evidence can intensify defense when revision threatens a whole-life narrative rather than one prediction.
- Practical help may fail if accepting it is experienced as proof that the self-story was false.
- Parents can transmit the lock by making children evidence that the family belief will eventually be vindicated.
- Admitting ordinariness can be liberating when exceptional success has become a compulsory identity.
- Loosening the lock requires separating changed judgment from total condemnation of the past self.

## Evidence
- Organizing belief: [[vol-355-tuixiaoyuan-zhisi-naxie-shabusi-wode-weishenme-yizhi-zai-dawo-gkwriw4ols9xawr6vqtifmya]] identifies Willy's conviction that being liked and connected guarantees success.
- Refused alternatives: [[vol-355-tuixiaoyuan-zhisi-naxie-shabusi-wode-weishenme-yizhi-zai-dawo-gkwriw4ols9xawr6vqtifmya]] says he can accept offered work and family assistance but cannot bear what acceptance would mean about his life.
- Family transmission: [[vol-355-tuixiaoyuan-zhisi-naxie-shabusi-wode-weishenme-yizhi-zai-dawo-gkwriw4ols9xawr6vqtifmya]] contrasts Biff's movement toward self-recognition with Happy's decision to continue Willy's project.
- Fatal preservation: [[vol-355-tuixiaoyuan-zhisi-naxie-shabusi-wode-weishenme-yizhi-zai-dawo-gkwriw4ols9xawr6vqtifmya]] reads the insurance plan as an attempt to prove usefulness without abandoning the success creed.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Persistence in a belief is not automatically identity lock-in; evidence may be incomplete, and long-term projects often require endurance through temporary failure. The concept applies when belief defense repeatedly overrides available evidence and viable alternatives because revision is experienced as annihilation of identity. It is interpretive, not a diagnosis.

## What Changed
- Created the concept from Willy's refusal of materially available alternatives and Biff's partial escape from inherited exceptionality.
