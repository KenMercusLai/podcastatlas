# Feng Shui Vital-Energy Theory / 风水生气论

Feng shui vital-energy theory / 风水生气论 is the source's frame for kan-yu and fengshui in [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-jie-shangji-704051550]]. Through [[GuoPu|郭璞]] and [[Zangshu|《葬书》]], the episode presents site selection as a search for qi that is stored, protected, and benefited by wind, water, and landscape form.

The concept includes attribution caution. The episode names Guo Pu as a traditional fengshui ancestor while also noting that the received Zangshu may be a later work falsely attributed to him.

## Key Claims
- Fengshui in the source is a heaven-earth technique rather than only domestic decoration.
- Qi, burial, wind, water, and landscape enclosure make place morally and cosmologically active.
- The later four-symbol layout shows how directional animal imagery can organize spatial judgment.
- Textual attribution should remain source-scoped when dating is uncertain.
