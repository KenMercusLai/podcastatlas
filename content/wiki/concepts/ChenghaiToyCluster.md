# Chenghai Toy Cluster

Chenghai Toy Cluster is the local industry example in [[no-205-chaoshan-wangshi-qiaopi-jingji-tequ-he-meiyou-denglaide-shenzhen-qiji-989721046]]. The episode uses Chenghai, now part of [[Shantou]], to show how [[Chaoshan]]'s familiar trust networks and overseas trade channels could support small workshops and export-oriented toy production.

The concept is intentionally limited. It is evidence that the region could form manufacturing pockets, but the episode argues that pockets of workshop activity did not amount to a full [[MerchantToIndustrialCityUpgrade]]. The cluster therefore sits between [[DiasporaCapitalManufacturingClusters]] and [[DiasporaCapitalReturnLimits]]: hometown ties and coordination helped, while scale, public credit, and broader industrial depth remained constraints.

## Key Claims
- Familiar local trust can reduce coordination costs for small workshops and subcontracting.
- Export-oriented clusters can emerge from diaspora trade channels and local craft specialization.
- A cluster can be real and productive without transforming the whole city economy.
- Small-workshop success does not erase the effects of [[RegionalAdministrativeFragmentation]] or [[ExportTaxFraudCreditCrisis]].
