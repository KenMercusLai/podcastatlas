# Peace Infrastructure

## Definition
Peace infrastructure is the episode's idea that peace needs durable institutions, physical spaces, trust systems, education, dialogue channels, and transparent operating mechanisms rather than aspiration alone.

## Current Synthesis
In [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-97450677c47f502770248abb02f35793-163628914-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-97450677c47f502770248abb02f35793-163628914]], [[ShyalpaRinpoche]] uses the [[UniversalPeaceSanctuary]] to make peace practical. He describes halls for leaders, a museum, library, institute, ambassadors, clubs, stakeholder participation, funding, professional governance, and blockchain transparency. The concept remains aspirational in the source, but its useful wiki contribution is the shift from peace as sentiment to peace as infrastructure.

## Key Claims
- Peace is framed as something that must be built and maintained through institutions, not only preached.
- Physical spaces can support dialogue by giving leaders and communities a dedicated setting for trust-building.
- Peace-building requires seed money, capable teams, kind hearts, and shared ownership rather than a one-person project.
- Technology such as blockchain and AI is proposed as support for transparency, meaning, and human benefit.
- The source links public peace to individual "power over mind," making institutional peace and inner clarity mutually reinforcing.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source supplies a vision, not a detailed implementation plan or external audit. Claims about economic transformation, thousand-year durability, construction status, and technology use should remain source-scoped until other sources supply evidence.
