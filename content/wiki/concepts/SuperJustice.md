# Super Justice

Super Justice is [[BenjaminAlarie]]'s frame for using AI to relieve legal scarcity. In [[tech-20260805-0805-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260805-0805-mp-tech-pod-128]], he argues that legal reasoning and legal help could become more abundant, allowing institutions, frameworks, and policies to better support the rule of law.

The concept is optimistic but not automation-absolutist. Alarie says humans should remain at the core of legal systems, and the episode treats [[LegalAIVerificationAuditability]], [[HumanInTheLoopLegalAI]], and professional accountability as conditions for any credible access gain. Super Justice therefore differs from generic legal automation: the goal is more usable legal help without changing rights, weakening verification, or letting responsibility disappear into the tool.

## Key Claims
- Legal scarcity is a core access-to-justice problem because legal reasoning and help are costly, slow, and unevenly available.
- AI could make legal assistance more abundant if it is embedded in accountable institutions rather than treated as a stand-alone oracle.
- The frame requires [[PersonalizedLegalGuidance]] to explain and route people through law without giving different rights to different people.
- Cheapness is not enough; legal help must still be fair, verifiable, privacy-aware, and accountable.
