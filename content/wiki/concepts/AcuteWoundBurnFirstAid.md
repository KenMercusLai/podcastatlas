# Acute Wound and Burn First Aid / 急性伤口与烫伤急救

## Definition
Acute wound and burn first aid / 急性伤口与烫伤急救 is the household trauma boundary for separating superficial self-care from wounds, punctures, tendon signs, bleeding, contamination, and burns that need sustained cooling, protection, and medical evaluation.

## Current Synthesis
[[vol-222-aoye-xintiao-kuangbiao-shi-cusi-qianzhao-aed-hui-dian-cuo-huoren-dai-ni-bikai-zhiming-changshi-shijie-jijiu-ri-teji-lnemogq8gb9o3e2835jvtfx3emiy]] treats wound and burn care as simple but not casual. Minor superficial abrasions may fit iodine and dressings, but deep wounds, persistent bleeding, visible fat or tendon, contaminated punctures, movement loss, or burns with stuck clothing require more cautious routing.

The burn branch rejects folk applications such as toothpaste, soy sauce, and oil. The source's practical public action is sustained cooling with running water, avoiding high-pressure flow directly onto fragile tissue, and not tearing firmly stuck clothing from the burned area.

## Key Claims
- Superficial abrasions that stop bleeding quickly may be handled with simple cleaning and dressing.
- Wounds that are deep, gaping, visibly involve fat or tendon, or do not close naturally may need suturing and infection-risk management.
- Bleeding that does not stop after several minutes of pressure can signal deeper injury.
- Deep narrow punctures from nails, rusted objects, soil-contaminated objects, or similar sources raise tetanus-risk concern and need qualified care.
- Finger or toe movement loss after a small-looking wound can indicate tendon injury.
- Burns should be cooled long enough to remove heat, typically 15-30 minutes in the source's public account.
- Clothing stuck to burns should not be forcibly torn away; surrounding fabric may be cut if practical before care.

## Evidence
- Wound depth and bleeding: [[vol-222-aoye-xintiao-kuangbiao-shi-cusi-qianzhao-aed-hui-dian-cuo-huoren-dai-ni-bikai-zhiming-changshi-shijie-jijiu-ri-teji-lnemogq8gb9o3e2835jvtfx3emiy]] separates surface scratches from deep or non-closing wounds, persistent bleeding, infection risk, and possible suturing.
- Puncture and tendon warning signs: [[vol-222-aoye-xintiao-kuangbiao-shi-cusi-qianzhao-aed-hui-dian-cuo-huoren-dai-ni-bikai-zhiming-changshi-shijie-jijiu-ri-teji-lnemogq8gb9o3e2835jvtfx3emiy]] flags contaminated deep narrow wounds and small hand or foot wounds with lost motion.
- Burn cooling and clothing: [[vol-222-aoye-xintiao-kuangbiao-shi-cusi-qianzhao-aed-hui-dian-cuo-huoren-dai-ni-bikai-zhiming-changshi-shijie-jijiu-ri-teji-lnemogq8gb9o3e2835jvtfx3emiy]] recommends cooling with running water for a substantial duration and avoiding rough tearing of stuck clothing.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The page records podcast first-aid education, not a wound-care, burn-care, tetanus, surgery, or infection-management protocol. Burn size, depth, location, age, contamination, vaccination status, immune state, and function loss determine clinical urgency.

## What Changed
- VOL.222 creates a wound-and-burn first-aid concept that links everyday cuts, punctures, bleeding, tendon signs, tetanus risk, cooling, and folk-remedy avoidance.
