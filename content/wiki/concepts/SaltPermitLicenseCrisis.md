# Salt Permit License Crisis

Salt Permit License Crisis is the institutional failure described in [[no-211-huishang-wangshi-cong-qianshi-buxiu-dao-zuiniu-fengtou-1002275189]] when state-issued salt permits outgrew actual salt production and redemption capacity. In the source, the problem becomes acute in the Ming Lianghuai system, where accumulated old permits could no longer be cleanly honored.

The episode's key move is that crisis can be converted into license value. The gang/纲 registration process separated old and new permits, scheduled cleanup, and turned a formerly burdensome registry into a scarce operating qualification. Existing merchants who survived the crisis gained a barrier against new entrants.

This concept therefore links fiscal stress to monopoly formation. A permit is not just paper permission; when access is capped, transferable, and attached to a state monopoly, it can become a financial asset and a source of political exposure.
