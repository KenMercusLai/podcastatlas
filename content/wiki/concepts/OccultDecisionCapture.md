## Definition

Occult decision capture / 术数俘获式决策失控 is the failure mode where divination, omen-like objects, prophecy, or ritualized fraud overrides available political and military evidence under uncertainty, causing a leader to choose a worse course.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-690-jiaoyou-bushen-you-duo-kepa-kankan-tianrong-de-jingli-lpbms8pebzdo2ldiwho1cba81njk|Hanji 690]] provides two linked cases. [[TianRongYilingEasternHan|田戎]] has a concrete reason to submit after Qin Feng's defeat, but once [[XinChenEasternHan|辛臣]] contaminates the surrender channel, Tian Rong uses burned-shell divination to decide whether to submit. The cracked shell is read as inauspicious, and he rebels instead.

[[ZhangFengZhuoEasternHan|张丰]] gives the legitimacy-fraud version. A Daoist tells him he will become emperor and ties a stone to his arm in a multicolored bag as if it contains an imperial seal. The stone's emptiness is only exposed after capture, when the political damage has already happened.

The British cases broaden the mechanism beyond explicit fraud. [[RichardIIOfEngland|Richard II]] is presented as falling into “magical quietism,” allowing a providential self-image and interpreted signs to weaken pragmatic response. [[JohnDee]] retains genuine scholarship and practical usefulness, yet his desire for angelic counsel makes him accept a reported wife-sharing command. In both branches, the failure lies in the authority granted to an unverifiable interpretation rather than in symbolism or ritual alone.

The concept is not a claim about every historical divination practice. Its wiki function is narrower: in crisis settings, techniques and signs can become decision substitutes when evidence is frightening, ambiguous, socially compromised, or bound to a ruler's preferred identity.

## Key Claims

- Uncertainty makes delegated sign-reading more tempting when ordinary trust or strategy is already under strain.
- Divination can move a leader away from a prior evidence-based judgment if the result is treated as final authority.
- Fraudulent legitimacy objects can convert ambition into rebellion by imitating signs of mandate or imperial authorization.
- The damage often appears before the sign is disproved, because followers and principals act on it first.
- Flattering prophecy can capture judgment by making non-action or a preferred identity feel cosmically guaranteed.
- Occult capture differs from ritual legitimacy generally: the problem is not symbolism itself, but symbolism replacing reality testing.

## Evidence

- Tian Rong's reversal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-690-jiaoyou-bushen-you-duo-kepa-kankan-tianrong-de-jingli-lpbms8pebzdo2ldiwho1cba81njk|Hanji 690]] says Tian Rong wants to submit after Qin Feng's defeat, but after suspicion around Xin Chen he burns a turtle shell, reads the crack as inauspicious, and rebels.
- Zhang Feng's fake seal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-690-jiaoyou-bushen-you-duo-kepa-kankan-tianrong-de-jingli-lpbms8pebzdo2ldiwho1cba81njk|Hanji 690]] says a Daoist claims Zhang Feng will be emperor and uses a stone in a multicolored bag as supposed imperial-seal evidence.
- Disproof after capture: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-690-jiaoyou-bushen-you-duo-kepa-kankan-tianrong-de-jingli-lpbms8pebzdo2ldiwho1cba81njk|Hanji 690]] says the stone contains no seal, and Zhang Feng realizes only then that he was deceived.
- Magical quietism: [[174-merlin-magic-and-the-british-glt5141910845]] says Richard II's mystical world-emperor belief and interpretation of signs weakened pragmatic action.
- Mediated revelation: [[174-merlin-magic-and-the-british-glt5141910845]] says Dee accepted a purported angelic instruction reported by Edward Kelley despite grave doubts.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The evidence spans two interpretive podcast sources and does not settle the religious, cultural, or ritual meaning of divination across early China or Britain. It should not flatten all omen discourse, prophecy, astrology, or ritual into fraud. It applies when an omen, procedure, identity claim, or revelation overrides better-grounded strategic evidence or enables deception.

## What Changed

- Created the concept from Tian Rong's burned-shell decision and Zhang Feng's fake-seal rebellion in Hanji 690.
- Added magical quietism and mediated revelation as non-Chinese cases of identity-flattering or adviser-controlled capture.
