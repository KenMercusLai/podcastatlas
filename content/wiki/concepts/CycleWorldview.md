# Cycle Worldview / 周期世界观

## Definition

Cycle worldview is the belief that economic, social, market, and personal conditions move through recurring phases, so neither boom nor despair should be treated as permanent.

## Current Synthesis

[[176-niuyue-yinian-yige-beiguanzhuyizhe-de-huofa-725-shalong-shilu-lte-k8lw8e1txxw3nfxvo3wal8ah]] presents the worldview through [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]]: everything has cycles, which supports [[AssetAllocation]], diversification, behavioral friction, and anti-linear extrapolation. If cycles are real, no single asset, country, currency, industry, or life state should be assumed permanently dominant.

The Zhou Jintao version in [[ep94-chuanyue-zhoujintao-rensheng-facai-kao-kangbo-shouzhu-jiadi-kao-shao-cuo-lndimnd_sb98tfmzv-kukbbnbtg8]] says cycle awareness can explain era tailwinds and asset opportunities, but it becomes dangerous when ordinary investors turn it into date worship. The episode's correction is that catching a big wave matters less than preserving family resources through fewer large mistakes.

The worldview's danger is fatalism. Both sources resist the idea that cycles erase agency: one answers with present-tense work and learning, the other with capital-duration discipline, cash-flow protection, and preparation for the next opportunity.

## Key Claims

- Cycles make all-in extrapolation dangerous because good and bad states both change.
- Diversification fits a cycle worldview because no asset, country, currency, or industry stays permanently dominant.
- The worldview supports patience and cooldown discipline, but it should not become an excuse for passivity.
- Present-tense work is the antidote to cycle nihilism: action remains meaningful even if every condition eventually changes.
- Cycle opportunity and personal ability should be separated; a rising asset or industry does not prove the participant had superior skill.
- "人生发财靠康波" needs a household-risk companion: [[InvestmentRiskManagement]] preserves the ability to participate in later cycles.

## Evidence

- **Life and portfolio worldview:** [[176-niuyue-yinian-yige-beiguanzhuyizhe-de-huofa-725-shalong-shilu-lte-k8lw8e1txxw3nfxvo3wal8ah]] links cycle thinking to diversification, optionality, and present-tense creator work.
- **Zhou Jintao interpretation:** [[ep94-chuanyue-zhoujintao-rensheng-facai-kao-kangbo-shouzhu-jiadi-kao-shao-cuo-lndimnd_sb98tfmzv-kukbbnbtg8]] uses [[ZhouJintao]] and [[KondratievCycle]] to separate environment redounds from personal competence and to warn against exact-cycle trading.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

- Seeing cycles everywhere can become overfitting, fatalism, or vague pattern recognition if not tied to concrete mechanisms.
- Cycle awareness does not produce a portfolio until it is translated through goals, liquidity, and risk capacity.
