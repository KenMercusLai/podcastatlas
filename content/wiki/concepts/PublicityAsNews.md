# Publicity as News

## Definition
Publicity as news is the design of a real, visible, and narratable product demonstration that media outlets and audiences have reason to cover as an event rather than receive as an advertisement.

## Current Synthesis
[[HarryHoudini]] is the source's model: refine the underlying performance, create a challenge whose stakes are immediately understandable, stage it in public, and use emerging media to widen its reach. The approach earns attention by producing an event, but it is not a substitute for product quality. It also creates imitation pressure, making name recognition, secrets, legal protection, and the willingness to confront copycats part of the attention strategy.

## Key Claims
- A public demonstration becomes news when its stakes, difficulty, and outcome are legible without a long sales explanation.
- Technical preparation must precede spectacle because failed or hollow demonstrations can destroy rather than build trust.
- Challenges to police, institutions, or imitators can borrow existing authority and conflict to make a story more coverable.
- New media extend the life and geographic reach of an event beyond the people physically present.
- Publicity increases copycat risk, so distribution strategy and identity protection must develop together.
- Secrecy and formal intellectual-property protection can conflict when registration requires disclosure of the method.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Houdini's historical success does not show that spectacle is sufficient or transferable to every product. Newsworthiness depends on media incentives, public safety, novelty, trust, and cultural context. Provocation can become manipulation, create dangerous imitation, or overwhelm the product itself. The source is a brief retrospective and does not independently measure which publicity tactic contributed most to Houdini's fame.

## What Changed
- Established a craft-first model of earned attention through public demonstration.
- Added new-media adoption as an amplifier rather than the origin of newsworthiness.
- Made imitation and disclosure costs part of publicity strategy.
