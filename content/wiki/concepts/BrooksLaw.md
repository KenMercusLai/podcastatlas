# Brooks's Law

Brooks's Law appears in [[we-almost-had-a-smartphone-in-the-90s-why-did-it-fail]] as a warning that adding people to a late project can make it later. The episode uses it to interpret [[GeneralMagic]]'s large partner and talent base: more people, companies, and ideas increased coordination needs while the product remained unclear.

The concept's role in this wiki is project-management discipline. Extra capacity can help only when the work is decomposable, priorities are stable, and communication overhead does not outrun the added labor.

## Key Claims
- Adding people adds communication paths, onboarding load, and alignment work.
- The risk is larger when goals, customers, and interfaces are unresolved.
- Brooks's Law connects staffing decisions to [[ClearCustomerDefinition]] and [[ConstraintDrivenProductDiscipline]], not only to calendar math.
