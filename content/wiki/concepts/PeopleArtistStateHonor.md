# People's Artist State Honor

## Definition
People's Artist state honor is the source's frame for a Chinese state title that turns a performer into an official cultural symbol.

## Current Synthesis
In the Guo Lanying segment, the honor matters because it formalizes a life already remembered through revolutionary opera and patriotic song. The title compresses [[GuoLanying]]'s harsh apprenticeship, stage achievement, revolutionary identification, and mass audience recognition into a state-recognized national legacy.

## Key Claims
- State cultural honors can turn individual performers into official memory figures.
- The honor may validate public affection while also selecting which parts of a career become nationally legible.
- In Guo's case, the honor links voice, revolutionary identity, and patriotic song.

## Evidence
- Guo's title - [[out-numbered-ais-contentious-maths-milestone-6aa3ce622e8bb8424c920cfb]] says China gave Guo the People's Artist title in 2019.
- Cultural legacy - [[out-numbered-ais-contentious-maths-milestone-6aa3ce622e8bb8424c920cfb]] connects the honor to her status as a national treasure and to [[MyMotherlandSong|"My Motherland"]].

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This page records one source's use of the honor. It does not provide a full institutional history of the title or compare Guo with other honorees.
