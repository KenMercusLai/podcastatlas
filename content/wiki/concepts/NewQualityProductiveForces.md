# New Quality Productive Forces / 新质生产力

New quality productive forces is the source's long-term China technology and productivity bucket inside [[vol-115-quanqiu-hongguan-he-ziben-shichang-2025-zhanwang-duanqi-wenti-bu-jiejue-jiu-meiyou-zhongqi-he-changqi-le-lrfl2e5nuf7-ubq-shybqytjhuhx]]. [[Ricky]] uses it as one side of [[AHShare2025Barbell]]: assets tied to future technological breakthrough, industrial upgrade, and policy-favored productivity can have upside, but much of that value is priced through future probability rather than current cash flow.

The concept is therefore linked to both optimism and discipline. It complements [[ChinaDivergentTechnologyRoute]] and [[ChinaCorporateAntiInvolution]], but the episode's sequencing rule says these assets need a stronger short-term economy and repaired confidence before the long-term story can be carried by public-market buyers indefinitely.

## Key Claims
- New quality productive forces are treated as a long-term productivity and technology theme, not as an immediate earnings guarantee.
- In a barbell portfolio, the source frames this bucket as the high-upside side paired with [[DefensiveDividendAssets]].
- The investment risk is that many companies in the bucket are valued on breakthrough probability, policy direction, and future market size before cash flow is visible.
- Technology breakthrough may require tolerance for weak near-term shareholder return if firms prioritize research, capability, and avoiding external constraints.
- The theme becomes more investable if [[ShortTermDemandBeforeLongTermReform|short-term demand]] and corporate profit indicators improve enough to support risk appetite.
