# Online Game Social Organization

Online game social organization is the mechanism by which multiplayer games turn individual play into durable groups, status, conflict, and obligation. [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]] adds the concept through [[LegendOfMir|《传奇》]], especially guilds, PK, red-name risk, scarce equipment, and Sabak siege warfare.

The episode argues that Legend's draw was not only killing monsters or leveling up. It let ordinary users experience organized conflict, hierarchy, taxation-like privileges, collective honor, and virtual property, which made [[InternetCafePointCardDistribution]] and [[ChineseOnlineGameCommercialization]] more powerful than a simple paid-software model.
