## Definition

Immortality dependency and scarcity is the political-economic problem created when extended youth or life requires a continuing, interruptible input controlled by a limited place, supplier, or institution.

## Current Synthesis

[[BuSiYaoNovel|《不死药》]] separates biological benefit from control over its continuation. The medicine arrests aging, increases resilience, and repairs injury, but users become infertile and progressively lose intelligence if dosing stops. A user therefore does not simply possess immortality; the user enters a continuing relationship with whoever controls the plant, processing, transport, and future supply.

That relationship changes the distributional question. 骆之谦's plan to kill the islanders and monopolize production would make existing users governable through withdrawal while allowing the supplier to choose who receives health and time. If access is expensive or inherited, early beneficiaries can accumulate wealth, knowledge, and political influence across ordinary lifetimes, making an initial access gap self-reinforcing. Population limits, life rights, and compulsory endings then become institutional questions rather than private preferences alone.

## Key Claims

- A recurring treatment creates ongoing dependency even when each dose provides a real benefit.
- Withdrawal harms can turn supply control into coercive power over existing users.
- Geographic or technical concentration creates incentives for secrecy, monopoly, sabotage, and violence.
- Infertility may reduce one population pressure while imposing a major bodily cost that requires informed choice.
- Unequal early access can compound across time through health, capital, knowledge, and institutional influence.
- Individual willingness to accept risk does not resolve collective allocation, population, or political legitimacy.
- Destroying the only supply ends one monopoly while also abandoning dependent users and avoiding institutional resolution.

## Evidence

### Dependence and bodily cost

- [[ltte46cfjwcjc-gwcbkqb7zo7qh1]] reports lifelong dosing, cognitive collapse after interruption, infertility, enhanced healing, and continued vulnerability to fatal injury.

### Monopoly and coercion

- [[ltte46cfjwcjc-gwcbkqb7zo7qh1]] describes 骆之谦's attempt to kill the islanders, control the plant, and recruit a newly dependent 卫斯理 into commercial exploitation.

### Distribution and government

- [[ltte46cfjwcjc-gwcbkqb7zo7qh1]] extends the discussion through 《百年法》 to expensive access, accumulated advantage, population control, elections, and conflict over a mandatory end to life rights.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The mechanism comes from fictional works and podcast hypotheticals, not a demonstrated drug, demographic model, or policy proposal. The source does not specify production capacity, dose, storage, substitution, enforcement, heritability, migration, or the actual population effects of infertility. Scarcity need not produce one inevitable regime: public provision, distributed production, rationing, price control, legal rights, or new substitutes could alter the incentives. Conversely, eliminating the resource does not by itself protect people already dependent on it.

## What Changed

- Established the concept by separating the value of extended life from control over the continuing input that sustains it.
