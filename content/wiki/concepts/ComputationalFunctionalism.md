# Computational Functionalism

Computational functionalism is the position in [[i-robot-ai-and-consciousness-6a880840b0d5e24ea40c7015]] that what matters for consciousness is the computation being performed, not whether it runs in biological neurons or silicon. [[AlokJha]] presents it as the view under which future AI consciousness cannot be ruled out in principle.

The source contrasts this with biological views that treat living, messy bodily processes as potentially essential. That contrast makes computational functionalism a hinge between current LLM skepticism, [[GlobalWorkspaceTheory|workspace-like]] internal structure, and future [[OrganoidComputing|biological-computing]] hybrids.

## Key Claims
- If computation is sufficient for consciousness, silicon systems could in principle become conscious.
- If biology is necessary, current digital simulations may omit the decisive substrate.
- The source uses the view to keep future AI consciousness open without claiming that current [[Claude]], [[ChatGPT]], or [[Gemini]] are conscious.
- The position depends on what kind of consciousness is being discussed: [[PhenomenalAccessConsciousness|access-like function]] may be easier to compare than subjective experience.
