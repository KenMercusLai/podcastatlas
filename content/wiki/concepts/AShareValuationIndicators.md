# A-Share Valuation Indicators

A-share valuation indicators are the measurement cluster introduced by [[e145-shangzhongle-4000-dian-zhishang-de-xinli-anmo-lrk6eqfqkjnihzf5pimbek4kqm9e]] for judging market heat after A-shares moved above 4000. [[ZhangYizhen]] uses PE versus future returns, stock-bond relative value, deposit-to-market-cap ratios, three-year equity-fund annualized returns, log charts, confidence intervals, and moving-average deviation to shift attention from index points toward expected return, crowd behavior, and drawdown risk.

The core idea is not that one indicator can call a top. The source treats each indicator as a partial map: valuation says how much future return may already be priced in, fund-return history shows whether recent gains are being extrapolated, deposit ratios approximate available outside money, and log charts prevent point moves from being mistaken for equal percentage returns.

[[vol-115-quanqiu-hongguan-he-ziben-shichang-2025-zhanwang-duanqi-wenti-bu-jiejue-jiu-meiyou-zhongqi-he-changqi-le-lrfl2e5nuf7-ubq-shybqytjhuhx]] adds a macro-confirmation version. [[Ricky]] frames A/H repair as moving from valuation and sentiment to leading profit indicators and then to PPI plus enterprise earnings, making indicators useful for distinguishing a policy rally from a confirmed profit cycle.

## Key Claims
- PE and ROE-based indicators help separate valuation repair from a phase that requires actual profit recovery.
- Stock-bond relative value can reveal whether equity risk is still compensated compared with safer yield, but it is not a complete forecast.
- Deposit-to-market-cap ratios roughly compare outside cash with equity-market size, while remaining sensitive to IPOs, dividends, overseas flows, and deposit behavior.
- Three-year equity-fund returns work as a [[BehavioralInvestingBiases]] indicator because very high trailing gains can invite linear extrapolation.
- Log charts matter because 4000 to 5000 points is a different return than an earlier move of the same index-point size.
- Confidence intervals and deviation measures help describe historical extremes, but the market can still overshoot or break them.
- Indicators should inform [[PositionSizing]], exit discipline, and patience, not replace judgment.
- Vol.115 adds that PPI and company earnings are late but important confirmation variables after policy-driven valuation repair.
