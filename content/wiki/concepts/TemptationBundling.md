# Temptation Bundling

## Definition

Temptation bundling reserves an immediately enjoyable activity for simultaneous use with a worthwhile activity that is otherwise easy to avoid.

## Current Synthesis

In [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835|You, But Better]], [[KatyMilkman]] develops the method by listening to page-turning audiobooks only while exercising. The bundle raises the immediate appeal of the workout, can distract from discomfort, and limits the indulgence to a useful context. The same structure can pair entertainment with chores or a pleasant venue with a difficult meeting.

## Key Claims

- A delayed-benefit task becomes easier to start when it contains a reserved immediate reward.
- Exclusivity matters because consuming the temptation elsewhere weakens the bundle.
- The method can simultaneously increase a beneficial behavior and place a boundary around an indulgence.
- Bundles should be practical, proportionate, and compatible with safe performance of the primary task.
- Pairing can support repetition without proving that the underlying task becomes intrinsically enjoyable.

## Evidence

- Exercise case - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835]] describes Milkman's audiobook-only-at-the-gym practice.
- Transfer examples - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835]] suggests podcasts with chores, treats with studying, and a favorite restaurant with a difficult meeting.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source summary does not establish durability, effect size, or whether the method works across tasks and populations. Entertainment can impair attention or safety during driving, complex work, study, or exercise. A bundle may also become unavailable, lose novelty, or crowd out intrinsic motivation if it changes the meaning of an activity that was already valued.

## What Changed

- Established the method from Milkman's exercise example and its two-sided reward boundary.
