# Platform Affordance / 平台可供性

Platform affordance is the set of actions a platform makes possible, easy, visible, or socially meaningful. In [[164-suanfa-de-tuzi-dong-weishenme-ni-zong-zai-kanwan-xinwen-hou-huaxiang-yule-duitan-huangshengchun-jiaoshou-lo6ik8cj2njvu-cy2jdj-kl1jpuq]], [[HuangShengchun|黄圣淳]] uses comments, likes, reposts, and podcast-comment areas to show that interface features are not neutral switches. They define relationships among users, creators, platforms, and information.

The concept helps bridge product design and media effects. A podcast platform with comments creates a different creator-listener relation from a podcast platform without them. A social platform where comments are more visible than likes can turn conflict into [[AlgorithmicAngerEngagement]], while autoplay and infinite scroll can lower the friction that would otherwise let a user stop.

## Key Claims
- Platform features shape social relations before any single post is interpreted.
- The same content category behaves differently when comments, likes, reposts, recommendations, or autoplay are arranged differently.
- Affordances create the data that feeds [[AlgorithmicPredictionLoop]] and [[PlatformFeedbackLoop]].
- Users need [[AlgorithmicMediaLiteracy]] because design makes some reactions feel natural and others costly.
