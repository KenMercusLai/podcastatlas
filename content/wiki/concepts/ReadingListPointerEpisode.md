# Reading List Pointer Episode

Reading list pointer episode is the format exemplified by [[kaitianchuang-zhi-lu-jian-tuijian-ji-ben-shu-726703080]]. Instead of developing one book at length, the host uses a short "开天窗" slot to route listeners toward an off-platform exclusive episode, preview current reading, and mark possible future topics without making firm programming promises.

[[kaitianchuang-moyu-ganxie-chui-dali-553650149]] adds an earlier version that is less a formal list than a moving-status update with future leads. It previews [[AngelaCarter|Angela Carter]], [[HuangyeShangDeDashi|《荒野上的大师》]], [[SamuraiWilliam|《武士威廉》]], [[TuLiBuTuQi|《土里不吐气》]], and [[NewMigrantLiterature|新移民文学]], while its main purpose remains explaining why a normal episode is not ready.

[[yi-fen-laizi-bingchuang-shang-de-xiangguan-shudan-598284093]] adds a sickbed version of the format. The hosts do not have energy for a deep single-book episode, so they turn illness into a curated public-health and medicine list spanning [[ZhangWenhongShuoChuanran|《张文宏说传染》]], [[BingMeigui|《病玫瑰》]], [[ZhongshijiDeShenti|《中世纪的身体》]], [[PaleRiderBook|《苍白的骑士》]], [[DongbeiBoyi|《东北博弈》]], [[PlagueNights|《瘟疫之夜》]], [[ShixinPublicHealth|《失信：公共卫生体系的崩溃》]], and [[DakayiKexin|《打开一颗心》]].

## Key Claims
- The format is useful for the wiki because it records leads, not settled arguments.
- Book pages created from this kind of source should stay thin and source-scoped until fuller discussions are ingested.
- The format still matters: it shows how [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] builds continuity among reading life, platform routing, future episodes, and informal audience relationship.
- A pointer episode can be mixed with a production-status update; the wiki should separate confirmed source claims from "maybe later" programming signals.
- It is adjacent to [[ReadingAsLifeExperience]] because the host's reading life, mood, schedule, and curiosity shape the episode as much as the books' formal arguments.
- A sickbed pointer episode can also become a public-health map when personal illness is used to route listeners toward disease history, system memory, and medical uncertainty rather than only comfort reading.
