# Counterinsurgency Legitimacy Trap

Counterinsurgency legitimacy trap is the pattern where a security action may produce immediate operational gains while damaging the political legitimacy needed to isolate armed groups. In [[699-the-troubles-return-of-the-ira-part-3-glt9519811642]], the [[FallsCurfew]] finds weapons, but the curfew, searches, arrests, and alleged soldier misconduct damage Catholic trust in the [[BritishArmy]] and help the [[ProvisionalIRA]] recruit.

The same pattern sharpens through [[OperationDemetrius]]. [[InternmentWithoutTrial]] is meant to break republican violence, but poor intelligence, all-Catholic arrests, riots, displacement, deaths, [[LongKesh]], and the [[FiveTechniques]] make British policy look partisan and repressive. The concept therefore extends [[StatePolicingLegitimacyCrisis]] from policing into military and detention policy.

## Key Claims
- Tactical success can become strategic failure when the target community experiences the operation as collective punishment.
- Poor intelligence and uneven application make coercive policy appear sectarian even when leaders claim security necessity.
- Abuse allegations and detention practices can supply an armed group with a stronger recruitment story than propaganda alone.
