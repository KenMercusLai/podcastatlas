# Military Presence Theater / 军力声势表演

Military presence theater / 军力声势表演 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-947-dongzhuo-shoufu-lvbu-kaiqi-chengba-zhilu-lvr-24l6j8f6zvpfcmqgknmgywa1|Hanji 947]] as [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s first Luoyang force-magnification tactic. The source says Dong Zhuo initially has only about three thousand infantry and cavalry, so he sends soldiers out secretly at night and has them reenter by day in a visible way, making [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] observers believe fresh reinforcements keep arriving.

The pattern differs from ordinary battlefield deception. It does not primarily lure an enemy into a specific tactical position; it changes the perceived size, momentum, and inevitability of an armed presence inside a political capital. In Hanji 947, that perception buys time for actual consolidation: He-family troops come over, [[LyuBu|吕布]] kills [[DingYuanLateHan|丁原]], and Dong Zhuo can convert apparent force into [[CourtOfficeCapture|朝廷开府任官失序]].

The concept sits beside [[BattlefieldInformationControl|战场信息控制]] and [[XingshiqingStrategicAssessment|行势情战略评估]]. Battlefield information control names deception across a military contest; xingshiqing assessment names the need to read the real condition behind appearances. Military presence theater names the governing-side act of making a thin force look like a thickening fact before opponents such as [[YuanShao|袁绍]] decide whether to strike.

## Key Claims

- A small force can gain coercive value by making observers believe reinforcements are repeatedly arriving.
- Presence theater is most useful in a power vacuum where other actors are deciding whether resistance is still practical.
- The tactic works only if perception quickly becomes material control through defections, appointments, or troop absorption.
- The same staged momentum can expose an opponent's assessment failure when a narrow attack window is allowed to close.
- Military presence theater is not stable legitimacy; it is a bridge from fragile arrival to the harder forms of control tracked by [[CourtOfficeCapture|朝廷开府任官失序]] and [[CoercedTalentLegitimacy|胁迫征士合法性]].
