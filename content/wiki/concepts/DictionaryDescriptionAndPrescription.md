# Dictionary Description and Prescription / 词典的描述与规范

## Definition

Dictionary description and prescription is the lexicographical tension between recording how a linguistic community actually uses words and directing how that community ought to use them.

## Current Synthesis

The episode presents older public dictionaries as strongly normative educational tools and modern professional consensus as more descriptive. That shift does not eliminate standards. Readers consult dictionaries for guidance, and editors still choose evidence, decide when a usage is established, explain why a form remains disputed, and balance current frequency against expected durability.

The best current formulation is therefore a constrained descriptive practice. A dictionary should not pretend that stable language can be imposed from above, but neither can it mechanically reproduce every novel or frequent expression. Print-era page limits made this selectivity visible through periodic addition and deletion; the internet removes physical scarcity while leaving evidence quality, editorial caution, and [[LanguagePrecision]] unresolved.

## Key Claims

- Language changes continuously, so a dictionary cannot permanently fix correct usage.
- Description is itself selective because editors choose corpora, evidence thresholds, labels, examples, and inclusion timing.
- Readers’ demand for guidance gives even descriptive dictionaries a residual normative role.
- Contested usage is better explained with context and qualification than reduced to a simple right-or-wrong verdict.
- Shared standards remain necessary for communication even though total standardization and complete neutrality are impossible.
- Digital abundance removes page limits but not the need for editorial judgment, durability, and accuracy.

## Evidence

- Historical shift: [[306-cidian-you-shenme-yong-lj-xsj-bvpmo0imc3qnv8njlgzso|306-词典有什么用？]] locates a broad movement from eighteenth- through mid-twentieth-century prescription toward modern descriptive lexicography.
- Residual guidance: [[306-cidian-you-shenme-yong-lj-xsj-bvpmo0imc3qnv8njlgzso|306-词典有什么用？]] notes that people still consult dictionaries to learn what to say or write and uses *literally* as an example of recording a common disputed usage with explanation.
- Editorial selection: [[306-cidian-you-shenme-yong-lj-xsj-bvpmo0imc3qnv8njlgzso|306-词典有什么用？]] contrasts periodic print revision and deletion with the effectively unbounded online dictionary.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The prescriptive–descriptive history is supplied by one popular podcast rather than a comparative study of dictionaries, languages, and editorial schools. Descriptive evidence can be incomplete or institutionally biased, while prescription can support intelligibility without making one prestige dialect morally superior. The episode’s Chinese pronunciation examples are explicitly outside the host’s specialist knowledge and should not establish a general theory of Chinese dictionary revision.

## What Changed

- Created a constrained-descriptivist synthesis in which usage evidence guides entries but does not remove editorial judgment or public standards.
- Separated digital capacity from editorial adequacy: unlimited space does not make inclusion automatic or neutral.
