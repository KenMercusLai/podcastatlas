## Definition

稀缺情境下的伦理分诊 / Ethical triage under scarcity is the decision pattern where a person or institution must choose among human claims when capacity is too limited to satisfy all of them, forcing comparison across life, talent, fairness, utility, weakness, relationship, and institutional mission.

## Current Synthesis

The episode uses deliberately harsh dilemmas to expose hidden values behind "势利." In the human-survival thought experiment, participants debate whether to choose randomly, preserve diversity, prefer young people, or include scientists and skilled people because the explicit goal is civilizational continuity. The example turns age, sex ratio, knowledge, fertility, and labor capacity into morally charged selection criteria.

The lifeboat and school-admission problems make the same structure more personal. Choosing between children with different cognitive capacities pushes intelligence snobbery to an extreme boundary; the panel repeatedly returns to randomization because every substantive criterion damages some value. The school case pits one gifted poor student against a mediocre donor-backed student whose family's laboratory gift could benefit thousands.

The concept's current synthesis is that scarcity can make value ranking unavoidable without making it morally clean. The source treats randomization, fairness, utility, weakness protection, and personal rescue schemes as partial repairs rather than complete solutions.

## Key Claims

- Scarcity exposes values that ordinary abundance lets people leave implicit.
- Random choice can protect equal dignity when available criteria feel morally contaminating.
- Utility arguments gain force when a decision affects many future beneficiaries, as in the donated laboratory case.
- Fairness arguments resist converting institutional access into purchasable advantage.
- Personal conscience repairs, such as privately helping the excluded person, may reduce harm but do not erase the original institutional tradeoff.
- The source keeps these dilemmas unresolved, treating discomfort as evidence rather than failure.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source is not a formal ethics text and does not distinguish all philosophical schools involved. It uses improvised panel responses to reveal practical moral conflict, so the concept should remain source-bounded until other evidence adds systematic ethical analysis.
