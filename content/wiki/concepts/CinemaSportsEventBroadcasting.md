# Cinema Sports Event Broadcasting / 影院体育赛事直播

## Definition
Cinema sports event broadcasting is the use of movie theaters as licensed live-viewing venues for football, tennis, esports, and other events, turning installed screens, sound systems, seating, and social atmosphere into revenue outside ordinary film exhibition.

## Current Synthesis
The source presents sports broadcasting as a demand-smoothing response to weak cinema recovery rather than a new technical invention. Chinese theaters have experimented with it for nearly two decades, but closer cooperation between rights-holding platforms and cinema chains became more practical after the 2020 copyright-law revision. The model can raise venue utilization and create collective viewing value, but it remains dependent on rights, scheduling, audience density, and economics that the episode does not quantify.

## Key Claims
- Cinemas can reuse fixed exhibition assets during periods when film supply or attendance is weak.
- Live sport adds communal uncertainty and participation that home viewing does not reproduce exactly.
- Rights clearance and platform-chain cooperation are operating prerequisites, not peripheral legal details.
- Event programming diversifies cinema revenue but does not by itself repair weak film demand.

## Evidence
- Venue adoption: [[qianyu-jia-yingyuan-zhibo-tiyu-saishi-shangbannian-guonei-jinshi-xiaofeiliang-xiadie-sancheng-gkwridooqlkafaplwtreagx]] reports that more than one thousand cinemas had tried live football, tennis, esports, or related events.
- Industry pressure: [[qianyu-jia-yingyuan-zhibo-tiyu-saishi-shangbannian-guonei-jinshi-xiaofeiliang-xiadie-sancheng-gkwridooqlkafaplwtreagx]] says box office since 2023 had not returned to 2017-2019 levels and that 740 cinemas closed in the prior year.
- Rights infrastructure: [[qianyu-jia-yingyuan-zhibo-tiyu-saishi-shangbannian-guonei-jinshi-xiaofeiliang-xiadie-sancheng-gkwridooqlkafaplwtreagx]] links closer rights-holder and cinema-chain cooperation to the 2020 copyright-law revision.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not provide attendance, ticket price, concession revenue, rights cost, or repeat-use data. A national participation count therefore shows experimentation, not proven unit economics or a substitute for successful films.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to separate live-event venue reuse from film-format premiumization.
