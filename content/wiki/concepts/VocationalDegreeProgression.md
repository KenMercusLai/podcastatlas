# Vocational Degree Progression / 职业教育升学通道

Vocational degree progression / 职业教育升学通道 is the pathway that lets vocational students continue from junior-college-level study toward bachelor's programs, vocational undergraduate degrees, public-sector exams, or other credential routes. [[ep241-xiaoqi-hezuo-shi-xinyidai-de-tiefanwan-ma-lnmpsbvoqbexeardxyx88hywufig]] links it to the policy idea of a "职业立交桥" that breaks a rigid divide between academic and vocational tracks.

The source treats this as both opportunity and tension. More progression can reduce stigma and give students mobility, but it also means many students in [[VocationalEducation|职业教育]] may treat employment-oriented [[SchoolEnterpriseCooperation|校企合作]] as a temporary stage rather than a final work path.

## Key Claims
- Further-study routes give vocational students more agency and reduce the lock-in of early educational sorting.
- The same routes can weaken enterprise cooperation when most students prioritize exams over work readiness.
- Vocational undergraduate upgrading changes student expectations, especially in fields such as elder care where students may pursue civil-service or compiled-position exams.
- Degree progression should not erase [[ProjectBasedVocationalLearning|project-based skill confidence]] or [[CareerCognitionEducation|career cognition]].
