# Foundational Immune Health Framework

## Definition
Foundational immune health framework is the episode's use of nutrition, exercise, water, sunlight, temperance, air, rest, and trust as background conditions that may shape resilience before disease-specific interventions are considered.

## Current Synthesis
[[RogerSeheult]] presents the NEW START mnemonic as a whole-person checklist rather than a promise to prevent infection. Minimally processed nutrition, moderate movement, hydration, daylight, clean air, sleep, restraint around harmful exposures, and supportive community can influence metabolic, respiratory, circadian, and psychological conditions in which immune responses occur.

The useful synthesis is terrain plus exposure, not terrain instead of exposure. Baseline health may affect risk and recovery, but pathogens, dose of exposure, vaccination, protective equipment, diagnosis, and treatment remain independently important. Trust can mean social, spiritual, or community support, but spiritual discussion should be invited rather than imposed.

## Key Claims
- Immune resilience is shaped partly by everyday sleep, nutrition, movement, hydration, light, and air conditions.
- Moderate exercise is distinguished from excessive training that may increase short-term vulnerability.
- Water matters through hydration, heat transfer, sweating, kidney function, and respiratory losses, not as a cure by itself.
- Temperance includes avoiding smoking, vaping, and other unnecessary lung exposures.
- Rest includes both adequate sleep and a strong contrast between brighter days and darker nights.
- Trust and community may support coping and recovery but are optional, culturally situated components.
- Foundational habits complement rather than replace pathogen-specific prevention and medical care.

## Evidence
- Framework: [[how-to-enhance-your-immune-system-dr-roger-seheult-scim4509990462]] presents nutrition, exercise, water, sunlight, temperance, air, rest, and trust as NEW START.
- Exercise and recovery: [[how-to-enhance-your-immune-system-dr-roger-seheult-scim4509990462]] favors mild-to-moderate exercise while warning that excessive athletic load can create vulnerability.
- Air and temperance: [[how-to-enhance-your-immune-system-dr-roger-seheult-scim4509990462]] advises against smoking and vaping and treats fresh air as part of respiratory health.
- Trust boundary: [[how-to-enhance-your-immune-system-dr-roger-seheult-scim4509990462]] connects faith, gratitude, forgiveness, and community with coping while emphasizing permission in clinical settings.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The framework bundles interventions with very different evidence bases and should not be read as a validated package or an "immune boost." Severe infection, immunocompromise, dehydration, cardiopulmonary symptoms, and persistent post-infection symptoms require condition-specific evaluation. Spiritual practices and community support may be meaningful for some people and unwelcome or insufficient for others.

## What Changed
- Created an umbrella for the episode's foundational health pillars.
- Made explicit that baseline resilience and exposure-specific protection are complementary.
- Preserved consent and evidence boundaries around spiritual and community support.
