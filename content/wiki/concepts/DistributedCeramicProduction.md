# Distributed Ceramic Production / 分布式瓷业生产

Distributed Ceramic Production / 分布式瓷业生产 is the episode's account of how traditional [[Jingdezhen]] operated without being a single modern factory. In [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]], clay preparation, throwing, trimming, painting, firing, packing, selling, fuel, transport, and kiln control were split across many specialized actors.

The concept makes [[ProcessKnowledge]] concrete. Saggar use, kiln loading, temperature, atmosphere, fuel quality, and the judgment of kiln masters could decide whether wares survived firing, while low yields on difficult forms turned craft into risk management.
