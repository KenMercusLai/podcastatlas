# Fatty Liver Cause-Directed Management

## Definition
Fatty liver cause-directed management is the source's framework for treating a fatty-liver finding as the start of a causal investigation rather than as either a harmless label or an inevitable catastrophe.

## Current Synthesis
The episode describes a possible progression from simple fat accumulation through inflammation, fibrosis, cirrhosis, and more serious disease, but its practical emphasis is upstream: identify relevant alcohol, food, activity, weight, medicine, supplement, and nutritional-balance factors. Lifestyle change is the first broad intervention in the source, with medication and monitoring reserved for clinician-guided use when warranted.

## Key Claims
- Simple fat accumulation does not equal end-stage disease, but it can become more serious when causes persist.
- Cause review should include alcohol, energy-dense food and drink, activity, body weight, medicines, supplements, and dietary balance.
- Vegetarian eating is not automatically protective if the overall diet is imbalanced; the claim is about nutrient structure, not meat consumption alone.
- Sustainable food and activity changes are more central than “liver-protection” products.
- Medication, treatment duration, and monitoring require clinician judgment.

## Evidence
- Progression and cause review: [[vol-156-aigan-hugan-diyibu-ni-jiu-zoucuo-le-chenmo-de-renti-huagongchang-zijiu-zhinan-lpi_sy3oluvwt46bgdgg81dzs2qb]] describes simple fatty liver, steatohepatitis, fibrosis, and cirrhosis while asking why fat accumulated in the first place.
- Lifestyle and treatment boundary: [[vol-156-aigan-hugan-diyibu-ni-jiu-zoucuo-le-chenmo-de-renti-huagongchang-zijiu-zhinan-lpi_sy3oluvwt46bgdgg81dzs2qb]] prioritizes habit change, exercise, and diet structure before clinician-guided medication when necessary.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source gives broad examples rather than a complete differential diagnosis. Its progression windows, vegetarian-diet mechanism, exercise-risk mechanism, and treatment claims are not independently validated here. Individual fatty-liver causes, fibrosis risk, exercise needs, and medication decisions require qualified assessment.

## What Changed
- Initial synthesis separates a fatty-liver finding from both complacency and inevitability.
- Cause review and sustained habit change become the organizing management frame.
