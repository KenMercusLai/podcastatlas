# Fatty Liver Cause-Directed Management

## Definition
Fatty liver cause-directed management is the source's framework for treating a fatty-liver finding as the start of a causal investigation rather than as either a harmless label or an inevitable catastrophe.

## Current Synthesis
The sources describe a spectrum from mild or moderate fat accumulation that may improve through cause-directed change to inflammation, fibrosis, cirrhosis, and more serious disease. Their practical emphasis is upstream: identify relevant alcohol, energy intake, carbohydrate pattern, activity, body weight, medicines, supplements, and nutritional-balance factors rather than treating the imaging label itself. Lifestyle change is the first broad intervention, while severity assessment, medication, cancer-risk interpretation, and monitoring remain clinician-guided.

## Key Claims
- Simple fat accumulation does not equal end-stage disease, but it can become more serious when causes persist.
- Cause review should include alcohol, energy-dense food and drink, activity, body weight, medicines, supplements, and dietary balance.
- Dietary review should include total energy and carbohydrate pattern rather than focusing on visible dietary fat alone.
- Vegetarian eating is not automatically protective if the overall diet is imbalanced; the claim is about nutrient structure, not meat consumption alone.
- Sustainable food and activity changes are more central than “liver-protection” products.
- Severity, fibrosis, cancer risk, medication, treatment duration, and monitoring require clinician judgment.

## Evidence
- Progression and cause review: [[vol-156-aigan-hugan-diyibu-ni-jiu-zoucuo-le-chenmo-de-renti-huagongchang-zijiu-zhinan-lpi_sy3oluvwt46bgdgg81dzs2qb]] describes simple fatty liver, steatohepatitis, fibrosis, and cirrhosis while asking why fat accumulated in the first place.
- Food, activity, and reversibility: [[vol-22-zhongliu-gandan-waike-guanyu-ganyan-ganyinghua-he-ganai-women-yinggai-zhidao-zhexie-shi-lmmkgp8rpb2mvtk7slmtatq_jheg]] describes milder fatty liver as often reversible, emphasizes carbohydrate as well as fat intake, and summarizes management as eating and moving differently.
- Lifestyle and treatment boundary: [[vol-156-aigan-hugan-diyibu-ni-jiu-zoucuo-le-chenmo-de-renti-huagongchang-zijiu-zhinan-lpi_sy3oluvwt46bgdgg81dzs2qb]] prioritizes habit change, exercise, and diet structure before clinician-guided medication when necessary; [[vol-22-zhongliu-gandan-waike-guanyu-ganyan-ganyinghua-he-ganai-women-yinggai-zhidao-zhexie-shi-lmmkgp8rpb2mvtk7slmtatq_jheg]] adds that more advanced fatty liver may change cancer-risk and surveillance conversations.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The sources give broad examples rather than a complete differential diagnosis. Their progression windows, reversibility and malignancy claims, vegetarian-diet mechanism, carbohydrate framing, exercise-risk mechanism, and treatment claims are not independently validated here. Individual fatty-liver causes, fibrosis and cancer risk, exercise needs, surveillance, and medication decisions require qualified assessment.

## What Changed
- Added the distinction between potentially reversible milder disease and advanced disease requiring risk assessment.
- Expanded food review beyond visible fat to total energy, carbohydrates, and nutritional balance.
