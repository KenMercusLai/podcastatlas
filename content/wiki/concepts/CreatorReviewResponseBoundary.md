# Creator Review Response Boundary / 创作者评价回应边界

## Definition
Creator review response boundary / 创作者评价回应边界 is the source's distinction between ordinary ratings or criticism, review-system noise, malicious attacks, public rebuttal, and self-protection when online comments create reputational, platform, or physical risk.

## Current Synthesis
[[lir8w53xewcmvbqrb1x1osairafg-lir8w53xewcmvbqrb1x1osairafg]] begins the review discussion with everyday service ratings: [[ShiLifen|史礼芬]] is inclined to reward effort when a driver or delivery worker asks politely, [[LinJianxinStandup|林简欣]] dislikes being directly asked, and [[LuoYonghao|罗永浩]] says service quality still matters because inflated good reviews add noise to a public scoring system.

The creator half raises the stakes. [[CaoGuoStandup|曹国]] says he notices and may reply to hostile comments; Luo treats some public rebuttals as legitimate when a claim has factual reach; Lin describes being attacked and doxxed after hometown-related material; Shi says feedback that threatens account survival, safety, or local misunderstanding can change how he makes videos. The boundary is therefore not "ignore all criticism" or "fight everyone," but sort feedback by truth, harm, platform affordance, and risk.

## Key Claims
- Good reviews can reward effort, but they can also pollute a scoring system when disconnected from service quality.
- Review interpretation is selective: bad reviews may deter one person while attracting another if the complaint signals desired context.
- Creators can be psychologically affected by reviews even when they understand that comments are partial or performative.
- Public rebuttal is treated as more justified when criticism distorts facts and has public reach.
- Doxxing, local backlash, account threats, and safety risks move criticism from evaluation into self-protection.
- Platform affordances matter because some spaces make reply, escalation, or public counter-speech easier than others.

- Safety escalation - [[lir8w53xewcmvbqrb1x1osairafg-lir8w53xewcmvbqrb1x1osairafg]] has Lin Jianxin describe doxxing after regional material and Shi Lifen describe changing style to avoid local or account-threatening backlash.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept is based on creator anecdotes and service-review discussion, not platform policy research. It should not be treated as legal advice about defamation, privacy, harassment, or consumer-rating compliance.
