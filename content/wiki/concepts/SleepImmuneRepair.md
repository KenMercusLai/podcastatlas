# Sleep Immune Repair

## Definition
Sleep immune repair is the source's frame for sleep as a period when immune cells redistribute, tissues are cleaned and repaired, and vulnerability to illness can change.

## Current Synthesis
The episode treats sleep as active immune work rather than passive shutdown. [[MaxKrummel]] says immune cells appear to return to bone marrow at night, while neutrophils populate tissues and may support repair. The discussion links that movement to immune resetting, tissue cleanup, lymphatic clearance, and the everyday observation that poor sleep can raise illness vulnerability.

This concept extends the wiki's broader [[SleepAsDailyHealthAccount]] without rewriting that legacy page. Here the focus is specifically immune repair: sleep is not only attention, mood, and metabolic recovery, but also a timing state in which immune-cell location and tissue maintenance may change.

## Key Claims
- Sleep is presented as a biological state with immune-system consequences.
- Some immune cells may return to bone marrow at night.
- Neutrophils and other immune actors may populate tissues during sleep-linked repair processes.
- Tissue cleanup, repair, and lymphatic clearance are part of the episode's sleep-immunity frame.
- Poor sleep is linked to increased vulnerability to illness in the source discussion.
- The source does not convert sleep-immunity links into a precise individualized protocol.

## Evidence
- Night immune-cell movement: [[how-your-immune-system-works-how-to-improve-it-dr-max-krummel-scim7009523546]] says immune cells appear to return to bone marrow at night.
- Tissue repair: [[how-your-immune-system-works-how-to-improve-it-dr-max-krummel-scim7009523546]] links neutrophils in tissues with repair and cleanup processes.
- Practical health boundary: [[how-your-immune-system-works-how-to-improve-it-dr-max-krummel-scim7009523546]] connects sleep disruption to illness vulnerability while keeping mechanisms explanatory.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not specify a validated sleep prescription for immune optimization. It also does not settle how sleep duration, continuity, circadian timing, illness state, or individual baseline should change recommendations.
