# Long-Horizon Work Evaluation / 长时间尺度工作评估

Long-horizon work evaluation / 长时间尺度工作评估 is the closing frame in [[ep244-jizhe-yanzhong-de-hao-gongzuo-shenme-yang-lk8iz9cllzx2jou5-sw9yt2ogpyb]], attributed to [[YuanChanggeng|袁长庚]]'s time-dimension view. Instead of judging a job only through the current boss, conflict, or frustration, the episode asks how work fits a long life process.

The concept reframes "good work" as an ongoing relation to time. If a person spends a third or more of life working, then pay, dignity, body, family, curiosity, and meaning have to be reevaluated across changing life stages rather than settled once at career entry.

## Key Claims
- A single workplace conflict should be interpreted inside a longer life pattern, not treated as the whole answer.
- Long-horizon evaluation keeps practical needs visible while still asking whether the work is wanted or needed.
- The frame supports rebuilding a better relation to work rather than only finding a perfect job category.
- Time scale matters because family, health, curiosity, stamina, and risk tolerance change over a working life.
