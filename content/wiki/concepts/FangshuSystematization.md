# Fangshu Systematization / 方术系统化

Fangshu systematization / 方术系统化 names the process in [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-jie-shangji-704051550]] by which scattered techniques of omens, immortality, healing, ghost control, alchemy, talismans, pacing rituals, breathing, divination, and site selection become more organized religious and textual systems. The episode treats Daoist traditions as the major integrator of earlier fangshi material, while also showing Confucian and Buddhist crossings.

The concept does not mean the practices became modern science or one unified doctrine. It means the practices gained lineages, texts, moral claims, ritual sequences, and cosmological explanations that made them transmissible and authoritative.

## Key Claims
- Fangshu becomes durable when technique, text, master lineage, and cosmic explanation reinforce one another.
- Systematization can make supernatural practice more politically and socially powerful.
- Daoist, Confucian, and Buddhist elements can share techniques without becoming identical.
- The source keeps individual miracle stories source-scoped while using them to track institutionalization.
