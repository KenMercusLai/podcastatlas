# Fangshu Systematization / 方术系统化

Fangshu systematization / 方术系统化 names the process in the two-part [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] episode 87 branch by which scattered techniques of omens, divination, soul work, immortality, healing, ghost control, alchemy, talismans, pacing rituals, breathing, and site selection become more organized religious and textual systems.

[[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-wei-wan-daixu-704051997]] supplies the pre-systematic layer: [[OracleBoneDivination|甲骨占卜]], [[ZhouyiDivination|《周易》占卜]], [[AncientChineseSoulSummoning|招魂]], [[ChineseAstrologicalPolitics|星占]], dream interpretation, [[YinYangFivePhasesPoliticalTheory|阴阳五行]], early immortal belief, Qin-Han imperial求仙, [[Huainanzi|《淮南子》]], and [[WuguPoliticalPanic|巫蛊政治恐慌]].

[[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-jie-shangji-704051550]] then shows the Han-to-Wei-Jin continuation, where Daoist traditions become a major integrator of earlier fangshi material while Confucian and Buddhist crossings remain active.

The concept does not mean the practices became modern science or one unified doctrine. It means the practices gained lineages, texts, moral claims, ritual sequences, and cosmological explanations that made them transmissible and authoritative.

## Key Claims
- Fangshu becomes durable when technique, text, master lineage, and cosmic explanation reinforce one another.
- Systematization can make supernatural practice more politically and socially powerful.
- Daoist, Confucian, and Buddhist elements can share techniques without becoming identical.
- The source keeps individual miracle stories source-scoped while using them to track institutionalization.
- The first part shows why systematization had so much raw material: court divination, soul ritual, astrology, dream interpretation, five-phase legitimacy, immortality seeking, alchemy, and curse panic were already available before later Daoist synthesis.
