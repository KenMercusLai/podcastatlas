## Definition

Female Marriage Agency Across Periods / 女性婚姻能动性的历史变动 is the source-scoped proposition that widow remarriage, female-initiated divorce, and the moral meaning assigned to women's intimate choices vary across political, legal, material, and intellectual settings rather than following a simple path of continuous improvement.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-plus-pan-dian-shi-shang-de-hao-se-nv-ren-men-lgsf8_asfrictdmsptsyfizykpon|Hanji 616 plus]] contrasts an episode-level picture of relatively tolerated Han widow remarriage and female-initiated divorce with stronger Song-Ming chastity pressure associated with Cheng-Zhu moral orthodoxy. [[PingyangPrincessWesternHan|平阳公主]]'s remarriage to [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]] is the clearest example, while [[JingwuPrincessWesternHan|靖武公主]] supplies a more complicated elite household case.

The useful claim is historical variation, not a blanket verdict that all Han women were free or that all later women were uniformly trapped. Status, money, family backing, local practice, law, and political rank distribute agency unevenly inside every period. Royal remarriage establishes possibility at the top; it does not measure ordinary women's exit costs.

## Key Claims

- Widow chastity and remarriage stigma are historically variable rather than timeless constants.
- Female-initiated divorce in the episode complicates a one-directional progress story about women's marital freedom.
- Rank and resources alter the cost of remarriage and intimate choice.
- Elite examples demonstrate institutional possibility but cannot stand in for all women.
- Later moral doctrine can narrow legitimate choices even when a society is chronologically “later.”

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

One popular-history podcast cannot establish population-wide legal practice across centuries. The source does not distinguish statute from custom, elite from commoner, region from empire, or widow remarriage from free partner choice. Its Cheng-Zhu explanation may also compress the multiple institutions behind later chastity pressure. The current judgment therefore preserves variation and stratification while withholding broad prevalence claims.

## What Changed
