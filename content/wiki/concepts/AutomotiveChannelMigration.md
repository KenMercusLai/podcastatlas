# Automotive Channel Migration / 汽车渠道迁移

Automotive channel migration is the pattern in [[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] where consumer-electronics product and retail capability moves into car sales, delivery, service, and user operations. The source's Huawei case turns [[OfflineHandsetChannelSystem|phone-era offline-channel learning]] into an automotive asset.

The source argues that [[Huawei]] did not enter cars only with parts. It brought user insight, launch events, configuration and pricing discipline, retail explanation, high-frequency store traffic, sales training, and service expectations from phones into [[AITO|问界]] and later [[HongmengZhixing|鸿蒙智行]]. As the system matured, channels expanded from Huawei mall/store experience centers into sales-delivery-service user centers and partner networks with luxury-car sales and repair experience.

## Key Claims
- Automotive sales can benefit from consumer-electronics style product explanation when intelligent cockpit and assisted-driving features need buyer education.
- Store traffic helps only if the vehicle product, price, delivery, and service can convert attention into orders.
- Channel migration creates leverage for Huawei but also raises partner-execution pressure because delivery and quality failures become more visible.
- Multi-brand channel sharing can strengthen service coverage while making portfolio clarity and customer ownership more important.
