# Ceramic Industrialization Transition / 陶瓷工业化转型

Ceramic Industrialization Transition / 陶瓷工业化转型 is the problem of moving Jingdezhen porcelain from workshop and imperial systems into modern industry. [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]] links this transition to European hard-paste porcelain competition, Taiping-war destruction, late Qing public-private efforts, ceramic schools, Republican-era factories, and wartime interruption.

The concept shows that industrialization is not just buying machinery. In the source, institutions, financing, education, fuel, skilled workers, markets, and political stability all had to change before [[Jingdezhen]] could rebuild capacity, later feeding into the [[StateOwnedCeramicFactorySystem]] and [[AdvancedCeramicsIndustrialUpgrade]].
