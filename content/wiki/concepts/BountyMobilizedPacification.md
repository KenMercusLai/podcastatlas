## Definition
Bounty-mobilized pacification is the pattern where the state uses public rewards, titles, and money to mobilize local actors alongside appointed commanders. It is not mere mercenary outsourcing: the reward works when credible command, local knowledge, and visible merit conversion align.

## Current Synthesis
Hanji 826 supplies the initial case through the Xu-Yang anti-bandit campaign. Liang Na appoints Teng Fu and related commanders; Teng Fu then announces rewards that bring local participants into the suppression field. Xie An of Xiapi becomes the clearest case: after Xu Feng escapes and burns Dongcheng, Xie An leads kin and associates to kill him and is made Marquis of Pingxiang.

The concept sits between [[MeritBasedRewardPunishment|因功赏罚]] and [[RootCausePacification|查因安抚式治理]]. The reward must be credible enough to move local action, but pacification still depends on commander quality and post-victory order rather than bounty money alone.

## Key Claims
- Rewards can extend state capacity by mobilizing local people with knowledge of terrain, fugitives, and kinship networks.
- Bounties work best when credible commanders create a field where local action can safely attach to public authority.
- Merit conversion must be visible: Xie An's marquisate matters because it proves that decisive local action will be recognized.
- Reward-backed suppression can reduce bandit networks, but it does not by itself solve every adjacent disorder node.
- The pattern is vulnerable if rewards become arbitrary, captured, or detached from real service.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The current concept rests on one source note. It should not be treated as a universal recipe for rebellion suppression, because reward systems can produce false reporting, bounty hunting, or postwar grievance if not tied to trustworthy command. Zhao Xu's execution for fearfulness and false merit reporting in the same episode is a caution that campaign reward environments also require verification.

## What Changed
- Created the concept from Hanji 826's Xu-Yang reward and local-ambush sequence.
- Connected Xie An's Pingxiang marquisate to visible merit conversion rather than treating it as a stray reward notice.
