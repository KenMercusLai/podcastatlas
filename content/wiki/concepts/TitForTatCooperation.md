# Tit-For-Tat Cooperation / 一报还一报合作

Tit-for-tat cooperation is the simple reciprocity strategy introduced in [[79-gewei-lingdao-danfan-zan-xuedian-boyilun-690004262]] through [[RobertAxelrod]] and 《合作的进化》. In the episode, it shows why human society is not doomed to a "dark forest" version of [[PrisonersDilemma]].

The concept's source-scoped lesson is that cooperation can be designed. A participant can begin cooperatively, respond to defection, and return to cooperation when the other side does, making behavior legible without requiring permanent naivete.

## Key Claims
- Cooperation is more durable when actions have visible consequences across repeated interactions.
- Reciprocity can protect against exploitation while still leaving a path back to cooperation.
- The strategy supports institutional optimism: people can escape mutually destructive traps when the game has memory and feedback.
