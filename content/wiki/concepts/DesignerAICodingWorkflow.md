# Designer AI Coding Workflow

## Definition
Designer AI coding workflow is the practice of using visual intent artifacts such as sketches, Figma files, screenshots, and photos together with natural-language instructions so AI coding agents can turn design ideas into working software.

## Current Synthesis
[[anyway-194-anyway-194]] frames AI coding as especially meaningful for designers because it lets them communicate in the media they already use: mental pictures, paper sketches, Figma mocks, screenshots, and iterative comments. In this view, tools such as [[Codex]] do not simply replace typing code; they translate design intent into implementation while the human keeps steering taste, product judgment, and acceptance.

The same source keeps a hard boundary around public reliability. Designer-led AI coding can rapidly produce a personal prototype, but real users expose bugs, compatibility gaps, privacy worries, and interaction details that require engineering verification and maintenance.

## Key Claims
- AI coding can make design artifacts into implementation inputs rather than only specifications handed to engineers.
- The workflow is strongest when the designer has a clear mental or visual target and can iterate with screenshots, Figma, paper sketches, or MCP-provided context.
- The human role shifts toward intent, taste, inspection, and acceptance rather than disappearing.
- Self-use tools tolerate known rough edges, while public products require much stronger verification and support.

## Evidence
- Visual-to-code workflow - [[anyway-194-anyway-194]] describes a designer starting from mental imagery, Figma, paper sketches, photos, screenshots, MCP context, and natural-language communication with Codex.
- Capability expansion - [[anyway-194-anyway-194]] says AI tools make simple or bounded personal software feasible for people who are not traditional programmers.
- Human control - [[anyway-194-anyway-194]] records a preference for Human in the loop and continued hands-on code participation.
- Public-product boundary - [[anyway-194-anyway-194]] uses 默默语 2.0 and general app complaints to show that broad release raises bugs, privacy, compatibility, and polish demands.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is based on host experience rather than a measured productivity study. Designer AI coding does not remove architecture, accessibility, performance, security, or maintenance work. It may also create false confidence if visual fidelity is mistaken for durable software quality.
