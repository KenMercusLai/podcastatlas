# Narrative Misdirection / 叙事误导

## Definition

Narrative misdirection / 叙事误导 is the practice of making one center, culprit, motive, or genre explanation attentionally dominant before a later reveal reorganizes the meaning of earlier evidence.

## Current Synthesis

The Hitchcock source shows misdirection at the scale of an entire plot. In [[PsychoFilm|《惊魂记》 / Psycho]], stolen money appears to be the story engine before the motel, mother story, investigation, and hidden personality displace it. The rocket-launch story moves suspicion through sabotage, jealousy, and food logistics before the same details imply a darker hidden passenger. A [[MacGuffin|MacGuffin]] can help by holding attention on an object whose causal importance is temporary.

False-suspect construction adds a second scale inside a school mystery. Snape's actual hostility, punitive conduct, and visible spell-casting make the wrong explanation plausible; Quirrell remains less salient even when his presence supplies relevant evidence. The reveal works because it changes the causal reading of the Quidditch scene—Snape's words were protective—without claiming that his abrasive behavior was imaginary. Effective misdirection can therefore depend on true negative evidence attached to the wrong accusation.

The “百花错” questions add a compact riddle scale. Several clues accumulate around the answer a knowledgeable audience is expected to retrieve, while a final condition or an alternate meaning of an earlier word excludes that answer and identifies another character. Unlike plot misdirection, the pleasure is immediate recognition of a deliberately induced mental set: shared canon knowledge makes the trap possible, and the reveal must make the whole clue sequence newly legible.

## Key Claims

- Misdirection works by making a provisional explanation plausible, not by withholding all logic.
- Attention can be redirected through a temporary plot object, an emotionally vivid suspect, or a familiar genre expectation.
- Effective reversal changes how earlier evidence reads rather than merely adding an unrelated surprise.
- A false suspect may genuinely behave badly; disproving the central accusation does not erase those reasons for distrust.
- Quieter counterevidence must remain available if retrospective verification is part of the intended pleasure.
- The device differs from fair-play deduction: it can overlap with mystery, but its primary effect is attentional and emotional redirection.
- In riddle form, shared category knowledge and ambiguous wording can make the most available answer feel certain before one discriminating clue overturns it.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The sources are podcast interpretations of famous stories and an entertainment quiz rather than a complete narratology or evidence of authorial intent. Retrospective fit can be overstated when readers repair missing setup after a reveal. Misdirection becomes arbitrary if decisive information is unavailable, and its overlap with fair-play mystery varies by genre and audience knowledge. The “百花错” examples depend heavily on familiarity with many Jin Yong characters and on oral wording; without that shared knowledge, the intended mental set may never form.

## What Changed

- Added false-suspect construction in which genuine hostility supports the wrong causal accusation.
- Distinguished reversal of guilt from moral exoneration of the suspect's other behavior.
- Extended the concept from plot-scale and suspect-scale redirection to short clue riddles built around category expectation and lexical ambiguity.
