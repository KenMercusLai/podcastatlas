# 京城诗社交性 / Capital Poetry Sociality

Capital poetry sociality is the episode's use of "京城诗" to explain how Tang poems could emerge from capital occasions before becoming later anthology classics. [[ep264-tafang-tangshi-zhilu-shengtang-qixiang-shaonianxin-loo2dfuxffizpbl4si5nwcixnngi]] uses [[WangWei|王维]] in [[ChangAnTang|唐代长安]] to show poetry as court performance, princely-mansion exchange, banquet writing, farewell writing, exam and selection display, and reputation circulation.

The concept does not reduce poetry to networking. It says that early fame and form were often produced inside social scenes, while later poets and poems could break open that pattern through stronger personal feeling, landscape practice, and new voices arriving from outside the capital.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-131-3-liqingzhao-zhongnian-fuqi-libie-hejiu-jie-xiangsi-lnzxhrj2iulahccy2-pkm-wphdei]] adds a wine-centered Chang'an example through [[DuFu|杜甫]]'s 《饮中八仙歌》. The host stresses that the figures Du Fu writes about had long lived in Chang'an and shared a poetic, unconstrained drinking culture. That makes the poem a compact case where capital sociality, reputation, drinking scene, and literary portrait become inseparable.

## Key Claims
- Capital-centered poems often carry traces of the occasion that produced them.
- Patronage, official selection, and literati exchange can shape poetic voice before poems become timeless-looking anthology objects.
- [[WangWei|王维]]'s later [[Wangchuan|辋川]] landscape poetry matters partly because it moves beyond formulaic social writing without leaving the capital region behind.
- Reading the social occasion strengthens, rather than cheapens, the poem's historical meaning.
