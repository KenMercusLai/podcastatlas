# Customer-Earned Expansion

## Definition

Customer-earned expansion is the rule that a company should add locations, products, channels, licenses, or markets after customer behavior demonstrates a valued core use case, rather than using expansion itself to manufacture demand.

## Current Synthesis

[[InsomniaCookies]] supplies the location-level case: broad ambitions remain credible only when local awareness and affection are strong enough to support another bakery. [[Cahindo]] applies the rule to products and wholesale channels, [[SaunaSociete|Sauna Société]] to adjacent apparel, and [[Desden]] to licenses and sports. Across the cases, expansion is not prohibited; it is sequenced behind focused proof and the capacity to repeat what customers already value.

The evidence gate differs by unit. Stores require local awareness and sales, fashion requires sell-through, return, review, and occasion evidence, unfamiliar accessories require comprehension and trial, and licensed products require team-level performance and end-user relevance. [[CustomerPull]] therefore supplies the signal, while [[LocalMarketProof]] and [[SustainableGrowthPace]] determine whether it can be replicated safely.

## Key Claims

- Expansion is a replication decision, not a substitute for proving demand.
- The evidence gate should match the unit being expanded: store, SKU, channel, category, license, or market.
- A focused core makes customer behavior easier to interpret than a broad assortment or channel portfolio.
- Founder conviction remains useful for choosing experiments but should not override repeated behavioral evidence.
- Operating capacity and quality control matter alongside demand because growth can destroy the value customers selected.

## Evidence

- Location rollout: [[advice-line-with-seth-berkowitz-of-insomnia-cookies-vmp8144262595]] says Insomnia Cookies previously opened too quickly in some markets and could have used local awareness measures earlier.
- Product and channel focus: [[advice-line-with-seth-berkowitz-of-insomnia-cookies-vmp8144262595]] recommends that Cahindo build around a small number of bestsellers and use their data in boutique outreach.
- Category adjacency: [[advice-line-with-seth-berkowitz-of-insomnia-cookies-vmp8144262595]] treats Sauna Société's wider apparel range as a later possibility rather than current proof.
- License expansion: [[advice-line-with-seth-berkowitz-of-insomnia-cookies-vmp8144262595]] recommends analyzing Desden's current licenses and testing only one or two teams in another sport.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Some markets reward speed, network coverage, scarce-site capture, or early licensing breadth, and waiting for complete proof can miss a temporary window. Customer behavior can also lag awareness-building investment. The concept does not require certainty; it requires a proportionate signal and bounded test before the cost and complexity of expansion become hard to reverse.

## What Changed

- Initial synthesis established across location, SKU, category, channel, and license expansion.
- Distinguished demand evidence from the operating capacity needed to preserve quality.
