# 先制士气震慑 / Preemptive Morale Shock

## Definition
先制士气震慑 / Preemptive Morale Shock is a defensive move in which a smaller force attacks before a larger enemy fully concentrates, aiming to weaken the attacker's expectations and stabilize its own side before the numerical imbalance can be fully applied.

## Current Synthesis
The initial case is the 215 [[HefeiBattle215|合肥之战]]. [[ZhangLiao|张辽]] judges that relief cannot arrive in time and that passive waiting will let [[SunQuan|孙权]]'s larger army convert numbers into siege pressure. A selected assault therefore targets the psychological and organizational setup of the campaign: it shocks the attackers, demonstrates defender agency, and gives the garrison confidence to hold the walls afterward.

The mechanism does not mean that courage erases material disadvantage. It depends on an incomplete enemy concentration, a narrow timing window, disciplined role assignment, and the ability to disengage into a defensible position after the shock.

## Key Claims
- A smaller defender may gain leverage by acting before the larger force completes assembly.
- The immediate target is both enemy momentum and friendly confidence.
- Selection, surprise, and timing matter more than sustaining open battle against the full opposing force.
- Preassigned roles allow the shock force and fortress defense to complement rather than compete with each other.
- The tactic succeeds as expectation management only if the smaller side can survive after the initial action.

## Evidence
**Timing and role assignment**
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1154-caocao-ruhe-jinping-yimei-jinnang-podi-10-wan-lmxtopnmefh5wdum5fiyjodngxxf|Hanji 1154]] assigns Zhang Liao and Li Dian to attack and Yue Jin to defend, then has Zhang Liao argue for action before Wu concentration.

**Two-sided morale effect**
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1154-caocao-ruhe-jinping-yimei-jinnang-podi-10-wan-lmxtopnmefh5wdum5fiyjodngxxf|Hanji 1154]] presents the assault as damaging Wu morale while making the Hefei garrison more confident.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source gives one dramatized historical case, not a general causal estimate. Reported force sizes, casualty effects, dialogue, and the relative contribution of epidemic, logistics, fortifications, and command error remain uncertain. A failed shock attack could instead consume scarce defenders or expose the city.

## What Changed
- Created the concept from the episode's explicit sequence of early assault, enemy demoralization, and defender stabilization.
