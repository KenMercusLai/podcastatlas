# Autonomous Enterprise

Autonomous enterprise is the SAP vision discussed in [[ai-chongji-qiye-ruanjian-jutou-yu-sap-yuanxin-liao-damoxing-to-b-de-dianfu-yu-bianjie-1-174-1]], where enterprise software moves from recording business activity toward helping execute it. The source describes [[SAPJoule|SAP Joule / Joule Work]] as a front-end layer that understands user intent, dispatches agents, and can expose both SAP and non-SAP data to agent workflows.

The source keeps the autonomy claim bounded. In financial close, compliance, data quality, and exception handling, agents may automate large shares of work, but exchange-rate differences, bad debt, data errors, approvals, and responsibility boundaries still require [[HumanJudgmentUnderAI]].

## Key Claims
- Autonomy means thinner application layers and more executable process/data access, not the disappearance of enterprise systems.
- The practical architecture depends on [[EnterpriseAgentGovernance]], [[EnterpriseOperationalMemory]], and reliable structured data.
- Human-in-the-loop review remains central for complex, regulated, or judgment-heavy work.
- The concept is one enterprise-software path through [[BusinessLedAITransformation]] rather than a generic model-as-product thesis.
