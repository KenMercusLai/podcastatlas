# Temporary Family Healing / 临时家庭式修复

## Definition
Temporary family healing / 临时家庭式修复 is the repair pattern where unrelated, wounded, or lonely people temporarily become a family-like support structure through recognition, responsibility, and care rather than blood, marriage, or romance.

## Current Synthesis
In [[ep281-duihua-wuzhenyu-kun-zai-chuangshang-li-de-women-jueding-zhongxin-zhaohui-ziji-lvvreapoykpj5xtlryaaupu1xo7r]], [[YuanYumei|袁玉梅]] uses [[ChanDrama|《蝉》]] to describe Yu Songfan, Ding Ning, Zhao Yijie, and Awu as lonely people who do not fit a conventional love line but still form a temporary family. The point is not that found family instantly cures trauma. It gives damaged people a relational scene where responsibility, pity, confrontation, play, and recognition can make continuing to live more possible.

The concept is especially important for Yu Songfan. The source says Awu becomes his most important "grip" on life: a responsibility, hope, and long-missing familial feeling that interrupts his wish to time his own death.

## Key Claims
- Family-like repair can emerge outside blood, marriage, or romance when people become responsible to one another.
- A temporary family can include conflict, rivalry, and mutual testing; warmth alone is not the whole mechanism.
- Vulnerable children or childlike trust can give an isolated adult a reason to remain attached to life.
- The repair is bounded and contingent, not a guaranteed cure for trauma, aging, grief, or guilt.
- Drama can make this structure visible by showing everyday care, meals, address terms, and shared danger rather than explaining healing abstractly.

## Evidence
- Non-romantic structure - [[ep281-duihua-wuzhenyu-kun-zai-chuangshang-li-de-women-jueding-zhongxin-zhaohui-ziji-lvvreapoykpj5xtlryaaupu1xo7r]] has Yuan say the drama drew her partly because it lacked a standard love line and instead followed lonely strangers who become mutually important.
- Awu as life grip - [[ep281-duihua-wuzhenyu-kun-zai-chuangshang-li-de-women-jueding-zhongxin-zhaohui-ziji-lvvreapoykpj5xtlryaaupu1xo7r]] says Awu becomes Yu Songfan's responsibility, hope, and long-missing family feeling.
- Everyday intimacy - [[ep281-duihua-wuzhenyu-kun-zai-chuangshang-li-de-women-jueding-zhongxin-zhaohui-ziji-lvvreapoykpj5xtlryaaupu1xo7r]] highlights the breakfast scene and Awu calling Yu Songfan "grandpa" as moments where emotion almost overflows.
- Shared woundedness - [[ep281-duihua-wuzhenyu-kun-zai-chuangshang-li-de-women-jueding-zhongxin-zhaohui-ziji-lvvreapoykpj5xtlryaaupu1xo7r]] frames the major characters as isolated people whose bonds come through recognition and compassion.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source treats temporary family as a dramatic and emotional structure, not a universal therapy prescription. Care can be reparative only when it does not become coercion, dependency, or denial of the underlying harm.
