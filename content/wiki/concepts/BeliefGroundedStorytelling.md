# Belief-Grounded Storytelling / 信念落地的叙事

## Definition
Belief-grounded storytelling is the claim that a creator and performers must sincerely accept a story's characters, situation, and moral possibility before genre technique, comedy, or formal innovation can persuade an audience.

## Current Synthesis
[[DapengDirector|大鹏]] applies the principle most directly to [[PostTruthFilm|《保你平安》]]. Some viewers doubt that a person would pursue justice for an unrelated dead client, while Dapeng says he believes such people exist. That conviction keeps the film from rewriting its central figure into a more cynical character merely to fit assumed realism.

The principle also shapes comedy. A joke should emerge from what a person wants and what a situation permits; stopping the story for an isolated sketch weakens belief. Genre categories therefore have no intrinsic hierarchy in this account. Comedy, drama, suspense, and historical spectacle differ in method, but each succeeds only if the human action remains credible inside the work.

## Key Claims
- Creator disbelief tends to deform a story toward a different moral and emotional world.
- Audience skepticism is relevant feedback but does not automatically override an author's grounded conviction.
- Comedy works best when laughter follows character and situation rather than interrupting them.
- Genre hierarchy is less useful than testing whether the work sustains belief in its people and stakes.
- Sincere belief is necessary but not sufficient; execution, structure, performance, and audience legibility still matter.

## Evidence
- Moral plausibility - [[daoyan-dapeng-luoyonghao-wo-zhe-liang-beizi-liytv2s219yn3ox5dhr4z4mrovyt]] records Dapeng's insistence that people like Wei Ping'an exist.
- Comedy integration - [[daoyan-dapeng-luoyonghao-wo-zhe-liang-beizi-liytv2s219yn3ox5dhr4z4mrovyt]] has Dapeng argue that comic effect should arise within character and situation rather than as a detached skit.
- Genre equality - [[daoyan-dapeng-luoyonghao-wo-zhe-liang-beizi-liytv2s219yn3ox5dhr4z4mrovyt]] has him reject a hierarchy among comedy, drama, suspense, crime, and thriller when the audience can believe the work.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Personal conviction cannot by itself establish social representativeness, historical accuracy, ethical adequacy, or artistic success. A creator can sincerely believe a false or harmful claim, and audiences may reasonably reject execution even when the underlying belief is genuine.

## What Changed
- Created a film-craft concept linking moral conviction, character plausibility, integrated comedy, and genre equality.
