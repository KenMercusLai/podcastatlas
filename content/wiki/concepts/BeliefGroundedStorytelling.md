# Belief-Grounded Storytelling / 信念落地的叙事

## Definition
Belief-grounded storytelling is the claim that creators and performers must sincerely accept the human experience, situation, or moral possibility they express before genre technique, formal innovation, or polished craft can persuade an audience.

## Current Synthesis
[[DapengDirector|大鹏]] applies the principle to characters and moral worlds. Some viewers doubt that a person would pursue justice for an unrelated dead client in [[PostTruthFilm|《保你平安》]], while Dapeng says he believes such people exist. That conviction keeps the film from rewriting its central figure into a more cynical character merely to fit assumed realism. Comedy follows the same boundary: laughter should emerge from character and situation rather than stop the story for a detachable sketch.

Across music and lyrics, songs become credible when concrete experiences, ordinary objects, felt states, and imagined details can be expressed as something the creator believes rather than as an empty concept. Technical correctness, rhyme, theme, arrangement, and polish can complete a song, but they do not alone make a listener trust what is being said. [[LiRonghao|李荣浩]] supplies this practitioner account.

## Key Claims
- Creator disbelief tends to deform a work toward a different moral and emotional world.
- Concrete lived detail can strengthen credibility without requiring every work to be literal autobiography.
- Genre, rhyme, polish, and technique cannot substitute for believable motive and human stakes.
- Audience skepticism is relevant feedback but does not automatically override grounded creator conviction.
- Sincere belief is necessary but not sufficient; execution, structure, performance, ethics, and audience legibility still matter.

## Evidence
- Moral plausibility - [[daoyan-dapeng-luoyonghao-wo-zhe-liang-beizi-liytv2s219yn3ox5dhr4z4mrovyt]] records Dapeng's insistence that people like Wei Ping'an exist.
- Character-led form - [[daoyan-dapeng-luoyonghao-wo-zhe-liang-beizi-liytv2s219yn3ox5dhr4z4mrovyt]] has Dapeng argue that comic effect should arise within character and situation and that genre categories have no intrinsic hierarchy when the work sustains belief.
- Lived musical material - [[vol-57-duitan-lironghao-gai-fangxia-jiu-fangxia-gai-buyao-jiu-buyao-lujrbxdhqr5-hrqmyj746w6uap5]] has Li connect songs such as 《戒烟》 and 《乌梅子酱》 to specific ordinary experience rather than preplanned inspiration sessions.
- Lyric credibility - [[vol-57-duitan-lironghao-gai-fangxia-jiu-fangxia-gai-buyao-jiu-buyao-lujrbxdhqr5-hrqmyj746w6uap5]] separates technically complete, rhymed lyrics from words that make listeners believe the represented experience.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Personal conviction cannot by itself establish social representativeness, factual or historical accuracy, ethical adequacy, originality, or artistic success. A creator can sincerely believe a false or harmful claim, and audiences may reasonably reject execution even when the underlying belief is genuine. Concrete detail can also be fabricated persuasively, so felt authenticity is not proof of truth.

## What Changed
- Extended the concept from film characters and genre into songwriting, lyrics, ordinary experience, and imagined-but-credible expression.
- Clarified that concrete detail can ground belief without reducing art to literal autobiography.
