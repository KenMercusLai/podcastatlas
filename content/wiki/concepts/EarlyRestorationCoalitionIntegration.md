## Definition

早期复汉联盟整合 / Early restoration coalition integration is the process by which fragmented anti-Xin forces are made operationally cooperative through restoration legitimacy, personal persuasion, material hospitality, oath-making, rest, and coordinated action.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-662-liuxiu-shi-ruhe-huajie-tuandui-neidou-de-lsuin9kf1epqfo2yjlk-q1-zwx2|Hanji 662]] shows the pattern before [[LiuXuanGengshiEmperor|更始帝刘玄]] is elevated in Hanji 663. After Xiaochang'an damages Liu-family forces and makes New Market and Pinglin troops consider flight, [[LiuYanLiuXiuBrother|刘縯]] seeks Xiajiang cooperation. [[WangChangXiajiangGengshi|王常]] accepts the claim that [[WangMang|王莽]] has lost public support and that Liu-family restoration can unite the field, then persuades other Xiajiang leaders.

The integration is practical rather than merely rhetorical. Liu Yan's side visits in person, hosts the joined forces with food and drink, makes an oath, rests the troops, and then converts the alliance into a six-route night attack on Lanxiang. Legitimacy matters because it makes cooperation honorable; logistics and operational coordination matter because legitimacy alone would not recover the defeated army.

## Key Claims

- Defeat can make coalition integration urgent by exposing morale, troop, and confidence gaps.
- Restoration legitimacy works as a shared language only when a credible intermediary carries it into another armed group.
- Personal contact and hospitality help turn agreement into a usable alliance.
- Oath and rest convert a fragile bargain into short-term command readiness.
- The strongest evidence of integration is operational follow-through, not the meeting itself.

## Evidence

- Defeat trigger: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-662-liuxiu-shi-ruhe-huajie-tuandui-neidou-de-lsuin9kf1epqfo2yjlk-q1-zwx2|Hanji 662]] says Xiaochang'an kills many Liu-family members and leaves New Market and Pinglin troops considering escape.
- Personal approach: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-662-liuxiu-shi-ruhe-huajie-tuandui-neidou-de-lsuin9kf1epqfo2yjlk-q1-zwx2|Hanji 662]] says Liu Yan takes Liu Xiu and Li Tong to the Xiajiang camp to seek cooperation.
- Persuasive bridge: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-662-liuxiu-shi-ruhe-huajie-tuandui-neidou-de-lsuin9kf1epqfo2yjlk-q1-zwx2|Hanji 662]] says Wang Chang accepts Liu Yan's argument and persuades Cheng Dan and Zhang Ang by appealing to Wang Mang's loss of support and public memory of Han.
- Operational proof: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-662-liuxiu-shi-ruhe-huajie-tuandui-neidou-de-lsuin9kf1epqfo2yjlk-q1-zwx2|Hanji 662]] says Liu Yan hosts the forces, makes an oath, rests them for three days, then sends six routes at night to capture Lanxiang and its supplies.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Hanji 662 supports short-term coalition integration, not a settled institution. The next known sequence still has factional bargaining over Liu Xuan's elevation and later lethal suspicion around Liu Yan. Cheng Dan, Zhang Ang, Li Tong, exact oath formula, food logistics, route plan, and troop counts remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Added a pre-Gengshi coalition-integration pattern that explains how Liu Yan and Wang Chang repair morale after Xiaochang'an before Hanji 663's ruler-selection conflict.
