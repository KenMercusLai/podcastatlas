## Definition

宫廷女性同性亲密关系 / Palace Female Same-Sex Intimacy is an evidence-boundary concept for historical records that describe intimate, domestic, erotic, or spouse-like relationships between women in a palace setting without automatically converting those descriptions into settled modern identities.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 270-1 introduces the concept through [[EmpressChenAjiaoWesternHan|陈阿娇]] and [[ChuFuWesternHan|楚服]]. The episode attributes to 《汉武故事》 a description in which Chu Fu wears male clothing, shares Chen's sleeping quarters, and is “相爱若夫妇” with her. That wording supports a transmitted record of spouse-like female intimacy, while the surrounding ritual dependency and wugu accusation make the relationship difficult to isolate from hostile court narration.

The host calls the pair China's first documented female homosexual relationship and connects them to “对食,” said first to denote female-female relations and later women-eunuch pairings. The supplied source does not establish either priority or semantic history through textual comparison. The current synthesis therefore preserves the relationship record, the host's interpretation, and the terminology claim as three distinct layers.

## Key Claims

- “相爱若夫妇” is meaningful evidence of a transmitted spouse-like relationship, not merely a generic friendship description.
- Male dress and shared sleeping quarters add gendered and domestic detail but do not alone settle identity, role, consent, or sexual practice.
- A court accusation context can preserve otherwise hidden intimacy while also distorting it through scandal, hostile interpretation, and punishment.
- Modern labels can help formulate questions but should not be projected backward as if historical actors used identical identity categories.
- Claims of a national or chronological “first” require broader corpus comparison than one popular-history episode supplies.
- The meaning and historical range of “对食” remain open here because the episode cites no lexical or historiographic debate.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source is a compact popular-history retelling rather than a textual study of 《汉武故事》, 《汉书》, or the history of “对食.” The relationship appears inside a wugu scandal and may be mediated by moralizing or hostile narrative conventions. Nothing supplied establishes supernatural manipulation, coercion, exclusive preference, a stable sexual identity, or chronological priority. The concept records evidence of intimacy while keeping those stronger claims unresolved.
