# AI Revenue Legibility

## Definition
AI revenue legibility is the degree to which investors and operators can identify, attribute, and audit the revenue, margin, cash-flow, or accepted-work contribution created by AI.

## Current Synthesis
The bounded record distinguishes visible demand from proved economic durability. Standalone subscriptions, API use, coding-agent revenue, cloud segments, and public model-company financials are relatively bright-line signals that customers will pay. AI-improved advertising, bundled cloud effects, internal productivity, and broad labor substitution are darker lines because outsiders cannot isolate the causal dollars.

Revenue is therefore one level in a longer evidence chain, not the endpoint. Model-company growth must pass through application acceptance, customer profit or cost reduction, provider margins, retention, and infrastructure payback. The newest source makes listed model and application-company revenue growth one of three practical bubble indicators, but explicitly pairs it with GPU rental prices, credit spreads, and free cash flow so that revenue growth is not mistaken for system-wide return quality.

## Key Claims
- Standalone subscriptions, API use, coding agents, cloud segments, and public financials are more legible than claimed gains embedded in legacy businesses.
- Revenue growth demonstrates willingness to pay but not by itself retention, customer ROI, provider margins, or demand durability.
- Coding and bounded workflow automation currently provide clearer revenue evidence than broad white-collar replacement.
- Public disclosure can separate weak sector demand from company-specific share or execution changes.
- Similar capex receives different treatment when revenue, free cash flow, utilization, and spending discipline are easier to observe.
- Model-layer revenue, accepted application work, enterprise profit, and infrastructure cash conversion are related but noninterchangeable evidence levels.
- Revenue growth becomes a stronger cycle signal when paired with compute pricing and credit conditions.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Dark-line benefits can be economically real before segment reporting isolates them, and early products can create learning or strategic option value before improving EPS. Conversely, annualized run rates can obscure discounts, concentration, inference cost, churn, related-party demand, or accounting differences. The cited company revenue, valuation, listing, and adoption figures remain source-scoped rather than audited conclusions.

## What Changed
- Added listed model and application-company revenue growth as a cycle-monitoring indicator.
- Bound revenue evidence more explicitly to compute pricing, credit spreads, and free cash flow.
- Clarified the chain from model revenue through accepted work to customer and infrastructure returns.
