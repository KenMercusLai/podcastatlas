# Ageotypes

## Definition
Ageotypes are proposed individualized patterns in which aging-related change appears more strongly in particular organs or pathways, such as metabolic, immune, hepatic, renal, cardiovascular, inflammatory, or oxidative-stress systems.

## Current Synthesis
Ageotypes shift the aging question from one universal biological-age score toward multiple trajectories that may differ within and between people. In the episode, the concept matters because a dominant changing pathway could suggest a more targeted behavior or monitoring response. Its value depends on reproducible markers, stable classification, clinically meaningful outcomes, and evidence that acting on a pattern improves health rather than merely changing a measurement.

## Key Claims
- Aging can be multidimensional rather than reducible to one scalar score.
- People may show different dominant organ or pathway trajectories.
- Repeated measurement is necessary to distinguish a trajectory from a one-time fluctuation.
- Actionability requires validated interventions and outcomes, not merely a personalized label.

## Evidence
- Proposed dimensions - [[transform-your-metabolic-health-longevity-by-knowing-your-unique-biology-dr-michael-snyder-scim5906827357]] names metabolic, immune, liver, kidney, cardiovascular, inflammatory, and oxidative-stress pathways.
- Longitudinal basis - [[transform-your-metabolic-health-longevity-by-knowing-your-unique-biology-dr-michael-snyder-scim5906827357]] grounds the framework in repeated multimodal measurement while people are healthy.
- Actionability claim - [[transform-your-metabolic-health-longevity-by-knowing-your-unique-biology-dr-michael-snyder-scim5906827357]] reports that some participants improved markers through targeted behavior change.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The supplied summary does not establish classification stability, reference populations, causal pathways, effect sizes, or hard clinical outcomes. Marker improvement may not equal slower aging or longer life, and organ systems interact rather than aging independently.

## What Changed
- Established ageotypes as a multi-trajectory alternative to one-number biological age.
- Clarified that measurement and marker movement are not themselves proof of longevity benefit.
