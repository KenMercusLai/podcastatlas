# 兰亭雅集 / Lanting Gathering

## Definition

The Lanting Gathering was a 353 Eastern Jin elite gathering at Shanyin organized by [[WangXizhi|王羲之]], associated with winding-stream drinking, verse composition, and [[LantingjiXu|《兰亭集序》]].

## Current Synthesis

The gathering matters less in the source as an isolated banquet than as a cultural event whose poetry, calligraphy, and later reception transformed shared pleasure into an encounter with time. Landscape, wine, friendship, and composition make the present vivid; the preface then turns toward aging, changing emotion, and death. The source contrasts this response with banquet stories organized by assassination, coercion, or nihilistic luxury, arguing that art can acknowledge impermanence without either denying it or reducing life to immediate consumption.

Its status was not fixed in 353. Comparison with the earlier Jingu gathering, possible political overtones among Eastern Jin elites, Tang admiration for Wang's calligraphy, and uncertainty over the received preface all make Lanting a layered reception history rather than a perfectly recoverable event.

## Key Claims

- The gathering combined elite sociability, landscape, drinking, and rule-bound verse composition.
- Its surviving cultural force depends on the preface and calligraphic transmission.
- Awareness of pleasure's transience becomes an artistic rather than nihilistic response to mortality.
- Possible political meaning remains inferential, whether expressed through discussion or deliberate distance from politics.
- Later canonization and textual uncertainty are part of the event's history, not external footnotes.

## Evidence

- Event and practice: [[24-dongxifang-de-fanju-zuihou-de-wancan-yu-lanting-yaji-lraekm-bgowhg1wxysfshcwoz5xp]] dates the gathering to 353 and describes the participants, winding stream, verse, and drinking penalty.
- Mortality and art: [[24-dongxifang-de-fanju-zuihou-de-wancan-yu-lanting-yaji-lraekm-bgowhg1wxysfshcwoz5xp]] reads the preface as moving from pleasure toward aging, death, and artistic transformation.
- Reception and uncertainty: [[24-dongxifang-de-fanju-zuihou-de-wancan-yu-lanting-yaji-lraekm-bgowhg1wxysfshcwoz5xp]] connects Tang prestige, transmitted copies, the Jingu comparison, and the *Linhe Xu* variant.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source does not prove that the gathering had a coordinated political agenda or that withdrawal from politics was its shared purpose. Participant lists, textual formation, calligraphic transmission, and stories about Wang's reaction to the Jingu comparison remain source-scoped. The comparison with the Last Supper concerns cultural memory and mortality, not equivalent institutions or historical evidence.

## What Changed

- Created a synthesis joining event, artistic response, canonization, and textual uncertainty.
