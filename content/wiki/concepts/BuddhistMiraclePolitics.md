# Buddhist Miracle Politics / 佛教神通政治

Buddhist miracle politics / 佛教神通政治 names the episode's account of religious supernatural power becoming politically useful. In [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-jie-shangji-704051550]], [[Fotucheng|佛图澄]] gains authority near Later Zhao rulers through stories of longevity, fasting, luminous body, lotus miracles, ghost command, rescue, and prophecy.

The concept is not simply admiration for miracle stories. The source uses Fotucheng to show how a foreign religious specialist could gain elite protection and spread influence by providing signs, counsel, spectacle, and a moral counterweight inside violent politics.

## Key Claims
- Miracle narratives can help legitimate religious specialists in court settings.
- Supernatural charisma can become a tool of patronage, protection, and political advice.
- The ethical problem is unresolved when religious authority serves violent rulers while preserving a positive memory.
- Buddhist influence also enters the episode through mantra and spell transmission into [[DaoistRitualTechnology]].
