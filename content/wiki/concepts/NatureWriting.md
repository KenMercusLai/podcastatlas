# Nature Writing

## Definition

Nature writing is nonfiction that joins observation, field experience, memory, research, and reflection to make relations among organisms, places, people, and the observer legible without requiring the observer to disappear.

## Current Synthesis

The bounded sources reject both people-free wilderness prose and nature reduced to human therapy or decoration. [[RenNing]] keeps body, mood, memory, city life, taxonomy, and field conditions inside natural observation; [[YinQiCheDundun|尹琪]] includes neighbors, waste, safety, markets, and unmanaged woods in [[UrbanEdgeNature]]; [[DouglasAdams]] and [[MarkCarwardine]] use comic travel and human embarrassment to reveal extinction and conservation systems; the Yunnan source joins mountain perception to fungi, harvesting rules, bodily danger, and local history.

[[episode-229-diao-yi-tiao-da-yu-8209942018-431605]] sharpens the evaluative standard through [[NatureWritingObjectThickness]]. First-person frequency does not decide whether nature has been subordinated to the writer. The better question is whether organisms and places gain histories, pressures, interests, and resistant detail of their own, or whether every bird, failure, quotation, and landscape serves only to certify the author's identity.

## Key Claims

- Human presence can belong in nature writing without making nature merely human decoration.
- Field notes preserve detail, while delayed reflection can reveal relations that immediate recording misses.
- Nature can include beauty, suffering, extraction, survival, waste, danger, and ordinary social life without becoming only therapy or sanctuary.
- Humor and bodily awkwardness can deepen ecological attention when habitats, species, and institutions remain independently legible.
- Place-based food and fiction can border on nature writing when ecology, embodied risk, local practice, and harvesting limits remain visible.
- Authorial absence is impossible at the level of selection and form; the relevant issue is the relationship built between observer and subject.
- Strong nature writing lets concrete subjects complicate or overturn the writer's prepared meaning.

## Evidence

### Embodied observation without nature-as-therapy
- [[ganguan-fangda-shijie-he-renning-liao-guanniao-ziran-yu-ziyou-e583dac2-bad8-4208-8d35-0c3de8594779]] presents field notes, recordings, bodily weather, memory, taxonomy, and delayed prose while rejecting nature as only hospital or church.

### Mixed human-nonhuman places
- [[85-chedundun-yeshiji-zou-a-bandao-xiangxia-zuan-xiaoshuliner-qu-700935571]] makes bats, swallows, insects, woods, markets, trash, neighbors, and safety calculations part of one city-edge ecology.
- [[150-chi-jun-he-acuo-liao-yunnan-de-jizong-songrong-jianshouqing-ca-koushui-897563165]] connects mountain contact to mushroom ecology, poisoning risk, foraging restraint, fiction, transport, and local memory.

### Comic conservation and human implication
- [[186-rang-ni-xiao-dao-tou-diao-reng-bu-wang-sikao-weida-de-xiaoshi-shijie-manyou-zhinan-998655210]] uses humor, confusion, travel failure, and bodily exposure to keep endangered species and human systems in the same account.

### Object thickness rather than author disappearance
- [[episode-229-diao-yi-tiao-da-yu-8209942018-431605]] argues that nonhuman subjects become thicker when their own histories and pressures can resist the writer's intended story.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

- Including the writer does not guarantee thickness; a first-person work can still convert every subject into self-validation.
- Removing “I” does not guarantee objectivity because selection, naming, syntax, metaphor, and structure remain authorial acts.
- Technical detail and length are not sufficient: factual accumulation can still leave a subject with only one narrative function.
- The Yunnan episode is adjacent to nature writing rather than a direct literary-critical source, and its food-safety claims remain source-scoped.
- Comic conservation writing can expose anthropocentrism while still centering a charismatic human narrator.

## What Changed

- Migrated the page to the synthesis-first schema using all four prior sources.
- Replaced author visibility as the main evaluative question with the writer-subject relationship.
- Added the thick-versus-thin test for nonhuman history, interests, and resistance to interpretation.
