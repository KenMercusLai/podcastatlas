# TCM Summer Pattern Differentiation / 中医夏季寒湿湿热辨别

## Definition
TCM summer pattern differentiation is [[MaoZheTCMDoctor|毛哲]]'s source-scoped framework for varying summer self-care according to cold-damp or damp-heat patterns, digestion, subjective constitution, environment, symptoms, and illness severity rather than applying one universal “dampness-removing” routine.

## Current Synthesis
The framework's most defensible behavioral contribution is anti-extremism: hot weather does not make deliberate overexposure to sun, total rejection of air conditioning, unrestricted cold food, forced sweating, or one recipe appropriate for everyone. The two episodes ask listeners to notice tolerance, place, weather, exposure duration, symptoms, and health context; to use ordinary cooling and hydration sensibly; and to place serious illness above lifestyle experimentation. The rain-exposure episode adds [[RainExposureFootCareBoundary|prompt rinsing, drying, and removal from prolonged wet footwear]] without requiring a cold-damp diagnosis.

Its diagnostic and treatment content requires a firmer boundary. The sources map tongue coating, thirst, urine, bowel movements, sweating, limb temperature, fatigue, appetite, heaviness, region, and rain exposure onto cold-damp or damp-heat, then assign foods, drinks, herbs, patent medicine, soaking, induced sweating, acupressure, and gua sha accordingly. These are TCM interpretations supplied by one podcast guest; the sources provide no validation, diagnostic-performance data, doses, interaction review, or individualized examination. They therefore organize the episodes' claims without becoming established clinical rules.

## Key Claims
- Summer self-care should vary by individual tolerance, symptoms, place, weather, and severity rather than follow a universal seasonal rule.
- Fatigue, heaviness, sleepiness, poor appetite, and head fog are treated as broad “dampness” experiences in the source but do not independently distinguish a pattern or establish a medical diagnosis.
- The episode's cold-damp versus damp-heat distinction is a TCM classification, not a substitute for evidence-based assessment of dehydration, infection, heat illness, gastrointestinal disease, medication effects, sleep problems, or other causes.
- Moderate sun, comfortable air conditioning without direct exposure, hydration, and non-extreme food choices form the practical lifestyle layer, while herb, medicine, scraping, and induced-sweating claims require greater caution.
- After rain or standing-water exposure, prompt cleaning and drying are separable from claims that systemic cold-damp must be expelled through heat, sweating, food, ginger, or 藿香正气.
- Severe or worsening heat-related symptoms override the self-care framework and require prompt qualified assessment.

## Evidence
- Individualization and anti-extremism: [[vol-126-tianre-mei-weikou-shuibuxing-fali-qiduan-zhongyi-jiao-ni-chushi-fangfa-zhongshu-jingshi-you-han-zaixian-ltokazmbcf7gttvl9s-xsypc6tzt]] repeatedly rejects universal bans, deliberate overexposure, and fighting one's perceived constitution.
- Pattern construction: [[vol-126-tianre-mei-weikou-shuibuxing-fali-qiduan-zhongyi-jiao-ni-chushi-fangfa-zhongshu-jingshi-you-han-zaixian-ltokazmbcf7gttvl9s-xsypc6tzt]] attributes the cold-damp and damp-heat symptom map, spleen relationship, and food or herb matching to Mao Zhe.
- Safety boundary: [[vol-126-tianre-mei-weikou-shuibuxing-fali-qiduan-zhongyi-jiao-ni-chushi-fangfa-zhongshu-jingshi-you-han-zaixian-ltokazmbcf7gttvl9s-xsypc6tzt]] explicitly prioritizes timely medical care for serious illness and professional help for scraping while leaving mild-discomfort practices source-scoped.
- Rain exposure and foot care: [[vol-64-zhongyike-tongqin-lushang-linyu-tangshui-hou-jishi-zuo-zhe-liang-jian-shi-xiaji-futian-yangsheng-chi-zhexie-ba-lmefsxrzdkopwgt_bsxfdry539rk]] adds duration, region, contaminated-water cleaning, drying, wet-footwear, soaking, formulation, and impairment boundaries.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The sources supply no modern clinical evidence that their signs reliably identify cold-damp or damp-heat, that summer air-conditioning or direct fan exposure causes the proposed mechanisms, that rain-related symptoms require sweating, or that prior cold exposure explains most heat illness. They also do not establish the efficacy or safety of 藿香正气, induced sweating, foot soaking, ginger, lamb, porridge, American ginseng, dried citrus peel, cardamom, coix seed, adzuki bean, winter melon, bitter melon, watermelon, sour-plum drink, acupressure, or gua sha for a particular listener. Serious heat illness, significant contamination, skin injury, vascular risk, or worsening symptoms must not be delayed by pattern differentiation or lifestyle experimentation; alcohol-containing medicines and illness-related impairment also require conservative driving decisions.

## What Changed
- Added rain exposure, prompt foot cleaning and drying, and wet-footwear duration to the practical behavior layer.
- Expanded cold-damp and damp-heat qualification from heat illness to rain, soaking, sweating, food, and patent-medicine claims.
- Added vascular, contamination, medicine-formulation, impairment, and driving-safety boundaries.
