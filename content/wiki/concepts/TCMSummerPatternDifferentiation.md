# TCM Summer Pattern Differentiation / 中医夏季寒湿湿热辨别

## Definition
TCM summer pattern differentiation is [[MaoZheTCMDoctor|毛哲]]'s source-scoped framework for varying summer self-care according to cold-damp or damp-heat patterns, digestion, subjective constitution, environment, symptoms, and illness severity rather than applying one universal “dampness-removing” routine.

## Current Synthesis
The framework's most defensible behavioral contribution is anti-extremism: hot weather does not make deliberate overexposure to sun, total rejection of air conditioning, unrestricted cold food, forced sweating, or one recipe appropriate for everyone. The episode asks listeners to notice tolerance and context, use ordinary cooling and hydration sensibly, and place serious illness above lifestyle experimentation.

Its diagnostic and treatment content requires a firmer boundary. The source maps tongue coating, thirst, urine, bowel movements, sweating, limb temperature, fatigue, appetite, and heaviness onto cold-damp or damp-heat, then assigns foods, drinks, herbs, patent medicine, acupressure, and gua sha accordingly. These are TCM interpretations supplied by one podcast guest; the source provides no validation, diagnostic-performance data, doses, interaction review, or individualized examination. They therefore organize the episode's claims without becoming established clinical rules.

## Key Claims
- Summer self-care should vary by individual tolerance, symptoms, place, weather, and severity rather than follow a universal seasonal rule.
- Fatigue, heaviness, sleepiness, poor appetite, and head fog are treated as broad “dampness” experiences in the source but do not independently distinguish a pattern or establish a medical diagnosis.
- The episode's cold-damp versus damp-heat distinction is a TCM classification, not a substitute for evidence-based assessment of dehydration, infection, heat illness, gastrointestinal disease, medication effects, sleep problems, or other causes.
- Moderate sun, comfortable air conditioning without direct exposure, hydration, and non-extreme food choices form the practical lifestyle layer, while herb, medicine, scraping, and induced-sweating claims require greater caution.
- Severe or worsening heat-related symptoms override the self-care framework and require prompt qualified assessment.

- Pattern construction: [[vol-126-tianre-mei-weikou-shuibuxing-fali-qiduan-zhongyi-jiao-ni-chushi-fangfa-zhongshu-jingshi-you-han-zaixian-ltokazmbcf7gttvl9s-xsypc6tzt]] attributes the cold-damp and damp-heat symptom map, spleen relationship, and food or herb matching to Mao Zhe.
- Safety boundary: [[vol-126-tianre-mei-weikou-shuibuxing-fali-qiduan-zhongyi-jiao-ni-chushi-fangfa-zhongshu-jingshi-you-han-zaixian-ltokazmbcf7gttvl9s-xsypc6tzt]] explicitly prioritizes timely medical care for serious illness and professional help for scraping while leaving mild-discomfort practices source-scoped.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source supplies no modern clinical evidence that its signs reliably identify cold-damp or damp-heat, that summer air-conditioning exposure causes the proposed mechanism, or that prior cold exposure explains most heat illness. It also does not establish the efficacy or safety of 藿香正气, induced sweating, American ginseng, dried citrus peel, cardamom, coix seed, adzuki bean, winter melon, bitter melon, watermelon, sour-plum drink, acupressure, or gua sha for a particular listener. Serious heat illness can progress rapidly; pattern differentiation must not delay cooling, hydration, emergency response, or medical evaluation.

## What Changed
- Created a bounded concept for the episode's individualization and anti-extremism message.
- Separated the practical behavior layer from unvalidated TCM diagnostic and treatment claims.
- Made serious heat illness an explicit override to seasonal self-care experimentation.
