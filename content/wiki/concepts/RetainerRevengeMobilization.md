# Retainer Revenge Mobilization / 臣下复仇动员

Retainer revenge mobilization / 臣下复仇动员 is the pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-73-lishishang-ziyou-lianai-de-kaichuangzhe-shi-shui-lkumfsfialqgdl6qpb3c-hnz7h9u]] draws from [[WangSunGu|王孙古]]. After [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]] is killed, Wang Sun Gu's mother frames his inaction as a failure of subject duty. That shame becomes public action when he calls people together in the market.

The source's mechanism is conversion: private loyalty and household rebuke become a visible pledge, marked by baring the right arm. More than four hundred young men join Wang Sun Gu and attack [[ZhuoChi|淖齿 / 卓齿]], killing the man who had killed Qi Min Wang.

This is adjacent to [[RetainerReciprocityEthic]], but it is not only a one-person honor code. It names the moment when retainer duty recruits a temporary fighting public and changes the political facts on the ground, helping clear the path for [[TianFazhang|田法章]] to become [[QiXiangWang|齐襄王]].

## Key Claims
- Retainer revenge can start from shame before it becomes organized violence.
- A public pledge ritual can turn scattered resentment into a temporary force.
- The mobilization is politically consequential because it removes the immediate killer and forces the hostile army to withdraw.
- The concept should stay bounded to crisis politics; it does not make revenge a general legal norm.
