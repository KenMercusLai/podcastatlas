# Operational Gap Ownership / 主动补组织短板

Operational Gap Ownership / 主动补组织短板 is the management pattern introduced by [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-994-genzhe-dongwu-zhongchen-lvfan-xuexi-zhichang-zhihui-lgqp2vqmh1mss96gcwmflp-ccpfu|Hanji 994]] through [[LuFan|吕范]]. The pattern appears when a capable person identifies a real organizational gap, accepts the work even when it is low-status, and frames the task as protecting the shared mission rather than doing personal favor work for the boss.

In the source, [[SunCe|孙策]]'s expanding camp needs discipline. Lu Fan asks to serve as wartime `都督`, receives formal credentials, and makes the military camp more orderly. The host reads this as a workplace lesson: good strategy, culture, and制度 still need someone willing to drive enforcement and execution downward.

This concept is related to but narrower than [[ProactiveAdviserInitiative|主动型谋士补位]]. The earlier adviser concept centers on supplying strategic direction from a subordinate position; Operational Gap Ownership centers on taking responsibility for practical execution, discipline, and coordination work that may not carry prestige.

## Key Claims
- Low-status tasks can be strategically important when they remove an execution bottleneck.
- A subordinate's initiative is stronger when framed through shared fate, not personal sacrifice theater.
- Formal authority still matters: Lu Fan's request becomes effective after Sun Ce grants credentials and office.
- Execution capacity complements talent attraction; a leader's good personnel choices still need someone who can enforce order.
