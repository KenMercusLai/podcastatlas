# Qi Wei Wang Political Turnaround / 齐威王一鸣惊人式转向

Qi Wei Wang political turnaround / 齐威王一鸣惊人式转向 is the leadership pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-14-qiweiwang-buming-zeyi-yiming-jingren-lrs6rbifyiqrojxg0mccpg7sf9rv]] takes from [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]]'s "不鸣则已，一鸣惊人" story. The episode first presents the crisis: Qi Wei Wang drinks, neglects state affairs, delegates to [[ChenLiao|陈聊]], and leaves [[QiState|齐国]] exposed to neighbors.

After [[ChunyuKun|淳于髡]]'s [[AllusiveRemonstrance|隐语进谏]], the same ruler abruptly shifts into active rule. He audits local officials, rewards [[JimoDafu|即墨大夫]], punishes [[AyiDafu|阿邑大夫]], reorganizes military capacity, and forces neighboring states to return territory.

The source keeps one interpretive possibility open: Qi Wei Wang may have been deliberately appearing useless until the moment was right. The wiki records that as a bounded hypothesis, not as established fact.

## Key Claims
- A ruler's dramatic turnaround should be judged by institutional actions, not only by a clever phrase.
- The official-audit story makes the turnaround administrative, while the military recovery makes it strategic.
- Remonstrance can trigger change, but it does not prove the ruler had no prior plan.
- The "strategic feigning" reading remains possible but source-scoped.
