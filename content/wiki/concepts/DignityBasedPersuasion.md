# Dignity-Based Persuasion / 尊严激将式说服

Dignity-based persuasion / 尊严激将式说服 is the repeated speech pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-32-shishang-shouwei-lianheguo-mishuzhang-suqin-lgfkk45yq1c0yblequ-d1t7mol3]] highlights in [[SuQin|苏秦]]'s hezong tour. Su Qin does not rely only on threat analysis. He first praises a ruler's state strength, then argues that serving [[QinState|秦国]] means endless concessions, and finally makes submission feel like a loss of face that a worthy ruler should refuse.

The clearest case is [[HanXuanhuiwang|韩宣惠王]]. Su Qin says Han's land is finite while Qin's appetite is not, then caps the argument with the "宁为鸡口，无为牛后" proverb. The Qi case adjusts the same method to [[Linzi|临淄]]: the city's population, prosperity, entertainment, and confident public life become evidence that [[QiState|齐国]] should not lower itself before Qin.

## Key Claims
- Material argument and status provocation work together: the ruler must see both the strategic cost and the humiliation of submission.
- The pattern belongs inside [[ChuaimoTechnique|揣摩]] because the dignity lever changes by audience.
- In this episode, dignity rhetoric helps turn [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] from a strategic plan into an emotionally acceptable choice for multiple rulers.
- The method is not moral idealism. It is a practical way to make resistance feel safer and more honorable than appeasement.
