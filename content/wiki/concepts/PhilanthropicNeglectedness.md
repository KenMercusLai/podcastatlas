# Philanthropic Neglectedness

## Definition
Philanthropic neglectedness is the principle that a funding target's value depends not only on importance but also on how much attention, money, talent, and workable capacity it already receives.

## Current Synthesis
The source treats neglectedness as dynamic marginal analysis. Early AI safety or longevity work could be attractive because a small contribution might change a thin field; once a cause becomes crowded, the next dollar may matter less. Global public health can become neglected again as political support declines. Resource scarcity and method failure must also be separated because more money does not repair a wrong approach.

## Key Claims
- Importance alone does not determine a donation's marginal value.
- Cause priority changes with funding, talent, politics, and institutional capacity.
- A thin field can become crowded, while a mature field can become neglected again.
- Resource shortage must be distinguished from methodological failure.
- Donors need monitoring rather than permanent loyalty to one issue or institution.

## Evidence
- **Changing balance:** [[e257-duihua-vitalik-ai-anquan-beihoude-zhexue-sixiang-youxiao-lita-zhuyi-ruhe-yingxiang-guigu-7a1e61d3-f02c-498b-ac84-cf68f59b4ca9]] attributes to [[VitalikButerin|Vitalik Buterin]] the judgment that AI safety is less neglected while public-health gaps have re-emerged.
- **Institutional drift:** [[e257-duihua-vitalik-ai-anquan-beihoude-zhexue-sixiang-youxiao-lita-zhuyi-ruhe-yingxiang-guigu-7a1e61d3-f02c-498b-ac84-cf68f59b4ca9]] uses his disagreement with the [[FutureOfLifeInstitute|Future of Life Institute]] to show why donors reassess direction.
- **Method boundary:** [[e257-duihua-vitalik-ai-anquan-beihoude-zhexue-sixiang-youxiao-lita-zhuyi-ruhe-yingxiang-guigu-7a1e61d3-f02c-498b-ac84-cf68f59b4ca9]] distinguishes problems needing resources from problems using the wrong method.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode does not quantify marginal impact or establish that every AI-safety subfield is adequately funded. Attention, spending, tractability, talent, legitimacy, and useful capacity differ. Donor disagreement does not prove misuse or low impact.
