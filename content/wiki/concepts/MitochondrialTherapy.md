# Mitochondrial Therapy

Mitochondrial therapy is the longevity modality discussed in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bryan-johnson-i-just-took-the-most-powerful-dose-of-dmt-in-the-world-heres-what-it-was-like-40626165]] after the psychedelic section. [[BryanJohnson|Bryan Johnson]] says he has a mitochondrial therapy lined up with a third-party company, while the hosts discuss transporting younger or better-matched mitochondria into cells and limiting immune attack.

The concept belongs in the wiki as a frontier intervention rather than a settled protocol. The episode mentions possible sprint-performance measurement and maternal matching ideas, but does not report completed outcome data. That keeps mitochondrial therapy tied to [[SelfExperimentation]], [[MedicalRiskManagement]], and [[ClinicalDevelopmentCapability]] rather than ordinary health advice.

## Key Claims
- The source frames mitochondria as a performance and aging target because they shape cellular energy capacity.
- Delivery, immune compatibility, source matching, and measurement are presented as central unresolved issues.
- Johnson's planned intervention is a source-scoped self-experiment, not evidence that the modality works generally.
- The modality sits beside gene therapy, cell therapy, [[PatientDerivedOrganoids|organoid testing]], and cellular reprogramming inside the episode's longevity stack.
