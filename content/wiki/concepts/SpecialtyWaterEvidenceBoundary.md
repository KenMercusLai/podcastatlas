# Specialty Water Evidence Boundary

## Definition
The specialty water evidence boundary separates changes in water composition or laboratory behavior from demonstrated absorption, biomarker, clinical, or long-term health outcomes.

## Current Synthesis
[[how-to-optimize-your-water-quality-intake-for-health-scim6169879512]] discusses alkaline, hydrogen-rich, electrolyzed reduced, deuterium-depleted, and structured water without treating them as one category. It rejects the simple claim that alkaline water meaningfully alkalizes the whole body, while proposing that pH may affect aquaporin transport or gut absorption. It also describes a short hydrogen-rich-water study with inflammatory and apoptosis markers and acknowledges that strong peer-reviewed evidence for desired biological outcomes from ingesting structured water is essentially absent.

The boundary is therefore outcome-specific: cleaner filtration or mineral composition may explain some perceived benefits; mechanistic plausibility does not establish clinical value; short biomarker studies do not prove durable benefit; and the existence of molecular ordering near surfaces does not show that bottled or prepared structured water improves human health.

## Key Claims
- Whole-body pH regulation should not be confused with the local pH or composition of drinking water.
- Proposed aquaporin or absorption effects require direct human evidence before becoming health recommendations.
- Hydrogen-rich water has limited study signals, but short biomarker changes do not establish broad clinical benefit.
- Deuterium-depleted and electrolyzed reduced water claims need product-, dose-, comparator-, and outcome-specific evidence.
- Structured water as a physical proposal is distinct from proof that ingesting a marketed product produces a desired biological outcome.

## Evidence
- Alkaline-water distinction: [[how-to-optimize-your-water-quality-intake-for-health-scim6169879512]] says alkaline water does not meaningfully alkalize the whole body while proposing faster aquaporin-mediated absorption at higher pH.
- Hydrogen-water signal: [[how-to-optimize-your-water-quality-intake-for-health-scim6169879512]] summarizes a four-week, 1.5-liter-per-day study reporting inflammatory and apoptosis-marker changes, with benefits described only in participants older than 30.
- Structured-water boundary: [[how-to-optimize-your-water-quality-intake-for-health-scim6169879512]] acknowledges little strong peer-reviewed evidence that ingesting structured water produces specific desired biological outcomes.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source note does not provide study citations, randomization details, sample sizes, comparators, effect sizes, replication, adverse events, or clinical endpoints. Its claims about aquaporins, pH, immune function, antioxidant behavior, molecular hydrogen, and deuterium remain source-scoped. Magnesium-based hydrogen tablets are not ordinary magnesium supplements, and product instructions, contraindications, and qualified medical advice still matter.

## What Changed
- Added an evidence hierarchy for alkaline, hydrogen-rich, deuterium-depleted, electrolyzed, and structured waters.
- Separated physical or biomarker claims from clinical outcomes and whole-body alkalization.
