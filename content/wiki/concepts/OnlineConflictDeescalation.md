# Online Conflict De-escalation / 网络冲突降温

## Definition
Online conflict de-escalation is the practice of preserving response choice under digital provocation through delay, perspective shift, selective engagement, humor where safe, evidence preservation, platform boundaries, and proportionate legal escalation.

## Current Synthesis
The current source rejects automatic reciprocal attack. A hostile comment can trigger fight, flight, or freezing, and immediate retaliation can copy the attacker's method while surrendering control of attention. Delay creates a gap in which the recipient can distinguish correction from abuse, decide whether a response serves the audience or purpose, and notice whether the commenter retracts or deletes an impulsive remark.

Understanding the attacker's possible recognition or superiority needs can reduce personal capture without excusing harm. Humor and reframing can sometimes lower emotional charge, and criticism may occasionally reveal a need for clearer public communication, but neither move requires accepting harassment. Ignoring, deleting, blocking, documenting, reporting, and legal recourse remain available according to severity; the governing principle is proportionate choice, not passive endurance.

## Key Claims
- Immediate reciprocal attack can reproduce the aggressor's behavior and narrow the recipient's response options.
- Delay and cooling-off time help separate useful correction, noise, and abuse before action.
- Perspective-taking can reduce emotional capture without requiring agreement, forgiveness, or continued access.
- Humor and reframing are optional tools, not duties imposed on a target.
- Non-engagement, platform moderation, documentation, and legal recourse belong on one proportional response ladder.
- Public educators can learn from valid criticism while refusing personal attack and maintaining audience-accessible communication.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not compare intervention effectiveness or specify legal thresholds across jurisdictions. Humor, reframing, or waiting may be inappropriate when threats, doxxing, stalking, discrimination, coordinated harassment, or acute safety risks require immediate documentation and escalation. Psychological interpretation should not become victim-blaming, compulsory empathy, or pressure to keep an aggressor in one's audience.

## What Changed
- Established a proportional ladder from delay and perspective shift to moderation, documentation, and legal escalation.
- Clarified that understanding motive does not require access, agreement, or forgiveness.
- Kept humor and learning from criticism optional and severity-dependent.
