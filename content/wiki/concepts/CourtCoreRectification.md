## Definition

朝廷心腹整顿 / Court-Core Rectification is the governance claim that local order depends first on the integrity of central offices, inner-court access, appointment rules, and command channels, because the court functions as the state's heart, source, root, and measuring standard.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-814-dangguan-buwei-min-zuozhu-buru-huijia-mai-hongshu-lqlrfpuenzhr1tbrdd3urtolfw2p|Hanji 814]] grounds the concept through [[LiGuLateHan|李固]]'s response to [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]] after the Xuande Hall ground split. Li Gu argues from a series of metaphors: Shangshu is the ruler's throat and tongue, the court is the heart and belly, provinces and commanderies are the limbs, the upper standard shapes the lower line, the water source shapes the flow, and the tree root shapes the branches. The point is institutional priority, not poetic ornament: if the center is ill, local officials cannot remain healthy for long.

This does not mean that local governance is unimportant. Li Gu's later wiki profile shows real local pacification work. In Hanji 814, however, he treats disasters, bandits, floods, and droughts as secondary to a corrupted central command core. That priority links [[OmenCodedRemonstrance|天象灾异包装式进谏]] with [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|君臣反馈失灵]]: disaster gives the ruler a reason to ask, but the answer is to repair the court before blaming only outer problems.

## Key Claims

- Central offices shape local governance because orders, appointments, and review standards flow outward from the court.
- Shangshu and near-ruler offices are high-risk posts because they receive memorials and transmit imperial commands.
- Metaphors of heart, limbs, source, root, and measuring standard all make the same institutional claim: a crooked center reproduces crooked execution.
- Court repair should include personnel selection, fair command channels, and reduced inner-court interference.
- Local crises such as theft, disaster, drought, and rebellion can remain symptoms if the central appointment and feedback structure is diseased.
- The concept emphasizes priority of repair, not the irrelevance of local officials or local causes.

## Evidence

- Shangshu centrality: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-814-dangguan-buwei-min-zuozhu-buru-huijia-mai-hongshu-lqlrfpuenzhr1tbrdd3urtolfw2p|Hanji 814]] says Li Gu compares Shangshu to the Northern Dipper and the ruler's throat and tongue because they receive memorials and transmit commands.
- Center-local body metaphor: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-814-dangguan-buwei-min-zuozhu-buru-huijia-mai-hongshu-lqlrfpuenzhr1tbrdd3urtolfw2p|Hanji 814]] says Li Gu calls the court the heart and belly and the provinces-commandories the limbs.
- Source and root metaphors: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-814-dangguan-buwei-min-zuozhu-buru-huijia-mai-hongshu-lqlrfpuenzhr1tbrdd3urtolfw2p|Hanji 814]] uses standard, water-source, and tree-root language to argue that lower levels follow the condition of the upper level.
- Inner-court reduction: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-814-dangguan-buwei-min-zuozhu-buru-huijia-mai-hongshu-lqlrfpuenzhr1tbrdd3urtolfw2p|Hanji 814]] has Li Gu propose reducing eunuch and inner-court posts to a small number of upright or capable attendants.
- Priority claim: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-814-dangguan-buwei-min-zuozhu-buru-huijia-mai-hongshu-lqlrfpuenzhr1tbrdd3urtolfw2p|Hanji 814]] says that after the court's heart and belly are settled, theft, flood, and drought would not be the greatest worry.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept is not a license to explain every local failure only by the capital. Hanji 814 gives Li Gu's court-facing argument during a disaster inquiry, so the diagnosis is shaped by that political occasion. Other wiki pages show local taxation, frontier command, corruption, military finance, and official fit as real causes. Court-core rectification should therefore be used as a priority rule for central repair, not as a complete causal map of all disorder.

## What Changed

- Established Li Gu's heart-belly and limbs metaphor as a standalone institutional concept.
- Connected Shangshu integrity, inner-court reduction, and appointment rules as one central-court repair program.
- Qualified the claim so it does not erase local causes already tracked in frontier, taxation, and pacification pages.
