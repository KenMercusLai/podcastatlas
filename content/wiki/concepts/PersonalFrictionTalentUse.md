## Definition

Personal friction–talent use separation is the leadership judgment that dislike, disrespect, or prior opposition should not automatically erase a person's task-relevant value, while neither usefulness nor independence excuses harmful conduct.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 1145 develops the concept through [[LiuBei|刘备]] and [[LiuBa|刘巴]]. Liu Ba reportedly avoids Liu Bei, warns Liu Zhang against him, and later insults [[ZhangFei|张飞]] through silence and status contempt. Liu Bei is angered but still protects and employs him because the new Yizhou regime faces a fiscal problem for which Liu Ba has scarce expertise.

The useful distinction is not between emotion and emotionlessness. A leader can recognize an insult, preserve boundaries, and still ask whether the organization needs the person's capacity. The same case also limits the principle: Sun Quan's defense of Liu Ba's independence does not settle whether refusing ordinary respect was justified, and later utility does not retroactively make contempt virtuous.

## Key Claims

- Personal offense and task-relevant capability are different evaluation dimensions.
- Prior political opposition does not by itself establish present uselessness or disloyalty.
- Retention is strongest when a concrete organizational need matches demonstrable expertise.
- Refusing retaliation does not require approving contemptuous behavior.
- Independence from flattery and basic interpersonal respect can conflict and require separate judgment.

## Evidence

- Prior opposition: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1145-liubei-jiao-ni-ruhe-yingdui-bishi-ziji-de-ren-lo1sgktqtshlndfbbeb2wh15ijwa|Hanji 1145]] presents Liu Ba's avoidance of Liu Bei and repeated warnings to Liu Zhang before his eventual incorporation.
- Personal friction: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1145-liubei-jiao-ni-ruhe-yingdui-bishi-ziji-de-ren-lo1sgktqtshlndfbbeb2wh15ijwa|Hanji 1145]] reports Liu Ba's refusal to converse with Zhang Fei and Liu Bei's anger at the insult.
- Capability-based retention: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1145-liubei-jiao-ni-ruhe-yingdui-bishi-ziji-de-ren-lo1sgktqtshlndfbbeb2wh15ijwa|Hanji 1145]] links Liu Bei's continued use of Liu Ba to an urgent post-conquest fiscal shortage and Liu Ba's relevant expertise.
- Competing interpretation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1145-liubei-jiao-ni-ruhe-yingdui-bishi-ziji-de-ren-lo1sgktqtshlndfbbeb2wh15ijwa|Hanji 1145]] contrasts Zhang Zhao's criticism of narrowness with Sun Quan's defense of scholarly independence.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept rests on one popular-history summary and does not establish Liu Bei's private reasoning, Liu Ba's complete conduct, or a general causal rule. “Useful talent” language can excuse abuse, factional sabotage, or unequal standards if capability is asserted rather than demonstrated. The case supports bounded, need-specific employment—not immunity from accountability, unlimited trust, or the claim that interpersonal respect is irrelevant.

## What Changed

- Established a bounded distinction among personal offense, political opposition, interpersonal accountability, and task-relevant capability.
