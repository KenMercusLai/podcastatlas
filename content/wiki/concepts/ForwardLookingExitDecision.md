# 向前看的退出决策 / Forward-Looking Exit Decision

## Definition
向前看的退出决策 / forward-looking exit decision is the practice of judging whether to stop, change fields, or release a path from today's expected cost, value, motive, and downside rather than using the future to vindicate an irreversible past.

## Current Synthesis
The [[TianzhenButianzhen|天真不天真]] episode uses a proposed midlife move from an established technology-company role into psychology to separate role loss from capability loss. Title, employer prestige, current income, and automatic deference may disappear; judgment, leadership, commercial understanding, relationships, and experience remain portable, although their value in the new field has not yet been proved.

The decision is therefore neither a literal reset nor a celebration of courage for its own sake. It asks whether the person is moving toward a tested interest or merely away from dissatisfaction, whether savings and family obligations can absorb three to five years of weaker income, whether the desired outcome is research, practice, learning, or status, and whether the process remains worthwhile without quick recognition. Prior success is an asset when it expands these choices and a burden when it makes ordinary status, lower income, or beginnerhood intolerable.

## Key Claims
- External role and portable capability should be evaluated separately during a transition.
- Courage is a cost a person may accept for a chosen end, not sufficient evidence that the end is worthwhile.
- Testing the unglamorous parts of a field before a full exit helps distinguish attraction from escape.
- Economic, vocational, and psychological downside should be measured before the decision, including the least favorable acceptable result.
- Past investment is irreversible; the live comparison is between future commitments and future alternatives.
- A prior success becomes constraining when preserving its income, status, or self-image closes otherwise valuable choices.

## Evidence
### Capability survives role loss
- [[vol-58-youwenbida-cong-1-dao-0-ruhe-fangqi-que-bu-fouding-ziji-ljf7qgb2ckoql1xo5bkcqq6pgcin|vol.58]] distinguishes a disappearing title or employer halo from accumulated commercial, leadership, relationship, and judgment capacity.

### Motive and field testing
- [[vol-58-youwenbida-cong-1-dao-0-ruhe-fangqi-que-bu-fouding-ziji-ljf7qgb2ckoql1xo5bkcqq6pgcin|vol.58]] recommends part-time study, courses, research, or practice before resigning so interest includes the field's tedious work rather than only its imagined identity.

### Three-part downside measurement
- [[vol-58-youwenbida-cong-1-dao-0-ruhe-fangqi-que-bu-fouding-ziji-ljf7qgb2ckoql1xo5bkcqq6pgcin|vol.58]] separates economic runway, vocational purpose, and the ability to continue without external validation.

### Forward cost and stopping rules
- [[vol-58-youwenbida-cong-1-dao-0-ruhe-fangqi-que-bu-fouding-ziji-ljf7qgb2ckoql1xo5bkcqq6pgcin|vol.58]] redirects attention from whether the prior fifteen years were worthwhile to the cost of the next commitment and recommends defining conditions for stopping before emotional escalation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Portable capability does not guarantee employment, clinical qualification, income, recognition, or fit in a new field. Age can bring self-knowledge and resources while also increasing caregiving duties, financial pressure, health constraints, and the opportunity cost of training. A forward-looking decision can rationally favor staying when future value remains positive; the framework rejects automatic continuation and automatic exit alike.
