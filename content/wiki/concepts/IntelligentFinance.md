# Intelligent Finance

Intelligent finance is [[WuKai]]'s Airwallex thesis in [[11-nian-110-yi-meijin-ranhou-ne-duihua-airwallex-wu-kai-ai-shidai-xiayizhan-1000-yi-lr4tvdrq25by7fugoqkqojw6vwdk]]: a finance platform can combine accounts, payments, FX, cards, acquiring, capital products, transaction data, policies, and AI agents into a larger operating entry point for business finance. In this framing, [[Airwallex]] wants to move from being a [[GlobalFinancialNetwork]] toward a financial intelligence layer.

The source distinguishes this from a generic chatbot. [[KaiAirwallex]] is embedded in the web app; [[AirwallexAgentOS]] exposes callable financial actions; [[T0Finance]] tries to automate finance operations for young companies; and [[ARID]] moves checkout and future agent payment into the same strategy. The claim is that broad data and transaction authority matter because isolated finance SaaS tools each see only part of the workflow.

## Key Claims
- AI can lower the threshold for finance automation because smaller companies may no longer need a dedicated engineering team to connect APIs and workflows.
- Finance agents need transaction data, policy context, approval logic, accounts, and payment authority; text reasoning alone is not enough.
- Narrow finance SaaS tools may be absorbed or weakened if a broader system can deliver reconciliation, reporting, cash forecasting, payment, and policy work through one agent entry.
- Proprietary workflow data becomes strategically important because financial agents must learn how real businesses recognize revenue, approve expenses, handle exceptions, and manage cash.
- Human confirmation and audit remain central because financial actions move money, change records, and create liability.
