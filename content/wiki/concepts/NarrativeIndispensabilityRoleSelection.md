# Narrative Indispensability Role Selection

## Definition
Narrative indispensability role selection is an actor's criterion for judging a part by whether removing the character would materially change the story, rather than by lead status, billing, screen time, or surface likability.

## Current Synthesis
[[NeilNitinMukesh]] describes role value through causal contribution. A brief or supporting character may be attractive when it reorganizes the plot, changes other characters' choices, or supplies information without which the story would differ. Conversely, substantial screen time can be weak if the character is removable without consequence.

The criterion links artistic judgment to career strategy. An unconventional debut or compact antagonist role can display range more clearly than a generic lead, and working with strong collaborators may supply learning beyond the part's length. Yet indispensability is not fully objective: scripts change, ensemble stories distribute causality, commercial positioning matters, and an actor's interpretation can overstate a role's necessity.

## Key Claims
- Story consequence is distinct from screen time, billing, protagonist status, and moral likability.
- A brief character can be central when removing it would alter plot logic or other characters' decisions.
- Unconventional or antagonistic roles can demonstrate range more effectively than a conventional launch part.
- Role choice can include learning from collaborators as well as the character's visible size.
- Narrative necessity is a judgment under script and production uncertainty, not a mechanically measurable property.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The framework comes from one actor's retrospective and does not establish that consequence-led choices consistently improve career outcomes, artistic quality, pay, or audience response. A character can be thematically rich without driving plot, while ensemble or atmospheric works may resist a removal test. Actors may also lack sufficient access to apply the criterion freely.

## What Changed
- Created a role-selection framework separating narrative consequence from role size.
- Added learning value and access constraints as qualifications to the removal test.
