# Low-Data Media Access

Low-data media access is the advantage of information channels that work when mobile data is expensive, unreliable, or unnecessary. In [[in-arms-way-gaza-deal-sticking-points-6a71b49647235c354d3c9b74]], FM radio remains useful across Africa because cheap phones often include radios and listening does not consume mobile data.

The concept complements [[AfricaConnectivityInfrastructure]]. Faster broadband, fiber, and satellite links matter for heavy digital uses, but the source shows that low-data or no-data channels can still be the most robust way to reach people during commutes, traffic jams, or weak connectivity.

## Key Claims
- Access should be measured by actual cost and device fit, not only by whether a newer digital medium exists.
- Low-data media can remain competitive for news and live conversation because the listener's friction is low.
- Traffic and commuting can create recurring listening slots that video or text media do not fill as easily.
- Digital media can coexist with low-data broadcast media when presenters and stations use social platforms for reach and monetization.
