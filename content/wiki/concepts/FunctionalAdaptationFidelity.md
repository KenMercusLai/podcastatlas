## Definition

Functional adaptation fidelity is fidelity to a source work's character logic, narrative effects, growth, and thematic function rather than to literal event order, dialogue, or exhaustive plot coverage.

## Current Synthesis

The bounded [[LegendOfTheCondorHeroes|《射雕英雄传》]] discussion treats adaptation as reconstruction under a new medium's constraints. Novels can explain motive through narration and interiority, while screen stories must make motive perceptible through speech, acting, staging, editing, and action. A changed scene can therefore be more functionally faithful than a literal one if it lets viewers understand the same concealed knowledge, relationship development, or decision logic.

This standard does not license arbitrary rewriting. Changes still have to produce coherent conflict and resolution, preserve the character tensions that drive action, and sustain the work's larger arc. The sources' criticism of “魔改” is thus directed less at deviation itself than at deviations that flatten motives, lose causal support, or replace earned wuxia development with generic fantasy spectacle.

The chronological survey sharpens the distinction by comparing the 1983 and 2017 television versions. A visibly altered sequence can remain faithful when its new causal chain is coherent, while a production can reproduce the same event but remove the context that explains why it occurs. It also separates direct fidelity from artistic value: a work such as 《东邪西毒》 can achieve powerful cinema through transformation without serving as a close retelling.

The [[BiaoRenComic|《镖人》]] comparison adds a single film in which different functions transfer unevenly. [[BiaoRenFilm2026|《镖人：风起大漠》]] rebuilds 地听's motive and final-opponent role coherently and preserves martial pleasure through [[TraditionalWuxiaActionCraft|readable action]], yet cuts much of the imperial pressure, rebel philosophy, religious background, and ensemble history that explain why other characters follow, refuse, or sacrifice. It also shows that event retention is not enough: 老莫's death remains in the plot but loses force when prior relationship development, framing, pacing, and reactions are weakened.

A supernatural-romance case adds a further split. In [[AChineseGhostStory1987|《倩女幽魂》(1987)]], Ning Caichen's softened characterization, reciprocal protection, and the move from household reunion to tragic release create clear emotional causality. Yan Chixia's tolerated predation and the sudden power jump in the Black Mountain finale leave adjacent behavioral and action functions under-prepared. Fidelity can therefore succeed in romance while failing in world and climax logic.

The [[TsuiHark|徐克]] career retrospective generalizes the test across radical wuxia transformations. A major departure can establish an autonomous classic when performance, image, emotion, and new dramatic logic cohere; literal loyalty is not the controlling value. The same retrospective preserves the negative boundary: truncated ensemble arcs, missing character motivation, or spectacle without tension remain functional losses even when art, action, music, or technology are individually impressive.

The focused critique of [[LegendOfTheCondorHeroesTheGallants2025|《射雕英雄传：侠之大者》]] tests that boundary within a late-chapter film. The starting point can in principle concentrate “何为英雄,” war, and the use of power, but it also removes the prior formation that gives those questions weight. According to the hosts, a rapid flashback preserves names and incidents without restoring grief, intimacy, hierarchy, or cause; effects-led action and a final appeal to protect ordinary people then state functions the intervening film has not rebuilt. Partial success in 华筝, Mongolian characterization, supporting performance, language, and music does not convert the total adaptation into a coherent transfer.

## Key Claims

- Literal similarity and functional fidelity are separate judgments; event fidelity without causal context can be less faithful than coherent rewriting.
- A successful change preserves or rebuilds character causality in a form viewers can perceive.
- Compression requires selection, but selection should preserve the causal and relational support that makes motives, political stakes, growth, and emotional turns intelligible.
- Casting resemblance cannot substitute for performance of a character's internal contrasts.
- Screen conflict must be set up and resolved as drama rather than relying on the novel's explanatory authority.
- Thematic fidelity must be earned through the adapted plot rather than asserted as a slogan.
- Radical transformation can create an autonomous classic, but iconic imagery does not excuse missing motive, ensemble development, or dramatic tension.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept is derived from six informal, multi-host comparisons whose judgments vary by performer, version, nostalgia, source familiarity, and personal taste. “Character core” can become a vague defense unless tied to setup, motive, choice, consequence, staging, and reaction. Success in action, romance, image, music, or one rewritten character does not erase losses in politics, language, pacing, ensemble structure, world logic, or cultural texture. Artistic autonomy, commercial effectiveness, and fidelity remain separate axes, while production, deletion, censorship, and authorship claims remain source-scoped.
