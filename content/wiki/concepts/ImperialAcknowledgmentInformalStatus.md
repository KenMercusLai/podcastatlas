## Definition

帝王承认与非正式身份 / imperial acknowledgment of informal status is the process by which a ruler's public words, gestures, seating, dress, hospitality, or access privileges make an otherwise private and institutionally ambiguous relationship socially recognizable without creating a regular office or title.

## Current Synthesis

The current case is [[DongYanWesternHan|董偃]]. [[LiuPiaoWesternHan|馆陶长公主刘嫖]] can support him through household wealth and proximity, but those resources alone do not settle how outsiders should treat her companion. [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] changes that field by asking to see the “主人翁,” raising Dong Yan, granting clothing, permitting him to sit and drink, and treating the couple's arrangement as publicly tolerable.

Recognition here is performative rather than statutory. Liu Piao and Dong Yan first lower themselves through servant clothing, bare-footed apology, prostration, and plain presentation; Wudi can then elevate them through visible clemency and favor. The ritual preserves hierarchy while authorizing an exception.

The effect is real but bounded. The first episode says others can no longer lightly despise Dong Yan after the emperor's acknowledgment, yet it does not show that he gains a defined legal status, office, or durable immunity. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-271-xihan-mishi-wudi-yuemu-yu-yangzi-de-bulunlian-2-loajctyv6no5zfho92kces8pbiel|Hanji 271-2]] supplies the missing limit: [[DongfangShuo|东方朔]] blocks Dong Yan from a main-hall banquet, Wudi moves it to the North Palace, and favor reportedly cools afterward.

Recognition is therefore contextual as well as informal. Permission to sit and drink in one audience does not create unrestricted entry to every state venue. [[CourtCeremonialSpaceBoundary|Ceremonial space]] can narrow an acknowledged relationship without requiring the ruler to repudiate it completely.

## Key Claims

- A ruler's public address and treatment can make an ambiguous private relationship socially legible without issuing a formal title.
- Ceremonial submission can lower the political cost of granting an exceptional favor by reaffirming the ruler's superior authority.
- Clothing, seating, drinking, bodily gesture, and naming can carry status effects when performed before a court audience.
- Recognition can deter social disrespect while leaving legal category, office, succession, and long-term protection unresolved.
- Acknowledgment in one setting does not guarantee admission to a more formal state venue or immunity from later remonstrance.
- The mechanism requires evidence of a visible acknowledgment act, not merely private affection or household patronage.

## Evidence

Public naming and treatment:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-271-xihan-mishi-wudi-yuemu-yu-yangzi-de-bulunlian-1-loeg6lrncynofm27ekpgtjkodrwf|Hanji 271]] treats Wudi's “主人翁” address, clothing grant, shared seating, and banquet participation as the acts that change Dong Yan's public standing.

Hierarchy-preserving submission:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-271-xihan-mishi-wudi-yuemu-yu-yangzi-de-bulunlian-1-loeg6lrncynofm27ekpgtjkodrwf|Hanji 271]] says Liu Piao removes ornaments and shoes and Dong Yan appears as a servant and prostrates before Wudi raises and dresses him.

Social consequence:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-271-xihan-mishi-wudi-yuemu-yu-yangzi-de-bulunlian-1-loeg6lrncynofm27ekpgtjkodrwf|Hanji 271]] explicitly interprets imperial acknowledgment as the reason other people can no longer dismiss Dong Yan.

Contextual limit:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-271-xihan-mishi-wudi-yuemu-yu-yangzi-de-bulunlian-2-loajctyv6no5zfho92kces8pbiel|Hanji 271-2]] says Dongfang Shuo prevents Dong Yan's entry to Weiyang Palace's main hall and Wudi relocates the banquet to the North Palace.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept currently rests on two popular-history summaries from one series and one court-favorite case. The first interprets “主人翁” as legalization, but the evidence establishes visible favor more clearly than a defined legal category; the second's successful venue restriction reinforces that distinction. Exact dialogue, audience and banquet sequence, emotional reactions, public response, and the cause of fading favor remain source-scoped. Future cases should distinguish acknowledgment from office, ennoblement, marriage recognition, legal immunity, unrestricted access, or durable succession rights.

## What Changed

- Qualified performative recognition as venue-specific, reversible, and open to later institutional restriction.
