# Materialized Memory Worldbuilding / 记忆实体化世界构建

## Definition

Materialized-memory worldbuilding is a speculative-fiction device in which remembered scenes become spatially and physically accessible, allowing characters to encounter or alter a composite past without claiming that they have entered an ordinary historical location.

## Current Synthesis

In [[KunlunShrineGhostBlowsOutTheLight|《昆仑神宫》]], as reconstructed by [[105-quhua-gui-chuideng-zhi-kunlun-shengong-p2-jiuceng-yaota-yu-jiyi-zhicheng-lp6vqufwvlwdl8tcrchvk7j285we]], 恶罗海城 first looks like a city arrested at one impossible instant: lamps burn without residents, beef remains hot, and a severed ox head appears to continue dying. Yet eaten food does not return, the visitors' watches keep moving, and the outside world changes, weakening a simple time-freeze explanation.

The later mural explanation treats the city as a physical composite assembled from a ghost mother's salient memories through the snake god's fictional “行境幻化” power. Hot food, a blood handprint, and an unfinished slaughter can come from different remembered moments while occupying one navigable setting. The device therefore combines subjective selection with objective consequence: memory determines what appears, but the explorers can touch, consume, and change what has appeared.

This mechanism is useful because it converts exposition about a lost civilization into an environment that characters must interpret. Its cost is ontological strain. Once memory can become durable matter, the story needs rules for persistence, alteration, ownership, and escape; otherwise “memory” risks becoming a label for unrestricted supernatural convenience.

## Key Claims

- A materialized memory can combine non-simultaneous fragments without behaving like a documentary reconstruction of one moment.
- Physical interaction distinguishes the device from hallucination, dream, or purely visual replay.
- Contradictory temporal clues can operate as evidence against simple time stoppage and toward a composite-memory explanation.
- The remembered environment reveals selection and salience as well as historical setting.
- Strong versions of the device need constraints on persistence, alteration, entry, and exit to keep speculation from becoming arbitrary.

## Evidence

### Composite remembered space

- [[105-quhua-gui-chuideng-zhi-kunlun-shengong-p2-jiuceng-yaota-yu-jiyi-zhicheng-lp6vqufwvlwdl8tcrchvk7j285we]] presents the hot beef, blood handprint, lit buildings, and slaughterhouse as fragments that may derive from different moments remembered by the ghost mother.

### Physical but not ordinarily historical

- [[105-quhua-gui-chuideng-zhi-kunlun-shengong-p2-jiuceng-yaota-yu-jiyi-zhicheng-lp6vqufwvlwdl8tcrchvk7j285we]] says the food can be eaten and does not reset, even though the city is not the original archaeological site.

### Explanatory escalation

- [[105-quhua-gui-chuideng-zhi-kunlun-shengong-p2-jiuceng-yaota-yu-jiyi-zhicheng-lp6vqufwvlwdl8tcrchvk7j285we]] uses normal watch movement and changing external light to reject a total time-freeze hypothesis before introducing “行境幻化.”

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept is derived from one podcast's account of one fictional setting. “虚数空间,” preserved snake-god consciousness, immortal spirit, and memory-to-matter conversion are story-world language, not physics or neuroscience. The hosts themselves divide over whether touchable, edible memory is imaginative or implausible, and the supplied source does not establish a complete rule set for how the city persists or changes.

## What Changed

- Created the concept from 恶罗海城's shift from apparent time anomaly to physically accessible composite memory.
- Distinguished materialized memory from hallucination, replay, time stoppage, and an ordinary ruin.
