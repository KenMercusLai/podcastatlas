# Apparel OEM Vertical Integration / 服装代工纵向一体化

## Definition

Apparel OEM vertical integration / 服装代工纵向一体化 is the strategy of colocating or controlling key textile and garment processes so an OEM supplier can deliver speed, quality, compliance, and customer coordination that rivals cannot easily match.

## Current Synthesis

[[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]] makes [[ShenzhouInternational|申洲国际]] the core case. The episode breaks down knitwear production from yarn, knitting, dyeing and finishing, printing, embroidery, cutting, sewing, ironing, inspection, and shipment, then argues that Shenzhou's integrated park structure can compress lead times and reduce customer switching friction.

The concept is a corrective to the idea that OEM work is necessarily low-end. When a supplier becomes responsible for many process stages, environmental thresholds, quality systems, and customer-specific capacity, OEM depth itself becomes strategic.

## Key Claims

- Vertical integration can turn apparel OEM from simple sewing capacity into a system moat.
- Speed matters because fashion, sports events, and retail windows can expire quickly.
- Quality and compliance are part of the integration problem, especially for international customers.
- Customer-specific factories and high integration reduce switching convenience for large brands.
- Integration also creates concentration risk when a small number of large customers dominate revenue.

## Evidence

- **Process-chain breakdown:** [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]] lists the production stages behind a knit sports shirt.
- **Lead-time compression:** [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]] says Shenzhou can compress delivery from ordinary multi-month cycles toward much shorter lead times.
- **Customer and compliance moat:** [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]] connects wastewater treatment, Uniqlo delivery, core global sportswear customers, and dedicated factories to supplier trust.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

- The concept is not a universal apparel prescription; integration requires capital, process management, and sufficient order volume.
- Customer concentration is a real qualification, not just proof of efficiency.
- Source figures on customers, capacity, and lead time remain episode-scoped.

## What Changed

- Added apparel OEM vertical integration as Shenzhou's central operating moat in the Ningbo episode.
