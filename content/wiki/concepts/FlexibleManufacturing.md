# Flexible Manufacturing / 柔性制造

Flexible manufacturing / 柔性制造 is [[ep271-tanfang-rouxing-zhizao-gongchang-shi-shui-rang-ni-xiangyao-jiu-neng-maidao-lqyrioptggnbb8hl-llej0-5ow0v]]'s frame for factories and supply chains that can handle stable mass production, [[SmallOrderQuickResponse|small orders]], customization, and sudden demand spikes without losing quality or delivery windows. The episode treats the term broadly: the factory is only one layer, because the company also has to sense demand, choose what to make, design the product, arrange materials, and execute production.

The concept matters because it explains why "wanting and buying" can feel nearly simultaneous for consumers. Fast logistics is only the visible last step; upstream, firms such as [[DeliGroup|得力]], [[HaierGroup|海尔]], [[YagorGroup|雅戈尔]], and [[ShenzhouInternational|申洲国际]] must connect product imagination, order systems, materials, production scheduling, [[ManufacturingDigitalTwin|digital twins]], [[ModularCustomization|modular design]], and [[AIVisualQualityInspection|AI inspection]].

## Key Claims
- The narrow definition is production flexibility: a plant can switch among standardized volume, small batches, custom requirements, and burst orders.
- The broader definition includes demand and product definition: "for whom" and "what" come before "how to manufacture."
- Consumer-product flexibility often depends on [[ConsumerBrandMoat]] and [[StoryLedConsumerBranding]] because firms need to know which demand signals are real enough to produce.
- Large-equipment flexibility can require [[ManufacturingDigitalTwin|digital simulation]] and [[ModularCustomization|module recombination]] rather than ordinary line switching.
- Apparel flexibility depends heavily on [[SmallOrderQuickResponse|small-order quick response]], scheduling, cutting, and the ability to insert new orders without stopping the whole flow.
- Flexible manufacturing is not automatically right for every firm; capital cost, market sensing, technical staff, and demand volatility decide whether the upgrade pays.
