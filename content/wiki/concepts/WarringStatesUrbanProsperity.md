# Warring States Urban Prosperity / 战国城市繁荣

Warring States urban prosperity / 战国城市繁荣 is the social-history layer [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-32-shishang-shouwei-lianheguo-mishuzhang-suqin-lgfkk45yq1c0yblequ-d1t7mol3]] adds through [[Linzi|临淄]]. The episode uses Su Qin's Qi speech to show a capital with dense households, military manpower, music, games, public entertainment, traffic, crowds, and visible confidence.

The concept matters because urban prosperity becomes political evidence. Su Qin's point is not simply that [[QiState|齐国]] is rich; it is that a city this populous and self-confident should feel shame at submitting to [[QinState|秦国]]. The source therefore connects city life to [[DignityBasedPersuasion|dignity-based persuasion]] and [[HezongAlliance|合纵]].

## Key Claims
- Urban density and entertainment can be used as evidence of state capacity, not only cultural description.
- Linzi's prosperity supports a persuasion strategy built around pride, dignity, and resistance.
- The episode treats Warring States cities as socially and militarily consequential, with population, wealth, and public morale all entering diplomacy.
