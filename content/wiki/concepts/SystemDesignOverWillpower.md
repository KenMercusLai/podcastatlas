# System Design Over Willpower / 系统设计优先于意志力

## Definition
System design over willpower is the behavior-change principle that helpful environments, defaults, reminders, modular workflows, and preserved slack are more reliable than asking a bandwidth-depleted person to try harder.

## Current Synthesis
EP87 turns scarcity psychology into a design problem. If scarcity captures attention and lowers bandwidth, then a good intervention should reduce cognitive load rather than add instructions. The source repeatedly shifts from blame to design: make good choices default, make important tasks visible, modularize learning, plan known emergencies early, preserve slack, and redesign tools or environments so predictable errors are harder to make.

The episode's slogan is that people fall to the bottom of their systems. In this frame, better systems do not remove responsibility; they make responsibility executable for a tired, anxious, busy, or resource-constrained person.

## Key Claims
- Scarcity conditions make willpower an unreliable primary intervention.
- Systems should lower the bandwidth required to start, continue, recover, and make good decisions.
- Important non-urgent work needs reminders and review cadences so it enters attention before it becomes urgent.
- Defaults matter because many beneficial actions should happen unless a person actively opts out.
- Fire drills should be separated from true emergencies; recurring predictable crises belong in planning systems.
- Error-prone environments should be redesigned rather than handled only through blame or training.

## Evidence
- Low-bandwidth design evidence: [[ep87-xique-ruhe-sheji-yige-rang-ziji-bian-congming-de-xitong-gkwrirwoeedlbme1xqtv2orv]] recommends modular training and experience-based teaching for people already under scarcity.
- Attention-design evidence: [[ep87-xique-ruhe-sheji-yige-rang-ziji-bian-congming-de-xitong-gkwrirwoeedlbme1xqtv2orv]] argues that systems should put important non-urgent matters into the tunnel through reminders and weekly review.
- Default evidence: [[ep87-xique-ruhe-sheji-yige-rang-ziji-bian-congming-de-xitong-gkwrirwoeedlbme1xqtv2orv]] uses automatic savings and "letting neglect equal consent" as examples of beneficial defaults.
- Fire-drill evidence: [[ep87-xique-ruhe-sheji-yige-rang-ziji-bian-congming-de-xitong-gkwrirwoeedlbme1xqtv2orv]] suggests reviewing weekly emergencies and asking which could have been planned three months earlier.
- Error-design evidence: [[ep87-xique-ruhe-sheji-yige-rang-ziji-bian-congming-de-xitong-gkwrirwoeedlbme1xqtv2orv]] uses the wartime cockpit-control example to show that changing interface design can prevent repeated human error better than scolding.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
System design is not a promise that all barriers are solvable individually. Poverty, gendered labor expectations, institutional rules, and unsafe environments may require collective or structural changes. The page also should not imply that every useful habit can be automated without values, judgment, or ongoing review.
