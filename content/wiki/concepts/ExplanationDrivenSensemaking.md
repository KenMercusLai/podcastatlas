# Explanation-Driven Sense-Making

## Definition
Explanation-driven sense-making is the construction of a causal or functional model that turns observations into an account usable for prediction, action, learning, and communication.

## Current Synthesis
[[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-d08500083dda91b814b053f30bd7b557-87666361-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-d08500083dda91b814b053f30bd7b557-87666361|The Story of Stories]] treats explanation as a basic cognitive activity. A useful explanation joins observations at a level broad enough to guide future cases: understanding why an event occurred can change responsibility, prevention, reproduction, and response. Stories often foreground people, sequence, emotion, and concrete detail, while explanation extracts transferable structure, but ordinary reasoning usually combines the two.

The drive is productive without being self-validating. Coherence can motivate curiosity and discovery, and an incomplete model may outperform no model, yet an elegant account can be false or only partly predictive. Explanatory usefulness must remain separate from evidential truth and from the pleasure of an “aha” moment.

## Key Claims
- Explanations organize variable observations into models that support prediction and response.
- Causal understanding changes what people try to reproduce, prevent, blame, excuse, or investigate.
- Stories and explanations overlap, but concrete narrative and generalizable structure are different emphases.
- Culture and education transmit accumulated explanatory models beyond direct experience.
- A model can remain useful despite exceptions, but fit, scope, and rivals determine confidence.

## Evidence
- Everyday causal unification - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-d08500083dda91b814b053f30bd7b557-87666361-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-d08500083dda91b814b053f30bd7b557-87666361]] uses a child's repeated change of clothes to show one explanation unifying observations that isolated accident stories did not.
- Prediction and response - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-d08500083dda91b814b053f30bd7b557-87666361-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-d08500083dda91b814b053f30bd7b557-87666361]] connects explanation to anticipating recurrence, assigning responsibility, and choosing prevention or reproduction.
- Knowledge transmission - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-d08500083dda91b814b053f30bd7b557-87666361-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-d08500083dda91b814b053f30bd7b557-87666361]] presents education and culture as ways explanatory models accumulate across generations.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is a structured public-education summary, not a complete theory of explanation or review of competing models. Coherence, generality, simplicity, and usefulness do not prove causal truth, and explanations that fit most cases may fail dangerously in a particular one. Some situations require timely action before the exact cause is known.

## What Changed
- Established a functional model of explanation spanning observation, prediction, response, learning, and transmission.
