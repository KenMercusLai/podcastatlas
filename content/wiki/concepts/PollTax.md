# Poll Tax / 人头税

Poll tax / 人头税 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-18-zhongguo-lishishang-di-yi-li-rentoushui-lgqnequk0rxejqgihwdqxqyqnsvq]], where the host explains [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]]'s 348 BCE tax reform as a likely first Chinese case of taxing by person. The episode links the change to the earlier [[ShangYangReforms|商鞅变法]] abolition of well-field boundaries and opening of qianmo: once land institutions change, the older one-tenth tax no longer fits.

The source treats the tax as part of [[QinState|秦国]]'s growing administrative capacity rather than as an isolated fiscal footnote. It sits beside [[QinAdministrativeStandardization|county, land, and measurement standardization]] and [[HouseholdSeparationLaw|household separation]] as another way the state makes households and bodies legible for extraction.

## Key Claims
- A poll tax charges by person rather than only by land harvest.
- The episode makes the reform conditional on prior land-system change: new fields require new fiscal methods.
- The claim is source-scoped and probabilistic; the host says the reform "very likely" produced the first Chinese poll tax.
- Poll tax belongs to Qin's state-building branch because fiscal registration supports administrative and military capacity.
