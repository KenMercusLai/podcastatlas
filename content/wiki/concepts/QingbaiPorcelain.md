# Qingbai Porcelain / 青白瓷

Qingbai Porcelain / 青白瓷 is the early Jingdezhen ware discussed in [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]]. The episode describes it as a blue-white glazed porcelain, also later called yingqing, with a thin body and translucent effect.

The source uses Qingbai to explain [[Jingdezhen]]'s rise before the later fame of blue-and-white and imperial wares. It connects early local craft to the [[NorthernSong|Northern Song]] period, [[Fuliang]], and the evolving [[JingdezhenPorcelainProductionSystem]].
