# Huawei Smart Selection Car Model / 华为智选车模式

## Definition

Huawei Smart Selection Car Model / 华为智选车模式 is a vehicle cooperation model where automakers keep qualification, manufacturing, supply chain, and vehicle delivery responsibility while Huawei shapes product definition, intelligent systems, retail explanation, launch, marketing, service, and user operations.

## Current Synthesis

The first source defines the model through the [[Seres]] / [[AITO|问界]] breakthrough: Huawei is deeper than a parts supplier but still not the legal whole-vehicle manufacturer. EP279 adds what that influence looks like after the portfolio matures. Product definition now includes assisted-driving trust, cabin living-space use, outdoor hardware, physical controls, oxygen, and feedback channels, not only brand launch or store traffic.

## Key Claims

- The model is deeper than ordinary component supply because Huawei participates in customer-facing product decisions and sales channels.
- It is not Huawei directly making cars because the automaker keeps qualification, manufacturing, vehicle ownership, and quality obligations.
- The model works only if product competitiveness, delivery capacity, retail explanation, after-sales service, and user feedback align.
- [[HongmengZhixing|鸿蒙智行]] generalizes the model into a portfolio, with later brands adjusted to partner capabilities and use cases.
- EP279 shows that product definition extends into scenario design: assisted driving, outdoor controls, rest, oxygen, work, and camping all become part of the vehicle job.

## Evidence

- **Division of labor:** [[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] says Seres handles qualification, factories, supply chain, manufacturing, and quality while Huawei handles product definition, target users, configuration, pricing, launches, marketing, intelligent cockpit, assisted driving, electric drive, and retail channels.
- **Portfolio generalization:** [[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] uses [[Luxeed|智界]], [[Stelato|享界]], [[Shangjie|尚界]], and [[Maextro|尊界]] to show that Hongmeng Zhixing adapts the model to different partners.
- **Scenario product definition:** [[ep279-dang-fangxiangpan-manman-songkai-women-ruhe-yu-che-xiangchu-lq2elbnjy6xrhixxqjs-y7erhlnv]] has Peng Lei explain the G9 through hard-SUV comfort gaps, camping equipment, all-terrain hardware, oxygen, and physical buttons.
- **Feedback and iteration:** [[ep279-dang-fangxiangpan-manman-songkai-women-ruhe-yu-che-xiangchu-lq2elbnjy6xrhixxqjs-y7erhlnv]] describes store feedback, questionnaires, NPS, interviews, and owner complaints as inputs to product choices.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

- Strong Huawei product influence can raise coherence, but it can also expose partner automakers to delivery and quality pressure they still legally carry.
- Scenario-rich product definition does not prove that every feature will be used or that off-road and assisted-driving claims will perform under independent testing.
- The model's success depends on channel explanation, service, product competitiveness, and manufacturing execution aligning at the same time.
