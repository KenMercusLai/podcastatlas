# Huawei Smart Selection Car Model / 华为智选车模式

Huawei Smart Selection Car Model / 华为智选车模式 is the cooperation model that [[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] uses to explain the early [[AITO|问界]] breakthrough. The model divides responsibility between automaker manufacturing capacity and Huawei's product, technology, brand, retail, and service capability.

In the source's [[Seres]] case, Huawei's terminal business group handles product definition, target users, configuration, pricing, launch events, marketing, and store traffic. Huawei's automotive technology teams deliver intelligent cockpit, assisted driving, electric drive, and vehicle-control systems. Seres supplies whole-vehicle qualification, factories, supply chain, manufacturing, and quality management.

## Key Claims
- The model is deeper than ordinary component supply because Huawei participates in customer-facing product decisions and sales channels.
- It is also not Huawei directly making cars because the automaker keeps qualification, manufacturing, and vehicle ownership.
- The model works only if product competitiveness, delivery capacity, retail explanation, and after-sales service align.
- [[HongmengZhixing|鸿蒙智行]] generalizes the model into a portfolio, but the source stresses that later brands are adjusted to each partner's capabilities.
