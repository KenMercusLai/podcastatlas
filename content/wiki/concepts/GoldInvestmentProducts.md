# Gold Investment Products / 黄金投资产品

Gold investment products / 黄金投资产品 are the implementation layer mapped in [[ep239-he-daka-liaoliao-jinjia-you-shuang-ruo-zhuo-biao-le-putongren-hai-neng-shangche-ma-lgcy2trgzt7expvr5bcybybrummq]]. [[WangLixin|王立新]] separates physical retail gold such as bars and coins from financial products linked to spot gold, and then separates both from futures, options, and other tools better suited to professionals.

The episode's practical distinction is that gold exposure is not one product. Jewelry can store some value but includes design and workmanship premiums; bars and coins depend on trusted purchase and buyback channels; ETF-like products can lower the minimum purchase and improve liquidity; futures and options add leverage, expiration, and expertise requirements. Product choice is therefore part of [[PortfolioSuitability]], not an afterthought.

## Key Claims
- Physical gold should be bought through reliable channels; saving a small amount on price can be a poor tradeoff if authenticity, buyback, or liquidation is uncertain.
- Jewelry is a hybrid of consumption and investment, so workmanship fees and resale discounts make it unsuitable as a pure investment substitute.
- Spot-linked financial products can make gold divisible and easier to buy or sell, especially when backed by standardized vault holdings.
- Gold ETFs lower the access threshold by letting investors buy less than a full gram of exposure, but they still require understanding fees, tracking, custody, and redemption rules.
- Futures and options are treated as professional tools because leverage, margin, and time structure can turn a safe-asset narrative into a high-risk trade.
- The product route should follow [[StrategicGoldAllocation|strategic role]] and [[InvestmentLiquidityTradeoff|liquidity need]], not the other way around.
