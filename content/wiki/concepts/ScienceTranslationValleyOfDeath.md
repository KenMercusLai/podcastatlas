# Science Translation Valley of Death

## Definition

The science translation valley of death is the gap between academic discovery and work mature, reliable, integrated, or usable enough for a startup or established company to adopt.

## Current Synthesis

The gap is partly technical and partly institutional. A discovery may require sustained engineering, shared datasets, tooling, validation, or cross-disciplinary coordination that is insufficiently rewarded by papers yet too early or nonexclusive for commercial investment. [[FocusedResearchOrganizations]] are one proposed bridge, especially when the output is public infrastructure rather than a proprietary product.

## Key Claims

- Publication can demonstrate discovery without producing an adoptable tool or system.
- Commercial actors may wait until technical and market uncertainty has fallen further.
- Shared infrastructure is especially vulnerable because its value is distributed and difficult to capture privately.
- Bridging organizations need engineering, coordination, validation, and maintenance, not only additional research funding.

## Evidence

- Institutional gap: [[cash-test-can-europe-support-ukraine-alone-60e0fa97fc3b0dcfbac470b2da9e20ba]] explicitly places some private-science institutions between academic research and startup- or company-ready work.
- Incentive mismatch: [[cash-test-can-europe-support-ukraine-alone-60e0fa97fc3b0dcfbac470b2da9e20ba]] says engineering-heavy and use-driven outputs may fit neither academic publishing nor conventional product incentives.
- Public-resource example: [[cash-test-can-europe-support-ukraine-alone-60e0fa97fc3b0dcfbac470b2da9e20ba]] uses [[Cultivarium]] to illustrate a shared research resource rather than a paper series or ordinary product.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Not every research result should be translated, and a failed transition may reflect weak science, absent demand, regulation, cost, or implementation difficulty rather than an institutional funding gap. The source does not quantify how often focused organizations close the gap or how resulting tools should be maintained after a temporary organization ends.

## What Changed

- Added an institutional definition separating discovery from adoptable research tools and systems.
