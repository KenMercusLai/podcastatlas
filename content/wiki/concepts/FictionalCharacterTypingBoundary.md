# Fictional-Character Typing Boundary / 虚构人物类型化边界

## Definition

Fictional-character typing boundary is the rule that a personality typology can organize comparison among recurring fictional choices without becoming a diagnosis, a definitive identity, or a substitute for narrative context.

## Current Synthesis

The bounded source uses MBTI's T/F and J/P preferences as questions rather than verdicts: when does a character prioritize impersonal reasoning or interpersonal value, and when do they seek closure and order or preserve flexibility? This can make patterns across decisions, relationships, work, and martial style visible.

The method becomes unreliable when a single act proves an entire type, when morality is smuggled into a neutral preference, or when plot role is mistaken for psychology. Role demands, changing habits, mixed motives, authorial convention, and reader disagreement must remain part of the interpretation. A contested classification can therefore be more informative than a confident label because it exposes which evidence and assumptions each reading privileges.

## Key Claims

- Repeated choices across different situations are stronger typing evidence than one dramatic scene or quoted trait.
- A preference axis does not establish virtue, empathy, intelligence, competence, emotional depth, or likely narrative outcome.
- Office, danger, relationship, habit, and plot function can change how a tendency is expressed without proving a hidden immutable type.
- Disputed cases should preserve competing explanations rather than forcing a single four-letter answer.
- Literary typing is interpretive comparison, not clinical assessment or psychometric validation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source is an entertainment podcast applying popular MBTI language to fictional characters, not a psychometric study or a systematic survey of Jin Yong's complete corpus. Characters are authored constructions whose actions serve theme, genre, and plot as well as simulated psychology. The episode sometimes conflates feeling with emotionality and perceiving with impulsiveness, so its individual classifications remain examples of the method and its failure modes rather than settled results.

## What Changed

- Created the concept to separate comparative literary typing from definitive character diagnosis.
- Made disagreement, context, moral neutrality, and repeated-choice evidence explicit safeguards.
