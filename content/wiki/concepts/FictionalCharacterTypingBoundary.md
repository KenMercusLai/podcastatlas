# Fictional-Character Typing Boundary / 虚构人物类型化边界

## Definition

Fictional-character typing boundary is the rule that a personality typology can organize comparison among recurring fictional choices without becoming a diagnosis, a definitive identity, or a substitute for narrative context.

## Current Synthesis

The bounded sources use all four MBTI preference pairs as questions rather than verdicts: where does a character appear to regain energy, what kind of information do they foreground, how do they prioritize decisions, and when do they seek closure or preserve flexibility? This can make patterns across solitude, relationships, perception, imagination, work, and martial style visible.

The method becomes unreliable when visible isolation is treated as inner introversion, social skill as extraversion, creativity as intuition, literal error as sensing, or a single act as proof of an entire type. Morality cannot be smuggled into a neutral preference, and plot role cannot stand in for psychology. Trauma, upbringing, role demands, changing habits, mixed motives, authorial convention, and reader disagreement must remain part of the interpretation. A contested classification can therefore be more informative than a confident label because it exposes which evidence and assumptions each reading privileges.

## Key Claims

- Repeated choices across different situations are stronger typing evidence than one dramatic scene, living arrangement, quoted trait, or plot outcome.
- A preference axis does not establish virtue, empathy, intelligence, competence, emotional depth, or likely narrative outcome.
- Upbringing, trauma, office, danger, relationship, habit, and plot function can change outward expression without proving a hidden immutable type.
- Disputed cases should preserve competing explanations rather than forcing a single four-letter answer.
- An analyst's own type identification and uneven familiarity can bias which characters look representative, especially when one pole is selected mainly through negative examples.
- Literary typing is interpretive comparison, not clinical assessment or psychometric validation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The sources are entertainment podcasts applying popular MBTI language to fictional characters, not psychometric studies or a systematic survey of Jin Yong's complete corpus. Characters are authored constructions whose actions serve theme, genre, and plot as well as simulated psychology. The first episode sometimes conflates introversion with isolation and sensing with literalness, while the second can conflate feeling with emotionality and perceiving with impulsiveness. Their individual classifications therefore remain examples of the method and its failure modes rather than settled results.

## What Changed

- Extended the boundary from T/F and J/P to I/E and N/S while retaining the original evidence inventory.
- Added distinct-cause safeguards for solitude, social skill, trauma, abstraction, perception, and analyst bias.
- Preserved disagreement, context, moral neutrality, and repeated-choice evidence as the governing tests.
