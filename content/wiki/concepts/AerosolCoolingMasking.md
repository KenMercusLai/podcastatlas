# Aerosol Cooling Masking

Aerosol cooling masking is the source's climate-policy tension where sulphur pollution reflects sunlight and cools the planet while also damaging human health. In [[taking-the-shine-off-albedo-and-global-temperatures-6a730fdaca067b295bab9760]], [[OliverMorton]] says sulphur levels have fallen quickly after peaking over the past 20 years, reducing Earth's reflectivity.

The concept does not argue against cleaner air. It shows that air-quality success can reveal warming that had been partly masked by reflective aerosols, which is why the source connects [[InternationalMaritimeOrganization]] shipping rules, [[China]] sulphur reductions, [[AlbedoFeedback]], and [[EarthEnergyImbalance]].

## Key Claims
- Sulphur aerosols reflect sunlight and can therefore cool the climate system.
- Cutting sulphur improves air quality but removes part of that cooling mask.
- The policy lesson is not to preserve harmful smog, but to account for the warming that cleaner air can uncover.
