# Male-Default Medical Knowledge / 男性默认医学知识

## Definition
Male-default medical knowledge is the practice of treating male anatomy, symptoms, dimensions, or sexual function as the general human reference while presenting female bodies as reproductive special cases, deviations, or incomplete variants.

## Current Synthesis
The episode's clitoral case shows that default status shapes both description and investment. When the penis becomes the model for naming a homologous organ, extensive knowledge of male sexual function looks medically ordinary while comparable female knowledge needs a special justification. The same pattern can extend beyond genital anatomy into disease recognition and products built around assumed body dimensions. This does not mean every sex difference is bias: clinically relevant variation should be studied. The problem arises when one body supplies the baseline and the other is researched only where reproduction, pathology, or accommodation forces attention.

## Key Claims
- A reference body determines which anatomy appears universal and which requires special explanation.
- Homology does not guarantee equal knowledge when research value is assigned through gendered clinical and social priorities.
- Male-default framing can affect textbooks, research funding, surgical protection, symptom recognition, and physical design.
- Correcting the default requires sex- and gender-aware evidence, not simply replacing one universal body with another.
- Comparative examples are signals of a research-design problem, not proof that every outcome difference has one cause.

## Evidence
- **Anatomical reference:** [[af3491ca3aca3a270aadb44a7f572e93-af3491ca3aca3a270aadb44a7f572e93]] says nineteenth-century medicine described the clitoris through the penis and sometimes as an incompletely developed version of it.
- **Unequal investment:** [[af3491ca3aca3a270aadb44a7f572e93-af3491ca3aca3a270aadb44a7f572e93]] contrasts detailed penile neurovascular teaching and earlier nerve mapping with missing or outdated clitoral material and weaker protection of female sexual function in surgery.
- **Wider default effects:** [[af3491ca3aca3a270aadb44a7f572e93-af3491ca3aca3a270aadb44a7f572e93]] uses heart-attack care and male-sized pianos, steering wheels, and seats to argue that the assumed standard body can affect health and daily use.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode supplies illustrative comparisons, not a comprehensive causal review of cardiovascular care, ergonomics, anatomy curricula, or research funding. Biological differences can make methods and clinical needs genuinely non-identical. A valid correction therefore needs population-specific evidence and outcome testing rather than symmetry for its own sake.

## What Changed
- Established the reference-body mechanism connecting a focused anatomy gap to wider medicine and design.
- Preserved clinically relevant difference while rejecting unexamined male universality.
