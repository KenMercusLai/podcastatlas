# Everyday Creative Agency / 日常创造能动性

## Definition
Everyday creative agency is the capacity to use making, play, perception, and personal expression without first qualifying as a professional artist or proving market value.

## Current Synthesis
[[ep163-bei-hulianwang-chongji-de-yishu-ckwriaieiyu2abaaaacscgxf|EP163]] treats creativity as a capacity that can be suppressed by standardized evaluation, technical comparison, and fear that a work is not good enough, but not necessarily erased. Children's drawing supplies the episode's clearest model: attention and expression precede a fixed target. Adult recovery therefore begins by loosening likeness, correctness, prestige, and price as compulsory tests, then rebuilding sensitivity through repeated making and looking.

## Key Claims
- Artistic participation does not require professional identity or market recognition.
- Standardized evaluation can narrow willingness to make without proving that creative capacity has disappeared.
- Low-stakes making can reconnect emotion, attention, memory, and communication.
- Technical training can support craft while also creating habits that later require selective unlearning.
- Curiosity and sustained attention matter more than declaring oneself naturally talented.

## Evidence
- Childhood model - [[ep163-bei-hulianwang-chongji-de-yishu-ckwriaieiyu2abaaaacscgxf]] describes children drawing without a predetermined result or external value test.
- Adult inhibition - [[ep163-bei-hulianwang-chongji-de-yishu-ckwriaieiyu2abaaaacscgxf]] connects schooling and standardized judgment to withdrawal from creative practice and the belief that art belongs to others.
- Reopening practice - [[ep163-bei-hulianwang-chongji-de-yishu-ckwriaieiyu2abaaaacscgxf]] recommends beginning from expression and observation rather than likeness or price.
- Training boundary - [[ep163-bei-hulianwang-chongji-de-yishu-ckwriaieiyu2abaaaacscgxf]] uses [[JinLaoshiArtist|金老师]]'s later unlearning of early painting constraints as a professional version of the same problem.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source offers a humane practice frame, not developmental or educational evidence that all children are equally creative, that schooling generally extinguishes creativity, or that unstructured expression alone produces artistic skill. Reopening participation does not remove the roles of technique, feedback, time, disability, resources, or professional standards.

## What Changed
- Created a bounded concept separating everyday creative participation from professional status and market value.
