# Everyday Creative Agency / 日常创造能动性

## Definition
Everyday creative agency is the capacity to use making, play, perception, and personal expression without first qualifying as a professional artist or proving market value.

## Current Synthesis
[[ep163-bei-hulianwang-chongji-de-yishu-ckwriaieiyu2abaaaacscgxf|EP163]] treats creativity as a capacity that can be suppressed by standardized evaluation, technical comparison, and fear that a work is not good enough, but not necessarily erased. Children's drawing supplies the clearest model: attention and expression precede a fixed target. Adult recovery therefore begins by loosening likeness, correctness, prestige, and price as compulsory tests, then rebuilding sensitivity through repeated making and looking.

An extreme adult case appears in [[the-worst-poet-in-the-world-from-cautionary-tales-the-worst-poet-in-the-world-from-cautionary-tales|The Worst Poet in the World]]. [[WilliamMcGonagall]] kept writing and performing despite critical contempt and public ridicule. The episode does not ask ordinary makers to imitate his craft or endure abuse; it uses his persistence to separate the personal value of creative action from professional quality and market value. Generative AI may alter the economics and scarcity of output without removing the value people gain from attempting expression themselves.

## Key Claims
- Artistic participation does not require professional identity or market recognition.
- Standardized evaluation can narrow willingness to make without proving that creative capacity has disappeared.
- Low-stakes making can reconnect emotion, attention, memory, and communication.
- Technical training can support craft while also creating habits that later require selective unlearning.
- Curiosity and sustained attention matter more than declaring oneself naturally talented.
- Comparison with masterworks can turn a quality benchmark into a participation barrier for ordinary makers.
- Technological or market changes can alter the value of outputs without eliminating the value of creative activity.

## Evidence
- Childhood model - [[ep163-bei-hulianwang-chongji-de-yishu-ckwriaieiyu2abaaaacscgxf]] describes children drawing without a predetermined result or external value test.
- Adult inhibition - [[ep163-bei-hulianwang-chongji-de-yishu-ckwriaieiyu2abaaaacscgxf]] connects schooling and standardized judgment to withdrawal from creative practice and the belief that art belongs to others.
- Reopening practice - [[ep163-bei-hulianwang-chongji-de-yishu-ckwriaieiyu2abaaaacscgxf]] recommends beginning from expression and observation rather than likeness or price.
- Training boundary - [[ep163-bei-hulianwang-chongji-de-yishu-ckwriaieiyu2abaaaacscgxf]] uses [[JinLaoshiArtist|金老师]]'s later unlearning of early painting constraints as a professional version of the same problem.
- Persistence under judgment - [[the-worst-poet-in-the-world-from-cautionary-tales-the-worst-poet-in-the-world-from-cautionary-tales]] uses McGonagall's continued writing and performance to show creative action surviving hostile evaluation.
- Value distinction - [[the-worst-poet-in-the-world-from-cautionary-tales-the-worst-poet-in-the-world-from-cautionary-tales]] separates commercial value and professional quality from personal expression and participation.
- Technology boundary - [[the-worst-poet-in-the-world-from-cautionary-tales-the-worst-poet-in-the-world-from-cautionary-tales]] argues that generative AI can change creative economics without making human making meaningless.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The sources offer humane practice frames, not developmental or educational evidence that all children are equally creative, that schooling generally extinguishes creativity, or that unstructured expression alone produces artistic skill. Reopening participation does not remove the roles of technique, feedback, time, disability, resources, or professional standards. McGonagall's persistence does not make ridicule desirable, prove his comic intention, or show that all repeated practice improves craft. The generative-AI comparison is a broad analogy rather than labor-market or cultural-impact evidence.

## What Changed
- Added adult persistence as evidence that creative participation can retain value under failed professional and market tests.
- Added a technology boundary separating changes in output economics from the human value of making.
