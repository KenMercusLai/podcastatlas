# Negative-Attention Relationship Game / 负向关注关系游戏

## Definition
A negative-attention relationship game is a repeated interaction in which conflict, betrayal, injury, or performance is unconsciously maintained because painful attention feels more confirming than no response at all.

## Current Synthesis
Episode 477 uses transactional-analysis language to distinguish positive stimulation, negative stimulation, and no stimulation. Its listener case suggests that a person may consciously recognize danger yet keep testing or trusting a likely betrayer because the resulting hurt still confirms that someone is present and responding.

The repair begins by translating the apparent wish for pain into the underlying need to be seen, noticed, and confirmed as existing. Healthy relationships, therapy, honest feedback, and reduced performance can then provide experiences in which attention does not require injury.

## Key Claims
- Negative attention can be reinforcing when the alternative is experienced as invisibility or abandonment.
- Rational danger recognition may not override a relationship pattern learned through earlier deprivation.
- Personas and provocation can obtain response while preventing the person from learning how others see their less-performed self.
- The underlying need is recognition and contact, not pain itself.
- New relational experience can improve the ability to distinguish interest, care, conflict, exploitation, and indifference.

## Evidence
- Attention hierarchy - [[shi-di-fu-shuo-477-zixunshi-huixin-lijie-shenme-shi-e-caineng-zhudong-xuanze-weishan-lj-c37-abvr5oa0a7vbhvl93pvia]] proposes that no response may feel more threatening than negative response for someone shaped by insufficient attention.
- Harmful repetition - [[shi-di-fu-shuo-477-zixunshi-huixin-lijie-shenme-shi-e-caineng-zhudong-xuanze-weishan-lj-c37-abvr5oa0a7vbhvl93pvia]] reports a listener performing a persona and continuing to trust people she expects may betray or hurt her.
- Corrective feedback - [[shi-di-fu-shuo-477-zixunshi-huixin-lijie-shenme-shi-e-caineng-zhudong-xuanze-weishan-lj-c37-abvr5oa0a7vbhvl93pvia]] recommends naming the wish to be seen, gaining healthier relationship experience, and asking trusted people for concrete impressions rather than relying on imagined judgment.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source lacks the listener's detailed developmental history, so attention deprivation is a hypothesis rather than a diagnosis. Continued contact with harmful people can also reflect coercion, dependency, economics, fear, isolation, or practical barriers. The concept should not be used to blame victims for abuse or to reduce deliberate harm to a mutual game.
