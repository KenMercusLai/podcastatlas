# Signal Design / 信号设计

Signal design is the source's frame for making hidden quality, intent, effort, or reliability legible. [[79-gewei-lingdao-danfan-zan-xuedian-boyilun-690004262]] uses reputation, personal image, credentials, prices, discounts, and workplace behavior as signals inside incomplete contracts.

The episode treats signals as neither fake nor pure. A degree, brand, discount, promise, public behavior, or boss-facing performance can communicate useful information, but signals can also inflate, mislead, or crowd out the thing they were meant to reveal.

## Key Claims
- Signals lower distrust and contract cost when underlying quality is hard to observe.
- A signal has value only if others believe it is costly, consistent, or hard enough to fake.
- Signal competition can become wasteful when everyone escalates the same proof mechanism.
- Bad signal design can make organizations reward appearance, flattery, or discounting instead of capability, quality, or truth.
