# Fiat Capital-Market Decentralization / 纸币时代的资本市场去中心化

Fiat capital-market decentralization / 纸币时代的资本市场去中心化 is [[jifa-dongwu-jingshen-chuangzao-gengduo-jihui-lndyfdmtgo-9l5cr8oy82t1xnlfe]]'s answer to a problem in paper-money systems: fiat money has no simple gold- or Bitcoin-like [[ProofOfWorkMonetaryValidation|proof of work]], so central banks must balance conflicting goals through discretionary judgment. [[ZhouLuohua|周洛华]] argues that capital markets can become a decentralized channel inside this centralized base-money system.

In the source, listed companies partly earn this role by applying technology, raising productivity, improving welfare, and helping the monetary system meet growth and employment goals. The danger is that bad governance, insider cash-out, opaque voting, or weak minority-shareholder rights can turn the channel from opportunity into extraction.

## Key Claims
- Fiat base money is centrally issued, but a healthy society still needs decentralized opportunity channels.
- Stock exchanges and, historically, real estate can let ordinary people turn productive participation into asset and status mobility.
- Listed companies become more legitimate when they improve productivity and distribute value rather than merely absorb liquidity.
- Blockchain-like share registration, transparent voting, dividend-linked founder cash-out, and collective litigation are source proposals for reducing governance risk.
- The concept ties monetary legitimacy to [[ManagementShareholderAlignmentRisk]] and [[InvestmentRiskManagement]], not only to central-bank policy.
