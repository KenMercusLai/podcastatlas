# 造势型政治表演 / Charismatic Political Performance

造势型政治表演 / charismatic political performance is the pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-6-dingji-tianshilun-touziren-lvgong-lhqqja27zxdiypgf6wuv1abgkw3j|Qinji 128-6]] draws from [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s "贺钱一万" at [[LuGongLiuBangFatherInLaw|吕公]]'s banquet. Liu Bang has no invitation and no money, but he loudly claims a gift far beyond his status and then sits as if the claim has already made him important.

The source treats the performance as politically meaningful because it changes the social field around him. [[XiaoHe|萧何]] tries to contain the embarrassment, but Lu Gong reads Liu Bang's confidence, bearing, and disregard for ordinary rank as evidence of future possibility. The episode's sharper claim is that Liu Bang's bragging is not ordinary boastfulness; it is the ability to make others interact with a future status before that status materially exists.

This differs from [[MoralReputationPoliticalCapital|道德名望政治资本]], where visible virtue creates trust or legitimacy. Here the capital comes from audacity, timing, and embodied certainty. It also differs from [[ClassMobilityPerformance|阶级上升表演]] because Liu Bang is not carefully learning upper-status codes; he is imposing a status claim through performative force.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-162-1-ta-ti-liubang-qu-si-rujin-chengle-zhengzhou-de-shouhushen-li3su2zlmbm1ift9nibujgtcrxwm|Hanji 162-1]] adds a darker crisis version. The host speculates that [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s hesitation before accepting [[JiXin|纪信]]'s plan to impersonate him at [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]] may have been a performance of reluctance after the survival decision was already clear. The page records this as source-scoped: the claim is useful for reading Liu Bang's political theater, but the transcript itself marks it as interpretation.

## Key Claims
- A status performance can work before resources exist if the performer makes others treat the imagined status as socially present.
- The performance needs an audience with room to reinterpret the act; without Lu Gong's recognition, Liu Bang's claim could remain mere fraud or clowning.
- Political charisma in this source is not polished respectability. It includes shamelessness, risk tolerance, and the ability to inhabit a future role.
- The concept links personal style to early coalition formation because visible confidence can attract or reassure people who must decide before outcomes are known.
- Hanji 162-1 adds that charisma can also work through managed reluctance in crisis, making a lethal subordinate sacrifice look morally acknowledged rather than simply exploited.
