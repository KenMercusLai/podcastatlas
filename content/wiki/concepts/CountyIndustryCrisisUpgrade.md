# 县域产业危机升级 / County-Industry Crisis Upgrade

## Definition

County-industry crisis upgrade is the process by which a local production cluster responds to an input, demand, regulatory, or reputational shock by recombining inherited skills and networks, then building higher-cost capabilities that change who can remain in the industry.

## Current Synthesis

[[Pingjiang|平江]] supplies a two-stage case in [[no-221-zhongguo-latiao-jianghu-1020016798]]. First, the 1998 flood and soybean-price shock threatened the sauce-dried-tofu business. Workshops substituted wheat flour, adapted rice-noodle machinery, and reused apprenticeship, wholesale, transport, and hometown networks to create and spread麻辣面筋.

Second, safety scandals and regulatory conflict turned the industry's own low-price success into a new crisis. Clean workshops, laboratories, mechanical lines, formula changes, standards, museums, and vocational education converted informal craft diffusion into a more institutionalized cluster, but only after many smaller producers exited.

## Key Claims

- Crisis adaptation often recombines existing capabilities rather than starting from nothing.
- Local peer exchange and hometown networks can spread a product faster than formal R&D systems.
- A low-cost innovation can create a price war that later undermines quality and category legitimacy.
- Regulatory and reputational shocks can force a second upgrade into equipment, testing, training, and brand trust.
- Cluster upgrading redistributes survival toward firms able to finance compliance and differentiation.
- Regional identity can become an asset through museums, education, origin narrative, and specialized taste.

## Evidence

- **Input shock and recombination:** [[no-221-zhongguo-latiao-jianghu-1020016798]] connects the flood and soybean-price rise to wheat substitution and adapted rice-noodle equipment.
- **Network diffusion:** [[no-221-zhongguo-latiao-jianghu-1020016798]] describes apprenticeships, open technical exchange, machinery orders, wholesalers, and hometown sales networks.
- **Second-stage upgrade:** [[no-221-zhongguo-latiao-jianghu-1020016798]] traces standards, shutdowns, clean rooms, laboratories, formula changes, and firm exit.
- **Institutionalization:** [[no-221-zhongguo-latiao-jianghu-1020016798]] links Malawangzi and Pingjiang to a museum, vocational training, technical advisers, and county branding.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

- The episode is a single industry case and does not show that crisis reliably produces successful upgrading.
- The process imposed losses, layoffs, closure, and long periods of weak sales; adaptation was not smooth or evenly shared.
- Official origin honors and regional branding are part of the cluster's narrative infrastructure, not uncontested proof of invention.
