# Innovative Drug Commercial Insurance Catalog

Innovative drug commercial insurance catalog is the payment-policy branch added by [[156-shengwu-yiyao-de-2026-dang-shichang-bu-zai-wei-bd-zaodong-zhongguo-yaoqi-de-xingchen-dahai-cai-ganggang-zhankai-lil-ugrzq8uvzviq3f8i-wm9ilup]]. The episode says China's first commercial-insurance innovative-drug catalog was formally created in 2025, with a July setup and a December first catalog in the source's timeline.

The catalog is not the same as basic national medical insurance. The source emphasizes that it is a recommendation to commercial insurers, with final inclusion still depending on insurer decisions. Its significance is that Chinese innovation drugs may receive a payment channel outside the basic医保 price-control logic.

## Key Claims
- Payment policy affects innovation-drug commercialization as much as approval or BD.
- The first catalog included negotiated high-price drugs such as CAR-T products in the source's account.
- Commercial insurers still decide what they actually cover, so policy signal and real access can diverge.
- The policy shift supports [[BiotechBDDataValidationPhase]] by making 2026 a test of payment adoption, not only clinical data.
