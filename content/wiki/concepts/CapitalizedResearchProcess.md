# Capitalized Research Process / 研究资本化

## Definition
Capitalized Research Process is the practice of turning investment research, case reviews, logic chains, and mistakes into reusable assets that compound across future decisions. It treats research work as durable process memory rather than a one-off report consumed by the next trade.

## Current Synthesis
The source introduces the phrase through Zou Peixuan's case-review method. A researched company, model, or mistake should leave behind a reusable structure: what was assumed, what evidence mattered, what changed, and how the case should update future judgments.

This links investment research to the wiki's broader knowledge-management and decision-logging branches. The same discipline that makes narrative falsifiable also makes research auditable after the outcome is known.

## Key Claims
- Investment research compounds when conclusions, assumptions, process notes, and case reviews can be reused.
- Case review should test whether the original narrative, valuation, and evidence chain were correct rather than only whether the trade made money.
- Research assets are more useful when they preserve both logic and error, because mistakes become future calibration material.
- A shared framework across financial reports, valuation, and narrative makes research easier to accumulate across companies.
- Capitalized research strengthens decision process by making future analysis faster, more comparable, and less memory-dependent.

## Evidence
- **Reusable research claim:** [[caibao-de-gen-guzhi-de-jing-xushi-de-guoshi-lolxytqwzzxq9kzjbtkmfolafavb]] presents "capitalized research" as a way to avoid discarding analyst work after one report or case.
- **Case-review emphasis:** [[caibao-de-gen-guzhi-de-jing-xushi-de-guoshi-lolxytqwzzxq9kzjbtkmfolafavb]] describes using historical case reviews to refine the framework and reuse prior thinking.
- **Framework accumulation:** [[caibao-de-gen-guzhi-de-jing-xushi-de-guoshi-lolxytqwzzxq9kzjbtkmfolafavb]] connects financial-report reading, valuation, and narrative so that research artifacts can be compared across situations.
- **Process memory:** [[caibao-de-gen-guzhi-de-jing-xushi-de-guoshi-lolxytqwzzxq9kzjbtkmfolafavb]] treats logic and falsification as part of the research output, not only the final recommendation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- Reusable research can preserve stale assumptions if review discipline is weak.
- The episode describes the principle but does not give a full research-template implementation.
- Some opportunities may still require fresh primary research rather than extrapolation from prior cases.
- Process memory improves judgment only when later users understand the original context and evidence limits.
