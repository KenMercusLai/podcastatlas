# New Ruler Reform Signaling / 新君改革铺垫信号

New ruler reform signaling / 新君改革铺垫信号 is the accession pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-35-lishishang-diyi-qibing-jiaofu-shi-ta-lnxgnu1czkl-ftfb3gq9kzxwnxdk]] sees in [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]]'s first moves. After [[ZhaoSuhou|赵肃侯]] dies, the new ruler appoints broad-learning advisers, creates left and right posts for pointing out his faults, and honors the senior minister [[FeiYi|肥义]].

On the surface, the gestures signal humility, willingness to learn, criticism tolerance, and respect for old ministers. The episode's stronger reading is that they may also prepare political space: by giving established ministers recognition early, Zhao Wuling Wang lowers suspicion before later asking them to accept a much more disruptive reform program.

The concept is adjacent to [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|court feedback collapse]] because it builds channels for correction, but it is not only a feedback mechanism. It is also succession management: a new ruler can use deference and consultation to stabilize the court while quietly defining the terms on which future action will be judged.

## Key Claims
- Early appointments and honorific gestures can be practical political signals, not empty ritual.
- Public willingness to receive criticism can lower resistance from inherited elites.
- Respect for old ministers may create obligations that matter once reform begins.
- Reform preparation can look conservative before it becomes disruptive.
