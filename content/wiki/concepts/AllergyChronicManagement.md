# Allergy Chronic Management / 过敏慢病管理

## Definition
Allergy chronic management is the episode's frame for treating recurrent rhinitis, asthma, dermatitis, and severe allergy burden as long-running conditions that need symptom relief, trigger management, adherence, and professional follow-up.

## Current Synthesis
[[vol-201-weishenme-jinjinian-guomin-de-ren-yuelaiyue-duo-guomin-tongshike-lgmmw7ejv8ulj0y5gokkv7wieu0z]] ends by moving allergy out of the "忍一忍" category. Acute symptoms can be relieved with appropriate medicines, but chronic allergic rhinitis, asthma, atopic dermatitis, severe eye or skin involvement, pregnancy allergy, and major quality-of-life disruption are presented as management problems rather than one-time fixes.

The sources also broaden chronic management beyond medicine alone. Stress, sleep, anxiety, depression, chronic inflammation, nasal obstruction, brain-fog-like fatigue, household allergen exposure, pets, humidity, season, and emotional burden can all shape lived impact and adherence. Testing can narrow likely triggers without covering every exposure, so contextual observation may complement qualified assessment. [[vol-191-biyan-weishenme-zongshi-fanfu-zhongyi-xiyi-zheci-yiqi-geini-liaoqingchu-lmkf14v4m6n8p0ntpllowcxk8vek]] adds that stop-start treatment can leave symptoms ready to recur and that anatomy or sinus disease may need separate assessment. It also connects allergic rhinitis and asthma through a [[UnifiedAirwayRhinitisAsthma|shared-airway frame]]. Good care therefore combines indicated treatment, correct technique, exposure control, symptom relief, reassessment, and patience while keeping deliberate allergen exposure and unsupported constitutional cures outside self-directed experimentation.

## Key Claims
- Recurrent allergy should be managed as a chronic condition rather than endured until a season passes.
- Acute symptom relief and long-term disease control are different but complementary goals.
- Asthma and atopic dermatitis deserve special caution because inflammation, breathing symptoms, sleep, mood, and quality of life can compound.
- Psychological distress can worsen allergy burden, and repeated allergy symptoms can worsen anxiety, depression, attention, and fatigue.
- Treatment adherence matters; the episode presents asthma control as possible only when patients continue appropriate medication and follow-up.
- Allergen testing is informative but incomplete, so symptom context still matters; deliberately consuming a suspected trigger is not a safe substitute for supervised evaluation or immunotherapy.
- Recurrence despite self-care may reflect poor adherence, technique, structural obstruction, sinus disease, or lower-airway involvement rather than allergy alone.

## Evidence
- Chronic-care frame: [[vol-201-weishenme-jinjinian-guomin-de-ren-yuelaiyue-duo-guomin-tongshike-lgmmw7ejv8ulj0y5gokkv7wieu0z]] says allergy formation is often long-running and patients should be psychologically prepared for long-term management.
- Symptom versus control: [[vol-201-weishenme-jinjinian-guomin-de-ren-yuelaiyue-duo-guomin-tongshike-lgmmw7ejv8ulj0y5gokkv7wieu0z]] allows old allergy patients to keep common medicines for early symptom relief while stressing physician-guided long-term treatment.
- Quality-of-life burden: [[vol-201-weishenme-jinjinian-guomin-de-ren-yuelaiyue-duo-guomin-tongshike-lgmmw7ejv8ulj0y5gokkv7wieu0z]] links chronic inflammation, nasal blockage, sedating medicines, atopic dermatitis, appearance, depression, anxiety, and brain-fog-like fatigue to lived burden.
- Adherence and severe disease: [[vol-201-weishenme-jinjinian-guomin-de-ren-yuelaiyue-duo-guomin-tongshike-lgmmw7ejv8ulj0y5gokkv7wieu0z]] treats asthma, severe dermatitis, biologics, and pregnancy cases as professionally managed conditions.
- Testing, recurrence, and self-exposure: [[vol-154-chuntian-yang-dao-naoxin-poju-guomin-jie-de-shengcun-zhinan-lnxaxys733qzi9rkcwvgqt6uzfur]] says panels cannot cover every trigger, recurrent rhinitis or urticaria may return after short-term improvement, and intentional exposure has unpredictable intensity and duration.
- Adherence, differential assessment, and shared airway: [[vol-191-biyan-weishenme-zongshi-fanfu-zhongyi-xiyi-zheci-yiqi-geini-liaoqingchu-lmkf14v4m6n8p0ntpllowcxk8vek]] links recurrence to stop-start treatment and continuing exposure while distinguishing structural, sinus, pediatric, and asthma-related branches.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This concept is source-scoped public education. It does not prove cure rates, compare treatment regimens, define asthma remission, or substitute for specialist care. VOL.191's treatment-duration, dosing-time, irrigation, TCM constitution, acupressure, heat, and food-therapy claims remain source-scoped. The VOL.154 claim that longer TCM treatment changes constitution is not established here; neither are its broad elimination diet, nasal-rinsing, herbal-product, tea, or acupressure recommendations. Emergency allergy and possible anaphylaxis require urgent care, not chronic-management experimentation.

## What Changed
- Added adherence and technique as recurrence variables alongside exposure control.
- Added structural, sinus, pediatric, and lower-airway reasons to reassess an assumed allergy-only explanation.
- Added coordinated rhinitis-asthma management without implying inevitable progression.
