# Forced Sterilization By Eugenics / 优生学强制绝育

Forced sterilization by eugenics / 优生学强制绝育 is the state-violence pattern where a person labeled unfit is denied reproductive autonomy in the name of heredity, public welfare, or social improvement. [[37-zhishang-ceshi-qingwen-ni-shi-zhili-poluomen-ma-608020679]] grounds the concept in [[CarrieBuck|Carrie Buck / 卡丽·巴克]] and the broader American eugenics movement.

The episode presents forced sterilization as the moment where [[IntelligenceTesting|intelligence testing]] and [[EugenicGovernance|eugenic governance]] leave the domain of labels and become bodily harm. Buck's case is especially important because sexual victimization, institutional interest, class judgment, legal weakness, and claims about inherited intelligence converged to make coercion appear lawful.

## Key Claims
- Forced sterilization turns diagnostic labels into irreversible bodily control.
- The source treats gendered blame and institutional conflict of interest as central, not incidental, to the harm.
- Later evidence that Buck and her daughter Vivian were not intellectually disabled undermines the confidence of the original legal and medical label.
- The concept shows why [[RationalHumility]] is not a soft value when state power is involved.
