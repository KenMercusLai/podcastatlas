# Quantitative Research Discipline / 量化研究纪律

## Definition

Quantitative research discipline is the practice of selecting consequential questions, grounding models in statistics and market structure, validating behavior rigorously, and resisting ad hoc intervention when noisy outcomes challenge a strategy.

## Current Synthesis

The episode defines research taste through two linked abilities: deciding where effort belongs and recognizing what is wrong with a result or experiment. AI can compress coding, documentation, and onboarding, but this raises the value of mathematical judgment, hypothesis formation, failure analysis, and careful review. Subjective expertise belongs upstream in model construction and validation; arbitrary live overrides can destroy the repeated evidence needed to evaluate a system.

## Key Claims

- Valuable research begins with problem selection, not parameter volume.
- Statistical judgment and market understanding are needed to distinguish weak signal from search noise.
- New models should add information meaningfully different from existing signals.
- AI-generated code and reasoning require review at the level of assumptions and behavior.
- Researchers who stop understanding generated layers lose the ability to diagnose later failures.
- Subjective knowledge should shape models and tests rather than become improvised trade-by-trade intervention.
- Drawdown, bias, risk distribution, aggressiveness, and trading preference matter alongside profit.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Human judgment can itself overfit, rationalize, or interfere destructively. Discipline does not mean blind obedience to a model when data pipelines, execution, assumptions, or safety controls fail. The source gives a practitioner framework rather than a controlled comparison of research organizations or training methods.

## What Changed

- Established problem selection and error detection as distinct complements to AI-enabled implementation.
- Distinguished principled model governance from arbitrary live intervention.
