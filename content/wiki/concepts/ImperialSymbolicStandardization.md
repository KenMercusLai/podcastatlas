## Definition
帝国符号标准化 describes [[QinState]]'s post-unification packaging of imperial titles, official language, cosmological motifs, spatial display, rituals and inscriptions as signs of one sovereignty. “VI design” is a podcast analogy, not a Qin policy term.

## Current Synthesis
After the [[ConquestCommanderyTransition]], the core claim concerns who may name and publicly represent the new political order. The title and water-virtue package are relatively direct; the celestial capital and ritual-meaning reconstructions are more tentative. Weapons cast into figures, sacred travel and Langya text extend symbolic claims into visible places, but their administrative and coercive purposes should not be confused with mere branding.

## Key Claims
- New imperial titles and exclusive document language distinguish emperor from the earlier category of king.
- Removing posthumous judgment and numbering successors change dynastic memory and future accountability.
- Water-virtue symbols make rule repeatable through calendar, color, number and place names, while law's supposed equality excludes the ruler.
- Conquest can be materialized at the center, even when disarmament is simultaneously a coercive measure.
- Capital layouts and eastern rites project cosmic and territorial legitimacy, but their procedures and motives require source-critical limits.
- Public inscriptions turn a desired post-conquest order into claims visible in formerly rival territory.

## Evidence
- Language and cosmology: [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-120-2-qinchao-di-yi-tao-vi-sheji-linian-lno-aeljks2vt1k3wjrizsanirtq]] recounts [[QinShiHuang]] with [[LiSi]] and [[QinWangWan]] selecting 皇帝, 制/诏 and exclusive 朕; honoring his father Qin Zhuangxiangwang as 太上皇 and abolishing [[PosthumousNamePolitics]] in favor of numbered rulers; in the episode’s naming account, “始皇帝” is a retrospective label, not a lifetime self-title. [[ZouYan]]'s [[YinYangFivePhasesPoliticalTheory]] frames Qin water overcoming Zhou fire; black, an October new year, six, “德水,” [[QinWengong]]'s black dragon and “黔首” create a symbolic bundle. The episode admits the “level law” ideal has an imperial exception and connects water's yin aspect to harsh punishment, a [[LegalistRulerTechnique]] idiom rather than an equality guarantee.
- Conquest made visible: [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-120-4-bing-liuguo-hou-qinshihuang-jiao-ni-ruhe-shou-jiangshan-lgwsd0c3wkl-cphma8sm3ftkowwr]] reports seized weapons cast as twelve palace figures and six-state palaces copied near the Wei River at [[Xianyang]]: [[PostConquestDisarmament]] also creates a public sign of possession, while [[ImperialHomogenization]] concerns its control effect.
- Spatial projection: [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-121-yingzheng-zao-shijie-zuizao-gaosu-gonglu-2200nian-huangcao-busheng-ls1u8dmuvs5bbrzadf2dtd00js-p]] interprets [[QinJimiao]], likely [[Taiyi]] worship, [[Lishan]] roads and covered passages, and the Wei River/Milky Way analogy as [[QinCosmicCapitalPlanning]]; the ritual identification and layout are the host's reconstruction. [[QinChidao]]'s standardized imperial movement is infrastructure as well as display.
- Sacred and written projections: [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-122-1-jiemi-qinshihuang-fengchan-weishenme-xuan-taishan-lkcyayuqrtsgy-k4bhdvy-l-lapo]] reports a 219 BCE [[Taishan]] [[FengshanRitualLegitimacy]] ceremony, with [[LiangfuMountain]] in the paired mountain-rite geography, after more than 70 Qi-Lu scholars disagreed on procedure; it drew on Qin's own heaven-sacrifice practices, not an uncontested ancient script. [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-122-2-ribenren-daodi-shi-bushi-xufu-de-houdai-lhgbbhg3zw8rlx1jjb7lpb5ihihh]] describes [[QiEightSpirits]] worship and the [[LangyaInscriptionPoliticalProgram]] at [[LangyaMountain]]: agriculture praised, commerce restrained, writing and measures unified, law obeyed and imperial reach proclaimed in former [[QiState]] space. The inscription states aspiration, not universal compliance.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Five notes come from the same history series and interpret transmitted accounts, not five independent attestations. [[AuspiciousOmenPolitics]] is retrospective legitimacy rhetoric, not proof of heavenly sanction. Cosmic capital mapping and exact fengshan steps are debated reconstructions. The water-phase law ideal is limited by the emperor's exception; armed seizure and cultural incorporation did not remove resentment. [[MandateOfHeavenLegitimacy]] is claimed rather than observed.

## What Changed
- Distinguished state vocabulary, succession memory, cosmological package, material display and public inscription.
- Kept uncertain Taiyi and ritual reconstructions separate from the more direct naming account.
