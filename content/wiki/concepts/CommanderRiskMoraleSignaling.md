# 主将冒险式士气信号 / Commander Risk Morale Signaling

## Definition
主将冒险式士气信号 is a battlefield leadership pattern in which a commander personally accepts conspicuous danger to recover trapped personnel and demonstrate commitment to subordinates.

## Current Synthesis
Hanji 1112 presents [[CaoRen|曹仁]]'s extraction of [[NiuJinLateHan|牛金]] and other surrounded soldiers as both rescue and signal. The action preserves people directly, while [[ChenJiaoLateHan|陈矫]]'s astonishment and the garrison's renewed confidence show how visible risk can communicate that troops will not simply be abandoned.

The case does not make extreme personal exposure a general command rule. The same episode preserves [[ZhangHongLateHan|张纮]]'s warning that a ruler or supreme commander should not treat vanguard bravery as his primary duty. The synthesis is therefore conditional: personal risk can have exceptional morale value, but command continuity and strategic responsibility may impose the opposite requirement.

## Key Claims
- A successful extraction can restore both personnel and confidence.
- Visible personal exposure makes commitment costly and therefore credible to observers.
- The morale value depends on success and context; reckless loss of the commander could damage the whole force.

## Evidence
- Rescue sequence: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1112-bei-digu-de-caowei-mingjiang-zhanchang-shang-you-duo-meng-lkqecaphucolkdcsutsvjyxounln|Hanji 1112]] says Cao Ren reaches Niu Jin, returns for other soldiers, and comes back alive.
- Observer effect: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1112-bei-digu-de-caowei-mingjiang-zhanchang-shang-you-duo-meng-lkqecaphucolkdcsutsvjyxounln|Hanji 1112]] attributes Chen Jiao's “真天人也” response and renewed defender morale to the action.
- Command boundary: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1112-bei-digu-de-caowei-mingjiang-zhanchang-shang-you-duo-meng-lkqecaphucolkdcsutsvjyxounln|Hanji 1112]] also has Zhang Hong distinguish Sun Quan's strategic duty from a forward fighter's role.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The case is a successful-action narrative and cannot reveal the full distribution of failed rescue attempts or reckless commander losses. Exact force sizes, route, repeated entry, casualties, dialogue, and morale effects remain source-scoped; the concept should not be read as a blanket endorsement of personal heroics.

## What Changed
- Established the concept while preserving the episode's own warning about commander-role asymmetry.
