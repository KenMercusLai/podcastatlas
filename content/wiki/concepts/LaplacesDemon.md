# Laplace's Demon / 拉普拉斯妖

Laplace's demon is the predictability image used in [[ep256-ai-shidai-ziyou-yizhi-hai-cunzai-ma-lk9aci8oqnwerk26jy683nbdddcx]] to explain how modern science pressures [[FreeWill|free will]]. If an intelligence knew all particle positions, states, and laws, it could in principle calculate the future, making choice look like part of a completed causal chain.

The episode treats the demon as a useful challenge, not as the final answer. [[TuMotuo|土摩托]] notes that quantum uncertainty and emergence complicate simple one-link-after-another explanations, while the larger free-will question also includes [[BiologicalAgency]], meaning, and responsibility.

## Key Claims
- Laplace's demon turns lawful nature into a maximum-prediction thought experiment.
- The thought experiment is strongest as a pressure on naive self-sovereignty, not as a complete account of lived choice.
- Emergence and life make causal explanation harder to reduce to a clean mechanical chain.
