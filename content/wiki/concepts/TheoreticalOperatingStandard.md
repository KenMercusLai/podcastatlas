# Theoretical Operating Standard / 理论上该有的样子

Theoretical operating standard is [[Albert]]'s phrase in [[youhua-shenglv-erfei-peilv-ba-yi-jian-shi-zuodao-lilun-shang-gaiyou-de-yangzi-duitan-lianxu-chuangyezhe-albert-lu0vamaawctwva3qblnsf99esar2]] for trying to make a chosen thing as it should theoretically be. He distinguishes it from deciding what is correct to do: it is a "how to" discipline, not the original "why."

In the source, the standard gives [[WinRateStartupStrategy]] an execution ethic. A founder may choose a more grounded opportunity, but the work is still difficult because real products, organizations, culture, strategy, and management are usually far from the ideal state.

## Key Claims
- The standard is aspirational rather than descriptive: the real product or company will usually be far away from the ideal.
- It pushes against both sloppy opportunism and cargo-cult imitation of strong companies such as [[ByteDance]].
- Albert connects it to [[DuanYongping]]'s emphasis on simple underlying business principles such as right business and right people.
- The idea supports customer orientation: the product should be judged by whether it produces the result users need, not by whether it matches a financing story.
