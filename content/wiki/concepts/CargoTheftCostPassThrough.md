# Cargo Theft Cost Pass-Through

## Definition
Cargo theft cost pass-through is the process by which a stolen load creates replacement, disruption, insurance, freight, and risk-management costs that spread from the immediate victim to carriers, producers, retailers, new entrants, and consumers.

## Current Synthesis
The theft cases in [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356]] show that invoice value is only the first loss. Reproduction consumes packaging and production capacity; claims can raise premiums; unavailable or expensive coverage changes carrier viability; freight rates can rise; and producers may embed higher logistics costs in prices. Fear of premium increases can also suppress reporting, weakening the data used to price and prevent theft.

## Key Claims
- Replacement cost includes coordinated inputs and lost production capacity, not only product value.
- Insurance repricing can move a single theft into future operating costs.
- Carriers can pass higher risk costs into freight rates where market conditions permit.
- Producers and retailers can transmit freight increases to consumers.
- Coverage withdrawal can reduce small-business entry or survival even without a new theft.
- Underreporting creates a feedback problem for both measurement and insurance pricing.

## Evidence
- Replacement burden - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356]] says recreating the bourbon shipment required packaging materials and production planning.
- Insurance and rate pathway - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356]] connects theft to premiums, carrier rates, producer prices, and coverage withdrawal.
- Entry and reporting effects - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356]] reports concern that insurance scarcity deters owner-operators and that premium fear encourages firms to absorb losses without reporting.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Pass-through is conditional, not automatic: contracts, competition, deductibles, coverage, margins, and bargaining power determine who ultimately absorbs each loss. The episode does not estimate the consumer price effect or separate theft-driven premium changes from broader insurance-market pressures.

## What Changed
- Initial synthesis distinguishes direct cargo value from replacement, insurance, market-entry, and measurement effects.
