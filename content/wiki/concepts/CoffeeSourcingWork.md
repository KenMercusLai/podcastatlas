# Coffee Sourcing Work / 寻豆工作

## Definition
Coffee sourcing work is the repeated operational practice of finding, testing, comparing, and buying green coffee through samples, roasting, cupping, producer contact, and trade judgment.

## Current Synthesis
The source treats sourcing as a disciplined bridge between the cup and the field. [[YuWenjie|余文杰]]'s work in [[Yunnan|云南]] shows that finding good coffee is not just discovering a romantic hidden lot; it requires sampling networks, sensory calibration, local residence, farmer contact, and judgment about whether a lot can become a viable purchase or direct-trade product.

## Key Claims
- Sourcing depends on repeated sample handling: receiving beans, roasting samples, cupping, comparing, and selecting.
- Blind cupping helps separate cup quality from origin labels and seller descriptions.
- Long residence in a producing area can reveal small farmers, plots, and batches that ordinary trade channels may miss.
- Sourcing includes commercial judgment about direct trade, import, value, and product fit.
- The role connects sensory skill to supply-chain and agricultural reality.

## Evidence
- Repeated sample work: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] lists sample collection, roasting, cupping, selection, purchasing, and direct-trade decisions as Yu Wenjie's daily work.
- Blind cupping: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] says blind tests are used to reduce the effect of external information on sensory judgment.
- Producing-area residence: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] says living in Mangshi helped Yu Wenjie find small farmers, plots, and small processing batches.
- Commercial judgment: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] describes evaluating whether beans are suitable for direct trade or import.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not provide a general job taxonomy for every coffee buyer. It defines sourcing through one practitioner's Yunnan-centered work.
