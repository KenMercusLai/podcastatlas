# 恩赏负债式忠诚压力 / Grace-Debt Loyalty Pressure

恩赏负债式忠诚压力 / grace-debt loyalty pressure is the mechanism [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-39-lishishang-zhangyi-shi-zenyang-nudui-qiangdi-de-lv2gc2wtcxuhugfgixk7rbbhpa6x]] uses to explain [[YingSi|秦惠文王]]'s treatment of [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]. Zhang Yi has not succeeded in making [[WeiState|魏国]] accept his [[LianhengStrategy|连横]] plan, yet the Qin ruler still secretly gives him more favor.

The episode reads this not as simple generosity but as pressure through obligation. A subordinate who receives benefit without matching achievement may feel shame, anxiety, and a need to repay the ruler later. In Zhang Yi's case, that emotional debt helps explain why a man formally serving Wei can remain oriented toward [[QinState|秦国]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-165-2-hanxin-weihe-zhisi-ye-bu-beipan-liubang-lrgtvbdyc9cwbfhaix1aabsmqhhb|Hanji 165-2]] extends the mechanism from excess favor to remembered survival and recognition. [[HanXin|韩信]] says [[LiuBang|刘邦]] gave him a chariot, clothing, food, and status, and uses that memory to reject [[KuaiChe|蒯彻 / 蒯通]]'s proposal that he preserve himself as a third pole. The pressure here is not shame over undeserved reward alone; it is the feeling that past recognition creates a continuing burden of another person's troubles.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-166-jiran-xiangyu-bujiang-wude-kan-liubang-ruhe-fuchou-ltwwbn9rzeqzzimmxxfihb9-drj1|Hanji 166]] shows that pressure hardening into refusal. Han Xin not only remembers Liu Bang's earlier gifts; he also believes that such merit and gratitude make betrayal wrong and future dispossession unlikely. The episode leaves that belief as honorable but politically risky.

## Key Claims
- Hanji 166 adds that grace-debt can make a warning emotionally unacceptable even when the adviser names real future risk.
- Favor can bind more tightly when it exceeds visible merit because it creates an unsettled debt.
- The mechanism works through the recipient's fear of not deserving the reward, not through explicit punishment.
- In this source, the pressure converts Zhang Yi's ambiguous Wei post into a stronger Qin-facing alignment.
- The concept helps separate personal loyalty pressure from institutional office: Zhang Yi's title is in Wei, but his obligation points toward Qin.
- Hanji 165-2 adds that benefit memory can overpower a self-preservation argument even when the subordinate's later success has made him politically dangerous to the patron.
