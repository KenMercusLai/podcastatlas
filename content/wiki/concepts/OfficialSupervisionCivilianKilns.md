# Official Supervision, Civilian Kilns / 官监民烧

Official Supervision, Civilian Kilns / 官监民烧 is the Yuan-era production arrangement named in [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]]. The episode uses the 1278 Fuliang porcelain office to show that the state could supervise and order porcelain without directly owning every kiln.

The concept matters because it sits between private craft and full official factory. In [[Jingdezhen]], court demand, market capacity, and skilled civilian production were linked early, prefiguring later [[ImperialKilnSystem]] interactions between official orders and local workshops.
