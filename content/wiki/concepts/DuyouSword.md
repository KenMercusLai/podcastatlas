# Duyou Sword / 杜邮剑

Duyou Sword / 杜邮剑 is the cultural allusion [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-97-1-baiqi-zhisi-lrqoq1sbxljbvuayq3o1hxebxhu6]] builds from [[BaiQi|白起]]'s forced death. After the failed Qin pressure around [[Handan|邯郸]], [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] strips Bai Qi of rank, sends him away, and then sends a sword when Bai Qi reaches 杜邮. The episode treats the object and place together as a later shorthand for the lethal end of an overmighty or politically exposed successful minister.

The allusion carries two moral directions at once. From a court-politics angle, Bai Qi's case warns that enormous military merit does not remove a minister's subordinate position. A commander who refuses orders, speaks resentfully, and becomes unusable during crisis can turn earlier indispensable value into a perceived future threat. From the Changping angle, the same death also recalls the mass killing of surrendered Zhao troops: Bai Qi himself identifies that act as enough reason to die, linking the allusion to [[SurrenderedPrisonerSecurityDilemma|杀降安全困境]] and [[WarringStatesWarBrutalization|战国战争残酷化]] rather than only to unfair court politics.

The concept therefore sits beside [[PowerExitTrap|权力退场困境]] but is more specific. It names the literary and historical code produced when merit, ruler suspicion, command refusal, and remembered atrocity converge in a forced suicide. Later writers can use "杜邮" or "杜邮剑" to mourn a功臣, condemn a killer, or argue for功成身退, depending on whether they foreground Qin's political disposal of Bai Qi or Bai Qi's own moral debt after [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]].

## Key Claims
- "杜邮剑" is not only a death scene; it is a reusable allusion for a功臣 whose accumulated merit cannot protect him from ruler suspicion and court danger.
- The allusion remains morally double: Bai Qi can be pitied as not dying for the immediate charge while still bearing the guilt of Changping.
- The episode uses the allusion to connect military success, political subordination, and exit failure rather than treating Bai Qi's death as a simple battlefield consequence.
- Because different later readers can emphasize different parts of the story, the wiki should preserve the allusion as contested memory rather than a single verdict.
