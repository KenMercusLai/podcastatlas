# Human Meaning Under AI / AI 下的人类意义

## Definition
Human meaning under AI is the question of what reasons, values, relationships, or experiences justify human continuation when artificial intelligence can imitate, augment, or outperform many human outputs.

## Current Synthesis
[[caikangyong-luoyonghao-zai-canku-de-shijie-xunzhao-zizai-yu-hejie-baochi-cibei-yu-zhenshi-lqbw_yofwhs8rpt3w0xse_g9ajj1]] raises this question through [[LuoYonghao|罗永浩]] and [[CaiKangyong|蔡康永]] rather than through technical AI forecasting. Luo frames AI as a deep conversation partner and possible species-level threat; Cai resists the conclusion that humans will simply be eliminated, because humans will keep finding reasons and methods to live. The concept's current value is not a prediction, but a bridge between AI capability, human relation, and meaning-making under finitude.

## Key Claims
- AI can become a self-reflection partner when used over time with context rather than as casual chat.
- Synthetic imitation can reduce public-figure scarcity without settling whether emotional effect, consent, and authorship are legitimate.
- Existential AI debate often hides a meaning debate: can humans make a case for themselves beyond efficiency?
- Human value may lie in lived meaning, relationship, love, mortality, and process rather than output alone.
- The source preserves disagreement: Luo is more worried about non-malicious displacement, while Cai is more confident humans will find a way to continue.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This concept is philosophical and source-scoped. The episode does not provide technical evidence about timelines, alignment, model capability, or actual AI governance. It also does not prove that human meaning will persuade any future system; it records a human-facing debate about why continuation matters.
