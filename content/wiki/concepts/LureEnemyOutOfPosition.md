# Lure Enemy Out of Position / 诱敌离位

## Definition
Lure Enemy Out of Position / 诱敌离位 is the tactic of using bait, false signals, exposed targets, concessions, withdrawals, or incentives to make an opponent leave a safer formation, posture, route, base, or force distribution before the prepared strike lands.

## Current Synthesis
The current synthesis treats the pattern as a position-control tactic that scales from battlefield formation to campaign design. Early sources show exposed small forces, false weakness, feigned retreat, decreasing stoves, and frontier livestock bait drawing commanders into pursuit or encirclement. Hanji 1038 adds the named [[SunCe|孙策]] case of 调虎离山: a strong enemy is pulled away from his [[RootBasePriority|root base]] so the rear can be seized. Hanji 1023 adds a retreat-path version at [[AnzhongLateHan|安重]], where Cao Cao makes withdrawal look like flight so Zhang Xiu and Liu Biao's blocking force leaves its advantage before the ambush. Hanji 1043 adds a relief-operation version at [[BaimaReliefLateHan|White Horse]], where a feigned Yellow River crossing and rear threat pull Yuan Shao's troops west while Cao's light force strikes the actual siege point. Hanji 1057 adds a burned-camp version at [[BowangSlopeBattleLateHan|Bowang]], where [[LiuBei|刘备]] makes withdrawal look like weakness and draws [[XiahouDun|夏侯惇]] into an ambush despite [[LiDian|李典]]'s warning.

## Key Claims
- A strong enemy can be made weaker by changing its position, formation, operating condition, base security, or force distribution before the decisive strike.
- Bait has strategic value only when paired with a prepared attack, ambush, encirclement, rear-base seizure, or rapid relief strike.
- The false signal can be visible weakness, concession, decreasing camp stoves, exposed people or livestock, feigned retreat, gifts, promised gain, threatened rear movement, or intentional camp burning.
- Opponent psychology matters: contempt, greed, desire for visible merit, overconfidence, urgency, or defensive concern can make the lure persuasive.
- Terrain often converts movement into vulnerability, especially when pursuit enters narrow, wooded, riverine, or otherwise constraining spaces.
- The pattern scales from tactical pursuit to campaign-level displacement, political misreading, diversionary relief, and warning failures inside the deceived command.

## Evidence
- Foundational position-control pattern: [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-16-sunbin-pangjuan-enyuan-jiuge-guiling-zhi-zhan-lryhzkc1-xsvfjhvicu8yxa9d2jr|Zhouji 16]] presents Sun Bin's answer to a stalemate as exposing a weaker force and attacking once the enemy leaves its strong position; [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-19-maling-zhi-zhan-lhbapt09x2ptdqx0uopkp8apbada|Zhouji 19]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-20-pangjuan-si-ci-shu-xia-ls-8-3jhz5q1c5ca11mhdzmz4wg4|Zhouji 20]] extend the same logic through Maling pursuit and decreasing stoves.
- False weakness and concession signals: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-175-bikan-liubang-de-huangjia-liyi-ke-los9hsomndnhyqpxafstgyquomzf|Hanji 175]] uses Modu's concessions to Donghu, [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-177-beikun-baidengshan-liubang-mingxuan-yixian-ljdmxf6bej8ju0gmvm9cp48gy0on|Hanji 177]] uses weak-looking Xiongnu displays before Baideng, and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-925-tuoxie-bushi-rensong-xuehui-tuoxie-lu-geng-changliang-limafqskgblrwetestyh39w-hzm4|Hanji 925]] uses Huangfu Song's weak visible posture before the Changshe fire attack.
- Terrain, pursuit, and contempt: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-164-2-xiangyu-touhao-dajiang-zenme-shu-gei-hanxin-de-lhe-lmb1zrzipbongg02gmnmb4l|Hanji 164-2]] shows Long Ju abandoning a safer posture to chase Han Xin into the Weishui trap, while [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-105-zhanzheng-huayang-zhaoguo-limu-zui-hui-shua-ljtqdg46a8o13zzsjv-p-fpol5oh|Qinji 105]] shows Li Mu building Xiongnu overconfidence over years before exposing people and livestock.
- Base-emptying campaign pattern: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1038-sanshiliu-ji-zhi-diaohu-lishan-sunce-zhiqu-wancheng-lhomk8hfdidl61degwbqtj2xkj9z|Hanji 1038]] explicitly labels Sun Ce's Lujiang operation 调虎离山: gifts and deferential language draw [[LiuXunLateHan|刘勋]] toward Shangliao, while Sun Ce and [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] take [[WanChengLujiang|皖城]].
- False escape from blocked retreat: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1023-jiemi-caocao-yu-zhangxiu-de-bingfa-boyi-lphbnmfdu0x5w7zjra7k0o8cyel4|Hanji 1023]] shows Cao Cao opening a narrow night path at Anzhong and making withdrawal look like flight so Zhang Xiu and Liu Biao commit to pursuit before the ambush lands from front and rear.
- Diversionary relief at White Horse: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1043-yuanshao-tongma-caocao-jing-zhihaole-tade-piantouteng-ltibjj6xizsrynpgr9oyf-q3ydna|Hanji 1043]] says Cao Cao first pretends to cross the Yellow River and attack Yuan Shao's rear, drawing Yuan forces west, then moves light troops rapidly to White Horse before Yan Liang can prepare.
- Burned-camp withdrawal at Bowang: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1057-da-bu-liao-zhang-de-jiangjun-bushi-youxiu-de-shufajia-ljhy16leevjd8i-7vmuxtqm96ytg|Hanji 1057]] says Liu Bei burns his own camp and withdraws, Li Dian recognizes the retreat plus narrow wooded terrain as ambush signals, and Xiahou Dun pursues anyway before being rescued.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Not every retreat, gift, concession, camp burning, or diversion is a lure. The pattern requires a false or selectively displayed opening, a predictable enemy misread, and a prepared exploitation plan. Hanji 1023 shows that a lure can coexist with real danger: Cao Cao's retreat route is genuinely under pressure, but the displayed escape path is still used to manage enemy movement. Hanji 1043 shows a lure that divides force rather than drawing all of it into a trap. Hanji 1057 shows that warning may exist inside the deceived camp, so the tactic's success can depend on command hierarchy overriding correct diagnosis.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 1057's Bowang burned-camp withdrawal as a deliberate retreat-bait variant.
- Extended the terrain qualification to include narrow and wooded pursuit routes.
- Added warning failure inside the target command as a factor in successful lures.
