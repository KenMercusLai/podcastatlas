# Software-Defined Vehicle / 软件定义汽车

## Definition

A software-defined vehicle is a car whose capabilities, user experience, and service life are materially shaped by software, connectivity, electronic control, over-the-air updates, purchasable functions, and persistent user settings after the physical vehicle is delivered.

## Current Synthesis

The episode presents electric vehicles as especially compatible with software-mediated control because motors and electronic systems can respond directly to digital commands. OTA updates, replaceable components, connected services, automated parking and cruise functions, paid feature packages, vehicle-to-vehicle communication, and portable user profiles can make the car an evolving platform rather than a fixed mechanical product.

That promise has hard boundaries. A vehicle has a much longer service life and higher safety burden than a phone; rapid iteration can strand early buyers, connectivity creates cybersecurity and control risks, and entertainment or social functions cannot displace transport reliability. Software definition therefore expands the vehicle's lifecycle only when hardware, safety, fallback behavior, support, and long-term compatibility remain credible.

## Key Claims

- Electronic propulsion and control make some vehicle functions more directly software-addressable.
- OTA updates and modular services can improve or extend a delivered vehicle after purchase.
- Persistent settings can make users experience different shared or rented vehicles as personalized environments.
- Rapid feature cycles can increase perceived obsolescence in a long-lived, expensive product.
- Connectivity and automation make cybersecurity, functional safety, and control fallback core vehicle requirements.
- The car remains a transport system first; entertainment, social, and service layers are subordinate to safe mobility.

## Evidence

- Post-sale evolution: [[ep142-shizhijinri-ni-hai-kanbuqi-guochan-xinnengyuanche-ma-cmcoosidyoetcpz-zabeibq]] describes OTA updates, component replacement, and software feature packages.
- Direct control: [[ep142-shizhijinri-ni-hai-kanbuqi-guochan-xinnengyuanche-ma-cmcoosidyoetcpz-zabeibq]] connects electronic motor control to adaptive cruise, automated parking, and later automation.
- Personalization: [[ep142-shizhijinri-ni-hai-kanbuqi-guochan-xinnengyuanche-ma-cmcoosidyoetcpz-zabeibq]] imagines shared vehicles loading seat and preference profiles across locations.
- Safety boundary: [[ep142-shizhijinri-ni-hai-kanbuqi-guochan-xinnengyuanche-ma-cmcoosidyoetcpz-zabeibq]] raises intrusion and control-loss risk and insists that transport utility precede added functions.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source is a 2020 discussion and does not evaluate specific software architectures, update reliability, security incidents, support periods, subscription economics, repairability, interoperability, or automated-driving performance. Electric propulsion can simplify some control paths, but it does not by itself establish safe autonomy or secure remote operation. The shared-car and mobile-social-space examples are forward-looking scenarios rather than verified adoption outcomes.
