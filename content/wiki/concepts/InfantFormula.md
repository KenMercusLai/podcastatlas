# Infant Formula

Infant formula is the regulated baby-food category at the center of [[bobbie-laura-modi-how-a-baby-formula-startup-took-market-share-from-two-industry-giants-4488842b-74d3-40ed-8776-5831c5934e69]]. The episode presents formula as a necessary alternative for many families rather than a simple competitor to breastfeeding, while also stressing that it is not an ordinary direct-to-consumer food product.

The category matters to the wiki because it combines emotional, nutritional, regulatory, manufacturing, and policy constraints. [[Bobbie]] has to manage [[FormulaFeedingStigma]], [[FoodAndDrugAdministration|FDA]] requirements, compliant claim language, long production lead times, incumbent concentration around [[Enfamil]] and [[Similac]], and access limits through [[WICFormulaContracting]].

## Key Claims
- Formula can be both medically and emotionally necessary even when breast milk is treated as the ideal.
- Product differentiation is constrained by nutrition science, FDA rules, label claims, and manufacturing standards.
- Customer pull can appear through unmet demand, but supply capacity and public-benefit contracts determine who can actually be served.
- In infant formula, trust is not only brand preference; parents must believe the product is safe enough to feed a baby repeatedly.
