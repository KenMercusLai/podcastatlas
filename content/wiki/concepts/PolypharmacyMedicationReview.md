# Polypharmacy Medication Review / 多重用药审查

## Definition
Polypharmacy medication review is the structured reconciliation of every medicine a person uses so that indications, duplication, interactions, doses, timing, formulations, adverse effects, and practical adherence can be assessed together.

## Current Synthesis
VOL.224 treats multi-drug safety as more than asking whether two pills “conflict.” 大盆 reports chronic lipid- and urate-lowering medicines plus episodic cold and other drugs, tells new prescribers what he already takes, and worries enough about combinations to invent twenty-minute gaps that turn dosing into a two-hour process. The useful behavior is complete disclosure; the fragile behavior is designing a complex schedule without drug-specific advice.

The episode identifies pharmacists, pharmacy departments, and medication clinics as possible coordination points when several conditions and prescribers make the regimen difficult. Review includes whether a tablet may be split, because modified-release or specially coated formulations can behave differently, and whether simplifying the plan can reduce error without changing therapeutic intent.

## Key Claims
- A complete current medicine list is the starting point for interaction and duplication review across conditions and prescribers.
- Safe coordination includes indication, dose, timing, formulation, adverse effects, monitoring, and adherence burden rather than only pairwise interactions.
- Arbitrary time gaps do not reliably prevent interactions and can increase complexity, delay, or missed doses.
- Not every tablet can be safely split; formulation-specific instructions matter.
- Pharmacist-led review can complement prescriber decisions when several medicines or conditions make the regimen hard to manage.

- Pharmacy support: [[vol-224-tuokouxiu-yanyuan-dapen-de-jiankangju-meiganjue-de-bing-cai-zui-weixian-dai-ni-raokai-kanbing-lushang-de-yinxingkeng-lnndb6nhzfm4xvtw2eg9rvb6zkty|VOL.224]] describes pharmacy departments or pharmacist clinics as routes for reviewing complex regimens and participating in inpatient consultation.
- Formulation boundary: [[vol-224-tuokouxiu-yanyuan-dapen-de-jiankangju-meiganjue-de-bing-cai-zui-weixian-dai-ni-raokai-kanbing-lushang-de-yinxingkeng-lnndb6nhzfm4xvtw2eg9rvb6zkty|VOL.224]] warns that modified-release or specially coated tablets may not be suitable for splitting.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not identify every product, strength, formulation, schedule, organ-function constraint, or actual interaction in the guest's regimen. It therefore cannot show that his medicines should be combined, separated, split, stopped, or continued in a particular way. Access to pharmacist clinics varies, and medication reconciliation does not replace urgent assessment for serious adverse effects or overdose.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to distinguish complete medicine disclosure from improvised interaction management.
- Added formulation, simplification, and pharmacist-review branches to the safety model.
