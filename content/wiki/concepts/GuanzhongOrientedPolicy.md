## Definition
Guanzhong-Oriented Policy is Chen Yinke's label for measures by which [[YuwenTai|Yuwen Tai]] recast diverse people and forces as a Guanzhong-centered political community able to compete with the stronger east.

## Current Synthesis
The policy is best understood as coalition construction under scarcity. Registered origins were redirected toward Guanzhong, commanders received Xianbei state surnames, and military hierarchy and classical institutions supplied shared positions and symbols. These measures did not simply restore an old ethnic order: they created a usable identity across different backgrounds and paired military cohesion with literate administration. The episode finds this integrative program less controversial than claims about how long the resulting [[GuanlongGroup|Guanlong Group]] endured.

## Key Claims
- The policy responds to Western Wei's weaker population, production, and military resources.
- Regional identity can be administratively produced rather than merely inherited.
- Bestowed surnames bind commanders symbolically to the ruling order.
- Military and bureaucratic redesign give the shared identity organizational form.
- Xianbei symbolism and classical Zhou offices serve different constituencies within one integration project.
- Formation of a coalition does not establish its unchanged survival through Sui and Tang.

## Evidence
- Structural motive: [[ltxaost4pgqhjdirwas15vizersj-ltxaost4pgqhjdirwas15vizersj]] contrasts Yuwen Tai's weak base with Gao Huan's advantages.
- Identity measures: [[ltxaost4pgqhjdirwas15vizersj-ltxaost4pgqhjdirwas15vizersj]] reports origin reassignment and bestowed Xianbei surnames.
- Organizational measures: [[ltxaost4pgqhjdirwas15vizersj-ltxaost4pgqhjdirwas15vizersj]] joins identity construction to fubing and the Eight Pillars.
- Dual idiom: [[ltxaost4pgqhjdirwas15vizersj-ltxaost4pgqhjdirwas15vizersj]] interprets Xianbei revival and Zhouli archaism as complementary recruitment tools.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
“Policy” is a retrospective analytical label and may imply more central design and coherence than uneven measures possessed in practice. The episode summary does not independently establish implementation scale, local reception, or whether every reform belonged to one master plan.

## What Changed
- Established the policy as constructed regional identity joined to military and administrative organization.
