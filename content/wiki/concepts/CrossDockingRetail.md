# Cross-Docking Retail

Cross-docking retail is the logistics pattern in [[costco-1]] where goods move through distribution centers quickly from inbound supplier trucks to outbound store trucks instead of sitting in storage. [[Costco]] uses this with pallet handling and warehouse stores to reduce unpacking, sorting, and shelf-facing labor.

The source contrasts Costco with more conventional replenishment models and makes cross-docking part of the broader [[WarehouseClubModel]]. It works best when the retailer has high-volume SKUs, predictable demand, and stores designed to receive palletized goods.

## Key Claims
- Cross-docking lowers handling and storage time when demand is predictable enough.
- The pattern reinforces [[LimitedSKUOperatingModel]] because fewer high-volume items are easier to route quickly.
- Palletized store operations reduce labor relative to item-by-item shelf presentation.
- Logistics simplicity supports low markup only when it is actually passed through to customers.
