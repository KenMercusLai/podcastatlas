# Dangerous Guest Release Risk / 放虎归山式宾客释放风险

## Definition
Dangerous guest release risk is the failure mode where a protected or dependent high-potential actor is allowed to leave with enough resources or authorization to rebuild independent power.

## Current Synthesis
Hanji 1040 turns [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s handling of [[LiuBei|刘备]] into the source case. Earlier shelter logic makes killing Liu Bei costly for Cao Cao's reputation, but releasing him on a military assignment creates a different risk: Liu Bei can use legitimate movement, troops, and the [[YuanShu|袁术]] interception pretext to escape containment. Once outside, he kills [[CheZhouLateHan|车胄]], regains [[Xuzhou|徐州]], links with local forces, and approaches [[YuanShao|袁绍]].

## Key Claims
- Protection and release are distinct decisions; sparing a guest does not require giving that guest independent command space.
- The risk is highest when the guest has latent reputation, loyal followers, and an old regional base to reactivate.
- Adviser warnings matter because they often identify the difference between reputational protection and operational freedom.
- A release may look like a normal assignment while functioning as an exit route from surveillance.
- The failure becomes visible only after local actors join the released guest and convert movement into renewed force.

## Evidence
- Adviser warning: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1040-luanshi-yingxiong-caocao-weihe-dudu-kanhao-liubei-lrazzwo4bjspvwmnkx5hoar1iq3x]] reports that Cheng Yu, Guo Jia, and Dong Zhao oppose letting Liu Bei leave with troops.
- Operational exit: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1040-luanshi-yingxiong-caocao-weihe-dudu-kanhao-liubei-lrazzwo4bjspvwmnkx5hoar1iq3x]] says Cao Cao sends Liu Bei and Zhu Ling to intercept Yuan Shu and regrets it too late.
- Rebuilt base: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1040-luanshi-yingxiong-caocao-weihe-dudu-kanhao-liubei-lrazzwo4bjspvwmnkx5hoar1iq3x]] says Liu Bei kills Che Zhou, leaves Guan Yu at Xiapi, returns to Xiaopei, and gathers tens of thousands.
- Wider alignment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1040-luanshi-yingxiong-caocao-weihe-dudu-kanhao-liubei-lrazzwo4bjspvwmnkx5hoar1iq3x]] says Liu Bei secretly contacts Yuan Shao after restoring Xuzhou force.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept does not prove Cao Cao should have killed Liu Bei. It distinguishes the reputation cost of killing a sheltered talent from the operational risk of allowing that same actor to leave with military autonomy. A safer containment strategy is implied by the source but not fully specified.
