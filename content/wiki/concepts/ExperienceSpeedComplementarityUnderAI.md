# Experience–Speed Complementarity Under AI / AI时代的经验—速度互补

## Definition
Experience–speed complementarity under AI is the proposition that rapid tool-enabled research and long-cycle judgment are different but potentially reinforcing forms of professional value.

## Current Synthesis
In EP393, AI compresses information gathering, report reading, and paper decomposition in investment work, which can increase the value of tool fluency, learning speed, and high-volume execution. That shift may favor younger workers or make age-based replacement anxiety more salient.

The counterweight is not seniority by itself. Experience becomes valuable when it has been converted into pattern recognition across market cycles, abstraction, resource coordination, leadership, and accountable final judgment. AI can retrieve history and monitor indicators, but the episode retains responsibility for action with the investor.

## Key Claims
- AI can materially accelerate research and information preparation in investing.
- Tool fluency and familiarity with emerging founders can shift hiring demand toward younger workers.
- Years of service are not automatically differentiated value if the role remains interchangeable execution.
- Complete-cycle experience can improve interpretation of sharp reversals and regime changes.
- Human judgment remains necessary where decisions are uncertain, consequential, and accountable.

## Evidence
- Workflow acceleration: [[ep393-30-zhaogongzuo-tiaocao-women-zai-danyou-shenme-yiqi-duguo-rensheng-de-aodesai-shike-gkwrirwoyp8hael-1gt5tggj]] reports extensive AI assistance in research, report reading, and paper analysis.
- Age and hiring: [[ep393-30-zhaogongzuo-tiaocao-women-zai-danyou-shenme-yiqi-duguo-rensheng-de-aodesai-shike-gkwrirwoyp8hael-1gt5tggj]] describes institutions seeking younger workers for high-volume AI opportunity discovery.
- Cycle judgment: [[ep393-30-zhaogongzuo-tiaocao-women-zai-danyou-shenme-yiqi-duguo-rensheng-de-aodesai-shike-gkwrirwoyp8hael-1gt5tggj]] contrasts workers formed in a rising market with practitioners who have experienced reversals.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not measure productivity, investment returns, age effects, hiring prevalence, or causal substitution. Younger workers can have strong judgment, senior workers can be highly tool-fluent, and experience can preserve obsolete assumptions. Complementarity depends on continued learning, feedback, decision quality, and the actual task rather than age alone.

## What Changed
- Established a task-level distinction between AI-enabled speed and cycle-tested accountable judgment.
- Qualified age-based claims by making learning and converted experience, not age itself, the mechanism.
