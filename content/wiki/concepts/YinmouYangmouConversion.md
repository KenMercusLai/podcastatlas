# 阴谋阳谋转换 / Yinmou-Yangmou Conversion

阴谋阳谋转换 / yinmou-yangmou conversion is the strategic pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-38-gao-jianzhi-de-zhangyi-bu-yiban-lobhcpz679psy54k0iduccqkfcrn]] extracts from [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]'s move from [[QinState|秦国]] to [[WeiState|魏国]]. Zhang Yi first proposes a hidden design to [[QinWuwang|秦武王]]: if he goes to Wei, [[QiState|齐国]] will attack Wei because it hates him, and Qin can exploit the disorder.

The conversion happens when the hidden design is disclosed. After Qi attacks Wei, Zhang Yi sends [[FengXiWarringStates|冯喜]] to reveal the earlier pitch. That makes the Qi ruler rethink the campaign: the same information that would normally expose the plot now makes continuing the attack look like serving Zhang Yi's design.

The concept matters because it separates "clever secrecy" from a stronger diplomatic move. Zhang Yi's plan does not collapse when known; knowledge of the plan changes the other side's incentives. The episode summarizes this as a movement between false and real, hidden and open, where disclosure becomes another stage of manipulation.

## Key Claims
- A hidden plot can become an open strategy when disclosure changes the opponent's payoff calculation.
- The tactic depends on the opponent caring about not being used by the strategist.
- Disclosure can be timed after the opponent has already committed enough to fear embarrassment or manipulation.
- In this source, the move belongs to [[ZonghengjiaDiplomacy|纵横家外交]] because it uses reputation, emotion, state rivalry, and messenger control as diplomatic instruments.
