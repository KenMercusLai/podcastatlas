# Cursus Honorum / 晋升官阶

## Definition

Cursus honorum / 晋升官阶 is the Roman political ladder through which ambitious men moved toward the consulship, the highest ordinary office and the usual summit of a republican political career.

## Current Synthesis

Episode 701 uses the cursus honorum to explain why [[GaiusMarius]]'s rise was difficult and destabilizing. The ladder was not only a neutral career sequence. It was embedded in family prestige, aristocratic patronage, age and office expectations, and competition for public honor. For a novus homo from Arpinum, even one consulship seemed an extraordinary achievement, which is why the Plutarch omen of seven consulships is treated as almost impossible.

The concept becomes politically important when office ambition meets military command. Marius's effort to move from deputy under [[QuintusCaeciliusMetellusNumidicus|Metellus]] to consul and African commander shows how the highest republican office could become a route to taking over war command from an aristocratic patron.

## Key Claims

- The cursus honorum structured elite political ambition through staged offices and the consulship goal.
- Family tradition and aristocratic networks shaped who could plausibly reach the top.
- A novus homo could use military reputation to challenge lineage expectations.
- The consulship mattered because it could carry command authority, not only status.
- The ladder became unstable when electoral success was used to displace established commanders.

## Evidence

- Office ladder: [[701-roman-civil-war-rise-of-the-general-part-1-glt1262857909]] describes the cursus honorum as the Roman political ladder with the consulship as its finishing line.
- Improbable achievement: [[701-roman-civil-war-rise-of-the-general-part-1-glt1262857909]] explains why seven consulships would have seemed extraordinary in the omen story about Marius.
- Outsider constraint: [[701-roman-civil-war-rise-of-the-general-part-1-glt1262857909]] identifies Marius as a novus homo whose lack of high-office ancestry made the normal path harder.
- Command stakes: [[701-roman-civil-war-rise-of-the-general-part-1-glt1262857909]] says Marius's 107 BC consulship brought Metellus's African command to him.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The page is not a full constitutional guide to every Roman office. It records the concept as episode 701 uses it: a career ladder whose social filters and command consequences illuminate Marius's rise.
