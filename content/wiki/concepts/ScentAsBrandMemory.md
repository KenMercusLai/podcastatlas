# Scent As Brand Memory / 气味作为品牌记忆

Scent as brand memory / 气味作为品牌记忆 is the use of ambient smell as a repeatable recognition surface for a hotel, retail store, museum, cultural space, or other commercial environment. [[ep263-xianxiang-jueqi-faxian-zhongshi-xiangqi-de-meili-lq1lsqcprqyr7auo5gslwyymuiv]] discusses space-scent designers, hotel lobbies, museum exhibitions, and branded spaces to show that smell can bind memory to place much like a visual identity does.

The concept is different from selling a perfume bottle. The object is the environment: a lobby can feel calm, a cultural exhibition can feel materially richer, and a retail store can become easier to recognize if scent, visual design, service, and brand promise align.

## Key Claims
- Scent can become a brand cue when repeated across visits and tied to a place's function or mood.
- Spatial fragrance extends brand design beyond logo, lighting, music, and interior design.
- Museum and cultural-space scent can act as interpretation, not only decoration, when it helps visitors imagine materials, gardens, trade, or ritual.
- The mechanism depends on restraint: an intrusive or mismatched scent can harm the experience rather than deepen memory.
- Scent-memory design connects product fragrance, hospitality, and experiential retail.
