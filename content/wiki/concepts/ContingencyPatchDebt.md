# Contingency Patch Debt / 应急补丁债务

## Definition

Contingency patch debt is the downstream risk created when an emergency substitution restores immediate delivery without resolving the dependency, identity, relationship, or accountability problem that caused the disruption.

## Current Synthesis

In [[TaibaiJinxingYouDianFan|《太白金星有点烦》]], Sun Wukong leaves the pilgrimage to mourn the elder monkey who raised him. Li Changgeng protects the scheduled White Bone Ridge script by asking the Six-Eared Macaque to impersonate him. The replacement fills the operational gap, but success depends on concealed identity and creates a new conflict between the substitute, the absent participant, and the team's later account of events. The patch therefore moves risk forward rather than eliminating it.

## Key Claims

- An emergency workaround can achieve the immediate milestone while worsening the system's later state.
- Hidden substitution creates identity and trust risk beyond ordinary capacity replacement.
- Patch debt grows when the organization preserves the original script instead of revising assumptions after reality changes.
- Reporting can become part of the workaround when leaders use ambiguity to avoid deciding who was right, harmed, or responsible.
- A patch is not inherently a mistake; the debt lies in unresolved consequences that survive the immediate recovery.

## Evidence

### Immediate continuity through concealed substitution

- [[090-quhua-maboyong-taibai-jinxing-youdianfan-p3-changsheng-bulao-de-zhenxiang-lrfapwwjbvam-oix0lljif_3-a-x]] describes the Six-Eared Macaque replacing Sun Wukong so the planned killings and expulsion at White Bone Ridge can proceed despite the latter's absence.

### Downstream relationship and reporting risk

- [[090-quhua-maboyong-taibai-jinxing-youdianfan-p3-changsheng-bulao-de-zhenxiang-lrfapwwjbvam-oix0lljif_3-a-x]] treats the impersonation as a future conflict seed and the vague final summary as a way to preserve delivery without resolving incompatible accounts of Xuanzang and Sun Wukong.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The present evidence is a fictional project-management analogy. Temporary substitutions often prevent larger harm and need not create meaningful debt when authority, identity, handover, and follow-up are explicit. The concept should not imply that every contingency plan is deceptive or inferior to delay; it identifies the residual obligations that remain after the immediate recovery.

## What Changed

- Established concealed substitution and ambiguous reporting as two linked forms of downstream contingency debt.
