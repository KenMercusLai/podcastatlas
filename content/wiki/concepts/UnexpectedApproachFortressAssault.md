# Unexpected-Approach Fortress Assault / 意外进路式要塞突袭

## Definition
Unexpected-approach fortress assault / 意外进路式要塞突袭 uses selected troops, terrain knowledge, and a route defenders treat as unusable to bypass a fortress's strongest frontage and turn limited penetration into wider defensive shock.

## Current Synthesis
The concept comes from the source's comparison of [[GanNing|甘宁]] at [[YilingLateHan|夷陵]] and [[AlexanderTheGreat|亚历山大大帝]] at [[SogdianRock|粟特岩堡]]. In both retellings, the attacker avoids the prepared entrance. A small force is matched to terrain-specific movement, approaches quickly or under darkness, and appears where the defense assumes no enemy can appear.

The mechanism is not surprise alone. Local knowledge or specialized skill makes the route usable; selection keeps the force mobile; timing protects it; and visible penetration changes the defender's estimate of whether the whole position remains defensible.

## Key Claims
- Strong terrain can create a cognitive blind spot when defenders classify one approach as impossible.
- Small-force quality matters when matched to the terrain's movement problem.
- Speed, concealment, and route choice can substitute for mass at entry, but not necessarily for follow-on support.
- Local penetration pays off when it changes beliefs about encirclement or positional safety.
- Cross-period analogy isolates mechanism but does not establish institutional equivalence.

## Evidence
- Terrain knowledge and speed: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1108-ganning-qixi-yiling-zhanshu-jing-he-yalishanda-zhuangche-lia4azq39ja4pt6yfpjggjl-g3vc|Hanji 1108]] attributes Yiling to local knowledge, selected troops, speed, and surprise.
- Specialized movement: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1108-ganning-qixi-yiling-zhanshu-jing-he-yalishanda-zhuangche-lia4azq39ja4pt6yfpjggjl-g3vc|Hanji 1108]] describes experienced climbers making a night ascent at Sogdian Rock.
- Psychological conversion: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1108-ganning-qixi-yiling-zhanshu-jing-he-yalishanda-zhuangche-lia4azq39ja4pt6yfpjggjl-g3vc|Hanji 1108]] treats appearance from an impossible direction as surrender-inducing shock.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not establish identical routes, force structures, or surrender mechanisms. Its “special forces” vocabulary is modernizing, and the Sogdian Rock numbers are unverified here. Surprise entry can also leave a force isolated; Hanji 1109 suggests seizure and sustainable control are separate problems.

## What Changed
- Created the concept from the Yiling-Sogdian Rock comparison.
- Separated access, selection, surprise, and psychological effect into causal steps.
