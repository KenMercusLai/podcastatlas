# Chain Executive Import / 餐饮高管引入

## Definition
Chain executive import is the practice of a consumer chain hiring managers and marketers trained at large restaurant or retail groups to replace founder-driven operations with professional systems during a category reshuffle.

## Current Synthesis
The wiki records chain standardization and franchise expansion as operating models, but has less on the labor layer that installs those models. The current source adds it through [[Luxihe|泸溪河]]: the Chinese bakery chain hired Domino's China's former chief marketing officer as chief growth officer and a former [[McDonalds|McDonald's]] China executive as general manager after a March funding round, which industry media read as a shift from scale expansion toward fine-grained operations as supermarkets, fresh-snack retailers, and tea chains crowd the bakery category. Store count is above 650 with the earlier pace of more than 100 annual openings discontinued, and insiders say the new management is building a new store model.

## Key Claims
- Imported executives carry chain-scale playbooks: site models, marketing systems, and operating metrics.
- The hiring pattern tends to appear when a category moves from expansion to margin and efficiency competition.
- Executive import separates growth functions from operating functions, as the growth-officer and general-manager split shows.
- A slowed store-opening pace and a new store model are the operational counterpart of the management change.
- The approach can fail when a bakery, coffee, or specialty format has different freshness, spoilage, and assortment economics from quick-service restaurants.
- Channel mix matters: mall-heavy footprints may conflict with the breakfast and community occasions the source suggests.

## Evidence
- Hiring pattern - [[tulasi-pingguo-cheng-jinnian-aimeijiang-de-zuida-yingjia-xiecheng-erjidu-youying-zhuankui-1015756412]] reports the chief-growth-officer appointment from Domino's China and the general-manager appointment from McDonald's China after a March funding round.
- Category context - [[tulasi-pingguo-cheng-jinnian-aimeijiang-de-zuida-yingjia-xiecheng-erjidu-youying-zhuankui-1015756412]] says industry media attribute the hires to a deep bakery-chain reshuffle that turns competition from scale expansion into refined operations.
- Store-model change - [[tulasi-pingguo-cheng-jinnian-aimeijiang-de-zuida-yingjia-xiecheng-erjidu-youying-zhuankui-1015756412]] describes more than 650 stores, a break from prior annual openings above 100, and a new store model under the new management.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source reports appointments and industry interpretation rather than results, and gives no revenue, margin, turnover, or retention data. Executive import can also create friction with founder-led product culture, and the success of a new store model is explicitly left open in the episode.

- Created the concept to capture management professionalization as part of chain strategy.
