# Protector-Captor Ambiguity / 护驾与挟持模糊性

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-988-shoujin-lingru-ta-shi-hanmo-beican-de-huangdi-lsdkpakvk5u-omegcri-wstup-zl|Hanji 988]] adds the lodged-emperor version of the ambiguity before Hanji 989's route and supply cases. [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]]'s camp can claim proximity to the emperor while its Qiang-Hu soldiers harass the lodging, and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]]'s soldiers can block the carriage during departure. Protection, hosting, and obstruction sit in the same armed field, so imperial safety depends on [[JiaXu|贾诩]]'s bargain and [[LiuAiLateHan|刘艾]]'s public identification rather than on ordinary court control.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-989-ningwei-taipingquan-buzuo-luanshi-ren-lkoycygmjnltuvpq-6wjv-ls61s6|Hanji 989]] adds the route-and-supply version of the ambiguity. [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]] can describe route preference as protection while trying to keep [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] west, and [[DuanWeiLateHan|段煨]]'s real supplies at [[HuayinLateHan|华阴]] can still be framed by rivals as a capture attempt. The episode shows that escort, obstruction, and assistance are judged inside a rumor-heavy armed field, not by stated intention alone.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-990-lijue-guosi-shengsi-yuanjia-weihe-turan-lianshou-lmop5mcrsjuq-ixuv0sfho5mvc-f|Hanji 990]] backfills the pre-pursuit version of the pattern. [[YangDingLateHan|杨定]], [[DongChengLateHan|董承]], and [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]] speak through the language of protecting [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] and attacking [[DuanWeiLateHan|段煨]], while [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]] reunite under a rescue pretext; in each case, the protective vocabulary is inseparable from the desire to control the emperor's route and body.

Protector-captor ambiguity / 护驾与挟持模糊性 is the late-Han pattern made explicit by [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-991-zhengduo-xiandi-zhizhan-shui-neng-zuizhong-qusheng-lkbsl5uzqxouzm6mlewrnuns0x6p|Hanji 991]]. [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] is still valuable because his body and title can legitimate commands, appointments, and future strategic claims, but every armed group close enough to protect him is also close enough to control, redirect, threaten, or abandon him.

In Hanji 991, [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]], [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]], and [[ZhangJiLateHan|张济]] pursue the emperor as a prize, while [[DongChengLateHan|董承]], [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]], [[BaiboArmy|白波军]] leaders, and [[QubeiSouthernXiongnu|去卑]] try to keep him moving. The category problem is that "escort" and "capture" are not clean opposites. The emperor's survival requires soldiers, yet soldiers turn court ritual, road choice, ministerial safety, and river access into coercive facts.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-999-sanguo-luanshi-renxing-you-duome-cuiruo-li8tz30elca7bnipei6dfqdxmmeu|Hanji 999]] adds the downstream version. After the immediate Li Jue-Guo Si danger, the emperor falls among [[DongChengLateHan|董承]], [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]], [[HanXianLateHan|韩暹]], [[ZhangYangLateHan|张杨]], and other actors whose protection remains interest-divided. This keeps the court alive but also makes it vulnerable to the next stronger platform, eventually [[CaoCao|曹操]].

The concept differs from [[LegitimacyWindowSeizure|合法性窗口抢占]]. Legitimacy-window seizure names the strategic opportunity for an outside actor who can protect and operationalize the emperor. Protector-captor ambiguity names the prior danger: before the opportunity is captured, the emperor's recognized status attracts coercive protection from actors who cannot or will not restore ordinary sovereignty.

## Key Claims
- Hanji 988 adds that hosting the emperor can coexist with soldier harassment and route blockage before the more explicit flight crisis.
- Hanji 989 adds that food supply and camp access can become protector-captor evidence when rivals control the rumor channel.
- Hanji 990 adds that "protecting" the emperor can first appear as an argument over whether to attack a supposed threat, not only as direct pursuit.
- A recognized ruler can be politically powerful and physically helpless at the same time.
- Protection becomes ambiguous when the protector controls routes, food, armed access, and communication.
- The same imperial body can attract rescue, pursuit, and coercive hosting.
- Court symbols and offices keep value, but their practical use depends on the armed field around them.
- This ambiguity creates the precondition for later legitimacy-window seizure by a more capable actor.
