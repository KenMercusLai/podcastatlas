# Flying Geese Model

Flying geese model is [[OliverKim]]'s label in [[getting-entrepreneurial-in-korea-summer-school]] for the historical pattern where industries and expertise move from earlier industrializers to later ones. The episode describes textile capability moving from [[Japan]] to [[SouthKorea|South Korea]] and [[Taiwan]], then later from South Korea and Taiwan toward poorer countries such as [[Bangladesh]].

The model frames development as moving knowledge, not just money. The source also keeps the pattern limited: transfer can launch a garment sector, but the [[IndustrialLadder]] can stall when later upgrading into more complex goods does not happen.
