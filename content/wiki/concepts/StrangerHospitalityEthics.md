# Stranger Hospitality Ethics / 陌生人宾客伦理

## Definition
Stranger hospitality ethics is the minimum civil rule that a society or household reveals by how it treats people who arrive as guests, outsiders, refugees, beggars, children, or unknown others.

## Current Synthesis
The 展开讲讲 source makes hospitality a cross-work diagnostic. [[TheOdyssey|《奥德赛》]] supplies "宾客之道" and the repeated crossing of doors, caves, palaces, and homes; [[JourneyToTheWest|《西游记》]] supplies a Chinese comparison around knocking, lodging, alms, and stranger tests; 《欢迎来龙餐馆》 moves the issue into war-adjacent everyday ethics, asking whether cooking and ordinary labor can restore a child's social future before violence claims him. The shared question is whether people can withhold enemy judgment long enough for a humane relation to appear.

The 电影巨辩 episode strengthens the Odyssey branch by naming the rule as [[ZeusLawTrustEthic]]. Stranger hospitality becomes a trust infrastructure: if gifts, guests, hosts, and entrances can be weaponized without limit, social life loses the minimum confidence needed to meet unknown people without immediate enemy logic.

## Key Claims
- Door-crossing scenes make identity unstable: the same person can appear as guest, host, king, foreigner, refugee, worker, or threat.
- Hospitality ethics works as a low civilizational baseline before richer political agreement exists.
- Stranger treatment is not only kindness; it determines whether social order can survive fear and uncertainty.
- Food, lodging, labor, and ordinary care can become moral infrastructure in stories about war or collapse.
- Cross-cultural comparison is useful only with qualification: Greek xenia, Buddhist pilgrimage, and modern war storytelling have different sources.
- The Trojan Horse shows the negative case: weaponized trust can make future hospitality feel dangerous.

- Civil baseline: [[bayue-guangbo-dianshibao-zenme-suoyouren-de-qingtongshidai-dou-zai-bengkui-gkwrijioazzebf7pfatp6hhf]] says Nolan's film clarifies a minimum rule for facing strangers.
- Social-order survival: [[bayue-guangbo-dianshibao-zenme-suoyouren-de-qingtongshidai-dou-zai-bengkui-gkwrijioazzebf7pfatp6hhf]] contrasts hospitality logic with strict revenge and accounting in the Odyssey discussion.
- Food and labor care: [[bayue-guangbo-dianshibao-zenme-suoyouren-de-qingtongshidai-dou-zai-bengkui-gkwrijioazzebf7pfatp6hhf]] reads 《欢迎来龙餐馆》 through cooking, eating, restaurant labor, and pulling a child back from war.
- Qualified comparison: [[bayue-guangbo-dianshibao-zenme-suoyouren-de-qingtongshidai-dou-zai-bengkui-gkwrijioazzebf7pfatp6hhf]] warns that [[TheOdyssey|《奥德赛》]] and [[JourneyToTheWest|《西游记》]] have different religious and cultural sources.
- Weaponized gift and trust collapse: [[dang-xila-lishi-meiguo-zhengzhi-nuolan-meixue-zai-aodesai-li-da-qilai-gkwrijiov2xkbtdknqthj-ue]] reads Nolan's Odyssey through Zeus law and the Trojan Horse as a betrayal of gift, guest, host, and stranger meaning.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept is an interpretive bridge built from culture-review and film-analysis sources. It should not flatten Greek guest-host law, Chinese pilgrimage fiction, and modern war film into the same historical institution.

## What Changed
- Added Zeus-law trust as the Odyssey-specific version of the stranger-hospitality problem.
- Added the Trojan Horse as the negative case where gift and entry become weaponized.
