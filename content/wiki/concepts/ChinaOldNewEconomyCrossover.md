# China Old–New Economy Crossover / 中国新旧经济贡献交叉

## Definition

China's old-new economy crossover is the transition point at which growing contributions from AI, new manufacturing, and related industries exceed the declining contribution of real estate, without implying that property adjustment or household losses have ended.

## Current Synthesis

The episode locates this crossover in 2025 using the [[CF40ResearchInstitute|CF40 Research Institute]] report. Two movements produce it: new activity expands from a smaller base, while property continues to contract from a historically large one. The analytical value lies in separating a change in marginal macro drivers from a broad recovery in prices, wealth, jobs, or confidence.

The lower-cost environment created by property adjustment may help new firms and later market entrants through cheaper housing, rent, and financing. Distribution remains asymmetric, however: indebted owners, workers tied to the old economy, and families whose wealth is property-heavy bear legacy costs and may not become the next growth cycle's direct beneficiaries.

## Key Claims

- A shrinking sector can remain painful while becoming less important as the dominant marginal macro drag.
- A contribution crossover can result from both new-sector growth and old-sector contraction.
- Lower property-related costs can improve entry conditions for new firms and younger households.
- Legacy debt and asset losses prevent automatic transfer of gains from later entrants to earlier participants.
- A macro turning point does not establish a nationwide property bottom or rapid household recovery.

## Evidence

- **Reported crossover:** [[lpwbbhak2umzdd-u4t2wgstlmave-lpwbbhak2umzdd-u4t2wgstlmave]] and [[lvnr1afmbkgg3gjzumqricvnleu8-lvnr1afmbkgg3gjzumqricvnleu8]] attribute to the CF40 report an estimate that new-economy contribution exceeded real estate's in 2025.
- **Property remains unfinished:** [[lpwbbhak2umzdd-u4t2wgstlmave-lpwbbhak2umzdd-u4t2wgstlmave]] says major-city stabilization and lower-tier adjustment can remain uneven for years.
- **Household timing and distribution:** [[lvnr1afmbkgg3gjzumqricvnleu8-lvnr1afmbkgg3gjzumqricvnleu8]] emphasizes that production, productivity, and profits can improve before household wealth, jobs, income, or security, while [[lpwbbhak2umzdd-u4t2wgstlmave-lpwbbhak2umzdd-u4t2wgstlmave]] links lower housing, rent, and interest costs to new activity without erasing legacy burdens.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Neither supplied summary defines “new economy,” reproduces contribution calculations, or tests the estimate against alternative sector classifications; both discuss substantially the same report. A crossover may be statistically real while remaining too small, concentrated, or supply-heavy to improve household demand. Property timing varies by city tier, and “later phase” is not the same as completed clearing.
