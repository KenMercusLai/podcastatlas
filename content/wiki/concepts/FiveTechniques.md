# Five Techniques

The Five Techniques appear in [[699-the-troubles-return-of-the-ira-part-3-glt9519811642]] as the interrogation methods used by the army and [[RoyalUlsterConstabulary|RUC]] on some internees: wall-standing, hooding, white noise, minimal food and water, and sleep deprivation. The episode says these techniques had earlier appeared in British colonial conflicts and that senior ministers had approved the methods.

In the source, the Five Techniques turn detention from a security measure into a moral and constitutional scandal. Reports from [[LongKesh]], journalistic investigation, and Lord Gardiner's later condemnation helped critics frame British policy in [[NorthernIreland]] as secret, illegal, and alien to democratic traditions.

## Key Claims
- Interrogation methods can become strategic liabilities when they undermine the state's stated legal identity.
- Abuse scandals connect local security practice to wider imperial or colonial memories.
- Denial by security institutions can worsen legitimacy damage once documentation emerges.
