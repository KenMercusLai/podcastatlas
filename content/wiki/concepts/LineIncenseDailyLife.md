# Line Incense Daily Life / 线香日常化

Line incense daily life / 线香日常化 is [[ep263-xianxiang-jueqi-faxian-zhongshi-xiangqi-de-meili-lq1lsqcprqyr7auo5gslwyymuiv]]'s account of incense moving from high-threshold 香席, literati gatherings, and raw-material appreciation into ordinary home scenes. The episode says contemporary users often light incense while reading, meditating, sleeping, decompressing after work, writing, or creating a quieter room atmosphere.

The concept belongs partly to [[EmotionEconomy|情绪经济]], but it is not only mood consumption. Line incense also depends on [[ChineseFragrance|中式香气]] vocabulary, [[AgarwoodIndustrialization|agarwood supply]], [[FragranceProductStorytelling|brand storytelling]], and consumer willingness to treat smell as part of daily self-arrangement.

## Key Claims
- The new threshold is lower than formal 香道: users can begin with line incense, electric burners, cheaper planted material, powder, or 香篆 rather than rare raw agarwood.
- The demand side is inward-facing: home, reading, sleep, solitude, handwriting, and meditation are more central than outward social display.
- The source links the post-pandemic mood, economic uncertainty, and young people's anxiety to the search for small calming rituals.
- Daily use makes blended incense more commercially important because it can serve more scenes and taste preferences than single-material appreciation.
- The concept should not be overmedicalized; the source suggests calming or sleep-supporting effects cautiously and mostly through user and reporter experience.
