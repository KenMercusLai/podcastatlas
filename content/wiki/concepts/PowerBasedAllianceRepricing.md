# Power-Based Alliance Repricing / 实力重估式阵营转向

Power-based alliance repricing / 实力重估式阵营转向 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-152-xiangyu-3wanren-ruhe-wansheng-liubang-56wan-dajun-2-lq9ecpjb4r-ij0-e-iu-ezfavowj|Hanji 152 part 2]] as the political aftermath of the [[PengchengBattle|彭城之战]]. After [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s huge coalition is shattered, many lords who had recently followed him no longer treat him as the safer or more valuable camp.

The episode's host summarizes the logic socially: when a leader is strong, relationships look friendly; when strength disappears, "network" can become hollow. The concept records that as an alliance mechanism rather than a general moral slogan: alignment is repriced when protection, retaliation risk, reward access, and future victory probability visibly change.

The source gives several versions. [[SimaXin|司马欣]] and [[DongYiQin|董翳]] again submit to [[XiangYu|项羽]], [[WeiBao|魏豹]] withdraws to West Wei and turns back toward Chu, [[ChenYu|陈馀]] and [[ZhaoXie|赵歇]] shift away from Liu Bang, and [[TianHeng|田横]] reconciles with Xiang Yu after defeating [[TianJia|田假]]. At the same time, [[ZhangEr|张耳]] and [[HanWangXin|韩王信]] remain with Liu Bang, showing that repricing is strong but not mechanically universal.

This concept is related to [[WarringStatesAllianceSwitching|战国同盟转向]], but it is narrower. Alliance switching can come from marriage, hostages, land returns, or diplomacy; power-based repricing specifically names the rapid post-defeat recalculation that follows a visible change in military credibility.

## Key Claims
- Coalition loyalty becomes fragile when the coalition's protective value collapses in public.
- A battlefield result can alter diplomacy even before a formal settlement is renegotiated.
- Actors may switch because they seek protection from the stronger side, fear retaliation, or doubt the defeated camp's ability to pay future rewards.
- Repricing is not universal; residual loyalty, old ties, geography, or future expectations can keep some actors in the defeated camp.
- The concept translates the host's "实力社交" lesson into a source-scoped statecraft pattern.
