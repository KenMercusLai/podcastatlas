# Marriage Idealization and Mismatch / 婚姻理想化与错配

## Definition

Marriage idealization and mismatch is the source-scoped pattern in which partners treat one another as instruments of intellectual, spiritual, social, or professional aspiration before understanding their actual character, expectations, resources, and capacity for shared life.

## Current Synthesis

[[Middlemarch|《米德尔马契》]] supplies contrasting cases. Dorothea projects wisdom and shared vocation onto Casaubon, while he expects an admiring and subordinate assistant. Lydgate expects an elegant wife and supportive domestic base for medical reform; Rosamond expects status and material refinement. Neither marriage fails because one ambition is automatically illegitimate: idealized assumptions conceal incompatible projects until debt, work, status, and daily decisions expose them.

Mary and Fred provide the corrective. Affection does not suspend judgment; Mary refuses commitment until Fred can work, accept responsibility, and support a shared life. Compatibility therefore requires knowledge and demonstrated conduct, while historical limits on divorce and women's options determine how costly an initial error becomes.

## Key Claims

- Admiration for talent, status, beauty, or ideals does not establish relational compatibility.
- Projection turns a partner into a role inside one's own life project and prevents accurate mutual knowledge.
- Marriage joins affection to money, work, household expectations, status, and responsibility.
- Blame should remain distributed where both partners enter with untested or incompatible assumptions.
- Demonstrated capacity for labor and shared responsibility is stronger evidence than romantic or intellectual idealization.
- Exit rules and gendered constraints shape whether mismatch becomes a temporary error or lifelong tragedy.

## Evidence

- Scholarly idealization: [[38-mideermaqie-yinglun-xiangcun-aiqing-lixiang-pomie-ruhe-guo-zhenshi-shenghuo-lte-eogr0nzaqwkfgyfcduwc4gtt]] reads Dorothea and Casaubon as mutually instrumentalizing rather than genuinely knowing one another.
- Vocation and status mismatch: [[38-mideermaqie-yinglun-xiangcun-aiqing-lixiang-pomie-ruhe-guo-zhenshi-shenghuo-lte-eogr0nzaqwkfgyfcduwc4gtt]] attributes Lydgate and Rosamond's failure to incompatible expectations, debt, professional structure, consumption, and personal choice rather than unilateral fault.
- Responsibility test: [[38-mideermaqie-yinglun-xiangcun-aiqing-lixiang-pomie-ruhe-guo-zhenshi-shenghuo-lte-eogr0nzaqwkfgyfcduwc4gtt]] reads Mary's repeated refusal of Fred until he can support himself as a test of practical partnership rather than a story of a woman simply reforming a man.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept is derived from a podcast interpretation of fictional marriages, not a population-level theory or clinical model. The source also treats Dorothea and Will as a more compatible union while preserving doubt about compensatory guilt and sacrificial devotion. Historical marriage law, gender, property, and limited exit mean that individual discernment cannot explain outcomes by itself.

## What Changed

- Established the concept by comparing idealization, incompatible marriage expectations, and demonstrated readiness across three *Middlemarch* relationships.
