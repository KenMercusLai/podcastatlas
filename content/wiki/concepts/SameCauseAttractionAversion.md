# 喜恶同因 / Same-Cause Attraction and Aversion

## Definition
喜恶同因 / Same-cause attraction and aversion is the recognition that a person's, institution's, or choice's valued benefit and disliked cost may arise from the same underlying trait, incentive, boundary, or causal chain.

## Current Synthesis
The concept is stronger than the generic claim that everything has two sides. It asks whether the reassuring and disturbing outcomes are coupled: a cockpit barrier protects because it restricts entry, and that same restriction can obstruct intervention; a job makes leave feel safe because it supplies a known return, while the return also ends the freedom; a gap removes external control while also removing predictable income and structure.

In relationships and life choices, this frame resists selective fantasy. Responsibility may shade into forcefulness, gentleness into hesitation, romance into greater emotional demand, and a low-interruption job into lower pay or slower advancement. The conclusion is not that every cost must be accepted or that change is impossible, but that informed choice requires identifying which disliked consequences are separable defects and which are produced by the feature one values.

## Key Claims
- Attraction and aversion can be different evaluations of one causal feature rather than independent reactions to separate features.
- A valued arrangement cannot always retain its benefit after the mechanism producing its cost is removed.
- Context, timing, intensity, and perspective can turn the same trait from useful to burdensome.
- Recognizing causal coupling supports more realistic choice without requiring emotional indifference or passive acceptance.
- The practical test is whether the cost is intrinsic to the valued mechanism, merely correlated with it, or reducible through boundaries and redesign.

### Rest and employment
- [[zong-di-073-qi-zheng-shi-na-ling-wo-anxin-de-rang-wo-gandao-buan-cmv2j36hg07bm01xg92961dsw]] argues that a holiday's endpoint both limits freedom and makes rest feel secure, while an open-ended work gap expands freedom but increases uncertainty.

### Traits and life choices
- [[zong-di-073-qi-zheng-shi-na-ling-wo-anxin-de-rang-wo-gandao-buan-cmv2j36hg07bm01xg92961dsw]] pairs responsibility with forcefulness, gentleness with hesitation, romance with emotional demand, and protected personal time with possible pay or advancement costs.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Not every benefit and cost share a cause. The frame can become an excuse for abuse, unsafe design, unfair work, or avoidable dysfunction if causal coupling is asserted without evidence. Traits and institutions can often be moderated, complemented, or redesigned, and a person may reasonably reject the whole package when its costs are too high. The episode offers interpretive examples rather than a validated psychological law.

## What Changed
- Created a causal account of ambivalence that distinguishes coupled trade-offs from generic pros-and-cons lists.
- Added a separability test to prevent the concept from becoming automatic resignation.
- Connected institutional design, employment security, rest, personality, and life choice within one qualified frame.
