# Science Value Transmission / 科学价值观传入

[[ep262-gaokao-guohou-zhishi-guiling-women-xuyao-zenyang-de-kexue-jiaoyu-ls8ecqcdpe9d5ioytyca3jqdf-3r]] adds the school-education version. In this source, scientific values are taught through [[ScienceEducationAsCivicLiteracy|public literacy]], [[ProjectBasedScienceLearning|project inquiry]], [[BigConceptScienceTeaching|big concepts]], and [[HPSScienceEducation|history/philosophy/society]] rather than only through late-Ming intellectual exchange.

Science value transmission is the late-stage argument in [[ep259-yu-miao-shifu-mantan-xu-guangqi-sibainian-qian-ruhe-rangren-biancongming-lkirczxnx8skbbfpmz5-m-mivs4e]] that knowledge is never simply bare technique. [[MiaoWeiSanlian|苗伟]] cites a book rendered in the source as 《自由的科学》 to argue that science tends to carry values such as anti-authoritarianism, communication freedom, association freedom, experiment, dignity, and self-respect.

The concept grows from [[LateMingWesternLearning|晚明西学东渐]] but extends beyond it. [[XuGuangqi|徐光启]] learns geometry, agriculture, and astronomy as concrete tools, yet the episode argues that fully absorbing science would also require absorbing norms around reasoning, evidence, openness, and institutional exchange.

The concept is not naive universalism. The episode repeatedly shows that science travels through missionaries, calendars, imperial offices, cultural anxieties, and modern state competition. That means values move with knowledge unevenly: they can be blocked by [[YangGuangxian|杨光先]]-style purity politics, distorted by power, or restricted by today's chip and AI rivalry.

## Key Claims
- Scientific knowledge carries social practices and values, not only formulas.
- Technical adoption without communicative and anti-authoritarian norms may remain shallow.
- Cultural self-confidence can make knowledge exchange easier; cultural insecurity can turn it into betrayal discourse.
- The episode connects early modern Western learning to present-day technology competition to show that knowledge restriction remains current.
- Science value transmission must be distinguished from missionary or geopolitical domination, even when historically entangled with both.
