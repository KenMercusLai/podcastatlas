# ERP Trust Moat

ERP trust moat is the defensibility of enterprise resource planning systems that comes from governed processes, trusted data, compliance, localization, auditability, and industry know-how rather than code alone. [[ai-chongji-qiye-ruanjian-jutou-yu-sap-yuanxin-liao-damoxing-to-b-de-dianfu-yu-bianjie-1-174-1]] adds the concept through [[SAP]] and [[EnterpriseResourcePlanning|ERP]].

The source does not deny that AI coding and agents threaten older software economics. Its distinction is that an AI-generated tool or copied codebase does not automatically reproduce the business rules, standard workflows, external trust, and global localization needed by a large company whose records must be accepted by auditors, regulators, banks, suppliers, tax systems, and managers.

## Key Claims
- Code is only one layer of enterprise software value; processes, data models, audit trails, and local compliance are harder to copy.
- [[VibeCoding]] and disposable software can help small teams with low-risk, short-lived internal needs, but large enterprises need shared platforms that make data trusted by outsiders.
- AI can strengthen the moat if it lowers customization cost and hides complexity through [[SAPJoule]] without breaking governance.
- The moat weakens when incumbents leave interaction, implementation speed, and pricing untouched while AI-native competitors offer clearer outcomes.
- The concept extends [[SaaSTrustMoat]] into the failure-intolerant ERP layer.
