# Classical Prose Reading / 古文阅读

Classical prose reading / 古文阅读 is [[164-guwen-de-liliang-qing-shou-xia-zhe-fen-qiannian-wei-bian-de-shenqing-dongjian-he-yongqi-940236576]]'s frame for reopening familiar Chinese prose texts after school memorization has made them feel inert. The source argues that prose differs from short lyric poetry because it can carry longer scenes, reasoning, political relations, and life situations in a looser form.

The episode's method is to treat old prose as concrete experience before it becomes quotation or test material. [[XiangJiXuanZhi|《项脊轩志》]] preserves a room and a family; [[YouBaochanShanJi|《游褒禅山记》]] turns a travel record into ideal and failure; [[ShangShumiHanTaiweiShu|《上枢密韩太尉书》]] links writing to "养气"; [[HongmenYan|《鸿门宴》]] makes ritual detail political evidence.

## Key Claims
- Grammar explanation can help, but it cannot replace asking what situation the prose is trying to preserve.
- Classical prose can train attention to domestic objects, public ambition, youth, patronage, ritual, and power.
- A text remembered from school may need adult life experience before its stakes become visible.
