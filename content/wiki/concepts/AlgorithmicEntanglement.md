# Algorithmic Entanglement / 算法与实践纠缠

Algorithmic entanglement is the reciprocal shaping between users, creators, platform interfaces, and algorithmic systems. [[159-suanfa-de-liufu-miankong-ta-shi-ruhe-cong-chuli-shuju-biancheng-dingyi-women-shi-shui-de-lh5ng5mbvkworfxlq9l-coscepc7]] presents this as the fifth face of algorithms: people do not only receive algorithmic outputs; they adapt their speech, timing, tags, titles, images, and self-presentation so the system can recognize and distribute them.

The source names "algorithmic recognizability" as a practical condition of platform life. Hashtags, keywords, click-oriented titles, cover images, publishing time, pinyin abbreviations, and even deliberate misspellings become ways to be seen, avoid demotion, or route around moderation. Creators with MCNs, SEO experts, API access, ad budgets, or insider knowledge gain a visibility advantage over ordinary users guessing at the rules.

## Key Claims
- Algorithmic distribution changes expression before a post is published.
- User feedback and creator optimization are not outside the algorithm; they become part of the system's operating environment.
- Creator anxiety grows when self-expression and traffic optimization cannot be cleanly separated.
- Platform expertise becomes a new inequality: advertisers, MCNs, SEO specialists, developers, and large creators can buy or infer more visibility than ordinary users.
- AI slop, marketing content, and platform ads can make a feed feel less human even if engagement metrics keep moving.
- Cultural production can adapt to algorithmic incentives, as with shorter music hooks, faster payoffs, and creator-facing short-video formats.
