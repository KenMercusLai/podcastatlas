# Revenge as Self-Defeating Inheritance / 复仇的自毁性继承

## Definition

Revenge as self-defeating inheritance is a tragic narrative pattern in which one person's answer to injury becomes the next person's grievance, moving retaliation across generations and relationships until it destroys the restoration each participant sought.

## Current Synthesis

[[YanYuMengMeng|《烟雨濛濛》]] supplies the bounded case. Lu Zhenhua's early loss becomes conquest and repetition; Xueyi answers the household order through betrayal and flight; Yiping turns exclusion and humiliation into a plan against the Lu family; Shuhuan answers her concealed motive by using Rǔping; and Mengping's rebellion is also read as resistance to paternal control. These actions differ morally and causally, but together they show retaliation migrating rather than concluding.

The pattern matters because revenge does not remain between an original offender and victim. It recruits third parties, converts love into an instrument, and gives later actors injuries they can treat as fresh permission to strike back. Tragedy arises not from fate alone but from repeated choices made inside unequal power and accumulated hurt.

## Key Claims

- Retaliation can be inherited through relationships even when later actors did not cause the original injury.
- Using a third party to punish someone else expands rather than settles the grievance.
- Love and care become unstable when they are also instruments of revenge.
- Explanatory trauma does not make each retaliatory act morally equivalent or inevitable.
- The cycle defeats itself because no participant receives durable repair, recognition, or restored intimacy.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept is an interpretation of one novel supplied through a podcast summary, not a claim that all revenge narratives or all responses to injustice are equivalent. The actors operate with unequal power, knowledge, and prior injury, so identifying a cycle must not flatten coercive domination into the same moral category as reactive anger. The episode preserves disagreement over responsibility and whether Lu Zhenhua's backstory explains or excuses him.

## What Changed

- Established the concept from 《烟雨濛濛》's multi-person chain of injury and retaliation.
- Distinguished migrated third-party harm from a closed exchange between offender and victim.
- Kept causal explanation separate from moral equivalence or acquittal.
