# Anxiety Decomposition / 焦虑拆解

## Definition
Anxiety decomposition is the practice of turning diffuse anxiety into smaller, named, and workable pieces across body, situation, expectation, history, and support resources.

## Current Synthesis
Episode 472 treats anxiety as a demand-resource mismatch: the person experiences the current pressure, expectation, or uncertainty as larger than available capacity and support. The first intervention is therefore not abstract reasoning, but lowering stimulus intensity and making the feared thing concrete enough to inspect.

The source distinguishes horizontal and vertical decomposition. Horizontally, the person separates work, relationship, family, body, cognition, and immediate events. Vertically, the person asks how temperament, growth history, parental anxiety, trauma, and older shame states may be enlarging the current problem.

## Key Claims
- Anxiety becomes workable when vague pressure is translated into specific feared objects, contexts, and needs.
- Demand-resource mismatch explains why an externally "small" task can feel unmanageable when extra expectations or identity stakes are attached.
- Horizontal decomposition separates current domains such as work, family, body, relationship, and thought loops.
- Vertical decomposition traces how old experiences, shame, family atmosphere, or trauma may intensify the present scene.
- Decomposition should usually follow stabilization because fight, flight, freeze, and tunnel attention make analysis less reliable.
- Support, ability, confidence, and self-affirmation are part of anxiety reduction because they change the perceived resource side of the equation.

## Evidence
- Definition and mismatch - [[shi-di-fu-shuo-472-zhao-jinzhao-huajie-jiaolv-zuijia-fangfa-chaijie-wenti-wenrou-xiangdai-lqw9h-jqfzrsrveagfslnakhcgjh]] has [[ZhaoJinzhao|Claire]] define anxiety as anticipatory fear of the unknown and describe it as present pressure exceeding perceived ability and resources.
- Horizontal and vertical method - [[shi-di-fu-shuo-472-zhao-jinzhao-huajie-jiaolv-zuijia-fangfa-chaijie-wenti-wenrou-xiangdai-lqw9h-jqfzrsrveagfslnakhcgjh]] explicitly separates horizontal life-domain decomposition from vertical developmental and trauma-history decomposition.
- Expectation inflation - [[shi-di-fu-shuo-472-zhao-jinzhao-huajie-jiaolv-zuijia-fangfa-chaijie-wenti-wenrou-xiangdai-lqw9h-jqfzrsrveagfslnakhcgjh]] says a task can become much harder when it is used to prove worth to parents or avoid error.
- Stabilization before analysis - [[shi-di-fu-shuo-472-zhao-jinzhao-huajie-jiaolv-zuijia-fangfa-chaijie-wenti-wenrou-xiangdai-lqw9h-jqfzrsrveagfslnakhcgjh]] links high anxiety to sympathetic activation, fight/flight/freeze, tunnel thinking, and catastrophizing.
- Resource building - [[shi-di-fu-shuo-472-zhao-jinzhao-huajie-jiaolv-zuijia-fangfa-chaijie-wenti-wenrou-xiangdai-lqw9h-jqfzrsrveagfslnakhcgjh]] recommends increasing ability, confidence, social support, and daily self-recognition.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept is psychoeducational and source-scoped. It does not replace diagnosis or treatment for panic disorder, obsessive-compulsive disorder, eating disorders, health anxiety, trauma, suicidality, or medical conditions. The episode also warns against reducing broad social anxiety to only individual responsibility; the practical move is to locate concrete handles without denying structural pressures.
