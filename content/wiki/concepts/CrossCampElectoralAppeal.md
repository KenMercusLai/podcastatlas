# 跨阵营选举号召力 / Cross-Camp Electoral Appeal

## Definition
Cross-camp electoral appeal is a candidate's demonstrated capacity to attract voters beyond a party's normal base, distinguished from winning because opponents split votes, turnout differs, or the party baseline is already favorable.

## Current Synthesis
The concept turns victory from an endpoint into an attribution problem. [[JiangWanan|蒋万安]] won a legislative race with non-[[Kuomintang|国民党]] votes divided and won the 2022 Taipei mayoralty in a three-way contest. [[b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26-b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26mp3aid6af3219e1f5126ac85b049f0chid66b07190af99592]] therefore treats those outcomes as evidence that he can win under those structures, not as clean evidence that he personally expands the party coalition.

## Key Claims
- A win proves that a candidate prevailed under one electoral configuration, not why.
- Vote splitting can lower the share needed to win without increasing cross-camp support.
- A strong party geography can conceal a limited personal-vote premium.
- Close reelection polling can update earlier assumptions about candidate strength even without erasing prior victories.
- Independent-voter behavior and turnout provide a stronger test of coalition expansion than core-party consolidation alone.

## Evidence
- Divided legislative field: [[b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26-b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26mp3aid6af3219e1f5126ac85b049f0chid66b07190af99592]] reports Chiang winning with 46.7% while two non-KMT candidates divided votes.
- Three-way mayoral field: [[b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26-b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26mp3aid6af3219e1f5126ac85b049f0chid66b07190af99592]] reports a 42.3% win in 2022.
- Current reassessment: [[b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26-b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26mp3aid6af3219e1f5126ac85b049f0chid66b07190af99592]] says a close contest against [[ShenBoyang|沈伯洋]] challenges expectations derived from incumbency, party advantage, and earlier wins.
- Independent-voter test: [[b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26-b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26mp3aid6af3219e1f5126ac85b049f0chid66b07190af99592]] makes former [[HuangShanshan|黄珊珊]] voters pivotal to the current outcome.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode does not supply precinct transfer estimates, voter files, comparable two-candidate races, or a model of the party baseline. A candidate may have cross-camp appeal even in a divided field, and close polls can reflect challenger strength, temporary issues, sampling error, or turnout assumptions rather than an incumbent's fixed ceiling.

## What Changed
- Established a framework separating observed victory from the personal coalition expansion inferred from it.
- Added vote division and soft independent turnout as key identification limits.
