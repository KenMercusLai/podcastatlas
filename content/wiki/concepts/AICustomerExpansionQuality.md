# AI Customer Expansion Quality

## Definition

AI customer expansion quality is the degree to which reported customer growth and retention represent diversified, repeatable, ROI-backed production use rather than a small starting base, temporary experiments, subsidized demand, or concentration in a few buyers.

## Current Synthesis

Customer count, concentration, spending thresholds, and net revenue retention answer different questions. Low concentration reduces single-account exposure, while rapid expansion suggests customers are buying more; neither establishes why they buy, whether the spending produces accepted value, or whether the rate persists after the cohort matures.

The source therefore treats customer quality as a cohort problem. Investors need starting spend, renewal behavior, workload mix, gross versus net revenue treatment, customer overlap across spending bands, and measurable ROI before turning a very high retention percentage into a mature-demand conclusion.

## Key Claims

- Customer diversification lowers single-buyer risk but does not prove broad economic value.
- Net revenue retention must be interpreted against cohort age and starting spend.
- Spending thresholds do not reveal whether a customer is experimenting or running core production workloads.
- Repeated purchases are stronger demand evidence when tied to accepted work and measurable return.
- Provider share and aggregate token demand can move in different directions.

## Evidence

- **Concentration:** [[16de99b4-9940-4d7e-bd08-9ff0d4423df0-16de99b4-9940-4d7e-bd08-9ff0d4423df0]] reports roughly one-fifth of revenue from the top five customers and no more than roughly 10% from the largest.
- **Expansion rate:** [[16de99b4-9940-4d7e-bd08-9ff0d4423df0-16de99b4-9940-4d7e-bd08-9ff0d4423df0]] reports net revenue retention near 900% while warning that a low base can inflate the apparent maturity signal.
- **Cohort ambiguity:** [[16de99b4-9940-4d7e-bd08-9ff0d4423df0-16de99b4-9940-4d7e-bd08-9ff0d4423df0]] says Fortune 500 penetration and counts above spending thresholds lack enough overlap and workload detail to show deployment depth.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Very high expansion can be genuine evidence of product value, especially in a young market where small pilots become production systems. Conversely, lower retention can reflect price declines, model routing, or efficiency improvements rather than falling utility. The episode provides no audited cohort table, churn, contract duration, customer identity, discounts, workload mix, or ROI study.

## What Changed

- Established a cohort-based framework separating concentration, penetration, spending bands, and retention.
- Added starting spend and deployment depth as necessary qualifications for extreme retention figures.
