# Factory Discipline And Sensory Numbing / 工厂规训与感知麻木

Factory discipline and sensory numbing names the condition [[ZhanTeacher|占老师]] calls "无所适从" in [[ruci-dagong-30-nian-liushuixian-shang-de-miankong-yu-shijian-lodsuktwtcfxnhmzwum5iesbzbme]]. The source says his photo series of wearing cleanroom clothing in natural landscapes is not a simple expression of freedom; it shows a person trained by segmented factory procedures becoming estranged from society, nature, and bodily perception.

The concept connects [[TayloristFactoryTime|factory time]] to [[WorkSelfWorthBoundary|work self-worth boundary]]. The problem is not only fatigue from a hard job; it is the risk that the worker's perceptual and evaluative life becomes organized by replaceable tasks, discipline, and numb endurance.

## Key Claims
- Factory discipline can outlast the shift because the body continues carrying production habits into non-work settings.
- Sensory numbing is different from laziness or lack of ambition; it can be an adaptation to repetitive, tightly controlled work.
- Rebuilding perception requires practices that restore attention, judgment, and self-construction, such as [[WorkerPhotographicArchive|archival photography]] and learning.
- The concept gives the wiki a factory-side analogue to AI-era [[WorkplaceAIReadinessGap|workplace anxiety]] and [[AIJobSecurityAnxiety]]: both involve losing confidence in one's future role.
