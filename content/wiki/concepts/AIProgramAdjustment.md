# AI-Assisted Program Adjustment / AI辅助专业调整

AI-assisted program adjustment / AI辅助专业调整 is the vocational-school use of AI, industry data, job postings, policy signals, and supply-chain information to decide which majors, micro-specializations, or courses to keep, change, or avoid. [[ep241-xiaoqi-hezuo-shi-xinyidai-de-tiefanwan-ma-lnmpsbvoqbexeardxyx88hywufig]] describes a school working with a Beijing company to track industry chains, enterprise demand, and policy information for program planning.

The source treats AI as a planning aid, not a substitute for judgment. Schools still need to decide whether a hot direction actually fits vocational training, whether the likely jobs require graduate-level talent, whether safety problems such as battery repair are manageable, and whether a real enterprise can support practice.

## Key Claims
- AI can help schools see labor-demand signals faster than traditional curriculum cycles.
- Micro-specializations can respond to fast-changing industries, but they risk becoming shallow if not tied to real equipment and employer need.
- Program adjustment needs [[HumanJudgmentUnderAI|human judgment]] because job titles, degree requirements, and safety constraints can make a seemingly hot field unsuitable.
- AI also changes student work directly as learners use tools for homework, summaries, programming, wiring, and project debugging.
