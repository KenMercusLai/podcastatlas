# Domestic Labor Double Burden

Domestic labor double burden is the relationship strain in [[taking-the-shine-off-albedo-and-global-temperatures-6a730fdaca067b295bab9760]] where wives who earn more money can still remain responsible for more housework and childcare. [[DougDawson]] says dissatisfaction is understandable when a person earns most of the income and also does most household work.

The concept extends [[FamilyLaborBoundaries]] and [[GenderPowerAndUncertaintyCost]]. The problem is not only whether a woman can earn more; it is whether income power changes the distribution of care, time, status, and emotional cost inside the household.

## Key Claims
- Higher income does not automatically redistribute unpaid care work.
- Female-breadwinner relationships can face strain when provider and domestic expectations both fall on the wife.
- Relationship satisfaction depends on labor allocation as well as earnings comparison.
