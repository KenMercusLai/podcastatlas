# Economy-Class Syndrome Travel Thrombosis / 经济舱综合征旅行血栓

## Definition
Economy-class syndrome travel thrombosis / 经济舱综合征旅行血栓 is the travel-health frame for lower-limb venous thrombosis risk caused by prolonged immobility, cramped posture, reduced calf-muscle pumping, venous stasis, dehydration, and high-coagulation contexts during flights, trains, car rides, or other long sitting.

## Current Synthesis
[[vol-204-jingjicang-zonghezheng-duanwu-jiaqi-chuxing-chengche-chengji-zuojiu-le-tuizhang-xie-fajin-xueguan-waike-yisheng-de-yinghe-pailei-zhinan]] reframes "economy-class syndrome" away from cabin class and toward bodily conditions: limited movement slows venous return, raises lower-limb venous pressure, and gives blood more opportunity to stagnate and coagulate. The ordinary experience may be leg swelling or tight shoes, but the severe risk is thrombosis that can extend or embolize to the lungs.

The concept is a boundary between mild travel discomfort and possible [[VenousThromboembolismTriage|venous thromboembolism triage]]. The episode asks listeners to notice pattern, laterality, pain, calf hardness, risk factors, and timing rather than relying on the name of the travel setting.

## Key Claims
- The syndrome is not limited to airplane economy cabins; high-speed rail, buses, cars, and any prolonged constrained sitting can create similar risk conditions.
- Lower-limb venous return depends partly on calf-muscle contraction, so long immobility weakens the pump that helps blood return to the heart.
- Venous stasis, dehydration, blood concentration, endothelial injury, and high-coagulation states interact rather than acting as isolated causes.
- Most post-travel swelling is not thrombosis, but the consequence of missed thrombosis can be severe enough to justify red-flag awareness.
- Pulmonary embolism is the high-consequence endpoint that makes travel thrombosis a medical-risk-management topic.

## Evidence
- Mechanism: [[vol-204-jingjicang-zonghezheng-duanwu-jiaqi-chuxing-chengche-chengji-zuojiu-le-tuizhang-xie-fajin-xueguan-waike-yisheng-de-yinghe-pailei-zhinan]] explains calf-muscle contraction as a venous-return aid and long sitting as a stasis-promoting condition.
- Travel scope: [[vol-204-jingjicang-zonghezheng-duanwu-jiaqi-chuxing-chengche-chengji-zuojiu-le-tuizhang-xie-fajin-xueguan-waike-yisheng-de-yinghe-pailei-zhinan]] applies the risk beyond airplanes to high-speed rail, car travel, and prolonged sedentary states.
- Severe endpoint: [[vol-204-jingjicang-zonghezheng-duanwu-jiaqi-chuxing-chengche-chengji-zuojiu-le-tuizhang-xie-fajin-xueguan-waike-yisheng-de-yinghe-pailei-zhinan]] names pulmonary embolism as the feared consequence when thrombus dislodges.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source emphasizes that incidence is not high and that ordinary travel swelling is often venous pressure and stasis rather than confirmed thrombosis. The concept should not be used to self-diagnose clots or to infer that every swollen leg after travel is dangerous.

## What Changed
- VOL.204 creates the travel-thrombosis concept and ties it to immobility, venous stasis, high-risk contexts, prevention, and emergency routing.
