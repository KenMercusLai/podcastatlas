# Freight Identity Takeover

## Definition
Freight identity takeover is the compromise or fraudulent replication of a carrier's or dispatcher's credentials, communications, reputation, or registration so criminals can direct legitimate logistics activity and collect cargo.

## Current Synthesis
The bourbon case in [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356]] shows that control of phone, email, website, and dispatch communication can be more operationally valuable than control of the truck itself. Routine contract links, speed-sensitive load boards, lightly policed carrier-number rules, and fragmented verification let attackers borrow legitimate infrastructure while obscuring who knows the load is stolen.

## Key Claims
- A routine-looking transaction can be the entry point for credential compromise.
- Multi-channel impersonation is stronger than a single forged document because it survives cross-checks.
- Legitimate drivers and equipment can execute a theft without knowingly joining it.
- Carrier history can be stolen, fabricated, or deliberately cultivated before abuse.
- Verification delay has commercial costs, so prevention competes with booking speed.

## Evidence
- Dispatcher compromise - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356]] reports control of a real dispatcher's phone, website, email, and communications after a likely malicious-link click.
- Legitimate execution - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356]] says the impostors dispatched a real truck to collect the bourbon.
- Registration and reputation - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356]] describes low-cost carrier registration, weak one-number enforcement, and a company completing real deliveries before theft.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The precise initial compromise in the bourbon case is described as likely rather than forensically established in the supplied note. The source does not quantify how often attackers use account takeover, fabricated companies, insiders, or coercion relative to other methods.

## What Changed
- Initial synthesis frames freight fraud as control over trust channels rather than only forged paperwork.
