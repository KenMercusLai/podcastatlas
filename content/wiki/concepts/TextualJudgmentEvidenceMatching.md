# Textual Judgment Evidence Matching / 文本判断的证据匹配

## Definition

Textual judgment evidence matching is the discipline of scaling evidence to the kind of conclusion being made: a reading response needs honest description, a textual claim needs passages, a motive inference needs observable cues and alternatives, and a personality judgment needs evidence beyond an isolated work or disliked style.

## Current Synthesis

[[episode-229-diao-yi-tiao-da-yu-8209942018-431605]] separates four levels that online criticism often compresses. “This irritated me” reports an experience; “the work overuses citation” describes a feature; “the citations are meant to display learning” infers intent; and “the author is narcissistic” diagnoses a person. Movement up that ladder is possible, but each step increases the evidential burden.

The framework protects sharp criticism rather than requiring politeness. A critic can identify unsupported factual claims, decorative references, flattened natural subjects, sales tactics, or fear appeals when those patterns are visible in the text. The failure occurs when a portable character label replaces the missing account of what the work actually does.

## Key Claims

- Reading response, textual description, motive inference, and character judgment are distinct claim types.
- Factual allegations require examples that can be checked, not only confidence or repetition.
- Text can support bounded motive inference when strategies such as selling, intimidation, or incitement leave identifiable traces.
- Personality judgments require more evidence than a disliked passage, style, or citation density.
- Critics should translate broad labels into textual mechanisms whenever possible.
- Authors are subject to the same rule when they speculate about reviewers' jealousy, anxiety, or identity.

## Evidence

### Four levels of judgment
- [[episode-229-diao-yi-tiao-da-yu-8209942018-431605]] distinguishes felt annoyance, excessive citation, display motive, and narcissistic personality as progressively stronger conclusions.

### Checkable factual criticism
- [[episode-229-diao-yi-tiao-da-yu-8209942018-431605]] accepts that the book may contain errors while arguing that “many errors” becomes responsible criticism only when passages and corrections are supplied.

### Symmetrical restraint
- [[episode-229-diao-yi-tiao-da-yu-8209942018-431605]] records Ren Ning applying the same standard to his own unsupported thoughts that reviewers might be jealous, anxious about knowledge, or hostile to male authors.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

- Interpretation rarely reaches mathematical proof; converging patterns can justify qualified motive claims without direct confession.
- Review length limits how much evidence can be shown, but brevity does not convert a personality label into a demonstrated conclusion.
- The source is the criticized author's response, so the framework is more durable than its application to any particular reviewer.
- A pattern across many works and public actions may support stronger character conclusions than one passage can.
