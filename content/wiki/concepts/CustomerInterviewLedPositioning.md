## Definition
Customer-interview-led positioning is the practice of deriving a brand's clearest value proposition from recurring customer language about attraction, purchase, repeat use, and emotional meaning rather than from the founder's full origin story alone.

## Current Synthesis
Vivi's Vacation shows why authentic brand material is not yet positioning. Hawaii, Brazil, sustainability, fabric, construction, design credentials, and surfing are all plausible assets, but direct conversations with buyers must reveal which combination creates value. The output should be a fast, consistent proposition backed by proof across the site, product, packaging, imagery, email, and social presence.

## Key Claims
- Founder authenticity supplies credible material but does not determine which message customers will understand or repeat.
- Existing and repeat customers can reveal emotional purchase motives that analytics or feature lists miss.
- Recurring language matters more than an isolated compliment because positioning must work beyond one buyer.
- A strong first message should be understood within seconds, with deeper provenance and product detail available as supporting proof.
- Narrow audience focus can improve learning and cultural visibility before broader expansion.

## Evidence
### Discovering the reason to believe
- [[advice-line-with-tara-bosch-of-smartsweets-8aab80a1-b0c5-4103-b875-f5d4823d07ce]] advises Vivi's Vacation to call current and repeat customers, identify recurring emotional themes, and connect them to fabric, construction, Hawaii, and Brazil.

### Compressing and expressing the position
- [[advice-line-with-tara-bosch-of-smartsweets-8aab80a1-b0c5-4103-b875-f5d4823d07ce]] proposes a three-second comprehension standard and consistent founder-product storytelling across customer-facing surfaces.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- Interviews capture stated interpretation and can be distorted by politeness, memory, or a tiny customer sample; purchase, repeat purchase, referrals, and willingness to pay remain necessary behavioral checks.
- A narrow initial niche improves message precision only if it contains enough reachable customers and does not erase the product's strongest wider use cases.
- The source does not establish which specific Vivi's Vacation message will win.

## What Changed
- Established the concept from an early-stage apparel case where many authentic attributes still require customer-led prioritization.
