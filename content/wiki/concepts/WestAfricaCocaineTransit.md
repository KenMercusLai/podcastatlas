# West Africa Cocaine Transit

West Africa cocaine transit is the route pattern described by [[OriOgunbiyi]] in [[taking-the-shine-off-albedo-and-global-temperatures-6a730fdaca067b295bab9760]]. The episode says [[WestAfrica]] has become one of the biggest hubs for cocaine flows to Europe because it sits between South American production and European demand.

The source describes a logistics chain: bulk shipments arrive in West Africa, cocaine is repackaged, loaded onto mother vessels, and transferred at sea to fast boats headed toward Europe. That makes the problem geographic, institutional, and maritime at once.

## Key Claims
- Geography makes West Africa attractive to traffickers after direct South America-Europe routes face more enforcement.
- The route depends on maritime transfers, repackaging, port access, and local corruption.
- Large seizures do not automatically mean effective control when profits remain high enough to absorb losses.
