# China Animal Protection Law Gap / 中国动物保护立法空白

## Definition
[[ChinaAnimalProtectionLawGap]] is the source's claim that mainland China lacks a dedicated animal-protection law, leaving animal harm to be handled indirectly through food safety, public health, property, city management, or public-order categories.

## Current Synthesis
The 旺旺 episode uses the legal gap to explain why public anger after extreme cruelty does not easily become an institutional remedy. [[ZhangBoAnimalLaw|张博]] argues that current legal attention often appears when animal harm can be translated into human interests, while animal suffering itself has weak legal visibility. The concept does not claim that no animal-related rules exist; it marks the absence of a unified legal baseline against abuse, abandonment, unclear rescue duties, and weak enforcement responsibility.

## Key Claims
- Dedicated animal-protection legislation is absent in the source's mainland framing, despite repeated proposals since 2009.
- Existing animal-related rules often route protection through human interests such as health, food safety, property, or order.
- The lack of a clear legal baseline leaves volunteers, platforms, venues, and local officials negotiating animal-cruelty cases through unstable informal channels.
- Legislative difficulty is presented as a mix of industry interests, enforcement cost, and social-idea conflict, not only official indifference.
- A proposed baseline law is framed as anti-abandonment, rescue-duty, and enforcement-responsibility infrastructure rather than equal legal rights for all animals.

## Evidence
- Legal gap and repeated proposals: [[liulangquan-wangwang-zhisi-he-yichang-kuajing-dongbao-shengyuan]] says China has no dedicated animal-protection law and notes repeated National People's Congress proposals since 2009.
- Human-interest translation: [[liulangquan-wangwang-zhisi-he-yichang-kuajing-dongbao-shengyuan]] reports [[ZhangBoAnimalLaw|张博]]'s view that animal interests often need to become human interests before law sees them.
- Advocacy strain: [[liulangquan-wangwang-zhisi-he-yichang-kuajing-dongbao-shengyuan]] reports [[JiangHongAnimalVolunteer|江宏]] and other volunteers facing platform, venue, police, and grassroots-administration barriers.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode is not a full survey of Chinese law. It identifies the absence of a dedicated animal-protection statute while acknowledging that scattered animal-related rules exist in other regulatory domains.
