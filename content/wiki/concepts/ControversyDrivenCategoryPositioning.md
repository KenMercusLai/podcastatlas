# Controversy-Driven Category Positioning

## Definition
Controversy-driven category positioning is the use of a sharp comparative claim to make a product-category distinction memorable, with attention amplified by opponents, media coverage, or legal conflict.

## Current Synthesis
The episode's "世纪水战" case shows both the commercial force and the epistemic danger of this approach. [[NongfuSpring|农夫山泉]] contrasted natural water with purified water through health-oriented claims and demonstrations; competitors challenged the campaign as misleading and pseudoscientific, and a court found unfair competition. The dispute nevertheless made the category distinction nationally salient. Commercial effectiveness therefore cannot be treated as evidence that the underlying health claim was true or fair.

## Key Claims
- A comparative claim can reposition an entire category rather than merely advertise one brand.
- Industry opposition and legal conflict can amplify awareness beyond paid media.
- Memorable demonstrations can outrun their scientific validity or relevance.
- Winning attention or sales does not resolve truth, consumer-protection, or competition-law questions.
- A later category reversal can expose the difference between durable brand positioning and a permanent product commitment.

## Evidence
- Category contrast: [[fengwei-chihe-nongfu-shanquan-zhongshanshan-ruhe-sandu-wending-zhongguo-shoufu-gkwrijiotjlral0wegtwycfd]] describes the 2000 decision to stop purified water and claim natural water was more suitable for health.
- Amplification and sanction: [[fengwei-chihe-nongfu-shanquan-zhongshanshan-ruhe-sandu-wending-zhongguo-shoufu-gkwrijiotjlral0wegtwycfd]] records opposition from 69 companies, an unfair-competition finding, public apology requirements, and increased exposure.
- Strategic reversal: [[fengwei-chihe-nongfu-shanquan-zhongshanshan-ruhe-sandu-wending-zhongguo-shoufu-gkwrijiotjlral0wegtwycfd]] says Nongfu Spring returned to purified water roughly 24 years later while reframing the choice as consumer optionality.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is not the judgment, scientific evidence, or a verified market-share dataset. It reports the paradox as "lost the lawsuit, won the market," but that shorthand must not normalize misleading health claims or imply that controversy reliably produces durable growth.

## What Changed
- Created a positioning concept that keeps commercial amplification separate from scientific and legal validity.
