# 开封定都取舍 / Kaifeng Capital Tradeoff

Kaifeng capital tradeoff / 开封定都取舍 is the episode's account of why [[ZhaoKuangyin|赵匡胤]] kept the [[NorthernSong|北宋]] capital at [[Kaifeng|开封]] despite its military vulnerability. In [[169-songtaizu-zhisi-zhuying-fusheng-yinmoulun-zhong-de-renxing-mituan-shang-957052909]], the decisive constraint is logistics: southern grain and canal transport could reach Kaifeng more easily than Luoyang or Chang'an.

The tradeoff is that transport convenience increased defense pressure. The source says Kaifeng was geographically exposed and required heavy garrisoning, contributing to the "守内虚外" pattern and connecting capital geography to [[SongMilitaryCentralization|宋初军事集权]].

The concept matters because it prevents a simple hindsight judgment. The episode presents the capital choice as a real constraint problem: economic integration, supply routes, military geography, and regime security had to be balanced, and every answer carried costs.

## Key Claims
- Capital choice is an institutional design problem, not only a symbolic preference.
- Logistics can force a ruler toward a militarily weaker location.
- Defensive vulnerability can reshape troop distribution and central military policy.
- A decision can be rational under immediate constraints while still creating long-term risk.
