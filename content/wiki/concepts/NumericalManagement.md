## Definition

Numerical management is the institutional capacity to make public affairs calculable, comparable, verifiable, accountable, and repeatedly executable through shared standards rather than merely recording numbers.

## Current Synthesis

In the episode's account of [[RayHuang|黄仁宇]], numerical management links land, taxation, debt, military pay, weapons, logistics, and official performance to common procedures. Its opposite is not the absence of arithmetic. It is a system in which records cannot reliably travel across levels, like cases receive inconsistent treatment, responsibility is difficult to trace, and implementation depends on personal judgment or patronage.

[[ZhangJuzheng|张居正]] and [[QiJiguang|戚继光]] show partial advances through appraisal, land and tax work, detailed training, and military standards. Their vulnerability after political reversal shows that measurement tools become durable only when organizations can preserve and reproduce them.

## Key Claims

- Numerical management requires shared definitions and procedures, not figures alone.
- Verification and accountability connect measurement to governing capacity.
- Standardization can reduce dependence on each official's moral interpretation.
- Local technical success is not institutionalization unless it survives personnel and patronage change.
- Measurement remains political because standards determine visibility, responsibility, and resource allocation.

- Administrative reform: [[vol-362-wanli-shiwunian-da-shibai-shidai-de-ren-hai-neng-nadao-da-jieguo-ma-gkwriw4o2at-a-vjhw-t-4dvr|Vol.362]] treats Zhang's appraisal and fiscal measures as a partial capacity-building case.
- Technical standards: [[vol-362-wanli-shiwunian-da-shibai-shidai-de-ren-hai-neng-nadao-da-jieguo-ma-gkwriw4o2at-a-vjhw-t-4dvr|Vol.362]] treats Qi's detailed manual as both practical achievement and evidence of wider standardization gaps.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The episode does not establish a quantitative comparison of Ming capacity or prove that numerical administration alone would resolve political conflict. Measures can be false, incomplete, coercive, or detached from local knowledge. The concept should not be collapsed into modern managerialism or treated as morally neutral.

## What Changed

- Established the distinction between number keeping and reproducible administrative capacity.
