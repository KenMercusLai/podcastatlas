# Private-Label Lookalike Risk / 自有品牌近似包装风险

## Definition
Private-label lookalike risk is the legal and brand-trust risk that a retailer-owned product uses packaging, color, shape, or visual cues close enough to a mature brand that it may create confusion or unfair-competition claims.

## Current Synthesis
The source uses [[Shiseido|资生堂]]'s suit against [[Aldi|奥乐齐]] as the case. Aldi's discount-retail model depends partly on private-label products that lower price and recognition cost, but beauty lookalikes can trigger a different boundary: shoppers may understand the value cue faster, while the premium brand may see its identity being borrowed too aggressively.

## Key Claims
- Private-label packaging can reduce consumer decision cost by referencing known category codes.
- The same resemblance can become legal risk if it appears to trade on a premium brand's distinctiveness.
- Discount retailers face a sharper boundary because own-brand value is central to the format.
- Beauty and skincare categories are especially sensitive because packaging, shelf visual identity, and premium trust carry much of the product meaning.

## Evidence
- Lawsuit evidence - [[tulasi-zishengtang-qisu-pingjia-shangchao-aoleqi-alo-qianyue-wangyibo-1014854463]] says Shiseido sued Aldi over unfair competition and that the case was due for a November hearing.
- Lookalike evidence - [[tulasi-zishengtang-qisu-pingjia-shangchao-aoleqi-alo-qianyue-wangyibo-1014854463]] says industry media found Aldi private-label cosmetics similar in appearance to [[CleDePeauBeaute|CPB / Clé de Peau Beauté]].
- Format evidence - [[tulasi-zishengtang-qisu-pingjia-shangchao-aoleqi-alo-qianyue-wangyibo-1014854463]] says Aldi's low-price private-label goods are a core competitiveness source but lack the marketing budgets of mature brands.
- Pattern evidence - [[tulasi-zishengtang-qisu-pingjia-shangchao-aoleqi-alo-qianyue-wangyibo-1014854463]] says Aldi has faced similar overseas suits and references internal-design documents using existing brands as comparison samples.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The case is pending in the source account. The page does not decide infringement or unfair competition; it only records the risk pattern and the alleged resemblance.
