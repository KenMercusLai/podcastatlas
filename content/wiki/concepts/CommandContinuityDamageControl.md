# 统帅连续性危机处置 / Command Continuity Damage Control

## Definition
统帅连续性危机处置 / Command Continuity Damage Control is the practice of preventing a leader's injury, death, or visible incapacity from becoming collective command collapse. It focuses on public continuity signals, staff substitution, and rapid successor or proxy performance.

## Current Synthesis
The concept now has both nonfatal and fatal cases. At Guangwu, [[LiuBang|刘邦]] hides the seriousness of [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s arrow wound and uses [[ZhangLiang|张良]] as a visible stabilizer. In Hanji 1045, [[SunCe|孙策]] cannot hide the outcome, so damage control shifts to successor designation: seal transfer to [[SunQuan|孙权]], [[ZhangZhaoLateHan|张昭]]'s camp tour with the young heir, and [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]]'s armed presence in [[WuCommanderyLateHan|吴郡]].

## Key Claims
- The first threat after a commander's injury can be morale and legitimacy collapse rather than the wound alone.
- Public narrative matters: a leader may minimize injury, recode the event, or project continuity through deputies.
- Trusted staff can substitute for the leader's visible presence when the principal cannot safely appear.
- When the leader is dying, the key move shifts from concealment to rapid succession performance.
- The pattern follows [[LeaderPersonalExposure|领导者轻身涉险]] when exposed movement or combat creates damage to the command center.

## Evidence
- Nonfatal concealment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-164-1-liubang-geqiang-chaofeng-qi-de-xiangyu-shejian-touxi-lhs6miq2aa3avnny-prxtvkfxgvy|Hanji 164-1]] shows Liu Bang publicly minimizing Xiang Yu's arrow wound and presenting it as a minor toe injury.
- Deputy visibility: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-164-1-liubang-geqiang-chaofeng-qi-de-xiangyu-shejian-touxi-lhs6miq2aa3avnny-prxtvkfxgvy|Hanji 164-1]] has Zhang Liang patrol the camp to stabilize order while Liu Bang cannot openly expose weakness.
- Fatal succession handoff: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1045-ta-shi-jiangdong-baye-de-kaichuangzhe-kexi-si-de-tai-wonang-lgjjdmcfw1qkuba2xblgvvlfpy9d|Hanji 1045]] has Sun Ce hand the seal to Sun Quan and instruct advisers to assist him after the assassination wound.
- Visible successor performance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1045-ta-shi-jiangdong-baye-de-kaichuangzhe-kexi-si-de-tai-wonang-lgjjdmcfw1qkuba2xblgvvlfpy9d|Hanji 1045]] shows Zhang Zhao taking Sun Quan through the camps and Zhou Yu remaining as central guard.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Concealment and visible succession are different tools and should not be merged mechanically. Liu Bang's case preserves the existing leader's authority, while Sun Ce's case transfers authority to a successor; both fit because each prevents damaged command from becoming public collapse.
