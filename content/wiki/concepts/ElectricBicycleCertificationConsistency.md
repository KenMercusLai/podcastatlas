# Electric Bicycle Certification Consistency / 电动自行车认证一致性

## Definition
Electric-bicycle certification consistency is the requirement that mass-produced vehicles continue to match the design and characteristics of the samples that received compulsory product certification.

## Current Synthesis
Certification is not only a pre-market test; it is a continuing production-control obligation. When factory inspections find that sold vehicles differ from certified samples, certificate suspension can block registration and move compliance costs rapidly through manufacturers, dealers, and buyers. New material and smart-function requirements can improve standardization while raising redesign cost and accelerating industry consolidation.

## Key Claims
- A passed sample does not establish ongoing compliance unless mass production remains consistent with it.
- Certificate suspension can convert an upstream factory-control failure into an immediate retail and vehicle-registration problem.
- Tighter material limits and smart-function requirements create redesign, sourcing, weight, and price tradeoffs.
- Smaller manufacturers may face disproportionate exit pressure when compliance costs rise.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode does not list affected certificate numbers, suspension duration, failure rates, remedies, accident evidence, or the exact standard text. Coverage of leading brands does not mean every product failed, and anticipated small-firm exit or demand recovery remains an industry forecast rather than an observed result.

## What Changed
- Added continuing production conformity as the key distinction between certification and one-time sample approval.
- Connected certificate suspension to registration, redesign cost, and possible consolidation.
