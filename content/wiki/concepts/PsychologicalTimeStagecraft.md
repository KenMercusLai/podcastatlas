# Psychological Time Stagecraft / 心理时间舞台化

## Definition
Psychological time stagecraft is the use of scenery, movement, lighting, sound, and spatial rules to make memory, fantasy, and present perception coexist visibly on stage rather than separating them through narration or conventional scene breaks.

## Current Synthesis
[[DeathOfASalesman|《推销员之死》]] supplies the founding case. The episode describes a two-act design in which transparent scenery, lighting, music, and actors' relation to walls distinguish present reality from remembered or imagined action. The house is physically bounded in the present, while memory can pass through those boundaries.

This makes [[WillyLoman|Willy Loman]]'s unstable temporal experience an event the audience sees and hears rather than a condition explained after the fact. It also clarifies the medium difference identified by the hosts: prose can expand interior history directly, while theatre can make incompatible times occupy the same live space.

## Key Claims
- Stage space can encode mental boundaries through repeatable physical rules.
- Breaking a spatial rule can signal entry into memory or fantasy without stopping the performance.
- Lighting and music can orient the audience while preserving ambiguity between psychological and external events.
- Simultaneous stage presence gives past experience continuing pressure on present action.
- Performance meaning depends on actors' bodies, pauses, interruptions, and movement as well as dialogue.
- Prose adaptation may gain interior explanation while losing live temporal coexistence.

## Evidence
- Permeable set: [[vol-355-tuixiaoyuan-zhisi-naxie-shabusi-wode-weishenme-yizhi-zai-dawo-gkwriw4ols9xawr6vqtifmya]] describes transparent scenery and changing wall rules for reality and memory.
- Continuous transition: [[vol-355-tuixiaoyuan-zhisi-naxie-shabusi-wode-weishenme-yizhi-zai-dawo-gkwriw4ols9xawr6vqtifmya]] says many shifts occur without curtain closure or blackout.
- Embodied contradiction: [[vol-355-tuixiaoyuan-zhisi-naxie-shabusi-wode-weishenme-yizhi-zai-dawo-gkwriw4ols9xawr6vqtifmya]] emphasizes tension between what characters say and what bodies, gestures, and voices reveal.
- Adaptation tradeoff: [[vol-355-tuixiaoyuan-zhisi-naxie-shabusi-wode-weishenme-yizhi-zai-dawo-gkwriw4ols9xawr6vqtifmya]] contrasts a novel's expanded psychology with theatre's simultaneous past and present.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept describes a formal affordance, not a claim that theatre always represents memory more effectively than prose or film. Transparent sets and spatial rule-breaking require production choices and audience legibility; other media can create temporal simultaneity through montage, voice, typography, or interactive form.

## What Changed
- Created the concept from the episode's account of permeable scenery, live transition, embodied contradiction, and adaptation tradeoffs.
