# Face-to-Face Creative Exchange / 面对面创造性交流

## Definition
Face-to-face creative exchange is the source's claim that co-presence, informal conversation, and shared attention matter most when collaborators do not yet know the goal, method, or evaluation frame.

## Current Synthesis
The source does not reject remote work for execution. It distinguishes execution tasks from exploratory work: remote collaboration may preserve performance on defined tasks, but it can reduce the accidental collisions, shared code reading, lunch conversations, and hallway checks that help more disruptive ideas form.

The Transformer paper example gives the concept its AI-history hook. The episode says participants ate together, talked near water coolers, looked at one another's code, and developed ideas through shared presence, making physical proximity part of the innovation story.

## Key Claims
- Co-presence matters most when the target is unclear and the work is exploratory.
- Informal encounters can move ideas faster than scheduled meetings in early discovery.
- Remote work may be adequate for execution while still weaker for creative recombination.
- Face-to-face exchange supports oral defense, show-and-tell, and apprenticeship-style learning in education.
- The value of co-presence depends on serious prior thought; conversation cannot substitute for preparation.

## Evidence
- Remote-work distinction: [[401-bianyu-manju-manzou-women-haineng-yinglai-lingwai-yige-datemao-si-de-xiatian-ma-f7495e05]] says research suggests remote work may not hurt execution but may reduce more creative or disruptive ideas.
- Transformer example: [[401-bianyu-manju-manzou-women-haineng-yinglai-lingwai-yige-datemao-si-de-xiatian-ma-f7495e05]] describes the 2017 Transformer contributors eating lunch together, talking informally, and checking code in person.
- Education use: [[401-bianyu-manju-manzou-women-haineng-yinglai-lingwai-yige-datemao-si-de-xiatian-ma-f7495e05]] argues for reviving face-to-face exchange through one-on-one conversation, oral exams, and show-and-tell.
- Preparation boundary: [[401-bianyu-manju-manzou-women-haineng-yinglai-lingwai-yige-datemao-si-de-xiatian-ma-f7495e05]] has Xu Tao note that Dartmouth-style collision depends on participants having already done deep thinking.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source's claim is task-dependent. It does not show that all remote work is inferior, only that undefined creative work and deep education may lose something when interaction becomes overly scheduled or mediated.
