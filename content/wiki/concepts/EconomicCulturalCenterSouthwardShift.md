# 经济文化中心南移 / Economic-Cultural Center Southward Shift

Economic-cultural center southward shift / 经济文化中心南移 is one of the major structural changes that [[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]] attaches to [[FiveDynastiesTenKingdoms|五代十国]]. The episode argues that the period's southern regimes were not peripheral because their relative stability helped preserve population, production, culture, and institutions during northern upheaval.

The source connects this shift to places and regimes such as [[Wuyue|吴越]], [[FormerShu|前蜀]], [[LaterShu|后蜀]], [[SouthernTang|南唐]], and [[SouthernHan|南汉]]. It also makes later [[NorthernSong|北宋]] prosperity easier to understand as an inheritance of southern-regional development, not only as a sudden Song achievement.
