# Overseas Inventory Forecasting Mismatch / 海外库存预测错配

## Definition
Overseas inventory forecasting mismatch is the simultaneous appearance of excess stock and stockouts when a global consumer business must forecast demand earlier than its domestic supply chain can replenish or rebalance foreign markets.

## Current Synthesis
The [[PopMart|泡泡玛特]] case shows why aggregate inventory can conceal SKU- and market-level scarcity. Long ordering, production, export, and customs cycles turn small forecast errors into stale stock in one place and missed demand in another; cross-region transfers add time and cost. High prior-year sales can worsen reported growth comparisons, but they do not remove the operating problem of matching product, place, and timing.

## Key Claims
- Longer replenishment lead times increase the cost of forecast error.
- Excess inventory and stockouts can coexist across SKUs and regions.
- Aggregate inventory totals do not reveal whether the right products are in the right markets.
- Cross-border reallocation is slower and more expensive than domestic correction.
- A high comparison base can explain year-on-year decline without explaining inventory quality.

## Evidence
- **Lead-time amplification:** [[dafeixin-pingguo-xin-xitong-keyi-jinzhi-yaoyiyao-guanggao-zhangyiming-zai-deng-hurun-baifu-bangshou-gkwridoot6kmafx6cwtxdrfn]] says Pop Mart's overseas goods depend largely on a domestic supply chain with longer order-to-export cycles.
- **Simultaneous mismatch:** [[dafeixin-pingguo-xin-xitong-keyi-jinzhi-yaoyiyao-guanggao-zhangyiming-zai-deng-hurun-baifu-bangshou-gkwridoot6kmafx6cwtxdrfn]] reports that foreign operations can face both accumulated stock and missing products when demand forecasts diverge from actual demand.
- **Reporting context:** [[dafeixin-pingguo-xin-xitong-keyi-jinzhi-yaoyiyao-guanggao-zhangyiming-zai-deng-hurun-baifu-bangshou-gkwridoot6kmafx6cwtxdrfn]] cites a historically strong prior-year quarter as a qualification on the scale of the reported revenue decline.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not provide SKU-level inventory, sell-through, write-downs, store cohorts, regional transfer costs, or audited explanations for the decline. Supply-chain mismatch is therefore a plausible operating mechanism, not a complete causal decomposition of Pop Mart's performance.

## What Changed
- Established a cross-border inventory concept that separates total stock from product-location fit.
- Preserved high-base effects as a reporting qualification rather than an operational explanation.
