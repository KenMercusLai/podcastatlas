# Music Access Infrastructure / 音乐接触基础设施

## Definition
Music access infrastructure is the combined network of media, copying, peer exchange, equipment, institutions, and digital distribution that determines what listeners and musicians can encounter, study, circulate, and make.

## Current Synthesis
For early Chinese rock, access was not one channel. Radio, affordable flexi discs, privately copied cassette compilations, campus exchange, cut-out imports, record labels, instruments, studios, and later internet downloads formed successive layers. Scarcity could intensify attention and curiosity, while greater abundance expanded imagination but also made music easier to treat as background consumption.

## Key Claims
- Informal peer exchange can function as cultural infrastructure when formal markets are thin.
- Listening access and production access are distinct but mutually reinforcing.
- Record companies can operate as schools and resource hubs when technical institutions are immature.
- Abundance expands stylistic possibility without guaranteeing attentive listening.
- Audience labor matters because unfamiliar art requires active interpretation, not only availability.

## Evidence
- Listening network - [[ep292-duitan-liwan-zhongguo-yaogun-zai-dasha-bengta-zhong-dansheng-lskxe_5_cj3wvi-xqdyoljx8ltcgg]] traces radio, flexi discs, copied tapes, campus exchange, cut-outs, and downloads.
- Production network - [[ep292-duitan-liwan-zhongguo-yaogun-zai-dasha-bengta-zhong-dansheng-lskxe_5_cj3wvi-xqdyoljx8ltcgg]] links instruments, recording equipment, labels, musicians, and studios to professional development.
- Scarcity and abundance - [[ep292-duitan-liwan-zhongguo-yaogun-zai-dasha-bengta-zhong-dansheng-lskxe_5_cj3wvi-xqdyoljx8ltcgg]] contrasts early hunger for unknown music with later consumption patterns.
- Active reception - [[ep292-duitan-liwan-zhongguo-yaogun-zai-dasha-bengta-zhong-dansheng-lskxe_5_cj3wvi-xqdyoljx8ltcgg]] argues that art asks listeners to invest effort in unfamiliar experience.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Scarcity can exclude people and restrict diversity; it should not be romanticized as inherently superior. Abundance does not necessarily produce shallow attention, and the episode does not measure access, pricing, piracy, regional inequality, or listening behavior systematically.

## What Changed
- Unified successive physical, social, institutional, and digital channels into one access-and-learning framework.
