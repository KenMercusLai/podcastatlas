## Definition

名分倒置式求生 / Status-Reversal Survival Bargain is a political-survival pattern in which a threatened actor accepts an intentionally abnormal public rank relationship, often together with material concession, to demonstrate submission and remove the appearance of rivalry.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-195-lvhou-zhangquan-liubang-zhangzi-jimang-renmei-wei-niang-lokajlf0bhft1b7fo-hwazyqretn|Hanji 195]] supplies the bounded case. After an honored banquet seat allegedly makes [[LiuFeiQiKing|刘肥]] a target of [[LuZhi|吕雉]], he gives [[ChengyangCommandery|城阳郡]] to [[LuYuanPrincess|鲁元公主]] as a [[TangmuYi|汤沐邑]] and honors his younger half-sister as queen mother. The combined signal is stronger than ordinary apology: territory pays a durable material price, while the reversed kinship title publicly lowers Liu Fei and enlarges Lu Yuan's status.

The concept does not assume that humiliation alone creates safety. In this source, it operates because Liu Fei can make a credible concession from [[QiKingdomEarlyHan|齐国]], the beneficiary is Lu Zhi's daughter, and the new title reduces the threatened ruler's visible status claim. The private motives, poison plan, ceremony, and exact efficacy remain source-scoped.

## Key Claims

- Survival bargaining can work through public rank as well as through money, land, or military surrender.
- A deliberately excessive concession can signal that the threatened actor accepts subordination and is not contesting the center.
- Material transfer and symbolic demotion reinforce each other when the beneficiary is close to the dominant powerholder.
- The bargain preserves the actor by changing how dangerous the actor appears; it does not establish trust or justice.
- A successful outcome in one reported episode does not make self-humiliation a universally available or reliable strategy.

## Evidence

Territorial and symbolic concession:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-195-lvhou-zhangquan-liubang-zhangzi-jimang-renmei-wei-niang-lokajlf0bhft1b7fo-hwazyqretn|Hanji 195]] says Liu Fei yields Chengyang as Princess Lu Yuan's bathing estate and honors his younger half-sister as queen mother after the banquet crisis.

Threat reduction:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-195-lvhou-zhangquan-liubang-zhangzi-jimang-renmei-wei-niang-lokajlf0bhft1b7fo-hwazyqretn|Hanji 195]] interprets the arrangement as removing Lu Zhi's immediate concern and enabling Liu Fei's return to his kingdom, while keeping her intent and the settlement's exact causal force episode-attributed.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The supplied source is a structured popular-history summary, not an independent reconstruction of court procedure. It does not establish that the wine was poisoned, that Lu Zhi's sole concern was Liu Fei's rank, or that the concession alone secured release. “Recognizing his sister as mother” compresses a political title change into family language. The pattern should therefore be used as a source-grounded analytical lens, not as proof of private psychology or a general rule that submission guarantees survival.

## What Changed

- Created the concept from the Liu Fei-Princess Lu Yuan settlement.
- Distinguished symbolic rank reversal from the accompanying territorial concession.
- Made credible threat reduction, rather than humiliation itself, the operative mechanism.
