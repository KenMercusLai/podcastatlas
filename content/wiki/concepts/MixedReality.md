# Mixed Reality / 混合现实

## Definition
Mixed reality is an interface mode that blends virtual content with a mediated view of the physical world, often through cameras and displays in a headset.

## Current Synthesis
The RayNeo source places MR closer to VR-style headsets than to ordinary daily glasses. [[LiHongwei|李鸿伟]] says helmet or ski-goggle-like devices can use cameras to see the outside world and create MR, while still being heavier, more immersive, and more content-driven than all-day AI glasses.

The concept matters here because [[VisionPro|Vision Pro]] is treated as a useful MR/AR foundation device but not the final daily-wear form. MR can explore eye tracking, gestures, immersive applications, and spatial content, while AI glasses must solve a different adoption problem around comfort, transparency, battery, and ordinary social presence.

## Key Claims
- MR can merge virtual content with camera-mediated real-world perception.
- In this source, MR headsets remain closer to VR devices than to ordinary eyeglasses.
- MR is valuable for interaction and content experimentation even when all-day wear is unlikely.
- Smart glasses need lower friction than headset-style MR to become daily AI terminals.
- The source uses MR as a product-category distinction, not as a full technical taxonomy.
