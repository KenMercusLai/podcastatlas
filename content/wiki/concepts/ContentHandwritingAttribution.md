## Definition

内容—笔迹归因 / content-handwriting attribution is an investigation pattern that first uses a document's subject knowledge, vocabulary, or cultural references to narrow likely authors, then compares a separately obtained writing sample as corroboration.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1128-meiyou-heikeji-guren-ruhe-diaocha-nimingxin-lqdm57jzw9wqo_umlasz2j3vlzqh|Hanji 1128]] grounds the pattern in [[GuoYuanInvestigatorLateHan|国渊]]'s anonymous-letter case. The letter's connection to [[ZhangHengLateHan|张衡]]'s *Er Jing Fu* functions as a capability clue: rather than compare every person's hand, Guo Yuan searches among people able to understand the text. Agents then pose as students, request a written letter, and return it for comparison with the accusation.

The sequence matters more than the episode's “ancient black technology” framing. Content analysis reduces the candidate field; elicitation supplies a comparison sample; handwriting resemblance supports an identity hypothesis; arrest and interrogation follow. The source does not show modern validation standards, so the durable lesson is structured narrowing plus corroboration, not infallible identification.

## Key Claims

- Anonymous writing can reveal author attributes through knowledge and reference choice even when it omits a name.
- A content clue is a filter, not proof of authorship.
- A comparison sample becomes more useful when collected from a plausibly narrowed candidate set.
- Covert elicitation can reduce deliberate disguise but raises procedural and ethical questions.
- Handwriting similarity requires independent corroboration because resemblance and expert judgment can err.

## Evidence

- Knowledge-based filter: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1128-meiyou-heikeji-guren-ruhe-diaocha-nimingxin-lqdm57jzw9wqo_umlasz2j3vlzqh|Hanji 1128]] says the inquiry targets people able to explain *Er Jing Fu*.
- Sample elicitation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1128-meiyou-heikeji-guren-ruhe-diaocha-nimingxin-lqdm57jzw9wqo_umlasz2j3vlzqh|Hanji 1128]] says agents pose as students and ask the target to write a letter.
- Attribution outcome: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1128-meiyou-heikeji-guren-ruhe-diaocha-nimingxin-lqdm57jzw9wqo_umlasz2j3vlzqh|Hanji 1128]] says comparison identifies the suspected author before arrest and questioning.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The single source gives no original documents, candidate count, examiner independence, comparison rubric, error rate, alternative author hypothesis, chain of custody, confession reliability, or adjudication record. It therefore cannot validate handwriting comparison as conclusive forensic science or establish that the suspect was correctly convicted. The modern-sounding concept name describes the information sequence, not the reliability of every historical step.
