# Brand Narrative Evolution

## Definition
Brand narrative evolution is the deliberate widening of what a brand means as it moves from a concrete product difference toward a durable idea, place, or value system.

## Current Synthesis
[[NongfuSpring|农夫山泉]] is presented in three stages: "有点甜" made a taste difference memorable; "大自然的搬运工" made natural sources and minimal intervention central; and "什么样的水源，孕育什么样的生命" extended the story toward ecology, life, and responsibility. The sequence shows how a brand can preserve a recognizable core while changing the level of abstraction at which it competes.

## Key Claims
- Early narratives work best when they make a concrete product difference easy to repeat.
- Middle-stage narratives can turn product attributes into a broader organizing idea.
- Later narratives can add place, ecology, culture, or responsibility when the business has enough proof to carry them.
- Narrative continuity matters: each stage should remain legible as an extension rather than an unrelated campaign.
- Broader meaning cannot substitute for product quality, scientific validity, or operating execution.

## Evidence
- Taste stage: [[fengwei-chihe-nongfu-shanquan-zhongshanshan-ruhe-sandu-wending-zhongguo-shoufu-gkwrijiotjlral0wegtwycfd]] identifies the 1998 "有点甜" campaign as a simple natural-water taste distinction.
- Source stage: [[fengwei-chihe-nongfu-shanquan-zhongshanshan-ruhe-sandu-wending-zhongguo-shoufu-gkwrijiotjlral0wegtwycfd]] describes the 2008 "大自然的搬运工" line as a shift toward naturalness and source value.
- Ecology stage: [[fengwei-chihe-nongfu-shanquan-zhongshanshan-ruhe-sandu-wending-zhongguo-shoufu-gkwrijiotjlral0wegtwycfd]] links the 2017 water-source and life campaign to documentary-style ecological storytelling.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source interprets these campaigns as a coherent progression, but it does not measure their independent sales effect or establish the company's ecological performance. Narrative elevation can become image laundering if operational evidence does not support the values claimed.

## What Changed
- Created a three-stage account of product, idea, and ecological brand meaning.
