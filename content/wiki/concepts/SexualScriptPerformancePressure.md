# Sexual Script and Performance Pressure / 性脚本与表现压力

## Definition
Sexual script and performance pressure is the anxiety produced when a narrow sequence or outcome—especially penetration, erection, ejaculation, orgasm, or experience count—is treated as the definition of complete and successful sex.

## Current Synthesis
The source argues that absent formal sex education, pornography and popular culture can become default teachers. Their scripts often foreground visible intensity and a male-centered sequence rather than the slower, negotiated work of safety, arousal, touch, feedback, and mutual pleasure.

Once this script becomes an achievement standard, sex can resemble a task. Men may treat erection as the central proof of competence, while any partner may interpret interrupted desire or absent orgasm as evidence of unattractiveness or failure. A wider script includes embracing, touching, external stimulation, pacing, hygiene, communication, products, and stopping without defining every variation as deficiency.

## Key Claims
- Penetration-to-ejaculation is one possible sequence, not the universal definition of complete sex.
- Pornographic visibility and intensity are poor substitutes for reciprocal real-world education.
- Performance metrics can turn pleasure and curiosity into tests of competence or attractiveness.
- Good sexual interaction depends on feedback, pacing, safety, and partner-specific learning.
- Broadening the script does not remove the need to assess persistent pain, distress, or function concerns.

## Evidence
- Standard-process critique - [[ep293-xing-bu-rongyi-qinmi-geng-nan-women-que-de-shi-yuwang-haishi-heshi-de-ren-ljrvwz7kavgep3r4t8uxelvhxbql]] rejects treating male penetration as the start and ejaculation as the required ending of complete sex.
- Alternative intimacy - [[ep293-xing-bu-rongyi-qinmi-geng-nan-women-que-de-shi-yuwang-haishi-heshi-de-ren-ljrvwz7kavgep3r4t8uxelvhxbql]] includes embracing, touching, external stimulation, rhythm, and attention among legitimate forms of bodily intimacy.
- Performance identity - [[ep293-xing-bu-rongyi-qinmi-geng-nan-women-que-de-shi-yuwang-haishi-heshi-de-ren-ljrvwz7kavgep3r4t8uxelvhxbql]] describes erection, orgasm, uninterrupted desire, and experience as possible self-worth metrics.
- Learning environment - [[ep293-xing-bu-rongyi-qinmi-geng-nan-women-que-de-shi-yuwang-haishi-heshi-de-ren-ljrvwz7kavgep3r4t8uxelvhxbql]] links rough, task-like interaction to missing education and porn-shaped behavioral scripts.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Pornography exposure does not determine behavior, and preferred sexual activities vary. Broadening the script should not minimize pain, unwanted contact, persistent dysfunction, infection risk, pregnancy risk, or the need for qualified care. The source offers journalistic and counseling observations rather than prevalence estimates.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to separate mutually learned sexual interaction from narrow completion and adequacy tests.
