# Trust-Based Distribution

Trust-based distribution is the pattern where a product reaches customers through people, media, institutions, or communities that already carry credibility with the buyer. In [[advice-line-with-chris-riccobono-of-untuckit-c087247f-1f85-4c15-b4aa-4ffea0368c4c]], [[SnugSafety]] has to sell reassurance to seniors living alone and their adult children, so [[GuyRaz]] points toward earned media, local publications, doctors, churches, and other trusted intermediaries.

[[ChrisRiccobono]] adds a measurement layer by suggesting radio and audio-read tests that can explain Snug's story to the right demographic. The case shows that trust-based distribution is not only offline relationship work: it can combine paid audio, word of mouth, earned media, and professional/community referral channels as long as each channel is tested separately.

## Key Claims
- Trust-heavy products often need a messenger the buyer already believes.
- The true buyer may differ from the user, as with seniors using Snug and adult children possibly paying attention or paying.
- Trusted intermediaries can lower explanation friction, but they may require sales work and careful sequencing.
- Earned media can act as durable trust proof when the problem is emotional and safety-related.
- Trust-based distribution needs [[MeasuredChannelTesting]] because credibility channels can still fail to convert.
