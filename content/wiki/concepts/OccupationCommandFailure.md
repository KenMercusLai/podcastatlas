# Occupation Command Failure

## Definition
Occupation command failure is the compounding breakdown that occurs when political complacency, divided authority, poor logistics, vulnerable basing, and delayed decisions prevent an occupying force from responding coherently to resistance.

## Current Synthesis
The [[FirstAngloAfghanWar|First Anglo-Afghan War]] case in [[88-the-first-anglo-afghan-war-glt5156371854]] begins with apparent ease: Afghan opponents avoid a pitched battle, and the invading force reaches Kandahar and Kabul. That success encourages occupation routines detached from the force's actual exposure.

Failure then compounds. The Kabul cantonment is outside defensible high ground, Afghan fighters seize Company guns, winter closes relief routes, commanders disagree, and negotiations give opponents time to prepare the retreat route. Logistics and command therefore connect the occupation's political weakness to the column's destruction.

## Key Claims
- Easy entry can conceal the difference between taking a capital and controlling a political system.
- Poor basing converts surrounding terrain into an opponent's weapon.
- Mixed institutions and rival commanders impair coherent response.
- Luxury-heavy logistics do not guarantee food, fuel, maps, or mobility where needed.
- Delayed withdrawal allows resistance forces to shape routes and timing.
- Operational failure magnifies an already weak political settlement.

## Evidence
- Invasion logistics: [[88-the-first-anglo-afghan-war-glt5156371854]] describes the huge baggage train, desert crossing, and lack of a proper map.
- Kabul position: [[88-the-first-anglo-afghan-war-glt5156371854]] describes an exposed camp overlooked by hills and bombarded with captured guns.
- Command division: [[88-the-first-anglo-afghan-war-glt5156371854]] emphasizes rivalry among forces and commanders.
- Retreat preparation: [[88-the-first-anglo-afghan-war-glt5156371854]] says prolonged negotiation gave Afghan fighters time to prepare barriers and ambushes.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The framework should not erase Afghan agency by treating victory only as British failure. The source also emphasizes deliberate resistance, effective weapons, terrain use, intelligence, and ambush planning.

## What Changed
- Established a compound command-and-logistics model for the Kabul occupation and retreat.
