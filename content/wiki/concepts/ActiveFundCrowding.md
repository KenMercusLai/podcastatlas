# Active Fund Crowding / 主动基金抱团

Active fund crowding is the [[175-gongmu-jijin-erjibao-jizhi-de-baotuan-yu-gelie-zhihou-lv20uprxrcw0tde5ugohu29tmlg6]] frame for the repeated tendency of Chinese active public-equity funds to cluster in the same sectors, narratives, and flagship holdings. [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] argues that the 2026 second-quarter technology-growth concentration had reached an extreme: technology-growth allocation was cited around 70%, while financial-real-estate, medicine, and consumption were near low historical allocations.

The concept is narrower than [[MarketBreadthNarrowing]] but can help cause it. Breadth narrowing describes the whole market's shrinking participation; active fund crowding describes one institutional channel that reinforces that narrowing when similar fund managers, research sources, sales channels, and investor preferences all reward the same main line.

The source's main institutional claim is that crowding is not simply a failure of courage by individual managers. It follows from [[FundManagerRankingIncentives|fund manager ranking incentives]], shared sell-side research, style benchmarks, fund-sales lists, investor preference for recent winners, and a public-fund ecosystem where scale and relative performance are tightly linked.

## Key Claims
- Active fund crowding can happen in both growth and value cycles; the 2026 source case is AI, compute, optical electronics, and semiconductors.
- Concentration becomes more fragile when it is justified by high long-term demand but expressed through near-term upstream bottlenecks.
- A crowded portfolio can keep working while the main narrative has marginal inflows or forced benchmark participation, but the same structure can make drawdowns severe after style rotation.
- This episode distinguishes the 2026 technology crowding cycle from the 2021 core-asset cycle by noting that active-equity scale grew largely through profit rather than clear net subscriptions.
- Crowding has a holder-return layer: investors who buy a star manager after rankings and scale surge may experience losses even if the fund's calendar-year return remains high.
