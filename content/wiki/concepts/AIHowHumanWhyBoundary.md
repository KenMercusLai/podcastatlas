# AI How, Human Why Boundary

## Definition
The AI how, human why boundary is the claim that AI can support execution, data use, and method selection, but humans must still define purpose, judgment, values, relationships, and the reason for acting.

## Current Synthesis
[[AraceliSegarra]] uses climbing rather than office software as the root analogy in [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-842831df1049b1024b0dfd8bf11ec723-108598757-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-842831df1049b1024b0dfd8bf11ec723-108598757]]. More data does not erase risk on a mountain, and more AI assistance does not erase the need for judgment. People still have to decide why they are climbing, which tradeoffs matter, whose perspectives are missing, and when a model's answer is only one input.

The education and early-career evidence in [[276-dang-ai-geichu-suoyou-daan-nianqingren-ruhe-zhaodao-ziji-de-wenti-ljjh4ya6beiypsghj49ywicw1c6t]] makes the remaining “why” operational when AI compresses research, drafting, coding, and routine office work: choose the problem, define quality, identify the missing real-world context, refuse a misleading local optimum, and accept the consequences of the result. Together, the sources show that purpose is not an abstract slogan after execution; it is a constraint on what gets delegated and a responsibility test for the final decision.

## Key Claims
- AI can help with the "how" by improving methods, data use, and execution support.
- AI cannot supply the "why" because purpose, values, and meaning remain human responsibilities.
- Data reduces some uncertainty but does not eliminate risk or the need to decide under incomplete conditions.
- Curiosity and judgment are named as leadership skills for an AI-shaped world.
- Diversity of viewpoints and social relationships remain part of decision quality when technology accelerates action.
- The pace of technological change creates an acclimatization problem: fatigue may become the hidden constraint.
- Problem definition, quality criteria, and responsibility for consequences turn the human “why” into concrete work practices.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Neither source evaluates specific AI systems or demonstrates a fixed technical boundary. Their claim is normative and practice-oriented: even strong execution tools leave purpose, responsibility, social context, and final judgment with people, while people themselves can still exercise those roles badly.

## What Changed
- Added problem definition, explicit quality criteria, authorship, and responsibility for consequences as concrete expressions of the human “why.”
