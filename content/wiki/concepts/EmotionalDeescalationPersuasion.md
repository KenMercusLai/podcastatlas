# Emotional De-escalation Persuasion / 情绪降温式说服

Emotional de-escalation persuasion / 情绪降温式说服 is the pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-91-chulong-ruhe-qiaokai-shougua-zhaotaihou-de-men-1-lmgd7kdl1nrfwcrtkqttumtlpqok]] draws from [[ChuLong|触龙]]'s opening approach to [[ZhaoWeihou|赵威后]]. When the target is angry, defensive, and already expecting another argument, the persuader first changes the emotional setting before presenting the policy point.

The source case is the hostage dispute over [[ChangAnJunZhao|长安君]]. Zhao Weihou has rejected ministers urging her to send him to [[QiState|齐国]] so Zhao can receive aid against [[QinState|秦国]]. Chu Long does not begin with the hostage issue. He enters slowly, talks about age, legs, health, appetite, and his own youngest son, letting the conversation move from threat to shared parental concern. Only then can he distinguish keeping a child close from planning for a child's long-term position.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-91-chulong-ruhe-qiaokai-shougua-zhaotaihou-de-men-2-liy5yulg2ysop-jwfigvdkub1pk7]] shows the payoff. Once Zhao Weihou is already in the emotional frame of children, grief, and long-range parental care, Chu Long can make the harder claim that giving Chang'an Jun privilege without public contribution may leave him exposed after her death. The episode's later critique matters for the concept: de-escalation can make an argument effective even when the argument's historical explanation is only partly right.

## Key Claims
- Directly demanding that an angry person calm down can intensify resistance because it challenges the emotion before the relationship is reopened.
- De-escalation works by lowering perceived threat: pace, posture, topic choice, and self-disclosure matter before the argument itself.
- The method can make analogy persuasive because the listener helps supply the emotional premise instead of merely receiving an instruction.
- In this source, parental love becomes the bridge from private feeling to state action: the same care that protects a child can justify a painful political placement.
- Emotional sequencing and factual accuracy are separable: a persuasion can succeed by fitting the listener's frame even when its institutional diagnosis needs later correction.
- The pattern overlaps with [[ChuaimoTechnique|揣摩]], but it focuses on emotional sequencing rather than only audience-specific interest analysis.
