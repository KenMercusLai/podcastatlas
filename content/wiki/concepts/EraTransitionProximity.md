# Era Transition Proximity / 时代远走与新文明靠近

Era transition proximity / 时代远走与新文明靠近 is [[JiaZhangke|贾樟柯]]'s formulation in [[ep276-duihua-jiazhangke-shang-yige-shidai-zouyuan-yiding-shi-yinwei-lingyige-shidai-zai-kaojin-ni-lqzsj-ujv-gpem-796cvncekdewc]] that an era does not simply move away; it moves away because another era is approaching. He ties this feeling to the making of [[FengLiuYiDai|《风流一代》]], the perceived end of globalization, new community forms, changed social management, changed emotional methods, and AI.

The concept is useful because it avoids pure nostalgia. The older era is felt as receding only because new civilizational forms are already present. This makes EP276 a bridge between Jia's older-film reception and EP277's more direct discussion of [[FilmRealityBeyondRealism]] and [[AIVideoProductionWorkflow]].

## Key Claims
- Era change is relational: one period becomes distant because a new period reorganizes attention, community, labor, and emotion.
- AI is not treated only as a tool in this episode; it is a sign that a new civilizational environment is approaching.
- Younger people may gain freedom from older burdens while losing some historical and interpersonal continuity.
- Internet community labels can intensify generation gaps by making culture visible within narrower groups.
