# Grain Leverage Alliance Betrayal / 粮食诱导式同盟背叛

Grain leverage alliance betrayal / 粮食诱导式同盟背叛 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-999-sanguo-luanshi-renxing-you-duome-cuiruo-li8tz30elca7bnipei6dfqdxmmeu|Hanji 999]]. [[YuanShu|袁术]] is attacking [[LiuBei|刘备]] in [[Xuzhou|徐州]], but he cannot easily break Liu Bei from the front while [[LyuBu|吕布]] remains a possible factor. He therefore offers Lü Bu grain in exchange for attacking [[Xiapi|下邳]].

The concept names a narrower mechanism than general [[InterestStructuredAllianceFragility|利益结构式联盟脆弱性]]. Here the switch is pulled by a wartime life-support resource. Grain is not only payment; under late-Han hunger it is the condition for soldiers, city control, and political survival. That is why the source says grain can matter more than gold.

The betrayal becomes decisive because resource leverage meets an inside breach. [[ZhangFei|张飞]] kills [[CaoBaoLateHan|曹豹]], [[XuDanLateHan|许耽]] opens Xiapi to Lü Bu, and Liu Bei's family falls into Lü Bu's hands. The result is a [[ThreatDrivenAllianceReversal|威胁感驱动的同盟反转]] prelude: before Lü Bu later attacks Liu Bei because Liu Bei has grown too strong, Lü Bu first becomes Liu Bei's superior by accepting Yuan Shu's food-backed invitation.

## Key Claims
- In famine politics, food can buy action that ordinary friendship or status language cannot restrain.
- A resource offer becomes more dangerous when the target has an internal city faction or gate-opening path.
- The visible betrayal can outlive the payment: Yuan Shu stops delivering grain, but Lü Bu still keeps Xiapi and the new superior position.
- Resource-induced betrayal differs from later threat-induced reversal because the trigger is immediate supply need and opportunity, not fear of an ally's future strength.
