# Private Interest As Public Use / 私利转公功

Private interest as public use / 私利转公功 is the incentive-design idea [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-75-4-weishenme-shuo-guanzhong-caishi-jingji-zhan-de-bizu-lrdleqozazh4-xdwas4karzgbmza]] attributes to [[GuanZhong|管仲]]. The episode says Guan Zhong does not try to suppress profit-seeking or self-interest; he tries to organize them so that many private motives become labor, trade, tax revenue, consumption, and state strength.

The source's phrase is "集大家之私成社会之功." This makes Guan Zhong different from a purely moralizing ruler: the point is not that people become selfless, but that institutions make ordinary self-interest socially useful. The idea underlies the episode's treatment of resource taxation, merchant attraction, luxury consumption, and public works.

## Key Claims
- Self-interest is treated as durable human material, not as a defect policy can simply remove.
- Governance works by channeling motives into productive labor and circulation.
- The state gains more when it structures incentives than when it only condemns greed.
- The concept helps explain why the same episode can discuss merchants, luxury, taxes, and employment as parts of one Guan Zhong program.
