# Somatization As Body Alarm / 躯体化作为身体报警

## Definition
Somatization as body alarm / 躯体化作为身体报警 is the episode's frame for emotional distress appearing as bodily symptoms that may warn a person to stop, assess, relax, seek help, or rule out physical disease.

## Current Synthesis
[[JiangTaoPsychiatrist|江涛]] explains somatization as distress crossing from emotion into body. Fear, anger, anxiety, regret, resentment, and related states can appear through heart, breathing, bladder, mouth, jaw, muscle, neck, shoulder, pain, or other symptom channels.

The episode keeps two boundaries together. First, bodily symptoms can be meaningful emotional signals rather than random failures. Second, they still require medical judgment: organic disease may need to be excluded through appropriate examination, imaging, neurological checks, or other tests before a symptom is treated as primarily somatic expression of distress.

## Key Claims
- Distress can appear as bodily symptoms before the person has usable emotional language for it.
- Somatization can function as an alarm telling the person to pause, lower load, or seek help.
- Body symptoms should not be dismissed as "just psychological" without appropriate medical exclusion.
- Online symptom matching can intensify fear when symptoms are interpreted without clinical context.
- Regulation methods should match the person's temperament: quiet practices, movement, sport, singing, or hobbies can all work for different people.
- The body-emotion boundary is especially important when achievement pressure or excessive goals exceed capacity.

## Evidence
- Symptom conversion - [[shidai-zhenghou-yu-anding-cixin-lphaw0qek8ibr0jt44yygou6akql]] explains somatization as negative emotions transforming into physical symptoms.
- Symptom examples - [[shidai-zhenghou-yu-anding-cixin-lphaw0qek8ibr0jt44yygou6akql]] mentions heart-neurosis-like symptoms, overbreathing, exam-related urination, bladder complaints, burning mouth, jaw issues, fascia pain, and neck-shoulder pain as possible branches.
- Medical exclusion - [[shidai-zhenghou-yu-anding-cixin-lphaw0qek8ibr0jt44yygou6akql]] distinguishes organic neurological or bodily disease from psychiatric interpretation through tests such as EEG, CT, or MRI where appropriate.
- Body alarm - [[shidai-zhenghou-yu-anding-cixin-lphaw0qek8ibr0jt44yygou6akql]] says somatization can signal that the body or mind is warning the person to stop and look.
- Regulation fit - [[shidai-zhenghou-yu-anding-cixin-lphaw0qek8ibr0jt44yygou6akql]] says emotional release differs by person, from meditation and mindfulness to boxing, cycling, parkour, dance, singing, or hobbies.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This concept must not be used to dismiss pain, neurological symptoms, cardiopulmonary symptoms, urinary symptoms, oral symptoms, or musculoskeletal symptoms. The source explicitly keeps organic disease exclusion and professional judgment in scope. It is a mental-health literacy frame, not a diagnostic answer.
