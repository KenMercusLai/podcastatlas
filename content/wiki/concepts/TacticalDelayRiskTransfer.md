## Definition

Tactical delay risk transfer / 延时转责式政治化解 is a political maneuver that turns a dangerous immediate act into a defensible slower process, preserving obedience in form while preventing irreversible exposure until the authority structure or facts change.

## Current Synthesis

The concept now has both a Western Han court case and an Eastern Han outer-relative case. In Hanji 150 part 4, [[ChenPing|Chen Ping]] and [[ZhouBo|Zhou Bo]] receive [[LiuBang|Liu Bang]]'s order to kill [[FanKuai|Fan Kuai]]. Killing Fan Kuai would create danger with [[LuZhi|Lu Zhi]]'s side, but open refusal could offend Liu Bang. They convert execution into arrest, custody, and transport, leaving the final decision with the ruler and allowing timing to change before the irreversible act occurs. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-193-liubang-zhisi-yiyan-baohanchao-sibainian-jiangshan-2-lkw7bhp0z4quyzwu9vkukwubgpgp|Hanji 193 part 2]] repeats the event within the same podcast series and adds the operational payoff: Zhou Bo maintains army command, Liu Bang dies before final disposition, Chen Ping enters the successor court quickly, and Fan Kuai is released and restored.

Hanji 767 adds [[LiHeEasternHan|Li He]] as a non-lethal version of the same structure. The Hanzhong administrator fears not sending gifts to [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]], while Li He judges that Dou is near disaster. Li He accepts the mission, travels slowly, and lets Dou's fall occur before the association is completed. In both cases, delay is not passivity; it is a structured action that keeps the actor legible while shifting final risk away from immediate execution.

## Key Claims

- In lethal or purge-prone politics, delaying an irreversible act can preserve agency without open defiance.
- The tactic works only when delay has an operational wrapper, such as custody, transport, mission travel, or reporting.
- Risk is transferred away from the subordinate's immediate hands and back toward higher authority, changed timing, or altered public facts.
- The maneuver can protect life, office, or reputation by preventing premature finality.
- Success may require follow-through after the delay, because changed authority can create a second danger field for the official who deferred the order.
- Delay becomes irresponsible when it lacks a concrete process or when the actor merely avoids duty without changing the risk structure.

## Evidence

- Execution-order conversion: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-150-chenping-weihe-bei-cheng-zhiwei-chenggong-zhengzhijia-yangban-4-lhhprrkjcuzijgndi2uuazaalern|Hanji 150 part 4]] says Chen Ping and Zhou Bo arrest and transport Fan Kuai rather than execute Liu Bang's dangerous order immediately.
- Upward risk transfer: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-150-chenping-weihe-bei-cheng-zhiwei-chenggong-zhengzhijia-yangban-4-lhhprrkjcuzijgndi2uuazaalern|Hanji 150 part 4]] frames the prison-cart solution as leaving final responsibility to the sovereign and changed succession context.
- Operational payoff and transition follow-through: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-193-liubang-zhisi-yiyan-baohanchao-sibainian-jiangshan-2-lkw7bhp0z4quyzwu9vkukwubgpgp|Hanji 193 part 2]] reports continuing army command, Fan Kuai's transport and release, and Chen Ping's rapid palace return after Liu Bang's death.
- Gift-mission conversion: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-767-congming-ren-dou-shanchang-yanxi-lq3ol7y-jdh7dnnfkmtfdg4ibryq|Hanji 767]] says Li He volunteers to carry the Hanzhong gift to Dou Xian but travels slowly while watching events.
- Avoided association: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-767-congming-ren-dou-shanchang-yanxi-lq3ol7y-jdh7dnnfkmtfdg4ibryq|Hanji 767]] says Dou's family falls before Li He completes the gift mission, sparing the Hanzhong administrator from removal as a Dou associate.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept should not be generalized into a blanket defense of delay. Both distinct evidence cases involve concrete administrative form and a real risk diagnosis. Hanji 150 part 4 and Hanji 193 part 2 are retellings within the same podcast series, not independent corroboration. Simple procrastination, refusal to decide, or delay that leaves others exposed without changing the risk structure does not meet the pattern.

## What Changed

- Added the Fan Kuai maneuver's operational division of labor, transition risk, and reported release outcome.
- Clarified that a successful delay may still require rapid repositioning under the successor authority.
