# Integrity as Inner Coherence

## Definition
Integrity as inner coherence is [[MarthaBeck]]'s frame for becoming less internally divided: beliefs, words, choices, bodily response, and lived direction increasingly point toward the same life rather than pulling against one another.

## Current Synthesis
The source distinguishes integrity from moral superiority. Beck uses the older sense of wholeness or being one thing, then treats suffering, persistent unease, and bodily constriction as signals that a person may be maintaining incompatible commitments or overriding an important truth. The signal opens an inquiry; it does not automatically dictate an action.

The framework uses paired tests. A belief or direction should create some sense of bodily freedom or relaxation while also remaining logically coherent. [[PerfectDayImagination]] can reveal desired conditions, compassionate attention can reduce defensive struggle, and “one-degree turns” can test a direction through small actions. This makes integrity a process of course correction rather than a demand for dramatic confession or immediate life rupture.

## Key Claims
- Integrity here means reduced inner division, not proof that one is morally better than others.
- Recurring suffering or unease can prompt examination of roles, relationships, or goals that feel internally false.
- Bodily freedom and logical coherence work as paired tests; neither makes judgment infallible.
- Small behavioral turns can test and strengthen alignment without requiring an all-at-once integrity cleanse.
- Social success can coexist with misalignment when achievement depends on repeatedly overriding important internal signals.
- Truth-telling includes timing, silence, consequences, and care rather than indiscriminate disclosure.

- Suffering as signal - [[access-your-best-self-with-mind-body-practices-belief-testing-imagination-dr-martha-beck-scim3796933031]] repeatedly treats anxiety, grinding unease, and self-division as prompts to investigate misalignment.
- Paired truth tests - [[access-your-best-self-with-mind-body-practices-belief-testing-imagination-dr-martha-beck-scim3796933031]] joins bodily relaxation or freedom to logical coherence rather than choosing feeling over thought.
- Gradual correction - [[access-your-best-self-with-mind-body-practices-belief-testing-imagination-dr-martha-beck-scim3796933031]] recommends one-degree turns after describing the severe disruption caused by Beck's own year of no lying.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Distress can arise from illness, trauma, anxiety disorders, unsafe environments, grief, material constraint, or other causes that cannot be reduced to failed integrity. Bodily relief can also reflect avoidance, short-term reward, or wishful thinking. The source offers a coaching and autobiographical framework, not a validated diagnostic test; major medical, psychiatric, financial, or relationship decisions need broader evidence and appropriate support.
