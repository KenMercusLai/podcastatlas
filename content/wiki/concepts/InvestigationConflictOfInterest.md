## Definition

Investigation conflict of interest is the risk that people or institutions connected to a disputed activity, regulator, funding stream, or prior policy become the gatekeepers of inquiry into whether that activity contributed to a failure.

## Current Synthesis

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bill-gurley-searching-for-feynman-42956628|Bill Gurley's presentation]] treats conflict as a structural reason to design independent investigations, not as automatic proof of guilt. The comparison cases use Boeing and the FAA, TEPCO and NISA, and COVID-era virology institutions to argue that expertise can coexist with incentives to narrow scrutiny.

## Key Claims

- Relevant expertise does not erase institutional or personal incentives.
- Implicated actors may contribute evidence but should not be the sole judges of scope, disclosure, or conclusion.
- Independence should be operationalized through membership rules, document authority, and transparent reporting.
- Labeling a question illegitimate can function as gatekeeping when the underlying evidence remains unresolved.
- A conflict justifies safeguards and scrutiny; it does not by itself prove misconduct, concealment, or a causal hypothesis.

## Evidence

- Regulatory comparison: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bill-gurley-searching-for-feynman-42956628|Bill Gurley's presentation]] describes the FAA-Boeing and NISA-TEPCO relationships as capture risks.
- Commission design: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bill-gurley-searching-for-feynman-42956628|Bill Gurley's presentation]] praises [[KiyoshiKurokawa]]'s commission for excluding members connected to the regulator or operator.
- COVID application: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bill-gurley-searching-for-feynman-42956628|Bill Gurley's presentation]] identifies virology funding, research advocacy, and public dismissal of a possible laboratory accident as potential conflicts requiring independent review.
- Epistemic boundary: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bill-gurley-searching-for-feynman-42956628|Bill Gurley's presentation]] stops short of saying that the conflict evidence establishes the origin of COVID-19.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

This concept is grounded in Gurley's interpretation and does not independently adjudicate the conduct of Anthony Fauci, Francis Collins, Peter Daszak, Jeremy Farrar, Ralph Baric, EcoHealth Alliance, NIH, journals, or universities. Domain expertise is also necessary for technically credible inquiry, so independence must be joined to competence rather than treated as its replacement.

## What Changed

- Created the conflict-design concept while making the conflict-versus-proof boundary explicit.
