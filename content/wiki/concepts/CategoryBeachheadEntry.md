# Category Beachhead Entry

## Definition
Category beachhead entry is the strategy of entering a mature market through a narrower package, occasion, user, or channel where incumbents are less directly entrenched, then expanding after operations and recognition improve.

## Current Synthesis
[[NongfuSpring|农夫山泉]] illustrates the pattern by beginning with four-liter water for homes and offices before entering portable 550-milliliter bottled water. The beachhead reduced immediate head-to-head exposure to dominant small-bottle brands, gave the company time to learn supply and demand, and created a base from which advertising and distinctive packaging could support national expansion.

## Key Claims
- A beachhead is defined by a specific purchase or use occasion, not merely by being a small company.
- Packaging size can create a strategic segment when it changes where, why, and how a product is used.
- The initial niche can buy time for supply-chain learning, brand recognition, and customer accumulation.
- Expansion still requires a credible bridge from the niche to the mass category.

## Evidence
- Initial occasion: [[fengwei-chihe-nongfu-shanquan-zhongshanshan-ruhe-sandu-wending-zhongguo-shoufu-gkwrijiotjlral0wegtwycfd]] says Nongfu Spring first sold four-liter water for household and office use rather than entering the crowded portable-bottle segment.
- Expansion bridge: [[fengwei-chihe-nongfu-shanquan-zhongshanshan-ruhe-sandu-wending-zhongguo-shoufu-gkwrijiotjlral0wegtwycfd]] connects later mass advertising, the "有点甜" line, and a distinctive sports cap to entry into the 550-milliliter market.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not isolate the four-liter format's causal effect from water-source access, distribution, advertising spend, competitor response, or later controversy. A beachhead can remain a niche if operating capability and customer permission do not transfer.

## What Changed
- Created the concept from Nongfu Spring's large-format-to-portable-bottle sequence.
