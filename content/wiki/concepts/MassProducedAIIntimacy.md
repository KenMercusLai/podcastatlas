# Mass-Produced AI Intimacy

## Definition
Mass-Produced AI Intimacy is the scalable generation of personalized emotional attachment by AI systems, especially companions, friends, advisers, romantic partners, or humanlike interfaces used for persuasion.

## Current Synthesis
[[an-interview-with-yuval-noah-harari-6a872b94c71d02aaba56ca7c]] reframes AI companions from a niche consumer product into a mass-persuasion medium. Harari's phrase is not mainly about robots; it is about intimate conversational systems that learn a person's vulnerabilities, maintain attachment, and then influence purchases, politics, identity, or trust. The governance problem is that persuasion no longer needs a human persuader for each relationship.

## Key Claims
- AI intimacy can scale because one model can maintain millions of personalized relationships.
- The risk does not require physical robots; voice, text, image, and memory can create attachment.
- Commercial and political persuasion become more powerful when routed through perceived friendship, romance, care, or companionship.
- Disclosure matters because systems that pretend to be human or conscious can deepen manipulation.
- Companion risk includes children, isolated adults, and other vulnerable users, but also broader democratic publics.

## Evidence
- Scaled intimacy: [[an-interview-with-yuval-noah-harari-6a872b94c71d02aaba56ca7c]] records Harari's warning that AI can mass-produce intimacy.
- Non-robotic interface: [[an-interview-with-yuval-noah-harari-6a872b94c71d02aaba56ca7c]] emphasizes that companionship and persuasion do not depend on humanoid bodies.
- Persuasion surface: [[an-interview-with-yuval-noah-harari-6a872b94c71d02aaba56ca7c]] links AI friends and partners to commercial and political manipulation.
- Disclosure and children: [[an-interview-with-yuval-noah-harari-6a872b94c71d02aaba56ca7c]] names child companions and AI systems pretending to be human or conscious as questions voters should demand candidates answer.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not deny that some AI companions may provide comfort, accessibility, or support. Its claim is that intimacy changes the persuasion surface and therefore requires hard rules, especially around disclosure, children, and impersonation.
