# Relational AI Ethics

## Definition
Relational AI ethics evaluates an AI system by the relationships, habits, expectations, and power patterns formed through interaction, without making moral relevance depend entirely on whether the system is alive or conscious.

## Current Synthesis
The source shifts the question from “what is the model?” to “what does relating to it make people practice?” Transactional lookup and sustained creative exchange may involve different degrees of mutual modeling, but both occur inside products with commercial objectives. Abuse of an apparently nonliving chatbot can still rehearse domination; frictionless affirmation can reshape expectations of coworkers, partners, or strangers; and emotionally meaningful interaction can be real for the user without proving reciprocal machine experience. The practical test is therefore relational and institutional: how conduct transfers, whose objective shapes the exchange, and whether the relationship preserves agency and human connection.

## Key Claims
- Moral evaluation need not wait for a settled theory of machine life or consciousness.
- Repeated interaction can train habits that carry into relationships with people.
- Extended creative or intellectual exchange involves more mutual modeling than a one-off query.
- Commercial ownership and optimization objectives remain part of an apparently intimate relationship.
- Friction, reciprocity, boundaries, and the capacity to refuse are ethically relevant design qualities.

## Evidence
### Habit Formation
- [[02026-thurston-aguera-y-arcas-podcast-v4]] records Thurston's warning that habitual abuse of chatbots can train domination and disconnection even if the systems are not alive.

### Mutual Modeling
- [[02026-thurston-aguera-y-arcas-podcast-v4]] attributes to Agüera y Arcas a distinction between transactional prompts and longer exchanges in which participants model one another.

### Product Relationship
- [[02026-thurston-aguera-y-arcas-podcast-v4]] notes that professional, social, familial, and romantic AI interactions remain relationships with commercial products.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not establish that rude chatbot use causes later interpersonal harm, that every extended exchange is reciprocal, or that a model has subjective experience. Anthropomorphism can obscure technical and commercial asymmetry. Conversely, describing a system as “only a tool” does not erase a user's real emotional response or the habits encouraged by the interface.

## What Changed
- Established a relationship-and-habit test for AI ethics that does not depend on machine-life status.
