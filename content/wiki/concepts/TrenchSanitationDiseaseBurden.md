# Trench Sanitation Disease Burden

## Definition

Trench Sanitation Disease Burden describes the way trench warfare harms soldiers through sanitation failure, disease, heat, bodies, insects, inadequate shelter, and exhaustion, not only through direct enemy fire.

## Current Synthesis

In [[676-the-first-world-war-churchills-calamity-part-6-glt1275431911]], Gallipoli's soldier experience is defined by primitive trenches and environmental breakdown. Dysentery, poor latrines, lack of toilet paper, unburied dead, summer heat, flies, dust, and weather make the campaign feel worse to some veterans than the Western Front.

The concept matters because it changes how battlefield cost is counted. Casualties are not only produced at the moment of attack; they also accumulate through daily bodily degradation in a failed operational environment.

## Key Claims

- Trench warfare creates health burdens through infrastructure failure as well as weapons.
- Gallipoli's terrain made trenches, dugouts, burial, water, and sanitation especially difficult.
- Disease and exhaustion can weaken armies before or between formal attacks.
- The presence of bodies and flies turned no man's land into a continuous health hazard.
- Soldier suffering at Gallipoli cannot be understood only through casualty lists from named battles.

## Evidence

- Daily conditions: [[676-the-first-world-war-churchills-calamity-part-6-glt1275431911]] says Gallipoli trenches were shallow and primitive, with dust, bodies, flies, heat, and poor dugouts.
- Disease load: [[676-the-first-world-war-churchills-calamity-part-6-glt1275431911]] makes dysentery central, citing poor toilet facilities and evacuation cases such as Clement Attlee's illness.
- Body-management crisis: [[676-the-first-world-war-churchills-calamity-part-6-glt1275431911]] describes the May 1915 truce to bury dead after bodies lay in heat between the lines.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source is a public narrative episode, not a medical or logistical study. Exact morbidity rates, sanitation-system details, and comparisons with other fronts remain source-scoped.
