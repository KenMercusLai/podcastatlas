# Security Compliance-Capability Gap

## Definition
The security compliance-capability gap is the distance between satisfying required procedures, reports, certifications, or audits and demonstrating that an organization can discover, resist, contain, repair, and learn from real attacks.

## Current Synthesis
Compliance can establish minimum duties, documentation, escalation paths, and accountability, but it is evidence about a control system only to the extent that controls operate under realistic conditions. Practical security also requires skilled people, maintained assets, current threat information, adversarial testing, incident authority, and repair capacity. Treating either formal governance or informal offensive expertise as sufficient creates a blind spot; the more durable model joins enforceable baselines with continuously tested operational capability.

## Key Claims
- Passing a formal requirement does not prove that systems withstand current attack methods.
- Operational capability includes discovery, prioritization, containment, patching, recovery, and learning under time pressure.
- Compliance remains useful when it creates minimum controls, responsibility, records, and consequences rather than becoming a substitute for outcomes.
- A healthy research and practitioner ecosystem supplies adversarial knowledge that paperwork cannot generate by itself.
- Security owners and system operators retain responsibility for exposure and maintenance even when regulators or auditors are present.

## Evidence
### Governance-versus-operations distinction
- [[24354fbed76f-24354fbed76f]] contrasts compliance- and state-centered “northbound security” with practitioner attack-and-defense “southbound security,” arguing that formal certification does not guarantee technical safety.

### Information and execution capacity
- [[24354fbed76f-24354fbed76f]] recommends maintained systems, vulnerability scanning, timely information channels, reduced exposure, and executable response plans rather than reliance on slower official notice alone.

### Researcher ecosystem
- [[24354fbed76f-24354fbed76f]] argues that punitive handling of security research can weaken the discovery and disclosure capacity on which practical remediation depends.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- The northbound/southbound terminology and claims about domestic governance trends are speaker framing, not a comparative institutional study.
- Offensive skill without authorization, accountability, or safe disclosure can create harm; practical capability is not a license to ignore law or governance.
- Audits and certifications differ substantially in technical depth, evidence quality, sampling, and enforcement, so the concept should not treat all compliance as empty ritual.

## What Changed
- Established a two-sided model in which enforceable governance and tested technical capability are complementary rather than interchangeable.
- Added information speed, researcher space, and incident execution as dimensions of security capacity.
