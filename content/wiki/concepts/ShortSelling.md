# Short Selling

## Definition
Short selling is the sale of borrowed or otherwise sourced securities in expectation of buying them back at a lower price. It can express forensic research or hedge other exposure, but its payoff is asymmetric: the maximum gain ends at zero while loss can keep growing as price rises.

## Current Synthesis
The evidence treats short selling as a useful but structurally unforgiving discipline. Strong shorts identify fraud, financing weakness, accounting problems, or business-model deterioration; valuation alone is not enough. The GameStop case adds crowding risk: when many funds share the same short and available shares are constrained, price increases, risk limits, and margin pressure can force simultaneous buying through a [[ShortSqueeze]].

## Key Claims
- Short research can expose accounting, financing, and business-model weaknesses that long-only analysis overlooks.
- The payoff is asymmetric because gains are capped while losses can be unbounded in principle.
- An expensive security is not automatically a good short; timing, borrow, momentum, catalyst, and crowding matter.
- A position that begins small can become portfolio-threatening as the stock rises.
- Crowded shorts can create forced demand and losses far larger than the position's intended contribution.

## Evidence
- **Research discipline:** [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-dan-loeb-the-lost-art-of-short-selling-and-why-stock-picking-is-back-41552605]] uses fraud hunting and a homebuilder thesis to show why a structural reason matters more than valuation alone.
- **Asymmetric and crowding risk:** [[ep159-gamestop-gupiao-touzi-yulehua-weishenme-jiazhi-touzi-bu-chixiangle-ckwrirwd-dwbabaaaachv6pg]] uses GameStop to show a small hedge expanding rapidly, triggering risk controls and forced repurchase.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Crowding and squeeze risk do not prove that the underlying business is healthy or that every short thesis is wrong. The GameStop source's claims about stock-loan verification and aggregate short exposure are a contemporaneous practitioner account rather than a complete securities-lending audit. Short positions can also hedge a portfolio rather than represent a standalone conviction.

## What Changed
- Added portfolio-path risk: a small short can grow into a dominant exposure as price rises.
- Added crowded-short feedback and forced covering through GameStop.
