# Self-Generated Emotional Capacity / 造血能力

Self-generated emotional capacity / 造血能力 is [[18-ruhe-shibie-taoli-bing-bimian-chengwei-jingshen-xixuegui-563629000]]'s repair frame for avoiding [[PsychicVampirePersonality|psychic-vampire]] tendencies. The episode argues that everyone needs emotional nourishment, but people become extractive when they cannot generate enough stability, interest, responsibility, or appreciation without pulling it from other people's reactions.

The practice is deliberately small: notice what is excellent or interesting in others, do good things without expecting repayment, take responsibility for one's own behavior and history, and reduce dependence on immediate feedback. This turns relationship energy from demand into contribution.

## Key Claims
- The goal is not emotional independence in the sense of needing nobody, but non-extractive reciprocity.
- Appreciation of others is treated as a skill that can be practiced before admiration becomes spontaneous.
- Doing something kind without waiting for return trains the person away from transaction and attention hunger.
- Responsibility means focusing on what one can control rather than treating every disappointment as another person's failure to feed the self.
