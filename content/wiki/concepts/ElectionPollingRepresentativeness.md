## Definition

Election polling representativeness is the degree to which a survey's effective sample reflects the electorate relevant to the estimate, including differences in who can be contacted, who responds, how responses are weighted, and who ultimately votes.

## Current Synthesis

Representativeness is not guaranteed by a large response count. Biased frames, interviewer selection, unequal nonresponse, contact-mode constraints, and incorrect adjustment variables can all create systematic error. Random selection reduces discretionary selection bias, while weighting can repair measured imbalances; neither solves differences that are unobserved or wrongly modeled.

## Key Claims

- Sample composition matters more to population inference than raw sample size alone.
- Random selection limits interviewer choice but cannot force selected people to answer.
- Nonresponse becomes bias when willingness or ability to participate correlates with political preference or turnout.
- Weighting can correct known imbalance only through variables that meaningfully distinguish respondents from missing voters.
- Poll accuracy therefore depends on contact operations, measurement design, adjustment, and turnout modeling as a linked system.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

- The episode supplies illustrative history and one field experiment, not a comparative audit of polling organizations or a formal error decomposition.
- Its response-rate, cost, and group-response claims are source-reported and may vary by survey design and election cycle.
- A close forecast can result from offsetting errors, so accuracy alone does not prove that a sample or model is unbiased.
- Text outreach can change coverage and response behavior; it should not be assumed equivalent to telephone interviewing without validation.

## What Changed

- Added a polling-specific synthesis that separates random selection, nonresponse, weighting, and turnout as distinct sources of representativeness error.
- Established that weighting repairs modeled imbalance but cannot automatically recover unobserved missing groups.
