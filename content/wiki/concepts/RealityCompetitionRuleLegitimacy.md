# Reality Competition Rule Legitimacy / 真人秀竞赛规则正当性

## Definition
Reality competition rule legitimacy is the audience trust produced when a program's consequential rules are understandable, disclosed in advance, sufficiently stable, and applied consistently enough that results appear meaningfully connected to performance.

## Current Synthesis
Competition rules do more than select winners: they create goals, conflict, suspense, and a sense that audiences can affect outcomes. The same architecture becomes a legitimacy liability when selection order, voting methods, or elimination conditions change without a clear prior framework. Complexity can be entertaining, but opacity expands perceived producer discretion and makes viewers less able to distinguish performance outcomes from editorial construction.

In the source's account of [[SistersWhoMakeWaves|《乘风破浪的姐姐》]], repeated rule changes, uneven editing, and movement away from live singing weakened the program's initial claim to challenge ordinary talent-show conventions. This is a perceived-fairness judgment; it is not proof that producers actually manipulated a result.

## Key Claims
- Rules generate narrative stakes and audience participation as well as formal outcomes.
- Undisclosed or changing rules increase perceived producer discretion.
- Excessive complexity makes consistent application harder for audiences to evaluate.
- Editing and production standards affect legitimacy when they unevenly mediate comparable performances or eliminations.
- Suspicion created by opacity is not itself evidence of outcome manipulation.

## Evidence
- **Changing mechanics:** [[ep136-er-liao-lang-jie-zhichang-yiran-you-hen-duo-zhi-de-jiyi-de-shanguangdian-wkg5hf9t5qetspydbxyk6to0ea4442]] says later rounds altered song-selection order, voting methods, and elimination rules rather than disclosing a complete framework at the start.
- **Complexity and trust:** [[ep136-er-liao-lang-jie-zhichang-yiran-you-hen-duo-zhi-de-jiyi-de-shanguangdian-wkg5hf9t5qetspydbxyk6to0ea4442]] records the hosts describing the rules as exceptionally complex and inferring that dynamic adjustment expanded room for producer control.
- **Uneven mediation:** [[ep136-er-liao-lang-jie-zhichang-yiran-you-hen-duo-zhi-de-jiyi-de-shanguangdian-wkg5hf9t5qetspydbxyk6to0ea4442]] points to unequal farewell editing and a shift from fully live vocals toward recording and post-production as departures from the opening promise.
- **Participation value:** [[ep136-er-liao-lang-jie-zhichang-yiran-you-hen-duo-zhi-de-jiyi-de-shanguangdian-wkg5hf9t5qetspydbxyk6to0ea4442]] also acknowledges that elimination creates clear goals, emotional intensity, and a feeling that viewers can decide who remains.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not include production documents, complete rulebooks, vote audits, raw footage, or testimony from organizers. Some variation may be necessary for different performance formats. Claims of manipulation and voting failure are relayed allegations, so the supported judgment concerns transparency and perceived legitimacy rather than proven fraud.

## What Changed
- Created a concept separating perceived discretion from demonstrated manipulation.
- Integrated rule disclosure, editing, and production standards into one audience-trust account.
- Preserved the entertainment and participation benefits of elimination mechanics.
