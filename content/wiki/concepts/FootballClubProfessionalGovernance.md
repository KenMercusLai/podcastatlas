## Definition
Football club professional governance is the institutional capacity to turn football expertise into coherent recruitment, staffing, communication, performance review, succession, and development decisions while keeping authority and accountability legible.

## Current Synthesis
The [[ChengduRongcheng|成都蓉城]] case shows that receiving expert advice is not the same as possessing professional governance. A club must decide who owns sporting judgment, how recommendations become action, how starters and substitutes are treated, how coach contracts and replacements are planned, and how first-team ambition connects to the academy and reserve side.

Procedure and accountability are not enemies of expertise, but timing matters. Formal compliance without football succession capacity can make midseason correction difficult; sudden post-failure dismissals can demonstrate accountability without proving that the underlying decision system has improved.

## Key Claims
- Professional advice creates value only when authority, evidence, and implementation responsibility are clear.
- Recruitment should cover tactical functions and risk, not merely accumulate recognizable players.
- Internal communication and equal institutional respect affect whether starters, substitutes, coaches, and administrators remain aligned.
- Coach evaluation needs pre-agreed objectives, review points, and credible succession options before crisis arrives.
- First-team results, reserve competition, academy relationships, and age renewal belong to one sporting strategy.
- Leadership resignation or dismissal can assign responsibility without repairing the process that produced failure.

## Evidence
- Decision translation: [[vol-231-chengdu-diuguan-zhushude-yazi-zenme-feile-ft-qinyun-gkwrijioygv7adhjrwt48vcx]] says outside football advice was sought but not consistently converted into recruitment and roster choices.
- Communication and cohesion: [[vol-231-chengdu-diuguan-zhushude-yazi-zenme-feile-ft-qinyun-gkwrijioygv7adhjrwt48vcx]] treats a selective meal and prior coach-management conflict as signs of relationship and respect problems, not proven standalone causes.
- Succession and accountability: [[vol-231-chengdu-diuguan-zhushude-yazi-zenme-feile-ft-qinyun-gkwrijioygv7adhjrwt48vcx]] links midseason inertia to contract, procedure, and successor scarcity, then reports immediate post-season departures.
- Integrated sporting strategy: [[vol-231-chengdu-diuguan-zhushude-yazi-zenme-feile-ft-qinyun-gkwrijioygv7adhjrwt48vcx]] connects first-team ageing, B-team relegation, recruitment imbalance, and weaker academy linkage.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode supplies observer testimony rather than board minutes, organizational charts, contracts, recruitment files, or interviews with all parties. State-owned-enterprise procedure may prevent arbitrary decisions as well as slow change. The timing of a meal, defeat, dismissal, or resignation does not by itself establish causation or governance quality.

## What Changed
- Established professional club governance as the translation layer between expertise and repeatable sporting decisions.
- Separated visible post-failure accountability from institutional repair.
