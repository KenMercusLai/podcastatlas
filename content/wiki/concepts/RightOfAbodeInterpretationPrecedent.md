# Right-of-Abode Interpretation Precedent / 居港權釋法先例

## Definition
Right-of-abode interpretation precedent is the source's frame for [[TungCheeHwa|董建華]]'s 1999 request for a mainland interpretive decision over Hong Kong right-of-abode issues.

## Current Synthesis
The episode treats the right-of-abode interpretation as a constitutional turning point. It argues that Tung's request created a precedent in which Hong Kong legal questions could be decided by a mainland political institution behind closed doors, reshaping the relationship between local law and Beijing authority.

## Key Claims
- The 1999 right-of-abode case is presented as a legal and institutional precedent, not only an immigration dispute.
- The precedent shifted Hong Kong legal settlement toward mainland political interpretation.
- The episode treats this as one starting point for later Hong Kong-mainland institutional tension.

## Evidence
- Precedent claim: [[dong-jianhua-shishi-aiguozhe-yuanlao-de-xianggang-rensheng-7d743a8515cf65334add1a55cd20148e]] says Tung actively sought the interpretation over right of abode.
- Legal-political meaning: [[dong-jianhua-shishi-aiguozhe-yuanlao-de-xianggang-rensheng-7d743a8515cf65334add1a55cd20148e]] argues this opened the precedent that Hong Kong law could be decided by a mainland political organ.
- Integration legacy: [[dong-jianhua-shishi-aiguozhe-yuanlao-de-xianggang-rensheng-7d743a8515cf65334add1a55cd20148e]] connects the precedent to the wider reorder of Hong Kong-mainland relations.
