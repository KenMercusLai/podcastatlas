# Political Authority Collapse Cascade

## Definition

A political authority collapse cascade occurs when a visible policy failure forces reversals and personnel sacrifices that reveal rather than repair a leader's loss of control, encouraging further ministerial, parliamentary, market, and public defection.

## Current Synthesis

The Truss case in [[246-the-fall-of-liz-truss-glt3484252092]] shows authority failing as a sequence rather than at one instant. The mini-budget damaged credibility; [[KwasiKwarteng]] was dismissed; Jeremy Hunt reversed the programme; cabinet and whipping discipline broke down; and [[LizTruss]] could no longer plausibly present herself as directing government.

The concept distinguishes correction from authority recovery. Reversing a failed policy may be substantively necessary, but if another actor visibly takes control and the leader discards the ally most identified with the original programme, correction can confirm that the leader's project and judgment have collapsed.

## Key Claims

- Authority depends on credible control of policy, personnel, and parliamentary coordination, not officeholding alone.
- A forced reversal can repair policy while further weakening the leader who authorized it.
- Sacrificing a close ally may expose shared responsibility instead of isolating blame.
- Once elites expect imminent failure, resignation, defection, and discipline breakdown can reinforce one another.
- Material consequences arise when political credibility changes financing expectations for households, firms, or the state.

## Evidence

- Policy shock: [[246-the-fall-of-liz-truss-glt3484252092]] treats the mini-budget as the initiating loss of economic and political credibility.
- Failed sacrifice: [[246-the-fall-of-liz-truss-glt3484252092]] compares Kwarteng's removal with historical favourites whose dismissal shredded a ruler's remaining authority.
- Control transfer: [[246-the-fall-of-liz-truss-glt3484252092]] presents Hunt's reversal of the programme as public evidence that Truss no longer directed policy.
- Discipline failure: [[246-the-fall-of-liz-truss-glt3484252092]] connects ministerial departure and chaotic whipping to the final collapse.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept is built from one fast-moving British case and historical analogies, not a tested general model. Policy reversal or ministerial dismissal can restore authority under other conditions. Claims about market reaction, interest-rate counterfactuals, and the precise ordering of political causes require evidence beyond this episode.
