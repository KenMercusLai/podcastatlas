# Incense Formula Reconstruction / 古方香复刻

Incense formula reconstruction / 古方香复刻 is the practice of turning old incense recipes, names, and textual fragments into contemporary smell. [[ep263-xianxiang-jueqi-faxian-zhongshi-xiangqi-de-meili-lq1lsqcprqyr7auo5gslwyymuiv]] grounds the concept through [[WuQingIncense|吴清]] and "鹅梨帐中香": an old formula may say one thing, but the maker still has to test whether pear flesh, pear juice, shell materials, powders, and heat behavior actually work.

The source's important caution is that ancient fragrance was often imagistic. Recipes for "梅花" may point to different scenes, temperatures, and poetic moods rather than a precise modern smell target. Reconstruction therefore mixes philology, craft trial, sensory judgment, and interpretation.

## Key Claims
- Old incense formulas are not automatically executable recipes; ingredients, quantities, and procedures may be incomplete, ambiguous, or technically implausible.
- Reconstruction should test the craft logic, not only copy characters from a text.
- A formula name can guide imagination as much as composition: "帐中香" or "梅花" carries scene, intimacy, season, and literary association.
- Pop culture can revive interest in old formulas, but it can also create overconfident assumptions about effects, danger, or historical exactness.
- Reconstructed formulas help define [[ChineseFragrance|中式香气]] by making old scent imagination smellable in contemporary products.
