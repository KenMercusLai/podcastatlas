# Founder Vision-Timing Mismatch

Founder vision-timing mismatch is the gap between seeing a future direction correctly and being able to commercialize it in the current technical, regulatory, organizational, and user-behavior environment. [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]] adds the concept through [[ChenTianqiao]]'s post-[[LegendOfMir|Legend]] bets at [[Shanda|盛大]].

The episode's main example is [[ShandaBox|盛大盒子]]. A living-room internet-entertainment hub anticipated later smart-TV and streaming-platform behavior, but the product arrived before broadband, content licensing, pricing, hardware design, and broadcast regulation lined up. [[ShandaInnovationInstitute|盛大创新院]] adds a second version: high-talent experimentation can still fail to compound if the parent platform and timing are weak.
