# Equity Incentive Alignment / 股权激励相容

Equity incentive alignment is [[79-gewei-lingdao-danfan-zan-xuedian-boyilun-690004262]]'s management frame for giving stock or ownership claims to employees whose effort is hard to measure and whose long-term risk can be tied to company outcomes. The source discusses startup "future equity" promises and later uses [[Alibaba]]'s stock-incentive pattern as an example.

The source is cautious. Equity can reduce shirking by binding employee upside to company value, but it is not a universal substitute for salary. It fits core people who want to remain, can contribute to hard-to-measure outcomes, and are willing to bear company risk.

## Key Claims
- Equity works best when the recipient is a core contributor rather than a short-term worker.
- Employees may misread equity as compensation for underpayment if the risk and value are not clear.
- Equity is weaker in businesses where outcomes are mostly luck rather than controllable effort.
- Alignment requires more than issuing shares; the employee must understand the tradeoff between cash, risk, time, and upside.
