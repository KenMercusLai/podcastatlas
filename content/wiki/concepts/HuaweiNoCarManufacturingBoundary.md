# Huawei No-Car Manufacturing Boundary / 华为不造车边界

Huawei No-Car Manufacturing Boundary / 华为不造车边界 is the distinction in [[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] between formal whole-vehicle manufacturing and deep control over intelligent-vehicle product systems. The source says [[RenZhengfei]] repeatedly signed internal documents emphasizing that [[Huawei]] would not make cars and that whole-vehicle promotion should not use Huawei branding as if Huawei were the automaker.

The boundary is not a claim of weak involvement. In the source, Huawei still shapes product definition, intelligent cockpit, assisted driving, electric drive, launch events, retail channels, service standards, and user experience inside [[HongmengZhixing|鸿蒙智行]]. The formal automaker partner keeps qualification, manufacturing, vehicle ownership, and revenue主体, while Huawei earns through parts, systems, services, and broader supplier work through [[ShenzhenYinwang|引望]].

## Key Claims
- "Not making cars" means Huawei avoids direct whole-vehicle manufacturing qualification and formal automaker identity.
- It does not mean Huawei is a passive parts vendor in the deepest cooperation mode.
- Trademark transfer to partners and Yinwang's supplier-platform design are governance mechanisms that make the boundary more credible.
- The boundary creates a hybrid accountability problem: customers see Huawei's influence, but manufacturing, delivery, and quality obligations still sit heavily with partner automakers.
