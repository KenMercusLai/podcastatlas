# AI Authorship Presence

## Definition
AI authorship presence is the trust and responsibility question of whether a human author's attention, point of view, process, voice, and judgment remain meaningfully present when AI helps produce or edit content.

## Current Synthesis
Authorship presence is softer than formal provenance but harder than surface quality. Readers may reject AI-heavy writing because they expected to encounter a person's thinking process, not only a useful set of propositions. Product and platform cases make the same boundary commercial: people respond differently to human-led work with AI assistance than to AI-led work with human cleanup, especially when they expect emotion, identity, judgment, or a paid human point of view.

Creator-side process supplies an additional test. The question is not only whether audiences can detect AI or whether the final passage is good enough. A creator may decide that AI-generated arguments or paragraphs are not "theirs" if the model handled the most important part of the process. Another creator may accept signature when they challenge, rewrite, reframe, and remain responsible for the result. Authorship presence therefore depends on both audience expectation and creator labor, including voice, verification, and final accountability.

## Key Claims
- Readers and listeners may value the author's thinking process, not only the propositions delivered by the work.
- AI-heavy writing can break expectations when audiences assumed the words, voice, or point of view came from a person.
- Trust can be strengthened when audiences see the author's real frame, sources, process statement, voice, or judgment.
- Authorship order matters: human-led work with AI assistance is different from AI-led work with human editing.
- Formal provenance, detector output, or watermarking can show AI involvement but cannot by itself settle meaningful authorship presence.
- Creator-side authorship can turn on process: prompt labor, critique, rewriting, and responsibility may or may not be enough for the creator to sign the work.
- Authorship presence is commercially and ethically sharper in domains tied to identity, emotion, expertise, subscription trust, or voice.

## Evidence
- Reader-process evidence: [[dushu-jiushi-zai-du-yige-ren-de-f-li4qt9zs2bss4tklnj3yg9y-quo1]] says reading a book means reading an author's frame, and records discomfort when a reader senses AI mediation instead of the author's formed attention.
- Commercial authorship evidence: [[tech-20260413-0413-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260413-0413-mp-tech-pod-128]] says consumers respond worse when AI appears to create a communication and humans only edit it, but the penalty can disappear when humans develop the work and AI assists.
- Subscription expectation evidence: [[tech-20260810-0810-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260810-0810-mp-tech-pod-128]] has [[Substack]] and [[ChrisBest]] frame reader trust around receiving a human point of view rather than substantially machine-generated prose.
- Provenance ambiguity evidence: [[tech-20260814-tech-pod-128-tech-20260814-tech-pod-128]] says [[Claude]] text watermarking can mark human writing that was edited by AI, leaving authorship presence unresolved even when AI involvement is detectable.
- Creator-process evidence: [[zong-di-070-qi-wu-zhounian-taiqing-teji-da-zhubo-vs-xiao-boke-xia-ai-daodi-you-sha-haoyong-de-cmswlihjk12g601zlfjov42ht]] has [[DuBaoTeleread|读报teleread / 独报]] discuss whether an AI-generated passage or argument can be signed as one's own, distinguishing process-centered and final-control-centered answers.
- Voice and verification evidence: [[zong-di-070-qi-wu-zhounian-taiqing-teji-da-zhubo-vs-xiao-boke-xia-ai-daodi-you-sha-haoyong-de-cmswlihjk12g601zlfjov42ht]] ties authorship to voice-based scriptwriting and to the responsibility of checking facts before publishing.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The sources do not make AI use automatically inauthentic. Substack's framing, Mark-style creative assistance in adjacent pages, and the 读报teleread host's "AI as reporter to challenge" workflow all leave room for AI-assisted authorship when the human point of view, review, rewriting, and accountability remain dominant. The boundary is also audience- and genre-dependent: low-stakes utility text, entertainment filler, and personal voice writing do not create the same expectation.
