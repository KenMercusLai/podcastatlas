# Digital Public Sphere Epistemic Authority / 数字公共领域知识权威

## Definition
Digital public sphere epistemic authority is the episode's framework for deciding when a person or institution has a justified knowledge advantage in a specific domain, task, and audience relationship.

## Current Synthesis
The episode separates epistemic authority from mere KOL status. A KOL can be one public form of authority, but the authority relation requires three elements to line up: someone socially claims authority, an audience subjectively recognizes it, and the claim is objectively justified by actual knowledge advantage and reliable guidance.

The digital public sphere makes those elements easier to decouple. Some actors become false authorities by performing expertise, gaining institutional or platform visibility, and attracting audience recognition without adequate knowledge advantage. Others may be unrecognized authorities because they have stronger competence but weaker presentation, branding, or distribution.

## Key Claims
- Epistemic authority is a relation around a task, not a fixed identity that transfers unchanged across domains.
- Healthy authority requires social claim, subjective recognition, and objective justification to overlap.
- Digital platforms can reward presentation, branding, and recognition while weakening the link to knowledge advantage.
- False authority and unrecognized authority are paired failure modes.
- The aim is not to abolish authority but to make trust more precise, revisable, and task-specific.

## Evidence
- Three elements: [[mianfei-de-quanwei-fufei-de-tiyan-cmtwkgbmq01wt01tp3mgj78e4]] summarizes the paper's elements as socially claimed, subjectively recognized, and objectively justified.
- False authority: [[mianfei-de-quanwei-fufei-de-tiyan-cmtwkgbmq01wt01tp3mgj78e4]] says some people are good at display and recognition while having weak knowledge advantage.
- Unrecognized authority: [[mianfei-de-quanwei-fufei-de-tiyan-cmtwkgbmq01wt01tp3mgj78e4]] notes that people with more actual knowledge may be missed because they lack platform visibility or self-presentation skill.
- Anti-abolition stance: [[mianfei-de-quanwei-fufei-de-tiyan-cmtwkgbmq01wt01tp3mgj78e4]] frames epistemic authority as a necessary fast track to knowledge in modern society.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is an episode summary of an academic paper rather than a full reading of the paper's methodology. It does not provide a universal test for authority, and its examples are task-dependent: movie-format advice, medical knowledge, law, climate, news, philosophy, culture, and emotional advice require different standards.
