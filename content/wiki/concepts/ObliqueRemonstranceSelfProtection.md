## Definition

Oblique remonstrance self-protection / 迂回进谏自保 is the strategy of preserving corrective speech by changing the route, angle of criticism, named target, or risk surface when direct accusation would likely trigger suppression before correction can work.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-890-xiang-dangguan-xian-xue-duifu-xiaoren-ltnvh6qu-mr8flsdlxf-e6rknpis|Hanji 890]] defines the factional-court version through [[YangCiLateHan|杨赐]]'s snake-omen answer to [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]. Yang Ci does not openly demand justice for [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] and [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]], and he does not directly name [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] or the eunuch faction. Instead, he interprets the omen as a sign that royal authority, inner-court boundaries, harem favoritism, and outer-relative power need correction.

The contrast is [[XieBiLateHan|谢弼]]. Xie Bi's memorial is morally and institutionally sharper, but it also exposes him to retaliation that ends in prison death. The concept therefore sits between [[AllusiveRemonstrance|隐语进谏]] and [[PoliticalSurvivalFirst|安全第一政治生存]]: it is not hiding for comfort, but keeping enough position and life to continue acting.

The late-Xin hierarchy-route version appears through [[FanShengXinOfficial|范生]] in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-656-zhichang-buhui-shuohua-de-ren-hen-nan-hunchutou-lpn15smz7r1glekugzwtm2tpw-tl|Hanji 656]]. Fan Sheng criticizes [[WangMang|王莽]]'s priorities, mistimed labor mobilization, hunger, and domestic resentment, but sends the petition through [[WangYiXinGeneral|王邑 / 王毅]] rather than directly to the ruler. The route protects him better than it protects the message: Wang Yi ignores the petition, so the warning survives personally but fails institutionally.

## Key Claims

- The strategy keeps corrective intent alive while reducing direct collision with the ruler, superior, or faction most able to retaliate.
- It works by reframing a sensitive political accusation as a ruler-centered responsibility problem, often through omen, ritual, or role language.
- It can also work by routing a warning through an intermediate superior, though this creates a new failure point if the intermediary ignores it.
- It is ethically ambiguous because it may spare the speaker while leaving victims or structural abuse unnamed.
- It depends on accurate reading of which target can be criticized without immediately triggering lethal counterattack.
- Self-protection and policy effectiveness can diverge: an indirect warning may keep the speaker safe while doing nothing for the crisis.

## Evidence

- Reduced collision: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-890-xiang-dangguan-xian-xue-duifu-xiaoren-ltnvh6qu-mr8flsdlxf-e6rknpis|Hanji 890]] says Yang Ci avoids directly defending Dou Wu and Chen Fan or criticizing the eunuch faction, unlike Xie Bi's broader indictment.
- Ruler-centered framing: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-890-xiang-dangguan-xian-xue-duifu-xiaoren-ltnvh6qu-mr8flsdlxf-e6rknpis|Hanji 890]] has Yang Ci explain the snake omen through royal authority, yin-yang imbalance, inner/outer separation, harem affection, and restraint of queen-family power.
- Ethical ambiguity: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-890-xiang-dangguan-xian-xue-duifu-xiaoren-ltnvh6qu-mr8flsdlxf-e6rknpis|Hanji 890]] explicitly contrasts Yang Ci's survival with Xie Bi's death, making survival useful but not identical with full justice.
- Hierarchy-route protection: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-656-zhichang-buhui-shuohua-de-ren-hen-nan-hunchutou-lpn15smz7r1glekugzwtm2tpw-tl|Hanji 656]] says Fan Sheng sends his domestic-crisis warning through Wang Yi instead of directly angering Wang Mang.
- Effectiveness limit: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-656-zhichang-buhui-shuohua-de-ren-hen-nan-hunchutou-lpn15smz7r1glekugzwtm2tpw-tl|Hanji 656]] says Wang Yi ignores Fan Sheng's petition, making the route safer but politically ineffective.
- Risk reading: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-890-xiang-dangguan-xian-xue-duifu-xiaoren-ltnvh6qu-mr8flsdlxf-e6rknpis|Hanji 890]] concludes that struggle is long and that staying alive and on the field can matter more than one frontal statement, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-656-zhichang-buhui-shuohua-de-ren-hen-nan-hunchutou-lpn15smz7r1glekugzwtm2tpw-tl|Hanji 656]] gives the narrower case of avoiding direct ruler offense.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

- Obliqueness can become mere evasion if the adviser never returns to the real abuse or only protects personal position.
- The strategy may fail if the ruler sees the indirection as manipulation or if hostile actors can still decode and punish it.
- The intermediary route may neutralize the warning before it reaches the decision-maker.
- Hanji 656 and Hanji 890 support the concept across two cases, but both are court-politics examples; broader organizational generalization should stay cautious.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 656's Fan Sheng case as a hierarchy-route version where indirection protects the speaker but not the message.
- Preserved Hanji 890's Yang Ci/Xie Bi contrast as the factional-court version.
- Sharpened the qualification that self-protection and political effectiveness can diverge.
