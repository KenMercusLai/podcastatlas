## Definition

Oblique remonstrance self-protection / 迂回进谏自保 is the strategy of preserving corrective speech by changing the angle of criticism, the named target, and the risk surface when direct accusation would only empower hostile intermediaries.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-890-xiang-dangguan-xian-xue-duifu-xiaoren-ltnvh6qu-mr8flsdlxf-e6rknpis|Hanji 890]] defines the pattern through [[YangCiLateHan|杨赐]]'s snake-omen answer to [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]. Yang Ci does not openly demand justice for [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] and [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]], and he does not directly name [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] or the eunuch faction. Instead, he interprets the omen as a sign that royal authority, inner-court boundaries, harem favoritism, and outer-relative power need correction.

The contrast is [[XieBiLateHan|谢弼]]. Xie Bi's memorial is morally and institutionally sharper, but it also exposes him to retaliation that ends in prison death. The concept therefore sits between [[AllusiveRemonstrance|隐语进谏]] and [[PoliticalSurvivalFirst|安全第一政治生存]]: it is not hiding for comfort, but keeping enough position and life to continue acting.

## Key Claims

- The strategy keeps corrective intent alive while reducing direct collision with the faction most able to retaliate.
- It works by reframing a sensitive political accusation as a ruler-centered responsibility problem, often through omen, ritual, or role language.
- It is ethically ambiguous because it may spare the speaker while leaving victims or structural abuse unnamed.
- It depends on accurate reading of which target can be criticized without immediately triggering lethal counterattack.

## Evidence

- Reduced collision: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-890-xiang-dangguan-xian-xue-duifu-xiaoren-ltnvh6qu-mr8flsdlxf-e6rknpis|Hanji 890]] says Yang Ci avoids directly defending Dou Wu and Chen Fan or criticizing the eunuch faction, unlike Xie Bi's broader indictment.
- Ruler-centered framing: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-890-xiang-dangguan-xian-xue-duifu-xiaoren-ltnvh6qu-mr8flsdlxf-e6rknpis|Hanji 890]] has Yang Ci explain the snake omen through royal authority, yin-yang imbalance, inner/outer separation, harem affection, and restraint of queen-family power.
- Ethical ambiguity: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-890-xiang-dangguan-xian-xue-duifu-xiaoren-ltnvh6qu-mr8flsdlxf-e6rknpis|Hanji 890]] explicitly contrasts Yang Ci's survival with Xie Bi's death, making survival useful but not identical with full justice.
- Risk reading: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-890-xiang-dangguan-xian-xue-duifu-xiaoren-ltnvh6qu-mr8flsdlxf-e6rknpis|Hanji 890]] concludes that struggle is long and that staying alive and on the field can matter more than one frontal statement.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

- Obliqueness can become mere evasion if the adviser never returns to the real abuse or only protects personal position.
- The strategy may fail if the ruler sees the indirection as manipulation or if hostile actors can still decode and punish it.
- Hanji 890 gives one source case; broader validation should wait for more examples before treating it as a general rule of court politics.

## What Changed

- Created the concept from Hanji 890's Yang Ci/Xie Bi contrast.
- Distinguished survivable indirect remonstrance from direct death-facing remonstrance and from coded allusive speech.
- Added the ethical qualification that self-protection preserves agency but can leave the central injustice unnamed.
