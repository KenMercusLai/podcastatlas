## Definition

受胁群体退出设计 / coercive coalition exit design is the deliberate weakening of an armed group by distinguishing committed leaders from coerced followers and making individual departure safer, materially worthwhile, socially credible, and administratively absorbable.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1128-meiyou-heikeji-guren-ruhe-diaocha-nimingxin-lqdm57jzw9wqo_umlasz2j3vlzqh|Hanji 1128]] grounds the concept in [[ZhengHunLateHan|郑浑]]'s response to [[LiangXingLateHan|梁兴]]. Zheng Hun refuses to withdraw because official retreat could make coerced followers believe Liang Xing is the only durable authority. He repairs defenses so the state remains visibly present, but does not rely on force alone.

The exit path has several linked parts. Civilians retain most recovered goods, making resistance materially sustainable. Men seeking abducted wives must release women seized from other groups, turning family recovery into reciprocal de-escalation. Respected intermediaries communicate surrender terms, and counties prepare to receive defectors. The coalition weakens before [[XiahouYuan|夏侯渊]] assists final military suppression.

## Key Claims

- A coercive coalition may be less cohesive than its visible armed strength suggests.
- State withdrawal can unintentionally convert temporary coercion into durable alignment.
- Defense and exit incentives reinforce each other: security makes promises credible, while defections reduce the force defense must face.
- Family recovery and property rewards can redirect private incentives toward public pacification.
- Persuasion requires a believable reception system; surrender messages fail if defectors expect punishment or abandonment.
- Political erosion can reduce an enemy before force resolves the remaining hard core.

## Evidence

- Cohesion diagnosis: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1128-meiyou-heikeji-guren-ruhe-diaocha-nimingxin-lqdm57jzw9wqo_umlasz2j3vlzqh|Hanji 1128]] says Zheng Hun sees many Liang Xing followers as coerced and rejects withdrawal.
- Material incentives and exchange: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1128-meiyou-heikeji-guren-ruhe-diaocha-nimingxin-lqdm57jzw9wqo_umlasz2j3vlzqh|Hanji 1128]] describes retained spoils, recovered women, and conditional reciprocal release.
- Credible reception: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1128-meiyou-heikeji-guren-ruhe-diaocha-nimingxin-lqdm57jzw9wqo_umlasz2j3vlzqh|Hanji 1128]] says reputable officials and civilians encourage surrender while counties arrange settlement.
- Force after fragmentation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1128-meiyou-heikeji-guren-ruhe-diaocha-nimingxin-lqdm57jzw9wqo_umlasz2j3vlzqh|Hanji 1128]] says Liang Xing retreats with remnants before Xiahou Yuan assists the final suppression.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source tells the policy from the victorious administration's perspective and does not preserve follower testimony, captive women's agency, collateral harm, legal standards, recidivism, or long-term reintegration results. Incentive design can also become coercive or abusive. The concept should not imply that every armed group is mostly unwilling, that surrender is safe merely because promised, or that military force became unnecessary.
