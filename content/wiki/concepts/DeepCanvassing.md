# Deep Canvassing / 深度说服

## Definition
Deep canvassing is a one-to-one persuasion method based on permission, rapport, open questions, reflective listening, personal story, confidence exploration, and reciprocal self-examination rather than direct fact bombardment.

## Current Synthesis
The two sources converge on lowering identity threat so that a person can examine their own reasons. The Chinese-language book discussion describes a sequence of rapport, consent, view elicitation, reflection, definition checking, confidence scoring, and questions about why confidence is not higher or lower. Listening is the mechanism, not conversational decoration, and a “step 0” ethics check asks whether persuasion is warranted and what relationship or value is at stake.

The Hidden Brain episode adds vulnerability through activist Dave Fleischer's intimate story about being gay and having had a girlfriend in high school while discussing abortion. A personal account invites reciprocal openness without guaranteeing agreement. It also adds an essential research-integrity boundary: an early study was retracted for fabricated data; later rigorous studies reportedly found similar opinion change, but the supplied summary does not provide their methods or effect sizes. Deep canvassing is presented as slow, awkward, difficult to teach, and potentially useful precisely because persuasion across identity and disagreement is difficult.

## Key Claims
- Permission and rapport reduce threat before substantive disagreement is explored.
- Questions, accurate reflection, and confidence scoring help people inspect their own reasons rather than merely defend a prompted position.
- Personal vulnerability can invite reciprocal openness and make values emotionally legible.
- Listening is a causal part of the proposed method, not a polite prelude to an evidence dump.
- An ethics check should precede persuasion by testing purpose, relationship, value, safety, and whether the conversation should occur.
- The method is labor-intensive and may change only a minority of views, but small shifts can matter in close relationships or electoral margins.
- Evidence for effectiveness must exclude the fabricated early study and rest on independently conducted, transparent research.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The fabricated early study supplies no support for effectiveness. The later findings are summarized without study identities, samples, measures, effect sizes, durability, topics, implementation fidelity, or replication detail, so broad efficacy claims remain source-scoped. Listening and vulnerability can be manipulative when used deceptively, and openness should not be demanded in unsafe, abusive, coercive, or bad-faith encounters. A small average effect does not establish that every person is persuadable, that facts are irrelevant, or that the canvasser's goal is correct. The method's time and training demands limit scale.

- Added the explicit fabricated-study exclusion and bounded later-evidence qualification.
