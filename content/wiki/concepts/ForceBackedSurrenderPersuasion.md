# Force-Backed Surrender Persuasion / 武力背书式劝降

## Definition
Force-backed surrender persuasion is a negotiation pattern in which the persuader gives the target face, reasons, or trust while making clear that military imbalance, resource limits, and continued pressure leave surrender as the least damaging choice.

## Current Synthesis
Hanji 1039 defines the pattern through Yu Fan's mission to Hua Xin. Yu Fan does not simply threaten [[HuaXin|华歆]]; he praises Hua Xin and Wang Lang, compares [[YuzhangCommandery|豫章郡]] against [[KuaijiCommandery|会稽郡]], invokes [[SunCe|孙策]]'s victories, and gives a specific deadline for sending the welcoming document. Persuasion works because it combines respect, asymmetry, precedent, and impending force.

Hanji 1054 adds a siege-and-guarantee variant through [[ZhangLiao|张辽]] and [[ChangXiLateHan|昌豨]]. Zhang Liao reads wavering inside a months-long siege, uses old acquaintance to open contact, stresses [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s strength and Chang Xi's survival interest, then gives a personal guarantee and visits Chang Xi's family alone. This variant has less formal deadline pressure than Yu Fan's speech, but stronger trust repair inside an armed standoff.

## Key Claims
- Face-saving praise or old acquaintance can lower resistance before the hard comparison arrives.
- Material comparison matters: food, weapons, people, terrain, and siege endurance define whether resistance is credible.
- Precedent, reputation, or promised office can make surrender imaginable by showing that submission need not mean humiliation or death.
- Immediate decision pressure can come from a formal deadline, a shrinking resource base, or the risk that a siege will resume.
- Personal guarantees can turn abstract promises from a ruler into locally credible trust signals.
- The method remains coercive; its moral and political evaluation depends on whether it prevents worse harm and whether the settlement holds after surrender.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept should not romanticize coercion as dialogue. Both source cases work because an army already stands behind the persuader. Hanji 1039's formal deadline differs from Hanji 1054's trust-and-siege variant, so "deadline" is not a necessary feature of the whole pattern. Chang Xi's later rebellion also shows that a successful surrender conversation can fail to produce durable pacification.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 1054's Zhang Liao-to-Chang Xi case as a siege, observation, and personal-guarantee variant.
- Generalized the concept from a deadline-heavy Yu Fan model to a broader pressure-plus-trust persuasion pattern.
- Added durable-settlement qualification through Chang Xi's later rebellion.
