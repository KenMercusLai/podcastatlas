# Force-Backed Surrender Persuasion / 武力背书式劝降

## Definition
Force-backed surrender persuasion is a negotiation pattern in which the persuader gives the target face and reasons while making clear that military imbalance, resource limits, and a deadline leave surrender as the least damaging choice.

## Current Synthesis
Hanji 1039 defines the pattern through Yu Fan's mission to Hua Xin. Yu Fan does not simply threaten [[HuaXin|华歆]]; he praises Hua Xin and Wang Lang, compares [[YuzhangCommandery|豫章郡]] against [[KuaijiCommandery|会稽郡]], invokes [[SunCe|孙策]]'s victories, and gives a specific deadline for sending the welcoming document. Persuasion works because it combines respect, asymmetry, precedent, and impending force.

## Key Claims
- Face-saving praise can lower resistance before the hard comparison arrives.
- Material comparison matters: food, weapons, people, and fortification capacity define whether resistance is credible.
- Precedent makes surrender imaginable by showing that comparable actors have already yielded or failed.
- A deadline converts general pressure into an immediate decision.
- The method remains coercive; its moral evaluation depends on whether it prevents worse harm or merely launders conquest.

## Evidence
- Face and respect: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1039-dongwu-diyi-zuipao-qie-kan-ta-ruhe-quanjiang-huaxin-lhre-ih73ynzfwujo0lnxyjdguyd|Hanji 1039]] has Yu Fan praise Hua Xin and Wang Lang before pressing the surrender case.
- Material asymmetry: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1039-dongwu-diyi-zuipao-qie-kan-ta-ruhe-quanjiang-huaxin-lhre-ih73ynzfwujo0lnxyjdguyd|Hanji 1039]] compares Yuzhang's grain, weapons, people, and reserves unfavorably with Kuaiji.
- Precedent and threat: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1039-dongwu-diyi-zuipao-qie-kan-ta-ruhe-quanjiang-huaxin-lhre-ih73ynzfwujo0lnxyjdguyd|Hanji 1039]] invokes Sun Ce's treatment of Liu Yao and Wang Lang's defeat to make refusal look futile.
- Decision deadline: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1039-dongwu-diyi-zuipao-qie-kan-ta-ruhe-quanjiang-huaxin-lhre-ih73ynzfwujo0lnxyjdguyd|Hanji 1039]] says Yu Fan requires Hua Xin's welcoming document by the next noon.
- Moral ambiguity: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1039-dongwu-diyi-zuipao-qie-kan-ta-ruhe-quanjiang-huaxin-lhre-ih73ynzfwujo0lnxyjdguyd|Hanji 1039]] pairs the successful persuasion with Sun Sheng's criticism and the host's civilian-protection defense.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept should not romanticize coercion as dialogue. Hanji 1039 works because Sun Ce already has force behind the messenger, and the source gives only one detailed case. Later evidence should test whether the pattern produces genuine settlement or only temporary compliance.
