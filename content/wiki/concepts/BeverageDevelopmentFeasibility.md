# Beverage Development Feasibility / 饮品研发可落地性

## Definition
Beverage-development feasibility is the translation of a creative drink idea into a repeatable product that fits a brand's flavor logic, economics, workflow, safety requirements, supply conditions, and intended production scale.

## Current Synthesis
A recipe is commercially incomplete until it can be operated and manufactured. Hospitality development works backward from a menu window, brand position, and intended experience while testing cost, ingredient count, preparation, staff execution, safety, and supply. Packaged-beverage development adds aroma construction, functional ingredients, shelf-stable formulation, and a test-batch pathway. Across both settings, constraints force an idea to become legible and repeatable; a feasible prototype still needs evidence of demand and access to distribution.

## Key Claims
- Brand position, intended occasion, and flavor logic define the solution space before recipe optimization.
- Cost, ingredient count, preparation steps, and production minimums determine whether an idea can leave the laboratory or competition stage.
- Safety, formulation stability, and reliable supply are core product properties rather than downstream procurement details.
- Aroma, mouthfeel, functional ingredients, and packaging or service conditions must work as one system.
- Lead times and repeatability require developers to anticipate future menu or market conditions.
- A manufacturable prototype is evidence of technical feasibility, not proof of product-market fit.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The sources describe practitioner methods and a reported company visit rather than comparative operating data. Exact thresholds for cost, step count, shelf life, ingredient safety, production minimum, and supply reliability depend on format, jurisdiction, customer, and channel. The supplied Planet Money summary does not document a full stability, sensory, regulatory, or commercial test of its prototype.

## What Changed
- Extended feasibility from menu and competition service into packaged-beverage formulation and test manufacturing.
- Added the boundary between a feasible prototype and a viable distributed product.
