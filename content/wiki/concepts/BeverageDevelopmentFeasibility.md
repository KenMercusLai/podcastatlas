# Beverage Development Feasibility / 饮品研发可落地性

## Definition
Beverage-development feasibility is the translation of a creative drink idea into a repeatable product that fits a brand's flavor logic, economics, workflow, safety requirements, and supply conditions.

## Current Synthesis
A recipe is commercially incomplete until it can be operated. Development therefore works backward from a future menu window, brand position, and intended experience while testing ingredient cost and count, preparation steps, staff execution, food safety, and stable sourcing. Constraints do not merely suppress creativity: they force an idea to become legible, repeatable, and useful in an everyday business. Stable inputs also preserve attention for the variable work that matters most, whether customer service or competition presentation.

## Key Claims
- Brand position and flavor logic define the solution space before recipe optimization.
- Cost, ingredient count, and preparation steps determine whether service can repeat the idea.
- Safety and stable supply are core product properties rather than downstream procurement details.
- Three-to-six-month lead times require proposals to anticipate future menu and market needs.
- Operational constraints can give experimental ideas practical “烟火气” without eliminating originality.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source describes one consultant's method rather than comparative operating data. Exact thresholds for cost, step count, safety controls, shelf life, and supply reliability depend on the brand and service environment.
