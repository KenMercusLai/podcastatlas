# Human-like Assisted Driving / 拟人化辅助驾驶

## Definition
Human-like assisted driving is the product-quality goal of making automated vehicle behavior stable, natural, legible, and predictable enough that drivers and passengers remain comfortable, while preserving safety and legal responsibility as separate requirements.

## Current Synthesis
The concept marks a shift from feature presence and difficult-scene completion toward sustained experience. A system can avoid a collision or eventually pass a narrow road yet still lose user trust through steering oscillation, abrupt action, hesitation, or behavior outside passenger expectations. “Human-like” therefore means a screened common denominator of safe professional driving—early anticipation, smooth control, and predictable intent—not imitation of every human driver's aggression or habits.

## Key Claims
- Collision avoidance is the safety floor; natural and predictable motion is an additional experience requirement.
- Completion of a difficult scene does not prove product quality if the behavior repeatedly triggers fear or takeover.
- The useful human reference is a filtered common denominator of safe driving rather than individual style or rule-bending.
- Long-duration calm use matters more than short clips of aggressive or spectacular maneuvers.
- Stability, anticipation, comfort, and legibility can become differentiators as basic scene coverage converges.

## Evidence
### Safety floor and experience layer
- [[tefan-cong-hui-kaiche-dao-hui-zuoshi-ai-ruhe-jinru-zhenshi-shijie-gkwrijeopcilaoo4hwtn2dns]] distinguishes avoiding collisions from earlier slowing, natural cornering, stable steering, and actions that keep passengers at ease.

### Scene completion versus sustained trust
- [[tefan-cong-hui-kaiche-dao-hui-zuoshi-ai-ruhe-jinru-zhenshi-shijie-gkwrijeopcilaoo4hwtn2dns]] says ring-road or narrow-road completion can still be a poor experience when steering oscillates or movement exceeds passenger expectations, and contrasts short-video spectacle with calm commutes lasting tens of minutes.

### Filtered human behavior
- [[tefan-cong-hui-kaiche-dao-hui-zuoshi-ai-ruhe-jinru-zhenshi-shijie-gkwrijeopcilaoo4hwtn2dns]] defines human-like behavior as learning the common elements of selected safe driving rather than copying each driver's personality or gray-area conduct.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- Naturalness is not a substitute for collision, disengagement, injury, compliance, or fallback evidence.
- Passenger comfort is partly subjective and can vary by road, culture, driver experience, interface, and vehicle tuning.
- Conservative behavior can improve perceived safety but can also create hesitation or traffic-flow problems if poorly calibrated.
- The current evidence is a company-centered discussion without comparative independent testing.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to separate safe task completion from predictable, sustained assisted-driving experience.
