# Rationalist Community

## Definition
The rationalist community is the source's internet-rooted culture of reducing cognitive bias, revising beliefs with evidence, and pursuing curiosity through essays, fiction, mathematics, salons, and thought experiments.

## Current Synthesis
The episode distinguishes rationalism from effective altruism while explaining their overlap. Rationalism asks how to think more accurately; effective altruism applies evidence and reasoning to helping effectively. Their convergence around AI risk supplied shared people, venues, concepts, and debate, but neither community has a single identity or policy position.

## Key Claims
- Rationalism is an epistemic culture rather than a synonym for effective altruism.
- Belief revision and curiosity are central norms.
- Blogs, fiction, mathematics, gatherings, and informal social spaces form knowledge infrastructure.
- Overlap with effective altruism helped AI safety move into research and institutions.
- Resistance to organizational discipline can preserve freedom while limiting coordination.
