# Mental Bandwidth / 大脑带宽

## Definition
Mental bandwidth / 大脑带宽 is the limited cognitive capacity needed for fluid reasoning, working memory, self-control, planning, and emotional regulation.

## Current Synthesis
EP87 frames bandwidth as the inner capacity that scarcity quietly consumes. When worries, unfinished tasks, money problems, hunger, anxiety, or social threat keep running in the background, the person has fewer resources left for reasoning and execution. The result can look like poor discipline, but the source argues that the underlying problem is often an overloaded system.

The practical implication is compassionate but demanding: first restore bandwidth, then analyze and act. Relaxation, physiological sighing, meditation, movement, time in nature, daily review, gratitude practice, and nonjudgmental environments are presented as ways to bring the mind back online before relying on strategy.

## Key Claims
- Bandwidth is finite and can be occupied by worry, working-memory load, and unresolved decisions.
- Scarcity lowers fluid intelligence and executive control more than it changes stored knowledge.
- Self-control failures can follow from bandwidth depletion rather than fixed character weakness.
- Relaxation and body-based downshifting can be prerequisites for clear analysis under anxiety.
- Review and gratitude practices can restore felt abundance by making small gains and resources visible.

## Evidence
- Working-memory evidence: [[ep87-xique-ruhe-sheji-yige-rang-ziji-bian-congming-de-xitong-gkwrirwoeedlbme1xqtv2orv]] uses number-memory and table-manners experiments to connect occupied memory with weaker self-control.
- Scarcity-IQ evidence: [[ep87-xique-ruhe-sheji-yige-rang-ziji-bian-congming-de-xitong-gkwrirwoeedlbme1xqtv2orv]] describes car-repair-cost and sugarcane-farmer studies where money pressure tracks lower fluid-reasoning and control performance.
- Noise metaphor evidence: [[ep87-xique-ruhe-sheji-yige-rang-ziji-bian-congming-de-xitong-gkwrirwoeedlbme1xqtv2orv]] compares scarcity worries to trains or browser tabs that cannot be closed.
- Recovery evidence: [[ep87-xique-ruhe-sheji-yige-rang-ziji-bian-congming-de-xitong-gkwrirwoeedlbme1xqtv2orv]] recommends physiological sighs, meditation, exercise, nature contact, daily review, gratitude, and accepting environments before heavy reasoning.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The page should not be used to diagnose a person clinically or reduce every failure to bandwidth. The source's study summaries and numerical IQ-drop claims remain source-scoped. Relaxation practices may help restore capacity, but they do not erase material poverty or structural pressure.
