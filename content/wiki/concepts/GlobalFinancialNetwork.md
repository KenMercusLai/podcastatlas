# Global Financial Network

Global financial network is the Airwallex strategy in [[11-nian-110-yi-meijin-ranhou-ne-duihua-airwallex-wu-kai-ai-shidai-xiayizhan-1000-yi-lr4tvdrq25by7fugoqkqojw6vwdk]]: build local bank partnerships, licenses, clearing access, APIs, and product coverage across many markets so businesses can receive, hold, convert, pay, and reconcile money internationally. [[WuKai]] contrasts this with a region-first payment network, arguing that the largest payment opportunities often cross continents.

The concept extends [[MoneyMovementInfrastructure]] by emphasizing global coverage and local compliance at the same time. Faster rails alone are not enough; the network has to combine payment execution, account structures, FX, cards, acquiring, ERP integration, support, and trust-heavy local operations.

## Key Claims
- Cross-border payment infrastructure is slow to build because bank partnerships, local licenses, and clearing-network access are market-by-market work.
- Global reach can be a strategic choice before it is an immediate revenue engine; the source says Airwallex spent several early years building product and rails before meaningful revenue.
- A global network becomes more valuable when it supports both pay-in and pay-out products rather than only one money-transfer action.
- Traditional banks and fintech platforms compete not only on fees, but on network reach, API access, product breadth, and finance workflow integration.
- Local market execution remains necessary even for a global network because compliance, marketing, hiring, and customer behavior differ by country.
