# Expectation-Reversal Comedy / 预期反转喜剧

## Definition

Expectation-reversal comedy / 预期反转喜剧 establishes a familiar narrative, moral, linguistic, or visual pattern strongly enough that the audience predicts its completion, then substitutes an incongruous outcome that remains intelligible within the scene.

## Current Synthesis

In the bounded [[WulinWaizhuan|《武林外传》]] case, reversals occur at several scales. The mother begins what sounds like a moral objection to cheating but completes it as advice not to get caught; she warns against killing but grounds the warning in fear of harming someone powerful. Each line borrows the shape of ethical instruction before replacing its expected principle with partiality and practical calculation.

The largest reversal is delayed across the story. The岳飞 reference, needle, fire, and four-character inscription lead both hosts and viewers toward “精忠报国.” The claim that displaying the tattoo will frighten an enemy creates a small inconsistency, but the text withholds resolution until “好汉饶命” is exposed. This answer is maximally unlike martial heroism while still matching the mother's fear of losing her son. The deception's collapse works similarly: obvious vocal and logistical flaws survive, but one taste of impossibly bad “imperial” porridge reveals the truth.

## Key Claims

- Reversal requires a sufficiently legible expectation; randomness alone does not create the same effect.
- Familiar moral formulas can become comic when their pragmatic completion violates the implied principle.
- Delayed disclosure lets an audience participate by filling a gap with the most culturally available answer.
- The strongest reversal changes the immediate meaning while remaining consistent with established character motives.
- Scale contrast can intensify comedy when an elaborate system fails through a trivial sensory detail.
- Repeated reversals can organize a whole plot rather than functioning only as isolated punchlines.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

This synthesis comes from one episode's interpretation of one sitcom story. Cultural familiarity affects which expectation feels inevitable, and a viewer unfamiliar with the岳飞 tattoo story may experience the reveal differently. The hosts infer deliberate design from the finished episode; the source does not include writers' drafts or production testimony.

## What Changed

- Created a multi-scale account joining line-level moral reversals to plot-level delayed disclosure.
- Added character consistency as the boundary between earned incongruity and arbitrary surprise.
- Identified disproportion between elaborate preparation and trivial failure as a distinct comic form.
