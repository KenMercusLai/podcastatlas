# Healthspan Three Domains

## Definition
Healthspan three domains is [[PeterAttia]]'s source-described frame that longevity should protect physical, cognitive, and emotional function rather than only extend binary lifespan.

## Current Synthesis
The episode treats lifespan as simple alive-or-dead status and healthspan as the harder question of what kind of life remains available. Attia rejects a thin definition of healthspan as merely freedom from diagnosed disability or disease because it misses physical decline, cognitive decline, and emotional suffering.

The synthesis is that a longevity plan has to defend all three domains at once. Cardiovascular prevention, cancer screening, brain-health levers, exercise capacity, fall prevention, and emotional repair are not separate optimization hobbies; in this source they become one healthspan portfolio whose value depends on function, presence, relationships, and agency.

## Key Claims
- Lifespan is treated as binary, while healthspan is multidimensional.
- A disease-free person can still have poor healthspan through physical, cognitive, or emotional decline.
- Physical healthspan depends on strength, stability, aerobic capacity, power, and fall resistance.
- Cognitive healthspan depends on vascular, metabolic, sleep, head-injury, genetic, and neurodegenerative risk context.
- Emotional healthspan depends on connection, purpose, presence, relationship repair, and self-regulation.
- The three domains prevent longevity from collapsing into biomarkers alone.

## Evidence
- Lifespan distinction: [[improve-vitality-emotional-physical-health-lifespan-dr-peter-attia-scim5907126956]] says Attia defines lifespan as binary survival.
- Three-domain frame: [[improve-vitality-emotional-physical-health-lifespan-dr-peter-attia-scim5907126956]] has Attia define healthspan through physical, cognitive, and emotional dimensions.
- Physical branch: [[improve-vitality-emotional-physical-health-lifespan-dr-peter-attia-scim5907126956]] connects falls, fast-twitch fiber loss, strength, stability, zone 2 work, and VO2 max to physical healthspan.
- Cognitive branch: [[improve-vitality-emotional-physical-health-lifespan-dr-peter-attia-scim5907126956]] links dementia risk to APOE, amyloid uncertainty, sleep, insulin sensitivity, LDL/ApoB, exercise, and head injury.
- Emotional branch: [[improve-vitality-emotional-physical-health-lifespan-dr-peter-attia-scim5907126956]] treats connection, purpose, presence, emotional regulation, therapy, and repair as core healthspan components.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is an interview summary, not a formal healthspan measurement standard. It does not settle how to weight physical, cognitive, and emotional domains for different ages, illnesses, disabilities, cultures, or care contexts.
