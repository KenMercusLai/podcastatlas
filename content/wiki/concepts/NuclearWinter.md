# Nuclear Winter

Nuclear winter appears in [[sp-01-yuzhou-kaer-sagen-de-xingchen-dahai-he-renlei-mengxiang-543901074]] as a serious scientific concept associated with [[CarlSagan|Carl Sagan]] and other researchers during the Cold War. The source presents it as evidence that Sagan's public science was not limited to astronomy or wonder: he also used scientific modeling to warn that human civilization could damage Earth's climate and food systems through nuclear war.

The episode notes that nuclear winter later entered literature, games, and popular culture, but emphasizes that it began as a scientific and political warning. That makes it a bridge between Sagan's cosmic perspective and Earth's immediate civilizational risks.

## Key Claims
- Nuclear winter makes nuclear war a planetary environmental problem, not only a military or geopolitical event.
- In the source, Sagan's anti-nuclear stance follows from scientific responsibility rather than generic pacifist sentiment.
- The concept shows how public science can intervene in policy fear, popular imagination, and civilizational self-preservation at once.
- It connects Sagan's cosmic humanism to the same question raised by other wiki pages: whether technological civilizations can survive their own destructive powers.
