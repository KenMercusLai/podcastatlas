# Hardware Prototype-to-Production Gap / 硬件原型到量产鸿沟

## Definition
The hardware prototype-to-production gap is the operating distance between demonstrating that a device can work and repeatedly manufacturing, shipping, supporting, and financing a device that works reliably in real use.

## Current Synthesis
[[nk78yhgkzrza-pj5ex42hwz7n352-nk78yhgkzrza-pj5ex42hwz7n352]] treats confusing a demo with a product as a formative error in [[Insta360|Insta360's]] early years. The missing capabilities included manufacturing-process design, yield, supplier cooperation, environmental and vibration reliability, battery-contact behavior, quality control, and the ability to absorb failures after launch. A visually persuasive prototype proves possibility; production readiness proves repeatability under cost and service constraints.

## Key Claims
- A functional demo validates a technical path but not manufacturing repeatability, field reliability, or unit economics.
- Yield and process design can determine viability even when the product concept and prototype are strong.
- Reliability requirements arise from actual use conditions, such as vibration and replaceable-battery contact, not only laboratory operation.
- Supplier and manufacturing partners contribute specialized knowledge that a software-oriented founding team may not possess.
- Post-launch defects compound through replacements, support, reputation damage, and the time needed for later products to rebuild trust.

## Evidence
- Early operating gap: [[nk78yhgkzrza-pj5ex42hwz7n352-nk78yhgkzrza-pj5ex42hwz7n352]] says the team initially treated making a demo and making a mass-produced product as too similar.
- Manufacturing evidence: [[nk78yhgkzrza-pj5ex42hwz7n352-nk78yhgkzrza-pj5ex42hwz7n352]] describes low yield, weak process knowledge, reluctant factories, and later collaboration with an experienced GoPro manufacturer.
- Reliability evidence: [[nk78yhgkzrza-pj5ex42hwz7n352-nk78yhgkzrza-pj5ex42hwz7n352]] uses action-camera vibration and removable-battery disconnection as an example of hidden field requirements.
- Reputation evidence: [[nk78yhgkzrza-pj5ex42hwz7n352-nk78yhgkzrza-pj5ex42hwz7n352]] records Luo Yonghao's view that hardware quality failures may require several later products to repair trust.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The exact gap varies by category, volume, certification regime, component maturity, and outsourcing model. Experienced contract manufacturers can reduce but not transfer away the brand owner's product, quality, cash-flow, and support responsibility. The episode supplies practitioner testimony rather than comparative failure-rate or yield data.

## What Changed
- Established production readiness as a system of repeatability, reliability, cost, support, and reputation rather than a larger prototype run.
- Added use-environment testing and manufacturer knowledge as explicit parts of the gap.
