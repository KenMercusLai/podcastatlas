# Restaurant Delivery Extension

## Definition
Restaurant delivery extension is the strategy of moving a restaurant brand beyond its original dine-in occasion by creating delivery-friendly products, stations, and formats that fit different meal contexts.

## Current Synthesis
The Haidilao case in [[waimai-chengwei-haidilao-zengzhang-zuikuai-de-yewu-legao-biaoshi-buhui-yong-ai-lai-sheji-chanpin-1008870590]] shows delivery extension as more than putting existing dishes on a platform. Haidilao uses one-person meal products such as bibimbap to move from group hot-pot occasions into ordinary daily meals, while denser delivery sites make the operational layer more explicit. The qualification is profitability: delivery and new brands can grow faster than restaurants while still carrying lower margins.

## Key Claims
- Delivery extension works best when the product is redesigned for the delivery occasion rather than merely copied from dine-in.
- One-person meals can let a group-dining brand reach daily solo meal demand.
- Delivery stations and site density turn brand extension into an operations problem.
- Fast revenue growth does not prove mature profitability or brand fit.

## Evidence
- Occasion shift: [[waimai-chengwei-haidilao-zengzhang-zuikuai-de-yewu-legao-biaoshi-buhui-yong-ai-lai-sheji-chanpin-1008870590]] says Haidilao used one-person meal products to move delivery beyond hot-pot scenes into daily meals.
- Growth signal: [[waimai-chengwei-haidilao-zengzhang-zuikuai-de-yewu-legao-biaoshi-buhui-yong-ai-lai-sheji-chanpin-1008870590]] says delivery was Haidilao's fastest-growing business and reached roughly 9% of revenue.
- Profitability caution: [[waimai-chengwei-haidilao-zengzhang-zuikuai-de-yewu-legao-biaoshi-buhui-yong-ai-lai-sheji-chanpin-1008870590]] cites a research view that delivery and new-brand margins remain lower.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not prove that Haidilao's delivery model can scale profitably or preserve the service-heavy brand experience that made hot pot distinctive.

## What Changed
- Created the concept using Haidilao's delivery growth and one-person meal expansion.
