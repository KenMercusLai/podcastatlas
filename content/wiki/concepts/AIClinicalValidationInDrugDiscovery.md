# AI Clinical Validation In Drug Discovery

AI clinical validation in drug discovery is the source's check on AI drug enthusiasm in [[vol-117-shengwu-yiyao-de-2025-chaodi-zhongguo-yanfa-jiaolv-he-xinwang-jiwei-lmhral0rmq6tohiqdwsgmfapnyn7]]. [[XiaoPTeacher|小P老师]] treats AI as a 2025 watch direction for molecular design, large-molecule sequence optimization, and target selection, but says prior AI-drug clinical disclosures cooled the market when data looked weak.

This concept complements [[AIDrugDiscoveryPlatform]]. The platform page focuses on model and business design; this page names the clinical burden: a generated molecule or target hypothesis only matters if it survives wet-lab, translational, safety, and human outcome tests.

## Key Claims
- AI can help with molecular design, protein or large-molecule sequence optimization, and earlier target selection.
- Platform narratives are insufficient without clinical evidence.
- Weak clinical data can quickly reprice AI-drug enthusiasm even if the model story remains attractive.
- [[AIVerification]] is harder in drug discovery because verification may require long, expensive biological and clinical loops.
