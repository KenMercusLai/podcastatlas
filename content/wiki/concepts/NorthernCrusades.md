# Northern Crusades / 北方十字军

## Definition

The Northern Crusades were crusading campaigns and associated state-building, conversion, migration, and colonization in the Baltic and northeastern Europe rather than expeditions directed primarily toward Jerusalem.

## Current Synthesis

In the bounded episode, the [[TeutonicOrder|Teutonic Order]] is the main institutional case. A Polish invitation to fight pagan Old Prussians became a basis for conquest and territorial rule, supported by castles, estates, military-religious organization, mercenaries, and visiting aristocrats.

The framework must retain multiple processes without flattening their power differences. German-speaking settlement could occur by invitation and peaceful migration while crusading institutions also conquered land, displaced or assimilated communities, and imposed political and religious authority.

## Key Claims

- Northern crusading belonged to the wider crusade movement but had a Baltic territorial center.
- Conversion, warfare, colonization, settlement, and state formation operated together.
- Military orders depended on administrative and economic infrastructure as well as battlefield force.
- Invited migration and coercive conquest can coexist without becoming morally equivalent.
- Later national narratives can misdescribe these medieval conflicts as timeless ethnic wars.

## Evidence

- Geographic and institutional scope: [[47-tiaodun-qishituan-de-qianshi-jinsheng-yu-bolan-minzuzhuyi-xiankeiweiqi-shizijunqishi-lqoeehv6bw8thlpgr1wvvrv_jk2t]] distinguishes Baltic crusading from Jerusalem-centered campaigns and traces the Teutonic shift eastward.
- Conquest and settlement: [[47-tiaodun-qishituan-de-qianshi-jinsheng-yu-bolan-minzuzhuyi-xiankeiweiqi-shizijunqishi-lqoeehv6bw8thlpgr1wvvrv_jk2t]] retains both the destruction of Old Prussian autonomy and non-coercive migration invited by local rulers.
- State capacity: [[47-tiaodun-qishituan-de-qianshi-jinsheng-yu-bolan-minzuzhuyi-xiankeiweiqi-shizijunqishi-lqoeehv6bw8thlpgr1wvvrv_jk2t]] connects castles, estates, administration, mercenaries, and trans-European aristocratic participation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The episode is an introductory survey, not a comprehensive history of every northern crusade, people, order, or region. Foundation dates, invitations, conversion processes, migration patterns, branches, and the disappearance of Old Prussian language and identity remain source-scoped. A mixed-process account must not be used to soften conquest or collective destruction.

## What Changed

- Established the Baltic crusades as a distinct geographic and institutional field.
- Joined military conversion campaigns to settlement, administration, and territorial state formation.
- Made the asymmetry between invited migration and conquest explicit.
