# Doctoral Research Operating Method / 博士研究操作法

## Definition
Doctoral research operating method is a workflow that combines foundational learning, consequential problem selection, minimal-assumption testing, rapid feedback, disciplined execution, and increasing responsibility for other researchers and real-world constraints.

## Current Synthesis
The episode rejects a simple choice between “slow research” and speed. Problem choice should be patient enough to identify something difficult and important, but the decisive assumption should be isolated and tested cheaply before months of full-scale work. Once a direction survives, AI, parallel execution, peers, mentors, and community feedback can compress the loop without substituting activity for evidence.

## Key Claims
- Strong fundamentals remain necessary even when AI can explain concepts or generate implementations.
- A valuable hard problem is one where a rigorous partial advance matters, not merely one that consumes substantial effort.
- The smallest decisive assumption should be tested before scaling an algorithm, dataset, or engineering program.
- Early feedback from advisers, senior peers, users, or the research community reduces avoidable isolation and error.
- Public artifacts such as code, tools, small experiments, and research notes can create a verifiable cold-start path when compute or institutional access is limited.
- Doctoral growth expands from independent craft toward supervision, leadership, and adaptation to industrial constraints.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The method comes from AI/NLP, where small experiments and public artifacts may be cheaper than in clinical, wet-lab, hardware, or field research. Fast tests can reject a poor proxy rather than the real hypothesis, public feedback can reward novelty or confidence over truth, and “hard but important” can become a prestige story unless importance and tractability are independently examined.

## What Changed
- Established “choose slowly, validate quickly, execute quickly” as a bounded doctoral-research workflow.
- Added public artifacts and external feedback as cold-start mechanisms rather than substitutes for fundamentals.
- Connected later-stage doctoral formation to leadership and industry adaptation.
