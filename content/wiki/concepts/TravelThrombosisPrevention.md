# Travel Thrombosis Prevention / 旅行血栓预防

## Definition
Travel thrombosis prevention / 旅行血栓预防 is the ordinary-traveler practice of reducing lower-limb venous stasis during long sitting through movement breaks, ankle-pump exercise, hydration, posture changes, and selected use of medical compression stockings when appropriate.

## Current Synthesis
The VOL.204 [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] source treats prevention as mechanical and behavioral before it is pharmaceutical. Since long sitting reduces calf-muscle pumping and venous return, the basic intervention is to make the calf pump work again: stand or walk briefly when possible, move the ankle when confined to a seat, and avoid treating stillness as harmless merely because the person is young.

Compression stockings enter as a pressure-gradient tool rather than a fashion accessory or universal cure. The episode frames them as useful in postoperative, bedbound, immobile, venous-stasis, and long-travel contexts when chosen correctly, while keeping drug prevention inside clinician assessment.

VOL.136 shows that the same calf-pump mechanism is not travel-exclusive. Desk work and prolonged standing also benefit from position change and ankle movement, while compression class, fit, timing, established varicose-vein damage, and possible acute thrombosis remain reasons not to turn stockings into a universal self-prescription.

## Key Claims
- Movement is the central prevention behavior because calf-muscle contraction helps venous return.
- Ankle-pump exercise gives seated travelers a low-space way to activate the lower-leg muscle pump.
- Reasonable hydration matters because dehydration and blood concentration can worsen stasis-related risk.
- Medical compression stockings can reduce pooling by applying graduated pressure, with higher pressure distally and lower pressure proximally.
- Prevention advice changes once thrombosis is suspected; at that point unnecessary walking, massage, or forceful movement may increase risk.
- The transferable mechanism is fixed-posture interruption, not travel branding: seated work and prolonged standing also require movement and individual context.

## Evidence
- Movement and ankle pumping: [[vol-204-jingjicang-zonghezheng-duanwu-jiaqi-chuxing-chengche-chengji-zuojiu-le-tuizhang-xie-fajin-xueguan-waike-yisheng-de-yinghe-pailei-zhinan]] recommends getting up briefly when possible and doing deliberate ankle movement when constrained.
- Hydration: [[vol-204-jingjicang-zonghezheng-duanwu-jiaqi-chuxing-chengche-chengji-zuojiu-le-tuizhang-xie-fajin-xueguan-waike-yisheng-de-yinghe-pailei-zhinan]] links reasonable water intake to avoiding excessive blood concentration while warning against overdrinking.
- Compression stockings: [[vol-204-jingjicang-zonghezheng-duanwu-jiaqi-chuxing-chengche-chengji-zuojiu-le-tuizhang-xie-fajin-xueguan-waike-yisheng-de-yinghe-pailei-zhinan]] describes medical elastic stockings as graduated-pressure tools used in surgical, bedbound, immobile, and travel settings.
- Workplace transfer: [[vol-136-ni-ye-bei-yizi-yingkong-le-dafu-kuai-jiujiu-jiuzuo-dagongren-ba-lovi71tk-wgo30v9myvd2f1gjdwu]] applies ankle pumps and selected compression use to long sitting and standing while distinguishing prevention from acute-thrombosis care.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The sources give public prevention principles, not individualized travel or occupational medicine. People with pregnancy, cancer, hormone use, prior thrombosis, surgery, severe swelling, suspected clot, arterial disease, skin problems, or other medical complexity need clinician-specific advice before relying on stockings, drugs, or behavior changes. VOL.136's stocking classes and wear-time examples remain source-scoped rather than universal prescriptions.

## What Changed
- VOL.136 extends calf-pump activation and compression boundaries from travel into sedentary and standing work.
