# Enterprise-Run Society / 企业办社会

Enterprise-run society / 企业办社会 is the pattern where a large work unit provides not only jobs but housing, education, leisure, welfare, identity, and social infrastructure. [[chengshi-jiushi-zheyang-21-shiyan-yizuo-yin-qiche-erqi-you-bugan-bei-qiche-paoxia-de-chengshi-1001770066]] adds the concept through [[Shiyan]] and [[SecondAutomobileWorks|二汽]].

The source describes old Dongfeng and 二汽 worker communities with auditoriums, outdoor swimming pools, leisure spaces, residential areas, and a shared "汽车人" identity. In Shiyan, enterprise-run society was not a side benefit; it helped convert a strategic factory location into a functioning migrant industrial city.

The concept is adjacent to but different from [[CompanyTownLaborControl]]. The Shiyan episode emphasizes memory, welfare, mission, and urban formation more than coercive labor lock-in, while still showing how a dominant employer can make city identity depend on corporate decisions such as [[DongfengMotor|Dongfeng]]'s headquarters move.
