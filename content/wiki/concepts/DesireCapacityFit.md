# Desire-Capacity Fit / 欲望与能力匹配

## Definition
Desire-capacity fit / 欲望与能力匹配 is the episode's closing life-regulation principle that psychological steadiness often requires lowering, narrowing, or recalibrating desires so they match actual ability, personality, family conditions, role obligations, and life stage.

## Current Synthesis
The source frames "安定此心" as a fit problem rather than a slogan about optimism. [[JiangTaoPsychiatrist|江涛]] argues that people under intense competition, shrinking opportunity, information overload, family expectation, and achievement comparison can harm themselves when desire, capacity, and social reality are badly mismatched.

This is not an anti-effort concept. The episode allows striving, responsibility, and role duty, especially for middle-aged adults supporting elders and children. The boundary is self-damage: a goal may need to be escaped, lowered, delayed, or redesigned when it outruns the person's health, temperament, resources, and current life conditions.

## Key Claims
- Psychological steadiness can require reducing desire rather than only increasing willpower.
- A good goal must fit ability, personality, growth history, family environment, role, and current pressure.
- "Escape" can be legitimate when a person has overestimated capacity or accepted an externally imposed goal.
- Achievement environments should be chosen with the person's mental resilience, not only prestige, in mind.
- Hope itself can become pathological when it turns into rigid future accounting or endless pressure.
- Middle-aged responsibility can coexist with a lower-demand ethic: do what must be done without exhausting or injuring the self.

## Evidence
- Lowering desire - [[shidai-zhenghou-yu-anding-cixin-lphaw0qek8ibr0jt44yygou6akql]] gives "lower desire" as Jiang Tao's first answer to how the mind can settle.
- Fit variables - [[shidai-zhenghou-yu-anding-cixin-lphaw0qek8ibr0jt44yygou6akql]] says desire should match ability, character, growth, development, family environment, and role.
- Escape boundary - [[shidai-zhenghou-yu-anding-cixin-lphaw0qek8ibr0jt44yygou6akql]] says escaping is not shameful when anxiety, depression, or compulsion are tied to goals set too high or imposed by others.
- Achievement setting - [[shidai-zhenghou-yu-anding-cixin-lphaw0qek8ibr0jt44yygou6akql]] uses "chicken head or phoenix tail" to argue that personal resilience matters when choosing a high-pressure environment.
- High-pressure cases - [[shidai-zhenghou-yu-anding-cixin-lphaw0qek8ibr0jt44yygou6akql]] recounts students, a professor, and an older patient whose stress, fixation, or despair escalated into severe outcomes.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Lowering desire can become harmful if it is used to excuse coercion, deprivation, learned helplessness, untreated illness, discrimination, or institutional abandonment. The source's useful boundary is fit and self-protection, not telling people to accept every constraint as natural.
