# New Engineering Education / 新工科教育

New engineering education / 新工科教育 is the Chinese engineering-education reform frame discussed in [[ep266-dang-ai-zhonggou-daxue-women-gai-ruhe-dingyi-hao-zhuanye-llbzcgsefktcvkq-f37hoan79dd]]. [[HuangZiyiEducationReporter|黄子翊]] describes it as an effort, promoted since 2017 through policy language such as "复旦共识" and "天大行动", to move engineering education away from standardized transmission and toward system problem solving, industry collaboration, and innovation around real production problems.

The episode argues that AI increases the urgency of this reform. Traditional engineering could train excellent executors for known production links, but AI and industrial restructuring raise the value of students who can define problems, connect hardware and software, work across disciplines, and create new standards or categories.

## Key Claims
- New engineering education is not just adding an AI course to old engineering curricula.
- It emphasizes system-level problem solving, real enterprise topics, industry-school collaboration, and graduation projects tied to concrete problems.
- AI makes local optimization and standardized execution easier to automate, so higher-level design, integration, and judgment become more important.
- Embodied intelligence pushes mechanical, electronic, control, multimodal, perception, and decision systems closer together.
- The source remains cautious about current hot fields: if mechanical design becomes modular and standardized, parts of today's demand can also become future AI-compressed work.
- Long training cycles make curriculum reform urgent because students may graduate into a different technology and labor market from the one visible at admission.
