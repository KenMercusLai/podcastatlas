# Creative Experience Bottleneck / 创作经验瓶颈

## Definition
Creative experience bottleneck is the constraint that generative tools can multiply drafts and execution speed far faster than creators can accumulate the lived observation, emotion, conflict, and personal stakes that make new work worth expressing.

## Current Synthesis
The concept emerges from [[KKAIDirector|KK]] and [[ShanYinAIDirector|山音]]'s distinction between production capacity and experiential input. AI can propose premises, expand scripts, generate shots, revise assets, and compress a film into days, but it cannot give the creator a life that has been directly seen, heard, felt, or endured.

This bottleneck does not require every work to be autobiographical. It means that human creative differentiation increasingly moves toward situated attention and judgment when generic execution becomes abundant. Experience supplies pressures and perceptions; the creator's ability to reject a formulaic output turns them into form. Once accumulated experience has been repeatedly mined, sustainable creation may require returning to life rather than simply increasing generation volume.

## Key Claims
- Generation speed and experiential accumulation operate on different clocks.
- Real experience can supply emotional specificity even when the finished work is fictional, fantastic, or highly stylized.
- AI-generated options are useful material, but the creator must recognize which option connects to something genuinely observed or felt.
- Lower production barriers can expose rather than eliminate the shortage of meaningful premises.
- Sustainable creative practice may alternate intensive making with periods of living, observing, and incubation.
- Personal experience is not automatic proof of quality; it still requires craft, selection, transformation, and ethical judgment.

## Evidence
- Experience depletion - [[ai-wuxian-rensheng-youxian-duitan-kk-shanyin-yixian-ai-chuangzuozhe-lmk4luckjgn-cpivd-7dy9vmsl0u]] has KK describe an early pace of two or three pieces per day that slowed as existing experience was converted into content.
- Situated input - [[ai-wuxian-rensheng-youxian-duitan-kk-shanyin-yixian-ai-chuangzuozhe-lmk4luckjgn-cpivd-7dy9vmsl0u]] uses KK's “在现场” to argue that seeing, hearing, and experiencing precede emotionally credible film language.
- Personal pressure as premise - [[ai-wuxian-rensheng-youxian-duitan-kk-shanyin-yixian-ai-chuangzuozhe-lmk4luckjgn-cpivd-7dy9vmsl0u]] connects 《阿明的一天》 to Shanyin's fragmented work life and another film, 《积木》, to his grandfather's final months with cognitive decline.
- Judgment as transformation - [[ai-wuxian-rensheng-youxian-duitan-kk-shanyin-yixian-ai-chuangzuozhe-lmk4luckjgn-cpivd-7dy9vmsl0u]] defines “眉头一皱的能力” as noticing generic or wrong AI output and discovering the creator's own answer through rejection.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The evidence comes from two practitioners and does not prove that AI-originated premises cannot move audiences or that high-volume creators lack depth. Experience alone can produce self-indulgent or poorly crafted work, and research, imagination, collaboration, historical material, and formal play can all extend creation beyond direct autobiography. The claim is narrower: abundant generation does not by itself replenish the human stakes and situated judgment needed for distinctive work.

## What Changed
- Created a creator-side concept separating nearly unlimited generative throughput from slowly accumulated lived material.
- Linked experiential input to both story formation and rejection-based judgment.
