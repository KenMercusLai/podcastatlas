## Definition

以直报怨式平反 / straight redress without revenge is the Hanji 770 pattern where a ruler answers a real grievance by restoring truth, status, and concrete treatment, while refusing to turn redress into total retaliation against a related person whose role still carries legitimate obligations.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 770 frames the problem with [[Laozi]]'s "repay resentment with virtue" and [[Confucius]]' "repay resentment with straightness, repay virtue with virtue." The source then applies that distinction to [[EmperorHeOfHan|Emperor He]] after he learns that [[LiangGuirenHeLateHan|Liang Guiren]] was his birth mother and had died under pressure from [[EmpressDowagerDouHeLateHan|Dou Taihou]].

The emperor's answer has two halves. He accepts [[ZhangPuEasternHan|Zhang Pu]]'s advice to recognize Liang Guiren, restore maternal relatives, rebury her, and honor her father. But when officials ask to remove Dou Taihou's title and deny burial with [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Emperor Zhang]], he refuses because he had served Dou as mother for ten years.

The concept therefore names a bounded correction. It is not passive forgiveness, because Liang and [[SongGuirenZhangHeLateHan|Song Guiren]] receive posthumous ritual repair. It is also not revenge, because Dou's adoptive-mother status and burial dignity remain protected. The political value lies in separating accountability to truth from symbolic humiliation.

## Key Claims

- Redress can be active without becoming retaliatory: Emperor He restores Liang Guiren's status while preserving Dou Taihou's title and burial relationship.
- The source's "straightness" means public ordering of claims and relationships, not emotional neutrality.
- Posthumous status is a governance problem because title, tomb placement, and sacrificial support decide whose role the court recognizes.
- A ruler may owe duties to multiple maternal relationships at once, especially when birth, adoption, and imperial succession are separated.
- The pattern has an aftereffect risk: repairing a wronged maternal line can elevate new outer relatives who later create another power problem.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept is currently grounded in one podcast source and should not be generalized to every historical redress case. It also does not prove Dou Taihou's full culpability record beyond the episode's claim. The host's favorable judgment of Emperor He's conduct is preserved as source-scoped evaluation, while the page's broader claim is limited to the structure of the settlement.

## What Changed

- Created the concept from Hanji 770's ethical frame and Emperor He's two-sided settlement.
