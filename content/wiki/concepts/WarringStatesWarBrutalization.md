# Warring States War Brutalization / 战国战争残酷化

Warring States war brutalization is the pattern in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-82-daqin-de-hewuqi-baiqi-laile-lo0w8mifkijhzmx7fdwcimmv7ade]] where interstate war no longer stops at defeating armies or taking cities. The episode uses [[BaiQi|白起]]'s attack on [[ChuState|楚国]] to show a lower wartime floor: the capital falls, Yiling is burned, ancestral tombs become targets, and a water attack produces mass drowning inside the city.

The concept matters because the source treats these acts as political and psychological warfare as well as military operations. Destroying or humiliating a royal ancestral site attacks the ruler's symbolic order; flooding a city collapses the boundary between siegecraft and civilian catastrophe; and [[NanCommandery|南郡]] shows that violent rupture can be followed by administrative absorption.

This page is source-scoped for now. It extends the wiki's [[WarringStatesMilitaryTransformation|Warring States military transformation]] branch by emphasizing brutality, terror, and institutional conquest rather than only force scale, tactics, or command efficiency.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-2-lvqctrazn8ddkcnp-lhspzzmzmns]] extends the brutality frame from attack on a city and ancestral landscape to starvation inside an encircled field army. After forty-six days without food, Zhao soldiers secretly kill and eat one another. The episode therefore adds deprivation and internal collapse to the wiki's account of how late-Warring-States war pushes beyond ordinary battle defeat.
