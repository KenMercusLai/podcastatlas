# Premature Ecosystem Build

Premature ecosystem build is the pattern where a team tries to create a whole platform, device, infrastructure layer, and service world before the first customer problem is narrow enough to carry the effort. In [[we-almost-had-a-smartphone-in-the-90s-why-did-it-fail]], [[GeneralMagic]] was building hardware, chips, operating-system software, network servers, server software, user interface, applications, and touchscreen technology at the same time.

The source does not say ecosystem ambition is always wrong. Its point is timing and sequencing: General Magic's smartphone-like vision was prescient, but the surrounding infrastructure and user habits were not yet ready, and the company had not narrowed the first product around a specific demand.

## Key Claims
- Platform ambition can hide the absence of a first product that customers understand.
- Building too many layers at once multiplies coordination and delays [[FastFeedbackLoops]].
- Ecosystem creation needs sequencing: a first wedge, real use, and then expansion.
- [[IPhone]] later made some General Magic ideas feel obvious because the market, infrastructure, and product frame had changed.
