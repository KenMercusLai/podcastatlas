# Normal Work Baseline / 正常工作基线

Normal work baseline / 正常工作基线 is [[ep244-jizhe-yanzhong-de-hao-gongzuo-shenme-yang-lk8iz9cllzx2jou5-sw9yt2ogpyb]]'s lowered version of [[GoodWorkNewStandard|good work]]. A job can count as merely normal when pay is not delayed, leaders do not abuse people, income covers basics, annual leave and sick leave exist, social insurance is present, and the worker can still grow.

The concept matters because the source treats several of these conditions as ordinary expectations that are often marketed as benefits. That turns job quality from a lifestyle-choice question into a labor-rights and organizational-health question.

## Key Claims
- Normal work is not glamorous; it is a minimum condition for dignity, stability, and future planning.
- When basic protections are uncertain, a "good job" discussion becomes a baseline labor-infrastructure discussion.
- Normal work includes both formal benefits and daily relational conditions such as not being humiliated or overcontrolled.
- Growth remains part of the baseline because a job that only extracts time can still trap the worker.
