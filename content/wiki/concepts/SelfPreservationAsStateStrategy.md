# Self-Preservation as State Strategy / 以自保包装国策

Self-preservation as state strategy / 以自保包装国策 is the maneuver [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-55-zhangyi-tongshi-guiren-qinhuiwang-lsclfbwn06fgzooipf5up9sdkedy]] identifies in [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]'s exit from [[QinState|秦国]] after [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] dies. Zhang Yi faces [[PatronSuccessionExposure|靠山更替暴露风险]] under [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] and cannot simply ask to flee, so he frames his departure as a way to help Qin.

The mechanism is the conversion of private danger into ruler-facing value. Zhang Yi says [[QiState|齐国]] hates him enough to attack whichever state accepts him; if Qin sends him to [[WeiState|魏国]], Qi will attack Wei, leaving Qin free to pressure [[HanState|韩国]], advance into [[SanchuanWarringStates|三川]], and threaten the [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]]. The host reads the attack-Han plan as a pretext, with Zhang Yi's real goal being distance from Qin's hostile court.

This concept is narrower than [[WarringStatesCareeristMobility|乱世职业经理人式流动]], which names the broader cross-court labor market for persuaders. It is also different from [[PowerExitTrap|权力退场困境]]: Shang Yang becomes trapped because leaving power strips away protection, while Zhang Yi finds an exit by making the new ruler see his departure as useful.

## Key Claims
- A threatened court actor can survive by translating a personal exit into the ruler's strategic language.
- The plan must give the successor a reason to authorize departure rather than merely tolerate retreat.
- Personal reputation can become a tool: Zhang Yi's hatred in Qi is a liability that he converts into a predicted diplomatic effect.
- The maneuver still depends on later execution; Zhang Yi must make Qi withdraw through [[YinmouYangmouConversion|阴谋阳谋转换]] after the war begins.
