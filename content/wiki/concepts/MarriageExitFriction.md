# Marriage Exit Friction

## Definition
Marriage exit friction is the combined legal, administrative, evidentiary, financial, cultural, and psychological cost of ending a marriage.

## Current Synthesis
Exit friction is cumulative rather than reducible to one waiting period. The sources show how a cooling-off rule can interact with repeated mutual-consent requirements, contested-divorce proof, litigation delay, property dependence, unpaid care, stigma, and uncertainty about whether the other spouse will cooperate. Some friction can create time for reconsideration or negotiation, but its value depends on whether it slows a rash decision or gives a coercive party another veto. Good family-law design therefore has to distinguish deliberation from entrapment and formal remedies from remedies that are safe, provable, timely, and affordable in practice.

## Key Claims
- A single neutral-looking procedural step can become a serious barrier when combined with evidence, money, housing, safety, or cooperation constraints.
- Continued mutual consent is not equivalent to free agreement when one spouse can use delay or withdrawal as leverage.
- Formal access to court does not erase friction created by proof standards, repeated filings, long timelines, and emotional exhaustion.
- Exit friction is gendered when income, assets, unpaid care, and exposure to violence are distributed unequally.
- Delay can support reconsideration in some relationships while increasing danger or coercive control in others.
- Making exit harder may reduce willingness to enter marriage, but these sources do not establish the population-wide magnitude of that response.

### Cumulative procedure and uncertainty
- [[ep158-cong-lihun-lengjingqi-kan-hunyin-ziyou-ckwrijed9jaoabaaaace9iqg]] traces a path from administrative application through cooling-off, renewed attendance, possible withdrawal, contested litigation, and proof requirements.

- [[ep158-cong-lihun-lengjingqi-kan-hunyin-ziyou-ckwrijed9jaoabaaaace9iqg]] uses domestic-violence, asset-opacity, lost-income, and child-care cases to show why identical rules can produce unequal burdens.
- [[fear-jerker-americas-ai-backlash-6a3cf783d760508ebaecd9fd]] adds property allocation, domestic-labor compensation, and cultural censorship to the mechanisms that shape usable exit.

### Entry effects and policy substitution
- [[fear-jerker-americas-ai-backlash-6a3cf783d760508ebaecd9fd]] argues that stronger exit barriers may weaken marriage entry and substitute for improving equality or relationship quality.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The practitioner source explicitly allows that waiting can help some couples reconsider, and neither source measures net policy effects. Administrative and judicial procedures differ by jurisdiction and change over time. Domestic violence, coercive control, ordinary conflict, regret, and under-negotiated separation require different safeguards; the concept should not assume every delay is abusive or every rapid exit is well considered.

## What Changed
- Expanded the concept from a list of barriers into a cumulative interaction among consent, proof, time, safety, and material dependence.
- Added psychological uncertainty and the strategic use of withdrawal or delay.
- Made the deliberation-versus-entrapment design test explicit.
