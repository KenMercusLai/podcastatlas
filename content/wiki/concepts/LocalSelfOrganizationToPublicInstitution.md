# Local Self-Organization to Public Institution / 地方自组织的公共机构转化

## Definition

Local self-organization to public institution is the conversion of a temporary or inherited community organization into a standing body with stable revenue, succession, and wider public functions.

## Current Synthesis

The [[BeibeiDistrict|重庆北碚]] case begins with militia traditions activated by disorder. After 1918, local leaders sought permanent defense capacity; [[HuNanxian|胡南仙]] defended local tax revenue, and [[LuZuofu|卢作孚]] later widened [[XiangfangBureau|峡防局]] into municipal, industrial, educational, and research work. The durable insight is functional conversion: modernization may reuse organizational legitimacy, networks, revenue, and coordination capacity rather than begin from institutional zero.

Reuse is not automatically legitimate or successful. A militia-derived institution can retain coercive habits, elite dominance, weak representation, and opaque taxation even while delivering public goods. The source also shows that local capacity has a scale boundary: expanded projects eventually sought more stable central administrative and fiscal support.

## Key Claims

- Existing organizations can lower the coordination cost of creating standing local capacity.
- Stable revenue and credible succession matter as much as visionary leadership.
- Functional conversion can preserve useful networks while changing an institution's purpose.
- Security capacity can become a platform for development, but coercive authority requires separate accountability.
- Local institutional autonomy can enable experimentation without being sufficient for larger-scale finance and administration.

## Evidence

- Crisis-to-standing transition: [[509-tongwang-zhanshi-peidu-zhi-lu-luzuofu-jianshe-chongqing-beibei-wangshi-lresn7lo3olbhoolqz6cl09su-wj]] contrasts episodic militia activity with permanent organization after 1918.
- Revenue and succession: [[509-tongwang-zhanshi-peidu-zhi-lu-luzuofu-jianshe-chongqing-beibei-wangshi-lresn7lo3olbhoolqz6cl09su-wj]] attributes fiscal stabilization and the 1926 handoff to [[HuNanxian|胡南仙]].
- Functional conversion: [[509-tongwang-zhanshi-peidu-zhi-lu-luzuofu-jianshe-chongqing-beibei-wangshi-lresn7lo3olbhoolqz6cl09su-wj]] says [[LuZuofu|卢作孚]] expanded [[XiangfangBureau|峡防局]] from anti-bandit defense to municipal, industrial, educational, and scientific functions.
- Scale boundary: [[509-tongwang-zhanshi-peidu-zhi-lu-luzuofu-jianshe-chongqing-beibei-wangshi-lresn7lo3olbhoolqz6cl09su-wj]] describes the later move toward a centrally supported experimental district.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

One successful-looking retrospective case cannot establish that inherited organizations generally modernize well. The episode underexamines coercion, representation, tax consent, elite capture, project failure, and resident resistance. Repurposing a traditional institution can preserve liabilities as well as capacity.

## What Changed

- Created the concept from the Beibei transition while making legitimacy and accountability separate tests from output.
