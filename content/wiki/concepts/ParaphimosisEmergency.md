# Paraphimosis Emergency / 包皮嵌顿急症

## Definition
Paraphimosis is the urgent condition in which a tight foreskin has been retracted behind the glans and cannot be returned to its normal position, creating a constricting ring with progressive swelling and possible compromise of blood flow.

## Current Synthesis
[[vol-78-shengzhi-yixueke-nanke-tade-baopi-gebuge-lai-ting-sanjia-yisheng-zenme-shuo-weishenme-hpv-jiancha-nansheng-hui-chang-chuxian-jiayinxing-lvg2m7yu6whcctt79kmvl38egnh|VOL.78]] separates this emergency from both a normally retractable long foreskin and phimosis that has not been forcibly retracted. The episode's practical signal is positional: after retraction or sexual activity, the foreskin is trapped behind the glans and cannot be brought forward again.

The constricting band can worsen edema and circulation, with color change described as progressing from red or purple toward black in severe cases. The source says clinicians may first attempt reduction and may need an operative release if that fails; the public-facing action is prompt emergency assessment, not forceful self-treatment.

## Key Claims
- Paraphimosis is distinct from uncomplicated long foreskin and from unretracted phimosis.
- A tight foreskin trapped behind the glans can act as a constricting band and worsen swelling.
- Increasing pain, swelling, inability to return the foreskin, or dark color change raises urgency because circulation may be threatened.
- The episode routes failed return promptly to emergency care, where reduction or operative release can be considered.
- After elective retraction, returning the foreskin to its normal position is a practical prevention step in the source's explanation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode does not provide a complete diagnostic examination, severity scale, contraindication list, or safe lay reduction protocol. Swelling, discoloration, severe pain, trauma, urinary difficulty, or inability to return a retracted foreskin needs urgent qualified assessment; forceful manipulation can worsen injury.
