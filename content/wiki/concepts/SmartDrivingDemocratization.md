# Smart Driving Democratization / 智驾平权

Smart driving democratization is the product claim that advanced driver-assistance or intelligent-driving features should move from expensive flagship cars into lower-priced mass-market vehicles. [[143-wei-xiao-li-de-gaoduan-tansuo-feng-tou-quan-1-143-1]] adds the concept through [[XPengMONA03]], whose marketing is summarized around "智驾平权" and fashion-oriented appeal to younger buyers.

The source treats the phrase as both a real product promise and a brand-risk mechanism. It can help [[XPeng]] win volume at a lower price point, but if the company's sales become too dependent on the cheapest intelligent-driving vehicle, the same success can make [[HighEndEVBranding]] harder.

## Key Claims
- Lower-priced intelligent-driving features can expand adoption by reducing the gap between flagship technology and ordinary buyers.
- The claim depends on user trust that the lower-priced implementation is useful and safe enough, not only labeled as smart.
- A democratization story can strengthen volume while weakening premium perception if the brand becomes remembered mainly for cheap access.
- The concept connects pricing to [[AutonomousDrivingDataFlywheel]] because more deployed vehicles can create more user feedback and driving data.
