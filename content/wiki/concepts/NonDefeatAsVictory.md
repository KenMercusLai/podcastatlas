# Non-Defeat as Victory / 不败即胜

## Definition

Non-defeat as victory / 不败即胜 is an asymmetric strategy in which a weaker participant replaces an unattainable conventional win condition with survival, denial, delay, or preserved autonomy, making the stronger participant bear the burden of decisive success.

## Current Synthesis

In [[BorrowedCorpseReturnsSoulGuLong|《借尸还魂》]], [[ChuLiuxiang|楚留香]] does not claim that he can overpower [[XueYirenGuLong|薛衣人]] in a direct sword contest. He announces that avoiding defeat will count as victory, then uses conversation to erode certainty, declines a weapon, and relies on movement rather than matched force. When the sword becomes lodged in a tree, he does not exploit the temporary opening with an attack; 薛衣人 concedes under the revised terms.

The strategy changes both action and interpretation. Evasion is no longer failure to fight properly, while the stronger swordsman's inability to finish becomes evidence relevant to the declared contest. Restraint matters too: refusing a cheap strike makes the outcome a test of control and dignity rather than merely a loophole victory.

## Key Claims

- Asymmetric competitors can improve their position by changing what counts as success before the contest unfolds.
- A survival or denial condition transfers pressure to the stronger side, which must now produce a decisive result.
- Psychological preparation can matter as much as physical technique when certainty and tempo support superior force.
- Evasion becomes positive performance only when it serves an explicit strategic condition.
- Restraint after gaining an opening can preserve legitimacy and the opponent's dignity.
- The revised result does not prove equality in raw capability.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

This concept is abstracted from a fictional duel in one podcast reading. The declared condition matters because the opponent accepts the encounter's moral logic; in real contests, institutions and other participants may reject a unilateral redefinition of victory. Survival, delay, or denial can also conceal failure if they are asserted only after the original objective is lost.
