# Bodily Resolution

## Definition
Bodily resolution is Portal's term for fine-grained bodily perception, extended in the episode to the broader capacity to perceive emotional, conceptual, social, and spatial experience with usable detail rather than coarse labels.

## Current Synthesis
The Portal episode treats resolution as something that can deteriorate or improve with use. Without novelty and attention, bodily schemas can become coarse, and emotional life can collapse toward black-and-white categories. With practice, the person can develop finer distinctions in sensation, movement, relationship, language, ambiguity, and aesthetic experience.

The concept is broader than vocabulary. Words can help point to experience, but the source stresses that words are not complete containers. Symbolic texts, parables, difficult films, contemporary dance, music, silence, and live bodies can all train contact with ambiguity and incompleteness, helping a person tolerate experience that cannot be fully reduced to a clean explanation.

## Key Claims
- Bodily perception can lose resolution when novelty and attention are absent.
- Resolution is not only physical; emotional, conceptual, social, and spatial schemas can also become coarse or refined.
- Emotional granularity depends on lived perception and language, but words remain pointers rather than complete containers.
- Ambiguity, incompleteness, art, and difficult media can train finer contact with experience.
- Depression and rigid states can narrow emotional life toward simplified black-and-white categories.
- Changing body schema can change movement and felt experience, but that claim remains source-scoped and context-dependent.

- Language and ambiguity: [[movement-practice-to-strengthen-your-mind-body-connection-ido-portal-scim2496765743]] has Huberman discuss nuanced emotional language and Portal say ambiguity can be trained through symbolic texts, parables, difficult films, and contemporary dance.
- Body-schema change: [[movement-practice-to-strengthen-your-mind-body-connection-ido-portal-scim2496765743]] discusses Feldenkrais, body illusions, and body-model changes as ways movement and experiences such as back pain can shift.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not establish a validated clinical scale for bodily resolution or show that art, movement, or language practices treat depression, pain, or trauma. The strongest claim is a practice-based account of granularity and model flexibility.
