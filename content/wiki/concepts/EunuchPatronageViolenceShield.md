## Definition

Eunuch patronage violence shield / 宦官庇护下的地方暴行 is the late-Han pattern in which a locally punishable offense becomes politically protected because the offender, target, or complaint channel has palace-connected eunuch access, causing enforcement to risk reversal unless the ruler accepts the enforcing official's legal framing.

## Current Synthesis

The current synthesis now has an early countercase before the stronger reversal branches. Hanji 869 shows [[ZhangShuoLateHan|张硕]], brother of [[ZhangRangLateHan|张让]], hiding in Zhang Rang's Luoyang house after corrupt and violent conduct as Yewang magistrate. [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]] captures and executes him, and Zhang Rang complains to [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]]. Here the shield fails: Huan accepts Li Ying's defense, treats Zhang Shuo's crimes as self-caused, and leaves Li Ying's enforcement standing. The branch clarifies that eunuch connection creates shelter and complaint access, but not automatic impunity.

Hanji 871 gives the first rebound chain where the shield works more fully. [[ZhangFanWanLateHan|张汎]] uses harem ties, luxury gifts, and palace access to become locally dominant at Wan; [[ChengJinLateHan|成瑨]], urged by [[CenZhiLateHan|岑晊]] and [[ZhangMuLateHan|张牧]], kills him and a large network after an amnesty. In Taiyuan, [[LiuZhiTaiyuanLateHan|刘质]] sends [[WangYunLateHan|王允]] to arrest and kill [[ZhaoJinLateHan|赵津]], a small yellow-gate eunuch whose conduct harms a county. [[HouLanLateHan|侯览]] then uses Zhang Fan's wife's petition and eunuch complaint channels to help turn both enforcement actions against Cheng Jin and Liu Zhi.

Hanji 872 gives two more branches. In the Hou Lan branch, [[ZhangJianLateHan|张俭]] attacks illegal property and tomb-building, but Hou Lan can intercept memorials before they reach the ruler. In the [[XuXuanLateHan|徐宣]] branch, private sexual coercion and killing receive political protection because Xu Xuan is tied to [[XuHuangLateHan|徐璜]].

The concept is not just "corrupt official commits crime." Its distinctive mechanism is inversion. [[HuangFuDonghaiLateHan|黄浮]] executes the offender, [[ZhaiChaoLateHan|翟超]] and Zhang Jian stand on the enforcement side, and [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] argues they acted for the people. Yet Huan later punishes the enforcers after eunuch complaint, so the court response teaches officials that law can be more dangerous than complicity when palace access is captured.

## Key Claims

- Local violence becomes harder to punish when the offender, target, or surviving complainant can reach the palace.
- The shield works through both physical shelter and privileged complaint channels.
- The shield is not automatic; enforcement can survive if the ruler accepts the legal framing before the eunuch grievance controls interpretation.
- Enforcement can be inverted into offense when the ruler receives the patron's grievance before the victim-side evidence.
- Subordinates can correctly read the danger of enforcement even when the law-enforcement act is morally and administratively justified.
- Remonstrance after the fact may diagnose the institution, but diagnosis still fails if the ruler preserves the favorite channel.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Hanji 869 is a built-in qualification: Zhang Shuo has eunuch-family shelter and Zhang Rang's complaint reaches the ruler, but the enforcement still stands. The concept therefore tracks a risk mechanism, not an iron law. It remains grounded in the Hanji 869-872 source window and should not be generalized to every eunuch relationship or every late-Han local crime. The later Zhang Jian and Hou Lan pages cover 169 CE accusation and flight under Emperor Ling; this concept tracks the enforcement-inversion mechanism, not a complete chronology of every subsequent party-prohibition consequence.

## What Changed

- Hanji 869 adds a countercase where eunuch-family shelter and complaint access fail because Huan backs Li Ying's enforcement.
- The definition now treats ruler-side acceptance of the eunuch grievance as the condition that turns protection risk into actual reversal.
- Zhang Shuo is added as the early failed-shield branch before the Hanji 871-872 successful rebound cases.
