## Definition

Eunuch patronage violence shield / 宦官庇护下的地方暴行 is the late-Han pattern in which a locally punishable offense, corrupt acquisition, witness-suppression attempt, or law-enforcement case becomes politically protected because the offender, target, or complaint channel has palace-connected eunuch access, causing enforcement to risk reversal unless the ruler accepts the enforcing official's legal framing.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-851-hanhuandi-weihe-zhuding-yichou-wannian-ltgm1ix6cgqb-kqo9bztfrzjgeun|Hanji 851]] now adds the earliest current branch, a 159 CE witness-suppression case. [[ShanKuangLateHan|单匡]] uses [[ShanChaoLateHan|单超]]'s power while serving as Jiyin administrator; [[FifthZhongLateHan|第五种]] and [[WeiYuLateHan|魏宇]] expose huge corruption; Shan Kuang hires [[RenFangLateHan|任芳]] to kill Wei Yu and then seeks a jailbreak after Ren Fang is caught. [[YangBingLateHan|杨秉]]'s request to bring Shan Kuang to Luoyang is punished, while Shan Chao later turns [[ShusunWujiLateHan|叔孙无忌]]'s disorder into a charge against Fifth Zhong. This shows the shield protecting not only completed violence but also investigation and witness handling.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-853-jiemi-hanchao-ban-de-zhaoshi-gu-er-lvsa2pby8iyfevfoobobpik52mij|Hanji 853]] then gives a servant-and-retainer reversal. [[HouLanLateHan|侯览]] and [[DuanGuiLateHan|段珪]] have servants and guests who rob travelers near Jibei; [[TengYanLateHan|滕延]] executes dozens of them, but the protected side complains to [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] and Teng Yan is dismissed.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-865-zhichang-boss-ye-rongyi-bei-zhezhong-ren-beici-ljfhfevljqnx6zceakjfbw5gwfma|Hanji 865]] supplies a successful breach. [[HouCanLateHan|侯参]] relies on Hou Lan's power while serving as Yizhou inspector, but Yang Bing turns the corruption case into a precedent-backed impeachment of Hou Lan. Huan removes Hou Lan after Yang Bing's side defeats the claim that palace eunuchs sit outside Three Excellencies oversight.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-866-zhenzheng-huomingbai-de-ren-dongde-sanbuzhan-lhhmmyjfo6mkf-qs6bhhzu8hhln6|Hanji 866]] shows familiar reversal forms: [[YangYuanqunLateHan|羊元群]] bribes eunuchs so [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]] becomes the punished investigator; [[LiuYouLateHan|刘祐]] is punished after trying to confiscate underpriced holdings acquired by [[SuKangLateHan|苏康]] and [[GuanBaLateHan|管霸]]; and [[FengGunLateHan|冯绲]] is punished after a protected-relative custody case is turned against him.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-869-liying-weihe-bei-chengwei-tianxia-diyi-kaimo-lltckcioe1n9v91yy4c9vvwmz4mo|Hanji 869]] provides an important countercase. [[ZhangShuoLateHan|张硕]], brother of [[ZhangRangLateHan|张让]], hides in Zhang Rang's Luoyang house, but Li Ying captures and executes him and Huan rejects Zhang Rang's complaint. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-871-cheng-e-yang-shan-weihe-fanbei-wuxian-ruyu-lujkihgvh-cafkh9bfak6-vttpwt|Hanji 871]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] then return to stronger shield cases: [[ZhangFanWanLateHan|张汎]], [[ZhaoJinLateHan|赵津]], and [[XuXuanLateHan|徐宣]] show local harm being protected through harem ties, eunuch status, kinship, petitions, complaint channels, or intercepted memorials.

The concept is not just "corrupt official commits crime." Its distinctive mechanism is inversion. Li Ying, Feng Gun, Liu You, [[HuangFuDonghaiLateHan|黄浮]], [[ZhaiChaoLateHan|翟超]], Zhang Jian, Yang Bing, Wei Yu, and Fifth Zhong stand on the enforcement or evidence side in different branches, yet court response can punish the enforcer, block the report, or endanger the witness.

## Key Claims

- Local violence, corrupt acquisition, and witness suppression become harder to punish when the offender, patron, retainer, or surviving complainant can reach the palace.
- The shield works through physical shelter, privileged complaint channels, document interception, and control over whether evidence reaches confrontation.
- Eunuch protection can extend through kinship and household networks, not only through eunuch officeholders themselves.
- The shield is not automatic; enforcement can survive if the ruler accepts the legal framing before the eunuch grievance controls interpretation.
- Senior ministers can sometimes pierce the shield by tying a concrete abuse case to office jurisdiction and precedent.
- Enforcement can be inverted into offense when the ruler receives the patron's grievance before the victim-side evidence.
- Subordinates can correctly read the danger of enforcement even when the law-enforcement act is morally and administratively justified.

## Evidence

- Witness-suppression branch: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-851-hanhuandi-weihe-zhuding-yichou-wannian-ltgm1ix6cgqb-kqo9bztfrzjgeun|Hanji 851]] says Shan Kuang's corruption report is followed by his hiring of Ren Fang to kill Wei Yu.
- Failed confrontation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-851-hanhuandi-weihe-zhuding-yichou-wannian-ltgm1ix6cgqb-kqo9bztfrzjgeun|Hanji 851]] says Yang Bing is punished after asking to bring Shan Kuang to Luoyang.
- Inspector retaliation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-851-hanhuandi-weihe-zhuding-yichou-wannian-ltgm1ix6cgqb-kqo9bztfrzjgeun|Hanji 851]] says Shan Chao uses Shusun Wuji's disorder to frame Fifth Zhong and send him toward exile danger.
- Servant-and-retainer reversal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-853-jiemi-hanchao-ban-de-zhaoshi-gu-er-lvsa2pby8iyfevfoobobpik52mij|Hanji 853]] says Hou Lan and Duan Gui's servants and guests rob travelers near Jibei and Teng Yan is dismissed after punishing them.
- Precedent-backed breach: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-865-zhichang-boss-ye-rongyi-bei-zhezhong-ren-beici-ljfhfevljqnx6zceakjfbw5gwfma|Hanji 865]] says Yang Bing's impeachment of Hou Can becomes successful pressure against Hou Lan after Three Excellencies authority is defended.
- Corruption-bribery and property reversals: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-866-zhenzheng-huomingbai-de-ren-dongde-sanbuzhan-lhhmmyjfo6mkf-qs6bhhzu8hhln6|Hanji 866]] says Yang Yuanqun, Su Kang, Guan Ba, and a protected-relative custody case turn enforcement back against Li Ying, Liu You, and Feng Gun.
- Failed shield case: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-869-liying-weihe-bei-chengwei-tianxia-diyi-kaimo-lltckcioe1n9v91yy4c9vvwmz4mo|Hanji 869]] says Zhang Shuo hides in Zhang Rang's house, but Li Ying captures and executes him and Emperor Huan rejects Zhang Rang's complaint.
- Complaint-channel rebound: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-871-cheng-e-yang-shan-weihe-fanbei-wuxian-ruyu-lujkihgvh-cafkh9bfak6-vttpwt|Hanji 871]] says Hou Lan uses Zhang Fan's wife's petition and eunuch complaint channels to accuse Cheng Jin and Liu Zhi after enforcement actions.
- Blocked reporting and protected offender: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] says Hou Lan intercepts Zhang Jian's memorials and that Xu Xuan's kinship tie to Xu Huang protects a violent Xiapi case until enforcement rebounds against Huang Fu and Zhai Chao.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Hanji 865 and Hanji 869 are built-in qualifications. In Hanji 865, Yang Bing pierces the shield through a senior-minister jurisdiction argument; in Hanji 869, Zhang Shuo has eunuch-family shelter and Zhang Rang's complaint reaches the ruler, but Li Ying's enforcement still stands. Hanji 851 is stronger for mechanism than final adjudication: it does not show a formal acquittal of Shan Kuang or the full outcome of Ren Fang's custody. The concept tracks a risk mechanism, not an iron law, and remains grounded in the listed Huan-era source notes.

## What Changed

- Hanji 851 adds a 159 CE witness-suppression and failed-confrontation branch through Shan Kuang, Wei Yu, Ren Fang, Yang Bing, and Fifth Zhong.
- The concept now explicitly includes evidence control and witness danger, not only completed violence, property extraction, or complaint-channel reversal.
- Hanji 853, Hanji 865, Hanji 866, Hanji 869, Hanji 871, and Hanji 872 remain as later reversal, breach, countercase, and rebound layers.
