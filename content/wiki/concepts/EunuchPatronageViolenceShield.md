## Definition

Eunuch patronage violence shield / 宦官庇护下的地方暴行 is the late-Han pattern in which a locally punishable offense becomes politically protected because the offender or target has a palace-connected eunuch patron, causing enforcement to rebound against the officials who act.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] gives the source case through two linked branches. In the [[HouLanLateHan|侯览]] branch, [[ZhangJianLateHan|张俭]] attacks illegal property and tomb-building, but Hou Lan can intercept memorials before they reach the ruler. In the [[XuXuanLateHan|徐宣]] branch, private sexual coercion and killing receive political protection because Xu Xuan is tied to [[XuHuangLateHan|徐璜]].

The concept is not just "corrupt official commits crime." Its distinctive mechanism is inversion. [[HuangFuDonghaiLateHan|黄浮]] executes the offender, [[ZhaiChaoLateHan|翟超]] and Zhang Jian stand on the enforcement side, and [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] argues they acted for the people. Yet [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] punishes the enforcers after eunuch complaint, so the court response teaches officials that law can be more dangerous than complicity when palace access is captured.

## Key Claims

- Local violence becomes harder to punish when the offender's family tie reaches the palace.
- The shield works through both blocked upward reports and privileged complaint channels.
- Enforcement can be inverted into offense when the ruler receives the patron's grievance before the victim-side evidence.
- Subordinates can correctly read the danger of enforcement even when the law-enforcement act is morally and administratively justified.
- Remonstrance after the fact may diagnose the institution, but diagnosis still fails if the ruler preserves the favorite channel.

## Evidence

- Blocked reporting: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] says Hou Lan intercepts Zhang Jian's memorials against his household wealth, buildings, tomb, and related abuses.
- Protected offender: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] says Xu Xuan is Xu Huang's nephew and uses his Xiapi position violently against Li Gao's household.
- Risk-aware enforcement: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] says Huang Fu's subordinates warn him before he executes Xu Xuan.
- Retaliation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] says Emperor Huan punishes Huang Fu and Zhai Chao after eunuch complaint.
- Institutional diagnosis: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] says Chen Fan identifies the larger danger as inner-court favorite power and blocked忠言 rather than ordinary external disorder.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

This concept is grounded in one source case and should not be generalized to every eunuch relationship or every late-Han local crime. The source also preserves a chronology caution: the supplied episode note dates the material to 166 CE, while later Zhang Jian and Hou Lan pages cover the 169 CE accusation and flight sequence. The concept tracks the enforcement-inversion mechanism, not a complete chronology of every subsequent party-prohibition consequence.

## What Changed

- Hanji 872 creates the concept from the Zhang Jian/Hou Lan blocked-memorial branch and the Xu Xuan/Huang Fu punishment branch.
