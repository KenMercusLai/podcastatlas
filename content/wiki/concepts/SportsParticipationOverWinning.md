## Definition
Sports participation over winning / 上场比赢重要 is the frame that treats entering the field, acting under real conditions, and gaining experience as a prior value to the final result.

## Current Synthesis
The Zhao Lina source does not deny competition, scoring, or winning. It argues that a result becomes meaningful only after a person has been present enough to act: a child joins football before knowing whether they are elite, a reserve goalkeeper feels the stadium before becoming a starter, a nervous athlete often calms once the whistle starts play, and a retired player can use "getting on field" as a broader life rule. In the wiki, this concept links sports fandom, youth access, embodied training, and post-retirement identity under one participation-first claim.

## Key Claims
- Participation can begin before expertise; watching live, gathering with friends, wearing or discussing football culture, and playing small-sided games all create entry points.
- "Getting on field" changes the unit of evaluation because even a loss can become embodied experience, feedback, and future confidence.
- The frame shifts football development from elite extraction toward ordinary access for children and communities.
- It extends action-first agency concepts because the person does not wait until fear, uncertainty, or imperfect skill disappears before acting.
- It qualifies sports lifestyle and fandom ideas by naming a non-commercial value: being present with other people and one's own body.

## Evidence
- Entry before expertise: [[zhao-lina-zaichang-bi-ying-zhongyao-menjiang-de-xinxin-zhijue-yu-reai-229fde7d-d39d-4f7c-a33d-607f5370fc60]] says non-fans can start with live atmosphere, social viewing, players, teams, clothing, or highlights rather than full tactical comprehension.
- Playing as evaluation threshold: [[zhao-lina-zaichang-bi-ying-zhongyao-menjiang-de-xinxin-zhijue-yu-reai-229fde7d-d39d-4f7c-a33d-607f5370fc60]] closes by converting competitive sport into a life frame where the priority is to get on field first.
- Youth and community access: [[zhao-lina-zaichang-bi-ying-zhongyao-menjiang-de-xinxin-zhijue-yu-reai-229fde7d-d39d-4f7c-a33d-607f5370fc60]] links meaningful football to children, schools, amateur competitions, local atmospheres, and non-professional future roles.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Elite sport still requires selection, competition, scoring, and result pressure; treating participation as sufficient can hide unequal coaching, venue, time, and family resources. The current evidence is one reflective interview, so the concept should stay source-scoped until other sports cases test it.
