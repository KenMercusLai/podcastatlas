# Mentalizing Projection / 心智化投射

## Definition
Mentalizing projection is E46's social-cognition mechanism where the mind models another person's knowledge, motives, and intentions using materials drawn from its own experience, fears, and desires. The capacity makes group life possible, but it can also turn another person into a self-confirming model.

## Current Synthesis
The concept belongs to the fourth layer of [[EvolutionaryMisrecognition]]. Group-living primates need to infer what others see, know, and intend, but the machinery for understanding others grows out of machinery for understanding oneself. That means the model of the other person is never cleanly external. In [[GoodFriendsFilm|《好朋友们》]], Hyun-tae's image of In-chul as someone who abandons friends can be read as a projection of Hyun-tae's own fear and conduct.

Episode 468 adds a trauma-and-shame application. When a person feels unsafe, mentalizing can narrow rather than expand: a classmate's seating choice, a romantic rival, or a social omission is read through one hostile or self-devaluing explanation. Steve's corrective is to deliberately generate multiple possible motives so the other person becomes psychologically complex again and the self is not automatically blamed.

## Key Claims
- Social cognition requires building models of other minds rather than only observing behavior.
- Those models borrow from self-experience, so projection is structurally likely.
- Interpersonal models are harder to falsify than many physical models because relationships generate ambiguous evidence.
- Once a person is modeled as a type, later actions can be filtered to confirm that type.
- Mentalizing is therefore both a cooperative capacity and a source of misrecognition.
- Felt insecurity can degrade mentalizing by making one hostile or shame-based interpretation feel certain.
- Multiple-explanation practice can restore humility by separating observed behavior from guessed motive.

## Evidence
- Evolutionary layer: [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] places mentalizing in primate group life, where status, food, mates, alliances, and betrayal all require reading others.
- Self-derived material: [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] says the machine for understanding others is built from the model for understanding oneself.
- Film application: [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] treats Hyun-tae's suspicion of In-chul as generated from Hyun-tae's own hidden abandonment and fear.
- Insecurity application: [[shi-di-fu-shuo-468-zixunshi-huixin-duzi-nuli-zhangda-de-ren-rongyi-xianru-quanneng-zilian-de-xianjing-llwra0a8mt-ocndm9eokzxaryb-j]] says unsafe states can reduce mentalizing so a person sees behavior but not the range of motives behind it.
- Corrective practice: [[shi-di-fu-shuo-468-zixunshi-huixin-duzi-nuli-zhangda-de-ren-rongyi-xianru-quanneng-zilian-de-xianjing-llwra0a8mt-ocndm9eokzxaryb-j]] asks the listener to imagine several reasons for others' behavior instead of defaulting to isolation or inferiority as the only explanation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Mentalizing projection does not mean other people are unknowable or that every suspicion is self-projection. The sources' caution is narrower: models of others need humility because they can mix evidence with unrecognized self-material, old danger, shame, and current body state.
