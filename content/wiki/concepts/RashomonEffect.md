# Rashomon Effect / 罗生门效应

Rashomon effect / 罗生门效应 is the pattern where multiple accounts of the same event conflict, each account can feel internally plausible, and no final public truth fully settles the matter. [[17-diyubian-zhongsheng-jie-ku-yi-nian-ru-mo-561780565]] adds the concept through [[InAGrove|《竹林中》]] and [[RashomonFilm|黑泽明《罗生门》]].

The episode's useful distinction is source layering. [[RashomonAkutagawa|《罗生门》]] and [[InAGrove|《竹林中》]] are separate [[AkutagawaRyunosuke|芥川龙之介]] stories; [[AkiraKurosawa|黑泽明]]'s film joins them so powerfully that the film title becomes the global shorthand for truth fracture. In the wiki, the concept should preserve both facts: the cultural term is "Rashomon," but the testimony machine largely comes from 《竹林中》.

## Key Claims
- Contradictory testimony can reveal self-justification rather than only factual disagreement.
- A later adaptation can rename how a literary technique is remembered.
- The effect is not simply "someone is lying"; shame, desire, reputation, and narrative position can each shape what truth becomes speakable.
- A fourth or later viewpoint can feel like truth without escaping the same reliability problem.
