# Gratitude Resentment Return Frame / 感恩怨恨回向框架

Gratitude resentment return frame / 感恩怨恨回向框架 is [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-161-caiyun-he-renji-guanxi-de-liangzi-jiuchan-2-lttnuxlypca4pafz1mzdr3aizfcv|Hanji 161 part 2]]'s claim that gratitude, resentment, kind speech, hostile speech, empathy, and jealousy feed back into the speaker's own life. The host frames this as "回向": appreciation and good words are said to raise life energy and form good ties, while resentment and harsh language return as injury, illness, or bad conditions.

The concept belongs inside [[RelationalFateNetwork|关系命运网络]] because the episode treats speech and emotion as relationship events rather than private inner weather. It also extends [[QuantumEntanglementFateMetaphor|量子纠缠命运隐喻]] by making ordinary comments, criticism, compassion, and online language part of the source's non-isolated field of cause.

The wiki keeps this source-scoped. The useful practical pattern is that repeated language changes the social environment and the speaker's own attention. The stronger spiritual claim that all resentment returns as fate or illness is recorded as the host's worldview rather than as settled psychology or medicine.

## Key Claims
- Gratitude is treated as an outward act that also changes the grateful person's own condition.
- Resentment and hostile speech are treated as relational harm that returns to the speaker.
- Online comments matter because public language can comfort, strengthen, shame, or injure at scale.
- The frame turns criticism and jealousy into occasions for self-observation rather than only diagnosis of others.
- The concept sits between spiritual "回向" language and ordinary social feedback from repeated speech.
