# Chinese Standard EMU

Chinese Standard EMU is the standardization program in [[no-201-zhongguo-gaotie-jianshi-976314772]] that leads from mixed [[Hexiehao]] platforms to [[Fuxinghao]]. The episode says the older Hexiehao family unified branding but still carried different foreign technology lineages, consoles, parts, and rescue-compatibility constraints.

The concept matters because it turns technology absorption into standard-setting. After the [[ChinaRailwayCorporation]] era began, Chinese railway actors worked to unify interfaces, components, operating practices, and platform architecture. In the source, [[Fuxinghao]] and later [[CR450]] are proof points that [[ChineseHighSpeedRail]] had moved beyond importing trainsets toward defining its own high-speed rail standard.

## Key Claims
- Imported platforms can solve launch speed while creating long-term compatibility problems.
- Standardization is a capability layer after technology transfer, not a paperwork exercise.
- Domestic standards become more credible when they reduce energy use, drag, interoperability friction, and rescue complexity.
