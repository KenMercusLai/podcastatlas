# AI Fitness Coaching

AI fitness coaching is the consumer-wellness pattern added by [[tech-20260204-0204-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260204-0204-mp-tech-pod-128]]: chatbots, wearables, apps, and connected hardware use AI to suggest workouts, adapt plans, motivate users, and sometimes evaluate movement. The episode compares free chatbot plans with [[AppleWorkoutBuddy]], [[FitbitAIHealthCoach]], and [[Peloton]] to show that the category ranges from cheap generic advice to expensive hardware-mediated feedback.

The concept is useful when AI lowers the starting barrier for beginners, adapts to personal context, or makes exercise feedback more immediate. Its limits are equally important: [[AIHallucination]], camera errors, hardware costs, subscription walls, and [[AIFitnessAccountabilityGap]] mean the system can guide and motivate without reliably replacing human trainers.
