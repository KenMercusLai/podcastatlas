# Original Character Commission Economy / 设圈约稿经济

Original character commission economy is the market described by [[WangYiranSanlian|王依然]] in [[ep242-duli-youxi-shi-yitiao-gaoqian-hao-saidao-ma-lrqbzrceumbuggzuero5qb4jv3zu]]. Participants create or maintain original characters, commission setting images, write stories around them, and sometimes treat the character as part of their online identity.

The source connects this economy to [[Mihuashi|米画师]], QQ groups, independent-game art, commercial-mark design, pre-made character auctions, OC games, and [[BJDDollMakeupCommission|BJD妆面约稿]]. It is a small but useful example of [[SkillBasedSideIncome]] because buyers are often paying for taste, fidelity, style, and trust, not only for an interchangeable image file.

## Key Claims
- Character design can function as identity infrastructure for online life.
- Commission markets turn drawing, design, and makeup skills into small-service income.
- Games and OC communities overlap when players want their characters represented inside a playable world.
- Obvious AI-generated art can damage reputation in this market because the buyer expected human craft and accountability.
