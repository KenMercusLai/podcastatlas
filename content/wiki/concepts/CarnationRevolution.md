# Carnation Revolution

## Definition

The Carnation Revolution was the 24-25 April 1974 military overthrow of Portugal's [[EstadoNovoPortugal|Estado Novo]], named for flowers placed in soldiers' rifles and vehicles.

## Current Synthesis

The coup was unusually rapid and comparatively bloodless because the [[ArmedForcesMovementPortugal|Armed Forces Movement]] coordinated key units while most loyalist forces defected or declined to fight. Its peaceful iconography is historically important, but the coup should be distinguished from the longer and more conflictual [[PortugueseRevolutionaryProcess|revolutionary process]] it opened.

## Key Claims

- Two radio songs served as staged operational signals to conspirators.
- Captain Salgueiro Maia led troops into Lisbon after framing the choice as an end to the existing state.
- Government military resistance largely collapsed through defection rather than battle.
- Caetano's rank-sensitive surrender required [[AntonioDeSpinola|Spinola]] to receive it.
- Four deaths caused by secret-police gunfire qualify the common "bloodless" label.
- Carnations turned military takeover into a durable public image of peaceful liberation.

## Evidence

- Operational evidence: [[273-portugal-the-carnation-revolution-glt3297148398]] follows the song signals, troop movement, mass defection, and Largo do Carmo surrender.
- Casualty evidence: [[273-portugal-the-carnation-revolution-glt3297148398]] records four deaths from secret-police fire rather than none.
- Symbolic evidence: [[273-portugal-the-carnation-revolution-glt3297148398]] recounts carnations placed in rifles while qualifying the restaurant-anniversary origin story.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source calls the takeover unusually bloodless, not casualty-free. The familiar account of where the flowers came from may be partly mythologized, and the democratic endpoint was not guaranteed on 25 April.

## What Changed

- Added a distinction between the short coup, its peaceful symbolism, and the unstable transition that followed.
