# Imperial Examination Opportunity Equality / 科举与机会平等

Imperial examination opportunity equality is [[150-zhongguo-ruhe-tiaochu-pinkun-xianjing-xiandaihua-zhi-lu-you-hui-zouxiang-hefang-lgpvp04rngagkpubte2ac6i5ji3g]]'s modernization-history reading of the 科举 system. Through [[LiLu|李璐]], the episode treats the exam as a major opportunity-equality device inside agricultural civilization: it offered a public path into status and official service that was not purely hereditary.

The same source also stresses institutional aging. A mechanism that once widened opportunity can become conservative when it narrows knowledge, rewards textual orthodoxy, and blocks the scientific, product, and market experimentation needed for [[ModernizationAsCompoundGrowth]]. This extends but differs from [[ImperialExaminationAsOnlyExit]], which focuses more on the social damage of one credential path.

## Key Claims
- Opportunity equality can be a real institutional innovation even before modern market society.
- Institutions can change function as their environment changes.
- The source treats 科举 as both achievement and constraint: it helped order agricultural society but could not generate the [[FreeMarketScientificLoop]].
