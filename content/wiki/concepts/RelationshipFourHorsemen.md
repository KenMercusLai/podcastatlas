# Relationship Four Horsemen / 关系四骑士

## Definition
Relationship Four Horsemen are the four destructive communication patterns the source attributes to the Gottman relationship framework: criticism, contempt, defensiveness, and stonewalling.

## Current Synthesis
The Culture Limited episode uses the four horsemen as a compact warning vocabulary for relationship breakdown. The value of the concept is that it turns "we fight a lot" into distinguishable interaction patterns: attacking character, looking down on the other person, redirecting responsibility, or withdrawing from communication entirely.

Contempt is the hinge in the source's account. Criticism, defensiveness, and stonewalling can all damage repair, but contempt most directly attacks the respect and admiration that intimate repair needs. That makes the concept a bridge between communication practice and deeper relationship valuation.

## Key Claims
- Criticism becomes dangerous when it attacks the person rather than naming a specific problem.
- Contempt adds superiority, mockery, or disgust and therefore corrodes respect.
- Defensiveness blocks responsibility by making the partner the whole problem.
- Stonewalling shuts down communication rather than repairing conflict.
- These patterns matter most when they become habits rather than one-off reactions.
- Contempt is the strongest warning sign in the source because it undermines the premise that the partner deserves care.

## Evidence
- Four-pattern definition: [[vol-349-yexu-ni-gai-zhao-ge-ren-liaoliao-shuochu-tongku-huode-yongqi-gkwrimaoywpmaycmmatldgrv]] names criticism, contempt, defensiveness, and stonewalling as the four horsemen.
- Criticism boundary: [[vol-349-yexu-ni-gai-zhao-ge-ren-liaoliao-shuochu-tongku-huode-yongqi-gkwrimaoywpmaycmmatldgrv]] distinguishes criticism as person- or behavior-directed negation.
- Contempt boundary: [[vol-349-yexu-ni-gai-zhao-ge-ren-liaoliao-shuochu-tongku-huode-yongqi-gkwrimaoywpmaycmmatldgrv]] associates contempt with mockery, sarcasm, and a high-low posture.
- Defensiveness and stonewalling: [[vol-349-yexu-ni-gai-zhao-ge-ren-liaoliao-shuochu-tongku-huode-yongqi-gkwrimaoywpmaycmmatldgrv]] describes defensiveness as pushing responsibility onto the other person and stonewalling as avoiding communication.
- Habit and prediction: [[vol-349-yexu-ni-gai-zhao-ge-ren-liaoliao-shuochu-tongku-huode-yongqi-gkwrimaoywpmaycmmatldgrv]] says the horsemen become relationship-breakdown signals once they become communication habits.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept is a public-facing relationship warning frame, not a diagnostic label for a partner or a substitute for couple therapy. It should not be used to flatten coercion, abuse, fear, or safety risks into symmetrical "communication problems."
