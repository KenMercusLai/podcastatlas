# Static Ranking and Dynamic Performance / 静态排名与动态表现

## Definition

Static ranking and dynamic performance is the distinction between an ordering produced from observed ability at one time and the outcome produced later under changing condition, opportunity, incentives, and interaction.

## Current Synthesis

The bounded fictional case is [[WeaponsRankingGuLong|兵器谱]]. Its upper ranks remain informative, but they cannot update for aging, practice, injury, confidence, obsession, concealed technique, or new entrants. A result can therefore overturn the order without proving the original list random: [[ShangguanJinhongGuLong|上官金虹]] improves as 天机老人 declines, while [[JingWumingGuLong|荆无命]] and [[AFeiGuLong|阿飞]] reveal ability the list did not capture.

Performance is also relational. [[LiXunhuan|李寻欢]] may never miss once he releases the knife yet remain unable to release it against an opponent who controls timing and space. Test exchanges, mortal combat, technical execution, and access to an opportunity are different measurement conditions. The concept therefore treats rankings as bounded summaries rather than self-executing predictions.

## Key Claims

- A ranking can contain useful signal while becoming stale as participants improve, age, recover, or decline.
- Outcome depends on state and matchup as well as general reputation or prior placement.
- Reliable execution of one technique does not guarantee access to the conditions required to use it.
- Hidden ability and excluded populations create coverage error, not merely measurement error.
- Public belief can make an imperfect ranking socially consequential by shaping confidence, pressure, and behavior.
- An upset should trigger causal analysis before either preserving the hierarchy unchanged or declaring all comparison meaningless.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The current evidence is one fictional and interpretive case, so the concept should not be generalized into a validated model of sport, hiring, education, finance, or clinical prediction. Not every upset implies ranking failure, and not every list is equally stale or biased. The episode provides no formal measurement rule, longitudinal dataset, or complete alternative ordering.

## What Changed

- Created the concept to separate informative ordering from context-sensitive prediction.
