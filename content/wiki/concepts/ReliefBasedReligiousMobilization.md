# Relief-Based Religious Mobilization / 救济型宗教动员

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-922-ruhe-zai-fanchen-zhong-xiulian-qingshang-lsxiqneyyeod6aeeqxuhs517pn1x|Hanji 922]] supplies the earliest growth phase for this concept. [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]] uses Huang-Lao language, confession of sins, and talisman-water healing; believers who see recovery or hear recovery stories treat [[TaipingDao|太平道]] as practical help before it is legible as an uprising network.

The episode also clarifies the low-friction growth channel. Disciples spread the teaching broadly, some people sell property and travel to seek Zhang Jiao, and local officials do not intervene because they read the movement as moral exhortation. Relief-based mobilization therefore becomes dangerous partly because its first public face looks like care rather than command.

Relief-based religious mobilization / 救济型宗教动员 is the pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-923-chengnianrende-dingji-henjin-guzi-lide-juejue-lifzh-gohpqnmdznnfv72tndqf8h|Hanji 923]] draws from [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]] and [[TaipingDao|太平道]]. In the episode, epidemic, poverty, and weak medical access make ordinary people search for practical help before they are ready for rebellion. Zhang Jiao's healing role gives him trust, and that trust is then folded into religious authority, local organization, and coordinated action.

The concept is narrower than [[InstrumentalizedReligiousPower|工具化宗教权力]]. It does not claim that all religious care is merely manipulative. It names a specific mobilization path: relief or cure claims answer a real distress, create dependence and shared identity, and can then support a political network if the organizer turns care into command.

In Hanji 923 this path becomes visible through the thirty-six `fang`, the "甲子" signs, [[MaYuanyiLateHan|马元义]]'s regional coordination, and the rapid response after [[TangZhouLateHan|唐周]] exposes the plan. The movement has already become infrastructure before open revolt begins.

## Key Claims
- Hanji 922 adds that healing and moral-exhortation activity can build a mass base before officials recognize a political-command layer.
- Material distress can make religious organization persuasive before it becomes openly political.
- Healing, charity, or relief can create trust and obligation faster than abstract doctrine alone.
- Mobilization becomes dangerous when practical aid, sacred authority, and command hierarchy merge.
- The source's Yellow Turban case connects epidemic history to rebellion without reducing the uprising to disease alone.
