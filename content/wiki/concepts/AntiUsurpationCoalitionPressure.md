# Anti-Usurpation Coalition Pressure / 讨逆同盟压力

## Definition
Anti-usurpation coalition pressure / 讨逆同盟压力 is the forced-response pattern created when a claimant attacks a still-recognized sovereign title. Rivals may remain self-interested and mutually hostile, but public non-response can look like recognition, so the claimant's title move creates an unusually easy legitimacy language for opposition.

## Current Synthesis
The current Yuan Shu synthesis has four stages. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1001-weishenme-quan-ni-buyao-fanzhongnu-lolurkcjpie14nnczk0tu7iqerke|Hanji 1001]] shows internal pressure before the claim is public: generals do not support [[YuanShu|袁术]], and advisers warn that virtue and people-support cannot be replaced by force, prophecy, or the [[HeirloomImperialSeal|传国玉玺]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1002-yuanshu-chengdi-hou-sunce-chengwei-zui-da-ying-llya3c2-pefujwxdxefvqxwxjizjo|Hanji 1002]] adds ally exit: [[SunCe|孙策]] warns that [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] has not forfeited the throne and uses the warning to separate from Yuan Shu before becoming complicit.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1015-sanguo-luanshi-weishenme-yuanshu-gan-chengdi-lim0jemkwyjcesj2nvsr3jndhxfk|Hanji 1015]] makes the pattern explicit after self-coronation at [[Shouchun|寿春]]: [[CaoCao|曹操]], [[LiuBei|刘备]], Sun Ce, [[YuanShao|袁绍]], and [[LyuBu|吕布]] all gain strategic or reputational reasons to oppose the claim. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] adds the terminal stage: Yuan Shu's old retainers refuse him, Cao blocks his attempt to move north, and the seal returns to court. The pressure is therefore not just an attack pretext; it steadily narrows refuge, alliance, title inheritance, and symbolic continuity.

## Key Claims
- Anti-usurpation pressure can begin inside the claimant's own camp before armies mobilize.
- A still-recognized weak emperor can remain politically strong enough to make a rival title claim costly.
- Ally exit is part of the mechanism because associates must avoid looking like guarantors of the claimant's title.
- The pressure joins moral language to self-interest: rivals can punish a claimant while protecting their own reputation and strategic room.
- The mechanism does not require a sincere or durable coalition; it explains why the first response against the claimant becomes unusually easy to justify.
- Hanji 1033 shows the endpoint: unsupported usurpation can leave the claimant unable to find refuge, transfer title, or keep the core symbol in his camp.

## Evidence
- Internal refusal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1001-weishenme-quan-ni-buyao-fanzhongnu-lolurkcjpie14nnczk0tu7iqerke|Hanji 1001]] says Yuan Shu's generals do not support his title discussion, while Yan Xiang and Zhang Cheng warn that strength and symbols do not equal legitimate rule.
- Ally exit: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1002-yuanshu-chengdi-hou-sunce-chengwei-zui-da-ying-llya3c2-pefujwxdxefvqxwxjizjo|Hanji 1002]] shows Sun Ce turning remonstrance into a clean break from Yuan Shu's title project.
- Public coalition pressure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1015-sanguo-luanshi-weishenme-yuanshu-gan-chengdi-lim0jemkwyjcesj2nvsr3jndhxfk|Hanji 1015]] explains why Cao Cao, Liu Bei, Sun Ce, Yuan Shao, and Lü Bu all have reasons to oppose Yuan Shu once he claims the imperial title.
- Terminal isolation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] shows Yuan Shu refused by old retainers, intercepted on the way to Yuan Shao, and followed by the seal's return to court.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The pressure does not prove that the opposing actors are loyalists in a pure moral sense. Hanji 1015 explicitly treats opposition as opportunity as well as principle, and Hanji 1033 shows Yuan Shao himself tempted by imperial-title thinking after defeating Gongsun Zan. The concept should therefore name a public legitimacy constraint, not a stable righteous coalition.

## What Changed
- Hanji 1033 extends the concept from pre-claim warning and public coalition logic to Yuan Shu's final isolation and the seal's return to court.
