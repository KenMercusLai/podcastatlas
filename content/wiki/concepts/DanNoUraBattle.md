# Dan-no-ura Battle

## Definition
The Dan-no-ura Battle is the April 25, 1185 naval battle in which [[MinamotoNoYoshitsune]] defeats the [[TairaClan]] and ends Taira military power in [[661-dawn-of-the-samurai-the-shogun-triumphant-part-4-glt7837412375]].

## Current Synthesis
The episode presents Dan-no-ura as both tactical culmination and memory catastrophe. By the battle, the Minamoto have ships through captured shipyards and defections, and Yoshitsune forces the Taira fleet into narrow waters where tide and rocky shore make retreat impossible.

The battle's memory centers on death as much as victory. Antoku's grandmother carries the child emperor into the sea, many Taira follow by weighing themselves down, Kenreimon-in survives by being pulled from the water, and later legend imagines drowned Taira spirits in Heikegani crabs.

## Key Claims
- Dan-no-ura completes Yoshitsune's run of victories over the Taira.
- Minamoto naval capacity is acquired during the war rather than assumed at the beginning.
- Geography and tide turn the Taira's naval world against them.
- The battle becomes an unprecedented mass-suicide and ghost-memory story in samurai history.
- Yoshitsune's triumph immediately increases his danger to Yoritomo.

## Evidence
- Battle setup: [[661-dawn-of-the-samurai-the-shogun-triumphant-part-4-glt7837412375]] dates the battle to April 25, 1185, and describes Minamoto ship acquisition and narrow-water pressure.
- Taira collapse: [[661-dawn-of-the-samurai-the-shogun-triumphant-part-4-glt7837412375]] describes the riptide, rocky shore, Antoku's drowning, mass suicide, Kenreimon-in's rescue, and Munemori's capture.
- Political aftermath: [[661-dawn-of-the-samurai-the-shogun-triumphant-part-4-glt7837412375]] says the victory begins Yoshitsune's fall because Yoritomo sees his fame as a threat.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source gives a narrative military account but does not adjudicate technical debates about fleet size, exact tidal mechanics, or later legendary layers.
