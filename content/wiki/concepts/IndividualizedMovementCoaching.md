# Individualized Movement Coaching

## Definition
Individualized movement coaching is the practice of adapting cues, methods, event fit, and technical solutions to a person's structure, capacities, training history, personality, and authentic movement expression while retaining stable task principles.

## Current Synthesis
The source distinguishes principle from imitation. Sprinting still requires force, speed of force application, direction, body-mass management, posture, hip extension, stiffness, and control. But an athlete should not copy Usain Bolt's stride, Andre DeGrasse's personality, or a coach's preferred acceleration model when their body and disposition demand a different solution.

Coaching therefore becomes diagnostic and protective. A coach observes rhythm, ease, sound, posture, mood, and repeatability, then uses technical cues before connecting them to words or states that fit the athlete. Youth athletes should explore multiple events before early specialization, and adult progressions should begin from current capacity rather than identity or ambition.

## Key Claims
- Stable movement principles can support many legitimate methods and visible styles.
- Anatomy, action capability, training history, and personality constrain useful technique.
- Copying an elite athlete's mechanics or persona can suppress rather than reveal performance.
- Coaches should use observation to rediscover an athlete's workable solution instead of imposing a universal ideal.
- Technical cues can be connected to athlete-specific mood words once basic skill is established.
- Youth event specialization should be delayed enough for performance, enjoyment, and self-understanding to reveal fit.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Individualization does not mean every self-selected movement is safe or effective. The source provides coaching examples rather than controlled comparisons of individualized and standardized methods. Technical freedom remains bounded by task demands, symptoms, measurable outcomes, safeguarding, and qualified medical or rehabilitation input when needed.
