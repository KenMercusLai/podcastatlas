# Compensating Differentials

Compensating differentials are the wage premiums that should compensate workers for undesirable job attributes. In [[the-real-horror-of-alien-and-how-it-explains-why-were-not-paid-enough]], [[ArinDube|Arin Dube]] uses [[AlienFilm|Alien]] to ask whether workers facing extreme [[NegativeJobAmenities]] are actually being paid enough for the risk they carry.

The concept depends on labor-market competition. If workers have real alternatives and job risks are visible, employers must pay more for dangerous or unpleasant work. If [[LaborMonopsony]], [[ShroudedJobAttributes]], or [[LaborSearchFrictions]] weaken outside options, the expected premium may not appear.
