## Definition
Amateur farming vs livelihood agriculture / 业余种地与生计农业 is the distinction between farming as chosen experience and farming as income-dependent survival.

## Current Synthesis
In [[yu-duan-zhiqiang-tan-chongming-xiangye-bowuzhi-wo-zhu-changjiang-wei-8212963740-775078]], [[DuanZhiqiang|段志强]] can treat planting, weeding, crop failure, and bodily soreness as observation and writing material because his main livelihood is not the harvest. The concept sharpens [[AgriculturalSystemsReality]] by showing that the same agricultural system has different psychological meaning depending on whether output is optional, supplemental, or essential.

## Key Claims
- Farming as experience can produce attention, pleasure, embarrassment, and writing even when crops fail.
- Farming as livelihood imposes cost, yield, timing, and self-blame pressures that cannot be romanticized.
- Weather, missed seasons, injury, soil, and neighbor judgment remain real constraints even in amateur farming.
- Family and neighbor labor reveal the difference between an author's selected farm experience and a full farmer's routine.
- The concept helps keep rural nonfiction from turning hardship into pastoral scenery.

## Evidence
- Different psychological weight: [[yu-duan-zhiqiang-tan-chongming-xiangye-bowuzhi-wo-zhu-changjiang-wei-8212963740-775078]] has Duan say he can enjoy farm labor partly because his output is not only the corn in the field.
- Material constraints still matter: [[yu-duan-zhiqiang-tan-chongming-xiangye-bowuzhi-wo-zhu-changjiang-wei-8212963740-775078]] describes failed watermelons, poor corn, delayed work, finger pain, leg injuries, and typhoon/rain pressure.
- Farmer contrast: [[yu-duan-zhiqiang-tan-chongming-xiangye-bowuzhi-wo-zhu-changjiang-wei-8212963740-775078]] contrasts Duan's work schedule with parents and older neighbors whose routines make the land look properly tended.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Amateur farming is not fake farming, but it is not equivalent to full agricultural livelihood. The concept should preserve both truths: bodily participation gives real knowledge, while economic dependence changes the moral and emotional stakes.
