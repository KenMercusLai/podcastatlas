# Party-Ban Crisis Amnesty / 党锢危机赦免

Party-ban crisis amnesty / 党锢危机赦免 is the emergency repair move [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-924-1-de-cuowu-100-de-shibai-lmeo7vufm6ib5lni6p49rponazut|Hanji 924]] draws from the 184 CE [[YellowTurbansLateHan|Yellow Turban]] crisis. [[HuangfuSongLateHan|皇甫嵩]] and [[LyuQiangLateHan|吕强]] both argue that the court cannot safely suppress [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]] while continuing to exclude party figures who might otherwise help the state.

The concept differs from [[PartyProhibitionRehabilitation|党人翻案合法性]]. Dong Zhuo's later rehabilitation of party figures is a legitimacy-packaging move after coercive takeover; Hanji 924's amnesty is a defensive crisis measure under [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] that tries to rebuild talent, trust, and public alignment before the rebellion grows worse.

## Key Claims
- Crisis amnesty can repair capacity by returning alienated elites and families to legal life.
- The move can be pragmatic even when the ruler has not accepted the deeper moral critique of earlier repression.
- The repair remains fragile if palace intermediaries can still destroy advisers who expose corruption.
- Hanji 924 treats amnesty, military funding, and official evaluation as linked tools rather than separate acts.
