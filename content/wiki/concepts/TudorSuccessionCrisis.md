# Tudor Succession Crisis

Tudor succession crisis is the episode's frame for why [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]] matters beyond ordinary imprisonment in [[696-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-killing-the-queen-of-scots-part-6-glt2334035496]]. As [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]]'s cousin and an obvious Catholic heir if Elizabeth died, Mary could convert assassination, claimant politics, foreign invasion, and domestic religious fracture into one crisis.

The concept connects personal dynastic status to state security. Mary's body and correspondence become strategic because Protestant ministers believe her survival keeps a replacement regime available to English Catholics, Scottish interests, and continental powers.

## Connections
- [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]] and [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]] - claimant and reigning monarch.
- [[BabingtonPlot]], [[ActForTheQueensSafety|Act for the Queen's Safety]], and [[ConfessionalWarEscalation]] - mechanisms by which succession risk becomes legal and geopolitical crisis.
