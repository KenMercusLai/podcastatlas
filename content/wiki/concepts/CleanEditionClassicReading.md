# Clean Edition Classic Reading

Clean edition classic reading is the episode's method for approaching a difficult or reputationally scandalous classic by reducing explicit material while preserving enough plot, structure, character pressure, and historical texture for real reading to remain possible. [[fanwai-15-banlatte-jiang-jinpingmei-la-erqie-haishi-luse-de-991508433]] makes this explicit through [[Banlatte|半拿铁]]'s "绿色版" retelling of [[JinPingMei|《金瓶梅》]].

The source treats the cleaned version as an entry point, not a claim that nothing has been lost. Its argument is narrower and stronger: the novel's value does not sit only in explicit sexual passages, because [[UrbanEverydayFiction]], [[HouseholdAsSocialWeb]], social history, food, commerce, clothing, law, religion, and reception history remain visible after those passages are bracketed. [[ZhangZhupo|张竹坡]]'s "非淫书" reception frame gives the method an older critical precedent.

## Key Claims
- Removing explicit material can make a source easier to enter, but it should not pretend to be the full original.
- A cleaned retelling is most defensible when the omitted material is not the only carrier of character, structure, or theme.
- The method should preserve the uncomfortable moral world rather than turning a dark classic into harmless edutainment.
- It works best with [[ClassicReadingComplexity]]: cleaned access should lead back toward better reading, not replace interpretation with reputation management.
