## Definition
Founder pressure compartmentalization is the ability to isolate one high-stress problem from another so the founder can keep acting on the decision immediately in front of them.

## Current Synthesis
The current evidence comes from [[ChangpengZhao|CZ]]'s account of operating under Binance and legal pressure. He describes a childhood pattern of keeping family conflict from affecting school, then extends that pattern to founder work: after a high-pressure U.S. legal meeting, he could move into a product meeting and stay calm. This is not a full emotional-health method; it is an operating stance that narrows attention to controllable action and treats uncertain outcomes as something to accept after effort.

## Key Claims
- Compartmentalization can preserve execution when a founder faces simultaneous legal, product, organizational, and personal stress.
- The mechanism depends on isolating domains, not denying that the pressure exists.
- CZ links the practice to an action-first worldview: solve what can be solved, then accept outcomes that remain uncertain.
- The pattern helps explain why pressure tolerance can be a founder capability alongside product, technology, IQ, and EQ.

## Evidence
- Domain isolation: [[kangya-yingbian-yu-zhongsheng-dongli-cz-de-renshi-he-tianming-1736950d-420c-4021-9a56-a180810612d7]] has CZ say he could keep childhood family conflict from shaping school performance and later switch from a U.S. legal meeting into a product meeting without visible disruption.
- Uncertainty handling: [[kangya-yingbian-yu-zhongsheng-dongli-cz-de-renshi-he-tianming-1736950d-420c-4021-9a56-a180810612d7]] identifies the U.S. case as especially stressful because the outcome and boundary were uncertain, then frames response as doing one's part and accepting the result.
- Founder capability: [[kangya-yingbian-yu-zhongsheng-dongli-cz-de-renshi-he-tianming-1736950d-420c-4021-9a56-a180810612d7]] says pressure tolerance is under-discussed compared with more visible founder traits.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The current page describes one founder's self-account. It should not be generalized into a universal stress-management prescription or used to minimize cases where pressure requires rest, legal support, medical care, or organizational redesign.
