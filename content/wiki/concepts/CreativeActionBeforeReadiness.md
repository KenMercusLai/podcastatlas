# Creative Action Before Readiness / 条件未齐时先行动

## Definition
Creative action before readiness is the practice of beginning a bounded work before financing, equipment, expertise, distribution, or confidence is complete, then using visible commitment to discover missing capacity and attract collaboration.

## Current Synthesis
[[JiaZhangke|贾樟柯]]'s early films make the principle concrete. Television-writing income, borrowed money, borrowed equipment, classmates, later collaborators, low shooting ratios, and unexpected film-stock support did not remove constraint; they turned a project from an intention into something others could help finish. “先做起来” therefore names staged commitment under uncertainty, not magical voluntarism.

The practice works only with scope control and honest dependence. Starting creates learning and coordination opportunities, but it can also shift unpaid risk onto friends, crews, or family. The episode supports persistence and resourcefulness while leaving production ethics, repayment, sustainability, and outcome quality to be judged case by case.

## Key Claims
- Incomplete conditions need not prevent a small, bounded first commitment.
- Early tangible work can reveal collaborators, bottlenecks, and forms of help unavailable to a purely hypothetical project.
- Constraint can shape format, shooting ratio, staffing, and sequence without automatically improving the art.
- Paid work can subsidize authored work when the creator keeps the service relationship and artistic boundary explicit.
- Persistence remains dependent on networks, material support, and fair treatment rather than individual will alone.

## Evidence
- Initial production: [[jiazhangke-luoyonghao-chengwei-jiazhangke-shang-lhruzjxljcfha9bk5k2hu_wdqcir]] describes 《小山回家》 being made from television-writing savings, loans, friends, and borrowed equipment.
- Scope under scarcity: [[jiazhangke-luoyonghao-chengwei-jiazhangke-shang-lhruzjxljcfha9bk5k2hu_wdqcir]] describes 《小武》 as a feature conceived under short-film-scale resources, 16mm stock, and a very low shooting ratio.
- Help after commitment: [[jiazhangke-luoyonghao-chengwei-jiazhangke-shang-lhruzjxljcfha9bk5k2hu_wdqcir]] recounts a Kodak employee personally funding additional film stock and the Hong Kong short-film award opening a durable collaborator network.
- Livelihood boundary: [[jiazhangke-luoyonghao-chengwei-jiazhangke-shang-lhruzjxljcfha9bk5k2hu_wdqcir]] describes television, columns, children's programming, and advertising as income-generating work that supported filmmaking without defining the films' content.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The account is survivor testimony: many projects begun under constraint fail or exploit participants, and later success can make improvisation look more inevitable than it was. Beginning early does not excuse unsafe practice, unclear obligations, unpaid labor, weak planning, or financial irresponsibility. The exact budgets, loans, earnings, and contributions are not independently verified here.

## What Changed
- Established a bounded execution concept from Jia's early production history.
- Added collaborator, labor, scope, and survivor-bias qualifications to the “just start” maxim.
