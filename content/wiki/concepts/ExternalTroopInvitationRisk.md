# External Troop Invitation Risk / 外兵入京升级风险

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-944-xiang-zai-luanshi-zhong-cunhuo-xian-guan-zhu-zui-lp7omiak9mpcigrj4f1nummfwntr|Hanji 944]] supplies the first proposal before the named [[DongZhuo|董卓]] approach in Hanji 945. [[YuanShao|袁绍]] urges [[HeJin|何进]] to summon strong outside commanders and local heroes into the capital field, while [[ChenLinLateHan|陈琳]] and [[CaoCao|曹操]] warn that He Jin already has enough force and that wider mobilization makes the plan harder to keep secret.

This adds [[LethalPlanLeakageRisk|致命计划泄露风险]] to the concept's precondition. Outside troops are dangerous not only because they carry independent ambition, but because calling them expands the audience, the timeline, and the number of armed actors who can reinterpret the purge before it happens.

External troop invitation risk / 外兵入京升级风险 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-945-zhinian-tai-shen-de-ren-zhihui-yuelaiyue-tongku-lm6xaiy2pcqunke04yzqwwxsxxl|Hanji 945]] as the institutional danger in [[HeJin|何进]]'s plan to summon outside military force into [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] against the [[TenAttendantsLateHan|十常侍]]. The source treats the plan as more than a bad choice of ally: once outside commanders are invited, the court dispute becomes a military opportunity that the inviting actor may no longer control.

The source case centers on [[DongZhuo|董卓]]. [[ZhengTaiLateHan|郑泰]] and [[LuZhiLateHan|卢植]] warn that He Jin already has formal authority and should not borrow Dong Zhuo's force; [[ZhongShaoLateHan|种劭]] can temporarily stop Dong Zhuo only because reputation, troop number, and soldier sentiment still constrain him. The risk is therefore dynamic: an external army may be stoppable before entry, but the summons gives it legitimacy, direction, and a reason to approach the capital.

This concept is adjacent to [[XingshiqingStrategicAssessment|行势情战略评估]] and [[PalaceCoupViolenceCascade|宫廷政变暴力级联]]. It names the moment before the cascade: a leader tries to solve palace access limits through coercive pressure, but the cure changes the field and creates the opening later used by the third actor.

## Key Claims

- Hanji 944 adds the first proposal: outside-force mobilization multiplies information leakage and makes a narrow purge harder to execute.
- Inviting outside troops can convert an internal court conflict into a wider military crisis.
- Borrowed force is not neutral; it carries the invited commander's incentives, troops, reputation, and ambition.
- Formal summons can give an outsider a legitimate route toward the center even if the inviter later regrets the escalation.
- Delay increases the risk because summoned forces continue moving while palace factions gain time to adapt.
- Temporary restraint of an outside commander depends on concrete constraints such as troop numbers, officer reputation, and soldier loyalty, not merely on court orders.
- The pattern helps explain why Hanji 946's palace collapse and Hanji 947's Dong Zhuo entry are linked rather than separate accidents.
