# Mystery Narrative Overfitting

## Definition

Mystery narrative overfitting is the construction of a single dramatic explanation that makes many ambiguous clues appear mutually confirming even when each clue has weak provenance, multiple ordinary interpretations, or no demonstrated causal connection.

## Current Synthesis

The [[SomertonManCase]] invites overfitting because its materials resemble detective fiction: an unidentified well-dressed body, removed labels, suspected poison, a hidden literary fragment, apparent code, a nearby nurse, and a Cold War weapons range. Espionage and doomed-love narratives gain force from how well those details sound together, not from a demonstrated chain connecting them.

The reported identification of [[CarlWebb]] shows how new evidence can reduce the exoticism of individual clues. Thomas Keane may explain a clothing name; poetry may contextualize the *Rubaiyat*; horse betting may offer an alternative to code. These possibilities do not prove an ordinary death either. The corrective is disciplined comparison of narratives, including the possibility that some clues are unrelated.

## Key Claims

- Narrative coherence is not the same as evidentiary dependence among clues.
- Period atmosphere can generate hypotheses without proving personal participation in espionage or politics.
- Emotional reactions, physical resemblance, and coincidental interests are especially easy to overcombine.
- New identity evidence can normalize clues without itself proving an alternative causal story.
- A responsible account permits leftover facts and multiple unresolved mechanisms.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Clusters of clues can reflect a real common cause, and espionage, secret relationships, poisoning, and deliberate concealment are not impossible merely because they are dramatic. The concept is not a preference for mundane explanations. It is a demand that the proposed links be evidenced independently and that disconfirmation—such as the reported paternity result—change the narrative rather than be absorbed into it.
