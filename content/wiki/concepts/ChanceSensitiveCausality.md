# Chance-Sensitive Causality

## Definition
Chance-sensitive causality is the view that outcomes can depend on small, context-specific inputs whose later importance is real but difficult or impossible to identify, measure, or predict in advance.

## Current Synthesis
[[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-0a3c8347ab08972a4dce8dba455358cf-53039697-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-0a3c8347ab08972a4dce8dba455358cf-53039697|Fluke: How Chance and Chaos Shape Our Lives]] joins several kinds of sensitivity. A lost ball becomes a flotation aid; a vacation shapes a later target decision; cloud over Kokura redirects an atomic bomb; trouser fabric helps determine whether a coup succeeds; rounded inputs make a weather simulation diverge. [[silicon-valley-on-the-couch]] adds an innovation-geography case: [[WilliamShockley]] may have chosen Mountain View partly to be near [[MayBradfordShockley|his mother]], after which recruitment, destructive management, coordinated departure, and local home ownership helped convert a private location decision into a regional company genealogy.

The shared claim is not that these events lack causes or that the vivid small cause explains everything. It is that models and stories can discard personal, material, or local details as noise before discovering that those details entered a causal path alongside institutions, capital, infrastructure, and prior technical capability.

The account also limits retrospective moralization. An ordinary action can contribute to a later tragedy or rescue without becoming morally identical to that outcome, because causal contribution and responsible intent are different judgments. Causal humility therefore means keeping multiple scales and dependencies visible, resisting sole-author stories, and distinguishing lawful causation from practical predictability.

## Key Claims
- Small inputs can become decisive when systems are sensitive to initial conditions or linked through long causal chains.
- What a model treats as noise can contain an outcome-relevant variable omitted for tractability.
- Retrospective stories often compress many enabling conditions into one legible cause.
- A small input can select the location or timing at which structural capacities begin to compound.
- Causal contribution does not automatically establish foresight, intent, blame, or moral equivalence.
- Deterministic structure and practical unpredictability can coexist.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept should not turn every coincidence into a privileged explanation or imply that structural forces, base rates, institutions, and large causes do not matter. Counterfactual importance is often impossible to establish from a vivid anecdote, and chaos theory has a precise mathematical meaning that should not be reduced to a metaphor for any surprising event. Shockley's proximity to his mother is an inferred motive rather than documented intent, so the case illustrates a possible causal chain rather than proving a single secret cause of Silicon Valley. The current evidence consists of structured episode summaries rather than the underlying historical records, datasets, correspondence editions, or scientific literature.

## What Changed
- Extended the concept from accident and physical sensitivity to innovation geography, where a private preference may select the place at which institutional capacities compound.
- Added an explicit boundary between a plausible small-cause hypothesis and a proven sole-cause explanation.
