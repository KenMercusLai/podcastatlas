# Chance-Sensitive Causality

## Definition
Chance-sensitive causality is the view that outcomes can depend on small, context-specific inputs whose later importance is real but difficult or impossible to identify, measure, or predict in advance.

## Current Synthesis
[[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-0a3c8347ab08972a4dce8dba455358cf-53039697-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-0a3c8347ab08972a4dce8dba455358cf-53039697|Fluke: How Chance and Chaos Shape Our Lives]] joins several kinds of sensitivity. A lost ball becomes a flotation aid; a vacation shapes a later target decision; cloud over Kokura redirects an atomic bomb; trouser fabric helps determine whether a coup succeeds; rounded inputs make a weather simulation diverge. The shared claim is not that these events lack causes. It is that models and stories can discard details as noise before discovering that those details entered the causal path.

The account also limits retrospective moralization. An ordinary action can contribute to a later tragedy or rescue without becoming morally identical to that outcome, because causal contribution and responsible intent are different judgments. Causal humility therefore means keeping multiple scales and dependencies visible, resisting sole-author stories, and distinguishing lawful causation from practical predictability.

## Key Claims
- Small inputs can become decisive when systems are sensitive to initial conditions or linked through long causal chains.
- What a model treats as noise can contain an outcome-relevant variable omitted for tractability.
- Retrospective stories often compress many enabling conditions into one legible cause.
- Causal contribution does not automatically establish foresight, intent, blame, or moral equivalence.
- Deterministic structure and practical unpredictability can coexist.
- Limited individual control does not remove influence, because ordinary actions alter the conditions inherited by later events.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept should not turn every coincidence into a privileged explanation or imply that structural forces, base rates, institutions, and large causes do not matter. Counterfactual importance is often impossible to establish from a vivid anecdote, and chaos theory has a precise mathematical meaning that should not be reduced to a metaphor for any surprising event. The current evidence is one structured episode summary rather than the underlying historical records, datasets, or scientific literature.

## What Changed
- Established a distinction among causation, predictability, model omission, and moral responsibility.
