# Bipolar Junction Transistor

Bipolar junction transistor, or BJT, is the incumbent transistor technology used as the contrast case in [[zhenzheng-gaibian-shijie-de-jishu-weishenme-yikaishi-dou-bu-bei-kanhao-s10e16-8c95b3dc-d75a-4bdd-84d4-2c06fd2d85b1]]. The source says BJT devices were faster and more stable than early [[MOSFET|MOS]] devices, making them a better fit for [[ATT|AT&T]]'s communication-switching needs.

The episode does not portray BJT preference as simple foolishness. It uses the comparison to show why incumbent evaluation metrics can be locally rational while missing a challenger technology's long-run scalability.
