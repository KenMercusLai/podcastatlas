# Nonfatal Shooting Measurement

## Definition

Nonfatal shooting measurement tracks people struck, wounds, or shooting incidents independently of whether the victim dies, so changes in violence are not conflated with changes in emergency and trauma survival.

## Current Synthesis

The episode proposes “bullet-to-skin contact” as a direct exposure measure because homicide counts omit survivors and aggravated-assault categories may mix shootings with physically different events. [[NatalieHipple]]'s Indianapolis work reportedly joined manual police-record review to medical-facility reports when no consistent federal statistic was available.

No single count is sufficient. Bullets striking people, people shot, shooting incidents, injury severity, treatment, and deaths answer different questions and can diverge. A useful reporting system would retain those distinctions, document coverage and linkage, and avoid interpreting improved survival as proof that underlying violence fell.

## Key Claims

- Homicide is an outcome measure, not a complete measure of shooting exposure.
- Broad aggravated-assault categories can obscure firearm-specific trends.
- Police and medical records can complement each other but require careful linkage and deduplication.
- Bullets, wounds, victims, incidents, severity, and fatalities are distinct units that should not be silently interchanged.
- Consistent nonfatal data are necessary to test whether apparent safety gains reflect less violence or better survival.
- A vivid label does not remove the need for stable coding rules, auditability, and uncertainty estimates.

## Evidence

### Federal and local data gap

- [[guns-part-4-moral-hazard]] reports that no consistent federal statistic captured bullet-to-skin contact and that police often grouped nonfatal shootings inside aggravated assault.

### Record reconstruction

- [[guns-part-4-moral-hazard]] describes Hipple's team reviewing thousands of Indianapolis police records, incorporating medical-facility reports, and finding homicide and contact trends moving differently.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source does not provide the underlying study or enough detail to assess completeness, inter-rater reliability, record linkage, changes in reporting practice, wound counting, population adjustment, or statistical uncertainty. Bullet counts may overweight multi-wound events, while victim counts and incident counts discard other severity information. The concept therefore favors a linked set of measures rather than one replacement headline number.

## What Changed

- Created a measurement framework that separates firearm exposure, injury, treatment outcome, and death.
