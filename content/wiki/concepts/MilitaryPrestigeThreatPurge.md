# Military Prestige Threat Purge / 军功声望威胁清洗

Military prestige threat purge / 军功声望威胁清洗 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-984-liangzhouxi-junfa-neidou-ta-weihe-di-yige-daoxia-lurb2qfzsa7e-owh-dl-m-sccze5|Hanji 984]]. It names the pattern where a commander's battlefield usefulness becomes a threat to a superior or co-ruler once that commander's army support, independent reputation, and ambiguous outside contact can be read as future defection or rival legitimacy.

The source case is [[FanChouLateHan|樊稠]]. Fan Chou defeats [[MaTeng|马腾]] and [[HanSui|韩遂]], which should strengthen [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]]'s side inside the [[LiangzhouWarlordClique|凉州系军阀]]. Instead, Fan Chou's bravery and soldier support sharpen Li Jue's jealousy. When [[LiLiLateHan|李利]] reports Fan Chou's friendly conversation with Han Sui, courtesy and shared hometown language become evidence of possible independent alignment.

The concept is narrower than general suspicion. It requires a useful military subordinate or peer whose success changes the threat calculation. It also differs from [[PostRestorationMeritThreat|Post-Restoration Merit Threat]], where the restored ruler's insecurity after regime recovery is the central mechanism, and from [[TallPoppyGovernance|Tall Poppy Governance]], where public intimidation of conspicuous merit is the main pattern. Hanji 984's mechanism is more immediate: prestige plus troops plus ambiguous contact create a purge trigger inside an armed coalition.

## Key Claims
- Battlefield success can increase a commander's vulnerability when the superior values control more than marginal military strength.
- A report does not need to prove treason to matter; it only needs to make an already threatening commander legible as unsafe.
- Shared hometown ties, private courtesy, or long conversation with an adversary can become political evidence when trust is already thin.
- Death-mechanism variants can still support the same causal pattern when both center on jealousy of military prestige and fear of army support.
- The purge can destabilize the whole coalition by telling other generals that victory and popularity are dangerous.
