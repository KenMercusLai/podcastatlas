# Risk Knowledge-Action Gap / 风险知识行动鸿沟

## Definition

The risk knowledge-action gap is the difference between knowing or being warned that an action may cause harm and actually changing behavior, policy, or deployment in response.

## Current Synthesis

[[Prometheus]] warns [[Epimetheus]] not to accept a gift from [[Zeus]], but the warning does not prevent acceptance of [[Pandora]]. The episode uses that failure to distinguish information from action: evidence can identify danger without defeating attraction, short-term benefit, distrust, organizational incentives, or inability to coordinate.

Risk governance must therefore do more than publish a forecast. It must connect evidence to decision rights, credible communication, incentives, alternatives, enforcement, and the capacity to stop or modify action.

## Key Claims

- Advance warning is necessary in many cases but rarely sufficient for prevention.
- Desire, interest, trust, and immediate benefit can override acknowledged long-term danger.
- Scientific uncertainty does not eliminate responsibility to act on credible risk.
- Institutions need pathways that translate evidence into authority, incentives, and execution.
- Failure to act should not automatically be misdescribed as failure to know.

## Evidence

- Mythic warning: [[369-panduola-shi-renlei-zuizao-de-keji-emeng-ma-hesie-de-gongzuo-yu-shiri-lkr0ibzoden-ip-es5xpnhnbvz7]] has Epimetheus accept Pandora despite Prometheus's warning.
- Modern analogy: [[369-panduola-shi-renlei-zuizao-de-keji-emeng-ma-hesie-de-gongzuo-yu-shiri-lkr0ibzoden-ip-es5xpnhnbvz7]] distinguishes scientific risk communication from willingness and ability to surrender immediate interests.
- Governance implication: [[369-panduola-shi-renlei-zuizao-de-keji-emeng-ma-hesie-de-gongzuo-yu-shiri-lkr0ibzoden-ip-es5xpnhnbvz7]] identifies desire, interest, trust, and execution capacity as barriers beyond missing knowledge.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Not acting on a warning can be rational when evidence is weak, costs are disproportionate, or alternatives carry greater risks. The concept therefore requires calibrated evidence and explicit tradeoffs; it should not turn every disputed forecast into a command.

## What Changed

- Created a reusable distinction between recognizing risk and converting recognition into preventive action.
