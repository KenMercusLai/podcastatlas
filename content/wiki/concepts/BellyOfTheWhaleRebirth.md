# Belly of the Whale Rebirth / 鲸鱼之腹与重生

[[BellyOfTheWhaleRebirth|Belly of the whale rebirth / 鲸鱼之腹与重生]] is the [[HeroJourneyNarrative]] stage emphasized in [[29-qianmian-yingxiong-edipusi-zhengzai-xiaban-ji-ditie-590112891]]. The source uses [[JosephCampbell|Campbell]]'s phrase for stories where the hero enters a closed, dangerous, or unknown interior before returning changed.

The episode's examples include Jonah, [[AdventuresOfPinocchio|Pinocchio]], [[SunWukong|孙悟空]] entering bellies or furnaces, Theseus entering the labyrinth, swallowing and dismemberment myths, and stories where a tomb-like or womb-like space becomes a scene of symbolic death and rebirth. The point is structural rather than literal: the hero enters a space where ordinary identity is suspended, then emerges with a new status or capacity.

## Key Claims
- The "belly" is a threshold interior: cave, monster, furnace, labyrinth, body, or enclosed underworld can all perform the role.
- The stage connects death, gestation, trial, and transformation.
- It is useful only when tied to the story's actual movement, not when every dark place is automatically treated as rebirth.
