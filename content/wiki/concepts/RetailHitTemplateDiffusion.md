# Retail Hit Template Diffusion / 零售爆款模板扩散

## Definition
Retail hit-template diffusion is the pattern where a visible product success in an influential channel validates demand and prompts competing retailers and brands to copy its category, flavor, positioning, or format.

## Current Synthesis
The episode presents [[SamsClub|Sam's Club]]'s black-truffle ham crackers as more than one successful SKU. Their reported repurchase visibility reduced uncertainty around truffle-flavored snacks, after which other brands and retailers extended the same premium-savory formula into chips, crackers, cheese snacks, mooncakes, and more. The channel therefore acts as a demand laboratory whose winners become industry templates.

## Key Claims
- A visible retailer hit reduces perceived demand risk for imitators.
- Product copying often transfers a positioning bundle, not only a flavor.
- Influential channels can accelerate cross-category diffusion.
- Fast imitation can saturate and weaken the original differentiation cue.
- Reported popularity is a signal, not proof that every copied product will succeed.

## Evidence
- Demand validation - [[tulasi-kafeidou-yuebing-shupian-dou-dashang-heisonglu-biaoqian-gaoduan-shicai-weihe-biande-wuchubuzai-1016110497]] reports sustained popularity and high repurchase visibility for Sam's Club black-truffle ham crackers.
- Follow-on products - [[tulasi-kafeidou-yuebing-shupian-dou-dashang-heisonglu-biaoqian-gaoduan-shicai-weihe-biande-wuchubuzai-1016110497]] lists subsequent truffle products from other retailers and food brands.
- Saturation - [[tulasi-kafeidou-yuebing-shupian-dou-dashang-heisonglu-biaoqian-gaoduan-shicai-weihe-biande-wuchubuzai-1016110497]] warns that repeated use makes the formerly premium cue feel ordinary.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode gives a plausible diffusion sequence but does not establish that Sam's Club caused every later launch. Brands may have observed the same supply, flavor, or consumer trends independently. Repurchase and timing figures remain source-scoped.

## What Changed
- Added a warehouse-club case where one successful snack becomes a cross-category product template.
- Added saturation as the downstream cost of rapid retail imitation.
