# Time Affluence

## Definition
Time affluence is the subjective sense of having enough discretionary time and enough control and attention to use it in ways that feel restorative, relational, or meaningful.

## Current Synthesis
The concept distinguishes clock time from usable time. A person may possess small free intervals yet experience time famine when those intervals are fragmented, pre-committed, mentally occupied, or automatically surrendered to a phone. Conversely, protecting and intentionally assigning even modest blocks can make time feel more available.

The episode's “time confetti” image is therefore ambivalent: fragments can disappear into default scrolling, but they can also support rest, presence, contact, movement, or delight. Individual scheduling cannot solve overwork, caregiving load, poverty, disability, precarious employment, or unequal control over time, so time affluence remains partly structural.

## Key Claims
- Felt time availability is related to but not identical with the number of free minutes.
- Fragmentation, work leakage, and habitual phone use can make discretionary time less usable.
- Protecting small blocks and choosing their purpose in advance can increase perceived control.
- Time can support wellbeing through rest, social contact, movement, presence, and valued activity.
- Time poverty often reflects institutional and material constraints, not merely poor personal discipline.

## Evidence
- Definition and burden: [[how-to-achieve-true-happiness-using-science-based-protocols-dr-laurie-santos-scim5061134579]] defines time affluence as felt free time and compares severe time famine with other major wellbeing burdens.
- Fragmentation: [[how-to-achieve-true-happiness-using-science-based-protocols-dr-laurie-santos-scim5061134579]] uses “time confetti” for small intervals that can be lost to default phone use.
- Intentional use: [[how-to-achieve-true-happiness-using-science-based-protocols-dr-laurie-santos-scim5061134579]] recommends protecting small blocks and assigning them to activities more nourishing than automatic scrolling.
- Cultural context: [[how-to-achieve-true-happiness-using-science-based-protocols-dr-laurie-santos-scim5061134579]] contrasts work leakage with social clubs, fitness, family time, and hygge in its discussion of national wellbeing.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not provide enough methods to establish a universal dose of free time or a causal equivalence between time famine and unemployment. Advice to reclaim fragments can become blame when people lack schedule control because of work, care, health, housing, or income constraints. Phone use can also provide necessary coordination, accessibility, safety, learning, or genuine connection.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to distinguish subjective, usable time from nominal free minutes.
