# 治安名义政变 / Palace Coup Under Policing Pretext

治安名义政变 / Palace coup under policing pretext is the mechanism [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-138-wudu-qiannian-de-lishi-zhenxiang-zhaogao-bushi-taijian-2-lvtievjrclb1lxxxxr6k-q7kryto|Qinji 138 part 2]] builds from [[YanLe|阎乐]]'s attack on [[WangyiPalace|望夷宫]]. A faction packages armed seizure of the ruler as law enforcement, making the first visible story about thieves, hostages, and negligent guards rather than open treason.

In the episode, [[ZhaoGao|赵高]] hides Yan Le's mother in his own house while the conspirators claim palace thieves have kidnapped her. Yan Le brings trusted soldiers, blames the palace gate official for admitting thieves, kills him, and then uses the created emergency to push toward [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]]. The pretext turns palace access and armed force into a supposedly urgent policing action.

## Key Claims
- A policing pretext can give armed entry a public vocabulary before the target understands that a coup is underway.
- The story transfers blame to gate and guard personnel, making the palace itself appear already breached.
- This pattern extends [[CrisisInformationBlockade|危机信息封锁]]: after bad news is hidden, the ruler is shown a false emergency that masks the operator's own attack.
