# Dark Adviser Craft / 毒士灰度谋略

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-976-sanguo-moushi-dapandian-shui-shi-ni-xinzhong-de-shen-ljgpbosq-9xiowpgaeyossmgst6x|Hanji 976]] supplies the immediate preview for this concept. After describing advisers as state architects, the episode closes with the "poison and honey" metaphor and says the next discussion will turn toward advisers' darker political rules.

Dark adviser craft / 毒士灰度谋略 is the interpretive frame [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-977-yi-zheng-yi-xie-luanshi-dushi-de-guijue-fengyun-lgtpgxl5anxial8ym2nt8dmuc3ee|Hanji 977]] gives to Three Kingdoms advisers. The source does not define a school or doctrine; it groups several techniques under one metaphor: the adviser is a "drug-maker" whose prescription contains both useful medicine and poison.

The frame is broader than [[CovertPoliticalTechnology|阴谋式政治技术]]. Covert political technology centers on hidden inducement, rumor, and information work. Dark adviser craft includes those hidden tools, but also moral pressure, selective visibility, legal-administrative revenge, and taking dirty reputational cost for the ruler. Its distinctive claim is that the adviser manages both the deed and the public language around the deed.

The concept also overlaps with [[PoliticalSurvivalFirst|安全第一政治生存]]. In the source's reading, a top adviser adjusts posture as the political field changes: [[JiaXu|贾诩]] can be poisonous when splitting enemies and restrained after joining [[CaoCao|曹操]], while [[ChengYu|程昱]] can become useful by accepting moral stain that the ruler cannot publicly carry.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-967-ta-canyu-mousha-dongzhuo-jingneng-taoguo-sishen-weilie-lv24biyswj9ljdld5htynhauhph3|Hanji 967]] makes the Jia Xu branch earlier and morally sharper. His advice to [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]] solves an immediate survival problem for Dong Zhuo's remnant commanders, but the executors' violent character turns success into the plunder of [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]]. The source-scoped lesson is that dark adviser craft must judge the executor, not only the tactical move.

## Key Claims
- Hanji 967 adds executor character as a constraint on dark adviser craft: a correct survival prescription can become catastrophe when violent actors gain the city.
- Advisers in disorder may serve rulers by doing work that cannot be made fully moral in public.
- The same actor may alternate between ruthless technique, ritual language, silence, and self-protection depending on patron and risk.
- Moral vocabulary can be an instrument of control when it narrows what a ruler or rival can honorably refuse.
- Selective information can shape a ruler's world before formal advice is even offered.
- Law and administrative procedure can conceal personal vengeance when office power and private resentment overlap.
- Stigma absorption is itself a service: an adviser may keep the ruler's image cleaner by taking blame for harsh measures.
- The concept should remain source-scoped until later sources verify individual details behind the episode's rapid examples.
