# Self-Worth Judgment Recovery / 价值判断权收回

Self-worth judgment recovery / 价值判断权收回 is [[ep273-npc-haishi-npd-dou-bie-xiang-nazou-women-rensheng-de-yaokongqi-lqqnd-e29xvhx2mtf6uuzx13wwvf]]'s "take back the remote control" concept. In [[GongXueping|龚学平]]'s account, a painful relationship becomes especially binding when a person lets someone else decide whether they are good enough, lovable enough, or valuable enough.

The episode does not make recovery abstract. It shows judgment recovery in small acts: riding the bicycle one wants to ride, saying "I think that person is impressive" after a partner belittles the person, noticing bodily discomfort, or asking why one must always be the person who compromises. These acts matter because they return perception, decision, and action to the person who had outsourced them.

## Key Claims
- The central loss in a controlling relationship is often not only freedom of movement but authority over one's own value.
- Recovering judgment starts before a final breakup or family confrontation; it can begin with one specific refusal or one defended perception.
- [[SelfFeelingTrust|Trusting one's feeling]] does not mean treating feeling as infallible; it means allowing pain, unease, and depletion to count as evidence.
- Mature self-confirmation differs from needing to be the most important person in another person's world.
- Action matters because insight without different behavior can become another way to remain stuck in the old pattern.
