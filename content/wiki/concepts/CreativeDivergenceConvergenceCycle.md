# Creative Divergence-Convergence Cycle

## Definition
The creative divergence-convergence cycle is a two-mode account in which flexible association generates multiple possibilities and focused evaluation selects, tests, and develops those that are useful or revealing.

## Current Synthesis
[[the-science-of-creativity-how-to-enhance-creative-innovation-scim5096680097]] presents creativity as an ordered alternation rather than a single flash of originality. Divergent thinking loosens association and explores many routes from a starting point; convergent thinking applies persistence, constraints, validity checks, and craft. Neither mode is sufficient alone: novelty without selection can remain incoherent, while evaluation imposed too early can suppress the unusual combinations it is meant to assess. The cycle operates within a session and can recur, complementing the longer project stages in [[CreativePhaseSeparation]].

## Key Claims
- Divergent thinking expands the option set through flexible and relatively loose association.
- Convergent thinking narrows the option set through focus, persistence, testing, and fit to a purpose.
- Creative output requires both novelty and a meaningful form of utility, insight, delight, or revelation.
- Premature convergence can suppress unusual possibilities before they become legible.
- Divergence without later convergence can produce novelty that never becomes a coherent work or solution.
- The two modes can alternate repeatedly rather than forming a one-pass linear pipeline.

## Evidence
- Two-mode structure - [[the-science-of-creativity-how-to-enhance-creative-innovation-scim5096680097]] distinguishes possibility generation from binding and selecting a focused answer.
- Novelty boundary - [[the-science-of-creativity-how-to-enhance-creative-innovation-scim5096680097]] uses arbitrary novelty to argue that unusual combination alone does not establish creativity.
- Different task demands - [[the-science-of-creativity-how-to-enhance-creative-innovation-scim5096680097]] associates divergence with flexibility and convergence with persistence, focus, and testing.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source gives a useful process model, not a complete theory or a validated universal sequence for every artistic, scientific, technical, or collaborative domain. Generation and evaluation can overlap, expert intuition can perform fast selection, and practical work may require constraints from the outset. The model should not turn normal creative variation into a neurological diagnosis.

## What Changed
- Established a within-session creativity cycle that is distinct from longer project-phase models.
