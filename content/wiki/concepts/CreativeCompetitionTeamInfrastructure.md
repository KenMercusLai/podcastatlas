# Creative Competition Team Infrastructure / 创意赛事团队基础设施

## Definition
Creative competition team infrastructure is the distributed professional, emotional, logistical, technical, and organizational support that makes an individual's judged performance possible.

## Current Synthesis
A short championship presentation can compress years of work and many people's labor into one visible competitor. Coaching manages project cadence and challenges the theme, family support stabilizes emotion, logistics staff protect travel and ingredients, technical specialists control materials, and an employer supplies time and space for iteration. Effective support is neither committee authorship nor obedience: members speak candidly within shared values, while the competitor retains final responsibility for the work.

## Key Claims
- Championship outcomes can depend on a support system larger than the person on stage.
- Coaching combines domain criticism with project management across theme, script, and recipe.
- Emotional and logistical support protect performance capacity as directly as technical preparation.
- Organizational slack—time, space, and repeated trials—is a material creative resource.
- Shared values and clear final authority allow candid disagreement without decision paralysis.

- Long preparation: [[kafei-shijian-he-zhongguo-shouwei-shijie-kafei-yu-liejiu-dasai-guanjun-andy-liaoliao-chuangyi-tediao-gkwrijiohqdbargqkqtalwpn]] dates the preparation from the 2024 runner-up result across a cycle exceeding two years.
- Decision structure: [[kafei-shijian-he-zhongguo-shouwei-shijie-kafei-yu-liejiu-dasai-guanjun-andy-liaoliao-chuangyi-tediao-gkwrijiohqdbargqkqtalwpn]] says the team offers different views while recognizing Andy's final judgment over the theme.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is a retrospective success account and cannot isolate the causal contribution of each team role. Team structure, preparation length, and organizational support may differ substantially across competitors and disciplines.

## What Changed
- Made the hidden emotional, logistical, technical, and organizational labor behind creative competition explicit.
