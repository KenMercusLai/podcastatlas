# 五代阶层流动 / Five Dynasties Social Mobility

Five Dynasties social mobility is [[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]]'s account of how war broke older aristocratic, clan, and bureaucratic barriers. The episode treats this mobility as double-edged: it created extraordinary openings for military men and local strongmen while also destroying lives, institutions, and security.

[[WangJianFormerShu|王建]] is the source's clearest example. His low-status family background makes the rise of [[FormerShu|前蜀]] visible as more than elite succession; it shows how command of armed force could reorder status in the transition from Tang to Song.

## Connections
- [[HuangChao|黄巢]] - late Tang disruption that begins the longer fragmentation logic.
- [[MilitaryPersonalization]] - power attached to commanders and armed networks.
- [[WudaiSuccessionCrisis]] - upward mobility did not solve inheritance or institution-building.
