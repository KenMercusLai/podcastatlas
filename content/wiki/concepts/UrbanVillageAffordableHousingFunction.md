# Urban Villages as Affordable Housing Infrastructure / 城中村的可负担住房功能

## Definition

Urban-village affordable housing function is the role dense, incrementally built neighborhoods play in supplying lower-rent, work-proximate homes, everyday services, and entry points for migrants even when their buildings and tenure do not match formal urban ideals.

## Current Synthesis

The Shenzhen discussion treats urban villages as part of the city's productive system. They allow young and service-sector workers to live near jobs at prices unavailable in formal housing, supporting both job-housing balance and the labor supply behind rapid growth.

This functional defense is not a claim that existing conditions should be frozen. Safety, sanitation, infrastructure, tenancy, and building quality can require intervention. The planning test is whether improvement preserves or replaces affordability and social access rather than counting demolition and tower construction as self-evident progress.

## Key Claims

- Low-cost, work-proximate housing is urban and labor-market infrastructure.
- Urban villages can produce practical job-housing balance for migrants and service workers.
- Informal retail and community networks add everyday value beyond shelter alone.
- Redevelopment can remove affordable supply and social relations even when it improves buildings.
- Upgrading should evaluate replacement affordability, displacement, safety, services, and tenure together.

## Evidence

- Housing and work access - [[ep128-shiwunianyigao-de-chengshi-guihua-jiujing-zai-guihua-shenme-cmcocqgc36nfb4cpjgba98wr|EP128]] says Shenzhen urban villages house many young and service-sector workers near employment at relatively low rent.
- Growth function - the episode argues that this population and housing supply supported Shenzhen's long period of rapid development. [[ep128-shiwunianyigao-de-chengshi-guihua-jiujing-zai-guihua-shenme-cmcocqgc36nfb4cpjgba98wr]]
- Redevelopment risk - the source notes continuing replacement by high-rise projects and the resulting change in low-cost supply and community relations. [[ep128-shiwunianyigao-de-chengshi-guihua-jiujing-zai-guihua-shenme-cmcocqgc36nfb4cpjgba98wr]]

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source does not measure rents, safety, tenure security, infrastructure, displacement, or replacement housing. Functional importance should not romanticize overcrowding or exempt landlords and government from habitability obligations; it changes the burden of proof for redevelopment.

## What Changed

- Created a framework that treats urban villages as housing and labor infrastructure while preserving the case for safety and service improvement.
