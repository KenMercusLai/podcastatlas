# Ordinary Investor Macro Boundary

## Definition

Ordinary investor macro boundary is the discipline of separating macro relevance from macro tradability for household investors. Macro events and cycles may matter to work, savings, housing, and allocation, but that does not mean every headline or long-cycle story should become a trade.

## Current Synthesis

The AI-and-war information-flow version comes from [[160-ruhe-yingdui-zhongguo-zichan-niushi-de-tiaozhengqi-xinshu-fenxianghui-chengdu-chang-shilu-lm8degdbgmgi6nnwspjy-gdzwqfz]]. [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] says ordinary investors usually cannot tell whether events such as Strait of Hormuz risk are underpriced or overpriced, so those headlines are better treated as context than direct trade signals. The same boundary applies to AI anxiety: if anxiety comes mainly from self-media, group chats, and peer pressure rather than the person's actual work exposure, the portfolio response should be slower and more structured.

The core rule comes from [[135-hongguan-dashi-pinfa-qi-ruhe-baochi-dingli-touzi-zhang-2025-banniandu-fupan-lism1-w05rt4jdwnun3l8wscymlq]]: macro data can often be ignored by ordinary investors, macro events may create opportunities, and macro trends deserve serious allocation attention. The page preserves that hierarchy while stressing that most household action should still run through [[AssetAllocation]], cash reserves, and behavior controls.

The long-cycle version comes from [[ep94-chuanyue-zhoujintao-rensheng-facai-kao-kangbo-shouzhu-jiadi-kao-shao-cuo-lndimnd_sb98tfmzv-kukbbnbtg8]]. The episode treats [[KondratievCycle]], [[InventoryCycle]], and [[JuglarCycle]] as useful context, but repeatedly refuses to answer ordinary-investor questions as "which year should I buy housing or stocks?" Before macro can become action, the investor must define the money source, use date, family obligations, liquidity need, action type, and ability to survive being wrong.

## Key Claims

- Macro information does not become useful merely because it is urgent, emotional, widely forwarded, grand, or attached to a famous past call.
- Ordinary investors should usually reduce news frequency, not increase trading frequency, during fast macro reversal.
- A person making a major asset-allocation decision, such as deploying house-sale proceeds or preparing a house purchase, may need more macro attention than a person with a stable long-term portfolio.
- Restless investors can isolate experimentation in a small short-term account, then compare it honestly with the long-term account.
- The boundary complements [[MacroNarrativeAnxiety]] because it accepts that world events matter while refusing to let them occupy every portfolio decision.
- Money needed for near-term life, tuition, elder care, emergency reserves, or mortgage service should not be turned into cycle speculation.

## Evidence

- **Macro event filtering:** [[135-hongguan-dashi-pinfa-qi-ruhe-baochi-dingli-touzi-zhang-2025-banniandu-fupan-lism1-w05rt4jdwnun3l8wscymlq]] separates data, events, and trends, and connects the rule to tariffs, gold, dollar volatility, Middle East risk, and portfolio review.
- **AI and war anxiety:** [[160-ruhe-yingdui-zhongguo-zichan-niushi-de-tiaozhengqi-xinshu-fenxianghui-chengdu-chang-shilu-lm8degdbgmgi6nnwspjy-gdzwqfz]] says China-asset, AI, war, and gold narratives should be translated through human capital, household liabilities, family cash flow, and information-source discipline.
- **Cycle misuse:** [[ep94-chuanyue-zhoujintao-rensheng-facai-kao-kangbo-shouzhu-jiadi-kao-shao-cuo-lndimnd_sb98tfmzv-kukbbnbtg8]] uses [[ZhouJintao]]'s cycle framework to warn that [[KondratievCycle]] context does not remove liquidity, leverage, or time-horizon constraints.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

- Professional macro allocators may have reasons, tools, and mandates that make macro trading more central than it is for households.
- Some macro events can create genuine opportunities, but the ordinary investor still needs a defined action, evidence threshold, and risk budget.
- This boundary does not dismiss macro; it slows the conversion from macro story to portfolio action.
