## Definition

Wuxia intertextual craft is the conversion of inherited literature, philosophy, religion, medicine, folklore, history, and genre convention into action, character, institutions, and emotional form rather than decorative allusion alone.

## Current Synthesis

The first source shows the wide system: names such as 北冥神功, 凌波微步, 降龙十八掌, 九阴真经, 空明拳, and 黯然销魂掌 make classical language, body imagination, political control, and emotional state operational inside wuxia. [[JinYong|金庸]] and [[LiangYusheng|梁羽生]] represent a learned branch, while [[GuLong|古龙]] is presented as taking a different route through suspense and mystery.

The [[YueNvJian|《越女剑》]] source sharpens how inherited material can be transformed through narrative design. Older Yue-maiden and white-ape motifs become [[AQingYueNvJian|阿青]]'s natural practice; historical Fan Li, Xi Shi, Goujian, and the Wu-Yue conflict become a triangular love story and political critique; and “西子捧心” becomes the visible remainder of an invisible romantic defeat. Intertextual craft therefore includes selection, omission, structural delay, and changed causal meaning, not only recognizable references.

## Key Claims

- Martial-art names can carry philosophy, poetry, religion, character state, and political discipline simultaneously.
- Allusion is functional when it changes how readers understand action, personality, institutions, or emotional pressure.
- Wuxia can inherit older literature without becoming reducible to quotation or one-to-one adaptation.
- Historical and legendary figures can be recombined so an inherited image acquires a new fictional cause.
- Indirect staging and omission are part of intertextual transformation because they determine how old material is experienced.
- Genre surface without a working cultural or emotional function is a thinner form of reference.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Recognizing an affinity does not establish a single source, authorial intention, or historical truth. The source claims about Jin Yong's learning, Daoist design, older records, and project history are podcast interpretations or reports unless independently documented. The distinction between deep transformation and decorative reference is analytical rather than a measurable hierarchy that automatically ranks whole authors or genres.
