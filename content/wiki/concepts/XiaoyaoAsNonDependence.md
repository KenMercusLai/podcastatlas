# Xiaoyao As Non-Dependence / 逍遥作为不被外物左右

Xiaoyao as non-dependence / 逍遥作为不被外物左右 is the episode's reading of freedom through [[Zhuangzi|庄子]] and [[LiChuang|李闯]]'s Wudang experience. In [[shan-shang-shan-xia-jie-renjian-wushi-xiaoyao-ji-shenxian-lhjiguq74flhatdxnbrnzhjg-acp]], "逍遥" is not presented as having every condition solved before happiness begins. It is closer to loosening the grip of external results, social clocks, money targets, and self-victimizing interpretation.

The concept extends [[SpiritualFreedomUnderConstraint]] and [[FinancialFreedomVsLifestyleFreedom]]. The source rejects the idea that freedom begins only after wealth, mystical ability, perfect certainty, or a clean exit from society. It asks how a person can eat, sleep, work, study, and face failure while being less completely controlled by external scoring systems.

## Key Claims
- Xiaoyao is not the absence of limits; it is reduced dependence on limits as the sole source of meaning.
- The source contrasts spiritual level with technical power: stronger abilities do not automatically remove anxiety if the same social logic remains.
- Desire inflation can make financial freedom unreachable if each new condition becomes a new prerequisite for freedom.
- Reframing failure can be a practical act when it shifts a person out of passive injury without denying the event happened.
