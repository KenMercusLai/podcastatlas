# Malthusian Trap / 马尔萨斯陷阱

The Malthusian trap enters the wiki through [[150-zhongguo-ruhe-tiaochu-pinkun-xianjing-xiandaihua-zhi-lu-you-hui-zouxiang-hefang-lgpvp04rngagkpubte2ac6i5ji3g]] as [[LiLu|李璐]]'s core description of agricultural civilization's limit. In the source, agricultural societies can improve technique and administration, but population pressure, land limits, war, famine, disease, climate shocks, and state failure repeatedly consume surplus.

The concept matters because the episode defines modernization by escape from this trap. [[ModernizationAsCompoundGrowth]] is the positive state that becomes possible when productivity, market coordination, science, and industrial technology compound faster than subsistence pressure.

## Key Claims
- Agricultural civilization can produce order and sophistication while still being structurally vulnerable to subsistence limits.
- Escaping the trap requires more than moral improvement or a capable court; it requires a growth mechanism that compounds.
- The source treats [[China]]'s historical state strength as real but insufficient to generate modern science and industrial capitalism on its own.
