## Definition

角色转换中的能力陷阱 / role-transition capability trap is the risk that a capability responsible for success in one role remains dominant after the person moves into a role whose value depends on different behaviors, turning a former strength into a constraint or system-level vulnerability.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 1164 uses [[XiahouYuan|夏侯渊]] to distinguish the work of a front-line opener from that of a theater commander. Speed, courage, improvisation, and personal exposure can solve early or local problems, but a senior command role also makes the leader's survival, listening, warning uptake, delegation, and system continuity part of the mission. Hanji 1163 supplies the episode's operational basis: pressure on Zhang He induces Xiahou Yuan to divide his force, after which he personally accompanies a small group fighting a fire and repairing defenses before Huang Zhong's fatal attack.

The trap is not high capability itself. It appears when identity, reinforcement, or organizational expectations keep one successful behavior dominant after the task has changed. The two notes are consecutive parts of the same popular-history interpretation, so the concept remains a management hypothesis rather than an independently demonstrated causal diagnosis of Xiahou Yuan's death.

## Key Claims

- A promotion or strategic transition can change the unit of value from personal output to decision quality and system continuity.
- A former strength becomes risky when it crowds out complementary behavior such as caution, listening, delegation, or protection of critical nodes.
- Repeated success can fuse a method with professional identity, making role-appropriate adaptation feel like weakness or passivity.
- The remedy is not suppressing the original strength but changing when, where, and through whom it is exercised.

## Evidence

Role change and critical-node risk:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1164-jingti-zhichang-tai-nenggan-de-xianjing-lrw8byuza9fzasd0ewwszkoj3mu|Hanji 1164]] argues that Xiahou Yuan continued to solve danger through personal front-line action after his position made his own survival part of the defensive system.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1163-gaogu-ziji-shibushi-zui-da-de-yuchun-lokzo7kcz69kf_lhgskx8kie-9_c|Hanji 1163]] supplies the divided-force, fire-suppression, repair, and attack sequence behind that interpretation.

Capability crowd-out:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1164-jingti-zhichang-tai-nenggan-de-xianjing-lrw8byuza9fzasd0ewwszkoj3mu|Hanji 1164]] pairs courage with reduced caution, decisiveness with reduced listening, and confidence with reduced warning sensitivity as possible transition failures.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept rests on two consecutive structured popular-history summaries and a modern analogy applied to an ancient command case. Xiahou Yuan's exact responsibilities, decision process, force allocation, fire and repair task, battlefield circumstances, and death mechanism require broader evidence. Personal action can remain valuable in senior roles, and caution can also become paralysis; the relevant question is whether behavior fits the changed responsibility and risk structure, not whether boldness or decisiveness is intrinsically defective.
