## Definition

Core-before-periphery strategy / 先内后外式战略顺序 is the decision rule that a ruler or commander should secure closer, easier, and more foundational regions before spending scarce attention on distant targets whose capture would not stabilize the center.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-690-jiaoyou-bushen-you-duo-kepa-kankan-tianrong-de-jingli-lpbms8pebzdo2ldiwho1cba81njk|Hanji 690]] grounds the concept through [[FuZhanEasternHan|傅湛]]'s remonstrance to [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]]. Liu Xiu plans to personally attack [[PengChongEasternHan|彭宠]] in Yuyang, but Fu Zhan argues that nearer regions such as Yan, Yu, Qing, and Yi still have unresolved disorder.

The concept is not anti-expansion or anti-punishment. It is about sequencing. Peng Chong remains a rebel, but the proposed personal campaign would move the ruler toward a distant difficult target while easier internal disorder remains unpacified. Liu Xiu accepts the advice, showing the concept at work as a corrected allocation of imperial attention.

## Key Claims

- Strategic priority depends on distance, difficulty, and contribution to central stability.
- A legitimate target can still be the wrong immediate target.
- Personal attention from the ruler is a scarce resource and should not be pulled toward peripheral campaigns too early.
- Nearer domestic disorder can be more important than a conspicuous rebel at the edge.
- Good remonstrance can change strategy by reframing the question from "should this enemy be fought" to "when should this enemy be fought."

## Evidence

- Proposed distant campaign: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-690-jiaoyou-bushen-you-duo-kepa-kankan-tianrong-de-jingli-lpbms8pebzdo2ldiwho1cba81njk|Hanji 690]] says Liu Xiu reaches Lu Nu and plans to personally attack Peng Chong.
- Nearer unsettled regions: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-690-jiaoyou-bushen-you-duo-kepa-kankan-tianrong-de-jingli-lpbms8pebzdo2ldiwho1cba81njk|Hanji 690]] says Fu Zhan points to Yan, Yu, Qing, and Yi disorder as prior concerns.
- Accepted correction: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-690-jiaoyou-bushen-you-duo-kepa-kankan-tianrong-de-jingli-lpbms8pebzdo2ldiwho1cba81njk|Hanji 690]] says Liu Xiu accepts Fu Zhan's advice and stops the personal expedition.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept is currently grounded in one campaign decision. It does not prove that peripheral threats should always wait; a frontier enemy can become urgent if it threatens the core or closes future options. Fu Zhan's claim works in this source because the domestic regions are still unsettled and Yuyang is comparatively remote.

## What Changed

- Created the concept from Fu Zhan's near-before-far remonstrance in Hanji 690.
