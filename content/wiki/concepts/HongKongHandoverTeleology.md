# Hong Kong Handover Teleology / 香港九七目的论

## Definition
Hong Kong handover teleology / 香港九七目的论 is the source's critique of stories that look back at 1997 Hong Kong from a later endpoint and convert uncertainty into a predetermined movement toward state order and cross-border integration.

## Current Synthesis
The concept is extracted from the source's reading of [[KongqiangFilm|《空枪》]]. The episode argues that older Hong Kong films often treated 1997 as an unsettled space: police, gangsters, capital, mainland arrivals, colonial administration, and local identity could compete without a single final authority feeling fully installed. 《空枪》, by contrast, looks back from 2026 with the result already known.

This later viewpoint makes disorder narratively solvable. Private violence is defeated, capital violence is morally condemned but not structurally analyzed, and mainland-Hong Kong police cooperation appears as the route to restored order. The source treats this as both the film's organizing power and its historical loss: the audience gets clarity, but the messy ambiguity of 1990s Hong Kong becomes a problem waiting for a predetermined solution.

## Key Claims
- Handover teleology turns 1997 from an uncertain threshold into an already-known destination.
- It makes state order the narrative answer to competing private, capital, and colonial/postcolonial powers.
- It can explain cross-border policing and integration while losing the suspended identity mood of older Hong Kong cinema.
- Capital violence is condemned affectively but often left structurally unresolved.
- The frame is inseparable from a later mainland vantage point rather than a 1990s Hong Kong self-description.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source's old-Hong Kong contrast is interpretive and selective. The concept needs additional sources before it can map the full range of pre- and post-handover Hong Kong film treatments.
