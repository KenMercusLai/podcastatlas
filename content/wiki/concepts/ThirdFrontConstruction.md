# Third Front Construction / 三线建设

Third Front Construction / 三线建设 is the strategic inland industrialization context in [[chengshi-jiushi-zheyang-21-shiyan-yizuo-yin-qiche-erqi-you-bugan-bei-qiche-paoxia-de-chengshi-1001770066]]. The episode describes it as a 1960s-era response to tense international conditions, with factories placed in central and western mountainous interiors for concealment and resilience.

The source uses [[SecondAutomobileWorks|二汽]] as a concrete Third Front case. Locating the project in [[Shiyan]] created a new industrial city, drew workers from other auto centers, and embedded factories in valleys, producing both strategic advantages and later logistics, land, and talent constraints.

Third Front Construction therefore feeds directly into [[AutomobileCityPathDependence]]. A site chosen for security and state mobilization can become a durable local capability, but the same geography and institutional design may be less suited to later passenger-car joint ventures, global supply chains, and headquarters functions.
