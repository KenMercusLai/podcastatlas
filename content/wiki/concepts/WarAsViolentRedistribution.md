# War as Violent Redistribution / 战争的暴力再分配

## Definition
War as Violent Redistribution / 战争的暴力再分配 is the source-scoped frame that war forcibly reallocates survival space: territory, population, ships, families, food, authority, and future security.

## Current Synthesis
Hanji 1038 introduces the frame through Sun Ce's Lujiang operation. The episode's strategic narrative is not only that [[SunCe|孙策]] defeats [[LiuXunLateHan|刘勋]], but that the victory transfers the practical base of survival: [[WanChengLujiang|皖城]], people, ships, family captives, and commandery appointment power move into Sun Ce's order after Liu Xun is lured away.

The concept should remain bounded. The source extends the claim to modern competition in broad language, but the wiki's current evidence is the late-Han campaign case rather than a general theory of all social or economic conflict.

## Key Claims
- War reallocates survival conditions through coercion, not only through battlefield casualties.
- Bases, people, supplies, ships, and family hostages can matter as much as the named victory.
- A successful campaign can combine positional deception with post-victory absorption of resources and authority.
- The concept is strongest when tied to concrete transfers rather than used as a loose metaphor for all competition.

## Evidence
- Material transfer after Wan City: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1038-sanshiliu-ji-zhi-diaohu-lishan-sunce-zhiqu-wancheng-lhomk8hfdidl61degwbqtj2xkj9z|Hanji 1038]] records Sun Ce taking Yuan Shu and Liu Xun family members, population, and ships after seizing Wan City.
- Authority transfer in Lujiang: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1038-sanshiliu-ji-zhi-diaohu-lishan-sunce-zhiqu-wancheng-lhomk8hfdidl61degwbqtj2xkj9z|Hanji 1038]] has Sun Ce appoint Li Shu to hold Lujiang, showing conquest converted into administrative control.
- Follow-on pressure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1038-sanshiliu-ji-zhi-diaohu-lishan-sunce-zhiqu-wancheng-lhomk8hfdidl61degwbqtj2xkj9z|Hanji 1038]] follows the Lujiang transfer with Liu Xun's failed reliance on Huang Zu and Sun Ce's further capture of ships and family members in the Huang Zu branch.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The current evidence is a single source's interpretive frame. It should not be promoted into a universal law without additional sources, and the host's modern extensions remain illustrative rather than independently evidenced by this episode.
