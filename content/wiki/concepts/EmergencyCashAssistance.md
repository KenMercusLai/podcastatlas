# Emergency Cash Assistance / 紧急现金援助

## Definition
Emergency cash assistance / 紧急现金援助 is direct monetary support used in humanitarian settings to let recipients obtain food or other essentials when in-kind distribution is limited, impractical, or reduced.

## Current Synthesis
The South Sudan episode presents emergency cash as both flexible and insufficient. After [[UNHCR]] registration, [[WorldFoodProgramme|WFP]] can provide newly arrived refugees with emergency cash and high-energy biscuits; longer-term refugees also depend on periodic cash because the fuller food basket has been cut.

The source's core lesson is that cash assistance works only inside a usable survival ecology. If refugees lack jobs, farmland, government support, and stable benefit levels, cash becomes the thin line between survival and hunger rather than a complete replacement for food aid.

## Key Claims
- Cash can speed and simplify assistance after registration.
- Cash-only aid becomes risky when it replaces a fuller food basket under funding cuts.
- Recipient autonomy does not remove the need for adequate transfer size and market access.
- Aid cards become high-stakes identity and payment infrastructure when they are the only route to support.
- Extreme need can push families to pawn future aid access for present food.

## Evidence
- Post-registration support: [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] says WFP provides emergency cash and high-energy biscuits after registration.
- Food-basket reduction: [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] says earlier grain and food-basket support stopped, leaving reduced cash assistance.
- Aid-card fragility: [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] describes a mother pawning her WFP card to obtain food for her children.
- Lack of alternatives: [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] says camp residents have little work, limited farming space, and few fallback survival routes.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not compare cash assistance against all possible food-distribution models. It shows a cash program under sharp funding and livelihood constraints, so the qualification is adequacy and context rather than a blanket rejection of cash aid.
