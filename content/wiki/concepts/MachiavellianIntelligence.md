# Machiavellian Intelligence / 马基雅维利智能

Machiavellian intelligence / 马基雅维利智能 is the social-cognition frame introduced in [[83-shumao-bagua-ji-yuyan-de-jinhua-renji-guanxi-dengbashu-zan-bu-fu-bu-xing-698132861]]. In the episode, it means the ability to track not only direct relationships, but also how other individuals relate to third parties, so that one can choose allies, avoid danger, repair conflict, or join a coalition.

The concept is distinct from [[MachiavellianRealism]], the wiki's political-reading page around Machiavelli. Here "Machiavellian" points to social intelligence under group pressure, not to a doctrine that power alone decides politics.

## Key Claims
- Social life becomes cognitively hard when relationships among third parties matter.
- Alliance tracking makes grooming, gossip, reputation, and conversation more valuable.
- The concept helps explain why group size and social complexity can place pressure on cognition.
- It should not be treated as proof that all social behavior is cynical manipulation.
