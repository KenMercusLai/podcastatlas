# Vertical Fandom Crowdfunding / 垂直圈层众筹

Vertical fandom crowdfunding is the funding and early-distribution pattern shown by [[DragonSpiritGameSeries|龙魂]] in [[ep242-duli-youxi-shi-yitiao-gaoqian-hao-saidao-ma-lrqbzrceumbuggzuero5qb4jv3zu]]. A creator does not begin with a general audience; they begin with a concentrated group whose identity, taste, or character investments make the project unusually legible.

In the source, dragon enthusiasts could support the project and sometimes see their own dragon designs appear in the game. This creates stronger attachment than ordinary pre-ordering, but it also creates production risk: as quality standards rise, user-submitted characters require more art direction, animation work, and consistency checks.

## Key Claims
- A small fandom can provide money, feedback, legitimacy, and early word of mouth before mainstream demand exists.
- The product must genuinely fit the fandom; generic marketing into a niche is not enough.
- Letting supporters enter the work can strengthen identity attachment but can also create quality-control debt.
- The pattern is adjacent to [[CommunityEmbeddedBusiness]], but narrower: it describes source funding and participation, not a whole institution embedded in a community.
