# Psychiatric Functional Diagnosis / 精神科功能受损诊断

## Definition
Psychiatric functional diagnosis / 精神科功能受损诊断 is the episode's clinical boundary that mental-health diagnosis depends on symptoms, course, context, and functional impairment, not on isolated traits, online scales, or self-applied labels.

## Current Synthesis
The source treats psychiatric diagnosis as a professional judgment under context. [[JiangTaoPsychiatrist|江涛]] says clinicians need to understand onset, symptoms, life history, education, culture, religion, family personality patterns, and social function before deciding whether a person's condition requires medication, psychotherapy, hospitalization, or other support.

The functional threshold is central. A person can sound unusual without being clinically abnormal if work, relationships, family role, communication, and self-care remain intact. Conversely, emotional, cognitive, behavioral, or psychotic symptoms become more urgent when they impair daily functioning or create self-harm, suicide, or public-safety risk.

The 史蒂夫说 source extends this boundary to popular diagnostic language. Jiang Tao cautions that ADHD, NPD, schizophrenia, adolescent self-harm, and school refusal cannot be diagnosed from online vocabulary or single traits. VOL.108 makes the functional rule concrete for pediatric ADHD: clinicians compare attention, activity, impulse, learning, emotion, relationships, and daily execution with developmental expectations and settings rather than treating “active,” “naughty,” “smart,” or one high-performance moment as diagnosis. Serious illness must not be shamed, but fashionable labels can also trivialize or distort the clinical work of assessment.

## Key Claims
- Psychiatric diagnosis should not be reduced to online self-tests, symptom checklists, or popular vocabulary; professional scales remain auxiliary to clinician-guided interview and judgment.
- Social function is a major clinical threshold: work, relationships, family role, communication, and self-care matter.
- Cultural, religious, developmental, family, and life-history context affects how symptoms should be interpreted.
- Severe psychotic illness may involve impaired insight, so "I am worried I have schizophrenia" does not equal a schizophrenia diagnosis.
- Early help in the gray zone matters because functional decline can deepen if ignored.
- Popular labels can distort diagnosis when ADHD, NPD, schizophrenia, or adolescent crisis language is applied without developmental history, impairment, risk, and face-to-face assessment.
- For pediatric ADHD, age-relative and cross-setting function matters more than activity level, grades, or isolated high performance.

## Evidence
- Intake scope - [[shidai-zhenghou-yu-anding-cixin-lphaw0qek8ibr0jt44yygou6akql]] describes psychiatric first visits as covering course of illness, symptoms, life history, education, and background.
- Function threshold - [[shidai-zhenghou-yu-anding-cixin-lphaw0qek8ibr0jt44yygou6akql]] says work ability, relationship ability, family ability, and self-care impairment can indicate a need for intervention.
- Normal-abnormal spectrum - [[shidai-zhenghou-yu-anding-cixin-lphaw0qek8ibr0jt44yygou6akql]] rejects a simple line between normality and abnormality and treats social function as a key marker.
- Self-test boundary - [[shidai-zhenghou-yu-anding-cixin-lphaw0qek8ibr0jt44yygou6akql]] warns that online scales lack proper instructions, reliability, validity, and professional interpretation.
- Insight boundary - [[shidai-zhenghou-yu-anding-cixin-lphaw0qek8ibr0jt44yygou6akql]] distinguishes ordinary self-worry from severe psychosis where insight may be absent.
- Internet-label boundary - [[shi-di-fu-shuo-466-jiangtao-5wan-jingshenke-yisheng-dui-1yi-jingshen-jibing-huanzhe-lpdsk96akpwtccxyesjdgtw8skbn]] warns against casual ADHD, NPD, and schizophrenia labeling and says adult ADHD needs professional scales, interview, and diagnosis rather than online self-diagnosis.
- Adolescent-function boundary - [[shi-di-fu-shuo-466-jiangtao-5wan-jingshenke-yisheng-dui-1yi-jingshen-jibing-huanzhe-lpdsk96akpwtccxyesjdgtw8skbn]] treats school refusal and self-harm as requiring family, school, developmental, symptom, and risk context rather than symptom naming alone.
- Pediatric ADHD function - [[lvfirgzbmtwypcjqyqry291qz6cw-lvfirgzbmtwypcjqyqry291qz6cw]] uses learning, classroom rules, homework, emotional control, peer conflict, and daily execution to separate ordinary variation from clinically relevant impairment.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This page is not a diagnostic guide. The sources are public podcast conversations and do not provide DSM, ICD, medication, intelligence-testing, risk-assessment, or hospitalization protocols. Functional impairment is a central warning signal in the sources, but it is not the only criterion and cannot replace professional evaluation.
