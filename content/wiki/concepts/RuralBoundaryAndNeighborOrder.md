## Definition
Rural boundary and neighbor order / 乡村边界与邻里秩序 is the local social pattern where sharing, help, scrutiny, field boundaries, and property limits coexist.

## Current Synthesis
The source resists the fantasy that rural generosity means weak boundaries. In [[Chongming|崇明]], neighbors can repair a shed, share know-how, recognize each other as farmers, and tolerate interaction, while still treating planted plots, bamboo shoots, field lines, and unauthorized planting as serious boundary matters.

## Key Claims
- Rural neighborliness can be generous and boundary-conscious at the same time.
- Field lines and planted areas carry social meaning even when outsiders mistake them for open nature.
- Help can create gratitude and embarrassment because rural skill is publicly visible.
- Family members and neighbors become informal evaluators of whether a plot looks properly tended.
- The concept keeps rural community from being idealized as either pure warmth or pure conflict.

## Evidence
- Boundary examples: [[yu-duan-zhiqiang-tan-chongming-xiangye-bowuzhi-wo-zhu-changjiang-wei-8212963740-775078]] describes mistaken wild-vegetable gathering, bamboo-shoot conflict, and landlord guidance on field edges.
- Help and scrutiny: [[yu-duan-zhiqiang-tan-chongming-xiangye-bowuzhi-wo-zhu-changjiang-wei-8212963740-775078]] describes an older neighbor correcting Duan's melon shed and Duan later retreating to yard planting partly from embarrassment.
- Farmer recognition: [[yu-duan-zhiqiang-tan-chongming-xiangye-bowuzhi-wo-zhu-changjiang-wei-8212963740-775078]] says neighbors ask when Duan's father will return because his work makes the land look more like it is handled by a real farmer.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode gives local anecdotes rather than a general sociology of village property relations. The concept should be applied as a pattern visible in this source, not as a universal rule for all rural communities.

## What Changed
- Created a concept for the episode's property-boundary and neighbor-order material.
- Separated the social order of rural sharing from the material order of reuse.
