## Definition

Multidimensional mentor quality / 多维良师评价 is the evaluation of a teacher through distinct dimensions of domain ability, teaching ability, character and emotional stability, care for learners, reputation, and relationship or institutional access.

## Current Synthesis

The framework begins by refusing the most common shortcut: being excellent at a craft does not prove that someone can teach it. Instruction requires diagnosing the learner, sequencing difficulty, explaining principles, designing practice, and adjusting methods to personality and foundation. The episode's comparisons among [[ZhangSanfengJinYong|张三丰]], the Jiangnan Seven Freaks, Feng Qingyang, Xiaolongnü, and the divine condor show that different mentors can be strong in comprehensive guidance, long-term care, conceptual breakthrough, intimate instruction, or practice design.

Care, steadiness, reputation, and networks matter because education is not only information transfer. A mentor can protect a learner, create belonging, lend social credibility, open access to other teachers, or damage learning through jealousy, volatility, factional prejudice, concealment, and exploitation. The six dimensions should therefore remain separate enough to reveal tradeoffs rather than collapse into a single popularity or power score.

The [[ljyhr2aom69hckwuywi4czovrhcu|Top 3 roundtable]] independently converges on this separation through desired teachers rather than a full scorecard. Zhang Sanfeng is valued for warmth, humility, medical effort, and instruction adapted to different disciples; the divine condor is valued for rapid practice design, environmental training, and material support. The contrast reinforces that care and effective learning design can appear in different forms, while joking selections such as Kangxi also reveal that “ideal teacher” may partly encode a learner's wish to avoid hardship or institutional control.

The [[lowwo0ixlp--ouxy87p42cult3k4|《鹿鼎记》 episode]] adds [[AjiuBixueJian|九难]] as a case where learner fit, practical transfer, and relationship quality converge. Rather than demand the complete 铁剑门 foundation, she gives [[WeiXiaobao|韦小宝]] 神行百变, which matches his aptitude and need to escape. Mutual care and shared danger deepen the teaching relationship, but her treatment of 阿珂 and 阿琪 also sharpens an ethical boundary: accurate adaptation for one learner does not establish consistently benevolent mentorship across all pupils.

The earlier Teachers' Day episode on Hai Dafu and Chen Jinnan separates the dimensions still further. [[HaiDafu|海大富]] has no formal master status and acts coercively, yet transmits techniques and political caution that Wei immediately retains. [[ChenJinnan|陈近南]] is the formal master but contributes most through recognition, trust, moral example, and surrogate fatherhood rather than martial progress. Effective transmission, legitimate authority, care, and moral influence can therefore vary independently; political idealization can also limit an otherwise admired mentor.

## Key Claims

- Domain mastery and teaching ability are related but non-equivalent capacities.
- Effective teaching adapts method, sequence, and practice to the learner's foundation, temperament, and situation.
- Emotional stability and genuine care affect whether authority becomes protective, jealous, punitive, or exploitative.
- Reputation and networks can widen opportunity, but prestige cannot compensate for poor teaching or harm.
- A mentor may be valuable through long companionship, brief conceptual reframing, or well-designed practice rather than one universal style.
- Good teachers need not demand exclusive loyalty and can welcome instruction that better serves the learner.
- Formal title and ethical quality do not guarantee practical transfer, and practical transfer does not prove ethical quality.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The six-part framework and follow-up cases come from entertainment-oriented literary discussions, not a validated teacher-evaluation instrument. Reputation and network access may reproduce hierarchy as easily as they expand opportunity, and affection can become control when boundaries are weak. Desired teachers can reflect fantasy benefits, personal affection, or avoidance of effort rather than educational outcomes. A successful method with one pupil does not prove fair treatment of others, while a learner's later affection does not retroactively excuse coercion. Character judgments, martial rankings, political ideals, teaching speed, and inferred motives remain source-scoped. The framework is most useful as a checklist that prevents expertise, prestige, warmth, learner fit, practical transfer, or preference from standing in for the whole teaching relationship.

## What Changed

- Added Hai Dafu to separate practical transfer from formal authority, care, and ethical quality.
- Added Chen Jinnan to distinguish moral formation and surrogate parenthood from martial teaching outcomes.
- Added political judgment as a qualification on mentor idealization.
