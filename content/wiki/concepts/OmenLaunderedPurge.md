# Omen-Laundered Purge / 天象包装式清洗

Omen-laundered purge / 天象包装式清洗 is the pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] extracts from [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s killing of [[ZhangWenLateHan|张温]]. A court astrologer predicts that a minister will be killed, and Dong Zhuo uses that sign to accuse Zhang Wen of secret contact with [[YuanShao|袁绍]], beat him to death publicly, and then present the death as fulfillment of celestial warning.

The concept is a negative branch inside [[ChineseAstrologicalPolitics|中国星占政治]] and [[CelestialOmenPoliticalResponsibility|天象政治责任]]. The omen does not hold the ruler accountable; it gives the coercive actor a narrative scaffold. Private resentment and political clearing are laundered through a public sign so the killing appears less like personal revenge and more like an event already authorized by Heaven.

Hanji 957 also stresses audience psychology. Officials do not strongly resist because the omen frame, Dong Zhuo's violence, and self-preservation combine to make the event feel survivable for everyone except Zhang Wen. In that sense the purge works through fear management as much as through formal accusation.

## Key Claims

- A celestial sign can be used to explain a killing after the actor has already selected a target.
- Omen language can shift attention from private motive to public inevitability.
- The technique depends on an audience that already believes signs matter or at least fears challenging the person interpreting them.
- The concept does not endorse celestial causation; it records a political use of sign language.
