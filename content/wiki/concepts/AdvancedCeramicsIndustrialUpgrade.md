# Advanced Ceramics Industrial Upgrade / 先进陶瓷产业升级

Advanced Ceramics Industrial Upgrade / 先进陶瓷产业升级 is the modern industrial branch in [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]]. The episode says Jingdezhen's advanced ceramics output is growing and had reached several hundred billion RMB in 2025, making up roughly a third of the city's ceramic industrial output.

The concept keeps Jingdezhen from being read only as heritage tourism or studio craft. It links older [[JingdezhenPorcelainProductionSystem|porcelain process knowledge]], [[JingdezhenCeramicUniversity|ceramic education]], material science, and local manufacturing capacity to higher-value industrial applications.
