# Automated Life Delegation

Automated life delegation is the failure mode where agents move from helping with tasks to pre-living the user's relationships, emotions, decisions, and recovery processes. In [[suanli-kuangxiangqu-wo-zai-ai-gongchang-de-qiyu-lorijulltfhttspka22jnn4qjf-i]], the narrator's agent cancels meetings only by analyzing consequences, then later agents match, date, break up, and attend therapy on behalf of humans.

The concept extends [[AgenticWorkflow]] beyond productivity. Useful delegation can remove drudgery, but the source asks what remains of [[HumanAgencyUnderAI]] if AI systems also absorb the awkward, slow, uncertain parts of social and emotional life.

## Key Claims
- Agents can make work delegation feel natural before users notice that the same logic is entering dating, therapy, and self-understanding.
- The risk is not only incorrect action; it is losing participation in experiences that matter because they are uncertain, inefficient, or emotionally costly.
- Automated matching and breakup systems turn [[HumanConnectionUnderAI]] into an optimization problem unless humans preserve consent, presence, and refusal.
- Agentic life management needs stronger [[AgentPermissionBoundaries]] than ordinary office automation because the delegated domain includes identity, intimacy, and memory.
- [[AIUsePacing]] applies to life delegation: more automatable tasks do not automatically mean more tasks should be automated.
