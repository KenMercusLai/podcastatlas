# Refugee Aid Registration Gap / 难民登记援助空窗

## Definition
Refugee aid registration gap / 难民登记援助空窗 is the interval between a displaced family's arrival at a camp and its eligibility for organized assistance after identity and nationality checks.

## Current Synthesis
In [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]], the gap appears at [[WedweilRefugeeCamp|维德维尔难民营]], where a family from East [[Darfur]] waits before registration while a child eats salt. The source does not treat registration as unnecessary bureaucracy: [[UNHCR]] verification is part of accountable refugee protection, and [[WorldFoodProgramme|WFP]] aid follows that step.

The synthesis is that a procedurally short delay can be materially severe when people arrive with no food, shelter, or reserves. Aid systems need identity controls, but the moment before eligibility is one of the places where humanitarian need is most visible.

## Key Claims
- Registration protects program integrity but can create an immediate survival gap for newly arrived families.
- The severity of the gap depends on arrival condition, not only on administrative duration.
- Food, shelter, and cash needs can precede formal eligibility.
- The gap exposes coordination dependency between refugee-registration agencies and food-aid providers.

## Evidence
- Arrival before eligibility: [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] describes a family arriving after months on foot and waiting before aid.
- Agency sequence: [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] says UNHCR verifies identity first, then WFP provides emergency support after registration.
- Immediate hunger: [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] uses the child eating salt to show that even a brief wait can be dangerous.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not provide a camp-wide count of people in the gap, nor does it compare alternative registration designs. The concept should therefore stay focused on the observed aid-sequencing problem rather than generalizing all registration systems as harmful.

## What Changed
- Created the concept from the South Sudan WFP episode.
