# New Lab Organization

New Lab Organization is the hybrid research-company form described in [[149-qinli-zhongmei-new-labs-ziben-kuangchao-he-qinghua-liuziming-liao-ai-for-ai-jizhi-kejieshixing-he-max-tegmark-lm33q4n6w8tzcd2fxdbuk9unc2xv]]. The source says 2026 brought a wave of young, elite-research-background AI teams in China and abroad that can raise at large valuations before ordinary product proof, especially around [[AIForScience]], [[WorldModels]], and [[AIForAI]].

[[LiuZiming|Liu Ziming]] defines the form by its temporary ambiguity. A New Lab begins closer to a research institute, giving itself six to twelve months for R&D, demos, and product-shape discovery. If it "groks" the route, it can then operate more like a company. The source argues this may be necessary when a direction is too risky for large companies and too slow for traditional academia.

## Key Claims
- New Labs target pain points that the current dominant AI paradigm does not solve well.
- Their early advantage is belief, risk appetite, speed, and dense young research talent rather than mature product distribution.
- The form relies on venture capital accepting research uncertainty for a limited period.
- Auto Research and world models are especially suitable because they require research before easy engineering scale-up.
- The form can create a tension between scientific exploration and product/commercialization pressure after the first research window.
