# Value-Investing Prerequisite Stack / 价值投资前提栈

## Definition
The value-investing prerequisite stack is the episode's framework for the conditions beneath patient business ownership: investable capital that can wait, continuing personal cash flow, operating understanding, valuation discipline, and behavioral mechanisms that keep the investor from breaking the method under pressure.

## Current Synthesis
The framework distinguishes an investment idea from the life system capable of carrying it. Buying below estimated value and holding for business results can work only if living expenses, client redemptions, leverage, status consumption, or emotional urgency do not force an exit before the thesis matures. Stable capital is therefore not merely a larger starting balance; it is money whose time horizon matches the asset.

Knowledge is equally practical. Financial statements and public stories do not automatically reveal hiring difficulty, customer concentration, employee loss, sales friction, internal incentives, competitive response, taxes, fees, or the difference between a people-dependent firm and one whose standardized capacity can scale with capital. Because few opportunities will fall inside a genuinely informed circle of competence, the method requires saying no often. For many people, the episode's reproducible alternative is to build human capital and cash flow while using diversified index exposure rather than imitating a famous investor's positions.

## Key Claims
- Patient investing requires capital whose holding period is not dictated by near-term living costs, debt, leverage, or client withdrawals.
- Continuing earned or passive cash flow reduces the pressure to make the portfolio produce immediate income or price gains.
- Business understanding includes operating frictions and incentives that accounting data and founder narratives may omit.
- Valuation gains and sentiment rerating can be profitable without being the same return source as long-term participation in business cash flow.
- A narrow circle of competence makes opportunity rejection a core skill rather than a sign of inactivity.
- Behavioral self-binding can protect a sound prior decision when market movement, family excitement, or social comparison makes willpower unreliable.
- Broad index investing can be a rational endpoint for people who do not possess or want to build the full active-investing stack.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The framework comes from one guest's retrospective experience rather than comparative performance research. Operating experience may improve judgment without guaranteeing valuation skill, honesty, or investment returns, and relevant industry expertise can be acquired through paths other than founding a company. Passive investing still carries market, horizon, concentration, currency, and behavior risks. The stack is best used as a readiness test, not a credential that predicts success or personalized financial advice.

- Preserved index investing as a legitimate endpoint rather than treating active selection as the inevitable advanced stage.
