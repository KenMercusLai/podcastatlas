## Definition
Financial AI agents help people or organizations interpret financial data, structure decisions, monitor changing conditions, and sometimes execute governed finance workflows without erasing compliance, authorization, risk, or human accountability.

## Current Synthesis
The evidence spans three layers. For ordinary investors, agents can explain terminology, organize filings and evidence, compare bullish and bearish views, maintain watchlists, and prompt disciplined follow-up without becoming autonomous stock pickers. For personal finance, transaction access can support budgeting, cash-flow review, and asset organization when bank APIs and consent controls exist. For business finance, agents can connect accounts, policies, expenses, reconciliation, forecasts, reporting, and payments when authority is explicit and auditable.

Across all three, access to broader financial data increases utility and risk together. Useful agents need current context, clear limits, compliance, privacy, reversible actions, and escalation; stronger reasoning does not remove market uncertainty or make investment advice safe to follow blindly.

## Key Claims
- Financial agents lower the knowledge and organization burden by translating terminology, structuring questions, and maintaining evidence over time.
- Retail-investment agents should support research, thesis comparison, watchlists, alerts, and decision logging rather than issue unqualified buy or sell instructions.
- Personal-finance agents become more useful when authorized bank APIs expose transactions, balances, budgets, and cash flow continuously.
- Business-finance agents need governed access to accounts, policies, workflows, and payment authority; without an action layer they remain explanation tools.
- Broader data access can improve context while increasing privacy, security, fraud, and overreach risk.
- Compliance, suitability, authorization, auditability, and human responsibility remain core product boundaries.
- Multi-agent disagreement is useful when it exposes catalysts, risks, and uncertainty instead of manufacturing false consensus.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Lowering the information barrier does not create investment edge, eliminate behavioral bias, or guarantee better outcomes. Bank access varies by jurisdiction, institution, and regulation, and the latest episode offers a host-level market comparison rather than an API or regulatory survey. Automated categorization can be wrong; payment or trading authority magnifies fraud and operational risk. The cited product and workflow accounts do not establish long-term returns, consumer benefit, compliance sufficiency, or safe autonomy.

## What Changed
- Added personal budgeting and cash-flow management as a layer between investment research and business finance operations.
- Made open banking or bank-API availability an explicit deployment condition.
- Strengthened the warning that model capability does not transfer investment responsibility.
