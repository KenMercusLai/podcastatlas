# Regional Airport Public-Service Value / 中小机场公共服务价值

## Definition
Regional airport public-service value is the access, resilience, and travel-time benefit an airport can provide to remote or difficult terrain even when route revenue and airport accounts do not cover its costs.

## Current Synthesis
The [[JiuzhaiHuanglongAirport|Jiuzhai Huanglong Airport]] case shows the commercial weakness of a tourism-heavy airport: seasonal demand, limited routine local traffic, and improved rail or roads can remove enough passengers to collapse scheduled service. Yet a profit-only test can miss network and public benefits. The correct comparison is not airport loss in isolation but the incremental access, time savings, emergency and territorial connectivity, subsidy requirement, and opportunity cost relative to road, rail, or no project.

## Key Claims
- Tourism dependence creates volatile and seasonal route economics.
- Thin local business and family-travel demand reduces year-round resilience.
- Better rail and roads can substitute for air service and shrink catchment demand.
- Commercial losses do not by themselves prove that an airport lacks social value.
- Public value must still be compared with subsidies, alternatives, and actual usage.

## Evidence
- **Commercial fragility:** [[dafeixin-pingguo-xin-xitong-keyi-jinzhi-yaoyiyao-guanggao-zhangyiming-zai-deng-hurun-baifu-bangshou-gkwridoot6kmafx6cwtxdrfn]] links reported flight loss at Jiuzhai Huanglong Airport to tourism seasonality and weak everyday traffic.
- **Substitution:** [[dafeixin-pingguo-xin-xitong-keyi-jinzhi-yaoyiyao-guanggao-zhangyiming-zai-deng-hurun-baifu-bangshou-gkwridoot6kmafx6cwtxdrfn]] says high-speed rail and improved roads diverted potential airport demand.
- **Public-service case:** [[dafeixin-pingguo-xin-xitong-keyi-jinzhi-yaoyiyao-guanggao-zhangyiming-zai-deng-hurun-baifu-bangshou-gkwridoot6kmafx6cwtxdrfn]] argues that airports can materially shorten travel in large, mountainous, and border regions and compares their construction cost with linear road and rail infrastructure.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source provides no route subsidy, operating-cost, passenger-time, emissions, emergency-access, or alternative-project model. Construction cost per airport is not directly comparable with cost per kilometer of rail or road without network coverage, capacity, maintenance, and usage assumptions.

## What Changed
- Added a cost-benefit frame that separates airport profitability from social access value.
- Made alternative modes, subsidy burden, and realized use necessary qualifications.
