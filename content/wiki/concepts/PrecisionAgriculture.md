# Precision Agriculture

Precision agriculture is the farm-management pattern where land is measured and acted on at finer resolution than a whole field or farm. In [[tech-20260107-0107-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260107-0107-mp-tech-pod-128]], [[AndrewNelson]] names GPS-guided tractors as a technology that lets farmers break land into smaller parts and scrutinize what is happening there.

The wiki role of the concept is to connect [[DigitalAgriculture]] to operational decisions. Better location, imagery, and field-state data only matter if they help with planting, spraying, weed control, or input allocation under real equipment and price constraints.

## Key Claims
- Precision agriculture depends on making field variation visible and manageable.
- GPS-guided equipment can turn a field from one broad operating unit into many smaller decision zones.
- [[DroneCropMapping]] extends the same logic by using imagery and AI analysis to find weed patterns the farmer may miss.
- Precision tools remain decision support: the farmer still decides whether the detected variation warrants action.
