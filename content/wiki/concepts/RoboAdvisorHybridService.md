# Robo-Advisor Hybrid Service / 人机结合投顾

Robo-advisor hybrid service is the [[145-jijin-tougu-zhide-xinren-ma-lrckug0zjqolcczni8ajikb0k5mi]] comparison model for combining automated portfolio construction with human consultation. The episode contrasts pure robo-advice, human-plus-algorithm advice, and traditional human advisory to show how different cost structures and client needs can coexist in a mature wealth-management market.

The source uses [[Wealthfront]] as a robo-advisor reference and [[CharlesSchwab]] as an example of tiered service. It also uses [[Vanguard]] to show how online questionnaires, algorithmic allocation, and scheduled human calls can serve mid-asset clients while more complex clients receive deeper planning.

## Key Claims
- Robo-advice can lower advisory cost by automating asset allocation, rebalancing, and questionnaire-based matching.
- Hybrid advice is useful when clients need human clarification around tax, retirement, family assets, or anxiety during market moves.
- Automation does not solve [[GoalBasedClientProfiling]] by itself; the profile still has to capture the client's real goal and constraints.
- A tiered model can serve small accounts with low-cost automation while reserving human time for clients whose situation is more complex.
- China's fund advisory gap is not only technological; the source argues that service norms, fee trust, and post-purchase accompaniment are also missing.
