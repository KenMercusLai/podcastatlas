# Anti-Lyricism / 反抒情

[[AntiLyricism|Anti-lyricism / 反抒情]] enters the wiki through [[12-man-dang-milan-kundela-yi-ju-zhengjing-meiyou-549335675]] as a way to describe [[MilanKundera|Kundera]]'s resistance to beautiful self-expression that hides comedy, dirt, or self-deception. The episode connects this to [[SlowKundera|《慢》]], where crude words, sexual awkwardness, and farce keep breaking lyrical elevation.

The concept is close to [[KitschAsWasteDenial]] but emphasizes style and posture. Where kitsch denies the dirty remainder of life, anti-lyricism refuses to let characters turn shame, desire, memory, or politics into noble song too quickly.

## Key Claims
- Anti-lyricism uses ugliness, interruption, and irony to resist emotional beautification.
- It can protect literature from moral overcleaning, but it can also feel hostile or unserious to readers expecting dignity.
- In the Kundera source, the method keeps [[PublicMoralPerformance]] and sexual self-staging from becoming heroic.
