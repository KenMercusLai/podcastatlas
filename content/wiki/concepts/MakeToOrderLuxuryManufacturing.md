# Make-To-Order Luxury Manufacturing

Make-to-order luxury manufacturing is the operating model where production begins from a customer order and customization path rather than from dealer inventory. In [[ferrari-1]], [[Ferrari]] uses this model in [[Maranello]] to make each car feel specific, scarce, and connected to the factory.

The source treats the apparent inefficiency as part of the value proposition. Custom paint, materials, options, delivery rituals, and factory control are not only costs; they help customers believe they are buying participation in Ferrari rather than simply acquiring a vehicle.

## Key Claims
- Make-to-order can support margins when customers value uniqueness, waiting, and provenance.
- Flexible manufacturing can be strategically valuable even if it looks less efficient than mass production.
- The model depends on demand visibility, allocation discipline, and trust that the brand will not flood the market.
