# Manufacturing Digital Thread / 制造数字主线

Manufacturing digital thread / 制造数字主线 is the operational data chain that links orders, materials, warehouse systems, equipment, workers, production state, inspection, and delivery. [[ep271-tanfang-rouxing-zhizao-gongchang-shi-shui-rang-ni-xiangyao-jiu-neng-maidao-lqyrioptggnbb8hl-llej0-5ow0v]] makes the concept concrete through [[DeliGroup|得力]]'s pen factory: orders are matched to materials and inventory, AGVs deliver material to line-side shelves, workers scan to receive parts, and the system tracks progress and remaining stock.

The concept explains why [[FlexibleManufacturing|柔性制造]] cannot be reduced to a flexible machine. When ordinary SKUs, IP-linked variants, rush orders, and customized tasks share equipment or workers, the plant needs a common data layer to prevent wrong materials, hidden delays, and quality leakage. In the [[HaierGroup|海尔]] case, the digital thread connects with [[COSMOPlat|卡奥斯]], [[ManufacturingDigitalTwin|digital twin]] simulation, and [[ModularCustomization|module recombination]].

## Key Claims
- A digital thread turns a production order into a live coordination object across warehouse, materials, workers, machines, and status dashboards.
- Scanning and error alerts matter because flexible production raises the chance of residue, wrong parts, or configuration mistakes between orders.
- The digital thread is different from pure automation: it can coordinate human work, AGVs, machines, inspection, and planning in one process.
- The more variable the product mix, the more valuable shared operational state becomes.
- A useful digital thread should reduce rework, waiting, and confusion rather than only produce dashboard data.
