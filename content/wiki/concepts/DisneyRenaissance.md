# Disney Renaissance

Disney Renaissance is the animation revival arc in [[disney-the-renaissance-and-the-empire-1]], where [[TheWaltDisneyCompany]] moves from weak post-Walt animation to a run of culturally central animated musicals. The source ties the shift to [[MichaelEisner]], [[FrankWells]], [[JeffreyKatzenberg]], Peter Schneider, stronger internal process, and musical-theater collaborators such as Howard Ashman and Alan Menken.

The concept matters because the renaissance repairs the [[EntertainmentIPFlywheel]]. New animated stories do not stop at theatrical box office: home video, merchandise, Broadway, parks, soundtracks, and repeat generational attachment convert creative renewal into business-system renewal.

## Key Claims
- Disney's post-Walt flywheel could harvest old IP, but it needed new emotional fuel to remain culturally central.
- Broadway-musical structure gave animated films clearer character desire, songs, and repeatable emotional arcs.
- The renaissance was both creative and operational: story process, technology, home video, pricing, merchandising, and distribution all mattered.
- The revival later decayed, which is why [[Pixar]] becomes a second [[CreativeCoreRenewal]] mechanism.
