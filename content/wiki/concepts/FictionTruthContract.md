## Definition

Fiction–truth contract is the distinction between openly invented narration that asks readers to interpret imagined events and deceptive narration that claims factual authority for what it cannot support.

## Current Synthesis

[[ATrueStory|《真实的故事》]] announces that its voyage never happened and could not happen, then imitates the exact measures, troop counts, ethnographic detail, and confident posture of history and travel writing. The work does not become factually true; instead, the declared invention gives readers a stable basis for recognizing the parody.

That contract permits fiction to disclose real patterns without presenting imagined events as evidence. Lunar kingdoms, male pregnancy, and detachable organs can make authority, conquest, hierarchy, embodiment, and desire visible precisely because the reader is not asked to mistake them for reportage.

## Key Claims

- Acknowledged invention and false factual assertion make different truth claims.
- Precise style can simulate authority without supplying evidence.
- Fiction can reveal recurring social patterns without becoming literal testimony.
- Satire depends on enough shared convention for readers to recognize the target of imitation.
- The ethical boundary lies partly in the expectation established between narrator and audience.

## Evidence

- Declared invention: [[371-liangqian-nianqian-jiu-you-ren-xiangxiang-chu-saiboge-nanren-duli-shengyu-xingji-zhanzheng-le-ma-zhenshi-de-gushi-lije6fkmg3ilr65bi-7zovbns-r1]] says Lucian explicitly denies the reality and possibility of the coming events.
- Simulated authority: [[371-liangqian-nianqian-jiu-you-ren-xiangxiang-chu-saiboge-nanren-duli-shengyu-xingji-zhanzheng-le-ma-zhenshi-de-gushi-lije6fkmg3ilr65bi-7zovbns-r1]] emphasizes exact time, distance, force size, custom, and historical tone.
- Truth through impossibility: [[371-liangqian-nianqian-jiu-you-ren-xiangxiang-chu-saiboge-nanren-duli-shengyu-xingji-zhanzheng-le-ma-zhenshi-de-gushi-lije6fkmg3ilr65bi-7zovbns-r1]] reads the impossible societies as disclosures of familiar greed, hierarchy, anxiety, and curiosity.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Declaring fiction does not make every representation harmless, insightful, or immune from criticism. Readers may disagree about what the satire targets, and modern genre or ethical vocabulary should not be projected backward as Lucian's explicit theory without textual support.
