# Non-Compete Agreements

Non-compete agreements are employment restrictions that limit where workers can work after leaving a job. In [[the-real-horror-of-alien-and-how-it-explains-why-were-not-paid-enough]], [[ArinDube|Arin Dube]] treats them as a form of [[MonopsonyByArtifice]] because they reduce worker mobility by rule rather than by natural market geography.

The episode argues that non-competes are not only a trade-secret tool. By naming examples such as fast food and summer camps, it shows how the restriction can reach ordinary workers and strengthen [[LaborMonopsony]] by making outside offers less credible.
