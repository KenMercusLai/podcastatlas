## Definition

Harem succession risk control / 后宫继承风险控制 is the attempt to manage ruler desire, consort hierarchy, and childbearing as public political risks before they produce jealousy, rival heirs, or factional disorder.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-504-nanren-haose-zenmeban-kexi-guren-zhezhao-ni-xuebuhui-lsevoh__1vxdtzqhlzq6_an3xdq6|Hanji 504]] creates the concept through [[DuQinWesternHan|杜钦]]'s advice to [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]]. Du Qin does not treat [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]'s desire as merely private morality. He argues that if the palace has no limiting rule, the ruler can keep adding women, the empress and consorts can become suspicious or ambitious, and childlessness can turn sexual favor into succession instability.

The Xiongnu branch in the same source supplies a comparative counterpart. [[HuhanyeChanyuWesternHan|呼韩邪单于]] faces a consort-and-son problem of his own: the favored younger son and the older politically credible candidate imply different risks. His settlement names [[FuzhuleiRuodiChanyu|雕陶莫皋]] successor while requiring later transfer to 且莫车, showing another way consort rank, age, affection, and stability have to be made into a visible order.

Hanji 427 adds an earlier Xuan-era selection case. After [[XuPingjunWesternHan|许平君]]'s death and Huo Chengjun's threat to crown prince [[HanYuandi|刘奭 / 汉元帝]], [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] needs an empress who will not produce a rival heir or use maternal status against the existing crown prince. The childless and low-favor [[QiongchengTaihouWesternHan|邛成太后]] is therefore selected as a politically safe foster mother. This is risk control by choosing a low-threat empress, not by setting a formal harem rule.

The Yuan-era warning version also does not require a numerical harem rule. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-484-gudai-huangdi-de-hunyin-shenghuo-you-duo-mafan-lv7yfmymwhqdejvr0hxntjszlqwe|Hanji 484]], [[KuangHengWesternHan|匡衡]] warns [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] that favor toward [[FuZhaoyiWesternHan|傅昭仪]] and [[LiuKangDingtaoKingWesternHan|刘康]] can unsettle [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] and crown prince [[HanChengdi|刘骜 / 后来的汉成帝]] because empress-consort and嫡庶 hierarchy must remain visible. This makes harem succession control not only a question of limiting desire, but also of keeping household ranks legible enough that favorites cannot become a rival succession channel.

## Key Claims

- Ruler sexuality becomes a state problem when childbearing, palace hierarchy, and heir recognition depend on it.
- A harem rule can be proposed as preventive governance, not only as moral restraint.
- Empress selection can itself function as succession-risk control when the ruler chooses a childless foster mother to protect an established crown prince.
- Even without a formal rule proposal, ministers can frame consort favor and household intimacy as succession-risk management.
- Early timing matters: regulation is easier before favorites, jealousies, and succession expectations become entrenched.
- Consort hierarchy can generate rival stability arguments rather than one obvious heir answer.
- A planned transfer arrangement can try to balance age, maternal status, and political acceptance, but its durability remains source-scoped.

## Evidence

- Preventive timing: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-504-nanren-haose-zenmeban-kexi-guren-zhezhao-ni-xuebuhui-lsevoh__1vxdtzqhlzq6_an3xdq6|Hanji 504]] says Du Qin urges action while Chengdi is young, childless, and not yet publicly criticized for closeness to consorts.
- Rule as limit: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-504-nanren-haose-zenmeban-kexi-guren-zhezhao-ni-xuebuhui-lsevoh__1vxdtzqhlzq6_an3xdq6|Hanji 504]] presents the "nine wives" rule as a way to provide heirs without indefinite harem expansion.
- Succession danger: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-504-nanren-haose-zenmeban-kexi-guren-zhezhao-ni-xuebuhui-lsevoh__1vxdtzqhlzq6_an3xdq6|Hanji 504]] says Du Qin worries about empress suspicion, consort ambition, and stories where favor disrupts heir order.
- Low-threat empress selection: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-427-yiren-zhengfu-shacheguo-xihan-fengfengshi-zenme-zuodao-de-loxxi2qynbrbmfqgokos30sypsxp|Hanji 427]] says Xuan wants a childless and unambitious empress after Huo Chengjun's attempt to harm the crown prince.
- Foster-mother safety: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-427-yiren-zhengfu-shacheguo-xihan-fengfengshi-zenme-zuodao-de-loxxi2qynbrbmfqgokos30sypsxp|Hanji 427]] says Wang shi's lack of favor keeps her safe and suited to raising Liu Shi without threatening him.
- Rank legibility: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-484-gudai-huangdi-de-hunyin-shenghuo-you-duo-mafan-lv7yfmymwhqdejvr0hxntjszlqwe|Hanji 484]] says Kuang Heng warns Yuan to distinguish empress and consorts,嫡 and庶 sons, old and new attachments, and high and low rank.
- Intimacy exposure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-484-gudai-huangdi-de-hunyin-shenghuo-you-duo-mafan-lv7yfmymwhqdejvr0hxntjszlqwe|Hanji 484]] says the ruler's meals, pleasures, and closeness can be watched and exploited by bad actors.
- Xiongnu comparative case: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-504-nanren-haose-zenmeban-kexi-guren-zhezhao-ni-xuebuhui-lsevoh__1vxdtzqhlzq6_an3xdq6|Hanji 504]] says Huhanye chooses Diaotao Mogao and arranges future transfer to Qiemo Che to stabilize the Xiongnu succession.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Hanji 504 does not prove the nine-wives proposal was institutionally feasible in Han law; the dowager rejects it precisely because Han precedent is lacking. The source also does not show whether Huhanye's planned transfer succeeded in the long run. Hanji 427 gives a successful selection logic but not the full Wang empress biography, inner palace procedure, or every motive behind Xuan's choice. Hanji 484 does not show a completed heir change or a formal palace rule; it adds a warning about favor and rank legibility. This concept should not absorb every harem, marriage, or favorite case: it names cases where consort access is explicitly tied to heir production, rank order, and political stability.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 427's Xuan-era low-threat empress selection as a non-numerical harem-succession control case.
- Preserved Hanji 484's Yuan-era rank-legibility warning as a later non-numerical harem-succession control case.
