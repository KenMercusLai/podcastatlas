# Chest Pain Emergency Escalation / 胸痛急症升级

## Definition
Chest pain emergency escalation / 胸痛急症升级 is the public triage boundary for treating chest pain, chest tightness, palpitations, sweating, dizziness, or impending-doom sensations as possible urgent cardiac events when risk factors and symptom pattern make delay dangerous.

## Current Synthesis
[[vol-222-aoye-xintiao-kuangbiao-shi-cusi-qianzhao-aed-hui-dian-cuo-huoren-dai-ni-bikai-zhiming-changshi-shijie-jijiu-ri-teji-lnemogq8gb9o3e2835jvtfx3emiy]] separates everyday anxiety about palpitations from the emergency logic of possible myocardial infarction. The episode does not ask laypeople to diagnose the cause; it asks them to notice risk factors, symptom distribution, duration, sweating, near-syncope, and uncertainty, then call 120 or seek care early.

The synthesis is escalation-oriented: chest symptoms with dangerous features should not first be routed through relatives, social media, or endurance. Emergency dispatch can give instructions while help is moving.

## Key Claims
- Risk factors such as male sex, postmenopausal status, hypertension, hyperlipidemia, glucose problems, smoking, drinking, and family history increase concern when chest symptoms occur.
- Central chest or precordial pain can matter more when it radiates to the left shoulder, left arm, left back, teeth, or throat.
- Sweating, dizziness, blacking out, palpitations, and a strong sense of impending death lower the threshold for urgent care.
- Pain linked clearly to posture, pressing, breathing, infection, or soft-tissue causes may suggest other pathways, but public uncertainty should still be routed through qualified care.
- The episode gives the public a shorter practical threshold than the clinical 30-minute warning language, recommending 120 or care-seeking after roughly 10-15 minutes of concerning symptoms.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept does not diagnose myocardial infarction, panic attack, pulmonary disease, pericarditis, musculoskeletal pain, or any other cause of chest symptoms. It records a public education boundary; individual evaluation belongs to emergency clinicians.

## What Changed
- VOL.222 creates a chest-pain escalation concept that links cardiac risk factors, symptom radiation, sweating, dizziness, duration, and early emergency calling.
