# Short-Form Business-Tech Audio / 短时长商业科技音频

Short-form business-tech audio is the compressed-news format promised by [[ShengdongZaokafei|声动早咖啡]] in [[trailer-ding-xiazhouyi-zaochen-lai-bei-shengdong-zaokafei-ba-431629439]]. The trailer defines the product as fresh, concentrated business and technology information in less than 15 minutes, initially delivered on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday mornings.

The format is different from long-form conversation. It asks whether a show can provide enough selection, framing, and freshness to justify habitual listening in a narrow morning slot. Its risk is also visible in the trailer: the source defines positioning and cadence, but does not yet demonstrate segment structure, topic judgment, or sustained editorial depth.

## Key Claims
- Short-form business-tech audio competes on selection and compression, not only on completeness.
- A sub-15-minute promise makes the show compatible with washing up, breakfast, commuting, or leaving home.
- Fixed morning updates make the format closer to a daily briefing ritual than to an evergreen archive.
- The format needs a clear editorial filter because a short show cannot cover everything.
- The trailer shows launch positioning but leaves future content quality as an open question.
