# Creative Self-Preservation Under Repetitive Labor / 重复劳动中的创作性自我保存

## Definition
Creative self-preservation under repetitive labor is the use of reading, writing, music, performance, friendship, and limited control over time to keep perception, memory, and agency active inside work that otherwise narrows life to output, wages, and repetition.

## Current Synthesis
Xiaohai's account supplies a mechanism rather than an escape myth. Writing on factory waste paper gave form to dislocation; memorized Tang poems enlarged the mental world available during industrial routine; modern poetry made worker life speakable; and rock, Ginsberg, Dylan, guitar, and performance added rhythm, companionship, and embodied expression. Later, a low-paid but more flexible job and a literary community increased the time available for practice.

These resources preserve a self that labor does not fully define, but they do not cancel material constraint. Creative agency depends on time, income, housing, social support, bodily endurance, and permission to move. Its achievement is partial freedom and continuity of attention, not proof that exploitative or numbing work is harmless.

## Key Claims
- Repetitive labor can narrow the worker's perceptual world to schedules, output, wages, and endurance.
- Reading and memorization can supply language and temporal depth unavailable in the immediate workplace.
- Writing preserves specific states and memories that routine might otherwise erase.
- Music and performance can convert private feeling into rhythm, bodily expression, and social connection.
- Flexible time and creative community may matter as much as nominal occupational status for sustaining practice.
- Creative survival remains materially bounded and should not be used to romanticize low pay, insecurity, or exhaustion.

## Evidence
- Factory writing and reading - [[dagong-shiren-xiaohai-zuo-yikuai-gunshi-nisha-juxia-ye-butingxia-lqz5ox0rp0kzxkjgxambqqt6qx12]] connects night-shift writing, Tang-poem memorization, Hai Zi, and the expansion of a world otherwise organized by production.
- Music and embodied expression - [[dagong-shiren-xiaohai-zuo-yikuai-gunshi-nisha-juxia-ye-butingxia-lqz5ox0rp0kzxkjgxambqqt6qx12]] links rock, 《嚎叫》, Dylan, guitar, singing, and live poetry to companionship and felt existence.
- Time and community - [[dagong-shiren-xiaohai-zuo-yikuai-gunshi-nisha-juxia-ye-butingxia-lqz5ox0rp0kzxkjgxambqqt6qx12]] presents secondhand-shop work and the “poetry shop” community as low-income but comparatively enabling conditions.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Creative practice is not universally available, sufficient, or curative. Severe hours, injury, surveillance, family responsibility, poverty, or lack of education and community can prevent it; artistic recognition may also reproduce market labels and new pressures. The source is one retrospective account and cannot establish that creativity reliably protects workers' mental health or mobility.

## What Changed
- Created a mechanism joining factory routine, reading, writing, music, performance, time control, and community.
- Made material limits explicit so creative persistence is not mistaken for liberation from labor conditions.
