# Daoist Alchemy / 道教炼丹

Daoist alchemy / 道教炼丹 is the immortality-oriented transformation practice discussed in [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-jie-shangji-704051550]] through [[WeiBoyang|魏伯阳]], [[ZhouyiCantongqi|《周易参同契》]], [[GeHong|葛洪]], and [[Baopuzi|《抱朴子》]]. The source emphasizes coded vocabulary, mercury-sulfur examples, gold elixirs, and ranked elixir systems as parts of a religious pursuit of becoming immortal.

The concept is adjacent to [[AlchemyChemistryContinuity]], but its source emphasis is different. The episode is less interested in a direct path to modern chemistry than in how material transformation, secrecy, cosmology, bodily salvation, and textual authority support one another.

## Key Claims
- Alchemy in the source is a religious practice before it is a health supplement or chemical curiosity.
- Coded language makes alchemical knowledge both protected and interpretively dense.
- Material operations are embedded in yin-yang, five-phase, and immortality cosmology.
- Later readers should not detach famous slogans from their elixir and longevity context.
