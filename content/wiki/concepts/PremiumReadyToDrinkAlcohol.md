# Premium Ready-to-Drink Alcohol

## Definition
Premium ready-to-drink alcohol is the attempt to sell convenient canned or pre-mixed cocktails through higher-quality positioning, lower sweetness, and adult brand cues rather than the low-status or youth associations of older alcopops.

## Current Synthesis
The category is both a product and perception problem. [[drone-truth-russia-has-the-upper-hand-6aad03f98a1508074de8337e]] says newer brands use premium presentation to reach changing occasions, while extensions built around established spirits brands have generally captured only a small market share.

## Key Claims
- Premium cues help distinguish new canned cocktails from older alcopops.
- Convenience alone is insufficient if taste or brand meaning is weak.
- Established spirits brands do not automatically transfer strength into ready-to-drink formats.
- Acquiring startups can give incumbents access to social-media knowledge and faster product experimentation.

## Evidence
### Positioning and performance
- [[drone-truth-russia-has-the-upper-hand-6aad03f98a1508074de8337e]] contrasts premium, less-sweet startup products with older alcopops and says established-brand extensions have generally performed less well.

### Incumbent response
- [[drone-truth-russia-has-the-upper-hand-6aad03f98a1508074de8337e]] describes spirits companies pursuing acquisitions and startup capabilities to enter the category.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- The source offers no category-wide sales series or named acquisition case.
- Premium positioning cannot by itself reverse the broader [[AlcoholDemandReset]].

## What Changed
- Added a concept separating premium canned cocktails from older ready-to-drink alcohol positioning.
