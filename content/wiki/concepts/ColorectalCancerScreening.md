# Colorectal Cancer Screening

## Definition
Colorectal cancer screening is the structured use of stool testing, blood or risk tools, colonoscopy, and polyp removal to detect colorectal cancer or precancerous lesions early.

## Current Synthesis
The Ding Kefeng episode frames colorectal screening as neither a tumor-marker shortcut nor a colonoscopy-for-everyone reflex. The central hierarchy is clear: tumor markers cannot rule cancer out, stool testing is a useful low-friction screen, emerging blood and AI tools may concentrate high-risk groups, and colonoscopy remains the gold standard when diagnosis or polyp removal is needed. The prevention logic is especially important because identifying and removing precancerous polyps can interrupt progression.

## Key Claims
- Colorectal-cancer screening matters because early disease and polyps can be silent for years.
- Tumor markers are insufficient as stand-alone early-cancer screening or reassurance.
- Stool testing can detect bleeding or inflammation clues and can help route people toward further evaluation.
- Colonoscopy remains the source's gold-standard diagnostic test for colorectal cancer.
- Screening around age 45 and above receives special emphasis in the episode's China-facing discussion.
- Screening is meant to find cancer and precancerous polyps, not only explain current symptoms.
- The best screening path depends on symptoms, risk, test availability, and follow-up quality.

## Evidence
- Silent progression: [[vol-203-kao-bingmeishi-tongbian-yi-jinzhang-jiu-fuxie-nide-cuipi-changdao-xuyao-zhefen-shiyongshu-lvtxc2z3hn5ukh3va9rzijremcih]] notes that polyps can remain asymptomatic for years and that tumor symptoms may worsen stepwise.
- Tumor-marker limit: [[vol-203-kao-bingmeishi-tongbian-yi-jinzhang-jiu-fuxie-nide-cuipi-changdao-xuyao-zhefen-shiyongshu-lvtxc2z3hn5ukh3va9rzijremcih]] says tumor markers can be normal even in early, middle, or advanced disease and cannot prove absence of tumor.
- Colonoscopy and stool tests: [[vol-203-kao-bingmeishi-tongbian-yi-jinzhang-jiu-fuxie-nide-cuipi-changdao-xuyao-zhefen-shiyongshu-lvtxc2z3hn5ukh3va9rzijremcih]] treats stool testing as useful and colonoscopy as the gold standard.
- Age and prevention: [[vol-203-kao-bingmeishi-tongbian-yi-jinzhang-jiu-fuxie-nide-cuipi-changdao-xuyao-zhefen-shiyongshu-lvtxc2z3hn5ukh3va9rzijremcih]] says China recommends attention to screening after 45 and explains that removing polyps can block precancerous progression.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This is not a screening guideline. Exact age, interval, modality, urgency, bowel-prep suitability, anticoagulant handling, family-history threshold, and post-polyp surveillance should be individualized by qualified clinicians and local standards.
