# Jingdezhen Porcelain Production System / 景德镇瓷业生产系统

Jingdezhen Porcelain Production System / 景德镇瓷业生产系统 is the core frame of [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]]. The episode argues that [[Jingdezhen]]'s "world porcelain capital" status came from an ecosystem of materials, transport, orders, labor division, firing knowledge, merchants, fuel supply, institutions, and later education and markets.

The concept keeps the city's history from becoming a list of famous wares. [[QingbaiPorcelain]], [[YuanBlueAndWhitePorcelain]], the [[JingdezhenImperialKilnFactory]], the [[StateOwnedCeramicFactorySystem]], and [[JingpiaoCreativeCeramicEcology]] are different phases of the same system adapting to changing demand and governance.

## Key Claims
- The production system depends on complementary inputs: [[Kaolin]], porcelain stone, water transport, fuel, artisans, kilns, orders, and sales channels.
- It contains both official and market mechanisms, from [[OfficialSupervisionCivilianKilns]] and [[ImperialKilnSystem]] to export orders, small studios, tourism, and livestream ecommerce.
- The system's advantage is cumulative [[ProcessKnowledge]]: repeated practice stores knowledge in workshops, kiln masters, material suppliers, schools, and market intermediaries.
