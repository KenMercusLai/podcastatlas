## Definition

使者示弱式危机降温 / de-escalatory envoy signaling is the use of respectful status recognition, narrow questioning, and deliberately non-punitive presentation to prevent a fearful or hostile target from treating an inquiry as the opening of an attack.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 295 presents a bounded success. [[LiuQianHuainanCrownPrinceWesternHan|刘迁]] allegedly plans to kill the imperial envoy and rise if the mission appears punitive, but the envoy approaches [[LiuAn|刘安]] deferentially and limits the visible inquiry to [[LeiBeiWesternHan|雷被]]'s complaint. Liu An then withdraws the ambush and troops, allowing the envoy to return safely with observations.

The mechanism buys time and prevents an immediate clash; it does not produce reconciliation. The later removal of two counties deepens Liu An's resentment in the episode's account, and preparation continues. De-escalatory signaling should therefore be judged by its immediate objective and follow-on policy, not mistaken for resolution of the underlying conflict.

## Key Claims

- Status-respecting presentation can reduce the chance that an inquiry is interpreted as an imminent arrest or attack.
- Narrow visible questioning can preserve room for observation when openly disclosing the full suspicion would trigger violence.
- Tactical de-escalation can protect an envoy and prevent premature rebellion without changing the target's long-term intent.
- A later sanction may reverse the psychological benefit if the target interprets calibrated punishment as humiliation.
- The method depends on credible restraint; it is not evidence that the underlying allegation is false or resolved.

## Evidence

Immediate conflict avoidance:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-295-doufu-de-famingzhe-jing-shi-zaofan-de-fanwang-libnwsqalvpzymlnbaewua4s6miw|Hanji 295]] says the envoy's deference and limited questioning disrupt the planned ambush, after which Liu An dismisses the hidden attackers and prepared troops.

Bounded rather than durable success:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-295-doufu-de-famingzhe-jing-shi-zaofan-de-fanwang-libnwsqalvpzymlnbaewua4s6miw|Hanji 295]] says the envoy returns safely and the court avoids immediate prosecution for rebellion, but Liu An responds to the two-county penalty by intensifying preparation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept currently rests on one narrative episode and an attributed reconstruction of private intent. The account does not prove the envoy consciously followed a formal doctrine, that deference alone caused Liu An to stand down, or that a more direct mission would certainly have triggered rebellion. Immediate safety and long-term political success are separate outcomes.

## What Changed

- Created the concept from the contrast between successful immediate stand-down and continued long-term escalation.
