# Jiangnan Seasonal Cuisine / 江南时令饮食

## Definition

Jiangnan seasonal cuisine coordinates local ingredients, annual cycles, preservation, kitchen technique, and social occasions through the principle “不时不食.”

## Current Synthesis

The episode presents seasonality as a food system rather than a slogan. Crab, fish, wild greens, cakes, pork preparations, fermented foods, duck, eel, bamboo shoots, and freshwater plants become appropriate at different moments because ecology, texture, preservation, labor, and social expectation change through the year. Refinement lies in recognizing these differences and applying the right treatment, not only in expensive ingredients or elaborate presentation.

This system is dynamic. Aquaculture, administrative expansion, restaurant growth, migration, sanitation, and changing supply can add new ingredients or remove old ones. A living seasonal cuisine therefore combines memory with continuing observation of what is locally available and materially good.

## Key Claims

- Seasonality coordinates ingredient quality, dish choice, preservation, technique, and social occasion.
- Locality matters through varieties, habitats, freshness, and supply relationships rather than geographic labels alone.
- Fine cooking can reside in ordinary foods when they require precise cutting, curing, drying, extraction, or temperature control.
- A regional cuisine includes banquet dishes, household foods, country cooking, snacks, wild plants, and preserved ingredients.
- Seasonal knowledge changes when ecology, aquaculture, sanitation, urban boundaries, and supply chains change.

- Locality evidence: [[512-yu-wangkai-manhua-jiangnan-shishi-cong-wenren-xieyan-dao-xiangye-tucai-lh0w1a0jwafuydxpdghqppuwvbzy]] connects flavor with crab water and feed, fresh water shield, regional ferments, local sugar and vinegar, bamboo shoots, ham, duck, and surrounding county ingredients.
- Change evidence: [[512-yu-wangkai-manhua-jiangnan-shishi-cong-wenren-xieyan-dao-xiangye-tucai-lh0w1a0jwafuydxpdghqppuwvbzy]] describes ingredient substitution, city expansion, popular stronger flavors, restaurant-chain growth, and movement of high-end attention toward seafood-rich areas.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source is a structured conversational summary, not a comprehensive Jiangnan culinary survey. Seasonal calendars and regional boundaries vary by household, city, climate, and period. “Local” does not automatically mean superior, and modern cultivation or distribution can preserve access as well as weaken distinctiveness.
