# Exchangeable Capability / 可交换能力

Exchangeable capability / 可交换能力 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-930-ruhe-pochu-tanxin-he-buganxin-lkyysh5jbgxwjq8ayrfar4gsjdcd|Hanji 930]] as the episode's practical answer to greed, resentment, and waiting for patrons. The host says relationships often work through interest exchange, so a person cannot rely only on background, favors, or imagined noble helpers; the first task is to build value, skill, and ability that can be recognized.

The concept is not a claim that all relationships are crude transactions. In the source's historical branch, value appears in different forms: [[GaiXunLateHan|盖勋]]'s integrity wins hostile respect, [[ZhuJunLateHan|朱儁]]'s command judgment wins time and victory, and [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]]'s battlefield ability becomes useful once Zhu Jun gives him a role.

This makes exchangeable capability adjacent to [[MoralReputationPoliticalCapital|道德名望政治资本]] and [[TalentAsStateTreasure|以贤臣为宝]]. The difference is perspective: moral reputation and talent value describe how others use or protect capable people; exchangeable capability describes the personal preparation that makes recognition possible in the first place.

## Key Claims
- Relationships, patrons, and background help only when the person has something real to bring into the exchange.
- Capability can be ethical, strategic, technical, or martial; it is not limited to money or status.
- Resentment about missing support becomes less useful than building a visible capacity that changes how others evaluate the person.
- Recognition still matters: Sun Jian's old merit becomes more consequential only when Zhu Jun actively uses him.
- The concept should remain source-scoped as the host's life-advice frame, not as a universal theory that reduces every relationship to calculation.
