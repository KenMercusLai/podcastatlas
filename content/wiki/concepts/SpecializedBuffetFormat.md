# Specialized Buffet Format / 细分自助餐

Specialized buffet format is the restaurant pattern in [[kafeidou-he-niu-ziyou-cheng-zizhu-canting-maidian-guijia-guanghuan-cong-he-er-lai-1004978054]], where broad hotel-style buffets give way to narrower categories such as self-service barbecue, pizza, or wagyu sukiyaki. The source's main case is [[NiuniuSukiyaki]], which narrows the experience around [[Wagyu|wagyu]] and sukiyaki hot pot instead of trying to offer every cuisine at once.

The operating logic is that fewer SKUs can simplify procurement, increase purchasing leverage for the core ingredient, reduce waste, and make the value proposition easier for customers to understand. Specialization therefore turns self-service from "many things cheaply" into "one premium thing with controlled abundance."

## Key Claims
- Specialization can make a buffet more operationally legible than a traditional all-category buffet.
- Narrow SKU design can concentrate spending on the ingredient customers came for.
- The format works best when paired with [[BuffetBudgetLockIn]]: the customer knows the total spend and can evaluate the offer before sitting down.
- Premium self-service still needs [[RestaurantSupplyChainLocalization]] and [[ChainRestaurantStandardization]] because abundance magnifies sourcing, freshness, and loss-control pressure.
