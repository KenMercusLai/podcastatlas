# Fast-Food Beverage Occasion Strategy / 快餐饮品时段策略

## Definition

Fast-food beverage occasion strategy is the use of distinctive, customizable, or limited drinks to create visits beyond standard meal times, increase ticket size, and give customers a reason to return apart from the chain's core food.

## Current Synthesis

The episode presents refreshers, dirty sodas, energy drinks, and other colorful cold beverages as a shift from standardized fountain soda toward menu innovation. These products can attract younger customers, afternoon traffic, social sharing, and add-on spending, while giving pressured chains an alternative to discount-only competition. The same strategy increases dependence on formulation capability, specialized ingredients, supplier flexibility, equipment, training, and repeatable store execution. A novel drink can therefore create a temporary traffic spike without becoming a durable demand engine.

## Key Claims

- Distinctive drinks can create a visit occasion separate from breakfast, lunch, or dinner.
- Limited flavors and visual customization can support repeat visits and higher average spend.
- Beverage strategy can diversify a chain's demand response beyond price discounts and core-food launches.
- Supplier value increasingly includes joint product development and sales growth, not only reliable fountain-soda delivery.
- Customized drinks can weaken exclusive single-supplier logic when a chain needs ingredients or formats outside one vendor's portfolio.
- Operational complexity and fading novelty can erase the traffic and margin benefits of innovation.

## Evidence

- **Occasion and ticket growth:** [[dafeixin-paoqi-hezuo-jin-40-nian-de-baishi-meiguo-kendeji-zai-zheteng-shenme-gkwrijeotnadah8swtwi4nj]] links new drinks to off-peak visits and reports higher average spending among limited-product buyers.
- **Supplier capability:** [[dafeixin-paoqi-hezuo-jin-40-nian-de-baishi-meiguo-kendeji-zai-zheteng-shenme-gkwrijeotnadah8swtwi4nj]] says KFC chose Coca-Cola after a competitive process and wanted joint development aligned with changing tastes.
- **Portfolio flexibility:** [[dafeixin-paoqi-hezuo-jin-40-nian-de-baishi-meiguo-kendeji-zai-zheteng-shenme-gkwrijeotnadah8swtwi4nj]] describes McDonald's adding suppliers and developing dirty sodas, refreshers, and energy drinks beyond its traditional Coca-Cola relationship.
- **Durability boundary:** [[dafeixin-paoqi-hezuo-jin-40-nian-de-baishi-meiguo-kendeji-zai-zheteng-shenme-gkwrijeotnadah8swtwi4nj]] says traffic gains following beverage-supplier switches often fade after the first months.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source provides industry examples and attributed figures rather than controlled store comparisons, unit economics, customer cohorts, or disclosed supplier contracts. Higher ticket size does not establish incremental profit after ingredients, labor, equipment, waste, training, and slower throughput. Novelty, social-media visibility, and supplier changes may create only temporary traffic.

## What Changed

- Established the fast-food drink as an independent traffic occasion rather than only a meal accompaniment.
- Connected beverage innovation to supplier flexibility and chain-scale execution limits.
