## Definition

Embedded-agent operational value / 潜伏者的行动价值 is the contribution a hidden insider makes through relevant reporting, timely warning, preserved access, and effective intervention, assessed separately from declared loyalty, duration undercover, personal sacrifice, or eventual reputation.

## Current Synthesis

[[FanYaoJinYong|范遥]] produces a mixed case in [[HeavenSwordDragonSaber|《倚天屠龙记》]]. He preserves an exceptionally demanding disguise and access to 赵敏's circle, yet the episode identifies no stable handler, reporting channel, or warning before 成昆 nearly destroys Mingjiao. Those omissions weaken any claim that endurance alone makes him a good agent.

The 万安寺 rescue prevents the opposite simplification. Fan Yao's access, antidote assistance, and coordination with 张无忌 create a consequential result that an outsider would struggle to reproduce. The concept therefore evaluates an embedded agent across several dimensions and timescales: information flow, activation timing, access quality, intervention effect, relationship management, and accountability after return.

## Key Claims

- Time spent undercover is not equivalent to useful intelligence work.
- Preserved access has option value, but it becomes operational value only when information or intervention reaches the represented organization.
- Missing warnings during an existential threat weigh against later claims of consistent service.
- A decisive activation can materially improve a mixed record without erasing earlier silence.
- Costly sacrifice or self-punishment can signal loyalty but cannot substitute for operational evidence.
- Genuine attachment to the infiltrated side can coexist with service to the original organization and complicate, rather than settle, loyalty judgment.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The framework is derived from a fictional case discussed with modern espionage vocabulary. Secret reporting may be absent from the episode summary without being impossible in the fictional world, and fragmented Mingjiao authority may have left no safe or legitimate recipient. Waiting for a worthy leader, disillusionment, self-preservation, and opportunism remain competing explanations. Operational success also does not by itself establish moral legitimacy: deception, killing, and betrayal require separate judgment.

## What Changed

- Created the concept to separate access, reporting, warning, activation, effect, and loyalty signaling.
