## Definition
Embodied training against fear / 反本能身体训练 is the practice of making frightening or counter-instinctive actions safer by repeating small movements until the body can act without panic.

## Current Synthesis
The Zhao Lina source generalizes from goalkeeper training to a broader theory of action. A beginner's body wants to avoid a fast ball, tense before impact, or freeze in front of a camera; training works by breaking the impossible-looking act into contact, posture, rhythm, repetition, and feedback. Fear does not disappear through explanation alone. It becomes manageable when the body learns how to land, breathe, continue, and recognize that the action can be survived.

## Key Claims
- Some skills are counter-instinctive because the untrained body protects itself in ways that make performance and safety worse.
- Repetition converts abstract courage into specific movements, such as diving, landing, singing, dancing, speaking, or asking questions.
- Fear can increase injury risk when it produces awkward bracing or panic movement.
- Training can transfer across domains because the same action-before-confidence pattern appears in sport, performance, camera presence, and interviewing.
- The concept extends failure desensitization by emphasizing body learning rather than only emotional tolerance.

## Evidence
- Counter-instinctive goalkeeper action: [[zhao-lina-zaichang-bi-ying-zhongyao-menjiang-de-xinxin-zhijue-yu-reai-229fde7d-d39d-4f7c-a33d-607f5370fc60]] says ordinary people dodge a fast ball while goalkeepers must move toward it, receive it, or turn it into attack.
- Landing and injury risk: [[zhao-lina-zaichang-bi-ying-zhongyao-menjiang-de-xinxin-zhijue-yu-reai-229fde7d-d39d-4f7c-a33d-607f5370fc60]] says fear around landing can make the body support itself in strange ways and increase injury risk.
- Cross-domain transfer: [[zhao-lina-zaichang-bi-ying-zhongyao-menjiang-de-xinxin-zhijue-yu-reai-229fde7d-d39d-4f7c-a33d-607f5370fc60]] compares sport training with singing, dancing, water performance, camera presence, guitar practice, skiing, climbing, and podcast expression.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Not every fear should be trained through exposure; some danger signals are accurate and require better equipment, coaching, rest, or refusal. The source also does not provide medical or coaching protocol, so the concept should not be treated as safety advice.
