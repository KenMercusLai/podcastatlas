# Workplace Bullying / 职场霸凌

## Definition
Workplace bullying is repeated or structurally enabled harm at work, including humiliation, blame transfer, exclusion, surveillance, coercive workload, and pressure that makes the worker doubt their competence or worth.

## Current Synthesis
VOL.214 treats workplace bullying and workplace PUA as concrete power practices rather than only a personality label. A superior may pressure a subordinate because the organization transfers accountability downward, because the superior wants more labor, or because control-oriented traits make that pressure easier to use.

The episode's strongest contribution is to connect harm and response. Bullying becomes harder to resist when the worker depends on supervisor approval for achievement, income, contract renewal, or self-worth; response therefore starts by naming the structure, preserving evidence, reducing self-blame, and finding outside witnesses or institutional channels.

## Key Claims
- Workplace bullying can be nonphysical and still harmful when it works through public shaming, repeated blame, hidden exclusion, overwork, or close behavioral monitoring.
- The pattern often depends on [[StructuralPowerImbalance|structural power imbalance]]: one side evaluates, assigns, or fires, while the other side risks livelihood and reputation.
- Workplace PUA is useful only when tied to observable behavior; it should not replace diagnosis of task scope, authority, incentive, and organizational process.
- High-performing workers can be especially vulnerable when they internalize poor treatment as proof they are not good enough.
- Preservation of messages, recordings, timelines, and complaint records can matter because bullying is often denied or reframed after the fact.
- Expression, clinical support, and formal complaint paths can protect the victim's reality-testing before the conflict becomes pure self-blame.

- Overload case: [[vol-214-weishenme-yue-youxiu-de-ren-yue-rongyi-zai-zhichang-bei-pua-dao-huaiyi-ziji-cong-tiequan-jiaoyu-liaodao-tiequan-zhichang-lmx4ewrw7kvcqpyh-q3bur35ybgz]] uses an AI-redesign assignment to show how novelty enthusiasm can become weekend labor, insomnia, and stress when scope and expertise are mismatched.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Not every difficult manager, harsh critique, or failed task is workplace bullying. The source itself distinguishes structural interests, personal traits, role conflict, and legally or clinically actionable harm only at a high level. Korean complaint mechanisms and evidence norms are source-scoped and should not be read as legal advice for other jurisdictions.
