# Post-Coalition Spoils Imbalance / 联军战后利益失衡

Post-coalition spoils imbalance / 联军战后利益失衡 is the problem [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-73-lishishang-ziyou-lianai-de-kaichuangzhe-shi-shui-lkumfsfialqgdl6qpb3c-hnz7h9u]] draws from the aftermath of [[LeYi|乐毅]]'s anti-Qi victory. A [[CommonEnemyAlliance|common-enemy alliance]] can win the initial war, but the allied states have different geography, occupation capacity, and satisfaction thresholds after victory.

In the source, Le Yi sends distant [[QinState|秦国]] and [[HanState|韩国]] back with treasure, lets [[WeiState|魏国]] and [[ZhaoState|赵国]] take nearer gains, and keeps [[YanState|燕国]] advancing in [[QiState|齐国]]. The host treats Qin as the dangerous outlier: treasure is not enough for a powerful expansionist state, so Qin soon attacks Wei, takes [[AnchengWarringStates|安城]], and threatens [[Daliang|大梁]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-76-shui-geile-chuqingxiangwang-de-zixin-lsut4cfic7xbbrmbmimrruf7n6y0]] carries the same imbalance into the Zhao front. Qin asks [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] to join an attack on Qi; when Zhao refuses after being warned by Su Li on Qi's behalf, [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] attacks Zhao. The source reads this as Qin's dissatisfaction after the anti-Qi coalition continuing to search for compensation, leverage, and punishment.

The concept extends [[CommonEnemyAlliance|共同敌人联盟]] by naming the post-victory phase. The enemy's defeat does not settle allied incentives; it can expose which ally received land, which received only money, and which sees the new balance as a threat.

## Key Claims
- Coalition victory can create a second incentive problem after the common enemy is beaten.
- Geography matters because nearby states can occupy land more easily than distant allies.
- Treasure compensation may satisfy a weak or distant ally but fail with a strong expansionist ally.
- A post-victory allocation can trigger new wars against former or recent partners.
- In this episode, Qin's attack on Wei is read as both territorial extraction and a move to disturb a possible Yan-Zhao-Wei post-Qi balance.
- Zhouji 76 adds that Qin's dissatisfaction can also become punitive pressure on Zhao when Zhao refuses to keep helping Qin pursue Qi.
