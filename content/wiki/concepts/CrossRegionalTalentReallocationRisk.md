## Definition

[[CrossRegionalTalentReallocationRisk|跨区域人才调配风险]] is the possibility that moving a capable official to strengthen one critical region removes relationship-specific control, information, or legitimacy from another.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 1162 presents Cao Cao responding to Wang Bi's death by drawing Pei Qian from successful northern administration toward the political center. Pei Qian warns that local Wuhuan will not accept his successor, and revolt follows. The case frames personnel as embedded governance capacity: a transfer can solve an urgent vacancy while exposing a latent frontier dependency.

## Key Claims

- An official's value may depend on local trust and accumulated knowledge, not only portable skill.
- Filling a central vacancy can externalize risk to a frontier or secondary region.
- Exit warnings are useful evidence about hidden succession and legitimacy dependencies.
- Military recovery after revolt does not erase the cost of preventable governance disruption.

## Evidence

Transfer and warning:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1162-jinyi-zhisi-xiang-ying-jiu-bie-xinji-lpjdh6wohap6bqbghnvltu2oey5i|Hanji 1162]] says Pei Qian is recalled after Wang Bi's death and predicts resistance to the replacement authority.

Realized frontier risk:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1162-jinyi-zhisi-xiang-ying-jiu-bie-xinji-lpjdh6wohap6bqbghnvltu2oey5i|Hanji 1162]] connects the later Wuhuan revolt and Cao Zhang's campaign to the personnel transition.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Temporal sequence does not prove sole causation. The episode does not isolate tribal politics, replacement quality, taxation, military pressure, or wider frontier conditions. Retaining every locally embedded official can also prevent development of successors and leave urgent vacancies elsewhere.
