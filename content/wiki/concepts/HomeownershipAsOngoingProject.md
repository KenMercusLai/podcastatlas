# Homeownership as Ongoing Project

## Definition

Homeownership as ongoing project is the view that closing transfers not just an asset and living space but a continuing queue of maintenance, improvement, coordination, learning, cost, and care.

## Current Synthesis

In [[ep160-wo-zai-meiguo-ziji-jian-fangzi-meiguo-ye-you-xuequ-fang-ckwriueecxq5abaaaacki6jm|EP160]], even a newly built house arrives unfinished from the owner's perspective. Curtains, lighting, wiring, furniture, a gym, wall finishes, garage work, solar, landscaping, planting, and maintenance extend the project beyond handover. High labor quotes can make DIY economically rational, but saving cash converts work into owner time, skill acquisition, safety judgment, tools, and responsibility.

## Key Claims
- Closing ends the purchase process but begins a new operating and improvement cycle.
- Total ownership cost includes tax, energy, maintenance, furnishings, landscaping, installation labor, and the owner's own time—not only price and mortgage payment.
- DIY can reduce cash expense when labor is costly, but it transfers execution, safety, scheduling, and defect risk to the owner.
- Early infrastructure decisions can expand later options and reduce destructive rework.
- Emotional attachment can grow through repeated care and modification rather than appearing automatically at key transfer.

## Evidence
- Project backlog: [[ep160-wo-zai-meiguo-ziji-jian-fangzi-meiguo-ye-you-xuequ-fang-ckwriueecxq5abaaaacki6jm|EP160]] says Ricky tracked more than twenty post-handover tasks across pending, active, and completed states.
- Labor substitution: [[ep160-wo-zai-meiguo-ziji-jian-fangzi-meiguo-ye-you-xuequ-fang-ckwriueecxq5abaaaacki6jm|EP160]] contrasts modest material prices with much higher installation quotes for tile and curtains.
- Infrastructure and operations: [[ep160-wo-zai-meiguo-ziji-jian-fangzi-meiguo-ye-you-xuequ-fang-ckwriueecxq5abaaaacki6jm|EP160]] connects wiring, smart controls, solar, batteries, central vacuum, and desert landscaping to continuing household operation.
- Attachment through work: [[ep160-wo-zai-meiguo-ziji-jian-fangzi-meiguo-ye-you-xuequ-fang-ckwriueecxq5abaaaacki6jm|EP160]] reports emotional anticlimax at closing followed by stronger belonging as the owner changed and used the house.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source reflects one detached new-build home and one owner who enjoys technical and DIY work. Renters, condominium owners, owners with disabilities or limited time, households in service-rich markets, and buyers of fully finished homes face different task loads. DIY is not automatically cheaper once tools, mistakes, permits, warranties, injury risk, and opportunity cost are counted.
