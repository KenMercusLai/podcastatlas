## Definition

Urbanized supernatural worldbuilding translates ghosts, ritual tools, underworld administration, reincarnation, and folk-occult rules into the objects, occupations, buildings, and logistics of modern city life.

## Current Synthesis

The 佳佳大厦 arc of [[MyDateWithAVampire|《我和僵尸有个约会》]] does not treat tradition and modernity as separate layers. [[MaXiaoling|马小玲]] carries exorcism equipment as professional work gear; cinnabar resembles lipstick; glasses enable ghost sight; elevators transport souls; underworld agents wear office clothes; and an apartment building becomes a dense social and supernatural system.

These translations make the fantastic both playful and usable. Viewers can understand where a ritual object is stored, who operates it, how neighbors move through the threat, and how supernatural procedure intersects with familiar infrastructure. Explicit rules also create suspense, but their value depends on consistency: 三破日, protective candles, possession, 厄修罗 strength, and reincarnation timing become weaker when introduced mainly to force a scene or contradicted by later capability.

## Key Claims

- Modern objects can preserve a supernatural function while changing its visual and social form.
- Urban buildings make neighbor relations, logistics, secrecy, and haunting part of the same spatial system.
- Treating exorcism as work gives magical expertise tools, clients, procedures, and professional concealment.
- Bureaucratic and transport imagery can make death and reincarnation legible without removing wonder or humor.
- Rule-based supernatural suspense depends on limits remaining intelligible across scenes.
- Emotional causality can remain the final solution even in a heavily procedural occult plot.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

This concept is grounded in one arc as reconstructed by one podcast and does not establish a general history of Hong Kong supernatural television. “Traditional” elements may already be modern inventions or mixtures, and 三破日 is explicitly described as inconsistent folk usage rather than a securely attested orthodox-calendar rule. Worldbuilding effectiveness and childhood fear are reception judgments.

## What Changed

- Established the concept through a modern apartment, professional exorcist tools, bureaucratic afterlife, and rule-based haunting.
