# Folk Maker Art Interpretation / 民间手艺艺术化解读

## Definition
Folk maker art interpretation is the process of reading self-taught craft objects through art language while keeping their maker, material, humor, and use-context intact.

## Current Synthesis
The 手工耿 episode frames a village workshop and hardware-store imagination as art-adjacent without converting the maker into a conventional contemporary artist. [[LuoYonghao]] uses Dadaist and modern-art language to explain why objects can be absurd, serious, and memorable; [[ShougongGeng|手工耿]] responds from a maker's position, asking whether art can remain art if it becomes fixed theory.

The current synthesis is therefore double: art language can help explain why the work matters, but it should not erase the labor, tool knowledge, stainless-steel materiality, danger, and functional intent that make the work legible to ordinary viewers.

## Key Claims
- Art interpretation can name value that ordinary utility language misses, especially absurdity, surprise, material presence, and future misreading.
- A maker's lack of formal art training can be part of the work's public meaning rather than evidence against artistic value.
- Folk craft and modern art can overlap when objects transform everyday materials into strange, memorable, semi-functional artifacts.
- Art-theory labels should remain secondary when the maker's own vocabulary is usefulness, making, testing, and audience enjoyment.
- Durable interpretation depends on preserving both the comic surface and the serious physical labor behind it.

## Evidence
- Art language naming value: [[shougonggeng-luoyonghao-cunkou-de-wujindian-li-chuxianle-yige-dada-zhuyi-yishujia-lgbdj-juqslyga0ulvvrqwnedga1]] describes the host placing 手工耿 between useful and useless, serious and funny, folk craft and modern art.
- Lack of formal training: [[shougonggeng-luoyonghao-cunkou-de-wujindian-li-chuxianle-yige-dada-zhuyi-yishujia-lgbdj-juqslyga0ulvvrqwnedga1]] says the host calls him a folk artist without contemporary-art training or need for contemporary-art endorsement.
- Everyday material transformation: [[shougonggeng-luoyonghao-cunkou-de-wujindian-li-chuxianle-yige-dada-zhuyi-yishujia-lgbdj-juqslyga0ulvvrqwnedga1]] includes wood, stone, stainless steel, scrap vehicles, discarded materials, and childhood toys turned into strange objects.
- Maker vocabulary: [[shougonggeng-luoyonghao-cunkou-de-wujindian-li-chuxianle-yige-dada-zhuyi-yishujia-lgbdj-juqslyga0ulvvrqwnedga1]] records 手工耿 insisting he often starts from usefulness and questioning fixed art-theory routes.
- Labor behind comedy: [[shougonggeng-luoyonghao-cunkou-de-wujindian-li-chuxianle-yige-dada-zhuyi-yishujia-lgbdj-juqslyga0ulvvrqwnedga1]] details shaping, welding, polishing, safety testing, failed builds, and cost control.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source offers an interview-based interpretation, not an art-historical consensus. Calling the work Dadaist or modern-art-like is a productive reading, but the wiki should keep it source-scoped until more art-critical sources are ingested.

- Preserved the tension between outside interpretation and the maker's own practical vocabulary.
- Linked art value to material labor and audience comprehension.
