# AI Infra Crypto Analogy

AI infra crypto analogy is [[WangTianfan]]'s warning in [[reai-yige-hangye-15-nian-de-liyou-shi-shenme-duitan-wang-tianfan-woyao-tou-zhenzheng-de-kuaile-tou-zui-chun-de-yuanjing-tou-renxing-de-guanghui-gonglu-boke-lu98aa1byafbbljyjrn8oquiezk]] that the rush into AI infrastructure can resemble the earlier rush into crypto infrastructure. The common pattern is high valuation flexibility around upstream systems whose use, durability, or economic capture is hard to prove quickly.

The source does not reject infrastructure. Wang says upstream research and infrastructure investment can still benefit the whole industry. The risk is that market incentives give founders the wrong reward signal: build labs, models, or infra narratives while losing contact with whether anyone uses the output.

## Key Claims
- Infrastructure narratives can attract capital before their demand is falsifiable.
- Investors can overvalue upstream position when downstream usage, application pull, or customer willingness to pay remains uncertain.
- The danger is strongest when founders are rewarded for category language rather than product use.
- The analogy should discipline valuation and usage checks, not collapse AI into crypto or dismiss all infrastructure work.
