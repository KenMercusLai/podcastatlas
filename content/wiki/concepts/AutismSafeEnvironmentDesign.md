# Autism Safe Environment Design

## Definition
Autism safe environment design is the support pattern of reducing sensory, memory, predictability, communication, and social threat before asking an autistic person to participate, learn, or tolerate a situation.

## Current Synthesis
The original source built the concept from practical cases: a dental clinic, a cruise, an art class, and a disrupted outing all become easier when adults explain, sequence, lower threat, and preserve choice. The 不合时宜 episode extends the same logic into clinical and public systems. Sensory overload, pain sensitivity, cold sensitivity, social communication, and school inclusion are not minor preferences; they are part of how support becomes possible.

The current synthesis is that safe environments are not avoidance by default. They are the conditions under which participation, learning, and communication can occur without every task becoming a test of endurance.

## Key Claims
- Safety is sensory, cognitive, social, and communicative, not only physical.
- Explanation, preparation, and choice can turn feared events into tolerable or positive experiences.
- Accommodation can reveal ability that punishment or forced endurance hides.
- Safe environments include schools, clinics, homes, workplaces, community spaces, and public language.
- Environment design should work with [[AutismEarlyIntervention]] and [[AutismSupportSystems]], not replace professional support.

## Evidence
- Practical accommodation cases - [[46-zhe-shijie-weiyi-de-ni-qing-xiangxin-na-duo-manman-zhanfang-de-hua-623717720]] uses a rocking chair in a dental clinic, calm explanation on a cruise, and outing preparation to show how lowered threat changes behavior.
- Behavior interpretation - [[46-zhe-shijie-weiyi-de-ni-qing-xiangxin-na-duo-manman-zhanfang-de-hua-623717720]] links refusal, fear, aggression, or distress to environmental overload, memory triggers, and limited expressive tools.
- Sensory and clinical description - [[cong-jiyin-bianji-nvtong-zhisi-shuoqi-zibizheng-bushi-xuyao-bei-xiufu-de-cuowu-lg7dofyeqv-7noq9w74oh6hsyk4b]] describes autistic experience through social communication, sensory overload, and unusual reactions to pain or cold.
- School and community design - [[cong-jiyin-bianji-nvtong-zhisi-shuoqi-zibizheng-bushi-xuyao-bei-xiufu-de-cuowu-lg7dofyeqv-7noq9w74oh6hsyk4b]] discusses inclusive education, embedded therapy services, employment examples, and integrated living spaces as broader environmental support.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Safe environment design is not a claim that every demand should disappear or that all distress has a simple environmental fix. The sources keep professional intervention, family practice, and individualized judgment in view.
