# Turkish Opposition Realignment

Turkish opposition realignment is the rapid shift described in [[brave-new-whirl-turkeys-opposition-overhaul-6a69c7766043035f89eb097f]], where [[OzgurOzel]] leaves the [[RepublicanPeoplesParty|CHP]], creates the [[NewPartyTurkey|New Party]], and attracts enough defecting MPs to make the new vehicle the second-largest force in Parliament.

The concept is not simply party formation. The source presents realignment as a response to pressure: courts remove leaders, police enter party headquarters, mayors and local officials are arrested, and [[EkremImamoglu]] remains imprisoned. The opposition therefore has to become electorally viable while also surviving [[JudicializedOppositionSuppression]].

## Key Claims
- Realignment can happen through defections when an old opposition party is captured, hollowed out, or made electorally inert.
- A new party can inherit a base without automatically broadening the coalition to nationalists, Kurdish voters, or other needed blocs.
- Polling momentum matters only if candidates, mayors, and party infrastructure survive legal and police pressure.
- The same move can strengthen opposition visibility while exposing it to sharper state resistance.

## Connections
- [[OzgurOzel]], [[NewPartyTurkey]], and [[RepublicanPeoplesParty]] - realignment actors.
- [[RecepTayyipErdogan]] and [[JusticeAndDevelopmentParty]] - incumbent side.
- [[EkremImamoglu]] and [[MansurYavas]] - candidate-pressure cases.
- [[JudicializedOppositionSuppression]] and [[OppositionPartyHollowing]] - mechanisms driving the realignment.
