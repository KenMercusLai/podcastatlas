# Production Investment City / 产投城市

Production Investment City / 产投城市 is the distinction [[no-211-huishang-wangshi-cong-qianshi-buxiu-dao-zuiniu-fengtou-1002275189]] draws from the popular "venture-capital city" label for [[Hefei]]. The episode quotes the local political framing that Hefei is not simply gambling on financial upside; it is trying to build production capacity, employment, supply chains, and city position.

The concept matters because local public investment has more objectives than fund return. A production investment city may accept concentrated early risk if a project can anchor suppliers, talent, infrastructure, tax base, and strategic industry presence. That makes evaluation harder: a financially mediocre investment can still have spillovers, while a paper gain may not be enough if the industrial ecosystem fails to deepen.

The source keeps the risk visible. A city that behaves like industrial capital can misjudge a technology route, overpay for a weak company, or stay exposed to cyclical losses even after an early rescue looks successful.
