# Organoid Computing

Organoid computing is the biological-computation boundary raised in [[i-robot-ai-and-consciousness-6a880840b0d5e24ea40c7015]] when [[AlokJha]] mentions work involving human brain cells on silicon chips and organoids used for computation. The source uses it as a future-facing complication for the [[AIConsciousnessBoundary|AI consciousness boundary]].

The concept is not presented as proof of consciousness. Its importance is that it weakens a simple digital-versus-biological divide: if computation begins to use living neural tissue, then debates over [[ComputationalFunctionalism]], [[BiologicalAgency]], and [[ConsciousnessMeasurement]] become harder to separate.

## Key Claims
- Biological computing can make AI-consciousness questions less cleanly about silicon alone.
- Brain-cell or organoid systems may be more "brain-like" without automatically becoming conscious.
- The source treats organoid computing as a future possibility, not as evidence that current LLMs are conscious.
- Ethical and measurement questions will become sharper if living neural tissue becomes part of computing systems.
