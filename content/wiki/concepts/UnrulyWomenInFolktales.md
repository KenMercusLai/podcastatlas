# Unruly Women In Folktales / 民间故事中的悍妇

Unruly women in folktales is the episode 38 concept for female characters who are vivid because they are not obedient, pure, or morally cleaned. In [[38-anjila-kate-jingguai-gushiji-o-mirende-hanfu-609607139]], [[JingguaiGushiji|《精怪故事集》]] supplies women who deceive husbands, pursue lovers, rescue children, attack spouses, trick men through literacy, refuse to concede, and sometimes punish rivals with grotesque violence.

The concept extends [[FemaleSelfPossession]] into a less respectable register. A woman can be active, funny, selfish, hungry, sexual, cruel, maternal, or clever without becoming a role model. The source treats that moral discomfort as part of the pleasure and force of old stories.

## Key Claims
- Female agency in old tales is not always virtuous agency.
- The episode's "悍妇" is an admiring category for women who act directly rather than waiting for male rescue or moral permission.
- These stories often work through [[BlackComicMoralInversion]]: cruelty and laughter coexist without resolving into a clean lesson.
- The concept qualifies [[GenderedFairyTalePunishment]] by showing women as tricksters and punishers as well as punished bodies.
