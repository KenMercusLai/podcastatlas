# Translation Path Dependence / 翻译路径依赖

## Definition
Translation path dependence is the process by which an early or widely circulated rendering becomes the default through repetition, education, publishing, and institutional use, making later alternatives costly even when they clarify important distinctions.

## Current Synthesis
[[323-ai-shifou-keyi-qudai-fanyi-cuowu-de-fanyi-ruhe-suzao-xianshi-luw-q1sjef4uttao4ghe1yx7rkyf]] argues that terminology competition is not a pure contest between truth and error. Familiarity, pronounceability, dissemination, political context, and existing intellectual traditions help determine which term survives. Once embedded, a rendering also becomes training data for [[AITranslation]], so automated systems can reinforce the convention that historical uptake produced.

Path dependence does not make established language useless or frozen. It creates a reason to compare defeated alternatives, reconstruct the inherited associations of standard terms, and distinguish coordination value from conceptual precision.

## Key Claims
- Widespread adoption is evidence of coordination success, not proof of unique semantic accuracy.
- Early lexical choices can accumulate institutional advantage through publishing, schooling, and repetition.
- A receiving language's older religious, philosophical, and political vocabulary shapes how imported concepts are heard.
- AI systems can amplify established renderings because their training material reflects accumulated usage.
- Critical comparison can recover obscured distinctions without assuming that society can or should replace every standard term.

## Evidence
- Terminology competition: [[323-ai-shifou-keyi-qudai-fanyi-cuowu-de-fanyi-ruhe-suzao-xianshi-luw-q1sjef4uttao4ghe1yx7rkyf]] contrasts Yan Fu's alternatives with Japanese-coined Chinese terms that became common usage.
- Political vocabulary: [[323-ai-shifou-keyi-qudai-fanyi-cuowu-de-fanyi-ruhe-suzao-xianshi-luw-q1sjef4uttao4ghe1yx7rkyf]] uses “权利/权力,” “自由,” and “经济” to argue that established forms carry interpretive consequences.
- Intellectual mediation: [[323-ai-shifou-keyi-qudai-fanyi-cuowu-de-fanyi-ruhe-suzao-xianshi-luw-q1sjef4uttao4ghe1yx7rkyf]] describes Western concepts entering Chinese through Japanese, German-idealist, Buddhist, Confucian, and Anglo-American layers.
- AI reinforcement: [[323-ai-shifou-keyi-qudai-fanyi-cuowu-de-fanyi-ruhe-suzao-xianshi-luw-q1sjef4uttao4ghe1yx7rkyf]] argues that model outputs tend toward majority forms in inherited corpora.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode does not establish the precise diffusion history or causal effect of every example. Common usage can change a word's meaning beyond its etymology, and stable terminology also enables shared discourse. Path dependence therefore identifies a mechanism and research question, not a blanket verdict that standard translations are wrong.

## What Changed
- Added a mechanism linking historical terminology competition to present-day AI output.
- Distinguished coordination success from conceptual accuracy.
