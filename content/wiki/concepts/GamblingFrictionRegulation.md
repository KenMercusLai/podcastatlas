# Gambling Friction Regulation

## Definition
Gambling friction regulation uses payment, communication, or frequency limits to slow the transition from an impulse or loss into another wager.

## Current Synthesis
[[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-90166871d561fa45ba428e0a0fbc3e7e-20290737-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-90166871d561fa45ba428e0a0fbc3e7e-20290737]] presents Colorado's approach as a public-health experiment: prohibit credit-card deposits, restrict sportsbook push notifications and texts, and cap daily deposits. The design targets chase behavior by adding pauses at the funding and re-engagement points, but its numerical limits are openly acknowledged as unvalidated.

## Key Claims
- Payment friction can separate wagering from instant unsecured borrowing.
- Communication limits can reduce platform-triggered re-engagement during vulnerable moments.
- Deposit-frequency caps target the escalation pattern in which repeated small losses prompt progressively larger reloads.
- Regulation can create a natural experiment even before it supplies proof of reduced harm.
- A public-health frame widens the relevant outcomes from individual losses to debt, bankruptcy, family harm, and suicide risk.

## Evidence
### Funding and communication controls
- [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-90166871d561fa45ba428e0a0fbc3e7e-20290737-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-90166871d561fa45ba428e0a0fbc3e7e-20290737]] attributes Colorado's credit-card ban, messaging restrictions, and six-deposit limit to a bipartisan law discussed by [[MattBallColorado|Matt Ball]].

### Chase behavior as the intervention target
- [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-90166871d561fa45ba428e0a0fbc3e7e-20290737-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-90166871d561fa45ba428e0a0fbc3e7e-20290737]] describes escalating reloads after losses as the behavioral pathway the law is intended to interrupt.

### Policy diffusion
- [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-90166871d561fa45ba428e0a0fbc3e7e-20290737-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-90166871d561fa45ba428e0a0fbc3e7e-20290737]] says other states already restrict credit-card deposits and that legislators were asking Colorado about its approach.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- The source explicitly says lawmakers lacked outcome data and were partly guessing about the limits.
- Users may substitute other payment methods, accounts, or operators, so transaction friction does not guarantee lower total gambling.
- Constituent stories establish plausible harm pathways but not population-level policy effectiveness.

## What Changed
- Added a policy concept for slowing betting deposits and platform prompts.
- Preserved the law's experimental status rather than treating friction as proven harm reduction.
