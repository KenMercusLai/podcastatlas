# Platform Moderation Compliance Labor / 平台合规劳动

Platform moderation compliance labor / 平台合规劳动 is the hidden work needed to keep a live performance inside platform rules while still making it visually effective. In [[baiwan-ge-jingguan-shehui-tingshuo-ni-ye-xiang-dang-zhubo-lgd0yapclwvsessg7xnhaarpyiwy]], clothing, collar height, trouser legs, exposed skin, belly-button coverage, lighting color, camera angle, and AI image-recognition errors all become part of the production problem.

The concept extends [[AlgorithmicEntanglement]] into live bodily performance. Creators are not only optimizing titles or covers for distribution; they are tuning bodies, costumes, lights, and frames so automated moderation does not misread the scene and restrict traffic during a session.

## Key Claims
- Compliance is productive labor, not only a legal or policy checklist.
- AI moderation can create false positives, and a live-stream restriction can damage the session before appeal resolves anything.
- Costume and styling workers operate inside a double constraint: they must make performers attractive while avoiding rule-triggering visual patterns.
- Review after suspected violations becomes cross-functional work involving operators, hosts, anchors, lighting, and other teams.
- Moderation pressure can shape aesthetic norms because safe visibility becomes part of what can be performed.
