# City-Type Sampling / 城市类型抽样

## Definition
City-type sampling uses quantitative rankings and administrative knowledge to select urban cases, then interprets each case within a bounded regional, historical, industrial, and geographic type instead of treating one middle-ranked city as a national average.

## Current Synthesis
[[chengshi-jiushi-zheyang-22-zhongguo-de-zhongweishu-chengshi-shi-nazuo-1021674577|城市就是这样22]] develops the method through a failed premise. [[DavidFishman|David Fishman]] initially chose [[Linfen|临汾]] because it sat near the middle of a 337-city list, but fieldwork showed that rank position could not erase north-south variation, resource history, city-county differences, or rapid change. The revised method keeps rankings as a way to narrow the search while moving representativeness claims to plural types such as northern post-industrial cities, Central Plains cities, and southwestern minority mountain cities.

## Key Claims
- A list median is a property of selected indicators and weights, not proof that the selected city represents a national population.
- Regional geography, industrial history, administrative structure, and migration patterns define more useful comparison strata.
- The relevant observation scale may be below the prefecture-level city because central districts and outer counties can follow different trajectories.
- Static comparisons need longitudinal checks when infrastructure, population settlement, environment, and consumer services change quickly.
- Quantitative selection becomes more informative when paired with interviews about concrete household choices.

## Evidence
- Rank-to-type correction: [[chengshi-jiushi-zheyang-22-zhongguo-de-zhongweishu-chengshi-shi-nazuo-1021674577|城市就是这样22]] shows Fishman revising Linfen from "China's median city" to a northern resource-dependent, post-industrial case.
- Within-city heterogeneity: [[chengshi-jiushi-zheyang-22-zhongguo-de-zhongweishu-chengshi-shi-nazuo-1021674577|城市就是这样22]] contrasts more pessimistic interviews in Linfen's old urban area with more optimistic accounts in some outer counties.
- Temporal qualification: [[chengshi-jiushi-zheyang-22-zhongguo-de-zhongweishu-chengshi-shi-nazuo-1021674577|城市就是这样22]] uses a Jishou new-district revisit and Kangbashi comparison to show why a single empty-looking moment cannot settle whether a development is a permanent "ghost city."

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The proposed city types are analytical categories, not demonstrated statistical strata, and the episode does not specify a reproducible classification model. Small, convenience-based interview samples cannot estimate prevalence within a type. Rankings may still be useful for explicit indicator comparisons, while field impressions can introduce their own selection and observer effects.

## What Changed
- Replaced a single national median-city proxy with plural regional and historical comparison types.
- Added district and county heterogeneity to the unit-of-analysis decision.
- Added repeated observation as a requirement for claims about fast-changing urban areas.
