# App Recommendation Media / 应用推荐媒体

## Definition
App recommendation media is a mobile-internet content form that helps users discover, evaluate, combine, and understand applications through hands-on selection rather than only rankings, downloads, or platform promotion.

## Current Synthesis
In the No.229 source, [[Sspai|少数派]] begins by turning early mobile-app abundance into a curation problem. As iOS and Android applications multiplied, users needed someone to try products, explain actual use, identify flaws, and show workflows that combined apps, hardware, and everyday scenes.

The source also shows why this media category could become broader. Once apps connected to hardware, productivity, maps, food, mobility, and other life services, app recommendation could expand into digital-life guidance. The value moved from "which app should I install" toward how people use digital tools to organize work and life.

## Key Claims
- Early mobile-app abundance created demand for trusted filtering and explanation.
- Hands-on use mattered because app value depended on workflow, context, and tradeoffs, not only feature lists.
- Application media could expand into hardware and life services as apps became interfaces to everyday activity.
- Author systems and contributor payment can make app recommendation deeper than short, low-cost listicles.
- The category becomes vulnerable to shallow recommendation formats unless it preserves experience, judgment, and user trust.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This concept currently rests on 少数派's source-scoped origin story and does not map the full Chinese app-recommendation market. The source contrasts 少数派 with shallower recommendation formats but does not name or systematically analyze those competitors.
