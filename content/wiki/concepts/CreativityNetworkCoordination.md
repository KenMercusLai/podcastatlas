# Creativity Network Coordination

## Definition
Creativity network coordination is the source's account of creative cognition as interaction among spontaneous memory-based generation, salience-based selection, and executive control rather than the product of one brain region.

## Current Synthesis
[[the-science-of-creativity-how-to-enhance-creative-innovation-scim5096680097]] assigns complementary roles to three large-scale systems. The default mode network supplies internally generated material and recombines memory; the salience network marks what appears interesting or relevant; and executive systems suppress, organize, test, and act on selected possibilities. The useful claim is coordination across modes, not a rigid one-network-per-stage map. This aligns with [[BrainNetworkModularity]] and [[ExecutiveFunction]] in resisting simple localization while remaining a source-scoped explanatory model.

## Key Claims
- Spontaneous imagination draws on stored experience rather than appearing from nowhere.
- Salience processes help select which internally or externally generated possibilities merit attention.
- Executive control constrains, organizes, tests, and implements candidate ideas.
- Creativity depends on interaction among systems that can otherwise compete for control.
- No single network or anatomical location is sufficient to explain creative work.

## Evidence
- Network triad - [[the-science-of-creativity-how-to-enhance-creative-innovation-scim5096680097]] names executive, default mode, and salience networks as major contributors.
- Functional differentiation - [[the-science-of-creativity-how-to-enhance-creative-innovation-scim5096680097]] links spontaneous memory-based imagination to default mode activity, relevance selection to salience, and suppression or deliberate control to executive function.
- Process mapping - [[the-science-of-creativity-how-to-enhance-creative-innovation-scim5096680097]] uses the network account to explain why broad generation and focused selection require different but coordinated operations.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source summary does not establish exclusive functions, a fixed activation order, or causal necessity for each network in every creative task. Large-scale network labels compress heterogeneous regions and dynamic interactions, and creativity also depends on domain knowledge, sensory and motor systems, culture, tools, collaboration, and practice.

## What Changed
- Established a distributed-network account of creative generation, selection, and control.
