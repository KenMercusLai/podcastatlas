## Definition

Wuxia indirect characterization is the construction of martial scale, beauty, and emotional attachment through delayed entrance, consequences, witnesses, reactions, and meaningful omission rather than exhaustive technical or psychological explanation.

## Current Synthesis

The [[YueNvJian|《越女剑》]] case shows several indirect devices working together. [[AQingYueNvJian|阿青]] enters after the plot has made Yue's military weakness visible; even partial exposure to her swordplay changes trained soldiers; and her passage through massed guards demonstrates a level the narrator does not reduce to named moves. The audience therefore measures her by changed conditions, not by a technical manual.

Emotion uses the same grammar. [[FanLi|范蠡]]'s remembered description of [[XiShi|西施]] leaves Ah Qing unheard, the white ape's attacks imply attachment without naming it, and Ah Qing's halted strike makes Xi Shi's beauty visible through a rival's reaction. Omission does not remove motive; it keeps several motives possible and asks action, sequence, and response to carry the characterization.

## Key Claims

- Delayed entrance can make a character's arrival resolve pressure that the narrative has already taught the reader to feel.
- A fighter's scale can be conveyed through defeated opponents, transformed students, breached defenses, and aftermath rather than enumerated techniques.
- Reaction can characterize both the observed figure and the observer at the same time.
- Dialogue that fails to connect can reveal unequal attention more efficiently than an explicit declaration of neglect.
- Strategic omission preserves interpretive plurality when several motives fit the same action.
- Indirection becomes weak evidence if readers promote one possible motive into settled psychology without qualification.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

This framework currently rests on one close reading of one novella. Delayed entrance is not always characterization, unexplained action is not automatically rich ambiguity, and reader inference can exceed the text. Claims about love, jealousy, Daoist naturalness, and authorial intention should therefore remain tied to observable sequence and explicitly marked as interpretations.
