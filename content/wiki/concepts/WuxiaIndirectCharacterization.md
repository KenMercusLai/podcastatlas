## Definition

Wuxia indirect characterization constructs martial scale, ethical principle, attachment, and identity through delayed entrance, consequences, choices, dialogue, witnesses, reactions, and meaningful omission rather than exhaustive technical or psychological explanation.

## Current Synthesis

Indirect characterization lets changed conditions measure a person. In [[YueNvJian|《越女剑》]], [[AQingYueNvJian|阿青]] enters only after Yue's military weakness is visible; partial exposure to her swordplay changes trained soldiers, and her passage through massed guards communicates power without a technical catalogue. In [[BloodSeaFragranceGuLong|《血海飘香》]], [[ChuLiuxiang|楚留香]]'s non-killing, convivial speech, care for friends, and advice to [[HeiZhenzhuGuLong|黑珍珠]] make love of life observable, while [[ZhongyuanYidianhong|中原一点红]] becomes a friend through repayment and rescue.

Emotion and identity use the same grammar of attention, reaction, and omission. [[FanLi|范蠡]]'s remembered description of [[XiShi|西施]] leaves Ah Qing unheard, the white ape's attacks imply attachment without naming it, and Ah Qing's halted strike makes Xi Shi's beauty visible through a rival's reaction. 黑珍珠's blushes, jealousy, and omissions likewise imply identity and attachment without explicit disclosure. Direct summary can flatten an arc that action has already earned, but omission is not a license to convert one plausible reading into fact.

## Key Claims

- Delayed entrance can make a character's arrival resolve pressure that the narrative has already taught the reader to feel.
- A fighter's scale can be conveyed through defeated opponents, transformed students, breached defenses, and aftermath rather than enumerated techniques.
- Reaction can characterize both the observed figure and the observer at the same time.
- Repeated conduct can ground an ethical trait more strongly than narrator declaration.
- Debt repayment, rescue, jealousy, and embarrassment can make relational change visible through action and reaction.
- Strategic omission preserves interpretive plurality when several motives fit the same action.
- Indirection becomes weak evidence if readers promote one possible motive into settled psychology without qualification.

- [[031-quhua-gulong-chuliuxiang-chuanqi-xuehai-piaoxiang-ljyk3bfdjhkzwhnu-29oqs8ugeps]] reads 楚留香's life ethic and 一点红's opponent-to-friend arc through dialogue, restraint, repayment, and aid.
- [[031-quhua-gulong-chuliuxiang-chuanqi-xuehai-piaoxiang-ljyk3bfdjhkzwhnu-29oqs8ugeps]] treats 黑珍珠's reactions and the absence of explicit disclosure as productive but not conclusive implication.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

This framework rests on two close readings of fiction, not universal evidence about reader response. Delayed entrance is not always characterization, unexplained action is not automatically rich ambiguity, and dialogue can tell rather than show. Claims about love, jealousy, gender, Daoist naturalness, or authorial intent should remain tied to observable sequence and marked as interpretation.

## What Changed

- Extended the framework from martial display and reaction to ethical conduct, friendship arcs, dialogue, and identity implication.
- Added a distinction between earned explicit clarification and explanation that weakens already sufficient action.
