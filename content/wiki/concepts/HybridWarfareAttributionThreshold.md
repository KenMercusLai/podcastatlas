# Hybrid Warfare Attribution Threshold

## Definition
Hybrid warfare attribution threshold is the evidence boundary between recognizing that an incident fits a known pattern of deniable state pressure and concluding that a particular state planned, directed, or enabled that incident.

## Current Synthesis
[[foiled-plot-did-iran-plan-british-base-attack-6aba32b170b66274d96fb345]] develops the concept through a suspected explosives plot near [[RAFFairford]]. The base's use in Iran-related operations, Iranian warnings, and Britain's wider record of alleged Iran-backed plots create a plausible hypothesis. They do not establish responsibility for this case. The episode also keeps [[Russia]] in the candidate set because it has used hybrid methods in Europe.

The threshold matters more when the target and method imply escalation. A kinetic attack on a sovereign British military base could demand a response and be harder to deny than recruitment through intermediaries, attacks on dissidents, or cyber disruption. Higher consequences increase the need for incident-specific evidence rather than lowering it.

## Key Claims
- Pattern fit can justify investigation and preparedness without proving authorship.
- Strategic motive, prior warnings, and target relevance are circumstantial indicators rather than complete attribution.
- Criminal intermediaries, social-media recruitment, and low-level operatives can widen distance between a sponsor and an attack.
- Alternative capable actors must remain visible until evidence meaningfully discriminates among them.
- The more an attribution could trigger military or diplomatic retaliation, the more explicitly confidence and evidentiary gaps should be stated.

## Evidence
- Pattern and motive: [[foiled-plot-did-iran-plan-british-base-attack-6aba32b170b66274d96fb345]] connects Fairford's operational role, Iranian warnings, and Britain's history of alleged Iran-backed plots.
- Unresolved case evidence: [[foiled-plot-did-iran-plan-british-base-attack-6aba32b170b66274d96fb345]] says authorities had not confirmed the responsible group and identifies [[Russia]] as another possible actor.
- Escalation threshold: [[foiled-plot-did-iran-plan-british-base-attack-6aba32b170b66274d96fb345]] distinguishes a possible kinetic strike on [[RAFFairford]] from more deniable threats to dissidents, community targets, or infrastructure.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode offers a reasoned suspicion, not a forensic record, official attribution, or adjudicated case. Reported explosives, evacuation, target proximity, and geopolitical context do not by themselves identify command, sponsorship, or intended operational outcome. The suspected plan to damage aircraft with explosives or drones is explicitly speculative.

## What Changed
- Established a general attribution standard from the unresolved Fairford case.
- Separated broad Iran-linked threat reporting from incident-specific responsibility.
