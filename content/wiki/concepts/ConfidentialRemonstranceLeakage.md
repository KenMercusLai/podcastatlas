# Confidential Remonstrance Leakage / 密奏泄露风险

Confidential remonstrance leakage / 密奏泄露风险 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-912-mimi-daodi-gai-bugai-shuo-chukou-lhsgipxwc17pahc-r6jl7xdyrs2a|Hanji 912]] as the failure mode where truthful advice depends on secrecy and patron protection, but the channel is exposed before correction can become policy. [[CaiYong|蔡邕]] asks [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] to keep his memorial private because it names harem, eunuch, personnel, and appointment problems; [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] reads the emperor's reaction, sees the memorial, and leaks it to the criticized network.

The source makes the risk different from ordinary indiscretion. Cai Yong's facts and loyalty are not enough once [[PalaceAccessMonopoly|宫廷接触垄断]] lets a targeted faction see the accusation first and prepare retaliation. The leak sets up [[ChengHuangLateHan|程璜]]'s anonymous accusation and the punishment/exile branch in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-913-kan-dong-saiweng-shima-wu-tou-rensheng-fuhuo-lkfbrwtgsjma85bzugqonzk3hy4g|Hanji 913]], making confidentiality a condition of [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|君臣反馈失灵]] repair rather than a decorative request.

This differs from [[LethalPlanLeakageRisk|致命计划泄露风险]]. A leaked plan exposes intended action; a leaked remonstrance exposes what the speaker knows, whom he blames, and whether the ruler can protect him. Both overlap with [[CommunicationBoundarySetting|Communication Boundary Setting]] and [[AdversitySilenceDiscipline|逆境守口]], but this page is specifically about high-risk advice routed through a supposedly private official channel.

## Key Claims
- Confidentiality can be part of the advice itself when the content names protected interests.
- A ruler who hears frank correction but cannot protect the channel may teach future officials to stay silent.
- Inner-court observers can convert a private memorial into actionable intelligence for the criticized faction.
- Loyal advice can fail at the protection and aftermath stage even when the substance is politically serious.
- Remonstrance leakage is adjacent to plan leakage, but the exposed asset is the speaker's accusation and trust relationship rather than an executable operation.
