# Martyrdom Staging

Martyrdom staging is the deliberate use of dress, prayer, ritual, and composure to turn defeat into testimony. [[696-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-killing-the-queen-of-scots-part-6-glt2334035496]] applies this to [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]]' execution at [[FotheringhayCastle]].

Mary's black outer garments, white veil, Catholic prayers, English prayer for [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]], and revealed crimson clothing let supporters read her death as Catholic witness rather than only treason punishment. The source's point is not that the legal judgment disappears, but that execution remains a symbolic contest even after the state has won coercively.
