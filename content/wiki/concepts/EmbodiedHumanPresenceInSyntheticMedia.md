# Embodied Human Presence in Synthetic Media / 合成媒介中的真人在场

## Definition
Embodied human presence in synthetic media is the audience value attributed to situated bodily response, shared physical experience, reciprocal attention, and unpredictable interaction when generated text and images make polished expression easier to produce and harder to authenticate.

## Current Synthesis
The episode argues that reality programming retains value not simply because its stories are unscripted, but because real people enter concrete situations together. Trembling hands, pauses, discarded paper, eating habits, wind moving clothing, awkward travel, disagreement, and incomplete control become evidence that an encounter occurred even though the program still selects and edits that evidence.

This is not a claim that bodies guarantee truth or that AI cannot imitate emotional form. It is a narrower media judgment: shared, reciprocal, and materially constrained interaction provides provenance and contingency that a standalone polished text does not supply by itself.

## Key Claims
- Specific sensory detail often carries more interpersonal credibility than abstract emotional summary.
- Bodily response can show that an encounter matters without proving the speaker's historical account or private motive.
- Shared environments constrain participants and create reciprocal events that no single narrator fully controls.
- Editing can amplify authentic reactions while still narrowing chronology and interpretation.
- Uncertainty about generated prose can increase demand for legible human provenance and situated creation.
- Older literature and known human-made works may also function as trusted emotional anchors when authorship provenance matters.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- Visible emotion can be performed, selectively edited, misread, or detached from relevant context.
- The source offers audience interpretation rather than controlled evidence that AI growth causes demand for reality programming.
- Generated media can support human authorship, and human-produced media can be formulaic, deceptive, or emotionally empty.
- Provenance, embodiment, factual accuracy, and artistic value are distinct dimensions and should not be collapsed into one authenticity score.

## What Changed
- Created a qualified account of why bodily and reciprocal interaction can matter amid generated media.
- Separated emotional credibility from factual proof.
- Treated editing as a mediation layer rather than evidence that all observed response is false.
