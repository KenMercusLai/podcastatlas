# Tool AI Human Control

Tool AI human control is the alternative AI-development path [[SabinaNong]] describes in [[tech-20260720-0720-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260720-0720-mp-tech-pod-128]]. Rather than treating superintelligence as a necessary target, the [[FutureOfLifeInstitute|Future of Life Institute]] prefers AI systems that remain tools under human control.

The source uses the idea as a counter to the "responsible actor reaches superintelligence first" narrative. Tool AI does not mean weak AI or no automation; it means capability should be bounded by human direction, public consent, governance, and control strategies before companies pursue systems that may improve themselves or act with less human oversight.

## Key Claims
- Tool AI treats human direction and accountability as design requirements, not as afterthoughts.
- The concept challenges the assumption that racing to superintelligence is inevitable or socially authorized.
- The control standard becomes harder as systems gain long-horizon agency, tool use, and [[RecursiveSelfImprovement]] capacity.
- A tool-AI path fits [[HumanJudgmentUnderAI]] because the human remains responsible for goals, interpretation, and final authority.
- Tool AI needs institutional support from [[AIAlignmentGovernance]], [[AIGovernanceAndCompliance]], and regulation; interface design alone is not enough.
