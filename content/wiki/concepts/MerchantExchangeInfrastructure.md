## Definition

Merchant exchange infrastructure / 商人交换基础设施 is the episode's frame for merchants as the human system that lowers transport, storage, matching, and transaction costs so specialized producers and buyers can exchange more efficiently.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 396-5 treats commerce as an extension of specialization. Once different people become good at different production tasks, exchange becomes necessary; merchants then make that exchange cheaper, more continuous, and more geographically flexible than direct barter or occasional market trips.

The concept has two levels. At the ordinary market level, itinerant merchants move goods while sedentary merchants store, display, and match goods to buyers, earning less than the seller's self-sale cost would have been. At the larger historical level, merchant routes become channels for goods, knowledge, technologies, tastes, and regional contact.

## Key Claims

- Specialization creates the need for exchange, and exchange creates a durable merchant role.
- Merchants reduce transaction costs by handling transport, storage, matching, and sale timing.
- Merchant profit can be socially productive when it costs less than the alternatives faced by producers and buyers.
- Merchant mobility can make commercial actors early carriers of cultural, technical, and regional exchange.
- The concept does not make every merchant action beneficial; it captures the source's positive functional baseline.

## Evidence

- Specialization and exchange: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-396-5-shui-zai-biandi-shangren-youxiehua-butubukuai-liwmisetjnlvfzarfmzpqq4-wdsq|Hanji 396-5]] uses hunters, growers, food, clothing, and animal skins to explain how differentiated skills create exchange.
- Transport and storage: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-396-5-shui-zai-biandi-shangren-youxiehua-butubukuai-liwmisetjnlvfzarfmzpqq4-wdsq|Hanji 396-5]] distinguishes itinerant merchants earning transport value from sedentary merchants earning storage and selling value.
- Cost reduction: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-396-5-shui-zai-biandi-shangren-youxiehua-butubukuai-liwmisetjnlvfzarfmzpqq4-wdsq|Hanji 396-5]] argues that sellers use merchants only when the merchant's margin is below the cost of selling directly.
- Exchange carrier role: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-396-5-shui-zai-biandi-shangren-youxiehua-butubukuai-liwmisetjnlvfzarfmzpqq4-wdsq|Hanji 396-5]] describes merchants as pioneers of economic, cultural, and technical exchange.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source is a short popular-history defense of merchants. It does not cover monopoly, hoarding, crisis profiteering, fraud, coercive trade, or state-backed commercial privilege. Use [[LegitimateProfitBoundary|正当逐利边界]] and [[StateCommercialMonopolyExtraction|官营商业垄断式汲取]] when the moral or institutional boundary of profit is the main issue.
