## Definition

Sworn brotherhood as social infrastructure / 结拜作为社会基础设施 is the use of ritualized fictive kinship to increase trust, create obligations, coordinate complementary capabilities, and reduce danger among people who lack ordinary family or institutional bonds.

## Current Synthesis

The episode treats brotherhood in [[LuDingJi|《鹿鼎记》]] as a spectrum rather than a single virtue. At one end, [[WeiXiaobao|韦小宝]] and 杨溢之 cross factional boundaries through genuine mutual esteem. Elsewhere, private affection combines with official or material interest. At the most expedient end, an oath creates enough temporary confidence to escape an immediate threat without overriding either party's longer-term interests.

Ritual still matters even when motives are mixed. Oaths, shared address, and inherited stories of义气 raise the moral cost of defection and offer a public script for cooperation. The network then distributes ability: officials provide access, generals command forces, Jianghu figures supply protection, and opponents can become temporary partners. Its ethical limit appears when obligations collide. Brotherhood creates claims, but does not establish that all brothers are equal or that loyalty outranks every other attachment.

## Key Claims

- Ritualized fictive kinship can create usable trust beyond household, office, sect, or faction.
- Sincere affection, mutual advantage, and emergency calculation can coexist inside one sworn relationship.
- Brotherhood networks distribute capability by connecting people with different authority, knowledge, and force.
- Oaths matter partly by increasing reputational, moral, or supernatural costs, even when participants hedge their promises.
- Limited reciprocity can persist without unconditional loyalty when helping does not destroy a participant's core interest.
- Conflicting ties reveal a hierarchy of obligations rather than a universal brotherhood rule.

- [[lowwo0ixlp--ouxy87p42cult3k4]] presents 杨溢之 as a sincere brother chosen despite service to a political enemy, and treats Wei's later grief as evidence that the bond is not merely instrumental.

### Mixed-interest alliance

- [[lowwo0ixlp--ouxy87p42cult3k4]] describes ties with 索额图, 康亲王, generals, and other officials as combinations of personal familiarity, access, reciprocal aid, and political benefit.

### Emergency truce and bounded reciprocity

- [[lowwo0ixlp--ouxy87p42cult3k4]] interprets the bonds with 桑结 and 葛尔丹 as danger-management devices while noting later assistance when it does not violate Wei's main interests.

### Ranked obligation

- [[lowwo0ixlp--ouxy87p42cult3k4]] uses Wei's attack on 多隆 to show one brotherly claim being subordinated to relationships he treats as more important.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept comes from one literary discussion and should not be treated as a complete history of Chinese sworn brotherhood. Ritual can enable coercion, exclusion, criminal coordination, false confidence, or betrayal as well as mutual aid. The episode's inferences about sincerity, fear of divine punishment, and moral ranking depend on fictional actions and host interpretation rather than direct access to inner motive.

## What Changed

- Created the concept to separate genuine friendship, mixed-interest alliance, and emergency truce while explaining their shared coordinating function.
