# European Space Capability Dependence

## Definition
European space capability dependence is Europe's reliance on American intelligence, surveillance, satellite communications, missile warning, and launch capacity when domestic or allied substitutes lack equivalent scale, speed, integration, or readiness.

## Current Synthesis
The episode treats dependence as a stack rather than a single gap. [[OneWeb]] gives Europe a communications foundation, [[ICEYE]] supplies commercially procured radar surveillance, and France and Germany are planning warning satellites, but none yet reproduces the combined American system.

Industrial organization is part of the constraint. The scale difference between OneWeb and [[Starlink]], the years required for missile-warning deployment, and [[Ariane]]'s low launch cadence show that technical knowledge does not automatically become operational capacity. Faster commercial procurement, less fragmented work-sharing, and interoperable pooling with Japan, Canada, and South Korea offer a resilience strategy short of total self-sufficiency.

## Key Claims
- Space dependence spans intelligence, surveillance, communications, missile warning, and launch rather than one platform.
- Temporary access interruption can create strategic leverage even when service is later restored.
- Existing European systems close selected gaps but do not yet reproduce American scale and integration.
- Commercial procurement can accelerate narrow surveillance capability.
- Fragmented multinational procurement can raise cost and slow launch or satellite deployment.
- Interoperable allied pooling may provide resilience sooner than complete European self-sufficiency.

## Evidence
- Wartime leverage: [[songs-of-disenchantment-the-sorrow-of-nigeria-6275426999307c5f9d5c7073a5b16e0d]] describes temporary loss and later restoration of unique American missile intelligence for Ukraine.
- Communications gap: [[songs-of-disenchantment-the-sorrow-of-nigeria-6275426999307c5f9d5c7073a5b16e0d]] compares OneWeb's scale and terminals with Starlink.
- Selective commercial acceleration: [[songs-of-disenchantment-the-sorrow-of-nigeria-6275426999307c5f9d5c7073a5b16e0d]] uses ICEYE and Sweden as a faster surveillance-procurement case.
- Warning and launch gaps: [[songs-of-disenchantment-the-sorrow-of-nigeria-6275426999307c5f9d5c7073a5b16e0d]] places a European warning replacement in the early 2030s and contrasts Ariane and SpaceX launch cadence.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source supplies a strategic comparison rather than a complete capability audit. Satellite and launch counts do not by themselves measure usable capacity, sovereignty, wartime resilience, sensor quality, integration, or mission fit. Allied pooling also creates dependence, though it can diversify veto points and failure modes.

## What Changed
- Created a layered framework separating communications, surveillance, warning, and launch dependence.
- Identified commercial procurement, reduced fragmentation, and allied pooling as distinct mitigation paths.
