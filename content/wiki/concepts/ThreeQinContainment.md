# Three Qin Containment / 三秦压制

Three Qin containment / 三秦压制 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-143-xiangyu-shi-ruhe-feijin-xinji-nongsi-chuhuaiwang-mixin-lstfzfvbd2h9i39f7y5dxrbxxwv2|Hanji 143]] as [[XiangYu|项羽]] and [[FanZeng|范增]]'s arrangement for constraining [[LiuBang|刘邦]] after the [[HongmenYan|鸿门宴]] crisis. Instead of honoring the [[HuaiwangAgreement|怀王之约]] by giving Liu Bang direct rule over [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]], Xiang Yu makes Liu Bang Han king in [[BaShu|巴蜀]] and [[Hanzhong|汉中]], with the capital at [[Nanzheng|南郑]].

The containment layer is built from surrendered Qin commanders. [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] becomes Yong king west of Xianyang, [[SimaXin|司马欣]] becomes Sai king east of Xianyang toward the Yellow River, and [[DongYiQin|董翳]] becomes Di king in the Shang commandery area. Together they make the Three Qin, a northern and eastern blocking system between Liu Bang's assigned base and the old Qin heartland.

The device is politically double-edged. It rewards Qin elites who surrendered to Xiang Yu and gives them reason to defend their new positions, but it also advertises that Liu Bang's first-entry merit has been neutralized through geography and title wording. This turns an apparent compromise into a likely future conflict point inside the broader [[ZhouQinHybridOrder|周秦混合制]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-144-xiangyu-jituan-fumie-de-yuanyin-1-lr0ft8-jupxgvutn6c89mfs8r4wb|Hanji 144]] adds the contained side's response. [[XiaoHe|萧何]] accepts that Liu Bang cannot break the containment through immediate battle, but reframes the assignment as a temporary base: govern [[Hanzhong|汉中]] and [[BaShu|巴蜀]], gather people and resources, and wait for a later opening toward [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]]. The containment therefore creates both a lock and the conditions for [[StrategicRetreatBaseBuilding|退让式根据地经营]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-147-pimei-zhugeliang-longzhongdui-de-hanzhongdui-lqucctzhipgtcvtjlixvj5qbdeai|Hanji 147]] gives the opening that Xiao He's counsel had left implicit. In [[HanzhongDui|汉中对]], [[HanXin|韩信]] argues that the Three Qin are not a stable Qin-core shield because Zhang Han, Sima Xin, and Dong Yi are precisely the men Guanzhong people resent: they led Qin's sons through destructive campaigns, survived while common surrendered soldiers were killed at [[XinanMassacre|新安坑降]], and were then imposed as kings by Xiang Yu. The containment layer therefore becomes Liu Bang's first exploitable weakness.

## Key Claims
- The Three Qin convert surrendered enemies into border guardians against a former ally.
- The arrangement preserves a nominal claim that Liu Bang received part of the Guanzhong world, while operationally moving him into a constrained and difficult base.
- The strategy answers the immediate Liu Bang threat without resolving the legitimacy problem created by the Huaiwang promise.
- The Three Qin show how Xiang Yu's settlement mixes reward, punishment, route control, and hostage-like geography.
- Hanji 144 shows that containment can be reinterpreted by the constrained actor as a reason to preserve strength and build a base rather than attack immediately.
- Hanji 147 shows that containment can fail politically before it fails militarily when the blocking rulers lack local legitimacy.
