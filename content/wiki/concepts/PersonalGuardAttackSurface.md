## Definition
Personal guard attack surface is the security reversal in which people with routine close access to a leader's body, gate controls, or domestic command channels become either the most effective protection layer or the easiest route for resentment, fear, and conspiracy to reach the leader.

## Current Synthesis
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-693-panchu-liuxiu-de-pengchong-ruhe-yin-beipan-er-wang-lqler4fu9wqztsrgzsxhs1519nm]] extends the concept from armed guard units to household infrastructure. [[ZiMiEasternHan|Zi Mi]] is not important because he overpowers [[PengChongEasternHan|Peng Chong]] militarily; he succeeds because he already has inner access, can exploit a fasting interval, can block officials with plausible household explanations, and can force an order that opens gates.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-962-dongzhuo-yu-lvbu-daodi-weishenme-juelie-lgp3kxfqnhokibf6kpli6p4j8iti|Hanji 962]] defines the armed-proximity failure case through [[DongZhuo|Dong Zhuo]] and [[LyuBu|Lyu Bu]]: the trusted guard already has proximity, so psychological fracture can turn protection into attack. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1039-dongwu-diyi-zuipao-qie-kan-ta-ruhe-quanjiang-huaxin-lhre-ih73ynzfwujo0lnxyjdguyd|Hanji 1039]] adds a second branch from [[CaoCao|Cao Cao]]'s guard unit: [[XuTaLateHan|Xu Ta]]'s resentment under strict discipline creates assassination risk, while [[XuChu|Xu Chu]]'s vigilance detects the anomaly and restores the close-guard layer.

## Key Claims
- Close personal access is both shield and standing access channel to the protected body.
- Household servants, gate managers, and bodyguards can all become security risks because they operate inside ordinary trust routines.
- Fear, humiliation, private scandal, punitive discipline, or opportunity can turn proximity into lethal exposure.
- Outside conspirators may not need to penetrate security if they can convert someone already inside it.
- Guard discipline is necessary but can create resentment if the governed guards experience it as intolerable pressure.
- The same layer can recover protection when a trusted commander notices behavioral anomalies and acts quickly.

## Evidence
- Household access and gate control: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-693-panchu-liuxiu-de-pengchong-ruhe-yin-beipan-er-wang-lqler4fu9wqztsrgzsxhs1519nm]] shows Zi Mi binding Peng Chong during ritual fasting, shutting out officials, controlling servants, forcing a written order, and using gate access before the killing.
- Misdirected suspicion: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-693-panchu-liuxiu-de-pengchong-ruhe-yin-beipan-er-wang-lqler4fu9wqztsrgzsxhs1519nm]] says Peng Chong fears danger from inside but focuses on Hou Lanqing rather than the household slave with immediate access.
- Guard-as-access channel: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-962-dongzhuo-yu-lvbu-daodi-weishenme-juelie-lgp3kxfqnhokibf6kpli6p4j8iti|Hanji 962]] shows Dong Zhuo depending on Lyu Bu's strength and proximity, which later gives Lyu Bu the route to kill him.
- Psychological fracture: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-962-dongzhuo-yu-lvbu-daodi-weishenme-juelie-lgp3kxfqnhokibf6kpli6p4j8iti|Hanji 962]] ties Lyu Bu's reversal to Dong Zhuo's violent temper, the hand-halberd incident, affair exposure fear, and Wang Yun's persuasion.
- Insider resentment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1039-dongwu-diyi-zuipao-qie-kan-ta-ruhe-quanjiang-huaxin-lhre-ih73ynzfwujo0lnxyjdguyd|Hanji 1039]] says Xu Ta plots against Cao Cao because he resents the strictness of Xu Chu's guard discipline.
- Protective recovery: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1039-dongwu-diyi-zuipao-qie-kan-ta-ruhe-quanjiang-huaxin-lhre-ih73ynzfwujo0lnxyjdguyd|Hanji 1039]] shows Xu Chu returning unexpectedly, reading Xu Ta's group as suspicious, and killing the conspirators.
- Concept boundary: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-693-panchu-liuxiu-de-pengchong-ruhe-yin-beipan-er-wang-lqler4fu9wqztsrgzsxhs1519nm]], [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-962-dongzhuo-yu-lvbu-daodi-weishenme-juelie-lgp3kxfqnhokibf6kpli6p4j8iti|Hanji 962]], and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1039-dongwu-diyi-zuipao-qie-kan-ta-ruhe-quanjiang-huaxin-lhre-ih73ynzfwujo0lnxyjdguyd|Hanji 1039]] distinguish standing insider proximity from one-time access credentials.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept should not imply that all household servants or strict guard systems are inherently unsafe. Hanji 1039 shows discipline producing both resentment in Xu Ta and reliable protection through Xu Chu. Hanji 693 also shows the danger of misdirected suspicion: Peng Chong tries to manage one possible insider, Hou Lanqing, while missing the more immediate access channel. The more precise claim is that proximity must be governed with trust, monitoring, role separation, and command judgment because insider access magnifies both loyalty and grievance.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 693's Zi Mi and Peng Chong branch, broadening the concept from armed guard reversal to household servant, gate-control, and domestic-command failure.
- Preserved Hanji 962 as the Dong Zhuo-Lyu Bu bodyguard fracture branch.
- Preserved Hanji 1039 as the Xu Ta-Xu Chu guard-layer failure and recovery branch.
