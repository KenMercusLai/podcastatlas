# Dopamine Baseline Maintenance

## Definition
Dopamine baseline maintenance is the episode's practical frame for preserving background motivation capacity through foundational behaviors, cautious tool use, and avoidance of repeated sharp dopamine peaks.

## Current Synthesis
In [[leverage-dopamine-to-overcome-procrastination-optimize-effort-scim2602950288]], dopamine baseline is not something to maximize constantly. The episode treats sleep, non-sleep deep rest or yoga nidra, nutrition that supplies dopamine precursors, morning sunlight, and regular exercise as baseline-supporting inputs. It also discusses deliberate cold exposure, prescription stimulants, L-tyrosine, and mucuna pruriens as stronger levers that can create useful effects or crashes depending on timing, dose, medical context, and whether they are stacked with other rewards. The practical synthesis matches [[SustainableHealthOptimization]]: protect the background system first, then use sharper tools only with proportion, spacing, and safety boundaries.

## Key Claims
- Sleep is presented as a primary way to restore dopamine reserves and general motivation capacity.
- Non-sleep deep rest or yoga nidra is presented as a possible way to replenish dopamine availability.
- Nutrition matters because tyrosine-containing foods supply dopamine-synthesis precursors.
- Morning sunlight and regular exercise are treated as broad baseline-supporting behaviors.
- Deliberate cold exposure can raise dopamine for hours but carries stress, training-timing, and safety constraints.
- Prescription stimulants and wakefulness drugs are clinician-bound; supplements such as L-tyrosine and mucuna pruriens remain source-scoped and context-dependent.
- Avoiding repeated high peaks and dopamine stacking is part of maintaining baseline motivation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This is not a dosing guide or medical protocol. The source's claims about NSDR, supplements, stimulants, cold exposure, addiction recovery, training adaptation, and psychiatric conditions remain source-scoped and require appropriate professional judgment where risk is material.
