# Dopamine Baseline Maintenance

## Definition
Dopamine baseline maintenance is the episode's practical frame for preserving background motivation capacity through foundational behaviors, cautious tool use, and avoidance of repeated sharp dopamine peaks.

## Current Synthesis
Across the two overlapping episodes, dopamine baseline is not something to maximize constantly. [[scim6449668176-scim6449668176]] emphasizes recent reward history, intermittent use of optional stimulation, healthy social connection, nighttime-light restraint, and avoiding routine stacking around activities. [[leverage-dopamine-to-overcome-procrastination-optimize-effort-scim2602950288]] adds sleep, non-sleep deep rest or yoga nidra, nutrition, morning sunlight, exercise, and a sharper distinction between foundational support and potent cold, supplement, stimulant, or prescription levers. The practical synthesis matches [[SustainableHealthOptimization]]: protect the background system first, then use sharper tools only with proportion, spacing, and safety boundaries.

## Key Claims
- Sleep and a stable light-dark rhythm are presented as primary supports for general motivation capacity.
- Non-sleep deep rest or yoga nidra is presented as a possible way to replenish dopamine availability.
- Nutrition matters because tyrosine-containing foods supply dopamine-synthesis precursors.
- Morning sunlight and regular exercise are treated as broad baseline-supporting behaviors.
- Deliberate cold exposure can raise dopamine for hours but carries stress, training-timing, and safety constraints.
- Prescription stimulants and wakefulness drugs are clinician-bound; supplements such as L-tyrosine and mucuna pruriens remain source-scoped and context-dependent.
- Avoiding repeated high peaks and intermittently omitting optional music, phones, caffeine, or social reinforcement are presented as ways to keep an activity from requiring a fixed stimulation stack.

## Evidence
- Foundational inputs: [[leverage-dopamine-to-overcome-procrastination-optimize-effort-scim2602950288]] links sleep, NSDR or yoga nidra, nutrition, morning sunlight, and exercise to baseline dopamine support.
- Cold-exposure lever: [[leverage-dopamine-to-overcome-procrastination-optimize-effort-scim2602950288]] presents deliberate cold exposure as a strong behavioral dopamine tool with safety and timing boundaries.
- Drug and supplement boundary: [[leverage-dopamine-to-overcome-procrastination-optimize-effort-scim2602950288]] discusses prescription stimulants, L-tyrosine, and mucuna pruriens while emphasizing caution and context.
- Peak avoidance: [[leverage-dopamine-to-overcome-procrastination-optimize-effort-scim2602950288]] says repeated sharp peaks and stacked stimuli can deepen troughs or reduce later motivation.
- Earlier anti-stacking frame: [[scim6449668176-scim6449668176]] connects recent reward history, intermittent reinforcement, phone layering, effort reward, nighttime light, and healthy social connection to later motivation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This is not a dosing guide or medical protocol. The two sources overlap and do not independently establish the proposed baseline mechanisms. Claims about dopamine depletion, NSDR, nighttime light, supplements, stimulants, cold exposure, addiction recovery, training adaptation, and psychiatric conditions remain source-scoped and require appropriate professional judgment where risk is material.
