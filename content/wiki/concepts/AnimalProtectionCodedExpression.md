# Animal Protection Coded Expression / 动保暗语表达

## Definition
[[AnimalProtectionCodedExpression]] is the source's pattern in which animal-protection supporters use euphemisms, homophones, substitute images, and softened slogans when direct references to a case or anti-cruelty demand are filtered or discouraged.

## Current Synthesis
In the 旺旺 case, supporters reportedly moved from direct references to [[WangwangDog|旺旺]] toward phrases such as joining a brand, daily check-ins, written variants, snack names, cloud images, and AI dog animations. Offline, organizers softened projection language from direct anti-abuse demands to generic care slogans and cartoon imagery, yet still saw displays removed. The concept extends [[DomesticServiceCensorship]] into a low-politics animal-protection case where public memory becomes dependent on oblique references.

## Key Claims
- Coded expression can preserve public memory when direct keywords become hard to post.
- Substitute images and homophones can turn a case name into a distributed recognition system among supporters.
- Softened wording does not guarantee safety or durability when the underlying event has become sensitive.
- Offline venues can become a censorship surface when commercial risk, official notice, and public-order concern converge.
- The pattern shows censorship operating through ordinary social-media review, advertising approval, mall management, and police contact rather than one visible ban.

## Evidence
- Online coded language: [[liulangquan-wangwang-zhisi-he-yichang-kuajing-dongbao-shengyuan]] reports substitute phrases, rewritten forms of 旺旺, snack references, cloud imagery, and AI dog animations.
- Offline softening and removal: [[liulangquan-wangwang-zhisi-he-yichang-kuajing-dongbao-shengyuan]] reports [[JiangHongAnimalVolunteer|江宏]]'s projection attempt, venue restrictions, altered slogans, takedown, and police contact.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode does not prove a single centralized censorship order for every platform or venue decision. It documents a pattern of filtering and discouragement as reported by participants and the transcript.
