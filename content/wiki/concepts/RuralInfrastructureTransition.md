# Rural Infrastructure Transition / 乡村基础设施转折

Rural infrastructure transition is the before-and-after threshold described in [[144-nvxia-duan-sihe-he-tade-jiaxiang-mapingguan-women-ceng-yiwei-you-dian-you-lu-zhishi-meng-linmjqt-n-sa5sarpqgdxgtoeejt]] through [[Mapingguan|马坪关]]'s electricity and road access. The source says the village received electricity in 2009 and a road in 2015, turning what residents once imagined as a dream into normal public infrastructure.

The episode keeps the transition emotionally mixed. No-electricity life had firepit time, night visits, daylight discipline, and village intimacy; electricity and roads brought access, safety, reception, schooling, travel, phones, and the ability to connect beyond the village.

## Key Claims
- Infrastructure is not only physical access; it changes time, visiting, education, guest reception, commercial possibilities, and children's imagination of the village.
- Nostalgia for pre-infrastructure rhythm does not equal rejection of modernization.
- [[LocalGovernanceAsCare]] can include mundane road repair, bridge restoration, school support, and electricity access rather than only crisis relief.
- The source's strongest formulation is intergenerational: future children should know that "electricity and roads" were once outside ordinary expectation.
