# Root-Base Priority / 根本根据地优先

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-964-bie-jiaoao-bie-jiaoao-bie-jiaoao-lo2-edzffu0v9ixoabc06rx-oqoq|Hanji 964]] adds the origin point before Yanzhou becomes Cao Cao's root base in later episodes. [[LiuDaiLateHan|刘岱]] dies because the province cannot manage the [[QingzhouYellowTurbans|青州黄巾军]] crisis, after which [[BaoXin|鲍信]], [[WanQianLateHan|万潜]], and local officials invite [[CaoCao|曹操]] to take over. The base therefore begins as emergency succession and defense, not as a settled possession.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-973-changpingguan-zhizhan-shengsi-zhidi-zhanhou-jing-chonggui-yuhao-lgazc9dewfayb8qnkncfkfa12yet|Hanji 973]] adds the immediate negative case before the later adviser explanation. [[CaoCao|曹操]] can threaten [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] and defeat [[LiuBei|刘备]] in [[Xuzhou|徐州]], but when [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] and [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] invite [[LyuBu|吕布]] into [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]], the offensive must stop because the base itself is collapsing.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-974-caocao-xiang-ta-tuo-qi-xian-zi-weihe-zhuanshen-ai-daozi-lhfabab7xv2vfsgnhr9ozwqhmhd|Hanji 974]] adds the pre-military failure mode. A root base can begin to collapse before enemy movement becomes visible if the local elite coalition no longer believes it is safe under the ruler. In [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]], [[BianRangLateHan|边让]]'s killing, [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]]'s fear, and [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]]'s persuasion turn local security anxiety into the betrayal that later forces base-first recovery.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-975-yanzhou-zhiluan-xunyu-ruhe-bang-caocao-shou-dabenying-ln0hh4jrokkwtqsmxmqhdjzcosdr|Hanji 975]] adds the crisis-detection layer before the base-first principle is verbalized. [[CaoCao|曹操]] is away attacking [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] in [[Xuzhou|徐州]] when [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] and [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] invite [[LyuBu|吕布]] into [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]]. [[XunYu|荀彧]] reads the danger early and recalls [[XiahouDun|夏侯惇]] to [[JuanchengLateHan|鄄城]], showing that preserving the base starts with detecting the rear's political shift.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-978-gushen-quantui-shuwan-dijun-ta-zenme-zuodao-de-lhgd8vsvuaf5oi9aefh0wxabmzwv|Hanji 978]] adds the morale and alignment layer before the base-first principle is later verbalized. [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s remaining base holds because [[XunYu|荀彧]] keeps [[JuanchengLateHan|鄄城]] from panicking before [[GuoGongLateHan|郭贡]], and [[ChengYu|程昱]] helps [[JinYunLateHan|靳允]] hold [[FanCountyLateHan|范县]] despite [[LyuBu|吕布]]'s family-hostage leverage.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-979-zaoyu-tongliao-beici-ta-ruhe-wei-caocao-liwan-kuanglan-luu58a2ww11g-radh-5g4l7fcis|Hanji 979]] adds the concrete preservation stage before [[XunYu|荀彧]] later states the base-first principle. [[ChengYu|程昱]] blocks [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] at [[CangtingFordLateHan|仓亭津]], [[ZaoZhi|枣祗]] holds [[DongALateHan|东阿]], and [[CaoCao|曹操]] still has a place to return to after much of [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] turns against him.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-986-tianyu-qiwang-bixian-lingqi-kuang-lvlvu057-tnz3hal6oodhydvzvbd|Hanji 986]] supplies the trigger for the root-base question. After [[CaoCao|曹操]] defeats [[LyuBu|吕布]] in [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]], [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] dies and [[LiuBei|刘备]] takes [[Xuzhou|徐州]], making an outward attack look timely. The episode stops at the moment a counselor is about to object, so it prepares rather than fully states the principle.

Root-base priority / 根本根据地优先 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-987-dingtao-zhizhan-caocao-he-lvbu-dianfeng-duijue-lofq0pq8n9rrfvfttunahisy0ave|Hanji 987]] as [[XunYu|荀彧]]'s answer to [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s temptation to take [[Xuzhou|徐州]]. The principle is not anti-expansion. It says expansion becomes dangerous when the actor's own base is still exposed, under-harvested, or politically unsettled.

The source case is [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]]. Xun Yu argues that Cao Cao's root base is the place where his people trust him, where grain can be gathered, and where later attack or retreat remains possible. [[LyuBu|吕布]] and [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] make that base unsafe, so chasing Xuzhou after [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]]'s death would trade a real foundation for an attractive but uncertain gain.

The concept differs from [[StrategicRetreatBaseBuilding|退让式根据地经营]]. Strategic retreat base-building concerns turning an imposed fallback position into future power. Root-base priority concerns refusing an optional outward move until the existing foundation is secure. It also extends [[WarCostTempoDiscipline|战争成本与速决纪律]] upstream: the cost problem is decided before the campaign begins, through harvest, rear security, local acceptance, and whether the target can feed the army.

## Key Claims
- Hanji 964 adds the origin point: a later root base may begin as crisis succession, local invitation, and costly defense rather than clean appointment.
- Hanji 973 adds the warning event: a successful-looking external attack becomes untenable as soon as the home province is mostly lost.
- Hanji 974 adds that root-base risk can begin as local elite security panic before it becomes a visible military breach.
- Hanji 975 adds that base priority begins before formal strategy: if the rear is leaking signs of betrayal, detection and emergency command recall are already base-preservation work.
- Hanji 978 adds that a root base can be preserved through morale control and local alignment choices before the later route, boat, and city-defense mechanics become visible.
- Hanji 979 adds that a root base may be preserved before grand strategy is verbalized, through crossings, boats, city defense, officials, and people organization.
- A tempting expansion can be strategically wrong if it exposes the actor's base to a nearby unresolved enemy.
- Hanji 986 shows the tempting-expansion setup before Hanji 987 supplies Xun Yu's full base-first argument.
- A base is not only territory; it is harvest, manpower,民心, route safety, and a credible fallback.
- Historical precedent is useful when it identifies the function of a base, not when it becomes a slogan for passivity.
- Refusing a campaign can be an active strategy when it preserves the conditions for a later decisive campaign.
- The concept marks a boundary on opportunism: a target's weakness does not matter enough if one's own rear cannot hold.
