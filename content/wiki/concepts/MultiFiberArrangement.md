# Multi-Fiber Arrangement

The Multi-Fiber Arrangement appears in [[getting-entrepreneurial-in-korea-summer-school]] as the 1974 quota regime that limited how much clothing and textiles each country could sell to the [[UnitedStates]]. The source says Korean firms had already hit their export limits, which made producing through [[Bangladesh]] attractive.

In this source, the arrangement is an unintended [[TextileKnowledgeTransfer]] mechanism. A trade restriction meant to protect U.S. textile jobs helped push garment production and know-how from [[SouthKorea|South Korea]] into Bangladesh through [[Daewoo]], [[AbdulMajidChowdhury]], and [[DeshGarments]].
