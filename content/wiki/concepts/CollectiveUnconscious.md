# Collective Unconscious / 集体无意识

[[CollectiveUnconscious|Collective unconscious / 集体无意识]] is the [[CarlJung|Jung]] concept that [[29-qianmian-yingxiong-edipusi-zhengzai-xiaban-ji-ditie-590112891]] uses to explain why similar mythic figures and plots might appear across cultures. The episode presents it as one possible basis for [[JosephCampbell|Campbell]]'s [[Monomyth|单一神话]]: human beings may share deep symbolic structures that surface in dreams, myths, and stories.

The source keeps this idea under a confidence boundary. Collective unconscious can help explain why mythic images feel powerful, but it does not remove the need for [[StoryMotifTransmission]], historical context, or local textual evidence.

## Key Claims
- The concept makes cross-cultural resemblance psychologically legible.
- It remains a heuristic in the episode, not a proof that all myths have one source.
- Its strongest use is to hold recurring symbols and emotional force together while keeping evidence limits visible.
