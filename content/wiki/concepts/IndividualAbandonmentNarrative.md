# Individual Abandonment Narrative / 个体被抛弃叙事

## Definition
Individual abandonment narrative / 个体被抛弃叙事 is a film-critical frame in which a person's central injury comes from being left behind, denied recognition, or made invisible by family, comrades, institutions, government, or historical time.

## Current Synthesis
The source uses this frame to connect [[FengXiaogang|冯小刚]]'s serious works across different genres. The abandoned individual is not always simply a pure victim, and the abandoning side is not always portrayed as sadistic. The drama often comes from the gap between unavoidable historical or institutional action and the person's need for a name, explanation, or recognition.

In this reading, Feng's mature serious films become public-emotion works because they turn private grievance into social recognition. 《集结号》 seeks a proper name for sacrifice, 《唐山大地震》 turns family choice into lifelong trauma, 《1942》 shows famine victims disappearing inside state priorities, 《我不是潘金莲》 turns legal and bureaucratic failure into a demand for restored truth, and 《芳华》 frames ordinary people as discarded by their collective and era.

## Key Claims
- Abandonment can come from intimate relations, military command, bureaucracy, government, class group, or historical change.
- The narrative's emotional center is often recognition: a name, verdict, explanation, or restored relation matters as much as material compensation.
- The frame allows institutional criticism without necessarily rejecting institutions altogether.
- It becomes strongest when the work preserves both injury and the difficulty of repair.
- It weakens when historical suffering is made too redemptive, useful, or easily reconciled.

- Cross-film pattern: [[zai-wuhui-zhuizong-mianqian-zhuatewu-suan-shenme-jiantan-fengxiaogang-de-yansu-yimian-gkwriw4oefk6bnp1eqsshjxf]] explicitly groups Feng Xiaogang's serious works through examples of people abandoned by comrades, family, government, spouse, bureaucracy, and era.
- Recognition demand: [[zai-wuhui-zhuizong-mianqian-zhuatewu-suan-shenme-jiantan-fengxiaogang-de-yansu-yimian-gkwriw4oefk6bnp1eqsshjxf]] connects the pattern to "讨一个说法" and to fear of exclusion or non-recognition.
- Institutional ambiguity: [[zai-wuhui-zhuizong-mianqian-zhuatewu-suan-shenme-jiantan-fengxiaogang-de-yansu-yimian-gkwriw4oefk6bnp1eqsshjxf]] reads 《集结号》 as admitting institutional error while still pursuing official recognition and emotional repair.
- Repair limit: [[zai-wuhui-zhuizong-mianqian-zhuatewu-suan-shenme-jiantan-fengxiaogang-de-yansu-yimian-gkwriw4oefk6bnp1eqsshjxf]] praises 《芳华》 for holding nostalgia and cruelty together while criticizing [[ZhuaTewu|《抓特务》]] for resolving inherited injury too cleanly.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept is derived from one critical episode and one filmmaker's body of work as interpreted by that episode. It should not be generalized to all Chinese serious cinema without additional sources.
