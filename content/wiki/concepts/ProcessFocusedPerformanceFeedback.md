# Process-Focused Performance Feedback

## Definition
Process-focused performance feedback describes controllable actions such as attention, effort, persistence, strategy, help-seeking, correction, and returning after error instead of turning one result into a fixed identity label.

## Current Synthesis
[[how-to-enhance-performance-learning-by-applying-a-growth-mindset-scim1082295503]] presents a feedback contrast grounded in research associated with [[CarolDweck]] and [[ClaudiaMueller]]. Calling someone smart or talented can make later difficulty threaten the praised identity, encouraging image protection, easier task selection, or concealment. Describing what the learner did makes success and failure easier to inspect without equating either with the whole person.

The mature form is not indiscriminate effort praise. Feedback should identify which behaviors contributed to a result, distinguish persistence from ineffective repetition, and point toward a viable next attempt. Error review then becomes causal analysis: what was attended to, attempted, misunderstood, omitted, or insufficiently supported?

In [[defaultmp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b318e4ceab011646c1e8d6daa46342bf-87870981-defaultmp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b318e4ceab011646c1e8d6daa46342bf-87870981]], elite-sport coaching redirects artistic swimmers from medals as the sole success measure toward daily progress, innovation, bodily adaptation, and the purpose behind difficult work. The case broadens process feedback from language after success or error into an ongoing attention system: outcomes remain visible, but they no longer consume the whole meaning of practice.

This practice operationalizes [[GrowthMindset]] and complements [[MentorMindsetFeedback]]. Process language supplies actionable detail, while demanding support preserves standards, confidence, and resources. Together they make feedback about learning rather than flattery, identity protection, or denial of competitive results.

## Key Claims
- Identity labels can make later errors feel like evidence that the praised or criticized self is false or defective.
- Action verbs keep feedback closer to behaviors that can be repeated, changed, combined with a new strategy, or supported.
- Useful error review acknowledges the result and redirects attention toward causes and next actions.
- Process feedback should include strategy, attention, correction, and help-seeking rather than praise effort regardless of effectiveness.
- Feedback can shape later challenge choice, honesty about results, persistence, and learning orientation.
- The practice can be used in teaching, parenting, coaching, self-talk, and performance review, but credibility depends on truthful standards and real opportunity to improve.
- Outcome goals can orient effort while daily feedback remains centered on improvement, adaptation, strategy, and shared learning.

## Evidence
- Praise conditions - [[how-to-enhance-performance-learning-by-applying-a-growth-mindset-scim1082295503]] contrasts intelligence labels with feedback about trying, persistence, and applied effort.
- Challenge and performance - [[how-to-enhance-performance-learning-by-applying-a-growth-mindset-scim1082295503]] reports easier task selection after intelligence praise and harder task selection plus stronger later performance after effort praise.
- Honest reporting - [[how-to-enhance-performance-learning-by-applying-a-growth-mindset-scim1082295503]] reports more favorable score misrepresentation after intelligence praise and more faithful reporting after effort praise.
- Error analysis - [[how-to-enhance-performance-learning-by-applying-a-growth-mindset-scim1082295503]] recommends acknowledging error and examining the actions that produced it rather than suppressing emotion or attaching a negative label.
- Self-feedback - [[how-to-enhance-performance-learning-by-applying-a-growth-mindset-scim1082295503]] applies the same shift from nouns and labels to verbs in internal narratives about memory, music, and other abilities.
- Elite coaching application - [[defaultmp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b318e4ceab011646c1e8d6daa46342bf-87870981-defaultmp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b318e4ceab011646c1e8d6daa46342bf-87870981]] records Fuentes moving team attention away from medals alone and toward daily progress, innovation, adaptation, and explanation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Effort is not automatically effective, and praising visible struggle can become hollow when a learner needs a different strategy, prerequisite instruction, recovery, accommodation, material support, or a safer environment. Process feedback should not deny aptitude differences, current performance gaps, chronic stress, disability, discrimination, real task limits, competition outcomes, or medical warning signs. The first episode's child-study and brain-response findings remain source-scoped because the supplied summary omits complete methods and effect sizes; Fuentes's team results do not establish the causal effect of process focus.
