## Definition

Technological change–political continuity is the pattern in which new capacities transform bodies, movement, production, or warfare while inherited struggles over rule, territory, wealth, status, and coercion persist.

## Current Synthesis

In [[ATrueStory|《真实的故事》]], travel reaches the Moon, giant creatures and engineered surfaces support war in space, male bodies reproduce, and organs can be replaced. Yet Moon and Sun remain kingdoms, fight over a colony, use siege and deprivation, exchange hostages, demand payment, and preserve unequal status.

The point is not that political improvement is impossible. It is that capability does not specify institutions or purposes. A vehicle can reach a new world without deciding how its inhabitants should live together, and a body can escape one natural constraint while entering a redesigned hierarchy.

## Key Claims

- Expanded capability does not automatically produce political progress.
- New environments can carry old conflicts over territory, resources, and status.
- Technical systems amplify the scale and reach of political action without selecting just ends.
- Removing one biological constraint does not dissolve monarchy, military organization, or hierarchy.
- Political improvement requires institutional and normative change in addition to technical possibility.

## Evidence

- Interplanetary colonial war: [[371-liangqian-nianqian-jiu-you-ren-xiangxiang-chu-saiboge-nanren-duli-shengyu-xingji-zhanzheng-le-ma-zhenshi-de-gushi-lije6fkmg3ilr65bi-7zovbns-r1]] connects extraordinary travel and engineering to a conventional struggle over settlement.
- Coercive continuity: [[371-liangqian-nianqian-jiu-you-ren-xiangxiang-chu-saiboge-nanren-duli-shengyu-xingji-zhanzheng-le-ma-zhenshi-de-gushi-lije6fkmg3ilr65bi-7zovbns-r1]] records siege, blocked sunlight, tribute, hostages, and alliance terms.
- Bodily change without social equality: [[371-liangqian-nianqian-jiu-you-ren-xiangxiang-chu-saiboge-nanren-duli-shengyu-xingji-zhanzheng-le-ma-zhenshi-de-gushi-lije6fkmg3ilr65bi-7zovbns-r1]] preserves marriage roles, kings, armies, and rich–poor difference in a male-reproducing lunar society.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

One satirical text does not establish a universal law of technological development or prove that institutions never improve. The Moon and Sun kingdoms are literary constructions shaped by ancient political vocabulary; modern spaceflight, reproductive medicine, and prosthetics require separate empirical analysis.

## What Changed

- Created the concept from the contrast between Lucian's radical physical invention and conventional political order.
