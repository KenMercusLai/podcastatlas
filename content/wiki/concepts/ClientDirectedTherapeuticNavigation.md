# Client-Directed Therapeutic Navigation / 来访者主导的治疗导航

## Definition
Client-directed therapeutic navigation is the episode's metaphor that the client holds the steering wheel while the therapist occupies the passenger seat as a navigator who can clarify routes, risks, patterns, strengths, and options without taking ownership of the person's life.

## Current Synthesis
The model connects professional help to autonomy. A therapist can listen, reflect, identify overlooked possibilities, teach skills, notice danger, and support movement toward a chosen goal, but cannot decide the destination or drive the client's life for them. This distinguishes therapy from both passive companionship and directive rescue.

The same boundary applies to ordinary support. When someone discloses pain without requesting advice, immediate solutions or reassurance can override what they actually need. Listening, emotional presence, and asking what kind of help is wanted preserve agency while leaving room for qualified escalation when safety or severe impairment is present.

## Key Claims
- Therapeutic expertise should expand informed choice rather than replace client agency.
- Listening and emotional presence can be active forms of help even when no immediate solution exists.
- Advice is more respectful when it follows consent, context, and the person's stated goal.
- The therapist remains responsible for competence, boundaries, referral, and risk recognition despite not controlling the client's decisions.
- Client autonomy does not require clinicians or supporters to ignore imminent danger or pretend every option is equally safe.

- Navigation metaphor - [[ep67-bie-kangju-xinli-zixun-keneng-bing-bushi-ni-xiang-de-nayang-wkglefzxkovwczzbbbvx2la4e4c899]] compares the therapist to a passenger-seat navigator and the client to the driver.
- Listening boundary - [[ep67-bie-kangju-xinli-zixun-keneng-bing-bushi-ni-xiang-de-nayang-wkglefzxkovwczzbbbvx2la4e4c899]] advises against unsolicited solutions and empty reassurance when another person is disclosing distress.
- Professional responsibility - [[ep67-bie-kangju-xinli-zixun-keneng-bing-bushi-ni-xiang-de-nayang-wkglefzxkovwczzbbbvx2la4e4c899]] pairs autonomy with continuing education, countertransference awareness, and recognition of suicide or other danger signals.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The metaphor is not a complete account of informed consent, capacity, involuntary treatment, mandated reporting, safeguarding, emergency intervention, or legal duties. The balance between choice and protection depends on risk, jurisdiction, setting, age, and professional role. The source is public education rather than a psychotherapy ethics standard.
