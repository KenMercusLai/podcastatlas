# Negative Job Amenities

Negative job amenities are the undesirable features of work in [[the-real-horror-of-alien-and-how-it-explains-why-were-not-paid-enough]]. [[ArinDube|Arin Dube]] uses the Nostromo crew's dangerous, isolated, long-haul job in [[AlienFilm|Alien]] as the episode's clearest bad-job example.

The concept matters because bad jobs are not only low-wage jobs. Danger, distance from home, physical strain, schedule rigidity, weak exit options, and hidden obligations can all reduce job quality. In a competitive market, those burdens should connect to [[CompensatingDifferentials]]; under [[LaborMonopsony]], workers may bear them without enough pay or protection.
