# Self-Concordant Goal Selection

## Definition
Self-concordant goal selection is the process of choosing aims that fit a person's values, interests, and deeper inclinations rather than mainly expressing external pressure, status competition, or an inherited image of who that person should be.

## Current Synthesis
[[KenSheldon]] moves the goal problem upstream. Planning, persistence, and skill can help a person reach a destination without establishing that the destination will improve life. [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-db5f9d45fb650a533486e6eb38780ebf-49695184-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-db5f9d45fb650a533486e6eb38780ebf-49695184|Wellness 2.0: Who Do You Want To Be?]] therefore treats conscious desire as evidence to examine rather than a final answer, because family expectation, consumer culture, prestige, competition, and prior commitments can all shape what feels desirable.

The episode's discovery method resembles a creative cycle: formulate a real question, allow incubation, notice possible answers near the edge of awareness, and verify them through action. This is neither pure introspection nor an instruction to obey intuition. A direction becomes more credible when experiments produce durable engagement, value fit, and improved well-being while surviving practical constraints and reconsideration.

## Key Claims
- Goal quality cannot be inferred from achievement difficulty, prestige, income, or social approval alone.
- Conscious goals may reflect external expectations even when they feel personally chosen.
- Interests, values, affective signals, and recurring inclinations provide hypotheses about fit, not infallible commands.
- Questioning, incubation, insight, and behavioral verification form an iterative discovery loop.
- Self-concordant goals can strengthen motivation without making every task enjoyable.
- Discomfort after commitment can sometimes be useful evidence that deliberation should reopen.
- Material and structural constraints determine which fitting directions are currently feasible.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The supplied source does not establish a reliable test that can separate an authentic inclination from fear, habit, fantasy, social influence, or retrospective storytelling. Amy is a composite example, and the law-student and lawyer findings are reported without full methods. A better-fitting goal can remain financially inaccessible or conflict with caregiving, health, safety, immigration status, debt, discrimination, or labor-market conditions. The concept supports experimentation and revision, not the idea that everyone has one hidden calling or that leaving a difficult career is always the right response.

## What Changed
- Established goal selection as an upstream problem distinct from goal-execution technique.
- Added the question-incubation-insight-verification loop for testing possible directions.
- Added material feasibility and post-commitment reconsideration as explicit boundaries.
