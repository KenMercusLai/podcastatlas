# AI Coworkers

## Definition
AI coworkers are role-based agents that participate in work as persistent collaborators with goals, memory, tools, permissions, and communication surfaces, rather than as one-off chat boxes.

## Current Synthesis
The concept now spans three levels: enterprise deployment, AI-native workspace design, and coding-agent work. The enterprise branch says useful coworkers need governance, liability boundaries, workflow redesign, and employee buy-in. The Moxt branch says a coworker can be trained like a role: it receives goals, skills, memory, workspace access, and feedback from the human. The agent-coding branch adds that coworkers may also appear as members of Slack-like rooms or command centers where the human gives instructions, reviews diffs, and steers multiple agents.

The stable synthesis is that coworker framing is not mainly about replacing a worker cheaply. It changes what the human does: set direction, provide taste, grant or deny permission, review output, and decide what is meaningful enough to keep.

## Key Claims
- AI coworkers are easier to manage when they have bounded roles, goals, memories, and tools instead of behaving as one generic digital clone.
- The coworker pattern depends on onboarding and feedback; the human trains the agent by correcting what was wrong, missing, risky, or misaligned.
- Enterprise AI coworkers require adoption design, including workflow change, governance, compliance, liability, and employee trust.
- Coding-agent coworkers move the human role toward instruction, review, feedback, and acceptance rather than continuous line-by-line implementation.
- Chat and IM surfaces make AI coworkers feel socially reachable, but real work still needs files, tools, permissions, state visibility, and verification.
- The coworker metaphor increases the need for privacy, attribution, permission boundaries, and clarity about which workspace values the agent should serve.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
AI coworkers can easily become vague anthropomorphic marketing if the product lacks durable memory, tools, permission boundaries, and observable work state. Enterprise use also has a harder risk profile than personal use because a coworker may expose confidential data, blur accountability, or disrupt real teams. The Moxt source explicitly resists low-cost-replacement framing, so the wiki should keep amplification and human judgment separate from digital-labor rhetoric.

## What Changed
- Agent-coding workflows now count as an AI coworker branch, not only enterprise assistants or Moxt-style workspace agents.
- IM and command-center surfaces are now treated as coworker interaction surfaces, with verification and work-state visibility as limits.
- The synthesis now distinguishes coworker rhetoric from the actual operational requirements that make a coworker useful.
