# 汉代拐卖惩治 / Han Trafficking Law Deterrence

## Definition
Han trafficking law deterrence is the episode's interpretation that early Han legal punishment for abducting, selling, and buying people made trafficking a highly visible offense with severe consequences across the transaction chain.

## Current Synthesis
The source uses [[ErNianLuLing|《二年律令》]] from [[ZhangjiashanHanmuZhujian|张家山汉墓竹简]] to show a strong ancient boundary against trafficking. The important analytical move is chain liability: abduction, resale, knowing purchase, and even claimed-ignorant purchase are all described as punishable, so the law is presented as attacking both supply and demand.

## Key Claims
- The episode says early Han law named trafficking conduct "虐人."
- Abductors could be punished whether or not the person had already been sold.
- Knowing buyers were treated as participating in the same wrong rather than as innocent recipients.
- Buyers claiming ignorance still faced punishment, which narrowed the market's excuse space.
- The severe penalty descriptions are used as moral deterrence, not as an endorsement of modern cruelty.

## Evidence
- Legal source: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-527-2-choujinbapi-gudai-de-renfanzi-zhen-huogai-ljfol4zmcqwewv5h4z4rby5vfw5p]] identifies [[ErNianLuLing|《二年律令》]] as the Han legal material for the trafficking discussion.
- Abductor punishment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-527-2-choujinbapi-gudai-de-renfanzi-zhen-huogai-ljfol4zmcqwewv5h4z4rby5vfw5p]] says abductors faced severe punishment even before completed sale.
- Buyer punishment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-527-2-choujinbapi-gudai-de-renfanzi-zhen-huogai-ljfol4zmcqwewv5h4z4rby5vfw5p]] states that knowing buyers and claimed-ignorant buyers were both punishable, though not necessarily in the same way.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not quote the statutory text in full or distinguish manuscript readings, legal categories, or penalty equivalents in modern terms. Its deterrence claim should remain tied to the episode until more legal-history sources are ingested.
