# Ulysses Contract

## Definition
A Ulysses contract is a commitment device through which a present self changes future options, incentives, or friction before a predictable temptation or low-control state arrives.

## Current Synthesis
The source treats self-control as a coordination problem among temporally different versions of the same person. A present intention is often weakest at the moment of temptation, so precommitment can remove access to a phone, alcohol, or cash; add social or financial consequences; or place desired tools such as running shoes where action becomes easier. The useful principle is environmental design, not proof that every commitment device is safe, fair, or effective.

[[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835|You, But Better]] supplies the classical Ulysses-and-the-sirens metaphor and extends the framework through cash stakes, deadlines, automated saving, and future contribution commitments. These examples place commitment devices on a spectrum from friction and default design to hard restriction; stronger constraints require stronger safeguards.

## Key Claims
- Present intentions do not guarantee future behavior under craving, fatigue, distraction, or changed state.
- Restricting access before temptation can outperform relying on willpower at the point of choice.
- Social and financial stakes can make commitments more durable.
- Lowering friction for a desired action is the constructive counterpart to blocking an undesired action.
- Commitment devices work best when the target, constraint, and foreseeable side effects are explicit.

## Evidence
- Access restriction - [[science-tools-of-learning-memory-dr-david-eagleman-scim5564931565]] gives locked phones, frozen cash, removed alcohol, and limited cash as examples.
- Social stakes - [[science-tools-of-learning-memory-dr-david-eagleman-scim5564931565]] uses meeting someone at the gym and putting money or reputation on the line.
- Positive friction design - [[science-tools-of-learning-memory-dr-david-eagleman-scim5564931565]] describes placing running shoes near the door before sleep.
- Classical model and financial stakes - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835]] uses Ulysses, cash forfeiture, and a smoking-cessation study to illustrate chosen constraints before temptation.
- Softer precommitment - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835]] connects deadlines, automated transfers, and future raise allocations to future-self protection.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The sources offer illustrative examples and episode-level study summaries rather than a universal protocol. The reported smoking-cessation difference lacks full methods in the supplied note. Restricting money, communication, medication, or movement can create safety, coercion, or access problems; recovery and clinical contexts require qualified support rather than improvised deprivation. A commitment chosen now may also become misaligned with later information or legitimate need.

## What Changed

- Added explicit evidence limits and stronger reversibility, safety, and coercion boundaries.
