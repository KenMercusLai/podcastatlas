# Administrative Archive Capture / 行政档案接管

Administrative archive capture / 行政档案接管 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-139-ta-zaici-yingxiang-hanchao-lp1zujuq0o3ohxc79xqi1phpb5zo|Hanji 139]] as [[XiaoHe|萧何]]'s choice after [[LiuBang|刘邦]] enters [[Xianyang|咸阳]]. While other generals seek treasury wealth, Xiao He goes to the chancellor's office and secures Qin maps, registers, and administrative records.

The episode frames those records as governing infrastructure. Maps reveal terrain and strategic passes; household registers and land data reveal population, wealth, and extraction capacity. In this reading, captured archives are not passive documents but a bridge from conquest to administration, making them central to Liu Bang's later ability to understand and hold [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] and the wider former Qin order.

## Key Claims
- Occupying a capital gives access not only to wealth but to the information system that made the state governable.
- Choosing records over treasure marks a shift from short-term plunder to long-term state capacity.
- Administrative archives can turn conquered territory from unknown space into actionable terrain, population, and resource knowledge.
