# Self-Similarity In Intimacy / 亲密关系中的自我相似

Self-similarity in intimacy is the source's argument for choosing a partner who is very much like oneself. In [[chuantai-ni-chi-xiangcai-ma-nvshengban-ni-yuanyi-he-xingzhuan-zhihou-de-ziji-zai-yiqi-ma-555771723]], [[XiaoShiHost|小时]] and [[KeZiHost|柯子]] initially prefer the gender-swapped self because similar background, taste, values, and known defects seem to reduce relationship risk.

The episode treats similarity as emotionally efficient but not automatically healthy. It can make daily life easier, lower negotiation costs, reduce explanation burden, and support [[SincerityAsRelationshipTrust]]. It can also create an intimate version of [[InformationCocoon]] when both partners share the same blind spots, short boards, or emotional collapse points.

## Key Claims

- Similarity can be attractive because it promises recognition before persuasion.
- Known defects can feel safer than unknown incompatibilities.
- Shared values reduce daily conflict only when shared weaknesses do not become mutually reinforcing.
- A similar partner may improve communication, but may also narrow viewpoint diversity and correction.
- Similarity works best when paired with [[RelationshipNeedDiversification]] so the partner is not asked to supply every form of sameness or companionship.
