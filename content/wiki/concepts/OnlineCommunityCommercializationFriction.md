# Online Community Commercialization Friction / 社区商业化摩擦

Online Community Commercialization Friction is the source's main explanation for why emotionally important communities often fail to become high-growth, high-profit internet companies. In [[no-212-cengjing-de-jingshen-jiaoluo-douban-zhihu-tieba-hupu-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-25-1003510980]], the problem appears across [[Douban|豆瓣]], [[Zhihu|知乎]], [[BaiduTieba|百度贴吧]], and [[Hupu|虎扑]].

The source's logic is relational. When a community is valuable because members feel ownership over norms, taste, identity, and shared memory, ordinary monetization tools can look like intrusion. Ads,导购, paid services, platform-led recommendation, bar sales, KPI pressure, creator restrictions, and aggressive app/product expansion can all weaken the atmosphere that made the community valuable.

## Key Claims
- Pure person-to-person communities are harder to monetize than communities attached to transaction, decision, recruiting, training, or tool demand.
- [[Douban|豆瓣]] protected atmosphere through restraint, but that restraint also made growth and mobile transition harder.
- [[Zhihu|知乎]] monetized through knowledge products and later paid stories, but creator incentives and community identity became harder to align.
- [[BaiduTieba|百度贴吧]] shows the trust cost when a platform treats community territory as sellable or KPI-managed inventory.
- [[Hupu|虎扑]] shows that a community can incubate a separate transaction business such as [[Dewu|得物]] while the original forum remains commercially awkward.
