# Pharmacist As Second Doctor / 药剂师第二医生体系

Pharmacist as second doctor is the professional-role reform described in [[vol-120-riben-yiliao-tixi-de-bengkui-yu-zhongsheng-yiyuan-yaopin-yibao-sanjiao-kunju-de-yizhong-jiefa-lp-p5p1cybbldphe0qajteggsitt]]. The episode says Japan lengthened pharmacist education, added clinical medical training, improved pay, and asked pharmacists to maintain medication records, explain drugs, watch repeat prescriptions, and support long-term use.

The concept matters because pharmacists become a bridge between [[JapaneseMedicalTriageSystem|clinics]], [[GenericDrugTrustRebuilding|generic-drug trust]], [[CommunityIntegratedCare|community care]], and chronic disease management. They reduce some burden on doctors while giving patients a local professional contact after the consultation.

## Key Claims
- Medication management is part of healthcare delivery, not just retail dispensing.
- Chronic disease and old-age polypharmacy need repeated support outside the physician visit.
- Generic-drug substitution works better when a trusted professional can explain equivalence, side effects, and use records.
