# Order-Class Vocational Education / 订单班

Order-class vocational education / 订单班 is the employer-oriented class form discussed in [[ep241-xiaoqi-hezuo-shi-xinyidai-de-tiefanwan-ma-lnmpsbvoqbexeardxyx88hywufig]]. In this model, a school and company organize a cohort around expected jobs, skills, or hiring needs, giving students a clearer employment channel than ordinary classroom study.

The episode treats order classes as useful but limited. They can relieve school employment pressure and help enterprises identify students earlier, but they are usually shallower than [[DualSystemVocationalEducation|双元制]] unless curriculum, assessment, training scenes, and management are deeply redesigned.

## Key Claims
- Order classes can make employment more concrete for students and schools.
- The model does not eliminate student choice: students may later choose further study, public-sector exams, other firms, or different industries.
- Companies face retention risk if they invest in a cohort but cannot provide attractive pay, progression, or identity.
- Order classes are most credible when connected to real practice and [[CareerCognitionEducation|career cognition]], not only a promised placement.
