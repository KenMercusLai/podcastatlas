# Travel Autonomy / 旅行自主性

## Definition

Travel autonomy is the degree to which a traveler can choose departure time, route, stops, pace, companions, and revisions during a journey rather than merely reaching a distant destination quickly.

## Current Synthesis

The episode separates mobility range from control. Railways sharply widened where more people could go, but passengers selected among routes and departure times created by an operator and then followed a timetable and fixed stopping pattern. Private automobiles shifted more operational decisions toward the traveler, making unscheduled pauses, detours, privacy, and route changes more feasible.

This autonomy is relative rather than absolute. Roads, fuel, traffic law, congestion, parking, purchase cost, maintenance, and unequal access still structure car travel. The useful distinction is therefore not train unfreedom versus car freedom, but network-scale accessibility versus traveler-level control, with different transport systems distributing agency differently.

## Key Claims

- Faster or longer-range mobility does not by itself establish traveler control.
- Scheduled collective transport widens access while locating many route and timing decisions with the operator.
- Private vehicles can increase stop, detour, pacing, privacy, and companion choice.
- Infrastructure, law, ownership cost, and congestion constrain apparent vehicle autonomy.
- Travel systems should be compared through both collective accessibility and individual control.

## Evidence

- Fixed-network access - [[lmjsukmfoaeb_6b0_ndo2as7ov8e-lmjsukmfoaeb_6b0_ndo2as7ov8e|Episode 514]] contrasts a passenger's initial ticket choice with the train's fixed route, stations, and timetable after departure.
- Vehicle-level control - [[lmjsukmfoaeb_6b0_ndo2as7ov8e-lmjsukmfoaeb_6b0_ndo2as7ov8e|Episode 514]] uses steering, accelerator, brake, privacy, stops, and route changes to explain the attraction of automobile travel.
- Social distribution - [[lmjsukmfoaeb_6b0_ndo2as7ov8e-lmjsukmfoaeb_6b0_ndo2as7ov8e|Episode 514]] follows cars from elite status goods toward mass household tools as income, production, roads, and leisure expanded.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The train-car contrast is stylized and Western European. Railways can provide affordable, safe, high-capacity access unavailable to non-drivers, while car dependence can reduce autonomy for people unable to drive or pay. Roads and vehicles also externalize land, pollution, injury, and congestion costs. The episode establishes a useful agency dimension, not a universal ranking of transport modes or proof that private ownership maximizes freedom.

## What Changed

- Established a distinction between destination access and control during the journey.
- Made automobile autonomy conditional on infrastructure, law, cost, and social access.
