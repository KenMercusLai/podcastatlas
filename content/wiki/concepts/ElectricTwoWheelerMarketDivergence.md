# Electric Two-Wheeler Market Divergence

## Definition
Electric two-wheeler market divergence is the pattern where legacy manufacturers and smart-feature challengers move in different directions inside the same weakening vehicle category.

## Current Synthesis
The source uses China's first-half electric two-wheeler results to show that category decline can hide divergent firm outcomes. [[Yadea|雅迪]] and [[AimaTechnology|爱玛]] decline as the total market shrinks, trade-in demand is pulled forward, new standards force production adjustment, and raw materials rise. [[Ninebot|九号]] and [[NiuTechnologies|小牛]] grow by appealing to younger users through intelligent controls and lower-priced model mix.

## Key Claims
- A shrinking category can still contain growth pockets when product positioning differs.
- Policy can pull demand forward and make later comparisons look worse.
- New standards can create redesign and production-line costs before benefits are visible.
- Smart controls and younger-user positioning can differentiate challengers from older scale manufacturers.
- Input-cost inflation can pressure the whole category even when some brands grow.

## Evidence
- Market decline: [[waimai-chengwei-haidilao-zengzhang-zuikuai-de-yewu-legao-biaoshi-buhui-yong-ai-lai-sheji-chanpin-1008870590]] says domestic electric two-wheeler sales fell by more than 12% in the first half.
- Legacy decline: [[waimai-chengwei-haidilao-zengzhang-zuikuai-de-yewu-legao-biaoshi-buhui-yong-ai-lai-sheji-chanpin-1008870590]] says [[Yadea|雅迪]] and [[AimaTechnology|爱玛]] saw revenue and profit decline.
- Challenger growth: [[waimai-chengwei-haidilao-zengzhang-zuikuai-de-yewu-legao-biaoshi-buhui-yong-ai-lai-sheji-chanpin-1008870590]] says [[Ninebot|九号]] and [[NiuTechnologies|小牛]] grew, helped by intelligent central controls and younger-user appeal.
- Cost and regulation: [[waimai-chengwei-haidilao-zengzhang-zuikuai-de-yewu-legao-biaoshi-buhui-yong-ai-lai-sheji-chanpin-1008870590]] cites new national standards and higher aluminum, copper, iron, and plastic prices.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not prove that smart features alone caused the challenger growth; channel strategy, price mix, brand perception, and replacement timing may also matter.
