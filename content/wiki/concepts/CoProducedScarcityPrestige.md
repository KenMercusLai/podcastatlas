# Co-Produced Scarcity Prestige / 共谋式稀缺声望

## Definition

Co-produced scarcity prestige is a status arrangement in which suppliers, gatekeepers, and recipients jointly preserve an exaggerated scarcity story because exclusivity raises the standing of both the object and the people allowed to receive it.

## Current Synthesis

The ginseng-fruit discussion in [[TaibaiJinxingYouDianFan|《太白金星有点烦》]] separates physical supply from social rarity. Zhenyuanzi allegedly reports only thirty fruits per ten thousand years despite a much shorter productive cycle, gaining discretion to distribute the undeclared supply through favors. The heavenly recipients are not treated as merely deceived: maintaining the “special supply” image also lets them display privileged access. Scarcity therefore persists as a coordinated status convention rather than a seller's claim alone.

## Key Claims

- Physical scarcity and socially maintained exclusivity are different sources of perceived value.
- A supplier gains discretion when reported supply is lower than actual supply.
- Gatekeepers and elite recipients may preserve a scarcity story because privileged access raises their own status.
- Reciprocal endorsement can stabilize the arrangement: prestigious recipients validate the object, and the object marks recipients as prestigious.
- Visible abundance can puncture the story, although comic disclosure does not by itself show how the surrounding market would respond.

## Evidence

### Underreported supply and discretionary allocation

- [[090-quhua-maboyong-taibai-jinxing-youdianfan-p3-changsheng-bulao-de-zhenxiang-lrfapwwjbvam-oix0lljif_3-a-x]] presents Zhenyuanzi's short production cycle and public ten-thousand-year claim as a way to preserve special-supply status and retain fruit for private favors.

### Recipient-side prestige

- [[090-quhua-maboyong-taibai-jinxing-youdianfan-p3-changsheng-bulao-de-zhenxiang-lrfapwwjbvam-oix0lljif_3-a-x]] argues that the heavenly court benefits from treating ginseng fruit as rare because receiving it signals taste, rank, and privileged access.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The evidence is a podcast interpretation of a fictional economy, not proof of an actual coordinated marketing arrangement. Limited supply can be real, and recipients can sincerely misunderstand production. The concept applies only when multiple parties have incentives to preserve exclusivity; it should not turn every premium, allocation rule, or scarce asset into an allegation of deception.

## What Changed

- Established a distinction between supplier-manufactured scarcity and scarcity jointly sustained by status-bearing recipients.
