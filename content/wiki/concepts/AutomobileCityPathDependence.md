# Automobile City Path Dependence

Automobile city path dependence is the pattern where a city is shaped so deeply by auto manufacturing that industry structure becomes urban identity, labor system, supplier base, land use, and future constraint. [[chengshi-jiushi-zheyang-21-shiyan-yizuo-yin-qiche-erqi-you-bugan-bei-qiche-paoxia-de-chengshi-1001770066]] adds the concept through [[Shiyan]], which the source treats as a city born from [[SecondAutomobileWorks|二汽]] rather than simply a city with an auto sector.

The concept has two sides. Auto manufacturing creates large output, suppliers, jobs, skills, transport demand, and local pride; the source says this once made Shiyan a strong "汽车城." But when headquarters, passenger cars, joint ventures, and new-energy narratives moved toward [[Wuhan]] or newer clusters, the same specialization became a vulnerability.

The episode's useful distinction is between cities where auto is one important pillar and cities whose urban formation depends on auto. [[LiAuto]] in [[Changzhou]] belongs to a newer battery-and-passenger-EV cluster story, while Shiyan is a legacy case where [[CommercialVehicleIndustrialBase|commercial vehicles]] remain central after the main corporate spotlight moved.
