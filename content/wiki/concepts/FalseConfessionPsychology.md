# False Confession Psychology

## Definition
False confession psychology is the study of why a person may admit an act they did not commit and why investigators, jurors, and observers may nevertheless treat that admission as decisive.

## Current Synthesis
[[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-7b40091055d1394cc6a3435ffecc95c8-53960014-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-7b40091055d1394cc6a3435ffecc95c8-53960014|Did I Really Do That?]] distinguishes three routes. Voluntary false confessions arise without direct police pressure, including protection, attention-seeking, or psychological disturbance. Compliant confessors know they are innocent but seek immediate relief or an expected short-term benefit. Internalized confessors come to doubt their memory and temporarily accept an externally supplied account.

The synthesis is situational rather than character-based. Authority, isolation, exhaustion, prolonged questioning, false evidence, threats, promises, leading information, and small escalating admissions can narrow attention toward ending the present ordeal. The confession's detail is not automatically independent proof because investigators may have disclosed facts or shaped the narrative before recording began.

## Key Claims
- False confessions can be voluntary, compliant, or internalized, with different motives and belief states.
- Immediate relief can dominate distant legal consequences under acute physical and psychological pressure.
- False evidence and authoritative certainty can make a person distrust memory and adopt an external explanation.
- Isolation weakens emotional support and prevents checking investigators' claims against outside reality.
- Long interrogation and exhaustion raise reliability concerns without proving that any particular confession is false.
- Apparently accurate details establish independent knowledge only when investigators can show that those facts were protected from disclosure or contamination.
- Complete process recording and open-ended interviewing make reliability more assessable.

- Experimental support - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-7b40091055d1394cc6a3435ffecc95c8-53960014-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-7b40091055d1394cc6a3435ffecc95c8-53960014]] reports Kassin's forbidden-key experiment and observer-accuracy research as laboratory models of false admission and mistaken evaluation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept does not imply that all confession evidence is unreliable, that pressure alone proves falsity, or that the three categories exhaust every case. The episode's cases, disputed interrogation claims, averages, percentages, and laboratory effects remain source-scoped because the underlying records and studies were not supplied. Legal rules and recording requirements vary by jurisdiction.

## What Changed
- Established a three-route synthesis joining motivation, compliance, and memory internalization.
- Added process recording and protected crime facts as reliability safeguards.
