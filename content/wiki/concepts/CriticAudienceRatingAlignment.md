# Critic–Audience Rating Alignment

## Definition
Critic–audience rating alignment is the measured relationship between professional film reviews and audience ratings, distinguished from differences in which films each group chooses to review.

## Current Synthesis
The episode’s analysis rejects a simple critics-versus-public divide. Across more than 10,000 films, ratings showed substantial but imperfect agreement, while the larger selection gap concerned critics’ greater attention to documentaries, foreign-language films, and low-budget work and audiences’ preference for action and science fiction. Agreement weakened around popular big-budget action and family films before rising again from about 2022. Critical scores were associated with box-office sales, but the analysis does not isolate whether reviews caused revenue, reflected quality, or moved alongside marketing, distribution, franchise strength, and word of mouth.

## Key Claims
- What critics and audiences choose to rate differs more starkly than their average judgments of shared films.
- A correlation around 0.6 indicates meaningful agreement without interchangeability.
- The earlier divergence was concentrated in particular commercial categories rather than the whole film market.
- Recent convergence is measured, while the proposed pandemic-era explanation remains speculative.
- Review scores and box office are associated, but association alone does not establish causal influence.

## Evidence
- **Selection and baseline agreement:** Critics favored documentaries, foreign-language films, and lower-budget productions; audiences favored action and science fiction; shared-film ratings correlated at about 0.6 from 2005 to 2016. [[bye-bye-bibi-israels-election-results-6ae1b4ac092a0dff193c7811]]
- **Category-specific divergence:** The decline in agreement was not explained by genre, budget, sequel status, or studio in aggregate, but was driven mainly by popular big-budget action and family films; franchise fatigue is offered as a possible interpretation. [[bye-bye-bibi-israels-election-results-6ae1b4ac092a0dff193c7811]]
- **Convergence and commerce:** From around 2022 the two groups aligned more closely, and higher critical scores were associated with substantially higher box-office sales. [[bye-bye-bibi-israels-election-results-6ae1b4ac092a0dff193c7811]]

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The supplied source does not provide the full model specification, sampling rules, platform effects, geography, score normalization, or causal identification. Historical examples of initially mixed reviews do not measure typical disagreement. The claim that critics became more sympathetic during cinema’s post-pandemic crisis is explicitly speculative, and lower-than-pre-COVID box office can coexist with many changes in attendance, prices, release schedules, and streaming behavior.

## What Changed
- Created a data-bounded concept separating review selection, score agreement, temporal change, and box-office association.
