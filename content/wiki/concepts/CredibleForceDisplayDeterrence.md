# Credible Force-Display Deterrence / 可信武力展示威慑

## Definition

Credible force-display deterrence / 可信武力展示威慑 is the use of a visible, interpretable demonstration to make an audience believe that resistance will trigger effective coercion, allowing behavior to change without first requiring a full battle.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1008-ruhe-pingjia-caocao-de-tuntianzhi-ls8wvh9ox-pcjmt0z8w82opbha-k|Hanji 1008]] supplies a bounded mediator case. [[LyuBu|吕布]] makes both camps accept a condition, hits the side branch of a halberd in public, and uses the shot as evidence that he can and may intervene. The display works because its meaning is agreed in advance and because capability is joined to perceived willingness.

The Macedonian cases extend the mechanism to battlefield and campaign scale. In Illyria, a silent deep phalanx performs coordinated pike movements, then abruptly faces the observers, strikes weapons, and shouts; the display creates space for withdrawal before archers and siege engines cover the river crossing. The unexpected Danube crossing, return night attack, and destruction of [[Thebes]] operate at increasing scales, but they are not morally or mechanically identical: disciplined display can avoid immediate combat, while exemplary destruction communicates through realized mass violence. [[dchha72-mania-for-subjugation-ii-dchha72-mania-for-subjugation-ii]]

The shared mechanism is legibility, not spectacle alone. An audience must connect the display to future cost, and the actor must retain enough capability and willingness for that inference to remain credible. A successful moment can still produce long-term hatred, overconfidence, or unstable compliance.

## Key Claims

- A display deters only when the audience can interpret it as evidence of relevant capability and possible use.
- Prior agreement can make a bounded demonstration substitute for battle, as in Lü Bu's halberd shot.
- Discipline, coordination, surprise, and controlled escalation can make a force appear more dangerous than immediate contact alone would show.
- A display can create temporary movement space without producing durable political trust.
- Demonstrated violence may frighten other opponents while also creating moral horror, hatred, and future resistance.

## Evidence

- Agreed test: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1008-ruhe-pingjia-caocao-de-tuntianzhi-ls8wvh9ox-pcjmt0z8w82opbha-k|Hanji 1008]] links the public condition, difficult shot, and belief in Lü Bu's intervention to withdrawal without battle.
- Battlefield display: [[dchha72-mania-for-subjugation-ii-dchha72-mania-for-subjugation-ii]] links the Illyrian phalanx drill, sudden battle cry, enemy retreat, and covered river crossing.
- Campaign credibility: [[dchha72-mania-for-subjugation-ii-dchha72-mania-for-subjugation-ii]] connects unexpected crossings, rapid return, and repeated victory to submissions by nearby rulers.
- Realized terror: [[dchha72-mania-for-subjugation-ii-dchha72-mania-for-subjugation-ii]] connects Thebes's destruction to panic across Greece while preserving sympathy, refugee shelter, and lasting hatred.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The cases differ sharply. Lü Bu's agreed demonstration aims to prevent a battle; the Illyrian drill supports a fighting withdrawal; Thebes communicates through massacre, enslavement, and destruction after resistance. Grouping them identifies a signaling mechanism, not moral equivalence. Arrian's casualty-free retreat, exact drill sequence, audience response, and ancient numbers remain source-scoped. The Hanji source's archery estimates and nuclear analogy are explanatory commentary rather than proof of equivalence. Deterrence observed after victory does not establish that terror was the sole motive or that compliance would endure.

## What Changed

- Extended the concept from an agreed mediator test to battlefield formation display and campaign-scale coercion.
- Distinguished bloodless demonstration from realized exemplary violence.
- Added temporary movement space, long-term hatred, and unstable compliance as separate outcomes.
