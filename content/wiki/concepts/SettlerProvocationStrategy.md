# Settler Provocation Strategy

Settler provocation strategy is the source's warning that some settlers may seek, or at least benefit from, a wider [[WestBank|West Bank]] confrontation. In [[settler-bet-a-west-bank-flashpoint-6a842bb539e73ea8f3c13a2d]], [[AnshelPfeffer]] says a larger Palestinian uprising could force the Israeli army to deploy more forces and enter Palestinian cities, creating conditions under which settlers might take more land and perpetuate occupation.

The concept is different from ordinary local violence. It treats clashes around [[Kusra]] and [[TelTelPiyot]] as potentially strategic because conflict itself can alter deployment, access, and political incentives. That makes the pattern part of [[PalestinianQuestionInIsraeliPolitics]] and [[IsraeliElection2026]], not only a village-level security issue.

## Key Claims
- Provocation can be useful if it moves the state from selective enforcement into broad military deployment.
- The strategy depends on state ambiguity: soldiers may evict settlers in some cases while assisting or tolerating them in others.
- Far-right coalition leverage can make prevention weaker even when the formal state controls the territory.
- International pressure may affect single incidents without changing the overall incentive structure.
