# Seasonal Wellness Marketing Boundary / 节气养生营销边界

## Definition
Seasonal wellness marketing boundary is the practice of separating a product's price, age, rarity, appearance, packaging, novelty, and calendar story from evidence that it is needed, effective, or safe for routine health use.

## Current Synthesis
The episode's strongest consumer-health contribution is its rejection of category drift. A medicine described for acute rescue does not become a daily supplement because a seasonal-health account recommends it, and a familiar ingredient does not become clinically superior merely because it is older, darker, rarer, more attractive, or more expensive. The appropriate questions remain product identity, intended use, evidence, dose, formulation, contraindications, interactions, and the user's actual need.

That skepticism also applies to the episode's own positive claims. Rejecting premium aged citrus peel or black goji hype does not establish that new dried citrus peel, red goji berries, chrysanthemum, flower tea, ginger, moxibustion, or another recommended product produces the proposed health effect. Anti-marketing literacy must therefore evaluate both the product being criticized and the substitute being offered.

## Key Claims
- Seasonal timing and traditional language do not convert an acute medicine into a routine preventive product.
- Price, age, provenance, color, rarity, packaging, and novelty are not substitutes for comparative clinical value.
- A cheaper or more ordinary alternative is not automatically effective merely because a premium claim is weak.
- Product identity, indication, evidence, dose, formulation, contraindications, interactions, and user need should precede purchase or use.
- Severe or persistent symptoms should not be converted into shopping prompts when qualified assessment is warranted.

- Premium-ingredient skepticism: [[vol-103-zhongyike-zhongyi-dafu-jiao-ni-shipuo-chunji-yangsheng-yingxiao-pianju-fang-guomin-chunji-yinshi-zheme-chi-liocs7vqytyxu6y4oaqlwj57h0jp]] questions paying for high-year dried citrus peel and treats black goji berries as unnecessary novelty relative to ordinary red goji berries.
- Substitute boundary: [[vol-103-zhongyike-zhongyi-dafu-jiao-ni-shipuo-chunji-yangsheng-yingxiao-pianju-fang-guomin-chunji-yinshi-zheme-chi-liocs7vqytyxu6y4oaqlwj57h0jp]] recommends multiple teas, foods, herbs, patent medicines, moxibustion, and acupoints without comparative evidence, making its own suggestions subject to the same claim-checking standard.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not provide product testing, regulatory review, comparative trials, formulation analysis, dosing, interaction checks, or a complete account of emergency indications. It therefore supports skepticism toward routine or premiumized use, not a definitive efficacy ranking among products. This page does not advise using or withholding 安宫牛黄丸 in an emergency; emergency symptoms require professional emergency response.

## What Changed
- Created a category boundary between emergency medicine and seasonal supplementation.
- Added age, provenance, color, rarity, packaging, and novelty to the health-claim checklist.
- Applied the same evidentiary test to the episode's proposed substitutes.
