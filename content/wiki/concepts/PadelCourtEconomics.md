# Padel Court Economics

Padel court economics is the business-and-access pattern added by [[spring-then-fall-a-weakened-muslim-brotherhood-6a7aef1b616564bf00d9b354]]. The episode says roughly three [[Padel]] courts can fit into the footprint of one tennis court, giving clubs a strong revenue reason to build padel capacity.

That same economics can make access difficult. The source says Britain's courts are among the busiest and priciest in the world, with median indoor bookings around 44 pounds and some London courts at 100 pounds per hour. The result is a sport that feels easy and social on court but can still carry an "elitist element" through venue scarcity and price.
