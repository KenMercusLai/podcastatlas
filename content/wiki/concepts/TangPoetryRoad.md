# 唐诗之路 / Tang Poetry Road

唐诗之路 / Tang Poetry Road is the route frame introduced by [[ep264-tafang-tangshi-zhilu-shengtang-qixiang-shaonianxin-loo2dfuxffizpbl4si5nwcixnngi]]. The episode uses a [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] cover report to move from single-poet biography into three spatial systems: [[ChangAnTang|唐代长安]] around [[WangWei|王维]], [[ZhedongTangPoetryRoad|浙东山水]] around [[MengHaoran|孟浩然]], and western-frontier travel around [[CenShen|岑参]].

The concept does not mean that every Tang poem has a precise tourist route. It is a reading method: poems are connected to roads, waterways, passes, monasteries, courts, landscapes, offices, social visits, archaeological traces, and remembered cultural prestige. The source repeatedly marks uncertainty where route reconstruction, poem dating, or relic identification cannot be settled.

## Key Claims
- Tang poetry becomes more legible when read through the spaces and institutions that produced it.
- The route frame joins capital sociality, southern landscape tradition, and frontier service rather than reducing Tang poetry to one style.
- Travel writing, tourism, and field reporting can revive classical poems only when they stay accountable to evidence and uncertainty.
- The concept relies on [[TangPoetryCulturalGeography]] and extends [[ReadingAsLifeExperience]] into literary travel.
