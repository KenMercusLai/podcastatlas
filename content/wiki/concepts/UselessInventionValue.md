# Useless Invention Value / 无用发明价值

## Definition
Useless invention value is the idea that an object can create value even when it looks impractical, because entertainment, playful function, maker performance, audience memory, and serious use sit on different levels of usefulness.

## Current Synthesis
The 手工耿 source uses "uselessness" as a classification problem rather than a simple verdict. [[ShougongGeng|手工耿]] says he usually makes things toward usefulness, while fans and viewers often enjoy the mismatch between practical intent and comic result. [[LuoYonghao]] resolves part of the tension by separating pure entertainment value, entertainment-oriented function, and serious functional use.

This makes the concept adjacent to product design and art interpretation. A welded object may fail as a mass-market product yet succeed as a video, performance, souvenir, tactile object, or proof of craft.

## Key Claims
- "Useless" is often an audience label for objects whose value is not mainly efficient problem solving.
- Entertainment value can be a real form of usefulness when a work reliably creates joy, surprise, or shared attention.
- Playful functional value sits between joke and tool: the object works enough to make the performance meaningful.
- Serious usefulness remains present in selected works, but it is not the only value standard.
- The value of a useless-looking invention depends on actual making and testing; if the object is only a prop, the audience's trust weakens.

## Evidence
- Audience label versus maker intent: [[shougonggeng-luoyonghao-cunkou-de-wujindian-li-chuxianle-yige-dada-zhuyi-yishujia-lgbdj-juqslyga0ulvvrqwnedga1]] opens by asking whether any 手工耿 work is truly useless and later records his objection to saying all his things are useless.
- Entertainment value: [[shougonggeng-luoyonghao-cunkou-de-wujindian-li-chuxianle-yige-dada-zhuyi-yishujia-lgbdj-juqslyga0ulvvrqwnedga1]] says the works give many viewers happiness and includes fan comments that jokingly threaten to leave if he invents something too useful.
- Playful function: [[shougonggeng-luoyonghao-cunkou-de-wujindian-li-chuxianle-yige-dada-zhuyi-yishujia-lgbdj-juqslyga0ulvvrqwnedga1]] discusses cutting watermelon, opening beer, roasting food, tearing newspaper, and other cases where comic effect depends on functioning hardware.
- Serious usefulness: [[shougonggeng-luoyonghao-cunkou-de-wujindian-li-chuxianle-yige-dada-zhuyi-yishujia-lgbdj-juqslyga0ulvvrqwnedga1]] mentions a wheelchair aid for helping his grandmother go uphill and a toilet-paper heater as examples with practical intent.
- Making and testing: [[shougonggeng-luoyonghao-cunkou-de-wujindian-li-chuxianle-yige-dada-zhuyi-yishujia-lgbdj-juqslyga0ulvvrqwnedga1]] repeatedly emphasizes that objects must be sturdy and usable enough because filming introduces unpredictable failure.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Entertainment usefulness does not make every object safe, scalable, ethical, or commercially viable. The same source includes safety risks, maintenance concerns, abandoned or unpublished builds, and doubts about turning works into a theme-park-like offline operation.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to capture the source's utility framework for 手工耿's inventions.
- Distinguished entertainment, playful function, and serious use as separate value standards.
- Added safety, reliability, and commercialization limits.
