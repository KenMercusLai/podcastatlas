# Ecological Public Space Renewal / 生态型公共空间更新

## Definition

Ecological public-space renewal repairs environmental function, reconnects pedestrian access, and then supports ongoing civic use and programming rather than treating landscape construction as a standalone beautification project.

## Current Synthesis

The Liangma River case supplies a sequence: ecological remediation, landscape work, repair of walking discontinuities under bridges, and later boats, business, and events. Its public value comes from joining cleaner water and continuous access to repeatable use.

The sequence matters. Commercial programming can sustain attention and operations, but it should follow rather than substitute for ecological repair and basic connectivity. Green public space can also be a relatively robust land use under uncertainty, although that does not eliminate maintenance costs or debate over alternative uses.

## Key Claims

- Environmental repair is a foundation, not merely a visual layer.
- Continuous walking access can matter more than isolated showcase segments.
- Public activity and commerce can extend use after physical construction.
- Operations and maintenance determine whether renewal remains valuable.
- Green space is resilient across several futures but still has land and fiscal opportunity costs.

## Evidence

- Sequenced renewal: [[406-cong-jingshan-qi-dao-sanlitun-zai-jietou-xunzhao-beijing-de-shinian-henji-shizhounian-tebie-jiemu-2f9cb979]] dates Liangma River ecological repair before its landscape and programming phases.
- Connectivity: the episode highlights under-bridge passages that remove pedestrian breaks and create a continuous river route. [[406-cong-jingshan-qi-dao-sanlitun-zai-jietou-xunzhao-beijing-de-shinian-henji-shizhounian-tebie-jiemu-2f9cb979]]
- Continued operation: later boats, commerce, and activities show the space being programmed rather than treated as finished after construction. [[406-cong-jingshan-qi-dao-sanlitun-zai-jietou-xunzhao-beijing-de-shinian-henji-shizhounian-tebie-jiemu-2f9cb979]]

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source reports an observer's favorable assessment and does not supply water-quality data, maintenance spending, ecological baselines, displacement effects, or systematic user surveys. Programming can also commercialize a commons or privilege some users, so popularity alone is not a complete performance measure.

## What Changed

- Created a sequence-based framework linking remediation, connectivity, landscape, and post-build operation.
