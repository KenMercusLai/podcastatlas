# Phenomenal and Access Consciousness

Phenomenal and access consciousness are the two consciousness senses [[AlokJha]] separates in [[i-robot-ai-and-consciousness-6a880840b0d5e24ea40c7015]]. Phenomenal consciousness concerns what it feels like to be something; access consciousness concerns how information becomes available for wider use in the brain.

The distinction helps the source avoid treating all information processing as felt experience. [[GlobalWorkspaceTheory]] can speak to access and broadcasting, while the harder [[AIConsciousnessBoundary|AI consciousness boundary]] remains whether a system has subjective experience rather than only usable internal representations.

## Key Claims
- Phenomenal consciousness is the subjective "what it is like" side of mind.
- Access consciousness is the availability of information for cognition, report, and action.
- A system could look sophisticated at access-like processing without settling whether it has phenomenal experience.
- The distinction is necessary before comparing brains, [[Claude]], [[ChatGPT]], [[Gemini]], or [[OrganoidComputing|organoid computing]].
