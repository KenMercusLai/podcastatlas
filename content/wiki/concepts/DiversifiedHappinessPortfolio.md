# Diversified Happiness Portfolio

## Definition
A diversified happiness portfolio is Tye's practical frame for maintaining several meaningful life domains so that identity and wellbeing do not depend entirely on success in one domain.

## Current Synthesis
Work can provide deep purpose without being the only source of worth. Tye connects scientific work, family, friendship, surfing, movement, solitude, and creative activity as partially independent supports: failure in the lab is less totalizing when life contains other valued commitments, while injury or loss in one domain can make another source of meaning protective. The aim is not effortless balance or equal daily allocation, but deliberate priorities that preserve flexibility, engagement, and recovery.

## Key Claims
- Concentrating all happiness in achievement makes setbacks disproportionately destabilizing.
- Multiple meaningful domains can provide psychological redundancy and perspective.
- Movement, community, family, solitude, hobbies, and work can support different needs.
- Workaholism can narrow identity and reduce creativity or engagement even when output remains high.
- Balance is an active allocation of priorities rather than a claim that every role is easy to maximize simultaneously.

## Evidence
- Setback resilience - [[the-biology-of-social-interactions-and-emotions-dr-kay-tye-scim4105255786]] records Tye's claim that bad lab news is harder when all happiness is tied to work.
- Cross-domain support - [[the-biology-of-social-interactions-and-emotions-dr-kay-tye-scim4105255786]] uses a serious dance injury and continued scientific work as an example of one domain supporting another.
- Daily structure - [[the-biology-of-social-interactions-and-emotions-dr-kay-tye-scim4105255786]] describes a routine integrating surfing, friends, family, trainees, meals, and early sleep.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This is a source-scoped life-design principle, not a clinical intervention or evidence that everyone has equal access to time, care, money, movement, or flexible work. Multiple commitments can also create overload when resources and boundaries are inadequate.

## What Changed
- Added a resilience frame based on multiple sources of meaning rather than work achievement alone.
