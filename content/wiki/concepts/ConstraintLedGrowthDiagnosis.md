# Constraint-Led Growth Diagnosis

## Definition
Constraint-led growth diagnosis is the practice of identifying the specific factor currently limiting useful growth before selecting a tactic, adding capacity, or expanding scope.

## Current Synthesis
The four cases show why “how do I grow?” is too broad. [[Newlane]] may be constrained by adoption positioning and channel economics; [[Playfield]] by founder-dependent operations during a foreseeable absence; [[TheNailRoom]] by first-site capacity and reproducible experience; and [[ISlide]] by leadership attention spread across many channels. The method does not prescribe one growth playbook. It asks which bottleneck, if relieved, would improve the system without creating a more damaging constraint elsewhere.

## Key Claims
- Early traction does not identify the next constraint automatically.
- The binding constraint can sit in positioning, operations, capacity, founder availability, economics, or attention allocation.
- A tactic should be judged by whether it relieves the diagnosed constraint rather than by whether it is generally associated with growth.
- Successful relief can expose a new bottleneck, so diagnosis must recur as the company changes.
- Personal goals and family capacity can be real system constraints rather than external distractions from strategy.

## Evidence
- Positioning and economics: [[advice-line-with-tope-awotona-of-calendly-vmp4254648132]] redirects Newlane from scale alone toward non-use, gifting, manufacturing cost, and visible distinction.
- Founder continuity: [[advice-line-with-tope-awotona-of-calendly-vmp4254648132]] makes Playfield's immediate objective continued operation during Ivana's reduced availability.
- Capacity before replication: [[advice-line-with-tope-awotona-of-calendly-vmp4254648132]] advises The Nail Room to maximize and refine its first site before opening more.
- Attention allocation: [[advice-line-with-tope-awotona-of-calendly-vmp4254648132]] recommends that iSlide put most leadership resources behind one or two opportunities.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source offers short advisory conversations rather than operational audits, so each diagnosis is a hypothesis. A visible bottleneck can mask cash, demand, labor, quality, or unit-economic constraints not quantified in the episode. Over-focusing on one constraint can also reduce resilience when channels or market conditions change.
