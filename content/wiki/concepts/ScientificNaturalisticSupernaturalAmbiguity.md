# Scientific-Naturalistic Supernatural Ambiguity / 科学化解释与灵异留白

## Definition

Scientific-naturalistic supernatural ambiguity gives an apparently occult event a medical, ecological or mechanical vocabulary while preserving uncertainty that the explanation is incomplete, fictional or unable to account for every danger.

## Current Synthesis

In the bounded [[LonglingMazeGhostBlowsOutTheLight|《龙岭迷窟》]] discussion, the eye-shaped curse is described through falling blood iron, delayed symptoms with distance and inheritance across generations. This language makes the threat bodily and patterned without turning the podcast into medical evidence or fully explaining the ghost cave's causal power.

The hosts value the oscillation rather than total disenchantment. Some strange events receive naturalistic-sounding mechanisms, while corpse transformation, occult danger and residual anomalies remain available to horror. Explanation creates a temporary sense that the world is learnable; the remainder protects uncertainty and fear.

## Key Claims

- Physiological or ecological vocabulary can make a supernatural premise feel materially consequential.
- Patterned symptoms create investigable rules without establishing real-world scientific validity.
- Complete explanation can reduce horror when it removes all ontological uncertainty.
- Residual anomalies let rational inquiry and supernatural possibility coexist.
- Plausible-sounding language must remain clearly separated from verified medicine or science.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source is literary conversation rather than horror theory or scientific analysis. “Naturalistic” here describes narrative presentation, not accuracy: iron loss, inherited curse mechanics, toxic insects and other pseudo-technical explanations remain fictional. Ambiguity can also become incoherence when several incompatible mechanisms are introduced without a stable boundary or useful consequence.
