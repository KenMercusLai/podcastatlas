# Monopsony By Artifice

Monopsony by artifice is [[ArinDube|Arin Dube]]'s phrase in [[the-real-horror-of-alien-and-how-it-explains-why-were-not-paid-enough]] for employer power created by restrictions rather than by natural scarcity. The episode's main example is [[NonCompeteAgreements]], which can stop workers from taking competing jobs even when other employers might want to hire them.

The concept sits between [[LaborMonopsony]] and [[ContractTheory]]. It shows that a labor market can look competitive on paper while legal clauses, employer agreements, or administrative barriers reduce real worker choice.
