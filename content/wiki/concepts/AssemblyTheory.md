# Assembly Theory

## Definition
Assembly theory is presented as a framework that studies the construction steps and copy numbers associated with complex objects in order to infer accumulated causal history.

## Current Synthesis
The episode uses assembly history to distinguish objects or outputs that look alike but arrive through different lineages. A living rose and a Lego rose share form without sharing a route into existence; likewise, matching statements from a person and a language model do not establish identical underlying systems. Terrestrial AI belongs to a history containing organisms, language, industry, data centers, and human design, so unfamiliar performance alone does not make it independently alien.

## Key Claims
- Appearance cannot reveal a complete causal history.
- Repeated complex objects can embody accumulated lineage.
- Assembly index and copy number are proposed as evidence-bearing measures.
- Similar outputs do not imply identical processes or kinds of mind.
- Truly alien life would arise from a lineage independent of Earth's.
