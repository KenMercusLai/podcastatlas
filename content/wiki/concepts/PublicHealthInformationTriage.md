# Public Health Information Triage

## Definition
Public health information triage is the source's method for judging outbreak or health-risk claims by source quality, update timing, emotional framing, and professional grounding before converting a headline into fear or behavior.

## Current Synthesis
VOL.205 adds this concept through hantavirus news. [[ZihanDoctor|子涵医生]] and the host treat misinformation as a parallel risk: sensational titles, unverified claims, and decontextualized numbers can spread faster than useful prevention behavior. The episode recommends checking official public-health bodies and professionally supported science communication, then translating information into proportionate action: do not panic, do not dismiss real exposure risk, and update judgments as official evidence changes.

## Key Claims
- Outbreak information should be checked against official public-health agencies and credible professional sources.
- Emotional headline signals such as "shocking," "urgent," or "never do this" are risk markers for poor information quality.
- Recording-time outbreak figures should remain provisional when later official updates may change the facts.
- Good information triage supports proportionate action: prevention, symptom awareness, and care-seeking without generalized panic.
- Misinformation can distort both directions, making people either overreact to low general risk or underreact to true exposure and red flags.

## Evidence
- Official-source rule: [[vol-205-xiangbi-hantanbingdu-ni-genggai-liaojie-hantan-hancheng-yaxing-chuxian-zhege-zhengzhuang-yiding-like-qu-yiyuan-lhze9i64h8ifso_on-1cmnskxtng]] recommends WHO, national and local health agencies, CDC-style bodies, and mainstream or professionally supported medical-science channels.
- Emotional-title warning: [[vol-205-xiangbi-hantanbingdu-ni-genggai-liaojie-hantan-hancheng-yaxing-chuxian-zhege-zhengzhuang-yiding-like-qu-yiyuan-lhze9i64h8ifso_on-1cmnskxtng]] warns against sensational titles and urges listeners to trace information to its source.
- Proportionate behavior: [[vol-205-xiangbi-hantanbingdu-ni-genggai-liaojie-hantan-hancheng-yaxing-chuxian-zhege-zhengzhuang-yiding-like-qu-yiyuan-lhze9i64h8ifso_on-1cmnskxtng]] pairs official information with concrete prevention, medical trust, and non-panic.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept does not say official information is instantly complete during a developing event. It says provisional claims should be source-checked and updated, especially when the episode's own outbreak counts are tied to recording-time reports.
