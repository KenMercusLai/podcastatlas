# Abraxas Whole-God Symbolism / 阿布拉克萨斯完整神象征

[[AbraxasWholeGodSymbolism|Abraxas Whole-God Symbolism / 阿布拉克萨斯完整神象征]] is the source's frame for why [[Abraxas|Abraxas / 阿布拉克萨斯]] matters in [[DemianNovel|《德米安》]]. In [[20-demian-gaiyin-de-etou-shang-na-shenmi-de-yinji-567302734]], Abraxas names a god-image that contains both divine and demonic sides, answering [[MaxDemian|德米安]]'s earlier claim that worshipping only the good half of the world leaves experience split.

The concept turns [[LightAndDarkWorldDuality]] into a religious and psychological problem. The source's point is not that every desire or taboo should be acted out, but that moral labels become false when they deny the reality of shadow, sexuality, aggression, fear, and self-knowledge.

## Key Claims
- A whole-god symbol lets the novel refuse a purified universe that ignores half of lived reality.
- Abraxas joins religious imagery to [[SelfDiscoveryAgainstMassConformity]] because Sinclair has to know his own desire and fear before he can act responsibly.
- The symbol is source-scoped; it is stored as Hesse's literary and religious device, not as a general theological claim by the wiki.
