# Longitudinal Elite Biographical Dataset

## Definition
A longitudinal elite biographical dataset is a consistently structured record of people treated as prominent across successive periods, allowing status composition, careers, institutions, habits, and self-presentation to be compared over time.

## Current Synthesis
[[WhosWho|Who’s Who]] illustrates how a reference work can acquire analytical value beyond its original directory function. Repeated fields across 179 editions make long-run comparison possible, while changes in addresses, hobbies, technologies, and jokes preserve details that formal political histories may omit. The same selection bias that prevents population-level inference helps reveal which offices, schools, locations, occupations, and identities an elite-recognition system privileges.

## Key Claims
- Long-run consistency can be more valuable for comparison than exhaustive biographical depth.
- Selection bias is evidence about status formation when it is measured rather than ignored.
- Incidental self-description can record social and technological change.
- Contemporary entries preserve reputations before hindsight reorganizes historical importance.
- Commercial discontinuation can destroy future continuity even when past volumes survive.

## Evidence
- **Comparable structure:** More than 125,000 entries across 179 editions reportedly use stable enough fields to compare elite life from the nineteenth century to the present. [[all-eyes-on-eisenkot-israels-could-be-prime-minister-6af19d18e7c6d309d1116743]]
- **Selection as evidence:** Automatic office-based entry, secretive discretionary selection, and strong gender, racial, geographic, and educational concentration expose the boundaries of recognized prominence. [[all-eyes-on-eisenkot-israels-could-be-prime-minister-6af19d18e7c6d309d1116743]]
- **Incidental historical texture:** Telegram addresses, registrations, hobbies, pets, and social-media handles trace changing elite routines and self-presentation. [[all-eyes-on-eisenkot-israels-could-be-prime-minister-6af19d18e7c6d309d1116743]]
- **Continuity risk:** The unresolved sale of *Who’s Who* creates uncertainty about whether new observations will remain comparable with the historical series. [[all-eyes-on-eisenkot-israels-could-be-prime-minister-6af19d18e7c6d309d1116743]]

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Consistent publication does not guarantee unchanged definitions, complete response, accurate self-report, or transparent selection. The directory cannot represent Britain as a whole, and demographic percentages from one contemporary edition should not be projected backward. Digitization may preserve access to past entries without preserving the institutional process that adds comparable future observations.

- Created the concept to separate a directory’s historical-data value from its original reference purpose.
