# Hotel Direct Booking Channels

Hotel direct booking channels are the apps, websites, mini-programs, loyalty portals, and reservation systems through which hotel groups book guests without relying on an [[OnlineTravelAgency]]. In [[140-jiudian-jituan-weishenme-dou-rezhongyu-dazao-huiyuan-he-jifen-tixi-chuantai-yuanxingzhe-yu-suibingjiang-lj-xa3wxzjog6-8zm9nfakh6ulew]], direct booking is the commercial reason that free breakfast, member rates, points, and status can be rational for hotel groups.

The source frames direct booking as a channel-cost and user-ownership problem. If [[Ctrip]], [[Fliggy]], [[BookingHoldings]], [[ExpediaGroup]], or another OTA controls discovery and booking, the hotel pays commission and loses part of the guest relationship. A strong loyalty channel can make the hotel app or mini-program cheaper and more useful for repeat users.

## Key Claims
- Direct booking can turn OTA commission savings into member benefits.
- Breakfast is especially effective because it is concrete, easy to compare, and visible at booking time.
- Large groups such as [[HuazhuGroup]] can defend direct share more easily than independent hotels because they have more brands, members, and booking occasions.
- OTA loyalty programs can still pull users back to platforms, so direct booking is a continuing channel contest rather than a settled win.
