# Worker-Artist Identity Reduction / 工人艺术家身份简化

## Definition
Worker-artist identity reduction occurs when a socially informative label such as “migrant worker poet” becomes the dominant explanation and valuation of a work, flattening an artist into representative hardship or sociological evidence.

## Current Synthesis
The Xiaohai episode does not ask readers to ignore labor. Factory discipline, wages, migration, global supply chains, and insecure work are indispensable to the writing. Its objection is to category capture: once “worker” becomes a complete identity, pleasure, curiosity, formal development, humor, ambition, music, friendship, and artistic judgment become secondary or invisible.

The corrective is additive rather than substitutive. A work can be worker testimony, social history, and art at once. Ethical reception should examine material context while also asking about form, voice, influence, performance, change over time, and the author's own account of value.

## Key Claims
- Social labels can improve discoverability and historical legibility while still narrowing interpretation.
- Hardship-centered reception may reward expected suffering and underread joy, curiosity, craft, and change.
- Removing labor context entirely is not the solution because economic and production relations materially shape the work.
- Literary value should not require either denying worker identity or using that identity as a substitute for artistic judgment.
- Translation and criticism can resist reduction by preserving experiential specificity, formal choices, and the author's complexity.

- Additive reading - [[dagong-shiren-xiaohai-zuo-yikuai-gunshi-nisha-juxia-ye-butingxia-lqz5ox0rp0kzxkjgxambqqt6qx12]] keeps factory conditions and global production visible while emphasizing artistic formation, pleasure, and reader connection.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Identity labels are not inherently reductive. They can help marginalized writers become findable, connect texts to labor history, and prevent aesthetic discussion from erasing material conditions. The problem arises when the label closes inquiry or makes suffering the only credible source of value. The episode supplies one author-translator conversation rather than a comparative study of worker-literature markets or reception.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to separate necessary labor context from totalizing identity labels.
- Added translation and criticism as possible sites of both reduction and repair.
