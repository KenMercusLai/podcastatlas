## Definition

Corrective and preventive action, or CAPA, is a regulated failure-response sequence that detects a problem, investigates it, confirms root cause, corrects the failure, and prevents recurrence.

## Current Synthesis

In [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bill-gurley-searching-for-feynman-42956628|Bill Gurley's presentation]], CAPA supplies the procedural spine connecting engineering disasters to the unresolved origin of COVID-19. The durable claim is not that every disaster has one simple cause, but that prevention becomes speculative when investigators skip causal confirmation.

## Key Claims

- Detection begins the process but does not substitute for investigation.
- Root-cause confirmation separates evidence-based correction from intuitive reaction.
- Correction addresses the discovered failure; prevention changes the conditions that could reproduce it.
- CAPA can include institutional and governance causes alongside physical mechanisms.
- Applying CAPA to COVID requires keeping competing origin hypotheses open until evidence supports a defensible conclusion.

## Evidence

- Five-stage sequence: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bill-gurley-searching-for-feynman-42956628|Bill Gurley's presentation]] states detection, investigation, root-cause confirmation, correction, and prevention.
- Regulated use: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bill-gurley-searching-for-feynman-42956628|Bill Gurley's presentation]] says CAPA is required in fields such as pharmaceuticals, medical devices, and aerospace.
- Disaster comparisons: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bill-gurley-searching-for-feynman-42956628|Bill Gurley's presentation]] uses Boeing, Katrina, Fukushima, Challenger, and Surfside to illustrate prevention following causal inquiry.
- COVID boundary: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bill-gurley-searching-for-feynman-42956628|Bill Gurley's presentation]] argues for applying the method without claiming that the root cause has already been found.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The episode offers CAPA as a high-level framework rather than a complete technical standard. Complex disasters can have multiple interacting causes, and a procedural label does not guarantee investigator independence, evidence access, or correct causal inference.
