# Bullet Comment Community / 弹幕共同体

Bullet Comment Community describes the community feeling created when comments are attached to a video timestamp and displayed during playback. [[no-214-xunzhao-tonglei-xiaohongshu-bilibili-yiji-wuhuabamen-de-naxie-shequ-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-26-1005977305]] traces the format from [[Niconico]] to [[AcFun]] and [[Bilibili]].

The source's point is that bullet comments are not only a visual feature. They let users encounter other viewers across time, react to the same moment, create running jokes, and feel that a video is being watched together even when everyone is actually asynchronous.

## Key Claims
- Time-coded comments turn solitary video playback into a shared event-like experience.
- Bullet comments help explain why Bilibili is more than a conventional video platform in the source.
- The format raises governance and density questions because comments can create atmosphere, distraction, harassment, or insider culture depending on norms and moderation.
- [[TimestampedCommentCommunity]] is the audio-side analogue the episode attaches to [[Xiaoyuzhou|小宇宙]].
