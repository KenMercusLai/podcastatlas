# Market Expectation Gap

Market expectation gap is the difference between a piece of news and what market prices had already expected before that news became visible. [[cockroaches-1-modi-0-indias-remarkable-protests-6a6729a33e2f0b6f92d26ab8]] adds the concept through the [[AdvanceNewsTradingExperiment]], where people saw tomorrow's headlines but still guessed market direction only slightly better than chance.

The concept explains why better information does not automatically become [[InvestmentEdge]]. A headline can be objectively important and still move markets in the opposite direction if prices were positioned for something even worse, if policy expectations dominate, or if the trader chooses the wrong asset expression.

The source pairs this with [[PositionSizing]]: even when a trader reads the expectation gap correctly, too much leverage or poor confidence calibration can still destroy the result.
