# Local Elite Proxy Selection / 地方豪强代理人选择

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-973-changpingguan-zhizhan-shengsi-zhidi-zhanhou-jing-chonggui-yuhao-lgazc9dewfayb8qnkncfkfa12yet|Hanji 973]] adds the pre-succession material basis for Liu Bei's later selection. [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] does not simply admire [[LiuBei|刘备]] from afar; he gives him four thousand Danyang soldiers, recommends him as Yuzhou inspector, and stations him at [[PeiCounty|小沛]], making Liu Bei a concrete local option before Tao Qian's death.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-981-sanguo-zui-gang-moushi-chengyu-yi-ju-hua-rang-caocao-tanfu-lhlhlrmf2gvspwyvjkxg6kz44fth|Hanji 981]] supplies the handoff scene that the later concept develops. After [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] dies, [[MiZhu|糜竺]], [[ChenDeng|陈登]], and [[KongRong|孔融]] converge on [[LiuBei|刘备]], while Liu Bei initially hesitates and points toward [[YuanShu|袁术]]. Chen Deng and Kong Rong's arguments show the first layer of the selection: Xuzhou actors want a protector whose legitimacy and dependence keep the local order governable.

Local elite proxy selection / 地方豪强代理人选择 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-982-ba-yi-ba-liubei-ru-xu-beihou-de-liyi-zhi-zheng-ljajdc95xtylhkn5teefz26yyyz1|Hanji 982]], where [[MiZhu|糜竺]], [[ChenDeng|陈登]], and [[KongRong|孔融]] support [[LiuBei|刘备]] for [[Xuzhou|徐州]] instead of accepting [[YuanShu|袁术]]. The episode's interpretation is that local actors may prefer a weaker but reputable proxy over a stronger outsider whose power would absorb them.

The concept is not simple weakness preference. Liu Bei works in this source because he combines enough reputation and Han-clan legitimacy to represent the province, while his limited current force makes him less threatening to Xuzhou's economic and gentry interests. Yuan Shu fails the same test because [[HuainanRegion|淮南]] power, the [[HeirloomImperialSeal|传国玉玺]], and imperial ambition make him a likely absorber rather than a safe sponsor.

## Key Claims
- Hanji 973 adds that proxy selection can be prepared by earlier troop transfer, office recommendation, and stationing before formal succession.
- Hanji 981 adds the direct succession scene: Liu Bei accepts only after local officials, people, arguments, and money converge.
- Local elites can choose a leader by balancing legitimacy, controllability, military protection, and absorption risk.
- A stronger outside patron can be rejected when the cost is loss of local autonomy.
- Reputation and symbolic legitimacy can make a weak actor useful as a front-stage ruler.
- The concept differs from ordinary alliance formation because the key question is who will govern the local order without consuming it.
