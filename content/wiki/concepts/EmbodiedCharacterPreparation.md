# Embodied Character Preparation / 具身化人物准备

## Definition

[[EmbodiedCharacterPreparation|Embodied character preparation]] is the process of converting documents, observation, language, coaching, rehearsal, and material conditions into playable speech, posture, timing, action, and relationship rather than leaving research as background information.

## Current Synthesis

[[JiangQiming|蒋奇明]]'s preparation for [[FeixingjiaFilm|《飞行家》]] joins photographs, documents, documentaries, short-video observation, dialect correction, theater training, and long set discussions. Ordinary behavior—factory routines, bathing, cards, village conversation, pauses, and vocal endings—matters because it supplies how a person occupies a scene, not just facts about the period. Dialect is a particularly clear bridge: speech rhythm changes posture, so vocal work becomes body work.

The episode also makes preparation conditional on medium and institution. Theater lets an actor carry a continuous performance before a live audience; film may begin with a final scene, fragment work across waiting and camera setups, and later reorganize it through editing. Extreme immersion is not merely personal dedication: time, contracts, insurance, specialist teaching, and production protection determine what can be attempted safely and credibly.

## Key Claims

- Research becomes performance knowledge only when it changes repeatable choices in voice, body, timing, action, or relation.
- Everyday observational material can reveal behavioral texture that formal background summaries omit.
- Dialect work is embodied when altered rhythm changes posture and physical action.
- Theater continuity and nonlinear screen production train different forms of performance control.
- Immersive preparation depends on institutional time, expertise, insurance, and safety support.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept is grounded in one actor-writer-host conversation and does not establish that a specific preparation method causes a better performance. Short videos can be selective or performative representations of daily life, accent coaching can still flatten regional diversity, and immersion can create safety or labor risks. Different roles may be built through appearance, environment, biography, rehearsal, technical constraint, or other methods; no single stack is universally required.
