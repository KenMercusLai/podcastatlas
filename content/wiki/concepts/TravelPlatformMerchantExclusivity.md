# Travel Platform Merchant Exclusivity

Travel platform merchant exclusivity is the OTA version of "二选一": a lodging merchant receives traffic, display, or ranking benefits from a platform in exchange for limiting supply on rival channels. [[ep91-dingfang-dingpiao-dingjiangshan-xiecheng-51-yi-wei-aoman-maidan-lovhfkz4rklv1ik-uqyeswrdf3uw]] describes [[Ctrip]]'s alleged "特牌" arrangement as this kind of exclusive cooperation.

The concept matters because it turns [[OnlineTravelAgency]] convenience into a supplier-control problem. A hotel may still be free in a formal sense, but when search ranking, exposure, and order flow depend on platform status, refusing exclusivity can carry practical commercial penalties.

## Key Claims
- Exclusivity is most coercive when the platform controls a large share of demand.
- Merchant choice depends on ranking, search visibility, and booking conversion, not only on the written contract.
- Exclusivity can reinforce [[OTAPlatformConcentration]] by making rival platforms less able to assemble comparable supply.
- Regulatory review needs [[PlatformDataRegulation]] because traffic allocation and demotion may be invisible to outside observers.
