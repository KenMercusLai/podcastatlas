# Cooking as Civilizational Labor / 烹饪作为文明劳动

## Definition

Cooking as civilizational labor is the reading frame in which acquiring food, transforming ingredients, feeding bodies, and transmitting recipes are historical infrastructure rather than private background.

## Current Synthesis

In [[TheFlounder|《比目鱼》]], women first use fire for cooking and nurture, while important women across eras appear as cooks. Recipes connect household survival to class, hunger, identity, political crisis, and even murder. This makes history dependent on repetitive bodily maintenance that grand narratives often hide.

The frame does not romanticize the kitchen or assign care naturally to women. Its force lies in showing that war, philosophy, art, industry, and political ambition remain materially dependent on food work, even when men distance themselves from that work or magnify small domestic contributions.

## Key Claims

- Feeding and food preparation are productive historical labor, not merely private consumption.
- Recipes carry material memory across persons, classes, regions, and eras.
- Hunger and dependence expose the bodily limit of heroic or ideological self-description.
- Gendered invisibility can make foundational labor appear secondary while its beneficiaries claim civilizational authorship.
- Recognizing cooking as infrastructure should not naturalize women's responsibility for it.

## Evidence

- **Fire and nurture:** [[26-junte-gelasi-bimuyu-danze-deguo-nvxing-pengren-yu-renlei-de-lishi-lnn6bsdseydyq0t98pxdttca_htz]] describes women acquiring fire and first applying it to cooking and human sustenance.
- **Cross-era continuity:** [[26-junte-gelasi-bimuyu-danze-deguo-nvxing-pengren-yu-renlei-de-lishi-lnn6bsdseydyq0t98pxdttca_htz]] identifies major women across the novel's nine periods with cooking roles, ingredients, recipes, and food knowledge.
- **Grand-narrative limit:** [[26-junte-gelasi-bimuyu-danze-deguo-nvxing-pengren-yu-renlei-de-lishi-lnn6bsdseydyq0t98pxdttca_htz]] reads eating and kitchens as a repeated interruption of claims about war, philosophy, honor, and saving the world.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept comes from a literary interpretation, not a complete economic history of cooking or a universal claim about gender. Kitchens can also be sites of confinement, hierarchy, danger, and coerced labor. The source's praise of women's nourishment must therefore remain separate from biological destiny or a prescription that women should continue to bear unpaid care work.

## What Changed

- Established cooking as historical infrastructure that reveals the bodily dependency of civilizational achievement.
