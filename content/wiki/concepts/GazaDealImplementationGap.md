# Gaza Deal Implementation Gap

## Definition

Gaza deal implementation gap is the distance between a declared diplomatic or reconstruction breakthrough and the authority, access, security, materials, services, and political decisions required to change conditions inside [[Gaza]].

## Current Synthesis

[[in-arms-way-gaza-deal-sticking-points-6a71b49647235c354d3c9b74]] identifies unresolved sequencing, verification, territorial control, governance, and Israeli electoral incentives after a headline deal. [[unrest-in-peace-misery-in-gaza-145c0cf22e24027ee45e02b7352ac088]] supplies a later outcome test: the [[BoardOfPeace]] and a $2.5 billion recovery presentation coexist with continuing attacks, displacement, food queues, restricted heavy machinery, absent reconstruction materials, and a technocratic body kept outside Gaza. The gap is therefore no longer only missing plan detail; it is the persistence of material and administrative non-implementation after institutions and funding claims have been announced.

## Key Claims

- A diplomatic announcement can be real while remaining non-operational on territory, security, services, and reconstruction.
- Divided Israeli and Hamas control prevents a single administrative or rebuilding sequence.
- Institutions do not govern merely because they are named; they need entry, staff, budget, territory, security, and local legitimacy.
- Restrictions on machinery and dual-use materials can block debris removal and restoration even when financing is announced.
- Civilian survival needs can displace long-horizon reconstruction when food, shelter, sanitation, and safety remain unstable.
- Electoral and factional incentives on both Israeli and Palestinian sides can preserve an arrangement that formal plans claim to replace.

## Evidence

- Unresolved operating design: [[in-arms-way-gaza-deal-sticking-points-6a71b49647235c354d3c9b74]] details disarmament-withdrawal sequencing, unclear verification, fragmented territory, and underspecified bodies.
- Material non-implementation: [[unrest-in-peace-misery-in-gaza-145c0cf22e24027ee45e02b7352ac088]] reports continued attacks, displacement, shortages, and restrictions on equipment and materials.
- Administrative blockage: [[unrest-in-peace-misery-in-gaza-145c0cf22e24027ee45e02b7352ac088]] says the proposed technocratic authority remains in Egypt because Israel has not admitted it.
- Political blockage: [[unrest-in-peace-misery-in-gaza-145c0cf22e24027ee45e02b7352ac088]] describes limited Israeli electoral variation on Gaza and Palestinian distrust of both Hamas and Fatah.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Restricted journalist access makes conditions difficult to verify independently. Casualty figures, territorial shares, membership fees, plan values, individual deprivation stories, and political intentions remain source-reported. Slow implementation in an active conflict does not by itself prove that every institution or funding commitment is fictitious.

## What Changed

- Moved the judgment from an underspecified plan to documented non-implementation after further announcements.
- Added border access, machinery, materials, and basic-service capacity as operating gates.
- Added the exclusion of the technocratic authority as evidence that governance transition has not begun.
- Added reporting restrictions as an explicit evidence limitation.
