# Gaza Deal Implementation Gap

Gaza deal implementation gap is the distance between a declared diplomatic breakthrough and the practical steps needed to change conditions inside [[Gaza]]. In [[in-arms-way-gaza-deal-sticking-points-6a71b49647235c354d3c9b74]], the source says [[DonaldTrump]] announced progress on [[Hamas]] disarmament and [[Israel|Israeli]] withdrawal, but the real sticking points remain sequencing, verification, territorial control, governance, and domestic political willingness.

The concept is a Gaza-specific extension of [[ThinDiplomaticFrameworks]]. The episode describes a plan with named bodies, a timetable, and reconstruction goals, yet still emphasizes that no one has settled who moves first, who verifies disarmament, who governs which parts of Gaza, and what happens before [[IsraeliElection2026]].

## Key Claims
- A headline deal can be real as a diplomatic event while still incomplete as an operating plan.
- Territorial fragmentation makes implementation hard because Israeli-controlled areas, Hamas-controlled areas, and militia enclaves do not map neatly onto a single withdrawal step.
- Humanitarian and displacement facts affect implementation because displaced civilians need governance and services before reconstruction claims become meaningful.
- New bodies do not solve the problem unless their powers, territory, staffing, procedures, and escalation paths are clear.
