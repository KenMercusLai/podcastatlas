# 有限观看 / Limited Watching

## Definition
有限观看 / Limited Watching is the episode's middle path between being unseen and being overexposed: a person needs recognition from trusted others, but also needs parts of life protected from public metrics, social-media persona, and constant evaluative watching.

## Current Synthesis
[[vol-352-wuren-guankan-ziwo-chayixing-jida-waibu-xiangsixing-jiqiang-de-women-gkwrijiofxaxaq8boatxoohj]] develops the idea through [[UnwatchedNovel|《无人观看》]]. The hosts say people cannot simply live without being seen, because human beings need relation, understanding, and feedback. But being fully handed over to public visibility is also damaging: life becomes performance, and the self becomes what can be displayed, ranked, consumed, or approved.

Limited watching therefore names a boundary practice. It asks which needs should be brought to intimate and trustworthy relations, which parts of life should remain private, and how a person can return to felt experience rather than only meeting the standards of the imagined audience.

## Key Claims
- Recognition is a human need, not a weakness to eliminate.
- Public visibility can distort selfhood when it turns ordinary life into persona maintenance.
- Trusted relationships matter because not every feeling or uncertainty should be routed through platforms, strangers, or AI.
- Private space is not isolation; it is room to remain more complex than a watchable image.
- Returning to felt experience can resist the internalized standard of what kind of person is worth watching.

## Evidence
- Need for recognition: [[vol-352-wuren-guankan-ziwo-chayixing-jida-waibu-xiangsixing-jiqiang-de-women-gkwrijiofxaxaq8boatxoohj]] records 星光's view that people still need others to see, understand, and respond to them.
- Overexposure risk: [[vol-352-wuren-guankan-ziwo-chayixing-jida-waibu-xiangsixing-jiqiang-de-women-gkwrijiofxaxaq8boatxoohj]] says fully surrendering to public eyes can turn life into performance and replace selfhood with persona.
- Trusted relation boundary: [[vol-352-wuren-guankan-ziwo-chayixing-jida-waibu-xiangsixing-jiqiang-de-women-gkwrijiofxaxaq8boatxoohj]] distinguishes emotional needs that can be shown to trusted people from parts that should remain self-held.
- Felt experience as countermeasure: [[vol-352-wuren-guankan-ziwo-chayixing-jida-waibu-xiangsixing-jiqiang-de-women-gkwrijiofxaxaq8boatxoohj]] has 超哥 propose returning to feeling rather than only abstract thought or external standards.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Limited watching is a normative concept inferred from a literary discussion. It is not a clinical protocol, privacy law, or universal rule for all relationships. Some people may need more public witness for safety, justice, or creative work, while others may need stronger withdrawal from performance environments.
