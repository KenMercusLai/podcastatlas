## Definition
Outer-relative court power is the late-Han pattern in which imperial in-laws convert accession choice, dowager authority, marriage, harem rank, offices, retainers, estates, tribute access, reward claims, and succession control into a governing bloc. Its danger is not only private enrichment: it can kill a ruler who threatens control, choose the next ruler for self-protection, distort reward and appointment, criminalize upright ministers, punish social contact, suppress warnings, and try to control public memory after death.

## Current Synthesis
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-827-yi-ju-hua-yinfa-de-diwang-zhisi-lnf6rz1i2vmgsclgiejbh0qevzc2|Hanji 827]] now defines the earliest Liang-family coercion layer. [[LiangJiLateHan]]'s dominance has already reached the point where [[EmperorZhiOfHan]] can publicly name him "跋扈将军"; Liang responds by poisoning the child emperor, blocking water at the deathbed, and treating [[LiGuLateHan]]'s grief and questioning as a cover-up risk. The source also shows senior officials first recommending [[LiuSuanLateHan]] before [[CaoTengLateHan]]'s private resentment begins to undo that recommendation.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-828-da-jiangjun-liangji-shanquan-feili-de-donghan-jianchen-llqnqijagtmdlgitulddxspncu0e|Hanji 828]] then shows how murder becomes succession capture. Cao Teng persuades Liang to choose Liu Zhi over Liu Suan because Liu Suan would threaten Liang's guests and eunuch fortunes. Liang pressures senior officials into ratification, while Li Gu and [[DuQiaoLateHan]] resist and Li Gu is removed through [[EmpressDowagerLiangNaLateHan]]'s authority.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-829-junzi-weishenme-doubuguo-xiaoren-lthvnstkfu8n9thx1sz6yezl4yqq|Hanji 829]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-830-donghan-ligu-wo-sui-si-yourong-lt-j7n9gotiw-negqxojoacf6ji|Hanji 830]] show the next stages. [[EmperorHuanOfHan]] rewards Liang and allied actors for the accession settlement, Du Qiao criticizes unmerited reward, and Liang later frames Li Gu and Du Qiao as conspirators until both die in prison. Yet body display and mourning bans also reveal the limit of terror: students, friends, and retainers convert the intended warning into [[FuneraryDefianceUnderPoliticalTerror]].

Hanji 833 and Hanji 834 show the same power ecology after Liang Na's later transition. Emperor Huan increases Liang Ji's estate base, [[SunShouLateHan]] receives revenue-bearing honor, [[QinGongLateHan]] turns household service into office-access brokerage, and Liang estates become sites where private pleasure and property extraction take on lethal public force. [[ZhuMuLateHan]] warns Liang to restrain extraction and appointments, while [[ChenFanLateHan]] and [[YanDuLateHan]] show that officials can still punish Liang-linked agents only in bounded cases.

Hanji 835 through Hanji 848 add public check, kinship surveillance, reward appetite, frontier-witness suppression, empress marriage, household crisis, crime-record saturation, and liquidation. [[ZhangLingLateHan]] can make Liang's sword-bearing breach visible, but sanction is limited; Huan can limit only part of Liang's reward while granting extraordinary access; [[ChenGuiDuliaoGeneralLateHan]]'s warning dies before policy; and Huan finally defeats Liang only by sealing communications, aligning with eunuch insiders, seizing command symbols, and abolishing the Liang office network. The cure weakens one in-law bloc while strengthening eunuch-centered crisis power.

## Key Claims
- Outer-relative dominance can include killing a ruler who threatens control, then turning the vacancy into self-protective succession selection.
- Marriage, dowager authority, household access, estates, and harem rank make private kinship a public governing channel.
- Warnings, lawful enforcement, visitor contact, omitted deference, property refusal, and mourning can all become punishable when a family bloc monopolizes political safety.
- Formal ritual and office procedure can expose overreach, but bounded sanctions do not equal systemic control.
- Reward and ceremonial privilege can normalize dominance without satisfying the favored powerholder.
- Terror can kill critics while failing to control moral reputation and funeral memory.
- Liquidation of an entrenched outer-relative bloc requires control over palace communication, command symbols, and appointment authority.

## Evidence
- Child-emperor killing and cover-up risk: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-827-yi-ju-hua-yinfa-de-diwang-zhisi-lnf6rz1i2vmgsclgiejbh0qevzc2|Hanji 827]] says Liang has Emperor Zhi poisoned after the "跋扈将军" remark, blocks the water request, and fears Li Gu's grief and questioning after the death.
- Initial recommendation and grievance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-827-yi-ju-hua-yinfa-de-diwang-zhisi-lnf6rz1i2vmgsclgiejbh0qevzc2|Hanji 827]] says Li Gu, Hu Guang, Zhao Jie, and Du Qiao recommend Liu Suan, while Cao Teng objects from a prior deference grievance.
- Succession selection and ratification: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-828-da-jiangjun-liangji-shanquan-feili-de-donghan-jianchen-llqnqijagtmdlgitulddxspncu0e|Hanji 828]] shows Liang accepting Cao Teng's self-protective accession argument, pressuring senior officials to accept Liu Zhi, and removing Li Gu after continued resistance.
- Accession reward and procedural resistance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-829-junzi-weishenme-doubuguo-xiaoren-lthvnstkfu8n9thx1sz6yezl4yqq|Hanji 829]] shows Huan rewarding Liang Ji and Liang relatives, while Du Qiao criticizes unmerited reward and refuses Liang Ji's empress-rite and corrupt-candidate requests.
- Ministerial criminalization and body politics: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-830-donghan-ligu-wo-sui-si-yourong-lt-j7n9gotiw-negqxojoacf6ji|Hanji 830]] shows Liang Ji framing Li Gu and Du Qiao, overcoming release pressure, displaying their bodies, banning mourning, and facing defiant burial loyalty.
- Privilege-to-extraction layer: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-833-tanguan-neng-you-duo-lipu-kanwan-dadieyanjing-lqudlijjyqmcro-wsmrvwkodgt72|Hanji 833]] says Huan increases Liang Ji's food-estate base, grants Sun Shou high-status revenue, and leaves the Liang-Sun household with expanding public and private power.
- Household access, estate law, and wealth seizure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-833-tanguan-neng-you-duo-lipu-kanwan-dadieyanjing-lqudlijjyqmcro-wsmrvwkodgt72|Hanji 833]] says Qin Gong brokers access, private parks and villas bend law and labor, and Sun Fen's partial compliance ends in false accusation, prison deaths, and confiscation.
- Rejected self-limitation advice: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-828-da-jiangjun-liangji-shanquan-feili-de-donghan-jianchen-llqnqijagtmdlgitulddxspncu0e|Hanji 828]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-834-lishi-gaosu-ni-xiaoren-weihe-chang-qiqi-lj8z2vfu6lpggumcakwejoi6z9hc|Hanji 834]] show Zhu Mu warning Liang Ji at different stages, first about emperor education and qualified staffing, later about extraction, luxury, gifts, and unfit appointments.
- Proxy enforcement: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-834-lishi-gaosu-ni-xiaoren-weihe-chang-qiqi-lj8z2vfu6lpggumcakwejoi6z9hc|Hanji 834]] says Chen Fan punishes Liang's envoy and Yan Du kills Liang's retainer, showing that officials can still strike Liang-linked agents at personal risk.
- Court-law check: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-835-donghan-diyi-quanchen-weihe-yao-gui-ta-lpwezt-z-2qloitwd3t4gyxznqbo|Hanji 835]] says Zhang Ling publicly rebukes Liang Ji for wearing a sword into the palace audience, while Emperor Huan imposes only a bounded salary penalty.
- Brother-household surveillance and visitor punishment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-836-xiongdi-fanmu-beihou-ruzhe-he-jianchen-de-jiaofeng-lq9wjn5c8mwt-onrtslum4fw53eq|Hanji 836]] says Liang keeps watchers outside Liang Buyi's household and turns Ma Rong and Tian Ming's visit into accusation, shaving, flogging, exile, and death on the road.
- Reward and access excess: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-838-tanlan-de-youhuo-rang-ta-kuangbiao-zai-zuosi-de-lushang-lrydilyni6o6yzhk1-e74m6aigwr|Hanji 838]] says Emperor Huan limits only part of Liang's reward while still granting exceptional etiquette, wealth, residence, court seating, and Shangshu access.
- Frontier critic suppression: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-844-ruhe-liyu-bubaizhidi-lsp49uw8atyiufrf-ryo-9ctos5z|Hanji 844]] says Chen Gui is recalled after Liang's accusation, later memorializes Emperor Huan against Liang, and dies after the ruler does not act.
- Empress marriage and body inspection: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-845-cong-dangdai-hunyin-guan-liao-gudai-de-hunjian-zhidu-lr0i25twkoxm2wen7pnfbwt3qdsn|Hanji 845]] shows Liang Ying's family-backed empress title, pre-marriage inspection, childlessness, and death as the household layer before Huan's anti-Liang opening.
- Clan saturation and office control: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-846-bahu-jiangjun-bingshan-yijiao-de-fanzui-jilu-lv7fbvoqr1nureqgvwiwixjyk4pq|Hanji 846]] describes the Liang family's concentration of marquisates, harem titles, generals-in-chief, high offices, palace spies, tribute preemption, protected retainers, and retaliatory killings.
- Household and harem mechanisms: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-847-nvren-biande-haoming-cong-dongde-zhe-liang-zi-kaishi-lrlov43jtz0vq-o-5m8r2cqo0etx|Hanji 847]] shows Sun Shou and Empress Deng Huan turning private conflict into political vulnerability.
- Palace coup mechanics and aftereffect: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-848-shunjian-kuatai-wo-weihe-shuo-ta-si-you-yu-gu-lgqvgmxlwga2fknmtxpj3ya9chk0|Hanji 848]] records sealed communications, eunuch alignment, command-symbol seizure, Liang-family office removal, and eunuch reward after the victory.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This concept is grounded mainly in the Liang-family case and should not be treated as a complete typology of all Eastern-Han outer-relative politics. Hanji 827 gives the direct child-emperor poisoning layer but does not by itself reconstruct every mechanism of concealment after the death. Hanji 828 gives the direct accession-choice layer but does not reconstruct every participant's private reasoning. Hanji 830 shows that public reputation can resist terror, but it does not show that reputation alone can protect ministers from prison death. Hanji 833-846 preserve source-scoped numbers, income figures, estate details, punishment forms, and victim lists. The episode set also keeps an existing timing tension between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-847-nvren-biande-haoming-cong-dongde-zhe-liang-zi-kaishi-lrlov43jtz0vq-o-5m8r2cqo0etx|Hanji 847]], where Xuan survives a failed assassination attempt and helps catalyze the anti-Liang move, and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-848-shunjian-kuatai-wo-weihe-shuo-ta-si-you-yu-gu-lgqvgmxlwga2fknmtxpj3ya9chk0|Hanji 848]], where Xuan is compressed into a death-triggered lead-up.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 827 as the earliest current Liang-family layer: Emperor Zhi's killing, deathbed obstruction, Li Gu cover-up risk, and the first Liu Suan recommendation.
- Reframed Hanji 828 as succession capture after imperial murder rather than the beginning of the Liang-family coercion arc.
- Kept Hanji 829's reward and appointment distortions as downstream of the accession bargain.
- Kept Hanji 830 as the ministerial criminalization, prison death, corpse display, and failed reputation-control layer.
- Refined the judgment that outer-relative power can dominate law and bodies while still failing to command public moral memory.
