# Airline Meal Industrialization

## Definition
Airline meal industrialization is the operating system that turns food into a safe, schedulable, aircraft-ready service through menu design, batch production, cold-chain storage, transport, reheating, and cabin distribution.

## Current Synthesis
The episode treats airline food as an industrial service rather than a normal restaurant meal. The product must be prepared on the ground, held safely, moved through airport logistics, loaded against flight schedules, reheated by crew, and served in a cabin where pressure, humidity, and noise weaken taste perception.

This explains the gap between passenger expectations and onboard reality. Airlines favor durable dishes, sauce-based proteins, rice, noodles, and reheatable formats because reliability, hygiene, space, timing, and safety matter before culinary expressiveness.

## Key Claims
- Airline meals are constrained by production scale, flight schedules, cold-chain handling, and aircraft galley limits.
- Menu design favors foods that tolerate storage, transport, reheating, and simplified service.
- Cabin conditions can make food taste weaker even when the ground recipe is acceptable.
- The industrial system gives airlines adjustable levers by route, cabin, flight length, pre-order behavior, and cancellation of unwanted meals.

## Evidence
- Historical scaling: [[nan-yi-yingxiang-goupiao-juece-de-feijican-weihe-chengwei-hangsi-shengji-zhongdian-1009809273]] moves from early boxed meals to flight kitchens, postwar jet-era volume, and modern ground catering.
- China aviation catering: [[nan-yi-yingxiang-goupiao-juece-de-feijican-weihe-chengwei-hangsi-shengji-zhongdian-1009809273]] describes the 1980 Beijing-Hong Kong airline food venture as bringing modern equipment, hygiene, management, and menu development into mainland civil aviation.
- Menu constraints: [[nan-yi-yingxiang-goupiao-juece-de-feijican-weihe-chengwei-hangsi-shengji-zhongdian-1009809273]] says airlines prefer reheatable rice, noodles, and sauced meat while avoiding fragile vegetables or bony foods in economy menus.
- Cost and operations: [[nan-yi-yingxiang-goupiao-juece-de-feijican-weihe-chengwei-hangsi-shengji-zhongdian-1009809273]] frames meal standards as adjustable by route, cabin, flight time, pre-ordering, and voluntary meal cancellation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Industrialization does not mean meal quality cannot improve. It means improvements must fit cold-chain safety, reheating, service time, galley space, cost, and passenger expectation constraints. The source gives broad industry claims rather than a detailed audit of individual catering suppliers.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to separate operating constraints from passenger taste complaints.
- Added Chinese civil-aviation catering history as an evidence layer.
- Added cabin taste loss and reheating as reasons meal quality can underperform expectations.
