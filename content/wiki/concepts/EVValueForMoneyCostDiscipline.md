# EV Value-for-Money Cost Discipline / 电动车性价比成本纪律

## Definition
EV value-for-money cost discipline is the automaker strategy of avoiding large brand premiums and instead using cost control, self-manufacturing, platform reuse, and configuration transparency to make buyers feel they receive more car for the same price.

## Current Synthesis
The Leapmotor interview frames this discipline through the "car-world Uniqlo" label. Zhu Jiangming accepts that Leapmotor does not seek high premium pricing; instead it prices from cost and tries to exceed user expectation through configuration and quality. The idea is commercially attractive only if the company can maintain quality, service, and warranty economics while competing in a low-margin, high-capex industry.

## Key Claims
- Value-for-money EV branding needs credible quality, not only low sticker price.
- Cost-based pricing can become a trust asset when buyers believe the configuration and warranty claims.
- Self-manufacturing can support value pricing by taking margin from the supplier layer.
- The model creates marketing pressure because users still need to understand why the low-premium product is not low-quality.
- The strategy has to coexist with broader price-band expansion, or the brand may remain trapped as a low-price-only option.

- Supplier-margin evidence: [[lingpao-qiche-zhujiangming-luoyonghao-lingpao-shinian-buhui-jiang-gushi-de-ren-ruhe-maicheng-diyi-lhei72y0mlqrh2xxbetnqdp-0stb]] says full-stack self-research can capture roughly the supplier profit space.

- Upmarket evidence: [[lingpao-qiche-zhujiangming-luoyonghao-lingpao-shinian-buhui-jiang-gushi-de-ren-ruhe-maicheng-diyi-lhei72y0mlqrh2xxbetnqdp-0stb]] says D99 is partly meant to prove Leapmotor can cover RMB 60,000-300,000 rather than only value-priced entry vehicles.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The current evidence does not independently prove Leapmotor's quality, warranty reserve adequacy, gross margin durability, or customer NPS. Low-premium positioning can also cap brand aspiration if buyers treat it as cheapness rather than disciplined value.
