# AI辅助诗歌细读 / AI-Assisted Poetry Close Reading

## Definition
AI-assisted poetry close reading is the use of language models and speech tools to question a poem word by word, inspect grammar and formal motion, hear pronunciation, and slow the reader's attention without delegating final interpretation to the tool.

## Current Synthesis
[[ep284-yu-miao-shifu-mantan-li-er-ke-naxie-lingxing-juexing-de-shike-lkkydmjftqa-fjhg2uaym_8teinq|EP284]] presents AI less as a summarizer than as a patient language companion. Miao Wei uses it to parse German, ask about separable verbs, hear words, and notice how repeated present participles make “Roman Fountain” feel continuously in motion. The benefit is a denser field of questions and slower reading; the limit is that fluent poetic explanation does not disclose its own evidentiary reliability.

## Key Claims
- AI can lower the linguistic barrier to original-language poetic form without eliminating that form's difficulty.
- Fine-grained grammatical questions can reveal meaning generated by syntax, tense, movement, and sound.
- Speech output can supplement silent reading when the reader lacks confident pronunciation.
- The chief benefit in this source is deceleration and sustained questioning, not faster content extraction.
- Human judgment remains responsible for checking whether an elegant interpretation is grounded.

## Evidence
### Grammar and formal motion
- [[ep284-yu-miao-shifu-mantan-li-er-ke-naxie-lingxing-juexing-de-shike-lkkydmjftqa-fjhg2uaym_8teinq|EP284]] says AI helped identify the many present participles in “Roman Fountain” and connect them to the continuous movement of water.

### Patient questioning and sound
- [[ep284-yu-miao-shifu-mantan-li-er-ke-naxie-lingxing-juexing-de-shike-lkkydmjftqa-fjhg2uaym_8teinq|EP284]] describes using AI for word-level German, separable verbs, sentence analysis, and pronunciation, allowing many small questions that a reader might otherwise leave unasked.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not independently audit the model's answers or show that generated literary interpretations are reliable. Pronunciation output and grammatical explanation can orient a learner, but neither replaces competent speakers, editions, dictionaries, translators, or the reader's own comparison of evidence.

## What Changed
- Established a poetry-specific branch of AI-assisted reading centered on deceleration.
- Identified grammar, pronunciation, and repeated questioning as distinct affordances.
- Made provenance and interpretive verification explicit limits.
