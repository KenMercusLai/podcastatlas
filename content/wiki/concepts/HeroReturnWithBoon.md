# Hero Return With Boon / 英雄携赐归来

[[HeroReturnWithBoon|Hero return with boon / 英雄携赐归来]] is the return-stage pattern [[29-qianmian-yingxiong-edipusi-zhengzai-xiaban-ji-ditie-590112891]] draws from [[JosephCampbell|Joseph Campbell / 约瑟夫·坎贝尔]]. After departure and initiation, the hero has to bring something back: wisdom, treasure, peace, scripture, healing, or a changed relation to ordinary life.

The episode also stresses failed or complicated return. A hero may refuse to return, need rescue, flee through magical pursuit, or come back unable to balance otherworldly experience with social reality. This makes return more than plot closure: the final test is whether transformation can be made shareable without destroying the hero's human life.

## Key Claims
- Return completes the hero journey only when the achieved boon can re-enter the ordinary world.
- Refusal of return shows that bliss, sleep, immortality, or otherworldly escape can become another failure of life.
- Magic flight and pursuit stories make the return dangerous, comic, or morally compromised.
- A failed balance between eternity and everyday life can make the hero fall back into ordinary mortality.
