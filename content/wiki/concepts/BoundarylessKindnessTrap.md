# Boundaryless Kindness Trap / 无边界善良陷阱

Boundaryless kindness is the pattern named in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-919-xiang-huode-hao-xian-tikai-shanliang-de-ziji-lrqxfzir4bqxt0v3ccvfef8faade|Hanji 919]] where a person gives care, compliance, or labor without boundary, reciprocal protection, or value exchange. The source frames this as the "old good person" problem: goodness becomes exploitable when it is detached from self-protection and from any institutional or relational return.

The concept extends [[GoodIntentionsPoliticalLimits]] from political virtue into personal self-governance. It does not reject kindness; it warns that kindness without boundary setting can become a route by which others outsource work, emotion, and responsibility to the most compliant person.

## Key Claims
- Kindness needs boundaries to remain a virtue rather than a mechanism of extraction.
- A social relation without reciprocal value or protection is unstable and politically naive.
- Personal self-protection is treated as the micro-level analogue of institutional feedback and accountability.
