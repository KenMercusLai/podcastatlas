## Definition

Fictional factional inference boundary / 虚构派系解读边界 is the distinction between what a story states, what recurring conduct reasonably supports, and what an entertaining power-struggle reading merely makes possible.

## Current Synthesis

The episode's Wudang discussion begins from real succession conditions: 宋远桥 has seniority, [[YuLianzhouJinYong|俞莲舟]] has exceptional strength and judgment, and 张翠山 has talent and 张三丰's affection. It then builds possible camps from who mediates for whom, 莫声谷's timely discovery of 宋青书, mutual praise, and eventual succession. These details can make rivalry imaginable without proving coordinated factions, surveillance, jealousy, or concealed plans.

The same boundary governs [[FanYaoJinYong|范遥]]. Silence, disappearance, rejected love, 韩千叶's poisoning, and later explanations create motive hypotheses, but none alone proves that Fan Yao killed a romantic rival or fabricated his service to Mingjiao. The useful practice is graded reading: retain explicit action as fact, present motive as inference with alternatives, and label conspiratorial extensions as play rather than silently promoting them into plot.

## Key Claims

- A plausible opportunity structure does not prove an organized faction.
- Coincidence, mediation, praise, and later promotion can support questions without establishing hidden coordination.
- Genuine affection can coexist with rivalry, making either pure harmony or total conspiracy too simple.
- Motive inference should remain weaker than stated action and visible consequence.
- Multiple compatible explanations should be preserved when the text leaves causation open.
- Playful overreading can enrich discussion if its confidence level remains explicit.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Fiction routinely communicates through implication, so lack of explicit narration does not make all inference invalid. Repeated patterns, structural payoff, point of view, and excluded alternatives can strengthen a reading. This concept instead resists confidence inflation: the supplied document is a structured podcast summary, not the full novel, and the hosts' own warnings are part of the evidence. The framework should not flatten literary ambiguity into only literal statements.
