# Pre-Call Qualification

## Definition
Pre-call qualification is the practice of asking screening questions before a sales conversation so that only prospects who understand and can meet the commitment book time with the founder. Instead of trying to create urgency at the decision stage, the questions surface the time, money, and identity commitments the buyer has to make, letting mismatched prospects self-select out and improving the quality of the calls that remain.

## Current Synthesis
[[KariAngoff]] reports the mismatch in [[advice-line-with-michelle-wahler-of-beyond-yoga-c2626aae-0d71-4025-842f-3b854ccc3ca6]]: [[ElectiveStudyAbroad]] generates high click rates, specific email questions, and steady requests for calls, but few enrolments, and deadlines and discounts have not fixed it. [[MichelleWahler]] responds with a qualification layer rather than more urgency: ask whether the prospect is ready for a seven-day immersive experience, whether they are willing to invest roughly $6,000 for a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity, and whether they are committed to being a lifelong learner. The mechanism is a trade of lead volume for fit, and it belongs with the peer-group and experience positioning that the rest of the call develops.

## Key Claims
- Interest and readiness are different signals, and a funnel can be full of the first while producing little of the second.
- Deadlines and discounts push on the wrong lever when the real obstacle is unaddressed commitment risk.
- Naming the commitment explicitly — duration, price, and identity — converts vague enthusiasm into a self-selected sales conversation.
- A smaller number of better-matched calls can be worth more than a large pipeline of exploratory conversations for a founder-led business.
- Qualification questions belong before the call, so the conversation starts after the buyer has already accepted the frame.
- The approach fits high-ticket experiences where a participant commits time, money, and exposure to a group, and where the fit of the cohort matters.
- The evidence should be tracked as conversion quality and completion, not only as call volume.

## Evidence
- Funnel description: [[advice-line-with-michelle-wahler-of-beyond-yoga-c2626aae-0d71-4025-842f-3b854ccc3ca6]] records high click rates, detailed email questions, and steady call bookings for Elective Study Abroad alongside low enrolment.
- Failed urgency tools: [[advice-line-with-michelle-wahler-of-beyond-yoga-c2626aae-0d71-4025-842f-3b854ccc3ca6]] says deadlines and discounts produced little change, which is what prompts the search for another mechanism.
- Recommended questions: [[advice-line-with-michelle-wahler-of-beyond-yoga-c2626aae-0d71-4025-842f-3b854ccc3ca6]] records the three screening questions about a seven-day immersive trip, roughly $6,000 of investment, and lifelong-learner commitment.
- Price context: [[advice-line-with-michelle-wahler-of-beyond-yoga-c2626aae-0d71-4025-842f-3b854ccc3ca6]] gives about $6,200 as the average course price, which is the number the qualification question makes explicit.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The recommendation is untested in the source and comes from a single call with no conversion data before or after. Screening can also filter out buyers who would have converted after a conversation, and hard questions about money can read as gatekeeping if the offer's value has not been established first. Qualification works only when there is enough qualified demand to absorb the volume loss, so it is a fit for high-ticket, founder-led selling rather than for early experiments that still need broad customer learning.
