## Definition
Public-facing historical writing turns rigorous research into a question, structure, and narrative that can matter to readers beyond a narrow specialist exchange.

## Current Synthesis
The episode treats audience awareness as an intellectual discipline. Teaching forces a scholar to connect a specialized archive to questions students can recognize; editing asks whether a claim can be understood without assuming disciplinary ritual; narrative selects a line that makes heterogeneous evidence cumulative rather than merely abundant.

Public orientation is not identical to market accommodation. The standard is whether the writer identifies a real interlocutor, preserves evidence and qualification, and transmits the most important problem effectively. A book such as [[XitaBook|《西塔》]] may therefore produce scholarly value without decomposing neatly into one conventional journal article.

## Key Claims
- Teaching can expose which specialist questions connect to broader historical understanding.
- Reader awareness improves selection, sequence, explanation, and the testing of significance.
- Detail remains necessary, but expert hierarchy should not substitute for public explanation.
- Narrative is an evidentiary architecture, not merely decoration added after research.
- Public writing should preserve uncertainty and complexity while avoiding self-enclosed jargon.

## Evidence
- Teaching and public relevance: [[cong-dongbei-dao-dongya-lishi-shi-yige-guocheng-ren-yeshi-lgolueqq4aiwj4bg5jk-im4qhzc5]] says teaching pushed Song beyond dissertation-scale specialization toward questions shared with students.
- Editorial training: [[cong-dongbei-dao-dongya-lishi-shi-yige-guocheng-ren-yeshi-lgolueqq4aiwj4bg5jk-im4qhzc5]] links Song's reader awareness to translating scholars' prose for general newspaper readers.
- Narrative form: [[cong-dongbei-dao-dongya-lishi-shi-yige-guocheng-ren-yeshi-lgolueqq4aiwj4bg5jk-im4qhzc5]] presents 《西塔》 as a multi-period narrative whose overall value cannot be reduced to a standard article.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Accessibility can become simplification, spectacle, or false certainty, while specialist forms sometimes protect necessary precision. Audience response and reading volume are feedback signals, not direct measures of historical quality.
