# Unmet Medical Need Demand Pull

Unmet medical need demand pull is the episode's reminder that patients, hospitals, physicians, and disease burden are core innovation-drug actors. [[130-shui-cai-shi-shengwu-yiyao-hangye-fazhan-de-hexin-qudongli-jiantan-chuangxinyao-chanyelian-de-jiaose-fengong-lnau0sacx1jm1t-2locdc5nszakh]] argues that China's large patient population and high disease burden, including some tumor types with distinctive local prevalence, helped make innovation-drug development urgent and investable.

The concept does not say demand alone creates drugs. Demand has to connect to clinical evidence, regulators, reimbursement, hospitals, doctors, capital, and companies before it becomes a sustainable innovation system.

## Key Claims
- Patients, physicians, and hospitals belong in the industry map, not only pharma companies and investors.
- Disease burden can pull attention and capital toward therapeutic areas with real unmet needs.
- Once past breakthroughs improve outcomes, the remaining unmet-need threshold changes: future drugs must clear higher clinical-benefit bars.
- Reimbursement and access decide whether demand becomes broad treatment adoption.
