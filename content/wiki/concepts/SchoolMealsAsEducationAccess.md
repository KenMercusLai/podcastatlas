# School Meals as Education Access / 校餐作为教育入口

## Definition
School meals as education access / 校餐作为教育入口 is the pattern where providing food at school makes attendance possible for children whose hunger would otherwise push them out of class.

## Current Synthesis
The South Sudan episode uses a school across from Wedweil refugee camp to show humanitarian aid as more than short-term calories. Before WFP support, the source says many children left after brief attendance because they were too hungry. After WFP-funded meals, enrollment and retention are described as far stronger.

The concept matters because the meal is both nutrition and institutional attachment. For families under food stress, school becomes plausible when it also solves one immediate survival problem; education access therefore depends partly on food security.

## Key Claims
- Hunger can break school attendance even when a school physically exists.
- A daily meal can make school valuable to families facing immediate food scarcity.
- School meals link emergency relief to longer-term human-capital outcomes.
- The education effect depends on continued funding and procurement capacity, not goodwill alone.

## Evidence
- Attendance before meals: [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] says a nearby school lost many initial students because children were too hungry to stay.
- WFP program design: [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] says WFP funds school food procurement so students can receive a meal.
- Reported outcome: [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] says the school later registered about 1500 students with little dropout.
- Life-chance frame: [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] adds an Uganda example where food drew children into school and they continued to higher education.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source gives illustrative school accounts, not a controlled evaluation. The concept should stay qualified around attendance and opportunity rather than claiming school meals alone cause later graduation.
