## Definition

Reflective desire autonomy is the capacity to identify, examine, rank, revise, satisfy, substitute, or refuse desires and redirect life accordingly, rather than treating freedom as either unlimited satisfaction or automatic suppression.

## Current Synthesis

[[372-dang-keji-nenggou-manzu-yiqie-yuanwang-women-hai-zhidao-ziji-xiangyao-shenme-ma-maluo-fushide-boshi-de-beiju-ltehzo-lehi9vpgy5ukexbcrtcpr]] supplies the capability-side warning: [[FaustusMarlowe|Faustus]] gains extraordinary options while losing attention, direction, and practical capacity to reconsider the bargain. More means do not determine worthy ends, and immediate diversion can narrow agency even as external choice expands.

The listener stories add the restraint-side warning: autonomy is also weakened when inherited thrift, status anxiety, or a perpetually deferred future prevents a person from acknowledging a durable desire. Writing down the object and the hoped-for function can uncover comfort, memory, recognition, identity, or connection, after which the person can buy, test, substitute, postpone, or decline with greater specificity.

## Key Claims

- More available actions do not necessarily produce greater freedom.
- Preference satisfaction differs from reflective endorsement of a preference.
- Reflexive denial can restrict agency just as immediate gratification can.
- Attention and time are required to distinguish an object from the need it is expected to meet.
- Self-command means being able to rank, revise, satisfy, or refuse desire rather than suppressing every desire.
- A worthy purpose cannot be inferred from either the efficiency of fulfillment or the moral prestige of restraint.

## Evidence

### Capability without direction

- [[372-dang-keji-nenggou-manzu-yiqie-yuanwang-women-hai-zhidao-ziji-xiangyao-shenme-ma-maluo-fushide-boshi-de-beiju-ltehzo-lehi9vpgy5ukexbcrtcpr]] gives Faustus unusual mobility and command while his life narrows toward vanity, entertainment, and control.
- [[372-dang-keji-nenggou-manzu-yiqie-yuanwang-women-hai-zhidao-ziji-xiangyao-shenme-ma-maluo-fushide-boshi-de-beiju-ltehzo-lehi9vpgy5ukexbcrtcpr]] shows repeated awareness of consequences displaced by immediately available diversion.

### Reflection through articulation

- [[e258-zhege-dongxi-wo-zhende-hao-xiang-yao-xiaojiuguan-gushihui-lmb4vjrqekll_dhzaff4gjtbewgn]] separates the desired objects from needs for daily comfort, family memory, identity closure, career commemoration, self-directed enjoyment, and a record of ordinary life.
- [[e258-zhege-dongxi-wo-zhende-hao-xiang-yao-xiaojiuguan-gushihui-lmb4vjrqekll_dhzaff4gjtbewgn]] shows reflection producing different actions: purchase, gift acceptance, substitution, a smaller experiment, changed criteria, and continued non-purchase.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

External options and resources are genuine conditions of freedom, and reflection cannot remove debt, scarcity, coercion, or other material constraints. The evidence consists of a literary interpretation and listener narratives, not a validated decision method. Articulation can rationalize a preselected answer, and neither strong desire nor disciplined restraint proves that a choice is autonomous.

## What Changed

- Added reflexive suppression as a second failure mode alongside unexamined satisfaction.
- Added writing, substitution, and small experiments as practical ways to separate an object from its underlying need.
