# 求财先明道 / Wealth Through Dao Frame

求财先明道 / wealth through Dao frame enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-920-mingwu-zhege-daoli-caineng-zhuan-daqian-lqmabg3bpyz6ct2v4arx-iun57ws|Hanji 920]] as the source's opening wealth ethic. The host says money is visible while Dao is hidden, so seeking money directly misses the rules, trends, human feeling, value creation, humility, learning, and moral bearing that make wealth durable.

The frame overlaps with [[CognitionAsFortuneRoot|认知通达决定福禄]] but is narrower. Cognition as fortune root treats worldview as shaping fate, health, relationships, and opportunity; this concept names the specific claim that money follows a grasp of order and conduct. [[FanLi|范蠡]] becomes the episode's example because he is said to understand pattern and human nature, leave politics, rebuild wealth, and repeatedly give it away.

The wiki keeps the strong claim source-scoped. It should not be read as a complete explanation for poverty or wealth; its practical version is that durable earning depends on perception, value, trust, timing, and ethical constraint rather than on chasing money alone.

## Key Claims
- The source treats money and Dao as paired visible/invisible layers.
- "Dao" in this source includes rule, trend, value, human feeling, humility, learning, and moral capacity, not only metaphysics.
- Wealth is framed as a cultivation problem because unearned or unjust wealth can exceed a person's capacity to hold it.
- Fan Li supplies the positive case: exit judgment, human-nature reading, rebuilding capability, and charitable dispersal.
- The claim remains source-scoped and should not erase structural, institutional, or luck-based causes of wealth and poverty.
