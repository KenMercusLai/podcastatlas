# Product Variable Judgment

## Definition

Product variable judgment is the practice of deciding whether a product opportunity is real by identifying the underlying technological, infrastructural, cost, behavior, or supply-chain variable that has changed enough to make a new product possible.

## Current Synthesis

Ren uses this judgment style to connect two otherwise different periods: early Douyin and Shumei Wanwu. Douyin is framed as a response to mobile network, phone screen, GPU, camera, and expression-format changes. Shumei Wanwu is framed as a response to AI generation, 3D modality, desktop manufacturing equipment, Chinese supply-chain depth, and the unmet desire to turn concepts into physical goods.

The concept is not pure intuition. The source presents Ren as feeling-driven at the first moment, then rationally decomposing whether the variable is large, whether it creates efficiency or cost discontinuity, and whether users reveal real demand.

## Key Claims

- A startup or new product should be tied to a changed variable, not only a founder's desire to start something.
- The variable can be technical, infrastructural, behavioral, supply-chain-based, or cost-based.
- Transferable product experience helps only after the new domain's non-transferable constraints are learned.
- Feeling can point to an opportunity, but verification requires decomposition, user feedback, and operational tests.
- Large-variable judgment can prevent blindly copying a prior product playbook into a new domain.
- The standard asks whether the change is large enough to produce qualitatively different efficiency, cost, or user possibility.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept does not prove that every claimed variable becomes a durable business. A large shift can still fail if timing, distribution, product quality, cost, compliance, or execution is wrong. The source also comes from one founder's retrospective account, so hindsight may make variables look cleaner than they felt at the time.

## What Changed

- Created the concept from Ren Lifeng's comparison between early Douyin and AI 3D manufacturing.
- Defined the judgment as a method for opportunity evaluation rather than a claim that Shumei Wanwu will succeed.
