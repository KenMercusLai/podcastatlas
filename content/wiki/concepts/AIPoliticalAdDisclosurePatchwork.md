# AI Political Ad Disclosure Patchwork

## Definition

The AI political ad disclosure patchwork is the state-by-state system of labels, timing rules, restrictions, penalties, and constitutional boundaries governing AI-generated or manipulated election advertising in the absence of one federal standard.

## Current Synthesis

The bounded evidence shows that campaign AI is broader than spectacular deepfakes. It accelerates writing, targeting, analysis, persuasion, and creative production, while synthetic endorsements and altered likenesses create a particularly legible deception risk. State responses differ in label size and duration, covered media, pre-election windows, bans, penalties, and available defenses; larger campaigns may be better equipped to manage that compliance burden.

Disclosure improves legibility but does not resolve authorship, truth, consent, distribution, or platform responsibility. Obvious defects may reveal a low-quality fake, yet voters can still encounter the content before correction, and constitutional challenges limit how far governments can go beyond disclaimers.

## Key Claims

- Operational speed and targeting can matter as much as high-profile synthetic video.
- Disclosure rules differ materially across states and campaign periods.
- Synthetic endorsements and likeness use raise consent and authenticity problems even when visual defects expose the fabrication.
- Disclosure can add evidence without proving who authored, approved, or believed a message.
- Patchwork compliance may favor campaigns with larger legal and vendor capacity.
- Restrictions beyond disclosure face stronger First Amendment and overbreadth challenges.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The sources do not establish the effectiveness of any particular disclosure format, the prevalence of deceptive AI ads, or the causal effect of one synthetic ad on an election. The newest example is summarized without the underlying creative, campaign filing, platform record, or affected people's testimony. A visibly artificial ad may be satire, incompetence, or deception depending on context, which is why labeling and enforcement require precise definitions.
