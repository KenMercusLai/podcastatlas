# Wisdom Over Intelligence / 智慧稀缺论

Wisdom over intelligence is [[WangTianfan]]'s central AI-application frame in [[reai-yige-hangye-15-nian-de-liyou-shi-shenme-duitan-wang-tianfan-woyao-tou-zhenzheng-de-kuaile-tou-zui-chun-de-yuanjing-tou-renxing-de-guanghui-gonglu-boke-lu98aa1byafbbljyjrn8oquiezk]]. He argues that model intelligence is becoming abundant, while wisdom remains scarce because wisdom needs context, feedback, experience, reflection, value judgment, and care for others.

The concept extends [[HumanJudgmentUnderAI]] from a review boundary into a product-design thesis. A useful AI product should not only make a task faster; it should help a person understand what matters, notice themselves and others, and convert generated intelligence into better situated judgment.

## Key Claims
- Intelligence can be commoditized by stronger models, but wisdom depends on user-specific context and lived feedback.
- AI applications become more valuable when they help users reflect, choose, and care rather than only produce more output.
- [[AIContextMachine|Context machines]] are one proposed route because they collect signals that a generic chatbot does not have.
- Productivity-only framing can miss consumer AI products whose value is memory, joy, attention, or social connection.
