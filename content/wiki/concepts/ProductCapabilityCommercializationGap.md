# Product Capability–Commercialization Gap / 产品能力与商业化能力差距

## Definition
The product capability–commercialization gap is the distance between designing a compelling product and assembling the financing, production, explanation, channel, brand, sales, service, and localization capabilities that let it reach customers sustainably.

## Current Synthesis
The [[YeHongxin|Ye Hongxin]] account separates several layers that founder stories often collapse. An aircraft can demonstrate technical ambition without adequate endurance, capital, or suppliers; a scooter can win design interest while failing reliability and delivery; and a high-speed hair dryer can reach production yet sell only a few units per day because buyers do not understand or trust it. Product strength is therefore necessary but not self-distributing. Commercialization is a coordinated system that turns a device into repeatable adoption without erasing quality or surrendering most of the value to a better-capitalized channel partner.

## Key Claims
- Technical feasibility, product appeal, production readiness, and commercial scale are separate thresholds.
- Weak financing and sales capability can force a product team into exclusive or asymmetric partnerships even when the object is valued.
- Marketing is not only promotion; for novel hardware it includes credible explanation of why the product matters.
- Production, quality, channels, service, and localization remain product responsibilities because their failures alter the user outcome.
- Commercial success in one category does not prove the organization can repeat the whole system elsewhere.

## Evidence
- Aircraft boundary: [[ll4ekwtvjnpxit2gdkvcfphf0eli-ll4ekwtvjnpxit2gdkvcfphf0eli]] describes a flying prototype constrained by endurance, reliability, components, funding, and supply chain.
- Scooter boundary: [[ll4ekwtvjnpxit2gdkvcfphf0eli-ll4ekwtvjnpxit2gdkvcfphf0eli]] contrasts first-generation design reception with quality problems and later reliance on an overseas exclusive seller.
- Hair-dryer boundary: [[ll4ekwtvjnpxit2gdkvcfphf0eli-ll4ekwtvjnpxit2gdkvcfphf0eli]] records low initial sales despite lengthy engineering work, followed by growth after detailed product explanation reached a large audience.
- Expansion boundary: [[ll4ekwtvjnpxit2gdkvcfphf0eli-ll4ekwtvjnpxit2gdkvcfphf0eli]] identifies marketing, overseas brand operation, compliance, patents, retail, and product-team development as continuing gaps.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Commercialization cannot rescue a product with weak safety, reliability, usefulness, or economics. Viral attention can produce temporary orders without retention or brand trust. The source does not supply audited cohort, margin, channel, service, or international performance data, so it establishes a founder's operating interpretation rather than the relative causal contribution of product, content, price, or timing.
