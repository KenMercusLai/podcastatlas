# Summit Ceremony-Substance Gap

## Definition

The summit ceremony-substance gap is the distance between highly visible diplomatic hospitality and the limited, temporary, or ambiguous policy outcomes actually secured.

## Current Synthesis

The Trump-Xi meeting pairs a personal greeting, military music, cannon fire, flyovers, and public language of goodwill with a two-month trade pause and continued rare-earth exports. The display communicates mutual recognition and may lower immediate escalation risk, but it does not itself settle tariffs, AI safety, Taiwan, or future security bargaining.

## Key Claims

- Ceremony can signal respect and willingness to keep negotiating without proving policy convergence.
- Short-term de-escalation is substantive but should not be mistaken for a durable settlement.
- Public goodwill language can coexist with unresolved strategic competition.
- The more elaborate the spectacle, the more important it becomes to separate signaling effects from negotiated commitments.

## Evidence

- Pageantry: [[big-meet-little-meat-the-trump-xi-summit-6ab6401bbd67ce4b534215db]] reports a personal greeting, military music, cannon fire, and flyovers.
- Narrow agreement: [[big-meet-little-meat-the-trump-xi-summit-6ab6401bbd67ce4b534215db]] limits concrete results to delayed tariff escalation and continued rare-earth supply for two months.
- Unresolved agenda: [[big-meet-little-meat-the-trump-xi-summit-6ab6401bbd67ce4b534215db]] reports no major AI-safety mechanism or Taiwan breakthrough.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Symbolic diplomacy is not necessarily empty: respectful treatment and continued dialogue can reduce miscalculation or prepare later agreements. The source judges immediate outcomes, not the summit's longer-term effects.
