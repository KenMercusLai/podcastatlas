# Mother Complex / 母亲情结

[[MotherComplex|Mother complex / 母亲情结]] is the episode's frame for how unresolved maternal attachment can shape [[PuerAeternus|eternal-youth]] psychology. In [[07-yong-rongge-lilun-fenxi-xiaowangzi-yongheng-shaonian-ji-qita-539091165]], the hosts connect [[AntoineDeSaintExupery|Antoine de Saint-Exupery / 圣埃克苏佩里]]'s early father loss, dependence on his mother, separation anxiety, and his mother's grief to the emotional architecture of [[TheLittlePrince|《小王子》]].

The source uses this concept to read the story's women and animals without making them only biographical equivalents. The rose can be linked to [[ConsueloDeSaintExupery|Consuelo de Saint-Exupery / 康苏爱萝]] and conflicted marriage; the fox can be linked to a more protective, guiding lover figure; both become tests of whether the eternal youth can accept real relation instead of seeking a perfect maternal image.

## Key Claims
- In the source, mother complex helps explain why the eternal-youth figure may pursue ideal love while fleeing actual commitment.
- The concept makes the rose and fox morally more complicated: they are not only symbols serving the prince's self-discovery, but figures who wait, teach, and are left behind.
- Maternal attachment can feed imagination and a sense of destiny, but it can also make ordinary adult reciprocity feel like a fall from the ideal.
- The episode treats these claims as source-scoped psychological interpretation, not as a clinical diagnosis of Saint-Exupery.
