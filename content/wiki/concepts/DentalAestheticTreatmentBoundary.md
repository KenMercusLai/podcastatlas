# Dental Aesthetic Treatment Boundary / 牙齿美学治疗边界

## Definition
The dental aesthetic treatment boundary distinguishes normal tooth color, removable surface stain, chemical bleaching, and structural procedures such as veneers or crowns so that cosmetic goals do not obscure diagnosis, sensitivity, tissue risk, irreversibility, or professional scope.

## Current Synthesis
[[vol-132-bao-zhu-ni-qian-bao-bi-lei-na-xie-keng-ren-de-kou-qiang-hu-li-chan-pin-he-wei-ke-pu-lmjeye3oacbawtn3omsskuqeubeh]] rejects pure whiteness as the default measure of oral health. Surface staining from drinks can differ from intrinsic change due to trauma, tetracycline exposure, fluorosis, or other causes. Polishing, peroxide-based bleaching, adhesive strips, veneers, and crowns therefore solve different problems and carry different burdens; “celebrity same model” is marketing, not an indication or quality standard.

## Key Claims
- Healthy teeth are not necessarily bright white, so appearance should not be used as a stand-alone health diagnosis.
- External surface stain and internal discoloration require different assessment and response.
- Polishing removes or reduces surface deposits but does not create the same change as chemical bleaching.
- Peroxide-based whitening may cause sensitivity or damage, especially when use is prolonged, poorly fitted, or unguided.
- Veneers and crowns are clinical procedures with structural and qualification implications, not ordinary beauty products.
- Celebrity association and price do not establish patient suitability, technical quality, or provider competence.

- Color and cause: [[vol-132-bao-zhu-ni-qian-bao-bi-lei-na-xie-keng-ren-de-kou-qiang-hu-li-chan-pin-he-wei-ke-pu-lmjeye3oacbawtn3omsskuqeubeh]] distinguishes natural tooth color, external stain, trauma-related change, tetracycline staining, and fluorosis.
- Whitening mechanisms: [[vol-132-bao-zhu-ni-qian-bao-bi-lei-na-xie-keng-ren-de-kou-qiang-hu-li-chan-pin-he-wei-ke-pu-lmjeye3oacbawtn3omsskuqeubeh]] separates physical surface cleaning from peroxide or carbamide-peroxide bleaching.
- Product and procedure risks: [[vol-132-bao-zhu-ni-qian-bao-bi-lei-na-xie-keng-ren-de-kou-qiang-hu-li-chan-pin-he-wei-ke-pu-lmjeye3oacbawtn3omsskuqeubeh]] cautions against prolonged unguided whitening-strip use and places veneers and crowns in qualified medical settings.
- Marketing boundary: [[vol-132-bao-zhu-ni-qian-bao-bi-lei-na-xie-keng-ren-de-kou-qiang-hu-li-chan-pin-he-wei-ke-pu-lmjeye3oacbawtn3omsskuqeubeh]] treats “celebrity” language as separate from technique, appropriateness, region, institution, and clinician.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The episode does not compare specific products, concentrations, protocols, materials, longevity, prices, or complication rates. Whitening and restorative options may be reasonable after diagnosis and informed discussion. Individual suitability depends on the cause of discoloration, existing restorations, enamel and gum condition, sensitivity, bite, expectations, and the proposed procedure.

## What Changed
- Created a mechanism-and-burden ladder from normal color and surface stain to bleaching and structural restoration.
- Separated celebrity branding and price from clinical indication and provider competence.
