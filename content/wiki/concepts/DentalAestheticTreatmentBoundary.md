# Dental Aesthetic Treatment Boundary / 牙齿美学治疗边界

## Definition
The dental aesthetic treatment boundary distinguishes normal tooth color, removable surface stain, chemical bleaching, and structural procedures such as veneers or crowns so that cosmetic goals do not obscure diagnosis, sensitivity, tissue risk, irreversibility, or professional scope.

## Current Synthesis
The sources reject pure whiteness as the default measure of oral health: translucent enamel can reveal naturally yellow dentin. They distinguish removable stain from tobacco, coffee, tea, or red wine from intrinsic change associated with trauma, loss of pulp vitality, tetracycline exposure, fluorosis, or other causes. Cleaning and polishing, abrasive stain-removal toothpaste, peroxide-based strips or in-office bleaching, veneers, and crowns therefore solve different problems and carry progressively different burdens. Examination of sensitivity, cracks, recession, periodontal health, restorations, and the cause of discoloration should precede escalation; celebrity branding, price, or a beauty setting is not an indication or quality standard.

## Key Claims
- Healthy teeth are not necessarily bright white, so appearance should not be used as a stand-alone health diagnosis.
- External surface stain and internal discoloration require different assessment and response.
- Polishing removes or reduces surface deposits but does not create the same change as chemical bleaching.
- Peroxide-based whitening may cause sensitivity or damage, especially when use is prolonged, poorly fitted, or unguided.
- Pre-existing sensitivity, cracks, recession, periodontal disease, or other untreated oral problems can change whether and when whitening is appropriate.
- Veneers and crowns are clinical procedures with structural and qualification implications, not ordinary beauty products.
- Celebrity association and price do not establish patient suitability, technical quality, or provider competence.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The sources do not compare specific products, concentrations, otherwise matched light-assisted and non-light protocols, materials, prices, or complication rates. The stated one-to-two-year duration is source-scoped and behavior-dependent rather than a guarantee. Whitening and restorative options may be reasonable after diagnosis and informed discussion. Individual suitability depends on the cause of discoloration, existing restorations, enamel and gum condition, cracks, recession, sensitivity, bite, expectations, and the proposed procedure.

## What Changed
- Added natural enamel translucency and visible dentin to the explanation of normal non-white tooth color.
- Added pre-treatment review of sensitivity, cracks, recession, periodontal health, and unresolved disease.
- Qualified duration and light-assisted whitening claims rather than treating either as guaranteed benefit.
