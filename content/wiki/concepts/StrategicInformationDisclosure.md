## Definition

Strategic information disclosure is the deliberate release of true or credible information to selected audiences because their reactions are more valuable than continued secrecy.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1169-2026-gai-zenme-shou-zhu-zishen-de-fuqi-lu-3dpvj0nbqhise2aqx-a3zi3kp|Hanji 1169]] presents [[DongZhaoLateHan|董昭]] advising [[CaoCao|曹操]] to outwardly accept [[SunQuan|孙权]]'s secrecy request while having the attack message delivered into both Fancheng and [[GuanYu|关羽]]'s camp. Disclosure is meant to reassure the besieged defenders, pressure Guan Yu to reconsider the siege, and keep Sun Quan and Guan Yu consuming one another's attention.

The mechanism differs from a simple leak. It identifies audiences, predicts distinct reactions, and uses a credible message to reshape a multi-actor decision field. Its value is conditional: disclosure can also reveal sources, damage trust, accelerate an opponent, or cause the sender's partner to revise cooperation.

## Key Claims

- Information value depends on what recipients do with it, not only on whether it remains secret.
- One disclosure can target different reactions across defenders, opponents, and temporary partners.
- True information can be used strategically without becoming deception, although the handling of a secrecy promise creates a trust cost.
- Timing and delivery channels determine whether disclosure changes morale, withdrawal, preparation, or alliance behavior.
- Short-term operational benefit must be weighed against source exposure and longer-term credibility.

## Evidence

### Multi-audience disclosure at Fancheng
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1169-2026-gai-zenme-shou-zhu-zishen-de-fuqi-lu-3dpvj0nbqhise2aqx-a3zi3kp|Hanji 1169]] attributes to Dong Zhao the plan to copy Sun Quan's message and send it into both Fancheng and Guan Yu's position so the same information changes morale and decision pressure on opposing sides.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The supplied episode is a structured popular-history summary and does not establish the precise wording, delivery, timing, or causal effect of the messages. Guan Yu's failure to withdraw may reflect confidence in Jiangling and Gong'an, objective value, communication limits, timing, or other constraints rather than disclosure failure alone. Breaking an explicit secrecy expectation may also reduce future cooperation even when it improves the immediate position.
