# Embodied Intelligence / 具身智能

Embodied intelligence is [[ep256-ai-shidai-ziyou-yizhi-hai-cunzai-ma-lk9aci8oqnwerk26jy683nbdddcx]]'s claim that intelligence is not only in the brain or language system; it is distributed through body, sensing, action, emotion, and physical-world feedback. [[TuMotuo|土摩托]] uses walking, grasping a cup, road-surface judgment, and the sense of motion in a vehicle to show that tasks humans treat as natural can be difficult for machines.

The source uses this to clarify the human-AI boundary. [[DeepBlue]] and [[AlphaGo]] demonstrate strong game intelligence, and current models can generate impressive text, but human intelligence is also shaped by pain, pleasure, fatigue, proprioception, movement, and bodily needs. That connects the episode to [[EmbodiedAI]], [[EmbodiedJudgment]], [[MindBodyUnion]], and [[WetStateHumanAgency]].

## Key Claims
- Human intelligence is not separable from evolved bodily capacities.
- Machine tasks that look high-level to humans can be easier than ordinary embodied action.
- Humanoid form is partly practical because human environments are built around human bodies, not because all intelligence must look human.
- AI replacement will be slower where tasks depend on living-body perception, force control, and situated judgment.
- Embodiment is also relevant to [[AIFreeWillRisk]] because goal-directed AI with action channels is riskier than a disembodied language interface.
