# Personalized Film Industrialization / 个性化电影工业

## Definition
Personalized film industrialization is the organization of finance, schedules, specialists, technology, performers, and preparation so that production scale preserves a distinctive authorial system instead of standardizing it.

## Current Synthesis
[[BiGan|毕赣]] frames industrialization as capacity whose use must be directed. [[LongDaysJourneyIntoNightFilm|《地球最后的夜晚》]] exposes a default throughput logic: time saved by the system tends to become more shooting, while the director may need the same time for reflection. [[ResurrectionBiGanFilm|《狂野时代》]] represents a later attempt to redirect that capacity toward distinct chapter aesthetics, extensive preparation, actor confidence, star casting, complex long takes, and whole-film editing.

This is not anti-industry romanticism. The source treats planning, equipment, specialist coordination, and recognizable actors as potentially valuable. The question is whether those resources enlarge the film's own language or make the work conform to a reusable production template.

## Key Claims
- Industrial efficiency does not determine whether saved time becomes throughput or reflection.
- A personal film at scale may require several production systems rather than one standardized visual template.
- Stars and authored cinema are compatible when performance fit and role demands align.
- Actor safety can be built through playback, evidence of effective work, preparation, and concrete adjustment.
- Editing remains a whole-work judgment even after expensive production creates attachment to individual material.
- Personalization requires capacity and constraint management, not the absence of organization.

## Evidence
- Throughput-reflection tension: [[daoyan-bigan-luoyonghao-qingxing-shenke-duyiwuer-de-zaomengzhe-lhsut9wu8n73dbdyhps50ymyf9wa]] records Bi's difficulty adapting to [[LongDaysJourneyIntoNightFilm|《地球最后的夜晚》]]'s formal production system.
- Multi-system production: [[daoyan-bigan-luoyonghao-qingxing-shenke-duyiwuer-de-zaomengzhe-lhsut9wu8n73dbdyhps50ymyf9wa]] describes [[ResurrectionBiGanFilm|《狂野时代》]] as effectively several films with separate aesthetics, research, styling, and equipment needs.
- Performance support: [[daoyan-bigan-luoyonghao-qingxing-shenke-duyiwuer-de-zaomengzhe-lhsut9wu8n73dbdyhps50ymyf9wa]] records playback for actors, concrete direction, advance environmental experience for a child performer, and first-choice star casting.
- Whole-film control: [[daoyan-bigan-luoyonghao-qingxing-shenke-duyiwuer-de-zaomengzhe-lhsut9wu8n73dbdyhps50ymyf9wa]] records removing locally impressive work when it fails the complete cut and defending a longer runtime as the current best form.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source gives the director's preferred production theory, not an independent account of whether the process was efficient, safe, equitable, or successful. Large budgets and elaborate systems can preserve personal expression while still creating commercial, labor, or access costs. Conversely, standardized practices can protect crews and clarify work rather than merely suppress individuality.

## What Changed
- Created the concept around the allocation of industrial capacity between standardized throughput and authored form.
