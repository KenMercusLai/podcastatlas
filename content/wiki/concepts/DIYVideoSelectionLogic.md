# DIY Video Selection Logic / DIY视频选题逻辑

## Definition
DIY video selection logic is a project filter that pairs a recognizable everyday need with a solution capable of producing an immediate visual spectacle.

## Current Synthesis
In [[HeTongxue|何同学]]'s account, ordinary frustration supplies the audience's entry point: missing a trash bin, failing at ring toss, or forgetting to erase a meeting-room whiteboard. Engineering then exaggerates the answer into a moving bin, a high-success launcher, or a machine that erases and redraws. Need provides recognition; spectacle provides payoff.

The resulting object is optimized for a video, not necessarily a market. It must generate a clear premise, visible transformation, build process, and satisfying test. Product usefulness, novelty, and manufacturability can matter, but they do not replace entertainment legibility.

## Key Claims
- Everyday need gives an engineered premise immediate audience recognition.
- Visual spectacle turns a practical solution into a shareable entertainment payoff.
- The best project serves the narrative arc of problem, build, failure, and reveal rather than product utility alone.
- Originality is scarce because a concept must satisfy need, spectacle, feasibility, and novelty at once.
- Discovering that another creator has already executed a similar idea can invalidate substantial sunk work.
- A video object may be licensed for production, but manufacturing fit is a separate judgment from content fit.

## Evidence
- Two-part rule: [[liprfske7d5elpqdhiorn-ztvxzh-liprfske7d5elpqdhiorn-ztvxzh]] explicitly identifies real-life need and visual spectacle as the studio's two strongest selection tests.
- Concrete transformations: [[liprfske7d5elpqdhiorn-ztvxzh-liprfske7d5elpqdhiorn-ztvxzh]] contrasts missed trash, ring toss, and whiteboard cleaning with moving, launching, erasing, and drawing machines.
- Scarcity and collision: [[liprfske7d5elpqdhiorn-ztvxzh-liprfske7d5elpqdhiorn-ztvxzh]] says qualifying ideas may appear only monthly and describes projects weakened when similar work is discovered elsewhere.
- Video-product boundary: [[liprfske7d5elpqdhiorn-ztvxzh-liprfske7d5elpqdhiorn-ztvxzh]] distinguishes building for a good-looking video from founding a manufacturing business, while allowing selective licensing.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The rule is a creator's retrospective method, not controlled evidence that the two traits reliably predict performance. Audience response can also depend on title, cover, personality, timing, editing, platform distribution, prior channel trust, and execution quality. “Need” may be comic or contrived rather than socially important.

## What Changed
- Created the need-plus-spectacle selection model.
- Separated content fit from product and manufacturing fit.
- Added novelty collision and idea scarcity as constraints on repeatable output.
