# What Over How Work Shift

What over how work shift is the AI-era movement from humans directly performing implementation steps toward humans defining goals, constraints, acceptance criteria, and judgment. [[jia-yangqing-wo-suo-jingli-de-rengongzhineng-yisi-dao-ai-dianfu-shijie-de-shunian-jubian-chuantai-shengdongjixi-s10e24-a3884ade-4669-4d5c-ab2e-f98aa580f429]] adds the concept through [[JiaYangqing|Jia Yangqing]]'s argument that AI increasingly handles the "how" while humans must get better at specifying the "what."

The concept is not a claim that humans disappear from work. It makes human responsibility more concentrated around problem framing, taste, validation, and accountability, linking it to [[HumanJudgmentUnderAI]], [[AICodingVerification]], [[AgentReliabilityVerification]], and [[AIOrganizationDesign]].

## Key Claims
- AI can take over more execution detail, especially in code generation and routine knowledge-work production.
- The human role shifts toward defining the task, desired result, constraints, and evaluation method.
- Teams that keep judging only the generated code miss the deeper question of whether the product outcome is correct.
- Hiring and review need process visibility: candidates or employees can use AI, but they should explain prompts, guidance, decisions, and their own judgment.
- The shift raises social trust questions because AI adoption affects how colleagues and the public interpret layoffs, productivity, and fairness.
