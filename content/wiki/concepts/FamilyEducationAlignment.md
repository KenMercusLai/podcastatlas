# Family Education Alignment / 家庭教育协同

## Definition
Family education alignment is the coordination of adult participation, decision responsibility, emotional regulation, and consistent boundaries so that a child is not required to navigate unresolved parental conflict about schooling and development.

## Current Synthesis
[[ep180-feidianxing-beijing-mama-de-foxi-jiwa-women-jiujing-xiang-peiyang-zenyang-de-haizi-ckwridoewbb3abaaaadgpbgf|EP180]] rejects both total delegation to one disengaged parent and rigid gender-role prescriptions. A family may have one primary coordinator or share the work, but both adults should remain connected to the child and teacher, understand the agreed bottom line, and avoid turning course selection into intense conflict in front of the child.

Alignment does not require identical opinions or one permanent decision-maker. Its purpose is to make responsibility legible, keep adult anxiety from becoming the child's coordination burden, and preserve room for revising choices as the child's needs become clearer.

## Key Claims
- Sustained participation matters more than assigning education work to a fixed gender role.
- A primary coordinator can reduce confusion when responsibility and review remain shared and visible.
- Adults need explicit agreement on non-negotiable boundaries and room for revisable preferences.
- Intense parental conflict in front of a child can make rules and expectations unstable.
- Emotional steadiness helps adults resist fear-driven escalation and marketing pressure.
- Alignment should remain responsive to the child's needs rather than becoming a united front for coercion.

## Evidence
- Participation: [[ep180-feidianxing-beijing-mama-de-foxi-jiwa-women-jiujing-xiang-peiyang-zenyang-de-haizi-ckwridoewbb3abaaaadgpbgf|EP180]] contrasts increasing father participation with cases where one parent refuses even basic school communication.
- Responsibility design: [[ep180-feidianxing-beijing-mama-de-foxi-jiwa-women-jiujing-xiang-peiyang-zenyang-de-haizi-ckwridoewbb3abaaaadgpbgf|EP180]] supports either a primary responsible adult or a jointly established bottom line for course and school decisions.
- Conflict boundary: [[ep180-feidianxing-beijing-mama-de-foxi-jiwa-women-jiujing-xiang-peiyang-zenyang-de-haizi-ckwridoewbb3abaaaadgpbgf|EP180]] advises parents not to stage intense education disputes in front of the child.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The framework comes from participant observation rather than comparative family research. Two-parent assumptions do not cover single parents, extended-family caregivers, separated households, or unsafe relationships, and visible agreement can conceal coercion. Alignment must not override the child's safety, disability needs, autonomy, or access to qualified support.

## What Changed
- Created a role-neutral coordination frame for family education decisions and conflict boundaries.
