## Definition

Infeasible public-works cancellation is the governance pattern where a costly infrastructure project is stopped after honest field assessment shows that it cannot be completed or would consume disproportionate labor and money. The positive act is not anti-infrastructure; it is refusing to keep spending lives and resources on a project that field evidence has made untenable.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 749 creates the concept through Deng Xun's inspection of the Hutuo/Shijiu canal project. The project is meant to solve transport problems, but field conditions make it impractical. Deng Xun reports that the work cannot be completed, and Zhangdi cancels the project.

The source emphasizes burden relief rather than mere budget trimming. Replacing the project with donkey-cart transport reportedly saves enormous annual expense and avoids large-scale deaths among forced laborers. This makes cancellation a responsible administrative decision when it is paired with an alternative logistics method.

The concept belongs beside broader labor-burden and transport-route pages, but it is narrower: the core claim is that honest feasibility review can turn stopping work into better governance.

## Key Claims

- Public works should be judged by field feasibility, not only by the administrative desire for a transport solution.
- Continuing an impossible project converts infrastructure ambition into labor death and fiscal waste.
- Honest local inspection matters because the center may not see technical constraints from the capital.
- Cancellation is strongest when paired with a workable substitute rather than simple abandonment.
- The pattern treats saved lives and reduced corvee burden as governance gains.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Hanji 749 does not provide a full engineering analysis of the canal route or a complete fiscal audit. The reported savings and death avoidance should be treated as source claims. The concept also does not imply that all difficult infrastructure should be stopped; it applies when field review shows the project is infeasible and a less destructive alternative exists.

## What Changed

- Created the concept from Deng Xun's Hanji 749 canal inspection and Zhangdi's cancellation.
- Connected infrastructure cancellation to burden relief rather than to simple anti-construction caution.
