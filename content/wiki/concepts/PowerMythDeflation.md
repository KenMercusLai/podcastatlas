# Power Myth Deflation / 权力神话拆解

Power myth deflation / 权力神话拆解 is [[77-ta-shi-ziyou-de-yegou-ta-shi-falaqi-685710187]]'s central reading of [[OrianaFallaci|Oriana Fallaci]]. The episode opens by asking whether powerful people are truly wiser or better than ordinary people, then uses Fallaci's answer to cut rulers down to scale: they may have more ambition and nerve, but their office does not make them sacred.

The concept begins in Fallaci's childhood under [[ItalianFascism|Italian fascism]], where propaganda tried to make leaders appear grand and inevitable. It continues through her interviews with [[MohammadRezaPahlavi|Mohammad Reza Pahlavi]] and [[RuhollahKhomeini|Ruhollah Khomeini]], her distrust of post-victory oppression, and her refusal to let [[WoBuXiangxinShenhua|《我不相信神话》]] turn her into a myth in reverse.

## Key Claims
- Political authority becomes more dangerous when people treat office, ideology, religion, revolution, or fame as proof of moral superiority.
- Demythologizing power does not mean denying courage or historical importance; it means refusing reverence as a substitute for judgment.
- Journalism can perform this deflation by asking concrete questions that make abstract authority answerable.
- The same method should apply to the journalist herself, otherwise anti-myth work becomes another heroic myth.
