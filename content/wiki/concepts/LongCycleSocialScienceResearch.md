# Long-Cycle Social-Science Research / 社会科学长周期研究

## Definition
Long-cycle social-science research is the extended process through which a question moves from literature and data feasibility to replication, analysis, draft, seminar feedback, revision, peer review, publication, or abandonment.

## Current Synthesis
The visible writing period is only one portion of research time. Durable work also requires identifying a question with explanatory reach, securing suitable data, reproducing known findings, testing assumptions, interpreting results, exposing claims to criticism, and deciding whether weak projects should continue. A four- or five-year publication cycle therefore should not be confused with every idea consuming that long or with uninterrupted progress toward a guaranteed paper.

## Key Claims
- Question quality depends on novelty, depth, data availability, method feasibility, and explanatory reach beyond one transient sample.
- Replication can train data handling and validate code before a researcher attempts a novel claim.
- Drafting may take months while seminars, feedback, revision, journal review, and resubmission extend the complete cycle to years.
- Failed or shallow projects are informative but should sometimes be stopped rather than protected by sunk-cost reasoning.
- Disagreement is normal in social science because rival theories can organize the same domain without one result producing universal assent.

## Evidence
- Research pipeline: [[ep170-yige-feidianxing-jinrong-nvboshi-de-zishu-ckwriueey8kfabaaaacln4fb]] separates topic discussion, data acquisition, initial testing, draft writing, conference feedback, revision, and journal publication.
- Replication and data quality: [[ep170-yige-feidianxing-jinrong-nvboshi-de-zishu-ckwriueey8kfabaaaacln4fb]] explains the use of mature U.S. mutual-fund literature and detailed data to check code and research method.
- Project filtering: [[ep170-yige-feidianxing-jinrong-nvboshi-de-zishu-ckwriueey8kfabaaaacln4fb]] notes that some projects are abandoned after one or two years because results, data, or question depth do not justify continuation.
- Contestability: [[ep170-yige-feidianxing-jinrong-nvboshi-de-zishu-ckwriueey8kfabaaaacln4fb]] uses efficient-market and behavioral-finance perspectives to illustrate continuing theoretical disagreement.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Cycle length varies by discipline, project, dataset, team, journal, and quality threshold. The episode's four- or five-year estimate concerns a good finance paper from idea to publication, not the time required for every project or a verified average across social science. Mature U.S. datasets support some questions while potentially narrowing geographic relevance.

## What Changed
- Distinguished drafting time from the complete research, feedback, review, and publication cycle.
- Made project abandonment part of research judgment rather than treating every stopped project as failure.
