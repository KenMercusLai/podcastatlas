# Migration Weaponization

Migration weaponization is the use or threatened use of cross-border migrant flows as diplomatic pressure. [[taken-littorally-spains-sudden-crisis-in-ceuta-6a70712c034f16a52ebfaed7]] develops the concept through [[Ceuta]], where thousands of people crossed from [[Morocco]] into [[Spain]] after a court ruling was read as limiting immediate returns.

The source keeps the current event partly uncertain: [[MichaelReid]] says the big question is what role Moroccan authorities played. But it grounds the concept in a clearer 2021 precedent, when Morocco reportedly told border guards to step aside after Spain admitted [[BrahimGhali]] of the [[PolisarioFront]] for medical treatment.

## Key Claims
- Migration can become a pressure instrument when a state can relax or tighten border cooperation.
- The tactic works because it creates humanitarian strain, domestic political pressure, and EU-level institutional conflict at the same time.
- It is most powerful where legal ambiguity, weak border infrastructure, and public attention converge.
- The concept should not erase migrant agency or suffering; the source treats the crossing as both a humanitarian emergency and a state-pressure problem.
