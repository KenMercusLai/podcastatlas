# Commercial Film Audience Logic / 商业片观众逻辑

## Definition
Commercial film audience logic / 商业片观众逻辑 is the view that a film's professional obligation is to attract paying viewers through genre payoff, emotion, star use, pacing, and risk control.

## Current Synthesis
王晶 gives the wiki a blunt commercial-film theory. He does not describe audience orientation as a compromise made after art fails; he treats it as the working standard of professional filmmaking. The ticket-buying audience, midnight-screening response, and long-term box-office record become evidence about whether a film did its job.

The concept does not deny craft or taste. Wang's gambling-film explanation shows that commercial logic still requires understanding what viewers actually want: they are not simply asking for cards or technical rules, but for revenge, comedy, winning and losing, star charisma, and emotional release. Commercial logic therefore includes interpretation, not only calculation.

A critic-side version of the same boundary now matters. A commercial film can be politically thin yet still work for many viewers if its story, performance, pacing, and emotional causality are persuasive. In [[WelcomeToLailongRestaurant|《欢迎来龙餐馆》]], external event logic can be strained while relationship setup and payoff remain effective; in [[KongqiangFilm|《空枪》]], genre energy and affective anti-capital cues can carry audience response even when the historical and structural analysis is simplified.

## Key Claims
- Audience payment is treated as a legitimate evaluative signal, not merely a low-status market metric.
- Genre surfaces matter less than the emotional payoff viewers seek from them.
- Commercial film work includes controlling cost and downside as well as chasing upside.
- Fast production can be a trained competence when deadlines, stars, scripts, and audience response are integrated.
- Critical labels such as "bad film" can hide incompatible evaluation standards rather than settle quality.
- Commercial logic can coexist with selective support for more art-oriented films, but it ranks sustainability first.
- Political or historical complexity is not the only commercial test; viewers may accept a film when emotional causality and genre satisfaction are strong.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The Wang Jing source is a practitioner's defense of his own commercial practice, so it can underweight artistic, labor, gender, political, or long-term cultural criticisms. The 电影巨辩 source adds that audience satisfaction does not settle whether a film has adequately handled political or historical complexity. Audience response is evidence of demand and emotional functioning, not a complete account of value.

## What Changed
- Added the critic-side distinction between emotional effectiveness and political adequacy.
- Created the concept to preserve Wang Jing's commercial-film theory without flattening it into anti-art rhetoric.
