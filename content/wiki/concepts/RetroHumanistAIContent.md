# Retro Humanist AI Content

## Definition
Retro humanist AI content is [[ChenAnni]]'s term for judging AI-era creative work by whether it moves people, rather than by whether it visibly uses the newest AI technique.

## Current Synthesis
The current synthesis is source-scoped to one interview. Chen's position is not anti-AI: she is building [[LIVO]] as an AI-native product. The humanist qualification is that AI is a carrier, mirror, and tool whose value depends on the creator's intention, worldview, theme, character appeal, and desire to affect another person. This is a counterweight to treating AI content as a technical novelty or traffic shortcut.

## Key Claims
- AI can create new content carriers without removing the need for human creative intention.
- The core of a story remains the creator's will to move someone, not the visible amount of AI usage.
- For existing comics, forcing AI use can be unnecessary if the technology does not improve the work's emotional or creative core.
- AI-generated characters and worlds still need human choices about worldview, theme, values, and character charm.
- AI may amplify the user's or creator's inner orientation, so weak intention can produce shallow AI content even with strong tools.

## Evidence
- New carrier without replacement: [[ta-beipian-guo-beijiu-guo-beipeng-shang-shentan-guo-beitengtong-jidao-guo-jueding-zai-ai-shidai-jixu-maoxian-duitan-chen-anni-lomvn15fklk80bhkreied1iotuof]] has Chen compare AI to electricity enabling film while arguing creators still matter.
- Emotional core: [[ta-beipian-guo-beijiu-guo-beipeng-shang-shentan-guo-beitengtong-jidao-guo-jueding-zai-ai-shidai-jixu-maoxian-duitan-chen-anni-lomvn15fklk80bhkreied1iotuof]] recounts her realization that early comics worked because she wanted to make the person across the screen laugh or feel moved.
- Comics boundary: [[ta-beipian-guo-beijiu-guo-beipeng-shang-shentan-guo-beitengtong-jidao-guo-jueding-zai-ai-shidai-jixu-maoxian-duitan-chen-anni-lomvn15fklk80bhkreied1iotuof]] says Kuaikan's comics side does not force heavy AI use because current AI is not yet strong at the innermost creative layer.
- Human choices in worlds: [[ta-beipian-guo-beijiu-guo-beipeng-shang-shentan-guo-beitengtong-jidao-guo-jueding-zai-ai-shidai-jixu-maoxian-duitan-chen-anni-lomvn15fklk80bhkreied1iotuof]] says future agent-performed life dramas still need creator will to define worldview, theme, values, and character appeal.
- Mirror metaphor: [[ta-beipian-guo-beijiu-guo-beipeng-shang-shentan-guo-beitengtong-jidao-guo-jueding-zai-ai-shidai-jixu-maoxian-duitan-chen-anni-lomvn15fklk80bhkreied1iotuof]] frames AI as a mirror without its own inclination, reflecting what the user or creator brings.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not settle whether AI will eventually handle more of the creative core. The concept should be kept as Chen's normative creative standard, not a general proof that AI cannot generate high-quality story ideas.
