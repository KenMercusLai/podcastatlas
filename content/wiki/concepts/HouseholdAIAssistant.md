# Household AI Assistant

## Definition
A household AI assistant is an ambient, shared-space AI system designed to remain available across home devices and reduce domestic coordination work through conversation, context, service access, and task execution.

## Current Synthesis
The Marketplace Tech source presents [[AlexaPlus|Alexa+]] as a household assistant whose value comes from joining generative conversation to calendars, lists, reservations, tickets, shopping, entertainment, and purpose-built Echo hardware. The category differs from a browser chatbot because the assistant is meant to be immediately available in different rooms and to carry responsibilities through to completion.

That integration also raises unresolved boundaries. A system that acts inside a shared home may affect multiple household members, purchases, schedules, private routines, and third-party services. The source advocates the convenience case but does not evaluate recognition errors, household identity, permissions, privacy, reliability, or recourse.

## Key Claims
- Household usefulness depends on immediate availability and repeated integration into domestic routines, not only answer quality.
- Purpose-built devices can fit bedside, living-room, entertainment, and other household contexts better than one generic screen.
- End-to-end service access distinguishes an action-completing household assistant from a conversational smart speaker.
- Installed hardware and smart-home operating experience can create an advantage, but they do not prove reliable autonomous execution.
- Expansion into earbuds and glasses can turn a home assistant into a cross-location personal system.
- Shared-space assistants need identity, permission, privacy, confirmation, and recovery rules that the source does not resolve.

## Evidence
- Household integration: [[tech-20260928-0928-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260928-0928-mp-tech-pod-128]] describes Echo devices, calendars, lists, reservations, shopping, tickets, and generated media as parts of Alexa+'s home role.
- Hardware fit: [[tech-20260928-0928-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260928-0928-mp-tech-pod-128]] records Amazon's claim that bedside clocks, living-room systems, and other purpose-built forms reflect a decade of home experience.
- Cross-location direction: [[tech-20260928-0928-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260928-0928-mp-tech-pod-128]] points to Alexa-enabled earbuds, glasses, and ambient memory technology as extensions beyond the home.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is an interview with an Amazon executive rather than a comparative deployment study. It does not establish that Alexa+ completes tasks accurately or safely, that installed hardware creates a durable moat, or that users want an assistant to follow them outside the home. Multi-user identity, child access, bystander consent, purchases, account security, failure recovery, and data retention remain unexamined.

- Created the concept to distinguish home-integrated, shared-space assistance from generic chatbots and individual wearable interfaces.
