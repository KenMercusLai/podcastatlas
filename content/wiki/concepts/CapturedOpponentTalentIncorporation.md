# 降敌任用式立威 / Captured Opponent Talent Incorporation

降敌任用式立威 / captured opponent talent incorporation is the leadership pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1028-xiaobawang-sunce-liwei-chengba-jiangdong-lgur-3fmpr55wy3aji-0rm38y7x|Hanji 1028]] draws from [[SunCe|孙策]]'s treatment of [[ZuLangLateHan|祖郎]] and [[TaishiCi|太史慈]]. A ruler first proves coercive capacity by capturing dangerous opponents, then converts that victory into broader authority by releasing, appointing, and visibly including them.

The source gives two paired cases. Zu Lang had earlier attacked Sun Ce near [[JingCountyLateHan|泾县]], and later receives a seal from [[YuanShu|袁术]] to stir [[Shanyue|山越]] resistance. Sun Ce captures him at Lingyang, sets aside the old grievance, releases him, and appoints him as `门下贼曹`. Taishi Ci has enough local support to become Sun Ce's remaining Jiangdong opponent after [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]] flees, but Sun Ce also releases him after capture and appoints him as `门下督`.

This differs from [[OfficeBasedRebelIncorporation|授官式招安]], where a weak court grants office because it cannot suppress a force. Here the appointment follows victory. It also differs from [[FormerEnemyLoyaltyReframing|旧敌忠义重释]], which morally reclassifies service to a defeated lord; Hanji 1028 emphasizes the victor's ability to turn captured rivals into visible proof of magnanimity and usable manpower.

## Key Claims
- Capturing the opponent first matters: clemency is persuasive because the power hierarchy has already been made clear.
- Releasing and appointing a former opponent can advertise that the new regime values ability more than private revenge.
- The move can stabilize local forces when named leaders bring followers, reputation, and territorial knowledge with them.
- The pattern is risky if the ruler misreads character or cannot actually control the incorporated actor.
- The episode treats Sun Ce's strength as the combination of force, restraint, and people-use, not as courage alone.
