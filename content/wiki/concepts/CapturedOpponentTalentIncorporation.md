## Definition

降敌任用式立威 / captured opponent talent incorporation is the leadership pattern in which a victor proves coercive capacity first, then converts the defeated side's usable people into public evidence of authority, restraint, and talent recognition.

## Current Synthesis

Hanji 1028 gives the cleanest model through [[SunCe|孙策]]. [[ZuLangLateHan|祖郎]] had earlier attacked Sun Ce near [[JingCountyLateHan|泾县]] and later receives [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s seal to stir [[Shanyue|山越]] resistance. Sun Ce captures him at Lingyang, sets aside the old grievance, releases him, and appoints him as `门下贼曹`. [[TaishiCi|太史慈]] has enough local support to become Sun Ce's remaining Jiangdong opponent after [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]] flees, but Sun Ce also releases him after capture and appoints him as `门下督`. Placing both men visibly in the returning force turns private pardon into army-facing political theater.

Hanji 1026 adds a harder Cao Cao variant after [[Xiapi|下邳]]. [[CaoCao|曹操]] executes [[LyuBu|吕布]], [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]], and [[GaoShun|高顺]], but incorporates [[ZhangLiao|张辽]], treats [[ChenJiLateHan|陈纪]] and [[ChenQun|陈群]] politely, and uses [[ZhangBaLateHan|张霸]] to settle local forces including former defectors [[XuXiLateHan|徐翕]] and [[MaoHuiLateHan|毛晖]]. This overlaps with [[StrategicDefectorIncorporation|纳叛安抚式整合]] because the security problem includes former betrayal, not only captured opposition.

Hanji 1069 extends the concept from military captives to hostile literary talent. [[ChenLinLateHan|陈琳]] had written [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s anti-Cao proclamation before the [[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]], attacking Cao Cao's family and legitimacy. After the Yuan camp collapses, Cao Cao pardons him and keeps using his writing. The captured talent is not a commander with troops but a reputational weapon; incorporating him shows that the victor can turn the enemy's sharpest rhetoric into new-regime capacity.

## Key Claims
- Capturing the opponent first matters: clemency is persuasive because the power hierarchy has already been made clear.
- Releasing and appointing a former opponent can advertise that the new regime values ability more than private revenge.
- The move can stabilize local forces when named leaders bring followers, reputation, and territorial knowledge with them.
- The concept includes non-military talent when a hostile writer, adviser, or symbolic figure can be converted into new-regime capacity.
- The pattern is risky if the ruler misreads character or cannot actually control the incorporated actor.
- It differs from weak accommodation: the appointment follows victory and is meant to display the victor's discretion.

## Evidence

- Sun Ce and Zu Lang: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1028-xiaobawang-sunce-liwei-chengba-jiangdong-lgur-3fmpr55wy3aji-0rm38y7x|Hanji 1028]] says Sun Ce captures Zu Lang, sets aside an earlier grievance, releases him, and appoints him after Yuan Shu had tried to use him against Sun Ce.
- Sun Ce and Taishi Ci: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1028-xiaobawang-sunce-liwei-chengba-jiangdong-lgur-3fmpr55wy3aji-0rm38y7x|Hanji 1028]] says Sun Ce captures Taishi Ci, praises his courage and loyalty, appoints him, and displays both incorporated opponents at the head of the returning force.
- Xiapi settlement: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1026-jiemi-sanguo-di-yi-mengjiang-lvbu-de-luomu-lhjf-agujuliglqchumtff3tftxg1|Hanji 1026]] contrasts Cao Cao's execution of untrustworthy or non-reusable leaders with his appointment and polite handling of Zhang Liao, Chen Ji, Chen Qun, Zhang Ba, and local forces.
- Former-defector complication: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1026-jiemi-sanguo-di-yi-mengjiang-lvbu-de-luomu-lhjf-agujuliglqchumtff3tftxg1|Hanji 1026]] says Cao Cao praises Zhang Ba's protection of former defectors Xu Xi and Mao Hui and employs them, making surrender survivable enough to pacify the area.
- Chen Lin's hostile talent: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1069-chenlin-taocao-xiwen-mingzhen-tianxia-weihe-zuizhong-touxiang-caocao-lh9uzrzkgcaz45h-winquox-7jxf|Hanji 1069]] says Cao Cao pardons Chen Lin after the anti-Cao proclamation and continues using his official writing skill.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept does not claim that every former opponent should be spared. Hanji 1026 keeps Lü Bu, Chen Gong, and Gao Shun outside the incorporation set, showing that trustworthiness, utility, and political risk still constrain pardon. It also differs from [[OfficeBasedRebelIncorporation|授官式招安]], where office may be granted from weakness before coercive victory, and from [[FormerEnemyLoyaltyReframing|旧敌忠义重释]], which focuses on morally reclassifying old-lord service rather than exploiting the incorporated actor's usable capacity.
