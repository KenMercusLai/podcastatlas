# Fate Change Through Virtue Practice / 积善修心式改命

Fate change through virtue practice / 积善修心式改命 is [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-161-caiyun-he-renji-guanxi-de-liangzi-jiuchan-4-lml7lpd3u9l7esryknmhoampgx6v|Hanji 161 part 4]]'s answer to whether destiny can be changed. The episode distinguishes inherited命数 from changeable命运, then uses [[YuanLiaofan|袁了凡]], [[YunguChanshi|云谷禅师]], and [[LiaofanSixun|《了凡四训》]] to argue that strong intention only matters when joined to修心,发心,积德行善, and sustained action.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-916-ming-hao-he-yun-hao-nage-geng-zhongyao-lmzudjq5zfkfsukmhdeiumesm9qu|Hanji 916]] adds a shorter reprise of the same fate-change vocabulary before the late-Han court narrative. The host separates fixed "命" from adjustable "运", then links self-knowledge, action, faith, classics, [[ZengGuofan|曾国藩]], [[InamoriKazuo|稻盛和夫]], and [[LiaofanSixun|《了凡四训》]] to the claim that people should work on the part of fate that can be moved.

The concept extends the earlier [[QuantumEntanglementFateMetaphor|量子纠缠命运隐喻]] branch. Parts 1-3 described fate as relational, emotional, meditative, and cosmological; part 4 makes the method more explicitly moral and practical. Fate is changed by becoming a different kind of actor inside the network, not by merely wishing for money, status, children, or longevity.

The wiki keeps the concept source-scoped. Its practical value is that repeated conduct, speech, attention, and decisions can change relationships and opportunities. The stronger metaphysical claim that virtue directly rewrites fate is stored as the host's worldview, not as a general empirical rule.

## Key Claims
- 命数 and 命运 are separated: inherited conditions are not treated as the final verdict.
- Hanji 916 reprises the distinction between fixed inherited conditions and adjustable fortune, with action and self-knowledge as the practical hinge.
- Intention must be paired with physical practice and accumulated good action.
- Fate change is presented as moral formation rather than a quick technique.
- The source uses Yuan Liaofan's story to connect traditional text, Buddhist instruction, wealth, children, office, and longevity.
- Later reception through Zeng Guofan and Inamori Kazuo makes the frame useful beyond private spirituality.
