## Definition

Chicken-bone divination / 鸡骨占卜 is the episode's ritual system for producing and interpreting omen patterns from a specially prepared chicken bone and inserted bamboo pieces after prayer, sacrifice, offering, and worship. It is recorded here as a historical and ethnographic claim, not as a validated predictive method.

## Current Synthesis

The supplied source presents chicken-bone divination as a chain linking animal selection, sacrificial handling, communal worship, bone preparation, material pattern formation, specialist observation, and textual interpretation. Its wiki significance is procedural: the authority of the result comes from the whole ritual sequence and learned interpretive tradition, not from an isolated bone. The source also connects local Yue practice to Han Wudi's court and describes a later oral-to-written transmission history involving Chinese and old Zhuang scripts.

## Key Claims

- The omen is produced through a staged sacrificial procedure rather than by casually inspecting a leftover bone.
- Material preparation and bamboo insertion create the form treated as the divinatory sign.
- Specialist interpretation depends on both observation and comparison with a transmitted text.
- Oral knowledge can become a durable textual corpus when bilingual or biscriptal recorders codify procedure and judgments.
- Court adoption can relocate a regional ritual into an imperial sacrificial setting without erasing its local transmission history.

## Evidence

- Ritual production: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-364-1-yong-jigutou-ce-jixiong-de-mimi-liynaii4kc2v1-d_ndckhnxbaswg|Hanji 364-1]] describes selection of a healthy rooster, prayer, killing, bloodied feathers, prepared offering, recitation, and group worship before the bone is read.
- Material and interpretive procedure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-364-1-yong-jigutou-ce-jixiong-de-mimi-liynaii4kc2v1-d_ndckhnxbaswg|Hanji 364-1]] says a hip bone is cleaned to expose cavities, sharpened bamboo pieces are inserted, and the resulting form is repeatedly inspected and compared with a divination text.
- Transmission and movement: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-364-1-yong-jigutou-ce-jixiong-de-mimi-liynaii4kc2v1-d_ndckhnxbaswg|Hanji 364-1]] links the practice to Yue specialists at Han Wudi's court and says later writers using Chinese and old Zhuang scripts recorded formerly oral manuals, with a surviving corpus in Guangxi and Yunnan.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source does not demonstrate predictive validity, provide the underlying manuals, define the omen taxonomy, or document the claimed corpus sufficiently for independent verification. Its anatomy, live-spider reference, dating, regional distribution, script history, and count of more than sixty manuals remain source-scoped. [[OracleBoneDivination|甲骨占卜]] is related through the use of animal bone and specialist interpretation but is not evidence that the two systems share one origin or method.

- Separated procedural description from claims of supernatural efficacy.
