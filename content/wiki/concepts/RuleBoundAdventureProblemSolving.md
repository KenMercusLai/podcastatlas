# Rule-Bound Adventure Problem Solving / 规则约束式冒险解题

## Definition

Rule-bound adventure problem solving creates suspense by giving a character several interacting constraints, incomplete knowledge and limited time, then making the solution depend on interpretation and improvisation rather than force alone.

## Current Synthesis

The bounded [[LonglingMazeGhostBlowsOutTheLight|《龙岭迷窟》]] discussion centers the pattern in [[ZheguShao|鹧鸪哨]]'s initiation test. He must recover burial clothing while observing 摸金 rules involving a candle and cockcrow; an unstable corpse, wind and a cat then create new hazards. Shooting down a roof tile to shield the flame and using sound and marksmanship to redirect the cat preserve several constraints at once.

The sequence works because the rules are neither decoration nor a single password. Their collision creates ambiguity over whether success occurred before or after the prohibited threshold, and the character's truthful report becomes part of the evaluation. A strong rule-bound scene therefore tests judgment and character as well as technical skill.

## Key Claims

- Multiple constraints create richer suspense when satisfying one can endanger another.
- Time limits turn interpretation into action before complete certainty is available.
- Improvisation feels earned when it recombines established tools, environment and rules.
- Ambiguous compliance can reveal character through confession, concealment or argument.
- Force remains useful but does not replace understanding the constraint system.

### Improvised compliance

- [[071-quhua-gui-chuideng-zhi-qianchen-wangshi-banshan-daoren-zhegu-shao-lousokjtb96twcfuz1tros-vkgcf]] shows 鹧鸪哨 using a dislodged roof tile, ventriloquism and shooting to keep operating inside the rules.

### Character judgment

- [[071-quhua-gui-chuideng-zhi-qianchen-wangshi-banshan-daoren-zhegu-shao-lousokjtb96twcfuz1tros-vkgcf]] makes his candid report of near-simultaneous cockcrow, candle failure and retrieval part of why 了尘长老 accepts him.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept currently rests on one podcast's reading of one fictional test. Real ritual, occupational or tomb-raiding rules are not verified by the source. Constraint density alone does not guarantee quality: arbitrary rules, retroactive exceptions or tools introduced only at the moment of escape can make a puzzle feel authorially imposed rather than solvable.
