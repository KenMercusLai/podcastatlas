# Revolutionary Martyrdom Image-Making / 革命殉道图像制造

## Definition

Revolutionary martyrdom image-making is the political transformation of a dead activist or leader into a purified victim whose image, body, relics, rituals, and memory authorize collective loyalty and vengeance.

## Current Synthesis

Marat's afterlife shows that martyr-making exceeded a single famous painting. [[JacquesLouisDavid|Jacques-Louis David]]'s [[DeathOfMaratPainting|The Death of Marat]] removed bodily disease, violence, and the assassin from view, while the funeral, preserved heart, hair, commemorative objects, devotional language, hairstyles, and renamed towns created a wider civic cult. Roman spectacle and Christian forms supplied a sacred grammar even to anti-clerical revolutionaries.

## Key Claims

- Martyr images purify contested actors by selecting which bodily and political details remain visible.
- Omission denies excluded actors motive, dignity, and moral complexity.
- Funeral ritual and relics can extend visual propaganda into repeated social practice.
- Secular revolutionary movements may reuse religious forms of sainthood, sacred heart, sacrifice, and devotion.
- Martyrdom can make vengeance appear morally necessary and convert assassination into political escalation.
- Counter-images can reopen judgment by restoring the excluded actor to view.

## Evidence

### Painting and omission

- [[125-mala-zhisi-beibi-de-ansha-huo-zhengyi-de-chujue-783972516]] shows David making Marat clean, calm, charitable, and saint-like while excluding Corday.

### Funeral, body, and relic culture

- [[707-the-terror-an-assassination-in-paris-part-1-glt6580043397]] adds David's Roman-style funeral, the practical problem of Marat's decomposing body, the preserved heart, hair, objects, hairstyles, renamed places, and explicit comparison to Jesus and a god.

### Political consequence

- [[707-the-terror-an-assassination-in-paris-part-1-glt6580043397]] connects the cult to demands for vengeance, intensified conspiracy fear, and harsher treatment of Girondins.

### Counter-memory

- [[125-mala-zhisi-beibi-de-ansha-huo-zhengyi-de-chujue-783972516]] describes later images that restore Corday as visible, composed, and politically serious.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

- The concept does not reduce David's painting or revolutionary ritual to propaganda alone; aesthetic force and political simplification can coexist.
- The sources establish a Marat case, not a universal claim that all revolutionary funerals or relics have the same effect.
- Christian resemblance may reflect inherited cultural forms without implying theological continuity.

## What Changed

- The concept now spans painting, funeral spectacle, bodily display, relics, devotional language, and popular commemoration.
- Martyr-making is linked directly to political escalation after assassination.
- Roman and Christian symbolic repertoires are integrated into the current synthesis.
