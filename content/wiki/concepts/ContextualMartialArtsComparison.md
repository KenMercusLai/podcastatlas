## Definition

Contextual martial-arts comparison / 情境化武功比较 evaluates fictional fighting systems through interacting conditions—speed, internal power, technique range, tactical information, contact requirements, aptitude, bodily cost, and practitioner judgment—rather than assuming one universal ranking.

## Current Synthesis

The [[ljyhr2aom69hckwuywi4czovrhcu|纸醉金迷FM Top 3 roundtable]] compares the Nine Yin and Nine Yang manuals, Northern Darkness Divine Skill, Sunflower Manual, Dugu Nine Swords, and the achievements associated with Dugu Qiubai, Zhang Sanfeng, and the Xiaoyao tradition. The disagreements reveal why “strongest” is underspecified. A technique may offer broad internal and external training, rapid speed, energy absorption, flaw recognition, or creative principles, yet each advantage carries conditions involving access, reaction time, prior cultivation, talent, self-harm, or the opponent's style.

The useful unit is therefore not a freestanding manual but a matchup among system, practitioner, situation, information, and cost. Hypothetical combinations can expose complementary strengths—speed may make a flaw-seeking sword method more usable—but they remain counterfactual reasoning rather than canonical outcomes. Comparative play is most rigorous when it states assumptions and preserves uncertainty instead of treating cross-novel feats as a common measurement scale.

## Key Claims

- “Strongest” must be decomposed into speed, power, versatility, defense, learning demands, activation conditions, and cost.
- A technique's ceiling cannot be separated from the practitioner's temperament, experience, creativity, physical condition, and tactical judgment.
- Matchup conditions matter: touch-dependent energy absorption, reaction-based countering, and speed dominance create different advantages against different opponents.
- Self-harm, rare aptitude, long cultivation, and narrow access are part of a system's evaluation rather than incidental footnotes.
- Cross-system combinations are useful thought experiments only when their assumptions and non-canonical status remain explicit.
- Cross-novel rankings organize interpretation and fandom debate; they do not establish a mechanically consistent fictional physics.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept is grounded in one entertainment-oriented roundtable and a supplied summary, not a complete edition-by-edition feat analysis. Named arts operate across different novels, narrative periods, and dramatic needs; descriptions, transmission completeness, and practitioner performance do not form a stable quantitative scale. The framework improves the clarity of comparison but cannot turn literary invention into a settled combat simulation.

## What Changed

- Created a multi-variable framework for comparing fictional martial systems without asserting a universal winner.
- Made prerequisites, matchup conditions, and bodily cost part of the strength judgment.
