## Definition

Conservative operations, aggressive product is a risk-allocation strategy that protects company survival and financing capacity while concentrating creative and technical ambition inside the product.

## Current Synthesis

The principle rejects both indiscriminate caution and company-wide gambling. Operating conservatism seeks runway, controlled obligations, ownership clarity, and resilience if a release underperforms. Product aggression then uses that safety margin to pursue a distinctive work rather than merely copying visible market trends.

In the source, the strategy emerges after expansion, multi-project incubation, hot-sector combinations, capital ambition, and a temporary retreat from large single-player development. It is therefore a learned separation between existential risk and creative risk, not a claim that ambitious products become safe.

## Key Claims

- Company survival risk and product ambition should be managed as different layers.
- Financing is useful when it extends creative runway without making external status the goal.
- Trend following does not substitute for internal capability, taste, or product coherence.
- Concentrating on one difficult product can be more rational than diffuse portfolio expansion for a creator-led studio.
- Self-publishing can preserve positioning and tone while adding execution and market responsibility.

## Evidence

### Learning from strategic drift

- [[156-xia-dui-ying-zhi-ren-ling-zhizuoren-liangqiwei-6-xiaoshi-fangtan-lvnhry4glcc3ut-6nmjn7qrmd-dan]] recounts expansion, incubated projects, fashionable genre combinations, and capital plans that did not automatically produce success.

### Financial buffer and ownership

- [[156-xia-dui-ying-zhi-ren-ling-zhizuoren-liangqiwei-6-xiaoshi-fangtan-lvnhry4glcc3ut-6nmjn7qrmd-dan]] reports profits used for share repurchases and later equity and convertible financing intended to control survival risk.

### Concentrated product risk

- [[156-xia-dui-ying-zhi-ren-ling-zhizuoren-liangqiwei-6-xiaoshi-fangtan-lvnhry4glcc3ut-6nmjn7qrmd-dan]] links the protected operating base to the ambitious development and self-publishing of 《影之刃零》.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The boundary between operating and product risk is porous: a delayed or failed flagship can still threaten reputation, staff, financing, and company survival. Reported funding, ownership, profitability, and risk control are founder claims without audited evidence in the supplied document. The principle may not transfer to studios with different portfolios, contracts, or capital structures.

## What Changed

- Established the concept as separation and concentration of risk rather than generic prudence.
- Added the qualification that a flagship product can transmit creative risk back into company survival.
