# Event Intent Matching

## Definition

Event intent matching is the use of a bounded event's participants, timing, location, declared goals, backgrounds, needs, and resources to recommend valuable introductions before or during attendance.

## Current Synthesis

The [[ArticulerAI|Articuler.ai]] case treats an event as a temporary solution to the cold-start and density problems of broad professional networking. People have already committed to a shared time and place, organizers can describe a target audience, and attendees often arrive seeking customers, investors, talent, collaborators, or peers. Matching can therefore connect an abstract profile to an immediate, low-friction meeting opportunity.

The source also separates this layer from ticketing and registration. Organizer-side invitation helps assemble the right room; attendee-side recommendations help people use it; existing platforms can continue handling payment, registration, and check-in. A successful match may increase both the value of attending and the behavioral evidence available for future recommendations.

## Key Claims

- Events create temporary local density that makes relationship recommendations more actionable than a sparse general social network.
- Declared intent adds information that a static résumé or public profile cannot supply by itself.
- Organizer-side audience recruitment and attendee-side introductions are linked but distinct products.
- Timing matters: recommendations shortly before an event can convert an abstract match into a feasible meeting.
- Matching can improve attendance motivation when registrants know that useful people are expected to be present.
- A narrow event wedge can let a young network create value before it has enough internal users for universal matching.

## Evidence

### Bounded density and intent
- [[lnvlrnkk-mvyhiy31eq-gw4qopje]] says attendees often arrive with explicit relationship goals yet cannot identify the relevant person nearby, while a shared event supplies place, time, and commitment.

### Two-sided event value
- [[lnvlrnkk-mvyhiy31eq-gw4qopje]] describes targeted organizer invitations followed by pre-event attendee recommendations and argues that useful introductions provide an additional reason to show up.

### Wedge before full network scale
- [[lnvlrnkk-mvyhiy31eq-gw4qopje]] presents each event as a small temporal and spatial network that can work before the company has broad platform density.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The source provides no controlled evidence that matching causes higher attendance, more valuable relationships, or durable business outcomes. Organizer criteria and public-profile inference can reproduce exclusion, stale information, popularity bias, and unwanted outreach. Declared goals may be strategic or incomplete, and a recommendation does not establish trust, compatibility, safety, or mutual value. The reported thresholds, conversion rates, and event outcomes remain source-scoped founder claims.

- Created a synthesis distinguishing event-bounded relationship matching from ticketing, registration, and general social networking.
