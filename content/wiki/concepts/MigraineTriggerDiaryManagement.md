# Migraine Trigger and Diary Management

## Definition
Migraine trigger and diary management uses structured longitudinal records to identify personal attack associations and test sustainable changes without assuming that every commonly reported trigger applies to everyone.

## Current Synthesis
[[vol-157-zui-touteng-de-wenti-jiushi-touteng-ft-dawu-shiye-banmajiang-lrkqhlcwhxomw_aegpd_rzco8vio|VOL.157]] describes a person's trigger pattern as a personal “switch.” Sleep loss, anxiety, stress, emotion, cold exposure, menstrual or other hormonal transitions, certain foods, and exercise may be relevant, but the same exposure can matter greatly for one person and not at all for another.

A headache diary turns memory into a record of timing, symptoms, meals, exposures, sleep, activity, medication, and possible hormonal context. The goal is not maximal avoidance: it is to find repeatable associations, discuss them with a clinician, and adjust exercise or daily habits around an individual's tolerable range.

## Key Claims
- Trigger lists are hypotheses, not universal rules.
- A diary can make attack timing, food, sleep, stress, activity, and medication patterns visible.
- Hormonal timing may be useful context for some patients but does not establish one cause for all women.
- Exercise can support general health while also triggering attacks at certain intensities for some people.
- Sustainable management adjusts exposures and thresholds rather than eliminating ordinary life indiscriminately.

## Evidence
- Individual variation: [[vol-157-zui-touteng-de-wenti-jiushi-touteng-ft-dawu-shiye-banmajiang-lrkqhlcwhxomw_aegpd_rzco8vio]] contrasts 薛医生's cold exposure trigger with food examples that do not affect her.
- Diary method: [[vol-157-zui-touteng-de-wenti-jiushi-touteng-ft-dawu-shiye-banmajiang-lrkqhlcwhxomw_aegpd_rzco8vio]] recommends recording attacks, meals, and exposures to identify repeatable personal associations.
- Adapted activity: [[vol-157-zui-touteng-de-wenti-jiushi-touteng-ft-dawu-shiye-banmajiang-lrkqhlcwhxomw_aegpd_rzco8vio]] recommends gradual or gentler activity when a particular exercise intensity repeatedly precedes typical attacks.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Temporal association does not by itself prove causation, and over-monitoring can encourage unnecessarily restrictive eating, movement, or daily routines. Pregnancy, lactation, menstrual association, perimenopause, frequent attacks, or changing neurologic symptoms require individualized clinical interpretation.
