# Female Friendship Recognition / 女性友谊的看见

Female friendship recognition is the source's frame for women helping one another name experience. In [[ep254-you-xiaoying-x-wenqi-x-du-sujuan-nvxing-zhe-yisheng-dou-zai-fanfu-xuexi-xuke-ziji-lm-w7ryj4zay6vldsrkadohj997d]], the speakers treat the friend figure in [[WoXukeFilm|《我许可》]], real-life girlfriends, and online women commenters as supports for [[SelfPermission]].

The concept is not only emotional comfort. For [[WenQi|文淇]], reading other women's expressions online gives her language and courage. For [[YouXiaoying|游晓颖]] and [[DuSujuan|杜素娟]], friendship is also a way to compare body experience, recognize pain, and stop treating private shame as private failure.

## Key Claims
- Friendship can work as recognition infrastructure: one woman's story lets another woman identify her own experience.
- Online women-to-women comments can extend friendship-like witness beyond intimate circles, especially for people who are less comfortable asking friends directly.
- Recognition supports [[SelfFeelingTrust]] because a felt discomfort becomes easier to believe when others can name similar patterns.
- The concept supports [[CommunicationBoundarySetting]]: being heard by women friends can make later refusal or asking more plausible.
