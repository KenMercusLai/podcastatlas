# Hard-Tech Direction Selection

Hard-tech direction selection is the founder judgment pattern shown by [[YaoSong]] in [[173-duihua-yao-song-shenjian-dongfang-kongjian-zaichufa-tiancai-shaonian-shinian-hou-1-173-1]]. The source does not treat his path from [[DeePhiTech]] to [[Orienspace]] to [[StridingAI]] as random curiosity. Yao says he looks for problems that matter to human development and where he can turn a technical origin into a product used by many people.

This concept sits between [[FounderRiskCalibration]], [[StartupTimingWindows]], and [[DeepTechProductFocus]]. A direction has to be important, technically reachable, commercially legible within a few years, and suited to the founder's operating strengths. It also has to leave enough fallback capacity that a failed attempt does not end the founder's ability to continue.

## Key Claims
- Direction choice can begin with social importance, but it must pass through market, engineering, and product timing.
- The founder's own motivation matters because hard-tech cycles are too long for prestige or default peer pressure to sustain the work.
- Post-exit exploration can be useful when it exposes the founder to multiple possible windows before choosing the next hard-tech problem.
- China-specific infrastructure needs can make a direction attractive even when global analogies are incomplete.
