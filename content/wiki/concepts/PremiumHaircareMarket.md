# Premium Haircare Market / 高端洗护市场

Premium haircare market / 高端洗护市场 is the source's category frame for hair-care growth moving away from basic cleaning and toward higher-priced, more specific, and more professional products. In [[meizhuang-jutou-jiti-dingshang-toufa-xihu-shengyi-weihe-you-re-qilai-1005860109]], the premium branch includes anti-hair-loss products, scalp repair, hair oils, damaged-hair repair after dyeing and perming, scent and decompression benefits, and professional salon brands.

The source does not say ordinary shampoo demand disappears. Its point is that shampoo volume is less attractive than categories where users see risk, fear stopping, need visible results, or trust a professional recommendation. This makes hair care a good example of [[ProductLedWillingnessToPay]] under appearance anxiety and repeated routine use.

## Key Claims
- Basic shampoo is stable but crowded; growth and pricing power come from more specific problems and higher-ticket routines.
- Anti-hair-loss products can hold price when consumers fear stopping, even though the source treats this as demand behavior rather than clinical validation.
- Hair oils combine function, sensory feel, scent, and finishing effect, making them easier to sell as both care and beauty.
- The episode expects polarization: high-end professional brands and mass-value products may both perform better than undifferentiated middle-price products.
- Category premiumization depends on credible problem definition, visible effect, trust channel, and repeat use.
