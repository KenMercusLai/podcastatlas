# Kaolin / 高岭土

Kaolin / 高岭土 is the porcelain clay term highlighted in [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]]. The episode says the name derives from [[GaolingVillage|高岭村]] near [[Jingdezhen]], making it a stronger case of Jingdezhen entering global vocabulary than the popular but weak "China from Changnan" story.

In the source, kaolin matters technically because it helped potters overcome the limits of porcelain stone alone. Its higher refractory quality made [[PorcelainStoneClayBinaryFormula]] possible, improving firing stability and expanding the scale and complexity of wares.
