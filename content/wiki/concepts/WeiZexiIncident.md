# 魏则西事件

## Definition
The Wei Zexi incident is a 2016 Chinese medical-trust case in which a patient found a promoted treatment route through [[Baidu]] search, received treatment associated with a hospital department and disputed claims, and died after his condition worsened.

## Current Synthesis
The two sources place the case at the intersection of paid search, unclear promotional labeling, public-hospital authority, outsourced or cooperative departments, private medical capital, and misleading treatment information. Its importance is systemic: users could interpret ranking and hospital branding as clinical validation while the commercial and regulatory chain remained difficult to see.

## Key Claims
- Wei's treatment route began with search, making ranking and ad disclosure part of the medical trust chain.
- Hospital branding lent authority to activity whose commercial cooperation was not legible to patients.
- The case connected platform responsibility to provider, hospital, and regulator responsibility rather than reducing failure to one actor.
- A joint investigation found excessive weight for bidding in commercial promotion and inadequate identification of promoted results.
- The hospital was found to have improper cooperative-department activity, false information, and misleading medical advertising.
- The case accelerated contraction in medical advertising and a more cautious regulatory period for internet healthcare.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- The sources establish a connected trust and regulatory failure; they do not show that every search result, private provider, public-hospital partnership, or Putian-linked institution used the same conduct.
- The broader episode cautions that “莆田系” is not one organization, so the case should be attributed to the actors and control relationships actually involved.
- The page summarizes source accounts and does not independently reproduce the full joint-investigation record or clinical file.

## What Changed
- The case is now situated within the longer history of department contracting and renamed cooperation structures.
- Responsibility is distributed across ranking, promotion, provider claims, hospital authority, and regulatory jurisdiction.
- The loose-network boundary prevents generalizing the case to every Putian-origin operator.
