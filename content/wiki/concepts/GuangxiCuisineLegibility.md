# Guangxi Cuisine Legibility / 广西菜可识别化

Guangxi cuisine legibility / 广西菜可识别化 is the source's account of why [[Guangxi|广西]] cuisine may be promising but still less clearly understood by outsiders. In [[ep245-yinian-yidu-xunwei-zhilv-jinnian-qu-chi-chuangyi-cai-li9wtavemokcckialbbj7unpmqsz]], [[WuLiwei|吴立伟]] treats it as closer to a "1.0" recognition stage: before high-concept innovation, restaurants and writers need to identify stable ingredients, dishes, and taste memories.

The episode uses 螺蛳粉, 柠檬鸭, 老友鱼, 啤酒鱼, and 爆炒生料 to show possible memory points. The distinction from [[GuizhouCuisineFlavorTransfer|Guizhou sour-spicy transfer]] is important: Guangxi sourness can come from several preserved ingredients at once, creating a sharper, cleaner, and more distributed acid profile.

## Key Claims
- A cuisine can have real depth while still lacking a clear external memory point.
- Luosifen made acid-spicy Guangxi more visible, but one dish cannot carry the whole province's culinary grammar.
- Lemon duck and old-friend flavor structures may be more useful entry points because they show preservation, acidity, aromatics, meat, and regional seasoning together.
- Legibility is a precondition for deeper creative cuisine: diners must first know what they are recognizing.
