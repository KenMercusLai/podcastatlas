# Cain Mark Reinterpretation / 该隐印记重释

[[CainMarkReinterpretation|Cain Mark Reinterpretation / 该隐印记重释]] is the first major interpretive shock in [[20-demian-gaiyin-de-etou-shang-na-shenmi-de-yinji-567302734]]'s reading of [[DemianNovel|《德米安》]]. After a Bible lesson on Cain and Abel, [[MaxDemian|德米安]] suggests that Cain's mark might not signal cowardly guilt. It may instead be a sign that others recognized strength, courage, and dangerous individuality.

The point is not to praise murder. The source treats the reinterpretation as an epistemic rupture: old stories may be true, but inherited explanations may have been written by the frightened or victorious. [[EmilSinclair|辛克莱尔]] is shaken because the idea makes the bright orthodox world less complete than he had believed.

## Key Claims
- Reinterpretation can expose power inside moral storytelling without automatically reversing morality.
- The mark becomes a sign of difference, fear, and recognition as much as a sign of punishment.
- The episode uses this moment to connect [[LightAndDarkWorldDuality]] to later [[AbraxasWholeGodSymbolism]].
