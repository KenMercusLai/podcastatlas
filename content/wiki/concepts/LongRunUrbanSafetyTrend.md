# Long-Run Urban Safety Trend

Long-run urban safety trend is the source's preference for comparing a city with itself over time rather than relying only on cross-city rankings. In [[spring-then-fall-a-weakened-muslim-brotherhood-6a7aef1b616564bf00d9b354]], the crime segment says many major cities have become safer over time even while social-media narratives can make them feel newly dangerous.

The episode's strongest examples are homicide and robbery trends. It says [[NewYorkCity|New York City's]] murder count has fallen by about 90% over 35 years, murder rates in London and New York are lower than at any time since record-keeping began, and personal robbery in Paris has nearly halved over the past decade.
