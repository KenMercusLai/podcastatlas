# Toy Design Human Creativity Boundary

## Definition
Toy design human creativity boundary is the product-design line where AI may support standardized, repetitive, or documentation work, while the central creative judgment remains assigned to human designers.

## Current Synthesis
The Lego segment in [[waimai-chengwei-haidilao-zengzhang-zuikuai-de-yewu-legao-biaoshi-buhui-yong-ai-lai-sheji-chanpin-1008870590]] makes the boundary explicit. [[Lego|乐高]] is investing in software and digital products, but its CEO frames AI as useful for standardized tasks such as instructions rather than independently designing products. This keeps creativity, taste, play pattern, and brand trust inside human design responsibility.

## Key Claims
- AI can assist standardized design-adjacent work without owning the core creative direction.
- Consumer-facing toy design carries emotional, tactile, and brand-trust judgments that are hard to reduce to automation.
- A public human-design boundary can protect brand meaning when customers expect craft, care, or play insight.
- The boundary is not anti-digital; Lego also increases software engineering and smart-brick investment.

## Evidence
- AI boundary: [[waimai-chengwei-haidilao-zengzhang-zuikuai-de-yewu-legao-biaoshi-buhui-yong-ai-lai-sheji-chanpin-1008870590]] says Lego's CEO positioned AI for standardized work such as instructions while reserving product creativity for designers.
- Digital investment: [[waimai-chengwei-haidilao-zengzhang-zuikuai-de-yewu-legao-biaoshi-buhui-yong-ai-lai-sheji-chanpin-1008870590]] says Lego expanded software engineering and introduced smart bricks with light and sound.
- Brand-growth context: [[waimai-chengwei-haidilao-zengzhang-zuikuai-de-yewu-legao-biaoshi-buhui-yong-ai-lai-sheji-chanpin-1008870590]] says Lego's first-half revenue reached a record, so the AI boundary is presented from a position of growth rather than distress.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not explain Lego's internal design workflow or prove that AI could not contribute earlier in ideation; it records the company's stated boundary.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to capture Lego's AI-assistance versus human-creativity distinction.
