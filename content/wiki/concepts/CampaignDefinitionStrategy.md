# 选战定义策略 / Campaign Definition Strategy

## Definition
Campaign definition strategy is the attempt to make an election turn on a contrast that both disadvantages an opponent and establishes a credible positive identity for one's own candidate.

## Current Synthesis
[[b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26-b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26mp3aid6af3219e1f5126ac85b049f0chid66b07190af99592]] describes elections as a “definition game,” but its central qualification is that a negative label needs issue salience, target fit, and a believable inverse. [[JiangWanan|蒋万安]] tried to define [[ShenBoyang|沈伯洋]] as socially divisive through recall politics and permissive on drugs through an old essay. The episode argues that neither attack controlled the contest: many swing voters may simply have been indifferent to the recall, and the drug argument's simplification of harm reduction drew professional criticism and forced Chiang to clarify his own position.

## Key Claims
- Effective definition joins an opponent's weakness to one's own credible positive contrast.
- An issue can energize core supporters without persuading voters who do not consider it salient.
- An attack transfers poorly when the target's public identity is not tied to the attacked issue.
- Reusing a successful attack form without its original context can produce weak results.
- Simplifying technical policy can redirect scrutiny toward the attacker and consume campaign time.
- Positive first-impression formation can outperform repeated rebuttal of an imposed label.

## Evidence
- Recall frame: [[b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26-b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26mp3aid6af3219e1f5126ac85b049f0chid66b07190af99592]] contrasts recall turnout with mayoral turnout and infers that many additional voters may lack a strong recall position.
- Missing positive contrast: [[b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26-b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26mp3aid6af3219e1f5126ac85b049f0chid66b07190af99592]] says Chiang and his party had not established an equally clear record of producing unity.
- Context mismatch: [[b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26-b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26mp3aid6af3219e1f5126ac85b049f0chid66b07190af99592]] contrasts the drug attack with a 2022 pandemic attack on a candidate whose identity was closely tied to pandemic management.
- Backfire and self-definition: [[b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26-b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26mp3aid6af3219e1f5126ac85b049f0chid66b07190af99592]] says professional criticism made Chiang explain methadone policy while Shen continued building a municipal and responsive image.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not experimentally identify campaign effects, and an attack can influence agenda, enthusiasm, or late undecided voters without moving topline favorability immediately. Its public-health interpretation is brief and does not provide a full account of drug treatment, decriminalization, or harm-reduction policy.

## What Changed
- Established issue salience, target fit, and positive inverse as three conditions of effective definition.
- Added technical-policy simplification as a route by which an attack can redirect scrutiny.
