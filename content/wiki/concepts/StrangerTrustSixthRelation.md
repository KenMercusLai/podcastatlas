# Stranger Trust Sixth Relation / 陌生人第六伦

Stranger trust sixth relation is the source's reading of [[LiLu|李璐]]'s proposal that modern [[China]] needs an ethical relation among strangers in addition to the traditional five relations. In [[150-zhongguo-ruhe-tiaochu-pinkun-xianjing-xiandaihua-zhi-lu-you-hui-zouxiang-hefang-lgpvp04rngagkpubte2ac6i5ji3g]], this "第六伦" centers honesty as a modern trust basis outside family, hierarchy, and familiar networks.

The concept links cultural renewal to modernization. Early development could use [[InformalPropertyRightsProtection]] and familiar networks, but a mature market and service society needs trust among strangers, professional roles, contracts, public speech, and institutions that do not depend on clan or hometown ties.

## Key Claims
- Familiar-network trust can start markets but cannot fully support modern scale.
- Stranger trust requires norms, institutions, and public ethics that extend beyond kinship or hierarchy.
- The source connects this to [[Mozi|墨子 / Mozi]] because Li Lu sees Mohist themes of individual value, universal care, justice, logic, and proto-scientific spirit as useful for a technological civilization.
- The concept is part of the episode's answer to [[ChineseModernizationCulturalGap]].
