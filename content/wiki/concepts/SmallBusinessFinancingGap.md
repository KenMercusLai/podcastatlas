# Small Business Financing Gap

Small business financing gap is the constraint illustrated by [[LariatAlhassan]] and [[LarkluxPaint]] in [[piles-of-cash-and-a-town-of-solutions-in-kenya-nigeria-summer-school]]. The episode argues that tiny firms can have customers and growth ideas while still being unable to access the capital needed for staff, equipment, records, premises, or credibility.

The source links this gap to [[AsymmetricInformation]]. Banks struggle to tell which one-person businesses are promising because many lack electronic records, spreadsheets, tax filings, collateral, or trusted legal enforcement. The result is a [[MissingMiddle]] pattern where many firms remain small and few become durable employers.

## Key Claims
- Lack of capital can trap firms below the scale where they can prove their own quality.
- Informal records and weak documentation make cash-flow lending hard.
- [[DirectEntrepreneurGrants]] can bypass early lending frictions, but do not solve follow-on financing.
- Stronger accountants, property rights, legal systems, and financial records are part of the long-run fix.
