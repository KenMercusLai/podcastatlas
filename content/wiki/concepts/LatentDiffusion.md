# Latent Diffusion

Latent diffusion appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-open-source-wins-agi-is-here-and-scorseses-ai-toolkit-with-ceos-of-cerebras-black-forest-labs-42029880]] through [[RobinRombach|Robin Rombach]]'s explanation of the [[StableDiffusion|Stable Diffusion]] and [[BlackForestLabs|Black Forest Labs]] lineage. He describes it as compressing natural data such as images, video, and audio into more efficient representations before training generative models.

The episode uses latent diffusion as the bridge from image generation to broader multimodal systems. Once visual data is represented more efficiently, the same family of techniques can support richer control layers, video generation, audio-visual modeling, and eventually action prediction.
