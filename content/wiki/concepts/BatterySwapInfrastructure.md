# Battery Swap Infrastructure / 换电体系

## Definition
Battery swap infrastructure is the physical network, compatible vehicle-and-pack standards, inventory, and operating system that lets an EV user exchange a depleted battery for a charged one instead of relying only on charging.

## Current Synthesis
The bounded sources make battery swapping both a service-model enabler and a network-utilization problem. [[NIO]]'s long investment supports [[EVBatteryLeasing]], reduces the visible upfront vehicle price, and creates an imitation barrier. The reported [[GeelyAutomobile|Geely]] partnership adds another route to value: outside capital and partner vehicles can spread fixed network costs across more users. Yet faster charging narrows swapping's pure time advantage, while interoperability, station density, pack inventory, capital cost, and realized utilization determine whether the network becomes a durable asset rather than an expensive differentiator.

## Key Claims
- Swapping turns battery condition from an individual-car anxiety into a network-managed asset question.
- The network supports [[EVBatteryLeasing]] because users can rent battery access rather than mentally own one aging pack.
- Swap infrastructure creates an imitation barrier because stations, battery standards, operations, capital, and user habits must be built before the financing model works.
- Partner automakers can raise network value by adding vehicle demand and capital without rebuilding a separate full network.
- Fast charging and swapping are competing but potentially parallel routes; the better fit depends on user scenario, density, utilization, compatibility, cost, and brand promise.

## Evidence
- Leasing and moat evidence: [[143-wei-xiao-li-de-gaoduan-tansuo-feng-tou-quan-1-143-1]] treats NIO's decade of swap investment as the physical prerequisite for battery leasing, lower upfront payment, residual-value management, and a model competitors cannot quickly copy.
- Partnership and utilization evidence: [[jingdong-kaichu-gouwuzhongxin-weilai-yu-jili-tuijin-chongdian-huandian-hezuo-1019779523]] reports that Geely Holding will acquire 30% of NIO Energy, Geely will develop consumer swap vehicles using its service, and NIO will invest in Geely's charging affiliate.
- Route-competition evidence: [[jingdong-kaichu-gouwuzhongxin-weilai-yu-jili-tuijin-chongdian-huandian-hezuo-1019779523]] says new fast-charging technology from Geely and [[BYD]] is reducing swapping's time advantage.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The partnership terms, valuation, vehicle plans, and reciprocal investment are source-scoped reports, not independently verified completed outcomes. More compatible vehicles can improve utilization only if customers adopt them, stations and packs are available where needed, and operational revenue covers capital and inventory costs. Faster charging does not make swapping obsolete, but swapping speed alone no longer establishes superiority.
