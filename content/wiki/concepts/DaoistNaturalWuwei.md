# Daoist Natural Wuwei / 道家自然无为

## Definition
Daoist natural wuwei / 道家自然无为 is the Hanji 456 Laozi-centered claim that human conduct should follow natural arising, decline, work, rest, and self-organization rather than be overdriven by moral display, anxious service, or rigid correction.

## Current Synthesis
The Hanji 456 sources use Laozi's replies to [[Confucius|孔子]] to distinguish living nature from proclaimed virtue. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-2-zhe-liangwei-shengren-de-lundao-cangzhe-renlei-de-zhongji-mimi-lsjl6awahc0gyhtwh3tagh1utqx4|Hanji 456-2]] starts from the Yellow River, life and death, and the claim that heaven, earth, sun, moon, stars, and living beings order themselves without forced command. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-3-rang-ni-chushi-gengshang-yige-jingjie-laozi-jiao-ni-qiangu-zhihui-llaynmqfl8wfwo7ckkacdxommu9|Hanji 456-3]] then pushes the critique toward forced renyi and moral display. Wuwei here means less passivity than non-forcing: do not keep calling the child back so insistently that the child runs farther away.

Hanji 244 adds a source-scoped official-conduct application through [[ZhiBuyiWesternHan|直不疑]]. The host attributes his continuity with inherited procedures, low visibility, reluctance to advertise merit, and [[AccusationResponseRestraint|restraint under accusation]] to Huang-Lao learning. This extends non-forcing from philosophical teaching into political demeanor, but it does not establish that every act of silence is Daoist, prudent, or historically caused by doctrine.

## Key Claims
- Natural wuwei is a critique of forced moralization rather than a license for indifference.
- The source treats human nature as something easily disturbed by over-insistent renyi language.
- Wuwei works through letting people and things follow their own patterns of action, rest, life, and death.
- The Yellow River teaching connects wuwei to being born from nature, dying into nature, and letting self-cultivation follow natural principle.
- The episode's child-running-away analogy makes coercive correction itself part of the problem.
- The concept belongs to philosophical self-cultivation, not to the wiki's ritual-technology or alchemical Daoist practice cluster.
- A Huang-Lao official style can express non-forcing through continuity, low visibility, and non-contention, while remaining dependent on institutional stakes.

- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-2-zhe-liangwei-shengren-de-lundao-cangzhe-renlei-de-zhongji-mimi-lsjl6awahc0gyhtwh3tagh1utqx4|Hanji 456-2]] uses heaven, earth, sun, moon, stars, and living beings to argue that order can arise without being artificially pushed.

Naturalness over moral display:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-3-rang-ni-chushi-gengshang-yige-jingjie-laozi-jiao-ni-qiangu-zhihui-llaynmqfl8wfwo7ckkacdxommu9|Hanji 456-3]] has Laozi ask whether renyi is human nature, then criticize universal-love and selflessness claims when they detach from violent social reality and hidden self-interest.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The Hanji 456 sources are interpretive retellings of Laozi instructing Confucius, not full historical or philological arguments about early Daoism. Hanji 244 adds a later biographical interpretation rather than proof of Zhi Buyi's motive. The concept should not be generalized into a rule that all moral language is harmful, that administrative continuity is always wise, or that silence under accusation is always safe.

## What Changed
- Added Zhi Buyi as a bounded Huang-Lao official-conduct application and separated non-forcing from universal advice to remain silent.
- Added Hanji 456-2's Yellow River, natural life-death, and self-ordering world examples as the first-meeting layer behind natural wuwei.
