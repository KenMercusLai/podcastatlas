# Quasi-Royal Qin Hegemony / 准天子式秦霸权

Quasi-royal Qin hegemony / 准天子式秦霸权 names the pattern in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-100-1-huangdi-zhong-de-chaochang-daiji-wang-guoshi-le-lt75scavlm8qjn6ulpd-b2kwzwjf]] where [[QinState|秦国]] has not yet founded an empire, but other states already behave as if [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] occupies a superior, near-royal position. The episode reads "天下来宾," Qin's punishment of late-arriving [[WeiState|魏国]], a tianzi-like sacrifice at old Qin Yong city, and [[HanHuanhuiwang|韩桓惠王]]'s personal mourning as signs that real hierarchy has outrun formal constitutional language.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-100-2-liuguo-jingran-tongshi-wei-ta-diaosang-ln6-5v-b-ybqmf5ucepusdo3lf68]] extends the same pattern by focusing on the funeral itself. The six states' senior mourning delegations and [[HanState|韩国]]'s "衰绖入吊" make Qin's practical superiority visible through [[PoliticalFuneral|political funeral]] behavior, while the discussion of "薨" after Zhou's collapse links Qin's status to a broader breakdown of the old [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]] hierarchy.

The concept extends [[PowerEtiquetteReading|权力礼仪细读]]. Military pressure matters, but the source asks the reader to notice ritual and timing: who attends, who is late, who mourns personally, and who has enough status to make negligence punishable. The result is a pre-imperial order in which [[QinEastwardPressure|秦国东进压力]] is no longer only battlefield expansion; it also produces visible diplomatic deference.

## Key Claims
- Qin's dominance becomes legible through attendance, mourning, sacrifice, and punitive reaction to protocol failure.
- Formal Zhou kingship has not been replaced, but the behavior of the lords increasingly treats Qin as the practical center.
- Ritual submission and military deterrence reinforce each other; neither by itself explains the episode's political atmosphere.
- Han's special deference shows how exposed geography can translate into unusually intense symbolic compliance.
- Qin Zhaoxiang Wang's death works as a hierarchy test: the mourning scene shows which states treat Qin as more than a peer.
