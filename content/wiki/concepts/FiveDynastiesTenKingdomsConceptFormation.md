# 五代十国概念形成 / Five Dynasties Ten Kingdoms Concept Formation

Five Dynasties Ten Kingdoms concept formation names [[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]]'s point that "五代十国" is not just a natural label copied from the period itself. The "五代" sequence is comparatively clear, but "十国" was stabilized by later history writing and political judgment.

The episode says early Song treatments could include more local regimes, while later formulas such as five, nine, or ten kingdoms reflected changing state interests, the status of frontier regions, post-Chanyuan political imagination, and大一统 assumptions. [[OuyangXiu|欧阳修]]'s [[NewFiveDynastiesHistory|《新五代史》]] is central to this canonical narrowing.
