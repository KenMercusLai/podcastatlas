# Provisional Creative Judgment

## Definition

Provisional creative judgment is the practice of expressing evaluations of work or ability as current, revisable assessments rather than timeless facts about the object or the self.

## Current Synthesis

In [[from-broken-record-rick-rubin-in-conversation-with-malcolm-gladwell-from-broken-record-rick-rubin-in-conversation-with|From Broken Record]], [[MalcolmGladwell]] proposes qualifying a negative response with “right now,” while [[RickRubin]] pairs inability with “yet.” Both formulations separate present evidence from a fixed verdict. The point is not compulsory positivity: a person may still dislike a work or lack a skill, but language can preserve the possibility that repeated exposure, context, practice, or changed attention will alter the judgment.

Rubin's Trouble Funk and Nine Inch Nails examples show why entry cost matters. Familiar work is easier to parse, whereas an unfamiliar genre may initially register as displacement or noise before its structure becomes audible. Provisional judgment creates time for learning without guaranteeing eventual appreciation.

## Key Claims

- A current response is evidence about the judge's present relation to a work, not necessarily the work's permanent value.
- Unfamiliar forms may require repeated exposure before their structure becomes intelligible.
- Adding a time boundary to inability preserves room for practice without pretending competence already exists.
- Multiple independent recommendations can justify renewed attention to a category a person normally avoids.
- Revisability supports curiosity but does not require indefinite exposure or eventual approval.

- Taste qualification - [[from-broken-record-rick-rubin-in-conversation-with-malcolm-gladwell-from-broken-record-rick-rubin-in-conversation-with]] records Gladwell's time-bounded alternative to declaring a work simply bad.
- Ability qualification - [[from-broken-record-rick-rubin-in-conversation-with-malcolm-gladwell-from-broken-record-rick-rubin-in-conversation-with]] records Rubin adding a future-facing boundary to a claim of inability.
- Delayed intelligibility - [[from-broken-record-rick-rubin-in-conversation-with-malcolm-gladwell-from-broken-record-rick-rubin-in-conversation-with]] uses Rubin's changing response to Trouble Funk and Nine Inch Nails as cases of rejection becoming appreciation.
- Recommendation signal - [[from-broken-record-rick-rubin-in-conversation-with-malcolm-gladwell-from-broken-record-rick-rubin-in-conversation-with]] says repeated unrelated recommendations can warrant attention despite category-level aversion.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

Some negative judgments remain stable and well informed, and unfamiliarity does not make every work valuable. Repeated exposure can create mere familiarity, waste attention, or normalize harmful material. Provisional language should improve calibration rather than prevent clear refusal, criticism, or recognition of present limits.

## What Changed

- Established a time-bounded judgment practice spanning both taste and ability.
- Distinguished curiosity-preserving revision from compulsory positivity or endless exposure.
