# Computer Vision Form Correction

Computer vision form correction is the use of cameras and AI models to evaluate exercise movement and provide feedback. In [[tech-20260204-0204-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260204-0204-mp-tech-pod-128]], [[NicoleNguyen]] identifies [[Peloton]]'s form feedback as the most impressive AI fitness capability she tested, especially for strength training.

The source also keeps the concept bounded. Peloton sometimes missed reps Nguyen believed she had completed correctly, and the better feedback depended on costly connected hardware and a subscription. The concept therefore extends [[AIFitnessCoaching]] by adding visual context, but it does not remove the need for user judgment, error tolerance, or human training when precision matters.
