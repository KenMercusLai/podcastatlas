# Internment Without Trial

Internment without trial is the source's term for imprisoning suspects without ordinary criminal trial. In [[699-the-troubles-return-of-the-ira-part-3-glt9519811642]], [[JamesChichesterClark]] initially resists the policy because intelligence is poor and unrest likely, while [[BrianFaulkner]] later presses [[EdwardHeath]] to approve it amid rising explosions, shootings, and unionist pressure.

The episode treats internment as a failed security shortcut. [[OperationDemetrius]] arrests 342 Catholics and no Protestants, uses stale [[RoyalUlsterConstabulary|RUC]] files, triggers violence and displacement, and feeds the [[ProvisionalIRA]] narrative that British rule is coercive and sectarian.

## Key Claims
- Emergency detention can destroy procedural legitimacy when intelligence is weak.
- One-sided application in a sectarian conflict can turn security policy into group punishment.
- Detention without trial can move a state from law enforcement into [[CounterinsurgencyLegitimacyTrap]].
