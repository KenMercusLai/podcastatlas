# Membership Retail

Membership retail is the model in [[costco-1]] where shoppers pay for access before buying goods. [[Fedco]], [[FedMart]], [[PriceClub]], and [[Costco]] use membership to pool buying power, restrict access, create recurring fee income, and change shopper psychology.

For Costco, membership turns low prices into a relationship rather than a one-off promotion. Members have a reason to return and recover the fee through savings, while the company earns a high-margin fee stream that supports lower merchandise markups.

## Key Claims
- Membership can convert price savings into habit because the customer has already paid to participate.
- A membership fee can select for customers with cash flow, storage space, and planned repeat shopping.
- The model only stays trusted if product prices remain visibly good; otherwise membership becomes another extraction layer.
- Executive membership adds a loyalty layer by linking higher fees to cash back and high annual spend.
