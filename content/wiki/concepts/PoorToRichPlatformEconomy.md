# Poor-to-Rich Platform Economy

## Definition
Poor-to-rich platform economy is [[ChetanBhagat]]'s “P2R” description of digital businesses that organize relatively inexpensive labor to deliver convenience to more affluent consumers.

## Current Synthesis
[[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-fe4d0dc018bd4482039d4a6798e610cf-124292559-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-fe4d0dc018bd4482039d4a6798e610cf-124292559]] does not claim that platform work has no value: delivery and domestic-service systems can create income and solve real coordination problems. Its criticism is that low prices and rapid scale may depend on large wage gaps, weak benefits, limited security, and few progression paths. The app can make this distributional structure feel like neutral convenience by turning a worker into a brief service event.

The concept is therefore a test, not a complete business taxonomy. It asks who receives convenience, who supplies flexibility, which risks remain with the worker, and whether the job creates mobility beyond the next task.

## Key Claims
- Platform coordination can create work while still distributing security, dignity, bargaining power, and convenience unequally.
- Very low service prices may reflect labor-cost gaps rather than superior technology alone.
- Transaction design can hide the worker's household, health, debt, and career circumstances from the consumer.
- Job quantity and job quality are separate questions.
- A mobility test should examine benefits, income volatility, skill formation, progression, and the worker's ability to refuse tasks.

## Evidence
- Price and scale mechanism - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-fe4d0dc018bd4482039d4a6798e610cf-124292559-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-fe4d0dc018bd4482039d4a6798e610cf-124292559]] compares the scalability of Indian delivery and domestic services with much higher-cost labor markets.
- Worker-quality test - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-fe4d0dc018bd4482039d4a6798e610cf-124292559-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-fe4d0dc018bd4482039d4a6798e610cf-124292559]] explicitly questions security, benefits, dignity, and long-term progression even while acknowledging that platforms provide work.
- Visibility mechanism - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-fe4d0dc018bd4482039d4a6798e610cf-124292559-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-fe4d0dc018bd4482039d4a6798e610cf-124292559]] argues that short app-mediated encounters can separate consumer convenience from awareness of workers' personal circumstances.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source provides conversation and interpretation rather than worker-level data, platform comparisons, or causal research. Platform work may be preferable to available alternatives for some workers, and conditions differ by occupation, company, city, contract, regulation, and worker preference. “P2R” should not be generalized to every Indian internet business.

## What Changed
- Established a distributional lens for evaluating convenience platforms beyond employment counts or consumer prices alone.
