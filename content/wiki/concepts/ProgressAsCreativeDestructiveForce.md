# Progress as Creative and Destructive Force / 进步的创造—毁灭双重性

## Definition

Progress as creative and destructive force is the claim that the same desire, knowledge, and technical capacity can improve practical life while expanding domination, war, extraction, pollution, and ideological violence.

## Current Synthesis

The episode reads [[TheFlounder|《比目鱼》]] against a simple ascent narrative. Fire cooks food and later powers conquest; masculine wanting produces bridges, vehicles, aviation, and spaceflight alongside weapons, submarines, political persecution, and environmental damage. The result is not an argument for passivity but a demand that invention be judged together with who controls it, whose labor sustains it, and where its costs land.

## Key Claims

- Technical usefulness and destructive capacity can arise from the same knowledge system.
- Desire is historically productive without being morally self-validating.
- Grand narratives of the future often hide care work, casualties, class burdens, and ecological cost.
- Criticism of progress need not deny invention; it changes the unit of evaluation from achievement alone to distributed consequence.
- A change in the gender of rulers does not by itself remove information control, coercion, or hierarchy.

## Evidence

- **Fire's double use:** [[26-junte-gelasi-bimuyu-danze-deguo-nvxing-pengren-yu-renlei-de-lishi-lnn6bsdseydyq0t98pxdttca_htz]] contrasts cooking and nurture with war, industry, and conquest.
- **Masculine desire:** [[26-junte-gelasi-bimuyu-danze-deguo-nvxing-pengren-yu-renlei-de-lishi-lnn6bsdseydyq0t98pxdttca_htz]] transfers fairy-tale insatiability into demands for victory, buildings, vehicles, submarines, and space travel.
- **Historical cost:** [[26-junte-gelasi-bimuyu-danze-deguo-nvxing-pengren-yu-renlei-de-lishi-lnn6bsdseydyq0t98pxdttca_htz]] uses repeated war, famine, class domination, political repression, and pollution to deny technique a one-directional moral measure.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

The concept is derived from one literary reading and does not show that every invention has equal benefit and harm or that technological change is inherently masculine. The episode itself does not reject transport, engineering, or exploration; it preserves creation and destruction as simultaneous possibilities whose balance requires case-specific evidence.

## What Changed

- Established a consequence-centered account of progress that retains invention's value while refusing a morally automatic ascent story.
