# Good Roads Movement

The Good Roads Movement is the road-improvement campaign discussed in [[no-199-zixingche-200-nian-973489465]] through [[AlbertPope]] and [[PopeManufacturingCompany]]. The source says Pope used advocacy groups, magazines, lobbying, and engineering education support to attack the road-quality problem that made early bicycles uncomfortable and risky.

The movement matters because it turns bicycle history into infrastructure history. The bicycle did not only adapt to existing roads; bicycle entrepreneurs and users pushed for better roads that later automobile users and motor industries also benefited from, making the campaign part of [[BicycleIndustrialSpillovers]].

## Key Claims
- Product adoption can require public or shared infrastructure rather than only better private hardware.
- Road quality was a real bottleneck for early bicycle comfort, safety, and social acceptance.
- Infrastructure advocacy can outlive the industry that first pushed it; cars later inherited much of the road-improvement logic.
