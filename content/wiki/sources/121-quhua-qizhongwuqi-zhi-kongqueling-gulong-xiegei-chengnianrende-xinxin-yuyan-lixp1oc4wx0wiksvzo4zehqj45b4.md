# 121 趣话《七种武器》之孔雀翎：古龙写给成年人的信心寓言

## Summary

This [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] episode retells and debates [[PeacockFeatherGuLong|《孔雀翎》]], the second story in [[GuLong|古龙]]'s 《七种武器》. Its strongest reading is that the supposedly invincible weapon works chiefly as belief and deterrence: 高立 defeats 麻锋 without firing it because believing he carries it restores his capacity to act. The hosts also distinguish the force of that theme from weaknesses in the plot that delivers it, especially the unexplained loss of the borrowed object and 秋凤梧's apparent decision to kill a trusted friend to preserve a family secret.

## Key Claims

- [[PeacockFeatherGuLong|《孔雀翎》]] turns an invincible device into [[ConfidenceAsWeapon|a confidence mechanism]]: possession need not be real if belief restores action, while reputation can deter violence before combat begins.
- 高立 and 双双 model [[ReciprocalCareAndDignity|reciprocal care rather than one-way rescue]]. She receives love and dignity, while he receives calm, joy, and confirmation that he remains human beyond his life as a killer.
- The hosts disagree about 双双's extreme physical description. It can intensify the rejection of conventional beauty, but it can also make her body an overengineered reversal rather than a fully independent characterization.
- 秋凤梧's decision to lend a counterfeit weapon is presented as friendship and psychological insight, yet revealing that the real weapon was lost makes the family secret dangerous enough to place friendship against responsibility for the entire Peacock Manor.
- [[ThemePlotCoherence|Thematic force and causal credibility are separate judgments]]: the confidence allegory remains legible even though the hosts find the borrowing rationale, unnoticed loss, and implied poisoning insufficiently motivated.
- The theory that 双双 stole the weapon or secretly leads the Green Dragon Society is treated as unsupported reader repair, not as a fact established by the novel.
- 金开甲 argues that a concealed weapon is still a weapon, and that moral blame belongs to treacherous use rather than to the object category itself.

## Key Quotes

> "第二种武器并非孔雀翎，而是信心。" - the episode's statement of the story's governing allegory.

> "主题有力量" and "情节是否足以支撑主题" are treated as two different critical questions.
