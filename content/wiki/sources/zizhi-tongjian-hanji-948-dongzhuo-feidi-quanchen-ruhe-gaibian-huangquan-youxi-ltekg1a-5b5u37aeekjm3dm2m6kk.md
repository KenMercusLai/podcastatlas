# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》948丨董卓废帝：权臣如何改变皇权游戏？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate pre-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-949-lishishang-de-dongzhuo-daodi-duo-canbao-lk6idogwmc54nk1sdfc5wonyvgbb|Hanji 949]] late-Han court seizure. It follows [[DongZhuo|董卓]] from testing [[YuanShao|袁绍]] on replacing the young emperor, through [[LuZhiLateHan|卢植]]'s objection, [[YuanWeiLateHan|袁隗]]'s coerced procedural role, and the forced replacement of [[LiuBianHongnongWang|刘辩 / 弘农王]] by [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]. The distinct contribution is that Dong Zhuo's takeover is not treated as raw violence alone: he removes the He-family base, borrows scholar and office prestige, rehabilitates Party Prohibition victims, and thereby turns coercion into a staged claim of institutional repair.

## Key Claims

- In 189 CE, after [[HeJin|何进]]'s death and Dong Zhuo's control of [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]], Dong Zhuo proposes deposing Liu Bian and installing Liu Xie, later [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], because a ruler he elevates will be easier to control.
- [[YuanShao|袁绍]] objects that the Han dynasty has ruled for nearly four centuries and that the young emperor has no public fault serious enough to justify deposition; Dong Zhuo's threat and Yuan Shao's armed exit expose the court's shift from deliberation to coercion.
- Dong Zhuo convenes officials and invokes Yi Yin, Huo Guang, and Tian Yannian-style precedent to make opposition to deposition feel physically dangerous, turning classical precedent into intimidation.
- [[LuZhiLateHan|卢植]] distinguishes the old precedents from the present case, arguing that Taijia and the Prince of Changyi had faults while Liu Bian is merely young. Dong Zhuo wants to kill him, but [[CaiYong|蔡邕]] and others warn that killing a famous upright official would alarm the realm.
- [[YuanWeiLateHan|袁隗]] becomes the procedural hinge of the deposition: under Dong Zhuo's pressure, he removes the seal from Liu Bian and transfers ritual position to Liu Xie.
- [[EmpressDowagerHe|何太后]] is forced out of the political center, moved to another palace, and poisoned, while [[HeMiaoLateHan|何苗]] and [[WuyangJun|武阳君]] are posthumously or bodily humiliated to frighten the court.
- Dong Zhuo's title and office package combines personal military authority with court-facing legitimacy: he takes taiwei, forward-general, tally, axe, guards, and Mei marquis status while using [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]], [[YangBiao|杨彪]], and [[HuangWanLateHan|黄琬]] as prestige-bearing appointments.
- The rehabilitation of [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]], [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]], and Party Prohibition victims functions as [[PartyProhibitionRehabilitation|党人翻案合法性]]: it lets Dong Zhuo wear a justice-and-repair mask while governing through fear.
- [[NiuFuLateHan|牛辅]] is introduced before his later remnant-commander role as Dong Zhuo's son-in-law and field commander sent against the [[BaiboArmy|白波军]] in [[HedongCommandery|河东郡]].

## Key Quotes

> "天下事岂不在我" - Dong Zhuo's coercive answer to Yuan Shao's objection.

> "百官听了之后，大家都很害怕" - the court atmosphere after Dong Zhuo raises the deposition.

> "正义的面具" - the source's framing of Dong Zhuo's rehabilitation of Party Prohibition victims.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with existing wiki pages is recorded. This source fills the missing pre-Hanji-949 and pre-Hanji-950 layer: the actual forced deposition, He-family liquidation, and early legitimacy packaging before Luoyang terror and open coalition formation.
- Source-scope caution: Dong Zhuo's motives for each appointment and for the treatment of He-family bodies are host interpretation grounded in the episode narrative, not independently established motive evidence.
- Naming caution: [[LuZhiLateHan|卢植]] is deliberately disambiguated from existing [[LuZhi|吕雉 / Lu Zhi]].
