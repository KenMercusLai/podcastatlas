# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》979丨遭遇同僚背刺，他如何为曹操力挽狂澜？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate pre-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-980-lvbu-xiansha-caocao-kankan-yi-ju-hua-ruhe-niuzhuan-lishi-lgggw3vmonu-3wvzabvg943v5qcg|Hanji 980]] crisis in [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]]. [[ChengYu|程昱]] blocks [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] at [[CangtingFordLateHan|仓亭津]], [[ZaoZhi|枣祗]] organizes the defense of [[DongALateHan|东阿]], and the remaining cities give [[CaoCao|曹操]] a home base to return to before the Puyang fighting. The Chang'an branch shows [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]], [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]], [[FanChouLateHan|樊稠]], and later [[YangDingLateHan|杨定]] hollowing out appointment order through kaifu-style offices, while [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] exposes famine-relief fraud by [[HouWenLateHan|侯汶]] but cannot restore systemic control.

## Key Claims
- The transcript appears to render several names phonetically or incorrectly; this source page normalizes 陈玉 to [[ChengYu|程昱]], 早知 to [[ZaoZhi|枣祗]], 侯文 to [[HouWenLateHan|侯汶]], and 韩丹商 to [[HandanShangLateHan|邯郸商]] while preserving the claim as source-scoped.
- [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] tries to reach the remaining Yanzhou strongholds through the Yellow River crossing at [[CangtingFordLateHan|仓亭津]], but [[ChengYu|程昱]] sends cavalry ahead, holds the ford, and removes boats to the opposite bank.
- [[ZaoZhi|枣祗]] fortifies [[DongALateHan|东阿]], gathers officials and people, and organizes adults for defense; together with Cheng Yu's movement at the ford, this preserves the three-city base until [[CaoCao|曹操]] returns.
- Cao Cao's reported thanks to Cheng Yu makes survival a base-preservation problem, not only a battlefield-bravery problem: without the remaining cities, he has no secure place to reassemble after the Yanzhou betrayal.
- [[LyuBu|吕布]] abandons the chance to seize [[JuanchengLateHan|鄄城]] or cut the Taishan-Kangfu grain route and instead returns toward Puyang, which the host reads as a strategic-judgment failure.
- In Chang'an, Guo Si becomes 后将军, Fan Chou becomes 右将军, and both receive privileges to open offices like the Three Excellencies; with Li Jue's existing privilege, court appointment channels become an armed selection field.
- The episode's "six offices" and later "seven offices" language is a source-scoped way to describe [[CourtOfficeCapture|朝廷开府任官失序]]: formal offices multiply while normal selection authority weakens.
- The [[HexiFourCommanderiesLateHan|河西四郡]] request separation from Liangzhou because distance and road disorder make ordinary administration impractical, leading the court to create [[YongzhouLateHan|雍州]] and appoint [[HandanShangLateHan|邯郸商]] as inspector.
- Chang'an's drought, earthquakes, grain price spike, and cannibalism create an extreme [[FamineEntitlementFailure|famine entitlement failure]] in which imperial relief also becomes vulnerable to corruption.
- Emperor Xian's gruel test shows residual administrative intelligence: he has rice and beans measured in front of him, proves the relief output is being diverted, and orders punishment of Hou Wen. The same scene shows why individual audit capacity is not the same as restored state power.

## Key Quotes

> "米豆各五升." - the quantity Emperor Xian uses to test the famine-relief gruel.
