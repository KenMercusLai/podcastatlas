# 154.四十岁感言：不做那只温水里的青蛙

## Summary
This [[QizhulouYanBinke|起朱楼宴宾客]] episode is [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]]'s forty-year-old personal retrospective on leaving the [[PeoplesBankOfChina|People's Bank of China]] system, surviving the 2015 stock-market crash in private funds, discovering content work, entering a top brokerage platform through a mentor, and later leaving finance. Its core synthesis is that prestige, income, platform, AI, and information flow can all become "warm water" unless a person keeps testing fit, purpose, habits, and autonomy.

## Key Claims
- [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] treats his 2015 exit from the [[PeoplesBankOfChina|People's Bank of China]] system as a major career turn, but ten years later he reads the old grievance with more managerial empathy about resources, fairness, and opportunity allocation.
- High-status jobs create [[CareerExitFriction|career exit friction]]: good pay, titles, institutions, and accumulated benefits can make leaving slower even when the role no longer fits.
- Joining a small private fund in June 2015 exposed him immediately to the crash, rescue-market period, later crash waves, and the 2016 circuit-breaker episode, teaching market humility and sharper [[FinancialCareerRisk|financial career risk]] awareness.
- The private-fund year also clarified a boundary: secondary-market investing may suit him as an interest or side pursuit, but not as a full-time occupation.
- His later investor-education work showed that translating central-bank, macro, Bitcoin, and overseas-market knowledge into ordinary language could become a real skill, not only side writing.
- The episode argues that early finance roles benefit from large platforms because [[FinancePlatformSocialCapital|finance platform social capital]] compounds through trusted colleagues, referrals, client context, and reusable reputation.
- The same platform advantage has a political downside: a powerful mentor can accelerate growth, but when the sponsor leaves, faction labels, KPI reassignment, and office politics can expose the worker's weak spots.
- The 2022 Shanghai lockdown sharpened the deeper decision to leave finance: even with income, assets, and status, he felt trapped in a [[TelosCrisis|telos crisis]] rather than merely stressed.
- Family support, savings, and a near-term desire to study Japanese made exit possible; the source does not present the move as a universally copyable career recipe.
- At forty, he frames "不惑" less as certainty about everything than as clearer self-knowledge: what he can sustain, what he is bad at, and when "persistence" is actually interest, strength, and [[HabitPositiveFeedback|positive feedback]] rather than pure willpower.
- His examples of habit include decade-long exercise, bookkeeping since 2009, investment records, needing seven to eight hours of sleep, and recently reducing drinking after discussing the issue with [[Gemini]].
- The source extends [[InvestmentWorldviewFit]] beyond portfolio choice: investing, living, and social relationships all become "votes" for a worldview, so disagreement often reflects different values rather than only factual error.
- The closing concern is [[AutonomyUnderInformationFlow|autonomy under information flow]]: phones, feeds, and AI answers can increase efficiency while weakening attention, independent thinking, and contact with concrete life.
- Long-form podcasts, books, writing, and reading are presented as autonomy practices because they preserve a complete chosen argument against fragmented feed capture.

## Key Quotes
> "不做那只温水里的青蛙" — the episode's career and life-design warning.

> "去河边拿一块石头" — the [[PaoWaimai|《跑外卖：一个女骑手的世界》]] image used for stepping out of life's conveyor belt.
