# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》707丨敢与刘秀争雄的他，为何惨淡逃亡？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianwu 8 / 32 CE before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-708-jizun-bingshi-weihe-yinfa-baiguan-jingkong-lhfuvjyiiirlaf-ugrufkaspuauq|Hanji 708]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-709-mian-dui-bangjia-an-guren-weihe-buguan-renzhi-sihuo-luknor8yw839qwxogeyzwluo6qe2|Hanji 709]]. It centers on [[DouRongEasternHan|窦融]] leading western forces into [[LiuXiu|刘秀]]'s camp, using court ritual to mark submission, and trying to exchange regional authority for family security while Liu Xiu keeps him in place for western stabilization.

The second half contrasts that controlled submission with [[WeiXiaoEasternHan|隗嚣]] and [[ZhangBuEasternHan|张步]]. Wei Xiao loses large subordinate groups, rejects Liu Xiu's final surrender offer, is rescued from Xicheng by [[GongsunShuEasternHan|公孙述]]-backed forces, and briefly retakes commanderies; Zhang Bu reads the wider surrender field too late, flees with family, and is executed. The episode also uses [[WuHanEasternHan|吴汉]]'s failure to disperse surplus troops and [[CenPengEasternHan|岑彭]]'s rearguard retreat to make logistics part of political consolidation rather than a separate battlefield problem.

## Key Claims

- Dou Rong's submission is framed as a [[SecurityForAuthorityExchange|security-for-authority exchange]]: he gives up independent power to protect his household, while Liu Xiu gives honor and continued office to stabilize the west.
- Court ritual matters politically because Dou Rong asks how to perform imperial audience protocol and Liu Xiu turns that deference into a model for other officials.
- Wei Xiao's position weakens sharply when major commanders, counties, and populations surrender, but refusal of Liu Xiu's offer keeps the conflict alive.
- Liu Xiu's western campaign is not a clean march to victory: interior unrest pulls him back east, Gongsun Shu's rescue force breaks the Xicheng encirclement, and Wei Xiao recovers Anding, Beidi, Tianshui, and Longxi.
- Wu Han's failure to disperse excess troops turns military mass into a food crisis, desertion risk, burned supplies, and retreat.
- Kou Xun's Yingchuan pacification and Geng Chun's Dong commandery result show that local trust and reputation can end disorder without heavy fighting.
- Zhang Bu's execution supplies the negative contrast to Dou Rong: late flight after the surrender cascade has turned can leave a former regional actor with neither trust nor military space.

## Key Quotes

> "两人要的都是安全" - the host's summary of Dou Rong and Liu Xiu's converging interests.

> "人苦不知足，既平陇，复望蜀" - Liu Xiu's self-critical campaign reflection as rendered in the episode.

> "借寇君一年" - the popular retention request for Kou Xun in Yingchuan.
