# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1083丨为何刘备早期空有顶级战力，却无立足之地？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode interprets [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s repeated early dependence and base loss as a gap between strong execution and an actionable strategy. It presents [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] and the [[LongzhongPlan|隆中对]] as the point where a mobile coalition gains explicit exclusions, a territorial sequence around [[Jingzhou|荆州]] and [[YizhouLateHan|益州]], cooperation with [[SunQuan|孙权]], and a longer Han-restoration objective.

The durable contribution is [[StrategicExclusionAndFocus|战略排除与聚焦]]: strategy allocates scarce resources by naming what not to pursue, while remaining adjustable when conditions change. The later sections extend the theme into founder-adviser power, Jingzhou talent appraisal, and [[TaoKan|陶侃]]'s brick-carrying discipline, but claims about hidden Liu Bei-Zhuge Liang conflict, [[GuanYu|关羽]]'s death, factional causality, and personal motives remain source-scoped speculation.

## Key Claims

- The episode contrasts Liu Bei's access to [[GuanYu|关羽]], [[ZhangFei|张飞]], and [[ZhaoYun|赵云]] with his lack of a stable base, arguing that execution strength cannot compensate for unclear strategic direction.
- It defines strategy first through exclusion: do not confront Cao Cao directly, do not try to absorb Sun Quan, and do not scatter limited resources across every available opportunity.
- Its rendering of the Longzhong Plan combines a Jingzhou-Yizhou territorial base, alliance with Sun Quan against [[CaoCao|曹操]], and a conditional later move toward restoring the Han.
- The plan is treated as a directional guide rather than an immutable script; changed markets, opponents, force, and timing can require a new sequence.
- Liu Bei's backing of the younger Zhuge Liang over veteran followers is presented as founder sponsorship of strategic expertise, while the episode's wider claims about cofounder obedience and later power struggle are modern organizational analogies.
- [[PangDegongLateHan|庞德公]] and [[SimaHuiLateHan|司马徽]] are presented as evaluators who recognize [[PangTong|庞统]] before wider opinion does, illustrating how trusted appraisal can make latent talent visible.
- Pang Degong's refusal of Liu Biao's recruitment is read as independent scholar conduct, but the episode does not establish a complete account of his political position.
- The [[TaoKan|陶侃]] brick-carrying story is used to frame deliberate hardship as protection against comfort-driven decline and loss of readiness.

## Key Quotes

> “战略不是做什么，而是清晰知道不做什么。” - the host's compact definition of strategic exclusion.

> “孤之有孔明，犹鱼之有水也。” - the episode's rendering of Liu Bei's public support for Zhuge Liang.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is recorded. The source adds an explicit exclusion-and-focus reading to the Longzhong framework already synthesized from Hanji 1098-1099.
- The claim that Liu Bei had “no strategy” before Zhuge Liang is a simplifying causal thesis. Existing pages show accumulated followers, reputation, offices, alliances, and Xinye capacity before the Longzhong Plan, so the new source is retained as a distinction between portable capacity and an integrated territorial program rather than as absence of all prior judgment.
- The episode says strategy must adapt, which is compatible with Hanji 1099's alliance-first sequence change; it does not establish that every later deviation, including the Yiling campaign, followed directly from rigid adherence to an old plan.
- Source-scope caution: exact dialogue, co-sleeping language, Guan Yu and Zhang Fei's reactions, Fa Zheng's displacement of Zhuge Liang, alleged founder-adviser mutual suspicion, the absence of advice before the Wu campaign, and a factional explanation of Guan Yu's death require broader textual evidence.
- Text caution: the “近日不错” explanation and its connection to Tao Kan's “朝夕运甓” story appear textually unstable in the supplied transcript and are not promoted as a verified idiom history.
