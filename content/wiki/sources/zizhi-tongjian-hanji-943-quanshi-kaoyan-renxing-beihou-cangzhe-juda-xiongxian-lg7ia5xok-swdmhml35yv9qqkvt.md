# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》943丨权势考验人性，背后藏着巨大凶险

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the succession crisis immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-944-xiang-zai-luanshi-zhong-cunhuo-xian-guan-zhu-zui-lp7omiak9mpcigrj4f1nummfwntr|Hanji 944]]. It follows [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s failure to name an heir, [[JianShuoLateHan|蹇硕]]'s failed attempt to kill [[HeJin|何进]] and install Liu Xie, later [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], [[LiuBianHongnongWang|刘辩 / 弘农王]]'s accession under [[EmpressDowagerHe|何太后]], and the purge of [[EmpressDowagerDongLateHan|董太后]]'s side through [[DongZhongLateHan|董重]]. The distinct contribution is a pre-Hanji-944 succession layer: non-designation turns family, foster-care, eunuch command, and outer-relative power into a fast-moving death field before the anti-eunuch plan even begins.

## Key Claims

- In 189 CE, [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] dies without having formally named a crown prince, leaving two surviving sons and their backing factions in immediate competition.
- [[LiuBianHongnongWang|刘辩 / 弘农王]] is [[EmpressDowagerHe|何太后]]'s son and is associated with the "史侯" label because he had been raised by [[ShiZimiaoLateHan|史子眇]].
- Liu Xie, later [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], is [[WangMeirenLateHan|王美人]]'s son, is raised by [[EmpressDowagerDongLateHan|董太后]], and is associated with the "董侯" label.
- The source says Emperor Ling considered Liu Bian frivolous and lacking majesty, preferred Liu Xie privately, and entrusted Liu Xie to [[JianShuoLateHan|蹇硕]] while ill.
- [[JianShuoLateHan|蹇硕]] plans to kill [[HeJin|何进]] before enthroning Liu Xie, using a palace summons as the intended entry point.
- [[PanYinLateHan|潘隐]], a subordinate of Jian Shuo and old acquaintance of He Jin, warns He Jin with his eyes; He Jin turns back to his own camp and avoids entering the palace.
- On 189-05-13, Liu Bian becomes emperor, Empress He becomes empress dowager and handles court affairs, and Liu Xie is made Bohai Wang.
- [[YuanWeiLateHan|袁隗]] is appointed taifu and shares memorial-handling authority with He Jin, giving the Liu Bian succession a Yuan-family and outer-relative court base.
- After taking power, He Jin resents Jian Shuo's attempted killing and begins planning to eliminate him.
- [[YuanShao|袁绍]] uses [[ZhangJinLateHan|张津]] to propose a broad anti-eunuch purge, and He Jin trusts Yuan Shao and [[YuanShu|袁术]] because of their family prestige and social reputation among heroes.
- He Jin recruits figures such as [[HeYongLateHan|何颙]] and [[ZhengTaiLateHan|郑泰]] as close aides, placing more than twenty people into court positions as his inner network.
- Jian Shuo writes to [[ZhaoZhongLateHan|赵忠]] and other regular palace attendants, arguing that He Jin and his brother are monopolizing power and plotting against the late emperor's trusted servants.
- [[GuoShengLateHan|郭胜]] rejects Jian Shuo's proposed preemptive strike because he is tied to the He family; he gives the letter to He Jin, who has Jian Shuo arrested and killed.
- After Jian Shuo's death, the palace guard forces under his command pass to He Jin, strengthening the He side before the later anti-eunuch struggle.
- [[EmpressDowagerDongLateHan|董太后]] and [[DongZhongLateHan|董重]] become the next targets because they represent the Liu Xie succession side and a rival maternal/foster-family power center.
- He Jin and the Three Excellencies accuse Dong Taihou's side of extracting and storing wealth in Yongle Palace, then argue that a fief-state queen mother should not remain in the capital.
- He Jin surrounds Dong Zhong's general's residence; Dong Zhong is removed from office and kills himself, while Dong Taihou soon dies under accounts that differ between source traditions.
- The episode distinguishes source layers: [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] says Dong Taihou died of fear and illness, [[HouHanshu|《后汉书》]] records pressure toward suicide, and [[RomanceOfTheThreeKingdoms|《三国演义》]] fictionalizes poisoning by He Taihou.
- The source ends by stressing that He Jin has defeated immediate rivals, but court ministers and eunuchs remain irreconcilable, setting up Hanji 944-946.

## Key Quotes

> "人，只能筛选，无法教育" - source-scoped opening claim about power revealing rather than reforming character.

> "先下手为强" - the survival logic the episode reads into Jian Shuo and He Jin's mutual fear.

> "表面风平浪静，内在隐藏巨大争斗危机" - closing frame for the unresolved court conflict.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with existing wiki pages is recorded. This source fills the missing pre-Hanji-944 layer: the non-designated succession, Jian Shuo's failure, Liu Bian's accession, He Jin's first purge, and the Dong-side elimination.
- Source-scope caution: He Jin's reputation loss, Emperor Ling's private preference, Yuan Shao's motive, and the host's "people can only be screened" judgment are recorded as the episode's interpretation.
- Source-variant caution: Dong Taihou's death is preserved as an account split between fear/illness, pressured suicide, and later novelized poisoning rather than collapsed into one cause.
