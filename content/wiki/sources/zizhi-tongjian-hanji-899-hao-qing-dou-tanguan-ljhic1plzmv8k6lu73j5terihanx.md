# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》899丨好清斗贪官

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the opening 172 CE ritual layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-900-zhichang-quanshu-dou-budong-gai-zenyang-shengcun-lmhjwosy4eswyhfppdghfsmfycqr|Hanji 900]]. It begins with a source-scoped bribery story about Jin Ren and Wu Zhi, then turns to [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] visiting [[LiuXiu|光武帝]]'s Yuanling and [[CaiYong|蔡邕]] explaining why tomb sacrifice preserves a solemn order that cannot be reduced to expendable ceremony.

The episode's core contribution is a ritual theory before the later burial-legitimacy dispute. Cai Yong treats "礼有烦而不可省者" as a governance and affect claim: procedure can make hierarchy, memory, solemnity, sacredness, and personal participation feel real, while the opening bribery story shows the opposite pressure of money flowing through corrupt official networks.

## Key Claims

- The opening story presents Jin Ren as a wealthy local wrongdoer and Wu Zhi as a young official who initially wants to punish him.
- Wu Zhi rejects smaller bribes but trembles before a much larger bribe because he believes Jin Ren could use the same money to buy his superiors and endanger him.
- The source uses "钱能通神" as a corruption-network warning, not merely as a claim about one greedy official.
- The main chronicle setting is 172 CE, Emperor Ling's first year of Xiping, when he goes to Yuanling to sacrifice to Guangwu.
- Cai Yong says he once knew the older claim that ancient rulers did not all personally visit tombs and that tomb sacrifice might be simplified, but direct experience of the rite changes his understanding.
- Cai Yong interprets the solemn tomb-sacrifice scene as evidence of [[EmperorMingOfHan|汉明帝]]'s deep filial intention toward Guangwu.
- The episode quotes "礼有烦而不可省者" to argue that some rites look cumbersome but cannot be omitted without losing their function.
- The host links this to [[LiAsPoliticalOrder|礼制政治秩序]]: ritual is not only decoration, because it creates visible order and seriousness around roles and memory.
- The host's modern extension treats ritual sense as an embodied form that can produce solemnity, sacredness, order, and self-realization.
- Cai Yong's biography is introduced through learning, music, literature, calligraphy, the flying-white style, [[CaiWenji|蔡文姬]], and the "倒屣相迎" anecdote about Wang Can.
- [[HuGuangLateHan|胡广]] is previewed as an upcoming "官场不倒翁", but the transcript does not yet develop his main story.

## Key Quotes

> "钱能通神。" - Wu Zhi's explanation for why the largest bribe changes the risk calculation.

> "礼有烦而不可省者，此之谓也。" - Cai Yong's ritual judgment after witnessing the tomb sacrifice.

> "仪式中的神圣、庄严氛围，会让参与者形成不可亵渎的严肃感受。" - host's ritual-sense extension.
