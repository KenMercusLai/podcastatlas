# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1078丨曹操靠这招瞒天过海，美军至今在研究

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode explains how [[TianChou|田畴]]'s local knowledge lets [[CaoCao|曹操]] replace a rain-blocked, defended coastal approach with a surprise march through [[LulongRoadLateHan|卢龙道]] against [[Wuhuan|乌桓]]. Cao Cao pairs the hidden route with a conspicuous roadside notice claiming that the army will return in autumn or winter, leading Wuhuan scouts to read the visible withdrawal as genuine while the real force moves toward their rear.

The episode presents the campaign as a chain joining motive, advice, terrain, engineering, and deception rather than as a single trick. Tian Chou's anger over Wuhuan raids and belief that only Cao Cao can defeat them explain why he accepts Cao Cao's summons after refusing [[YuanShao|袁绍]]; the road-opening march then carries the army toward the encounter developed in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1079-bi-sharen-hai-du-caocao-fang-diren-neidou-zuoshou-yuli-lvzyzsytejoxu_qth-hhgvee6lrv|Hanji 1079]].

## Key Claims

- Tian Chou's earlier service to [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]], settlement at [[XuWuMountain|徐无山]], and knowledge of the northern frontier make him a locally credible guide rather than a generic court adviser.
- The episode explains Tian Chou's refusal of Yuan Shao and later cooperation with Cao Cao through a changed practical objective: Wuhuan attacks [[YoubeipingCommandery|右北平]], and Tian Chou believes Cao Cao has the force needed to defeat them.
- Summer rain and Wuhuan control of the normal route immobilize Cao Cao's original plan, so Tian Chou proposes a feigned withdrawal and a march through the long-disused Lulong Road toward the lightly defended rear.
- Cao Cao leaves a roadside board saying heat and bad roads have postponed the campaign until autumn or winter; Wuhuan scouts reportedly accept the message, making an apparently official and plausible signal cover the real movement.
- The episode interprets the notice as [[BattlefieldInformationControl|战场信息控制]]: successful deception works through the opponent's existing expectations about season, terrain, documentary authority, and what an army can physically build or traverse.
- Tian Chou guides the force across Xu Wu Mountain and through more than five hundred li of road-opening work; Wuhuan reportedly learns of the advance only when Cao Cao is roughly two hundred li from Liucheng.
- The closing preview places [[YuanShang|袁尚]], [[YuanXiLateHan|袁熙]], [[TadunLateHan|蹋顿]], Louban, and Nengchendizhi before the coming battle but stops before narrating the [[BailangMountainLateHan|白狼山之战]] outcome.

## Key Quotes

> "今夏暑，道路不通，且俟秋冬，乃复进军。" - the episode's rendering of the notice left beside the apparent withdrawal route.

> "兵者，诡道也。" - the military-deception frame used to interpret the route and notice together.
