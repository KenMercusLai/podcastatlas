# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》286｜惨被误解千年，朱买臣休妻的反转真相（1）

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode reopens the familiar story of [[ZhuMaichenWesternHan|朱买臣]] and [[CuiShiZhuMaichenWife|崔氏]] by separating the account attributed to the *Book of Han* from the later opera plot of “马前泼水” and “覆水难收.” Instead of treating Cui as a status-seeking wife who abandons a poor husband and later begs to return, the episode presents a long marriage strained by subsistence pressure, Zhu's public recitation while gathering firewood, and incompatible judgments about work, learning, and a credible future.

The source therefore connects [[CharacterBasedRomanticCompatibility]] with [[HistoricalReputationConstruction]]. It argues that leaving can reflect an inability to sustain a shared life rather than simple betrayal, while also showing how a memorable theatrical scene can harden a moral verdict into popular historical memory. This is part 1: it stops before fully reconstructing Zhu's rise, Cui's later fate, or the transmission history of the opera version.

## Key Claims

- The episode attributes to the *Book of Han* an account in which Zhu Maichen remains poor into his forties, earns a living by cutting and selling firewood, and recites texts while carrying his load.
- Cui Shi objects not merely to poverty but to conduct she regards as impractical and humiliating; Zhu answers that he expects wealth and rank at fifty and promises to repay her endurance.
- Cui does not accept that forecast and leaves, which the episode interprets as a conflict over work, responsibility, values, and life direction rather than straightforward contempt for poverty.
- The episode says Cui had helped sustain the household and had already endured prolonged hardship, complicating the later stereotype of an opportunistic spouse.
- In the familiar opera version, Cui returns after Zhu becomes governor, is challenged to recover water poured on the ground, and dies in shame; the episode treats this as later dramatic framing rather than the account established in its cited historical layer.
- The concluding relationship lesson favors an orderly separation over forced continuation, while preserving that judgment as the host's modern interpretation rather than a universal rule.

## Key Quotes

> “道不同不相为谋。” - the episode's concise interpretation of the couple's incompatible life directions.

> “缘起时欢喜，缘尽时体面的结束。” - the closing relationship ethic drawn from the historical story.

## Contradictions

- The episode directly challenges the popular opera image of Cui as a wife who despises poverty, abandons Zhu for wealth, and later seeks reunion only after his promotion.
- The supplied document is a structured episode summary, not the quoted *Book of Han* passage or a history of the opera's textual transmission. Details about household earnings, Cui's motives, jealousy, emotional states, Zhu's certainty, and which spouse initiated a formal divorce remain episode-attributed.
- The source's claims that Zhu became successful precisely at fifty and that the couple's conflict is best described as modern “三观不合” are interpretive compressions requiring comparison with the primary record.
- Part 1 does not establish Cui's later fate, the historical origin of “马前泼水,” or how “覆水难收” became attached to Zhu Maichen; those questions remain open for the sequel or independent sources.
