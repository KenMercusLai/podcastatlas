# 《资治通鉴·周纪》69丨必看！古代帝王奇葩死法大盘点

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode turns from [[SongJunYan|宋康王 / 宋君偃]]'s destruction to [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]]'s dangerous post-victory moment. It presents 285 BCE as a hinge: [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] meets [[MiHeng|楚顷襄王]] and [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]], [[MengAo|蒙敖]] attacks [[QiState|齐国]], Qi Min Wang kills remonstrants, and [[YanZhaowang|燕昭王]] with [[LeYi|乐毅]] prepares a multi-state revenge coalition. The second half uses a source-scoped [[RulerNonNaturalDeathTypology|帝王非命死法类型]] to preview Qi Min Wang's coming end while connecting back to earlier starvation cases such as [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]].

## Key Claims
- The episode frames Qi's conquest of [[SongState|宋国]] as the beginning of danger rather than as a stable triumph: Qi Min Wang moves from victory into [[VictoryOverreachFailure|数胜必亡式胜利反噬]].
- In Zhou Nan Wang's thirtieth year, 285 BCE, Qin Zhaoxiang Wang's meetings with Chu and Zhao are read as part of the anti-Qi strategic environment after Song's fall.
- The source corrects the [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] commander notice: the Qin general attacking Qi is identified as [[MengAo|蒙敖]], not Meng Wu, with [[Shiji|《史记》]] used as the supporting layer.
- Qin's capture of nine Qi cities matters not only as a raid but as administrative pressure: the episode says Qin turns them into counties directly managed by Qin officials.
- Qi Min Wang's plan to attack Chu, the Three Jin, and the East/West Zhou polities converts success into isolation; the episode makes this a ruler-level overreach case.
- [[HuYanQiMinister|胡衍]] and [[ChenJuQiMinister|陈举]] become the episode's direct-remonstrance casualties. Their executions show [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|君臣反馈失灵]] becoming lethal before the state faces external collapse.
- The episode quotes the Li Ke / Wei Wenhou warning that repeated victory can exhaust the people and inflate the ruler, making "数胜必亡" a political mechanism rather than a slogan.
- Yan Zhaowang's earlier [[TalentAttractionThroughRespect|礼贤下士式人才吸附]] now pays off operationally: Le Yi advises that Yan should not attack Qi alone and should instead coordinate Zhao, Chu, and Wei.
- The anti-Qi coalition is treated as a [[CommonEnemyAlliance|共同敌人联盟]] case: Qi's arrogance and attacks make other states willing to join Yan's revenge.
- The closing death-method list groups poisoning, strangling, weapon killing, and starvation as remembered modes of ruler death, but the wiki records that material as source-attributed typology, not as independent biographies for every named example.

## Key Quotes
> "数胜必亡" - the episode's governing warning for Qi's post-Song position.

> "杀正臣者必亡" - the host's judgment after Qi Min Wang executes remonstrants.

> "子系中山狼，得志便猖狂" - the episode's literary shorthand for victory turning into arrogance.
