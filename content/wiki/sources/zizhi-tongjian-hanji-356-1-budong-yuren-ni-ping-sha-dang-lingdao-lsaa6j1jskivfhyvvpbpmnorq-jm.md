# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》356-1｜不懂驭人，你凭啥当领导？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode uses [[YangPuWesternHan|楼船将军杨仆]] after the conquest of Nanyue to discuss how a ruler handles a capable but increasingly self-important commander. [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] does not treat victory as blanket immunity: he lists five alleged faults, demands renewed submission, and then converts the confrontation into another assignment against [[YuShanDongyueKing|东越王余善]].

The source's durable contribution is the combination of accountability and continued use. Yang Pu's alleged inflation of battlefield credit, incomplete pursuit, public boasting, delayed return, and failure to explain himself are framed as hierarchy and command risks; his promise to fight to redeem the faults restores a usable relationship without settling whether every charge was proportionate. The transcript stops at the multi-route deployment against Dongyue, so neither the campaign outcome nor Yu Shan's death is established here.

## Key Claims

- The host contrasts “有功不矜” with the danger of treating military achievement as permission to boast, delay reporting, or disregard the ruler.
- After the Nanyue campaign, Yang Pu is accused of treating surrenderers and exhumed bodies as battlefield results, failing to pursue fully, displaying his merit at home, lingering after recall, and not promptly appearing or explaining himself.
- Han Wudi uses the five charges to reassert hierarchy but does not discard Yang Pu's military capacity.
- Yang Pu responds by offering to fight to redeem his faults, which supplies the submissive signal the episode says the emperor wanted.
- Han Wudi then includes Yang Pu in a multi-route campaign against Yu Shan's Dongyue rebellion, alongside other commanders and surrendered Nanyue leaders.
- The episode presents this sequence as a modern leadership analogy, but its “老板—员工” framing is interpretation rather than neutral proof of imperial motive.

## Key Quotes

> “德者功而不矜。” — the episode's moral contrast for achievement without self-inflation.

> “愿意去拼死效力，去赎罪。” — Yang Pu's reported response after the imperial rebuke.

## Connections

- [[YangPuWesternHan|杨仆]] - tower-ship general whose merit, alleged faults, submission, and redeployment anchor the episode.
- [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] - ruler who couples reprimand with continued military use.
- [[YuShanDongyueKing|余善]] - Dongyue ruler whose rebellion creates the new campaign assignment.
- [[Minyue|闽越]] - southeastern Yue political setting from which the Dongyue conflict develops.
- [[HighMeritThreatManagement|功高不震主]] - expanded from contributor self-protection to a ruler's management of a high-merit subordinate.
- [[MeritShieldedAccountability|因功免责]] - contrast because Yang Pu's achievement does not erase the alleged faults, even though usefulness affects the response.

## Contradictions

- Name normalization: the supplied transcript repeatedly writes “杨溥” and “于善”; the wiki routes them to the received historical forms [[YangPuWesternHan|杨仆]] and [[YuShanDongyueKing|余善]] rather than creating duplicate identities.
- No settled contradiction with the existing Yang Pu profile is adopted. This episode supplies an earlier Nanyue-to-Dongyue career layer; Hanji 366-1 and 366-2 later show renewed command conflict, defeat, arrest, and punishment in the Wiman Joseon campaign.
- The five accusations, their legal weight, Yang Pu's inner attitude, and Han Wudi's desire for a particular display of submission remain episode-attributed pending primary-text verification.
- The episode announces Yu Shan's eventual death but ends at mobilization; the outcome is therefore not incorporated as evidence from this source.
