# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》723丨比孔家更牛的家族，凭啥是裴氏？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode closes the third Hanji volume by pairing a lineage-history opening on [[HedongPeiClan|河东裴氏]] with Jianwu 22 / 46 CE late [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] notices. It introduces the Pei clan's unusually long official afterlife through [[LineageRuleDurability|family-rule durability]], then turns to Liu Xiu's Chang'an trip, eclipse and earthquake notices, the Liu Kun virtue stories, Xiongnu famine and heqin request, [[Wuhuan|乌桓]] pressure, and the Western Regions request for a restored protectorate.

The durable statecraft synthesis is [[CapacityBoundFrontierCommitment|capacity-bound frontier commitment]]. [[BanGuEasternHan|班固]] contrasts [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s costly Western Regions expansion through the [[HexiFourCommanderiesLateHan|河西四郡]] and Yumen route with Liu Xiu's refusal to promise envoys, troops, or a protectorate to [[ShanshanKingdomLateHan|鄯善]] and [[CheshiHouKingdomLateHan|车师]] before Han capacity could sustain the commitment.

## Key Claims

- The episode presents [[HedongPeiClan|河东裴氏]] as a rare long-lived elite lineage: Qin-Han rise, Sui-Tang peak, later decline without disappearance, and a Wenxi/Pei Bai "Chancellor Village" memory.
- Its family-rule explanation is source-scoped: the host says Pei durability depended on twelve family instructions and ten admonitions, but this transcript does not enumerate them.
- Jianwu 22 is a compact late-Guangwu annalistic year: Liu Xiu visits Chang'an, returns to Luoyang, faces eclipse and earthquake notices, removes Zhu Fu, and appoints Du Lin as Grand Minister of Works.
- Liu Kun's fire and tiger stories make virtue governance visible through miracle-like signs, while his modest answer turns the scene into a ruler-approved model of moral language.
- Xiongnu famine, disease, and succession churn lead Punu to request heqin from Han, but Wuhuan attack rather than Han invasion forces the Xiongnu north and opens the desert-south zone.
- Shache King Xian exploits Han absence in the Western Regions by attacking Shanshan and killing the Qiuci king, pushing Shanshan King An to ask for renewed hostage submission and a Han protectorate.
- Liu Xiu refuses to restore the Western Regions Protectorate because the court lacks practical capacity for envoys and troops; Shanshan and Cheshi then attach to Xiongnu.
- Ban Gu's comparison treats Han Wudi's Western Regions opening as strategically intelligible against Xiongnu but fiscally and socially expensive enough to make Liu Xiu's restraint look timing-aware rather than indifferent.

## Key Quotes

> "天下第一家" - the host's label for the Pei clan's exceptional lineage status.

> "有心无力" - Liu Xiu's reply frame for why Han cannot yet send Western Regions envoys, troops, or a protector.

> "切断匈奴右臂" - the strategic logic attributed to Han Wudi's Western Regions and Hexi program.
