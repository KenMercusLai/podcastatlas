## Summary
This [[HubermanLab]] Essentials episode features [[AndrewHuberman]] speaking with neurosurgeon [[CaseyHalpern]] about [[DeepBrainStimulationPsychiatry]], [[FocusedUltrasoundNeuromodulation]], [[ObsessiveCompulsiveDisorder]], craving, binge eating, addiction, obesity, depression, and suicidality. Its central synthesis is [[UrgeDespiteRiskCircuit]]: severe compulsive or impulsive symptoms may involve circuits that keep driving reward pursuit or safety rituals despite obvious harm. The episode extends [[CircuitBasedPsychiatry]] by showing how invasive recording, stimulation, and future non-invasive tools can move psychiatry from broad symptom labels toward measurable brain targets.

## Key Claims
- [[DeepBrainStimulationPsychiatry]] uses implanted electrodes to deliver electrical stimulation to small brain regions, with therapeutic aims but possible temporary emotional side effects when nearby circuits are engaged.
- [[ObsessiveCompulsiveDisorder]] is framed as a spectrum from useful controlled precision to severe, impairing obsessions and compulsions; about 30% of patients are described as still suffering despite available therapies.
- Surgical options for severe refractory OCD include DBS and capsulotomy, but Halpern presents them as high-stakes interventions with partial responder rates rather than guaranteed cures.
- [[UrgeDespiteRiskCircuit]] links OCD checking or washing, binge eating, bulimia, addiction, obesity, and some suicidal or depressive states through the shared pattern of pursuing an urge despite danger.
- The ventral striatum and nucleus accumbens are presented as reward-gating regions that may help explain harmful craving and compulsive reward-seeking.
- [[FocusedUltrasoundNeuromodulation]] is FDA approved for tremor ablation and may eventually support modulation, blood-brain-barrier opening, or targeted drug delivery, but psychiatric targeting remains the hard problem.
- [[IntracranialCircuitMapping]] uses invasive recording, awake procedures, stereo-EEG, laboratory provocation, eye tracking, and machine learning to identify craving or obsession signals that might later guide scalable tools.

## Key Quotes
> "medication" - Halpern's analogy for electricity delivered through DBS.

> "craving" - the patient-recognizable target his team studies in binge eating and obesity.

> "where to target it" - the core bottleneck for applying powerful stimulation tools to psychiatric disease.
