# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》156｜兵仙韩信的封神之战（2）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the [[JingxingPass|井陉口]] battle sequence opened in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-156-bingxian-hanxin-de-fengshen-zhizhan-1-ltkz7bwqgkcqessdfbn9unf5e05l|Hanji 156 part 1]]. It argues that [[ChenYu|陈馀]] rejected [[LiZuoChe|李左车]]'s plan not because he was ignorant of deception, but because he wanted an open, politically legible victory and badly underestimated [[HanXin|韩信]]'s force. The battle then unfolds as a [[BackwaterBattle|背水一战]] chain: spy-confirmed enemy error, hidden red-flag cavalry, backwater deployment, feigned defeat, Zhao camp seizure, morale collapse, Chen Yu's death, and [[ZhaoXie|赵歇]]'s capture.

## Key Claims
- Chen Yu is presented as familiar with [[SunziBingfa|《孙子兵法》]]'s deception logic, so his refusal of Li Zuoche's plan is treated as political and psychological judgment rather than simple tactical illiteracy.
- Chen Yu wants to defeat Han Xin and [[ZhangEr|张耳]] openly so [[LiuBang|刘邦]] and [[XiangYu|项羽]] will see Zhao strength, and so other lords will not read Zhao as timid.
- The source rejects Chen Yu's estimate that Han Xin's "several tens of thousands" were really only several thousand; after reinforcements and local recruitment, Han Xin may still have had roughly fifty thousand troops.
- Han Xin uses spies before entering the pass and is pleased only after learning that Chen Yu has rejected Li Zuoche's supply-cut plan.
- Han Xin sends two thousand elite cavalry ahead with red flags, ordering them to hide near the eastern pass mouth and seize Zhao's camp once Zhao troops leave it.
- Han Xin has a forward force cross the Mianman water, identified by the source with today's Ye River near Jingxing, and form with water at its back.
- Zhao commanders laugh at the backwater formation because orthodox military teaching treats water-backed ground as dangerous with no easy retreat.
- Han Xin and Zhang Er then use visible flags and drums to draw Zhao into battle, feign defeat, abandon flags and drums, and retreat toward the river camp.
- Zhao troops pursue and attack the river-side Han camp, while the hidden cavalry enters the emptied Zhao camp and replaces Zhao flags with Han red flags.
- When Zhao troops return and see Han flags in their camp, they assume the rear and possibly Zhao Xie have been taken; the resulting panic destroys order despite officers killing fleeing soldiers.
- Han forces exploit the collapse, defeat Zhao, kill Chen Yu during the pursuit, and capture Zhao Xie.
- The source frames the victory as a coordinated tactical chain rather than a lucky "death ground" gamble; the deeper explanation of why backwater deployment works is left to the next episode.

## Key Quotes
> "兵者诡道也" - the maxim used to show that Chen Yu should have understood deception.

> "背水一战" - the battle frame completed by this episode.

> "背后是河，无法后退" - the source's explanation of why the Han river-side force had to fight hard.
