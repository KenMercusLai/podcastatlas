# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1065丨职场生存的顶级哲学：学荀彧、贾诩or郭子仪？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode is a reflective sequel to [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1064-xuyou-zhisi-gonggao-zhenzhu-yu-xingge-quexian-de-beige-lswmvj55p7ip97ts5e30xfdubwth|Hanji 1064]]'s [[XuYouLateHan|Xu You]] case. It generalizes Xu You's public credit overreach into a broader [[HighMeritThreatManagement|high-merit threat management]] lesson: private closeness, old service, and real achievement do not remove public hierarchy or the superior's changing risk calculation.

The episode contrasts strategies associated with [[XunYu|荀彧]], [[JiaXu|贾诩]], [[FanLi|范蠡]], [[ZhangLiang|张良]], [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]], [[GuoZiyi|郭子仪]], [[HanXin|韩信]], [[XiaoHe|萧何]], [[NianGengyao|年羹尧]], and [[ZengGuofan|曾国藩]]. Its modern workplace formula is that safety rises when current indispensability is high and perceived threat is low; when contribution decays but arrogance, public superiority, or power appetite remain visible, merit can become danger.

## Key Claims

- Public hierarchy matters even inside private intimacy: when third parties are present, family, friendship, school ties, or old comrade status should not erase the superior-subordinate frame.
- [[XuYouLateHan|Xu You]]'s old merit with [[CaoCao|曹操]] becomes unsafe because he treats past contribution as permanent entitlement and publicly reminds Cao that Jizhou depended on him.
- The episode presents three self-protection routes: [[XunYu|Xun Yu]] lets credit return upward to Cao Cao; [[JiaXu|Jia Xu]] reduces visibility and private networking after achievement; and useful commanders keep enough current value that the ruler prefers continued use to removal.
- Post-victory danger grows when a ruler no longer needs the high-merit actor's extraordinary service but still sees that actor's reputation, troops, secrets, or public standing as a threat.
- [[FanLi|Fan Li]] and [[ZhangLiang|Zhang Liang]] model voluntary exit or low visibility after success, while [[HanXin|Han Xin]] and [[WenZhongYue|Wen Zhong]] show the risk of remaining too exposed after the common enemy disappears.
- [[WeiQingHanGeneral|Wei Qing]] and [[GuoZiyi|Guo Ziyi]] are used as mature examples of humility, credit-sharing, and authority surrender after great military service.
- The source-scoped workplace formula is: safety coefficient equals indispensability divided by perceived threat.
- The episode closes by distinguishing disorder from settled rule: in crisis, ability is more tolerated; after consolidation, loyalty, etiquette, and threat reduction matter more.
