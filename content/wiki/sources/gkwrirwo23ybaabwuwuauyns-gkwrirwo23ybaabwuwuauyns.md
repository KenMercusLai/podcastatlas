# EP395-AI漫剧的天量风口，究竟是谁拿到了大结果？

## Summary
This [[WushichaYanjiusuo|无时差研究所]] episode interviews [[MiaAIShortDrama|Mia]], founder of [[JuwubaAIStudio|剧无霸]], about the production and economics of [[AIShortDrama|AI漫剧]]. Her studio's experience connects a winner-take-most revenue profile, declining platform payouts, a widening gap between one-click output and boutique production, multi-model workflows, overseas experiments, and pressure on [[LiveActionShortDrama|真人短剧]] employment.

The strongest synthesis is that cheaper generation does not make high-quality drama nearly free. In the guest's account, cost moves from actors, locations, sets, and shooting days toward scripts, reusable character and scene assets, specialized labor, model calls, iteration, editing, review, and platform distribution. Her team therefore treats [[AIShortDramaBoutiqueStrategy|精品化]] as a domestic survival strategy rather than assuming that maximum output creates durable profit.

## Key Claims
- [[MiaAIShortDrama|Mia]] says fewer than 10% of her company's boutique works generate roughly 90% of revenue, mostly through platform revenue sharing, while more than 90% of current revenue is domestic.
- The source says paid comic-drama ROI has fallen to roughly 1.1 and that some platform payouts per 10,000 plays have dropped to single-digit RMB, reinforcing [[AIShortDramaSupplyGlut|supply-glut]] pressure.
- A roughly 100-minute one-click work may cost only several thousand RMB and can sometimes be produced by one person in a day, while a boutique work may cost hundreds of thousands of RMB and require weeks or months.
- Boutique production requires scripts, character and scene assets, props, costume and makeup design, model orchestration, repeated generation, editing, and specialized collaboration; some projects may include hundreds of character designs.
- In the guest's studio, compute is reported as about 25% of boutique-project cost because labor remains dominant; in low-labor one-click work, compute may exceed 70% of cost.
- The studio's one-click domestic experiments mostly lost money, while boutique works produced the main returns. It is willing to test lower-cost output overseas, where the guest believes current quality expectations differ.
- AI comic drama reduces dependence on actors, locations, and crews and expands fantasy or overseas visual settings, but the source also reports fewer opportunities for actors and shrinking activity around [[HengdianWorldStudios|横店]].
- [[ShuangshengMengshou|《双生猛兽》]] is presented as a recent boutique hit with a platform “爆剧” label and nearly 700,000 collections.
- The guest expects AI production capabilities to extend into interactive drama, games, animation, documentary, and film, while warning newcomers that creative access is real but reliable profit is not.

## Key Quotes
> "不到10%的精品剧贡献了90%的收入" - the guest's account of concentrated studio revenue.

> "一键流的我们基本上全亏了" - the studio's domestic result from low-labor production.

> "我们只能靠爆款，靠质量，靠专业度" - the guest's preferred basis for a sustainable team.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is adopted. The episode's loss-making one-click experiments and boutique success qualify exceptional low-cost solo-creator cases without disproving them; production route, platform, story, quality grade, distribution, and timing differ.
- The source strengthens earlier supply-glut evidence by adding studio-level payout compression and daily launch volume, but its ROI, payout, revenue-share, cost, staffing, audience, collection, and overseas-performance figures remain participant-reported rather than audited industry statistics.
- AI reduces some live-production demand, but the source does not establish that AI alone caused the wider slowdown in actor work or Hengdian activity.
