# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1049丨官渡之战结局揭幕，曹操终于以弱胜强

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode narrates the decisive climax of the [[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]]: [[CaoCao|曹操]] trusts [[XuYouLateHan|许攸]]'s intelligence, personally leads five thousand troops in a night raid against [[WuchaoRaidLateHan|乌巢]], and burns [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s grain depot. Yuan Shao misallocates his response between rescuing Wuchao and attacking Cao's camp, [[GuoTu|郭图]] later protects himself by slandering [[ZhangHeLateHan|张郃]], and Zhang He plus [[GaoLanLateHan|高览]] surrender to Cao. The episode also covers [[ChunyuQiong|淳于琼]]'s capture and execution, Yuan Shao's flight across the Yellow River, Cao's mass killing of remaining surrendered troops, and [[JuShou|沮授]]'s loyal but doomed captivity.

## Key Claims
- Cao Cao's Wuchao decision is presented as a forced high-risk move: his own army is nearly out of grain, Xu You's intelligence might be a trap, and the target sits behind Yuan Shao's lines.
- The raid turns logistics into the decisive battlefield because burning Wuchao destroys Yuan Shao's material endurance and shocks morale faster than frontal attrition could.
- Yuan Shao's pivotal error is to send only a light rescue force to Wuchao while committing his heavier force to attack Cao Cao's camp, despite Zhang He's warning that Wuchao must be saved.
- Cao Cao refuses to divide his force when Yuan reinforcements appear, choosing to keep all pressure on the depot and force a breakthrough.
- Guo Tu's self-protective slander after the failed attack makes Zhang He and Gao Lan's position unsafe, turning Yuan-side internal blame into a surrender cascade.
- Cao Cao accepts Zhang He and Gao Lan's surrender after Cao Hong hesitates and Xun You argues that Zhang He defected because correct advice was rejected.
- Ju Shou is portrayed as a loyal captured adviser: Cao Cao initially honors him, but kills him after repeated attempts to return to Yuan Shao.
- The episode treats Guandu as a "system war" where intelligence, logistics, command judgment, morale, and defector incorporation reverse a nominal 10-to-1 strength gap.

## Key Quotes
> "乌巢粮草一烧，整个袁绍集团的心理防线就彻底崩了。" — The episode frames Wuchao as both a material and psychological breaking point.

> "曹操没有分兵，反而下令继续猛攻，必须拿下乌巢。" — Cao's refusal to dilute the strike is presented as the raid's operational hinge.
