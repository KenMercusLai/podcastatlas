## Summary
This [[TheIntelligence]] episode links three transitions: [[Russia]]'s temporary advantage in long-range drone and missile attacks on [[Ukraine]], drinks companies' response to declining alcohol consumption, and [[GloriaSteinem]]'s move from discriminatory magazine assignments to co-founding [[MsMagazine|Ms. magazine]]. The war segment distinguishes a broadly balanced front from a widening [[CivilianInfrastructureAirCampaign]] against energy, transport, retail, and population centers, while the business segment connects [[AlcoholDemandReset]] to alcohol-free beer, premium canned cocktails, and new drinking occasions. The obituary presents [[FeministMediaInstitutionBuilding]] as the conversion of shared experience into a durable editorial and organizing platform.

## Key Claims
- [[AlexTurnerEconomist|Alex Turner]] says [[Russia]] has gained a temporary development-and-production advantage in systems able to strike across [[Ukraine]], even though the front line remains broadly balanced.
- Jet-powered [[Shahed136|Shahed-style drones]] fly higher and faster than earlier petrol-engine versions, while increased ballistic-missile production and declining Ukrainian interceptor stocks intensify [[AirDefenseSaturation]].
- [[CivilianInfrastructureAirCampaign]] links attacks on railways, warehouses, commuter trains, retail supply chains, energy, heat, and water to economic damage, emigration pressure, family stress, and possible military distraction.
- [[Ukraine]] has four or five fast-interceptor prototypes, but scaling them requires money, direct contracts with Ukrainian firms, outside technical expertise, and potentially Western defense-industry intellectual property.
- The [[NemesisDroneUnit]] combines intelligence, planning, and long-range quadcopter operations against preselected targets behind the front.
- [[AlcoholDemandReset]] is driven in the episode by several years of weaker global sales, falling large-company valuations, and younger consumers drinking and going out less.
- Alcohol-free beer has performed better than alcohol-free wine partly because conventional beer can be brewed and then dealcoholized, whereas acceptable wine substitutes remain harder to make.
- [[Campari]]'s Aperol spritz on tap and [[PremiumReadyToDrinkAlcohol]] show spirits companies competing in concerts, sports venues, and packaged formats traditionally associated with beer.
- CBD and mushroom-extract drinks are presented as early alternatives for consumers who still want a social or mood-altering beverage, but the source does not establish their eventual market size.
- [[GloriaSteinem]]'s 1963 undercover Playboy Club exposé revealed exploitation while also leaving her trapped in gendered assignments that she described as a journalistic “glass box.”
- [[MsMagazine|Ms. magazine]] turned feminist subjects such as unpaid housework, body hair, abortion, and political power into national glossy-magazine coverage owned, managed, and written by women.
- Steinem's organizing grew from hearing women discuss illegal abortions openly; her later life also showed that gains such as *Roe v. Wade* could be reversed and that barriers to women's political power persisted.
