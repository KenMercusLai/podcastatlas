# 《资治通鉴·周纪》07丨两起刺杀（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] Zhou-annals sequence through [[ZhouAnWang|周安王]]'s first four years, moving from Qin-Wei fighting and Han-Zhao-Qin successions into [[ZhengState|郑国]]'s geopolitical and internal crisis. Its core synthesis is that the early [[WarringStatesPeriod|战国时期]] is not only a chronology of states at war: aristocratic exile networks, omen interpretation, small-state buffer diplomacy, and assassination politics all show the older [[SpringAndAutumnPeriod|春秋时期]] rule-bound order breaking down.

## Key Claims
- In 401 BCE, [[QinState|秦国]] attacks [[WeiState|魏国]], reaching Yanggu, establishing direct Qin-Wei military pressure early in [[ZhouAnWang|周安王]]'s reign.
- In 400 BCE, [[HanState|韩国]], [[ZhaoState|赵国]], and [[WeiState|魏国]] attack [[ChuState|楚国]] together, while [[ZhengState|郑国]] takes advantage of [[HanJinghou|韩景侯]]'s death to besiege Han-held Yangdi.
- The episode treats the deaths and accessions of [[HanJinghou|韩景侯]] to [[HanLiehou|韩烈侯]], [[ZhaoLiehou|赵烈侯]] to [[ZhaoWuhou|赵武侯]], and [[QinJianGong|秦简公]] to [[QinHuiGong|秦惠公]] as a first-generation succession stress test for new Warring States powers.
- [[WangziDing|王子定]]'s flight to [[JinState|晋国]] illustrates [[AristocraticPoliticalAsylum]]: royal and princely exiles could draw on kinship, noble status, and future restoration value.
- The Guo mountain landslide and Yellow River blockage are read through [[NaturalDisasterPoliticalOmen]], with [[WangYinglin|王应麟]] using [[ZuoZhuan|《左传》]]'s "国主山川" idea to connect landscape disorder to state fate.
- The episode says [[SimaGuang|司马光]] did not explain why [[ChuState|楚国]] besieged [[ZhengState|郑国]] or why Zheng killed [[ZiyangZheng|子阳]], then uses [[QinghuaJianXinian|清华简《系年》]] to fill the narrative gap.
- [[ZhengState|郑国]] is framed as a classic [[SmallStateBufferDiplomacy]] case: siding with Jin angered Chu, but siding with Chu would expose Zheng to Jin.
- [[ZiyangZheng|子阳]]'s killing and [[ZhengKangGong|郑康公]]'s later installation by Ziyang's faction show [[SpringAutumnToWarringStatesPoliticalViolence]]: elite power struggle shifts from exile, ritual constraint, and preserved lineage toward assassination and factional elimination.
- The source stops at part 1 of the "two assassinations" discussion, so its assassination synthesis is provisional and should be read as setup for the later continuation.

## Key Quotes
> "不讲武德，没礼数，没规矩" - the source's judgment on Zheng attacking Han during mourning.

> "国主山川" - the Zuo Zhuan phrase used in the episode's omen discussion.

> "从职业拳手的擂台竞技，变成地痞流氓的街头拼命" - the episode's analogy for the shift from Spring-Autumn rules to Warring States violence.
