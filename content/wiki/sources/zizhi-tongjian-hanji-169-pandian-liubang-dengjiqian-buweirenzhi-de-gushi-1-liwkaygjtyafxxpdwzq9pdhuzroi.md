# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》169｜盘点刘邦登基前不为人知的故事（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-168-bawangbieji-qian-fashengle-shenme-2-liqgict85wh-bf4fn2bytygmk-ca|Hanji 168 part 2]] by moving from [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s death to [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s conversion of victory into founding order. It covers the pacification of Chu territory, the handling of Xiang Yu's body and clan, the capture of [[GongWei|共尉]], the stripping and redeployment of [[HanXin|韩信]]'s army, the grants to Han Xin and [[PengYue|彭越]], and the formal accession that founds [[WesternHanDynasty|西汉]]. Its core synthesis is that Liu Bang's advantage over Xiang Yu appears most clearly after the battle: he treats military victory as only the beginning of [[BattlefieldVictoryToFoundingOrder|战场胜利到建国秩序]].

## Key Claims
- After Xiang Yu's death, Liu Bang rapidly deploys the coalition to settle Chu-linked territory; [[LuCountyChuHan|鲁县]] remains loyal until Liu Bang shows Xiang Yu's head and the city accepts surrender.
- Liu Bang buries Xiang Yu with the rites of Duke of Lu at Gucheng, personally mourns him, spares Xiang-family relatives, grants marquisates to figures including [[XiangBo|项伯]], and gives some Xiang kin the Liu surname.
- [[XiangXiang|项襄]] becomes a concrete example of early Xiang-clan realignment: he had already judged Xiang Yu's cause poorly and submitted to Liu Bang before later receiving the Tao marquisate.
- The episode uses [[SimaQian|司马迁]] and [[YangXiong|扬雄]] to argue that Xiang Yu's failure should not be reduced to Heaven's will; the deeper problem is that he relied on force and personal cleverness without political integration.
- [[GongWei|共尉]], the Linjiang king and son of [[GongAo|共敖]], refuses to submit. [[LuWan|卢绾]] and [[LiuJia|刘贾]] attack him for months before Han reinforcements take Jiangling and capture him.
- At Dingtao, Liu Bang enters Han Xin's camp and removes his military command again, then changes the former Qi king into Chu king with Huai River north territory and capital at [[Xiapi|下邳]].
- Liu Bang makes Peng Yue king of Liang over old Wei territory with capital at [[Dingtao|定陶]], treating his rear-pressure and anti-Chu service as worthy of formal territorial reward.
- Liu Bang issues a broad postwar pardon below capital crimes, then receives an accession petition from [[HanXin|韩信]], [[HanWangXin|韩王信]], [[YingBu|英布]], [[WuRui|吴芮]], [[ZhangAo|张敖]], and [[ZangTu|臧荼]].
- [[ShusunTong|叔孙通]] designs the accession rite using Qin precedents, but Liu Bang chooses a simplified ceremony rather than a full ritual burden.
- The episode ends at the Luoyang south-palace banquet, where Liu Bang asks senior officials why he won the realm and Xiang Yu lost it, setting up a more explicit explanation of Han victory.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction with existing wiki content was found.
- The episode's Yang Xiong-based critique of Xiang Yu's "Heaven" explanation complements, rather than overrides, earlier [[PeopleBasedPoliticalSecurity|民心型政治安全]] pages: prior pages read "Heaven" as lost people-support, while this episode stresses Xiang Yu's failure to mobilize and retain people.
- Liu Bang's treatment of Xiang Yu and the Xiang clan is recorded as a postwar pacification move, not as a claim that Liu Bang was consistently merciful in every later political conflict.
