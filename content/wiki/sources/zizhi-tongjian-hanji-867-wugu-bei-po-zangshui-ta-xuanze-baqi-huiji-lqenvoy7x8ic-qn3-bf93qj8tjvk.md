# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》867丨无辜被泼脏水？他选择霸气回击

## Summary
This episode backfills the 165 CE layer immediately before the late-Han court episodes already in the wiki. It pairs [[ChenQiuLateHan|陈球 / 陈裘]] defending [[LinglingCommanderyLateHan|零陵郡]] against [[ZhuGaiLateHan|朱丐]] and [[HuLanLateHan|胡兰]] with [[ZhangPanLateHan|张磐 / 张盘]] refusing release under a general amnesty until [[DuShangLateHan|度尚 / 杜尚]] confronts his false accusation. The closing adds an earlier frontier-merit marker for [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]] and an early office-discipline marker for [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]].

## Key Claims
- The episode frames late-Han actors through conduct rather than fixed moral labels, emphasizing that officials can combine real service with serious fault.
- In 165 CE, Jingzhou soldier [[ZhuGaiLateHan|朱丐]] rebels and joins [[HuLanLateHan|胡兰]], expanding from attacks on [[GuiyangCommanderyLateHan|桂阳郡]] to pressure on [[LinglingCommanderyLateHan|零陵郡]].
- [[ChenQiuLateHan|陈球 / 陈裘]] refuses to evacuate his household ahead of public duty, improvises city defenses, and turns the attackers' flood tactic back against them.
- [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] appoints [[DuShangLateHan|度尚 / 杜尚]] as 中郎将; Du suppresses Zhu Gai and Hu Lan, is restored as [[Jingzhou|荆州]] inspector, and benefits from the court's need for pacification talent.
- [[ZhangPanLateHan|张磐 / 张盘]] suppresses Hu Lan's remnants in [[CangwuCommanderyLateHan|苍梧郡]], but Du Shang falsely reports the movement of Cangwu bandits to shift blame away from himself.
- Zhang Pan refuses to leave prison under amnesty because a pardon would not prove innocence; his demand for confrontation with Du Shang exposes the false accusation.
- The court spares Du Shang despite the proven false report because his suppression merit and usefulness outweigh ordinary accountability.
- [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]] gains an early title after a Western Qiang victory, providing prehistory to his later hard-line frontier record.
- [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] becomes Taiwei and tries to yield the office to [[HuGuangLateHan|胡广]], [[WangChangShumaoLateHan|王畅]], and [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]], showing a title-discipline posture before his later anti-eunuch conflicts.

## Key Quotes
> "无辜被泼脏水" — the title frames Zhang Pan's case as wrongful blame rather than normal amnesty politics.

> "赦免之恩对他而言只是虚妄" — the episode's paraphrase of Zhang Pan's refusal to treat amnesty as exoneration.

> "朝廷需要人才平乱" — the explanation for why Du Shang is spared after the accusation is exposed.
