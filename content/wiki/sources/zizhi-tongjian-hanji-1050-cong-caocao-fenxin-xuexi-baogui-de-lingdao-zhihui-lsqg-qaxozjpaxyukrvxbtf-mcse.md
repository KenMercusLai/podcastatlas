# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1050丨从曹操焚信学习宝贵的领导智慧

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the post-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]] field after [[CaoCao|曹操]] defeats [[YuanShao|袁绍]]. Cao Cao discovers letters from his own officials and generals to Yuan Shao, burns them publicly, and explains that when Yuan was strong even he could barely protect himself, let alone others; the host reads this as a [[PostVictoryAmnestySignal|胜后既往不咎信号]] that stabilizes a frightened camp. The episode then contrasts [[LiuXiu|刘秀]] burning letters, Yuan Shao's killing of [[TianFengLateHan|田丰]], [[LiuBang|刘邦]] apologizing to [[LouJing|娄敬]], and [[FengJiLateHan|逢纪]] defending [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]] despite private hostility to show how leaders handle loyalty, advice, suspicion, and resentment after victory or defeat.

## Key Claims
- After the 200 CE Guandu victory, Cao Cao finds many letters between Yuan Shao and Cao-side officials or officers, then burns them before the generals rather than turning them into a purge list.
- Cao Cao's explanation reframes the letters as survival anxiety under Yuan Shao's former strength, not settled treason requiring retroactive punishment.
- The host compares Cao Cao's act with Liu Xiu's burning of letters after defeating Wang Lang, treating both as organization-stabilizing clemency.
- Yuan Shao regroups at Liyang through Jiang Yiqu, but the episode shifts the defeat's meaning toward his inability to absorb correct criticism.
- Tian Feng predicts his own death because Yuan Shao's shame after defeat will make correct prior advice intolerable; Feng Ji then claims Tian Feng laughed at the loss, and Yuan Shao orders him killed.
- Cao Cao's reported comment that Yuan Shao would have been hard to beat had he used Tian Feng reinforces Tian Feng's strategic value and Yuan Shao's adviser-conversion failure.
- The Liu Bang/Lou Jing comparison gives a counterexample: Liu Bang imprisons Lou Jing before the Baideng disaster but releases, apologizes to, rewards, and ennobles him after being proven wrong.
- Shen Pei is temporarily suspected after two sons are captured by Cao Cao, but Feng Ji says private disagreement should not override public judgment and defends Shen Pei's integrity.
- The episode uses Sima Guang's criticism of Yuan Shao as a synthesis point: Yuan had apparent grace and restraint, but was "short at following good advice" and stubborn into failure.
- The host's Guandu lesson is that advantage comes from commander decision quality, team coordination, information control, and continued adjustment rather than raw force alone.

## Key Quotes
> "绍之强，孤犹不能自保，而况众人乎." - Cao Cao's explanation for burning the letters.

> "短于从善，固执于败." - the episode's cited Sima Guang judgment on Yuan Shao.
