# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》935丨人活一口气，难成的是自己

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 185 CE late-Han crisis between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-933-weiguan-zhidao-yousuo-wei-yousuo-buwei-lopulnufsagoqyx7nngvcqi2nhrj|Hanji 933]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-936-luanshi-xian-sha-shengmu-xin-zhehua-dui-ma-lml-kuyc7yys2ijh2kt46pwnugar|Hanji 936]]. It follows [[LiuTaoLateHan|刘陶 / 刘桃]]'s warning about Yellow Turban and western rebellion pressure, the eunuch slander that pushes him into prison and self-killing, [[ZhangWenLateHan|张温]]'s campaign against [[BianZhangLateHan|边章]] and [[HanSui|韩遂]], and [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]]'s ignored advice to cut the rebel grain road before [[ZhouShenLateHan|周慎]] loses his own supply line.

## Key Claims

- The opening uses a "mature person" frame to argue that the hardest struggle is often internal clarity rather than external talent alone.
- In 185 CE, [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] appoints Zhang Wen as chariot-and-cavalry general against the western rebels, with [[DongZhuo|董卓]] and Zhou Shen among the commanders under him.
- [[LiuTaoLateHan|刘陶 / 刘桃]] warns that the realm has moved from [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]]'s Yellow Turban disorder into Bian Zhang's western rebellion, with rebel pressure moving close enough to threaten [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]].
- The eunuch faction reframes Liu Tao's crisis report as pessimism, secret knowledge of unreported affairs, and possible rebel contact, so Emperor Ling orders him jailed in the eunuch-controlled north prison.
- Liu Tao's death is presented as both a personal act of loyal anger and a sign that direct feedback can no longer reach the ruler once palace intermediaries control interpretation.
- The episode says Liu Tao's death shocked officials and was remembered with broad grief, but that moral shock did not repair the late-Han court's dependence on eunuch access.
- Zhang Wen is defeated at Meiyang by Bian Zhang and Han Sui before Dong Zhuo and right Fufeng-side forces counterattack and drive the rebels toward Yuzhong.
- Sun Jian advises Zhou Shen to take advantage of the rebels' food shortage by cutting their external supply road with 10,000 troops while the main army supports from behind.
- Zhou Shen ignores Sun Jian's plan, besieges Yuzhong directly, and is forced to retreat after the rebels cut the Han army's own grain road at Kuiyuan Gorge.
- The episode makes Sun Jian's early importance visible before his later military-law advice in Hanji 936 and his Changsha and anti-Dong-Zhuo campaigns in later sources.

## Key Quotes

> "人活一口气，难成的是自己那颗难以降伏的心" - the opening frame for self-command and moral resolve.

> "遂闭气而死" - the chronicle wording the host treats as a reliable record of Liu Tao's prison death.

> "天下莫不痛之" - the reported reaction after Liu Tao's death.
