# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》322-1｜汉武帝如何割商人韭菜？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode presents [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s merchant-property assessment as a response to war, construction, waterworks, and court expenditure that had depleted earlier Han accumulation. It distinguishes the initial self-reporting and assessment stage, normalized here as 算缗, from the later [[RewardedDenunciationFiscalEnforcement|告缗式奖励告发财政执法]] that rewarded successful informants with part of confiscated property.

The episode's most useful addition is the behavioral sequence between the two measures. It says merchants concealed assets, converted money into goods, or sold taxable vehicles; the program then attributes market disorder and rising prices to those avoidance responses before presenting rewarded denunciation, confiscation, frontier punishment, and land seizure as the court's escalation. Exact terminology, rates, exemptions, chronology, and market effects remain source-scoped because the supplied transcript is short and explicitly warns of transcription uncertainty.

## Key Claims

- The episode attributes the fiscal pressure behind the measures to prolonged Xiongnu war, waterworks, construction, and imperial spending.
- It says commercial operators had to assess and report their own property, with merchants charged one 算 of 120 cash per 2,000 cash of assets and artisans per 4,000 cash.
- It also places vehicles within the assessment while naming officials, northern cavalrymen, and 三老 among exemptions, but the transmitted categories and wording require textual verification.
- It says merchants responded by hiding property, converting money into stockpiled goods, and selling vehicles, showing how a self-reported asset levy can alter asset-holding behavior before punitive enforcement begins.
- The episode attributes market disorder and higher prices to that conversion into goods, but does not supply price data or separate the tax response from war, monopoly, monetary, or supply effects.
- It presents告缗 as the escalation: successful accusations could receive half the confiscated property, while concealment could bring confiscation and one year of frontier service.
- It says renewed restrictions on merchant landholding made land another object of confiscation and that denunciation quickly reached many households of middling wealth, not only the richest merchants.

## Key Quotes

> “只要举报属实，财产充公后会分给举报者百分之五十。” - the episode's description of the informant incentive.

> “当时中等资产的人大部分都被告了。” - the episode's claim about the breadth of denunciation.
