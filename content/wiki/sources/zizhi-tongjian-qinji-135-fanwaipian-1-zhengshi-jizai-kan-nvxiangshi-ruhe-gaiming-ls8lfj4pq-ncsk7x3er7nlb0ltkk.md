# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》135｜番外篇1 正史记载 看女相师如何改命？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]]番外 episode shifts from the main [[JuluBattle|巨鹿之战]] line into the legend-colored biography of [[XuFuPhysiognomist|许负 / Xu Fu (physiognomist)]]. The transcript frames her through [[ZhouyiDivination|《周易》占卜]], 八卦, and 相术, then follows her from birth omens and [[HuangshiGong|黄石公]]'s book gift to a politically dangerous refusal of [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]]'s summons. The available markdown ends just as she recognizes [[LiuBang|刘邦]] and tells her father to surrender the county seal, so the source page records only the incomplete episode segment.

## Key Claims
- The episode uses ancient 占卜, 八卦, 看相, and 知命 traditions to place [[XuFuPhysiognomist|许负]] inside a long-lived Chinese fate-reading culture.
- The host says [[Shiji|《史记》]] mentions the female physiognomist three times, while ordinary audiences know her less than male figures such as 袁天罡, [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]], or [[HuangshiGong|黄石公]].
- The transcript says she was born in 221 BCE, the year [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]] unified the realm, in Wen County of Henei Commandery, around present-day Wen County in Jiaozuo, Henan.
- The source presents her birth through miraculous details: blue light, a jade object bearing Wenwang's bagua image, and a reward from Qin Shi Huang to her family.
- As a child, she is said to smile or cry at visitors in ways later read as predictions of fortune or disaster.
- She studies with a local Confucian teacher, shows unusual memory and understanding of [[ZhouyiDivination|Zhouyi]] and 八卦, then is sent toward [[HuangshiGong|黄石公]] after the teacher decides he cannot instruct her further.
- [[HuangshiGong|黄石公]] is said to visit her family and give her a text called 《心器密纸》, which she combines with yin-yang and bagua learning to form her own 相人 method.
- When Qin Shi Huang later summons her to [[Xianyang|咸阳]], she feigns grave illness and refuses, telling her father that [[QinState|秦]] is about to collapse and that reading the emperor's face would be a death risk.
- After Qin Shi Huang dies at [[Shaqiu|沙丘]], the transcript says she changes her name out of guilt for failing to repay imperial favor.
- The transcript ends after she sees [[LiuBang|刘邦]], brings him home, and asks her father to hand over the county seal; later Liu Bang-related claims are not available in this source file.

## Key Quotes

> "让父亲交出县令的大印" - the point where the available transcript cuts off after Liu Bang appears.
