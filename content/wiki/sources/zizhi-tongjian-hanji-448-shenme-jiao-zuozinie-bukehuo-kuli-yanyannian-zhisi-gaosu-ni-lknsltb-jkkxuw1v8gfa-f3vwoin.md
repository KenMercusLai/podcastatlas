# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》448｜什么叫自作孽不可活？酷吏严延年之死告诉你

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode uses [[YanYannianWesternHan|严延年]] to argue that rule through terror can look efficient while destroying the trust a governor needs to survive. Yan neglects a Henan locust crisis, resents [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] for canceling his expected Zuo Fengyi promotion, is denounced by a terrified subordinate, and is finally executed by public beheading.

The second half continues the [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] succession-fragmentation thread from [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-446-yi-daode-bangjia-shenan-hanyanshou-shi-bushi-haoguan-liajvwsimqt0nbvcn-3rkgbmtjkr|Hanji 446]] into the collapse of Woyanqudi Chanyu. His killing and loss of support let [[HuhanyeChanyuWesternHan|呼韩邪单于]] rise, while the emergence of Tuqi Chanyu sets up the later five-chanyu crisis in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-449-yichu-huangdan-naoju-xiongnu-wuda-chanyu-duoquan-huopin-lo39-mygg6xxo8cwcyvpqzcpzhrm|Hanji 449]].

## Key Claims

- Yan Yannian's harsh reputation, skilled punitive writing, and frequent death sentences make even favored subordinates fear him.
- The Henan locust episode shows Yan's loss of public-duty focus after his expected promotion is canceled.
- The subordinate's self-killing accusation illustrates a terror-rule backfire: fear does not produce loyalty, but can push insiders toward irreversible exposure.
- Yan's mother interprets his fate as accumulated retribution for killing too many people and failing to govern through humane teaching.
- The episode treats Yan as a type case of the Western Han "cruel official," not merely as an unlucky individual.
- Woyanqudi Chanyu's cruelty similarly destroys internal Xiongnu support, causing his troops to scatter and his brother to refuse aid.
- Huhanye's rise and Woyanqudi's suicide mark the end of a long Xiongnu unity phase and lead into the later multi-chanyu struggle.

## Key Quotes

> "暴力绝不是治世的良药" - the episode's opening judgment on violent justice as a governing method.

> "自作孽不可活" - the title-level moral reading of Yan Yannian's end.
