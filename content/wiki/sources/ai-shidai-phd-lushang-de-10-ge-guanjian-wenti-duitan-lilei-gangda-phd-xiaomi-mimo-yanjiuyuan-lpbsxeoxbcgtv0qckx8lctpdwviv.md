# AI 时代，PhD 路上的 10 个关键问题｜对谈李磊：港大 PhD、小米 MiMo 研究员

## Summary
This [[ShizilukouCrossing|十字路口Crossing]] interview follows [[LiLeiXiaomiMiMo|李磊]], a University of Hong Kong NLP doctoral student and [[Xiaomi]] large-model post-training researcher, across research method, advisor choice, academic communication, industry transition, and AI-era career anxiety. Its central synthesis is [[DoctoralResearchOperatingMethod]]: choose consequential problems, isolate the smallest decisive assumption, test it cheaply, seek feedback early, and only then accelerate execution with AI and parallel work.

The episode also develops [[ResearchImpactBeyondAcceptance]], arguing that publication is a knowledge-sharing mechanism rather than the final reward, and [[BenchmarkAdoptionDesign]], which joins a meaningful new capability to realistic tasks, reproducibility, low-friction tooling, and ecosystem compatibility. Its later discussion links [[RecursiveSelfImprovement]], [[ResearchTaste]], large-scale reinforcement learning, and high-quality data: models can increasingly execute defined searches and improvement loops, while problem choice and the definition of valuable progress remain major human bottlenecks.

## Key Claims
- [[DoctoralResearchOperatingMethod]] combines strong fundamentals, important problem selection, rapid minimal-assumption tests, early feedback, and accelerated execution after the direction survives validation.
- Difficult and important problems can reward a small but rigorous advance, whereas easy crowded tasks may be copied or surpassed quickly; difficulty alone is not sufficient without consequence and testability.
- [[DoctoralTrainingCareerAlignment]] makes a PhD most defensible as protected time for deep curiosity and research formation, not as a required ticket to a large-company job.
- Advisor fit matters more than nearby school-ranking differences; applicants should inspect graduate outcomes and wellbeing, speak privately with current and former students, and ideally test the relationship through research-assistant work.
- [[ResearchImpactBeyondAcceptance]] treats code, data, maintenance, real adoption, and community feedback as evidence beyond paper counts, while recognizing that download and attention metrics can also be manipulated.
- Academic social media can shorten feedback cycles and help work cross an overloaded attention system, but it should amplify rather than replace rigorous research.
- [[BenchmarkAdoptionDesign]] requires a consequential capability, realistic use cases, reproducibility, usability, and compatibility with common evaluation tooling; [[VideoMME]] is presented as a long-video example.
- Model-assisted data generation, parameter search, and algorithm optimization are partial forms of [[RecursiveSelfImprovement]], but current loops usually retain human control over objectives and research direction.
- High-quality data is model-relative: examples that are useful for one capability level can be too easy, too hard, or economically unhelpful for another.

## Key Quotes
> "只要一直 build，就有好事发生" - the episode's shorthand for creating a persistent public trail of useful work rather than waiting for one outreach message to succeed.

> "论文迟早会中，饭要吃、觉要睡" - the closing reminder that publication anxiety does not suspend basic care.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction found. The episode's claim that papers are becoming an employment “bonus” rather than a gold standard is a practitioner hiring judgment, not evidence that publications have lost importance across institutions or disciplines.
- The research method is grounded mainly in AI and NLP, where experiments and feedback can be comparatively fast; it may transfer poorly to wet-lab, clinical, field, hardware, or other long-cycle research.
- Comparisons among mainland China, Hong Kong, and the United States are explicitly experience-limited, especially for U.S. visas, internships, and doctoral life.
- Biographical achievements, reviewer workload, model capability, training transparency, data-industry economics, labor-market change, and enrollment trends remain episode-attributed rather than independently audited here.
- Download counts, social reach, paper acceptance, and benchmark leadership are all partial and potentially gameable signals; none independently proves scientific importance or real-world value.
