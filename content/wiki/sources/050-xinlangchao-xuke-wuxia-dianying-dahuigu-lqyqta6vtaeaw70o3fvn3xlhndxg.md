# 050 新浪潮：徐克武侠电影大回顾

## Summary

This [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] episode surveys [[TsuiHark|徐克]]'s wuxia cinema from 《蝶变》 and 《新蜀山剑侠》 through the 1990s peak and later films such as 《七剑》, 《龙门飞甲》, and 《智取威虎山》. It identifies fast, emotionally forceful editing, distinctive art and costume design, stage-like action, and persistent technical experimentation as the durable authorial pattern, while treating narrative discontinuity, aggressive adaptation, crowded ensembles, and spectacle-first construction as recurring risks. Its central critical standard is that deviation from a source is not itself failure: a transformation succeeds when it builds coherent character, emotion, and cinematic form.

## Key Claims

- [[TsuiHark|Tsui Hark]]'s career is framed as early experimentation, a 1990s high point, and a post-2000 turn in which digital effects and technical exploration become increasingly prominent.
- 《蝶变》 and 《新蜀山剑侠》 establish a drive to mix genres, accelerate editing, and use new effects even when commercial or narrative results remain uneven.
- [[TechnologyDrivenWuxiaModernization|Technology-driven wuxia modernization]] operates both inside the stories, through guns, cameras, printing, telescopes, and mechanisms, and at the production level, through wirework, effects, 3D, montage, and spatial spectacle.
- [[TraditionalWuxiaActionCraft|Wuxia action craft]] can be airy and theatrical or bloody and bodily; 《刀》, 《七剑》, and later work show that roughness, pain, dust, and visible consequence can also organize martial spectacle.
- [[FunctionalAdaptationFidelity|Functional adaptation fidelity]] matters more than literal loyalty: 《笑傲江湖Ⅱ东方不败》 can depart radically yet create a persuasive new screen icon, while compression or rewriting fails when motives, ensemble relations, or causal links no longer hold.
- A “Tsui Hark film” may describe a recognizable system of creative control across producing, writing, casting, design, financing, and set work, but uncertain recollections do not erase credited directors or prove scene-level authorship.
- Later technical ambition remains productive when it supports pace, character, and dramatic tension; visual novelty alone cannot compensate for thin characterization or weak narrative propulsion.
