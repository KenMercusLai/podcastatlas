# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》386-1｜汉武帝时期，仁慈的太子刘据为何造反

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] short episode fills the missing escalation bridge between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-385-2-wugu-zhi-huo-zui-da-de-muhou-heishou-juran-shi-ta-lstnjhb3l6atr-syl4_dhlsgqgte|Hanji 385-2]]'s palace-search trap and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-386-2-diwang-wuqing-huanghou-weizifu-beipo-zisha-lrvdfib62oczcy7loqss4-prkgl|Hanji 386-2]]'s open Chang'an fighting. It presents [[LiuJuCrownPrinceWesternHan|卫太子刘据]] as initially wanting to reach [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] and explain himself, but shifting to forged-command violence after [[JiangChongWesternHan|江充]] controls palace access and direct appeal fails.

The durable synthesis is that Liu Ju's rebellion frame hardens through information failure as much as through armed action. Liu Ju kills Jiang Chong, gains [[EmpressWeiZifuWesternHan|卫子夫]]'s support for palace vehicles, weapons, and guards, and announces Jiang Chong's treason, while [[SuWenWesternHan|苏文]], a cautious messenger, and [[LiuQuliWesternHan|刘屈氂]] help move Han Wudi from initial father-son mitigation toward a formal suppression order.

## Key Claims

- Liu Ju's first reaction to [[ShiDeWesternHan|Shi De]]'s advice is hesitation: he wants to go to Han Wudi, plead, and clear himself rather than immediately forge an edict or kill ministers.
- Jiang Chong's control of the palace blocks Liu Ju's access to Han Wudi, turning ordinary appeal into a closed route.
- Liu Ju sends men disguised as imperial envoys to arrest Jiang Chong and others; Han Shuo resists and is killed, while Zhang Gan escapes to report at Ganquan.
- After committing false-command violence and still not knowing whether Han Wudi is alive, Liu Ju decides to mobilize.
- Wei Zifu's support lets Liu Ju draw on palace vehicles, weapons, and guards, making his action materially larger than a private killing of Jiang Chong.
- Su Wen escapes Liu Ju's arrest and reports rebellion to Han Wudi; Wudi first reads Liu Ju as frightened and angry at Jiang Chong rather than purely treasonous.
- The decisive messenger report says the crown prince's rebellion is settled, after which Han Wudi orders Liu Quli to seal Chang'an and suppress the rebellion.

## Key Quotes

> "太子害怕，又痛恨江充" - the episode's reported first judgment by Han Wudi before the messenger report hardens the rebellion frame.

> "太子造反" - the Chang'an rumor and final official frame that turns the father-son crisis into military suppression.
