# 022 武侠：成人童话|漫话古龙《陆小凤传奇2：绣花大盗》

## Summary

This [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] episode reads [[GuLong|古龙]]'s [[EmbroideredBanditGuLong|《绣花大盗》]] as an effective but causally uneven martial-arts mystery. It values [[LuXiaofeng|陆小凤]] and [[GongsunDaniangGuLong|公孙大娘]] matching wits through three contests, the light-heavy reversal in the final fight with [[JinJiulingGuLong|金九龄]], and one economical scene in which [[HuaManlou|花满楼]] gives a blinded man reason to live. At the same time, it questions the culprit's motive, retroactive information-sharing devices, thin inference, and female characterization built from labels more often than sustained conduct.

The hosts use those contrasts to describe Gu Long's wuxia as a [[WuxiaAdultFairyTale|wuxia adult fairy tale]]: friendship, trust, pleasure, survival, and the expectation that evil will lose create an idealized emotional world even when the mystery cannot bear exhaustive scrutiny. [[XueBingGuLong|薛冰]] supplies the book's most domestic romantic warmth, while 公孙大娘 offers a more equal contest of force and intelligence; the difference also exposes the episode's concern that Gu Long more reliably differentiates male friends and rivals than women.

## Key Claims

- The novel uses a recurring Gu Long mystery structure: spectacular crimes establish a hidden mastermind, the investigator passes through staged suspects and traps, and a trusted participant in the investigation proves to be the culprit.
- [[XueBingGuLong|薛冰]] and 陆小凤 gain warmth through teasing, shared food, anger, and his anxiety after her disappearance, but the hosts find her shifts among shyness, quietness, volatility, and jealousy insufficiently prepared.
- [[GongsunDaniangGuLong|公孙大娘]] is more distinctive through martial skill, intelligence, disguise, command, and her morally mixed loyalty. The hosts see her as 陆小凤's strongest equal in the episode without treating their rivalry as a canonical romance.
- Gu Long's male friendships are presented as a more dependable center than fixed romance: protagonists may change lovers, but characters such as [[HuaManlou|花满楼]] provide trusted companionship and a different moral or emotional capacity.
- The three contests between 陆小凤 and 公孙大娘 work through fatigue, strategic weakness, disguised targets, smell, and later cooperation. Their reversals are memorable, although the secret exchange of information can feel like a retrospective patch.
- [[JinJiulingGuLong|金九龄]] is revealed as the embroidered bandit. His expensive tastes and desire to design a perfect crime provide motives, but the hosts find the money explanation and the inference chain weaker than the final surprise.
- The final duel reverses the expected qualities of its weapons: an eighty-seven-jin iron hammer becomes quiet and agile, while a tiny embroidery needle produces force and pressure. The design is praised even as the final thrown-blade kill is questioned.
- [[HuaManlou|花满楼]] telling the newly blinded 江重威 that he too is blind and lives well gives the character's kindness behavioral force with very little exposition, strengthening [[WuxiaIndirectCharacterization|indirect characterization]].
- The film adaptation's merger of 薛冰 and 公孙大娘 preserves the romance and simplifies alignment, but loses the latter's mature, forceful independence; nostalgia and first exposure also shape the hosts' casting judgments.

## Key Quotes

> “若是没酒喝，我为什么要活到三百岁。” - 木道人's line, used by the hosts to connect pleasure in living with Gu Long's adult-fairy-tale tone.

> “花满楼总在设法让别人活下去。” - the episode's summary of the character established by his brief exchange with 江重威.
