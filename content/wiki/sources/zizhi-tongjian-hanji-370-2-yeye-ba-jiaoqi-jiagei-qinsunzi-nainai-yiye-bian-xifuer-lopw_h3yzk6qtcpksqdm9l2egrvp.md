# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》370-2｜爷爷把娇妻嫁给亲孙子，奶奶一夜变媳妇儿？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Yuanfeng 6 / 105 BCE and centers on [[LiuXijunPrincessWesternHan|刘细君]] after her marriage to [[LiejiaomiWusun|猎骄靡]], the elderly ruler of [[Wusun|乌孙]]. Language difference, separate residence, competing Han and [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] marriage ties, and Wusun succession custom make her household position part of a three-sided frontier contest.

The durable synthesis is that marriage diplomacy can produce strategic alignment by transferring its adjustment costs onto the bride. When Liejiaomi asks Xijun to marry his grandson and successor [[JunxumiWusun|军须靡]], her appeal to [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] is rejected with an order to follow Wusun custom because Han wants Wusun help against Xiongnu. Xijun complies and has a daughter, Shaofu; after Xijun's death, [[XieyouPrincessWesternHan|解忧公主]] is sent to continue the same alliance route.

## Key Claims

- Han sends Xijun to Wusun with a large escort and material endowment, but wealth does not remove language isolation, unfamiliar custom, or the large age gap with Liejiaomi.
- Liejiaomi makes the Han princess right wife, while a Xiongnu princess becomes left wife; because the episode says Wusun ranks the left above the right, household status mirrors Wusun's balancing between two stronger powers.
- The elderly Liejiaomi asks Xijun to marry his grandson and designated successor Junxumi under Wusun custom.
- Xijun objects and writes to Han, but Wudi orders her to follow local custom because he wants Wusun cooperation against Xiongnu.
- Xijun marries Junxumi, has a daughter named Shaofu, and is credited by the episode with helping keep Han-Wusun relations friendly.
- After Xijun's death, Han sends Xieyou to marry Junxumi and continue the alliance; the episode summarizes Xieyou's later remarriages and eventual return to Chang'an.
- The episode's claim that Xijun died around 87 BCE at age forty-four conflicts with the sequence it narrates and is retained as source-scoped rather than adopted as settled chronology.

## Key Quotes

> “从其国俗，欲与乌孙共灭胡。” - the imperial reply as given by the episode, making the alliance objective override Xijun's objection.

> “无论有什么屈辱，刘细君都必须忍下来。” - the host's interpretation of what the reply demanded from Xijun.

## Contradictions

- The episode places Xijun's death around Han Wudi's Houyuan 2 / 87 BCE, but it also makes her death precede Xieyou's Wusun mission and appears in the Yuanfeng 6 / 105 BCE sequence immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-371-2-shangdi-shuyu-zhongguo-zhexia-shichui-le-ltllclatwddfxlawrdpgziqlp3xq|Hanji 371-2]]'s Taichu 1 / 104 BCE material. The death year and age are therefore not adopted.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-413-1-yiren-jia-sanwang-tade-gushi-yuanbi-zhaojun-chusai-geng-chuanqi-ltmt4y60eusm8a_mwthnfg5cys6p|Hanji 413-1]] compresses Xieyou's early Wusun story by moving directly from Xijun's death to Wengguimi; this source instead says Xieyou first married Junxumi. The new detail qualifies that earlier summary rather than displacing its later Wengguimi alliance layer.
- Transcript normalization: “列郊弥” is routed to [[LiejiaomiWusun|猎骄靡]], and “君须弥” to [[JunxumiWusun|军须靡]]. “昆莫 / 昆弥” and “岑陬” are ruler or office titles in the received account, not additional personal names here.
- Scope caution: Xijun's exact age, cause and date of death, the private emotional course of either marriage, the rank rule for left and right wives, and the claim that she personally secured later military results remain bounded to the episode unless independently supported by another source note.
