# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》980丨吕布险杀曹操？看看一句话如何扭转历史

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s [[PuyangBattleLateHan|濮阳之战]] crisis immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-981-sanguo-zui-gang-moushi-chengyu-yi-ju-hua-rang-caocao-tanfu-lhlhlrmf2gvspwyvjkxg6kz44fth|Hanji 981]]. [[PuyangTianClan|濮阳田氏]] lure Cao Cao into [[PuyangLateHan|濮阳]] with a false inside-response signal, [[DianWei|典韦]] stabilizes the front, and Cao Cao survives after misleading a [[LyuBu|吕布]] cavalryman with the yellow-horse line. The host turns the episode into [[CrisisDeceptionSurvival|临危欺敌求生]] and a late-Han logistics lesson: morale repair, weapons preparation, locust damage, and grain exhaustion make [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] recovery depend on more than personal bravery.

## Key Claims
- [[PuyangTianClan|濮阳田氏]] pretend to act as Cao Cao's inside supporters, and Cao Cao accepts the signal strongly enough to enter [[PuyangLateHan|濮阳]] and burn the east gate behind him.
- [[LyuBu|吕布]] personally presses the fight, and Cao Cao's side spends the day in a dangerous city battle rather than an orderly planned assault.
- [[DianWei|典韦]] holds the forward position under arrows, waits until the enemy is close, and drives back Lü Bu's troops, creating the opening for Cao Cao's withdrawal.
- Cao Cao is reportedly seized by a Lü Bu cavalryman who does not recognize him; Cao Cao points the pursuer toward a rider on a yellow horse and escapes.
- The episode treats that moment as [[CrisisDeceptionSurvival|临危欺敌求生]]: a commander's survival can depend on calm deception after the battlefield plan has already failed.
- Cao Cao rewards Dian Wei by promoting him and assigning him to guard the command tent, turning rescue behavior into a visible morale and security signal.
- Cao Cao then reassures the troops and prepares tools and weapons for another attack, which the host reads through [[MilitaryMoraleThroughSharedHardship|同甘共苦式士气]] rather than as simple boldness.
- The later hundred-day fighting around Puyang becomes an attrition problem. Locusts damage food supply, Lü Bu's grain runs out, and both sides withdraw.
- After the withdrawal, Cao Cao returns toward Juancheng while Lü Bu attacks [[ChengshiCountyLateHan|乘氏县]], is defeated by [[LiJinLateHan|李进]], and moves east toward Shanyang.
- The host uses [[SunziBingfa|《孙子兵法》]], [[Liutao|《六韬》]], and [[Wuzi|《吴子》]] to frame Cao Cao as flexible and Lü Bu as brave but insufficiently strategic; those evaluations remain source-scoped.

## Key Quotes
> "骑黄马逃跑的就是曹操." - Cao Cao's reported misdirection to the Lü Bu cavalryman.

> "败中求活，以诈制胜." - host summary of the escape moment.

> "以正合，以奇胜." - [[SunziBingfa|《孙子兵法》]] phrase the host uses to read the battle.
