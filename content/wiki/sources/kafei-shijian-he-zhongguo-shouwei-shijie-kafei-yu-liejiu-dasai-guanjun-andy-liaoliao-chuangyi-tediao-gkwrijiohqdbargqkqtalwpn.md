# 咖啡时间：和中国首位世界咖啡与烈酒大赛冠军Andy聊聊创意特调

## Summary
Recorded at the sixth CBT Coffee Youth Festival, this [[DaxiaoDianbo|大小电波]] conversation has [[LiuZhanhong|柳展鸿 Andy]] explain the method behind his Saturn-themed work and his victory at the [[WorldCoffeeInGoodSpiritsChampionship|World Coffee in Good Spirits Championship]]. He treats coffee, spirits, and other ingredients as equal design materials joined by flavor balance, then makes theme, aroma, texture, finish, visuals, language, and atmosphere serve one coherent experience. The episode also connects competition creativity to commercial beverage consulting: an idea becomes useful only when cost, steps, ingredient count, safety, supply stability, teamwork, and brand fit make it repeatable.

## Key Claims
- [[ThemeLedBeverageDesign]] begins with a position or theme, selects ingredients that embody it, and translates the idea into language an unfamiliar audience can understand.
- Coffee need not dominate a coffee-and-spirits drink; Andy describes the developer as a bridge among coequal ingredients whose job is to create mutual support and overall balance.
- [[MultisensoryBeverageExperience]] makes good flavor a baseline rather than the endpoint: aroma, mouthfeel, finish, visual presentation, atmosphere, and emotional connection determine whether a drink remains memorable.
- [[BeverageDevelopmentFeasibility]] turns creative proposals into operational products by accounting for brand logic, cost, steps, ingredient count, safety, stable supply, and lead times of three to six months.
- [[CreativeCompetitionTeamInfrastructure]] explains championship performance as more than individual talent: coaching, emotional support, travel and ingredient logistics, roasting, company resources, and more than two years of iteration supported the final presentation.
- Stable ingredients reduce preventable variance and free a competitor to focus on service, performance, and language, while unverifiable ingredient stories should remain separate from direct tasting judgments.
- Andy reframes innovation as first changing one's own mind and behavior—“创心”—while keeping final responsibility for the work's theme after listening to team advice.

## Key Quotes
> “创心” — Andy's compressed account of innovation beginning with a change in mindset.

> “还可以这样做” — the modest influence he hopes championship visibility can provide.

> “拿到一个麦克风” — his description of the title as an opportunity for continued public sharing.
