## Summary
This [[ZhixingXiaojiuguan|知行小酒馆]] conversation with [[ShaonanFlomo|少楠]] argues that abundant AI answers have shifted information anxiety toward [[AIDecisionAnxiety|decision anxiety]]: people still have to choose which problem matters, which answer fits, and where to commit finite time. It reframes [[Flomo]] and note-taking around [[PersonalContextKnowledge|personal context]] rather than generic collection, and treats reading, writing, and sustained practice as [[AttentionCommitmentLearning|attention commitments]] that AI can support but not compress away. Personalized systems can become an [[AIAsPersonalMirror|AI mirror]] or emotional container, but their value depends on voluntary records, interpretation, verification, and retained human judgment.

## Key Claims
- AI can produce many conditionally valid answers without knowing which conditions match a user's purpose; the scarce capability therefore moves from retrieval toward problem selection, judgment, and commitment.
- Learning every new tool can become avoidance of the harder question—what the user actually wants to do—and keeping every life option open can intensify anxiety rather than preserve freedom.
- Domain experience determines whether a user can evaluate and modify AI output; practical experiments generate stronger evidence than abstract AI FOMO about whether a tool deserves further investment.
- Generic excerpts and searchable facts have declining archival value, while records of personal experience, emotional state, changed beliefs, decisions, and consequences create useful [[PersonalContextKnowledge]].
- Reading and writing remain valuable because understanding requires sustained attention, friction, association, and memory formation; a fast summary does not eliminate that time requirement.
- Personalized AI can detect patterns in voluntary records and help a user feel seen, but it may weight context poorly, retain what a person wants forgotten, miss changing salience, or offer comfort without full human understanding.
- AI can filter information toward durable work, intellectual difference, and serendipity, but recommendations remain dependent on model quality, user context, and verification.
