# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1082丨诸葛亮一段话点破乱世风云，太敢说了

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[SimaHuiLateHan|司马徽]] and [[XuShuLateHan|徐庶]] recommending [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]], [[LiuBei|刘备]] visiting him three times, and the resulting [[LongzhongPlan|隆中对]]. It presents the plan as a joined political, diplomatic, territorial, and governing program: avoid direct confrontation with [[CaoCao|曹操]], cooperate with [[SunQuan|孙权]], acquire [[Jingzhou|荆州]] and [[YizhouLateHan|益州]], manage frontier relations, and strengthen internal administration.

The episode's distinctive interpretation is that “兴汉” works as a legitimacy claim even if literal restoration is doubtful. By comparing Zhuge Liang's later formula with [[LuSu|鲁肃]]'s earlier [[TaShangCeBipartiteStrategy|榻上策二分天下]], it distinguishes hard diagnosis from mobilizing political language. The closing claim that [[GuanYu|关羽]] and [[ZhangFei|张飞]] resisted a new adviser and new operating rules remains source-scoped pending the next installment.

## Key Claims

- Sima Hui's “卧龙、凤雏” appraisal and Xu Shu's separate recommendation give Liu Bei repeated signals that Zhuge Liang deserves direct attention.
- Liu Bei's three visits are presented as visible deference that makes his demand for strategic counsel credible rather than as a routine summons.
- The Longzhong Plan links opponent assessment, alliance choice, territorial sequencing, frontier accommodation, domestic administration, and talent recruitment into one program.
- The host reads Han restoration as a legitimacy symbol that lets Liu Bei's Han-clan identity compete politically with Cao Cao's control of the emperor.
- Lu Su's earlier claim that the Han cannot be restored and Zhuge Liang's later claim that it can be revived are treated as different strategic scripts built on a shared recognition of dynastic breakdown.
- The episode argues that talent matters only when a leader can recognize, approach, and use it; this modern management lesson is interpretive rather than independent historical evidence.
- Guan Yu and Zhang Fei's alleged jealousy is reframed as resistance to new decision rules, but the transcript does not establish their motives or the full organizational conflict.

## Key Quotes

> “曹操不可争锋，孙权可以为援而不可图。” - the episode's compressed opponent-and-ally boundary in the Longzhong Plan.

> “汉室可兴。” - the legitimacy-bearing formula that the host contrasts with Lu Su's earlier diagnosis.
