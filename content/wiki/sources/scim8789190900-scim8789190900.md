# Master Stress: Tools for Managing Stress & Anxiety

## Summary
This early solo [[HubermanLab]] episode has [[AndrewHuberman]] frame stress as a generic brain-body mobilization system whose value depends on timescale, context, and recovery. It connects [[PhysiologicalSigh]], [[StressResponseRecovery]], [[AutonomicStressTraining]], [[AcuteStressImmunePriming]], and [[BrainBodyEmotionMapping]] through real-time breathing, deliberate bounded arousal, panoramic vision, sleep, and social connection. Its serotonin, tachykinin, immune, supplement, and disease-related claims remain source-scoped public education rather than individualized treatment.

## Key Claims
- Stressors are triggers, while stress is the physiological and psychological response; acute mobilization can support action, focus, and short-lived immune responses, whereas persistent activation and sleep disruption mark increasing cost.
- [[PhysiologicalSigh]] is presented as a double inhale followed by a long exhale that can recruit collapsed alveoli and reduce acute arousal; exhale-emphasized breathing is distinguished from inhale-emphasized activation.
- [[AutonomicStressTraining]] uses safe, voluntary activation followed by calm mental regulation to raise stress tolerance, with cold, intense exercise, and cyclic breathing offered as examples rather than universal prescriptions.
- Panoramic visual attention is proposed as a way to loosen stress-linked tunnel vision and separate mental panic from bodily activation, but the supplied summary does not establish effect size or clinical efficacy.
- [[AcuteStressImmunePriming]] gains early provenance from the discussed human endotoxin challenge, while symptom reduction is not equated with infection prevention, pathogen clearance, or treatment.
- Long-term stress management prioritizes sleep, movement, diet, trusted social connection, and delight; serotonin and [[SocialIsolationTachykinin|tachykinin]] are proposed mechanisms, not complete explanations of human wellbeing or isolation.
- L-theanine, ashwagandha, and melatonin are discussed with medical cautions and should not be converted from public education into unsupervised dosing or treatment advice.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is adopted. The episode strengthens the wiki's existing distinction between useful acute activation and harmful unrecovered stress.
- The source's simplified heart-rate, serotonin, tachykinin, stress-timescale, panoramic-vision, immune, and supplement explanations lack complete methods or individualized context in the supplied summary and remain source-scoped.
- The endotoxin challenge models an inflammatory response rather than ordinary infection; fewer symptoms do not establish improved pathogen clearance or clinical treatment.
- Hyperventilation or breath holding must not be practiced near water, while driving, or where fainting could cause injury. Cold, intense exercise, breathing practices, melatonin, L-theanine, and ashwagandha also require person-specific medical and medication context.
