# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1030丨手握好牌，公孙瓒为何打得稀烂？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] branch around [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s pressure on [[YijingFortress|易京]]. It contrasts [[SunCe|孙策]]'s ability to understand and trust [[TaishiCi|太史慈]] with Gongsun Zan's damaged command relationships, then argues that walls, grain, and apparent good cards cannot compensate for lost initiative and reciprocal trust.

Yuan Shao first seeks reconciliation or cooperation by letter, but Gongsun Zan ignores him and relies on the belief that no one can maintain a multi-year siege. When Yuan Shao advances to Yijing, Gongsun Zan's earlier refusal to rescue surrounded subordinates returns as organizational failure: southern commanders surrender, flee, or refuse support, forcing him to send [[GongsunXuLateHan|公孙旭]] toward the [[HeishanArmy|黑山军]] and then abandon a sortie plan after [[GuanJingLateHan|关静]] warns that leaving would break the remaining garrison.

## Key Claims
- The opening Taishi Ci case turns talent use into a trust problem: Sun Ce can authorize Taishi Ci because he understands his promise-keeping character.
- Yuan Shao's letter shows that the northern war still has a diplomatic off-ramp, but Gongsun Zan treats it as irrelevant and tightens defense instead.
- Gongsun Zan believes Yijing's stores and walls will outlast Yuan Shao's ability to remain in the field.
- His earlier policy of refusing to rescue surrounded generals is meant to force self-reliance, but it destroys confidence that command will reciprocate risk.
- When Yuan Shao attacks, Gongsun Zan's southern commanders do not rescue him; some surrender and some disperse.
- Gongsun Zan sends [[GongsunXuLateHan|公孙旭]] to seek help from [[HeishanArmy|Black Mountain]] commanders.
- Gongsun Zan considers leaving Yijing with elite cavalry, joining Black Mountain forces, counterattacking [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]], and cutting Yuan Shao's retreat.
- [[GuanJingLateHan|关静]] stops the plan by pointing out that the remaining troops are held together by family presence inside the fortress and Gongsun Zan's visible command.
- The episode sharpens [[FortressSecurityIllusion|筑城避世安全幻觉]]: Yijing gives physical defense but cannot repair broken rescue obligations, morale, or strategic initiative.
- The host's lesson "消极自保必亡" is treated as source-scoped strategy language, not as a complete biography of Gongsun Zan.
