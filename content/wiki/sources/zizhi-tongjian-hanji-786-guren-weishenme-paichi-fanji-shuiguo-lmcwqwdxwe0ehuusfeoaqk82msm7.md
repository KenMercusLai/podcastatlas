## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 110-114 CE [[EmperorAnOfHan|汉安帝]] and [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|和熹邓皇后]] regency layer before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-787-chuanqi-huanghou-yanji-shi-ruhe-yibubu-shangwei-de-lslngjnedmqgh8h5nfc-qmyahqnm|Hanji 787]]. It moves from Zhang Bolu's defeat and a [[HanyangCommanderyLateHan|汉阳郡]] rebellion into the 112 CE seasonal-fresh-food edict, then uses repeated locusts, droughts, earthquakes, landslides, eclipses, and Qiang campaigns to frame a compound An-era crisis.

The durable synthesis is that the "out-of-season fruit" order is not just a food taboo. The episode makes it a case of [[SeasonalRitualConsumptionRestraint|时令礼法式饮食约束]] inside a wider disaster and tribute-burden environment, while the closing Han-Qiang discussion links [[MaXianLateHan|马贤]], [[XianlingQiang|先零羌]], [[DuJigongLateHan|杜季贡]], [[LinChangQiang|陵昌]], and climate stress to the longer economic drain of frontier war.

## Key Claims

- In 110 CE, Zhang Bolu attacks Donglai again, is defeated by Qingzhou inspector Fa Xiong, flees to Liaodong, and is killed by Li Jiu, temporarily settling the Qingzhou pirate problem.
- Hanyang rebels Du Qi, Du Jigong, Wang Xin, and others join Qiang forces and occupy Shanggui; Hanyang administrator Zhao Bo has Du Qi assassinated, while Du Jigong and Wang Xin continue resistance.
- In 112 CE, the court criticizes fresh tribute foods that violate seasonality, including items artificially warmed by fire or dug out when only sprouting, and orders imperial tomb, ancestral-temple, and palace foods to be offered only after ripening.
- The seasonal-edict branch joins ritual timing to local burden: elite demand for rare fresh foods becomes politically suspect when disasters and frontier war are already pressuring commanderies.
- Repeated locusts, drought, landslides, earthquakes, ground-splitting, eclipses, amnesties, office changes, and pay restoration place Deng-regency administration inside [[RegencyUnderCompoundCrisis|复合危机下的摄政]] before the later 115-121 layers.
- Tang Xi defeats and kills Wang Xin; Du Jigong flees to the Qiang leader Dianling, and after Dianling's death the young successor Lingchang uses Langmo and appoints Du as a general.
- In 113 CE, Hou Ba and [[MaXianLateHan|马贤]] attack a [[XianlingQiang|先零羌]] branch in [[AndingCommandery|安定郡]], adding an earlier Ma Xian and Xianling Qiang layer before their already indexed 116-120 CE sequence.
- In 114 CE, the court builds forts and stores grain at passes, while Qiang attacks on Yong, Wudu, Hanzhong, and the Didao defeat show frontier stress widening into state-capacity pressure.
- The host argues that Han-Qiang war damaged Eastern Han economic foundations by hollowing out Guanzhong, population, and farmland, but the episode keeps the full climate-war explanation for the next installment.
- The first large Han-Qiang war is framed as more than ethnic oppression: a Han official's seizure of a Qiang woman, the husband's killing of the official, and escalating official pursuit create fear that helps turn a local incident into tribal rebellion.

## Key Quotes

> "不合乎时令的东西不吃" - classical dietary and ritual timing frame cited by the episode.

> "用火熏暖催熟" - example of artificial early production criticized by the 112 CE edict.

> "气候因素很重要" - host's closing pointer toward a climate-war explanation.
