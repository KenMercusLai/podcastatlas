# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》324-1｜李广舍去脸面，求了武帝什么事？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the mobilization prequel to the [[MobeiBattleWesternHan|漠北之战]] and [[LiGuangHanGeneral|李广]]'s final campaign. It presents [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] as exploiting [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] confidence that Han forces could not cross the desert in fighting condition, then describes two years of preparation, a large cavalry and logistics mobilization, and separate commands under [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]] and [[HuoQubing|霍去病]] whose departure routes change after new intelligence about the chanyu.

Li Guang repeatedly asks to join despite Wudi's concern about his age and is eventually appointed front general under Wei Qing. The permission is qualified from the start: Wudi privately warns Wei Qing that Li Guang has been unlucky and must not obstruct the campaign. The episode treats that warning as important to Li Guang's later fate, but it stops before the reassignment, route failure, inquiry, and suicide developed in Hanji 325-2.

## Key Claims

- Wudi plans a deep-desert search for the Xiongnu main force because he believes Xiongnu expects Han cavalry to lose combat power while crossing the desert.
- The episode says the court prepares for two years after the Hexi campaigns, trains one hundred thousand elite cavalry, mobilizes a much larger logistics force, and requisitions fourteen wan horses plus extensive supplies.
- Wei Qing and Huo Qubing each receive fifty thousand cavalry; the episode presents the strongest deep-assault riders as assigned to Huo.
- The original departure plan sends Huo from Dingxiang toward the chanyu and Wei from Dai, but intelligence that the chanyu has moved east causes Wudi to reverse those routes.
- Li Guang repeatedly petitions to serve because he sees the campaign as a final opportunity to erase earlier shame and win distinction.
- Wudi first refuses because Li Guang is old, then yields and appoints him front general under Wei Qing alongside [[GongsunHeWesternHan|公孙贺]], [[ZhaoShiqiWesternHan|赵食其]], and [[CaoXiangWesternHan|曹襄]].
- Wudi privately tells Wei Qing that Li Guang has been unlucky in prior campaigns and should not be allowed to endanger the objective; the episode presents this distrust as a precursor to the later reassignment.

## Key Quotes

> "李广难封。" - the inherited reputation frame used to introduce Li Guang's last campaign opportunity.

> "命不好。" - the episode's compressed rendering of Wudi's private warning about Li Guang.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with existing wiki content was found. This episode ends before Hanji 325-2 and supplies the petition, appointment, distrust, and campaign-planning prehistory to that later account.
- Wudi's desire to destroy the Xiongnu people is treated as the episode's total-war rhetoric, not as a demonstrated literal operational outcome; the chanyu survives Mobei and Xiongnu power continues.
- The two-year preparation period, troop and horse totals, force-quality allocation, original and revised routes, intelligence report, exact appointments, repeated dialogue, Li Guang's motive, and Wudi's private words remain episode-attributed pending primary-text comparison.
- The episode says Wudi's warning greatly determines Li Guang's end. It establishes prior distrust but does not by itself prove that Wudi ordered the later reassignment, intended Li Guang to fail, or caused the missing-guide episode.
