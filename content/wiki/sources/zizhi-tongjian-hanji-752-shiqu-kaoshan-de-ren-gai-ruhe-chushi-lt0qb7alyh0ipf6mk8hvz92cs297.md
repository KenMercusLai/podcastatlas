# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》752丨失去靠山的人该如何处世？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the early [[EmperorZhangOfHan|汉章帝]] layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-753-banchao-beigao-heizhuang-lt-sksxrz30sawit81eqbeonhzv|Hanji 753]]. It follows [[LiangGuirenHeLateHan|梁贵人]]'s family being destroyed after [[EmpressDowagerDouHeLateHan|窦皇后]] treats [[EmperorHeOfHan|刘肇]]'s crown-prince elevation as a threat to Dou-family maternal status, then contrasts that with the [[MaFamilyZhangEraEasternHan|Zhang-era Ma family]] losing protection after [[EmpressMaMingdeEasternHan|明德马皇后]]'s death.

The second half shifts from the vulnerable to the overprotected. [[DouXianEasternHan|窦宪]] and [[DouDuEasternHan|窦笃]] gain palace access and rewards, [[FifthLunEasternHan|第五伦]] warns against outer-relative overreach, Dou Xian forcefully takes [[QinshuiPrincessEasternHan|沁水公主]]'s estate and tries to suppress the answer before Zhangdi, and [[ZhouYuLuoyangEasternHan|周纡]] later shows that enforcing order against imperial relatives can still endanger the enforcer. The durable synthesis is a three-part power warning: lost patronage makes display dangerous, gained patronage makes restraint harder, and known wrongdoing left unpunished weakens deterrence.

## Key Claims

- Liang Guiren's family becomes dangerous to Dou interests because Liu Zhao's crown-prince status could make the Liang household visible as the prince's birth-mother line.
- Empress Dou's jealousy and Dou-family accusation route turn Liang Song's alleged rebellion case into imprisonment, family exile to Jiuzhen, and the deaths of Liang Guiren and her sister in grief.
- After Empress Ma dies, Ma Liao remains personally cautious but fails to restrain the Ma household, making his brothers' guests, luxury, horses, and Qiang-Hu exactions politically actionable.
- [[YangZhongEasternHan|杨终]]'s warning to Ma Liao frames post-patronage survival as lowering display, disciplining juniors, and avoiding guests who convert status into risk.
- Zhangdi's restrictions and removals show that a formerly protected in-law family can lose guests and leverage quickly once imperial confidence shifts.
- Fifth Lun warns that Dou Xian's empress-side kinship, guard command, access, and guest network are already forming an outer-relative danger.
- Dou Xian's forced purchase of Qinshui Princess's estate and attempted silencing of the answer before Zhangdi show power desire moving from palace prestige into property coercion and truth suppression.
- Zhangdi rebukes Dou Xian with the Zhao Gao "pointing deer as horse" comparison and forces return of the estate, but his refusal to punish becomes the episode's clearest [[KnownMisconductNonPunishment|known-misconduct non-punishment]] warning.
- Zhou Yu's Luoyang enforcement targets imperial relatives rather than ordinary strongmen, and his arrest after Huo Yan blocks Dou Du shows that correct enforcement can still rebound when it touches protected kin.

## Key Quotes

> "没有靠山，低调谨慎" - the episode's practical frame for vulnerable actors after protection disappears.

> "指鹿为马" - Zhangdi's comparison for Dou Xian's attempt to block truthful response about the estate.

> "知奸恶而不能去" - the Sima Guang warning attached to Zhangdi's non-punishment of Dou Xian.
