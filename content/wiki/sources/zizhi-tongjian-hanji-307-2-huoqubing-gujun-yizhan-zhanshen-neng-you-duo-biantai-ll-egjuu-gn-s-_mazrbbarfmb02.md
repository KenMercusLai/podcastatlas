# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》307-2｜霍去病孤军一战，战神能有多变态？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode closes the [[FirstHexiCampaignWesternHan|第一次河西之战]] begun in Hanji 307-1. It says [[HuoQubing|霍去病]] pursues [[HunyeKingWesternHan|浑邪王]] and [[XiutuKingWesternHan|休屠王]] north and south through the Hexi field until geography near Gaolan Mountain and the Yellow River leaves their forces little room to continue withdrawing, after which the [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] side chooses battle.

The episode presents the resulting engagement as the culmination of Huo's high-tempo cavalry method: speed creates the encounter, while close combat and a claimed Han weapons advantage turn it into a major victory. Reported results include 8,900 killed from a 13,000-person opposing force, rulers and senior officers killed or captured, the seizure of the Xiutu group's sacrificial golden figure, a 2,200-household reward from [[HanWudi|汉武帝]], and construction of a Yellow River fort called Jincheng. These names, figures, motives, tactical details, trophy meaning, fort identification, and causal claims remain source-scoped rather than independently verified.

## Key Claims

- Huo Qubing continues moving north and south around the Yanzhi Mountain area while pursuing Hunye King and Xiutu King.
- The episode says the opposing force is compressed toward Gaolan Mountain, the Yellow River, and the Han frontier, making renewed battle preferable to continued flight.
- It describes Huo as actively wanting decisive combat after a long pursuit and attributes similar eagerness to Han soldiers seeking merit, but these inner states are narrative interpretation.
- The battle is presented as cavalry collision and close combat in which Han weapons provide an advantage.
- The episode reports 8,900 opposing dead from a force of 13,000, the death or capture of rulers and officials, and the escape of Hunye King and Xiutu King with a small remainder.
- A sacrificial golden figure associated with the Xiutu group is said to become Han war booty, giving the victory symbolic as well as military weight.
- The host summarizes Huo's method as speed against slowness, strength against weakness, deep penetration, and local replenishment, then says Wudi adds 2,200 households to Huo's fief and Huo builds a Yellow River fort named Jincheng.

## Key Quotes

> “深入敌境，利于速战。” - the episode's compact explanation of why Huo seeks a decisive engagement after deep pursuit.

> “以快打慢、以强打弱、深入敌后、就地补给。” - the host's summary of the operational method attributed to Huo.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. Hanji 307-1 supplies the campaign's opening method, while this episode supplies its pursuit and closing engagement; neither compact summary is a complete campaign reconstruction.
- Hanji 307-1 reports seven thousand losses from Huo's ten-thousand force, while this episode again describes a ten-thousand-person Han force after two thousand li of continuous fighting. The sources do not reconcile whether these figures refer to different accounting points, rhetorical compression, or incompatible summaries.
- “孤军,” the north-south route, Yanzhi and Gaolan identifications, distance, force balance, casualty ratio, battle sequence, weapons advantage, soldiers' motives, names and ranks of killed or captured leaders, booty inventory, reward, and escape details require primary-source and specialist comparison.
- The source's “医知察的儿子,” “泽兰王,” and “奴侯王” renderings are not secure enough to create canonical identities without additional evidence.
- The sacrificial golden figure's exact ritual meaning and ownership, the claim that this victory surpassed Wei Qing's earlier achievement, and the “new war god” language are source interpretation or rhetoric.
- The reported Jincheng fort is not treated here as proof of the later city's origin or exact site; its builder, location, chronology, and institutional role remain episode-attributed.
