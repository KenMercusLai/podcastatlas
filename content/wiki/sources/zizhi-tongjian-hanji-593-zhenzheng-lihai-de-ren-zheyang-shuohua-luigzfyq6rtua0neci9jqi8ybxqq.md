# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》593丨真正厉害的人这样说话

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills an earlier part of [[WangJiaWesternHan|王嘉]]'s remonstrance against [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]'s extraordinary favor toward [[DongXian|董贤]]. It catalogs Dong Xian's mansion, garden, household provisioning, market escort, servant rewards, and large land grants, then shows Wang Jia moving from praise of Aidi into a public-institution warning about private favor, disaster pressure, rumor, and historical precedent.

The durable synthesis is that Wang Jia's "厉害" speech is not soft flattery but hierarchy-aware sequencing. He praises Aidi first, states the chancellor's rescue duty through Confucian language, lists concrete abuses, and finally reframes restraint as protection for Dong Xian himself: excessive affection can make the favored person politically unsafe.

## Key Claims

- Han Aidi's favor for Dong Xian has expanded from personal intimacy into public construction, state-funded household support, market intimidation, and land distribution.
- Dong Xian's residence, garden water, utensils, family illness support, guest and wedding expenses, and servant rewards show private favor drawing on official labor and resources.
- The emperor's emissaries accompanying Dong-family market purchases make ordinary merchants fearful and turn palace favor into visible social disturbance.
- Aidi's simultaneous reduction of imperial parks and grant of more than two thousand qing of land to Dong Xian creates a contradiction between public austerity and private reward.
- Wang Jia connects Dong Xian's overfavor to disaster, epidemic, rumor, and public resentment, making it a governance and legitimacy problem rather than only a moral complaint.
- Wang Jia's rhetorical structure gives Aidi an acceptance path: he begins with praise, invokes chancellor duty, cites history, and argues that wrong love harms the person being favored.
- Aidi is displeased, and the episode previews [[DuYeWesternHan|杜邺]] as another critic of Dong-family rise.

## Key Quotes

> "爱之，适足以害之" - Wang Jia's warning that excessive and wrongly directed love can endanger Dong Xian.

> "天下危而不持，颠而不扶，则将焉用彼相矣" - the duty frame Wang Jia invokes for why a chancellor must remonstrate.
