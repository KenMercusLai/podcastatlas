# 565. The Great Northern War: Revenge of the Cossacks (Part 2)

## Summary

This [[TheRestIsHistory]] episode follows [[CharlesXII|Charles XII]]'s 1707-1709 invasion of Russia from apparent Swedish momentum to the brink of the [[BattleOfPoltava|Battle of Poltava]]. [[PeterTheGreat|Peter the Great]] avoids the decisive battle Charles wants, applies [[ScorchedEarthSupplyDenial|scorched-earth supply denial]], destroys the relief column at the [[BattleOfLesnaya|Battle of Lesnaya]], and rapidly eliminates the Ukrainian base promised by [[IvanMazepa|Ivan Mazepa]] at [[Baturin]]. Distance, weather, logistics, alliance failure, and Charles's refusal to retreat leave the [[SwedishEmpire|Swedish army]] starving, wounded, and strategically trapped before the battle begins.

## Key Claims

- Charles's march on Moscow depends on defeating Peter decisively and sustaining a long overland army, but Russian withdrawal, destroyed roads and settlements, poisoned wells, and removed food make geography and supply into weapons.
- Swedish tactical victories at Grodno and Holowczyn do not solve the manpower problem: Russia can replace losses more readily, while every Swedish casualty and delay narrows Charles's options.
- Count Lewenhaupt's late, mud-bound relief column is separated from Charles and defeated at Lesnaya, costing Sweden thousands of men and the wagons carrying clothing, powder, weapons, medicine, cannon, and food.
- Charles's turn south converts a failed Moscow route into a Ukrainian gamble centered on Mazepa's promised supplies and Cossack support.
- Mazepa's defection is presented as a risky attempt to protect Cossack autonomy rather than simple personal treachery, but only a small retinue immediately joins him.
- Peter's destruction of Baturin removes the supplies, shelter, and political rallying point Charles expected while using massacre, denunciation, ritual degradation, and excommunication to deter further defections.
- The severe winter, failed local attacks, wet powder, rotting equipment, Charles's foot wound, and absent Tatar and Polish allies leave the Swedish army materially unfit even as Charles insists on battle.

## Key Quotes

> "a trifle" - Charles's reported response to an ensign who had lost heels and toes to frostbite.

> "belt of total destruction" - the episode's description of Peter's planned supply-denial zone along the Swedish routes.
