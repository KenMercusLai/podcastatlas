## Summary
This [[HubermanLab]] Essentials episode features [[AndrewHuberman]] discussing depression, psychedelic-assisted therapy, and neuromodulation with [[NolanWilliams]]. Williams argues that severe psychiatric states are better understood through [[CircuitBasedPsychiatry]] than through a simple chemical-imbalance story. The episode connects [[TranscranialMagneticStimulationForDepression]], [[StanfordNeuromodulationTherapy]], [[MemoryReconsolidationPsychiatry]], and [[PsychedelicClinicalSupervisionBoundary]] while keeping SSRIs, ketamine, MDMA, psilocybin, ibogaine, and ayahuasca clinically bounded.

## Key Claims
- Depression is framed as a major disabling illness that worsens other medical and psychiatric conditions, including cardiovascular risk.
- Williams rejects a simple serotonin-deficit account of depression and describes a shift from chemical-imbalance psychiatry toward [[CircuitBasedPsychiatry]].
- [[TranscranialMagneticStimulationForDepression]] is presented as a way to externally engage prefrontal control systems and change mood-related brain-heart circuitry.
- [[StanfordNeuromodulationTherapy]] compresses high-dose, spaced TMS into a five-day protocol for high-acuity depression, with rapid remission reported in trial and open-label contexts.
- MDMA, psilocybin, ketamine, ibogaine, and ayahuasca are discussed as possible ways to alter plasticity, connectivity, or trauma-related memory processing, but their evidence strength and durability differ.
- [[MemoryReconsolidationPsychiatry]] is the episode's trauma-change frame: altered states may let maladaptive rules or memories be re-experienced and updated.
- [[PsychedelicClinicalSupervisionBoundary]] is explicit: Williams treats psychedelics as powerful clinical tools requiring rigorous evidence, screening, and medical supervision rather than recreational use.

## Key Quotes
> "too powerful to be recreational drugs" - Williams's supervision boundary for psychedelics.

> "turn on, stay on, remember to stay on" - his shorthand for the Stanford neuromodulation signal.

> "psychiatry 3.0" - the episode's label for the circuit-focused model.
