# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》510丨汉成帝的这道圣旨，为何大臣敢阳奉阴违？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Heping 1-2 / 28-27 BCE under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]], between the harem-fiscal pressure of [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-509-lishi-gaojie-nvren-weishenme-yao-jingji-duli-lu9ngwdmqsetzuvce0ccx4i7z3|Hanji 509]] and the Ye Lang frontier sequence in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-512-xihan-chuzheng-yelang-wo-budang-dage-haoduonian-lufpmcdwb20dhx2qxfjws7-qdpkb|Hanji 512]]. It first revises the one-note image of Chengdi by noting his learning, cultural and institutional activity, then centers his edict to reduce capital statutes and simplify law.

The durable synthesis is that good policy language does not execute itself. Chengdi's legal-simplification edict stalls because revision work requires organized expertise and sustained labor, while complex law also protects official discretion and private benefit. The second half turns to [[YiYanMoyan|一言莫衍]]'s attempted Xiongnu defection, where [[GuYongWesternHan|谷永]] and [[DuQinWesternHan|杜钦]] advise refusal because accepting one minister could damage the submissive chanyu relationship, create moral bad faith, and possibly fall into a test or counterintelligence trap.

## Key Claims

- The episode complicates Chengdi's reputation: indulgence, indecision, Zhao Feiyan/Zhao Hede, Zhang Fang, and Wang-family empowerment remain real, but he is also presented as learned and institutionally active.
- [[PingDangWesternHan|平当]] asks to restore sacrifices to Liu Bang's father, the Taishanghuang, and Chengdi accepts the argument.
- Han law grows from Liu Bang's "three articles" into Xiao He's Nine Chapters plus later court, military, and administrative additions, making legal text difficult even for specialist officials.
- Chengdi's edict criticizes excessive capital statutes, proliferating regulations, and the danger that ordinary people cannot know the law that may kill them.
- The responsible officials answer the edict only with minor adjustments, and the host explains the failure through implementation burden plus bureaucratic self-interest.
- Complex law gives officials interpretive and penalty discretion; simplifying it would reduce the power and possible benefits attached to case handling.
- When Xiongnu envoy [[YiYanMoyan|一言莫衍]] asks to defect to Han, Gu Yong and Du Qin argue that the context differs from earlier eras because the chanyu now submits to Han.
- Accepting the defection could trade one person for the trust of a whole polity, and might be a deliberate test or counterintelligence move by a newly enthroned chanyu.
- Chengdi follows the refusal advice, questions Yi Yan Moyan through a palace envoy, and sends him back after he claims earlier madness.

## Key Quotes

> "法律越复杂，司法解释越复杂，而司法解释意味着权力" - the host's explanation of why officials benefit from legal complexity.

> "为了得到一个人而失去一国之心" - Gu Yong and Du Qin's diplomatic-risk argument as summarized by the episode.
