# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》694丨《王命论》揭露东汉什么隐秘？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the Jianwu 5 / 29 CE layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-695-ta-xinku-da-xia-qianli-jiangshan-weihe-gongshou-songren-lttlnoazrrg5dtdvfqmngcbfeody|Hanji 695]]. It centers [[BanBiaoEasternHan|班彪]]'s `王命论` as a warning to [[WeiXiaoEasternHan|隗嚣]]: local force, cleverness, and opportunity do not by themselves justify claiming rulership over the realm.

The durable synthesis is that Ban Biao uses [[MandateOfHeavenLegitimacy|天命合法性]], Liu-family restoration language, [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s talent-absorbing founding capacity, and failed claimant precedents to argue for title restraint. When Wei Xiao does not accept the warning, Ban Biao leaves for Hexi, attaches to [[DouRongEasternHan|窦融]], and helps turn Dou Rong toward service under [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]].

## Key Claims

- Ban Biao writes `王命论` because Wei Xiao's ambition grows in Jianwu 5 and he wants to move beyond preserving himself toward becoming king or contesting the realm.
- The episode frames Wei Xiao's choice as `求不败` versus `求全胜`: preserving survival and descendants is contrasted with a dangerous bid for total victory.
- `王命论` argues that imperial authority is not a deer anyone can seize through speed or force; it requires mandate, historical basis, virtue, and human support.
- Ban Biao builds the Han-restoration case through Yao-Shun-Yu succession, fire-virtue and Liu-family legitimacy, and Liu Bang's Red Emperor son narrative.
- Liu Bang is presented as more than a lucky winner: his capacity to receive advice, use [[ZhangLiang|张良]], [[LiShiqi|郦食其]], [[HanXin|韩信]], and [[ChenPing|陈平]], and ignore low origins helps explain why the mandate story can attach to him.
- Failed figures such as Han Xin, [[YingBu|英布 / 黥布]], [[XiangLiang|项梁]], [[XiangYu|项羽]], and [[WangMang|王莽]] are used as warnings that forceful or opportunistic claims can end in destruction.
- [[ChenYing|陈英 / 陈婴]]'s mother and [[WangLingMother|王陵母亲]] are cited as examples of political judgment: restraint, correct attachment, and hostage-risk refusal can protect a family better than title ambition.
- Ban Biao's later move to Dou Rong shows adviser self-protection and patron selection after a ruler refuses historically grounded counsel.

## Key Quotes

> "求不败还是求全胜" - the episode's frame for Wei Xiao's central choice.

> "逐鹿天下" - the political temptation Ban Biao rejects in `王命论`.

> "如果不贪念帝位，就能把福分留给子孙" - the source's summary of Ban Biao's warning.
