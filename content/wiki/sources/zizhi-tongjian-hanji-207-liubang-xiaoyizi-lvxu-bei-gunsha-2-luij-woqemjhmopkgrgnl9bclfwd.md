# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》207｜刘邦小姨子，吕媭被棍杀（2）

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[LiuHeng|代王刘恒]] from the ministers' invitation through repeated risk checks, ritual refusal, palace transfer, military appointments, and accession as Emperor Wen. [[ZhangWuWesternHan|张武]] initially treats the invitation as a possible trap, while [[SongChangWesternHan|宋昌]] argues from Liu-family strength, popular stability, and the anti-Lü military outcome that the offer is credible.

The episode presents the accession as a staged transfer rather than a single ceremony. Liu Heng sends [[BoZhaoWesternHan|薄昭]] and Song Chang to verify intent, refuses a private conversation and immediate public receipt of the seals, accepts the throne only after a formal ministerial petition at the Dai residence, enters a cleared palace, and appoints Song Chang and Zhang Wu to control the armies and palace security. The subsequent killing of [[LiuHongYoungEmperorWesternHan|少帝刘弘]] and other young princes connects the installation to the prior [[SuccessionLegitimacyPurge|继位合法性清洗]], while the source's claim that this moment opens the [[WenJingProsperityTradeoff|文景之治]] remains a broad retrospective frame.

## Key Claims

- Zhang Wu and other Dai officials advise Liu Heng to plead illness and wait because the veteran ministers who destroyed the Lü group already control Chang'an and may be setting a trap.
- Song Chang answers that Liu rule is structurally secure through lineage, interlocking royal kingdoms, public settlement, and the Northern Army's demonstrated preference for the Liu house.
- Liu Heng seeks multiple forms of confirmation: consultation with [[BaoJi|薄太后]], divination, Bo Zhao's meeting with [[ZhouBo|周勃]], and Song Chang's inspection of the reception at the Wei River bridge.
- Song Chang blocks a private meeting with Zhou Bo, and Liu Heng declines to accept the imperial seals in public at the bridge, which the host interprets as protection against the appearance of private conspiracy or immediate usurpation.
- [[ChenPing|陈平]] and the ministers formally petition Liu Heng at the Dai residence, where repeated ritual refusals and a change in orientation mark the transition from guest-host relations to ruler-minister relations.
- [[LiuXingjuWesternHan|刘兴居]], [[XiahouYing|夏侯婴]], and others remove Liu Hong from the palace before Liu Heng enters; armed guards initially still identify the incumbent inside as emperor.
- Liu Heng's first appointments put Song Chang over the northern and southern armies and Zhang Wu over inner-palace security, making trusted Dai followers the operational basis of the new reign.
- The episode says Liu Heng orders the deaths of Liu Hong and three other young princes, issues a public anti-Lü justification, grants amnesty and rewards, and thereby begins the era later called Wen-Jing rule.
