# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》852丨当美女如云，是福还是 “祸”？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 159 CE post-Liang-clan layer before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-857-xuehui-gongying-cai-shi-zhenzheng-de-yingjia-lh3zx6pw-4ib7owiz8c68bv-9htn|Hanji 857]] and the later 163-167 Huan-era court sequence. It opens with a source-scoped lesson on desire as a force that needs limits, then turns to [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]]'s favor-based rewards, excessive palace women, and intimacy with [[DengWanshiLateHan|邓万世]] after the Liang threat has been removed.

The durable contribution is a compact early case for [[ImperialPrivateDesireGovernanceRisk|帝王私欲治理风险]] and partial remonstrance conversion. [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] criticizes non-merit enfeoffment and harem expense; Huan releases more than five hundred palace women but still grants key rewards. [[YuanHongChenliuLateHan|袁闳]] then describes Huan as a middle-grade ruler who can be led toward good by worthy ministers or toward disorder by close attendants, but his later sealed omen memorial receives no answer and he withdraws from office.

## Key Claims

- The opening "欲望" frame treats desire as productive when disciplined and politically destructive when it becomes unbounded appetite.
- After clearing the Liang family in 159 CE, Emperor Huan is portrayed as losing restraint: he grants irregular rewards, indulges palace closeness, and strengthens a new eunuch-adjacent power field.
- Chen Fan invokes the Han rule that marquisates should follow great merit, criticizing Huan's honors for Deng Zun, Deng Wanshi, and the restored Huang-family title.
- Chen Fan links palace women to state finance: thousands of women require food, clothing, cosmetics, and service labor, making imperial desire a public-resource burden.
- Huan partially accepts the criticism by releasing more than five hundred palace women, but he still carries out the Deng Wanshi and Huang-family rewards, so correction remains partial.
- Yuan Hong tells Huan that he is a middle-grade ruler because Chen Fan's governance improves the realm while eunuch and yellow-gate involvement disorders it.
- Huan initially tolerates the blunt evaluation and promotes Yuan Hong, showing that direct criticism can be accepted under some conditions.
- Yuan Hong's later sealed memorial uses an anomalous star to criticize Huan's overlarge favor toward Deng Wanshi, palace gambling intimacy, and companion choice; after no response, Yuan Hong claims illness and returns home.
- The closing practical lesson treats withdrawal after ignored counsel as a form of political self-protection rather than as mere abandonment.

## Key Quotes

> "其嗜欲深者，其天机浅" - the source's opening Zhuangzi frame for desire weakening judgment.

> "盗不过五女门" - Chen Fan's household-cost proverb applied to Huan's large harem.

> "既可以与贤人一起做善事，也可以和奸人一起做恶事" - Yuan Hong's middle-ruler diagnosis.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with existing wiki pages was found. The source backfills an earlier 159 CE layer before the currently ingested 163-167 Huan-era court sequence.
- Identity disambiguation: this episode's [[YuanHongChenliuLateHan|袁闳]] is treated as a Chenliu attendant/remonstrant, distinct from [[YuanHongLateHan|袁闳 / Yuan Hong (late Han)]], the Yuan-family recluse in Hanji 896.
