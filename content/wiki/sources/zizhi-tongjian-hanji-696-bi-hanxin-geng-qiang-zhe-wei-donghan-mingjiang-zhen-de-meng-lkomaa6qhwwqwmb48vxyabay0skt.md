# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》696丨比韩信更强？这位东汉名将真的猛！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianwu 5 / 29 CE immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-697-yi-kou-qi-kan-dong-donghan-mingjiang-gengyan-de-chuanqi-rensheng-lpbhbmedezq7pjuz5l3q-s1unh7c|Hanji 697]] by narrating [[GengYanEasternHan|耿弇]]'s defeat of [[ZhangBuEasternHan|张步]] around [[Linzi|临淄]]. It follows Geng Yan's false announcement against Xi'an, surprise attack on Linzi, provocation of Zhang Bu, prepared camp defense, visible weakness, battlefield composure after a thigh wound, and pursuit ambush after Zhang Bu's night retreat.

The episode's main synthesis is that Geng Yan's battlefield strength is an integrated chain of judgment rather than only courage: he reads city strength, enemy psychology, march exhaustion, supply risk, troop morale, and retreat routes before each move. The conclusion highlights [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]]'s praise comparing Geng Yan to [[HanXin|韩信]] and grounding the idiom "有志者事竟成" in this campaign.

## Key Claims

- In 29 CE, Zhang Bu defends the Qi region by having his brother Zhang Lan hold Xi'an with more than 20,000 elite troops while another force holds Linzi about forty li away.
- Geng Yan judges Linzi easier to take than Xi'an, publicly orders a five-day preparation to attack Xi'an, then marches at dawn to take Linzi instead.
- After capturing Linzi in half a day, Geng Yan forbids plunder but deliberately says the army will wait for Zhang Bu before taking property, provoking Zhang Bu into a confidence-driven attack.
- Geng Yan predicts that Zhang Bu's march from Ju will leave him tired and hungry, so he plans to hold fortified ground, lure attack, and win within ten days.
- Before the main fight, Geng Yan avoids crushing Chong Yi too early and instead shows weakness so Zhang Bu will keep advancing.
- When a stray arrow wounds his thigh, Geng Yan hides the injury, cuts off the arrow shaft, and continues commanding until dark to preserve morale.
- Geng Yan refuses to wait passively for Liu Xiu's rescue, attacks again before the emperor arrives, and then uses pre-positioned ambushes to punish Zhang Bu's night retreat.
- Liu Xiu later praises the achievement by saying Han Xin had beaten an already-submitted army, while Geng Yan had independently defeated a stronger enemy.

## Key Quotes

> "有志者事竟成" - Liu Xiu's closing praise as framed by the episode.

> "不是把盗贼留给皇上收拾" - Geng Yan's refusal to wait for Liu Xiu before fighting again.
