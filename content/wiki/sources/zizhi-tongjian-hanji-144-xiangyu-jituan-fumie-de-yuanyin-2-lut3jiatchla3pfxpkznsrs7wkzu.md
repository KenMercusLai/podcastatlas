# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》144｜项羽集团覆灭的原因（2）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-144-xiangyu-jituan-fumie-de-yuanyin-1-lr0ft8-jupxgvutn6c89mfs8r4wb|Hanji 144 part 1]] by following [[LiuBang|刘邦]] into [[Hanzhong|汉中]] and then tracking the immediate eastern backlash to [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s settlement. [[ZhangLiang|张良]] uses [[XiangBo|项伯]] as a gift-mediated channel for Liu Bang, then recommends [[BurningGalleryRoads|burning the gallery roads]] to lower Xiang Yu's suspicion while protecting against pursuit. The episode argues that [[TianRong|田荣]], [[PengYue|彭越]], and [[ChenYu|陈馀]] turn [[RewardAllocationBacklash|reward allocation backlash]] into armed revolt, pulling Xiang Yu east and opening a western window for Liu Bang's later return toward [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]].

## Key Claims
- Zhang Liang's gift transfer to Xiang Bo extends the Hongmen relationship channel into post-settlement territorial bargaining.
- Burning the gallery roads works as both defensive infrastructure denial and political signaling: it tells Xiang Yu that Liu Bang is accepting confinement in Hanzhong.
- The people who follow Liu Bang into Hanzhong include disappointed fighters who gained little from the settlement; [[HanXin|韩信]] appears here as the representative future talent.
- Tian Rong's rebellion begins as a Qi settlement dispute but quickly becomes a broader anti-Xiang military front when he attacks [[TianDu|田都]], kills [[TianShi|田巿]], claims Qi kingship, and mobilizes Peng Yue.
- Peng Yue's value in this episode is not only local discipline but independent armed pressure: Tian Rong gives him a general's seal, and Peng Yue defeats Tian An and later Xiang Yu's dispatched force.
- Chen Yu uses resentment over Zhang Er's kingship and his own small reward to ask Tian Rong for troops, framing a Zhao restoration around [[ZhaoXie|赵歇]] as useful to Qi.
- Xiang Yu's distribution standard is criticized as overly crude: he mainly rewards those who followed him into the pass while ignoring older local power, perceived merit, and actual military capacity.
- By July 206 BCE, the combined Qi-Zhao pressure forces Xiang Yu to defend his new order in the east, which creates the strategic opening Liu Bang needs in the west.
- [[HanCheng|韩成]]'s detention, demotion, and killing show Xiang Yu revising his own settlement through coercion rather than repairing legitimacy.
