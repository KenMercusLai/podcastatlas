## Summary
This [[TomorrowToday]] episode has [[ShekharNatarajan]] question [[DrewHarris]] and [[AlanKadish]] about [[ApexReplicate]], a platform intended to represent a named professional's judgment through a [[DigitalProtege]]. The guests distinguish this from a generic model or task-executing agent: the protégé asks for context, answers within the represented expert's perspective, acknowledges its boundary, and escalates unfamiliar cases to the human. The durable synthesis joins [[InstitutionalKnowledgeTransfer]], [[ExpertiseAmplifiedAIUse]], and [[SocraticExpertDialogue]] while preserving unresolved questions about worker incentives, verification, intellectual property, displacement, and cognitive offloading.

## Key Claims
- [[ApexReplicate]] is presented as capturing how a particular expert interprets circumstances and makes decisions, not merely storing general factual material.
- The reported onboarding process combines an approximately one-hour interview with uploaded materials and prior conversations; the guests describe the initial result as roughly 50% faithful, but provide no independent evaluation method.
- A [[DigitalProtege]] is supposed to ask contextual questions, admit when it does not know, escalate the unfamiliar case to its expert, and learn from the expert's answer.
- The guests describe two business models: a marketplace where professionals sell lower-cost access to a protégé and an enterprise product for retaining internal expertise as experienced employees retire.
- Named provenance is part of the trust proposition: users choose a represented professional and still have to evaluate specialization, reputation, and fit when experts disagree.
- The guests distinguish protégés from agents by assigning judgment and advice to the former and task execution to the latter, although a real deployment could combine both roles.
- The proposed “Group Think” roadmap would route questions among complementary expert protégés and mediate their answers rather than merge them into one anonymous composite persona.
- The episode treats AI as leverage for existing expertise: experienced users can direct and correct AI work more efficiently, while novices may lack the context needed to recognize waste or error.
- The host presses an unresolved labor problem: aggregate claims that technology creates jobs do not show that an individually displaced worker can retrain into the new work.
- [[SocraticExpertDialogue]] is presented as the core differentiator because contextual questioning may help users reason through a problem instead of receiving only a finished output.
