## Summary
This [[LongNow]] conversation has [[BaratundeThurston]] and [[BlaiseAgueraYArcas]] rethink AI through biology, Indigenous democracy, ecology, colonial history, and human relationships. Agüera y Arcas uses [[Symbiogenesis]] to argue that intelligence and evolutionary complexity arise through mergers and networks as well as competition, while Thurston uses [[HaudenosauneeConfederacy]] governance and land stewardship to question ownership, extraction, and obedient-machine fantasies. Their shared conclusion is that AI should be judged as a relationship that changes human habits and power, with [[InterdependenceWithDistinctSelf]], [[NestedCooperationGovernance]], care, and [[CognitiveLibertyUnderAI]] providing stronger tests than capability or productivity alone.

## Key Claims
- [[RelationalAIEthics]] evaluates AI interaction by the habits, expectations, and power relations it trains in people, without requiring proof that a model is alive.
- [[Symbiogenesis]] shows that major evolutionary transitions can emerge when formerly separate organisms become parts of a new whole; competition is therefore not evolution's only organizing story.
- The [[CartesianWound]] separates human from animal, culture from nature, and technology from biology, making dependencies easier to misrecognize as externalities.
- [[DominationModelOfAI]] carries master-servant assumptions into promises of unlimited obedient labor and can reduce human empathy, reciprocity, friction tolerance, and agency.
- Interdependence requires differentiated participants: [[InterdependenceWithDistinctSelf]] treats selfhood, boundaries, and division of labor as conditions of cooperation rather than failures of union.
- Cooperation is not automatically good; [[NestedCooperationGovernance]] asks whether coordination helps or harms the larger network and requires collectively chosen standards of health and accountability.
- Technological augmentation can expand capability while weakening retained skill or power, making [[CognitiveLibertyUnderAI]] and control over knowledge systems governance questions.
- Acceleration increases the need for care, transition support, and collective deliberation rather than removing responsibility for those bearing the costs of change.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is adopted. The episode qualifies optimizer-centered accounts of people, firms, evolution, and AI by arguing that durable cooperation can create larger systems with plural and changing purposes.
- Interdependence is not treated as inherently beneficial: collusion, cartels, cancer, coercive merger, and races to the bottom show that cooperation and competition must be evaluated across levels.
