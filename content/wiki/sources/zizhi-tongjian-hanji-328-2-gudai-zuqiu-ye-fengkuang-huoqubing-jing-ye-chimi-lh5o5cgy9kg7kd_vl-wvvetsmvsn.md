# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》328-2｜古代足球也疯狂，霍去病竟也痴迷？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode places [[HuoQubing|霍去病]]'s battlefield ability beside a harsher command-style judgment: it says provisions remained on the imperial kitchen carts while nearby soldiers went hungry, and that Huo discarded surplus food rather than sharing it. The episode then uses the report that he organized cuju during a food-short frontier campaign to introduce [[HanDynastyCuju|汉代蹴鞠]] as recreation, military conditioning, court spectacle, and a practice said to have spread beyond armies and elites.

The durable synthesis is a tension rather than a heroic sports anecdote. Cuju could support fitness and morale, but organized play did not by itself demonstrate care for soldiers when rations, exhaustion, and command insulation remained unresolved. The closing comparison with [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]] reinforces that distinction by presenting Wei's poorer upbringing, restraint, and concern for subordinates as a contrast to Huo's limited experience of hardship.

## Key Claims

- The episode says Huo Qubing grew up in privilege and could fail to understand the hunger and exhaustion experienced by ordinary soldiers.
- It reports that surplus food remained in the campaign kitchen carts, yet Huo ordered it discarded instead of distributing it to hungry troops.
- It attributes to the *Hanshu* biography a report that Huo still had a temporary cuju ground prepared when supplies were failing during a frontier expedition.
- Han cuju is presented as a structured game played with a hair-filled leather ball on a square field with twelve crescent-shaped goal pits, two teams, and an official.
- The episode places cuju in military skills literature and says peacetime or leisure play helped maintain soldiers' fitness and fighting spirit.
- It also presents Han cuju as court entertainment and a wider social practice involving commoners, a skilled non-Han captive, and women represented in stone reliefs.
- Wei Qing's poverty-shaped empathy, humility, and kindness are used as a source-scoped character contrast with Huo Qubing.

## Key Quotes

> "仗照样打，球还得踢。" - the host's compressed description of warfare and cuju continuing together.

> "绿色鸦片" - a joking modern comparison for intense enthusiasm toward playing on open grass, not evidence for Huo Qubing's campaign motive.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with existing wiki content was found. The episode adds a command-welfare and cuju layer to Huo Qubing rather than revising the existing campaign, protected-killing, family, or cause-of-death branches.
- The source does not reproduce the cited *Hanshu* passages or the text of *Cuju ershiwu pian*. The food-disposal account, temporary-field wording, military-training requirement, ball construction, twelve-goal and twenty-four-player rules, popular reach, captive-player story, and interpretation of women's stone reliefs remain episode-attributed pending primary-text and archaeological comparison.
- "中国足球最好的代言人," the golf comparison, and the suggestion that open grass helped motivate the Xiongnu campaign are explicit jokes or promotional rhetoric, not historical findings.
- Cuju's presence in an army does not resolve whether it improved morale under food shortage; the same source instead preserves a conflict between conditioning or recreation and material care.
