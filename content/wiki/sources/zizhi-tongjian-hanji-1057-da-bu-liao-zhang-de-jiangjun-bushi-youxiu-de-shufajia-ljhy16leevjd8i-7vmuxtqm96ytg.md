## Summary
This episode pairs an opening source-scoped psychology segment on father-child relationship patterns with Jian'an 7 military and diplomatic episodes. Its main historical arc shows [[ZhongYao|钟繇]] refusing to abandon [[PingyangDefenseLateHan|平阳]], defeating [[GuoYuanLateHan|郭元]] by striking during a Fen River crossing, and absorbing [[PangDe|庞德]]'s beheading of Guo Yuan into a court-duty frame despite kinship. It then follows [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s burned-camp withdrawal and [[BowangSlopeBattleLateHan|博望坡]] ambush of [[XiahouDun|夏侯惇]], plus the setup for [[SunQuan|孙权]]'s hostage-demand dilemma continued in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1058-shaonian-sunquan-chuzhang-jiangdong-ruhe-yingdui-caocao-fanan-lgqs4-a9qgf21woeh-ppinw4z9jg|Hanji 1058]].

## Key Claims
- The opening psychology segment argues, source-scoped, that father-child relationship patterns affect courage before authority, social conflict, and "the world"; the episode does not turn this into a durable historical claim.
- [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s son [[YuanShang|袁尚]] tries to open a western pressure line through Guo Yuan, the [[SouthernXiongnu|Southern Xiongnu]], and a hoped-for link to [[MaTeng|马腾]].
- Zhong Yao calms Ma Teng first, then refuses to abandon Pingyang because Guo Yuan is proud and careless, making a river-crossing timing window exploitable.
- The source presents Zhong Yao's northwestern success as a sequence of attacking plans, disrupting alliances, and only then fighting: 上兵伐谋，其次伐交，其次伐兵.
- Pang De, serving in Ma Teng's line under Ma Chao in the source's account, kills Guo Yuan without knowing Guo Yuan is Zhong Yao's nephew; Zhong Yao replies that Guo Yuan rebelled against the court, so Pang De need not apologize.
- Liu Bei's burned-camp withdrawal at Bowang is treated as a deliberate lure; [[LiDian|李典]] reads the warning signs, Xiahou Dun ignores them, and Li Dian's rescue prevents a worse Cao-side defeat.
- The episode contrasts historical Bowang with [[RomanceOfTheThreeKingdoms|《三国演义》]]: in the source's reading of official history and the chronicle, Liu Bei rather than Zhuge Liang is the actor behind the burned-camp ambush.
- Cao Cao's request that Sun Quan send a brother or son to court service is introduced as a hostage-backed pressure test, with Zhang Zhao and Qin Song hesitant before Sun Quan consults Zhou Yu and Lady Wu.

## Key Quotes
> "敌未毕济，宜急击之." - Zhang Ji's advice to strike Guo Yuan before all of his troops cross the Fen River.

> "郭元虽吾甥，国贼也." - Zhong Yao's source-framed response after Pang De presents Guo Yuan's head.
