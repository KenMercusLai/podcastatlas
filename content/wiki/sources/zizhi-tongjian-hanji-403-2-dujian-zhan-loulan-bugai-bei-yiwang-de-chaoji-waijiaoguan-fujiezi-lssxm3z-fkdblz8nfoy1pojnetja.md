# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》403-2｜独剑斩楼兰，不该被遗忘的超级外交官：傅介子

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-403-1-xisha-xiongnu-shizhe-xihan-zui-tiexue-waijiaoguan-jingshi-ta-lum-xolrqlwjgmv5xscssenogyx|Hanji 403-1]] by narrating [[FuJieziWesternHan|傅介子]]'s second Western Regions mission under [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|汉昭帝刘弗陵]] and [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]]. Fu carries valuables to [[LoulanKingdomWesternHan|楼兰]], uses the promise of Han gifts to isolate [[LoulanKingAnguiWesternHan|安归]], kills him inside the palace compound, and then intimidates the nobles into accepting [[WeiTuqiWesternHan|魏屠耆]] as Han-backed king.

The durable synthesis is double-edged. Operationally, the source completes Fu's small-party coercive diplomacy arc: gifts create access, targeted killing produces immediate shock, hostage kingship converts succession into a Han instrument, and a tiny Yixun屯田 detachment helps bind the new order. Normatively, [[SimaGuang|司马光]] criticizes the诱杀 method as thief-like and trust-damaging, so the episode records both frontier deterrence success and the diplomatic-credit cost of achieving it through deception.

## Key Claims

- In 77 BCE, Fu Jiezi returns west with a small "diplomatic" group, valuables, and Huo Guang-backed authorization to target Loulan.
- Fu manipulates Loulan king Angui's interest in Han gifts, first pretending to leave and then displaying gold, silver, jewels, and silk to draw him back into a private meeting.
- Two Han warriors stab Angui from behind after Fu isolates him; Fu then displays the severed head and threatens Loulan elites with imminent Han military destruction if they resist.
- Fu announces Angui's offenses against Han, declares Wei Tuqi, a Loulan hostage in Chang'an, the new king, and renames Loulan as Shanshan.
- Han Zhao rewards Fu as Yiyang Hou with seven hundred households, while the two warriors become palace attendants; Western Regions states reportedly react with fear and submission missions.
- Wei Tuqi requests a Han military-agricultural presence at Yixun, and Han sends a small sima-led detachment of forty men to help stabilize the new Shanshan order.
- Sima Guang criticizes the assassination because punishing a ruler who had already confessed through inducement and trickery could make future rebels less willing to return to submission.
