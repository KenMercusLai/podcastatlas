# 109 趣话《鬼吹灯》之昆仑神宫P3：牺牲者谁，祭坛两难

## Summary

This [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] episode follows 胡八一、王胖子、Shirley 杨、明叔、阿香 through the blindfolded tunnel and into the sacrificial chamber of [[KunlunShrineGhostBlowsOutTheLight|《昆仑神宫》]]. Its main contribution is an ethical reading of the apparent rule that one living companion must die: the hosts distinguish voluntary self-sacrifice from choosing another person's death, reject the idea that age or injury makes 明叔 disposable, and treat a five-person lottery as procedurally equal only if no alternative exists and everyone genuinely accepts the rule. 明叔's feigned injury, theft of the 雮尘珠, and proposal to sacrifice 阿香 turn that abstract dilemma into a test of kinship, autonomy, bargaining power, and survival.

## Key Claims

- The blindfolded crystal tunnel intensifies fear by removing vision, amplifying sound and touch, and making uncertain rules harder to test; 胡八一 eventually uses 明叔 to probe whether opening one's eyes is actually fatal.
- The sacrificial mural and crystal timer make the group believe that a living person must be killed before time expires, but the hosts argue that the characters accept this interpretation too quickly after already encountering deceptive tests.
- [[SacrificingOthersEthics|Sacrificing others]] differs morally from self-sacrifice because a person may volunteer their own life but cannot convert another person's life into a collective resource without remainder.
- 王胖子's proposal to use the older, injured 明叔 exposes the danger of treating age, health, or expected lifespan as a license to decide whose life counts less.
- Shirley 杨's willingness to volunteer follows from guilt over the expedition and fits her established autonomy and concern for 阿香, while 胡八一 interrupts the offer rather than accepting it as an easy solution.
- 明叔's false deathbed offer and later demand that 阿香 repay his care through death show how affection can coexist with self-preserving calculation and become coercive when one party claims ownership over another's life.
- A lottery can distribute risk evenly, but it does not by itself justify killing: its fairness depends on necessity, informed consent, and the absence of an overlooked alternative; here it also functions as 胡八一's temporary bargaining tactic.

## Key Quotes

> “牺牲一个人拯救多数人” - the dilemma the episode refuses to treat as automatically righteous.

> “人有权决定自己的生死，却无权替别人作出死亡决定” - the hosts' distinction between self-sacrifice and sacrificing another person.
