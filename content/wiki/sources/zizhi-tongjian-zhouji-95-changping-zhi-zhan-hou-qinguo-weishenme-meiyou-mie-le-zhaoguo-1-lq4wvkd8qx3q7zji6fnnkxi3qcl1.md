# 《资治通鉴·周纪》95丨长平之战后秦国为什么没有灭了赵国（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] from battlefield victory into the failed Qin follow-through against [[ZhaoState|赵国]]. It argues that [[QinState|秦国]] did not immediately destroy Zhao because post-victory exhaustion, [[Handan|邯郸]]'s defensive depth, [[BaiQi|白起]]'s terror-signaling tempo, [[SuDai|苏代]]'s diplomatic intervention, and [[FanJu|范雎]]'s rivalry anxiety all converged.

The episode first reads Bai Qi's killing of surrendered troops, the Bai Qi platform / jingguan memory, and the release of 240 young Zhao captives as [[PostBattleTerrorSignaling|战后恐怖震慑]]. It then shows Qin's next attacks under [[WangHe|王龁]] and [[SimaGeng|司马梗]] creating a plausible road toward Handan, before Su Dai persuades Fan Ju that letting Bai Qi destroy Zhao would make Bai Qi too powerful. Qin accepts land cessions from Han and Zhao, stops the offensive, and opens the Bai Qi-Fan Ju grievance that will shape the next branch.

## Key Claims
- After Changping, Zhao is terrified by the killing of surrendered soldiers and the return of 240 young survivors; the episode treats this as psychological warfare, not only atrocity display.
- Qin has also paid a heavy cost: the source repeats that Qin is in a "国虚民饥" condition, so a direct assault on Handan would still be difficult.
- Bai Qi's logic is not simple overconfidence; because both Qin and Zhao need recovery time, he wants to press while Zhao's shock and disorder are still fresh.
- In Zhou Nan Wang's fifty-sixth year, 259 BCE, [[WangHe|王龁]] attacks Zhao's [[WuanWarringStates|武安]] and [[Pilao|皮牢]], while [[SimaGeng|司马梗]] attacks [[TaiyuanWarringStates|太原]] and [[Shangdang|上党郡]].
- The episode notes source variation: [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] describes two Qin attack routes, while [[Shiji|《史记》]] traditions record a three-route version.
- [[HanState|韩国]] and [[WeiState|魏国]] fear that Qin's next victory over Zhao would leave them exposed, so they send Su Dai with gifts to lobby Fan Ju.
- Su Dai reframes Zhao's destruction as Fan Ju's personal problem: if Bai Qi captures Handan and destroys Zhao, Bai Qi's merit could outrank Fan Ju and reach the highest ministerial tier.
- Su Dai also argues that conquering Zhao land may not yield Zhao people, because people who do not want to become Qin subjects can flee into neighboring states.
- Fan Ju advises [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] to stop and rest the army; Qin takes [[Yuanyong|垣雍]] from Han and six Zhao cities, pausing the campaign.
- According to the episode's [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] layer, [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] also personally goes to Qin for peace, is detained, and is later released while [[ZhaoHao|赵郝]] remains to negotiate territorial transfer details.

## Key Quotes
> "士兵死亡过半" - the episode's reminder that Qin's victory was also costly.

> "攻心为上" - the host's frame for Bai Qi's terror-signaling after Changping.

> "平地起波澜" - the host's phrase for Su Dai manufacturing Fan Ju's personal pain point.
