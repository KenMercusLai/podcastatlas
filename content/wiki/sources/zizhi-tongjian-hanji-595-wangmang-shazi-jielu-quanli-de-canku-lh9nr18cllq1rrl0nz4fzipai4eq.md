# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》595丨王莽杀子，揭露权力的残酷

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the late [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] court before the Dong Xian succession crisis. It centers on [[WangMang|王莽]]'s return path after retreating to his fief: his son's killing of a household slave becomes a public morality event when Wang Mang forces the son to suicide and wins reputation for impartial severity.

The episode's durable synthesis is that Wang Mang's public virtue is politically productive rather than merely personal. In the source's reading, a deed that now appears brutal and hypocritical could be praised in its social context as "大义灭亲," creating broad official and popular support for his return; the later sections then show [[DongXian|董贤]] using a solar-eclipse explanation to remove Fu-family figures and [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]]'s death opening a new cleanup field.

## Key Claims

- After offending Fu Taihou, Wang Mang retreats to his fief and performs a low-profile posture of closed-door self-protection.
- Wang Mang's political capital depends heavily on staged restraint, moral image, and the conversion of public praise into court leverage.
- In Yuanshou 1 / 2 BCE, Wang Mang forces his son to die after the son kills a household slave; the episode reads this as a brutal kin-sacrifice performance that wins praise because of the period's hierarchy around enslaved dependents.
- The host compares Yi Ya, Jin Midi, and Hai Rui to distinguish different forms of child sacrifice or child-killing: flattery, preemptive removal of danger, and moral-reputation obsession.
- Hundreds of officials and commoners, along with Zhou Hu and Song Chong, petition or argue for Wang Mang, leading Aidi to recall Wang Mang and Wang Ren to Chang'an to serve Wang Zhengjun.
- Dong Xian uses a solar eclipse as an accusation channel against Fu Yan and Xifu Gong, showing his influence over Aidi even against important Fu-family ties.
- Fu Taihou's death and burial with Han Yuandi create a ritual-political issue that later gives Wang Mang a handle for attacking Fu-family honors.
- After Fu Taihou dies, Xifu Gong, Sun Pang, and dozens of attendants or palace officials are removed, showing how patronage protection collapses when the patron dies.
