# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》159-2｜张良说了啥救下汉朝？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-159-1-liubang-xijiao-de-yuren-zhishu-lga3puexunjzeu3s8pvt8saaa8n3|Hanji 159-1]] cliffhanger around [[ZhangLiang|张良]]'s "borrowing chopsticks" refutation of [[LiShiqi|郦食其]]'s proposal that [[LiuBang|刘邦]] restore the six old kingdoms. Zhang Liang argues that the [[SixStateRestorationAntiQinStrategy|六国复国式反秦策略]] that once could multiply enemies against Qin would now split Liu Bang's own land, rewards, followers, and command authority while [[XiangYu|项羽]] remains strong. The episode then uses [[XunYue|荀悦]]'s "行、势、情" framework to generalize the lesson: strategy has to be judged by conduct, temporary situation, and people's real commitments rather than copied from historical precedent.

## Key Claims
- Zhang Liang's objection is not that enfeoffment is always wrong. His point is that [[ShangTang|商汤]] and [[ZhouWuwang|周武王]] could honor old lineages after they already controlled the outcome, while Liu Bang is still in the middle of the [[XingyangStandoff|荥阳相持]] and cannot yet disarm, demobilize, reward everyone, or command the restored kings.
- Restoring six kingdoms would redirect Liu Bang's expected reward pool. The very followers who left families, graves, and old places to serve Liu Bang for future rank and land would have reasons to return to local restored kings.
- The plan could strengthen Xiang Yu rather than weaken him. If new six-state rulers judge Xiang Yu to be the stronger pole, Liu Bang would have created outside centers that he cannot direct.
- Liu Bang accepts the correction immediately: he spits out his food, curses the proposer, and orders the prepared seals and ribbons destroyed.
- Xun Yue's framework separates three dimensions of strategy: "行" as broad gains and losses, "势" as situational timing and advance-retreat conditions, and "情" as whether people's will and commitments are firm.
- The same six-state restoration method differs between [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] and Liu Bang: Chen Sheng would be giving away enemy-held Qin territory to multiply anti-Qin fronts, while Liu Bang would be cutting away territory already tied to his own camp.
- [[SongYi|宋义]]'s wait-and-watch logic resembles a valid exhausted-enemy tactic in some interstate settings, but the episode argues that it fails in the urgent Qin-Zhao-Chu rescue situation before [[JuluBattle|巨鹿之战]].
- The [[BackwaterBattle|背水一战]] contrast between [[HanXin|韩信]] at Jingxing and Liu Bang at the [[PengchengBattle|彭城之战]] shows that similar terrain does not produce similar results. Troop morale, enemy shock, command discipline, and the whole strategic field decide whether "back to water" creates resolve or rout.

## Key Quotes

> "权不可预设，变不可先图" - the episode's summary of why strategy must move with changing circumstances.
