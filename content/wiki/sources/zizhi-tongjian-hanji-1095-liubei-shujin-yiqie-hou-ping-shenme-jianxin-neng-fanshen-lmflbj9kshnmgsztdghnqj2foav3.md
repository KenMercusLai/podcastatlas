# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1095丨刘备输尽一切后，凭什么坚信能翻身？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens with [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s severe defeat at [[ChangbanBattle|长坂坡之战]], which the host turns into a source-scoped lesson about retaining belief after the loss of family, baggage, troops, money, and status. The historical center then shifts to [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s 208 CE takeover of [[Jingzhou|荆州]]: [[LiuCongLateHan|刘琮]] receives a court title, [[HanSongLateHan|韩嵩]] is released, and former Liu Biao officials such as [[KuaiYue|蒯越]], [[LiuXianLateHan|刘先]], [[DengXiLateHan|邓羲]], and [[CaiMao|蔡瑁]] enter the new personnel order.

The episode's strongest case is [[WenPing|文聘]]. He delays appearing before Cao Cao because he is ashamed that he failed to preserve Liu Biao's territory, then weeps while explaining his obligations to the old regime. Cao Cao calls him a loyal official, rewards him, preserves his command, and appoints him administrator of Jiangxia. The source reads this as both [[FormerEnemyLoyaltyReframing|旧敌忠义重释]] and [[CapturedOpponentTalentIncorporation|降敌任用式立威]]: demonstrated loyalty to a former lord can become evidence of reliability under a victor who needs local capacity.

## Key Claims
- The Changban defeat strips Liu Bei of most visible resources, but the host argues that loss becomes final only when belief and willingness to continue disappear.
- After taking Jingzhou in 208 CE, Cao Cao gives Liu Cong the Qingzhou inspector title and a marquis rank, using court office to place the surrender inside a new hierarchy.
- Cao Cao releases Han Song and appoints him grand herald, completing the earlier imprisonment story in which Liu Biao punished Han Song for court-facing loyalty.
- Kuai Yue, Liu Xian, Deng Xi, and Cai Mao receive offices or rank, making conquest a rapid transfer of local elite capacity as well as territory.
- [[JianglingLateHan|江陵]] is presented as the strategic hinge that brings Cao-side pressure closer to [[SunQuan|孙权]] and Jiangdong.
- The episode credits the young Sun Quan with retaining a capable Jiangdong team and making his own decision at the approaching Red Cliffs crisis.
- Wen Ping's delayed surrender and grief are treated as evidence of old-lord loyalty rather than resistance that must be punished.
- Cao Cao's appointment of Wen Ping to Jiangxia preserves local military knowledge and converts a morally credible former opponent into frontier capacity.

## Key Quotes
> “真正的破产，从来不是失去城池兵马，而是灵魂深处的信念清零。” — the host's motivational reading of Liu Bei after Changban.

> “真忠臣也。” — Cao Cao's judgment of Wen Ping as rendered by the episode.

- [[CaoCao|曹操]], [[LiuCongLateHan|刘琮]], and [[Jingzhou|荆州]] - conquest, surrender, and reconstruction of the provincial personnel order.
- [[HanSongLateHan|韩嵩]], [[KuaiYue|蒯越]], [[LiuXianLateHan|刘先]], [[DengXiLateHan|邓羲]], and [[CaiMao|蔡瑁]] - Liu Biao-era officials absorbed through court appointments or rank.
- [[WenPing|文聘]], [[FormerEnemyLoyaltyReframing|旧敌忠义重释]], and [[CapturedOpponentTalentIncorporation|降敌任用式立威]] - old loyalty reclassified as evidence for trusted reuse.
- [[JianglingLateHan|江陵]] and [[SunQuan|孙权]] - corridor geography and the rising strategic pressure on Jiangdong.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is recorded. The source fills the missing transition between the Changban background used in Hanji 1098-1099 and Hanji 1096's pre-Red-Cliffs diplomacy.
- The opening claims about inner strength, spiritual bankruptcy, and inevitable recovery are motivational interpretation rather than historical proof of Liu Bei's private mental state.
- The source first says Liu Bei lost his wife and children, then specifically says Cao Chun captured two daughters; the exact family-loss sequence is therefore kept source-scoped pending verification against the underlying historical text.
- Official titles, ages, distances, Wen Ping's exact dialogue, the label “纯臣,” and the claim that he guarded Jiangxia for decades require checking against primary historical records before strict factual reuse.
