# 《资治通鉴·周纪》79丨史上用耕牛 打胜仗第一人

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the [[TianDan|田单]] counterattack opened in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-78-qiguo-mingyun-jing-bei-chengguan-gei-jiule-lpt3reyr6ok8g2lhcxrnd5qctyug|Zhouji 78]]. After [[YanHuiwang|燕惠王]] replaces [[LeYi|乐毅]] with [[QiJie|骑劫]], Tian Dan uses a layered sequence of divine-sign theater, rumor bait, enemy atrocity, false surrender, bribery, and the [[FireOxNightRaid|fire-ox night raid]] to break the Yan siege at [[JimoWarringStates|即墨]]. The later section returns [[QiXiangWang|齐襄王]] to [[Linzi|临淄]], rewards Tian Dan, and revisits [[JunWangHou|君王后]] and [[TaishiJiao|太史敫 / 太史角]] through [[MarriageRitualOrthodoxy|marriage ritual orthodoxy]] drawn from [[MenciusText|《孟子》]].

## Key Claims
- [[LeYi|乐毅]] flees to [[ZhaoState|赵国]] after the successful rumor operation, while [[TianDan|田单]] judges that the replacement commander [[QiJie|骑劫]] is far easier to exploit.
- Tian Dan first stages ritual and rumor effects: food offerings attract birds, enemy observers hear that a divine teacher has appeared, and Tian Dan publicly frames his orders as coming from that figure.
- His next rumor bait tells Yan that Qi fears mutilated prisoners and dug ancestral graves; Qi Jie acts on both, turning Yan's occupation from [[OccupationPacificationStrategy|pacification]] into [[AtrocityBackfireMobilization|atrocity backfire mobilization]].
- The visible fate of nose-cut prisoners and desecrated graves makes Jimo's defenders angrier and more willing to fight, while already occupied Qi cities also become less stable under Yan control.
- Tian Dan then creates a surrender signal: old, weak, and female defenders appear on the walls, hidden soldiers prepare below, and wealthy Jimo households send gold to Yan officers to make the surrender look credible.
- The [[FireOxNightRaid|fire-ox night raid]] combines physical shock with psychological preparation: decorated cattle with blades and burning tails charge from prepared wall openings at night, followed by five thousand warriors and citywide noise.
- The attack kills [[QiJie|骑劫]], routs Yan forces, and triggers the return of seventy-plus Qi cities to [[QiState|齐国]].
- Tian Dan escorts [[QiXiangWang|齐襄王]] from [[JuWarringStates|莒城]] back to [[Linzi|临淄]] and is made Anping Jun.
- The episode then reframes [[JunWangHou|君王后]]'s earlier crisis relationship with [[TianFazhang|田法章]]: her father [[TaishiJiao|太史敫 / 太史角]] refuses to see her because the marriage did not follow parental command and matchmaker ritual.
- Jun Wang Hou is still praised in the source for maintaining respect toward her father after becoming queen, so the episode does not treat the father-daughter conflict as a simple villain/hero split.
- [[YanHuiwang|燕惠王]] later regrets replacing Le Yi and fears [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] might use Le Yi against Yan, setting up the next episode's letter exchange.
