# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》940丨得民心者得天下，你信不信？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 188 CE Yizhou and disaster layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-941-shenchu-guanchang-ta-jing-xiang-ganfan-huangdi-liu-fqiz9pi7u6cltrn-cjmjz-r|Hanji 941]]. It follows [[FanLingLateHan|樊陵]]'s brief taiwei appointment, [[MaXiangLateHan|马相]] and [[ZhaoZhiLateHan|赵祗]]'s uprising at [[MianzhuLateHan|绵竹]], [[JiaLongLateHan|贾龙]]'s local counterattack, and [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]'s entry into [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] through lenient pacification. The closing flood segment connects late-Han disorder to [[DisasterResponseStateCapacity|disaster response state capacity]] and [[DisasterReliefCorruption|救灾物资贪腐]].

## Key Claims

- In 188 CE, the court appoints [[FanLingLateHan|樊陵]] from Yongle shaofu to taiwei in May and removes him in June, making high office look unstable before the later succession crisis.
- [[MaXiangLateHan|马相]] and [[ZhaoZhiLateHan|赵祗]] rise at [[MianzhuLateHan|绵竹]] under a Yellow Turban banner.
- The source says [[XiJianLateHan|郗俭]], the Yizhou inspector, had long alienated the population through heavy extraction, so the rebels can kill him quickly once the uprising begins.
- The revolt expands toward [[BaCommanderyLateHan|巴郡]] and [[QianweiCommanderyLateHan|犍为郡]], reaching several commanderies and tens of thousands of followers in about a month.
- [[MaXiangLateHan|马相]] then proclaims himself Son of Heaven, which the host reads as short-run military success turning into unchecked power fantasy rather than durable rule.
- [[JiaLongLateHan|贾龙]], a Yizhou staff official, gathers officials and commoners, fights for several days, drives [[MaXiangLateHan|马相]] away, and restores temporary order.
- [[JiaLongLateHan|贾龙]] then sends people to welcome [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]] into [[YizhouLateHan|益州]], because the province needs a governing center after the revolt.
- [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]] moves the provincial seat to [[MianzhuLateHan|绵竹]], applies lenient policy toward unsettled people and rebels, and uses [[YizhouPacificationByLeniency|益州宽惠安抚]] to gather support.
- The episode's title claim is that in disorder, people-support becomes a practical foundation of rule, not merely a moral slogan.
- The same year, floods hit seven commandery-states; some officials work on relief, while others steal relief materials and profit from disaster.
- The source treats disaster as a moral and institutional test: crisis exposes both officials who protect people and officials who convert suffering into private gain.
- The ending frames 188 CE as an important late-Han turning point and previews [[CaoCao|曹操]], then thirty-three, as a figure whose later rise will change the disorder.

## Key Quotes

> "得民心者得天下" - the episode's title frame for [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]'s Yizhou settlement.

> "小混混突然捡到AK" - the host's metaphor for [[MaXiangLateHan|马相]]'s power inflation after rapid success.

> "发国难财" - the moral label applied to officials who embezzle relief materials after flooding.
