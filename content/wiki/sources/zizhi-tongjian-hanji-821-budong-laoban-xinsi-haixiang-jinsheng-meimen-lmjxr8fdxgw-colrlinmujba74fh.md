# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》821丨不懂老板心思还想晋升？没门！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 141 CE [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]] layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-822-chailang-dangdao-ta-jiang-maotou-zhizhi-huangdi-yuefu-lqhpmayvd3p07byr-zoiv-jyhdv|Hanji 822]]. It pairs a workplace [[PromotionPositionFit|promotion-position-fit]] frame with Shun's Qiang-frontier decisions: [[MaXianLateHan|马贤]] delays after being chosen over objections, [[MaRongLateHan|马融]] and [[HuangfuGuiLateHan|皇甫规]] warn that the campaign is being mismanaged, and Ma Xian dies at Shegu Mountain with his sons.

The second half follows [[ZhaoChongLateHan|赵冲]]'s partial pursuit-and-surrender success, Huangfu Gui's root-cause diagnosis of frontier corruption and exhausted soldiers, and the Liang-family transition after [[LiangShangLateHan|梁商]] dies. [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] becomes Grand General despite known violence, which the source treats as an early causal layer for later [[OuterRelativeCourtPower|outer-relative court power]].

## Key Claims

- Promotion is framed as more than effort: an employee needs to understand superior demand, build trust, solve visible problems, and reduce organizational risk.
- Emperor Shun insists on using Ma Xian against the Western Qiang despite Liang Shang's opposition, and Ma Xian's delayed campaign lets Qiang forces link up before Han troops act decisively.
- Ma Rong, then associated with Wudu commandery administration, asks for 5,000 troops from Guandong, criticizes Ma Xian's slow and luxurious command style, and argues that immediate action could defeat the rebels quickly.
- Huangfu Gui also warns that Ma Xian will lose; the court ignores both Ma Rong and Huangfu Gui before Ma Xian is defeated and killed at Shegu Mountain on 141-01-21.
- The defeat worsens frontier pressure: Eastern and Western Qiang link up, Gongtang Qiang move toward Longxi and the Guanzhong/Sanfu field, and Western Han imperial tombs are burned and looted.
- Zhao Chong kills or captures several hundred pursuers, induces more than two thousand surrenders, and receives command over the Hexi four-commandery local troops, but the wider Qiang problem remains unresolved.
- Huangfu Gui's memorial argues that corrupt frontier governors, wasted campaign funds, cruel officials, hungry soldiers, and unreliable command incentives caused the Qiang revolt; his request to attack with 5,000 selected troops is rejected.
- Liang Shang dies in 141 after asking for a frugal burial, but the court gives lavish funerary honors; Liang Ji succeeds as Grand General and Liang Buyi becomes Henan Yin.
- Sima Guang criticizes Shun for empowering Liang Ji despite already knowing Liang's violence, while Liang Shang's recommendation of Zhou Ju leads Shun to appoint Zhou Ju as remonstrance adviser.

## Key Quotes

> "晋升不是单纯奖励努力" - the episode's workplace frame for promotion as role, trust, and demand fit.

> "一个月之内就能平定叛军" - the source's summary of Ma Rong's confidence if he received 5,000 Guandong troops.

> "梁冀凶恶暴虐，著于平昔" - the Sima Guang judgment quoted by the episode about Shun's choice to empower Liang Ji.
