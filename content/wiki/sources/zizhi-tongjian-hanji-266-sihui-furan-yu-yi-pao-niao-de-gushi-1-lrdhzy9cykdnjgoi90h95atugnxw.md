# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》266｜“死灰复燃”与一泡尿的故事（1）

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode introduces the “死灰复燃” idiom through [[HanAnguoWesternHan|韩安国]]'s reported prison exchange with the jailer Tian Jia, then backfills Han's earlier service to [[LiuWuLiangKingWesternHan|梁孝王刘武]]. After Liu Wu's post-Rebellion-of-the-Seven-States prestige reportedly grows into court protocol comparable to the emperor's, Han recognizes that [[HanJingdi|汉景帝]]'s anger threatens both the prince and the wider imperial order.

Han's mediation works through two moves that the source keeps in tension. Through [[LiuPiaoWesternHan|馆陶长公主刘嫖]], he reframes Liu Wu's excess as an immature display of imperial and dowager favor rather than a bid for the throne, giving Jingdi a less threatening interpretation. He then warns Liu Wu that family affection cannot indefinitely excuse conduct outside ministerial and princely limits. The episode therefore extends [[KinshipProtocolBoundary|亲情名分边界]]: effective court mediation can soften perceived motive without denying the breached boundary.

## Key Claims

- The episode attributes the idiom “死灰复燃” to Han Anguo's exchange with jailer Tian Jia after Han is imprisoned and repeatedly humiliated.
- It briefly closes the earlier Tian Fen-Dou Ying branch by reporting Dou Ying's winter execution, Tian Fen's death soon afterward, and Han Wudi's later discovery of Tian Fen's dealings with Liu An.
- Liu Wu's contribution during the Rebellion of the Seven States gives him prestige, but his later use of emperor-like court protocol reportedly alarms Han Jingdi.
- When Jingdi and Empress Dowager Dou both decline to receive him, Han Anguo approaches Liu Piao as an indirect family communication channel.
- Han's plea does not deny Liu Wu's improper conduct; it recasts the prince's motive as boastful reliance on affection rather than disloyal ambition.
- Jingdi accepts the less threatening interpretation, receives Han Anguo, and rewards him.
- Han also warns Liu Wu that continued reliance on brotherly affection cannot override law, rank, and the duties of a vassal prince.

## Key Quotes

> “死灰独不复燃乎？” - the threat attributed to Han Anguo after repeated humiliation in prison.

> “燃即溺之。” - Tian Jia's reported answer, rendered by the episode as urinating on any rekindled ash.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with the current wiki was found. Hanji 275 covers Han Anguo's final frontier failure; this episode supplies an earlier case of his political mediation and the prison anecdote that Hanji 275 only alludes to.
- Chronology caution: the source places Dou Ying's execution on a winter twelfth-month date and Tian Fen's death in “the same year's” spring. That phrasing is internally difficult under ordinary seasonal order and should not be used to fix an exact chronology without checking the underlying calendar and texts.
- Source-scope caution: the exact prison dialogue, Tian Jia's conduct, the first-use claim for the idiom, audience sequence, tears, private motives, speech wording, rewards, and the claim that Han's intervention alone resolved the crisis require comparison with the transmitted records.
- Interpretation caution: describing Liu Wu's conduct as mere pride in family favor is Han Anguo's persuasive reframing inside the episode, not independent proof that the prince had no succession ambition or political threat.
- Transcript boundary: the supplied document explicitly ends during the evaluation of Han Anguo's political skill, so the continuation, later rehabilitation, and complete “死灰复燃” outcome are not established here.
