# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》854：历史告诉你，选官用官很重要！

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode reads the 160-161 CE [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] layer as a lesson in selection and appointment: upper-level conduct and the fit between person and office determine whether local officials create order or amplify collapse. The historical core moves across [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]]'s western Qiang pursuit, [[XiaFangLateHan|夏方]]'s Jiaozhi appointment, [[ZongZiLateHan|宗资]] and [[HuangfuGuiLateHan|皇甫规]] in the Taishan bandit crisis, [[LiuJuLateHan|刘矩]]'s Yongqiu mediation, and the fiscal stress behind pay cuts, noble rent loans, and priced rank sales. The transcript contains several ASR-name variants, so the source note normalizes them against the received Tongjian passage while keeping uncertain details source-scoped.

## Key Claims
- Good or bad company forms conduct, and the episode maps that domestic-moral lesson onto government: if senior officials act loosely, grassroots execution becomes disorderly.
- [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]]'s 160 CE campaign shows that hard frontier capacity can still produce results, even under brutal logistical pressure and long pursuit.
- [[XiaFangLateHan|夏方]] and [[HuangfuGuiLateHan|皇甫规]] show the episode's preferred answer to disorder: appoint a person whose authority, reputation, and method fit the local problem.
- [[LiuJuLateHan|刘矩]] supplies the humane local-governance example, resolving lawsuits through ritualized instruction and restraint rather than immediate coercion.
- Repeated fires, epidemic, landslides, hail, earthquakes, and frontier/local revolts mark the Huan court as administratively stressed rather than simply unlucky.
- Salary cuts, compulsory-like borrowing from kings and marquises, and sale of ranks such as 关内侯, 虎贲, 羽林缇骑, 营士, and 五大夫 extend [[RankSaleDisasterFinance|鬻爵赈灾融资]] into the late-Han crisis field.

## Key Quotes
> "善人同处，则日闻嘉训；恶人从游，则日生邪情。" — opening moral frame for companionship and environmental influence.

> "其身正，不令而行；其身不正，虽令不从。" — governing analogy linking official conduct to compliance below.

> "争怒可忍，县官不可入。" — Liu Ju's remembered warning that anger in ordinary disputes can be endured before entering litigation.
