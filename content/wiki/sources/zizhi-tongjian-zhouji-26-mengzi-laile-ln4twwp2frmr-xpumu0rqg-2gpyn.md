# 《资治通鉴·周纪》26丨孟子来了！

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode moves from [[ShenBuhai|申不害]]'s quiet death in 337 BCE to [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]]'s 336 BCE meeting with [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王 / 梁惠王]]. It uses the brief [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] notices to connect three layers: [[HanState|韩国]]'s Zhang-family continuity behind [[ZhangLiang|张良]], the uncertain omen of [[TaiqiuShe|太丘社]] disappearing in [[SongState|宋国]], and the [[MenciusText|《孟子》]] opening debate over profit, renyi, and statecraft. The episode's distinct contribution is [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s reconciliation of [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]] and [[Zisi|子思]] on benefit: renyi is not anti-benefit, but the ordering of benefit under renyi matters.

## Key Claims
- In 337 BCE, [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] records only [[ShenBuhai|申不害]]'s death; the episode contrasts his quiet end with [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]]'s dramatic collapse.
- [[ZhangKaidi|张开地]] succeeds as Han's chief minister after Shen Buhai; his son [[ZhangPingHan|张平]] also serves Han, making [[ZhangLiang|张良]]'s later anti-Qin and pro-Han motive a family-political inheritance.
- In 336 BCE, the chronicle pairs the disappearance of [[TaiqiuShe|宋太丘社]] with [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]] visiting Wei; the source treats the first as an unresolved omen and the second as the episode's core event.
- The episode explains Taiqiu She as probably a shrine or marker associated with the soil/community god, while stressing that its disappearance and meaning remain unsettled.
- The host contrasts a Wei-state view of Mencius, where he is a famous scholar to be received politely, with a Confucian-textual view where his arrival has major symbolic weight.
- [[MenciusText|《孟子》]] is presented as more continuous and readable than [[Analects|《论语》]], with [[ZhuXi|朱熹]] cited for the view that even possible disciple material was reviewed by Mencius.
- The opening of [[MenciusText|《孟子》]], "梁惠王" / "King Hui of Liang," is treated as an unusually fitting excerpt for [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] because it states Mencius's core problem immediately.
- [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王 / 梁惠王]] asks what will benefit his state; the episode reads this as a request for a useful reform or policy program, not idle conversation.
- Mencius's answer, "何必曰利，仁义而已," is not treated as hostility to practical benefit. The episode argues that Mencius puts renyi first and treats benefit as a secondary outcome.
- The [[Zisi|子思]] story appears to reverse Mencius: Zisi tells Mencius that governing people should begin by benefiting them, because renyi exists to bring people real benefit.
- [[SimaGuang|司马光]] reconciles the two by saying both statements point to the same relation between renyi and benefit; Mencius avoids talking benefit to Wei Hui Wang because an unren ruler would misunderstand the order.
- The episode says Sima Guang personally disliked Mencius and wrote [[YiMeng|《疑孟》]], while [[WangAnshi|王安石]] helped raise Mencius's status in Song intellectual and political debate.

## Key Quotes
> "叟不远千里而来，亦将有利吾国乎？" - Wei Hui Wang's opening question to Mencius.

> "何必曰利，仁义而已。" - Mencius's reply, as the source's central renyi-benefit passage.

> "仁义本来就是用来给百姓带来利益的" - the episode's paraphrase of Zisi's position.

> "司马光其实不大喜欢孟子" - the host's bridge from the ancient passage into Song reception politics.

## Contradictions
- No direct contradiction found.
