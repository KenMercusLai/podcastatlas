# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》362-1｜他被称为皇帝的钱袋子，到底有啥能耐？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode explains why [[SangHongyangWesternHan|桑弘羊]] became a central fiscal official under [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]. It credits him with administering salt and iron state operations, using the [[PingzhunLawWesternHan|平准法]] to buy and sell goods countercyclically, and expanding public grain and material reserves through contribution-for-office and redemption measures.

The durable synthesis is that fiscal success and distributional burden are different measures. The episode presents large treasury, granary, and transport gains without a formal tax increase, while later notes document objections to official market participation, poor monopoly goods, forced sales, and household costs. It therefore adds the strongest current positive case for Sang Hongyang's administrative capacity without settling the broader welfare judgment.

## Key Claims

- Sang Hongyang, presented as the son of a merchant, becomes a leading fiscal administrator able to turn commercial knowledge into state capacity.
- He administers salt and iron state operations while serving in the central agricultural-finance apparatus; the exact office wording in the supplied summary remains uncertain.
- The pingzhun mechanism uses public stocks and purchasing power to buy when prices are low and sell when prices are high, aiming both to steady prices and to limit extraordinary merchant profit.
- Local tribute goods and interregional transfer are coordinated with central buying and selling, making the policy a logistics system as well as a price intervention.
- Contribution-for-office and redemption-through-grain are presented as ways to enlarge state stocks; the episode reports full central and frontier granaries, abundant textiles, and no increase in ordinary taxation.
- Han Wudi rewards Sang Hongyang with the Left Shuzhang rank and gold, and later keeps him among the officials entrusted to assist the young [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|刘弗陵]].
- The closing reflection treats Sang's career as a question about whether commercial ability should build private wealth or serve wider public purposes.

## Key Quotes

> “贵的时候卖、贱的时候买” - the episode's plain-language explanation of the pingzhun mechanism.
