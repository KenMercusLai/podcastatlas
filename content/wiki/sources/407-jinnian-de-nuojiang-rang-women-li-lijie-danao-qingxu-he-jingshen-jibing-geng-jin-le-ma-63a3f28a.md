## Summary
This [[ShengdongJixi|声东击西]] publication has [[WuChenghan|吴承翰]] and [[Yaxian|雅贤]] explain [[Optogenetics]] as a causal neuroscience tool while preserving the distance between animal behavior, human subjective experience, psychiatric diagnosis, and clinical treatment. It connects the method to [[PsychiatryMeasurementGap|psychiatry's measurement gap]], [[PsychiatricDiagnosisMechanismBoundary|the boundary between diagnosis and mechanism]], and the [[NeurotechnologyAgencyBoundary|ethical boundary between treatment and control]]. ## Key Claims
- [[Optogenetics]] uses genetically delivered light-sensitive proteins and targeted illumination to activate or inhibit selected neurons, enabling stronger causal tests than observation alone.
- Avoidance, freezing, sweet preference, swimming, eating, and other animal behaviors are measurable experimental outcomes but do not directly reveal subjective experience or establish an equivalent human disorder.
- SSRI efficacy does not by itself prove that depression is caused by serotonin deficiency; treatment response and disease cause are different claims.
- Anxiety, fear, depression, and compulsion can contain separable physiological, behavioral, subjective, and circuit components rather than one neural switch.
- Interviews, scales, self-report, behavior, and functional impairment remain central to psychiatric diagnosis because imaging and electrophysiology are not decisive standalone tests for most disorders.
- Deep-brain stimulation, TMS, and other neuromodulation approaches may help selected patients, but target uncertainty, invasiveness, broad stimulation, and variable response constrain translation.
- Retinal applications and drug-discovery platforms suggest nearer-term clinical routes for optogenetics than routine direct control of human psychiatric states.
- Manipulating feeding, fear, aggression, desire, or personality-relevant behavior makes consent, autonomy, identity continuity, and protection against coercion part of the clinical question.
- Basic research can become a transformative tool without beginning from an immediate product or treatment goal.
