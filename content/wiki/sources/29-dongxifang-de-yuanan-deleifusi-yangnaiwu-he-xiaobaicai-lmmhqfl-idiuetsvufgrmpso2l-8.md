# 29 东西方的冤案：德雷福斯、杨乃武和小白菜

## Summary

This [[WeirdHistoryPodcast|怪东西 Weird History]] episode compares the [[DreyfusAffair|Dreyfus Affair]] with the [[YangNaiwuXiaoBaicaiCase|杨乃武与小白菜案]]. It argues that neither miscarriage of justice persisted because the facts were unusually difficult: prejudice, coercion, secret or defective procedure, bureaucratic deference, and institutional reputation made officials defend conclusions after contrary evidence appeared.

The comparison also identifies a contingent correction coalition. Family petitioning, insiders willing to test the record, patrons and officials able to reopen procedure, newspapers willing to sustain attention, and changing political conditions eventually overcame institutional resistance. Yet pardon or acquittal did not restore all lost liberty, status, health, or reputation, so formal correction remained materially incomplete.

## Key Claims

- [[AlfredDreyfus|Alfred Dreyfus]] was selected through circumstantial suspicion shaped by his Jewish identity, Alsatian origin, knowledge of German, and military anxiety after France's defeat by Prussia.
- Secret evidence, disputed handwriting analysis, forged documents, and the army's defense of institutional honor converted an initial weak case into the long-running [[DreyfusAffair|Dreyfus Affair]].
- Picquart's internal investigation, Dreyfus's family, Émile Zola's public intervention, wider press attention, and political change gradually made continued suppression more costly.
- Presidential pardon freed Dreyfus without declaring him innocent; only the 1906 annulment removed the conviction and restored his military status.
- In the [[YangNaiwuXiaoBaicaiCase|杨乃武与小白菜案]], rumor and local hostility directed suspicion toward [[YangNaiwu|杨乃武]] and [[XiaoBaicai|小白菜]], while torture produced confessions unsupported by reliable proof of poison purchase or delivery.
- Successive local and provincial reviews largely repeated the original finding, showing how hierarchical deference and responsibility avoidance can turn one defective inquiry into a system-wide judgment.
- Family appeals, [[HuXueyan|胡雪岩]]'s financial and network support, Beijing officials, and sustained [[ShenBao|《申报》]] reporting helped move the case beyond the local system and obtain a capital retrial and autopsy.
- Both cases show [[WrongfulConvictionPersistenceCorrection|wrongful conviction persistence and correction]] as a political and institutional process, not a simple moment when truth defeats error.

## Key Quotes

> “谁是真凶”并不是核心难题；更困难的是在真相已经可见时，让制度承认并纠正错误。 - episode's comparative thesis, paraphrased from the source summary.

> 赦免并不等于无罪。 - distinction drawn between Dreyfus's release and eventual exoneration.
