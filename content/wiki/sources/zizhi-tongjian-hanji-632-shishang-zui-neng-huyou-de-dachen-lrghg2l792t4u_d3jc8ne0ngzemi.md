# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》632丨史上最能忽悠的大臣

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode fills the 8 CE accession gap between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-631-gudai-sharen-zhenhui-ba-baigu-duichengshan-lgwrpwwu51hp6-lgqmna2ab7yvab|Hanji 631]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-633-nuza-chuanguo-yuxi-ta-jing-ruci-ganglie-lnmnwbk28ayllz5fip1a1kzm1ais|Hanji 633]]. It follows [[WangMang|王莽]] turning the "赤厄三七" prophecy, a new well, stone animals, copper signs, and court procedure into a public claim that [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]] rule had expired and Heaven required him to become emperor.

The decisive episode is [[AiZhangXinOfficial|哀章]]'s manufactured copper casket. By writing a Heaven-backed transfer document, inserting Wang Mang's ministers, and adding himself to the future government list, Ai Zhang turns sacred-sign production into self-appointment. Wang Mang then receives the casket at Gaozu's temple, issues an accession proclamation, founds the [[XinDynasty|新朝]], and recodes calendar, colors, sacrifices, and daily time while still claiming he will eventually return authority to [[RuziYing|孺子婴 / 刘婴]].

## Key Claims

- Wang Mang interprets "赤厄三七" as a timetable for the exhaustion of Han rule, converting a flexible prophecy into an argument that dynastic transfer is already due.
- Reports of a new well, stone ox, inscribed stone, and copper sign accumulate as mutually reinforcing public evidence rather than isolated marvels.
- Removing the word "摄" from Wang Mang's public authority and renaming the era make title and administrative vocabulary part of the accession process before the final enthronement.
- Wang Mang continues to invoke the Duke of Zhou and promises to return rule to Liu Ying after adulthood, even while requesting the forms of full imperial authority.
- Ai Zhang fabricates a copper casket and mandate texts, names eight existing Wang Mang ministers, adds Wang Xing and Wang Sheng, and inserts himself with an office and title.
- Ai Zhang's maneuver shows that fuming can allocate careers as well as legitimate rulers: a useful prophecy author can write himself into the order he claims merely to reveal.
- Wang Mang's reception of the casket at Gaozu's temple, accession proclamation, calendar change, yellow regalia, sacrificial rules, and timekeeping change combine sacred transfer with institutional recoding.
- The episode ends with [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] retaining the [[HeirloomImperialSeal|传国玉玺]] and Wang Mang sending [[WangShunXinTaishi|王顺]] to demand it, setting up Hanji 633's coerced transfer.

## Key Quotes

> "赤厄三七，错即三六" - the prophecy Wang Mang uses to frame Han rule as having reached its limit.

> "天告地符，献者封侯" - the reported copper-sign formula joining Heaven's message to reward for the presenter.

> "天地行玺金匮图" - one of Ai Zhang's labels for the fabricated copper casket.
