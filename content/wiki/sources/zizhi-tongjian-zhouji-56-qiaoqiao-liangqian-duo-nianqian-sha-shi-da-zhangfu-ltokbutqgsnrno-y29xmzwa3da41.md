# 《资治通鉴·周纪》56丨 瞧瞧两千多年前啥是大丈夫

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode pauses after [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]'s death branch to reassess the broader [[ZonghengjiaDiplomacy|纵横家外交]] field around [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s fifth year, 310 BCE. It contrasts [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]]'s [[MencianGreatManCriterion|大丈夫标准]] with the practical careers of Zhang Yi, [[SuQin|苏秦]], and [[GongsunYan|犀首公孙衍]], then uses [[YangXiong|扬雄]]'s [[Fayan|《法言》]] to shift from talent and resilience to motive and moral legitimacy. The episode's core synthesis is that persuasive ability alone is not enough: a strategist may be brilliant, resilient, and effective, but [[MotiveBasedTalentEvaluation|动机导向的才干评价]] asks whether the talent serves public rescue, private富贵, or morally suspect manipulation.

## Key Claims
- The source places the discussion in [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s fifth year, 310 BCE, when the chronicle summarizes the vogue for persuaders and strategists after Zhang Yi and Su Qin's successes.
- Zhang Yi and Su Qin gained wealth and office through persuasion, making later figures such as [[GongsunYan|犀首公孙衍]], [[SuDai|苏代]], [[SuLi|苏厉]], [[ZhouZui|周最]], and [[LouHuan|楼缓]] part of a wider world of court-to-court diplomatic mobility.
- [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]] rejects the idea that making rulers fear or making wars stop is sufficient proof of being a "great man."
- Mencius defines the real great man as someone who stands in the correct place, follows the correct way, works with the people when successful, preserves himself when unsuccessful, and cannot be corrupted by wealth, changed by poverty, or bent by force.
- The host admires the Mencian statement but reopens the judgment of Zhang Yi and Su Qin: Zhang Yi stayed with [[LianhengStrategy|连横]] for Qin, Su Qin stayed with [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] for the eastern states, and both endured humiliation, failure, and pressure.
- The episode therefore creates a live evaluation tension: under a resilience-and-consistency test Zhang Yi and Su Qin may still look like "great men," while under a Mencian moral-direction test their status remains suspect.
- [[YangXiong|扬雄]]'s [[Fayan|《法言》]] rejects the claim that the arts of Zhang Yi and Su Qin, learned under [[Guiguzi|鬼谷子]], should be praised because they produced years of relative peace.
- The same Yang Xiong passage distinguishes [[Zigong|子贡 / 端木赐]] from Zhang Yi and Su Qin: Zigong's diplomacy is framed as preventing chaos and saving his own state, while Zhang Yi and Su Qin are judged as pursuing wealth and rank.
- The closing point is that rare talent, independent method, and spectacular success do not automatically make a talent morally acceptable.

## Key Quotes
> "富贵不能淫，贫贱不能移，威武不能屈" - Mencius's standard at the center of the episode.

> "君子强学而力行" - Yang Xiong line used as the episode's closing moral frame.
