# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》373-1｜公孙贺泣跪辞丞相，揭开西汉的遮羞布

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the 103 BCE appointment scene behind [[GongsunHeWesternHan|公孙贺]]'s later cautious chancellorship. After Chancellor Shi Qing dies, [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] appoints the cavalry general and marquis Gongsun He, who weeps, kneels, describes himself as a horseman and archer without the necessary talent, and accepts only after imperial insistence turns refusal into its own danger.

The durable synthesis is that visible promotion can increase exposure rather than safety. The source reads Gongsun He's refusal through [[HighOfficeRefusalAsRiskAvoidance|高位拒受式避险]]: military merit and proximity to [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]] make him eligible for the premiership, but do not guarantee competence or protection in an office carrying broad responsibility under a demanding ruler. The same year joins horse requisition, locust damage, imperial travel and sacrifice, and the distant [[DayuanWesternHan|大宛]] war, giving his fear a concrete administrative and fiscal setting.

## Key Claims

- In Taichu 2 / 103 BCE, Chancellor Shi Qing dies and Han Wudi appoints Gongsun He, a marquis and former anti-Xiongnu cavalry commander, as chancellor.
- Gongsun He responds by collapsing, weeping, kowtowing, and asking Han Wudi to withdraw the appointment rather than treating it as an uncomplicated honor.
- He explains that he entered office through riding and archery and lacks the talent to bear chancellor-level responsibility.
- Han Wudi remains moved but insists; the episode interprets the ruler's growing anger as making continued refusal potentially lethal, so Gongsun He accepts.
- The host treats Gongsun He's reaction as foresight rather than cowardice: high rank concentrates accountability and danger when role demands exceed the officeholder's capacity.
- The same annalistic year includes Wudi's Hedong journey and Houtu sacrifice, state registration and requisition of official and civilian horses, and an autumn locust disaster.
- The episode places these burdens beside the Han-Dayuan war and emphasizes distance as a major Dayuan defense: the host locates the polity in the Ferghana Valley and gives the Chang'an route as more than 3,300 kilometers across deserts, mountains, and sparsely inhabited terrain.

## Key Quotes

> "自己本来没有什么本事，只是靠骑马射箭为官。" - the episode's summary of Gongsun He's capacity objection.

> "害怕因此受到重责。" - the episode's summary of the danger Gongsun He says he anticipates after accepting office.
