# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》814丨当官不为民作主，不如回家卖红薯

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 133 CE [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]] layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-815-kebei-gudai-zhongchen-de-jiafeng-zhishang-llkhbxo-63pjw-gk9pihnbfr94v|Hanji 815]]. It starts from the Luoyang Xuande Hall ground split and follows [[LiGuLateHan|李固]] using the disaster inquiry to criticize private-favor titles and offices for [[SongELateHan|宋娥]], the Liang empress's kin, and near-attendant children, then to argue that Shangshu and inner-court posts must be disciplined because the court is the empire's "heart and belly."

The second historical voice is [[MaRongLateHan|马融]], then Right Fufeng merit officer, who tells Shun that people have heard many promises of good government without receiving concrete benefit. The durable contribution is a paired governance diagnosis: natural disaster creates a permissible route for institutional remonstrance, while actual repair requires protecting appointment rules, reducing near-attendant power, and turning benevolent policy language into household-level relief.

## Key Claims

- The Xuande Hall ground split is treated as a political warning inside the Han disaster-omen frame, giving ministers a formal opening to criticize governance.
- Li Gu distinguishes private reward from public office: the ruler may give money to favored intimates, but titles and offices affect the people and should not be granted as personal favor.
- Li Gu warns that empress-kin and near-attendant children entering office through privilege or coerced recommendation erodes ordinary appointment rules.
- The Mingdi and Guantao princess example shows why even a small office matters more than a large cash gift when the officeholder can harm the public.
- Li Gu treats Shangshu and central court posts as the empire's command core: if the heart, source, root, or measuring standard is crooked, local governance will also fail.
- Li Gu's most sensitive proposal is to reduce eunuch and inner-court power, keeping only a small number of upright close attendants and capable minor officials.
- Ma Rong argues that good government must become visible benefits: lighter marriage and funeral burdens, timely farming, stable families, and household savings reduce disorder more than additional edicts.
- The opening emotional-practice and course-promotion segment is source-scoped and does not drive the historical synthesis.

## Key Quotes

> "光打雷不下雨" - host's gloss for Ma Rong's warning that people hear policy promises without receiving benefits.

> "心腹有病，四肢也会坏" - Li Gu's court-and-locality metaphor as summarized by the episode.

> "当官不为民作主，不如回家卖红薯" - episode title used as the public-duty frame for Ma Rong's advice.
