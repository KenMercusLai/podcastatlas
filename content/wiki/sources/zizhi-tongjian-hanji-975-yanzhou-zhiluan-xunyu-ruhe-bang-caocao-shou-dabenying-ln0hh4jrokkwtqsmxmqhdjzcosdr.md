# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》975丨兖州之乱：荀彧如何帮曹操守大本营？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the missing prelude to [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] crisis before the later [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-978-gushen-quantui-shuwan-dijun-ta-zenme-zuodao-de-lhgd8vsvuaf5oi9aefh0wxabmzwv|Hanji 978]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-979-zaoyu-tongliao-beici-ta-ruhe-wei-caocao-liwan-kuanglan-luu58a2ww11g-radh-5g4l7fcis|Hanji 979]] base-preservation sequence. While Cao Cao is attacking [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] in [[Xuzhou|徐州]], [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] and [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] welcome [[LyuBu|吕布]] as Yanzhou governor, and [[XunYu|荀彧]] detects the danger from title wording, route direction, and the weakened rear. The episode also contrasts Xun Yu's fast crisis judgment with [[TianFengLateHan|田丰]]'s warning inside [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s camp, setting up the following adviser-system discussion.

## Key Claims
- The episode frames durable achievement as a combination of large strategic direction and attention to concrete details, using [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s concern for grain, terrain, weather, and people as the setup for [[DetailBasedCrisisDetection|细节式危机识别]].
- In 194 CE, Cao Cao's attack on [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] leaves the [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] rear exposed, creating the opening for [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] and [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] to invite [[LyuBu|吕布]] into the province.
- [[XunYu|荀彧]] treats Zhang Miao's envoy wording as political data: calling Cao Cao "曹公" rather than "兖州牧" suggests that the envoy is avoiding Cao Cao's provincial authority.
- Xun Yu also notices that Lü Bu's southward movement from Henei does not match a story that he is coming to help Cao Cao attack Xuzhou, making route direction part of [[XingshiqingStrategicAssessment|行势情战略评估]].
- Xun Yu urgently recalls [[XiahouDun|夏侯惇]] from [[PuyangLateHan|濮阳]] to stabilize [[JuanchengLateHan|鄄城]], where Xiahou Dun interrogates officials and kills dozens implicated in the rebellion.
- Xiahou Dun's departure lets Lü Bu seize Puyang, but the episode says Xun Yu then uses an old-woman informant and street rhyme as source-scoped information tactics; the wiki records this as dramatic [[BattlefieldInformationControl|战场信息控制]] rather than independently settled chronology.
- [[TianFengLateHan|田丰]] is shown identifying Zhang Miao's grain request to Yuan Shao as support for Lü Bu, reinforcing the pattern that adviser value depends on whether a leader converts warning into decision.
- The closing question links the episode to [[AdviserStateArchitecture|谋士政权架构]]: intelligence alone does not automatically make a counselor a "王佐之才"; adviser effectiveness also depends on temperament, fit with the ruler, and executable authority.
