# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1122丨曹操假意和谈，背后却把马超往死里坑？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the psychological and operational resolution of the 211 CE [[WeinanBattleLateHan|渭南之战]]. [[CaoCao|曹操]] exploits weak trust between [[MaChao|马超]] and [[HanSui|韩遂]] through a conspicuously friendly battlefield conversation and an apparently altered letter, then attacks after the coalition's coordination and morale have deteriorated.

The episode also gives Cao Cao's retrospective explanation of the wider campaign: fix the coalition at Tong Pass, let [[XuHuangCaoGeneral|徐晃]] and [[ZhuLingLateHan|朱灵]] secure the western crossing, build protected routes and camps, feign weakness, provision the army, and accept peace terms only long enough to relax the enemy. Its central synthesis is therefore not a single trick but a sequence joining route control, delay, [[RelationalSignalCoalitionFracture|关系信号式离间]], rest, and concentrated attack.

## Key Claims

- Han Sui's warm field conversation with Cao Cao concerns old capital acquaintances rather than military terms, but Ma Chao interprets the intimacy and Han Sui's vague report as possible private alignment.
- The episode says Ma Chao had earlier persuaded a reluctant Han Sui to rise by renouncing his own father and asking Han Sui to accept him as a son; it treats this crisis-made relationship as emotionally powerful but institutionally weak.
- Cao Cao's later letter to Han Sui contains visible deletions and alterations that look as though Han Sui changed it, converting an ambiguous relationship signal into stronger evidence for Ma Chao's suspicion.
- Once coalition trust and morale weaken, Cao Cao first uses light troops to engage and then attacks the flanks with elite cavalry; the source reports a rapid collapse of the larger coalition force.
- Ma Chao, Han Sui, and Liang Xing escape while other leaders are killed or dispersed; [[XiahouYuan|夏侯渊]] continues western operations in the following year.
- Cao Cao explains that fixing the enemy at Tong Pass opened a western route for Xu Huang and Zhu Ling, after which protected corridors, river crossing, fortified camps, and apparent passivity made the coalition more complacent.
- Feigned acceptance of peace terms gives Cao Cao's force time to rest while lowering the coalition's guard; the episode distinguishes this operational lull from a genuine negotiated settlement.
- Cao Cao welcomes the enemy's concentration because separate forces holding difficult positions would require repeated campaigns, whereas a large coalition without unified command creates one decisive target.
- The episode links the victory to the later execution of [[MaTeng|马腾]]'s household at [[YeLateHan|邺城]], but exact numbers, legal framing, and causal responsibility remain source-scoped.
- The closing note says Cao Cao's army is supplied from Hedong and defers the responsible administrator's identity to the next installment.

## Key Quotes

> “正人行邪法，邪法亦正；邪人行正法，正法亦邪。” - the episode's moral opening, used to frame later questions of method and character.
