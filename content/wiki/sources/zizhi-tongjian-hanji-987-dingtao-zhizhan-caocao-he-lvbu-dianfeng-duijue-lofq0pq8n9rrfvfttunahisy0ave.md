# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》987丨定陶之战：曹操和吕布巅峰对决

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s 195 CE [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] base-recovery problem immediately before the later Emperor Xian branch in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-988-shoujin-lingru-ta-shi-hanmo-beican-de-huangdi-lsdkpakvk5u-omegcri-wstup-zl|Hanji 988]]. [[XunYu|荀彧]] persuades Cao Cao not to chase the tempting [[Xuzhou|徐州]] opening after [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]]'s death, because [[LyuBu|吕布]] and [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] still threaten Cao Cao's root base. The episode then turns that strategic advice into battlefield outcome: Cao Cao uses terrain and伏兵 to defeat Lü Bu at [[DingtaoBattleLateHan|定陶之战]], after which Lü Bu flees to [[LiuBei|刘备]] and creates an etiquette breach through [[LadyYanLyuBu|严氏]].

## Key Claims
- Xun Yu treats [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] as Cao Cao's equivalent of [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] for Liu Bang or [[HeneiCommandery|河内]] for Liu Xiu: a secure base from which attack and retreat both remain possible.
- The apparent Xuzhou opportunity is dangerous because Tao Qian's death and Liu Bei's weak roots do not erase Cao Cao's own rear-area problem.
- Xun Yu's argument combines grain, manpower,民心, and route security: if Cao Cao leaves too many troops behind he cannot attack, but if he leaves too few, harvest, firewood, and city defense become unsafe.
- Xuzhou's people are unlikely to welcome Cao Cao because his earlier campaign left deep resentment, and its wheat has already been harvested, so a large army could quickly run out of supply.
- Cao Cao's acceptance of Xun Yu's advice makes the episode a new case for [[RootBasePriority|根本根据地优先]] and [[XingshiqingStrategicAssessment|行势情战略评估]].
- At Dingtao, Lü Bu and [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] bring more than ten thousand troops while Cao Cao has fewer than one thousand soldiers in camp because most are out harvesting wheat.
- Cao Cao turns disadvantage into a terrain trap by hiding soldiers behind a western dike, using the southern forest and a small exposed force to draw Lü Bu forward, then releasing the伏兵.
- Lü Bu's defeat at [[Dingtao|定陶]] lets Cao Cao recover surrounding counties and close the immediate Yanzhou branch later remembered in the Zhang Miao and Zang Hong sources.
- Lü Bu's first reception by Liu Bei is politically awkward: by seating Liu Bei on Lady Yan's bed, asking her to salute, and calling Liu Bei "old brother," he blurs public/private and host/guest status boundaries.
- The closing preview shifts toward [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]], and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]], setting up the next source's late-Han court crisis.
