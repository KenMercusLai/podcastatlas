# No.229 对话少数派老麦：从 Palm、WebOS 到 AI 硬件，依然不想成为多数派

## Summary
This [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] episode has [[LiuFei|刘飞]] interviewing [[LaoMaiSspai|老麦 / Michael]] about moving from early smartphone tinkering into [[ZhiJiWang|主机网]] and then [[Sspai|少数派]]. The discussion uses [[Palm]], [[WebOS|webOS]], [[Huaqiangbei|华强北]], BBS forums, carrier content demand, app recommendation, author payments, paid courses, product co-creation, offline spaces, and AI-era content dilution to explain how a niche technology community can become a durable media and product platform.

The durable contribution is a community-business lifecycle case. [[ZhiJiWang|主机网]] began as an [[EnthusiastDeviceCommunity|enthusiast device community]] around Palm and webOS, then lost its platform future when HP abandoned webOS hardware and [[WeChat]] could not be supported; [[Sspai|少数派]] converted trust, application knowledge, authors, users, products, and offline gathering into a [[CreatorProductCoCreationLoop|creator-product co-creation loop]] rather than chasing undifferentiated traffic.

## Key Claims
- [[LaoMaiSspai|老麦 / Michael]] entered internet work through personal interest, early smartphones, ROM sharing, and community participation rather than a formal media or startup plan.
- [[ZhiJiWang|主机网]] grew from a QQ-group response to Palm blog readers and later expanded across Palm, BlackBerry, Android, and iPhone boards, with Palm and webOS remaining its identity center.
- [[WebOS|webOS]] gave the community a peak and then a platform shock: HP's hardware exit undermined device supply and developer expectations even though users and developers kept trying to sustain the ecosystem.
- The episode treats [[WeChat]] absence as a practical endpoint for webOS in China because a smartphone platform without the central social app could no longer remain viable for ordinary users.
- [[Sspai|少数派]] began partly from carrier demand for mobile-app content and training, then used that revenue to fund a user-facing application-recommendation media product.
- [[AppRecommendationMedia]] was valuable in the early mobile-app boom because users needed trusted filtering, hands-on usage explanation, and combinations of apps and devices rather than only download rankings.
- [[Sspai|少数派]] built an author system early, paid contributors, and later used columns, courses, memberships, brand advertising, product co-creation, and offline space to make community value more concrete.
- [[NicheCommunityValue]] is framed as density rather than smallness: 老麦 treats one million registered users as small beside large platforms but valuable because the users and authors have high creative capacity.
- In the AI era, the episode argues that [[HumanExperienceContentPremium|real human experience and deep content]] become scarcer as cheap generated content dilutes feeds.
- The product-co-creation branch is presented as an extension of community trust: creators, users, brands, product taste, AI capability, and Shenzhen hardware context can turn content relationships into physical products and spaces.
