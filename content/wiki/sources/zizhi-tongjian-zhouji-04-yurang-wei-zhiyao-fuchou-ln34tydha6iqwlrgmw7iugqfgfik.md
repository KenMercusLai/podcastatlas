# 《资治通鉴·周纪》04｜豫让 为智瑶复仇

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode uses [[ZhiYao|智瑶]]'s failure, [[YuRang|豫让]]'s revenge, [[ZhaoXiangzi|赵无恤 / 赵襄子]]'s succession decisions, and [[ZhouWeilieWang|周威烈王]]'s recognition of Han, Zhao, and Wei to connect morality, revenge, and state formation. Its strongest synthesis is that early [[WarringStatesPeriod|战国时期]] politics turned on several thresholds at once: personal virtue could decide whether talent became destructive, retainer loyalty could become lethal self-fashioning, and formal Zhou titles eventually ratified power that the Jin families had already seized.

## Key Claims
- [[SimaGuang|司马光]] explains [[ZhiYao|智瑶]]'s fall through [[TalentVirtueDistinction|才德之分]]: talent without virtue is more dangerous than incompetence because capability magnifies bad intention.
- The episode treats the first two lessons of [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] as programmatic: "礼治名器" defines political order, while "才德之分" defines personnel judgment.
- After the Battle of Jinyang in 453 BCE, Han, Zhao, and Wei divide Zhi territory; the episode treats this as a practical beginning of the [[WarringStatesPeriod|战国时期]] while later entries in the same wiki also preserve 403 BCE recognition and 376 BCE Jin extinction as other thresholds in [[PartitionOfJin|三家分晋]].
- [[ZhaoXiangzi|赵无恤]]'s hatred of [[ZhiYao|智瑶]] is shown through the episode's account of using Zhi Yao's lacquered skull as a drinking vessel, making the revenge story personal as well as political.
- Zhao Wuxu's conquest of [[DaiState|代国]] is framed as ruthless state expansion through kinship deception: he kills his brother-in-law, absorbs Dai, and indirectly drives his own sister to suicide.
- [[YuRang|豫让]]'s first assassination attempt depends on disguise and proximity, but Zhao Wuxu releases him because revenge for a destroyed lord can still look like loyal service.
- Yu Rang's self-mutilation and refusal to serve Zhao before killing him make his revenge a case of [[RetainerReciprocityEthic|patron-recognition reciprocity]], not only tactical assassination.
- The [[Shiji|《史记》]] version emphasizes Yu Rang's distinction between ordinary patronage and being treated as a "国士"; the [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] version supplies the famous "士为知己者死" formula.
- [[LiangQichao|梁启超]]'s 1904 reading in 《中国之武士道》 is used to widen Yu Rang into a comment on how unified autocratic rule could weaken earlier heroic or knightly honor cultures.
- Zhao Wuxu's decision to pass over his own sons and restore [[ZhaoBolu|赵伯鲁]]'s line creates a succession conflict involving [[ZhaoHuanzi|赵桓子]] and [[ZhaoXianzi|赵献子]], but the clan's later restoration of Zhao Xianzi shows the force of a recognized designation.
- [[ZhouWeilieWang|周威烈王]]'s formal appointment of [[WeiWenhou|魏斯 / 魏文侯]], [[ZhaoLiehou|赵籍 / 赵烈侯]], and [[HanJinghou|韩虔 / 韩景侯]] as vassals turns de facto Jin-family power into de jure status through [[ThreeJinVassalRecognition|三晋受封]].

## Key Quotes
> "才胜德" - the episode's shorthand for Sima Guang's judgment on Zhi Yao.

> "君子挟才以为善，小人挟才以为恶" - the principle used to explain why talent needs moral direction.

> "士为知己者死，女为悦己者容" - the Zhanguo Ce formula attached to Yu Rang's reply.
