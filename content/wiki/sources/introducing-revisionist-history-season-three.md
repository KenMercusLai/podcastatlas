# Introducing Revisionist History Season Three

## Summary

This short [[RevisionistHistory|Revisionist History]] trailer has [[MalcolmGladwell]] introduce [[MassDelusion]] as season three's central subject: collective certainty in a false belief that can remain resistant to reminders, evidence, and education. Its montage previews disputed data, poisoned schoolchildren, victims of mass hysteria, a heroic woman, an OSS officer traveling with a famous harmonica player, and comic exchanges about fear of flying.

The preview establishes a question and a tone rather than a demonstrated explanation. Later full episodes develop some of its fragments, but this trailer alone does not identify the cases clearly enough or supply evidence sufficient to decide whether they share one mechanism.

## Key Claims

- Gladwell presents mass delusion as the announced theme of the third season.
- The trailer characterizes delusion as difficult to recognize from within and potentially resistant to evidence or education.
- Its previewed cases span collective illness, institutional judgment, disputed data, personal harm, and historical anecdote.
- Serious subjects are paired with self-deprecating and conversational humor, continuing the tone attributed to the show's first two seasons.
- Season three was announced to premiere on May 17, 2018, with subscription through Apple Podcasts or another podcast platform.
- The montage does not provide enough case detail or evidence to establish a general theory of mass delusion.
