# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1114丨官员集体装穷，和洽劝谏能叫醒曹操吗？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the official-selection effects introduced in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1087-simayi-zhuangbing-ju-caocao-yi-tingshuo-yao-bei-zhua-miao-song-likur-xfjqbk3kr-ew6ftvtwixq|Hanji 1087]]. In the episode's Jian'an 14 setting, officials turn worn clothing, walking, and austere daily objects into visible signs of integrity; [[HeQiaLateHan|和洽]] warns [[CaoCao|曹操]] that a reasonable preference for frugality has become an extreme, exhausting, and easily imitated moral performance.

The episode then moves to Cao Cao's Jian'an 15 talent order and uses Meng Gongchuo and [[GuanZhong|管仲]] to argue for role-specific appointment and practical capacity rather than one undifferentiated image of moral perfection. Its durable synthesis joins two cautions: visible austerity cannot prove integrity, and useful ability does not erase the need to judge motive, office fit, and the risks created by flawed but capable people.

## Key Claims

- The source presents an official culture in which new clothes, good transport, or comfortable living attract suspicion, while worn clothes, walking, and rough utensils become reputational assets.
- [[HeQiaLateHan|和洽]] does not reject frugality; he argues that a sound norm must remain balanced because an extreme standard burdens officials and rewards eccentric or false display.
- The remonstrance adds a corrective layer to [[SelectionDrivenVirtueSignaling|选官驱动的德行表演]]: once the signal becomes legible and cheap to imitate, the selection system may reward performance instead of the underlying quality.
- The episode combines [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s demand for impartial recommendation with [[WangFuzhi|王夫之]]'s warning about conspicuously extreme displays of humility or austerity.
- Cao Cao's Jian'an 15 talent order uses Meng Gongchuo as a role-fit example: a person's suitability depends on the office and task, not on a single status or reputation ranking.
- The host reads “唯才是举” as a practical challenge to Eastern Han recommendation practices that could compress diverse offices into a common moral-reputation standard.
- [[GuanZhong|管仲]] is used as the difficult example: disputed luxury and ritual conduct coexist with major governing capacity, so personal controversy does not by itself settle office fitness.
- The episode's references to processed luxury goods, elite consumption, employment, and “仓廪实而知礼节” connect Guan Zhong to [[LuxuryConsumptionEconomicCirculation|奢侈消费的经济循环]] and material foundations of order.

## Key Quotes

> “俭素为美，中而已矣。” - the remonstrance's balance principle as rendered by the episode.

> “唯才是举，吾得而用之。” - the episode's compact statement of Cao Cao's recruitment rule.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with existing wiki synthesis is recorded. The source strengthens Hanji 1087's account of performative austerity while adding He Qia's explicit warning that the norm has become counterproductive.
- The episode creates a qualification rather than a flat reversal inside [[TalentVirtueDistinction|才德之分]]: urgent or specialized roles can justify using imperfect people, but ability remains an amplifier whose motive, scope, and risks still require control.
- Source-scope caution: the scale and sincerity of official “pretended poverty,” the precise wording and dating of the remonstrance and talent order, the account of Eastern Han recommendation practice, and the causal claim that the order was an institutional innovation require broader textual verification.
- Source-scope caution: the Meng Gongchuo analogy, Guan Zhong's household and ritual details, the economic reading of luxury consumption, exact dialogue, and the host's modern management analogies should not be treated as complete historical proof.
