# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》302-1｜西汉叔侄内斗，结果坑爹也坑爷

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies an earlier layer of [[LiuAn|淮南王刘安]]'s rebellion planning. Liu An reportedly welcomes reports that [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] lacks an heir or governs badly and becomes angry at contrary news, then imprisons the parents of an adviser rendered as “吴皮” until the adviser will discuss rebellion. The adviser temporarily deters him by comparing Liu An's weak position with [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s Qin-collapse opportunity and the failed Rebellion of the Seven States.

The episode then shifts from political misjudgment to household fracture. Liu An, his queen, and crown prince [[LiuQianHuainanCrownPrinceWesternHan|刘迁]] allegedly marginalize the elder non-heir son [[LiuBuhaiWesternHan|刘不亥]]; Liu Buhai's son resents the crown prince, discusses removing him, and is seized and tortured after the plan leaks. The durable synthesis is that selective information, coercion of dissent, and unequal household treatment weaken both strategic judgment and family containment before the later Huainan case becomes public.

## Key Claims

- Liu An and his retainers reportedly study maps, routes, and operational details after the [[LeiBeiWesternHan|雷被]] affair strengthens his resolve to rebel.
- Reports that Han Wudi has no son or that court government is corrupt please Liu An, while reports of an imperial son or competent government anger him; the episode interprets this as a self-reinforcing imperial fantasy.
- An adviser transcribed as “吴皮” rejects rebellion as ruinous, after which Liu An imprisons the adviser's parents until he agrees to offer counsel.
- The adviser contrasts Liu Bang's success amid Qin collapse with the defeat of [[LiuBiWuKing|吴王刘濞]] and the Seven States, arguing that Liu An has much less force and faces a much more stable realm.
- Liu An reportedly cries, stands, and temporarily abandons rebellion after hearing that he would lose his kingship and die before his followers.
- Liu An, his queen, and Liu Qian are said to neglect or demean [[LiuBuhaiWesternHan|刘不亥]], while Liu Buhai himself endures the treatment.
- Liu Buhai's son turns resentment at his father's treatment into a plan against his crown-prince uncle; the plan leaks before action, and the crown prince has him arrested and tortured.

## Key Quotes

> “亡国之言” - the adviser's characterization of the proposed rising.

> “军队不及吴楚联军十分之一” - the episode's comparative estimate of Liu An's weakness.

## Contradictions

- The source repeatedly calls the remonstrating adviser “吴皮,” whereas the adjacent Huainan notes identify the pessimistic adviser and later confessor as [[WuBeiWesternHan|伍被]]. The sequence strongly suggests a transcription variant, but this ingest does not treat the spelling as independently verified.
- The final segment calls Liu An's elder son both 刘不亥 and 刘不怀, and appears to call both the crown prince and his nephew 刘谦. The wiki normalizes only the clearly identified elder son to [[LiuBuhaiWesternHan|刘不亥]] and leaves the nephew unnamed rather than merging him with Liu Qian or creating a speculative identity.
- No settled contradiction with the later Hanji 303 notes is adopted. This episode says counsel temporarily stops Liu An, while those later sources show renewed planning, failed mobilization, confession, and destruction.
- The source is a compact structured summary rather than a primary-text dossier. Private reactions, exact dialogue, troop ratios, the duration of Liu An's retreat from rebellion, household motives, and the nephew's plan and torture remain episode-attributed.
