# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》669：算命先生靠忽悠也能当上皇帝？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Gengshi 2 / 24 CE immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-670-juejing-qiusheng-liuxiu-xianru-shengsi-da-weiji-ltz1nciofev4cqjkod-p8mmwovhe|Hanji 670]]. It shows [[WangLangHebeiClaimant|王郎]] turning a false Liu Ziyu / Emperor Cheng lineage claim into real Hebei pressure, strong enough that market crowds treat Wang Lang as more credible than [[LiuXiu|刘秀]] and Liu Xiu's party is pushed into flight.

The same source expands the weak-center background around [[LiuXuanGengshiEmperor|更始帝刘玄]]: his Chang'an entry and amnesty briefly stabilize the capital, but court awkwardness, indiscriminate enfeoffment, Zhao Meng's favoritism, killing of a remonstrating lang officer, and overlapping appointments alienate Guanzhong. [[GengYanEasternHan|耿弇]] emerges as an early political judge by rejecting Wang Lang's claim, seeking Liu Xiu at Lunu, and advising the northern route toward Shanggu/Yuyang resources; Liu Xiu's Jizhong escape and Raoyang inn bluff add a concrete early case of [[CrisisComposureCoverStory|crisis composure]].

## Key Claims

- Wang Lang manufactures legitimacy by claiming to be Liu Ziyu, Emperor Cheng's son, and the claim becomes credible enough in Hebei to endanger Liu Xiu directly.
- Liu Xuan's arrival at Chang'an still carries restored-Han stabilizing power through amnesty, preserved urban infrastructure, and market recovery.
- Liu Xuan's court behavior then undermines that title: personal awkwardness, palace drinking, Zhao Meng's power, arbitrary killings, and overlapping offices make Guanzhong turn against the Gengshi regime.
- Geng Yan's refusal to join Wang Lang shows that early alignment is a judgment about legitimacy and durability, not only a calculation of immediate force.
- Geng Yan's northern-route advice gives Liu Xiu a practical alternative to retreating south and makes Shanggu/Yuyang cavalry resources strategically visible.
- Liu Xiu's flight from Jizhong and hardship through cold, hunger, and closed gates make Wang Lang's claim a bodily survival crisis rather than only a propaganda problem.
- The Raoyang inn scene shows Liu Xiu switching from fear to role-consistent stillness: once flight is impossible, he survives the "Handan general" test by staying inside the cover identity.

## Key Quotes

> "北道主人" - Liu Xiu's phrase for Geng Yan after the northern-route advice.

> "邯郸将军到了" - the Raoyang inn officials' test shout.

> "算命先生靠忽悠也能当上皇帝" - the title-level frame for Wang Lang's fraudulent claimant story.
