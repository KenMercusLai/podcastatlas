# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》305-1｜因出轨离婚的她，为何要谋害亲哥？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens a Western Han 衡山 royal-house conflict around [[LiuCiHengshanKingWesternHan|衡山王刘赐]], his successor, and rival maternal branches. After the death of the first queen, the new queen [[XuLaiHengshanQueen|徐来]] and [[JueJiHengshan|厥姬]] compete for security and influence; 厥姬 turns [[LiuShuangHengshanCrownPrince|太子刘爽]] against 徐来, while 徐来 later recruits the crown prince's siblings [[LiuXiaoHengshanPrince|刘孝]] and [[LiuWucaiHengshanPrincess|刘无采]] into a temporary anti-heir coalition.

The durable synthesis is a family-political feedback loop rather than a complete account of the later case. Maternal-branch insecurity, heir displacement, rumor, sexual shaming, sibling grievance, and personal violence reinforce one another, but the partners opposing Liu爽 want different outcomes: 徐来 wants her own son 刘广 advanced, 刘孝 wants the succession for himself, and 刘无采 primarily wants freedom from her brother's control.

## Key Claims

- The episode frames 刘赐's contemplated resistance to the Han court as structurally weakened by his inability to judge either external power or disorder inside his household.
- After first queen 乘舒 dies and 徐来 becomes queen, 厥姬 alleges that 徐来 used a southern maid's sorcery to kill 乘舒, intensifying 刘爽's hostility toward his stepmother.
- 刘爽 wounds 徐来的 brother during a drinking encounter, converting succession suspicion into reciprocal personal violence.
- 徐来 repeatedly damages the crown prince's standing with 刘赐 and seeks to replace him with her own son 刘广.
- 刘无采, resentful after 刘爽 condemns her sexual conduct, and 刘孝, who wants the heirship, join 徐来 in denouncing the crown prince.
- The anti-Liu爽 alignment is tactical rather than durable because its members share an immediate target but not a common post-removal settlement.
