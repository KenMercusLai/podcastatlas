## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode closes [[ChenSheng|陈胜]]'s active rule after [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-2-lishishang-hejiu-neng-wu-duo-da-shi-zhuan-gei-jiugui-laogong-kan-zhe-ji-lvzxpoxclwdrr66bwejd-bga8dp|Qinji 129-2]] by showing the [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]] failing at external command, central authority, and personal trust at the same time. [[QinJiaRebel|秦嘉]] kills Chen Sheng's monitor [[WuPingJunPan|武平君畔]], [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] presses toward Chen County with help from [[SimaXin|司马欣]] and [[DongYiQin|董义]], and Chen Sheng is finally killed by his driver [[ZhuangGu|庄谷]]. The episode's synthesis is that Chen Sheng opened the anti-Qin wave but, after kingship, alienated old friends, generals, and subjects through arrogance, suspicion, and [[PunitiveInspectionLoyaltyTrap|苛察忠诚陷阱]], leaving his historical meaning larger than his personal ending.

## Key Claims
- After [[LiLiangZhaoGeneral|李良]] kills [[WuChen|武臣]], [[ChenYu|陈馀]] uses his Zhao networks to learn the news early and escapes [[Handan|邯郸]] with [[ZhangEr|张耳]].
- [[QinJiaRebel|秦嘉]] and [[ZhuJishi|朱吉石]] raise troops and surround the Donghai commandery governor at Tan, but Qin Jia refuses to obey [[WuPingJunPan|武平君畔]], the monitor Chen Sheng sends to supervise the army.
- Qin Jia calls Wu Ping Jun Pan a young appointee rather than a true field commander, then falsely claims Chen Sheng's order and kills him, showing that Zhang Chu's central command can no longer reliably bind local rebel armies.
- [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] sends [[SimaXin|司马欣]] and [[DongYiQin|董义]] to assist [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] against the rebels.
- Zhang Han has already defeated Wu Feng and killed [[CaiCi|蔡赐]], then reaches the area below Chen County, forcing Chen Sheng to supervise the defense personally.
- [[ZhangHeZhangChu|张鹤]] holds Chen Sheng's remaining base force after the main armies have been consumed under Zhou Wen and Tian Zang; his force is defeated west of Chen County and Zhang He dies.
- Chen Sheng moves toward Ruyin to raise another force, but on the return through Xiachengfu he is killed by his driver [[ZhuangGu|庄谷]], who then surrenders to Qin.
- The host explains Chen Sheng's death through [[RebelFounderAlienation|起义首领人心疏离]]: after becoming king, Chen Sheng treats even his father-in-law arrogantly, kills old friends who mention his past, and makes intimacy around him dangerous.
- Chen Sheng appoints [[ZhuFangZhangChu|诸坊]] and [[HuWuZhangChu|胡武]] to inspect officials; their harsh, extrajudicial punishments turn severity toward generals and people into proof of loyalty.
- [[LuChenQinRebel|吕臣]], a former close attendant and later general, raises the Cangtou Army from Xinyang, retakes Chen County, kills Zhuang Gu, and reburies Chen Sheng at Dang County as Yin Wang.
- The host argues that Chen Sheng's own conduct was often ugly, but his role as the first anti-Qin spark remains historically decisive; [[LiuBang|刘邦]] later assigns thirty households to guard and maintain Chen Sheng's tomb rites.

## Key Quotes
> "王侯将相，宁有种乎" - the founding slogan set against Chen Sheng's death by his own driver.

> "再也没有人敢和陈胜亲近" - the episode's summary of how killing old friends destroyed intimacy around Chen Sheng.
