# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1007丨从神童到覆亡：孔融真实面目曝光！

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate prelude before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1008-ruhe-pingjia-caocao-de-tuntianzhi-ls8wvh9ox-pcjmt0z8w82opbha-k|Hanji 1008]]. It opens with [[WhiteBearEffect|白熊效应]] and the host's "转移、替代、升华" response, then turns to [[KongRong|孔融]] in 196 CE as a case of [[PrestigeCapacityGap|名望能力落差]]: lineage, childhood fame, learning, and literary standing do not become late-Han governing or military capacity. The core historical chain is Kong Rong's isolated rule in [[BeihaiCommandery|北海郡]], his killing of [[ZuoChengzu|左承祖]] after a realistic alliance recommendation, [[LiuYixun|刘义逊]]'s departure, and [[YuanTan|袁谭]]'s eventual capture of Beihai.

## Key Claims
- The opening psychology segment presents [[WhiteBearEffect|白熊效应]] as a rebound pattern: trying to force painful thoughts, cravings, or regrets out of mind can make them return more strongly.
- The host's practical response is not suppression but "转移、替代、升华": redirect attention toward more nourishing or constructive objects rather than repeatedly commanding oneself not to feel pain.
- The historical section places [[KongRong|孔融]] in 196 CE, the first year of Jian'an, as [[BeihaiCommandery|北海太守]] with a large ambition to settle disorder but little concrete accomplishment.
- Kong Rong's cultural status is presented through his descent from [[Confucius|孔子]], the "四岁让梨" childhood story, and his later standing among the Jian'an literary circle, but the episode argues that literature and ritual fame do not automatically supply wartime administration.
- Kong Rong honors [[ZhengXuan|郑玄]] by renaming his village "郑公乡", showing respect for high Confucian prestige.
- Kong Rong hosts [[ZuoChengzu|左承祖]] and [[LiuYixun|刘义逊]] as respected figures but does not use them as serious strategic interlocutors.
- When Yellow Turban forces pressure Beihai and Kong Rong lacks troops and grain, Zuo Chengzu advises him to rely on a stronger neighboring power such as [[YuanShao|袁绍]], [[CaoCao|曹操]], or [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]].
- Kong Rong refuses that advice and kills Zuo Chengzu; the episode treats this as the decisive sign that he lacks tolerance for corrective speech.
- Liu Yixun leaves after seeing Kong Rong's lack of forbearance, so the failed advice channel also becomes a talent-loss event.
- [[YuanTan|袁谭]] attacks Beihai from spring into summer; Kong Rong's forces shrink to a few hundred, and the source emphasizes the contrast between reading calmly under arrows and fleeing when the city falls at night.
- Kong Rong escapes toward Dongshan, while his wife and children are captured by Yuan Tan; [[CaoCao|曹操]] later summons Kong Rong to court because of their old relationship.
- The closing Yuan Tan branch says his early wins against [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]]'s Qingzhou appointee Tian Kai and against Kong Rong bring wide reputation, but pride, poor companions, and indulgence soon damage that reputation.

## Key Quotes
> "转移、替代、升华" - the host's compact response to the thought-suppression problem.

> "高谈阔论" - the source's shorthand for Kong Rong's gap between talk and execution.
