# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》263｜为了升官，人可以多卑微？（2）

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode fills the transition between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-263-weile-shengguan-ren-keyi-duo-beiwei-1-lgewf4qfarqfjfgp50nss2vf_7y|Hanji 263 part 1]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-264-tianfen-hunyan-xianchang-ruhe-chengle-guanfu-de-duantoutai-lux-kqkfsqpjldtocrk65j5pvl8p|Hanji 264]]. It says [[TianFenWesternHan|田蚡]] follows the more accomplished [[DouYingWesternHan|窦婴]] while [[EmpressDowagerDouWesternHan|窦太后]] remains dominant, but a Confucian-policy confrontation removes both men from office and leaves them with unequal protection: Tian Fen retains his sister [[EmpressDowagerWangWesternHan|王太后]], while Dou Ying's relationship with Dowager Dou has broken down.

After Dowager Dou's death, the episode presents Tian Fen's return as chancellor and Dou Ying's exclusion as a decisive power reversal. The displaced Dou Ying bonds with the similarly demoted [[GuanFuWesternHan|灌夫]]; a forgotten visit, a strained drinking scene, and Tian Fen's request for southern land then convert changed rank into personal grievance and prepare the wedding conflict narrated in Hanji 264. Exact dialogue, motives, appointment causation, and the land transaction remain source-scoped.

## Key Claims

- While Dowager Dou remains the senior court authority, Tian Fen follows Dou Ying in promoting Confucian government because Dou has greater military, administrative, and patronage standing.
- A proposal that Han Wudi stop routinely consulting the grand empress dowager is presented as the trigger for Dowager Dou to remove Dou Ying, Tian Fen, and Wang Zang from office.
- Tian Fen remains able to discuss politics through Empress Dowager Wang, whereas Dou Ying's earlier opposition to making the Liang king heir and the new policy dispute leave him estranged from Dowager Dou.
- After Dowager Dou dies, Wang's stronger position helps Tian Fen return as chancellor; the episode says he promotes retainers aggressively while Dou Ying remains excluded and resentful.
- Dou Ying and Guan Fu become close as displaced officeholders, and Guan Fu offers to reconnect Dou with powerful people.
- Tian Fen's casual promise to visit Dou Ying produces an elaborate overnight reception, but his late arrival and refusal to join Guan Fu's dance make the changed hierarchy visible and deepen resentment.
- Tian Fen's request for a large tract south of the capital turns a status grievance into a material dispute; Dou Ying refuses, Guan Fu denounces the request, and Tian Fen later invokes his past service to Dou's family.

## Key Quotes

> “皇帝应该独立掌权，不应事事询问太皇太后。” - the episode's summary of the proposal that provokes the senior dowager's response.

> “服侍窦婴从来没有不听他的。” - Tian Fen's episode-attributed retrospective on the earlier relationship after the land refusal.
