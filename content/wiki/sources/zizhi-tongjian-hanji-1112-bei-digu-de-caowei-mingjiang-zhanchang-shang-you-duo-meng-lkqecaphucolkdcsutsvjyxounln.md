# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1112丨被低估的曹魏名将，战场上有多猛？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode joins the two major Jian'an 14 fronts after [[RedCliffsBattle|赤壁之战]]. At [[HefeiLateHan|合肥]], [[JiangJiLateHan|蒋济]] makes a small and delayed relief force appear to be forty thousand troops and induces [[SunQuan|孙权]] to abandon a long siege; at [[JianglingLateHan|江陵]], [[CaoRen|曹仁]] leads a small mounted group through Wu troops to extract [[NiuJinLateHan|牛金]] and other surrounded soldiers.

The source therefore adds two different ways to preserve a pressured position. Jiang Ji changes the enemy's estimate by letting apparently intercepted messages authenticate one another, while Cao Ren accepts immediate personal danger to restore trapped manpower and defender morale. The closing question—whether Cao Ren advanced through kinship or ability—is not resolved as a binary: Cao-clan trust helps explain access, while this episode supplies a concrete battlefield-performance case.

## Key Claims

- After Red Cliffs, Cao Cao treats the Jiang-Huai line as a renewed defensive and logistical problem, rebuilding naval capacity and using Hefei as a forward strongpoint.
- Sun Quan's long siege of Hefei coincides with rain-damaged walls and a Cao relief force reportedly reduced or delayed by epidemic disease.
- Jiang Ji proposes announcing that Zhang Xi has arrived with forty thousand infantry and cavalry, then sends multiple messages so that deliberate interception makes the false reinforcement story credible.
- Sun Quan withdraws and burns siege equipment after accepting the reinforcement report; the episode attributes Hefei's preservation to deception rather than to the arrival of the advertised force.
- Cao Cao later reaches Hefei, repairs the Shaobei irrigation works, and promotes tuntian, linking frontier defense to water and grain infrastructure.
- Zhang Liao defeats Chen Lan and Mei Cheng, after which Cao Cao assigns Zhang Liao, Yue Jin, and Li Dian with a reported seven thousand troops to Hefei.
- After Gan Ning takes Yiling, Zhou Yu and Cheng Pu defeat the responding Cao force and intensify pressure on Jiangling.
- Cao Ren sends three hundred soldiers under Niu Jin against a much larger Wu vanguard; when they are surrounded, he rides out with only a small mounted group, extracts Niu Jin, returns for other trapped soldiers, and comes back alive.
- Chen Jiao's reported exclamation and the defenders' renewed confidence make the action evidence of command signaling as well as personal courage.
- The episode's opening marriage advice is contemporary commentary rather than evidence for the Jian'an campaign and is not generalized into a historical claim.

## Key Quotes

> “将军真天人也。” — the reaction attributed to Chen Jiao after Cao Ren returns from the rescue.

> “四万援军将至。” — the false reinforcement claim at the center of Jiang Ji's deception.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is recorded. The source fills the operational gap between Hanji 1108-1109's Yiling action and Hanji 1113's account of Cao Ren's eventual withdrawal from Jiangling.
- Outcome qualification: Cao Ren's successful rescue does not reverse the longer campaign outcome; Jiangling is later abandoned after a prolonged defense.
- Causal qualification: kinship and demonstrated ability are not mutually exclusive explanations for Cao Ren's trust and advancement.
- Source-scope caution: force sizes, siege duration, wall damage, epidemic effects, messenger mechanics, Shaobei history, casualty figures, exact dialogue, the number of riders, and private motives require comparison with underlying historical texts.
