# 《资治通鉴·周纪》66丨中国历史上哪些帝王是被活活饿死的（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s twentieth year, 295 BCE, and shifts the [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王 / 赵主父]] branch from bold reform into succession breakdown. After Zhao destroys [[ZhongshanState|中山国]], Zhao Wuling Wang compensates the elder son [[ZhaoZhang|赵章]] with a Dai-region power base and [[TianBuli|田不李]], while the younger ruler [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] holds the formal court with [[FeiYi|肥义]] and allied officials. The source's core contribution is [[PartialAbdicationPowerSplit|退位留权式权力分裂]]: the "主父" arrangement leaves enough retained authority, family grievance, and command ambiguity to turn a planned Shaqiu trip into the prelude to [[ShaqiuCrisis|沙丘之变]].

## Key Claims
- In 295 BCE, [[SimaCuo|司马错]] attacks [[WeiState|魏国]] as Qin's guowei, while Qin also removes [[LouHuan|楼缓]] as chancellor and returns [[WeiRan|魏冉]] to that office.
- [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]] destroys [[ZhongshanState|中山国]] with [[QiState|齐国]] and [[YanState|燕国]] support, then moves the Zhongshan ruler to Fushi.
- Zhao Wuling Wang gives [[ZhaoZhang|赵章]] the title Anyang Jun and places him in the Dai region with [[TianBuli|田不李]] as a chancellor-like adviser, creating what the host calls a "state within a state."
- [[LiDui|李队]] warns [[FeiYi|肥义]] that Zhao Zhang's strength, arrogance, followers, and resentment combine dangerously with Tian Buli's violent temperament.
- Li Dui advises Fei Yi to feign illness and hand affairs to [[ZhaoChengWarringStates|赵成]], but Fei Yi refuses because Zhao Wuling Wang entrusted [[ZhaoHe|赵何]] to him.
- Fei Yi prepares for forged commands: if someone claims the 主父 summons the king, he wants to inspect the situation first before Zhao He obeys.
- Zhao Wuling Wang sees the elder Zhao Zhang bowing to the younger Zhao He and becomes emotionally unsettled, then considers dividing Zhao so Zhao Zhang can also become king in Dai.
- Zhao He rejects the plan to split Zhao, leaving Zhao Wuling Wang politically isolated despite his retained "主父" status.
- The host frames Zhao Wuling Wang as an early 太上皇-like figure, but emphasizes that the office does not work as designed because formal royal authority and the ministerial center gather around Zhao He.
- Zhao Wuling Wang chooses Shaqiu because Handan is too controlled by Zhao He's side; the episode ends with father and sons arriving there before the starvation outcome is narrated.

## Key Quotes
> "国中之国" - the host's description of Zhao Zhang's Dai-region setup.

> "董事长" and "总经理" - the modern analogy for Zhao Wuling Wang's intended role split with Zhao He.

> "一场大戏即将上演" - the cliffhanger as the party reaches Shaqiu.
