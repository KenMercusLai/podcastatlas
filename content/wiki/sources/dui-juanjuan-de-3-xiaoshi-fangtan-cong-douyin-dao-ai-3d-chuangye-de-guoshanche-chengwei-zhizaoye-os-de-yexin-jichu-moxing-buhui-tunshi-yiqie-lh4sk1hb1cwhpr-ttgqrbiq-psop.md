# 对卷卷的3小时访谈：从抖音到 AI 3D、制造业 OS 与创业修行

## Summary
This [[ShizilukouCrossing|十字路口Crossing]] episode has [[Koji]] interviewing [[RenLifeng|任立峰 / 卷卷]], founder and CEO of [[ShumeiWanwu|树美万物]], about moving from [[ByteDance]] and early [[Douyin]] product work into AI-generated 3D for physical manufacturing. The episode frames [[AI3DManufacturingPipeline|AI 3D manufacturing pipelines]] as a stack of model, data, post-processing, file formats, materials, equipment, production scheduling, and delivery rather than a pure asset-generation demo. It also uses Ren's founder reflections to connect [[ProductVariableJudgment|product variable judgment]], [[MakerOperatingSystem|Maker operating systems]], and [[PhysicalManufacturingApplicationMoat|physical manufacturing application moats]] to the wider debate over whether foundation models will absorb application-layer value.

## Key Claims
- Ren Lifeng says Douyin's original opportunity came from mobile-network, smartphone, camera, GPU, and short-video expression changes, while Shumei Wanwu's opportunity comes from AI, 3D modality, desktop manufacturing equipment, and China's manufacturing supply chain.
- [[ShumeiWanwu|Shumei Wanwu]] is building AI-generated 3D models and downstream products such as [[Hi3D]] and [[Zaohaowu|造好物]] to lower the barrier from image or concept to manufacturable physical object.
- The episode argues that production-grade AI 3D requires geometry and texture consistency, post-processing, semantic segmentation, splitting, connector placement, auto-arrangement, material parameters, and export into equipment-readable files.
- Ren says base multimodal models may satisfy some game and film 3D needs, but physical manufacturing still needs explicit 3D, format, process, material, equipment, and delivery closure.
- The company's current consumer demand centers on figures, pets, toys, family images, creative characters, and expanding flexible-fabric work such as crochet, embroidery, felt, plush toys, and pattern making.
- The business is validated not only by visual model demos but by high-concurrency, nonstandard personalized orders, with the source reporting a T+7 shipping-information target and 90%-95% on-time rate.
- Ren frames the long-term goal as first becoming a Maker-industry OS and later exploring a broader manufacturing OS that connects multiple physical-production categories.

## Key Quotes
> "雕刻空间" — Ren's contrast between Douyin as a tool for sculpting time and AI 3D as a way to help users shape physical space.

> "一站式 AI3D" — his description of the goal from model acquisition to manufacturing landing.

> "事烦心不烦" — the management state Ren attributes to Zhang Yiming and treats as a founder lesson.

## Contradictions
