# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》598丨古代朝堂的斗争有多残酷？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills a late-[[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] court conflict before the Yuanshou 2 / 1 BCE succession crisis. It follows [[LiangXiangWesternHan|梁相]] questioning flaws in the Dongping king Liu Yun rebellion case, Aidi reading that procedural caution as disloyal delay, and [[WangJiaWesternHan|王嘉]] later recommending Liang Xiang's group as usable officials.

The durable synthesis is that the late-Aidi court turned correction into personal threat. Wang Jia's effort to preserve capable officials and his later refusal to endorse the [[DongXian|董贤]] favor package leave him exposed; Aidi reopens the Liang Xiang matter, [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]] and others recommend sending him to the Tingwei prison, and Wang Jia rejects the expected poison-suicide script before submitting to arrest.

## Key Claims

- Liang Xiang finds many contradictions in the Liu Yun rebellion case and asks to bring the prisoners to Chang'an for review by senior court officials.
- Han Aidi interprets the review request as an attempt to delay execution past winter rather than as ordinary legal caution.
- Wang Jia later recommends Liang Xiang's group again, arguing that the ruler should weigh officials' merit more than their faults.
- Aidi treats that recommendation as a challenge to imperial judgment, especially after Wang Jia has already offended him over Dong Xian's fief-enlargement edict.
- Kong Guang and other officials recommend sending Wang Jia to the Tingwei prison, which the episode treats as a near-death signal under court convention.
- Some officials argue Wang Jia's inconsistency warrants removal or punishment but not humiliating lethal treatment of a senior minister.
- Wang Jia refuses the poison prepared by his staff, saying a Three Excellencies minister should die publicly under law if he must die, not privately like a child.

## Key Quotes

> "请君赴死" - the host's shorthand for the Tingwei summons.

> "即使要死，也应在都市受刑示众" - Wang Jia's stated reason for refusing private poison.
