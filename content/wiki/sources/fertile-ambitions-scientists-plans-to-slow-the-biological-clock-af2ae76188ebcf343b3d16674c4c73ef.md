# Fertile ambitions: scientists’ plans to slow the biological clock

## Summary
This [[EconomistPodcasts]] episode connects three kinds of preservation. Its science report describes [[OvaLabs]] and [[EuploidBiotechnologies]] pursuing preclinical [[OocyteChromosomeStabilization|oocyte chromosome-stabilization]] treatments for IVF; its Belarus report follows [[AlexanderLukashenko]]'s post-2020 repression of national culture alongside growing dependence on [[Russia]]; and its food-history report traces [[Biltong]] from Indigenous preservation and colonial trade through South African everyday life and global adaptation.

## Key Claims
- Egg supply and egg quality are distinct: IVF can retrieve more eggs but does not currently reverse age-related chromosome-handling deterioration.
- Cohesin holds paired chromosomes together, while shugoshin-1 protects cohesin during the division that should leave an egg with 23 chromosomes.
- [[OvaLabs]]' proposed Embryo Protect One imitates shugoshin-1's protective role, whereas [[EuploidBiotechnologies]]' Liventa is described as diverting the enzyme that degrades it.
- [[OocyteChromosomeStabilization]] aims to increase chromosomally normal eggs before embryo formation, but neither startup had entered clinical trials in the supplied summary.
- After the disputed 2020 election, [[AlexanderLukashenko]]'s government associated Belarusian language, clothing, books, performance, and the white-red-white flag with opposition.
- [[BelarusianCulturalSuppression]] combines removal of national expression from public life with stronger Russian narratives in theatres, museums, concerts, textbooks, and youth organizations.
- Culture has moved underground and into exile as people inside [[Belarus]] discard banned books while communities abroad document repression and sustain language and identity.
- [[Biltong]] combines Indigenous Khoisan meat drying with vinegar and spices linked to Dutch settlement and Asian trade, complicating any single-community origin story.
- Biltong remains ordinary South African food across petrol stations, rugby, township butcheries, homes, and wineries while migration and protein demand carry it abroad.
- Slow air drying, seasoning, historical memory, and national attachment make biltong culturally distinct from American jerky even where both preserve meat.
