# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》334-2｜为什么老板更喜欢做表面文章的人？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode revisits [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s anger over an unrepaired road to [[GanquanPalace|甘泉宫]] under [[YiZongWesternHan|义纵]] and carries the story forward to Yi's arrest after detaining personnel involved in告缗 enforcement. Its durable contribution is interpretive rather than new chronology: in a power-unequal relationship, a visible operational lapse may be generalized into a judgment about attention, respect, loyalty, or long-term reliability.

The host translates the incident into workplace advice about [[ExecutionDetailAttitudeAttribution|执行细节的态度归因]] and [[UpwardManagement|upward management]]. Work a superior directly sees or feels can carry signal value beyond its practical importance, but the episode's account of Wudi's inner chain of suspicion, its universal claims about senior leaders, and its suggestion that unexpected praise may conceal criticism remain source-scoped interpretations rather than verified rules.

## Key Claims

- The episode repeats that the damaged Ganquan route leads Wudi to infer that Yi Zong did not expect his recovery or no longer took him seriously.
- It uses an employee who is seen checking a phone during a brief pause to illustrate how one visible snapshot can be overgeneralized into a judgment about habitual idleness.
- The host argues that work directly visible or perceptible to a leader has both practical and signaling value, because completion may be read as evidence of respect for the leader and organization.
- Tasks that seem trivial to the executor may matter to the person who assigned them, so priority judgments should include stakeholder expectations rather than only the executor's view of intrinsic value.
- The episode says Yi later arrests people carrying out告缗 enforcement and is arrested for obstructing an imperial order, but it does not supply additional procedural detail beyond the neighboring account in Hanji 335.
- The host suggests that sudden praise can sometimes be an indirect corrective signal when a superior avoids explicit criticism; the supplied text provides an anecdotal consultation frame, not evidence that praise should generally be decoded this way.
- The conclusion presents Yi's fall as the accumulation of small visible failures and deeper imperial dissatisfaction, while leaving Yi's actual thoughts and the precise causal role of the road incident unproven.

## Key Quotes

> “领导能看到的，能够感受到的，就叫做表面观。” - the host's label for visible work as a basis of managerial judgment.
