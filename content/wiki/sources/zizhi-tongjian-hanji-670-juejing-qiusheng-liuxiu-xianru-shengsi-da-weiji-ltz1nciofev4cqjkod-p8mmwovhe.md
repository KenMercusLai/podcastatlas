# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》670：绝境求生，刘秀陷入生死大危机

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Gengshi 2 / 24 CE immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-671-nide-lingdao-shifou-zhide-zhuisui-jinlai-duibi-xia-lrjwus-s14y1x2y4r3f-dvri1nv|Hanji 671]]. It follows [[LiuXiu|刘秀]] after [[WangLangHebeiClaimant|王郎]]'s pursuit turns the Hebei mission into a survival crisis: the Hutuo River crossing, storm shelter at Nangong, and arrival at Xindu show Liu Xiu moving from flight into a defensible base.

The durable synthesis is that Liu Xiu's counteroffensive begins before he looks secure. [[RenGuangEasternHan|任光]] and [[PiTongEasternHan|邳彤]] keep Xindu from joining Wang Lang, Pi Tong urges Liu Xiu to remain in Hebei rather than retreat to Chang'an, Ren Guang turns proclamations and torch displays into perceived strength, [[LiuZhiChangchengEasternHan|刘植]] and [[GengChunEasternHan|耿纯]] add committed local force, and [[JiZunEasternHan|祭遵]]'s strict punishment of Liu Xiu's own household servant makes military law credible only after Liu Xiu accepts correction.

## Key Claims

- Wang Lang's pursuit makes Liu Xiu's Hebei mission a bodily survival problem before it becomes a conquest story.
- The Hutuo River crossing, Nangong shelter, and white-clad elder's Xindu direction mark the episode's transition from flight to search for a base.
- Xindu matters because most surrounding commanderies have submitted to Wang Lang, while Ren Guang and Pi Tong remain outside his camp.
- Pi Tong's advice changes the strategic question from safe return to Chang'an into staying in Hebei to attack Wang Lang while his claim is still unstable.
- Liu Xiu's side compensates for weak troop numbers through recruitment, appointments, proclamations, and torch displays that make county officials believe a large army has arrived.
- Liu Zhi, Geng Chun, and other local actors turn Xindu from refuge into expanding armed support; Geng Chun's house-burning order makes commitment irreversible for his kin group.
- Liu Yang, the Zhending king, is drawn away from Wang Lang by persuasion and a marriage alliance with Guo Shengtong.
- Jia Fu and Chen Jun arrive through Liu Jia's recommendation, adding a talent-transfer layer before their later Guangwu roles.
- Ji Zun's execution of a young Liu-household servant tests whether Liu Xiu's discipline order reaches his own people; Liu Xiu first reacts with anger, then accepts the legal signal and promotes Ji Zun.
