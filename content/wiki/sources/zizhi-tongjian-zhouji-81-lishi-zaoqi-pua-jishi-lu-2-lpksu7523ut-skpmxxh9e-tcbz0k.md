# 《资治通鉴·周纪》81丨历史早期PUA记实录（2）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode closes the [[TianDan|田单]] danger branch opened in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-81-lishi-zaoqi-pua-jishi-lu-1-ljxbn4gznuohegzyee0-tudi9z-y|Zhouji 81 part 1]]. [[DiaoBo|刁伯]] directly rebukes [[QiXiangWang|齐襄王]] for dishonoring the minister who restored [[QiState|齐国]], after which the king kills the nine favored ministers and rewards Tian Dan. The episode then uses [[LuZhonglian|鲁仲连]]'s diagnosis of Tian Dan's failed attack on Di to show that battlefield resolve can decay not only through comfort, but also through a high-merit minister's need to appear harmless under ruler suspicion.

## Key Claims
- [[DiaoBo|刁伯]] returns from [[ChuState|楚国]], attends Qi Xiang Wang's reception banquet, and warns that casually summoning Tian Dan by name is politically dangerous.
- Diao Bo contrasts Qi Xiang Wang with rulers who honored great helpers, including [[JiangTaigong|姜太公]] under the Zhou founding memory and [[GuanZhong|管仲]] under [[QiHuanGong|齐桓公]].
- He reminds Qi Xiang Wang that Tian Dan held [[JimoWarringStates|即墨]] with limited forces, defeated [[YanState|燕国]], recovered Qi territory, and still brought the king and queen back instead of making himself ruler.
- Qi Xiang Wang responds by killing the nine favored ministers who had separated him from Tian Dan, exiling their families, and adding ten thousand households at Ye to Tian Dan's Anping Jun title.
- The episode argues that the king's suspicion is not explained only by court slander; it also comes from the insecurity of a restored ruler who knows his throne depends on Tian Dan.
- A later notice says Zhao Xiaocheng Wang trades three cities to Qi so Tian Dan can serve as Zhao chancellor and be enfeoffed as Douping Jun; this is kept source-scoped because the episode does not develop the Zhao branch.
- The host pairs [[LeYi|乐毅]] and Tian Dan as rare restoration-level talents whose later use is blocked by ruler fear and court darkness.
- [[LuZhonglian|鲁仲连]] appears as a Jixia-associated strategist who refuses office but sees that Tian Dan will not quickly take Di.
- Lu Zhonglian explains that Tian Dan once fought at Jimo with a death resolve shared by soldiers and civilians, whereas later rank, wealth, and hesitation weaken the same command energy.
- Tian Dan accepts the warning, personally faces arrows and stones the next day, drums the assault, and finally takes Di.
- The host complicates Lu Zhonglian's diagnosis: Tian Dan is not merely softened by wealth; after Qi Xiang Wang's suspicion, he dares not bind himself too visibly to ordinary soldiers lest shared hardship become another sign of dangerous popular power.
- The closing Mengchangjun note says [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]] flees Qi after [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]] turns against him, serves [[WeiState|魏国]], joins the anti-Qi alignment, later returns to [[XueWarringStates|薛地]] outside direct lordship, and leaves a line destroyed after his sons fight over succession.
- The final preview turns toward [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] and [[BaiQi|白起]] attacking [[ChuState|楚国]].

## Key Quotes
> "亡国之言" - Diao Bo's warning about treating Tian Dan as an ordinary callable subordinate.

> "必死之心" - Lu Zhonglian's explanation for why Tian Dan's Jimo command once worked.
