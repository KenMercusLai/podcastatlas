# 24 东西方的饭局：最后的晚餐与兰亭雅集

## Summary

This [[WeirdHistoryPodcast|怪东西 Weird History]] episode has [[LuDapeng|陆大鹏]] and [[ZhangDabai|张大白]] compare the [[LastSupper|Last Supper]] with the [[LantingGathering|兰亭雅集]]. It approaches the Gospel narrative as literature and historically situated religious tradition rather than offering a theological ruling, then treats [[WangXizhi|王羲之]]'s gathering and [[LantingjiXu|《兰亭集序》]] as an artistic response to transience whose later cultural status depends on textual transmission, calligraphic reception, and Tang imperial patronage.

The shared synthesis is [[MortalityBoundedMeaning|mortality-bounded meaning]]. The Last Supper turns impending betrayal, fear, sacrifice, and death toward [[Eucharist|ritual remembrance]] and the command to love; Lanting turns aging, temporal passage, and death toward poetry, calligraphy, and serious attention to present life. The comparison identifies different cultural responses to finitude without claiming that Christian sacrament and Eastern Jin literati gathering are historically or institutionally equivalent.

## Key Claims

- The episode places [[Jesus]]'s final meal within Roman rule, Passover crowds, temple conflict, and cooperation between Roman and Judean authorities, while keeping the historicity of individual Gospel details unresolved.
- Foot washing, bread and wine, betrayal, the command to love, Gethsemane, arrest, and [[ApostlePeter|Peter]]'s denial make the Passion narrative a study of service, solidarity, fear, failure, and repentance as well as a theological text.
- [[JudasIscariot|Judas]]'s betrayal raises a free-will problem in the hosts' reading because the narrative combines personal action, prophecy, and satanic influence.
- The bread-and-wine tradition grounds [[Eucharist|the Eucharist]], while Catholic and Protestant interpretations differ over real presence, symbolism, and spiritual participation.
- The 353 [[LantingGathering|Lanting Gathering]] joined drinking, winding-stream verse composition, elite sociability, and awareness of age and death; its political purpose remains inferential.
- [[LantingjiXu|《兰亭集序》]] and transmitted calligraphic copies gave the gathering a long cultural afterlife, but the relation between the received text and the shorter *Linhe Xu* leaves authorship and textual formation open.
- The source contrasts the Lanting response with stories of banquet assassination, coercion, and life-disregarding luxury: awareness of transience can support nihilism or immediate gratification, but it can also be transformed through love, art, memory, and responsible present living.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with the existing wiki is adopted. The source agrees with [[288-jesus-christ-the-history-part-2-glt4776258433]] that the Last Supper tradition is early while applying a more literary and explicitly non-theological reading to the Gospel narrative.
- The hosts' summaries of Roman Judea, Gospel episodes, denominational Eucharistic doctrine, and later Peter tradition remain source-scoped and should not be treated as a full historical or theological survey.
- The gathering's political intent, the authenticity and formation of the received *Lantingji Xu*, the reliability of banquet anecdotes, and claims about Wang Xizhi's response to comparison with the Jingu gathering remain disputed or interpretive.
- The Last Supper and Lanting Gathering comparison is thematic, not proof of civilizational equivalence; their sources, institutions, participants, reception histories, and religious claims differ substantially.
