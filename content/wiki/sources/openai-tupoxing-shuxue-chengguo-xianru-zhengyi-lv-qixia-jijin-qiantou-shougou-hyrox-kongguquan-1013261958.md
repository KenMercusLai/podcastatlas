This [[ShengdongZaokafei|声动早咖啡]] episode is a compact daily business scan covering [[OpenAI]]'s contested claimed progress on the [[NavierStokesEquations|Navier-Stokes equations]], [[DeepSeek]] API price cuts, [[Alibaba]] B2B integration, [[KweichowMoutai|贵州茅台]] channel-price divergence, [[Tesla]] China promotions, [[LCatterton|L Catterton]]'s reported control investment in [[HYROX]], and retail execution problems in snack franchises. ## Key Claims
- The episode says [[OpenAI]] announced on 2026-09-08 that it had made a breakthrough on the [[NavierStokesEquations|Navier-Stokes problem]], while reporting controversy around prior related work by a New York University mathematician and an [[Anthropic]] employee who had used [[Claude]] and [[Codex]]-style tools.
- [[OpenAI]] is reported as saying its proof differs materially from the other team's work and that it did not access specific user data, so the episode keeps authorship, cooperation, and data-use questions unresolved.

- [[Alibaba]] reportedly moved 1688 and Alibaba International Station into one B2B operating structure, suggesting a tighter domestic-wholesale and cross-border-wholesale interface.
- [[KweichowMoutai|贵州茅台]]'s self-operated store price for 53-degree 500 ml Feitian Moutai reportedly rose while the iMoutai app price did not, implying different channel roles for supply, resale pressure, and terminal-price control.
- [[Tesla]] China and other automakers reportedly used limited-time discounts, insurance subsidies, interest-free policies, and other benefits as September sales-window tools rather than only as simple list-price cuts.
- [[LCatterton|L Catterton]] reportedly led a consortium acquiring a controlling stake in [[HYROX]] at an estimated $700 million valuation, adding an investment validation signal to HYROX's standardized event, team-participation, and social-sharing model.
- The snack-retail segment reports consumer complaints about weighing errors at discount-snack stores and frames the issue as a franchise-execution gap around calibration, staff training, and incentive costs.
