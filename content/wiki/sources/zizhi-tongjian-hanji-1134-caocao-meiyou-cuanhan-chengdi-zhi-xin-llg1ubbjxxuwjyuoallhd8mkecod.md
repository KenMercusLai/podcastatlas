# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1134丨曹操没有篡汉称帝之心？？？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[CaoCao|曹操]] in 213 CE from a classicizing reorganization of the provinces to elevation as Duke of Wei with the [[JiuXiRitualPromotion|九锡礼遇]]. The episode reads the restoration of the legendary Nine Provinces as [[StrongTrunkWeakBranchCentralization|强干弱枝式集权]] because enlarging Ji Province also enlarges the territory directly attached to Cao Cao as its governor.

Its central policy case is Cao Cao's attempt to move civilians away from the Yangtze frontier. [[JiangJiLateHan|蒋济]] warns that circumstances differ from the earlier Guandu evacuations and that settled civilians will panic rather than cooperate; Cao Cao proceeds, and the episode reports that more than one hundred thousand households flee south into [[SunQuan|孙权]]'s territory. The result extends [[CoerciveFrontierRelocationBackfire|强迁避敌反噬]] from direct civilian destruction and rebel recruitment to the transfer of population and productive capacity to a rival regime.

## Key Claims

- The 213 CE consolidation of fourteen provinces into a classicizing Nine Provinces arrangement is presented as Cao Cao's policy operating through an imperial edict.
- Removing Sili, Liang, You, and Bing as separate units and enlarging Ji Province increases the region under Cao Cao's direct gubernatorial control.
- Cao Cao proposes moving civilians away from Sun Quan's frontier and cites the earlier evacuation of Yan and Baima during the Guandu campaign as precedent.
- Jiang Ji argues that the political and military context has changed: the population is settled, is not disloyal, and may flee if coerced.
- Cao Cao does not adopt the warning; the episode reports panic across Lujiang, Jiujiang, Qichun, and Guangling and the southward flight of more than one hundred thousand households.
- Cao Cao later acknowledges that the policy produced the opposite of its intended result and appoints Jiang Ji administrator of Danyang, a title the episode treats as largely honorary because the commandery is under Sun control.
- Emperor Xian formally makes Cao Cao Duke of Wei, grants ten commanderies as his fief, and awards the Nine Bestowments, making near-sovereign ritual status visible without completing a dynastic transfer.
