# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1138丨乱世三国：赵昂的忠诚与牺牲

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows resistance to [[MaChao|马超]] around Ji and Licheng. [[ZhaoAngLateHan|赵昂]] and [[WangYiZhaoAngWife|王异]] accept their son Zhao Yue's hostage exposure while [[YangFuLateHan|杨阜]], [[JiangXuLateHan|姜叙]], Zhao Qu, and Liang Kuan coordinate an uprising and induce Ma Chao to leave Ji; Ma Chao then kills Jiang Xu's mother and Zhao Yue before [[XiahouYuan|夏侯渊]]'s relief helps drive him toward [[ZhangLuFivePecks|张鲁]] in Hanzhong.

The second half turns from war to the early Wei administrative lineup and especially [[YuanHuanLateHan|袁涣]]. His refusal to denounce former patron [[LiuBei|刘备]] under [[LyuBu|吕布]]'s threat, refusal to celebrate a false report of Liu Bei's death, distribution of rewards, and preference for books over plunder are presented as a single ethic of gratitude and independence. The source closes by introducing [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s proposed restoration of mutilating punishment, developed in Hanji 1139.

## Key Claims

- Ma Chao requires Zhao Ang to send his son Zhao Yue as a hostage; Wang Yi supports the decision despite the danger because she prioritizes resistance to Ma Chao.
- Yang Fu and Jiang Xu raise forces, while Zhao Ang joins the Qishan position and Zhao Qu and Liang Kuan persuade Ma Chao to leave Ji before closing the city against him.
- After Ma Chao's wife and children are killed inside Ji, he attacks Licheng, kills Jiang Xu's defiant mother, and also kills Zhao Yue.
- Yang Fu reportedly continues fighting after seven kinsmen die and he receives multiple wounds; Xiahou Yuan's arrival helps defeat Ma Chao and force his flight toward Zhang Lu.
- The episode interprets Ma Chao's collapse as a gap between real military capacity and the political trust, restraint, and judgment required to preserve a base.
- Cao Cao appoints a Wei administrative group including Xun You, Liang Mao, Mao Jie, Cui Yan, Chang Lin, Xu Yi, He Kui, Wang Can, Du Xi, Zhong Yao, Wang Xiu, Yuan Huan, and Chen Qun; exact offices and sequencing remain source-scoped.
- Yuan Huan is portrayed as materially unaccumulating, grateful to Liu Bei across political realignment, and unwilling to let current power dictate retrospective praise or blame.
- The closing proposal to restore mutilating punishment is a transition into the debate covered by [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1139-jiemi-gudai-rouxing-yige-bi-yige-xiaren-lgnj-8smphspf8nud-2o3xh_lggk|Hanji 1139]].

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. Hanji 1140 places Ma Chao's Hanzhong refuge before a later borrowed-force Qishan attack; this episode's earlier Ji/Qishan resistance belongs to the preceding flight sequence and should not be conflated with that later campaign.
- The episode's strongest judgments about Ma Chao's arrogance, cruelty, filial failure, and lack of political maturity remain host interpretation. The reported killings support severe credibility damage but do not by themselves establish a complete psychological diagnosis.
- Names, exact offices, sequence, casualty counts, wounds, hostage dialogue, literary attributions, and the mechanics of Ji's fall require comparison with transmitted histories and specialist scholarship.
- The supplied summary says Zhang Lu made Ma Chao “都讲祭酒”; the title, duties, and claim that it removed him from military command require textual verification and are not treated here as settled institutional fact.
- Yuan Huan's conduct is internally consistent across this and Hanji 1009, but the false death report, court congratulations, reward distribution, book-taking, and Cao Cao's grief remain source-scoped anecdotes rather than a complete biography.
