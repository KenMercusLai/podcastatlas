# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》338-2｜因为一场“按摩”引发的血案

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode reconstructs the opening of [[ZhangTangWesternHan|张汤]]'s downfall in Han Wudi's Yuanding 2 / 115 BCE. It joins three pressure lines: [[LuYezhuWesternHan|鲁谒居]] secretly helps Zhang eliminate rival [[LiWenWesternHan|李文]], [[LiuPengzuZhaoKingWesternHan|赵敬肃王刘彭祖]] turns Zhang's unusually intimate sickbed care for Lu into an accusation of hidden collusion, and [[ZhuangQingdiWesternHan|庄青翟]] becomes Zhang's target after the theft of burial money from Emperor Wen's funerary park.

The durable synthesis is that the foot massage is a clue, not the underlying cause. Zhang's dependence on imperial trust, protection of a favored subordinate, harsh treatment of rivals, and attempt to shift responsibility onto the chancellor have accumulated enemies and created several routes into the same investigation. When Zhang only pretends not to recognize Lu Yezhu's imprisoned brother, the brother reads caution as abandonment and discloses the Li Wen plot; [[JianXuanWesternHan|减宣]] then pursues the case while withholding an early report because he fears imperial protection for Zhang.

## Key Claims

- Zhang Tang's favored clerk Lu Yezhu covertly arranges an accusation against Li Wen after Li searches official documents for material that could harm Zhang; the resulting case is assigned to Zhang, who has Li executed.
- Zhang conceals Lu Yezhu's role when Han Wudi asks who first reported Li Wen, turning subordinate protection into personal exposure.
- When Lu becomes ill, Zhang visits and massages his feet; Liu Pengzu cites this status-incongruent intimacy as evidence that Zhang and Lu may share a serious hidden scheme.
- After Lu dies and his brother is imprisoned, Zhang tries to help while outwardly pretending not to know him; the brother mistakes concealment for betrayal and reveals the plot against Li Wen.
- Jian Xuan, who also has a prior grievance against Zhang, investigates aggressively and delays reporting because he fears Han Wudi may still shield Zhang.
- When burial money is stolen from Emperor Wen's funerary park, Zhuang Qingdi asks Zhang to join him in apologizing; Zhang remains silent because he regards inspection as the chancellor's responsibility.
- Han Wudi orders Zhang to examine Zhuang's responsibility, and Zhang seeks to accuse the chancellor of knowing about the theft and failing to report it, converting a shared audience into open conflict.

## Key Quotes

> “汤自往视疾，为谒居摩足” - the transmitted detail behind the episode's title and Liu Pengzu's accusation of concealed collusion.

> “至前，汤独不谢” - the responsibility split that turns Zhang Tang and Zhuang Qingdi into direct opponents.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with existing wiki content was found. The episode fills an earlier and much more detailed Zhang Tang career layer before Hanji 431 uses his death as a warning for Zhang Anshi.
- Transcript normalization: “鲁业居,” “贤轩,” and “庄青宅” are normalized to 鲁谒居, 减宣, and 庄青翟 because the Zhang Tang account and transmitted wording identify those figures. The source's broad “廷尉” phrasing and modern descriptions remain source-scoped.
- Causality caution: the massage does not independently prove conspiracy; its force comes from hierarchy, prior favoritism, the Li Wen case, and Liu Pengzu's hostile interpretation.
- Scope caution: motives attributed to Liu Pengzu, Jian Xuan, Zhang Tang, Lu Yezhu's brother, and Zhuang Qingdi are reconstructed by the episode and should not be treated as direct access to private intent. The exact theft, jurisdiction, procedure, chronology, and legal charges require fuller primary-text comparison.
