# 《资治通鉴·周纪》67丨战国“灭霸”白起的军事才能究竟如何

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode moves the 周纪四 frame from the aftermath of [[ShaqiuCrisis|沙丘之变]] into 294-290 BCE, when [[QinState|秦国]]'s eastward strategy accelerates under [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]]. Its center is [[BaiQi|白起]]'s first major appearance: after [[WeiRan|魏冉]] recommends him, Bai Qi defeats the Han-Wei coalition at [[YiqueBattle|伊阙之战]] by exploiting allied disunity, attacking the weaker side first, cutting into the rear, and destroying the coalition in detail. The episode then adds Qin-Chu coercive marriage diplomacy, Wei Ran's resignation and return, the territorial chronology of [[YuanjunWarringStates|垣郡]] and [[Dingtao|陶 / 定陶]], and the complicated reputation of [[MangMao|芒卯]].

## Key Claims
- In 294 BCE, Qin defeats Wei at Xiecheng, keeping [[QinEastwardPressure|秦国东进压力]] active after Chu Huaiwang's death and Zhao's Shaqiu crisis.
- In 293 BCE, Qin's national strength and post-[[ShangYangReforms|商鞅变法]] institutions allow Qin Zhaoxiang Wang to pursue a larger strategy of attacking the Three Jin and seeking wider control.
- [[MilitaryMeritRankSystem|军功爵制]] helps explain why a non-aristocratic military talent such as Bai Qi can rise quickly once he produces battlefield results.
- Wei Ran recommends Bai Qi to command against the Han-Wei force, replacing the previous commander rendered in the source as "令乡寿."
- At [[YiqueBattle|伊阙]], Bai Qi reads the Han-Wei coalition as internally disunited, avoids the strong point, strikes weak points first, and concentrates Qin forces to defeat coalition units separately.
- The episode reports the destructive result as 240,000 Han-Wei troops killed and [[GongsunXiWarringStates|公孙喜]] captured, after which Bai Qi rises from 左庶长 to 国尉 and then 大良造.
- A [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] dialogue with [[FanJu|范雎]] makes Bai Qi's own explanation central: he treats the victory as the result of measuring terrain, enemy incentives, and alliance fracture rather than as a miracle.
- Qin Zhaoxiang Wang threatens [[MiHeng|芈横 / 楚顷襄王]] after the earlier Qin-Chu coercion, and Chu responds by renewing peace through marriage to a Qin princess.
- [[SimaGuang|司马光]] condemns Chu for swallowing the killing of [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] and marrying into Qin, using the case to argue that territory without governing ability can still leave a state enslaved by an enemy.
- Wei Ran temporarily resigns as chancellor, a figure rendered as 卒寿 takes the office, and Qin later removes him and brings Wei Ran back.
- The episode distinguishes Qin's attacks on Chu-held and Han-held portions of [[YuanjunWarringStates|垣郡]], making the place a source-critical territorial case.
- The host argues that [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] misplaces Wei Ran's enfeoffment at Tao, because [[Dingtao|陶 / 定陶]] still belongs outside Qin in this year and only becomes available after Qi's conquest of Song and the later anti-Qi campaign.
- [[MangMao|芒卯]] enters as a Wei figure known for "诈说"; the episode keeps him morally and historically complex rather than reducing him to either a trickster or a sage.

## Key Quotes
> "这些都是衡量地利形势之后顺理成章的事" - Bai Qi's source-framed explanation of Yique as analysis rather than accident.

> "君主如果善于运用权力和治国之道" - the governing standard the episode draws from Sima Guang's criticism of Chu.
