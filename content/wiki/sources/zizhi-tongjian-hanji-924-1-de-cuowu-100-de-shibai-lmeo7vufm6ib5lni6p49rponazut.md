# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》924丨1%的错误=100%的失败

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the opening 184 CE Yellow Turban emergency before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-925-tuoxie-bushi-rensong-xuehui-tuoxie-lu-geng-changliang-limafqskgblrwetestyh39w-hzm4|Hanji 925]]. [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] appoints [[HeJin|何进]] to defend [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]], accepts advice from [[HuangfuSongLateHan|皇甫嵩]] and [[LyuQiangLateHan|吕强]] to release banned party figures and fund the army, and sends [[LuZhiLateHan|卢植]], Huangfu Song, and [[ZhuJunLateHan|朱儁]] against [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]] and the [[YellowTurbansLateHan|黄巾军]].

The deeper point is that emergency correction is immediately undermined by protected court access. The same court that briefly repairs capacity through [[PartyBanCrisisAmnesty|党锢危机赦免]] also lets the [[TenAttendantsLateHan|十常侍]] hide luxury, shift blame, destroy [[LyuQiangLateHan|吕强]], imprison [[XiangXuLateHan|项诩]], and kill [[ZhangJunLateHan|张钧]], making the host's "1% error" frame a case of [[SystemicErrorAmplification|细小错误系统性放大]].

## Key Claims

- The episode uses "1%的错误=100%的失败" to argue that small errors become decisive when they sit inside a stressed system.
- In March 184, Emperor Ling makes He Jin grand general, gives him capital troops, and adds pass defenses around Luoyang as Yellow Turban pressure becomes a central-security problem.
- Huangfu Song and Lyu Qiang both argue that the court cannot suppress the rebellion while continuing to alienate party figures and starve military incentives.
- Emperor Ling's amnesty excludes Zhang Jiao but lets banished party figures and families return, turning a political grievance into emergency capacity repair.
- Lu Zhi is sent against Zhang Jiao, while Huangfu Song and Zhu Jun are sent toward the Yingchuan Yellow Turban field that Hanji 925 develops.
- The eunuch favorites are not only corrupt beneficiaries; they control what Emperor Ling sees, hears, and treats as proof.
- Feng Xu and Xu Feng's role as Zhang Jiao insiders lets Emperor Ling recognize the contradiction between persecuted party figures and protected palace attendants, but the recognition does not become durable reform.
- Lyu Qiang, Xiang Xu, and Zhang Jun show three feedback-collapse paths: forced suicide, prison accusation, and death under a framed Yellow Turban charge.
- Zhang Mancheng killing a local administrator closes the episode by showing that front-line escalation continues while court correction is being destroyed.

## Key Quotes

> "1%的错误足以导致100%的失败。" - opening frame for small-error amplification.

> "张常侍似我父，赵常侍似我母。" - source quotation of Emperor Ling's dependence on Zhang Rang and Zhao Zhong.

> "黄巾于外揭竿而起，而朝廷之内却还在为各自的利益而勾心斗角。" - host's synthesis of external revolt and internal corrosion.
