# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》926丨真正的强者，都懂得“造势”

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s pre-Yellow-Turban reputation field before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-927-zuo-shengyi-de-zuigao-jingjie-shi-shenme-lngfbjn6xui9k-9lp-ihdqkhpbpr|Hanji 927]]. It argues that late-Han action required recognized public reputation, so Cao Cao had to overcome both a eunuch-family label through [[CaoSong|曹嵩]] and [[CaoTengLateHan|曹腾]] and a youthful reputation for unruly conduct.

The main chain runs through [[HeYongLateHan|何颙]], [[QiaoXuanLateHan|桥玄]], and [[XuShao|许劭]]. He Yong's early praise and Qiao Xuan's senior endorsement give Cao Cao elite backing, while Xu Shao's monthly appraisal culture turns the ambiguous "治世之能臣，乱世之奸雄" judgment into a portable public label; Cao Cao's earlier killing of [[JianShuoLateHan|蹇硕]]'s uncle as Luoyang north commandant then becomes a conduct signal aimed against his inherited eunuch association.

## Key Claims

- The episode treats reputation as an entry ticket to late-Han elite politics: without recognized名望, military skill or family resources alone are not enough to move in士人 circles.
- Cao Cao's family background is presented as contested. The source notes the [[Sanguozhi|《三国志》]] claim that the Cao line descended from [[CaoShen|曹参]], but emphasizes that [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] leaves the earlier Cao family background unclear and identifies Cao Song as Cao Teng's adopted son.
- The host's explanation of Cao Cao's youth is interpretive: losing maternal discipline, having a distant father, loving hunting and roaming, and using a fake-stroke trick against his uncle are recorded here as source-scoped character framing rather than independent proof of psychology.
- [[HeYongLateHan|何颙]] supplies an early evaluator layer. The episode connects him to Taixue, Party-figure recognition by Chen Fan and Li Ying, Yuan Shao's奔走友 circle, his "王佐之才" praise of [[XunYu|荀彧]], and his claim that Cao Cao could pacify the world.
- [[QiaoXuanLateHan|桥玄]] gives a higher-status elder endorsement: he tells Cao Cao that the realm is heading into disorder and that Cao Cao may be the person able to settle it, then directs him to seek Xu Shao's judgment.
- [[XuShao|许劭]] and Xu Jing's月旦品 are presented as a reputational certification system in Runan: officials and ambitious men care about the monthly judgment because a recognized evaluator can convert conduct into circulated reputation.
- Xu Shao initially refuses to evaluate Cao Cao, but after Cao Cao angrily pressures him, he gives the famous double-edged phrase "治世之能臣，乱世之奸雄"; Cao Cao is pleased because the phrase spreads.
- As Luoyang north commandant, Cao Cao kills Jian Shuo's uncle, making a visible anti-eunuch signal despite being from a family tied to a powerful eunuch. The source treats this as part of Cao Cao's effort to recode his public identity.

## Key Quotes

> "王佐之才" - He Yong's compressed appraisal of Xun Yu, used here to establish He Yong's evaluator authority.

> "天下将乱，非命世之才不能济也，能安之者，其在君乎" - Qiao Xuan's warning and endorsement to Cao Cao in the episode.

> "治世之能臣，乱世之奸雄" - Xu Shao's judgment of Cao Cao after Cao Cao demands an evaluation.
