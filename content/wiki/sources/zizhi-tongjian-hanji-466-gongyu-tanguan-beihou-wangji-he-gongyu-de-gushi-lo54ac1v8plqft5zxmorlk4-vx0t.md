# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》466｜“贡禹弹冠”背后：王吉和贡禹的故事

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]]'s Chuyuan 1 / 48 BCE accession context before the later Xiao Wangzhi and Gong Yu sequence. It explains how Yuan's early disaster relief, palace-cost reduction, and recruitment of [[WangJiWesternHan|王吉]] and [[GongYuWesternHan|贡禹]] set up the idiom "贡禹弹冠": Gong Yu prepares to reenter office after hearing that his like-minded friend Wang Ji has been summoned.

The durable synthesis is that early Gong Yu combines two themes that later sources separate: shared political expectation among principled scholars, and direct thrift remonstrance against palace, harem, horse, manufacturing, and burial expenditures during famine and epidemic pressure. The episode also leaves a forward tension because later [[SimaGuang|司马光]] criticism will ask why Gong Yu could speak sharply on spending but remain insufficiently direct about deeper court dangers.

## Key Claims

- Chuyuan 1 opens with Han Xuandi's burial, general amnesty, Wang Zhengjun's elevation as empress, and relief loans of seed grain and food to poor commoners.
- Epidemic, hardship, flood, famine, and even cannibalism reports make palace thrift and emergency relief a live political issue at the start of Yuan's reign.
- Yuan summons Wang Ji and Gong Yu because both are known for classics learning and upright conduct; Wang Ji dies en route, while Gong Yu reaches court and becomes Jianyi Dafu.
- "贡禹弹冠" is explained as Gong Yu dusting off his official cap after Wang Ji's summons signals that their shared values may again have court space.
- Gong Yu's remonstrance praises ancient and near-ancient frugality, then criticizes court manufacturing, excessive palace women, royal horses, thick burial, tomb goods, and women sent to mausoleum service.
- Gong Yu's concrete remedies include reducing luxury goods and carriage display by two-thirds, keeping only a small number of worthy palace women, returning unchildbearing mausoleum women, limiting imperial horses, and shrinking hunting grounds.
- Yuan is said to accept Gong Yu's thrift advice, but the episode ends by foreshadowing later criticism that Gong Yu may still fail to speak on the most consequential problem.

## Key Quotes

> "贡禹弹冠" - the idiom the episode uses to connect Wang Ji's summons with Gong Yu's expectation of reentering public office.

> "知而不言" - the later criticism previewed at the close, marking the unresolved tension in Gong Yu's evaluation.
