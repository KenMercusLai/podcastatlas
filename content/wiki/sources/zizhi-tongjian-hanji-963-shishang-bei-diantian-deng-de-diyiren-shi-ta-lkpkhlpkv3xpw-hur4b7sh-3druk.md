# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》963丨史上被“点天灯”的第一人，是他！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode directly narrates the 192 CE killing of [[DongZhuo|董卓]]: [[WangYunLateHan|王允]] and [[LyuBu|吕布]] use [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]'s court meeting at [[WeiyangPalace|未央宫]] to stage an ambush, with [[ShiSunRuiLateHan|士孙瑞]] drafting the edict and [[LiSuLateHan|李肃]] attacking at the north gate. It then follows the celebration, family purge, [[MeiwuFortress|郿坞]] confiscation, and corpse-burning spectacle that make Dong Zhuo a case of [[PublicCorpseHumiliation|公开尸体羞辱]]. The second half turns to [[CaiYong|蔡邕]]'s imprisonment and death, where Wang Yun's refusal to spare a historian extends [[HistoricalRecordAuthority|史官记录话语权]] and [[PostSuccessArrogance|得势后的骄傲]] before the follow-on Hanji 964-965 crisis.

## Key Claims

- In the episode's account, Emperor Xian's recovery and court assembly create the access window for the anti-Dong-Zhuo plot.
- Wang Yun limits knowledge of the plan, has Shi Sunrui write the edict, and hands the authority channel to Lü Bu.
- Lü Bu arranges Li Su and other armed men as disguised guards at the gate, making the court-security layer part of the assassination mechanism.
- Dong Zhuo survives Li Su's first strike because of armor, but Lü Bu announces the imperial order against a traitorous minister and kills him.
- The edict's public wording tells soldiers that only Dong Zhuo is targeted, which stabilizes the armed crowd long enough for the coup to hold.
- Chang'an's celebration and the killing of Dong Zhuo's relatives show that a targeted act can quickly become broader vengeance after a hated ruler falls.
- The episode's "pointing the sky lantern" title comes from the source's graphic corpse-treatment scene, where Dong Zhuo's body is turned into a public spectacle.
- Confiscating Meiwu's gold, silver, silk, and treasures turns Dong Zhuo's private hoard back into an object of court seizure after his death.
- Emperor Xian gives Wang Yun the Shangshu affairs and rewards Lü Bu with general rank, `假节`, and the Wen marquis title, placing both men at the center of the new order.
- Cai Yong's reaction to Dong Zhuo's death is interpreted by Wang Yun as private gratitude overriding Han loyalty.
- Cai Yong asks to survive even under mutilating punishment so that he can finish Han history; many scholar-officials sympathize with him.
- Ma Ridi argues that killing Cai Yong would damage Wang Yun's reputation and deprive the court of a historian.
- Wang Yun's refusal invokes the danger of a Sima Qian-like historian writing compromising material into the record.
- The host's source-scoped interpretation is that Wang Yun fears Cai Yong's historical pen because Cai Yong had seen Wang Yun's compromised behavior under Dong Zhuo.
- Cai Wenji's later exile-and-ransom branch makes Cai Yong's afterlife literary and familial rather than only an execution story.

## Key Quotes

> "我儿奉先何在" - Dong Zhuo calls for Lü Bu in the episode's death scene.

> "有诏讨贼臣" - Lü Bu frames the killing as imperial command.

> "王司徒纵容" - the feared historical wording in Wang Yun's source-scoped anxiety.
