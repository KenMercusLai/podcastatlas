# No.222 程泓宁：从庙堂之上的投资人，到在真实的泥地里打滚

## Summary
This [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] episode has [[LiuFei|刘飞]] interviewing [[ChengHongningYuzhouEBike|程泓宁 / 陈鸿宁]], founder of [[YuzhouEBike|宇宙 E-Bike]], about leaving investment work for a physical-product startup. The conversation links auditing, public-market investing, primary-market investing, listed-company management, and brand operations into [[InvestorToOperatorLearning]], where capital-market analysis is treated as incomplete until tested against product, supply chain, sales, service, cash flow, and organization.

The durable contribution is a concrete China-side e-bike operator case. [[YuzhouEBike|宇宙 E-Bike]] uses [[ElectricAssistBicyclePremiumPositioning]] to argue that the Chinese market differs from Europe because electric mopeds already exist, while good riding experience still depends on motors, batteries, sensors, control tuning, frame design, service budget, and brand trust. The episode also adds [[AINativeManufacturingTransformation]] as a founder-led manufacturing-company target rather than a generic software adoption slogan.

## Key Claims
- [[ChengHongningYuzhouEBike|程泓宁 / 陈鸿宁]] describes his pre-founder path as audit, secondary-market research and fund management, primary-market investment, listed-company board and management work, family-office capital management, and later e-bike entrepreneurship.
- The source frames [[InvestorToOperatorLearning]] as a correction to investor distance: an investor can study industries deeply but still lacks direct control over product, manufacturing, sales, cash flow, and customer trust.
- [[YuzhouEBike|宇宙 E-Bike]] is positioned around electric-assist bicycles rather than throttle-only electric mopeds: the rider still pedals while motors, batteries, and sensors provide assistance.
- The episode says Europe has much higher electric-assist penetration than China, but warns against simple analogy because China already has electric mopeds while European rules constrain throttle-driven two-wheelers more tightly.
- [[ElectricAssistBicyclePremiumPositioning]] is justified through riding experience and operating economics: 10,000-30,000 yuan products can fund stronger parts, R&D, tuning, and after-sales service, while lower prices make long-term service harder.
- The product thesis emphasizes system integration over single parts: motor assistance may matter greatly, but geometry, components, battery structure, sensor tuning, frame design, and Chinese rider fit shape the total experience.
- [[AINativeManufacturingTransformation]] appears as a small-manufacturing-company ambition: the founder says AI transformation must be pushed by the top person because data exposure, workflow redesign, employee capability, and role replacement are organization-level decisions.
- Several market and company performance claims are source-reported, including the brand's domestic position in its price band, 70%-90% desired share in that band, recent profitability, European penetration ratios, Chinese penetration ratios, and overseas trust difficulties.

## Key Quotes
> "说了不算" - Cheng/Chen's shorthand for the investor's lack of operating control.
