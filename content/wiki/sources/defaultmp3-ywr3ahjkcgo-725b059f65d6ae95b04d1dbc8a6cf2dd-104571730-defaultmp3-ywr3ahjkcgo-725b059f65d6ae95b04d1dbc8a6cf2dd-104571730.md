# The 10 Challenges That Will Shape Our Future with Jorge Brown x Shekhar Natarajan

## Summary
This [[TomorrowToday]] episode has [[ShekharNatarajan]] interview futurist [[JorgeBrown]] about AI, democratic legitimacy, energy and water, multilateral institutions, longevity, inequality, automation, warfare, commerce, and responsibility to future generations. Brown's central frame is temporal and systemic: decisions made in the next few years may harden into institutions that last for decades, while changes in one domain alter the constraints in several others.

The episode's strongest contribution is [[InterdependentFutureGovernance]], which treats technology, resources, employment, public finance, security, and legitimacy as one coupled policy system. It also proposes [[MeasurablePublicDeliberation]] as a response to polarization and institutional inertia: bring opposing views into structured contact, protect nonviolent disagreement, and convert discussion into commitments that can be checked on a short horizon.

## Key Claims
- [[JorgeBrown]] argues that AI capability and user-contributed information are concentrating influence inside a small number of firms before accountable ethical boundaries have been agreed.
- [[GlobalAIGovernanceFramework|Global AI governance]] must address embedded objectives, access to evidence, institutional representation, enforcement, and the values behind automated decisions rather than focusing only on technical capability.
- [[InterdependentFutureGovernance]] links AI, employment, education, energy, water, longevity, retirement finance, security, inequality, and trade because an intervention in one domain changes the others.
- Liberal democracy requires meaningful participation, pluralism, and compromise between elections; algorithmic filtering and political tribalism can make attention control and group identity substitutes for substantive argument.
- AI infrastructure makes electricity, cooling, water, land, and community consent part of technology policy, while the episode leaves the scale and best energy mix unresolved.
- Longer lives, automation, and possible basic-income systems require joint redesign of work, taxation, retirement, care, and intergenerational power rather than separate policy fixes.
- Cheap drones and autonomous systems can shift national-security economics by distributing destructive capability, pressuring expensive defenses, and exposing civilian infrastructure.
- [[MeasurablePublicDeliberation]] combines protected disagreement with time-bounded commitments, implementation checks, and willingness to revise a position.
- Human value under AI rests partly in contextual perception, moral judgment, relationships, and purpose that are not captured by speed, output, wealth, or technical efficiency alone.

- The episode supports freedom to question prevailing views while limiting incitement to violence, but it does not supply a complete rule for misinformation, harassment, professional standards, or platform moderation.
