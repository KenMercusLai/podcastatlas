# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1046丨他凭什么被称作“三国隐藏最深的牛人”？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode uses the Analects line about acting only from profit to frame [[ZhaoYanLateHan|赵俨]] as a late-Han official whose value lies in reading people, timing, and institutions. In Jian'an 5 / 200 CE, [[LiTongLateHan|李通]]'s [[YangAnLateHan|阳安]] locality remains Cao-aligned while surrounding areas are unstable; Zhao Yan warns that forcing cotton-silk levies could turn fiscal loyalty into local disturbance, routes the warning through [[XunYu|荀彧]], and [[CaoCao|曹操]] returns the levied goods.

The episode then pairs that case with [[HeKui|何夔]]'s advice from [[ChangguangCommanderyLateHan|长广郡]]: newly attached and remote regions should receive lighter taxes, looser legal implementation, and local discretion until ordinary life settles. It closes by explaining why cotton-silk mattered so much to Cao Cao's war order: in a damaged currency environment, portable textile goods could serve as store of value, reward material, diplomatic gift, military supply, and eventually part of a grain-plus-textile fiscal system.

## Key Claims
- The opening "profit brings resentment" frame is used as a governance lens: single-minded extraction can cost the support needed to get anything done.
- [[ZhaoYanLateHan|赵俨]]'s earlier strict enforcement against [[LiTongLateHan|李通]]'s wife's uncle is recalled to show that his later caution is not softness or evasion.
- In [[YangAnLateHan|阳安]], the immediate conflict is between proving loyalty to [[CaoCao|曹操]] by sending cotton-silk and preserving local stability while surrounding commanderies are rebelling.
- Zhao Yan's practical strength is channel design: he persuades Li Tong to pause, writes to [[XunYu|荀彧]], and turns local risk into a decision Cao Cao can accept.
- Cao Cao's return of the levied cotton-silk shows a tactical willingness to delay extraction when the political cost of collection is higher than the immediate fiscal gain.
- [[HeKui|何夔]] generalizes the same logic for [[ChangguangCommanderyLateHan|长广郡]] and other newly attached border areas: lighter taxes and flexible law can buy time for people to settle before full uniform rule.
- [[WartimeTextileFiscalResource|战时绵绢财政资源]] explains why the decision was costly: cotton-silk was not decorative luxury but a money-like, portable, and militarily usable fiscal resource.
- The episode therefore links [[TaxationStabilityTradeoff|税赋与地方稳定权衡]] with [[NewlyAttachedRegionLeniency|新附地区宽缓治理]] rather than presenting leniency as simple benevolence.

## Key Quotes
> "放于利而行，多怨" - opening Analects frame for why pursuit of immediate benefit can destroy support.

> "千里不运粮" - logistics shorthand used to explain why grain is harder to move than high-value textile goods.
