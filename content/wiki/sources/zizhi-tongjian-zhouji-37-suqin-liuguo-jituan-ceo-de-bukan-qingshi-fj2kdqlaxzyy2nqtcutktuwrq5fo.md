# 《资治通鉴·周纪》37丨苏秦：六国集团CEO的不堪情史

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode covers a short but dense 324-323 BCE transition in [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]]: [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] attacks [[WeiState|魏国]] and takes [[ShanchengWarringStates|陕城]], [[SuQin|苏秦]] leaves [[YanState|燕国]] for [[QiState|齐国]] after a private scandal, and [[GongsunYan|犀首公孙衍]] begins a more operational anti-[[QinState|秦国]] alliance line. The core reading is that Warring States diplomacy now works through mixed instruments: selective territorial pressure, covert court placement, induced extravagance, title politics, and persuader rivalry.

## Key Claims
- In 324 BCE, the episode pairs two main notices: Zhang Yi attacks Wei, and Su Qin enters Qi, placing the two famous persuaders in parallel activity.
- Zhang Yi's Qin campaign against Wei keeps the pattern summarized as "只要土地，不要人头": Qin occupies [[ShanchengWarringStates|陕城]] but lets the local people return to Wei.
- The source says Shancheng had been returned to Wei in 327 BCE and is again taken by Qin in 324 BCE, making it part of the Qin-Wei territorial back-and-forth around [[QinEastwardPressure|秦国东进压力]].
- [[SuQin|苏秦]]'s departure from Yan is triggered by [[YanYiwang|燕易王]] learning that Su Qin had been involved with [[YanWengong|燕文公]]'s wife.
- Su Qin tells Yan Yi Wang that staying in Yan offers little value, but going to Qi can help Yan; Yan Yi Wang agrees, and Su Qin pretends to flee after committing a crime.
- In Qi, [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]] appoints Su Qin as guest minister, and Su Qin urges him to expand palaces and gardens.
- The episode reads this as [[StrategicLuxurySabotage|奢侈诱导式削弱]]: Su Qin is not simply hiding from scandal but trying to drain Qi's resources for Yan's benefit.
- In 323 BCE, Zhang Yi meets the chief ministers of Qi and [[ChuState|楚国]] at [[Niesang|啮桑]], which the episode reads either as a Qin attempt to improve relations with Qi and Chu or as a move against Gongsun Yan.
- The same year, Han and Yan rulers have claimed kingly title, while [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]] refuses the title and asks Zhao people to call him "jun" because name and reality should match.
- Zhao Wuling Wang's refusal extends [[TitleRealityRestraint|名实相符式名号节制]]: he will not accept royal name without royal substance, even as other Warring States rulers inflate status.
- [[GongsunYan|犀首公孙衍]] leaves Qin for Wei and begins contacting other lords on Wei's behalf, which the episode calls the opening of history's "real hezong" work.
- After the Nie Sang meeting, Zhang Yi also leaves Qin and goes to Wei as chief minister, setting up a more complex Qin-Wei-anti-Qin diplomatic contest.

## Key Quotes
> "只要土地，不要人头" - the episode's summary of Zhang Yi's Qin-Wei campaign style.

> "真正的合纵事业" - the episode's description of Gongsun Yan's anti-Qin coalition work from Wei.

## Contradictions
- This source extends the existing [[QiChronologyDispute|齐威王编年争议]]: it has [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]] appointing [[SuQin|苏秦]] in 324 BCE, but [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-29-suqin-lizhi-shuangwen-zhishi-gaibian-mingyun-lrf5cqvoriyep2lkbozju-lyjaxv|Zhouji 29]] records the host's correction that Qi Xuan Wang's first year should be 319 BCE. The wiki should keep this as [[ChronicleChronologyDrift|编年错位]] rather than silently reconciling the dates.
- The episode's claim that [[GongsunYan|犀首公孙衍]] opens the "real" hezong business qualifies, rather than erases, the earlier Su Qin hezong arc: Su Qin remains the legendary and rhetorical hezong figure, while Gongsun Yan is presented here as the later practical organizer from Wei.
