# EP138-“来了就是深圳人”？

## Summary

This [[WushichaYanjiusuo|无时差研究所]] episode compares three lived relationships to [[Shenzhen|深圳]]: growing up there, living there for nine years and recognizing the identity only after leaving, and moving from Beijing around 2018. It presents Shenzhen as a migrant-built city whose belonging is produced through residence, participation, shared school and neighborhood memories, and continued city-making more than ancestry alone.

The episode also tests the limits of “来了就是深圳人.” Fast growth, comparatively open settlement, youth, mixed regional backgrounds, and a culture of experimentation can lower the symbolic threshold for membership, while high housing costs, intense work, limited cultural life, and shortfalls in education and healthcare make durable settlement harder. Its strongest synthesis joins [[MigrantCityBelonging|移民城市归属]] to [[GrowthToLivabilityTransition|从增长速度到城市宜居性]]: attracting mobile workers is not the same as enabling families to remain and build a full life.

## Key Claims

- [[MigrantCityBelonging]] can form through lived participation rather than birthplace: residents retain family origins while also becoming Shenzhen people.
- The former special-economic-zone border between “inside” and “outside” the checkpoint shows that today's more continuous Shenzhen was produced through spatial expansion and integration.
- Early builders, military engineering units, schools, landmark construction, land experiments, finance, and technology are used to explain a city ethos of daring to try rather than an ancient unitary tradition.
- Shenzhen's young and nationally mixed population can reduce the social cost of arriving without local ancestry, language, or an established network.
- Environmental comfort, proximity to the sea, new urban spaces, urban villages, street food, and personal room coexist with a work-centered culture in which leisure can remain saturated by business talk.
- Education, healthcare, housing, cultural provision, and commuting burdens determine whether newcomers can move from temporary opportunity to durable family settlement.
- [[GrowthToLivabilityTransition]] becomes necessary as growth slows: the city must preserve productive strengths while making ordinary life less costly, pressured, and institutionally thin.

## Key Quotes

> "来了就是深圳人" - the slogan used to express low-threshold migrant belonging.

> "敢想、敢拼、敢闯、敢试" - the episode's summary of Shenzhen's experimental city ethos.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction was found. The episode's positive account of low-threshold belonging is explicitly qualified by the difficulty of housing, family formation, schooling, healthcare, cultural life, and work-life separation.
- The discussion is based on three personal trajectories and a September 2020 perspective, not a representative urban study. Claims about school progression, housing supply, talent housing, rent, GDP growth, population age, cultural provision, healthcare behavior, business relocation, and regional discrimination remain date-bounded and source-scoped.
- The Guangzhou comparison is interpretive and does not establish that either city has one uniform culture or migrant experience.
