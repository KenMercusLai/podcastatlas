# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》660-2丨一集看懂赤眉军的诞生

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 22 CE late-[[XinDynasty|新朝]] crisis before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-661-cong-shusheng-dao-kaiguo-huangdi-liuxiu-shoudeng-lishi-wutai-lmw-rgyoraks9loeouuxk5lyxfs5|Hanji 661]]. It begins with [[WangMang|王莽]]'s extravagant Nine Temples ceremony, then turns to eastern famine, [[FanChongChimei|樊崇]]'s organized [[ChimeiRebellion|赤眉]] movement, official-army civilian abuse under [[WangKuangXinGeneral|王匡]] and [[LianDanXinGeneral|廉丹]], [[LulinRebellion|绿林]] fragmentation into Xiajiang and New Market troops, and corrupt relief that leaves refugees dying after they enter Guanzhong.

The durable synthesis is that Chimei's birth belongs to a wider state-capacity collapse rather than a simple bandit origin story. The episode pairs symbolic overreach at court with local hunger, corrupt granary distribution, predatory market administration under [[WangYeXinOfficial|王业]], command constraints criticized by [[YanYouXinGeneral|严尤]], and [[FengYanEasternHan|冯衍]]'s warning to Lian Dan that public sentiment had shifted back toward Han.

## Key Claims

- Wang Mang's Nine Temples procession projects sacred majesty while officials privately read the spectacle as ominous, sharpening the contrast between court ritual and collapsing local order.
- Fan Chong's group becomes [[ChimeiRebellion|赤眉]] after adopting cinnabar-painted eyebrows for battlefield identification, and its internal rules and offices show more organization than a pure mob.
- Xin official armies worsen the revolt field when Wang Kuang and Lian Dan allow soldiers to abuse civilians, making people prefer encountering Chimei to encountering the court's commanders.
- Wang Mang's grass-and-tree paste relief advice, stolen relief grain, and Wang Ye's false market report turn famine into [[DisasterReliefCorruption|救灾物资贪腐]] and [[DisasterResponseStateCapacity|disaster response state capacity]] failure.
- Lulin forces split after disease: [[WangChangXiajiangGengshi|王常]] and Cheng Dan move toward Xiajiang troops, while Wang Feng, Wang Kuang, and Ma Wu move toward New Market troops.
- Yan You's complaint that generals are sent without full command authority shows Xin's military response constrained by approval bottlenecks even as rebellion spreads.
- Feng Yan's counsel to Lian Dan frames late-Xin politics as a public-sentiment problem: heroes did not sincerely accept Xin, and ordinary people remembered Han favors.

## Key Quotes

> "宁逢赤眉，不逢太师；太师尚可，更始杀我" - popular saying contrasting Chimei with abusive Xin commanders.

> "用朱砂涂眉" - the battlefield-identification practice behind the Red Eyebrows name.

> "天下英雄豪杰没有人心悦臣服" - Feng Yan's warning that Xin lacked genuine assent.
