## Summary
This [[ShangyeJiushiZheyang|商业就是这样]] “金字招牌” special uses functional drinks, small appliances, travel apps, and short-video platforms to examine innovation outside fast-growing mass markets. Its strongest synthesis is [[NicheInnovationInMatureMarkets|niche innovation in mature markets]]: changing health language, household structure, channel capability, service integration, and AI production can create new demand, but rapid entry also moves bottlenecks toward brand durability, operational scale, trusted data, distribution, and scarce audience attention.

## Key Claims
- Functional beverages remain a relatively small part of China's drink market, but vitamin, electrolyte, protein, botanical, and lightly flavored products can lower trial friction by giving consumers a specific use occasion under [[FunctionalBeverageOccasionCreation]].
- Retailers such as [[Hema]] and convenience-store operators can turn channel access and fast product development into rapid flavor and ingredient experiments, although frequent rotation does not automatically create durable brand equity.
- Small appliances can keep growing inside a mature household-appliance market because ecommerce aggregates scattered demand, short development cycles support iterative launches, and independent living or one-person meals create new use cases under [[LifestyleDrivenSmallApplianceDemand]].
- The air fryer illustrates demand reactivation: an expensive product with weak early use guidance became more legible during pandemic home cooking, then benefited from lower prices, better recipes, and compatible prepared foods.
- [[OnlineTravelAgency|OTAs]] increasingly add value through cross-provider planning, transfers, local tours, rental cars, airport services, insurance, and visas rather than merely reselling a single ticket or room.
- [[Ctrip]]'s scale is attributed to domestic consolidation, international assets, and integrated supply, but the source also notes regulatory action around hotel price parity and merchant pricing autonomy.
- [[AITravelPlanning]] may favor platforms with live inventory and transaction data if they can connect planning, comparison, booking, and service recovery in one reliable loop.
- [[Hongguo]] is presented as a mass-market short-drama platform whose free, advertising-funded model displaced much of the earlier mini-program paid-unlock logic and drew on [[FanqieNovel]] content plus [[Douyin]] traffic.
- Generative video sharply lowered short-drama production cost and expanded output, but the resulting [[AIShortDramaSupplyGlut]] shifted the binding constraints toward story quality, platform intake, distribution, payout, and viewer time.
- [[JiangyouWenhua]] is used as a scale-and-contraction case: the episode reports expansion to roughly 1,200 workers, later reduction to roughly 600, and platform limits on new releases after supply outpaced demand.
