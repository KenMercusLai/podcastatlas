# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1109丨习凿齿怒批曹操：多年基业毁于一旦！

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows the redistribution of power after Red Cliffs. [[SunQuan|孙权]]'s side presses into the Jingzhou corridor, [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]] protects the surrendered commander [[XiSuLateHan|袭肃]] from losing his troops and helps [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] relieve [[GanNing|甘宁]], while [[LiuBei|刘备]] uses [[LiuQiLateHan|刘琦]]'s provincial title to take the southern Jingzhou commanderies and gain a durable tax base.

The second half connects [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s dismissive reception of [[ZhangSongLateHan|张松]] to a Yizhou alignment shift: Zhang Song returns and persuades [[LiuZhang|刘璋]] to break with Cao Cao and approach Liu Bei. [[XiZaochi|习凿齿]]'s later criticism turns the episode into a case of [[PostSuccessArrogance|得势后的骄傲]], but the source itself qualifies the moral reading by acknowledging that military, geographic, and multi-actor changes also helped produce the Three Kingdoms balance.

## Key Claims
- Xi Su judges his position exposed after Wu's post-Red-Cliffs advance and surrenders early rather than wait for isolation.
- When Zhou Yu proposes transferring Xi Su's troops to Lyu Meng, Lyu Meng argues that a sincere defector should retain and even strengthen his command; Sun Quan accepts the advice.
- Lyu Meng proposes leaving [[LingTong|凌统]] to hold the rear while he and Zhou Yu relieve Gan Ning, and the resulting action defeats Cao Ren's force and captures three hundred horses.
- Gan Ning's calm behavior under missile fire is presented as a command signal that stabilizes troops in crisis.
- Sun Quan's attacks on Hefei and Dangtu fail, but Liu Bei uses Liu Qi's Jingzhou-inspector title to receive the surrender of Wuling, Changsha, Guiyang, and Lingling.
- Liu Bei's southern expansion gives him stable revenue, making the post-Red-Cliffs moment a transition from mobile survival to territorial rule.
- Liu Bei appoints [[ZhaoYun|赵云]] to Guiyang and [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] to supervise revenue and administration across Lingling, Guiyang, and Changsha.
- Cao Cao receives Zhang Song coldly after his Jingzhou success and rejects Yang Xiu's suggestion that Zhang Song be recruited.
- Zhang Song returns to Yizhou, persuades Liu Zhang to end the Cao relationship, and advocates alliance with Liu Bei.
- Xi Zaochi interprets Cao Cao's conduct as success-induced arrogance that squandered accumulated advantage and helped make tripartite division possible.
- The closing modern analogy uses entrepreneur Huang Ming's reported cash-and-debt reversal to argue that abundant resources enlarge the cost of lost restraint.

## Key Quotes
> "数十年辛苦建立的事业在片刻之间毁于一旦" - the episode's paraphrase of Xi Zaochi's criticism of Cao Cao.

> "个人或组织真正的风险，常常不是弱小时缺乏机会，而是成功后失去敬畏" - the episode's closing synthesis.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction found. The episode extends existing Liu Zhang integration weakness and post-success arrogance patterns into the post-Red-Cliffs alignment field.
- Causal qualification: the claim that Cao Cao's arrogance produced the Three Kingdoms balance is Xi Zaochi's and the host's moralized interpretation; military losses, geography, fiscal consolidation, and the choices of Wu, Liu Bei, and Yizhou actors remain independent causes.
- Source-scope caution: Gan Ning's drinking scene, the exact three-hundred-horse capture, Zhang Song's physical description, Xi Zaochi wording, Huang Ming's cash and debt figures, and the management analogy remain bounded to this short popular-history source.
