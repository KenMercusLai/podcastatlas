# AMA #12: Thoughts on Longevity Supplements (Resveratrol, NR, NMN, Etc.) & How to Improve Memory

## Summary
This [[HubermanLab]] premium AMA has [[AndrewHuberman]] separate longevity mechanisms, personal supplement use, subjective effects, and demonstrated human lifespan benefit. Resveratrol, NR, NMN, intravenous NAD, metformin, berberine, and rapamycin are discussed through [[NADTherapyEvidenceBoundary]] and [[MechanismOutcomeEvidenceHierarchy]]: biological plausibility or a personal response does not establish longer human life. The episode reinforces [[SustainableHealthOptimization]] by placing sleep, exercise, nutrition, light, stress regulation, and social connection ahead of speculative longevity compounds.

## Key Claims
- Resveratrol may affect cell function, but the source says direct evidence that it extends human lifespan was not established in the discussion.
- [[AndrewHuberman]] reports taking grape seed extract for vascular or blood-flow reasons, not as a demonstrated longevity intervention, and does not rank it among his highest-priority supplements.
- [[NADTherapyEvidenceBoundary]] separates the role of NAD in cellular energetics from proof that NR, NMN, or direct NAD administration extends human lifespan.
- Huberman reports subjective mental and physical energy while taking NR and sublingual NMN, but explicitly does not treat that experience as evidence of lifespan extension.
- His two intravenous NAD experiences involved substantial nausea and pressure-like discomfort followed by perceived sleep or vigor improvement; the uncontrolled sequence does not establish efficacy.
- Metformin, berberine, fasting, and rapamycin are connected to glucose or mTOR pathways, but the source does not treat pathway modulation as direct evidence of longer human life.
- [[SustainableHealthOptimization]] remains foundations-first: quality sleep, sufficient exercise, nutrition, morning light, circadian alignment, stress control, and social connection are presented as more consequential than the discussed drugs or supplements.
- Supplement and prescription changes remain clinician-guided decisions rather than podcast-derived treatment or longevity protocols.
