# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》905丨富贵如何险中求？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 174-175 CE late-Han layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-906-lihai-de-ren-weishenme-xiangshou-chongtu-lvc3wedrifdzdu-yv1qhocl8uss2|Hanji 906]]. It uses [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]]'s role in suppressing [[XuShengLateHan|许生]] and his youth bluff against river bandits as a source-scoped [[CrisisOpportunityConversion|危机机会转化]] case, then turns to [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s [[XipingStoneClassics|熹平石经]] project at [[Taixue|太学]]. The episode's core synthesis is that late-Han disorder produces two opposed responses: local military initiative can become personal political capital, while the court tries to stabilize classical authority and loyal-familial order through official text standardization.

## Key Claims

- In 174 CE, [[ZangMinLateHan|臧旻]] and the Danyang commandery administration suppress the rebel [[XuShengLateHan|许生]], while [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] recruits more than one thousand local fighters and turns the crisis into an early armed reputation base.
- The youth story about Sun Jian bluffing river bandits works as character setup: courage, theatrical command gestures, and willingness to act under fear make him legible as someone who can exploit disorder.
- The host's "富贵险中求" lesson is kept source-scoped: the episode praises risk-aware initiative under crisis, not reckless risk as a general rule.
- In 175 CE, [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] orders official correction of the Five Classics, and [[CaiYong|蔡邕]] writes the corrected text in three scripts before it is carved as the [[XipingStoneClassics|熹平石经]] outside [[Taixue|太学]].
- The stone classics function as a pre-printing official reference text: students and copyists can compare manuscripts against a public standard rather than rely only on divergent handwritten transmission.
- The episode reads the project politically as well as philologically: amid rebellions and disorder, the court tries to reaffirm Confucian loyalty, filial hierarchy, and shared textual authority.
- The later damage to Taixue and the stone classics after [[DongZhuo|董卓]] burns [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] shows how a cultural-stabilization project can be overtaken by the deeper late-Han collapse.
- The birth notice for [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] in 175 CE functions as a small chronological bridge from Sun Jian's emergence toward the later Sun-Zhou Jiangdong alliance.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is recorded.
- Transcript variants and legendary material are bounded: "藏民" is normalized to [[ZangMinLateHan|臧旻]], the Danyang governor's name is left source-scoped from the transcript, and Sun-family ancestral-cloud imagery is treated as later heroic coloration rather than factual proof.
- The episode's risk lesson is preserved as a source-scoped reading of Sun Jian, not as a universal claim that every crisis should be pursued aggressively.
