# 273.逛完外滩大会，发现蚂蚁找到了新位置

## Summary
This [[Luanfanshu|乱翻书]] episode uses the Bund Conference as a field report on [[AntGroup|蚂蚁集团]]'s post-fintech repositioning. The discussion argues that Ant is no longer legible only as [[Alipay|支付宝]] plus consumer finance: its new frame combines [[AntAbao|阿宝]], [[AntAfu|阿福]], [[AgentPaymentInfrastructure|AI payment]], [[AlipayTapNetwork|碰一下]], [[OceanBase]], [[DigitalAnt|蚂蚁数科]], [[AntLingbo|蚂蚁灵波]], and [[AntInternational|蚂蚁国际]]. Its central synthesis is that Ant is looking for an agent-era position where personal life agents, health services, payment trust, offline touchpoints, enterprise data, physical-world AI, and global wallet networks reinforce one another.

## Key Claims
- The Bund Conference is presented as a more everyday and applied AI window than generic technology expos, because its exhibits connect AI to payments, folding clothes, tea, office agents, embodied intelligence, health, and merchant operations.
- The episode argues that [[AntGroup|Ant Group]]'s old "fintech company" label no longer captures the business after regulatory rectification, AI First strategy, health investment, enterprise technology, and global payments expansion.
- [[AntAbao|阿宝]] is framed as [[Alipay|Alipay]]'s service shelf turned into a [[PersonalLifeAgent|personal life agent]]: the user states intent and the system routes them toward services instead of making them search through app entrances.
- The episode says Ant's agent advantage comes from payment, orders, bills, mini-programs, and service connections, while noting that unrealized desire often originates on WeChat, Xiaohongshu, Douyin, and other attention platforms.
- [[AntAfu|阿福]] is treated as a strategic health entrance rather than a simple medical chatbot, because insurance codes, appointments, medical digitization, Haodf assets, village-doctor assistance, and long-term records all point toward [[AIHealthAgent|AI health agents]].
- [[AgentPaymentInfrastructure|AI payment]] is presented as the agent-era extension of Ant's trust, risk-control, dispute, refund, authorization, and liability experience: agents need scoped permission, visible process, traceable results, and anti-fraud controls before they can search, decide, order, pay, and fulfill for users.
- [[AlipayTapNetwork|碰一下]] is described as a dense offline interaction network with tens of millions of touchpoints and high daily transaction volume, giving merchant agents such as 小语 a possible physical base for [[OfflineAgentNetwork|offline agent networks]].
- The organizational split of [[AntInternational|蚂蚁国际]], [[OceanBase]], [[DigitalAnt|蚂蚁数科]], and [[AntLingbo|蚂蚁灵波]] is read as market-pricing and talent-incentive work that turns formerly internal assets into independently valued AI and technology businesses.
- [[OceanBase]] is framed as moving from financial-grade distributed database infrastructure toward [[AIDatabaseContextLayer|AI database context infrastructure]], where structured, unstructured, and vector data have to support agent context and enterprise sovereign AI needs.
- [[AntInternational|Ant International]] is presented as a global wallet and risk-control network through Alipay+ and WorldFirst/万里汇 rather than only an outbound Chinese-tourist payment tool.
