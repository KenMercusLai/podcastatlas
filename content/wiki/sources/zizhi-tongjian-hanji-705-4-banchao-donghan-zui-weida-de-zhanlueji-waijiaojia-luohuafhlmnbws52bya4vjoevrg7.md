# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》705-4丨班超：东汉最伟大的战略级外交家

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode synthesizes [[BanChaoEasternHan|班超]]'s late Western Regions strategy from the [[ShuleKingdom|Shule]] crisis through the campaigns and diplomacy that restored Han influence. Its durable contribution is a strategic capstone: Ban Chao identifies [[QiuciKingdom|Qiuci]] and [[YanqiKingdomLateHan|Yanqi]] as key nodes, uses local supplies and allied polities, defeats [[ShacheKingdomLateHan|Shache]] by misdirection, checks the [[Yuezhi|Yuezhi/Kushan]] attack through supply denial, and then preserves the settlement through restrained diplomacy and light-touch governance.

## Key Claims

- After stabilizing a Shule crisis, Ban Chao frames the Western Regions problem as a key-node strategy: Qiuci is the principal anti-Han force, and Yanqi is the second remaining hinge.
- Ban Chao's 80 CE memorial treats local resources and local legitimacy as strategic assets: Shule, Shache, Kangju, Khotan, and other aligned states can supply and staff operations while Qiuci-backed Gumo and Wensu rulers are vulnerable because their local support is thin.
- [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Emperor Zhang]] backs Ban Chao with limited support rather than a massive expedition, sending [[XuGanEasternHan|Xu Gan]] and later pursuing Wusun contact while refusing to credit [[LiYiEasternHan|Li Yi / 李邑]]'s slander.
- In 87 CE, Ban Chao defeats Shache by letting enemy spies carry false-retreat information, pulling the relief force away, and striking the exposed Shache center.
- The source identifies the later Yuezhi power with Kushan and says Ban Chao defeats its large attack by waiting out the army's food problem, intercepting the Qiuci grain mission, and then choosing rapprochement rather than humiliating a distant great power.
- Northern Xiongnu defeat under [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]] weakens Qiuci's external support, enabling Qiuci's surrender and a broader Western Regions return to Han alignment.
- The episode closes Ban Chao's public profile through Dingyuan marquisate, productive and restrained Western Regions governance, diplomacy with farther states, and his 102 CE return to [[LuoyangLateHan|Luoyang]] shortly before death.

> "彻底安定西域" - the episode's frame for Ban Chao's post-Shule strategic turn.
