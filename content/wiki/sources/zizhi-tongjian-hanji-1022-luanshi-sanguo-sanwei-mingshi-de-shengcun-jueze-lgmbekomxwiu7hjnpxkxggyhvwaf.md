# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1022丨乱世三国，三位名士的生存抉择

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 197-198 CE transition immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1023-jiemi-caocao-yu-zhangxiu-de-bingfa-boyi-lphbnmfdu0x5w7zjra7k0o8cyel4|Hanji 1023]]. It uses [[DuXiLateHan|杜袭]], [[ZhaoYanLateHan|赵俨]], and [[FanQinLateHan|繁钦]] to show how displaced Yingchuan talent can accept [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s hospitality without mistaking it for final allegiance, then shifts to Zhao Yan's legal case against [[LiTongLateHan|李通]]'s wife's uncle as a [[RuleOverKinshipPower|law-over-kinship]] governance signal inside [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s camp. The closing setup has [[XunYou|荀攸]] advise waiting before attacking [[ZhangXiu|张绣]], and records the court order for [[DuanWeiLateHan|段煨]] to attack [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]].

## Key Claims
- The opening "藏拙" frame treats strategic silence as active self-protection: capable people should hide strength until timing makes action useful rather than exposing themselves for display.
- In 197 CE, the source's three Yingchuan refugees are normalized to [[DuXiLateHan|杜袭]], [[ZhaoYanLateHan|赵俨]], and [[FanQinLateHan|繁钦]]; they enter [[Jingzhou|荆州]] to survive disorder, not because Liu Biao is already their final ruler.
- Liu Biao receives the three as honored guests, but Du Xi warns Fan Qin not to keep showing brilliance before a host they do not judge capable of restoring order.
- After Cao Cao receives [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] and establishes [[Xudu|许都]], Zhao Yan judges Cao Cao as the viable actor, moves north with his family, and receives the Langling magistrate role.
- Zhao Yan's prosecution and execution of Li Tong's wife's uncle, together with Li Tong's refusal to plead private kinship against public duty, becomes a case where family pressure yields to camp law.
- Xun You's advice against an immediate Zhang Xiu campaign rests on supply and alliance timing: Zhang Xiu depends on Liu Biao's grain, so waiting or recruiting him may be stronger than forcing Zhang Xiu and Liu Biao together.
- Cao Cao does not follow Xun You's advice and attacks the [[Rangcheng|穰城]] branch anyway, creating the immediate context that Hanji 1023 develops into the Anzhong blocked-retreat and ambush sequence.

## Key Quotes
> "巧藏于浊，用晦而明" - opening formula for hiding capacity without losing clarity.

> "如龙潜身渊，待时飞舞" - image used for Du Xi's warning that talent should wait for the right moment.

> "法不必亲" - the episode's shorthand for refusing private affection as an override of law.
