# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》261｜四十万人的战争，为何打了场寂寞？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the [[MayiAmbushWesternHan|马邑之谋]] arc begun in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-260-mayi-zhimou-hanwudi-fanji-xiongnu-diyizhan-luji7sarjbc2y4l8ljsnilld8xjb|Hanji 260]]. [[JunchanChanyu|军臣单于]] reportedly becomes suspicious because the approach to [[Mayi|马邑]] is implausibly empty, captures a Han censor at a watch post, learns of the ambush, and withdraws before the massed armies fight.

[[WangHuiWesternHan|王恢]], positioned forward with thirty thousand troops to strike the Xiongnu rear and baggage, decides that attacking the retreating main force without timely support would destroy his unit. [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] nevertheless has him condemned after the campaign produces no result. The episode interprets Wang's death as more than individual liability: it is presented as a visible command signal that reprices caution, declares the end of accommodation, and pressures later generals to fight rather than remain inactive. That organizational-effect claim, the assignment of responsibility, and the motives attributed to Wudi remain source-scoped interpretations.

## Key Claims

- The lure becomes self-defeating when an unusually empty route and unattended livestock make Junchan Chanyu suspect a trap.
- A captured Han censor reportedly discloses the ambush plan and deployment, allowing the Xiongnu force to withdraw before entering the kill zone.
- Wang Hui declines to attack because his thirty-thousand-man forward detachment would face a much larger force without assured support.
- The episode treats Wang's decision as tactically intelligible while arguing that Wudi judges visible inaction unacceptable after nationwide mobilization.
- A twenty-four-wan-liang gold payment to [[TianFenWesternHan|田蚡]] and an appeal routed through [[EmpressDowagerWangWesternHan|王太后]] reportedly fail to change Wudi's decision.
- The host interprets Wang's coerced suicide as an exemplary punishment meant to close pro-accommodation debate and make aggressive execution the safer career choice for later commanders.
- Continued frontier raiding and continued border-market access show that military rupture does not eliminate material exchange between Han and Xiongnu.

## Key Quotes

> “眼睁睁看着匈奴大军逃跑。” - the episode's rendering of Wudi's charge that Wang Hui allowed the target to leave without even attacking its rear.

> “杀一儆百。” - the host's summary of the deterrent purpose attributed to Wang Hui's punishment.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with current wiki content was found. Hanji 260 intentionally stopped before execution and outcome; this episode completes that open arc by supplying discovery, withdrawal, non-pursuit, punishment, and later interpretation.
- Force-count caution: Hanji 260 says the chanyu committed ten thousand cavalry toward the bait, while this episode describes a ten-wan-person Xiongnu force against three hundred thousand Han troops. The figures may refer to different stages or may be source variation; they are preserved rather than silently reconciled.
- Responsibility caution: the episode calls the plan nearly perfect and places the direct trigger on the captured censor's disclosure, yet it also shows that the deliberately emptied route helped create suspicion. Wang Hui's death therefore does not establish that he caused the operation's failure.
- Motive caution: the claim that Wudi killed Wang to repair prestige, silence accommodation advocates, and condition later generals is the host's political interpretation, not a demonstrated private intention.
- The captured censor's identity, watch-post sequence, “天王” title, exact deployments, support timing, bribe amount, judicial procedure, dialogue, suicide circumstances, later commander behavior, continuing market policy, and causal effect of the punishment remain episode-attributed pending direct textual comparison.
