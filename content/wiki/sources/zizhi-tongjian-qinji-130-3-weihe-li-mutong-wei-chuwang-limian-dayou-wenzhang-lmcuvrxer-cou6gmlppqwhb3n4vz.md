# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》130-3｜为何立牧童为楚王，里面大有文章

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-130-2-lishi-jilu-shouci-tucheng-lm4kuzdpnqn-hicyuvvb4p2wdudm|Qinji 130-2]] after [[ChenSheng|陈胜]]'s death becomes confirmed inside the anti-Qin camp. [[FanZeng|范增]] persuades [[XiangLiang|项梁]] that rebellion needs old Chu royal legitimacy, leading Xiang Liang to find the shepherd [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]] and install him as a new Chu Huai Wang. The episode then follows [[ZhangLiang|张良]]'s restoration of [[HanCheng|韩成]], [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]]'s continuing battlefield strength, and the way Qi succession conflict makes Xiang Liang's planned Qi-Zhao-Chu joint attack fail.

## Key Claims
- [[XiangLiang|项梁]] receives secret confirmation that [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] has died and convenes a meeting at Xue County, with [[LiuBang|刘邦]] present.
- [[FanZeng|范增]] argues that Chen Sheng's failure is unsurprising because he did not restore a [[ChuState|楚国]] royal descendant, but made himself king.
- Fan Zeng uses [[ChuHuaiwang|old Chu Huai Wang]]'s death in Qin captivity and [[ChuThreeHouseholdsAntiQinMemory|楚虽三户式反秦记忆]] to explain why Chu resentment can still mobilize anti-Qin legitimacy.
- Xiang Liang accepts the advice, searches among the people, and finds [[MiXin|芈心]], a grandson of Chu Huai Wang who is living as a shepherd.
- Xiang Liang installs Mi Xin as Chu Huai Wang in the sixth month, using the old ruler's name and reputation to increase the Xiang army's appeal.
- [[ChenYing|陈英 / 陈婴]] is made pillar of state and receives five counties while accompanying Mi Xin to Xuyi.
- Xiang Liang calls himself Wu Xin Jun; the episode reads the title together with his destruction of [[QinJiaRebel|秦嘉]] and [[JingJu|景居 / 景驹]] as a claim to be Chen Sheng's rightful anti-Qin successor.
- [[ZhangLiang|张良]] recommends restoring the old Han royal line through [[HanCheng|韩成]], and Xiang Liang appoints Han Cheng as Han king with Zhang Liang as situ.
- Zhang Liang and Han Cheng lead a small force west, recover several old [[HanState|韩国]] cities, lose them again to Qin, and continue around Yingchuan as a guerrilla force.
- [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] attacks [[WeiJiu|魏咎]] at Linji; [[ZhouShiRebel|周市]] asks Qi and Chu for aid, but Zhang Han defeats the relief force by surprise, killing [[TianDanQinRebel|田儋]] and Zhou Shi.
- Wei Jiu negotiates to spare Linji's civilians and then burns himself to death.
- [[WeiBao|魏豹]] escapes to Chu, where [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]] gives him troops to try to recover Wei territory.
- [[TianRong|田荣]] gathers defeated Qi troops at Dong'e, is pursued by Zhang Han, and is rescued when Xiang Liang attacks Zhang Han and wins at Dong'e.
- Xiang Liang sends [[XiangYu|项羽]] and [[LiuBang|刘邦]] toward Chengyang, where the city is taken and massacred; then Xiang Liang continues pressing Zhang Han around Puyang.
- After [[TianRong|田荣]] returns to Qi, he rejects [[TianJia|田假]]'s accession, drives him to Chu, and makes [[TianDanQinRebel|田儋]]'s son king while serving as chancellor himself and naming [[TianHeng|田横]] general.
- When Xiang Liang asks Qi and Zhao to join a decisive attack on Zhang Han, Tian Rong makes participation conditional on Chu killing Tian Jia and Zhao killing [[TianJiao|田角]] and [[TianJianQiGeneral|田间]].
- Chu and Zhao refuse to kill men who have fled to them, so Tian Rong refuses to send Qi troops; the anti-Qin coalition loses a chance to concentrate against Zhang Han.

## Key Quotes
> "楚虽三户，亡秦必楚" - Fan Zeng's slogan for Chu's usable anti-Qin memory.

> "使用楚怀王这个名号" - the episode's explanation of why Mi Xin's title matters politically.

> "全凭自己的本事" - the abrupt closing fragment's praise of Zhao Gao, treated here as a next-topic hook.
