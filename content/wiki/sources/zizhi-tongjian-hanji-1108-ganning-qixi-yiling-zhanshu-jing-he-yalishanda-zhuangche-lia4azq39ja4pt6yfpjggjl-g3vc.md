# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1108丨甘宁奇袭夷陵，战术竟和亚历山大撞车？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens the post-Red-Cliffs struggle around [[JianglingLateHan|江陵]]. [[CaoCao|曹操]] leaves [[CaoRen|曹仁]] and [[XuHuangCaoGeneral|徐晃]] to hold the corridor, while [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] and Cheng Pu confront them; the source presents Jiangling as a military, fiscal, political, and upstream-expansion hinge for [[SunQuan|孙权]] and [[LiuBei|刘备]].

The tactical center is [[GanNing|甘宁]]'s small-force seizure of [[YilingLateHan|夷陵]]. The episode compares his use of local terrain knowledge, selected troops, speed, and surprise with [[AlexanderTheGreat|亚历山大大帝]]'s night ascent at [[SogdianRock|粟特岩堡]], abstracting both as [[UnexpectedApproachFortressAssault|意外进路式要塞突袭]]: avoid the strongest frontage, appear from a route defenders treat as impossible, and let physical penetration become psychological collapse.

## Key Claims
- After Red Cliffs, Cao Cao leaves Cao Ren and Xu Huang at Jiangling and strengthens Hefei under Zhang Liao before withdrawing his main force north.
- Jiangling anchors the middle Yangtze, local political alignment, economic resources, Cao-side legitimacy in the south, and possible movement toward Yizhou.
- Gan Ning proposes taking Yiling with several hundred selected troops while the main armies remain locked around Jiangling.
- The episode attributes his success to local knowledge, troops suited to mountain and river fighting, speed, and surprise.
- At Sogdian Rock, the source says Alexander sends three hundred experienced climbers up an apparently impassable cliff at night, making their dawn appearance sufficient to induce surrender.
- The comparison argues that selected troops can offset fortification and numerical disadvantage when specialized movement and psychological shock reinforce one another.

> "面对险要堡垒，精锐小队、非常规路线和心理震慑，有时比正面投入大军更有效。" - the source summary's tactical synthesis.
