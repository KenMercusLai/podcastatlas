# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》989丨宁为太平犬，不做乱世人

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate prelude to [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-990-lijue-guosi-shengsi-yuanjia-weihe-turan-lianshou-lmop5mcrsjuq-ixuv0sfho5mvc-f|Hanji 990]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-991-zhengduo-xiandi-zhizhan-shui-neng-zuizhong-qusheng-lkbsl5uzqxouzm6mlewrnuns0x6p|Hanji 991]]. In 195 CE, [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] is granted only ceremonial room to maneuver while [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]], [[ZhangJiLateHan|张济]], [[YangDingLateHan|杨定]], [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]], and [[DongChengLateHan|董承]] struggle over his route. The episode also links the emperor's desperate eastward movement to [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s rise at [[YongqiuLateHan|雍丘]] and to [[DuanWeiLateHan|段煨]]'s contested supply effort at [[HuayinLateHan|华阴]].

## Key Claims
- The court's grants to Zhang Ji, Guo Si, Yang Ding, Yang Feng, and Dong Cheng show [[MingqiLegitimacy|名器合法性]] under collapse: titles still matter, but they do not restore the emperor's control over armed escorts.
- [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]] resists the move toward Hongnong because he reads the emperor's route as control over a political asset; [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] can answer only with explanation and refusal to eat.
- At Xinfeng, Guo Si's plan to force the emperor back west fails after Zhong Ji warns Yang Ding, Dong Cheng, and Yang Feng; Guo Si then flees into Zhongnan without his army.
- [[CaoCao|曹操]] surrounds Yongqiu, [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] tries to leave for [[YuanShu|袁术]], and [[LiuYiLateHan|刘翊]] kills Zhang Miao before he can join Cao Cao's enemy.
- Cao Cao's appointment as Yanzhou governor makes the source a bridge between the emperor-flight sequence and Cao Cao's entry into the central late-Han competition.
- At [[HuayinLateHan|华阴]], [[DuanWeiLateHan|段煨]] prepares clothes, food, vehicles, and other supplies for the emperor and officials, but his old conflict with Yang Ding lets rumor turn assistance into suspected rebellion.
- [[YangBiao|杨彪]], Zhao Wen, Liang Shao, and other officials defend Duan Wei, yet the emperor still avoids entering Duan Wei's camp and spends the night outside because the armed rumor field is too dangerous.
- The host's "宁为太平犬，不做乱世人" frame is a source-scoped moral summary of late-Han disorder: even an emperor's survival can depend on hunger protest, fire escape, rumor management, and food access.

## Key Quotes
> "宁为太平犬，不做乱世人." - episode title and host frame.

> "不吃饭." - host's shorthand for Emperor Xian's desperate protest against Guo Si.
