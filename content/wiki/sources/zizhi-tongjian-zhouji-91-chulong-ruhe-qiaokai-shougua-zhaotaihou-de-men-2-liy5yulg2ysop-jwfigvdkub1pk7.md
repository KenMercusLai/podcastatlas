# 《资治通鉴·周纪》91丨触龙如何敲开守寡赵太后的门（2）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes [[ChuLong|触龙]]'s persuasion of [[ZhaoWeihou|赵威后]] after [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-91-chulong-ruhe-qiaokai-shougua-zhaotaihou-de-men-1-lmgd7kdl1nrfwcrtkqttumtlpqok|Zhouji 91 part 1]]. By drawing Zhao Weihou deeper into maternal feeling and then asking what happens to unmerited noble descendants after their protector dies, Chu Long turns the hostage demand into a question of long-term protection for [[ChangAnJunZhao|长安君]].

Zhao Weihou accepts the argument, Chang'an Jun goes to [[QiState|齐国]] as hostage, Qi sends aid, and [[QinState|秦国]] withdraws from [[ZhaoState|赵国]]. The episode then questions Chu Long's historical explanation: the result is effective, but the host argues that Warring States noble decline came mainly from centralizing reforms that weakened hereditary fiefs, not simply from a failure to earn personal military merit.

## Key Claims
- [[ChuLong|触龙]] finishes the persuasion by linking Zhao Weihou's love for [[ChangAnJunZhao|长安君]] to his future political standing after her death.
- The argument works because it follows [[EmotionalDeescalationPersuasion|情绪降温式说服]]: the emotional state is softened before the strategic demand appears directly.
- [[ZhaoWeihou|赵威后]]'s agreement resolves the immediate hostage-aid crisis. Chang'an Jun travels to Qi with more than one hundred carriages, Qi sends troops, and Qin withdraws.
- The host reads Zhao Weihou's weakness, congee diet, and "old woman" self-description through mourning protocol after [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]]'s death, not through extreme age.
- The episode separates persuasive effectiveness from explanatory accuracy: Chu Long's noble-decline argument is useful rhetoric, but may not be sound institutional history.
- In the host's reading, Warring States hereditary aristocrats lose secure fief transmission because strong states push centralizing reforms, making the case an extension of [[WarringStatesReformBacklash|战国变法反噬]] rather than only a lesson about personal merit.
- After the Zhao crisis, [[TianDan|田单]] commands Zhao troops against [[YanState|燕国]] and then [[HanState|韩国]], while [[QiXiangWang|齐襄王]] dies and [[QiWangJian|齐王建 / 田建]] succeeds under [[JunWangHou|君王后]]'s regency.

## Key Quotes
> "父母之爱子，则为之计深远" - Chu Long's parental-love principle in the episode's reading.

> "结果好，过程就一定对吗？" - host's transition from persuasion success to historical critique.

> "吃了没学历史的亏" - host's warning that weak historical understanding makes plausible rhetoric harder to test.
