# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》873丨他如何利用天象痛骂帝王？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills 166 CE immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-874-zhenjing-muzi-xiangcan-tianli-nanrong-lpr3eglbnwbve1clrttapw6k5zdq|Hanji 874]] and the first Party Prohibition trigger. It follows [[XiangKaiLateHan|襄楷]] submitting two memorials to [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]], using planets, mansions, frost, hail, extreme cold, the Yellow River turning clear, and the emperor's lack of an heir as a language of [[OmenCodedRemonstrance|天象灾异包装式进谏]].

The episode's core contribution is that omen discourse works as political criticism rather than neutral cosmology. Xiang Kai turns [[CelestialOmenPoliticalResponsibility|天象政治责任]], [[NaturalDisasterPoliticalOmen|自然灾害政治征兆]], and religious teachings around [[Laozi|老子]] and [[ShakyamuniBuddha|释迦牟尼佛]] into accusations about eunuch favor, harsh punishment, wrongful killing, ruler desire, and contradiction between imperial ritual and conduct.

## Key Claims

- Xiang Kai's first memorial translates star positions near the emperor and descendant-linked mansions into warnings about Huan's rule and lack of a son.
- Extreme cold, frost, hail, thunder, rain, and other anomalies are used to argue that the court has powerful corrupt actors and excessive punishment.
- The cases of Liu Zhi and Chen Jin are presented as examples of capable local officials endangered because Huan listens to eunuch slander.
- Xiang Kai ties wrongful killing and exterminated families, including the Li Yun and Du Zhong case, to dynastic and reproductive consequences.
- The Yellow River turning clear is not treated as simple auspiciousness; Xiang Kai reads it beside plague, anomalies, and usurpation anxiety, citing [[JingFang|京房]]'s omen tradition.
- The second memorial turns Huan's worship of Laozi and the Buddha against him, arguing that desire, killing, and harsh punishment contradict the restraint, quietude, and non-killing ideals he ritually honors.
- Huan does not execute Xiang Kai, but the court still sentences him to two years, showing that omen-coded criticism could be tolerated only within limits.
- The episode also presents Huan as the first Chinese emperor to personally favor Buddhism, with palace worship of Buddha and Laozi expanding Buddhist visibility and translation activity.

## Key Quotes

> "天虽然不说话，但会通过天上星星的变化传达意思" - the episode's summary of Xiang Kai's omen premise.

> "清心寡欲" - the shared moral standard Xiang Kai invokes against Huan's conduct.

> "黄河河水清澈" - the ambiguous sign that Xiang Kai refuses to treat as straightforwardly auspicious in the surrounding crisis.
