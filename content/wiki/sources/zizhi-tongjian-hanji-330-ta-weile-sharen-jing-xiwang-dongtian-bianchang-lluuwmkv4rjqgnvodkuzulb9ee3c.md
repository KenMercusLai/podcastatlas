# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》330｜他为了杀人，竟希望冬天变长

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[YiZongWesternHan|义纵]] and [[WangWenshuWesternHan|王温舒]] through the violent local enforcement that preceded their promotion under [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]. Yi destroys [[NingChengWesternHan|宁成]]'s household and later kills more than four hundred prisoners, defendants, and visitors in Dingxiang; Wang recruits coercive local intermediaries, threatens noncompliant associates with old cases, and extends a Henei crackdown across more than one thousand households.

The episode's durable claim is institutional rather than biographical: visible quiet and rapid case approval can make mass violence legible as administrative success when superior power wants a coercive instrument. It therefore adds [[HarshOfficialInstrumentalRule|酷吏工具化统治]], while keeping the proposed blank authorization, exact approval logistics, killing totals, and seasonal-execution explanation source-scoped.

## Key Claims

- Yi Zong investigates Ning Cheng's family after taking office in Nanyang and destroys the household, showing one harsh official displacing another rather than ending coercive rule.
- In Dingxiang, Yi is said to have executed more than four hundred people in one day by combining prisoners and defendants with visiting relatives and friends.
- Wang Wenshu's earlier criminal background is followed by a governing method that recruits powerful or violent local men, protects cooperation, and revives old offenses against refusal.
- Wang's Guangping administration gains a road-safety reputation, but the episode interprets that order as fear-backed silence rather than proof of law-bound governance.
- After taking office in Henei, Wang arrests people across more than one thousand households and seeks collective execution, death, or confiscation penalties; the source says court approval returns within two or three days.
- Wang's wish for another month of winter is presented as evidence that killing has become a personal appetite as well as an official method.
- The host argues that neither official could conduct violence at this scale alone: court approval, personnel, communications, and political sponsorship convert brutality into promotion-worthy performance.
- Wang later attaches himself to [[ZhangTangWesternHan|张汤]], while Wudi's centralizing priorities make reputed severity useful to the court.

## Key Quotes

> “假如冬天再延长一个月，就足够我发挥了。” - the episode's paraphrase of Wang Wenshu regretting the end of the execution season.

> “酷吏看似手握生死，实际上只是当权黑手的工具。” - the host's institutional conclusion.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with existing wiki content was found. The episode adds Yi Zong's earlier mass-violence layer and Wang Wenshu's rise before later sources describe Yi's policy resistance and Wang's fall.
- The supplied document does not independently establish the exact victim and household totals, titles and office sequence, Ning Cheng case procedure, Wang's youth offenses, travel time, approval workflow, or seasonal execution rule.
