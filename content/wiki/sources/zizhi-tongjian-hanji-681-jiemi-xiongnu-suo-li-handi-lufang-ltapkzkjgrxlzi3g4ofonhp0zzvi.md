## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianwu 1 / 25 CE before the later [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-682-jiemi-gudai-dihuang-de-yongrenzhidao-lk1eq46vftaul1hazk2ggptrtvdv|Hanji 682]] Guangwu sequence. It uses [[WeiXiaoEasternHan|隗嚣]]'s Tianshui talent recruitment, [[MaYuanEasternHan|马援]]'s early border-life wealth ethics, and [[DouRongEasternHan|窦融]]'s Hexi self-preservation bloc to frame early Eastern Han as a fragmented regional field. The core claimant case is [[LuFangEasternHan|卢芳]], who invents Liu-family and Xiongnu ancestry, wins support around Anding/Sanshui, and is installed by [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] as a "Han emperor" after Liu Xuan falls.

The durable synthesis is that Jianwu 1 frontier politics turns legitimacy into usable infrastructure. Wei Xiao attracts scholars through courtesy, Dou Rong converts Hexi geography, troops, and local trust into a defensive alliance, and Xiongnu uses Lu Fang's claimed Han and Xiongnu genealogy as a proxy title against Liu Xiu's restoration order.

## Key Claims

- In 25 CE, the Xiongnu remain a watching northern power even after earlier Western Han pressure under [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] and [[WeiQing|卫青]].
- Wei Xiao returns to Tianshui, calls himself West State shangjiangjun, and builds prestige by receiving Sanfu refugees and literati with respectful treatment.
- Ma Yuan's early profile joins frontier hardship, late-blooming ambition, accumulated livestock and grain, and a principle that wealth matters because it can be distributed to kin and friends.
- Dou Rong chooses Hexi as a defensive base because his family knows the region, the Yellow River gives protection, Zhangye shuguo cavalry matters, and the five-commandery network can govern and train together.
- Lu Fang manufactures legitimacy by claiming descent from Han Wudi and from a Xiongnu royal line, then converts that story into Sanshui Qiang/Hu backing.
- After Liu Xuan's fall, Xiongnu treats Lu Fang's Liu-line claim as useful enough to install him as a rival "Han emperor" with cavalry support in Anding.

## Key Quotes

> "穷当益坚，老当益壮" - Ma Yuan's hardship-and-age self-description.

> "凡殖货财产，贵其能施赈也" - Ma Yuan's principle that accumulated wealth should be useful through distribution.

> "匈奴单于立芳为汉帝" - the endpoint of Lu Fang's sponsored claimant branch.
