# VOL.114心内科｜不就是高血压么？你别吓唬我了！不量血压就不高 长期吃药没必要？

## Summary
This [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode with [[SunZhijunCardiology|孙志军]] explains why one elevated reading is not identical to a confirmed hypertension diagnosis, yet feeling well or avoiding measurement does not remove risk. It connects [[HypertensionDiagnosisAndTargets|diagnosis and individualized targets]], [[HomeBloodPressureMeasurement|reliable home measurement]], [[AntihypertensiveMedicationAdherence|continuous treatment]], and [[HypertensionTargetOrganDamage|brain, heart, kidney, eye, vascular, and aortic harm]] into a long-term control system. The practical conclusion is that established measurement, lifestyle, medication, and follow-up tools are adequate for many patients, but misconceptions and poor adherence often prevent their effective use.

## Key Claims
- A single high reading can reflect exertion, emotion, fatigue, or measurement conditions; the episode distinguishes this from repeated elevation used to diagnose hypertension.
- [[HypertensionDiagnosisAndTargets]] should consider repeated readings, possible secondary causes, age, comorbid coronary, heart-failure, kidney, or diabetes risk, and clinician-set targets rather than one universal number.
- Certified mainstream electronic upper-arm monitors can support home care, while mercury devices require trained technique and both approaches remain vulnerable to positioning, cuff, rhythm, and calibration problems.
- [[HomeBloodPressureMeasurement]] is most useful when the person rests, avoids talking, supports the back and arm, keeps the arm near heart level, fits the cuff correctly, and records repeated readings rather than relying on one occasional clinic value.
- Hypertension may be asymptomatic while cumulative pressure damages the brain, heart, kidneys, eyes, arteries, and aorta; symptoms are therefore an unsafe substitute for measurement.
- Lifestyle measures and medication are complementary. Many patients need more than one drug, and a single-pill combination can reduce pill burden, but treatment initiation and selection remain individualized.
- [[AntihypertensiveMedicationAdherence]] means continuing clinician-guided treatment after readings improve rather than treating normalization as proof that medication is no longer needed.
- Laboratory follow-up can help detect comorbidity, organ function changes, and drug effects; pregnancy, older age, high cardiovascular risk, and unstable control change the monitoring and medication boundary.
- Controlled hypertension can be compatible with ordinary life and light-to-moderate aerobic activity, while extreme exercise, high-altitude travel, and wearable blood-pressure estimates require individual risk judgment.
