# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》251｜懵懂少年混成四朝元老，石奋的顶级家风（2）

## Summary

Despite its title, this short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode does not discuss 石奋 or family education. Its supplied body instead follows [[EmpressChenAjiaoWesternHan|陈阿娇]] from an accession-linked marriage and childlessness into lost favor after [[EmpressWeiZifuWesternHan|卫子夫]] enters the palace, while [[EmpressDowagerWangWesternHan|王太后]] urges the newly enthroned [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] not to confront [[LiuPiaoWesternHan|馆陶长公主刘嫖]] before his position is secure.

The most durable new contribution is the earliest rescue-and-promotion chain for [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]]. Liu Piao reportedly has him seized and marked for death as an indirect attack on Wei Zifu; [[GongsunAoWesternHan|公孙敖]] and companions rescue him, and Wudi responds by appointing and rewarding Wei Qing. The episode then briefly adds the creation of Maoling County and growing resentment between central officials and princely kingdoms, but it does not develop either subject enough to settle chronology, motive, or institutional effect.

## Key Claims

- Liu Piao's support for Liu Che's elevation and the marriage of her daughter Chen Ajiao to him make Chen's empress position part of an earlier political alliance, while Liu Piao's demands later burden the emperor.
- The episode attributes Chen's deteriorating position to eleven childless years, jealousy, attempts to control Wudi's other relationships, and increasingly dramatic self-harm threats after Wei Zifu gains favor.
- Wang warns Wudi that ministers are not yet fully obedient, Dowager Dou is already displeased by the Mingtang issue, and an open break with Liu Piao would expose him to punishment; Wudi therefore hides his resentment.
- Pingyang Princess presents household singer Wei Zifu to Wudi after his Bashang ritual journey, and Wei's new favor changes the palace balance.
- Liu Piao reportedly retaliates by seizing Wei Qing, but Gongsun Ao and companions rescue him before he is killed.
- Wudi answers the attack by appointing Wei Qing to Jianzhang Palace service and as an inner attendant, rewarding him, elevating Wei Zifu, and later making Wei Qing a senior palace official.
- The closing section says the court establishes Maoling County and that central officials' aggressive investigations of princely administrations deepen royal resentment.
