# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》677丨刘玄帝当年真是一手好牌打稀烂

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianwu 1 / 25 CE immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-678-liupenzi-wode-huangdi-shi-zhuajiu-lai-de-lkamwdktxz0ksazh2h4am8fjmfiq|Hanji 678]] and the later [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-680-ming-sang-chimei-zhe-wei-qiyi-huangdi-maigu-hechu-lurtvfqerb0vf3a33msmd-txr9pv|Hanji 680]] Liu Xuan death layer. It follows [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]]'s newly proclaimed regime as [[DengYuEasternHan|邓禹]] defeats Gengshi forces in [[HedongCommandery|河东]], while [[ChimeiRebellion|赤眉]] pressure near Zheng and [[HuayinLateHan|华阴]] pushes [[LiuXuanGengshiEmperor|更始帝刘玄]]'s Chang'an commanders toward plunder, retreat, and armed rupture.

The durable synthesis is that Gengshi collapse begins before Chimei formally controls [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]]. Deng Yu's Hedong recovery sends military pressure back toward the capital; Zhang Ang's group no longer treats defending the regime as the priority; and Liu Xuan's attempted counter-trap against dissenting generals turns suspicion into palace fighting, killing Shentu Jian while letting [[WeiXiaoEasternHan|隗嚣]] exit toward Tianshui.

## Key Claims

- In Jianwu 1 / 25 CE, Liu Xiu has proclaimed himself emperor while Deng Yu is still trying to take [[Anyi|安邑]] in Hedong from Gengshi forces.
- Fan Can crosses from Dayang and is defeated and killed by Deng Yu south of Xie, giving Deng Yu a first local win in the Hedong field.
- Wang Kuang, Cheng Dan, Liu Jun, and others gather a much larger force against Deng Yu; Deng Yu first loses, then regroups after the enemy misses an attack window and defeats them the next day, killing Liu Jun and Hedong taishou Yang Bao while Wang Kuang flees to Chang'an.
- In Chang'an, Zhang Ang's group fears Chimei forces near Zheng and Huayin and proposes plundering the capital before retreating toward Nanyang or becoming mountain-and-marsh bandits.
- Liu Xuan refuses the plunder-and-retreat proposal and sends Wang Kuang, Chen Mu, Cheng Dan, and Zhao Meng to Xinfeng, while Li Song remains in Chang'an to defend the city.
- Zhang Ang, Liao Zhan, Hu Yin, Shentu Jian, and Wei Xiao plan to seize Liu Xuan during an autumn ancestral rite; Liu Xuan learns of the plan and summons them into the palace intending to kill them.
- Wei Xiao avoids the summons by claiming illness, gathers Wang Zun, Zhou Zong, and troops for self-defense, and escapes the crisis; Zhang Ang, Liao Zhan, and Hu Yin flee, Shentu Jian is killed, rebels burn the palace gate, and Liu Xuan flees to Zhao Meng at Xinfeng.
- Liu Xuan later suspects Wang Kuang, Chen Mu, Cheng Dan, and Zhang Ang; he kills Chen Mu and Cheng Dan, after which Wang Kuang joins Zhang Ang inside Chang'an.

## Key Quotes

> "先掠城中财物，再南还南阳" - the plunder-and-retreat proposal in the source.

> "有变" - the suspicion that sends Zhang Ang, Liao Zhan, and Hu Yin fleeing the palace.

> "一手好牌打稀烂" - the title-level judgment on Liu Xuan's lost position.
