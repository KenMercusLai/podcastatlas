- Industrial documentary production adds research, shot planning, bounded field periods, budgets, distributed teams, and editorial consistency to individual observation; authorship moves toward governing method and collaboration.
- Food documentary widened audience and commercial support because it can combine sensory pleasure, people, knowledge, history, and culture, but popularity also exposes work to parody, platform pressure, and meanings the creator cannot control.
