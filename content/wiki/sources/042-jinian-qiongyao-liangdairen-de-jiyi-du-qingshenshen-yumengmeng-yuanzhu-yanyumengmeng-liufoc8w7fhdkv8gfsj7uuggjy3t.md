# 042 纪念·琼瑶|两代人的记忆：读《情深深雨濛濛》原著《烟雨濛濛》

## Summary

This [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] memorial episode rereads [[YanYuMengMeng|《烟雨濛濛》]] as more than a love triangle: revenge passes from Lu Zhenhua through Yiping, Shuhuan, and other family members, while the father-daughter movement from hatred toward care becomes the novel's deeper emotional line. Comparing the novel with [[QingShenShenYuMengMeng|《情深深雨濛濛》]], the hosts argue that successful adaptation can change setting, characterization, and outcome when the revised ending grows from a newly built character arc. The discussion also places [[QiongYao|琼瑶]] within generational reading memory, classical-poetry aesthetics, changing attitudes toward tragedy, and a carefully qualified conversation about autonomy at the end of life.

## Key Claims

- Qiong Yao and Jin Yong formed complementary landmarks in mainland readers' encounter with Hong Kong and Taiwan popular literature, while television circulation often preceded and prompted novel reading.
- Titles, imagery, and songs drawn from classical poetry helped Qiong Yao translate literary atmosphere into accessible modern emotional language.
- [[YanYuMengMeng|《烟雨濛濛》]] builds melodramatic conflict through a causal chain: Yiping's poverty and humiliation motivate revenge, revenge contaminates love, Shuhuan retaliates through Rǔping, and the family collapse gives each later injury additional force.
- The novel's central relationship is arguably Yiping and Lu Zhenhua rather than only Yiping and Shuhuan. Aging, illness, loneliness, gifts, and small domestic acts gradually expose the vulnerable person inside paternal authority without erasing Lu's violence or possessiveness.
- Lu Zhenhua's lost first love explains his repeated pursuit of women who resemble Pingping, but the hosts disagree over whether that backstory deepens him or excuses domination after the fact.
- Rǔping's death follows not only romantic rejection but family collapse, abandonment, and entrenched inferiority; the episode nevertheless assigns Shuhuan substantial responsibility for offering marriage as retaliation and then withdrawing it.
- [[RevengeAsSelfDefeatingInheritance|self-defeating inherited revenge]] links Lu Zhenhua, Xueyi, Yiping, Shuhuan, and Mengping: each attempt to answer injury produces additional harm rather than the promised restoration.
- [[QingShenShenYuMengMeng|《情深深雨濛濛》]] changes the setting, adds characters, strengthens Rǔping's agency, and gives her a survivable wartime path; the hosts judge that brighter ending as earned by the revised characterization rather than automatically inferior to tragedy.
- The closing discussion refuses a universal judgment about Qiong Yao's death, distinguishes her message to younger people from her own late-life choice, and treats dignity, fear of incapacity, and a lifelong preoccupation with death as possible rather than settled explanations.

## Key Quotes

> “晚晚类卿” - the episode's shorthand for Lu Zhenhua seeking fragments of Pingping in later wives.

> “认清爱情之后，仍然选择去爱” - the closing formulation of love as worthwhile without denying its risks.
