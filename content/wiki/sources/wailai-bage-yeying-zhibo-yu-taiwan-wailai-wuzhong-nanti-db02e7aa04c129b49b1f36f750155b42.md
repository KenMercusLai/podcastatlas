# 「人人喊打」的八哥，與台灣的「外來物種」難題

## Summary
This [[DuanwenNewsPodcast|端闻 / 端传媒新闻播客]] episode uses a Taiwan [[TaiwanNighthawk|nightjar]] nest livestream, where a [[CommonMynaTaiwan|common myna]] pecked an unhatched egg, to examine how introduced species can become moral enemies in public discussion. [[XuZhengfu|許正甫]] and [[FengMengjie|馮孟傑]] distinguish native, alien, and invasive-species definitions from hatred-based labeling, then argue for evidence-based [[TaiwanMynaManagement|Taiwan myna management]] that considers risk, habitat change, cost, and animal welfare.

## Key Claims
- The nightjar livestream turned one visible egg incident into a public anger cycle, but the source keeps causality qualified because the second egg already showed hatching problems before the myna pecked it.
- [[InvasiveSpeciesMoralization]] is the episode's central public-discourse warning: alien-species status can become a moral label even when ecological impact has not been carefully assessed.
- Native, alien, and invasive status depend on time boundaries, spatial boundaries, human-mediated movement, and demonstrated harm; they are not simple categories of good and bad species.
- [[CommonMynaTaiwan|Common myna]] and [[WhiteVentedMynaTaiwan|white-vented myna]] are clearly human-introduced alien birds in Taiwan, while [[CrestedMynaTaiwan|crested myna]] is treated by current Taiwan ornithological consensus as native despite past boundary debate.
- Taiwan currently lacks detailed evidence showing that introduced mynas directly caused native bird decline; the episode compares direct-driver explanations with [[DriverPassengerInvasionModel|passenger-model]] explanations tied to urbanization and habitat change.
- Management should be differentiated: early detection and rapid removal in new invasion zones, local control in sensitive habitats or breeding areas, long-term monitoring and habitat improvement where populations are already established, and humane professional removal where killing is necessary.
- Public education should explain why a life is being removed, if removal is justified, rather than relying on hatred to lower ethical resistance.

## Key Quotes
> "外來種身份只是對物種來源的描述" - the episode's boundary between origin classification and moral judgment.

> "為何而殺" - Feng Mengjie's ethical test for any lethal management choice.
