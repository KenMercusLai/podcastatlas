# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》156｜兵仙韩信的封神之战（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens the [[HanXin|韩信]] versus Zhao campaign after the West Wei victory in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-155-2-zhuan-gei-ni-piqi-da-de-pengyou-weiwang-panhan-jing-shi-liubang-ai-maren-ls5nvunmhakvefd2oxn4zdyoob4l|Hanji 155-2]]. It frames the coming "背水一战" through unfavorable preconditions: Han Xin and [[ZhangEr|张耳]] must pass through [[JingxingPass|井陉口]] with a mixed, supply-constrained army, while [[ZhaoXie|赵歇]] and [[ChenYu|陈馀]] hold terrain and numerical advantage. The central decision is [[LiZuoChe|李左车]]'s rejected proposal to cut Han supply lines while Zhao refuses battle from fortified positions.

## Key Claims
- In the tenth month of Han Gaozu's third year, 204 BCE, Han Xin and Zhang Er lead several tens of thousands east against restored [[ZhaoState|赵国]].
- Zhao Xie and Chen Yu prepare at Jingxing with a claimed 200,000 troops, intending to strike when the Han army exits the pass.
- Jingxing is treated as a severe military chokepoint: the road is narrow, extended, difficult for supply, and suitable for ambush.
- Han Xin stops thirty li from the eastern mouth of Jingxing, lets the troops eat and rest, then moves secretly at midnight with flags lowered and drums silent.
- Li Zuoche diagnoses Han's soft point as logistics: Han Xin and Zhang Er are far from their base, their grain must travel a long route, and the pass will stretch the marching column.
- Li Zuoche asks for 30,000 men to move by side paths and cut the Han supply line, while Chen Yu entrenches and refuses battle; he expects Han Xin and Zhang Er to be trapped within ten days.
- The source treats Li Zuoche's plan as feasible, partly because Jingxing had already been hard to break in an earlier Qin-Zhao setting.
- Chen Yu rejects the plan by invoking a "righteous army" posture and refusing deception or stratagem, while also fearing that avoiding direct attack would make other lords see Zhao as timid.
- The episode supplements [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] with [[Shiji|《史记》]]'s fuller wording around Chen Yu's "义兵不用诈谋奇计" stance, making this a source-comparison case.
- The transcript stops after Zhao troops see Han red flags in their camp, so the full backwater battle sequence remains source-scoped to later material.

## Key Quotes
> "背水一战" - the idiom used to introduce the campaign.

> "义兵不用诈谋奇计" - the Shiji wording the episode uses to explain Chen Yu's stated refusal of stratagem.

> "战马不能并排而行" - the source's description of Jingxing's narrow road condition.
