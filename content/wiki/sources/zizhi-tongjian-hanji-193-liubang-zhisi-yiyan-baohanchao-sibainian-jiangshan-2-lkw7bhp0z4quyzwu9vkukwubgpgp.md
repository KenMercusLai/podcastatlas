# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》193｜刘邦之死，遗言保汉朝四百年江山（2）

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the [[FanKuai|樊哙]] execution-order branch opened in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-193-liubang-zhisi-yiyan-baohanchao-sibainian-jiangshan-1-loppqvgdajwnfwjx4cd-v9itdji7|Hanji 193 part 1]]. [[ChenPing|陈平]] and [[ZhouBo|周勃]] arrest Fan Kuai but transport him toward the capital instead of carrying out [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s irreversible order; after Liu Bang dies, Chen Ping reaches the palace first, publicly mourns, and asks to guard the coffin, while Fan Kuai is later released and restored.

The second branch follows [[LuZhi|吕雉]]'s move against [[QiJi|戚姬 / 戚夫人]] and [[LiuRuyi|刘如意]]. [[ZhouChang|周昌]] repeatedly blocks summons to the young Zhao king, but Lu Zhi recalls Zhou Chang first and then renews the order; [[LiuYing|刘盈]] meets Liu Ruyi and keeps him close, temporarily replacing official protection with personal proximity. The episode ends before the poisoning and human-swine sequence narrated in Hanji 194 part 1.

## Key Claims

- Chen Ping and Zhou Bo judge that immediate execution would expose them if Liu Bang reversed his anger, so they convert the order into arrest, custody, and transport.
- Zhou Bo remains with the army while Chen Ping escorts Fan Kuai, preserving frontier command continuity as the lethal decision is deferred upward.
- After hearing of Liu Bang's death, Chen Ping goes directly to the palace rather than following a reported order to proceed to Yingyang; public mourning and a request to guard the coffin place him inside the new court before hostile accusations can settle.
- Lu Zhi reportedly appoints Chen Ping langzhongling and tutor to Liu Ying, while Fan Kuai is released and regains rank and fief after reaching the capital.
- Lu Zhi humiliates Qi Ji through confinement, shaving, restraints, prison clothing, and forced grain pounding; the episode links Qi Ji's song about her son to the renewed summons of Liu Ruyi.
- Zhou Chang refuses repeated summons by invoking Liu Bang's entrustment and openly stating that he fears Lu Zhi intends to kill Liu Ruyi.
- Lu Zhi overcomes the protection arrangement by recalling Zhou Chang to Chang'an before summoning Liu Ruyi again, showing that a single protector can be neutralized separately from the protected person.
- Liu Ying meets Liu Ruyi and keeps him beside him day and night, temporarily preventing Lu Zhi from acting but leaving safety dependent on uninterrupted personal proximity.
