# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》503丨给王家长脸！揭秘西汉势力最大的外戚

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianshi 1-2 / 32-31 BCE under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]], before the later Wang-family warning and harem-pressure notes. It opens with the historical danger of outer relatives, then follows Chengdi's early enfeoffment of Wang-family maternal uncles, court criticism through yellow-fog omen language, [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]]'s attempted resignation, and [[XueXuanWesternHan|薛宣]]'s memorial on harsh local administration and failed regional inspection.

The durable synthesis is that Chengdi's reign begins with two linked pressure fields: Wang-family elevation is recognized immediately as a structural outer-relative risk, while disaster interpretation also becomes a way to criticize officials, local governance, social trust, and ritual policy. The episode then moves through sacrifice-site reform, tax reduction, Yanling construction, and the establishment of [[EmpressXuWesternHan|许皇后]], making early Chengdi politics a mix of kinship empowerment, omen-coded criticism, administrative correction, and ritual adjustment.

## Key Claims

- The host frames outer-relative power as a repeated Han danger: imperial in-laws can begin as helpers during youth or weakness and grow into internal dynastic threats.
- In Jianshi 1 / 32 BCE, Chengdi enfeoffs his maternal uncle Wang Chong as Ancheng Hou and grants Wang Tan, Wang Shang, Wang Li, Wang Gen, and Wang Fengshi the rank of Guannei Hou, publicly strengthening the Wang-family bloc.
- Yellow fog becomes a court-criticism opening: Yang Xing, Shi Dan, and others invoke Gaozu's principle that non-meritorious men should not be enfeoffed and criticize the excessive honoring of dowager relatives.
- Wang Feng reacts to the memorial by asking to retire and resign, but Chengdi refuses and comforts him, showing that criticism does not yet reduce Wang-family power.
- Xue Xuan's memorial accepts disaster language but moves the diagnosis toward governance: cruel administration, uncoordinated instruction, regional-inspector misconduct, private profit, and harassment of counties and commoners.
- Xue argues that bad local governance damages social trust, kinship feeling, hospitality, relief practices, and the circulation of human relations, using yin-yang language to connect administration and social order.
- Chengdi accepts Xue's advice, while subsequent unusual signs, sacrifice-site construction, abolition of older Ganquan/Fenyin/Yong ritual facilities, tax reduction, Yanling construction, and northern/southern suburban sacrifices show early-reign ritual and policy repair.
- Chengdi establishes Xu as empress in Jianshi 2 / 31 BCE, tying the episode's outer-relative theme to later harem and consort-family politics.

## Key Quotes

> "非功臣不得封侯" - the Gaozu precedent used against Wang-family enfeoffment.

> "给王家长脸" - the episode's title frame for Chengdi's public elevation of his maternal relatives.
