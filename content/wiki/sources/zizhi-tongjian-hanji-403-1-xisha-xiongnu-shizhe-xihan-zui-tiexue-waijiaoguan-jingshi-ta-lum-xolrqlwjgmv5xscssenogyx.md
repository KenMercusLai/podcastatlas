# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》403-1｜袭杀匈奴使者，西汉最铁血外交官竟是他！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Yuanfeng 4 / 77 BCE under [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|汉昭帝刘弗陵]], between the earlier [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-401-suihong-zhiming-hanzhaodi-tuiwei-rangxian-huoguang-zaile-ll6pyzpjl0mlcfn0g1zjot2wf8s8|Hanji 401]] regency layer and later succession episodes. It begins with Han Zhao's capping ceremony, [[TianQianqiuWesternHan|田千秋]]'s death, and the burning of Emperor Wen's ancestral temple, then turns to [[FuJieziWesternHan|傅介子]] as a low-ranking horse official who volunteers for a dangerous Western Regions mission.

The durable synthesis is that Fu Jiezi's first mission makes small-party coercive diplomacy visible before his later Loulan assassination story. Facing [[LoulanKingdomWesternHan|楼兰]], [[QiuciKingdom|龟兹 / 丘辞]], and [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] influence, Fu uses imperial authority, intelligence from local rulers, and a surprise killing of Xiongnu envoys to restore Han deterrence without a large expedition. The episode ends as a prelude: renewed Western Regions disorder leads Fu to seek [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]]'s backing for a second, low-cost mission against Loulan.

## Key Claims

- In 77 BCE, Han Zhao is capped at seventeen, Tian Qianqiu dies as Fumin Hou, and the Emperor Wen temple fire leads to punishment of ritual officials after an amnesty avoids death sentences.
- Fu Jiezi begins from a modest military and horse-administration position, but reads a dangerous Western Regions mission as a chance to win merit and restore family glory.
- Loulan and other small Western Regions polities are presented as caught between Han and Xiongnu, often leaning toward Xiongnu because Xiongnu pressure is nearer and more immediate.
- Fu's first mission uses the pretext of retrieving Dayuan horses, direct rebuke of Loulan and Qiuci, and local intelligence about Xiongnu envoys.
- Fu kills the returning Xiongnu envoys with a small party at the relay station, turning envoy violence into an immediate warning for Western Regions rulers.
- The action wins a short-term submission signal from Loulan and a promotion for Fu, but later renewed attacks on Han envoys show that deterrence does not permanently solve the regional problem.
- Fu's second-mission proposal depends on both imperial trust and Huo Guang's support: he argues for a few elite soldiers plus valuables instead of a costly mass expedition.
