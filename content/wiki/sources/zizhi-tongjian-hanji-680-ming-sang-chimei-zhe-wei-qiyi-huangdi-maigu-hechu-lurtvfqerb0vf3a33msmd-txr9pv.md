## Summary

This episode backfills Jianwu 1 / 25 CE before the later Chimei-collapse notes. It contrasts [[ChimeiRebellion|赤眉]] disorder after [[LiuPenziEasternHan|刘盆子]] enters [[ChanglePalace|长乐宫]] with [[DengYuEasternHan|邓禹]]'s disciplined and capacity-aware Guanzhong strategy. It also records the killing, concealment, and Guangwu-ordered burial of [[LiuXuanGengshiEmperor|更始帝刘玄]], plus [[ZhaoXiEasternHan|赵熙 / 赵熹]]'s aid to Liu Xuan's relatives.

## Key Claims

- After Liu Penzi enters Changle Palace, Sanfu commandery and camp leaders send envoys, but Chimei troops rob goods and sometimes kill people, driving officials and civilians back toward local self-defense.
- Deng Yu's army wins support because it is disciplined, avoids harming civilians, and comforts submitters rather than only accepting nominal allegiance.
- Deng Yu refuses his generals' call for an immediate Chang'an attack because he has many attached people but few battle-ready troops, and lacks forward supply and rear transport.
- Deng Yu judges Chimei as temporarily rich in food and goods but strategically short-sighted, so he moves north toward Shangjun, Beidi, and Anding to gather provisions, rest, train, and wait for Chimei exhaustion.
- Liu Xiu's wider campaign continues in parallel through Cen Peng's Jingzhou assignment, Liu Yong's Suiyang imperial claim, and Liu Xiu's return to Luoyang.
- Sanfu sympathy for the deposed Liu Xuan makes him dangerous to Zhang Ang's group; the source says he is strangled at night under Chimei control.
- Liu Gong hides Liu Xuan's body, Liu Xiu later orders Deng Yu to bury him at Bali, and Zhao Xi provides money, food, and escort help for Liu Xuan's hungry relatives near Wuguan.

## Key Quotes

> "军纪严明、不欺负百姓" — the episode's explanation for why Deng Yu's army attracts daily submissions.

> "缺乏远见的强匪" — Deng Yu's source-scoped judgment that Chimei is strong in plunder but weak in durable strategy.

> "希望能将他从赤眉军手中救出来" — the Sanfu sympathy that makes Liu Xuan a continuing rallying figure.
