# Compassion Can Change the World | Kailash Satyarthi on Child Labour, AI and the Future of Humanity | Tomorrow Today

## Summary
This [[TomorrowToday]] episode has [[ShekharNatarajan]] interview [[KailashSatyarthi]] about child-labour rescue, consumer organizing, [[GoodWeave]], courage, institutional failure, artificial intelligence, and the proposed [[CompassionQuotient]]. A railway-station encounter after one successful rescue convinced Satyarthi that case-by-case intervention could not stop a replenishing trafficking system, leading him toward an international campaign and [[EthicalSupplyChainCertification]].

The conversation then generalizes that experience through [[TransformativeCompassion]]: suffering must be felt as morally connected to oneself and converted into mindful, accountable action against structural injustice. [[CompassionateAI]] applies the same demand across problem definition, data, algorithms, monitoring, repair, and deployment while preserving human dignity, relationships, and judgment against efficiency-only optimization.

## Key Claims
- A successful rescue did not alter the system replenishing child labour; durable intervention required consumer leverage, supply-chain monitoring, certification, and adult replacement labor.
- [[GoodWeave]], first called Rugmark, gave consumers an affirmative child-labour-free purchasing signal rather than relying only on protest or boycott.
- [[TransformativeCompassion]] differs from pity or episodic charity by joining awareness, connectedness, feeling, action, responsibility, and willingness to confront institutions.
- Compassionate leadership is presented as a cross-sector requirement because organizations can lose their founding moral purpose as hierarchy, resources, and self-preservation take priority.
- [[CompassionateAI]] asks builders to carry moral responsibility through the full AI lifecycle rather than treating regulation, abstract ethics, or technical efficiency as sufficient.
- [[CompassionQuotient]] proposes awareness, connectedness, feeling, and action as trainable dimensions of individual and institutional compassion.
- AI concentration, violent training data, automated moral decisions, and companion substitution are presented as risks to equality, dignity, relationships, and human agency.

## Key Quotes
> "mindful problem solving" - Satyarthi's condensed description of compassion joined to action.

> "power troika" - his term for the convergence of information or knowledge, wealth, and state power.

> "laboratory of compassion" - his description of an ashram that learns from children who survived abuse and slavery.
