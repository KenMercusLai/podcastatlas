# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1137丨陇南之战如何成为马超一生的污点？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the setup for the Ji resistance continued in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1138-luanshi-sanguo-zhaoang-de-zhongcheng-yu-xisheng-lmnxifntpnaat1q4wmx043joh8hg|Hanji 1138]]. After [[MaChao|马超]] takes Ji and kills the surrendering inspector [[WeiKangLateHan|韦康]], [[YangFuLateHan|杨阜]] and other nominal subordinates retain a revenge motive; Yang Fu uses leave for his wife's burial to recruit [[JiangXuLateHan|姜叙]], whose mother supports armed resistance rather than allowing family concern to block it.

Yang Fu and Jiang Xu then connect outside forces under [[ZhaoAngLateHan|赵昂]], Yin Feng, and Li Jun with insiders Zhao Qu and Liang Kuan. The episode closes when Ma Chao demands Zhao Ang's son Zhao Yue as a hostage and Zhao Ang asks [[WangYiZhaoAngWife|王异]] whether refusing would expose the plot. An opening list of ten kinds of people to avoid frames the history as a lesson about boundaries and broken trust, but its categorical personality judgments remain source-scoped advice rather than established general rules.

## Key Claims

- The opening advice treats privacy leakage, public aggression, envy, mistreatment of family, emotional volatility, repeated harm, chronic complaint, humiliating humor, petty exploitation, and recurring money requests as warning signs in friendship.
- The episode says Ma Chao killed Wei Kang after Wei Kang opened Ji and surrendered, leaving Yang Fu and more than ten colleagues outwardly subordinate but inwardly committed to revenge.
- Yang Fu obtains leave after his wife's death, meets his maternal cousin Jiang Xu during the burial period, and argues that the loss of Ji, Wei Kang's death, and local humiliation require action.
- Jiang Xu's mother supports the uprising and says that fear for her safety should not prevent her son from acting, removing a household obstacle to resistance.
- Yang Fu and Jiang Xu coordinate Zhao Ang, Yin Feng, and Li Jun outside Ji with Zhao Qu and Liang Kuan inside, turning personal revenge into a multi-node conspiracy.
- Ma Chao demands Zhao Yue as a hostage because Zhao Ang controls military force; refusal risks exposing the resistance plan, while compliance exposes the child to retaliation.
- The episode ends before Wang Yi's answer or the uprising's outcome, both of which are developed in Hanji 1138.
