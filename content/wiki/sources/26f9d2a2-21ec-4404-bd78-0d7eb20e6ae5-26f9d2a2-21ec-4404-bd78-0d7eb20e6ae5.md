# 189: Geodesic顾全全：预训练有点“无聊”了，但用scaling改变AI制药才刚开始

## Summary
This [[LateTalk]] interview follows [[GuQuanquan|顾全全]] from leading protein-model work at [[ByteDance]] into [[Doubao]] pretraining and then a new large-molecule drug-design venture. Its central thesis is that [[ScalingProteinDesign|protein-design scaling]] must join larger structure models, much more structural data, disciplined small-to-large experiments, and a self-operated wet-lab feedback loop; the same episode uses [[PretrainingReliabilityEngineering|pretraining reliability engineering]] to explain why frontier-model advantage depends on a complete technical plan, training stability, cross-team review, and execution rather than one secret. The supplied file is a structured Chinese episode summary, so performance, cost, market, and launch-timing claims remain attributed to the guest rather than independently verified.

## Key Claims
- [[GuQuanquan|顾全全]] says his ByteDance teams worked across [[ProteinLanguageModels|protein language models]], structure prediction, dynamic conformations, structure design, cryo-EM models, and later Doubao optimization and scaling.
- He distinguishes protein-structure prediction from design: prediction is an enabling step, while drug design must produce structures that bind a target and then survive experimental validation.
- [[ScalingProteinDesign|Scaling protein design]] is presented as a joint parameter-data-compute problem. The guest reports a prior roughly 900-million-parameter structure model trained on about 26.5 million structures and contrasts it with a claimed public pool near 1.3 billion structures, while proposing eventual tens-of-billions scale.
- Sequence-only protein modeling is treated as useful but subordinate to three-dimensional, potentially all-atom structure for binding-centered design; this is a source position rather than a settled universal hierarchy.
- A durable [[ExperimentGroundedProteinModelLoop|experiment-grounded loop]] requires both GPU infrastructure and wet-lab assays for binding, toxicity, synthesizability, and specificity, with measured results potentially serving as later reinforcement-learning rewards.
- Fair model comparison requires the same benchmark, metric, and candidate-sampling budget; generating hundreds of candidates and selecting the best cannot be compared directly with producing only a few.
- [[PretrainingReliabilityEngineering|Frontier pretraining]] is described as a cross-team system spanning architecture, data, optimization, scaling, and infrastructure, with loss spikes, gradient spikes, or stagnant downstream metrics requiring early diagnosis and clear decision authority.
- The guest says ByteDance prohibited distillation data in the relevant pretraining work when he left, combining compliance concerns with a strategic view that a would-be leader must learn to generate, filter, and organize high-quality data rather than depend on stronger models.
- He argues that academia's compute disadvantage makes frontier-scale model work difficult, while still crediting doctoral training for research taste, method, and innovation; unpublished industrial work creates a separate visibility and attribution cost.
