## Summary
This [[HowIBuiltThis]] Advice Line episode pairs [[GuyRaz]] with [[BobbiBrown]] of [[JonesRoadBeauty]] to advise founders from [[CabbageLabs]], [[AbbyRose]], and [[TannenOralCare]]. Brown's second-company experience after [[BobbiBrownCosmetics]] frames the advice around scrappy testing, product quality, founder storytelling, and avoiding expensive channel expansion before the core loop is clear. Its main wiki contribution is a consumer-brand case where [[ProductLedBrandLongevity]], [[FounderLedLiveSelling]], [[NicheOriginStoryRepositioning]], [[TrialSizeProduct]], [[MeasuredChannelTesting]], and [[RelationshipLedGrowth]] all help founders scale without damaging the trust that created early traction.

## Key Claims
- [[BobbiBrown]] says she launched [[JonesRoadBeauty]] in 2020 when her 25-year non-compete expired, after wanting cleaner makeup textures and more control than a large corporation allowed.
- Brown frames second-time founding as scrappier and more resource-conscious than her first beauty-company build, reinforcing [[SecondTimeFounderOperatingJudgment]].
- Brown says products that look like trends can last if founders keep improving the product, watch repeat purchase, and add new products deliberately.
- [[MarkSokolowski]] introduces [[CabbageLabs]] and [[CabDab]], a cabbage-leaf-extract muscle and joint rub informed by his orthopedic patients' use of cabbage wraps.
- Cab Dab has been profitable from the start, has sold or distributed about 30,000 tubes, and has another 10,000 tubes ordered, but still depends heavily on Sokolowski's personal doctor-patient trust.
- Brown advises Cab Dab to turn full-size giveaways into smaller samples with a card and discount, while Guy suggests trusted channels such as chiropractors, medical communities, conferences, and free trials.
- [[AbbyRosalier]] built [[AbbyRose]] from a teen founder project into a family-run custom phone-case brand with more than $2 million in total sales and strong TikTok and Instagram audiences.
- Brown warns Abby Rose not to chase wholesale or paid advertising simply because growth is available; the first task is to understand which live-selling, community, collaboration, and owned-content experiments fit the brand.
- Abby Rose faces [[FounderLedLiveSelling]] dependency because sales slow when Rosalier is not online; Brown and Guy suggest training family members, friends, guests, or commission-based reps to make the format repeatable.
- [[HenryDavis]] introduces [[TannenOralCare]], originally a post-coffee mouth rinse whose longer-term opportunity may be broader natural oral care.
- Brown and Guy advise Tannen to treat coffee as an origin story rather than a hard category limit, clarifying benefits around oral microbiome, ingredients, taste, and non-coffee use cases such as wine, tea, and general freshness.
- Brown's final advice is to test small before making big moves, grow the business before assuming funding will solve the problem, and study competition before repositioning.

## Key Quotes
> "the best brown eyeshadow" - Brown's example of how small she once imagined starting again.

> "dip a toe" - Brown's phrase for testing new channels before committing heavily.

> "coffee will remain part of Tannen's origin story" - closing update on how Tannen is reframing the brand.
