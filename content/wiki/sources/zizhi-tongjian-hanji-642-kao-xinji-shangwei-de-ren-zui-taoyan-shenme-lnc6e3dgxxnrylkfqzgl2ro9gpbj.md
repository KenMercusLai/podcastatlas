# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》642丨靠心机上位的人，最讨厌什么？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills an early 10 CE [[WangMang|王莽]] layer between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-641-bi-zhi-gaige-ruhe-daozhi-wangmang-wanjie-bufu-liktnv0uewxo8awbo-m703jx8mhn|Hanji 641]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-643-weihuo-zhengfa-xiongnu-weihe-hui-bei-bishi-lr-5-qxjm4fqlirvwhdwelo1elg5|Hanji 643]]. It shows [[XinDynasty|新朝]] mandate-sign politics reversing after accession: the fuming or omen reports that helped Wang Mang gain the throne become threats once officials and commoners keep fabricating them for reward.

The central case is the [[ZhenFengXinOfficial|甄丰]] and [[ZhenXunXinOfficial|甄寻]] affair. [[ChenChongXinOfficial|陈崇]] warns that uncontrolled fuming claims let opportunists chase rank and confuse Heaven's mandate; Wang Mang then restricts reports, but Zhen Xun uses the same device to seek marriage to [[EmpressWangPingdi|汉平帝王皇后]], provoking a purge that kills or punishes hundreds. The closing turn to fangshi Su Yue, costly ritual construction, and renewed anti-[[Xiongnu|匈奴]] ambition links fuming control to Wang Mang's wider mix of [[AuspiciousOmenPolitics|祥瑞政治]], court fear, and [[FumingMonopolyBackfire|符命垄断反噬]].

## Key Claims

- The episode treats fuming, rendered in the transcript as 撫命 and normalized here as 符命/fuming, as one of the legitimacy technologies that helped Wang Mang replace Western Han.
- Rewarding mandate-sign reports during the usurpation encouraged officials and commoners to fabricate them after Wang Mang's accession.
- Chen Chong's memorial frames uncontrolled fuming as a danger because it lets self-interested actors seek office and confuse the language of Heaven's mandate.
- Wang Mang's restriction of fuming reports is not presented as ethical reform; it is a move to monopolize a legitimacy tool after it has become politically inconvenient.
- Zhen Xun's proposed marriage to Wang Mang's daughter crosses a boundary because it uses Wang Mang's own sign technology to touch imperial family order and dynastic dignity.
- The Zhen affair widens into a punitive purge, showing how mandate-sign anxiety can become political terror.
- Su Yue's immortal advice, the costly Ba Feng Tai project, and the coming Xiongnu campaign keep Wang Mang inside a broader pattern of omen, fangshi, ritual, and frontier overreach.

## Key Quotes

> "撫命是扶持王莽当上皇帝的大功臣之一" - the episode's explanation of why the device mattered before accession.

> "混乱天命" - Chen Chong's warning about what uncontrolled fuming claims would do.

> "国母" - Wang Mang's boundary term for the former Han Pingdi empress after Zhen Xun proposes marriage to her through fuming.
