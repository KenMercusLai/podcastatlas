# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》576丨挡熊救皇帝的她，为何被情敌逼死？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the Jianping 1 / 6 BCE case in which [[FengYuanWesternHan|冯媛 / 冯太后]] is accused of cursing [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] and [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]]. It follows [[ZhangYouWesternHan|张尤]] turning his own dereliction into a false report, Fu Taihou seizing the accusation because of older jealousy from the black-bear rescue scene under [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]], and [[ShiLiWesternHan|史立]] converting the case into torture, treason framing, and multiple deaths.

The durable synthesis is that a weak accusation becomes lethal when it enters a court already shaped by resentment and power asymmetry. Feng Yuan reads Shi Li's reference to the decades-old bear incident as proof of palace direction, kills herself, and leaves the later officials [[SunBaoWesternHan|孙宝]], Tang Lin, [[FuXiWesternHan|傅喜]], and Gong Sheng able to win only a limited procedural reversal for Sun Bao rather than true redress for the dead.

## Key Claims

- Zhang You's false accusation begins as self-protection after he leaves Zhongshan without authorization while assigned to attend the young Zhongshan king Liu Jizi.
- Fu Taihou's old jealousy of Feng Yuan's bear-rescue favor under Han Yuandi gives the accusation a ready political sponsor.
- The initial investigation by Ding Xuan produces no case, so Fu Taihou turns to Shi Li, whose incentive is to make the accusation actionable.
- Shi Li tortures Feng-family women, reports a treason plot to curse Aidi and enthrone Liu Jizi, and helps create a case in which multiple family members die.
- Feng Yuan's suicide is framed as recognition that an old palace grudge, not legal evidence, is driving the prosecution.
- Sun Bao asks Aidi to reopen the case, but Fu Taihou's anger gets him jailed until Tang Lin, Fu Xi, and Gong Sheng add enough pressure for his release.
- Zhang You and Shi Li are rewarded despite the source presenting the case as an injustice, showing accusation success outlasting evidentiary truth.

## Key Quotes

> "欲加之罪，何患无辞" - the host's frame for Fu Taihou replacing an unproductive inquiry with a usable investigator.

> "挡熊旧事" - the remembered act of courage that lets Feng Yuan infer palace-directed revenge when Shi Li mentions it during interrogation.
