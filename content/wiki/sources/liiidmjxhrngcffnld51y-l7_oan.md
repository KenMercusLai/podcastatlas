# 044 乱世情仇乱世哀：金庸《碧血剑》中的爱情书写

## Summary

This [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] episode reads [[BixueJian|《碧血剑》]] through the mismatched attachments connecting [[WenQingqing|温青青]], [[YuanChengzhi|袁承志]], [[AjiuBixueJian|阿九]], [[HeTieshou|何铁手]], [[XiaXueyi|夏雪宜]], [[WenYiBixueJian|温仪]], and [[HeHongyao|何红药]]. It links jealousy, emotional passivity, sacrifice, revenge, and coercion to character formation while preserving responsibility for harmful conduct. Its strongest literary contributions are [[AbsentPresenceCharacterDesign|absent-presence character design]] and [[EditionRevisionExplicitnessTradeoff|the tradeoff between revision, explicitness, and motive coherence]].

## Key Claims

- [[WenQingqing|温青青]] combines courage, competence, generosity, jealousy, and impulsiveness; the hosts connect her threat sensitivity to insecure status in the 温 family without treating background as acquittal.
- [[YuanChengzhi|袁承志]] appears more active in protection than in emotional declaration. His bond with Wen Qingqing mixes promise, responsibility, pity, shared history, and affection, while his feeling for [[AjiuBixueJian|阿九]] may be more intense but less actionable.
- Wen Qingqing's reaction to A Jiu differs from her responses to lesser perceived rivals: private withdrawal replaces public confrontation when she recognizes that the threat may be real.
- A Jiu and Yuan Chengzhi form a mirror of restrained feeling, pride, and passivity. Political collapse, bereavement, injury, identity, and the existing relationship make mutual feeling insufficient for a shared life.
- The older version gives [[HeTieshou|何铁手]] a mistaken attachment to the disguised Wen Qingqing and then a capacity to recover; removing that line in revision makes her break with the Five Poisons Cult and pursuit of Yuan Chengzhi less emotionally motivated.
- [[XiaXueyi|夏雪宜]] remains an [[AbsentPresenceCharacterDesign|absent but governing presence]] whose revenge, love, martial legacy, treasure, and death continue to organize the novel.
- Xia Xueyi and [[WenYiBixueJian|温仪]] briefly make love a route out of interfamily revenge, but the Wen elders' greed and suspicion destroy that possibility.
- [[HeHongyao|何红药]] sacrifices status, body, and future for Xia Xueyi, who exploits her attachment without accepting responsibility; her later captivity and punishment of him turn unreturned devotion into possession and self-destruction.
- Revision can clarify an emotional line while weakening another: making Yuan Chengzhi and A Jiu more explicit intensifies their romance but makes his final nonselection harder to reconcile, while deleting He Tieshou's infatuation thins her motive.
