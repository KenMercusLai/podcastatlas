# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1027丨荆州之主刘表，为何难成霸主？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode uses workplace factional alignment as a modern analogy for [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s late-Han positioning in [[Jingzhou|荆州]]. It argues that Liu Biao's acceptance of Li-Guo court titles while staying close to [[YuanShao|袁绍]] created short-term safety but long-term [[FenceSittingStrategicExposure|骑墙式战略暴露]]. The historical branch then follows [[DengXiLateHan|邓羲]]'s failed warning and the [[ZhangXianLateHan|张献]]-[[HuanJieLateHan|桓阶]] breakaway in [[SouthernJingzhouLateHan|荆南]], showing how local commandery fissures let [[CaoCao|曹操]] turn Jingzhou's internal weakness into strategic pressure before Guandu.

## Key Claims
- The opening workplace frame treats factional alignment as a predictable feature of larger organizations, not only a moral defect; the source recommends reading informal power flows while building portable [[FactionalAlignmentExitCapital|派系站队退出资本]].
- In 198 CE, while [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]] still shaped the captured court field, Liu Biao accepted a title package as Zhengnan general, Jingzhou governor, and Chengwu marquis, then resumed tribute.
- Liu Biao simultaneously kept close ties with Yuan Shao, creating the two-sided posture the episode criticizes as protecting Jianghan while waiting on the wider struggle.
- [[DengXiLateHan|邓羲]], Liu Biao's governing aide, warns that this double alignment is dangerous; after Liu Biao dismisses the concern as nitpicking, Deng Xi falls silent and resigns on illness grounds.
- The episode reads Deng Xi's departure as an early talent-retention warning: Liu Biao's inability to absorb correction helps explain why younger Jingzhou talents such as [[PangTong|庞统]] and [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] later turn toward [[LiuBei|刘备]].
- The host cites [[WangFuzhi|王夫之]] to sharpen the negative evaluation: Liu Biao does not truly secure Jingzhou so much as preserve it until larger Cao-Sun competition reaches the region.
- [[ZhangXianLateHan|张献]], the Changsha administrator, faces higher taxation and joins Huan Jie's proposal to combine [[ChangshaCommanderyLateHan|长沙郡]], [[LinglingCommanderyLateHan|零陵郡]], and [[GuiyangCommanderyLateHan|桂阳郡]] against Liu Biao.
- The source reads this southern move as structural, not merely personal: [[SouthernJingzhouLateHan|southern Jingzhou]] is separated from Liu Biao's [[XiangyangLateHan|襄阳]] center by geography, local-elite networks, and north-south economic and cultural differences.
- Zhang Xian's force submits to Cao Cao, who uses court authority to appoint Zhang Xian Zhengnan general; the move prevents Liu Biao from confidently aiding Yuan Shao before the [[GuanduCampaign|官渡]] horizon.
- Liu Biao answers by using [[HuangZu|黄祖]]'s navy to block the Yangtze line and by courting [[WuxiMan|五溪蛮]] leader [[ShaMoke|沙摩柯]], but the episode frames these as costly reactions after strategic initiative has slipped.

## Key Quotes
> "保江汉之间，观天下之变" - the source's compact formula for Liu Biao's wait-and-see policy.

> "中立本质上只是强者博弈的缓冲带" - the host's generalization about neutral space in warlord competition.
