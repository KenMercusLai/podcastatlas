## Summary
This episode backfills the early Zhangdi years, emphasizing [[EmpressMaMingdeEasternHan|Mingde Empress Ma]] as a childless but authoritative maternal model, the cautious treatment of the Ma clan, the 79 CE [[BaihuGuanConference|Baihu Guan conference]], and [[BanChaoEasternHan|Ban Chao]]'s early Western Regions strategy. It frames Eastern Han politics as a mix of household legitimacy, classics codification, and low-cost frontier operations.

## Key Claims
- [[EmpressMaMingdeEasternHan|Empress Ma]] becomes an exemplary empress dowager without biological children because her authority rests on raising [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Emperor Zhang]], education, restraint, and courtly judgment rather than childbirth alone.
- [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Emperor Zhang]] honors the Ma family as his maternal kin, but [[EmpressMaMingdeEasternHan|Empress Ma]] and her brothers try to prevent title rewards from becoming direct office power.
- [[YangZhongEasternHan|Yang Zhong]]'s proposal leads [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Emperor Zhang]] to convene officials and Confucian scholars at [[BaihuGuanConference|Baihu Guan]], producing a recorded effort to settle disputes over the Five Classics.
- [[BanChaoEasternHan|Ban Chao]] argues that the Western Regions can be advanced through hostage kingship, local supplies, Wusun coordination, and an "use barbarians to attack barbarians" strategy rather than a large central expedition.
- [[XuGanEasternHan|Xu Gan]] volunteers to reinforce [[BanChaoEasternHan|Ban Chao]] and joins the suppression of Pan Chen's rebellion in Shule.
