# 与段志强谈崇明乡野博物志：《我住长江尾》

## Summary
This [[BowuzhiPodcast|博物志]] episode has [[DuanZhiqiang|段志强]] discuss farming in [[Chongming|崇明]], rural neighbor relations, weather, childhood farm memory, and the making of [[WoZhuChangjiangWei|《我住长江尾》]]. It frames amateur planting as neither pure pastoral fantasy nor full agricultural livelihood: because Duan is not dependent on a few ears of corn for income, hardship can become observation, writing, and intellectual play. The episode's strongest synthesis is that embodied rural practice changes how texts, food history, land, time, and nonfiction form become legible.

## Key Claims
- [[AmateurFarmingVsLivelihoodAgriculture|Amateur farming vs livelihood agriculture]] is the episode's central distinction: the same weeding, planting, injury, delay, and crop failure feels different when it is a chosen life practice rather than the household's main income.
- [[AgriculturalPracticeAsTextualEntry|Agricultural practice as textual entry]] explains why Duan treats farming as a right to read agricultural books, classical literature, [[XuGuangqi|徐光启]], and food history from a more equal position.
- [[RuralCircularUseEconomy|Rural circular use economy]] appears in corn husks, stalks, ash, kitchen waste, furniture reuse, water use, and improvised trellises, where things keep moving through use rather than becoming immediate waste.
- [[RuralBoundaryAndNeighborOrder|Rural boundary and neighbor order]] is shown through vegetable gathering, bamboo shoots, landlord-neighbor repair help, field lines, and a social world where generosity coexists with strict property boundaries.
- [[AntiThesisWriting|Anti-thesis writing]] names the book's refusal to reduce everyday sensory experience into one clean central argument; the episode treats looseness as part of how rural life and memory actually arrive.
- [[Xiaohongshu|小红书]] appears as a practical farming-reminder and creator-platform case: feeds can supply seasonal tips while still pushing creators toward vertical labels that simplify a person's mixed life.
- The publication branch treats [[PhysicalBookDesignTradeoff|physical book design]] and [[BookPublishingEconomics|book publishing economics]] as aesthetic and organizational choices, including title selection, editorial work, AI typo checking, limited signatures, and rejection of jacket/obi/celebrity blurbs.
