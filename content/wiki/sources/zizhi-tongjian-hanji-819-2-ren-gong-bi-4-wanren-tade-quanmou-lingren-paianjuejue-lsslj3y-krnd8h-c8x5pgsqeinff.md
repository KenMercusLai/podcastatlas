# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》819丨2人功比4万人，他的权谋令人拍案叫绝！

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills 138 CE under [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]]. It tracks southern-frontier unrest in [[JiaozhiCommandery|交趾郡]], Jiuzhen, and Rinan, then centers [[LiGuLateHan|李固]]'s memorial against a 40,000-soldier expedition and his replacement plan based on fitted appointments, temporary Rinan retrenchment, rewards, and surrender strategy.

The second half extends the same personnel theme into court recommendation ethics. [[ZhouJuLateHan|周举]] impeaches [[ZuoXiongLateHan|左雄]] for recommending a previously corrupt official, Liang He refuses to recommend beyond his knowledge, and the episode closes by setting up the Liang Shang, Cao Teng, and Meng Wen false-accusation case continued in Hanji 820.

## Key Claims
- Shun's court initially reads the Jiaozhi crisis as a scale problem and favors sending a major general with 40,000 troops from Jingzhou, Yangzhou, and Yuzhou.
- Li Gu argues that forced distant recruitment would multiply risk through unrest in the source regions, repeated conscription, disease, exhaustion, food cost, and likely desertion or rebellion.
- Li Gu's counterproposal treats the crisis as a personnel and method problem: appoint brave, wise, humane, and authoritative local officials rather than overextending the center.
- The Rinan branch supplies [[TemporaryFrontierRetrenchment|临时边防收缩]]: temporarily move officials and people north when the outpost lacks troops and grain, then return when conditions stabilize.
- Li Gu's reward plan extends [[BountyMobilizedPacification|赏募动员式平乱]] by using money, cloth, internal division, and heavy rewards for turning or killing enemy leaders.
- [[ZhangQiaoLateHan|张乔]] and [[ZhuLiangLateHan|祝良]] validate [[AppointmentQualityAsStateCapacity|任官适任性国家能力]] when Zhang Qiao's reassurance and Zhu Liang's single-carriage entry induce surrender without the planned mass expedition.
- Zhou Ju's challenge to Zuo Xiong and Liang He's refusal to recommend show that recommendation quality includes boundary-setting, not only naming candidates.
- The closing Qiang, eclipse, and eunuch-accusation branch links the 138 CE source to Hanji 820's later handling of [[LiangShangLateHan|梁商]], [[CaoTengLateHan|曹腾]], Zhang Kui, and Meng Wen.
