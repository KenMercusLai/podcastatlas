# 《资治通鉴·周纪》54丨我尊你是王，你当我是屁

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the 311 BCE [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]] frame after [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] survives [[ChuState|楚国]] in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-53-qinhuiwang-yong-zhangyi-huan-dipi-lvmo2cwbafcg2gnxliuo18fewimk|Zhouji 53]]. Zhang Yi moves through [[HanState|韩国]], [[QiState|齐国]], [[ZhaoState|赵国]], and [[YanState|燕国]], repeatedly turning each ruler's geography, military limits, and lost allies into a Qin-facing argument. The episode shows [[LianhengStrategy|连横]] as serial [[EncirclementThreatPersuasion|包围威胁式说服]]: Zhang Yi makes serving [[QinState|秦国]] feel like the least dangerous option because every alternative is framed as isolation. It closes with [[YingSi|秦惠文王]]'s death and [[QinWuwang|秦武王]]'s accession, exposing Zhang Yi to [[PatronSuccessionExposure|靠山更替暴露风险]].

## Key Claims
- After leaving Chu alive, Zhang Yi goes first to [[HanXiangwang|韩襄王]] and argues that Han is mountainous, grain-poor, lightly staffed by comparison with Qin, and vulnerable if Qin takes [[YiyangWarringStates|宜阳]] and controls Chenggao.
- Zhang Yi tells Han that submission to Qin and an attack on Chu would turn danger away from Han; Han accepts the proposal.
- [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] rewards Zhang Yi with six cities and the title Wuxin Jun, then sends him east to lobby [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]].
- Zhang Yi's Qi speech attacks the premise of [[HezongAlliance|合纵]]: he claims Qin and Chu are already brother states, Han and Wei have yielded key positions, and Zhao has entered Qin's court and ceded land.
- Zhang Yi warns Qi that if it refuses Qin, Qin can drive Han, Wei, and Zhao pressure against Qi; Qi accepts the proposal.
- Zhang Yi then approaches [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]] by first praising Zhao's past ability to check Qin, then turning to Qin's expansion through [[BaShu|巴蜀]], [[Hanzhong|汉中]], the two Zhou polities, and key Yellow River crossings.
- In the Zhao speech, Zhang Yi describes three Qin-led military lines and a four-state plan to divide Zhao, then recommends a direct Qin-Zhao meeting and a brother-state alliance; Zhao accepts.
- The transcript only briefly mentions Zhang Yi lobbying [[YanZhaowang|燕昭王]], then says that while returning toward [[Xianyang|咸阳]], Zhang Yi learns Qin Huiwen Wang has died and Qin Wuwang has succeeded.
- The episode stresses that Qin Wuwang disliked Zhang Yi when still crown prince, making Zhang Yi's prior favor under Qin Huiwen Wang suddenly uncertain.

## Key Quotes
> "千钧之重压在鸟卵之上" - image used to convey Qin's overwhelming pressure against a weak state.

> "一朝天子一朝臣" - the host's transition frame for Zhang Yi after Qin Huiwen Wang's death.
