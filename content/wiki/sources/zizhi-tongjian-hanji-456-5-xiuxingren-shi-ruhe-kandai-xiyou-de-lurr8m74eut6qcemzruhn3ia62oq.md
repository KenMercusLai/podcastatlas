# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》456-5｜修行人是如何看待西游的？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] weekend episode continues the symbolic rereading of [[JourneyToTheWestConqueringDemons|《西游降魔篇》]], focusing on the film's early and middle scenes as a cultivation map inside [[ChenXuanzangConqueringDemons|陈玄奘]]'s mind. It reads the water demon as wounded compassion, [[ZhuBajie|猪八戒]] / 朱刚烈 as desire and fixation, [[DuanMissConqueringDemons|段小姐]] as hard-to-cut worldly affinity, and [[SunWukong|孙悟空]] as ideal and heart-direction. The episode sharpens the earlier Hanji 456-4 synthesis by stressing that seeing through an illusion is not the same as subduing it, and that formal abstinence can still hide attachment.

## Key Claims

- [[JourneyToTheWestConqueringDemons|《西游降魔篇》]] is read less as an external demon world than as one practitioner's inner pilgrimage through compassion, desire, affinity, ideal, and heart-demon.
- The water-demon story makes [[ShaWujing|沙和尚]] a symbol of Chen Xuanzang's compassionate nature: wrongly harmed, still close to suffering, and not primarily verbal.
- [[ZhuBajie|猪八戒]] / 朱刚烈 represents desire and fixation; Chen can see through the seductive illusion of 高老庄 but cannot yet subdue the force behind it.
- [[DuanMissConqueringDemons|段小姐]] represents 情缘, including romantic and family attachment, which the source presents as especially hard for a practitioner to cut.
- The master's goose-leg lesson criticizes [[FormalizedCultivationCritique|形式化修行批判]]: external abstinence matters less than whether the attachment remains in the heart.
- [[SunWukong|孙悟空]] is introduced here as Chen Xuanzang's ideal and 心之所向, preparing the later heart-demon reading.

## Key Quotes

> "世间没有真正的妖魔鬼怪" - the episode's interiorizing premise.

> "心中没有鹅腿" - the master's critique of surface-level abstinence.

> "情缘最难缠、最难断" - the source's summary of Duan Miss's function.
