# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1047丨官渡大战在即，刘备为何紧急逃离袁绍？

## Summary
This episode of [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] follows the Guandu prelude after [[LiuBei|刘备]] is sent by [[YuanShao|袁绍]] to disturb [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s southern flank around [[RunanCommanderyLateHan|汝南]] and the transcript's "尹川" area, likely the Yingchuan/Xudu zone. [[CaoRen|曹仁]] defeats Liu Bei, after which Liu Bei uses a proposed Liu Biao alliance as an exit route from Yuan Shao, returns to Runan with [[GongDuLateHan|龚都]], and kills [[CaiYangLateHan|蔡阳]]. The episode then shifts to [[JuShou|沮授]]'s rejected attrition advice at [[YangwuGuanduLateHan|阳武]], Yuan Shao's advance to [[ShaqiuGuanduLateHan|沙丘]], and the fixed-camp engineering contest before Cao Cao's grain crisis.

## Key Claims
- Liu Bei's departure from Yuan Shao is interpreted as a strategic safety judgment rather than simple betrayal, because Yuan Shao is still strong and Liu Biao is not treated as Cao Cao's ally in this account.
- Cao Ren's cavalry counterattack shows that Liu Bei's newly assigned Yuan-side troops are not yet a controllable or stable force.
- Runan becomes a mobile pressure theater where Yuan Shao can threaten Cao Cao through Liu Bei, Gong Du, and local rebel remnants even before the main Guandu collision.
- Ju Shou's advice reframes Yuan Shao's advantage as time and logistics: Yuan has more men and grain, while Cao Cao has better fighting quality but a weaker supply base.
- Yuan Shao's rejection of the long-war plan pushes the campaign toward direct camp confrontation, where engineering measures replace quick decisive battle.
- The high towers, earth hills, thunderbolt carts, tunnels, and trenches make Guandu a contest of material pressure and countermeasures before the later Wuchao turning point.

## Key Quotes
> "胜兵先胜而后求战，败兵先战而后求胜。" — The episode uses this Sunzi contrast to explain why waiting can be an active strategic choice.

> "刘备为什么这么着急要离开袁绍呢？" — The host frames Liu Bei's exit as the episode's central interpretive puzzle.
