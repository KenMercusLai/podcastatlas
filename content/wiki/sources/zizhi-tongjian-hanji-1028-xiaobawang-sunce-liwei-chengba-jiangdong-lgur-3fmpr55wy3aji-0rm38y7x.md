# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1028丨小霸王孙策立威，称霸江东！

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode turns [[SunCe|孙策]]'s Jiangdong rise from campaign momentum into political consolidation. [[CaoCao|曹操]] recognizes Sun Ce through titles, marriage ties, and hostage-like summons, while [[YuanShu|袁术]] tries to disrupt him through [[ZuLangLateHan|祖郎]] and [[Shanyue|山越]] forces after Sun Ce's break with Yuan Shu.

The core contribution is [[CapturedOpponentTalentIncorporation|降敌任用式立威]]: Sun Ce captures both Zu Lang and [[TaishiCi|太史慈]], but releases and appoints them instead of killing them. The episode frames this as power that combines force, restraint, and talent absorption; it also sets up [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1029-guoshi-wushuang-taishici-yinuo-qianjin-yi-dangxian-lvckwlhz5g-oappy89thye9oyrto|Hanji 1029]] by leaving [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]]'s leaderless Yuzhang-adjacent followers unresolved.

## Key Claims
- Zhang Xian's attachment to Cao Cao and Huan Jie's advice are presented as earlier Jingzhou pressure signals, but the episode's main branch is Sun Ce's Jiangdong consolidation.
- [[SunCe|孙策]] sends [[ZhangHongLateHan|张纮]] to court, and [[CaoCao|曹操]] recommends Sun Ce as anti-rebel general and Marquis of Wu while using marriage and court service requests for [[SunQuan|孙权]] and [[SunYiLateHan|孙翊]] to pull Sun Ce into the Xu-court orbit.
- [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] and [[LuSu|鲁肃]] reject [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s county-level appointments after judging Yuan Shu's imperial project as failing, then cross the Yangtze toward Sun Ce.
- [[WangLangLateHan|王朗]] is sent by Sun Ce to the court after Cao Cao asks for him; the source reads Cao Cao's later use of Wang Lang as both scholar-gentry recruitment and possible balancing of the [[XunYu|荀彧]] adviser field.
- After Yuan Shu declares himself emperor and Sun Ce breaks with him, Yuan Shu secretly gives [[ZuLangLateHan|祖郎]] a seal and asks him to mobilize [[Shanyue|山越]] forces against Sun Ce.
- [[TaishiCi|太史慈]] moves into the Wuhu/Jing County mountain zone, claims the Danyang administrator title, and gains support among Shanyue groups while [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]] has fled toward [[YuzhangCommandery|豫章郡]].
- Sun Ce captures Zu Lang at Lingyang, explicitly sets aside the earlier ambush grievance, releases him, and appoints him as `门下贼曹`.
- Sun Ce then captures Taishi Ci, recalls their earlier [[ShentinglingBattle|神亭岭之战]] encounter, praises his courage and loyalty, and appoints him as `门下督`.
- The army sees Zu Lang and Taishi Ci placed at the front of Sun Ce's returning force, making visible that Sun Ce can turn defeated opponents into part of the new order.
- After Liu Yao dies, his remaining force wants [[HuaXin|华歆]] to lead them, but Hua Xin refuses, leaving the soldiers without a clear chief and setting up Sun Ce's next move.

## Key Quotes
> "不好说" - Taishi Ci's answer when Sun Ce asks what would have happened if Taishi Ci had captured him at Shentingling.

> "过去的事情都不用再计较" - Sun Ce's message to Zu Lang after capturing him.
