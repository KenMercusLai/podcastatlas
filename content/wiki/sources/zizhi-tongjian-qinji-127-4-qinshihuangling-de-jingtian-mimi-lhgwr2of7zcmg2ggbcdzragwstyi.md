## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode closes the Qin Shi Huang death-and-burial branch by describing [[QinShiHuangMausoleum|秦始皇陵]] as a monumental, secretive, and violent funerary project: deep tomb, bronze sealing, treasures, crossbow mechanisms, mercury waterways, starry ceiling, lamps, concubine burial, and artisan killing. It then follows [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] and [[ZhaoGao|赵高]] into the purge of [[MengTian|蒙恬]] and [[MengYiQin|蒙毅]], with [[ZiyingQin|子婴]] warning that killing loyal pillars repeats the state-destroying errors of [[ZhaoState|赵国]] and [[QiState|齐国]]. The final judgment complicates Meng Tian: [[YangXiong|扬雄]], [[SimaGuang|司马光]], and [[SimaQian|司马迁]] can praise his loyal death while still treating his role in [[QinZhidao|秦直道]], long-wall work, and forced labor as moral liability.

## Key Claims
- Qin Shi Huang is buried at [[Lishan|骊山]] roughly two months after death, in a mausoleum said to have been under construction since he first became Qin king.
- The source presents the tomb as engineered cosmic kingship: underground rivers and seas made with mercury, celestial patterns above, treasures within, and perpetual lamps fed by "人鱼膏."
- The mausoleum also becomes a secrecy system: childless concubines and knowledgeable artisans are trapped or killed so the burial and mechanisms cannot be exposed.
- After accession, Hu Hai follows Zhao Gao's line and kills the Meng brothers despite Ziying's remonstrance that [[LiMu|李牧]]'s killing helped destroy Zhao and that [[QiWangJian|齐王建 / 田建]] lost his state after killing old ministers.
- Meng Tian frames his refusal to rebel and his suicide as loyalty to ancestral service and to Qin Shi Huang.
- Yang Xiong, Sima Guang, and Sima Qian all prevent a simple "wronged loyal minister" reading: Meng Tian's loyalty does not erase his service to harsh Qin rule and his labor-consuming projects.
- The episode compares Meng Tian with [[BaiQi|白起]]: both are celebrated Qin military figures whose forced deaths are remembered alongside mass killing or mass labor burdens.
- Sima Qian rejects Meng Tian's "截断地脉" explanation and instead blames failure to advise rest for the people after unification.

## Key Quotes
> "太残忍了" - the host's judgment on burying Qin Shi Huang's childless concubines alive.

> "忠诚不足以抵罪" - the episode's compressed reading of Yang Xiong's position on Meng Tian.

> "关地脉什么事" - the host's shorthand for Sima Qian's rejection of Meng Tian's geomantic excuse.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction found. The episode continues the traditional post-accession Meng-family purge while leaving the earlier Qinji 127-3 source conflict over Hu Hai's establishment unresolved.
- Evidence boundary: the episode relays traditional tomb details such as mercury rivers, mechanisms, and lamps as part of the source narrative, while the transcript itself notes limited separation between transmitted account and archaeological verification.
- Evaluation tension: Meng Tian is both a loyal minister unjustly killed by Hu Hai's regime and a Qin servant implicated in harsh labor extraction.
