# 《资治通鉴·周纪》92丨历史上第一位著名舔狗是谁？（2）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-92-lishishang-di-yi-wei-zhuming-tiangou-shi-shui-1-lmwf0q0-3hxb6d4hro8fdmc3zdxp|Zhouji 92 part 1]] hostage crisis. After [[MiWan|芈完 / 楚考烈王]] escapes from [[QinState|秦国]], [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]] stays behind, admits the plan only after the prince is safe, and survives because [[FanJu|范雎]] convinces [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] to treat the failed hostage leverage as a chance to preserve influence in [[ChuState|楚国]].

The episode then reviews [[MiHeng|楚顷襄王]] as a politically weak but partially reparative ruler: his early hostage killing, failure to avenge [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]], and losses to Qin define his reputation, while [[ZhuangXin|庄辛]]'s "亡羊补牢" counsel gives his late reign a limited recovery arc. The closing turn uses [[SongYu|宋玉]], [[WushanGoddess|巫山神女]], 《高唐赋》, and 《神女赋》 to explain [[XiangwangYouyiShennvWuxin|襄王有意，神女无心]], making the title's "著名舔狗" frame point to Chu Qingxiang Wang's literary afterlife as a figure of one-sided longing.

## Key Claims
- [[FanJu|范雎]] argues that killing [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]] after [[MiWan|太子完]] has already escaped would only satisfy anger; sending Huang Xie back can still create future Qin leverage over Chu.
- [[MiWan|芈完 / 楚考烈王]] succeeds [[MiHeng|楚顷襄王]] three months later and rewards Huang Xie with the chancellorship, a large Huai River-region fief, and the title Chunshenjun.
- The episode contrasts the [[WarringStatesFourLords|战国四公子]] by presenting Chunshenjun's rise as more dependent on concrete service, courage, and long hostage-court companionship than on birth alone.
- The source-scoped Shanghai/申城 and Huangpu River associations are treated as cultural memory around Chunshenjun, not as independently verified topographical history.
- [[MiHeng|楚顷襄王]] is judged harshly because his life includes hostage violence, military collapse, loss of the old Chu center, and long accommodation to Qin after [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]]'s death.
- [[ZhuangXin|庄辛]]'s recall and "亡羊补牢" advice give Mi Heng a late but limited repair story: disaster has already happened, yet the ruler can still listen, appoint him, and recover some territory.
- [[SongYu|宋玉]] becomes the bridge from political history to literary memory: the episode credits him with preserving the Chu kings' dream encounters with [[WushanGoddess|巫山神女]] through 《高唐赋》 and 《神女赋》.
- [[XiangwangYouyiShennvWuxin|襄王有意，神女无心]] is used as the title resolution: Chu Qingxiang Wang's pursuit of the goddess becomes a classical idiom for unreciprocated desire.
- The episode closes by marking the transition from the Chu Qingxiang Wang era to the [[MiWan|楚考烈王]] and [[Chunshenjun|春申君]] era.

## Key Quotes
> "别跟沉没成本怄气" - the host's modern gloss on Fan Ju's advice.

> "亡羊补牢，犹为未晚" - the Zhuang Xin recovery frame.

> "襄王有意，神女无心" - the idiom used to resolve the episode title.
