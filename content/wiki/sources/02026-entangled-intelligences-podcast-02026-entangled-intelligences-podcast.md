## Summary
This [[LongNow]] event examines intelligence across cells, organisms, ecosystems, institutions, machines, and imagined extraterrestrial lineages. [[AshwairaKhanduja]] frames intelligence as responsible action under incomplete knowledge; [[ClaireIsabelWebb]], [[MichaelLevin]], [[DavidGruber]], [[SaraImariWalker]], and [[DavidEagleman]] then connect collective biological agency, embodiment, causal history, animal communication, and sensory difference. Its speculative scenes extend those arguments into [[CrossSpeciesTranslationLimits]], [[AnimalUpliftEthics]], and [[MultispeciesGovernance]], where better communication expands political responsibility without dissolving conflict.

## Key Claims
- [[IntelligenceUnderUncertainty]] treats sensing, memory, coordination, action, and revision without complete information as central capacities rather than defects to eliminate.
- [[CollectiveBiologicalAgency]] interprets development and regeneration as coordinated cellular problem-solving shaped by bioelectric state, embodiment, and remembered anatomical goals, not DNA alone.
- [[CognitiveLightCone]] measures a mind by the spatial and temporal scale of goals it can represent and pursue, while warning that unfamiliar scale or embodiment can produce mind blindness.
- [[AssemblyTheory]] asks how construction history and copy number distinguish superficially similar outputs; on this account, terrestrial AI remains part of Earth's biological and cultural lineage.
- [[PerceptualGreatFilter]] proposes that human senses, concepts, and instruments may hide unfamiliar life even when it is present.
- [[CrossSpeciesTranslationLimits]] separates signal translation from access to another species' Umwelt, interests, norms, and lived experience.
- [[AnimalUpliftEthics]] asks whether enhancing other species would reduce suffering or impose human capacities, anxiety, and values without consent.
- [[MultispeciesGovernance]] shows that recognition and representation do not remove ecological dependence, unequal bodies, competing interests, or disputes over political units.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is adopted. The episode broadens intelligence beyond human cognition without claiming that all coordinated behavior is conscious or that intelligence and consciousness are identical.
- Lamarckian questions about organismal agency are revived without endorsing all of Lamarck's inheritance mechanisms or overturning natural selection and genetics.
- Translation, uplift, and regenerative freedom are presented as possibilities with persistent consent, welfare, access, conflict, and governance problems rather than automatic moral progress.
