# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》516丨古代中国为何会与这个国家断交？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens with a Confucian-management frame: selecting capable people is not enough unless a leader can recognize, trust, and use them, and the leader's own cultivation shapes that judgment. It then places [[ZhangYuWesternHan|张瑜 / 张禹]]'s rise from crown-prince teacher to chancellor beside a Western Regions crisis involving [[JibinKingdomWesternHan|罽宾]] and [[YinmofuJibinKing|阴末赴 / 殷墨傅]].

The durable frontier synthesis is a gap between recognition and enforceable commitment. A Han envoy helps Yinmofu replace a Jibin king and Han supplies a seal, but distance prevents the court from reliably protecting later envoys or punishing violence. After Yinmofu detains Zhao De and kills his deputy and more than seventy attendants, the episode says the court rejects the apology mission and ends relations rather than launch an expedition it cannot sustain.

## Key Claims

- The opening lesson treats personnel choice as a ruler-capacity problem: worthy officials matter only if the leader can identify, respect, and employ them.
- The episode connects knowledge of others to self-cultivation through `取人以身，修身以道` and `自天子以至于庶人，一是皆以修身为本`.
- Zhang Yu teaches the future Chengdi the `Lunyu`, receives office and favor after accession, tries to withdraw from a politically exposed memorial role, and is instead made chancellor and Anchang Hou.
- Jibin treats distance as protection from Han enforcement and repeatedly attacks Han envoys.
- Han envoy Wen Zhong works with Yinmofu, described as a subordinate king, to kill the existing Jibin ruler; Han then recognizes Yinmofu and gives him a royal seal.
- The episode identifies Yinmofu with the Greek king Hermaeus and describes his capital as near present-day Kabul, but does not supply the underlying scholarly argument.
- After Yinmofu detains envoy Zhao De and kills his deputy and more than seventy attendants, the court rejects his apology mission and cuts off contact because it lacks the capacity for a distant response.

## Key Quotes

> "取人以身，修身以道" - the episode's link between self-cultivation and judging people.

> "自以绝远，汉兵不能至" - the distance-and-enforcement logic attributed to Jibin.

## Contradictions

- Internal chronology conflict: the episode dates the narrative to Heping 4 / 25 BCE under Han Chengdi but later says Han Yuandi made the final decision concerning Jibin. The source does not resolve this contradiction, so the wiki does not transfer that action to Yuan's profile.
- Name normalization: the transcript repeatedly uses `季宾`; this ingest routes the polity to [[JibinKingdomWesternHan|罽宾 / Jibin]] and preserves the transcript form as a qualification rather than creating a duplicate entity.
- Identity qualification: the equation of Yinmofu with the Greek king Hermaeus and the claim that this was the only Greek king invested by a Central Plains dynasty are preserved as episode-attributed claims, not independently settled facts.
