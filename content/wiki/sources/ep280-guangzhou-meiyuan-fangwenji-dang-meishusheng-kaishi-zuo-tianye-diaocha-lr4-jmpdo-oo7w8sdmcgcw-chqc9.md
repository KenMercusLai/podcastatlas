# EP280 广州美院访问记：当美术生开始做田野调查

## Summary
This [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] episode with [[GaoYiding|高一丁]], [[WeiQianSanlian|魏茜]], and [[QinSi|秦思]] uses a roughly two-year undergraduate reform at [[GuangzhouAcademyOfFineArts|广州美术学院]] to ask what university can still do for students whose education has become abstract, competitive, and outcome-driven. The reform sends first-year art students into wet markets, antique markets, urban villages, communities, family conversations, campus service work, and everyday occupations so that observation, field notes, interviews, handmade books, and documentary video can become part of art learning. Its strongest contribution is to connect [[UndergraduateArtFieldwork]], [[ExperienceFormingEducation]], [[UnlearningExamExpression]], and [[TeacherStudentCoFieldLearning]]: fieldwork can reopen perception and teacher-student relations, but it does not erase grading, professional training, employment pressure, or social evaluation.

## Key Claims
- The Guangzhou Academy of Fine Arts reform is framed as more than ordinary social practice: it is a test of whether university can create real experience rather than only coursework, credentials, and deliverables.
- [[UndergraduateArtFieldwork]] uses concrete sites such as brush sellers, antique markets, food markets, urban villages, cleaners, body models, square dancing, and community notices to make art students start from observation rather than an assigned grand theme.
- Students initially resist or feel confused because the course delays familiar professional training and exposes weak stranger-interview skills, social fear, and fear of rejection.
- Field notes and slow discussion are treated as more important than polished PPTs because students often jump too quickly to abstractions such as cultural preservation, suffering, or social value before noticing what is actually happening.
- [[ExperienceFormingEducation]] names the episode's distinction, attributed through [[XiangBiao|项飙]], between life events that reshape a person as experience and frictionless "user experience" that passes over the body without becoming durable.
- [[UnlearningExamExpression]] is central to the episode: art students have sensitivity, but gaokao-style studio training and school writing routines can leave them producing formulaic images, concepts, and moral summaries.
- Teachers are also learners in the reform. Training, shared field entry, uncertainty, and long conversations make authority less hierarchical and create [[TeacherStudentCoFieldLearning]] rather than one-way instruction.
- The reform's boundary is explicit: a first-year fieldwork "utopia" can open curiosity and improve relationships, but students still return to specialized majors, grading curves, employment choices, repetitive technical practice, and marketable skill pressure.

## Key Quotes
> "赶到社会上去上课" - the reform's blunt outside-campus premise.

> "你到底看见了什么" - the field-note question used to push students back toward observation.

> "经验和体验" - the conceptual distinction used to separate durable formation from passing consumption.

> "unlearn" - Qin Si's name for undoing inherited learning routines before new observation can begin.
