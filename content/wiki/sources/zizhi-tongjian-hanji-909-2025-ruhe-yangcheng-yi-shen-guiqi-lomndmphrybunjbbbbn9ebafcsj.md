# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》909丨2025如何养成一身贵气？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode uses "贵气" as a personal self-command frame, then backfills the 177 CE late-Han decision to launch a deep expedition against [[Xianbei|鲜卑]]. [[CaiYong|蔡邕]] argues against the campaign by weighing national capacity, frontier leakage, Xianbei strength, internal unrest, and long-war cost, but [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] still accepts the offensive line pushed through [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]]. The resulting three-route expedition by [[XiaYuLateHan|夏育]], [[TianYanLateHan|田晏]], and [[ZangMinLateHan|臧旻]] is crushed by [[TanshihuaiXianbei|檀石槐]], turning the episode into a compact case of [[OverextendedFrontierExpedition|边境远征过度]], [[WarCostTempoDiscipline|战争成本与速决纪律]], and [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|君臣反馈失灵]].

## Key Claims

- "贵气" is presented source-scopedly as the visible result of inner strength, self-expression, taking responsibility, and reducing relational over-analysis.
- The episode's practical formula "先思危，再思变，后思退" is mapped onto both personal relationships and state military decision-making.
- [[XiaYuLateHan|夏育]] frames repeated Xianbei raids as a reason for decisive cross-frontier attack, but the source reads his confidence as underestimating cost and failure risk.
- [[TianYanLateHan|田晏]] reaches command through [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]]'s palace access, making the expedition partly a case of military appointment through inner-court brokerage.
- [[CaiYong|蔡邕]] opposes the campaign by comparing Han capacity unfavorably with [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s earlier Xiongnu wars and [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]]'s long western-Qiang campaigns.
- Cai Yong's warning is systemic: [[Xianbei|鲜卑]] strength, metal leakage, Han fugitives serving Xianbei, weak border enforcement, and unsettled internal thieves make victory unlikely and cost escalation likely.
- The three-route expedition's destruction by [[TanshihuaiXianbei|檀石槐]] confirms the ignored risk assessment inside the source narrative.
- The aftermath splits responsibility oddly: commanders redeem themselves from prison through money and connections, while solar eclipse and earthquake notices help drive senior-office removals involving [[LiuKuanLateHan|刘宽]], [[ChenQiuLateHan|陈球]], [[MengYuLateHan|孟郁]], [[ChenDanLateHan|陈耽]], and [[YangCiLateHan|杨赐]].
- The source treats the failed expedition as the Eastern Han's last large-scale external campaign and as a worsening point for late-Han fiscal and frontier strain.
- The [[CaoCao|曹操]] and [[CaiWenji|蔡文姬]] anecdote is useful as a Cai Yong cultural-network aside, but it is secondary to the expedition and remonstrance mainline.

## Key Quotes

> "人一旦变强，气质就变了。" — opening frame for the episode's "贵气" reading.

> "先思危，再思变，后思退。" — practical formula used to connect personal judgment and policy caution.

> "蔡邕的上奏没有被皇帝采纳。" — hinge between accurate warning and failed decision conversion.
