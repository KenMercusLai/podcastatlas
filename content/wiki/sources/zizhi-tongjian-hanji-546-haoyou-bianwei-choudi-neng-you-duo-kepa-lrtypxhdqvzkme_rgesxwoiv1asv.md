# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》546丨好友变为仇敌，能有多可怕？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Yuan延 1 / 12 BCE under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]], after the earlier friendship-rupture account in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-542-zhichang-xinlixue-shengzhi-hou-weihe-geng-rongyi-zao-paiji-lua_jsiusjojpwlwlroehw7jebgm|Hanji 542]]. It first presents [[GuYongWesternHan|谷永]] and [[LiuXiang|刘向]] using disaster and eclipse signs to warn Chengdi that moral failure, palace disorder, inner-court danger, rebellion risk, and popular distress have become dynastic danger signals.

The second half returns to [[ChenXianWesternHan|陈贤]] and [[ZhaiFangjinWesternHan|翟方进]]. [[WangLiWesternHan|王立]] recommends Chen Xian as 方正, Chengdi reappoints him as Guanglu Dafu and Jishi Zhong, and Zhai immediately blocks the restoration by reviving Chen's prior corruption and recommendation-history problems. The durable synthesis is that old friendship can become harsher than ordinary rivalry when promotion, inquiry pressure, old injury, and renewed office access make both sides see the other as a live threat.

## Key Claims

- Chengdi seeks advice after repeated disasters and anomalies, creating a sanctioned opening for high-risk remonstrance.
- Gu Yong argues that ruler virtue and Heaven's response are linked, warning that continued misrule can lead to dynastic transfer.
- Gu Yong ties recent disasters to internal palace/court danger, possible rebellion, consort disorder, unsafe outings, extravagance, and the need to reduce burdens on the people.
- Liu Xiang uses frequent solar eclipses to argue that calamities arise from human political failure and imperial-family faults; the episode notes that Chengdi ultimately does not adopt his advice.
- Wang Li's 方正 recommendation lets Chen Xian return to court as Guanglu Dafu and Jishi Zhong after earlier dismissal.
- Zhai Fangjin treats Chen's reappointment as intolerable, memorializing that a formerly high-ranking official removed for corruption and harm should not serve as a close imperial attendant.
- Zhai also impeaches Wang Li for improper recommendation, but Chengdi removes Chen while protecting Wang Li from further attack because Wang Li is the emperor's uncle.
- The episode explicitly links this later clash to the old promotion and funeral-inquiry rupture: friendship has become a political injury memory, not a protective tie.

## Key Quotes

> "兄弟背叛" - the opening emotional frame for reading the Chen Xian and Zhai Fangjin case.

> "让时间去治愈一切" - the closing return from court feud to ordinary relationship pain.
