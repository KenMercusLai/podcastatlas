# Big shot: does a cancer vaccine work?

## Summary
This episode of [[TheIntelligence]] moves from [[SteveYoungMelanomaPatient]]'s personalized mRNA melanoma-vaccine trial to [[Canada]]'s oil-sands boom and a remembrance of [[DollyParton]]. The cancer segment treats the [[Moderna]]-linked treatment as promising but unfinished: tumor sequencing, AI-assisted target selection, mRNA manufacturing, and checkpoint inhibitors make the approach more plausible, while survival, side effects, dropout rates, cost, and access remain unresolved. The Canada segment frames oil as an economic bright spot because high global prices, new [[TransMountainExpansion]] capacity, tariff exemptions, and infrastructure politics align; the Parton segment frames artistic talent, rights control, philanthropy, faith, humor, and political restraint as the basis of unusually broad cultural appeal.

## Key Claims
- Personalized mRNA cancer vaccines are presented as therapeutic and patient-specific, not universal preventive cancer shots.
- [[SteveYoungMelanomaPatient]]'s melanoma case illustrates the workflow: tumor removal, tissue sequencing, mRNA mapping, first dosing in 2024, and ongoing monitoring inside a seven-year trial.
- The episode's cancer optimism remains conditional because overall survival, side effects, dropouts, added benefit over checkpoint inhibitors, cost, and access are still unsettled.
- [[CancerVaccinePlatform]] now looks more feasible than older attempts because neoantigen selection, mRNA manufacturing speed, and checkpoint inhibitors address some earlier failure modes.
- [[CanadianOilSandsBoom]] is tied to high oil prices, a weak Canadian dollar, nearly tripled [[CenovusEnergy]] quarterly profits, record adjusted [[CanadianNaturalResourcesLimited]] quarterly profits, and new pipeline capacity.
- [[TransMountainExpansion]] is treated as an infrastructure timing case: a long-delayed pipeline came online in 2024 just as Canadian producers wanted more export capacity and less dependence on the [[UnitedStates]].
- [[MarkCarney]]'s [[MajorProjectsOfficeCanada]] is framed as a response to trade unpredictability and the desire to speed nationally important infrastructure approvals.
- [[DollyParton]] is remembered as a songwriter, performer, business actor, philanthropist, and [[CrossCoalitionCulturalIcon]] whose rights discipline and public restraint helped preserve autonomy.
- The episode does not present any of its three topics as settled: cancer vaccines need trial evidence, oil-sands gains sit beside climate and access politics, and Parton's legacy is a selective obituary-style synthesis.

## Key Quotes
> "not yet known whether the treatment improves overall survival" - the cancer segment's evidence boundary.
