# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》829丨君子为什么斗不过小人？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills 147 CE, immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-830-donghan-ligu-wo-sui-si-yourong-lt-j7n9gotiw-negqxojoacf6ji|Hanji 830]] and the later Liang-family sequence. It opens with source-scoped self-cultivation advice, then follows [[DuQiaoLateHan|杜乔]] as he becomes taiwei, criticizes reward and appointment inversion, refuses two [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] requests, and is removed after a Luoyang earthquake.

The durable contribution is that "gentlemen lose to petty actors" as a structural claim, not only a moral complaint. Du Qiao can state the standards of worthy appointment and fair reward, but [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] gives no response, Liang Ji treats procedural resistance as disobedience, and [[TangHengLateHan|唐衡]] plus [[ZuoGuanLateHan|左悺]] can turn the earlier succession dispute around [[LiGuLateHan|李固]] and [[LiuSuanLateHan|刘蒜]] into resentment inside Huan.

## Key Claims

- In 147 CE, Jianhe first year, eclipse, amnesty, dragon, earthquake, and senior-office turnover create an unstable court setting in which Hu Guang is removed and Du Qiao becomes taiwei.
- Li Gu's fall is tied to his earlier preference for Liu Suan, which touched the interests of [[EmpressDowagerLiangNaLateHan|梁妠 / 梁太后]] and the Liang outer-relative bloc.
- After Liu Hong dies without a son, Liang Na installs Huan's younger brother [[LiuKuiBohaiKing|刘亏]] as [[BohaiKingdomLateHan|渤海王国]] king and Liu Hong's successor.
- Huan rewards Liang Ji, Liang relatives, Hu Guang, Zhao Jie, Yuan Tang, and eunuchs including Liu Guang, concentrating accession benefits around outer relatives and close attendants.
- Du Qiao's memorial argues that appointing the worthy and rewarding or punishing justly is the state's first task; he criticizes unmerited rewards to Liang relatives and eunuchs, and Huan gives no answer.
- After [[EmpressLiangYingLateHan|梁盈]] is made empress, Du Qiao blocks Liang Ji's attempt to use empress-level wedding ritual and refuses to recommend a corrupt shangshu candidate, making Liang resent him.
- Tang Heng and Zuo Guan slander Du Qiao and Li Gu by saying they opposed Huan's accession, and Huan comes to resent both men.
- [[LiuWenQingheRebelLateHan|刘文]] and [[LiuWeiNanjunRebelLateHan|刘伟]] attempt to install Liu Suan; [[XieHaoQingheLateHan|谢浩]] refuses their promised promotion and is killed, after which Liu Suan is demoted, exiled, and dies by suicide.

## Key Quotes

> "不用贤能、不执行完善制度" - Du Qiao's warning about failed rule.

> "没有功劳却得到官位、俸禄和封地" - the episode's summary of Du Qiao's criticism of Liang and eunuch rewards.

> "占据皇帝身边的话语通道" - the host's explanation of how slander turns political memory into personal resentment.
