# 《资治通鉴·周纪》94丨杀神白起，长平之战中的“草包”名将？（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode moves the [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] branch from [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-93-zai-mengli-cangzhe-toutian-gaiming-2-lrlulw0b6v0fb2xpr2i5ifem1h4n|Zhouji 93 part 2]] into the 260 BCE command crisis. [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] rejects [[YuQing|虞卿]]'s advice to rebuild [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] leverage with [[ChuState|楚国]] and [[QiState|齐国]], sends [[ZhengZhu|郑朱]] to [[Xianyang|咸阳]], and lets [[QinState|秦国]] turn a peace feeler into diplomatic isolation.

The battlefield branch then compounds the diplomatic failure. [[LianPo|廉颇]] shifts to defense after Zhao losses, [[FanJu|范雎]] uses a [[CounterintelligenceRumorWedge|反间流言楔入]] to make [[ZhaoKuo|赵括]] look more desirable than the experienced commander, and Zhao replaces Lian Po despite warnings from [[LinXiangru|蔺相如]], [[ZhaoShe|赵奢]], and [[ZhaoKuoMother|赵母]]. The episode closes with a Qin contrast: [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] secretly installs [[BaiQi|白起]] over [[WangHe|王龁]] and orders secrecy, while Zhao's public commander replacement gives Qin the clearer information position.

## Key Claims
- [[YuQing|虞卿]] argues that Zhao should first send senior figures and gifts to Chu and Qi to rebuild outside leverage, then negotiate with Qin.
- [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] follows [[LouChang|娄昌]]'s direct-peace logic instead, sending [[ZhengZhu|郑朱]] to Qin before Zhao has restored alliance support.
- Qin's reception of Zheng Zhu becomes a signaling trap: the other lords see Zhao seeking peace, but Qin does not actually settle.
- Repeated defeats under [[WangHe|王龁]] push [[LianPo|廉颇]] into a defensive posture, which Zhao Dan misreads as timidity or incompetence.
- [[FanJu|范雎]]'s rumor operation says Qin fears [[ZhaoKuo|赵括]] and not Lian Po, exploiting Zhao Dan's existing frustration with Lian Po.
- [[LinXiangru|蔺相如]] warns that appointing Zhao Kuo by reputation is like "胶柱鼓瑟": theoretical knowledge cannot substitute for flexible command.
- [[ZhaoShe|赵奢]] and [[ZhaoKuoMother|赵母]] both identify deeper problems in Zhao Kuo, including lightness toward war, arrogance toward officers, private accumulation of rewards, and lack of shared hardship with soldiers.
- Qin's secret replacement of Wang He with [[BaiQi|白起]] turns command secrecy into a strategic advantage, contrasting with Zhao's visible and rumor-driven replacement of Lian Po.

## Key Quotes
> "秦国不会跟赵国真正的议和。" - Yu Qing's warning as summarized by the episode.

> "凭借名声任命赵括，等于胶柱鼓瑟。" - Lin Xiangru's objection to Zhao Kuo's appointment.

> "敢泄露白起为将者斩。" - Qin's secrecy order around Bai Qi's appointment.
