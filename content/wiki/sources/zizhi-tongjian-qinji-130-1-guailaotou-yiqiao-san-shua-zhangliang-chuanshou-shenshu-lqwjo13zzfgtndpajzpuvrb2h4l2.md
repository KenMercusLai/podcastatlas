# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》130-1｜怪老头圯桥三耍张良 传授神书

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode reprises [[ZhangLiang|张良]]'s post-[[Bolangsha|博浪沙]] encounter with [[HuangshiGong|黄石公 / 圯上老人]], emphasizing the three tests, the gift of [[TaigongBingfa|《太公兵法》]], and the host's theme that "人生有度，过则为灾." It then continues the post-[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-4-jiuquangui-zhangliang-ruhe-zai-liubang-shouxia-anshen-lp6qwg3ma5hdy15g4box7pe23jav|Qinji 129-4]] anti-Qin field: [[LiuBang|刘邦]] and Zhang Liang join [[JingJu|景居 / 景驹]], Liu Bang fights Qin forces and again fails to recover [[FengCounty|丰邑 / 丰县]], while [[ZhaoPingRebel|昭平]] uses [[ChenSheng|陈胜]]'s name to push [[XiangLiang|项梁]] and [[XiangYu|项羽]] west from [[KuaijiCommandery|会稽郡]]. The closing [[ChenYing|陈英 / 陈婴]] story makes the episode's distinct contribution: in a fractured rebellion, refusing to claim kingship and choosing attachment can be a form of [[ClaimRestraintThroughAttachment|有所归属式名位节制]].

## Key Claims
- [[LiuBang|刘邦]] later praises [[ZhangLiang|张良]] as the strategist whose planning helps decide victory from far away, and the episode uses Zhang Liang's later "功成身退" as a positive example of measured life choices.
- After the failed [[Bolangsha|博浪沙]] attack in 218 BCE, Zhang Liang reaches Xiapi and meets [[HuangshiGong|黄石公 / 圯上老人]] at a bridge.
- Huangshi Gong drops his shoe below the bridge, orders Zhang Liang to retrieve it, and accepts Zhang Liang's kneeling assistance as the first test of patience and humility.
- Huangshi Gong twice rebukes Zhang Liang for arriving later than him at dawn; on the third appointment, Zhang Liang arrives before midnight and finally passes the test.
- Huangshi Gong gives Zhang Liang a book, promises that it can make him a teacher of kings, and says the text will become useful when the world falls into disorder ten years later.
- The episode identifies the book as [[TaigongBingfa|《太公兵法》]], also called [[Liutao|《六韬》 / 《太公六韬》]], and says it contains six juan and sixty chapters.
- The source describes the text as broadly concerned with war and related problems, with strategic and tactical argument as its strongest parts.
- In the current Qin Er Shi year, [[JingJu|景居 / 景驹]] is stationed at Liu County, and [[LiuBang|刘邦]] plus [[ZhangLiang|张良]] formally join his side.
- Liu Bang wants help from Jing Ju to counterattack [[FengCounty|丰邑 / 丰县]] after [[YongChi|雍齿]]'s defection, but urgent Qin pressure interrupts that plan.
- A subordinate of [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] attacks through the Xiao-Dang line; Liu Bang and Ning Jun fight him near Xiao County, suffer a slight setback, then recover enough to take Dang County and absorb nearly six thousand troops.
- Liu Bang later takes Xiayi, returns against Feng, and again fails to retake it from Yong Chi.
- [[ZhaoPingRebel|昭平]], originally sent by Chen Sheng toward Guangling, hears of Chen Sheng's death and crosses the Yangtze to see [[XiangLiang|项梁]] as [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]]'s troops approach.
- Zhao Ping falsely claims Chen Sheng has appointed Xiang Liang as Chu upper pillar of state and urges him to lead the already pacified Jiangdong / [[KuaijiCommandery|会稽郡]] troops west against Qin.
- Xiang Liang accepts the appointment, and he and [[XiangYu|项羽]] cross the Yangtze with eight thousand men.
- When Xiang Liang asks [[ChenYing|陈英 / 陈婴]] to ally, Chen Ying's mother warns that sudden fame without ancestral standing is inauspicious and advises him to attach himself to a stronger house rather than claim kingship.
- Chen Ying follows his mother's advice and does not dare become king, making him the episode's second case, after Zhang Liang, for knowing limits in disorder.

## Key Quotes
> "人生有度，过则为灾" - the episode's explicit framing lesson for Zhang Liang and Chen Ying.

> "孺子可教" - Huangshi Gong's judgment after Zhang Liang bears the bridge test.

> "读此可以为王者师" - the source's summary of Huangshi Gong's promise about the military text.
