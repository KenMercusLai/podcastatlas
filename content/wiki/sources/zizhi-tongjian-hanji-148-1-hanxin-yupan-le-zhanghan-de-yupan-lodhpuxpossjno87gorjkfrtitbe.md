# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》148-1｜韩信预判了章邯的预判

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues directly from [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-147-pimei-zhugeliang-longzhongdui-de-hanzhongdui-lqucctzhipgtcvtjlixvj5qbdeai|Hanji 147]] by moving [[HanXin|韩信]]'s [[HanzhongDui|汉中对]] from strategic diagnosis into the campaign that breaks [[ThreeQinContainment|三秦压制]]. It argues that Han Xin's success is not reducible to the familiar "暗渡陈仓" formula: before the strike, he pushes [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s army through [[HanInheritsQinSystem|汉承秦制式军制改造]], then uses [[LayeredFeintCentralBreakthrough|多路佯动中央突破]] to make [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] overread the Han plan. The result is the defeat of Zhang Han around [[ChenCang|陈仓]] and Haozhi, the containment collapse through [[SimaXin|司马欣]] and [[DongYiQin|董翳]]'s surrender, and the conversion of [[Hanzhong|汉中]] from confined base into recovered [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] access.

## Key Claims
- Han Xin's two linked tasks after becoming commander are a route plan and "申军法": the episode treats military organization as a condition of tactical success.
- The source reads Han army reform through [[MilitaryMeritRankSystem|军功爵制]], [[ShangYangReforms|商鞅变法]], and the broader Qin institutional inheritance later summarized as [[HanInheritsQinSystem|汉承秦制]].
- Liu Bang's campaign does not simply rebuild the gallery roads; it uses a less expected path through old roads and small routes toward Zhang Han's center.
- The episode notes that "明修栈道，暗渡陈仓" is not from standard histories but from the Yuan drama 《气英布》, so the idiom is a later reception package around a more complex operation.
- Han Xin sends [[GuanYing|灌婴]] through the Ziwu route and [[FanKuai|樊哙]] through the Qishan route, while [[CaoShen|曹参]] attacks Xiabian to deepen the false impression that the Han main force is moving on the flank.
- Zhang Han is treated as a competent veteran rather than a fool; his mistake is that he thinks he has recognized Han Xin's feint and therefore commits attention to the side threats.
- Han Xin's main force breaks through by the center at [[ChenCang|陈仓]], turning Zhang Han's anti-feint confidence into an opening.
- After Zhang Han loses at Chen Cang and Haozhi, he retreats to Feiqiu; Sima Xin and Dong Yi recognize the collapse of the Three Qin position and surrender.
- The host contrasts Han Xin's successful return from Hanzhong to [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]]'s later failed northern expeditions, emphasizing that a route cannot be copied apart from timing, opponents, organization, and conditions.
- [[SunziBingfa|《孙子兵法》]] and [[Laozi|《老子》]] are used as interpretive frames for hidden defense, sudden attack, and adaptive action through terrain.

## Key Quotes
> "申军法" - the episode's shorthand for Han Xin's army-reform task after taking command.

> "明修栈道，暗渡陈仓" - the later idiom whose reception the episode separates from the full campaign design.

> "善守者藏于九地之下，善攻者动于九天之上" - the Sunzi frame used to explain why the final attack feels sudden after concealed preparation.
