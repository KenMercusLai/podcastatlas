# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》682丨揭秘古代帝皇的用人之道

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills a Jianwu 2 / 26 CE layer before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-683-fangniuwa-liupenzi-buxiang-dang-huangdi-dan-meiyudi-lt7hmrorydsazpkuwby1ntd7t1eh|Hanji 683]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-684-zhending-wang-liuyang-weishenme-beipan-guangwudi-liuxiu-lhtdqtfxihyua1nj8ofsdxl8il8d|Hanji 684]]. It centers on [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]]'s personnel reading under disorder: [[DengYuEasternHan|邓禹]] diverges from Liu Xiu's Chang'an order, Deng Yu's officers fall into mutiny, and Liu Xiu predicts that Huang Fang can capture Feng Yin by reading the closest-tie structure.

The second half pairs two authority failures with two authority repairs. [[ChimeiRebellion|赤眉]] palace disorder exposes [[LiuPenziEasternHan|刘盆子]] as a frightened adolescent figurehead before the failed-abdication scene in Hanji 683, while [[WangHongEasternHan|王闳]] uses court mandate and personal nerve to make [[ZhangBuEasternHan|张步]] yield Langya affairs despite Wang Hong's weaker military position. The durable synthesis is that early Guangwu consolidation depends on reading people, relationships, and formal authority before force can become usable order.

## Key Claims

- Liu Xiu presses Deng Yu to attack Chang'an quickly and soothe the people, but Deng Yu instead attacks counties in Shang commandery, gathers soldiers and grain, and returns to Dayao.
- Feng Yin and Zong Xin fight for power inside Deng Yu's army; Feng Yin kills Zong Xin, threatens Deng Yu, and is later seized by Huang Fang exactly as Liu Xiu predicts after asking who is closest to him.
- Zong Guang receives the surrender of Wang Kuang, Hu Yin, Cheng Dan, and other Liu Xuan-side generals, but executes them at Anyi after Wang Kuang attempts flight.
- Wei Xiao defeats Feng Yin at Gaoping after Feng Yin's westward move against Tianshui, and Deng Yu appoints Wei Xiao West State General with authority over Liangzhou and Shuofang affairs.
- Chimei's laji banquet collapses into palace gate-breaking, wine-and-meat seizure, and mutual killing, leaving Liu Penzi frightened and often crying alone.
- Liu Xiu brings Yin Lihua, Princess Huyang, and Princess Ningping to Luoyang, makes Yin Lihua a guiren, and summons Li Tong, who had married Ningping.
- Wang Hong, appointed Langya governor by Liu Xuan, cannot defeat Zhang Bu militarily but confronts him in person, invokes court appointment, and makes Zhang Bu apologize and hand over commandery affairs.
