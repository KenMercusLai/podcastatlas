# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》188｜古代中国的“天下无税”县

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the [[YingBu|英布 / 黥布]] rebellion branch opened in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-187-yingbu-panluan-liubang-weihe-tuobingti-qinzheng-lvjafotlnvmyqmwaeav3novz1dox|Hanji 187]]. [[LiuBang|刘邦]] follows [[ZhangLiang|张良]]'s caution, waits behind fortifications near Qi County until [[LiuFeiQiKing|刘肥]] and [[CaoShen|曹参]] arrive with Qi troops, and then defeats Ying Bu through a multi-sided Han attack by Cao Shen, [[LiShang|郦商]], and [[GuanYing|灌婴]]. The second half turns the victory into a late-life homecoming: wounded and aware of decline, Liu Bang returns to [[PeiCounty|沛县]], sings the Great Wind Song, grants Pei permanent tax and corvee exemption under [[TangmuYi|汤沐邑]] language, and finally extends the same privilege to [[FengCounty|丰邑 / 丰县]] after old grievances over [[YongChi|雍齿]] are pleaded past by Pei-origin ministers.

## Key Claims
- Ying Bu remains tactically dangerous, but Hanji 188 confirms Hanji 187's bounded-strategy reading: once Liu Bang waits for reinforcements instead of rushing into a duel, Ying Bu can be defeated by coordinated Han pressure.
- Zhang Liang's late advice matters in operational form, not only as sickbed counsel: Liu Bang fortifies, waits, and attacks after Qi-side reinforcements arrive.
- [[CaoShen|曹参]], [[LiShang|郦商]], and [[GuanYing|灌婴]] convert the campaign from a Liu Bang-Ying Bu confrontation into an enveloping army problem that Ying Bu cannot solve.
- Liu Bang's arrow wound in this campaign is linked to his earlier [[GuangwuChuHan|广武]] wound, making the victory part of his physical decline rather than a clean return to command vitality.
- The Pei County return joins memory, emotion, and rule: Liu Bang's song, tears, and feast are personal, but the tax/corvee exemption turns hometown feeling into state privilege.
- The episode uses [[TangmuYi|汤沐邑]] language in a reversed way: instead of assigning a locality's revenue for rulerly use, Liu Bang treats Pei as his personal root while exempting its people from rent, taxes, and corvee.
- Feng's successful petition shows that local memory remains contested. Liu Bang remembers being betrayed through Yong Chi's old defection, but the earlier reward of Yong Chi and the ministers' pleading let him move the old grievance into reconciliation.
- The episode ends with the succession problem still unresolved, previewing how Liu Bang will handle [[LiuYing|刘盈]], [[QiJi|戚姬 / 戚夫人]], and the favored-heir branch near death.

## Key Quotes
> "大风起兮云飞扬，威加海内兮归故乡，安得猛士兮守四方" - the Great Wind Song as framed in the episode.

> "世世代代免除租税徭役" - the material privilege Liu Bang grants to Pei County and later Feng.

> "人之将死总思乡" - the episode's late-life reading of Liu Bang's return to Pei.
