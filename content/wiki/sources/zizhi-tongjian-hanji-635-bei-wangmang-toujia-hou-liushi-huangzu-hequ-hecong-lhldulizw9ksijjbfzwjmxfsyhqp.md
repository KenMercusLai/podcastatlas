# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》635丨被王莽偷家后，刘氏皇族何去何从？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 9 CE opening consolidation of [[WangMang|王莽]]'s [[XinDynasty|新朝]] between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-634-huozai-nanren-yingzi-beihou-de-ta-can-can-can-lm3wub0l70yxwwyvwi15j93i9lwk|Hanji 634]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-636-wangmang-dang-huangdi-hou-shenjing-cuoluan-le-lq-essrurb6jdefzjrfgoujw5uby|Hanji 636]]. It asks why the many descendants of the [[WesternHanDynasty|西汉]] imperial house so rarely resisted, then follows Wang Mang's removal of Liu royal kinsmen from commandery posts, confinement of [[RuziYing|孺子婴 / 刘婴]] in Ding'an Palace, administrative renaming, Wang-clan title distribution by mourning-grade categories, and downgrading of former Han kings and marquises.

The durable synthesis is that early Xin rule neutralizes the old dynasty through status redesign rather than only violence. Wang Mang separates Liu kinsmen from territorial command, isolates the child dynastic symbol, rewrites offices and place names to make Xin categories look normal, constructs a Wang-centered kinship hierarchy, and demotes former Han titles before later stripping them away.

## Key Claims

- Wang Mang reduces immediate Liu royal capacity by moving Liu-surname commandery governors into central remonstrance posts where they lose local administrative bases.
- Liu Ying's Ding'an Palace treatment is presented as protective settlement in form but practical confinement in substance, including control over his attendants and speech environment.
- The episode treats mass official and place-name changes as symbolic recoding: old administrative language is replaced so Xin rule appears institutionally complete.
- Wang Mang uses the five mourning grades as a template for ranking Wang-family relatives, converting kin distance into title hierarchy.
- Former Han princes and marquises are downgraded from kings to dukes and from marquises to sons before eventually losing titles, weakening old-dynasty public status.
- The source links old-royal containment, administrative renaming, and title redesign as one early consolidation package, not as isolated curiosities.
- The closing question about Xin's five-surname imperial kinship sets up Hanji 636's genealogy and surname-privilege expansion.

## Key Quotes

> "很少有人提出异议" - the opening puzzle about Liu royal silence after Wang Mang's accession.

> "实际上更接近软禁" - the episode's judgment on the Ding'an Palace arrangement for Liu Ying.

> "五服" - the kinship-mourning system Wang Mang uses to grade Wang-family titles.
