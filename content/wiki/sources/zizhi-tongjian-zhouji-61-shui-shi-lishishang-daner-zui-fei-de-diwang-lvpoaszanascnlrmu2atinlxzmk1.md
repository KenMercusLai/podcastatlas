# 《资治通鉴·周纪》61丨谁是历史上胆儿最肥的帝王？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode covers [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s fourteenth through sixteenth years, 301-299 BCE, while moving the Qin-Chu and Zhao branches forward. Its central figure is [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]], who abdicates to ten-year-old [[ZhaoHe|赵何]], calls himself主父, and then personally enters [[QinState|秦国]] in disguise to inspect terrain and [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]]. The episode treats him as a daring strategic innovator whose plan to bypass [[HanguPass|函谷关]] through the northern route toward [[Xianyang|咸阳]] is inseparable from the succession risk created by favoring the younger son.

## Key Claims
- In 301 BCE, the episode records a solar eclipse, Qin's attack on [[HanState|韩国]], the execution of the Shu governor Ying Yun, Qin's revenge against [[ChuState|楚国]] after the [[MiHeng|芈横]] incident, Zhao's campaign against [[ZhongshanState|中山国]], and [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]]'s death followed by [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]]'s accession.
- The Ying Yun notice is source-critical: the episode says [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] can read like a rebellion case, while 《华阳国志》 makes it look more like a court-intrigue injustice.
- Qin joins Han, [[WeiState|魏国]], and [[QiState|齐国]] against Chu at Chongqiu after Mi Heng kills a Qin official and flees; Chu commander Tang Mei is killed and Chu loses the battle.
- In 300 BCE, Qin's Mi Rong attacks Chu, kills Jing Que, and takes Xiangcheng; frightened [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] sends crown prince Mi Heng to Qi as a hostage to seek repair.
- The episode says the Tongjian notice that [[LouHuan|楼缓]] immediately replaced [[Chulizi|樗里疾 / 樗里子]] as Qin chancellor is likely wrong. It follows [[Shiji|《史记》]] in sequencing [[WeiRan|魏冉]], [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]], Lou Huan, and then Wei Ran again across 300-295 BCE.
- In 299 BCE, Zhao Wuling Wang transfers the throne to ten-year-old Zhao He, appoints [[FeiYi|肥义]] as chancellor, and keeps a military role for himself as主父.
- Zhao Wuling Wang disguises himself as a Zhao envoy to Qin so he can inspect Qin's mountains and rivers and judge Qin Zhaoxiang Wang in person.
- Qin Zhaoxiang Wang senses the envoy is extraordinary only after the meeting; by the time Qin tries to detain him, Zhao Wuling Wang has crossed the border.
- Zhao Wuling Wang's anti-Qin plan is to avoid the conventional Hangu Pass approach and instead move from Yunzhong and Jiuyuan south toward Xianyang.
- The episode's central tension is that Zhao Wuling Wang's bold strategic imagination and his succession decision are linked: leaving day-to-day rule lets him focus on war planning, but favoring Zhao He over the elder branch seeds the later Shaqiu disaster.

## Key Quotes
> "历史上第一个为王的间谍" - the episode's label for Zhao Wuling Wang's disguised Qin mission.

> "谋事在人，成事在天" - the closing frame for the gap between ambitious planning and historical outcome.

## Contradictions
- Clarifies [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-49-mengzi-yue-wo-jiao-ni-zhiguo-ni-que-ba-wo-dang-huaping-lhy14pbf9thcfdiaulcmf8dwcef8|Zhouji 49]] on [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]]'s death: Zhouji 49 already flagged its death/accession notice as too early; this episode places the death in 301 BCE and treats [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]]'s accession as belonging here.
- Contradicts the received Tongjian chancellor notice as presented by the episode: instead of [[LouHuan|楼缓]] immediately succeeding [[Chulizi|樗里疾 / 樗里子]] after Chulizi's death, the episode follows [[Shiji|《史记》]] and places [[WeiRan|魏冉]] first, then [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]], then Lou Huan, then Wei Ran again.
