# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》308｜被唐诗吹成神的他，竟是个路痴？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens the second Hexi campaign with [[HuoQubing|霍去病]] proposing a long desert detour intended to reach the rear of the [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] force rather than drive it away through a frontal attack. Huo and [[GongsunAoWesternHan|公孙敖]] take separate routes toward Juyan, while the episode postpones their result and follows a parallel force under [[LiGuangHanGeneral|李广]] and [[ZhangQianWesternHan|张骞]].

Li Guang's four thousand cavalry becomes separated from Zhang Qian's larger contingent and is surrounded by a force attributed to the Left Wise King. [[LiGanWesternHan|李敢]] rides through the opposing formation to steady the troops; Li Guang then uses an outward-facing circular defense, holds arrows until close range, personally shoots several opposing officers, and survives until reinforcements arrive. The episode treats his courage and morale control as real while also preserving the court's harsher result: severe losses make his contribution功过相当 and bring no reward, while Zhang Qian is punished for delay.

## Key Claims

- Huo Qubing proposes bypassing the narrow Hexi corridor through the eastern desert so that cavalry can cut the enemy's retreat instead of merely pursuing a withdrawing force.
- The source presents the march as a high-risk operation of more than two thousand li through heat, sparse vegetation, and limited water, with Huo and Gongsun Ao expected to reunite near Juyan and its water supply.
- Li Guang leads four thousand cavalry from Youbeiping while Zhang Qian leads ten thousand on a separate route; the contingents become too widely separated for timely mutual support.
- After a much larger Xiongnu force surrounds Li Guang, Li Gan's small mounted probe is used as a confidence signal before the force adopts a circular defense.
- Li Guang conserves scarce arrows by ordering soldiers to wait for close range, personally shoots several enemy deputy commanders, and repeatedly steadies frightened troops.
- Reinforcements arrive on the following day and the opposing force withdraws, but the episode says Li Guang's severe losses lead the court to judge功过相当 and grant neither reward nor punishment.
- Zhang Qian's delay is treated as a military offense; the episode says he avoids execution through payment and loses rank.

## Key Quotes

> "匈奴人没什么了不起。" - Li Gan's reported reassurance after riding through the opposing formation.

> "功过相当，不赏不罚。" - the episode's summary of the court's judgment on Li Guang.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. The source adds an earlier Li Guang encirclement to a profile previously centered on his final Mobei route failure; the two campaigns must not be merged.
- The title's “路痴” is promotional framing. The supplied episode says the Li Guang and Zhang Qian contingents became separated but does not say Li Guang got lost in this battle; Li Guang's documented missing-guide and lost-route story belongs to his later final campaign.
- The episode says Li Guang completed a diversion or coordination task, while the court's功过相当 judgment emphasizes his losses. The wiki preserves this evaluative tension rather than independently deciding that the reward ruling was unjust.
