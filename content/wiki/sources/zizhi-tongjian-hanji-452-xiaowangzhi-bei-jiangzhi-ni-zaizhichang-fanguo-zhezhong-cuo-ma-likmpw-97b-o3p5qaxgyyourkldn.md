# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》452｜萧望之被降职，你在职场犯过这种错吗

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the [[XiaoWangzhiWesternHan|萧望之]] branch after the [[HanYanshouWesternHan|韩延寿]] case by explaining why [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] removes Xiao from the yushi dafu post and makes him crown-prince tutor. The trigger is Xiao's contempt toward [[BingJiWesternHan|丙吉]], but the deeper issue is relational and political: Bing Ji is not only an old chancellor but the official who saved the infant Liu Xun during the witchcraft-disaster aftermath.

The second half extends the [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] civil-war sequence after [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-449-yichu-huangdan-naoju-xiongnu-wuda-chanyu-duoquan-huopin-lo39-mygg6xxo8cwcyvpqzcpzhrm|Hanji 449]]. [[HuhanyeChanyuWesternHan|呼韩邪单于]] defeats and absorbs rivals, some Xiongnu nobles surrender to Han, but new claimants later appear in the west and east, showing that the five-chanyu crisis contracts and then re-fragments rather than ending cleanly.

## Key Claims

- Xiao Wangzhi's demotion follows his visible contempt toward Bing Ji, whom Emperor Xuan deeply respects because Bing Ji had saved and cared for the future emperor in childhood.
- The episode reads contempt as a political mistake because disrespecting one person can also disrespect the relationship network and emotional order behind that person.
- The chancellor's secretary provides a formal impeachment route by accusing Xiao of arrogance toward Bing Ji and improper private use of subordinates and funds.
- Xuan does not destroy Xiao, but removes him from the powerful yushi dafu office and makes him taizi taifu while appointing [[HuangBaWesternHan|黄霸]] as yushi dafu.
- The host treats the case as a workplace warning: even real dislike or superiority judgments should not be publicly performed when the other person's hidden relationships and symbolic value are unknown.
- Huhanye defeats Tuqi Chanyu's forces, Tuqi dies, Cheli submits, and Xiongnu appears briefly reduced from five chanyus to one dominant claimant.
- Continued Xiongnu disorder sends nobles into Han submission and then produces new eastern and western chanyu claimants, restoring a three-chanyu field.

## Key Quotes

> "害人之心不可有，防人之心不可无" - the episode's opening conduct frame.

> "轻视丙吉，就等于轻视汉宣帝" - the host's compressed political reading of Xiao's mistake.
