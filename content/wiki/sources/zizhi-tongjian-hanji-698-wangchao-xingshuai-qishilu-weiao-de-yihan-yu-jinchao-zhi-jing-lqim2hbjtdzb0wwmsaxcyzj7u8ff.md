# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》698丨王朝兴衰启示录：韦敖的遗憾与今朝之镜

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the Jianwu 5 / 29 CE layer before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-699-gan-yu-liuxiu-zhengxiong-de-ta-weihe-candan-shouchang-lth-8mndudj3khf9cnsouj86c1-z|Hanji 699]] and the later [[WeiXiaoEasternHan|隗嚣]] breakaway sequence. [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] tests Wei Xiao by asking him to attack [[GongsunShuEasternHan|公孙述]], then reads Wei Xiao's refusal, court-avoidance, and hostage-only compromise through [[WeiXunEasternHan|隗恂]] as evidence that his allegiance remains non-credible.

The episode also explains why Wei Xiao's camp begins to thin out before open rebellion. [[ZhengXingEasternHan|郑兴]] leaves through a filial-burial request, [[MaYuanEasternHan|马援]] moves east with his household, [[WangYuanWeiXiaoRetainer|王元]] pushes a terrain-backed holdout strategy, and [[ShentuGangEasternHan|申屠刚]] unsuccessfully urges submission to Liu Xiu.

## Key Claims

- Liu Xiu uses the proposed attack on Gongsun Shu as a behavioral loyalty test for Wei Xiao, not merely as a military request.
- Wei Xiao's refusal cites Sanfu weakness and [[LuFangEasternHan|卢芳]]'s northern threat, but the episode reads the answer as two-sided positioning rather than a full strategic commitment.
- [[LaiXiEasternHan|来歙]] and Ma Yuan repeatedly urge Wei Xiao to attend court in Luoyang, while Wei Xiao responds with deferential language that avoids actual arrival.
- Wei Xiao sends his eldest son Wei Xun to Luoyang as a hostage and court-service signal after hearing that Liu Yong and [[PengChongEasternHan|彭宠]] have been killed.
- Zheng Xing's exit and Ma Yuan's household move show that trusted advisers can leave when a patron's suspicion and indecision make the future unsafe.
- Wang Yuan argues for closing [[HanguPass|函谷关]], relying on Tianshui's intact forces and waiting while Gongsun Shu, Lu Fang, and other claimants keep the field unsettled.
- Shentu Gang argues the opposite line: human support and mandate have turned toward Liu Xiu, so continued fear and desire will make Wei Xiao lose both loyalty and reputation.

## Key Quotes

> "人心苦，来自不知足。" - the opening moral frame for Wei Xiao's dissatisfaction.

> "脚踏两只船。" - the host's interpretation of Wei Xiao's refusal to attack Gongsun Shu.

> "你到底在害怕什么？又在贪图什么？" - Shentu Gang's challenge to Wei Xiao as summarized by the episode.
