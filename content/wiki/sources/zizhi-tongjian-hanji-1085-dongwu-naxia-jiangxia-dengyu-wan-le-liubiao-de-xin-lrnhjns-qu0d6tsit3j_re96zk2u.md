# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1085丨东吴拿下江夏，等于剜了刘表的心？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode reconstructs [[SunQuan|孙权]]'s 208 CE attack on [[HuangZu|黄祖]] at [[JiangxiaCommanderyLateHan|江夏郡]]. Following [[GanNing|甘宁]]'s recommendation, Wu treats [[MianKouLateHan|沔口]] as a river chokepoint: [[DongXiLateHan|董袭]] and [[LingTong|凌统]] break the anchored mengchong barrier, [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]] kills [[ChenJiuLateHan|陈就]], and the campaign ends with the fall of Xiakou and Huang Zu's death.

The episode argues that victory transfers more than a city. Captured population, shipbuilding capacity, trained naval manpower, and access along the Yangtze-Han corridor strengthen Sun Quan while removing Liu Biao's eastern naval shield; the host also presents the attack as Sun-family revenge and as preparation for the approaching Cao Cao confrontation.

## Key Claims

- Gan Ning advises Sun Quan that taking Jingzhou and controlling the Yangtze require first removing Huang Zu from Jiangxia.
- The episode presents Jiangxia and Mian Kou, near the Han-Yangtze confluence, as the gateway that blocks Wu's westward movement.
- Huang Zu anchors two hide-covered mengchong warships across Mian Kou and uses massed archery to deny approach.
- Dong Xi and Ling Tong each lead a small armored assault party; Dong Xi cuts the mooring cables and opens the channel for the main force.
- Huang Zu sends naval commander Chen Jiu to respond, and Lyu Meng kills him in the fighting.
- Wu captures Xiakou, kills Huang Zu, and reportedly takes tens of thousands of people.
- The host interprets Jiangxia's population, shipyards, and naval-training system as a transfer of military-economic capacity, not merely territorial gain.
- The campaign fulfills the strategic direction attributed to Gan Ning and supplies a forward position before the Red Cliffs crisis.

## Key Quotes

> “杀一个黄祖，胜过十万大军叩门。” - the host's formulation of Huang Zu's strategic and psychological importance.

> “等于剜走了刘表的水军心脏。” - the episode's interpretation of Jiangxia's naval-economic loss.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is recorded. The episode fills the missing campaign between Hanji 1061's interrupted attack on Huang Zu and Hanji 1086's post-victory clemency scene, while supplying detail behind Hanji 1091's compressed reference to Huang Zu's death.
- Transcript normalization: “皇祖／皇族” is normalized to [[HuangZu|黄祖]], “猛冲” to mengchong warships, and “棉口” to [[MianKouLateHan|沔口]].
