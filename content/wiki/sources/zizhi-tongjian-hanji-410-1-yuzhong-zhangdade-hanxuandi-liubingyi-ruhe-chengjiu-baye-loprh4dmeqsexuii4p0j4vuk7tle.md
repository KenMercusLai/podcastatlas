# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》410-1｜狱中长大的汉宣帝刘病己，如何成就霸业？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode fills the accession bridge between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-409-hanxuandi-liubingyi-cong-yuzhong-zouchulai-de-chuanqi-huangdi-ljyxm9nd5rvheusna29wkxuraqux|Hanji 409]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-411-hanxuandi-liubingyi-dengji-hou-de-diyichang-huangwei-daweiji-lks_pia3lxwv_qao02pm4mv-xype|Hanji 411]]. It follows [[EmperorXuanOfHan|刘病已 / 后来的汉宣帝]] after his prison survival: [[BingJiWesternHan|丙吉]] and [[ZhangHeWesternHan|张贺]] help move him into safer care, Zhang He arranges his marriage to [[XuPingjunWesternHan|许平君]] through [[XuGuanghanWesternHan|徐广汉]], and the later court selects him after [[LiuHeHaihunHou|刘贺]] is deposed.

The durable synthesis is that Xuan's accession rests on both personal kindness and institutional calculation. His commoner life, study, friendships, and knowledge of local officials give the episode a "民间经验" explanation for later rulership, while Bing Ji, [[DuYannianWesternHan|杜延年]], [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]], and [[ZhangAnshiWesternHan|张安世]] turn that hidden candidate into a formally installed emperor under the empress dowager's authority.

## Key Claims

- Liu Bingyi's later reputation as a ruler is framed through early hardship: prison survival, commoner life, village knowledge, and sympathy for ordinary suffering.
- Bing Ji continues the rescue arc by finding family care for the child and helping place him into the Ye Ting system.
- Zhang He, formerly attached to crown prince Liu Ju, uses his position and personal resources to educate, support, and arrange marriage for Liu Bingyi.
- Zhang Anshi's refusal of Zhang He's granddaughter marriage plan shows that Liu Bingyi is still politically marginal before accession.
- Zhang He instead brokers the marriage between Liu Bingyi and Xu Guanghan's daughter Xu Pingjun, with his own wealth helping overcome household resistance.
- After Liu He is deposed in 74 BCE, Bing Ji recommends Liu Bingyi to Huo Guang as a morally and educationally suitable imperial great-grandson.
- Du Yannian reinforces the recommendation, and Huo Guang, Zhang Anshi, and the assembled ministers petition [[ShangguanTaihouWesternHan|上官太后]] to install Liu Bingyi.
- Liu Bingyi is ceremonially washed, brought to the zongzheng office, presented to the empress dowager, made Yangwu Hou, and then receives the imperial seal and cords.
- [[YanYannianWesternHan|严延年]] immediately impeaches Huo Guang for unauthorized deposition and installation, showing that even a formally completed succession carried controversy around regent power.
