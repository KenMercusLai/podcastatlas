# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》804丨给你“糖”的人，不一定是贵人！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 125 CE palace-coup layer before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-805-tongtou-renxing-de-nvren-jing-neng-yuce-jixiong-ll3bzoyrkbtnvt-aqu9nqelqonew|Hanji 805]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-806-xiangyao-chengshi-zhaodui-ren-hen-guanjian-luswh-p-oyutysxue6shweqcwqxs|Hanji 806]]. It follows [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝 / Liu Bao]]'s enthronement through seal and palace control, the destruction of [[YanXianLateHan|阎显]] and the Yan outer-relative network, the rewarding of [[SunChengLateHan|孙程]] and nineteen eunuchs, and the merit-list risk around [[WangKangLateHan|王康]] and [[MiaoGuangLateHan|苗光]].

The durable synthesis is that restoring a ruler does not end political danger; it changes where danger sits. Once the Yan family falls, the same eunuch merit bloc that made restoration possible must rank rewards, manage hidden hesitation, and prevent internal rivalries from becoming a new threat field.

## Key Claims

- The opening workplace advice treats helping interest-competitor colleagues as risky because gratitude is uncertain and intervention can expose the helper; this remains a source-scoped analogy rather than a historical claim.
- The episode explains Eastern Han outer-relative dominance through short-lived emperors, young successors, empress-dowager regency, and great-landlord family power; eunuchs and outer relatives become structurally opposed palace actors.
- In 125 CE, Shun's supporters seize the imperial seal in the North Palace, bring Liu Bao to Jiade Hall, arrest and execute Yan Xian and his brothers, exile the Yan family, and move Yan Taihou to a detached palace.
- Sun Cheng and the nineteen eunuchs are heavily rewarded after the restoration, with Sun Cheng receiving the top title, Wang Kang and [[WangGuoEunuchLateHan|王国]] receiving the next level, and Li Run excluded because he joined midway rather than at the first commitment point.
- The Zhangtai Gate killing of Yan-side generals Liu An and Chen Da functions as a point-of-no-return act that distinguishes committed coup participants from hesitant followers.
- Wang Kang's quiet inclusion of Miao Guang on the merit list creates a hidden liability; Miao Guang later panics when no certificate arrives and confesses the manipulation to the Huangmen Ling.
- The host reads Sun Cheng's decision not to punish Wang Kang and Miao Guang as calculated alliance management: it wins Wang Kang, displays magnanimity to other eunuchs, keeps Wang Kang available to balance Wang Guo, and leaves Sun Cheng with leverage.
- Shun's restored court recalls or promotes older attendants and supporters, while [[CuiYuanLateHan|崔瑗]] becomes a source-scoped survival case after refusing to reopen his risky pre-coup advice through petitions or testimony.

## Key Quotes

> "给你糖的人，不一定是贵人" - title-level warning about gifts that may create risk rather than protection.

> "事不关己，高高挂起" - opening phrase reframed as prudence when another person's interest conflict is not yours.

> "外部矛盾消失之后，内部矛盾就成了主要矛盾" - host's transition from Yan-family enemy to eunuch merit-bloc rivalry.
