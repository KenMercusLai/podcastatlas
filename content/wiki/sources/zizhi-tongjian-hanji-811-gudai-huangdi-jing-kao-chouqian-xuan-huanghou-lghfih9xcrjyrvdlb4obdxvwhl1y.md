# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》811丨古代皇帝竟靠抽签选皇后？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 132 CE layer of [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]]'s reign immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-812-guren-40-sui-caineng-dangguan-lordzuaqhzghyd93tpm0fql1cigv|Hanji 812]]. It centers on ministers blocking a lottery-based empress selection, [[EmpressDowagerLiangNaLateHan|梁妠]] being elevated through a virtue-and-deference narrative, and [[LiangShangLateHan|梁商]] receiving status through his daughter's appointment.

The second half turns to [[ZuoXiongLateHan|左雄]]'s personnel reform agenda: stabilize good local officials, punish lawless and absconding officials beyond ordinary amnesty, reduce grassroots extraction, and test Xiaolian candidates by age and administrative competence. The episode frames these measures as an early Shun-era attempt to repair appointment quality, inspection truth, and local administrative capacity.

## Key Claims
- [[HuGuangLateHan|胡广]] and Guo Qian rejected [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]]'s plan to choose an empress by lottery or divination, arguing that empress selection required ritual and institutional standards rather than randomness.
- The proposed empress-selection standard ranked candidates by virtue first, then age, then appearance, creating a public legitimacy rationale for choosing [[EmpressDowagerLiangNaLateHan|梁妠]].
- Liang Na's repeated refusal to monopolize imperial favor was presented as filial restraint and public-minded virtue, which strengthened the justification for making her empress.
- [[LiangShangLateHan|梁商]]'s elevation to Tejin and then Zhijinwu after Liang Na's appointment shows how empress selection could immediately alter outer-relative status.
- [[ZuoXiongLateHan|左雄]] diagnosed local government failure as a personnel system problem: short official tenure, fake performance metrics, bribery, resignation games, amnesty loopholes, and low-level extraction from commoners.
- Zuo Xiong's local-official proposal tied [[LocalOfficialTenureStability|地方官任期稳定]] to performance verification, rewarding officials who loved the people while permanently excluding lawless officials from renewed appointment.
- Zuo Xiong's Xiaolian reform set a default age threshold, required candidates to report for examination, separated classic-learning tests from clerical drafting tests, and allowed exceptions for unusually talented candidates.
- The episode also records external and regional disorder: drought at Luoyang, Zhang He's Yangzhou rebellion across six commanderies, and Wuhuan forces fighting Xianbei under Geng Ye's direction.

## Key Quotes
> "雨露均沾" — Liang Na's reported advice that imperial favor should be shared among the palace women.

> "政事关键在于用对人" — the episode's frame for why Zuo Xiong's proposals focus on personnel quality.

> "不能用占卜的方法" — Hu Guang and Guo Qian's core objection to choosing the empress by lottery or divination.
