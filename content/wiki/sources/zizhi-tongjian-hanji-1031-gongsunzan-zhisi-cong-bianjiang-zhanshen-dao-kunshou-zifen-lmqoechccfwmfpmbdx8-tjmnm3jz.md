# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1031丨公孙瓒之死：从边疆战神到困兽自焚

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] endgame after [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1030-shouwo-hao-pai-gongsunzan-weihe-da-de-xilan-lpkntyov4viqyzgxgcujn36qhv8|Hanji 1030]]. In 199 CE, [[ZhangYanLateHan|张燕]] and [[GongsunXuLateHan|公孙旭]] move to relieve [[YijingFortress|易京]], but [[YuanShao|袁绍]] intercepts Gongsun Zan's fire-signal plan, lures him into a failed sortie, and then uses tunnel-and-fire siege work to break toward the central tower.
The episode's durable judgment is that Gongsun Zan's defeat is not only tactical. His earlier retreat into Yijing, fear of battle, harsh treatment of subordinates, and refusal to rescue endangered commanders leave him without the command trust needed to turn walls, grain, family concentration, or outside relief into survival.

## Key Claims
- The opening self-knowledge frame argues that people often swing between overestimating and underestimating themselves; terms such as [[DunningKrugerEffect|Dunning-Kruger effect]], Pygmalion effect, and "know thyself" are used as source-scoped commentary.
- In Jian'an 4, spring third month, [[ZhangYanLateHan|张燕]] and [[GongsunXuLateHan|公孙旭]] lead a nominal 100,000-person relief force toward Gongsun Zan.
- Gongsun Zan's secret letter tells Gongsun Xu to ambush with 5,000 cavalry in a northern lowland and wait for a fire signal while Gongsun Zan sorties from Yijing.
- [[YuanShao|袁绍]] intercepts the letter, lights the agreed signal himself, and defeats Gongsun Zan after luring him out.
- Yuan Shao's forces dig tunnels toward the tower line, prop the tunnel with timber, and burn the supports so the structure collapses, bringing the siege toward Gongsun Zan's central tower.
- Gongsun Zan kills his sisters, wife, and children before trying to burn himself; Yuan Shao's troops reach him and cut off his head.
- [[GuanJingLateHan|关静]] refuses to escape after Gongsun Zan's fall and dies charging Yuan Shao's army, while [[TianKaiLateHan|田楷]] and Gongsun Xu also disappear in the wider Yuan-side victory sequence.
- The episode sharpens [[RescueReciprocityCommandTrust|救援互惠式统帅信任]] and [[FortressSecurityIllusion|筑城避世安全幻觉]]: Yijing's defenses cannot repair a command network whose members no longer believe danger will be shared.
