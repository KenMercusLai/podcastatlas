# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》367-2｜教你一招，应对职场中的“不公”

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode begins with workplace grievances: credit appropriation, unrecognized help, rule-following that loses to hidden practices, exclusion, and apparently undeserved rewards. Its practical answer is [[OrganizationalFairnessExitJudgment|组织公平感与退出判断]]: understand how the organization defines useful contribution, but do not confuse that perspective-taking with a duty to remain in an unhealthy or development-poor environment; if exit is warranted, leave without needless relationship destruction.

The historical closing moves briefly through 108-107 BCE under [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]. It reports suppression of a revolt by the Wudu Di people, coerced relocation toward Jiuquan and other northwestern districts, and then an imperial tour, sacrifices, a limited amnesty, and summer drought. These annalistic notices are compressed and transcript-sensitive, so the durable synthesis remains the workplace judgment rather than a broad theory of Western Han frontier governance.

## Key Claims

- Employees may experience unfairness when supervisors take credit, assistance becomes expected, rule-following is punished, informal favoritism dominates, or low-effort colleagues receive recognition.
- The episode's “老板思维” asks workers to consider whether their conduct contributes to organizational development instead of treating personal feeling as the only evaluation frame.
- Organizational perspective-taking does not make a harmful environment acceptable or give an ordinary worker the power to repair systemic corruption.
- The proposed decision rule is conditional: remain when the role still offers development and the costs are tolerable; leave when development has disappeared and continued anger is harming the person.
- Exit should preserve dignity and future optionality rather than becoming an impulsive act of blame, provocation, or relationship destruction.
- The historical segment says Han forces suppressed a Wudu Di revolt and forcibly relocated groups toward Jiuquan and other northwestern areas.
- For 107 BCE, the episode records Han Wudi's travel and sacrifices, a limited pardon after the Houtu sacrifice, and a summer drought.

## Key Quotes

> “世界是否公平，不是站在个人角度评定。” - the episode's shift from felt grievance toward organizational perspective.

> “老板思维。” - the episode's shorthand for evaluating work through the organization's development logic.

> “分手见人品。” - the episode's argument for a controlled, relationship-preserving exit.

## Contradictions

- The “老板思维” frame can explain how organizations evaluate contribution, but it does not establish that organizational interest is fair, lawful, healthy, or morally superior to an employee's grievance. The wiki therefore treats it as one diagnostic lens rather than a verdict against the worker.
- The opening retelling of Song Ci comes from a television drama and functions as an analogy for principled isolation; it is not used here as evidence about the historical Song Ci.
- Transcript forms such as “五都敌人,” “九泉,” “福禄敌人,” “庸,” “武至,” “交官,” “明泽湖,” “戴帝,” and “何东军” are unstable. The source note normalizes only the contextually clear forms Wudu Di people (武都氐人), Jiuquan (酒泉), and Han Wudi (汉武帝), while leaving the exact itinerary and relocation subgroups source-scoped.
- No settled contradiction with existing wiki synthesis was found. The episode adds a compact workplace-exit framework and a sparse annalistic bridge after Hanji 367-1.
