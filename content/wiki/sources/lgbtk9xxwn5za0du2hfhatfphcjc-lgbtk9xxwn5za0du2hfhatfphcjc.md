# 【年终复盘②】RSI可能是NeoLab的机会、PA真的有用、创业要造灭火器还是砖头？｜对话谷雨

## Summary
This [[ShizilukouCrossing|十字路口Crossing]] interview asks [[GuYu|谷雨]] of [[NewYorkCognition|New York Cognition]] to revisit ten AI judgments that changed during 2026. Its strongest technical contribution is [[ApprenticeBench]] and [[SequentialWorkplaceLearningEvaluation]]: long-horizon intelligence is framed as learning across successive production tasks, feedback, rules, and organizational context rather than merely running one task for a long time. The episode also separates [[AgentOperationalCognition|business cognition]] from computer-use execution, argues that AI may give greenfield RSI startups more room to rebuild research infrastructure, and connects research taste to [[TechnicalCommercialClosure|commercial closure]] without treating founder forecasts or company claims as established fact.

## Key Claims
- [[GuYu|谷雨]] says [[NewYorkCognition|New York Cognition]] has 17 full-time staff, four interns, and about US$40 million in seed financing; these are guest-reported company facts rather than independently audited disclosures.
- The guest revises his prior skepticism about startup-led [[RecursiveSelfImprovement|RSI]] after seeing small teams build strong models quickly, arguing that AI-assisted research and the absence of legacy infrastructure can partly offset incumbents' scale while leaving compute, capital, and data constraints intact.
- [[AgentOperationalCognition]] separates the “hand” of reliable software and web operation from the “brain” of understanding company rules, workflows, exceptions, and task intent.
- [[ApprenticeBench]] evaluates [[SequentialWorkplaceLearningEvaluation|sequential workplace learning]] through 100 construction-accounting tasks whose later solutions can depend on feedback and knowledge from earlier tasks.
- [[ContinualLearning]] is presented as a frontier-model differentiator; memory-note quality and long-session interaction data may help, but the guest says the training recipe remains unsettled.
- The guest revises his view that high-level [[AIForMath|AI mathematics]] must depend on formal systems such as [[LeanTheoremProver|Lean]], citing reported progress in informal reasoning while preserving formal verification as a distinct reliability mechanism.
- Memory notes are presented as more scalable than he previously expected because stronger models can write and use them better; this does not establish that external notes replace parameter learning, provenance, or precise retrieval.
- The guest argues that AI conference reviewing and paper counts are losing signal under submission growth and low-quality generated text, motivating [[AIAcademicEvaluationSignalCollapse|evaluation based on demonstrated understanding, reasoning, judgment, and contribution]].
- [[TechnicalCommercialClosure]] requires a research company to map a technically coherent system onto a real user problem, product form, and business value, then release early enough to obtain market evidence.
- The discussion links AI video, personal agents, education, and the relative value of intelligence to [[HumanTasteCreativeBoundary|taste]], direction choice, emotional value, persistent interaction, and personal motivation; these remain broad judgments rather than measured forecasts.

## Key Quotes
> “手已经基本解决” — the guest's shorthand for computer-use execution becoming less limiting than task and organizational cognition.

> “从一个新人变成一个对这个公司非常熟悉的老员工” — the employee-development analogy behind Apprentice Bench's sequential-learning design.

> “灭火器与砖头” — the source's metaphor for a simple urgent solution beating a polished product when customer pain is acute.

## Contradictions
- No settled factual contradiction is adopted.
- The claim that computer-use “hands” are nearly solved is qualified by existing wiki evidence on reliability, permissions, latency, privacy, behavioral verification, and human-review boundaries.
- Reported model capabilities, company headcount and financing, benchmark design and scores, conference-system decline, startup valuations, video consistency, provider rankings, and next-year RSI forecasts remain source-scoped because the supplied file is a structured summary rather than a transcript, benchmark release, audited filing, systematic study, or independent evaluation.
- The source's optimism about memory notes does not erase the existing distinction between compressed behavioral learning and exact, inspectable, source-grounded recall.
