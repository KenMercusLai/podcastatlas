# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》391-1｜为了立太子，汉武帝为何先杀宠妃钩弋夫人

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate prelude to [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-391-2-jiemi-hanwudi-siqian-renming-de-tuogu-dachen-fnit0yqfd43fqfhyyzuwm_b932dm|Hanji 391-2]] by explaining why [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] killed [[LadyGouyiWesternHan|钩弋夫人]] before making her son [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|刘弗陵]] heir. It frames the decision as an inversion of the usual "母以子贵" and "子以母贵" logic: Liu Fuling's rise does not protect his mother, because the host says Han Wudi treats Lady Gouyi as both a hidden participant in the [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]] against [[LiuJuCrownPrinceWesternHan|卫太子刘据]] and a future harem/outer-relative risk if a child ruler succeeds.

The durable synthesis is source-scoped and two-layered. Publicly, Han Wudi justifies the killing through the "young son, strong mother" danger and the precedent of [[LuZhi|吕雉]]'s regency power. Privately, the episode claims he has discovered Lady Gouyi's role behind [[JiangChongWesternHan|江充]] and [[SuWenWesternHan|苏文]] in the destruction of Liu Ju, but suppresses that charge to protect Liu Fuling's succession legitimacy.

## Key Claims

- The episode introduces the usual palace logic that a mother's status can raise a son and a son's heir status can raise his mother.
- Han Wudi's killing of Lady Gouyi is presented as the reverse pattern: Liu Fuling's heir value makes her death more likely rather than safer.
- The host claims, source-scopedly, that Lady Gouyi helped Jiang Chong frame Crown Prince Liu Ju during the Wugu affair and was therefore not merely an innocent harem casualty.
- The episode says Han Wudi avoids publicizing that alleged crime because Liu Fuling is already the intended successor and his mother's disgrace would damage his legitimacy.
- Han Wudi's stated public rationale is that child rulers with young, strong mothers can produce harem and outer-relative domination.
- [[LuZhi|吕后]] is used as the warning precedent for a powerful mother of a weak or young ruler.
- The Zhou Gong image sent to [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]] links this source directly to the next episode's entrusted-minister arrangement.

## Key Quotes

> "子弱母壮，必乱天下" - the episode's reported public rationale for killing Lady Gouyi.

> "子贵母死" - the host's compact formulation of Han Wudi's inversion of ordinary maternal-status logic.
