# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》542丨职场心理学：升职后为何更容易造排挤？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the earlier relationship rupture among [[ZhaiFangjinWesternHan|翟方进]], [[ChenXianWesternHan|陈贤]], and [[FengXinWesternHan|冯信]] around the Yushi Dafu competition and the rushed-funeral investigation under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]. It reads Chen Xian and Feng Xin's pressure on Zhai during the inquiry, and Zhai's later counterattack through the [[ChenTangWesternHan|陈汤]] case, as a sequence where old friendship is repriced after promotion, rivalry, shame, self-protection, and revenge enter the relationship.

The durable synthesis is that promotion can break peer equilibrium before anyone openly declares hostility. When one member of a close group rises, the others may experience the change not as distant success but as comparative loss; if that feeling later meets a real procedural opening, the relationship can slide from resentment into mutual preemptive attack.

## Key Claims

- Chen Xian, Feng Xin, and Zhai Fangjin are presented as once-close officials whose friendship is destabilized when Zhai wins the Yushi Dafu post despite Chen and Feng's seniority.
- Chen Xian's harsh posture in the later funeral inquiry is interpreted as both an office-seeking move and an emotional release of earlier frustration.
- Zhai Fangjin misreads the old friendship as still reliable after his promotion and expects help from Chen Xian and Feng Xin during danger.
- Once Chen Xian and Feng Xin fail to help, Zhai shifts from disappointment to enmity and looks for a usable counterattack.
- Zhai's later memorial uses Chen Xian and Feng Xin's association with Chen Tang to recast their earlier advancement as improperly obtained through recommendation.
- The episode generalizes the case into [[PromotionStatusRelationshipRupture|promotion-status relationship rupture]]: status, wealth, or authority changes can reorder close relationships because familiar peers are the most painful comparison group.
- The workplace lesson is not only to distrust colleagues; it is to notice how jealousy, wounded entitlement, and self-protection can make both sides explain their own aggression as necessary.

## Key Quotes

> "只要有差异，就一定会有矛盾" - the episode's compact formula for relationship instability after status divergence.

> "把自己放进去" - the closing method for reading history as self-examination rather than only judging others.
