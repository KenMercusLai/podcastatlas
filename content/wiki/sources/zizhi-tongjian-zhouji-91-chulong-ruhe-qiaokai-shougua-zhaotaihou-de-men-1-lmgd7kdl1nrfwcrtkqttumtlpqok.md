# 《资治通鉴·周纪》91丨触龙如何敲开守寡赵太后的门（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows directly from [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-90-jiemi-xianqin-zui-wanmei-de-fuchou-liu7gbl-bffknkw6dx5udqcq4tcm|Zhouji 90]] by moving [[FanJu|范雎]]'s revenge pressure into the [[ZhaoState|赵国]] crisis created by [[WeiQiWarringStates|魏齐]]'s refuge with [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]]. In 265 BCE, [[QinState|秦国]] attacks Zhao while [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] is newly enthroned and [[ZhaoWeihou|赵威后]] is acting through a queen-mother regency.

The central story is the first half of [[ChuLong|触龙]] persuading Zhao Weihou to send [[ChangAnJunZhao|长安君]] to [[QiState|齐国]] as a hostage in exchange for aid. Rather than challenging her anger directly, Chu Long lowers the emotional temperature with slow entry, bodily weakness, health questions, and a request about his own youngest son, then uses Zhao Weihou's daughter Yan Hou to distinguish indulgent closeness from long-range planning for a child.

## Key Claims
- Qin's attack on Zhao is not necessarily a contradiction of [[YuanjiaoJingongStrategy|远交近攻]]: Qin is also attacking nearby Han, and Zhao is vulnerable because of a young new ruler, queen-mother regency, and the Wei Qi asylum problem.
- [[ZhaoWeihou|赵威后]]'s refusal to send [[ChangAnJunZhao|长安君]] as hostage shows how maternal attachment can collide with state emergency and alliance requirements.
- [[QiState|齐国]]'s demand for a Zhao hostage makes rescue conditional on credible leverage, extending the wiki's [[HostageDiplomacyRupture|质子外交]] branch from alliance repair into wartime aid.
- [[ChuLong|触龙]] succeeds first by avoiding direct confrontation. The episode treats "请您冷静" style advice as counterproductive when the target is angry and already defensive.
- Chu Long's method is a source case for [[EmotionalDeescalationPersuasion|情绪降温式说服]]: he creates a non-threatening conversation before introducing the real policy issue.
- The daughter-marriage example reframes parental love as "thinking long-term" rather than keeping a child physically near the parent.
- The episode stops mid-argument, before the final resolution of whether Chang'an Jun becomes hostage.

## Key Quotes
> "请您冷静" - modern counterexample used to explain what Chu Long avoids.

> "必勿使反" - Zhao Weihou's prayer that her married daughter not be sent back from Yan.

> "父母爱孩子，就会替孩子做长远打算" - host's summary of Chu Long's argumentative turn.
