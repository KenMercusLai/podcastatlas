# 《资治通鉴·周纪》96丨毛遂自荐（2）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the [[MaoSui|毛遂]] branch opened in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-1-lvqbmaabsvbdz2zibgmseidx5phe|Zhouji 96 part 1]]: after [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] and [[MiWan|楚考烈王]] talk from sunrise to sunset without result, Mao Sui enters the negotiation by pressing his sword, closing the physical distance, and making Chu's humiliation by [[QinState|秦国]] the reason to commit to [[HezongAlliance|合纵]]. The second half turns the successful Chu agreement back into military reality: [[ChuState|楚国]] still needs transit through [[WeiState|魏国]], [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]] stops [[JinBi|晋鄙]] at [[YeWarringStates|邺城]] after Qin's threat, and [[XinyuanYan|新垣衍]] brings a surrender-like [[SubmissionThroughStatusElevation|尊秦为西帝]] proposal into [[Handan|邯郸]].

## Key Claims
- Mao Sui succeeds because he does not merely explain the abstract advantage of alliance; he converts Chu's past humiliation by [[BaiQi|白起]] into a face and survival problem for [[MiWan|楚考烈王]].
- His sword-at-hand posture first breaks the hierarchy of the audience, then lets him reframe strength: Chu's land and troops should make submission to Qin feel shameful, not prudent.
- The source treats the court blood oath as commitment locking: Mao Sui presses the king from verbal willingness into immediate [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] ritual.
- Mao Sui's rise validates the self-recommendation branch from part 1, but also shows that hidden retainer talent has to perform under extreme pressure once admitted to the mission.
- [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]] is notably absent from the direct negotiation, even though he had both a prior relationship with Zhao's coalition overtures and an anti-Qin inclination.
- Chu's agreement does not solve Zhao's operational crisis because Chu and Zhao do not border each other; rescue depends on Wei transit or on a much harder attack toward Qin.
- Wei's behavior is split: it sends Jin Bi with a large rescue army, but Qin's warning makes Wei Anxi Wang freeze that army at Ye.
- Xin Yuan Yan's "honor Qin as western emperor" proposal is presented as more than a title concession; the episode reads it as close to submission because it would recognize Qin's superior order to buy withdrawal.
- The episode ends with [[ZhaoState|赵国]] still forced toward self-rescue: if military victory is unavailable, it must choose between costly resistance and a politically dangerous appeasement attempt.

## Key Quotes
> "十步之内楚王性命在他手里" - Mao Sui's near-distance threat as summarized by the source.

> "合纵是为了楚国好，不只是为了赵国" - the episode's framing of Mao Sui's persuasion.

> "尊秦昭襄王为西帝" - Xin Yuan Yan's proposed settlement route.
