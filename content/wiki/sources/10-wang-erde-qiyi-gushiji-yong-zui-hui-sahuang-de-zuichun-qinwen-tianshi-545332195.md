# 10.王尔德奇异故事集：用最会撒谎的嘴唇亲吻天使

## Summary
This [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] episode brings [[QinZong|秦总]], [[Beimin|北明]], and translator [[FeiBi|菲比]] together around [[OscarWilde|Oscar Wilde / 王尔德]] and [[WildeStrangeStories|《王尔德奇异故事集》]]. It moves from Wilde's Irish background, social celebrity, aphoristic language, and relationship with [[AlfredDouglas|Lord Alfred Douglas / 波西]] into the stories' craft: [[LordArthurSavilesCrime|《阿瑟·萨维尔勋爵的罪行》]], [[ThePortraitOfMrWH|《W.H.先生的画像》]], and [[TheCantervilleGhost|《坎特维尔的幽灵》]] show black comedy, self-seducing interpretation, ghost-story tenderness, and Wilde's insistence that fiction can be beautiful lying rather than moral instruction. The source extends the wiki's Wilde branch from literary gossip into close reading, translation, and aesthetic theory.

## Key Claims
- [[OscarWilde|Wilde]] should not be reduced to either a tragic queer martyr or a decorative aphorism machine; the episode treats his life, social performance, sexuality, courtroom disaster, prison writing, and story craft as mutually entangled.
- [[WildeStrangeStories|《王尔德奇异故事集》]] corrects the narrow memory of Wilde as only the author of sad fairy tales like [[TheHappyPrince|《快乐王子》]] and [[TheNightingaleAndTheRose|《夜莺与玫瑰》]].
- [[LordArthurSavilesCrime|《阿瑟·萨维尔勋爵的罪行》]] turns moral logic upside down: a respectable young aristocrat tries to commit murder as if it were a responsible premarital duty.
- [[ThePortraitOfMrWH|《W.H.先生的画像》]] makes forged evidence and readerly obsession contagious; the episode uses it as a case where aesthetic truth, documentary proof, and self-persuasion become hard to separate.
- [[TheCantervilleGhost|《坎特维尔的幽灵》]] uses an English ghost and a practical American family to turn supernatural terror into comic cultural conflict and eventual tenderness.
- Wilde's [[LifeImitatesArt|life-imitates-art]] stance makes lying, posing, storytelling, and aesthetic self-fashioning part of art rather than merely personal vice.
- The source's treatment of [[ArtAgainstMoralInstruction]] connects Wilde's aestheticism to resistance against direct moral preaching, while still recognizing Christian imagery, sacrifice, redemption, and tragic beauty in his work.
- The late discussion of science fiction and serious literature extends [[GenreLiteraryLegitimacy]]: the issue is not whether a story has prestige labels, but whether its speculative or genre tools make something newly feelable.

## Key Quotes
> "用最会撒谎的嘴唇亲吻天使" - episode-title formulation for Wilde's fiction-making and charm.

> "生活模仿艺术" - the source's shorthand for Wilde's reversal of realist common sense.

> "不是艺术模仿生活，而是生活模仿艺术" - the episode's explanation of Wilde's story about invention preceding perception.
