# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》132｜5万完爆40万，项羽此战封神

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode returns from [[LiSi|李斯]]'s court-death branch to the anti-[[QinState|秦国]] battlefield after [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-131-1-qinxiang-lisi-yisheng-jingming-que-zao-yaozhan-liupvalcghtas2ibfyjrtdz6ctg7|Qinji 131-1]]. It follows [[SongYi|宋义]]'s warning that [[XiangLiang|项梁]]'s victories have produced dangerous arrogance, [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]]'s night attack at [[Dingtao|定陶]] that kills Xiang Liang, and the resulting move of [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]] to [[Xuzhou|彭城]]. The core turn is [[HuaiwangAgreement|怀王之约]]: Mi Xin reorganizes the Chu armies, promises kingship to whoever first enters [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]], and assigns Song Yi, [[XiangYu|项羽]], and [[FanZeng|范增]] to rescue [[Julu|巨鹿]] while [[LiuBang|刘邦]] is sent west.

## Key Claims
- The episode opens with host context about difficult contemporary conditions, then uses that tone to frame the historical section as a moment of order-seeking in chaos.
- [[HuaiwangAgreement|怀王之约]] is presented as a decisive political device, not just a slogan: whoever first enters [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] and destroys Qin will be made king there.
- After repeated wins at Dong'e and Dingtao, [[XiangLiang|项梁]] becomes overconfident; [[SongYi|宋义]] warns that a victorious army can fail when its commander grows arrogant and soldiers grow slack.
- Xiang Liang ignores Song Yi and sends him to [[QiState|齐国]], where Song Yi tells a Qi envoy not to hurry toward Xiang Liang because Xiang Liang will soon be defeated.
- [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] reinforces [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]], who night-attacks Xiang Liang at [[Dingtao|定陶]] and kills him.
- [[XiangYu|项羽]], [[LiuBang|刘邦]], and [[LuChenQinRebel|吕臣]] are unable to take Waihuang, turn toward Chenliu, then retreat after hearing of Xiang Liang's death.
- Xiang Yu, Liu Bang, and Lu Chen move [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]] from Xuyi to [[Xuzhou|彭城]], where the Chu center becomes more directly royal rather than Xiang Liang-dominated.
- Mi Xin merges Lu Chen's and Xiang Yu's armies under his own command, appoints Liu Bang as Dang commandery governor and Wu'an Hou, makes Xiang Yu Chang'an Hou and Lu Gong, appoints Lu Chen as situ, and makes Lu Chen's father Lu Qing lingyin.
- [[WeiBao|魏豹]] recovers more than twenty old Wei cities and is recognized by Mi Xin as Wei king.
- Zhang Han reads Chu as broken after Xiang Liang's death, crosses the Yellow River north, attacks [[ZhaoState|赵国]], breaks [[Handan|邯郸]], and helps create the crisis around [[Julu|巨鹿]].
- [[ZhaoXie|赵歇]] and [[ZhangEr|张耳]] flee to Julu; [[WangLiQin|王离]] surrounds the city, [[ChenYu|陈馀]] camps north with several tens of thousands, and Zhang Han camps to the south at Jiyuan.
- Chu decides to rescue Zhao because past Warring States failures showed that isolated anti-Qin states can be defeated one by one.
- A Qi envoy identified in the source as Gaoling Jun recommends Song Yi to Mi Xin because Song Yi had accurately predicted Xiang Liang's defeat.
- Mi Xin appoints Song Yi as chief general, Xiang Yu as second general, and Fan Zeng as last general for the Zhao relief army.
- Xiang Yu asks to accompany Liu Bang west because he wants revenge for Xiang Liang's death, but older Chu generals argue that Xiang Yu is too brutal, citing the [[XiangchengQin|襄城]] massacre.
- Mi Xin therefore sends Liu Bang west with a smaller force, because Liu Bang is framed as more generous and better suited to pacifying Qin's exhausted population.
- Liu Bang begins taking territory westward and defeats two Qin forces around Chengyang and Gangli.
- The episode title signals Xiang Yu's coming Julu fame, but this transcript mostly covers the political and command setup before the main battle.

## Key Quotes
> "先入关中者王" - the compact rule behind the Huaiwang agreement.

> "骄兵必败" - the host's summary of Song Yi's warning about Xiang Liang's victorious army.

> "狡诈残暴" - the older generals' judgment on why Xiang Yu should not lead the western pacification route.
