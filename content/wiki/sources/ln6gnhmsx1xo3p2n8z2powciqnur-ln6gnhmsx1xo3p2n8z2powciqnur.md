## Summary
This [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] and [[SixiaoComedy|四孝喜剧]] live panel has [[LuoYonghao|罗永浩]], [[LiuHongweiStandup|刘宏伟]], [[SunShuhengStandup|孙舒恒]], [[WangJiye|王继业 / 王季叶]], and [[XuanZongStandup|选总]] answer relationship thought experiments generated by AI. The episode tests whether technology should guarantee fidelity, replace partners with perfect AI companions, split emotional pain, delete memories of infidelity, or reveal a 100% romantic match. Its main synthesis is [[RelationshipTechnologyEthics]]: better experience is tempting, but the panel keeps returning to agency, responsibility, common history, species authenticity, privacy, and whether pain or imperfection is part of what makes intimacy human.

## Key Claims
- The "loyalty drug" thought experiment turns fidelity into [[RelationshipTechnologyEthics|relationship-technology ethics]]: several speakers resist a chemically guaranteed bond because it may remove choice, challenge, responsibility, or social diversity from love.
- [[LuoYonghao|罗永浩]] treats the loyalty-drug decision as life-stage dependent, arguing that older people may rationally value stability over preserving future romantic possibility.
- [[SunShuhengStandup|孙舒恒]] consistently rejects technologies that quantify, erase, or overdetermine intimacy because he wants ambiguity, possibility, and unprogrammed life narrative to remain.
- The perfect AI partner debate extends [[AICompanionAuthenticityBoundary]] into romance: some panelists would choose perfect subjective experience, while others cannot accept a partner once they know it is not a human of the same species.
- The pain-sharing device becomes [[SharedPainTechnologyBoundary]] because a mechanism that helps one partner share suffering also forces the other partner to receive pain, exposes inner states, and can be abused through asymmetrical relationships.
- The infidelity-memory question extends [[MemoryDeletionEthics]]: deleting pain can make continued cohabitation easier, but keeping memory can preserve responsibility, warning, and accountability after betrayal.
- The 70% versus 100% match question becomes [[AlgorithmicRomanticCompatibility]]: measured compatibility may create opportunity-cost pressure, but marriage, shared history, "义气," remaining lifespan, and uncertainty about perfection all affect the decision.
- Across the episode, [[LuoYonghao|罗永浩]] uses repeated clarification and counterfactual pressure to convert comic answers into explicit value rankings around experience, obligation, authenticity, pain, and common past.
