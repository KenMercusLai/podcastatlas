# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》305-2｜衡山王刘赐竟因儿子勾引小妈误国

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the royal-house crisis opened in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-305-1-yin-chugui-lihun-de-ta-weihe-yao-mouhai-qinge-ljbwegjf-hve9we3gq0mrbipjvnp|Hanji 305-1]]. [[XuLaiHengshanQueen|徐来]] first uses a maid to compromise [[LiuXiaoHengshanPrince|刘孝]], while crown prince [[LiuShuangHengshanCrownPrince|刘爽]] follows [[BaiYingHengshan|白盈]]'s advice and unsuccessfully tries to seduce his stepmother. The failed schemes turn succession rivalry into detention, attempted heir replacement, and reciprocal denunciation among [[LiuCiHengshanKingWesternHan|刘赐]] and his sons.

The denunciations expose Liu Ci's alleged military preparations to the central court. The episode says an imperial search finds a participant in Liu Xiao's residence, Liu Xiao confesses his father's conspiracy, Liu Ci, Liu Shuang, Liu Xiao, and Xu Lai are ordered to die, and the [[HengshanKingdomWesternHan|Western Han 衡山国]] is abolished and converted into directly administered territory.

## Key Claims

- In 121 BCE, an attack on Xu Lai's nurse and Liu Shuang's failure to attend his sick father deepen Liu Ci's determination to replace the crown prince.
- Xu Lai wants her own son Liu Guang advanced rather than Liu Xiao, so she arranges for a maid to compromise Liu Xiao and uses the encounter as leverage while temporarily supporting him against Liu Shuang.
- Bai Ying advises Liu Shuang to secure his position by seducing Xu Lai; Liu Shuang attempts the plan at her birthday gathering, but she refuses him and reports him to Liu Ci.
- After Liu Ci beats him, Liu Shuang accuses Liu Xiao of sleeping with the king's favored maid, repeats allegations about Liu Wucai's sexual conduct, and threatens to report Liu Ci's recruitment and weapons program to the emperor.
- Liu Ci confines Liu Shuang, entrusts Liu Xiao with bows, arrows, and chariots, and in 123 BCE petitions the court to replace Liu Shuang with Liu Xiao.
- The proposed replacement produces three competing reports: Bai Ying carries Liu Shuang's accusation against Liu Xiao, Liu Ci accuses Liu Shuang of unfilial conduct, and Liu Xiao accuses his father of rebellion.
- The source says the imperial investigation ends with the deaths of Liu Ci, Liu Shuang, Liu Xiao, and Xu Lai and the abolition of the Hengshan kingdom.
