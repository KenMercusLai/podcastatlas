# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1075丨揭秘被《三国志》掩盖的亲密关系谋杀案

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies a highly dramatized prehistory for the killing of [[HandanShangLateHan|邯郸商]] by [[ZhangMengLateHan|张猛]], the event whose mourning aftermath opens [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1076-ta-fanpan-caocao-sanci-zuizhong-jing-si-yu-jiuyou-shouzhong-lrulyifphhp2vao79ac5hky7srzv|Hanji 1076]]. It portrays the two men as Taixue friends whose relationship collapses after Handan Shang recommends Zhang Meng for Wuwei, Zhang Meng builds an autonomous frontier force, and Handan Shang responds through grain and information controls.

The durable interpretive contribution is narrower than the narrative detail. The host uses the alleged friendship-to-murder arc to extend [[PartnershipBoundaryRisk|合伙关系边界风险]]: personal trust does not remove the need to define authority, information, resources, and critical-command boundaries. The episode's dates, names, quotations, private motives, and cinematic murder details conflict internally or lack traceable citations, so they are retained as source-scoped storytelling rather than settled late-Han biography.

## Key Claims

- The transcript calls Handan Shang “韩南商” and Zhang Meng “张萌”; the wiki normalizes them to [[HandanShangLateHan|邯郸商]] and [[ZhangMengLateHan|张猛]] because the Wuwei killing and Hanji 1076 continuation identify the intended late-Han figures.
- The episode portrays the two as close Taixue contemporaries and says Handan Shang later recommends Zhang Meng as Wuwei administrator, but it provides no checkable primary-text citation for this relationship.
- In the episode's account, Zhang Meng expands his force to thirty thousand Qiang and Hu soldiers who obey his tally rather than the inspector, making Handan Shang formally responsible but operationally weak.
- Handan Shang allegedly retaliates by centralizing grain distribution, under-supplying Zhang Meng's force, and intercepting messengers to Xuchang; the source reads these as bureaucratic coercion after informal trust has failed.
- The relationship becomes openly hostile when Handan Shang allegedly breaks Zhang Meng's writing brush and requires his memorials to pass through the inspector's office.
- The episode stages the killing at an iced “Rende Bridge,” where false rebellion news draws away guards and Zhang Meng shoots Handan Shang with three crossbow bolts; these cinematic specifics are unsupported within the supplied transcript.
- Zhang Meng allegedly sends Handan Shang's head and financial records to [[CaoCao|曹操]], whose silence is interpreted by the host as strategic tolerance of an unavoidable violation.
- The host generalizes that close allies should not control an actor's critical command line and that changed rank or leverage requires relationships and responsibilities to be redefined.
- The episode says Zhang Meng dies the following year while suppressing a rebellion, looking at a jade pendant from Handan Shang; this ending is part of the same literary friendship frame and is not independently established here.

## Key Quotes

> "能杀死你的，从来都是最了解你的人。" - the host's concluding warning about intimacy and concentrated leverage.

> "权力场上的友谊本质上是一颗定时炸弹。" - the episode's strongest, deliberately universalized formulation of the case.

## Contradictions

- Chronology conflict: this episode dates the killing to Jian'an 9 / 204 CE and says Zhang Meng dies the next year, while Hanji 1076 places its Handan Shang aftermath in Jian'an 11 / 206 CE. The wiki does not reconcile the two without a formal textual source.
- Internal chronology conflict: the episode places the men's Taixue friendship in 190 CE but also says the friendship ends twenty years later, while its own murder date is 204 CE, only fourteen years later.
- Source-scope caution: the Taixue beating, Handan Shang's wife sewing a robe, Cao Cao's praise, “同寝如影随形,” thirty-thousand-man force, seven messengers, brush-breaking, Rende Bridge trap, head-and-ledger delivery, Cao Cao's private reaction, burial ruler, and jade-pendant death scene are presented without verifiable source text and should be treated as literary construction.
- Citation caution: references to 《魏略》, 《曹操传》, and alleged old sayings are not accompanied by quotations that can be checked, and the transcript itself omits the sentence supposedly spoken by Cao Cao.
- Interpretive caution: claims that friendship is inherently a time bomb, that a leader should maintain strategic silence toward violations, or that confidants should never hold critical responsibility are the host's management generalizations, not established historical findings.
