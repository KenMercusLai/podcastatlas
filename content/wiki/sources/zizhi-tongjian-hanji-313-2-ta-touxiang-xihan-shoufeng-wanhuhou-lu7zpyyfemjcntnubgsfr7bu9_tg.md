# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》313-2｜他投降西汉，受封万户侯

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-313-1-duchuang-huxue-huoqubing-shouxiang-hunyewang-lij9eecrq-igxpvftkey5shu8395|Hanji 313-1]] from the armed surrender crisis into [[HunyeKingWesternHan|浑邪王]]'s reception at Chang'an. It uses the Northern Army formation, the scale of [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] and [[WeiyangPalace|未央宫]], and an imagined prostration before [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] to present ceremonial space and military display as instruments for making imperial hierarchy visible.

The court then converts surrender into differentiated status: the episode says Hunye King receives a ten-thousand-household marquisate, several associated leaders receive smaller fiefs, and the Xiutu King's two sons become palace horse keepers under the shaofu rather than marquises. This supplies the public benefit side of [[SurrenderReceptionDomesticBurden|归降接纳的内外成本冲突]], while the teaser about later court disputes leaves the consequences incomplete.

## Key Claims

- Han forces and the Northern Army receive the surrendered group outside Chang'an in a display the episode interprets as both welcome and deterrent.
- The episode uses Chang'an and Weiyang Palace's monumental scale to dramatize the submitted group's encounter with Han imperial order.
- Hunye King's prostration is explicitly presented through an imagined scene rather than a securely documented eyewitness description.
- The episode says Hunye King was enfeoffed as a marquis with a ten-thousand-household fief.
- Several associated Xiongnu leaders are said to have received smaller marquisates, making reward rank-sensitive rather than uniform.
- The Xiutu King's two sons are said to have been assigned as official slaves and palace horse keepers under the shaofu, showing that incorporation could also preserve sharp status inequality.
- The closing teaser says the apparently sincere surrender still provoked court disputes, but postpones their cause and substance.

## Key Quotes

> “福祸相匿” - the host's closing formula for the surrender's unresolved benefits and costs.

> “啥事都有两面，甚至好几面” - the episode's warning against treating submission as an unqualified success.
