# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1067丨三国最霸气的女人：为报夫仇，拿自己当诱饵！

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows the Jian'an 9 [[DanyangCoupLateHan|丹阳郡变]] in which [[SunYiLateHan|孙翊]] is assassinated by [[BianHongLateHan|边鸿]] after [[GuiLanLateHan|妫览]] and [[DaiYuanLateHan|戴员]] plot against him. It then centers [[LadyXuSunYi|徐夫人]] as the actor who reads the killers' weakness, pretends to accept Gui Lan's forced marriage demand, secretly mobilizes [[SunGaoLateHan|孙高]], [[FuYingLateHan|傅婴]], and dead-set retainers, and kills the conspirators under the cover of mourning and remarriage ritual.

The core contribution is a late-Han case connecting [[FemaleRevengeAndPoliticalOrder|female revenge and political order]], [[DesireDrivenCommandExposure|desire-driven command exposure]], and [[RetainerRevengeMobilization|retainer revenge mobilization]]. The source frames Lady Xu's revenge as neither brute force nor passive chastity: her victory depends on psychological reading, delayed timing, reputation sacrifice, hidden coordination, and an enemy whose desire makes him underestimate danger.

## Key Claims
- The episode opens with a general claim that desire can overpower judgment, using Dong Zhuo/Diaochan and *Jin Ping Mei* examples to set up Gui Lan's later vulnerability.
- In Jian'an 9, Gui Lan and Dai Yuan buy off Sun Yi's intimate Bian Hong, who kills Sun Yi during a county-official banquet and lets the conspirators blame the murder on Bian Hong's supposed madness.
- Lady Xu identifies the conspiracy rather than accepting the cover story, but she cannot defeat Gui Lan and Dai Yuan by direct force.
- [[SunHeLateHan|孙河]] comes to investigate after Sun Yi's death and is also killed by Gui Lan and Dai Yuan, making the conspiracy a wider threat to Sun-family control in Jiangdong.
- Gui Lan moves into Sun Yi's residence and demands Lady Xu as wife; Lady Xu asks to wait until the end-of-month mourning rite, using ritual propriety to buy time.
- Lady Xu secretly contacts Sun Gao and Fu Ying, who gather more than twenty dead-set followers and prepare the assassination.
- Lady Xu first performs mourning, then changes clothes, bathes, perfumes herself, and appears ready for marriage so Gui Lan's observers will report that she is compliant.
- When Gui Lan enters her room, Lady Xu gives the signal; Sun Gao and Fu Ying kill Gui Lan, while the retainers kill Dai Yuan and other conspirators.
- Lady Xu then returns to mourning dress and uses the enemies' heads to sacrifice to Sun Yi, turning the staged remarriage back into a revenge ritual.
- Sun Quan arrives from Jiaoqiu, destroys the remaining conspirator faction and households, and rewards Sun Gao, Fu Ying, and other contributors.
