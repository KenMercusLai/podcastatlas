# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》518丨揭秘西汉最后的诤臣：王章

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens the Yangshuo 1 / 24 BCE [[WangZhangWesternHan|王章]] sequence completed by [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-519-aiguozhe-wangzhang-de-si-yuanyugen-cuo-le-zhuzi-lt5hzlh9t-gryds1lpn0ieqw3cvn|Hanji 519]]. It places his planned sealed memorial against [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]] inside a court where [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] cannot appoint a close attendant without Wang Feng's approval and where an eclipse has already been used to send [[LiuKangDingtaoKingWesternHan|定陶王刘康]] away from the capital.

The durable synthesis is that moral courage and political effectiveness are not identical. Wang Zhang's early poverty, repeated willingness to confront powerful insiders, and refusal to become his patron's dependent establish his integrity; his wife's two interventions expose the other side of the judgment, because a correct cause pursued without a protected channel, adequate leverage, or a durable principal can turn principled action into household catastrophe.

## Key Claims

- Wang Feng's control is illustrated by Chengdi's inability to appoint Liu Xin as Zhongchangshi before obtaining the general's approval.
- [[DuQinWesternHan|杜钦]] advises Wang Feng to imitate the Duke of Zhou's humility, reduce display and desire, and deny small men opportunities to create conflict.
- Wang Feng interprets the eclipse as a warning about Liu Kang's prolonged presence at court and secures the Dingtao king's departure despite Chengdi's affection for him.
- Wang Zhang had entered office through Wang Feng's recommendation but did not accept political dependence in return.
- As a poor student in Chang'an, Wang Zhang became gravely ill under a rain-covering ox blanket; [[WangZhangWifeWesternHan|his wife]] recalled him to ambition rather than letting him surrender.
- Wang Zhang later lost office after direct conflict with [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]], returned under Chengdi, became Sili Xiaowei, and then Jingzhao Yin through Wang Feng's recommendation.
- Because Wang Feng controlled Shangshu affairs and the ordinary memorial route, Wang Zhang planned a sealed memorial that could reach Chengdi directly.
- His wife then urged restraint by invoking the same poverty they had escaped, but Wang Zhang treated the confrontation as a matter of justice worth mortal risk.

## Key Quotes

> "太刚则折，太柔则废" - the episode's framing warning about excess rigidity and excess compliance.

> "以卵击石，自不量力" - the host's judgment on confronting entrenched power without sufficient leverage.
