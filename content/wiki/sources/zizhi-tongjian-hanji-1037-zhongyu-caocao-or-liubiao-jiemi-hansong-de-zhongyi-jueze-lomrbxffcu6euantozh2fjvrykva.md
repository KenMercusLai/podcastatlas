# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1037丨忠于曹操or刘表？揭秘韩嵩的忠义抉择

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the Jingzhou-Cao court branch immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1038-sanshiliu-ji-zhi-diaohu-lishan-sunce-zhiqu-wancheng-lhomk8hfdidl61degwbqtj2xkj9z|Hanji 1038]]. [[HanSongLateHan|韩嵩]] returns from [[Xudu|许都]] with court appointments, praises the Han court and [[CaoCao|曹操]], and urges [[LiuBiao|刘表]] to send a son to court; Liu Biao reads the advice as betrayal and imprisons Han Song even after torture of the mission staff fails to prove treason.

The episode's second thread follows [[LiuYeLateHan|刘晔]] before his Hanji 1038 warning role. Liu Ye kills [[ZhengBaoLateHan|郑宝]] at a banquet, uses Cao Cao's envoy as an authority signal, refuses to absorb Zheng Bao's undisciplined followers, and shifts those thousands to [[LiuXunLateHan|刘勋]], whose later food pressure is worsened when [[YuanYinLateHan|袁胤]] arrives with Yuan Shu remnants and households.

## Key Claims
- [[HanSongLateHan|韩嵩]] receives the posts of attendant-in-ordinary and [[LinglingCommanderyLateHan|Lingling]] administrator after reaching Xudu, indicating that the court-Cao side treats him as politically useful.
- Han Song's advice to send Liu Biao's son to the court functions like a loyalty and hostage signal, making court allegiance visible while also reducing Liu Biao's autonomy.
- The episode uses "圣达节，次守节" to frame Han Song as choosing fixed role-duty rather than opportunistic flexibility: he cannot act like a sage who transcends norms, so he preserves the duty he understands.
- Liu Biao's rage exposes the limit of his [[DefensiveLocalGovernance|defensive local governance]]: court-facing loyalty, elite opinion, and personal authority become inseparable once Han Song praises Cao Cao in Jingzhou.
- The host reads Liu Biao's cruelty toward Han Song and the mission officials as a sign of [[SuspicionDrivenEliteAlienation|suspicion-driven elite alienation]], foreshadowing later Jingzhou elites turning toward Cao Cao.
- In the Yangzhou-Lujiang branch, Liu Ye kills Zheng Bao decisively but refuses the resulting troops because armed followers without discipline, food, and money can become a burden rather than a free asset.
- Liu Xun's acceptance of Zheng Bao's followers and Yuan Yin's refugee/remnant group creates a grain burden that helps explain the later Shangliao grain expedition and the vulnerability exploited in Hanji 1038.

## Key Quotes
