# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》427｜一人征服莎车国，西汉冯奉世怎么做到的？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills a Yuan康 2 / 64 BCE layer under [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]], before the existing Hanji 428 source. It follows [[ShacheKingdomWesternHan|莎车国]] after its king dies without an heir, the installation and killing of Wusun-linked Wannian, and [[FengFengshiWesternHan|冯奉世]]'s unauthorized mobilization of Western Regions cavalry to force regime change.

The durable synthesis is that "one person conquering Shache" means field judgment plus borrowed imperial credibility, not lone heroics. Feng acts before Chang'an can respond, gathers allied cavalry under a false imperial command, and wins when overwhelming pressure causes Shache nobles to kill Hutuzheng and surrender; Xuan rewards the result but refuses marquisate-level endorsement because the precedent of envoy-led unauthorized war is dangerous.

## Key Claims

- Shache is presented as a small but strategically exposed Western Regions polity near the Kunlun-Congling-Pamir field, pressured by Qiang groups and Qiuci.
- The old Shache king's request to install Xieyou Princess's son Wannian is framed as a bid for Han and Wusun protection.
- Wannian lacks local Saka legitimacy and rules abusively, creating the opening for Hutuzheng to kill him and the Han envoy.
- Feng Fengshi treats distance from Chang'an and the risk of wider Western Regions disorder as reasons not to wait for formal authorization.
- Feng's success comes from using Han envoy authority to gather about fifteen thousand allied cavalry against a Shache capital with far fewer troops.
- Shache collapses internally when nobles kill Hutuzheng and offer his head to Feng, after which Feng installs another son of the Shache king.
- Emperor Xuan praises and promotes Feng but does not make him a marquis after Xiao Wangzhi warns that rewarding unauthorized mobilization would destabilize frontier command norms.
- The closing Wang-empress branch frames Xuan's selection of a childless, cautious consort as succession-risk control after the Huo Chengjun threat to crown prince Liu Shi.

## Key Quotes

> "聪明反被聪明误" - the host's transition from frontier action to Wang empress's safer lack of favor.

> "矫诏通知西域诸侯国发兵" - the episode's description of Feng Fengshi's decisive but legally risky mobilization.
