# EP245 一年一度寻味之旅：今年去吃创意菜

## Summary
This [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] annual food-issue episode has [[WuLiwei|吴立伟]], [[QiuLian|邱连]], [[Heimai|黑麦]], and [[XuePeng|薛鹏]] discuss why the magazine topic moved from familiar annual food searching toward "new flavor." The source treats [[CreativeChineseCuisine|创意中餐]] as a recombination problem rather than a break with tradition: regional flavors, chef training, modern cooking technique, dining-space format, and drink pairing are being reassembled across [[Guizhou|贵州]], [[Yunnan|云南]], [[Sichuan|四川]], [[Guangxi|广西]], and [[Fujian|福建]]. Its strongest synthesis is that novelty becomes durable when it preserves enough [[CulinaryGrammar|culinary grammar]] for diners to recognize a place while giving restaurants new forms such as bistros, tasting menus, rice wine, tea drinks, cocktails, and stir-fry counters.

## Key Claims
- The episode defines the "new" in the annual food issue through three linked shifts: emerging regional cuisines, younger chefs reworking tradition, and new restaurant formats such as small bistros.
- [[GuizhouCuisineFlavorTransfer|贵州酸辣风味迁移]] works because Guizhou cuisine has portable hooks: fermented sour systems, multiple chili forms, and [[RegionalFlavorTransfer|regional flavor transfer]] through ingredients such as 木姜子.
- [[YunnanCuisineMenuNarrative|云南菜路线叙事]] emerges from complexity. The province's highlands, tropical regions, minority food systems, long and short fermentation practices, and city adaptations make Yunnan hard to summarize but rich for menu design.
- [[SichuanCuisine|川菜]] innovation happens inside a strong tradition: chefs update soup bases, fish-fragrant chicken, thick-cut tongue, heat control, brining, vacuum treatment, low-temperature cooking, and aroma extraction without abandoning the cuisine's recognizability.
- [[GuangxiCuisineLegibility|广西菜可识别化]] is still earlier-stage than the Guizhou, Yunnan, or Sichuan cases. Luosifen gives outsiders one acid-spicy memory point, but dishes such as 老友鱼 and 柠檬鸭 are used to build a broader map.
- [[ChineseBistroLocalization|中式小酒馆本土化]] shifts the bistro from natural wine plus small plates toward Chinese food with wine, cocktails, rice wine, or tea-based nonalcoholic drinks.
- [[LocalAlcoholPairing|地方酒饮餐配]] is not only matching alcohol strength to dishes; it can translate local ingredients, smoke, fat, grain, microbial fermentation, and dish memory into the drink itself.
- [[StirFryRevival|爆炒与小炒回潮]] appears as a counterweight to polished fine dining: rougher, hotter, fresher, and more daily formats can become newly attractive when diners tire of over-refined restaurant design.

## Key Quotes
> "年里新味" - the annual food issue's shift from familiar yearly deliciousness toward newer regional and restaurant forms.

> "川菜黔味" - the older label that shows how Guizhou cuisine was long read through Sichuan before becoming more independently visible.

> "有很多抓手" - the episode's practical explanation for why Guizhou flavor elements can move into other cuisines.

> "1.0阶段" - the way the source describes Guangxi cuisine's current need to establish clear memory points before deeper innovation.
