# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》575丨古代大臣泄密下场有多惨？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianping 1 / 6 BCE under [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]], between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-574-gudai-de-manfen-zuowen-shi-shenme-yangzi-lotqprwr3rk5lke0rjn94riljuvh|Hanji 574]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-576-dangxiong-jiu-huangdi-de-ta-weihe-bei-qingdi-bisi-ll3pgr_smwlebpgo1vlufhe6qll6|Hanji 576]]. It follows [[ShiDanWesternHan|施丹]] opposing Aidi's effort to elevate the Dingtao parental line, then being removed after inconsistent advice on currency reform and a supposedly sealed memorial leaks through a subordinate copy.

The durable synthesis is that secrecy, ritual rank, and factional opportunity converge. Shi Dan's ritual argument that imperial supremacy cannot have a second center is politically strong but personally costly; once his confidential memorial circulates, Ding/Fu-aligned opponents can turn procedural leakage into a charge of "great irreverence," while Tang Lin's later plea wins only a reduced restoration as guannei marquis.

## Key Claims

- Aidi's court debate over Dingtao titles, attendants, vehicles, garments, and a capital temple is really a dispute over whether birth-parent filial feeling can alter imperial lineage order.
- Shi Dan argues from ritual hierarchy: once Aidi has inherited Chengdi's line and ancestral temple, the Dingtao parental line cannot receive treatment that creates a second supreme status.
- Aidi's anger at Shi Dan makes the official vulnerable before the leakage case, so later procedural faults land in an already hostile political field.
- Shi Dan's inconsistent answers on replacing coinage with older natural-money forms give opponents a "reversal" argument against his judgment.
- The leaked confidential memorial becomes the decisive handle: a subordinate copies and circulates Shi Dan's sealed submission, letting Ding/Fu-linked actors accuse him of letting common people see a private imperial communication.
- The Tingwei escalates the leak into "大不敬," making secrecy failure a violation of ruler-minister order rather than merely clerical negligence.
- Shen Xian, Que Qin, and Tang Lin argue that Shi Dan is a rare learned elder and former imperial teacher, but Aidi still removes him before later accepting Tang Lin's plea to change the outcome to guannei marquis status.

## Key Quotes

> "至尊不能有二" - Shi Dan's ritual objection to giving the Dingtao parental line court treatment that would blur supreme status.

> "忠臣不显谏" - the court's principle that loyal remonstrance should not be displayed publicly.

> "大不敬" - the legal-moral category used to escalate the leaked memorial into a grave offense.
