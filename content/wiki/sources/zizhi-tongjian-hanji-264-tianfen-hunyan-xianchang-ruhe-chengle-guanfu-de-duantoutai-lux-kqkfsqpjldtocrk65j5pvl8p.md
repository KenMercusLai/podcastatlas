# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》264｜田蚡婚宴现场，如何成了灌夫的断头台

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the banquet prequel to the two-part [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-265-dongchaotingbian-ruhe-yanbian-cheng-jieduandazhan-1-lil81vjxn5mait2wapdx_t4wy5hr|Hanji 265 court debate]]. After [[GuanFuWesternHan|灌夫]] resents the visible difference between guests' deference to the ascendant [[TianFenWesternHan|田蚡]] and the displaced [[DouYingWesternHan|窦婴]], drinking and prior hostility turn successive toasts into public insults; Tian Fen detains him and reframes the scene as grave disrespect toward a banquet attended under the empress dowager's order. Dou Ying, who had pressed a reluctant Guan Fu to attend, accepts responsibility, fails to secure private intercession, and petitions [[HanWudi|汉武帝]], moving a wedding conflict into formal court adjudication.

## Key Claims

- Tian Fen's failed attempt to obtain Dou Ying's land and Guan Fu's abusive response create a prior grievance before the wedding.
- Guan Fu anticipates the risk and initially declines the invitation because he has repeatedly offended Tian Fen while drunk, but Dou Ying says the conflict has been resolved and presses him to attend.
- Guests show much greater deference to Tian Fen than to Dou Ying, making changed political rank visible through banquet ritual and intensifying Guan Fu's anger.
- Guan Fu uses his toasts to needle Tian Fen and then openly insults other guests; when urged to apologize after detention, he refuses.
- Tian Fen invokes the empress dowager's order that imperial relatives and ministers attend, accuses Guan Fu of grave disrespect, and expands the investigation to Guan Fu's earlier conduct and clan.
- Dou Ying treats his own invitation as a moral obligation to rescue Guan Fu, spends money seeking intercession, and then petitions Han Wudi despite the danger to his title and life.
- Han Wudi initially accepts Dou Ying's proportionality argument and orders a public debate, which is the starting point of Hanji 265 rather than the resolution of the case.

## Key Quotes

> “年方少，勿饮酒；饮酒醉，最为丑。” - the episode's opening moral frame for linking intoxication, loss of control, and Guan Fu's fatal escalation.

> “即杀臣头，穴臣胸，何知程、李乎！” - the defiance attributed to Guan Fu when Tian Fen objects to his public abuse of senior guests.

## Connections

- [[GuanFuWesternHan|灌夫]] - reluctant attendee whose intoxication, status resentment, public abuse, and refusal to apologize trigger detention and wider prosecution.
- [[DouYingWesternHan|窦婴]] - displaced outer relative who brings Guan Fu to the banquet and later risks his own position to seek a proportionate judgment.
- [[TianFenWesternHan|田蚡]] - ascendant chancellor whose wedding, prior land dispute, arrest order, and legal reframing turn personal hostility into a state case.
- [[TemperateDrinkingSelfControl|节制饮酒与自我控制]] - the episode's explicit moral frame, qualified by the fact that intoxication amplifies rather than creates the prior power conflict.
- [[OuterRelativeBalanceUnderImperialPower|皇权下的外戚制衡]] - political setting in which a status reversal between two imperial in-laws shapes ritual deference, vulnerability, and access to judgment.
- [[PowerContingentSocialTies|权势依附型交往]] - adjacent pattern made visible when guests rise fully for Tian Fen but respond weakly to the fallen Dou Ying.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. This episode ends when Han Wudi orders the debate that [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-265-dongchaotingbian-ruhe-yanbian-cheng-jieduandazhan-1-lil81vjxn5mait2wapdx_t4wy5hr|Hanji 265 part 1]] narrates and therefore supplies its immediate prehistory.
- Causal caution: drinking lowers restraint and helps trigger the scene, but the supplied note also establishes prior hostility, changing outer-relative power, ritual humiliation, and Tian Fen's prosecutorial discretion; intoxication is not treated as a complete explanation.
- Legal caution: the exact offense, applicable Han law, authority for detention, scope of the expanded investigation, and later clan sentence require comparison with transmitted legal and historical records.
- Source-scope caution: the land request, reconciliation claim, guest gestures, words spoken, motives, sequence of toasts, private guilt, intercession spending, and Han Wudi's initial reaction remain episode-attributed.
