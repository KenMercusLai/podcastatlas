# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》983丨只会骗人的老板永远不是好老板

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills [[SunCe|孙策]]'s early dependency on [[YuanShu|袁术]] before the Jiangdong campaign developed in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-992-luanshi-you-duo-kepa-jiemi-changan-de-xuese-mengyan-ltuxzrb-1x5y2ctwo4drggxn5stz|Hanji 992]] through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-994-genzhe-dongwu-zhongchen-lvfan-xuexi-zhichang-zhihui-lgqp2vqmh1mss96gcwmflp-ccpfu|Hanji 994]]. It frames leadership credibility through Yuan Shu's repeated promises to give Sun Ce commandery offices, then follows Sun Ce from entrusting his family to [[ZhangHongLateHan|张纮]], recruiting through [[WuJingLateHan|吴景]] and [[SunBenLateHan|孙贲]] in [[DanyangCommandery|丹阳郡]], surviving [[ZuLangLateHan|祖郎]] near [[JingCountyLateHan|泾县]], and attacking [[LuKangLateHan|陆康]] in [[LujiangCommandery|庐江郡]]. The core contribution is [[PromiseCredibilityTalentRetention|承诺信用与人才流失]]: Yuan Shu can use Sun Ce's military value in the short run, but his broken Jiujiang and Lujiang promises make Sun Ce's later exit more intelligible.

## Key Claims
- The opening "诚信" lesson supplies the episode's interpretation frame: reliability means giving people follow-through, closure, and a response, not only speaking well.
- [[YuanShu|袁术]] tries to control Jiangdong indirectly by placing [[WuJingLateHan|吴景]] over [[DanyangCommandery|丹阳郡]] and appointing [[SunBenLateHan|孙贲]] to a military post there.
- The source presents Danyang as a valuable troop and supply base; its mountain terrain and strong population make it more than an ordinary commandery.
- [[SunCe|孙策]] entrusts his mother and younger siblings to [[ZhangHongLateHan|张纮]] in [[GuanglingCommandery|广陵]], making Zhang Hong a family-trust and gentry-backing figure before he becomes part of the Jiangdong staff in Hanji 994.
- Sun Ce invokes [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]]'s old alliance with Yuan Shu and asks to continue serving under Yuan Shu, but Yuan Shu withholds the full Sun Jian old force because Sun Ce is young.
- Yuan Shu sends Sun Ce to recruit through Wu Jing and Sun Ben; Sun Ce travels with [[LuFan|吕范]] and [[SunHeLateHan|孙河]] to [[QuALateHan|曲阿]] and receives several hundred Danyang troops.
- The episode treats early reliance on family and kinship resources as practical startup capacity rather than as a denial of Sun Ce's own ability.
- While moving through Danyang, Sun Ce is attacked by local strongman Zu Lang near Jing County, showing that Jiangdong entry faces local resistance rather than a smooth takeover.
- After the attack, Yuan Shu gives Sun Ce more than one thousand of Sun Jian's old troops and recommends him as `怀义校尉`, giving him a legitimate military command platform.
- Sun Ce's killing of an offender hiding in Yuan Shu's camp creates fear among Yuan Shu's soldiers and shows Sun Ce's willingness to enforce discipline even across camp boundaries.
- Yuan Shu first promises Sun Ce the [[JiujiangCommandery|九江郡]] office, then gives it to [[ChenJiLateHan|陈纪]] instead.
- Yuan Shu later demands grain from Lu Kang; when Lu Kang refuses, Yuan Shu sends Sun Ce to attack Lujiang and promises him the commandery if he succeeds.
- Lu Kang's refusal is explained through Han court legitimacy, Jiangdong elite interests, the size of Yuan Shu's grain demand, and Yuan Shu's bad reputation from Nanyang.
- After Sun Ce takes Lujiang, Yuan Shu again reneges and appoints [[LiuXunLateHan|刘勋]], making repeated broken promises the direct source of Sun Ce's disillusionment.
- [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]] enters as the Han-appointed Yangzhou governor whose official seat at Shouchun is unusable because Yuan Shu controls [[Shouchun|寿春]].
- Liu Yao first receives Wu Jing and Sun Ben at Qu'a, then expels them when he fears absorption by Yuan Shu and Sun Ce.
- Liu Yao sends [[FanNengLateHan|樊能]] and [[YuMiLateHan|于糜]] to hold [[HengjiangLateHan|横江]] and [[ZhangYingLateHan|张英]] to hold [[DanglikouLateHan|当利口]], while Yuan Shu counters by appointing [[HuiQuLateHan|惠衢]] as Yangzhou governor and ordering an attack.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is recorded. The episode's opening says Jian'an 1 / 196 CE while narrating setup material that existing wiki pages place before Sun Ce's 195 CE Jiangdong crossing; the wiki preserves this as source-scoped chronology compression rather than replacing the sequence in Hanji 992-994.
- The "boss" language and modern family-resource analogy are preserved as host interpretation, not treated as independent historical terminology.
