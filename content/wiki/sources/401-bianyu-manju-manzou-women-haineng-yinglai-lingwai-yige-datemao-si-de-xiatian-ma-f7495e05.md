# #401 边域、「慢聚漫奏」：我们还能迎来另外一个达特茅斯的夏天吗？

## Summary
This [[ShengdongJixi|声东击西]] episode has [[XuTao|徐涛]] interview [[YangBin|杨彬]] on the 1956 [[DartmouthAIWorkshop|Dartmouth AI workshop]], which Yang calls "达夏." The episode argues that the workshop mattered less as a formal consensus-making meeting than as a loose, peripheral, psychologically safe field where young researchers could let symbolic AI, neural networks, probabilistic reasoning, and machine-learning ideas coexist before the field hardened. It extends that history into organizational innovation, counterculture, [[Patagonia]], and university education under AI pressure.

## Key Claims
- The 1956 [[DartmouthAIWorkshop|Dartmouth AI workshop]] is framed as an origin point for the phrase "artificial intelligence" and as a field of unresolved possibilities rather than a single technical consensus.
- [[SlowGatheringImprovisation|慢聚慢奏]] names the episode's preferred innovation mode: slow gathering, loose conversation, improvisation, and enough time for unclear questions to stay open.
- [[PeripheralInnovationField|边域式创新场域]] matters because a non-mainstream location, mixed disciplines, young participants, and weaker authority pressure can let nonstandard ideas surface.
- [[ToleranceOfDifference|容差异]] is treated as more important than generic "tolerance of failure," because new work often needs protection before anyone can know whether it is right or wrong.
- [[EmergentInnovationStrategy|emergent innovation strategy]] is contrasted with efficiency-first planning; the episode links this to AI coding, agents, internal tools at [[Anthropic]], and Tencent work-body experiments.
- [[FaceToFaceCreativeExchange|face-to-face creative exchange]] is presented as hard to replace when goals are still undefined, using the Transformer paper's lunch, code, and hallway interactions as an example.
- [[AIUniversityCampusReframing|AI university campus reframing]] argues that AI may weaken the centrality of courses and homework while increasing the value of campus life, mentorship, friendship, entrepreneurship, oral defense, and apprenticeship-like learning.

## Key Quotes
> "慢聚慢奏" - Yang Bin's compact phrase for the Dartmouth-style innovation mode.

> "没大没小、没对没错、没皮没脸" - Yang's shorthand for psychological safety in the workshop atmosphere.

> "容差异" - the episode's sharper alternative to simply saying organizations should tolerate mistakes.
