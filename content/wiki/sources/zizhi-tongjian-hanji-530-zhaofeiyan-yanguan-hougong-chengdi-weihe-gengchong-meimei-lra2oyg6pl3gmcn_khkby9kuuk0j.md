# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》530丨赵飞燕艳冠后宫，成帝为何更宠妹妹？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate palace setup before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-531-mian-dui-zao-huangyao-ta-de-banfa-zhide-ni-xuexi-lsi7k-67uel46ea024kaubj2l_qh|Hanji 531]]'s false-accusation defense. [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] hears that [[ZhaoFeiyanWesternHan|赵飞燕]] has a beautiful younger sister, rewards and reassures Zhao Feiyan, brings [[ZhaoHedeWesternHan|赵合德]] into the palace, and then concentrates favor on the sisters while [[EmpressXuWesternHan|废后许氏]] and [[BanJieyuWesternHan|班婕妤]] lose ground.

The durable synthesis is that favorite politics becomes dangerous before the later formal scandal record. Chengdi's indulgence creates a winner-take-all palace field; Xu Ye's private ritual gives Zhao Feiyan a usable accusation against Empress Xu; Ban Jieyu survives by answering the wugu charge through rational motive analysis and then removes herself to Changxin Palace. The host extends the story into emotional restraint, retreat judgment, and a Jungian `Anima` reading of Chengdi's desire, but those psychological and literary frames remain source-scoped commentary.

## Key Claims

- Chengdi brings Zhao Hede into the palace only after using material rewards and housing arrangements to soften Zhao Feiyan's likely jealousy.
- Zhao Hede is presented as more physically captivating than Zhao Feiyan, making Chengdi's preference a mix of beauty, novelty, and unmanaged desire rather than only formal status.
- Zhao Feiyan's favor shapes palace fashion: the episode links "环肥燕瘦" to Zhao Feiyan's thinness and says palace women imitate her by extreme dieting.
- The Zhao sisters' exclusive favor displaces Empress Xu and Ban Jieyu, turning Chengdi's private preference into palace security risk.
- Empress Xu's complaint to her sister Xu Ye becomes dangerous when Xu Ye privately arranges ritual prayer and Zhao Feiyan reframes it as cursing the emperor and court.
- Ban Jieyu survives the same investigative field by arguing that cursing is irrational whether spirits exist or not, then requests service at Changxin Palace to avoid further Zhao-sister pressure.
- The host uses Kundera and `Anima` as modern interpretive language for Chengdi's emotional immaturity, treating him as stuck in desire rather than responsibility or wisdom.

## Key Quotes

> "死生有命，富贵在天" - Ban Jieyu's reported opening frame for rejecting the logic of curse-based self-help.

> "最大的一次心理成长" - the host's closing prompt for turning the palace story into self-reflection.
