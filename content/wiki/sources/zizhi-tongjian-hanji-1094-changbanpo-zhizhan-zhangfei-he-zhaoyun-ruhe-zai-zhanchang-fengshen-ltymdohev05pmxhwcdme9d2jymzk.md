# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1094丨长坂坡之战，张飞和赵云如何在战场封神？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode reconstructs the 208 CE [[ChangbanBattle|长坂坡之战]] as both a battlefield collapse and the opening of a recovery sequence. [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s elite cavalry overtakes [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s civilian-heavy movement; [[ZhangFei|张飞]] covers the retreat with a small force and a broken bridge, while [[ZhaoYun|赵云]] turns back through the disorder, rescues Liu Bei's family members, and returns carrying [[LiuChan|刘禅]].

The episode then shifts from individual heroism to [[PostDefeatForceReconstitution|败后兵力重组]]. Liu Bei abandons the blocked Jiangling route, moves toward Hanjin, reconnects with [[GuanYu|关羽]], and receives support from [[LiuQiLateHan|刘琦]] before regrouping around Xiakou. Its central strategic claim is that a routed organization can recover by changing route, preserving trusted commanders, joining separated forces, and converting political relationships into a new operating base.

## Key Claims
- Cao Cao's cavalry pursuit scatters Liu Bei's movement, captures people and baggage, and turns the Changban retreat into a severe organizational collapse.
- Zhang Fei covers Liu Bei's withdrawal with a small mounted group, destroys or blocks the bridge, and deters immediate pursuit; the episode distinguishes this from the more dramatic Romance account of his shout killing an enemy officer.
- A report that Zhao Yun has fled north is interpreted as plausible in a field where many people are defecting, but Liu Bei rejects it and Zhao Yun returns after rescuing Liu Chan and other family members.
- [[CaoChunLateHan|曹淳]] is credited with capturing two of Liu Bei's daughters and baggage; the episode's further claims about their later fate are not securely sourced.
- Liu Bei changes direction from Jiangling toward Hanjin, while Guan Yu redirects his separate force to meet him and Liu Qi brings Jiangxia support.
- The Hanjin-Xiakou sequence converts scattered survivors and allied troops into a force the episode gives as twenty thousand before the Red Cliffs alignment.
- The source presents Cao Cao's rapid cavalry pursuit as operationally successful but physically expensive, while its exact distance, losses, and battlefield imagery require verification.
- The episode uses the battle to contrast historical-source material with later literary amplification and to shift attention from isolated heroic scenes to route choice, force reunion, and political recovery.

## Key Quotes
> “子龙不弃我走也。” — Liu Bei's confidence in Zhao Yun as rendered by the episode.

> “我张翼德在此。” — the episode's compact rendering of Zhang Fei's bridge defense.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is recorded. This source supplies the battle and regrouping sequence that Hanji 1095 and Hanji 1099 previously used mainly as background or aftermath.
- The episode explicitly separates the historical bridge-defense account from the Romance claim that Zhang Fei's shout frightened Xiahou Jie to death.
- The capture of Liu Bei's two daughters is source-attributed to the episode's reading of the Cao Chun biography; the alleged later Copper Bird Terrace fate is presented only as rumor and is not adopted as settled history.
- Exact troop totals, distances, one-day-and-night pursuit speed, cavalry losses, dialogues, promotions, and the host's judgments about Liu Bei's family priorities remain source-scoped pending primary-source verification.
