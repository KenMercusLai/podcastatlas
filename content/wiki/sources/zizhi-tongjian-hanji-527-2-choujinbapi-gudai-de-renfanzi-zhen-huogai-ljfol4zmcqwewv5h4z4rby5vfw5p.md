# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》527-2丨抽筋扒皮？古代的人贩子真活该！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] short episode begins from contemporary Chinese trafficking news, especially the [[YuHuayingCase|于华英案]] and a Shenzhen father's successful search for his abducted son, then turns to ancient legal punishment for abducting and selling people. Its central synthesis is that trafficking destroys families across generations, so both ancient law and modern public attention are framed as responses to a crime that cannot be reduced to a private transaction.

The source uses [[ZhangjiashanHanmuZhujian|张家山汉墓竹简]] and [[ErNianLuLing|《二年律令》]] to argue that early Han law punished abductors, knowing buyers, and even buyers who claimed ignorance. It then connects Ming harsh punishment, the film [[BlindMountainFilm|《盲山》]], and the image of a chained woman to [[PublicAttentionAgainstTrafficking|反拐公共关注]], treating thinking and speaking as the beginning of refusing social blindness.

## Key Claims

- The episode frames trafficking in people as a destructive market in human beings, not only as isolated criminal behavior.
- The [[YuHuayingCase|于华英案]] and Shenzhen father-search story are used to foreground the lasting family trauma behind every abducted child.
- The host says [[ErNianLuLing|《二年律令》]] called trafficking conduct "虐人" and imposed severe punishment even before resale was completed.
- Knowing buyers are treated as part of the same criminal chain, while buyers who claimed ignorance still faced punishment such as tattooing, hard labor, or penal service.
- The episode's legal-history point is less technical codicology than moral deterrence: ancient law is presented as making the social boundary unmistakable.
- [[BlindMountainFilm|《盲山》]] and the chained-woman image extend the problem into modern coercive marriage, local obstruction, and rescue difficulty.
- The ending argues that [[PublicAttentionAgainstTrafficking|反拐公共关注]] begins with refusing to look away: thinking and speaking are framed as the start of being "not blind."

## Key Quotes

> "思考与发声，就是一个不盲的开始" - the episode's closing civic-attention line.

> "人，是这个世界上最丧心病狂的商品" - the host's opening moral framing of trafficking.
