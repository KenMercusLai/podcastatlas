# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》401｜眭弘指名汉昭帝退位让贤？霍光：宰了！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|汉昭帝刘弗陵]] regency layer before the later Hanji 404 death-and-succession episode. It presents [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]] as the effective court decision-maker: he promotes [[ZhangAnshiWesternHan|张安世]] and [[DuYannianWesternHan|杜延年]], dominates appointments while [[TianQianqiuWesternHan|田千秋]] remains cautious, and handles advice through a strict-but-buffered decision process.

The durable synthesis is that the [[SuiHongWesternHan|眭弘]] case turns omen interpretation into a succession threat. Standing stones, revived trees, white crows, and worm-marked leaves are read through classical learning as signs that a commoner or Gongsun figure should rise, and Sui Hong then advises Han Zhao to seek a worthy man and abdicate. Huo Guang's execution of Sui Hong and the forwarding friend shows how quickly omen-coded abdication language becomes intolerable when it challenges the reigning Liu line.

## Key Claims

- Huo Guang is described as holding decisive power over Han Zhao's court: "政事一决大将军光" frames policy and personnel authority around the regent rather than the chancellor.
- Zhang Anshi's right-general and Guanglu Xun appointment, and Du Yannian's promotion to Taipu, show Huo Guang building a trusted administrative circle under Han Zhao.
- Tian Qianqiu's cautious non-intervention reflects survival under an overmighty regent rather than active chancellor leadership.
- Huo Guang's harsh law enforcement is paired with Du Yannian's more lenient review of petitions, giving the regime a black-face/white-face operating style.
- The source treats Han Zhao-era selection and one-year official review as relatively fair within the episode's account, while still allowing punishment for misconduct.
- Xiongnu frontier pressure remains present, but Han military success and Xiongnu fear lead toward indirect heqin feelers rather than immediate open rapprochement.
- Sui Hong interprets Taishan stones, revived shrine wood, revived willows, white crows, and "公孙病已立" marks through Gongyang and Dong Zhongshu-style reasoning as signs of a coming transfer of the throne.
- Sui Hong's memorial urges Han Zhao to follow the Yao-Shun model, seek a worthy man, yield the empire, and withdraw to a small fief; Huo Guang has Sui Hong and the intermediary executed for dangerous false omen speech and great rebellion.

## Key Quotes

> "政事一决大将军光" - the compact court-power description used for Huo Guang under Han Zhao.

> "汉昭帝应及时寻找贤人，将帝位禅让给对方" - the episode's summary of Sui Hong's memorial.
