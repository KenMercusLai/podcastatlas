# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》110-1｜李斯：米仓老鼠和下水道老鼠你怎么选？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode moves from [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-109-2-chunshenjun-bei-huangdi-dajiuge-quanzu-miemen-locma2plu7v-vsssm5dr5frc3szw|Qinji 109-2]]'s [[LiSi|李斯]] hook into [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]]'s tenth year, 237 BCE. After [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]] is removed from central office, Qin royal-clan ministers urge a [[GuestExpulsionOrder|逐客令]] against foreign guest officials, using [[LaoAi|嫪毐]], Lu Buwei, and [[ZhengGuoHydraulicEngineer|郑国]] as evidence that outsiders threaten Qin.

The episode then backfills Li Si before the full [[JianZhuKeShu|《谏逐客书》]] argument appears. Li Si is presented as a low-origin [[ChuState|楚国]] clerk whose contrast between toilet mice and granary mice becomes [[MousePhilosophy|老鼠哲学]]: fate changes with environment, so an ambitious person must move toward richer opportunity structures, including study with [[Xunzi|荀子]] and eventually Qin service.

## Key Claims
- [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]]'s fall opens space for Qin royal-clan ministers to attack foreign guest officials as a security and factional risk.
- The source frames the [[GuestExpulsionOrder|逐客令]] as both a national-security argument and a court-interest argument: native Qin nobles resent important offices being occupied by outside talent.
- [[ZhengGuoHydraulicEngineer|郑国]]'s exposed canal mission is used here not only as a [[SelfDefeatingResourceDrainStrategy|fatigue-Qin backfire]] but also as political evidence for anti-guest suspicion.
- [[LiSi|李斯]] is caught in the order because he is a Chu guest minister, turning the expulsion crisis into his moment of emergence.
- Li Si's early life is described as low-status and overworked: a minor Chu official with little money, no impressive family background, and strong dissatisfaction with routine clerical life.
- The toilet-mouse and granary-mouse contrast gives Li Si a concrete social theory: the same kind of being lives differently because its environment, resources, and exposure to danger differ.
- The host reads [[MousePhilosophy|老鼠哲学]] as essentially profit-seeking, but also as a practical self-diagnosis that pushes Li Si toward education and mobility.
- Li Si's decision to study under [[Xunzi|荀子]] is presented as deliberate self-investment: without birth status or family power, learning becomes the route to a higher arena.
- The episode ends before fully presenting Li Si's memorial or revealing the spy-case mastermind behind the cliffhanger.

## Key Quotes
> "近者悦，远者来" - the older talent-attraction norm the host contrasts with Qin's mass expulsion.

> "老鼠哲学" - the host's label for Li Si's environment-and-interest lesson.

> "本质是逐利" - the host's interpretive judgment on Li Si's conclusion.
