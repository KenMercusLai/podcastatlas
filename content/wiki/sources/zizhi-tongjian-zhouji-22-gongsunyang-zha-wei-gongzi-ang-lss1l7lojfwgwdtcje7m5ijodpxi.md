# 《资治通鉴·周纪》22丨公孙鞅诈魏公子卬

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode covers 340 BCE under [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]], focusing on [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]]'s attack on [[WeiState|魏国]] and his capture of [[GongziAng|公子卬 / 公子昂]]. Shang Yang persuades [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]] that Qin and Wei are strategic rivals, then personally leads the campaign because Qin's [[FarmingWarStatecraft|耕战逻辑]] makes war the highest path to reward. The episode reads Gongzi Ang's acceptance of a friendly peace parley as [[MotivatedBias|motivated bias]] and [[PeaceParleyEntrapment|和谈诱捕]]: he trusts old ties and a humane withdrawal proposal, but fails to ask why the invading Qin side would accept a no-gain retreat.

## Key Claims
- In 340 BCE, the chronicle records three events: Shang Yang attacks Wei, Qi and Zhao jointly attack Wei, and [[ChuXuanwang|楚宣王]] dies with 楚威王 succeeding him.
- The episode says [[SimaGuang|司马光]] passes quickly over the Qi-Zhao and Chu succession notices but gives more space to Shang Yang's attack on Wei.
- [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]] tells [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]] that [[QinState|秦国]] and Wei cannot remain harmless neighbors: if Wei is strong it threatens Qin, and if Qin attacks while Wei is weakened it can expand eastward.
- Shang Yang argues that Wei's recent losses to [[QiState|齐国]] and loss of attached powers make this the right moment for Qin to strike.
- If Wei cannot hold its western territory and has to lean eastward, Qin can occupy the western advantage and build toward wider control over the lords.
- Shang Yang's personal command is explained through [[FarmingWarStatecraft|耕战逻辑]]: farming produces state strength but limited personal reward, while battlefield success can produce large rank and political capital under the [[MilitaryMeritRankSystem|军功爵制]].
- [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王]] sends [[GongziAng|公子卬 / 公子昂]] to face Shang Yang after Wei has already suffered heavy blows, including the earlier capture of [[PrinceShenWei|太子申]] after [[MalingBattle|马陵之战]].
- Shang Yang writes to Gongzi Ang as an old acquaintance, frames the war as public duty rather than private enmity, and proposes a meeting, alliance oath, drink, and mutual withdrawal.
- The episode says the proposal should have looked suspicious: Qin is the invader, so mutual withdrawal without territorial gain would mean Wei's defense has succeeded and Qin has failed.
- Under Qin's military-reward system, a voluntary empty-handed retreat would also disappoint the army and contradict Shang Yang's own incentive logic.
- Gongzi Ang accepts because old friendship, peace rhetoric, and desire for a bloodless success fit what he wants to believe.
- During the post-oath drinking, Shang Yang's hidden armed men seize Gongzi Ang, turning a peace ritual into battlefield deception.
- The episode frames the trap as a new reading of "兵以诈立" and closes by asking whether capturing the commander can become leverage for negotiation.

## Key Quotes
> "兵以诈立" - the episode's frame for Shang Yang's capture of Gongzi Ang.

> "人容易相信自己愿意相信的事" - the psychological warning applied to Gongzi Ang's acceptance of the parley.

> "不战而屈人之兵" - the [[SunziBingfa|《孙子兵法》]] principle used as the next-episode hook.
