# 如此打工 30 年：流水线上的面孔与时间

## Summary
This [[Mianji|面基]] episode uses [[ZhanTeacher|占老师]]'s more than 30 years of work in [[Dongguan|东莞]] and [[ChanganTown|长安镇]] to connect migrant-worker life, [[PearlRiverDeltaManufacturingTransformation|珠三角制造业变迁]], factory discipline, and long-term documentary photography. The source's strongest contribution is to make [[WorkerPhotographicArchive|工人摄影档案]] an inside method: dorms, canteens, commute routes, QQ posts, wages, certificates, insurance gaps, and factory details become evidence rather than background color. Its durable synthesis is that "打工三十年" is no longer only endurance; it requires preserving perception, learning, and self-construction while production systems, orders, factories, and tools keep changing.

## Key Claims
- [[ZhanTeacher|占老师]] is presented as a worker-photographer, not a full-time outside observer: after leaving military service he came to Guangdong, worked from security roles into long-term factory life, and kept photographing coworkers and industrial districts while still holding a day job.
- The episode uses [[ChanganTown|长安镇]] as a manufacturing-density case where agriculture has largely disappeared and the landscape is dominated by factories, dormitories, industrial roads, and worker services.
- [[TayloristFactoryTime|工厂时间]] replaced agricultural seasonality with machine time: two-shift factories, 12-hour shifts, short breaks, strict hierarchy, and QR/SAP-style tracking made time, tools, packaging, and bodies more measurable.
- [[FactoryDisciplineAndSensoryNumbing|无所适从]] names the affective cost of long production discipline: workers can become detached from social, natural, and bodily perception even outside the line.
- The source extends [[FlexibleManufacturing|柔性制造]] historically: long-batch assembly-line work gave way to multi-batch, small-volume orders, automation, black-light factories, and [[ManufacturingMicroFragmentation|小微化]] where small shops absorb narrower process slices and more operating risk.
- First-generation migrant workers often accepted dorms, canteens, overtime, and closed management because cash income and [[MigrantFamilyRemittanceObligation|remittance]] created visible hope compared with rural alternatives.
- [[MigrantWorkerSocialInsuranceLegacy|农民工社保遗留问题]] appears decades later: early nonstandard identities, missing records, vanished employers, and unpaid contributions turn old informal hiring into retirement and proof problems.
- The source compares white-collar AI anxiety with older factory automation: manufacturing workers have long been forced through [[AutomationDisplacementEffect|task-level displacement]] and new-process learning.
- [[WorkerPhotographicArchive|工人摄影档案]] matters because repeated photography becomes self-directed labor rather than interchangeable line labor; the archive can be reorganized by future questions.
- The episode's practical ethic is [[ContinuousLearningAgainstDisplacement|别放弃自我]]: learn tools, languages, factory systems, and concepts before crisis, because the issue is less whether one can work for 30 years than whether a specific factory or role will still exist.

## Key Quotes
> "无所适从" - the photo-series title used to describe factory-trained dislocation.

> "别放弃自我" - the episode's recurring worker-facing advice.

> "看到" / "看见" - the source's distinction between visual contact and meaningful perception.

## Contradictions
- No direct contradiction found.
- The source qualifies simple [[FlexibleManufacturing|柔性制造]] optimism: flexibility can mean smarter systems and customer fit, but it can also mean process fragmentation, risk shifting, and continuous worker adaptation.
- The source qualifies [[AutomationDisplacementEffect|automation displacement]] pessimism: machinery can reduce line labor and skill control, but workers and small shops can sometimes survive through accumulated process knowledge, niche specialization, and learning.
