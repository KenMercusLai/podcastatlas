## Summary

This [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] roundtable uses relationship rankings, cross-novel pairings, clue puzzles, battle favorites, and screen adaptations to compare ways of reading [[JinYong|金庸]]. Its strongest contribution is methodological: a fictional romance can be judged by timing, recognition, reciprocity, agency, and whether the partners let one another flourish; imagined pairings then work as controlled character experiments rather than claims about canon. The later segments add the “百花错” puzzle as a compact form of [[NarrativeMisdirection|narrative misdirection]], distinguish ensemble battle convergence from simple power escalation, and show how childhood access and audiovisual craft jointly shape adaptation preference.

## Key Claims

- The hosts' preferred relationships differ, but their recurring standard is mutual recognition: a persuasive pair understands each person's needs and lets both characters become less constrained rather than merely rewarding persistence or sacrifice.
- Cheng Lingsu and Hu Fei concentrate the episode's disagreement over unreturned devotion. Her intelligence, protection, and death make the relationship tragic, while the group also asks whether self-erasing sacrifice should be romanticized.
- The Linghu Chong–Yue Lingshan debate separates canonical outcome from readerly regret: an early bond can remain emotionally meaningful even if both later form other attachments.
- Cross-novel “乱配” treats partner substitution as a character test. Pairing Guo Jing with Cheng Lingsu or Wei Xiaobao with Li Mochou asks whether different attitudes toward beauty, competence, status, and emotional reassurance would alter a tragic pattern.
- “百花错” makes a familiar answer accumulate through several true-looking clues, then uses a final qualification or ambiguous word to reveal another character; its pleasure comes from both knowledge and recognition of the induced mental set.
- Guangming Summit is praised because Zhang Wuji's abilities, restraint, medical knowledge, relationships, and moral choices converge without reducing the sequence to stronger and stronger opponents.
- Shaoshi Mountain is praised as a different convergence model in which long-separated characters, identities, rivalries, and revelations meet in one escalating ensemble event.
- Screen favorites mix craft and reception history: performance, pacing, music, landscape, action, and production scale remain assessable, but formative viewing and repeated access help determine which version feels definitive.
