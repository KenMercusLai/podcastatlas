# 018 趣谈金庸处女作《书剑恩仇录》|百花错，开头是错，结尾还是错

## Summary

This [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] episode places [[BookAndSword|《书剑恩仇录》]] at the beginning of [[JinYong|金庸]]'s development as a wuxia novelist. It argues that the novel gains familiarity, credibility, and legendary force by joining the Qianlong parentage story, anti-Qing restoration, regional memory, and Jianghu adventure, but that the known failure of the political cause also limits surprise and gives the story a tragic horizon from the outset.

The discussion then evaluates [[RedFlowerSociety|红花会]] as an early ensemble experiment and reads weapons and martial arts as characterization. Distinct appearance, occupation, weapon, and side plot help separate fourteen leaders, while common goals and weak internal conflict leave some figures schematic. The final reading treats 百花错拳 as more than a combat device: its deliberate wrongness condenses [[ChenJialuo|陈家洛]]'s repeated errors in judgment, love, leadership, and trust, while 袁士霄's earlier regret gives the same martial form an intergenerational echo.

## Key Claims

- Historical persons, places, political causes, and local legends make an invented Jianghu feel concrete, while fixed outcomes constrain counterfactual freedom and suspense.
- Jin Yong's early combination of history and legend is judged less integrated than in later novels but already establishes a durable narrative method.
- A large ensemble becomes memorable through differentiated roles, conflicts, relationships, weapons, and independent arcs rather than headcount or equal exposition alone.
- The Red Flower Society's shared purpose and limited internal conflict weaken differentiation, while 文泰来's rescue function and 余鱼同's romantic-adventure line give them stronger narrative positions.
- Weapons can encode occupation, rank, temperament, and narrative function; exotic equipment helps initial recognition but cannot replace consequential action.
- The movement from elaborate weapons toward sword, empty-hand skill, internal power, and bearing is interpreted as a shift from external spectacle toward character-centered martial aesthetics.
- 百花错拳's “错” links technique to fate: Chen repeatedly misreads people and situations, trusts Qianlong, loses love, and helps produce political and personal tragedy.
