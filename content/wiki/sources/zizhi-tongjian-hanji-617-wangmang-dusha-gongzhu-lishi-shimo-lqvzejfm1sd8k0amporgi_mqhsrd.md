# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》617丨王莽毒杀公主历史始末

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 3 CE aftermath of the [[DogBloodCase|狗血案]], between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-you-xinji-de-ren-you-duo-kepa-lnhkk70905eqg4lqwh3mp2sxzamc|Hanji 616]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-618-wangmang-weihe-shi-duoquan-de-touhao-wanjia-lqcaoli6r7lbdzqy7emw8sbzs0wq|Hanji 618]]. It shows [[WangMang|王莽]] turning the case against [[WangYuXinPrince|王宇]], [[WuZhangWesternHan|吴章]], and [[LuKuanDogBloodCase|吕宽]] into a broader political purge that reaches royal kin, Wang-family rivals, senior officials, and [[JingwuPrincessWesternHan|靖武公主]].

The durable synthesis is that Wang Mang uses court procedure, dowager language, kinship morality, and elite relationship networks to widen a specific palace case into [[CaseExpansionPoliticalPurge|案件扩大化政治清洗]]. [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]'s approving edict praises Wang Mang for not letting family feeling harm state hierarchy, but the episode treats that language as an authorization structure for killings that continue for more than a year. Jingwu Princess's forced poisoning becomes the sharpest case: Wang Mang allegedly uses her private sexual scandal as the charge, sends poison under Wang Zhengjun's name, and then tells Wang Zhengjun that her sister died of illness to prevent a funeral visit from exposing the coercion.

## Key Claims

- Wang Mang presents Wang Yu's punishment through a Zhougong analogy, comparing his own killing of a son to suppressing rebellion for public order.
- Zhen Han's arranged edict from Wang Zhengjun turns "不以亲亲害尊尊" into official praise of Wang Mang's family-sacrifice posture.
- The dog-blood case expands because Western Han elites are densely connected by kinship, marriage, title, office, friendship, and old grudges.
- Jingwu Princess is forced to drink poison after Wang Mang links her to the case through Xue Kuang and uses her private relationship with a stepson as the direct charge.
- Liang King [[LiuLiWesternHan|刘丽 / 刘立]], Wang-family figures Wang Li and Wang Ren, He Wu, Wang An, Xin Qingji's sons, and Bao Xuan are presented as victims of the widened purge.
- Feng Meng's flight to Liaodong shows how the purge changes elite political calculation: remaining near the court becomes a personal disaster risk.
- Wang Mang hides the truth of Jingwu Princess's death from Wang Zhengjun, because a dowager funeral visit could reveal that her name had been used for coercion.

## Key Quotes

> "不以亲亲害尊尊" - the edict formula praising Wang Mang's punishment of his own son.

> "新账旧账一起算" - the episode's summary of Wang Mang using the case to settle old and new enemies.

> "天下震怖" - the political atmosphere after the extended purge.
