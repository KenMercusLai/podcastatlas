# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》941丨身处官场，他竟想干翻皇帝

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 188 CE court layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-942-yue-youxiu-de-ren-huode-yue-lei-heli-ma-lkwlrcgnt-kxxc2qqz6z6wkg7e5z|Hanji 942]]. It follows [[WangFenLateHan|王芬]]'s failed plan with [[XuYouLateHan|许攸]], [[ChenYiLateHan|陈逸]], and others to seize [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]], kill the eunuchs, and install [[HefeiMarquisLateHan|合肥侯]], while [[CaoCao|曹操]] and [[HuaXin|华歆]] refuse the risk. The second half links the failed coup to the [[WestGardenEightColonels|西园八校尉]], [[JianShuoLateHan|蹇硕]]'s military command, Cao Cao's dianjun xiaowei appointment, [[MaRidiLateHan|马日磾]]'s taiwei promotion, [[DongZhongLateHan|董重]]'s rise, renewed [[QingzhouYellowTurbans|青州黄巾军]] disorder, and Emperor Ling's Pingle Guan military display.

## Key Claims
- In 188 CE, [[WangFenLateHan|王芬]] uses a report about [[HeishanArmy|黑山贼 / 黑山军]] attacks as the pretext for mobilizing troops in [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]].
- [[WangFenLateHan|王芬]], [[XuYouLateHan|许攸]], [[ChenYiLateHan|陈逸]], and their co-plotters plan to strike while [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] travels north toward his Hejian old residence.
- The plan combines three high-risk moves: seizing the emperor, killing the eunuchs, and replacing the ruler with [[HefeiMarquisLateHan|合肥侯]].
- [[CaoCao|曹操]], then serving as yilang, refuses [[WangFenLateHan|王芬]]'s invitation because imperial deposition is dangerous even for models such as Yi Yin and Huo Guang.
- [[HuaXin|华歆]] discourages [[TaoqiuHongLateHan|陶丘洪]] from joining after judging [[WangFenLateHan|王芬]] rough, militarily weak, and unable to manage the risk of emperor replacement.
- A red vapor or qi appears across the northern night sky; the court astrologer reads it as a northern plot warning, and [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] cancels the trip.
- Once ordered to dismiss his troops and come to court, [[WangFenLateHan|王芬]] flees and kills himself, turning the plot into a case of [[ImperialDepositionCoupRisk|imperial deposition coup risk]] rather than successful anti-eunuch action.
- [[MaRidiLateHan|马日磾]] is appointed taiwei after serving as shesheng xiaowei, linking scholarly prestige, family background, and high-office risk.
- The court establishes the [[WestGardenEightColonels|西园八校尉]], with [[JianShuoLateHan|蹇硕]] as shangjun xiaowei and even the general-in-chief placed under his command in this arrangement.
- [[YuanShao|袁绍]] becomes zhongjun xiaowei and [[CaoCao|曹操]] becomes dianjun xiaowei, giving both later powerholders earlier military footholds inside the capital command system.
- [[DongZhongLateHan|董重]] is appointed piqi jiangjun through his relationship to the Yongle empress dowager line, prefiguring the succession-side exposure developed in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-943-quanshi-kaoyan-renxing-beihou-cangzhe-juda-xiongxian-lg7ia5xok-swdmhml35yv9qqkvt|Hanji 943]].
- [[QingzhouYellowTurbans|青州黄巾军]] and Xuzhou Yellow Turban forces rise again in late 188, keeping civilian suffering and local disorder active before later Cao Cao and Gongsun Zan branches.
- After a wangqi warning that Luoyang will see fighting between the two palaces, [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] holds a major review at Pingle Guan and calls himself "无上将军"; [[GaiXunLateHan|盖勋]] criticizes the display as martial boasting in the wrong place.

## Key Quotes
> "废立皇帝是天下至险之事" - the risk frame [[CaoCao|曹操]] and [[HuaXin|华歆]] use against [[WangFenLateHan|王芬]]'s plan.

> "机关算尽" - the episode's judgment on [[WangFenLateHan|王芬]] after the plot collapses.

> "无上将军" - [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s self-title during the Pingle Guan military display.
