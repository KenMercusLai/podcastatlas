# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》432｜开纪念功臣之先河！揭秘麒麟阁十一功臣

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Yuankang 4 / 62 BCE under [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] before the later Zhao Chongguo Qiang-campaign sequence. It uses the later [[QilinPavilionMeritHonors|麒麟阁功臣荣典]] list to foreground [[ZhangAnshiWesternHan|张安世]], then returns to that year's edicts, [[WeiXuanchengWesternHan|韦玄成]]'s title-refusal story, the Cheshi king Wugui transfer, the early Qiang-Xiongnu warning signs before [[ZhaoChongguoWesternHan|赵充国]]'s campaign, and the low grain-price image of Zhaoxuan prosperity.

The durable synthesis is that Xuan-era restoration joins memory, reward, restraint, and frontier anticipation. Zhang Anshi survives Huo Guang-era politics through cautious competence, low visibility, generosity, and timely separation from the Huo family; Wei Xuancheng's forced succession shows the court converting personal refusal into usable moral office; and Zhao Chongguo's warning shows frontier crises forming before open rebellion when Qiang groups begin repairing internal divisions and seeking Xiongnu help.

## Key Claims

- Xuan's later order to place eleven meritorious ministers in the Qilin Pavilion is presented as an early model for commemorating founding or restoration merit.
- Zhang Anshi is framed as the second Qilin Pavilion minister after Huo Guang and as a "一门三侯" elder whose survival depended on caution, low profile, broad-mindedness, few words, and accurate reading of power shifts.
- Zhang Anshi's remembered ability includes a story in which he reconstructs three lost books for Han Wudi almost exactly, leading to office as Guanglu Dafu.
- Xuan's Yuankang 4 edicts pardon most criminals over eighty, praise the clean official Yin Wenggui, and reward descendants of Gaozu-era meritorious ministers.
- [[WeiXuanchengWesternHan|韦玄成]] refuses or delays taking his father's title by feigning madness, but Xuan eventually orders him to inherit while also appointing him Henan taishou.
- Wusun sends the Cheshi king Wugui to Chang'an after Han pressure, keeping [[CheshiKingdomWesternHan|车师国]] and [[Wusun|乌孙]] inside the wider Western Regions pressure field.
- The Qiang branch of the episode shows the pre-crisis layer before later Zhao Chongguo material: Xianling Qiang ask to cross the Yellow River for pasture, Yi Qu Anguo grants permission before reporting, Zhao warns against unauthorized frontier discretion, and Qiang leaders later make peace with each other, exchange hostages, and seek Xiongnu aid.
- The episode ends with the Han Shu image of years of harvest and grain as low as five coins per shi, treating it as a sign of Zhaoxuan-era social prosperity.
