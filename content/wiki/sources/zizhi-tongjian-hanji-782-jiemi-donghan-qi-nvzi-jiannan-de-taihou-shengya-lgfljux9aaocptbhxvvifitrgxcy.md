# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》782丨揭秘东汉奇女子艰难的太后生涯

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 108-109 CE opening layer before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-783-renxing-qian-guize-nide-jiazhi-jueding-yiqie-lq5qbki6crhieafbv7g5kc07unqe|Hanji 783]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-784-donghan-diaoyu-zhifa-diyiren-yuxu-ls6zezfw2pvaytoo94igdscfwkpt|Hanji 784]]. It combines [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Empress Dowager Deng]]'s early fiscal and frontier crisis, [[DengZhiLateHan|Deng Zhi]]'s failed Qiang campaign and continued favor, [[PangCanLateHan|Pang Can]]'s imprisoned policy memorial, [[RenShangLateHan|Ren Shang]]'s early command failure, [[DianlingQiang|Dianling]]'s Qiang coalition, Luoyang famine, and emergency sale of ranks and court-adjacent identities.

The durable synthesis is that Deng's austerity was real but insufficient. Qiang war, transport costs, famine, and revenue exhaustion forced the state toward [[RankSaleDisasterFinance|rank sale disaster finance]] and official-pay pressure, while the frontier debate already showed the tension between retrenchment, civilian relocation, and future defense cost.

## Key Claims

- The regency enters the current wiki under simultaneous Qiang war, famine, disaster, fiscal exhaustion, and administrative manpower strain before the later Hanji 783-785 layers.
- Deng Zhi sends Ren Shang and others against Dianling's Qiang forces at Pingxiang, but Ren Shang is defeated and more than 8,000 soldiers are killed.
- Huangzhong grain rises to an extreme price, hunger deaths become uncountable, and long-distance military grain transport becomes a core policy problem rather than a background detail.
- Pang Can's son Pang Jun memorializes from outside the prison system to transmit Pang Can's argument that continued western campaigning and transport are destroying both soldiers and civilians.
- Pang Can recommends recalling Deng Zhi, leaving Ren Shang in field command, moving Liangzhou officials and people toward Sanfu, reducing corvee and requisitions, and lightening taxes.
- Deng accepts enough of the critique to summon Pang Can, appoint him yezhe, send him to supervise troops in Sanfu, recall Deng Zhi, and leave Ren Shang stationed at Hanyang.
- Deng Zhi's later grand ceremony and General-in-Chief appointment show that Deng-family proximity can survive battlefield failure when the regent still trusts the kin network.
- Dianling proclaims himself emperor in Beidi, draws in Wudu Canlang plus Shangjun and Xihe Qiang groups, blocks the Long road, attacks Sanfu, and helps spread pressure into Yizhou and Hanzhong.
- The 109 CE Luoyang famine and cannibalism notice makes fiscal emergency concrete before the Three Excellencies propose selling ranks and court-adjacent identities for money or grain.
- The host reads rank and office sale plus salary reduction as a short-term fiscal patch with future damage: status and office channels become monetized when ordinary revenue is exhausted.

## Key Quotes

> "邓太后执政期间天灾和战争不断" - the episode's framing of Deng's early regency field.

> "千里运粮" - Pang Can's policy problem: western campaigning consumes the state through transport before victory arrives.

> "这是东汉王朝第一次开始买官" - the host's interpretation of the 109 CE rank and identity sale.

> "一利一弊" - the closing judgment on austerity, salary reduction, and emergency sale.
