# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》722丨让刘秀险死的头痛病，怎么回事？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianwu 20-21 / 44-45 CE before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-723-bi-kongjia-gengniu-de-jiazu-pingsha-shi-peishi-lqti3stvdcw7utgm6uzwv5giazhj|Hanji 723]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-724-xiongnu-neiluan-donghan-weishenme-bu-chenji-yao-ta-ming-lpqksa0j1y0tskwv6fpflhovg9b8|Hanji 724]]. It begins with [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]]'s severe headache and dizziness, his emergency entrusting of affairs to [[YinXingGuangwuMaternalKin|阴兴]], and Yin Xing's later refusal of the great marshalship, then moves through Zhang Zhan's withdrawal, Guo-family compensation, [[MaYuanEasternHan|马援]]'s return from Jiaozhi, northern frontier pressure, and Western Regions requests under Shache pressure.

The durable synthesis is that late Guangwu governance was already capacity-constrained before the later Xiongnu split. Liu Xiu can trust restrained outer relatives and compensate the displaced Guo branch, but his court still faces illness risk, northern raids by [[Xiongnu|匈奴]], [[Wuhuan|乌桓]], and [[Xianbei|鲜卑]], and a Western Regions request that he answers with a narrow hostage-retention signal rather than a full protectorate commitment.

## Key Claims

- In 44 CE, Liu Xiu suffers severe headache and dizziness, thinks he may die, and summons Yin Xing to entrust affairs in the Yuntai Guangshi.
- After recovery, Liu Xiu wants Yin Xing to replace the dead Wu Han as great marshal, but Yin Xing tearfully refuses to protect the emperor's reputation and avoid outer-relative overreach.
- Zhang Zhan withdraws from court after Empress Guo's deposition, and Liu Xiu's attempts to recall him fail; the court then appoints Cai Mao, Zhu Fu, and Liu Long to senior posts.
- Liu Xiu changes Liu Fu from Zhongshan king to Pei king and compensates the Guo family through Guo Kuang's high office, visits, and large gifts.
- Ma Yuan returns from Jiaozhi, asks to fight the northern raiders, receives ritual sendoff, and states the martial ideal later remembered as dying on the frontier wrapped in horsehide.
- Ma Yuan warns Liang Song and Dou Gu that wealth and rank can fall back into poverty and low status, so high-position people must discipline speech and conduct.
- Liu Shang's southwestern campaign succeeds against Dong Chan, while Wuhuan, Xiongnu, and Xianbei pressure keeps the northern border damaged and thinly populated.
- Zhan Rong's Liaodong victory over Xianbei shows local frontier command can deter raids, but the wider northern situation remains unsettled.
- Shache King Xian's pressure on Western Regions states prompts eighteen small polities, including Shanshan, Yanqi, and the Cheshi front kingdom, to seek Han backing through hostages and tribute.
- Liu Xiu refuses a full Western Regions protectorate but allows the hostages to stay after Pei Zun reports that the small states need this as a deterrent signal toward Shache.

## Key Quotes

> "男儿当战死边野，以马革裹尸还葬" - Ma Yuan's martial ideal as rendered in the episode.

> "富贵者也可以复归贫贱" - Ma Yuan's warning to Liang Song and Dou Gu.

> "有心无力" - the later Hanji 723 frame anticipated by Liu Xiu's refusal to take on a full Western Regions commitment here.
