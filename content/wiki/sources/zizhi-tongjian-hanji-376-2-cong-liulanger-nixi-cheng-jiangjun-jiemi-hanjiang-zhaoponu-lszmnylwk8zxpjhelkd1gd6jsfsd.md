# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》376-2｜从流浪儿逆袭成将军，揭秘汉将赵破奴

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the immediate [[SuWuWesternHan|苏武]] coercion sequence begun in Hanji 376-1: [[WeiLuWesternHan|卫律]] fails to induce surrender, Xiongnu imprisons Su Wu without food, then sends him to the North Sea with an impossible release condition while he preserves Han-facing ritual loyalty under deprivation.

The second half centers on [[ZhaoPonuWesternHan|赵破奴]], a former wanderer in Xiongnu territory who rises in [[HuoQubing|霍去病]]'s military era, campaigns in the Western Regions, is captured after leaving his army at night during a 103 BCE expedition, escapes back to Han with his son, and is later exterminated in the [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]]. The durable synthesis is not a simple heroic ascent: the episode uses Zhao Ponu's victories, defeat, captivity, return, and undocumented final implication as a compressed image of the expansion and political catastrophe of [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s reign.

## Key Claims

- After Wei Lü's threats fail, the Xiongnu chanyu avoids killing Su Wu, confines him without food, and later sends him far north while falsely reporting his death.
- Su Wu survives snowbound deprivation, is assigned rams under an impossible release condition, and continues facing south with his Han envoy staff, while [[ChangHuiWesternHan|常惠]] and other non-surrendering mission members are dispersed.
- In 103 BCE, Zhao Ponu leads twenty thousand cavalry against the Xiongnu Left Wise King but is captured after leaving the army at night to seek water.
- Xiongnu keeps Zhao Ponu alive in the hope of winning over a senior Han commander familiar with steppe society from his youth.
- Zhao Ponu later escapes with his son Zhao Anguo and returns to Han, making this the episode's second captivity-and-return loyalty case.
- The episode places Zhao Ponu's earlier rise in Huo Qubing's victorious era, his Western Regions service and Loulan victory, and his later defeat inside the changing military fortunes of Han Wudi's reign.
- Zhao Ponu is eventually exterminated after implication in the Wugu affair, but the source explicitly says the record does not reveal why he was implicated or which political position he took.
- The host's suggestion that Zhao Ponu may have supported Crown Prince Liu Ju because of his Huo Qubing connection is an inference, not a settled fact.

## Key Quotes

> "羝乳乃得归。" - the impossible condition attached to Su Wu's release in the episode's telling.

> "后坐巫蛊族。" - the terse record used to summarize Zhao Ponu's final destruction.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with existing wiki content was found; the Su Wu material fills the interval between Hanji 376-1's failed coercion and Hanji 398's later persuasion-and-return branch.
- Scope caution: the episode gives 103 BCE, troop strength, the night search for water, capture mechanics, Loulan force size, and escape details in compressed podcast form; these remain source-scoped pending comparison with primary texts.
- The claim that Xiongnu generally spared Han generals in order to recruit them is broader than the two cases narrated here and is not adopted as a universal rule.
- Zhao Ponu's alleged support for Crown Prince Liu Ju is explicitly speculative. The source says his precise role in the Wugu affair is unknown, so the wiki preserves uncertainty rather than converting kinship-by-association into guilt or allegiance.
