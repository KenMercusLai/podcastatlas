# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1140丨自我认知缺失，如何毁掉一代枭雄马超？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[MaChao|马超]] after the [[WeinanBattleLateHan|渭南之战]] through refuge under [[ZhangLuFivePecks|张鲁]], a failed attempt to recover Liangzhou by attacking Qishan, and eventual defection to [[LiuBei|刘备]]. It interprets the sequence through [[CapabilityBoundarySelfKnowledge|能力边界自知]]: personal bravery, military reputation, and tactical performance do not by themselves establish political judgment, coalition management, or the capacity to sustain independent rule.

The episode contrasts Ma Chao with [[CaoCao|曹操]] through [[TacticalStrategicCapabilityGap|战术—战略能力落差]]. Ma Chao is framed as concentrating on immediate combat and personal military value, while Cao Cao combines force movement, legitimacy, alliance fracture, logistics, and political consequence. The durable addition is the post-Weinan career sequence and its trust effects; the source's claims about inner motive, arrogance, and moral responsibility remain interpretive rather than settled biography.

## Key Claims

- After defeat in the Guanzhong campaigns, Ma Chao takes refuge with Zhang Lu in [[Hanzhong|汉中]], where his prestige makes him useful but his loyalty and treatment of family make him difficult to trust fully.
- Zhang Lu reportedly considers marrying a daughter to Ma Chao, but an officer identified as Yang Bai objects on character and filial grounds; the source reads the failed marriage as evidence that Ma Chao can be used without entering the ruling core.
- Ma Chao asks Zhang Lu for troops to recover Liangzhou and attacks Qishan, leaving [[PangDe|庞德]] and family members behind in Hanzhong.
- When the Qishan defenders seek help, [[XiahouYuan|夏侯渊]] reportedly acts without waiting for distant approval and sends [[ZhangHeLateHan|张郃]] with five thousand troops; Ma Chao withdraws quickly and abandons siege equipment.
- The defeat removes much of Ma Chao's remaining value as an independent military entrepreneur and increases the danger that Zhang Lu could trade him to Cao Cao.
- Ma Chao then leaves Hanzhong for Liu Bei without recovering all family members; the episode says Zhang Lu later kills Ma Chao's son and that Liu Bei does not entrust Ma Chao with independently raised forces.
- The source presents Ma Chao's career as a cumulative failure of self-assessment, consequence acceptance, credibility, alliance management, and strategic judgment rather than as the result of one lost battle.
- Long accumulation can remain valuable before visible success, but the episode's opening life lesson is distinct from the historical claims about Ma Chao.
