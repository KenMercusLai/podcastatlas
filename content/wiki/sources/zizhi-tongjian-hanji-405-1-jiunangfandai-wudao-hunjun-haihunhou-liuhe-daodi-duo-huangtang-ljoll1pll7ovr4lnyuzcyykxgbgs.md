# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》405-1｜酒囊饭袋？无道昏君？海昏侯刘贺到底多荒唐？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the political and character setup immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-406-tianshang-zaxia-ge-huangwei-huangtang-de-liuhe-za-jiuchengle-huangdi-lgs9uy4ohcd50v9qeirvsdo2x3ad|Hanji 406]]. It explains why [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]] avoids the harder-to-control Guangling king [[LiuXuGuanglingKingWesternHan|刘须]] and lets [[LiuHeHaihunHou|刘贺]] become the succession candidate after Han Zhaodi's death.

The durable synthesis is that Liu He's accession failure is prefigured before the succession summons. [[WangJiWesternHan|王吉]] and [[GongSuiWesternHan|龚遂]] repeatedly try to redirect him toward classics study, restraint, principled companions, and distance from flattering attendants, but Liu He briefly accepts correction and then returns to hunting, drinking, and close association with servants and cooks.

## Key Claims

- Huo Guang's selection problem is framed as regent self-preservation: he cannot easily choose Liu Xu, a stronger and less controllable royal kinsman, so Liu He becomes politically convenient.
- Before accession, Liu He is already described as a reckless Changyi king who prefers hunting, entertainment, speed, and indulgence to classics learning and ritual restraint.
- Wang Ji's remonstrance joins moral education, health discipline, and political prudence: Liu He should study under good teachers, learn earlier ruler models, and avoid behavior that offends imperial expectations.
- Liu He can recognize a warning momentarily and reward the adviser, but the episode stresses that he does not sustain self-correction.
- Gong Sui escalates remonstrance by blaming the surrounding officials, crying before Liu He, and treating association with drivers, cooks, and sycophants as a survival-level danger.
- Gong Sui uses the Jiaoxi king Liu Duan as a cautionary example and tries to install classics-literate, upright companions around Liu He, but they are soon expelled.
- The strange dog omen is preserved as the episode's moral-political warning that Liu He's crowned attendants behave like dogs and must be removed if he is to survive.

## Key Quotes

> "长君少君，强者为王" - the host's opening frame for succession as power competition.

> "左右皆冠狗也" - Gong Sui's interpretation of the strange dog as a warning about Liu He's inner circle.
