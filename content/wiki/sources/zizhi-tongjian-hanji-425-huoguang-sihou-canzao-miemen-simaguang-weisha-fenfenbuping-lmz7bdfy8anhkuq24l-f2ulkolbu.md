# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》425｜霍光死后惨遭灭门，司马光为啥忿忿不平？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the early [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] aftermath of [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]]'s death. It presents [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s judgment as double-sided: the Huo family brought danger on itself through arrogance, office concentration, and conspiracy, but Xuan also let the family remain in military and political power too long before punishing it with total extinction.

The second half turns to [[GongSuiWesternHan|龚遂]]'s Bohai commandery governance. Gong Sui reframes famine-era banditry as a livelihood and official-care failure, wins room from Xuan to govern without rushed interference, stops treating tool-bearing peasants as bandits, opens granaries, and restores agricultural production.

## Key Claims

- Sima Guang praises Huo Guang's loyalty to Han but criticizes his failure to return power once Xuan was old and capable enough to rule personally.
- Huo Guang's long authority and private network left the ruler angry and officials and commoners resentful, so his descendants inherited an unsafe power position.
- Sima Guang also faults Xuan for not settling the Huo descendants with titles and stipends while removing them from command authority; instead, Xuan allowed fear and resentment to accumulate until conspiracy and extermination followed.
- The episode treats the total killing of Huo Guang's descendants as excessively harsh because Huo Guang's loyal service should not have had its sacrificial line completely cut off.
- Xuan's later administrative notices include lowering salt prices and requiring local reporting on punished or starved prisoners so central officials could grade local administrators.
- Gong Sui's Bohai strategy treats "bandits" as hungry commoners first: tools mark them as ordinary people, weapons mark them as robbers.
- Gong Sui's relief-and-production program combines granary release, thrift, official example, household-level farming and livestock targets, inspection, and persuasion until households regain stores and cases decline.
- The episode closes with a Western Regions notice: [[XieyouPrincessWesternHan|解忧公主]]'s daughter marries the [[QiuciKingdom|龟兹]] king Jiangbin, who asks to come with her to Chang'an.

## Key Quotes

> "威福，人主之器也。" - Sima Guang's ruler-authority principle as the episode presents it.

> "是欲臣胜之邪，将安之也？" - Gong Sui's question distinguishing suppression from pacification.

> "持田器者皆为良民，吏无得问；持兵者乃为盗贼。" - the rule separating peasants from armed robbers.
