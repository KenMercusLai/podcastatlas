# 《资治通鉴·周纪》66丨中国历史上哪些帝王是被活活饿死的（2）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the [[ShaqiuCrisis|沙丘之变]] branch that [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-66-zhongguo-lishishang-naxie-diwang-shi-bei-huohuo-e-si-de-1-lvl1uxjadiedqwad-skxrwf-usnq|Zhouji 66 part 1]] left at Shaqiu. [[ZhaoZhang|赵章]] uses a forged summons in [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王 / 赵主父]]'s name, [[FeiYi|肥义]] goes first to protect [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] and is killed, and Zhao He's side suppresses the coup through [[GaoXinWarringStates|高信]], [[ZhaoChengWarringStates|赵成]], and [[LiDui|李兑]]. The source's core contribution is the starvation ending: Zhao Cheng and Li Dui kill Zhao Zhang, then avoid direct regicide by trapping Zhao Wuling Wang in the palace until he dies, making the episode a case of [[ResponsibilityAvoidantKilling|避责式杀害]] inside [[PartialAbdicationPowerSplit|退位留权式权力分裂]].

## Key Claims
- Zhao Zhang falsely claims Zhao Wuling Wang has summoned Zhao He, turning the ambiguous authority of the 主父 arrangement into an operational coup device.
- Fei Yi anticipates the trap, insists on going first, discovers Zhao Wuling Wang is not present, and is killed by Zhao Zhang's side.
- Gao Xin and Zhao He resist Zhao Zhang's attack while a messenger returns to Handan for support.
- Zhao Cheng and Li Dui arrive from Handan, use troops to defeat Zhao Zhang's forces, and drive Zhao Zhang back into Zhao Wuling Wang's quarters.
- Zhao Cheng stops Li Dui from asking Zhao He how to handle Zhao Zhang and Zhao Wuling Wang, because any explicit order would force the young king into an impossible brother-and-father decision.
- Zhao Cheng and Li Dui kill Zhao Zhang, then decide they cannot safely withdraw after surrounding Zhao Wuling Wang's palace.
- The two men do not directly kill Zhao Wuling Wang; instead, they empty the palace, block him from leaving, deny food, and leave him to survive on birds and eggs until he dies after about three months.
- The episode reads Zhao He's silence during the three-month siege as politically meaningful: the court has become Zhao He's court, and no one intervenes for the former ruler.
- Zhao Cheng becomes chancellor under the title Anping Jun, Li Dui becomes sikou, and Zhao Huiwen Wang's era begins only after Zhao Wuling Wang's death is announced.
- The host praises Zhao Wuling Wang as an extraordinary reforming ruler but treats his voluntary abdication and attempted retained power as a major political mistake.

## Key Quotes
> "一山不容二虎，一国没有二主" - the host's summary of Zhao Wuling Wang's failed authority design.

> "大权一旦旁落，再想重新捡起来，比登天还难" - the lesson the episode draws from Zhao Wuling Wang's attempted power recovery.
