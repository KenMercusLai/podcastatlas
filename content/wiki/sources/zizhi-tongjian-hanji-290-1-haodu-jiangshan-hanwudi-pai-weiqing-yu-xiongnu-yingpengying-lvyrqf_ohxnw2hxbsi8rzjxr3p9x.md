# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》290-1｜豪赌江山，汉武帝派卫青与匈奴硬碰硬

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens a reported 124 BCE Han campaign against the [[Xiongnu|匈奴]]. After repeated raids and the consolidation of Shuofang defenses, [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] assigns [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]] the main force against the Right Wise King while a separate eastern force is meant to contain the Left Wise King.

The supplied text ends before battle or reward. Its durable contribution is therefore the campaign setup: centralized command, concentration against one target, and [[MainEffortFlankContainment|主攻与侧翼牵制]] intended to reduce reinforcement risk. It does not establish the victory teased by the title, Wei Qing's promotion to grand general, or the campaign's final strategic effect.

## Key Claims

- The episode frames “以直报怨” as an interpretive bridge from Confucian political language to Han Wudi's choice to answer repeated frontier attacks with prepared military force rather than indefinite accommodation.
- It reports Xiongnu incursions into Dai, Yanmen, and Shuofang from roughly 126 to 124 BCE, with deaths, captures, and reconnaissance pressure on the new Shuofang position.
- Han Wudi is said to mobilize more than one hundred thousand troops for a two-axis operation centered on the Right Wise King's force.
- Wei Qing reportedly leads thirty thousand elite cavalry from Gaoque, while Su Jian, Li Ju, Gongsun He, and Li Cai operate under his unified command from the Shuofang direction.
- Li Xi and Zhang Cigong reportedly leave Right Beiping to contain the Left Wise King and reduce the chance of westward reinforcement.
- The episode recalls Wei Qing's earlier defeat of the Loufan and Baiyang kings as the basis for psychological pressure on the Right Wise King's side.
- After scouts warn of the Han advance, the episode says the Right Wise King retreats north; Wei Qing reaches an empty camp, rests and resupplies, and begins planning a deeper pursuit.

## Key Quotes

> “以直报怨，以德报德。” - the ethical frame used to introduce the move from endurance and defense to prepared counterattack.

> “卫青并没有因此而放弃进攻。” - the episode's transition from an empty enemy camp to renewed pursuit planning.

- [[GongsunHeWesternHan|公孙贺]], [[SuJianWesternHan|苏建]], [[LiJuWesternHan|李沮]], and [[LiCaiWesternHan|李蔡]] - commanders placed under Wei Qing in the episode's main-force deployment.
- [[MainEffortFlankContainment|主攻与侧翼牵制]] - operational distinction between attacking the selected target and limiting reinforcement from another sector.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. This is a separate 124 BCE Gaoque-Shuofang operation and must not be merged with the 123 BCE [[FirstDingxiangCampaignWesternHan|first Dingxiang sortie]].
- The supplied text stops during pursuit planning. It does not narrate the final battle, captures, casualties, Wei Qing's promotion, or why the complete campaign should be judged a turning point.
- Dates, raid totals, total mobilization, the thirty-thousand cavalry figure, routes, titles, command relationships, retreat sequence, scout avoidance, and the private reasoning of either side remain episode-attributed pending primary-text and specialist comparison.
- The claim that Han Wudi's response operationalized Confucian “以直报怨,” and the portrayal of the Loufan, Baiyang, and Right Wise Kings' fear, are interpretive narrative frames rather than independently established motives.
- “李息” is left unlinked because the existing [[LiXiWesternHan|李息]] page does not yet merge its court-warning subject with this briefly mentioned military commander; the episode alone does not resolve that identity question.
