# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》860丨惊为天人！东汉郭泰有多神？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 164 CE [[GuoTaiLateHan|郭泰]] reputation and character-reading layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-861-shenme-yang-de-ren-kancheng-laoshi-kaimo-lu0kadsc9aetzip9cedyv01wndrh|Hanji 861]]. It opens with a self-cultivation frame, then uses [[HuangQiongLateHan|黄琼]]'s funeral, [[XuZhiLateHan|徐稚]]'s selective speech, [[FuRongLateHan|符融]]'s introduction, [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]]'s praise, and [[MaoRongLateHan|茅容]]'s filial household conduct to show how Guo Tai's authority emerges from both public endorsement and fine-grained people judgment.

The durable contribution is a prequel to the wiki's Guo Tai cluster: before Hanji 861's teacher-model stories, Hanji 860 shows Guo Tai interpreting behavior that others misread, becoming famous through an elite endorsement chain, and turning Mao Rong's ordinary etiquette and filial allocation into evidence of future moral capacity. No settled contradiction with existing pages was found; the source mainly deepens [[MoralMentorshipTalentCultivation|人师型人才成全]], [[SituationalCharacterTests|五观识人法]], and [[EliteEndorsementReputationMaking|名士背书式造势]].

## Key Claims

- In 164 CE, [[HuangQiongLateHan|黄琼]] dies, and thousands of well-known figures attend the funeral.
- [[XuZhiLateHan|徐稚]] had been close to Huang Qiong before Huang's rise, then stopped visiting once Huang became powerful; at the funeral he mourns deeply but leaves without identifying himself.
- When [[MaoRongLateHan|茅容]] catches up with Xu Zhi, Xu Zhi refuses to discuss court affairs but speaks freely about farming and harvests.
- Guo Tai rejects the crowd's surface reading of Xu Zhi as rude, arguing that Xu Zhi's silence reflects selective speech and judgment of audience rather than incompetence.
- [[FuRongLateHan|符融]] introduces Guo Tai to [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]], and Li Ying's strong praise helps make Guo Tai famous in [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]].
- The sendoff scene at the Yellow River turns Li Ying and Guo Tai's association into public spectacle, with officials and scholars watching the two men depart as if seeing immortals.
- Guo Tai's main ability is framed as discerning people: he sees virtue in Mao Rong's upright conduct while others shelter casually from rain.
- Mao Rong's household meal clarifies that his best food is reserved for his mother rather than the guest, leading Guo Tai to bow to him and urge study.
- The opening and closing frame the historical material as self-cultivation through ordinary roles, humility, and ambition rather than as office-holding alone.
