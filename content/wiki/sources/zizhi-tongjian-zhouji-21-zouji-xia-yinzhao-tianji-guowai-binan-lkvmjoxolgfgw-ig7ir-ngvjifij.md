# 《资治通鉴·周纪》21丨邹忌下阴招 田忌国外避难

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows the court aftermath of [[MalingBattle|马陵之战]]. [[TianJi|田忌]]'s repeated victories raise his prestige above [[ZouJi|成侯邹忌]], so Zou Ji plants a market-divination story implying Tian Ji wants to rebel. The trap is crude but effective because a treason accusation destabilizes trust between [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] and Tian Ji; [[SunBin|孙膑]] proposes a high-risk counterstroke, but Tian Ji flees to [[ChuState|楚国]] and Sun Bin leaves Qi with him.

## Key Claims
- Zou Ji had previously tried to use war against [[WeyState|卫国]] to contain Tian Ji, but Tian Ji's victories increased Tian Ji's own prestige rather than Zou Ji's control.
- After the rescue-Han campaign and Maling victory, Tian Ji's battlefield reputation threatens Zou Ji's relative standing inside the [[QiState|齐国]] court.
- The episode frames the rivalry through a general organization-politics choice: reconcile with a rising rival or escalate into a zero-sum fight.
- Zou Ji chooses escalation and sends someone with money to a market diviner while pretending to be Tian Ji's agent.
- The planted agent says Tian Ji has won three battles and wants to "do great things," turning ambiguous ambition language into possible rebellion.
- The episode compares source wording: [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] has "欲行事," while [[Shiji|《史记》]] and [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] have "欲为大事."
- The host argues that the story has obvious practical flaws because a great general plotting rebellion would not normally outsource divination to a random market stall.
- The accusation can still work because rebellion suspicion is politically radioactive: a ruler may feel forced to investigate, and the accused may read investigation itself as distrust.
- The episode says arrested participants confess before Qi Wei Wang, and the news reaches Tian Ji while he is still returning in victory.
- The source notes that Zizhi Tongjian omits some Zhanguo Ce confrontation material before Qi Wei Wang, so the wiki keeps the episode's source-variant warning visible.
- Sun Bin asks whether Tian Ji can "do great things" in the opposite sense and proposes rushing Yongmen before it closes, avoiding a formal siege.
- Tian Ji rejects Sun Bin's proposal, flees from Qi to Chu, and Sun Bin leaves with him because he belongs to Tian Ji's side of the court conflict.
- The episode closes by saying Zou Ji wins the immediate court fight but cannot feel secure because displaced talent, especially a victorious general, can still matter in the interstate talent market.

## Key Quotes
> "欲行事" - the Zizhi Tongjian wording the episode contrasts with other sources.

> "欲为大事" - the Shiji and Zhanguo Ce wording used to explain treason-coded ambition.

> "雍门未闭也" - the opening Sun Bin wants Tian Ji to exploit before the city is sealed.
