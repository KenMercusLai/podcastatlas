# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》387-2｜汉武帝一生难抹的污点：太子刘据之死！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] short episode backfills the death scene of [[LiuJuCrownPrinceWesternHan|卫太子刘据]] in 91 BCE before the later remorse account in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-388-2-yin-chanyan-cuosha-taizi-hanwudi-wannian-zuidade-huihen-lm9e_lrbbcc7a6kf22hbid04_u73|Hanji 388-2]]. It says local officials surround Liu Ju, he refuses capture and hangs himself, Zhang Fuchang breaks open the door, Xin'an magistrate Li Shou lowers the body, the householder and two imperial grandsons are killed, and [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] later rewards participants while remaining emotionally shaken.

The episode also adds [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s educational critique. Han Wudi had built Bowang Yuan and let the crown prince gather guests according to his own taste; Sima Guang treats that permissiveness as a failure to surround the heir with upright teachers and friends. The closing notes connect the destroyed Liu Ju household to the survival of the infant [[EmperorXuanOfHan|刘病已 / 后来的汉宣帝]] under [[BingJiWesternHan|丙吉]], setting up the later prison-survival arc.

## Key Claims

- In 91 BCE, Liu Ju's flight ends when local officials surround him and he chooses suicide rather than capture.
- Zhang Fuchang and Li Shou appear as named arrest-scene actors; the householder is killed, and Liu Ju's two fleeing sons also die.
- Han Wudi's rewards to Li Shou and Zhang Fuchang sit beside grief, showing a politically unresolved response before the fuller remorse branch.
- Sima Guang criticizes Han Wudi's Bowang Yuan arrangement because crown-prince companionship should be curated around righteous teachers and friends rather than private preference.
- The episode links Liu Ju's destroyed household to Liu Bingyi's survival: Liu Ju's consort, son Liu Jin, daughter-in-law Wang Wengxu, daughters, granddaughters, and two grandsons are killed, while Liu Bingyi is secretly protected by Bing Ji.
- Liu Bingyi's survival is presented as a foreshadowing of the future Emperor Xuan and the later question of how a prison child becomes emperor.
- The episode is a compact bridge between the Wugu death scene and later episodes on Linghu Mao, Han Wudi's recognition of slander, and the political aftermath.

## Key Quotes

> "近朱者赤，近墨者黑" - the episode's compact reading of Sima Guang's warning about the crown prince's companions.

> "刘病已后来就是西汉第十位皇帝" - the closing survival hook for the future Emperor Xuan.
