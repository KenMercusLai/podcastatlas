# 《资治通鉴·周纪》97-3丨吕不韦：历史上风投第一人

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode bridges the [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] and [[LuZhonglian|鲁仲连]] aftermath into the first developed appearance of [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]]. It argues that Warring States "士" status is shifting from inherited rank toward [[MoralizedShiIdentity|moralized conduct]], then contrasts that honor world with merchants' profit-seeking reputation through [[MerchantAristocraticValueConflict|aristocratic-commercial value conflict]].

Against that background, [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]] sees [[QinZhuangxiangwang|异人 / 子楚]] in [[Handan|邯郸]] as a political asset whose current value is low but whose upside is enormous if Qin succession can be rerouted through [[HuayangFuren|华阳夫人]]. The episode frames this as [[PoliticalVentureInvestment|political venture investment]]: money, access, family placement, and personal sacrifice are used to turn a neglected hostage into a future ruler.

## Key Claims
- [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]]'s response to [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] reframes "士" away from noble pedigree and toward whether a person deserves respect by conduct and character.
- [[LuZhonglian|鲁仲连]]'s refusal of Pingyuanjun's fief and gold reinforces an aristocratic ideal in which solving another person's crisis without taking payment is morally superior to transactional gain.
- The episode treats noble disdain for merchants as a values conflict, not as proof that merchants necessarily act immorally.
- [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]] is introduced as the figure who pushes commercial calculation into politics: he treats dynastic succession as an investable field.
- [[QinZhuangxiangwang|异人]]'s low status is central to the opportunity: he is a Qin royal grandson, but he is neglected in Qin, disliked in Zhao, and materially poor as a hostage.
- The strategic opening depends on [[QinXiaowenwang|安国君柱]] having many sons while his favored wife [[HuayangFuren|华阳夫人]] has none.
- [[MaternalStatusSuccessionLeverage|子以母贵式继承杠杆]] is the mechanism Lu Buwei identifies: if Huayang Furen adopts Yiren, a politically marginal son can gain嫡子-like succession value.
- Lu Buwei's pitch to Yiren is not charity; he explains that his own household can become great only if Yiren's household becomes great.
- The episode uses Lu Buwei's gift of his beloved woman to Yiren as the extreme cost side of the investment, ending with the birth of [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]].

## Key Quotes
> "奇货可居" - idiom used for Lu Buwei's reading of Yiren as an unusual political asset.

> "朋友妻不可亲" - line the episode uses when narrating Lu Buwei's staged anger before giving his beloved woman to Yiren.

> "君子喻于义，小人喻于利" - value frame used to explain why merchant calculation looked ugly from the aristocratic moral angle.
