# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》186｜一场因巴结领导老婆引发的灭门惨案

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens the eve of [[YingBu|英布 / 黥布]]'s rebellion after the Hanji 183-184 sequence has already made [[HanXin|韩信]] and [[PengYue|彭越]] warning cases for early [[WesternHanDynasty|西汉]] merit-holders. [[PengYue|彭越]]'s execution and corpse-paste distribution push Ying Bu into secret military preparation, but the immediate trigger comes from [[BenHe|奔鹤]], a [[HuainanKingdomEarlyHan|淮南国]] official who tries to gain favor through Ying Bu's favorite concubine, is suspected of sexual misconduct, flees to [[LiuBang|刘邦]], and accuses Ying Bu of rebellion. The episode's main contribution is [[InnerCourtAccessBrokerageRisk|内廷通道攀附风险]]: a private access gambit becomes an informant event, a Han investigation, and finally a vassal's judgment that revolt is the only survivable path.

## Key Claims
- Ying Bu's fear is structural before Ben He appears: [[HanXin|韩信]] and [[PengYue|彭越]] have already shown that a surviving different-surname king may read demotion, accusation, or summons as a path toward death.
- [[PengYue|彭越]]'s execution affects Ying Bu directly because the Han court sends the corpse-paste to vassal kings; Ying Bu stops hunting, returns to the palace, and secretly prepares troops.
- [[HuainanKingdomEarlyHan|淮南国]] is treated as a semi-autonomous royal polity with its own troops and officials appointed by Ying Bu, not as an ordinary commandery.
- [[BenHe|奔鹤]] is a middle official whose attempt to enter the ruler's attention through a doctor and a favorite concubine crosses a dangerous boundary between outer officials and inner quarters.
- Ying Bu interprets Ben He's gifts, banquets, and praised reputation through sexual suspicion, which makes a private patronage maneuver politically lethal.
- Ben He's flight and accusation at [[ChanglePalace|长乐宫]] turn self-preservation into a formal rebellion report against his own ruler.
- [[XiaoHe|萧何]] initially advises caution: detain Ben He and send investigators to Huainan rather than immediately arresting Ying Bu.
- The secret investigation still functions as a trigger because Ying Bu reads the arrival of Han investigators after Peng Yue's death as proof that disaster is unavoidable.
- [[PingyuanjunZhuJian|平原君诸建]] counsels against rebellion, but Ying Bu accepts [[LiangfuHou|梁富侯]]'s harder view that revolt is the only remaining path.
- The episode extends [[InvestigationDrivenRebellionEscalation|调查推动式反叛升级]] from [[ChenXi|陈豨]] to Ying Bu: inquiry may be reasonable from the center's view while still convincing the target that compliance has become fatal.
- The episode stops at Ying Bu's decision to raise troops, so it does not yet cover the campaign or defeat promised by the introduction.

## Key Quotes
> "秘密整军备战" - the episode's description of Ying Bu's reaction after Peng Yue's death.

> "告发英布谋反" - Ben He's move after fleeing from Huainan to Chang'an.

> "祸患已经不可避免" - Ying Bu's interpretation once Han investigators arrive.
