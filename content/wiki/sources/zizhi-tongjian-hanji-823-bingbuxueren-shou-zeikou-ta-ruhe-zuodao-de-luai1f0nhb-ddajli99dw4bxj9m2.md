# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》823丨兵不血刃收贼寇，他如何做到的？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 142 CE [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]] layer immediately after [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-822-chailang-dangdao-ta-jiang-maotou-zhizhi-huangdi-yuefu-lqhpmayvd3p07byr-zoiv-jyhdv|Hanji 822]] and before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-824-weishenme-women-yao-xue-shouzhuo-lpssy8ltykugdpvmgrzwcpf6-9v9|Hanji 824]]. It completes [[ZhangGangLateHan|张纲]]'s Guangling story: after [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] sends him to [[GuanglingCommandery|广陵郡]], Zhang Gang goes alone to [[ZhangYingGuanglingLateHan|张婴]]'s camp, acknowledges official abuse as the grievance behind rebellion, and pairs promised reintegration with a clear warning about imperial force.

The durable contribution is that pacification is shown as a settlement sequence rather than a speech trick. Zhang Gang's success depends on trusted presence, naming the rebels' grievance, making surrender materially livable through housing, land, and office paths, and keeping coercion as the alternative; the same episode then contrasts [[RenJunLuoyangLateHan|任峻]], [[SuZhangLateHan|苏章]], and [[WuYouLateHan|吴祐]] as different late-Han local-administration styles.

## Key Claims

- Liang Ji uses Zhang Gang's Guangling appointment as a dangerous retaliation channel after Zhang Gang's direct impeachment of Liang Ji and Liang Buyi.
- Zhang Gang refuses the normal request for additional troops and enters Guangling with a single carriage, signaling that he means to negotiate before escalating force.
- Zhang Gang persuades Zhang Ying by first recognizing that predatory two-thousand-shi officials caused legitimate anger, then saying rebellion against the court remains outside the proper order.
- The surrender offer combines status, pardon, and future office opportunity with a credible threat that Jing, Yang, Yan, and Yu troops could be mobilized if Zhang Ying refuses.
- Zhang Ying surrenders with more than ten thousand followers and families, and Zhang Gang stabilizes the outcome by dispersing followers, choosing housing and farmland, and drawing Zhang Ying's descendants into local office.
- Zhang Gang's blocked reward and mourned death show both the limits of Liang Ji's court environment and the depth of local gratitude for successful settlement.
- Ren Jun, Su Zhang, and Wu You provide three comparative official types: forceful staffing and detection, public-law impartiality over private friendship, and humane/simple adjudication that reads remorse through filial motive.
- The closing speech-restraint lesson turns Zhang Gang's success into a communication claim: harsh truth, ridicule, and argumentative victory can worsen conflict when the goal is practical conversion.

## Key Quotes

> "若鱼游釜中" - Zhang Ying's image for living under doomed rebellion before surrender.

> "今夕苏孺文与故人饮者，私恩也；明日冀州刺史案事者，公法也." - Su Zhang's distinction between private friendship and public law.

> "观过斯知仁矣" - Wu You's reading of Sun Xing's offense through filial intention and repentance.
