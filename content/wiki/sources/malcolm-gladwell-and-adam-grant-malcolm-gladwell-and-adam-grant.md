# Malcolm Gladwell and Adam Grant

## Summary

In this [[RevisionistHistory]] conversation, [[MalcolmGladwell]] and [[AdamGrant]] use [[TheBomberMafiaBook|The Bomber Mafia]] and [[ThinkAgainBook|Think Again]] to examine moral judgment under severe constraint, the gap between technological vision and real-world execution, intrinsically rewarding forms of obsession, and the discipline of revising beliefs. Their strongest shared conclusion is that durable principles need not require rigid plans: people can take context and difficulty seriously without excusing harm, and can remain committed to values while testing strategies as hypotheses.

## Key Claims

- [[ContextualMoralJudgment]] evaluates outcomes alongside constraints, alternatives, and execution difficulty without turning context into automatic exoneration.
- The Bomber Mafia's precision-bombing vision joined technological ambition to a humane aim, but weather, accuracy, operations, and war conditions exposed an [[InnovationVisionExecutionGap]].
- [[HaywoodHansell]] retained the precision-bombing ideal while [[CurtisLeMay]] adopted incendiary urban bombing; the episode presents their choice set as morally grave and historically constrained rather than cleanly heroic or villainous.
- Attempt difficulty and execution quality are distinct: attempting an ambitious project does not establish that it was implemented competently or responsibly.
- [[HarmoniousPassion]] distinguishes intrinsically rewarding, socially shared mastery from externally pressured compulsion, while restraint, recovery, pleasure, and consequences remain important tests.
- [[ScientistModeThinking]] replaces preaching, prosecuting, and politicking with hypotheses, observation, disconfirming conversation, humility, curiosity, and revision.
- [[PrincipleStrategySeparation]] preserves causes and core values while treating the plans used to advance them as revisable.
- The BlackBerry exchange uses [[MikeLazaridis]] to show that personal achievement, employee consequences, lost products, and regional spillovers can support different evaluations of the same refusal to rethink.

## Contradictions

- No settled factual contradiction was adopted. Grant's concern about civilian suffering qualifies Gladwell's contextual sympathy: constrained choices and difficult execution can explain conduct without resolving its moral permissibility.
- The episode's treatment of precision bombing, firebombing, atomic bombing, casualties, and counterfactual war duration is interpretive and incomplete; the supplied summary does not provide the archival or historical record needed to adjudicate those claims.
- Gladwell and Grant initially differ over whether obsession is inherently unhealthy, then narrow the disagreement by distinguishing pressured compulsion from pleasurable, restrained, socially shared mastery.
- The BlackBerry discussion yields competing units of evaluation rather than a contradiction: Lazaridis's personal success, employee losses, discontinued products, and the later southern Ontario technology ecosystem do not answer the same question.
