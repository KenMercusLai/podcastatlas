# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》483-2丨历史揭秘：混职场的“江湖规矩”

## Summary
This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode broadens Jianghu from wuxia imagery into a layered social condition spanning court politics, literati withdrawal, commercial timing, and marginal survival. It uses [[Zhuangzi|庄子]] and [[FanLi|范蠡]] to show two forms of exit: spiritual release from attachment and practical retreat after success. Its most concrete workplace-adjacent claim is that surviving Jianghu requires reading situations clearly, speaking adaptively, and recognizing how hunger, insecurity, desire, and informal networks reshape conduct.

## Key Claims
- Jianghu is defined as the world formed wherever people create grudges, obligations, status, livelihood, and relationship entanglements.
- The court is itself a Jianghu: officials who leave power may still remember the center, while experienced insiders operate amid rules, struggle, and danger.
- The literati Jianghu, represented through Zhuangzi's "相忘于江湖," treats release from attachment as a higher answer than clinging to old mutual dependence.
- Fan Li represents commercial Jianghu as a problem of entering, profiting, reading the moment, changing names, and exiting before success turns dangerous.
- Street or underclass Jianghu is driven by unstable livelihood: people outside ordinary economic and kinship structures may survive through craft, fraud, violence, or predatory association.
- The episode's practical "混江湖" skills are sharp observation and flexible speech: seeing people and situations clearly, then using口才 and improvisation to keep moving.

## Key Quotes
> "有人的地方就有江湖" - the episode's broad definition of Jianghu.

> "相濡以沫，不如相忘于江湖" - the Zhuangzi phrase used to contrast dependence with release.
