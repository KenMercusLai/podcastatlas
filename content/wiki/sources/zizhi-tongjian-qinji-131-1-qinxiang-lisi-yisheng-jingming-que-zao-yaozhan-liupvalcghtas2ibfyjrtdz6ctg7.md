# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》131-1｜秦相李斯一生精明却遭腰斩

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-130-4-ruhe-dinglun-qinxiang-lisi-de-xiachang-ljnz0vflgr62hyktrg5cigz7c-7x|Qinji 130-4]] after [[LiSi|李斯]]'s arrest. It follows [[ZhaoGao|赵高]] as he controls the investigation, tortures Li Si into a confession, intercepts his memorial to [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]], and uses staged fake reviews to create a [[CoercedConfessionTrap|逼供认罪陷阱]]. The episode then turns Li Si's execution at [[Xianyang|咸阳]], the killing of his family, and the [[DongmenYellowDog|东门黄犬]] regret idiom into a retrospective judgment on ambition, failed exit, and destroyed ordinary life.

## Key Claims
- The episode opens from Li Si's imprisonment after the court crisis in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-130-4-ruhe-dinglun-qinxiang-lisi-de-xiachang-ljnz0vflgr62hyktrg5cigz7c-7x|Qinji 130-4]], contrasting [[ChenYing|陈英 / 陈婴]]'s measured restraint with Li Si's "无度" pursuit of office and his role beside Zhao Gao in Hu Hai's accession.
- Zhao Gao is assigned to investigate alleged treason by Li Si and [[LiYou|李由]], arrests Li Si's relatives and guests, and beats Li Si more than a thousand times until he admits rebellion.
- Li Si does not kill himself because he believes his state service, rhetorical skill, and actual innocence can still save him.
- In prison, Li Si memorializes Hu Hai by reviewing his decades of service: weakening and annexing the six states, expelling northern peoples, pacifying Baiyue, and standardizing weights, measures, and writing.
- Zhao Gao intercepts and discards the memorial, arguing that a prisoner has no standing to write to the emperor.
- Zhao Gao then sends fake censors and palace officials to interrogate Li Si. When Li Si denies guilt, he is tortured again, teaching him that denial only increases suffering.
- When Hu Hai later sends a real reviewer, Li Si can no longer distinguish real from fake and repeats the forced confession.
- Hu Hai reads the report as proof that Zhao Gao saved him from Li Si.
- A real imperial envoy reportedly finds no evidence that Li You rebelled in [[SanchuanWarringStates|三川]], but Zhao Gao tells Hu Hai the opposite.
- Li Si is sentenced to the five punishments and then waist-chopped in the Xianyang marketplace; his son is executed with him, and his three clans are destroyed under a [[CollateralPunishmentAndGuiltByAssociation|连坐式牵连惩罚]] logic.
- The episode uses Li Si's last memory of leaving [[Shangcai|上蔡]]'s east gate for ordinary hunting pleasure as the source of [[DongmenYellowDog|东门黄犬]] and [[DongmenYellowDog|东门逐兔]].
- The host's judgment keeps Zhao Gao as the direct killer while also blaming Li Si's own desire, inability to tolerate rivals such as [[HanFei|韩非]], weak relationship network, and failure to withdraw after success.
- After Li Si dies, Hu Hai appoints Zhao Gao as chancellor, and Zhao Gao handles major and minor state affairs.
- The episode closes by introducing [[SongYi|宋义]], a former Chu lingyin who joins [[XiangLiang|项梁]] as the anti-Qin field continues.

## Key Quotes
> "一个囚犯没有资格给皇帝上书" - Zhao Gao's stated reason for discarding Li Si's memorial.

> "东门黄犬" - the remembered ordinary-life image attached to Li Si's final regret.

> "李斯不容人" - the host's diagnosis of why no official network rescues him.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction found. The episode extends [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-130-4-ruhe-dinglun-qinxiang-lisi-de-xiachang-ljnz0vflgr62hyktrg5cigz7c-7x|Qinji 130-4]] by showing what happens after Li Si's arrest.
- Source-scope refinement: Qinji 130-4 records Zhao Gao's accusation against [[LiYou|李由]] as the pressure surface. This episode adds that an actual imperial envoy reported no evidence of Li You's rebellion, while Zhao Gao falsely reported the opposite to Hu Hai.
- The host's claim that Li Si may have escaped disaster if he had supported [[FuSu|扶苏]] is treated as counterfactual commentary, not as a settled historical outcome.
