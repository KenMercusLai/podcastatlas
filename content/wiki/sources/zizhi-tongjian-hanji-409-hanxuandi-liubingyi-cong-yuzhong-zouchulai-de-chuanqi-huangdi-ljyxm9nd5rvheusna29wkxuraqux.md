# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》409｜汉宣帝刘病已：从狱中走出来的传奇皇帝！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode bridges [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]]'s deposition of [[LiuHeHaihunHou|刘贺]] and the search for a new Western Han ruler. It records Liu He's post-deposition settlement, the execution or punishment of most Changyi followers, [[WangShiChangyiTeacher|王式]]'s survival through documented teaching remonstrance, and the infant [[EmperorXuanOfHan|刘病已 / 后来的汉宣帝]]'s survival in prison under [[BingJiWesternHan|丙吉]].

The durable synthesis is that Xuan's later "prison-to-restoration" profile begins as a chain of institutional and personal exceptions. Huo Guang uses ritual procedure to make deposition look like public state protection, Liu He is punished but not annihilated, Wang Shi shows that failed remonstrance can still count when it leaves a credible record, and Bing Ji's refusal to execute innocent prisoners preserves the future succession line.

## Key Claims

- After the empress dowager approves Liu He's removal, Huo Guang makes the deposition visible through edict, seal removal, exit through Jinma Gate, and the claim that he would rather fail Liu He than fail the state.
- Liu He loses the imperial title and Changyi kingdom, but the court allows him to return to Changyi with household property and support, while converting Changyi into Shanyang commandery.
- Most of Liu He's roughly two hundred Changyi followers are executed; Wang Ji, the langzhongling, and Wang Shi survive because their earlier admonition or teaching gives them a defensible record.
- Wang Shi answers the charge of not remonstrating by saying he repeatedly taught the full Shi jing to Liu He and cried over loyal-minister, filial-son, and failed-state passages.
- Liu Bingyi is identified as the great-grandson of [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] through crown prince Liu Ju, Liu Jin, and the imperial-grandson line.
- After the witchcraft disaster, the infant Liu Bingyi is imprisoned under guilt by association; Bing Ji knows Liu Ju lacked real criminal evidence, selects Hu Zu and Guo Zhengqing to nurse the child, visits him repeatedly, and uses personal pay and food to sustain care.
- When Han Wudi orders prison killings after an omen report of imperial qi in Chang'an prisons, Bing Ji locks the gate against Guo Rang and argues that innocent prisoners should not be killed, especially the emperor's great-grandson; the standoff leads to a broader amnesty.
- The host frames Liu He's fall as a moral warning about refusing good advice, and Liu Bingyi's survival as a dramatic setup for a ruler shaped by prison, commoner life, hardship, thrift, learning, and kindness.

## Key Quotes

> "宁负刘贺，不敢负社稷" - the episode's summary of Huo Guang's state-protection posture during deposition.

> "他每天早晚为昌邑王讲《诗经》三百零五篇" - Wang Shi's defense that teaching itself had been sustained admonition.

> "这里有皇曾孙，其他人尚且不应无罪被杀，更何况是皇上的曾孙" - Bing Ji's gate-blocking argument as summarized by the episode.
