# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》280｜河朔大捷，卫青如何一战封侯？（1）

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode narrates [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]]'s campaign to recover the [[HetaoRegionWesternHan|河套／河南地]]. It says Wei advances from Yunzhong along the southern Yinshan route, takes Jiuyuan and Gaoque, cuts the retreat of the Xiongnu Loufan and White Sheep kings, and attacks from the rear while [[LiXiWesternHan|李息]] watches for reinforcement from Dai. The victory reportedly captures thousands of people and more than one million livestock and leads to Wei Qing's enfeoffment as Marquis of Changping.

The durable synthesis extends beyond the battlefield. [[ZhuFuYan|主父偃]] argues that fertile land and the Yellow River barrier can turn recovery into a base against Xiongnu, and [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] creates [[ShuofangCommanderyWesternHan|朔方郡]], repairs walls, moves settlers, stations troops, develops farming and salt, and incorporates captives and submitters. The source therefore joins maneuver, territorial recovery, and [[FrontierResourceIntegration|边疆资源整合治理]], while preserving the very large construction, fiscal, migration, casualty, and livestock figures as episode-attributed claims.

## Key Claims

- The Hetao's water, pasture, and horse-raising conditions make it a strategic resource base rather than merely open frontier land.
- The episode says Meng Tian took the region for Qin and built forty-four forts, but Xiongnu recovered it during Qin's collapse.
- After Xiongnu raids in Shanggu and Yuyang, Wudi identifies the White Sheep and Loufan fields in the Hetao as a weaker part of the frontier system.
- Wei Qing reportedly advances from Yunzhong through Jiuyuan to Gaoque, making an apparent northward penetration before turning the position into a block on the defenders' retreat and attacking from behind.
- Li Xi reportedly departs from Dai to watch or block a larger Xiongnu relief force while Wei Qing conducts the main maneuver.
- The source reports several thousand Xiongnu killed or captured and more than one million livestock taken, followed by Wei Qing's enfeoffment as Marquis of Changping and rewards for Su Jian and Zhang Cigong.
- Zhufu Yan recommends permanent development because the land is fertile, the Yellow River offers a barrier, and local production can reduce transport pressure while creating a northern base.
- The court reportedly establishes Shuofang Commandery, builds a city, repairs Qin fortifications, moves settlers, stations troops, farms locally, develops salt, and settles captives and submitters, but the project imposes severe fiscal, transport, and labor costs.

## Key Quotes

> “卫青沿阴山南路快速进军，攻下九原后直扑高阙塞。” - the episode's route summary for the rearward maneuver.

> “国库几乎因此空虚。” - the source's strongest warning about the cost of converting victory into durable control.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with the existing wiki was adopted. Parts (2) and (3) do not narrate this campaign; they move into broad Eurasian migration and Chinese-Roman comparison, so part (1) supplies the missing event rather than duplicating them.
- Naming caution: the episode title says “河朔大捷,” while the body repeatedly identifies the recovered territory as 河南地／河套. The canonical event uses the descriptive “河南地之战” rather than assuming a single official battle name.
- Route caution: the Yunzhong-Jiuyuan-Gaoque sequence, feint interpretation, retreat-blocking geometry, Li Xi's exact containment mission, and the defenders' focus on the south remain source-scoped pending primary campaign reconstruction.
- Scale caution: troop numbers, killed and captured totals, livestock above one million, forty-four Qin forts, one hundred thousand builders and settlers, project cost, treasury depletion, and precise salt-development claims require primary or specialist verification.
- Policy caution: long-term incorporation is a state project rather than an instantaneous consequence of victory. Forts, commandery government, migration, production, and garrisoning can deepen control while also imposing coercive labor, fiscal, and population costs.
