# 《资治通鉴·周纪》03｜魏驹韩虎无间道

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode reconstructs the [[BattleOfJinyang|晋阳之战]] as a contest among coercive power, accumulated public support, and changing alliance incentives. [[ZhiYao|智瑶]] floods [[Jinyang|晋阳]] after [[ZhaoXiangzi|赵无恤]] refuses to surrender territory, but [[ZhangMengtan|张孟谈]] persuades [[WeiJu|魏驹]] and [[HanKangzi|韩虎 / 韩康子]] that Zhao's destruction would expose them next; they reverse the coalition, redirect the water into Zhi's camp, and destroy the Zhi clan.

## Key Claims
- Zhao Wuxu rejects Changzi because its people have just borne heavy labor and rejects Handan because officials have already stripped its people of wealth; he chooses Jinyang because Yin Duo's lenient rule has preserved a loyal social base.
- The episode treats public support as operational defense: even after floodwater destroys hearths, food disappears, and the city reaches the reported horror of exchanging children for food, Jinyang does not immediately surrender.
- Zhi Yao uses river engineering instead of direct assault, but his remark that water can destroy a state makes Wei Ju and Han Hu imagine the same tactic against Anyi and Pingyang.
- [[ChiCi|絺疵]] infers the Wei-Han defection risk from their anxiety when Zhao is near collapse, but Zhi Yao rejects the warning and reveals it to the suspected allies.
- Zhang Mengtan's “唇亡齿寒” appeal succeeds because it articulates a danger Wei and Han already recognize rather than asking for sympathy.
- Zhao troops kill the dike guards and reverse the flood; Wei and Han attack from both wings, Zhao captures and kills Zhi Yao, and the Zhi clan is destroyed apart from the source's account of Zhi Guo surviving under the Fu surname.
- The host presents surrender as a real civilian-protection dilemma, not an easy moral failure: continued resistance risks the ruler, the population, and the city as well as preserving the Zhao lineage.

## Key Quotes
> "唇亡齿寒" - Zhang Mengtan's compact statement of Wei and Han's shared survival interest.

> "水可以亡人之国" - Zhi Yao's reported observation, which exposes the future danger his allies face.
