# 商业小样48 | 不要在财报中创造指标

## Summary
This [[ShangyeJiushiZheyang|商业就是这样]] short episode uses [[LaoPuGold|老铺黄金]]'s interim-report disclosures to argue that supplemental operating metrics need stable definitions, historical traceability, peer comparability, and enough base data for investors to verify them. The host criticizes Lao Pu Gold's use of third-party rankings and store-productivity indicators from sources such as [[FrostSullivan|弗若斯特沙利文]], saying that changing metric scopes across prospectus, annual report, and interim report makes the same "number one" style claims difficult to test. The episode frames the issue as a disclosure-trust problem, not as proof that Lao Pu Gold's underlying business is weak.

## Key Claims
- Financial reports can include operating data beyond required accounting statements, but useful metrics should be traceable across periods and comparable with peers.
- [[LaoPuGold|老铺黄金]]'s reported rankings and productivity measures shift across industry scope, geography, denominator, and metric name, making trend comparison difficult.
- Third-party consulting labels do not solve verification if basic store count, sales, area, and adjustment-treatment disclosures are insufficient.
- Counting productivity by shopping mall rather than by store can materially change the denominator for a small store base, especially where multiple outlets sit in one mall.
- Comparisons with luxury brands are weak if those peers do not disclose the same operating metrics.
- The episode reads the metric choices as part of a possible luxury-brand repositioning, but warns that hard-to-verify metrics can increase investor confusion and distrust.

## Key Quotes
> "往期可追溯，以及同行可对比" - the host's minimum standard for useful operating indicators in financial reports.

> "这些数据无法证实，也无法证伪" - the critique of third-party metrics that lack enough supporting base disclosure.

> "不要在财报中创造指标" - the episode's closing rule about investor-facing reporting.
