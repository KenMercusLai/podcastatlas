# VOL.04医美｜正规有资质是医美的前提条件｜中韩医美整容的环境对比

## Summary
This early [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode frames injections, water-light treatment, radiofrequency, ultrasound, laser, liposuction, and cosmetic surgery as medical acts rather than low-cost beauty purchases. It connects [[MedicalAestheticProviderVerification|institution and operator verification]], [[UnapprovedMedicalAestheticProducts|product traceability]], and [[CosmeticProcedureExpectationManagement|realistic benefit and maintenance expectations]] to a practical rule: credentials, sterile technique, device skill, and complication capacity come before price or promised convenience.

## Key Claims
- Medical-aesthetic procedures should take place in a qualified medical setting and be performed by appropriately trained, credentialed personnel; a hotel, home, beauty salon, or short course is not an equivalent care environment.
- Injectable products require lawful provenance, correct preparation, sterile handling, and traceability; unapproved or informally sourced products can add infection, granuloma, composition, and accountability risks.
- Device possession does not establish safe use. Energy, depth, treatment layer, consumables, and operator judgment affect outcomes, while errors can cause burns, blistering, infection, or other injury.
- Price is not a complete quality test, but a quote below plausible consumable, staffing, facility, and compliance costs is a reason to investigate rather than assume a bargain.
- Minimally invasive or fast-recovery procedures remain medical interventions; home water-light injection and other self-treatment bypass sterile technique, drug-compatibility judgment, correct tissue depth, and complication response.
- Device-based tightening is presented as periodic maintenance with bounded effects rather than a one-time reversal of marked laxity, and photograph-based requests must be reconciled with individual anatomy and three-dimensional structure.
- The episode attributes parts of South Korea's medical-aesthetics reputation to case volume, market density, and aesthetic environment, but its class-background and national-aesthetic comparisons are personal observations rather than systematic evidence.
