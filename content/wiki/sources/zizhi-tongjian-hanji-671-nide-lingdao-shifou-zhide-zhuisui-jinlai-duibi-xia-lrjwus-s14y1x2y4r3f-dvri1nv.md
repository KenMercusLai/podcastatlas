# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》671：刘秀为何值得追随

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Gengshi 2 / 24 CE immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-672-shenchu-zhichang-hui-zhuang-cai-shi-gaoji-zhihui-ls35j8plj-cqnblgufc4plbzpnby|Hanji 672]]. It asks why people would follow [[LiuXiu|刘秀]] before his Hebei position looked secure, then follows [[DengYuEasternHan|邓禹]]'s confidence in virtue and capacity, [[KouXunEasternHan|寇恂]]'s Shanggu-Yuyang alignment work, [[WuHanEasternHan|吴汉]]'s forged-proclamation intervention, [[GengYanEasternHan|耿弇]]'s reunion with Liu Xiu, and [[GengChunEasternHan|耿纯]]'s advice to strike Handan instead of remaining stalled at Julu.

The durable synthesis is a [[LeaderWorthFollowingAssessment|leader-worthiness assessment]] pattern: under uncertainty, followers weigh not only present territory or troop strength, but whether a leader has durable reputation, accepts good advice, recognizes usable talent, lets new allies keep command enough to build merit, and can shift strategy when conditions change. The episode also adds an earlier active layer for [[BaoYongEasternHan|鲍永]] under the Gengshi regime and an origin layer for [[JingDanEasternHan|景丹]] in the Shanggu-Yuyang cavalry force.

## Key Claims

- Deng Yu tells Liu Xiu that people in disorder seek an able and virtuous ruler, so rise depends on 德才 rather than current territorial scale.
- Kou Xun and Min Ye advise [[GengKuangEasternHan|耿况]] that Handan's future is uncertain while Liu Xiu is known for treating worthy people respectfully.
- Kou Xun's contact with [[PengChongEasternHan|彭宠]] and Wu Han's Yuyang intervention convert border-commandery hesitation into an armed coalition for Liu Xiu.
- Wu Han is presented as poor at emergency speech but calm, strategic, and able to use field information to force a political opening.
- The Shanggu-Yuyang force gives Liu Xiu both political reinforcement and direct cavalry effectiveness, especially through Jing Dan and other raiders at Nanluan.
- Liu Xiu's immediate rewards let new followers retain their original troops and receive status, turning late alignment into a merit-building opportunity.
- Geng Chun's advice to abandon the prolonged Julu siege and strike Handan shows Liu Xiu's advantage as a leader who can accept timely strategic correction.
- Wang Lang's surrender negotiation fails because Liu Xiu refuses to preserve a legitimacy claim that could keep attracting allegiance.

## Key Quotes

> "关键在品德和才能，而不是地盘大小" - Deng Yu's argument that present territory is not the decisive measure of a rising ruler.

> "刘秀礼贤下士" - the reason Kou Xun and Min Ye give for preferring Liu Xiu over the Handan claim.

> "共同建立功勋" - Liu Xiu's reward frame after the Shanggu and Yuyang troops arrive.
