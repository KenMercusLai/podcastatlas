## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode joins four episodes from the early reign of [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]: [[LiuShengZhongshanKingWesternHan|中山王刘胜]]'s complaint about official pressure on royal kin, [[Minyue|闽越]]'s attack on [[DongouWesternHan|东瓯]] and the disputed Han rescue, a talent-recruitment drive that first elevates [[YanZhuWesternHan|严助]], and incognito hunting encouraged by [[HanYanWesternHan|韩嫣]]. Its most distinctive contribution is a governance-risk sequence: concealed imperial identity and crop damage make local resistance reasonable from the farmers' perspective, while recognition of an imperial token narrowly prevents violent escalation.

The episode says Wudi pardons the residents and compensates their losses but continues hunting. The wiki therefore treats the incident as evidence for [[IncognitoImperialHuntingRisk|微服游猎治理风险]], not proof that an unverified crowd literally came within one act of killing the emperor; exact numbers, geography, dialogue, and motives remain source-scoped.

## Key Claims

- Liu Sheng weeps at a 138 BCE court banquet and says officials use factional pressure to estrange the emperor from royal kin; Wudi responds by improving princely treatment and reducing constraints.
- The episode contrasts that grievance with Liu Sheng's later richly furnished tomb and criticizes the distance between princely wealth and ordinary living conditions.
- After Minyue attacks and surrounds Dongou, Tian Fen advises nonintervention, while Yan Zhu argues that refusing aid despite having the capacity to help would weaken imperial authority.
- Wudi sends Yan Zhu to mobilize Kuaiji troops without using the normal tiger tally; the commandery initially refuses, Yan kills an opposing military official, and Minyue withdraws before the relief force arrives.
- Dongou then requests relocation, and the episode says its ruler and forty thousand people are resettled in the Yangtze-Huai region.
- Wudi recruits memorialists outside routine channels, differentiating policy advisers and appointees from literary or humorous companions whose talent does not produce formal office.
- After political frustration under the empress dowager, Wudi follows Han Yan into repeated incognito hunting and uses the identity of the Pingyang marquis.
- Hunting followers damage crops near Zhongnan Mountain; farmers and local militia surround the unidentified group until an official recognizes an imperial token.
- Wudi pardons the residents and compensates losses, but the episode says he does not abandon the risky practice.

## Key Quotes

> “如果有能力保护求援小国，就不应坐视。” - the episode's paraphrase of Yan Zhu's intervention argument.

> “百姓不知情。” - the rationale attributed to Wudi when he declines to punish the residents.
