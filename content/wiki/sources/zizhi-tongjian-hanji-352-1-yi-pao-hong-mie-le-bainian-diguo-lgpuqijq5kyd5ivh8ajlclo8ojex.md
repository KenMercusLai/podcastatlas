# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》352-1｜一炮轰灭了百年帝国

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the political breakdown immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-352-2-hanwudi-weihe-fengshang-baijun-zhi-jiang-li16pfjb6p0owys5htecscfomhch|Hanji 352-2]]'s failed vanguard. [[ZhaoXingNanyue|赵兴]] and [[QueenDowagerJiuNanyue|樛太后]] favor closer submission to Han, but an attempted banquet confrontation does not remove chancellor [[LyuJiaNanyue|吕嘉]]. After [[ZhuangCanWesternHan|庄参]] refuses an undersized two-thousand-person mission, [[HanQianqiuWesternHan|韩千秋]] volunteers to act with three hundred brave men and is instead sent with two thousand troops and the queen dowager's brother [[JiuLeNanyue|樛乐]].

Their entry triggers Lü Jia's coup. He casts the young king, the Han-born queen dowager, and her relationship with a Han envoy as threats to Nanyue's people and wealth, kills the king, queen dowager, and Han envoys, and installs [[ZhaoJiandeNanyue|赵建德]]. The host frames the episode as a lesson in personnel and decisive action, but the exact feasibility of killing Lü Jia at the banquet, the public reach of his mobilization claims, and the actors' inner motives remain source-scoped.

## Key Claims

- Zhao Xing and Queen Dowager Jiu are presented as willing to move Nanyue toward closer Han incorporation, while Lü Jia resists.
- A banquet confrontation creates an opportunity to attack Lü Jia, but Han envoys hesitate and Zhao Xing prevents the queen dowager from acting directly.
- Han Wudi first asks Zhuang Can to lead two thousand people; Zhuang Can refuses because a peaceful mission needs only a few people and a coercive mission needs more than two thousand.
- Former Jibei chancellor Han Qianqiu volunteers to kill Lü Jia with three hundred brave men because the king and queen dowager will supposedly support him.
- Wudi sends Han Qianqiu and Jiu Le with two thousand troops into Nanyue in 112 BCE.
- Lü Jia answers the incursion with rebellion, mobilizing against the royal party through claims about foreign influence, sexual impropriety, treasure transfer, and enslavement.
- Lü Jia's faction kills Zhao Xing, Queen Dowager Jiu, and the Han envoys, informs local rulers and commanderies, and installs Zhao Jiande as king.

## Key Quotes

> “以好往，数人足矣；以武往，二千人无足以为也。” - Zhuang Can's reported distinction between diplomacy and coercion.

> “愿得勇士三百人，必斩嘉以报。” - Han Qianqiu's reported confidence that royal support would make a small strike sufficient.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with existing wiki content is adopted. The episode supplies the coup and mission-selection prequel that Hanji 352-2 presupposes.
- Name normalization: the supplied summary's “纠太后,” “纠勒,” and “夹县” are routed to the received forms [[QueenDowagerJiuNanyue|樛太后]], [[JiuLeNanyue|樛乐]], and 郏. The underlying summary provides no textual apparatus, so this is normalization rather than a claim about its transcription method.
- Hanji 352-2 renders the slain envoy and rewarded son as “樛罗” and “樛广德.” This source's 樛乐 makes identification plausible but does not independently settle the received text or the son's identity.
- The host treats killing Lü Jia at the banquet as a low-cost path to peaceful incorporation. That counterfactual is not established by the event sequence and does not account fully for Lü Jia's local support.
- The allegations about the queen dowager's sexual relationship and planned transfer of treasure or people appear inside hostile mobilization rhetoric. They are not adopted as neutral fact.
- Troop totals, speeches, dialogue, exact chronology, psychological motives, and the extent of popular support remain episode-attributed.
