## Summary
This episode backfills the Jianwu 12 / 36 CE endgame in Shu immediately before the later Guangwu consolidation episodes. It follows [[GongsunShuEasternHan|Gongsun Shu]]'s final Chengdu battle, [[YanCenEasternHan|Yan Cen]]'s surrender, and [[WuHanEasternHan|Wu Han]]'s post-surrender killing, clan destruction, looting, and palace burning, which [[LiuXiu|Liu Xiu]] condemns as a breach after Chengdu's officials and civilians had already submitted. The second half contrasts Gongsun Shu's coercive summons of Shu scholars with Liu Xiu's post-conquest repair through honors, compensation, appointments, and recognition of local loyalists.

## Key Claims
- Gongsun Shu personally enters the Chengdu battle, sends Yan Cen against Zhang Gong, and is fatally wounded after Wu Han reinforces with Gao Wu and Tang Han.
- Yan Cen inherits command from the dying Gongsun Shu and offers Chengdu in surrender the next day.
- Wu Han treats the surrender punitively: he kills Gongsun Shu's household and clan, destroys Yan Cen's clan, and allows looting and burning in [[Chengdu]].
- Liu Xiu rebukes Wu Han and Liu Shang because Chengdu had already surrendered and the officials and civilians had already submitted, making the violence a [[BreachOfSurrenderTrust|breach of surrender trust]] rather than ordinary battle damage.
- Gongsun Shu's attempted recruitment of Shu scholars relies on poison threats, hostage families, and office/title inducements, making the episode a case of [[CoercedTalentLegitimacy|coerced talent legitimacy]].
- Li Nie, Qiao Xuan, Wang Hao, Wang Jia, Fei Yi, Ren Yong, Feng Xin, and Wen Qi represent different forms of [[PrincipledOfficeRefusal|principled office refusal]], from martyrdom to ransom-mediated survival and feigned incapacity.
- Liu Xiu's post-Shu settlement honors or compensates refusers, summons surviving talent, uses former Gongsun Shu officers Cheng Ru and Li Yu, and rewards [[WenQiYizhouEasternHan|Wen Qi]], presenting conquest legitimacy as civilian repair and local-elite incorporation.
- The episode closes by setting up Ma Cheng's appointment as acting Da Sikong and the next Ma Yuan-centered storyline.

## Key Quotes
> "成都已降三日，吏民从服。" - Liu Xiu's rebuke frame in the episode.

> "怎么忍心纵火焚烧。" - Liu Xiu's moral question after the Chengdu sack.

> "死也没有遗憾。" - Qiao Xuan's refusal frame before his son offers ransom.

## Contradictions
- No settled wiki contradiction is resolved here, but the episode sharply qualifies any simple reading of Wu Han as only a disciplined founding commander: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-715-shishang-zui-zhihui-de-yici-duoquan-loqkawl247maw9fn-nomaw5gzkgu|Hanji 715]]'s later honor for Wu Han has to be held beside this episode's account of Liu Xiu rebuking him for post-surrender killing, plunder, and burning.
- Yan Cen's surrender also qualifies [[SurrenderAsCivilianProtectionTradeoff]]: offering a city can aim to preserve people, but the protection depends on the receiver honoring the surrender.
