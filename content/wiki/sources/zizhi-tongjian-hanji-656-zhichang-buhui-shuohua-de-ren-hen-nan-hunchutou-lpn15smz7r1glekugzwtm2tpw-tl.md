# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》656丨职场不会说话的人，很难混出头!

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 19-20 CE late-[[XinDynasty|新朝]] crisis before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-658-erzi-queshi-buyinggai-wangmang-nusha-taizi-ln4tu6ly8eie6jspvrurzn2rjjp|Hanji 658]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-659-youxie-hexin-rencai-buyao-qingyi-dong-lg1nzal53ihdw-krici2thek-ij|Hanji 659]]. It uses [[FanShengXinOfficial|范生]], [[TianKuangXinOfficial|田邝]], and [[HanBoXinOfficial|韩伯]] to compare three speech styles under [[WangMang|王莽]]: indirect warning through a superior, revenue-pleasing policy advice, and sarcastic symbolic critique.

The durable synthesis is that a collapsing regime does not simply lack information; it rewards some messages and punishes others. Fan Sheng identifies domestic grievance and mistimed corvee as more dangerous than frontier anxiety, Tian Kuang is rewarded for enabling another property tax extraction, and Han Bo is killed after using the "巨无霸" report to mock Wang Mang's taste for prodigy and omen performance.

## Key Claims

- [[FanShengXinOfficial|范生]]'s petition to [[WangYiXinGeneral|王邑 / 王毅]] is a late-Xin case of [[ObliqueRemonstranceSelfProtection|迂回进谏自保]]: it preserves criticism of the ruler's priorities while routing the risk through an immediate superior.
- Fan Sheng's substantive diagnosis is [[RootCauseRemonstrance|root-cause remonstrance]]: domestic resentment, hunger, mistimed labor mobilization, and abandoned fields are more dangerous than treating border trouble as the only threat.
- [[TianKuangXinOfficial|田邝]]'s earlier tax memorial shows a morally compromised branch of crisis advice: he tells the center that property estimates are too low, Wang Mang imposes another thirty-to-one levy, and Tian Kuang receives title and money.
- The repeated levy pushes Qingzhou and Xuzhou civilians from grievance into flight, death, and banditry, making [[FiscalExtractionRebellionSpiral|财政压榨叛乱螺旋]] visible before later Chimei and field-command notes.
- [[HanBoXinOfficial|韩伯]]'s "巨无霸" memorial is a satirical allusive attack on Wang Mang's prodigy politics, but Wang Mang decodes the insult and kills him.
- Wang Mang's 20 CE response combines amnesty, era-name change, street-terror rules, and a proliferation of military titles, increasing fear and administrative burden rather than restoring order.

> "巨无霸" - Han Bo's absurd prodigy figure, used in the episode as satirical criticism of Wang Mang.
