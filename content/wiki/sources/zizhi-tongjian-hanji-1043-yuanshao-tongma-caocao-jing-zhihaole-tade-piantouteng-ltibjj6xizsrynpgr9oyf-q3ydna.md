# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1043丨袁绍痛骂曹操，竟治好了他的偏头疼？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode fills the missing bridge between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1042-congming-haishi-wangu-tianfeng-yin-dingzhuang-yuanshao-ruyu-lk5x6if2rouarmgichtynzhl3cgs|Hanji 1042]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1044-guanyu-qianli-zou-danqi-xu-ganxie-caocao-busha-enqing-lpptlnc-e4zu4lcjr49bquxdw0pm|Hanji 1044]] in the immediate pre-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]] sequence. [[YuanShao|袁绍]] imprisons [[TianFengLateHan|田丰]], sends [[ChenLinLateHan|陈琳]]'s anti-[[CaoCao|曹操]] proclamation through the realm, and moves south through [[LiyangYellowRiverLateHan|黎阳]], while [[JuShou|沮授]] reads the expedition as a likely disaster.

The second half centers [[BaimaReliefLateHan|白马之围]]: [[ChengYu|程昱]] protects [[JuanchengLateHan|鄄城]] by refusing extra troops, Yuan Shao sends [[YanLiangLateHan|颜良]] against [[LiuYanDongCommandery|刘延]] at White Horse, Cao Cao uses a feigned Yellow River/rear attack to divide Yuan's force, and [[GuanYu|关羽]] kills Yan Liang to lift the siege.

## Key Claims
- Chen Lin's proclamation is a legitimacy indictment against Cao Cao, attacking his treatment of loyal officials, emperor-control, Xuzhou violence, tomb-robbing offices, and eunuch-descended ingratitude.
- Cao Cao's response separates literary force from war capacity: he acknowledges the writing's bite but says the decisive question is real ability and command.
- Ju Shou's family-property distribution is not simple cowardice; it combines a loss-of-power survival warning with a diagnosis of Cao's emperor-backed position, Yuan troops' exhaustion, Yuan Shao's arrogance, and generals' luxury.
- Cheng Yu's refusal of 2,000 reinforcements at Juancheng turns weakness into concealment: seven hundred troops look too small for Yuan Shao to attack, while a larger garrison might make the city worth taking.
- Ju Shou warns that Yan Liang is impulsive and narrow despite bravery, making him unsafe as an independent commander; Yuan Shao ignores the warning.
- Cao Cao's White Horse relief uses a diversionary crossing threat to split Yuan Shao's army westward before a fast light-force strike on White Horse.
- Guan Yu's killing of Yan Liang resolves the White Horse siege and supplies the concrete merit that bridges his Hanji 1041 repayment promise with the Hanji 1044 departure.

## Key Quotes
> "光会写文章不行，还得有真本事、会带兵打仗才行." - Cao Cao's reported distinction between prose and command capacity.

> "袁军很可能全军覆没." - Ju Shou's pre-departure risk judgment as summarized by the episode.

> "必须设法分散袁军." - the tactical premise behind Cao Cao's White Horse relief.
