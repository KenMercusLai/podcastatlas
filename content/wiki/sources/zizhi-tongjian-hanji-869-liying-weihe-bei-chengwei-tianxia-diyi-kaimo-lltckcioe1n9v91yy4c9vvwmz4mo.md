# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》869丨李膺为何被称为天下第一楷模？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 165 CE [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] field immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-870-gudai-tizhinei-buganle-meiyou-tuilu-lpwbniu9ifpt6cp4ip9czqwsh5xd|Hanji 870]] and the later Party Prohibition setup. It contrasts a failed tearful petition by [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] with [[YingFengLateHan|应奉]]'s more effective memorial, which frames pardoning [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]] and other officials as necessary to state security and frontier stability.

The episode's durable contribution is the earlier reputation basis for Li Ying. After pardon and reappointment as Sili xiaowei, Li Ying arrests and executes [[ZhangShuoLateHan|张硕]], the abusive brother of [[ZhangRangLateHan|张让]], survives Zhang Rang's complaint before Huan, frightens palace attendants, and turns admission to his circle into the status marker later called "登龙门."

## Key Claims

- In 165 CE, [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] repeatedly pleads for wrongly punished officials, but emotional sincerity does not persuade Emperor Huan.
- Ying Feng's memorial reframes the same case through state capacity: loyal officials are the court's backbone, past service should count, and border instability makes capable officials hard to discard.
- The memorial uses precedents from Emperor Wu's use of Han Anguo and Emperor Xuan's recall of Zhang Chang to argue for practical talent use after error.
- Emperor Huan accepts Ying Feng's framing and removes punishment from Li Ying and the other officials discussed in the source.
- Li Ying's later return as Sili xiaowei becomes a visible enforcement test when Zhang Shuo, Zhang Rang's brother and Yewang magistrate, hides in Zhang Rang's Luoyang house after corrupt and violent conduct.
- Li Ying breaks into the hiding space, imprisons Zhang Shuo, and executes him after taking testimony, despite Zhang Shuo's eunuch-family protection.
- When Zhang Rang complains, Li Ying answers through the Confucius and Shao Zhengmao precedent and asks for more time to correct law, after which Emperor Huan upholds him.
- Palace eunuchs become visibly afraid of Li Ying, while scholar-officials treat being received by him as "登龙门," strengthening the later [[QingyiReputationPressure|清议声誉压力]] field.
- The closing preview introduces Huan's favorite Tian Sheng and the coming rivalry with Dou Miao, which [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-879-si-zai-nvren-shenshang-de-hunyong-nanren-lqz5-mpi0waa-xmuhjhwkaecjjrc|Hanji 879]] develops.
