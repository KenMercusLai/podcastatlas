# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》206｜诛吕安刘：史上第一灭门案（1）

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows the first moves after [[LuZhi|吕雉]]'s death. [[LiuXiangQiKingWesternHan|齐王刘襄]] detains [[LiuZeLangyeKingWesternHan|琅邪王刘泽]], absorbs Langye's troops, and issues an anti-Lü mobilization call; [[GuanYing|灌婴]] then halts at [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]] rather than strengthen the Lü faction by defeating Qi.

The episode also challenges a simple "Lü rebellion followed by Liu restoration" account. It reads Liu Xiang and [[LiuZhangChengyangKingWesternHan|刘章]] as possible succession seekers, while the Chang'an side remains militarily uncertain: [[LuLuWesternHan|吕禄]] and [[LuChanWesternHan|吕产]] control the capital armies but hesitate, and [[ChenPing|陈平]] with [[ZhouBo|周勃]] use pressure on [[LiShang|郦商]] to bring [[LiJiWesternHan|郦寄]] into the effort to detach Lü Lu from command.

## Key Claims

- Liu Zhang reportedly asks Liu Xiang to mobilize from Qi while he and [[LiuXingjuWesternHan|刘兴居]] act as internal allies in Chang'an.
- Liu Xiang lures Liu Ze to Linzi with an offer to entrust Qi's military affairs to him, detains him, and has [[ZhuWuWesternHan|祝午]] absorb Langye's forces into the Qi army.
- Liu Ze gains release by affirming Liu Xiang's imperial prospects and claiming that, as the senior Liu-family prince, he should go to the capital to influence the ministers.
- Qi attacks Jinan and circulates a proclamation accusing the Lü faction, but the host argues that Liu Xiang's methods and Liu Zhang's message may indicate an accession project rather than only defense of the young emperor.
- Lü Chan sends Guan Ying against Qi, yet Guan Ying stops at Xingyang because defeating the regional force would strengthen the Lü household; he instead coordinates a wait-and-see settlement with Liu Xiang and other kingdoms.
- Lü Lu and Lü Chan reportedly hesitate to act inside Chang'an because they fear Zhou Bo and Liu Zhang internally and Qi, Chu, and a possible Guan Ying defection externally.
- Chen Ping and Zhou Bo, lacking direct control of the troops, coerce the elderly and ill Li Shang into sending his son Li Ji to persuade his close friend Lü Lu.
- Li Ji argues that Lü Lu should return the general's seal and Northern Army command to Zhou Bo, while Lü Chan should relinquish the chancellorial seal and return to his kingdom, so that Qi loses its pretext and the Lü household can retain its grants.

- [[GuanYing|灌婴]] and [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]] - commander and strategic pause that prevent an immediate Qi-Lü battle.
- [[LuLuWesternHan|吕禄]], [[LuChanWesternHan|吕产]], [[ChenPing|陈平]], [[ZhouBo|周勃]], [[LiShang|郦商]], and [[LiJiWesternHan|郦寄]] - capital command holders and the personal-pressure channel used to challenge them.

## Contradictions

- The episode explicitly disputes the clean transmitted sequence of "the Lü clan rebels, Liu Zhang responds, and Qi restores order." It argues that Liu Zhang and Liu Xiang may have sought the throne, but their private intent is not independently established.
- The claim that no minister or prince disputed [[LiuHongYoungEmperorWesternHan|少帝刘弘]]'s legitimacy is part of the host's critique; this episode does not compare all succession statements or transmitted versions.
- The alleged Lü coup plan, Liu Zhang's message, Liu Xiang's intended accession, Liu Ze's calculation, Guan Ying's coordination, and the motives of Chen Ping, Zhou Bo, Li Shang, and Li Ji remain episode-attributed.
- Exact titles, dates, troop strengths, routes, proclamations, dialogue, command relationships, and the identities of the young princes retained in Chang'an require comparison with transmitted histories.
- The source ends before Lü Lu answers Li Ji's proposal. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-206-zhu-lv-an-liu-shishang-diyi-miemen-an-2-lpsj8exoquanifh7njkrhqszrhkh0|Hanji 206 part 2]] supplies the subsequent surrender of the seal and Northern Army command.
