# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》140｜项羽与刘邦是真友情还是相互利用？（2）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the pre-[[HongmenYan|鸿门宴]] warning channel opened in part 1. [[XiangBo|项伯]] rides from Xiang Yu's camp to [[Bashang|霸上]] to save [[ZhangLiang|张良]], Zhang Liang refuses to abandon [[LiuBang|刘邦]], and Liu Bang turns Xiang Bo's personal loyalty into a de-escalation path by honoring him as an elder, invoking shared Chu-camp comradeship, and asking him to carry a loyalist explanation back to [[XiangYu|项羽]].

## Key Claims
- Xiang Bo is decisive because he sits at the intersection of [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s kinship trust and Zhang Liang's old life-saving favor.
- Zhang Liang's refusal to flee alone turns a private rescue offer into a political opportunity for Liu Bang's camp.
- Liu Bang quickly reads Xiang Bo's personality and uses wine, elder-brother address, marriage alliance language, and loyalty claims to transform an attack crisis into a face-saving meeting proposal.
- Liu Bang's explanation is framed as performative and expedient: he says the pass closure was anti-bandit security and that he only sealed Qin archives and warehouses while waiting for Xiang Yu.
- Xiang Bo's return argument pressures Xiang Yu through family authority, Liu Bang's prior merit in entering Guanzhong, and the moral claim that attacking a meritorious ally would be "unrighteous."
- Xiang Yu is portrayed as emotionally susceptible rather than purely instrumental: old comradeship, Xiang Bo's elder status, and the language of "义" move him to cancel the next-morning attack.
- The episode distinguishes a "灭刘派" around [[FanZeng|范增]] from a "存刘派" around Xiang Bo, and warns against judging the factions only by later knowledge that Liu Bang eventually defeats Xiang Yu.

## Key Quotes
> "不义" - the moral label Zhang Liang and Xiang Bo each use, in different positions, to reject abandonment or attack.

> "不敢有二心" - Liu Bang's loyalty claim to Xiang Bo, presented as useful crisis performance.

> "存刘派" - the episode's label for Xiang Bo's camp-faction position before the Hongmen meeting.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is recorded. The episode softens neither Liu Bang's opportunism nor Xiang Yu's strategic weakness; it shows how both become mediated through old obligation, kinship, etiquette, and moral language.
- The host notes that hindsight makes Fan Zeng's "灭刘" position look correct, but keeps the source-level judgment that Xiang Bo's "存刘" argument had stronger public footing at that moment because Fan Zeng had not supplied a convincing pretext.
