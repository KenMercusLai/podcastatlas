# 《资治通鉴·周纪》45｜以史为鉴，君王到底该不该杀人成性？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens the show's 周纪三 range, from 320 to 298 BCE, and uses [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]]'s meeting with [[WeiXiangwang|魏襄王]] to ask whether violent rulers can truly settle the realm. Mencius answers that only a ruler who does not love killing can unify, and the host reads that claim through Qin's short-lived conquest, [[XiangYu|项羽]] versus [[LiuBang|刘邦]], the failed five-state assault on [[HanguPass|函谷关]], and [[SongJunYan|宋康王 / 宋君偃]]'s brutal rise and destruction. The episode's core distinction is between taking power through force and keeping order through people-based legitimacy.

## Key Claims
- 周纪三 covers twenty-three years from 320 to 298 BCE, spanning [[ZhouShenliangwang|周慎靓王]]'s six-year reign and the first seventeen years of [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]].
- In 320 BCE, Wei's ruler lowers his title amid decline, and [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] dies with [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]] succeeding; this is compatible with the wiki's existing correction that Qi Xuan Wang's first regnal year belongs around 319 BCE.
- In 319 BCE, [[QinState|秦国]] attacks [[HanState|韩国]] and takes Yanling, while [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王 / 梁惠王]] dies and [[WeiXiangwang|魏襄王]] succeeds.
- Mencius judges Wei Xiang Wang as lacking kingly presence, then answers his question about settling the realm by saying unity is necessary and that the unifying ruler will be one who does not love killing.
- The host treats [[NonKillingUnification|不爱杀人者能一之]] as a durable-order claim rather than a naive denial that violent states can win short-term victories.
- Qin's eventual conquest appears to challenge Mencius, but the host notes that the Qin dynasty was short-lived, while the later Chu-Han contrast makes [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s comparatively less-killing image the founder of a long Han order against [[XiangYu|项羽]].
- In 318 BCE, [[ChuState|楚国]], [[ZhaoState|赵国]], [[WeiState|魏国]], [[HanState|韩国]], and [[YanState|燕国]] attack Qin with [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] as alliance leader, while Qi promises support but has [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]] make the Qi army move slowly.
- The five-state army reaches Hangu Pass but fails because each state wants to preserve strength and avoid attacking first; Qin cuts Chu's grain route, Chu withdraws, and the rest follow.
- [[SongJunYan|宋康王 / 宋君偃]] claims kingly title in 318 BCE after the earlier Song coup branch, expands against Qi, Chu, Wei, Xue, and Teng, and makes Song briefly strong while creating enemies on all sides.
- Song Kang Wang's cruelty toward advisers and ordinary people destroys domestic support; Qi, Chu, and Wei attack, Song's people scatter rather than defend him, he is killed, and [[SongState|宋国]] disappears.

## Key Quotes
> "不爱杀人者能一之" - Mencius's answer to who can unify the realm.

> "像盼雨一样归顺" - the source's paraphrase of Mencius's image of people turning toward a non-killing ruler.

> "桀宋" - the hostile label applied to Song Kang Wang because of his cruelty.

## Contradictions
- No direct contradiction found. The episode's 320 BCE Qi Wei Wang death / Qi Xuan Wang succession notice should be read with the existing [[QiChronologyDispute|齐威王编年争议]] correction: Qi Xuan Wang can succeed after Qi Wei Wang's death while his first regnal year is still treated as 319 BCE.
- The source also does not fully overturn Mencius by noting Qin's later conquest. It explicitly distinguishes violent acquisition from durable rule: Qin can conquer, but its short life and the later Liu Bang/Xiang Yu contrast are used to keep [[NonKillingUnification|不爱杀人者能一之]] as a long-term legitimacy claim.
