# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1029丨国士无双太史慈：一诺千金义当先

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode pairs a modern windfall-loss caution with the late-Han story of [[SunCe|孙策]] trusting [[TaishiCi|太史慈]] after [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]]'s death. Sun Ce sends Taishi Ci to calm Liu Yao's remaining followers and inspect [[YuzhangCommandery|豫章郡]], while others warn that Taishi Ci may not return; Taishi Ci keeps his sixty-day promise and reports that [[HuaXin|华歆]] is morally upright but unable to control local actors around [[LulingLateHan|庐陵]] and [[HaihunLateHan|海昏]].

The source's durable contribution is [[CharacterReadingDelegationTrust|识人授权信任]]: Sun Ce's trust is not blind optimism, but a judgment that Taishi Ci's promise-keeping and search for a worthy lord make delegation workable. Taishi Ci's intelligence then turns personal trust into strategic information about Yuzhang's fragmentation.

## Key Claims
- The opening windfall story treats sudden money as a test of restraint rather than a stable reward, extending [[WindfallDecisionTrap|无故之利]] into personal-finance and self-knowledge language.
- After Liu Yao dies, his remaining troops become a political-military opening that Sun Ce wants to pacify rather than immediately attack.
- Sun Ce gives Taishi Ci a limited mission: reassure Liu Yao's followers, invite willing soldiers to come over, and observe Hua Xin's ability in Yuzhang.
- Taishi Ci asks to take only a few dozen men because the mission is framed as calming and investigation, not battle.
- Sun Ce's advisers treat Taishi Ci's departure as a defection risk, but Sun Ce judges that Taishi Ci will not betray a ruler who understands him.
- Taishi Ci keeps the promised return date and reports that Hua Xin has virtue but lacks the strategy and control needed to settle Yuzhang.
- Taishi Ci's report names [[TongZhiLateHan|僮芝]] in Luling and [[PanyangLocalForces|番阳民帅]] around Haihun as signs that Yuzhang is no longer tightly governed from the commandery center.
- The episode links Sun Ce and Taishi Ci's earlier [[ShentinglingBattle|神亭岭之战]] encounter to later mutual recognition and loyalty.

## Key Quotes
> "横财不是奖励，而是考题" - the opening lesson used to frame windfall, restraint, and self-knowledge.

> "最多60天" - Taishi Ci's promised return window before leaving on the Yuzhang mission.
