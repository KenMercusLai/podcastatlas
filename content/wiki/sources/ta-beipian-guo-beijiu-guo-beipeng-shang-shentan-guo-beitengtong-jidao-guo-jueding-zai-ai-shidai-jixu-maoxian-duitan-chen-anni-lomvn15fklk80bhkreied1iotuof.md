## Summary
This [[ShizilukouCrossing]] episode interviews [[ChenAnni]], founder and CEO of [[KuaikanManhua]], about the company's new AI-native content product [[LIVO]] and the founder history behind that bet. Chen frames LIVO as a "real-time living AI world" where users enter authored worlds, interact with agentic characters, and influence a continuing narrative fate rather than merely chatting with a role or watching generated media. The episode connects AI product design to Kuaikan's earlier crises around cash flow, algorithmic traffic pressure, IP quality, commercialization, and an organizational "game spirit" for continuing to experiment under uncertainty.

## Key Claims
- [[LIVO]] is positioned as an [[AINativeNarrativeWorlds|AI-native narrative world]] rather than a traditional game, chatbot, or comic with AI features added on top.
- Chen argues that the key product unit is [[NarrativeFateInteraction|fate]]: users need meaningful stories, relationships, and consequences, not only an observable multi-agent simulation.
- LIVO's characters are described as agents with occupations, goals, memory-like continuity, boundaries, and evolving choices, making [[AgenticCharacterSubjectivity]] central to the product thesis.
- [[KuaikanManhua]]'s content-company advantage is not simply its comic archive; Chen presents it as accumulated storytelling taste, IP judgment, creator relationships, and willingness to rebuild the format around AI.
- The 2018-2019 algorithm episode is used as evidence that content platforms may need [[ContentAestheticOverMetrics|aesthetic judgment over short-term metrics]] when low-quality traffic gains threaten long-term IP value.
- The 2021 cash-flow crisis changes Chen's view of ideals and business: commercialization, user willingness to pay, and reasonable profit become prerequisites for protecting creators, employees, and products.
- Chen's [[GameSpiritOrganization|game spirit]] describes a high-uncertainty operating stance: lower the fear of failure, keep curiosity active, make feedback less defensive through "yes and hope," and let the product self-evolve through group intelligence.
- [[RetroHumanistAIContent|Retro humanist AI content]] is Chen's answer to AI technology anxiety: AI may create new carriers, but content value still depends on whether creators try to move people.
