# Revisionist History Season 7: The Experiment Experience

## Summary

This brief [[RevisionistHistory]] trailer has [[MalcolmGladwell]] frame the seventh season around experiments: thought experiments, what-if scenarios, natural experiments, and constraint-removing “magic wand” experiments. It treats uncertainty and the possibility of error as reasons to test a question rather than as reasons to conceal a hypothesis.

The montage previews popular culture, science, historical locations, and a blinded sensory comparison without supplying the later episodes' evidence or conclusions. Its durable contribution is therefore methodological and promotional: it introduces [[ImpossibleExperimentDesign]] and an error-tolerant stance toward inquiry, while the full episodes must carry the evidentiary burden.

## Key Claims

- Experiments provide the seventh season's organizing idea across science, history, popular culture, and field reporting.
- The preview distinguishes thought experiments, what-if experiments, natural experiments, and “magic wand” experiments.
- Removing practical constraints lets experts state the study they most want to run and the uncertainty they most want to resolve.
- A hypothesis being wrong is presented as an acceptable part of inquiry rather than a reason not to articulate or test it.
- The season's premise is that unknowns outnumber settled answers and should be put to a test where possible.
- The trailer previews subjects and locations but does not provide enough context to evaluate their arguments or findings.
- The season was announced for June 30, 2022, with Pushkin Plus early access beginning June 23.
