## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode links [[HouYinLateHan|侯音]]'s unplanned Nanyang revolt with [[XiahouYuan|夏侯渊]]'s death at [[DingjunMountainBattleLateHan|定军山]] through a warning about confidence without judgment. Hou Yin detains [[DongliGunLateHan|东里衮]] over renewed levies, accepts [[ZongZiqingLateHan|宗子卿]]'s deceptive release proposal, and is killed when [[CaoRen|曹仁]] retakes the city; the episode says civilians are then massacred as collective deterrence.

The Hanzhong branch gives the missing mechanics between the stalled offensive in Hanji 1162 and the aftermath in Hanji 1164. [[LiuBei|刘备]] and [[FaZheng|法正]] shift the contest to Dingjun Mountain, pressure [[ZhangHeLateHan|张郃]], burn a position in Zouma Valley, and induce Xiahou Yuan to divide his force and expose himself while repairing defenses; [[HuangZhong|黄忠]] then attacks and kills him. [[DuXiLateHan|杜袭]] and [[GuoHuaiLateHan|郭淮]] rally the surviving army around Zhang He, preventing the commander's death from becoming immediate organizational collapse.

## Key Claims

- Heavy levies provide the immediate grievance for Hou Yin's seizure of Dongli Gun, but the episode portrays the revolt as lacking a viable political or military completion plan.
- Zong Ziqing exploits Hou Yin's uncertainty by presenting Dongli Gun's release as a way to gain popular support, then escapes with the released official to Cao Ren.
- Cao Ren kills Hou Yin after retaking the city; the episode says the subsequent massacre deters nearby counties, while also extending one leader's failed decision onto civilians.
- Liu Bei abandons the long Yangping standoff for Dingjun Mountain and uses repeated pressure against Zhang He's sector and a fire attack to force Cao-side redistribution.
- Xiahou Yuan reportedly sends half his force to support Zhang He and then takes only four hundred men to fight the fire and repair the abatis, creating the opening Fa Zheng identifies.
- Huang Zhong's attack kills Xiahou Yuan and the episode's unnamed Yizhou inspector, turning tactical dislocation into a campaign-level command loss.
- Du Xi and Guo Huai place Zhang He in command, restore morale, and adopt a set-back river defense intended to strike during a crossing; Liu Bei declines to cross after reading the danger.
