## Summary
This episode backfills the 118-119 CE layer immediately before Hanji 791, linking Deng-regency frontier pressure, Qiang-war finance, Deng-family privilege management, Yizhou pacification, and Western Regions recovery planning. Its main interpretive frame is that Ren Shang's real military achievement became politically fatal when he competed for credit with Deng Zun, a Deng-family commander closer to the regency power center. The episode also adds Yang Song's force-and-leniency campaign in Yizhou, Tang Tan's refusal to present an auspicious omen under outer-relative dominance, and Cao Zong and Suo Ban's prelude to the Western Regions crisis.

## Key Claims
- In 118 CE, disasters and rebellions across Qiang, Goguryeo, Xianbei, and the southwest show the Deng regency operating under a compound crisis rather than a single-front problem.
- [[RenShangLateHan|Ren Shang]] and [[MaXianLateHan|Ma Xian]] won a costly pursuit and battle against the Xianling Qiang leader Langmo, but Langmo's escape left credit and completion ambiguous.
- [[DengZunLateHan|Deng Zun]] converted surrender management and proxy assassination into the decisive court-recognized achievement, while Ren Shang's resentment and credit competition exposed him to charges of inflated kills and embezzlement.
- [[DengZhiLateHan|Deng Zhi]] punishing his wife and son Deng Feng for accepting Ren Shang's horse complicates a simple picture of Deng-family privilege by showing visible household discipline inside an otherwise powerful outer-relative bloc.
- [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Empress Dowager Deng]]'s schooling of royal and Deng-family children appears as institution-building but also as a source of suspicion because it gathered dynastic and outer-relative youth under her supervision.
- [[YangSongLateHan|Yang Song]]'s victory over Feng Li illustrates [[YizhouPacificationByLeniency|Yizhou pacification by leniency]]: battlefield success, generous treatment of defeated leaders, tribal submission, and punishment of abusive local officials worked together.
- The Western Regions material extends [[StrategicFrontierAbandonment|strategic frontier abandonment]] by showing Northern Xiongnu suzerainty before Han re-entry, Cao Zong's protection request, Suo Ban's Yiwu garrison, and Shule's succession-driven regional rebalancing.
- The modern workplace advice, summarized as not taking work or relationships that threaten superior-linked interests, maps the historical case into [[SuperiorCreditConflictRisk|superior credit conflict risk]].
