# 《资治通鉴·周纪》08丨一代名将吴起惨死（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] Zhou-annals branch by moving [[WuQi|吴起]] from battlefield leadership into [[WeiState|魏国]] court politics under [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]]. Its central synthesis is that a state cannot rely on terrain without political legitimacy, and that high achievement does not automatically make a minister suitable during a fragile succession.

## Key Claims
- [[SimaGuang|司马光]] places the famous West River conversation between [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]] and [[WuQi|吴起]] under [[ZhouAnWang|周安王]]'s fifteenth year.
- Wei Wuhou praises mountains and rivers as strategic treasure, while Wu Qi argues through [[VirtueOverNaturalBarriers]] that a state depends on "德" rather than on geographic barriers.
- Wu Qi's historical examples make terrain secondary to political order: even states with lakes, passes, and large territories can collapse if governance decays.
- The host flags a tension in Wu Qi's use of "德": the episode presents him as a harsh Legalist-style figure whose own moral reputation was contested, so "virtue" here may be a practical technique of rule rather than pure ethical self-cultivation.
- After the West River dialogue, the episode previews three later Wu Qi threads: conflict with [[TianWenWeiChancellor|田文]], departure from Wei, and later activity in [[ChuState|楚国]].
- When the Wei chancellorship becomes vacant after Wei Wuhou's accession, Wu Qi believes his military command, civil administration, treasury-strengthening, West River defense, and pressure on [[QinState|秦国]], [[HanState|韩国]], and [[ZhaoState|赵国]] make him stronger than Tian Wen.
- Tian Wen concedes Wu Qi's superior achievements but argues that a moment of young-ruler accession, mutual suspicion among senior ministers, and weak popular confidence requires stabilizing fit rather than maximal reforming talent.
- Wu Qi accepts Tian Wen's answer, making [[TransitionFitOverMerit]] the episode's main organizational-politics lesson: ability and contribution do not always decide who should hold power.
- After Tian Wen, [[GongshuWeiChancellor|公叔]] becomes chancellor and fears Wu Qi's record and ambition.
- Gongshu's servant designs a court trap: ask Wei Wuhou to offer a princess to Wu Qi, then stage Gongshu's princess-wife humiliating him so Wu Qi will reject the match and appear disloyal or unwilling to stay in Wei.
- The episode ends before Wu Qi's final decision, using the marriage proposal as a cliffhanger for his departure from Wei and later fate.

## Key Quotes
> "国家的宝藏是德，而不是天险" - the episode's summary of [[WuQi|吴起]]'s reply to [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]].

> "主少国疑" - [[TianWenWeiChancellor|田文]]'s diagnosis of why stability mattered more than Wu Qi's stronger resume.

> "功劳和适任并不总是一回事" - the episode's organizational reading of the chancellor dispute.
