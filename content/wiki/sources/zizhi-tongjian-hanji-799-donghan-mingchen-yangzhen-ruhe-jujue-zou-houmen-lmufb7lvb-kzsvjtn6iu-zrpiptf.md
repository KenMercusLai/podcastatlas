# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》799丨东汉名臣杨震，如何拒绝走后门？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills 123 CE under [[EmperorAnOfHan|汉安帝]] immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-800-yangzhen-zhisi-yi-chang-jianchen-dui-qingguan-de-jiaosha-lk9qcfs4xl-rjd-zpwdtsblybxa8|Hanji 800]]. It opens with source-scoped self-improvement advice, then follows [[ZhangQiaoLateHan|张乔]] suppressing the Maoniu Yi rebellion, [[ZhangDangLateHan|张珰]] and [[ChenZhongLateHan|陈忠]] opposing withdrawal from the Western Regions, and [[BanYongLateHan|班勇]] receiving the Liuzhong posting.

The main source contribution is an earlier [[YangZhenLateHan|杨震]] layer. After becoming taiwei, Yang refuses appointment requests from [[GengBaoGrandGeneralLateHan|耿宝]] on behalf of [[LiRunLateHan|李润]]'s elder brother and from [[YanXianLateHan|阎显]] for a close associate; he then criticizes [[WangShengPalaceActorLateHan|王圣]]'s mansion and the [[FanFengEunuchLateHan|樊丰]]/[[ZhouGuangLateHan|周广]]/[[XieYunLateHan|谢恽]] favorite network. The durable synthesis is that Emperor An's court can still accept prudent frontier advice while appointment channels and palace favor are already decaying before Yang Zhen's fatal Hanji 800 confrontation.

## Key Claims

- The opening advice about improving oneself to leave a poor social circle is listener guidance and remains source-scoped.
- In spring 123 CE, Maoniu Yi rebels are defeated by Yizhou inspector [[ZhangQiaoLateHan|张乔]].
- Northern Xiongnu and Cheshi incursions into Hexi prompt a court proposal to close Yumen Pass and Yangguan.
- Dunhuang administrator [[ZhangDangLateHan|张珰]] argues that abandoning the Western Regions would make [[HexiFourCommanderiesLateHan|河西四郡]] unable to stand alone, then offers upper, middle, and lower plans for military pressure, Liuzhong garrisoning, or limited fallback.
- [[ChenZhongLateHan|陈忠]] opposes abandonment because Shanshan and other states would have to attach to Northern Xiongnu, strengthening Xiongnu wealth, courage, and possible contact with Qiang forces.
- Emperor An accepts Chen Zhong's argument and appoints [[BanYongLateHan|班勇]] as Western Regions chief clerk with five hundred troops stationed at Liuzhong.
- Emperor An enfeoffs his wet nurse [[WangShengPalaceActorLateHan|王圣]] as Yewang Jun and later orders a large mansion built for her despite disaster, poverty, frontier war, and empty treasury.
- Yang Zhen refuses appointment requests from Geng Bao and Yan Xian because no imperial edict has authorized the recommended candidates, turning "走后门" into a procedural boundary.
- Yang Zhen's memorial criticizes Wang Sheng's construction and says Zhou Guang, Xie Yun, and favored palace actors solicit offices, accept bribes, and let barred corrupt officials occupy important posts.
- Chen Zhong recommends [[ZhouXieLateHan|周燮]] and [[FengLiangLateHan|冯良]] as learned and upright men, but both refuse the imperial summons by returning to their counties and claiming illness.

## Key Quotes

> "走后门" - title-level frame for Yang Zhen's refusal of appointment favors.

> "黑白混淆、清浊不分" - Yang Zhen's critique of corrupt appointment outcomes.

> "时机未到" - Zhou Xie's explanation for refusing office.
