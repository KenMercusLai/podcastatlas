## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens the eighth volume of [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] in Qin Er Shi's second year, carrying the Qin-collapse branch from the post-[[DazexiangUprising|大泽乡起义]] spread into simultaneous military reverses and local advances. [[LiuBang|刘邦]] wins around Huling and Xue, [[ZhouWenRebel|周文 / 周章]] is repeatedly defeated by [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] and dies, while [[WuGuang|吴广]] stalls before [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]] against [[LiYou|李由]]. Its central tension is [[TianZang|田臧]]'s killing of Wu Guang under a claimed military crisis and the source-scoped inference that [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] may have silently accepted the act because he punished no one and made Tian Zang upper general.

## Key Claims
- The episode frames Qin Er Shi's second year, 208-207 BCE, as a compressed field where many rebel figures appear and die quickly.
- [[LiuBang|刘邦]] defeats Qin forces around Huling after the Sishui commandery supervisor Ping first besieges him and then surrenders under persuasion from [[XiahouYing|夏侯婴]] and [[XiaoHe|萧何]].
- Liu Bang leaves [[YongChi|雍齿]] to hold [[FengCounty|丰邑 / 丰县]] and moves north toward Xue County, where [[CaoWushang|曹无伤]] captures and kills the Sishui commandery governor Zhuang.
- [[ZhouWenRebel|周文 / 周章]] retreats east after [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] defeats the westward Zhang Chu army, loses again at Caoyang and Mianchi, then kills himself as his force collapses.
- [[WuGuang|吴广]], as the second major [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚]] leader after [[ChenSheng|陈胜]], besieges [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]], a strategic route and granary-adjacent position tied to Qin's Sanchuan commandery.
- [[LiYou|李由]], son of [[LiSi|李斯]] and Sanchuan commandery governor, holds Yingyang against Wu Guang's repeated attacks.
- [[TianZang|田臧]] and other Zhang Chu generals argue that continuing the Yingyang siege will leave them trapped between the city and approaching Qin relief forces.
- The generals say Wu Guang is arrogant and poor at military command, then kill him by falsely claiming Chen Sheng's order and send his head to Chen Sheng.
- Chen Sheng is angry but does not punish Tian Zang; instead he grants him the Chu lingyin seal and appoints him upper general.
- The host presents Chen Sheng's possible prior knowledge or tacit approval as a dark-side inference, not as settled fact: the concrete evidence is the lack of punishment and the promotion after the killing.
- The truncated ending turns toward ruler technique and concentrated authority, but the transcript cuts off before its full argument can be established.

## Key Quotes
> "假借陈胜的命令" - the episode's wording for the immediate form of Tian Zang's killing of Wu Guang.

> "不但未被追责，反而被任命为上将军" - the host's reason for treating Chen Sheng's role as suspicious.
