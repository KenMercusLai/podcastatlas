# 《资治通鉴·周纪》10丨 以家族单位 有蚂蚁吃大象的精神（2）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[ZhouLiewang|周烈王]]'s fifth and sixth years through Han court assassination, Wei succession disorder, Qi official evaluation, and Chu/Song succession notices. Its strongest added value is historiographical and organizational: the host flags possible [[ChronicleChronologyDrift|chronology drift]] in [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]], then turns [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]]'s failure to appoint an heir and [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]]'s secret audit of local officials into reusable governance cases.

## Key Claims
- In 371 BCE, [[WeiState|魏国]] attacks [[ChuState|楚国]] and takes Luyang, while [[HanState|韩国]] enters crisis after [[YanZhongzi|严遂]] sends an assassin against [[HanGui|韩廆]] and [[HanAihou|韩哀侯]] dies protecting him.
- The episode treats the Han killing as a source-variant problem: [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] appears to fold the [[NieZheng|聂政]] / [[XiaLei|侠累]] assassination and Yan Sui's killing of Han Aihou into one event, while [[Shiji|《史记》]] and [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] keep them separate.
- [[HanYihou|韩懿侯]] succeeds Han Aihou, preserving Han's ruler sequence after the court killing.
- [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]] dies without clearly naming a successor, leaving [[LiangHuiWang|魏罃]] and [[GongzhongHuan|公中缓]] to fight for power; the episode says this probably belongs under Zhou Lie Wang's sixth year and uses it as [[SuccessionNonDesignationRisk|succession non-designation risk]].
- The host generalizes from Wei to imperial and modern organizational succession: a ruler or founder who will not settle an heir can leave the organization to resolve the question through fear, faction, or civil conflict.
- [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]]'s reported visit to the Zhou court is treated as likely misplaced because the surrounding chronology still appears to be in a Tian-family Qi Huan Gong era; this becomes a broader warning about thin and damaged early Warring States records.
- In the same sixth-year cluster, [[ZhaoState|赵国]] attacks Qi to Juan, while Wei defeats Zhao at Huai.
- The Qi official-evaluation story makes [[IndependentOfficialAudit|independent official audit]] central: Qi Wei Wang secretly checks local reality before rewarding [[JimoDafu|即墨大夫]] and punishing [[AyiDafu|阿邑大夫]] and flattering courtiers.
- The source says Qi's officials become afraid to fake performance after the Jimo/Ayi decisions, and Qi broadly turns from disorder toward strength.
- [[ChuSuwang|楚肃王]] dies without a son and is succeeded by his younger brother [[ChuXuanwang|楚宣王]], while [[SongPigong|宋辟公]] dies and [[SongTicheng|宋剔成]] succeeds.

## Key Quotes
> "齐威王来朝，天下以此益贤威王" - the [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] line the host quotes before challenging the chronology.

> "皇权带来的快感只能独享，不能分配" - the host's explanation for why rulers may resist naming successors.

> "不敢再弄虚作假" - the episode's summary of how Qi officials react after the Jimo and Ayi punishments.

## Contradictions
- The Han assassination is explicitly variant-sensitive. The wiki preserves both possibilities: [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] links [[NieZheng|聂政]], [[XiaLei|侠累]], and [[HanAihou|韩哀侯]], while this episode says [[Shiji|《史记》]] and [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] treat Yan Sui's killing of Han Aihou as a separate later event.
- The Qi Wei Wang material is recorded as a source claim plus chronology problem. The episode itself says the event is probably misdated in the Zhou Lie Wang sixth-year position, so the wiki does not use it to settle a clean Qi timeline.
- The Wei succession notice is likewise marked as possibly misplaced by the host, who says it should probably fall in 370 BCE rather than the immediately preceding annalistic slot.
