# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》894丨一场逃亡引发的大屠杀

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 169 CE party-prohibition terror immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-895-xuexing-tudao-xia-de-gurou-qingshen-llsplyyrjxhqkpi2aiuqzulgm6w3|Hanji 895]]. After a brief opening on female self-building and intimate-relationship energy, the episode turns to [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s court, where party-figure accusations, private vendettas, and eunuch power convert political labeling into executions, exile, dismissal, and family ruin.

The historical core follows [[GuoTaiLateHan|郭泰]] grieving the destruction of famous scholars before retreating to teaching, and [[ZhangJianLateHan|张俭]] fleeing after [[HouLanLateHan|侯览]]'s accusation. [[LiDuLateHan|李笃]] shelters Zhang Jian, [[MaoQinLateHan|毛钦]] chooses not to arrest him after a coded exchange, and the route through [[XiziRanLateHan|戏子然]] helps Zhang escape beyond the frontier while leaving many helpers and relatives exposed to punishment.

## Key Claims

- The 169 CE party-prohibition case kills more than one hundred people, exiles wives and children, and leaves roughly six or seven hundred people executed, exiled, or removed from office.
- Eunuch-side accusation expands the party label to respected scholars and moral public figures, while private enemies use the purge to settle old resentments.
- Guo Tai, a leading scholar known for judging talent, survives because he avoids fierce political speech, but he treats the destruction of名士 as evidence that the Eastern Han order is nearing exhaustion.
- Guo Tai's response is constrained withdrawal: he closes his door, teaches thousands of students, and dies at forty-two in the same Jian'ning year.
- Zhang Jian is forced into flight after Hou Lan's accusation, and his reputation leads many households to shelter him despite knowing the risk.
- Li Du's handling of Mao Qin turns refuge into a moral test: both men understand that arresting Zhang Jian is legally available, while letting him go preserves a share of仁义.
- Zhang Jian eventually escapes through Donglai, Beihai, Yuyang, and beyond the frontier, but the sheltering chain brings lethal punishment to many helpers and their families.
