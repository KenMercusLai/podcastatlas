# 《资治通鉴·周纪》48丨君王为啥不能传位宰相

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode fills the missing middle between [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-47-yanwangkuai-de-lixiangzhuyi-lhgl6rqkv9nonzxc9xqquzzlz5pr|Zhouji 47]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-49-mengzi-yue-wo-jiao-ni-zhiguo-ni-que-ba-wo-dang-huaping-lhy14pbf9thcfdiaulcmf8dwcef8|Zhouji 49]]: [[YanWangKuai|燕王哙]]'s transfer of authority to [[ZiZhi|子之]] turns into [[YanState|燕国]] civil disorder, [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]] intervention, and the deaths of both Yan Wang Kuai and Zi Zhi. The source uses [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s language of 礼 to argue that political order depends on recognizable ruler-minister and father-son roles, making the case a concrete sequel to [[RuleBreakingAbdication|破坏规则式禅让]]. It also records the transition from [[ZhouShenliangwang|周慎靓王]] to [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]] and three early Zhou Nan Wang notices showing continued [[QinState|秦国]] pressure on [[YiquState|义渠]], [[WeyState|卫国]], and [[HanState|韩国]].

## Key Claims
- In 315 BCE, Zhou Shenliang Wang dies and his son succeeds as Zhou Nan Wang, the final Zhou Son of Heaven and the longest-reigning Zhou ruler in the source's framing.
- The episode previews Zhou Nan Wang's later debt-funded anti-Qin attempt, linking his first appearance to the later [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]] decline story.
- After Zi Zhi controls Yan for three years, Yan falls into disorder because appointment power and dynastic legitimacy no longer align.
- [[ShiBei|市被]] and the Yan crown prince move against Zi Zhi after Qi Xuan Wang promises support in the language of restoring ruler-minister and father-son order.
- The anti-Zi-Zhi move fails, Shi Bei turns against the crown prince, and the prince's flight leaves Yan's crisis deeper rather than resolved.
- Qi Xuan Wang then sends [[ZhangZiQiGeneral|张子]] with Qi forces into Yan; Yan soldiers do not resist and the capital opens to Qi.
- Qi captures Zi Zhi, kills him brutally, and also kills Yan Wang Kuai, turning Yan Wang Kuai's reputation-seeking abdication into personal, familial, and state disaster.
- The episode presents Sima Guang's "礼" not as empty ceremony but as [[LiAsPoliticalOrder|a rule system of ranked responsibilities]] that keeps authority legible.
- The Qin-side notices show Qin attacking Yiqu, suppressing unrest connected to Wey people at [[QuwoWarringStates|曲沃]], and defeating Han at [[AnmenWarringStates|岸门]] before [[HanXiangwang|太子韩仓]] is sent as a hostage.
- The ending sets up Zhouji 49: after occupying Yan under a moral pretext, Qi Xuan Wang needs a justification for keeping it and turns toward [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]].

## Key Quotes
> "燕王哙失职于礼" - the episode's compact judgment on Yan Wang Kuai's failure.

> "子之德不配位" - the paired judgment on Zi Zhi's unfitness for the authority he accepts.

> "君君臣臣，父父子子" - the Confucian role formula used to explain the rule problem.

## Contradictions
- No direct contradiction found in the Yan sequence; this episode bridges Zhouji 47's setup and Zhouji 49's conquered-Yan aftermath.
- Source-scoped tension: this episode's wording says Yiqu had previously been destroyed or incorporated as a Qin county before rebelling, while existing wiki synthesis from [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-35-lishishang-diyi-qibing-jiaofu-shi-ta-lnxgnu1czkl-ftfb3gq9kzxwnxdk|Zhouji 35]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-85-xuantaihou-weihe-sitong-30-nian-hou-ansha-le-laoqingren-lg7zy6vqtdjcufou4-mox5qkg2ct5|Zhouji 85]] treats Qin's earlier Yiqu victory as partial and Yiqu's final destruction as later.
- The Quwo notice is kept source-scoped because earlier [[QuwoWarringStates|曲沃]] entries frame Quwo primarily as a Wei-Qin frontier place, while this episode connects Quwo to unrest among Wey people.
