## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the first 75 CE [[GengGongEasternHan|耿恭]] siege layer before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-745-donghan-zui-xuexing-zhi-zhan-zhengjiu-dabing-genggong-lhg3pppaovl5mxawgmj-dgjgth4d|Hanji 745]]. It follows [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|北匈奴]] attacking [[CheshiHouKingdomLateHan|车师后王国]], Geng Gong using poisoned arrows, storm timing, desperate well digging, and water display to survive at Jinpu and a Shule fortress, then shifts to [[EmperorMingOfHan|汉明帝]]'s death and [[EmperorZhangOfHan|汉章帝]]'s accession.

The durable synthesis is that frontier survival and court succession are interleaved: Geng Gong's tactical defense buys time but does not solve the rescue problem, while Mingdi's frugal death instructions, outer-relative restraint, and [[LiuCangDongpingKing|刘苍]]'s anti-tomb-city remonstrance make early Zhangdi rule inherit a restraint standard before later rescue and Ma-family debates.

## Key Claims

- In 75 CE, Northern Xiongnu destroys Geng Gong's first 300-person relief detachment, kills Cheshi Hou king Ande, and attacks Jinpu.
- Geng Gong combines poisoned arrows, prophetic messaging, storm timing, and a sortie to make the Xiongnu retreat from Jinpu.
- After moving to a Shule fortress, Geng faces a siege in which water is cut off and the defenders survive extreme thirst before a deep well produces spring water.
- Pouring water from the wall turns recovered supply into psychological signaling and prompts another Xiongnu withdrawal.
- Mingdi's death interrupts the frontier crisis and leaves Geng Gong's isolated force waiting while Zhangdi consolidates court order.
- Mingdi is framed as a frugal and restraint-minded ruler who blocks consort-family advancement and orders a simplified burial.
- Zhangdi's opening rule honors Empress Ma, rewards Yang Ren's palace-gate discipline, accepts Liu Cang's anti-tomb-city remonstrance, and appoints senior ministers including Fifth Lun.

## Key Quotes

> "汉家神箭，中创者必有异" - Geng Gong's psychological frame for poisoned arrows.

> "榨马粪中的汁液来饮用" - the source's extreme survival detail from the Shule-fortress water crisis.

> "不要为他兴建祭庙" - Mingdi's death instruction as summarized by the episode.
