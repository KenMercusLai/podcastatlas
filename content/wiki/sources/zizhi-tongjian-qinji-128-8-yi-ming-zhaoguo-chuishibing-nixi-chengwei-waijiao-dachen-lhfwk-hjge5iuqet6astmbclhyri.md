## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the Qin-collapse field after [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-7-liubang-he-xiangyu-youhe-butong-llwcqatndck4zita2piufwhjpxp1|Qinji 128-7]] by widening the stage beyond [[LiuBang|刘邦]] and [[XiangYu|项羽]]: [[TianDanQinRebel|田儋]] restores [[QiState|齐国]], [[HanGuang|韩广]] is made Yan king, [[WuChen|武臣]] is captured by [[YanState|燕国]], [[ZhouShiRebel|周市]] restores [[WeiState|魏国]] through [[WeiJiu|魏咎]], and [[WeyState|卫国]] finally disappears under [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]]. Its central story is [[ZhaoServantEnvoy|赵厮养卒]] using [[LowStatusStrategicPersuasion|低位者战略说服]] to make Yan release Wu Chen by arguing that killing him would empower [[ZhangEr|张耳]] and [[ChenYu|陈馀]] with a punitive-war pretext. The core synthesis is that the anti-Qin uprising is now less one rebellion than a rapidly fragmenting restoration market where old royal names, local strongmen, and low-status actors all convert Qin's collapse into political opportunity.

## Key Claims
- [[XiangLiang|项梁]] and [[XiangYu|项羽]] leave the Kuaiji commandery coup behind and begin using commandery troops to subdue nearby counties that do not join them.
- [[TianDanQinRebel|田儋]] uses a staged request involving his household slave to draw out and kill the Di county magistrate, then claims Qi royal descent and becomes Qi king.
- Tian Dan's move makes old [[QiState|齐国]] restoration part of the same anti-Qin wave as [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]], [[LiuBang|刘邦]] in Pei, and the Xiang line in Kuaiji.
- [[WuChen|武臣]], originally sent by [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] with [[ZhangEr|张耳]] and [[ChenYu|陈馀]] as assistants, has already become Zhao king and now sends [[HanGuang|韩广]] to take Yan territory.
- Yan-region notables install Han Guang as Yan king; Han Guang initially worries that his mother is still in [[Handan|邯郸]], but accepts after being told [[ZhaoState|赵国]] is too pressured by Qin and Zhang Chu to retaliate.
- Zhao later safely returns Han Guang's mother and family, showing that hostage-family risk can be managed without restoring command unity.
- Wu Chen reacts angrily to Han Guang's kingship and moves along the Yan-Zhao frontier, but Yan forces capture him and demand Zhao land for his release.
- Zhao envoys repeatedly fail and are killed before [[ZhaoServantEnvoy|赵厮养卒]], a low-status camp servant responsible for menial military support work, volunteers to go.
- The servant envoy does not plead for mercy. He argues that [[ZhangEr|张耳]] and [[ChenYu|陈馀]] may privately benefit if Yan kills Wu Chen, because they can then attack Yan under the banner of avenging Zhao's king.
- The argument persuades Yan's commander and [[HanGuang|韩广]], so Yan releases [[WuChen|武臣]].
- The episode treats "养卒说燕" and "左提右挈" as idiomatic residues of this unnamed low-status actor's brief historical appearance.
- [[ZhouShiRebel|周市]] captures old Wei territory for Zhang Chu but refuses to make himself king, insisting that the former Wei royal house gives the restoration stronger moral and political standing.
- [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] eventually releases [[WeiJiu|魏咎]] to Wei territory after repeated requests; Wei Jiu becomes Wei king, while Zhou Shi serves as chancellor.
- [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] deposes [[WeyJunJiao|卫君角]] as a commoner, ending [[WeyState|卫国]], which the episode frames as a very long-lived but already hollow remnant polity.

## Key Quotes

> "左提右挈" - idiom the episode connects to the same persuasion story.

> "在道义上站得住脚" - the episode's explanation of why Zhou Shi insists on restoring a Wei royal descendant.
