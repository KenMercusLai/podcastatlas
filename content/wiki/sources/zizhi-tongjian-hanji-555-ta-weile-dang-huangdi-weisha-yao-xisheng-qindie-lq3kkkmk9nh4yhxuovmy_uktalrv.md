# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》555丨他为了当皇帝，为啥要牺牲亲爹？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Suihe 1 / 8 BCE after [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] has designated the Dingtao king [[HanAidi|刘欣 / 后来的汉哀帝]] as crown prince. It follows Chengdi's compensatory arrangement for Liu Xin's birth-father line, Liu Xin's disputed thank-you memorial, the punishment of [[ZhaoXuanWesternHan|赵璇]], and the controlled access granted to [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]] while excluding [[DingJiWesternHan|丁姬]].

The durable synthesis is that adoption-line succession required more than formal designation. [[YanCongWesternHan|严从]] understands that Liu Xin must not publicly act as a grateful member of his birth family once he has become Chengdi's son, while Zhao Xuan treats thanks as ordinary filial gratitude. Chengdi's anger shows that the court reads birth-family attachment as a succession risk, and [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]'s later intervention softens but does not erase the boundary.

## Key Claims

- Chengdi's grant of Dingtao succession continuity through Liu Jing is not just kindness; it compensates Liu Xin's birth line while marking that Liu Xin himself must belong to Chengdi's line.
- Yan Cong tells Liu Xin not to thank Chengdi because a crown prince adopted into the imperial line should not publicly treat birth-family benefit as his own interest.
- Zhao Xuan advises the opposite from a gratitude and filial-feeling frame, and Liu Xin follows Zhao Xuan because of Zhao's higher rank.
- Chengdi's anger at the thank-you memorial reveals that he wants the birth-family tie cut politically, not merely managed emotionally.
- Zhao Xuan's demotion and [[ShiDanWesternHan|施丹]]'s appointment as taifu distinguish loyalty from political judgment.
- Chengdi allows Fu Taihou and Ding Ji to live in the capital at the Dingtao residence but initially forbids them from seeing Liu Xin.
- Wang Zhengjun persuades Chengdi to let Fu Taihou visit as a wet-nurse or foster-care figure, while Ding Ji remains excluded because she did not raise Liu Xin.
- The episode uses [[EmpressXuWesternHan|废后许氏]] only as a next-episode bridge, not as a fully developed case here.

## Key Quotes

> "太子已为成帝后" - the ritual-political premise behind cutting Liu Xin off from his birth kin.

> "不能再顾念原本的骨肉亲人" - the source's summary of Chengdi's succession-boundary logic.
