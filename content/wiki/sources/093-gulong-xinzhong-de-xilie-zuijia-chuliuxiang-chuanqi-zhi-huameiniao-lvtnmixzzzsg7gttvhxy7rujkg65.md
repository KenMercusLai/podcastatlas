# 093 古龙心中的系列最佳：《楚留香传奇之画眉鸟》

## Summary

This [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] episode retells [[GuLong|古龙]]'s [[PaintingThrushGuLong|《画眉鸟》]] as a sequence of hostage leverage, assassination attempts, a six-person sword formation, and a confrontation with [[ShuiMuYinJi|水母阴姬]]. Its strongest craft reading is [[ChuLiuxiang|楚留香]]'s disruption of a supposedly invincible formation by inducing one swordsman to accept an extra weapon, while its strongest epistemic reversal lets 柳无眉 and 水母阴姬 speak sincerely from incompatible beliefs. The hosts praise the novel's suspense, setup, and tragic restraint but sharply qualify the skin-breathing and “death kiss” mechanics of the final underwater fight.

## Key Claims

- [[PaintingThrushGuLong|《画眉鸟》]] makes 李玉函 and 柳无眉's hospitality, 苏蓉蓉's whereabouts, repeated attacks, and 拥翠山庄's reputation parts of one coercive design rather than disconnected adventures.
- [[HabitTriggeredSystemDisruption|Habit-triggered system disruption]] explains the six-sword formation's defeat: 楚留香 does not overpower the coordinated system but introduces a seventh sword that one member accepts reflexively, breaking the formation's expected state.
- The solution is prepared through 楚留香's unusual grip, the older swordsman's established two-sword habit, 李玉函's weaker participation, and the simultaneous effort to free the hostages.
- 柳无眉's fear of poisoning motivates attempted murder, while 李玉函 turns love into permission to spend family prestige, wealth, and other people's lives on her survival.
- [[SincereFalseBelief|Sincere false belief]] allows 柳无眉 to report real suffering and 水母阴姬 to deny a real poison without requiring either statement to be a deliberate lie; psychological suggestion remains the episode's interpretation rather than a proved diagnosis.
- [[ShuiMuYinJi|水母阴姬]] joins overwhelming force to emotional vulnerability, but the final underwater victory depends on biological mechanisms the hosts find much less credible than the sword-formation solution.
- 柳无眉's death and 李玉函's refusal to acknowledge it preserve the pair's tragedy without erasing their responsibility for kidnapping and attempted murder.

## Key Quotes
