# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》611丨历史无名的孙宝，有多爷们？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills a 2 CE [[HanPingdi|汉平帝]] court scene immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-612-jiemi-gudai-yingxiao-daka-wangmang-llghwpfe5c0ck91vkldmfdjowmqi|Hanji 612]]'s disaster-relief image-management layer. It centers [[SunBaoWesternHan|孙宝]], the Grand Minister of Agriculture, who publicly objects when [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]], [[MaGongWesternHan|马宫]], and other ministers use a reported yellow-dragon omen to praise [[WangMang|王莽]] as Duke-of-Zhou-like.

The durable synthesis is that Wang Mang's praise environment was already risky before the later petition, Nine Bestowments, and coercive nonparticipation cases. Sun Bao names the gap between court celebration and material distress by saying Han's treasure and gold are exhausted and common people lack clothing and food; [[ChenChongXinOfficial|陈崇]] then uses a filial-duty accusation about Sun Bao's mother to remove him, making direct truth-telling vulnerable to procedural retaliation.

## Key Claims

- In 2 CE, a reported yellow dragon in the Yangtze becomes material for ministers to praise Wang Mang's virtue and compare him to the Duke of Zhou.
- Sun Bao objects in public, arguing that even Zhougong and Shaogong could disagree and that disagreement did not erase their virtue.
- Sun Bao's core remonstrance shifts attention from omen praise to fiscal and livelihood distress: depleted treasury resources and commoners lacking clothing and food.
- Zhen Han stops the discussion after Sun Bao's speech, showing how quickly open disagreement can become dangerous in a praise-heavy court.
- Chen Chong impeaches Sun Bao over a family-duty issue after Sun Bao's mother falls ill en route to Chang'an and remains with Sun Bao's younger brother.
- Sun Bao accepts the accusation rather than expecting a usable defense, is removed from office, and later dies at home.
- The episode also records Pingdi's change from Liu Jizi to Liu Kan, Wang Chong's fear-based request to resign, and Wang Mang's continued personnel and kinship-reward image building.

## Key Quotes

> "百姓困乏，无有衣食" - Sun Bao's contrast between court praise and material distress.

> "这是《国王的新衣》呀" - the host's analogy for Sun Bao exposing the praise environment.
