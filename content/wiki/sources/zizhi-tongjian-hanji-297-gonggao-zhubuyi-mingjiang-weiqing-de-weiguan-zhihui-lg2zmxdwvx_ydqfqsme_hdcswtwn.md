# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》297｜功高主不疑，名将卫青的为官智慧

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the direct command-law prehistory behind [[SuJianWesternHan|苏建]]'s later redemption and restoration. After [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]] wins his own engagement but learns that Su Jian's wing has been destroyed following [[ZhaoXinWesternHan|赵信]]'s surrender to [[Xiongnu|匈奴]], advisers divide over whether the returned commander should be executed to display authority.

Wei Qing chooses neither acquittal nor immediate execution: he imprisons Su Jian and sends him to [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] for judgment. The episode interprets this as bounded command authority—preserving military accountability while refusing to use a subordinate's death as personal theater—and then generalizes the case into a distinction between position-based authority and authority earned through judgment, character, and willing followership.

## Key Claims

- After winning his own engagement, Wei Qing checks the other formations rather than treating a local victory as complete situational success.
- The episode says Su Jian's roughly three thousand troops are trapped by a much larger Xiongnu force, fight for a day, and collapse after Zhao Xin surrenders; Su Jian alone returns to camp.
- Su Jian's lost force exposes him to capital military punishment, but Wei Qing also recognizes that the defeat involved enemy concentration, Zhao Xin's defection, and the larger command deployment rather than simple cowardice.
- 议郎周霸 urges execution because Wei Qing has not yet killed a subordinate commander and could thereby display military authority.
- [[RenAnWesternHan|任安]] and the officers arguing for restraint say Su Jian fought until his force was exhausted, returned without defecting, and should not be killed in a way that would deter later defeated officers from coming back.
- Wei Qing says his imperial appointment already gives him authority and that, despite possessing field execution power, his favored position makes private exercise of that power especially sensitive.
- Wei Qing imprisons Su Jian and returns him to Han Wudi for final judgment, preserving both accountability and a higher review channel.
- The episode presents the choice as an example of authority that combines formal office with judgment, ability, character, and influence rather than relying on fear alone.

## Key Quotes

> “不患无威，而霸说我以明威，甚失臣意。” - Wei Qing's rejection, as rendered by the episode, of killing Su Jian merely to display authority.

> “虽古名将，不过也。” - the episode's quoted appraisal of Wei Qing's handling of the case.

## Connections

- [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]] - commander who preserves punishment and review while refusing immediate execution as authority theater.
- [[SuJianWesternHan|苏建]] - defeated right general whose return, imprisonment, and referral to the emperor form the central case.
- [[RenAnWesternHan|任安]] - adviser associated with the argument that Su Jian's hard fighting and return should count against immediate execution.
- [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] - ruler to whom Wei Qing returns the final judgment rather than treating delegated power as unlimited personal discretion.
- [[ZhaoXinWesternHan|赵信]] and [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] - defection and opposing force that help explain the destruction of Su Jian's command.
- [[CommandDisciplineAuthority|军法威严]] - concept qualified by the difference between visible accountability and execution performed mainly to display personal authority.
- [[HighMeritThreatManagement|功高不震主]] - adjacent political pattern because Wei Qing treats high imperial favor as a reason for greater restraint in delegated power.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-299-2-xihan-caizheng-da-weiji-hanwudi-ruhe-gao-qian-fqf4vv3uhemqey3qqk6cdiht_2cc|Hanji 299-2]] supplies the next stage: Su Jian is spared through redemption, reduced to commoner status, and later restored as governor of Dai Commandery.
- The episode's claim that Wei Qing “saved” Su Jian is a counterfactual interpretation: referral preserved the possibility of survival, but Han Wudi's later judgment and redemption mechanism also determined the outcome.
- The exact force sizes, battlefield geometry, the role and title of 郝贤, Su Jian's escape mechanics, the advisers' offices and wording, Wei Qing's legal authority, Su Jian's palace-guard role, and the causal weight of each factor remain episode-attributed pending primary-text and specialist comparison.
- The distinction between “法定权威” and “自然权威,” the Drucker attribution, and the modern-management application are the host's interpretive framework rather than ancient institutional terminology.
