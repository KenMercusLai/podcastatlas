# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1069丨陈琳讨曹檄文名震天下，为何最终投降曹操？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows the aftermath of [[YuanTan|袁谭]]'s defeat in Jian'an 10 / 205 CE. [[WangXiuLateHan|王修]] asks [[CaoCao|曹操]] to let him bury Yuan Tan, then refuses to execute [[GuanTongLateHan|管统]] because Guan Tong's holdout can be reclassified as old-lord loyalty rather than unforgivable resistance.

The second half centers [[ChenLinLateHan|陈琳]]. After writing [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s famous anti-Cao proclamation before the [[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]], Chen Lin later surrenders to Cao Cao; Cao Cao questions the attack on his father and grandfather, accepts Chen Lin's "arrow on the string" explanation, and keeps using his literary skill. The episode's core contribution is that Cao Cao turns hostile reputation, old loyalty, and enemy rhetoric into a public talent-absorption signal.

## Key Claims

- The opening leadership frame defines good leadership through warm communication, forceful involvement, and specific feedback, then uses Cao Cao's personnel handling as the historical case.
- After Yuan Tan's death at Nanpi, Wang Xiu requests permission to collect and bury the body, and Cao Cao grants it rather than treating mourning for a defeated enemy as rebellion.
- Cao Cao orders Wang Xiu to attack and kill Guan Tong, but Wang Xiu captures him, removes his restraints, and argues that Guan Tong should be spared as a loyal old-retainer figure.
- Cao Cao pardons Guan Tong and brings Wang Xiu into the sikong office, making old-lord loyalty and new-regime service compatible enough for post-victory settlement.
- After taking Yuan Tan's territory, Cao Cao accepts advice to recruit notable people from [[QingzhouLateHan|青州]], [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]], [[YouzhouLateHan|幽州]], and [[BingzhouLateHan|并州]].
- The episode returns to Chen Lin's pre-Guandu proclamation, emphasizing its attacks on Cao Cao's family background, conduct, violence, tomb-robbing accusation, and public loyalty claims.
- Chen Lin's later surrender works because he acknowledges the offense while assigning it to compelled service under Yuan Shao; Cao Cao treats the writing as usable talent rather than only private insult.
- The episode reads Cao Cao's pardon and appointment of Chen Lin as a power move: a victor can convert the defeated camp's sharpest text-worker into his own instrument.

## Key Quotes

> "箭在弦上，不得不发" - Chen Lin's explanation for writing the anti-Cao proclamation.

> "陈琳的笔从此为曹操所有" - the episode's closing interpretation of Cao Cao's talent conversion.
