# 《资治通鉴·周纪》33丨历史第一爽文男主角苏秦

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[SuQin|苏秦]] immediately after the completed [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] tour in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-32-shishang-shouwei-lianheguo-mishuzhang-suqin-lgfkk45yq1c0yblequ-d1t7mol3|Zhouji 32]], focusing on his triumphant return through [[Luoyi|洛阳 / 洛邑]]. Using [[Shiji|《史记》]] and [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]], it contrasts his earlier family humiliation with the later scene where relatives dare not look directly at him, making [[QianjuHougongReversal|前倨后恭]] the social meaning of the comeback. The episode also records Su Qin repaying old help, catalogs idioms attached to his story, and ends by flagging a source tension between Shiji's "fifteen years" claim and the [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] sequel.

## Key Claims
- The episode keeps the frame at 333 BCE, Zhou Xian Wang's thirty-sixth year, while continuing the source-scoped Su Qin chronology already treated as [[ChronicleChronologyDrift|编年错位]].
- [[Shiji|《史记》]] gives the dramatic triumphant-return scene: after Su Qin's hezong success, [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]] sends people to clear the road and receive him as he passes through [[Luoyi|洛阳 / 洛邑]].
- [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] supplies the more detailed low-point contrast: after repeated failed lobbying of [[YingSi|秦惠文王]], Su Qin returns poor, worn out, and ashamed, while his wife keeps weaving, his sister-in-law refuses food, and his parents ignore him.
- [[QianjuHougongReversal|前倨后恭]] is presented as the episode's core social observation: the same relatives who neglected Su Qin in poverty become deferential once he has rank, wealth, and public recognition.
- Su Qin's sister-in-law's apology is treated as brutally clear evidence that kinship behavior is not insulated from status and money.
- Su Qin's reflection that a small estate near Luoyang might have made him settle down recasts his rise as pressure-driven: the lack of comfort helped force his later breakthrough.
- The "hundred cash / hundred gold" repayment story makes Su Qin's old borrowing and later gratitude part of the comeback narrative rather than a pure revenge fantasy.
- The episode treats idioms such as "侧目而视," "前倨后恭," and "不可同日而语" as reception evidence: Su Qin's story survives not only as diplomacy but as language, allusion, and moral shorthand.
- The closing problem is historiographical: [[Shiji|《史记》]] says Qin troops did not dare leave [[HanguPass|函谷关]] for fifteen years after hezong, but [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] soon records renewed Qin military action and the six-state alliance's collapse.

## Key Quotes
> "前倨而后恭" - Su Qin's rebuke to his sister-in-law in the homecoming story.

> "侧目不敢仰视" - the family posture that dramatizes fear and deference after his rise.

> "秦兵不敢窥函谷关十五年" - the Shiji-style efficacy claim the episode treats as a coming source problem.

## Contradictions
- The episode flags a source tension rather than resolving it: [[Shiji|《史记》]]'s claim that Qin troops did not dare leave [[HanguPass|函谷关]] for fifteen years after Su Qin's [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] success conflicts with the coming [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] sequence in which Qin soon fights again and the alliance collapses. This extends the existing [[ChronicleChronologyDrift|编年错位]] caution around Su Qin rather than creating a new settled chronology.
