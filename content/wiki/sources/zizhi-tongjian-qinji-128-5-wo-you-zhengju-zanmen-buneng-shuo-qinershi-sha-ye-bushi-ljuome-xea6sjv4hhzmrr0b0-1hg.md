## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows the anti-Qin revolt after [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-3-chensheng-wuguang-zudui-chenggong-locnaq-fo1fztyud5nghwkteams|Qinji 128-3]], showing how [[KuaiChe|蒯彻]] helps [[WuChen|武臣]] win cities by political surrender, how [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] misreads [[ZhouWenRebel|周文]]'s advance as proof that Qin is collapsing, and how [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] still has usable capital-defense capacity. The title's claim rests on Hu Hai's earlier recruitment and training of fifty thousand brave soldiers near [[Xianyang|咸阳]], plus his acceptance of [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]]'s emergency proposal to arm pardoned [[Lishan|骊山]] convicts. The second half turns from Qin response to rebel fragmentation: [[ZhangEr|张耳]] and [[ChenYu|陈馀]] help Wu Chen become Zhao king, then urge him to expand Zhao rather than attack Qin, making [[AntiQinCoalitionFragmentation|反秦阵营诸侯化分裂]] visible almost immediately.

## Key Claims
- [[KuaiChe|蒯彻]] persuades [[WuChen|武臣]] that battle is not the only way to take territory; letters, seals, and modeled rewards can make other cities surrender after one visible case.
- Wu Chen sends Kuai Che with vehicles, cavalry, and noble seals to receive Xu Gong, and the surrender creates a [[PoliticalSurrenderCascade|招降示范级联]] across more than thirty Yan and Zhao-region cities.
- [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] becomes careless after sending [[ZhouWenRebel|周文]] west, despite [[KongFu|孔鲋]] warning that military planning should rely on one's own defensibility rather than the enemy's failure to arrive.
- Zhou Wen reaches [[HanguPass|函谷关]] with a large gathered force and advances close to [[Xianyang|咸阳]], but the episode stresses that his army is huge without being comparably trained or hardened.
- The episode complicates the simple "Qin Er Shi was useless" view: in 208 BCE, [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] had already recruited fifty thousand brave soldiers into Xianyang and trained them in mounted archery.
- [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] proposes that nearby emergency recruitment is too slow, so the state should pardon and arm the people laboring at [[Lishan|骊山]]; this becomes [[LishanConvictMobilization|骊山刑徒应急动员]].
- Zhang Han's force defeats the Chu army and forces Zhou Wen to flee, showing that early rebel momentum does not equal the ability to beat trained Qin capital forces in a hard fight.
- After Zhou Wen's retreat and news that Chen Sheng kills returning commanders on slander, [[ZhangEr|张耳]] and [[ChenYu|陈馀]] urge Wu Chen to declare himself Zhao king.
- [[CaiCi|蔡赐]] persuades Chen Sheng not to murder Wu Chen's family while Qin remains undefeated; Chen Sheng instead keeps the family near the palace, recognizes Zhao, and orders Wu Chen to move against [[HanguPass|函谷关]] and Wu Pass.
- Zhang Er and Chen Yu then argue that Chen Sheng's recognition is only expedient: if Zhang Chu defeats Qin, Zhao will later be brought under command.
- Wu Chen follows their advice to attack Yan, Dai, Henei, Changshan, and Shangdang instead of moving west against Qin, turning the earlier [[SixStateRestorationAntiQinStrategy|六国复国式反秦策略]] into a self-strengthening Zhao route.
- The host emphasizes a historiographical cut: [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] omits a [[Shiji|《史记》]] motive that Zhang Er and Chen Yu resented Chen Sheng for not using their plan and only appointing them as commandants. The omission shifts moral pressure away from Zhang Er and Chen Yu and toward Chen Sheng.
- The closing introduces [[LiuBang|刘邦]] as the next major line: a forty-eight-year-old Pei County man with no grand background and only a small group of followers at the start.

## Key Quotes
> "不能说秦二世啥也不是" - the episode's title-level correction to a flat incompetence reading of Hu Hai.

> "用兵不应寄望敌人不来攻" - Kong Fu's warning as rendered in the source summary.

> "张耳、陈馀怨陈王不用其策" - the Shiji motive the episode says Zizhi Tongjian removes.
