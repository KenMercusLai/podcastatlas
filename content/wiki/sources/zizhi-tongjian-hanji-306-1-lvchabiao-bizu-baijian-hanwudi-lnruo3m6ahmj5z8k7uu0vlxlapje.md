# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》306-1｜绿茶婊鼻祖拜见汉武帝！

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] excerpt bridges the destruction of the Huainan and Hengshan royal houses to three Yuan Shuo 1 / 128 BCE developments under [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]: an amnesty after the two large cases, the appointment of the seven-year-old [[LiuJuCrownPrinceWesternHan|刘据]] as crown prince, and a Xiongnu raid into Shanggu. The host treats the amnesty as morally compromised because it follows mass punishment, while Liu Ju's appointment is presented as a stabilizing answer to the dynasty's previously uncertain succession.

The closing minute introduces [[DianKingdomWesternHan|滇国]] through the fictional Yunnan setting of *Ghost Blows Out the Light*, then identifies the historical polity as centered on the Dian Lake region of central and eastern Yunnan. The excerpt stops before narrating the title's promised encounter with Han Wudi, so it supplies geographic framing and a transition into the next episode rather than the substantive Dian-Han relationship.

## Key Claims

- The host revisits the Huainan and Hengshan cases as mass political violence whose reach cannot be explained by assuming that every punished person secretly joined a rebellion.
- The subsequent general amnesty is criticized as reputational mercy after irreversible killing rather than as evidence that earlier punishment was cautious or just.
- Liu Ju is appointed crown prince at age seven, and the episode interprets a recognized heir as a major reduction in uncertainty around Han Wudi's throne and succession.
- The source links earlier succession uncertainty to Han Wudi's long lack of a son and to the ambitions it attributes to [[LiuAn|淮南王刘安]].
- A Xiongnu force is said to enter Shanggu and kill several hundred people, placing frontier pressure beside internal consolidation.
- Dian is introduced as a real southwestern polity centered on the Dian Lake area and extending across parts of central and eastern Yunnan.
- The popular-fiction discussion raises questions about Dian's chronology and culture but does not answer them within the supplied excerpt.

## Key Quotes

> “用诈术表示仁慈的时候，仁慈就变成了邪恶。” - the host's judgment on presenting amnesty as benevolence after large-scale execution.

> “皇位从根本上得到了稳固。” - the episode's interpretation of Liu Ju's appointment as crown prince.
