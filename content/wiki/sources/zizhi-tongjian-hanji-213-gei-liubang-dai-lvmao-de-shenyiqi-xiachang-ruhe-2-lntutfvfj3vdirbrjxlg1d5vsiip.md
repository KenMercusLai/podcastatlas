# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》213｜给刘邦戴绿帽的审食其，下场如何（2）

## Summary

Despite its title, the supplied [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode document does not discuss [[ShenYiji|审食其]]. Its body follows [[LiuHeng|汉文帝刘恒]] from a Xiongnu Right Wise King raid through an inspection and reward visit to his former Dai base, then explains [[LiuXingjuWesternHan|济北王刘兴居]]'s failed rebellion through the unresolved reward politics of the anti-Lü settlement.

The episode's strongest contribution is the sequence by which military preparation and a time-limited surrender offer work together. Liu Heng recalls [[GuanYing|灌婴]] toward Chang'an, sends [[ChaiWu|柴武]] against Jibei, and promises pardon to officials and residents who submit before the punitive army arrives; the source interprets the offer as a cohesion-breaking device that precedes Liu Xingju's defeat and suicide.

## Key Claims

- A Xiongnu Right Wise King reportedly entered the Henan region and raided Shang commandery, leading Liu Heng to deploy Guan Ying with eighty-five thousand cavalry toward Gaonu while separately reinforcing Chang'an.
- The Xiongnu force withdrew beyond the frontier after learning of the Han deployment, so the immediate crisis ended without a developed battle narrative.
- Liu Heng then visited Taiyuan and rewarded people associated with his former Dai regime, including a three-year tax exemption for Jinyang and Zhongdu in the episode's account.
- [[LiuXiangQiKingWesternHan|齐王刘襄]], [[LiuZhangChengyangKingWesternHan|城阳王刘章]], and Liu Xingju are presented as contributors to the overthrow of the Lü faction whose expected political rewards did not match their contribution.
- After Liu Xiang and Liu Zhang died, Liu Xingju reportedly mistook Liu Heng's northern travel for a personal campaign against Xiongnu and rebelled while the emperor was away.
- Liu Heng sent Chai Wu with a large punitive force and stationed another force at Xingyang while recalling Guan Ying to secure the capital.
- An imperial offer pardoning Jibei officials and residents who surrendered before the army arrived is presented as the decisive political instrument that fractured rebel confidence before Liu Xingju's defeat and suicide.
- The ending only previews Zhang Shizhi's later conflicts over imperial authority and law; it does not develop those cases.
