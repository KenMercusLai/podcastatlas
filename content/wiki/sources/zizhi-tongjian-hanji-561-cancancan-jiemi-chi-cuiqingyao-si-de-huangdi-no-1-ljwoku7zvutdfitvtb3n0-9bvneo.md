# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》561丨惨惨惨！揭秘吃催情药死的皇帝NO.1！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Suihe 2 / 7 BCE immediately after [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-559-mingming-wuzui-weihe-tianxiaren-dou-xiang-ta-si-lu3ckuxwujdbj4zdoqhvo2cg1rs0|Hanji 559]]'s [[ZhaiFangjinWesternHan|翟方进]] scapegoat death and before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-562-han-aidi-jiwei-shi-you-duo-biequ-lpv8_ks5b3c0ow_3o9_mvxi9n3xu|Hanji 562]]'s [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] accession. It centers on [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]'s sudden death in [[WeiyangPalace|未央宫]], [[ZhaoHedeWesternHan|赵合德]]'s suicide after [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] orders an investigation, and the later story that Chengdi died after overusing a fangshi-style aphrodisiac or "immortal pill."

The durable synthesis is cautionary rather than medical. The episode preserves the dramatic `Zhao Feiyan Waizhuan` / Bo Yang-style drug-death tradition, but it explicitly marks many details as rumor or non-standard-history material and keeps the political point in view: Chengdi's sudden death creates a public-answer problem, and Zhao Hede becomes the easiest blame target because palace intimacy, Zhao-family weakness, and court incentive all align.

## Key Claims

- In March 7 BCE, Han Chengdi dies suddenly at Weiyang Palace after appearing functional the previous evening: he had received visiting kings and had pending business around appointing Kong Guang as chancellor.
- The death scene is narrated as sudden physical collapse while dressing in the morning, with Zhao Hede present and aware of what happened.
- Wang Zhengjun orders Wang Mang, the censorial and legal offices, and senior ministers to investigate Chengdi's daily conduct and death circumstances.
- Zhao Hede kills herself after the investigation begins, with the episode reading her position as politically hopeless whether or not every rumor about her is true.
- The episode recounts a non-standard-history tradition, associated with `Zhao Feiyan Waizhuan` and Bo Yang's retelling, that Chengdi died after taking too much of an aphrodisiac "immortal pill."
- The host stresses that the pill-making details and some death-sequence details are legendary or absent from standard histories, so they should not be treated as settled forensic fact.
- Politically, the court cannot publicly explain Chengdi's death as a shameful sexual-drug episode, so responsibility is concentrated on Zhao Hede and the Zhao sisters.
- The episode contrasts Chengdi's praised appearance, learning, and remonstrance tolerance with his inability to restrain desire, favorite relationships, and outer-relative politics.
- Zhang Fang's grief at Chengdi's death lets the host shift from palace scandal into the dangers of love without loyalty and destructive intimacy.
