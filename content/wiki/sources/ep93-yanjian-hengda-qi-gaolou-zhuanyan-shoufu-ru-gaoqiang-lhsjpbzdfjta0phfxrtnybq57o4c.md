# EP93 眼见恒大起高楼，转眼首富入高墙

## Summary
This [[YiLaoYongYi|一劳永逸]] episode reconstructs [[Evergrande]] and [[XuJiayin]]'s rise from a small Guangzhou developer into a highly leveraged national real-estate conglomerate. Its core synthesis is that [[RealEstateHighTurnoverLeverage]] worked only while low-price fast sales, [[HousingPresaleFinancingRisk|presale cash]], contractor credit, listed-company financing, dollar bonds, perpetual bonds, commercial paper, and wealth products kept rolling. The collapse then moved from balance-sheet stress into ordinary lives through [[EvergrandeWealth]], supplier bills, unfinished homes, and the [[GuaranteedHomeDeliveryCrisis|保交楼 crisis]].

## Key Claims
- The source says Evergrande's first successful playbook combined cheap land, tiny upfront deposits, land-collateral borrowing, contractor advances, low-price selling, and presale cash recovery.
- [[HousingPresaleFinancingRisk]] is central: presale lowered purchase thresholds and accelerated developer cash return, but transferred completion risk to buyers when the developer's funding chain broke.
- After the 2008 IPO failure, Xu Jiayin's Hong Kong investor network and the 2009 listing turned Evergrande's listed-company status into a stronger credit lever for borrowing and land expansion.
- The source presents the 2010s expansion as a debt stack rather than simple growth: dollar bonds, perpetual bonds, trust funding, commercial acceptance bills, employee/customer wealth products, and strategic-investor buyback obligations all increased fragility.
- [[ThreeRedLinesPolicy|Three red lines]] and "房住不炒" changed the financing game by limiting new borrowing for developers that breached leverage and liquidity thresholds.
- [[EvergrandeWealth]] and commercial-paper defaults made the crisis social: employees, owners, small suppliers, construction teams, and already-paid homebuyers were exposed, not only banks and bondholders.
- Evergrande football, Evergrande Spring, cars, finance, healthcare, film, insurance, and Faraday Future are used as examples of diversification stories that sustained confidence more than they proved durable operating capability.
- The episode closes by treating Evergrande as a warning against assuming Chinese property prices only rise or that housing is a low-risk household investment.

## Key Quotes
> "成本价售房" - the early low-price sales slogan used to explain the first high-turnover experiment.

> "房住不炒" - the policy slogan the episode uses as the pivot away from permissive property speculation.

> "我可以一无所有，但恒大财富的投资者不能一无所有" - Xu Jiayin's quoted promise at the Evergrande Wealth meeting.
