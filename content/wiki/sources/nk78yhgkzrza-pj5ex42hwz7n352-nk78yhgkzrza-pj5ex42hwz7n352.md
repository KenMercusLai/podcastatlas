# 影石Insta360 创始人刘靖康×罗永浩！比生存更重要的是那些微小的念头

## Summary
This [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] interview follows [[LiuJingkang|刘靖康]] from childhood computing and university experiments through the founding and category expansion of [[Insta360|影石 Insta360]]. Its central synthesis is that creator-hardware innovation joins technical curiosity to user workflow, while manufacturing, reliability, market education, litigation, capital responsibility, and public-company governance bound what a founder can pursue. The episode adds [[CaptureNowReframeLater]], [[HardwarePrototypeToProductionGap]], and [[MissionLedCategoryAdjacency]] while extending [[PortableCreatorCameras]].

## Key Claims
- Liu describes an early preference for applied computing and making products rather than algorithm competition, alongside university episodes that taught him the social and legal boundaries of technical experimentation.
- His first startup attempt, a mobile live-streaming product, failed partly because the team underestimated operations, market formation, and business-model clarity; the later camera company initially entered 360-degree imaging through the VR wave.
- [[CaptureNowReframeLater]] helped panoramic cameras escape dependence on VR viewing: users can record the whole scene first, choose the view later, and export conventional shareable video.
- [[HardwarePrototypeToProductionGap]] separates a compelling demo from a reliable consumer device; yield, vibration, battery contacts, manufacturing process, after-sales exposure, and supply-chain collaboration determine whether a hardware idea survives scale.
- The source frames [[Insta360]]'s growth against [[GoPro]] and [[DJI]] as category expansion rather than a simple goal of defeating an incumbent, while retaining litigation, price competition, and technical rivalry as real operating constraints.
- [[MissionLedCategoryAdjacency]] explains the move toward panoramic drones: a company organized around recording and sharing may enter a risky adjacent category when the user outcome fits its mission and the incumbent has not solved the same workflow.
- Liu says the company adopted AI both organizationally and technically, including moving automatic editing from detection-plus-rules toward end-to-end training; he nevertheless treats authentic recording as durable even when generative tools alter expression and some capture-device demand.
- The episode distinguishes a founder's personal preference for creation from a public company's duties to survive, commercialize, operate efficiently, earn returns, develop employees, and avoid catastrophic mistakes.

## Key Quotes
> “think bold” — the company slogan Liu says was selected to express innovation, customer spirit, and internally courageous creation.

> “life is sharp, play more” — an earlier slogan associated with recording distinctive life experiences.

> “chase adventure” — an intermediate slogan aligned with the company's movement into action cameras.
