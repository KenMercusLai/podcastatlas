# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》570丨信仰不可缺失！英雄不容诋毁！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Suihe 2 / 7 BCE under [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]], immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-571-hanchengdi-qinshou-shazi-shi-zhen-shi-jia-lhjrwpg-t6izp__tshwzem1t0dyzh|Hanji 571]]'s Chengdi death-and-heir accusation sequence. It centers on [[GengYuWesternHan|耿育]]'s earlier memorial defending [[ChenTangWesternHan|陈汤]] and [[GanYanshouWesternHan|甘延寿]] after their distant campaign against [[ZhizhiChanyu|郅支单于]], arguing that a court cannot publicly use heroic merit while privately leaving the hero disgraced.

The durable synthesis is about merit, fault, and state credibility. The episode does not deny that Chen Tang later had real misconduct around the Changling works; it argues that proportional judgment matters because erasing or degrading great service chills future strategic initiative and weakens the cultural memory through which a polity teaches people what sacrifice is worth.

## Key Claims

- Geng Yu's memorial recalls Gan Yanshou and Chen Tang as commanders who fought far from the Han center, killed a defiant Xiongnu ruler, and restored Han prestige after years of humiliation.
- The memorial says the prior emperor had praised the victory, announced it as righteous achievement, and wanted the commanders' merit properly graded.
- Geng Yu blames [[KuangHengWesternHan|匡衡]] for obstructing full recognition and leaving the two men with only several hundred households, making the reward disproportionate to the service.
- Chen Tang later falls into imprisonment and exile-like marginalization despite age, innocence claims, and prior achievement; Geng Yu asks Aidi to recall him or at least stop letting slander drive him farther away.
- The episode adds that Chen Tang's later Changling-related work alienated officials and powerful households, and that the host accepts some corruption fault while rejecting wholesale erasure of his battlefield merit.
- Geng Yu's larger warning is institutional: if a person with extraordinary service can be crushed for lesser faults or factional hostility, later capable people may refuse to offer plans or risk death for the state.
- The host extends the historical case into a broader hero-memory claim: heroic action can be military, civic, occupational, or quietly ethical, and public culture loses something when it mocks or denies such models.

## Key Quotes

> "安不忘危，盛必虑衰" - Geng Yu's warning that settled strength still requires danger awareness.

> "记人之功，忘人之过" - the episode's proportional-judgment frame for Chen Tang's case.
