# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》270｜陈阿娇秘史：后宫之中，她为何独宠女官？（1）

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the factual sequence before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-270-chenajiao-mishi-hougong-zhizhong-ta-weihe-duchong-nvguan-2-lllzlb2knjj0ovmuc8tiid34m0gp|Hanji 270-2]]: [[EmpressChenAjiaoWesternHan|陈阿娇]] loses [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s favor after [[EmpressWeiZifuWesternHan|卫子夫]] enters the palace, relies on female ritual specialist [[ChuFuWesternHan|楚服]], and is deposed after [[ZhangTangWesternHan|张汤]] investigates the alleged wugu practices. The episode connects hidden ritual harm, palace rivalry, and state punishment while treating the relationship reportedly described as “相爱若夫妇” as an example of [[PalaceFemaleSameSexIntimacy|宫廷女性同性亲密关系]].

The durable synthesis is that the Chen-Chu relationship must be separated into evidentiary layers. The episode attributes male dress, shared sleeping quarters, ritual instruction, and the quoted phrase to 《汉武故事》, but its claims that this is China's first documented lesbian relationship, that “对食” originally meant female homosexuality, and that Chu used a technique to make Chen love her are broader interpretations that the supplied text does not independently establish.

## Key Claims

- The episode places Chen's crisis after Wei Zifu enters the palace in 139 BCE, receives Wudi's favor, and becomes the target of Chen's jealousy and alleged attempts at harm.
- After Wudi withdraws from Chen, Chu Fu promises ritual methods that can restore his affection and teaches sacrifice, curse practices directed at Wei Zifu, and what the host calls “魅术.”
- The episode quotes 《汉武故事》 as saying that Chu Fu wore male clothing and lived and slept with Chen, “相爱若夫妇.”
- The host interprets that phrase as evidence of a female same-sex relationship and links it to the later court term “对食,” but does not compare textual variants or competing scholarship.
- When Wudi learns of the wugu conduct, he orders Zhang Tang to investigate; the episode reports more than three hundred implicated deaths and Chu Fu's public execution.
- Chen is ordered to surrender the empress seal and move to Changmen Palace after an edict accuses her of violating ritual order, praying to spirits, and seeking harm against others.
- The episode explains Chen's actions through childlessness, lost favor, jealousy, and emotional dependence, but those inner motives remain the host's reconstruction.
