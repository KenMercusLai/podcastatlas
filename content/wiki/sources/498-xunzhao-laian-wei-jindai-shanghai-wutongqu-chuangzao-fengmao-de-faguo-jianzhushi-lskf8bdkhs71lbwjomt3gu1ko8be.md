# 498 寻找赉安：为近代上海梧桐区创造风貌的法国建筑师

## Summary
This [[HuzuoHuyou|忽左忽右]] episode has host [[ChengYanliang|成言梁]] interview architectural culture researcher [[WuFeipeng|吴飞鹏]] about [[LaiAnArchitect|赉安 / 赖安]], the French architect whose work shaped [[ShanghaiFrenchConcessionArchitecture|Shanghai French Concession architecture]] and the [[HengfuHistoricDistrict|衡复风貌区]]. The discussion argues that Lai An should be treated as a central figure in modern Shanghai architecture, not a footnote beside [[HudecShanghaiArchitect|乌达克]], because his apartments, villas, and club buildings helped bring [[ShanghaiArtDecoArchitecture|Shanghai Art Deco architecture]] and [[ModernistArchitecture|modernist architecture]] into local urban life. It also frames old-building research as a form of [[UrbanArchitecturalMemory|urban architectural memory]] sustained through archives, newspapers, field walking, public plaques, and corrections to earlier claims.

## Key Claims
- [[LaiAnArchitect|Lai An]] is presented as a key architect of the former French Concession, especially around today's [[HengfuHistoricDistrict|Hengfu historic district]], rather than a minor name overshadowed by [[HudecShanghaiArchitect|Hudec]].
- [[WuFeipeng|Wu Feipeng]] says his Lai An research began with a 2015 encounter and was later deepened through an architectural list from Lai An's grandson, Paris travel, Shanghai archives, newspapers, and field investigation.
- The episode treats 1927 to 1937 as a commonly named golden decade for Shanghai construction, while also arguing that important modern architecture had already begun earlier in the 20th century.
- [[ShanghaiArtDecoArchitecture|Shanghai Art Deco]] is explained as part of a broader modern shift after industrialization: simpler geometry, concrete, glass, elevators, prefabricated materials, and less classical ornament.
- [[GardenHotelShanghai|花园饭店 / the former French Club]], [[GascoigneApartments|盖斯康公寓]], and [[MajesticApartmentsShanghai|迈奇公寓]] are used as examples of Lai An's durable, high-quality design and distinctive rounded or semi-circular balcony vocabulary.
- The episode challenges a simple "French-style Shanghai" story: the former French Concession's culture and built environment were also shaped by British firms, Russian emigres, Jewish refugees, Chinese architects, and local adaptation.
- [[ModernistHeritageRecognitionGap|Modernist heritage can be harder to protect]] because simple modern forms may not look "old" or valuable to the public, even when they carry strong architectural and memory value.
- The source emphasizes correction and uncertainty: Wu Feipeng limits the confirmed Lai An building count to 122, notes unverified candidates beyond that, and corrects a published mistake about Lai An's second wife Anna.

## Key Quotes
> "122栋" - Wu Feipeng's bounded count for buildings confirmed through Shanghai archival materials.

> "赖安阳台" - a shorthand used for Lai An's small balcony and rounded balcony vocabulary.

> "东方的法式风情" - the simplified French-Concession imagination the episode explicitly qualifies.
