# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》847丨女人变得好命，从懂得这俩字开始

## Summary
This episode backfills the 159 CE setup immediately before the anti-Liang coup narrated in Hanji 848. It opens with a "choice" frame about partners, friends, bosses, social circles, and environments, then applies that frame to [[LiangJiLateHan|Liang Ji]], [[SunShouLateHan|Sun Shou]], [[EmpressDengHuanLateHan|Deng Meng]], [[XuanDengMotherLateHan|Xuan]], [[BingZunLateHan|Bing Zun]], and [[EmperorHuanOfHan|Emperor Huan of Han]]. Its historical contribution is to show that the visible palace coup begins earlier as a chain of household influence, harem placement, assassination risk, and secret selection of palace allies.

## Key Claims
- "Choice" is presented as a life-shaping discipline: partners, friends, bosses, and habitual surroundings become environments that shape later fate.
- Liang Ji's outer-relative power is not only formal court domination; it also runs through household authority, sexual politics, harem placement, and adoption or surname strategy.
- Sun Shou is portrayed as an active household and cultural power: she controls Liang Ji at home, punishes his outside mistress, spreads Luoyang beauty fashions, and helps send Deng Meng into the palace.
- Deng Meng's rise begins as a Liang-Sun channel into Emperor Huan's harem, but Liang Ji's attempt to adopt or rename her turns her natal household into a political fault line.
- Bing Zun warns Xuan that Liang Ji will not last and should not be allowed to absorb Deng Meng into the Liang surname; Liang Ji kills him and then tries to kill Xuan.
- Xuan's survival after the failed assassination attempt gives Emperor Huan the immediate trigger to seek help against Liang Ji.
- Emperor Huan's anti-Liang coalition starts through palace proximity: he privately consults [[TangHengLateHan|Tang Heng]], recruits the future [[FiveMarquisesHuanEra|Five Marquises]], and binds them through a blood oath before the public coup.

## Key Quotes
> "选择" - the episode's opening frame for relationship and environment agency.

> "跋扈将军" - the remembered label for Liang Ji's domineering position.

> "且听明天分解" - the cliffhanger leading into the Hanji 848 coup episode.
