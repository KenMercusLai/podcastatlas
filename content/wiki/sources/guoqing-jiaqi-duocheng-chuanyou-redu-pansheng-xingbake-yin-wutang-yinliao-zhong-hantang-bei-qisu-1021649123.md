## Key Claims
- [[Qunar]]-attributed reporting says National Day bookings involving hotels in two or more cities rose more than 30% year over year, with longer leave combinations, denser rail-road-air networks, crowd avoidance, county destinations, and local craft experiences supporting [[MultiCityItineraryTravel]].
- The episode says [[Skydance]] completed a USD 110 billion acquisition of [[WarnerBrosDiscovery|Warner Bros. Discovery]] after antitrust litigation was settled, with more than USD 50 billion of debt and a five-year commitment to release at least 30 films annually and invest another USD 1.5 billion in U.S. production.
- [[FrancisHalzen|Francis Halzen]] is described as the sole 2026 Nobel physics laureate for high-energy neutrino work associated with Antarctic detection, while the physiology or medicine award is described as recognizing three optogenetics researchers.
- [[Apple]] and [[LGElectronics|LG Electronics]] are reported to be developing LG-branded doorbells and thermostats for Apple's forthcoming home hub, while a separate macOS change would require explicit risk-aware authorization for full-device data access.
- [[Nike]] is reported to have combined Greater China with Asia-Pacific during a regional restructuring after June-August revenue fell 4% overall and China sales fell 26%; the source says local decision rights and lower-tier-market investment would remain.
- [[AmazonPrimeVideo|Amazon Prime Video]] is reported to have obtained global Emmy ceremony streaming rights from 2027, with viewing available without a Prime subscription, replacing the long-running rotation among ABC, CBS, Fox, and NBC.
- [[Huawei]] and [[Qualcomm]] are reported to have reached broad [[PatentCrossLicensing|patent cross-licensing]] terms spanning 5G, computing, and AI, while Qualcomm would acquire some Huawei U.S. patents; the same segment says Huawei and [[Seres]] began a new five-year [[AITO]] cooperation without clarifying final control over product, design, and sales.
- From 10 October, the episode says China's domestic [[AviationFuelSurcharge|aviation fuel surcharge]] would rise to RMB 50 for routes of 800 km or less and RMB 90 for longer routes amid conflict-driven oil-price pressure and thinner global inventories.
- A Seattle lawsuit alleges eight [[Starbucks]] protein drinks labeled sugar-free contained sugar; Starbucks says the sugar came from milk, no sugar was added, sugar-free syrup was used, and nutrition information was disclosed. The dispute extends [[NutritionLabelSugarTraps]] by showing that no added sugar and no sugar are not interchangeable consumer claims.
- The source also says Starbucks transferred control of franchise operations covering more than 1,100 stores in seven Asian markets to [[DFIRetailGroup|DFI Retail Group]].
