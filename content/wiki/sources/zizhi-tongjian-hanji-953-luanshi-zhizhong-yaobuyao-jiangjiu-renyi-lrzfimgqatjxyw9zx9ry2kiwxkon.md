# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》953丨乱世之中，要不要讲究仁义？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate pre-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-954-renxingbenshan-haishi-renxingbene-lkurz9dbb25jrjyl5c-rrahda39h|Hanji 954]] late-Han field through [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s entry into [[Jingzhou|荆州]] and [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s first direct anti-[[DongZhuo|董卓]] action. It frames "仁义" as politically useful but not sufficient by itself: [[KuaiLiang|蒯良]] supplies the people-support argument, [[KuaiYue|蒯越]] supplies the harsher local-force absorption plan, and Liu Biao stabilizes Jingzhou by combining both. The Cao Cao branch shows the opposite coalition problem: even accurate judgment and a coherent [[StrategicPassControl|pass-control]] plan cannot move an [[AntiDongZhuoCoalition|反董卓联盟]] whose members preserve themselves, leaving [[BianshuiBattleLateHan|汴水之战]] and the [[SuanzaoCamp|酸枣大营]] breakup as an early case of [[CoalitionSelfPreservationFailure|联盟自保失灵]].

## Key Claims
- The episode opens by asking whether "善良" or "仁义" matters in a chaotic age; its answer is pragmatic rather than sentimental, because benevolence must be tied to institutions, force, and timing.
- [[LiuBiao|刘表]] enters [[Jingzhou|荆州]] with a title but without an effective army or stable base, making local elite management his first survival problem.
- [[KuaiLiang|蒯良]] argues that people fail to attach because officials fail to love them; if Liu Biao practices benevolent government, local attachment can help repair the shortage of troops and base.
- [[KuaiYue|蒯越]] gives the hard-edged complement: [[YuanShu|袁术]] is arrogant and strategically shallow, while Jingzhou's armed local clan heads are greedy and violent, so Liu Biao should lure them in, remove the most dangerous leaders, and absorb their followers.
- Liu Biao executes fifty-five local strongmen, incorporates their troops, moves the Jingzhou administrative center to [[XiangyangLateHan|襄阳]], and stabilizes the Jiangnan half of Jingzhou; this becomes a later refuge for displaced scholars and the social background in which [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] grows up.
- After Dong Zhuo burns Luoyang and moves the emperor west, Cao Cao reads the moment as an opening: the coalition should attack while Dong Zhuo is vulnerable rather than stay in camp.
- [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] sends [[WeiZiLateHan|魏兹]] to support Cao Cao, but Cao Cao's small westward force is defeated by [[XuRongLateHan|徐荣]] near Xingyang/Bianshui; Cao is wounded, loses his horse, and survives because [[CaoHong|曹洪]] gives him his own mount and escorts him out.
- Xu Rong withdraws because Cao Cao's small force still fights for a full day, making him infer that the larger Suanzao camp may not be easy to crush.
- Cao Cao returns to Suanzao, denounces the coalition armies for drinking and waiting, and proposes a containment design: [[YuanShao|袁绍]] should hold Henei/Mengjin, the Suanzao armies should control [[Chenggao|成皋]] and [[Aocang|敖仓]], and other forces should block Huanyuan, Taigu, and [[Wuguan|武关]] approaches while waiting for Dong Zhuo's internal disorder.
- The coalition refuses to act; Cao Cao later recruits with [[XiahouDun|夏侯惇]] in Yangzhou, while the Suanzao camp disperses under food pressure, confirming that the anti-Dong alliance has a command and cost-sharing failure rather than only a military problem.

## Key Quotes
> "但行好事，莫问前程。" - opening moral frame for asking whether benevolence matters in a disorderly world.

> "天下可以没有曹洪，但不能没有曹操。" - Cao Hong's explanation for giving his horse to Cao Cao during the Bianshui defeat.
