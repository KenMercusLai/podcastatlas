# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》663丨更始帝刘玄是滩扶不上墙的烂泥？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the Gengshi 1 / 23 CE formation layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-664-kunyang-zhizhan-jueding-guoyun-de-lishi-zhuanzhedian-lihrwawp4x1w1pn4aibdohq4-l-4|Hanji 664]]. It explains why anti-[[WangMang|王莽]] commanders elevate [[LiuXuanGengshiEmperor|更始帝刘玄]] as a Liu-family emperor: his lineage gives the [[LulinRebellion|绿林]]-Han coalition a usable restoration banner, while New Market and Pinglin commanders prefer him over [[LiuYanLiuXiuBrother|刘縯]] because he seems easier to control.

The episode then follows the immediate pre-[[KunyangBattle|昆阳之战]] setup. Liu Yan argues for delaying imperial title until Wang Mang and [[ChimeiRebellion|赤眉]] are defeated, [[ZhangAngGengshi|张卬 / Zhang Ang]] forces the elevation decision, Liu Xuan's awkward accession exposes limited personal authority, and Wang Mang answers the crisis with image management, amnesty, commanders, threats of a million-man army, and the march of [[WangYiXinGeneral|王邑]] and Wang Xun toward Kunyang.

## Key Claims

- Liu Xuan's elevation is presented as [[RestoredRoyalFigureheadLegitimacy|restored royal figurehead legitimacy]] inside a Han-restoration coalition: lineage and controllability matter more than proven command capacity.
- Liu Yan offers the strategic objection: claiming emperor before Wang Mang is destroyed and before Chimei is settled risks turning Liu-family restoration into premature internal competition.
- Zhang Ang's sword-and-floor intervention shows how forceful faction pressure can override a broader but fragile consensus inside an improvised rebel coalition.
- Liu Xuan's accession scene, office distribution, and disappointed followers make the Gengshi center look symbolically useful but administratively weak from the start.
- Wang Mang's dyed hair, new empress, amnesty, and punitive expedition planning show a crisis ruler trying to preserve authority through image, ritual, personnel, and intimidation.
- The episode sets the Kunyang crisis before the battle itself: Wang Yi and Wang Xun gather a huge army, commanders panic at the scale, and Liu Xiu redirects them from flight toward concentrated defense.
- Liu Xiu's night exit with thirteen riders makes Kunyang a survival-and-mobilization problem before it becomes the battlefield victory narrated in Hanji 664.

## Key Quotes

> "素懦弱" - the source tradition's compact characterization behind Liu Xuan's controllability.

> "大事不疑" - Zhang Ang's pressure phrase after throwing his sword down.

> "城中兵谷既少，而外寇强大" - Liu Xiu's survival framing for Kunyang's commanders.
