# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》574丨古代的满分作文是什么样子？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianping 1 / 6 BCE under [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]], before the later Ding/Fu, Wang, Zhu Bo, and Dong Xian sequences. It centers on [[GengYuWesternHan|耿育]]'s memorial defending [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]'s decision to make Aidi heir and urging Aidi to stop expanding the investigation of [[ZhaoFeiyanWesternHan|赵飞燕]]-linked palace accusations.

The durable synthesis is that the memorial works as political narrative repair. Geng Yu takes a potentially damaging story about palace scandal, succession irregularity, and favorite-consort jealousy, then recasts it as Chengdi's foresight: like the Zhou royal precedent of Taibo yielding to Jili and Ji Chang, an unusual succession choice can be justified as a protective decision for ancestral temples and political order.

## Key Claims

- Geng Yu begins from orthodox succession law, then uses the Zhou precedent of Taibo, Zhongyong, Jili, and Ji Chang to argue that exceptional circumstances can justify exceptional arrangements.
- The memorial reframes Chengdi's heir decision as prudent prevention of a child ruler, maternal regency, ministerial noncompliance, and disorder.
- Geng Yu argues that continued palace investigation would damage Chengdi's reputation by making him appear ruled by beauty, jealousy, and inner-palace disorder rather than dynastic foresight.
- The memorial gives Aidi a face-saving rationale to end the case: praise the deceased ruler's virtue, clarify rumor, complete the father's intention, and prevent scandal from spreading to later generations.
- Aidi stops pursuing the detailed accusations after reading the memorial, helped by the fact that Zhao Feiyan had supported his designation as crown prince and remained tied to Fu Taihou's gratitude.
- The decision creates no clean harmony: [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] and the Wang family resent Aidi's closure of the case.
- The episode treats the memorial as a "full-score essay" because its value lies less in ornament than in matching political need, legitimate precedent, filial language, and public narrative.

## Key Quotes

> "非常之变，然后乃有非常之谋" - the memorial's principle for treating an irregular succession arrangement as crisis statecraft.

> "一人之便，重于九鼎之宝；三寸之舌，强于百万之师" - the host's closing assessment of the memorial's political force.
