# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》271｜西汉秘史：武帝岳母与养子的不伦恋（1）

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[LiuPiaoWesternHan|馆陶长公主刘嫖]] from high-status royal broker and mother of the deposed Chen empress into a wealthy but politically diminished household where she raises the adolescent [[DongYanWesternHan|董偃]] and later treats him as an intimate companion. The durable contribution is not the title's scandal language but the mechanism by which household wealth, long-term personal service, staged deference, and [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s “主人翁” address convert a private relationship into recognized social standing.

The supplied document is a structured popular-history summary rather than a transcript or source-critical study. It repeatedly writes the two central names as “刘飘” and “董衍”; the canonical pages normalize them to the transmitted forms 刘嫖 and 董偃 while preserving the episode spellings here as source variants. The episode stops after imperial recognition and therefore does not cover later remonstrance against Dong Yan or the decline of his favor.

## Key Claims

- Liu Piao is presented as Emperor Wen and Empress Dou's daughter, Emperor Jing's sister, and Han Wudi's aunt and mother-in-law; the episode credits her with important support for Liu Che's accession.
- After her daughter Chen's deposition and her husband's death, Liu Piao retains exceptional wealth but loses political centrality, which the host uses to frame luxury and intimacy as responses to status loss.
- Dong Yan enters the princess's household through his jewel-merchant mother, is retained at about thirteen, and receives education in riding, archery, music, arithmetic, calligraphy, and elite recreation.
- By eighteen, Dong Yan is described as a gentle and attentive household companion who manages close personal service and carriage driving; the episode then treats the relationship as sexual after Liu Piao's widowhood.
- Liu Piao first rebuilds emotional access to Han Wudi through a staged illness, repeated visits, and a gift of ten million cash before arranging Dong Yan's presentation.
- Han Wudi's request to meet the “主人翁,” followed by clothing, seating, and drinking privileges, publicly signals that Dong Yan may be treated as the princess's recognized household partner even without a formal office.
- Dong Yan's modest dress, submission, social tact, and flattery are presented as active skills in converting the princess's patronage into imperial favor.
- The episode uses the green head-covering and the claim that later princesses followed this precedent to moralize the relationship, but it does not establish the full textual or institutional history of either claim.

## Key Quotes

> “愿谒主人翁。” — the pivotal address attributed to Han Wudi and treated by the episode as public recognition.

> “我替你母亲养你吧。” — the episode's rendering of Liu Piao's initial offer to keep the young Dong Yan in her household.
