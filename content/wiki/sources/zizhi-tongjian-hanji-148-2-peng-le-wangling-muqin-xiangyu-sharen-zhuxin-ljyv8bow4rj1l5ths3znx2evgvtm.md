# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》148-2｜烹了王陵母亲 项羽杀人诛心

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-148-1-hanxin-yupan-le-zhanghan-de-yupan-lodhpuxpossjno87gorjkfrtitbe|Hanji 148-1]] by showing how [[HanXin|韩信]]'s Guanzhong victory changes [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s room for action and brings [[WangLingHanMinister|王陵]] closer to the Han side. Its central scene is [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s attempt to coerce Wang Ling through [[WangLingMother|王陵母亲]], whose suicide removes the hostage leverage and turns Xiang Yu's later corpse-burning order into a political backfire. The episode then widens to Liu Bang's continued use of the [[HuaiwangAgreement|怀王之约]] claim while [[TianRong|田荣]], [[PengYue|彭越]], Yan unrest, and [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]]'s forced relocation keep Xiang Yu's settlement under pressure.

## Key Claims
- Han Xin's victory in Guanzhong gives Liu Bang's group a credible path out of Hanzhong confinement and raises Han Xin's prestige inside the army.
- Liu Bang sends people to retrieve [[LiuTaigong|刘太公]], [[LuZhi|吕雉]], and his son, while also trying to connect with Wang Ling in the Nanyang area.
- Wang Ling is not the older [[WangLing|Qin general named Wang Ling]]; this episode's Wang Ling is a Pei County notable whose early status and ties to [[YongChi|雍齿]] make his loyalty to Liu Bang initially uncertain.
- Wang Ling becomes more willing to support Liu Bang after Liu Bang retakes the Three Qin, showing the source's practical theme that rising strength attracts wavering allies.
- Xiang Yu captures Wang Ling's mother and treats her with formal respect before Wang Ling's envoy, hoping to use family obligation to induce surrender.
- Wang Ling's mother privately tells the envoy that Liu Bang is the worthier future ruler and asks Wang Ling not to become double-minded because of her.
- Her suicide converts the hostage from leverage into moral release; Xiang Yu's order to cook the body then pushes Wang Ling more firmly toward Liu Bang in the source's reading.
- Liu Bang still avoids openly framing his campaign as a direct anti-Chu war, claiming that he is restoring the Huaiwang promise and only taking the Guanzhong kingship owed to him.
- Tian Rong's Qi challenge, Peng Yue's attacks, and Yan's Han Guang/Zang Tu conflict buy Liu Bang time by pulling Xiang Yu's attention east and north.
- Zhang Liang's transfer of Liu Bang's self-justifying letter and Tian Rong's anti-Xiang letter helps Xiang Yu prioritize the Qi rebellion before attacking Liu Bang.
- The episode treats the broader crisis as a consequence of Xiang Yu's personally driven partition: demotions, exclusions, and forced relocations now turn into simultaneous rebellions and defections.

## Key Quotes
> "好好侍奉汉王" - Wang Ling's mother tells the envoy to carry this instruction back to her son.

> "宽厚长者" - the episode's phrase for her judgment that Liu Bang is the ruler Wang Ling should follow.

> "恢复怀王之约" - Liu Bang's public explanation for the Guanzhong campaign rather than an open declaration of war on Xiang Yu.
