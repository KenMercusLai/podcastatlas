## Summary
This [[BuHeShiYi|不合时宜]] episode interviews [[WangXiaowei|王小伟]] about [[ReEnteringLifeTechEraBook|《重新回到生活：科技时代的工作、情绪、爱情和死亡》]], using [[TechnologyPhilosophy|technology philosophy]] to examine AI-era intimacy, work, emotion, education, governance, and everyday life. Its core synthesis is that AI's smoothness and efficiency are real, but they can also weaken the friction, waiting, bodily presence, human cost, and independent judgment through which people remain agents in their own lives.

## Key Claims
- [[WangXiaowei|王小伟]] frames [[TechnologyPhilosophy|technology philosophy]] as inquiry into how technology mediates human beings, life-worlds, self-understanding, and ethical relations rather than only tool performance.
- AI companionship can be legitimate for lonely or vulnerable adults, but [[AICompanionAuthenticityBoundary]] and [[RelationshipTechnologyEthics]] become sharper because AI can provide smooth recognition without human vulnerability, mortality, or reciprocal cost.
- The episode adds [[AIJudgmentInfrastructure]]: once people ask a few capital-backed models for intimate, family, educational, or moral advice, model value defaults start shaping ordinary judgment.
- [[LifeTimeVsMachineTime]] captures the work critique: AI can compress loops so aggressively that meals, chats, walks, boredom, waiting, and fatigue are treated as inefficiency rather than as parts of life.
- The discussion of "荣光社会" and "幽暗社会" treats modern work anxiety as a loss of collective narrative plus rising demand for self-realization, recognition, and personal authorship.
- [[PsychiatricEverydayLanguage]] is treated as both progress and risk: diagnostic language reduces stigma and names real suffering, but labels such as NPD, PUA, gaslighting, or PTSD can prematurely close relational understanding.
- In higher education, AI makes detector-only responses brittle; philosophy, law, literature, and similar fields may need more classroom dialogue, oral defense, process evidence, and everyday teacher-student contact.
- The closing "return to life" argument resists turning anti-AI living into another KPI: offline body, friends, scenery, idle time, and uncertainty matter partly because they refuse optimization.
