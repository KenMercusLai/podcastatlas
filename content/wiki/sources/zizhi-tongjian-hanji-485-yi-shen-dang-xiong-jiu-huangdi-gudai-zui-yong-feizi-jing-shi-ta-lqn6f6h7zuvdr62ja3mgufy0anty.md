# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》485丨以身挡熊救皇帝！古代最勇妃子竟是她？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the early [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] Jian Zhao sequence immediately before later Jing Fang and Chen Tang material. It moves from Yellow River and Tunshi River hydrology, celestial and ritual notices, Hejian king punishment, and Bo Taihou cemetery removal into the tiger-enclosure incident in which [[FengYuanWesternHan|冯媛 / 冯昭仪]] steps between Yuan and a black bear.

The durable synthesis is that Feng Yuan's bear rescue begins as an act of courage and loyalty but immediately becomes palace capital: Yuan promotes and favors her, while the source presents [[FuZhaoyiWesternHan|傅昭仪]] as a contemporaneous consort whose jealousy starts from this differential favor. The episode also supplies earlier hydrological background for [[PreventiveFloodGovernance|预防性水患治理]]: the Tunshi River first reduced flooding by adding drainage capacity, but later drying and breach shifts made Yellow River management unstable again.

## Key Claims

- The episode opens with a modern black-bear encounter to make the danger of Feng Yuan's palace rescue legible.
- In 39 BCE, the Yellow River's earlier northern breach had created the Tunshi River, whose added drainage capacity reduced some downstream flood pressure.
- By 39 BCE the Qinghe-area breach and reduced flow into the Tunshi River leave the Tunshi channel drying, setting up later flood-governance concerns.
- In 38 BCE, the episode records a Liang-area meteorite, Yuan's Yong sacrifices, the demotion of Hejian king Liu Yuan after a killing accusation, and the removal of Bo Taihou's cemetery staff.
- During a tiger-enclosure viewing, a black bear approaches Yuan; most attendants and consorts flee, while Feng Yuan steps in front of the emperor.
- Yuan rewards the rescue by promoting Feng Yuan to Zhaoyi and increasing his favor toward her.
- The rescue and promotion make Feng Yuan a palace focus and generate jealousy or estrangement from Fu Zhaoyi.

## Key Quotes

> "如果抓住一个人也许就会停止攻击" - Feng Yuan's explanation for why she put herself between the bear and the emperor, as paraphrased in the episode.

> "虎父无犬女" - the host's closing evaluation after noting Feng Yuan's father as a left general.
