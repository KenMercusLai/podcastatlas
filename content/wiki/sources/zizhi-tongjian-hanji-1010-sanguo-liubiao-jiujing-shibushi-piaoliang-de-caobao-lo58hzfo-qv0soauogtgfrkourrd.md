# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1010丨三国刘表，究竟是不是漂亮的草包？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate prelude to [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1011-miheng-zhisi-zuiju-beiju-secai-de-jiedao-sharen-ltrzpchwdfcyp4ntmum9giu-5mva|Hanji 1011]] by centering [[LiuBiao|刘表]] in 196 CE. [[ZhangJiLateHan|张济]] enters [[Jingzhou|荆州]], dies while attacking [[Rangcheng|穰城]], and Liu Biao turns the crisis into [[MourningFramedMilitaryAbsorption|丧礼化收编]] by refusing congratulations, mourning the death, and absorbing the remaining troops. The episode then uses [[JiaXu|贾诩]]'s move from [[DuanWeiLateHan|段煨]] to [[ZhangXiu|张绣]] and his judgment of Liu Biao to argue that Liu Biao was not a fool, but a capable [[DefensiveLocalGovernance|保境安民式守成治理]] figure whose virtues had a wartime strategic ceiling.

## Key Claims
- In winter 196 CE, [[ZhangJiLateHan|张济]] leads troops from Guanzhong into [[Jingzhou|荆州]] and attacks [[Rangcheng|穰城]] in [[NanyangCommandery|南阳郡]], trying to secure a base.
- Zhang Ji dies during the fighting; the wiki treats the transcript's "刘建" as a likely arrow/stray-arrow rendering rather than as a new person.
- When Jingzhou officials congratulate [[LiuBiao|刘表]], Liu Biao says Zhang Ji came because he had nowhere else to go, and that the clash was not what he intended.
- Liu Biao sends people to comfort and reassure Zhang Ji's remaining soldiers, and the host reads their submission as a successful moral and political reframing of a military collision.
- The episode treats this as Liu Biao's hidden sharpness: he appears gentle, but can convert danger into absorption without making the other side feel openly conquered.
- The source says Liu Biao had earlier entered Yicheng, worked with [[KuaiYue|蒯越]] and [[CaiMao|蔡瑁]], suppressed local armed clans, and gained broad control of northern Jingzhou while [[NanyangCommandery|南阳郡]] still sat in a contested late-Han field.
- After Zhang Ji's death, [[ZhangXiu|张绣]] inherits the troops and stations at [[WanCounty|宛县]], shifting the former Dong Zhuo remnant toward a more settled survival pattern.
- [[JiaXu|贾诩]] leaves [[DuanWeiLateHan|段煨]] despite good treatment because he judges Duan Wei's suspicion and Jia Xu's own prestige as a future danger.
- Jia Xu chooses Zhang Xiu because Zhang Xiu lacks a strategist and is therefore more likely to value him; he also predicts Duan Wei will treat Jia Xu's family well after his departure.
- Jia Xu advises Zhang Xiu to attach to Liu Biao, and Liu Biao receives Jia Xu with guest ritual.
- Jia Xu's later evaluation is balanced: Liu Biao could be a high minister in a peaceful age, but in disorder he cannot read change well enough, lacks decisiveness, and is too suspicious for larger achievement.
- Liu Biao's strength is local governance: he treats commoners and scholar-officials well, preserves relative peace in Jingzhou, and attracts many displaced scholars.
- The episode's final ritual detail shows Liu Biao backing away from courtyard court music when warned that such music belongs to the emperor, making his ambition bounded by [[LiAsPoliticalOrder|礼制政治秩序]].

## Key Quotes
> "受吊，不受贺" - the historical formula the episode uses for Liu Biao's refusal to celebrate Zhang Ji's death.

> "看似柔弱，实藏霹雳手段" - the host's compact evaluation of Liu Biao's political style.
