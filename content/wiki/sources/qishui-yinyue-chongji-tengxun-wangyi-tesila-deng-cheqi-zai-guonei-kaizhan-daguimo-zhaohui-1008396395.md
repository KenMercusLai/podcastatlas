# 汽水音乐冲击腾讯、网易，特斯拉等车企在国内开展大规模召回

## Summary
This [[ShengdongZaokafei|声动早咖啡]] episode is a compact business-news scan across auto recalls, [[Apple]] hardware, livestream retail, sportswear, hotels, appliances, real estate brokerage, football sponsorship, and online music. Its strongest recurring pattern is a shift from simple scale expansion toward [[EfficiencyOverScaleExpansion|efficiency, channel control, product proof, and user-entry competition]]. The most concrete new branches are [[VehicleEmergencyHandleUsability|vehicle emergency-handle usability]] in a China recall affecting [[Tesla]], [[GeelyAutomobile|Geely Automobile]], [[DongfengMotor|Dongfeng Motor]], and [[Xiaomi]], plus [[ShortVideoMusicDistribution|short-video-driven music distribution]] through [[QishuiMusic|汽水音乐]]'s pressure on [[TencentMusic|Tencent Music]] and [[NetEaseCloudMusic|NetEase Cloud Music]].

## Key Claims
- [[Tesla]] and eight other automakers started a China recall involving about 4.3 million vehicles, with Tesla accounting for roughly 3 million; the source says the immediate fix centers on adding warning labels and, for some companies, window-control software upgrades.
- The recall reason is not drivetrain failure but emergency usability: mechanical interior emergency door handles blend into interior trim, may be hard to identify or operate, and can delay escape after a crash.
- [[Apple]]'s reported foldable [[IPhone|iPhone]] is described as a premium China-market test with a price above USD 2,000, no telephoto lens, and Touch ID rather than Face ID, extending [[FoldablePhoneProductivity]] and [[ConsumerElectronicsLifecycle]] pressure.
- [[VisionPro|Vision Pro]] team cuts are linked to expensive immersive-video production and limited active-user scale, making the product a continued post-phone form-factor caution for Apple.
- [[OrientalSelection|东方甄选]] says self-operated products have become more than half of platform GMV, while management reduces reliance on star-anchor traffic through supply-chain, commission, and team adjustments.
- [[LiNing|Li-Ning]] reports modest first-half revenue growth and renewed profit growth while reducing stores, testing categories cautiously, and using [[CurryBrand|Curry Brand]] cooperation as a brand-depth move under [[AthleteSponsorshipStrategy]].
- [[Atour|亚朵]]'s pillow and bedding retail business has grown close to its franchised-hotel revenue scale in the quarter, turning hotel sleep experience into [[HotelRetailFlywheel|retail demand]] through online channels and creator marketing.
- [[Dreame|追觅]]'s car project is described as having shrunk sharply after fast hiring, concept-car announcements, and unresolved factory, manufacturing, and financing plans.
- [[Beike|贝壳]] reports lower revenue but much higher profit, with second-hand housing transactions rising and growth increasingly attributed to operational efficiency rather than more stores or agents.
- [[PremierLeague|Premier League]] front-of-shirt gambling sponsorship restrictions are pushing clubs toward other global sponsors, extending [[SportsBettingSponsorshipExit]] and [[GlobalSportsSponsorship]].
- [[QishuiMusic|汽水音乐]] reached a source-cited 139 million monthly active users in June 2026, surpassing [[NetEaseCloudMusic|NetEase Cloud Music]] and pressuring [[TencentMusic|Tencent Music]] through [[Douyin]] traffic and music-signal advantages.
