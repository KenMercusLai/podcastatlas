# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》839丨你能做到问心无愧吗？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 153 CE [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-840-yanguang-changyuan-de-ren-you-duo-lihai-lvzfg8zbp0nugjg0iybgukz-cxgv|Hanji 840]]. It opens with a source-scoped lesson about recognizing one's resources, limits, effort, and desire boundaries, then turns "问心无愧" into a duty-completion frame rather than a guarantee of success.

The historical center is [[ZhuMuLateHan|朱穆]]'s appointment as [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]] inspector during locusts, Yellow River flooding, famine, and large-scale displacement. Zhu punishes corrupt officials and investigates [[ZhaoZhongLateHan|赵忠]]'s father for burial with imperial garments, is sentenced after Huan becomes angry, and is then pardoned after [[LiuTaoLateHan|刘陶 / 刘桃]] leads thousands of [[Taixue|太学]] students in a collective petition. The closing branches add [[YingFengLateHan|应奉]]'s Wuling pacification and a [[CheshiHouKingdomLateHan|车师后王国]] succession crisis in which [[YanXiangCheshiLateHan|阎祥]] restores [[AluoduoCheshiLateHan|阿罗多]] to prevent wider [[Xiongnu|北匈奴]] involvement.

## Key Claims

- The opening "问心无愧" lesson is source-scoped: the host ties adult awakening to recognizing class background, family resources, personal capacity, desire boundaries, and the need to act without self-deception.
- In 153 CE, locusts, Yellow River flooding, famine, and mass displacement make [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]] the worst-hit region and create the crisis context for Zhu Mu's appointment.
- [[ZhuMuLateHan|Zhu Mu]]'s reputation is already strong enough that more than forty local officials reportedly abandon office after hearing he has crossed the Yellow River.
- Zhu's enforcement reaches beyond ordinary corrupt officials when he checks whether [[ZhaoZhongLateHan|Zhao Zhong]] used imperial garments to bury his father.
- Huan's punishment of Zhu shows how enforcing rank and ritual against eunuch-family privilege can be recoded as an offense against palace-connected power.
- [[LiuTaoLateHan|Liu Tao]] and thousands of Taixue students turn Zhu's case into a public accusation that eunuch relatives dominate local offices, oppress commoners, and make law unreliable.
- The petition works narrowly: Huan is moved enough to pardon Zhu, but the episode does not claim that eunuch power or court feedback is structurally repaired.
- Court office changes place [[HuGuangLateHan|胡广]] and [[HuangQiongLateHan|黄琼]] in high office around the same Huan-era instability field.
- [[YingFengLateHan|Ying Feng]]'s Wuling surrender work supplies an earlier layer before his later campaign-aide and memorial roles.
- The Cheshi branch shows a frontier-recognition solution: after Aluoduo rebels, flees, and returns with local support, Yan Xiang restores him as king to avoid driving the crisis toward Northern Xiongnu intervention.

## Key Quotes

> "问心无愧" - title-level value used to connect self-cultivation with public duty.

> "尽人事" - the practical standard for judging one's own action under limited control.

> "不因虚度年华而痛悔" - the opening frame for not wasting life.
