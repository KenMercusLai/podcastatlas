# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1013丨典韦之死，竟是曹操好色害了他？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the Wancheng disaster immediately after [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1012-haose-caocao-luanshi-zhong-de-dute-qingshi-llvrucihuv2buvp1-lisz4usbprh|Hanji 1012]] and before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1014-caocao-de-15-ge-laopo-dou-you-shui-lv-hyvprg73k8w8jspxjosqwmc69|Hanji 1014]]'s household aftermath. It opens with [[LadyDuCaoCao|杜夫人]] and [[LadyYinCaoCao|尹夫人]] to establish [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s desire pattern, then turns to the Wancheng figure this transcript calls [[LadyZouWancheng|周氏]], [[ZhangXiu|张绣]], [[JiaXu|贾诩]], [[HuCheEr|胡车儿]], and [[DianWei|典韦]] at [[WanCounty|宛城]]. Its core synthesis is [[DesireDrivenCommandExposure|私欲驱动的统帅暴露]]: Cao Cao's private indulgence, perceived insult to a new surrender, attempted control of Zhang Xiu's strong subordinate, and guard disarming combine into a command-security disaster that kills Dian Wei, [[CaoAng|曹昂]], and [[CaoAnmin|曹安民]].

## Key Claims
- [[LadyDuCaoCao|杜夫人]] is introduced as [[QinYilu|秦宜禄]]'s wife, left in [[Xiapi|下邳]] with [[QinLang|秦朗]] while Qin Yilu is held by [[YuanShu|袁术]]; [[GuanYu|关羽]] repeatedly asks Cao Cao for her before Cao Cao sees and takes her himself.
- [[LadyYinCaoCao|尹夫人]] is introduced as [[HeJin|何进]]'s daughter-in-law and [[HeYan|何晏]]'s mother; when Cao Cao takes her in, He Yan becomes Cao Cao's foster son and later a bridge toward [[WeiJinStyleCulturalAfterlife|魏晋风度文化后效]].
- At Wancheng, Cao Cao takes the woman this transcript calls [[LadyZouWancheng|周氏]], Zhang Xiu's aunt by marriage, as a concubine and spends his time with her after Zhang Xiu's surrender.
- Cao Cao then gives gold and silver to [[HuCheEr|胡车儿]], a fierce subordinate of Zhang Xiu, making Zhang Xiu read the situation as both humiliation and direct threat.
- When Cao Cao learns of Zhang Xiu's resentment, he prepares secretly to kill him, but the plan leaks; Zhang Xiu follows [[JiaXu|贾诩]] and moves first.
- Jia Xu's plan has Zhang Xiu request a camp relocation, pass through Cao Cao's camp in armor, and exploit Cao Cao's trust at the moment when Cao Cao's judgment is lax.
- Hu Che'er neutralizes Dian Wei by drinking with him, getting him drunk, and stealing his heavy double halberds before the night attack.
- During the attack, Dian Wei wakes, cannot find his main weapon, fights with a waist sword, kills many attackers, and dies holding the entrance long enough for Cao Cao to escape.
- Cao Cao escapes from the rear, but Cao Anmin is killed, Cao Ang gives his horse to Cao Cao and dies after losing his mount, and Cao Cao is wounded before [[YuJin|于禁]] defeats Zhang Xiu's continued pursuit.
- [[LadyDingCaoCao|丁夫人]] breaks with Cao Cao over Cao Ang's death; the source treats the rupture as a household consequence of the earlier Wancheng command failure.
- Zhang Xiu later retreats to Rangcheng and reconnects with [[LiuBiao|刘表]], while Cao Cao collects the remnants and defends at Wuyin.

## Key Quotes
> "这一切就是为了一个女人" - the episode's compressed judgment on the cost of the Wancheng disaster.

> "九死一生" - the source's summary of Cao Cao's escape.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction found. The source deepens the Wancheng branch already present in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-131-2-pandian-huangdimen-jiuhou-xiaopihao-livpg3zh7lsqffgca2pomiqnlvyd|Qinji 131-2]], [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1012-haose-caocao-luanshi-zhong-de-dute-qingshi-llvrucihuv2buvp1-lisz4usbprh|Hanji 1012]], and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1014-caocao-de-15-ge-laopo-dou-you-shui-lv-hyvprg73k8w8jspxjosqwmc69|Hanji 1014]] rather than reversing them.
- Source-scope caution: the host's claim that the disaster was "for one woman" is recorded as an interpretive causal compression; this page keeps the fuller chain of surrendered-force resentment, leaked murder plan, Jia Xu's tactic, guard disarming, and night attack.
- Naming caution: this transcript's [[LadyZouWancheng|周氏]] is treated as a source-scoped variant for the Wancheng woman whom Hanji 1012 names [[LadyZouWancheng|邹氏]]. It remains distinct from [[ZhouShiRebel|周市 / Zhou Shi (Rebel)]], whose source transcript also rendered the name as "周氏."
