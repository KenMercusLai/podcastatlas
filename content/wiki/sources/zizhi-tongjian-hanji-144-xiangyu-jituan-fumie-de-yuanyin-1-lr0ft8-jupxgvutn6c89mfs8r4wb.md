# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》144｜项羽集团覆灭的原因（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the post-Qin settlement thread from [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-143-xiangyu-shi-ruhe-feijin-xinji-nongsi-chuhuaiwang-mixin-lstfzfvbd2h9i39f7y5dxrbxxwv2|Hanji 143]] by asking why [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s division of the world produced rebellion rather than gratitude. It argues that Xiang Yu's partition carried a severe [[RewardAllocationBacklash|reward allocation backlash]]: old kings and claimants such as [[LiuBang|刘邦]], [[ZhaoXie|赵歇]], [[HanGuang|韩广]], [[WeiBao|魏豹]], and [[HanCheng|韩成]] were demoted or angered, while Xiang Yu also failed to reward his own commanders adequately. The episode then turns to Liu Bang's response: [[XiaoHe|萧何]] reframes the [[Hanzhong|汉中]] assignment as survivable base-building in [[BaShu|巴蜀]], and the host suggests Liu Bang's anger may have been a staged performance to align officers before a [[StrategicRetreatBaseBuilding|strategic retreat base-building]] choice.

## Key Claims
- Xiang Yu's nineteen-king settlement is presented as personally biased rather than as a stabilizing settlement accepted by the main stakeholders.
- The source distinguishes a "倒项派" including Liu Bang, Zhao Xie, the Qi Tian line, Han Guang, Wei Bao, and Han Cheng from a larger "拥项派" that received most of the commanderies.
- Xiang Yu assumes that the "拥项派" can contain the "倒项派" because it controls roughly thirty of forty-three commanderies and holds better connected territory.
- The settlement still fails because the disappointed side keeps grievance, and Xiang Yu also undercuts his own camp by not adequately rewarding his direct subordinates.
- [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]]'s forced move toward [[ChenxianChangsha|郴县]] creates resentment around symbolic authority, not just a geographical relocation.
- The Yan, Qi, Zhao, Wei, and Han examples show the same pattern: commanders aligned with Xiang Yu receive or keep better land, while earlier kings or rival claimants are pushed aside.
- Han Cheng's later killing turns [[ZhangLiang|张良]]'s old Han restoration tie into a deeper anti-Xiang alignment with Liu Bang.
- Liu Bang's initial threat to attack Xiang Yu is read as possibly sincere anger, but the host treats it more plausibly as a "双簧" with Xiao He to manage the morale of [[FanKuai|樊哙]], [[ZhouBo|周勃]], [[GuanYing|灌婴]], and other officers.
- Xiao He's argument converts exile to Hanzhong into a strategy: survive, govern the people, recruit talent, use Ba-Shu resources, and later return toward [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]].
- Liu Bang's gift channel through Zhang Liang and [[XiangBo|项伯]] shows that personal relationships remain part of territorial bargaining even after the formal settlement.

## Key Quotes
> "打天下、分天下、分钱" - the episode's opening frame for why post-victory distribution can destabilize a coalition.

> "百战百败" - Xiao He's warning that immediate war with Xiang Yu would destroy Liu Bang's force.

> "一场双簧" - the host's interpretation of Liu Bang's anger and Xiao He's restraint as possible internal political theater.
