# 《资治通鉴·周纪》92丨历史上第一位著名舔狗是谁？（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode moves from the aftermath of [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-91-chulong-ruhe-qiaokai-shougua-zhaotaihou-de-men-2-liy5yulg2ysop-jwfigvdkub1pk7|Zhouji 91 part 2]] into [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s fifty-first and fifty-second years. It explains why [[QiWangJian|齐王建 / 田建]] is named without a posthumous title, then follows [[FanJu|范雎]]'s [[YuanjiaoJingongStrategy|远交近攻]] into [[BaiQi|白起]]'s attack on [[HanState|韩国]], the cutting of [[TaihangRoad|太行道]], and the isolation of [[Shangdang|上党]].

The second half turns to [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]] and [[MiWan|芈完 / 楚考烈王]] in [[QinState|秦国]]. When [[MiHeng|楚顷襄王]] becomes gravely ill, Huang Xie tries to make Qin see the hostage prince's return as useful, then proposes that Mi Wan escape disguised as a Chu envoy's driver while Huang Xie remains behind to cover him.

## Key Claims
- [[QiWangJian|齐王建 / 田建]] is introduced by personal name because his later conquest by Qin leaves no practical space for a conventional posthumous-name treatment.
- [[BaiQi|白起]]'s attack on Han in 264 BCE and the 263 BCE capture of [[HanNanyangWarringStates|韩国南阳]] plus the [[TaihangRoad|太行道]] belong to [[FanJu|范雎]]'s [[YuanjiaoJingongStrategy|远交近攻]], not to scattered city-taking.
- The strategic point is [[TransportCorridorSeverance|交通线切割]]: by threatening Xingyang, Chenggao, Nanyang, and Taihang links, Qin can divide Han and make [[Shangdang|上党]] lose its route back to Xinzheng.
- The isolated Shangdang commander does not want to surrender to Qin and instead wants to attach the territory to [[ZhaoState|赵国]], setting up the later large Qin-Zhao annihilation-war branch.
- [[FanJu|范雎]] also attacks [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] through [[InterestBasedCoalitionSabotage|利益瓦解合纵]]: if coalition activists mainly seek wealth and status, money and feasting can produce internal quarrels faster than battlefield defeat.
- [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]] argues that [[MiWan|太子完]] is useful to Qin if he returns and becomes Chu king, but worthless if another Chu prince succeeds while Mi Wan remains in Xianyang.
- Qin's suspicion shows the hostage side of succession politics: a prince in a foreign capital is leverage only while the home-state succession remains unsettled and controllable.
- The episode stops with Huang Xie's escape plan and does not yet resolve whether the plan succeeds or how the title's "著名舔狗" judgment will be completed.

## Key Quotes
> "花钱不用记账、钱用完继续拨款" - the episode's opening image for Fan Ju's money-based coalition disruption.

> "正式执行了范雎'远交近攻'的战略方案" - the host's framing of Qin's Han campaign.

> "历史上最早、规模最大的歼灭战" - the source's preview of the coming Shangdang-to-Qin-Zhao war branch.
