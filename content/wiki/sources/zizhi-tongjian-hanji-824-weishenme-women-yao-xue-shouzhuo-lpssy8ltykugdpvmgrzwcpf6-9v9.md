# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》824丨为什么我们要学“守拙”

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 143-144 CE close of [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]] immediately before the [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-825-lishi-gaosu-ni-dang-ni-meiyong-shi-renxing-zui-zisi-luv-8z9axuejnfhlkgsuxee3uked|Hanji 825]] succession crisis. It links [[ZhaoChongLateHan|赵冲]]'s Qiang-front victories and death, [[HuangQiongLateHan|黄琼]]'s expanded xiaolian criteria, [[ZhongHaoLateHan|种暠]]'s sword-backed credential check when [[GaoFanLateHan|高梵]] tries to fetch Crown Prince Liu Bing without edict credentials, and the accession of Liu Bing as [[EmperorChongOfHan|汉冲帝]].

The durable contribution is that "守拙" is treated as disciplined restraint rather than weakness: Zhong Hao does not perform cleverness in ordinary conditions, but when crown-prince safety, edict authority, and irreversible risk converge, he blocks the transfer until verifiable authorization appears. The episode also adds the first bounded [[HuangfuGuiLateHan|皇甫规]] court-remonstrance layer: after Shun's death, Huangfu Gui uses the worthy-and-upright policy question to attack eunuch corruption and urge [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] and [[LiangBuyiLateHan|梁不疑]] toward restraint, triggering Liang Ji's retaliation before the later Huan-era frontier sources.

## Key Claims

- In 143 CE, Zhao Chong and Zhang Gong defeat the Shaodang Qiang, Emperor Shun invests Southern Xiongnu Shouyi Wang Dulouchu as chanyu, Ma Shi has 句龙吾斯 assassinated, Liangzhou suffers repeated earthquakes, and Huang Qiong expands the xiaolian selection criteria beyond儒学 and文吏.
- In 144 CE, Zhao Chong dies pursuing rebel Qiang after hundreds of surrendered Hu defect, but his prior victories leave the Qiang weakened and his son receives a亭侯 title.
- Emperor Shun names Liu Bing crown prince, places him in Chengguang Palace, and appoints Zhong Hao to supervise the crown-prince household.
- Gao Fan arrives from the inner palace without edict or credential to fetch the crown prince; Du Qiao and others hesitate, but Zhong Hao blocks the cart with a sword and demands authorization before allowing transfer.
- The episode interprets Zhong Hao's conduct as "守拙": restrained, non-showy competence that becomes decisive when the cost of a mistaken release is too high.
- Emperor Shun dies in August 144, Liu Bing succeeds at age two, the empress becomes empress dowager and regent, and Shun's Xianling is soon reported robbed after earthquake damage.
- Huangfu Gui's policy answer blames near attendants, bribery, office-selling, and elite excess for disorder, urges removal of bad常侍 and restraint from the Liang brothers, and leads Liang Ji to sideline him for more than ten years.
- Yangzhou, Xuzhou, Jiujiang, Liyang, Rinan, Dangtu Mountain, and Hefei disorder show local unrest spreading before Hanji 825 and Hanji 826 continue the succession and anti-bandit sequence.

## Key Quotes

> "太子，国之储副，人命所系。" - Zhong Hao's reason for refusing an unauthorized crown-prince transfer.

> "今常侍来，无诏信，何以知非奸邪？" - the credential check that turns suspicion into a rule-bound boundary.

> "小事糊涂，大事杀伐果断" - the host's practical definition of restraint with decisive limits.
