## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode connects [[GuanYu|关羽]]'s anger at being ranked alongside [[HuangZhong|黄忠]] with his 219 CE northern offensive at the [[FanchengBattle219LateHan|Battle of Fancheng]]. [[FeiShiLateHan|费诗]] reportedly waits for Guan Yu's outburst to subside, then restores the ruler-minister and collective-mission frame while making refusal and regret visible; the episode reads this as [[RespectBasedConflictDeescalation|respect-based conflict de-escalation]] under status anxiety.

The military branch follows the Han River flood that destroys [[YuJin|于禁]]'s field army, Yu Jin's surrender, [[PangDe|庞德]]'s refusal and execution, and Guan Yu's pressure on Fancheng. [[ManChong|满宠]] and [[CaoRen|曹仁]] reject evacuation, stabilize the defense, and hold until [[XuHuangCaoGeneral|徐晃]]'s relief, giving [[CaoCao|曹操]] time to coordinate with [[SunQuan|孙权]]; force totals, dialogue, motives, and the host's psychological interpretation remain source-scoped.

## Key Claims

- Liu Bei appoints Guan Yu as 前将军, but Guan Yu reacts angrily because Huang Zhong receives a comparable rank after the Hanzhong campaign.
- Fei Shi does not contest the outburst immediately; after it subsides, he reframes the appointment as a ruler-minister and group decision, states that he can report refusal, and warns that Guan Yu may regret it.
- Guan Yu accepts the appointment, while the episode interprets his anger as insecurity about unique status and recognition rather than proof that every angry reaction has the same cause.
- A related anecdote has Zhuge Liang praise Guan Yu as singularly superior after Guan Yu asks to compete with Ma Chao, again using status confirmation to reduce rivalry.
- In 219, Guan Yu advances north from Jingzhou while Mi Fang and Fu Shiren remain behind; sustained rain and Han River flooding destroy the position of Yu Jin's seven armies.
- Yu Jin surrenders, while Pang De continues fighting, is captured after a failed withdrawal, rejects Guan Yu's offer, and is executed.
- With Fancheng damaged and defenders considering withdrawal, Man Chong argues that the flood will recede and that abandoning the city could expose the region south of the Yellow River; Cao Ren and the garrison swear to hold.
- Guan Yu's advance produces defections and severe pressure near Xu, but Cao Ren's continued defense and Xu Huang's relief preserve time for Cao Cao's later coordination with Sun Quan.
