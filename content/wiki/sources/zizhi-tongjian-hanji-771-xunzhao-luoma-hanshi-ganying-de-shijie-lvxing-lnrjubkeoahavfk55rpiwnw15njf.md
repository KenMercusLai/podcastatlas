# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》771丨寻找罗马！汉使甘英的世界旅行

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 98 CE [[EmperorHeOfHan|汉和帝]] layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-772-ta-weihe-ningyuan-chengwei-taofan-ye-buyuan-jicheng-jiaye-lrou5naye7opb5favwuz6bqbggae|Hanji 772]]. It centers [[BanChaoEasternHan|班超]] sending [[GanYingEasternHan|甘英]] toward [[DaQin|大秦]] / [[RomanEmpire|罗马帝国]] and [[TiaozhiKingdom|条支国]], with [[ParthianEmpire|安息 / 帕提亚帝国]] acting as the route intermediary whose sea-risk description stops the mission short.

The durable synthesis is that Gan Ying's failure to reach Rome is still a knowledge event. The mission uses Ban Chao's Western Regions position to gather far-western information, while the episode's geography discussion turns the "great sea" into a case of [[IntermediaryRouteGatekeeping|中介路线门控]] and contested route interpretation rather than simple personal timidity.

## Key Claims

- Ban Chao sends Gan Ying to Da Qin, identified by the episode with the Roman Empire, and also toward Tiaozhi.
- Gan Ying reaches the western boundary of Parthia, which the host maps broadly to today's Iran region.
- Parthian officials tell Gan Ying that the route to Rome requires dangerous sea travel, sometimes needing months or even years and large food stores.
- Gan Ying turns back after hearing this route account and reports what he saw and heard to Emperor He.
- The episode explains "Da Qin" through the `Hou Hanshu` idea that the far-western people looked orderly and somewhat like Chinese people, so Han writers used a familiar prestige name.
- The host favors the Persian Gulf as the more likely "great sea" Gan Ying faced, while noting a Mediterranean interpretation associated with Bo Yang and keeping the Red Sea direction as related geography.
- The annal layer also records a Luoyang flood, Han Leng's death and Chao Kan's appointment as sikong, punishment of Liu Shang and Zhao Shi by removal rather than death, Mi Tang's surrender, and Liu Kai's title-refusal teaser.

## Key Quotes

> "顺风也要三个月" - the sea-route warning Gan Ying hears from Parthian officials.

> "有类中国" - the cited explanation for why Han sources called the far-western polity Da Qin.

> "波斯湾最靠谱" - the episode's preferred reading of the sea Gan Ying reached.
