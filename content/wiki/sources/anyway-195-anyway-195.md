# №195: 设计师的黄金时代过去了吗？和 UX Coffee 聊 AI、书店与「离开屏幕」

## Summary
This [[AnywayFM|Anyway.FM 设计杂谈]] crossover catches up with [[UXCoffee|UX Coffee]] after several years of changed work, travel, and post-pandemic expectations. The first half follows UX Coffee's move toward an offline Chinese-language bookstore and cultural retail project in the United States, while the second half turns to [[AIUXDesignValueCompression]], [[AIJobSecurityAnxiety]], and the possibility that AI-enabled small tools and side projects can become a personal outlet. Its main wiki contribution is a bridge between [[ChineseBookstoreCulturalWindow]], [[ScreenExitCreativePractice]], [[BookstoreSerendipity]], and [[AIEnabledSelfEmployment]].

## Key Claims
- UX Coffee's recent work has shifted from screen-centered UI/UX discussion toward an offline bookstore, market stalls, objects, crafts, and possible future interviews with makers and craftspeople.
- The bookstore project is framed as both a personal demand for Chinese books in the United States and a cultural window that can present contemporary Chinese design, art books, ceramics, glassware, and everyday goods to local visitors.
- The source treats offline retail as materially demanding: sourcing from Chinese platforms, freight forwarding, inventory, packing, supplier schedules, neighbor complaints, market stalls, and weekend labor all make the project unlike late-night podcast production.
- The AI discussion is pessimistic about routine UI/UX labor because the speakers believe AI can compress the number of designers needed and move product value toward model quality rather than interface refinement.
- The episode qualifies that pessimism with a builder-oriented upside: AI lowers the barrier for designers and ordinary people to make small tools or side projects, even though engineering quality, QA, and the last mile from a prototype to a polished product remain hard.
- The source connects career revaluation to changed work effort: the guests describe moving away from 150% work intensity, seeing diminishing returns from overwork, and treating the bookstore as their own "摸摸鱼" or outlet.

## Key Quotes
> "不要卖谷歌股票" - the joking answer to what the guest would tell her earlier self.

> "摸摸鱼" - the episode's shorthand for a personal outlet outside the main tech-work track.

> "从 0 到 1 变容易" - the positive AI framing that still leaves craft and engineering quality difficult.
