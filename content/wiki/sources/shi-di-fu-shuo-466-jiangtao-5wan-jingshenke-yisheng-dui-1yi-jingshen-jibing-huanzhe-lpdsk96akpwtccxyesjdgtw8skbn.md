# 史蒂夫说466期 - 姜涛 - 5万精神科医生对1亿精神疾病患者

## Summary
This [[ShiDiFuShuo|史蒂夫说]] episode has [[SteveShiDiFuShuoHost|Steve]] interview [[JiangTaoPsychiatrist|姜涛]], a psychiatrist at [[BeijingAndingHospital|北京市安定医院]], around his book [[AndingCiXinBook|《安定此心》]] and three decades of psychiatric practice. It extends the wiki's mental-health branch from diagnosis and destigmatization into [[AdolescentSchoolRefusalMentalHealth]], [[ChinaPsychiatricWorkforceGap]], [[PsychiatryPsychotherapyCollaboration]], [[MentalHealthHelpSeekingScamRisk]], and [[PsychiatricProfessionalBoundary]].

The core synthesis is that psychiatric care needs both normalization and limits. Jiang Tao argues against mystifying or shaming mental illness, but he also warns against casual internet labels, overdiagnosis, informal "healing" sellers, family overcorrection, and the expectation that a psychiatrist can solve school, family, treatment, resource, and social-support problems alone.

## Key Claims
- [[JiangTaoPsychiatrist|姜涛]] describes psychiatry as long-term work across outpatient care, inpatient care, crisis risk, medication, rehabilitation, family support, and social-function recovery.
- [[PsychiatricFunctionalDiagnosis]] is reinforced by the episode's warning that schizophrenia, ADHD, NPD, depression, anxiety, self-harm, and school refusal all require professional context rather than online self-labeling.
- [[MentalIllnessDestigmatization]] has two sides in the source: mental illness should not be shameful or mysterious, but diagnostic terms should also not become casual identity stickers.
- [[AdolescentSchoolRefusalMentalHealth]] is presented as a hard system problem involving symptoms, family stance, education pressure, bullying, sleep, self-control, therapy access, and school environment.
- [[ChinaPsychiatricWorkforceGap]] names the shortage behind tense access: the source contrasts roughly 50,000 psychiatrists with very large psychiatric-patient demand and scarce reliable psychotherapists.
- [[PsychiatryPsychotherapyCollaboration]] separates psychiatry, psychotherapy, family therapy, and counseling while showing how they should refer to and support one another.
- [[MentalHealthHelpSeekingScamRisk]] captures Jiang Tao's warning that desperate patients and families can spend large sums on unqualified online "healers" instead of regular care.
- [[PsychiatricProfessionalBoundary]] is central: doctors can reduce risk, diagnose, treat, and guide rehabilitation, but cannot guarantee rescue when family cooperation, social resources, or patient collaboration fail.

## Key Quotes
> "他算老几" - a 13-year-old patient's blunt rejection, used to show the difficulty of adolescent treatment.

> "不要给自己随便贴标签" - Jiang Tao's warning against casual ADHD, NPD, and other self-diagnosis.

> "把钱花在刀刃上" - his advice to seek regular treatment instead of expensive informal healing services.
