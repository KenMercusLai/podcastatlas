# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1014丨曹操的15个老婆，都有谁？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode bridges the [[CaoCao|曹操]] Wancheng aftermath and the later [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1015-sanguo-luanshi-weishenme-yuanshu-gan-chengdi-lim0jemkwyjcesj2nvsr3jndhxfk|Hanji 1015]] strategic branch. It opens with Cao Cao's household and [[FenxiangMailu|分香卖履]] deathbed arrangement, then turns to [[YuJin|于禁]] restoring order during the Wancheng retreat through [[CrisisFirstMilitaryDiscipline|临危先务军纪]]. The final section has [[XunYu|荀彧]] and [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] answer [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s pressure with [[TenVictoriesTenDefeats|十胜十败论]], a leadership-comparison frame that makes Cao Cao's weaker force look strategically viable before the later Yuan Shao confrontation.

## Key Claims
- The episode lists Cao Cao's household as seven fu-ren, one zhaoyi, and seven ji: 丁夫人, [[LadyBianCaoCao|卞夫人]], 刘夫人, 桓夫人, 杜夫人, 秦夫人, 尹夫人, 王昭仪, 陈姬, 孙姬, 李姬, 周姬, 刘姬, 宋姬, and 赵姬.
- [[LadyDingCaoCao|丁夫人]] is presented as Cao Cao's original wife and [[CaoAng|曹昂]]'s foster mother; Cao Ang's death after Wancheng becomes the rupture point between her and Cao Cao.
- [[FenxiangMailu|分香卖履]] is read as a household-settlement story: Cao Cao divides incense, refuses to bury it with him, tells the women to support themselves through handiwork if needed, and allows remarriage rather than demanding death-bound loyalty.
- During the Wancheng retreat, [[YuJin|于禁]] keeps his own troops in order, suppresses plundering by Cao Cao's Qingzhou soldiers, and prioritizes fortification against pursuit before defending himself against accusations.
- Cao Cao praises Yu Jin for restoring order and securing the camp under chaotic conditions, then rewards him as 益寿亭侯; the episode makes this a case where [[MeritBasedRewardPunishment|因功赏罚]] follows crisis performance.
- After receiving Yuan Shao's arrogant letter, Cao Cao wants to fight but admits he is materially weaker; Xun Yu and Guo Jia respond by comparing the two leaders across conduct, legitimacy, governance discipline, talent use, decisiveness, sincerity, care, clarity, reward-and-punishment, and military ability.
- The episode treats the ten-part comparison as more than morale talk: troop count matters, but leadership systems, adviser confidence, organizational discipline, and usable talent can change the perceived balance.
- The host notes a source-layer difference: the 《三国志》 tradition has Xun Yu discuss the comparison with Cao Cao, while [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] records Xun Yu and Guo Jia together; later literary transmission also uses the frame.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction found. The source extends the existing Wancheng and late-Han Cao Cao branch by adding household consequences, Yu Jin's retreat conduct, and the ten-victories comparison before Hanji 1015.
- Source-scope caution: the full household list is preserved as this episode's enumeration; only Ding夫人 and Bian夫人 receive canonical pages because the source gives them concrete relational roles.
- Source-scope caution: the modern confidence and relationship lessons are recorded as the host's interpretive extension, not as a standalone historical claim about why Cao Cao later defeated Yuan Shao.
