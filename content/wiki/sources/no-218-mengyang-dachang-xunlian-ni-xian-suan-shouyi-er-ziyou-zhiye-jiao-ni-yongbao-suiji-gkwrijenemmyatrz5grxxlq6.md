# No.218 孟阳：大厂训练你先算收益，而自由职业教你拥抱随机

## Summary
This [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] episode has [[LiuFei|刘飞]] interviewing [[MengYangStandup|梦阳 / 孟阳]] about a career spanning accounting, [[KPMG]], English teaching, Phoenix Finance, [[Tencent]], [[TencentWeishi|腾讯微视]], entrepreneurship, short video, and stand-up comedy. The conversation contrasts legible retrospective career stories with the contingent reality of personal motives, platform timing, experimentation, and feedback.

Its durable contribution is [[ROIMindsetCreativeSpillover|大厂 ROI 思维的创作外溢]]. Planning, growth targets, bad-case avoidance, and return calculations can be useful inside large organizations, yet become premature filters when transferred wholesale into creative discovery. The episode instead joins [[StandupComedyIteration]], [[ExperimentalLifeDesign]], and repeated publishing: action generates information about fit, audience response, excitement, and opportunity that prior analysis cannot fully supply.

## Key Claims
- [[MengYangStandup|梦阳 / 孟阳]] describes herself as an introverted, low-energy person who can nevertheless enter a high-expression state onstage or before an audience; one-to-one social monitoring can be more draining than public performance.
- Her route from accounting at the University of Macau through [[KPMG]], English teaching, Phoenix Finance, and [[Tencent]] was shaped by both ability and contingency, including personal motives and mobile-internet timing that a polished career narrative can hide.
- Her [[TencentWeishi|腾讯微视]] account complicates simple failure stories: the team reportedly reached about 40 million DAU and used strategies similar to competitors, yet still remained behind [[Douyin]], suggesting that timing, ecosystem, organization, and product inheritance matter alongside visible execution errors.
- A failed Alibaba interview made her notice that middle management had pulled her toward aggregate traffic, followers, process, and competitive comparison while away from the details of comedy content that actually interested her.
- [[ROIMindsetCreativeSpillover|大厂 ROI 思维的创作外溢]] appears when growth, return, and “cannot fail” habits become self-screening rules for short videos, podcasts, new channels, or freelance projects before real feedback exists.
- [[StandupComedyIteration]] supplied unusually direct occupational feedback: after only a few open-mic performances, audience laughter connected her own stories and viewpoints with an immediate craft signal.
- Short video began as promotion for live performance but became part of the same creative system; concise videos establish a premise or phenomenon, while stand-up can develop the underlying thought more deeply.
- Both speakers argue that repeated action is more reliable than waiting for a fully resolved mindset: publishing and performing reveal what works, loosen imagined quality thresholds, and make the next problem concrete.
- [[LiuFei|刘飞]] says the later returns from podcasting, including [[Banlatte|半拿铁]], were not fully predictable in advance and depended partly on platform and content-supply timing.
- Freelance uncertainty changes the needed temperament: a corporate strength in challenging plans and preventing bad cases can turn inward as paralysis when the same person must choose and judge every experiment.
- Mengyang's commitment becomes clearest when she imagines changing her cost of living to continue stand-up rather than immediately changing careers to preserve her existing lifestyle.

## Key Quotes
> “单口喜剧是上舞台演自己” - Mengyang on why audience laughter feels like direct recognition of her own story and viewpoint.

> “创作者的心态永远不可能完全调整好” - her argument for maintaining output instead of waiting for certainty.

> “做什么都能 challenge 出问题” - her description of a large-company risk habit turning against self-directed work.

> “拥抱不确定性” - the closing frame for creative work and freelance life.
