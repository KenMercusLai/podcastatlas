# 《资治通鉴·周纪》53丨秦惠王用张仪换地皮

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] and [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] branch after the failed [[Shangyu|商於]] promise, the [[DanyangBattle|丹阳之战]], and [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-52-chenzhuang-panqin-duanqi-wan-chifan-fangxia-wan-ma-niang-ls13ixmbhflepjgpelxgu6idjqa2|Zhouji 52]]'s cliffhanger. In 311 BCE, [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] proposes trading Qin land around Wuguan/Shangyu for Chu's [[Qianzhong|黔中]], but Chu Huaiwang replies that he wants Zhang Yi instead of land. Zhang Yi enters hostile Chu, survives imprisonment through [[CourtBackchannelSurvival|宫廷后门自救]] involving [[ZhengXiu|郑袖]], and then reframes the Qin-Chu balance through threats from [[HanState|韩国]], [[WeiState|魏国]], [[BaShu|巴蜀]], and [[Wuguan|武关]]. The episode's governing synthesis is that Zhang Yi's survival is not just eloquence: it depends on backchannel preparation, palace incentives, Qin military pressure, and a ruler who may treat even a valued minister as a [[MinisterAsExchangeableAsset|可交换功臣]].

## Key Claims
- In 311 BCE, Qin Huiwen Wang asks to exchange Qin land east of Wuguan and around Shangyu for Chu's Qianzhong.
- Chu Huaiwang says he does not want land exchange; if Qin hands over Zhang Yi, Chu will give Qianzhong to Qin.
- Zhang Yi requests permission to enter Chu even though Qin Huiwen Wang warns that Chu Huaiwang hates him and may kill him.
- Zhang Yi argues that [[QinState|秦国]] is strong and [[ChuState|楚国]] is weak, so Chu Huaiwang will not lightly kill him while Qin Huiwen Wang is alive.
- Zhang Yi also says he has a good relationship with a Chu favorite, and that this favorite can influence Zheng Xiu, who is favored by Chu Huaiwang.
- After Zhang Yi reaches Chu, Chu Huaiwang imprisons him and prepares to kill him.
- The Chu favorite persuades Zheng Xiu that Qin may redeem Zhang Yi with [[Shangyong|上庸]]'s six counties and a beautiful woman, threatening Zheng Xiu's position.
- Zheng Xiu then repeatedly pleads with Chu Huaiwang, arguing that Zhang Yi was serving his own ruler, that killing him may anger Qin, and that she and her children should be moved south to avoid Qin's retaliation.
- Chu Huaiwang releases Zhang Yi and gives him high ceremonial treatment.
- Zhang Yi then warns that hezong is like driving sheep against a tiger: Qin can press Han and Wei into attacking Chu, use Ba-Shu to threaten Chu from the west, and use Wuguan to sever Chu's northern territory.
- Chu Huaiwang accepts Zhang Yi's proposal for Qin and Chu to become brother states because he has already obtained Zhang Yi and does not want to cede Qianzhong.
- The host argues through [[Shiji|《史记》]]'s Zhang Yi biography that Qin Huiwen Wang may have calculated both outcomes: if Chu uses Zhang Yi, Qin can receive Qianzhong; if Chu kills Zhang Yi, Qin can use the [[EnvoyKillingTaboo|不斩来使]] norm as a war pretext.
- The episode reads Zhang Yi's willingness to go as partly strategic self-rescue and partly a bitter recognition that Qin Huiwen Wang did not value him above the territorial bargain.

## Key Quotes
> "驱群羊而攻猛虎" - Zhang Yi's image for an anti-Qin coalition attacking Qin.

> "两国相争不斩来使" - diplomatic norm the host uses to frame the possible Qin war-pretext calculation.
