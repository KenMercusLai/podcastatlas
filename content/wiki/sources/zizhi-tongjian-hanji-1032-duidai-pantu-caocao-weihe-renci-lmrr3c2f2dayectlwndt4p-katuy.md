# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1032丨对待叛徒，曹操为何仁慈？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode bridges [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]]'s fall and the [[GuanduCampaign|官渡]] horizon. It follows [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s northern-rear settlement through Wuhuan and Youzhou actors, [[TianYuLateHan|田豫]]'s advice that [[XianyuFu|鲜于辅]] submit to Cao Cao's emperor-backed court, and [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s Yellow River operation against [[SuiGuLateHan|眭固]] at [[ShequanLateHan|射犬]]. Its central Cao-side point is [[WeiZhongLateHan|魏种]]: a former Cao recommendee who fled during the Yanzhou crisis, was captured, and was still pardoned and appointed because Cao valued his talent.

## Key Claims
- After Gongsun Zan's destruction, Yuan Shao holds the Qing, You, Bing, and Ji field but still has to stabilize his northern rear before a direct Cao-Yuan confrontation.
- Yuan Shao works the Wuhuan frontier by favoring [[TadunLateHan|蹋顿]], Nanlou, Supuyan, and Wuyan with chanyu seals and by treating [[YanRou|阎柔]] well after the anti-Gongsun coalition.
- Tian Yu advises Xianyu Fu that Cao Cao's emperor-backed command makes submission to Cao the more legitimate path; Emperor Xian then appoints Xianyu Fu Jianzhong general and lets him oversee six Youzhou commanderies.
- Cao Cao crosses the Yellow River, sends Shi Huan and [[CaoRen|曹仁]] against Sui Gu, and later accepts Shequan's surrender after Sui Gu is killed while trying to reach Yuan Shao.
- Cao Cao had once recommended Wei Zhong as xiaolian and expected him not to betray during the Yanzhou crisis; after capturing him at Shequan, Cao personally unbinds him and appoints him commandery governor because of talent.
- The episode presents the pre-Guandu field as a multi-front pressure map: Yuan Shao in the north, Guanzhong watching from the west, Liu Biao and Zhang Xiu in the south, Sun Ce in the southeast, and Liu Bei as an unreliable temporary subordinate.
- Liu Bei is framed as a small but resilient mobile group after Lü Bu's earlier seizure of Xuzhou.

## Key Quotes
> "为其才也" - Cao Cao's explanation for sparing Wei Zhong.

> "曹操尊奉天子" - Tian Yu's legitimacy argument as summarized by the episode.
