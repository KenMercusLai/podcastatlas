# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1021丨曹操手握重权，为什么容不下杨彪？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the [[YangBiao|杨彪]] case from [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1020-sanguo-baijiazi-yuanshu-ruhe-ba-hao-pai-da-de-xilan-lhwr9hfmanr-zktleufy3kvl38u0|Hanji 1020]] by explaining why [[CaoCao|曹操]] releases Yang Biao but removes him from the taiwei office in 197 CE. The episode frames the decision as court-control and elite-risk management: Cao Cao wants to weaken old-Han loyalist resistance around [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], but Yang Biao's prestige and [[KongRong|孔融]]'s protest make execution too costly. It then widens to [[MaRidiLateHan|马日磾]] and [[JinShang|金尚]] after [[YuanShu|袁术]], Cao Cao's [[HuyangLateHan|胡阳]] and [[WuyinLateHan|舞阴]] operation against the [[ZhangXiu|张绣]]-[[LiuBiao|刘表]] line, and the collapse of [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]] and [[HanXianLateHan|韩暹]] as former [[BaiboArmy|白波军]] commanders.

## Key Claims
- Cao Cao's release of Yang Biao is not full reconciliation: the episode treats release plus removal from taiwei as a balance between striking an old-Han symbol and avoiding an elite backlash.
- The Yang Biao case is read as a public break between Cao Cao and old Han-court ministers: it warns loyalists, weakens Emperor Xian's practical authority, and signals pressure toward large clans such as the Runan Yuan and Yingchuan Xun.
- [[MaRidiLateHan|马日磾]]'s returned body reopens the question of how much honor a court envoy deserves after [[YuanShu|袁术]] humiliates him and seizes the imperial tally.
- [[JinShang|金尚]] is honored because he refuses Yuan Shu's office and is killed, making posthumous mourning a court-side loyalty signal under [[MingqiLegitimacy|名器合法性]].
- Cao Cao's winter attack on [[ZhangXiu|张绣]] is framed as more than revenge for Wancheng: by taking [[HuyangLateHan|胡阳]] and [[WuyinLateHan|舞阴]] and capturing [[DengJiLateHan|邓济]], he strikes [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s northern screen and pressures [[NanyangCommandery|南阳郡]].
- Yang Feng and Han Xian's end shows the limits of mobile armed prestige: after escorting Emperor Xian and switching coalitions, they still lack food, discipline, and durable political judgment.
- [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s killing of Yang Feng at Pei is treated as removal of an unstable armed actor rather than a durable alliance with him.

## Key Quotes
> "打击以杨彪为代表的保皇派" - the episode's summary of Cao Cao's political intent.

> "削弱献帝权威，震慑士族大族" - the court-control interpretation of the Yang Biao decision.

> "东汉末年流动性武人集团的典型代表" - the episode's characterization of Han Xian, Yang Feng, and related White Wave remnants.

## Contradictions
