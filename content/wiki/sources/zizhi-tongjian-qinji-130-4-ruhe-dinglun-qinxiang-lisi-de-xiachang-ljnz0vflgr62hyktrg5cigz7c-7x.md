# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》130-4｜如何定论秦相李斯的下场

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode returns from the anti-Qin field to [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]]'s court, where [[ZhaoGao|赵高]] persuades Hu Hai to stop appearing before ministers and turns imperial seclusion into [[PalaceAccessMonopoly|宫廷接触垄断]]. Zhao Gao then traps [[LiSi|李斯]] through badly timed remonstrance, accusations against [[LiYou|李由]], and control over Hu Hai's trust. The episode's central judgment is that Li Si is both a victim of Zhao Gao and Hu Hai and a participant whose earlier compromises and late self-protection made his downfall politically intelligible.

## Key Claims
- [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]]'s slaughter of brothers and sisters creates a terror climate after accession, and the episode says [[LiSi|李斯]] shares responsibility because of his earlier role in the succession order.
- [[ZhaoGao|赵高]] tells Hu Hai that imperial dignity depends on being heard but not seen, and recommends that he review memorials inside the palace with only a few law-familiar attendants.
- Hu Hai accepts the advice, stops holding regular court, and stops meeting senior officials, letting Zhao Gao monopolize access between ruler and outer court.
- Li Si resents being unable to see Hu Hai even as chancellor; Zhao Gao uses that resentment to lure him into remonstrance.
- Zhao Gao repeatedly arranges for Li Si to request audience while Hu Hai is privately enjoying himself, making remonstrance feel to Hu Hai like harassment rather than loyal correction.
- Zhao Gao then frames Li Si as dissatisfied after the [[ShaqiuCrisis|沙丘]] transition and alleges that [[LiYou|李由]], governor of [[SanchuanWarringStates|三川]], has tolerated or corresponded with rebels linked to Li Si's home region.
- Hu Hai sends investigators to [[Luoyi|洛阳 / 洛邑]] to examine the accusation against Li You.
- Li Si realizes the trap and memorializes against Zhao Gao, warning that Zhao Gao's power over punishment and reward is becoming emperor-like.
- Li Si uses [[TianChang|田常 / 田恒]] as a warning case: a minister can steal rulerly favor and authority, then overthrow the ruler.
- Hu Hai trusts Zhao Gao, rebukes Li Si for attacking him, and hands Li Si's memorial to Zhao Gao, completing the feedback failure.
- As rebellion grows, right chancellor [[FengQuji|冯去疾]], left chancellor Li Si, and general [[FengJieQin|冯劫 / 冯杰]] jointly ask Hu Hai to suspend [[AfangPalace|阿房宫]] construction and reduce frontier rotations because labor and taxes are worsening the crisis.
- Hu Hai rejects the remonstrance, argues that imperial power means being able to do what he wants, accuses the three ministers of failing to stop rebels while trying to abolish Qin Shi Huang's policies, and orders them jailed under other charges.

## Key Quotes
> "赵高由此一手遮天" - the source's summary of the access shift after Hu Hai stops meeting officials.

> "切断皇帝与外朝的信息联系" - the source's inference about Zhao Gao's palace-seclusion advice.

> "李斯既是受害者，也是此前政治选择的参与者" - the episode's source-scoped judgment on Li Si's end.
