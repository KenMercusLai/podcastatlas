# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》818丨官场为什么要多交友？能救命！

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 136-137 CE [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]] layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-819-2-ren-gong-bi-4-wanren-tade-quanmou-lingren-paianjuejue-lsslj3y-krnd8h-c8x5pgsqeinff|Hanji 819]]. It centers on [[WangGongTaiweiLateHan|王龚]] surviving a eunuch-backed accusation because [[LiGuLateHan|李固]] appeals to [[LiangShangLateHan|梁商]], who then persuades Shun to cancel the investigation.

The episode then links court protection failure to local and frontier instability. [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] murders the informer Lu Fang and turns the cover-up into mass punishment; [[YuXuEasternHan|虞诩]] warns against increasing [[WulingManLateHan|武陵蛮]] taxes but is ignored; the Wuling and Xianglin crises show how fiscal extraction and long-distance conscription can turn submitted or auxiliary groups into rebels. [[FaZhenEasternHan|法真]]'s refusal to serve and Wang Gong's later retreat from impeaching [[ZhangFangEunuchLateHan|张防]] keep the episode's survival theme explicit.

## Key Claims
- Wang Gong's anti-eunuch memorial triggers retaliatory accusation, but Li Gu and Liang Shang create a protective court chain that lets Shun cancel the inquiry.
- The episode turns "多交友" into [[CourtAllyProtectionNetwork|朝廷盟友保护网络]]: a vulnerable official needs credible supporters who can reach decision-makers before hostile accusations harden.
- Liang Ji's early Henan Yin layer already contains informer murder, blame-shifting, and manipulated prosecution before his later Grand General, succession, and terror record.
- Yu Xu's rejected warning about Wuling taxes shows that uniform extraction from recently submitted frontier people can cost more stability than it gains revenue.
- The Wuling revolt and later Li Jin pacification show both sides of frontier governance: burden triggers rebellion, while capable local administration can restore order after military suppression.
- Xianglin shows [[FrontierConscriptionBackfire|边疆征发反噬]] in realized form: troops drafted from Jiaozhi and Jiuzhen for a distant rescue rebel themselves and leave the original crisis unresolved.
- Fa Zhen's refusal of repeated summons contrasts with Wang Gong's office danger, making withdrawal from service another form of survival under a risky court.
- Wang Gong's later abandoned impeachment of Zhang Fang, after relatives cite [[YangZhenLateHan|杨震]], shows that moral courage is constrained by remembered retaliation.

## Key Quotes
> "朝廷还得有人支持，才好做官" - host's summary after Li Gu and Liang Shang protect Wang Gong.

> "所得不偿所费" - Yu Xu's warning that added tax revenue would not cover the cost of rebellion.

> "百世之师" - Guo Zheng's praise of Fa Zhen's refusal to seek office or reputation.
