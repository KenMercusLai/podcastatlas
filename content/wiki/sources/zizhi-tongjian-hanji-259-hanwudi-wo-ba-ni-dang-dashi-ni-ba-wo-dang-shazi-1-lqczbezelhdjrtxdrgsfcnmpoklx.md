# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》259｜汉武帝：我把你当大师，你把我当傻子？（1）

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode places [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s early immortality seeking in 133 BCE, when a tour toward the Bohai coast and longing for [[Penglai|蓬莱]] precede the arrival of [[LiShaojunWesternHan|李少君]]. Li Shaojun reportedly establishes an extraordinary-age persona through knowledge of an old hunt and an ancient vessel, then joins stove sacrifice, spirit access, cinnabar-to-gold transformation, gold vessels, [[AnQisheng|安期生]], and fengshan into a staged route toward long life.

The observable result is court action rather than verified immortality: Wudi gives Li Shaojun responsibility for medicines, performs the stove sacrifice, funds material transformation, and sends searchers to sea. After Li Shaojun dies, Wudi interprets a dragon-ascent dream as evidence that the fangshi escaped death and orders [[KuanShuWesternHan|宽舒]] to continue the coastal search, showing how a claimant's death can be absorbed into rather than falsify the supernatural premise.

## Key Claims

- The episode dates an early turn in Wudi's immortality seeking to his 133 BCE tour, sacrifice at Yong, and coastal desire to reach Penglai.
- Li Shaojun's credibility is built cumulatively: claimed great age, knowledge of an elderly man's grandfather, identification of a Qi-period bronze vessel, and asserted personal access to An Qisheng.
- His prescription is a dependency chain rather than one trick: sacrifice to the stove deity, summon spirits, transform cinnabar into gold, use gold vessels, meet Penglai immortals, and finally perform fengshan.
- Wudi converts the claims into office, ritual, material experimentation, expenditure, and maritime search.
- Li Shaojun's death does not end the program because Wudi interprets it through his prior dream as transformation and ascent.
- The account adds an early court-alchemy case in which material transformation is valuable because it promises bodily longevity and sacred access, not ordinary monetary production alone.

## Key Quotes

> “祠灶则致物，致物而丹砂可化为黄金。” - the episode's reported sequence from stove sacrifice to spirits and material transformation.

> “臣尝游海上，见安期生。” - Li Shaojun's claimed personal access to an immortal associated with Penglai.
