## Summary
This [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] and [[SixiaoComedy|四孝喜剧]] live panel has [[LuoYonghao|罗永浩]], [[KuaigeStandup|快哥]], [[HulanStandup|呼兰]], [[WangJiye|王继业 / 王季叶]], and [[XuanZongStandup|选总]] discuss "势利" as more than money, power, and status worship. The episode expands it into intelligence, education, cultural taste, appearance, fashion, moral values, career opportunity, comedy-industry hierarchy, leader-employee pressure, and romantic or luxury consumption. Its main synthesis is that [[SnobberyAsValueRanking|snobbery]] is morally uncomfortable because it is both a status vice and a practical resource-allocation habit under limited attention, money, time, and institutional capacity.

## Key Claims
- The episode reframes "势利" as [[SnobberyAsValueRanking|value ranking]] across money, power, intelligence, education, taste, appearance, fashion, morality, and values rather than as only vulgar wealth worship.
- [[DunbarNumber|邓巴数]] is used to explain why people cannot distribute attention equally, making selective social investment a structural pressure rather than only a character flaw.
- Comedy-industry examples show [[StatusFilteredSocialInvestment|upward networking and downward betting]]: established figures receive attention, while promising newcomers may be helped because their future status is still uncertain.
- Extreme choice problems create [[EthicalTriageUnderScarcity|ethical triage under scarcity]]: preserving 1,000 humans, choosing a child for a lifeboat, or admitting a donor-backed mediocre student forces value comparison that no answer fully cleans up.
- Workplace and family examples turn snobbery into priority ordering among opportunity, friendship, family responsibility, and hidden authority pressure.
- [[AfterHoursPowerBoundary|after-hours power boundaries]] matter because employees may appear willing to socialize with a boss while actually feeling unable to refuse.
- The company-crisis discussion frames [[CrisisOptimismTransparencyTradeoff|crisis optimism versus transparency]] as a leadership dilemma between morale, delivery, and employee exit rights.
- [[KaihuaClub|开花俱乐部]] is used as a small-organization case where sincerity and sympathy can support survival but may blur authority and efficiency.
- Diamond rings, Valentine's Day, flowers, and luxury goods become [[DiamondRomanceMarketingTrap|romantic consumption traps]] or [[LuxuryStatusSignalEfficiency|status-signal tools]] when love, class recognition, and business communication are attached to costly symbols.
