# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》720丨东汉开国太子，为何反复打辞职报告？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianwu 18-19 / 42-43 CE before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-721-ta-sha-jianu-ma-gongzhu-weihe-fanbei-shang-lryzcejdnrm4-92esnddfvyjkqli|Hanji 721]]. It places [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]]'s remaining military, frontier, and ritual work beside the central succession problem: after [[GuoShengtong|郭圣通]] is deposed, [[LiuQiangDonghaiKing|皇太子刘强]] repeatedly asks to leave the crown-prince position and retire to a fief.

The durable synthesis is that Liu Qiang's withdrawal is neither simple virtue theater nor ordinary dislike of power. The source reads it as a survival and filial strategy under changed maternal status: Liu Xiu eventually accepts the resignation, names Liu Yang crown prince, renames him Liu Zhuang, and turns him into the later [[EmperorMingOfHan|汉明帝]], while Yuan Hong criticizes the heir replacement as institutionally unsound despite the family's later emotional repair.

## Key Claims

- In 42 CE, Shu commandery rebels led by Shi Xing attack Zhang Mu, while Liu Xiu sends [[WuHanEasternHan|吴汉]] and other commanders to suppress the revolt.
- Lu Fang's renewed defection after a postponed court audience shows that surrender remains fragile when fear and Xiongnu backing persist.
- [[MaYuanEasternHan|马援]] advances through Jiaozhi, defeats and kills Zheng Ce and Zheng Er, then uses institutional clarification to stabilize the southern frontier after the military victory.
- Liu Xiu's court changes state-pastor posts back into inspector posts and revises temple arrangements, tying administration and ancestral ritual to restored-Han legitimacy.
- At Yuanwu, Liu Yang advises loosening a failed siege so coerced rebels can flee, and the tactic helps collapse the force around Shan Chen and Fu Zhen.
- After Guo Shengtong's deposition, Liu Qiang loses his strongest maternal backing and becomes uneasy about remaining crown prince.
- Zhi Yun advises Liu Qiang that staying in an unstable heir position is dangerous and inconsistent with serving his mother, so voluntary withdrawal becomes a protective option.
- Liu Qiang repeatedly asks, through Liu Xiu's trusted channels and royal kin, to resign as crown prince and retire to a fief.
- In 43 CE, Liu Xiu finally changes the succession: Liu Qiang becomes Donghai King, Liu Yang becomes crown prince, and Liu Yang is renamed Liu Zhuang.
- Yuan Hong's comment preserves the institutional objection: an established heir without grave wrongdoing should not be casually replaced, even if Liu Qiang's yielding and Mingdi's later brotherly treatment avert a worse rupture.

## Key Quotes

> "围师必阙" - tactical frame used for Liu Yang's Yuanwu siege advice.

> "坚决谦让" - the succession edict's political framing of Liu Qiang's repeated resignation.

> "不应轻易变动" - Yuan Hong's principle that a crown prince without grave fault should not be displaced.
