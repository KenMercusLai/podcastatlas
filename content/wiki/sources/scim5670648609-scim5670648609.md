## Summary
This short launch episode introduces [[AndrewHuberman]], his academic role, and the mission of [[HubermanLab]]: explaining science and science-based tools for everyday life. It establishes [[AudienceResponsiveDeepDivePodcasting|month-long topic arcs shaped partly by listener requests]] and a [[ScienceBasedToolTaxonomy|broad tool taxonomy]] spanning behavior, avoidance, nutrition, supplements, prescription drugs, brain-machine interfaces, and monitoring or intervention devices. It also introduces [[CostelloDog|Costello]] as a recurring presence in the recording environment.

## Key Claims
- The show is intended to connect scientific explanation with practical tools rather than treat science as purely theoretical.
- Broad subjects such as motivation or focus will receive month-long treatment covering mechanisms, knowns, unknowns, tools, and barriers.
- Solo teaching, in-studio guests, and remote guests can all contribute to a topic arc.
- Listeners are invited to propose and upvote topics, giving audience demand a bounded role in programming.
- “Tools” includes beneficial actions, behaviors to avoid, nutrition, supplements, prescription drugs, brain-machine interfaces, and monitoring or intervention devices.
- Benefits and disadvantages are part of evaluating a tool; inclusion in the show’s scope is not endorsement.

## Key Quotes
> "Science and science-based tools for everyday life." — Huberman's launch description of the show's mission.

> "There are many different kinds of tools." — introduction to the show's deliberately broad intervention scope.
