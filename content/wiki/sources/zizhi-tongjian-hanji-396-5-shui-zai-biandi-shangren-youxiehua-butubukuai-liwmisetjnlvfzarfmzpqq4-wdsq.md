# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》396-5｜谁在贬低商人？有些话不吐不快

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] short episode argues against the inherited low status of merchants in ancient China. It moves from [[FanLi|范蠡]] / Tao Zhu Gong as an exceptional merchant ancestor to a broader defense of merchants as [[MerchantExchangeInfrastructure|exchange infrastructure]]: they lower transport, storage, and transaction costs, widen circulation, and carry economic, cultural, and technical exchange across regions.

The episode then links [[HeavyAgricultureLightCommercePolicy|重农轻商政策]] to [[PowerDominatedWealthInsecurity|power-dominated wealth insecurity]]. In the host's view, when political power is the safest route to wealth and private commercial property is insecure, people turn from productive business toward political investment, official rent-seeking, and consumption, weakening economic vitality and encouraging bureaucratic corruption.

## Key Claims

- Individual success stories such as [[FanLi|范蠡]] / Tao Zhu Gong do not erase the lower inherited status and legal restrictions placed on ancient merchants.
- Commerce arises from specialization and exchange; merchants lower the cost of moving, storing, matching, and selling goods.
- Itinerant merchants earn by moving goods across place, while sedentary merchants earn by storage and market access; both can make goods cheaper for buyers and sales less costly for producers.
- Merchants function as early carriers of regional, economic, cultural, and technical exchange, often moving faster and more persistently than armies, bandits, missionaries, or formal explorers.
- The episode uses the Macartney embassy and later Sino-British per-capita GDP comparison as a source-scoped argument that long-term commercial suppression contributed to economic divergence.
- Under power-dominated wealth rules, the safest way to keep wealth is official status rather than business competence, encouraging political speculation such as the [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]] example.
- Suppressing merchants and commerce is presented as a driver of lower economic vitality, weaker reinvestment, official corruption, and heavier local extraction.

## Key Quotes

> "商人究竟重要不重要" - the opening question framing the episode.

> "商人还有一个重要角色，即经济、文化、科技交流的先驱" - the source's clearest positive definition of merchants.
