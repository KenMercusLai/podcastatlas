# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》700丨历史告诉你：别让认知偏差阻碍你的成功

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the Jianwu transition after the eastern theater is pacified and before [[WeiXiaoEasternHan|隗嚣]]'s open break in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-701-liuxiu-de-diwang-quanshu-zhezhong-ren-bixu-sha-lmsoxwg1k3k931ccnpeaomxfcu3q|Hanji 701]]. [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] lets exhausted commanders rest at Luoyang while sending letters to Wei Xiao and [[GongsunShuEasternHan|公孙述]], rejecting Gongsun Shu's fuming self-authorization and pressing both western rivals to recognize the danger of delay.

The main case is Gongsun Shu's failure to turn a temporary opportunity into action. [[JingHanEasternHan|荆邯]] urges him to strike before Liu Xiu finishes unification, but Gongsun Shu retreats from the plan after opposition inside Shu, while iron money, micro-management, ceremonial display, and family-first rewards expose a wider [[RulerScalePathDependence|统治者尺度错配型路径依赖]] problem.

## Key Claims

- Liu Xiu treats Wei Xiao and Gongsun Shu as the two remaining unresolved rival powers after Wu Han's eastern victories, but he chooses rest, letters, and pressure rather than immediate western war.
- Liu Xiu's letter to Gongsun Shu rejects prophecy-backed legitimacy, frames Gongsun Shu as old and isolated, and warns that imperial display cannot substitute for realistic political judgment.
- Jing Han argues that Gongsun Shu's only viable window is to act before Liu Xiu consolidates the whole realm, using the Jiangling/Wu-Chu and Hanzhong/Sanfu-Tianshui-Longxi lines to split Liu Xiu's attention.
- Gongsun Shu first accepts Jing Han's proposal but withdraws after Shu generals and his younger brother Gongsun Guang warn against a distant all-in campaign without allies.
- The episode frames Gongsun Shu's iron-coin policy, minute governance, and office-name tinkering as local-official habits scaled badly into imperial rule.
- Gongsun Shu's ceremony and premature enfeoffment of his two sons make status display and household preference politically costly before the war is decided.
- The host's incomplete enterprise analogy extends the point to organizational governance: internal rupture can destroy an organization before external competition finishes it.

## Key Quotes

> "公孙皇帝" - Liu Xiu's contemptuous label for Gongsun Shu's imperial posture as rendered by the episode.

> "胜而不进则亡" - Jing Han's warning as the episode summarizes his opportunity-window argument.

> "没有远大志向的表现" - the remonstrance judgment against rewarding Gongsun Shu's sons before the realm is settled.
