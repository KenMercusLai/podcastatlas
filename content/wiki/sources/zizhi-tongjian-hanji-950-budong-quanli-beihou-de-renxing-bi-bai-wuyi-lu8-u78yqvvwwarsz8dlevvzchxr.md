# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》950丨不懂权力背后的人性，必败无疑

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate pre-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-951-tianjiang-hengcai-jiujing-shi-haoshi-haishi-huaishi-lovwgng2wrtfno-s9ttpybbhksvh|Hanji 951]] late-Han field through the first formation of the [[AntiDongZhuoCoalition|反董卓联盟]], [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s selection as盟主, [[BaoXin|鲍信]]'s early recognition of [[CaoCao|曹操]], [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s killing of [[LiuBianHongnongWang|刘辩 / 弘农王]], and [[ZhengTaiLateHan|郑泰]]'s warning that the allied armies lack durable cohesion. It also introduces Dong Zhuo's plan to move from [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] to [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]], [[ZhuJunLateHan|朱儁]]'s refusal to cooperate, and the use of [[ChenweiPolitics|谶纬政治]] to dress a security-driven relocation as Heaven-backed policy. The distinct contribution is that the coalition looks strongest at the moment of assembly, yet the episode already reads its human incentives, prestige hierarchy, logistics split, and fear of Dong Zhuo as sources of later failure.

## Key Claims

- In early 190 CE, Guandong provinces and commanderies raise armies against [[DongZhuo|董卓]] and select [[YuanShao|袁绍]] as leader, giving the [[AntiDongZhuoCoalition|反董卓联盟]] a public center before it has a working command structure.
- Yuan Shao's leadership rests on family prestige, prior anti-eunuch and anti-Dong-Zhuo posture, and visible military preparation, making the episode a case of [[PrestigeBasedCoalitionLeadership|prestige-based coalition leadership]].
- The allied deployment is geographically broad: Yuan Shao and [[WangKuangLateHan|王匡]] sit in [[HeneiCommandery|河内]], [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]] remains at [[YeLateHan|邺城]] to handle grain, [[KongZhouLateHan|孔伷]] is at [[YingchuanCommandery|颍川]], several commanders including [[LiuDaiLateHan|刘岱]], [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]], [[ZhangChaoLateHan|张超]], [[QiaoMaoLateHan|乔瑁]], [[YuanYiLateHan|袁遗]], Bao Xin, and Cao Cao gather at [[SuanzaoCamp|酸枣大营]], and [[YuanShu|袁术]] stations at Luyang.
- Bao Xin sees Cao Cao as the actor most likely to plan a restoration, shifting the episode's attention from visible status to latent executive capacity.
- Dong Zhuo has [[LiRuLateHan|李儒]] poison the deposed Hongnong Wang Liu Bian, turning court control into royal murder and deepening the moral basis for opposition.
- Zheng Tai discourages Dong Zhuo from attacking immediately by arguing that rule depends on virtue, that Guandong leaders are militarily and organizationally weak, and that a large levy would damage Dong Zhuo's authority.
- The episode frames the coalition's core defect as [[InterestStructuredAllianceFragility|利益结构式联盟脆弱性]] and [[CoalitionSelfPreservationFailure|联盟自保失灵]]: shared outrage does not by itself align risk, food, hierarchy, or postwar incentives.
- Dong Zhuo's proposed relocation to Chang'an begins as fear of the coalition and a western-security calculation, but he then uses apocryphal prophecy to make the move sound like cosmic and popular necessity.
- Zhu Jun's refusal to accept Dong Zhuo's deputy-like appointment and his warning against westward relocation show that some court officials still understand the strategic and popular cost, even when fear keeps most ministers silent.
