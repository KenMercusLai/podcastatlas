## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]]'s 219 BCE eastern tour after the Langya and Xu Fu branch. It follows the failed search for [[Jiuding|九鼎]] at [[Xuzhou|彭城 / 徐州]], the storm at [[XiangshanShrine|湘山祠]], and the explanation that [[XiangJun|湘君]] is a local sacred figure connected to Yao and Shun traditions. The episode contrasts Qin's respectful incorporation of former-Qi sacred geography with punitive violence against a Chu-linked local deity space, then introduces [[ZhangLiang|张良]] as a Han noble preparing revenge against Qin.

## Key Claims
- Qin Shi Huang's post-Langya return route keeps the eastern tour inside [[ImperialTourPoliticalTheater|帝国巡游政治剧场]], but the tone shifts from inscription and incorporation to frustrated search and punitive domination.
- The failed Pengcheng search for [[Jiuding|九鼎]] makes the ding a missing legitimacy object: Qin has unified the realm but cannot physically recover every inherited Zhou-world symbol.
- The Xiangshan storm is read through local sacred geography. A natural obstruction becomes, in the ruler's interpretation, [[XiangJun|湘君]] refusing imperial passage.
- Qin Shi Huang's order to send convicts to cut Xiangshan's trees turns sacred conflict into visible landscape punishment, linking imperial anger, labor coercion, and humiliation of a Chu-associated local god.
- The episode contrasts former-Qi ritual incorporation with Chu sacred-landscape punishment, suggesting that Qin's conquest order handles local traditions unevenly.
- The host's suggestion that Qin Shi Huang may be imitating [[Huangdi|黄帝]]'s route remains a source-scoped interpretation, not a settled route reconstruction.
- The closing [[ZhangLiang|张良]] introduction turns the tour narrative toward anti-Qin assassination: destroyed [[HanState|韩国]] family memory becomes the motive for seeking an assassin.

## Key Quotes
> "一方水土养一方人，一方神管一方事" - the episode's local-religion frame for why Xiangshan's storm would be attributed to Xiangjun.

> "女流之辈" - the dismissive gendered reading the host attributes to Qin Shi Huang's anger toward Xiangjun.

> "汉初三杰" - the later status frame used to introduce Zhang Liang beside Han Xin and Xiao He.
