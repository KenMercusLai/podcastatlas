# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1086丨孙权备好装人头的木盒，为何偏偏放过苏飞？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode closes [[SunQuan|孙权]]'s campaign against [[HuangZu|黄祖]] through an act of clemency. Although Sun Quan reportedly prepares boxes for Huang Zu and [[SuFeiLateHan|苏飞]]'s heads, [[GanNing|甘宁]] kneels, injures himself while kowtowing, and offers his own life as security for Su Fei, whose earlier help made Gan Ning's escape and later service possible. Sun Quan accepts the [[PersonalGuaranteeClemency|私人担保式赦免]], then separately manages [[LingTong|凌统]]'s blood feud with Gan Ning by forbidding revenge and moving Gan Ning away.

The episode interprets Gan Ning's wider career through [[ViolenceToInstitutionalLegitimacy|暴力资本的体制化转换]]: skills first exercised as the “锦帆贼” become sustainable command, resources, status, and protection only after entry into Sun Quan's organization. The closing transition records Cao Cao's appointment as chancellor and the staffing of his office, setting up [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1087-simayi-zhuangbing-ju-caocao-yi-tingshuo-yao-bei-zhua-miao-song-likur-xfjqbk3kr-ew6ftvtwixq|Hanji 1087]] rather than fully developing that institutional branch.

## Key Claims

- In 208 CE, Sun Quan reportedly prepares two wooden boxes for the heads of Huang Zu and Su Fei before the campaign.
- At the victory banquet, Gan Ning pleads for Su Fei, says Su Fei previously saved him, and offers his own head if the pardoned man escapes.
- Sun Quan accepts Gan Ning's guarantee and spares Su Fei; the episode grounds the decision in Gan Ning's loyalty, gratitude, and pledged accountability rather than in a general rule of amnesty.
- Ling Tong seeks revenge because Gan Ning had killed his father [[LingCaoLateHan|凌操]] while defending Huang Zu; Sun Quan forbids the feud and later stations Gan Ning elsewhere.
- The host interprets Gan Ning's movement from “锦帆贼” to officer as converting personal violence, organization, and reputation into institutionally backed force, resources, rank, and legitimacy.
- The episode credits Sun Quan with using Gan Ning's strengths while containing the internal danger created by his violent past.
- In the closing 208 CE court sequence, the Three Excellencies are abolished, the chancellor and imperial secretary offices are restored, and [[CaoCao|曹操]] becomes chancellor.
- [[CuiYanLateHan|崔琰]], [[MaoJie|毛玠]], Sima Lang, [[SimaYi|司马懿]], and Lu Yu are named in the new chancellor-office staffing, which leads into Hanji 1087's personnel-selection discussion.

## Key Quotes

> “如果逃跑了，就用我的脑袋来代替。” - the episode's rendering of Gan Ning's guarantee for Su Fei.

> “将不可持续的江湖暴力，兑换成体制内可持续的权力、地位和合法性。” - the host's interpretation of Gan Ning's career transition.
