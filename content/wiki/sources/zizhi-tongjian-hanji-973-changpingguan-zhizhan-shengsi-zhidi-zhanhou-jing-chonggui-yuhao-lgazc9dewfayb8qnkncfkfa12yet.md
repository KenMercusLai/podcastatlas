# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》973丨长平观之战：生死之敌战后竟重归于好？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 194 CE setup before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-974-caocao-xiang-ta-tuo-qi-xian-zi-weihe-zhuanshen-ai-daozi-lhfabab7xv2vfsgnhr9ozwqhmhd|Hanji 974]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-975-yanzhou-zhiluan-xunyu-ruhe-bang-caocao-shou-dabenying-ln0hh4jrokkwtqsmxmqhdjzcosdr|Hanji 975]]. It moves from [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]'s hollow coming-of-age and [[WangMeirenLateHan|王美人]]'s posthumous honor to [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] giving [[LiuBei|刘备]] Danyang troops in [[Xuzhou|徐州]]. The core military branch is [[ChangpingguanBattleLateHan|长平观之战]], where [[MaTeng|马腾]] and [[HanSui|韩遂]] fail against [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]]'s side, before [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] and [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] invite [[LyuBu|吕布]] into [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] and force [[CaoCao|曹操]] to abandon the Xuzhou campaign.

## Key Claims
- In 194 CE, [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] formally comes of age and can in principle handle government, but the episode treats this as ceremonial because actual power remains in the hands of armed Chang'an actors.
- The court's reburial and posthumous honoring of [[WangMeirenLateHan|王美人]] as 灵怀皇后 shows that formal ritual still functions, even while sovereign capacity is thin.
- [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] asks [[TianKaiLateHan|田楷]] and [[LiuBei|刘备]] for help against [[CaoCao|曹操]], then gives Liu Bei four thousand Danyang soldiers, recommends him as Yuzhou inspector, and stations him at [[PeiCounty|小沛]].
- The episode frames this as a major step in Liu Bei's career: he moves from a small force under Tian Kai toward a higher warlord tier through troops, office recognition, and a Xuzhou foothold.
- Cao Cao's first pause against Tao Qian is caused by grain exhaustion, while the later recall from Xuzhou is caused by the Yanzhou rebellion; in both cases, expansion is constrained by rear capacity and supply.
- [[MaTeng|马腾]]'s conflict with [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] is presented through layered triggers: Li Jue's refusal to lend western troops, a source-scoped pasture feud in 《九州春秋》, and Ma Teng's refusal of imperial mediation.
- [[HanSui|韩遂]] arrives from Jincheng with Qiang-Hu cavalry as a mediator, but the source dramatizes his shift toward Ma Teng as a wine-catalyzed alliance reversal.
- [[ZhongShaoLateHan|种劭]], [[MaYuLateHan|马宇]], and [[LiuFanLateHan|刘范]] try to coordinate an inside response in [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] against Li Jue, but the plot leaks and the conspirators flee to Huaili.
- [[FanChouLateHan|樊稠]], [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]], and [[LiLiLateHan|李利]] defeat Ma Teng and Han Sui at [[ChangpingguanBattleLateHan|长平观之战]] and then attack Huaili, where the inside-plot actors are killed.
- Han Sui's later appeal to shared hometown ties turns direct battlefield hostility into drinking and conversation with Fan Chou, supplying the earlier field-contact layer that [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-984-liangzhouxi-junfa-neidou-ta-weihe-di-yige-daoxia-lurb2qfzsa7e-owh-dl-m-sccze5|Hanji 984]] later makes dangerous for Fan Chou.
- The episode records a source variant in Han Sui's demoted title: 《资治通鉴》 gives 安乡将军, while 《后汉纪》 gives 安羌将军; the host favors 安羌 because it pairs more plausibly with Ma Teng's 安狄将军.
- [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] and [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] welcome [[LyuBu|吕布]] into [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] while Cao Cao is in Xuzhou, leaving Cao Cao with only [[JuanchengLateHan|鄄城]], [[FanCountyLateHan|范县]], and [[DongALateHan|东阿]] and setting up Hanji 974's motive explanation.
