# 19 张向荣、陆大鹏：两位80后作家年少时读过的好书

## Summary

This [[WeirdHistoryPodcast|怪东西 Weird History]] World Book Day episode uses the childhood and adolescent reading memories of [[LuDapeng|陆大鹏]] and a co-speaker whom the title identifies as [[ZhangXiangrong|张向荣]] but the supplied internal summary calls [[ZhangDabai|张大白]]. School libraries, family shelves, newspapers, magazines, classmates, postal ordering, comics, popular science, myths, epics, fantasy, and later used-book websites form a shared [[ChildhoodReadingEcology]] in which access and curiosity matter more than a tidy curriculum.

The second half follows reading into work. Lu describes translation exercises, historical-book translation, and the long move toward an original history book; the summary-grounded Zhang Dabai branch moves through imitation, newspaper submissions, film reviews, book reviews, editorial invitations, and increasingly long assignments. Together they ground [[ReadingToWritingPractice]]: sustained reading supplies models and interests, but translation and authorship become capabilities only through repeated production, feedback, and completed work. Their closing emphasis on beginning before every difficulty or return can be known also extends [[ImperfectActionPractice]].

## Key Claims

- Libraries, home bookshelves, periodicals, bookstalls, classmates, postal orders, and later online used-book markets jointly shaped the speakers' early reading access.
- Fairy tales, comics, popular science, newspapers, magazines, classical novels, mythology, epic, fantasy, and history belonged to one mixed ecology rather than a strict hierarchy of serious and unserious reading.
- [[ZhengYuanjie|郑渊洁]] and [[TonghuaDawang|《童话大王》]] are remembered not only as entertainment but as sources of satire, social criticism, skepticism toward authority, and early imitation.
- Lu's path from dictionary-assisted translation exercises to historical translation and original writing presents translation as close textual training rather than a purely mechanical transfer task.
- The summary-grounded Zhang Dabai branch presents public submission, editorial feedback, regular assignments, and progressively longer reviews as a way to build confidence through demonstrated production.
- The speakers expect [[AITranslation|AI translation]] to absorb more functional work while leaving literary judgment, voice, and human collaboration as unresolved areas rather than declaring human translation obsolete.
- Writing a book or starting a podcast becomes tractable only after action exposes the real problems; waiting to know every obstacle, payoff, or outcome can prevent the work from beginning.
