# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》529丨美艳姐妹花！西汉最大的“祸水”登场！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens the Zhao-sister arc by narrating [[ZhaoFeiyanWesternHan|赵飞燕]]'s early life, reported abandoned-infant origin, dance training, and first encounter with [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] at [[YangAPrincessWesternHan|阳阿公主]]'s household. It also introduces [[ZhaoHedeWesternHan|赵合德]] through a variant twin-sister birth story, while keeping the episode's tone closer to popular biography than strict source criticism.

## Key Claims

- Zhao Feiyan's origin is narrated through a harsh abandoned-child motif: `Han Shu` is quoted for the claim that she was not raised at birth and survived three days before being taken back.
- A second version makes Zhao Feiyan, originally Zhao Yizhu, and Zhao Hede twin sisters born from a scandalous liaison and abandoned before being recovered alive.
- Zhao Feiyan's light body, dance training, and beauty become her route from Yang'a Princess's household into Chengdi's attention.
- The episode places the decisive entry scene in 18 BCE, when Chengdi visits Yang'a Princess with [[ZhangFangWesternHan|张放]], watches the singers, and is captivated by Zhao Feiyan's appearance and dance.
- Yang'a Princess reads Chengdi's interest and gives Zhao Feiyan to him, after which Zhao Feiyan quickly receives the rank of jieyu.
- The episode uses later labels such as "祸水" and "千古第一荡妇," but it does not itself separate reliable record from legend or later moralized reputation.

## Key Quotes

> "出生时父母不举，三日不死后才被收养" — the episode's `Han Shu`-based frame for Zhao Feiyan's abandoned-child origin.

> "轻盈身姿和舞蹈天赋" — the episode's explanation for how Zhao Feiyan's body and training become palace-entry capital.
