## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode fills the gap between Zhang Chu's formation in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-3-chensheng-wuguang-zudui-chenggong-locnaq-fo1fztyud5nghwkteams|Qinji 128-3]] and the western and Zhao-front reversals in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-5-wo-you-zhengju-zanmen-buneng-shuo-qinershi-sha-ye-bushi-ljuome-xea6sjv4hhzmrr0b0-1hg|Qinji 128-5]]. It shows [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] punishing accurate rebellion reports until officials minimize the crisis, while [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] disperses forces toward Yingyang, Zhao, Jiujiang, Wei, Nanyang, Hangu Pass, and Wuguan. Its central internal case is [[GeYing|葛婴]]: after independently installing [[XiangJiang|襄疆]] as Chu king and then killing him when Chen Sheng's kingship becomes known, Ge Ying is still executed, deepening mistrust inside [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]].

## Key Claims
- Eastern officials initially report the scale of revolt truthfully, but Hu Hai imprisons bearers of bad news; later officials call the rebels scattered bandits already under control, making the Qin court's information environment conform to the ruler's preference.
- Chen Sheng appoints Wu Guang acting king and sends multiple commanders toward Yingyang, Zhao, Jiujiang, Wei, Nanyang, Hangu Pass, and Wuguan, turning the uprising into a geographically distributed military regime.
- [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]] and nearby [[Aocang|敖仓]] combine route control with grain supply, making Wu Guang's stalled siege a strategic and logistical bottleneck rather than a merely local fight.
- Ge Ying, previously sent out after Qixian, installs the claimed Chu royal descendant Xiang Jiang as king without Chen Sheng's authorization; after learning that Chen Sheng has already taken the title, he kills Xiang Jiang but is arrested and executed anyway.
- The episode treats Ge Ying's killing as both the loss of an effective commander and a signal that initiative under Chen Sheng can become retroactively fatal, worsening subordinate mistrust.
- [[LiYou|李由]], son of [[LiSi|李斯]] and governor of Sanchuan, holds Yingyang against [[WuGuang|吴广]], while [[ZhouWenRebel|周文 / 周章]] and [[SongLiu|宋刘]] are assigned bypass routes toward Hangu Pass and Wuguan.
- [[WuChen|武臣]] crosses the Yellow River into former Zhao territory, recruits through local elites, and adopts [[KuaiChe|蒯彻]]'s proposal to reward the surrender of Fan Yang's magistrate as a visible model for other cities.
- The closing contrast between Zhou Wen's very large but untrained force and Qin's trained troops warns that rapid recruitment and numerical scale do not equal battlefield capacity.
- The episode claims that Ge Ying's descendants received a Zhu County title under Emperor Wen and combined the place-name 诸 with 葛 to form the Zhuge surname, making Ge Ying an ancestor of [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]]; the genealogy is retained as source-scoped rather than settled.

## Key Quotes
> "如实汇报的官员全都被关进监狱" - the episode's account of how Hu Hai teaches officials to suppress bad news.

> "不战而下城，不攻而略地" - Kuai Che's surrender-based alternative to city-by-city assault as rendered in the episode.

> "葛婴的死对张楚政权影响很大" - the episode's judgment that the execution costs both military capacity and internal trust.
