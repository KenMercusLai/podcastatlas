# 《资治通鉴·周纪》76丨谁给了楚顷襄王的自信？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode moves through [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s thirty-third and thirty-fourth years, 282-281 BCE, with [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] punishing [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] after Zhao refuses a joint attack on [[QiState|齐国]]. It then turns to the odd report that [[MiHeng|芈横 / 楚顷襄王]] wants to join [[QiState|齐国]] and [[HanState|韩国]] against Qin while also plotting against the [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]], a move the host treats as source-questionable because Mi Heng has recently been cautious and afraid of Qin. The strongest synthesis is that late Zhou weakness does not erase symbolic cost: [[DongzhouWugong|东周武公]] persuades Chu that attacking a tiny royal domain for [[Jiuding|九鼎]] would bring little material gain but severe legitimacy danger.

## Key Claims
- In 282 BCE, [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] attacks [[ZhaoState|赵国]] after [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] refuses Qin's proposal for a joint campaign against [[QiState|齐国]].
- The episode reads the attack through [[PostCoalitionSpoilsImbalance|post-coalition spoils imbalance]] after [[LeYi|乐毅]] and the anti-Qi coalition: Qin spent effort, received less than it wanted, and stayed dissatisfied.
- The host treats the refusal to attack Qi and the recent [[WanbiGuizhaoDiplomacy|完璧归赵]] humiliation as possible paired causes for Qin's punitive move against Zhao.
- In 281 BCE, Qin again attacks Zhao and takes [[ShichengWarringStates|石城]], located in the episode near modern southwest Shijiazhuang.
- The same year, Qin appoints [[WeiRan|魏冉]] as chancellor again, continuing the Qin court sequence around [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]].
- The source says [[ChuState|楚国]] wants to join Qi and Han in attacking Qin while also considering an attack on the Zhou royal domain.
- The host questions the plausibility of [[MiHeng|楚顷襄王]] suddenly becoming aggressive toward Qin and Zhou after earlier fear, marriage accommodation, and the trauma of [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]]'s death in Qin.
- [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]] sends [[DongzhouWugong|东周武公]] to persuade Chu's令尹昭子 that the royal house is not worth attacking.
- Dongzhou Wugong's argument is material and symbolic: Zhou territory and people are too small to enrich or strengthen Chu, while attacking the nominal Son of Heaven would look like regicide.
- The [[Jiuding|九鼎]] are treated as a tempting object but also a trap: moving them to Chu could invite broader opposition against Chu.
- The episode uses the Zhou background to clarify the difference between Western Zhou as a dynasty period and the later [[WesternZhouPolity|西周国]] / [[EasternZhouPolity|东周国]] split inside the diminished royal domain.
- The closing preview says Qin later invites Zhao Huiwen Wang to meet at [[Mianchi|渑池]], which recalls the earlier Qin trap against Chu Huaiwang at [[Wuguan|武关]].

## Key Quotes
> "谁给了楚顷襄王的自信？" - the episode's title question about the plausibility of Chu's reported boldness.

> "土地很小，总面积不过百里" - Dongzhou Wugong's material framing of Zhou's limited value.

> "九鼎运到楚国时，天下伐楚的大军恐怕也会到来" - the episode's summary of symbolic trophy risk.

## Contradictions
- Source-critical tension, not a settled contradiction: earlier pages such as [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-67-zhanguo-mieba-baiqi-de-junshi-caineng-jiujing-ruhe-ljw0lltowxd4qz75v-wcnlkwqskv|Zhouji 67]] portray [[MiHeng|楚顷襄王]] as swallowing humiliation and renewing marriage diplomacy under Qin pressure. This episode's report that Chu suddenly wants to attack Qin and menace Zhou is therefore kept as a questioned source claim rather than treated as stable character evidence.
- No direct contradiction found in the Qin-Zhao chronology. The source extends [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-73-lishishang-ziyou-lianai-de-kaichuangzhe-shi-shui-lkumfsfialqgdl6qpb3c-hnz7h9u|Zhouji 73]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-74-huanyuan-wanbi-guizhao-zhenxiang-lg1irvbbc1wp8oph2kmpatfoxebk|Zhouji 74]] by moving from post-Qi spoils and the He Shi Bi exchange to direct Qin attacks on Zhao and the coming Mianchi meeting.
