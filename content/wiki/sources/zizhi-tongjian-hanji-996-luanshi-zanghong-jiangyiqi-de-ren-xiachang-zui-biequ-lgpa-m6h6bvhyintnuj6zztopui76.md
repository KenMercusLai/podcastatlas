# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》996丨乱世臧洪：讲义气的人，下场最憋屈？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the lead-in to [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-997-zanghong-zhisi-shi-yibaoyuntian-haishi-qingjie-nandu-lln2liblq-kyjrsvitnbh7cspbax|Hanji 997]] by narrating why [[ZangHong|臧洪]] breaks with [[YuanShao|袁绍]]. [[ZhangChaoLateHan|张超]] is trapped at [[YongqiuLateHan|雍丘]] by [[CaoCao|曹操]], Zang Hong begs Yuan Shao for rescue troops, Yuan Shao refuses, and Zhang Chao's death turns an old-superior debt into rebellion.

The episode makes Zang Hong's loyalty both moving and frightening. His [[DongCommandery|东郡]] defense inspires followers to stay, but the siege descends into starvation, shared thin gruel, the killing of a beloved concubine for soldiers' food, and mass death; the host therefore treats "义气" as a force that can preserve honor while crossing into [[LoyaltyCollateralDilemma|忠义牵连困境]] and [[EmotionalAttachmentPoliticalDistortion|情执政治判断失衡]].

## Key Claims
- The opening uses late-Han disorder, assassination, famine, and religious consolation to frame a world where public order has lost force and survival pressure remakes moral behavior.
- [[ZhuZhiLateHan|朱治]]'s move into Wu and [[YanBaihu|严白虎]]'s role as a local refuge show regional authority fragmenting before the Zang Hong story begins.
- [[ZhangChaoLateHan|张超]] expects rescue because Zang Hong had once served him in [[GuanglingCommandery|广陵]] and is remembered as someone who does not forget old恩.
- Zang Hong hears that Zhang Chao is in danger and begs [[YuanShao|袁绍]] for troops, first asking Yuan Shao to send an army and then asking for a smaller force he can lead himself.
- Yuan Shao refuses, [[CaoCao|曹操]] captures [[YongqiuLateHan|雍丘]], and Zhang Chao plus his family and relatives are killed.
- Zang Hong's later refusal to deal with Yuan Shao is grounded in this failed rescue rather than in a prior personal feud.
- [[ChenLinLateHan|陈琳]]'s persuasion letter gives Zang Hong a route back into Yuan Shao's camp, but Zang Hong answers that preserving a current relationship cannot erase the duty owed to an old superior.
- The source treats Zang Hong as sincerely high-minded: he knows defeat is likely, yet chooses a "虽败犹荣" posture rather than submission.
- The same posture becomes destructive under siege. Zang Hong lets civilians and followers leave, but their refusal to abandon him turns his private loyalty conflict into collective starvation.
- The host marks the human limit of the story through the siege sequence: eating rats and leather, refusing to keep the final rice for himself, killing a concubine to feed soldiers, and allowing thousands to die without surrender.
- The comparison to [[ZhangLiao|张辽]] and [[GaoShun|高顺]] points toward [[PoliticalSurvivalFirst|安全第一政治生存]] as the missing survival wisdom that Hanji 997 will develop more explicitly.
