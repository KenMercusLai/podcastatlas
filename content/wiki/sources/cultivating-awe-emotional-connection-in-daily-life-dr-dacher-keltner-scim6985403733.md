## Summary
This [[HubermanLab]] episode has [[AndrewHuberman]] interview [[DacherKeltner]] about [[AweEmotion]], emotion science, social bonding, embarrassment, teasing, psychedelics, community life, and the design problem of digital life. Its central synthesis is that awe is a measurable emotion and social orientation: facial expression, vocalization, goosebumps, vagal tone, inflammation, attention, time perception, self-focus, music, sport, moral beauty, and shared rituals all become evidence for how humans connect to something larger than the isolated self.

## Key Claims
- [[DacherKeltner]] frames [[AweEmotion]] as a measurable emotion rather than only a vague spiritual feeling, with markers including facial expression, vocalization, goosebumps, vagal tone, inflammation, and brain changes.
- The episode extends [[EmotionsAsFunctionalControlStates]] by emphasizing that awe shifts attention from narrow self-focus toward vast patterns, temporal distance, and social or ecological belonging.
- [[AweWalks]] are presented as a practical low-cost protocol: slow walks in surprising places, with attention moving from small details to larger patterns, increased awe and kindness and reduced pain in an older-adult study described by Keltner.
- [[EmbodiedCollectiveAwe]] appears through music, chanting, dancing, sports fandom, mosh pits, martial arts, campfires, saunas, museums, concerts, and other shared bodily settings.
- [[MoralBeautyAwe]] expands awe beyond natural scale: courage, kindness, strength, justice, integrity, and full-person expression can become sources of awe.
- [[SocialMediaDesignRegulation]] is extended by a design diagnosis: online systems often weaken eye contact, embodied synchrony, shared time, and awe while amplifying rage, numbing, siloing, or self-focus.
- [[EmbarrassmentTrustSignal]] and [[TeasingBelongingBoundary]] show Keltner's broader social-emotion thesis: embarrassment can signal commitment to group norms, while healthy teasing keeps people inside the group and harmful teasing humiliates or excludes.
- Psychedelics are discussed as possible awe-inducing self-dissolution and ecological-connection tools, but both speakers emphasize safety, guidance, cultural context, and caution around casual microdosing.

## Key Quotes
> "the great enemy of awe is meanness" - Keltner's compact warning about self-focused social states.

> "awe walks" - Keltner's practical term for slow, attention-widening walks.
