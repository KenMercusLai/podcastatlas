# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1128丨没有黑科技，古人如何调查匿名信？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode connects four 212-213 CE governance and strategy problems. [[GuoYuanInvestigatorLateHan|国渊]] narrows an anonymous-letter case through literary knowledge and then compares a deliberately obtained handwriting sample; [[ZhengHunLateHan|郑浑]] combines fortification, rewards, captive exchange, persuasion, and resettlement to detach coerced followers from [[LiangXingLateHan|梁兴]].

The episode then turns to [[SunQuan|孙权]]'s move to Moling/Jianye and [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]]'s argument for [[RuxuFortressLateHan|濡须坞]] as a fallback position if troops cannot reboard under pursuit. It closes with [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] granting [[CaoCao|曹操]] the Nine Bestowments, extending [[JiuXiRitualPromotion|九锡礼遇]] from the Wang Mang case to a late-Han minister whose exceptional status is nearing the formal limit below dynastic replacement.

## Key Claims

- Guo Yuan's investigation works in two stages: the letter's literary content narrows the field to people able to understand *Er Jing Fu*, and a pretext produces a comparison sample before arrest and interrogation.
- The source presents handwriting comparison as practical corroboration inside a targeted inquiry, not as modern forensic certainty.
- Cao Cao rewards Guo Yuan with promotion to taipu; the episode also portrays Guo Yuan as personally frugal and generous with salary and rewards.
- After [[MaChao|马超]]'s rebellion, his father [[MaTeng|马腾]] and a large part of the clan are executed, while Liang Xing continues armed predation in the Zuopingyi region.
- Zheng Hun refuses a withdrawal that would advertise weakness, repairs defenses, lets civilians retain most recovered property, uses captive exchange to create return incentives, sends respected intermediaries to persuade followers, and arranges reception for surrenderers.
- Liang Xing's force erodes as followers gain a credible exit; [[XiahouYuan|夏侯渊]] later assists the campaign that kills Liang Xing and suppresses the remainder.
- Sun Quan's relocation to Moling/Jianye is framed as strategic base-building, while Lyu Meng's Ruxu fortification proposal treats defeat and delayed embarkation as risks that must be designed for before battle.
- The Nine Bestowments make Cao Cao's near-imperial standing publicly visible without themselves completing a dynastic transfer.

## Key Quotes

> “世上没有不透风的墙” - the host's lesson from the anonymous-letter case.

> “兵有利钝，战无百胜” - Lyu Meng's premise for building a defensible fallback at Ruxu.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. The source extends the Weinan aftermath and later Cao-Sun confrontation sequence already represented in the wiki.
- Evidentiary caution: the episode does not establish a modern validation method, error rate, chain of custody, or whether handwriting resemblance alone determined guilt; the final identity claim and interrogation outcome remain source-scoped.
- Chronology and detail caution: exact execution totals for Ma Chao's clan, the four imperial sons' political function, Moling/Stone City chronology, Qin-era geomancy legend, exact Ruxu construction form, dialogue, motives, and the Nine Bestowments date and item sequence require direct textual corroboration.
