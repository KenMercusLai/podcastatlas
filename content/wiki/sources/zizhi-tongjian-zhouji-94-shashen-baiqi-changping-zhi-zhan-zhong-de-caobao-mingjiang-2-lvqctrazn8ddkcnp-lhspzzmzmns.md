# 《资治通鉴·周纪》94丨杀神白起，长平之战中的“草包”名将？（2）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-1-loxyybm3njrtnudydafwzu9jp9jz|Zhouji 94 part 1]] from the command replacement into the decisive [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] encirclement. [[ZhaoKuo|赵括]] abandons [[LianPo|廉颇]]'s defensive attrition line and attacks across the Dan River, while [[BaiQi|白起]] uses feigned retreat, prepared barriers, flank movement, cavalry insertion, and fieldworks to cut the Zhao army away from its camp and supplies.

The episode then widens the battle from tactics to state capacity and diplomacy. [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] personally moves toward Henei and mobilizes Qin males over fifteen to seal off Zhao reinforcements and food, while [[HanState|韩国]], [[WeiState|魏国]], [[ChuState|楚国]], [[YanState|燕国]], and especially [[QiState|齐国]] fail to provide effective help. [[QiWangJian|齐王建 / 田建]] and [[JunWangHou|君王后]] refuse Zhao's grain request despite [[ZhouziQiMinister|周子]]'s lips-and-teeth warning, leaving Zhao troops starving for forty-six days.

## Key Claims
- [[BaiQi|白起]] can plan around [[ZhaoKuo|赵括]] because Qin knows Zhao's new commander while Zhao does not know Bai Qi has secretly taken command.
- Zhao Kuo changes both operations and personnel after taking over, crosses the Dan River with the main force, and attacks Qin's prepared position.
- Qin's initial retreat is a lure: once Zhao pursues, Qin falls back to fortified barriers that Zhao cannot easily assault without heavy siege equipment.
- Bai Qi's side forces then cut Zhao's retreat and supply channels: a 25,000-person detachment circles behind the Zhao force, while 5,000 cavalry cut the army into separated parts.
- Qin follows the mobile cut-off with field engineering, using chariots, infantry, ditches, walls, and fortified lines to make the separation durable.
- Qin Zhaoxiang Wang treats the severed Zhao grain route as a state-level emergency opportunity, personally moving to Henei and mobilizing males over fifteen to prevent relief.
- The other states do not convert fear of Qin into rescue. Han and Wei are too constrained by Qin, Chu is cautious after earlier damage, Yan is focused elsewhere, and Qi refuses to lend grain.
- Zhouzi warns Qi that Zhao is a shield for Qi and Chu, but Qi Wang Jian and Jun Wang Hou still choose short-term non-intervention.
- After forty-six days without food, the trapped Zhao army descends into internal killing and cannibalism, showing the battle as a starvation collapse rather than only a command duel.

## Key Quotes
> "赵之于齐、楚，扞蔽也。" - Zhouzi's warning about Zhao as a shield state.

> "赵卒不得食四十六日。" - the starvation stage as summarized in the episode.

> "灭六国者，六国也，非秦也。" - Du Mu's judgment as invoked by the host.
