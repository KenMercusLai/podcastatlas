# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》701丨刘秀的帝王权术：这种人必须杀！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the Jianwu 6 / 30 CE layer between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-699-gan-yu-liuxiu-zhengxiong-de-ta-weihe-candan-shouchang-lth-8mndudj3khf9cnsouj86c1-z|Hanji 699]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-702-fengyi-de-yichang-ju-ruhe-zhansha-weixiao-liangwanren-lqckx-aclchkzqqe3pttnklu9vot|Hanji 702]]. It connects [[FengYiEasternHan|冯异]]'s return to court and remembered hardship with [[WeiXiaoEasternHan|隗嚣]]'s refusal to attack [[GongsunShuEasternHan|公孙述]], [[LaiXiEasternHan|来歙]]'s dangerous envoy mission, Wei Xiao's open rebellion, and [[MaWuEasternHan|马武]]'s rearguard after a Han defeat.

The administrative half adds [[PostwarAdministrativeRetrenchment|战后行政裁并]] and an early [[LocalOfficialTenureStability|地方官任期稳定]] case. [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] cuts counties and officials after war has reduced the population, then accepts [[ZhuFuEasternHan|朱浮]]'s eclipse memorial that short local-official terms create false performance, bureaucratic churn, and short-term governance.

## Key Claims

- Liu Xiu publicly honors Feng Yi by recalling early hardship and old service, while Feng Yi answers with a reminder not to forget the Hebei struggle.
- Wei Xiao's refusal to attack Gongsun Shu marks the shift from usable ally to non-credible western actor in Liu Xiu's judgment.
- Lai Xi's envoy mission makes Wei Xiao's hesitation visible; the confrontation nearly turns into an envoy-killing crisis before Wang Zun and western gentry intervene.
- Wei Xiao's rebellion follows quickly after Liu Xiu's western deployment, and his blockade at Longdi contributes to a first Han defeat.
- Ma Wu's selected cavalry rearguard prevents the retreat from becoming a larger disaster after Wei Xiao's pursuit.
- Liu Xiu's county and official reductions are framed as matching administrative scale to a war-shrunken population.
- Zhu Fu's memorial argues that frequent local-official replacement rewards superficial achievement and deception instead of durable public benefit.

## Key Quotes

> "不忘河北之难" - Feng Yi's reminder to Liu Xiu as summarized by the source.

> "官吏本应为百姓服务" - the episode's summary of Liu Xiu's administrative-reduction edict.

> "看到三十年之后" - Zhu Fu's long-horizon advice as summarized by the source.
