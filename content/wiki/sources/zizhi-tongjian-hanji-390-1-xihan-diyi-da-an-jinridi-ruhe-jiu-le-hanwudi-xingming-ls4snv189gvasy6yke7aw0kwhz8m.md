# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》390-1｜西汉第一大案，金日磾如何救了汉武帝性命？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode narrates the 88 BCE palace assassination plot in which [[MaHeluoWesternHan|马何罗]] and his brothers, fearing punishment after the [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]] purge of [[JiangChongWesternHan|江充]]'s faction, attempt to attack [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] at Lingguang Palace. [[JinMidiWesternHan|金日磾]] detects their abnormal behavior, quietly shadows them, guards the emperor's sleeping quarters, and seizes Ma Heluo before the blade reaches Wudi.

The second half turns the rescue into a character profile. Jin is presented as a Xiongnu-origin court servant whose loyalty comes from political dependence, gratitude, and awareness that he has no separate Han-court base; the episode also links his caution to his mother's discipline, his ritual mourning before her portrait, and his willingness to kill a favored son who violated palace boundaries.

## Key Claims

- Ma Heluo's plot is framed as a self-protective rebellion after Han Wudi punished Jiang Chong and associated Wugu actors.
- Jin Midi's effectiveness lies in quiet observation and close-protection discipline: he does not prematurely accuse the conspirators, but stays near Han Wudi until the attack appears.
- The Lingguang Palace rescue gives concrete narrative grounding to the vigilance and "third eye" reputation explained in Hanji 390-2.
- Han Wudi's warning to guards not to injure Jin during the arrest shows the ruler's extraordinary trust and concern for him.
- Jin's loyalty is explained through Xiongnu origin, lack of independent Han power base, and dependence on Han Wudi's favor.
- Jin's mother supplies the episode's family-education layer: her discipline produces cautious sons, and Wudi honors her with a portrait at Ganquan Palace.
- Jin's killing of his elder son after improper behavior with palace women is presented as severe boundary enforcement rather than ordinary household cruelty.
