# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》903丨为什么劝你冤冤相报何时了？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the pre-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-904-juezhao-neiguan-huode-xinling-da-ziyou-de-mijue-lopn-okbzr4ljvqmgzcsb7qav0n2|Hanji 904]] late-Han layer. It pairs [[SuBuweiLateHan|苏不韦]]'s escalating revenge against [[LiHaoLateHan|李昊]] with [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]]'s extermination of Su's household, then turns to [[LiuKuiBohaiKing|刘亏]]'s failed attempt to use [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]]'s inner-court access to regain the [[BohaiKingdomLateHan|渤海王国]] title. The episode's core synthesis is that private grievance, unpaid political brokerage, forged treason evidence, and collateral punishment can turn personal conflict into household and institutional catastrophe under [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]].

## Key Claims

- [[SuBuweiLateHan|苏不韦]]'s failed revenge against [[LiHaoLateHan|李昊]] escalates from attempted assassination to [[TombDesecrationPoliticalViolence|tomb desecration]] and [[PublicCorpseHumiliation|public corpse humiliation]], converting family vengeance into public terror.
- Amnesty and a public funeral do not remove Su's risk. They make him visible again, allowing [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]] to test him with a private post and then punish refusal through a subordinate coerced by threats against his own father.
- Duan's killing of more than sixty members of Su's household makes the revenge story a case of [[AtrocityRetaliationCycle|retaliatory escalation]], [[PrivatePunishmentWithoutProcedure|private punishment without procedure]], and [[CollateralPunishmentAndGuiltByAssociation|collateral punishment]].
- [[LiuKuiBohaiKing|刘亏]] regains his Bohai kingship by promising [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]] fifty million cash through palace access, but the restoration creates a debt relationship that turns lethal when Liu refuses payment.
- Wang Fu recodes Liu Kui's resentment and his ties to [[ZhengSaLateHan|郑萨]] and [[DongTengLateHan|董腾]] into a treason accusation, with [[LianZhongLateHan|廉中]] forging evidence that Zheng and others plotted to install Liu as emperor.
- [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] accepts the accusation and orders a [[JizhouLateHan|Jizhou]] inspector to interrogate Liu in [[BohaiKingdomLateHan|Bohai]], leading to Liu's suicide and a wide purge of his family, entertainers, and fief officials.
- The court rewards Wang Fu and twelve other actors after the Liu Kui case, showing how [[PersonalRevengeThroughStatePower|personal revenge through state power]] and a [[TreasonAccusationTrap|treason accusation trap]] can be converted into official merit.
- The episode also backfills [[XuShengLateHan|许生]]'s [[KuaijiCommandery|Kuaiji]] uprising, [[Xianbei]] pressure on [[BingzhouLateHan|Bingzhou]], [[SouthernXiongnu|Southern Xiongnu]] succession, and [[YuanWeiLateHan|袁隗]]'s appointment as situ as early markers of Emperor Ling-era military and office churn.
