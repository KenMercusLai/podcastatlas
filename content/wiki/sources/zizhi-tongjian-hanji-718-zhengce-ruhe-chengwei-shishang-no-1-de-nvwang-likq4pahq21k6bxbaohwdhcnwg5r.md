# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》718丨征侧如何成为史上NO.1的女王？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianwu 16 / 40 CE before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-719-guangwudi-liuxiu-suanbusuan-zhanan-lk8eme3wwyk5qkjjfxkp0zsc77ix|Hanji 719]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-720-donghan-kaiguo-taizi-weihe-fanfu-da-cizhi-baogao-lslizspo8rywnf3cebyhincdhvz0|Hanji 720]]. It opens the early Jiaozhi revolt branch through [[ZhengCeJiaozhi|征侧]] and [[ZhengErJiaozhi|征贰]], then moves to [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]]'s 40 CE domestic order work: land-and-grain fraud punishment, anti-bandit incentives, [[LuFangEasternHan|卢芳]]'s surrender diplomacy, and five-zhu coin restoration at [[MaYuanEasternHan|马援]]'s recommendation.

The durable synthesis is that Guangwu-era stabilization is presented as a multi-front problem rather than one frontier or palace story. Southern local resentment becomes female-led sovereignty in [[JiaozhiCommandery|交趾郡]], officials are punished for damaging land and grain administration, bandit pacification works by correcting incentives for both commoners and local officials, and frontier diplomacy with Xiongnu remains fragile even when a nominal surrender reaches Luoyang.

## Key Claims

- In spring 40 CE, [[ZhengCeJiaozhi|征侧]] and [[ZhengErJiaozhi|征贰]] revolt in Jiaozhi after governor [[SuDingJiaozhi|苏定]] attempts to punish Zheng Ce under court law.
- Jiuzhen, Rinan, Hepu, and other groups respond to the revolt; Zheng Ce takes sixty-five cities, declares herself king, and makes Miling her capital.
- The episode treats Zheng Ce as a historically unusual female political founder in the Jiaozhi minority-frontier setting, not just as a local rebel name.
- The source jumps ahead to 43 CE, when [[MaYuanEasternHan|马援]] suppresses the revolt, kills Zheng Ce and Zheng Er, and sends their bodies to Luoyang.
- After a March eclipse, Liu Xiu has Henan Yin Zhang Ji and more than ten commandery administrators executed for cheating in land measurement and grain affairs.
- Ma Yuan tells Liu Xiu that the executed officials deserved death because their fraud damaged the people, shifting the emperor from regret toward acceptance.
- Widespread bandit disorder in Qingzhou, Xuzhou, Youzhou, Yizhou, and other commanderies is handled through a mutual-reporting policy: groups that jointly kill a bandit can be pardoned, local officials are not punished merely for delay or fear, and sheltering bandits is punished.
- Bandit leaders who surrender or are captured are moved to other commanderies and given land and grain, turning suppression into controlled resettlement.
- Lu Fang and [[MinKanEasternHan|闵堪]] surrender; Liu Xiu enfeoffs Lu Fang as Dai King and Min Kan as Dai Chancellor, but the Xiongnu bounty dispute makes the settlement unstable.
- Ma Yuan recommends restoring five-zhu coins, and Liu Xiu adopts the recommendation.

## Key Quotes

> "死得其罪" - Ma Yuan's judgment that the land-and-grain fraud officials deserved execution.

> "赏罚是为王之道" - the episode's management frame for why law credibility matters even when punishment feels harsh.

> "五铢钱对百姓使用十分方便" - the source's reason for restoring the five-zhu coin.
