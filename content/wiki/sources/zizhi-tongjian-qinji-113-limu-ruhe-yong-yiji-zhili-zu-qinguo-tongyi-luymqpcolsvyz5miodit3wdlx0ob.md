# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》113｜李牧如何用一己之力阻秦国统一

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode moves from [[HanFei|韩非]]'s death in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-112-3-hanfei-yu-yaojia-lunzhan-qinwang-dang-caipan-ljxxlok4uh-ajjirmolybodlspv9|Qinji 112-3]] into [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]]'s fifteenth through eighteenth years, when [[QinState|秦国]] begins converting pressure into the destruction of states. It frames [[LiMu|李牧]] as the last serious military variable on the Zhao front: he cannot permanently reverse Qin's structural advantage, but he can delay unification enough that Qin chooses rumor, bribery, and commander removal rather than a clean head-on test.

The episode also widens the setting around that military problem. [[YanTaiziDan|燕太子丹]]'s mistreatment in Qin foreshadows later Yan hostility, [[HanState|韩国]] and [[WeiState|魏国]] concede land under pressure, Han is destroyed in 230 BCE, and [[ZhaoState|赵国]] is weakened by earthquake, famine, drought, rumor, and a compromised court. Once [[GuoKai|郭开]] helps remove Li Mu and [[SimaShang|司马尚]], the source treats [[WangJianQin|王翦]]'s path toward Zhao's fall as essentially open.

## Key Claims
- In 232 BCE, Qin attacks Zhao in two directions, reaches the Ye and Taiyuan fronts, takes Langmeng and Fanwu in the source account, but withdraws after encountering Li Mu.
- The host argues that Qin's army is not invincible by default; late-six-state collapse also depends on the eastern states' repeated failure to preserve military capacity and internal trust.
- Yan Taizi Dan and Ying Zheng are presented as former hostage-era acquaintances, but Dan receives poor treatment in Qin after Ying Zheng's rise and escapes back to Yan.
- In 231 BCE, Han offers Nanyang to Qin and Wei offers Li Yi, while Zhao suffers a major earthquake, collapsing walls, heavy casualties, displacement, and then famine conditions.
- In 230 BCE, Qin captures [[HanAn|韩王安 / Han An]], destroys Han, and establishes Yingchuan commandery in the old Han territory; the same year [[HuayangFuren|华阳夫人 / 华阳太后]] dies.
- In 229 BCE, Qin attacks disaster-weakened Zhao with Wang Jian and [[YangDuanhe|杨端和]], while Li Mu and Sima Shang command Zhao's defense.
- [[DunRuo|顿弱]] is named in the source as a Qin intelligence actor spreading panic in Zhao during the famine, including rumors and manufactured ominous signs.
- Qin's anti-Li-Mu plan works through heavy bribery of Guo Kai, who persuades [[ZhaoYoumiuWang|赵迁 / 赵幽缪王]] that Li Mu and Sima Shang are plotting rebellion.
- Zhao replaces Li Mu and Sima Shang with [[ZhaoCong|赵葱]] and [[YanJuZhao|颜聚]], a decision the host treats as damaging even before judging the replacements' competence.
- Sima Shang yields command, while Li Mu refuses the order and is arrested or executed.
- The host ranks Li Mu with [[BaiQi|白起]], Wang Jian, and [[LianPo|廉颇]] among the four famous Warring States generals, while giving Li Mu the highest tactical praise in this source.
- The episode reads Li Mu's death as a case of Zhao "destroying its own Great Wall": Qin's external strength succeeds because Zhao's ruler and court discard the one commander Qin most fears.
