# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》526丨汉朝奇葩制度：皇帝死了就能迁户口？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the institutional prequel to the later Changling controversy. It explains how early Western Han [[GuanzhongEliteRelocation|关中豪强迁徙]] develops into [[MausoleumCountyEliteRelocation|陵县式豪强迁徙]]: wealthy Guandong households and high officials' families are moved near imperial mausoleums, strengthening the capital region while weakening regional power bases.

The durable synthesis is that [[ChenTangWesternHan|陈汤]]'s proposal to establish Changlingyi under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] mixed public statecraft and private family interest. The policy could answer late-Western-Han land concentration, fiscal weakness, and local-power growth, but it also served Chen Tang's wish to secure permanent capital-region household status, land, and residence for his wife and children.

## Key Claims

- Western Han's strong-trunk weak-branch policy includes moving Guandong wealthy households and senior officials' families toward imperial mausoleums near the capital.
- Mausoleum counties become prosperous high-status settlements; they are attractive to smaller families, new rich households, and officials seeking durable capital-region status.
- [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] stops the policy after ministers argue that forced relocation separates people from ancestral graves, damages livelihoods, violates classical norms, and unsettles elites.
- [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] initially follows Yuan's precedent by building Yanling without a new mausoleum county.
- [[XieWannianWesternHan|谢万年]] urges Chen Tang to support a renewed mausoleum county because it could improve both men's family residence, land, and status prospects.
- Chen Tang proposes establishing Changlingyi and moving five thousand households with assets above five million from the commanderies and kingdoms.
- The host reads the proposal as morally mixed: Chen Tang has a family-hukou motive, but the policy also targets real late-Western-Han problems of land concentration, fiscal weakness, and powerful local magnates.

## Key Quotes
