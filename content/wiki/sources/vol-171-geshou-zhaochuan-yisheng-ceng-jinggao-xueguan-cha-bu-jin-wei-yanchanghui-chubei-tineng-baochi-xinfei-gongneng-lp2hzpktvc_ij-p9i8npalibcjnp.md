# VOL.171歌手赵传：医生曾警告“血管插不进”！为演唱会储备体能保持心肺功能

## Summary
This [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] interview presents singer [[ZhaoChuan|赵传]] through the relationship among long-term performance, physical conditioning, vocal care, walking, and later-life renewal. A past episode of feeling unwell prompted him to treat health as a condition of continuing his music career; basketball, resistance training, swimming, core work, walking, and avoidance of irritating foods then became parts of a personal maintenance system. The source reinforces [[PerformerHealthAsCareerInfrastructure]] while keeping medical, cardiopulmonary, metabolic, and voice-mechanism claims autobiographical and source-scoped.

## Key Claims
- [[ZhaoChuan|赵传]] says a past medical visit after a period of heavy work changed his attitude toward physical maintenance, although the episode does not provide a diagnosis, test result, or treatment history.
- He frames singing and his stage style as physical work whose demands become more visible with age; body weight, vocal power, appearance, endurance, and injury risk therefore have to be balanced rather than optimized independently.
- His personal routine combines basketball for enjoyment and cardiopulmonary effort, gradual resistance training at home, swimming when impact or injury limits basketball, plank work for core stability, and ordinary walking.
- He repeatedly qualifies exercise by present capacity: load should increase gradually, and activity should remain within what the body can tolerate, aligning with [[ExerciseLoadManagement]].
- He avoids very sour, spicy, or otherwise irritating foods for his voice and believes exercise supports singing, but the source supplies neither clinical voice assessment nor a general vocal-health protocol.
- Walking functions as both movement and observation: city streets, people, venues, and travel memories can replenish daily life and contribute to musical inspiration through [[TravelSensoryRecovery]].
- The album 《老不休》 and discussion of possible retirement frame later life as another period of practice and renewed participation rather than a simple withdrawal from work.

## Key Quotes
> “身体是革命的本钱。” - Zhao Chuan's reason for treating physical condition as necessary to sustained musical work.

> “能吃、能睡、能动，就是福。” - his closing health message to listeners.
