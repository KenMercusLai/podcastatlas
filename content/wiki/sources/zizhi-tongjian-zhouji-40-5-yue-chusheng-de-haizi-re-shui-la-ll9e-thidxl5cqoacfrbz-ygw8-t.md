# 《资治通鉴·周纪》40丨5月出生的孩子惹谁啦？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode closes [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]]'s forty-eighth year in 321 BCE with terse succession notices for Zhou and Yan, then turns to [[TianYing|田婴]] and his son [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]]. Its main contribution is twofold: the "sea big fish" advice makes Tian Ying's proposed wall-building at [[XueWarringStates|薛地]] a lesson in [[FiefSecurityIllusion|封地筑城安全幻觉]], while Tian Wen's May-fifth birth story introduces [[BirthTabooSuccessionExclusion|出生日禁忌与继承排斥]] before his later rise begins.

## Key Claims
- The episode frames 321 BCE as Zhou Xian Wang's forty-eighth year and says the chronicle records five main events in that year.
- [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]] dies after a long nominal kingship, and his son succeeds as [[ZhouShenliangwang|周慎靓王 / 周慎亮王]].
- [[YanYiwang|燕易王]] also dies, and [[YanWangKuai|燕王哙]] succeeds; the source treats both royal deaths as brief annalistic notices.
- The episode's developed narrative shifts to [[QiState|齐国]] and the Tian-family branch around [[TianYing|田婴]], who is enfeoffed at [[XueWarringStates|薛地]] and called Jingguo Jun.
- Tian Ying first gains control by urging the Qi ruler to handle many documents personally; once the ruler tires of administrative work, he delegates affairs to Tian Ying.
- After gaining power in Qi, Tian Ying wants to build walls at his fief, but retainers warn him that local fortification cannot substitute for the protection supplied by the Qi state.
- The decisive remonstrance uses a large sea fish analogy: the fish is safe while in the sea but helpless once out of water.
- The episode reads Qi as Tian Ying's "sea": if he keeps Qi, he does not need Xue's walls; if he loses Qi, Xue's walls will not save him.
- [[Mengchangjun|田文]] is introduced as Tian Ying's son and later famous Mengchang Jun, but the episode stops before his adult success.
- The source says Tian Ying had more than forty sons, while Tian Wen's mother had low concubine status, leaving Tian Wen outside the obvious inheritance path.
- Tian Wen is born on the lunar fifth day of the fifth month, which Tian Ying treats as dangerous enough that he orders the mother not to raise him.
- Tian Wen's mother secretly raises him; when he later confronts Tian Ying, Tian Ying explains the taboo by saying a May-born child who grows as tall as the door will harm the parents.
- The episode ends at Tian Wen's question to his father, setting up the next installment rather than resolving how Tian Wen wins recognition.

## Key Quotes
> "齐国就是田婴的大海" - the episode's modernized summary of the fief-security warning.

> "五月子者，长与户齐，将不利其父母。" - Tian Ying's stated reason for rejecting the May-fifth-born child.
