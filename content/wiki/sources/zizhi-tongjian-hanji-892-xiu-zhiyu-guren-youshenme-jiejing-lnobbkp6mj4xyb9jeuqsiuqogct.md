# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》892丨修止语，古人有什么捷径？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens with a source-scoped "修止语" practice of restraining speech, then backfills the 169 CE late-Han layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-893-rongyu-bang-ruhe-biancheng-siwang-mingdan-lmxzqoauf79-swwnk-0cevav0gq|Hanji 893]]. It pairs [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]]'s pacification of the [[DongqiangLateHan|东羌]] with [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s criticism of indiscriminate killing, then closes with the士人 reputation lists that later become party-prohibition targets.

The core historical synthesis is that frontier violence and court reputation politics share an exposure problem. Military victory can hide bad governance when rebellion comes from extraction and harsh local rule, while public moral fame can shift from social honor into target legibility once hostile power treats士人 networks as danger.

## Key Claims

- The opening "修止语" practice treats deliberate silence as a self-warning against complaint, verbal conflict, and loss of inward steadiness rather than as a medical claim about jade.
- [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]]'s Eastern Qiang campaign is presented as a completed military pacification with high casualty, cost, and livestock-capture numbers before his court reward.
- [[SimaGuang|司马光]] criticizes treating non-Han frontier peoples as killable animals and argues that they share the desire for life, security, and escape from disaster.
- The host accepts the root-cause diagnosis: Qiang rebellion is explained by commandery-level exploitation and harshness, so capable military and civil governance matters more than exterminatory revenge.
- The [[JiaXu|贾诩]] anecdote is used source-scopedly to show that Duan's name still carried fear or prestige among Qiang robbers.
- Brief Jiangxia Man and Danyang [[Shanyue|山越]] notices show simultaneous local rebellion outside the main Eastern Qiang case.
- The closing lists 三君, 八俊, 八顾, 八及, and 八厨 as士人 honor categories, then warns that praise and fame will soon become lethal exposure.
- [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]] remains respected despite dismissal, making him the bridge from moral reputation to the [[ReputationListPersecutionTarget|名望榜单迫害目标化]] case developed in Hanji 893.

## Key Quotes
