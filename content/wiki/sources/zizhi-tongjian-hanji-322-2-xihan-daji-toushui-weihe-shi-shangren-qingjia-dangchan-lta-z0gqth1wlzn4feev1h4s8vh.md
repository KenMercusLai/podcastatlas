# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》322-2｜西汉打击偷税，为何使商人倾家荡产

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode presents the Yuan鼎三年 renewal of [[RewardedDenunciationFiscalEnforcement|告缗式奖励告发财政执法]] as a nationwide escalation of merchant-property investigation. It says [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] placed [[DuZhouWesternHan|杜周]] over告缗 cases and sent central legal and supervisory officials to verify accusations, producing broad denunciation, confiscation, and the ruin of many above-median merchant households.

The episode gives the policy a two-sided fiscal judgment. Confiscated money, land, houses, and enslaved people strengthen the treasury, public land, war finance, and central control, while the same process weakens commerce and the social middle. It then places the end of the campaign beside [[SangHongyangWesternHan|桑弘羊]]'s recommendation to stop it and later grain-for-immunity provisions, but its broad claims about redistribution, inequality, prices, and long-run commercial collapse remain source-scoped.

## Key Claims

- The episode dates a renewed告缗 order to Yuan鼎三年 / 114 BCE and says informant rewards generated accusations across the empire.
- It places Du Zhou over告缗 cases and says censors,廷尉 personnel, and other central officials were sent to localities to verify and decide them.
- It treats算缗 as the reporting-and-assessment rule and告缗 as the punitive denunciation mechanism, making them complementary parts of one fiscal campaign.
- It says confiscated cash, land, houses, and enslaved people filled state stores and public-land holdings, with different offices managing imperial and military uses.
- It argues that stronger treasury and war capacity came with merchant bankruptcy, weaker private capital formation, and damage to a social middle between peasants and elites.
- It says Sang Hongyang recommended ending the campaign in Yuan鼎五年 / 112 BCE and that a Yuan封元年 / 110 BCE grain-payment provision offered lasting immunity from later告缗.
- It uses [[BuShiWesternHan|卜式]] as evidence of uncompromising enforcement, claiming that concealed property caused his transfer to crown-prince tutor, but this causal account conflicts with another episode's policy-remonstrance explanation.

## Key Quotes

> “中家以上大抵皆遇告” - the historical formulation used by the episode to describe the breadth of denunciation among merchant households.

> “民儒干食好衣，不事畜藏之业” - the episode's quoted description of a society discouraged from productive accumulation.

## Contradictions

- The episode says Bu Shi concealed assets and was therefore moved from Yushi Dafu to Taizi Taifu. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-357-2-ai-yipian-zhenxin-buru-qian-zhongyao-ls192t04t2duiyxmxkf9gizctlqu|Hanji 357-2]] instead places the transfer after his criticism of salt-iron and junshu-pingzhun burdens and reports “不善文辞” as the stated reason. The supplied summaries do not resolve whether concealment, policy opposition, formal justification, or some combination best explains the change.
- No settled quantitative conclusion is adopted. Confiscation scale, the six-percent profit comparison, land redistribution to ordinary people, inequality relief, administrative allocation, the relationship between deflation and rising prices, long-run commercial collapse, and war or Western Regions causation remain episode-attributed claims requiring primary-source and economic comparison.
- The source is a structured popular-history summary, not a full reconstruction of the算缗 and告缗 statutes. Exact chronology, institutional jurisdictions, regional variation, termination mechanics, and the legal role assigned to Zhang Tang and Du Zhou remain source-scoped.
