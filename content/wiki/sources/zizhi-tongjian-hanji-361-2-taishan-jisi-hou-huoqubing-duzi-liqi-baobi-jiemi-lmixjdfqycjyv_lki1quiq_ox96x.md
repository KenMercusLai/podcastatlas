# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》361-2｜泰山祭祀后，霍去病独子离奇暴毙揭秘

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s [[Taishan|泰山]] ceremony after the restricted summit ascent in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-361-1-hanwudi-liuche-mixin-guishen-de-gushi-lpgclrt_xauneyhm0ri9bi-uenje|Hanji 361-1]]. It follows the earth sacrifice, court congratulations, Yuanfeng era proclamation, route-based tax relief, and a rule for five-year imperial tours, showing [[FengshanRitualLegitimacy|封禅礼制合法性]] extending from secret sacred access into public celebration and administrative benefit.

The second half returns to [[ImmortalityQuestPolitics|求仙政治]]. Fangshi confidence in a meeting at [[Penglai|蓬莱]] encourages Wudi to consider going to sea, but [[DongfangShuo|东方朔]] accepts the emperor's supernatural premise while arguing that a destined meeting needs no forced voyage. Soon afterward [[HuoShanSonOfHuoQubing|霍嬗 / 霍子侯]], the son of [[HuoQubing|霍去病]] who had accompanied the summit rite, dies after a sudden one-day illness. The source supplies no cause, investigation, or evidence of foul play, so the title's “离奇” describes narrative abruptness rather than a solved mystery.

## Key Claims

- After descending Taishan, Han Wudi performs an earth sacrifice at nearby 肃然山 with yellow clothing, music, three-ridged wild rice, and a five-colored earth altar; reported light and cloud are treated as auspicious signs within the episode.
- Court congratulations and the imperial edict connect the rite to the emperor's claimed anxiety about insufficient virtue, the worship of eight spirits, received omens, and heaven-and-earth sacrifice.
- The Yuanfeng 1 settlement combines ritual commemoration with exemptions from land rent, arrears, and poll tax along the travel route, a one-rank grant to titled commoners, a five-year touring rule, and lodging expectations for feudal states near Taishan.
- Fangshi claims after the rainless ceremony renew Wudi's desire to meet Penglai immortals and lead him to consider a personal sea voyage.
- Dongfang Shuo prevents the voyage without directly denying immortals: if the meeting is destined, haste is unnecessary; if it is not, reaching Penglai would not help.
- Huo Zihou dies suddenly after an illness the episode glosses as symptomless onset followed by death within one day; the cited historical notice offers no further explanation.
- Wudi then continues a long northern and western circuit before returning to Ganquan, and the episode previews the fiscal policy branch that follows the expensive tour.

## Key Quotes

> “与神仙相遇要出于自然” - the episode's rendering of Dongfang Shuo's premise-preserving argument against a forced sea voyage.

> “奉车子侯暴病，一日死” - the terse death notice attributed to the historical record, without a stated cause.
