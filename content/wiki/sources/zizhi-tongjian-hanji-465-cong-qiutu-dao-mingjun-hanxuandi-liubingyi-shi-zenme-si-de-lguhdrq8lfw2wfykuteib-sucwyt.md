# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》465｜从囚徒到明君，汉宣帝刘病已是怎么死的？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode closes the Han Xuan-era branch by connecting [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝 / 刘询 / 刘病已]]'s final illness and death with the south-north Xiongnu split, [[HuhanyeChanyuWesternHan|呼韩邪单于]]'s stronger Han-backed position, and [[ZhizhiChanyu|郅支单于]]'s westward expansion. The transcript says Xuan becomes gravely ill, entrusts affairs to [[ShiGaoWesternHan|史高]], [[XiaoWangzhiWesternHan|萧望之]], and [[ZhouKanWesternHan|周堪]], and dies at Weiyang Palace; it does not identify a specific disease or direct medical cause.

The durable synthesis is that Xuan's death is treated as a Western Han turning point rather than an isolated personal event. The host frames Xuan as a rare ruler whose prison childhood, popular experience, post-Huo-Guang consolidation, internal administrative repair, Xiongnu diplomacy, and Western Regions policy together support the later "孝宣中兴" judgment, while [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]]'s accession opens the wiki's subsequent decline-and-court-capture branch.

## Key Claims

- In Ganlu 4 / 50 BCE and Huanglong 1 / 49 BCE, Han favors Huhanye's envoys over Zhizhi's envoys, reinforcing the split the episode describes as Southern Xiongnu versus Northern Xiongnu.
- Zhizhi Chanyu reads Huhanye's Han-backed position as hard to defeat, moves west, kills and absorbs rival forces, defeats Wusun troops, attacks Wujie, Jiankun, and Dingling, and shifts his base far from the old chanyu court.
- Xuan becomes gravely ill after omen language around a comet and chooses Shi Gao, Xiao Wangzhi, and Zhou Kan as entrusted officials for the succession.
- Xuan dies at Weiyang Palace after giving final instructions; the episode states illness and death but gives no specific disease or direct cause.
- The host evaluates Xuan as a ruler shaped by extreme early suffering: survivor of the witchcraft disaster, protected by Bing Ji, raised partly in prison and among common people, then placed on the throne by Huo Guang.
- Xuan's reign is credited with repairing internal disorder, disciplining officials, stabilizing livelihood, using Xiongnu internal division, reducing the Xiongnu threat, incorporating the Western Regions, and establishing the [[WesternRegionsProtectorate|西域都护府]].
- [[BanGuEasternHan|班固]] and [[LiuXiang|刘向]] are invoked as high evaluators of Xuan's statecraft, reward-punishment clarity, personnel assessment, and northern-frontier authority.
- Crown prince Liu Shi succeeds as Han Yuandi, and the host presents his reign as beginning after the Western Han has already started to show decline pressure.
