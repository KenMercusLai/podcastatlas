# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》507丨他斩杀匈奴单于，为何反被下狱？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianshi 4 / 29 BCE under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]], between the flood-rumor politics of [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-505-xihan-de-huicheng-yaoyan-da-le-shui-de-lian-ljuwccxx_aexasafkpbl-y2rvos5|Hanji 505]] and the later Changling and Chen Tang memory sequence. It moves from ignored Yellow River prevention advice and disaster rescue to [[WangZunWesternHan|王尊]]'s rapid suppression of Nanshan bandits, then centers [[ChenTangWesternHan|陈汤]] being impeached by [[KuangHengWesternHan|匡衡]], imprisoned after a false Kangju prince-hostage report, and spared through [[GuYongWesternHan|谷永]]'s proportional-merit defense.

The durable synthesis is that Chengdi-era governance repeatedly tests whether capacity, warning, and merit can survive court incentives. Flood prevention is deferred for cost and followed by emergency rescue and official blame; Wang Zun shows local capacity when command fit is right; Chen Tang shows the harder rule that great frontier service can justify leniency, but not cleanly erase greed, false reporting, or political vulnerability.

## Key Claims

- Feng Qu warns that blocking the Tunshi River leaves lower Yellow River areas exposed, but the court accepts Xu Shang's cost-based advice not to dredge it.
- Three years later the Yellow River breaches at Guantao and Dongjun Jindi, flooding four commanderies and thirty-two counties before Chengdi orders grain relief, boats, and relocation of trapped residents.
- Yushi Dafu Yin Zhong is blamed for an impractical rescue plan and kills himself, showing disaster response as both material logistics and office liability.
- Wang Zun is recommended to Wang Feng as a capable Jingzhao-yin candidate and suppresses Peng Zong's Nanshan bandits within a month after a year of failed military action.
- Kuang Heng impeaches Chen Tang for taking confiscated Kangju property and telling subordinates that distant Western Regions conduct would not be scrutinized.
- Chen Tang later reports that Kangju's hostage is not a real prince; when the hostage is verified as genuine, Chen Tang is imprisoned for deceiving the ruler and faces death.
- Gu Yong argues that a commander who killed a Xiongnu ruler and deterred frontier powers should not be treated like an ordinary offender for a report error.
- Chengdi releases Chen Tang because of past service but strips his title, preserving both merit-based leniency and punishment for misconduct.

## Key Quotes

> "记人之功，忘人之过" - Gu Yong's proportional-judgment principle as presented by the episode.

> "廉洁重在自觉，贵在持久，难在彻底" - the host's closing self-discipline lesson from Chen Tang's fall.
