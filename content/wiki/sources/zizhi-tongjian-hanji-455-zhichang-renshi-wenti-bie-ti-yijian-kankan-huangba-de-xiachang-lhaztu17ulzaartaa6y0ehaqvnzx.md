# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》455｜职场人事问题别提意见！看看黄霸的下场

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode reads [[HuangBaWesternHan|黄霸]]'s recommendation that [[ShiGaoWesternHan|史高]] be made taiwei as a boundary violation under [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]]. The host places the incident against Western Han office structure: the Three Excellencies formally include chancellor, taiwei, and yushi dafu, but inner-court centralization after [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] has already reduced chancellor initiative, and taiwei touches military authority.

The durable synthesis is that Huang Ba's problem is not simply bad taste in personnel. By voluntarily recommending a ruler's trusted outer relative for a military-linked high office, he enters the emperor's appointment domain, risks looking as if he is courting Shi Gao, and disrupts Xuan's timing. The episode converts the historical case into a modern workplace warning about [[SensitivePersonnelRecommendationBoundary|sensitive personnel recommendation boundaries]]: energy and initiative need to stay inside the authority lane a superior has actually opened.

## Key Claims

- The episode frames the Han Three Excellencies as chancellor, taiwei, and yushi dafu, while emphasizing that taiwei is not continuously installed and is especially sensitive because of military power.
- After the earlier bird-omen embarrassment, Huang Ba's political position is insecure, so recommending Shi Gao can be read as an attempt to repair standing or gain support.
- Shi Gao is introduced as a Le陵侯 and shizhong close to Xuan through the emperor's grandmother's family, making him likely to be used by Xuan on Xuan's own schedule.
- Xuan angrily tells Huang Ba that the taiwei office has been abolished, that Huang's duty is moral instruction, grievance transmission, and reduction of unjust imprisonment and theft, and that senior appointment decisions belong to the ruler.
- The host reads the recommendation as touching three sensitive zones at once: personnel authority, military command, and possible chancellor-outer-relative alignment.
- The source emphasizes "数日乃决": Xuan lets Huang Ba wait several days before deciding not to pursue the matter, making delay itself a pressure and warning mechanism.
- Huang Ba responds by removing his cap and apologizing, then never again makes such a request; the episode treats this as the correct survival response once the ruler has read the act as overreach.
- The closing chronology records Xuan's Hedong tour, sacrifice to Houtu, poll-tax reduction, amnesty below capital crimes, Xiongnu settlement in Xihe and Beidi dependent states, and the appointment of [[DuYannianWesternHan|杜延年]] as yushi dafu.
