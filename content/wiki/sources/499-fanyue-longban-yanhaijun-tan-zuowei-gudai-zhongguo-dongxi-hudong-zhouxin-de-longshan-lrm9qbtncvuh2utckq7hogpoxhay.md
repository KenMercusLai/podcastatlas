# 499 翻越陇坂：阎海军谈作为古代中国东西互动轴心的陇山

## Summary
This [[HuzuoHuyou|忽左忽右]] episode uses [[YanHaijun|严海军 / 阎海军]]'s historical-geography writing to argue that [[LongshanGansuShaanxi|陇山 / 龙山]] is a durable hinge in ancient China's east-west interaction. The discussion connects [[Gansu|甘肃]], [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]], Longyou, Hexi, the [[WesternRegionsProtectorate|Western Regions]], [[QinState|秦国]], [[HanWudi|汉武帝]], [[Xiongnu|匈奴]], and modern transport into one route-centered explanation of state expansion and frontier order.

## Key Claims
- [[LongshanGansuShaanxi|陇山 / 龙山]] is presented as a modest but strategically decisive mountain system because routes between [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]], Longyou, Hexi, and the [[WesternRegionsProtectorate|Western Regions]] repeatedly had to cross or work around it.
- The episode frames [[LongshanEastWestCorridor|Longshan east-west corridor logic]] as a driver of ancient Chinese state formation: Qin moved east from the loess highland into Guanzhong, while Western Han later pushed west toward Hexi and the Western Regions.
- [[GuanlongAncientRoad|关陇古道]] is treated as both a local path system and an imperial infrastructure layer that later carried soldiers, grain, documents, and state movement.
- [[TianxiaChina|天下中国]] is defined broadly in the source: not only direct territorial rule, but also cultural recognition, suzerainty, protectorates, jimi offices, and shared order.
- [[YanHaijun|严海军 / 阎海军]] grounds the macro argument in field experience, including walking old route traces, reading place names such as Zhangmianyi, and connecting war history to foot travel through mountain terrain.
- The source distinguishes cautious inference from settled fact when discussing Zhang Qian-related place memory, Qin origins, and local legends.

## Key Quotes
> "龙山虽然不算高大，却长期处在中国东西互动的关键位置." - summary of the episode's central geographic claim.

> "历史并不是抽象叙事，而是嵌在山川、道路、地名、诗歌和人的身体经验之中." - source summary of Yan Haijun's method.

## Contradictions
- The source title uses 阎海军 while the body repeatedly uses 严海军. The wiki keeps one [[YanHaijun|严海军 / 阎海军]] page and treats the name variance as source-scoped.
- The source uses 龙山 in much of the body while the title and historical terms point to 陇山 / 陇坂. The wiki normalizes the discussed mountain system as [[LongshanGansuShaanxi|陇山 / 龙山]] rather than treating the wording as two separate places.
- No settled contradiction with the existing [[QinState|秦国]], [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]], [[HanWudi|汉武帝]], [[Xiongnu|匈奴]], or [[WesternRegionsProtectorate|Western Regions Protectorate]] pages is recorded; this source adds a corridor-centered synthesis rather than revising their existing event chronology.
