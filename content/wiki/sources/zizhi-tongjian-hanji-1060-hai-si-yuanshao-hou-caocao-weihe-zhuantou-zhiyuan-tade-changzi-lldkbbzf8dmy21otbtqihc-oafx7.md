# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1060丨害死袁绍后，曹操为何转头支援他的长子？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the Jian'an 8 target-order debate after [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1059-yuanjia-xiongdi-neidou-liubiao-weihe-zhudong-dang-heshilao-lrgnhcx-fokuitrtfufdtzl1wfos|Hanji 1059]]. [[CaoCao|曹操]] is tempted by wealthy [[Jingzhou|荆州]] and considers letting [[YuanTan|袁谭]] and [[YuanShang|袁尚]] keep fighting, but [[XinPiLateHan|辛毗]] argues through [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] that the Yuan brothers are already exhausted, internally divided, and facing famine. Cao accepts the argument and decides to support Yuan Tan, not as simple trust in Yuan Tan, but as a route into [[DisorderCollapseExploitation|取乱侮亡]] and the final northern campaign window.

## Key Claims
- Cao Cao briefly sets aside the Liu Biao campaign, then reconsiders because Jingzhou's wealth makes a southern move attractive.
- Xin Pi notices Cao Cao's hesitation, reaches him through Guo Jia, and reframes the question away from whether Yuan Tan's request is deceptive.
- Xin Pi argues that Yuan Tan's appeal itself shows desperation, while Yuan Shang's inability to finish Yuan Tan shows that Yuan Shang is also near exhaustion.
- The Yuan-family bloc is described as militarily defeated, internally purged of advisers, territorially divided, and socially weakened by drought, locusts, and famine.
- Attacking [[YeLateHan|邺城]] would force Yuan Shang into a dilemma: abandon the city or return and expose himself to Yuan Tan's rear attack.
- Jingzhou is rich but not yet an exposed target, while Hebei shows both internal disorder and collapse signs; this is the episode's practical reading of [[DisorderCollapseExploitation|取乱侮亡]].
- Cao Cao's decision to aid Yuan Tan is therefore an intervention strategy, using one Yuan claimant's request to cut into the Yuan-family war rather than a charitable rescue or trust-based alliance.

## Key Quotes
> "取乱侮亡" - Xin Pi's classical timing formula for attacking a disordered and collapsing rival.

> "像狂风扫落叶一样容易" - the episode's image for striking exhausted opponents.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is recorded. Hanji 1059 emphasizes Xun You's argument that Cao should not wait too long, while this source continues the same decision window by making Xin Pi's direct persuasion the decisive final push.
- Source-scope caution: the claim that Hebei would fall as easily as leaves before wind is treated as Xin Pi's persuasive strategic forecast, not proof that the subsequent campaign had no costs.
