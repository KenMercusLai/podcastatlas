# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》773丨东汉美女政治家邓绥，如何逆袭成皇后？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 102 CE [[EmperorHeOfHan|汉和帝]] layer before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-774-banchao-jiebanren-ruhe-gaoluan-xiyu-lnbnoc-uys8kee7pz9yecxecrpyd|Hanji 774]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-775-gan-xiang-mingyun-shuo-no-bukuishi-you-tounao-de-nvzhumu-lh0erdh890ooow6dn0wvf1fbzedf|Hanji 775]]. It first follows [[CaoFengLateHan|曹凤]]'s diagnosis of [[ShaodangQiang|烧当羌]] strength around the West Sea, then turns to [[EmpressYinHeLateHan|殷皇后]]'s jealousy toward [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|邓绥]], the reported threat against the Deng family, and the wugu case that ends with Yin's deposition.

The durable synthesis is that Emperor He's 102 CE court shows two forms of political control under stress. On the frontier, Cao Feng proposes [[FrontierResourceIntegration|边疆资源整合治理]] through garrison farming, migration, and state management of agriculture and salt; in the palace, favor competition and wugu accusation turn harem rivalry into lethal household politics before Deng's later empress and regency arc.

## Key Claims

- Cao Feng argues that repeated Qiang rebellion is anchored in the Shaodang group's access to fertile valleys, West Sea fishery, and salt resources, not only in military defiance.
- His policy answer combines tuntian, Han migration, intermarriage or cultural blending, and state administration of agriculture and salt to make frontier resources legible to the court.
- Emperor He accepts the proposal, restores the former West Sea commandery area, moves the Jincheng western commandant office there, appoints Cao Feng, and expands garrison farming before later An-era Qiang rebellion interrupts completion.
- The episode keeps a short court-ritual and southern-frontier notice: Emperor He visits [[Taixue|太学]], holds a village archery rite, grants amnesty, and later defeats and relocates the Wushan Man under Xu Sheng.
- Empress Yin's jealousy rises after Emperor He's favor shifts toward Deng Sui, who had entered the palace as a guiren in 96 CE.
- Yin reportedly says that if she gains power after Emperor He's death, she will wipe out the Deng family; Deng hears this and nearly takes poison before a palace attendant stops her.
- A wugu accusation involving Yin and her maternal grandmother Deng Zhu leads Zhang Shen and Chen Bao to impeach the case as great rebellion, and several Yin/Deng Zhu relatives die or are exiled.
- On 102-06-22, Empress Yin is deposed and moved to Tong Palace; the episode presents this as the immediate political opening before Deng's later elevation.

## Key Quotes

> "总是在骄傲里，就会毁了自己" - opening moral frame for refusing correction.

> "绝不让邓氏全家留一个活口" - reported threat that makes Deng Sui hear palace rivalry as family-survival danger.

> "大逆无道" - charge language attached to the wugu impeachment in the source summary.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is recorded. This source backfills the 102 CE layer before Hanji 774's Ban Chao handoff and Hanji 775's fuller Deng Sui formation narrative.
- Scope caution: the title promises Deng's rise to empress, but the source summary mainly reaches Empress Yin's deposition; the later appointment is developed more fully by Hanji 775.
- Identity caution: Empress Yin's maternal grandmother Deng Zhu shares the Deng surname but is not merged with Deng Sui's natal family; Zhang Shen, Chen Bao, Yin Gang, Yin Fu, Yin Yi, Yin Chang, Liu Ce, Liu Zhang, Xu Sheng, and exact exile details remain source-scoped.
