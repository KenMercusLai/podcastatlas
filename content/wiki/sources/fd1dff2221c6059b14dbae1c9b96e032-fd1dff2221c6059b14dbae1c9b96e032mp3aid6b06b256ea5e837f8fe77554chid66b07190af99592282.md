# AI數據標注，能否為縣城「寶媽」帶來「就業賦能」？

## Summary
This [[DuanwenNewsPodcast|端聞 / 端傳媒新聞播客]] episode follows women doing [[DataAnnotationLabor|AI data annotation]] at [[YongheAidoTechnology|永和爱豆科技]] in [[YongheCounty|山西永和县]]. The work briefly combined office conditions, caregiving-compatible hours, and unusually high local earnings, but also imposed piece-rate monitoring, bodily strain, and exposure to sexual and violent material.

The episode's central qualification is that a growing AI-data market did not produce durable local advancement. As models automated simple labeling and demand shifted toward specialized, degree-dependent work, the county base could not supply the requested workforce; many women returned to casual work or services without portable credentials or a protected transition path.

## Key Claims
- Around 2019, technology companies, local governments, and philanthropic organizations promoted annotation bases in less-developed central and western counties as digital poverty relief and women's employment.
- [[YongheAidoTechnology|永和爱豆科技]] reportedly employed more than 170 people from 2021 to 2023, many of them mothers constrained by childcare and limited local alternatives.
- Piece-rate targets, speed and accuracy thresholds, peer-visible progress, and public failure lists made ostensibly advanced digital work resemble a tightly disciplined production line.
- Some workers reviewed thousands of sexual, violent, or graphic images per day without professional psychological support; prolonged computer work also produced eye, back, and limb problems.
- At the reported peak, ordinary earnings exceeded local service work and some workers reached nearly RMB 8,000 or more than RMB 10,000 per month, but the highest incomes depended on abundant simple tasks and long overtime.
- Models increasingly performed the first pass on basic annotation while unit prices fell; newer work demanded language, medical, historical, or 3D point-cloud expertise and often a cohort of university graduates.
- Market growth and local job loss coexisted: demand moved up the skill ladder and toward in-house teams, while Yonghe's small and out-migrating population could not meet project hiring requirements.
- The episode argues that short-lived employment access is not durable empowerment when a project leaves workers without transferable skills, credentials, retraining, or protection against technological displacement.

## Key Quotes
> “我们把AI训练出来了，简单的东西现在AI自己就可以做了。” - one former annotator on the shift from manual first-pass labeling to human checking.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is recorded. The episode qualifies simple claims that AI-data-market growth necessarily preserves the same jobs, firms, skills, or locations.
- The supplied file summarizes one county and several workers rather than a representative national sample. The 70% female share, market forecasts, outsourcing share, wage levels, unit prices, workforce counts, and dates are episode-reported and are not independently verified here.
- Worker testimony establishes serious exposure and perceived harm but cannot determine long-term clinical effects, the frequency of these conditions across the industry, or the intent of every company, government, or philanthropic participant.
