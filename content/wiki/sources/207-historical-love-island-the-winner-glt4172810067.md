# 207. Historical Love Island: THE WINNER

## Summary

This [[TheRestIsHistory]] results episode concludes the first historical Love Island contest by awarding [[StanleyBaldwin]] and [[Theodora]] 47 percent of the audience vote. General Custer and Lola Montes place second, [[LordByron|Lord Byron]] and Sporus third, and Jimmy Carter and Olympias fourth, making the outcome an example of [[HistoricalFiguresAsRealityTVArchetypes|historical figures recast as reality-TV archetypes]] rather than a conventional historical ranking.

The comic result also opens a serious preview of Theodora's partnership with [[JustinianI]], the transition from the Roman to the [[ByzantineEmpire|Byzantine world]], reconquest, [[Procopius]], [[HagiaSophia|Hagia Sophia]], sainthood, and the reign's long geopolitical consequences. Later dedicated episodes provide the wiki's fuller treatment of those subjects.

## Key Claims

- Stanley Baldwin and Theodora win decisively with 47 percent of the vote; Custer and Montes receive 22.3 percent, Byron and Sporus 16.4 percent, and Carter and Olympias 14.3 percent.
- Audience voting turns speculative compatibility into a present-day popularity judgment shaped by charm, comic contrast, scandal, and familiarity rather than historical importance.
- Baldwin's appeal is broadened through Lucy Baldwin's maternity advocacy and the couple's acceptance of their son Oliver's unconventional life, though the evidence remains a brief comic sketch.
- Byron's scandalous conduct and Olympias's associations with snakes and murder show how moral reputation can outweigh glamour within the format.
- The Theodora discussion shifts from comedy toward a serious claim that she and Justinian formed a consequential political partnership at a hinge between late Roman and Byzantine history.
- The format depends on callbacks and listener participation, so it works better as a conclusion to the earlier contest than as a stand-alone history lesson.
