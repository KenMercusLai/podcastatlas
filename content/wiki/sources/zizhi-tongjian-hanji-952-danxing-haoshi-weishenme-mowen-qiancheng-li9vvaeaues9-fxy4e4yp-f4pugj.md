# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》952丨但行好事，为什么莫问前程？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate pre-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-953-luanshi-zhizhong-yaobuyao-jiangjiu-renyi-lrzfimgqatjxyw9zx9ry2kiwxkon|Hanji 953]] late-Han field through [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s destruction of [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]], [[WangYunLateHan|王允]]'s hidden waiting posture, and [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]]'s coercive rise before joining [[YuanShu|袁术]]. It uses the maxim "但行好事，莫问前程" to frame moral self-governance, then tests that frame against arson, tomb looting, torture, clan killing, forged orders, loose speech, and supply coercion. Its distinct contribution is that anti-Dong-Zhuo disorder is already morally and institutionally broken before the formal coalition story: Dong Zhuo destroys the capital's living and ancestral order, while Sun Jian's early effectiveness is mixed with private vengeance and fear-based compliance.

## Key Claims

- The opening moral frame links conscience and security: the person who does wrongful things cannot fully sleep, while "但行好事" is presented less as an outcome guarantee than as a discipline of not betraying one's own heart.
- After forcing [[EmperorXianOfHan|Emperor Xian]] west, [[DongZhuo|Dong Zhuo]] remains around [[LuoyangLateHan|Luoyang]] and burns palaces, offices, homes, and the surrounding urban field into scorched earth.
- Dong Zhuo's looting of imperial mausoleums and elite graves makes [[TombDesecrationPoliticalViolence|tomb-desecration political violence]] part of the Luoyang disaster: the episode treats grave robbery as an assault on the dead, cultural inheritance, and public order, not merely as plunder.
- Dong Zhuo's cruelty extends to burning captured Shandong soldiers and killing more than fifty members of [[YuanShao|Yuan Shao]]'s Yuan clan, including children, tying military terror to kinship extermination.
- [[WangYunLateHan|Wang Yun]] handles court affairs after Emperor Xian reaches [[ChangAnHanCapital|Chang'an]], outwardly submitting to Dong Zhuo while waiting for a chance to remove him.
- In [[Jingzhou]], [[WangRuiLateHan|Wang Rui]]'s contempt for [[SunJianLateHan|Sun Jian]] and threat against [[CaoYinLateHan|Cao Yin]] create the opening for Cao Yin's forged court order and Sun Jian's unverified attack.
- Wang Rui's suicide after Sun Jian's accusation becomes a case of [[SpeechAsMoralDiagnostic|speech as moral diagnostic]] and [[VirtuePracticeInternalization|virtue-practice internalization]]: loose contempt and threats become political survival risks.
- Sun Jian's killing of [[ZhangZiLateHan|Zhang Zi]] after a Nanyang grain refusal shows supply coercion inside the anti-Dong field before his later coalition reputation is stabilized.
- [[YuanShu|Yuan Shu]]'s sponsorship at Luyang gives Sun Jian nominal titles, including acting General Who Smashes the Caitiffs and Yu Province inspector, but the episode stresses that these appointments function through Yuan Shu's control because the imperial court is captured by Dong Zhuo.

## Key Quotes

> "但行好事，莫问前程。" - opening moral frame.

> "管不住身口意。" - host's diagnosis of Wang Rui's failure.

> "愚昧无知。" - Sun Jian's stated accusation against Wang Rui in the source account.

## Connections

- [[DongZhuo|Dong Zhuo]] - destroys Luoyang's built environment, loots tombs, burns captives, and extends violence to the Yuan clan.
- [[LuoyangLateHan|Luoyang (late Han)]] - becomes the urban and ritual casualty of Dong Zhuo's forced relocation politics.
- [[TombDesecrationPoliticalViolence]] - captures the episode's emphasis on grave robbery as cultural and ancestral violence.
- [[WangYunLateHan|Wang Yun]] - embodies hidden compliance under a captured court before the later anti-Dong-Zhuo coup arc.
- [[SunJianLateHan|Sun Jian]] - appears as both effective anti-Dong military actor and coercive local operator whose early rise includes forged authority and supply violence.
- [[WangRuiLateHan|Wang Rui]], [[CaoYinLateHan|Cao Yin]], and [[ZhangZiLateHan|Zhang Zi]] - form the local Jingzhou/Nanyang sequence through which Sun Jian's pre-Yuan-Shu violence is narrated.
- [[YuanShu|Yuan Shu]] and [[AntiDongZhuoCoalition|Anti-Dong-Zhuo Coalition]] - connect Sun Jian's local campaign behavior to the wider anti-Dong field.
- [[HeartFieldEthics|Heart-field ethics]], [[VirtuePracticeInternalization|virtue-practice internalization]], and [[SpeechAsMoralDiagnostic|speech as moral diagnostic]] - provide the moral-analysis vocabulary for the episode's "身口意" and "莫问前程" frame.
- [[RomanceOfTheThreeKingdoms]] and [[LyuBu|Lü Bu]] - the source notes a novelistic version in which Lü Bu is sent to plunder graves, making the literary scene useful but not identical to the chronicle account.
