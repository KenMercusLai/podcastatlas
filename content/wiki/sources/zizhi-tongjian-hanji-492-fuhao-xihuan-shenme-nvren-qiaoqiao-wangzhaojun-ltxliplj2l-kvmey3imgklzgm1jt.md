# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》492丨富豪喜欢什么女人？瞧瞧王昭君！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode fills the [[WangZhaojun|王昭君]] departure story previewed in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-491-zhongguo-waijiao-zui-qiangying-de-shike-jing-shi-zai-xihan-lpgajp_vky9dx7jvnoahaxdfjflk|Hanji 491]] and followed by the frontier-policy sequence in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-493-xiongdi-shi-hehuo-weisha-douhui-chouren-shi-sanhuo-lishi-gaosu-ni-llquknhihdms-6jyjbtfdfhvwv4g|Hanji 493]]. It presents [[HuhanyeChanyuWesternHan|呼韩邪单于]]'s 33 BCE court visit and marriage request, Wang Zhaojun's years of palace invisibility, her voluntary request to marry into Xiongnu, and [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]]'s belated regret after seeing her.

The durable synthesis is that the host turns the Wang Zhaojun story into a case about beauty as resource and risk. Beauty can create opportunity, but only when paired with judgment, timing, communication, and self-command; otherwise it can be hidden by institutions, consumed by others' jealousy, or become fragile capital that fades without inner capacity.

## Key Claims

- The episode frames beauty as a resource that needs the ability to manage it; unmanaged beauty can become danger rather than advantage.
- Huhanye Chanyu's court visit and request to become a Han son-in-law make Wang Zhaojun's marriage a diplomatic event, not only a romantic or tragic story.
- The host follows the image-painter tradition: Wang Zhaojun refuses to bribe the painter, is represented badly, and therefore remains unseen by Han Yuandi.
- Wang Zhaojun's voluntary request to go to Xiongnu is interpreted as active situation judgment: she compares palace stagnation with the improved Han-Xiongnu relationship and chooses the better opening.
- Her banquet appearance is treated as prepared social performance, combining beauty, bearing, speech, and composure rather than raw looks alone.
- Han Yuandi's regret and the painters' punishment show how palace systems can hide value and then violently correct only after the ruler notices the loss.
- The host uses Wallis Simpson, Qingwen, You Sanjie, and other literary figures to argue that lasting attraction and life stability depend on intelligence, emotional value, character, and continued learning more than youth or beauty alone.

## Key Quotes

> "美貌是一把双刃剑" - the episode's risk frame for beauty as advantage and danger.

> "智慧不能驾驭美貌" - the closing warning that beauty without judgment can become destructive.
