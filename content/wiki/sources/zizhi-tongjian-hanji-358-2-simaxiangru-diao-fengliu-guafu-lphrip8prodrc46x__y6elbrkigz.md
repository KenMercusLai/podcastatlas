# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》358-2｜司马相如钓风流寡妇

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[SimaXiangru|司马相如]] through two forms of mediated visibility. [[YangDeyiWesternHan|杨得意]] turns literary talent into access to [[HanWudi|汉武帝]], while [[WangJiLinqiongWesternHan|临邛县令王吉]] repeatedly visits Sima and helps make him appear important enough to enter [[ZhuoWangsunWesternHan|卓王孙]]'s banquet circle.

The second half retells the “琴挑文君” tradition: [[FengQiuHuang|《凤求凰》]] reaches [[ZhuoWenjun|卓文君]] across the banquet's social boundary, followed in the episode by a maid-carried message and an elopement to Chengdu. The durable synthesis is that talent, access, reputation staging, and intimate persuasion interact in both the court and romance stories; the episode's stronger claims about deception, private feeling, authorship, and later betrayal remain popular-history narration rather than settled fact.

## Key Claims

- Han Wudi's admiration for Sima Xiangru's fu becomes actionable only after the close attendant Yang Deyi identifies the living writer and arranges access.
- The episode uses this recommendation to argue that ability alone may remain invisible when no trusted intermediary carries it into a scarce-attention setting.
- Sima's court rise is presented as literary success with an ethical qualification: the host cites [[SuShi|苏轼]]'s criticism that the grand fu could flatter imperial desire through exaggeration.
- In Linqiong, Wang Ji's repeated public visits and Sima's selective refusals create a reputation signal that makes local elites treat Sima as an exceptional guest.
- Zhuo Wangsun's banquet supplies the setting in which Sima performs 《凤求凰》 and Zhuo Wenjun hears it from behind a screen.
- The episode narrates the later message and elopement as a deliberately engineered courtship, then foreshadows Sima's later attachment to another woman and Zhuo Wenjun's response.

## Key Quotes

> “杨意不逢，抚凌云而自惜。” - the *Tengwang Ge Xu* allusion used to connect Yang Deyi's recommendation with the problem of unseen talent.

> “愿得一人心，白头不相离。” - the line the episode associates with Zhuo Wenjun; its textual attribution is retained as source-scoped.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with existing wiki content was found.
- The episode first links Yang Deyi to an already circulating 《上林赋》 and later says Sima writes 《天子游猎赋》 after the summons. The exact work sequence and title relationship are left source-scoped rather than harmonized here.
- Sima Xiangru's birthplace, Yang Deyi's claimed hometown connection, Wang Ji and Sima's exact plan, the maid and paper message, Zhuo Wenjun's private reaction, and the later-betrayal account require fuller primary-source comparison.
- The authorship and transmission of 《凤求凰》 and the attribution of “愿得一人心，白头不相离” to Zhuo Wenjun are not established by this episode alone.
- [[WangJiLinqiongWesternHan|The Linqiong magistrate Wang Ji]] is kept distinct from [[WangJiWesternHan|the later Western Han remonstrant]], [[WangJiQin|the Qin figure]], and [[WangJiLateHan|the late-Han official]].
