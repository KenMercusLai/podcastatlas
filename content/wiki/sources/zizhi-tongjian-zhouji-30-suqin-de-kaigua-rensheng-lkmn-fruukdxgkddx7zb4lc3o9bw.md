# 《资治通鉴·周纪》30丨苏秦的“开挂”人生

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[SuQin|苏秦]]'s post-failure biography after the family humiliation developed in the prior source. It shifts from broad study to deep reading of [[YinfuJing|《阴符》]], where Su Qin extracts [[ChuaimoTechnique|揣摩]] as a combined reading of political situation and counterpart psychology. The episode then slows the success story down: Su Qin still fails with [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]], [[YingSi|秦惠文王]], and [[ZhaoState|赵国]] before persuading [[YanWengong|燕文公]], receiving Yan backing, and turning a personal comeback into the first step toward [[HezongAlliance|合纵]].

## Key Claims
- [[SuQin|苏秦]]'s recovery begins with a second learning project: after reading broadly, he decides that real leverage requires reading one text deeply rather than merely collecting books.
- The episode distinguishes textual labels for [[YinfuJing|《阴符》]]: [[Shiji|《史记》]] calls it 《周书阴符》, while [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] calls it 《太公阴符之谋》.
- The host treats the [[Liutao|《六韬》]] identification of 《太公阴符之谋》 as less persuasive because that short 《阴符》 chapter is mainly about military signaling rather than the broad persuader art Su Qin needs.
- [[ChuaimoTechnique|揣摩]] is explained as more than ordinary guessing: it joins clear reading of the external situation with insight into the listener's motives and fears.
- Su Qin's first post-study attempt with [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]] fails because familiar local observers do not grant him new authority.
- His attempt with [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] also fails because [[QinState|秦国]] has just executed [[ShangYang|商鞅]] and is not receptive to persuader politics.
- [[ZhaoState|赵国]] initially rejects him as well, so the episode does not present self-study as immediate success.
- [[YanState|燕国]] becomes the breakthrough because Su Qin persuades [[YanWengong|燕文公]] that Yan's safety depends on Zhao functioning as a geographic shield against Qin.
- Yan Wen Gong's funding is Su Qin's "first bucket of gold": it is financial support, but also a status credential that lets him return to Zhao as Yan's diplomatic agent.
- The episode's closing alliance lesson is [[CommonEnemyAlliance|共同敌人联盟]]: shared threat can be more useful than shared interest when trying to bind states together.

## Key Quotes
> "揣摩" - the compact term the episode uses for Su Qin's breakthrough method after reading 《阴符》.

> "先知在本乡本地得不到尊重" - the analogy for why Su Qin's first renewed attempt fails near home.

> "第一桶金" - the episode's label for Yan Wen Gong's material support and political credentialing.

> "共同利益不如共同敌人重要" - the closing lesson attached to the coming Yan-Zhao alliance work.
