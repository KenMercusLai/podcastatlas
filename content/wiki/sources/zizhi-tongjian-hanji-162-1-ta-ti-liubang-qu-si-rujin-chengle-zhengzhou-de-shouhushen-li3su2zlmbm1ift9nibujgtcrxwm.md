# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》162-1｜他替刘邦去死 如今成了郑州的守护神

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the [[XingyangStandoff|荥阳相持]] after [[ChenPing|陈平]]'s anti-[[FanZeng|范增]] counterintelligence by narrating [[JiXin|纪信]]'s decision to impersonate [[LiuBang|刘邦]] and die so Liu Bang can escape. It then follows Liu Bang's post-escape delegation to [[HanWangXin|韩王信]], [[ZhouKe|周苛]], [[WeiBao|魏豹]], and [[CongGong|枞公]], the execution of Wei Bao inside the garrison, and [[YuanShengChuHan|袁先生 / 袁生]]'s strategy of drawing [[XiangYu|项羽]] south through [[Wuguan|武关]]. The source links Ji Xin's sacrifice to later cult memory in [[Chenghuang|城隍]] worship and present-day [[Zhengzhou|郑州]], while keeping the host's judgments about Liu Bang's political performance and Xiang Yu's reactivity as source-scoped interpretation.

## Key Claims
- In the fifth month, Xiang Yu intensifies the siege of [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]], making Liu Bang's position inside the [[XingyangStandoff|荥阳相持]] critical after Fan Zeng's exit.
- [[JiXin|纪信]], described as a trusted Liu Bang-side general and Hongmen attendant, volunteers to imitate Liu Bang, draw Chu attention, and give Liu Bang a chance to escape.
- The host speculates that Liu Bang's initial hesitation may have been performative, showing a loyal-subordinate drama after the survival plan was already acceptable.
- [[ChenPing|陈平]] sends two thousand women out the east gate to distract Chu troops before Ji Xin rides out in Liu Bang's carriage with the yellow canopy and announces that Han has run out of food and will surrender.
- Chu troops crowd toward the east gate to watch the supposed surrender, while Liu Bang escapes through the west gate with several dozen cavalry.
- Liu Bang leaves [[HanWangXin|韩王信]], [[ZhouKe|周苛]], [[WeiBao|魏豹]], and [[CongGong|枞公]] to hold Xingyang after his departure.
- Xiang Yu discovers that the carriage carries Ji Xin rather than Liu Bang; when Ji Xin says Liu Bang has already gone, Xiang Yu has Ji Xin burned alive.
- After the empire is settled, Liu Bang is said to build a Zhongyou temple in Ji Xin's Sichuan hometown, while Xingyang people honor Ji Xin as a [[Chenghuang|城隍]] protecting the city.
- The source identifies ancient Xingyang with present-day [[Zhengzhou|郑州]] in this cult-memory frame and says the city-god temple survives through repeated destruction and repair.
- Zhou Ke and Cong Gong judge Wei Bao untrustworthy because he has repeatedly changed sides; they execute him and use the act to commit the garrison to survival or death.
- The host argues that Liu Bang's ability to attract repeated death-risking loyalty cannot be explained only by rewards, because offices and titles do not outweigh life itself.
- [[YuanShengChuHan|袁先生 / 袁生]] advises Liu Bang not to return directly to Xingyang, but to move through [[Wuguan|武关]] toward Wan, Ye, and Chou, hold fortified positions, and lure Xiang Yu south.
- Liu Bang accepts the plan, reaches the Wan area, recruits along the route, and refuses battle, giving Xingyang, [[Chenggao|成皋]], and [[HanXin|韩信]] time to recover and consolidate.
- Xiang Yu follows south, but [[PengYue|彭越]] cuts Chu logistics around the [[SuiRiver|睢水]], leaving Xiang Yu caught between Liu Bang's fortified front and pressure on his rear supply line.

## Key Quotes
> "愿意代替刘邦出面欺骗项羽" - Ji Xin's proposed sacrifice as summarized by the source.

> "粮草已尽、汉王投降" - the deception announced from Liu Bang's carriage.

> "再丰厚的封爵奖赏也比不上性命" - the host's frame for why rewards alone cannot explain Liu Bang-side loyalty.
