# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》828丨大将军梁冀：擅权废立的东汉奸臣

## Summary
This episode backfills the 146 CE succession crisis immediately after [[EmperorZhiOfHan]]'s death. [[CaoTengLateHan]] advises [[LiangJiLateHan]] to install Liwu Hou Liu Zhi, later [[EmperorHuanOfHan]], rather than [[LiuSuanLateHan]], because a strict and clear ruler would threaten Liang Ji's guests and the eunuch bloc's fortunes.

The episode then traces how Liang Ji forces court consensus, how [[LiGuLateHan]] and [[DuQiaoLateHan]] resist, how [[EmpressDowagerLiangNaLateHan]] removes Li Gu from office, and how [[ZhuMuLateHan]]'s corrective advice fails to redirect the regime. The closing Teng Fu case extends the same pattern into merit politics: an upright official with anti-bandit merit loses office after eunuch hostility and [[HuGuangLateHan]]'s impeachment.

## Key Claims
- Liang Na had summoned Liu Zhi to Luoyang before Emperor Zhi's death because she intended to marry her younger sister to him, placing him close enough to become a succession option when the throne became vacant.
- Cao Teng reframed the succession for Liang Ji as self-preservation: [[LiuSuanLateHan]]'s severity and clarity would endanger Liang Ji's retainers and eunuch fortunes, while Liu Zhi would protect the existing power bloc.
- Liang Ji converts Cao Teng's argument into institutional action by pressing the Three Excellencies and Nine Ministers to accept Liu Zhi; Hu Guang and Zhao Jie submit, while Li Gu and Du Qiao continue to object.
- Li Gu's further letter to Liang Ji turns opposition into personal danger; Liang Ji becomes angrier and has Liang Na remove Li Gu from the taiwei office.
- Liu Zhi enters the South Palace as [[EmperorHuanOfHan]], but because he is fifteen and not yet capped, Liang Na continues hearing government from court.
- Zhu Mu, serving on Liang Ji's staff, warns that the new emperor needs upright teachers and qualified classical officials; Liang Ji does not adopt the advice.
- Teng Fu's dismissal shows that proven merit could be defeated by eunuch resentment and senior-official accommodation of the palace power structure.
- Du Qiao's continued uprightness after Li Gu's dismissal foreshadows the next stage of conflict between principled ministers and Liang Ji's regime.

## Key Quotes
