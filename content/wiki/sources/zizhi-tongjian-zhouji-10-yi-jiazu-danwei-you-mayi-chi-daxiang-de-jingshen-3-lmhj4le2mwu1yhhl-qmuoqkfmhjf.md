# 《资治通鉴·周纪》10丨 以家族单位 有蚂蚁吃大象的精神（3）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the [[WeiState|魏国]] succession crisis introduced in part 2: [[GongzhongHuan|公中缓]] seeks outside help, [[HanState|韩国]] and [[ZhaoState|赵国]] defeat Wei at [[ZhuozeBattle|浊泽]], but their disagreement over the settlement lets [[LiangHuiWang|魏罃 / 梁惠王]] survive. Its main statecraft lesson is that [[SuccessionNonDesignationRisk|unclear succession]] can invite external intervention, and even a winning coalition can fail if its members cannot agree whether they want immediate gains or long-term threat removal.

## Key Claims
- In 369 BCE, [[ZhouLiewang|周烈王]]'s seventh year brings a solar eclipse, Zhou Lie Wang's death and [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]]'s accession, and the continuation of the [[WeiState|魏国]] succession war between [[LiangHuiWang|魏罃]] and [[GongzhongHuan|公中缓]].
- [[WangChuo|王绰]], a Wei official, flees to [[HanState|韩国]], giving Han a reason to judge Wei as internally vulnerable.
- [[GongsunQi|公孙齐]] urges [[HanYihou|韩懿侯]] to attack Wei while Wei is distracted by civil conflict.
- The episode generalizes the Wei crisis into [[SuccessionCrisisIntervention|succession-crisis intervention]]: a losing claimant can seek external help, while neighboring states intervene because victory may produce land, influence, or a dependent ruler.
- [[GongzhongHuan|公中缓]] goes to Zhao's capital to ask for aid, and [[HanYihou|韩懿侯]] joins [[ZhaoChenghou|赵成侯]] in attacking Wei.
- Han and Zhao defeat Wei in the [[ZhuozeBattle|Battle of Zhuoze]] and besiege [[Anyi|安邑]], Wei's capital.
- Zhao proposes killing Wei Ying, installing Gongzhong Huan, and taking territory; Han rejects this as both reputationally cruel and too short-term.
- Han proposes dividing Wei between Wei Ying and Gongzhong Huan so that Wei no longer remains a first-tier threat.
- Because Han and Zhao cannot align on a postwar settlement, Han withdraws at night and Zhao also abandons the siege, turning victory into [[CoalitionSettlementFailure|coalition settlement failure]].
- Wei Ying then kills Gongzhong Huan and becomes Wei's ruler, later known as [[LiangHuiWang|梁惠王]], whose later name anchors the opening scene of [[MenciusText|《孟子》]].
- The episode cites [[SimaQian|司马迁]]'s judgment that Wei Ying survived and Wei was not divided because Han and Zhao's plans did not match.

## Key Quotes
> "内外勾结" - the episode's label for the pattern in which an internal claimant seeks outside aid.

> "分赃方案没有谈拢" - the episode's explanation for why the victorious Han-Zhao coalition breaks apart.

> "孟子见梁惠王" - the later classical frame used to identify Wei Ying's historical afterlife.
