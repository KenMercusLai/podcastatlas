# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1054丨惊叹！曹操、袁绍、刘备的命运转折

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows the post-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]] transition in Jian'an 6. [[XunYu|荀彧]] persuades [[CaoCao|曹操]] to keep pressure on [[YuanShao|袁绍]] rather than move against [[LiuBei|刘备]], leading into the [[CangtingBattleLateHan|Cangting]] victory and Yuan's further decline. It then treats Liu Bei's flight to [[LiuBiao|刘表]] and years at [[XinyeLateHan|新野]] as [[ActivePoliticalDormancy|active political dormancy]], before showing [[ZhangLiao|张辽]] persuading [[ChangXiLateHan|昌豨]] to surrender through observation, old ties, interest framing, and personal guarantee.

## Key Claims
- Jian'an 6 Cao Cao initially considers attacking Liu Bei, but Xun Yu argues Yuan Shao's post-Guandu weakness is the urgent target because leaving him time to recover could expose Cao's rear.
- Cao Cao follows the advice, advances along the Yellow River, and defeats Yuan Shao's Cangting force, making Cangting a post-Guandu continuation rather than a separate isolated victory.
- Liu Bei is later defeated by Cao Cao, flees to Jingzhou, receives Liu Biao's guest treatment and troops, and stations at Xinye for several years.
- The "髀肉复生" anecdote is read as Liu Bei's anxiety over stalled achievement rather than simple comfort.
- Liu Bei's Xinye period is framed as strategic hiding and accumulation: low profile, frontier shelter, Jingzhou elite and refugee support, and patient waiting for timing.
- Zhang Liao reads Chang Xi's reduced arrows and repeated looking as surrender hesitation, then uses old ties, Cao Cao's strength, promised office, and personal guarantee to make surrender credible.
- Chang Xi's surrender and appointment as Donghai taishou show successful short-term incorporation, but his later rebellion and execution by [[YuJin|于禁]] keep the case qualified.

## Key Quotes
> "老骥伏枥，志在千里；烈士暮年，壮心不已." - the episode uses Cao Cao's later line as a contrast to Liu Bei's quieter persistence.

> "蛰伏不等于躺平." - the host's interpretation of Liu Bei's Xinye years.
