# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》162-2｜刘邦如何收拾位高权重的下属

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the late [[XingyangStandoff|荥阳相持]] after [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-162-1-ta-ti-liubang-qu-si-rujin-chengle-zhengzhou-de-shouhushen-li3su2zlmbm1ift9nibujgtcrxwm|Hanji 162-1]] by showing how [[PengYue|彭越]]'s rear raids force [[XiangYu|项羽]] to shuttle between fronts. Its central scene is [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s emergency seizure of [[HanXin|韩信]] and [[ZhangEr|张耳]]'s seals and troop tokens at Xiao Xiuwu after [[Chenggao|成皋]] falls again. The source frames the move as [[CommandAuthorityRecapture|统帅权再收束]]: Liu Bang does not destroy Han Xin's usefulness, but strips immediate troop control, redeploys Zhang Er to hold Zhao, and sends Han Xin toward [[QiState|齐国]].

## Key Claims
- The episode opens with a general political question: a ruler facing powerful, high-status subordinates must prevent delegated authority from becoming a separate center that can delay or threaten the ruler's larger plan.
- After the [[PengchengBattle|彭城之战]] collapse, [[PengYue|彭越]] loses some captured cities but resumes mobile pressure along the Yellow River, supporting the trapped Han side and cutting Chu food routes.
- Peng Yue's move toward [[Xiapi|下邳]] matters because the area is close to [[Xuzhou|彭城]] and tied to river transport; pressure there threatens the Chu rear and forces Xiang Yu to leave the central front.
- [[LiuBang|刘邦]] uses Xiang Yu's eastward move to attack and retake [[Chenggao|成皋]], while Xiang Yu later defeats Peng Yue and returns west to retake Xingyang and Chenggao.
- The host contrasts tactical and strategic outcomes: Xiang Yu repeatedly wins visible local battles, but those victories require exhausting marches between Chenggao, Yuan, Xiapi, and Xingyang.
- [[ZhouKe|周苛]] and [[CongGong|枞公]] are captured after the fall of the Xingyang/Chenggao line; Xiang Yu offers Zhou Ke rank and a large fief, but Zhou Ke refuses and is boiled, while Cong Gong is also killed.
- Liu Bang judges Chenggao impossible to hold against Xiang Yu's direct pressure and flees north with [[XiahouYing|夏侯婴]], crossing the Yellow River toward Han Xin and Zhang Er at Xiao Xiuwu.
- Liu Bang stays quiet on arrival, then enters the Zhao army camp before dawn by claiming to be a Han king's envoy.
- Han Xin and Zhang Er have not yet risen; Liu Bang enters their sleeping quarters, takes their seals and military tokens, and uses Han Xin's seal to summon the troops and issue new orders.
- Han Xin and Zhang Er only realize afterward that Liu Bang himself has taken command, leaving them shocked but unable to resist the completed authority transfer.
- Liu Bang sends Zhang Er to patrol and remain in Zhao, while making Han Xin chancellor and ordering him to gather unlevied Zhao manpower for the campaign against [[QiState|齐国]].
- Han generals who escaped Chenggao later return to Liu Bang, giving him a reassembled central force after the flight.
- The source ends by turning toward the Qi field: in seventh month, a broom star appears near Dajiao, [[GongAo|共敖]] dies and [[GongWei|共尉]] succeeds in Linjiang, and a Han envoy goes to [[Linzi|临淄]] to persuade Qi to submit as an eastern vassal.

## Key Quotes
> "位高权重、非常有话语权" - the opening problem frame for dangerous subordinates.

> "闯到卧室里边，把他们的印信兵符全都拿走了" - the episode's summary of Liu Bang's seizure of Han Xin and Zhang Er's command symbols.

> "战略上的成功足够抵消战术上的失利" - the host's summary of Liu Bang's multi-front position against Xiang Yu.
