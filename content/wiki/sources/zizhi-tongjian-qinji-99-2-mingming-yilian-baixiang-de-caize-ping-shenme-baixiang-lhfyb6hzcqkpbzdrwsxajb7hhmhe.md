# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》99-2｜明明一脸“败相”的蔡泽 凭什么拜相

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the [[CaiZe|蔡泽]] and [[FanJu|范雎]] confrontation opened in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-99-1-lishishang-diyi-chounan-nixi-zuo-qinxiang-lvmdrgbhvpbiyngm-nviunour0sk|Qinji 99-1]]. Cai Ze turns [[PowerExitTrap|功成身退]] from a warning into an office-transfer argument: Fan Ju's revenge and merit are complete, while [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] is old and Fan Ju's associates have become liabilities.

The episode then pivots to [[Xunzi|荀子]] under [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]], using the appointment as [[LanlingWarringStates|兰陵 / Lanling]] magistrate to show cultural-political governance in newly occupied Lu territory. Xunzi's Lanling success and later dismissal by slander extend the wiki's branches on [[ConfucianIdealGovernance|儒家理想治理]], [[OccupationPacificationStrategy|占领区怀柔治理]], and court suspicion of capable officials.

## Key Claims
- Cai Ze argues that officials want both功名 and safe retreat, and ranks preserving both body and reputation above dying with reputation or surviving after disgrace.
- Cai Ze uses [[ShangYang|商鞅]], [[WuQi|吴起]], and [[WenZhongYue|文种]] to press the question of whether Fan Ju's merit and Qin Zhaoxiang Wang's forbearance are strong enough to keep Fan Ju safe.
- The proverb "日中则移，月满则亏" becomes the episode's compressed rule for timing retreat when achievement, revenge, and risk have reached their peak.
- [[FanLi|范蠡]] functions as the positive contrast: after helping [[Goujian|越王勾践]], he leaves before completed merit becomes danger.
- Fan Ju accepts the warning, treats Cai Ze as an honored guest, recommends him to Qin Zhaoxiang Wang, and then resigns by claiming illness.
- Qin Zhaoxiang Wang first appoints Cai Ze as keqing and then as chancellor, but the episode stresses that Cai Ze's chancellorship lasts only a few months.
- Cai Ze's earlier career is marked by repeated failed lobbying, expulsion from Zhao, travel through Han and Wey, and robbery on the road, making the Qin opening a late opportunistic reversal.
- The host reads Fan Ju's recommendation of Cai Ze partly as a way to hand off danger after [[ZhengAnping|郑安平]] and [[WangJiQin|王稽 / 王继]] have made Fan Ju politically exposed.
- The episode treats Fan Ju's death as likely natural rather than judicially punished, while keeping that inference tied to the host's reading of sparse records.
- Chunshenjun appoints Xunzi as Lanling magistrate after Chu takes territory from [[LuState|鲁国]], using a major scholar to govern and soothe a culturally weighty place.
- Xunzi's earlier Qin-facing advice is summarized as a call for virtue, people protection, and kingly rule rather than conquest-first霸道.
- The source raises, but does not resolve, the question of how Xunzi's Chu service at Lanling relates to the [[LinwuJun|临武君]] military debate before [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]].
- Xunzi's Lanling governance is presented as effective and prosperous, but his border position near [[QiState|齐国]] and his earlier Jixia ties make him vulnerable to slander.

## Key Quotes
> "日中则移，月满则亏" - Cai Ze's timing rule for political retreat.

> "欲而不知止，失其所以欲；有而不知止，失其所以有" - Fan Ju's realization after Cai Ze's warning.

> "文化打不过军事，但国亡不等于文化消失" - the host's frame for why Lu cultural identity still matters after conquest.
