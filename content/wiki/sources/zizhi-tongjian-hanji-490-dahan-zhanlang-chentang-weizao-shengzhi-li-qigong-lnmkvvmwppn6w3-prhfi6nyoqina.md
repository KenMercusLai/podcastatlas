# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》490丨大汉战狼陈汤，伪造圣旨立奇功！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 36 BCE operational layer of [[ChenTangWesternHan|陈汤]] and [[GanYanshouWesternHan|甘延寿]]'s campaign against [[ZhizhiChanyu|郅支单于]], immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-491-zhongguo-waijiao-zui-qiangying-de-shike-jing-shi-zai-xihan-lpgajp_vky9dx7jvnoahaxdfjflk|Hanji 491]]'s victory-publication aftermath. It traces Chen Tang from poverty, begging, study, boldness, and money hunger into the forged-edict mobilization of more than forty thousand troops drawn from tuntian soldiers and submitted Western Regions forces.

The durable synthesis is that the Zhizhi campaign was not only a later reward dispute but a live operational gamble. Chen Tang combines military judgment, diplomatic management, local intelligence, discipline inside [[Kangju|康居]], and hard coercive shock; the same episode also preserves the moral ambiguity that later sources develop, because childhood deprivation and desire for elevation help explain his drive without cleansing greed or procedural violation.

## Key Claims

- Chen Tang's early poverty, begging, rough survival conduct, study, eloquence, and risk appetite are used to explain both his upward mobility and his later greed vulnerability.
- In 36 BCE Chen Tang and Gan Yanshou falsely transmit imperial command, assemble more than forty thousand troops, submit a self-accusing memorial, and depart before the center can approve or stop the campaign.
- The army advances in six routes through Western Regions space, including routes through Congling, Dayuan, Wensu, Wusun, and toward Kangju territory.
- When Kangju deputy king Baotian raids Wusun and seizes Han rear supplies, Chen Tang sends Western Regions troops to defeat him, return captives to Wusun's greater kunmi, and use animals as field provisions.
- Inside Kangju, Chen Tang enforces discipline against plunder, meets Kangju noble Tumo, and gains guidance from Kaimou, whose hatred of Zhizhi Chanyu gives the Han army intelligence on the royal court.
- Before the assault, Gan Yanshou and Chen Tang use the language of welcoming a submitted chanyu to Chang'an while probing Zhizhi Chanyu's position and stressing Han supply exhaustion.
- The final battle combines crossbow intimidation, shield-and-crossbow assault, fire attack, failed Xiongnu breakout, resistance by Kangju cavalry, and the storming of the palace area where Zhizhi Chanyu is killed.
- The host frames the victory as a shock to surrounding groups and a precondition for Huhanye Chanyu's later loyal-vassal language, while quoting the "犯强汉者，虽远必诛" posture.

## Key Quotes

> "犯强汉者，虽远必诛" - the episode's closing expression of the campaign's deterrent meaning.

> "怀赤子之心做小人之事" - the quoted evaluation used to hold Chen Tang's vitality and moral ambiguity together.
