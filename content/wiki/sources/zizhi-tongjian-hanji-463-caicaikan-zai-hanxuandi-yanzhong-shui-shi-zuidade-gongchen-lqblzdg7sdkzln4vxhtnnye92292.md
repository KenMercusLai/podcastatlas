# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》463｜猜猜看，在汉宣帝眼中谁是最大功臣？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Ganlu 3 / 51 BCE under [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]]. It links [[HuhanyeChanyuWesternHan|呼韩邪单于]]'s request to remain south of the desert, Han military escort and grain support, wider Western Regions obedience, and Xuan's decision to honor the Qilin Pavilion meritorious ministers into one late-Xuan high-point scene.

The durable synthesis is that Xuan-era achievement is presented as both frontier settlement and institutional memory. Huhanye's protected submission turns Han aid into regional leverage; the Qilin Pavilion list turns frontier and administrative consolidation into a visible honor order; classics standardization gives Confucian learning a court-authorized form; and the episode's late palace material introduces [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] and [[HanChengdi|刘骜 / 后来的汉成帝]] as the succession seed of a much darker late-Western-Han branch.

## Key Claims

- In 51 BCE, Huhanye asks to stay south of the desert and to retreat into border fortifications in an emergency, and Xuan approves the request.
- Han sends Dong Zhong, Han Chang, and more than sixteen thousand cavalry to escort and protect Huhanye, leaves forces near him, and supports him against Xiongnu groups that will not submit.
- Han ships thirty-four thousand hu of grain and dried provisions to Huhanye's side; after his court visit, Western Regions polities from Wusun westward and those near Xiongnu become more obedient to Han command.
- Xuan orders portraits of eleven meritorious ministers in the Qilin Pavilion, identifying their offices, noble titles, and names; Huo Guang is marked by office and surname rather than full personal name.
- The episode treats the Qilin Pavilion honor roll as proof that Xuan's "middle restoration" is not one-person rule but the outcome of capable ministers across frontier, administrative, and succession work.
- Xuan convenes scholars on disputed Five Classics interpretations, has [[XiaoWangzhiWesternHan|萧望之]] and others report, decides personally, and authorizes standard interpretive lines with doctoral offices.
- [[XieyouPrincessWesternHan|解忧公主]] is allowed to return from [[Wusun|乌孙]] to Chang'an in old age, while [[FengLiaoWesternHan|冯嫽]] returns in her seventies to stabilize the young Wusun ruler.
- The death of Sima Liangdi creates the immediate palace context for Wang Zhengjun entering the crown prince's household; Wang Zhengjun gives birth to Liu Ao, the later [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]].
- [[WangHeWesternHan|王贺]] is used as a Wang-family prehistory: his leniency as embroidered-uniform censor cost him office but is framed by the host as a hidden condition for later family survival and rise.
