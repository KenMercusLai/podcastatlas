# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》800丨杨震之死：一场奸臣对清官的剿杀

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills 124 CE immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-801-han-andi-weishenme-henxin-fei-taizi-lgnhbsqnp6vdigsmqfkxiqmx2vdy|Hanji 801]]. It opens with source-scoped advice about altruism and inner turmoil, then records [[BanYongLateHan|班勇]] using diplomacy, pressure, and allied troops to restore Han leverage around Loulan, Qiuci, and Cheshi Qian.

The main source contribution is [[YangZhenLateHan|杨震]]'s death under [[EmperorAnOfHan|汉安帝]]. [[FanFengEunuchLateHan|樊丰]], [[ZhouGuangLateHan|周广]], and [[XieYunLateHan|谢恽]] forge imperial instructions to use state grain, funds, and labor for private mansions; Yang Zhen attacks the abuse through disaster-remonstrance and evidence gathering, but the court turns the case against him. The durable synthesis is that Emperor An-era feedback is captured enough that public correction, omen-coded warning, and documentary evidence can be recoded as resentment and disloyalty, leaving moral reputation unable to protect Yang Zhen until Shun's later posthumous repair.

## Key Claims

- The opening "altruism heals inner turmoil" frame is source-scoped listener advice and does not drive the historical claim set.
- In early 124 CE, Ban Yong reaches Loulan, wins Shanshan's renewed submission, pressures Qiuci's king Bai Ying into joining Han alignment with Gumo and Wensu, drives away a Xiongnu Yili king near Cheshi Qian, and returns to Liuzhong for garrison farming.
- During Emperor An's eastern tour, Fan Feng, Zhou Guang, Xie Yun, and other favorites become bolder because Yang Zhen's repeated advice has not moved the emperor.
- Fan Feng's group forges imperial instructions to requisition Da sinong resources and Da jiang labor for private houses, gardens, pools, towers, and pavilions.
- Yang Zhen uses earthquake, lack of winter snow, lack of spring rain, eunuch power, and luxury construction as an [[OmenCodedRemonstrance|omen-coded remonstrance]] against favored insiders.
- [[ZhaoTengLateHan|赵腾]]'s blunt memorial is treated as deception and grave disloyalty; Yang Zhen pleads for mitigation, but An executes Zhao Teng.
- After Yang Zhen obtains evidence of the forged orders, Fan Feng's group uses a taishi star report to slander him as resentful after Zhao Teng's death and as a former Deng-family dependent.
- On 124-03-29, after returning to Luoyang and resting at Taixue, Emperor An strips Yang Zhen of the taiwei seal; Fan Feng's side then has [[GengBaoGrandGeneralLateHan|耿宝]] accuse him of resentful noncompliance.
- Yang Zhen is sent back to his home commandery, stops at Xiyang Pavilion, leaves instructions for a plain burial, and kills himself by poison.
- Hongnong administrator Yi Liang humiliates Yang Zhen's funeral procession under Fan Feng's influence, while [[LaiLiLateHan|来历]] later criticizes Geng Bao for siding with corrupt actors against loyal men.
