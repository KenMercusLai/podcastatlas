# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》474丨谷吉慷慨赴死，我为何认为他愚蠢？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the earlier envoy-killing layer behind [[ZhizhiChanyu|郅支单于]]'s later conflict with Han. After [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] returns Zhizhi Chanyu's hostage son, [[GongYuWesternHan|贡禹]] and [[KuangHengWesternHan|匡衡]] advise that the escort stop at the frontier, but [[GuJiWesternHan|谷吉]] insists on going to the chanyu's court and is killed with his party.

The durable synthesis is that courage without judgment can become wasteful exposure. The episode uses Confucius, Yan Hui, and Zilu to distinguish bravery from rashness, then extends the same risk logic to [[Kangju|康居]] inviting Zhizhi Chanyu against [[Wusun|乌孙]] and to [[ZhouYouwang|周幽王]]'s fall after outside force enters the Western Zhou crisis.

## Key Claims

- Han Yuandi releases Zhizhi Chanyu's hostage son even though Zhizhi has already shown hostility toward Han envoys.
- Gong Yu and Kuang Heng advise limiting the escort mission to the frontier because Zhizhi has not sincerely submitted.
- Gu Ji insists on escorting the hostage to the chanyu's court and treats his possible death as a national-security trade.
- The host rejects that self-sacrifice frame, arguing that foreseeable death from poor judgment is not the same as useful courage.
- Confucius's answer to Zilu is used to define desirable courage as cautious, planning-aware, and able to complete the task.
- Kangju's invitation to Zhizhi Chanyu is framed as a dangerous-ally mistake: a strong outsider can solve one enemy problem while creating a larger internal threat.
- The Western Zhou comparison and [[QinXiangGong|秦襄公]]'s rise show that the same disorder can destroy one actor and become another actor's opportunity.

## Key Quotes

> "有勇无谋是莽夫" - the episode's main judgment on Gu Ji's mission choice.

> "事还没成，人先折了" - the host's summary of reckless courage that spends the actor before achieving the goal.
