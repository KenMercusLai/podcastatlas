# The Revisionist History Holiday Sampler

## Summary

This [[RevisionistHistory]] sampler previews [[BadWomenTheRipperRetold|Bad Women: The Ripper Retold]], in which [[HallieRubenhold]] reconstructs [[ElizabethStride|Elizabeth Stride's]] life before Whitechapel. Stride's movement from a Swedish farming family into domestic service, pregnancy, police registration, invasive syphilis inspection, confinement, stillbirth, stigma, and sex work makes [[GenderedDiseaseControl]] a mechanism of [[VictorianWomenPrecarity]] rather than a neutral medical response.

A shorter follow-up revisits COVID-detection dogs after their move into concert-tour operations. The dogs screened backstage crews rapidly and became popular tour members, but the supplied summary does not provide diagnostic-performance data, confirmatory protocols, or comparative outcomes sufficient to establish [[CanineDiseaseDetectionScreening]] as a validated substitute for conventional testing.

## Key Claims

- Rubenhold's victim-centered history challenges the reduction of the women associated with Jack the Ripper to prostitution labels or their manner of death.
- Stride grew up in a comparatively prosperous rural Swedish household before entering domestic service in Gothenburg, where young women faced dependence on employers and gendered sexual vulnerability.
- After an unmarried pregnancy, authorities registered Stride as a “public woman,” restricted her movement, and subjected her to repeated genital inspections that targeted women while exempting male carriers from comparable control.
- A syphilis diagnosis led to coercive confinement and painful treatment; Stride delivered a stillborn daughter while held in the Cure House.
- Registration, hospitalization, and stigma narrowed access to respectable employment, supporting the source's inference that the control system could help produce the sex work it claimed only to identify.
- COVID-detection dogs later screened concert crews for tours associated with Eric Church, the Black Keys, and Metallica, reportedly taking about a second to sniff each worker's empty hand.
- The tour account demonstrates operational deployment and user acceptance, not diagnostic accuracy, clinical validity, or the dogs' effect on transmission.
