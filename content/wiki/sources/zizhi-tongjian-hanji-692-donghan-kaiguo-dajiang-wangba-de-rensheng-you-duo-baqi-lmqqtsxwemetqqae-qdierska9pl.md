## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills a Jianwu 5 / 29 CE layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-693-panchu-liuxiu-de-pengchong-ruhe-yin-beipan-er-wang-lqler4fu9wqztsrgzsxhs1519nm|Hanji 693]] by centering [[WangBaEasternHan|王霸]] as a Guangwu commander who treats battle timing as morale and exhaustion management. It follows his refusal to rescue [[MaWuEasternHan|马武]] at the enemy's high-morale moment, the delayed rear-cavalry strike against [[SuMaoEasternHan|苏茂]] and [[ZhouJianEasternHan|周建]], and a second fortified refusal that lets the enemy's short provisions and internal fracture decide the field. The closing biography extends Wang Ba from battlefield patience into long northern-frontier work against [[LuFangEasternHan|卢芳]], [[Xiongnu|匈奴]], and [[Wuhuan|乌桓]], including Feihu-route defenses, heqin advice, and Wenshui transport.

## Key Claims
- The episode frames high military skill as not merely winning a battle, but first avoiding a bad battle by managing morale, position, timing, and the enemy's expectations.
- Wang Ba refuses Ma Wu's request for immediate rescue because Su Mao and Zhou Jian are fresh and strong while Han troops are fearful; an immediate sortie could lose both forces.
- The visible absence of rescue changes the psychology of the field: Su Mao and Zhou Jian overpress Ma Wu, while Ma Wu's troops fight for survival instead of waiting passively for aid.
- Wang Ba converts accumulated enemy fatigue into action only after the fighting has dragged on, opening a rear gate and sending elite cavalry into the enemy's back.
- When Su Mao and Zhou Jian return to challenge him, Wang Ba keeps the camp fortified, stays visibly calm despite arrows entering the feast area, and reads the enemy's poor provisions as a reason to refuse their desired tempo.
- The enemy withdrawal is followed by Zhou Song's gate closure at Chuihui, Zhou Jian's death on retreat, Su Mao's flight to Dong Xian, and the Liang king's flight; these remain source-scoped campaign details.
- Wang Ba's later biography links battlefield patience to frontier governance: service in Shanggu, Feihu-route defenses, long fighting against Xiongnu and Wuhuan, repeated heqin advice, and Wenshui transport recommendations.

## Key Quotes
> "兵法不是战法，而是不战之法；不是战胜之法，而是不败之法" - opening military principle.

> "能治己之气，亦能治敌之气" - host's frame for Wang Ba's morale-tempo command.

> "开营后，出精骑袭其背" - Wang Ba's delayed switch from closed camp to rear cavalry strike.
