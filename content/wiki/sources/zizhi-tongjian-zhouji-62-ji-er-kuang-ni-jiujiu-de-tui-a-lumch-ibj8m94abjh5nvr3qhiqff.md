# 《资治通鉴·周纪》62丨稷儿，诓你舅舅的“腿”啊

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s 299 BCE frame by narrating how [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] lures [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] to [[Wuguan|武关]], seizes him, and detains him in [[Xianyang|咸阳]] after Chu refuses to cede [[WuJunWarringStates|巫郡]] and [[Qianzhong|黔中]]. The episode argues that Qin's deception is real, but Chu Huaiwang is vulnerable because years of alliance reversal, ignored remonstrance, and failed personnel judgment have left [[ChuState|楚国]] isolated. It then follows Chu and [[QiState|齐国]] as they manage the succession problem around [[MiHeng|芈横 / 楚顷襄王]], before setting up [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]] entering Qin as chancellor.

## Key Claims
- In 299 BCE, [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] invites [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] to meet at [[Wuguan|武关]], framing the meeting through old Qin-Chu kinship, the earlier [[MiHeng|芈横]] hostage incident, and the need to renew an agreement.
- [[ZhaoJu|昭雎]] warns Chu Huaiwang not to go and calls Qin untrustworthy, but [[MiLan|芈兰]] supports attending because of Qin-Chu marriage ties and the family relationship through [[XuanTaihou|芈八子 / 宣太后]].
- Qin sets a trap at Wuguan: a Qin general impersonates the king, troops close the pass, and Chu Huaiwang is captured and taken to [[Xianyang|咸阳]].
- In Xianyang, Qin demands [[WuJunWarringStates|巫郡]] and [[Qianzhong|黔中]]. Chu Huaiwang refuses, and the episode treats these regions as strategic enough that losing them could expose Chu's capital.
- The episode's main judgment is causal rather than only moral: Qin acts faithlessly, but Chu Huaiwang has spent down diplomatic trust by repeatedly betraying or abandoning allies, so no strong outside support protects him.
- Chu's court does not mount a rescue effort. Because Chu Huaiwang is detained in Qin and [[MiHeng|芈横]] is in Qi, Chu elites consider making another prince king; Zhao Ju argues against bypassing both king and crown prince.
- Chu asks Qi to return Mi Heng by falsely reporting that Chu Huaiwang has died. [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]] debates whether to detain him for territory north of the Huai River, but finally lets him return.
- Mi Heng returns to Chu and succeeds as Chu Qingxiang Wang, making the same figure who once broke a Qin hostage arrangement into the continuation of Chu royal legitimacy.
- The episode links Chu Huaiwang's abandonment to [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|君臣反馈失灵]]: figures like [[QuYuan|屈原]] have been demoted or exiled, so the ruler lacks loyal correction and eventually lacks loyal rescue.
- The closing setup says Qin sends a royal brother as hostage to Qi in order to bring [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]] to Xianyang as Qin chancellor.

## Key Quotes
> "秦国是虎狼之国，不可信任" - Zhao Ju's warning as summarized in the episode.

> "天作孽犹可违，自作孽不可活" - the quoted judgment applied to Chu Huaiwang.
