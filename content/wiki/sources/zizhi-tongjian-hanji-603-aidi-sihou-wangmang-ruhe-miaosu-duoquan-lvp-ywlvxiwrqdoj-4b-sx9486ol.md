# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》603丨哀帝死后，王莽如何秒速夺权？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate Yuanshou 2 / 1 BCE succession shock between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-602-tanxun-guoxue-zhihui-pojie-2024nian-fantaisui-zhi-mi-lvrwnpanicgjpf43cbmveigtxqj|Hanji 602]]'s late-[[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] court scene and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-604-xihan-wangmang-duoquan-hou-ruhe-qingxi-quanli-geju-ltwscyyiobc1ocvgti3fd-deajru|Hanji 604]]'s Da Sima recommendation contest. It follows Aidi's death at Weiyang Palace, [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]'s immediate seizure of the imperial seal and command channels, [[DongXian|董贤]]'s inability to manage the funeral and succession crisis, and [[WangMang|王莽]]'s rapid removal of Dong Xian from palace access and office.

The durable synthesis is that Wang Mang's return begins before the formal Da Sima vote. Wang Zhengjun's senior authority gives him control over troop tallies, memorial channels, palace gates, and guard forces; Wang Mang then uses an impeachment over Dong Xian's failure to attend Aidi's illness to bar him from the inner palace, remove his seal and ribbon, and make Dong Xian's death the entry point for his own restoration to central power.

## Key Claims

- Han Aidi dies suddenly in the sixth month of Yuanshou 2 / 1 BCE at age twenty-five, with no son and a court already reorganized around Dong Xian, Kong Guang, and Peng Xuan.
- Wang Zhengjun immediately enters Weiyang Palace and takes the imperial seal and ribbon, giving her control over the symbolic center of succession.
- Dong Xian is nominally Da Sima and Wei General, but the episode portrays him as unable to answer basic questions about funeral and succession procedure.
- Wang Zhengjun recalls Wang Mang because he had managed a prior imperial funeral and could plausibly assist with the emergency.
- The decisive transfer is administrative as well as ceremonial: troop-dispatch tallies, memorial handling, central palace gates, and guards are placed under Wang Mang.
- Wang Mang first strips Dong Xian of real access by having him impeached for not attending Aidi's illness and barring him from palace inner doors.
- Dong Xian is then removed from office, sent home, dies with his wife the same day, and is later subject to coffin inspection because Wang Mang fears feigned death.

## Key Quotes

> "有治国之志，却无治国之才" - the host's summary judgment on Han Aidi.

> "德不配位，必有灾殃" - the episode's moral frame for Dong Xian's collapse.

> "真正的目的是为了夺权" - the host's reading of Wang Mang's motive in removing Dong Xian.
