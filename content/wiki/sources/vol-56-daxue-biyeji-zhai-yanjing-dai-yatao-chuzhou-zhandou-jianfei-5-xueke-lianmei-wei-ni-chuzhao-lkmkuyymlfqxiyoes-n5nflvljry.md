## Key Claims
- Refractive surgery is elective optical correction. The source places candidacy after adulthood, relative prescription stability, examination of ocular conditions, personal need, and occupational or activity context rather than after degree alone.
- Full- and half-femtosecond laser procedures differ in flap, incision, dry-eye, trauma, corneal, and prescription considerations; the episode does not treat the newer-sounding option as automatically suitable for everyone.
- Marked inter-eye prescription difference can create visual-fusion strain, while astigmatism can be corrected through several optical or surgical routes depending on severity and the eye examination.
- [[OrthodonticTreatmentContinuity]] joins appliance choice to diagnosis, periodontal condition, bite, possible extraction or jaw surgery, repeated review over years, retention, patient cooperation, and likely future city.
- Medical-aesthetic requests should begin with actual anatomy, need, decision maturity, and qualified assessment. Young age or a trending look does not by itself justify tightening devices, fillers, injections, breast surgery, eyelid surgery, or facial reshaping.
- [[MedicalAestheticProviderVerification]] matters because non-medical or poorly regulated settings can expose patients to unsuitable products, inflated prices, allergy, injection complications, and weak follow-up.
- [[InflammatorySkinConditionTriage]] scales acne care from gentle routine and topical treatment through clinician-selected oral or retinoid treatment, while discouraging picking and keeping peels, acids, and photosensitivity inside tolerability and supervision boundaries.
- [[SkinBarrierRoutine]] remains simple: cleansing, moisturization, and ultraviolet protection precede procedure shopping or marketing-led routines.
- [[RapidWeightLossSafetyBoundary]] rejects crash dieting, disguised restriction, and abrupt overexercise; low energy availability can affect metabolism, recovery, and menstrual function, while rapid loss is difficult to maintain.
- A sustainable calorie deficit can come from slightly smaller portions, slower attentive eating, routine movement, and balanced food quality; a fasting window or low calorie total does not make a nutritionally poor pattern healthy.
