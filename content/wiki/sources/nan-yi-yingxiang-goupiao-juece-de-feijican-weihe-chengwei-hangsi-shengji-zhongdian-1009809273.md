# 难以影响购票决策的飞机餐，为何成为航司升级重点？

## Summary
This [[ShengdongZaokafei|声动早咖啡]] episode combines short business updates on [[Meituan]], [[MixueBingcheng|蜜雪冰城]], [[LPDDR6]], and [[Insta360|影石]] with a main explainer on why airlines are upgrading in-flight meals even though meals rarely decide ticket purchase. It frames airline meals as a constrained service product: ground catering, cold-chain logistics, reheating, cabin taste loss, and flight schedules limit quality, but meals can still shape repeat purchase, recommendation, brand memory, cost control, and paid-service design. The source is useful mainly for [[AirlineServiceDifferentiation]], [[AirlineMealIndustrialization]], and [[AirlineMealMonetization]] rather than for independent verification of current company results.

## Key Claims
- [[Meituan]] returned to quarterly profitability in the source's account after local-core commerce recovered from delivery subsidies and regulators pushed the market away from extreme subsidy competition.
- [[MixueBingcheng|蜜雪冰城]] reported modest revenue growth but profit pressure, then moved toward fresh-fruit, fresh-milk, and coffee-machine upgrades while [[Guming|古茗]] and [[Chagee|霸王茶姬]] were framed as stronger performers.
- The transcript says a domestic memory company began [[LPDDR6]] mass production for a forthcoming Xiaomi foldable phone, while [[SKHynix|SK Hynix]] was also preparing LPDDR6 supply as AI demand raised attention to low-power memory.
- [[Insta360|影石]] grew revenue but faced profit pressure from memory-chip costs, R&D, marketing, and broader competition with [[DJI]] across panoramic, drone, and gimbal-style creator-camera categories.
- [[AirlineMealIndustrialization]] explains why airplane food often disappoints: meals must survive batch production, cold-chain transport, timetable discipline, reheating, and altered cabin taste conditions.
- Airline meals are weak as first-purchase drivers: the source cites [[McKinsey]]'s 2025 traveler survey saying price and baggage allowance matter more than onboard food and drinks.
- Meals still matter inside [[AirlineServiceDifferentiation]] because they can influence repeat purchase, recommendation, and concrete brand memory, especially on long-haul flights.
- [[AirlineMealMonetization]] turns some catering from pure cost into paid optional service, member benefit, or preference-data layer while preserving the fairness risk around economy-cabin baseline experience.

## Key Quotes
> "购买决策影响不大" - the source's puzzle around why meals still receive airline investment.

> "复购、推荐和长期品牌关系" - the episode's answer for why meal experience can matter after purchase.

> "从成本项转化为收入项" - the source's paid-meal monetization frame.
