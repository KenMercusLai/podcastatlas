# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》449｜一出荒诞闹剧！匈奴五大单于夺权火拼

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Wufeng 1 / 57 BCE under [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]], before the later [[HuhanyeChanyuWesternHan|呼韩邪单于]] submission and Chang'an-audience sequence. It explains how Xiongnu distrust and opportunistic self-enthronement produce a "five chanyus" crisis, while the Han court debates whether to exploit the disorder by force or answer through mourning, aid, and moral prestige.

The durable synthesis is that Xiongnu fragmentation creates a genuine military opportunity for Han, but [[XiaoWangzhiWesternHan|萧望之]] argues that a hegemonic strike would squander the political value of restraint. Xuan accepts Xiao's recommendation to comfort and aid the weakened Xiongnu rather than attack, making this episode an early layer for both Huhanye's later dependence and [[GuestRiteSubmissionDiplomacy|guest-rite submission diplomacy]].

## Key Claims

- In 57 BCE, Xuan performs the crown-prince capping rite for [[HanYuandi|刘奭 / later 汉元帝]], and the host frames the father-son difference as "霸道" Xuan versus "王道" Liu Shi.
- Tuqi Chanyu stations forces in the northeast to guard against Huhanye, showing that Xiongnu division is already militarized before the five-chanyu cascade.
- False accusation against the Right Worthy King causes Tuqi Chanyu to execute him and his son, then to execute Weilidanghu after discovering the accusation was false.
- Hujie Wang flees and proclaims himself Hujie Chanyu; You'aojian Wang then proclaims himself Cheli Chanyu; Wuji Duwei follows as Wuji Chanyu, producing five simultaneous chanyus.
- Tuqi defeats Cheli and Wuji; Cheli, Wuji, and Hujie then combine forces and reduce their claims by backing Cheli because of stronger lineage legitimacy.
- Han officials mostly urge an attack to destroy Xiongnu during internal chaos, but Xiao Wangzhi invokes ritual morality, earlier heqin overtures, and long-term prestige to oppose opportunistic war.
- Xiao recommends sending envoys to mourn and assist the weakened Xiongnu so that surrounding peoples see Han benevolence and a restored Xiongnu ruler may submit out of gratitude.
- Xuan accepts Xiao's recommendation, which the host reads as evidence that Xiao's status in court decision-making has risen above ordinary ministers.

## Key Quotes

> "五单于并立时代" - the episode's label for the Xiongnu civil-war phase.

> "趁人之危，幸灾乐祸" - Xiao Wangzhi's moral framing against attacking Xiongnu during disorder.
