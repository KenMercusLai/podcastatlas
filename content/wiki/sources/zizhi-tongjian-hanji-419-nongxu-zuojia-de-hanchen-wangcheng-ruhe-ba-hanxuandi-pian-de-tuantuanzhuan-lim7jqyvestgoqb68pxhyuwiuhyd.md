# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》419｜弄虚作假的汉臣王成，如何把汉宣帝骗得团团转？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the Yuan康 1 / 65 BCE layer between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-418-huoguang-sihou-weihe-huojia-zhuding-bei-miezu-lq-zlaum6yoxja6npjldfhscnsxu|Hanji 418]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-420-cong-jiexiaqiu-dao-zhongxingzhizhu-hanxuandi-nixi-zhilu-jingli-le-shenme-lpxbuonbdepadujbrcgkla5bmqgp|Hanji 420]]. It pairs [[WangChengWesternHan|王成]]'s false local-performance report under [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] with the succession politics around [[HanYuandi|刘奭]], [[HuoXianWesternHan|霍显]], [[HuoChengjunWesternHan|霍成君]], [[XuGuanghanWesternHan|徐广汉]], [[XuShunWesternHan|徐顺]], and [[ShuGuangShuShouWesternHan|疏广与疏受]].

The durable synthesis is that information and kinship both need institutional filtering. Wang Cheng's inflated report shows how written assessment systems can reward fabricated achievement once a ruler publicly endorses a case too early, while Shu Guang's answer to Xu Guanghan's proposal turns a private family request into the public principle that crown-prince education belongs to the realm.

## Key Claims

- Qin-Han official assessment through 考课 and 上计 could create incentives for [[AdministrativePerformanceFraud|政绩述职造假]] when promotion, reward, and reputation depended on reported outcomes.
- [[WangChengWesternHan|王成]] gained an imperial commendation and Guannei Hou rank by reporting exaggerated Jiaodong achievements, but later review found that his claimed return of more than 80,000 displaced people was fabricated.
- The episode reads the court's silence around Wang Cheng as a feedback problem: once [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] publicly labeled him a good official, informed officials had less incentive to correct the record.
- The same Yuan康 1 layer makes [[HanYuandi|刘奭]]'s crown-prince installation a direct threat to the Huo household, leading [[HuoXianWesternHan|霍显]] to push [[HuoChengjunWesternHan|霍成君]] toward poisoning attempts.
- [[XuGuanghanWesternHan|徐广汉]]'s proposal to have [[XuShunWesternHan|徐顺]] oversee the crown prince's household is framed as outer-relative positioning around the next generation.
- [[ShuGuangShuShouWesternHan|疏广]] successfully answers the proposal by saying the crown prince is a state heir whose teachers and associates should be realm-level worthy people rather than maternal kin.
- The host turns the Shu Guang scene into a workplace lesson about using a credible third party to express a necessary refusal when direct rejection would damage relationship or face.
