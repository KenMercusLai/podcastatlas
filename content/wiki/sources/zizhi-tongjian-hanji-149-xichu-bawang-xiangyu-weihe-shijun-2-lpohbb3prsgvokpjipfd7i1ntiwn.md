# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》149｜西楚霸王项羽为何弑君（2）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-149-xichu-bawang-xiangyu-weihe-shijun-1-lsqxs2hzu0ngnrpmzsklkqhtyuvw|Hanji 149 part 1]] by shifting from [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]]'s killing to the rapid territorial consequences of [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s eastward move. Liu Bang absorbs [[ShenYang|申阳]]'s Henan territory, installs [[HanWangXin|韩王信]] after [[ZhengChangHanKing|郑昌]] surrenders, moves his capital to [[LiyangQinHan|栎阳]], and pushes into Longxi, Beidi, Wei, and Henei. The counterpoint is [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s punitive Qi campaign: after [[TianRong|田荣]] is killed, Xiang Yu continues killing, burning, and plundering, turning possible submission into renewed resistance.

## Key Claims
- After killing [[HanCheng|韩成]], Xiang Yu makes [[ZhengChangHanKing|郑昌]] king of Han, but the choice exposes his reliance on old Xiang-line loyalists rather than a durable Han restoration settlement.
- [[LiuBang|刘邦]] receives [[ShenYang|申阳]]'s surrender, converts the former Henan kingdom into [[HenanCommanderyChuHan|河南郡]], and uses commandery administration to absorb settlement lords.
- [[HanWangXin|韩王信]] is distinct from the general [[HanXin|韩信]]; Liu Bang uses him as an old Han royal descendant to reclaim Han legitimacy and administer Yingchuan.
- Zheng Chang's surrender lets Liu Bang replace Xiang Yu's Han king with Han Wang Xin, pulling old Han territory further into the Liu Bang camp.
- Liu Bang moves his capital to [[LiyangQinHan|栎阳]], takes [[LongxiCommandery|陇西郡]], then takes [[BeidiCommandery|北地郡]] after defeating [[ZhangPingQinGeneral|章平]].
- The episode frames the Guanzhong rear as Liu Bang's base: [[XiaoHe|萧何]] governs, [[HanXin|韩信]] handles military affairs, and frontier repairs guard against Xiongnu pressure.
- [[XiangYu|项羽]] defeats [[TianRong|田荣]] in Qi, but Tian Rong's death does not pacify Qi because Xiang Yu continues killing surrendered troops, destroying cities, and plundering civilians.
- Xiang Yu's Qi violence is treated as a major strategic error: it turns local willingness to submit into forced resistance, extending [[AtrocityBackfireMobilization|暴行反噬式动员]] from a siege or hostage case to occupation policy.
- [[WeiBao|魏豹]] surrenders to Liu Bang quickly because Xiang Yu's partition has reduced and reshaped his territory; grievance over allocation becomes a direct defection channel.
- Liu Bang captures [[SimaAng|司马卬]], turns the occupied space into [[HeneiCommandery|河内郡]], and breaks the Yellow River containment line that Xiang Yu had relied on.
