# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》930丨如何破除贪心和不甘心？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 184 CE late-Han military layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-931-rensheng-jiannan-chu-bianshi-xiuxin-shi-lpzmvxop8lmlwvxhoojzj5ok9qd6|Hanji 931]]. It opens by treating greed and unwillingness-to-settle as distorted versions of wanting better conditions, then translates the lesson into [[ExchangeableCapability|可交换能力]]: background, patrons, and networks matter only when a person has value that others can recognize and use.

The historical branch follows [[GaiXunLateHan|盖勋]] in the Qiang field, [[ZhuJunLateHan|朱儁]] in the Nanyang Yellow Turban campaign, and [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] as a capable subordinate whose opening finally arrives under Zhu Jun. The source's core contribution is a three-part late-Han competence pattern: Gai Xun's reputation can make even a hostile Qiang leader spare him, Zhu Jun wins by changing the rebels' choice structure rather than only tightening force, and Sun Jian converts recognition into battlefield merit when the timing appears.

## Key Claims

- The opening distinguishes productive dissatisfaction from uncontrolled greed and resentment: wanting a better life can become anxiety when one cannot stop demanding more or cannot accept failure.
- The host argues that relying on background, patrons, or relationships is insufficient unless the person first builds [[ExchangeableCapability|exchangeable capability]].
- [[GaiXunLateHan|盖勋]] reaches Ji County and rebukes [[BianZhangLateHan|边章]], who says earlier advice might have saved him but that his current crimes are already too deep to reverse.
- Bian Zhang then withdraws the siege of Ji County, giving Gai Xun an early example of reputation and moral pressure affecting an armed enemy.
- Gai Xun is later defeated while rescuing the protector of the Qiang; wounded and nearly surrounded, he refuses to flee and says that if he dies he should simply be left by the road.
- [[DianWuQiang|滇吾]], a Qiang leader, regards Gai Xun as worthy, stops others from killing him, and tries to give him a horse, making Gai Xun a case for [[MoralReputationPoliticalCapital|道德名望政治资本]] under battlefield loss.
- [[YangYongLateHan|杨雍]] later recommends Gai Xun to hold [[HanyangCommanderyLateHan|汉阳郡]], extending his clean-official reputation before the later anti-eunuch branch.
- After [[ZhangManchengLateHan|张曼成]] is killed, the [[YellowTurbansLateHan|黄巾军]] remnant elects [[ZhaoHongLateHan|赵弘]] and grows again to more than 100,000 people.
- [[ZhangWenLateHan|张温]] defends Zhu Jun against recall by citing [[BaiQi|白起]] and [[LeYi|乐毅]], arguing that a long siege should not be interrupted when a commander already has a plan.
- Zhu Jun kills Zhao Hong, then faces [[HanZhongLateHan|韩忠]], who occupies the city and asks to surrender after Zhu Jun's feint against the southwest and assault on the northeast.
- Zhu Jun first rejects surrender on deterrence grounds, but then recognizes that a trapped enemy with no path to surrender or escape will fight to the death; he opens the encirclement and attacks when Han Zhong's force loses cohesion.
- [[QinJieLateHan|秦颉]] cuts off Han Zhong's head; the remaining Yellow Turbans then elect [[SunXiaLateHan|孙夏]], after which Zhu Jun attacks again and the movement collapses in this theater.
- Zhu Jun specifically calls in Sun Jian, makes him left-army sima, and Sun Jian leads Jiangdong recruits in the campaign; the host reads his success as an example of grasping a short opportunity window.

## Key Quotes

> "人际关系的内核就是利益交换" - the opening relationship-and-capability frame.

> "马上让自己有价值，有本事，有能力" - the practical answer to waiting for patrons or background.

> "围得太紧了" - Zhu Jun's diagnosis that the rebels are being forced into death-ground resistance.

> "时来易失，赴机在速" - the Sun Jian timing lesson quoted by the host.
