# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》385-1｜汉朝第一冤案，“巫蛊之祸”是如何发生的？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] short episode backfills the origin-condition layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-385-2-wugu-zhi-huo-zui-da-de-muhou-heishou-juran-shi-ta-lstnjhb3l6atr-syl4_dhlsgqgte|Hanji 385-2]]'s manufactured-evidence stage. It explains why [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]] could become politically lethal: curse practice was treated as hidden harm against rulers, palace women and officials were already caught in mutual accusation, [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] was old, ill, frightened by dreams, and deeply suspicious, and [[JiangChongWesternHan|江充]] had both a private fear of future revenge and a new mandate to investigate.

The durable synthesis is that Jiang Chong's later frame-up of [[LiuJuCrownPrinceWesternHan|卫太子刘据]] does not begin from neutral law enforcement. This episode presents the precondition as a fused panic system: ritual fear, harem denunciation, mass killing, imperial illness, and Jiang Chong's grudge against Liu Ju and [[EmpressWeiZifuWesternHan|卫子夫]] give a hostile investigator both motive and authority.

## Key Claims

- The episode defines wugu as hidden curse practice using wooden figures, ritual specialists, burial, and incantation, and stresses that such accusations were taken seriously in a court shaped by ghost, spirit, Heaven, and sacrifice beliefs.
- In 91 BCE, fangshi and shamans gather in [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]], while palace women use hidden wooden figures and rites to avoid disaster and then accuse one another.
- Han Wudi responds to the palace wugu accusations by killing hundreds of implicated palace women and court officials.
- The source portrays late Han Wudi as physically ill and psychologically unstable: after repeated wugu incidents, he fears plots against himself and is shaken by a dream of wooden figures attacking him.
- Jiang Chong has prior conflict with Liu Ju and Wei Zifu and fears retaliation if Liu Ju succeeds after Han Wudi's death.
- When Han Wudi asks about wugu, Jiang Chong says someone is using it to harm the emperor, and Wudi gives him full authority to investigate.
- The episode sets up Hanji 385-2 by showing how Jiang Chong's private survival fear and Wudi's suspicion produce the mandate that later becomes a case against Liu Ju.
