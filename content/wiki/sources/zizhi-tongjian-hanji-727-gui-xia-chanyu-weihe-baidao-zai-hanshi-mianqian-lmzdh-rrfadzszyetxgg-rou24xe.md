# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》727丨跪下！单于为何拜倒在汉使面前？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 50-51 CE late [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] frontier layer before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-728-weihe-mingchen-nan-shanzhong-beihou-de-yuanyin-ni-gai-dong-ltjl2bgpyyz1bmwe-4mfcyd9e6cf|Hanji 728]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-729-bangu-tadie-jing-yeshi-ge-da-wenhao-loadjkevhjie2yur8u23ox2-gmce|Hanji 729]]. It follows Liu Xiu adjusting official salaries, planning a low-display tomb, accepting [[SouthernXiongnu|Southern Xiongnu]] submission through edict ritual, hostages, gifts, grain, livestock, protection, seasonal guard routines, and border-commandery restoration.

The durable synthesis is that late Guangwu frontier order combines hierarchy with material protection and restraint. Southern Xiongnu submission is made useful by guards, provisions, patrol roles, and returned border residents, while [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|Northern Xiongnu]] heqin contact and a proposed opportunistic attack are rejected because Southern Xiongnu confidence, defector signaling, disaster-era public welfare, and near-frontier recovery matter more than a distant strike.

## Key Claims

- Liu Xiu adjusts official salaries by favoring lower-ranking officials relative to the old Western Han benchmark, making compensation reform part of late-rule administrative repair.
- Liu Xiu plans his own tomb as small, low, drainage-focused, and without a visible mound or ornamental pools, extending [[FrugalImperialBurialRestraint|frugal imperial burial restraint]] before Fan Hong's later model.
- Han establishes the Southern Xiongnu royal court beyond the Wuyuan frontier and has the chanyu receive the imperial edict by kneeling, after which the chanyu asks not to be made to lose dignity before his ministers again.
- Southern Xiongnu submission becomes a protected client arrangement: hostages rotate at court, Han grants regalia, vehicles, horses, gold, silk, weapons, daily goods, grain, livestock, and guard troops.
- The protected Southern Xiongnu settlement helps reopen Yunzhong, Wuyuan, Shuofang, Beidi, Dingxiang, Yanmen, Shanggu, and Dai commanderies to returning displaced residents, wall repair, and grain support.
- Northern Xiongnu's 51 CE heqin approach is rejected after the crown prince argues that continued contact would unsettle Southern Xiongnu and deter potential defectors.
- Zhang Gong and [[MaWuEasternHan|Ma Wu]] urge attacking Northern Xiongnu while it is weakened by disease, drought, locusts, and losses, but Liu Xiu refuses because domestic welfare, disasters, public anxiety, and near-frontier repair make remote war unwise.

## Key Quotes

> "下了个跪有啥不好，条件马上好起来了。" - host's compressed reading of submission exchanged for protection.

> "舍近谋远者，劳而无功；舍远谋近者，逸而有终。" - Liu Xiu's near-over-far restraint frame as rendered in the episode.

> "不如息民。" - Liu Xiu's final preference for letting the people rest instead of launching a distant campaign.
