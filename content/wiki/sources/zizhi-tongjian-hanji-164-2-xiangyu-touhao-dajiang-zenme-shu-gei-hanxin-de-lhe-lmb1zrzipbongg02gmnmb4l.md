# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》164-2｜项羽头号大将 怎么输给韩信的

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the Qi-front setup from [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-164-1-liubang-geqiang-chaofeng-qi-de-xiangyu-shejian-touxi-lhs6miq2aa3avnny-prxtvkfxgvy|Hanji 164-1]] by narrating [[WeishuiBattle|潍水之战]]. [[LongJuChuGeneral|龙且]] rejects a defensive supply-cutting plan because he despises [[HanXin|韩信]] and wants decisive merit, so Han Xin uses a dam, feigned retreat, and flood timing to split and destroy the Chu rescue force. The episode then uses Long Ju's death and Qi's collapse to compare [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s under-delegating personal command style with [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s willingness to rely on [[XiaoHe|萧何]], [[ZhangLiang|张良]], and Han Xin.

## Key Claims
- Long Ju's defeat is framed as a layered failure, not just a smaller tactical mistake: contempt for Han Xin, desire for merit, and refusal of a safer supply strategy all make him vulnerable.
- The unnamed retainer's proposal would have avoided direct battle by holding the camp, letting [[TianGuangQiKing|田广]] summon occupied Qi cities back, and exploiting Han's distance from its base.
- Han Xin's Weishui plan extends [[BattlefieldInformationControl|战场信息控制]] and [[RiverCrossingDeception|渡河欺敌]]: he makes the Chu commander see a retreating enemy while the real weapon is the upstream dam and timed release.
- The episode treats Long Ju as a capable but underdeveloped commander: he had defeated [[YingBu|英布]] and counted among Xiang Yu's trusted people, but long service as Xiang Yu's deputy left little space for independent strategic maturity.
- Qi's end follows quickly after Long Ju's death: Tian Guang is captured at Chengyang, [[GuanYing|灌婴]] captures the Qi chancellor Tian Guang, and [[TianHeng|田横]]'s renewed kingship bid fails before he flees to [[PengYue|彭越]].
- The final comparison argues that Liu Bang's strength is organizational rather than personal: he can admit that he is weaker than Xiao He in logistics, Zhang Liang in strategy, and Han Xin in command, then still use them.

## Key Quotes
> "匹夫之勇" - the episode's final label for the kind of courage that loses to Han Xin's command intelligence.

> "军事天才" - the source's closing description of Han Xin's battlefield level in contrast with Long Ju.
