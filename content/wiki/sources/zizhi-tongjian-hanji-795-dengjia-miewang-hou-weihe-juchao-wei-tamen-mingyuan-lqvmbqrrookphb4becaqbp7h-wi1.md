## Summary
This episode backfills the immediate aftermath of [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Empress Dowager Deng]]'s fall and shows why the purge of the [[DengFamilyAnEraLateHan|Deng family]] did not become a clean legitimacy reset for [[EmperorAnOfHan|Emperor An of Han]]. [[ChenZhongLateHan|Chen Zhong]] is restored and repeatedly accuses the Deng household, but [[ZhuChongLateHan|Zhu Chong]] answers with an extreme coffin memorial arguing that the purge lacks evidence, interrogation, and proportional settlement. Emperor An only partially corrects course by blaming local over-enforcement, allowing Deng burials at Bei Mang, and returning cousins to Luoyang, while the post-Deng vacuum is filled by Geng, Song, Yan, eunuch, wet-nurse, and favorite-household networks that [[YangZhenLateHan|Yang Zhen]] immediately warns against.

## Key Claims
- The Deng purge is politically understandable as a post-regency reset, but Zhu Chong frames it as a [[ProcedurelessPoliticalPurge|procedureless political purge]] because charges, proof, and cross-examination are missing.
- Chen Zhong's post-Deng restoration is shaped by family memory: his father Chen Chong had opposed honors for Deng Xun, and Chen Zhong's earlier frustration under Deng rule makes his later anti-Deng accusations look less neutral.
- Public sympathy matters: common mourning and Zhu Chong's memorial do not rehabilitate the Deng household, but they force Emperor An to halt escalation and permit limited burial and survivor relief.
- The fall of one outer-relative network immediately creates space for new ones, especially the Geng, Song, and Yan families around Emperor An's maternal and marital household.
- Palace favorites and eunuchs, including [[JiangJingLateHan|Jiang Jing]], [[LiRunLateHan|Li Run]], [[WangShengPalaceActorLateHan|Wang Sheng]], [[BoRongLateHan|Bo Rong]], and [[FanFengEunuchLateHan|Fan Feng]], rise in the same transition that claims to be correcting Deng domination.
- Yang Zhen's memorial attacks the new favorite network before the later Hanji 796-800 episodes, warning that wet-nurse privilege, Bo Rong's traffic, and palace-intimate access are already corrupting appointment order.

## Key Quotes
> "不足于信" — Zhu Chong's procedural objection to relying on one-sided palace speech against the Deng family.

> "输转钱贿" — Yang Zhen's charge that Bo Rong transmits bribes through palace-favorite access.

> "乘势放纵" — Yang Zhen's description of Wang Sheng's post-reward conduct after nursing Emperor An.
