# VOL.204 经济舱综合征：端午假期出行乘车乘机坐久了腿胀？鞋发紧？血管外科医生的硬核“排雷”指南

## Summary
This [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode has [[Atang|阿汤]] interview vascular surgeons [[ChenBinVascularSurgery|陈斌]] and [[FengYanfeiVascularSurgery|冯燕飞]] from [[ZhejiangUniversitySecondAffiliatedHospital|浙江大学医学院附属第二医院]] about [[EconomyClassSyndromeTravelThrombosis|economy-class syndrome]]. The practical synthesis is that the danger is not the cabin class itself but prolonged immobility, venous stasis, dehydration, and high-coagulation contexts that can progress from leg swelling to deep venous thrombosis and pulmonary embolism. The episode builds [[TravelThrombosisPrevention]], [[VenousThromboembolismTriage]], and [[AnticoagulationSelfMedicationBoundary]] by separating ordinary post-travel leg swelling from red flags that require urgent evaluation and reduced movement.

## Key Claims
- "Economy-class syndrome" is a shorthand for travel- or immobility-related venous thrombosis risk, not a problem limited to airplane economy cabins.
- Deep venous thrombosis risk rises when venous return is slowed by long sitting, cramped posture, dehydration, endothelial injury, high-coagulation states, surgery, pregnancy, tumors, infection, trauma, hormones, or prior thrombosis.
- Ordinary travel leg swelling often reflects venous pressure and stasis, but sudden unilateral swelling, continuous swelling below a level, pain, calf hardness, or marked whole-limb swelling should raise concern for thrombosis.
- Prevention for ordinary travelers centers on movement, hydration, ankle-pump exercises, avoiding long fixed posture, and properly selected medical compression stockings.
- If thrombosis is strongly suspected, the first question is how to get to care: avoid walking, massage, or unnecessary muscle contraction, use a wheelchair or stretcher when possible, and route high-risk cases through emergency evaluation.
- Anticoagulation, antiplatelet drugs, and thrombolysis are different tools; laypeople should not take aspirin or anticoagulants casually for travel prevention without clinician assessment.

## Key Quotes
> "首先要考虑怎么去医院" - 陈斌 reframes suspected thrombosis as a transport and emergency-routing problem before department selection.

> "抗凝不是消除血栓" - 陈斌 distinguishes anticoagulation from clot dissolution.
