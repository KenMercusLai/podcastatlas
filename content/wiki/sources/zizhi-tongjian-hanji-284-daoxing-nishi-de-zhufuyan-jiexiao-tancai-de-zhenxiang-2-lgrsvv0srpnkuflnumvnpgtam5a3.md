# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》284｜倒行逆施的主父偃，揭晓贪财的真相（2）

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[ZhuFuYan|主父偃]] at the height of his influence under [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]. It presents his knowledge of princely-household secrets as political leverage: an accusation against [[LiuDingguoYanKingWesternHan|燕王刘定国]] ends in the king's suicide and the abolition of Yan, while a later attack on [[QiLiwangWesternHan|齐厉王]] joins royal scandal, the wealth and strategic value of Linzi, and Zhufu Yan's resentment after a rejected marriage proposal.

The episode's durable contribution is the collision between public enforcement and private motive. Zhufu Yan's appointment as Qi chancellor gives him official investigative power, but the source interprets the resulting coercive inquiry and the Qi king's suicide through personal grievance as well as central control. [[LiuPengzuZhaoKingWesternHan|赵王刘彭祖]] then anticipates that he may be the next target and reports Zhufu Yan for bribery and for forcing the Qi king's death.

## Key Claims

- Zhufu Yan's earlier residence in Yan, Qi, and Zhao gives him knowledge of royal-household secrets that can be converted into court accusations.
- A brother of the executed Feiru magistrate Yin Ren reports Liu Dingguo's alleged incest and Yin Ren's retaliatory killing; Zhufu Yan forwards the accusation to Han Wudi.
- After court deliberation, Liu Dingguo is ordered to commit suicide and the Yan kingdom is abolished.
- As officials and princely retainers begin fearing and bribing him, Zhufu Yan rejects Kong Che's warning to restrain himself and explains his urgency through the phrase “倒行逆施.”
- The episode attributes Zhufu Yan's hostility toward Qi partly to Empress Dowager Ji's rejection of his proposed marriage connection with the Qi king.
- Zhufu Yan combines Linzi's wealth, the Qi king's increasingly distant kinship from the emperor, and an alleged incestuous relationship with Princess Ji Weng in a memorial urging scrutiny.
- Sent to Qi as chancellor, Zhufu Yan arrests and tortures palace eunuchs; their testimony implicates the Qi king, who then drinks poison and dies.
- Liu Pengzu responds preemptively by accusing Zhufu Yan of taking large bribes and forcing the Qi king's death.

## Key Quotes

> “倒行逆施” - Zhufu Yan's self-explanation in the episode for acting urgently after decades of frustration.

> “以私心办公事” - the episode's judgment on the mixture of public office and private resentment in the Qi investigation.
