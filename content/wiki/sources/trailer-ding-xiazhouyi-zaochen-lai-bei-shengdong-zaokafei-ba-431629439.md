## Summary
This trailer introduces [[ShengdongZaokafei|声动早咖啡]], a short workday-morning business and technology news podcast from [[ShengdongHuopo|声动活泼]]. [[MengYi|孟依]] frames the show through ordinary morning routines, team members' wake-up habits, and the metaphor of a concentrated coffee shot. The source's main wiki contribution is a launch-positioning case where [[MorningAudioRitual]] and [[ShortFormBusinessTechAudio]] turn information consumption into a manageable start-of-day habit.

## Key Claims
- The trailer begins from a familiar weekday morning pattern: alarms, reluctant waking, washing up, quick breakfast, and a busy workday.
- [[MengYi|孟依]] identifies herself as a [[ShengdongHuopo|声动活泼]] producer and says she is also the kind of content worker who struggles with early mornings.
- The team members' morning rituals include listening to podcasts, looking after plants, stretching, music, coffee, opening windows, cleaning the home, playing with a cat, and planning the day.
- The trailer repeatedly links wakefulness to small routines rather than to one universal productivity prescription.
- [[ShengdongZaokafei|声动早咖啡]] is positioned as a fresh, concentrated business-and-technology news show for workday mornings.
- The stated episode length is under 15 minutes, making the show fit pre-commute, breakfast, washing-up, and departure windows.
- The announced launch cadence is Monday, Wednesday, and Friday mornings, with an ambition to become a daily morning companion once production mechanisms mature.
- The coffee metaphor does real positioning work: the show is presented as short, energizing, concentrated, and repeatable rather than as a heavy briefing.
