# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》641丨币制改革，如何导致王莽万劫不复？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Wang Mang's 10 CE pressure point before the already-ingested later [[XinDynasty|新朝]] collapse sequence. It links anti-[[Xiongnu|匈奴]] mobilization, large-scale military logistics, a multi-type currency redesign, coercive enforcement, popular preference for Han [[WuzhuCoinRestoration|五铢钱]], and proliferating omen/fuming claims into one early legitimacy and state-capacity problem.

The durable synthesis is that [[WangMang|王莽]]'s currency reform failed as an interaction between design complexity, market trust, and coercive administration. The six-name, twenty-eight-rank money system was too hard for common users to recognize, repeated policy reversals weakened [[CurrencyCredit|货币信用]], and attempts to force acceptance through collective liability, travel checks, and speech punishment turned a technical monetary reform into social and economic disorder.

## Key Claims

- In 10 CE, Wang Mang prepares a large anti-Xiongnu expedition by appointing many generals, mobilizing convicts and adult men, and moving clothes, arms, and grain from distant regions to the northern frontier.
- The proposed Xiongnu strategy aims to divide the chanyu's population into fifteen parts ruled by descendants of Huhanye Chanyu, echoing earlier divide-and-rule logic used against internal kingdoms.
- Wang Mang's currency design tries to solve large-versus-small transaction inconvenience through six named money categories and twenty-eight ranks, but the system is too complex for ordinary circulation.
- Popular confusion and nonacceptance force another narrowing of legal tender to small one-cash coins and large fifty-cash coins, showing that policy reversals themselves damage monetary confidence.
- Coercive enforcement expands from anti-counterfeiting into collective punishment, official and neighbor liability, travel restrictions, and palace-entry checks requiring possession of the new money.
- Common users continue to prefer Han five-zhu coins and spread rumors that the large coin will be abolished, making currency acceptance a question of trust rather than decree alone.
- Wang Mang's reliance on auspicious signs and fuming legitimacy creates imitation incentives: once fabricated omens bring rewards, fake signs proliferate beyond his control.

## Key Quotes

> "老百姓根本分不清" - the host's explanation of why the many-ranked currency system fails in daily circulation.

> "百姓心中自有判断" - the host's point that money acceptance depends on public trust and usability.

> "造假、伪称符命的事情层出不穷" - the episode's transition from currency-credit failure to omen-politics loss of control.
