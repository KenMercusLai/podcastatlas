# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》872丨他娶妾不成，竟杀人全家

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 166 CE pre-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-873-ta-ruhe-liyong-tianxiang-tongma-diwang-lqebkodcbcrsvyf80qwxnobydbhc|Hanji 873]] court field through emotion-control commentary, [[ZhangJianLateHan|张俭]]'s enforcement against [[HouLanLateHan|侯览]], [[XuXuanLateHan|徐宣]]'s killing of [[LiGaoRunanLateHan|李暠]]'s daughter after a failed concubine demand, and [[HuangFuDonghaiLateHan|黄浮]]'s execution of Xu Xuan at [[Xiapi|下邳]].

The episode's durable contribution is [[EunuchPatronageViolenceShield|宦官庇护下的地方暴行]]: local officials can identify abuse and punish violence, but eunuch access lets offenders or patrons convert enforcement into retaliation against the officials. [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]]'s memorial makes the problem institutional rather than episodic by arguing that petty external disorder matters less than the ruler's blocked counsel and favorite-protected inner court.

## Key Claims

- The opening emotion-control frame is used to prepare the historical problem: anger, desire, and status entitlement can turn private frustration into irreversible harm.
- [[ZhaiChaoLateHan|翟超]] appoints [[ZhangJianLateHan|张俭]] as eastern-area supervisory official, and Zhang attacks [[HouLanLateHan|侯览]]'s illegal wealth, buildings, and tomb construction.
- Hou Lan intercepts Zhang Jian's memorials, showing that normal reporting channels can be blocked before the ruler sees local evidence.
- [[XuXuanLateHan|徐宣]], backed by his uncle [[XuHuangLateHan|徐璜]], abuses his Xiapi office, abducts Li Gao's daughter after being refused a concubine arrangement, and kills her.
- [[HuangFuDonghaiLateHan|黄浮]] treats Xu Xuan as a state-level criminal and executes him despite subordinates warning that eunuch retaliation will be dangerous.
- After Xu Huang complains, [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] punishes Huang Fu and Zhai Chao instead of treating their enforcement as protection of the people.
- [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] and [[LiuMaoLateHan|刘茂]] petition for the officials; Liu Mao retreats after pressure, while Chen Fan continues and frames eunuch favor and blocked忠言 as the court's heart-level disease.
- Huan does not adopt Chen Fan's advice, and eunuch hostility toward Chen Fan intensifies, foreshadowing later blocked memorials and anti-eunuch conflict.

## Key Quotes

> "上善若水" - the opening frame for softness, restraint, and emotional self-command.

> "今日杀之，明日坐死，足以瞑目" - Huang Fu's stance that executing Xu Xuan is worth personal risk.

> "心腹大患" - Chen Fan's diagnosis that the court's internal favorite structure is more dangerous than ordinary external disorder.
