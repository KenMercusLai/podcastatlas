# 《资治通鉴·周纪》14丨齐威王 不鸣则已一鸣惊人

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode covers the 358-356 BCE cluster under [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]], moving from Qin, Wei, Han, Chu, Zhao, Yan, Qi, and Song diplomacy into a source-critical reading of [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]]. Its main contribution is double: it sharpens the [[QiChronologyDispute|齐威王编年争议]] around [[Shiji|《史记》]] and [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]], then retells [[ChunyuKun|淳于髡]]'s [[AllusiveRemonstrance|隐语进谏]] that leads to Qi Wei Wang's "一鸣惊人" turnaround, official audits, military recovery, and possible strategic feigning.

## Key Claims
- In 358 BCE, [[QinState|秦国]] defeats [[HanState|韩国]] at West Mountain, while [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王]] has [[LongJia|龙甲]] build a western wall as part of [[WeiGreatWallAntiQinDefense|魏长城防秦]].
- The same year, [[YouyinHei|右尹黑]] goes from [[ChuState|楚国]] to Qin to receive a bride, which the episode reads as likely for [[ChuXuanwang|楚宣王]] rather than for Youyin Hei himself.
- The Qin-Chu marriage notice supports [[WarringStatesMarriageDiplomacy|战国联姻外交]] but does not imply durable alignment; interstate interest can later override marital ties.
- In 357 BCE, [[WeiState|魏国]] and Han hold a high-level meeting, probably between Wei Hui Wang and [[HanZhaohou|韩昭侯]], against a background of land exchange and Qin pressure.
- In 356 BCE, Zhao and Yan meet at Ajuan, while Zhao, Qi, and Song meet at Pinglu, showing dense [[EarlyWarringStatesInterstateWar|early Warring States]] diplomacy rather than stable blocs.
- The episode argues that early Tian-family Qi records are thin and that [[Shiji|《史记》]] and [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] preserve serious chronology problems around Qi Wei Wang.
- The host says Qi Wei Wang's accession was probably moved twenty-one years too early in the received sequence; the corrected placement would have him accede in 357 BCE, making 356 BCE his first year.
- [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s notice that Qi Wei Wang visited [[ZhouLiewang|周烈王]] in 370 BCE is treated as impossible because Zhou Lie Wang was already dead; if the visit happened, the ruler should be [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]].
- In the narrative layer, Qi Wei Wang neglects government, delegates affairs to [[ChenLiao|陈聊]], and leaves [[QiState|齐国]] exposed to neighboring attacks.
- Chunyu Kun uses a riddle about a large silent bird because direct criticism is dangerous and Qi Wei Wang likes hidden speech.
- Qi Wei Wang understands the allusion and answers with the line that becomes the idiom "一鸣惊人."
- Qi Wei Wang then audits seventy-two local officials, rewards [[JimoDafu|即墨大夫]], punishes [[AyiDafu|阿邑大夫]], reforms military capacity, and recovers land taken by neighboring states.
- The episode leaves open the possibility that Qi Wei Wang had been deliberately appearing useless, so the remonstrance may have supplied a trigger rather than created the whole change.

## Key Quotes
> "不飞则已，一飞冲天；不鸣则已，一鸣惊人。" - Qi Wei Wang's answer to Chunyu Kun's hidden remonstrance.

## Contradictions
- The source directly challenges the received placement of Qi Wei Wang in the early annals, especially the 370 BCE Zhou-court visit attributed to [[ZhouLiewang|周烈王]]. This extends the existing [[ChronicleChronologyDrift]] warning rather than contradicting current wiki synthesis.
- No direct contradiction found with existing wiki pages. The source confirms the earlier Jimo/Ayi audit branch while relocating it into a more explicit Qi Wei Wang chronology problem.
