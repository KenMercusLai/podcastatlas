# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》498丨太子刘骜荒淫无度，汉元帝为何不废了他？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the late-[[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] succession crisis immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-499-liubang-fuwoqilai-dasi-liushi-zhegezeng-zengsunzi-lndfgjehpe7qc36hejxba3znlz0p|Hanji 499]]'s death-and-accession layer. It explains why Yuan did not replace crown prince [[HanChengdi|刘骜 / 后来的汉成帝]] with [[LiuKangDingtaoKingWesternHan|山阳王刘康]] despite Liu Ao's adult indulgence, Liu Kang's favored position, and [[FuZhaoyiWesternHan|傅昭仪]]'s illness-bed proximity.

The durable synthesis is that late-life affection and bedside access can create heir-replacement pressure, but an established crown prince may survive when a minister reframes the ruler's preference as a state-risk problem. [[ShiDanWesternHan|史丹 / 施丹]] does not argue that Liu Kang is bad; he stresses Liu Ao's嫡长 status, long-standing designation, and the likelihood that senior officials would resist a change, forcing Yuan to preserve the existing succession.

## Key Claims

- The episode frames heir designation as a core stability problem, not a private family matter, using later succession struggles as comparative examples.
- Liu Ao is described as cautious, broad-minded, and fond of Confucian classics when young, but later as disappointedly indulgent in wine and women.
- Yuan's affection shifts toward Liu Kang because Liu Kang has musical skill, is attached to the favored Fu Zhaoyi, and remains near Yuan during illness while Wang Zhengjun and Liu Ao are less visible.
- Yuan repeatedly asks about Emperor Jing's replacement of his crown prince with the future Han Wudi, suggesting that he is testing whether Liu Ao can be replaced.
- [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] and [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]] recognize the danger and seek ways to protect Liu Ao, but Wang Zhengjun's maternal position makes direct advocacy awkward.
- Shi Dan's decisive intervention turns the issue from personal preference into institutional cost: replacing a long-established嫡长 crown prince would provoke the Three Excellencies and Nine Ministers to fight the decision.
- After Shi Dan's tearful remonstrance, Yuan states that he has no plan to change the crown prince, asks Shi Dan to assist Liu Ao, and stops asking about the Emperor Jing precedent.

## Key Quotes

> "皇位继承问题" - the episode's opening frame for why the issue is political rather than merely domestic.

> "历史才是最好的教科书" - the closing formula connecting the succession case to broader learning.
