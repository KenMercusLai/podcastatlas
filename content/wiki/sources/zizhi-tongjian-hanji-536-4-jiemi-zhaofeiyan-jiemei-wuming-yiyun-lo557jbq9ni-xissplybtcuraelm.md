# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》536-4丨揭秘赵飞燕姐妹污名疑云

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode pauses the [[ZhaoFeiyanWesternHan|赵飞燕]] and [[ZhaoHedeWesternHan|赵合德]] scandal sequence to ask why later history and literature so easily turn favored women into "红颜祸水." It treats the Zhao sisters' reputation as part of a larger gendered blame pattern that also includes Mei Xi, Da Ji, [[BaoSi|褒姒]], [[XiaJiSpringAutumn|夏姬]], and the comparative example of [[HelenOfTroy|海伦]].

The durable synthesis is historiographical. The source does not adjudicate every allegation against the Zhao sisters; instead, it argues that male rulers, ministers, and writers often redirect responsibility for desire, political disorder, and military disaster onto women whose beauty makes them vivid blame targets.

## Key Claims

- The episode says the Zhao sisters' later image as "千古荡妇" deserves scrutiny because the alleged facts behind the label are not as firm as the moralized reputation suggests.
- It treats "美人误国" as a narrative shortcut that can shift responsibility away from rulers, ministers, and institutional failure.
- The Han童谣 example is used to show how blame can migrate from one sister to the other when attacking an empress is rhetorically more satisfying than attacking a zhaoyi.
- The [[XiaJiSpringAutumn|夏姬]] story is used as a parallel: Chen Ling Gong, his ministers, Hei Yao, and Wu Chen make consequential choices, yet later memory condenses the damage into the image of a "licentious" woman.
- The [[HelenOfTroy|Helen]] comparison lets the host contrast Chinese "红颜祸水" language with a Greek-war story in which the woman is not framed in exactly the same moral vocabulary.
- The episode reads some literary condemnation of beautiful women as public moral performance mixed with desire, envy, and power anxiety.
- The closing qualification says not all writers fit this pattern; the next episode will turn to [[GengYuWesternHan|耿育]] as a counterexample of literary and political skill.

## Key Quotes

> "谣言举于智者" - the host's claim that reputation attacks can be crafted by clever people rather than only spread by the ignorant.

> "吃不到葡萄说葡萄酸" - the host's metaphor for moralized condemnation that may hide frustrated desire or envy.
