# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1064丨许攸之死：功高震主与性格缺陷的悲歌

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the Jian'an 9 aftermath of [[CaoCao|曹操]] taking [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]] after the fall of [[YeLateHan|邺城]]. It uses the contrast between [[QianZhaoLateHan|牵招]]'s timely submission, [[CuiYanLateHan|崔琰]]'s public-interest remonstrance, and [[XuYouLateHan|许攸]]'s fatal overfamiliarity to develop a lesson about credit, hierarchy, public speech, and restraint.

The central event is Xu You's death. After helping Cao Cao win the [[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]] and later gain Jizhou, Xu You repeatedly boasts in public that Cao Cao would not have obtained Jizhou without him, even addressing Cao as "曹阿瞒"; Cao Cao laughs outwardly but becomes resentful and later kills him.

## Key Claims

- In 204 CE / Jian'an 9, Emperor Xian appoints Cao Cao as Jizhou governor, and Cao resigns the Yan Province governorship he had also held.
- [[QianZhaoLateHan|Qian Zhao]] had been sent by [[YuanShang|袁尚]] to supervise grain transport in Shangdang; when Yuan Shang fled toward Zhongshan and [[GaoGanLateHan|高干]] ignored Qian Zhao's proposal to receive him, Qian Zhao submitted to Cao Cao.
- Cao Cao keeps Qian Zhao as a Jizhou official, and the host frames Qian Zhao's later northern-frontier career as evidence that stable border administration can be as important as battlefield fame.
- Cao Cao appoints [[CuiYanLateHan|Cui Yan]] as biejia; Cui Yan sharply criticizes Cao for first noticing Jizhou's conscription potential instead of the suffering of its people.
- Cao Cao apologizes to Cui Yan, but the host treats this as evidence of Cao Cao's composure and of Cui Yan's moral force, not as permission for all bluntness in hierarchy.
- [[XuYouLateHan|Xu You]] had real merit from the Guandu defection and later Jizhou outcome, but he turns that merit into danger by publicly diminishing Cao Cao's authority.
- Xu You's killing is framed as a credit-and-survival problem: contribution matters, but claiming superiority over the leader in public converts merit into status threat.
- The workplace analogy warns that closeness to a superior does not erase public boundaries; nicknames, shoulder-patting, and jokes in front of clients or colleagues can be interpreted as status competition.

## Key Quotes

> "没有我许攸，你曹阿瞒得不到冀州。" - Xu You's boast as summarized by the episode.

> "功劳可以有，但不能当众撕老板的脸。" - the host's modern workplace framing of Xu You's error.

> "别人尊重你，不一定是因为你优秀，也可能是因为别人有涵养。" - lesson drawn from Cao Cao's response to Cui Yan.
