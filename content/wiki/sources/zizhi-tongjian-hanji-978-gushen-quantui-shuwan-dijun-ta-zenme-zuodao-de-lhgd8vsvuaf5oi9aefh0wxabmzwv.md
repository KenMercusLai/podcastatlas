# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》978丨孤身劝退数万敌军，他怎么做到的？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the immediate prelude to [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-979-zaoyu-tongliao-beici-ta-ruhe-wei-caocao-liwan-kuanglan-luu58a2ww11g-radh-5g4l7fcis|Hanji 979]] in [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] crisis. [[XunYu|荀彧]] meets [[GuoGongLateHan|郭贡]] outside [[JuanchengLateHan|鄄城]] and uses composure to make a large uncertain force withdraw, then sends [[ChengYu|程昱]] toward [[DongALateHan|东阿]] and [[FanCountyLateHan|范县]] to stabilize the remaining cities. The final branch turns [[JinYunLateHan|靳允]]'s choice to kill [[FanYiLateHan|范仪]] despite family-hostage pressure into a source-scoped debate over loyalty, filial duty, and political survival.

## Key Claims
- [[GuoGongLateHan|郭贡]] arrives below [[JuanchengLateHan|鄄城]] with several tens of thousands of soldiers while rumor says he may cooperate with [[LyuBu|吕布]], creating panic inside [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s remaining base.
- [[XiahouDun|夏侯惇]] warns [[XunYu|荀彧]] not to leave the city because Xun Yu is the main stabilizing figure in Juancheng, but Xun Yu judges that Guo Gong has not yet fixed his alignment.
- Xun Yu's calm meeting turns uncertainty against the attacker: Guo Gong sees no visible panic, suspects hidden strength or a trap, and withdraws without fighting.
- The episode names [[JuanchengLateHan|鄄城]], [[FanCountyLateHan|范县]], and [[DongALateHan|东阿]] as the remaining Cao-side strongholds after most of [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] turns toward Lü Bu and [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]].
- Xun Yu asks [[ChengYu|程昱]] to return east because Cheng Yu's local prestige in Dong'e can persuade hesitant counties before [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] and Lü Bu's side consolidate.
- Cheng Yu persuades [[JinYunLateHan|靳允]] that Lü Bu and Chen Gong lack durable leadership capacity while Cao Cao remains the better long-term alignment, making the Fan County case a [[XingshiqingStrategicAssessment|行势情战略评估]] example.
- [[JinYunLateHan|靳允]] keeps [[FanCountyLateHan|范县]] for Cao Cao by ambushing and killing [[FanYiLateHan|范仪]] after Fan Yi enters the county.
- [[XuZhongJin|许众]] later criticizes Jin Yun because Jin Yun had no settled lord-subject bond with Cao Cao and should have prioritized his captured mother.
- The host compares [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s answer to [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s threat against [[LiuTaigong|刘太公]] to argue that disorder-era hostage choices cannot be reduced to one universal filial or loyal rule.

## Key Quotes
> "绝不会有二心." - Jin Yun's pledge after Cheng Yu's persuasion.

> "煮了之后分他一杯羹." - the host's paraphrase of Liu Bang's answer to Xiang Yu's hostage threat.
