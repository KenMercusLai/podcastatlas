# E250 为什么学了这么多知识，却还是做不好投资？

## Summary
This [[ZhixingXiaojiuguan|知行小酒馆]] conversation with [[ShirleyInvestmentCoach|Shirley]] treats investing as a behavior and life-design problem before it becomes a product-selection problem. Shirley describes a small, one-to-one coaching practice that begins with why the person invests, what kind of life the money should support, which losses and workload they can actually bear, and whether advanced market participation is needed at all.

Her own account links the 2015, 2018, and 2022–2024 market cycles to [[InvestmentStrategyFit|strategy fit]], [[InvestorSelfKnowledge|investor self-knowledge]], and [[InvestmentDecisionLogging|contemporaneous records]]. The episode argues that broad ETF exposure can make average-market participation operationally simple, but no tool removes fear, greed, uncertain waiting, or the need to distinguish luck from repeatable process. Coaching, precommitted rules, environmental friction, and full-cycle experience can help turn knowledge into action, while final decisions and losses remain the investor's responsibility.

## Key Claims
- Investment should begin with purpose, time horizon, family context, risk capacity, and the desired life, not with a product or maximum-return target.
- [[InvestmentBehaviorCoaching]] differs from standardized instruction or delegated planning by using long-term dialogue to help a person construct and eventually own a personally workable process.
- Average-market exposure through broad ETFs can be simple to implement, but holding it through fear, greed, and long drawdowns remains behaviorally difficult.
- [[InvestmentStrategyFit]] includes daily workload and lifestyle: short-term trading, stock research, and long-term ETF value investing demand different attention, skill, and emotional paths.
- Contemporaneous records make forecasts, feelings, decisions, and non-decisions reviewable before hindsight rewrites them; repeated comparison can separate process, luck, and market environment.
- Individual investors can use patient, self-directed capital as an advantage over professionals facing rankings and redemptions, provided patience is supported by suitability and process rather than confidence alone.
- Money is useful when it creates time, refusal power, and chosen work; investment need not remain the center of life after a sufficient system is established.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is adopted. The episode strengthens existing claims that simple products do not make investor behavior simple and that market participation can legitimately be simplified or declined.
- Shirley's returns, trade timing, cycle interpretation, client outcomes, and descriptions of rich lists and long-run investor performance are retrospective or episode-attributed rather than independently audited.
- Her ETF-value framework depends partly on long-run economic growth and valuation judgments; surviving a prior cycle does not prove that the same rules will work in every future regime.
- The discussion is educational and autobiographical, not individualized investment advice. Trust in a coach cannot replace suitability, independent understanding, or responsibility for decisions and losses.
