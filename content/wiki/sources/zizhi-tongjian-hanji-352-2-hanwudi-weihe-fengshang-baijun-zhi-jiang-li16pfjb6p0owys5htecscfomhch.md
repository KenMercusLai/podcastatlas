# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》352-2｜汉武帝为何封赏败军之将？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[HanQianqiuWesternHan|韩千秋]]'s initially successful entry into [[Nanyue|南越]] to the destruction of his force near Panyu by [[LyuJiaNanyue|吕嘉]]. After learning that Lü Jia has killed Han personnel, returned the imperial tallies with a false apology, and fortified the frontier, [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] shifts from failed pressure to a large conquest plan.

The episode explains why defeat does not prevent honor. Wudi describes Han Qianqiu's group as courageous vanguard troops despite their failure to establish merit, supports the dead soldiers' families, makes Han Qianqiu's son [[HanYannianWesternHan|韩延年]] marquis of Cheng'an, and also rewards the son of the envoy rendered here as 樛罗. The source presents these measures as [[FallenVanguardHeirReward|阵亡先锋遗属封赏]]: compensation and public recognition can sustain later mobilization even when the first force is annihilated. It closes with a ten-wan-person southern army and the selection of [[LuBodeWesternHan|伏波将军路博德]] and [[YangPuWesternHan|楼船将军杨仆]], leaving their division of labor and the campaign result to later installments.

## Key Claims

- Han Qianqiu's force wins several early engagements after entering Nanyue but is destroyed about forty li from Panyu by Lü Jia's troops.
- Lü Jia returns the slain Han envoys' tallies in a box with a professed apology and strengthens the frontier passes, combining symbolic de-escalation with military preparation.
- Han Wudi treats the killing as open rebellion, compensates the dead soldiers' families, and begins planning the conquest of Nanyue.
- Han Qianqiu's failure to complete the mission does not erase the court's judgment that his group served as a courageous vanguard.
- Wudi makes Han Qianqiu's son Han Yannian marquis of Cheng'an and, in the episode's transcription, makes 樛罗's son 樛广德 marquis of Longkang.
- Earlier Qin and Empress Lü campaigns are used to explain the terrain, disease, logistics, and command risks of a southern expedition.
- Wudi mobilizes convicts and naval forces from south of the Yangtze and Huai rivers into a reported one-hundred-thousand-person army.
- With [[HuoQubing|霍去病]] dead and [[WeiQing|卫青]] aging and still needed against [[Xiongnu|匈奴]], Lu Bode and Yang Pu are selected to share the southern command.

## Key Quotes

> “虽无成功，亦军锋之冠。” - the episode's explanation for honoring the destroyed vanguard despite the failed operation.

> “千军易得，一将难求。” - the host's frame for the command-selection problem after mobilization.
