# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1072丨杜畿单骑守河东，竟使百姓愿为他赴死？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the [[HedongCommandery|河东郡]] crisis opened by [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1070-jiaochu-shaxue-weimeng-quan-buxia-panjiang-caocao-lqvyfglj3qefhyxyebfvvda8om5o|Hanji 1070]]. [[DuJiLateHan|杜畿]] abandons the commandery seat, relies on county-level defense and a previously cultivated support network, and holds the rebel coalition until outside forces associated with [[MaTeng|马腾]] break it. The source reads the result as an interaction among local trust, denied rebel supply, and external reinforcement rather than a simple battlefield victory.

The episode then follows Du Ji's sixteen-year governorship. Lenient dispute handling, farming and sericulture, local schooling, fortification, weapons production, and troop training turn Hedong into a wealthy and defensible strategic base for [[CaoCao|曹操]]. A closing discussion of [[XunYue|荀悦]] and *Shenjian* distinguishes reputation from results and argues that persuasive political writing has limited force when speech and military command are institutionally separated.

## Key Claims

- [[WeiGuLateHan|卫固]], [[FanXianLateHan|范先]], [[GaoGanLateHan|高干]], and [[ZhangShengLateHan|张盛]] combine pressure against Du Ji during the Hedong revolt.
- Du Ji gives up the commandery seat but retains the wider region through county defenses and a previously organized four-thousand-person support network, a source case for [[DistributedLocalResilience|分布式地方韧性]].
- The rebels fail to take Du Ji's camp or obtain grain from surrounding counties, so their inability to replace local order with food and security weakens their position.
- [[ZhangJiCaoOfficialLateHan|张既]] is sent west to seek help from Ma Teng; the resulting outside attack defeats Zhang Sheng's side, kills Wei Gu and a transcript-uncertain Zhang Yan/Yan figure, and is followed by amnesty for remaining followers.
- Du Ji's sixteen-year governorship combines lenient civil administration, agriculture and sericulture, schooling, fortification, weapons production, and troop training.
- The episode treats [[HedongCommandery|河东郡]] as a salt-and-iron and Yellow River crossing hub connecting Guanzhong and the Central Plains, making its stability a strategic resource rather than a local achievement only.
- Xun Yue's “名必有实，事必有功” supplies a result-accountability principle, while the episode's “政出曹门” frame qualifies the practical reach of counsel without coercive power.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. This episode supplies the outcome that Hanji 1070 leaves open: Du Ji survives the contested appointment and turns Hedong into a durable Cao-side base.
- Name caution: the supplied summary says “张琰” was killed, while Hanji 1070's related canonical page is [[ZhangYanHedongLateHan|张衍]]. The episode itself warns that names in the transcript are unstable, so this ingest does not merge or rename the identity and keeps the death claim source-scoped.
- Source-scope caution: the “distributed warfare,” “highest rule makes subjects think they chose,” and “speech power versus military power” formulations are the host's modern interpretations, not independently verified historical terminology.
- Factual caution: exact household counts, resource geography, force totals, battle allocation, superlative performance rankings, quoted wording, and the precise genealogy linking Xun Yue, Xun Yu, and Xun You require textual verification before broader use.
