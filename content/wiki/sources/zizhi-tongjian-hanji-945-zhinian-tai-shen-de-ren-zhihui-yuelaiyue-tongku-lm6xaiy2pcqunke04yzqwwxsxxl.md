# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》945丨执念太深的人，只会越来越痛苦

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate prelude to [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-946-zhichang-douzheng-shi-yourouguaduan-you-duo-zhiming-lpyyweaajonzg7kxuhwm9z7ae7p5|Hanji 946]]. It uses "执念" as a source-scoped psychological frame for [[HeJin|何进]]'s refusal to abandon the plan of summoning outside forces into [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] against the [[TenAttendantsLateHan|十常侍]], despite warnings from [[ChenLinLateHan|陈琳]], [[CaoCao|曹操]], [[ZhengTaiLateHan|郑泰]], and [[LuZhiLateHan|卢植]]. The distinct contribution is [[ExternalTroopInvitationRisk|外兵入京升级风险]]: a court powerholder can try to borrow military pressure for an internal purge, but the invitation gives actors such as [[DongZhuo|董卓]] their own entry point and makes later [[PalaceCoupViolenceCascade|宫廷政变暴力级联]] harder to contain.

## Key Claims

- In 189 CE, after [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s death, [[HeJin|何进]] accepts [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s line of action and plans to summon outside commanders and local forces into the capital field.
- He Jin's aim is to pressure [[EmpressDowagerHe|何太后]] into authorizing the killing of the [[TenAttendantsLateHan|十常侍]] and other eunuch actors.
- The source says [[ChenLinLateHan|陈琳]] and [[CaoCao|曹操]] have already warned against this approach, but He Jin remains fixed on the escalation plan.
- [[ZhengTaiLateHan|郑泰]] warns that [[DongZhuo|董卓]] is cruel, ambitious, and unreliable; he argues that He Jin already has enough rank and authority to punish guilty eunuchs without inviting Dong Zhuo.
- Zheng Tai also invokes the [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] precedent as a warning that delayed anti-eunuch action lets the situation change before the outside actor can control it.
- [[LuZhiLateHan|卢植]] separately tells He Jin that allowing Dong Zhuo into the capital will certainly create disorder, but He Jin still refuses to change course.
- He Jin sends [[WangKuangLateHan|王匡]] and [[BaoXin|鲍信]] to raise troops, calls [[QiaoMaoLateHan|乔瑁]] to garrison [[Chenggao|成皋]], and orders [[DingYuanLateHan|丁原]] into [[HeneiCommandery|河内郡]], where the source says Ding Yuan burns Mengjin.
- The mobilization turns a palace problem into a wider military crisis: the source describes Luoyang as anxious while forces move toward the capital.
- Dong Zhuo welcomes the summons and memorializes against [[ZhangRangLateHan|张让]] and other eunuchs, asking to advance on Luoyang under the name of purging court disorder.
- When He Jin begins to waver, he sends [[ZhongShaoLateHan|种劭]] with an order to stop Dong Zhuo; Dong Zhuo first ignores the order, then retreats as far as Xiyang Pavilion after Zhong Shao confronts him.
- The source explains Zhong Shao's leverage through reputation and force balance: his grandfather Zhong Hao had prestige among Bing-Liang soldiers, while Dong Zhuo has only about three thousand troops and cannot afford to alienate his men or the capital.
- Yuan Shao fears He Jin will reverse course and presses him again; He Jin appoints Yuan Shao as sili xiaowei with jiajie authority and makes [[WangYunLateHan|王允]] Henan yin.
- After gaining formal authority, Yuan Shao monitors eunuchs with armed pressure and urges Dong Zhuo and others forward, forcing Empress Dowager He to dismiss many palace attendants.
- When the dismissed eunuchs come to He Jin in fear and ask to be dealt with, He Jin scolds them and tells them to return to their fiefs, but does not accept Yuan Shao's request to execute them all.
- Yuan Shao then sends letters to provinces and commanderies under He Jin's name, ordering arrests of eunuchs; the delayed and leaking plan gives the eunuch side time to respond.
- Zhang Rang uses palace-family ties: through his daughter-in-law, [[WuyangJun|武阳君]], and Empress Dowager He's softness, he obtains permission for the dismissed eunuchs to reenter the palace.
- The episode ends before the killing of He Jin, making Hanji 945 the setup for Hanji 946's trap, retaliation, imperial flight, and Dong Zhuo opening.

## Key Quotes

> "世界上最大的监狱就是你的内心" - the opening maxim used to frame obsession as self-imprisonment.

> "董卓进京，必生大乱" - Lu Zhi's warning as summarized by the source.

> "且听我明天分解" - the cliffhanger before Hanji 946's palace-coup payoff.
