# 《资治通鉴·周纪》97-4丨史上为事业送老婆第一人

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]]'s backing of [[QinZhuangxiangwang|异人 / 子楚]] by showing how the plan is converted from insight into court action. [[YangquanJun|阳泉君]] and [[HuayangFuren|华阳夫人]] become the persuasion route, while the carved jade tally makes Yiren's adoption a [[WrittenSuccessionCredential|written succession credential]] rather than only a private promise.

The episode then follows [[ZhaoJi|赵姬]] from Lu Buwei's household to Yiren and treats [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]]'s paternity rumor as a historiographical problem. It compares [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] and [[Shiji|《史记》]], concluding that the Lu Buwei paternity story is doubtful but that later readers' willingness to believe it reveals how anti-Qin memory could shape scandal.

## Key Claims
- [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]] does not spread risk widely; he puts large resources behind [[QinZhuangxiangwang|异人 / 子楚]] as a concentrated [[PoliticalVentureInvestment|political venture investment]].
- The persuasion sequence works by making [[YangquanJun|阳泉君]] and [[HuayangFuren|华阳夫人]] feel future insecurity before offering Yiren's adoption as the solution.
- [[HuayangFuren|华阳夫人]] is not merely manipulated; by requiring a carved jade tally, she turns adoption into a [[WrittenSuccessionCredential|written succession credential]] that protects her future status.
- [[QinXiaowenwang|安国君柱 / 秦孝文王]]'s recognition of Yiren as嫡子 turns [[MaternalStatusSuccessionLeverage|子以母贵式继承杠杆]] from an opportunity into a formal succession claim.
- The episode doubts the report that Lu Buwei served as Yiren's师傅, because a merchant tutor for a future ruler would have damaged Yiren's elite image.
- [[ZhaoJi|赵姬]] is named as the woman Lu Buwei gives to Yiren; the source keeps the transfer as part of Lu Buwei's personal-cost logic.
- The episode treats the claim that [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]] was Lu Buwei's biological son as weak: it cites孤证, internal contradiction, and similarity to the [[Chunshenjun|春申君]] story as reasons for caution.
- The persistence of the paternity rumor matters even if false, because it makes anti-Qin feeling visible through a scandal about merchant origin, sexual legitimacy, and imperial bloodline.

## Key Quotes
> "玉符" - the material detail the episode reads as Huayang Furen's political safeguard.

> "名正言顺" - the legitimacy standard used to explain why Yiren could not later deny Huayang Furen without weakening himself.

> "孤证不立" - the source-critical reason attached to modern doubts about the Lu Buwei paternity rumor.
