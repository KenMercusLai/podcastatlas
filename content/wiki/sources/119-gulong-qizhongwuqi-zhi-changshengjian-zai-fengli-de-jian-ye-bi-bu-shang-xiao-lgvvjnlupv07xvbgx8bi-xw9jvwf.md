# 119 古龙《七种武器》之长生剑：再锋利的剑，也比不上笑

## Summary

This [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] episode introduces [[SevenWeaponsGuLong|《七种武器》]] and retells [[LongevitySwordGuLong|《长生剑》]] as a nested contest of false treasure, concealed allegiance, betrayal, and psychological inference. Although [[GuLong|古龙]] names “笑” as the weapon stronger than the titular sword, the hosts argue that the plot more consistently demonstrates how desire, vanity, trust, and overconfidence can make people cooperate in their own deception. They enjoy the compact suspense and reversals while questioning the Green Dragon Society's sale plan, several character decisions, and whether the final allegory is adequately earned.

## Key Claims

- [[SevenWeaponsGuLong|《七种武器》]] is presented as six generally accepted independent stories sharing a Jianghu setting and the Green Dragon Society, with each physical weapon ultimately displaced by a psychological or moral capacity.
- In [[LongevitySwordGuLong|《长生剑》]], the stolen Peacock Feather blueprint is itself counterfeit. Its power comes from the participants' belief that a treasure acquired through secrecy, bloodshed, and difficulty must be genuine and valuable.
- [[GreedDrivenNarrativeTrap|Greed-driven self-entrapment]] explains the episode's “cicada and oriole” pattern: each faction expects to exploit the others, yet greed and self-confidence make nearly every would-be winner part of someone else's plan.
- Bai Yujing defeats Fang Longxiang by making him believe that the sword-hilt hiding place is his own deduction. The scene treats self-authored inference as more persuasive than information supplied by an opponent.
- Yuan Zixia's turn against Wei Tianying remains deliberately ambiguous. Love may motivate it, but she may also recognize that Bai Yujing has recovered and switch sides for survival.
- The hosts find the declared “smile conquers hearts” theme less fully integrated than the story's sustained mechanisms of charm, apparent weakness, emotional leverage, and calculation.
- Reversal density produces both momentum and fatigue: repeated identity and loyalty shifts keep the plot moving but teach the audience to distrust every apparent resolution.

## Key Quotes

> “第一种武器并不是剑，而是笑。” - the story's explicit closing allegory as summarized by the episode.

> “人人都以为自己是黄雀，最终却发现自己是蝉。” - the hosts' stronger summary of the plot's recurring self-entrapment.
