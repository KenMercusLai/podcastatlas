# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》841丨绝不手软！揭秘战争的残酷真相

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 155 CE layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-842-shenme-shi-haoguan-ta-wei-baixing-gan-pinming-lj2okcbcvcmrfqrj3d-k1ylqdi2si|Hanji 842]]. It uses [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] autumn raids and [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s spring attacks to frame war as a problem of season, grain, horses, population continuity, and livestock reproduction rather than battlefield courage alone.

The episode then introduces [[ZhangHuanLateHan|张奂]] through a Qiang-frontier gift-refusal story. Zhang refuses gifts from Qiang leaders while fighting them, which the host reads as both integrity and long-range authority: by not taking immediate benefit, he keeps himself unentangled and preserves superior bargaining position.

## Key Claims

- [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] autumn raids are explained through [[SeasonalWarLogistics|战争季节后勤]]: autumn brings harvest grain on the Han side and strong horses on the Xiongnu side, making winter provisioning part of the campaign calendar.
- [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s spring attacks on Xiongnu are presented as a brutal [[ReproductiveAttritionStrategy|繁衍打击战略]] aimed at pregnant women, childbearing continuity, and spring livestock breeding, not only at current combatants.
- The episode treats war as economics and logistics: armies often stop because grain runs out, and campaign timing can matter as much as tactical violence.
- The reproductive-attrition reading is morally harsh even in the host's account; it explains strategic cruelty without normalizing it as ordinary victory language.
- [[ZhangHuanLateHan|张奂]]'s refusal of Qiang gifts is presented as [[GiftRefusalAsAuthority|拒礼立威]]: no small profit, no entanglement, and more room to negotiate or command later.
- The transcript's forms 黄腐归, 张焕, 段九, and 梁州三民 are best treated as ASR variants for [[HuangfuGuiLateHan|皇甫规]], [[ZhangHuanLateHan|张奂]], [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]], and [[LiangzhouLateHan|凉州]]'s later "三明" frontier cohort.

## Key Quotes

> "秋马肥健" - phrase used to explain why Xiongnu raids often come when horses are strongest.

> "匈奴孕重堕殰" - cited phrase behind the episode's spring-campaign reproductive-attrition reading.

> "不图小利者必有大谋" - host's maxim for Zhang Huan's refusal of Qiang gifts.

> "粮尽而回" - compact expression for war's grain-bound tempo.
