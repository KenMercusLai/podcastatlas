# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1026丨揭秘三国第一猛将吕布的落幕

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode closes [[LyuBu|吕布]]'s late-Han arc at [[Xiapi|下邳]]: [[CaoCao|曹操]] besieges him, subordinates turn, and [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s warning helps turn Lü Bu's surrender bid into execution. The second half follows Cao Cao's settlement policy after the victory, contrasting the execution of Lü Bu, [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]], and [[GaoShun|高顺]] with the absorption of [[ZhangLiao|张辽]], [[ChenJiLateHan|陈纪]], [[ChenQun|陈群]], [[ZhangBaLateHan|张霸]], and former defectors such as [[XuXiLateHan|徐翕]] and [[MaoHuiLateHan|毛晖]]. The host reads this as [[StrategicDefectorIncorporation|纳叛安抚式整合]] before the looming [[YuanShao|袁绍]] and [[GuanduCampaign|官渡]] field.

## Key Claims
- In 198 CE, Cao Cao besieges Lü Bu at Xiapi and uses water pressure against the city, while Wei Xu and other subordinates turn against Lü Bu from inside.
- Lü Bu tries to survive by offering himself as Cao Cao's cavalry commander, arguing that Cao can command infantry while he commands cavalry.
- Liu Bei blocks that survival path by reminding Cao Cao of Lü Bu's earlier betrayals of [[DingYuanLateHan|丁原]] and [[DongZhuo|董卓]], after which Cao Cao orders Lü Bu strangled.
- The episode uses [[ChenShou|陈寿]] and [[WangFuzhi|王夫之]] to reinforce the final judgment that Lü Bu had extraordinary martial force but lacked durable strategy, loyalty, and political trustworthiness.
- Chen Gong tells Cao Cao that Lü Bu failed because he did not follow Chen Gong's advice, then goes calmly to execution after Cao Cao says he will care for Chen Gong's family.
- Gao Shun is executed with Chen Gong, closing the earlier branch in which Lü Bu repeatedly underused a loyal and disciplined commander.
- Cao Cao treats Chen Ji and Chen Qun politely after the surrender, using the elite family channel as part of the post-Xiapi settlement.
- Zhang Liao leads a surrender and receives appointment as zhonglangjiang, showing that Cao Cao separates usable military capacity from Lü Bu's personal unreliability.
- Zhang Ba becomes the key local-force broker after hiding from Cao Cao's bounty; Cao Cao uses him to bring Wu Dun, Yin Li, Sun Guan, and nearby groups into submission.
- Zhang Ba refuses to hand over Xu Xi and Mao Hui, former Cao subordinates who had betrayed during the Yanzhou crisis, but Cao Cao praises the refusal as old-style loyalty and employs them instead.
- The source reads Cao Cao's appointments of Zhang Ba, Xu Xi, Mao Hui, and other local actors as an anti-revenge settlement signal: surrender and even past betrayal can be survivable if the actor accepts Cao's hierarchy.
- This eastern settlement is presented as preparation for the later Yuan Shao/Yuan Tan confrontation, because absorbing the Taishan and Shandong local-force field reduces Cao Cao's exposure before Guandu.

## Key Quotes
> "真正的强者懂得藏锋守拙" - opening frame for the episode's strength-and-restraint lesson.

> "天下之至柔，驰骋天下之至坚" - Daoist-inflected formula the host uses to contrast hard force with flexible control.
