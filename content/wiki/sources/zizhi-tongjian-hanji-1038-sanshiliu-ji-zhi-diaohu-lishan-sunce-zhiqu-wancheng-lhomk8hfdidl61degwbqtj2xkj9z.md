# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1038丨三十六计之调虎离山：孙策智取皖城

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 199 CE Jiangdong-Lujiang branch immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1039-dongwu-diyi-zuipao-qie-kan-ta-ruhe-quanjiang-huaxin-lhre-ih73ynzfwujo0lnxyjdguyd|Hanji 1039]]. [[SunCe|孙策]] uses gifts, humility, and the promise of support to draw [[LiuXunLateHan|刘勋]] out toward Shangliao, while [[LiuYeLateHan|刘晔 / 刘业]] correctly warns that the move will expose Liu Xun's rear.

The core contribution is a named [[LureEnemyOutOfPosition|调虎离山 / 诱敌离位]] case: Sun Ce avoids a direct assault on strong [[LujiangCommandery|庐江郡]], waits until Liu Xun's main force is away, and then takes [[WanChengLujiang|皖城]] with [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]]. The episode then follows Sun Ce's defeat of Liu Xun, pressure on [[HuangZu|黄祖]], and move toward [[YuzhangCommandery|豫章郡]], while preserving the host's broad but source-scoped claim that war reallocates survival space by force.

## Key Claims
- The opening self-knowledge frame prepares the Liu Xun case: Liu Xun's failure is treated as overconfidence plus refusal to hear a capable adviser's warning.
- After [[YuanShu|袁术]] dies, Sun Ce treats Liu Xun's Lujiang position as too strong for a simple frontal attack.
- Sun Ce learns that Liu Xun's relative Liu Xie is struggling to gather grain at Shangliao, then sends Liu Xun gifts and deferential language that make the expedition look profitable and low-risk.
- Liu Ye warns that Shangliao is fortified and hard to take quickly, and that Sun Ce will likely attack if Liu Xun's main force leaves the rear empty.
- Liu Xun ignores the warning, reaches Haihun, and finds Shangliao's people and goods already gone.
- Sun Ce has [[SunBenLateHan|孙贲]] and [[SunFuLateHan|孙辅]] guard Pengze, while he and Zhou Yu attack Wan City with a larger force and seize Liu Xun's base.
- Sun Ce captures Yuan Shu and Liu Xun's family members, population, and ships, then assigns Li Shu to hold Lujiang with a garrison.
- Liu Xun is beaten again while returning toward Pengze and then after seeking help from Huang Zu; he eventually flees north to [[CaoCao|曹操]].
- Sun Ce continues the offensive against Huang Zu at Shaxian, defeating support sent by [[LiuBiao|刘表]] and capturing Huang Zu's family and ships.
- The closing Yuzhang setup shows Sun Ce mixing military confidence with lower-cost persuasion: he sends [[YuFan|虞翻]] to prepare [[HuaXin|华歆]]'s surrender rather than immediately forcing a city battle.
