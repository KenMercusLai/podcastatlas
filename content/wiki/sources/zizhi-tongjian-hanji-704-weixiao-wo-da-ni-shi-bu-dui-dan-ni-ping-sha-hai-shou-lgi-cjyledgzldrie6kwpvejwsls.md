# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》704丨隗嚣：我打你是不对，但你凭啥还手？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the pre-Hanji 705-1 Guangwu western layer around [[WeiXiaoEasternHan|隗嚣]] after defeat. It follows [[MaYuanEasternHan|马援]] warning [[YangGuangWeiXiaoRetainer|杨广]] that Wei Xiao's two-commandery position cannot survive against the rest of the realm, Wei Xiao's apologetic but conditional letter to [[LiuXiu|刘秀]], Liu Xiu's refusal to kill Wei Xun and his counteroffer of title preservation through restraint and another hostage, and Wei Xiao's eventual turn to [[GongsunShuEasternHan|公孙述]].

The closing widens the same moment into early northern frontier pressure: [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] and [[LuFangEasternHan|卢芳]] continue raiding while Liu Xiu sends Guide Hou Liu Sa to seek renewed [[HanXiongnuHeqinPolicy|Han-Xiongnu heqin]] relations. The durable synthesis is that [[FaceSavingSubmissionFailure|face-saving conditional surrender]] can destroy a viable exit: Wei Xiao tries to apologize without truly submitting, and Liu Xiu reads the test as non-credible.

## Key Claims

- Wei Xiao's self-justification after defeat is framed as inability to accept subordination, not as absence of any exit.
- Ma Yuan's letter to Yang Guang uses territorial arithmetic and dual obligation to urge remonstrance or departure: Wei Xiao holds two commanderies against the rest of the realm, while Yang Guang has both ruler-minister and private friendship duties to speak.
- Yang Guang's nonresponse keeps him source-bounded but shows how risk inside Wei Xiao's camp can block open remonstrance.
- Wei Xiao's apology letter claims臣节 while recasting resistance as self-protection and temporary evasion.
- Liu Xiu refuses to kill Wei Xun and instead offers to preserve Wei Xiao's title and stipend if Wei Xiao restrains himself and sends Wei Xun's younger brother as hostage.
- Wei Xiao turns to Gongsun Shu once he understands that Liu Xiu will not accept a face-saving but noncommittal apology.
- Xiongnu and Lu Fang raiding persists, so Liu Sa's envoy shows heqin as constrained frontier diplomacy under pressure rather than settled peace.

## Key Quotes

> "死鸭子嘴硬" - the host's opening character frame for Wei Xiao after defeat.

> "大棍打就跑，小棍打就承受" - Wei Xiao's Shun analogy for self-protective evasion.

> "如果隗嚣不愿接受条件，就不用答复。" - Liu Xiu's no-more-testing reply frame.
