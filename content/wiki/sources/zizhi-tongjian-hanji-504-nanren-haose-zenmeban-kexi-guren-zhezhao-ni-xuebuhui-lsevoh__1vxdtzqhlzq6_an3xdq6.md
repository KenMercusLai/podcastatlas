# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》504丨男人好色怎么办？可惜古人这招你学不会

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianshi 2 / 31 BCE under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]], between the Wang-family elevation of [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-503-gei-wangjia-zhanglian-jiemi-xihan-shili-zuidade-waiqi-lgjgpal0s0he4qrwwhdrdbmp8aut|Hanji 503]] and the flood-rumor / harem-omen branch of [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-505-xihan-de-huicheng-yaoyan-da-le-shui-de-lian-ljuwccxx_aexasafkpbl-y2rvos5|Hanji 505]]. It centers [[DuQinWesternHan|杜钦]]'s advice to [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]] that an ancient "nine wives" rule could limit Chengdi's harem expansion before desire, jealousy, and heir politics hardened into court disorder.

The second half turns to [[HuhanyeChanyuWesternHan|呼韩邪单于]]'s succession settlement among Xiongnu consorts and sons. The durable synthesis is that the episode treats sexual favor, consort hierarchy, and heirs as public-order problems: Du Qin tries to regulate Chengdi's harem through ritual precedent, while Huhanye chooses [[FuzhuleiRuodiChanyu|雕陶莫皋]] and requires future transfer to 且莫车 as a stabilizing compromise.

## Key Claims

- The host frames Chengdi's reputation for desire as politically important because an emperor's private appetite can become a public succession and harem-governance risk.
- Du Qin argues that ancient ritual limited the Son of Heaven's first marriage to nine women so the ruler could have sons without endlessly expanding the harem.
- The advice is preventive: because Chengdi is young, childless, and still early in his reign, Du Qin wants a rule before habit, faction, and favorite attachment become impossible to control.
- Du Qin links abandoned ritual to jealousy inside the palace, possible rivalry against the empress, and heir instability, citing earlier succession-disorder stories as warnings.
- Wang Feng relays Du Qin's proposal to the dowager, but the dowager rejects it because Han has no such precedent and Wang Feng cannot create law on his own authority.
- Du Qin and Wang Feng are presented as mutually useful: Wang Feng needs competent advisers to compensate for limited ability, while Du Qin needs Wang Feng's position to make his proposals matter.
- Huhanye Chanyu's consort field repeats the succession theme from another political system: two yanzhi offer different stability arguments, one favoring the young beloved son, the other favoring an older candidate who can command confidence.
- Huhanye chooses Diaotao Mogao as successor and makes him promise future transfer to Qiemo Che, trying to balance age, maternal status, affection, and Xiongnu political stability.
- After Huhanye's death, Diaotao Mogao becomes Fuzhulei Ruodi Chanyu and marries [[WangZhaojun|王昭君]] according to Xiongnu custom.

## Key Quotes

> "男人好色怎么办？" - the episode's title frame for turning private desire into a political problem.

> "趁着现在皇上年纪还小，没有嫡子" - Du Qin's timing logic as summarized by the episode.
