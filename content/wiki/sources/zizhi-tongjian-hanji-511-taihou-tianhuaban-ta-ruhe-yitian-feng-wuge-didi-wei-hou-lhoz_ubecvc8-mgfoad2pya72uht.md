# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》511丨“太后”天花板！她如何一天封五个弟弟为侯？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Heping 2 / 27 BCE under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]], after the harem and Wang-family pressure of Hanji 509 and before the Ye Lang frontier sequence in Hanji 512-513. It pairs [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]'s expansion of natal-family privilege, especially the same-day enfeoffment of the [[ChengdiFiveMarquisesWesternHan|成帝五侯]], with [[WangZunWesternHan|王尊]]'s impeachment and partial rehabilitation through [[GongchengXingWesternHan|公乘兴]]'s memorial.

The durable synthesis is that Chengdi-era politics tests two forms of judgment at once: kinship privilege can become public status before formal regency appears, while official impeachment needs a way to distinguish real misconduct from private resentment and manufactured charges.

## Key Claims

- Chu receives unusually large hail in 27 BCE, and the host uses disaster-omen comparison as historical framing without treating the omen as independently proven causation.
- [[LiuKangDingtaoKingWesternHan|刘康]] is moved from Shanyang king to Dingtao king, creating an early Dingtao-line marker before later succession politics.
- [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] does not formally rule like [[WuZetian|武则天]], but the episode presents her as exercising influence through [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] for Wang-family benefit.
- Chengdi enfeoffs [[WangTanWesternHan|王谭]], [[WangShangWesternHan|王商]], [[WangLiWesternHan|王立]], [[WangGenWesternHan|王根]], and [[WangFengMarquisWesternHan|王逢]] on the same day, creating the [[ChengdiFiveMarquisesWesternHan|成帝五侯]] reward bloc.
- Wang Zhengjun also wants [[GouCanWesternHan|苟参]], her half-brother through her mother Li's remarriage to [[GouBinWesternHan|苟宾]], made marquis by analogy to Tian Fen, but Chengdi refuses the marquisate and gives only offices.
- [[ZhangZhongWesternHan|张忠]] impeaches [[WangZunWesternHan|王尊]] as cruel and arrogant, causing Wang Zun's dismissal.
- [[GongchengXingWesternHan|公乘兴]] argues that the impeachment came from [[YangFuWesternHan|杨辅]], who had private resentment against Wang Zun, and asks the court to review both Wang Zun's conduct and the accusers' responsibility.
- Chengdi responds by appointing Wang Zun inspector of [[Xuzhou|徐州]], making the result a partial restoration rather than full vindication.

## Key Quotes

> "能受天磨真铁汉，不遭人忌是庸才" - the opening maxim used to introduce slander, jealousy, and tested character.
