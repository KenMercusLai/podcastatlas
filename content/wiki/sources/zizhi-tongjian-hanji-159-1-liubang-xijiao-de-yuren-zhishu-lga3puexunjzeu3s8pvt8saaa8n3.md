# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》159-1｜刘邦洗脚的驭人之术

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-158-koucai-hao-de-tianhuaban-yiren-ding-baiwan-dajun-lnup-vmw-ff-nqmuh6-x8si0k8zu|Hanji 158]] from [[YingBu|英布]]'s forced break with [[XiangYu|项羽]] into his arrival at [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s camp. Liu Bang receives Ying Bu while having his feet washed, then gives him lodging and attendants at Han-king level, which the episode reads as [[StatusSuppressionThenEqualTreatment|先挫锐气后同等厚待]] rather than simple rudeness. The second half shifts to the [[XingyangStandoff|荥阳相持]]: [[LongJuChuGeneral|龙且]] restores Jiujiang for Chu, Chu captures [[Aocang|敖仓]] and attacks Han's food corridor, and Liu Bang nearly accepts [[LiShiqi|郦食其]]'s plan to restore the six kingdoms before [[ZhangLiang|张良]] returns and stops it.

## Key Claims
- Ying Bu's defection remains politically valuable but personally delicate: he has just lost in Jiujiang, his family is killed by the Chu side, and he arrives as a proud, high-merit king rather than as an ordinary refugee.
- Liu Bang's foot-washing reception humiliates Ying Bu at the first meeting, making him regret defecting and even want to die, but the matching residence, vessels, and attendants reverse the emotional reading.
- The episode summarizes Liu Bang's method as first lowering the newcomer's arrogance, then showing concrete sincerity and reliable reward. It compares the pattern to his earlier foot-washing meeting with Li Shiqi, but inverts the result: Li Shiqi rebukes Liu Bang into courtesy, while Ying Bu is first pressed downward and then materially honored.
- After Ying Bu reaches Han, Liu Bang gives him troops and holds around Chenggao, turning the Jiujiang defection from a diplomatic break into a usable military attachment.
- The military situation worsens anyway. Long Ju defeats Ying Bu's remaining Jiujiang base, then joins Xiang Yu near Xingyang; Chu takes Aocang, controls Yellow River transport, and repeatedly attacks the Han food corridor.
- Liu Bang's acceptance of Li Shiqi's six-kingdom restoration plan is framed as panic under logistical pressure, not as settled strategy.
- Zhang Liang's return interrupts the plan after the seals have been prepared. The transcript stops as Zhang Liang asks to borrow chopsticks for his analysis, so the full "borrowing chopsticks" refutation is left for a later installment.

## Key Quotes
> "先挫其锐气，后推心置腹" - the episode's formula for Liu Bang's treatment of Ying Bu.

> "馊主意" - Zhang Liang's immediate judgment on the plan to restore the six kingdoms.
