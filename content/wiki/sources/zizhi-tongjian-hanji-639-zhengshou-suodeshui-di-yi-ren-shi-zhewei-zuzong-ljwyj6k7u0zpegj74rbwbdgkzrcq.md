# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》639丨征收所得税第一人，是这位祖宗！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills a 10 CE [[WangMang|王莽]] policy-and-frontier layer between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-638-xuemai-qinqing-keyi-you-duo-wuqing-lv-gtknrhlipsmrtt3c4d7gcr-pc|Hanji 638]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-640-qipa-wangmang-ruhe-chedi-bifan-xiongnu-lhxbx6keibmfe8z6bcsmlpnr6eht|Hanji 640]]. It presents idle-land, treeless-residence, and non-work penalties alongside a levy on declared net occupational profit, a liquor-monopoly proposal, and civilian crossbow-and-armor restrictions, then moves to Wuhuan-Xiongnu tribute violence and the Xin mission that exchanges the chanyu's seal.

The durable synthesis separates the episode's modern “first income tax” hook from its stronger institutional evidence. The source describes [[ProfitBasedOccupationalTax|按净利计征的职业所得税]] across mining, hunting, fishing, herding, textile work, crafts, medicine, divination, and petty trade, but it does not establish priority across world tax history. Its frontier branch shows the prelude to [[SymbolicStatusDowngradeBackfire|symbolic status downgrade backfire]]: the Xin envoys withhold the new seal wording, the chanyu reluctantly hands over the old seal, and [[ChenRaoXinEnvoy|陈饶]] destroys it before inspection can reopen the exchange.

## Key Claims

- Wang Mang's court penalizes owners of uncultivated land, urban households without planted trees, and able-bodied people represented as idle or non-working.
- A wide range of occupational earners must declare revenue and costs to local government and pay one tenth of the stated net profit; noncompliance brings confiscation and one year of official labor in the episode's account.
- The source presents this as an early income-tax analogue, but the “first person to levy income tax” label is a host framing rather than a demonstrated global-priority finding.
- Lu Kuang's liquor-monopoly proposal and the ban on privately held crossbows and armor place fiscal collection beside direct commodity control, labor discipline, and civilian disarmament.
- After Xin tells Wuhuan to stop making tribute payments to Xiongnu, Xiongnu coercion, the killing of its envoy, retaliatory attack, captive-taking, and failed ransom transfer widen the dispute.
- [[WangJunXinGeneral|王骏]] and the Xin mission conceal the consequential wording change from “匈奴单于玺” to “新匈奴单于章” while obtaining the old seal.
- [[ChenRaoXinEnvoy|陈饶]] destroys the old seal to prevent the chanyu from reversing the exchange once the downgrade is discovered, turning diplomatic concealment into an irreversible act.

## Key Quotes

> "除其本，计其利，十一分之" - the episode's cited basis for deducting costs and levying one tenth of profit.

> "匈奴单于玺" - the old seal wording contrasted with the Xin-issued “新匈奴单于章.”

> "以斧破之" - Chen Rao's destruction of the old seal to prevent its return.
