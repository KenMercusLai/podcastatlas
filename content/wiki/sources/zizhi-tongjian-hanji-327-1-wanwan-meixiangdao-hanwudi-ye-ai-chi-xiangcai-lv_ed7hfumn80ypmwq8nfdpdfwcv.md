# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》327-1｜万万没想到，汉武帝也爱吃香菜

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode gives the strategic and political aftermath of the [[MobeiBattleWesternHan|漠北之战]]. It presents the campaign as an extraordinary Han victory that drove [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] power north and produced the “漠南无王庭” situation, while also reporting nearly thirty thousand Han casualties, more than one hundred thousand horses lost, immense material consumption, and an ensuing pause in large-scale offensive war.

The second half follows a shift in court patronage. [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]] receives no new reward after failing to destroy the chanyu's main force, while [[HuoQubing|霍去病]] becomes increasingly prominent; former associates move from Wei's household toward Huo in search of office and benefit. [[RenAnWesternHan|任安]] is singled out as the person who continues to visit Wei, making the episode a case of both [[PowerContingentSocialTies|权势依附型交往]] and loyalty that persists after advantage declines.

## Key Claims

- The episode credits the combined Wei Qing–Huo Qubing Mobei campaign with an unprecedented Han victory and with pushing Xiongnu power north of the desert.
- It aggregates Wei Qing's seven campaigns at more than fifty thousand Xiongnu killed or captured and Huo Qubing's six at more than one hundred ten thousand, then estimates total Xiongnu mounted losses above two hundred thousand; these figures remain episode-attributed.
- Han victory is presented as materially exhausting: nearly thirty thousand soldiers and more than one hundred thousand horses are said to have been lost, alongside very large supply expenditure.
- The episode says the Han army largely stopped major offensive operations against Xiongnu after the campaign, treating victory and depleted follow-on capacity as the same strategic outcome.
- Wei Qing and the officers around him receive no comparable reward because the chanyu's main force was not destroyed, while Huo Qubing's favor and prestige increase.
- Many of Wei Qing's former friends and retainers reportedly transfer their allegiance to Huo Qubing because Huo can provide appointments or material advantage.
- Ren An alone is said to continue visiting Wei Qing; the host connects this persistence to Sima Qian's praise of his refusal to bend toward the powerful.
- The opening says Zhang Qian brought coriander, sesame, carrots, and other goods from the Western Regions and that Han Wudi especially liked coriander, but the preference is explicitly introduced as hearsay.

## Key Quotes

> "漠南无王庭。" - the episode's formula for the strategic displacement after Mobei.

> "只有任安还始终出入于卫青的门下。" - the episode's contrast between durable loyalty and patronage migration.

## Connections

- [[MobeiBattleWesternHan|漠北之战]] - victory whose strategic displacement and severe human, animal, and material costs are synthesized together.
- [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]] - victorious commander whose incomplete operational objective is followed by no new reward and declining household attraction.
- [[HuoQubing|霍去病]] - increasingly favored commander who becomes the alternative destination for office-seeking retainers.
- [[RenAnWesternHan|任安]] - former Chang'an envoy in Wei Qing's camp who continues the relationship after Wei's political position weakens.
- [[PowerContingentSocialTies|权势依附型交往]] - framework for the movement of associates toward the patron with greater current appointment power.
- [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] and [[ZhangQianWesternHan|张骞]] - ruler and envoy used in the opening food-history hook, with the coriander preference kept as hearsay.
- [[SimaQian|司马迁]] and [[Shiji|《史记》]] - later moral-memory frame for Ren An's refusal to follow changing power.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with existing wiki content was found. The source reinforces the existing conclusion that Mobei was a decisive but capacity-consuming victory and adds its immediate patronage aftermath.
- Hanji 325-1 says Wei Qing defeated and dispersed the chanyu's main force but did not capture the chanyu; this episode's “未完成剿灭主力” language is therefore treated as an unmet annihilation objective, not as denial that Wei won his arm of the battle.
- The episode's casualty, campaign, horse, and percentage totals are aggregated in a short secondary account and are not independently verified here. “汉军基本停止用兵” is preserved as a large-offensive pause rather than a claim that all Han-Xiongnu military activity ceased permanently.
- Han Wudi's love of coriander is introduced as “据传.” The named Western Regions foods, their exact introduction route, and the claim that Zhang Qian personally brought each one require separate historical verification.
- The source summary renders rewarded officers as Chang Hui and Sui Cheng, but the names, offices, rewards, chronology, and transcription are not secure enough here to create new identities or settled claims.
- The claim that every departing retainer acted only for office or money, Ren An was literally the sole exception, and Sima Qian's brief notice proves all private motives remains source-scoped.
