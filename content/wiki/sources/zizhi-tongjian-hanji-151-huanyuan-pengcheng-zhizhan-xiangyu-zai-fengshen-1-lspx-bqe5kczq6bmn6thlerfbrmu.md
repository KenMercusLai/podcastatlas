# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》151｜还原彭城之战：项羽再封神（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens the [[PengchengBattle|彭城之战]] branch by explaining the political and strategic setup before the famous battle itself. [[DongGongXincheng|新城董公]] gives [[LiuBang|刘邦]] the argument that [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s killing of Yi Emperor [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]] can be turned into a public mourning rite and punitive call to the lords. The episode then follows Liu Bang's coalition-building through [[ChenYu|陈馀]], the northern cleanup after [[SimaAng|司马卬]]'s defeat, [[TianHeng|田横]]'s renewed Qi resistance around [[JuWarringStates|莒城 / 城阳]], and finally Liu Bang's capture of [[Xuzhou|彭城]], where victory quickly becomes indulgence and lowered vigilance.

## Key Claims
- The episode frames Pengcheng as the first large Chu-Han battle and states the later result: Xiang Yu's smaller elite force will break Liu Bang's much larger coalition, but this installment stops before the tactical battle is narrated.
- Dong Gong's advice turns Yi Emperor Mi Xin's murder from an existing grievance into a usable [[MoralizedWarPretext|道德化战争借口]]: Liu Bang should mourn publicly, name Xiang Yu a regicidal rebel, and call the lords to punish him.
- Liu Bang's mourning ritual matters because it reduces the moral cost for lords who already have reasons to leave Xiang Yu's order; opposition can now be styled as punishing disorder rather than opportunistic defection.
- Liu Bang secures Zhao participation through a deception: he preserves [[ZhangEr|张耳]] but sends [[ChenYu|陈馀]] a substitute head to satisfy Chen Yu's demand for Zhang Er's death.
- After Liu Bang captures [[HeneiCommandery|河内郡]], the Yellow River north becomes much less favorable to Xiang Yu, while Chen Yu's Zhao controls Handan and Julu areas.
- [[TianHeng|田横]] gathers Qi remnants, installs [[TianGuangQiKing|田广]] as Qi king, and holds out near Chengyang/Ju, keeping Xiang Yu tied down in Qi.
- The source uses the Chengyang/Ju note to connect this Chu-Han moment with older Qi survival memory: Ju had been one of the last anti-Yan holdouts after Le Yi's conquest.
- Liu Bang captures Pengcheng while Xiang Yu's main force is away in Qi, but the capture immediately produces the source's warning sign: Liu Bang and his soldiers indulge in treasure, women, drinking, and celebration.
- Even [[ZhangLiang|张良]] and [[ChenPing|陈平]] are described as momentarily silent inside the sudden victory mood, marking the failure as broader than one man's pleasure-seeking.
- Xiang Yu's reaction is immediate anger and rapid movement, but the source ends at the moment he starts responding.

## Key Quotes
> "顺德者昌，逆德者亡" - Dong Gong's moral-political frame for why the campaign needs a public name.

> "收其货宝美人，日置酒高会" - the source's cited record of Liu Bang's conduct after entering Pengcheng.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is recorded. The episode explicitly limits itself to the pre-battle setup and the moment Xiang Yu learns of Pengcheng's fall, so the famous three-ten-thousand-versus-fifty-six-ten-thousand battle process remains for a later source.
