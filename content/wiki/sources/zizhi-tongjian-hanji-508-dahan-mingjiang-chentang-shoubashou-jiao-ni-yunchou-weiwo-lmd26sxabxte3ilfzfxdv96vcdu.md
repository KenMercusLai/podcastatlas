# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》508丨大汉名将陈汤，手把手教你运筹帷幄

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianshi 4 / 29 BCE under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]. When Western Regions protector [[DuanHuizongWesternHan|段会宗]] reports that [[Wusun|乌孙]] forces have surrounded him and asks for relief troops, the court remains undecided until [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]] recommends consulting [[ChenTangWesternHan|陈汤]].

The durable synthesis is that Chen Tang's apparently prophetic forecast is a structured military judgment. He combines comparative combat quality, an attacker-to-defender ratio, the speed and mobilization delay of possible relief forces, local knowledge, and accumulated experience to conclude that Wusun cannot sustain the siege and that news of relief should arrive within five days; the report arrives on the fourth. The episode preserves the moral qualification that strategic competence did not remove Chen Tang's bribery problem or later demotion.

## Key Claims

- Duan Huizong reports that Wusun forces have surrounded him and requests troops from Western Regions states and Dunhuang, but the chancellor, Wang Feng, and hundreds of officials cannot quickly decide.
- Chen Tang judges that Wusun weapons and bows are inferior and that the older rule of five Hu soldiers to one Han soldier has narrowed only to about three to one.
- He applies an attack-to-defense rule requiring roughly twice the defenders' strength and concludes that the surrounding force is insufficient to defeat Duan.
- He also calculates that light troops can travel about fifty li per day and heavy troops about thirty, so a newly mobilized relief force would arrive too late to rescue the immediate situation.
- Chen Tang treats the Wusun force as a loose coalition unable to sustain a long attack and predicts that the siege is probably already over.
- A frontier report arrives on the fourth day confirming that Duan has escaped the siege, and Wang Feng asks that Chen Tang again be used for military judgment.
- The host attributes the result to experience, regional knowledge, and military regularities rather than divination, while retaining Chen Tang's later bribery and demotion as a character boundary.

## Key Quotes

> "不出五日就能听到前线好消息" - Chen Tang's time-bounded forecast after assessing force quality, siege capacity, and communication delay.

> "运筹帷幄，决胜千里之外" - the episode's frame for judgment that shapes action far from the battlefield.
