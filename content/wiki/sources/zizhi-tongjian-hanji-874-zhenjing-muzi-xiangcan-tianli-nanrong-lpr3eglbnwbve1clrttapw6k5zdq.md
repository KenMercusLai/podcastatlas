# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》874丨震惊：母子相残，天理难容！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 166 CE first Party Prohibition trigger before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-875-li-dapu-weihe-ta-yi-ruyu-wei-rong-lgffkjk-0yu-ehfsw59anoych-ld|Hanji 875]], [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-876-zhichang-zong-pengbi-shuoming-ni-meiyou-zhaodui-guanxi-lrjob-a07t29hdr0iogrmcsjr7qp|Hanji 876]], and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-877-ta-weihe-lengluo-jiuming-enren-loofs4rugl-q8kunfb-jpj5mgvhi|Hanji 877]]. It pairs [[JiaBiaoLateHan|贾彪]]'s timing-and-collateral judgment and local infanticide enforcement with [[ZhangChengAstrologerLateHan|张成]]'s amnesty-gaming murder case, [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]]'s execution of Zhang Cheng father and son, the eunuch-backed [[LaoXiuLateHan|劳修]] accusation, [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]]'s refusal to sign vague arrest orders, and the prison conduct of [[ChenShiLateHan|陈寔]] and [[FanPangLateHan|范滂]].

The core source contribution is to show how private grievance, legal abuse, reputation networks, and court access become a political persecution chain. Local moral-order enforcement and criminal accountability are not separate from Party Prohibition politics; they become the pretext field through which eunuch retaliation can recode士人 association as disorder.

## Key Claims

- The opening "借势借力" frame is tested by the historical material: effective action requires timing, leverage, and attention to who else may be implicated.
- Jia Biao refuses to shelter Chen Zhi because Chen Zhi's own coercive action helped create the disaster and should not drag others into liability.
- As Xinxi magistrate, Jia Biao forbids infanticide, treats child-killing as homicide, prioritizes a mother-child killing case as a deeper moral-order breach than ordinary bandit murder, and changes local custom.
- Zhang Cheng uses astrological amnesty prediction to let his son kill, and Li Ying executes Zhang Cheng father and son despite the general amnesty.
- Zhang Cheng's eunuch ties turn a criminal case into political retaliation when his disciple Lao Xiu accuses Li Ying and other士人 of party formation, mutual praise, court slander, and disordering customs.
- Emperor Huan authorizes arrests, while Chen Fan refuses to endorse vague charges and torture-oriented prosecution.
- Li Ying's testimony implicates roughly 200 people; Chen Shi voluntarily enters prison to steady others, and Fan Pang refuses ritual worship of Gao Yao because ritual cannot substitute for justice.

## Key Quotes

> "相时而动，无累后人" - Jia Biao's timing-and-collateral principle in the fugitive-shelter scene.

> "母亲杀亲生儿子违背天道" - the source's moral-order explanation for Jia Biao prioritizing the infanticide case.

> "如果高瑶知道自己无罪，自会替他向天地申诉" - Fan Pang's refusal to treat prison ritual as a substitute for justice.
