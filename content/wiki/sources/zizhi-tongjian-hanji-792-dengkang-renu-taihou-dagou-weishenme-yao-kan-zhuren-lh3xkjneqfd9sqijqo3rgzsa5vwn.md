## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 120 CE Yongning opening before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-793-lishi-zhuming-nvhuang-dengsui-zenme-si-de-lszfgj7waiuusrddzjmv5mbrp1i|Hanji 793]]. It combines [[MaXianLateHan|马贤]]'s Qiang-frontier campaign cycle, floods, earthquakes, office changes, and foreign tribute with the court conflict around [[DengKangLateHan|邓康]], [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|和熹邓皇后]], and the [[DengFamilyAnEraLateHan|邓氏]].

The durable synthesis is that the Deng regency's danger was visible before Deng's death and purge. Deng Kang argues from inside the family that court authority should be raised and outer-relative power weakened, but his failed withdrawal and insult to a palace attendant make him a target; meanwhile [[EmperorShunOfHan|刘保]]'s crown-prince appointment already creates the [[EmpressDowagerYanLateHan|阎后]] hostility that later becomes a succession crisis.

## Key Claims
- In 120 CE, Shendi Qiang attack around Zhangye, while Ma Xian's counterattack wins killings, captures, and surrender without ending mobile Qiang pressure.
- Floods, earthquakes, a solar eclipse, Sikong turnover, Liu Kai's retirement, Yang Zhen's elevation to situ, and Shanguo tribute place the court conflict inside a wider regency-crisis field.
- Deng Kang, Empress Dowager Deng's cousin and Yueqi xiaowei, repeatedly memorializes for strengthening court authority and reducing Deng-family outer-relative power.
- Deng's refusal and anger show that internal family warning did not become a controlled power transfer or self-limitation mechanism.
- Deng Kang's feigned illness lowers exposure only temporarily; insulting the former household servant who now comes as palace attendant converts status contempt into a reportable offense against the dowager.
- Deng Kang's dismissal, return to fief, and cancellation of registration are punished as disobedience and disrespect, but the host reads the removal as a possible later safety gain once the Deng family is purged.
- Liu Bao's formal crown-prince appointment does not settle succession security because Yan Hou is already hostile to him and is said to have killed his birth mother.

## Key Quotes
> "抬高朝廷威望、削弱外戚权力" - Deng Kang's repeated memorial position as summarized in the source.

> "免去邓康官职，将他遣回封国" - the episode's punishment endpoint after the attendant reports him.

> "因祸得福" - the host's reading of Deng Kang's removal as possible protection before the Deng purge.
