# 《资治通鉴·周纪》73丨历史上自由恋爱的开创者是谁？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the 284 BCE Qi-collapse branch after [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-72-shenme-yang-de-ren-cai-shi-zhongzhen-zhishi-logxegnqexgc7z-lodw6tr6-8f28|Zhouji 72]]. [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] turns from the anti-Qi settlement toward [[WeiState|魏国]], taking [[AnchengWarringStates|安城]] and threatening [[Daliang|大梁]], while [[TianFazhang|田法章]] hides in [[JuWarringStates|莒城]] at [[TaishiJiao|太史角]]'s household. The episode then follows [[WangSunGu|王孙古]]'s revenge mobilization against [[ZhuoChi|淖齿 / 卓齿]], Tian Fazhang's restoration as [[QiXiangWang|齐襄王]], and [[JunWangHou|君王后]]'s rise from crisis partner to queen.

## Key Claims
- Qin's participation in the anti-Qi coalition does not make it satisfied with treasure compensation; the episode reads Qin's attack on Wei as a renewed land-and-balance move after the coalition victory.
- [[LeYi|乐毅]]'s post-victory allocation sends distant [[QinState|秦国]] and [[HanState|韩国]] home with wealth, lets [[WeiState|魏国]] and [[ZhaoState|赵国]] take nearer gains, and leaves [[YanState|燕国]] to drive deeper into Qi.
- [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] meets [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] at Rangcheng in the same episode frame where Qin later captures [[AnchengWarringStates|安城]] and approaches [[Daliang|大梁]].
- [[TianFazhang|田法章]] survives because he hides his royal identity and accepts low-status labor in [[TaishiJiao|太史角]]'s household at [[JuWarringStates|莒城]].
- [[JunWangHou|君王后]], introduced as Taishi Jiao's daughter, notices Tian Fazhang's unusual bearing, treats him kindly, learns his identity, and enters a private relationship with him before his restoration.
- The episode's title uses "free love" for this crisis-formed relationship because it is not first arranged as interstate or dynastic marriage.
- [[WangSunGu|王孙古]] turns from discouraged follower into avenger after his mother shames him for failing to know or defend his ruler's fate.
- Wang Sun Gu's market speech and exposed-right-arm pledge convert personal retainer duty into a small public fighting group of more than four hundred young men.
- [[ZhuoChi|淖齿 / 卓齿]] is killed in the retaliatory attack, and the source says the Chu army retreats after losing its commander.
- Qi ministers in Ju seek the dead king's son; Tian Fazhang initially suspects a trap, then reveals himself and is installed as [[QiXiangWang|齐襄王]].
- Qi Xiang Wang's formal enthronement in Ju turns hidden survival into a restored royal-line resistance against Yan.
- [[JunWangHou|君王后]]'s later position is presented as a vindication of the earlier crisis relationship; the source also attributes to her a long later regency-like political role.

## Key Quotes
> "自由恋爱" - the episode's title frame for Tian Fazhang and Jun Wang Hou's non-arranged crisis relationship.

> "不义之财不能得" - host's moral summary of Zhuo Chi's quick reversal after killing Qi Min Wang.
