## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode links the failed 218 CE [[XuduRebellion218LateHan|许都之乱]], [[CaoZhangLateHan|曹彰]]'s northern campaign, and the opening phase of [[HanzhongCampaign218LateHan|218年汉中争夺]] through a shared warning against haste. ## Key Claims

- Jin Yi, Geng Ji, Wei Huang, Ji Ben, and Ji Ben's sons allegedly plan an Upper Yuan surprise attack on Wang Bi, the release of [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], and coordination with Guan Yu.
- The attack wounds Wang Bi but leaves Cao-side command and mobilization intact; Yan Kuang regroups trained troops and suppresses the scattered insurgents.
- Cao Cao reportedly purges hundreds of families and replaces people around Emperor Xian, turning investigation into political intimidation as well as punishment.
- Moving Pei Qian from Dai leaves a governance gap he had predicted; Wuhuan groups rebel, and Cao Zhang's aggressive pursuit defeats them and induces Xianbei submission.
- [[CaoHong|曹洪]] adopts Cao Xiu's inference that a genuine attempt to cut the rear would be concealed rather than loudly announced, then defeats the weaker Wu Lan wing.
- Liu Bei's renewed Hanzhong campaign becomes a sustainment problem after setbacks against [[XuHuangCaoGeneral|徐晃]] and [[ZhangHeLateHan|张郃]].
- Yang Hong argues that Hanzhong protects Yi's strategic access; Zhuge Liang mobilizes reinforcements and puts Yang Hong in charge of local administration, connecting front-line endurance to rear-area stability.
