# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》250｜甯成凭什么称为最渣的酷吏？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode reconstructs [[NingChengWesternHan|宁成]] as a Western Han harsh official whose coercive talent repeatedly becomes useful to the court. [[HanJingdi|汉景帝]] deploys him against local strongmen and later at the capital; after punishment and escape, [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] restores him despite [[GongsunHong|公孙弘]]'s warning that he governs people like a wolf tending sheep.

The episode distinguishes severity from integrity by contrasting Ning with [[ZhiDuWesternHan|郅都]] and [[ZhangTangWesternHan|张汤]]. Its durable contribution is not the host's superlative label but Ning's institutional cycle: an abusive official can be promoted because fear serves central power, discarded when protection changes, recalled when coercion is again useful, and finally return home as the kind of local strongman he once suppressed. [[YiZongWesternHan|义纵]] then destroys his household, extending [[HarshOfficialInstrumentalRule|酷吏工具化统治]] from promotion into agent transformation and replacement.

## Key Claims

- The host argues that a harsh official should be evaluated not only by severity but also by public purpose, competence, personal restraint, and treatment of both superiors and subordinates.
- Ning's appearance and palace access reportedly begin his career, while the phrase “滑贼任威” is used to characterize a pattern of undermining superiors and abusing subordinates.
- Jing deploys Ning to Jinan, where Ning and Zhi Du suppress local strongmen; Ning later succeeds Zhi Du as zhongwei but is portrayed as lacking Zhi Du's self-restraint.
- After Jing's death, Ning is dismissed and subjected to a humiliating punishment, then forges official documents and escapes to his home region rather than die to preserve elite honor.
- Gongsun Hong warns Wudi that Ning governs people like a wolf herding sheep; Wudi changes the proposed posting but still restores Ning to coercive office.
- The saying “宁见乳虎，无值宁成之怒” represents Ning's rule as fear exceeding ordinary strict enforcement.
- After leaving office, Ning becomes a powerful local magnate in Nanyang and is ultimately prosecuted with his household by Yi Zong.

## Key Quotes

> “宁见乳虎，无值宁成之怒。” - the episode's saying that one would rather meet a nursing tigress than encounter Ning Cheng's anger.

> “治民如狼牧羊。” - Gongsun Hong's attributed warning about returning Ning Cheng to authority over the population.

## Connections

- [[NingChengWesternHan|宁成]] - central figure whose career joins palace access, anti-strongman deployment, punishment, escape, restoration, public terror, and local magnate power.
- [[ZhiDuWesternHan|郅都]] - comparative harsh official whose coercion is paired in the episode with military ability and personal restraint.
- [[HanJingdi|汉景帝]] and [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] - rulers whose changing needs and protections shape Ning's removal and repeated usefulness.
- [[GongsunHong|公孙弘]] - former subordinate who warns Wudi against giving Ning direct authority over people.
- [[YiZongWesternHan|义纵]] - later harsh official who prosecutes Ning after Ning has become a local strongman.
- [[ZhangTangWesternHan|张汤]] - comparison figure used by the host to separate coercive office from personal corruption or lack of ability.
- [[HarshOfficialInstrumentalRule|酷吏工具化统治]] - institutional frame explaining why Ning's abusive capacity can be repeatedly used without becoming secure.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with the existing wiki is adopted. This episode supplies Ning's earlier career and restoration before Hanji 330 supplies the later household-destruction sequence.
- The source alternates broad geographical and office language around Jinan, the capital, “关东都尉,” and Ning's later post; titles, dates, jurisdictions, and sequence require comparison with transmitted records.
- “最渣,” the favorable comparison of Zhi Du and Zhang Tang, Ning's motives, the role of slander after Jing's death, and the shame-versus-survival interpretation are host judgments rather than independently established findings.
- The exact wording and provenance of “滑贼任威,” the mother-tiger saying, Gongsun Hong's warning, Ning's wealth ambition, forged escape documents, offenses after retirement, and Yi Zong's procedure remain source-scoped.
