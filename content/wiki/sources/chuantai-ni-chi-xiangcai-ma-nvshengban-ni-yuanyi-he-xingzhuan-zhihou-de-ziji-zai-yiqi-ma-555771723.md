# 串台.「你吃香菜吗」女生版：你愿意和性转之后的自己在一起吗？

## Summary

This [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] crossover with [[NiChiXiangcaiMa|你吃香菜吗]] uses the question "would you date a gender-swapped version of yourself?" as a playful [[GenderSwapSelfThoughtExperiment|gender-swap self thought experiment]]. [[XiaoShiHost|小时]] and [[KeZiHost|柯子]] initially prefer the safety and comprehension of a highly similar partner, while [[QinZong|秦总]] and [[ZhiZaoLaoYe|纸造/老爷]] initially prefer difference, mystery, and complementary support. The episode's core contribution is a relationship framework: [[SelfSimilarityInIntimacy|self-similarity in intimacy]] lowers friction and risk, but [[ComplementaryRelationshipSupport|complementarity]] can add growth, emotional balance, and perspective when sameness would amplify shared blind spots.

## Key Claims

- The initial 2-to-2 split turns the prompt from a gender fantasy into a test of [[SelfSimilarityInIntimacy|similarity]], [[RelationshipOptionality|relationship optionality]], and self-acceptance.
- The willing side values common background, taste, worldview, and predictable defects because these make intimacy feel lower-risk and easier to sustain.
- The unwilling side argues that romance needs unfamiliarity, mystery, and a person whose strengths can compensate for one's own weakest points.
- [[EmotionalCoRegulationFit]] matters more than abstract "stability": sometimes a calm partner is useful, but sometimes being angry together or feeling understood is the real support.
- The episode treats [[RelationshipNeedDiversification]] as a practical middle path: friends, hobbies, and separate communities can meet needs that do not all have to be loaded onto a romantic partner.
- [[SincerityAsRelationshipTrust]] becomes the strongest personal criterion through Xiaoshi's emphasis on truthfulness and her story about a former partner hiding a small but pointless age difference.
- The later discussion of "if my own personality were male" shows how the same traits can be valued differently under gendered dating expectations, linking the source to [[FemaleSelfPossession]].

## Key Quotes

> "你愿意和性转之后的自己在一起吗？" - the central prompt.

> "真诚、善良和勇敢" - Xiaoshi's named partner qualities.

> "多谈恋爱，多交朋友" - the closing relationship advice.
