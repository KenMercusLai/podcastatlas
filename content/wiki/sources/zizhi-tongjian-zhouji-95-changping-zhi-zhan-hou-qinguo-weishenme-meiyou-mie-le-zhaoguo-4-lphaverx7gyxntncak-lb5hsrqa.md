# 《资治通鉴·周纪》95丨长平之战后秦国为什么没有灭了赵国（4）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-3-lsrmq1-rjqnsja8g07bhaqvhxhpi|Zhouji 95 part 3]] by explaining why the people who helped [[WeiQiWarringStates|魏齐]] could still be admired even though [[FanJu|范雎]] had a strong revenge claim. It argues that [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]], [[YuQing|虞卿]], [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]], and [[HouYing|侯嬴]] were acting inside aristocratic friendship, guest-protection, and士人 recognition ethics rather than only an anti-Qin program.

The episode then widens Fan Ju's revenge into a social-transition story. [[SimaQian|司马迁]]'s revenge formula, [[YuRang|豫让]]'s "国士" repayment, [[XunYue|荀悦]]'s criticism of dead-party loyalty, [[LiBai|李白]]'s admiration for Yu Qing, [[WangAnshi|王安石]]'s officialdom-inflected warning, and [[WangShizhen|王世贞]]'s later detachment all become interpretive lenses for a world where aristocratic order is breaking, low-born talent can rise through disorder, and [[ImperialExaminationOpportunityEquality|科举与机会平等]] later offers a more peaceful route for status mobility.

## Key Claims
- The episode rejects a simple "Wei Qi tricked righteous men" reading because Wei Qi's final suicide fits a volatile honor personality better than a purely cynical manipulator.
- Pingyuanjun and Xinlingjun are not treated as stable anti-Qin figures; their choices are better explained through friendship, hospitality, and noble reputation than through a consistent foreign-policy line.
- Yu Qing's sacrifice of office is presented as a士人 recognition case: when a person entrusts life and family to you, that trust itself demands repayment.
- [[YuRang|豫让]] supplies the value analogy. Being treated as a "国士" creates a higher return obligation than ordinary service, even when the patron is morally flawed.
- Wei Qi's status as a former great-state chancellor raises the reputation pressure on potential hosts; rejecting him would make Yu Qing or Xinlingjun look unworthy of trust.
- Fan Ju's revenge is framed as part of the same Warring States ethos as his enemies' loyalty: gratitude and resentment both demand visible repayment.
- [[XunYue|荀悦]] criticizes this value world because [[DeathPartyLoyalty|死党之义与守职奉上]] can put factional loyalty above ruler, office, parents, and public duty.
- [[LiBai|李白]] and [[WangAnshi|王安石]] become contrasting reception lenses: Li Bai sees heroic friendship, while Wang Anshi sees political danger and unpredictable disaster inside high office.
- Fan Ju's rise is treated as historically significant because disorder opens a path that would have been unimaginable under older hereditary hierarchy.
- The episode contrasts violent mobility in the Warring States with the later examination path: if hereditary birth cannot be chosen, exams become the peaceful institutional route for changing status.
- The closing preview says [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] will continue after Wei Qi's death by pressing [[Handan|邯郸]] and trying to compel [[BaiQi|白起]] back into the campaign.

## Key Quotes
> "帮亲不帮理" - the episode's summary of Pingyuanjun's friend-protection logic.

> "一饭之德必偿，睚眦之怨必报" - the revenge-and-repayment formula attributed to Sima Qian's Fan Ju portrait.

> "死党之义" - Xun Yue's critical label for loyalty to a private group above office and ruler.
