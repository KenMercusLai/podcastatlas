# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》205｜女皇帝吕雉，竟然因狗而死？（2）

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode describes the political vacuum after [[LuZhi|吕雉]]'s death. [[LuLuWesternHan|吕禄]] and [[LuChanWesternHan|吕产]] retain the northern and southern armies in Chang'an, while [[LiuZhangChengyangKingWesternHan|刘章]] uses information from his Lü-family wife to contact [[LiuXiangQiKingWesternHan|齐王刘襄]] and coordinate an inside-outside challenge with [[LiuXingjuWesternHan|刘兴居]].

The episode's main contribution is the Qi mobilization sequence. Liu Xiang and his advisers neutralize the resisting chancellor [[ZhaoPingQiChancellor|赵平]], reorganize the kingdom's offices, and call other princes toward the capital under a restore-the-Liu-house justification. The account then connects Liu Xiang's action to the large Qi inheritance of [[LiuFeiQiKing|刘肥]] and to territory removed under the Lü regency, while explicitly postponing its promised source criticism to a later installment.

## Key Claims

- Lü Lu and Lü Chan reportedly obeyed Lu Zhi's deathbed arrangement by holding the northern and southern armies rather than attending her funeral, preserving surface order in Chang'an.
- The source says the Lü clan contemplated a coup but hesitated because of ministers and commanders such as [[ZhouBo|周勃]] and [[GuanYing|灌婴]].
- Liu Zhang learned of the alleged plan through his wife, Lü Lu's daughter, and secretly asked Liu Xiang to mobilize while he and Liu Xingju acted inside Chang'an.
- Liu Xiang, [[SiJunWesternHan|驷钧]], [[WeiBoWesternHan|魏勃]], and [[ZhuWuWesternHan|祝午]] supported action, while Zhao Ping tried to block it by surrounding the royal palace.
- Wei Bo obtained Zhao Ping's troops through deception, turned them against the chancellor's residence, and Zhao Ping died by suicide; Liu Xiang then reassigned the kingdom's senior offices and raised forces.
- Liu Xiang's legitimacy and capacity are tied to his position as Liu Fei's son and successor and to the inherited scale of early Han Qi.
- The episode presents Lü-regency transfers of Boyang and Langye from Qi as a source of accumulated grievance and a motive for Liu Xiang's rapid action.
- The claim that the Shiji-derived account contains serious flaws is only announced here; no specific inconsistency is demonstrated in the supplied document.

## Key Quotes

> “当断不断，反受其乱。” - the maxim attributed to Zhao Ping after losing control of the forces he had mobilized.

> “清君侧、安社稷。” - the episode's summary of the public justification assigned to Liu Xiang's mobilization.

## Connections

- [[LiuXiangQiKingWesternHan|齐王刘襄]], [[LiuZhangChengyangKingWesternHan|刘章]], and [[LiuXingjuWesternHan|刘兴居]] - external mobilizer and internal allies in the proposed anti-Lü action.
- [[LuLuWesternHan|吕禄]], [[LuChanWesternHan|吕产]], and [[PostRegencyInsideOutsideMobilization|摄政终结后的内外合击动员]] - military control in the capital and the coalition assembled against it.
- [[WeiBoWesternHan|魏勃]], [[ZhaoPingQiChancellor|赵平]], [[SiJunWesternHan|驷钧]], and [[ZhuWuWesternHan|祝午]] - Qi officials whose alignment determines whether the kingdom can mobilize.
- [[LiuFeiQiKing|刘肥]] and [[QiKingdomEarlyHan|齐国]] - dynastic and territorial foundation of Liu Xiang's claim and capacity.
- [[ZhouBo|周勃]] and [[GuanYing|灌婴]] - senior Han figures whose presence is presented as deterring immediate Lü action.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. The episode itself warns that the Shiji-derived narrative has weaknesses, but it does not identify or test them here.
- The alleged Lü coup plan, the wife's intelligence, Liu Zhang's intended enthronement of Liu Xiang, exact dates, offices, troop movements, speeches, and the causal weight of territorial grievance remain episode-attributed pending comparison with transmitted histories.
- “吕后打压齐国” is an interpretive compression. The territorial transfers and resulting reduction are the bounded claims; a single motive for every transfer is not established by this source.
- The title's suggestion that Lu Zhi died “because of a dog” is not discussed in the supplied body and therefore contributes no evidence about her cause of death.
