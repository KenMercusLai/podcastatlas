# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》189｜刘邦死前最后一搏

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode moves from the final cleanup after [[YingBu|英布 / 黥布]]'s defeat into [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s last succession attempt. After [[ZhouBo|周勃]] finishes the [[ChenXi|陈豨]] branch and Liu Bang reworks the former Jing field into [[WuKingdomEarlyHan|early Han Wu Kingdom]] for [[LiuBiWuKing|刘濞]], the core issue becomes whether Liu Bang can still replace crown prince [[LiuYing|刘盈]] with [[LiuRuyi|刘如意]]. [[ZhangLiang|张良]] resists by avoidance, [[ShusunTong|叔孙通]] resists by death-facing remonstrance, and [[ShangshanSiHao|商山四皓]] publicly attach to Liu Ying, making Liu Bang concede that the crown prince's support is already too strong to overturn.

## Key Claims
- The episode completes the succession thread opened in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-181-jiemi-liubang-feili-taizi-de-zhenshi-yuanyin-lgnbvnnfwxe4weupqgz-rh9cbp2s|Hanji 181]]: Liu Bang still wants to promote Liu Ruyi near death, but the attempt now fails against advisers, ritual-political arguments, and Liu Ying's visible support network.
- Zhang Liang's resistance is indirect. He had been assigned to support Liu Ying during the Ying Bu crisis, and now avoids helping Liu Bang reopen the replacement question.
- Shusun Tong's role expands beyond court ritual. He treats the crown-prince issue as the state's root, citing succession disasters as warnings against replacing an established heir through late personal preference.
- Shangshan Sihao function as symbolic political capital for Liu Ying. Their presence shows Liu Bang that scholars he could not attract will serve the crown prince, so the heir's "wings" are formed.
- Liu Bang's concession does not make [[QiJi|戚姬 / 戚夫人]] safe. His warning to her recognizes that [[LuZhi|吕雉]] will dominate after him, while Qi Ji's later "鱼藻宫" naming is presented as a provocation that deepens Lu Zhi's hatred.
- The episode strengthens [[AlternativeHeirProtectionFailure|备位继承人保护失灵]]: Liu Bang not only fails to replace Liu Ying, he lets the favored branch remain visible after the established heir's coalition has become unmistakable.

## Key Quotes
> "羽翼已成" - Liu Bang's recognition that Liu Ying's support has become hard to shake.

> "吕后真是你的主人了" - Liu Bang's warning to Qi Ji after the replacement effort fails.

> "太子为天下根本" - Shusun Tong's state-order framing of the heir question.

## Contradictions
- This source conflicts with [[ChenXi|陈豨]] / [[FanKuai|樊哙]] as currently recorded from [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-182-weishenme-shuo-chengye-xiaohe-baiye-xiaohe-2-lqk-jifupm9lbghfzaf09igfufgb|Hanji 182 part 2]]: Hanji 189 credits [[ZhouBo|周勃]] with pursuing to Dangcheng and killing Chen Xi, while the existing Hanji 182 branch says Fan Kuai defeats Chen Xi near Lingqiu, where Chen Xi dies.
- This source confirms rather than reverses Hanji 181: Liu Bang's affection-driven replacement pressure is real, but Liu Ruyi and Qi Ji still lack the institutional base needed to make the change durable.
- The source qualifies the Shusun Tong page by adding a high-stakes succession remonstrance role to his earlier ritual-order role.
- The source narrows any simple reading of Liu Bang's final authority: even near death, he cannot freely impose succession preference once ministers, ritual arguments, and scholar reputation have made Liu Ying's position politically entrenched.
