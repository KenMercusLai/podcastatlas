# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》362-2｜卜式为啥要烹杀桑弘羊？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode uses a drought in 110 BCE to stage [[BuShiWesternHan|卜式]]'s attack on [[SangHongyangWesternHan|桑弘羊]]: state revenue should come from taxes, he argues, not officials entering markets and pursuing commercial profit. His claim that cooking Sang would bring rain joins a fiscal-policy dispute to the period's political reading of disaster, while the later appearance of unusual stars is converted into favorable confirmation of [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s fengshan achievement.

The episode also records [[LiuHongQiKingWesternHan|齐王刘弘]]'s favored but short, childless life, then closes the show's *Hanji 12* sequence with a reflection on historical study. Its durable synthesis is that history can enlarge personal perspective and present judgment, but the episode's market, omen, and character claims remain compact popular-history interpretations rather than complete institutional or astronomical histories.

## Key Claims

- During a small drought in 110 BCE, Han Wudi orders rain-seeking rites and the frequently praised Bu Shi submits a severe criticism of Sang Hongyang.
- Bu Shi says clothing and food for the court should come from taxation, whereas Sang has officials sit in market shops, sell goods, and pursue profit; he concludes that only Sang's execution will bring rain.
- In autumn, officials interpret unusual stars, including a Saturn-like object described by the observer Wang Shuo, as a favorable celestial response to Han Wudi's unprecedented fengshan record.
- Han Wudi favors his second son Liu Hong and grants him wealthy Qi, but Liu Hong dies in adolescence without a son, so the kingdom is abolished.
- Completing *Hanji 12* prompts the host to describe sustained historical study as a way to feel civilizational depth, inhabit a longer human timeline, and compare earlier rise and decline with present gains and losses.
- The host links deep historical reading to inner richness and psychological strength, but this remains a reflective claim rather than evidence of a universal gendered effect.

## Key Quotes

> “烹弘羊，天乃雨。” - the compact formula attributed to Bu Shi in the episode.

> “见前世之兴衰，考当今之得失。” - the Sima Guang formulation used to summarize the present value of studying history.
