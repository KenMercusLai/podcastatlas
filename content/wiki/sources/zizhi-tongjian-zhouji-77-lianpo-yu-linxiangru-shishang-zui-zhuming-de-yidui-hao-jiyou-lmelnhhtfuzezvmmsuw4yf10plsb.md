# 《资治通鉴·周纪》77丨廉颇与蔺相如 史上最著名的一对“好基友”

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode moves through [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s thirty-fifth and thirty-sixth years, 280-279 BCE, with [[QinState|秦国]] alternating between pressure on [[ZhaoState|赵国]] and large-scale southern attacks on [[ChuState|楚国]]. It completes the [[Mianchi|渑池]] setup from Zhouji 76: [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] attends after advice from [[LianPo|廉颇]] and [[LinXiangru|蔺相如]], Lin forces [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] into a parity gesture after Qin humiliates Zhao, and Zhao returns without diplomatic disgrace. The second half turns Lin's promotion above Lian Po into [[JiangXiangHeInternalUnity|将相和式内部团结]]: Lin avoids private conflict because Qin is deterred by the two men together, and Lian Po's later apology converts rank resentment into cooperative state defense.

## Key Claims
- In 280 BCE, [[QinState|秦国]] sends [[BaiQi|白起]] against [[ZhaoState|赵国]], defeats Zhao troops, kills twenty thousand, and takes places rendered in the source as Dai and Guanglang.
- In the same campaign frame, Qin sends [[SimaCuo|司马错]] with Longxi forces through [[BaShu|巴蜀]] toward Chu's Qianzhong region, forcing [[ChuState|楚国]] to surrender territory around the Han River, Shangyong, and adjacent southern land.
- The host treats Qin's expansion as two alternating directions: eastward pressure against the Three Jin and southward pressure against Chu.
- The source uses a Zhanguo Ce letter to explain why Qin may prefer attacking Chu over Wei: attacking Wei risks reviving coalition rescue, while Chu looks isolated and vulnerable.
- In 279 BCE, Qin continues the southern front as Bai Qi attacks Chu and takes places rendered as Yan, Deng, and Xiling.
- [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] then invites [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] to meet at [[Mianchi|渑池]] under reconciliation language.
- Zhao He hesitates, but [[LianPo|廉颇]] and [[LinXiangru|蔺相如]] advise attending because refusal would make Zhao appear fearful.
- Lian Po escorts the ruler to the border and proposes a thirty-day contingency: if Zhao He does not return, Zhao should install the crown prince to deny Qin hostage leverage.
- At Mianchi, Qin Zhaoxiang Wang asks Zhao He to play the se, making Zhao perform for Qin in a status-lowering scene.
- Lin Xiangru demands that Qin Zhaoxiang Wang strike a fou for Zhao; when Qin resists, Lin closes distance and threatens immediate violence, forcing Qin to comply.
- Qin does not gain usable advantage at the meeting because Zhao remains militarily alert and Lin prevents one-sided humiliation.
- Zhao He promotes Lin Xiangru to shangqing after the meeting, placing him above Lian Po and creating a rank conflict between diplomatic and military merit.
- Lian Po resents Lin's rise, but Lin avoids confrontation and explains that private rivalry would remove the paired deterrent that keeps strong Qin from attacking Zhao.
- Lian Po hears the explanation, apologizes with the "负荆请罪" gesture, and the two become a model of "将相和" and "刎颈之交."

## Key Quotes
> "负荆请罪" - the idiom attached to Lian Po's apology.

> "将相和" - the later frame for the reconciliation of general and minister.

> "两虎相争" - Lin Xiangru's image for destructive internal conflict.
