# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》102-2｜孔子在鲁国的身世之谜

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-102-1-lvbuwei-shi-jiangziya-houren-lveg-wsmggroaxjsnxopaevexqod|Qinji 102-1]] by asking why [[LuState|鲁国]] survived so late despite weakness and why [[ChuState|楚国]], rather than nearby [[QiState|齐国]], finally destroyed it. It frames Lu as a culturally weighty old Zhou state with [[HistoricalRecordAuthority|史官记录话语权]], then explains its late survival through [[SmallStateSurvivalByDistraction|小国因大国无暇而存续]]: Qin, Han, Zhao, and Wei were absorbed by western pressure, Yan was too distant, and Qi was weakened, bribed, and politically isolated under [[HouSheng|后胜]].

The episode then turns to [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]]. It presents his role in restoring Chu's great-power posture, credits Chu with the capacity and appetite to absorb Lu, and then critiques his request to leave the exposed Huai north region for [[JiangdongWarringStates|江东]], a safer former Wu region protected by the Yangtze and distance from Qin. The close uses [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s low ranking of Chunshenjun among the [[WarringStatesFourLords|战国四公子]] to prepare the later story of his violent fall.

## Key Claims
- [[LuState|鲁国]] is presented as an unusually long-lived Zhou enfeoffed state, lasting from the source's 1045 BCE founding date to the late Warring States terminal stage.
- Lu's closeness to the [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]] and its role in preserving records give it [[HistoricalRecordAuthority|史官记录话语权]]: the episode says other lords risked being remembered badly if they offended Lu.
- The source links [[Confucius|孔子]]'s ability to edit or compile 《春秋》 and other historical material to Lu's unusually rich record environment.
- The source notes an irony: Lu helped preserve larger historical memory, but its own origins became difficult to reconstruct.
- Lu did not survive because it was strong; its survival depended on the strategic distractions and constraints of larger states.
- [[QinState|秦国]], [[HanState|韩国]], [[ZhaoState|赵国]], and [[WeiState|魏国]] were too occupied by the western Qin-front war field to make Lu a priority.
- [[QiState|齐国]] had proximity and possible interest in Lu, but after [[LeYi|乐毅]]'s five-state attack and Qin's bribery of [[HouSheng|后胜]], Qi under [[QiWangJian|齐王建 / 田建]] became too weakened and isolationist to absorb Lu.
- [[YanState|燕国]] was geographically too far away to be the practical actor against Lu in this source's explanation.
- [[ChuState|楚国]] under [[MiWan|楚考烈王]] and [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]] had both restored capacity and eastern expansion interest, making it the state able to destroy Lu.
- The source says the same late-Warring-States frame includes Qin destroying Eastern Zhou and moving the [[Jiuding|九鼎]] to Qin, but this chronology conflicts with the prior Qinji 102-1 placement of [[EasternZhouPolity|东周国]]'s terminal episode.
- In Qin Zhuangxiang Wang's second year, dated by the source to 248 BCE, [[LushiChunqiu|《吕氏春秋》]] begins compilation.
- [[MengAo|蒙敖]] attacks [[ZhaoState|赵国]] and takes thirty-seven cities including Yuci and Langmeng.
- Chunshenjun argues that Huai north borders Qi and needs commandery-county administration, then asks to be moved to [[JiangdongWarringStates|江东]].
- The source reads the Jiangdong move as safer for Chunshenjun: it is former [[WuState|吴国]] land, protected by the Yangtze and farthest from Qin.
- The episode criticizes Chunshenjun's strategic framing because Chu's main danger should be [[QinState|秦国]], not Qi; treating Qi as the urgent threat risks forcing Chu to confront both east and west.
- [[SimaGuang|司马光]] ranks Chunshenjun last among the [[WarringStatesFourLords|战国四公子]], below [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]], [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]], and [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]].

## Key Quotes
> "鲁国掌握历史话语权" - the episode's explanation of Lu's reputational leverage.

> "弱国无外交" - the source's compact formula for Lu's dependence on great-power choices.

> "楚国最安全的一片地方" - the source's characterization of Jiangdong as Chunshenjun's safer fief.

## Contradictions
- Chronology tension with [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-102-1-lvbuwei-shi-jiangziya-houren-lveg-wsmggroaxjsnxopaevexqod|Qinji 102-1]]: this episode places Lu's destruction, Qin's destruction of Eastern Zhou, and the movement of the [[Jiuding|九鼎]] in the "Qin Zhaoxiang Wang fifty-second year" frame, while Qinji 102-1 places Eastern Zhou's destruction under [[QinZhuangxiangwang|秦庄襄王]]'s first year through [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]]. The wiki preserves both as source-scoped chronology until reconciled by a later lint/source review.
- Title-scope caution: the episode title mentions a mystery around Confucius in Lu, but the available transcript summary mainly covers Lu's historical-record environment, Lu's survival/destruction, and Chunshenjun; it does not develop a detailed Confucius biography or family mystery.
