# 105 趣话《鬼吹灯》之昆仑神宫P2：九层妖塔与记忆之城

## Summary

This [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] episode follows [[KunlunShrineGhostBlowsOutTheLight|《昆仑神宫》]] from the ice-basin corpses and nine-story demon tower through the ice/fire ghost-insect battle and into the apparently inhabited but empty 恶罗海城. The hosts value how blue and white crystals, wolf blood, the water-crystal corpse, and the 雮尘珠 move from strange background objects into survival clues, giving the action the retrospective logic of a puzzle. Human-skin murals then reframe the hot food, unfinished slaughter, and lit city as materialized fragments of a ghost mother's memory produced through the snake god's fictional “行境幻化” power rather than as ordinary ruins or a fully frozen timeline.

## Key Claims

- [[NarrativeSetupAndPayoff|Narrative setup and payoff]] makes the tower sequence feel solvable: blue and white crystals, wolf blood, the ghost insects' two states, and the 雮尘珠 appear before their controlling or protective functions become clear.
- The ghost insects' “乃穷神冰” and “无量业火” forms are not merely two monsters; heat and the crystal controls convert one state into the other, so resolving the threat depends on understanding a system rather than applying more force.
- [[GreedDrivenNarrativeTrap|Greed-driven self-entrapment]] appears in the tomb defense: discovering an object is not enough to trigger catastrophe, but lifting the water-crystal corpse away from its place activates vibration and avalanche, making intent to remove treasure part of the mechanism.
- Wolf-king blood, the black donkey hoof, and the crystal controls each solve a different layer of danger, joining folk motifs, prior observation, and mechanical inference rather than relying on one universal tool.
- [[MaterializedMemoryWorldbuilding|Materialized-memory worldbuilding]] explains 恶罗海城 as an embodied composite of salient moments remembered by a ghost mother; the boiling beef, blood handprint, and slaughterhouse therefore need not belong to one historical instant.
- The hosts praise the first four books for varying their explanatory register across illusion, mechanism, monsters, and fictional virtual space while returning the larger curse mystery to the nine-story demon-tower field where it began.
- Secondary discussions of ancient color systems, mummification, natural fish defense, publication history, religious hierarchy, and “虚数空间” remain cultural commentary or story-world interpretation rather than independently verified history or science.
