# 《资治通鉴·周纪》88丨你怎么看待凤凰男（2）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[FanJu|范雎]]'s arrival in [[QinState|秦国]] after the origin crisis in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-88-ni-zenme-kandai-fenghuangnan-1-lpuxkdq-rvnifuoneq212g53w5z5|Zhouji 88 part 1]]. Fan Ju first uses [[ProtectiveRemonstranceSequencing|护身符式进言]] to secure [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]]'s attention and protection, then names the sensitive problem: Qin appears strong, but [[XuanTaihou|宣太后]] and [[WeiRan|穰侯魏冉]] still overshadow the ruler.

The strategic core is Fan Ju's critique of Wei Ran's remote attack on [[QiState|齐国]] and his proposal of [[YuanjiaoJingongStrategy|远交近攻]]. The episode presents the policy as a turning point that converts Qin's scattered, force-heavy campaigns into a clearer sequence: keep distant states diplomatically manageable, attack neighboring states, and make every captured unit of nearby land permanently useful to Qin.

## Key Claims
- Fan Ju's opening claim that Qin has "no king" is not mere insult; it is a calculated shock aimed at [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]]'s hidden frustration with [[XuanTaihou|宣太后]] and [[WeiRan|魏冉]].
- His repeated "唯唯" answers, risk statement, and request for protection form a [[ProtectiveRemonstranceSequencing|high-risk remonstrance sequence]]: he makes the ruler ask, then requires a political commitment before speaking against royal kin and ministers.
- Fan Ju remains alert to palace information leakage, delaying the sensitive critique until an apparent eavesdropper behind the screen leaves.
- The attack on Qi's Gang and Shou is criticized as strategically wrong for Qin because Qin must cross or depend on [[HanState|韩国]] and [[WeiState|魏国]], while any durable benefit is hard to retain.
- [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]]'s remote attack on [[ChuState|楚国]] is used as the warning example: distant victories can kill generals and cover territory without leaving the attacker with stable land.
- [[YuanjiaoJingongStrategy|远交近攻]] turns land distance into strategic sequence: distant states are managed diplomatically, neighboring states become the main targets, and nearby gains can be directly incorporated.
- Fan Ju's appointment as Qin keqing marks the shift from private survival under the alias 张禄 to public participation in [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]]'s military and court strategy.
- The episode ends by stressing that the new external strategy still requires an internal settlement: [[XuanTaihou|宣太后]] and [[WeiRan|魏冉]] remain the obstacle to implementation.

## Key Quotes
> "秦国没有王，只有太后和穰侯。" - Fan Ju's deliberate shock line as presented by the episode.

> "远交近攻。" - the strategic formula Fan Ju gives Qin.
