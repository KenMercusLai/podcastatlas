# The Lady Vanishes

## Summary

This [[RevisionistHistory]] episode follows [[ElizabethThompson|Elizabeth Thompson]] from the sensational 1874 reception of [[TheRollCall|The Roll Call]] to her near election and later marginalization by the [[RoyalAcademy|Royal Academy]]. [[MalcolmGladwell]] argues that celebrating one exceptional woman allowed the Academy to display openness without changing the male-controlled membership system around her.

The episode uses [[DanielEffron|Daniel Effron's]] account of [[MoralLicensing]] to connect Thompson's story with [[JuliaGillard|Julia Gillard]], Australia's first woman prime minister. A breakthrough can widen access, as the episode says happened after [[JackieRobinson|Jackie Robinson]], but it can also become moral proof that licenses continued exclusion, gendered abuse, and the burden placed on a lone representative.

## Key Claims

- *The Roll Call* humanized exhausted British soldiers after a Crimean War battle and became the Royal Academy exhibition's major attraction before Queen Victoria acquired it.
- Thompson entered an art system that denied women equal training and was elevated from outsider to national celebrity through prominent exhibition placement and extraordinary public demand.
- Her Royal Academy nomination failed by two votes; members then debated the social inconvenience of admitting a woman, limited the privileges any future woman member would receive, and gave her next painting an inferior exhibition position.
- No woman entered the Academy until 1936, supporting the episode's claim that Thompson's celebrated exception did not produce durable institutional access.
- Effron defines moral licensing as the possibility that an earlier good act or identity permits later conduct that would otherwise threaten a person's moral self-concept.
- Gillard's historic election coexisted with gendered and sexualized abuse, including attacks she believed she could not visibly acknowledge without burdening future women.
- [[SymbolicInclusionWithoutStructuralChange]] distinguishes a milestone from a reform: one entrant can become evidence of openness while authority, rules, incentives, and treatment remain largely unchanged.
