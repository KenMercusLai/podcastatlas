# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》309-1｜霍去病夺回河西走廊，中国必须统一

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the [[SecondHexiCampaignWesternHan|第二次河西之战]] operation opened in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-308-bei-tangshi-chui-cheng-shen-de-ta-jing-shi-ge-luchi-lsjtzqsil36lxejij8gazn58qeeb|Hanji 308]]. After [[GongsunAoWesternHan|公孙敖]] becomes lost and misses the planned reunion, [[HuoQubing|霍去病]] continues alone through the desert, reaches the rear of the western [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] position, and attacks forces associated in the episode with the Lesser Yuezhi and several subordinate rulers.

The durable synthesis is operational rather than triumphalist: the loss of a supporting column does not end the deep maneuver, and Huo's remaining force converts rear access into a major battlefield and political disruption. The episode links the result to the later weakening of Xiongnu control over the [[HexiCorridorWesternHan|西汉河西走廊]], but its exact route, two-thousand-li distance, casualties, captive ranks, “fatal” damage, total disappearance of Xiongnu from the northwest, and first-opening language remain compact source claims rather than independently settled findings. The closing Xiongnu raids on Dai and Yanmen also show that the victory did not end the wider war.

## Key Claims

- Gongsun Ao loses his way in the desert and misses the planned reunion near Juyan, depriving Huo Qubing of the supporting column described in Hanji 308.
- Huo does not withdraw after the rendezvous failure; the episode says he continues across the desert and appears behind the opposing force.
- The episode associates the attacked population with Lesser Yuezhi remnants under Xiongnu control and says Shanhuan King and Qiutu King surrender while other leaders resist.
- [[ZhaoPonuWesternHan|赵破奴]] is credited with defeating and capturing one resisting leader, while Gao Buzhi defeats another group whose ruler escapes.
- The campaign reaches the Qilian area and is said to kill or capture 30,200 people, including rulers, royal relatives, generals, and other senior officeholders.
- Huo receives five thousand additional households, several subordinate officers receive rewards, and Gongsun Ao redeems a capital sentence and becomes a commoner after missing the operation.
- The loss of Qilian and Yanzhi is presented through Xiongnu lament as loss of pasture, reproduction, resources, and strategic position, but subsequent raids on Dai and Yanmen show continuing Xiongnu military capacity.

## Key Quotes

> “失我祁连山，使我六畜不蕃息；失我焉支山，使我嫁妇无颜色。” - the lament used by the episode to express the material and cultural pain of territorial loss.

> “公孙敖在沙漠当中迷路了。” - the immediate reason given for the failed reunion and Huo Qubing's unsupported continuation.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. Hanji 308 stops before the second-Hexi outcome; this episode completes that deferred operation rather than revising it.
- The title's “夺回河西走廊” and “中国必须统一” are interpretive framing. The episode itself describes a decisive campaign and later strategic opening, but the closing raids and subsequent surrender sequence show that Han-Xiongnu conflict and Hexi consolidation were not completed in one action.
- “小月氏地盘,” the identities and spellings of Shanhuan King, Qiutu King, Chanshe, and Huyan Zhi, their political relation to Xiongnu, and Gao Buzhi's precise role remain transcript- and source-scoped.
- The route, two-thousand-li march, surprise mechanics, force balance, 30,200 result, captive-rank breakdown, two-campaign total, reward sizes, redemption procedure, Huo's age, elite selection, equipment comparison, and Qian Mu quotation require comparison with primary and specialist sources.
- “致命创伤,” “西北边境从此再无匈奴身影,” “第一个打通河西走廊,” and the claim that luck kept Huo from desperate situations are evaluative generalizations rather than independently established facts.
