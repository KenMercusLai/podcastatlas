# 270.大厂押注AI办公，飞书和钉钉却先成了配角

## Summary
This [[Luanfanshu]] episode uses the reported [[Feishu]] organization adjustment to map Chinese big-tech competition in [[AIOfficeAgent|AI office agents]]. [[ZhuangMinghao|明昊]], [[EricFeishu|Eric]], and [[YalanLuanfanshu|雅兰]] argue that the market has shifted from C-end chatbot traffic toward coding, long-horizon work, and office-agent workflows where [[Doubao]], [[DingTalk]], [[Qwen]], [[TencentWorkBody]], and enterprise data all matter. The core synthesis is that Feishu and DingTalk are becoming less like standalone protagonists and more like document, meeting, permission, workflow, and organizational-data substrates for model-led work products.

## Key Claims
- [[Doubao]]'s large C-end usage can be an asset or a liability because every active user can generate token, GPU, and electricity cost; the source mentions possible extremely high token usage and large RMB-scale cost without independently verifying the number.
- [[DoubaoEnterpriseEdition|Doubao enterprise edition]] is described by [[EricFeishu|Eric]] as closer to a Feishu enterprise product with a Doubao bot inside it: the customer buys AI, while Feishu supplies the work surface and enterprise context.
- [[Feishu]] and Volcano Engine are said to face overlapping customers and budgets, so separate commercialization teams can duplicate effort or conflict unless sales and product ownership are coordinated.
- The industry focus is described as moving from chatbot entry to coding, long tasks, and office agents because these workflows show clearer revenue, productivity, and enterprise-value signals.
- The source treats Open Cloud / "小龙虾" as a catalyst that helped big-company management teams understand what a work agent could look like, speeding changes at [[Alibaba]], [[ByteDance]], [[Feishu]], [[DingTalk]], and [[Tencent]].
- [[TencentWorkBody]] is framed as an "AI-era Office" attempt aimed at non-programmers who need natural-language help with documents, materials, tables, files, and task execution.
- [[Feishu]]'s potential advantage is not only an app shell: documents, meetings, org charts, permissions, approvals, and chat histories can become [[EnterpriseOperationalMemory]] when a company has digitized enough of its work.
- The payer for AI office may be ambiguous. Enterprise procurement remains important, but individual employees may pay directly for work productivity when the value is obvious.
- [[DingTalk]] and [[Feishu]] are described as becoming supporting roles in larger model and agent narratives: [[Qwen]], 螺丝快跑, Code Work, [[Doubao]], Volcano Engine, and WorkBody become the visible AI-office story layers.
- [[YalanLuanfanshu|雅兰]] summarizes three weak C-end monetization routes for Doubao: GMV/product cards, ads, and subscriptions. The episode argues that ads can damage answer trust, subscriptions convert weakly, and product-card volume is small relative to ByteDance's ad business.
- Doubao may still have indirect value if model memory and account links improve user profiling for [[OceanEngine]] and advertising ROI, but the source marks privacy and trust as unresolved risks.
- The episode argues that a general office agent may hide coding power behind an office UI: website building, spreadsheet handling, file modification, and business-process execution still depend on code-like action and verification loops.
- The source does not pick a winner among [[Tencent]], [[Alibaba]], and [[ByteDance]], saying product evidence from Doubao enterprise edition, Qwen office, WorkBody, and related office-agent products still needs time.
