# EP239 和大咖聊聊：金价又双叒叕飙了，普通人还能上车吗？

## Summary
This [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] episode has [[WangLixin|王立新]] of the [[WorldGoldCouncil|世界黄金协会]] explain gold after a sharp price run-up, shifting the ordinary-investor question from "is this the top" to what job gold should perform inside [[AssetAllocation]]. The episode frames gold as a financial asset, commodity, and industrial material whose investment value comes from independence from issuer credit, durability, liquidity, and long-run return history. It then maps [[GoldInvestmentProducts|gold investment products]] and [[GoldPortfolioProducts|黄金家组合产品]] as possible routes into [[StrategicGoldAllocation|strategic gold allocation]], while warning against all-in high-point buying, unreliable channels, product opacity, and mismatched risk preferences.

## Key Claims
- [[WangLixin|王立新]] says gold has three identities: financial product, ordinary commodity, and production material; the investment discussion should start from this mixed asset nature rather than only from recent price movement.
- The episode treats the 2002 founding of the [[ShanghaiGoldExchange|上海黄金交易所]] as a key marker in China's move from planned gold management toward a marketized gold system.
- Gold's investment attributes are framed as independence, stability or safety, liquidity, and long-term return: it is not issued by a company, government, or individual, yet can be traded through transparent channels.
- The source argues that gold belongs in [[AssetAllocation]] as a defensive or buffering sleeve, not as the portfolio "forward" that must score short-term returns.
- [[RayDalio]] is cited as an outside reference for raising a suggested portfolio gold allocation from 10% to 15% as credit-system risk rises.
- The episode explicitly discourages listeners from putting all their money into gold after hearing a bullish explanation; if the long-term role is accepted, the preferred route is staged accumulation, target sizing, and discipline.
- Jewelry can have some investment value, but workmanship premiums and resale friction make it different from pure investment exposure such as bars, coins, or gold ETFs.
- [[GoldInvestmentProducts|Gold investment products]] are separated into physical retail products, spot-linked financial products such as ETFs, and more professional futures or options tools that are not suitable for beginners.
- [[GoldPortfolioProducts|黄金家组合产品]] are described as professionally managed portfolios with a strategic gold sleeve, often at least 5%, that may appear in FOF, bank wealth-management, or private-fund formats.
- The source's ordinary-investor diagnosis is "recognition difficulty, entry difficulty, and holding difficulty"; professional portfolio products are presented as one possible way to add process discipline, not as a guarantee of return.

## Key Quotes
> "什么时候都能入场" - the source's long-horizon answer, conditioned on strategic allocation rather than short-term chasing.

> "不要把黄金作为战术性工具" - the episode's warning against using gold only as a timing trade.

> "认知难、下手难、持有难" - the source's summary of ordinary-investor friction around gold.
