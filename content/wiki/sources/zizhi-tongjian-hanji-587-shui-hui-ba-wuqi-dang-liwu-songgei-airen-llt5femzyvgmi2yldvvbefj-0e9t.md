# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》587丨谁会把武器当礼物送给爱人？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode directly continues [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-586-weile-aiqing-gudai-huangdi-you-duo-chongdong-lizfxc8a4do88e6vyrcupyet3vs|Hanji 586]]'s Dong Xian reward sequence by showing [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] sending palace officials to the state armory more than ten times to select weapons for [[DongXian|董贤]] and his household network. [[WuJianglongWesternHan|武将龙]], the Zhijinwu responsible for capital security, objects that armory weapons are public instruments funded by the realm and cannot be treated as private gifts.

The durable synthesis is that Aidi's favorite politics now crosses from title fabrication into military-resource privatization. Wu Jianglong distinguishes the court treasury managed by the Da Sinong from the emperor's private household resources managed by the Shaofu, invokes the rule that weapons and symbols such as axes and halberds belong to military command, frontier defense, and public authority, and later applies the same public-private boundary to [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]]'s underpriced purchase of official slaves.

## Key Claims

- Han Aidi's favor toward Dong Xian extends to repeated gifts of armory weapons to Dong Xian and related households.
- Wu Jianglong treats the imperial armory as a public military storehouse, not a private gift inventory.
- The remonstrance distinguishes Da Sinong public finance from Shaofu imperial-household finance, making the dispute institutional rather than merely frugal.
- Weapons, axes, halberds, and armor are framed as command and defense instruments that should move through military need, not personal affection.
- Wu Jianglong uses classical and ritual precedent to criticize favorite households accumulating weapons or status signs beyond their role.
- Fu Taihou's low-price purchase of official slaves becomes a second boundary case: court-connected private demand should still pay public value.
- Aidi punishes Wu Jianglong by demoting him to Pei commandery commandant, but past accession-related service keeps the punishment limited.

## Key Quotes

> "武库兵器属于天下人共有" - Wu Jianglong's public-instrument argument as summarized by the episode.

> "大臣的家里不能有兵甲" - the classical warning used to frame private possession of arms as political overreach.
