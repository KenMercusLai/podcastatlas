# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》788丨令敌人破大防！玩战术的心真脏？！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the Deng-regency Wudu and Qiang-front layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-789-shanliang-meiyou-dixian-ni-jiang-yiwen-buzhi-ljv5hgmd0vxoa-teckj2zkigvcoi|Hanji 789]]. Its historical core follows [[YuXuEasternHan|虞诩]] from a cavalry-mobility recommendation adopted through [[RenShangLateHan|任尚]] into his dangerous appointment as [[WuduCommanderyLateHan|武都郡]] taishou and the [[ChitingDefenseLateHan|赤亭守战]]. The durable synthesis is that Yu Xu survives an asymmetric frontier crisis by matching enemy mobility where possible and then manipulating enemy perception through false delay, [[IncreasingStovesDeception|增灶示强]], concealed crossbows, false reinforcements, night raids, and a half-river ambush.

## Key Claims

- The opening self-governance frame says success is a dangerous moment: social cooperation comes less from loose networking than from visible value, disciplined humility, savings, and judgment.
- Yu Xu argues that infantry-heavy Han forces cannot reliably catch mounted Qiang raiders; the court accepts his advice to demobilize county troops for farming while funding horses, and Ren Shang's light cavalry wins.
- [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Empress Dowager Deng]] hears that Yu Xu has command talent and appoints him Wudu taishou, but he receives only a small force and must reach the commandery through hostile territory.
- On the route to Wudu, Yu Xu falsely claims that he is waiting for reinforcements, lets Qiang forces disperse, then force-marches through the opening while increasing camp stoves to make the enemy believe help has arrived.
- The source explicitly contrasts Yu Xu's increasing stoves with Sun Bin's decreasing stoves: Sun Bin displayed weakness to invite pursuit, while Yu Xu displayed strength to slow pursuit.
- At Chiting, Yu Xu hides his strong crossbows until the Qiang attack closely, concentrates fire on individual targets, sorties after panic begins, and uses repeated night harassment to keep the besiegers unstable.
- Yu Xu then stages fake reinforcements through soldiers changing clothes and entering or leaving by different gates, leaks a decision to fight, and ambushes the Qiang during withdrawal, producing the source's "增灶破敌" outcome.
- Earthquakes, a Wuling Man rebellion, [[LiuKaiEasternHan|刘恺]]'s move to situ, [[YuanChangLateHan|袁敞]]'s move to sikong, and [[DengHongLateHan|邓鸿]]'s restrained posthumous treatment show the same regency field handling military, disaster, appointment, and family-honor decisions at once.

## Key Quotes

> "做人要低调，做事要高调" - opening social-value frame.

> "增灶破敌" - later summary of Yu Xu's Wudu campaign.

> "打铁还需自身强" - transition from cavalry advice to Yu Xu's dangerous appointment.
