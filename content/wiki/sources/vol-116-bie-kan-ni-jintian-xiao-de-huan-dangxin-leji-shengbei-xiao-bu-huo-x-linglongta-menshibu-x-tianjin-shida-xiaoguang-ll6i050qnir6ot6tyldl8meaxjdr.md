# VOL.116别看你今天笑得欢，当心乐极生悲“笑不活” X 玲珑塔门市部 X 天津师大校广

## Summary
This [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] crossover with [[LinglongtaMenshibu|玲珑塔门市部]] starts from [[HeSiqiTianjinNormalRadio|何思琪]]'s account of a friend laughing until her jaw dislocated, then asks clinicians to explain when laughter can become a physiological trigger. [[WuBinOralDoctor|吴斌]], [[WengJiayiCardiology|翁家毅]], [[JiaDoctorZheBing|贾建]], and unnamed respiratory and intensive-care voices connect wide mouth opening, acute arousal, unstable vascular disease, asthma, and diaphragmatic spasm to [[LaughterTriggeredHealthRisk]]. The episode then restores the positive side of humor through [[EverydayHumorEmotionRegulation]]: laughter, comic performance, relaxed conversation, and personal downtime can add relief and connection without making laughter itself the underlying disease.

## Key Claims
- Wide mouth opening during laughter, yawning, eating, sneezing, or dental care can precipitate mandibular-joint dislocation, especially for someone with prior dislocation or temporomandibular symptoms.
- Laughter can temporarily raise heart rate, breathing rate, and blood pressure; the source frames serious cardiac or cerebrovascular outcomes mainly through underlying coronary disease, recent infarction, heart dysfunction, or poorly controlled hypertension rather than through laughter alone.
- Large emotional swings, including laughter or intense sadness, may contribute to asthma exacerbation in susceptible people, particularly when other respiratory or allergic triggers are present.
- Laughter can stimulate the phrenic nerve and provoke hiccups; breath-holding is presented as a limited reflex maneuver, while persistent regular hiccups lasting more than 24 hours are treated as a reason for clinical assessment.
- [[HypertensionLongTermManagement]] matters because chronic disease control changes the risk attached to short episodes of excitement.
- The episode explicitly rejects fear of normal emotion: laughter, anger, crying, and sadness are ordinary expressions, while risk assessment should focus on disease state, intensity, recurrence, and warning signs.
- [[LinglongtaMenshibu|玲珑塔门市部]] describes crosstalk as pursuing shaped, measured laughter with aftertaste rather than maximal continuous laughter.
- [[EverydayHumorEmotionRegulation]] includes not only explosive laughter but also the relief of comedy, comfortable conversation, personal space, and an unhurried way of life.
