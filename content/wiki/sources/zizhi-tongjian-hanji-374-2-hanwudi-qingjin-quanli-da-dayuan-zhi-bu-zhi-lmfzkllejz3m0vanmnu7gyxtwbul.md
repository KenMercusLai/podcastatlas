# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》374-2｜汉武帝倾尽全力打大宛，值不值？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode narrates [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s second expedition against [[DayuanWesternHan|大宛]] as a state-scale response to the first campaign's failure. The court reinforces [[LiGuangliWesternHan|李广利]] with troops, transport animals, grain, weapons, engineers, frontier garrisons, and horse specialists; after the destruction of Luntai intimidates other oasis states, more than thirty thousand Han troops reach Dayuan, divert its water, and maintain a siege for over forty days.

The durable synthesis is a qualified [[DecisiveResourceCommitment|decisive-resource-commitment]] case. Overwhelming force and logistical preparation help Han obtain horses, provisions, a settlement, and a friendly king without completing the destruction of Dayuan, but the episode gives little accounting of civilian deaths, forced levies, transport losses, or the opportunity cost of the campaign. Its larger claim—that the expedition cut the [[Xiongnu|Xiongnu]]'s “right arm” and established Han prestige in the Western Regions—remains the program's strategic interpretation rather than a settled cost-benefit verdict.

## Key Claims

- Han Wudi turns the second Dayuan expedition into a national mobilization by sending more than sixty thousand troops toward Dunhuang, excluding privately equipped followers, plus large numbers of cattle, horses, donkeys, camels, grain, weapons, and tools.
- Intelligence that Dayuan's capital lacks wells leads Han to send hydraulic workers, while eighteen wan settlers or troops are placed north of Jiuquan and Zhangye and forces are stationed around Juyan and Xiutu to protect the corridor.
- The army includes recruited prisoners, fugitives, merchants, sons-in-law, and officials with offenses, as well as specialists appointed to select and drive horses after victory.
- Luntai resists, is taken after several days, and is massacred; the episode presents this violence as the intimidation that makes later oasis states open their gates and supply the Han army.
- Li Guangli reaches Dayuan with more than thirty thousand soldiers, defeats an attempted field defense, diverts the city's water, and maintains a siege for more than forty days.
- Dayuan nobles kill King Wugua, offer his head, horses, and grain, and warn that continued attack will cause them to kill the horses while Kangju relief threatens the Han rear.
- After learning that Dayuan still has grain and captured Han well-diggers, Li Guangli accepts the settlement, selects several dozen superior horses and more than three thousand other horses, and installs the Han-friendly noble Meicai as king.
- The host judges the campaign strategically valuable because it pressures Xiongnu influence, raises Han prestige among Western Regions states, and deepens contact, but the episode does not independently calculate whether those gains outweighed the full human and fiscal cost.

## Key Quotes

> "断匈奴右臂" - the episode's strategic justification for the Western Regions campaign.

> "即使最后攻下来，也什么都得不到" - Dayuan's bargaining threat that it would kill the horses if Han continued the siege.
