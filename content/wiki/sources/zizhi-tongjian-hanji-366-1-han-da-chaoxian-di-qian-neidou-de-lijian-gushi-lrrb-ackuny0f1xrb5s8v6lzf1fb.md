# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》366-1｜汉打朝鲜，敌前内斗的离间故事

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens the Western Han war against [[WimanJoseon|卫氏朝鲜]] before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-366-2-hanwudi-henxin-zhansha-gongsunsui-ls1izjhow_j4ubgmm0lm8ruaurox|Hanji 366-2]]. It traces escalation from envoy [[SheHeWesternHan|涉何]]'s killing of a Joseon escort officer and Joseon's retaliatory killing of him through [[YangPuWesternHan|杨仆]]'s initial defeat, [[XunZhiWesternHan|荀彘]]'s stalled advance, and [[WeiShanWesternHan|卫山]]'s failed surrender mission.

The near-surrender fails when the armed Joseon crown prince is ordered to disarm before crossing the Pei River and turns back out of fear. After Han Wudi executes Wei Shan, Xun Zhi advances to [[WangxianCity|王险城]], but the siege stalls again because Xun Zhi favors assault while Yang Pu pursues a separate surrender channel. The episode therefore makes distrust cumulative: coercive diplomacy triggers retaliation, reciprocal fear defeats a negotiated submission, and rival field channels turn caution into suspicion.

## Key Claims

- She He kills a Joseon escort officer after his demands are rejected, receives a frontier appointment instead of punishment, and is later killed in Joseon's retaliatory attack on Liaodong.
- Yang Pu advances with seven thousand Qi troops, is defeated by [[UgeoWimanJoseon|卫右渠]], hides in the mountains, and gradually regathers his scattered force.
- Xun Zhi also fails to break the Joseon force west of the Pei River, prompting Han Wudi to send Wei Shan to seek submission under military pressure.
- Ugeo offers submission, horses, provisions, and his son as a hostage or apologetic court visitor, but the prince turns back when the Han side demands that his large armed escort disarm before crossing the river.
- Han Wudi executes Wei Shan after the failed mission; Xun Zhi reads the punishment as a signal to attack, breaks the river defense, and joins Yang Pu in surrounding Wangxian City.
- Xun Zhi's assault preference and Yang Pu's surrender preference produce missed attack dates, separate negotiation channels, and an unsupported suspicion that Yang Pu may be disloyal.

## Key Quotes

> “将在外，君命有所不受。” — the episode's frame for the judgment demanded of distant commanders.

> “两个将军不能相互协调，就无法达成一致共同抗敌。” — the host's synthesis of the divided siege command.
