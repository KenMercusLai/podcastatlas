# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》714丨是谁第一个喊出“男儿当马革裹尸”？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianwu 12-13 / 36-37 CE before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-715-shishang-zui-zhihui-de-yici-duoquan-loqkawl247maw9fn-nomaw5gzkgu|Hanji 715]]. It begins with [[MaYuanEasternHan|马援]] defeating Canlang Qiang and other Qiang forces in Wudu, then uses his commandery governance and "horsehide burial" resolve to frame frontier service as a mix of military courage, delegated administration, and situational judgment.

The second half widens the episode into [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]]'s early consolidation style: flexible frontier orders, respectful integration of [[DouRongEasternHan|窦融]] and Hexi officials, praise for [[KongFenEasternHan|孔奋]]'s clean record, acceptance of [[RenTingEasternHan|任廷]] and [[ZhiYunEasternHan|郅恽]]'s bluntness, restraint toward tribute delicacies, and continued northern pressure from [[LuFangEasternHan|卢芳]], [[Xiongnu|匈奴]], and Wuhuan.

## Key Claims

- Ma Yuan's Wudu victory against Qiang forces is presented as both a military result and a window into his frontier-governance temperament.
- Ma Yuan's everyday commandery rule relies on delegation to clerks, while reserving direct intervention for powerful abuse, official misconduct, and real security threats.
- The episode uses the horsehide-burial idiom to summarize Ma Yuan's military life view, while the fuller quoted declaration remains associated with later Ma Yuan material in the wiki.
- Liu Xiu's frontier edict treats troop capacity and enemy conditions as reasons to adjust defense and pursuit instead of applying harsh delay law mechanically.
- Dou Rong's Luoyang entry and seal return show incorporation of the Hexi power field through ritualized submission, restored honor, and new appointments.
- Kong Fen and Ren Ting function as clean and blunt official models whom Liu Xiu can recognize rather than punish.
- The tribute, Zhi Yun gate, and Sui Yu surrender branches present early Guangwu rule as order rebuilding through restrained consumption, rule-bound enforcement, and pragmatic defector incorporation.

## Key Quotes

> "恩威并施" - the episode's summary of Ma Yuan's commandery governing style.

> "男儿当马革裹尸" - the idiom foregrounded as Ma Yuan's frontier-death ethos.

> "奉公守法" - Ren Ting's formulation of official integrity against mere upward harmony.
