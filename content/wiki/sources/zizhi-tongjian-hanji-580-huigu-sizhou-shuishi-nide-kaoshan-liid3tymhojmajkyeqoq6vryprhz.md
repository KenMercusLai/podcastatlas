# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》580丨回顾四周，谁是你的靠山？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianping 2 / 5 BCE under [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]], after [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-578-quanli-jiaofeng-shi-renshi-biangdong-zui-mingan-lnnrrbggajjfulskbig4kmgnif5s|Hanji 578]] and before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-581-han-aidi-zai-guishen-zhidao-you-duo-yuchun-ltok7kbzmuf-os84spseww0qjbrm|Hanji 581]]. It follows [[ZhuBoWesternHan|朱博]] and [[ZhaoXuanWesternHan|赵璇]] as they continue the post-[[ShiDanWesternHan|施丹]] attack against [[WangMang|王莽]], only for Aidi to preserve Wang Mang's title and fief because [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] remains his decisive patron.

The durable synthesis is that late Western Han court safety depends on visible backing, not only formal guilt. [[YangXuanWesternHan|杨宣]]'s secret memorial reframes leniency toward the Wang clan as part of Aidi's obligation to [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] and Wang Zhengjun, while Zhu Bo's separate州牧-to-刺史 proposal shows office rank itself changing officials' incentives to monitor local power.

## Key Claims

- Zhu Bo and Zhao Xuan argue that Wang Mang's earlier position as Da Sima makes his resistance to Aidi's filial-title settlement especially grave.
- Aidi does not strip Wang Mang of marquisate and fief, sending him back to his fief instead because Wang Zhengjun remains a protective senior patron.
- Yang Xuan's secret memorial pressures Aidi by connecting Chengdi's succession choice, service to Wang Zhengjun, public sympathy for the Wang family, and the shame Aidi should feel before Chengdi's tomb.
- Aidi answers that pressure with partial compensation, enfeoffing Wang Shang's second son Wang Yi as Chengdu Hou.
- Zhu Bo argues that州牧 rank has become too high and comfortable, weakening the inspector-like bite that lower-ranked刺史 once had.
- Aidi approves the abolition of州牧 and restoration of刺史, making office design a question of incentive and oversight rather than title alone.
- Ding Taihou's death and Dingtao burial show Aidi's intense concern for his birth parents' posthumous status, while setting up later conflict between personal filial piety and ancestral/dynastic ritual.

## Key Quotes

> "回顾四周，谁是你的靠山" - the episode's title question for Wang Mang's survival.

> "靠山就是他是太皇太后的亲侄子" - the source's compact explanation for why Wang Mang is not reduced to commoner status.
