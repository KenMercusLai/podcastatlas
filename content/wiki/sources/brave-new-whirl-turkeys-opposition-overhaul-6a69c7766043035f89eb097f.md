## Summary
This [[TheIntelligence]] episode links three magazine-style segments: [[TurkishOppositionRealignment]] after [[OzgurOzel]] forms the [[NewPartyTurkey|New Party]], [[SurrogacyPolicyHypocrisy]] after [[JensSpahn]] uses a surrogate abroad despite defending [[Germany]]'s ban, and [[AIGraduateCareerUncertainty]] for Gen Z workers entering an AI-shaped job market. The Turkey segment argues that the opposition's new momentum is inseparable from [[JudicializedOppositionSuppression]] under [[RecepTayyipErdogan]]. The career segment rejects simple passion advice and favors [[UsefulWorkCareerCompounding]]: become very good at helpful work, then let motivation and passion follow from competence and contribution.

## Key Claims
- [[OzgurOzel]]'s [[NewPartyTurkey|New Party]] is described as quickly becoming the second-largest force in Turkey's Parliament after at least 91 [[RepublicanPeoplesParty|CHP]] MPs defected.
- Polls cited in the episode put the New Party above 30% and ahead of Erdogan's [[JusticeAndDevelopmentParty|AK Party]], giving the opposition a credible opening on paper.
- [[RecepTayyipErdogan]] and the AK Party have led Turkey since 2002, with Erdogan serving first as prime minister and then as president.
- The source expects Erdogan to seek an early presidential election before the official 2028 date, reinforcing the wiki's existing [[AutocraticSuccession]] branch around formal rules and leader-centered workarounds.
- [[EkremImamoglu]] is presented as the candidate best placed to challenge Erdogan, but he has been imprisoned since March 2025 on charges the correspondent describes as trumped up and now faces roughly 2,300 years in prison.
- The episode says the New Party has inherited much of the CHP base but still has to prove it can draw in secular nationalists and Kurdish voters rather than merely clone the old party.
- A court removed Ozel as CHP leader and returned [[KemalKilicdaroglu]] to the post; the episode says Erdogan's fingerprints are visible in that ruling.
- The remaining CHP is framed as [[OppositionPartyHollowing|hollowed out]], with support falling into single digits and a membership ceremony reportedly populated by paid extras.
- The episode says at least 26 CHP mayors, plus hundreds of local officials and municipal workers, have been arrested in recent years, with rumors that [[MansurYavas]] could be next.
- [[JensSpahn]]'s position became untenable after revealing that he and his husband had become parents through surrogacy abroad, despite his defense of Germany's surrogacy ban.
- [[FriedrichMerz]] initially underestimated the scandal but eventually forced Spahn out and used the episode for a small reshuffle.
- The Germany segment says public anger came from the contradiction between Spahn's policy position and private conduct, not simply from his family decision.
- The source says the scandal could weaken Merz's [[ChristianDemocraticUnionGermany|CDU]] before eastern German state elections where [[AlternativeForGermany|AfD]] leads.
- The career segment says Gen Z graduates face both AI-assisted mass applications and AI-powered HR screening, extending [[AIHiringArmsRace]] into the graduate labor market.
- [[BillGurley]]'s *Running Down a Dream* and [[JodiKantor]]'s *How to Start* are treated as partly useful but vulnerable to [[CareerAdviceSurvivorshipBias]].
- [[BenjaminTodd]]'s [[EightyThousandHours|80,000 Hours]] is presented as the stronger career-advice frame because it rejects the [[PassionTrapCareerAdvice|passion trap]] and urges people to build useful skills that help others.
- Todd's impact-oriented approach draws on [[EffectiveAltruism]] and considers fields such as pandemic prevention and AI regulation, but the reviewer warns that maximizing impact can become demoralizing.
