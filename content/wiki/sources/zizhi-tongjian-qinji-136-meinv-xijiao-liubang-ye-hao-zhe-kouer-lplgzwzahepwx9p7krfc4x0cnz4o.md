# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》136｜“美女洗脚”刘邦也好这口儿

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the [[LiShiqi|郦食其]] setup from Qinji 135: he rebukes [[LiuBang|刘邦]] for receiving him while having his feet washed, then turns the meeting into concrete strategy by sending Liu Bang toward [[Chenliu|陈留]]. The episode then follows Liu Bang's westward line through failed pressure on [[Kaifeng|开封]], victory over [[YangXiongQinGeneral|杨熊]], the [[YingchuanCommandery|颍川郡]] massacre, [[ZhangLiang|张良]]'s renewed counsel, and the negotiated surrender of [[WanCounty|宛县]]. Its core shift is that Liu Bang's campaign moves from anti-Qin vengeance and terror toward rewarded surrender and pacification of future subjects.

## Key Claims
- Li Shiqi wins access not by flattering Liu Bang, but by forcing Liu Bang to see that a man claiming to punish "无道" cannot receive an elder in an undignified way.
- Liu Bang's response shows a recurring source pattern: he is coarse and arrogant, but he can correct himself quickly when an argument touches military usefulness or political legitimacy.
- Li Shiqi argues that Liu Bang's current force is not ready to attack Qin's core directly and should first seize [[Chenliu|陈留]], a route hub with grain, weapons, and recruitment value.
- When persuasion fails, Li Shiqi kills the Chenliu local head and helps Liu Bang take the city, making him useful but morally compromised in the episode's judgment.
- Chenliu becomes Liu Bang's preparation base: he distributes stored weapons and grain, stays for nearly three months, and recruits tens of thousands before continuing west.
- Liu Bang appoints [[LiShang|郦商]], Li Shiqi's younger brother, as a general and uses Chenliu's surrendered troops as part of his expanding force.
- Liu Bang cannot quickly take [[Kaifeng|开封]], then defeats Qin general [[YangXiongQinGeneral|杨熊]] around Baima and Quyu; [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] has Yang Xiong executed after the defeat.
- The episode treats Liu Bang's [[YingchuanCommandery|颍川郡]] massacre as deliberate terror meant to frighten other cities into surrender, complicating any simple "Liu Bang did not love killing" contrast with [[XiangYu|项羽]].
- Zhang Liang reappears on Liu Bang's line, helps him think about the route west, and later warns him not to leave Wan behind as a rear threat.
- [[SimaAng|司马卬]]'s attempt to move toward [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] creates a competitive-credit problem under [[HuaiwangAgreement|怀王之约]]; Liu Bang blocks the crossing to preserve his own westward lead.
- [[ChenHuiNanyang|陈恢]] persuades Liu Bang that storming or bypassing Wan both carry costs, while accepting surrender can save time, lives, and rear security.
- The surrender of [[NanyangCommandery|南阳郡]] marks a political turn: after Wan, Liu Bang begins treating Qin people less as mortal enemies and more as future subjects whose cooperation can speed entry into Guanzhong.
- The episode closes by returning to [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] after [[JuluBattle|巨鹿之战]]: [[WangLiQin|王离]]'s army is gone, [[XiangYu|项羽]] is across the river, and Qin Er Shi's rebuke adds court pressure to Zhang Han's military danger.

## Key Quotes
> "人的放纵是本能，自律才是修行" - opening moral frame for judging desire, restraint, and later political discipline.

> "既然要铲除无道，就不该用无道态度接见长辈" - Li Shiqi's rebuke of Liu Bang's reception posture.

> "不再把秦人当成生死仇敌，而开始把他们视作自己的子民" - the episode's summary of the post-Wan shift in Liu Bang's rule logic.
