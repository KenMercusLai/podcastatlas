# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》609丨西汉亡于王政君？你同意吗

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 1 CE layer between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-608-jiemi-xihan-aosika-zuijia-yanyuan-wangmang-llwfchwpkmuesz0nmdr48qpuwcjk|Hanji 608]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-610-1-jiemi-gudai-huangdi-niangjiaren-de-quanli-douzheng-lplz8f3wg1p0m5kmlh-w32jwnlzq|Hanji 610-1]]. It asks whether [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] caused [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]]'s fall, then narrows the question to how [[WangMang|王莽]] used her age, political fatigue, court memorials, and institutional delegation to move most government business away from her and into his own hands.

The durable synthesis is that Wang Zhengjun is not treated only as culprit or innocent victim. Wang Mang exploits her desire to be relieved of routine affairs, has ministers recommend that only enfeoffment matters still reach her, gains control over official evaluation and appointment-facing interviews, and then mixes gifts with removals to discipline the bureaucracy. He also begins isolating [[HanPingdi|汉平帝]] from his maternal [[EmpressDowagerWeiPingdi|Wei]] family by honoring [[WeiBaoWesternHan|卫宝]], [[WeiXuanWesternHan|卫玄]], and Pingdi's sisters while keeping them in Zhongshan rather than Chang'an.

## Key Claims

- The episode opens with the judgment that Wang Zhengjun bears responsibility for Western Han's fall, but the host counters that she also appears as an elderly senior deceived by Wang Mang.
- Wang Mang recognizes that Wang Zhengjun's continuing role in government blocks full personal control, so he uses her age and distaste for affairs as the entry point for delegation.
- Ministers recommend that Wang Mang examine newly appointed officials, recommended talents, and high administrators instead of requiring Wang Zhengjun to handle routine government.
- Wang Zhengjun's edict leaves only enfeoffment matters for her direct report, while Wang Mang and the Four Offices handle the rest.
- Wang Mang turns official interviews into a control channel by combining attentive reception and generous gifts with open denunciation and removal of officials who resist him.
- The establishment of the Xihe office, eclipse amnesty, and recommendation order sit inside the same court field where Wang Mang needs the appearance of loyal remonstrance but controls its terms.
- Wang Mang frames Pingdi's Wei maternal relatives as a repeat of Ding and Fu outer-relative danger, then gives them titles and honors while preventing them from entering Chang'an.
