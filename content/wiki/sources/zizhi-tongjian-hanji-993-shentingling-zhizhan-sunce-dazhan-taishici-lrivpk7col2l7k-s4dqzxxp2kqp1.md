# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》993丨神亭岭之战：孙策大战太史慈

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills [[SunCe|孙策]]'s 195 CE Jiangdong expansion immediately before the existing Hanji 994-995 branch. It contrasts Sun Ce's disciplined, people-winning, talent-using advance with [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]]'s refusal to make [[TaishiCi|太史慈]] a general because of status and reputation anxiety. The center is [[ShentinglingBattle|神亭岭之战]], where Taishi Ci meets Sun Ce while scouting and fights him despite being badly outnumbered, making the battle a compact case of ignored talent becoming visible in the field.

## Key Claims
- In 195 CE, [[SunCe|孙策]] crosses the Yangtze and advances through Jiangdong while maintaining strict military discipline; people who first fear his army later bring food and wine to reward it.
- The episode presents Sun Ce's charisma, openness to advice, and ability to use people as major reasons his force gains momentum beyond battlefield victory.
- Sun Ce takes Liu Yao's Niuzhu camp and its stores, then defeats Liu Yao-aligned positions connected with [[XueLiLateHan|薛礼]] and [[ZeRong|笮融]] before pressing toward Qu'a.
- [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]] receives [[TaishiCi|太史慈]] from Donglai while Sun Ce is attacking Qu'a, but does not appoint him as a major commander.
- Liu Yao's stated anxiety about [[XuShao|许劭]]'s opinion turns Taishi Ci into a case of [[StatusFilteredTalentMisrecognition|门第筛选式人才误判]] rather than merely a missed personnel slot.
- The host treats Taishi Ci's martial ability, archery, and lower-status background as part of the late-Han transition in which battlefield ability can outrun older status filters.
- At [[ShentinglingBattle|神亭岭]], Taishi Ci scouts with one rider and meets Sun Ce, who has thirteen riders including [[HanDang|韩当]] and [[HuangGai|黄盖]].
- Taishi Ci fights Sun Ce directly; Sun Ce seizes Taishi Ci's hand戟, while Taishi Ci seizes Sun Ce's headgear before both sides' troops arrive and the encounter breaks off.
- The episode notes that Romance of the Three Kingdoms also preserves the Shenting scene and a 光武帝庙 setting, while keeping the geographic detail source-scoped.
- After Liu Yao flees to Dantu, Sun Ce enters Qu'a, rewards his troops, offers broad amnesty to Liu Yao and Ze Rong's associates, avoids forced enlistment, and quickly draws more than twenty thousand recruits and over one thousand horses.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction found. The source backfills Sun Ce's positive Jiangdong discipline and recruitment branch before Hanji 994, and it gives Liu Yao an earlier status-filtered talent failure before Hanji 995's route-choice and Ze Rong sequence.
- Source-scope caution: the episode's explanation of Taishi Ci as a lower-status or 寒门 military talent is preserved as the host's interpretive frame rather than treated as a complete social-history proof.
