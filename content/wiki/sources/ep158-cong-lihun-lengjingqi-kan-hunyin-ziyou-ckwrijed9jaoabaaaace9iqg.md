# EP158-从离婚冷静期看婚姻自由

## Summary
This [[WushichaYanjiusuo|无时差研究所]] episode uses a lawyer's family-case experience to examine China's divorce cooling-off period, separating its stated goal of reducing impulsive separation from its effects on people who have already negotiated property, care, and relationship exit. It strengthens [[ChinaDivorceRestrictions]] and [[MarriageExitFriction]] by showing how repeated mutual-consent requirements, evidentiary burdens, and slow litigation can combine, especially where domestic violence or coercion is present. The cases also connect material independence to usable exit and distinguish divorce itself from the conflict, abandonment, and continuing care through which children experience separation.

## Key Claims
- The episode describes a 30-day cooling-off period after an uncontested-divorce application followed by a 30-day completion window; because both spouses must continue cooperating, either can interrupt the administrative route before registration is complete.
- The guest argues that many couples reaching the administrative-divorce stage have already discussed property and child arrangements, so a universal delay may burden considered decisions as well as impulsive ones.
- [[MarriageExitFriction]] becomes cumulative when administrative delay, unilateral withdrawal, contested proceedings, evidentiary requirements, and social shame operate together rather than as isolated rules.
- Domestic-violence relief depends on more than formal alternatives to administrative divorce: police and medical records, witness scarcity, protection-order access, legal assistance, and litigation time affect whether safety is practically reachable.
- Photos of injury, infidelity, or separate sleeping arrangements may not establish the legal facts a court must decide; the episode therefore illustrates a family-law version of the [[LegalTruthEvidenceGap|legal truth-evidence gap]].
- [[StructuralConditionsOfFemaleIndependence|女性独立的结构条件]] include continued income, knowledge of household assets, housing options, and evidence preservation, because dependence can weaken bargaining power when a marriage breaks down.
- [[ParentalSeparationProcessAndChildSecurity|父母分离过程与子女安全感]] turns on adult conduct and continuing responsibility: the cases depict children harmed when parents treat them as burdens, abandon them at court, or withdraw after forming a new household.
- The episode rejects divorce stigma and treats prior marital status as insufficient evidence of a person's character, while still urging careful entry into marriage and responsible planning for children.
