# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》368-1｜俄乌之战背后的谈判博弈！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode uses the Russia-Ukraine war as a brief modern frame for a 107 BCE Han-Xiongnu negotiation. After [[GuoJiWesternHan|郭吉]] is detained for demanding either battle or submission, [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] sends the frontier-born [[WangWuWesternHan|王乌]], who follows [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] entry protocol by leaving his tally outside, changing clothes, and marking his face before meeting the chanyu.

The durable synthesis is that protocol and substance can be separated in negotiation. Wang Wu yields on symbolic form and preserves the counterpart's dignity, but he does not abandon Han's substantive demand for submission; the chanyu's reported offer to send his son to Chang'an produces an apparent breakthrough whose implementation is not covered by this installment.

## Key Claims

- The episode opens with the Russia-Ukraine war to argue that prolonged conflict creates a continuing need for mediation, dialogue, and negotiation, but it does not provide a detailed modern-war analysis.
- By 107 BCE, repeated Han campaigns have reduced Xiongnu raids without eliminating Xiongnu as an independent great-power counterpart.
- Xiongnu repeatedly seeks renewed heqin or sends friendly signals, while the episode interprets the sequence as delay rather than settled submission.
- Guo Ji delivers Han Wudi's demand for either decisive battle or submission and is detained after the chanyu reacts angrily.
- Han Wudi selects Wang Wu because his northern-border upbringing gives him practical familiarity with Xiongnu custom and audience rules.
- Wang Wu leaves his tally with the camp, adopts the required clothing and face marking, and enters alone rather than making protocol refusal the central dispute.
- Wang Wu still presents submission as Han's core condition and offers material generosity; the chanyu reportedly agrees to send his son to Chang'an, but the episode ends before fulfillment or reversal can be assessed.

## Key Quotes

> “冲突战争没有赢家” - the modern diplomatic maxim used to introduce the episode's negotiation theme.

> “要么决战，要么称臣” - the episode's summary of the hard choice Guo Ji communicates to the chanyu.

> “要啥给啥” - the episode's compressed description of Wang Wu's material offer alongside the political demand.
