# E46 我们从未看见彼此

## Summary
This [[WurenZhixiao]] solo episode by [[MengYan]] asks what people actually see when they look at familiar others. It opens from the [[AIExpressionBaseline|AI-raised expression baseline]] and argues that human expression still matters when it carries sincere, personally grounded deviation from generic output. The main essay uses [[GoodFriendsFilm|《好朋友们》]], [[ABriefHistoryOfIntelligence]], [[JidduKrishnamurti]], and [[WhatIsOurProblem]] to connect cognitive evolution, projection, language labels, algorithmic feeds, and attention practice into [[InterpersonalImageProjection]] and [[TotalAttentionPractice]].

## Key Claims
- AI makes ordinary knowledge retrieval and expression easier, so Meng Yan treats worthwhile human expression as a matter of importance, sincerity, and personal deviation from the generic baseline rather than mere output.
- The episode reads [[GoodFriendsFilm|《好朋友们》]] as a tragedy of projection: Hyun-tae's seventeen-year belief that In-chul abandoned his friends may be built from fear, self-protection, and his own hidden capacity to abandon others.
- [[ABriefHistoryOfIntelligence]] is reinterpreted as a natural history of misrecognition: valuation, wanting, simulation, mentalizing, and language all help organisms survive while making direct perception of people difficult.
- [[DopamineWantingLoop]], [[PredictiveWorldModelAnxiety]], and [[MentalizingProjection]] make interpersonal judgment structurally fragile because the mind predicts, simulates, and fills other people with self-derived material.
- [[LanguageCompressionLabeling]] makes civilization and shared knowledge possible, but also turns complex people into labels that can harden into relationship judgments.
- [[AlgorithmicAmplification]] and [[AIInformationPollution]] intensify the language problem because feeds reward emotional labels, fast positive/negative signals, and sharable simplifications over patient accuracy.
- [[JidduKrishnamurti]]'s image/register frame gives the episode its practical endpoint: there may be no simple method, but noticing the image that is doing the seeing can loosen it.

## Key Quotes
> "我看到的是谁？" - Meng Yan's central question for the episode.

> "你眼中的别人其实是你自己。" - the lesson he draws from the film.

> "我们从未看见彼此。" - the closing relational thesis.
