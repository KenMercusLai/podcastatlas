# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》129-4｜旧权贵张良如何在刘邦手下安身

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-3-chensheng-zhisi-weishenme-dele-minxin-hanle-renxin-lnx4zs-vsi3zeoixnqskkpexmr3|Qinji 129-3]] by following the anti-Qin field after [[ChenSheng|陈胜]]'s death. [[SongLiu|宋刘]]'s surrender is answered with execution, [[YongChi|雍齿]] defects from [[LiuBang|刘邦]] at [[FengCounty|丰邑 / 丰县]], [[ZhangEr|张耳]] and [[ChenYu|陈馀]] restore [[ZhaoXie|赵歇]], and [[QinJiaRebel|秦嘉]]'s attempted alliance with [[TianDanQinRebel|田儋]] collapses into status conflict. The closing turn makes [[ZhangLiang|张良]]'s meeting with Liu Bang the positive counterpoint: an old Han aristocrat joins a weaker grassroots rebel because Liu Bang can understand and use his [[TaigongBingfa|《太公兵法》]]-based counsel.

## Key Claims
- After Chen Sheng's death, [[SongLiu|宋刘]] surrenders at [[NanyangCommandery|南阳郡]], and [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] orders him torn apart by chariots.
- The episode reads that punishment as [[PunitiveSurrenderDeterrence|杀降纳降反噬]]: terrorizing a surrenderer may deter later rebels from surrendering to Qin.
- [[ZhouShiRebel|周市]]'s Wei-side advance creates an opening to recruit [[YongChi|雍齿]], whom Liu Bang had left to hold [[FengCounty|丰邑 / 丰县]].
- Yong Chi has joined Liu Bang's anti-Qin force but looks down on him, so he hands Feng over to Zhou Shi's side when Liu Bang is away.
- Liu Bang fails to retake Feng immediately, turning Yong Chi's defection into an early personal and military setback.
- After [[LiLiangZhaoGeneral|李良]] kills [[WuChen|武臣]], Zhang Er and Chen Yu gather remaining troops, defeat Li Liang, and force him to surrender to [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]].
- On a guest's advice, Zhang Er and Chen Yu install old Zhao royal descendant [[ZhaoXie|赵歇]] as Zhao king at Xindu, using inherited royal name and local support to stabilize their position.
- [[QinJiaRebel|秦嘉]] and Ning Jun install [[JingJu|景居 / 景驹]] as a provisional Chu king and try to coordinate with Qi through [[GongsunQing|公孙庆]].
- [[TianDanQinRebel|田儋]] rejects Qin Jia's alliance posture because Zhang Chu had not consulted Qi before setting up Jing Ju; Gongsun Qing answers that Qi had also proclaimed a king without consulting Zhang Chu, and Tian Dan kills him.
- [[LuChenQinRebel|吕臣]] joins with [[YingBu|英布 / 黥布]] to defeat Qin forces and retake Chen County, keeping the Chu banner alive after Chen Sheng's death.
- Ying Bu's move from punished Qin convict at [[Lishan|骊山]] to bandit leader and then rebel commander shows how Qin collapse lets marginal armed groups enter state-making politics.
- [[WuRui|吴芮]] marries his daughter to Ying Bu and directs him against Qin, turning a bandit-convict force into a local-gentry military alliance.
- [[ZhangLiang|张良]] is originally on the way to join Jing Ju, but after meeting [[LiuBang|刘邦]] he changes course and joins Liu Bang instead.
- Liu Bang appoints Zhang Liang as a cavalry officer and repeatedly understands and adopts Zhang Liang's [[TaigongBingfa|《太公兵法》]] arguments.
- The episode's title claim is source-scoped through [[StrategicRecognitionAlignment|谋略知己式归附]]: Zhang Liang's security under Liu Bang begins less with Liu Bang's immediate strength than with being understood by a ruler who can use his strategy.

## Key Quotes
> "天纵奇才" - Zhang Liang's judgment of Liu Bang's capacity to grasp his counsel.

> "没有人能听懂" - the episode's description of Zhang Liang's frustration before Liu Bang understands him.
