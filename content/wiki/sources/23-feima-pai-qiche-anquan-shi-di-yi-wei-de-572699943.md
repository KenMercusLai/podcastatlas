# 23.飞马牌汽车：安全是第一位的！

## Summary
This [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] episode uses [[ZhengYuanjie|郑渊洁]] and [[FeimaPaiQiche|《飞马牌汽车》]] to show how children's literature can become sharp institutional satire. [[QinZong|秦总]] and [[Beimin|北明]] begin from [[TonghuaDawang|《童话大王》]], [[XunTuJi|《训兔记》]], [[Pipilu]], [[Luxixi]], [[ShukeBeta|舒克贝塔]], and [[GreyWolfRock|大灰狼罗克]], then focus on a bus that cannot open its doors because it has not reached a stop. The episode's central claim is that "safety first" can become [[SafetyAsControl|safety as control]] when rules, departments, fees, media events, and adaptive habits protect procedure more than people.

## Key Claims
- [[ZhengYuanjie|郑渊洁]] is treated as a child-respecting writer whose stories reject obedience-only childhood, adult concealment, and moral simplification.
- [[XunTuJi|《训兔记》]] makes "listening well" frightening by imagining children literally trained into rabbits, extending [[AntiAuthoritarianEducation]].
- [[FeimaPaiQiche|《飞马牌汽车》]] appears in [[MaWangDengji|《马王登基》]] and centers on the 250路 bus that remains trapped in traffic for twelve years.
- The rule that a bus may not open its doors before reaching a stop becomes [[RuleIdolatry]]: an abstract safety rule overrides work, urination, childbirth, crime response, marriage, and death.
- The driver, ticket seller, traffic police, criminal police, and officials are not mainly depicted as individual villains; they become examples of [[InstitutionalOvercompliance]] and [[BureaucraticRiskAvoidance]].
- The city responds by collecting data, holding meetings, guarding doors, sealing doors, creating a "public bus traffic-jam bureau," and routing basic needs through approval, showing [[BureaucraticAbsurdityEscalation]].
- Safety language becomes coercive because it preserves a rule while turning passengers' ordinary bodily needs into managed exceptions, extending [[ProtectionAsControl]].
- The bus interior copies the outside system: smokers, urination-rights claimants, passengers, and staff create associations, committees, representatives, and leadership roles.
- Food delivery, waste handling, remote childbirth, family visits, entertainment, research products, hotels, and fees turn the unresolved traffic jam into [[ProblemIndustrialization]].
- The story's late turn is not only that the bus starts moving again; it is that long stagnation changes desire. A vehicle built for speed learns to prefer stopping.
- The episode expands [[ChildrensLiteratureComplexity]] by showing that a story remembered from childhood can become more politically and institutionally legible when reread as an adult.

## Key Quotes
> "安全第一" - the phrase the episode uses to frame the rule's moral cover.

> "车没进站" - the repeated procedural reason for refusing to open the doors.

> "250" - the bus number that later becomes a fashionable national symbol inside the story.
