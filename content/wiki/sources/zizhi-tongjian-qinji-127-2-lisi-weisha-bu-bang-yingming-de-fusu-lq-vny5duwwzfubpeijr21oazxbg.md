## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]]'s death during the final tour into a transition crisis controlled by [[LiSi|李斯]], [[ZhaoGao|赵高]], and [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]]. It explains why Li Si does not support [[FuSu|扶苏]] by showing how Zhao Gao turns Fusu's bond with [[MengTian|蒙恬]] into Li Si's own future-status threat. The episode's core chain is death concealment, control of the imperial letter and seals, forged succession commands, Fusu's suicide, Meng Tian's imprisonment, and Hu Hai's public accession at [[Xianyang|咸阳]].

## Key Claims
- After Qin Shi Huang dies on tour, Li Si chooses [[ImperialDeathConcealment|秘不发丧式过渡控制]] because he fears princely disorder and popular unrest before the convoy reaches the capital.
- The convoy keeps normal performance running: food is still sent in, officials still submit business, and Qin Shi Huang appears to remain inside the travel vehicle.
- The episode treats the "温凉车" body-placement detail as source-scoped and questions whether a full coffin was already in use, because later odor concealment would otherwise be harder to explain.
- The key informed circle is narrow: Hu Hai, Zhao Gao, Li Si, and several favored attendants know the emperor is dead; [[MengYiQin|蒙毅]]'s absence removes a possible counterweight.
- Zhao Gao is motivated by both opportunity and old danger: [[MengYiQin|蒙毅]] once sentenced him to death, [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]] pardoned him, and Hu Hai is the prince closest to his survival interests.
- Zhao Gao first persuades Hu Hai to use the imperial letter and seals to make himself heir, then tells Li Si that without the chancellor's participation the plan cannot succeed.
- Li Si initially rejects the proposal as ruinous, but Zhao Gao uses [[StatusAnxietyPersuasion|位阶焦虑式说服]]: if Fusu succeeds, Meng Tian's talent, merit, popularity, and Fusu's trust may place him above Li Si.
- The forged order appoints Hu Hai as heir and separately attacks Fusu and Meng Tian, turning succession seizure into a [[ForgedEdictSuccessionCoup|伪诏继位政变]].
- Fusu receives the false order and kills himself despite Meng Tian's advice to verify it, while Meng Tian refuses suicide and is imprisoned at Yangzhou.
- The convoy reaches Jiuyuan, returns rapidly through [[QinZhidao|秦直道]], and only then announces Qin Shi Huang's death and Hu Hai's accession in Xianyang.
