# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》628丨如果穿越，教你如何当皇帝

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 5 CE succession crisis after [[HanPingdi|汉平帝]] dies and before the later 8-9 CE usurpation sequence in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-633-nuza-chuanguo-yuxi-ta-jing-ruci-ganglie-lnmnwbk28ayllz5fip1a1kzm1ais|Hanji 633]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-634-huozai-nanren-yingzi-beihou-de-ta-can-can-can-lm3wub0l70yxwwyvwi15j93i9lwk|Hanji 634]]. It follows [[WangMang|王莽]] excluding adult Liu-line royals through the "brothers cannot succeed one another" argument, summoning younger descendants of Emperor Xuan, and using the white-stone fuming inscription to move himself into "acting emperor" status.

The durable synthesis is that Wang Mang's imperial takeover is staged before formal usurpation through controllable succession, sacred-sign interpretation, court pressure, and ritual inflation. [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] sees the white stone as deception but lacks enough leverage to block it; Wang Shun and the ministers recode the sign as Heaven authorizing Wang Mang to act for a future child ruler; and the resulting "摄皇帝" package gives Wang Mang imperial clothing, audience ritual, sacrifices, edict language, and public address while preserving only a narrow formal boundary before the dowager and Ping's empress.

## Key Claims

- Han Pingdi's death creates a succession opening in which Wang Mang can separate lineage legitimacy from practical control.
- Wang Mang rejects adult descendants of Emperor Xuan and uses "兄弟不得相为后" as a public reason to search among younger imperial descendants.
- The "丹书白石" inscription, "告安汉公王为皇帝," is presented as the starting point for fuming's rise and as a likely manufactured legitimacy device.
- Wang Zhengjun recognizes the proposal as deception but is pressured into accepting a political reality she cannot reverse.
- Wang Shun's mediation packages Wang Mang's power increase as stabilizing regency rather than personal ambition.
- The "摄皇帝" settlement gives Wang Mang emperor-level ritual, sacrificial, processional, address, and edict powers while leaving a token ministerial posture before Wang Zhengjun and Ping's empress.
- The episode frames this as a gradual manufacture of legitimate appearance, not a sudden naked seizure of power.

## Key Quotes

> "告安汉公王为皇帝" - the white-stone inscription that turns Wang Mang's next step into a Heaven-sign claim.

> "摄皇帝" - the title produced by the court package after the white-stone report.

> "可" - the one-word approval attributed to the empress dowager after the ministers press the settlement.
