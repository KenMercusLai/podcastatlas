# vol.56 对谈咪仔：人生是认领自己与确立使命的过程

## Summary
This [[TianzhenButianzhen|天真不天真]] conversation follows [[MiziCrimePodcaster|咪仔]] from vocal study and traditional journalism through corporate public relations, pandemic-era underemployment in Switzerland, and the creation of [[BlackCatDetectiveAgency|黑猫侦探社]]. With [[YangTianzhen|杨天真 / 天真]], she connects [[AccumulatedSkillMissionRecognition|累积能力的使命识别]], public-interest fact discipline, [[EthicalPaidContentDesign|内容付费伦理设计]], the fragmented economics of Chinese podcasting, and the use of honest vulnerability and [[EmotionalTriggerSourceSeparation|情绪触发源分离]] in creator life.

## Key Claims
- A career mission can become legible retrospectively: music trained [[MiziCrimePodcaster|咪仔]]'s ear, journalism trained fact responsibility, family storytelling trained narrative instinct, and later podcast work combined those abilities.
- Traditional-media norms still matter after institutional journalism weakens; recording names, checking quotations, preserving public-event context, and distinguishing evidence from presentation remain part of creator credibility.
- [[EthicalPaidContentDesign]] keeps the public-interest account complete and sells genuine enrichment rather than engineering an information gap, anxiety, or unresolved suspense to force purchase.
- [[PodcastCommercializationFragmentation]] is especially severe for crime audio: brand fit is difficult, annual subscription has a high psychological threshold, and responsible creators may overproduce because charging triggers unworthiness.
- [[SuperCreatorPortfolioModel]] treats a creator as an expandable production system across podcasts, video, courses, books, products, and services, while combining editorial, performance, and commercial abilities that one person may not possess.
- Direct expression and vulnerability are not automatically cruelty or weakness; the practical work is to distinguish fact, responsibility, current criticism, and an older wound before reacting.
- Self-acceptance, emotion regulation, and support systems are learned practices rather than slogans or permanent states.
