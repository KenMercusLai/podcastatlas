# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》102-1｜吕不韦是姜子牙后人？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode moves from [[QinXiaowenwang|秦孝文王]]'s sudden three-day formal reign into [[QinZhuangxiangwang|子楚 / 秦庄襄王]]'s first year in 249 BCE. Its Qin-side focus is [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]]'s appointment as chancellor, his claimed Jiang-surname/Lu-clan link to [[JiangTaigong|姜太公 / 姜子牙]], his campaign against [[EasternZhouPolity|东周国]], his Wenxin Hou grant, and Qin's creation of [[SanchuanWarringStates|三川]] commandery after [[MengAo|蒙敖]] takes Chenggao from [[HanState|韩国]].

The episode then uses the title question to retell Jiang Taigong's early poverty, late recognition by [[ZhouWenwang|周文王]], service to [[ZhouWuwang|周武王]], founding role in [[QiState|齐国]], and later reputation as a military ancestor. That branch adds [[LateBloomingTalent|大器晚成式人才显现]] as a source-scoped lesson: long obscurity does not erase latent capacity, but capacity still needs timing, recognition, and a political stage.

## Key Claims
- [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]] is introduced as Jiang-surname, Lu-clan, named Buwei, from Puyang in Wey, and the source says he was a twenty-third-generation descendant of [[JiangTaigong|姜太公 / 姜子牙]].
- The episode uses Jiang Taigong's other name, Lü Shang, to explain why Jiang Ziya and Lu Buwei can be connected through a Lu-lineage narrative.
- [[QinZhuangxiangwang|子楚 / 秦庄襄王]] appoints Lu Buwei as Qin chancellor because Lu Buwei had made Zichu's rise possible.
- Jiang Taigong is presented as a figure who endured early poverty and uselessness in public status before meeting [[ZhouWenwang|周文王]] late in life.
- Zhou Wenwang's divination and meeting with the fishing Jiang Taigong become the source's route into the "姜太公钓鱼，愿者上钩" story.
- [[ZhouWuwang|周武王]] honors Jiang Taigong as 师尚父; Jiang Taigong helps destroy Shang, establish Zhou, and receives [[QiState|齐国]] as his fief.
- The episode credits Jiang Taigong's Qi governance with appointing the worthy, caring for the people, and developing fishing, salt, and commerce.
- The source says Jiang Taigong assists [[ZhouGong|周公]] after Zhou Wuwang's death and remains a four-generation elder through Zhou Kang Wang.
- Zhou Wenwang is tied to [[ZhouyiDivination|《周易》占卜]] and a "中" or middle-way idea later associated by the source with the Confucian Zhongyong tradition.
- [[EasternZhouPolity|东周国]] secretly plots with other lords against [[QinState|秦国]], and Qin Zhuangxiang Wang sends Lu Buwei to destroy it.
- The source says the Zhou royal sacrifices end when Eastern Zhou is destroyed and that only seven towns remain at the terminal stage.
- Qin grants Lu Buwei the Wenxin Hou title and gives him ten myriads of households around [[Luoyi|洛邑]] / Luoyang.
- Qin sends [[MengAo|蒙敖]] against [[HanState|韩国]], takes Chenggao, and establishes [[SanchuanWarringStates|三川]] commandery.
- [[ChuState|楚国]] destroys [[LuState|鲁国]], moves Lu Qing Gong to Biancheng, and turns him into a commoner; the episode stresses that Lu survived until very late in the Warring States period.

## Key Quotes
> "姜姓、吕氏、名不韦" - the source's lineage formula for Lu Buwei.

> "姜太公钓鱼，愿者上钩" - the idiom attached to Jiang Taigong's meeting with Zhou Wenwang.

> "老当益壮，不坠青云之志" - the episode's moralized reading of Jiang Taigong's late success.

> "周王朝的皇家祭祀到此断绝" - the source's summary of Eastern Zhou's destruction.
