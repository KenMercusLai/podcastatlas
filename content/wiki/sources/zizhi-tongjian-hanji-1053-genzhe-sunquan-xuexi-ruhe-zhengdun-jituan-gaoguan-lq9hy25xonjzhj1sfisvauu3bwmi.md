# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1053丨跟着孙权，学习如何整顿集团高管

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode fills a Jian'an 5 bridge after [[SunCe|孙策]]'s death and before the post-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]] transition. It frames [[SunQuan|孙权]]'s early rule as [[SuccessionAuthorityConsolidation|succession authority consolidation]]: he identifies [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]] through military performance, accepts [[LuoTong|骆统]]'s advice on honoring and observing talent, and suppresses [[SunFuLateHan|孙辅]] and [[LiShuLateHan|李术]] as internal threats before outside rescue can form. The second half contrasts this with [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s expansion and ritual overreach, plus [[LiuZhang|刘璋]]'s Yizhou trust crisis around [[ZhangLuFivePecks|张鲁]], [[PangXiLateHan|庞羲]], [[ZhaoWeiLateHan|赵韪]], and Dongzhou troops.

## Key Claims
- The opening "借力、借智、借势" frame is applied to Sun Quan: a young successor strengthens rule by seeing personal limits and making other people's capacity usable.
- Sun Quan does not accept Zhang Zhao's negative judgment of Lu Su as final, and the episode reuses Sun Ce's deathbed claim that Sun Quan is stronger at selecting people and preserving Jiangdong.
- Sun Quan inspects inherited commanders and promotes Lü Meng after seeing that his troops are orderly and trained, making actual unit condition a personnel test.
- Luo Tong urges Sun Quan to respect and recruit worthies, meet them personally, ask about their situation, and let them speak so ability and ambition can be observed.
- Sun Fu's secret appeal to Cao Cao and Li Shu's refusal of Sun Quan's control show that Sun-family kinship and Sun Ce-era appointments do not automatically become loyalty to Sun Quan.
- Sun Quan's attack on Li Shu is framed as his first authority-establishing campaign: he first warns Cao Cao about Li Shu and cuts off rescue, then captures Wan City, kills Li Shu, and relocates Li Shu's people, with the civilian slaughter recorded as a hard qualification.
- Liu Biao's conquest of Changsha, Lingling, and Guiyang expands Jingzhou, but stopping tribute and imitating emperor-level ritual turns regional strength into legitimacy overreach.
- Liu Zhang's Yizhou branch shows inherited provincial autonomy failing to integrate old subordinates, frontier religious-military power, private recruitment, Dongzhou troops, and local resentment.

## Key Quotes
> "举贤任能，各尽其心，以保江东，我不如卿." - Sun Ce's comparison, reused to frame Sun Quan's early advantage.

> "真正有能力、有智慧的人懂得借势而行." - the episode's opening management frame.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction found. The source backfills the missing Hanji 1053 bridge between the Sun Ce succession layer, the 1051 western Yangzhou branch, the 1054 post-Guandu continuation, and the 1055 Yizhou-Hanzhong sequel.
- Source-scope caution: the host's modern "集团高管" and career-management language is interpretive framing, not a separate historical claim.
- Violence caution: Sun Quan's Li Shu campaign is treated as authority consolidation while preserving the source's report of slaughter and forced relocation as a qualification, not as a purely positive leadership lesson.
- Identity caution: [[LiShuLateHan|李术]] is kept distinct from [[LiShui|《理水》]], [[LiShufu|李书福]], [[LiuXunLateHan|刘勋]], and [[LiuXunYizhouHeirLateHan|刘循]].
