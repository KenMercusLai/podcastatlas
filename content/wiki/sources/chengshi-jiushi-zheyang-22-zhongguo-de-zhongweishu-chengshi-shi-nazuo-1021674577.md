## Summary
This [[ChengshiJiushiZheyang]] episode follows [[DavidFishman|David Fishman]] from a ranking-based search for China's median city to a more qualified [[CityTypeSampling|city-type sampling]] method. A short visit to [[Linfen|临汾]] overturns the idea that one city can represent China: rankings can select cases, but regional histories, district-level differences, and rapid temporal change require statistical frames to be tested through [[UrbanFieldInterviewing|urban field interviews]] and repeated observation.

The episode also uses Shishi, Zixing, Jishou, Kangbashi, Zhoukou, Fuyang, and Nanyang to examine industrial specialization, tourism-enabled staying, low-visibility large cities, premature "ghost city" judgments, and administrative boundaries. Its stable methodological claim is not that these visits produce nationally representative findings, but that explicit sample boundaries and concrete questions about work, housing, children, and staying or leaving can turn a city visit into a source of testable hypotheses.

## Key Claims
- A city ranking can provide a sampling frame, but the city in the middle of one list is not a statistically or socially representative "average China."
- [[Linfen|临汾]] is more defensibly treated as a northern resource-dependent, post-industrial city type than as China's single median city.
- City comparison must account for horizontal regional difference, internal city-county difference, and temporal change within the same place.
- [[UrbanFieldInterviewing|Interviews]] about work, housing, children, hukou, and whether to stay or leave connect individual choices to local industry, education, migration, and expectations.
- The Zixing case suggests that tourism matters not only through output or visitor counts but through whether it creates acceptable local work that lets residents remain near family.
- Empty new districts require longitudinal judgment: Jishou's new area later gained residents, while Kangbashi remained inhabited but oversized, so a single empty-looking moment does not prove permanent urban failure.
- AI-assisted retrieval can expand the scale of local-document and historical-text research, but useful results still depend on clear questions, source checking, and administrative knowledge.

## Key Quotes
> "我只能代表上海，或者只能回答上海的问题。" - Fishman's boundary on generalizing from his own urban experience.

> "一个临汾不能代表整个中国。" - the episode's explicit correction to the median-city premise.

> "城市是我选择不同人群的一个抽样方法。" - the shift from ranking cities to using cities as sampling frames for people.
