# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》807丨班超之子为何会含冤入狱？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 126-127 CE opening layer of [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]] immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-808-ta-jiujing-shi-mingjia-haishi-pianzi-lhmsw7zusdkqddk-zu27oj6hfs2m|Hanji 808]]. It links decayed northern border infrastructure, [[Xianbei|鲜卑]] pressure on [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]], and renewed military training to [[BanYongLateHan|班勇]]'s Western Regions campaign.

The durable contribution is a paired frontier and court-information lesson. Ban Yong restores [[CheshiHouKingdomLateHan|车师后王国]] and other Western Regions polities to Han alignment, then plans a coordinated attack on [[YanqiKingdomLateHan|焉耆国]], but [[ZhangLangLateHan|张郎]] attacks early to clear his own legal exposure and receives the surrender first. The court reads the result as Ban Yong's delay, so the person managing the broader operation is recalled to [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]], jailed, and removed. The close adds [[XuJingLateHan|徐敬]] as a contrasting case of clean nonalignment under earlier outer-relative factions.

## Key Claims

- The episode opens with a host-level comparison frame: nearby success can trigger jealousy and rivalry more sharply than distant success.
- In 126 CE, damaged and unmanned defenses west of Shuofang make Southern Xiongnu requests for repair part of Shun's early frontier-security field.
- Shun's court answers by moving Liyang camp troops north, stationing border commandery units at the frontier, and restoring military training.
- Ban Yong reorders the Western Regions by installing Jiatenu as king of Cheshi Hou, replacing another local ruler, and bringing Cheshi and five other polities back under Han alignment.
- Ban Yong's anti-Northern Xiongnu campaign uses Western Regions troops and makes Cheshi personally execute a captured Xiongnu royal kinsman, deepening the dependency's commitment against Xiongnu reconciliation.
- The Yanqi campaign becomes a command-credit failure: Ban Yong plans a two-route meeting date, while Zhang Lang advances early, accepts Yanqi's surrender, clears his own penalty risk, and leaves Ban Yong blamed for lateness.
- Shun's recognition of his birth mother Li and later high-office appointments show court legitimacy and personnel turnover alongside the frontier material.
- Xu Jing's appointment as situ is explained through an older reputation for avoiding dependence on the Dou, Deng, and Yan outer-relative houses.
- The episode ends by setting up [[FanYingLateHan|樊英]] and the reclusion-reputation problem developed in Hanji 808.
