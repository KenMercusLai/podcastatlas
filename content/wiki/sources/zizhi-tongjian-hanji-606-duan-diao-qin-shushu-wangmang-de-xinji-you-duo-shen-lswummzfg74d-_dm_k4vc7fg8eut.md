# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》606丨端掉亲叔叔！王莽的心机有多深？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 1 BCE / Yuanshou 2 late-[[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]] court immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-607-jiemi-wangmang-de-quanli-boyi-lgnxg-zlf5gowjbirazpijvyks78|Hanji 607]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-608-jiemi-xihan-aosika-zuijia-yanyuan-wangmang-llwfchwpkmuesz0nmdr48qpuwcjk|Hanji 608]]. It follows [[WangMang|王莽]] removing his uncle [[WangLiWesternHan|王立]] through [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]]'s impeachment and [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]'s eventual approval, then tracks the deaths of Zhao Feiyan and Empress Fu, [[PengXuanWesternHan|彭宣]]'s resignation, and Kong Guang's awareness that he is being used.

The durable synthesis is that Wang Mang's early concentration of power worked through a persona-protected procedure. He avoided directly attacking a close relative, had others surface accusations, translated private threat into public-order language, performed grief and retreat when allies praised him, and distributed key functions to trusted operators in censorship, documents, military affairs, and court secrets.

## Key Claims

- Wang Li's risk to Wang Mang came less from current office than from his senior kinship and informal access to Wang Zhengjun.
- Kong Guang's impeachment supplied the formal accusation channel by tying Wang Li to Chunyuzhang's old offense and to a dangerous proposed imperial-heir arrangement.
- Wang Mang completed the removal by arguing that Wang Zhengjun must not let private feeling block ministerial judgment during a fragile child-emperor succession.
- Wang Mang's method combined indirect accusation, moral language, and emotional performance, letting him protect a humble and upright persona while clearing obstacles.
- His control network placed Wang Shun, Wang Yi, Zhen Feng, Zhen Han, Ping Yan, Liu Xin, and Sun Jian around key court, judicial, documentary, secretarial, and military functions.
- The same power field destroys or drives out others: Zhao Feiyan and Empress Fu die after demotion, while Peng Xuan resigns and is denied normal retirement gifts.
- Kong Guang is shown as both useful to Wang Mang and increasingly aware that remaining in office means continuing as an instrument.
