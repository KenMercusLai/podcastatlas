# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》359-2｜司马相如人生绝唱：《封禅文》

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode closes [[SimaXiangru|司马相如]]'s life with [[FengshanWen|《封禅文》]], a manuscript that [[ZhuoWenjun|卓文君]] gives to an imperial messenger after Sima's death. The episode presents the work as praise of [[HanWudi|汉武帝]], a rehearsal of legendary precedent, and an appeal for [[FengshanRitualLegitimacy|Taishan fengshan]], but does not provide the full text or a literary-historical comparison.

The second half supplies the decision prehistory to the later ceremony. Court scholars cannot reconstruct an agreed rite from classical texts, fangshi connect fengshan to immortality and divine contact, and [[NiKuanWesternHan|兒宽]] returns final ritual authority to the emperor; Wudi then devises the ceremony, adopts some Confucian ornament, and dismisses scholars who criticize the resulting vessels. The durable synthesis is that procedural uncertainty lets literary persuasion, specialist rivalry, sovereign authorship, and punishment of criticism jointly shape imperial ritual.

## Key Claims

- In 118 BCE, an imperial messenger reaches Maoling after Sima Xiangru has died, and Zhuo Wenjun says most of his writings have already passed into other hands.
- Zhuo Wenjun preserves one manuscript that Sima reportedly intended for an imperial envoy: 《封禅文》.
- The episode says the work invokes legendary rulers, auspicious signs, and Wudi's merit to urge a Taishan fengshan ceremony.
- After receiving the text and an auspicious tripod, Wudi consults ministers and classical scholars about a rite that has not been performed for a long time.
- Fangshi frame fengshan as a path to longevity and divine contact, whereas scholars spend years consulting classical texts without producing an agreed procedure.
- Ni Kuan acknowledges the textual gap and argues that only a sagely ruler can determine the rite, returning responsibility and authority to Wudi.
- Wudi devises the ritual with Confucian embellishment and dismisses scholars who criticize its vessels, showing that procedural debate is also a contest over sovereign authority.
