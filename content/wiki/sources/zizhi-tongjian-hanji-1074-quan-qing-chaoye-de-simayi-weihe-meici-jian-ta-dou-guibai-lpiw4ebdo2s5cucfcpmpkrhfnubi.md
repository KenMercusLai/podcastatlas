# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1074丨权倾朝野的司马懿，为何每次见他都跪拜？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode uses [[ChangLinLateHan|常林]] as a model of surviving late-Han and Cao-family regime change: he rejects [[YuanShao|袁绍]], waits until [[CaoCao|曹操]] controls Hebei before entering office, earns a reputation for local restraint and fiscal probity, and remains outside the later Sima-family power struggle. The host turns that career into an extension of [[GoodDeathPoliticalSurvival|乱局善终]], where timing, clean administration, and withdrawal matter more than dramatic battlefield achievement.

The second half extends [[ZhongchangTongLateHan|仲长统]] from institutional critic to mobile risk reader. He bluntly tells [[GaoGanLateHan|高干]] that ambition exceeds judgment and that liking worthy people is not the same as recognizing them, leaves when correction is unwelcome, and is later recommended by [[XunYu|荀彧]] to Cao Cao. The episode's account of `Changyan` adds [[HereditarySuccessionCapabilityDecay|世袭继承能力衰减]] as a source-scoped explanation of political cycles: inherited status does not reliably transmit governing ability, while long insulation from ordinary hardship can widen extraction and alienation.

## Key Claims

- The episode presents Chang Lin as poor but studious in youth, writing on leaves when he could not afford paper.
- Chang Lin refuses Yuan Shao's summons and hides in the mountains, then accepts office only after Cao Cao has pacified Hebei.
- The host credits Chang Lin with restraining local strongmen and land concentration, winning popular support, and later managing state finance without personal corruption.
- Chang Lin's distance from the 249 CE power struggle is used to explain his longevity, reputation, and “good death,” although the episode does not provide documentary citations for each career detail.
- Zhongchang Tong tells Gao Gan that his ambition exceeds his judgment and that liking worthy people is not the same as recognizing them; when Gao Gan rejects the criticism, Zhongchang Tong leaves Bingzhou.
- After Gao Gan's death, Xun Yu recommends Zhongchang Tong to Cao Cao, and the episode identifies his resulting post as Shangshu Lang.
- The host reads `Changyan` as connecting political rise-and-fall cycles to hereditary succession, declining successor fitness, elite insulation, forced labor, and heavy taxation.
- The closing “killing must be repaid” discussion is a preview of the next episode rather than a completed case in this source.

## Key Quotes

> “以谨慎避祸，以清廉留名。” - the host's compact interpretation of Chang Lin's survival path.

> “志向大却缺乏智谋，欣赏贤才却不擅长识别人才。” - the episode's paraphrase of Zhongchang Tong's criticism of Gao Gan.

## Contradictions

- Title/body gap: the title says Sima Yi knelt whenever he met Chang Lin, but the supplied episode summary only says the Sima family respected Chang Lin; the specific repeated-kneeling scene is therefore not treated as established.
- Internal status tension: the episode presents Chang Lin as poor in youth but later invokes a “士族背景” to explain Sima-family respect without clarifying the relationship between poverty, lineage, and status.
- Scope caution: the leaf-writing story, local land-policy achievements, exact offices, fiscal results, age, reasons for Sima-family respect, Zhongchang Tong dialogue, and causal account of Gao Gan's death are retained as podcast claims rather than independently verified history.
- Interpretive caution: “good death” as proof of wisdom and the hereditary decline model are the host's evaluative and structural readings, not a complete reconstruction of Chang Lin's career or `Changyan`.
