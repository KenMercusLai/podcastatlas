# 《资治通鉴·周纪》07丨两起刺杀（2）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] Zhou-annals sequence from [[ZhouAnWang|周安王]]'s fifth to eighth years. It links solar-eclipse omen politics, [[NieZheng|聂政]]'s assassination of [[XiaLei|侠累]], [[NieYing|聂英]]'s death for family honor, [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]]'s succession, [[SongState|宋国]]'s Shang-remnant legitimacy, chronicle gaps, and [[FushuCity|负黍]]'s repeated transfer into one argument about early [[WarringStatesPeriod|战国时期]] political order becoming faster, harsher, and less stable.

## Key Claims
- In 397 BCE, the source lists three events under [[ZhouAnWang|周安王]]'s fifth year: a solar eclipse, the assassination of [[HanState|韩国]] chancellor [[XiaLei|侠累]], and [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]]'s death.
- The episode treats eclipse records as political records, not only astronomical notices: the ruler and chief ministers were expected to read such events through [[CelestialOmenPoliticalResponsibility]].
- [[YanZhongzi|严仲子]] hires [[NieZheng|聂政]] to avenge himself on [[XiaLei|侠累]], but Nie Zheng delays until after his mother's death because filial duty makes self-sacrifice temporarily unavailable.
- Nie Zheng kills Xia Lei in a heavily guarded meeting, destroys his face and eyes to protect his family's identity, and dies by suicide; [[NieYing|聂英]] then identifies the body and dies beside him so his name will not disappear.
- The source interprets the Nie Zheng and Nie Ying story through [[AristocraticHonorOverLife]]: fame, family reputation, and noble-style obligation can outweigh survival.
- The episode records a [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] variant where Xia Lei holds onto [[HanAihou|韩哀侯]] and Nie Zheng kills both, making the assassination version a larger state-crisis account rather than only a ministerial revenge case.
- [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]] dies in the same year, and his son [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]] succeeds him, moving [[WeiState|魏国]] from the Wenhou generation into a new reign.
- In 396 BCE, [[ZiyangZheng|子阳]]'s remaining faction kills [[ZhengXugong|郑繻公]] and installs [[ZhengKangGong|郑康公]], extending the first half's Zheng political-violence sequence.
- The same year, [[SongDaogong|宋悼公]] dies and [[SongXiugong|宋休公]] succeeds; the source pauses to explain [[SongState|宋国]] as a high-status [[ShangRemnantStateLegitimacy|Shang-remnant state]] founded through [[WeiziQi|微子启]].
- The episode links [[Confucius|孔子]]'s ancestry to [[SongState|宋国]] nobility through [[KongfuJia|孔父嘉]], using genealogy to connect later classical authority back into pre-Qin aristocratic status.
- The source says 395 BCE has no recorded event, treating the blank year as an example of [[ChronicleSourceGaps]] rather than as authorial carelessness by [[SimaGuang|司马光]].
- In 394 BCE, [[QiState|齐国]] attacks [[LuState|鲁国]], while [[FushuCity|负黍]] defects back from [[ZhengState|郑国]] to [[HanState|韩国]] before soon being taken by [[ChuState|楚国]], making the city a compact example of [[TerritorialControlChurn]].

## Key Quotes
> "日有蚀之" - the [[Shijing|《诗经》]] line used to introduce eclipse records as political warning signs.

> "不愿因自己贪生怕死而使弟弟埋没英名" - the source's summary of [[NieYing|聂英]]'s reason for identifying [[NieZheng|聂政]].

> "各国攻伐脚步越来越快，战争规模也越来越大" - the episode's closing frame for the early Warring States trend.
