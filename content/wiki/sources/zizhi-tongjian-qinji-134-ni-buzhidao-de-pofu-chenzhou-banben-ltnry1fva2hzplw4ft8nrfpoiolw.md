# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》134｜你不知道的“破釜沉舟”版本

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the [[JuluBattle|巨鹿之战]] branch set up by [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-132-5wan-wanbao-40wan-xiangyu-cizhan-fengshen-lk87nb3fskg5zh6fcgptypixhzuw|Qinji 132]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-133-songyi-zhan-xiangyu-weihe-bei-fansha-lms7noyrm5omedv77gqfhjeu53zx|Qinji 133]]. It argues that [[XiangYu|项羽]] wins not by courage alone but by first having [[YingBu|英布]] and [[PuGeneral|蒲将军]] break the Qin supply corridor from [[Aocang|敖仓]] toward [[Julu|巨鹿]], then crossing the [[ZhangRiver|漳水]] and using [[BreakingCauldronsAndSinkingBoats|破釜沉舟]] to force death-ground morale. The episode treats the battle as the moment when Wang Li's besieging army collapses, the watching lords submit to Xiang Yu's command, and the anti-Qin war shifts decisively away from Qin.

## Key Claims
- After killing [[SongYi|宋义]], Xiang Yu's authority is accepted by the Chu army and then ratified by [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]] as formal chief command.
- [[LiuBang|刘邦]] continues his separate anti-Qin line, defeating a Qin force around Li County and joining with Huang Xin and Wu Man before defeating Qin again.
- Tian An, grandson of the former Qi king [[QiWangJian|齐王建 / 田建]], captures the Jibei area and follows Xiang Yu in the rescue-Zhao field.
- [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] keeps [[WangLiQin|王离]]'s besieging force supplied by building and guarding a fast corridor from the Zhang River crossing toward Julu.
- Wang Li's army has men and food, while [[ZhaoXie|赵歇]], [[ZhangEr|张耳]], and the Julu defenders lose grain, soldiers, and time inside the city.
- [[ChenYu|陈馀]] camps outside the siege but refuses repeated requests from Zhang Er, arguing that his limited force cannot save Zhao by directly attacking Qin.
- Zhang Yan and Chen Ze push Chen Yu to honor his life-and-death commitment; the five thousand elite troops he gives them attack Qin and are annihilated.
- Zhang Er's son Zhang Ao gathers more than ten thousand troops in Dai, but they also fear Qin and do not press the attack.
- Xiang Yu first sends Ying Bu and Pu General with twenty thousand Chu troops across the Yellow River to attack the Qin support units between Jiyuan and Julu.
- Ying Bu and Pu General break multiple parts of the Qin fortified corridor or yongdao, cutting food movement and separating Zhang Han's army from Wang Li's siege army.
- Xiang Yu then crosses with the Chu main force, follows across the Zhang River near Julu, sinks boats, destroys cooking vessels, burns the camp, and limits the army to three days of rations.
- The source emphasizes that the famous "破釜沉舟" crossing is the Zhang River crossing near Julu, not the earlier Yellow River crossing from Pingyuanjin.
- After nine engagements, Xiang Yu defeats Wang Li's army; Su Jiao is killed, Wang Wu is captured, and She Jian refuses surrender and burns himself.
- Other lords had built many camps near Julu but watched from their walls until Chu's victory made Qin break.
- After the battle, the lords fear Xiang Yu's battlefield power, accept him as coalition commander, and make Julu a political as well as military turning point.
- The episode cites Cai Dongfan's judgment that Julu was Qin's largest decisive battle and a key blow in the empire's fall.

## Key Quotes
> "破釜沉舟" - the idiom is placed after the Zhang River crossing and before the decisive attack on Wang Li.

> "渡的是漳水不是黄河" - the episode's geographic correction to the common simplified version.

> "巨鹿之战彻底打断了秦朝的脊梁骨" - the episode's compressed judgment on the battle's historical meaning.
