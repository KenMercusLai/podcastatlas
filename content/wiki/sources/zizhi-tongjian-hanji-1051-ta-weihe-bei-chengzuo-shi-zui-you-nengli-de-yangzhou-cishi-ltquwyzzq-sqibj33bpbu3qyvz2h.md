# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1051丨他为何被称作是最有能力的扬州刺史？

## Summary
This episode follows the 200 CE disorder in western [[YangzhouLateHan|Yangzhou]] after Lujiang's Li Shu kills inspector Yan Xiang and local armed groups gather between the Yangtze and Huai rivers. [[CaoCao|Cao Cao]] recommends [[LiuFuLateHan|Liu Fu]] as Yangzhou inspector, and Liu Fu uses the broken, nearly empty city of [[HefeiLateHan|Hefei]] as a weak formal base rather than an immediate battlefield. The host reads his success as [[WeakPositionPacification|weak-position pacification]]: title, reassurance, office grants, local autonomy, livelihood repair, schooling, and defense are sequenced to turn scattered hostile forces into a usable Jiang-Huai frontier.

## Key Claims
- The episode frames Liu Fu's achievement as a case where talent matters less than the courage and method needed to restart from a weak position.
- Yangzhou is not treated as a unified Cao-controlled province in 200 CE; only Jiujiang is plausibly usable, while Lujiang and much of the region remain fragmented by Yuan Shu remnants and local strongmen.
- Liu Fu's first move is administrative: he establishes the Yangzhou provincial office at Hefei and uses court appointment to create a legitimate center before seeking military confrontation.
- Liu Fu pacifies local armed groups by reassuring them, offering partial autonomy, seeking formal offices for their leaders, and converting tribute into a manageable sign of obedience.
- Water works, agriculture, crafts, education, walls, and defensive stores turn political submission into visible local benefit and make Hefei a durable base.
- The host presents Hefei's later Jiang-Huai military value as rooted in Liu Fu's non-battlefield state-building rather than in a single tactical victory.

## Key Quotes
> "才华只是入场券" - the opening frame for why Liu Fu's value lies in execution under constraint, not innate brilliance alone.

> "化敌为资" - the host's shorthand for turning former enemies and unattached armed groups into frontier resources.

> "先让别人安全、让别人发展，最后再谈忠诚" - the episode's practical lesson for sequencing trust before loyalty.
