# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》581丨汉哀帝在鬼神之道有多愚蠢？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianping 2 / 5 BCE under [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]], between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-578-quanli-jiaofeng-shi-renshi-biangdong-zui-mingan-lnnrrbggajjfulskbig4kmgnif5s|Hanji 578]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-582-nvren-de-chenhenxin-zui-duo-nanren-ming-ljiuneown6ah1yx5g_uchpb5escp|Hanji 582]]. It traces [[GanZhongkeWesternHan|甘忠可]]'s failed attempt to sell a [[Taipingjing|《太平经》]]-based "renewed mandate" cure to [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]], then follows [[XiaHeliangWesternHan|夏贺良]], [[LiXunWesternHan|李寻]], and [[JieGuangWesternHan|解光]] as they persuade Aidi to change reign title, imperial style, and timekeeping.

The durable synthesis is that illness, no-heir anxiety, disaster interpretation, and fangshi promise can make sacred legitimacy self-damaging. Aidi's adoption of "太初元年" and "陈圣刘太平皇帝" tries to repair Heaven's favor, but the quick reversal exposes judgment failure, damages the public aura of dynastic continuity, and shows how fangshi language can move from ritual cure to personnel-power redistribution.

## Key Claims

- Gan Zhongke packages Chengdi's no-heir anxiety as a mandate problem, claiming the Liu house needs renewed authorization from Heaven.
- Liu Xiang rejects Gan Zhongke's claim as fearmongering through spirits, and Chengdi chooses skeptical advice rather than ritual experiment.
- Xia Heliang preserves Gan Zhongke's method and reenters politics under Aidi through Li Xun and Jie Guang.
- Aidi's illness and court disorder make him receptive to disaster-omen claims that promise longevity, heirs, and relief from calamity.
- The adopted package is symbolically large: amnesty, reign-title change, imperial-title change, and altered water-clock scale.
- When the promised cure fails, Xia Heliang's group shifts toward replacing senior officials with Li Xun and Jie Guang, turning fangshi counsel into a power-allocation proposal.
- Aidi's reversal and execution of Xia Heliang preserve legal accountability, but the episode argues that "changed then revoked" ritual policy still weakens imperial credibility.

## Key Quotes

> "刘姓天命衰落，需要重新受命于天" - the source's summary of Gan Zhongke's renewed-mandate claim.

> "改了又收" - the episode's compact diagnosis of the legitimacy damage from Aidi's reversal.
