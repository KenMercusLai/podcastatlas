# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》458｜西汉宠妻达人张敞，犯死罪为何能活？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the [[ZhangChangWesternHan|张敞]] story behind [[FiveDayJingzhao|五日京兆]] before the later [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-469-weishenme-junzi-dou-buguo-xiaoren-kankan-xiaowangzhi-de-xiachang-lj8iji-c3scj6vznntavm6awolho|Hanji 469]] teacher-suitability discussion. It follows [[XuShunWesternHan|徐顺]] dismissing Zhang as a near-dismissed Jingzhao Yin, Zhang using legal custody and execution to punish the slight, [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] removing him, and then recalling him because capital and Jizhou banditry still need his administrative capacity.

The durable synthesis is that Zhang Chang's career cannot be reduced to either favorite-wife anecdote or capable-official praise. He is a strong public-order official who can break powerful criminal networks, but the Xu Shun case makes his authority ethically unstable because he routes personal anger through state punishment; this is why [[XiaoWangzhiWesternHan|萧望之]] later treats his ability as insufficient for crown-prince instruction.

## Key Claims

- The episode uses "祸从口出" to explain how Xu Shun's loose speech becomes the origin story for [[FiveDayJingzhao|五日京兆]].
- Zhang Chang is presented as a serious Jingzhao Yin, not as the negligent short-timer later implied by the idiom.
- After the Yang Yun / 杨恽 case, Zhang's enemies exploit rumors that he will be dismissed, and Xu Shun refuses an assignment because he thinks Zhang has only a few days left in office.
- Zhang imprisons, tortures, and executes Xu Shun before spring execution limits can block the sentence, mixing authority maintenance with personal rage.
- [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] dismisses Zhang after Xu Shun's family appeals, but later recalls him because crime returns in the capital and Jizhou.
- Zhang's self-defense admits he used law to vent anger while framing Xu Shun as a repeatedly forgiven, ungrateful subordinate.
- In Jizhou and Taiyuan, Zhang again proves able at cutting through protected criminal networks and local disorder.
- The episode connects Zhang's later teacher-suitability problem to [[TalentVirtueDistinction|才德之分]]: practical competence does not automatically make someone fit to form a crown prince's character.
