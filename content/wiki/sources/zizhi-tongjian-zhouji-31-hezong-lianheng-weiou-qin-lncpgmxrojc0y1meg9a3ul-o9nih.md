# 《资治通鉴·周纪》31丨合纵连横 围殴秦

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[SuQin|苏秦]] from Yan-backed diplomacy into the concrete design of [[HezongAlliance|合纵]]. It explains hezong as a north-south, anti-[[QinState|秦国]] coalition strategy rather than a peace project, then has Su Qin persuade [[ZhaoSuhou|赵肃侯]] that Zhao's safety depends on preventing Qin from swallowing [[HanState|韩国]] and [[WeiState|魏国]] first. When Qin attacks Wei, captures [[LongJia|龙甲]], and threatens to break the timing of the coalition, Su Qin manipulates [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] into entering Qin, where Zhang Yi wins [[YingSi|秦惠文王]]'s trust and temporarily protects Su Qin's room to build the alliance.

## Key Claims
- Hezong is explained first through geography: north-south alignment among the eastern states gives the policy its name, while Qin's west-east orientation supplies the contrast behind [[LianhengStrategy|连横]].
- The episode rejects a moralized peace reading of hezong. The coalition exists to isolate Qin and combine weaker states against the strongest state.
- Su Qin tells Zhao Suhou that Qin hesitates to attack Zhao directly because [[HanState|韩国]] and [[WeiState|魏国]] can threaten its rear, so Zhao cannot treat those states' survival as someone else's problem.
- Su Qin argues that the six eastern states have much more land and manpower than Qin, but only if they solve the coordination problem and resist being divided state by state.
- The proposed alliance mechanism uses a meeting at Huan Shui, hostage exchange, mutual rescue obligations, and collective punishment of defectors to address low trust among the states.
- Su Qin criticizes lianheng advocates as people who use Qin's power to scare rulers into ceding land that does not belong to the persuaders themselves.
- Qin's sudden attack on Wei, defeat of more than forty thousand Wei troops, capture of Long Jia, and seizure of Diaoyin create an immediate timing problem for Su Qin's coalition work.
- Su Qin's treatment of Zhang Yi is a practical extension of [[ChuaimoTechnique|揣摩]]: he reads Zhang Yi's pride, resentment, and ambition, then secretly funds the path that lets Zhang Yi become useful inside Qin.
- Zhang Yi's later promise not to trouble Su Qin while Su Qin lives makes their rivalry mutually enabling in this episode rather than simply oppositional.

## Key Quotes
> "与赵从亲，天下为一" - phrase used to frame the opening Yan-Zhao move toward hezong.

> "南北为纵，东西为横" - the episode's compact explanation of the names behind hezong and lianheng.

> "不是追求和平" - the episode's warning against reading hezong as idealistic pacifism.
