# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》191｜汉朝开国武将之首 为何叛逃匈奴

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode turns [[LuWan|卢绾]] from trusted childhood intimate and Yan king into the next exposed different-surname king after [[ChenXi|陈豨]], [[HanXin|韩信]], [[PengYue|彭越]], and [[YingBu|英布 / 黥布]]. [[ZhangShengWesternHan|张胜]] is persuaded in [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] by [[ZangYan|臧衍]] that fast suppression of Chen Xi will leave [[YanState|燕国]] as [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s next target; Lu Wan then secretly protects Zhang Sheng, sends [[FanQiLuWanEnvoy|范齐]] to keep Chen Xi resisting, and is exposed by surrendered sources. Liu Bang responds with investigation, then with [[FanKuai|樊哙]]'s attack on Yan and a public amnesty-and-reward edict that separates Lu Wan from Yan officials and commoners. The episode closes before narrating Lu Wan's final Xiongnu defection in full, then adds a source-scoped note about a South Sea kingship carved nominally from [[ZhaoTuo|赵佗]]'s [[Nanyue|南越]] field.

## Key Claims
- Lu Wan's crisis is presented as the strongest test of personal trust inside early Han different-surname king politics: lifelong intimacy with Liu Bang does not survive the structural fear created by repeated removals of non-Liu powerholders.
- Zhang Sheng's turning point is not simple bribery or treason. Zang Yan reframes Yan's anti-Chen-Xi effort as self-harm, because ending the northern rebellion quickly may free Liu Bang to remove Yan next.
- Lu Wan's covert policy is defensive delay: he secretly preserves Zhang Sheng, sends him back to Xiongnu as an envoy channel, and asks Fan Qi to tell Chen Xi not to settle the war quickly.
- The episode extends [[InvestigationDrivenRebellionEscalation|调查推动式反叛升级]]: once Lu Wan refuses summons, Liu Bang sends [[ShenYiji|审食其]] and [[ZhaoYao|赵尧]] to investigate, which makes Lu Wan hide more deeply and speak openly about fear of [[LuZhi|吕雉]].
- Liu Bang's final response combines military force and political separation. He sends Fan Kuai against Yan while offering pardon, rank, and safety to Yan officials and residents who do not follow Lu Wan.
- The source treats Lu Wan's motive as ambiguous and situational rather than as an immediate ambition to overthrow Han; his flight to the border is framed as waiting for Liu Bang's recovery and a chance to explain.
- The South Sea kingship note is presented as likely nominal because the episode says the title would carve [[NanhaiCommandery|南海郡]] out of Zhao Tuo's recognized Nanyue base but may not have carried practical control.

## Key Quotes
> "卢绾果然反了" - Liu Bang's conclusion after Xiongnu surrenderers confirm Zhang Sheng's protected envoy role.

> "比亲兄弟还要亲" - the episode's opening frame for Lu Wan and Liu Bang's childhood intimacy.

> "不追随卢绾的官吏" - the edict's separation of Yan officials from Lu Wan's fate.
