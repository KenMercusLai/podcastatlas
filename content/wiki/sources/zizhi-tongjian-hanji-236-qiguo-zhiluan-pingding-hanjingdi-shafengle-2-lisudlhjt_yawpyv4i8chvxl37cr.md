# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》236｜七国之乱平定，汉景帝杀疯了（2）

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the military and political settlement of the [[RebellionOfTheSevenStatesWesternHan|Rebellion of the Seven States]]. [[LiJiWesternHan|郦寄]] and [[LuanBu|栾布]] combine forces after a prolonged siege, divert river water into Handan, and bring [[LiuSuiZhaoKingWesternHan|赵王刘遂]]'s resistance to an end; the court then distinguishes coerced or strategically resistant kingdoms from the leading rebels.

The clearest case is [[LiuZhiJibeiKingWesternHan|济北王刘志]]. [[GongsunJueWesternHan|公孙爵]] reframes Jibei's hesitation as useful resistance that delayed and isolated the coalition, persuading [[LiuWuLiangKingWesternHan|梁孝王刘武]] to seek clemency from [[HanJingdi|汉景帝]]. The episode closes with pardon, selective restoration, reassignment of royal territories, and smaller grants, extending [[PostRebellionAccountability|post-rebellion accountability]] from punishment into differentiated reintegration and structural reduction of princely power.

## Key Claims

- Li Ji fails to take Handan for more than seven months; after the Wu-Chu defeat, Luan Bu returns from Qi, joins him, and the combined forces breach the city by diverting river water.
- Liu Sui dies by suicide after Handan's wall collapses, ending Zhao's remaining armed resistance in the episode's account.
- Jingdi treats Qi as coerced rather than willingly rebellious and permits the Qi crown prince Liu Shou to inherit.
- Gongsun Jue argues that Jibei's refusal to surrender deprived Wu of an ally, slowed the coalition, and prevented a broader eastern consolidation.
- Liu Wu transmits the argument to Jingdi; Liu Zhi avoids collective punishment and is reassigned as king of Zichuan.
- The episode attributes Wei Wan's appointment as commandant to military service, prior restraint around the crown prince, and Emperor Wen's deathbed recommendation.
- Jingdi pardons implicated officials, civilians, and military deserters, while [[EmpressDowagerDouWesternHan|窦太后]] blocks restoration of Wu but permits restoration of Chu.
- The final enfeoffment reallocates former rebel territory and gives most new kingdoms to Jingdi's sons; the host reads the smaller grants as a continuing reduction of royal power.

## Key Quotes

> “济北国坚守城池，始终不肯投降。” - the conduct claim on which Gongsun Jue builds the case for clemency.

> “如果坚持道义、有功劳的藩王仍被猜疑，会引发忠心藩王的恐惧。” - the political-risk argument used to move the request beyond private mercy.

- Name boundary: 公孙爵 is preserved as supplied. The exact written form, speech, and route to Liu Wu require primary-text comparison.
