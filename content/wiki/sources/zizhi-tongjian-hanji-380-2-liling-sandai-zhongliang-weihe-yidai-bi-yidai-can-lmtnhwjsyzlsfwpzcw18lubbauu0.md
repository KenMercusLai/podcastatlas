# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》380-2｜李陵三代忠良，为何一代比一代惨？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] short episode completes the immediate aftermath of [[LiLingWesternHan|李陵]]'s surrender introduced in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-380-1-liling-touxiang-xiongnu-ruhe-yanbian-cheng-gengdade-beiju-ls70jnzybc7z3of-tpfbdwb9ns89|Hanji 380-1]]. [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] reportedly regrets his handling of Li Ling and sends [[GongsunAoWesternHan|公孙敖]] to find him, but Gongsun Ao returns without success and reports that Li Ling is training [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] forces and making weapons; Wudi then orders Li Ling's family killed.

The episode says the accusation confused Li Ling with [[LiXuWesternHan|李绪]], another surrendered Han officer. Li Ling kills Li Xu after learning that his mother and wife died for the mistaken attribution, while the chanyu protects Li Ling, marries a daughter to him, and gives him military authority. A second branch follows Gongsun Ao through repeated command, defeat, capital sentences, escape, recapture, and final clan destruction in a Wugu case, before the host turns his career into a source-scoped lesson about ambition outrunning ability and self-knowledge.

## Key Claims

- Han Wudi is presented as trying to repair the Li Ling affair by sending Gongsun Ao into Xiongnu territory to find and retrieve him.
- After failing to find Li Ling, Gongsun Ao reports captive testimony that Li Ling is teaching Xiongnu forces to fight Han and manufacture weapons; Wudi responds by killing Li Ling's family.
- The episode later identifies Li Xu, not Li Ling, as the surrendered Han officer behind the reported military assistance, making the family punishment rest on mistaken attribution.
- Li Ling retaliates by having Li Xu killed, then survives hostility from the chanyu's mother because the chanyu protects and temporarily relocates him.
- The chanyu's daughter, title, and military authority place Li Ling inside Xiongnu elite power rather than treating him only as a passive captive.
- Liu Bo is enfeoffed as King of Changyi in 97 BCE and is presented as Han Wudi and Lady Li's favored younger son before Liu Fuling's birth.
- Gongsun Ao's repeated appointments, defeats, death sentences, escape, and eventual clan destruction are used to argue that desire for rank can become lethal when ability and self-knowledge do not match the role.

## Key Quotes

> "认清自己比认清别人更重要。" - the host's closing judgment on Gongsun Ao's career.

> "能力不行，但是他的欲望很强。" - the episode's source-scoped explanation for his repeated return to dangerous command.
