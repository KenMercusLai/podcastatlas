# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》135｜番外篇2 女相师因看命能力被刘邦封侯

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]]番外 episode completes the [[XuFuPhysiognomist|许负 / Xu Fu (physiognomist)]] branch opened in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-135-fanwaipian-1-zhengshi-jizai-kan-nvxiangshi-ruhe-gaiming-ls8lfj4pq-ncsk7x3er7nlb0ltkk|Qinji 135 番外篇1]]. It follows the podcast's three prophecy-payoff structure: Xu Fu recognizes [[LiuBang|刘邦]] and has Wen County surrender, [[WeiBao|魏豹]] misreads [[BaoJi|薄姬]]'s future-emperor child prediction, and later [[DengTong|邓通]] and [[ZhouYafu|周亚夫]] fulfill seemingly impossible starvation predictions. The ending shifts from story to method by linking 相术 to observing a person's spirit, bones, and bearing, while the wiki keeps those claims source-scoped rather than treating physiognomy as empirically proven.

## Key Claims
- The episode repeats the origin frame that Xu Fu was born in 221 BCE in Wen County of Henei Commandery and was raised as someone able to recognize the timing of a coming true ruler.
- When Liu Bang passes Wen County, Xu Fu identifies him as the destined ruler, brings him into her family home, and has her father hand over the county seal, making Liu Bang's acquisition of Wen nearly bloodless in the source's telling.
- After founding the Han, Liu Bang remembers Xu Fu's earlier support and, in the transcript's wording, grants her the title "明慈平侯"; this title claim is recorded as the episode's rendering.
- Wei Bao hears Xu Fu say Bao Ji will bear a future emperor and assumes the prediction favors his own line, but the episode presents this as a [[ProphecyFulfillmentDisplacement|prophecy fulfillment displacement]] because the child is later [[LiuHeng|刘恒]], son of Liu Bang.
- Liu Heng becomes Emperor Wen in 180 BCE, honors Xu Fu, and asks her to read Deng Tong; she says Deng Tong has a wealthy face but will ultimately starve in poverty.
- Emperor Wen tries to defeat the prediction by giving Deng Tong a copper mountain and minting privileges, but [[HanJingdi|汉景帝 / Emperor Jing of Han]] later confiscates his wealth and the episode says Deng Tong dies poor and hungry.
- Xu Fu also predicts that Zhou Yafu will inherit a marquis title, later become a major military and political figure, and finally die of hunger; the episode fulfills this through Zhou Yafu's imprisonment and self-starvation after his son's burial-armor purchase triggers suspicion.
- The closing discussion treats ancient 相术 as a discipline of observing "神" and "骨," mentioning texts such as 《神相全篇》, 《麻衣神相》, 《柳庄神相》, 《水镜神相》, and 《冰鉴》.

## Key Quotes
> "交出县令大印" - the surrender action attached to Xu Fu's recognition of Liu Bang.

> "最后会因为贫苦而饿死" - the podcast's compact form of the Deng Tong prediction.
