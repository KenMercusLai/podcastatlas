# 《资治通鉴·周纪》59丨一朝君王一朝臣

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s eighth and ninth years by closing the first resistance phase of [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]]'s [[HufuQishe|胡服骑射]] reform and moving [[QinState|秦国]] into the post-[[QinWuwang|秦武王]] court. Zhao Wuling Wang persuades [[ZhaoChengWarringStates|赵成]] by reframing Hu clothing as national defense against [[QiState|齐国]], [[ZhongshanState|中山国]], Yan, Donghu, Loufan, Qin, and Han rather than as cultural imitation. The episode then shows [[GanMao|甘茂]] losing security under [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] after the Yiyang victory: his advice to return [[WusuiWarringStates|武遂]] to [[HanState|韩国]] triggers resentment from [[XiangShou|向寿]] and the Gongsun faction, and he eventually flees to [[QiState|齐国]].

## Key Claims
- Zhao Wuling Wang answers elite resistance by making [[HufuQishe|胡服骑射]] a survival problem: Zhao is surrounded by strong states and frontier groups, lacks cavalry, and has suffered humiliation from [[ZhongshanState|中山国]].
- [[ZhaoChengWarringStates|赵成]] is persuaded enough to wear Hu clothing to court the next day, allowing the reform to move from ruler initiative to broader state implementation.
- The reform forces Zhao people to abandon wide-sleeved Central Plains clothing for short Hu dress, belts, boots, riding, and archery, aiming to build a cavalry force rather than only change appearance.
- After the [[YiyangWarringStates|宜阳]] victory, [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] sends [[XiangShou|向寿]] to comfort residents, settle boundaries, and regularize taxes and household registers.
- [[GanMao|甘茂]] advises returning [[WusuiWarringStates|武遂]] to Han to stabilize the new Qin-Han situation; Qin Zhaoxiang Wang accepts, but Xiang Shou and the Gongsun faction resent Gan Mao.
- Qin Wuwang's death changes the court balance. Gan Mao's prior military achievement cannot fully protect him once Chulizi's side gains room under the new ruler.
- Gan Mao flees when the army reaches Puban; [[Chulizi|樗里疾 / 樗里子]] cannot continue the offensive alone and makes peace before withdrawing.
- The host reads Gan Mao's flight as [[PatronSuccessionExposure|靠山更替暴露风险]] for an outsider minister: merit, office, and [[MilitaryMeritRankSystem|military rank rewards]] still remain vulnerable to identity, faction, and succession.
- Zhao Wuling Wang expands north toward Yuzhong, receives horses from Hu chiefs, sends envoys to Qin, Han, Chu, Wei, and Qi, appoints [[ZhaoGuWarringStates|赵固]] to manage Hu affairs, and recruits Hu fighters.
- [[ChuState|楚国]], Qi, and Han renew [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] against Qin while Qin's brothers fight over succession; [[WeiRan|魏冉]] uses force to secure Ying Ji's accession as Qin Zhaoxiang Wang.

## Key Quotes
> "一朝君王一朝臣" - the episode's frame for how a court transition can revalue even a successful minister.

> "以胡制胡" - the reform logic of learning frontier military practice to answer frontier threats.
