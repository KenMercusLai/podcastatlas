# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》863丨一把火烧出的“战斗力”

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 164 CE Huan-era layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-864-mingchen-zhihou-weihe-cansi-yu-diwang-zhishou-lqwexx6nchx10roxsrvh8c0ktkfq|Hanji 864]]. It opens with [[ParentalExpectationShame|父母期望羞耻化]] as a source-scoped family-relationship frame, then centers [[DuShangLateHan|度尚 / 杜尚]] defeating [[BuYangLateHan|卜阳]] and [[PanHongLateHan|潘鸿]] by burning captured wealth to restart military desire.

The second half shifts from battlefield motivation to court movement. [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]]'s southern tour creates heavy entourage costs for local officials, [[HuTengLateHan|胡腾]] proposes stronger inspection authority for the [[Jingzhou|荆州]] route, and [[YangBingLateHan|杨秉]] pushes back against casual 郎 appointments generated by eunuch and favorite access. The closing introduces [[KouRongLateHan|寇荣]]'s jealousy-risk prelude before Hanji 864's fatal petition case.

## Key Claims

- The opening argues that excessive parental expectation can turn love into pressure, causing children to hide their actual state from parents through shame.
- In 164 CE, [[DuShangLateHan|度尚 / 杜尚]] recruits local Man and Yi troops in [[Jingzhou|荆州]] and defeats a large group of rebels or bandits before pursuing the remaining [[GuiyangCommanderyLateHan|桂阳郡]] leaders.
- [[BuYangLateHan|卜阳]] and [[PanHongLateHan|潘鸿]] are presented as Guiyang bandit leaders who retreat into mountain strongholds after long-term plunder.
- Du Shang recognizes that captured treasure has made his soldiers satisfied and less willing to keep fighting, so he stages a hunt, secretly burns the camp stores, and turns the apparent loss into renewed hunger for action.
- [[SpoilsLossMobilization|战利品损失动员]] works in this episode because Du Shang couples the shock of lost wealth with a plausible promise of further enemy treasure and an immediate dawn attack plan.
- Huan's southern tour burdens local offices with retinue logistics; [[HuTengLateHan|胡腾]] responds by asking to raise Jingzhou inspection authority and strengthen discipline over accompanying officials.
- [[YangBingLateHan|杨秉]] argues that 郎 appointments should not become casual favors because those posts carry palace-guard and local-governance responsibility.
- [[KouRongLateHan|寇荣]] is introduced as an aloof, high-status court figure whose imperial marriage ties and eunuch-side jealousy set up Hanji 864's accusation and execution branch.
