# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》539丨汉成帝与张放的情感纠葛

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Yongshi 2 / 15 BCE under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]], immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-540-taihou-de-zangli-lnd6gmr_bk7dv8cbb1zzoagtbwsd|Hanji 540]]'s funeral-accountability sequence. It centers on Chengdi's intimate favorite [[ZhangFangWesternHan|张放]], [[BanBoWesternHan|班伯]]'s wine-and-Shang-Zhou remonstrance, [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]'s pressure to send Zhang Fang away, and the impeachment filed by [[XueXuanWesternHan|薛宣]] and [[ZhaiFangjinWesternHan|翟方进]].

The durable synthesis is that private attachment becomes a court-governance problem when a favorite's palace access, revelry, and household backing provoke dowager and ministerial intervention. Zhang Fang's exile does not end Chengdi's affection, but it shows that a favorite protected by ruler intimacy can still be constrained by dowager authority, outer relatives, and ministers; Xue Xuan's role is read as family self-protection and political cutting rather than simple moral courage.

## Key Claims

- Chengdi's carriage conversation around a Shang Zhou and Daji image gives Ban Bo a chance to redirect the story from blaming women toward warning against wine, disorder, and ruler indulgence.
- Zhang Fang reacts badly to Ban Bo's implicit correction and leaves the group, making his own sensitivity visible to palace observers.
- Wang Zhengjun uses the incident to press Chengdi to send Zhang Fang back to his fief, then mobilizes outer relatives and ministers when Chengdi appears reluctant.
- Xue Xuan and Zhai Fangjin impeach Zhang Fang for arrogant, dissolute, and abusive conduct by his followers and relatives.
- Chengdi is forced to move Zhang Fang out as Beidi commandant, later briefly recalls him when [[JingwuPrincessWesternHan|靖武公主]] is ill, and then sends him out again as Hedong commandant.
- Zhang Fang's protection rests on family status, marriage ties, imperial intimacy, and his role as Chengdi's drinking, travel, and pleasure companion.
- Xue Xuan's impeachment is framed as a politically useful cut from a risky household tie: Zhang Fang is not Xue's biological son, and defending him would expose Xue and Jingwu Princess to dowager and court anger.
