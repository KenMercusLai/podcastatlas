# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》451｜儒者萧望之：谁优秀我就弄死谁!

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills [[HanYanshouWesternHan|韩延寿]]'s death under [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] and explains why later Xiao Wangzhi notes refer to Xiao's earlier harmful conduct toward Han Yanshou. It follows a local-governance star whose popularity in Zuo Fengyi threatens [[XiaoWangzhiWesternHan|萧望之]]'s self-image and position: an old East Commandery spending accusation becomes an investigation, Han Yanshou counterattacks by accusing Xiao, and Xiao successfully reframes the counterattack as obstruction of lawful supervision.

The durable synthesis is that the case is not a clean innocent-victim story. The episode says Han Yanshou had real old-account exposure, including lavish examination display, public funds, and state materials, but Xiao's political skill lies in turning that exposure plus Han's failed counteraccusation into a capital case. [[BingJiWesternHan|丙吉]]'s initial wish to let the matter go after amnesty makes the death read as a discretionary escalation rather than inevitable routine discipline.

## Key Claims

- Han Yanshou's strong two-year record in Zuo Fengyi gives him popular legitimacy and creates a comparison pressure against Xiao Wangzhi's earlier tenure.
- Xiao Wangzhi's pursuit of Han Yanshou is interpreted by the host as a mixture of jealousy, insecurity, office competition, and self-protection, not as a simple public-interest investigation.
- Bing Ji tries to stop the case by pointing to the general amnesty, but Xiao orders an East Commandery investigation after Bing refuses to pursue it.
- Han Yanshou's counteraccusation against Xiao collapses because the alleged million-cash misuse is treated as fabricated and witness testimony as coerced.
- Xiao changes the case frame by presenting Han's counteraccusation as resistance to lawful central supervision rather than equal mutual impeachment.
- The investigation still finds Han's earlier East Commandery spending violations real, including lavish military-examination costs, use of stored copper, paid labor, and expensive carriage fittings.
- Xuan approves the death sentence after ministers treat Han as both guilty on the old accounts and guilty of falsely accusing a superior.
- The thousands of officials and commoners who weep and offer wine at Han's execution preserve the tension between local virtue, public affection, and central-political vulnerability.

## Key Quotes

> "谁优秀我就弄死谁" - the title's compressed judgment on Xiao Wangzhi's conduct toward Han Yanshou.

> "能力、政绩、名声并不必然带来安全" - the source summary's governing lesson from the case.
