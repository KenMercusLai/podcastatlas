## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianwu 1 / 25 CE immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-680-ming-sang-chimei-zhe-wei-qiyi-huangdi-maigu-hechu-lurtvfqerb0vf3a33msmd-txr9pv|Hanji 680]]. It ties [[ChimeiRebellion|赤眉]] entering [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] and [[LiuXuanGengshiEmperor|更始帝刘玄]]'s flight to [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]]'s first founding choices: protecting Liu Xuan in name, elevating [[ZhuoMaoEasternHan|卓茂]] as a virtue exemplar, guaranteeing [[ZhuWeiEasternHan|朱鲔]]'s survival to win [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]], backing [[DuShiEasternHan|杜诗]]'s discipline enforcement, and settling the capital at Luoyang.

The durable synthesis is that Liu Xiu's early order uses clemency and discipline together. Zhu Wei can surrender because Liu Xiu makes safety credible despite Liu Yan's murder, while Du Shi's execution of Xiao Guang shows that sparing a former enemy does not mean tolerating troops who harm civilians.

## Key Claims

- After Chimei occupies Chang'an, Liu Xuan flees, Liu Gong and Dingtao King Liu Zhi pursue him, Yan Ben controls or protects him near Gaoling, and Cao Jing dies resisting Chimei.
- Liu Xiu enfeoffs Liu Xuan as Huaiyang King, declares that killing Liu Xuan would be great rebellion, and offers marquisate reward for anyone who delivers him.
- Liu Xiu appoints Zhuo Mao as Taifu and Baode Marquis, using an elder humane official as a visible model for teaching officials by honoring goodness.
- Zhu Wei refuses to surrender because he had participated in Liu Yan's killing and opposed sending Liu Xiu north, but Liu Xiu promises to preserve his office and title and swears by the Yellow River.
- Cen Peng, a former Zhu Wei subordinate, becomes the trust intermediary; Zhu Wei tests him with a city-wall rope, then submits bound before Liu Xiu after the guarantee holds.
- Liu Xiu personally unbinds Zhu Wei, sends him back to Luoyang overnight, receives Zhu Wei, Su Mao, and their army's surrender the next day, and incorporates Zhu Wei with office and marquisate rather than revenge.
- Du Shi is sent to pacify Luoyang's people, kills Xiao Guang after Xiao Guang's troops continue abusing civilians, and is rewarded by Liu Xiu for protecting public order.
- Liu Xuan later surrenders to Chimei through Liu Gong after Liu Penzi offers terms, but Chimei commanders try to kill him; Liu Gong's plea and suicide attempt win a temporary reprieve before the Hanji 680 killing.

## Key Quotes

> "成大事者不计小怨" - the episode's frame for Liu Xiu's promise to spare Zhu Wei.

> "若河水在，吾不食言" - Liu Xiu's oath securing Zhu Wei's surrender.

> "陛下，我已经尽力了，让我先去死" - Liu Gong's plea when Chimei commanders move to kill Liu Xuan.
