# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》631丨古代杀人，真会把白骨堆成山？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 7-8 CE suppression of the [[ZhaiYiRebellion|翟义起义]] between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-629-liujiaren-shui-kangqi-fankang-wangmang-de-daqi-lmhws7-jptdh9tf45bqtlfvzdw55|Hanji 629]] and the 8-9 CE accession sequence in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-633-nuza-chuanguo-yuxi-ta-jing-ruci-ganglie-lnmnwbk28ayllz5fip1a1kzm1ais|Hanji 633]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-634-huozai-nanren-yingzi-beihou-de-ta-can-can-can-lm3wub0l70yxwwyvwi15j93i9lwk|Hanji 634]]. It follows [[WangMang|王莽]] publishing a Zhou-style war proclamation, defeating Zhai Yi, rewarding commanders, destroying Zhai family graves and kin, and piling rebel bodies at traffic routes as public warning.

The durable synthesis is that Wang Mang's pre-accession consolidation already joined coercion, ceremony, and sacred signs. He claims he will defend Han legitimacy and return power to [[RuziYing|孺子婴 / 刘婴]], while using military victory, five-rank title reform, family punishment, corpse-pile terror, filial mourning performance, clan punishment, and reported fuming/omens to make the removal of the "假" in "假皇帝" politically thinkable.

## Key Claims

- Wang Mang answers Zhai Yi's anti-Wang-Mang challenge with a Zhou-style public proclamation that presents him as guardian of Han succession rather than as usurper.
- Court forces defeat Zhai Yi's army, and Wang Mang converts victory into large-scale noble rewards and a five-rank title order modeled on Zhou categories.
- The punishment of Zhai Yi extends beyond battlefield defeat into grave destruction, clan extermination, corpse burial with poisonous creatures, and public corpse-pile display.
- The body piles at Puyang, Wuyan, Yu, Huaili, and Zhouzhi turn dead enemies into warning signs for the living, making the episode an early Wang Mang case of [[PublicCorpseHumiliation|公开尸体羞辱]].
- Wang Mang's light mourning for his mother is presented as a political posture: acting as Han's imperial successor requires public distance from his birth family.
- The Wang Guang and Dou Kuang case shows Wang Mang continuing to perform rule-bound impartiality by sacrificing kin, echoing earlier family-punishment reputation politics.
- Reports of new wells, a stone ox, and inscribed stone are accepted as fuming/auspicious evidence for Wang Mang's coming accession, extending [[FumingMonopolyBackfire|符命垄断反噬]] back into the pre-accession production phase.

## Key Quotes

> "反虏逆贼鲸鲵" - the sign text attached to the displayed rebel corpse piles.

> "把假皇帝的假字去掉" - the episode's formulation of Wang Mang's accession goal.

> "精官" / "金官" - the transcript's rendering for the corpse-pile tower used as intimidation.
