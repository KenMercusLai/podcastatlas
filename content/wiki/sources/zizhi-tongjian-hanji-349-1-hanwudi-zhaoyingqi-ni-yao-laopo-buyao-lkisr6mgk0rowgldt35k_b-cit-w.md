# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》349-1｜汉武帝：赵婴齐，你要老婆不要？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode develops the Chang'an-service prehistory of [[ZhaoYingqiNanyue|赵婴齐]]. It describes the [[Nanyue|南越]] heir as remaining near [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] in palace guard service for twelve years and interprets that proximity as an attempt to observe, influence, and cultivate a future ruler rather than as simple hospitality.

The episode also treats Wudi's arrangement of Zhao Yingqi's marriage to a woman from the old Zhao region as part of the same strategy. She bears Zhao Xing and Zhao Cigong and appears to be the later [[QueenDowagerJiuNanyue|樛氏]], but the supplied summary does not name her; the claim that she was a deliberately planted agent who influenced Zhao Yingqi through intimacy therefore remains source-scoped. The sequel matters to the judgment: Zhao Yingqi later shows selective accommodation, not complete obedience.

## Key Claims

- Zhao Hu sends his already married son Zhao Yingqi to Chang'an, where the episode describes him as a political hostage as well as a court servant.
- Zhao Yingqi reportedly serves near Wudi as a palace guard for twelve years and behaves cautiously in that role.
- The host interprets close service as a long-term mechanism for observation, relationship building, and influence over a future Nanyue king.
- Wudi arranges a marriage between Zhao Yingqi and a woman from the Handan region, linking her origin to the Zhao geography associated with Zhao Yingqi's ancestry.
- The marriage produces two sons, Zhao Xing and Zhao Cigong; the supplied summary does not name their mother.
- The episode interprets the wife as an inside influence channel and compares the arrangement to a beauty stratagem or espionage, but supplies no direct evidence of a formal intelligence commission.
- After Zhao Hu's death, Zhao Yingqi returns to inherit Nanyue and responds to Han pressure selectively rather than becoming fully obedient.

## Key Quotes

> “这是大事，不能心急。” - the episode's lesson attached to Wudi's reported twelve-year cultivation strategy.

> “赵婴齐并没有完全对汉武帝言听计从。” - the supplied summary's boundary on the strategy's result.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. The episode adds detail to Hanji 348-2's cultivation opening, while Hanji 349-2 shows that prolonged court exposure produces partial accommodation rather than full personal control.
- The supplied summary calls Zhao Yingqi a hostage and “palace bodyguard” but does not provide the formal terms, office title, coercive conditions, or primary-text wording for his Chang'an service.
- The wife is not named in the supplied summary. Her identification as the later 樛氏 is inferred from her being Zhao Xing's Han-region mother and from the adjacent succession account, not directly stated here.
- The common-region match, formal-agent status, “beauty stratagem,” pillow-talk mechanism, Wudi's private intention, and exact twelve-year influence process are the host's strategic interpretation rather than independently established facts.
- Zhao Yingqi's earlier wife and child, Zhao Cigong's later life, and the full circumstances of Zhao Yingqi's return and accession remain outside this episode's evidence.
