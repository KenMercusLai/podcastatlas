# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》276｜“霸陵呵夜”，将军李广为何被小兵欺凌

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[LiGuangHanGeneral|李广]] from loss of office to appointment as governor of [[YoubeipingCommandery|右北平]]. Its central “霸陵呵夜” story separates lawful restraint from heroic humiliation: a drunken Baling commandant refuses to let the former general violate the night ban, and Li Guang later summons and kills him after recovering official and military power. The host therefore treats the sequel as [[PersonalRevengeThroughStatePower|以国力行私仇]], not as a vindication of Li Guang's reputation.

The episode also attributes an offensive from Yanmen to [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]], supported by Li Xi and by Li Guang's eastern pressure, and reports several thousand Xiongnu killed before the force returns intact. It closes with a source-scoped account of a large Yemaek submission through Liaodong, the road-building burden imposed on Yan-Zhao residents, and a preview of the conflict between central authority and princely autonomy that [[ZhuFuYan|主父偃]] will address.

## Key Claims

- After Han Anguo's death, Han Wudi appoints Li Guang governor of Youbeiping, where his reputation is said to deter Xiongnu incursions for several years.
- While still a commoner, Li Guang returns at night from hunting and drinking near Lantian and is stopped at Baling; the commandant insists that even a serving general must obey the night ban.
- After regaining office, Li Guang has the commandant accompany him to the frontier and kills him; the host judges this as private revenge exercised through recovered public authority.
- The episode infers apology, apparent forgiveness, and deception before the killing, but the supplied document does not establish those steps directly.
- Han Wudi reportedly uses Li Guang's eastern pressure to hold Xiongnu attention while Wei Qing leads thirty thousand cavalry from Yanmen and Li Xi blocks possible reinforcement from the east.
- Wei Qing is said to kill several thousand Xiongnu and return with the army intact; the episode reads this as a probing offensive and as evidence of increasing imperial trust in him.
- A Yemaek leader named Nan Lü reportedly submits with 280,000 people through Liaodong, after which integration and road building impose labor and fiscal costs on Yan-Zhao residents.
- The closing deaths of the Lu and Changsha kings introduce the unresolved tension between imperial centralization and princely autonomy before the next Zhufu Yan episode.

## Key Quotes

> “今将军尚不得夜行，何乃故也。” - the commandant's insistence, as summarized by the episode, that former rank does not override the night ban.

> “公报私仇。” - the host's judgment on Li Guang's later killing of the commandant.
