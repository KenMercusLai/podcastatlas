# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》776丨汉史探密：蔡伦是怎样发明“造纸术”的？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 102-105 CE [[EmperorHeOfHan|汉和帝]] layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-777-bi-jian-wuzetian-nianya-cixi-zhege-nvren-liaobude-lk7j4qv-sqfopzbii6rc-ivbuxs|Hanji 777]]. It explains [[CaiLunLateHan|蔡伦]]'s paper-making contribution through [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|邓皇后]]'s literary taste, frugality, and preference for writing materials, then turns to He-era court administration: [[XuFangLateHan|徐防]]'s [[Taixue|太学]] examination reform, eclipse politics, southern-tour remonstrance, [[TangQiangLateHan|唐羌]]'s objection to Lingnan fruit tribute, Northern Xiongnu diplomacy, and [[GoguryeoLateHan|高句丽]]'s Liaodong attack.

The durable synthesis is that court demand could create both innovation and burden. Deng's preference for writing over rare treasures makes [[CourtDemandDrivenPapermaking|宫廷需求驱动造纸]] plausible in the source's telling, while Tang Qiang's memorial shows perishable luxury tribute turning into [[PerishableTributeLaborBurden|鲜贡劳役负担]] until Emperor He stops accepting it.

## Key Claims

- Deng is presented before her regency as cultured, fond of books and calligraphy, and hostile to extravagant tribute, allowing pens and ink to substitute for rare curiosities.
- Cai Lun's palace role as zhongchangshi and shangfang ling gives him access to imperial material needs and production capacity.
- The episode says Cai Lun uses cheaper fiber materials such as old hemp items and bark with a new process to make light, soft writing paper, presents it to Emperor He, receives praise, and sees the process spread.
- The later name "Cai Hou paper" is linked to Cai Lun's 114 CE Longting marquisate under Deng's continued favor.
- In 102 CE, Xu Fang criticizes Taixue testing for free-form opinion and mutual tolerance, then proposes source-backed standards drawn from recognized classical schools.
- In 103 CE, Emperor He treats a solar eclipse as his own responsibility and keeps royal brothers in the capital, then accepts ministerial remonstrance by turning back from a planned southern extension toward Jiangling.
- Tang Qiang reframes fresh longan and lychee tribute from a delicacy into a fatal transport burden for Jiaozhi-region households and relay workers; Emperor He orders the imperial kitchen not to accept such goods.
- In 104 CE, Northern Xiongnu envoys seek submission, tribute, heqin, and restoration of the old Huhanye chanyu covenant, but Emperor He withholds full recognition while still giving generous rewards.
- In 105 CE, Goguryeo king Gong raids Liaodong and plunders six counties before Liaodong administrator Geng Kui defeats the Goguryeo force.

## Key Quotes

> "蔡侯纸" - later name attached to Cai Lun's paper after his Longting marquisate.

> "五十个观点" - Xu Fang's proposed test basis for classical examination.

> "后来者可以挽救" - Tang Qiang's memorial logic for stopping lethal tribute transport before more people die.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is recorded. This source backfills the 102-105 CE layer before Hanji 777's death/accession threshold.
- Attribution caution: the episode title says Cai Lun "invented papermaking," but the source details support the narrower claim that Cai Lun improved, presented, and spread a low-cost fiber-paper process through court backing.
- Chronology refinement: Hanji 776 places Deng's literary and austerity profile before her later regency, making the Hanji 777-795 Deng page begin with a pre-regency cultural-material layer.
- Scope caution: Zheng Zhong or the unnamed da changqiu enfeoffment, Liu Sheng in the 103 prince-retention context, Zhang Pu, Chen Chong, the Northern Xiongnu envoy, Goguryeo king Gong, and exact disaster/office dates remain source-scoped unless later notes warrant standalone pages.
