# 《资治通鉴·周纪》44丨一个公司为什么不能有两位CEO？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode uses a [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] story about [[HanXuanhuiwang|韩宣惠王]] considering two chief ministers to discuss [[DualExecutivePowerRisk|双首长权力风险]]. [[MiuLiu|谬刘]] argues that appointing [[GongzhongHan|公众]] and [[GongshuHan|公叔]] together would create competing power centers, but the host complicates the lesson by noting that one unchecked minister can also become dangerous. The episode closes the show's 周纪二 sequence while linking Sima Guang's source selection to [[SongFactionalPolitics|宋代党争]] and the practical problem of choosing between hostile factions.

## Key Claims
- The source says the story is placed in 321 BCE, Zhou Xian Wang's forty-eighth year, but the host notes that [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] itself does not make the placement obvious.
- [[HanXuanhuiwang|韩宣惠王]] wants to appoint his two likely brothers, [[GongzhongHan|公众]] and [[GongshuHan|公叔]], as joint chief ministers.
- The host treats "公" as a clan-rank marker and "仲"/"叔" as ordering language, not as stable personal names.
- [[MiuLiu|谬刘]] opposes the plan by citing the Jin elite struggle before [[PartitionOfJin|三家分晋]], Qi Jian Gong's use of Tian Cheng and Kan Zhi, and Wei's paired use of [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] and [[GongsunYan|犀首公孙衍]] before losing Hexi.
- Miu Liu's logic is that two equally powerful executives will not remain equal: the stronger side builds domestic partisans and may hollow out the ruler, while the weaker side seeks foreign backing and may trade away state interests.
- The host says Miu Liu's examples are plausible but incomplete, because a single empowered minister can also monopolize power, usurp authority, or ruin the state if the person is not worthy.
- The deeper issue is therefore not only "one or two," but whether the chosen people are virtuous and capable, whether the power structure can handle conflict, and whether the adviser has hidden factional interests.
- The host speculates that Miu Liu may have been privately aligned with one of the two brothers and may have exaggerated the danger to help his own side.
- The episode connects the story to Sima Guang's Song context: when emperors tried to act, new-party and old-party coexistence often failed, forcing a choice rather than a stable middle arrangement.
- The episode ends by saying 周纪二 has concluded; the show has finished the first two of the 294 Zizhi Tongjian volumes.

## Key Quotes
> "一山能容二虎吗？" - the episode's governing question.

> "一个人就足以亡国" - the host's qualification against treating one chief minister as automatically safer.

> "周纪二就全部结束了" - closing marker for the show's second Zhou-annals volume.
