# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》468｜言多必失！萧望之如何因“嘴”吃大亏？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the setup immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-469-weishenme-junzi-dou-buguo-xiaoren-kankan-xiaowangzhi-de-xiachang-lj8iji-c3scj6vznntavm6awolho|Hanji 469]]. It frames early [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] court politics as a three-sided field among outer relatives led by [[ShiGaoWesternHan|史高]], Confucian teacher-ministers around [[XiaoWangzhiWesternHan|萧望之]] and [[ZhouKanWesternHan|周堪]], and Zhongshu eunuchs [[HongGongWesternHan|弘恭]] and [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]].

The durable synthesis is that factional politics becomes dangerous before the formal accusation begins. Xiao Wangzhi's side challenges eunuch control of Zhongshu, Shi Gao and the eunuchs converge against the Confucian group, and [[ZhengPengWesternHan|郑朋]] gains encouragement from Zhou Kan and Xiao by attacking the Xu-Shi outer-relative side. The episode closes on Zheng's rising expectations, making his later resentment in Hanji 469 more intelligible.

## Key Claims

- The episode opens with a factional-sociology frame: circles form around interests, enemies, protection, and hidden alliances, but access to an inner circle requires testing and carries risk.
- The host contrasts flexible "圆融" with rigid "执拗," arguing that achievement often requires adaptable, humane judgment rather than brittle moral insistence.
- In 48 BCE, [[HuhanyeChanyuWesternHan|呼韩邪单于]] asks Han for material aid, and the court sends grain from Yunzhong and Wuyuan while also establishing the Wuyi colonel office at Cheshi to strengthen Western Regions control.
- In early Yuan's court, Shi Gao and the Xu-Shi outer-relative families retain high status, while Hong Gong and Shi Xian control Zhongshu access and become increasingly important because Yuan is often ill.
- Xiao Wangzhi, Zhou Kan, [[LiuXiang|刘向 / 刘更生]], and Jin Chang form a Confucian-adviser bloc that uses ancient institutions to guide Yuan and wins some imperial approval.
- Xiao Wangzhi's memorial against Zhongshu eunuchs directly attacks Hong Gong and Shi Xian's power base by arguing that a national pivot office should not be held by eunuchs.
- Yuan does not decisively back Xiao's side: Liu Xiang is moved out of the core role from jishizhong to zongzheng, showing the Confucian bloc's weak practical position.
- Zheng Peng tries to attach himself to Xiao's side by accusing the Xu and Shi families, especially Shi Gao, of private patronage networks and treasonous ambition.
- Zhou Kan and Xiao Wangzhi respond positively to Zheng Peng's attack, and Xiao's promise that Zheng will benefit creates the expectation that later becomes politically dangerous.

## Key Quotes

> "建功立业者多须圆之事，愤事失机者必执拗之人" - the host's conduct frame for Xiao Wangzhi's rigidity problem.

> "盼望破灭会心生怨恨" - the closing warning that Zheng Peng's frustrated expectation will matter later.
