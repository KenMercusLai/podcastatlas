# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》767丨聪明人，都擅长“演戏”

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 92-93 CE [[EmperorHeOfHan|汉和帝]] layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-768-lishi-gaosu-ni-jidu-shi-shaxin-lgk8qoxvs5ax8oikgecrxqvispna|Hanji 768]]. It uses the opening "outer round, inner square" frame to connect [[LiHeEasternHan|李郃]]'s delayed gift mission, [[LiuQingQingheKing|清河王刘庆]]'s cautious self-preservation, [[ZhengZhongEasternHan|郑众]]'s trusted eunuch access, [[DengXunEasternHan|邓训]]'s posthumous frontier legitimacy, [[NieShangLateHan|聂尚]]'s failed conciliation of [[MiTangQiangLeader|弥唐]], and the [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|Northern Xiongnu]] / [[Xianbei|Xianbei]] transition after [[DouXianEasternHan|窦宪]]'s fall.

The durable synthesis is that political intelligence is not mere smoothness. Li He survives by turning delay into a defensible mission, Liu Qing preserves favor by staying cautious after trauma, Deng Xun's legitimacy outlives office, while Nie Shang's goodwill and speed collapse when they are not matched to trust, incentives, and frontier credibility.

## Key Claims

- The source's "聪明人会演戏" thesis means principled adaptability: "inner square" judgment combined with "outer round" timing and conduct.
- [[BanGuEasternHan|班固]] dies in prison after a private grievance is folded into the Dou-family cleanup, leaving [[BanZhaoLateHan|班昭]] to continue the unfinished `Han Shu`.
- [[LiHeEasternHan|Li He]] warns the [[HanZhongLateHan|Hanzhong]] administrator not to send gifts to Dou Xian, then slow-walks the mission until Dou's fall makes the administrator safe from the purge.
- Liu Qing receives post-Dou favor from Emperor He but remains cautious because his childhood displacement and his mother's death taught him how dangerous palace politics can be.
- Zheng Zhong refuses rewards after the Dou purge and gains Emperor He's trust, which the host treats as the beginning of eunuch power.
- Deng Xun's death draws mourning from Han officials, commoners, Qiang, Hu, and former Wuhuan subordinates, showing frontier legitimacy that survives the officeholder.
- Nie Shang's conciliation of Mi Tang fails when Mi Tang kills the escorting envoys and attacks Jincheng, making one-sided goodwill and credit hunger a frontier-governance risk.
- After Dou Xian's fall, Northern Xiongnu surrender arrangements unravel, Yu Chu is killed by Wang Fu and [[RenShangLateHan|Ren Shang]], and Xianbei occupy former Northern Xiongnu lands while many remnant households identify as Xianbei.

## Key Quotes

> "人生如戏全靠演技" - opening frame for political conduct under pressure.

> "内心方正，外表圆融" - the source's preferred definition of smart adaptability.

> "一厢情愿" - the host's judgment on Nie Shang's failed conciliation of Mi Tang.
