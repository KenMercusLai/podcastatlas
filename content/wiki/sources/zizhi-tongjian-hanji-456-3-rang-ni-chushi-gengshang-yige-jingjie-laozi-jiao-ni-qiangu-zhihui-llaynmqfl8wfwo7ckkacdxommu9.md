# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》456-3｜让你处世更上一个境界！老子教你千古智慧

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode retells [[Confucius|孔子]]'s second and third meetings with [[Laozi|老子]]. It first stages a disagreement over whether ritual, textual selection, and renyi reflect human nature or disturb it, then moves into Laozi's teaching on natural wuwei, observing sameness, value-equanimity, qi-gathering life and death, and the Way's presence in all things. The episode closes by comparing Confucius's opening to [[ShakyamuniBuddha|释迦牟尼佛]]'s awakening and by turning the story into a practical reminder that cultivation must return to inner practice rather than remain only heard or spoken.

## Key Claims
- Confucius approaches Laozi with texts he has revised and hopes to place in a royal collection, saying that he has used Zhou ritual and renyi as selection standards.
- Laozi challenges whether renyi is truly human nature, especially when claims of universal love and selflessness can become empty or secretly self-interested in a violent world.
- The episode contrasts Confucian moral-order language with Laozi's claim that over-marked renyi can disturb the very nature it hopes to protect.
- Laozi's alternative is [[DaoistNaturalWuwei|道家自然无为]]: people and things should follow their own natural arising, decline, work, and rest rather than be forced by artificial moral display.
- In the third meeting, Laozi teaches [[DaoistObservingSameness|观同入道]]: seeing only difference stays at the surface, while observing what things share opens the mind toward the Way.
- Confucius is shown loosening attachment to fame, ritual transmission, renyi distinction, body, honor, and profit after hearing Laozi describe life and death, right and wrong, noble and low, honor and disgrace as changing states within a larger unity.
- Laozi describes the Way as everywhere, inexhaustible, life-giving, and ordering heaven, earth, seasons, sun, moon, and beings without needing fixed possession.
- The episode's final lesson is practical cultivation: Daoist or Buddhist learning should reduce delusive distinction, rest in original nature, and become lived practice rather than verbal admiration.

## Key Quotes
> "仁义是人的本性吗" - Laozi's challenge to Confucius's moral standard.

> "观其同" - the teaching phrase for looking past difference toward shared root.

> "道即在吾身" - the episode's summary of Confucius's awakening after Laozi's guidance.
