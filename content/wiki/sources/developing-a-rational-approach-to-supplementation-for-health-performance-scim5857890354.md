# Developing a Rational Approach to Supplementation for Health & Performance

## Summary
This [[HubermanLab]] solo episode has [[AndrewHuberman]] frame supplements as biologically active, non-prescription compounds rather than harmless substitutes for food. Its decision hierarchy places sleep, light, exercise, social connection, and nutrition before supplementation; favors clear goals, single-ingredient trials, minimum effective doses, and one-variable-at-a-time testing; and keeps hormone-active products, persistent sleep problems, childhood use, medication interactions, and adverse effects inside medical boundaries. The episode extends [[TargetedSupplementNeedAssessment]], [[PerformanceSupplementHierarchy]], [[SleepSupplementBoundary]], [[AndrogenSupportSupplementBoundary]], and [[SupplementLabelAccuracy]] while treating its many named compounds and doses as source-scoped examples rather than a protocol.

## Key Claims
- [[TargetedSupplementNeedAssessment]] begins with the actual goal, sleep quality, food pattern, health context, and budget; zero supplements can be a rational result when foundations and function are already adequate.
- [[PerformanceSupplementHierarchy]] gains a general experiment-design rule: outside broad foundational formulas, prefer one ingredient at a time, start with the minimum effective dose, observe long enough to attribute benefit or harm, and combine only after individual effects are understood.
- Foundational formulas may help cover dietary gaps, especially with limited variety, but they do not replace minimally processed food; excess intake and ingredient accumulation remain relevant, particularly for fat-soluble vitamins.
- Fermented foods are presented as a food-first microbiome option, while high-dose prebiotic or probiotic capsules are described as potentially costly and capable of adverse effects; exact serving and mechanism claims remain source-scoped.
- [[SleepSupplementBoundary]] places caffeine and meal timing checks before sleep aids, discusses magnesium forms, apigenin, theanine, and myo-inositol as conditional options, and cautions against routine melatonin use and use in children.
- [[AndrogenSupportSupplementBoundary]] places calories, sleep, light, exercise, bloodwork, fertility goals, menstrual-cycle context, and medication interactions ahead of Tongkat Ali, Fadogia, shilajit, ashwagandha, L-carnitine, or maca.
- Caffeine, alpha-yohimbine, Alpha GPC, L-tyrosine, and omega-3s are discussed as distinct alertness, focus, or nutritional tools whose effects, risks, and dosing should be evaluated separately rather than inferred from a stack.
- Whole foods remain important even when powders or capsules are convenient because food supplies fiber, satiety, micronutrients, fats, and a broader nutritional matrix.
