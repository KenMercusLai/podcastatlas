# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》388-4｜揭秘古代最惨的省：它曾死了90%百姓！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] short episode uses the saying "离乱人不如太平犬" to frame war as mass civilian suffering rather than only elite regime change. Its central example is [[Sichuan|四川]], where the source links Song-Yuan war, the late-Ming and early-Qing collapse, [[ZhangXianzhong|张献忠]], other armies, famine, epidemic, and animal danger into a memory of extreme population devastation.

The episode then pivots from war mortality to institutional burden. It uses [[JingchengWangMing|敬城王]] and [[MingDynasty|明朝]] royal stipends to argue that commoners could be devastated by war while imperial kin and nobles multiplied under tax-funded support. The final comparison with medieval Europe presents [[UnifiedEmpireWarScale|大一统战争破坏范围]] and [[EmperorDreamClaimOpenness|皇帝梦与帝位开放想象]] as reasons the host thinks Chinese dynastic war could become more total than many localized European conflicts.

## Key Claims

- The episode frames "乱世" through ordinary people: war, famine, epidemic, and social collapse can make survival worse than the political narratives of victory or succession imply.
- [[SichuanWarDevastation|四川战乱人口毁伤]] is presented as the starkest case, combining Song-Yuan warfare, the late-Ming and early-Qing transition, Zhang Xianzhong, other armies, famine, disease, and tigers.
- The host cites Li Qiande's description of desolated Sichuan as evidence of regional social collapse, while leaving exact demographic measurement source-scoped.
- [[ImperialClanFiscalBurden|皇族宗藩财政负担]] is introduced through Jingcheng Wang's huge household and the claim that Ming provincial revenues could no longer cover local princely stipends.
- The episode argues that European medieval wars were also violent, but often more territorially limited because political authority was fragmented rather than organized around one all-under-heaven throne.
- The host treats "皇帝轮流坐" and Chen Sheng's "王侯将相，宁有种乎" as signs that imperial aspiration could be socially open in China, making dynastic struggle potentially broader.

## Key Quotes

> "离乱人不如太平犬" - the episode's opening proverb for wartime civilian suffering.

> "千百不存一二" - the quoted judgment on Song-Yuan Sichuan devastation.

> "皇帝轮流坐，明年到我家" - the folk formula the host uses for open-ended imperial aspiration.

> "王侯将相，宁有种乎" - Chen Sheng's line as the episode's symbol of status-crossing throne competition.
