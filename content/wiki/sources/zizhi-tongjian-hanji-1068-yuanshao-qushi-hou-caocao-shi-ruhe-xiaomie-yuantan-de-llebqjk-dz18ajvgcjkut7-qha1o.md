# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1068丨袁绍去世后，曹操是如何消灭袁谭的？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode bridges the [[DanyangCoupLateHan|丹阳郡变]] aftermath and the end of [[YuanTan|袁谭]]'s independent line. The opening shows [[SunShaoLateHan|孙韶]] gathering [[SunHeLateHan|孙河]]'s remaining troops, defending [[JingCountyLateHan|泾县]], and receiving formal command after [[SunQuan|孙权]] tests the city's readiness.

The main sequence moves to Jian'an 10 / 205 CE: [[CaoCao|曹操]] attacks Yuan Tan at [[NanpiLateHan|南皮]], hesitates under costly fighting, then accepts [[CaoChunLateHan|曹淳]]'s argument that a deep operation must win quickly or lose prestige and safety. Cao Chun pursues and kills Yuan Tan, [[LiFuLateHan|李孚]] stabilizes the newly taken city by using a surrendered trusted local voice, and Cao Cao executes [[GuoTu|郭图]] and his household as the episode turns Guo Tu into a concentrated blame figure for Yuan-camp misjudgment and factional corrosion.

## Key Claims

- After Sun He's killing, Sun Shao gathers his father's remaining troops, repairs Jing County defenses, and handles a night alarm well enough for Sun Quan to entrust him with formal command.
- Cao Cao's attack on Yuan Tan at Nanpi is difficult enough that Cao-side losses make withdrawal or slower pressure tempting.
- Cao Chun argues that because Cao Cao has gone deep into enemy territory, delay or retreat would be more dangerous than continuing the assault.
- Cao Cao personally beats the drum to renew the attack, and Cao's army takes Nanpi.
- Yuan Tan flees but is overtaken and killed by Cao Chun, ending his role as the Yuan-family claimant whom Cao Cao had first used against Yuan Shang.
- Li Fu argues that the city needs an order message delivered by someone recently surrendered but locally trusted, and his entry calms officials and commoners.
- Cao Cao executes Guo Tu and his household after the city is stabilized.
- The episode links Guo Tu's final punishment to earlier Guandu and succession failures: attack-Cao-camp advice at Wuchao, blame-shifting onto Zhang He and Gao Lan, and provocation of Yuan Tan against Yuan Shang.

## Key Quotes

> "咎由自取，自食恶果" - the episode's closing judgment on Guo Tu.

> "各安其职" - the order Li Fu carries into the newly captured city.
