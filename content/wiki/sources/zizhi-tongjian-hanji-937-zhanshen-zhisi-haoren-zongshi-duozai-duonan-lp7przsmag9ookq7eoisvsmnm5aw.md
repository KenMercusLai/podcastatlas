# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》937丨战神之死，好人总是多灾多难？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 187 CE Liangzhou collapse immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-938-lishi-de-jingxing-ruanruo-zhihui-dailai-quru-lnlnrketgfbadhfjbzdjjtrmqfsh|Hanji 938]]. It follows [[HanSui|韩遂]]'s absorption of [[BianZhangLateHan|边章]], [[BeigongBoyuLateHan|北宫伯玉]], and [[LiWenhouLateHan|李文侯]], [[GengBiLateHan|耿鄙]]'s failed expedition after trusting [[ChengQiu|程球]] and ignoring [[FuXie|傅燮]], and Fu Xie's final refusal to abandon [[HanyangCommanderyLateHan|汉阳郡]] before [[HuangYanLateHan|黄衍]]'s surrender mission and Fu Xie's death.

## Key Claims

- [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s 187 CE amnesty and [[HeMiaoLateHan|何苗]]'s appointment after suppressing Yingyang thieves occur alongside a western crisis the court cannot control.
- [[HanSui|韩遂]] consolidates rebel strength by killing or absorbing [[BianZhangLateHan|边章]], [[BeigongBoyuLateHan|北宫伯玉]], and [[LiWenhouLateHan|李文侯]], then attacks [[LongxiCommandery|陇西郡]] and draws [[LiXiangruLateHan|李相如]] into rebellion.
- The source notes a variant in which Bian Zhang dies of illness, but this episode follows the Zizhi Tongjian account that Han Sui killed him.
- [[GengBiLateHan|耿鄙]]'s expedition is structurally weak before battle because he relies on the corrupt [[ChengQiu|程球]], moves with a fresh six-commandery army, and rejects [[FuXie|傅燮]]'s advice to discipline the force, establish credibility, and wait for rebel infighting.
- The mutiny at [[DiDaoLateHan|狄道]] kills Cheng Qiu and Geng Bi, after which the rebel side besieges [[HanyangCommanderyLateHan|汉阳郡]] while Fu Xie has too few troops and no food.
- Fu Xie's earlier local reputation matters: Beidi Hu cavalry fighting with the rebels still kneel and offer to escort him home rather than see him die in the city.
- [[FuGan|傅干]] urges his father to survive a failed ruler and wait for a future wise sovereign, but Fu Xie chooses [[OfficeDutyMartyrdom|守官赴死]] as the boundary of his official responsibility.
- Fu Xie sends Fu Gan away under [[YangHuiLateHan|杨晖]]'s protection, using the Cheng Ying orphan-rescue analogy to separate his own death choice from his son's survival duty.
- [[WangGuoLateHan|王国]] sends [[HuangYanLateHan|黄衍]] to persuade Fu Xie to surrender and lead the rebels; Fu Xie condemns Huang Yan as a former Han official speaking for rebels, then fights and dies.
- The episode treats Fu Xie's death as both a personal moral completion and a sign that the Eastern Han court has effectively lost control over Liangzhou.

## Key Quotes

> "社稷之臣" - the Wang Fuzhi evaluation the host applies to [[FuXie|傅燮]].

> "程婴" - Fu Xie's analogy for entrusting [[FuGan|傅干]] to [[YangHuiLateHan|杨晖]].

> "伯夷" - Fu Xie's model for holding official duty even under a bad ruler.
