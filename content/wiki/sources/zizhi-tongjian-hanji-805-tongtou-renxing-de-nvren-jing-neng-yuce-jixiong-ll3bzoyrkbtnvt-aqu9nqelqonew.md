# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》805丨通透人性的女人，竟能预测吉凶？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate 125-126 CE settlement after [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]]'s eunuch-backed accession and before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-806-xiangyao-chengshi-zhaodui-ren-hen-guanjian-luswh-p-oyutysxue6shweqcwqxs|Hanji 806]]. It records Liu Yi / Beixiang Hou's princely burial, [[YangZhenLateHan|杨震]]'s vindication, the Yan-family purge, the debate over [[EmpressDowagerYanLateHan|阎太后]], and early personnel, frontier, and Xianbei pressures.

The second half turns to [[YuXuEasternHan|虞诩]] as Shun's cleanup instrument against Yan-side ministers and older palace eunuchs. The durable synthesis is that post-coup legitimacy depends on pairing hard liquidation of the defeated network with ritually visible treatment of the defeated dowager, while cleanup agents can quickly become targets of the bureaucracy they are used to discipline.

## Key Claims

- The opening human-nature frame is source-scoped; it mainly prepares the episode's reading of why a defeated palace actor might foresee her danger.
- In 125, Beixiang Hou Liu Yi is buried with princely rites, Liu Shou is removed, Tao Dun becomes sikong, and Yang Zhen is posthumously vindicated through appointments for his sons, reburial with Three Excellency rites, money, and sacrifice.
- Sun Cheng and the core eunuch group install Shun after the Yan-family overthrow, making accession credit and purge legitimacy part of the same opening settlement.
- Chen Chan proposes separating Yan Taihou because she has neither blood nor mother-son affection with Shun, but Zhou Ju warns through Li He that confinement followed by an unexpected death would damage Shun's ability to command the empire.
- Shun accepts the Li He / Zhou Ju path, visits Yan Taihou at the East Palace on 126-01-01, performs complete rites, and keeps the official account that she is settled before her 126-01-19 illness death.
- Ma Xian's victory over the Zhong Qiang gives the reign an early frontier success, while the Xianbei raid killing Li Chao shows that border pressure continues beside court cleanup.
- Yu Xu impeaches Feng Shi, Liu Xi, and eunuchs Cheng Huang, Chen Bing, Meng Sheng, and Li Run, turning Shun's succession cleanup against Yan-connected ministers and non-restoration eunuch rivals.
- The Three Excellencies attack Yu Xu for overreaching after amnesty, but Yu Xu defends law as social barrier and punishment as restraint, says official blame-shifting leaves people no route for complaint, and Shun does not punish him.

## Key Quotes

> "以苟容曲从为贤能" - Yu Xu's accusation that office culture rewards survivalist compliance.

> "法令者，俗之堤防" - Yu Xu's defense of law as the barrier that keeps social custom ordered.

> "势还没成" - host's closing frame for Shun's still-unfinished court power reshuffle.
