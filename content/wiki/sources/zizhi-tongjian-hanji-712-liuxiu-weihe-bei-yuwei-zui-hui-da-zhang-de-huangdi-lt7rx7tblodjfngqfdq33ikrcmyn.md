## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianwu 12 / 36 CE immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-713-liuxiu-nu-le-mingjiang-wuhan-jing-xuexi-chengdu-lv6555l99it33gw34uydsz4tkbje|Hanji 713]]. It explains [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]]'s military reputation through his remote reading of the Shu campaign: he orders [[WuHanEasternHan|吴汉]] to hold Guangdu, warns against pressing too close to [[Chengdu]], and predicts that a separated [[LiuShangEasternHan|刘尚]] camp will invite sequential attack. The second half follows Wu Han's night withdrawal and later pressure from [[GongsunShuEasternHan|公孙述]] and [[YanCenEasternHan|严岑 / Yan Cen]], while [[ZhangKanEasternHan|张堪]] stops a food-short withdrawal and urges a feigned-weakness response.

## Key Claims

- In 36 CE, Wu Han defeats Gongsun Shu's forces near Yufu, surrounds Wuyang, defeats Gongsun Shu's son-in-law Shi Xing, occupies Guangdu, and burns the Chengdu stone bridge with light cavalry.
- Liu Xiu writes to Gongsun Shu offering survival if he surrenders, but Gongsun Shu does not accept.
- Liu Xiu tells Wu Han that Chengdu still has more than 100,000 troops and that the safer strategy is to hold Guangdu, wait for the enemy to attack, and approach only after the enemy is tired.
- Wu Han disobeys the tempo order by moving more than 20,000 infantry and cavalry to a camp roughly ten li from Chengdu, while Liu Shang camps south of the river more than twenty li away.
- Liu Xiu predicts the resulting [[SeparatedCampMutualSupportRisk|separated-camp mutual support risk]]: Gongsun Shu's side can tie down Wu Han while attacking or isolating Liu Shang, leaving both Han camps unable to help each other.
- The prediction is quickly realized when Gongsun Shu sends Xie Feng and others with more than 100,000 troops against Wu Han while another force pins Liu Shang.
- Wu Han recovers by holding still for three days, using flags and fires to deceive the enemy, moving secretly at night, reuniting with Liu Shang, and then killing Xie Feng and Yuan Qi in renewed fighting.
- After returning to Guangdu, Wu Han reports his mistake; Liu Xiu replies by affirming Guangdu as the right position and continues to reason through Gongsun Shu's likely choices.
- A later Yan Cen counterattack uses a visible challenge at the Chengdu bridge and a cavalry strike behind Wu Han, defeating Wu Han's force and leaving him with only seven days of grain.
- Zhang Kan arrives, argues that retreat would be wrong, predicts Gongsun Shu's defeat, and persuades Wu Han to answer the food crisis by showing weakness and luring the enemy instead of withdrawing.
- The Li Tong side note presents an early Eastern Han founding-merit figure who seeks distance from power, returns the Da Sikong seal, keeps special-entry court status, and later sees Liu Xiu reward his son Li Xiong as Zhao-ling marquis.

## Key Quotes

> "不能掉以轻心。" - Liu Xiu's warning frame about the Chengdu force.

> "遇险时无法相互照应。" - the episode's explanation of Wu Han and Liu Shang's separated camps.

> "只剩七天粮草。" - the logistical crisis before Zhang Kan blocks withdrawal.
