# 《资治通鉴·周纪》57丨古代发毒誓还有这作用？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-56-qiaoqiao-liangqian-duo-nianqian-sha-shi-da-zhangfu-ltokbutqgsnrno-y29xmzwa3da41|Zhouji 56]] discussion of [[YangXiong|扬雄]] and [[Zigong|子贡 / 端木赐]] by narrating Zigong's rescue of [[LuState|鲁国]] through a multi-state diplomatic chain among [[QiState|齐国]], [[WuState|吴国]], [[YueState|越国]], and [[JinState|晋国]]. It then returns to [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s early reign: [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] suppresses the Shu rebellion, meets [[WeiXiangwang|魏襄王]], marries [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]] into a dangerous succession setup, creates Qin's first formal left/right chancellors, and sends [[GanMao|甘茂]] toward [[YiyangWarringStates|宜阳]]. The episode's core synthesis is that oaths can be practical political technology: Gan Mao uses [[RumorErosionOfTrust|rumor erosion of trust]] stories to make Qin Wuwang bind himself publicly through [[OathBoundCommandTrust|oath-bound command trust]] before a long campaign.

## Key Claims
- [[Confucius|孔子]] sends [[Zigong|子贡 / 端木赐]] to prevent [[QiState|齐国]] from attacking [[LuState|鲁国]], showing why [[YangXiong|扬雄]] can treat Zigong's diplomacy as public rescue rather than private wealth-seeking.
- Zigong persuades [[TianChang|田常]] not to attack weak Lu and instead redirect pressure toward [[WuState|吴国]], then persuades [[Fuchai|吴王夫差]] and [[Goujian|越王勾践]] in ways that draw Wu, Qi, Yue, and Jin into a larger chain.
- The episode says Wu defeats Qi, then loses to Jin; Yue exploits Wu's exhaustion, attacks Wu near its capital, and eventually drives Fuchai to suicide.
- The [[Goujian|越王勾践]] sequel turns victory into a ministerial danger case: [[FanLi|范蠡]] leaves after Wu's destruction, warns [[WenZhongYue|文种]], and Wen Zhong later dies after Goujian gives him a sword.
- In [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s fifth year, [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] sends forces to kill the rebel [[ChenZhuangWarringStates|陈庄]] in Shu, continuing the [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-52-chenzhuang-panqin-duanqi-wan-chifan-fangxia-wan-ma-niang-ls13ixmbhflepjgpelxgu6idjqa2|Zhouji 52]] Ba-Shu branch.
- Qin Wuwang and [[WeiXiangwang|魏襄王]] meet at Linjin, while [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]] marries [[WuWa|吴娃]], later mother of [[ZhaoHe|赵何]], creating a future conflict with the earlier legitimate son [[ZhaoZhang|赵章]].
- In 309 BCE, Qin first formally sets left and right chancellors: [[Chulizi|樗里疾 / 樗里子]] becomes right chancellor and [[GanMao|甘茂]] becomes left chancellor.
- In 308 BCE, Qin and Wei meet at Ying, and Qin Wuwang sends Gan Mao to persuade Wei to join an attack on [[HanState|韩国]].
- Gan Mao returns with Wei's agreement but tells Qin Wuwang that he personally does not advise attacking Han, because [[YiyangWarringStates|宜阳]] is a large, hard target reached only after a long and difficult march.
- Gan Mao uses [[ZengShen|曾参]]'s "Zeng Shen killed someone" story to argue that repeated accusation can shake even intimate trust.
- Gan Mao uses [[LeYang|乐羊]]'s Zhongshan campaign to argue that a general's success can depend less on ability than on whether the ruler can withstand slander during a long campaign.
- Gan Mao predicts that [[Chulizi|樗里疾 / 樗里子]] and the Gongsun faction will accuse him of prolonging the war or colluding with Han if Yiyang does not fall quickly.
- Qin Wuwang and Gan Mao swear before Heaven at Xirang; the source treats the oath as a public commitment device that makes later retreat politically harder.
- The next year, after Gan Mao has besieged Yiyang for five months without taking it, Chulizi and the Gongsun faction indeed begin attacking the campaign, confirming Gan Mao's earlier risk model.

## Key Quotes
> "君子强学而力行" - opening moral frame carried over from the Yang Xiong discussion.

> "飞鸟尽，良弓藏；狡兔死，走狗烹" - Fan Li's warning to Wen Zhong after Yue's victory.

> "曾参杀人" - Gan Mao's parable for repeated rumor overcoming trust.
