# Episode 228: 所以，奥德赛

## Summary
This [[ChizaoGengxin|迟早更新]] episode has [[RenNing]] connect [[TheBeatles|The Beatles]]' [[BecauseSong|"Because"]], [[LuoDayou|罗大佑]]'s [[SuoyiSong|《所以》]], [[Homer]]'s [[TheOdyssey]], and [[ChristopherNolan]]'s film adaptation as four texts about cause, consequence, return, technology, and responsibility. Its strongest contribution is to turn the wiki's Odyssey branch from adaptation reception into a wider cultural-ethical synthesis: classics do not change, but later readers and creators stand after new wars, space images, AI, data systems, and expanded human power.

The episode also adds a space-media thread. [[StewartBrand]], [[EarthrisePhoto|Earthrise]], [[BlueMarblePhoto|the 1967 whole-Earth color image]], and [[WholeEarthCatalog|Whole Earth Catalog]] become evidence for a paradox: advanced technology can take people far enough from home to see home newly. That distance then feeds [[TechnologicalResponsibilityRadius]]: once human action can reshape the world through war, capital, satellites, GPT, AI, data, and chips, the relevant question is not only whether something can be done, but what follows after it has been done.

## Key Claims
- [[PoeticCausality]] names the episode's reading of "because" and "所以": both songs use logical connectives to join images, moods, and historical pressure that do not work as strict proof.
- [[SuoyiSong|《所以》]] differs from [[BecauseSong|"Because"]] because its world is densely mediated by satellites, GPT, AI, data, chips, capital, climate, and systems; human beings are no longer only acted on by the world, but act on it.
- The episode reads [[TheOdyssey]] as asking what happens after victory: after the war, the city is destroyed, the [[TrojanHorse]] succeeds, and [[Odysseus]] tries to go home.
- [[MetisHeroicCunning]] is valuable but morally shadowed: the wooden raft and the Trojan Horse both show "having a way," but the horse is followed by slaughter and consequences that cannot simply be left behind.
- [[ChristopherNolan]]'s version is read as moving gods, fate, and order inward, making the hero's burden less external adventure than memory, guilt, and responsibility after violence.
- [[TechnologicalResponsibilityRadius]] captures the episode's ethical synthesis: as human capacity expands, responsibility expands too, but often only becomes visible after distance and delay.
- [[ClassicRevoicingFromNewPosition]] captures the closing claim that later creators do not merely correct earlier ones; they repeat old words from a historical, technological, and ethical position the earlier creators could not occupy.

## Key Quotes
> "行凶后别逃离现场" - lyric line the episode turns into an ethical command without a settled subject.

> "未来的过往" - phrase the host uses to imagine today being judged from a future vantage point.

> "We are as gods and might as well get good at it" - Stewart Brand line used as the episode's closing frame.
