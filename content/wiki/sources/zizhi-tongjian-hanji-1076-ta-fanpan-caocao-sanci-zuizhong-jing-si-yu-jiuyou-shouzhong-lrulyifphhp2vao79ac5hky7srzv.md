# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1076丨他反叛曹操三次，最终竟死于旧友手中

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode links three Jian'an 11 / 206 CE problems: personal loyalty after [[ZhangMengLateHan|张猛]] kills [[HandanShangLateHan|邯郸商]], the final revolt of [[ChangXiLateHan|昌豨]], and the consolidation and frontier preparations preceding [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1077-caocao-du-shang-quanbu-shenjia-jiusi-yisheng-tu-ge-sha-lp7dgomh7hilbtcw0rmwerjgqgsk|Hanji 1077]]. [[PangYuLateHan|庞淯]] publicly mourns Handan Shang and prepares revenge, but Zhang Meng spares him because punishing visible loyalty would further destroy trust.

The central case is Chang Xi's attempt to treat surrender as another negotiated reset. After [[YuJin|于禁]] and [[XiahouYuan|夏侯渊]] force his position to collapse, Chang Xi surrenders to Yu Jin through their old acquaintance; Yu Jin nevertheless executes him under the stated rule that surrender after encirclement is not pardoned. The closing branch says eight princely states are abolished while [[TadunLateHan|蹋顿]] consolidates Wuhuan and Yuan-aligned manpower, leading [[CaoCao|曹操]] to dig transport canals before the northern expedition.

## Key Claims

- Zhang Meng kills inspector Handan Shang and forbids mourning; Pang Yu resigns his county post, travels to Wuwei, mourns publicly, and carries a dagger intending revenge.
- Zhang Meng spares Pang Yu and permits him to conduct the funeral, reasoning that killing a visibly loyal man after killing his superior would make claims of honorable conduct even less credible.
- Chang Xi is presented as a Taishan local armed leader who repeatedly rebels and surrenders, using submission to preserve his force and bargaining position rather than treating it as durable allegiance.
- Cao Cao sends Yu Jin against Chang Xi and later reinforces him with Xiahou Yuan; the episode says pressure induces multiple camps to submit before Chang Xi himself seeks terms from Yu Jin.
- Yu Jin refuses other officers' proposal to send Chang Xi to Cao Cao, invokes the order that a defender who surrenders only after encirclement is not pardoned, bids farewell to his old acquaintance, and executes him.
- The episode treats the execution as a conflict between private relationship and impersonal command discipline: Chang Xi relies on friendship as bargaining capital, while Yu Jin treats compliance with the stated order as the stronger obligation.
- Emperor Xian's court abolishes eight princely states and retains a new Langya king; the host interprets the change as removal of wealthy, militarized, and potentially Yuan-connected local power, but the exact institutional details require independent textual checking.
- Tadun is said to unite Wuhuan and Yuan-aligned refugees into a major northern threat; Cao Cao responds by digging the Pinglu and Quanzhou canals to improve supply transport before the expedition continued in Hanji 1077.

## Key Quotes

> "围而后降者不赦。" - the command rule Yu Jin invokes against pardoning Chang Xi after encirclement.

> "是吾命也。" - the episode's rendering of Cao Cao's response to Chang Xi choosing surrender to Yu Jin rather than appearing before Cao Cao.
