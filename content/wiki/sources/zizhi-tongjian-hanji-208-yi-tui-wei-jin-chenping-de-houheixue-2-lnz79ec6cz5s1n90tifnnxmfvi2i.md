# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》208｜以退为进，陈平的厚黑学（2）

## Summary

Despite its [[ChenPing|陈平]] title, this [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode is about [[LiuHeng|汉文帝刘恒]]'s early reign, [[EmpressDowagerDouWesternHan|窦皇后 / later 窦太后]]'s rise, and the political management of her newly elevated family. It recounts Dou's unintended transfer to Dai, the birth of [[LiuPiaoWesternHan|刘嫖]] and [[HanJingdi|刘启]], and her reunion with her long-lost younger brother [[DouGuangguoWesternHan|窦广国]]. [[ZhouBo|周勃]] and [[GuanYing|灌婴]] then respond to the recent Lü-family crisis by arranging upright teachers and companions for the Dou siblings, making education and social environment an early form of [[OuterRelativeTutorshipRestraint|外戚师友式预防约束]].

The second half combines welfare and legitimacy. Liu Heng reportedly grants tax relief to vulnerable people, assigns monthly grain, wine, meat, and cloth support to older subjects, refuses a thousand-li horse as useless to ordinary imperial travel, discourages rare-goods tribute, and rewards officials who followed him from Dai. These acts support an early burden-limiting and confidence-building profile, while the episode's claims that the former Dai queen belonged to the Lü clan, that her four sons may have been politically eliminated, and that [[SimaQian|司马迁]] deliberately concealed the affair remain unverified inferences.

## Key Claims

- Dou reportedly enters the palace from poverty, is mistakenly assigned to Dai rather than Zhao, becomes Liu Heng's favored consort, and bears Liu Piao and Liu Qi before becoming empress after Liu Qi's designation as crown prince.
- Dou Guangguo, sold away as a young child and repeatedly transferred, survives a deadly mountain collapse, reaches Chang'an, and is recognized through detailed shared childhood memories.
- Zhou Bo and Guan Ying fear that sudden wealth and rank could turn the Dou siblings into another dangerous outer-relative household, so they arrange morally respected teachers and companions to shape conduct before power hardens.
- The episode reports tax relief for widowers, widows, orphans, and childless elderly people, along with age-tiered monthly food, drink, meat, cloth, and official-delivery duties for older subjects.
- Liu Heng returns a thousand-li horse because imperial travel is constrained by the larger procession, pays the presenter's return expenses, and asks the realm not to seek rare goods for tribute.
- Liu Heng rewards followers from Dai, including 宋昌, and the host interprets the recognition as a prelude to wider court reorganization.
- The former Dai queen's identity, the deaths of her four sons, Liu Heng's responsibility, and Sima Qian's alleged concealment are presented as suspicions rather than established facts.
