# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》410-2｜日利八千万！古代商人赚钱有多敢想？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode uses the [[RiliBaqianwanBeltHook|“日利八千万”鲤鱼形铜带钩]] from a Guizhou tomb as an entry point into [[WesternHanDynasty|西汉]] wealth desire, display objects, and merchant culture. It reads the phrase through [[WuzhuPurchasingPowerFrame|五铢钱购买力框架]], then turns to [[SimaQian|司马迁]] and [[Shiji|《史记》]]'s Huozhi Liezhuan to argue that legitimate profit-seeking, labor, and wealth creation can have social value when bounded by non-disruption.

## Key Claims

- The “日利八千万” inscription is treated as a direct and public wish for extraordinary daily profit rather than only a vague blessing.
- The episode uses five-zhu coins and grain purchasing power to make the number legible, while keeping the conversion closer to interpretive comparison than strict economic measurement.
- The host frames the昭宣-era social setting as giving wealth-seeking people more room to act than a simple anti-merchant stereotype would suggest.
- [[SimaQian|司马迁]]'s Huozhi Liezhuan is read as unusually affirming of human desire for wealth, work, business skill, and merchant social contribution.
- The episode's normative boundary is [[LegitimateProfitBoundary|正当逐利边界]]: earn through labor and intelligence, create material wealth, and avoid harming government order or disturbing ordinary people.

## Key Quotes

> "日利八千万" — inscription on the belt hook as presented by the episode.

> "天下熙熙皆为利来，天下壤壤皆为利往" — line used to ground the universality of profit-seeking.
