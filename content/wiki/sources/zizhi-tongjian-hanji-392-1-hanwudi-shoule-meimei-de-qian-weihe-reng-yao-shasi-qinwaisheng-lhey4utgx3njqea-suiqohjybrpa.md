# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》392-1｜汉武帝收了妹妹的钱，为何仍要杀死亲外甥？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the late [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] court before the Han Zhao transition by focusing on [[ZhaoPingjunWesternHan|赵平君]]: Wudi's nephew, son-in-law, and the only son of [[LongluePrincessWesternHan|隆虑公主]]. The story turns on an apparent contradiction: Longlü Princess had paid a large sum to secure future pardon for her son, yet Wudi still approves Zhao Pingjun's execution after he drunkenly kills a princess attendant.

The episode reads the case through three linked lenses. Politically, Wudi chooses [[RuleOverKinshipPower|法度压过亲缘特权]]: private affection, a deathbed request, and prior payment cannot override ancestral law without damaging legitimacy before the ancestral temple and the people. Familially, the story becomes [[ParentalIndulgenceBlindness|溺爱不明]] because protecting a child without discipline can teach exemption rather than capacity. Rhetorically, [[DongfangShuo|东方朔]]'s congratulatory speech turns the painful execution into a ruler-praising maxim: a clear ruler rewards without avoiding enemies and punishes without separating bone-and-flesh kin.

## Key Claims

- The episode uses "溺爱不明" from the Great Learning tradition to frame Longlü Princess's care as protective but politically and educationally dangerous.
- Zhao Pingjun's identity makes the case unusually high-stakes: he is both Han Wudi's nephew and son-in-law, and his mother had already tried to purchase future pardon.
- Han Wudi's final reasoning is legitimacy-based: breaking law for his sister would shame him before Gaozu's ancestral temple and fail the people.
- The case adds a Western Han branch to [[RuleOverKinshipPower|法度压过亲缘特权]] because the punished offender sits inside the emperor's own sibling and marital network.
- The source treats Zhao Pingjun's crime as serious, but also connects his collapse to a childhood without clear discipline or non-negotiable limits.
- Dongfang Shuo's congratulation reframes punishment of kin as ancient kingly impartiality, and Wudi rewards him with the zhonglang title.

## Key Quotes

> "溺爱者不明" - Zhu Xi's gloss, used by the episode to introduce the family-education problem.

> "法令是先帝创立的" - Han Wudi's reported reason for refusing to break law because of his sister's request.

> "不偏不党，王道荡荡" - Dongfang Shuo's praise frame for impartial reward and punishment.
