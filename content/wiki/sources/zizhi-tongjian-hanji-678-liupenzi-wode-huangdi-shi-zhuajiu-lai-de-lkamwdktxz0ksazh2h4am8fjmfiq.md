# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》678丨刘盆子：我的皇帝是抓阄来的！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianwu 1 / 25 CE immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-679-zhuwei-shahai-liuxiu-gege-weihe-reng-neng-shanzhong-lqns6ktym8rgv3y5tlczcyfyzxdb|Hanji 679]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-680-ming-sang-chimei-zhe-wei-qiyi-huangdi-maigu-hechu-lurtvfqerb0vf3a33msmd-txr9pv|Hanji 680]]. It centers on [[ChimeiRebellion|赤眉]] trying to turn military momentum into dynastic legitimacy as it approaches [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]]: a Qi shaman invokes Chengyang Jing King Liu Zhang, Fang Yang advises enthroning a Liu descendant, and the commanders use a lottery ritual to make [[LiuPenziEasternHan|刘盆子]] emperor.

The durable synthesis is that Chimei's new imperial title is manufactured before it becomes institutional authority. Liu Penzi's selection through bloodline and lots gives the army a political banner, but the terrified fifteen-year-old herdboy remains without command power, while [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] is shown building authority through appointments, rear-area stability, and the coming [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] campaign.

## Key Claims

- At Huayin, a Qi-region shaman claims Chengyang Jing King Liu Zhang is angry that Chimei remains thieves rather than claiming a heavenly role.
- Fang Yang advises Fan Chong and the other commanders to enthrone a Liu-family descendant so the army can command the realm through a Han royal name.
- At Zheng County, Chimei leaders search among seized Liu descendants; Liu Gong has gone to [[LiuXuanGengshiEmperor|更始帝刘玄]], while Liu Mao and Liu Penzi remain in the army as herdboys.
- The commanders make three wooden lots, mark one as "Shangjiangjun," build an altar north of Zheng, and have three candidates draw in age order.
- Liu Penzi, the youngest candidate, draws the marked lot and is worshiped as emperor, even though the source emphasizes his fear, crying, sweat, bare feet, torn clothing, and lack of consent.
- Liu Penzi names Xu Xuan, Fan Chong, Feng An, and others to offices, but continues reporting to Liu Xiaqing and receives little attention from the commanders, making the title mostly nominal.
- The episode contrasts Chimei's improvised legitimacy with Liu Xiu's appointments of [[DengYuEasternHan|邓禹]], [[WangLiangEasternHan|王梁]], [[WuHanEasternHan|吴汉]], and [[FuZhanEasternHan|伏湛]], Liu Mao's submission settlement, and the coordinated pressure on Luoyang.
- In Chang'an, Gengshi infighting culminates in Li Song's capture, Li Kuang opening the gate, and Chimei entering the city, setting up the Liu Xuan crisis developed in Hanji 679-680.

## Key Quotes

> "为何只做盗贼、不做天子" - the shamanic accusation that pushes Chimei toward a ruler title.

> "上将军" - the marked wooden lot used to select Liu Penzi.

> "没有真正权力" - the episode's summary of Liu Penzi's imperial position.
