# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》861丨什么样的人堪称老师楷模？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 164 CE [[GuoTaiLateHan|郭泰]] mentorship and talent-recognition layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-862-ta-yin-yiju-hua-xiuqi-zuizhong-shenbai-minglie-lrvklai847fvlp1l28coofzgbsyq|Hanji 862]]. It presents Guo Tai as a "人师" rather than only a learned instructor: he sees promise in [[MengMinLateHan|孟敏]], [[ShentuPanLateHan|申屠蟠]], and [[YuChengLateHan|庾乘]], tests [[WeiZhaoLateHan|魏昭]], redirects [[ZuoYuanLateHan|左原]], refuses office, and continues teaching despite reading the dynasty as difficult to save.

The source's durable contribution is [[MoralMentorshipTalentCultivation|人师型人才成全]]: a teacher-model works by discerning latent capacity, forming conduct, offering corrective trust after failure, and accepting counsel from others. The ending ties this model back to [[HuangYunLateHan|黄允]], whose talent forecast requires self-correction and is later qualified by Hanji 862's reputation-collapse case.

## Key Claims

- In 164 CE, Guo Tai is framed as a late-Han "Bole" whose defining skill is recognizing and forming people.
- [[MengMinLateHan|孟敏]] does not look back after a broken pottery vessel because regret cannot repair the loss; Guo Tai reads this as unusual decisiveness and encourages him to study.
- Guo Tai does not evaluate only by status: the source names [[ShentuPanLateHan|申屠蟠]] as a poor lacquer worker and [[YuChengLateHan|庾乘]] as a county gatekeeper whom Guo Tai still recognizes.
- [[WeiZhaoLateHan|魏昭]] asks for a teacher of conduct rather than only a teacher of texts; Guo Tai's repeated porridge rebuke tests whether Wei Zhao's respect survives humiliation.
- [[ZuoYuanLateHan|左原]] is treated as corrigible after an offense; Guo Tai uses hospitality, historical examples, and self-reflection to redirect him away from resentment.
- The source presents Guo Tai's association with a past offender as preventive rather than indulgent: excessive rejection can intensify retaliation, while trusted correction can activate shame before violence.
- [[FanPangLateHan|范滂]] describes Guo Tai as filial without being socially withdrawn and principled without being cut off from the world.
- [[SongChongLateHan|宋冲]] urges Guo Tai to enter office, but Guo Tai refuses because he sees the dynastic situation as beyond repair.
- [[XuZhiLateHan|徐稚]] cautions Guo Tai that one rope cannot hold a falling great tree; Guo Tai receives the admonition as a teacherly correction.
- The final [[HuangYunLateHan|黄允]] notice frames talent recognition as fallible and conditional: great promise still needs later self-correction.

## Key Quotes

> "经师易遇，人师难遭" - Wei Zhao's distinction between text instruction and moral instruction.

> "甑已破矣，视之何益" - Meng Min's broken-vessel answer, preserved as the decisiveness signal.

> "天之所废，不可支也" - Guo Tai's reason for refusing office despite continuing to teach.
