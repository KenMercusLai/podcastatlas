# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》896丨假如乱世降临，你该如何自保？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 169 CE party-prohibition layer before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-897-zhichang-bidu-shenme-cai-jiao-lingdaoli-lvrrmksykazlsb7w8abbdmnqfzqq|Hanji 897]]. It opens with a source-scoped borrowing-money boundary lesson, then turns to [[ChenShiLateHan|陈寔]], [[HeYongLateHan|何颙]], [[YuanShao|袁绍]], [[YuanHongLateHan|袁闳]], and [[ShentuPanLateHan|申屠蟠]] as different responses to persecution risk under [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]].

The episode's core synthesis is that disorder-era survival is not only moral courage. Chen Shi's earlier courtesy to [[ZhangRangLateHan|张让]] becomes later protection, He Yong and Yuan Shao quietly rescue party figures, while Yuan Hong and Shentu Pan lower exposure or withdraw before public righteousness turns into collective danger.

## Key Claims

- The opening personal-finance lesson says refusal is cleaner than evasive explanations when acquaintances ask for loans; if one chooses to help, the money should be treated as possibly unrecoverable.
- In 169 CE, Zhang Rang's father's funeral exposes the social isolation around eunuch power: many respected locals avoid the funeral, while Chen Shi attends and leaves Zhang Rang with a remembered obligation.
- When party figures are later pursued, Zhang Rang's gratitude protects Chen Shi and many people connected to him, turning small social courtesy into a survival buffer.
- He Yong, because of his ties to [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] and Li Ying, enters the danger field, changes name, hides between Nanyang and Runan, and cooperates secretly with young Yuan Shao in Luoyang rescue work.
- Yuan Shao's early political capital is presented as partly earned through crisis rescue and talent friendship, not only inherited from the Yuan family.
- Yuan Hong sees that the Yuan family's wealth and prestige make it a target in a corrupt age, rejects gifts from Yuan Feng and Yuan Wei, and eventually isolates in a doorless earthen hut for eighteen years.
- Shentu Pan reads the praise around figures such as Fan Pang as danger rather than protection, leaves the capital, lives by rough labor, and survives when repression soon falls.
- Sima Guang's comment distinguishes upright action under ordered conditions from public-opinion confrontation under captured power: unsupported moral denunciation can endanger self, kin, friends, disciples, and guests.
- Later annalistic notices add eclipse, Xianbei pressure in Bingzhou, Cao Jie's restored honors, Goguryeo's Liaodong incursion, and Geng Lin's response as background rather than the episode's main interpretive branch.

## Key Quotes

> "能不借就不借。" - opening loan-boundary advice.

> "救了陈寔，同时也救了很多和他相识的无辜之人。" - the host's summary of Zhang Rang's later protection.

> "审时度势，趋吉避凶。" - Sima Guang's frame for Guo Tai and Shentu Pan's crisis judgment.
