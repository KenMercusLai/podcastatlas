# 《资治通鉴·周纪》39丨历史上张仪是怎样怒怼强敌的

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]'s ambiguous posting in [[WeiState|魏国]] after [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-38-gao-jianzhi-de-zhangyi-bu-yiban-lobhcpz679psy54k0iduccqkfcrn|Zhouji 38]]. It explains why [[YingSi|秦惠文王]]'s continued favor pulls Zhang Yi back toward [[QinState|秦国]], then follows Zhang Yi's court struggle against [[HuiShi|惠施 / 惠师]] and his preemptive attack on [[ChenZhenWarringStates|陈枕]]. The ending uses [[ZuoShuang|左爽]]'s advice to Chen Zhen to frame Warring States survival as political performance, situational pivoting, and adaptation under hostile court conditions.

## Key Claims
- The episode continues the Zhou Xian Wang forty-seventh-year branch and says that year ends after this material.
- [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] reacts to Zhang Yi's failure in Wei not by punishing him, but by secretly increasing favor and reward.
- The source reads Qin Huiwen Wang's reward-without-achievement pattern as [[GraceDebtLoyaltyPressure|恩赏负债式忠诚压力]]: Zhang Yi feels ashamed and obliged rather than relaxed.
- Zhang Yi remains formally in [[WeiState|魏国]] but emotionally and strategically turns toward [[QinState|秦国]], even secretly linking Qin action against Wei.
- Zhang Yi's Wei policy is a [[LianhengStrategy|连横]] line: he wants Wei, Qin, and Han to attack [[QiState|齐国]] and [[ChuState|楚国]] together.
- [[HuiShi|惠施 / 惠师]] opposes that line, arguing that Wei should instead align with Qi and Chu and avoid new provocations.
- Most Wei ministers side with Zhang Yi, leaving Hui Shi suspicious that Zhang Yi has captured the court or been backed by Qin; Hui Shi eventually flees to Chu.
- [[FengHaoWarringStates|冯浩]] advises [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] to send Hui Shi onward to [[SongState|宋国]], because protecting Hui Shi is not worth provoking Zhang Yi.
- After Hui Shi is forced out, [[ChenZhenWarringStates|陈枕]] comes to Wei seeking office; Zhang Yi preemptively accuses him of serving Chu interests.
- [[ZuoShuang|左爽]] tells Chen Zhen to accept the situation and use Zhang Yi's own words to rebuild his standing in [[ChuState|楚国]], turning a blocked Wei career into a [[SituationalPoliticalPivot|因事立道式政治转身]].
- The episode reads these stories through "office politics" language: skill, narrative control, factional timing, and performative adaptation can matter more than straightforward effort in a chaotic political market.

## Key Quotes
> "无功失禄" - the episode's phrase for the anxiety created by reward without achievement.

> "因事立道" - the principle behind Zuo Shuang's advice to Chen Zhen.

> "人生如戏，全凭演技" - the closing survival formula drawn from the episode.

## Contradictions
- This episode names [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] as the active Qin patron behind Zhang Yi's continued pull toward Qin in the same late Zhou Xian Wang forty-seventh-year branch. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-38-gao-jianzhi-de-zhangyi-bu-yiban-lobhcpz679psy54k0iduccqkfcrn|Zhouji 38]] imported a [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] supplement involving [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] after Ying Si's death. The new source therefore reinforces the existing [[ChronicleChronologyDrift|编年错位]] warning: the Zhang Yi strategy story is useful, but ruler-name layers should remain source-scoped.
- The source transcript uses "惠师" while the existing wiki page uses [[HuiShi|惠施]]. This is treated as a source spelling/rendering variant, not a substantive contradiction.
