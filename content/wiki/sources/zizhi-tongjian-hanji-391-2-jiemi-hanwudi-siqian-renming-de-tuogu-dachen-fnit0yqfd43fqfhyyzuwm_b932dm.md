# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》391-2｜揭秘汉武帝死前任命的“托孤大臣”

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode records [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s final succession arrangement in 87 BCE: he names his youngest son [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|刘弗陵]] crown prince and entrusts the young ruler to [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]], [[JinMidiWesternHan|金日磾]], [[ShangguanJieWesternHan|上官桀]], and [[SangHongyangWesternHan|桑弘羊]]. The episode's durable synthesis is that Huo Guang's later regency authority begins as a deliberately chosen testamentary trust, grounded in long, error-free palace service and the "周公" analogy.

The note also frames the appointment as a dangerous court-power origin story. Huo and Jin both refuse or deflect the role at first, the chosen ministers receive office at the emperor's sickbed, and the closing segment turns the succession arrangement toward later palace struggle and the vulnerability of women drawn into court power.

## Key Claims

- Han Wudi's last succession decision is explicit: Liu Fuling is made crown prince, and Huo Guang is assigned a Zhou Gong-like assisting role.
- Huo Guang's fitness is tied to more than office rank; the episode stresses twenty-plus years of careful palace attendance and almost ritual precision in movement and positioning.
- Jin Midi's refusal language places regency legitimacy partly in foreign-policy prestige: as an outside-origin minister, he says Xiongnu would look down on Han if he led the regency.
- The testamentary field includes four core ministers: Huo Guang as Da Sima Da Jiangjun, Jin Midi as Chariots-and-Cavalry General, Shangguan Jie as Left General, and Sang Hongyang as Yushi Dafu.
- The episode uses the sickbed appointment to foreshadow later court conflict, especially palace struggle around power, women, and death.

## Key Quotes

> "立最小的儿子刘弗陵" - the episode's compact statement of the succession decision.

> "做周公" - the analogy used for Huo Guang's assisting role.
