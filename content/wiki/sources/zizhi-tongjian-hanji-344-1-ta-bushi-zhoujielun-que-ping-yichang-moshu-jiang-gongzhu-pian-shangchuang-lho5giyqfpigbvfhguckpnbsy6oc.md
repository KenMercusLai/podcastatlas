# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》344-1｜他不是周杰伦，却凭一场魔术将公主骗上床

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the entry stage of [[LuanDaWesternHan|栾大]]'s court career. Introduced to [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] through [[DingYiWesternHan|丁义]], Luan Da claims access to immortals, frames his own low status as the obstacle to obtaining their teachings, and uses the earlier death of 少翁 to negotiate patience and privileged treatment before any result is delivered.

The episode says Wudi still demands a visible demonstration, so Luan Da performs “斗棋,” making two pieces appear to collide without being touched. The host suggests a hidden magnetic mechanism but provides no direct evidence for it; the durable point is that a staged, observable effect turns an unverifiable promise into apparent credibility. The supplied material ends with Luan Da receiving several general titles and does not narrate the princess marriage promised by the title or his later fall, which are covered in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-344-2-hanwudi-dangnian-weihe-yaozhan-qinnvxu-llmavvgeaaxwcsz_pxirfulbfpaa|Hanji 344-2]].

## Key Claims

- Ding Yi, identified as 乐成侯, acts as the intermediary through whom Luan Da reaches Han Wudi; the episode places this introduction within a chain beginning with Ding Yi's sister, the widow of 胶东康王刘寄.
- Luan Da says he has met immortals such as 安期生 and 羡门 but that his low status and the limited rank of his former patron prevent those beings from entrusting him with immortality teachings.
- By invoking Wudi's regret over killing 少翁, Luan Da reframes delay as a necessary condition of success and warns that punishing failed fangshi too quickly will silence anyone who might reveal the desired method.
- Luan Da requests not merely resources but a status transformation: an immortal's messenger must be treated as an honored intimate before the teacher can be approached.
- Wudi still asks for proof, and Luan Da's “斗棋” performance supplies an immediate visible effect that the episode says wins the emperor's confidence.
- Wudi then grants Luan Da the titles 武利将军, 天士将军, 地士将军, and 大通将军 in the episode's account.
- The host interprets the exchange through desire and overreach: Luan Da reads Wudi's wish for extended life, while the emperor's promise to give him anything also implies severe consequences if he cannot deliver.

## Key Quotes

> “要什么给什么” - the episode's rendering of Wudi's promise once Luan Da presents himself as a route to immortals.

> “德不配位，必有灾祸” - the host's warning after Luan Da receives rapid, extraordinary promotion.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. This episode supplies the court-entry and confidence-building stage that Hanji 344-2 assumes before narrating Luan Da's promotion and fall.
- Name normalization: the supplied file writes “鸾大.” The wiki retains the established historical form [[LuanDaWesternHan|栾大]] and treats “鸾大” as a transcript or editorial variant, not a separate person.
- The title promises that Luan Da deceives a princess into sex, but the supplied document ends before any princess appears. That wording is not promoted into the summary or canonical profiles; the following episode presents an arranged imperial marriage instead.
- The hidden-magnet explanation for “斗棋,” the identification of Luan Da and 少翁 as literal fellow disciples, the exact wording and sequence of the audience, the listed general titles, Wudi's inner reactions, and the host's moral judgments remain source-attributed rather than independently established.
