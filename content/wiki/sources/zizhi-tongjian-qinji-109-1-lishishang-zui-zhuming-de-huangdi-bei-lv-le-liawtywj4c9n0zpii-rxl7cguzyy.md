# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》109-1｜历史上最著名的皇帝被“绿”了

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode turns from Qin's [[LaoAi|嫪毐]] crisis to a parallel [[ChuState|楚国]] succession scandal around [[MiWan|楚考烈王]], [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]], [[LiYuanWarringStates|李源]], and [[LiYuanSister|李源妹妹]]. It follows Li Yuan's staged access to Chunshenjun, his sister's pregnancy, the plan to send her to the childless king, her son's elevation as crown prince, and Li Yuan's later plot to eliminate Chunshenjun.

The episode's larger point is that an unresolved royal succession can turn private desire, fear, and household access into state-level fraud. It also uses the Chu story to support the wiki's cautious treatment of the [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]] paternity rumor: the host explicitly says she believes Ying Zheng was [[QinZhuangxiangwang|异人 / 子楚]]'s son and treats the [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]] paternity claim as likely enemy slander rather than settled fact.

## Key Claims
- [[MiWan|楚考烈王]] has no son late in his reign, leaving [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]] anxious because an unplanned succession could remove the ruler who has long trusted him.
- The host explains "不孝有三，无后为大" as a problem of having no successor, not simply no biological son, because adoption or succession from a close royal branch could still solve dynastic continuity.
- [[LiYuanWarringStates|李源]], a Zhao-origin retainer, wants his sister to enter the Chu king's palace but worries that failure to bear a son would leave her exposed and politically useless.
- Li Yuan joins Chunshenjun's household, overstays leave, then claims that the Qi king has sent envoys to seek his sister in marriage, making Chunshenjun curious about her beauty and availability.
- Chunshenjun takes [[LiYuanSister|李源妹妹]] into his own household, after which she soon becomes pregnant.
- Li Yuan's sister persuades Chunshenjun that if Mi Wan dies and a royal brother succeeds, Chunshenjun may lose status or even face disaster; if she enters the king's palace already pregnant and bears a boy, Chunshenjun's own son may become Chu's heir.
- Chunshenjun initially hesitates because Mi Wan has treated him like a brother, but the host says greed and fear overcome his moral restraint.
- Chunshenjun secretly relocates Li Yuan's sister, presents her to Mi Wan, and the child she bears is made crown prince while she becomes queen and Li Yuan rises as royal in-law.
- The host compares this plot with the story that Lu Buwei gave [[ZhaoJi|赵姬]] to Yiren while she was already pregnant, but argues that the Ying Zheng paternity rumor is weak and likely politically motivated.
- Chunshenjun's late-life fall is contrasted with his earlier heroism: he once saved Mi Wan's return from Qin and rose through merit, but in old age he becomes fearful, pleasure-seeking, and willing to betray his ruler.
- Li Yuan does not stop after becoming powerful; he secretly keeps assassins and prepares to kill Chunshenjun, either to hide the old pregnancy plot or to remove the person who can constrain his future court power.
- [[ZhuYingWarringStates|朱英]] warns Chunshenjun that "无妄之祸" can arrive unexpectedly and that he needs an "无妄之人," but this episode stops before the full assassination outcome.

## Key Quotes
> "不孝有三，无后为大" - the succession phrase the host glosses as an heir problem.

> "无妄之祸" - Zhu Ying's warning phrase for an unexpected disaster.

> "人心莫测" - the host's closing judgment on the conspiracy field.
