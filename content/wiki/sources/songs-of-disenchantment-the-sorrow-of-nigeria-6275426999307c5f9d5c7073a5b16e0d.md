# Songs of disenchantment: the sorrow of Nigeria

## Summary
This [[EconomistPodcasts]] episode connects three forms of institutional dependence. In [[Nigeria]], hardship, insecurity, identity, party organization, and low turnout may preserve [[BolaTinubu]]'s electoral advantage despite public discontent. In Europe, [[OneWeb]], [[ICEYE]], and planned warning satellites offer partial routes away from American space systems, but scale, launch capacity, procurement fragmentation, and long lead times keep [[EuropeanSpaceCapabilityDependence]] high. An obituary of [[JeffreyArcher]] closes the episode by holding commercial success, political ambition, charity, deception, perjury, and resilience in the same profile.

## Key Claims
- A Maiduguri primary school with almost 5,000 pupils is expected to lose aid funding, while food shortages already keep some children from attending.
- [[BornoState|Borno State]] combines jihadist violence, damaged livelihoods, weak public services, and severe malnutrition, making national reform claims difficult to reconcile with daily life.
- [[BolaTinubu]]'s fuel-subsidy removal and exchange-rate changes improved macroeconomic stability but imposed large near-term costs on poorer Nigerians amid food inflation, weak harvests, aid cuts, and higher energy prices.
- [[LowTurnoutIncumbencyAdvantage]] links Tinubu's position to ruling-party organization, campaign visibility, religious and ethnic loyalty, service provision, insecurity, and turnout below 20% in Borno in 2023.
- Europe depends on American intelligence, surveillance, satellite internet, missile warning, and launch capacity; temporary loss of unique U.S. intelligence in [[Ukraine]] demonstrates the leverage created by that dependence.
- Eutelsat's [[OneWeb]] has an existing constellation, but the source's roughly 600-versus-more-than-10,000 satellite comparison leaves it far short of [[Starlink]] in scale and terminal economics.
- Finnish radar-satellite company [[ICEYE]] shows that commercial procurement can close selected surveillance gaps faster than traditional multinational programs.
- Planned French and German warning satellites are not expected to replace American capability before the early 2030s, while [[Ariane]]'s low launch cadence illustrates the cost of fragmented industrial organization.
- Pooling interoperable systems with Japan, Canada, and South Korea could improve resilience without requiring complete European self-sufficiency.
- [[JeffreyArcher]] sold hundreds of millions of novels and rose in Conservative politics, but financial recklessness, fabricated claims, perjury, obstruction of justice, imprisonment, and institutional sanctions make his legacy irreducibly mixed.
