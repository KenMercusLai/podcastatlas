# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1098丨刘备的跳槽秘籍：带着资源，越跳越值钱！

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode interprets [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s repeated changes of patron as cumulative rather than merely unstable. From [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] through [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]], [[CaoCao|曹操]], [[YuanShao|袁绍]], [[LiuBiao|刘表]], and later [[LiuZhang|刘璋]], the host argues that Liu Bei repeatedly converts borrowed office, troops, reputation, relationships, and followers into [[PortablePoliticalCapital|可携带政治资本]] without killing former patrons.

The episode treats the seven years at [[XinyeLateHan|新野]] as the longest and most productive interval of [[ActivePoliticalDormancy|主动蛰伏式政治积累]], then presents [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]]'s [[LongzhongPlan|隆中对]] as the move from ambition without a path to an operational program. It closes after the [[ChangbanBattle|长坂坡]] defeat, when [[LuSu|鲁肃]] catches Liu Bei at Dangyang and asks where he will go, directly setting up Hanji 1099.

## Key Claims
- The host contrasts Liu Bei with [[LyuBu|吕布]]: changing patrons is presented as common in late-Han disorder, while killing former patrons crosses a reputational boundary that makes later trust harder.
- Liu Bei's six-patron sequence is framed as cumulative resource conversion: every new platform adds office, troops, networks, followers, or status that can remain useful after departure.
- The source identifies endurance after defeat, restraint under dependence, and a reputation for benevolence as three reasons Liu Bei's organization can survive repeated base loss.
- Liu Biao's use of Liu Bei as a northern buffer is reciprocal rather than one-sided: Xinye shelters Liu Bei while Liu Bei protects Jingzhou's frontier.
- The episode says Liu Bei spends seven years at Xinye and grows a force of more than six thousand while gathering people, family resources, and talent; the exact duration and count remain source-presented.
- The host treats "benevolence can be monetized" as a political-capital claim: reputation helps retain followers such as Guan Yu, Zhao Yun, and Zhuge Liang and attracts civilians, though motive and scale are not independently established here.
- The Longzhong Plan turns a general aspiration into three operational components: a Jingzhou-Yizhou base, restoration of the Han as the legitimating objective, and cooperation with Sun Quan against Cao Cao.
- Liu Biao's death, Liu Cong's surrender, and the Changban collapse strip away the Xinye accumulation, but Lu Su's arrival creates the next coalition opening.

## Key Quotes
> “看着是在给别人打工，其实永远是在为自己创业。” — the host's modern analogy for cumulative patron-platform conversion.

> “仁义是可以变现的。” — the episode's deliberately commercial language for reputation becoming followership and legitimacy.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is recorded. Hanji 1098 bridges Hanji 1097's early resource formation and Hanji 1099's post-Changban alliance turn.
- The six-change list compresses relationships of different legal and political kinds into a modern "job-hopping" analogy. It is retained as the host's organizing frame rather than a complete chronology of equal-status employment moves.
- The seven-year duration, Xinye population, force of more than six thousand, precise dialogue, and participants' inner motives require verification against the underlying historical sources.
- "Benevolence monetization" captures the episode's interpretation of reputation as followership and legitimacy, but it does not prove that every named follower or civilian acted for the same reason.
- The episode's simplified three-line Longzhong Plan and its claim that Lu Su "completely changed" Liu Bei's fate are narrative judgments rather than a complete strategic or causal account.
