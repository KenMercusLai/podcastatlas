# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》716丨乱世之中，律法有多重要？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianwu 14-15 / 38-39 CE immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-717-13-ge-zi-yinfa-de-diwang-zhinu-liz3jsbvw3snfapzsrqw917fqv7v|Hanji 717]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-718-zhengce-ruhe-chengwei-shishang-no-1-de-nvwang-likq4pahq21k6bxbaohwdhcnwg5r|Hanji 718]]. It starts with Ren Gui's commandery-data promotion, a Kuaiji plague notice, and Western Regions requests for restored Han protection under [[Xiongnu|Xiongnu]] pressure, then centers [[LiangTongEasternHan|Liang Tong]] and [[DuLinEasternHan|Du Lin]] debating how far criminal law should be tightened after the early Guangwu pacification.

The durable synthesis is that early Guangwu order was already a capacity and execution problem before the formal degree-field accountability case. [[LiuXiu|Liu Xiu]] refuses the Western Regions request because the center is newly pacified, shelves the Liang-Du penal-law debate rather than choosing either harshness or laxity, lets [[HanXinSituEasternHan|Han Xin / 韩歆]] die after blunt remonstrance is treated as intolerable offense, sends [[WuHanEasternHan|Wu Han]], [[MaChengEasternHan|Ma Cheng]], and [[MaWuEasternHan|Ma Wu]] against worsening Xiongnu raids, relocates more than 60,000 border officials and commoners, and opens land-survey checks that unsettle ordinary households before Hanji 717 turns the problem into [[DutianAccountabilityEasternHan|Dutian Accountability]].

## Key Claims

- In 38 CE, Western Regions states send tribute and ask Han to restore an office managing Western Regions affairs because Xiongnu extraction has become burdensome, but Liu Xiu refuses because the central realm has only recently been pacified.
- [[LiangTongEasternHan|Liang Tong]] argues that repeated death-penalty commutations under Emperors Yuan and Ai weakened deterrence and made officials and commoners treat murder too lightly.
- [[DuLinEasternHan|Du Lin]] objects that later Han law had become so detailed and severe that ordinary gifts could be treated as loot, minor matters could become capital cases, and officials could respond with non-enforcement and mutual shielding.
- Liang Tong's second memorial frames his position as moderation rather than crude harshness: punishment should be neither too light nor too heavy, and mercy toward crafty lawbreakers can injure good people.
- The debate is shelved, so the episode creates a penal-law problem space rather than proving an enacted Guangwu legal reform.
- [[HanXinSituEasternHan|Han Xin / 韩歆]] is dismissed after bluntly predicting severe famine before Liu Xiu; an imperial reprimand then drives Han Xin and his son Han Ying to suicide, prompting public resentment and posthumous appeasement.
- Xiongnu raids, border relocation, strengthened garrisons, and land-survey officials disturbing commoners show that early Guangwu law and order depended on frontier capacity and local implementation, not only court doctrine.
