# VOL.54口腔科｜拒绝牙科恐惧症 先和公立医院主任聊聊洗牙、拔牙、牙周疾病的那些常识和误解

## Summary
This [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode has oral-health clinician [[WuBinOralDoctor|吴斌]] explain oral surgery, wisdom teeth, professional cleaning, gingivitis and periodontitis, comfortable extraction, persistent oral lesions, restoration, and common listener symptoms. Its recurring rule is early, diagnosis-led care: [[WisdomToothClinicalAssessment]] and [[PeriodontalDiseaseProgression]] should be assessed before repeated inflammation or irreversible support loss, while [[DentalComfortCare]] treats pain control and anxiety reduction as parts of safe care rather than luxuries.

The episode extends [[LifecycleOralHealthPrevention]], [[DentalProsthesisMaintenance]], [[PersistentOralLesionEscalation]], and [[PeriodontalSystemicHealthBoundary]]. It is broad public education; pain, swelling, bleeding, mobility, non-healing lesions, missing teeth, suspected pulp disease, or restoration decisions require qualified examination and sometimes imaging.

## Key Claims
- [[WisdomToothClinicalAssessment]] rejects both “all wisdom teeth must be removed” and “leave every painless tooth alone.” Position, eruption, function, cleanability, prior inflammation, decay, damage to the neighboring tooth, cystic change, bite, and possible future restorative value all affect the decision.
- Multiple wisdom teeth can sometimes be removed in one session, but complexity, anesthesia setting, wound burden, infection risk, swelling, and the need to preserve a chewing side make staging an individualized decision.
- [[PeriodontalDiseaseProgression]] distinguishes reversible gingival inflammation from periodontitis with attachment or bone loss. Bleeding during brushing, swelling, mobility, pus, and weak chewing are assessment signals; cleaning removes causative deposits rather than loosening healthy teeth.
- Professional cleaning may reveal spaces, sensitivity, or mobility that calculus had obscured. That post-cleaning perception does not establish that cleaning created the underlying periodontal damage.
- [[DentalComfortCare]] combines anesthesia, sedation when appropriate, less traumatic technique, standardized procedure, and environmental or communication support; “micro-invasive” describes a system of care rather than one instrument or marketing label.
- [[PersistentOralLesionEscalation]] separates familiar self-limited recurrent ulcers from a fixed lesion that persists, enlarges, or deteriorates. Chronic irritation and betel-nut exposure raise concern but do not remotely diagnose oral cancer.
- [[PeriodontalSystemicHealthBoundary]] preserves the episode’s diabetes and cardiovascular associations without treating them as proof that an oral infection caused an individual systemic outcome.
- [[DentalProsthesisMaintenance]] begins before a prosthesis is placed: prolonged tooth loss can allow adjacent teeth to tilt and opposing teeth to over-erupt, while the selected bridge, denture, implant, or other restoration still requires ongoing maintenance.
- Deep decay, spontaneous or night pain, and persistent worsening pain after filling can indicate pulp involvement and warrant review. Root-canal-treated teeth may need clinician-selected structural protection and reduced hard loading.
- [[LifecycleOralHealthPrevention]] joins effective brushing, periodic examination and cleaning, early symptom review, tooth-preserving treatment when feasible, and timely restoration rather than assuming tooth loss is inevitable with age.
