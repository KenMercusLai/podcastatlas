# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1071丨单枪匹马闯河东，这位名臣有多牛？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode fills the transition between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1070-jiaochu-shaxue-weimeng-quan-buxia-panjiang-caocao-lqvyfglj3qefhyxyebfvvda8om5o|Hanji 1070]]'s contested appointment and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1072-duji-danqi-shou-hedong-jing-shi-baixing-yuan-wei-ta-fusi-ltiuwt7v-rjcjpjpovqf9ffl88hu|Hanji 1072]]'s revolt outcome. [[DuJiLateHan|杜畿]] argues that a large punitive force would push undecided households toward [[WeiGuLateHan|卫固]], enters [[HedongCommandery|河东郡]] alone, and survives [[FanXianLateHan|范先]]'s killing of more than thirty officials without displaying fear.

Once accepted, Du Ji gives Wei Gu and Fan Xian formal authority, then redirects mass conscription into voluntary recruitment and rotating leave. The source presents these moves as a low-visibility form of [[LayeredLocalEliteDemobilization|分层式地方武力拆解]]: apparent concession lowers resistance while reliable people move outward into a support network and concentrated hostile manpower disperses. The transcript stops after Du Ji leaves the commandery seat, gathers more than four thousand supporters, and faces the rebel coalition, so the defense and settlement outcome remains in Hanji 1072.

## Key Claims

- Du Ji judges that immediate large-scale attack would frighten undecided Hedong households into Wei Gu's camp and turn an elite dispute into broader rebellion.
- He crosses by an alternate route alone because Wei Gu has not yet declared open rebellion and is likely to preserve appearances by accepting the appointed governor.
- Fan Xian kills more than thirty officials to test or intimidate Du Ji, but Du Ji's outward composure prevents the display from forcing his retreat.
- Du Ji formally appoints Wei Gu to military and administrative roles and places troops under Fan Xian, trading visible authority for time and reduced suspicion.
- When Wei Gu proposes mass conscription, Du Ji substitutes voluntary recruitment; when recruitment is weak, he proposes rotating leave for officers and officials.
- The resulting dispersal separates more reliable personnel from hostile ones and weakens the local coalition's concentrated manpower, extending [[LayeredLocalEliteDemobilization|分层式地方武力拆解]] without Hanji 1073's family relocation and large-scale force.
- After armed threats widen, Du Ji moves to a defensible camp and receives county-official and popular support that grows his force beyond four thousand, setting up [[DistributedLocalResilience|分布式地方韧性]].
- The opening “clarity” discussion divides awakening into value, behavior, and personality clarity, but its universal causal claims and promised outcomes are motivational framing rather than verified findings.
