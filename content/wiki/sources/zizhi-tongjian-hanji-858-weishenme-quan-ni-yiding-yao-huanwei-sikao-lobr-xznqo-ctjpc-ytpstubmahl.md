# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》858丨为什么劝你一定要换位思考？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 163 CE [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] court field immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-859-gudai-haoren-weishenme-nan-dang-haoguan-llkyx18ssvserxncvrtkw7xfduad|Hanji 859]]. It opens with a fable about perspective-taking, then turns to disasters, frontier pressure, local rebellion, [[FengGunLateHan|冯绲]]'s dismissal after eunuch resentment, [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]]'s ignored hunting remonstrance, and the accepted cleanup proposal by [[ZhouJingLateHan|周景]] and [[YangBingLateHan|杨秉]].

The durable contribution is a contrast in how correction reaches power. Chen Fan states the public harms of imperial hunting amid barren fields, weak finances, empty stores, war, displacement, and rain-soaked farming work, but Huan does not answer. Zhou Jing and Yang Bing package the anti-eunuch problem as a restoration of institutional rules, a review of unqualified officeholders, and a concrete execution chain, making the source a case for [[AdviceFramingUnderHierarchy|等级场景中的进言包装]] and [[ThreeExcellenciesInnerCourtJurisdiction|三公监察内廷职权]].

## Key Claims

- The opening fable frames "换位思考" as a warning against judging another actor's reaction without understanding the stakes from that actor's position.
- In 163 CE, fires at imperial sites, [[Xianbei|鲜卑]] pressure, Guiyang unrest, and Wuling Man rebellion place Huan's court in a setting of disaster, frontier strain, and local disorder.
- [[FengGunLateHan|冯绲]] returns from campaign, but renewed local disorder gives eunuch enemies a handle to accuse him, leading to inquiry and dismissal.
- [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] remonstrates against [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]]'s hunting by naming public hardship: barren fields, tight finance, empty stores, unresolved war, displaced people, rain, wheat planting, labor requisition, and road repair.
- Chen Fan's warning is morally and administratively substantive, but it directly opposes the ruler's pleasure and receives no reply.
- [[ZhouJingLateHan|周景]] and [[YangBingLateHan|杨秉]] instead frame the eunuch problem through inherited rules against eunuch kin and guests holding power, unqualified appointments, official resentment, and popular suffering.
- Huan accepts Zhou Jing and Yang Bing's proposal to have relevant civil and military offices review dismissible personnel and report to the Three Excellencies.
- Yang Bing then impeaches more than fifty provincial and commandery officials, including Qingzhou inspector Yang Liang; some are killed and others removed, creating a visible cleanup effect.
