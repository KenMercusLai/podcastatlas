# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》921丨金钱如水：缺则渴死，贪则淹死

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills 181 CE, immediately before the Hanji 922 pre-Yellow-Turban layer. It frames money as a human-nature test, then shows [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] turning public resources into private palace accumulation, [[LyuQiangLateHan|吕强]] naming the public/private damage and appointment-accountability failure, and [[ZhaoZhongLateHan|赵忠]] inheriting [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]]'s inner-court position while Liu Xie is routed into [[EmpressDowagerDongLateHan|董太后]]'s care.

## Key Claims
- Money is not treated as inherently moral or immoral; the episode uses it as a pressure that amplifies desire, fear, trust, and conflict.
- [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s private-treasury demand makes all-under-heaven rhetoric practically hollow, because local officials convert imperial desire into additional requisition, transport, and fee burdens.
- [[LyuQiangLateHan|吕强]] argues that the emperor already owns the realm publicly, so creating a separate private-store demand damages both subjects and imperial legitimacy.
- The same memorial diagnoses personnel failure: when appointments bypass accountable Three-Excellencies selection and flow through Shangshu or direct edicts, responsibility becomes blurred and bad appointees are hard to punish upstream.
- [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]]'s death does not weaken eunuch power; [[ZhaoZhongLateHan|赵忠]] succeeds him as acting dachangqiu, carrying forward a harsher favorite channel.
- The recap of [[WangMeirenLateHan|王美人]]'s poisoning and Liu Xie's transfer to [[EmpressDowagerDongLateHan|董太后]] gives the later [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] succession story an early family-line foundation.

## Key Quotes
> "金钱并无善恶，它就是人性的试金石。" — opening interpretive frame

> "天下之财，莫非陛下之有。" — Lyu Qiang's remonstrance against dividing public and private imperial wealth

> "赵常侍是我母。" — court-favorite language attributed to Emperor Ling about Zhao Zhong
