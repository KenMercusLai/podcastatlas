# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》764丨他为了活命，竟间接灭了罗马帝国？

## Summary
This episode backfills the early Emperor-He layer before the Dou-family cleanup, presenting [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]] as both the victorious commander who breaks [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|Northern Xiongnu]] power and the arrogant outer-relative whose postwar patronage network intimidates the court. It turns the Northern Xiongnu defeat into a qualified trans-Eurasian association: westward Xiongnu movement, European Huns, Gothic displacement, and Roman pressure are linked as a historical chain, but the source itself warns against treating the chain as settled proof. The court layer follows [[YuanAnEasternHan|Yuan An]], [[RenKuiEasternHan|Ren Kui]], [[LeHuiEasternHan|Le Hui]], and [[HanLengEasternHan|Han Leng]] as different forms of resistance, while the frontier layer records [[BanChaoEasternHan|Ban Chao]]'s early protectorate consolidation through [[QiuciKingdom|Qiuci]], Gumo, Wensu, and Ba Ba.

## Key Claims
- Dou Xian's defeat of the Northern Xiongnu is framed as a real military achievement that later political abuse should not erase.
- The episode presents a long-range [[TransEurasianMigrationCascade|migration-cascade]] hypothesis from Northern Xiongnu displacement to European Huns, Gothic movement, and Roman pressure, but keeps the causal identity chain qualified.
- Dou Xian's postwar power turns his household and recommendation network into a practical authorization center, with Geng Kui, [[RenShangLateHan|Ren Shang]], Deng Die, Guo Huang, [[BanGuEasternHan|Ban Gu]], and Fu Yi serving different functions around his bloc.
- Yuan An and Ren Kui impeach dozens of Dou-linked officials, making bureaucratic opposition visible even while Dou Xian remains dangerous.
- Le Hui exposes officials, urges the young emperor's uncles to relinquish power for self-preservation, then dies by suicide after Dou Xian pressures local authorities following his retirement.
- Han Leng blocks other officials from saluting Dou Xian with "wansui," making ritual language a boundary against treating a minister like a ruler.
- The Eastern Han court reestablishes the Western Regions Protector and Wuji Colonel offices after Qiuci, Gumo, and Wensu submit.
- Ban Chao and [[YaoGuangLateHan|Yao Guang]] coerce Qiuci into replacing Youliduo with the pro-Han hostage prince Ba Ba, leaving Yanqi, Weixu, and Weili as the main hesitant states.

## Key Quotes
> "不作死就不会死" - the host's compressed judgment on Dou Xian's post-victory conduct.

> "居上不骄，为下不乱" - the maxim used to frame Han Leng's refusal to let officials salute Dou Xian as if he were the ruler.

> "万岁" - the court cry Han Leng treats as ritually impermissible for a minister.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is recorded.
- The Northern Xiongnu to European Huns to Rome sequence is retained as a source-scoped historical association, not a settled claim that Dou Xian directly or provably destroyed Rome.
- The source's "沃尔丁" and the exact identity of some Western Regions minor actors remain source-scoped until corroborated by later bounded notes.
