## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the opening of the 121 CE post-Deng transition before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-795-dengjia-miewang-hou-weihe-juchao-wei-tamen-mingyuan-lqvmbqrrookphb4becaqbp7h-wi1|Hanji 795]]. It pairs ordinary court business, Chen Zhong's plea for open remonstrance, and Xue Bao's filial refusal of office with the sharper court fracture around [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Empress Dowager Deng]], [[EmperorAnOfHan|Emperor An]], and the [[DengFamilyAnEraLateHan|Deng family]].

The durable synthesis is that the Deng purge begins before the later public defense: [[WangShengPalaceActorLateHan|Wang Sheng]], [[LiRunLateHan|Li Run]], and [[JiangJingLateHan|Jiang Jing]] help turn An's fear of replacement into palace slander, and after Deng's death an alleged plan to install [[LiuYiPingyuanWangLateHan|Pingyuan Wang Liu Yi]] triggers title abolition, office removals, confiscations, exile, forced death, starvation, and royal demotion.

## Key Claims
- Cai Lun's old accusation case, Xianbei-linked frontier losses, Geng Ji's honor, and Liu Chang's demotion show normal imperial administration continuing beside the succession-security fracture.
- [[ChenZhongLateHan|Chen Zhong]] warns Emperor An to tolerate sharp criticism before the worthy-and-upright recommendation process, and the exceptional examinee [[ShiYanLateHan|Shi Yan]] is appointed shizhong.
- Xue Bao's filial conduct and refusal of office give the episode a moral contrast to the palace politics that follow.
- Emperor An's insecurity grows before Deng's death: Wang Sheng fears he may be deposed, and Wang Sheng, Li Run, and Jiang Jing slander Empress Dowager Deng around that danger.
- The accusation against Deng Kui, Deng Hong, and Deng Fang centers on a rumored effort to obtain old deposed-emperor files and install Pingyuan Wang Liu Yi.
- The first Deng purge wave destroys titles, removes offices, confiscates property, exiles Deng Fang's household, forces or induces multiple deaths, and leaves Deng Zhi and Deng Feng to starve.
- Liu Yi survives by accepting demotion, returning to Hejian, closing his doors, and avoiding visible political contact.

## Key Quotes
> "迟迟不归政" - the source's description of the regency delay that feeds Wang Sheng's fear.

> "猛烈迅速" - the host's judgment on how fast the Deng purge moves after the dowager's death.

> "任何与改立传闻有关的人都可能被波及" - the episode's reading of the danger around Liu Yi.
