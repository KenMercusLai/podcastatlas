## Summary
This [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] interview uses [[ShougongGeng|手工耿]] to examine how welded objects can sit between useful product, joke, craft, and art. The episode follows his rural upbringing, unstable manual work, welding skill, 2017 shift into self-media, commercialization discomfort, brand-deal selectivity, slower update rhythm, and later discussion of whether "useless inventions" still create value. Its main synthesis is that 手工耿's objects are not simply failed products: [[UselessInventionValue]], [[ShortVideoCraftCreatorPath]], and [[FolkMakerArtInterpretation]] explain why entertainment, functional play, material labor, and outsider art interpretation coexist.

## Key Claims
- [[ShougongGeng|手工耿]] frames many works as attempts at usefulness even when audiences label them "useless"; [[LuoYonghao]] divides utility into pure entertainment, playful functional value, and serious functional use.
- His creator path depends on embodied craft skill: rural material familiarity, construction labor, welding learned from his father, stainless-steel preferences, and repeated testing shaped objects that could survive filming.
- Short-video platforms changed the payoff of handmade objects by making their making, testing, failure risk, and comic use part of the work's value.
- Commercialization remained selective and craft-constrained: early pricing felt embarrassing, exhibitions changed price perception, and brand deals worked only when client, fan, and maker satisfaction aligned.
- The episode treats 手工耿 as adjacent to modern art or Dadaist interpretation while preserving his self-understanding as a practical maker who does not need art theory to authorize the work.
