# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》100-1｜皇帝中的超长待机王过世了

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode answers the hook left by [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-99-4-shui-shi-kongzi-hou-zui-weidade-sixiangjia-lr4phoq9chc4tz0yb8y95gy21qdd|Qinji 99-4]]: the six states mourned [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] because late in his reign [[QinState|秦国]] already held a quasi-royal position among the lords. The episode reads "天下来宾," Wei's punished lateness, Qin's tianzi-like sacrifice, and Han Huanhui Wang's personal mourning as evidence of [[QuasiRoyalQinHegemony|准天子式秦霸权]] before formal empire.

The second thread contrasts hierarchy levels. [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]] bows before Qin after pressure on [[WeiState|魏国]], but then kills [[WeyHuaijun|卫怀君]] and installs [[WeyYuanjun|卫元君]], making [[WeakOverWeakPredation|弱者向更弱者转嫁压迫]] visible inside the same late-Warring-States order that carries [[QinXiaowenwang|安国君柱 / 秦孝文王]], [[QinZhuangxiangwang|子楚]], [[ZhaoJi|赵姬]], and young [[QinShiHuang|嬴政]] back into the succession foreground.

## Key Claims
- In 254 BCE, Qin attacks [[WeiState|魏国]] and takes Wu City; the host reads this as punishment for Wei arriving late to Qin's lord-attendance scene, not merely as ordinary expansion.
- "天下来宾" is treated as a political signal: the lords or their envoys effectively acknowledge [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] as a hegemonic center even before Qin becomes an empire.
- [[HanState|韩国]] is especially deferential because Qin's occupation of western Zhou territory and pressure around Shangdang make Han's western and northern situation exposed.
- [[HanHuanhuiwang|韩桓惠王]] personally attending Qin Zhaoxiang Wang's funeral in mourning dress is read as survival diplomacy, not simple reverence.
- Qin Zhaoxiang Wang's sacrifice at old Qin Yong city is presented as a tianzi-like ritual, reinforcing the episode's [[PowerEtiquetteReading|权力礼仪细读]].
- The episode places young [[YanTaiziDan|燕太子丹]] and [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]] together in [[Handan|邯郸]], foreshadowing later Yan-Qin hostility without developing it yet.
- [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]] appears as a ruler squeezed by Qin who then exercises arbitrary violence downward against [[WeyState|卫国]].
- The killing of [[WeyHuaijun|卫怀君]] and installation of [[WeyYuanjun|卫元君]] show that weak states can still dominate even weaker clients.
- In 251 BCE, [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] dies after fifty-six years on the throne, closing the longest Qin reign and a major stage in Qin's expansion.
- The episode credits Qin Zhaoxiang Wang's era with the use of figures such as [[FanJu|范雎]] and [[BaiQi|白起]], while keeping the evaluation tied to this source's perspective.
- After the death, fifty-three-year-old Anguo Jun succeeds as [[QinXiaowenwang|秦孝文王]], honors Tang Bazi as queen dowager, and makes [[QinZhuangxiangwang|子楚]] crown prince.
- [[QinZhuangxiangwang|子楚]]'s rise changes Zhao's incentives: [[ZhaoState|赵国]] quickly sends [[ZhaoJi|赵姬]] and young [[QinShiHuang|嬴政]] back to Qin to avoid deepening enmity.
- The host links Ying Zheng's later treatment of Handan enemies to this childhood danger, treating memory and revenge as part of his early political formation.

## Key Quotes
> "天下来宾" - the episode's compact phrase for Qin's late Zhaoxiang-era attendance scene.

> "韩王衰绖入吊祠" - the funeral detail used to mark Han's unusually deferential posture.

> "被强者欺负后再欺负更弱者" - the host's plain-language diagnosis of Wei's behavior toward Wey.
