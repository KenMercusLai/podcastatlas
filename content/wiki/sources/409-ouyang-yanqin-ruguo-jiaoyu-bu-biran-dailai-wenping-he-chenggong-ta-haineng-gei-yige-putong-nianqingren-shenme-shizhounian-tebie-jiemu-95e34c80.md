# #409 欧阳艳琴：如果教育不必然带来文凭和成功，它还能给一个普通年轻人什么？丨十周年特别节目

## Summary

This [[ShengdongJixi|声东击西]] tenth-anniversary interview has [[XuTao|徐涛]] speak with former investigative journalist [[OuyangYanqin|欧阳艳琴]] about the ten-year path from a migrant-children maker space in Dongguan to [[FoodSchool|食物学堂]]. The school began with a three-year combination of whole-person education and fixed specialties such as programming and graphic design, then used graduate employment experience and industry change to reconsider the promise embedded in that model. Since 2022 it has moved toward one year in school plus two years of transition support, emphasizing [[CareerCognitionEducation|career cognition]], [[SelfRelianceOrientedEducation|self-reliance-oriented education]], and [[RelativeAdvantageCareerMatching|relative-advantage career matching]] rather than a guaranteed credential, specialty, or class transition.

The episode's central judgment is that education for young people outside a secure academic path should protect vitality, provide rules and belonging, and create real encounters through which students can understand themselves and work. Kitchen coordination, community service, internships, employer conversations, and first jobs become evidence about reliability, communication, preference, responsibility, and fit. The goal is not an immediately perfect job but a supported starting point from which a young person can earn a living, ask for help, revise direction, and remain responsible for choices.

## Key Claims

- [[OuyangYanqin|欧阳艳琴]] moved from Caixin investigative reporting into migrant-child education after drawing on her own family connection to Dongguan factory communities.
- A Dongguan maker space treated factories, waste materials, trucks, local craftspeople, vending, nature exploration, and recycling businesses as educational resources rather than defining scarcity only by formal school inputs.
- Experience in a Beijing migrant-worker children's school exposed a sharp break between compulsory schooling and later study or work, motivating the creation of [[FoodSchool|食物学堂]] in 2018.
- The first three-year model combined life recovery, general education, and specialties, but graduate decisions and changing programming and design work showed that completing a course did not create a stable occupation or honest employment guarantee.
- The current one-plus-two model places one year of school before entry into work and continues support for two years, treating workplaces as learning environments without promising permanent placement.
- [[CareerCognitionEducation|Career cognition]] requires direct knowledge of industries, tasks, workplace conditions, and one's own preferences before accepting or rejecting an opportunity.
- [[RelativeAdvantageCareerMatching]] treats talent as a usable advantage relative to a concrete role, including reliability, structured thinking, tolerance for repetition, service orientation, coordination, or willingness to initiate interaction.
- [[SelfRelianceOrientedEducation]] makes agency more basic than generic employability skills: students should know why they choose an internship or job, accept uncertainty, seek help when needed, and adjust direction without surrendering responsibility.
- Real responsibilities such as meal coordination, administrative work, community service, commercial orders, and workplace practice can build confidence because students experience themselves completing consequential tasks for other people.
- The school does not promise class mobility or a standardized success outcome; it aims for economic foothold, dignity, vitality, and continuing development among ordinary young people.
