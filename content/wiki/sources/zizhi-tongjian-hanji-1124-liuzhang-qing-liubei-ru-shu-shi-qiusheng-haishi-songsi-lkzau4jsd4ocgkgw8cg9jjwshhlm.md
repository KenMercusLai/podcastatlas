# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1124丨刘璋请刘备入蜀，是求生还是送死？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode explains why [[LiuZhang|刘璋]] invites [[LiuBei|刘备]] into [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] after [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s western campaign makes [[Hanzhong|汉中]] appear vulnerable. [[ZhangSongLateHan|张松]] uses the external threat and claims of internal unreliability to present Liu Bei as the necessary defender, while [[FaZheng|法正]] serves as the welcoming envoy.

The episode also preserves explicit internal opposition. [[HuangQuanLateHan|黄权]] warns that Liu Bei cannot be treated safely as either an ordinary subordinate or a fully honored guest because the latter arrangement creates a rival power center, and [[WangLeiLateHan|王累]] protests dramatically at Chengdu's gate. Liu Zhang rejects both warnings, making the invitation a pre-delegation case of [[EntrustedResourcePowerTransfer|受托资源反向转移]] before the troop, supply, and command transfers narrated in Hanji 1125.

## Key Claims

- Cao Cao's move toward Hanzhong in 211 makes Liu Zhang fear that control of the northern gateway could expose Yizhou to a later attack.
- Liu Zhang and [[ZhangLuFivePecks|张鲁]] are long-standing enemies, yet Zhang Lu's control of Hanzhong also functions as a buffer once Cao Cao becomes the larger threat.
- The episode portrays Fa Zheng and Zhang Song as capable but dissatisfied insiders who see little future under Liu Zhang and identify Liu Bei as a more promising patron.
- Zhang Song combines an external-threat warning with allegations that [[PangXiLateHan|庞羲]], Li Yi, and other insiders may defect, narrowing Liu Zhang's perceived options until Liu Bei appears indispensable.
- Liu Zhang accepts the proposal and sends Fa Zheng with four thousand men to welcome Liu Bei, though the exact figure and arrangements require corroboration.
- Huang Quan warns that subordinating Liu Bei will not satisfy him, while honoring him as a guest will create two competing centers of authority inside Yizhou.
- Liu Zhang rejects Huang Quan's warning and sends him to Guanghan; Wang Lei then protests by hanging upside down at a Chengdu gate, but Liu Zhang again refuses to reverse course.
- The episode treats Liu Zhang's failure as a combination of external weakness, poor personnel use, fear-driven judgment, and dependence on a stronger ally rather than as betrayal alone.

## Key Quotes

> “若客有泰山之安，则主有累卵之危。” - the episode's version of Huang Quan's warning that a secure guest can make the host precarious.

> “一山不容二虎。” - the host's summary of the dual-power danger created by inviting Liu Bei into Yizhou.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. The episode fills the decision stage between Hanji 1123's covert Fa Zheng-Zhang Song alignment and Hanji 1125's transfer of troops, supplies, and command to Liu Bei.
- Chronology caution: the source says Cao Cao sent forces against Zhang Lu directly after the Tong Pass campaign; the exact operational sequence and how immediate the threat appeared require comparison with the underlying histories.
- Motive caution: Fa Zheng and Zhang Song's ambition, resentment, private conversations, and assessment of Liu Bei are interpretive reconstructions rather than independently established inner states.
- Source-scope caution: the four-thousand-man escort, Li Yi and Pang Xi allegations, Huang Quan's exact wording and reassignment, Wang Lei's protest mechanics, Liu clan genealogy, and Sun Quan's motive in recalling Lady Sun require direct textual corroboration.
