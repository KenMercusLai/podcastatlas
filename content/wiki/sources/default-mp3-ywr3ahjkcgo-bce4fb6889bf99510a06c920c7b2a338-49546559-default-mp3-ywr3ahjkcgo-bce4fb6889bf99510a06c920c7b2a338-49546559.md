# Healing Your Heart

## Summary
This [[HiddenBrain]] episode follows resilience researcher [[LucyHone]] after her 12-year-old daughter Abby, Hone's friend Sally, and Sally's daughter Ella were killed in a 2014 car crash. Hone rejects [[FiveStageGriefModel|the five-stage model]] as a universal script and develops [[GriefResponseAgency]]: physical and emotional grief reactions may arrive involuntarily, while attention, action, and small daily choices can sometimes be tested by whether they help or harm adaptation.

The episode places that agency inside [[DualProcessBereavementOscillation]], not above grief. Confronting loss can alternate with restorative activity, positive emotion, family attention, and ordinary life; [[StructuredGrievingPractice]] can include limiting open-ended counterfactual questions without suppressing sorrow. The aim is not to shrink grief, blame bereaved people, or promise self-sufficiency, but to let life expand around an enduring loss with support and professional care when needed.

## Key Claims
- [[FiveStageGriefModel]] began as a description associated with terminal illness and becomes misleading when treated as a required, ordered bereavement script.
- [[GriefResponseAgency]] distinguishes involuntary pain, shock, bodily arousal, and emotional swings from the thoughts, actions, and attentional choices through which a person responds.
- Asking whether a thought or action is helping or harming can guide choices about counterfactual rumination, photographs, social media, legal proceedings, and family attention without requiring emotional suppression.
- [[DualProcessBereavementOscillation]] normalizes movement between loss-oriented contact and restoration-oriented activity, including laughter, awe, sport, friendship, work, and hope.
- Deliberately noticing what remains good can widen attention during adversity, but the wording and practice need to fit the individual rather than become compulsory gratitude.
- Agency is offered as hope, not blame: the episode rejects both passive inevitability and demands that bereaved people recover by willpower alone.
- Grief can remain present while relationships, experiences, and responsibilities allow life to grow around it.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is adopted. The episode converges with the wiki's existing non-linear grief account while adding a stronger everyday agency test.
- Agency does not establish that every reaction is controllable, that adaptive choices prevent severe or prolonged grief, or that struggling reflects moral or psychological failure.
- The accident, counselor warnings, trial decision, family experience, and specific coping practices are one family's account and do not define a universal pathway.
- Claims about typical recovery, the five stages, resilience strategies, cognitive-behavioral framing, and the dual-process model remain episode-scoped because the supplied document is a structured summary rather than a transcript, clinical record, or systematic review.
