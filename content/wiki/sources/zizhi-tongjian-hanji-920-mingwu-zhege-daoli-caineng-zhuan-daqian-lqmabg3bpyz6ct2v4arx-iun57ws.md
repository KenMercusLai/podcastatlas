# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》920丨明悟这个道理，才能赚大钱

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills 181 CE immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-921-jinqian-ru-shui-que-ze-ke-si-tan-ze-yan-si-lj1dk4qdgcago9elr7ctuvre3zic|Hanji 921]]. It opens with [[WealthThroughDaoFrame|求财先明道]], then follows [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s court through horse-procurement price cornering, the [[JiaozhiCommandery|交趾郡]] rebellion suppressed by [[ZhuJunLateHan|朱儁]], disaster and office churn, [[Xianbei|鲜卑]] succession weakness, palace-market play, and the public/private treasury problem that the next episode develops.

## Key Claims
- The wealth opening is source-scoped: the host says lack of money is really lack of "Dao", meaning failure to see rules, trends, value, human feeling, humility, learning, and moral limits.
- [[FanLi|范蠡]] is used as the exemplary wealth figure because the source says he grasped rule and human nature, left politics, rebuilt wealth repeatedly, and treated wealth as something carried by virtue rather than only technique.
- The court's new office for receiving and feeding requisitioned grain and war horses creates a signal that local strongmen can capture. In the source, horse traders raise one horse to two million cash, turning state procurement into [[ProcurementSignalPriceCornering|征发信号价格垄断]].
- The southern crisis is treated as fast military repair: [[WuhuManJiaozhi|乌浒蛮]] rebellion and [[LiangLongLateHan|梁龙]]'s local rising break commanderies and counties until [[ZhuJunLateHan|朱儁]] is sent as Jiaozhi inspector and pacifies the rebellion in less than a month.
- The 181 CE court is surrounded by disorder signals: a general amnesty, egg-sized hail on June 19, a solar eclipse on September 1, a North Palace fire, Liu Kuan's dismissal, [[YangCiLateHan|杨赐]]'s dismissal, and [[ChenDanLateHan|陈耽]]'s appointment as situ.
- [[Xianbei|鲜卑]] pressure is paired with leadership transition. After [[TanshihuaiXianbei|檀石槐]] dies, [[HeLianXianbei|和连]] succeeds but is framed as less capable; after his death, [[KuitouXianbei|魁头]] takes power while a later succession contest disperses the group.
- [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s palace shops are not presented as ordinary commerce. The source treats palace women trading, quarrelling, fighting, stealing, and staging a miniature market for the emperor as [[PalaceMarketTheater|宫市游乐式权力错位]].
- The closing public/private treasury discussion foreshadows [[ImperialPrivateTreasuryExtraction|帝王私库进奉盘剥]]: a ruler who should govern from office instead treats the state as material for private amusement and accumulation.
