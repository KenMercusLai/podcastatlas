# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》326-1｜太威武！霍去病封狼居胥！

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]]'s victory and [[LiGuangHanGeneral|李广]]'s failed route with the eastern arm of the [[MobeiBattleWesternHan|漠北之战]]. It says [[HuoQubing|霍去病]] led cavalry from Dai and Youbeiping, crossed more than two thousand li, defeated the [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] Left Wise King's force, pursued north, and performed the heaven-and-earth rites remembered as “封狼居胥.”

The episode contrasts Wei Qing's deliberate chariot-cavalry formation with Huo's speed, surprise, direct assault, and pursuit. Its durable synthesis is double-sided: the campaign produced a major strategic displacement of Xiongnu power and strengthened Han access toward the northwest, but the reported loss of most expedition horses left Han unable to continue large-scale attack at the same tempo. Exact routes, locations, totals, ritual geography, and “opening the Silk Road” causation remain episode-attributed.

## Key Claims

- Huo Qubing leads the eastern Mobei force out from Dai and Youbeiping, crosses a great distance north of the desert, and meets the Xiongnu Left Wise King's main force rather than the chanyu's own force.
- The episode says Huo defeats that force, captures three princes and eighty-three senior officers, and reports 70,443 killed or captured before pursuing toward the northern lake region.
- Huo performs a heaven sacrifice at Langjuxu Mountain and an earth sacrifice at Guyan Mountain, turning extreme operational reach into the political memory of “封狼居胥.”
- Wei Qing is presented as using formation and chariot-cavalry coordination, while Huo is presented as favoring surprise when possible and direct shock followed by rapid pursuit when detected.
- The battle is said to push Xiongnu power north of the desert, contribute to the “漠南无王庭” situation, and support stronger Han control of the Hexi and western corridor.
- Victory also consumes campaign capacity: the episode reports that only thirty thousand of fourteen wan horses returned, leaving Han unable to sustain another immediate offensive of the same scale.
- Han Wudi rewards Huo with 5,800 additional households and also rewards subordinates including [[LuBodeWesternHan|路博德]], [[WeiShanWesternHan|卫山]], [[ZhaoPonuWesternHan|赵破奴]], and Zhao Anji.

## Key Quotes

> "封狼居胥山。" - the episode's compressed name for the rites that became Huo Qubing's most famous victory symbol.

> "漠南无王庭。" - the strategic outcome formula used after the Mobei victory and Xiongnu withdrawal.

## Connections

- [[HuoQubing|霍去病]] and [[MobeiBattleWesternHan|漠北之战]] - commander, eastern route, defeat of the Left Wise King, far-northern pursuit, and ritual culmination.
- [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]] and [[LiGuangHanGeneral|李广]] - parallel western command and missed-route background used to contrast different outcomes and command styles.
- [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] - opposing power whose eastern force is defeated and whose southern-steppe position is presented as weakened.
- [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] - ruler who assigns commands and rewards Huo and his officers after victory.
- [[MilitaryDoctrineEraFit|兵法的时代适配]] - Huo's speed, direct mounted attack, and pursuit extend the existing doctrine-adaptation case while horse losses expose its material limit.
- [[AncientSilkRoad|丝绸之路]] - wider corridor consequence invoked by the episode, with sole-cause and “complete opening” language kept qualified.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with existing wiki content was found. The episode supplies the missing Mobei operational and ritual layer before Hanji 327-2's Hexi doctrine discussion.
- The opening claim that Wei Qing had been Han Wudi's male favorite, the account of Huo being raised and personally instructed in the palace, and the exact nature of Wudi's intimate relationships are not established by the supplied summary and remain source-scoped.
- The more-than-two-thousand-li route, river and mountain identifications, capture and casualty totals, three-prince and eighty-three-official count, location of Langjuxu and Guyan, Lake Baikal identification, horse-loss total, and 5,800-household reward are compact episode claims rather than independently verified figures here.
- “扫荡河西走廊打开丝绸之路” and the claim that this battle completely secured the Tarim Basin and Central Asian trade routes compress a multi-stage process into one heroic narrative; the wiki preserves them only as broad consequences, not sole-cause findings.
- “突袭、硬冲” is the host's stylized contrast with Wei Qing, not a complete reconstruction of either commander's doctrine or formation system.
