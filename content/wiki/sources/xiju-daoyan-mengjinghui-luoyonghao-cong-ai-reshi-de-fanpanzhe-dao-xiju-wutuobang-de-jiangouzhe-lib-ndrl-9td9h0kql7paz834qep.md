# 戏剧导演孟京辉×罗永浩！从“爱惹事的反叛者”，到戏剧乌托邦的建构者

## Summary
This [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] interview has [[LuoYonghao|罗永浩]] and [[MengJinghui|孟京辉]] trace Meng's route from 1980s literary and modernist discovery through Central Academy of Drama experiments, 1990s small-theatre work, 《恋爱的犀牛》, rehearsal practice, and the building of [[WuzhenTheatreFestival|乌镇戏剧节]]. Its durable synthesis joins [[LiveTheatreCoPresence|live-theatre co-presence]] and [[TheatreFestivalCulturalEcology|theatre-festival cultural ecology]]: theatre survives media change through embodied, revisable encounters, while festivals give creators, audiences, venues, and imported work enough density to form an ecology.

The episode also connects artistic freedom to economic structure. Meng says the long-running success of 《恋爱的犀牛》 became a financial base for later experimentation, while rehearsal evolved from extreme director authority toward collective trial, absorption, correction, and renewed work with each cast. Phones, short video, short drama, and social-media check-ins compete for attention, but the conversation treats new media as possible future art forms rather than merely cultural decline.

## Key Claims
- Meng describes his theatre formation as an interaction among literature, absurdist and modernist drama, film, music, peer exchange, institutional resistance, and practical opportunities rather than one planned career path.
- His early 《等待戈多》 experiment and 1990s work such as 《思凡》 and 《盗版浮士德》 are presented as attempts to construct a personal relationship to the world during a weak commercial period for spoken drama.
- 《恋爱的犀牛》 spread largely through audience word of mouth, repaid a high-risk production bet, and became a durable repertoire asset that gave Meng greater financial and artistic freedom.
- Meng now describes directing as absorbing actors' and designers' ideas, integrating them through aesthetic judgment, and treating rehearsal as a space where the director can admit error and try again.
- [[LiveTheatreCoPresence|Live co-presence]] makes each performance responsive to actors, audiences, space, timing, and the director's renewed work with a new cast; recording can preserve an archive but does not fully substitute for the encounter.
- [[WuzhenTheatreFestival|乌镇戏剧节]] and other festivals are framed as dense cultural environments that join international work, young creators, audiences, venues, conversation, and informal social life.
- Phones and feed media fragment attention inside and outside theatres; Meng's responses include protected phone-free time, slow art, deliberate idleness, and theatrical experiences that are difficult to reproduce online.
- The conversation acknowledges short video and short drama as emerging forms that may develop artistic maturity even while they redirect talent, time, and audiences away from older media.

## Key Quotes
> “做艺术就是表达自己和世界的关系。” - Meng's account of artistic work as situated expression.

> “导演要和演员共同活在这个舞台上。” - Meng's reason for rehearsing a long-running work anew with each cast.

> “没有市场也不老实，要自己造一个市场。” - the episode's return to Meng's childhood “不要给他市场” story as a creative and institutional principle.
