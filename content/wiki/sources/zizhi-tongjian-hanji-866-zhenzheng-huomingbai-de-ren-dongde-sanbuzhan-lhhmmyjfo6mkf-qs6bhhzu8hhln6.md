# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》866丨真正活明白的人，懂得“三不沾”

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 165 CE Huan-era layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-867-wugu-bei-po-zangshui-ta-xuanze-baqi-huiji-lqenvoy7x8ic-qn3-bf93qj8tjvk|Hanji 867]], [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-868-ying-le-ye-shi-shu-jia-donghan-hougong-zhengyan-ji-lmmjgx23nlkeqr3mkzliij-zwe2v|Hanji 868]], and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-869-liying-weihe-bei-chengwei-tianxia-diyi-kaimo-lltckcioe1n9v91yy4c9vvwmz4mo|Hanji 869]]. It links [[YangYuanqunLateHan|羊元群]]'s bribery, [[FengGunLateHan|冯绲]]'s accusation, and [[LiuYouLateHan|刘祐]]'s failed confiscation order to [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]]'s protection of eunuch-linked interests. The second half contrasts that legal inversion with [[YangBingLateHan|杨秉]]'s clean-office discipline and [[LiuYuAstrologerLateHan|刘瑜]]'s memorial tying eunuch privilege, palace waste, judicial bribery, public hardship, and rebellion risk together.

## Key Claims
- In 165 CE, [[YangYuanqunLateHan|羊元群]] leaves [[BeihaiCommandery|北海郡]] with a corrupt record and uses eunuch bribery to make [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]]'s prosecution rebound onto the investigator.
- [[FengGunLateHan|冯绲]]'s handling of the jailed Shan Qian case becomes another example of enforcement being recoded as offense when eunuch relatives are involved.
- [[LiuYouLateHan|刘祐]] orders the confiscation of land and property that [[SuKangLateHan|苏康]] and [[GuanBaLateHan|管霸]] acquired cheaply, but [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] punishes Liu You, Li Ying, and Feng Gun instead.
- [[YangBingLateHan|杨秉]] embodies a counter-model of late-Han public service through clean compensation, gift refusal, anti-corruption enforcement, and the "three nots" toward wine, sexual entanglement, and money.
- [[LiuYuAstrologerLateHan|刘瑜]]'s memorial treats eunuch enfeoffment, palace women, construction labor, judicial bribery, popular distress, and rebellion as linked causes rather than separate incidents.
- The [[ZhuGaiLateHan|朱丐]] and [[HuLanLateHan|胡兰]] rebellion thread appears as the next downstream pressure point after official extraction and blocked redress.

## Key Quotes
> "美酒、美色、金钱都不爱" — the episode's compact formulation of Yang Bing's "三不沾" discipline.

> "星星之火可以燎原" — Liu Yu's warning that small disturbances can grow when grievances remain unresolved.

## Connections
- [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]] — appears before his later model-official reputation as an investigator punished after a eunuch-backed bribery reversal.
- [[FengGunLateHan|冯绲]] — legal official punished after a eunuch-relative prison case is turned against him.
- [[LiuYouLateHan|刘祐]] — fiscal official punished after trying to confiscate property held by Su Kang and Guan Ba.
- [[YangBingLateHan|杨秉]] — clean late-Han minister whose death frames Liu Yu's remonstrance and the episode's integrity theme.
- [[LiuYuAstrologerLateHan|刘瑜]] — Guangling scholar and omen reader whose role begins here as a political remonstrant recommended by Yang Bing.
- [[GuanBaLateHan|管霸]] and [[SuKangLateHan|苏康]] — eunuch officials whose property acquisitions show fiscal privilege before their later violent-protection role.
- [[EunuchPatronageViolenceShield|宦官庇护下的地方暴行]] — the episode broadens the pattern from violent kinship shields to property extraction and prosecution reversal.
- [[RootCausePacification|查因安抚式治理]] — Liu Yu frames rebellion risk as the result of fiscal, judicial, and palace-level causes that must be addressed before force.
- [[ThreeRefusalsIntegrity|三不沾式清廉自守]] — the episode's normative counter-model to late-Han court capture.
