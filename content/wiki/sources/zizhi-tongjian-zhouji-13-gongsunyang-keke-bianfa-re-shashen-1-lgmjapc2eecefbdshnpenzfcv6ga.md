# 《资治通鉴·周纪》13丨 公孙鞅苛刻变法惹杀身（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]] after he wins [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]]'s trust and prepares to remake [[QinState|秦国]] through severe reform. It frames the dispute with [[GanLong|甘龙]] as a clash between inherited custom and [[ReformWithoutPopularDeliberation|reform without popular deliberation]]. The episode then lays out the new order's concrete machinery: [[CollateralPunishmentAndGuiltByAssociation|什伍连坐]], informant rewards, heavy punishments, agricultural production incentives, [[MilitaryMeritRankSystem|军功爵制]], and [[XimuLixin|徙木立信]] before ending on [[YingSi|太子嬴驷]] violating the new law.

## Key Claims
- In 360 BCE, [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] gives no event notice for [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]]'s ninth year; the episode's substantive story begins in 359 BCE.
- [[ShangYang|公孙鞅]] gains [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]]'s strong backing and prepares to push [[ShangYangReforms|商鞅变法]] despite elite resistance in [[QinState|秦国]].
- Shang Yang argues that large reforms should not wait for ordinary people's prior consent, because the people can share the results after success even if they do not join the initial deliberation.
- [[GanLong|甘龙]] counters that government should follow inherited custom and existing law because abrupt change can unsettle society.
- Qin Xiao Gong resolves the dispute in Shang Yang's favor and appoints him 左庶长, a substantial rank inside Qin's twenty-level status order.
- The new law uses five-household and ten-household groups, mutual surveillance, informant incentives, and collective liability to turn local society into a state-control grid.
- Agricultural and textile overproduction are rewarded, while people treated as idle merchants or craftsmen can face severe household-wide punishment.
- [[MilitaryMeritRankSystem|军功爵制]] ties status, office, land, servants, and honor to battlefield merit, weakening old aristocratic inheritance and giving common soldiers a path upward.
- [[XimuLixin|徙木立信]] is used to prove the state will actually pay promised rewards before the reform order is issued.
- After more than a year, thousands complain about the new law, and the episode ends at the moment [[YingSi|太子嬴驷]] violates it.

## Key Quotes
> "民不可与虑始，而可与乐成" - Shang Yang's reform-before-consensus formula.

> "智者作法，愚者制焉" - the episode's severe legalist contrast between lawmakers and followers.
