## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode compresses [[BanChaoEasternHan|班超]]'s early Western Regions arc from his 73 CE emergence under [[DouGuEasternHan|窦固]] through [[ShanshanKingdomLateHan|鄯善]], [[KhotanKingdomLateHan|于阗]], [[ShuleKingdom|疏勒]], and the recall crisis after [[EmperorMingOfHan|汉明帝]]'s death. It reinforces the existing early Ban Chao synthesis: small-contingent success comes from military nerve joined to information reading, psychological shock, local political leverage, and responsibility to dependent allies.

The durable contribution is recap and integration rather than contradiction. The source ties the Shanshan night attack, Khotan shaman killing, Shule regime restoration, and Shule/Khotan pleas against recall into one story of [[AdvantageFirstCombatDoctrine|advantage-first action]], [[FrontierProxyConquestStrategy|local political replacement]], and [[MissionDrivenFrontierResponsibility|mission-driven frontier responsibility]].

## Key Claims

- In 73 CE, Ban Chao first proves himself in Dou Gu's army by anticipating a Xiongnu night attack and helping defeat it through empty-camp bait and outside ambush.
- Ban Chao accepts the Shanshan mission with only thirty-six familiar followers, making mobility, trust, and execution speed more important than headcount.
- At Shanshan, he reads the king's cooling attitude as evidence that [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|Northern Xiongnu]] envoys have arrived, attacks the envoy camp by night with fire, drums, shouting, and ambush positions, then forces Shanshan into Han alignment.
- At Khotan, Ban Chao turns the shaman's demand for a horse into a trap, kills the shaman, punishes the intermediary, and uses the local religious frame to push the king away from Xiongnu influence.
- At Shule, Ban Chao uses Duti's Qiuci-backed outsider status and local resentment to restore a royal-line successor, while sparing Duti to leave room for later Qiuci diplomacy.
- After Mingdi's death, Zhangdi-era mourning rules, drought, and renewed Western Regions instability lead the court to recall Ban Chao, but Shule and Khotan treat his departure as a security collapse.
- Ban Chao's decision to remain in the Western Regions is framed as a turning point where he becomes more than an envoy: his presence anchors local pro-Han trust.

## Key Quotes

> "只要求带熟悉的三十六人同行" - the source's compact description of Ban Chao's small-contingent choice.

> "大汉天子才是神的儿子" - the Khotan argument that turns local religious logic against the shaman's authority.

> "汉使如父母，不能离开" - the Khotan plea that frames Ban Chao's recall as a local security crisis.
