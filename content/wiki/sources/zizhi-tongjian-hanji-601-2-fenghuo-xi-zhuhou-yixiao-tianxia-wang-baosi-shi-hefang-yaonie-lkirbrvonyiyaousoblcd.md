# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》601-2丨烽火戏诸侯：一笑天下亡，褒姒是何方“妖孽”？

## Summary
This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode uses [[BaoSi|褒姒]] to separate memorable dynastic-collapse legend from recoverable history. It retells the [[LienuZhuan|《列女传》]]-style myth of dragon saliva, a sealed box, a black lizard, abandonment, and adoption in Bao, then shifts to [[ZhouYouwang|周幽王]], warning that the supernatural biography is closer to myth than reliable history.

## Key Claims
- The episode frames Bao Si through the later "倾国倾城" imagination: her smile becomes a shorthand for the fall of the [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]] and Western Zhou crisis.
- It groups [[BaoSi|褒姒]] with Mei Xi, Da Ji, and Li Ji as so-called "四大妖姬," but treats this as a traditional blame narrative rather than a complete explanation of state collapse.
- The source retells a mythic biography in which dragon saliva passes from Xia to Shang to Zhou, is released by King Li of Zhou, becomes a black lizard, and eventually leads to Bao Si's strange birth and abandonment.
- The episode says Bao was defeated in 779 BCE and offered Bao Si to Zhou Youwang; in 771 BCE Shen Hou, Quanrong, and Zeng attacked, Zhou Youwang was killed at Lishan, and Bao Si disappeared after being taken by Quanrong.
- The host explicitly qualifies the supernatural account as "当不得真," leaving Bao Si's historical profile complex, morally disputed, and mostly obscure.

## Key Quotes
> "前面讲的内容更像一段神话，当不得真" - the host's caution against reading the supernatural birth story as firm history.

> "复杂而多面的女人" - the episode's concluding frame for Bao Si's blurred historical image.
