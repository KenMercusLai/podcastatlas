# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》243｜百人吓退三千敌军，李广如何做到的？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode joins three late-[[HanJingdi|汉景帝]] branches. Jingdi further reduces flogging counts and regulates the implement, target area, and continuity of execution; [[LiGuangHanGeneral|李广]] then saves a force of roughly one hundred from several thousand [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] cavalry by refusing flight, dismounting, and making the enemy suspect a nearby ambush; and [[NingChengWesternHan|宁成]] is summoned to restore order around Chang'an after [[ZhiDuWesternHan|郅都]]'s death.

The durable military contribution is [[AmbiguityBasedDeterrentBluff|疑兵式心理威慑]]. Li Guang cannot make his small force physically stronger, but he can make its calm, exposed posture inconsistent with what the enemy expects from an isolated and frightened unit. The source also connects his reputation to shared water, food, rewards, and living conditions, while keeping troop totals, enemy reasoning, later deterrence, and the host's modern lesson about sincerity source-scoped.

## Key Claims

- Jingdi reduces a three-hundred-stroke sentence to two hundred and a two-hundred-stroke sentence to one hundred, while specifying a smoother bamboo implement, blows to the buttocks, and no change of executioner mid-punishment.
- The host presents this reform as harm reduction but speculates that lighter punishment could also weaken deterrence; the episode supplies no crime-rate evidence for that tradeoff.
- A Xiongnu incursion through Yanmen and Shangjun reportedly kills more than two thousand Han personnel and targets horses, while the Han court expands frontier horse raising.
- Li Guang and roughly one hundred riders encounter several thousand Xiongnu cavalry after pursuing enemy archers; he judges that fleeing toward a distant camp would expose them to mounted pursuit.
- Li Guang orders the group to stay, advance closer, dismount, and appear unafraid, causing the opposing force to suspect that the small party is bait for an unseen Han ambush.
- The two sides watch one another until night, when the Xiongnu force withdraws; the episode attributes survival to Li Guang's composure, enemy-modeling, and control of his soldiers' visible behavior.
- The source links Li Guang's “Flying General” reputation and broad mourning to riding and archery, repeated frontier fighting, material burden-sharing, and a quiet, sincere public character summarized by “桃李不言，下自成蹊.”
- After Zhi Du's death, Ning Cheng becomes Chang'an zhongwei; his cruelty and weaker integrity are contrasted with Zhi Du, while elite fear is said to restore some order.
- The episode closes with the death of Chengyang king Liu Xi and a preview of [[ZhouYafu|周亚夫]]'s imprisonment and self-starvation.

## Key Quotes

> “如果立刻逃跑，匈奴会追上并射杀他们。” - the episode summary's explanation of why retreat is more dangerous than holding position.

> “桃李不言，下自成蹊。” - the Sima Qian appraisal used to connect quiet conduct with earned reputation.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. This source supplies an earlier Li Guang bluff-and-withdrawal episode that complements, rather than replaces, later evidence for encirclement survival, loose administration, severe losses, route failure, accepted responsibility, and misuse of public power.
- The episode treats reduced flogging as both humane and potentially less deterrent, but provides no comparative crime or recidivism evidence; the deterrence concern remains the host's inference.
- Exact punishment terminology, dates, bamboo dimensions, casualty and horse figures, force sizes, distances, dialogue, tactical sequence, enemy beliefs, the duration of later frontier deterrence, Li Guang's lifetime battle total, and the scale of mourning require primary-text comparison.
- “民族英雄,” the contrast between sincerity and modern self-promotion, and the moral ranking of Ning Cheng against Zhi Du are evaluative frames rather than independently established historical findings.
