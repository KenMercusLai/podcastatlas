## Summary
This [[DuanwenNewsPodcast|端闻]] episode asks why incumbent Taipei mayor [[JiangWanan|蒋万安]] has not opened a decisive polling lead over [[ShenBoyang|沈伯洋]]. It connects inherited family and party resources to [[PoliticalFamilyResourceAdvantage]], distinguishes past victories from [[CrossCampElectoralAppeal]], and argues that unsuccessful attacks on recall politics and drug policy reveal the importance of [[CampaignDefinitionStrategy]]. Its closing judgment is that the contest may turn on [[SoftSupportTurnoutCompetition]] among voters who previously backed [[HuangShanshan|黄珊珊]].

## Key Claims
- [[JiangWanan|蒋万安]] entered politics with name recognition, media attention, and party access associated with the Chiang family and the network accumulated by his father [[JiangXiaoyan|蒋孝严]].
- The episode treats Chiang's legislative and 2022 mayoral victories as insufficient proof that he can attract voters beyond the [[Kuomintang|国民党]] baseline because both contests involved divided opposition or three-way competition.
- Chiang's campaign tried to define [[ShenBoyang|沈伯洋]] through social division, recall politics, and a twelve-year-old essay about drug policy, but the episode says these attacks did not make him defend the terrain the campaign selected.
- The drug-policy attack simplified a public-health question enough to provoke professional criticism and force Chiang to explain his own support for existing methadone and harm-reduction practice.
- Polling cited by the episode suggests that Shen converted previously uncommitted respondents into favorable views without materially expanding the share that disliked him.
- Voters who previously supported [[HuangShanshan|黄珊珊]] are presented as a pivotal but weakly attached bloc: Shen needs to raise their willingness to vote, while Chiang can benefit by lowering their enthusiasm even without winning them over.
