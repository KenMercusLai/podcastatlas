# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》708丨祭遵病逝，为何引发百官惊恐？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianwu 9 / 33 CE immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-709-mian-dui-bangjia-an-guren-weihe-buguan-renzhi-sihuo-luknor8yw839qwxogeyzwluo6qe2|Hanji 709]]. It centers on [[JiZunEasternHan|祭遵]] dying in camp: [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] mourns him intensely and keeps praising him in court until [[YaoQiEasternHan|姚期]] warns that the praise is making the living ministers feel judged by comparison.

The second half widens the same year into early Guangwu frontier pressure. [[GoguryeoLateHan|高句丽]] offers tribute and has its royal title restored, [[WeiXiaoEasternHan|隗嚣]] dies but his faction continues under Wei Chun with [[GongsunShuEasternHan|公孙述]] support, [[WuHanEasternHan|吴汉]] and [[DuMaoEasternHan|杜茂]] face unsuccessful northern fighting around [[MinKanEasternHan|闵堪]] and Jia Lan, [[LaiXiEasternHan|来歙]] plans a supply-backed Longxi/Tianshui offensive, and [[BanBiaoEasternHan|班彪]] frames Qiang unrest as a governance, language, and official-abuse problem.

## Key Claims

- Reward and merit allocation are framed as emotionally unstable because postwar followers can experience distribution as unfair even when no formula satisfies everyone.
- [[WenXuEasternHan|温序]]'s capture and suicide show Liu Xiu's side preserving a loyalty-martyr memory from the Wei Xiao war front before Ji Zun's death.
- [[JiZunEasternHan|Ji Zun]] is presented as an unusually clean, careful, disciplined commander who shares rewards with officers and soldiers, keeps troop behavior unobtrusive, follows Confucian selection standards, and asks for thin burial.
- Liu Xiu's repeated mourning praise for Ji Zun becomes [[ImperialPraiseComparisonPressure|imperial praise comparison pressure]] because living ministers hear the praise as an implicit judgment on their own service.
- [[YaoQiEasternHan|Yao Qi]] prevents the praise from becoming a court-management problem by telling Liu Xiu that ministers are ashamed and frightened; Liu Xiu accepts the warning and stops repeating the lament.
- Wei Xiao's death does not end the western war because Wang Yuan and Zhou Zong place Wei Chun in his position and Gongsun Shu continues military support.
- Lai Xi treats Longxi and Tianshui as the shield that lets Gongsun Shu keep resisting; his answer is to pair troops with stored grain and inducements for exhausted local soldiers and civilians.
- Xiongnu-backed pressure remains unresolved after Wu Han's unsuccessful attack, leading Liu Xiu to assign Zhu Hu, Wang Chang, Hou Jin, Wang Ba, and others to defensive posts.
- Ban Biao argues that Qiang unrest is rooted partly in official abuse, custom and language difference, lack of dependence, and poor communication, so the proposed remedy is restored frontier office, inspections, translators, and reconnaissance.

## Key Quotes

> "不公平的幻觉" - the opening frame for why reward allocation easily produces resentment.

> "怎样才能再得到像祭遵这样爱国奉公的人" - Liu Xiu's repeated court lament as summarized by the source.

> "羌人与汉人习俗、语言不同" - Ban Biao's frontier-governance diagnosis as summarized by the source.
