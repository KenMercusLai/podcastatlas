# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》324-2｜李广与卫青，两位名将的爱恨情仇

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the deployment prehistory of the [[MobeiBattleWesternHan|漠北之战]]. After field intelligence shows that the [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] chanyu remains north of the desert, [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]] and [[HuoQubing|霍去病]] exchange operational targets; Wei then refuses [[LiGuangHanGeneral|李广]]'s request to remain in the vanguard and sends him east with [[ZhaoShiqiWesternHan|赵食其]].

The reassignment combines command, imperial constraint, and patronage. The episode says Wei follows [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s private warning about Li Guang while also hoping that [[GongsunAoWesternHan|公孙敖]] can lead the vanguard, win merit, and recover rank. It then opens Wei's encounter with the chanyu: Xiongnu expects an exhausted Han army after the desert crossing, but Han logistics preserve combat condition, allowing Wei to form a wagon perimeter and commit cavalry before a dusk sandstorm obscures the field.

## Key Claims

- New intelligence places the chanyu's main force north of the desert and the Left Wise King and remnants of the right wing farther east, forcing Wei Qing and Huo Qubing to exchange operational targets without returning to court for new orders.
- Wei Qing pairs Li Guang with right general Zhao Shiqi on a longer eastern route with less water and grass, intended as a flanking movement rather than the direct attack Li Guang wants.
- Li Guang repeatedly asks to serve as vanguard because he regards a direct encounter with the chanyu as the culminating opportunity of his long anti-Xiongnu career.
- The episode says Wei Qing refuses under Han Wudi's private warning that Li Guang is old and unlucky, while also wanting his friend and former rescuer Gongsun Ao to win merit and regain a marquisate.
- Li Guang leaves without performing the expected courtesies after the order becomes final, showing open anger but not establishing that he disobeyed the deployment itself.
- Xiongnu expects the desert crossing to exhaust the Han force, yet the episode says strong logistics allow Wei's army to arrive in organized fighting condition.
- Wei Qing forms a defensive perimeter with armored wagons and sends five thousand cavalry against a reported opposing force of more than ten thousand before a dusk sandstorm disrupts visibility.

## Key Quotes

> "我从年少时就与匈奴作战。" - the episode's rendering of Li Guang's appeal for the vanguard.

> "严阵以待，杀气腾腾。" - the episode's description of the Han force after crossing the desert.
