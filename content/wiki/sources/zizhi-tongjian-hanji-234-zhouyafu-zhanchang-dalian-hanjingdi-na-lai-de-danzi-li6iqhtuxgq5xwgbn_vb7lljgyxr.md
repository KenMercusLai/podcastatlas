# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》234｜周亚夫战场打脸汉景帝，哪来的胆子？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode moves the [[RebellionOfTheSevenStatesWesternHan|Rebellion of the Seven States]] from the failed political sacrifice of Chao Cuo into the campaign against the Wu-Chu force. [[LiuBiWuKing|吴王刘濞]] rejects the court mission and presents himself as an eastern emperor, while [[YuanAngWesternHan|袁盎]] escapes confinement with help from a former beneficiary.

The military center is [[ZhouYafu|周亚夫]]. After agreeing with [[HanJingdi|汉景帝]] that Liang should absorb the coalition's pressure while the central army cuts its food route, Zhou follows [[ZhaoSheAdviserWesternHan|赵涉]]'s warning to avoid a likely ambush, declines both [[LiuWuLiangKingWesternHan|梁王刘武]]'s appeals and a later imperial relief order, fortifies rather than accepting battle, and waits for hunger and failed assaults to break the rebels. The episode therefore makes [[StrategicPlanProtectionUnderReliefPressure|strategic-plan protection under relief pressure]] the campaign's central command problem.

## Key Claims

- Killing Chao Cuo does not end the rebellion; 邓公 argues that Wu's preparations long predated the immediate territorial-reduction dispute and that the execution instead discourages loyal advisers.
- Liu Bi detains the imperial envoy Liu Tong, calls himself an eastern emperor, and rejects Mei Cheng's warning that continued advance will legitimize suppression and expose the coalition's rear and food route.
- Yuan Ang refuses to defect and escapes after a guard leader who remembers an earlier favor warns and assists him.
- Zhou Yafu proposes that Liang hold the Wu-Chu main force while the central army avoids immediate relief and attacks coalition logistics; Jingdi initially accepts the plan.
- Zhao She advises a southern detour through Lantian and Wuguan to avoid an ambush; later reconnaissance reportedly confirms the warning, and Zhou promotes him.
- Zhou continues the agreed campaign after Jingdi later orders immediate relief of Liang, while Han Tuidang's light cavalry cuts the coalition's supply route and Han Anguo and Zhang Yu defend Suiyang.
- Fortified refusal of battle, calm during a night panic, and reinforcement of the true northwest target after a southeast feint let Zhou outlast the final assaults before pursuing the starving coalition.

## Key Quotes

> “东帝” - Liu Bi's reported title after rejecting the court's effort to make him withdraw.

> “坚守不出” - the episode's compact description of Zhou's refusal to accept battle while the coalition's food shortage worsens.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. The episode adds the missing operational sequence before Hanji 236's military-outcome recap and agrees that Zhou wins through supply interdiction and refusal of an early decisive battle.
- Command qualification: the title's “打脸” framing overstates a simple commander-versus-emperor conflict. The episode itself says Jingdi had approved the logistics plan before later issuing the relief order, so Zhou's refusal is presented as protection of an agreed strategy under changed political pressure.
- Identity boundary: [[ZhaoSheAdviserWesternHan|赵涉]] is distinct from [[ZhaoShe|赵奢]], the Warring States Zhao general; the separate canonical route prevents a normalized-key collision.
