# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》991丨争夺献帝之战：谁能最终取胜？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the bloody flight segment before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-992-luanshi-you-duo-kepa-jiemi-changan-de-xuese-mengyan-ltuxzrb-1x5y2ctwo4drggxn5stz|Hanji 992]]. In December 195 CE, [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] is chased from Hongnong toward the Yellow River by [[ZhangJiLateHan|张济]], [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]], and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]], while [[DongChengLateHan|董承]], [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]], [[BaiboArmy|白波军]] commanders, and [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] forces try to keep the imperial party alive. Its main contribution is [[ProtectorCaptorAmbiguity|护驾与挟持模糊性]]: the emperor's recognized body remains politically valuable, but the pursuit, escort, river crossing, document loss, official deaths, and abandoned followers show how little institutional protection remains.

## Key Claims
- In December 195 CE, [[ZhangJiLateHan|张济]], [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]], and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]] continue pursuing [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] after he reaches Hongnong, defeating [[DongChengLateHan|董承]] and [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]] around Hongnong Dongjian.
- The battle destroys or scatters imperial objects, tallies, books, and archives, extending [[MingqiLegitimacy|名器合法性]] by showing that the emperor's title can survive while its material carriers are lost.
- A wounded loyal official rendered in the transcript as "菊菌" curses Li Jue's side for coercing the emperor and killing officials before being cut down, making loyal speech powerless against armed coercion.
- Dong Cheng and Yang Feng feign possible cooperation while secretly calling in [[LiLeLateHan|李乐]], [[HanXianLateHan|韩暹]], [[HuCaiLateHan|胡才]], and [[QubeiSouthernXiongnu|去卑]] for cavalry support.
- The outside reinforcements briefly defeat Li Jue's side and kill several thousand men, but the advantage does not hold; Li Jue attacks again as the imperial party moves east.
- The second defeat is worse than the earlier Hongnong Dongjian battle: senior officials die, guards are reduced to fewer than one hundred, and several high ministers are captured.
- [[JiaXu|贾诩]] stops Li Jue from killing the captured ministers, preserving lives without changing the underlying condition that soldiers can override court status.
- [[YangBiao|杨彪]] rejects Li Jue's proposal that the emperor travel down the Yellow River through dangerous water and instead organizes a secret night crossing.
- The river crossing turns court ritual into survival panic: [[FuShouEmpress|伏皇后]], Yang Biao, and only a small group cross with the emperor, while officials, palace women, and commoners left behind face plunder, humiliation, cold, and death.
- [[DongChengLateHan|董承]] uses quilts as a shield against possible arrows after Li Jue's scouts notice the crossing, and the party reaches Dayang before [[ZhangYangLateHan|张杨]] sends grain.
