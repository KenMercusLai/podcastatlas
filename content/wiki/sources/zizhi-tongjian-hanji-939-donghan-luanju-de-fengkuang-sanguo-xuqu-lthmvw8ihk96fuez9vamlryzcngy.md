# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》939丨东汉乱局的疯狂三国序曲

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 188 CE court, provincial, and frontier layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-940-de-minxin-zhe-de-tianxia-ni-xin-bu-xin-loipf7b0p5a-yh0rj2njnsml8a1j|Hanji 940]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-941-shenchu-guanchang-ta-jing-xiang-ganfan-huangdi-liu-fqiz9pi7u6cltrn-cjmjz-r|Hanji 941]]. It follows [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s amnesty and comet response, the renewed [[BaiboArmy|白波军]] rising under [[GuoDaLateHan|郭大]], [[ZhangYiLateHan|张懿]]'s killing in [[BingzhouLateHan|并州]], and [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]'s proposal to replace weaker inspectors with stronger provincial shepherds. The second half links [[DongFuLateHan|董扶]]'s [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] prophecy, [[ZhaoWeiLateHan|赵韪]]'s early attachment to Liu Yan, [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]]'s [[YouzhouLateHan|幽州]] appointment, [[QiangquChanyu|羌渠]]'s killing in the [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] revolt, [[YufuluoChanyu|于扶罗]]'s elevation, and [[CaoSong|曹嵩]]'s removal as taiwei.

## Key Claims

- In 188 CE, [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] issues a general amnesty, but the episode frames the act as a surface-level crisis response rather than a solution to late-Han disorder.
- A comet appearing in the Zi Gong star region is read as a political warning; Lingdi and the court respond through ritual apology and sacrifice under [[CelestialOmenPoliticalResponsibility|天象政治责任]].
- Yellow Turban remnants rise again at Xihe's Baibo Valley; [[GuoDaLateHan|郭大]] and the [[BaiboArmy|白波军]] attack Taiyuan and [[HedongCommandery|河东郡]], showing that earlier suppression has not restored local order.
- [[ZhangYiLateHan|张懿]], the inspector of [[BingzhouLateHan|并州]], is killed by the [[TugeHu|屠各胡]], making provincial office visibly unsafe before the later statewide office reform.
- [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]], then taichang, argues that weak provincial inspectors cannot control disorder and proposes stronger provincial shepherds, sharpening [[ProvincialShepherdAutonomy|州牧集权与地方割据]] before his later Yizhou autonomy.
- Liu Yan first wants the remote Jiaozhi post as a survival exit, but [[DongFuLateHan|董扶]] warns that the capital will fall into disorder and claims [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] has imperial qi, redirecting Liu Yan's request toward Yizhou.
- The source treats [[XiJianLateHan|郗俭 / 郤俭]]'s corruption in Yizhou as part of the office-failure background that makes Liu Yan's proposal and destination politically plausible.
- The court appoints [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]] as Yizhou shepherd, [[HuangWanLateHan|黄琬]] as Yu Province shepherd, and [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] as Youzhou shepherd.
- [[DongFuLateHan|董扶]] and [[ZhaoWeiLateHan|赵韪]] follow Liu Yan into Yizhou, making Liu Yan's move a small political entourage rather than a lone appointment.
- The court orders the [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] to support [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] against [[ZhangChunLateHan|张纯]]; [[QiangquChanyu|羌渠]] sends troops, but fear of repeated requisition contributes to a revolt.
- [[XiluoSouthernXiongnu|醯落]] and the [[TugeHu|屠各胡]] join forces, kill [[QiangquChanyu|羌渠]], and install his son [[YufuluoChanyu|于扶罗]] as chanyu, making the episode a source case for [[FrontierConscriptionBackfire|边疆征发反噬]].
- [[CaoSong|曹嵩]], [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s father, is removed as taiwei, giving Cao Song an earlier court-office layer before the later [[CaoSongMurder|曹嵩遇害]] branch.

## Key Quotes

> "益州分野有天子之气" - [[DongFuLateHan|董扶]]'s prophecy that redirects [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]] toward [[YizhouLateHan|益州]].

> "三国序曲" - the episode's frame for the 188 CE convergence of court weakness, provincial militarization, and frontier revolt.

> "持至尸逐侯单于" - the title assigned to [[YufuluoChanyu|于扶罗]] after the [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] revolt.
