# 《资治通鉴·周纪》11丨 世界风起云涌（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens the second volume of [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]]'s Zhou annals, covering the early years of [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]] from 368 BCE onward. It follows opportunistic war among [[QiState|齐国]], [[WeiState|魏国]], and [[ZhaoState|赵国]], then shifts to [[QinState|秦国]]'s mounting pressure on the Three Jin states. The episode's distinctive contribution is the link between [[ShimenBattle|石门之战]] and [[FufuRobe|黻服]], where ritual clothing becomes a political signal of Zhou recognition for Qin's military power.

## Key Claims
- [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]]'s reign opens the second Zhou-annals volume, covering 368-321 BCE across forty-eight years.
- In 368 BCE, [[QiState|齐国]] attacks [[WeiState|魏国]] and occupies Guanjing, while [[ZhaoState|赵国]] attacks Qi and takes a Qi-built wall or fortification line.
- The host reads Qi's attack on Wei as opportunism after the [[WeiState|魏国]] succession disorder that nearly let Han and Zhao destroy Wei.
- [[LiangHuiWang|魏罃 / 梁惠王]] is said to seek peace with Qi by giving official metal or funds, because consolidating internal rule mattered more than extending another external fight.
- Zhao's attack on Qi is interpreted as another opportunistic move, possibly because Zhao had failed to profit from Wei's succession crisis.
- In 367 BCE, [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] gives no entry for Zhou Xian Wang's second year, continuing the wiki's [[ChronicleSourceGaps|source-gap]] pattern.
- In 366 BCE, [[WeiState|魏国]] and [[HanState|韩国]] meet at Zaiyang, but [[QinState|秦国]] defeats their joint force at Luoyang Jinjiao.
- The host reads the Wei-Han meeting as a response to [[QinEastwardPressure|Qin eastward pressure]], because Qin's expansion forced former rivals into strained coordination.
- In 365 BCE, Wei attacks [[SongState|宋国]]; the host says [[Shiji|《史记》]] adds that Wei won and seized some Song territory.
- In 364 BCE, [[QinXianGong|秦献公]] wins the [[ShimenBattle|Battle of Shimen]] against the Three Jin coalition and is credited with killing sixty thousand enemy soldiers.
- [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]] gives Qin Xian Gong [[FufuRobe|黻服]], which the host reads as [[RitualRecognitionOfMilitaryPower|ritual recognition of military power]] rather than a decorative detail.
- The explanation of [[FufuRobe|黻服]] opens into [[TwelveInsigniaPoliticalSymbolism|twelve-insignia political symbolism]], with garment patterns functioning as rank, judgment, and legitimacy signs.
- In 363 BCE, the chronicle is again silent, but the host adds that Qin attacks Wei at Shaoliang and Zhao sends aid, suggesting the Qin-Wei conflict continues into the following year.

## Key Quotes
> "世界继续风起云涌，天翻地覆" - the episode's frame for Zhou Xian Wang's reign.

> "以求和为贵" - the host's summary of Wei Hui Wang's choice not to keep fighting Qi.

> "人口、战术和服装" - the three deeper themes the host says are hidden inside the Shimen notice.
