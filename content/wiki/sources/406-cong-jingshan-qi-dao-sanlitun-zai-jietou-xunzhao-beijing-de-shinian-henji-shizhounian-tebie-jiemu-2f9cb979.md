# #406 从景山骑到三里屯：在街头寻找北京的十年痕迹丨十周年特别节目

## Summary

This [[ShengdongJixi|声东击西]] tenth-anniversary episode has [[XuTao|徐涛]] and [[SongZhuangzhuang|宋壮壮]] cycle from Jingshan through Wanning Bridge, Gulou, Fangjia Hutong, Qianliang Hutong, the Liangma River, and Sanlitun to read a decade of change in [[Beijing]]. The route connects heritage return, road greening, tourism, storefront closure and recovery, river restoration, commercial rebuilding, and the capital's post-2016 planning direction. Its central judgment is that planning remains necessary, but [[AdaptiveUrbanPlanningUnderUncertainty|uncertain futures require humility, reversibility, and room for unplanned urban life]].

## Key Claims

- [[UrbanRouteFieldObservation]] makes large policy shifts legible through a traversable sequence of buildings, roads, shops, waterways, and everyday uses.
- Shouhuang Hall's transition from youth-palace use to restored heritage display illustrates a gain in conservation alongside a loss of informal civic access.
- [[LivingHeritageOperationalTradeoff]] is clearest at Wanning Bridge, where heritage protection, bus circulation, tourist safety, and ordinary movement require negotiated compromise rather than complete preservation by closure.
- Greening Ping'an Avenue and reconnecting the Liangma River show a move away from vehicle throughput as the only measure of street value toward walking, ecology, continuity, and public life.
- The closure of street-facing businesses in Fangjia Hutong reduced visible activity, but later small bars and restaurants reappeared nearby under tighter noise, exam-period, and closing-time constraints; [[UrbanGovernanceCommercialRecomposition]] therefore describes displacement and reconfiguration rather than simple elimination of demand.
- Gulou, Yonghegong Avenue, and Sanlitun show that tourism and commercial upgrading can standardize appearance while older subculture, neighborhood life, and new small venues persist unevenly.
- [[EcologicalPublicSpaceRenewal]] describes the Liangma River sequence of ecological repair, pedestrian reconnection, landscape construction, and later programming and commerce.
- The episode treats the 2016-era capital master-plan shift as a key organizing cause of subsequent spatial governance, while [[AdaptiveUrbanPlanningUnderUncertainty]] limits confidence in forecasts that cannot anticipate pandemics, AI, population change, or economic cycles.

## Key Quotes

> “封墙堵洞” - the episode's label for the 2017-era closure of many street-facing openings and businesses in hutong areas.

> “北京的布鲁克林” - a foreign-media comparison recalled for the earlier Gulou East Street subculture scene.

> “十年痕迹” - the episode's route-based frame for reading structural urban change through visible street evidence.
