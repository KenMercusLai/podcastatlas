# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》638丨血脉亲情，可以有多无情？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills a 10 CE [[WangMang|王莽]] and late-[[WesternHanDynasty|西汉]] institutional layer before the later Xin collapse notes. It uses [[BanGuEasternHan|班固]]'s comparison of Zhou, Qin, and Han royal-clan arrangements to explain why [[SameSurnameKingEnfeoffment|同姓王分封]] could once protect the dynasty but, after repeated削藩 and declining political capacity, could not stop Liu royal kin from submitting to Wang Mang.

The second half introduces the early form of [[WujunLiuguan|五均]] and 钱府 policy within [[WangMangReforms|王莽改制]]. The source presents price-setting, state purchase of unsold goods, market release during price spikes, and official lending as pro-livelihood design, but warns that officials who both set rules and operate markets can convert reform into rent extraction.

## Key Claims

- The episode frames kinship as politically unreliable when family feeling lacks institutional power and when self-preservation incentives dominate.
- Ban Gu's Zhou-Qin-Han comparison treats royal kin and vassal states as possible buffers for dynastic survival, but also as possible autonomy risks.
- Early Western Han's large same-surname kingdoms are presented as a response to Qin isolation and as a tool for guarding frontier and former-war regions.
- Wen, Jing, and Wu-era削藩 and the 推恩令 gradually turn Liu royal houses from substantial regional powers into title and tax-income holders with little political capacity.
- By Wang Mang's usurpation, many Liu royal descendants are distant from the throne, locally weak, and willing to hand over seals or praise the new regime.
- Wang Mang's 五均 and 钱府 policy claims to equalize wealth, steady prices, purchase unsold goods, release reserves when prices rise, and offer loans at a stated interest rate.
- The host's core policy warning is implementation capture: officials who set prices, verify costs, buy goods, sell reserves, and lend money can use that authority for private extraction.

## Key Quotes

> "天下熙熙皆为利来，天下攘攘皆为利往" - the episode's Sima Qian quotation for profit-seeking behavior.

> "既当运动员又当裁判" - the host's warning about officials who both regulate and participate in markets.

> "血脉亲情，可以有多无情" - the episode's title question, applied to Liu royal kin under Wang Mang.
