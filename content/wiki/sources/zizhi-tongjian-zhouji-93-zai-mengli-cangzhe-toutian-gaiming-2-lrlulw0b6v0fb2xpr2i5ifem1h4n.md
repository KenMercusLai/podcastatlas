# 《资治通鉴·周纪》93丨在梦里藏着偷天改命？（2）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-93-zai-mengli-cangzhe-toutian-gaiming-1-ljshdryvf9qgabtn-siontthzsok|Zhouji 93 part 1]] Shangdang decision. [[ZhaoBao|赵豹 / 平阳君]] warns that accepting [[Shangdang|上党]] means accepting a crisis that [[HanState|韩国]] has deliberately shifted onto [[ZhaoState|赵国]], but [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] follows [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]], receives the territory, and rewards [[FengTing|冯亭]] and the local officials.

The episode's distinctive addition is historiographical. It compares [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] with [[Shiji|《史记》]], arguing that the chronicle trims two meaningful [[Shiji|《史记》]] layers: Zhao Bao's fuller explanation of Qin's institutional advantages and Zhao Xiaocheng Wang's ominous dream. The dream discussion then widens into [[HistoricalDreamForeshadowing|梦兆叙事伏笔]], reading the repeated dreams in the Shiji Zhao lineage as literary and historical signs of later rise, disorder, and disaster.

## Key Claims
- [[ZhaoBao|赵豹 / 平阳君]] sees Shangdang's offer as a transfer of harm: Han and Shangdang avoid direct Qin submission by making Zhao the next object of [[QinState|秦国]] anger.
- His fuller argument in [[Shiji|《史记》]] gives Qin three practical advantages: cattle-assisted farming, water transport, and a reward mechanism that raises morale.
- The episode links those advantages to existing Qin capacity patterns such as [[FarmingWarStatecraft|耕战逻辑]], [[MilitaryMeritRankSystem|军功爵制]], and [[QinAdministrativeStandardization|秦国行政标准化]].
- [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] supports receiving Shangdang, and [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] sends him to take formal possession of the commandery.
- Zhao rewards [[FengTing|冯亭]] as Huayang Jun, gives him three large city grants, and broadly raises the rank of Shangdang's officials and commoners.
- Feng Ting's reaction remains source-variant and morally ambiguous: this episode says he weeps and refuses to present himself as a simple seller of his ruler's land, while [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] preserves a different return-to-Han version.
- [[Shiji|《史记》]] records Zhao Xiaocheng Wang's dream of mixed clothing, a flying dragon, falling midway, and gold-jade heaps shortly before the Shangdang offer; the episode treats it as ominous framing for the coming [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]].
- The host reads the repeated dreams in the Shiji Zhao lineage as a four-dream structure connecting [[ZhaoDun|赵盾]], [[ZhaoJianzi|赵简子]], [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]], and Zhao Xiaocheng Wang.
- [[SimaQian|司马迁]]'s judgment falls especially on [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]]: he can be an elegant noble but still "不识大体" when a territorial windfall blinds him to strategic consequence.

## Key Quotes
> "欲嫁其祸于赵也" - Zhao Bao's diagnosis of the Shangdang offer.

> "有气无食" - the dream interpretation's image of force without sustenance.

> "翩翩浊世之佳公子" / "不识大体" - Sima Qian's paired evaluation of Pingyuanjun.
