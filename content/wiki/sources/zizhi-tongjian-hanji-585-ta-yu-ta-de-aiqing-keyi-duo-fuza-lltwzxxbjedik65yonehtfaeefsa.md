# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》585丨他与他的爱情，可以多复杂？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the origin layer of [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]'s relationship with [[DongXian|董贤]] before the later Bao Xuan, Wang Jia, and Wang Mang sequence. It follows Dong Xian from Yuyang-born beautiful court attendant to favored郎官, 驸马都尉, 侍中, companion in bed and carriage, and beneficiary of extraordinary household, kinship, mansion, treasury, and funerary rewards.

The durable synthesis is that the "断袖" story is not only an anecdote about royal sexuality. The episode treats Aidi's affection as a political force that turns palace intimacy into office promotion, family advancement, ritual and architectural overreach, public-resource transfer, and lethal speech risk: [[ZhengChongWesternHan|郑崇]] is imprisoned after criticizing the overfavor, [[ZhaoChangWesternHan|赵昌]] weaponizes Aidi's anger through accusation, and [[SunBaoWesternHan|孙宝]] is removed after defending Zheng Chong.

## Key Claims

- Han Aidi's male favorites fit a broader Han imperial pattern, but the episode treats Dong Xian's favor as extreme in degree and public consequence.
- Dong Xian's rise begins with palace proximity: after being noticed during court communication, he is promoted and quickly becomes a highly visible near-body companion.
- The "断袖" anecdote records Aidi cutting his sleeve rather than waking Dong Xian, making intimacy part of Dong Xian's later political memory.
- Aidi extends favor from Dong Xian himself to his wife, sister, father, father-in-law, and younger in-law network, turning affection into family office and palace access.
- Dong Xian's mansion, treasure access, burial goods, and tomb complex cross ordinary status boundaries and make private favor visible in [[MingqiLegitimacy|public rank and ritual order]].
- Zheng Chong's correction shows the speech danger around Dong Xian: criticism of the favorite can be heard as an intrusion into the emperor's private relations.
- Zhao Chang's accusation and Sun Bao's failed defense show how personal resentment, ruler anger, and legal procedure can converge against a remonstrant.

## Key Quotes

> "断袖之癖" - the famous phrase attached to Aidi cutting his sleeve rather than waking Dong Xian.

> "门如市，心如水" - Zheng Chong's self-defense when Aidi challenges his crowded household visitors.
