# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》185｜一张利嘴，收复中国万里疆土

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the [[Nanyue|南越]] mission previewed in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-184-lishi-kongbupian-liubang-de-pengshi-roujiang-2-liebzrdrvo1okonpdjvrjatuwx9i|Hanji 184]] by tracing how [[ZhaoTuo|赵佗]] uses [[RenXiao|任嚣]]'s Lingnan handoff to build a southern polity and how [[LuJia|陆贾]] forces him into Han submission through identity pressure, military deterrence, and formal recognition. The episode then returns Lu Jia to [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s court, using [[Xinyu|《新语》]] to mark the move from conquest on horseback to civil governance, before closing with [[FanKuai|樊哙]]'s sickbed remonstrance and the coming [[YingBu|英布]] rebellion.

## Key Claims
- [[RenXiao|任嚣]]'s deathbed advice supplies the hinge between Qin's commandery order and [[ZhaoTuo|赵佗]]'s later [[Nanyue|南越]] regime: seal the Five Ridges routes, use Nanhai's distance and terrain, and wait out the collapse of the center.
- Zhao Tuo's takeover is active consolidation, not passive survival: after Ren Xiao dies, he blocks passes, removes Qin-appointed senior officials, installs trusted subordinates, absorbs [[GuilinCommandery|桂林郡]] and [[XiangCommandery|象郡]], and calls himself Nanyue Wuwang.
- [[LuJia|陆贾]]'s speech begins by denying Zhao Tuo a clean outsider identity: Zhao's kin, ancestral graves, and cultural origin remain in the Central Plains even though he has adopted southern dress and posture.
- Lu Jia pairs that identity argument with the hard power of [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s victory over [[XiangYu|项羽]], making Han recognition look like a dignified exit from a confrontation Zhao Tuo cannot win.
- The episode's Nanyue settlement exemplifies [[RecognitionBackedFrontierDiplomacy|册封威慑式边疆外交]]: title, seal, envoy rhetoric, family risk, and possible army movement combine to produce submission before war.
- Zhao Tuo's comparison questions about [[XiaoHe|萧何]], [[CaoShen|曹参]], [[HanXin|韩信]], and Liu Bang show that submission does not erase his self-regard; he accepts Han hierarchy while still imagining that he might have rivaled Liu Bang if he had stayed in the Central Plains.
- Lu Jia's return earns him appointment as taizhong dafu because the mission lets Han incorporate a large southern field without spending soldiers.
- The [[Xinyu|《新语》]] section repeats Lu Jia's core civil-order warning: a ruler may win the realm on horseback, but cannot govern it only through battlefield habits.
- The [[FanKuai|樊哙]] sickbed scene treats access to the ruler as a governance problem: when Liu Bang withdraws behind guards and eunuch attendance, Fan Kuai uses blunt loyal speech and the [[ZhaoGao|赵高]] precedent to pull him back toward ministers.

## Key Quotes
> "马上得天下" - Liu Bang's impatient answer to Lu Jia's classical talk.

> "马上治天下" - Lu Jia's counter-question about whether conquest methods can also govern.

> "南越没有可以交谈的人" - Zhao Tuo's explanation for why Lu Jia's visit matters to him.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction found. The source extends Hanji 184 by completing Lu Jia's mission rather than altering its setup.
- The source's "万里疆土" title is kept as episode rhetoric for large southern incorporation, not as a precise territorial measurement.
- Zhao Tuo's final submission is source-scoped to this early Han recognition moment; it should not erase later Han-Nanyue tensions or Zhao Tuo's continuing self-regard.
