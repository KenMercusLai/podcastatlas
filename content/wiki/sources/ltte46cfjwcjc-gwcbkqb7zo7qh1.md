# 066 大话卫斯理之《不死药》：永生是福还是祸？

## Summary

This [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] episode retells [[BuSiYaoNovel|《不死药》]], following [[WesleyNiKuang|卫斯理]] from a death-row escape and an identity reversal to an island whose plant-derived drug arrests aging, heals injuries, causes infertility, and produces cognitive collapse when supply stops. The hosts praise the early suspense and identity exchange while criticizing later character logic and the tsunami ending. Their wider discussion treats immortality as a conditional system of dependence, consent, unequal access, monopoly, loneliness, and political allocation rather than an uncomplicated personal benefit.

## Key Claims

- The prison break, pursuit, and revelation that 骆之谦 killed his brother and assumed his identity provide the novel's strongest suspense structure, while 卫斯理's later failures to search, restrain, or distrust him weaken causal credibility.
- The drug offers arrested aging, increased strength, and rapid healing but not invulnerability; the heart remains vulnerable, users become infertile, and interruption of a lifelong supply causes progressive loss of intelligence.
- 骆之谦's plan to kill the islanders and monopolize production turns bodily enhancement into control over a scarce, geographically concentrated dependency.
- [[ImmortalityTransformationConsent|Immortality-transformation consent]] depends on more than abstract willingness: duration of supply, disclosed side effects, reversibility, relationships, health, and decision-making under terminal illness all change whether a choice is usable.
- [[ImmortalityDependencyAndScarcity|Immortality dependency and scarcity]] can amplify wealth and political inequality because early access lets beneficiaries accumulate health, knowledge, capital, and control over later users.
- The hosts read the sudden tsunami that removes the island and drug supply as a mechanical closure, and the fourth-wall joke about the continuing protagonist as an explicit refusal of a fully resolved medical outcome.
- The episode connects [[NiKuang|倪匡]]'s speed and imaginative force to both narrative propulsion and rough logic; anecdotes about manuscripts, other authors, religion, smoking, and supernatural belief remain podcast retelling rather than independently verified biography.

## Key Quotes

> “人类普遍认为值得追求的事物，在真正得到之后未必会带来幸福。” - the episode's paraphrase of the novel's thematic premise.

> “永生是福还是祸？” - the question organizing the hosts' discussion of benefit, dependence, loneliness, and allocation.
