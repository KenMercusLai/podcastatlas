## Summary

[[ZhangJiaqiDiver|张家齐]] rejects a quick mother-daughter reset after describing absent support, achievement-centered recognition, parental access to her money, and repeated correction in place of listening. [[DaiLuwa|戴露娃]] describes physical punishment and emotional dismissal, while the episode also preserves her mother's demanding paid care work and financial support for acting-school preparation.

The comparison supports a qualified account rather than a simple bad-parent/good-child verdict.
