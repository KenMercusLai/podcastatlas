## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the pre-Hanji 776 and pre-Hanji 777 biography of [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|和熹邓皇后 / Deng Sui]] during [[EmperorHeOfHan|Emperor He of Han]]'s court. It traces Deng from childhood learning and family virtue through palace entry, self-effacing conduct toward [[EmpressYinHeLateHan|殷皇后]], refusal to let natal relatives linger in the palace, avoidance of rank and display competition, the illness-period threat against the Deng family, and her eventual appointment as empress.

The durable synthesis is that Deng's later regency capacity has a pre-regency formation layer: literacy, ritual deference, family-risk awareness, restraint of outer-relative rewards, and women-centered education all become legitimacy resources before the 106 CE accession crisis. The episode also supplies the immediate court-demand prelude to [[CourtDemandDrivenPapermaking|court-demand-driven papermaking]] by linking Deng's love of writing and painting to the search for a lighter writing material.

## Key Claims
- Deng is portrayed from childhood as unusually studious, with early reading of the classics and a family narrative that links Deng-line virtue to later good fortune.
- As a palace guiren, Deng practices visible deference: she lowers herself before Empress Yin, other consorts, palace servants, and rank markers such as dress color, seating, and standing position.
- Deng refuses prolonged palace residence for her mother and brothers during illness because she sees how natal-family access can create criticism of both ruler and consort.
- Empress Yin's jealousy becomes lethal politics when she reportedly threatens to destroy the Deng family if she gains power during Emperor He's illness.
- Deng's near-suicide and the maid Zhao Yu's false report of the emperor's recovery are source-scoped details showing how harem rivalry becomes family-survival danger.
- After Emperor He recovers, Empress Yin is deposed and Deng is appointed empress; Deng then refuses tribute to herself and blocks repeated attempts to raise Deng-family offices or titles.
- The episode presents Deng's later women-centered education work through royal and Deng-family schooling, North Palace women's study, and [[BanZhaoLateHan|Ban Zhao]]'s court teaching.
- The closing writing-material teaser anticipates Hanji 776's Cai Lun episode without directly naming the material or official in this source.

## Key Quotes
> "不爱红妆却爱诗书" - opening label for Deng's childhood and court identity.

> "绝不让邓家留下活口" - the reported threat that turns Empress Yin's jealousy into family-survival danger.

> "不要再向她进贡物品" - Deng's post-appointment order against personal tribute.
