# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》295｜豆腐的发明者，竟是造反的藩王？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the early public-exposure stage of [[LiuAn|淮南王刘安]]'s rebellion case. After [[LeiBeiWesternHan|雷被]] is prevented from leaving Huainan to seek military service, he appeals to the central court; investigators suspect a larger conspiracy, but [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] sends an envoy to inquire cautiously rather than arrest Liu An. The envoy's respectful manner disrupts crown prince [[LiuQianHuainanCrownPrinceWesternHan|刘迁]]'s alleged plan to kill him, and the court ultimately removes only two counties from Liu An's fief.

The limited punishment does not end the danger. The episode says Liu An interprets it as an unjust humiliation and intensifies preparation, while his brother [[LiuCiHengshanKingWesternHan|衡山王刘赐]] independently recruits retainers and prepares weapons before the formerly hostile brothers reconcile and agree to rise together. The durable synthesis is therefore a two-stage escalation: tactful signaling prevents an immediate ambush, but a grievance-centered ruler can treat calibrated punishment as another reason to continue a conspiracy.

## Key Claims

- Lei Bei's blocked attempt to seek central military service turns a small retainer dispute into a channel through which the court learns of a possible royal conspiracy.
- Court judicial officials reportedly favor arrest, but Han Wudi separates the provable obstruction of service from the not-yet-acted-upon rebellion allegation and sends an envoy to observe Liu An.
- Liu Qian allegedly proposes killing the envoy and rising if the mission proves punitive; the envoy's deference and narrow questioning induce Liu An to withdraw the ambush and troops.
- The court punishes Liu An by removing two counties while refusing to proceed on the rebellion allegation in this episode's account.
- Liu An treats the limited sanction as humiliation rather than an exit opportunity and expands his preparations.
- Liu Ci responds to the perceived danger around Liu An by recruiting retainers and preparing weapons, then meets Liu An and forms a secret alliance despite their earlier hostility.
- The Hengshan succession conflict already affects military preparation: Liu Ci confines [[LiuShuangHengshanCrownPrince|刘爽]], favors [[LiuXiaoHengshanPrince|刘孝]], and gives Liu Xiao authority to recruit and prepare matériel.

## Key Quotes

> “不矜细行，终累大德” - the maxim used by the host to frame how mistreatment of a minor retainer exposes a larger project.

> “关于造反这件事，任何人都不允许再提” - the episode's formulation of the court's decision to contain the unproved allegation after imposing a narrower penalty.

## Contradictions

- The episode repeatedly renders the Huainan crown prince as “刘谦,” the Hengshan king as “刘刺,” and the complainant as “雷备.” The surrounding identities and later notes align with the canonical 刘迁、刘赐、雷被; the wiki normalizes those links while preserving the transcript mismatch here.
- This episode says 徐来 wants 刘孝 made crown prince. Hanji 305-1 and 305-2 instead say she wants her own son 刘广 and supports Liu Xiao only tactically. The later, fuller household sequence qualifies this episode's compressed account.
- Hanji 304-2 emphasizes hostility and parallel recruitment between Liu An and Liu Ci. This episode adds a later reconciliation and secret alliance, so the accounts can describe successive stages rather than a settled contradiction.
- The headline repeats the popular attribution that Liu An invented tofu, but the supplied episode does not examine evidence for the claim. It is retained as title framing, not adopted as verified biography.
- The private ambush plan, envoy strategy and observations, judicial recommendations, exact legal basis, two-county penalty, intelligence network, troop preparations, forged seals, meeting dialogue, alliance, and motives remain episode-attributed pending comparison with received historical and legal texts.
