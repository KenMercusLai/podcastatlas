# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》891丨高段位的母亲，是家的定海神针

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens with a parenting frame: a mother under work, relationship, housework, and child-rearing pressure can make children insecure when anger becomes unpredictable household weather. The host argues that after scolding or hitting a child, the parent should apologize clearly, take responsibility, ask how the child experienced the event, and turn the incident into [[ParentalRepairAfterHarm|parental repair after harm]] rather than private regret.

The historical section backfills the 169 CE late-Han frontier layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-892-xiu-zhiyu-guren-youshenme-jiejing-lnobbkp6mj4xyb9jeuqsiuqogct|Hanji 892]]. It records Three Excellencies turnover under [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]], [[LiuXiaoLateHan|刘嚣]]'s promotion through eunuch flattery, and [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]]'s refusal of a surrender-first policy toward remaining [[DongqiangLateHan|东羌]] forces. Duan treats surrender as a possible provisioning trap, then uses [[TianYanLateHan|田晏]], [[XiaYuLateHan|夏育]], and [[ZhangKaiDuanJiongOfficer|张恺]] in a night-movement encirclement that the source presents as a Han victory and a Qiang catastrophe.

## Key Claims

- Children learn household safety from adult emotional predictability; repeated anger or action without warning trains them to watch faces and anticipate danger.
- Parent apology after hitting or scolding should name the parent's error, reject violence as a problem-solving method, and ask for the child's account once the child has calmed.
- "身教甚过严教" is the episode's parenting thesis: adult routines, sleep, energy, order, and self-management become the child's everyday model.
- A "high-level mother" is not defined as never making mistakes, but as someone who repairs mistakes and becomes a stable family anchor through health, optimism, and disciplined ordinary life.
- In 169 CE, [[WenrenXiLateHan|闻人袭]] and [[XuXunLateHan|许训]] are removed from high office, [[LiuChongLateHanMinister|刘宠]] moves from situ to taiwei, and Liu Xiao becomes sikong through favor-seeking toward palace eunuchs.
- [[FengChanLateHan|冯禅]] is sent to Hanyang to persuade remaining Qiang forces to surrender, but Duan Jiong argues that hunger, spring farm pressure, and state provisioning costs make the surrender path unreliable.
- Duan's campaign uses surprise cavalry, silent night movement with "衔枚", east-west pressure, and close encirclement before the source reports more than 19,000 Qiang killed.

## Key Quotes

> "解决问题不应该靠打骂" - parent-repair wording recommended by the host.

> "身教甚过严教" - the episode's condensed parenting thesis.

> "衔枚" - silent-march device used to prevent troops from speaking or exposing a night movement.
