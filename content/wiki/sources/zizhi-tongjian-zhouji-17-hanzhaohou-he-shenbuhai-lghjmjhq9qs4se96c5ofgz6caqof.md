# 《资治通鉴·周纪》17丨韩昭侯和申不害

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode covers 352-350 BCE under [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]], connecting [[QinState|秦国]]'s military pressure on [[WeiState|魏国]], Wei's reconciliation with [[ZhaoState|赵国]] and [[QiState|齐国]], and [[HanState|韩国]]'s internal strengthening under [[HanZhaohou|韩昭侯]] and [[ShenBuhai|申不害]]. Its Legalist branch is double: Shen Buhai supplies a ruler-technique lesson through [[MeritBasedRewardPunishment|因功赏罚]], while [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]]'s second reform round pushes [[ShangYangReforms|商鞅变法]] into [[Xianyang|咸阳]], household separation, counties, land boundaries, and measurement standards.

## Key Claims
- In 352 BCE, [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]] appears under the name 魏鞅, holds the Qin title 大梁造, and attacks Wei after the earlier Qin reform push.
- Qin's attack on [[Anyi|安邑]] and the contemporaneous siege of [[XianglingWarringStates|襄陵]] show [[WeiState|魏国]] under pressure from both Qin and a broader coalition field.
- In 351 BCE, Qin surrounds [[Guyang|固阳]], whose defenders surrender, while Wei returns [[Handan|邯郸]] to Zhao and forms the [[ZhangshuiAlliance|漳水之盟]].
- The episode uses [[ZhushuJinian|《竹书纪年》]] to add that [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王]] used Han forces to defeat a coalition at Xiangling, after which [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] sought peace with Wei through [[Jingshe|景舍]].
- The source reads Wei's reconciliation with Qi and Zhao as a response to [[QinEastwardPressure|秦国东进压力]], because Qin had crossed the Yellow River and taken or pressured Anyi and Guyang.
- The episode supplements [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] with [[Shiji|《史记》]]'s Zhao material, saying Qin also attacked Zhao's Lin area in the same year; the claim reinforces the shared Qin-threat background but remains source-specific.
- [[ShenBuhai|申不害]] is introduced as a low-status Zheng official with Huang-Lao and xingming learning who wins Han Zhaohou's trust and becomes Han's chief minister.
- The source says Shen Buhai's fifteen years in office make Han internally ordered and militarily strong, framing his value as administration and ruler technique rather than battlefield command.
- Han Zhaohou refuses Shen Buhai's request for an office for his cousin, arguing that accepting the private request would betray Shen Buhai's own doctrine of rewarding by merit.
- Han Zhaohou's worn-trousers story extends the same lesson: even small rewards should not be distributed by mood, because reward and punishment are the core instruments of management.
- In 350 BCE, Shang Yang begins a second reform round by moving Qin's capital from Liyang to [[Xianyang|咸阳]], which the source treats as the capital that remains through Qin unification and the Qin dynasty's fall.
- The second reform round includes [[HouseholdSeparationLaw|分居令]], which the episode interprets as separating adult men and in-laws into more proper domestic space rather than only breaking families apart for fiscal reasons.
- The reforms also include [[QinAdministrativeStandardization|秦国行政标准化]]: grouping settlements into counties with magistrates and deputies, abolishing older field boundaries, opening qianmo, redistributing land, and standardizing measures.
- A Qin-Wei meeting at [[TongWarringStates|同地]] likely marks a temporary accommodation rather than an end to rivalry; the episode notes that Shang Yang will die there twelve years later.
- [[ZhaoChenghou|赵成侯]] dies in the same 350 BCE cluster; [[GongziXie|公子泄]] loses a succession struggle, flees to Han, and the crown prince succeeds as [[ZhaoSuhou|赵肃侯]].

## Key Quotes
> "因功行赏" - Han Zhaohou's rule for refusing Shen Buhai's private request.

> "赏罚标准和执行都必须慎之又慎" - the episode's management reading of the worn-trousers story.

## Contradictions
- No direct contradiction found. The source extends the existing Shang Yang branch from first reform and prince-law cliffhanger into the second reform round.
- The source changes [[Anyi|安邑]]'s wiki role from a Wei capital besieged by Han and Zhao into a later Qin pressure point; this is sequence expansion, not conflict.
- The Qin-Wei peace at [[TongWarringStates|同地]] and the interpretation of [[HouseholdSeparationLaw|分居令]] are explicitly treated as the host's inference rather than bare chronicle fact.
