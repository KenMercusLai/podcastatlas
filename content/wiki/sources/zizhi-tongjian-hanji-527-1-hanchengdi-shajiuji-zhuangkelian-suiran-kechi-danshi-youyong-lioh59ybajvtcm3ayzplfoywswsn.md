# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》527-1丨汉成帝杀舅记：装可怜虽然可耻，但是有用！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] short episode backfills a late-[[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] outer-relative crisis around the Wang-family Five Marquises. It moves from [[WangShangWesternHan|王商]] wanting to use Mingguang Palace while ill, then cutting through Chang'an walls to bring water into an extravagant private landscape, to [[WangGenWesternHan|王根]] building a residence feature modeled on Weiyang Palace's White Tiger Hall.

The episode's durable synthesis is that Wang-family luxury becomes politically dangerous when it crosses status boundaries, city-defense space, and palace symbolism. Chengdi tries to discipline the maternal uncles through official accountability and the precedent of Emperor Wen forcing his uncle Bo Zhao to die, but Wang Shang, Wang Gen, and other Wang relatives convert the threat into a staged posture of awaiting execution, leaving the ruler's true intent unresolved.

## Key Claims

- The episode treats Wang-family luxury as a political transgression, not only as personal extravagance.
- Wang Shang's wish to use Mingguang Palace and his private water project blur the boundary between subject household, imperial palace, and public city wall.
- Wang Gen's imitation of the White Tiger Hall more directly touches imperial symbolic space, giving Chengdi a sharper trigger for anger.
- Chengdi responds through formal channels by rebuking monitoring officials, pressuring Wang Yin to punish the uncles, and searching for the Bo Zhao precedent.
- The Bo Zhao case functions as a warning that killing or forcing the death of an imperial uncle had precedent.
- The Wang-family response turns submission into strategy: crying to Wang Zhengjun, threatening mutilation, sitting on mats, and carrying execution implements make punishment politically harder.
- The episode leaves open whether Chengdi truly wanted to kill his uncles or only wanted to frighten them back inside the boundary.

## Key Quotes

> "皇帝杀舅舅并非没有先例" - the episode's reading of why Chengdi asks for the Bo Zhao precedent.

> "既狡猾又聪明" - the host's judgment on the Wang relatives' staged awaiting-punishment response.
