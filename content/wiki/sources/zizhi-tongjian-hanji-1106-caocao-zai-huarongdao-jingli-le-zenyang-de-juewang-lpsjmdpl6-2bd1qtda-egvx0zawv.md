# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1106丨曹操在华容道经历了怎样的绝望？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode reconstructs [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s retreat along [[HuarongRoadLateHan|华容道]] after [[RedCliffsBattle|赤壁之战]], then returns to the decisions that made the defeat possible. It combines Cao's rapid post-defeat defense around Jiangling and Xiangyang with a multi-causal account of the battle: northern troops' poor adaptation to river warfare, epidemic disease, unfamiliar terrain, linked ships, [[HuangGai|黄盖]]'s feigned surrender, and [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]]'s fire-attack plan.

The episode's main interpretation is [[PostSuccessArrogance|得势后的骄傲]]. After Guandu, the Wuhuan campaign, Liu Cong's surrender, and Liu Bei's Changban defeat, Cao Cao treats possession of Jingzhou and numerical advantage as near-certainty, rejects [[JiaXu|贾诩]]'s consolidation-first advice, and chooses an immediate high-risk campaign against Jiangdong before the Sun-Liu alliance can strengthen.

## Key Claims
- The episode depicts the Huarong retreat as a compounded mobility crisis in rain and mud, with horses, wounded soldiers, disease, and fear of pursuit slowing the column.
- Its most brutal detail says Cao Cao has reeds laid over the marsh and uses weak, wounded, and sick soldiers to make a passable route for mounted troops; the episode presents this as a desperate preservation of the military core.
- After reaching Jiangling, Cao Cao shifts from rapid unification to defense, sending his main mobile force north while assigning commanders to Jiangling and Xiangyang and preparing to deny stored supplies if a position falls.
- Jia Xu recommends pacifying and resting Jingzhou before another campaign, but Cao Cao fears that delay will let Sun Quan and Liu Bei consolidate an alliance.
- Northern soldiers' seasickness, weak river-warfare experience, epidemic illness, and the marshy Wulin position reduce the practical value of Cao Cao's larger nominal force.
- Linking ships stabilizes movement for soldiers and horses but concentrates the fleet into a shared fire vulnerability.
- Liu Bei and Zhou Yu cooperate against Cao Cao while preserving boundaries over forces, access, and the postwar political prize.
- Huang Gai identifies the linked-fleet vulnerability, prepares ten fire ships, and sends a false-surrender message that Cao Cao accepts before the attack developed in Hanji 1107.

## Key Quotes
> "确定性的幻觉" - the episode's phrase for turning recent victories and nominal advantages into confidence that the next outcome is already secured.

> "天命所归" - the source's characterization of Cao Cao's self-understanding after successive victories.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction found. The episode supplies the operational and decision prelude to Hanji 1107's fire attack and retreat account.
- Causal qualification: the source supports a combined explanation involving disease, river-war adaptation, terrain, ship configuration, deception, fire, and command judgment; none should be treated as the sole cause from this evidence.
- Source-scope caution: the exact post-retreat force counts, private dialogue with Xu Chu, riverside reflection, psychological burden, reed-road sequence, and use of weak soldiers as road material are dramatic or quantitative details that require broader source verification.
- Jia Xu's counterfactual that rest would have made Jiangdong submit, and the claim that pride explains Cao Cao's acceptance of Huang Gai's surrender, remain interpretive rather than independently demonstrated.
