# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》897丨职场必读！什么才叫领导力？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 170 CE late-Han layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-898-renzei-zuofu-kelian-zhiren-neng-you-duo-huangtang-lgfup6tyv85zhvnmzf6xlesmblcj|Hanji 898]]. It opens with a source-scoped warning that "connections" do not help people without [[ExchangeableCapability|可交换能力]], then turns to [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]] as a field-command case of [[MilitaryMoraleThroughSharedHardship|同甘共苦式士气]].

The second half contrasts usable leadership with access-manufactured officeholding. [[DongChongLateHan|董宠]] dies after falsely invoking [[EmpressDowagerDongLateHan|董太后]], [[GuYongLateHan|谷永]] pacifies a frontier population through trust and administrative incorporation, while [[MengTuoLateHan|孟陀]] turns a staged kneeling by [[ZhangRangLateHan|张让]]'s household servant into [[ManufacturedAccessBrokerage|假势通道套利]] and then fails at [[ShuleKingdom|疏勒]] after becoming [[LiangzhouLateHan|凉州]] inspector without matching command capacity.

## Key Claims

- The host argues that people who seek sudden wealth or panic under pressure are especially vulnerable to fake "relationship" opportunities.
- The opening workplace lesson says relationships and patrons are useful only when backed by visible value, skill, judgment, or other [[ExchangeableCapability|可交换能力]].
- The source places the chronicle setting around 170 CE, while the transcript also says "建宁二年"; that year-label tension is preserved as source-scoped chronology drift.
- A solar eclipse is followed by the recall of [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]] from long frontier service and his appointment as shizhong.
- Duan's credibility comes from fighting beside ordinary soldiers for years, sharing danger and hardship rather than only issuing orders from rank.
- The host reads Duan's case as "real leadership": a team follows when the leader shares risk and burden, not merely because the leader holds a title.
- The court removes and appoints several Three Excellencies-level officials, including the later appointment of [[QiaoXuanLateHan|桥玄]] as sikong.
- [[DongChongLateHan|董宠]], an imperial relative serving as zhijinwu, is imprisoned and killed after falsely claiming instructions from [[EmpressDowagerDongLateHan|董太后]] for private requests.
- [[GuYongLateHan|谷永]] uses trust and grace to induce a large frontier population to submit, after which the court grants civilizing status markers and establishes seven counties.
- The source's "官带" wording is normalized as "冠带" in the source note because the passage is about hat-and-belt markers of incorporation.
- [[MengTuoLateHan|孟陀]] first bribes a servant in Zhang Rang's household, then asks only for the servant to kneel to him publicly at Zhang Rang's gate.
- The kneeling scene makes outside gift-seekers believe Meng Tuo has privileged access to [[ZhangRangLateHan|张让]], and their gifts let Meng Tuo send larger gifts onward to Zhang.
- The host treats this as "借鸡生蛋": Meng Tuo manufactures a relationship signal, converts others' resources into real access, and finally becomes Liangzhou inspector.
- Meng Tuo's failed attack on [[ShuleKingdom|疏勒]] is read as the consequence of office obtained through access theater rather than military competence.

## Key Quotes

> "一个没有价值的人，拥有再多人脉也是无用的。" - opening workplace warning about relationships without capability.

> "和团队同呼吸、共命运。" - the host's summary of Duan Jiong's leadership mechanism.

> "借鸡生蛋。" - the host's label for Meng Tuo's staged-access brokerage.
