# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》383-2｜丞相公孙贺是如何被儿子坑死的？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] short episode supplies the immediate causal chain behind [[GongsunHeWesternHan|公孙贺]]'s destruction before the broader support-network reading in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-384-1-fanlian-wuqing-hanwudi-cuihui-weiqing-jiazu-lp-rwqpmccvlaochibspvikhb96z|Hanji 384-1]]. It follows [[GongsunJingshengWesternHan|公孙敬声]] from high office as taipu into embezzlement of North Army funds, then follows Gongsun He's attempt to rescue him by capturing the wanted outlaw [[ZhuAnshiWesternHan|朱安世]].

The durable synthesis is that the Gongsun case enters the [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]] through an incentive chain rather than a single accusation. Gongsun He tries to trade law-enforcement success for his son's pardon; Zhu Anshi retaliates by accusing Gongsun Jingsheng of adultery with Yangshi Princess and wugu cursing against [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]; Wudi's illness and fear of curse harm make the charge lethal; and [[JiangChongWesternHan|江充]]'s investigation turns the case into prison deaths and clan extermination.

## Key Claims

- Gongsun He's apparent political safety in 92 BCE collapses through his son's conduct rather than through a direct initial attack on the chancellor himself.
- Gongsun Jingsheng's embezzlement of a large North Army military fund is framed as a grave military-political offense that requires a response to preserve army trust.
- Gongsun He tries to save his son by offering Han Wudi a different prize: the capture of Zhu Anshi, a long-wanted outlaw whom officials had failed to arrest.
- The arrest tactic creates retaliation risk: Gongsun He pressures Zhu Anshi's relatives, prompting Zhu to surrender and then strike back through accusation.
- Zhu Anshi's accusation is politically fatal because it combines sexual scandal involving Yangshi Princess with wugu cursing against the old and ill Han Wudi.
- The episode says investigators find a wooden figure on an imperial road, giving the accusation apparent material support before Jiang Chong takes over the case.
- Gongsun He and his son die in prison, their clan is exterminated, and the case becomes a prelude to the later Wugu catastrophe.

## Key Quotes

> "丞相祸及宗矣" - Zhu Anshi's reported warning that Gongsun He has pulled disaster onto his own lineage.

> "在皇帝的专用道路上埋木偶" - the accusation detail that turns the case from corruption into alleged curse harm against Han Wudi.
