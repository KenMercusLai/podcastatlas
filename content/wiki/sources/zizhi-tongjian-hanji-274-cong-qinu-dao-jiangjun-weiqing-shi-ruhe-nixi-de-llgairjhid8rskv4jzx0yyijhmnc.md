# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》274｜从骑奴到将军，卫青是如何逆袭的？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]]'s low-status childhood, the opportunity created by [[EmpressWeiZifuWesternHan|卫子夫]]'s entry into the palace, and his first command in the 129 BCE four-route expedition against [[Xiongnu|匈奴]]. It places that appointment inside [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s costly cavalry build and a deliberate but intelligence-poor attempt to test Han mounted forces across a broad frontier.

The episode stops before the battle at Longcheng. Its durable contribution is therefore the pre-battle synthesis: kinship opened access, but the host also attributes Wudi's trust to Wei Qing's riding background, composure, judgment, and persistence; the source does not independently establish the private speeches, route details, or later battle result.

## Key Claims

- Wei Qing is presented as a poor, low-status dependent who considered avoiding abuse a more realistic hope than the prediction that he would become a marquis.
- Wei Zifu's rise brings Wei Qing into Jianzhang Palace and gives him access to Han Wudi and the developing imperial cavalry establishment.
- The episode says Wudi had spent heavily building mounted forces capable of answering Xiongnu mobility and wanted trusted people to command them.
- In 129 BCE, renewed Xiongnu raiding in Shanggu and the readiness of the new cavalry force lead Wudi to launch a first large active cross-border test.
- Wei Qing, [[GongsunAoWesternHan|公孙敖]], [[GongsunHeWesternHan|公孙贺]], and [[LiGuangHanGeneral|李广]] each receive ten thousand cavalry and advance from separate frontier sectors.
- Court criticism focuses on giving the untested Wei Qing a general's command while his sister is favored; the host answers that kinship created opportunity but Wudi also judged Wei steady, modest, unconventional, and determined.
- With poor knowledge of terrain and Xiongnu movements, Wei Qing reportedly continues deep into the grassland, fails to find the main force, and reaches Longcheng; the episode ends before narrating combat or outcome.

## Key Quotes

> "为人奴，得无笞骂即足矣，安得封侯事乎。" - the episode's rendering of Wei Qing's response to a prediction of future ennoblement.

> "用我必胜。" - the host's compact pre-battle pledge attributed to Wei Qing; exact wording and historicity require primary-text comparison.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with the current wiki was found. This source supplies an earlier origin and first-command layer without changing the later Gaoque, Dingxiang, or Mobei sequence.
- Sequence boundary: the episode ends at the discovery of Longcheng and does not establish the battle's conduct or result; those claims should not be inferred from its cliffhanger.
- Appointment boundary: Wei Zifu's favor clearly supplies access in the episode, but the host also attributes Wudi's choice to Wei Qing's observed qualities. Neither pure nepotism nor a purely meritocratic selection is established.
- Source-scope caution: the physiognomy story, Wudi's response to ministers, personality judgments, pledge, exact force totals and routes, absence of guides, distance marched, discovery mechanics, and characterization of the operation as Han's first active cross-border cavalry attack require comparison with primary records.
- Infrastructure boundary: the canal and transport discussion places fiscal and logistical capacity beside the campaign, but this short episode does not prove a direct project-to-operation supply chain.
