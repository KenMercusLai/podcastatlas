# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》446｜以道德绑架审案，韩延寿是不是好官？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills [[HanYanshouWesternHan|韩延寿]] before the later death-case notes. It credits him with sincere, effective local governance, but argues that his "closed-door self-reproach" style of dispute handling can slide from moral education into [[MoralPressureAdjudication|道德压力式审案]] when official posture replaces fact-finding, law, and normal complaint channels.

The second half supplies Han Yanshou's entry into office: his father is killed after opposing Yan king Liu Dan's rebellion, and Huo Guang later promotes Han Yanshou as part of court compensation for that loyal death. The closing Xiongnu notice gives an upstream layer for [[XiongnuSuccessionFragmentation|匈奴式继承碎裂]]: after Woyanqudi Chanyu seizes power, kills or removes opponents, and appoints his own son and brother, resentment and non-recognition spread until the Aoxian branch effectively leaves chanyu control.

## Key Claims

- Han Yanshou is treated as a genuinely effective Western Han official, not simply as a hypocrite or bad official.
- His dispute-handling method carries a procedural danger: moral pressure from a senior official can suppress factual inquiry and make complainants look socially deviant.
- The episode insists that disputes should be resolved by facts and law, while moral education and legal restraint cannot replace one another.
- Han Yanshou's official path begins after his father is killed for opposing Yan king Liu Dan's rebellion and Huo Guang promotes him as a capable son of a loyal victim.
- Han Yanshou's later offices include Jianyi Dafu, Huayang taishou, and Yingchuan taishou before the later Hanji 450/451 death-case arc.
- Woyanqudi Chanyu's seizure of power and harsh purge create internal Xiongnu resentment rather than stable authority.
- The Aoxian nobles' refusal to accept the chanyu's installed son shows succession fragmentation starting from coercive central appointment before the later five-chanyu crisis.

## Key Quotes

> "没有法治的德治是伪命题" - the episode's compressed judgment on morality without legal protection.

> "法治与德治不可能偏废任何一方" - the source's balance formula after criticizing moral coercion.
