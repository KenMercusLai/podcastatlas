# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》524丨为什么有些老板喜欢让高管内斗？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Hongjia 1 / 20 BCE under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]], immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-525-hanchengdi-weifu-sifang-weisha-zao-tianqian-lvtzpbemhvz31da6b2zgdws-khpw|Hanji 525]]. It starts from the aftermath of [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]]'s death: Chengdi appoints [[WangYinWesternHan|王音]] Da Sima Chariot-and-Cavalry General, gives [[WangTanWesternHan|王谭]] special-entry status, and leaves the two Wang-family figures resentful of each other.

The durable synthesis is that late Western Han personnel politics combines constrained balancing with practical capacity tests. Chengdi can only try to split and check a powerful outer-relative field, while the episode's positive countercase is [[DuanHuizongWesternHan|段会宗]]: an official whose value lies less in spectacular fame than in stabilizing [[WesternRegionsProtectorate|Western Regions]] risk before it becomes visible disaster.

## Key Claims

- After Wang Feng dies, Chengdi promotes Wang Yin and honors Wang Tan without giving Wang Tan the highest military office, creating resentment inside the Wang-family power field.
- [[GuYongWesternHan|谷永]] advises Wang Tan to step back from city-gate security authority, and the episode reads Chengdi's personnel arrangement as a constrained attempt to balance outer relatives rather than a clean recovery of imperial power.
- [[YuYongWesternHan|于永]]'s rise is framed through family prestige, later self-cultivation, filial reputation, and marriage into the imperial family, but he dies just before an expected chancellorship.
- [[WangJunWesternHan|王骏]]'s Jingzhao appointment draws on the reputation of his father [[WangJiWesternHan|王吉]], whose earlier remonstrance to Liu He helped distinguish him from officials punished after Liu He's deposition.
- [[DuanHuizongWesternHan|段会宗]] is presented as a Western Han official and diplomat whose worth comes from taking difficult Western Regions posts that many officials would avoid.
- The host treats Duan's quiet first term as success: no major incident can signal effective preventive governance when the office's job is to keep the border calm.
- Gu Yong's later recommendation of [[XueXuanWesternHan|薛宣]] argues that high office should be filled by looking beyond reputation to actual administrative effect and problem-solving capacity.

## Key Quotes

> "善战者无赫赫之功，善医者治未病而无名" - the episode's frame for Duan Huizong's quiet Western Regions success.

> "不负所学，济世安民" - the host's contrast between careerist office-seeking and Duan's public-service orientation.
