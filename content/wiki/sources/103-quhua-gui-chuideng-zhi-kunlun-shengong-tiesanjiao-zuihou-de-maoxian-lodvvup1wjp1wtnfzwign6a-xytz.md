# 103 趣话《鬼吹灯》之昆仑神宫：铁三角最后的冒险

## Summary

This [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] episode supplies the opening movement of [[KunlunShrineGhostBlowsOutTheLight|《昆仑神宫》]]: after obtaining the 雮尘珠 without knowing how to use it, 胡八一、Shirley 杨 and 王胖子 join 明叔's expedition toward the Tibetan 魔国遗迹 and the ice-buried nine-story demon tower. The hosts use corpse collecting, 湘西赶尸, 摸金门派, geomancy, divination, 天授唱诗人, 中阴身 and glacier hazards to ask how [[GhostBlowsOutTheLight|《鬼吹灯》]] combines real places, historical materials, religious vocabulary and folklore with invented knowledge. The episode ends after 阿东 and 铁棒喇嘛 are removed from the expedition, wolves pursue the group into the glacier, and 韩淑娜 disappears just as the demon tower is located.

## Key Claims

- The 雮尘珠 functions as both unresolved curse remedy and narrative bridge: the protagonists possess the object but must decode the 龙骨天书 and seek the 魔国 site before it can become useful.
- 明叔's search for an ice-glacier crystal corpse joins commercial interest in preserved bodies to the expedition's real objective, while the hosts use Xinjiang mummies and European mummy commerce as context rather than proof of the novel's specific claims.
- [[HistoricalFantasySourceLayering|Historical fantasy source layering]] gives the story a “possibly true” texture by placing invented devices such as the thirteen-whiskered porcelain cat and 背尸 trade beside real corpse-preservation history, Tibetan geography, archaeology, religious terms and circulating folklore.
- The 摸金、发丘、搬山 and 卸岭 branches show a world expanding through named lineages, signature techniques and representative characters; the hosts suspect some connections were added or revised as the series developed.
- The 山风蛊 divination does not mechanically force the journey. It gives 胡八一 psychological confirmation for a westward choice and lets chance, interpretation, agency and fatalism coexist inside the plot.
- The expedition is assembled as a complementary adventure party: combat, archaeology, geomancy, Tibetan cultural knowledge and supernatural perception are distributed across nine people, while 阿东's death and 铁棒喇嘛's poisoning begin reducing that group before the glacier.
- The 铁三角's relationship is already moving toward post-adventure life: Shirley 杨 asks 胡八一 to stop tomb raiding and move to the United States after the curse is lifted, and he no longer rejects that future outright.

## Key Quotes
