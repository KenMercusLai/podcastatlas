# 咖啡小课堂：口粮豆怎么买最划算？一份给普通人的咖啡避坑指南

## Summary
In the first “咖啡小课堂” installment for [[DaxiaoDianbo|大小电波]], [[JenniferCoffee|珍妮花]] of [[HeyCoffee]] offers an entry-level guide to buying coffee goods, judging cafes, and choosing home equipment. The episode's central contribution is [[CoffeeConsumerJudgment|coffee consumer judgment]]: treat roasted coffee as a perishable ingredient, separate product evidence from luxury storytelling, and match purchases to budget, usage, serviceability, and sensory ability. Its [[BachaCafe]] example makes the broader point that price, packaging, and brand narrative do not by themselves establish freshness or cup quality.

## Key Claims
- Roasted [[Coffee|coffee]] should be judged as a perishable ingredient: roast date, exposure after opening, and realistic monthly consumption matter more than the printed shelf-life alone.
- Whole beans usually preserve aroma better than pre-ground coffee, while individually sealed drip bags may be preferable to one large bag of grounds when convenience is required.
- Origin, subregion or estate, altitude, variety, processing method, and flavor description can make a package more legible, but labels are decision aids rather than proof that the cup will taste good.
- The speaker proposes a “middle rule” for inexperienced buyers: avoid both the cheapest and most expensive tier when evidence is limited, while recognizing that her per-gram price bands are personal heuristics rather than market standards.
- For routine cafe use, proximity and chain-level consistency may matter most; for exploratory specialty visits, the speaker suggests examining equipment, menu structure, staff explanations, cleanliness, milk handling, and cup size.
- Home-machine purchases should begin with budget, maintenance, after-sales support, product maturity, and willingness to clean or operate manually; a grinder is treated as essential when brewing from whole beans.
- [[BachaCafe]] is used as a cautionary case in which luxury retail design, historical storytelling, high prices, and long printed shelf life did not persuade the speaker that the sampled coffee was fresh.

## Key Quotes
> “最佳赏味期” - the episode's preferred frame for coffee freshness rather than relying only on shelf life.

> “中间法则” - the speaker's heuristic for avoiding both the cheapest and most expensive option when expertise is limited.

> “地板” - the speaker's description of Americano and latte as baseline tests of a cafe.
