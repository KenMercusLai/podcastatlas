# vol.57 对谈李荣浩：该放下就放下 该不要就不要

## Summary
This [[TianzhenButianzhen|天真不天真]] conversation has [[YangTianzhen|杨天真 / 天真]] interview [[LiRonghao|李荣浩]] about concrete lived experience, songwriting, arrangement, mixing, long skill practice, delayed audience reception, concerts, solitude, and AI music. Its durable synthesis joins [[BeliefGroundedStorytelling]], [[RelationalMusicProduction]], and [[CreativeFocusBySubtraction]]: credible expression begins from material the creator believes, finished sound emerges through restrained management of relationships among parts, and sustained depth may require refusing projects that do not justify their attention cost. The episode also extends [[LiveMusicHumanConnection]] and [[GenerativeAIMusic]] while keeping artistic forecasts, bodily anecdotes, and market judgments source-scoped.

## Key Claims
- [[LiRonghao|李荣浩]] says songs gain credibility when they arise from specific experiences, observations, and details the creator can believe rather than from an abstract demand to manufacture inspiration.
- [[RelationalMusicProduction]] treats arrangement and mixing as forms of restraint: instruments, frequencies, distance, brightness, force, consonants, and playback devices must be balanced so no part is either swallowed or unnaturally detached.
- Long mastery remains granular after professional success; Li describes continued guitar practice, technical study, plugin testing, fourteen mix versions for one song, and adjustments as small as 0.1 decibel.
- [[CreativeFocusBySubtraction]] links age and finite energy to deliberate refusal: he has reduced television and gala appearances so albums, mixing, concerts, and a few intrinsically absorbing activities receive deeper attention.
- Market response has a longer clock than release dashboards suggest. Songs including 《爸爸妈妈》 and 《戒烟》 reportedly grew well after release, while personal confidence in 《脱胎换骨》 persists despite weaker current attention.
- [[LiveMusicHumanConnection]] appears as a creative loop rather than playback alone: a song moves from private composition through release into collective recognition and audience singing, while performers exchange goodwill with the room.
- The episode's [[GenerativeAIMusic]] position is qualified rather than categorical: AI already produces fast, coherent functional demos and may pressure routine production work, but current output still leaves questions about stems, durable value, personal experience, motive, and productive imperfection.

## Key Quotes
> “该不要就不要” - the episode's compact rule for releasing low-value commitments and memories.

> “唱歌不是给音乐人发明的” - Li Ronghao's boundary against treating ordinary emotional singing as a professional accuracy test.

> “一罐质量很高的罐头” - his metaphor for efficient, polished, but presently standardized AI music.
