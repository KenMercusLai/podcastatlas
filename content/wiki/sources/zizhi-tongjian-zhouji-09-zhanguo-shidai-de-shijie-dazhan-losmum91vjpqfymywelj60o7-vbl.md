# 《资治通鉴·周纪》09丨战国时代的世界大战

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] [[ZhouAnWang|周安王]] chronology through years 22-26. It turns a terse cluster of events into an [[EarlyWarringStatesInterstateWar|early Warring States interstate war]] story, then uses [[Zisi|子思]]'s advice in [[WeiState|魏国]] to connect battlefield disorder with questions of talent use, remonstrance, and court feedback.

## Key Claims
- Around 380-379 BCE, the source frames [[QiState|齐国]]'s attack on [[YanState|燕国]], the Han-Zhao-Wei rescue of Yan, Zhao's failed attack on Wei, Qi's intervention, and [[ChuState|楚国]]'s opportunistic attack on Wei as a short but broad [[EarlyWarringStatesInterstateWar|interstate war]] in which nobody clearly profits.
- The episode stresses that early Warring States alliances are unstable: rescue, retaliation, and opportunistic expansion can be happening at the same time.
- The source says [[QiKangGong|齐康公]]'s death ends Jiang-line Qi, while the Tian family continues through the source's [[TianQiHuanGong|Tian-family Qi Huan Gong]] and [[TianYingqi|田英齐]].
- It also records the death of [[JinXiaogong|晋孝公]], accession of [[JinJinggong|晋静公]], and final deposition of Jin Jing Gong by Han, Zhao, and Wei in 376 BCE, making [[JinState|晋国]]'s extinction the other old-order endpoint beside Jiang Qi's fall.
- [[ZhongshanState|中山国]] revives after Di forces drive out [[WeiZhi|魏挚]], then still maintains relations with Wei and joins Wei against Zhao.
- [[ZhouAnWang|周安王]] dies in 376 BCE and is succeeded by [[ZhouLiewang|周烈王]], closing this stretch of the Zhou annals.
- The second half focuses on [[Zisi|子思]]: his recommendation of [[GouBian|苟变]] becomes a case for [[UseStrengthsOverFaults|using strengths over faults]] rather than discarding a military talent over a small prior blemish.
- Zisi's criticism of ruler-minister flattery becomes [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|court feedback collapse]]: if rulers and officials all assume they are right and no one corrects them, the state loses access to useful judgment.
- The source says [[SimaGuang|司马光]] includes Zisi's political discussions because they have direct "资治" value rather than because they advance the war chronology.

## Key Quotes
> "谁都没有捞到便宜" - the episode's verdict on the broad early Warring States melee.

> "两个鸡蛋" - the small blemish Zisi says should not outweigh Gou Bian's military usefulness.

> "俱曰予圣，谁知乌之雌雄" - the [[Shijing|《诗经》]] line Zisi uses to criticize universal self-satisfaction.

## Contradictions
- No direct contradiction found. The source extends the prior Zhouji branch from Wu Qi's reform politics into a broader interstate-war and old-order dissolution frame.
- The episode's Tian-family "齐桓公" is kept as [[TianQiHuanGong|Tian-family Qi Huan Gong]] so it is not conflated with the older [[QiHuanGong|齐桓公]] page from the Spring-Autumn branch.
- The transcript contains minor name instability around Zhao's ruler in the war narration; this ingest records the war primarily through [[ZhaoState|赵国]] rather than creating a new ruler page from a likely unstable transcription.
