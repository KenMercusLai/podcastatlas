# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》181｜揭秘刘邦废立太子的真实原因

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the early [[WesternHanDynasty|西汉]] Zhao and succession branch after [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-180-xihan-diyi-yinghan-guangao-de-gushi-2-lutkapdijrgpwfrz3saaowxtk-i2|Hanji 180 part 2]]. [[LiuBang|刘邦]] favors [[QiJi|戚姬 / 戚夫人]] and [[LiuRuyi|刘如意]], considers replacing crown prince [[LiuYing|刘盈]], and is checked by [[ZhouChang|周昌]]'s blunt remonstrance. The host's core reading is that Liu Bang creates danger for Qi Ji and Liu Ruyi by raising the succession question, but gives them only weak protection through Zhou Chang's reassignment to Zhao, leaving [[LuZhi|吕雉]] and the established heir branch structurally stronger.

## Key Claims
- The episode places the succession crisis after the [[GuanGao|贯高]] and [[ZhangAo|张敖]] branch: Liu Ruyi has become king of Zhao, but Liu Bang keeps him in Chang'an rather than sending him immediately to the kingdom.
- Qi Ji is introduced as Liu Bang's favored partner and Liu Ruyi's mother; the source uses her pressure for Liu Ruyi's advancement as the intimate-court side of the succession dispute.
- Liu Bang dislikes Liu Ying's gentle and benevolent temperament and thinks Liu Ruyi resembles him more, turning personal preference into [[FavoriteDrivenHeirDisplacement|favorite-driven heir displacement]] pressure.
- The source presents Liu Ying's vulnerability as political, not only familial: if the heir is changed, Lu Zhi's empress branch loses future security.
- Zhou Chang opposes the replacement plan in court, saying that even if he cannot fully articulate the reason, he knows the command is unacceptable and will not receive it.
- Lu Zhi's kneeling thanks to Zhou Chang marks the issue as a real threat to the crown prince rather than a harmless palace quarrel.
- [[ZhaoYao|赵尧]] advises Liu Bang that Liu Ruyi needs a Zhao chancellor whom Lu Zhi, Liu Ying, and the other kings will respect or fear.
- Liu Bang sends Zhou Chang to be Zhao chancellor and makes Zhao Yao yushi dafu, but Zhou Chang reads the appointment as demotion from central office.
- The host argues that Zhou Chang's protection is institutionally thin because he has neither central court power nor military command once he is in Zhao.
- The episode's main interpretive claim is [[AlternativeHeirProtectionFailure|alternative-heir protection failure]]: Liu Bang arouses Lu Zhi's fear and resentment but does not build a durable political base for Qi Ji and Liu Ruyi.
- No front-court ministerial bloc is shown backing Liu Ruyi; Qi Ji is framed as emotionally effective with Liu Bang but politically underpowered.
- The source therefore treats Liu Bang's failure to complete the heir change as realistic, since Liu Ruyi would likely have struggled to hold the throne without elite support.

## Key Quotes
> "废太子，臣期不奉诏" - Zhou Chang's refusal as rendered by the episode.

> "刘如意没有自己的政治势力" - the host's summary of the favored son's weakness.

> "没有给戚夫人和如意任何实质性的保护" - the host's critique of Liu Bang's arrangement.
