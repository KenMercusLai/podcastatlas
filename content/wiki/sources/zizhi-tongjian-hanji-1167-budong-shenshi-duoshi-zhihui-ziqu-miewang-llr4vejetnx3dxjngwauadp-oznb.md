## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode pairs the failed 219 CE plot of [[WeiFengLateHan|魏讽]] with [[DuXiLateHan|杜袭]]'s intervention when [[CaoCao|曹操]] wants to attack the insubordinate Guanzhong commander [[XuYouGuanzhongCommander|许攸]]. [[ZhongYao|钟繇]] first recommends the talented and influential Wei Feng, but [[ChenYiChangleWeiwei|陈祎]] reports the conspiracy to [[CaoPi|曹丕]] before it begins; Zhong Yao then loses office because sponsorship has made his personnel judgment politically consequential.

The second branch shows counsel working through emotional sequence rather than blunt contradiction. Du Xi first asks whether Cao Cao will correct a mistaken decision, then reduces the status injury by arguing that Xu You is too minor to define Cao Cao and too unimportant to justify a large campaign. The episode joins [[ConcealedCapabilityTiming|藏器待时]], [[RecommendationAccountabilityTalentSelection|举荐问责式选才]], and [[AngerDrivenWarDecision|因怒兴师]] while keeping its reconstructed dialogue, inner motives, and modern lessons source-scoped.

## Key Claims

- Wei Feng gains influence in Ye through talent, eloquence, and public reputation; Zhong Yao recommends him for office despite Zheng Mao's warning that his conduct signals danger.
- Wei Feng recruits associates and reportedly plans to exploit Cao Cao's external campaigns by acting inside Ye against Cao Pi.
- Chen Yi initially enters the plan but, after reconsidering the danger to himself and his family, reports it to Cao Pi before the uprising begins.
- Cao Pi arrests and executes Wei Feng and associated actors; the episode presents the response as both threat removal and a wider political purge.
- Zhong Yao is removed from office because recommending Wei Feng has tied his own personnel judgment and political credit to the nominee's conduct.
- The Guanzhong camp commander Xu You refuses Cao Cao's order and uses contemptuous language; this is a distinct person from [[XuYouLateHan|许攸]], Yuan Shao's adviser and Guandu defector.
- Du Xi interrupts Cao Cao's angry impulse to campaign by first securing permission to correct a mistake, then arguing that a minor subordinate's insult does not merit strategic attention.
- Cao Cao sends rank and gifts rather than an army, and the episode says Xu You returns to obedience without the proposed campaign.
