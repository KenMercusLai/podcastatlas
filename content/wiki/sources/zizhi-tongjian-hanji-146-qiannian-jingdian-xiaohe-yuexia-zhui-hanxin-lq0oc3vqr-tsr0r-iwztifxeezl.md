# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》146｜千年经典：萧何月下追韩信

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the [[HanXin|韩信]] branch after [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-145-junshi-qicai-hanxin-bolan-zhuangkuo-de-yisheng-2-npgife56ltayww2wt4wo-fiwo-ep|Hanji 145 part 2]] by moving him from latent talent into [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s command system. It follows Han Xin from [[XiangLiang|项梁]] and [[XiangYu|项羽]] through minor office, near execution, [[XiahouYing|夏侯婴]]'s first notice, [[XiaoHe|萧何]]'s stronger recognition, and the famous pursuit after Han Xin flees [[Nanzheng|南郑]]. The episode's main contribution is [[RitualizedCommandAppointment|仪式化拜将留才]]: Xiao He argues that if Liu Bang truly wants the empire rather than a confined Han kingship, Han Xin must receive public, ceremonial command authority.

## Key Claims
- Han Xin's earlier [[GreatCourageUnderHumiliation|忍辱大勇]] setup now becomes a career problem: his strategic preparation is real, but neither Xiang Yu nor Liu Bang initially gives him a matching role.
- The episode reconstructs Han Xin joining [[XiangLiang|项梁]] near [[Huaiyin|淮阴]] with military learning already in hand, then surviving the [[Dingtao|定陶]] defeat that kills Xiang Liang.
- After [[JuluBattle|巨鹿之战]], Han Xin serves [[XiangYu|项羽]] only as a langzhong / close attendant; his advice is repeatedly ignored.
- The host treats Han Xin's possible objections to Xiang Yu's [[Xianyang|咸阳]] violence and partition choices as inference, not directly recorded fact.
- Han Xin follows Liu Bang into [[Hanzhong|汉中]] because Liu Bang's reputation for winning people and the [[HongmenYan|鸿门宴]] scene make him look more promising than Xiang Yu.
- In Liu Bang's camp, Han Xin first holds only a minor or mid-level post, then nearly dies under military law before his "why kill a strong man if the Han king wants the world" challenge catches Xiahou Ying's attention.
- Xiahou Ying recognizes that Han Xin is unusual and reports him to Liu Bang, but Liu Bang's appointment of Han Xin as grain-supply commandant still does not treat him as decisive talent.
- The grain-supply post matters because it puts Han Xin near Xiao He, whose fiscal and logistical role gives him more chances to evaluate Han Xin closely.
- At Nanzheng, homesick soldiers and officers frequently desert; Han Xin's own flight shows that latent talent will leave if the organization cannot offer credible future use.
- Xiao He chases Han Xin without first reporting to Liu Bang because, in his judgment, Han Xin is not one replaceable officer among many but "国士无双."
- Xiao He's argument is explicitly strategic: if Liu Bang only wants to remain Han king, Han Xin is unnecessary; if he wants to fight for all under Heaven, Han Xin is the person with whom to plan it.
- Xiao He also insists on form. A casual summons into the tent is not enough; Liu Bang must choose an auspicious day, bathe, fast, build a platform, and appoint Han Xin with full public ceremony.

## Key Quotes
> "汉王不欲得天下乎？何为斩壮士！" - Han Xin's reported challenge before execution.

> "诸将易得耳，至如信者，国士无双。" - Xiao He's distinction between ordinary generals and Han Xin.

> "王必欲长王汉中，无所事信；必欲争天下，非信无所与计事者。" - Xiao He's strategic condition for using Han Xin.
