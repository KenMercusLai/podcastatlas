# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》875丨离大谱！为何他以入狱为荣？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills 166 CE immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-876-zhichang-zong-pengbi-shuoming-ni-meiyou-zhaodui-guanxi-lrjob-a07t29hdr0iogrmcsjr7qp|Hanji 876]]'s rescue path for the first Party Prohibition prisoners. It connects the moral prestige of imprisonment through [[HuangfuGuiLateHan|皇甫规]], [[DuMiLateHan|杜密]]'s public-duty critique of Liu Sheng, [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]]'s early clean-office profile, and [[ZhangHuanLateHan|张奂]]'s selective pacification of [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] and [[Wuhuan|乌桓]] submitters.

The frontier close introduces [[TanshihuaiXianbei|檀石槐]] as an organized [[Xianbei|鲜卑]] leader before the later 177-181 CE campaign and succession branches. The Han court tries title recognition and marriage alliance, but Tanshihuai refuses and intensifies raiding while dividing the occupied field into eastern, central, and western commands.

## Key Claims

- In the first Party Prohibition field, imprisonment of famous worthy men becomes a badge of moral alignment rather than only a penalty.
- [[HuangfuGuiLateHan|Huangfu Gui]] treats not being imprisoned as shame, self-reports party association, and is ignored by the court.
- [[DuMiLateHan|Du Mi]] is arrested alongside [[LiYingLateHan|Li Ying]], and the source grounds his reputation in active talent recognition and public responsibility rather than private purity alone.
- Du Mi's criticism of Liu Sheng rejects closed-door self-protection: worthy people should be recommended, and people who violate the right way should be exposed.
- [[DouWuLateHan|Dou Wu]] enters the 166 CE source field as a clean Chengmen Xiaowei who recruits noted people, refuses bribery, keeps his household austere, and distributes palace gifts to Taixue students or commoners.
- [[ZhangHuanLateHan|Zhang Huan]]'s return makes Southern Xiongnu and Wuhuan groups submit; he punishes only ringleaders and accepts the rest, giving an early selective-pacification layer before later Qiang policy disputes.
- [[TanshihuaiXianbei|Tanshihuai]] rejects Han royal title and marriage diplomacy, intensifies frontier raids, and organizes Xianbei territory into eastern, central, and western parts.

## Key Quotes

> "以未入狱为耻" - the source's summary of Huangfu Gui's Party Prohibition honor logic.

> "不举荐善良之人，也不揭发邪恶之事" - Du Mi's criticism of closed-door self-protection.

> "只诛杀其中煽动起兵造反的首恶分子" - Zhang Huan's selective-punishment rule toward submitters.
