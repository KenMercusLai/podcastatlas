# #397 从「责任分担」到「责任转移」：欧洲为何难以真正与美国「分道扬镳」？

## Summary
This [[ShengdongJixi|声东击西]] episode has [[XuTao|徐涛]] interview [[SunChenghao|孙承昊]] about the 2026 [[NATO]] summit, European defense spending, the Ukraine war, Trump-era alliance pressure, and the limits of [[EuropeanDefenseAutonomy]]. Its central contribution is [[TransatlanticBurdenShifting]]: the United States still values NATO, but increasingly wants Europe to absorb regional security costs so American attention can move toward global and Indo-Pacific competition. The episode also adds [[MilitarizedAITechnologyBlocRisk]], warning that AI, cloud, export controls, investment screening, and defense procurement can pull civilian technology into alliance blocs.

## Key Claims
- The summit's 5% target is split between 3.5% core defense spending and 1.5% broader security investment, but most members remain far from that level.
- [[NATO]] support for [[Ukraine]] is becoming more institutionalized: the episode says the summit discussed EUR 70 billion in military equipment, aid, and training for 2026 and similar support in 2027.
- [[EuropeanDefenseAutonomy]] is not anti-American military separation; in Sun's framing it means reducing dependence on any single great power while still recognizing that many European states need American security backing against [[Russia]].
- [[France]], [[Germany]], southern Europe, [[Canada]], and [[Turkey]] illustrate different alliance positions, so "Europe" should not be treated as one actor.
- [[DonaldTrump]]'s spending pressure is not only personal style; it reflects a broader U.S. debate that reframes allies from strategic assets into cost burdens.
- [[TransatlanticBurdenShifting]] differs from burden sharing because it asks Europe to take over responsibilities previously carried by the United States.
- The alliance agenda now reaches beyond military hardware into AI, cloud, intelligence, export controls, technology protection, investment screening, and supply-chain security.
- The episode argues that China-facing U.S. and European policies may converge in economic security, AI, and critical minerals, but Europe still moves more cautiously and keeps cooperation space open in multilateral governance, climate, public health, AI safety, and biosecurity.

## Key Quotes
> "责任转移" - Sun's distinction from ordinary burden sharing.

> "独立的灵魂" - the episode's shorthand for the political will missing from European autonomy.
