# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1088丨军营夜乱，他带几十人就镇住了？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 208 CE transition immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1089-shenwei-kongzi-ershishisun-kongrong-ruhe-chunu-caocao-lqz6drrrl4l4schcmtdkg2ofvo6i|Hanji 1089]]. It first shows [[ZhangLiao|张辽]] containing a night camp mutiny by ordering uninvolved troops to remain still, making active disorder visible; it then follows [[ZhaoYanLateHan|赵俨]] coordinating mutually hostile commands without direct troop control.

The western branch shows [[CaoCao|曹操]] reducing risk before the Jingzhou campaign by using [[ZhangJiCaoOfficialLateHan|张既]] to bring [[MaTeng|马腾]] into court office. Public reception arrangements make Ma Teng's promised journey hard to reverse, while command passes to [[MaChao|马超]] and the household's later move to Ye turns family into political collateral. The episode closes on the source's hostage paradox: Ma Teng does not rebel, but Ma Chao's later rising is said to bring collective execution on Ma Teng's household.

## Key Claims

- In Jian'an 13 / 208 CE, a night fire and mutiny break out as Zhang Liao is preparing to move to Changshe.
- Zhang Liao judges that only a minority is rebelling, orders nonparticipants to stay in place, stands in the camp center with several dozen men, and arrests the exposed leader after order returns.
- The host links the mutiny to Zhang Liao's recent receipt of [[CaoHong|曹洪]]'s former troops and interprets it as conflict over status and resources between old and new command groups.
- [[DisorderStabilizationByStillness|以静制乱式识别]] abstracts the immediate mechanism: restricting friendly movement reduces ambiguity and makes hostile movement easier to identify.
- [[YuJin|于禁]] and [[LeJinLateHan|乐进]] are presented as strategically important but personally incompatible commanders, so Cao Cao sends Zhao Yan to reconcile their forces through delegated authority rather than direct troop ownership.
- Before moving south against [[LiuBiao|刘表]], Cao Cao seeks to reduce western uncertainty around Ma Teng and [[HanSui|韩遂]].
- Zhang Ji's personal access, regional position, and grain leverage make him the intermediary for inviting Ma Teng into court as weiwei.
- After Ma Teng first agrees and then hesitates, Zhang Ji orders route supplies and formal receptions in advance, making the journey publicly expected and therefore politically costly to cancel.
- Ma Teng enters court service, Ma Chao retains command as a subordinate general, and the family later moves to [[YeLateHan|邺城]], creating a divided command-and-collateral arrangement.
- The episode says Ma Teng never rebels, but Ma Chao rises three years later and Ma Teng's household of 237 people is executed; the exact number and sequence remain source-scoped.
- Cao Cao's July advance against Liu Biao connects the western settlement to the wider Jingzhou campaign and leads directly into the Kong Rong sequence continued in Hanji 1089.

## Key Quotes

> “令未叛者原地不动、自率数十人立于营中。” - the episode summary's formulation of Zhang Liao's stabilizing response.

> “人质的政治，终极的悖论，就是人质永远有罪。” - the host's judgment on Ma Teng's later household punishment.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with existing wiki synthesis is recorded. The episode extends Zhang Liao's command profile and Ma Teng's contingent cooperation into the 208 court-relocation branch.
- Transcript normalization: “赵衍” is treated as [[ZhaoYanLateHan|赵俨]], “张继” as [[ZhangJiCaoOfficialLateHan|张既]], “于晋” as [[YuJin|于禁]], “岳晋” as [[LeJinLateHan|乐进]], and “中游” as [[ZhongYao|钟繇]] where the sequence is clear.
- Text caution: “夜忽者恐夜” likely reflects “夜呼者，恐也,” while “以静待化” may reflect a phrase such as “以静待哗”; neither corrupt transcript form is treated as a verified quotation from the *Art of War*.
