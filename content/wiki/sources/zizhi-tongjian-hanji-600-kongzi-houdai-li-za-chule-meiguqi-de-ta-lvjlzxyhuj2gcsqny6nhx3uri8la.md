# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》600丨孔子后代里，咋出了没骨气的他？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode fills the late-[[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] sequence between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-599-gan-dui-qianguize-shuo-bu-chengxiang-wangjia-you-duo-yong-lpptx-fzls9xbakw52b8n1n6xow0|Hanji 599]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-601-1-chule-yuhui-tade-wenhua-shuiping-ye-neng-gaiming-lnt4p2mo2nwfm3xqhg0dmfwiip1n|Hanji 601-1]]. After Wei Shang's brief tenure and death, Aidi appoints the twenty-two-year-old [[DongXian|董贤]] Da Sima and Wei General, leaves him continually near the palace, and gives him practical control over the channel through which officials submit memorials.

The durable synthesis is that favorite power changes both access and ritual behavior. Dong Xian's office and proximity elevate his family and make him a gatekeeper to the emperor, while [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]] treats this nominal fellow member of the Three Excellencies as a superior during a deliberately staged private visit; Aidi rewards Kong Guang's compliance by appointing two of his nephews, making etiquette an observable signal of adaptation to imperial preference.

## Key Claims

- After former Dingtao tutor and Guanglu Dafu Wei Shang briefly serves as Da Sima Chariot-and-Cavalry General and dies, Aidi appoints Dong Xian Da Sima and Wei General in the twelfth month of Yuanshou 1 / 2 BCE.
- Dong Xian is only twenty-two, remains frequently inside the palace, and manages memorial traffic so officials must pass through him to submit writings to Aidi.
- Dong Xian's father Dong Gong becomes Guanglu Dafu at the central two-thousand-shi grade, his brother Dong Kuanxin succeeds him as Fuma Duwei, and other relatives receive palace-access offices.
- The episode says Dong-family favor exceeds that of the Ding and Fu families, making Dong Xian's rise a family-network as well as an individual appointment story.
- Aidi sends Dong Xian privately to Kong Guang's residence as a test of how the chancellor will respond to the favorite.
- Although Kong Guang and Dong Xian are nominal peers among the Three Excellencies, Kong Guang uses guarded preparation, formal dress, gate-by-gate waiting, and highly deferential greeting and farewell rites associated with receiving a superior.
- Aidi interprets Kong Guang's reception as obedience and appoints two of Kong Guang's nephews as Jian Dafu and Changshi.

## Key Quotes

> "上有所好，下必从之" - the episode's summary of how the ruler's preference reorganizes subordinate behavior.

> "完美的官场老油条" - the host's judgment on Kong Guang's ability to read and accommodate the political field.
