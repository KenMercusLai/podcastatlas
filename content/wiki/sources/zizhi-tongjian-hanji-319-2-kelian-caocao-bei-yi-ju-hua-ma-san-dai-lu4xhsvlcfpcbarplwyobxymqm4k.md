# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》319-2｜可怜曹操，被一句话骂三代

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode joins two personnel-politics examples. In the late-Han branch, [[SimaYi|司马懿]] claims illness to avoid [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s summons but accepts office after an arrest threat; [[ChenLinLateHan|陈琳]]'s anti-Cao proclamation is then used to show how political invective attacks lineage as well as the immediate opponent.

The Western Han branch turns from [[DongfangShuo|东方朔]]'s *Da Ke Nan* complaint to [[GongsunHong|公孙弘]] as the host's model of talent that [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] found usable. Its durable contribution is [[PowerDefinedTalentUtility|权力中心定义式人才评价]]: ability does not automatically become political opportunity when one ruler controls recognition, appointment, and the acceptable form of advice. The source closes by connecting Wudi's repeated wars to an exhausted treasury and previewing later fiscal-extraction episodes.

## Key Claims

- Sima Yi reportedly resists Cao Cao's summons by claiming serious illness, then takes office after Cao Cao threatens arrest.
- The episode's supplied text repeatedly writes “司马逸”; the described Cao Cao recruitment story matches the existing Sima Yi narrative, so the wiki treats this as a transcript or summary error rather than a separate person.
- Chen Lin's proclamation for Yuan Shao uses “赘阉遗丑” to attack Cao Cao through the Cao family's connection to the eunuch [[CaoTengLateHan|曹腾]], making ancestry part of political warfare.
- The supplied text glosses “遗丑” as an attack on appearance, but that lexical claim is not independently established here and remains source-scoped.
- Dongfang Shuo's *Da Ke Nan* is presented as a complaint that capable people can remain underused when historical conditions and a ruler's preferences limit available roles.
- The host argues that Han Wudi recognizes talent according to whether a person can be used smoothly within imperial priorities, not by ability in isolation.
- Gongsun Hong is offered as the positive contrast: the source describes him as both capable and able to understand imperial intention, but gives no new event-level example beyond that comparison.
- Continuous warfare is said to have depleted Wudi's treasury, setting up the next discussion of revenue-raising measures.

## Key Quotes

> “赘阉遗丑，本无懿德。” - the episode's wording from Chen Lin's anti-Cao proclamation, used to explain lineage-targeted invective.

> “真正重要的是像公孙弘那样既有才又懂帝王心。” - the host's compressed standard for talent that becomes usable near concentrated power.

- [[ChenLinLateHan|陈琳]], [[YuanShao|袁绍]], [[CaoSong|曹嵩]], [[CaoTengLateHan|曹腾]], and [[LegitimacyIndictmentWarfare|正当性控诉战]] - the proclamation converts family origin into campaign rhetoric.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with existing wiki synthesis is adopted. The Sima Yi recruitment and Chen Lin proclamation substantially repeat the fuller Hanji 1087, 1043, and 1069 source notes.
- Naming correction: “司马逸” is treated as a transcript or summary error for [[SimaYi|司马懿]], based on the uniquely matching Cao Cao summons and feigned-illness episode.
- Genealogy and wording caution: Cao Song was adopted by Cao Teng; the source's “过继给宦官” summary is usable at that level, but “赘阉遗丑” remains hostile rhetoric rather than a neutral genealogical description.
- Source-scope caution: the meaning assigned to “遗丑,” Dongfang Shuo's motives in writing *Da Ke Nan*, Gongsun Hong's privileged understanding of “帝王心,” Wudi's willingness to kill unusable talent, and the direct fiscal causation all require broader textual support.
