# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》456-1｜以命祭皇位！揭秘汉武帝最奇葩儿子

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills a late-[[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] layer immediately before the Hanji 457-466 sequence. It centers on [[LiuXuGuanglingKingWesternHan|广陵王刘须]], a son of [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] whose imperial ambition turns into repeated witchcraft curses against Liu Fuling, Liu He, and Xuan, and whose final exposure leads to witness killing and suicide.

The second half connects the same period to state order beyond royal scandal: [[HuhanyeChanyuWesternHan|呼韩邪单于]] submits and sends a hostage, frontier garrisons can be reduced, and [[GengShouchangWesternHan|耿寿昌]] proposes both cheaper regional grain procurement and [[ChangpingGranarySystem|长平仓制度]] to stabilize grain prices and prepare for scarcity.

## Key Claims

- [[LiuXuGuanglingKingWesternHan|刘须]] is presented as Han Wudi's fourth son and the first Guangling king: physically strong, fond of pleasure and beast-fighting, and politically unfit in Wudi's eyes.
- Liu Xu wants the throne but lacks the nerve for open rebellion, so he turns to [[RoyalWitchcraftSuccessionDelusion|宗室巫术夺位幻想]] by using the Chu witch Li Nuxu to curse reigning or newly selected emperors.
- Liu Fuling's death and Liu He's rapid deposition make Liu Xu misread political events as proof that the curse works.
- When Xuan spares Liu Xu after an earlier implicated-treason scare, Liu Xu temporarily abandons the throne fantasy, but family punishment and lost territory later revive resentment.
- Liu Xu's final curse case becomes a legal and moral collapse: he poisons witches and more than twenty palace women, destroys ritual objects, is found out, and commits suicide in 54 BCE.
- The episode frames Huhanye's submission and hostage-sending as a major reversal from the Baideng-era Xiongnu threat, allowing Han to reduce part of its frontier garrison.
- [[GengShouchangWesternHan|耿寿昌]] argues that local purchasing around the capital can replace part of costly Guandong grain transport and then proposes [[ChangpingGranarySystem|长平仓制度]]: buying grain when cheap and selling when dear to avoid both farmer and consumer harm.

## Key Quotes

> "有贼心没贼胆" - the host's summary of Liu Xu's political temperament.

> "既避免谷贱伤农，又防止谷贵伤民" - the episode's summary of the Changping granary purpose.
