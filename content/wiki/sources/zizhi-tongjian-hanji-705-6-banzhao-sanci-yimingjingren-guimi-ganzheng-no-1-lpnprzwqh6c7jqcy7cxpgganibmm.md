# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》705-6丨班昭：三次一鸣惊人，闺蜜干政NO.1

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode presents [[BanZhaoLateHan|班昭 / Ban Zhao]] as a late Eastern Han intellectual whose influence spans court writing, `Han Shu` transmission, teaching, petitioning, and advisory politics. It begins with the [[ParthianEmpire|Anxi / Parthian]] tribute of lions and a large bird in 101 CE and Ban Zhao's `Da Que Fu`, then moves through [[EmperorHeOfHan|Emperor He]]'s favor, her petition for [[BanChaoEasternHan|Ban Chao]]'s return, and [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Empress Dowager Deng]]'s later trust in her advice.

The durable synthesis is that Ban Zhao's current wiki profile should not collapse into a single "talented woman" label. The episode links her public authority to historiographical continuation, teaching `Han Shu` to students such as [[MaRongLateHan|Ma Rong]], court access through women's education, Deng-regency counsel, and the contested afterlife of `Nujie`, where family instruction becomes a gender-norm legacy later praised, criticized, and politically repurposed.

## Key Claims

- The episode treats Ban Zhao's `Da Que Fu` after the 101 CE Anxi tribute as a visible literary-performance moment that helps explain Emperor He's recognition of her talent.
- Ban Zhao's petition for the aged Ban Chao turns family concern into a state-stability argument: leaving a veteran Western Regions commander to die abroad risks both personal tragedy and loss of accumulated frontier achievement.
- The episode presents Ban Zhao as a teacher of `Han Shu`, including Ma Rong as a male student, and frames that as a source-scoped breakthrough in women's public teaching.
- Empress Dowager Deng is described as deeply trusting Ban Zhao after taking power, using her as a policy adviser during disasters, Qiang pressure, and Deng-family mourning politics.
- Ban Zhao's advice about [[DengZhiLateHan|Deng Zhi]] and the Deng brothers' mourning request is framed as a way to protect public legitimacy while allowing filial withdrawal.
- `Nujie` is presented as a late family-instruction text rooted in admiration for [[BanJieyuWesternHan|Ban Jieyu]], but its later role in constraining women is treated as the most contested part of Ban Zhao's legacy.
- The episode praises Ban Zhao's literary and historiographical status while explicitly keeping her posthumous reputation morally mixed because of `Nujie`.

## Key Quotes

> "闺蜜干政" - the host's label for Ban Zhao's trusted advisory relationship with Empress Dowager Deng.

> "第一位女教师" - source-scoped description of Ban Zhao's teaching role after `Han Shu`.

> "男子压迫妇女的宣言书" - the episode's strong critical judgment on the later political use of `Nujie`.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with existing wiki pages was found. The source largely backfills and consolidates Ban Zhao material already split across Hanji 767, Hanji 774, Hanji 775, and Hanji 785.
- Scope caution: the claims that the Anxi bird was the first live ostrich to enter China, that Ban Zhao was China's first female teacher, that no officials or commoners objected to her political participation, and that `Nujie` directly functioned as a male-oppression manifesto are preserved as source-scoped claims rather than independently settled comparative history.
- Chronology caution: the episode ranges from 101 CE to Ban Zhao's reported death in 120 CE, so the wiki treats it as a topical Ban Zhao synthesis source rather than one annal year.
