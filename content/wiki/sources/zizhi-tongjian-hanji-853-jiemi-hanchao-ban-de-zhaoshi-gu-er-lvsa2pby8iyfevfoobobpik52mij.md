# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》853丨揭秘汉朝版的“赵氏孤儿”

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 160 CE [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] layer between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-852-dang-meinv-ruyun-shi-fu-haishi-huo-luml30ytbc-awpvk-7-23ehzbqpx|Hanji 852]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-854-lishi-gaosu-ni-xuanguan-yongguan-hen-zhongyao-lhin-iwk6y1xr8vvzdmnjch3s2um|Hanji 854]]. It opens with Huan's amnesty and search for [[LiGuLateHan|李固]]'s descendants, then reconstructs how [[LiWenjiLateHan|李文姬]] and [[WangChengLiGuStudent|王成]] hid and preserved [[LiXieLateHan|李燮]] after the Li-family disaster.

The second half links that survival story to eunuch-favorite violence and official vulnerability. [[ShanChaoLateHan|单超]]'s lavish burial and the remaining Huan-era marquises' indulgence frame a court ecology in which [[HouLanLateHan|侯览]] and [[DuanGuiLateHan|段珪]] can get [[TengYanLateHan|滕延]] dismissed after he executes their violent servants, while [[ZhaoQiLateHan|赵岐]] survives [[TangXuanLateHan|唐玹]]'s family-destruction retaliation only through flight, concealment, and [[SunSongLateHan|孙嵩]]'s shelter.

## Key Claims

- In 160 CE, Emperor Huan issues a general amnesty and orders a search for Li Gu's descendants, prompting the episode to recover the Li-family disaster.
- Li Wenji hides her younger brother Li Xie, spreads false information about his route, and entrusts him to Wang Cheng so that Li Gu's line can survive.
- Li Xie's survival requires name change, low-status labor in Xuzhou, and public non-recognition between protector and protected person.
- After [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] is killed, Li Xie returns quietly, completes mourning for Li Gu, and follows Li Wenji's warning to avoid contact and criticism that could reopen political danger.
- Shan Chao's lavish burial and the remaining Huan-era marquises' arrogance show imperial favor turning post-Liang reward into local predation by relatives, guests, and servants.
- Teng Yan's punishment of Hou Lan and Duan Gui's violent retainers rebounds onto Teng Yan himself, making lawful local enforcement politically unsafe.
- Zhao Qi's refusal to serve under [[ZuoShengLateHan|左胜]] and later flight from Tang Xuan's retaliation echo Li Xie's story: elite conflict can push officials into hidden, low-status survival.
- The closing Qiang notice moves from court and local violence toward renewed frontier pressure around Zhangye and [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]].
