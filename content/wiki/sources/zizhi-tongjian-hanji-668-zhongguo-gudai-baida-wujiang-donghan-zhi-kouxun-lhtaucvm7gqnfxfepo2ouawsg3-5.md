# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》668：中国古代百大武将：东汉之寇恂

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the Gengshi 1-2 / 23-24 CE setup immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-669-suanming-xiansheng-kao-huyou-yeneng-dangshang-huangdi-liygphsrxj01-wn3vvwoyu77-bkp|Hanji 669]]. It uses [[KouXunEasternHan|寇恂]] to open the "good ruler knows how to use people" theme, then follows [[LiuXuanGengshiEmperor|更始帝刘玄]]'s regional-pacification promises, [[LiuXiu|刘秀]]'s northern mission, [[DengYuEasternHan|邓禹]]'s early strategic advice, [[FengYiEasternHan|冯异]] and [[GengChunEasternHan|耿纯]] moving toward Liu Xiu, and [[WangLangHebeiClaimant|王郎]]'s emergence as a false Liu Ziyu claimant.

The durable synthesis is that early Eastern Han formation begins as a credibility contest before it is a battlefield sequence. Kou Xun protects the restored regime's promise by forcing a Gengshi envoy to return [[GengKuangEasternHan|耿况]]'s seal, Liu Xiu builds a cleaner political image in Hebei by reviewing officials, hearing lawsuits, abolishing Wang Mang-era harsh rules, and refusing gifts, while Wang Lang's lineage story and Red Eyebrows rumor show how fragile information environments can turn dynastic identity into mass belief.

## Key Claims

- Kou Xun's Shanggu seal intervention shows that restored-regime authority depends on keeping surrender promises visible, not only on issuing appointments.
- Liu Xuan's early pacification and enfeoffment policy can attract local elites, but unstable benefits and incomplete settlement leave both Red Eyebrows leaders and regional actors hard to hold.
- Liu Xiu's northern mission becomes the first field where he can build independent credibility through personnel review, legal correction, institutional restoration, and refusal of gifts.
- Deng Yu joins Liu Xiu for a larger restoration project rather than a narrow office-seeking motive, and immediately diagnoses Liu Xuan's court as politically shallow and self-interested.
- Feng Yi and Geng Chun both read Liu Xiu's discipline and organization as signs of a different military-political order.
- Liu Xiu rejects Liu Lin's proposed Yellow River flood tactic against the Red Eyebrows, preserving a source-scoped contrast between expedient destruction and his broader order-building image.
- Wang Lang's false Liu Ziyu story combines Han lineage, miraculous birth narrative, and rumor testing; Liu Lin and local Zhao actors turn belief into a Handan imperial claim.

## Key Quotes

> "天下初定，各郡国都在观望" - Kou Xun's warning that one broken promise in Shanggu would damage wider command credibility.

> "不是为求仕途而来" - Deng Yu's answer when Liu Xiu asks whether he has come seeking office.

> "赤眉军将拥立刘子舆为皇帝" - the rumor used to test public belief before Wang Lang is enthroned.
