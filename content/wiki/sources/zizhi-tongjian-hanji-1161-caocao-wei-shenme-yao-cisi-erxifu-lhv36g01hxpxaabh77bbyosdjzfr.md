## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode uses an angry-wolf fable to ask whether adversity produces retaliation or disciplined concern for others. It then follows [[CaoZhi|曹植]] after his succession defeat, attributing [[CuiShiCaoZhiWife|崔氏]]'s forced separation and death to a source-scoped combination of [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s household austerity, warning to Cao Zhi, protection of [[CaoPi|曹丕]]'s position, and pressure on the Qinghe Cui clan.

The military branch supplies the opening of the [[HanzhongCampaign218LateHan|Hanzhong campaign]]: [[FaZheng|法正]] urges [[LiuBei|刘备]] to exploit Cao Cao's departure, but dispersed Liu-side forces leave [[WuLanLateHan|吴兰]] vulnerable to [[CaoHong|曹洪]]. After [[LuSu|鲁肃]] dies and [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]] succeeds him, [[LuXunDongWu|陆逊]] responds to [[ChunyuShiLateHan|淳于式]]'s complaint by acknowledging civilian burdens and recommending the complainant, making non-retaliation serve both public interest and personnel judgment.

## Key Claims

- The opening fable treats uncontrolled anger as a secondary harm that can become more destructive than the initial injury.
- Cao Zhi remains despondent after losing the succession contest and the Sima Gate breach, but the episode does not establish a clinical diagnosis or a complete chronology of his decline.
- Cao Cao reportedly separates Cao Zhi from Cui Shi and later orders her death; the episode combines dress rules, succession discipline, and great-clan politics as possible motives rather than proving one cause.
- Fa Zheng interprets Cao Cao's withdrawal from Hanzhong as an opportunity and persuades Liu Bei to begin a probing offensive.
- Cao Hong avoids the stronger Zhang Fei–Ma Chao pairing and concentrates force against the weaker Wu Lan wing, which is defeated before the separated units can provide effective support.
- After Lu Su's death, Sun Quan appoints Lyu Meng to the inherited role and continues developing Lu Xun through field responsibility and marriage into the Sun family.
- When Chunyu Shi complains that Lu Xun's levies burden the population, Lu Xun acknowledges the disruption, credits Chunyu Shi's public concern, and recommends him instead of retaliating.
