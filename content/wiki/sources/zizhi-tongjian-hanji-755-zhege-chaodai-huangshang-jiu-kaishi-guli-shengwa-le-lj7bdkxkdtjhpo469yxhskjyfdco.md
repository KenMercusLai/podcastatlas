# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》755丨这个朝代皇上就开始鼓励生娃了！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 85 CE [[EmperorZhangOfHan|汉章帝]] layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-756-weishenme-quan-ni-de-rao-ren-chu-qie-rao-ren-ltn4tqzy1d-sodhjidncv8dyqxpj|Hanji 756]]. It centers Zhangdi's pregnancy grain and household tax relief policy, then connects population, local official standards, calendrical correction, classical ritual, teacher respect, royal-kin mourning, and the first Southern-versus-Northern Xiongnu restitution dispute.

The durable synthesis is that Zhangdi's governance is presented as household-aware statecraft: more births mean future tax and military capacity, but the policy works through concrete relief for pregnant women and husbands. The episode also gives the immediate setup for Hanji 756's forbearance decision: [[MengYunEasternHan|Meng Yun]] warns that [[SouthernXiongnu|Southern Xiongnu]] seizure of [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|Northern Xiongnu]] captives and livestock may damage Han credibility, while [[ZhengHongEasternHan|Zheng Hong]], [[FifthLunEasternHan|Fifth Lun]], [[HuanYuEasternHan|Huan Yu]], and [[YuanAnEasternHan|Yuan An]] split over whether restitution is necessary.

## Key Claims

- Zhangdi turns earlier birth-related tax relief into a more explicit [[FertilityBurdenReliefPolicy|fertility burden relief]] package: pregnant women receive fetal-nurture grain and husbands receive a one-year poll-tax exemption.
- The episode reads ancient population growth as state capacity because registered people supply taxes, labor, and soldiers.
- The broader historical comparison to [[GuanZhong|Guan Zhong]], [[LiuBang|Liu Bang]], Southern Dynasties, Tang, and Song policies treats reduced household burden as a recurring fertility-governance tool.
- Zhangdi's instruction to the Three Excellencies favors low-disturbance local administration: a county magistrate's worth lies in frugality, reduced harassment, and public trust, not only in severe enforcement.
- The ritual and classical-learning scenes link Zhangdi's authority to calendar reform, the ploughing rite, sacrifices to Taishan and [[Confucius|Confucius]], student protocol toward [[ZhangPuEasternHan|Zhang Pu]], and reverence for Kong descendants.
- The [[ZhengJunEasternHan|Zheng Jun]] and [[LiuCangDongpingKing|Liu Cang]] branches give Zhangdi's tour a personal and moral register: clean conduct, teacher honor, and loyal household service are rewarded.
- Northern Xiongnu weakness is already visible in 85 CE through defections, multi-front pressure, and migration, while Southern Xiongnu succession and aggression create a credibility problem for Han frontier diplomacy.
- The final debate over returning captives and livestock sets up Hanji 756's resolution, where forbearance becomes a concrete frontier policy rather than a vague moral preference.

## Key Quotes

> "胎养谷三斛" - the concrete pregnancy-grain benefit attached to Zhangdi's birth encouragement.

> "政省约不烦" - the local-administration standard attached to Liu Fang of Xiangcheng.

> "为利作赃，终身捐弃" - Zheng Jun's warning against his brother's corrupt income.
