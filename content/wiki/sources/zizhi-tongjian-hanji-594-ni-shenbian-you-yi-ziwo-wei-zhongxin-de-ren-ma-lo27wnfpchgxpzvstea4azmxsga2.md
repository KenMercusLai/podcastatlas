# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》594丨你身边有以自我为中心的人吗？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode directly follows [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-593-zhenzheng-lihai-de-ren-zheyang-shuohua-luigzfyq6rtua0neci9jqi8ybxqq|Hanji 593]] by moving from [[WangJiaWesternHan|王嘉]]'s Dong Xian remonstrance to [[DuYeWesternHan|杜邺]]'s answer to [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]'s fault-seeking order in Yuanshou 1 / 2 BCE. Du Ye uses yin-yang hierarchy, filial precedents, solar eclipses, earthquakes, and historical cases to argue that Aidi's court is letting dowager and outer-relative power distort offices, military authority, and legal restraint.

The durable synthesis is that a ruler can request criticism while still filtering out the criticism that matters. Du Ye gives Aidi a cosmic and ritual vocabulary for self-correction, but the episode reads Aidi's later turn to [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]] after another eclipse as evidence that he wanted an answer easier to hear than Du Ye's direct diagnosis.

## Key Claims

- Aidi orders ministers and worthies to state his faults after disasters, creating a formal opening for remonstrance.
- Du Ye answers by translating solar eclipses and earthquakes into warnings about yin over yang, female and outer-relative influence, and political imbalance.
- The memorial uses stories about Zheng Bo, King Xiang of Zhou, Empress Lu, Zeng Shen, and Min Ziqian to argue that filial obedience does not justify blind compliance with parental or dowager wishes.
- Du Ye criticizes relatives of the Fu and Ding side for holding major palace, guard, and military posts regardless of merit.
- The appointment of two Da Sima generals on the day of a solar eclipse becomes Du Ye's evidence that Aidi's deference and private kinship favor have reached a dangerous office-design level.
- Du Ye distinguishes easy hindsight from hard self-recognition: people condemn earlier rulers' mistakes but fail to see the same pattern when they are inside it.
- Aidi later summons Kong Guang about another eclipse and raises him to Guanglu Dafu and palace adviser status, which the episode treats as a likely search for more acceptable counsel.

## Key Quotes

> "由后视前" - Du Ye's warning that later observers see earlier mistakes more easily than actors see their own.

> "犯颜直谏" - the episode's frame for Du Ye's risky correction of Aidi.
