# 《资治通鉴·周纪》29丨苏秦励志爽文：知识改变命运

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the 333 BCE aftermath of [[XuzhouMutualKingship|徐州相王]] by showing [[WeiState|魏国]] standing aside while [[ChuWeiwang|楚威王]] pressures [[QiState|齐国]] and demands that [[TianYing|田婴]] be expelled. It records compact succession notices for Han, Qi, Yan, and Wei, but treats the Qi notice and [[SuQin|苏秦]]'s sudden six-state triumph as another [[ChronicleChronologyDrift|编年错位]] problem, connecting it to the existing [[QiChronologyDispute|齐威王编年争议]]. The back half turns to [[Shiji|《史记》]]'s Su Qin story: failed formal study, family mockery, closed-door self-study, and the later conversion of that biography into [[InspirationalNarrativeCommercialization|励志叙事商业化]].

## Key Claims
- After Chu's attack on Qi, [[WeiState|魏国]] does not rescue its recent title partner, because Wei had already hoped Chu would punish Qi after [[XuzhouMutualKingship|徐州相王]].
- [[ChuWeiwang|楚威王]] demands that [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] expel [[TianYing|田婴]], but [[ZhangChou|张丑]] saves Tian Ying by arguing that leaving him in office better serves Chu's interest than letting a stronger replacement reorganize Qi.
- The episode lists four succession notices: [[HanZhaohou|韩昭侯]] dies and [[HanXuanhuiwang|韩宣惠王]] succeeds; the chronicle says Qi Wei Wang dies and [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]] succeeds; [[YanWengong|燕文公]] dies and [[YanYiwang|燕易王]] succeeds; [[WeiChenghou|魏成侯]] dies and [[WeiPinghou|魏平侯]] succeeds.
- The Qi succession notice is treated as unreliable: the host says [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] has not yet died in 333 BCE and that [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]]'s first year should be placed at 319 BCE.
- The source also doubts the chronicle's placement of [[SuQin|苏秦]] suddenly becoming [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] leader and simultaneous chief minister for the six eastern states in the same year.
- [[Shiji|《史记》]] supplies the developed Su Qin biography, including his failed years after studying under [[Guiguzi|鬼谷子]] and the later "头悬梁、锥刺股" inspirational frame.
- The episode presents [[SuQin|苏秦]]'s later success as [[FailureDrivenSelfStudy|失败后的自学翻身]] rather than a simple "famous teacher produces famous student" story.
- Su Qin's family mockery matters because intimate relations cannot be escaped as easily as classmates or colleagues; shame and status pressure become part of his motivation to study.
- [[SuDai|苏代]] and [[SuLi|苏厉]] make Su Qin's household a broader diplomat family, while [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] becomes the paired rival whose later [[ZonghengjiaDiplomacy|纵横家外交]] contrasts with Su Qin's hezong line.
- [[SimaGuang|司马光]] later uses Su Qin and [[ZhuFuYan|主父偃]] in a poem to [[WuZhicai|吾之才]], showing that the Su Qin story had already become a durable consolation script for frustrated literati.

## Key Quotes
> "知识改变命运" - the episode title's modern shorthand for the Su Qin story.

> "头悬梁，锥刺股" - the familiar inspirational tag attached to Su Qin's hard-study legend.

> "六国之相" - the source's phrase for Su Qin's peak status in the legend.

## Contradictions
- The episode contradicts a literal reading of the [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] placement for Qi Wei Wang's death, Qi Xuan Wang's accession, and Su Qin's six-state hezong success in 333 BCE. This is not a new contradiction inside the wiki; it extends the existing [[QiChronologyDispute|齐威王编年争议]] and [[ChronicleChronologyDrift|编年错位]] guardrails.
- No direct contradiction found with the existing wiki's treatment of [[XuzhouMutualKingship|徐州相王]] or [[StatusOrderBacklash|名分秩序反弹]]; the source continues the same backlash arc by showing why Wei had little incentive to rescue Qi.
