# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》654丨揭秘西汉扬雄的传奇人生

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Wang Mang's Tianfeng 5 / 18 CE crisis between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-653-ba-shehuizhuyi-daihui-gudai-wangmang-youduo-shen-lo7fqi-zlujpd3cymg7uxsujy4yk|Hanji 653]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-656-zhichang-buhui-shuohua-de-ren-hen-nan-hunchutou-lpn15smz7r1glekugzwtm2tpw-tl|Hanji 656]], then turns from late-[[XinDynasty|新朝]] disorder to [[YangXiong|扬雄]] as the central cultural figure. It presents Yang Xiong as a poor, stuttering, learned Western Han writer whose fu, [[Fangyan|《方言》]], and later reception show how cultural value can be ignored in its own time and recognized after death.

The durable synthesis is twofold. Politically, [[FeiXingXinOfficial|费兴]]'s dismissed livelihood diagnosis and the unpaid-official corruption purge sharpen the wiki's Wang Mang pattern: root-cause advice is punished while coercive inspection expands fear. Culturally, Yang Xiong's life adds the positive counterpoint of long scholarly work, low-key integrity, and delayed canonization under [[HuanTanEasternHan|桓谭]]'s prediction that later generations would value works his contemporaries undervalued.

## Key Claims

- The episode opens from Liu Yuxi's "西蜀子云亭" allusion to ask why Yang Xiong could be placed beside Zhuge Liang in later cultural memory.
- In 18 CE, North Army South Camp burns, and Wang Mang appoints Fei Xing as Jingzhou governor before asking him how to govern the region.
- Fei Xing argues that mountain-and-marsh taxation under Wang Mang's control system damages Jing-Yang livelihoods and that drought has pushed people into banditry; Wang Mang answers by removing him.
- Long-unpaid officials use office for illegal gain, and Wang Mang tries to fight corruption by confiscating most illicit official wealth and encouraging mutual denunciation, including slave denunciation of masters.
- Wang Zong's imperial-dress image and private seals, plus the accusations against [[WangFangXinPrincess|王芳]], show Xin palace-family fear spreading inside Wang Mang's own clan.
- Yang Xiong is presented as stuttering but intelligent, widely read, personally frank, poor without self-deception, and literarily linked to [[SimaXiangru|司马相如]] through fu writing.
- [[Fangyan|《方言》]] is presented as China's first dialectological work, produced by Yang Xiong over twenty-seven years and used to explain regional word differences and linguistic change.
- Huan Tan's judgment makes Yang Xiong a delayed-recognition case: contemporaries slight him because his rank, salary, and appearance are unimpressive, but his works' depth and Confucian compatibility make later transmission likely.
