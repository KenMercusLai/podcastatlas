# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》147｜媲美诸葛亮隆中对的“汉中对”

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues directly from [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-146-qiannian-jingdian-xiaohe-yuexia-zhui-hanxin-lq0oc3vqr-tsr0r-iwztifxeezl|Hanji 146]] by moving [[HanXin|韩信]] from public appointment into the strategic program later framed here as [[HanzhongDui|汉中对]]. Han Xin first has [[LiuBang|刘邦]] acknowledge inferiority to [[XiangYu|项羽]], then decomposes Xiang Yu's apparent strengths into defects of delegation, reward, legitimacy, and popular support. The episode's conclusion is that [[ThreeQinContainment|三秦压制]] is weaker than it looks because [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]], [[SimaXin|司马欣]], and [[DongYiQin|董翳]] lack [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] support, while Liu Bang's earlier [[YuefaSanzhang|约法三章]] and non-plunder policy give him a path to take the Three Qin and reopen the eastward contest.

## Key Claims
- [[XiaoHe|萧何]]'s recommendation of Han Xin is high-risk because Han Xin is young, lacks visible command record, and would have to outrank established Liu Bang-side generals.
- The episode infers that Han Xin may have left because a minor role could not give him strategic authorship; this motive is treated as reconstruction, not direct record.
- Liu Bang's formal appointment of Han Xin also becomes a trust signal toward Xiao He: Liu Bang stakes his own authority on Xiao He's judgment.
- Han Xin begins by asking whether Liu Bang intends to go east and contest the empire, then forces a sober comparison with Xiang Yu on bravery, treatment of people, and power.
- Han Xin reads Xiang Yu's bravery as "匹夫之勇" because Xiang Yu can terrify people but cannot delegate to capable commanders.
- Han Xin reads Xiang Yu's kindness as "妇人之仁" because courtesy and sympathy do not translate into timely grants of titles and rewards.
- Xiang Yu's larger errors include refusing [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] as a base, violating the [[HuaiwangAgreement|怀王之约]], moving [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]] away as Yi Emperor, and using destructive violence against populations.
- Han Xin tells Liu Bang to invert Xiang Yu's pattern: use capable generals, distribute territory and rewards credibly, honor Yi Emperor and the old promise, and move with soldiers who want to return east.
- The Three Qin rulers are vulnerable because Qin people blame the surrendered commanders for years of losses and remember that the common surrendered soldiers were killed at [[XinanMassacre|新安坑降]] while the senior leaders survived.
- Liu Bang's earlier restraint in Qin territory gives him political leverage: the people of Guanzhong already expected him to become king there under the prior agreement.
- The strategic endpoint is that an eastward move from Hanzhong can begin by taking the Three Qin, with proclamation and legitimacy doing work before full battlefield collision.

## Key Quotes
> "此特匹夫之勇耳。" - Han Xin's diagnosis of Xiang Yu's bravery as force without delegating capacity.

> "此所谓妇人之仁也。" - Han Xin's critique of Xiang Yu's personal kindness without reward execution.

> "今大王举而东，三秦可传檄而定也。" - The strategic conclusion that Liu Bang can open the campaign by taking the Three Qin.
