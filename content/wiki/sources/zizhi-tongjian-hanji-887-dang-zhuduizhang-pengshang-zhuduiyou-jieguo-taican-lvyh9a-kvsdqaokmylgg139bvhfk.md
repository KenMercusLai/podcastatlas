# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》887丨当猪队长碰上猪队友，结果太惨！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the failed 168 CE anti-eunuch coup immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-888-luanshi-zhizhong-zhijing-bupasi-de-ren-ll7s9e5bh8reuy4xzzjwq0psowvq|Hanji 888]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-889-weishenme-haoren-bu-changming-huohai-yi-qiannian-lt-g7qgkcf-fhy3xccecjagwqhku|Hanji 889]]. It follows [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]]'s attempted palace rescue of [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]], [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]]'s public accusation, the eunuch side's use of forged authority and military pressure, and Dou Wu's final collapse.

The episode's interpretive center is a timing and execution failure under lethal palace politics. The host argues source-scopedly that Chen Fan saw the emergency more clearly than Dou Wu, while Dou's hesitation, poor secrecy, and incomplete action gave [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]], Wang Fu, and the eunuch side time to reverse the field, mobilize troops, and expand punishment from the coup leaders to families, followers, students, old subordinates, and recommended officials.

## Key Claims

- In 168 CE, Chen Fan leads officials plus more than eighty students and followers through Chengming Gate to support Dou Wu, calls Dou a restorer-protector of the state, and challenges the claim that Dou is rebelling.
- Wang Fu answers by attacking Dou Wu's family privilege, wealth, and factional conduct, then orders armed men to seize Chen Fan; Chen is sent to the Northern Prison and beaten to death the same day.
- The host treats Chen Fan's earlier demand to kill Zheng Sa and similar eunuch-linked figures immediately as a hard-line reading of crisis tempo, while Dou Wu's procedural hesitation is presented as a fatal delay.
- Cao Jie and the eunuch side use the information gap around Zhang Huan's return to Luoyang, forged imperial authority, Zhou Jing's temporary command, and the remaining Five Camps troops to build a counterforce.
- Wang Fu's battlefield shouting frames Dou Wu as a rebel and promises rewards to defectors, helping turn the Northern Army away from Dou between dawn and breakfast.
- Dou Wu and his nephew Dou Shao flee after the line collapses, then die by suicide; their heads are displayed at Luoyang's douting.
- The purge expands beyond the core plot: Dou-family kin, retainers, guests, and close associates are killed or exiled; Liu Yu and Feng Shu are exterminated; Liu Shu and Wei Lang die by suicide under accusation; Empress Dowager Dou Miao is moved to Nangong; and officials, students, followers, and former subordinates tied to Chen Fan and Dou Wu are removed and barred from office.
- The source's "臣不密则失身" frame makes secrecy, decisiveness, and role-capacity the episode's main political lesson, not a general defense of reckless violence.
