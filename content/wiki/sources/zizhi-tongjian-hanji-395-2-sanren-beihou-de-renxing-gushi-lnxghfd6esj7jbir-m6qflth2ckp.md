# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》395-2｜三人背后的人性故事

## Summary
This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode uses [[JeanPaulSartre|Jean-Paul Sartre / 萨特]]'s [[NoExit|No Exit / 《禁闭》]] to explain [[OtherAsHell|他人即地狱]] as a damaged relationship condition rather than a blanket rejection of human connection. The host emphasizes that the play's hell has no torture devices or executioner: Garcin, Ines, and Estelle become one another's spiritual punishment through mutual exposure, desire, contempt, and dependence on judgment.

## Key Claims
- [[NoExit|《禁闭》]] makes hell relational: three dead characters are trapped in a room where they cannot sleep, leave, possess one another, or escape one another's gaze.
- The episode reads Garcin, Ines, and Estelle as people who hide their pasts at first, then expose and attack one another after their motives and guilt become visible.
- The famous line "他人就是地狱" is not treated as meaning all other people are inherently unbearable.
- [[OtherAsHell|他人即地狱]] names a condition where relations with others have gone bad and a person becomes overdependent on other people's judgment.
- The source's practical conclusion is that freedom and changed behavior matter because a person may not be able to abolish others' gaze, but can resist being wholly confined by it.
- The episode's title invokes the Hanji series, but the visible transcript does not develop a direct [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] historical case.

## Key Quotes
> "他人就是地狱." - the line the episode uses to center Sartre's relational hell.

> "争取自由非常重要." - the host's closing practical interpretation.
