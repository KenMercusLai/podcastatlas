# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》377-1｜李陵请战匈奴，汉武帝为何不愿他当前锋？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] short episode supplies the deployment prequel to [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-377-2-yi-yi-di-shi-zhanli-baobiao-de-shashen-liling-likohxfnnybzg6l-q_dadkjeujri|Hanji 377-2]]. It presents [[LiLingWesternHan|李陵]] as an accomplished cavalry archer and troop leader shaped by the reputation of his grandfather [[LiGuangHanGeneral|李广]], then shows him rejecting a grain-transport assignment and volunteering to draw off [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] forces with five thousand infantry because [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] says no cavalry is available.

The decisive pre-battle failure is inside the Han command structure. Wudi approves the infantry plan and assigns [[LuBodeWesternHan|路博德]] as support, but Lu does not want the subordinate role and recommends waiting until spring because autumn favors Xiongnu horses. Wudi interprets the memorial as evidence that Li Ling has lost courage and induced Lu to intervene, then separates their missions and sends Li Ling out in September without the originally described support arrangement.

## Key Claims

- In 99 BCE, [[LiGuangliWesternHan|李广利]] returns from a Tianshan-area campaign with heavy losses after [[ZhaoChongguoWesternHan|赵充国]] leads a breakout and is promoted for his wounds and service.
- [[GongsunAoWesternHan|公孙敖]] and Lu Bode later rendezvous at Zhuotu Mountain but find no result, reinforcing the campaign's uneven multi-route setting.
- Li Ling is introduced as an attendant skilled in riding and archery who treats soldiers and worthy people well; Wudi makes him Cavalry Commandant and has him train five thousand Danyang and Chu men in archery near Jiuquan and Zhangye.
- Wudi initially assigns Li Ling to transport provisions for Li Guangli rather than command the main attack, while the host infers that Wudi is prioritizing Li Guangli's opportunity to win merit.
- Li Ling rejects a purely logistical role and offers to lead five thousand infantry toward the southern side of Lanyu Mountain to divide Xiongnu attention away from Li Guangli.
- Wudi first cites the lack of horses, then accepts Li Ling's infantry-only proposal and orders Lu Bode to support him on the route.
- Lu Bode's request to delay operations until spring is militarily intelligible as seasonal caution but also presented as resistance to serving beneath Li Ling.
- Wudi attributes Lu's memorial to Li Ling's supposed fear, changes both commands, and sends Li Ling from Juyan toward the Longle River and Dong Junji Mountain without the initially described support plan.

## Key Quotes

> "无所事骑，臣愿以少击众。" - the episode's rendering of Li Ling volunteering to fight without cavalry.

> "上怒，以为陵悔不欲出，而教博德上书。" - the command misattribution that changes the deployment.

## Connections

- [[LiLingWesternHan|李陵]] - central officer whose lineage, troop leadership, volunteer request, and unsupported infantry deployment begin the tragedy.
- [[LiGuangHanGeneral|李广]] - grandfather whose martial reputation and repeated desire for battle frame Li Ling's self-conception.
- [[LiGuangliWesternHan|李广利]] - main expedition commander whose campaign and intended merit position Li Ling in a supply and diversion role.
- [[LuBodeWesternHan|路博德]] - intended support commander whose memorial changes the deployment after Wudi misattributes its motive.
- [[ZhaoChongguoWesternHan|赵充国]] - officer whose breakout from Li Guangli's encirclement supplies an early-career valor episode.
- [[GongsunAoWesternHan|公孙敖]] - commander whose fruitless Zhuotu Mountain rendezvous forms part of the wider campaign background.
- [[CommandSynchronizationFailure|军令同步失灵]] - concept extended from the mismatch among Li Ling's request, Lu Bode's memorial, and Wudi's interpretation.
- [[TerrainFormationForceMultiplication|地形阵型战力放大]] - the next episode shows how Li Ling compensates tactically for the cavalry and reinforcement deficit established here.

## Contradictions

- Chronology mismatch: this episode explicitly places Li Ling's deployment and Li Guangli's preceding campaign in Tianhan 2 / 99 BCE, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-380-1-liling-touxiang-xiongnu-ruhe-yanbian-cheng-gengdade-beiju-ls70jnzybc7z3of-tpfbdwb9ns89|Hanji 380-1]] associates its surrounding large multi-route expedition with 97 BCE. The notes may be compressing different campaign stages; the wiki does not silently reconcile them.
- No settled contradiction was found in the core sequence from proposed support to separated deployment; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-377-2-yi-yi-di-shi-zhanli-baobiao-de-shashen-liling-likohxfnnybzg6l-q_dadkjeujri|Hanji 377-2]] begins after this episode and confirms that Li Ling ultimately has no reinforcement.
- The opening claim that heredity shapes personality, ability, interests, and even fate is a rhetorical framing device, not established evidence that Li Ling inherited a fixed destiny from Li Guang.
- Place identifications, titles, troop and casualty totals, Wudi's preference for Li Guangli's merit, Lu Bode's private motive, exact dialogue, and the causal interpretation of Wudi's anger remain episode-attributed pending primary-source verification.
