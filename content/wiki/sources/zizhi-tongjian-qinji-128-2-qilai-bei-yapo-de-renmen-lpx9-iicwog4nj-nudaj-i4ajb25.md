## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode moves from [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]]'s pleasure-seeking rule into the outbreak of the [[DazexiangUprising|大泽乡起义]]. It frames the revolt as the product of Hu Hai's untrained and irresponsible succession, [[ZhaoGao|赵高]]'s prior manipulation, harsher law, renewed construction and transport burdens, and a fatal deadline crisis for nine hundred conscripted soldiers. [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] and [[WuGuang|吴广]] turn that death trap into rebellion by killing the officers, mobilizing the soldiers, and manufacturing legitimacy through [[FuSu|扶苏]], [[XiangYan|项燕]], fish-belly writing, and fox-cry rumor.

## Key Claims
- Hu Hai is framed as Qin Shi Huang's favored youngest son but not as a prepared political heir; the source reads his accession as a bad outcome for ruler, state, officials, and people.
- After accession, Hu Hai's main political interest remains pleasure and removal of possible threats, rather than repair of the empire's widening crisis.
- Hu Hai intensifies Qin's already harsh law, continues heavy construction, accelerates [[AfangPalace|阿房宫]] after the [[QinShiHuangMausoleum|秦始皇陵]] work, and imposes new guard, supply, and transport burdens on the commanderies.
- The source treats this social pressure as the background for "官逼民反": law constrains below while rulers extract above.
- In Qin Er Shi's first year, 209 BCE, Chen Sheng from Yangcheng in Chen County and Wu Guang from Yangxia are part of a nine-hundred-person force being sent toward Yuyang when rain at Dazexiang makes the deadline impossible.
- Under the source's account of Qin law, missing the deadline means execution regardless of cause, while flight also risks death; this creates a [[FatalDeadlineRebellionTrigger|误期死局式起义触发]].
- Chen Sheng and Wu Guang kill the commanders, argue that death in a great undertaking is preferable to passive execution, and persuade the soldiers to rebel.
- The revolt uses [[RebelMandateManufacture|起义天命制造]]: the claim that Fusu and Xiang Yan still live, plus fish-belly writing and fox-cry rumor, turns ordinary desperation into a politically legible cause.
- The ending points toward [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]] fragility by noting that [[GeYing|葛婴]]'s death costs Chen Sheng a general and deepens suspicion between the ruler and subordinates.

## Key Quotes
> "官逼民反" - the episode's compressed social-causation frame for Qin's late burden and punishment environment.

> "王侯将相" - the rebel mobilization formula the source uses to summarize Chen Sheng's argument that high status can be seized.

> "陈胜王" - the manufactured sign phrase placed in a fish belly to make Chen Sheng's kingship look fated.
