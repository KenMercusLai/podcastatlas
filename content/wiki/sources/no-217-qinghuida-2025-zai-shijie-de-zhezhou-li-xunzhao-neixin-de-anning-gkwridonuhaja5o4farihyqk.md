# No.217 请回答 2025：在世界的褶皱里，寻找内心的安宁

## Summary
This annual [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] montage has [[LiuFei|刘飞]] and twelve contributors review 2025 through changes, lessons, people, places, objects, works, keywords, and questions for their future selves. Across career shifts, parenting, bereavement, retirement, health, AI use, travel, and creative work, the episode's shared movement is away from distant success symbols and toward controllable action, bodily life, relationships, direct experience, and continued accumulation under uncertainty.

## Key Claims
- Liu Fei names “充实” and “满足” as his 2025 frame because work, cities, friends, activities, and topics felt increasingly self-chosen.
- Several contributors distinguish what they can control—response, pace, narrative, boundary, practice, and health—from results or futures they cannot reliably command, extending [[ControllableLifeAnchors]].
- [[Koji]] describes moving from target-driven CEO work toward a process-driven life in media and investing, where direction and process quality matter more than short sprint metrics; this grounds [[ProcessOrientedLifeDesign]].
- [[XuTao|徐涛]] calls for seeing the “folds” of the world: direct contact with people, places, and irregular detail can resist the smoothing effect of internet and AI summaries, extending [[SituatedMediaObservation]].
- AI appears as companion, work accelerator, creative teacher, reflective questioner, and future presentation tool, but contributors also preserve boundaries around privacy, concentrated power, human direction, and mutual criticism between models.
- Parenting, grief, middle age, retirement, and job change repeatedly weaken the fantasy of a final “other shore”; current bodies, family ties, and ordinary days become part of the goal rather than merely its cost.
- Health is treated as infrastructure rather than a postponed reward: hiking, climbing, Pilates, weight loss, chronic-disease treatment, and bodily limits change what continued work and exploration are possible.
- [[ZhaoLina|赵丽娜]] connects post-retirement growth and public-welfare work to happiness through giving, while [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] stresses present attention and the limits of prediction.
- “继续” and “量变与质变” frame persistence without guaranteed breakthrough: repeated creation, reading, movement, and exploration may produce later thresholds, extending [[QuantityChangeQualityChange]].
- The episode is a cross-section of personal testimony rather than a controlled comparison; its claims describe contributors' 2025 interpretations and should not be generalized to a whole generation.

## Key Quotes
> “没有彼岸了。” — Yang Tiannan's rejection of treating current life only as a corridor to symbolic success.

> “世界的褶皱。” — Xu Tao's phrase for irregular detail flattened by online and AI-mediated information.

> “继续。” — Xu Tao's annual keyword for climbing, exploring, creating, and reading without requiring immediate breakthrough.
