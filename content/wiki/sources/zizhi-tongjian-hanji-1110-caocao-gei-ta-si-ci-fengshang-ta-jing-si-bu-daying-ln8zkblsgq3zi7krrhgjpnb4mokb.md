# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1110丨曹操给他四次封赏，他竟死不答应？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode revisits [[TianChou|田畴]]'s guidance of [[CaoCao|曹操]] through [[LulongRoadLateHan|卢龙道]] during the Wuhuan campaign, then focuses on Tian Chou's refusal of the resulting marquisate. Cao Cao repeatedly presses the award because unrewarded service could weaken the public credibility of merit recognition, while Tian Chou treats his contribution as service to the state rather than a route to title.

After four orders and an intervention by [[XiahouDun|夏侯惇]], Tian Chou threatens death rather than accept the enfeoffment. Cao Cao finally compromises by appointing him yilang, preserving recognition and advisory standing without forcing the original title; the episode uses the conflict to expose the difference between an individual's motive and an organization's need to make contribution visibly consequential.

## Key Claims
- After the Wuhuan campaign, Cao Cao credits Tian Chou's proposal to use Lulong Road for the successful surprise approach and seeks to reward him.
- After the Red Cliffs defeat, Cao Cao again orders that Tian Chou receive a tinghou title with a five-hundred-household fief.
- Tian Chou says he offered the plan to serve the state rather than to obtain a marquisate and refuses the award.
- Cao Cao worries that allowing major contribution to go unrewarded will discourage other capable people and weaken the merit-reward signal.
- Cao Cao issues four reward orders and asks Xiahou Dun, who is close to Tian Chou, to persuade him.
- Tian Chou responds that continued pressure would make him resist with his life, showing that the refusal is a substantive principle rather than ritual modesty.
- Cao Cao compromises by appointing Tian Chou yilang, an advisory office the episode describes as lacking substantial executive duty.
- Tian Chou later moves his followers and kin to Ye and dies of illness at forty-six.

## Key Quotes
> "自己献策是为了报国，并不是为了封侯" - the episode's account of Tian Chou's reason for refusing the marquisate.

> "如果连他也继续逼迫，自己便以死相抗" - the episode's account of Tian Chou's answer when Xiahou Dun urges acceptance.
