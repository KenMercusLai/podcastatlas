# Alice forgiven? Jensen Huang back at Nvidia

## Summary
This [[EconomistPodcasts]] episode examines three forms of institutional resilience. It reports [[JensenHuang|Jensen Huang]] returning to lead [[Nvidia]] after political intervention and an extraordinary market reaction, while unresolved conduct, export, national-security, and board-accountability questions remain. It then connects the return of endemic measles transmission in the [[UnitedStates]] to vaccination gaps, fading disease memory, misinformation, and weakened public-health capacity before profiling composer [[MaxRichter|Max Richter]] and his attempt to answer dystopian material with emotionally direct music and consolation.

## Key Claims
- The episode says Huang returned shortly after resigning even though Nvidia's board had earlier intended to pursue long-term succession; President [[DonaldTrump]] acknowledged speaking to the board, and Vice-President JD Vance was also reported to have intervened.
- Nvidia's share price reportedly rose by more than one-third after the return, adding close to $1 trillion in market value and reinforcing the episode's claim that investors treat Huang as unusually difficult to replace.
- The return resolved immediate leadership uncertainty without resolving allegations involving subordinate relationships or a Mega Speed executive, national-security questions, congressional scrutiny, or the governance consequences of executive-branch pressure on a public-company board.
- The company was described as having much of its production sold for several quarters and expecting 75% growth in the following year, while suppliers and customers may still seek alternatives and China-linked demand remains exposed to scrutiny.
- The Pan-American Health Organization reportedly concluded that endemic measles transmission had been re-established in the United States, Bolivia, and Mexico; the episode says the United States recorded more than 5,000 cases and 11 deaths during the year.
- Measles control requires roughly 95% vaccination coverage, while two-thirds of U.S. counties were said to have insufficient coverage in the 2024 school year and pandemic-era declines had not recovered.
- The source emphasizes severe complications, immune amnesia, delayed fatal brain disease, interstate transmission, adult immunity gaps, high outbreak-containment costs, and the need for a multi-year restoration effort.
- It connects the reversal to a roughly 30% reduction in [[CentersForDiseaseControlAndPrevention|CDC]] staffing since early 2025 and to vaccine misinformation attributed to senior officials, including [[RobertFKennedyJr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] and Trump.
- Richter's work combines classical instrumentation, minimalism, electronic sound, and broad popular accessibility; the episode uses *On the Nature of Daylight*, *Sleep*, *Hamnet*, and *While the Flood Rages* to illustrate that reach.
- *While the Flood Rages*, derived from music for a ballet based on [[MargaretAtwood|Margaret Atwood]]'s dystopian fiction, answers ecological destruction and corporate greed with color, consolation, and a qualified hope that ordinary human qualities can survive catastrophe.
- Richter's emotional directness and brand partnerships expand his audience while also producing criticism that his work can soothe more readily than it challenges.
