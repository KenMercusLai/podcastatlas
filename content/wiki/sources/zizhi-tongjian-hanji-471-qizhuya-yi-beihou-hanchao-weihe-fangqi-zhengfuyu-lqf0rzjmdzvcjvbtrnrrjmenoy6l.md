# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》471｜《弃珠崖议》背后，汉朝为何会放弃征服欲？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Chuyuan 3 / 46 BCE under [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]], between the Xiao Wangzhi death case and the later Hanji 472 Hainan-history follow-up. It centers [[JiaJuanzhiWesternHan|贾捐之]]'s "弃珠崖议": after repeated unrest in [[ZhuyaCommandery|珠崖郡]] and Dan'er, Jia argues that a distant island campaign would consume troops, money, and famine-era attention for little stable return.

The durable synthesis is that Zhuya abolition is framed as [[CapacityBoundFrontierCommitment|capacity-bound frontier commitment]] argued through classical precedent, welfare opportunity cost, logistics, and administrative durability. The host then pushes back from a modern territorial-security angle, stressing population, territory, and [[StrategicDepthDefense|strategic depth]] as state assets, so the episode preserves both the Han court's capacity logic and the later warning against casual territorial abandonment.

## Key Claims

- Han Wudi's post-Nanyue administration of Hainan through Zhuya and Dan'er produces repeated unrest, official deaths, and expensive suppression rather than stable incorporation.
- Jia Juanzhi opposes Yuan's impulse toward a large punitive expedition by arguing that ancient sage-kings did not force rule over places unwilling to accept governance.
- Jia contrasts Qin and Han Wudi expansion costs with Wen-di-style restraint, claiming that distant conquest can hollow the interior and intensify commoner burden.
- Jia foregrounds Guandong poverty, famine, family breakup, and people selling children as reasons not to divert resources toward a remote island campaign.
- The memorial devalues Zhuya's resources and stresses logistical danger: humid climate, insects, snakes, sea crossing, and heavy troop attrition.
- Yu Dingguo supports Jia's proposal while Cheng Wannian favors suppression, and Yuan ultimately abandons Zhuya and Dan'er.
- The host does not fully endorse the abandonment logic, instead treating territory, population, and depth as long-run foundations of national security.

## Key Quotes

> "此非所以救饥馑、保全元元也" - Jia Juanzhi's famine-and-people-centered objection as rendered by the episode.

> "土地永远是一个国家强大起来的底牌" - the host's modern territorial-security counterweight.
