# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》715丨史上最智慧的一次夺权

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianwu 13 / 37 CE immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-716-luanshi-zhizhong-lvfa-you-duo-zhongyao-lqxuinbrl-rop8d9yxgwx9z4fr4i|Hanji 716]]. It follows [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] after territorial unification as he reduces royal titles, expands rewards for founding merit, and turns military command into honor, income, and limited court access without leaving commanders in control of the army.

The durable synthesis is that Liu Xiu's "夺权" is a postwar centralization settlement rather than a massacre of the founding coalition. [[DengYuEasternHan|邓禹]], [[JiaFuEasternHan|贾复]], [[GengYanEasternHan|耿弇]], and other meritorious commanders surrender seals or withdraw into honored marquis status, [[WuHanEasternHan|吴汉]] receives high ceremony after returning from Shu, and [[DouRongEasternHan|窦融]] shows the opposite risk: a newly attached high official tries to withdraw too far, while Liu Xiu insists on retaining him.

## Key Claims

- The episode frames ruler-versus-founding-merit conflict as a recurring historical problem, contrasting Liu Xiu's settlement with more violent or incomplete later examples.
- In 37 CE, Liu Xiu lowers several Liu-family kings to marquis or duke status, reducing residual royal territorial power while preserving inheritable honor.
- Zhang Chun of Fuping receives an exceptional arrangement because Liu Xiu treats long service in palace security as reason not to erase the title entirely.
- Liu Xiu rewards the founding coalition at scale: 365 meritorious officials receive expanded fiefs, and 45 additional actors receive grants through outer-relative or special-favor channels.
- The episode presents the death or illness of many Yuntai-level commanders by Jianwu 13 as a background condition that reduces the difficulty of postwar military centralization.
- Deng Yu, Jia Fu, Geng Yan, and other commanders understand Liu Xiu's postwar preference for ending war and governing through ritual and civilian order, so they surrender military authority voluntarily.
- Honored withdrawal is institutionalized through marquis income, special-entry status, and occasional court participation without ordinary command or administrative power.
- Dou Rong's repeated attempts to resign office and title show how a non-original follower could feel overexposed after entering court, while Liu Xiu's refusal keeps him inside the settlement.
- The closing Xiongnu raid in Hedong shows that the Eastern Han realm is more unified than before but not fully free of frontier danger.

## Key Quotes

> "受爵不受官" - the host's summary of Liu Xiu's treatment of meritorious commanders.

> "用礼乐教化百姓" - the source's phrase for the postwar governing turn Deng Yu and Jia Fu perceive.

> "史上最智慧的一次夺权" - the title-level judgment on Liu Xiu's smooth recovery of effective power.
