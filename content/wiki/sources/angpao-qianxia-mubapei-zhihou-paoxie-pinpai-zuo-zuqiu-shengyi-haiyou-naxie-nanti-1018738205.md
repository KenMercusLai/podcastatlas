# 昂跑签下姆巴佩之后，跑鞋品牌做足球生意还有哪些难题？

## Summary
This [[ShengdongZaokafei|声动早咖啡]] episode explains why [[OnRunning|On Running]] is using a reported long-term partnership with [[KylianMbappe|Kylian Mbappe]] to enter football as its premium-running growth slows. The move combines LightSpray product transfer, athlete-assisted development, football apparel, and lifestyle footwear, but [[FootballFootwearMarketEntry|football-footwear entry]] requires much more than one famous endorser: credible products, sustained ecosystem spending, distribution, and conversion beyond narrow on-pitch use. Side items cover holiday travel, [[Meta]] AI hardware, [[IQIYI|iQIYI]]'s AI-assisted and medium-length drama strategy, and H&M's cost-led profit improvement.

## Key Claims
- Holiday travel demand reportedly favored long-haul domestic routes, small premium groups, and destinations in northwest China, Central Asia, Southeast Asia, and Europe as travelers combined the Mid-Autumn break with additional leave.
- [[Meta]] showed a watch-sized agent device, audio-only AI glasses, and lighter VR glasses, extending [[AIPlusTerminals]] while leaving the core product-fit question unresolved: the devices still need to do something a smartphone cannot replace.
- [[IQIYI|iQIYI]] is using AI across production and testing three-to-eight-hour "medium-length" dramas as a possible middle ground between padded long series and fast but unstable short-drama production.
- H&M's reported June-August profit improvement came mainly from a U.S. tariff refund rather than strong sales growth; logistics costs, China store contraction, inventory control, discount reduction, and competition from Zara, Shein, and Temu remain material pressures.
- [[OnRunning|On Running]] reportedly signed [[KylianMbappe|Kylian Mbappe]] after offering a package involving equity and performance-linked bonuses in addition to base compensation; the exact economics and term were not publicly disclosed, although reporting cited a roughly ten-year agreement.
- On's stated category logic combines slowing revenue growth, premium-price discipline, a footwear-heavy sales mix, and a need for new consumers rather than relying on discount-led expansion.
- The company plans to adapt LightSpray construction to football boots and extend the category into jerseys and everyday sports-lifestyle shoes, making the move broader than a single professional product.
- Mbappe is expected to participate in product development and testing, echoing On's earlier long-term product collaboration with Roger Federer rather than functioning only as an advertising face.
- [[Adidas]], [[Nike]], and [[Puma]] have built football positions through decades of tournaments, clubs, national teams, athletes, marketing, and product investment, creating a system-level barrier that a single endorsement cannot quickly reproduce.
- Under Armour, New Balance, and Skechers are used as cautionary cases: star partnerships and campaign visibility do not guarantee category share or commercial return.
- Studded football boots have a narrower everyday use case than running or tennis shoes, so Mbappe's attention may not convert directly into purchases without credible performance, football-ecosystem presence, and broader lifestyle products.

## Key Quotes
> “无法保证关注度转化为足球鞋销量” — the episode's central qualification of celebrity reach.

> “并不只是明星代言的曝光效果” — the closing distinction between attention and a sustainable football business.
