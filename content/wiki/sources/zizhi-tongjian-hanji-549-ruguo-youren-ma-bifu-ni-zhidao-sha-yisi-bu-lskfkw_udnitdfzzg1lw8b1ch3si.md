# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》549丨如果有人骂鄙夫，你知道啥意思不？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Yuanyan 1 / 12 BCE under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]], before the later Chunyu Zhang, Wang Mang, Zhai Fangjin, and Chengdi death sequence. It centers on [[ZhuYunWesternHan|朱云]]'s public request for the imperial sword to execute [[ZhangYuWesternHan|张瑜]], the broken-balustrade scene, and [[XinQingjiWesternHan|辛庆忌]]'s bloody intercession that saves Zhu Yun.

The durable synthesis is that Chengdi's reign contains a real remonstrance-tolerance layer alongside later responsibility evasion and palace scandal. Zhu Yun's speech turns the Confucian warning against `鄙夫` into a death-facing accusation against court complacency, while Xin Qingji gives Chengdi a path from rage to clemency; Chengdi's later choice to keep the broken railing makes the episode a remembered emblem of direct speech.

## Key Claims

- Zhu Yun asks to meet Chengdi in person and, before assembled high officials, accuses court ministers of holding rank without correcting the ruler or benefiting the people.
- Zhu Yun uses the `Lunyu` line about `鄙夫不可与事君` to define a dangerous official type: hungry for gain, afraid to lose status, flattering upward, and unwilling to speak truth.
- When Chengdi asks whom Zhu Yun means, Zhu Yun names Zhang Yu, Chengdi's teacher, turning general moral criticism into a direct attack on a protected figure.
- Chengdi initially orders Zhu Yun seized, and Zhu Yun breaks the palace railing while invoking loyal martyrs such as Long Feng and Bigan.
- Xin Qingji removes his cap and seal, kowtows until bleeding, and argues that Zhu Yun should be spared whether his words are correct or merely upright.
- Chengdi accepts the intervention, abandons execution, and later orders the broken railing preserved as an honor to direct ministers.
- The episode also records same-year side notices: the Xiongnu chanyu dies before entering the frontier, Zhang Fang is again sent away after Wang Zhengjun objects, Chengdi returns to Confucian study, and Xin Qingji dies after a career associated with frontier prestige.
