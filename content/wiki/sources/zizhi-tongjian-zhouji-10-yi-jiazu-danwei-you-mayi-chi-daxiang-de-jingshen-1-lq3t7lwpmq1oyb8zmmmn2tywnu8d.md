# 《资治通鉴·周纪》10丨 以家族单位 有蚂蚁吃大象的精神（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode begins [[ZhouLiewang|周烈王]]'s reign by reading [[HanState|韩国]]'s destruction of [[ZhengState|郑国]] as a case of [[GenerationalFamilyStrategy|multi-generation family strategy]]. It then supplements [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] with [[Shiji|《史记》]]'s account of [[TaishiDan|太史旦]] visiting [[QinXianGong|秦献公]], using [[QinState|秦国]]'s origin story and [[KinshipLegitimacyDiplomacy|kinship legitimacy diplomacy]] to explain why Zhou might try to pull Qin into a pro-Zhou political frame.

## Key Claims
- In 375 BCE, [[ZhouLiewang|周烈王]]'s first year brings a solar eclipse, [[HanState|韩国]]'s destruction of [[ZhengState|郑国]] and move to Xinzheng, and the death of [[ZhaoJinghou|赵靖侯]] followed by [[ZhaoChenghou|赵成侯]]'s accession.
- The episode argues that Han's conquest of Zheng was not a sudden one-ruler achievement. It presents [[HanKangzi|韩康子]], [[HanWuzi|韩武子]], [[HanJinghou|韩景侯]], [[HanLiehou|韩烈侯]], [[HanWenhou|韩文侯]], and [[HanAihou|韩哀侯]] as a lineage pursuing one strategic direction across generations.
- [[StrategicCapitalRelocation|Strategic capital relocation]] is the practical mechanism in the Han story: moving from Pingyang toward Yiyang, Yangdi, and finally Xinzheng lets Han tighten pressure on Zheng over time.
- In 374 BCE, [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] has no entry for Zhou Lie Wang's second year, so the episode turns to [[Shiji|《史记》]] for [[TaishiDan|太史旦]]'s Qin visit.
- Taishi Dan tells [[QinXianGong|秦献公]] that Zhou and Qin once shared an origin, split apart, would reunite after five hundred years, and would produce a hegemon seventeen years after reunion; the host reads this less as pure prophecy than as political relationship-building.
- The Qin-origin digression traces Qin from [[QinFeizi|非子]] and [[ZhouXiaowang|周孝王]] through [[QinXiangGong|秦襄公]], [[ZhouYouwang|周幽王]], [[ZhouPingwang|周平王]], and [[QinWengong|秦文公]], stressing Qin's movement from attached fief to recognized regional power.
- The episode speculates that Taishi Dan might be [[Laozi|老子]] or at least a Laozi-like wise archivist, but keeps the identification tentative.
- In 373 BCE, [[YanState|燕国]] defeats [[QiState|齐国]] at Linghu; [[LuState|鲁国]] and [[WeyState|卫国]] also attack Qi; [[YanXiGong|燕僖公]], [[SongXiugong|宋休公]], and [[WeyShenggong|卫声公]] die and are succeeded by [[YanHuanGong|燕桓公]], [[SongPigong|宋辟公]], and [[WeyChenggong|卫成公]].
- In 372 BCE, [[ZhaoState|赵国]] attacks [[WeiState|魏国]] and takes seventy-three "du-bi" settlements, while Wei later defeats Zhao at Beiling; the host stresses that "都鄙" should be read as towns, suburbs, and countryside rather than seventy-three major cities.

## Key Quotes
> "以家族为单位，有蚂蚁吃大象的精神" - the source's label for Han's slow, lineage-level pressure on Zheng.

> "都，是大城市；鄙，就是郊区农村" - the host's gloss on the scale of Zhao's captured settlements.

> "周和秦本来是合在一起的" - the Taishi Dan line the host reads as a Zhou-Qin relationship appeal.

## Contradictions
- No direct contradiction found. The source extends the existing Zhouji branch from [[ZhouAnWang|周安王]] into [[ZhouLiewang|周烈王]] and turns the earlier [[SmallStateBufferDiplomacy|Zheng buffer-state]] thread into an endpoint: Han finally absorbs Zheng.
- [[TaishiDan|太史旦]]'s possible identity with [[Laozi|老子]] is recorded as source speculation, not as a settled identification.
- Because 卫国 and 魏国 share the same common pinyin form, the source uses [[WeyState|卫国 / Wey State]] for 卫 and keeps [[WeiState|魏国 / Wei State]] for 魏 to avoid a page-name collision.
