# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》962丨董卓与吕布，到底为什么决裂？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the pre-assassination cause chain before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-963-shishang-bei-diantian-deng-de-diyiren-shi-ta-lkpkhlpkv3xpw-hur4b7sh-3druk|Hanji 963]]: [[DongZhuo|董卓]] centralizes power through kin, ritual usurpation, and [[MeiwuFortress|郿坞]], while his own violent temper turns [[LyuBu|吕布]] from trusted guard into a frightened liability. [[WangYunLateHan|王允]] and allies such as [[HuangWanLateHan|黄琬]], [[ShiSunRuiLateHan|士孙瑞]], and [[YangZanLateHan|杨瓒]] need Lü Bu because Dong Zhuo's personal security is hard to penetrate. The episode's durable contribution is [[PersonalGuardAttackSurface|贴身侍卫反噬]]: the person closest to protecting a ruler can become the route through which fear, resentment, and persuasion reach him.

## Key Claims

- The opening frames interpersonal rupture through interests, boundaries, and trust: a seemingly close relationship can break when benefits, safety, and proportion no longer hold.
- In 192 CE, Dong Zhuo's power is shown as increasingly private and dynastic: a son born to a concubine is immediately ennobled, [[DongMinLateHan|董旻]] receives a high military office, and [[DongHuangLateHan|董璜]] controls central troops.
- Dong Zhuo is presented as usurping the visible forms of imperial rank while hollowing out [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]'s practical authority.
- The court bureaucracy reports through Dong Zhuo's 太师府, making formal Han offices dependent on his personal command channel.
- Dong Zhuo's construction of [[MeiwuFortress|郿坞]] with extremely high walls and long-term grain stores shows a two-track plan: dominate if successful, survive behind walls if not.
- Dong Zhuo's arbitrary killings and anger create a palace environment where subordinates fear small verbal mistakes.
- Wang Yun, Huang Wan, Shi Sunrui, Yang Zan, and other officials plot against Dong Zhuo, but the source says killing him is difficult because he is martial and closely guarded.
- Lü Bu matters because Dong Zhuo relies on him as a strong rider, archer, and personal guard.
- The professed father-son relationship between Dong Zhuo and Lü Bu does not create reliable safety; Dong Zhuo's thrown hand halberd teaches Lü Bu that intimacy can still turn lethal.
- Lü Bu's affair with a woman near Dong Zhuo adds fear of exposure to earlier resentment.
- Wang Yun's persuasion works by separating kinship language from actual protection: Lü Bu and Dong Zhuo are not blood kin, and Dong Zhuo did not act like a father when angered.
- The source therefore backfills Hanji 963's assassination: before guard placement can become an [[AssassinationAccessCredential|assassination access credential]], Lü Bu must first be moved from loyal protector to unsafe insider.
