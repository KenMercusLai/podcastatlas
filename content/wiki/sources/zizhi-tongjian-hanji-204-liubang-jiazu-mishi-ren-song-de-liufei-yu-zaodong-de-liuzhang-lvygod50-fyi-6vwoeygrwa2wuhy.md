# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》204｜刘邦家族秘事：认怂的刘肥与躁动的刘章

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode presents [[LiuZhangChengyangKingWesternHan|刘章]], then Zhu Xu marquis, as an early public challenger to Lü-family power. At a palace banquet he obtains permission to enforce drinking rules under military law, sings an exclusionary farming song, and kills a Lü relative who leaves the feast; the episode treats the act as a warning that reassures the politically pressured Liu clan.

The second political line joins [[ChenPing|陈平]] and [[ZhouBo|周勃]]. [[LuJia|陆贾]] reads Chen Ping's anxiety over Lü-family expansion and the young ruler, then advises him to deepen cooperation with Zhou Bo; reciprocal gifts create a working relationship later important to the anti-Lü coalition. The episode closes with the death of Yan king Liu Jian, a psychologically framed account of [[LiuChangHuainanKing|刘长]]'s childhood, and a Han expedition under Zhou Zao against [[Nanyue|南越]].

## Key Claims

- Liu Zhang, identified as [[LiuFeiQiKing|刘肥]]'s son and a twenty-year-old Zhu Xu marquis, resents Lü-family pressure on the Liu clan while remaining favored by [[LuZhi|吕雉]] and connected to the Lü household by marriage.
- At a palace banquet Liu Zhang asks to enforce the drinking order under military law; after obtaining permission, he sings a farming song about removing unlike plants and kills an intoxicated Lü relative who leaves the feast.
- The episode reads the song and killing as deliberate political intimidation. Lu Zhi is angered but does not punish Liu Zhang, while Lü relatives become wary and Liu-clan figures take confidence from his action.
- Chen Ping worries that Lü-family power and a young ruler could produce a crisis but cannot identify a safe response on his own.
- Lu Jia diagnoses the concern and argues that political stability requires the chancellor and military commander to coordinate; he recommends that Chen Ping strengthen his relationship with Zhou Bo.
- Chen Ping reportedly sends Zhou Bo gold and valuable gifts for his birthday, Zhou Bo reciprocates, and their cultivated relationship becomes an early foundation for later joint action.
- Yan king Liu Jian dies, his posthumous son is killed on Lu Zhi's order in the episode, and the lack of an heir leads to abolition of his kingdom.
- The source describes Liu Chang as the surviving younger son of Liu Bang whose mother died after giving birth in confinement, but it additionally attributes her death to anger and suicide and interprets his later anger through childhood trauma.
- The court sends Zhou Zao against Nanyue after [[ZhaoTuo|赵佗]] claims an imperial title and attacks Han territory; the episode names Panyu as Nanyue's capital but stops before the campaign outcome.

- The farming song's anti-Lü target, the identity and status of the killed guest, Liu Zhang's precise authority, and the reactions of Lu Zhi, Lü relatives, and Liu-clan officials remain episode-attributed.
- Lu Jia's mind-reading, all dialogue, gift amounts, reciprocal intent, and the claim that the exchange directly created the later alliance remain source-scoped; the later collaboration does not by itself prove the episode's private causal sequence.
- Liu Jian's son's birth and killing, abolition procedure, Liu Bang's attitude toward Liu Chang, the trauma explanation for Liu Chang's later behavior, Zhou Zao's commission, Nanyue's alleged incursion, and exact chronology require comparison with transmitted histories.
