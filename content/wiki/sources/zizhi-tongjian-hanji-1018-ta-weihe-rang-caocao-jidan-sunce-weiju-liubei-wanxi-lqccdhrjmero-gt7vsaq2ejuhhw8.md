# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1018丨他为何让曹操忌惮、孙策畏惧、刘备惋惜？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode uses [[ChenDeng|陈登]]'s handling of [[LyuBu|吕布]] in 197 CE to connect late-Han politics with an interest-based reading of human behavior. [[CaoCao|曹操]] rewards Chen Deng and [[ChenGui|陈珪]] but withholds the [[Xuzhou|徐州]] governorship Lü Bu wanted, and Chen Deng turns Cao Cao's mistrust into the idiom-like diagnosis [[JiFuBaoYang|饥附饱扬]]. The second half shows [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s coalition against Lü Bu collapsing after Chen Gui and Lü Bu exploit the shallow interest alignment of [[HanXianLateHan|韩暹]] and [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]], then closes with [[GaoShun|高顺]] as the loyal adviser whose discipline and warnings Lü Bu cannot fully use.

## Key Claims
- The opening philosophical frame treats interest, position, and changing force relations as unavoidable facts of human action rather than as moral impurities outside politics.
- [[ChenDeng|陈登]] returns from [[CaoCao|曹操]] with appointments for himself and [[ChenGui|陈珪]], but not with the [[Xuzhou|徐州]] governor title [[LyuBu|吕布]] expected.
- Chen Deng survives Lü Bu's anger by reporting Cao Cao's "raise a tiger" judgment in a way that lets Lü Bu hear recognition rather than humiliation.
- [[JiFuBaoYang|饥附饱扬]] is used to describe interest-dependent attachment: the dangerous actor stays close while needy and leaves once secure.
- [[YuanShu|袁术]] attacks Lü Bu through [[ZhangXunYuanShu|张勋]], [[QiaoRui|桥蕤]], [[HanXianLateHan|韩暹]], and [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]] after Lü Bu breaks the marriage alliance.
- [[ChenGui|陈珪]] argues that Han Xian, Yang Feng, and Yuan Shu lack durable shared interests, so their coalition can be broken before its larger troop count decides the campaign.
- Lü Bu's letter to Han Xian and Yang Feng joins Han-court legitimacy to material spoils, reframing defection from Yuan Shu as both righteous and profitable.
- When Han Xian and Yang Feng turn, [[ZhangXunYuanShu|张勋]]'s force is caught unprepared, Yuan Shu loses commanders, and Lü Bu can raid toward Shouchun without needing a direct siege.
- [[ZhangBaLateHan|张霸]]'s refusal to share promised supplies exposes a different limit: Lü Bu can chase immediate goods but damage his standing when the target refuses.
- [[GaoShun|高顺]] and the "陷阵营" show that Lü Bu has disciplined loyal talent available, but his unstable trust and inability to receive advice keep that asset from becoming durable state capacity.

## Key Quotes
> "饥附饱扬" - the episode's compact phrase for need-based attachment and departure after security.

> "如养虎，当饱其肉" - Chen Deng's reported image for how to handle Lü Bu.

> "心无谦卑，日伴高人莫助" - the closing maxim applied to Lü Bu's inability to benefit from Gao Shun.
