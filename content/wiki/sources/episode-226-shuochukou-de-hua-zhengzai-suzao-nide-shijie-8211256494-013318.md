# Episode 226: 说出口的话，正在塑造你的世界

## Summary
This [[ChizaoGengxin|迟早更新]] episode is a crossover with [[QiaQiaXiaoBao|恰恰小豹]] about [[BirdsongPlaybackEthics|birdsong playback / 声诱]] in birdwatching. [[RenNing]] shifts the issue from a simple yes/no rule into a wider question of [[HumanExtractionPosture|how people carry themselves after knowingly taking from or disturbing nature]], then uses hunting ritual, [[ItadakimasuEverydayRitual|いただきます]], [[Confucius]], [[JLAustin]], [[LionelTrilling]], [[MichelFoucault]], and [[JeanJacquesRousseau]] to argue that words and rituals can slow action, preserve ambivalence, and train future restraint.

## Key Claims
- [[BirdsongPlaybackEthics]] contains two different questions: whether playback should be done, and how a person should account for themselves if they do it anyway.
- Modern rights language is useful for prohibitions and boundaries, but the episode argues that ordinary life already depends on extensive natural extraction, so ethics also needs a way to prevent numb entitlement.
- Hunting and food rituals are treated as practices that acknowledge killing, taking, and dependence without pretending the harm has disappeared.
- [[ItadakimasuEverydayRitual]] and Confucius's "尔爱其羊，我爱其礼" example make empty-looking form valuable because repeated form can preserve attention until meaning returns.
- [[SpeechActRitual]] links Japanese kotodama and Austin's performative utterances: some words matter because saying them performs a commitment, recognition, apology, or procedure, not because they describe a hidden inner state.
- The episode qualifies modern sincerity worship through Trilling, Foucault, Rousseau, and Austin, arguing that insincere or awkward ritual speech can still bind the speaker and shape later behavior.
- [[RitualizedApologyGratitude]] is proposed for playback: after disturbing a bird, say "谢谢，抱歉，打扰你了" as a small act that does not cancel harm but makes the action less smooth and more accountable.

## Key Quotes
> "尔爱其羊，我爱其礼" - Confucian example for preserving form.

> "祭如在，祭神如神在" - ritual stance built around "as if" seriousness.

> "谢谢，抱歉，打扰你了" - the episode's proposed small ritual after playback.
