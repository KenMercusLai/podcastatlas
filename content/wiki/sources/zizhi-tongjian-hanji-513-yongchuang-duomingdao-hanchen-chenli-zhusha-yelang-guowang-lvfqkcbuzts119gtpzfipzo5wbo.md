# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》513丨勇闯夺命岛！汉臣陈立诛杀夜郎国王！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-512-xihan-chuzheng-yelang-wo-budang-dage-haoduonian-lufpmcdwb20dhx2qxfjws7-qdpkb|Hanji 512]]'s Heping 2 / 27 BCE [[YelangStateWesternHan|夜郎]] crisis under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]. [[ChenLiWesternHan|陈立]], newly appointed governor of [[ZhangkeCommanderyWesternHan|牂柯郡]], stops waiting for delayed court authorization, enters Xing's territory under an inspection pretext, and kills [[YelangKingXingWesternHan|夜郎王兴]] at Qietong Pavilion.

The durable synthesis is that fitted frontier command combines courage with method. Chen Li's killing of Xing creates immediate [[CoerciveFrontierDeterrence|强制边疆震慑]], but the episode's fuller lesson comes afterward: when Xing's father-in-law Wengzhi and son Yewu continue resistance, Chen Li cuts grain roads, uses counterintelligence, seizes water, and lets internal collapse do the work that Wan Nian's premature assault cannot.

## Key Claims

- Wang Feng appoints Chen Li, formerly Jincheng Sima, as Zhangke governor after deciding that Ye Lang's crisis should be suppressed before the western Yi consolidate.
- Chen Li identifies Xing as the key node of the rebellion, asks permission to execute him, and acts before a reply arrives because distance and court delay may let the crisis harden.
- Chen Li uses an inspection pretext and a small group of tough officials to enter Xing's territory, then summons Xing to Qietong Pavilion.
- Xing arrives with thousands of troops and allied small rulers, but Chen Li's arranged ambush kills him before his numerical advantage can organize.
- Displaying Xing's head shocks the surrounding rulers and soldiers into surrender, and Gouding and Louwo then send grain, cattle, sheep, and valuables to comfort the Han force.
- Xing's father-in-law Wengzhi and son Yewu continue resistance by gathering remnants and coercing twenty-two villages.
- Chen Li defeats the residual revolt through food-route interdiction, spies and counterintelligence, water-source seizure, and disciplined timing, while Wan Nian's rash independent attack fails.

## Key Quotes

> "为人择官者乱，任贤必治，任有效必乱" - the episode's closing personnel lesson as rendered in the transcript.

> "智仁勇，勇排在最后" - the host's contrast between Chen Li's strategic courage and Wan Nian's rash bravery.
