# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》753丨班超被告黑状

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 83-84 CE [[EmperorZhangOfHan|汉章帝]] layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-754-gudai-ban-qieting-fengyun-jieju-shuangwen-ltvymh-2r-ic5diqfh-2ic9cudq2|Hanji 754]]. It centers [[LiYiEasternHan|李毅]]'s slander against [[BanChaoEasternHan|班超]], Zhangdi's refusal to trust the accusation without behavioral evidence, and Ban Chao's choice to send Li Yi back with the Wusun hostage prince rather than turn imperial permission into private retaliation.

The second half widens the same governing profile through [[ZhengHongEasternHan|郑洪]]'s safer Jiaozhi route and tribute relief, [[WeiBiaoEasternHan|韦彪]]'s warnings about selection and Shangshu staffing, Zhangdi's limits on cruel interrogation, burden-aware southern travel orders, and [[ZhuHuiZhangEraEasternHan|朱晖]]'s resistance to [[StateMonopolyBurdenRemonstrance|official salt and junshu restoration]]. The durable synthesis is that early Zhangdi-era rule is presented as judgment under pressure: accusations, appointments, punishments, travel, and fiscal policy all require checking surface claims against conduct, public burden, and office duty.

## Key Claims

- Li Yi reaches Khotan/Yutian during a Qiuci attack on Shule, fears advancing, and turns his own mission failure into an accusation that Ban Chao is attached to foreign comfort and no longer misses the Han center.
- Emperor Zhang rejects the slander by asking what the homesick Han troops' continued loyalty says about Ban Chao's actual command credibility.
- Ban Chao sends away his wife and children to remove the accusation's pretext, but he does not use Zhangdi's order to punish Li Yi; he sends Li Yi back with the Wusun hostage prince because private satisfaction is not loyal minister conduct.
- Zheng Hong's route reform shifts Jiaozhi tribute from dangerous sea passage toward Lingling and Guiyang mountain roads, saving cost and lives before his later tribute-relief proposal reduces corvee burden during drought and frontier alarm.
- Wei Biao links personnel quality to selecting the right two-thousand-stone officials and warns that Shangshu service needs large-affair judgment rather than only legal familiarity or quick answers.
- Zhangdi's interrogation edict reinscribes legal limits on beating tools and forbids neck-clamps and needle torture, treating cruel methods as a breakdown of adjudication.
- Zhangdi's southern-tour order tries to prevent local pre-stocking, envoy flattery, and field disturbance, making imperial travel a test of whether ceremony can avoid burdening households.
- Zhu Hui opposes Zhang Lin's proposal for state salt monopoly and restoration of the Han Wudi junshu system, arguing that officials would become merchant-like and salt producers would resent impoverishment.
- Zhangdi first reacts angrily to Zhu Hui's refusal, but later releases the Shangshu officials, says opposition is welcome, and rewards Zhu Hui after the latter refuses to sign a policy he judges infeasible.

## Key Quotes

> "虽有妻子，而不顾家，何也？" - Zhangdi's evidence question: if Ban Chao were merely enjoying comfort abroad, why would homesick soldiers still follow him?

> "我以李毅诽谤我，故遣李毅。" - Ban Chao's stated reason for sending Li Yi back rather than keeping him under command.

> "非忠臣也。" - Ban Chao's boundary against using public authority for private satisfaction.
