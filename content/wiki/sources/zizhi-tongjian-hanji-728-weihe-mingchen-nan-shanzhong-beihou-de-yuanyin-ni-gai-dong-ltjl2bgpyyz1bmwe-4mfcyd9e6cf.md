# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》728丨为何名臣难善终？背后的原因你该懂

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 51-52 CE late [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-729-bangu-tadie-jing-yeshi-ge-da-wenhao-loadjkevhjie2yur8u23ox2-gmce|Hanji 729]]. It connects [[ZhaoXiEasternHan|赵熙]]'s advice to send princes to their fiefs, [[FanHongEasternHan|樊弘]]'s cautious conduct and thin-burial will, [[MaYuanEasternHan|马援]]'s warnings about high-profile retainer networks, and the prince-guest purge that follows [[LiuLiShouguangMarquisEasternHan|寿光侯刘李]]'s killing of Liu Gong under [[LiuFuPeiKingEasternHan|沛王刘辅]]'s favor.

The final branch turns crown-prince education into a public-interest personnel question. [[ZhangYiEasternHan|张义]] blocks [[YinShiEasternHan|阴识]] as the easy kinship choice for tutor, [[HuanRongEasternHan|桓荣]] becomes crown-prince shaofu, and the episode links learning, office, and imperial recognition without making the host's life-advice commentary a settled historical claim.

## Key Claims
- In 51 CE, Liu Xiu asks how to preserve the imperial enterprise, and Zhao Xi answers that princes should return to their own fiefs.
- Fan Hong's humility, procedural caution, lawful household style, and thin-burial will become a model Liu Xiu praises before the court and says he wants to follow.
- Wang Pan's move from wealth-backed local youxia into capital elite circles confirms Ma Yuan's warning that high-profile association can become fatal when status outruns caution.
- Wang Su's continued presence in princely mansions after Wang Pan's execution helps make prince-guest networks legible as rebellion risk.
- Liu Li's guest-backed murder of Liu Gong, combined with Liu Fu's favor toward Liu Li, triggers Liu Xiu's anger, Liu Fu's brief imprisonment, and a national arrest campaign against princely guests.
- The 52 CE return of adult princes to their fiefs after the guest disaster makes territorial distance a concrete risk-control response.
- Zhang Yi's objection to Yin Shi reframes crown-prince tutor selection as service to the realm rather than service to the maternal family, while Huan Rong uses imperial gifts to exhort students toward study.

## Key Quotes
> "强良者不得其死" - opening Daodejing frame used to connect forceful conduct with failure to end well.

> "富贵盈溢" - Fan Hong family-instruction phrase used to introduce the difficulty of preserving full wealth and status.

> "如果为了天下，就应选天下贤才。" - Zhang Yi's criterion for choosing the crown prince's teacher.
