# 《资治通鉴·周纪》15丨酒不醇引发的世界大战

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode covers the 355-354 BCE cluster under [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]], moving from [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]]'s answer to [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王]] about true state treasure, through failed Qin-Wei diplomacy and renewed fighting, to [[WeiState|魏国]]'s siege of [[Handan|邯郸]]. Its main contribution is double: [[TalentAsStateTreasure|以贤臣为宝]] turns the Qi-Wei hunting dialogue into a statecraft contrast, while [[LuWineHandanWarCascade|鲁酒薄而邯郸围]] explains how a small tributary insult can cascade into multi-state war.

## Key Claims
- In 355 BCE, the episode presents three notices: Qi Wei Wang and Wei Hui Wang meet while hunting, [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]] and Wei Hui Wang meet at Duping, and [[LuGonggong|鲁共公]] dies with [[LuKanggong|鲁康公]] succeeding.
- Wei Hui Wang boasts that Wei has ten luminous pearls able to light a twelve-carriage train, treating rare objects as a ruler's treasure.
- Qi Wei Wang replies that his treasures are officials such as [[TanZi|檀子]], whose border defense, public order, and population-attraction effects protect [[QiState|齐国]] far beyond the reach of decorative wealth.
- The Qin-Wei Duping meeting is presented as a failed or inconclusive negotiation because Qin and Wei fight hard the following year.
- In 354 BCE, Qin defeats Wei at Yuanli and occupies [[Shaoliang|少梁]], extending [[QinEastwardPressure|秦国东进压力]] around a repeatedly contested Qin-Wei frontier city.
- The source links Qin's battlefield intensity to the [[MilitaryMeritRankSystem|军功爵制]], especially the formula that enemy heads convert into rank.
- Wei then attacks [[ZhaoState|赵国]] and surrounds Handan, setting up the later [[SunBin|孙膑]], [[TianJi|田忌]], and [[EncirclingWeiToRescueZhao|围魏救赵]] branch developed in the next episode.
- The episode quotes the [[Zhuangzi|《庄子》]]-derived explanation that "Lu wine was thin and Handan was besieged": [[ChuXuanwang|楚宣王]] resents Lu Gong Gong's late attendance and poor wine, Lu Gong Gong refuses to apologize, Chu and Qi attack Lu, and Wei reads Chu's distraction as a chance to strike Zhao.
- [[Jingshe|景舍]] is named as the Chu commander sent to aid Zhao, but the episode stops before the full debate over whether Chu should rescue Zhao.
- The source treats the wine story as a Chinese "butterfly effect": face, tribute quality, alliance obligations, and opportunistic timing can amplify an apparently minor slight into interstate war.

## Key Quotes
> "岂止照亮一个小小车队。" - Qi Wei Wang's contrast between useful officials and Wei's luminous pearls.

> "鲁国的酒味道不纯" - the episode's compact hook for the Handan siege causal chain.
