# 羽毛球价格回落，无印良品将向欧美复制中国经验

## Summary

This [[ShengdongZaokafei|声动早咖啡]] roundup connects local decision rights, overseas growth, vertical capability, channel restructuring, AI infrastructure, and consumer-price adjustment. Its most durable synthesis is that growth and resilience depend on operating systems rather than slogans: [[Muji|MUJI]] wants to export China-market localization practices, [[BYD]] combines overseas sales with battery and charging capability, [[SevenEleven|7-Eleven]] is adding Guangdong density and differentiated products, and [[Adidas]] and [[Nike]] are changing local product and channel control. The badminton segment adds the clearest price mechanism: recovered feather supply, new capacity, and accumulated inventory can reverse a multi-year input-cost shock through [[BadmintonInputCostInventoryCycle|a self-reinforcing price decline]].

## Key Claims

- [[Muji|MUJI]] reportedly plans renewed U.S. and European expansion, greater local decision authority, and more localized assortment, using its China operation as an organizational reference while seeking to lift overseas revenue above half of total revenue by 2029.
- The MUJI segment describes a shift from a narrower minimalist identity toward a broader lifestyle offer spanning health, beauty, food, and store-level assortment decisions, supporting [[RetailLocalizationDecisionRights]].
- U.S. chocolate sales reportedly fell by more than $30 million over the nine weeks before Halloween; earlier high-priced cocoa inventory delayed retail price relief, while ingredient scrutiny, weight-loss drugs, and a move toward smaller packs may also have affected demand.
- [[BYD]] reportedly returned to quarterly profit and revenue growth as overseas passenger-vehicle and pickup sales rose more than 90% over the first three quarters and exceeded 40% of sales; the episode also links the rebound to second-generation blade batteries, fast charging, and internally developed battery-system capabilities.
- Seven listed Chinese airlines reportedly remained under fuel-cost pressure, with jet fuel above 30% of operating cost and November 5 domestic fuel-surcharge increases transferring only part of the shock to travelers.
- [[Meta]] is reportedly working with [[FourierIntelligence|Fourier Intelligence / 傅利叶智能]] on a roughly 1.5-meter household humanoid that could embody the [[MetaMuseModels|Muse]] consumer agent, but U.S. restrictions on newer Chinese humanoid imports impede prototype testing and parts access.
- A [[Google]] orbital-compute experiment reportedly launched four Google AI chips and established ground communication, supplying an early hardware experiment without resolving [[OrbitalDataCenterEconomics]].
- [[Amazon]] reportedly expects about $220 billion of annual AI capital expenditure while rapid AWS growth coexists with cash-flow pressure, a still-dominant retail business, tighter household budgets, and slower-than-expected advertising growth.
- [[SevenEleven|7-Eleven]] plans to increase Guangdong stores from about 2,000 to 3,000 over four years while expanding private-label beverages and supplier-developed channel exclusives amid strong franchise competition from Meiyijia, Tianfu, and [[LawsonRetail|Lawson]].
- [[Adidas]] reportedly posted record quarterly revenue and operating profit, with Greater China reaching fourteen consecutive quarters of revenue growth; the episode attributes part of the local execution to China-based design, production, and marketing teams that shorten development cycles and reduce inventory needs.
- [[Nike]] is reportedly concentrating China online sales in owned and official flagship channels, while major distributor Topsports plans to stop selling Nike online and diversify into exclusive operation of other overseas sports brands.
- Chinese natural-feather badminton prices reportedly fell about 20% year over year after three years of increases as poultry capacity restored feather supply, producers expanded capacity, dealers accumulated excess stock, and falling prices accelerated inventory release and court participation.
