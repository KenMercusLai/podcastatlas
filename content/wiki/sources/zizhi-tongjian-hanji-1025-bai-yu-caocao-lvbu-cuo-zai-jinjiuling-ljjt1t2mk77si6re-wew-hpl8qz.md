# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1025丨败于曹操，吕布错在禁酒令？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the immediate collapse mechanics before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1026-jiemi-sanguo-di-yi-mengjiang-lvbu-de-luomu-lhjf-agujuliglqchumtff3tftxg1|Hanji 1026]]: [[LyuBu|吕布]] is trapped at [[Xiapi|下邳]], fails to send his daughter through [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s blockade to [[YuanShu|袁术]], and loses hoped-for relief from [[ZhangYangLateHan|张杨]]. [[XunYou|荀攸]] and [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] persuade Cao Cao not to withdraw, Cao floods the city with the Si and Yi rivers, and Lü Bu's ban-driven suspicion of [[HouChengLateHan|侯成]] turns internal strain into defection. The source reads Lü Bu's fall as cumulative strategic instability, not as a single "ban alcohol" mistake.

## Key Claims
- In 198 CE, Lü Bu fears Yuan Shu will not send relief because the promised marriage alliance has not been completed, so he ties his daughter onto horseback and tries to send her out of Xiapi.
- Cao Cao's blockade stops the attempted daughter transfer, leaving Lü Bu's Yuan Shu rescue channel practically unusable.
- The source frames Lü Bu's earlier moves among Liu Bei, Yuan Shu, and Cao Cao as an unstable diplomatic pattern that makes his final isolation more intelligible.
- Zhang Yang wants to help Lü Bu from Henei but lacks the strength to do more than camp east of Yewang; his later murder by Yang Chou breaks that possible relief line.
- Yang Chou kills Zhang Yang and tries to move toward Cao Cao, but Sui Gu kills Yang Chou and leads troops north toward Yuan Shao.
- Cao Cao considers withdrawing from the Xiapi siege after the troops tire, but Xun You and Guo Jia argue that Lü Bu's morale and Chen Gong's planning window are weak enough to press.
- Cao Cao diverts the Si and Yi rivers against Xiapi, turning water pressure into the siege's decisive external constraint.
- Lü Bu considers surrender from the city wall while Chen Gong opposes capitulation, showing a split inside the leadership core before the final betrayal.
- Hou Cheng's recovered-horse celebration and wine-meat offering trigger Lü Bu's anger because Lü Bu has just issued a ban on alcohol.
- The episode treats the alcohol ban as a trigger, not the root cause: Lü Bu's suspicion converts a loyalty gesture into evidence of conspiracy.
- Hou Cheng, Song Xian, and Wei Xu capture Chen Gong and Gao Shun and surrender their troops to Cao Cao, leaving Lü Bu no practical command base.
- Lü Bu's request that his soldiers cut off his head at White Gate Tower fails, after which he descends and surrenders.

## Key Quotes

> "禁酒令" - episode title hook, treated by the source as a proximate trigger rather than the whole cause of Lü Bu's defeat.
