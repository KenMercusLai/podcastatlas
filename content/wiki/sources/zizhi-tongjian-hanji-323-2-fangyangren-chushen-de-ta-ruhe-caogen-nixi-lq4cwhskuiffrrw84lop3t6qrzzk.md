# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》323-2｜放羊人出身的他，如何草根逆袭

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode presents [[BuShiWesternHan|卜式]] as a voluntary-service exemplar during the [[Nanyue|南越]] crisis. After the former shepherd and then Qi chancellor offers himself and his son for war, [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] grants him guannei-hou rank, gold, land, and later the Yushi Dafu office; the public example nevertheless produces no broad official response, and more than one hundred marquises are subsequently stripped of title over deficient ritual-gold contributions.

The episode then reads Bu Shi's rise through the politics of recognition. His earlier offer to donate half his wealth had met [[GongsunHong|公孙弘]]'s suspicion because an unofficial, low-background man appeared to be acting outside normal expectations. The host treats Wudi's willingness to look past origin as decisive, but the source supports a bounded claim about access and judgment rather than a general proof that Wudi consistently selected by merit.

## Key Claims

- As Qi chancellor, Bu Shi asks to join the Nanyue campaign with his son and frames service and possible death as a minister's response to the ruler's anxiety.
- Han Wudi publicizes Bu Shi's earlier wealth contributions and present offer, grants him guannei-hou rank, sixty jin of gold, and land, and later appoints him Yushi Dafu.
- The rewarded example does not trigger wider official enthusiasm; the episode then places the removal of more than one hundred marquises beside deficient ritual-gold contributions.
- Before entering office, Bu Shi's proposal to donate half his property was treated suspiciously by Gongsun Hong, whom the host portrays as filtering credibility through office, background, and social origin.
- The episode argues that Bu Shi's rise required both sustained personal contribution and a ruler willing to recognize ability and character outside an elite pedigree.
- Bu Shi's three-stage rise is presented as repeated willingness to risk wealth, self, and household for the state, while wealthy merchants' nonparticipation supplies the negative comparison.
- The closing reference to [[LiGuangHanGeneral|李广]] previews the next episode and does not add substantive evidence to his profile.

## Key Quotes

> “出身、背景和来路” - the host's compressed description of the social filters applied to unfamiliar candidates.

> “非遇其时，焉能致此位乎” - a transmitted *Hanshu* appraisal invoked by the episode to connect ability with political opportunity.

## Connections

- [[BuShiWesternHan|卜式]] - former shepherd and merchant whose donation, military offer, reward, and promotion anchor the episode.
- [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] - ruler who turns Bu Shi into a public example and opens an office path despite his nonelite origin.
- [[GongsunHong|公孙弘]] - senior official who distrusts Bu Shi's unconventional donation proposal.
- [[Nanyue|南越]] - campaign crisis that makes Bu Shi's voluntary personal and household service politically visible.
- [[EliteCrisisBurdenSharing|贵族危机共担动员]] - positive-example mechanism that fails to diffuse across officials and marquises.
- [[ZhoujinForfeiture|酎金夺爵]] - subsequent ritual-compliance punishment that the episode places beside failed elite response.
- [[StatusFilteredTalentMisrecognition|门第筛选式人才误判]] - personnel failure represented by treating low origin and lack of office as evidence against credibility.

## Contradictions

- No settled event-level contradiction is adopted. The Nanyue offer, public reward, failed wider response, and ritual-gold punishment substantially overlap Hanji 353-2, making this an independent but less detailed presentation of the same sequence.
- The supplied transcript calls the deficient contribution “税金”; the existing evidence identifies it more specifically as ritual 酎金, so the wiki treats “税金” as loose episode wording rather than a new tax category.
- The transcript attributes the “鸿渐之翼……非遇其时” appraisal to 班超. The received *Hanshu* places it in the chapter appraisal associated with 班固, so the wiki records 班超 as a transcript or attribution error rather than creating a conflict in the historical synthesis.
- The source does not prove that pedigree alone caused Gongsun Hong's suspicion, that Han Wudi consistently ignored status, or that every marquis forfeiture was retaliation for refusing voluntary service. Motives, exact office chronology, reward details, and broad claims about official selection remain source-scoped.
