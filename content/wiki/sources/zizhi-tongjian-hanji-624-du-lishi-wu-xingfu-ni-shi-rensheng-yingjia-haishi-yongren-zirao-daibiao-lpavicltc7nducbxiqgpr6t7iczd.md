# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》624丨读历史，悟幸福：你是人生赢家，还是“庸人自扰”代表？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills 4-5 CE, immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-625-jiemi-renxing-anmian-weihe-pangren-chenggong-zong-ling-women-xinsheng-jihen-li64ngkv8nujwegbix6iororjmbo|Hanji 625]] and the later [[HanPingdi|汉平帝]] succession crisis. It links the host's "庸人自扰" happiness frame to [[WangMang|王莽]]'s expanding late-Western-Han administration: [[XihaiExileAdministrativeBurden|西海郡流放和法令增殖]], the death of [[LiuLiWesternHan|梁王刘丽]], repeated territorial and office rearrangements, and the escalation of ceremonial reward politics.

The durable synthesis is that Wang Mang's pre-usurpation rise is shown here as both administrative overreach and ritualized status inflation. The court converts mass petitioning, staged refusal, [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]'s emotional endorsement, and [[ZengHanXinOfficial|曾涵]]'s reporting channel into [[JiuXiRitualPromotion|九锡礼遇]], while the closing lesson about "兼容并包" and avoiding black-and-white judgment keeps the episode's self-cultivation frame source-scoped rather than a settled psychological theory.

## Key Claims

- In 4 CE, the court establishes Xihai Commandery and adds fifty legal articles, with offenders sent into exile there.
- The source presents the Xihai rules as a small legal addition that produces large social burden once tens of thousands are reportedly exiled.
- Liu Li is stripped of his Liang kingship, exiled to Nanzheng, and dies by suicide after being accused of collusion with Han Pingdi's Wei-family remnants.
- Wang Mang's court then increases administrative churn through capital-region division, office-grade changes, population boundaries, and commandery or kingdom jurisdiction adjustments.
- In 5 CE, Ming Tang sacrifice and broad rewards to kings, marquises, Liu royal relatives, and officials make ritual an occasion for political distribution.
- Mass petitions over Wang Mang's refusal of Xinye land help stage his humility while increasing pressure to reward him.
- Wang Mang's professed wish to finish ritual institutions and retire becomes a reason for Wang Zhengjun to require completion of the Nine Bestowments rites and keep him central.

## Key Quotes

> "世上本无事，庸人自扰之" - opening maxim used to connect happiness, calculation, and Wang Mang's political style.

> "用心计较般般错，退步思量事事难" - quotation from Zengguang Xianwen used for the episode's happiness frame.

> "真正成熟的人，并不是只会用非黑即白的眼光去看世界" - closing self-cultivation lesson.
