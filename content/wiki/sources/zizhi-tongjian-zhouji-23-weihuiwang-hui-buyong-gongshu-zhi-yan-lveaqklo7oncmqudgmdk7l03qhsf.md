# 《资治通鉴·周纪》23丨魏惠王 悔不用公叔之言

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]] attack on [[WeiState|魏国]] after [[GongziAng|公子卬 / 公子昂]] is captured: [[QinState|秦国]] breaks Wei, [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王]] cedes Hexi territory, and Wei shifts from [[Anyi|安邑]] to [[Daliang|大梁]]. The source then compares Shang Yang's one-shot parley deception with [[SunBin|孙膑]]'s more reusable military method, turning the victory into a case of [[TacticalCredibilityCost|战术信用成本]]. It closes with [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]]'s death, [[YingSi|嬴驷]]'s succession, [[GongziQian|公子虔]]'s accusation, Shang Yang's failed flight to Wei, and the [[LegalistSelfBindingBackfire|作法自毙]] irony of a severe legal order trapping its own designer.

## Key Claims
- After Shang Yang captures Gongzi Ang, he attacks while the Wei army is leaderless and wins a major victory.
- The episode treats this battle as the point where the long-contested Hexi area effectively passes to Qin.
- Liang Hui Wang fears Qin, offers Hexi territory for peace, moves his capital to Daliang, and regrets not following the dying advice of the Wei chancellor [[GongshuWeiChancellor|公叔痤]] about Shang Yang.
- The host compares Shang Yang with Sun Bin: Shang Yang's trick produces larger immediate gains, but Sun Bin's deception has more strategic value because it is reproducible and less destructive to personal trust.
- [[LushiChunqiu|《吕氏春秋》]] is cited as criticizing Shang Yang for both non-repeatability and damaged credibility.
- The episode says Shang Yang's later attempt to flee to Wei fails partly because the Gongzi Ang incident makes Wei unwilling to trust his intentions.
- Shang Yang receives the [[Shangyu|商於]] fief of fifteen cities and becomes 商君 / Shang Jun, the basis for the later Shang Yang name.
- The annalistic sequence also records Qin and Zhao attacking Wei, [[ChuXuanwang|楚宣王]] dying, [[ChuWeiwang|楚威王]] succeeding, and 339 BCE being blank in [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]].
- In 338 BCE, Qin Xiao Gong dies after twenty-four years of rule; the episode says his goals of strengthening Qin, reclaiming Hexi, and forcing Wei to yield were largely complete before death.
- After Qin Xiao Gong dies, Shang Yang loses the ruler protection that had made his reforms politically survivable.
- Gongzi Qian's faction accuses Shang Yang of rebellion; Shang Yang flees toward Wei, is refused entry, returns to Qin, raises troops in Shangyu, is killed, and is posthumously dismembered with his family exterminated.
- The [[Shiji|《史记》]] lodging-story version says Shang Yang cannot stay at an inn without identity papers because his own law punishes the innkeeper, giving the episode its "作法自毙" closing irony.

## Key Quotes
> "吾悔不用公叔之言" - Liang Hui Wang's regret after ceding territory and moving the capital.

> "兵以诈立" - tactical frame now qualified by the later credibility cost.

> "法之弊，一至此哉" - Shiji-style self-trap line attributed to Shang Yang in flight.

## Contradictions
- No direct contradiction found. The source extends episode 22's [[PeaceParleyEntrapment|和谈诱捕]] by adding the victory's territorial outcome, Shang Yang's fief, and the later credibility cost when Wei refuses him refuge.
- The episode treats Gongzi Ang's capture as tactically successful but strategically costly for Shang Yang's reputation; this qualifies rather than contradicts the earlier "兵以诈立" framing.
