# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》995：慈悲外衣下的残暴权力术

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows the 195 CE Jiangdong and Yuzhang branch around [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]], [[XuShao|许劭]], and [[ZeRong|笮融]]. Xu Shao's advice treats [[KuaijiCommandery|会稽郡]] as rich but exposed because [[SunCe|孙策]] wants it too, while [[YuzhangCommandery|豫章郡]] preserves Liu Yao's official standing and future routes toward [[CaoCao|曹操]] and [[LiuBiao|刘表]]. The darker core is Ze Rong: after using [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]]'s grain to build Buddhist institutions at [[Xiapi|下邳]], he repeatedly kills hosts and benefactors in [[GuanglingCommandery|广陵]] and Yuzhang. The host reads him as a warning case for [[InstrumentalizedReligiousPower|工具化宗教权力]] and [[HospitalityPredation|接纳者掠杀]] rather than as proof that Buddhist belief itself caused the violence.

## Key Claims
- History matters because it trains pattern recognition in human nature, incentives, and power, not because isolated events repeat mechanically.
- [[XuShao|许劭]] advises [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]] against [[KuaijiCommandery|会稽郡]] because its wealth, coastal exposure, and attraction to [[SunCe|孙策]] make it unsafe.
- Xu Shao prefers [[YuzhangCommandery|豫章郡]] because it keeps Liu Yao near Yu and Jing routes, lets him settle displaced officials and people, and preserves a Han-recognized position that [[CaoCao|曹操]] or [[LiuBiao|刘表]] might later support.
- [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] gives [[ZeRong|笮融]] a grain-transport office across [[GuanglingCommandery|广陵]], [[Xiapi|下邳]], and Pengcheng; Ze Rong turns that resource into religious infrastructure and private following.
- Ze Rong's temples, sutra recitation, tax exemptions, and bathing-Buddha feasts attract thousands of Buddhist households, but the episode treats the system as a power device when separated from moral restraint.
- Ze Rong murders [[ZhaoYuLateHan|赵昱]], [[XueLiLateHan|薛礼]], and [[ZhuHaoLateHan|朱皓]] after receiving hospitality or operational trust, making him a concentrated case of no-reciprocity predation.
- [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s Yuzhang appointee [[ZhugeXuan|诸葛玄]] is displaced by Liu Yao's appointment of Zhu Hao, but Ze Rong's murder of Zhu Hao interrupts Liu Yao's plan.
- The later court appointment of [[HuaXin|华歆]] to Yuzhang closes the episode's administrative thread without making Yuzhang stable in this source.
- The episode compares [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]]'s [[TaipingDao|太平道]] and [[ZhangLuFivePecks|张鲁]]'s [[WudoumiDao|五斗米道]] as late-Han religious-mobilization cases, but it does not collapse them into Ze Rong's pattern.
