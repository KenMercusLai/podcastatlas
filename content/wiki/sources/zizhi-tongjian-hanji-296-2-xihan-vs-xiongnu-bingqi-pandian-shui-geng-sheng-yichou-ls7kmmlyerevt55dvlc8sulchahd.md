# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》296-2｜西汉VS匈奴兵器盘点，谁更胜一筹

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]] from Dingxiang into a three-route search for the [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] main force. Wei Qing's central army meets a similarly sized mounted force first, attacks while summoning the two wings, and wins a local rout, but the missing wings and absent chanyu leave the larger campaign unresolved.

The episode explains the local Han advantage through [[CombinedArmsAntiCavalrySystem|合成兵器体系压制骑兵机动]]: crossbows, longer iron close-combat weapons, spears and halberds, chariots, and organized depth are said to force a mobile cavalry opponent into sustained frontal contact. Its detailed claims about repeating crossbows, steelmaking, weapon dimensions, Xiongnu bronze equipment, force totals, and casualties remain episode-attributed rather than independently verified.

## Key Claims

- [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]] reportedly leaves Dingxiang with 50,000 troops after imperial pressure and responds to Xiongnu avoidance by dividing the search into central, left, and right routes.
- [[ZhaoXinWesternHan|赵信]] and [[SuJianWesternHan|苏建]] take 3,000 cavalry to the right, an officer rendered as 郝贤 searches the left, and Wei Qing advances in the center with [[ZhangQianWesternHan|张骞]] as guide.
- Wei Qing's central force, described as more than 40,000, encounters nearly 40,000 Xiongnu cavalry and immediately calls the wings inward while committing the center.
- The episode distinguishes Xiongnu strengths in mobility, horse-centered life, broad military participation, terrain familiarity, and mounted archery from the Han advantage in sustained formation battle.
- Han equipment is presented as a system rather than a single decisive weapon: crossbows provide ranged pressure; spears and halberds extend reach; ring-pommel sabres and other short weapons support close combat; and chariots contribute weight and formation depth.
- Wei Qing is credited with transferring frontal assault and massed advance from infantry practice into cavalry operations so that close contact restricts Xiongnu freedom to maneuver and shoot.
- The central engagement reportedly ends with the Xiongnu force routed and almost 10,000 dead, but neither wing arrives and Wei Qing concludes that the chanyu was not commanding the defeated formation.

## Key Quotes

> “汉军的武器装备比匈奴强多了，而且汉朝大军团作战能力是匈奴比不了的。” - the episode's central explanation for the claimed Han advantage in an equal-force frontal engagement.

> “汉军的强大还不仅仅是一个武器强大，而是它的兵器都形成了纵深集群作战队形。” - the episode's shift from individual weapon comparison to combined formation effects.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. The result is compatible with [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-297-gonggao-zhubuyi-mingjiang-weiqing-de-weiguan-zhihui-lg2zmxdwvx_ydqfqsme_hdcswtwn|Hanji 297]], which supplies the missing right wing's defeat, Zhao Xin's surrender, Su Jian's return, and Wei Qing's later review decision.
- The episode's descriptions of a repeating crossbow firing dozens of bolts in one action, mid-Western-Han 炒钢, ring-pommel sabre dimensions, widespread Xiongnu bronze short swords, and a nearly century-wide technology gap require archaeological and specialist comparison before being promoted beyond source scope.
- “全民皆兵,” equal-force superiority, the transfer of infantry frontal assault to cavalry, and the claim that Xiongnu troops then had only surrender or flight available are broad interpretive generalizations rather than demonstrated universal rules.
- The Dingxiang naming, force and casualty totals, precise three-route geometry, 郝贤's identity and office, Zhang Qian's guide role, the chanyu's location, and the explanation for both wings' delay remain episode-attributed.
