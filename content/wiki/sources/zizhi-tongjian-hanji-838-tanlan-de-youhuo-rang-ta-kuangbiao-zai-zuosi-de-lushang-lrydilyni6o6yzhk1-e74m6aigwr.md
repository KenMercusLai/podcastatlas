# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》838丨贪婪的诱惑，让他狂飙在作死的路上

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 151-152 CE [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] field immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-839-ni-neng-zuodao-wenxin-wukui-ma-lqvlswubf4ak5qfgzjf-owigneua|Hanji 839]]. It opens with a source-scoped warning about greed and then uses [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] to show how extraordinary reward, palace privilege, and political access can still leave a powerholder dissatisfied when reward is detached from public merit.

The second half follows the Western Regions incident in which [[JumiKingChengguoLateHan|居弥王成国]] accuses [[KhotanKingJianLateHan|于阗王建]] of arranging Zhao Ping's death, [[WangJingXiyuLateHan|王敬]] treats the charge as a chance for merit, and a banquet killing destabilizes [[KhotanKingdomLateHan|于阗国]]. [[ShuboKhotanLateHan|输僰]] kills Wang Jing and briefly seizes kingship before the Khotanese kill him and install [[AnGuoKhotanLateHan|安国]], making the incident a compact case of [[RumorDrivenFrontierIntervention|谣言驱动的边疆干预]].

## Key Claims

- The opening greed lesson is source-scoped: the host treats insatiability as a practical and moral danger rather than as a universal psychological theory.
- In 151 CE, Huan asks high officials to discuss adding rewards for Liang Ji; most speakers praise Liang in Zhougong-like terms and support heavy honors.
- [[HuangQiongLateHan|黄琼]] limits the proposal by pointing out that Liang already has a large food-estate base and that Liang's son has also been enfeoffed.
- Huan accepts the narrower territorial recommendation but still grants Liang unusual court etiquette, palace access, wealth, servants, vehicles, garments, a residence, and participation in Shangshu affairs.
- Liang remains dissatisfied with the ceremony and reward scale, making him the episode's case for [[UnboundedEliteRewardDemand|受宠权臣的无餍封赏]].
- The source contrasts Liang's reward appetite with the absence of public merit: the episode says his main "merit" is helping install Huan while also reminding listeners that he killed Emperor Zhi.
- In 152 CE, Wang Jing receives an investigation mission after Zhao Ping dies in Khotan, but the mission is captured by Jumi-Khotan rivalry and his own ambition.
- Chengguo's accusation gives Wang Jing a pretext to kill Khotan King Jian before the charge is verified.
- Khotanese actors reject Wang Jing's claim that he acted by imperial command, kill him, reject Shubo's brief self-enthronement, and install Jian's son Anguo.
- Huan's court stops Ma Da's punitive impulse by recalling him, but Song Liang's later attempt to manipulate Khotanese internal killing arrives after Shubo is already dead.
