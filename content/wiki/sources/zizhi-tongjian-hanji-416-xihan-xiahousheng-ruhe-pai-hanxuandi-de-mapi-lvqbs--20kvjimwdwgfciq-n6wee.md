# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》416｜西汉夏侯胜如何拍汉宣帝的马屁？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills a Benshi 3 / 71 BCE layer under [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]]. After a major earthquake and amnesty, [[XiahouShengWesternHan|夏侯胜]] and [[HuangBaWesternHan|黄霸]] are released from prison, promoted, and reinterpreted by Xuan as learned, upright, and useful rather than merely offensive.

The durable synthesis is that Xiahou Sheng's celebrated "flattery" is not simple servility. Because he already has a reputation for integrity, plain speech, learning, and lack of private motive, his praise can recode a breach of court confidentiality into a claim that the ruler's good words deserve historical circulation; the episode turns this into a case of [[PraiseAsPoliticalMemory|praise as political memory]] and ruler-minister mutual formation.

## Key Claims

- In 71 BCE, a large earthquake affects forty-nine commanderies and kingdoms, destroys walls and houses, kills more than six thousand people, and damages temples in Beihai and Langya.
- Xuan responds with disaster-statecraft measures: consultation with classicists, recommendation of worthy and upright people, general amnesty, plain clothing, and avoidance of the main palace hall for five days.
- Xiahou Sheng and Huang Ba are released by the amnesty after having been imprisoned together under a grave offense, and both are promoted rather than discarded.
- Xuan reportedly has them observed in prison and learns that they study every day, which supports his later judgment that Xiahou is a "teacher of the realm" and Huang can model official conduct.
- Xiahou Sheng is portrayed as upright, plain, and socially unguarded: he may call the emperor "jun" and use officials' courtesy names, but Xuan trusts him because he has no private agenda.
- When Xiahou repeats words Xuan had spoken privately, he answers the emperor's anger by saying good royal speech should be spread, just as Yao's words still circulate.
- The host reads this as high-level praise: Xiahou places Xuan inside a sage-ruler memory frame while also teaching that rulers care about what will be said of them after death.
- Xiahou's later role as crown-prince tutor, Jinwen Shangshu scholar, founder of the "Da Xiahou" tradition, and honored teacher is used to explain why directness and praise work only when backed by learning and long-term moral credibility.

## Key Quotes

> "陛下说得好，所以臣要传扬出去。" - Xiahou Sheng's defense as summarized by the episode.

> "为国要忠，为民要仁，为事要义，上不奉、下不欺。" - the host's summary of Xiahou Sheng's conduct standard.
