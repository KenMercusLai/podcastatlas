# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》313-1｜独闯虎穴！霍去病受降浑邪王

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode presents [[HuoQubing|霍去病]]'s reception of [[HunyeKingWesternHan|浑邪王]] as an unsettled command crisis rather than a completed surrender ceremony. After the planned joint submission with the Xiutu King breaks down and fighting erupts inside the Xiongnu camp, Huo crosses the river under arms, demands an immediate choice between battle and submission, and moves quickly to prevent further defection.

The episode's durable contribution is [[CrisisSurrenderStabilization|危机受降稳控]]: Huo combines visible coercion, promised rank, immediate separation of the surrendered ruler, and use of Hunye King's own forces against resisting Xiutu groups. This converts a divided and still-mobile armed population into a controlled southward movement, but the dialogue, cultural psychology, place names, exact force and casualty totals, and near-zero Han losses remain source-scoped dramatic claims.

## Key Claims

- Hunye King and the Xiutu King initially plan to surrender together, but the Xiutu side reverses course and internal fighting leaves the submission unstable.
- Huo Qubing approaches with Han forces, confronts Hunye King directly, and demands an immediate choice between fighting and surrendering.
- The episode interprets Huo's youth as a source of prestige among Xiongnu groups said to value young hunters and fighters, but this cultural explanation is not independently established here.
- Huo announces that those who surrender will live, resisters will die, and voluntarily submitting group leaders may receive marquis rank.
- Hunye King is required to guide the operation and order affiliated groups to block or attack Xiutu forces attempting to leave, turning part of the surrendering coalition into an enforcement arm.
- Huo then moves the surviving force south across the Yellow River and sends Hunye King ahead toward Chang'an to reduce the chance of renewed disorder.
- The episode reports eight thousand enemy dead, more than forty thousand accepted cavalry, very small Han losses, and a 1,700-household reward for Huo; these figures remain episode-attributed.

## Key Quotes

> "降者生，反者死。" - the episode's compressed surrender ultimatum.

> "一胡相攻，无损汉兵。" - the host's summary of using internal Xiongnu division to limit Han losses.

## Connections

- [[HuoQubing|霍去病]] - commander who combines armed pressure, incentive, delegation, separation, and rapid movement during the surrender crisis.
- [[HunyeKingWesternHan|浑邪王]] - surrendering ruler whose authority and forces become part of Huo's stabilization mechanism.
- [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] - ruler to whom the surrendered population is being transferred and who rewards Huo in the episode's account.
- [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] - political and military field divided by the failed joint surrender and subsequent enforcement action.
- [[ZhaoPonuWesternHan|赵破奴]] - officer named among the commanders accompanying Huo at the confrontation.
- [[CrisisSurrenderStabilization|危机受降稳控]] - synthesis of coercion, selective reward, delegated enforcement, leader separation, and rapid relocation.
- [[SurrenderReceptionDomesticBurden|归降接纳的内外成本冲突]] - sequel-side frame for the domestic transport, reward, labor, and legal costs of receiving the surrendered population.

## Contradictions

- The supplied document conflicts internally on the Xiutu King's death: the overview says Hunye King killed him, while the segmented summary says he killed himself after defeat became inevitable. This note does not choose between those versions.
- The episode's battlefield dialogue, knife-point confrontation, universal silence, leader-by-leader reactions, and detailed movements are dramatized and are not treated as verbatim historical records.
- The claim that Xiongnu custom generally privileged the young, and that Huo's age therefore produced submission, is a broad cultural-psychological inference rather than a demonstrated cause.
- The location rendered as the meeting of the Zhuanglang River and Yellow River, the identity and role of the Fuli group, the two-to-three-wan resisting force, forty-thousand-plus cavalry, later two-hundred-thousand-plus relocation, casualty total, Han loss claim, and reward figure require primary-text comparison.
