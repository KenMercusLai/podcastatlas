# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1070丨焦触歃血为盟，劝部下叛降曹操

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the post-[[YeLateHan|邺城]] northern settlement after [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1066-sanguo-zui-manheng-de-waijiaoguan-pingshenme-shi-qianzhao-ll17spch6x4zyo61vno-oi0lk7ft|Hanji 1066]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1067-sanguo-zui-baqi-de-nvren-wei-bao-fu-chou-na-ziji-dang-youer-lqc9lveve1-dcayvutdhx4ah3lho|Hanji 1067]]. [[YuanXiLateHan|袁熙]] and [[YuanShang|袁尚]] flee toward Wuhuan protection, while [[JiaoChuLateHan|焦触]] and [[ZhangNanLateHan|张南]] reverse against them, coerce Youzhou officials into a blood-oath surrender to [[CaoCao|曹操]], and make [[HanHengLateHan|韩恒]]'s refusal a visible loyalty test.

The second half shows that Cao Cao's Hebei advantage is not yet stable control. [[ZhaoDuLateHan|赵独]], [[HuoNuLateHan|霍奴]], Wuhuan forces, [[GaoGanLateHan|高干]], [[ZhangShengLateHan|张盛]], [[ZhangYanHedongLateHan|张衍]], and [[WeiGuLateHan|卫固]] create a chain of local and frontier disturbances, so [[XunYu|荀彧]] recommends [[DuJiLateHan|杜畿]] for [[HedongCommandery|河东郡]] as a traffic-hub commandery where local elite reliability matters.

## Key Claims

- [[LiuFangLateHan|刘放]] persuades [[WangSongLateHan|王嵩]] to bring Zhuo commandery under Cao Cao, giving Cao an early Youzhou-side personnel gain.
- [[YuanXiLateHan|袁熙]] is overturned by [[JiaoChuLateHan|焦触]] and [[ZhangNanLateHan|张南]], forcing Yuan Xi and [[YuanShang|袁尚]] toward Liaoxi Wuhuan.
- Jiao Chu's blood-oath assembly makes surrender a coercive public ritual: officials are ordered to abandon the Yuan family and face execution if they refuse.
- [[HanHengLateHan|韩恒]] refuses the oath on loyalty grounds, and Jiao Chu's decision to let him go creates a source case of [[LoyalRefusalLegitimacySignal|忠臣拒降合法性信号]].
- Cao Cao's enfeoffment of Jiao Chu and other surrendering actors shows [[StrategicDefectorIncorporation|纳叛安抚式整合]] continuing after Ye rather than ending with battlefield victory.
- [[ZhangYanLateHan|张燕]] brings the [[HeishanArmy|黑山军]] formally into Cao's hierarchy and receives marquis status, completing the timed-alignment path introduced in Hanji 1062.
- The Youzhou rebellion by [[ZhaoDuLateHan|赵独]] and [[HuoNuLateHan|霍奴]], plus Wuhuan pressure on [[XianyuFu|鲜于辅]] at Kuangping, shows that surrender cascades do not equal settled local order.
- [[GaoGanLateHan|高干]] rebels again from [[BingzhouLateHan|并州]] while Cao Cao is campaigning against Wuhuan, seizes the Shangdang administrator, and holds the Huguan route.
- [[ZhangShengLateHan|张盛]] and [[ZhangYanHedongLateHan|张衍]] widen the western disorder around Xiaoshan, Mianchi, Hongnong, and [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]]-facing routes.
- [[CaoCao|曹操]] treats [[HedongCommandery|河东郡]] as a strategic transportation hub whose governor must manage local elite defection risk, making [[DuJiLateHan|杜畿]]'s appointment a case of [[TransitHubCommanderyControl|交通枢纽郡控制]].

## Key Quotes

> "敢有违者，斩" - the coercive edge of Jiao Chu's blood-oath assembly.

> "曹操不只是在收编军事力量，也在经营政治声望" - the episode's interpretation of surrender, loyal refusal, and Cao Cao's image.

> "河东郡是天下交通枢纽" - Cao Cao's reason for asking Xun Yu to recommend a reliable governor.
