# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》383-1｜汉代最大悲剧，“巫蛊之祸”是如何发生的？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] short episode opens the late-[[HanWudi|汉武帝]] [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]] arc before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-383-2-chengxiang-gongsunhe-shi-ruhe-bei-erzi-kengsi-de-lt1yhocymjtgwjb7537yotbwoikz|Hanji 383-2]]. It frames the catastrophe as more than a single奸臣 story: old-age death fear, loss-of-power anxiety, belief in curse harm, and opportunistic court actors combine to make hidden accusations lethal.

The durable synthesis is that the incident's opening conditions are psychological, religious, and institutional. The Jianzhang Palace armed-man scare in 92 BCE shows Wudi already responding to perceived threats with execution, city lockdown, and mass search; the decline of the Wei-family outer-relative network then leaves [[LiuJuCrownPrinceWesternHan|卫太子刘据]] increasingly dependent on [[GongsunHeWesternHan|公孙贺]], whose later fall becomes the immediate prelude to the broader Wugu disaster.

## Key Claims

- The source presents the Wugu Incident as Han Wudi's largest late-life injustice and as a catastrophe that turns him into a lonely ruler.
- Han Wudi's early strengths are contrasted with late-life immortality seeking, fear of death, fear of losing power, suspicion, and harshness.
- In 92 BCE, the Jianzhang Palace armed-man scare leads Wudi to execute the gate official, mobilize Sanfu cavalry, search Shanglin Park, close Chang'an's gates, and maintain an eleven-day lockdown.
- The episode argues that the Wugu crisis is not an accidental one-off: court belief in hidden curse power, Wudi's sensitivity, and malicious opportunists together create the conditions for mass persecution.
- After [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]]'s death, the Wei-family outer support around Liu Ju is weakened, making Gongsun He especially important as a later support figure.
- Gongsun He is introduced as Wei Qing and Wei Zifu's brother-in-law, a former campaign follower of Wei Qing, a cautious chancellor, and Liu Ju's important late support.
- The episode stops at Gongsun He's uneasy chancellorship, setting up Hanji 383-2's account of how his family case opens the Wugu prelude.

## Key Quotes

> "孤家寡人" - the episode's description of what the late-Wudi catastrophe ultimately makes Han Wudi.

> "恐惧、最高权力、别有用心" - the source's compact three-part explanation for how the Wugu disaster begins.
