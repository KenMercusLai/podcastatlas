# EP94 穿越周金涛：人生发财靠康波，守住家底靠少错

## Summary
This [[YiLaoYongYi|一劳永逸]] episode uses a clearly labeled dramatized reconstruction of [[ZhouJintao]]'s public cycle research to explain [[KondratievCycle]], [[InventoryCycle]], [[JuglarCycle]], real-estate risk, commodity rebounds, technology bubbles, and ordinary household investing. Its main contribution is not a point forecast, but a discipline: define money source, use date, cash-flow need, risk capacity, and action type before translating macro cycles into [[AssetAllocation]] or trades. The episode extends [[OrdinaryInvestorMacroBoundary]] by arguing that cycles can clarify the environment while still failing as a precise timing tool.

## Key Claims
- [[ZhouJintao]]'s public reputation rests partly on calls around U.S. subprime risk, post-2015 asset pressure, 2016 commodity rebound, and 2019-ish low points, but the episode repeatedly warns against turning those cases into idol worship or trade recipes.
- Ordinary investors should begin with capital duration, family obligations, emergency funds, and purpose; money needed for tuition, living costs, elder care, or near-term housing should not be treated like speculative risk capital.
- [[KondratievCycle]] is useful as a long-horizon environment frame only when paired with mechanisms such as technology diffusion, production reorganization, infrastructure, and social adoption; it is not a fixed sixty-year clock.
- [[InventoryCycle]] and [[JuglarCycle]] show how short demand surprises can become longer supply mistakes: overstocking can be cleared faster than factories, equipment, employees, and new locations.
- New technologies can be economically real while early investors still overpay: railways, the internet, steam power, and AI all show that price cycles can lead productivity and profit cycles.
- The 2007 U.S. mortgage-risk example is framed as balance-sheet chain analysis: rising house prices, borrower refinancing, lender confidence, collateral values, and loan resale can reverse together when prices stop rising.
- The 2016 commodity rebound is explained through supply contraction and inventory exhaustion, not simply through bad news being fully priced.
- [[CycleStockValuationTrap]] matters because a cyclical company can look cheap at peak profit and expensive once normalized earnings return.
- Real estate should be evaluated through population flow, supply, liquidity, rent, age, maintenance, and leverage; a 20% property-price decline can erase much more than 20% of the buyer's equity when debt is large.
- The closing rule is that "人生发财靠康波" needs the second half: household survival depends on fewer large mistakes, not only catching one grand cycle.
