# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》106｜惊人发现信陵君的非正常死亡

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode moves the Qinji sequence into 243-242 BCE, linking [[MengAo|蒙敖]]'s renewed pressure on [[WeiState|魏国]], [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] and [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]] dying in the same year, Qin's response to locust and epidemic disaster, and [[YanState|燕国]]'s failed attack on [[ZhaoState|赵国]]. Its central interpretive addition is [[XinlingjunDeathSuspicion|信陵君非正常死亡疑云]]: because [[Shiji|《史记》]] places Xinlingjun's death before Wei Anxi Wang's in the same year, the host argues that the earlier "death by wine and women" may conceal a succession-security killing.

The second half turns to disaster and state capacity. The source says Qin faced "locusts and epidemic" but used [[RankSaleDisasterFinance|鬻爵赈灾融资]] to gather grain, then resumed [[QinEastwardPressure|秦国东进压力]] in 242 BCE by taking twenty Wei cities and creating [[DongCommandery|东郡]]; the closing uses [[JuXin|剧辛]]'s underestimation of [[PangNuan|庞煖]] to show [[YanWangXi|燕王喜]] again misreading Zhao and setting up [[Chunshenjun|春申君]]'s next anti-Qin coalition role.

## Key Claims
- In 243 BCE, Meng Ao attacks Wei and stops in the third month, while Zhao does not repay Xinlingjun's earlier help but instead prepares against Wei because [[LianPo|廉颇]] has taken refuge there.
- Wei Anxi Wang and Xinlingjun die in the same year, and [[WeiJingminWang|魏景湣王 / 魏增]] succeeds.
- The episode accepts the prior [[CounterintelligenceRumorWedge|反间流言楔入]] story but pushes beyond [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-103-2-simaqiande-idol-weihe-siyu-jiuse-lvmqg1ojmka9pqagfhgvo4albpjx|Qinji 103-2]] by treating the order of deaths as politically suspicious.
- The host's inference is that Wei Anxi Wang may have removed Xinlingjun before his own death to make Wei Zeng's succession safer.
- Once Qin's slander made Xinlingjun unusable, Qin could concentrate pressure on Wei; the source says Wei's later collapse made hezong harder because the central-plains support point disappeared.
- [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s later sacrifices to Xinlingjun at Daliang and his assignment of five households for tomb rites preserve Xinlingjun as a posthumous moral-political model.
- The terse record "蝗、疫" is expanded into locust crop damage, food shortage, corpse exposure, and possible epidemic spread; the host speculates that the epidemic may have been smallpox, but the source gives no decisive proof.
- Qin's response is to exchange one thousand dan of grain for the lowest rank, 公士, turning social title into relief finance without necessarily selling office.
- The episode distinguishes rank sale from office sale: the former mobilizes grain through status while supposedly avoiding direct bureaucratic corruption.
- In 242 BCE, Qin continues eastward despite disaster, sends Meng Ao against Wei, takes twenty cities, and establishes Dong Commandery.
- Yan Wang Xi attacks Zhao after asking Ju Xin about Pang Nuan; Ju Xin underestimates his old acquaintance, Pang Nuan defeats the Yan army, kills Ju Xin, and captures 20,000 soldiers.
- The source closes by criticizing Yan's short-sighted attacks on other eastern states and previewing Chunshenjun's renewed hezong effort against Qin.

> "信陵君极有可能属于非正常死亡" - the episode's opening thesis.
