# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》396-3｜“经济杀手”汉武帝，如何摧毁了西汉商业？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] short episode argues that [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] damaged Western Han commerce by combining state monopoly, merchant taxation, property reporting, and rewarded denunciation. It treats the禁榷-style control of profitable goods, 算缗-style assessment of merchants and related operators, and 告缗-style informant rewards as mutually reinforcing fiscal tools that financed war while eroding commercial security.

The episode then uses the [[NorthernSong|宋代]] and [[WangAnshi|王安石]] as a later comparison: even when commerce produced substantial state revenue, political elites could still mistrust merchants as market manipulators and social threats. The source therefore strengthens the wiki's thread linking [[HeavyAgricultureLightCommercePolicy|重农轻商政策]], [[StateCommercialMonopolyExtraction|官营商业垄断式汲取]], and property insecurity.

## Key Claims

- The episode frames Han Wudi's commercial policy as a response to the contradiction between a wealthy commercial society and a state needing war finance.
- 禁榷 is described as state monopoly over profitable goods such as salt, iron, tea, and liquor, removing high-margin fields from private merchants.
- 算缗 is described as a tax and reporting system requiring merchants, artisans, lenders, and capital operators to report transaction or property scale, with confiscation and frontier punishment for concealment.
- 告缗 is described as a rewarded denunciation system in which informants could receive half of a violator's confiscated property.
- The host argues that these policies produced a wave of denunciations, confiscations, enslavement, frontier exile, and commercial decline from the Wen-Jing-era height.
- The Song comparison says Song commercial taxes were fiscally important, but Wang Anshi still attacked merchants as people who manipulated markets, gained monopoly profits, commanded others' labor, and displayed excessive luxury.
- The episode presents anti-merchant policy as both fiscal extraction and status mistrust rather than a simple technical tax issue.

## Key Quotes

> "商人的好日子也就到头了" - the source's transition from Wen-Jing commercial prosperity to Han Wudi-era pressure.

> "一半的财产会给告密者作为奖励" - the episode's description of the告缗 incentive.
