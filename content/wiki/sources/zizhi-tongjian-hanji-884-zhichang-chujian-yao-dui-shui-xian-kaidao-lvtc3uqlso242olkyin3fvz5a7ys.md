# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》884丨职场除奸，要对谁先开刀？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens with a workplace frame about laziness, arrogance, over-effort, and unalert relaxation, then turns to the opening of [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s Jianning first year in 168 CE. It shows [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦妙 / 窦皇太后]] entrusting government to [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] and [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]], while [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]], [[DuMiLateHan|杜密]], [[YinXunLateHan|尹勋]], and [[LiuYuAstrologerLateHan|刘瑜]] enter the political core.

The historical center is the first anti-eunuch decision window before the later Hanji 885-887 disaster. [[ZhaoRaoLateHan|赵饶]], female palace staff, [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]], and [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]] gain influence through near-daily access to Dou Miao; Chen Fan urges Dou Wu to remove Cao Jie and Wang Fu before the window closes, but Dou Miao authorizes only selective punishment, beginning with [[GuanBaLateHan|管霸]] and [[SuKangLateHan|苏康]].

## Key Claims

- The opening contrasts two failure modes: ordinary people may fail through laziness, while capable people may fail through arrogance.
- The "穿针效应" workplace frame warns that excessive effort can distort judgment, negotiation, creativity, and relationships.
- The source also warns that relaxation without alertness can become dangerous when a live power field is already deteriorating.
- In 168 CE, Dou Miao's regency initially creates reform hope by giving Chen Fan and Dou Wu central authority and bringing renowned士人 into court.
- Palace proximity becomes political power: Zhao Rao, female palace staff, Cao Jie, and Wang Fu gain Dou Miao's favor through constant attendance, pleasing behavior, and intimate access.
- Chen Fan reads Cao Jie and Wang Fu as urgent removal targets and persuades Dou Wu that delay will make future action much harder.
- Dou Wu and Yin Xun use a solar eclipse as an opening to press an anti-eunuch plan, turning private concern into a formal palace request.
- Dou Miao rejects a blanket purge, choosing instead to punish lawbreakers first; Guan Ba and Su Kang are arrested and executed as the initial targets.

## Key Quotes

> "天下古今之庸人就一个懒字之败，天下古今之人才就一个傲字之败" - opening contrast between laziness and arrogance.

> "越是想用力做好，结果越是出错" - the episode's "穿针效应" framing.

> "窦太后主张只惩治犯法者，不能把宦官“一锅端”" - the source's account of Dou Miao's limited purge logic.
