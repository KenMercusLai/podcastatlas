# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》928丨职场不可明说：送对礼，办事更顺利

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 184 CE [[YellowTurbansLateHan|Yellow Turban]] campaign immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-929-duishi-buduiren-ni-xin-le-ma-lgaxj0jzopddmt3c5hibnexqlhb3|Hanji 929]]. It follows [[HuangfuSongLateHan|皇甫嵩]] and [[ZhuJunLateHan|朱儁]] suppressing Yellow Turban forces in the Runan-Chen field, while [[LuZhiLateHan|卢植]] surrounds [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]] at Guangzong.

The core turn is [[ZuoFengLateHan|左丰]]'s inspection: Lu Zhi refuses to send a gift, Zuo Feng's report convinces [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]], and [[DongZhuo|董卓]] is appointed to replace him. The episode therefore adds a concrete prelude to Dong Zhuo's failed replacement command in Hanji 929 and makes the removal of a winning front-line commander a case of [[InspectionGiftGatekeeping|巡查送礼关口]], [[PalaceAccessMonopoly|宫廷接触垄断]], and [[BattlefieldCommanderReplacementRisk|阵前换将风险]].

## Key Claims

- In 184 CE, Huangfu Song and Zhu Jun continue suppressing Yellow Turban forces around Runan commandery and [[ChenStateLateHan|陈国]], defeating Bo Cai around Yangdi and Peng Tuo at Xihua.
- Huangfu Song reports the victory by emphasizing Zhu Jun's merit, after which Zhu Jun is rewarded as Xixiang marquis and promoted to a campaign command.
- The court then assigns Huangfu Song toward the Dong commandery Yellow Turban front and Zhu Jun toward the Nanyang Yellow Turban front.
- Lu Zhi, serving as north zhonglang general, defeats Zhang Jiao's force, kills or captures more than ten thousand people, and surrounds Zhang Jiao at Guangzong with walls, ditches, and siege ladders.
- Emperor Ling is uneasy because Lu Zhi controls a large front-line force, so he sends the small yellow gate Zuo Feng to inspect the camp.
- Lu Zhi's subordinates suggest giving Zuo Feng a gift so the palace report will be favorable, but Lu Zhi refuses because he dislikes eunuchs and relies on upright conduct.
- Zuo Feng returns to [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] and claims that Guangzong is easy to take while Lu Zhi keeps the army resting behind fortifications.
- Emperor Ling believes the report, orders Lu Zhi brought back to Luoyang in a prison cart, and imposes a severe penalty short of execution.
- Dong Zhuo is sent to replace Lu Zhi, giving him another opportunity to handle troops before his later central-power rise.
- The host's workplace lesson is source-scoped: in a captured access system, loyalty and ability do not automatically protect a person who fails to manage the inspection and reporting channel.
