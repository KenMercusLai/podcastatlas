# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》898丨认贼作父？可怜之人能有多荒唐？

## Summary
This episode backfills 171 CE immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-899-hao-qing-dou-tanguan-ljhic1plzmv8k6lu73j5terihanx|Hanji 899]]. It frames [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] through dependence on [[ZhangRangLateHan|张让]] and [[ZhaoZhongLateHan|赵忠]], a guanli-and-amnesty notice that still excludes party figures, disaster and epidemic pressure, top-office churn, the [[DongMengLateHan|董萌]] case around [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦皇太后]], and an early [[Xianbei|鲜卑]] attack on [[BingzhouLateHan|并州]].

## Key Claims
- Emperor Ling's language for Zhang Rang and Zhao Zhong makes intimate palace dependence visible before later slander, finance, and crisis cases develop under [[PalaceAccessMonopoly|宫廷接触垄断]] and [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|君臣反馈失灵]].
- The 171 CE guanli and general amnesty explicitly do not pardon party figures, creating a negative prehistory for later [[PartyBanCrisisAmnesty|党锢危机赦免]] and party-prohibition repair.
- Earthquake, solar eclipse, and epidemic notices place the reign inside [[NaturalDisasterPoliticalOmen|自然灾害政治征兆]] and [[CelestialOmenPoliticalResponsibility|天象政治责任]], even though this episode uses them mostly as annalistic pressure markers rather than a full omen remonstrance.
- The host compares a 157 CE registry above fifty-six million with a circa-220 official registry of 7.67 million and treats war, natural disaster, epidemic, and social disorder as linked drivers of [[LateHanPopulationCollapse|东汉末年人口崩塌]]. This is a source-scoped demographic frame, not a complete verified mortality accounting.
- The episode lists repeated Three Excellencies changes, including [[QiaoXuanLateHan|乔玄]], [[LaiYanLateHan|来艳]], [[EmpressSongLateHan|宋皇后]], and other officeholders, as early evidence for [[CourtOfficeCapture|朝廷开府任官失序]] and late-Han top-office churn.
- Emperor Ling remembers Dou Miao and [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] as accession supporters and temporarily increases treatment for Dou Miao after Dong Meng speaks for her.
- [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] and [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]] convert their old grievance against Dou Wu into an accusation that Dong Meng has slandered [[EmpressDowagerDongLateHan|董太后]], leading to Dong Meng's imprisonment and death.
- Xianbei pressure appears at the end of this Hanji 48 segment through an attack on Bingzhou, moving the frontier branch earlier than the previous Hanji 903 marker.
