# EP132-“领证三天前，我恐婚了！”

## Summary
This [[WushichaYanjiusuo|无时差研究所]] episode uses 《三十而已》 and several participants' experiences to discuss marriage anxiety, cohabitation, childbearing, infidelity, divorce, domestic work, and the movement from individual choice toward shared household responsibility. Its strongest synthesis is that marriage cannot eliminate future uncertainty: [[PremaritalAlignmentInquiry|premarital alignment inquiry]] can test present conduct and clarify children, work, family, and responsibility, while accepting that illness, betrayal, or later change cannot be fully rehearsed.

The episode also develops [[CoupleLevelDecisionMaking|couple-level decision-making]]. Career moves, relocation, fertility, parenting, and household rules should make each person's costs visible and treat the couple or new family as a real decision unit without requiring either partner to erase an independent self. [[PreventiveRelationshipMaintenance]] and [[FamilyLaborBoundaries]] make that principle operational through explicit needs, appreciation, timely objections, shared experiences, differentiated parenting roles, and clear executable rules.

## Key Claims
- [[PremaritalAlignmentInquiry]] combines genuine affection with observation of character, cohabitation where appropriate, and direct discussion of children, family background, work, money, and responsibility.
- Fear before marriage cannot be resolved by hypothetical guarantees about every future crisis; judgment remains based on present conduct, known character, and experiences already shared.
- A partner's sacrifice, contribution, need, or dissatisfaction should be made explicit rather than treated as self-evident, and appreciation does not prevent the recipient from explaining what support would actually help.
- [[CoupleLevelDecisionMaking]] requires major career or city moves to include effects on housing, distance, cost, care, and the other partner's life rather than being presented as an individual decision with household consequences added later.
- Marriage should not require permanent self-erasure. Non-principled accommodation can alternate, but chronic unilateral sacrifice creates resentment and weakens the relationship's capacity to grow.
- [[PreventiveRelationshipMaintenance]] includes naming coldness early, creating shared goals and experiences, preserving physical affection, and making limited after-work time intentional rather than waiting for a crisis.
- [[FamilyLaborBoundaries]] do not require identical tasks: partners may contribute through different parenting or domestic roles when the division is visible, mutually accepted, and revisable.
- Accepting that marriage can end may reduce fear by separating relationship failure from personal worth, but it does not make safety, legal, financial, or parenting consequences trivial.
