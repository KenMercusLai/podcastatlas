# 写一份《纪律.md》，代替我入市投资

## Summary
This [[Mianji|面基]] episode turns [[TrendFollowing]] into [[ProgrammableTradingDiscipline|可编程交易纪律]]: choose the tradable universe, define entry and exit states, set position limits, then separate signals and execution from opinion and emotion. It extends [[NoPredictionTrading]] by treating repeated small losses as the cost of reaching a few [[FatTailRisk|fat-tail winners]], and it sharpens [[QuantitativeOverfitting]] by preferring simple cross-asset rules over historically optimized parameters. A second branch introduces [[AttentionAsAsset|注意力资产]], a proposed product frame for tracking marginal changes in public attention rather than only follower totals.

## Key Claims
- Trend trading is presented as a repeated-game system rather than a promise about the next trade: low hit rates can remain viable when losses are bounded and occasional winners are allowed to run.
- The proposed “纪律.md” has three layers: the eligible markets and instruments, explicit entry and exit states, and position rules at both instrument and portfolio level.
- A daily system can convert market data into signals, email or dashboard tasks, and target-position changes, while a fixed executor reduces interference from public commentary, profit excitement, and intraday emotion.
- The episode treats persistent decline as less damaging to trend systems than repeated high-level whipsaw, because a weak market may produce no entry while choppy markets generate false signals.
- Cross-asset comparison matters: the method scans equities, futures, bonds, and property rather than forcing trades in one depleted opportunity pool.
- Historical testing can build confidence, but repeated parameter changes to maximize Sharpe ratio or minimize drawdown can turn accidental history into [[QuantitativeOverfitting|overfit rules]].
- Rule-based trend systems and factor models embody different approaches: the first accepts limited prediction and engineers asymmetric payoffs, while the second combines variables for predictive power and may produce smoother returns until model failure.
- The proposed [[AttentionAsAsset|attention-asset]] product would measure changes in topic, industry, account, and content attention for creator benchmarking, advertising, and topic selection rather than rank accounts only by followers.
- Personal attention is treated as a finite daily cost, while attention received from others can become distribution, revenue, or opportunity.
