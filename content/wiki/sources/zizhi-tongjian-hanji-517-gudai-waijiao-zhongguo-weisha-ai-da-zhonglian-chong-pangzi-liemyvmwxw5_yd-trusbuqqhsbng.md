# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》517丨古代外交，中国为啥爱打肿脸充胖子？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the [[JibinKingdomWesternHan|罽宾]] rupture in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-516-gudai-zhongguo-weihe-hui-yu-zhege-guojia-duanjiao-llfmuseg49szpxvypvfsp4naimiy|Hanji 516]]. After Jibin again sends an apology mission, [[DuQinWesternHan|杜钦]] argues that its envoys are low-status merchants using tribute as trade, that Jibin's alignment has little effect on Western Regions security, and that a full escort would expose Han personnel to hunger, robbery, mountain illness, falls, and supply failure for little strategic return.

The durable synthesis is a two-sided [[DiplomaticCostRiskAccounting|diplomatic cost-risk account]]. Courts should not cut a modest expense when doing so risks a valuable relationship, but neither should they pay prestige costs automatically when the counterpart is remote, unreliable, and strategically marginal. The adopted compromise—escort only to Pishan—preserves limited ceremony while matching the commitment to Han's practical reach. The second half connects [[LoulanKingdomWesternHan|楼兰]], Central Asian artifacts, Greek and Roman imagery, Hermes, and the [[AncientSilkRoad|ancient Silk Road]], but its archaeological and transcontinental claims remain episode-attributed rather than independently verified here.

## Key Claims

- Jibin's earlier Han-backed ruler later rebelled and killed Han envoys, while distance made punishment difficult.
- The new delegation consists neither of royal relatives nor senior officials; Du Qin reads it as a merchant mission using tribute language to obtain trade and rewards.
- Jibin cannot readily threaten Han or neighboring Western Regions states, so its submission or refusal carries limited system-level security value.
- A full return escort would cross multiple polities beyond Han control and expose more than one hundred soldiers and envoys to robbery, hunger, poor provisioning, altitude or heat illness, narrow paths, and fatal falls.
- Du Qin therefore rejects an all-or-nothing choice and recommends escort only to [[PishanKingdomWesternHan|皮山国]], a bounded commitment that [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] accepts after Wang Feng reports it.
- The case extends [[TributeSystemEconomicIncentive|朝贡体系经济激励]] by showing merchants presenting commerce as tribute when imperial rewards and protected access make that framing profitable.
- The archaeology branch says Greek and Roman motifs reached Xinjiang and uses Hermes as a symbol joining travel, merchants, messengers, and cultural exchange along the ancient Silk Road.

## Key Quotes

> "其使者非贵人贵臣，皆贾市名献" - Du Qin's distinction between a political mission and merchants presenting trade as tribute.

> "送至皮山而还" - the compromise that limits the escort to the edge of a more defensible commitment.
