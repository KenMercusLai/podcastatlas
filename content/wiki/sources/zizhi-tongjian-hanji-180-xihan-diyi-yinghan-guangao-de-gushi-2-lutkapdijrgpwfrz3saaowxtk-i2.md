# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》180｜西汉第一硬汉贯高的故事（2）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the [[GuanGao|贯高]] case after [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-180-xihan-diyi-yinghan-guangao-de-gushi-1-li5u-dmsmb1lp4ny3rk02kaiyu4k|Hanji 180 part 1]]. [[LiuBang|刘邦]] pardons Guan Gao after [[ZhangAo|张敖]] is cleared, but Guan Gao kills himself once his duty is complete. The host then uses [[XunYue|荀悦]] and [[SimaGuang|司马光]] to separate personal loyalty, legal guilt, and policy causality: Guan Gao can be truthful and brave without becoming innocent, and Zhang Ao's loss of Zhao fits Liu Bang's wider rollback of non-Liu kings.

## Key Claims
- Liu Bang admires Guan Gao's courage and loyalty enough to pardon him, but Guan Gao treats the pardon as morally unusable because the charge of plotting regicide remains.
- Guan Gao's suicide completes [[TortureResistantExoneration|刑讯守口式申冤]]: he survived only long enough to prove Zhang Ao's innocence, then refused to live under the assassination stain.
- [[XunYue|荀悦]] supplies the strict legal judgment: private loyalty and truthful exoneration do not cancel the public crime of plotting against the ruler.
- The host accepts that Guan Gao deserves criticism even if his loyalty to Zhang Ao is emotionally compelling.
- [[SimaGuang|司马光]] divides responsibility three ways: Liu Bang's arrogance helps trigger betrayal, Guan Gao's violence harms Zhang Ao, and Zhang Ao loses his kingdom in the aftermath.
- The host qualifies Sima Guang's causality by arguing that Zhang Ao was likely to be removed anyway because Liu Bang was already reducing different-surname kings.
- The Zhang Ao case therefore extends [[DifferentSurnameKingSuspicion|异姓诸侯王猜忌]] and [[SameSurnameKingEnfeoffment|同姓王分封]]: an exonerated non-Liu king can still be stripped of title when his court network proves dangerous.
- [[TianShuHan|田叔]] and [[MengShuHan|孟叔]] follow Zhang Ao despite a threat of collective punishment, then receive Liu Bang's praise and official appointments after Zhang Ao is cleared.
- The host reads those appointments as more than admiration for loyal retainers: Liu Bang needs to pacify Zhao-region elites after Zhang Ao's removal and the deaths of Guan Gao and [[ZhaoWuZhaoMinister|赵午]].
- The source distinguishes [[Shiji|《史记》]] and [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] on the number of Zhang Ao followers and treats the claim that no Han ministers exceeded Tian Shu and Meng Shu as courtly overstatement.
- The closing chronology note questions the [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] placement of [[XiaoHe|萧何]]'s promotion to xiangguo, preferring the [[Shiji|《史记》]] Xiao Xiangguo biography's later Han eleventh-year explanation.

## Key Quotes
> "私人的品德无法抵消法律上的罪行" - the episode's summary of Xun Yue's judgment on Guan Gao.

> "汉朝大臣没有能胜过他们" - the Shiji-linked praise of Tian Shu and Meng Shu, treated by the host as obvious overstatement.

> "汉十一年才是萧何拜相国的真正时间" - the host's source-critical preference on Xiao He's promotion date.
