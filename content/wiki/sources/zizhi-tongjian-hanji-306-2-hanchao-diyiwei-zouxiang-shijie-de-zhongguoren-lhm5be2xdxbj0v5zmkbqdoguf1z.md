# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》306-2｜汉朝第一位走向世界的中国人！

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[ZhangQianWesternHan|张骞]]'s return by turning his report on [[DayuanWesternHan|大宛]], [[DaxiaWesternHan|大夏]], [[Wusun|乌孙]], and [[Kangju|康居]] into a route-planning problem. After seeing bamboo staffs and fine cloth associated with Shu in Daxia, Zhang infers that a trade path may connect southwest China to the polity the episode title calls [[ShenduWesternHan|身毒]], offering a possible alternative to routes exposed to Qiang or [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] control.

[[HanWudi|汉武帝]] authorizes a multi-route search from Shu and Qianwei. The missions fail to reach their intended destination because local closures, fragmented authority, robbery, and resistance around the Kunming groups block passage, but they do reach [[DianKingdomWesternHan|滇国]]. The durable synthesis is therefore one of [[CommodityTraceRouteInference|商品踪迹式路线推断]] under weak geographic knowledge: traded objects generate a strategic hypothesis, while failed reconnaissance still produces new information and redirects imperial attention toward the Southwestern Yi.

## Key Claims

- Zhang Qian describes a differentiated western political geography: Dayuan and Daxia have settled agriculture and cities, while Wusun, Kangju, Yancai, and Yuezhi are presented as more mobile and culturally closer to Xiongnu.
- Shu bamboo staffs and fine cloth found in Daxia become evidence for a possible southern trade connection rather than proof of a direct Han-controlled road.
- Zhang compares route risks and argues that departure from Shu may bypass the Qiang- and Xiongnu-exposed approaches.
- Han Wudi treats the report as an opportunity for diplomacy, trade, prestige, and expansion, then sends Wang Ranyu and other envoys along four exploratory routes from Shu and Qianwei.
- The missions advance only partway because access is blocked by local polities, fragmented groups, and robbery; the episode emphasizes that Han intent exceeds its geographic knowledge and coercive reach.
- The envoys reach Dian, where the ruler entertains them and helps search westward, but Kunming resistance prevents onward travel to Shendu.
- Although the route search fails at its stated objective, information about Dian and the Southwestern Yi encourages further Han interest in the region.

## Key Quotes

> “汉与我孰大？” - the episode's reported question from the ruler of Dian, used to illustrate the information limits created by geographic separation.

> “应该想办法让它归附。” - the episode's summary of the envoys' recommendation after learning more about Dian.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with the canonical wiki was found. The episode supplies an earlier observation-and-reconnaissance layer before the later recurring Western Regions missions represented on Zhang Qian and the ancient Silk Road pages.
- The title says 身毒, while the supplied summary repeatedly renders the destination as “渊都/运都国.” This note normalizes the intended target to [[ShenduWesternHan|身毒]] because of the title and narrative context, but preserves the transcription mismatch as source-scoped rather than silently treating the wording as certain.
- The presence of Shu-associated goods in Daxia supports indirect circulation and a route hypothesis; it does not prove a direct Shu-Daxia road, identify every intermediary, or establish that Zhang Qian personally traveled the proposed southwestern path.
- Distances, polity descriptions, route safety, named envoys, four-route design, travel extent, local motives, exact obstacles, Dian's scale, royal dialogue, and the recommendation to seek submission remain episode-attributed pending primary-source and archaeological comparison.
- The closing Huo Qubing material is a preview only. It does not provide the campaign's operations or outcome and therefore does not reopen his canonical profile.
