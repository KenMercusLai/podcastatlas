# 406. The Nazis in Power: Hitler’s Road to War (Part 3)

## Summary

This [[TheRestIsHistory]] episode follows [[AdolfHitler]] from the ideological claim that racial struggle made war necessary through the first major diplomatic and military gambles of [[NaziGermany|Nazi Germany]]. It connects [[NaziRearmamentWarEconomy|rearmament, economic recovery, import dependence, and future conquest]] rather than treating them as separate policies.

The decisive case is the [[RhinelandRemilitarization|remilitarization of the Rhineland]] in March 1936. Hitler repeatedly tested opponents whose guilt over the postwar settlement, fear of another war, and weakening cooperation made restraint look safer than confrontation; when France and Britain did not act against a still-vulnerable Germany, the success transformed the strategic balance and strengthened Hitler’s faith in escalating risk.

## Key Claims

- Hitler treated struggle, racial competition, eastern “living space,” and war as central to Germany’s future rather than as temporary rhetoric.
- Conservative military, business, and popular support could overlap with Hitler’s revisionism without sharing the full radical racial program.
- [[NaziRearmamentWarEconomy|The rearmament economy]] used military spending and national restoration to reduce unemployment and build support while worsening food, raw-material, foreign-exchange, and consumer constraints.
- Withdrawal from the League of Nations, the non-aggression pact with Poland, and plebiscitary politics combined nationalist symbolism with attempts to reduce Germany’s strategic exposure.
- [[BenitoMussolini]]’s 1934 defense of Austrian independence forced Hitler to retreat after [[EngelbertDollfuss]]’s murder, showing that firm external resistance could still constrain him.
- The Stresa Front weakened when Britain made a separate naval agreement with Germany and Italy’s invasion of Abyssinia fractured cooperation among the former Allies.
- [[RhinelandRemilitarization|The Rhineland operation]] was militarily reversible if France intervened but became politically transformative when no intervention came.
- Success increased Hitler’s domestic prestige, reduced France’s ability to threaten western Germany, and encouraged further autarky, armament, Axis alignment, and expansion toward [[Austria]].

## Key Quotes

> “living space” - the eastward territorial objective Hitler presented to army leaders soon after taking power.

> “worldwide Jewry” - Hitler’s conspiratorial racial framing of the coming struggle as reported by the episode.
