# Federica Fornaciari on Leadership and Why Business Transformation Fails | Tomorrow Today

## Summary
This [[TomorrowToday]] episode has [[ShekharNatarajan]] interview [[FedericaFornaciari]] about cross-cultural work, consulting, agency leadership, organizational transformation, crisis communication, personal resilience, and AI. Drawing on work at [[BainAndCompany]], [[Havas]], and with [[Telefonica]], Fornaciari argues that strategy creates little value when boards, executives, advisers, and operating teams separate thinking from delivery or use advisers to transfer responsibility.

The episode's central contribution is [[StrategyExecutionAccountability]]: boards set long-term direction, executives own implementation, advisers contribute independent perspective, and outcomes require explicit responsibility across the whole chain. [[TransformationAsAssumptionRevision]] makes transformation behavioral and cultural rather than merely technological, while [[CEOAsOrganizationalOperatingSystem]] and [[HumanLeadershipUnderAI]] preserve observation, coordination, judgment, verification, ethics, and belonging as AI compresses analytical work.

## Key Claims
- [[FedericaFornaciari]] presents cross-cultural experience, economics, marketing, teaching, consulting, and agency operations as complementary sources of transformation judgment.
- [[StrategyExecutionAccountability]] joins strategic thought to implementation and rejects consulting reports, delegated middle-management ownership, or prestigious advice as substitutes for executive responsibility.
- The RACI model can clarify who is responsible, accountable, consulted, and informed, while outcome-linked fees can make adviser incentives more visible.
- [[TransformationAsAssumptionRevision]] begins when leaders and employees question established beliefs and practices; technology cannot compensate for cultural resistance or organizational bottlenecks.
- [[CEOAsOrganizationalOperatingSystem]] treats the chief executive as the visible observer and coordinator of the enterprise, especially during crisis, restructuring, and M&A.
- [[HumanLeadershipUnderAI]] keeps criteria, verification, ethical intent, interpretation, education, and belonging with people while using AI to remove low-value work and improve end-to-end processes.
- AI-mediated discovery changes customer journeys because buyers may ask systems which suppliers to consider, but retention still depends on human operations and delivery.
- Productivity gains can coexist with job loss, polarization, cognitive surrender, and scaled organizational misalignment; responsible adoption therefore requires coordinated incentives and implementation.

## Key Quotes
> “Executives are called executives because they execute.” - the episode's summary of Fornaciari's accountability argument.

> “Everything and nothing is strategy.” - Fornaciari's warning that strategy without delivery becomes an empty label.
