# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1118丨赤壁战败后，曹操最怕发生什么事？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]]'s death in 210 CE, [[LuSu|鲁肃]]'s succession to his forces, and [[SunQuan|孙权]]'s decision to let [[LiuBei|刘备]] hold a larger Jingzhou base. It argues that [[CaoCao|曹操]] feared this post-[[RedCliffsBattle|赤壁之战]] political change more than the single battlefield loss because a territorially grounded Liu Bei and a more durable Sun-Liu alignment raised the long-run cost of southern conquest.

The episode then turns from coalition strategy to talent development. Sun Quan's reading advice helps [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]] expand beyond a brave but undereducated commander, while recommendations from Lu Su and [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] lead Liu Bei to reassess [[PangTong|庞统]] after an initially poor appointment. Together these cases distinguish initial visibility from developed capability and show that a leader can correct an early personnel judgment.

## Key Claims

- Zhou Yu dies while returning toward Jiangling, ending his proposed westward campaign and creating a command-succession problem for Eastern Wu.
- Lu Su succeeds to Zhou Yu's forces and continues an alliance-centered strategy toward Liu Bei rather than the episode's attributed Zhou Yu plan to constrain or eliminate him.
- Sun Quan accepts Lu Su's advice to let Liu Bei hold Jingzhou territory, while Cheng Pu receives the Nanjun appointment and Lu Su is stationed at Lukou amid wider administrative changes.
- The host distinguishes Red Cliffs as a tactical defeat from the later strategic problem of Liu Bei gaining territory, depth, revenue, and a more durable partnership with Sun Quan.
- Cao Cao's reported shock at the Jingzhou arrangement is read as fear that Sun Quan and Liu Bei can no longer be isolated and defeated separately.
- The episode presents Liu Bei's Jingzhou foothold as the point at which portable reputation, followers, and resilience become a territorial base capable of supporting a tripartite balance.
- Sun Quan urges Lyu Meng to read history for practical judgment rather than become a classical scholar; later conversation causes Lu Su to revise his view of Lyu Meng, producing the “士别三日，当刮目相待” story.
- Pang Tong is underused after Zhou Yu's death and is later dismissed from a county post under Liu Bei, but recommendations from Lu Su and Zhuge Liang prompt a deeper interview and appointment as `治中` and then `军师中郎将`.

## Key Quotes

> “手中之笔应声落地。” - the episode's image of Cao Cao's reported shock after learning that Liu Bei had gained the Jingzhou foothold.

> “士别三日，当刮目相待。” - the episode's summary of Lu Su's revised judgment after Lyu Meng's study.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is recorded. The source extends the existing Red Cliffs sequence from battle, retreat, and Jiangling competition into command succession and a new postwar distribution of strategic capacity.
- Strategic qualification: “借荆州” is a compressed and contested label for a complicated territorial settlement. The episode's claim that this single decision transformed a temporary coalition into a stable military alliance is retained as interpretation, not as a complete account of Sun-Liu relations.
- Causal qualification: Cao Cao's reported dropped brush, fear, and exact reasoning do not independently prove that Liu Bei's foothold mattered more than disease, military losses, geography, or other postwar constraints.
- Talent qualification: the claims that Sun Quan rejected Pang Tong because of appearance, that Pang Tong deliberately neglected county duties, and the exact dialogue and office sequence require comparison with underlying historical texts.
- The opening “物极必反” and modern business-cycle analogies are motivational framing, not evidence that adversity automatically reverses or that persistence guarantees political recovery.
