## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode explains Qin Shi Huang's thirty-third year, 214 BCE, through [[QinState|秦国]]'s continuing southern and northern campaigns after unification. It argues that Qin's expansion was not only [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]]'s personal will, but a consequence of [[ShangYangReforms|商鞅变法]], [[FarmingWarStatecraft|耕战逻辑]], and the [[MilitaryMeritRankSystem|军功爵制]] becoming a state engine. The episode also links the dangerous status of赘婿, fugitives, and merchants to [[MarginalStatusConscription|边缘身份征发]], follows the new [[Baiyue|百越]] commanderies of [[GuilinCommandery|桂林郡]], [[NanhaiCommandery|南海郡]], and [[XiangCommandery|象郡]], then continues [[MengTian|蒙恬]]'s northern war against [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] and [[ZhangLiang|张良]]'s encounter with [[HuangshiGong|黄石公]].

## Key Claims
- Qin's post-unification wars should not be reduced to Qin Shi Huang's personality: the episode reads them through [[PostUnificationWarInertia|统一后战争惯性]] created by a long-running military-reward order.
- [[ShangYangReforms|商鞅变法]] made war a social mobility channel, so a unified empire inherited soldiers and risk-seeking subjects who expected battlefield merit rather than a quiet return to farming.
- The source's title turns on赘婿: in the host's reading, a man who entered his wife's household occupied a low-status position and could be prioritized for dirty, hard, or dangerous state work.
- Qin's southern campaign drafts捕亡人,赘婿, and merchants because the state treats them as unstable or socially less useful, making conscription both military mobilization and social sorting.
- The Baiyue campaign is followed by direct administrative incorporation through [[GuilinCommandery|桂林郡]], [[NanhaiCommandery|南海郡]], and [[XiangCommandery|象郡]], plus the migration of fifty myriads of convicts to guard and live among southern Yue people.
- The episode extends [[ConquestCommanderyTransition|灭国后的郡县化过渡]] from defeated Warring States regions into the deeper Lingnan campaign after unification.
- The northern campaign continues the "亡秦者胡" branch from [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-123-1-shen-zhi-yuyan-wangqin-zhe-hu-jing-shi-zhi-lg7m3vfdbcweb4joxe4jvhxjx6y8|Qinji 123-1]]: Meng Tian defeats Xiongnu, recovers the Hetao area, sets up forty-four counties, links older northern walls, and sits at [[Shangjun|上郡]] for a long frontier war.
- Zhang Liang's story moves from failed assassin to future strategist when Huangshi Gong tests his patience and gives him [[TaigongBingfa|《太公兵法》]].
- The closing "焚书坑儒" mention is only a forward pointer; this source does not yet analyze the incident itself.
