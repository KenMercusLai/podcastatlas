# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》772丨他为何宁愿成为逃犯，也不愿继承家业？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 98-102 CE [[EmperorHeOfHan|汉和帝]] layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-773-donghan-meinv-zhengzhijia-dengsui-ruhe-nixi-cheng-huanghou-lsmm5ngthj368guumm2xbwc3vea5|Hanji 773]]. It completes [[LiuKaiEasternHan|刘恺]]'s title-refusal story: Liu gives the hereditary marquisate to his younger brother [[LiuXianEasternHan|刘宪]], flees rather than accept investiture, and later receives both imperial tolerance and appointment after [[JiaKuiEasternHan|贾逵]] reframes the case as ritual yielding.

The durable synthesis is that the source refuses a simple morality tale. Liu Kai's conduct violates hereditary succession order yet becomes [[RitualRenunciationPoliticalCapital|礼让辞封式政治资本]], while the frontier half shows [[MiTangQiangLeader|弥唐]]'s failed surrender, renewed [[ShaodangQiang|烧当羌]] rebellion, defeat, relocation, and the opening of [[CaoFengLateHan|曹凤]]'s resource-and-tuntian policy that Hanji 773 develops.

## Key Claims

- Liu Kai should inherit Liu Ban's Juchao marquisate, but he lets his younger brother Liu Xian take the position and remains in flight after the court initially refrains from punishment.
- Jia Kui argues that ordinary legal handling should not crush visible ritual yielding, and Emperor He allows Liu Xian to inherit while warning that the case is exceptional and should not become a general precedent.
- The host stresses that the episode is not only about virtue: Liu Kai's refusal also creates public fame, imperial praise, and a later court career as a lang and eventually a high minister.
- The title-refusal case therefore joins legal succession order, Confucian rhetoric, reputation, and office access rather than fitting cleanly into "selfless humility."
- In 98 CE, the Southern Xiongnu chanyu dies and Tan succeeds as Wanshi Shizhudi chanyu, keeping frontier succession visible in the same annal layer.
- Mi Tang's Qiang group arrives in Luoyang with fewer than two thousand hungry people, asks to remain near the capital, is ordered back to the large and small Yu valleys, stalls with hunger and road excuses, and then rebels again.
- After Mi Tang gathers Huangzhong Hu groups for raiding, Zhou Wei and Hou Ba defeat the Shaodang forces; more than six thousand surrenderers are moved to Hanyang, Anding, and Longxi, and Mi Tang's line collapses after he flees and dies.
- Emperor He's 99 CE classics discussion with Lu Pi, Jia Kui, Huang Xiang, and others models explicit, non-punitive debate over textual difficulties rather than empty deference.
- The court adjusts xiaolian recommendation cadence for Youzhou, Bingzhou, and Liangzhou because sparse frontier populations and heavy service burdens make ordinary personnel quotas less suitable.
- The closing Cao Feng memorial points toward garrison farming along Qinghai and the Huangshui, connecting Qiang disorder to frontier resource integration rather than only pursuit and punishment.

## Key Quotes

> "王法崇善，成人之美" - Emperor He's justification for allowing the succession exception.

> "以名换官" - the host's compact interpretation of Liu Kai's eventual payoff.

> "不应只讲相互退让" - Lu Pi's debate norm as summarized in the source.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is recorded. This source backfills the Emperor-He-era sequence before Hanji 773's fuller Cao Feng / West Sea policy and before Liu Kai's later An-era office record.
