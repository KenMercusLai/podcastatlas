# 176: 姚顺宇，来到腾讯300天

## Summary
This [[LateTalk]] episode uses [[GaoHonghao]]'s reporting on [[YaoShunyu]] to explain how [[TencentHunyuan]] changed after Yao joined [[Tencent]]. The source frames Hunyuan's improvement as an organizational restart rather than a simple benchmark breakthrough: younger research talent, new pretraining/post-training/evaluation/infra leaders, more executive backing, and closer product loops all matter. Its broader synthesis is that Tencent's AI problem sits between [[FederatedAIOrganization]] and centralization: Hunyuan wants shared frontier-model capability, while [[WeChatVLM]] remains separate because [[WeChat]] protects privacy, user data, product culture, and massive-scale reliability.

## Key Claims
- [[TencentHunyuan]]'s mood and execution had improved versus the prior year, but [[GaoHonghao]] cautions that this is not a sudden leap into the top frontier tier.
- The source treats Hunyuan 3 as a staged organizational delivery: a roughly 300B [[MixtureOfExperts|MoE]] model meant to rebuild morale, test the new team, and establish a baseline rather than immediately win the whole model race.
- [[DeepSeek]]'s 2025 impact pushed Tencent executives to rethink whether older search, advertising, and recommendation veterans were enough for frontier-model work.
- Tencent reportedly chose to keep the Hunyuan structure and replace key roles instead of building a fully separate young team to race against it.
- [[YaoShunyu]] entered Tencent's view through high-level recruiting conversations, then became more attractive after he wanted to return to China in 2025.
- Yao's "Second Half" logic is that the next phase of AI depends on defining useful problems and exploiting real context, which made Tencent and [[Meta]] appealing because they own social, office, search, content, music, video, and game surfaces.
- [[MartinLau]] is presented as Yao's strongest internal sponsor, giving him money, people, compute, time, and permission to replace key leaders while reporting also to [[LuShanTencent]].
- Hunyuan's early compute and talent base is described as thin: it began inside [[TencentTEG]], used only about 2,000 cards from the advertising side at first, and lacked enough frontier-research direction.
- Yao quickly replaced leaders across pretraining, post-training, evaluation, and infrastructure, including infra talent from the ByteDance Seed Infra system.
- Most displaced veterans reportedly stayed inside Tencent rather than being cut, making the change organizationally forceful but not framed as a large layoff.
- Hunyuan's hiring appeal improved because Yao had [[OpenAI]] credibility and could offer new leadership openings and stronger compensation to young researchers.
- Tencent AI applications are described as concentrated around [[TencentCSIG]], including [[Yuanbao]], [[TencentWorkBuddy]], CodeBuddy, IMA, Marvis, and design agents.
- Hunyuan is building a reinforcement-learning platform first tied to Yuanbao and WorkBuddy, making [[AgentRL]] a product-feedback and post-training infrastructure question rather than only a model-lab technique.
- [[WeChat]] and Hunyuan cooperate, but [[WeChatVLM]] remains independent under [[ZhouHaoTencent]], partly because [[ZhangXiaolong]] prioritizes privacy, data control, and product fit.
- The source predicts Hunyuan and WeChat VLM are likely to coexist for a long time, even though scarce compute makes duplicate model work strategically costly.
- The episode argues that AI may test Tencent's historical federation: business autonomy helped product judgment and edge innovation, but frontier AI may require more centralized resources and shared infrastructure.
- Tencent is still described as a chaser behind [[ByteDance]], [[Alibaba]], [[DeepSeek]], [[KimiK3]], and [[Qwen]] in parts of the Chinese AI competition; catching up would require first-tier model results, not only better organization.
- The episode says Chinese big-tech AI attention has moved from chatbot-entry fights toward coding and broader office agents, connecting [[AIProgrammingEngineShift]] to [[ModelProviderToolCompetition]].

## Key Quotes
> "组织恢复" - the episode's implicit frame for Hunyuan's near-term progress.

> "摸高" - how the source contrasts Hunyuan's ambition with WeChat's product-scale constraints.

> "拧成一股绳" - the centralization pressure Tencent executives may feel under AI compute scarcity.
