## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 88 CE accession and pre-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-760-lishi-jiemi-huanghou-chugui-bei-zhua-xiachang-can-bucan-lgz2sc9xt8ulltvwemiz2uzfghqi|Hanji 760]] layer around [[EmpressDowagerDouHeLateHan|窦太后]], [[DouXianEasternHan|窦宪]], and the opening Northern Xiongnu policy debate. It follows [[EmperorZhangOfHan|汉章帝]]'s death, the child [[EmperorHeOfHan|刘肇]]'s accession, Dou-family entry into palace authority, [[CuiYinEasternHan|崔骃]]'s warning and service to Dou Xian, [[DengBiaoEasternHan|邓彪]]'s use as a formal Taifu channel, Dou Xian's revenge killing tied to Han Yu's family, and a fake-edict maneuver that sends five royal princes away from Luoyang.

The frontier half turns Northern Xiongnu weakness into a policy dispute. [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] asks Han to join an attack on [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|北匈奴]], [[GengBingEasternHan|耿秉]] supports using one frontier group against another, while [[SongYiEasternHan|宋意]] argues that [[Xianbei|鲜卑]] pressure, campaign costs, and Guangwu-era jimi policy make negotiated outer-screen management safer than opportunistic war.

## Key Claims

- Han Zhangdi's death and Liu Zhao's child accession put Empress Dowager Dou in a regency position that lets Dou Xian and the Dou brothers enter the court's confidential channel.
- Cui Yin warns Dou Xian that external relatives usually fail when rank and favor outrun humility, but he also becomes one of the talents Dou Xian attracts.
- Deng Biao's Taifu role gives Dou Xian a procedural shell for turning private initiative into Shangshu and dowager-approved policy.
- Dou Xian's killing of Han Yu's son to avenge his father's case shows power being routed into private revenge before the later Liu Chang murder.
- The five princes' joint memorial briefly checks Dou Xian, but Cui Yin's fake-edict solution sends them back to their fiefs and removes that pressure.
- The Southern Xiongnu request creates a choice between Geng Bing's opportunistic offensive and Song Yi's restraint argument.
- Song Yi's memorial treats Xianbei incentives, campaign cost, and Northern Xiongnu surrender as frontier-balance reasons not to force an immediate campaign.

## Key Quotes

> "天狂必有雨，人狂必有祸" - opening moral frame for power arrogance.
