# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1009丨刘备投奔曹操，险些狼入虎口？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the late-Han sequence immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1010-sanguo-liubiao-jiujing-shibushi-piaoliang-de-caobao-lo58hzfo-qv0soauogtgfrkourrd|Hanji 1010]]. In 196 CE, [[LyuBu|吕布]] turns from helping [[LiuBei|刘备]] into attacking him once Liu Bei gathers more than ten thousand troops, creating a case of [[ThreatDrivenAllianceReversal|威胁感驱动的同盟反转]]. Liu Bei flees to [[CaoCao|曹操]], [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] argues against killing him because it would damage Cao Cao's talent-attraction reputation, and [[YuanHuanLateHan|袁涣]] later refuses Lü Bu's demand to write an abusive letter against Liu Bei.

## Key Claims
- In 196 CE, [[LyuBu|吕布]] had recently mediated a conflict involving [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s side and [[LiuBei|刘备]], but Liu Bei's growth to a force of more than ten thousand makes him look dangerous to Lü Bu.
- Lü Bu personally attacks Liu Bei, and Liu Bei loses the fight and flees to [[CaoCao|曹操]].
- Cao Cao treats Liu Bei well and has the court appoint him as Yuzhou governor, then gives him troops and grain to regroup near [[PeiCounty|小沛]] and continue resisting Lü Bu.
- When someone warns that Liu Bei is an ambitious hero who may become a future danger, [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] agrees about the danger but argues that killing a desperate guest would create a worse reputational cost.
- Guo Jia's advice is a case of [[ReputationConstrainedTalentProtection|名声约束下的人才保护]]: Cao Cao preserves his ability to attract capable people by not murdering a person who came to him for shelter.
- [[YuanHuanLateHan|袁涣]] refuses to write a letter abusing Liu Bei even under Lü Bu's threat, arguing that insult does not create moral shame and that slandering former patrons would make all future service dishonorable.
- The host reads Lü Bu's demand as [[MoralDisengagementRationalization|道德推脱式自我合理化]]: because he had benefited from Liu Bei before turning against him, public abuse would help him justify his own betrayal.
- The episode uses the [[DunningKrugerEffect|Dunning-Kruger effect]] source-scopedly to explain Lü Bu as a strong fighter whose low strategic self-awareness prevents learning and institutional upgrading.
- The final judgment is that Lü Bu and Liu Bei would have done better to remain aligned against Cao Cao, but Lü Bu's insecurity and short horizon push a possible ally into Cao Cao's camp.
