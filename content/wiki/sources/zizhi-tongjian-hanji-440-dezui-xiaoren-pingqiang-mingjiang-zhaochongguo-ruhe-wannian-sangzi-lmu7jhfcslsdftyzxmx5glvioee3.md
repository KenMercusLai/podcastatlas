# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》440｜得罪小人！平羌名将赵充国如何晚年丧子？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate aftermath of [[ZhaoChongguoWesternHan|赵充国]]'s Qiang campaign after [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-439-gongbi-weiqing-huoqubing-zhichang-guanlizhe-bangyang-zhaochongguo-lirqsoujwewu6efd-shb7mypqvu3|Hanji 439]]. It follows Zhao's refusal to recode the campaign as a victory for Xin Wuxian and Xu Yanshou, his objection to Xin Tang as Protector of the Qiang, Xin Tang's later drunken abuse of Qiang people, and the retaliatory accusation that leads Zhao's son Zhao Ang to die in prison.

The durable synthesis is that Zhao's frontier judgment remained strong after the battlefield ended, but truthful reporting and appointment opposition created court enemies. The episode also extends the frontier layer from military success into administrative settlement: Qiang groups surrender, Jincheng dependent-state arrangements are created, and the later Zhao Kuan stele lets the host read Zhao's family as becoming rooted in the Hehuang region.

## Key Claims

- Zhao Chongguo asks to dismiss the屯田 force only after reporting that most Qiang forces have been killed, drowned, starved, or surrendered and that the remaining fugitives are manageable through submitted leaders.
- A friend urges Zhao to attribute success to Xin Wuxian and Xu Yanshou for self-protection, but Zhao says military affairs require truthful reporting to the ruler and a model for later generations.
- Zhao's report leads [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] to remove Xin Wuxian from the broken-Qiang command and restore Zhao as rear general, creating an interest and reputation grievance.
- Qiang leaders including Dize, Yang Diao, Liang, and Miwang submit with the remaining people of Jiangong and Huangdi, and the court creates a Jincheng dependent state for surrendered Qiang.
- Zhao opposes Xin Tang's appointment as Protector of the Qiang because of drunkenness and impulsiveness; Xuan first accepts Zhao's warning, but Xin Tang later receives the post after Xin Linzhong falls ill.
- Xin Tang repeatedly gets drunk and abuses Qiang people, helping trigger renewed rebellion.
- Xin Wuxian retaliates by secretly accusing Zhao's son Zhao Ang of leaking secrets; Zhao Ang is handed to censors and dies in prison rather than endure humiliation.
- The Zhao Kuan stele and Ma Yuan allusion let the episode frame Zhao's family as a frontier-rooted lineage rather than only a one-campaign military family.

## Key Quotes

> "不能因为自己避免贪功的嫌疑，就欺骗皇上" - Zhao Chongguo's duty-to-truth posture as summarized by the episode.

> "湟中愿学赵充国，塞外当如马伏波" - the closing comparison between Zhao Chongguo and Ma Yuan as frontier models.
