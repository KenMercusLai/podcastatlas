# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》351｜卧底陪卧底睡？古代谍战破三观！

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode fills the diplomatic interval between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-349-2-ti-shang-kuzi-bu-ren-ren-ta-ruhe-rang-hanwudi-de-mouhua-paotang-lgmw_edznbj_oypxpnln93bzu7yr|Hanji 349-2]]'s succession opening and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-352-1-yi-pao-hong-mie-le-bainian-diguo-lgpuqijq5kyd5ivh8ajlclo8ojex|Hanji 352-1]]'s failed confrontation and coup. After [[ZhaoXingNanyue|赵兴]] succeeds in [[Nanyue|南越]], [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] sends [[AnGuoShaoJiWesternHan|安国少季]] and other envoys. The source frames both Shaoji and the Han-born [[QueenDowagerJiuNanyue|樛太后]] as long-planted agents and emphasizes their earlier intimate relationship, but formal agent status and private motives remain episode-attributed.

The queen dowager and Zhao Xing request a more interior-vassal-like relationship: triennial court attendance, removal of border barriers, Han-issued seals for senior officers, replacement of Nanyue punishments with Han law, and retention of envoys for pacification. Chancellor [[LyuJiaNanyue|吕嘉]], whose family holds extensive office and marriage ties, represents the locally rooted opposition. The episode therefore shows a proposed transition from [[NominalSubmissionLocalAutonomy|nominal submission with local autonomy]] toward institutional integration, not a completed peaceful annexation.

## Key Claims

- After Zhao Yingqi's death, Zhao Xing succeeds and his Han-born mother becomes queen dowager.
- Han Wudi sends a mission including An Guo Shaoji, whom the episode describes as the queen dowager's former lover and another long-positioned Han agent.
- The royal party favors closer Han incorporation, while Lü Jia's three-generation chancellorship, large officeholding kin network, royal marriages, and local support constrain the king and queen dowager.
- Queen Dowager Jiu asks that Nanyue be treated like an interior vassal polity, with court attendance every three years and the border barriers removed.
- Wudi approves the request, grants silver and other official seals, permits Zhao Xing to appoint remaining officials, replaces specified Nanyue punishments with Han law, and leaves envoys to reassure officials and residents.
- These measures separate several dimensions of incorporation—ritual attendance, border access, appointment, seals, law, and resident supervision—rather than reducing submission to a single declaration.

## Key Quotes

> “请比内诸侯，三岁一朝，除边关。” - the episode's formulation of the royal party's request for a more interior-vassal-like relationship.

> “用汉法，比内诸侯。” - the source's shorthand for the proposed legal and institutional convergence.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. This episode supplies the institutional proposal that explains why the royal party and Lü Jia are already divided in Hanji 352-1.
- Name normalization: the supplied summary writes “娇氏” and “少纪.” The wiki routes them to the established [[QueenDowagerJiuNanyue|樛太后]] and received [[AnGuoShaoJiWesternHan|安国少季]] forms rather than creating duplicate identities.
- The episode calls both figures “卧底” and says Wudi deliberately planted them over many years. The event sequence supports a Han-born queen dowager, a prior intimate tie, a Han envoy, and a pro-incorporation policy, but not by itself a documented formal intelligence commission for either person.
- The queen dowager's intimate relationship with Shaoji is politically consequential in the episode and later hostile rhetoric, but the source does not provide her perspective or establish how broadly the relationship was known.
- Lü Jia's exact number of officeholding relatives, comparative popularity, speeches, inner motives, and the claim that Wudi preferred peace remain episode-attributed.
- Approval of the reforms shows a proposed legal and administrative transition; it does not prove durable implementation before the later banquet crisis, coup, and war.
