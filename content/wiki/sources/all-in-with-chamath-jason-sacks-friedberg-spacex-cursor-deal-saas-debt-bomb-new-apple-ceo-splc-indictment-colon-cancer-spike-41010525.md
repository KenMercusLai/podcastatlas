All-In episode 270 connects AI strategy, software leverage, institutional accountability, and public-health science. The hosts discuss a reported [[SpaceX]]-[[Cursor]] arrangement involving [[XAI|xAI]], use [[Medallia]] and [[ThomaBravo|Thoma Bravo]] as a [[DebtFinancedSaaSRollupRisk]] case, debate [[Apple]] succession from [[TimCook|Tim Cook]] to [[JohnTernus|John Ternus]], and frame an alleged [[SouthernPovertyLawCenter]] indictment through [[NonprofitInformantAccountability]]. - A reported [[SpaceX]]-[[Cursor]]-[[XAI|xAI]] structure is framed as [[AICodingVerticalIntegration]]: application workflow, enterprise clients, training data, compute, and model ownership could be combined if the deal closes.
