# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》285｜他帮助汉武帝大一统，为何反被灭族？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the fall of [[ZhuFuYan|主父偃]] after [[LiuPengzuZhaoKingWesternHan|赵敬肃王刘彭祖]] reports him for bribery and for forcing [[QiLiwangWesternHan|齐厉王]] to his death. [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] initially judges that the admitted bribery does not require execution, but [[GongsunHong|公孙弘]] argues that leaving Zhufu Yan alive after the childless Qi king's suicide and the conversion of Qi into commanderies would make the court's conduct impossible to explain. The episode says the emperor then orders Zhufu Yan and his clan killed.

Its durable synthesis joins policy usefulness to political isolation. The same uncompromising conduct that made Zhufu Yan useful against princely houses, powerful families, and court colleagues also accumulated enemies and relationships based on fear or access rather than durable support. His abandonment at execution extends [[PowerContingentSocialTies|权势依附型交往]], while the source's reconstructions of Liu Pengzu's fear, Gongsun Hong's hidden warning to the emperor, Zhufu Yan's psychology, and the emperor's inability to resist collective pressure remain interpretations rather than settled inner motives.

## Key Claims

- Zhufu Yan admits taking bribes but denies causing the Qi king's death; Wudi initially considers releasing him because he does not regard the admitted offense as capital.
- Gongsun Hong argues that the Qi king died without an heir, Qi was absorbed into the commandery system, and the court cannot justify sparing the official blamed for that outcome.
- The episode presents the resulting execution and clan punishment as the product of the accusation, Gongsun Hong's intervention, wider court hostility, and imperial calculation rather than bribery alone.
- Zhufu Yan's attacks on princely houses, powerful families, colleagues, and [[DongZhongshu|董仲舒]] leave few actors willing to help when his access to Wudi fails.
- Thousands of former guests and fearful gift-givers do not become a rescue coalition; the episode says only one retainer comes to the execution ground.
- The host treats poverty, exclusion, and repeated rejection as possible contributors to Zhufu Yan's extremity, but does not establish a psychological causal chain.
- The closing [[KongZangWesternHan|孔臧]] branch contrasts lethal court struggle with an office choice: Kong declines the Yushi Dafu post and receives the Taichang office, where ritual, education, and classical learning better fit his stated aims.

## Key Quotes

> “杀主父偃以谢天下” - the episode's rendering of Gongsun Hong's argument that Zhufu Yan must die to answer for the Qi outcome.

> “宾客千人，无一人来送我” - the episode's image of status-based association disappearing at Zhufu Yan's execution.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with Hanji 284 was found. This episode continues its arrest setup by adding the trial, Gongsun Hong's intervention, execution, and social-isolation aftermath.
- The source calls Zhufu Yan the proposer of the 推恩令 and links that policy to unification, but it does not explain the measure's text, implementation, chronology, or exclusive authorship; those claims remain source-scoped rather than becoming a full policy synthesis.
- The episode's exact execution year, wording, number of guests, final encounter, and clan-punishment scope were not independently collated with primary texts here.
- Liu Pengzu's preventive fear, Gongsun Hong's implied warning about imperial land hunger, Wudi's inability to resist the ministers, and Zhufu Yan's possible deathbed remorse are narrative or psychological interpretations.
- The claim that Zhufu Yan stole Dong Zhongshu's memorial and nearly caused his execution adds an episode-attributed conflict but does not establish the full memorial text, procedure, or chronology.
- The Kong Zang branch is compact. His genealogy, exact office sequence, rewards, and the institutional reach of Taichang require fuller textual comparison.
