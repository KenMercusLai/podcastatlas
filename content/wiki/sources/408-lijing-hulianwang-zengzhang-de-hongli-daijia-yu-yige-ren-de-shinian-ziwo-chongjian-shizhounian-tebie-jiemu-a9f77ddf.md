# #408 李晶：互联网增长的红利、代价与一个人的十年自我重建丨十周年特别节目

## Summary

This [[ShengdongJixi|声东击西]] tenth-anniversary interview follows [[LiJingEducator|李晶]] from traditional retail and consulting into [[Alibaba]], a brief period at [[Airbnb]], family-education entrepreneurship, and therapeutic education for children with additional needs. Her account holds the rewards and costs of high-growth work together: exponential business growth, promotion, pay, equity, and team development made extreme effort compelling, while work absorbed family attention, hardened her management style, and carried pressure into her relationship with her son. Listening practice and later therapeutic-education study helped her connect external achievement, parental approval, emotional reactivity, and caregiving, then rebuild work and family choices around boundaries, health, felt meaning, and reflective review.

## Key Claims

- [[WorkaholismAsSociallyRewardedAddiction|Socially rewarded overwork]] can remain difficult to recognize when rapid growth, wealth, promotion, team achievement, and voluntary excitement all reinforce long hours.
- High effort and coercive availability are not equivalent: Li Jing still endorses bounded periods of chosen effort, but rejects mandatory presence without operational need and work that breaches irreversible caregiving or health boundaries.
- A management logic based on pressure, standards, and results can become harmful when transferred into parenting, especially when a child is treated as a subordinate expected to comply and excel.
- [[ParentalRepairAfterHarm|Repair after parental harm]] may begin with repeated listening and emotional recognition, but becomes credible through reduced anger, less attack and control, greater safety, and changed everyday interaction.
- [[ReflectiveChoiceFormation|Reflective choice]] combines action, periodic review, bodily and emotional response, realistic constraints, and explicit non-negotiables rather than copying family, peer, or social definitions of success.
- Work with meaning still faces economic, scale, and delivery constraints; creating value for another person does not by itself guarantee a viable market or equal access to lower-paid vocational transitions.
- The episode distinguishes source-reported family change from medical proof: one family's improvement does not establish a general treatment for ADHD, disability, anxiety, depression, diabetes, or gastrointestinal illness.
