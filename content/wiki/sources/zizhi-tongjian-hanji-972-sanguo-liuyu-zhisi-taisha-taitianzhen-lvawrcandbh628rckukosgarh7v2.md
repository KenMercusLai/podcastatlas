# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》972丨三国刘虞之死：太傻太天真

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the missing prelude to [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-998-baima-jiangjun-gongsunzan-cong-sanqian-baima-dao-kunshou-gucheng-lp-1l2gvbsbfnyznutdghxwwvqna|Hanji 998]] by narrating how [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] is defeated and killed by [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] in [[YouzhouLateHan|幽州]]. It frames Liu Yu as loved and humane but militarily ineffective: his order not to burn and to kill only Gongsun Zan creates a [[BenevolentCommandExecutionGap|仁厚指挥执行缺口]] when paired with undisciplined troops and an unresolved siege objective.

The second half turns to [[TianChou|田筹]], who carries Liu Yu's memorial to [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], refuses office, reads the court reply at Liu Yu's tomb, rebukes Gongsun Zan, and later gathers refugees in [[XuWuMountain|徐无山]]. Tian Chou's mountain settlement, law code, rites, schooling, and Wuhuan diplomacy create a positive contrast: posthumous loyalty becomes local order rather than only revenge rhetoric.

## Key Claims
- In 193 CE, [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] fortifies a small city southeast of Ji and repeatedly avoids [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]]'s summons by claiming illness, making Liu Yu read him as openly rebellious.
- Liu Yu attacks first with a large force, but the episode says the army is a loose crowd rather than a trained instrument.
- Liu Yu's humane command not to use fire and to kill only Gongsun Zan preserves a moral boundary but makes the attack hard to execute as siege warfare.
- Gongsun Zan's counterattack with a few hundred brave men breaks Liu Yu's larger force, showing the source's claim that discipline, command clarity, and timing outweigh numbers.
- Liu Yu flees to [[JuyongPass|居庸关]], is captured after three days, and is brought back to Ji with his wife and children.
- [[DuanXunLateHan|段迅]] arrives with court edicts increasing Liu Yu's fief and assigning him six-province oversight while also granting Gongsun Zan formal rank; Gongsun Zan converts that mediation moment into accusation and execution.
- Gongsun Zan accuses Liu Yu of colluding with [[YuanShao|袁绍]] and seeking the imperial title, coerces Duan Xun, and kills Liu Yu and his family in the marketplace.
- The killing deepens the [[AtrocityBackfireMobilization|暴行反噬式动员]] and [[PeopleBasedPoliticalSecurity|民心型政治安全]] pattern that Hanji 998 later shows through the anti-Gongsun coalition.
- Sun Jin and Zhang Zan's deaths, and Wei Dun's recovery of Liu Yu's head, are kept as source-scoped signs that Liu Yu's moral authority outlives his military defeat.
- [[TianChou|田筹]] is introduced as a young [[YoubeipingCommandery|右北平]] talent chosen by Liu Yu for a dangerous covert mission to Chang'an.
- Tian Chou avoids official display, travels with twenty horsemen through frontier routes including [[JuyongPass|居庸关]], Yinshan, and Shuofang, and delivers Liu Yu's memorial to [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]].
- Tian Chou refuses the qiduwei appointment because the emperor is displaced and humiliated, making office acceptance feel morally wrong in that context.
- After Liu Yu's death, Tian Chou reads the court reply at the tomb and directly rebukes Gongsun Zan, who releases him despite anger.
- Tian Chou later gathers kin and followers from [[WuzhongCountyLateHan|无终县]] into [[XuWuMountain|徐无山]], personally farms, creates law, rites, and schooling, and attracts thousands of households.
- The settlement's relations with [[Wuhuan|乌桓]] and other frontier groups show authority built through order, reputation, and restraint rather than coercive occupation alone.
