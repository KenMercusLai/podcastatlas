# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》414｜西汉第一权臣霍光，为何纵容妻子害死皇后？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the [[XuPingjunWesternHan|许平君]] poisoning arc after [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-413-2-shi-shui-dusha-le-hanxuandi-liubingyi-de-laopo-lguo142uuouzmhqjvewjpjkgqxa|Hanji 413-2]]. When [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] arrests the physicians who attended the empress, [[HuoXianWesternHan|霍显]] admits the murder to [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]], and Huo Guang chooses to protect the household by memorializing that Xu Pingjun died of illness rather than medical wrongdoing.

The second half shifts from palace concealment to frontier strategy. A large Han expedition against [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] produces limited gains, while [[Wusun|乌孙]] cavalry coordinated through Chang Hui strikes the Xiongnu right-side royal court, takes major captives and livestock, and is followed by a failed Xiongnu revenge campaign that snow, Wuhuan pressure, Wusun pressure, and Western Regions defections turn into a wider Xiongnu weakening sequence.

## Key Claims

- Huo Guang's concealment is presented as a conscious choice after Huo Xian confesses, not as ignorance of Xu Pingjun's poisoning.
- Xuan suspects the case but lacks practical control because Huo Guang still holds real power, so the investigation into the physicians stops.
- The source sharpens the later Huo-family purge arc by making Huo Guang's household-protection decision part of the hidden crime, not only Huo Xian's private murder.
- The 71 BCE five-general Han expedition against Xiongnu is framed as costly but militarily underwhelming.
- Wusun and Chang Hui's western strike produces more visible results than the main Han armies, showing the value of allied frontier pressure.
- Xiongnu's failed revenge against Wusun becomes a cascading defeat through weather, enemy attacks, livestock loss, population loss, and Western Regions defections.
- Huo Guang's private instruction to Chang Hui about Qiuci shows that he still shapes frontier action even when Xuan publicly refuses authorization.

## Key Quotes

> "这正是题目所问'为何纵容'的核心" - the source's framing of Huo Guang's choice to cover for Huo Xian.

> "匈奴的衰弱不只来自军事失败" - the source's synthesis of battlefield, weather, livestock, and alliance effects.
