# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》822丨豺狼当道，他将矛头直指皇帝岳父？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 141-142 CE [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]] court before the existing [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-827-yi-ju-hua-yinfa-de-diwang-zhisi-lnf6rz1i2vmgsclgiejbh0qevzc2|Hanji 827]] Liang-family sequence. It follows [[LiGuLateHan|李固]] pacifying thieves in [[Jingzhou|荆州]] and [[Taishan|泰山]] through pardon, credibility, reduced force, and anti-corruption action, then turns to [[ZhangGangLateHan|张纲]] refusing a routine regional inspection and directly impeaching [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] and [[LiangBuyiLateHan|梁不疑]].

The durable contribution is a paired governance lesson: local disorder can sometimes be repaired by trust-backed amnesty and credible restraint, while court disorder may require naming the protected central actor rather than pursuing minor "foxes." [[EmperorShunOfHan|顺帝]] recognizes Zhang Gang's charges but cannot immediately punish the Liang household, so the episode also adds an earlier warning layer for [[OuterRelativeCourtPower|外戚专权与清算]] before Liang Ji's later domination becomes lethal.

## Key Claims

- In 141 CE, Li Gu as Jingzhou inspector sends officials to comfort and pardon thieves; the leader Xia Mi and more than six hundred heads surrender bound, and the region becomes peaceful within about half a year.
- Li Gu keeps his pardon promise and uses the surrendered leaders to circulate court grace and law, making pacification depend on credibility rather than indiscriminate punishment.
- Li Gu then impeaches corrupt Nanyang administrator Gao Ci and others; when they bribe Liang Ji for protection, Li Gu intensifies the investigation instead of yielding.
- Liang Ji has Li Gu moved to Taishan to protect the bribing officials, but Li Gu again pacifies long-running thieves by dismissing most troops, selecting a small capable force, and relying on reputation plus grace.
- In 142 CE, Emperor Shun sends eight inspectors to examine provincial and commandery officials after an amnesty and renewed Southern Xiongnu pressure.
- Zhang Gang buries his carriage wheel at Luoyang and says wolves block the road, so it is wrong to chase only foxes; he directly impeaches Liang Ji and Liang Buyi on fifteen major charges.
- Emperor Shun knows Zhang Gang's charge is right and has enough evidence, but Liang-family harem favor and court network power prevent immediate legal action.
- The eight inspectors' accusations are first blocked because many accused officials are Liang Ji or eunuch relatives and associates; Chong Hao, Wu Xiong, and Li Gu later help force renewed review.
- Liang Ji hates Zhang Gang after the impeachment and waits for a chance to harm him, making truthful central accountability politically costly even before open punishment occurs.

## Key Quotes

> "豺狼当道，安问狐狸" - Zhang Gang's rationale for targeting Liang Ji instead of lesser local officials.

> "从不牺牲诚实来换取一段关系" - the episode's opening value frame for principled speech.

> "一个家庭或者团队中很需要一个靠谱的人" - the host's Li Gu-centered governance and relationship lesson.
