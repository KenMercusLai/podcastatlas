# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》810丨他拼个满门被斩，为何也要杀妻？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 129-131 CE opening layer of [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]]'s reign before the already-ingested Hanji 811 sequence. Its main throughline is selective early repair: Shun accepts [[YuXuEasternHan|虞诩]]'s advice to restore frontier commanderies, revives [[YiwuGarrisonLateHan|伊吾屯田]], repairs [[Taixue|太学]], and sends [[ShenJingLateHan|沈景]] to discipline [[LiuZhengHejianKing|河间王刘政]], while the [[BanShiLateHan|班始]] and [[YinChengPrincess|阴城公主]] case exposes extreme household, status, and collateral-punishment risk.

## Key Claims

- Yu Xu persuades Shun to restore [[AndingCommandery|安定郡]], [[BeidiCommandery|北地郡]], and [[Shangjun|上郡]] because the region combines arable land, pasture, water transport, and defensive cover for Chang'an and imperial tombs.
- The Khotan-Jumi episode shows Han suzerainty in the Western Regions as limited: Shun offers pardon if Khotan returns Jumi, but Fang Qian refuses.
- Ban Shi's killing of Yincheng Princess and her lover is framed as a tragedy of princess-marriage status imbalance; Shun's response extends punishment to Ban Shi's siblings and public corpse display.
- Shen Jing's Hejian appointment shows [[AppointmentQualityAsStateCapacity|appointment quality]] through ritual protocol, removal of abusive local actors, and release of wrongly imprisoned people.
- Yiwu garrison farming and Taixue rebuilding are institutional repairs: one secures the Western Regions route, the other reverses educational decay from Emperor An's neglect.
- Han Hao's too-close Huangzhong tuntian relocation scares neighboring Qiang groups into alliance, while [[MaXuLateHan|马续]]'s reversal calms the frontier.
- The opening "continue chasing" tiger metaphor is a host-level action ethic and remains source-scoped rather than a durable historical claim.
