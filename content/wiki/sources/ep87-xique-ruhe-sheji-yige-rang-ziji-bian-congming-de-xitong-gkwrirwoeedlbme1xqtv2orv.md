# EP87《稀缺》：如何设计一个让自己变聪明的系统

## Summary
This [[ZongHengSiHai]] episode uses the book 《稀缺》 to explain why people short on time, money, food, companionship, or psychological safety often make choices that deepen the shortage. The host's core synthesis is [[ScarcityMindset]]: scarcity captures attention, creates [[TunnelingTax]], reduces [[MentalBandwidth]], and removes [[QueueingTheorySlack|slack]], so self-blame and willpower are weaker interventions than [[SystemDesignOverWillpower|system design]]. The episode turns that diagnosis into practical design: restore bandwidth through relaxation and review, preserve slack, avoid [[TimeAndPaydayLoanTrap|short borrowing loops]], plan recurring fire drills, and make good defaults happen automatically.

## Key Claims
- [[ScarcityMindset]] is defined as having less than one feels one needs; it has objective and subjective components, and it can appear around money, time, food, social connection, status, safety, or self-worth.
- Scarcity has a short-term dividend because it captures attention, makes abstract tasks concrete, and can improve deadline-like focus.
- The same attention capture creates [[TunnelingTax]]: people see immediate relief inside the tunnel while missing future costs, side effects, and safety checks.
- [[MentalBandwidth]] falls under scarcity because worry, working-memory load, and unresolved tasks consume fluid intelligence and executive control.
- Slack is not waste; [[QueueingTheorySlack|spare capacity]] and safety margin reduce hard choices, absorb mistakes, and prevent predictable surprises from becoming emergencies.
- [[TimeAndPaydayLoanTrap|Short borrowing loops]] in money and time solve today's shortage by creating tomorrow's larger shortage.
- [[SystemDesignOverWillpower]] is the episode's remedy: lower bandwidth demands, put important non-urgent work into the attention tunnel, use defaults, and design environments that make helpful behavior automatic.

## Key Quotes
> "拥有的比需要的少" - the episode's working definition of scarcity.

> "它没那么重要" - the host's self-reminder for stepping out of a tunnel.

> "让疏忽等于默许" - the default-design principle for making good choices automatic.
