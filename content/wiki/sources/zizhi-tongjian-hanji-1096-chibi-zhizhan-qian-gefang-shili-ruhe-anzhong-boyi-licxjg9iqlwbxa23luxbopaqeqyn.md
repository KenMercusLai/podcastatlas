# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1096丨赤壁之战前，各方势力如何暗中博弈？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode uses the final years of [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s [[Jingzhou|荆州]] regime to compare how capable people judge an unsafe patron. [[HeKui|何夔]] withdraws from Liu Biao's court to Wuling, [[LiuWangzhiLateHan|刘望之]] is killed after resigning but failing to leave the political field, [[HanJiLateHan|韩暨]] is forced out of hiding into office, and [[PeiQianLateHan|裴潜]] moves toward Changsha after judging Liu Biao unable to convert ambition into durable power. After Cao Cao takes Jingzhou, several of these people enter his administration.

The episode then moves to the pre-[[RedCliffsBattle|赤壁之战]] diplomatic opening. [[LuSu|鲁肃]] asks [[SunQuan|孙权]] to let him travel under cover of mourning, assess Liu Biao's divided succession, contact [[LiuBei|刘备]], and seek an anti-[[CaoCao|曹操]] alignment while preserving Jiangdong's interest in Jingzhou. Its sharpest claim is that Sun Quan also leaves a fallback order: if Liu Bei collapses, Lu Su should submit a memorial at Xu. The resulting alliance is therefore presented as active cooperation built alongside an exit option, not as settled trust.

## Key Claims
- He Kui judges Liu Biao's court too dangerous for prolonged proximity and relocates from Xiangyang to Wuling despite having been treated as an honored guest.
- Liu Wangzhi's death shows that resignation alone does not create safety when a former official remains within the ruler's reach and ignores advice to leave.
- Han Ji's failed attempts to avoid recruitment and Pei Qian's move toward Changsha show different constrained responses between hiding, reluctant service, and geographic withdrawal.
- Cao Cao's takeover of Jingzhou redirects local and refugee talent into his administration, making regime change a personnel re-sorting process as well as a territorial conquest.
- Lu Su treats Liu Biao's death, conflict between his sons, divided commanders, and Liu Bei's hostility toward Cao Cao as a narrow coalition opportunity.
- The mourning mission has multiple purposes: gather information, test possible partners, organize resistance, and improve Jiangdong's position toward Jingzhou.
- Sun Quan's alleged fallback order to submit at Xu if Liu Bei disintegrates makes the emerging Sun-Liu relationship an example of [[AllianceContingencyHedging|联盟不确定性下的退路预留]].

## Key Quotes
> “乱世之中，君主身边不可久留。” — the episode's summary of He Kui's proximity-risk judgment.

> “如果刘备溃散，就向许昌献表。” — Sun Quan's fallback instruction as rendered and interpreted by the episode.

- [[LiuBiao|刘表]], [[Jingzhou|荆州]], and [[StrategicPatronExitJudgment|主公前途与安全退出判断]] - political environment in which access to a patron can become exposure.
- [[CaoCao|曹操]] and [[SimaZhiCaoOfficialLateHan|司马芝]] - post-conquest absorption of Jingzhou-linked personnel into Cao-side administration.
- [[LuSu|鲁肃]], [[SunQuan|孙权]], [[LiuBei|刘备]], and [[RedCliffsBattle|赤壁之战]] - mourning diplomacy, partner testing, and contingency planning before coalition commitment.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is recorded. The source supplies an earlier exploratory and contingent stage before Hanji 1099's post-Changban alliance proposal and Hanji 1101-1102's Jiangdong surrender debate.
- Transcript names are normalized cautiously: “何恰” to [[HeKui|何夔]], “韩记” to [[HanJiLateHan|韩暨]], “裴浅” to [[PeiQianLateHan|裴潜]], and “韩肃/陆素” to [[LuSu|鲁肃]].
- The transcript's “前往冀州投靠刘表” is treated as an error for Jingzhou because Liu Biao governed Jingzhou and the same passage immediately places He Kui at Xiangyang and Wuling.
- The fallback instruction is preserved as an episode-presented claim. Its exact wording, whether “献表” necessarily means surrender, and the host's sweeping conclusion that alliances contain only calculation require verification against the underlying historical text.
