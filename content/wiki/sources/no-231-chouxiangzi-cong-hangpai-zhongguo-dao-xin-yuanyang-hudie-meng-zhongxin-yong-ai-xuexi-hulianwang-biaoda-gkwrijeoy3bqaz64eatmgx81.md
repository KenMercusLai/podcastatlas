# No.231 抽象仔：从《航拍中国》到《新鸳鸯蝴蝶梦》，重新用 AI 学习互联网表达

## Summary
This [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] episode has [[LiuFei|刘飞]] interviewing [[Chouxiangzai|抽象仔 / 抽象宅]] about moving from CCTV documentary work, advertising, and internet content management into AI-video creator work and a small commercial team. It extends [[AIDirectorCoreWorkflow]], [[AICreativeCollaboration]], and [[ExpertiseAmplifiedAIUse]] by showing that AI lowers execution cost while amplifying preexisting craft in story, shot language, client communication, cultural meme literacy, and audience judgment.

The episode's durable contribution is a creator-side account of viral AI video as more than tool access. [[Chouxiangzai|抽象仔 / 抽象宅]] argues that C-side hits depend on topic selection, cultural contrast, meme fluency, real emotion, and sincere expression, while B-side commercialization depends on translating that attention into brand, platform, and client solutions without reducing the team to exhausting service labor.

## Key Claims
- [[Chouxiangzai|抽象仔 / 抽象宅]] moved from CCTV documentary projects such as 《航拍中国》 and 《楚国八百年》 through startup, advertising, and internet content work before entering AI video during a 2024 gap period.
- AI video let him reuse documentary and commercial-film skills: shot construction, story rhythm, fast problem solving, production coordination, and client communication remained valuable after generation tools changed execution.
- His early public AI-video work shifted from self-serious or niche experiments toward internet-native expression after observing that cultural contrast, meme timing, and familiar popular-culture hooks traveled better on platforms such as [[Douyin]] and [[Bilibili]].
- The Kanye-related gospel remix series and [[XinYuanYangHuDieMeng|《新鸳鸯蝴蝶梦》]] examples show how AI-video virality can combine fan affection, cross-cultural parody, visual contrast, and creator sincerity rather than only model novelty.
- The team uses C-side account visibility as a brand and sample-work surface, then converts it into B-side services for brands and platforms; the source names clients or counterparties including [[Xiaohongshu|小红书]], [[ByteDance|字节]], and 即梦-related business resources.
- AI project workflow remains organized like small-scale film production: brainstorming, asset setup, character and scene confirmation, generation division of labor, editing, and client expectation management.
- Client communication is a technical-production skill because rigid requests may need to be reframed into achievable shots, angles, props, or staging when a model cannot stably satisfy the literal instruction.
- In AI short drama, [[Chouxiangzai|抽象仔 / 抽象宅]] treats story clarity, surprise, user psychology, and ROI-positive distribution as more important than visible technical polish.
- The episode warns against believing that an agent platform or reusable "viral creator skill" can guarantee a hit; AI can improve steps, but virality remains non-repeatable because it depends on insight, timing, audience emotion, and authentic expression.
- The closing claim is that AI is closer to a "magic brush" or amplifier than a replacement: without expression desire, traffic understanding, beauty, and judgment, the tool does not produce meaningful content by itself.

## Key Quotes
> "爆款本质上不可复制" - Chouxiangzai's warning against treating viral AI content as a reusable skill.

> "AI 是放大器，不是替代品" - the episode's concluding frame for AI creative work.

> "神笔" - Chouxiangzai's metaphor for a powerful tool that still needs a capable creator.
