# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》423｜霍光死后，霍家为何决心谋反？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode fills the bridge between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-420-cong-jiexiaqiu-dao-zhongxingzhizhu-hanxuandi-nixi-zhilu-jingli-le-shenme-lpxbuonbdepadujbrcgkla5bmqgp|Hanji 420]]'s early post-[[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]] consolidation and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-424-huoguang-qushi-hou-huojia-shi-ruhe-fumie-de-ltisydawuqdhqpffdjv_xkwts90p|Hanji 424]]'s open Huo-family collapse. It argues that the Huo household's rebellion decision is triggered when [[HuoXianWesternHan|霍显]] admits she poisoned Empress Xu: the family's fear shifts from loss of office to likely extermination.

The episode then follows the Huo family's meetings, Zhang She's proposed use of [[ShangguanTaihouWesternHan|上官太后]] to remove Xu Guanghan and [[WeiXiangWesternHan|魏相]], the first leak and [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]]'s leniency, the attempt to bind sons-in-law into shared risk, Li Jing's later confession, and [[ZhangChangWesternHan|张敞]]'s secret memorial on how to remove Huo power without making frightened near-court actors more desperate.

## Key Claims

- Huo-family anxiety begins as fear of political marginalization after Xuan and the chancellor roll back Huo-era arrangements and memorials against the family circulate.
- Huo Xian's admission that she poisoned Empress Xu changes the family's risk model: they no longer expect mere loss of office, but death if the crime reaches Xuan.
- Zhang She's plan relies on empress-dowager authority and palace procedure, showing a court-coup design rather than open military rebellion.
- Xuan's decision not to punish the family after an early leak reflects gratitude toward Huo Guang, but the Huo family reads leniency as temporary and unsafe.
- The second family meeting turns fear into organization by trying to bind Huo daughters and their husbands into a shared survival bloc.
- Li Jing's confession moves the conspiracy from private planning into a court-known case, after which Xuan removes Huo Yun and Huo Shan from office and punishes Huo in-laws.
- Zhang Chang's secret memorial reframes the case as a structural outer-relative problem: Huo Guang's merit must be honored, but concentrated family command should be converted into titles, retirement, and ritualized withdrawal before fear hardens into revolt.

## Key Quotes

> "大祸来时谁也跑不掉" - the episode's summary of the Huo effort to bind in-laws into the conspiracy.

> "让天子近臣恐慌自危，并不是万全之策" - the source's reading of Zhang Chang's warning about frightened near-court actors.
