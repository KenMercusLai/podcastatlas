# No.225 对话 Yuri 尤栗打造者汗青：AI 虚拟偶像，越像真人越容易塌房？

## Summary
This [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] episode has [[LiuFei|刘飞]] interviewing [[HanqingUrey|汗青]], founder of [[AITalk|AI Talk]] and creator of [[UreyVirtualIdol|Yuri / 尤栗 / 优瑞]], about moving from celebrity-style AI dialogue videos into an original AI-native virtual idol. It connects [[AINativeVirtualIdol]], [[AICharacterPersonalitySystem]], [[AICreativeCollaboration]], [[GenerativeAIMusic]], and [[AIVideoProductionWorkflow]] by showing that AI content value comes from personality, taste, workflow orchestration, and long-term narrative rather than from a single model or novelty window.

The durable contribution is a creator-side account of AI-native IP after the demo stage. [[HanqingUrey|汗青]] argues that AI can lower execution cost and open new formats, but the scarce asset is still human judgment: choosing the character, voice, look, song, story, copyright boundary, and degree of real-time interaction that will not collapse user trust.

## Key Claims
- [[HanqingUrey|汗青]] began paying attention to AIGC in 2022 through newsletters, Silicon Valley sources, Hacker News, and early [[Midjourney]] image generation.
- [[AITalk|AI Talk]] combined generated text, digital-human tooling, lip sync, and TTS into virtual conversations; an early Steve Jobs and Elon Musk video reportedly neared 10 million plays on WeChat Channels in one evening.
- The early celebrity-dialogue format is treated as a training ground rather than the final business: by early 2024 the team decided to use authorized modern figures or public-domain historical figures and to shift toward original IP.
- [[UreyVirtualIdol|Yuri / 尤栗 / 优瑞]] launched on 2025-06-03 in the source account, with [[OmniHuman]] in March 2025 described as the model window that made expressive AI singing performance more plausible.
- Yuri's first song/MV workflow used many domestic and foreign models rather than one end-to-end system, with [[Suno]] named for music generation and video-model progress from [[Sora]], [[KlingAI|可灵]], and [[Seedance]] changing visual production.
- The project treats character personality, content tone, visual assets, voice, music, clothing, props, and narrative continuity as compounding IP assets.
- [[HanqingUrey|汗青]] says a virtual idol is not immune to "collapse": once real-time 2C dialogue opens, an AI character can say inappropriate things, so Yuri's consumer chat remains constrained.
- The source argues that AI-native characters should not only imitate humans; Yuri should acknowledge being AI and express a perspective different from human role-play.
- Both speakers frame AI anxiety as partly over-amplified by media and management KPI narratives; useful AI work still depends on direction, judgment, editing, testing, and final organization.
- The business remains source-scoped: cash flow is described as healthy under modest ambition, but scale depends on finding clearer product, platform, hardware, or distribution leverage beyond mobile short-video formats.

## Key Quotes

> "跟时间做朋友" - his argument for personality-based IP as a longer-term asset.
