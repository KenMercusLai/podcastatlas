# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》434｜一代能吏胆敢指责皇帝！揭秘汉朝奇人张敞

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Shenjue 1 / 61 BCE under [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]], before the later [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-454-yi-zhi-niao-ganfan-yige-chengxiang-huangba-you-duo-can-lrjgft-s8oi3yt3lrnsh0_m20sxj|Hanji 454]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-458-xihan-chongqi-daren-zhangchang-fan-sizui-weihe-neng-huo-lgla09-xws3c2sctxcojzmwbfvkb|Hanji 458]] Zhang Chang sequence. It opens with [[ZhangChangWesternHan|张敞]]'s eyebrow-painting story as a private loyalty and self-possession anecdote, then turns to Zhang's public role as a capable Jingzhao Yin who repeatedly remonstrates against Emperor Xuan's fangshi and luxury leanings.

The second half centers on [[WangJiWesternHan|王吉]]'s memorial. Wang Ji argues that amnesty and tax relief are benevolent but insufficient if the court does not repair governing roots: ritual order, frugality, selection of worthy officials, restraint of close kin in high office, and abolition of hereditary office privilege. The durable synthesis is that Xuan's reign includes both practical capacity and a feedback limit: Zhang's anti-fangshi criticism is partly accepted, while Wang Ji's larger root-cause program is dismissed as laughable.

## Key Claims

- Zhang Chang's "画眉" anecdote is presented as a private act rooted in an old injury story and as evidence that his official identity does not erase household tenderness.
- Zhang is described as a difficult-capital official: as Jingzhao Yin, he handles powerful families, fast decisions, and complex elite networks while remaining willing to speak directly.
- Zhang repeatedly urges Emperor Xuan to reject fangshi, immortal-medicine practices, and excessive display; the episode says Xuan is displeased but eventually sends the fangshi away.
- The episode identifies Xuan's indulgence toward Xu, Shi, and Wang relatives as an early outer-relative risk, even while preserving his status as an able revival emperor.
- Wang Ji's Shenjue 1 memorial treats amnesty and tax relief as favors rather than the root of governance.
- Wang Ji's proposed repairs include ritual order, careful selection of close executors, social frugality, abolition of hereditary official appointment privilege, and blocking intimates from important offices.
- Xuan does not respond to Wang Ji's memorial and regards it as laughable; Wang Ji then resigns on grounds of illness.

## Key Quotes

> "家门之内，夫妇之私，有过于画眉者" - Zhang Chang's reply when asked about the eyebrow-painting report.

> "大赦免租税之属，皆民之所喜也，然非所以为治也" - Wang Ji's distinction between popular favors and durable governance.
