# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》974丨曹操向他托妻献子，为何转身挨刀子？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the causal prelude to [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] collapse before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-975-yanzhou-zhiluan-xunyu-ruhe-bang-caocao-shou-dabenying-ln0hh4jrokkwtqsmxmqhdjzcosdr|Hanji 975]]. It asks why [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]], a friend trusted with Cao Cao's family, joined [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] in inviting [[LyuBu|吕布]] into Yanzhou. The episode explains the betrayal through layered pressure: [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s hostility, Zhang Miao's fear that Cao Cao might obey Yuan Shao, Lü Bu's alliance opportunity, and [[BianRangLateHan|边让]]'s killing as a signal that local elites were unsafe under Cao Cao.

## Key Claims
- [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] and [[CaoCao|曹操]] had a unusually deep prior bond: Cao Cao refused Yuan Shao's demand to kill Zhang Miao and, before attacking [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] in [[Xuzhou|徐州]], reportedly told his household to rely on Zhang Miao if he did not return.
- [[GaoRouLateHan|高柔]] predicts that the Cao-Zhang relationship cannot stay stable because Cao Cao's ambition exceeds one province while Zhang Miao has his own [[Chenliu|陈留]] base and room to calculate independently.
- [[LyuBu|吕布]]'s hand-clasping oath with Zhang Miao after leaving Yuan Shao makes Zhang Miao useful to Lü Bu and more suspect to Yuan Shao, deepening Zhang Miao's fear that Cao Cao could eventually act under Yuan Shao's pressure.
- The episode makes [[BianRangLateHan|边让]] the deeper trigger: his public humiliation of Cao Cao and attack on Cao Cao's legitimacy is followed by a harsh family purge, which the source reads as a shock to Yanzhou's local gentry.
- The host connects Bian Rang's opposition to gentry interests, land control, and source-scoped resistance to early [[TuntianSystem|屯田制]] or land-clearance pressure, while keeping that policy chronology bounded.
- [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]], [[XuSiLateHan|许汜]], [[WangKaiLateHan|王楷]], and [[ZhangChaoLateHan|张超]] secretly discuss betraying Cao Cao and persuade Zhang Miao that Cao Cao's absence in Xuzhou gives them a chance to invite Lü Bu and share control of Yanzhou.
- Zhang Miao's decision is framed as both fear and ambition: he is afraid of being purged like Bian Rang, but also sees a chance to act before Cao Cao returns.
- The betrayal becomes a turning point in Cao Cao's organization: after losing most of Yanzhou, he has to abandon the Xuzhou campaign and later diversify talent, strengthen clan military control, and give advisers such as [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] and [[XunYou|荀攸]] institutional decision roles.
