# Kicking Kyiv: Trump’s doubtful deals

## Summary
This [[EconomistPodcasts]] episode links three cases in which formal arrangements produce value only under specific material and social conditions. Its Ukraine segment argues that a proposed energy ceasefire would be an [[AsymmetricEnergyCeasefire]] favoring [[Russia]], while reported but unconfirmed [[Starlink]] restrictions expose [[PrivateInfrastructureWarPower]]. Its India segment updates the [[CockroachMovement]] from education protest to election-administration conflict and pre-emptive detention, and its final segment treats [[ParticipatorySinging]] as socially useful only when technical and group conditions do not turn ordinary voices into performance failures.

## Key Claims
- The proposed exchange would have [[Ukraine]] stop striking many economically important Russian refineries while Russia stopped striking Ukraine's single, already-damaged refinery, creating an asymmetry in assets protected and pressure surrendered.
- The episode questions whether promised Russian diesel exports could materially lower global prices and argues that sanctions relief, revenue, and propaganda value would accrue more clearly to [[VladimirPutin]].
- Ukrainian officials believed [[SpaceX]] had imposed a speed limit on Starlink terminals that could impair fast-moving drones; neither company had confirmed that the effect was deliberate.
- [[VolodymyrZelensky]] was presented as avoiding a direct rejection of [[DonaldTrump]] while pausing refinery strikes, publicizing continued Russian attacks, and selecting other targets.
- The [[CockroachMovement]] had moved from satirical online identity and education grievances toward opposition to alleged voter-list manipulation and executive influence over election administration.
- Police sealed [[Delhi]], restricted internet access, detained [[AbhijitTipke]] and other organizers before the protest, and dispersed or detained demonstrators; the episode argues that coercion may be deepening rather than ending youth opposition.
- Singing can hold infants' attention, support some parents with severe postnatal depression, carry historical identity, and increase social closeness, but technique, bodily change, embarrassment, professional comparison, and punitive choir culture can suppress participation.
