# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》705-5丨班昭：续编《汉书》，史笔千秋第一人

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the biographical prelude to [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-705-6-banzhao-sanci-yimingjingren-guimi-ganzheng-no-1-lpnprzwqh6c7jqcy7cxpgganibmm|Hanji 705-6]], presenting [[BanZhaoLateHan|班昭 / Ban Zhao]] through family learning, marriage and widowhood, [[BanGuEasternHan|Ban Gu]]'s prison death, and [[EmperorHeOfHan|Emperor He]]'s order that she continue the unfinished `Han Shu`. It makes Ban Zhao's court authority concrete through work at the Dongguan archive, completion of the eight tables, instruction of scholars such as [[MaRongLateHan|Ma Rong]], and her later role as teacher to palace women.

The durable synthesis is that Ban Zhao's unusual public role was not an isolated "talented woman" story. It depended on Ban-family scholarship, a state need to rescue an unfinished dynastic history, imperial patronage under Emperor He, and a court setting where historical expertise could cross from archival compilation into [[FemaleCourtEducationEasternHan|female court education]] and later advisory proximity to [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Deng Sui]].

## Key Claims

- Ban Zhao is framed as a multi-role Eastern Han woman: writer, scholar, political participant, royal teacher, and participant in dynastic history writing.
- The episode attributes Ban Zhao's early learning to Ban-family education under [[BanBiaoEasternHan|Ban Biao]], while stressing that ordinary women in the period lacked comparable educational access.
- Ban Gu's death during the [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]] fallout leaves `Han Shu` unfinished, creating the immediate court problem that brings Ban Zhao into state-sponsored historiography.
- Emperor He orders Ban Zhao to Luoyang to continue `Han Shu`, giving her access to the Dongguan archive and making him the first major patron in the episode's life-story frame.
- Ban Zhao is said to organize and supplement inherited Ban-family drafts and to complete the eight tables, while the source keeps the remaining Tianwen material as part of the unfinished-project background.
- Emperor He's court sends Ma Rong and other scholars to hear Ban Zhao explain the new `Han Shu`, making her teaching authority cross ordinary gendered scholarly boundaries.
- The episode says Emperor He orders empresses and consorts to study with Ban Zhao and calls her China's first royal female teacher in this source-scoped framing.
- Deng Sui is introduced as Ban Zhao's second major patron and a palace woman whose teacher-friend relationship with Ban Zhao becomes important in the sequel.

## Key Quotes

> "亦师亦友" - the source's description of Ban Zhao's relationship with Deng Sui.
