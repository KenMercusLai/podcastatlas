# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》648丨为什么我劝你必须积德？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Wang Mang's Tianfeng 1 / 14 CE crisis before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-650-gudai-laozuzong-ye-hui-bei-kou-jixiao-gongzi-lr77fw1gojgqrhah1ij6u25vtxaz|Hanji 650]] and the later [[XinDynasty|新朝]] collapse notes. It frames [[WangMang|王莽]]'s killing of a Xiongnu chanyu's son as a moral and political act whose consequences fall on border soldiers and commoners, then follows famine, frontier retrenchment, renewed [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] raids, Yizhou revolt, and another currency reset.

The durable synthesis is that late-Xin policy stress is visible earlier than the already-ingested 15-22 CE sequence. Wang Mang can still withdraw border deployments when supplies fail, but he also rewards Han Wei's grotesque anti-Xiongnu bravado, faces raids driven by grievance and interest, suppresses Yizhou unrest by requisitioning nearby commanderies, and keeps changing money in ways that the host reads as ordinary-people bankruptcy risk.

## Key Claims

- The episode uses "accumulating virtue" as a moral frame for political causality: a ruler's harmful act can rebound onto uninvolved border populations.
- In 14 CE, frontier famine and supply exhaustion make long-term border stationing unsustainable, leading Ru Pu to recommend withdrawing forces while Xiongnu relations are temporarily calm.
- Han Wei's boast that five thousand elite troops could ravage Xiongnu territory without grain shows the court's appetite for violent frontier fantasy, even though actual policy still follows retrenchment.
- Xiongnu outwardly preserves a peace or heqin-like posture for gifts, but revenge over Luanti Deng's execution and raiding incentives keep frontier attacks active.
- Yizhou revolt is attributed to Xin harsh governance; the response under Feng Mao pulls troops, grain, and pay from Ba, Shu, Qianwei, and neighboring populations.
- Wang Mang's currency reset restores gold, silver, turtle-shell, and cowry monies, cancels earlier large and small coins, introduces huobu and huoquan, and allows a six-year transition for the old large coin.
- The host treats repeated currency reform as direct commoner harm: every reset can destroy household wealth, push survival crime, and make "兴，百姓苦；亡，百姓苦" the episode's closing policy judgment.

## Key Quotes

> "兴，百姓苦；亡，百姓苦" - the host's summary of how dynastic disorder and policy churn land on commoners.

> "不用携带粮食" - Han Wei's violent boast about fighting Xiongnu without supply.

> "每改一次币制，百姓就跟着破产一次" - the host's judgment on repeated Wang Mang currency resets.
