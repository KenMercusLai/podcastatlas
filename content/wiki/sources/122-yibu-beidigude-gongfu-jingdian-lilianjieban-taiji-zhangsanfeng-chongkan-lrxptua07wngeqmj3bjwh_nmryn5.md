# 122 一部被低估的功夫经典｜李连杰版《太极张三丰》重看

## Summary

This [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] episode revisits [[TaiChiMaster1993|《太极张三丰》]] through childhood VHS, VCD, and television memories, then follows [[ZhangJunbaoTaiChiMaster|张君宝]] and [[DongTianbao|董天宝]] from Shaolin brotherhood to fatal opposition. The hosts praise [[YuenWooPing|袁和平]]'s action design, the music, and [[ChinSiuHo|钱小豪]]'s morally layered performance while arguing that the commercial film accelerates Tianbao's late villainy and sometimes substitutes rapid action or explicit exposition for fuller dramatic development.

## Key Claims

- [[AdaptationReceptionMemory|Reception memory]] is shaped by medium as well as age: VHS, VCD, television rebroadcasts, incomplete copies, music, and childhood imitation produced different remembered versions of the same film.
- [[TraditionalWuxiaActionCraft|Traditional wuxia action craft]] joins readable exchanges to character and environment. The Luohan-staff formation, the brothers' damaged-blade fight, water-vat insight, and rope-net finale each make tactics, space, objects, or changing footing part of the drama.
- [[ZhangJunbaoTaiChiMaster|张君宝]] and [[DongTianbao|董天宝]] begin with different ideas of strength—health and aid versus protection from humiliation—and the same political environment turns that difference into opposing moral choices.
- [[DongTianbao|董天宝]] is not excused by poverty, unfair treatment, ambition, or genuine affection for Junbao. Those pressures make his ascent intelligible, but betrayal, killing Xiaodonggua, and serving oppression remain chosen moral thresholds.
- [[ChinSiuHo|钱小豪]] gives Tianbao enough hesitation, attachment, and force that the antagonist can feel like a co-lead, while the film's late acceleration into cruelty reduces some of the nuance established earlier.
- Junbao's creation of tai chi connects martial and ethical growth: observing water and yielding force supplies the technique, while choosing to defend the oppressed supplies its purpose.
- The hosts' scores cluster around 8–9/10, but adult rewatching also reveals compressed motivation, shouted technique names, repeated shocks, and action density characteristic of a commercial genre film.
