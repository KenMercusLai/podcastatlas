# No.233 单口｜手机为什么要折叠？Apple 为什么要「跟风」？

## Summary
This [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] solo episode has [[LiuFei|刘飞]] reassess foldable phones after [[Apple]]'s [[IPhoneDuo|iPhone Duo]] launch. The core argument is that foldables are no longer ignorable, but they should be judged by whether they improve a user's existing device mix and high-frequency scenes rather than by screen size, novelty, or "future of phones" slogans.

## Key Claims
- [[FoldablePhoneProductivity]] depends on old-use-case improvement: a larger inner screen is valuable only when it replaces or improves scenes that a user's current phone, tablet, laptop, or second phone handles poorly.
- Foldable phones remain high-friction devices even when creases, cameras, battery life, and performance improve, because users still pay weight, thickness, durability anxiety, inner-screen opening cost, and protective-case costs.
- [[IPhoneDuo|iPhone Duo]] is framed as a commercially rational Apple move rather than proof of total innovation decline: Apple is entering a validated premium niche with supply-chain, ecosystem, software, and interaction advantages.
- Apple's strongest foldable advantage may be [[FoldableAppAdaptation]] and iPad-derived large-screen ecosystem experience, especially if the unfolded device feels like a mature small iPad rather than a blown-up phone.
- The China-market experience remains uncertain because national apps such as WeChat and Alipay, plus eSIM constraints, can decide whether the iPad ecosystem advantage translates into everyday Chinese use.
- Foldables are unlikely to replace slab phones even if price and technology improve; phone form factors may instead fragment into slab, small foldable, large foldable, tri-fold, wide slab, tablet-phone, and AI-glasses-adjacent devices.
- Longer-term AI and [[AIGlassesComputingPlatform|AI glasses]] may change the screen-size problem by shifting some work from GUI-heavy large screens toward voice, language, or first-person interfaces.
