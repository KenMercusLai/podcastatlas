# 《资治通鉴·周纪》52丨 陈庄叛秦 端起碗吃饭放下碗骂娘

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode resumes [[YanState|燕国]] after the [[QiState|齐国]] occupation, showing [[YanZhaowang|燕昭王 / 姬平]] rebuilding through mourning, shared hardship, and visible respect for [[GuoWeiWarringStates|郭隗]]. It then follows the talent influx of [[LeYi|乐毅]] and [[JuXin|剧辛]] before moving to [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s fourth year, where [[ChenZhuangWarringStates|陈庄]] kills [[ShuHouNingTong|蜀侯宁通]] and rebels against [[QinState|秦国]]. The episode's governing synthesis is that people decide state trajectories in two directions: trusted talent can rebuild Yan, while an empowered local appointee in resource-rich [[BaShu|巴蜀]] can turn delegated authority into secession risk.

## Key Claims
- After Qi's attack on Yan, Yan prince Ji Ping returns and becomes [[YanZhaowang|燕昭王]], comforting families of the dead and sharing hardship with survivors before seeking revenge.
- Yan Zhaowang does not attack Qi immediately; he first asks [[GuoWeiWarringStates|郭隗]] how to recruit worthy people and rebuild the state.
- Guo Wei uses the "千金买骨" story to argue that Yan must make a visible signal: honor the first adviser sincerely, and more capable people will believe the recruitment promise.
- Yan Zhaowang builds for Guo Wei and treats him as a teacher, turning humility toward one adviser into [[TalentAttractionThroughRespect|礼贤下士式人才吸附]].
- [[LeYi|乐毅]] and [[JuXin|剧辛]] come from [[ZhaoState|赵国]] to Yan; Le Yi is connected to [[LeYang|乐羊]]'s earlier Wei service and later becomes the key general in Yan's revenge against Qi.
- The episode contrasts three Yan personnel judgments: [[YanWangKuai|燕王哙]] mistakes [[ZiZhi|子之]] for talent and ruins Yan, Yan Zhaowang uses Le Yi and Ju Xin to strengthen Yan, and later Yan Hui Wang weakens the state by replacing Le Yi.
- The compact Han notice says [[HanXuanhuiwang|韩宣惠王 / 韩惠王]] dies and [[HanXiangwang|韩仓 / 韩襄王]] succeeds.
- In [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s fourth year, 311 BCE in the episode's frame, [[ChenZhuangWarringStates|陈庄]] kills [[ShuHouNingTong|蜀侯宁通]] and becomes a rebel against Qin.
- The episode traces the setup to Qin's earlier conquest of [[BaStateWarringStates|巴国]] and [[ShuStateWarringStates|蜀国]] after [[SimaCuo|司马错]] persuades [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] to attack Shu.
- Qin leaves Shu as a reduced marquisate and appoints Chen Zhuang as chancellor, giving him a platform inside a difficult, resource-rich region.
- The host attributes Chen Zhuang's rebellion to [[AppointedGovernorSecessionRisk|appointed-governor secession risk]]: hard terrain, the grain value of Ba-Shu, and the jump from chancellor to independent ruler make rebellion seem attractive.
- The source says Chen Zhuang misjudges Qin's strength; in the following year Qin sends [[GanMao|甘茂]] into Shu, kills Chen Zhuang, and ends the rebellion.
- The closing returns to [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]]'s old hatred of [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]], setting up Zhang Yi's dangerous entry into Chu after the earlier Shangyu deception and Chu defeats.

## Key Quotes
> "千金买骨" - Guo Wei's recruitment-signal story.

> "蜀道难，难于上青天" - the episode's shorthand for the terrain that made Chen Zhuang misread his odds.
