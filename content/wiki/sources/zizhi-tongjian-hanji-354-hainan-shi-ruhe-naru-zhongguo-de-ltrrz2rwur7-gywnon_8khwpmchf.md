# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》354｜海南是如何纳入中国的？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the Nanyue campaign deployed in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-353-1-yangpu-ruhe-dangshang-zhongguo-shouwei-haijun-siling-lmmirgukthvsh4zrhvbgtxhvz7|Hanji 353-1]]. [[YangPuWesternHan|杨仆]] attacks Panyu from the southeast while [[LuBodeWesternHan|路博德]] combines a northwestern position with lenient reception and recursive recruitment of surrenderers. Their complementary pressure collapses resistance; Nanyue king Zhao Jiande and [[LyuJiaNanyue|吕嘉]] flee by sea and are captured, after which local rulers and officials submit and [[Nanyue|南越]] ends.

The source then joins conquest to administration. It says [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] establishes nine commanderies in former Nanyue territory and that Yang Pu crosses from Xuwen the following year to occupy [[Hainan|海南]], where the Han creates [[ZhuyaCommandery|珠崖郡]] and [[DanerCommandery|儋耳郡]]. This supplies the founding layer missing from later Hanji 471-472, which describe the high cost, unrest, and eventual abolition of direct island rule. The episode's “first large-scale naval war” and “Hainan incorporated into China” formulations remain attributed shorthand rather than exhaustive claims about earlier water warfare, local society, or continuous effective control.

## Key Claims

- Yang Pu's tower-ship force attacks Panyu from the southeast while Lu Bode positions his troops to the northwest.
- Yang Pu applies direct assault and fire, while Lu Bode receives captives leniently, issues credentials, and uses surrenderers to induce further defections.
- Nanyue king Zhao Jiande and Lü Jia flee by sea after the defending force collapses; Han officers capture both men.
- Regional rulers and officials then submit, ending the Nanyue polity before three supporting routes arrive.
- Han Wudi creates nine commanderies in the former Nanyue field, including Zhuya and Dan'er.
- The episode says Yang Pu crosses from Xuwen to occupy Hainan the next year, after which Zhuya and Dan'er place the island within the Han commandery system.
- Rewards go to Lu Bode, Yang Pu, captors, and selected Nanyue surrenderers, linking battlefield success and cooperation to differentiated postwar status.

## Key Quotes

> “一个唱红脸，一个唱黑脸。” — the episode's shorthand for combining Yang Pu's assault with Lu Bode's lenient surrender policy.

> “汉朝在海南岛设置儋耳、珠崖两个郡。” — the administrative endpoint used to explain Hainan's incorporation.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. The episode completes Hanji 353-1's deployment and supplies the conquest-and-commandery origin that Hanji 471-472 presuppose.
- Name normalization: the supplied document's “杨溥” and “路波德” are routed to the established [[YangPuWesternHan|杨仆]] and [[LuBodeWesternHan|路博德]] identities.
- The claim that this was China's first large-scale naval war and the description of Yang Pu's force as a regular navy remain source-attributed because the episode does not compare all earlier waterborne warfare or establish modern institutional equivalence.
- The episode compresses the date and sequence of the nine-commandery settlement and Yang Pu's Hainan crossing. Exact chronology, commandery boundaries, Jiaozhou subordination, force totals, battle details, names of minor captors and surrenderers, and reward titles require primary-source comparison.
- “Incorporation” means the episode's reported military occupation and commandery establishment, not proof of uncontested or continuously effective control; Hanji 471-472 later document repeated unrest and abolition.
