# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》103-1｜司马迁看中的信陵君算是小人吗？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode moves the [[QinZhuangxiangwang|秦庄襄王]] chronicle into 247 BCE, with Qin expanding through [[Shangdang|上党]], setting up [[TaiyuanWarringStates|太原]], and sending [[MengAo|蒙敖]] against [[WeiState|魏国]]. That pressure forces [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]] to recall [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] from [[ZhaoState|赵国]], where [[MaoGong|毛公]] and [[XueGong|薛公]] persuade him that his reputation depends on the survival of Wei.

The episode then tests Xinlingjun's heroic reputation through [[GuanchengWarringStates|管城]]. After defeating Meng Ao with a five-state coalition, Xinlingjun tries to remove the Qin threat to [[Daliang|大梁]], but [[SuGao|苏高]] refuses to attack a city defended by his son and [[AnlingJun|安陵君]] refuses to hand Su Gao over. Su Gao's suicide, Xinlingjun's apology, and [[HuSanxing|胡三省]]'s later criticism make [[StateSurvivalKinshipDutyConflict|国存亡与父子君臣义冲突]] the episode's central problem rather than a simple verdict that Xinlingjun was a "小人."

## Key Claims
- The source places the opening in Qin Zhuangxiang Wang's third year, 247 BCE, when young [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]] becomes Qin king and Qin continues attacking unsubmitted Shangdang cities.
- Qin's administrative expansion now includes the first establishment of [[TaiyuanWarringStates|太原]] commandery and a reported total of fourteen Qin commanderies.
- [[HanState|韩国]] is presented as badly eaten away by Qin, with only one major commandery left in the source's summary.
- [[MengAo|蒙敖]] attacks Wei, takes Gaodu and Ji city, and creates the emergency that makes [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]] send for [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] after ten years in Zhao.
- Xinlingjun initially refuses even to hear Wei's summons, but [[MaoGong|毛公]] and [[XueGong|薛公]] argue that his honor and standing among the lords depend on Wei's ancestral state surviving.
- Once recalled, Xinlingjun is made supreme commander, draws help from the other lords, and defeats Meng Ao south of the Yellow River.
- The episode argues that Xinlingjun stands above the other [[WarringStatesFourLords|战国四公子]] not merely because he养士, but because he can identify real worthies and turn their judgment into action.
- [[GuanchengWarringStates|管城]] matters strategically because Qin-controlled [[Xingze|荥泽]] and [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]] create a water-control threat against [[Daliang|大梁]], likened to [[ZhiYao|智瑶]]'s earlier flooding of Jinyang.
- [[SuGao|苏高]] refuses Xinlingjun's appointment offer because father attacking and son defending the same city would violate both family duty and ruler-subject duty.
- [[AnlingJun|安陵君]] refuses to bind and surrender Su Gao because he treats his inherited guard duty and the old Wei constitutional order as stronger than Xinlingjun's field command.
- Xinlingjun's threat to send 100,000 troops against Anling makes Su Gao decide that his continued refusal will endanger his lord, so Su Gao kills himself at the Wei envoy's lodging.
- Xinlingjun's plain-clothing apology admits that his coercive speech caused Su Gao's death, but the source does not make that confession a settled moral condemnation of his entire career.
- [[HuSanxing|胡三省]] criticizes all sides: Su Gao's son should not defend a Qin-held city against Wei, Su Gao and Anling Jun should not ignore Wei's survival need, and Xinlingjun should not apologize for doing a commander's job.
- The ending previews Qin's next anti-Xinlingjun move: unable to beat him cleanly on the battlefield, Qin will try to damage his reputation with money and slander.

## Key Quotes
> "我魏无忌真是个小人" - Xinlingjun's apology after Su Gao's suicide.

> "在人上时要把别人当人，在人下时要把自己当人" - the episode's opening ethical frame.

> "战场上打不过信陵君，就会花钱搞臭他" - closing preview of Qin's next strategy.
