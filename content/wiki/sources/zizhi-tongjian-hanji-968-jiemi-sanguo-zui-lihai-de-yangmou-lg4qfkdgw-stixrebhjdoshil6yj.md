# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》968丨揭秘三国最厉害的阳谋

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the pre-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-969-yuanshi-xiongdi-neidou-shui-neng-jianlou-lsgj6sewo35jpsgfm-koivijtr6r|Hanji 969]] foundation of [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s early late-Han rise. It connects the surrender of Qingzhou Yellow Turban forces, the formation of [[QingzhouSoldiers|青州兵]], [[MaoJie|毛玠]]'s paired strategy of imperial legitimacy and agricultural logistics, and [[DongZhaoLateHan|董昭]] plus [[ZhongYao|钟繇]] opening a risky communication route to the [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] court.

The episode's core contribution is to make Cao Cao's "open strategy" a system rather than a slogan. [[DefeatedPopulationConversion|降众资源化]] turns a dangerous surrendered population into soldiers and grain producers; [[TuntianSystem|屯田制]] supplies the material base; and [[LegitimacyWindowSeizure|合法性窗口抢占]] starts before the emperor is physically welcomed, through envoys, recommendations, and court-side persuasion under [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]].

## Key Claims
- Around 192 CE, Cao Cao receives a large Yellow Turban surrender associated with Qingzhou, gaining soldiers and dependent population rather than only removing an enemy threat.
- Cao Cao integrates the strongest surrendering fighters as [[QingzhouSoldiers|青州兵]] while turning other people toward farming and supply, making the episode a concrete case of [[DefeatedPopulationConversion|降众资源化]].
- The source presents Mao Jie, a man from [[Chenliu|陈留]], as the adviser who gives Cao Cao a two-part design: politically serve the emperor to command noncompliant actors, and economically deepen farming, stockpile military supplies, and stabilize the base.
- In this source, the "奉天子" frame is not yet the later completed Xu-court order; it is an early strategic principle that still requires access to the captured Chang'an court.
- The host says legitimacy lets Cao Cao claim moral height, recruit local elites, and attract advisers such as [[XunYu|荀彧]] and [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] through a more credible platform.
- Cao Cao sends people through [[HeneiCommandery|河内郡]] to contact the Chang'an court, but [[ZhangYangLateHan|张杨]] initially rejects the passage.
- Dong Zhao persuades Zhang Yang that the [[YuanShao|袁绍]]-Cao Cao alliance will not last and that Cao Cao is the more promising future actor, so Zhang Yang should allow the envoy through and recommend Cao Cao.
- The episode frames Dong Zhao as politically mobile: after trouble inside Yuan Shao's camp, he leaves through a court-facing assignment and begins positioning himself around Cao Cao's future.
- Dong Zhao also sends Cao Cao-branded greetings to [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]], [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]], and other Chang'an powerholders according to their relative weight, lowering suspicion before the formal appointment branch in Hanji 969.
- Li Jue and Guo Si still suspect Cao Cao's envoy, making the court-contact route fragile even after Henei access opens.
- Zhong Yao argues that Cao Cao had earlier fought after Dong Zhuo burned Luoyang and that accepting Cao Cao would help keep eastern powers oriented toward the Han court rather than away from it.
- The source reads Zhong Yao's intervention as both prudent self-preservation and early preparation for Cao Cao's later use of imperial legitimacy.
