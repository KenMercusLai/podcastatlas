# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》108-1｜历史敢当面顶撞秦始皇第一人

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues directly from [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-107-zuihou-yici-hezong-faqin-fa-le-ge-jimo-lrzq1qks7z-oromd-5f-dgqjwjhw|Qinji 107]] by moving from the failed final [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] campaign into 238 BCE, Qin Shi Huang's ninth year. It keeps [[QinState|秦国]]'s eastward military pressure active through [[YangDuanhe|杨端和]]'s attack on [[WeiState|魏国]], then turns inward to the crisis around [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]]'s adulthood, [[ZhaoJi|赵姬]], [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]], and [[LaoAi|嫪毐]].

The episode's central scene is [[MaoJiao|茅焦]]'s direct remonstrance after Ying Zheng has suppressed Lao Ai, killed or punished the rebel network, confined Zhao Ji at [[YongchengQin|雍城]], and threatened death for anyone speaking on her behalf. Mao Jiao uses a death-defying challenge to reopen court feedback, warning that violence against mother, brothers, and remonstrants will dissolve support for Qin; Ying Zheng accepts the criticism, honors Mao Jiao, and personally brings Zhao Ji back to [[Xianyang|咸阳]].

## Key Claims
- Qin Shi Huang's ninth year, 238 BCE, is framed as the year Ying Zheng reaches twenty-two and prepares to rule personally after the crown ceremony at [[YongchengQin|雍城]].
- [[YangDuanhe|杨端和]] succeeds [[MengAo|蒙骜]] in the Wei campaign line, and Qin's eastward advance is presented as reaching the [[QiState|齐国]] border.
- The host interprets Qin's border connection with Qi as ending the remaining geographic basis for effective [[HezongAlliance|合纵]], extending [[TerminalHezongFailure|末次合纵失败]] beyond the 241 BCE battlefield failure.
- A fourth-month cold disaster in Qin is linked to [[NaturalDisasterPoliticalOmen|自然灾害政治征兆]] reasoning: the episode says ancient interpreters could connect abnormal weather with political disorder.
- [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]]'s earlier relationship with [[ZhaoJi|赵姬]] becomes politically dangerous after Ying Zheng nears personal rule.
- Lu Buwei introduces [[LaoAi|嫪毐]] to Zhao Ji as a supposed eunuch, and Lao Ai's later power grows from palace scandal into a political faction with a fief, retainers, and control over affairs.
- Lao Ai's exposure leads him to misuse Qin royal seals and mobilize forces toward the Yongcheng palace setting, turning private sexual scandal into open rebellion.
- [[ChangpingJun|昌平君]] and other Qin forces suppress Lao Ai's rebellion, after which Ying Zheng applies severe punishment to Lao Ai's kin, followers, and retainers.
- Ying Zheng also punishes Zhao Ji by moving her to Yongcheng and kills the two sons she had with Lao Ai, making the crisis a royal-family legitimacy rupture as well as a rebellion case.
- The source says twenty-seven remonstrants have already been killed for speaking about Zhao Ji before [[MaoJiao|茅焦]] asks to speak.
- Mao Jiao frames his own possible death through the twenty-eight lunar mansions, saying he can complete the count if Qin wants another corpse.
- Mao Jiao's substantive warning is political rather than sentimental: public knowledge that Ying Zheng punishes mother, brothers, and advisers will make people stop orienting themselves toward Qin.
- Ying Zheng reverses course after the remonstrance, raises Mao Jiao to upper minister, and personally brings Zhao Ji back to Xianyang, restoring the mother-son relationship in the source's account.
- The host reads Ying Zheng's rage partly as political severity and partly as the private pain of a humiliating family scandal.

## Key Quotes
> "有生者不讳死，有国者不讳亡" - Mao Jiao's opening principle as rendered in the episode.

> "天下闻之，尽瓦解无向秦者" - the political consequence Mao Jiao warns about.

> "历史敢当面顶撞秦始皇第一人" - the episode title's framing of Mao Jiao.
