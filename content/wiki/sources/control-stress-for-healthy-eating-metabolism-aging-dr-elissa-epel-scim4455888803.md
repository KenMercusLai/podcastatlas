## Summary
This [[HubermanLab]] episode has [[AndrewHuberman]] interview [[ElissaEpel]] about stress as a biological and psychological response that changes with duration, interpretation, coping resources, and recovery. The episode connects [[StressResponseRecovery]], [[ThreatChallengeStressReappraisal]], [[StressEatingRewardLoop]], [[MindfulEatingCueControl]], [[StressBiologicalAging]], [[RadicalAcceptanceChronicStress]], [[UncertaintyToleranceResilience]], and [[PositiveStressBreathwork]] while keeping biomarker, meditation, breathwork, and nutrition claims source-scoped. Its central synthesis is that stress harm is not determined by exposure alone: threat appraisal, rumination, recovery, environment design, body-based regulation, and acceptance practices all shape whether stress becomes damaging, useful, or growth-supporting.

## Key Claims
- [[ElissaEpel]] defines stress as the feeling that demands exceed available resources, and distinguishes acute, daily, moderate, and chronic stress by duration, interpretation, and recovery.
- [[StressResponseRecovery]] frames useful stress management as a three-route toolkit: top-down awareness and self-talk, body-based regulation, and changing scenes or safety cues.
- [[ThreatChallengeStressReappraisal]] separates threat physiology from challenge physiology, with challenge appraisal tied in the source to better problem-solving, oxygenation, recovery, inflammation markers, and telomere associations.
- [[StressEatingRewardLoop]] links stress to cravings, insulin resistance, reward-center activation, compulsive eating in a subset of people with obesity, and liquid-sugar environments.
- [[MindfulEatingCueControl]] combines hunger checks, craving surfing, body scans, mindful eating, and environmental cue removal; pregnancy intervention data are presented as stronger for glucose tolerance, child outcomes, and maternal mental health than for limiting gestational weight gain.
- [[StressBiologicalAging]] ties chronic stress to telomeres, telomerase, mitochondria, inflammatory gene expression, caregiving, meditation studies, and meaningful challenge, while repeatedly preserving measurement and causality limits.
- [[RadicalAcceptanceChronicStress]] and [[UncertaintyToleranceResilience]] treat uncontrollable stressors as practice fields for dropping futile resistance, accepting uncertainty, building coherent narrative, and redirecting energy toward what remains controllable.
- [[PositiveStressBreathwork]] records Epel's interest in controlled high-arousal practices, including exercise and the [[WimHof]] method, but keeps biomarker findings preliminary.
