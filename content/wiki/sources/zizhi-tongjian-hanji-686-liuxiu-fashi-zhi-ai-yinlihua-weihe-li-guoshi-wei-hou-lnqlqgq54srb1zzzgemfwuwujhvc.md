# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》686丨刘秀发誓只爱阴丽华，为何立郭氏为后？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianwu 2 / 26 CE immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-687-si-hou-200-nian-luzhi-jing-zao-qi-chi-da-ru-lhakzy89w1l4vhazjuyenbjrw9iz|Hanji 687]]. It connects [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]]'s early consolidation work with [[YanCenEasternHan|延岑]]'s Hanzhong/Wudu fighting, [[GongsunShuEasternHan|公孙述]]'s Nanzheng move, the first empress settlement around [[YinLihua|阴丽华]], [[GuoShengtong|郭圣通]], and [[LiuQiangDonghaiKing|刘强]], and the legal-order conflict between [[JiaFuEasternHan|贾复]] and [[KouXunEasternHan|寇恂]] in [[YingchuanCommandery|颍川]].

The durable synthesis is that early Guangwu consolidation is already a multi-order problem, not only a battlefield sequence. Liu Xiu is still clearing rivals and former Gengshi forces, but he is also formalizing royal kinship, empress succession, crown-prince status, and the rule that military merit cannot automatically shield a commander's subordinate from local law.

## Key Claims

- Yan Cen's Hanzhong-Wudu pressure creates a southwestern instability layer before the later Shunyang, Hanzhong, and Shu sequence in Hanji 690.
- [[LiuJiaHanzhongKingEasternHan|汉中王刘嘉]] loses Hanzhong, regathers troops, fails against Hou Dan at Nanzheng, and later defeats Yan Cen near the Sanguan route.
- Liu Xiu's return to Luoyang is paired with amnesty, southern campaign assignment, and continuing pressure on Gengshi remnants and regional rivals.
- [[YinLihua|阴丽华]] refuses the empress position because Guo has already borne a son, so Guo's elevation is tied to heir status and palace-order management rather than only Liu Xiu's personal preference.
- On 26 CE-06-07, Liu Xiu makes [[GuoShengtong|郭圣通]] empress and makes her son [[LiuQiangDonghaiKing|刘强]] crown prince, creating the maternal-status foundation later destabilized by Hanji 719 and Hanji 720.
- [[LiuYangZhendingKingEasternHan|真定王刘杨]] remains part of the Guo story because the source identifies Guo as his niece and recalls his prior death in Liu Xiu's consolidation field.
- Jia Fu's southern victories at Shaoling and Xinxī are followed by a subordinate's murder case in Yingchuan, where Kou Xun executes the offender publicly rather than letting military status override local law.
- Kou Xun defuses Jia Fu's anger through avoidance, supply, hospitality, and a Lin Xiangru/Lian Po precedent; Liu Xiu then turns private feud into public command discipline by seating the two generals together.
- The closing Wuxiao campaign setup keeps early Eastern Han consolidation open: the regime has solved some succession and command conflicts but has not completed military pacification.

## Key Quotes

> "阴丽华因为郭贵人已经为刘秀生下一个儿子，所以始终不接受皇后之位" - the source's direct explanation for Yin Lihua's refusal.

> "六月初七，刘秀立郭氏为皇后，并立她所生的儿子刘强为皇太子" - the empress and crown-prince settlement.

> "两位将军不能私下争斗" - Liu Xiu's settlement frame for Jia Fu and Kou Xun.
