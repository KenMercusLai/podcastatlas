# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》178｜冒顿为何要放过刘邦 这才是真相（2）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-178-maodun-weihe-yao-fangguo-liubang-zhe-cai-shi-zhenxiang-1-lumszkbr-klnlodxmxdf-n8p5wym|Hanji 178 part 1]] after [[LiuBang|刘邦]] escapes the [[BaidengEncirclement|白登之围]]. It first records Liu Bang's reward for [[ChenPing|陈平]] by making him marquis of [[QuniCounty|曲逆]], then follows the return route through [[Handan|邯郸]], where Liu Bang's coarse treatment of [[ZhangAo|张敖]] provokes [[GuanGao|贯高]] and [[ZhaoWuZhaoMinister|赵午]] into a source-scoped assassination conspiracy. The final sections move from [[LiuRuyi|刘如意]]'s appointment in [[DaiKingdomEarlyHan|代国]] to [[WeiyangPalace|未央宫]], [[XiaoHe|萧何]]'s palace-grandness argument, [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s [[UntimelyExtravagance|时绌举赢]]-style criticism, and Liu Bang's post-Baideng turn back toward [[HanXiongnuHeqinPolicy|汉匈和亲政策]].

## Key Claims
- Liu Bang's reward of Chen Ping at 曲逆 turns Baideng survival into a visible post-crisis merit grant: Chen Ping is not only useful in covert danger but receives a full county-level honor after repeated rescue contributions.
- The source frames [[QuniCounty|曲逆]] itself as part of the reward logic because Liu Bang is impressed by its houses and population after the Qin-Han wars.
- [[LuYuanPrincess|鲁元公主]]'s marriage to Zhang Ao is read as political alliance infrastructure: it connects Liu Bang's household with [[ZhangEr|张耳]]'s Zhao line and probably grew out of wartime Han-Zhao alignment.
- Liu Bang's public humiliation of Zhang Ao is not just personal roughness. In the episode, it creates a [[VassalHumiliationConspiracy|vassal humiliation conspiracy]] because Zhao ministers read insult to their king as intolerable status damage.
- Zhang Ao's response is deliberately loyal: he invokes Zhang Er's dependence on Liu Bang and refuses to let his ministers turn personal shame into regicide.
- Guan Gao and Zhao Wu nevertheless decide to act without implicating Zhang Ao, showing how loyal ministers can convert ruler-vassal etiquette failure into a private political-violence plan.
- The Dai branch shows that same-surname kingship is not stable by kinship alone: after [[LiuXiDaiKing|刘喜]] flees the Xiongnu/Han Wang Xin attack, Liu Bang replaces him with his young son Liu Ruyi.
- The Weiyang Palace scene carries two competing explanations: Xiao He defends grand palaces as imperial awe and future cost control, while Sima Guang criticizes luxurious construction during poverty and unsettled war.
- The host adds a source-scoped motive reading: Xiao He's explanation likely gives Liu Bang a public reason to inhabit the kind of imperial grandeur Liu Bang himself had admired since seeing Qin Shi Huang's Xianyang order.
- The setup of [[ZongzhengOffice|宗正]] shows the early Western Han court turning Liu-family kinship into an administrative object, not only a family fact.
- The closing returns to the Xiongnu policy pivot: after Baideng, Liu Bang abandons immediate conquest as the main answer and adopts Liu Jing's heqin strategy as a constrained frontier-management tool.
- The episode leaves [[LuZhi|吕雉]]'s response to heqin unresolved, so the wiki keeps the implementation and household conflict for later sources.

## Key Quotes
> "大丈夫当如此也" - the episode's reminder of Liu Bang's old reaction to Qin imperial grandeur.

> "以仁义为华丽，以道德为威严" - Sima Guang's moral standard against palace grandeur.

> "皇上何等无礼，辱骂我们大王" - the Zhao ministers' insult frame before the conspiracy.
