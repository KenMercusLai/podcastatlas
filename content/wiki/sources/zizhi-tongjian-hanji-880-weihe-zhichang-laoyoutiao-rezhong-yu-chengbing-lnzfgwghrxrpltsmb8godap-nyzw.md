# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》880丨为何职场老油条热衷于称病？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 168 CE transition just before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-881-shi-shui-zai-fa-zhanzheng-hengcai-lipglslijnbp0cuqfpps88q5wit6|Hanji 881]] and the Hanji 882-887 [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] / [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] crisis sequence. After [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] dies and [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] has not yet formally ascended, many shangshu officials claim illness to avoid the risky court, and Chen Fan invokes "事亡如存" to force officials back into administrative duty.

The second half adds the pre-approval stage of [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]]'s [[DongqiangLateHan|东羌]] strategy. Duan argues that surrender-first handling under [[ZhangHuanLateHan|张奂]] and Huangfu Gui has not ended the problem, then presents a troop, terrain, time, and money estimate for a decisive campaign against the [[XianlingQiang|先零羌]] and other Eastern Qiang forces. [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] approves the plan before dying, leaving the episode's final question about whether the policy can survive the succession transition.

## Key Claims

- In 168 CE, Emperor Huan dies while succession and mourning overlap; Dou Wu becomes general-in-chief, Chen Fan becomes grand tutor, and [[HuGuangLateHan|胡广]] oversees the shangshu offices.
- Before the new emperor formally ascends, many shangshu officials fear the unsettled situation and falsely claim illness rather than enter court.
- Chen Fan's letter uses "事亡如存" to argue that ministers owe continuing service to a deceased ruler's state, especially when public affairs are urgent.
- The officials become more frightened after Chen Fan's rebuke and return to court work, making the episode a source case for [[CrisisDutyContinuity|危局职守连续性]].
- Liu Hong arrives at Xiamenting, is received through Dou Miao and Dou Wu's formal accession procedure, and ascends as Emperor Ling the next day before Emperor Huan is buried at Xuanling.
- Duan Jiong has already pacified Western Qiang, but Xianling and other Eastern Qiang groups have not fully submitted.
- Duan acknowledges Zhang Huan's concern that military action could unsettle already-submitted Qiang groups, but argues that repeated rebellion, flat terrain, and enemy fatigue make decisive attack feasible.
- Duan frames hard suppression as a bounded project: five thousand cavalry, ten thousand infantry, three thousand vehicle units, three winters and two summers, and fifty-four hundred million cash.
- The memorial compares that projected cost with earlier multi-year Qiang wars that consumed far larger sums, making this a source case for [[BudgetedFrontierSuppression|预算化边疆清剿]].
- Emperor Huan approves Duan's plan before his death, so the episode leaves policy continuity under the new reign unresolved.

## Key Quotes

> "事亡如存" - Chen Fan's duty-continuity phrase for serving the deceased ruler's state.

> "狼子野心" - Duan Jiong's hostile description of rebellious Qiang groups.

> "五十四亿" - the projected campaign cost in Duan's memorial.
