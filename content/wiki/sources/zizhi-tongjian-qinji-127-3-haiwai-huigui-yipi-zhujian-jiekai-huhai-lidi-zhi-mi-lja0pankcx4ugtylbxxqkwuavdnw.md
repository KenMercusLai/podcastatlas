## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode places the familiar [[Shiji|《史记》]]-style account of [[ZhaoGao|赵高]], [[LiSi|李斯]], and [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] beside the alternate [[ZhaoZhengShu|《赵正书》]] account of Hu Hai's accession. In the alternate version, [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]] consults Li Si and [[FengQuji|冯去疾]] while dying, and Hu Hai is chosen because he is nearby rather than because Zhao Gao first forges the succession. The episode does not settle which source is true; it treats the disagreement as a source-critical problem and asks whether an [[EmergencySuccessionLegitimation|emergency nearby-heir choice]] could have been politically plausible.

## Key Claims
- The received narrative still presents the transition as hidden death, odor concealment with fish, rapid return through [[QinZhidao|秦直道]], and public mourning only after the convoy reaches [[Xianyang|咸阳]].
- The traditional branch says Zhao Gao fears revenge from [[MengTian|蒙恬]] and [[MengYiQin|蒙毅]], so he tells Hu Hai that Qin Shi Huang had wanted Hu Hai as heir but Meng Yi blocked the plan.
- [[ZhaoZhengShu|《赵正书》]] challenges the [[Shiji|《史记》]] sequence by moving the succession decision into Qin Shi Huang's deathbed consultation rather than making it only Zhao Gao's post-death plot.
- In that alternate account, Li Si insists on his dependence and loyalty to Qin Shi Huang before discussing the succession.
- Li Si and [[FengQuji|冯去疾]] argue that the court is too far from the capital and that summoning a wider ministerial discussion may create disorder, so the nearby Hu Hai should be established as heir.
- The episode flags an internal difficulty: Feng Quji's presence in the deathbed scene is hard to reconcile with other placement of him at Xianyang, and the host leaves this unresolved.
- Even under the [[ZhaoZhengShu|《赵正书》]] account, Hu Hai's reign quickly turns destructive: he kills Fusu and Meng Tian, restores Zhao Gao, kills siblings, damages law/textual archives, and harms the state.
- The host does not choose definitively between [[Shiji|《史记》]] and [[ZhaoZhengShu|《赵正书》]], but argues that choosing a nearby prince during a ruler's final illness is not impossible under the circumstances.

## Key Quotes
> "正大光明" - the host's shorthand for how the [[ZhaoZhengShu|《赵正书》]] version presents Hu Hai's establishment.

> "最不坏" - the source's pragmatic frame for a nearby-heir choice under urgent conditions.

> "这个矛盾解释不清" - the host's treatment of [[FengQuji|冯去疾]]'s contested presence.

## Contradictions
- Source-level contradiction with [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-127-2-lisi-weisha-bu-bang-yingming-de-fusu-lq-vny5duwwzfubpeijr21oazxbg|Qinji 127-2]]: Qinji 127-2 presents Hu Hai's accession as a Zhao Gao-Li Si-Hu Hai forged-edict coup after Qin Shi Huang's death, while this episode's [[ZhaoZhengShu|《赵正书》]] branch says Hu Hai may have been selected by Qin Shi Huang, Li Si, and Feng Quji before the emperor died.
- Unresolved internal tension: [[FengQuji|冯去疾]] appears in the [[ZhaoZhengShu|《赵正书》]] deathbed consultation even though the source says his location is hard to explain.
- Evaluation tension: the alternate account can make Hu Hai's establishment procedurally less conspiratorial without making Hu Hai's later rule competent or legitimate in outcome.
