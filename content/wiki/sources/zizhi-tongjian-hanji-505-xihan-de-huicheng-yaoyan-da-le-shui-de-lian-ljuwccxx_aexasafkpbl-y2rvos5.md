# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》505丨西汉的毁城谣言，打了谁的脸？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianshi 3 / 30 BCE under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]. It begins from a moral frame about knowing when to lower one's head after success, then centers a Chang'an flood rumor after more than forty days of autumn rain: [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]] proposes official evacuation onto boats and city walls, while [[WangShangWesternHan|王商]] argues that the report is rumor and that state orders would magnify panic.

The durable synthesis is that crisis judgment and blame assignment are both political. Wang Shang gains Chengdi's appreciation by refusing to turn an unverified panic into official action, Wang Feng loses face because his proposal would have validated fear, and later eclipse-earthquake interpretation lets [[DuQinWesternHan|杜钦]] and [[GuYongWesternHan|谷永]] move disaster pressure away from Wang-family outer relatives and toward [[EmpressXuWesternHan|许皇后]] and harem favoritism. The episode closes with [[KuangHengWesternHan|匡衡]] losing office for excess land and misappropriation, reinforcing the host's warning that high position without restraint can return a person to ruin.

## Key Claims

- Heavy rain in Guanzhong creates a Chang'an rumor that floodwater is about to destroy the city, producing public fear, stampede risk, and crying among the elderly and children.
- Wang Feng advises Chengdi to move the dowager, emperor, and palace women onto imperial boats and to order officials and commoners onto Chang'an's walls.
- Wang Shang rejects the flood report as rumor and warns that an official order to climb the walls would intensify public fear rather than protect the city.
- Chengdi accepts Wang Shang's judgment, the city calms after investigation, and Wang Shang's calm crisis handling makes Wang Feng look mistaken.
- The episode treats Wang Shang's later chancellorship as the beginning of a more direct rivalry with Wang Feng.
- Solar eclipse, earthquake, and palace shaking create an omen-inquiry opening; Du Qin and Gu Yong answer by blaming harem favoritism and heir risk rather than Wang-family outer-relative power.
- Gu Yong is read as attaching himself to Wang Feng by using disaster interpretation to shift pressure onto Xu-family and harem targets.
- Kuang Heng is removed to commoner status after accusations of occupying excess fief land and misappropriating administered property, making him a negative example of post-success arrogance.

## Key Quotes

> "懂得笑着低头的人才是聪明的人" - the host's opening frame for success, restraint, and Kuang Heng's fall.

> "一定是谣言" - Wang Shang's decisive reading of the flood panic in the episode.
