# 016 问世间，情为何物|李莫愁才是金庸《神雕侠侣》核心人物？

## Summary

This [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] episode reads [[ReturnOfTheCondorHeroes|《神雕侠侣》]] as an ensemble inquiry into what people do after love fails, with [[LiMochou|李莫愁]] as its central case. The hosts dispute whether rejection transformed her or amplified prior rigidity and self-centeredness, then contrast her revenge with 武三娘's sacrifice, 完颜萍's reorientation, and the mutually destructive marriage of 公孙止 and 裘千尺. The resulting account preserves Li Mochou's tenderness and longing without using injury to excuse violence.

## Key Claims

- [[LiMochou|李莫愁]]'s abandonment by 陆展元 and perceived exclusion in the Ancient Tomb help explain her grievance, but the hosts disagree whether one heartbreak changed her nature or released traits already present.
- Her repeated premise that she has done no wrong turns injury into an enlarging narrative of universal unfairness, whereas reflection, wider purpose, or a rebuilt life could have redirected the same pain.
- Her attempt to use 洪凌波 as a means of escape supplies evidence of self-preservation at another person's expense, while her care for infant 郭襄 and refusal to harm 陆无双 after seeing the divided handkerchief preserve tenderness and attachment.
- 武三娘's independent protection of others, 完颜萍's acceptance of an ordinary later life, and the varied outcomes of the 武 brothers show that romantic injury does not dictate revenge or permanent fixation.
- 公孙止 and 裘千尺 turn marital disrespect, control, betrayal, and self-preservation into reciprocal violence; their history does not reduce to the claim that a strong wife causes a weak husband's treachery.
- 公孙止's willingness to endanger his daughter briefly moves Li Mochou because it offers the fantasy of being chosen first, yet his conduct also exposes an ethical floor beneath even her compromised judgment.
- The handkerchief, 绝情谷, and Li Mochou's repeated half-stanza of 《摸鱼儿》 organize the novel's different answers to love: reciprocity, sacrifice, possession, release, loneliness, and self-destruction.
