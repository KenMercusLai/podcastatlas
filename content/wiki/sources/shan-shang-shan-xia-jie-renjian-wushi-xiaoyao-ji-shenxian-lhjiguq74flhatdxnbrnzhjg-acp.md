# 山上山下皆人间，无事逍遥即神仙

## Summary
This [[Mianji|面基]] episode uses [[LiChuang|李闯]]'s book [[CizhiShangShan|《辞职上山》]] to demystify quitting, going to [[WudangMountain|武当山]], and imagining Daoist life as an escape from ordinary society. Li Chuang's anthropology training, military service, publishing work, hutong shop, Wudang volunteer work, and Chinese-medicine study become one method for observing lived constraint. The episode's durable synthesis is [[RetreatRomanceDeflation|retreat romance deflation]]: mountain life, medicine, belief, fieldwork, and freedom all return to ordinary bodies, schedules, costs, relationships, and interpretation.

## Key Claims
- [[LiChuang|李闯]] is presented as a serial experimenter, but not as an anti-work fantasy figure: his choices are repeatedly shaped by money, health, study, family consent, institutional thresholds, and future options.
- [[CizhiShangShan|《辞职上山》]] is framed less as a quitting manual than as a record of [[RetreatRomanceDeflation|retreat romance deflation]], where Wudang is both sacred mountain and tourism site.
- [[WudangMountain|武当山]] is shown through [[DaoistTempleEverydayLabor]]: morning and evening lessons, altar duty, sweeping, offerings, night fire/security shifts, cold, damp, insects, animals, and interpersonal work.
- The episode adds [[QuanzhenDaoism|全真道教]] as Wudang's main institutional frame, while keeping specific practices source-scoped rather than treating every Daoist method as one doctrine.
- Scripture recitation is treated as embodied form as much as explanation: repetition, rhythm, and aesthetic immersion can work before a person has fully interpreted the text.
- The discussion of [[ZhenwuDadi|真武大帝]], local gods, crying pilgrims, and Fujian family practice extends [[BeliefAsLastResortSupport]]: folk belief can be a practical refuge when medicine, money, or social support has failed.
- [[FieldnoteToPublicWriting]] names Li Chuang's method of recording event, speech, expression, environment, and later self-judgment before turning lived material into stories.
- The source extends [[FieldworkAsKnowledgeMethod]] by treating Wudang, Xishuangbanna, a hutong shop, and a county hospital as places where everyday friction becomes evidence.
- [[MedicineAsWaySeeking|以医入道]] links Chinese-medicine study, county-hospital internship, diet, work rhythm, and social causes of illness to [[MedicalKnowledgeBoundary]] rather than to medical certainty.
- [[XiaoyaoAsNonDependence|逍遥作为不被外物左右]] is presented as freedom inside limits, not as the result of having enough money, technique, or mystical experience.
- [[CircularLinearTimeTension|环形时间与线性时间张力]] contrasts agricultural seasonal time with industrial progress time, while warning that both can pressure people in different ways.

## Key Quotes
> "经是用来读的，不是用来讲的" - the episode's recitation-before-explanation frame.

> "山上山下皆在人间" - the source's boundary against treating Wudang as escape from reality.

> "以医入道" - Li Chuang's provisional phrase for using medicine to approach life and natural order.

> "神仙吃完就该我们吃了" - the episode's image of Daoist belief as ordinary lived practice.

## Contradictions

- It qualifies [[ChineseFolkReligionLayering]] and [[MedicalKnowledgeBoundary]] by arguing that nonmedical or religious recourse often appears when people lack money, certainty, or institutional options; this is recorded as source-scoped social interpretation, not as a medical efficacy claim.
