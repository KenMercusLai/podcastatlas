# VOL.215 低GI蜂蜜、零蔗糖、抗糖丸…？这届控糖人到底踩了多少坑？ft.「大食话」

## Summary
This [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode has [[Atang|阿汤]] and [[YueWanrou|岳宛柔]] from [[DaShiHua|大食话]] turn sugar anxiety into [[PracticalSugarControl|practical sugar control]]. The discussion separates sugar, carbohydrates, staple foods, fruit, juice, added sugar, free sugar, sweeteners, glycemic-index claims, and "zero sucrose" labels so listeners can reduce high-risk sugar without moralizing every sweet food. Its practical center is simple: reduce liquid and unconscious sugar first, read ingredient and nutrition labels, keep staple foods structurally complex, and treat desserts as occasional enjoyment rather than a daily default.

## Key Claims
- Sweet preference is not only weak willpower; the source ties it to evolved energy seeking, emotional comfort, fatigue, stress, exercise, and modern [[FoodPleasureEngineering|food pleasure engineering]].
- [[PracticalSugarControl]] should distinguish ordinary carbohydrates and staple foods from fast liquid sugars, highly refined intake, and repeated unconscious sugar exposure.
- [[AddedFreeSugarDistinction]] matters because added sugars and free sugars include honey, syrup, fruit juice, concentrated juice, and sugars added by manufacturers, cooks, or consumers.
- [[LiquidSugarRisk]] is higher than whole-fruit intake because juicing removes chewing, disrupts food structure, lowers fiber effects, and can deliver glucose and fructose quickly.
- [[LowGIMarketingTrap]] appears when "low GI" claims hide total sugar load, fructose metabolism, or weak certification context; GL and total intake remain important.
- [[NutritionLabelSugarTraps]] include "zero sucrose" products, sour drinks, sweetened cereals, flavored yogurts, kombucha, concentrated juice, honey, syrup, and nutrition-panel carbohydrate clues.
- [[SweetenerUncertainty]] keeps artificial and non-sugar sweeteners from being treated as either poison or free license to eat unlimited sweet foods.
- [[GlycationSkinAnxiety]] is recognized but bounded: glycation and AGEs are real concepts, while skin aging, acne, inflammation, sleep, UV exposure, smoking, stress, and total diet are multivariable.
- Resistant starch from cooled rice, potatoes, or noodles can smooth post-meal glucose response, but the source does not treat it as a meaningful calorie-erasing or weight-loss trick.

## Key Quotes
> "控糖而非恐糖" - the episode's practical dietary posture.

> "零蔗糖不等于无糖" - the label-literacy warning.

> "主食不要过量、不要过精" - the staple-food boundary.
