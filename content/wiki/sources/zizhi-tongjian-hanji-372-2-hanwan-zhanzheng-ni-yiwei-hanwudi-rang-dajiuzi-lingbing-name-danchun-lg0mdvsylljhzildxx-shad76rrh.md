# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》372-2｜汉宛战争，你以为汉武帝让大舅子领兵那么单纯？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the political prelude to [[LiGuangliWesternHan|李广利]]'s first campaign against [[DayuanWesternHan|大宛]]. It presents the failed horse exchange and killing of Han envoys as the immediate trigger, but argues that [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] also wanted to convert favor toward [[LadyLiWesternHan|李夫人]]'s family into publicly defensible military merit for her brother. Li is therefore named Ershi General and given six thousand cavalry plus tens of thousands of recruited young men, while the wider campaign remains shaped by anger, overconfidence, Western Regions expansion, and the expectation that victory can justify ennoblement.

The episode uses [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s criticism to sharpen the institutional problem: preserving the outward rule that generals should not receive a marquisate without merit can be worse than openly breaking it if the ruler gives national command to an unqualified favorite and makes soldiers bear the risk. A separate annalistic branch records [[WangWenshuWesternHan|王温舒]]'s suicide amid corruption charges, the destruction of five connected households, and a Guandong locust disaster reaching Dunhuang, placing court favoritism and military ambition beside coercive-law failure and civilian suffering.

## Key Claims

- The failed horse exchange and violence against Han envoys are presented as the direct cause of the Han-Dayuan war, while anger, earlier Western Regions success, and a favorable envoy report encourage underestimation of the campaign.
- Wudi appoints Li Guangli, brother of favored Lady Li, as Ershi General and expects the campaign to produce the military merit needed to make a marquisate publicly defensible.
- The episode treats “no marquisate without military merit” as a real legitimacy constraint even under a powerful emperor, but not as an effective competence safeguard.
- Sima Guang's criticism is that using war and command to manufacture a procedural excuse for private reward exposes the state and soldiers to greater danger than a direct unmerited grant.
- Favor toward Lady Li extends to her brothers Li Guangli and [[LiYannianWesternHan|李延年]], but the source does not establish that family favor was the war's only motive.
- Wang Wenshu's corruption case and the destruction of five connected households are presented as the delayed collapse of authority built through cruelty and fear.
- The terse record of locusts flying from Guandong to Dunhuang is read as evidence of crop destruction, hunger, and displacement hidden behind compressed annalistic language.

## Key Quotes

> “欲侯宠姬李氏之兄广利” - the episode's rendering of Sima Guang's explanation for the appointment.

> “关东蝗大起，飞西至敦煌” - the compact disaster notice expanded by the host into a civilian-cost reading.

## Contradictions

- No settled factual contradiction with existing wiki content was found. The source adds the appointment motive and initial mobilization before Hanji 373-2's account of corridor denial, hunger, and defeat.
- Causal qualification: the episode presents private favor and hoped-for ennoblement as a central motive, while later sources also preserve anger over envoys and horses, Western Regions strategy, anti-Xiongnu pressure, and imperial-credibility concerns. The wiki does not reduce the war to one cause.
