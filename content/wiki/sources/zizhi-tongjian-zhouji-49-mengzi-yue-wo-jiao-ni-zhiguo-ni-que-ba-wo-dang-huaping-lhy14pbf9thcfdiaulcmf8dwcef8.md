# 《资治通鉴·周纪》49丨孟子曰：我教你治国 你却把我当花瓶

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode centers on [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]] after [[QiState|齐国]] conquers [[YanState|燕国]], with [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]] judging conquest by whether Yan's people welcome relief rather than by Qi's military success or claims of [[MandateOfHeavenLegitimacy|天意]]. Mencius's answer turns the campaign into a test of [[MencianPeopleBasedLegitimacy|孟子的民本合法性]], [[MencianBenevolentGovernment|孟子仁政]], and the gap between taking territory and governing people. The second half uses [[ChenJiaWarringStates|陈甲]]'s question about [[ZhouGong|周公]] to argue that rulers and managers lose trust less by erring than by rationalizing error instead of correcting it.

## Key Claims
- In the episode's frame, [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]] asks whether he should keep [[YanState|燕国]] after conquering it in about fifty days, and he interprets that speed as possible evidence of Heaven's will.
- [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]] does not give an absolute anti-war answer: conquest is acceptable only if the people of Yan genuinely welcome Qi as a rescuer.
- The episode contrasts [[ShangTang|商汤]]'s punitive campaign with Qi's behavior in Yan: a conquering army that kills, imprisons, plunders, and damages ancestral institutions forfeits the "saving the people" claim.
- Mencius's proposed remedy is concrete: release detained old and young people, stop taking treasure, consult Yan's people, install a new ruler, and withdraw while repair is still possible.
- Qi Xuan Wang does not accept that remedy; Yan's people soon rebel, confirming the episode's lesson that occupation is easier than governance.
- [[ChenJiaWarringStates|陈甲]] tries to soften Qi Xuan Wang's failure by asking whether even saints can err, using [[ZhouGong|周公]] and Guan Shu's rebellion as the example.
- Mencius accepts that understandable mistakes can happen, but says the ancient gentleman corrects errors while the present pseudo-gentleman invents excuses.
- The episode reads this as a direct rebuke of Qi Xuan Wang: shame over not listening to Mencius is not the same as policy correction.
- The closing notice says the text records Qi Xuan Wang's death and [[QiMinWang|田地 / 齐湣王]]'s accession, but the host flags this as wrong because Qi Xuan Wang dies about twelve years later.
- The side note on [[ZhongliChun|钟离春 / 钟无艳]] and [[JixiaAcademy|稷下学宫]] complicates Qi Xuan Wang's evaluation: he is not treated as a model ruler, but his reign still supports intellectual flourishing in Qi.

## Key Quotes
> "如果攻占燕国能让燕国百姓高兴，就可以攻占；如果不能让百姓高兴，就不要攻占。" - the episode's summary of Mencius's people-based test for conquest.

> "占领很容易，治理起来很难。" - the host's practical governance summary of the Yan problem.

> "古代君子有错就改；今时所谓君子有错却顺其自然，还捏造许多理由托词。" - Mencius's mistake-correction contrast as presented by the episode.

## Contradictions
- No direct contradiction found in existing wiki claims.
- The episode itself flags a chronology contradiction in the received notice: Qi Xuan Wang's death and Tian Di's accession are said to be placed too early, so the wiki records that under [[ChronicleChronologyDrift|编年错位]] and [[QiChronologyDispute|齐威王编年争议]] rather than treating it as a settled 314 BCE death.
- The episode also qualifies earlier Mencian idealism: it supports just war in principle but shows that the claim collapses when the occupying army cannot or will not practice benevolent governance.
