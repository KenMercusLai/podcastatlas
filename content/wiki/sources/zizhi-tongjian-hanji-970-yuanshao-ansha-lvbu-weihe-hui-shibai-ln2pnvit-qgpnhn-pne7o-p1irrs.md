# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》970丨袁绍暗杀吕布，为何会失败？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the northern late-Han branch before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-971-luanshi-miyun-shui-sha-le-caocao-laodie-ljh3hdhssigmv78bueo-hprfqwku|Hanji 971]] and the later Yanzhou-Lü Bu sequence. It follows [[YuanShao|袁绍]] through the [[WeiCommanderyLateHan|魏郡]] soldier revolt, the [[HeishanArmy|黑山军]] attack on [[YeLateHan|邺城]], [[TaoShengLateHan|陶生]]'s protection of Yuan Shao's family, and Yuan Shao's retaliatory campaigns against [[YuDuLateHan|余毒]] and [[ZhangYanLateHan|张燕]].

The core episode explains why the [[YuanShaoLyuBuAssassinationAttempt|袁绍刺吕布未遂]] fails. Yuan Shao and [[LyuBu|吕布]] temporarily cooperate against Zhang Yan, but the relationship collapses over Lü Bu's violent retinue and mutual insecurity; Yuan Shao then wraps a killing plan in appointment and escort language, while Lü Bu survives by detecting the escort threat, leaving sound in the tent, and escaping through a prepared passage before going again to [[ZhangYangLateHan|张杨]].

## Key Claims
- [[YuanShao|袁绍]] is at Boluojin when [[WeiCommanderyLateHan|魏郡]] soldiers revolt and connect with [[YuDuLateHan|余毒]]'s [[HeishanArmy|黑山军]] forces.
- The rebels attack [[YeLateHan|邺城]], kill the Wei commandery administrator, and frighten Yuan Shao's followers because many families are inside the city.
- [[TaoShengLateHan|陶生]], a Black Mountain-linked commander and former Neihuang minor official, shuts the western gate, protects Yuan Shao's household and state officials' families, and escorts them to Chiqiu.
- Yuan Shao rewards Tao Sheng as Jianyi zhonglangjiang after the family rescue, then shifts toward revenge against the Black Mountain forces.
- Yuan Shao attacks [[YuDuLateHan|余毒]] at Luchang Mountain around Zhaoge, breaks his force after five days, kills Yu Du and many followers, and continues north against multiple mountain leaders.
- Yuan Shao then fights [[ZhangYanLateHan|张燕]] in the Changshan field, where Zhang Yan has tens of thousands of strong troops and thousands of horses, with Xiongnu and Wuhuan elements also in the field.
- [[LyuBu|吕布]] joins Yuan Shao against Zhang Yan, and the joint campaign lasts more than ten days before both sides withdraw exhausted.
- The alliance quickly destabilizes because Yuan Shao dislikes the violent conduct of Lü Bu's soldiers, while Lü Bu reads Yuan Shao's hostility and no longer wants to stay under him.
- Yuan Shao uses an imperial-style appointment and armed escort to create an assassination opening against Lü Bu, making the episode a late-Han case of [[CourtesyCoveredElimination|礼遇包装式清除]] and [[AssassinationAccessCredential|刺杀接近凭证]].
- Lü Bu detects that the escort is suspicious, has people keep zither music sounding in his tent, and leaves through a pre-dug passage before the armed men destroy the bed and tent.
- Yuan Shao's response after learning Lü Bu is alive is defensive fear: he closes the city gates against possible retaliation.
- Lü Bu survives the failed attempt and again attaches himself to [[ZhangYangLateHan|张杨]], setting up the later route through Zhang Miao and Yanzhou.
- The closing preview introduces [[CaoSong|曹嵩]] leaving Langya toward [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] under the reception of [[YingShaoLateHan|应劭]], directly bridging to [[CaoSongMurder|曹嵩遇害]] in Hanji 971.
