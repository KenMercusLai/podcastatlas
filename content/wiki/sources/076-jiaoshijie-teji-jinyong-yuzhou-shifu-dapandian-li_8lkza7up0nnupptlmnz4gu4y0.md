# 076 【教师节特辑】金庸宇宙师父大盘点

## Summary

This [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] Teachers' Day roundtable uses masters and schools across [[JinYong|金庸]]'s fiction to distinguish martial ability from teaching ability, emotional stability, care, reputation, and network access. Its durable synthesis is that a good mentor adapts instruction to the learner, protects without becoming possessive, welcomes useful teaching from others, and helps a student find a fitting path rather than demanding exclusive loyalty.

The discussion then moves from wuxia into real education. It treats active teacher-student romance as unsafe because evaluation and resources remain unequal, defines a meaningful alma mater through durable support rather than prestige alone, and argues that schools or martial lineages can provide systems and peers while exceptional mastery still depends on multiple teachers, practice, opportunity, judgment, and personal fit.

## Key Claims

- [[MultidimensionalMentorQuality|Mentor quality]] cannot be inferred from expertise alone; the episode evaluates ability, teaching skill, character, care, reputation, and network access separately.
- [[ZhangSanfengJinYong|张三丰]] is presented as the strongest all-round mentor because he combines high martial ability, differentiated instruction, emotional steadiness, care, and relative freedom from sectarian prejudice.
- The Jiangnan Seven Freaks represent parent-like long-term care despite uneven teaching, while Feng Qingyang represents brief but transformative conceptual guidance and the divine condor represents effective training through environment and practice.
- [[MultiMentorMastery|High-level learning]] often combines formal lineage, several teachers, inherited systems, unusual opportunities, sustained effort, broad-mindedness, and a method suited to the learner.
- A school or sect can supply curriculum, peers, identity, reputation, and accumulated method, but scale, divided attention, rigid doctrine, and factional labels can also constrain learning.
- [[SchoolBelongingThroughCare|Alma-mater belonging]] is earned when an institution teaches, protects, and continues to value students rather than concentrating support on a few high performers for institutional metrics.
- Teacher-student romance during an active educational relationship is ethically compromised by unequal authority, evaluation, resources, and freedom; a later relationship requires genuinely ended authority and mature, equal standing.

## Key Quotes

> "能力和教学能力是两回事。" - the episode's central distinction between expertise and pedagogy.

> "爱情应该包含平等、自由、尊重和责任。" - the standard used to reject romance within an active teacher-student hierarchy.
