# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》180｜西汉第一硬汉贯高的故事（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the post-[[BaidengEncirclement|白登之围]] settlement after [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-179-lishishang-heqin-zenme-laide-lslks-ovbomneornk4j-q6-ns0x1|Hanji 179]]. It shows [[LiuBang|刘邦]] implementing a substitute-princess version of [[HanXiongnuHeqinPolicy|汉匈和亲政策]], then accepting [[LouJing|刘敬 / 楼敬]]'s recommendation to move six-state elites and local powers into [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] under [[GuanzhongEliteRelocation|关中豪强迁徙]]. The core drama is the exposure of [[GuanGao|贯高]]'s plot, [[ZhangAo|张敖]]'s arrest and demotion, and Guan Gao's [[TortureResistantExoneration|刑讯守口式申冤]]: he survives torture long enough to prove that Zhang Ao did not join the assassination plan, then dies after his lord is released.

## Key Claims
- Gaozu ninth year turns the stalled heqin plan from Hanji 179 into an implementation compromise: Liu Bang chooses another woman, names her legitimate elder princess, sends her to [[MaoDunChanyu|冒顿单于]], and has [[LouJing|刘敬 / 楼敬]] escort the mission.
- [[SimaGuang|司马光]] criticizes Liu Jing's heqin strategy as insufficiently rigorous because Modu's violence cannot be reliably transformed by kinship or gifts.
- Liu Jing's second proposal links frontier defense with domestic control: move six-state royal descendants, powerful local families, and famous figures into [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] so the capital region has people and the eastern elite bases are weakened.
- The episode records [[GuanzhongEliteRelocation|关中豪强迁徙]] as the beginning of a long Western Han policy, not just a one-off resettlement order.
- The report against [[GuanGao|贯高]] converts the earlier [[VassalHumiliationConspiracy|诸侯王羞辱触发谋刺]] from a failed ambush into a treason case against [[ZhangAo|张敖]] and his court.
- [[ZhaoWuZhaoMinister|赵午]] and several co-conspirators die after the plot is exposed, but Guan Gao refuses immediate suicide because someone still has to prove Zhang Ao's innocence.
- [[TianShuHan|田叔]] and [[MengShuHan|孟叔]] voluntarily follow the arrested Zhang Ao as degraded retainers, giving the source a second model of Zhao-court loyalty beside Guan Gao's courtroom resistance.
- Guan Gao's interrogation reverses the usual [[CoercedConfessionTrap|逼供认罪陷阱]] pattern: beating and piercing fail to make him implicate Zhang Ao, so his suffering becomes evidence for Zhang Ao rather than evidence against him.
- [[XieGong|谢公]] functions as a credibility bridge between prison and ruler: Liu Bang sends someone who knows Guan Gao to test whether the denial is trustworthy.
- Zhang Ao is released but stripped of his Zhao kingship and reduced to Xuanping marquis, while [[LiuRuyi|刘如意]] is moved from [[DaiKingdomEarlyHan|代国]] to Zhao, making the case another opportunity to reduce non-Liu royal autonomy.
- Guan Gao accepts the pardon only long enough to confirm Zhang Ao's release; once his duty is complete, he kills himself because the regicide stain remains morally unbearable.

## Key Quotes
> "贯高是一条好汉" - Liu Bang's reaction after hearing Guan Gao's testimony.

> "忍着不死" - Guan Gao's explanation that survival served Zhang Ao's exoneration.

> "有愧于心" - Guan Gao's reason for refusing to live after pardon.

## Contradictions
