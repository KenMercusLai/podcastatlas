# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》583丨都知道他有问题，为什么还提拔？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills a Jianping 3 / 4 BCE [[WesternHanDynasty|西汉]] personnel memorial by [[WangJiaWesternHan|王嘉]] under [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]. Wang Jia asks why officials with known faults can still be restored or promoted, arguing that the court must distinguish major guilt from small defects when capable officials are rare and local order depends on respected, usable officeholders.

The durable synthesis is that talent preservation and accountability are not treated as opposites. Wang Jia cites Wei Shang, Han Anguo, and Zhang Chang as flawed but useful precedents, contrasts Han Wendi and Han Xuandi's cautious use of officials with later punitive churn, and warns that excessive inspections, rapid replacement, and public humiliation of two-thousand-shi officials weaken local authority before crises such as Su Ling's rebellion.

## Key Claims

- Wang Jia frames local governors and commandery or kingdom heads as hard-to-find officials whose authority can exceed older princely roles in practical administration.
- The memorial uses Wei Shang, Han Anguo, and Zhang Chang as precedents for restoring people with past offenses when their capacity is still valuable.
- Wang Jia argues that Han Wendi-era office stability gave officials confidence, status, and incentives to govern rather than merely protect themselves.
- Later harsh inspections and frequent removals make middling officials avoid action, make weaker officials fearful, and teach subordinates and commoners to disrespect senior local officers.
- Su Ling's rebellion becomes evidence that degraded local authority harms crisis response: officials and armed men hesitate when their superiors no longer command trust.
- Wang Jia recommends remembering capable officials' good service, tolerating small faults, cultivating talent before emergencies, and using people who can actually solve public problems.
- Han Aidi accepts this memorial by restoring or appointing figures including Man Chang and Xiao Xian, making this an unusually successful Wang Jia correction before later Dong Xian conflicts.

## Key Quotes

> "才难，不其然乎" - Wang Jia's cited frame that capable people are difficult to find.

> "记人之功，忘人之过" - the episode's summary of Wang Jia's recommended personnel standard.

> "不管白猫、黑猫，能抓住老鼠的就是好猫" - the host's modern shorthand for the usable-capacity criterion.
