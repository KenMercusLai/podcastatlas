# EP172-从黑话到学术词汇的滥用，语言究竟有多大的力量？

## Summary
This [[WushichaYanjiusuo|无时差研究所]] episode examines internet-company jargon such as “赋能,” “抓手,” “闭环,” “对齐,” and “跨端联动” as both communication tools and social instruments. It argues that specialized vocabulary can compress shared context and coordinate insiders, but repeated use in weekly reports, planning documents, and resource competition can turn it into [[PseudoProductivity]], status display, organizational conformity, or a substitute for evidence.

Its strongest synthesis joins [[JargonBoundaryMaking]] to [[GroupBoundaryLanguage]]. Workplace terms, fandom abbreviations, memes, academic vocabulary, and parenting-circle codes can all create belonging, protect information, and speed exchange while also raising entry costs, disciplining expression, and dividing insiders from outsiders. The practical boundary is whether a term improves precision and verifiability or instead hides product reality, inflates weak work, and makes opacity itself a source of authority.

## Key Claims
- Specialized language is useful when shared definitions let participants communicate a precise idea faster than ordinary explanation would.
- [[JargonBoundaryMaking]] becomes harmful when terminology fills reports without adding data, replaces already adequate words, or makes exclusion and professional display part of the point.
- Weekly-report length contests, repeated planning rituals, and competition for scarce resources can reward polished strategic language even when the underlying work or product remains weak.
- A startup's technical vocabulary becomes especially risky when it substitutes for demonstrations, measurements, product maturity, or independently checkable evidence in communication with employees and investors.
- [[GroupBoundaryLanguage]] gives newcomers a way to signal competence and belonging, but the anxiety of not knowing the code also creates informal hierarchy and pressures members toward standardized expression.
- Fandom abbreviations, youth slang, memes, classical allusion, medical shorthand, corporate vocabulary, and parenting-circle terms share a compression-and-boundary structure even though their purposes and stakes differ.
- Insider codes can protect vulnerable communities or private information, so opacity should not automatically be read as vanity, deception, or linguistic decline.
- Popularized academic terms such as “内卷” can name a shared pattern efficiently, but overextension may erase distinctions, turn description into fatalism, or dismiss individual effort without examining the specific competition.
- Language choice signals education, profession, generation, taste, and group membership while also carrying biases that speakers may not consciously endorse.

## Key Quotes
> “黑话本身并非天然有害” - the episode's practical boundary between useful specialist language and abuse.

> “使用、大量运用、滥用” - the proposed progression from functional terminology to empty repetition.

> “当术语替代数据和实物” - the source's core warning about product and investment communication.
