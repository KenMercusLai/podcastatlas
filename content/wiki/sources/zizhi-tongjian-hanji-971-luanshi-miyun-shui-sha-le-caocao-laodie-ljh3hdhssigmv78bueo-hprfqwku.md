# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》971丨乱世迷云：谁杀了曹操老爹

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 193 CE [[CaoSongMurder|曹嵩遇害]] before the already-ingested [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-972-sanguo-liuyu-zhisi-taisha-taitianzhen-lvawrcandbh628rckukosgarh7v2|Hanji 972]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-973-changpingguan-zhizhan-shengsi-zhidi-zhanhou-jing-chonggui-yuhao-lgazc9dewfayb8qnkncfkfa12yet|Hanji 973]] branches. It preserves two attribution traditions: [[Sanguozhi|《三国志》]] makes [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] the direct sender of cavalry against [[CaoSong|曹嵩]], while [[HouHanshu|《后汉书》]] makes [[ZhangKaiAssassin|张闿]] the immediate killer who turns escort access into robbery and murder.

The episode then follows [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s revenge against [[Xuzhou|徐州]] into the [[XuzhouMassacreLateHan|徐州屠杀]]: a private family catastrophe becomes public military violence against cities and displaced civilians. The closing Youzhou note sets up [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] and [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]]'s supply, plunder, and memorial-writing conflict as the immediate bridge to Hanji 972.

## Key Claims
- In 193 CE, [[CaoSong|曹嵩]] is moving from Langya toward [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] after [[CaoCao|曹操]] asks [[YingShaoLateHan|应劭]] to receive and escort the family.
- The killing happens in the Taishan/Hua County route field; the episode describes many dead family members and missing carts of wealth, making [[CaoSongMurder|曹嵩遇害]] both a killing and a convoy robbery.
- [[YingShaoLateHan|应劭]] abandons office and flees because he cannot answer to Cao Cao after the disaster.
- [[Sanguozhi|《三国志》]]'s version assigns agency to [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]], saying he resented Cao Cao's attack on Xuzhou and sent cavalry to kill Cao Song.
- [[HouHanshu|《后汉书》]]'s version assigns the immediate killing to [[ZhangKaiAssassin|张闿]], who is escorting Cao Song under Tao Qian's side and murders him for the convoy wealth.
- [[CaoDeLateHan|曹德]] dies with Cao Song; the seal-and-black-market detail is kept source-scoped as a dramatic claim in the episode rather than independent proof.
- [[CaoCao|曹操]] answers the death of his father and brother by attacking [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] in [[Xuzhou|徐州]], taking multiple cities and defeating Tao Qian near Pengcheng before Tao Qian retreats to Tan.
- The source stresses that Xuzhou is full of refugees after the Dong Zhuo/Luoyang disorder, so Cao Cao's revenge hits an already displaced civilian population.
- The [[XuzhouMassacreLateHan|徐州屠杀]] branch includes killings at the [[SiRiver|泗水]] and the later taking and slaughter of Qulu, Suiling, and Xiaqiu.
- The episode frames Cao Cao's violence as morally catastrophic while also placing it in the late-Han warlord survival environment: personal revenge, deterrence, resource seizure, and order collapse become entangled.
- The closing [[YouzhouLateHan|幽州]] note says [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] reduces supplies to [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] because he sees repeated war and plunder as destructive, while Gongsun Zan accuses Liu Yu of withholding military grain.
- That Youzhou conflict directly sets up Hanji 972's account of Liu Yu's failed attack and execution.

## Key Quotes
> "坑杀男女数十万口于泗水，水为不流" - the episode's cited Hou Hanshu wording for the Si River massacre.

> "白骨露于野，千里无鸡鸣" - the host's literary compression of Xuzhou's civilian devastation.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction found. The source records a genuine attribution split between the Sanguozhi and Hou Hanshu lines, so the wiki preserves Tao Qian's possible political authorship and Zhang Kai's possible direct robbery-killing rather than choosing one as final fact.
- Source-scope caution: the episode's vivid seal, black-market, and atrocity-scene details are retained as the host's narrative presentation; they should not be generalized beyond this source without corroborating pages.
