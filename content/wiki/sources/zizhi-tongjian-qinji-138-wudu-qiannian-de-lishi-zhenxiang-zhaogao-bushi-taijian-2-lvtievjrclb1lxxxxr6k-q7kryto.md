# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》138｜误读千年的历史真相：赵高不是太监（2）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the coup branch opened in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-138-wudu-qiannian-de-lishi-zhenxiang-zhaogao-bushi-taijian-1-ljw5nzd-ap1a4hkdczxun3t0-vwy|Qinji 138 part 1]] by narrating [[YanLe|阎乐]]'s attack on [[WangyiPalace|望夷宫]], [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]]'s suicide, and [[ZhaoGao|赵高]]'s attempt to replace imperial rule with a reduced Qin kingship. It then follows [[ZiyingQin|子婴]]'s countercoup against Zhao Gao and the final military failure at [[Yaoguan|峣关]] and [[LantianLateQin|蓝田]], where [[ZhangLiang|张良]] helps [[LiuBang|刘邦]] turn failed surrender talks into an attack opening. The episode also gives a source-scoped argument that Ziying should be read as [[FuSu|扶苏]]'s son.

## Key Claims
- Zhao Gao responds to Hu Hai's questioning by coordinating with Yan Le and [[ZhaoChengQin|赵成]] to depose the ruler and install Ziying as Qin king.
- The host reads "二世之兄子" and "二世兄子婴" as evidence that Ziying is the son of Hu Hai's elder brother Fusu, while acknowledging the identity discussion as source-driven.
- Yan Le uses a thief-catching story around his supposedly kidnapped mother to turn the armed attack on Wangyi Palace into [[PalaceCoupUnderPolicingPretext|治安名义政变]].
- Hu Hai's final requests to see Zhao Gao, become a commandery king, become a ten-thousand-household marquis, or live as a commoner all fail; the episode frames his suicide as the return of earlier killings of the Meng brothers and [[LiSi|李斯]].
- Zhao Gao's post-coup title logic creates [[ImperialTitleRetrenchment|帝号退回王号]]: Qin no longer controls the empire it once claimed, so he reduces the ruler from emperor to Qin king.
- Ziying treats the accession ritual as a possible trap, feigns illness, draws Zhao Gao into the fasting chamber, kills him, and destroys his three clans.
- Ziying removes Zhao Gao but cannot restore Qin's strategic position; Liu Bang's army is already pressing the Yaoguan-Lantian line.
- Zhang Liang first advises deception and persuasion through [[LiShiqi|郦食其]] and [[LuJia|陆贾]], then rejects premature peace because generals might defect without their soldiers following.
- Liu Bang's bypass of Yaoguan and victories south and north of Lantian mark the collapse of Qin's last defensive space before the chronicle turns from Qinji to Hanji.

## Key Quotes

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is recorded. This source sharpens the earlier "嬴婴" caution by giving the host's [[Shiji|《史记》]]-based reading that Ziying is Fusu's son; the wiki keeps that as this source's position rather than overwriting every prior source-scoped uncertainty.
- [[LantianLateQin|蓝田 / Lantian (Late Qin)]] is disambiguated from the earlier [[LantianBattle|蓝田之战]] because this episode's Lantian fighting belongs to Liu Bang's Qin-collapse campaign, not the Warring States Chu-Qin campaign.
