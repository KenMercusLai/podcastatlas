# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》528丨才女班婕妤：我为丈夫纳新欢！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the positive-reputation layer of [[BanJieyuWesternHan|班婕妤]] before the later Zhao-sister rivalry and wugu-accusation episodes. It centers her refusal to ride in [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]'s carriage, [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]'s comparison of her to Fan Ji, and her recommendation of attendant Li Ping / later Wei Jieyu to Chengdi.

The durable synthesis is that Ban Jieyu's palace survival is not only defensive. Before she becomes a falsely accused rival, she is presented as a favored consort who converts proximity into restraint, counsel, and relationship management: she protects Chengdi's public image through ritual refusal, makes herself valuable beyond beauty, and turns non-jealous recommendation into a form of [[PalaceFavorSelfRestraint|宫廷宠幸自持]]. The host extends the lesson into workplace judgment about rule awareness, bounded competition, platform-level interest, and multi-party benefit.

## Key Claims

- Ban Jieyu refuses Chengdi's invitation to share his carriage by invoking images of sage kings with ministers and fallen kings with favored women.
- Wang Zhengjun's praise, "ancient Fan Ji, present Ban Jieyu," is treated as a high evaluation and an implied standard-setting message for the palace.
- The episode reads Ban Jieyu's recommendation of Li Ping as strategic non-jealousy: she avoids being defined only by beauty and creates broader value for the ruler.
- Fan Ji and Ban Jieyu are paired as women who influence rulers by counsel, restraint, and positive value rather than by monopolizing affection.
- The host translates the palace story into a workplace model: good subordinates know rules, keep proportion under favor, and prevent rivalry from damaging the whole platform.
- Competition and struggle are not denied, but they should remain bounded by shared institutional interest and possible multi-win outcomes.
- The episode is a short interpretive case, not a full biography of Ban Jieyu, Fan Ji, Li Ping, or Chengdi's harem.

## Key Quotes

> "古有樊姬，今有班婕妤" - Wang Zhengjun's praise as relayed by the episode.

> "不因宠而骄，不因重而狂，不因近而飘" - the host's workplace translation of the episode's favored-subordinate standard.
