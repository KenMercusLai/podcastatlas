# 010 一见杨过误终身|闲话金庸《神雕侠侣》里的爱情

## Summary

This [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] episode reads [[ReturnOfTheCondorHeroes|《神雕侠侣》]] through [[YangGuoJinYong|杨过]]'s unequal relationships with [[LuWushuangJinYong|陆无双]], [[ChengYingJinYong|程英]], [[GongsunLueJinYong|公孙绿萼]], and [[GuoFuJinYong|郭芙]]. It distinguishes playful attraction, restrained devotion, sacrificial love, and antagonistic attachment while arguing that rescue, flirtation, gratitude, social recognition, and lasting concern do not automatically establish equal desire or a viable partnership.

The discussion also reads the divided handkerchief, 玉女心经, 情花, 断肠草, and 绝情丹 as relationship devices. Its strongest qualification is that many conclusions remain interpretive: the hosts disagree about whether Yang Guo loved Lu Wushuang or Guo Fu, whether love is exclusive, and how much character names or objects encode authorial intent.

## Key Claims

- [[LuWushuangJinYong|陆无双]] and Yang Guo share unusually easy banter, danger, bodily proximity, and an unrealized plan to travel together, but his attraction never displaces [[XiaolongnuJinYong|小龙女]].
- [[ChengYingJinYong|程英]] expresses deep feeling through concealment, writing, protection, and attempted sacrifice; restraint makes her love less visible without making it less intense.
- [[GongsunLueJinYong|公孙绿萼]] receives little familial care, interprets brief praise and rescue as precious attachment, and finally gives her life and the 绝情丹 without gaining reciprocity.
- [[GuoFuJinYong|郭芙]] repeatedly moves between pleasure at seeing Yang Guo, pride, hostility, concern, and commitment to 耶律齐; the hosts treat her feeling as real but neither exclusive nor clearly primary.
- Yang Guo's charm combines beauty, teasing language, rescue, and responsiveness to kindness, creating meaningful bonds whose emotional weight is not equal on both sides.
- His restraint toward Guo Fu after grave harms may indicate attachment, but the stronger interpretation links her approval to the social recognition denied to him as a poor and stigmatized child.
- 玉女心经, 情花, 断肠草, and 绝情丹 externalize cooperation, sweetness, pain, unpredictability, healing, and the unequal valuation of sacrifice.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with the wiki is adopted. The episode strengthens the distinction between attachment and compatibility while leaving the direction and degree of several feelings disputed.
- The hosts disagree over whether Yang Guo genuinely loved Lu Wushuang, whether he loved Guo Fu more than she loved him, and whether Guo Fu's feelings for Yang Guo and 耶律齐 can coexist without one being false.
- The reading of the discarded 绝情丹 as devaluation of Gongsun Lü'e's life is emotionally powerful but competes with Yang Guo's decision to share Xiaolongnü's fate.
