# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1125丨兵不血刃捉刘璋，刘备为何不愿意？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[LiuBei|刘备]] into [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] after [[FaZheng|法正]], [[ZhangSongLateHan|张松]], and [[PangTong|庞统]] advocate taking the province from [[LiuZhang|刘璋]]. Liu Bei rejects the proposal to seize Liu Zhang during their meeting at Fu because he has not yet established local trust and because Liu Zhang arrives with a substantial armed escort.

The episode interprets the delay as a joint problem of force, reputation, and timing rather than as refusal of the strategic goal. Liu Zhang supplies troops, provisions, and command access for a northern campaign, while Liu Bei pauses at Jiameng and distributes favors instead of immediately attacking Zhang Lu. The resulting sequence lets an invited ally accumulate local support and practical capacity before the open rupture narrated in Hanji 1126.

## Key Claims

- Fa Zheng argues that Liu Zhang is weak and Zhang Song can provide internal assistance, while Pang Tong presents populous and extensive Yizhou as the territorial base Liu Bei lacks in Jingzhou.
- Liu Bei says his political distinction from Cao Cao rests on generosity, loyalty, and humane conduct; Pang Tong answers that disorder may require irregular means followed by a compensating settlement for Liu Zhang.
- Liu Bei accepts entry into Yizhou but leaves Zhuge Liang and Guan Yu to hold Jingzhou, showing acceptance of the territorial objective without acceptance of every proposed method.
- After Liu Bei moves west, Sun Quan sends for [[LadySunLiuBei|Lady Sun]], who tries to take [[LiuChan|刘禅]] with her; [[ZhangFei|张飞]] and [[ZhaoYun|赵云]] intercept the boats and recover him.
- [[YanYanLateHan|严颜]] warns that Liu Zhang is inviting a dangerous protector into his own territory, while Liu Zhang nevertheless receives Liu Bei generously and meets him with more than thirty thousand troops according to the episode.
- Zhang Song and Pang Tong recommend capturing Liu Zhang at the meeting, but Liu Bei refuses because he is newly arrived, lacks established恩信, and faces nontrivial military risk.
- Liu Zhang and Liu Bei exchange recommendations for high office, their forces mix for more than one hundred days, and Liu Zhang gives Liu Bei additional troops, supplies, and command over forces at Baishui.
- Liu Bei reaches Jiameng but does not immediately attack Zhang Lu; the episode says he instead distributes favor and cultivates local support.
- The episode treats Liu Bei's benevolent reputation as a real political asset that constrains how quickly he can seize power, but it does not independently establish his private sincerity or prove that every act was planned deception.

## Key Quotes

> “今指挥得益州，反掌之易。” - Fa Zheng's reported confidence in the opportunity created by Liu Zhang's weakness and Zhang Song's internal support.

> “独坐穷山，放虎自卫。” - Yan Yan's warning about inviting Liu Bei into Yizhou.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. The episode supplies the missing middle stage between Hanji 1123's transfer plan and Hanji 1126's exposed letter and open war.
- Motive caution: the claim that Liu Bei's hesitation was mainly strategic image management is the host's inference; concern about force, uncertainty, coalition cohesion, and genuine moral restraint are not separable from this source alone.
- Chronology and quantity caution: troop totals, gift values, the length of the joint encampment, offices, exact command transfers, and the sequence at Jiameng require comparison with the underlying historical texts.
- Agency caution: Lady Sun's intention in taking Liu Chan, Sun Quan's orders, the interception details, and the episode's suggestion that Sun Quan was already preparing to recover Jingzhou remain source-scoped.
