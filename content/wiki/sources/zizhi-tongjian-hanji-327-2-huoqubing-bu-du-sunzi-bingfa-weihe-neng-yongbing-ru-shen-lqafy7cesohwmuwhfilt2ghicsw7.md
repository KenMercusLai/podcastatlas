# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》327-2｜霍去病不读《孙子兵法》，为何能用兵如神？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode first offers a modern reflection on value, weakness, and status reversal through [[SimaQian|司马迁]], [[GongsunHong|公孙弘]], and [[SuQin|苏秦]]. Its historical center is [[HuoQubing|霍去病]]: [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] reportedly offers to teach him the Sun-Wu military texts and to build him a residence, while Huo answers that war depends on strategic judgment rather than literal constraint by ancient doctrine and that a home is premature while [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] remains undefeated.

The episode uses the 121 BCE Hexi campaigns to extend [[MilitaryDoctrineEraFit|兵法的时代适配]]. It contrasts the long-distance pursuit warning attributed to [[SunziBingfa|《孙子兵法》]] with Huo's source-reported high-tempo cavalry movement, arguing that some infantry-and-chariot-era prescriptions could not be transferred mechanically into Western Han mounted operations. The durable conclusion is narrower than “Sunzi became obsolete”: inherited principles still matter, but their direct application depends on force structure, logistics, opponent, and operational range.

## Key Claims

- The opening argues that social treatment often follows visible value and strength; its advice about withdrawal during low periods and generosity during strong periods is the host's modern life framing, not a historical finding.
- Huo Qubing is characterized as calm, concise, decisive, and quick to react.
- Han Wudi reportedly offers to teach Huo the Sun-Wu military texts; Huo answers that warfare lies in strategic judgment and should not be constrained by old doctrine.
- When Wudi offers to build him a residence, Huo reportedly answers that he has no need of a home while Xiongnu remains undefeated, increasing imperial favor.
- The episode treats Sunzi's warning about long-distance pursuit for advantage as guidance formed around infantry and chariot coordination rather than a timeless speed limit for every force.
- For the 121 BCE spring Hexi campaign, the episode reports that Huo crossed five Xiongnu polities or tribal units in six days, covered more than one thousand li, and killed more than nine thousand cavalry while averaging more than two hundred li per day.
- For the summer campaign, it reports that Huo led thirty thousand cavalry across two thousand li and struck several Xiongnu kings, substantially breaking Xiongnu power in the northwest.
- The host concludes that Huo's results justified rejecting literal transfer of some older rules, while also acknowledging that parts of Sunzi remained valid.

## Key Quotes

> "顾方略何如耳，不至学古兵法。" - the answer attributed to Huo Qubing when Han Wudi offers to teach him the Sun-Wu military texts.

> "匈奴未灭，无以家为也。" - the residence answer used to explain Han Wudi's increased favor.

- “孙武兵法已经落后于时代” is the host's broad rhetorical conclusion. The same episode concedes that some Sunzi principles remain valid, so the wiki adopts conditional non-transferability rather than wholesale obsolescence.
