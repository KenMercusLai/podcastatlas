## Summary
This [[ShengdongZaokafei|声动早咖啡]] business-news roundup links weak or uneven domestic demand with companies looking for overseas, advertising, health, IP, and operational-efficiency growth. It covers Chinese airlines' fuel and international-route pressure, [[BYD]]'s overseas sales buffer, China-bound oral GLP-1 competition, [[Kailas|凯乐石]] and [[Fuga]] entering Europe, [[MideaGroup|Midea]]'s overseas appliance growth, [[PayPal]]'s failed buyer talks, [[Bilibili|B站]] advertising becoming its largest revenue segment, [[Miniso|名创优品]]'s large IP-heavy stores, and [[Mengniu|蒙牛]] plus dairy-category recovery. The durable synthesis is that mature or pressured categories are shifting from volume expansion toward localization, monetization quality, experiential retail, and category-specific demand proof.

## Key Claims
- Chinese airlines remain under earnings pressure because fuel costs, incomplete international-route recovery, and weak ticket pricing offset summer travel volume; [[AirChina]], [[ChinaEasternAirlines]], and [[ChinaSouthernAirlines]] are named as the three major carriers with combined first-half losses above RMB 8 billion.
- [[BYD]] ended a profit-decline streak because global and premium strategy buffered a weak domestic auto market: second-quarter domestic sales fell while overseas sales nearly doubled, and a Hungarian plant plus new models are presented as near-term overseas levers.
- [[NovoNordisk]] and [[EliLilly]] are competing to bring oral GLP-1 weight-loss drugs into China, with the episode framing pills as a way to reduce injection fear, cold-chain friction, and user adoption barriers inside [[GLP1Agonists]].
- [[Kailas|凯乐石]] is using [[Fuga]] and trail running to enter Europe, opening its first directly operated store outside China in Chamonix and relying on race sponsorship plus professional athlete endorsements against incumbents such as [[Salomon]].
- [[MideaGroup|Midea]] and Haier are used to show that overseas appliance revenue can partially offset domestic home-appliance demand pressure when subsidy effects fade and regional markets diverge.
- [[PayPal]]'s reported potential buyers abandoned talks because valuation and regulatory risk remained hard, leaving its next opportunity source-scoped around AI-commerce payment embedding rather than acquisition certainty.
- [[Bilibili|B站]]'s advertising revenue surpassed value-added services for the first time, with young consumer demographics, creator trust, AI-learning content, and doubled AI-company ad spending making it a case for [[PlatformAdvertisingMonetization]] as well as [[CreatorTrustAdvertising]].
- [[Miniso|名创优品]] is betting on larger IP-heavy "land" stores where exclusives, limited products, experience, and check-in behavior support [[InterestConsumptionRetail]] even as overseas store expansion slows for operating-quality reasons.
- [[Mengniu|蒙牛]] and dairy peers show early recovery signs after raw-milk oversupply and price competition, with low-temperature fresh milk, cheese, functional nutrition products, and ice cream becoming growth points in [[DairyCategoryPremiumization]].

## Key Quotes
> "广告收入首次超过直播、大会员等增值服务" - the Bilibili segment's revenue-mix milestone.

> "兴趣消费和提供情绪价值的商品仍在高速增长" - the Miniso segment's reason for emphasizing IP-heavy large stores.

> "参与培育当地市场并推广这项运动" - the Kailas founder's source-reported frame for entering Europe's still-small trail-running market.
