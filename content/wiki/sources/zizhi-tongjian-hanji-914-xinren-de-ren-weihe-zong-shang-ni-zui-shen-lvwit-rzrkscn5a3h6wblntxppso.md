# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》914丨信任的人，为何总伤你最深？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 178-179 CE late-Han layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-915-shishang-meiyou-zhenzheng-de-kouwu-zhehua-dui-ma-lgxcodjxnaozvyegxqurgzji5gus|Hanji 915]]. It opens with a source-scoped trust-and-injury frame, then follows [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] using [[HongduMenxue|鸿都门学]] against the士人 field, [[YangQiuLateHan|阳球]]'s ignored memorial, and the Xidi sale-of-office system. The close adds [[YangQiLateHan|杨琦]]'s unsatisfying comparison of Emperor Ling with Emperor Huan, [[YangZhenLateHan|杨震]] family reputation, and the [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] succession notice in which [[HuzhengChanyu|呼征]] becomes chanyu.

## Key Claims
- The opening argues that people who hurt others have often already weighed benefit and value; this is filed as source-scoped relationship advice, not a settled psychological rule.
- The episode then reverses the frame inward: low cognition, external seeking, self-justification, and emotional depletion can make a person continue harming themselves.
- In 178 CE, [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] has Hongdu literary figures painted and praised, treating [[HongduMenxue|鸿都门学]] as more than ordinary cultural patronage.
- The host reads Emperor Ling's support for Hongdu Menxue as a political counterweight to the士人 world he resents after the [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] and [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] anti-eunuch crisis.
- [[YangQiuLateHan|阳球]] memorializes against Hongdu Menxue, accusing its favored figures of shallow learning, elite attachment, delegated writing, and advancement through favor rather than public competence.
- Emperor Ling ignores Yang Qiu's memorial, making the scene an early [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|君臣反馈失灵]] case before the Hanji 915-918 anti-eunuch sequence.
- The Xidi office-sale system prices offices by rank and revenue, discounts some normally selected candidates, and even allows later payment, turning public appointment into a debt-recovery problem.
- Money from office sale is placed in the West Garden private store, linking [[OfficeSaleLegitimacyDamage|买官名节损耗]] to [[ImperialPrivateTreasuryExtraction|帝王私库进奉盘剥]] before the later 181 CE private-tribute branch.
- [[YangQiLateHan|杨琦]] answers Emperor Ling's comparison with Emperor Huan in a way that does not satisfy the ruler's desire for validation, prompting Ling to invoke Yang Zhen family stubbornness.
- The [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] notice that [[HuzhengChanyu|呼征]] succeeds his father gives the baseline before Hanji 917 later narrates Zhang Xiu's unauthorized killing of Huzheng.
