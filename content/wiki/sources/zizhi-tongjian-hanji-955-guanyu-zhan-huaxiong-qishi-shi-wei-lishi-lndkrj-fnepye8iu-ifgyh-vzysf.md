# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》955丨关羽斩华雄其实是伪历史？？？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 191 CE anti-[[DongZhuo|董卓]] field immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-956-yongren-he-zhizhe-jiujing-chaju-zai-nali-lphee8cj-7gcmg-cynpiphjvauuf|Hanji 956]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-958-jiemi-shishang-zui-duanming-de-tongchoudikai-lve41q9gyijbop8wk-b7e6shogju|Hanji 958]]. It centers [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] as the active front-line commander: after surviving [[XuRongLateHan|徐荣]]'s attack, he defeats [[HuZhenLateHan|胡轸]] at [[YangrenBattleLateHan|阳人之战]], where [[HuaXiongLateHan|华雄]] is killed. The episode's historiographical hook is that the famous [[GuanYu|关羽]] "warm wine and Hua Xiong" scene belongs to [[RomanceOfTheThreeKingdoms|《三国演义》]] memory, while the source's historical frame credits Sun Jian; it then extends Yuan Shu's grain cutoff, Dong Zhuo's failed offer through [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]], Sun Jian's entry into [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]], the discovery of the [[HeirloomImperialSeal|传国玉玺]], and the setup for [[HuangfuSongLateHan|皇甫嵩]]'s later defensive reply.

## Key Claims

- The opening "pipe rather than container" reflection frames inner steadiness as letting negative information pass through without storing it as resentment or disorder.
- On 191-02-12, Dong Zhuo is appointed tai shi, and the episode treats that title as a visible escalation of his control over the court.
- The [[AntiDongZhuoCoalition|反董卓联盟]] has loud shared-enemy language but few determined attackers; [[CaoCao|曹操]] has limited force and is soon checked, while Sun Jian becomes the main active pressure against Dong Zhuo.
- [[XuRongLateHan|徐荣]] defeats Sun Jian near Liang, but [[ZuMaoLateHan|祖茂]] diverts pursuit by taking Sun Jian's headgear, allowing Sun Jian to escape, regroup, and move to Yangren.
- [[LyuBu|吕布]] undermines [[HuZhenLateHan|胡轸]] because Bingzhou and Liangzhou troops are already factionally tense and because Lü Bu resents being subordinated to Hu Zhen.
- At [[YangrenBattleLateHan|阳人之战]], Lü Bu's false reports create disorder, Sun Jian exploits the opening, Hu Zhen's force is defeated, and [[HuaXiongLateHan|华雄]] is killed.
- The episode explicitly rejects the [[RomanceOfTheThreeKingdoms|《三国演义》]] version in which [[GuanYu|关羽]] kills Hua Xiong, making the scene a case where novelized historical memory needs separation from chronicle-source claims.
- [[YuanShu|袁术]] cuts Sun Jian's grain after hearing that Sun Jian may become uncontrollable if he takes Luoyang, showing allied sabotage inside the anti-Dong-Zhuo cause.
- Sun Jian restores the grain channel by reframing his campaign as both service to the state and revenge for the Yuan family's private injury under Dong Zhuo, using [[WuQi|吴起]] and [[LeYi|乐毅]] as warning analogies.
- Dong Zhuo sends [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] to offer marriage alliance and appointment discretion, but Sun Jian refuses, keeping his anti-Dong-Zhuo commitment intact.
- Sun Jian later advances to Dagu, defeats Dong Zhuo, enters Luoyang, defeats Lü Bu again, cleans the imperial ancestral temples, performs sacrifices, and finds the [[HeirloomImperialSeal|传国玉玺]] in a palace well.
- The closing Chang'an scene sets up the later [[HuangfuSongLateHan|皇甫嵩]] branch: Dong Zhuo asks whether Huangfu Song fears him, leaving the old grievance and reply for the next episode.

## Contradictions

- No settled wiki contradiction is recorded. The episode contradicts the novelized/popular "Guan Yu kills Hua Xiong" memory, but the wiki treats that as a source-tradition boundary rather than as a conflict between two historical source pages.
- Source-scope caution: the source says "正史" credits Sun Jian for Hua Xiong's death, but this transcript segment does not quote the relevant classical passage, so the claim is recorded as the episode's historical-source framing.
- Source-scope caution: the host's evaluations of Yuan Shu's personality, Sun Jian's workplace-style communication, and the opening self-cultivation lesson are interpretive framing rather than independent evidence of inner motive.
