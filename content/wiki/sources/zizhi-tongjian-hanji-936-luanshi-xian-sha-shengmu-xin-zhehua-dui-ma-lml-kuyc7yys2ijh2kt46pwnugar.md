# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》936丨乱世先杀圣母心，这话对吗？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 185-186 CE late-Han layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-937-zhanshen-zhisi-haoren-zongshi-duozai-duonan-lp7przsmag9ookq7eoisvsmnm5aw|Hanji 937]]. It follows [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s surrounded western campaign and dam-covered retreat, [[ZhangWenLateHan|张温]]'s failed authority over him, [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]]'s advice to execute Dong Zhuo under military law, and the court's parallel drift into [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s hoarding, palace construction, eunuch patronage, and punitive politics.

## Key Claims

- Dong Zhuo extricates his roughly 30,000 troops from encirclement by repairing a dam as if to fish, withdrawing behind the cover of the waterwork, and then breaking the dam so pursuers cannot immediately follow.
- [[ZhangWenLateHan|张温]] summons Dong Zhuo by imperial order, but Dong Zhuo arrives late and arrogant, making the problem one of public command authority rather than only battlefield success or failure.
- [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] privately urges Zhang Wen to kill Dong Zhuo under military law, citing disrespect to the commander, failure to attack Bian Zhang and [[HanSui|韩遂]], and delayed response after returning without decisive merit.
- The episode does not reduce Zhang Wen to stupidity or simple cowardice: Dong Zhuo has western military prestige and lacks a single uncontestable capital offense, so Zhang Wen's restraint is procedurally understandable.
- The host's "圣母心" frame is therefore best captured as [[CommandDisciplineAuthority|军法威严]]: a powerful subordinate's visible contempt can weaken command discipline even when legal caution has reasons.
- [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] stores money and silk in West Garden and eunuch households, buys land and builds in his old fief, and orders new halls and palace devices while rebellions and frontier incursions continue.
- [[ZhaoZhongLateHan|赵忠]] uses Yellow Turban merit administration as a patronage channel by sending his brother Zhao Yan to solicit [[FuXie|傅燮]], but Fu Xie refuses the private favor route before being transferred to [[HanyangCommanderyLateHan|汉阳郡]].
- [[ZhaoCiLateHan|赵慈]]'s Jiangxia revolt kills Nanyang administrator Qin Jie, while [[Jingzhou|荆州]] inspector Wang Min later suppresses Zhao Ci; the court responds in part through amnesty and personnel churn.
- [[ZhangYanTaiweiLateHan|张延]] is removed, later appointed taiwei from outside the capital, repeatedly criticizes eunuchs, and dies in prison after false accusation, making him a separate official from [[ZhangYanLateHan|张燕]] of the Black Mountain field.
- In late 186, [[Xianbei|鲜卑]] incursions into [[YouzhouLateHan|幽州]] and [[BingzhouLateHan|并州]] prompt Zhang Wen's recall, adding another frontier pressure to the same court-failure layer.

## Key Quotes

> "士卒可下而不可骄" - the episode's formulation of how a commander can be close to soldiers without indulging arrogance.

> "为我者不为敌，为敌者不为我" - the Li Jing phrase the host uses to frame decisive enemy-versus-own-side distinction.

> "万金堂" - the named West Garden hoarding site that becomes a symbol of Emperor Ling's private treasury behavior.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with existing wiki pages was found. This episode backfills the pre-Hanji 937 layer by placing Dong Zhuo's command-insubordination problem before his later Luoyang seizure and before Zhang Wen's later death at Dong Zhuo's hands.
- Naming caution: [[ZhangYanTaiweiLateHan|张延 / Zhang Yan (late-Han taiwei)]] is kept distinct from [[ZhangYanLateHan|张燕 / Zhang Yan (Late Han)]], the Black Mountain leader, because they share pinyin but not identity.
- Source-scope caution: the title's "圣母心" claim is preserved as a command-authority warning, not generalized into a blanket rule that commanders should kill without legal ground.
