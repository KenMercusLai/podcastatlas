# Introducing Revisionist History Season Six

## Summary

This short [[RevisionistHistory|Revisionist History]] trailer has [[MalcolmGladwell]] present season six as a more mobile, confrontational, and unpredictable installment after a period confined at home. A rapid montage previews an Arizona pursuit, a challenge involving [[TheWaltDisneyCompany|Disney]], cycling with Lance Armstrong, communist history, three underwater episodes, and a retelling intended to appeal to nine-year-old girls.

The preview establishes tone and range rather than findings. Its fragments do not identify the arguments, evidence, or conclusions of the later stories, so they should be treated as promotional framing rather than substantive support for claims about Disney, Armstrong, communism, or the announced retelling.

## Key Claims

- Gladwell frames season six as less restrained and more outward-looking than earlier seasons, emphasizing renewed travel, confrontation, curiosity, and adventure.
- The opening preview follows Gladwell by plane to Arizona and into a pursuit near an Alamo Drafthouse outside Phoenix.
- A Jimmy Kimmel exchange presents Gladwell's Arizona trip as part of a challenge to Disney and the “Magic Kingdom.”
- The montage also previews Lance Armstrong discussing repeated car collisions, a story involving committed communists, and three episodes set under the sea.
- A fragment about building support among nine-year-old girls announces a retelling without identifying its subject or connection to the underwater stories.
- The ten-episode season was announced to begin June 24 as a [[PushkinIndustries]] production, with an interruption-free subscription option on Apple Podcasts.
