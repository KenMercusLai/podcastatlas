# How to Defeat Jet Lag, Shift Work & Sleeplessness

## Summary
This early solo [[HubermanLab]] episode has [[AndrewHuberman]] explain circadian timing through light, body temperature, activity, food, and internal biological time, then apply that model to travel, shift work, infancy, adolescence, aging, sleep anxiety, and supplements. Its durable contribution joins [[TemperatureMinimumClockShifting]], [[CircadianTravelAdaptation]], and [[ShiftWorkCircadianHealth]]: the same cue can advance or delay the clock depending on when it occurs, so destination clock labels and generic “morning light” advice are insufficient without phase context.

The episode also supplies early provenance for [[SleepArchitectureAcrossLifespan]], [[NonSleepDeepRestRecovery]], [[SleepAnxietyLoop]], and [[SleepSupplementBoundary]]. Its claim that adults can reduce total sleep through repeated 45-minute bouts conflicts with the later [[AdultPolyphasicSleepEvidenceBoundary]] and is retained as source-scoped counterevidence rather than current guidance.

## Key Claims
- [[TemperatureMinimumClockShifting]] estimates the daily low point from recent wake times and uses cues before or after it to reason about phase delay or phase advance.
- [[CircadianTravelAdaptation]] distinguishes travel fatigue from time-zone mismatch, treats eastward travel as often harder than westward travel, and coordinates pre-travel shifting, local meals, light, movement, and avoidance of mistimed cues.
- [[ShiftWorkCircadianHealth]] is presented as a schedule-stability problem: when possible, keep one shift for roughly two weeks, use light during the intended active period, and reduce light before intended sleep.
- [[SleepArchitectureAcrossLifespan]] moves from fragmented infant sleep toward adolescent phase delay and then toward earlier, often more fragile sleep in older adulthood.
- [[SleepAnxietyLoop]] can worsen when people become rigid or fearful about protocols; NSDR, yoga nidra, hypnosis, or meditation are presented as possible de-arousal tools rather than substitutes for sleep or clinical care.
- [[SleepSupplementBoundary]] places behavior before magnesium, theanine, apigenin, melatonin, 5-HTP, tryptophan, or tyrosine and keeps hormone, dream, dose, label, and next-day-effect claims source-scoped.
- The episode suggests that adults may reduce total sleep need by sleeping in repeated 45-minute blocks, but later sources represented in [[AdultPolyphasicSleepEvidenceBoundary]] reject extreme sleep-compression schedules as unsupported and potentially harmful.
