# 《资治通鉴·周纪》05｜光彩夺目的魏文侯（2）

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode joins two post-conquest problems under [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]]: how a ruler receives unwelcome criticism and how he judges a commander whose loyalty has become frighteningly extreme. [[RenZuoWarringStates|任作]] directly challenges the proposed assignment of [[ZhongshanState|中山国]] to the ruler's son, while [[DiHuang|翟黄]] gives Wei Wenhou a face-saving reason to recall the critic. The second half follows [[LeYang|乐羊]] through his son's killing and the meat-broth episode, arguing that sacrifice can prove task commitment while simultaneously destroying the trust it was meant to secure.

## Key Claims

- After Le Yang conquers Zhongshan, Wei Wenhou proposes assigning the detached and unsettled territory to his son, whom the transcript calls “魏姬.”
- Ren Zuo says taking Zhongshan without assigning it to a brother rather than a son does not look like the conduct of a humane ruler; Wei Wenhou becomes angry and Ren Zuo leaves.
- Di Huang answers that Wei Wenhou must be a humane ruler because only such a ruler could have a minister as direct as Ren Zuo, giving the ruler a dignified path back toward accepting criticism.
- Wei Wenhou sends Di Huang to recall Ren Zuo and personally descends the steps to welcome him.
- The host interprets the son's Zhongshan assignment as frontier-risk management: the newly conquered territory is unstable and geographically detached, so the ruler wants a close family member to hold it.
- Zhongshan captures Le Yang's son and tries to force the Wei commander to retreat; Le Yang treats command duty as public obligation that cannot be displaced by private paternal feeling.
- After Zhongshan kills the son and sends broth made from his flesh, Le Yang consumes it and continues the three-year campaign until Zhongshan surrenders.
- Wei Wenhou rewards Le Yang's military achievement but, after an adviser warns that a man able to consume his own son's flesh may spare nobody, no longer trusts him.
- The episode translates the case into a modern organizational question: how far should a professional subordinate display devotion when unlimited sacrifice may make the subordinate look morally dangerous rather than dependable?

## Key Quotes

> “有这样的直臣，才会有人君。” - Di Huang's face-saving inference as rendered by the episode.

> “赏其功，而疑其心。” - the episode's compact summary of Wei Wenhou's response to Le Yang.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. The transcript's “魏姬” is treated as a noisy form within the existing [[WeiWuhou|魏击 / 后来的魏武侯]] branch rather than as a new person; the later replacement by a brother remains source-scoped.
- The host's strategic explanation for appointing a son to Zhongshan is plausible within the episode but does not erase Ren Zuo's fairness critique or independently establish Wei Wenhou's motive.
- “为君吃子” is rhetorical compression: in the episode Le Yang does not kill or cook his son himself, but consumes broth sent after Zhongshan kills the captive.
- The dialogue, geography, three-year duration, the son's identity, the meat-broth episode, the adviser's warning, and Wei Wenhou's lasting distrust remain episode-attributed pending fuller textual comparison.
