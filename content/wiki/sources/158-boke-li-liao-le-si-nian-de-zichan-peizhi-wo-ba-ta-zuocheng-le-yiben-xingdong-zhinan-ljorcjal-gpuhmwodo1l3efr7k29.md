# 158. 播客里聊了四年的资产配置，我把它做成了一本《行动指南》

## Summary
This [[QizhulouYanBinke|起朱楼宴宾客]] episode by [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] explains how the 2024 "迈出资产配置第一步" podcast series became [[AssetAllocationActionGuide|《资产配置行动指南》]]. The source frames the book as [[PodcastToBookSystematization]] rather than transcript cleanup: it keeps the podcast series' chapter skeleton but adds rewriting, examples, editorial work, reading/listening guides, tools, and checklists. Its investing claim is that ordinary Chinese households face a post-real-estate and post-rigid-redemption environment where [[AssetAllocation]] has to start from [[InvestmentWorldviewFit]], life goals, and action readiness rather than "wealth code" chasing.

## Key Claims
- The book grew from the 2024 asset-allocation podcast series, including [[vol-110-touzi-jiushi-dui-shijieguan-de-toupiao-maichu-zichan-peizhi-di-yi-bu-wanjiepian-lk5jkgnzvzerymcixylsdqdtn7f8]], but the source insists it is not a simple audio-to-text product.
- A good editor and publisher process matter because the project had to pass topic selection, rewriting, review, copyediting, and title positioning before becoming a book through [[CITICPress|中信出版社]].
- [[AssetAllocation]] is presented as increasingly unavoidable for ordinary Chinese households because property and implicit-guarantee products can no longer carry the same wealth expectations.
- The source positions the book as an entry to concepts and worldview, not as an operating manual for one asset class or a promise of [[PortfolioSuitability|suitable]] model portfolios.
- The book's three-part structure, extension reading/listening guides, cards, tools, tables, and checklists are meant to turn investment education into action rather than only understanding.
- The source's final investment claim is that a durable approach has to match the investor's own worldview, risk sense, and life stage through [[InvestmentWorldviewFit]].

## Key Quotes
> "让人看懂，愿意行动" — the desired reader effect.

> "不是一张新地图" — the book's map-making metaphor.

> "与自己的世界观相契合，才是最稳的投资姿势" — the closing investment-fit thesis.
