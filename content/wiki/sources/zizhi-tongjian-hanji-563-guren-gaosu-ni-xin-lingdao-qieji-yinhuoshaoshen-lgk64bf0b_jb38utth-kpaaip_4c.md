# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》563丨古人告诉你，新领导切忌引火烧身！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Suihe 2 / 7 BCE under [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]], between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-562-han-aidi-jiwei-shi-you-duo-biequ-lpv8_ks5b3c0ow_3o9_mvxi9n3xu|Hanji 562]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-564-gudai-xueshu-dalao-bi-xiangxiang-de-gengniu-ljf28ze9-p218vuzjicjcwutvrhl|Hanji 564]]. It uses [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]'s attempt to cool Wang-Fu outer-relative tension, Aidi's public retention of [[WangMang|王莽]], and Aidi's abolition of Yuefu offices as a case of [[NewLeaderFirstMoveAuthoritySignal|新领导第一把火式权力宣示]].

The durable synthesis is that a new ruler's first visible action can establish authority only when the target is carefully chosen. Aidi's music-office cut has classical justification through [[Confucius|孔子]], fiscal savings, and low open-resistance risk, while the episode's modern Jiangyong county example warns that visible projects pursued for political face can become [[FaceProjectGovernance|面子工程式治理]] when they ignore public cost and organizational complexity.

## Key Claims

- Wang Zhengjun tries to reduce early Aidi-era factional heat by having Wang Mang withdraw from office, but Aidi publicly asks him to return, showing that formal accession still requires stabilizing gestures toward existing power centers.
- The episode treats "what to burn first" as more important than the mere fact of acting: the first reform target decides whether a new leader's authority signal is stabilizing or self-damaging.
- Han Chengdi-era music excess gives Aidi a useful target because it is associated with indulgence, court fashion, and fiscal waste rather than with a protected core institution.
- Aidi abolishes Yuefu offices by invoking Confucius's rejection of Zheng music, while preserving sacrificial and military music under other offices.
- The policy creates real labor displacement because more than half the court musicians lose official employment, and administrative command cannot immediately change entrenched elite and popular tastes.
- A visible reform works better when it combines normative justification, manageable opposition, and tangible effect; it becomes dangerous when it is driven by face, short-term achievement, or personal political capital.
- Large organizations require cautious power use because policy surfaces are connected: one visible intervention can create wider costs than the leader's original signal intends.

## Key Quotes

> "新官上任三把火究竟该怎么烧" - the episode's practical leadership question.

> "切入角度比动作本身更重要" - the host's core management lesson.

> "治大国如烹小鲜" - Laozi line used to stress caution in governing complex organizations.
