# EP279 当方向盘慢慢松开，我们如何与车相处？

## Summary
This [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] episode uses [[PengLeiHuawei|彭磊]] and three [[HongmengZhixing|鸿蒙智行]] owners to ask how people learn to trust assisted driving and how intelligent cars become daily living spaces. It connects Huawei's 2019 car-BU setup, 2023 mass delivery, no-high-definition-map route, active safety, and user-feedback processes to owner stories around [[AITO|问界]] M9, [[Luxeed|智界]] R7, the [[HongmengZhixingG9|鸿蒙智行 G9]], long-distance travel, high-altitude work, camping, rest, and family interaction. The strongest synthesis is that smart-vehicle adoption is embodied: trust forms through repeated concrete scenes, while the car's value expands from transport into private work, rest, social, outdoor, and recovery space.

## Key Claims
- Assisted-driving trust is built through first-hand, scene-specific experience: an M9 owner moved from avoiding the feature to using it routinely after a Sichuan mountain-road return, while another owner says automatic side parking convinced friends more effectively than explanation.
- [[PengLeiHuawei|彭磊]] frames Huawei's assisted-driving route around consumer safety, restraint before mass launch, 2023 delivery through M5/new M7, no-map perception, nationwide usability, and active safety.
- The episode keeps the responsibility boundary implicit but important: owners are learning to use assisted driving, not claiming that current consumer vehicles have removed human responsibility.
- [[SmartVehicleLivingSpace|Smart vehicle living space]] is the episode's central lifestyle claim: cars become mobile meeting rooms, offices, music rooms, nap spaces, family interaction sites, and routes to travel rather than only commuting machines.
- [[Luxeed|智界]] R7 is used as a cabin-work case, with a voice actor completing urgent high-altitude recording near Yamdrok Lake because the car provided power, space, insulation, and absorptive interior materials.
- [[HongmengZhixingG9|鸿蒙智行 G9]] is positioned as a hard-SUV-plus-life product, combining camping equipment, all-terrain hardware claims, oxygen functions, comfort, and physical controls for outdoor scenarios.
- Product definition is presented as a feedback loop: Huawei teams gather store feedback, questionnaires, NPS signals, city interviews, and on-site owner complaints, then translate some issues into engineering or control choices.

## Key Quotes
> "全国都能开" — 彭磊's summary of the no-map assisted-driving usability target.

> "让硬派更有生活，让豪华更有远方" — G9 positioning phrase quoted in the episode.
