# 三联串台｜神赐给人葡萄，世界微醺了

## Summary
This [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] crossover episode interviews [[Heimai|黑麦]], a [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] writer, about [[Wine|wine / 葡萄酒]], food writing, and sensory culture. The conversation refuses the image of wine as only expensive, pretentious, or expert-gated: wine is treated as an agricultural product and cultural archive where geography, weather, grape varieties, fermentation, market fashion, family memory, and taste vocabulary meet. Its strongest synthesis is that tasting is a trainable but personal practice: [[TasteTraining]] can make wine, tea, coffee, perfume, and food more legible, but the final point is [[FlavorAsSelfKnowledge|finding what one actually likes]] rather than proving status.

## Key Claims
- [[WineAsAgriculturalCulture]] is the episode's entry point: a bottle of wine compresses place, climate, farming, winemaking, market timing, and human stories rather than only price or brand prestige.
- [[Heimai|黑麦]]'s own path matters to the source. His childhood around a liquor-factory dormitory, later media work, Italian-restaurant kitchen experience, private-kitchen practice, and wine study make food writing a form of [[FoodWritingAsLifeExperience]].
- The discussion reframes [[WineTerroir|terroir / 风土]] as more than soil and climate. Human choices, generational handoff, surrounding smells, local habits, and winemaker style can all alter how a wine is understood.
- Early Chinese wine consumption is presented as shaped by brand advertising, gift culture, and difficult first encounters with cheap Bordeaux; the source says many consumers entered wine more easily through lighter, slightly sweet New World styles.
- [[NaturalWine]] is treated as one force that lowered the social barrier around wine in China around 2017-2019, even though its low-intervention style can also produce volatile, acidic, sweet, or odd flavors.
- [[DropsOfGod|《神之水滴》]], [[SidewaysFilm|《杯酒人生》]], [[SOMDocumentary|SOM]], and [[SourGrapesDocumentary|《酸葡萄》]] show how wine knowledge becomes story, training drama, self-projection, and fraud risk rather than just tasting notes.
- The episode places wine beside [[Coffee|coffee / 咖啡]], tea, perfume, and Chinese food to argue that sensory literacy often grows by comparison, vocabulary, and repeated contact instead of isolated judgment.
- The closing claim rejects "deserving" or "not deserving" good wine. Tasting food and drink is ultimately a way to learn one's own preferences, needs, memories, and changing body.

## Key Quotes
> "风土人情" - the episode's compact phrase for treating terroir as natural and human at once.

> "葡萄酒没有什么配不配" - 黑麦's response to status anxiety around good bottles.

> "有语法" - the closing frame for learning food and drink through repeated understanding.
