# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》969丨袁氏兄弟内斗，谁能捡漏？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows the court-contact setup from [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-968-jiemi-sanguo-zui-lihai-de-yangmou-lg4qfkdgw-stixrebhjdoshil6yj|Hanji 968]] and backfills the pre-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-970-yuanshao-ansha-lvbu-weihe-hui-shibai-ln2pnvit-qgpnhn-pne7o-p1irrs|Hanji 970]] late-Han field through [[ZhongYao|钟繇]]'s successful persuasion of [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]], [[YuanShao|袁绍]] and [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s fight over [[YangzhouLateHan|扬州]], and [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s reading that Yuan-family infighting creates an opening. It treats the competition for offices such as Yangzhou inspector, left general, Yangzhai marquis, and `假节` as a test of [[MingqiLegitimacy|名器合法性]] under a court already captured by military force.

The episode's other contribution is geographic and social. [[YangzhouLateHan|扬州]], [[Jingzhou|荆州]], and [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] are compared as Yangtze-line bases through [[RiverCorridorStrategicBase|长江走廊战略根据地]], while [[QingzhouLateHan|青州]] shows the civilian cost of proxy war: [[TianKaiLateHan|田楷]] and [[YuanTan|袁谭]] fight under the wider [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]]-Yuan Shao rivalry until food runs out and [[ZhaoQiLateHan|赵岐]] has to mediate a temporary settlement.

## Key Claims
- [[ZhongYao|钟繇]] persuades [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]] to release the stalled appointment and send [[CaoCao|曹操]] formal recognition as east-conquering general, plus horses, back to [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]].
- The episode frames the [[YingchuanCommandery|颍川]] Zhong, Xun, and Chen families as an elite network capable of reading military potential and converting cultural judgment into political investment.
- After [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] is checked after Longcou, Yuan Shao turns into a family conflict over [[YangzhouLateHan|扬州]] rather than only a northern battlefield rival.
- After [[ChenWenLateHan|陈温]] dies, Yuan Shao sends [[YuanYiLateHan|袁遗]] to lead Yangzhou, but [[YuanShu|袁术]] defeats him and the vacancy becomes another sign that formal appointment no longer equals territorial control.
- [[ChenYuWuCommandery|陈瑀]] is presented as a Yangzhou appointee who later refuses Yuan Shu entry after Cao Cao's pursuit, before fear pushes him back toward Xiapi.
- The episode compares Yangzhou, [[Jingzhou|荆州]], and [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] as the three major Yangtze-line provinces, with Yangzhou as the richest future Jiangdong base, Jingzhou as the pivot around [[JianglingLateHan|江陵]], and Yizhou as more remote.
- [[CaoCao|曹操]] reads Yuan Shao and Yuan Shu's conflict as a "two tigers" opening and prepares to profit from their mutual exhaustion.
- In 193 CE, Yuan Shu is pressured by [[LiuBiao|刘表]], supported by [[HeishanArmy|黑山军]] remnants and [[YufuluoChanyu|于扶罗]], then defeated and pursued by Cao Cao through Fengqiu, Xiangyi, Ningling, and toward Jiujiang.
- Yuan Shu eventually occupies [[Shouchun|寿春]], calls himself Yangzhou inspector and Xuzhou lord, and receives left-general, Yangzhai-marquis, and `假节` titles from [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]].
- The episode stresses that these court titles still offer political packaging, but the [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] court behind them is already under Liangzhou warlord control.
- In [[QingzhouLateHan|青州]], Tian Kai and Yuan Tan's two-year standoff exhausts armies and grain, turning the Gongsun Zan-Yuan Shao rivalry into civilian hunger and reported cannibalism.
- [[ZhaoQiLateHan|赵岐]] mediates by pressuring Gongsun Zan with an imperial edict and then using an old anti-Dong Zhuo poem as leverage with Yuan Shao, producing temporary retreat and settlement.

## Key Quotes
> "两虎共斗" - Cao Cao's framing of Yuan Shao and Yuan Shu's mutual conflict.

> "上方宝剑" - the episode's shorthand for the authority carried by `假节`.

> "人吃人" - the Qingzhou famine image attributed through Pei Songzhi's quotation of Jiuzhou Chunqiu.
