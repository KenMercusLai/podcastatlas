# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》182｜为什么说“成也萧何败也萧何”（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens the [[ChenXiRebellion|陈豨之乱]] branch after the early [[WesternHanDynasty|西汉]] Zhao and succession material in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-181-jiemi-liubang-feili-taizi-de-zhenshi-yuanyin-lgnbvnnfwxe4weupqgz-rh9cbp2s|Hanji 181]]. [[HanXin|韩信]] tells [[ChenXi|陈豨]] that repeated reports of rebellion will eventually pull [[LiuBang|刘邦]] out to campaign, while Han Xin can rise inside the capital. The episode then shows how [[ZhouChang|周昌]]'s warning, court investigation, Chen Xi's refusal to answer the funeral summons, and Liu Bang's mixed military, reward, symbolic, and bribery tactics turn suspicion into a broad northern rebellion and suppression campaign.

## Key Claims
- Chen Xi's appointment gave him control over Zhao, Dai, and northern frontier forces near [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] and [[HanWangXin|韩王信]]-linked danger zones, making his office both useful and politically exposed.
- Han Xin's secret talk with Chen Xi imagines an inside-outside rebellion: Chen Xi uses frontier troops while Han Xin waits in the capital for Liu Bang's absence.
- Han Xin's plan depends on a staged psychology of accusation: Liu Bang may ignore the first report, suspect the second, and personally react to the third.
- Chen Xi's large retainer train through [[Handan|邯郸]] turns personal style and patronage imitation of [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] into a security signal for Zhou Chang.
- Zhou Chang's report causes Liu Bang to begin with financial investigation of Chen Xi's guests, but the episode stresses that a politically directed inquiry can move from money to treason.
- The source frames this as [[InvestigationDrivenRebellionEscalation|investigation-driven rebellion escalation]]: the investigation itself helps produce Chen Xi's belief that returning to court is unsafe.
- Liu Taigong's death and funeral supply the summoning occasion; Chen Xi reads the call to attend as the likely prelude to punishment rather than a neutral ritual order.
- Chen Xi declares himself king of Dai and links with [[WangHuang|王黄]], [[ManqiuChen|曼丘臣]], [[HanWangXin|韩王信]], and [[ZhaoLi|赵利]], turning a local refusal into a northern anti-Han field.
- The episode treats Chen Xi's military choice around Handan and the Zhang River as weak because Liu Bang judges that abandoning Handan concedes initiative.
- Liu Bang refuses Zhou Chang's proposal to kill officials who failed to hold Changshan counties, distinguishing insufficient force from treason.
- Liu Bang uses heavy rewards for four Zhao local strongmen to create immediate local military motivation rather than waiting for normal merit accumulation.
- Liu Bang searches for [[LeYi|乐毅]]'s descendant [[LeShu|乐叔]], grants him Lecheng Jun status, and uses old Zhao symbolic memory to calm Zhao-region sentiment.
- Liu Bang bribes Chen Xi's merchant-background generals, making defection and coalition breakdown part of the suppression strategy.
- The episode links Chen Xi's rebellion downstream to the fates of Han Xin, [[PengYue|彭越]], Han Wang Xin, [[YingBu|英布]], and [[LuWan|卢绾]], so the revolt is treated as a hinge in the rollback of early Han autonomous power.

## Key Quotes
> "第一次不信，第二次起疑，第三次震怒" - the episode's compressed version of Han Xin's read on Liu Bang's suspicion cycle.

> "用四千户人家勉励赵国子弟是值得的" - Liu Bang's logic for rewarding local Zhao strongmen.

> "本集属于系列第一部分" - the source's own limitation: the episode stops before fully developing Xiao He's role in the idiom.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction with existing wiki content was found.
- The source complicates earlier Han Xin loyalty pages: Hanji 165-166 record his refusal to betray Liu Bang during the Chu-Han balance, while Hanji 182 presents a later post-demotion conspiracy claim. This is recorded as a changed political stage rather than a direct contradiction.
- The title promises the "成也萧何败也萧何" branch, but this first part does not yet explain Xiao He's decisive role; that remains pending for the sequel.
