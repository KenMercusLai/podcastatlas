# 030 大话卫斯理之《蓝血人》

## Summary

This first “大话卫斯理” episode from [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] retells [[BlueBloodManNiKuangNovel|《蓝血人》]], following [[WesleyNiKuang|卫斯理]] from the mystery of [[FangTianNiKuang|方天]]'s blue blood and mind-affecting technology to a stranded Saturn scientist's failed homecoming. The hosts value [[NiKuang|倪匡]]'s suspense, speed, cross-genre imagination, and nuclear-age human concern while criticizing scientific errors, repetitive pursuit, improvised rules, weakly developed ethical questions, and a later plot that grows less controlled than its opening mystery.

## Key Claims

- The episode presents 《蓝血人》 as an early breakthrough in the Wesley series, emerging when space-race and UFO imagery were added to a “modern wuxia” adventure framework.
- Blue blood, disappearance, unfamiliar writing, mind influence, and advanced devices make 方天's identity mysterious, although modern genre familiarity lets many readers infer the extraterrestrial answer before the formal reveal.
- [[WuxiaScienceFictionHybrid|Wuxia–science-fiction hybridity]] explains the novel's mixture of spacecraft, alien technology, espionage, miraculous medicine, exceptional physical skill, secret societies, and serial escape structure.
- 方天 changes from threatening mystery to homesick ally after revealing that he is a Saturn scientist stranded on Earth for roughly two centuries and needs a lost navigation device to return.
- The hosts treat the novel's orbital-longevity idea, visible-Saturn claim, humanoid aliens, mind control, invisible “flying demons,” and positron defense as imaginative fiction rather than reliable science.
- [[WeaponsRaceCivilizationalSelfDestruction|Weapons-race civilizational self-destruction]] gives the homecoming its moral force: Saturn's seven states prohibit planet-destroying weapons publicly while developing and testing them secretly until pollution destroys normal life.
- The ending is read as a Cold War and nuclear-age warning that weapons can ruin a civilization through competition, testing, and environmental damage even without a final battlefield exchange.
- Questions about killing people controlled by an alien force, violent resistance to an abusive cult, and 方天's dismissal of shorter human lives have ethical potential but are raised more fully than they are argued.

## Key Quotes

> “我的家乡在哪。” - Fang Tian's reported lament after discovering that the Saturn civilization he remembered no longer exists.

> “科学知识较弱，人文关怀较强。” - the hosts' compression of the novel's uneven science and durable moral concern.
