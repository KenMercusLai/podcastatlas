# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》623丨马屁还是真心？职场人如何在夸奖与谄媚间找平衡！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills 4 CE under [[HanPingdi|汉平帝]], between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-622-guanyuan-ruhe-zuo-mianzi-gongfu-lutgirh7woafk3nzjza4o-tlmf7j|Hanji 622]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-624-du-lishi-wu-xingfu-ni-shi-rensheng-yingjia-haishi-yongren-zirao-daibiao-lpavicltc7nducbxiqgpr6t7iczd|Hanji 624]]. It follows [[WangMang|王莽]] turning the prestige from Ming Tang and related projects into court praise, [[JiuXiRitualPromotion|九锡礼遇]] discussion, temple-ranking intervention, four-directions tribute narrative, and the Qiang land-offering story that leads toward Xihai Commandery.

The durable synthesis is that praise works here as a political instrument, not only a workplace speech tactic. Ministers, envoys, auspicious signs, and frontier submission reports launder Wang Mang's advancement as natural gratitude for virtue, while [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] and the child emperor approve ritual and administrative steps that make his authority more visible before formal usurpation.

## Key Claims

- The episode uses modern workplace flattery to frame how praise can smooth relations while also becoming strategic manipulation.
- In 4 CE, officials praise Wang Mang's rapid completion of Ming Tang-related work and compare him favorably to the Duke of Zhou, then ask Wang Zhengjun to raise his status.
- The court discussion of Nine Bestowments marks a threshold because the award makes a minister's exceptional status visible near the edge of imperial privilege.
- Wang Mang intervenes in Han ancestral-temple rankings and consort-side ritual arrangements, showing control over dynastic memory and ritual hierarchy before formal usurpation.
- Tribute from Xiongnu, eastern overseas polities, Huangzhi, and earlier white-pheasant stories is read as evidence that Wang Mang constructs a four-directions submission image.
- The Qiang land-offering report uses abundant auspicious language, harvest miracles,甘露, springs, phoenixes, and divine birds to explain submission as a result of Wang Mang and Wang Zhengjun's virtue.
- The proposed Xihai Commandery and twelve-province arrangement turn a praise-heavy frontier report into administrative expansion and archaizing order.
