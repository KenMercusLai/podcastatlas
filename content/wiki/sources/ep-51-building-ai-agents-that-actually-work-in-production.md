## Summary
This [[DataScienceWithSam]] episode has [[SamDataScienceWithSam|Sam]] interview [[ShawnKercher|Shawn Kercher]] of [[HummingAgent]] about building private, domain-specific agents around real business workflows rather than packaging a general model behind a new interface. Its durable synthesis is that production value comes from proprietary context, legacy-system integration, workflow design, and controlled authority: [[ProductionAgentDiscovery]] should begin with real data, [[ProgressiveAgentAutonomy]] should expand only after evidence and feedback, and [[ModelPortableAgentArchitecture]] should keep the application testable as models change.

## Key Claims
- [[HummingAgent]] begins with paid, fixed-scope discovery to understand a client's systems, APIs, operational rules, employee knowledge, and real data before implementation.
- [[ProductionAgentDiscovery]] should use a copy of the actual production data because clean demonstration data hides incomplete CRM records, missing fields, exceptions, and undocumented practices.
- A defensible agent product is the surrounding workflow and integration layer: company-specific rules, proprietary data, legacy software, security boundaries, and user trust are harder for a foundation-model provider to reproduce than a generic connector.
- [[ProgressiveAgentAutonomy]] starts with a narrow sandboxed capability, keeps people responsible for consequential actions such as sending email, and widens access only after testing and feedback.
- [[HumanInLoopAgentGovernance]] remains necessary because supervision, subject-matter expertise, and approval can catch contextual errors that a broadly authorized agent may turn into operational or security harm.
- [[ModelPortableAgentArchitecture]] avoids dependence on one provider, but changing models creates a continuing quality-assurance and evaluation burden because models behave differently across workflows.
- The episode presents narrow vertical systems as stronger near-term candidates than broadly autonomous agents and predicts pressure on per-seat SaaS pricing as agents perform more software-mediated work.
- A source-reported client case says AI search reduced research across 44 years of PDF documents from hours to minutes, but the supplied episode note provides no architecture, error rate, cost, or independent evaluation.
