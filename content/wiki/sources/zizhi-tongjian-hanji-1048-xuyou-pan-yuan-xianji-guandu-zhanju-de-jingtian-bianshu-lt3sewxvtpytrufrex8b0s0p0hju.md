# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1048丨许攸叛袁献计：官渡战局的惊天变数

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode fills the pre-[[WuchaoRaidLateHan|乌巢夜袭]] hinge inside the [[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]]. [[CaoCao|曹操]] nearly retreats because grain and morale are failing, but [[XunYu|荀彧]] persuades him to hold; meanwhile [[YuanShao|袁绍]] rejects [[JuShou|沮授]]'s convoy-protection warning and [[XuYouLateHan|许攸]]'s proposed raid on [[Xudu|许都]]. [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]] then arrests Xu You's family at [[YeLateHan|邺城]], pushing Xu You to Cao Cao with the intelligence that Yuan's supplies at Gushi and Wuchao are vulnerable.

## Key Claims
- Cao Cao's Guandu position is near collapse: grain is almost gone, soldiers are tired, and overburdened civilians are drifting toward Yuan Shao.
- Xun Yu's reply reframes retreat as the decisive danger, arguing that the first side to pull back in a standoff for "天下归属" will lose strategic standing.
- Xun You identifies Yuan convoy commander [[HanMengLateHan|韩猛]] as brave but careless, and Cao Cao sends [[XuHuangCaoGeneral|徐晃]] and [[ShiHuanLateHan|史涣]] to intercept and burn Yuan supplies.
- Ju Shou warns Yuan Shao to add [[JiangQiLateHan|蒋奇]] as an outer protective force around [[ChunyuQiong|淳于琼]]'s grain convoy, but Yuan Shao does not adopt the warning.
- Xu You proposes a light night attack on Xudu to seize the emperor-facing center and force Cao Cao to split attention, but Yuan Shao insists on frontal victory.
- Shen Pei's arrest of Xu You's family turns Yuan-camp factional pressure into battlefield defection.
- Xu You's Wuchao intelligence converts Yuan Shao's supply advantage into a target; Cao Cao immediately prepares a disguised night movement with five thousand troops.

## Key Quotes
> "这是决定天下归属的关键时刻." - Xun Yu's strategic framing of Cao Cao's decision not to retreat.

> "袁绍虽能招揽人才却不会使用." - Xun Yu's contrast between Yuan Shao's talent pool and Cao Cao's adviser use.

> "许攸投曹称为官渡之战中最大的意外." - the episode's framing of the defection as the key unexpected turn.
