# Using Science to Optimize Sleep, Learning & Metabolism

## Summary
This early solo [[HubermanLab]] office-hours episode has [[AndrewHuberman]] answer questions about light, exercise, sleep-dependent learning, [[NonSleepDeepRestRecovery|NSDR]], nootropics, supplements, temperature, cold exposure, food, and self-tracking. Its durable synthesis is timing dependence: the same environmental or behavioral input can support wakefulness, clock alignment, learning, or sleep differently according to biological time, intensity, and individual response. The episode supplies early provenance for [[DailyCircadianPerformanceRoutine]], [[MorningLightCircadianAnchoring]], [[TargetedMemoryReactivation]], [[SleepTemperatureToolkit]], [[SleepSupplementBoundary]], and [[ColdShiveringThermogenesis]] while keeping medical, supplement, mood, and metabolic claims source-scoped.

## Key Claims
- [[MorningLightCircadianAnchoring]] is extended beyond morning exposure: outdoor light early and late in the day is presented as more useful than random total light, while nighttime brightness matters more than a blue-only rule and ordinary moonlight or firelight is treated as comparatively weak.
- [[DailyCircadianPerformanceRoutine]] treats light, exercise, meals, temperature, cold exposure, and sleep as trainable timing cues; consistent routines can create anticipatory waking, movement, and hunger without requiring rigid one-size scheduling.
- [[TargetedMemoryReactivation]] gains early examples in which an odor or tone paired with spatial learning is replayed below the awakening threshold during sleep to improve later retention.
- [[NonSleepDeepRestRecovery]], yoga nidra, a nap, or hypnosis after a focused learning bout is presented as a possible consolidation or state-change aid, not a replacement for sleep or direct instruction.
- [[SleepSupplementBoundary]] keeps magnesium threonate, apigenin, passionflower, tryptophan, and 5-HTP behind individual response and medical context; the host reports rapid sleep followed by fragmentation after serotonin precursors.
- [[SleepTemperatureToolkit]] links the daily body-temperature curve with light, activity, cooling, and circadian timing rather than treating one measured temperature as universally correct.
- [[ColdShiveringThermogenesis]] separates two goals: resisting shivering as a proposed controlled-stress practice and allowing shivering when acute thermogenesis is the target.
- Meal timing, meal size, and food composition are presented as alertness and circadian inputs, while neurotransmitter, metabolism, sex-difference, and food-specific claims remain source-scoped.
