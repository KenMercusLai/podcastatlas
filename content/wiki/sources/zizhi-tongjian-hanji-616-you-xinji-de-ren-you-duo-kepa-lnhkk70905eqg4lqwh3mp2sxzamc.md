# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》616丨有心机的人有多可怕

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 3 CE "dog-blood case" under [[HanPingdi|汉平帝]], between the imperial-marriage context in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-615-chengmu-jieshe-gudai-caili-zheme-gao-lkllbe015xkb7wlhkobtastkifdo|Hanji 615]] and the adoptive-lineage purge defense in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-618-wangmang-weihe-shi-duoquan-de-touhao-wanjia-lqcaoli6r7lbdzqy7emw8sbzs0wq|Hanji 618]]. It follows [[WangYuXinPrince|王宇]], [[WuZhangWesternHan|吴章]], and [[LuKuanDogBloodCase|吕宽]] trying to help [[EmpressDowagerWeiPingdi|卫太后]] reach Pingdi, first through a conciliatory memorial and then through a staged frightening omen at [[WangMang|王莽]]'s gate.

The durable synthesis is that a small ritualized provocation became a purge opportunity. Lü Kuan's dog-blood act failed quickly, Wang Yu was imprisoned and forced to die, his pregnant wife [[LuYanWangYuWife|吕嫣]] was condemned after childbirth, Wu Zhang was executed and his students禁锢, and the Wei clan was nearly destroyed while Wang Mang received praise for "大义灭亲." The source therefore makes Wang Yu's later Hanji 634 household background concrete and adds a Wang Mang-era branch to [[WuguPoliticalPanic|巫蛊政治恐慌]]: hidden ritual fear could transform family mediation into state violence.

## Key Claims

- Wang Yu opposes Wang Mang's isolation of Pingdi from his Wei birth relatives but is too afraid to remonstrate directly.
- The first plan is conciliatory: Wei Taihou writes to praise Wang Mang and deny power ambition, but Wang Mang responds with money and status rather than allowing her to enter Chang'an.
- Wu Zhang's second plan exploits Wang Mang's fear of ghosts and portents by using a staged abnormal event rather than open persuasion.
- Lü Kuan's dog-blood act is quickly discovered and traced back to Wang Yu, turning a crude fright tactic into a major palace-political case.
- The case is treated as wugu-adjacent danger because curse,厌胜, and hidden ritual accusation can carry lethal palace consequences.
- Wang Mang converts the case into public moral capital by forcing his own son's death and accepting praise for "大义灭亲."
- The aftermath destroys Wang Yu's household, the Wei clan, and Wu Zhang's student network, while Yun Chang's burial of Wu Zhang becomes a rare teacher-student loyalty counterexample.

## Key Quotes

> "一桶狗血" - the episode's concrete image for a small act becoming a human catastrophe.

> "大义灭亲" - the public frame used to praise Wang Mang after Wang Yu's punishment.

> "变怪" - Wu Zhang's proposed method for frightening Wang Mang through an abnormal sign.
