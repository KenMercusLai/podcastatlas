## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 75 CE [[GengGongEasternHan|耿恭]] siege layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-746-shenmeyang-de-lingdao-zhide-bei-sixintata-zhuisui-lvhbdgfbc69t6sdw92k4dipii-rx|Hanji 746]]. It follows Geng Gong and a shrinking Han garrison holding out in a Shule fortress after [[EmperorMingOfHan|汉明帝]] dies, [[CheshiHouKingdomLateHan|车师]] renews rebellion, and [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|北匈奴]] presses a surrender offer.

The durable synthesis is that rescue is a state-credit problem, not only a tactical favor. [[BaoYuEasternHan|鲍昱]] argues that abandoning Geng Gong and Guan Chong would injure loyal defenders and embolden frontier opponents, while [[FifthLunEasternHan|第五伦]]'s separate warning about Ma-family appointment and gift networks adds an early [[OuterRelativePreventiveRestraint|outer-relative restraint]] layer before later Ma-family title and command episodes.

## Key Claims

- After Mingdi's death, the court's funeral emergency delays reinforcement while Cheshi and Northern Xiongnu pressure Geng Gong's isolated force.
- Geng Gong's defenders run out of grain, boil armor and bow leather to survive, and continue holding together even after the force is reduced to dozens.
- The Northern Xiongnu chanyu offers Geng Gong kingship and marriage if he surrenders; Geng refuses, kills the envoy, and continues resistance.
- Fan Ye's comparison of Geng Gong with Su Wu frames the episode's loyalty claim as national honor under captivity pressure, not only battlefield stamina.
- Fifth Lun opposes sending rescue forces, but Bao Yu argues that abandoning isolated commanders would externally encourage frontier enemies and internally wound loyal service.
- [[EmperorZhangOfHan|汉章帝]] accepts Bao Yu's rescue argument and orders Zhangye, Jiuquan, Dunhuang, and Shanshan forces, with [[GengBingEasternHan|耿秉]] stationed at Jiuquan.
- Fifth Lun separately warns Zhangdi that appointing [[MaLiaoEasternHan|马廖]], [[MaFangEasternHan|马防]], and Ma Guang while their household gathers guests and distributes large gifts risks ministerial "作威作福" and family-state disaster.

## Key Quotes

> "凡我大汉子民，虽远必救" - opening frame for the rescue obligation.

> "外则纵蛮夷之暴，内则伤死难之臣" - Bao Yu's logic for why rescue affects frontier credit and loyal service.

> "臣毋作威作福" - Fifth Lun's warning language against empowered ministers and outer relatives.
