# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》688丨从拥兵百万到溃不成军，邓禹经历了什么？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the Jianwu 3 / 27 CE Chimei settlement layer before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-689-1-lishishang-gan-yu-liuxiu-zhengxiong-de-shi-shui-lq1dq1srokorkzeib-oattni3sme|Hanji 689-1]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-690-jiaoyou-bushen-you-duo-kepa-kankan-tianrong-de-jingli-lpbms8pebzdo2ldiwho1cba81njk|Hanji 690]]. It contrasts [[DengYuEasternHan|邓禹]]'s face-and-status driven attacks on the [[ChimeiRebellion|赤眉]] with [[FengYiEasternHan|冯异]]'s patience, troop-condition judgment, and uniform-disguise ambush.

The second half follows [[LiuPenziEasternHan|刘盆子]]'s surrender to [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]], the feeding and settlement of Chimei surrendered followers, [[FuLongEasternHan|伏隆]]'s doomed mission to [[ZhangBuEasternHan|张步]], and [[PengChongEasternHan|彭宠]]'s Yan claim after Ji falls. The durable synthesis is that early Guangwu consolidation depends on timing and credible settlement as much as force: rash command can waste hungry soldiers, while surrender management, envoy risk, and campaign priority decide whether rival groups fracture or survive.

## Key Claims

- Deng Yu misreads Chimei movement after the Long'an/Chang'an struggle, repeatedly attacks with hungry troops, ignores Feng Yi's advice, and turns status pressure into battlefield failure.
- Feng Yi first advises gradual inducement and rest, then after suffering a setback with Deng Yu recovers by using Chimei-style clothing, concealed troops, and timing against a tired enemy.
- Chimei surrender becomes a large settlement problem: Liu Penzi submits with seal and ribbon, Liu Xiu displays military strength, feeds the remaining force, and relocates leaders' households to Luoyang with land and housing.
- Liu Penzi receives a survivable post-surrender role and later subsistence support after blindness, while Fan Chong and Feng An are killed after renewed plotting.
- Fu Long rejects Zhang Bu's attempt to keep him in Qing/Xu, sends a secret memorial asking Liu Xiu not to spare him, and is later killed by Zhang Bu.
- Peng Chong's self-styled Yan kingship, northern county seizures, Xiongnu bribery, and southern links look dangerous, but Liu Xiu reads his position as exposed and internally vulnerable.
- Liu Yong remains source-scoped here as the rival whom the host treats as more politically important than Peng Chong because a Liu-surnamed claimant has stronger imperial-legitimacy leverage.
