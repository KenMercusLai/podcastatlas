# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》248｜董仲舒如何改变中华民族的命运？（2）

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-248-dongzhongshu-ruhe-gaibian-zhonghua-minzu-de-mingyun-1-lnbdqi1evkkfbajdmhh_l5m70n0z|Hanji 248(1)]] by supplying more of [[DongZhongshu|董仲舒]]'s policy answer to [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]. It moves from defects in office recruitment to [[RecommendationAccountabilityTalentSelection|举荐问责式选才]], then joins long-term moral accumulation, correction of inherited institutions, and [[OfficialLivelihoodNoncompetition|官吏不与民争生计]] into an argument about how office should serve rather than prey on households.

The closing ideological branch invokes 《春秋》's “大一统” and proposes suppressing teachings outside the Six Classics or contrary to Confucius. The episode treats Wudi's approval and Dong's Jiangdu appointment as the turn toward long-lived Confucian predominance, but [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-249-dongzhongshu-wo-bushi-rujia-de-zuiren-livm-uxnbsm0mkbrm-lvnozqnail|Hanji 249]] later qualifies this as a dominant public framework rather than literal disappearance of all rival ideas and distinguishes Dong's argument from [[WeiWanWesternHan|魏绾]]'s explicit personnel-screening proposal.

## Key Claims

- The episode criticizes office access through official family background, purchase, and seniority because wealth and time served can displace demonstrated ability and conduct.
- Dong proposes that marquises, commandery governors, and senior officials recommend candidates annually, with rewards or penalties tied to the later performance of their nominees.
- Examination should test ability, while moral conduct should inform placement; tenure alone is not treated as proof of merit.
- Political order is presented as accumulated conduct: a ruler's apparently small good or bad choices can compound before their consequences become visible.
- Inherited institutions require correction when they no longer answer present disorder; the episode's Xia-Shang-Zhou scheme distinguishes a continuing “way” from historically adjustable forms.
- Officials already supported by public stipends should not use office and private production or commerce to crowd households out of their livelihoods.
- The closing “大一统” argument seeks ideological coordination through the Six Classics and Confucian teaching, but its textual history, implementation, and later duration are not established by this installment alone.

## Key Quotes

> “天不变，道亦不变” - the episode's compact distinction between a continuing normative way and institutions adjusted to inherited disorder.

> “有钱人要多去消费” - the host's modern economic extrapolation from the 公仪休 story, retained as commentary rather than as Dong Zhongshu's historical doctrine.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. The strongest tension is interpretive: this episode describes teachings outside the Six Classics or opposed to Confucius as something to eliminate, while Hanji 249 says later predominance did not literally erase all other intellectual methods. The wiki preserves both claims as source-bounded rather than flattening them.
- The supplied document is a structured summary, not a primary-text collation. The three office-entry categories, recommendation quota, salary threshold, reward-and-penalty mechanics, exam sequence, wording about the Three Dynasties, and appointment details remain episode-attributed.
- The 公仪休 story supports an official-ethics argument within the episode. The host's claim that wealthy consumption benefits ordinary people is a modern macroeconomic extrapolation and does not follow automatically from the ancient prohibition on competing with livelihoods.
- The direct causal line from one policy answer to Confucianism's dominance through the Qing, as well as the claim that loyalty and filiality persist in cultural “DNA,” is broad host framing rather than a demonstrated historical mechanism.
