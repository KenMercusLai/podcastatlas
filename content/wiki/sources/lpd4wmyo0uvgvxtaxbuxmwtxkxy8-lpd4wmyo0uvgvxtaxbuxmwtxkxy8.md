# 003 四女同舟，如果你是张无忌你爱谁？｜闲话金庸《倚天屠龙记》

## Summary

This [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] episode reads [[HeavenSwordDragonSaber|《倚天屠龙记》]] through [[ZhangWujiJinYong|张无忌]]'s different attachments to [[ZhouZhiruoJinYong|周芷若]], [[YinLiJinYong|殷离]], [[XiaoZhaoJinYong|小昭]], and [[ZhaoMinJinYong|赵敏]]. It separates respect, gratitude, pity, companionship, attraction, political compatibility, and willingness to share a life rather than treating all four bonds as interchangeable love. The hosts favor Zhao Min because her playful conflict, decisive action, emotional recovery, and eventual withdrawal from political identity produce the clearest reciprocal romance, while retaining qualifications about idealized sacrifice, developmental speculation, and their harsher moral judgment of Zhou Zhiruo.

## Key Claims

- [[ZhangWujiJinYong|张无忌]]'s hesitation is relationship-specific rather than proof that he lacks all judgment: his four attachments arise at different times and answer different needs.
- [[ZhouZhiruoJinYong|周芷若]] and [[ZhaoMinJinYong|赵敏]] are both intelligent and politically capable, but the episode contrasts Zhou's pursuit of Emei authority and public standing with Zhao's decision to abandon status and faction for love.
- Zhou Zhiruo's deception and violence remain blameworthy, yet the hosts connect her vigilance, ambition, and concern with face to poverty, parental loss, and an oppressive Emei environment rather than treating her as simply evil.
- Zhao Min's teasing, confrontation, practical help, and resilience repeatedly force Zhang Wuji to recognize and answer desire; relational movement is therefore evidence beyond admiration or sacrifice alone.
- [[YinLiJinYong|殷离]] loves a remembered childhood version of Zhang and defends romantic exclusivity after witnessing the damage of her father's plural household; Zhang's feeling toward her is read mainly as gratitude and pity.
- [[XiaoZhaoJinYong|小昭]] combines intelligence, insight, care, and self-lowering service. The hosts regard her as a plausible companion while questioning whether devotion and low conflict establish equal partnership.
- Family security and deprivation are used as literary explanations for confidence, control, volatility, or self-effacement, but these causal readings remain interpretive rather than clinical findings.
