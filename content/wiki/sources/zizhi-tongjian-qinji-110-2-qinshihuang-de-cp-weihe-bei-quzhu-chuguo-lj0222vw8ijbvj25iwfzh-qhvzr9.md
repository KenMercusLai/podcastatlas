# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》110-2｜秦始皇的CP为何被驱逐出国

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-110-1-lisi-micang-laoshu-he-xiashuidao-laoshu-ni-zenme-xuan-lmv-fw-kgsuokonssq8bkbfvdsb|Qinji 110-1]] by following [[LiSi|李斯]] from [[Xunzi|荀子]]'s school into [[QinState|秦国]] service. It shows Li Si entering through [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]], gaining [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]]'s trust, being endangered by the [[ZhengGuoHydraulicEngineer|郑国]] spy scandal and [[GuestExpulsionOrder|逐客令]], and then answering with [[JianZhuKeShu|《谏逐客书》]].

The episode's main contribution is to complete the memorial argument that 110-1 only previewed. Li Si reframes foreign guest officials from security risk into Qin's historical strength, persuades Ying Zheng to revoke the expulsion, and then reenters the conquest project through bribery, alienation of rival courts, and targeted violence before the episode closes on [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]]'s coming death.

## Key Claims
- [[LiSi|李斯]] and [[HanFei|韩非]] are presented as [[Xunzi|荀子]]'s two standout students: Li Si is practical, eloquent, and intensely ambitious, while Han Fei is royal-born, introverted, and textually incisive.
- Li Si chooses Qin after judging [[ChuState|楚国]] too weak and its ruler too unambitious to give him scope; the episode frames Qin as the richer political "granary" anticipated by [[MousePhilosophy|老鼠哲学]].
- Li Si first approaches [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]] because Lu Buwei is the practical route to the young Qin king; Lu Buwei initially treats him as an ordinary retainer but later recommends him into office.
- Once Li Si speaks directly to [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]], the episode says his governing ideas fit Ying Zheng's ambition, making him a trusted adviser and eventually a guest minister.
- [[ZhengGuoHydraulicEngineer|郑国]]'s exposed mission from [[HanState|韩国]] turns one infiltration case into a general suspicion of foreign guests, putting Li Si's own Qin career at risk.
- Li Si writes [[JianZhuKeShu|《谏逐客书》]] while being expelled, turning a personal survival problem into a state-strategy argument.
- The memorial's first move is historical: Qin's former rulers, including [[QinMugong|秦穆公]], [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]], [[YingSi|秦惠王 / 嬴驷]], and [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]], all benefited from outside talent.
- The memorial's second move is analogy: Qin accepts foreign beauties, music, pearls, and jade, so excluding people by birthplace is inconsistent when talent is more strategically useful than luxury goods.
- The memorial's final move is adversarial: expelling guests sends people, knowledge, and useful service toward enemy states rather than protecting Qin.
- Ying Zheng accepts the argument, recalls Li Si after he has reached Liyi, restores his office, and cancels the [[GuestExpulsionOrder|逐客令]].
- After Li Si's return, Qin uses money, persuaders, alienation tactics, and assassination against other states' elites before sending generals to conquer, linking talent retention inside Qin to coercive strategy outside Qin.
- The episode ends by previewing [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]]'s final crisis: the source says he does not retreat cleanly, and Ying Zheng's short letter will force his death.
