# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》100-2｜六国竟然同时为他吊丧

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-100-1-huangdi-zhong-de-chaochang-daiji-wang-guoshi-le-lt75scavlm8qjn6ulpd-b2kwzwjf|Qinji 100-1]] by reading [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]]'s funeral as an interstate [[PoliticalFuneral|political funeral]]. The six states' mourning, [[HanState|韩国]]'s especially deferential "衰绖入吊," and the post-[[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]] change in death terminology extend [[QuasiRoyalQinHegemony|准天子式秦霸权]] and [[PowerEtiquetteReading|权力礼仪细读]] from the previous episode.

The second half turns from Qin's hierarchy to Yan's mistake. [[YanWangXi|燕王喜]] accepts [[LiFuWarringStates|栗腹]]'s claim that [[ZhaoState|赵国]] is unusable after [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]], ignores [[LeJian|乐间]] and [[JiangQu|将渠]], attacks Zhao, and is routed by [[LianPo|廉颇]] in the [[HaodaiBattle|鄗代之战]]. The episode's synthesis is that late-Warring-States fear of Qin did not create solidarity among weaker states; it also produced [[PostDisasterPredationMisread|post-disaster predation misread]], where a wounded neighbor is mistaken for an easy target.

## Key Claims
- After [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] dies in 251 BCE, all six states send senior figures to mourn, showing Qin's practical authority before formal empire.
- [[HanState|韩国]]'s "衰绖入吊" is treated as unusually submissive funeral etiquette because the clothing resembles the mourning obligation of a subject toward a ruler.
- The episode reads Zhu Xi's use of "薨" for Qin Zhaoxiang Wang after Zhou's collapse as a sign that the old Zhou ritual order no longer organizes late-Warring-States status cleanly.
- Qin Zhaoxiang Wang's fifty-six-year reign gave [[QinState|秦国]] unusual policy continuity across major events such as the destruction of [[YiquState|义渠]], [[YiqueBattle|伊阙之战]], the anti-Qi coalition, the attack on Ying, and Changping.
- The host credits figures such as [[WeiRan|魏冉]], [[BaiQi|白起]], and [[FanJu|范雎]] while treating Qin Zhaoxiang Wang's long survival and stable reign as a strategic asset in itself.
- [[YanWangXi|燕王喜]] sends [[LiFuWarringStates|栗腹]] to inspect Zhao under alliance language; Li Fu returns convinced that Zhao's adult male losses after Changping make it vulnerable.
- [[LeJian|乐间]] warns that Zhao is a four-frontier fighting state whose people are accustomed to war, but Yan Wang Xi overestimates numerical advantage.
- [[JiangQu|将渠]] warns that attacking immediately after an alliance and gift exchange is both dishonorable and dangerous, but Yan Wang Xi physically rejects the warning.
- In the [[HaodaiBattle|鄗代之战]], [[LianPo|廉颇]] defeats Li Fu around Hao, defeats the Yan forces at Dai, pursues five hundred li, and surrounds Yan's capital region.
- Yan's settlement requires Jiang Qu to become chancellor and negotiate, making the ignored remonstrant the figure used to repair the failed policy.
- [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] dies in the same year, and the episode notes that his descendants keep the title until Zhao's extinction, an exception to many Warring States fief-title patterns.
- The closing hook says Yan also sent troops against [[QiState|齐国]], leaving that campaign for the next episode.

## Key Quotes
> "衰绖入吊" - the mourning detail used to mark Han's submissive posture toward Qin.

> "利令智昏" - the host's judgment on Yan Wang Xi and Li Fu's reading of Zhao.

> "不是替我自己拦您，是替您拦您" - Jiang Qu's remonstrance logic when he tries to stop Yan Wang Xi from leaving.
