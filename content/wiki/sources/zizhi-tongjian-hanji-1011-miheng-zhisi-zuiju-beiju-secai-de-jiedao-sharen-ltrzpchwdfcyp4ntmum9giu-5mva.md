# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1011丨祢衡之死：最具悲剧色彩的借刀杀人

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode centers on [[MiHengLateHan|祢衡]] before the [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1012-haose-caocao-luanshi-zhong-de-dute-qingshi-llvrucihuv2buvp1-lisz4usbprh|Hanji 1012]] Wancheng setup. [[KongRong|孔融]] recommends the brilliant but abrasive scholar to [[CaoCao|曹操]], Cao Cao turns him into a low-status drummer at a public banquet, and Mi Heng answers by naked drumming and public counter-humiliation. The source's main interpretation is [[BorrowedKnifeKilling|借刀杀人]]: Cao Cao avoids the reputational cost of killing Mi Heng himself by sending him to [[LiuBiao|刘表]], Liu Biao later passes him to [[HuangZu|黄祖]], and Mi Heng dies after another public insult.

## Key Claims
- [[MiHengLateHan|祢衡]] is introduced as a young Pingyuan talent whose eloquence and pride make him both memorable and dangerous in a late-Han power field.
- [[KongRong|孔融]] appreciates Mi Heng and recommends him to [[CaoCao|曹操]], but the first encounter becomes hostile because Mi Heng openly insults Cao Cao.
- Cao Cao stages a public status reduction by assigning Mi Heng the lowly drummer role during a banquet for envoys and officials.
- Mi Heng refuses the required coarse uniform, strips naked after being reproached, drums calmly before the assembled guests, and turns the attempted humiliation back on Cao Cao.
- The host says Cao Cao wants Mi Heng dead but does not want the reputation of personally killing a famous scholar for wounded pride.
- Cao Cao sends Mi Heng to [[LiuBiao|刘表]], whom the source describes through a transcript variant of the familiar "externally lenient, internally suspicious" character judgment.
- The host treats the accompanying recommendation channel as a deliberate information catalyst that keeps Mi Heng's insulting reputation active inside Liu Biao's circle.
- Liu Biao initially honors Mi Heng, but Mi Heng mocks people around him; the source names [[KuaiYue|蒯越]] and [[CaiMao|蔡瑁]] through transcript variants as Jingzhou elite figures who can turn private offense into slander.
- After rumors portray Mi Heng as privately calling Liu Biao indecisive, Liu Biao sends him to [[HuangZu|黄祖]] in Jiangxia without warning Huang Zu about his temperament.
- Huang Zu first receives Mi Heng well because he is sent by Liu Biao, then kills him after Mi Heng publicly insults him.
- The host summarizes the mechanism as risk outsourcing, reputation preservation, and low-cost removal: the highest-status actors remain absent from the killing scene while the lowest-status angry actor performs the lethal act.
- The closing lesson links Mi Heng's death to [[PoliticalSurvivalFirst|安全第一政治生存]]: talent and independence can become fatal when they are not paired with rule awareness, self-restraint, and self-protection; [[JiaXu|贾诩]]'s hiddenness is named as a contrast.
- The source uses [[Analects|《论语》]] and [[Confucius|孔子]]'s three cautions to frame youthful blood-energy, anger, and desire as forms of self-exposure.

## Key Quotes
> "风险外包、维护形象、零成本铲除" - the host's three-part summary of Cao Cao's borrowed-knife logic.

> "血性不可乱用，色心也不能乱起" - the closing moral bridge to the following episode on Cao Cao's desires.
