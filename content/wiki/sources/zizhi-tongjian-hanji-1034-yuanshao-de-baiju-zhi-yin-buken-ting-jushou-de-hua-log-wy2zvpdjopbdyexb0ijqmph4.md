# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1034丨袁绍的败局，只因不肯听沮授的话？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode frames the pre-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡]] contest as a communication and organization problem rather than a simple story that [[YuanShao|袁绍]] failed because he ignored [[JuShou|沮授]]. Ju Shou proposes rest, agricultural recovery, and harassment after the long war with [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]], but his blunt language collides with Yuan Shao's status psychology while [[GuoTu|郭图]] and [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]] offer flattering pro-war counsel. The [[CaoCao|曹操]] side is shown through Cao Cao and [[XunYu|荀彧]] diagnosing Yuan's command weaknesses, after which Cao deploys through [[LiyangYellowRiverLateHan|黎阳]], [[QingzhouLateHan|青州]], the Yellow River line under [[YuJin|于禁]], and Guandu before the [[ZhangXiu|张绣]] / [[JiaXu|贾诩]] recruitment hook carried into Hanji 1035.

## Key Claims
- Mature communication is not debate or self-display; the host presents it as adaptive expression that can flow like water, reflect like a mirror, and preserve integrity like bamboo.
- Ju Shou gives Yuan Shao the strongest strategic advice: after eight years against Gongsun Zan, Yuan's people and resources need rest, agriculture, and attritional harassment rather than immediate decisive war.
- Ju Shou's advice fails partly because the framing is too blunt for a hierarchy: calling Cao Cao the more legitimate side and Yuan Shao's campaign unjust gives Yuan Shao less face-saving room to accept.
- Guo Tu and Shen Pei succeed in the meeting by matching Yuan Shao's desired self-image, praising his divine martial capacity and Hebei's strength while downplaying Cao Cao.
- Yuan Shao's court meeting exposes factional consumption among Tian Feng, Feng Ji, Ju Shou, Guo Tu, and Shen Pei; the problem is not adviser scarcity but decision-process breakdown.
- Cao Cao and Xun Yu treat the same prewar moment analytically: Cao attacks Yuan's ambition-wisdom mismatch and command disunity, while Xun Yu distinguishes Yuan's territory, advisers, and generals from their usable effectiveness.
- Cao's deployments before Guandu show that opponent analysis is converted into field preparation: secure Qingzhou through Zang Ba, hold the Yellow River bank with Yu Jin, return to Xudu, and place troops at Guandu.

## Key Quotes
> "大志少智，色厉胆薄" - Cao Cao's compressed judgment of Yuan Shao.

> "只有匹夫之勇，可一战而擒" - Xun Yu's judgment of Yan Liang and Wen Chou.
