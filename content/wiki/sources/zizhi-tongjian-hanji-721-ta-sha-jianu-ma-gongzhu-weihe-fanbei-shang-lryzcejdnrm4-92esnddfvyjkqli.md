# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》721丨他杀家奴，骂公主，为何反被赏？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianwu 19-20 / 43-44 CE before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-722-rang-liuxiu-xiansi-de-toutongbing-zenme-huishi-logwccdsca7vm2zzssu8ubm8rwra|Hanji 722]]. It links crown-prince education, public-minded recommendation, and severe legal enforcement through [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]], [[YinShiEasternHan|阴识]], [[YinXingGuangwuMaternalKin|阴兴]], [[HuanRongEasternHan|桓荣]], and [[DongXuanEasternHan|董宣]].

The central synthesis is that late Guangwu personnel judgment is tested by protected households as much as by official appointments. Liu Xiu honors cautious maternal kin and scholars, but the sharper public signal comes when Dong Xuan kills [[HuyangPrincessEasternHan|湖阳公主]]'s murderous slave, refuses forced apology, and is rewarded after making the case about imperial government rather than private princess anger.

## Key Claims

- In 43 CE, Liu Xiu appoints Yin Shi to act as zhijinwu and has Yin Xing help guide Crown Prince Liu Zhuang, making maternal kinship useful only when paired with cautious conduct.
- Yin Shi is presented as loyal, careful in private speech, and useful as a model for royal relatives and attendants.
- Yin Xing recommends useful people without letting personal closeness decide state value, turning personnel judgment into a public-interest practice.
- Liu Xiu appoints Huan Rong as yilang and has him teach the crown prince classics, then rewards his learning and decorum after a Taixue discussion.
- Zhong Xing's refusal of reward and redirection of credit to his teacher Ding Gong keeps scholarly honor tied to lineage, humility, and public attribution rather than private capture.
- Dong Xuan, as Luoyang magistrate, waits outside the princess residence context and executes Princess Huyang's slave after the slave appears in her carriage.
- Dong Xuan's defense reframes the case: if Liu Xiu lets a royal household shelter murder, imperial restoration loses the legal credibility needed to govern.
- Liu Xiu first threatens Dong Xuan, then tries to force an apology, but finally releases and rewards him, making enforcement against protected households visible.
- The Nandun tax-remission scene shows Liu Xiu granting favor while resisting a ten-year fiscal promise; he adds only one more year after local elders tease him for stinginess.
- Liu Shang's southwestern campaign, the Three Excellencies punishment, and Wu Han's deathbed warning all extend the episode's concern with disciplined governance, restraint in amnesty, and self-control after merit.

## Key Quotes

> "用人太重要了" - opening personnel-selection frame.

> "不要轻易赦免罪犯" - Wu Han's deathbed warning as rendered by the episode.
