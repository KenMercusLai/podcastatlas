# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1042丨聪明还是顽固？田丰因顶撞袁绍入狱

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the immediate pre-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]] sequence after [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1041-jiemi-lishishang-zhenshi-de-guanyu-jiangcao-luisp0ndk1lfrc87byygn6r8myhx|Hanji 1041]]. [[LiuBei|刘备]] flees to [[YuanShao|袁绍]] through [[YuanTan|袁谭]], [[CaoCao|曹操]] returns to Guandu, and [[TianFengLateHan|田丰]] argues that Yuan should avoid a decisive attack on [[Xudu|许都]] and instead use defense, agriculture, preparation, alliances, and cavalry harassment to exhaust Cao Cao. Yuan Shao rejects the plan, treats Tian Feng's hard remonstrance as morale damage, and imprisons him, making the episode a compact case of correct strategy failing inside a prideful decision system.

## Key Claims
- The episode opens with a self-respect list and uses it as a loose frame for later questions about self-positioning, boundaries, and judgment.
- After losing to Cao Cao, Liu Bei escapes to Yuan Tan and then to Yuan Shao; Yuan Shao personally travels two hundred li from [[YeLateHan|邺城]] to welcome him.
- Yuan Shao only begins planning an attack on Xudu after Cao Cao has already returned to Guandu, reducing the opportunity Tian Feng had identified in Hanji 1041.
- Tian Feng warns that Cao Cao has defeated Liu Bei, is skilled and resilient despite fewer troops, and should not be underestimated.
- Tian Feng proposes a concrete attritional plan: hold defensible terrain, cultivate local support and agriculture, strengthen military preparation, connect with outside heroes, and send elite cavalry to harass the region south of the Yellow River.
- The host reads Tian Feng's proposal as a workable long-war, exhaustion, and guerrilla-like strategy that could convert Yuan Shao's stronger overall position into cumulative pressure.
- Yuan Shao rejects Tian Feng's persistence as destabilizing speech before campaign mobilization and orders him shackled and imprisoned.
- The episode portrays Yuan Shao's camp as internally fragmented: Tian Feng is intelligent but rigid, [[XuYouLateHan|许攸]] greedy, [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]] narrow and impractical, [[FengJiLateHan|逢纪]] self-willed, [[GuoTu|郭图]] jealous of [[JuShou|沮授]], and [[YanLiangLateHan|颜良]] and [[WenChouLateHan|文丑]] as brave but limited.

## Key Quotes
> "不出三年，坐收其利." - Tian Feng's expected payoff from attritional pressure as summarized in the episode.

> "色厉胆薄，好谋无断." - the host's compact judgment of Yuan Shao.

> "只有匹夫之勇." - the episode's judgment on Yan Liang and Wen Chou.
