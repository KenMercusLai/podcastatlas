# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》620丨史上最传奇的“全民大选”

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills 4 CE under [[HanPingdi|汉平帝]], just before the later Hanji 622-624 prestige and reward sequence. It follows [[WangShunXinTaishi|王顺]], [[ChenChongXinOfficial|陈崇]], [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]], and more than 8,000 officials and commoners as a petition channel pushes [[WangMang|王莽]] from Anhan Gong toward the higher "宰衡" status, expanded land grants, family ennoblement, and court protocol above the Three Excellencies.

The durable synthesis is that Wang Mang's ascent is presented as a managed legitimacy loop. Collective petitioning makes reward look externally demanded; [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]'s personal grant supplies senior Han authority; Wang Mang's refusal of his sons' rewards preserves humility credit; and ministerial counter-petitions convert that refusal into a reason to summon him back into government.

## Key Claims

- The episode uses a modern student joint-report case to frame ancient collective petitions as a form of political and reputational pressure.
- In 4 CE, Wang Shun and more than 8,000 officials and commoners petition the court to increase rewards for Wang Mang after Chen Chong's recommendation.
- The court raises Wang Mang by adding land, giving him the "宰衡" title, placing him above the Three Excellencies in protocol, and extending honors to his mother and sons.
- Wang Zhengjun personally confers the grant because Han Pingdi is young and Wang Mang's reward requires the highest available court endorsement.
- Wang Mang refuses his sons' seals, ribbons, titles, and fiefs, then claims illness and stays away from court, turning reward acceptance into another public humility performance.
- Kong Guang and other ministers answer by saying loyal-subject restraint should yield to the ruler's larger meaning, and they ask that Wang Mang be summoned back and that his refusal memorials be rejected.
- The final compromise reduces some fiefs while leaving Wang Mang's formal elevation and political centrality intact.

## Key Quotes

> "忠臣之节，亦宜自屈，而伸主上之义" - formula used to make Wang Mang's refusal yield to the court's reward decision.

> "叩头辞让" - historical wording the episode uses for Wang Mang's ritual refusal.

> "经营中最关键的是把握人心" - host's concluding management frame for Wang Mang's political method.
