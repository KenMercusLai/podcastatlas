# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》827丨一句话引发的帝王之死

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 146 CE death of [[EmperorZhiOfHan|汉质帝]] immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-828-da-jiangjun-liangji-shanquan-feili-de-donghan-jianchen-llqnqijagtmdlgitulddxspncu0e|Hanji 828]]. It pairs a major [[Taixue|太学]] expansion and classics-based appointment channel with the central political event: [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] poisons the child emperor after being publicly called a domineering general.

The later succession discussion starts here as well. [[LiGuLateHan|李固]], [[HuGuangLateHan|胡广]], Zhao Jie, and [[DuQiaoLateHan|杜乔]] argue for broad consultation and recommend [[LiuSuanLateHan|刘蒜]], while [[CaoTengLateHan|曹腾]]'s private resentment against Liu Suan foreshadows the self-protective succession turn developed in Hanji 828.

## Key Claims

- In 146 CE, the court orders commanderies, kingdoms, senior officials, external relatives, and lesser marquises to send classics-capable candidates or sons into Taixue, with examinations and office assignment after study.
- The episode presents Taixue enrollment rising to more than thirty thousand, making the school both a learning institution and a credentialed route into office.
- Liang Ji's power is already overbearing before the succession crisis: the source describes him as domineering in court under the protection of his empress-dowager kinship.
- Emperor Zhi publicly calls Liang Ji a "跋扈将军", making the child emperor's perception of Liang's overreach visible to the whole court.
- Liang Ji interprets the emperor's intelligence and hostility as future danger, bribes an attendant, and has poison mixed into the emperor's soup cake.
- Li Gu is summoned during the emperor's abdominal pain; Liang Ji blocks the emperor's request for water, and Emperor Zhi dies soon afterward.
- Li Gu's grief and questioning after the death make him a danger to Liang Ji, who fears exposure of the poisoning.
- After the emperor dies, Li Gu, Hu Guang, Zhao Jie, and Du Qiao urge careful consultation over the succession and recommend Liu Suan as the legitimate and morally stronger candidate.
- Cao Teng opposes Liu Suan because Liu Suan had previously ignored or failed to salute him, showing how private access grievances can enter a dynastic succession decision.

## Key Quotes

> "是跋扈将军也" - Emperor Zhi's public label for Liang Ji.

> "吃完汤饼后腹痛" - the emperor's deathbed explanation as summarized by the source.

> "立帝事关社稷" - the succession-warning logic attributed to Li Gu, Hu Guang, and Zhao Jie.
