## Summary
This [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] interview follows [[HeTongxue|何同学]] from childhood, university video-making, and his 5G breakthrough into the operating problems of [[HeTongxueStudio|何同学工作室]]. Its central synthesis is that a personal creator can build a larger production organization only by separating the creative core from manufacturing temptation, finding managerial complements, financing expensive work without letting advertising dictate it, and converting personal quality control into a repeatable team system.

The episode also develops a concrete DIY selection rule: begin with a recognizable everyday need, then transform its solution into a visual spectacle. [[LuoYonghao|罗永浩]] uses [[Smartisan]] and TNT as a comparison point for innovation under resource limits, while He repeatedly defines his own organization as an entertainment-video company rather than a technology or hardware company.

## Key Claims
- [[HeTongxue|何同学]] says video, rather than hardware manufacturing, is his durable craft and the core product of [[HeTongxueStudio|his studio]].
- Viral success increased future-work anxiety: after the 5G video, slower output and higher standards became responses to a perceived gap between attention and work quality.
- The studio's large DIY projects combine engineering labor with entertainment goals; possible product licensing does not change the organization's main identity.
- [[DIYVideoSelectionLogic|DIY selection]] works best when a real everyday frustration can be converted into an immediately legible visual spectacle.
- [[CreatorStudioScaling|Creator-studio scaling]] creates a control problem: the founder cannot inspect every frame while also managing projects, hiring specialists, and building organizational layers.
- High production costs and staff responsibility make commercialization unavoidable, but [[CreatorAdvertisingIntegrationConstraint|advertising integration]] fails when the sponsor's product, the video's premise, and expectations for a hit cannot be aligned.
- Personal-brand controversy can become company risk; He attributes lost commercial work and part of the studio's annual loss to his deleted ride-hailing post and delayed public apology.
- Overseas localization works differently from domestic publishing: the studio translates and dubs highly visual work, while short-form reach produces less revenue and weaker fan conversion than long-form viewing.
- [[Smartisan]]'s TNT remains, in Luo's account, a direction he considers valid despite insufficient resources, debatable timing, and pre-AI speech-recognition limits.
- He's stated long-term transition is from the person who touches every detail toward a coach who preserves standards through team coordination.
