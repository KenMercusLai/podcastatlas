# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》450｜清官必须死？揭秘汉臣韩延寿必死之谜

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens the [[HanYanshouWesternHan|韩延寿]] death case under [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]]. It presents Han as an effective former Yingchuan and East Commandery administrator whose popularity and local performance made the later investigation by [[XiaoWangzhiWesternHan|萧望之]] politically combustible.

The durable synthesis is that Han Yanshou's death is not read as a simple clean-official morality tale. The episode says Xiao's accusations about lending and carriage decoration had factual hooks, but the deeper danger came from Xuan-era security politics after Huo Guang: an official associated with Huo-era promotion, loved by local people, and connected to scholar-official networks could look unsafe even when his actual record was mainly local administration.

## Key Claims

- Han Yanshou is introduced as a successful Yingchuan and East Commandery governor whose local record and public affection make his execution puzzling.
- Xiao Wangzhi accuses Han of lending and lavish military-carriage decoration, while Han counteraccuses Xiao of misusing more than one million cash.
- The investigation clears Xiao's money issue and confirms Han's two listed violations, after which Han is condemned as cunning and disloyal.
- Thousands of officials and commoners reportedly escort Han toward execution at Weicheng, dramatizing the gap between legal-political judgment and local affection.
- The host argues that Han's fatal exposure is his place in post-Huo-Guang political cleanup rather than the two legal charges alone.
- Han's effective local governance becomes a liability because popular attachment, scholar-official friendships, and Huo-era promotion can be read as rival political capital.
- Xiao Wangzhi is framed as Xuan's trusted anti-Huo-aligned court actor, while Han, Yang Yun, and Wei Xiang are placed nearer to the scholar-official and Huo-era personnel field.
- The episode ends by making [[BingJiWesternHan|丙吉]]'s response the next question, since Xiao brings the case to Bing Ji for handling.

## Key Quotes

> "清官为何必死" - the episode's central puzzle.

> "死而无憾" - Han Yanshou's reported response to the officials and commoners who escorted him.
