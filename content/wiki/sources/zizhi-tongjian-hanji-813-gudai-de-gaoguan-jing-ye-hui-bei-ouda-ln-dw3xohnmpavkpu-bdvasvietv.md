# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》813丨古代的高官竟也会被殴打？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the first 133 CE [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]] layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-814-dangguan-buwei-min-zuozhu-buru-huijia-mai-hongshu-lqlrfpuenzhr1tbrdd3urtolfw2p|Hanji 814]]. It follows [[LangYiLateHan|郎顗]] recommending [[HuangQiongLateHan|黄琼]] and [[LiGuLateHan|李固]], [[ZuoXiongLateHan|左雄]] opposing private-favor enfeoffments for [[SongELateHan|宋娥]] and [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]], and the later [[LiuJuLateHan|刘矩]] beating case that makes [[MinisterialDignityProtection|九卿体面保护]] a concrete institutional repair.

The second half centers [[LiGuLateHan|李固]]'s disaster-policy answer. Li Gu treats wet-nurse titles and overpowered [[OuterRelativeCourtPower|outer relatives]] as connected dangers: private gratitude may be paid with money, but marquisates, land, offices, and palace posts change public authority and can destroy both the state and the favored family.

## Key Claims

- Lang Yi uses drought and unseasonable winds as a [[OmenCodedRemonstrance|disaster-remonstrance]] opening, arguing that real repentance matters more than empty sacrifice.
- Zuo Xiong distinguishes private gratitude from public rank: Song E may be rewarded materially, but wet-nurse status and Liang-family intimacy should not become marquisate.
- Liang Shang's repeated refusal of his son's title turns outer-relative restraint into a visible counterexample to raw family extraction.
- Emperor Shun accepts only part of the criticism: he drops the Liang title attempt and stops beating nine ministers, but keeps Song E's title.
- Liu Ju's treatment at Shangshu Tai shows that bureaucratic discipline can become institutional humiliation when high officials are rushed, shouted at, and beaten.
- Li Gu's `灾异策对` generalizes the same rule: titles, land, outer-relative posts, and palace access become dangerous when private favor becomes public power.
- The source sets up Hanji 814 as the continuation of Li Gu's answer, especially the later court-core and public-welfare repair arguments.

## Key Quotes

> "非刘氏不封王，非有功不封侯" - Zuo Xiong's Han founding-rule argument against private-favor marquisates.

> "赏以钱财，不若封以土地" - the episode's distinction between material reward and landed title.

> "九卿位亚三事，行有佩玉之节" - Zuo Xiong's dignity argument for why high ministers should not be beaten.
