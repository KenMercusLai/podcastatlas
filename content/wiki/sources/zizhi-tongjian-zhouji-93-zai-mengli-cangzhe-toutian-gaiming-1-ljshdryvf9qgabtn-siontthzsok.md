# 《资治通鉴·周纪》93丨在梦里藏着偷天改命？（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode moves from the [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-92-lishishang-di-yi-wei-zhuming-tiangou-shi-shui-2-lt1nwraiuuvpy2tzo-zwaoqsajzx|Zhouji 92 part 2]] resolution of [[MiWan|芈完 / 楚考烈王]] and [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]] into [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s fifty-third-year frame. [[MiWan|楚考烈王]] begins his reign by ceding land to [[QinState|秦国]], while [[HanState|韩国]]'s isolated [[Shangdang|上党]] crisis becomes the next major [[QinEastwardPressure|Qin pressure]] branch.

The main narrative centers on [[FengTing|冯亭]], who refuses to surrender Shangdang to Qin and offers its seventeen cities to [[ZhaoState|赵国]] in order to force a Han-Zhao anti-Qin alignment. [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] consults [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] and [[ZhaoBao|赵豹 / 平阳君]], but Zhao Bao's warning against "无故之利" is overwhelmed by the apparent windfall, making the episode a source for [[WindfallDecisionTrap|无故之利 / 利令智昏]].

## Key Claims
- [[MiWan|楚考烈王]]'s first regnal year is presented as another sign of [[ChuState|楚国]] decline because Chu cedes substantial land to [[QinState|秦国]] without fighting.
- [[BaiQi|白起]]'s earlier route-cutting leaves [[Shangdang|上党]] practically isolated from [[HanState|韩国]], turning a military geography problem into a local political choice.
- [[FengTing|冯亭]] is not simply switching allegiance to [[ZhaoState|赵国]]; he is trying to make Zhao bear the Qin threat and thereby create a Han-Zhao resistance structure.
- The episode explains Shangdang people's Zhao preference through Three Jin proximity, shared history, local familiarity, and fear of Qin as a "虎狼之邦."
- [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] is young and newly governing after [[ZhaoWeihou|赵威后]]'s death, so the episode frames his judgment as exposed to early-ruler overconfidence.
- [[ZhaoBao|赵豹 / 平阳君]] argues that a free gain without cause should be treated as disaster rather than blessing because accepting Shangdang will invite Qin's attack.
- The host treats Zhao Dan's response as [[WindfallDecisionTrap|利令智昏]]: the attraction of seventeen cities overpowers the strategic cost embedded in the offer.
- Source-scope caution: this installment stops with Zhao Dan resisting Zhao Bao's advice; it sets up but does not yet narrate the later final decision and consequences.

## Key Quotes
