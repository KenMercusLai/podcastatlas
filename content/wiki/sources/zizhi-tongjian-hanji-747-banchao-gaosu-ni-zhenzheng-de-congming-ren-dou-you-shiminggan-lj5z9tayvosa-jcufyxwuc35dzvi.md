## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 76-77 CE [[EmperorZhangOfHan|汉章帝]] layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-748-laozuzong-de-zhonggao-qinqi-bugongcai-gongcai-liangwulai-luqvnld4wbdgc3nrdumyhfhscs4z|Hanji 748]]. It follows [[BanChaoEasternHan|班超]] after the court recalls him and withdraws Western Regions offices, then turns to [[LiuCangDongpingKing|东平王刘苍]]'s tomb-frugality remonstrance, [[ZhengChunEasternHan|郑纯]]'s Yongchang rule, [[AilaoKingLeilao|哀牢王类牢]]'s rebellion, [[LiuYanFulingKingEasternHan|阜陵王刘延]]'s demotion, and early Xiongnu and Yiwu notices.

The durable synthesis is that capable governance is measured by responsibility to real conditions rather than by rank display or formal motion alone. Ban Chao returns to [[ShuleKingdom|疏勒]] after local collapse begins, Liu Cang blocks wasteful imperial-tomb counties, Zheng Chun keeps a frontier commandery stable through clean and custom-aware rule, and the 77 CE withdrawal from [[YiwuGarrisonLateHan|伊吾屯田]] shows how quickly retrenchment can become [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|Northern Xiongnu]] occupation.

## Key Claims

- When the court abolishes Western Regions offices and recalls Ban Chao, Shule and [[KhotanKingdomLateHan|Khotan]] react as if Han protection is their main security anchor.
- Ban Chao treats recall as subordinate to actual frontier danger: after Shule cities defect to [[QiuciKingdom|龟兹]] and Wutou, he returns, kills rebels, defeats Wutou, and stabilizes Shule.
- Liu Cang's earthquake-era advice and opposition to tomb counties show remonstrance that links disaster response, frugality, ancestral precedent, and public burden.
- Zheng Chun's decade in Yongchang is presented as clean, respectful, and locally effective; local Yi and Mo leaders submit under that style of rule.
- Zheng Chun's successor shows the opposite pattern: ignorance of local conditions and official arrogance trigger Ailao King Lei Lao's killing of local officials and rebellion.
- Zhangdi handles Liu Yan's renewed rebellion accusation with limited punishment rather than execution, demoting him to Fuling marquis and restricting official contact.
- The same frontier field pairs Southern Xiongnu military cooperation and famine relief with Yiwu withdrawal, after which Northern Xiongnu immediately reoccupies the position.
