# 《资治通鉴·周纪》12丨 公孙鞅求职路上的拉关系 走后门

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode fills the transition between [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]]'s pressured accession and [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]]'s reform authority. It reads Qin Xiao Gong's 361 BCE recruitment edict as a restoration message built from [[QinMugong|秦穆公]]'s old prestige, Qin's lost Hexi position, and a promise of high reward for state-strengthening plans. The second half follows Shang Yang's failure to be used by [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王 / 梁惠王]], [[GongshuWeiChancellor|公叔痤]]'s "use him or kill him" warning, and Shang Yang's entry into Qin through [[JingJian|景监]], making the episode a case of [[PrivateAccessTalentChannel|private-access talent channels]] before [[ShangYangReforms|商鞅变法]] begins.

## Key Claims
- In 361 BCE, Qin Xiao Gong issues a recruitment edict promising office and land to insiders or outside guests who can offer plans to strengthen [[QinState|秦国]].
- The edict's rhetoric first invokes Qin Mu Gong's hegemony and western prestige, then turns Qin's later internal disorder and loss of Hexi territory into a shame-and-restoration appeal.
- The episode treats this as political communication: historical glory, humiliation, and concrete reward are combined to attract useful talent.
- Shang Yang appears before his Qin reform career as 公孙鞅: a young Legalist-oriented figure who values law, order, and state-strengthening technique.
- In [[WeiState|魏国]], he serves in Gongshu's household but fails to gain Liang Hui Wang's direct trust.
- Gongshu recognizes Shang Yang's ability near death and tells Liang Hui Wang either to entrust the state to him or kill him so he cannot serve another power.
- Shang Yang correctly judges that Liang Hui Wang, having ignored the recommendation, will also ignore the killing advice; he therefore waits until Gongshu dies and then leaves for Qin.
- Shang Yang reaches Qin because Qin is recruiting talent, but the episode stresses that opportunity still requires access: he enters through Jing Jian, a favored "辟臣" around Qin Xiao Gong.
- The episode explains Jing Jian's title without equating him with a eunuch; it notes that earlier eunuch terms such as 寺人, 阍, 竖, and 小臣 make the "景监 was a eunuch" reading uncertain.
- Shang Yang's first Qin interviews do not work when he speaks in slower 帝道 and 王道 terms; he wins Qin Xiao Gong only after switching to practical rich-state, strong-army proposals.
- The episode ends by making this job-search sequence the immediate prelude to the harsh reform backlash developed in Zhouji 13 and later Shang Yang episodes.

## Key Quotes
> "高官和封地重赏" - the reward promise attached to Qin Xiao Gong's recruitment edict.

> "不用就杀" - the episode's compact framing of Gongshu's warning about Shang Yang.

> "拉关系走后门" - the host's framing of Shang Yang's need for access through Jing Jian.

> "辟臣" - the debated term used for Jing Jian's status near Qin Xiao Gong.
