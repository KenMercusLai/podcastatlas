# Saronic Founders: Autonomous Warships, China's 230X Advantage & Swarms of Robot Ships

## Summary
This [[AllIn|All-In]] interview has [[JasonCalacanis|Jason Calacanis]] speaking with [[DinoMavroukis]] and [[VibhAltakar]] of [[Saronic]] about [[AutonomousNavalVessels]], U.S. naval shipbuilding capacity, and the strategic pressure created by [[China]]'s much larger shipbuilding base. The episode frames autonomy as a production, cost, software, and procurement problem rather than only a robotics feature.

The strongest source contribution is [[NavalShipbuildingThroughput]]: Saronic argues that smaller autonomous vessels such as [[CorsairSaronic|Corsair]] and [[MarauderSaronic|Marauder]] can add attributable mass faster than traditional crewed ships while keeping people out of some dangerous missions. The company also uses [[PortAlpha|Port Alpha]] in [[Texas]] to present a [[PrivateCapitalDefensePrime]] model built around vertical integration, software-defined components, fixed-price contracting, and private R&D.

## Key Claims
- [[DinoMavroukis]] says a [[CorsairSaronic|Corsair]] vessel supported a recent [[StraitOfHormuz]] rescue mission that brought two pilots home without adding more service-member risk; operational details remain source-limited.
- Dino says the [[UnitedStates]] builds about 100,000 gross tons of ships per year while [[China]] builds about 23 million gross tons, creating the episode's "230-to-1" shipbuilding-capacity frame.
- Dino says U.S.-built ships can cost five to six times comparable Chinese-built ships and that the [[USNavy|U.S. Navy]] has 296 ships against a statutory 355-ship target.
- [[VibhAltakar]] argues that removing humans from a vessel removes doors, bathrooms, stairs, and other support systems, allowing naval architecture, payload, range, and control authority to be optimized differently.
- Dino says [[Saronic]]'s [[MarauderSaronic|Marauder]] is a 180-foot autonomous vessel that can carry the equivalent of 16 VLS tubes, making firepower per year a production-throughput question.
- The guests present the future Navy as hybrid: crewed ships remain relevant, while autonomous vessels take dangerous, repetitive, persistent, or high-volume missions where they fit.
- Vibh says [[SoftwareDefinedShipbuilding]] requires digitized components, software APIs, remote control, onboard compute, sensors, and selective in-house electronics or autonomy work.
- Dino criticizes cost-plus incentives and says Saronic has raised $2.5 billion in private capital, built Marauder through internal R&D, and wants more defense budget allocated to autonomous systems.
- Dino says Saronic can build 2,000 Corsair vessels per year at its Austin-area facility and is planning [[PortAlpha|Port Alpha]] as a Brownsville, Texas shipyard that could eventually create 10,000 jobs over 10 years.
- The autonomous-weapons section says AI can support identification and mission execution, but people still set mission intent and approval thresholds; the wiki records this as [[HumanAuthorizedAutonomousWeapons]] rather than as an independently verified use-policy claim.
