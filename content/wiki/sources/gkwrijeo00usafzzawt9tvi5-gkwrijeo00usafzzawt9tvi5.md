## Summary

This [[ShengdongZaokafei|声动早咖啡]] business-news episode reports that [[Nvidia]]'s board rejected [[JensenHuang|Jensen Huang]]'s possible return, while an interim chief executive said the company's operating plans and AI-cloud investment commitments remained unchanged. It reports that [[ZhaoLiying|赵丽颖]] sued [[HongguoShortDrama|红果短剧]] and [[Douyin]] over alleged AI face-swapping or face-blending, and that [[AnjoyFoods|安井食品]] is expanding from frozen hot-pot products into bakery through acquisition and business-to-business supply. These legal, financial, product, and market claims remain episode-attributed.

## Key Claims

- Nvidia's board reportedly refused to reappoint Huang after he said he would be willing to return, converting succession uncertainty into an explicit board-level decision in the episode's account.
- A four-use flight card limits airline liability and heavy-user exposure more tightly than an unlimited pass while still pre-selling weak-period seat inventory; blackout dates and extra charges materially narrow the headline value.
- AI-generated or blended faces create a portrait-rights identification problem when an output evokes a specific natural person without reproducing one original photograph.
- The episode reports that Hongguo had handled nearly 44,000 works with portrait-infringement issues by October, indicating a platform-governance problem as well as a dispute between an actor and individual producers.
- Informal “buy a face” recruitment and thin contracts can leave the scope, duration, sublicensing, model training, derivative use, and responsibility for later distribution unclear.
- Frozen bakery lets supermarkets, cafés, and convenience stores add fresh-seeming products with less on-site labor and production complexity, shifting competition upstream toward stable supply, recipes, differentiation, and buyer margin.
- Anjoy's reported bakery growth and acquisitions illustrate category expansion from frozen savory foods into bagels, toast, cakes, and desserts, while supplier-owned discount stores introduce potential channel conflict.
