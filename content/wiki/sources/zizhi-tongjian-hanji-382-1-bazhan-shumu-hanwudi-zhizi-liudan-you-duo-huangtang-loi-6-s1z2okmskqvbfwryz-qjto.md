# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》382-1｜霸占庶母，汉武帝侄子刘丹有多荒堂？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] short episode backfills the early career of [[JiangChongWesternHan|江充]] before the later [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]] sequence. It begins from a moral frame about capable people who choose harm, then narrates Jiang Chong's earlier identity as Jiang Qi, his conflict with [[LiuDanZhaoPrinceWesternHan|赵太子刘丹]], his accusation against Liu Dan at Chang'an, and his rise into emperor-direct surveillance under [[HanWudi|汉武帝]].

The durable synthesis is that Jiang Chong's later danger did not appear from nowhere. The episode shows him entering Wudi's trust through denunciation, gaining authority to police royal and elite overreach, and creating a personal grievance with [[LiuJuCrownPrinceWesternHan|卫太子刘据]] when he reports the crown prince's envoy for using the imperial-only Chi Road. It also supplies a bounded profile of [[LiuPengzuZhaoKingWesternHan|赵敬肃王刘彭祖]]'s Zhao branch and Liu Dan's sexual-political scandal before the broader Wugu arc.

## Key Claims

- Jiang Chong, originally Jiang Qi, is presented as a Zhao-state figure connected to Liu Dan by marriage before he becomes a Han Wudi favorite.
- Liu Dan is described as Zhao king Liu Pengzu's heir and Han Wudi's nephew, whose sexual misconduct with daughters, sisters, and his father's consorts makes him unfit to inherit the Zhao kingdom.
- Jiang Qi flees to Chang'an after Liu Dan tries to kill him, changes his name to Jiang Chong, and reports Liu Dan's conduct to the central court.
- Han Wudi initially wants Liu Dan executed, but Liu Pengzu's plea and offer to serve on the frontier lead to commutation: Liu Dan is spared death and reduced to frontier/commoner service status in the episode's account.
- Jiang Chong's appearance, speech, and accusation win Wudi's favor; he is made a Zhizhi Xiuyi envoy and later tasked with policing elite and royal violations of ritual and law.
- Jiang Chong's strict reporting creates enemies among powerful households and helps define the coercive edge of [[XiuyiEnvoyImperialSurveillance|绣衣使者皇权监察]].
- The Chi Road incident creates the first concrete Jiang Chong-Liu Ju grievance in this wiki: Jiang Chong detains Liu Ju's envoy for using the emperor's exclusive road, refuses Liu Ju's face-saving plea, and reports the matter to Wudi.
- The episode closes with Taishi 4 and Zhenghe 1 imperial travel, sacrifices, household-register reception, amnesty, and solar eclipse chronology rather than continuing directly into the Wugu explosion.

## Key Quotes

> "可作恶而不作恶是善" - the opening moral criterion used to frame capable but harmful court actors.

> "埋下祸根" - the episode's summary of the Chi Road grievance between Jiang Chong and Liu Ju.
