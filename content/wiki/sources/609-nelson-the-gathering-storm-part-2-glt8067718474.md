# 609. Nelson: The Gathering Storm (Part 2)

## Summary

This [[TheRestIsHistory]] episode follows [[HoratioNelson|Horatio Nelson]] from the Malta blockade and his controversial Palermo command through his return to Britain, separation from [[FannyNelson|Fanny Nelson]], and appointment under [[HydeParker|Hyde Parker]] for the Baltic expedition. It argues that scandal and strategic effectiveness coexisted: Nelson's relationship with [[EmmaHamilton|Emma Hamilton]] damaged his judgment, family, and professional standing, while his pressure on Malta and urgency against the [[LeagueOfArmedNeutrality|League of Armed Neutrality]] remained militarily consequential.

## Key Claims

- Nelson's Palermo base can be read as strategically connected to Naples, Sicily, and the Malta blockade, although parties, gambling, illness, Emma Hamilton, and friction with [[LordKeith|Lord Keith]] gave contemporaries grounds to question his focus and dignity.
- Nelson ignored Keith's order to move toward Menorca and helped capture *Les Genereux* on 18 February 1800, illustrating how successful results could reward a pattern of insubordination without settling whether the decision was justified beforehand.
- Malta surrendered soon after Nelson left the Mediterranean, and Britain retained it as a major strategic possession; the source credits Nelson's blockade effort without treating him as its sole cause.
- Nelson's overland return with Emma and [[SirWilliamHamilton|Sir William Hamilton]] intensified celebrity, expense, ridicule, and the public humiliation of Fanny.
- The January 1801 confrontation in which Nelson refused to renounce Emma effectively ended his marriage, while Horatia's concealed birth, mutual jealousy, and his harsh allowance letter deepened the domestic harm.
- Russia, Denmark, Sweden, and Prussia formed the League of Armed Neutrality amid conflict over neutral shipping, British blockade practices, Malta, and Baltic naval supplies.
- Nelson pressed the cautious Parker to attack Copenhagen quickly; the episode ends after Parker gives him operational command of the assault, before the [[BattleOfCopenhagen1801|Battle of Copenhagen]] itself.

## Key Quotes

> "no time should be lost" - Nelson's argument to Parker for immediate action against Copenhagen, as presented by the episode.

> "either Emma or herself" - the choice Fanny put to Nelson during the confrontation that effectively ended their marriage.
