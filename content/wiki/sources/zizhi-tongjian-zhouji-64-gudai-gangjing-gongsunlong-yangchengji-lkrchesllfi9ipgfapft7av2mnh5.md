# 《资治通鉴·周纪》64丨 古代“杠精”公孙龙养成记

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode closes the 周纪三 sequence in [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s seventeenth year, 298 BCE, by introducing [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] as a [[ZhaoState|赵国]] aristocratic patron and one of the [[WarringStatesFourLords|战国四公子]]. It centers on [[GongsunLong|公孙龙]], a retainer known for [[MingjiaDisputation|名辩]] arguments such as "白马非马" and "臧三耳," then uses [[KongChuan|孔川]] and [[ZouYan|邹衍]] to separate dazzling verbal victory from [[TruthSeekingDebateNorm|求明理的辩论规范]]. The episode's main contribution is not a full system of Gongsun Long's philosophy, but a compact standard for judging argument: speech should clarify reality, categories, and shared understanding rather than merely silence the other side.

## Key Claims
- In 298 BCE, [[Pingyuanjun|平原君 / 赵胜]] enters the narrative as a son of [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]] and younger brother of [[ZhaoHe|赵何]], who has become ruler of Zhao.
- Pingyuanjun is presented as a major retainer patron with thousands of guests, placing him beside [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]] inside [[WarringStatesRetainerPatronage|战国养士]] and the [[WarringStatesFourLords|战国四公子]] comparison.
- [[GongsunLong|公孙龙]] is one of Pingyuanjun's guests and is respected for "兼白同异" arguments, including "白马非马," "兼白论," and "一同论."
- The episode says pre-Qin discussions of "离坚白" and "一同" were treated as major philosophical problems, while keeping the account focused on Gongsun Long's reputation for debate rather than a full textual study.
- [[KongChuan|孔川]], described as a [[LuState|鲁国]] visitor, courtesy name Zigao, and seventh-generation descendant of [[Confucius|孔子]], debates Gongsun Long on "臧三耳."
- In the episode's explanation, "臧三耳" argues that a person can be said to have three ears: two external ears and an inner, subjective hearing capacity.
- Gongsun Long's argument initially leaves Kong Chuan unable to answer, but Kong Chuan later reframes the exchange for Pingyuanjun: proving three ears is difficult and false, while proving two ears is easy and true.
- Pingyuanjun accepts Kong Chuan's distinction and tells Gongsun Long not to debate Kong Chuan again, judging Kong Chuan as stronger in principle and Gongsun Long as stronger only in wording.
- When [[ZouYan|邹衍]] passes through Zhao, Pingyuanjun asks him to debate Gongsun Long on "白马非马," but Zou Yan refuses.
- Zou Yan defines proper debate as a practice that clarifies categories, principles, and purpose so that participants and listeners do not lose the thread of truth.
- Zou Yan criticizes arguments built from name-definition tricks, piling up terms, agile metaphors, and endless pressure until everyone stops responding.
- After Zou Yan's critique, the episode says Gongsun Long is gradually neglected, making the story a warning against substituting verbal dominance for reason.
- The episode closes 周纪三 and previews 周纪四, covering 297-273 BCE and returning to the question of whether [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] can escape Qin and safely return to [[ChuState|楚国]].

## Key Quotes
> "困难而虚假" - Kong Chuan's standard for why Gongsun Long's cleverer claim fails as knowledge.

> "义理胜过言辞" - Pingyuanjun's judgment of Kong Chuan over Gongsun Long in the episode.

> "白马非马" - the famous Gongsun Long thesis Zou Yan refuses to debate on Gongsun Long's terms.
