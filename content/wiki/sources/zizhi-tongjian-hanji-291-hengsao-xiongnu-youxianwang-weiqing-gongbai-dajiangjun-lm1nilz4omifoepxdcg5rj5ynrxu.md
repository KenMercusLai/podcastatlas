# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》291｜横扫匈奴右贤王，卫青功拜大将军

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the battle and reward sequence left open by Hanji 290-1 and 290-2. After [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]] surrounds the Xiongnu Right Wise King's camp, the king escapes north with several hundred guards, but the episode reports that the Han force captures more than ten lesser kings, over fifteen thousand people, and nearly one million livestock, leaving the right-wing polity badly weakened.

[[HanWudi|汉武帝]] then sends the grand-general seal to the frontier and gives Wei Qing command over the other generals. Wei refuses hereditary rewards for his three sons on the ground that they have no merit and credits the victory to the emperor and the officers who fought; Wudi answers by rewarding a wider group of commanders. The episode later connects this result to [[HuoQubing|霍去病]] and [[BanChaoEasternHan|班超]], but its strongest bounded additions are the completed [[GaoqueCampaignWesternHan|Gaoque campaign]] and the distinction between defeating an enemy force and capturing its leader.

## Key Claims

- Wei Qing reportedly launches a four-direction night attack after the pre-battle encirclement described in Hanji 290-2.
- The intoxicated Right Wise King escapes north with several hundred guards, so the attack does not achieve complete leadership capture.
- The episode nevertheless reports more than ten lesser kings and over fifteen thousand people captured, together with nearly one million livestock, and treats the Right Wise King's remaining power as severely reduced.
- The result is presented as a major morale and strategic gain that weakens one of the Xiongnu power groupings and helps prepare later Han offensives.
- Han Wudi sends the grand-general seal to the army, appoints Wei Qing over all generals, increases his fief, and initially grants marquisates to his three sons.
- Wei Qing refuses rewards for sons without military merit and redirects credit toward the emperor and participating commanders; the episode then lists a wider distribution of rewards.
- The later Ban Chao section treats local dependence and renewed instability as reasons for returning to the Western Regions after recall, echoing [[MissionDrivenFrontierResponsibility|mission-driven frontier responsibility]].

## Key Quotes

> “自己的儿子尚在襁褓之中，没有功劳。” - the episode's rendering of Wei Qing's objection to hereditary reward without service.

> “以夷狄攻夷狄。” - the strategy attributed to Ban Chao after he reassesses the consequences of abandoning the Western Regions.

## Connections

- [[GaoqueCampaignWesternHan|西汉高阙出击右贤王]] - completes the night attack, enemy-leader escape, captures, spoils, and immediate strategic result.
- [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]] - commander promoted in the field who refuses unearned rewards for his sons and shares credit with subordinate commanders.
- [[GuideEnabledDeepStrike|向导赋能纵深突袭]] - sequel showing that the insider-guided approach and reconnaissance were followed by a successful force-neutralizing attack, though not capture of the target leader.
- [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] - ruler who appoints Wei Qing grand general and broadens the reward distribution after Wei's objection.
- [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] - opposing power whose Right Wise King escapes while his people, subordinate kings, and livestock are captured.
- [[HuoQubing|霍去病]] - later Western Han commander used to extend the episode's anti-Xiongnu narrative beyond this campaign.
- [[BanChaoEasternHan|班超]] - Eastern Han frontier actor used as a diplomatic and strategic counterpart to the Western Han military branch.
- [[MissionDrivenFrontierResponsibility|使命驱动的边疆责任]] - existing frame for Ban Chao's decision to return after recall when withdrawal begins destabilizing dependent allies.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. This episode directly continues Hanji 290-2's pre-nightfall encirclement and supplies the previously missing outcome.
- Outcome boundary: the Right Wise King escapes, so descriptions such as “斩首” or “全歼” cannot be read literally as capture or destruction of every member of the targeted polity. The episode instead supports severe force and resource loss despite leadership escape.
- The four-direction assault, intoxication, escape party, captured lesser kings, population and livestock totals, distances, reward amounts, appointment ceremony, speeches, and full list of rewarded commanders remain episode-attributed pending primary-text and specialist comparison.
- “华夏武魂,” the three-part Xiongnu power map, the claim that the Western Regions were formally incorporated through the Wei-Huo victories, and the sword-and-shield comparison among Huo Qubing and Ban Chao are evaluative or chronologically compressed narrative frames.
- Ban Chao's thirty-six-person, fifty-state, and thirty-year formulations, the court's motive for recall, the exact dependence of Dou Xian's later victory on Ban Chao, and the Rome-Han comparison are broad source-scoped claims rather than a complete Eastern Han frontier reconstruction.
