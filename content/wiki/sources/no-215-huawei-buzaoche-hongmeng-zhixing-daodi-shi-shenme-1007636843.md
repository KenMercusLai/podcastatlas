# No.215 华为不造车，鸿蒙智行到底是什么？

## Summary
This [[Banlatte]] episode explains [[HongmengZhixing|鸿蒙智行]] as [[Huawei]]'s multi-automaker intelligent-vehicle ecosystem rather than a conventional Huawei-owned carmaker. It traces the route from early connected-car supplier ambitions through the 2019 intelligent-automotive BU, the post-smartphone-supply shock, the [[Seres]] / [[AITO|问界]] breakthrough, and the later [[Luxeed|智界]], [[Stelato|享界]], [[Shangjie|尚界]], and [[Maextro|尊界]] portfolio. The episode's central contribution is to separate legal manufacturing ownership from practical product power: automakers keep qualification, factories, vehicle ownership, and vehicle revenue, while Huawei contributes technology, product definition, retail channels, brand marketing, service systems, and later the [[ShenzhenYinwang|引望]] supplier route.

## Key Claims
- [[HongmengZhixing|鸿蒙智行]] is presented as a Huawei-led intelligent-vehicle technology and product ecosystem, not as Huawei directly taking whole-vehicle manufacturing qualification.
- [[Huawei]]'s car path grew from connected-car and supplier thinking, then accelerated after external restrictions damaged Huawei's smartphone business and made intelligent EVs a plausible replacement consumer-terminal field.
- [[AITO|问界]] began as the deep-cooperation template: [[Seres]] handled qualification, factories, supply chain, manufacturing, and quality, while Huawei handled product definition, intelligent cockpit, assisted driving, electric drive, pricing, launches, retail, and service experience through [[HuaweiSmartSelectionCarModel|华为智选车模式]].
- The new [[AITO|问界]] M7 turnaround and M9 high-end validation are used to show how [[AutomotiveChannelMigration|consumer-electronics retail capability can migrate into car sales]], especially when model competitiveness, Huawei store traffic, and delivery investment align.
- [[Luxeed|智界]], [[Stelato|享界]], [[Shangjie|尚界]], and [[Maextro|尊界]] are not copies of [[AITO|问界]]; each reflects a different automaker partner, target price band, and capability gap.
- [[Chery]] gives [[Luxeed|智界]] stronger manufacturing and export capacity, while [[BAICGroup|北汽]] gives [[Stelato|享界]] a high-end sedan platform and [[SAICMotor|上汽]] tries to use [[Shangjie|尚界]] to bring Huawei intelligence below RMB 200,000.
- [[JACGroup|江淮]] and Huawei use [[Maextro|尊界]] to attack the ultra-luxury segment, with the source treating the S800 as a manufacturing-quality and Chinese-brand-premium experiment rather than only a high-price model.
- [[RenZhengfei]]'s repeated "not making cars" boundary and Huawei's transfer of brand trademarks to partners are treated as real governance signals, but the episode also shows that [[HuaweiNoCarManufacturingBoundary|not making cars does not mean weak control over product, brand, channel, or technology]].
- [[ShenzhenYinwang|引望]] separates Huawei's intelligent-vehicle technology assets and personnel into a broader supplier platform, with [[Seres]] and [[Avatr]] buying minority stakes and Huawei trying to serve more automakers beyond 鸿蒙智行.
- The source keeps L3 and L4 automation legally bounded: current Chinese mass-market assisted driving still leaves the human as driver, while future L3/L4 standards may change the [[AutonomousDrivingResponsibilityBoundary|responsibility boundary]] after implementation.

## Key Quotes
> "华为不造车" — the boundary the episode repeatedly tests.

> "鸿蒙智行" — the alliance name for Huawei's smart-vehicle cooperation system.

> "不是简单复制问界模式" — the source's warning against flattening the five brands into one template.
