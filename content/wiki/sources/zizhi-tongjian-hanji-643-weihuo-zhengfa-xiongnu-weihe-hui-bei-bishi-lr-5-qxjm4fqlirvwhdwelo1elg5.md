# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》643丨卫霍征伐匈奴，为何会被鄙视？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills [[WangMang|王莽]]'s early [[XinDynasty|新朝]] Xiongnu crisis around 11 CE, before the later Hanji 645 and 647-655 layers. It centers on [[YanYouXinGeneral|严尤]]'s memorial comparing Zhou, Han, and Qin responses to northern nomadic pressure, then applies that cost logic to Wang Mang's planned 300,000-man campaign against [[Xiongnu|匈奴]].

The durable synthesis is that frontier strategy is judged by objective, cost, and support capacity rather than battlefield prestige alone. The episode does not deny the achievements of [[HanWudi|汉武帝]], [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]], or Huo Qubing, but it uses Yan You's critique to argue that deep punitive war can become a lower strategy when logistics, climate, disease, transport animals, march tempo, and terrain risk exceed state capacity.

## Key Claims

- Wang Mang tries to split Xiongnu politics by luring and coercively titling Luanti Xian, Luanti Deng, and Luanti Zhu, but the move angers chanyu Luanti Zhi and triggers repeated frontier raids.
- Yan You's three-way comparison treats Zhou's limited expulsion of Xianyun as the middle strategy, Han Wudi's deep offensive as the lower strategy, and Qin's Great Wall exhaustion as no strategy.
- "Lower strategy" does not mean battlefield incompetence; it means the war pursues a maximal aim at extreme and durable fiscal, demographic, and social cost.
- Qin's wall program is criticized as using maximum domestic cost for an effect no better than limited defense.
- Yan You identifies five practical problems in Wang Mang's planned Xiongnu expedition: delayed assembly and decaying morale, frontier supply shortage, transport-animal failure in dry steppe terrain, climate and disease burden, and slow vulnerable movement through dangerous terrain.
- Yan You recommends that already-arrived troops strike quickly if they must be used, but Wang Mang keeps transporting soldiers and grain according to the original large-plan design.
- The failure of Wang Mang's titled Xiongnu proxies shows that coerced recognition without durable control can intensify frontier hostility.

## Key Quotes

> "周得中策，汉得下策，秦无策" - Yan You's strategic ranking as presented in the episode.

> "用最大的代价，实现一个最小的目标" - the host's summary of the Qin wall policy critique.

> "历史上的下策并不等于战场无能" - the episode's implied distinction between tactical glory and strategic cost.
