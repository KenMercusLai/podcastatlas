## Summary
This episode backfills a Jianwu 5 / 29 CE layer immediately before the Ban Biao "王命论" sequence: Peng Chong's Yan regime ends when his household slave Zi Mi kills him from inside the palace, and Liu Xiu's later "不义侯" reward exposes the moral cost of rewarding useful but unrighteous violence. The same episode also tracks Guangwu consolidation through Guo Ji's Yuyang repair, Wu Han and Geng Yan's campaign against bandit forces, Pang Meng's suspicion-driven rebellion, Liu Ping's self-sacrificial protection of Sun Meng, Cen Peng's Shu-campaign preparation, and Ban Biao's institutional argument to Wei Xiao for Han restoration.

## Key Claims
- Peng Chong's downfall is framed less as battlefield defeat than as an internal reliability failure: he feared outside relatives and former hostages but missed the danger posed by close household access.
- Zi Mi's killing of Peng Chong removes a rebel enemy for Liu Xiu, yet the episode treats both Peng Chong's rebellion and Zi Mi's master-killing as crimes that should not be purified merely by political usefulness.
- Liu Xiu's enfeoffment of Zi Mi as "不义侯" preserves a moral label in the title while still diluting the Han meaning of meritorious rank.
- Guo Ji's appointment to Yuyang shows consolidation through repaired local trust, kind administration, soldier training, and deterrence against raiders.
- Pang Meng's rebellion shows how ambiguous command signals can generate a self-confirming revolt when a trusted subordinate does not verify the ruler's intent.
- Liu Ping's rescue of Sun Meng presents loyalty as bodily substitution: he shields the endangered superior, asks to die in his place, and sustains him afterward.
- Ban Biao argues to Wei Xiao that late Western Han and Wang Mang conditions differ from late Zhou because Han inherited Qin commandery-county administration, leaving rival powerholders without hereditary six-state bases.

## Key Quotes
> "兵从中起" - the divination frame for Peng Chong's fall from inside his own household.

> "不义侯" - the title Liu Xiu grants Zi Mi after Zi Mi sends Peng Chong's head to Luoyang.

> "汉承秦制，改立郡县" - Ban Biao's institutional contrast between Han conditions and late Zhou feudal fragmentation.
