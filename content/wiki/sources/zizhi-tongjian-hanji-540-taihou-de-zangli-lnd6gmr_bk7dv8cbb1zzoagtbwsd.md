# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》540丨太后的葬礼

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Yongshi 2 / 15 BCE under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]], before the later Chengdi succession, office-reform, and Zhai Fangjin omen-crisis episodes. It starts from a modern public-power ethics question, then follows [[QiongchengTaihouWesternHan|邛成太后]]'s death, the rushed funeral, [[XueXuanWesternHan|薛宣]]'s dismissal, [[ZhaiFangjinWesternHan|翟方进]]'s surprising promotion to chancellor, and [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]]'s promotion to Yushi Dafu.

The durable synthesis is that ritual failure can become a personnel and responsibility test. Chengdi treats a hurried dowager funeral as a failure of high-office duty, yet the punishment field does not map cleanly onto career outcome: Xue Xuan loses office, Zhai Fangjin is penalized and then promoted, and Kong Guang's value lies in procedure, precedent, and extreme palace confidentiality rather than forceful argument.

## Key Claims

- [[QiongchengTaihouWesternHan|邛成太后]]'s long status depended on [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] choosing a cautious, childless consort to raise the crown prince [[HanYuandi|刘奭 / 汉元帝]] after [[XuPingjunWesternHan|许平君]] was poisoned.
- Her death in 15 BCE exposed a court-duty problem because the funeral was rushed and funded through collected taxes rather than handled with fitting ritual care.
- [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] blamed top ministers for the rushed funeral, dismissing chancellor [[XueXuanWesternHan|薛宣]] and demoting Yushi Dafu [[ZhaiFangjinWesternHan|翟方进]].
- The next appointment complicates any simple blame logic: Zhai Fangjin, also punished in the funeral case, is promoted to chancellor and enfeoffed as Gaoling Hou.
- Zhai Fangjin is introduced as a classics-literate but severe enforcement official whose many impeachments made some people accuse him of private resentment or unfairness.
- [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]] is promoted to Yushi Dafu and characterized through cautious, precedent-aware, secret-keeping conduct in and out of court.
- The transcript's closing notice about Chengdi sacrificing at Yongcheng is only a setup in the available source and should remain source-scoped.
