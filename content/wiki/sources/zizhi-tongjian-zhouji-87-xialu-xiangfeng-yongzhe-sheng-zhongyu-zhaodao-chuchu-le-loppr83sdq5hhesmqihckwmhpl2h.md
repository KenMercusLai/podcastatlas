# 《资治通鉴·周纪》87丨“狭路相逢勇者胜”终于找到出处了！

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[ZhaoShe|赵奢]] from the fiscal-capacity story in Zhouji 86 into his famous battlefield role at [[EyuBattle|阏与 / 燕宇之战]]. After [[QinState|秦国]] attacks [[ZhaoState|赵国]] over a broken hostage-and-land settlement, [[LianPo|廉颇]] and another commander judge rescue too difficult, while Zhao She argues that a narrow, dangerous road can favor the braver commander. Zhao She then combines strict discussion control, spy deception, rapid movement, camp defense, and [[XuLiWarringStates|许历 / 许立]]'s high-ground advice to defeat Qin and lift the siege.

The episode also tees up the next [[FanJu|范雎]] arc, saying that Fan Ju accompanies a Wei envoy to [[QiState|齐国]], is honored by [[QiXiangWang|齐襄王]], and later suffers retaliation from Wei Qi. The transcript contains several likely homophone renderings, so this ingest keeps the source wording visible while normalizing stable names where the surrounding wiki already has pages.

## Key Claims
- [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] attacks Zhao after Zhao fails to complete a prior settlement involving the hostage 公子吴 and a land-return promise.
- Zhao He consults senior commanders first; [[LianPo|廉颇]] and another commander decline because the route is far and narrow and the Qin force appears too strong.
- [[ZhaoShe|赵奢]]'s maxim, "道远险狭，譬由两鼠斗于穴中，将勇者胜," is treated as the episode's source for the later idiom "狭路相逢勇者胜."
- Zhao She's victory depends on more than courage: he hides intention near [[Handan|邯郸]], punishes unauthorized military discussion, feeds a Qin spy false confidence, then force-marches toward [[EyuBattle|阏与 / 燕宇之战]].
- [[XuLiWarringStates|许历 / 许立]] improves the plan by warning that Zhao's battle line needs depth and by urging Zhao to seize the northern mountain before Qin does.
- The battle extends Zhao He's earlier promotion of Zhao She from tax official into military proof that fiscal discipline and battlefield command can belong to the same state-strengthening career.
- The Fan Ju teaser is source-scoped; this episode introduces the coming humiliation-revenge arc but does not yet narrate Fan Ju's Qin career.

## Key Quotes
> "道远险狭，譬由两鼠斗于穴中，将勇者胜。" — Zhao She's explanation of why the rescue is possible despite the difficult route.

> "狭路相逢，勇者胜。" — The host's idiom-level takeaway from the episode's Zhao She passage.
