# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》779丨历史告诉你：用错人竟会亡国灭家？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 106-107 CE early [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|和熹邓皇后]] and [[EmperorAnOfHan|汉安帝]] regency layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-780-zui-duo-zai-duo-nan-de-shi-dai-donghan-lvgcumrfjblrofer532ees3tdoa|Hanji 780]]. It opens with the [[TalentVirtueDistinction|才德之分]] framework, then tests the "用错人" problem through Fan Zhun's education memorial, [[LuGongLateHan|鲁恭]]'s judicial-calendar correction, and [[RenShangLateHan|任尚]]'s failed replacement of [[BanChaoEasternHan|班超]] in the Western Regions.

The durable synthesis is that appointment quality is not a generic preference for ability. [[FanZhunLateHan|樊准]] and Lu Gong show how the right adviser can repair institutions through learning and procedure, while Ren Shang shows that a famous military officer can be misfit for indirect frontier governance when he ignores local experience, applies harsh control, and turns a low-cost balance system into rebellion and withdrawal.

## Key Claims

- The opening restates Sima Guang's fourfold talent-virtue distinction: saint, fool, gentleman, and talented lesser person; the episode emphasizes that talent without virtue or judgment can magnify harm.
- In 106 CE, Qinghe Wang Liu Qing dies after asking to be buried beside Fan Qiang and Song Guiren; the court suspends the yulong manyan spectacle because of his death.
- Fan Zhun, presented as a Nanyang shangshulang, argues that court learning and Confucian instruction have decayed and asks the regency to seek hidden scholars and learned men for the future emperor's education.
- Empress Dowager Deng accepts Fan Zhun's advice and orders senior officials to recommend worthy and broadly learned scholars who can become doctors.
- In 107 CE, Lu Gong becomes situ and criticizes the handling of litigation calendars: light cases and settled cases should not drag farmers into summer interrogation, while death-penalty review should not be rushed at the eleventh month boundary.
- Lu Gong's advice is accepted, and the episode reinforces his profile with earlier Zhongmou governance based on moral instruction rather than reliance on punishment.
- Ren Shang is appointed Western Regions protector after Ban Chao's retirement despite the fact that his military reputation does not make him fit for the region's political ecology.
- Ban Chao warns Ren Shang that frontier soldiers and Western Regions polities require loose, simple governance and tolerance of minor faults; Ren dismisses the advice as ordinary and then governs harshly.
- Ren Shang's high-pressure rule triggers Western Regions rebellion, including an attack on him at Shule; Liang Jin later rescues the position tactically, but the court removes Ren and appoints Duan Xi.
- Qiuci remains loyal at first because Ban Chao had installed the pro-Han king Ba Ba, yet moving the protectorate seat into Qiuci provokes local nobles and their Gumo and Wensu allies into another attack.
- The court finally recalls Duan Xi, [[LiangJinLateHan|梁慬]], Zhao Bo, and the tuntian troops, abandoning the Western Regions because military cost, disaster pressure, and strategic priority make the inherited position look unaffordable.
