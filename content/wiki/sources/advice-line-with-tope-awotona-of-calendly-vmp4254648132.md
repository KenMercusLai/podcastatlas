## Summary
This [[HowIBuiltThis]] Advice Line episode pairs [[GuyRaz]] with [[TopeAwotona]] of [[Calendly]] to advise [[DomCotton]] of [[Newlane]], [[IvanaPlayfield]] of [[Playfield]], [[DanyNailRoom]] of [[TheNailRoom]], and [[JustinKittredge]] of [[ISlide]]. Across a folding bicycle helmet, a dog-walking bag, a nail salon, and customized footwear, the advice converges on [[ConstraintLedGrowthDiagnosis]]: identify whether the next bottleneck is positioning, founder capacity, site-level experience, or channel focus before adding growth complexity.

## Key Claims
- AI has reduced the cost of building an early software product, but cheap prototyping does not replace customer-problem validation or product-market fit.
- [[Calendly]] is repositioning from scheduling software toward a meeting assistant, while Awotona has added chief product officer duties to keep innovation central as the company matures.
- [[Newlane]] should sharpen who buys and influences helmet use, make its folded form socially visible, and position convenience as a way to overcome non-use.
- [[Playfield]] should prioritize continuity around childbirth by delegating operations while protecting the founder's distinctive product, brand, and partnership work through [[FounderUniqueValuePreservation]].
- [[TheNailRoom]] should maximize capacity and perfect a hard-to-copy hospitality experience at its first salon before testing additional sites through [[ExperienceBeforeLocationExpansion]].
- [[ISlide]] should place most leadership attention and resources behind one or two promising growth engines while assigning owners to maintain selectively useful secondary channels.
- Awotona's broader lesson is [[CustomerCenteredExpansion]]: serving trusted existing customers in additional ways is often easier than repeatedly acquiring unrelated customer groups.

## Key Quotes
> "the helmet you will actually use" - Awotona's proposed positioning for Newlane's convenience advantage.

> "build a business around a customer rather than around a product" - Awotona's central lesson from thirteen years at Calendly.
