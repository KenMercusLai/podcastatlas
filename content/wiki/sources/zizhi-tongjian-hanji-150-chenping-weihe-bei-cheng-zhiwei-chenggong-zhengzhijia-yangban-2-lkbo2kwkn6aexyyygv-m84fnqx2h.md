# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》150｜陈平为何被称之为成功政治家“样板”？（2）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-150-chenping-weihe-bei-cheng-zhiwei-chenggong-zhengzhijia-yangban-1-llale6fron0tkye7n0e-aanojqar|Hanji 150 part 1]] by moving [[ChenPing|陈平]] from village reputation into Chu-Han career danger. It follows his service under [[WeiJiu|魏咎]] and [[XiangYu|项羽]], his self-protective escape after the [[SimaAng|司马卬]] affair, and his arrival at [[Xiuwu|修武]] through [[WeiWuzhi|魏无知]]. The episode's core claim is that [[LiuBang|刘邦]] uses Chen Ping not only because he is talented, but because an outside newcomer can supervise old generals and signal that the Han side will reward mobile talent.

## Key Claims
- The episode resolves the previous source's Liu Bang teaser by showing Chen Ping's path from restored Wei to Xiang Yu and then to Liu Bang.
- Chen Ping's public fairness in the社祭分肉 scene is extended into later political allocation: the host says he understands both distributing rewards inside a camp and breaking opponents through less visible means.
- The source presents Chen Ping's historical image as morally ambiguous: his intelligence often appears as [[CovertPoliticalTechnology|阴谋式政治技术]] rather than open battlefield or ritual advice.
- Chen Ping leaves [[WeiJiu|魏咎]] after his advice is ignored and he is slandered, then serves [[XiangYu|项羽]] through major Chu events including the [[HongmenYan|鸿门宴]] aftermath.
- In the Sima Ang episode, Chen Ping first earns Xiang Yu's reward by forcing Sima Ang back, but later becomes exposed when Sima Ang turns toward Liu Bang; returning the seal and gold before fleeing shows his practical self-preservation.
- The Yellow River boat scene is used to show Chen Ping's acute risk reading: he notices the boatman's suspicion, realizes he may be killed for imagined hidden wealth, and strips down to prove he is carrying no treasure.
- [[LiuBang|刘邦]] appoints Chen Ping quickly as a duwei/canche-type figure and later uses him in护军 work, despite old generals' complaints.
- The host explains the appointment through [[VeteranMeritCliqueSupervision|功臣旧将监督]]: Chen Ping is new, socially unentangled with Liu Bang's old Pei County circle, and therefore useful for reporting on veteran commanders.
- The episode also frames Liu Bang's treatment of Chen Ping as [[OutsiderTalentSignaling|厚待外来人才信号]], especially after the successful use of [[HanXin|韩信]].

## Key Quotes
> "智谋多为阴谋" - the episode's compact label for Chen Ping's less public style of strategy.

> "如果使平得宰天下，亦如是肉矣" - Chen Ping's self-reading of the meat-distribution scene as governing promise.

> "官场不倒翁" - the closing description of Chen Ping's later political durability.
