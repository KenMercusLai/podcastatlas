# 026 韦小宝与他的七个老婆：爱情？还是反爱情？

## Summary

This [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] episode asks whether [[WeiXiaobao|韦小宝]]'s relationships with [[MuJianping|沐剑屏]], [[FangYi|方怡]], [[Shuanger|双儿]], [[ZengRou|曾柔]], [[PrincessJianning|建宁公主]], [[SuQuan|苏荃]], and [[AkeLuDingJi|阿珂]] count as love. It reads the seven relationships as distinct mixtures of youthful affection, sexual awakening, loyalty, hero worship, bodily and temperamental compatibility, practical partnership, beauty, possession, and responsibility rather than as seven interchangeable wives.

The hosts disagree over the verdict. Wei often appraises women through beauty, price, acquisition, and self-display, yet he can also understand coercive circumstances, respect a partner's boundary, provide long-term protection, and refuse abandonment. The episode therefore treats [[LuDingJi|《鹿鼎记》]] as an [[IdealizedLoveDeconstruction|idealized-love deconstruction]]: not proof that love is unreal, but a refusal to isolate it from sex, power, survival, household labor, pregnancy, nonexclusivity, and everyday coexistence.

## Key Claims

- Wei's upbringing in a brothel reduces his attachment to status and chastity norms while also normalizing transactional language and the appraisal of women as possessions or priced objects.
- [[MuJianping|沐剑屏]] represents uncomplicated youthful affection, while [[FangYi|方怡]] turns attraction into a lesson about deception under coercion and the need to understand survival pressure.
- [[Shuanger|双儿]] develops from gifted servant and grateful protector into the relationship closest to durable love because loyalty, shared danger, trust, principle, and respect for limits coexist.
- [[PrincessJianning|建宁公主]] has the clearest courtship and everyday marital compatibility with Wei; play, conflict, sexuality, and similar temperaments give the relationship unusual lived texture.
- [[SuQuan|苏荃]] functions as a mature strategic partner and household leader, while [[ZengRou|曾柔]] begins from hero worship and remains comparatively underdeveloped.
- [[AkeLuDingJi|阿珂]] is Wei's most intense beauty-centered fixation, but acquisition, valuation, coercive circumstances, pregnancy, and an underwritten change of mind make the relationship the episode's strongest challenge to romantic interpretation.
- The comparison with [[DuanZhengchun|段正淳]] separates expressive depth and willingness to die from continuous responsibility: romantic intensity and practical care do not always coincide.
- The novel's ending offers worldly marital success without proving spiritual fulfillment, exclusivity, equality, or a single ideal love.
