# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1056丨袁绍死后，他的儿子们直接反目成仇？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode fills the Jian'an 7 bridge between the post-Guandu decline of [[YuanShao|袁绍]] and the later [[YuanTan|袁谭]]-[[YuanShang|袁尚]] civil conflict. It shows Yuan Shao dying without a public successor, [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]] and [[FengJiLateHan|逢纪]] using a claimed last order to install Yuan Shang, Yuan Tan's resentment turning violent at [[LiyangYellowRiverLateHan|黎阳]], and [[CaoCao|曹操]] exploiting the unstable Yuan-family field. The western branch sets up [[GuoYuanLateHan|郭援 / 郭元]]'s Hedong campaign, [[JiaKuiLateHan|贾逵]]'s defense of Jiang County, [[ZhuGongdaoLateHan|祝公道]]'s rescue, and [[FuGan|傅干]] persuading [[MaTeng|马腾]] to help [[ZhongYao|钟繇]] rather than join Yuan Shang.

## Key Claims
- Yuan Shao's death after Guandu and Cangting leaves the Hebei bloc damaged but not immediately destroyed; the decisive internal problem is the missing public succession settlement.
- Yuan Shao privately favors Yuan Shang, influenced by his later wife Liu's preference, but does not clearly name him; sending Yuan Tan, Yuan Xi, and [[GaoGanLateHan|高干]] to provincial posts gives potential claimants their own bases.
- [[JuShou|沮授]] warns that succession must be explicit, making this episode a direct case of [[SuccessionNonDesignationRisk|未定继承人风险]].
- Shen Pei and Feng Ji, fearing retaliation from Yuan Tan's camp, use a claimed Yuan Shao last order to install Yuan Shang before Yuan Tan can return for mourning.
- Yuan Tan responds by styling himself chariots-and-cavalry general, holding Liyang, asking for more troops, and killing Feng Ji after Shen Pei's side refuses the request.
- Cao Cao's attack temporarily forces Yuan Tan and Yuan Shang to combine, giving the precondition later abstracted as [[PressureReleaseInfightingStrategy|撤压诱发内斗]].
- Yuan Shang tries to open a western pressure line by sending Guo Yuan to join Gao Gan and the [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] while also courting Ma Teng.
- Guo Yuan's treatment of Jia Kui at Jiang County shows coercive battlefield success weakening political trust, while local people and officials protect Jia Kui as a [[PeopleBasedPoliticalSecurity|民心型政治安全]] case.
- Fu Gan reframes Ma Teng's choice as a legitimacy and survival problem: helping Zhong Yao attack Guo Yuan is safer than waiting between Cao Cao and the Yuan-family coalition.

## Key Quotes
> "继承归属必须明确" - Ju Shou's warning as summarized by the episode.

> "顺德者昌" / "逆德者亡" - Fu Gan's moralized frame for Ma Teng's choice between Cao Cao and the Yuan-family side.
