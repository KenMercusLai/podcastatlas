# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》893丨荣誉榜如何变成死亡名单？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens with a source-scoped body-practice and aging reflection, then turns to the 169 CE second party-prohibition disaster under [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]. Its core historical claim is that a士人 reputation list originally meant to honor worthy people became a target list once eunuch power treated public moral prestige as organized political danger.

The episode centers on [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]], [[FanPangLateHan|范滂]], [[ZhangJianLateHan|张俭]], [[HouLanLateHan|侯览]], [[ZhuBiLateHan|朱璧]], and [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]]. Hou Lan's grievance moves through Zhu Bi's accusation against Zhang Jian, while Cao Jie explains "party people" to the young emperor as possible rebels; Li Ying and Fan Pang then choose prison and death-facing responsibility rather than flight, turning名节 into both witness and vulnerability.

## Key Claims

- The opening advice about aging, walking, seated practice, and sleep posture is a framing segment rather than the episode's main historical contribution.
- After the first party-prohibition disaster,士人 do not simply submit; they use public praise, mutual recommendation, and moral reputation as a non-military field of contest with eunuch power.
- A "贤达榜" that marks worthy scholars becomes dangerous because the same visibility that grants honor also gives hostile power a list of people to isolate, arrest, or kill.
- After [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] and [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] briefly revive Li Ying and other士人, their own deaths allow the eunuch side to remove those figures again.
- [[HouLanLateHan|侯览]] uses [[ZhuBiLateHan|朱璧]]'s accusation against [[ZhangJianLateHan|张俭]] and others, turning local resentment into a court command to arrest.
- [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] turns the vague "party" label into a ruler-security threat by telling the fourteen-year-old [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] that the party network may overthrow the court and emperor.
- [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]] refuses escape, enters prison, dies under torture, and leaves his students and former subordinates prohibited from office.
- [[JingYiLateHan|景毅]] self-reports because his son had studied with Li Ying but was missing from the arrest list, making omitted liability feel like a failure of名节 rather than a rescue.
- [[FanPangLateHan|范滂]] recognizes the arrest order, voluntarily goes to prison, refuses [[GuoYiLateHan|郭揖]]'s escape plan to avoid implicating him, and uses his farewell to his mother and son to condemn a court where doing no evil still ends in death.

## Key Quotes

> "侍奉君王不辞艰难、犯罪不逃避刑罚" - Li Ying's refusal-to-flee ethic as summarized in the source.

> "一个人既已得到美名，就不能奢望长寿，两者不可兼得。" - Fan Pang's mother on the tradeoff between reputation and long life.

> "想让你做坏事，坏事不可以做；想让你做好事，可我没有做坏事却落得如此下场。" - Fan Pang's final accusation against moral inversion in the court.
