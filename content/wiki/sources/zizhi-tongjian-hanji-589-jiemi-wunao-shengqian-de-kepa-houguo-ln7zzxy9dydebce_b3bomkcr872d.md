# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》589丨揭秘无脑省钱的可怕后果

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianping 4 / 3 BCE under [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]], when a [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] chanyu asks to come to Chang'an for court audience and Aidi initially accepts advice to refuse because reception would cost money and grain. [[YangXiong|扬雄]] objects that refusing a formally submissive chanyu would deepen suspicion and endanger the long Han-Xiongnu balance built through war, heqin, submission management, and gifts.

The durable synthesis is that fiscal restraint can become strategically reckless when it treats a visible expense as waste while ignoring the risk that diplomatic trust, frontier security, and past military investment may unravel. The episode uses Qin and Han precedents from [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]], [[MengTian|蒙恬]], [[LiuBang|刘邦]], [[MaoDunChanyu|冒顿单于]], [[LiuHeng|汉文帝]], [[HanWudi|汉武帝]], [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]], Huo Qubing, [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]], [[Wusun|乌孙]], and the older Western Han Huhanye chanyu to argue that the cost of keeping Xiongnu relations stable must be compared with the much larger cost of renewed war.

## Key Claims

- Han Aidi's first response shows a short-term fiscal frame: ministers treat the chanyu visit mainly as avoidable spending on reception, food, and gifts.
- Yang Xiong reframes the request as a trust and security problem, warning that refusal can turn a submissive counterpart toward suspicion and resentment.
- The episode's historical chain presents Xiongnu as a durable frontier threat from Qin through early and middle Western Han, not a minor visitor whose feelings can be ignored.
- Past military campaigns against Xiongnu required enormous expenditure, mass mobilization, and high risk, so diplomatic maintenance is not automatically wasteful.
- Heqin, reception, equal guest treatment, and continued gifts after Xiongnu submission are presented as ongoing costs of keeping a dangerous frontier relationship from relapsing.
- The host's "mindless saving" frame distinguishes prudent thrift from cuts that ignore second-order consequences for state security and credibility.
- The source leaves the deeper question of why Xiongnu was uniquely dangerous to Han for the following episode.

## Key Quotes

> "六经之治贵在未断，兵家之胜贵在未战" - Yang Xiong's frame for preventing rupture before open conflict.

> "五帝所不能臣，三王所不能制" - Yang Xiong's warning that Xiongnu cannot be treated as an ordinary weak dependency.

> "一劳永逸" - the source's phrase for costly war spending justified by a hoped-for durable peace.
