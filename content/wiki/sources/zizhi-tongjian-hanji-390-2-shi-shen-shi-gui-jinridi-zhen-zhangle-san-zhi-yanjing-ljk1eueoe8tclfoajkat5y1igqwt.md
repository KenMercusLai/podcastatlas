# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》390-2｜是神是鬼？金日磾真长了三只眼睛？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode explains why [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] trusted [[JinMidiWesternHan|金日磾]]: as a [[Xiongnu|匈奴]]-origin official without deep Central Plains court roots, Jin is portrayed as less entangled in local interests and therefore less likely to rebel. The episode then connects Jin's detection of a palace plot by 马何罗 to the folk image of [[MaWangye|马王爷]] and the "third eye," treating the legend as a metaphor for cautious proximity and political alertness rather than a literal anatomical claim.

The second half backfills the succession field after [[LiuJuCrownPrinceWesternHan|卫太子刘据]]'s death. [[LiuDanYanKingWesternHan|燕王刘旦]] believes seniority should make him crown prince, requests entry to Chang'an, is punished by Han Wudi, and later questions whether [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|汉昭帝刘弗陵]] is truly Wudi's son; [[LiuXuGuanglingKingWesternHan|广陵王刘须]] is also excluded because the source presents his conduct as politically and morally unfit.

## Key Claims

- Han Wudi's trust in Jin Midi is explained through political isolation: Jin's Xiongnu origin and lack of local court foundations make betrayal less useful and less likely.
- Jin's "third eye" is a later mythic reading of practical vigilance: the episode says careful, close service can make someone seem able to see hidden danger.
- The episode links Jin to Ma Wangye folklore, where a three-eyed horse deity recognizes malicious intent and punishes evil.
- Liu Dan's crown-prince ambition predates Wudi's death; he reads the deaths of Liu Ju and other possible heirs as opening a seniority-based claim.
- Han Wudi rejects Liu Dan's bid for proximity, kills the messenger, and cuts part of Yan's territory, signaling that Liu Dan is not a viable heir.
- Liu Xu is also ruled out because the episode presents him as violating court norms and living in an indulgent, disorderly style.
- After Wudi's death, Liu Dan attacks Han Zhao's legitimacy by claiming that the installed ruler is not Han Wudi's biological son.

## Key Quotes

> "最不可能谋反" - the episode's compact explanation for Jin Midi's trust value.

> "谨慎到一定程度，就会显得无所不知" - the source's interpretation of the "third eye" motif.
