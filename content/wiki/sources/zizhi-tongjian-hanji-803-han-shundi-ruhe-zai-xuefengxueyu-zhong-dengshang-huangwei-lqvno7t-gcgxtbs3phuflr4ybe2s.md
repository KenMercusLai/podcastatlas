# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》803丨汉顺帝如何在腥风血雨中登上皇位？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 125 CE succession crisis immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-804-gei-ni-tang-de-ren-bu-yiding-shi-guiren-lqongdqg-hgetoegpylv-os86buh|Hanji 804]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-805-tongtou-renxing-de-nvren-jing-neng-yuce-jixiong-ll3bzoyrkbtnvt-aqu9nqelqonew|Hanji 805]]. It follows [[BanYongLateHan|班勇]]'s Western Regions victory, [[LiuYiBeixiangHouLateHan|北乡侯刘裔]]'s illness and death, [[EmpressDowagerYanLateHan|阎太后]]'s concealed-death succession attempt, and [[SunChengLateHan|孙程]]'s eunuch coup that restores [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝 / Liu Bao]].

The durable synthesis is that Shun's accession is not a smooth correction of legitimacy but a race to control information, gates, seals, troops, and palace bodies before the Yan outer-relative side can choose another child ruler. The coup succeeds because Sun Cheng's group reaches the commitment point first, forces [[LiRunLateHan|李润]] into cooperation, prevents Yan Taihou from communicating outward, and survives the attempted counter-mobilization through [[FengShiLateHan|冯师]]'s refusal to commit and [[GuoZhenLateHan|郭镇]]'s capture of Yan Jin.

## Key Claims

- The opening self-cultivation frame treats endurance and waiting for the right counterattack moment as source-scoped listener advice, preparing the restoration story around Liu Bao's earlier displacement.
- In 125 CE, Ban Yong mobilizes Dunhuang, Zhangye, Jiuquan, Shule, Shanshan, and Cheshi-Qian forces against [[CheshiHouKingdomLateHan|车师后王国]], kills thousands, captures King Junjiu and a [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] envoy, and executes them at Suo Ban's death site.
- After Liu Yi / Beixiang Hou becomes gravely ill, Sun Cheng tells Changxing Xu that Liu Bao was the prior emperor's legitimate son and had been deposed through slander, then proposes killing Jiang Jin and [[YanXianLateHan|阎显]] if Liu Yi cannot recover.
- Yan Taihou and the Yan side also understand the succession race: after Liu Yi dies on 125-10-27, they conceal the death, summon royal descendants to Luoyang, close palace gates, station troops, and raise security.
- On 125-11-02, Sun Cheng, [[WangKangLateHan|王康]], [[WangGuoEunuchLateHan|王国]], Huang Long, Peng Kai, Meng Shu, Li Jian, and other eunuchs swear at the West Bell Tower and launch the palace coup.
- At Zhangtai Gate, Sun Cheng and Wang Kang kill Jiang Jin, Liu An, and Chen Da as Yan-side loyalists, then put a blade to Li Run's neck and force him to join the restoration of Liu Bao.
- Liu Bao is brought to the West Bell and enthroned as the eleven-year-old Jiyin king who becomes Emperor Shun; Sun Cheng's side summons shangshu officials, enters the South Palace, controls forbidden gates, and cuts off Yan Taihou's communication.
- Yan Xian tries to counterattack through Yan Taihou's authority, Feng Shi, Yan Chong, and bounties for capturing Liu Bao or Li Run, but Feng Shi kills the messenger Fan Deng and stays in camp rather than joining.
- Guo Zhen leaves sickbed, leads Yulin guards, announces the edict, and captures Yan Jin at Shengde Gate, making the Yan counter-move collapse before dawn.
- Sun Cheng's side forces Yan Taihou to surrender the imperial seal and moves her to Changle Palace; Yan Xian, Yan Yao, and Yan Yan are killed, while the wider Yan household is exiled to Bijiang.

## Key Quotes

> "懂得蛰伏的人值得敬畏" - opening frame for endurance before reversal.

> "密不发丧" - Yan Taihou's concealment of Liu Yi's death during the succession race.

> "封万户侯" - bounty promise for anyone who captured Jiyin Wang during the Yan counterattack.
