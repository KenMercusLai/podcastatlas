# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》856丨历史告诉你：人若心乱，阵脚自乱

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 162 CE [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] field between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-852-dang-meinv-ruyun-shi-fu-haishi-huo-luml30ytbc-awpvk-7-23ehzbqpx|Hanji 852]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-857-xuehui-gongying-cai-shi-zhenzheng-de-yingjia-lh3zx6pw-4ib7owiz8c68bv-9htn|Hanji 857]]. It pairs [[HuangfuGuiLateHan|皇甫规]]'s Qiang-front stabilization and corrupt-official impeachment with a southern [[Jingzhou|荆州]] crisis where [[MaMuLateHan|马睦]], [[LiuDuLateHan|刘度]], and [[LiSuNanCommanderyLateHan|李肃]] collapse under pressure while [[HuShuangLateHan|胡爽]] dies trying to hold official duty in place.

The durable contribution is a contrast between stabilizing the inside before confronting the outside and letting fear become organizational collapse. Huangfu Gui enters diseased camps and restores soldier trust before Qiang envoys surrender, while [[FengGunLateHan|冯绲]] later anticipates eunuch financial accusation by requesting supervision, brings [[YingFengLateHan|应奉]] into the campaign, pacifies the Wuling branch, refuses a large reward, and credits Ying Feng.

## Key Claims

- In 162 CE, imperial fires, earthquake, Qiang raids, Changsha/Lingling/Wuling unrest, and southern commandery failures make Huan's state look unstable across court, frontier, and province.
- Huangfu Gui's first achievement in the Qiang campaign is internal stabilization: despite road isolation and epidemic losses, he personally visits camps and restores morale.
- Huangfu Gui treats frontier rebellion as partly a governance failure by impeaching corrupt, violent, and incompetent officials before large Qiang surrender follows.
- The southern rebellion branch shows command panic spreading downward: Ma Mu and Liu Du flee after defeat, and Li Su abandons Nan Commandery's duty while Wuling Man forces press Jiangling.
- Hu Shuang's remonstrance defines the ethical counterpoint: an official responsible for connected cities should raise flags and drums to assemble aid, not abandon the seal and become a fugitive.
- Li Su's killing of Hu Shuang turns fear from dereliction into violence against corrective advice, prompting execution in the marketplace and punishment of Ma Mu and Liu Du.
- Feng Gun's campaign shows capable crisis command under court-political suspicion: he asks for eunuch financial monitoring to avoid later accusation, uses Ying Feng's local knowledge, and pacifies Jingzhou.
- Feng Gun's refusal of reward and crediting of Ying Feng extends the episode's theme from self-command in battle to self-protection and merit allocation after victory.
- The closing Qiang raids and Huangfu Gui's return set up Hanji 857's accusation that his surrender policy was bribery or staged submission.

## Key Quotes

> "军中又流行瘟疫" - the source's crisis setting before Huangfu Gui stabilizes the camps.

> "怎能抛弃守护一方安宁的重任" - Hu Shuang's duty argument as rendered by the episode.

> "人若心乱，阵脚自乱" - title-level lesson connecting fear to organizational collapse.
