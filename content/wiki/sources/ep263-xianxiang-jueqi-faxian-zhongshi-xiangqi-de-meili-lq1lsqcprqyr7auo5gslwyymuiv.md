# EP263 线香崛起：发现中式香气的魅力

## Summary
This [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] episode with [[QiuLian|邱连]], [[XuePeng|薛鹏]], and [[WuLiwei|吴立伟]] turns the recent visibility of line incense into a broader map of [[ChineseFragrance|中式香气]], fragrance branding, and [[Agarwood|沉香]] supply. It argues that [[LineIncenseDailyLife|线香日常化]] lowers the threshold from older 香席, 品香, and literati practice into reading, meditation, sleep, and home decompression. The strongest wiki contribution is the connection between aesthetic revival and material conditions: [[AgarwoodIndustrialization|沉香产业化]], [[Qinan|奇楠]] grafting, formula reconstruction, retail storytelling, and consumer taste all have to line up before "中式香气" becomes a scalable contemporary product category.

## Key Claims
- The source distinguishes earlier high-threshold incense culture from today's [[LineIncenseDailyLife|line incense daily-life]] use: contemporary consumers are often looking for private ritual, calm, and atmosphere rather than formal 香道 mastery.
- [[ChineseFragrance|中式香气]] is not a single ingredient. It combines familiar smells, understated expression, cultural memory, product naming, scene design, and materials such as [[Osmanthus|桂花]], [[Agarwood|沉香]], sandalwood, musk accords, and green plant notes.
- 国产原创香水 and line-incense brands are moving from simple smell restoration toward situation, philosophy, life experience, and "耐人寻味" expression.
- [[Osmanthus|桂花]] works as a Chinese-fragrance case because Chinese brands attach season, food, hometown, 桂花糕, 酒酿, and 满陇桂雨 memory to the smell rather than treating it only as a fruity floral note.
- [[IncenseFormulaReconstruction|古方复刻]] is presented as research, not mechanical recipe copying: "鹅梨帐中香" requires comparing texts, testing craft feasibility, and judging whether a reconstructed smell fits the intended poetic scene.
- [[Agarwood|沉香]] is not ordinary fragrant wood. It forms when the tree responds to injury and microbial or fungal invasion by producing resin around the wound.
- Wild agarwood scarcity and protection pushed the industry away from a collection economy and toward plantation production, especially around Guangdong Dianbai and Hainan-related aroma reputation.
- [[Qinan|奇楠]] grafting and artificial结香 success around 2015-2020 helped lower raw-material cost and made agarwood more available for line incense, oil, and daily products.
- The agarwood value chain moves from larger pieces for beads, to leftover material for incense powder, and then to residual powder for essential oil, so "natural" oil is not automatically the premium center of the chain.
- Single-material incense and blended incense serve different markets: single-origin-style agarwood tests raw-material quality, while blended incense extends traditional香谱 and creates broader taste, cost, and scenario flexibility.
- [[FragranceProductStorytelling|香气产品叙事]] matters because smell is hard to evaluate through text or e-commerce alone; packaging, shops, Xiaohongshu lifestyle content, names, and stories become part of the product.
- [[ScentAsBrandMemory|空间气味记忆]] is treated as a brand tool for hotels, cultural spaces, museums, and retail environments, extending fragrance from personal object into commercial atmosphere.
