# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》888丨乱世之中，致敬不怕死的人

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate 168 CE aftermath of the failed [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] and [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] anti-eunuch crisis before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-889-weishenme-haoren-bu-changming-huohai-yi-qiannian-lt-g7qgkcf-fhy3xccecjagwqhku|Hanji 889]]. It opens with a source-scoped contrast between君子 and小人, then moves into the pursuit, rewards, and cleanup after [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] and the eunuch side have won.

The historical center is [[DeathFacingResponsibility|赴死式责任承担]] under political terror. [[BaSuLateHan|巴肃]] refuses escape and accepts execution; [[ZhuZhenLateHan|朱震]] buries Chen Fan and protects [[ChenYiLateHan|陈逸]]; [[HuTengLateHan|胡腾]] buries Dou Wu and hides Dou's young grandson, showing that responsibility can take the form of self-surrender, funeral honor, orphan protection, and household risk rather than only direct remonstrance.

## Key Claims

- The opening君子/小人 frame treats self-reflection, respect for difference, and adult boundaries as part of moral conduct, while warning that kindness without self-protection can be exploited.
- In 168 CE, [[BaSuLateHan|Ba Su]] had participated in Dou Wu's plan but was not caught in the first discovery; he was initially barred from office and later faced arrest.
- When the arrest order arrives, Ba Su voluntarily goes to the county court; even after the magistrate offers to flee with him, he refuses flight and is executed.
- [[CaoJieLateHan|Cao Jie]] receives promotion and a marquisate, [[WangFuLateHan|Wang Fu]] continues as zhongchangshi and huangmenling, and [[ZhuYuLateHan|Zhu Yu]] is among the rewarded figures, making the winning faction's reward structure visible.
- [[ZhuZhenLateHan|Zhu Zhen]] risks his household to collect and bury Chen Fan's body and hide Chen Fan's son Chen Yi; even after imprisonment and torture, the family keeps the secret.
- [[HuTengLateHan|Hu Teng]] publicly mourns and buries Dou Wu, loses office eligibility, then shelters Dou Wu's two-year-old grandson as his own son and hides him in [[LinglingCommanderyLateHan|Lingling Commandery]] with help from Zhang Chang.
- The closing discussion of奸臣 and小人 outcomes reinforces [[MoralDesertPoliticalOutcomeGap|moral-outcome mismatch]]: the episode honors courageous actors without claiming history reliably punishes the powerful or malicious.

## Key Quotes

> "君子求诸己，小人求诸人" - opening distinction between self-demand and blaming others.

> "不伤害别人，但也不能委屈自己" - boundary-setting version of kindness.

> "留得青山在，何愁没柴烧" - survival alternative raised against Ba Su's death-facing choice.
