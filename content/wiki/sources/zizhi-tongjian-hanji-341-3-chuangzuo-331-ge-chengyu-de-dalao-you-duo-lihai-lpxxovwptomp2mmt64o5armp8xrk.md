# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》341-3｜创作331个成语的大佬，有多厉害？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode presents [[HanYu|韩愈]] as a Tang writer and official whose repeated examination failures, demotions, and personal losses did not displace his commitment to writing, remonstrance, or local administration. It moves from *Shishuo* and a source-attributed claim that Han left 331 idioms to his criticism of local extraction, his 819 memorial against welcoming the Buddha's relic, and his work in [[Chaozhou|潮州]], using the life as an example of [[ConscienceThroughDuty|尽责问心无愧]] under outcomes he could not control.

## Key Claims

- The episode treats Han Yu's literary afterlife through *Shishuo* and a claim that 331 idioms are attributable to his writings, including familiar expressions such as “轩然大波” and “不得而知.”
- Han Yu is presented as an orphan who persisted through repeated examination failure before entering office, making later fame compatible with prolonged early frustration rather than uninterrupted success.
- After reporting disaster suffering, official concealment, and excessive extraction, Han is said to have offended both implicated officials and Emperor Dezong and to have been demoted to Yangshan.
- In 819, Han's *Memorial on the Buddha's Relic* openly opposed Emperor Xianzong's relic-welcoming campaign; Pei Du and Cui Qun reportedly helped prevent execution, but Han was rapidly demoted to Chaozhou.
- The episode uses the journey south, the death of Han's fourth daughter, and the line “云横秦岭家何在，雪拥蓝关马不前” to keep the personal cost of principled speech visible.
- Han's Chaozhou service is framed as continuity of public duty after political punishment: the episode emphasizes practical work and narrates the *Sacrificial Text to the Crocodiles* as an attempt to protect local livelihoods.
- The closing judgment defines success less by an unbroken career than by literary contribution, willingness to confront power, responsibility in unwanted office, and conduct one can regard as free of self-betrayal.

## Key Quotes

> “云横秦岭家何在，雪拥蓝关马不前” — verse used by the episode to mark the hardship of Han Yu's journey after demotion.

> “只求无愧于心” — the episode's closing standard for interpreting Han Yu's life.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with existing wiki content is adopted. The episode extends the wiki's duty-under-constraint branch by adding a Tang case where demotion does not end public service.
- The supplied document is a narrative episode summary rather than a critical edition or primary-source study. The total of 331 idioms, attribution of individual idioms, examination count and sequence, official dates, “八千里,” the fourth daughter's death circumstances, exact speeches, and the claim that crocodile harm ceased for ten years remain source-scoped.
- The crocodile episode may carry literary, ritual, administrative, and legendary layers; this source does not establish that reciting the text caused any later ecological change.
- “朝奏夕贬” is retained as the episode's compressed account of rapid punishment, not treated as a precise administrative timestamp without further documentation.
