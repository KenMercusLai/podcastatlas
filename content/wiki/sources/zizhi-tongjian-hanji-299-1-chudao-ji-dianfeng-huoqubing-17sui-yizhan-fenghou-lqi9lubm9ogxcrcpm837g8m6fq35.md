# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》299-1｜出道即巅峰，霍去病17岁一战封侯

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the first-command sequence opened in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-298-shaonian-zhanshen-huoqubing-dengchang-lhwmfj24jl0ywwpcknytor9ujksh|Hanji 298]]. It says the seventeen-year-old [[HuoQubing|霍去病]] took eight hundred selected cavalry away from [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]]'s main force, used captive intelligence and rapid movement to locate a high-value [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] target, and returned with a result that led [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] to enfeoff him as Champion Marquis.

The durable addition is the joined preparation-to-execution chain now captured in [[FirstHuoQubingRaidWesternHan|霍去病首次八百骑兵突袭]]: opponent knowledge matters only when it can guide movement, target selection, surprise, and disciplined action. The episode's speeches, troop psychology, “iron cavalry special-operations team” label, exact route and losses, camp details, casualty total, captive identities, and claim of near-perfect freedom inside Xiongnu territory remain source-scoped dramatic or numerical claims.

## Key Claims

- Huo Qubing's eight hundred selected cavalry initially distrust a seventeen-year-old commander whom they associate with kinship privilege rather than demonstrated merit.
- The episode gives Huo a pre-departure speech that combines elite identity, voluntary withdrawal before departure, the absence of rear supply, and severe discipline after commitment.
- The detachment reportedly leaves the main army, advances several hundred li into Xiongnu territory, and uses previously studied geography to find multiple camps.
- Captive intelligence is said to identify a nearby gathering place for Xiongnu elites, which Huo turns into a high-value surprise target.
- The episode presents speed, intelligence, surprise, local consumption of livestock and dairy, and attack on elite personnel as the core of the raid.
- It reports more than two thousand killed or captured, including senior people and relatives of the chanyu, at very small Han cost.
- Han Wudi is said to reward the result by enfeoffing the seventeen-year-old Huo as Champion Marquis, making the raid the first demonstrated outcome after Hanji 298's preparation account.

## Key Quotes

> “刀在手，跟我走，砍完匈奴喝马酒。” - the host's theatrical compression of the attributed departure speech, not a verified historical quotation.

> “冠军侯。” - the title that makes the episode's reported battlefield result a political and biographical turning point.
