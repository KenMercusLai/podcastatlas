# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》464｜古代的“春宫图”到底有多野？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode uses the removal of [[LiuHaiyangGuangchuanKingWesternHan|广川王刘海阳]] in Ganlu 4 / 50 BCE as an entry into the history attributed to Chinese erotic imagery. It argues that images later grouped under “春宫图” should not be read only as pornography: in the episode's account they also circulated as sexual instruction, marriage material, fertility wishes, protective objects, tomb imagery, political warnings about excess, and collectible art.

The durable synthesis is methodological rather than a verified chronology of individual works. [[AncientChineseEroticImagery|中国古代春宫图的社会功能]] changed with setting and use: the same broad image category could teach, bless, memorialize, entertain, condemn, or become forbidden print and later museum evidence. The episode's attributions to [[ZhouFangTangPainter|周昉]] and [[TangYinMingPainter|唐寅]], the naming genealogy of “春宫图,” and several object histories remain source-scoped because the transcript does not supply independent documentation.

## Key Claims

- The episode defines 春宫图 as sexually themed imagery, also calling it 春工画, 密戏图, or 春画, while arguing against treating the category as only erotic entertainment.
- It attributes instructional, marital, fertility, fire-averting, book-protecting, and popular-belief functions to such images, linking them to 房中术 and the `素女经` tradition.
- In Ganlu 4 / 50 BCE, Liu Haiyang is removed as Guangchuan king and sent to Fangling after accusations involving killing, incestuous coercion, and a banquet staged around explicit images.
- Eastern Han tomb images of embracing couples are interpreted as memories of married life and wishes for conjugal unity, descendants, and lineage continuity.
- The episode says [[ZhouFangTangPainter|周昉]]'s lost `春宵密戏图` moved the subject into an inner-palace setting and helped establish the later “春宫图” label.
- It presents [[TangYinMingPainter|唐寅]] as a high point of Ming erotic painting and says a twenty-four-image group later called `风流绝唱图` was placed on a Qing prohibited-books list.
- The closing museum frame treats formerly private or prohibited images as evidence for social custom and art history.

## Key Quotes

> “不能单纯带着情色眼光看待春宫图。” - the episode's central interpretive instruction.

> “昔日藏在柜子里的春宫图走进了博物馆。” - the closing shift from private object to historical exhibit.
