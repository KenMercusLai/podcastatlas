# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》407｜在位仅27天，汉废帝刘贺如何被霍光废黜？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the pre-deposition planning layer before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-408-feidi-jie-tianhuaban-han-feidi-liuhe-xiagang-la-lgqd0gddiz5plifsvwteaub2emug|Hanji 408]]'s formal procedure. It follows [[LiuHeHaihunHou|刘贺]] through ignored warnings from the langzhongling, Zhang Chang, [[XiahouShengWesternHan|夏侯胜]], and another attendant, while [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]] moves from disappointment to secret planning with [[TianYannianWesternHan|田延年]] and [[ZhangAnshiWesternHan|张安世]].

The durable synthesis is that Liu He's removal begins before the public indictment. The episode shows failed remonstrance, omen interpretation, historical precedent, elite consent, and coercive meeting management becoming the infrastructure for deposition: Tian Yannian gives Huo Guang the Yi Yin and Taijia precedent, Yang Chang's wife pushes the chancellor toward alignment, and Tian's drawn-sword threat turns silence at the Weiyang Palace meeting into public pressure to join Huo's course.

## Key Claims

- Liu He's early reign is presented as a sequence of ignored warnings: a dream of flies and filth, the langzhongling's Shijing-based criticism of sycophants, Zhang Chang's complaint about promoting Changyi followers, and Xiahou Sheng's omen warning all fail to change his conduct.
- Huo Guang's deposition plan begins as disappointment with the emperor he helped install, then becomes actionable when Tian Yannian advises petitioning the empress dowager to install a worthier ruler.
- Tian Yannian legitimizes the plan by citing Yi Yin's removal and later restoration of Taijia, giving Huo Guang a loyal-minister precedent rather than only a power-politics rationale.
- Xiahou Sheng's cloudy-weather warning accidentally matches the real conspiracy Huo Guang is forming, which makes Huo fear that Zhang Anshi has leaked the plan before Xiahou explains the omen theory from the Hongfan tradition.
- The episode makes Yang Chang's household a consent pivot: Yang Chang is frightened and evasive, while his wife recognizes that Huo Guang has already decided and that hesitation could destroy the family.
- At the Weiyang Palace meeting, Huo Guang asks the assembled officials what to do about Liu He, and Tian Yannian uses a sword threat to force ministers to respond rather than remain silent.
- The episode stops before the formal deposition, making Hanji 407 the tense planning-and-alignment prelude to Hanji 408's dowager authorization and indictment.
