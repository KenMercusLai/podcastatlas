# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》178｜冒顿为何要放过刘邦 这才是真相（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the [[BaidengEncirclement|白登之围]] from [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-177-beikun-baidengshan-liubang-mingxuan-yixian-ljdmxf6bej8ju0gmvm9cp48gy0on|Hanji 177]] by moving from encirclement setup to release. It explains [[ChenPing|陈平]]'s reported secret stratagem through [[YanzhiXiongnuConsort|阏氏]]'s household-status fear, while arguing that [[MaoDunChanyu|冒顿单于]] also had battlefield reasons to open the siege: allied forces failed to appear, Han reinforcements were approaching, and direct conquest of Han territory was not his original goal. The episode then shifts from Liu Bang's escape to [[LouJing|刘敬 / 楼敬]]'s proposed [[HanXiongnuHeqinPolicy|汉匈和亲政策]], making Baideng the hinge between failed frontier war and marriage-based accommodation.

## Key Claims
- Liu Bang's Baideng crisis is severe because the trapped Han ruler and soldiers endure seven days without supplies in extreme cold, with freezing injuries and the possibility of military breakdown.
- The episode treats the historical record of Chen Ping's plan as deliberately opaque: the broad effect is known, but the full method is not recoverable.
- Chen Ping first approaches [[YanzhiXiongnuConsort|阏氏]] rather than Modu directly, using gifts and an image of a Han beauty to turn possible Han submission into her fear of being displaced.
- Yanzhi's advice reframes release as prudent: Xiongnu and Han each have their own ruler, Han territory would be difficult for Modu to govern, and Liu Bang is offering favorable terms.
- The host rejects a simple "pillow talk" explanation. Modu is shown as a ruler who has sacrificed close relationships for power before, so Yanzhi's intervention is only one part of the decision.
- Modu's allied field is unstable because Wang Huang and Zhao Li do not arrive as planned, creating suspicion that they may have reconnected with Han.
- Han reinforcements approaching Pingcheng, Qin-Han military memory, and the difficulty of governing the agrarian interior make killing or holding Liu Bang riskier than the visible Xiongnu advantage suggests.
- Modu's decision to open one side of the encirclement is therefore recorded as [[EncirclementReleaseCalculus|围困开口式风险权衡]], not merely as emotional persuasion.
- Chen Ping's insistence that Liu Bang withdraw calmly with guards and crossbows is part of the escape: panic could invite a Xiongnu reversal, while composure makes the withdrawal look controlled.
- After escaping and rejoining reinforcements, Liu Bang admits he was wrong to reject Liu Jing's warning, releases and rewards him, and punishes envoys who had misread Xiongnu weakness.
- Liu Jing's heqin proposal is presented as a strategic alternative after Baideng: if war cannot quickly solve the Xiongnu problem, a ruling-house marriage may create kinship leverage.
- The episode leaves the internal family question open because the proposal would affect Liu Bang and [[LuZhi|吕雉]]'s legitimate daughter, making frontier policy collide with imperial household stakes.

## Key Quotes
> "其计密，世莫得闻" — the source's boundary around the unrecoverable details of Chen Ping's stratagem.

> "捞一把就撤" — the episode's description of Modu's likely limited objective.

> "越是危机时越要表现得淡定从容" — Chen Ping's retreat advice as the Han army exits the opened encirclement.
