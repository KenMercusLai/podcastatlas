# 猪肉价格经历漫长下跌，为什么「猪周期」不再遵循老节奏？

## Summary
This [[ShengdongZaokafei|声动早咖啡]] episode opens with short updates on [[WeChat]], [[Huawei]], [[Xiaomi]], [[Audi]], tea-drink retail, and then uses low pork prices to explain why China's [[PorkCycle|猪周期]] is changing. The source argues that the current downcycle is being prolonged by [[ChinaPigFarmingScaleConsolidation|规模化养猪]], higher breeding and feeding efficiency, weaker pork-demand growth, and limited downstream or export absorption. Its figures and company/product details come from media, analysts, and official data cited by the episode rather than independent verification.

## Key Claims
- China's current [[PorkCycle|猪周期]] differs from earlier cycles because the price-up phase was short and the low-price phase has lasted longer after late 2022.
- [[ChinaPigFarmingScaleConsolidation|生猪养殖规模化]] weakens the old fast-clearing mechanism: large producers have financing, cost, and operating capacity to keep producing through losses.
- Productivity gains mean sow numbers alone are a weaker supply indicator; fewer breeding sows can coexist with higher pig output if piglets per sow, survival, and slaughter weights rise.
- [[PorkConsumptionSubstitution|猪肉消费替代]] limits demand-side recovery as poultry, beef, seafood, and health-oriented diets take more share from pork.
- [[DownstreamPorkAbsorptionLimits|下游猪肉吸收能力有限]] makes supply pressure hit prices more directly because fresh and chilled pork dominate consumption, while frozen, processed, and export channels absorb less.
- The side news items frame platform, handset, auto, and tea-drink companies as experimenting with AI interaction, foldable-phone timing, China-brand segmentation, and store-hour monetization.
