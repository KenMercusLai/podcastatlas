# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》460｜60岁刺杀亲夫！她是史上最彪悍的和亲公主

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Ganlu 1 / 53 BCE under [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]]. It pairs the [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] civil-war debate in which [[HuhanyeChanyuWesternHan|呼韩邪单于]] accepts submission to Han with the [[Wusun|乌孙]] succession crisis that forces [[XieyouPrincessWesternHan|解忧公主]] into marriage with [[NimiWusun|狂王泥靡]].

The durable synthesis is that Han frontier influence is rising, but it is mediated through vulnerable people and unstable local rules. Huhanye chooses Han-backed survival over steppe prestige, while Xieyou's Wusun marriage diplomacy turns from alliance work into coercive remarriage, domestic violence, assassination planning, and the failed banquet attack that leaves Han envoys and Xieyou besieged at Chigu City.

## Key Claims

- Huhanye's advisers split over whether submission to Han would preserve Xiongnu survival or destroy Xiongnu prestige.
- The Left Jizi King argues from changing power balance: Han is strong, Wusun and other states have leaned toward Han, and refusal may end in extinction.
- Huhanye accepts the realist argument, moves closer to the Han frontier, and sends his son to Chang'an as a hostage; [[ZhizhiChanyu|郅支单于]] also sends a son after hearing this.
- Wusun nobles bypass Xieyou's son Yuanguimi and follow an older succession arrangement that installs Nimi, a son of the former Wusun king and a Xiongnu consort.
- Xieyou is required by Wusun custom to marry Nimi, despite their mother-son status frame and her advanced age in the episode's telling.
- Nimi's violent behavior toward Xieyou and weak standing among Wusun subjects turn a marriage alliance into a succession and governance crisis.
- Xieyou, Han envoys Wei Heyi and Ren Chang, and dissatisfied Wusun forces plot to kill Nimi at a banquet, but the assassin wounds rather than kills him.
- Nimi's son Xishenshou then surrounds Chigu City, turning the failed assassination into a diplomatic and military emergency.

## Key Quotes

> "风水轮流转" - the host's contrast between Liu Bang's Baideng humiliation and Huhanye's later need to submit.

> "强弱形势会随时间变化" - the Left Jizi King's realist argument as summarized by the episode.
