# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》357-1｜张骞死后，西汉外交乱象令人乍舌

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode contrasts [[ZhangQianWesternHan|张骞]]'s reputation-backed opening of Western Regions relations with the loss of control after his death. [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] continues authorizing missions, but permissive recruitment, large delegations, weak provisioning, private appropriation of gifts, and poor-quality exchange goods turn diplomatic expansion into a burden on route states and damage Han credibility.

The durable synthesis is a governance chain rather than a simple clash of civilizations: envoy quality and logistics deteriorate, [[LoulanKingdomWesternHan|楼兰]] and the polity the episode identifies as Gushi/[[CheshiKingdomWesternHan|Cheshi]] resist or raid missions, Xiongnu pressure compounds the danger, and Han answers with cavalry sorties plus Zhangye-Dunhuang commandery and settlement expansion. The episode does not independently establish every date, identity, troop count, motive, or causal link in that chain.

## Key Claims

- Zhang Qian's missions create political and commercial access, and later envoys reportedly use his Bowang Hou title because it retains trust in the Western Regions.
- After Zhang Qian's death in the episode's 114 BCE chronology, Han Wudi broadly approves new mission requests and allows applicants to recruit their own parties.
- Mission frequency, group size, long travel times, weak screening, and inadequate provisioning create corruption and internal conflict as well as local burden.
- Some envoys appropriate or sell court gifts, make unreliable claims, and carry inferior goods, causing Western Regions communities to move from welcome toward distrust and refusal of supplies.
- Loulan and Gushi/Cheshi become corridor chokepoints where Han missions face obstruction and raids, sometimes alongside Xiongnu cavalry.
- In 111 BCE, the episode says [[GongsunHeWesternHan|公孙贺]] and [[ZhaoPonuWesternHan|赵破奴]] lead long-range cavalry sorties that fail to meet the raiders.
- Han then strengthens the northwest through Zhangye and Dunhuang commanderies and population transfer, shifting from mission expansion toward territorial and logistical support.

## Key Quotes

> “博望侯” - the trusted title later envoys reportedly borrow from Zhang Qian's reputation.

> “没有强大军事支撑的外交和商贸不可能长久” - the episode's explicit capacity claim linking exchange to route security.
