## Summary
This [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] interview has [[LuoYonghao|罗永浩]] talking with [[ZhuJiangming|朱江明]] about the path from Yiwu upbringing, electronics training, and [[DahuaTechnology|大华]] entrepreneurship into [[Leapmotor|零跑汽车]]. The episode treats Leapmotor as an engineering-led EV maker built around [[LeapmotorFullStackSelfResearch|全域自研]], manufacturing cost discipline, and a recovery arc from [[LeapmotorS01|S01]] product-definition failure to [[LeapmotorT03|T03]], [[LeapmotorC11|C11]], and [[LeapmotorD99|D99]]. It also frames [[LeapmotorInternational|零跑国际]] with [[Stellantis]] as a reverse joint-venture route for Chinese EV globalization.

## Key Claims
- [[ZhuJiangming|Zhu Jiangming]]'s founder path is presented as cumulative: early electronics hobby work, school-factory product training, [[DahuaTechnology|Dahua]]'s embedded DVR breakthrough, and set-top-box management prepared him for a second, heavier hardware company.
- [[Leapmotor|Leapmotor]] began with severe auto-industry and capital underestimation; Zhu says he initially thought several billion RMB would be enough and later concluded that roughly RMB 20 billion scale funding was needed.
- Leapmotor's operating thesis is that [[LeapmotorFullStackSelfResearch|full-stack vehicle self-research]], self-manufacturing, platform reuse, and supplier-margin compression can make high configuration possible at lower prices.
- The product arc runs from [[LeapmotorS01|S01]] as a wrong two-door coupe definition to [[LeapmotorT03|T03]] volume recovery and [[LeapmotorC11|C11]] as the key mid-price turning point during a funding squeeze.
- The episode positions [[LeapmotorD99|D99]] and the planned second brand as attempts to stretch Leapmotor beyond value-for-money cars into RMB 300,000 and mobile-living-space categories.
- [[Stellantis]]'s investment and the [[LeapmotorInternational|Leapmotor International]] joint venture are framed as an overseas expansion path where Chinese EV technology trades partial control for channels, service, finance, insurance, and local operating capacity.
- Zhu's source-scoped goal is world-class automaker status, with 4 million annual sales treated as the entry threshold and trade barriers treated as the largest long-run globalization risk.

## Key Quotes
> "无知者无畏" - Zhu's description of starting an auto company before fully understanding the capital and operational difficulty.

> "车界的优衣库" - Luo and Zhu's shorthand for Leapmotor's low-premium, quality-for-price positioning.

> "移动空间" - Zhu's description of the planned second brand's direction beyond ordinary commuting.
