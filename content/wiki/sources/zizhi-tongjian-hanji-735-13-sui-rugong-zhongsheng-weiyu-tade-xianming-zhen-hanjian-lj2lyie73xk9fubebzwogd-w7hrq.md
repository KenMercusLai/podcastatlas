## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Yongping 3 / 60 CE before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-736-shengzai-huangquan-xia-ta-pingshenme-neng-andu-wannian-lisegz1ia5najewghxaqirkafvup|Hanji 736]]. It centers [[EmpressMaMingdeEasternHan|明德马皇后]]'s selection as empress, raising of [[EmperorZhangOfHan|刘炟 / 汉章帝]], broad treatment of palace women, plain dress, learning, and refusal to seek family privilege, then contrasts that restraint with [[EmperorMingOfHan|汉明帝]]'s stopped North Palace construction, harsh temper, omen self-warning, and visible rewards for capable local officials.

The durable synthesis is that early Yongping order depends on checks that make virtue operational: Empress Ma stabilizes palace succession without turning motherhood or favor into private extraction, [[ZhongliYiEasternHan|钟离意]] turns celestial-warning language into a halt to people-burdening construction, [[LiuPingEasternHan|刘平]] and [[GuoHeEasternHan|郭贺]] show local trust as a public-governance asset, and [[YaoSongEasternHan|姚松]] exposes a small ritual check on Mingdi's punitive anger. The Western Regions closing gives an earlier [[ShacheKingdomLateHan|Shache]]-[[KhotanKingdomLateHan|Khotan]] layer before [[KhotanKingGuangdeLateHan|Guangde]]'s later king-making in Hanji 736.

## Key Claims

- Empress Ma's authority begins before Zhangdi's reign: she enters the palace young, raises Jia Guiren's son Liu Da as crown prince, treats other palace women generously, stays frugal and learned, and avoids private favor for her natal family.
- Mingdi's Yongping court shows both responsiveness and punitive risk: he can accept Zhongli Yi's construction remonstrance and issue eclipse self-warning, but his harsh temper and surveillance habit still frighten officials.
- Zhongli Yi converts the Tianchuan-star omen into a concrete burden argument, persuading Mingdi to stop North Palace construction, cut unnecessary spending, and apologize to ministers.
- Liu Ping's Quanjiao governance is remembered through trust signals: locals voluntarily report more property, claim younger ages for service, and leave prisons nearly empty.
- Yao Song's bed-side ritual quotation is a compact case of hierarchy-aware speech defusing imperial anger without directly challenging Mingdi.
- Guo He's public reward turns local administrative merit into an imperial display, while the same year still records floods in Luoyang and seven commanderies or kingdoms.
- The Shache-Khotan branch shows Western Regions kingship as unstable before Han intervention: conquest, governor killing, local enthronement, battlefield reversal, Guangde's installation, hostage-family return, and marriage alliance all precede later Khotan expansion.

## Key Quotes

> "天子穆穆，诸侯皇皇" - Yao Song's ritual-language deflection when Mingdi moves to strike him.

> "百姓不能按时回家种地" - Zhongli Yi's burden argument against the North Palace works.

> "贤后典范" - the episode's compressed framing of Empress Ma's political image.
