# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》725丨东汉名将祭肜：要保家卫国，就把敌人打趴下！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 49 CE late [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] sequence immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-726-ma-ge-guo-shi-de-ma-yuan-weihe-mengyuan-buneng-shuo-de-mimi-lpxrkrx2uffdzpyctiwwcaanfzbu|Hanji 726]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-727-gui-xia-chanyu-weihe-baidao-zai-hanshi-mianqian-lmzdh-rrfadzszyetxgg-rou24xe|Hanji 727]]. It begins with [[JiTongEasternHan|祭肜]] in Liaodong using gifts, trust, and [[PianHeXianbei|偏和]]'s Xianbei force against the Xiongnu, then follows the Northern/Southern Xiongnu split as [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] seeks Han protection, hostages, and renewed treaty order.

The second half supplies the prehistory to Ma Yuan's later disgrace. [[MaYuanEasternHan|马援]]'s private warning letter to his nephews contrasts [[LongBogaoEasternHan|龙伯高]] with [[DuJiliangEasternHan|杜季良]], but the letter leaks into court accusation, draws [[LiangSongLinxiangMarquis|梁松]] and [[DouGuEasternHan|窦固]] into Liu Xiu's rebuke, and creates resentment before Ma Yuan's final [[WulingManLateHan|武陵蛮]] campaign stalls at Hutou and ends with his illness and death.

## Key Claims

- Ji Tong treats the Liaodong frontier as a relationship-and-incentive problem: he recruits outside groups, rewards Pian He, and asks him to prove loyalty by attacking the Xiongnu.
- Pian He's delivery of enemy heads turns submission into repeated anti-Xiongnu commitment rather than a one-time diplomatic gesture.
- Southern Xiongnu attacks Northern Xiongnu relatives and absorbed defectors, then asks Han for oversight, hostage exchange, tribute order, and renewed treaty protection.
- Ma Yuan's admonition to Ma Yan and Ma Dun makes speech restraint a family-discipline problem: do not casually discuss other people's faults or current laws.
- The letter's Long Bogao/Du Jiliang comparison becomes politically dangerous after it leaves the family setting and is used by Du Jiliang's enemies as court evidence.
- Liu Xiu's rebuke of Liang Song and Dou Gu makes the leaked letter a resentment source that later matters when Liang is sent to question Ma Yuan.
- Ma Yuan's Wuling campaign stalls because he chooses the shorter Hutou route over [[GengShuEasternHan|耿舒]]'s flatter Chongxian route; heat, disease, river conditions, and high-ground defense turn the decision into crisis.

## Key Quotes

> "闻人过失，如闻父母之名" - Ma Yuan's speech-restraint maxim as summarized in the episode.

> "画虎不成，反类狗" - Ma Yuan's warning that imitating Du Jiliang badly could become unserious posturing.

> "心无谦卑，日伴高人莫助" - the closing host admonition.
