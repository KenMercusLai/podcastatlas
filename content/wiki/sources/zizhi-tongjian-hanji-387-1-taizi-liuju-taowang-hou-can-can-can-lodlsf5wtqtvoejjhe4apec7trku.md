# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》387-1｜太子刘据逃亡后，惨！惨！惨！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] short episode backfills the interval between [[LiuJuCrownPrinceWesternHan|卫太子刘据]]'s failed uprising in the [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]] and the death scene already covered in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-387-2-hanwudi-yisheng-nanmo-de-wudian-taizi-liuju-zhisi-fqn_yfiyam5durgrlflosljaho1h|Hanji 387-2]]. After [[EmpressWeiZifuWesternHan|卫子夫]] kills herself, [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] continues punishing Liu Ju's associates, court officials largely believe Liu Ju was wronged but stay silent, and the low-ranking elder [[LinghuMaoWesternHan|令胡茂]] risks a memorial arguing that Liu Ju killed [[JiangChongWesternHan|江充]] and mobilized troops from fear rather than treasonous intent.

The episode's bridge synthesis is that belated recognition does not immediately become rescue. Linghu Mao's memorial moves Han Wudi emotionally and gives him a way to see Liu Ju's action as coerced self-preservation, but the war's scale and imperial legitimacy cost keep Wudi from publicly pardoning the crown prince. Liu Ju then hides in Hu County, where poverty, dependence on hosts, and a failed attempt to contact a wealthy acquaintance lead toward the arrest scene continued in Hanji 387-2.

## Key Claims

- After Wei Zifu's suicide, Han Wudi keeps punishing people linked to Liu Ju, including alleged followers and those treated as rebellious associates.
- Chang'an's guarded gates and frightened households show the Wugu crisis expanding from palace accusation into public urban terror.
- Many officials reportedly know Liu Ju is wronged but stay silent because Han Wudi is still enraged.
- Linghu Mao's memorial reframes Liu Ju's action as fear-driven self-protection after Jiang Chong prevents direct access to the emperor.
- Han Wudi is moved by the memorial but does not publicly pardon Liu Ju because the armed conflict's consequences are too large.
- Liu Ju's Hu County refuge depends on poor hosts and fails when a request for help reaches arresting troops instead of reliable aid.

## Key Quotes

> "君子所以明者兼听也，其所以暗者偏信也" - the episode's warning about one-sided trust and court silence.

> "没有险恶之心" - the memorial's reported reframing of Liu Ju's military action as fear rather than malignant rebellion.
