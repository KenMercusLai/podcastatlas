# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》119-4｜史上能称巨婴的君王—齐王建

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the late-[[QiState|齐国]] crisis opened in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-3-lishishang-di-yi-wei-zhenzheng-yiyishang-chuilian-tingzheng-taihou-liq9d3itmbar00ij43zzed-mulul|Qinji 119-3]]. It presents [[QiWangJian|齐王建 / 田建]] as a ruler shaped by more than forty years of peace, weak succession support, and an information channel captured by [[HouSheng|后胜]] and Qin-favored guests. [[YongmenSima|雍门司马]] prevents the king's personal journey to Qin by reframing kingship as service to the state, but the intervention stops one dangerous action without producing a broader strategy.

[[JimoDafuLateQi|即墨大夫（齐末）]] then proposes using Qi troops and exiled officers from the Three Jin and Chu to restore their states and reverse Qin's expansion. The host treats the proposal as courageous but militarily inflated: Qi's decades without war have eroded readiness while Qin's commanders and soldiers have accumulated combat experience. Qi Wang Jian therefore ends in strategic suspension—neither entering Qin nor rebuilding for war—illustrating both [[DistantStateAppeasementTrap|远国安抚误读]] and [[LongPeaceReadinessAtrophy|长期和平战备退化]].

## Key Claims
- Qi's refusal to aid Zhao at Changping and absence from Xinling Jun's later five-state coalition are presented as a continuous policy of staying outside the anti-Qin struggle.
- [[JunWangHou|君王后]] tries on her deathbed to identify loyal and capable ministers for her son, but the transfer fails before the names can be recorded.
- [[HouSheng|后胜]] and Qi guests rewarded in Qin repeatedly return with a Qin-friendly interpretation, reinforcing the claim that Qi need not prepare for war.
- In Qin Shi Huang's twenty-fifth year, Hou Sheng recommends that Qi congratulate Qin at Xianyang and that [[QiWangJian|齐王建 / 田建]] attend personally.
- [[YongmenSima|雍门司马]] asks whether the king exists for the state or the state for the king; Qi Wang Jian accepts the logic and turns back. The episode also reports an old *Zhanguoce* version in which Yongmen Sima physically blocks the royal carriage with a halberd.
- [[JimoDafuLateQi|即墨大夫（齐末）]] proposes arming exiled Three Jin and Chu officers to recover their homelands and make Qi the last anti-Qin base.
- The host rejects the proposal's force estimates and practical optimism because Qin is battle-hardened while Qi's long peace has weakened usable military capacity.
- Qi Wang Jian's final non-decision avoids both personal submission and active resistance, leaving Qin to determine the next move.

## Key Quotes
> "君王当然是为社稷而立" - Qi Wang Jian's answer to Yongmen Sima's governing-purpose question.

> "大王为什么要离开社稷到秦国去" - Yongmen Sima's decisive challenge to the proposed Qin journey.

> "既不去秦国道贺，也不整军经武" - the episode's summary of Qi Wang Jian's suspended response.
