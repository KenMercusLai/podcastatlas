# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》840丨眼光长远的人有多厉害？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 154-155 CE Huan-era layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-841-juebu-shouruan-jiemi-zhanzheng-de-canku-zhenxiang-losza81gissyijywwe0vxadw5ia0|Hanji 841]]. It opens with a source-scoped lesson about long-range vision, then connects disasters, famine, local revolt, and [[LiuTaoLateHan|刘陶 / 刘桃]]'s ignored Taixue remonstrance to a court that sees warning but does not repair itself.

The second half gives the fuller [[ZhangHuanLateHan|张奂]] frontier story that Hanji 841 later recalls through gift refusal. Zhang arrives with only 200 soldiers, separates the [[DongqiangLateHan|东羌]] from a [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] rebellion, wins back Qiang trust, and refuses horses and gold earrings from Qiang leaders; the host reads this as long-range authority rather than small-profit opportunism.

## Key Claims

- The opening "long-range vision" lesson is source-scoped: the host links anxiety, impatience, shallow judgment, pressure, fear, greed, and overthinking to deficient preparation, experience, vision, maturity, expectation control, or desire discipline.
- In 154-155 CE, the episode places amnesties, court-office turnover, earthquakes, locusts, mountain collapses, an eclipse, local rebellion, and famine in the same Huan-era disorder field.
- [[LiuTaoLateHan|Liu Tao]] uses a Heaven-ruler-people frame to argue that disasters and suffering require political responsibility rather than imperial indifference.
- Liu Tao criticizes [[EmperorHuanOfHan|Emperor Huan]] for not having personally seen war and disaster, for entrusting state power to others, and for allowing eunuch-side power to harm both wealthy and poor subjects.
- Liu Tao invokes the Qin collapse and late Western Han disorder as precedents, then recommends [[ZhuMuLateHan|朱穆]] and [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]] as clean, upright state-capacity figures; the memorial is ignored.
- [[ZhangHuanLateHan|Zhang Huan]] arrives as Anding subordinate-state commandant with only 200 soldiers when Southern Xiongnu rebels attack Meiji and Eastern Qiang groups respond.
- Zhang first stabilizes key frontier points and recruits soldiers, then sends Wang Wei to persuade Eastern Qiang groups away from the Southern Xiongnu alignment.
- The episode presents that move as [[CoalitionFractureExploitation|coalition fracture exploitation]]: Zhang cuts the Southern Xiongnu-Eastern Qiang connection and uses Qiang chiefs against the Xiongnu side.
- After the victory and surrender, Qiang leaders offer Zhang 20 horses and eight pairs of gold earrings, but he refuses them publicly.
- The gift refusal is interpreted as [[GiftRefusalAsAuthority|gift refusal as authority]] because Zhang's clean distance contrasts with eight corrupt predecessors and makes his commands and teaching accepted in the Qiang field.

## Key Quotes

> "志远者岂在朝夕" - paired maxim for not reducing strategy to immediate gain.

> "不图小利者必有大谋" - host's reading of Zhang Huan's refusal of Qiang gifts.
