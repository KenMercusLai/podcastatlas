# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1020丨三国败家子袁术，如何把好牌打得稀烂？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode uses "势" as the frame for late-Han power judgment in 197 CE. [[CaoCao|曹操]] asks [[HeKui|何夔]] about [[YuanShu|袁术]] after Yuan Shu's imperial claim, and He Kui's answer turns Yuan Shu's failure into a three-part diagnosis: he misreads the moment, breaks trust, and cannot hold useful talent. The second half contrasts Cao Cao's capacity to absorb people and manage risk through [[XuChu|许褚]]'s local armed following and the [[YangBiao|杨彪]] case, where [[KongRong|孔融]], [[XunYu|荀彧]], and [[ManChong|满宠]] help push an accusation away from reputation-destroying punishment. The episode's core synthesis is that resources and position are not enough without [[XingshiqingStrategicAssessment|condition-sensitive assessment]], credible trust, and talent integration.

## Key Claims
- The episode frames historical success through "识势、蓄势、借势": strategy can point the way, but only situational judgment and execution make it usable.
- [[HeKui|何夔]] says [[YuanShu|袁术]] cannot win because he fails both trend and trust: he does not align with the age's political direction and cannot win durable popular support.
- [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s failure is summarized as losing timing, terrain, and people: he is out of step, unreliable, and unable to value capable people such as [[HeKui|何夔]].
- [[CaoCao|曹操]] is portrayed as severe but not blind to talent; the episode uses his treatment of He Kui and [[XuChu|许褚]] to distinguish harsh control from Yuan Shu's inability to retain useful people.
- [[XuChu|许褚]]'s fortified local following is treated as a late-Han order problem: a rural armed group can protect households during disorder, then become state capacity when its leader reads the larger trend and joins Cao Cao.
- [[YangBiao|杨彪]]'s arrest shows Cao Cao's court-control risk after moving [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] to Xu: Yang Biao's old-Han prestige and [[YuanShu|袁术]] marriage tie make him politically exposed even before evidence is secure.
- [[KongRong|孔融]]'s remonstrance and [[ManChong|满宠]]'s report both stress that punishing a famous old minister without proof would cost Cao Cao public confidence.
- [[ManChong|满宠]] is read as using harsh procedure to produce a release path: after interrogation yields no usable proof, he tells Cao Cao that conviction without evidence would lose popular support.
- [[YangBiao|杨彪]]'s later withdrawal marks the practical endpoint for many old-Han ministers under Cao Cao's control: continued court participation may be more dangerous than retreat.

## Key Quotes
> "有势者不可失，无势者不可强" - the opening frame for judging action by timing and momentum.

> "不识时务、不讲信用、不重视人才" - the episode's compact diagnosis of Yuan Shu's failure.

> "必定失去民心" - Man Chong's warning about convicting Yang Biao without solid evidence.
