# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》985丨反目成仇：李郭之乱的细节探究

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate pre-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-986-tianyu-qiwang-bixian-lingqi-kuang-lvlvu057-tnz3hal6oodhydvzvbd|Hanji 986]] late-Han sequence by showing the first fully symmetrical stage of the [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]]-[[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]] rupture. Li Jue holds [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] while Guo Si holds ministers such as [[YangBiao|杨彪]], [[ShiSunRuiLateHan|士孙瑞]], and [[ZhuJunLateHan|朱儁]], so the emperor and court officials become bargaining assets in a military feud. The episode also records [[FuShouEmpress|伏皇后]]'s appointment, [[FuWanLateHan|伏完]]'s office, Yang Biao's hostage-banquet rebuke, Li Jue's Qiang-Hu recruitment, [[ZhangBaoLateHan|张苞]]'s inner-camp defection, [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]]'s camp defense, and the emperor's food humiliation after being moved to Beiwu.

## Key Claims
- The host opens with a source-scoped moral frame: luxury, laziness, and arrogance make successful people and regimes vulnerable when they most need restraint.
- In 195 CE, the Li-Guo conflict has turned court bodies into coercive property: Li Jue holds Emperor Xian, while Guo Si detains the high ministers.
- The court can still perform formal acts, such as appointing Fu Shou as empress and Fu Wan as zhijinwu, but these acts occur inside armed captivity rather than restored sovereignty.
- Guo Si's hostage banquet shows that ministerial remonstrance remains morally legible but physically exposed; Yang Biao can rebuke him, yet Guo Si can nearly kill him on the spot.
- Li Jue's recruitment of Qiang-Hu supporters through gifts and promised women shows his force base being rebuilt through predatory incentives rather than institutional command.
- Guo Si's night attack, Zhang Bao's arson, and Zhang Bao's later defection show Li Jue's camp becoming internally porous even before the later settlement and flight episodes.
- Emperor Xian's movement into Beiwu, guarded doors, hungry attendants, and rotten beef bones show that imperial survival has collapsed into bodily deprivation and humiliation.
- [[YangQiLateHan|杨琦]]'s advice that the emperor swallow his anger turns survival into restraint under captivity: even a justified rebuke could trigger a worse relocation or retaliation.

## Key Quotes
> "家败离不开奢，人败离不开懒，好人贤离不开骄." - opening maxim used to frame the Li-Guo collapse as arrogance after power.

> "心无谦卑，日半高人莫助." - closing maxim tying the episode's military breakdown back to humility and help.
