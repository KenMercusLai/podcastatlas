# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》398｜如果你是李陵，会替匈奴劝降苏武吗

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the [[SuWuWesternHan|苏武]] return branch between the late-[[HanWudi|汉武帝]] recovery turn and the early [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]] regency crisis. It centers on [[LiLingWesternHan|李陵]] being sent by [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] to persuade Su Wu to surrender, Su Wu's refusal under extreme isolation, and [[ChangHuiWesternHan|常惠]]'s wild-goose message tactic that forces Xiongnu to admit Su Wu is still alive.

The durable synthesis is that Su Wu's loyalty is presented as embodied identity rather than successful calculation: the Han envoy's staff, mourning for Han Wudi, and refusal to surrender remain meaningful even when rescue seems impossible. Li Ling becomes the tragic contrast, a former Han officer who can understand Su Wu's righteousness but no longer believes he can return to Han without renewed humiliation.

## Key Claims

- Su Wu survives exile near the North Sea under hunger and cold while continuing to hold the Han envoy staff, making the staff a visible identity marker.
- Li Ling's persuasion relies on realistic pressure: no visible audience, family disaster, Han Wudi's violent unpredictability, and the shortness of life.
- Su Wu refuses the argument by treating service to the ruler as analogous to filial duty and by saying he would rather die than surrender.
- Li Ling is moved by Su Wu's refusal and frames both himself and Wei Lü as guilty in contrast to Su Wu's righteousness.
- After Han Wudi dies, Su Wu's mourning ritual reinforces that his attachment to Han survives exile and Xiongnu pressure.
- Chang Hui secretly informs a Han envoy that Su Wu is alive and invents the wild-goose message story, creating a diplomatic proof-of-life device that Xiongnu cannot easily deny.
- Su Wu's return in 81 BCE is both public honor and private loss: he leaves a Xiongnu wife and child behind, returns with a small remnant of the original mission, and is appointed Dian Shuguo.
- The episode closes by linking restored Han-Xiongnu relations and early Han Zhao policy to lighter burdens and social recovery after late-Han-Wudi exhaustion.

## Key Quotes

> "臣事君，犹子事父也。" - Su Wu's loyalty frame in the episode's telling.

> "足下还归，扬名于匈奴，功显于汉室。" - Li Ling's farewell praise for Su Wu's return.
