# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》359-1｜“愿得一心人，白头不相离”竟是写给渣男的？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the [[SimaXiangru|司马相如]]–[[ZhuoWenjun|卓文君]] story after their elopement. It follows the couple through severe poverty, their return to Linqiong to run a tavern, and [[ZhuoWangsunWesternHan|卓王孙]]'s eventual transfer of wealth and servants, interpreting Zhuo Wenjun's willingness to endure hardship as evidence that she did not leave home for money.

The episode then presents Sima's later wish to take another woman as a concubine and [[BaiTouYin|《白头吟》]] as Zhuo Wenjun's measured response. Its durable contribution is the contrast between active commitment under material hardship and a later fidelity crisis; the attribution and transmission of the poem, the Maoling-woman story, private motives, and the claim that literary restraint restored the marriage remain source-scoped popular-history narration.

## Key Claims

- Zhuo Wenjun accepts the material consequences of elopement after her father cuts off support, and the host treats this as evidence against a wealth-seeking interpretation of her choice.
- The couple sells their carriage and opens a tavern near Zhuo Wangsun, with Zhuo Wenjun selling wine and Sima doing visibly humble work.
- The episode says kin persuade Zhuo Wangsun to relent and that he gives the couple substantial property and servants.
- Sima's later attachment to a woman associated with Maoling creates the relationship crisis in which the episode places 《白头吟》.
- The host interprets the poem as a calm, boundary-setting form of “以退为进” rather than emotional collapse or direct confrontation.
- Sima's reported deathbed message to Zhuo Wenjun and memorial for Han Wudi close the story, but their exact content and the claim that he anticipated an imperial messenger are not independently established here.

## Key Quotes

> “愿得一人心，白首不相离。” - the line the episode attributes to Zhuo Wenjun within the 《白头吟》 tradition.

> “以分别，拉开了重逢的序幕。” - the host's interpretation of Zhuo Wenjun's response as strategic restraint.
