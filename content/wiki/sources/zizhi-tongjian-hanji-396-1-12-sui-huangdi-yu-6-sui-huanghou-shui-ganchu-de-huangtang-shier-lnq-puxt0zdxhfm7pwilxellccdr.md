# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》396-1｜12岁皇帝与6岁皇后，谁干出的荒唐事儿？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the first palace-marriage layer behind the later [[ShangguanJieWesternHan|上官桀]] versus [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]] rupture. It begins with the vulnerability of imperial women and live burial as a background frame, then turns to young [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|汉昭帝刘弗陵]], his much older sister [[GaiChanggongzhuWesternHan|鄂邑 / 盖长公主]], and the six-year-old [[ShangguanTaihouWesternHan|上官氏]] being pulled into the court through her father [[ShangguanAnWesternHan|上官安]] and grandfather Shangguan Jie.

The durable synthesis is that Shangguan Shi's entry is not a romantic or ordinary palace-selection story. Huo Guang initially refuses because the child is too young and because the marriage would strengthen the Shangguan branch. Shangguan Jie and Shangguan An then bypass him through [[DingWairenWesternHan|丁外人]], the princess's favored man, promising that helping the girl enter the palace could support Ding's own title ambitions. The episode therefore makes the six-year-old empress-to-be an early example of child marriage, inner-court access brokerage, and outer-relative competition before the later anti-Huo conspiracy.

## Key Claims

- Han Zhao's early household is politically unusual because his mother [[LadyGouyiWesternHan|钩弋夫人]] has already been killed by [[HanWudi|汉武帝]], so the older princess is brought into the palace to care for him.
- When Han Zhao reaches twelve, the princess begins considering a consort for him and initially favors a girl from the Zhouyang family.
- Shangguan Jie wants his own granddaughter to become the imperial spouse because she is both his granddaughter and Huo Guang's granddaughter through Shangguan An's marriage to Huo's daughter.
- Huo Guang refuses the plan because Shangguan Shi is only six and because the match would enlarge Shangguan political capital.
- Shangguan An turns to Ding Wairen, the princess's favored man, and links help with a possible marquisate that could improve Ding's standing with the princess.
- Ding Wairen persuades the princess, after which Han Zhao summons Shangguan An's daughter into the palace as jieyu and appoints Shangguan An as qiduwei.
- The episode treats the episode as a tragic power transaction: a six-year-old girl is made palace material by male kin and court brokers, and Huo Guang's discomfort becomes an early fissure with the Shangguan side.

## Key Quotes

> "她只有六岁" - the episode's central shock point about Shangguan Shi's age at palace entry.

> "这里为两人后来的关系埋下伏笔" - the host's framing of the Huo Guang and Shangguan Jie rupture.
