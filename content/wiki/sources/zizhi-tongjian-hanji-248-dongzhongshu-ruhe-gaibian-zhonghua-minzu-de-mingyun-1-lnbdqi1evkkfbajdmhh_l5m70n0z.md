# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》248｜董仲舒如何改变中华民族的命运？（1）

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode introduces [[DongZhongshu|董仲舒]] through an early [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] talent-selection event rather than through his mature doctrines. The host contrasts [[CaoShen|曹参]]-associated Huang-Lao non-intervention with Wudi's activist ambitions, then describes a call for 贤良方正、直言极谏 followed by ruler-led [[ImperialPolicyQuestioningTalentSelection|策问式选才]]. In the episode's account, this channel moves Dong from classical scholarship into an imperial policy arena and gives his Confucian ambition a possible route to state influence.

The supplied transcript stops before explaining Dong Zhongshu's substantive answers or the institutional adoption of his ideas. Its descriptions of the event as China's first “national civil-service examination,” more than one hundred candidates, one-to-one questioning, Dong's first-place rank, and a direct line to “罢黜百家，独尊儒术” therefore remain episode-attributed framing rather than settled institutional history.

## Key Claims

- The episode places the event in 140 BCE and presents the young Han Wudi as rejecting a passive Huang-Lao governing style in favor of active state-building.
- Wudi calls for people classed as 贤良方正 and 直言极谏, with local recommendation and imperial policy questions serving as the episode's recruitment sequence.
- The source says more than one hundred learned candidates entered the capital and describes the questioning as a ruler-to-candidate exchange rather than a modern standardized written examination.
- [[DongZhongshu|董仲舒]] is said to rank first, while [[ZhuMaichenWesternHan|朱买臣]], [[SimaXiangru|司马相如]], and [[DongfangShuo|东方朔]] are grouped into the wider talent-discovery story.
- The episode treats Dong's importance as the combination of scholarly argument, access to the ruler, and an ambition to move Confucian learning from marginal survival toward political predominance.
- [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s retention of extended policy answers is interpreted as both substantive statecraft and a model of indirect remonstrance through historical examples.
- The closing history of Confucian hardship supplies narrative background but does not yet demonstrate how Dong's doctrines changed institutions or later Chinese history.

## Key Quotes

> “这场考试堪称中国历史上第一届国家公务员考试” - the host's modern analogy for the recruitment event, retained as source framing rather than a settled classification.

> “让儒学一统江湖，独步天下” - the episode's description of Dong Zhongshu's intellectual ambition.
