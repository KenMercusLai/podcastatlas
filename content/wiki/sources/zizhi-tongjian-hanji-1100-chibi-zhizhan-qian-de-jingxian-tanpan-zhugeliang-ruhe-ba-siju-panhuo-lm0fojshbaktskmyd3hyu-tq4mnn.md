# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1100丨赤壁之战前的惊险谈判：诸葛亮如何把死局盘活？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] and [[LuSu|鲁肃]] to [[SunQuan|孙权]] after [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s Changban defeat and before the Jiangdong surrender debate. It reconstructs Zhuge Liang's case as a four-part sequence: force a choice between resistance and surrender, make capitulation dishonorable, show that Liu Bei retains usable forces while [[CaoCao|曹操]] is overextended, and present coalition victory and a tripartite balance as Sun Quan's upside.

The episode treats the exchange as a model of [[DignityBasedPersuasion|尊严激将式说服]] joined to operational analysis rather than as eloquence alone. It ends before Sun Quan's final commitment: Cao Cao's threatening letter reopens uncertainty, which is addressed in the later Hanji 1101-1102 surrender-debate sequence.

## Key Claims
- Zhuge Liang frames Sun Quan's immediate alternatives as resistance or formal surrender, removing continued hesitation as a stable middle position.
- When Sun Quan asks why Liu Bei does not surrender, Zhuge Liang invokes [[TianHeng|田横]] and Liu Bei's Han lineage to make refusal a matter of status, loyalty, and political identity.
- Sun Quan then asks whether a recently defeated Liu Bei can contribute materially, moving the discussion from emotional commitment to coalition capacity.
- Zhuge Liang answers that Guan Yu's naval force and Liu Qi's Jiangxia troops leave the Liu side with usable manpower despite Changban.
- He argues that Cao Cao's long pursuit has exhausted the army, northern troops are poorly adapted to water warfare, and newly absorbed Jingzhou people are not securely loyal.
- The persuasion closes the loop by pairing a claim of winnability with a benefit: a joint victory could force Cao Cao north and leave Sun Quan holding the southeast within a tripartite balance.
- The episode summarizes alliance as mutual use, but its own ending shows that the agreement is not yet secure because Cao Cao's threatening letter still has to be answered.

## Key Quotes
> “事急而不断，祸至无日矣。” — the episode's rendering of Zhuge Liang's demand that Sun Quan stop postponing the choice.

> “田横，齐之壮士耳，犹守义不辱。” — the dignity precedent used to make Liu Bei's surrender politically unthinkable.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is recorded. This source fills the diplomatic step between Lu Su's alliance opening in Hanji 1099 and Cao Cao's letter plus the Jiangdong surrender debate in Hanji 1101-1102.
- The exact dialogue, facial reactions, private motives, force totals, and claim that Sun Quan's doubts were fully removed are episode-level reconstruction rather than independently verified transcript.
- The source describes three Cao-side weaknesses while explicitly listing fatigue, poor northern water-war adaptation, and coerced Jingzhou allegiance; the count and grouping should not be treated as a fixed historical taxonomy.
- “Alliance is mutual use” is retained as the host's interpretive summary, not as proof that trust, legitimacy, and longer-term obligation were irrelevant.
