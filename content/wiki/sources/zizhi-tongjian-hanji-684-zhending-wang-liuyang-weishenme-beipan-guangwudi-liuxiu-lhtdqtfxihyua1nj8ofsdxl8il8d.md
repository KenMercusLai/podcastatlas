# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》684丨真定王刘杨为什么背叛光武帝刘秀？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills a Jianwu 2 / 26 CE layer between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-683-fangniuwa-liupenzi-buxiang-dang-huangdi-dan-meiyudi-lt7hmrorydsazpkuwby1ntd7t1eh|Hanji 683]] and the later [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-686-liuxiu-fashi-zhi-ai-yinlihua-weihe-li-guoshi-wei-hou-lnqlqgq54srb1zzzgemfwuwujhvc|Hanji 686]]-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-688-cong-yongbing-baiwan-dao-kuibuchengjun-dengyu-jingli-le-shenme-lpabdvtmhtrf37puwvgi4c9ajp60|688]] sequence. It contrasts [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]]'s construction of Luoyang ancestral, sheji, and heaven-earth sacrificial order with [[ChimeiRebellion|赤眉]] violence in [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] and the transfer of Western Han imperial tablets toward Luoyang.

The central political case is [[LiuYangZhendingKingEasternHan|真定王刘杨]]: he manufactures prophecy around an unusual neck growth, refuses ordinary summons, and is killed after [[GengChunEasternHan|耿纯]] lures him into a meeting. The episode then turns to [[BaoYongEasternHan|鲍永]] and [[FengYanEasternHan|冯衍]] as former Gengshi loyalists entering Liu Xiu's order, and to [[WangLiangEasternHan|王梁]] as a senior commander nearly executed for rejecting [[WuHanEasternHan|吴汉]]'s command authority.

## Key Claims

- Liu Xiu's Luoyang ritual construction is presented as restoration work: ancestral temple, sheji, and southern sacrifice sites make the new capital a Han-order center.
- Chimei disorder in Chang'an is described through plunder, burning, westward movement, and the movement of Western Han imperial tablets toward Luoyang.
- Liu Yang's rebellion risk is not an open campaign but a manufactured mandate claim using prophecy and bodily anomaly to disturb public allegiance.
- Geng Chun's killing of Liu Yang is covert and preemptive, while Liu Xiu later preserves the Zhending line by allowing Liu Yang's son Liu De to inherit.
- Bao Yong and Feng Yan surrender after Liu Xuan's death by mourning the old ruler, releasing dependents, sealing insignia, and approaching Liu Xiu without troops.
- Wang Liang's refusal to obey Wu Han turns status resentment into a military-law crisis; Zong Guang preserves imperial room by sending Wang Liang back for Liu Xiu's judgment rather than executing him on site.

## Key Quotes

> "天选之子" - the source's explanation of Liu Yang's self-presentation.

> "陈平处置樊哙" - the precedent used for Zong Guang's handling of Wang Liang.
