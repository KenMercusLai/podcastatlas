## Summary
This [[Budongqiu|不懂球]] episode with [[PanWeili|潘伟力]] reassesses the football legacy of [[Evergrande]] after [[XuJiayin]]'s reported life sentence and [[GuangzhouSportUniversity|广州体育学院]]'s purchase of [[EvergrandeFootballSchool|恒大足校]]. The discussion separates [[GuangzhouFC|广州队]]'s sporting and youth-development achievements from Evergrande's corporate and legal collapse, while tracing how the [[ChineseFootballGoldenYuanEra|中国足球金元时代]], club-national-team boundary blur, and post-club fan identity still shape [[ChineseSuperLeague|Chinese Super League]] football. It also treats [[GuangdongChenxingJuli|广东陈兴聚力]] and [[GuangzhouLeopard|广州豹]] as competing tests of whether Guangzhou football memory can continue through new institutions.

## Key Claims
- [[EvergrandeFootballSchool]] remains the most concrete asset in Evergrande's football legacy because students, elite teams, coaches, managers, and daily operations survived the transfer to [[GuangzhouSportUniversity]].
- The episode argues that Evergrande football should be judged by layers: [[XuJiayin]] and the property group need one judgment, while football-school infrastructure, [[GuangzhouFC]] memories, Asian titles, and damaged professional norms need separate judgments.
- The "万人足校" model is criticized as a scale-first early mistake, but the source says later age groups improved after selection became more standardized and professional.
- The source places the true [[ChineseFootballGoldenYuanEra]] around the 2016 multi-club arms race, not simply Evergrande's 2010 entry or its early championship run.
- Evergrande's high salaries, advertising-contract controversy, "全华班" promises, naturalized-player spending, and [[MarcelloLippi|Lippi]] national-team period are presented as cases where club success strained professional boundaries.
- Guangzhou football continuity is unsettled: [[GuangzhouLeopard]] may supply top-flight football, while [[GuangdongChenxingJuli]] and former academy players may carry more direct emotional continuity for some former Guangzhou FC supporters.

## Key Quotes
> "有多大能力做多大事" - Pan Weili's summary lesson from Evergrande's overextension.
