# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1045丨他是江东霸业的开创者，可惜死的太窝囊

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode turns [[SunCe|孙策]]'s earlier [[LeaderPersonalExposure|领导者轻身涉险]] warning into the fatal 200 CE outcome: while hunting, Sun Ce outruns his escort and is ambushed by retainers of [[XuGongLateHan|徐贡]], who use [[HanDang|韩当]]'s name as a false field cover before wounding him. The episode then follows Sun Ce's deathbed transfer of seal and command to [[SunQuan|孙权]], [[ZhangZhaoLateHan|张昭]]'s public stabilization of the young successor, and [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]]'s return from Baqiu to help hold [[WuCommanderyLateHan|吴郡]] and Jiangdong.

The closing branch shifts back to the pre-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]] Cao-Yuan field. [[LiuPiRunanLateHan|刘辟]] and other Runan Yellow Turban remnants answer [[YuanShao|袁绍]], but [[LiTongLateHan|李通]] rejects Yuan Shao's commission, kills the envoy, and sends the seal to [[CaoCao|曹操]], making local allegiance choice part of the same succession-era crisis landscape.

## Key Claims
- The opening "刚柔" lesson prepares the Sun Ce reading: excessive hardness, emotional entanglement, and inability to withdraw are treated as personal-risk patterns, not only manners.
- In Jian'an 5 / 200 CE, Sun Ce's hunting habit becomes fatal when his horse leaves the escort behind and three Xu Gong retainers can approach him without the normal guard boundary.
- The assassins' claim that they are Han Dang's men shooting deer is not enough to fool Sun Ce, but it buys the moment of proximity before one assassin is shot and the others wound Sun Ce in the face.
- Sun Ce's deathbed instructions frame Jiangdong as defensible through Wu-Yue and Three Rivers geography, but only if his staff helps Sun Quan turn the conquered base into a stable regime.
- Sun Ce gives Sun Quan the seal and distinguishes their capacities: Sun Ce is stronger at leading Jiangdong troops into contention, while Sun Quan is stronger at selecting people and defending the region.
- Sun Quan's first problem is not strategy but visible succession: Zhang Zhao stops his grief from becoming political paralysis by dressing him in office clothes, putting him on horseback, and touring the camps.
- Zhou Yu's return from Baqiu and decision to remain as central guard with Zhang Zhao makes the transition a civil-military stabilization effort rather than a private family mourning scene.
- The episode stresses the fragility of the handoff: Sun Ce holds six commanderies, but remoter districts and refugee 士大夫 are not yet tightly attached to Sun-family rule.
- In the Runan branch, Yuan Shao and Liu Pi try to pull Li Tong away from Cao Cao, but Li Tong reads the Cao-Yuan contest as a long-term judgment question and rejects Yuan Shao's visible strength.

## Key Quotes
> "江东之众，决机于两陈之间，与天下争衡，卿不如我；举贤任能，各尽其心，以保江东，我不如卿." - Sun Ce's comparison of his own and Sun Quan's governing capacities.

> "现在是哭的时候吗？" - Zhang Zhao's succession-stabilizing rebuke to the grieving Sun Quan.
