# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》588丨老板不称职，下属能有多惨？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianping 4 / 3 BCE under [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]], before the later Wang Jia, Du Ye, Dong Xian, and Wang Mang sequence. It centers [[BaoXuanWesternHan|鲍宣]]'s memorial on the people's "seven harms" and "seven deaths," linking disaster, tax and corvee burden, official predation, powerful-household land seizure, theft, harsh punishment, famine, and disease to a court that keeps rewarding outer relatives and [[DongXian|董贤]].

The durable synthesis is that Bao Xuan treats popular suffering as a governance diagnosis rather than background misery. His memorial argues that criminal law cannot stabilize people who are already being pushed toward ruin, and that Aidi must repair the personnel and favor structure by clearing Sun Chong, Xifu Gong, and other harmful speakers, restraining uneducated outer relatives, and recalling or relying on [[FuXiWesternHan|傅喜]], [[HeWuWesternHan|何武]], Shi Dan, [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]], and [[PengXuanWesternHan|彭宣]].

## Key Claims

- Bao Xuan's "seven harms" frame joins natural disaster, heavy taxation and labor, corrupt officials, land-grabbing strongmen, badly timed corvee, theft, and household ruin into one public-suffering diagnosis.
- His "seven deaths" frame says harsh officials, torture, wrongful punishment, robbery, private vendetta, famine, and disease can make criminal-law governance useless or counterproductive.
- The memorial locates the root cause in senior and local officials who use office to enrich households rather than educate and protect the people.
- Bao Xuan defines the emperor as parent and Heaven for the people, so failure to treat commoners impartially is framed as a failure of ruler responsibility.
- The episode contrasts starving and exposed commoners with outer relatives, Dong Xian, servants, and servant networks receiving money, guests, and luxury support.
- Bao Xuan's remedy is personnel correction: remove glib or harmful insiders, keep untrained outer-relative children out of office, and restore experienced old ministers.
- The host reads Aidi's nonpunishment of Bao Xuan as limited tolerance, not proof that the remonstrance was converted into policy.

## Key Quotes

> "七害" - Bao Xuan's compact name for the pressure field harming commoners.

> "七死" - Bao Xuan's compact name for lethal conditions facing the people.

> "治天下者，当用天下之心为心，不得自专快意而已" - the memorial's public-minded ruler standard.
