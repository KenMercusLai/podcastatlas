# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》959丨如果你穿越到三国，该如何存活？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 190-191 CE disorder before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-960-caowei-dingji-zhinang-xunyu-weihe-qi-yuan-tou-cao-lmor1txqpm88itt4incalusconjr|Hanji 960]]. It uses [[ZhuJunLateHan|朱儁]], [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]], [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]], [[ZhangLuFivePecks|张鲁]], [[ZhangXiuFivePecks|张修]], [[LiuBiao|刘表]], and [[GongsunDuLateHan|公孙度]] to ask how people survive when the Han court is still symbolically meaningful but no longer materially secure. The source's practical synthesis is split: capable actors should keep ability hidden until timing arrives, while ordinary people should lower exposure and choose safer environments where possible.

## Key Claims

- The opening frames late-Han war as a human-nature amplifier: courage, loyalty, fear, opportunism, and treachery become easier to see when violence breaks normal order.
- [[ZhuJunLateHan|朱儁]] returns to ruined [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]], defeats Dong Zhuo's appointee Yang Yi, then moves to Zhongmou and calls on regional forces to oppose [[DongZhuo|董卓]] rather than pretending Luoyang can still function as a stable base.
- [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]]'s recommendation of Zhu Jun and dispatch of three thousand elite troops is read as political signaling: even under Dong Zhuo's control, [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] remains the nominal common ruler and the language of Han loyalty still matters.
- The episode makes [[ProvincialShepherdAutonomy|州牧集权与地方割据]] central to late-Han fragmentation. [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]'s earlier proposal to strengthen provincial governors helps local crisis management, but in [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] it becomes a structure for semi-independent rule.
- Liu Yan's use of [[ZhangLuFivePecks|张鲁]] and [[ZhangXiuFivePecks|张修]] to attack Hanzhong, kill Su Gu, and block the [[XieguRoadLateHan|斜谷道]] turns road control into political separation from the Chang'an court.
- [[StrategicPassControl|兵家必争通道控制]] is defined through three conditions: strategic location, resources, and transport convenience. The episode uses Xiegu / Baoxie as the local case because it connects Guanzhong, Hanzhong, and the Shu-Ba route field.
- [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s memorial against Liu Yan's imperial-style carriage is read as a ritual-political warning: local power becomes dangerous when people begin to recognize the provincial ruler's symbols more than the emperor's.
- [[LiuZhang|刘璋]] appears as both court envoy and retained son. [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] sends him to persuade Liu Yan, but Liu Yan keeps him in Yizhou, creating the succession branch later developed in Hanji 981.
- [[GongsunDuLateHan|公孙度]]'s early career is framed as an opportunity story: family relocation, a chance relationship with the Xuantu governor, and a talent-recommendation edict make later Liaodong power possible.
- [[ConcealedCapabilityTiming|藏器待时]] is the source's survival lesson. If one has real capacity, ability should be stored, trained, and used when timing arrives; without such capacity, the safer path is low profile, humility, and reduced ambition.

## Key Quotes

> "兵者，国之大事" - the opening frame for why disorder makes survival judgment decisive.

> "君子藏器于身，待时而动" - the episode's advice for capable people in a violent transition.

> "兵家必争之地" - the strategic geography label attached to Xiegu / Baoxie-style routes.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is recorded. The episode backfills the pre-Hanji 960 setting and explains institutional fragmentation rather than reversing the later Yizhou, Hanzhong, or emperor-legitimacy branches.
- Source-scope caution: the "time travel to Three Kingdoms" question is treated as the host's interpretive frame, not as a historical claim.
- Source-scope caution: Zhang Xiu here is [[ZhangXiuFivePecks|张修]], the Hanzhong religious-military actor, not [[ZhangXiu|张绣]] of the Wancheng branch.
