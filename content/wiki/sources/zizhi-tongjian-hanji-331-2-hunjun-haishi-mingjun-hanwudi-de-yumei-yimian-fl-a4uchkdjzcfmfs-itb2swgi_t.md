# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》331-2｜昏君还是明君？汉武帝的愚昧一面

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies an aftermath to [[ShaowengWesternHan|少翁]]'s exposure and execution. While ill at 顶湖宫, [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] hears that the dead fangshi has been seen alive in Guandong; an opened grave allegedly contains no body and only a bamboo slip, which Wudi interprets as immortal escape and as a reason to regret the killing.

An unnamed fangshi then recommends an unnamed 上郡 shaman said to have gained spirit communication after a serious illness. The shaman performs a rite, announces that the possessing spirit has departed, and Wudi subsequently recovers, visits 甘泉宫, and establishes offerings at 寿宫. The sequence documents belief reinforcement through an ambiguous report, an uncontrolled grave inspection, fear-based illness attribution, and recovery after ritual; it does not establish that Shaoweng survived, a spirit caused the illness, or the rite caused the recovery.

## Key Claims

- The episode says Han Wudi becomes ill while sacrificing at 顶湖宫 and does not improve after physicians and shamans are summoned.
- An attendant reports that someone saw the already executed Shaoweng alive in Guandong.
- Wudi orders Shaoweng's grave opened; the episode says the coffin contains no body and only a bamboo slip.
- Wudi interprets the alleged empty coffin as evidence that Shaoweng escaped as an immortal, regrets having killed him, and fears that divine anger caused his illness.
- Another fangshi recommends an 上郡 shaman whose claimed spirit access is linked to a prior serious illness and possession experience.
- After performing a rite, the shaman says the possessing spirit has left and predicts Wudi's recovery.
- Wudi's subsequent improvement is treated by him as confirmation, leading to a meeting at 甘泉宫 and offerings at 寿宫.

## Key Quotes

> “棺材里头一个人都没有，只有一个竹简” - the episode's reported grave-inspection result.

> “附体的大仙已经请走了” - the shaman's claimed explanation before Wudi's recovery.
