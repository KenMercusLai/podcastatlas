# 《资治通鉴·周纪》80丨诸葛亮的偶像 到底有多厉害？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[LeYi|乐毅]] after [[TianDan|田单]]'s [[FireOxNightRaid|fire-ox]] restoration of [[QiState|齐国]] in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-79-shishang-yong-gengniu-da-shengzhang-diyi-ren-lsrl0posblloqwugox160xsicp2p|Zhouji 79]]. [[YanHuiwang|燕惠王]] regrets replacing Le Yi, fears [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] may use him against [[YanState|燕国]], and sends a blame-shifting letter that prompts Le Yi's [[BaoYanHuiwangShu|《报燕惠王书》]]. The episode's core move is to treat Le Yi not only as a famous general admired by later figures such as [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]], but as a model of [[LoyalMinisterExitEthic|loyal exit ethics]]: he refuses to return to a dangerous ruler, refuses to slander the old relationship, and refuses to turn new patronage into revenge.

## Key Claims
- After Tian Dan's counterattack, he is made Anping Jun and later becomes chancellor of [[QiState|齐国]], while Le Yi remains in [[ZhaoState|赵国]] under Zhao Huiwen Wang's protection.
- [[YanHuiwang|燕惠王]] regrets using [[QiJie|骑劫]] and losing the seventy-plus Qi cities, but his letter to Le Yi frames the problem as Le Yi's mistaken flight and possible betrayal of [[YanZhaowang|燕昭王]]'s trust.
- Le Yi answers through [[BaoYanHuiwangShu|《报燕惠王书》]], using [[WuZixu|伍子胥]] as a warning that a meritorious minister can die if he misjudges the difference between one ruler's breadth and another ruler's suspicion.
- The episode reads the [[WuZixu|Wu Zixu]] comparison as indirect criticism of Yan Huiwang: Le Yi can honor Yan Zhaowang's memory without entrusting his life to the successor who already removed him.
- [[CaoCao|曹操]] is introduced as a later reader moved by the letter, which the source uses to underline Le Yi's reputation as a model for politically exposed commanders.
- Le Yi says he will not use Zhao's favor to attack Yan, even while rejecting Yan Huiwang's implied demand that he return or confess wrongdoing.
- The letter's ethic is summarized through the idea that gentlemen do not speak evil after breaking relations and loyal ministers do not cleanse their own names after leaving a state.
- [[YanHuiwang|燕惠王]] is reassured enough to let [[LeXian|乐贤]] inherit the Changguo Jun title, while Le Yi continues to move diplomatically between Yan and Zhao.
- [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] had reason to use Le Yi against Yan, but Le Yi refuses because doing so would damage [[YanZhaowang|燕昭王]], [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]], and his own reputation.
- The source adds a backward explanation for Le Yi's earlier mobility: he had served Zhao Wuling Wang, but the [[ShaqiuCrisis|Shaqiu crisis]] and Zhao Wuling Wang's starvation pushed him toward Yan.
- Le Yi is later enfeoffed at Guanjin and titled Wangzhu Jun in Zhao, where he dies well rather than becoming another successful minister destroyed by a ruler.
- The closing contrast turns back to [[TianDan|田单]]: his restoration victory creates enormous merit, but [[QiXiangWang|齐襄王]] is portrayed as less broad-minded than Yan Zhaowang and surrounded by nine flattering ministers, setting up Tian Dan's coming self-preservation problem.

## Key Quotes

> "祸兮福所倚，福兮祸所伏" - transition frame for Tian Dan's post-victory danger.
