# EP386-十一出行不想当大冤种？咱们来听听资深领队的小巧思！

## Summary
This [[WushichaYanjiusuo|无时差研究所]] episode asks why China's National Day / Golden Week trips feel expensive, crowded, and hard to optimize. Guest [[LaobaoTravelLeader|老宝]], a senior leader with [[DaocaorenTravel|稻草人旅行]], explains peak-holiday pricing through concentrated public leave, seasonal hotel and airline economics, travel-agency resource constraints, and traveler confidence after the post-pandemic outbound recovery.

The practical synthesis is that [[HolidayPeakTravelPlanning|peak-holiday travel planning]] works better when travelers separate desired experience elements from famous destination IP: choose large cities when residents leave, avoid top scenic cores, use substitute landscapes, reverse the normal route, shift departure by a few days, set airfare thresholds, book cancellable hotels, and consider nearby-city flights. The episode also extends [[TravelAgencyItineraryOperations|travel-agency itinerary operations]] through Daocaoren's route research, leader training, launch-route preparation, and holiday backup system.

## Key Claims
- National Day travel inflation is not only platform or merchant exploitation; it also reflects concentrated Chinese public leave, strong long-trip demand, limited hotel and restaurant capacity, labor costs, and tourism businesses using peak months to support quieter seasons.
- Ordinary workers face a structural tradeoff: public holidays are expensive and crowded, but they are often the only periods when work can be fully interrupted.
- [[HolidayPeakTravelPlanning]] depends on element substitution more than pure bargain hunting: travelers should identify the landscape, season, or city experience they want, then avoid the most obvious IP if they also want lower crowding or price.
- Route design can reduce pressure through reverse direction, off-core attractions, nearby airports, a few days of schedule shift, cancellable hotels, and psychological airfare thresholds, while last-minute airfare "bottom fishing" remains risky.
- [[DaocaorenTravel|稻草人旅行]] is presented as a small-group travel operator whose National Day capacity depends heavily on leader availability, pre-booked resources, in-destination suppliers, route-development SOPs, and leader backup.
- [[LaobaoTravelLeader|老宝]] describes leader work as preparation-heavy: a new route may require weeks of reading, media watching, point-by-point route digestion, restroom and timing notes, and the ability to lead an unfamiliar destination as if already familiar.
- [[Egypt]] is presented as a high-cultural-density destination where guided travel can be rational for travelers who lack planning time, want interpretation, or dislike local uncertainty around negotiation and service friction.
- The episode's value judgment is that travel remains worthwhile even when costly or tiring because anticipation, shared hardship, scenery, music, photos, and later retelling can become durable memory and daily-work motivation.
