# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》203｜太皇太后吕雉 三杀赵王

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the kingship campaign begun in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-202-lishishang-yin-lengbaoli-bei-esi-de-ren-shi-ta-2-lrs7h5hhj0m0oixd6gw8flqpi0nd|Hanji 202 part 2]]. [[TianZichunWesternHan|田子春]] uses a favored palace intermediary identified only as 张氏 in the supplied summary to make [[LuLuWesternHan|吕禄]]'s enfeoffment serve [[LuZhi|吕雉]]'s family objective, then presents [[LiuZeLangyeKingWesternHan|刘泽]]'s kingship as compensation that can reduce Liu-family and ministerial resistance.

The durable synthesis is a form of [[InterestEmbeddedPoliticalBrokerage|多方利益嵌入式政治经纪]]: Tian does not ask directly for his client's title, but embeds it in a sequence that offers Lu Zhi dynastic advantage, the intermediary reward, opponents symbolic reassurance, and Liu Ze promotion. The account remains source-bounded, especially because the summary calls the intermediary 张氏 rather than securely identifying him, treats Lu Zhi's later regret as Tian's prediction, and ends after Liu Ze has departed Chang'an with the decree.

## Key Claims

- Tian Zichun's son invites the trusted palace intermediary to a banquet where Tian uses marquis-level display and a discussion of rank to awaken the guest's interest in advancement.
- Tian argues that Lu Zhi wants to make Lü Lu a king but hesitates because ministers oppose further Lü-family enfeoffment.
- After [[LiuHuiZhaoKingWesternHan|刘恢]]'s death leaves Zhao vacant, Lu Zhi reportedly proposes moving [[LiuHeng|刘恒]] from Dai to Zhao so that Lü Lu can receive Dai; Liu Heng declines the transfer.
- Tian urges the intermediary to have senior ministers petition for Lü Lu's enfeoffment and links success to the intermediary's own prospect of a large marquisate.
- The episode says the intermediary approaches Lü Chan and Chen Ping, after which Lü Lu is made king of Zhao and his father Lü Shizhi is posthumously honored with the same royal title.
- Tian refuses half of the intermediary's reported gold reward and instead proposes a second move: grant an older Liu relative a nominal kingship to reduce resentment over a Lü taking Zhao.
- Liu Ze is selected because the episode presents him as a senior Liu relative, a merit-holder and commander, and the son-in-law of Fan Kuai and Lu Xu; Lu Zhi then grants him Langye, reportedly carved from Qi territory.
- Tian warns Liu Ze to leave Chang'an immediately. Lu Zhi reportedly regrets the grant and sends to recover the decree, but Liu Ze has already departed with it.
- The episode recaps a succession of Zhao kings—Zhang Ao, Liu Ruyi, Liu You, Liu Hui, the proposed Liu Heng transfer, and Lü Lu—but not every death or removal in that chain is caused by Lu Zhi in the same way.
