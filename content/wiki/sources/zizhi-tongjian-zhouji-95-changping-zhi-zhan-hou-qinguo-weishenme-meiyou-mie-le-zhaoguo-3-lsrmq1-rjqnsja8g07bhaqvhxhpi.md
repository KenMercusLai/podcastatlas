# 《资治通鉴·周纪》95丨长平之战后秦国为什么没有灭了赵国（3）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the post-[[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] branch from [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-2-lutsvwvnwzsezoorhe-vu7yjthyx|Zhouji 95 part 2]] by moving from failed six-city settlement and difficult [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] revival into [[FanJu|范雎]]'s unresolved revenge against [[WeiQiWarringStates|魏齐]]. [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] invites [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] to Qin under the language of personal friendship, detains him, and forces [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] to choose between protecting Wei Qi and recovering a senior Zhao royal relative.

The episode's larger synthesis is that the post-Changping pause does not separate military conflict from aristocratic ethics. [[YuQing|虞卿]] gives up office to help Wei Qi flee, [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] and [[HouYing|侯嬴]] enter as Wei-side judges of whether a dangerous guest should be received, and Wei Qi's suicide lets Zhao exchange his head for Pingyuanjun. The episode closes by asking why people whom the histories praise would protect Fan Ju's enemy, making [[AristocraticFriendshipStateConflict|贵族义气与国家秩序冲突]] the new problem inside [[PersonalRevengeThroughStatePower|以国力行私仇]].

## Key Claims
- [[KongBin|孔斌]]'s "deadly illness" judgment frames the episode: [[QinState|秦国]] is preparing to annex the world, while the eastern states are too weak and divided for Zhao's renewed hezong hopes to be easy.
- Qin's pressure on Zhao is not only military. Qin Zhaoxiang Wang uses social etiquette, hostage detention, and Fan Ju's private revenge to turn Pingyuanjun's protection of Wei Qi into a state-level crisis.
- The episode uses [[Shiji|《史记》]] to explain why Pingyuanjun goes to Qin: the Qin king writes as if inviting a private drinking companion and future trusted friend, making refusal politically and ritually difficult.
- Pingyuanjun refuses to betray Wei Qi as a friend, but Zhao Dan prioritizes recovering Pingyuanjun and avoiding Qin's attack, so Zhao troops surround Pingyuanjun's house and hunt Wei Qi.
- [[YuQing|虞卿]] decides that he cannot persuade Zhao Dan to reverse the order, gives up his Zhao chancellor position, and flees with Wei Qi through [[Daliang|大梁]] toward a possible Chu refuge.
- [[HouYing|侯嬴]] pushes Xinlingjun to receive Yu Qing and Wei Qi by treating Yu Qing's abandoned office and loyalty to a friend as proof of character.
- Wei Qi's suicide resolves the immediate demand but exposes the honor system's volatility: hearing that Xinlingjun once hesitated is enough to make him choose death.
- Exchanging Wei Qi's head brings Pingyuanjun home but does not bring peace; [[WangLing|王陵]] attacks Zhao while [[BaiQi|白起]] is described as ill, and [[Handan|邯郸]] remains exposed after the loss of Shangdang.
- The episode leaves a value question open: Fan Ju's revenge is understandable given his earlier suffering, but the people who resist that revenge are also presented as morally luminous in the histories.

## Key Quotes
> "死病无良医" - Kong Bin's explanation for withdrawing from office in a world where Qin is rising and the six states are failing.

> "不疑之交" - the friendship language Qin Zhaoxiang Wang uses to draw Pingyuanjun into Qin.

> "范雎太有报仇理由了" - the host's modern-reader framing of why Fan Ju's vengeance feels morally intelligible.
