## Summary
This [[MarketplaceTech]] episode tours an [[Equinix]] data center inside a 1929 [[PacificTelephoneAndTelegraph]] building in downtown Palo Alto, using one building to connect telephone switching, early internet search, [[PaloAltoInternetExchange|PAIX]], cloud infrastructure, and AI traffic. [[EddieEspinosa]] frames the building as a concrete reminder that the internet and AI depend on physical rooms, racks, cages, fiber, and interconnection. The episode's strongest contribution is to add an interconnection layer to the wiki's AI-infrastructure branch: compute scale matters, but networks also need neutral places where many systems can meet.

## Key Claims
- The Palo Alto building was built in 1929 for telephone switchboards, showing that today's digital infrastructure sits on older communications infrastructure.
- [[Equinix]] is described as a colocation company where customers lease space for computing equipment and data traffic, making [[ColocationDataCenter]] a physical market structure rather than an abstract cloud service.
- The basement hosted [[AltaVista]] around 1995, tying the building to the early commercial search era before today's polished big-tech campus imagery.
- The first cage of [[PaloAltoInternetExchange|PAIX]] appeared in the building in 1996 and helped pioneer the [[NeutralInternetExchange]] model behind modern colocation.
- PAIX mattered because it let many networks connect in a neutral location, making the internet less dependent on a few research and telecom connection points.
- The episode links the old need to connect voice traffic with the newer need to connect bits, bytes, cloud services, and AI workloads.
- The source extends [[StrategicAIInfrastructureDependence]] by showing that AI infrastructure depends not only on chips, power, and capital, but also on dense physical network interconnection.
- The APM promo segment after the episode discusses geoengineering and is not treated as part of the main Marketplace Tech story.
