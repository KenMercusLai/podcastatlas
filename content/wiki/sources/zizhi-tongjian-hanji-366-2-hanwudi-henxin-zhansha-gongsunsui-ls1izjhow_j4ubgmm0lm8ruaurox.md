# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》366-2｜汉武帝狠心斩杀公孙遂

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode closes the Han conquest of [[WimanJoseon|卫氏朝鲜]] by joining battlefield delay, divided command, emergency delegation, court punishment, elite defection, regime collapse, and the creation of the [[HanFourCommanderies|汉四郡]]. [[GongsunSuiWesternHan|公孙遂]] uses an imperial tally to arrest [[YangPuWesternHan|楼船将军杨仆]] and merge his force into [[XunZhiWesternHan|左将军荀彘]]'s army, but [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] executes Gongsun Sui after the report; the host interprets that death as an attempt to redirect troop anger and stabilize command, while the received narrative itself establishes the sequence rather than the emperor's private motive.

The episode then follows the intensified assault, defections inside [[WimanJoseon|卫氏朝鲜]], the killing of [[UgeoWimanJoseon|卫右渠]], final resistance at [[WangxianCity|王险城]], rewards for defectors, punishment of both Han generals, and commandery rule. Its closing [[BanGuEasternHan|班固]] passage treats Jizi's education as the source of simple customs and later Han officials and merchants as a corrupting influence; the wiki retains this as transmitted moral historiography, not neutral evidence about an entire people.

## Key Claims

- The Han siege stalls because Xun Zhi and Yang Pu do not coordinate attack dates and each maintains a different surrender channel with Wiman Joseon.
- Gongsun Sui accepts Xun Zhi's accusation, summons Yang Pu under imperial authority, arrests him, and combines the armies without first returning to the emperor for a narrower ruling.
- Han Wudi executes Gongsun Sui after his report; the episode's explanation that this absorbs troop anger and protects imperial authority is plausible within its narrative but remains an attributed interpretation.
- Unified command increases pressure on Wiman Joseon, whose senior officials split from King Ugeo, defect, and eventually arrange his death and the suppression of remaining resistance.
- The conquest ends with the establishment of Lelang, Lintun, Xuantu, and Zhenfan commanderies and with differentiated punishment of Xun Zhi and Yang Pu.
- The Ban Gu passage contrasts Jizi's ritual, agricultural, and legal education with later official and merchant predation, making social order depend on institutional example as well as written law.

## Key Quotes

> “公孙遂也认同左将军的看法。” — the episode's turning point from investigation to one-sided command intervention.

> “制度与教化会影响社会风气。” — the episode's concluding moral synthesis of the Ban Gu passage.

## Connections

- [[GongsunSuiWesternHan|公孙遂]] - imperial investigator whose arrest and army merger are followed by his own execution.
- [[XunZhiWesternHan|荀彘]] - left general who accuses Yang Pu, receives the combined force, completes the campaign, and is later executed for command conflict.
- [[YangPuWesternHan|杨仆]] - tower-ship general whose separate surrender channel, arrest, casualties, and redemption to commoner status define the second command branch.
- [[UgeoWimanJoseon|卫右渠]] and [[WimanJoseon|卫氏朝鲜]] - king and polity defeated after internal defection and intensified Han attack.
- [[HanFourCommanderies|汉四郡]] - administrative aftermath presented as Lelang, Lintun, Xuantu, and Zhenfan.
- [[CommandSynchronizationFailure|军令同步失灵]] - explains how divergent attack timing and surrender channels prolong the campaign.
- [[EmergencyFrontierDiscretion|临机边疆裁量]] - Gongsun Sui is a negative boundary case in which broad field authority does not protect a consequential command decision from later punishment.
- [[BanGuEasternHan|班固]] - later historian whose Jizi-and-customs evaluation supplies the closing moral frame.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with existing wiki content is adopted. The source deepens the wiki's command-synchronization and emergency-discretion branches without proving that one management model explains every Han frontier campaign.
- Text normalization: the supplied summary's 王显城, 韩茵, 王家, 成吉, and 陆醉 are routed to the received forms [[WangxianCity|王险城]], 韩阴, 王唊, 成巳, and 路人之子最. The exact reading of 尼谿相参 and several personal names remains a textual issue beyond this episode.
- Motive qualification: the received account says Han Wudi killed Gongsun Sui after the report; the claim that he did so specifically to calm Yang Pu's soldiers and deflect anger from himself is the host's inference.
- Historical-scope qualification: the claim that Joseon had already been Yan territory in the Spring and Autumn period is not adopted; the episode does not supply the evidence needed to settle chronology or territorial extent.
- The Jizi, “East Yi,” sexual-morality, theft, and legal-growth claims are transmitted elite historiography. They should not be treated as timeless ethnic traits or as a complete social history of the region.
