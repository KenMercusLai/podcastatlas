# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》916丨命好和运好哪个更重要？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 179 CE late-Han layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-917-ruhe-xunsu-zhaodao-shengzhi-jiaxin-de-pojudian-lk2zt5c3onvui9a4ppbowshy66tg|Hanji 917]]. It opens with [[FateChangeThroughVirtuePractice|命运可修的行动框架]], then follows [[YuGuirenLateHan|于贵人]]'s funeral as [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] turns the public humiliation of [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]]'s corpse into a palace-side regrouping against [[YangQiuLateHan|阳球]]. The second half explains [[ShangshuTaiImperialAccess|尚书台近侍权力]] and records [[ShenZhongLateHan|沈忠]]'s shelved memorial against [[ZhuYuLateHan|朱瑀]] and wider eunuch corruption, sharpening [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|君臣反馈失灵]] before the Hanji 917-918 backlash.

## Key Claims
- The opening distinguishes fixed "命" from changeable "运"; its practical focus is self-knowledge, action, and effort, while its faith/energy claims remain source-scoped worldview material.
- [[YuGuirenLateHan|于贵人]]'s funeral creates the public scene in which [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] sees [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]]'s mutilated corpse abandoned by the road.
- Cao Jie turns that scene into a warning that palace eunuchs must reunite and persuade [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] that [[YangQiuLateHan|阳球]] cannot remain sili xiaowei.
- Yang Qiu's transfer to weiwei removes his immediate prosecutorial leverage and gives Cao Jie and [[ZhuYuLateHan|朱瑀]] room to recover.
- The shangshu ling office matters less because of formal rank than because it controls memorial/document flow and enables frequent direct access to the emperor.
- [[ShenZhongLateHan|沈忠]]'s memorial accuses Zhu Yu and the eunuch network of fabricating rebellion charges, monopolizing favor for relatives, and corrupting provincial selection channels.
- Shen Zhong treats disasters and omens as political warnings, offers direct confrontation, and stakes his body and family on the truth of the memorial; the court shelves the document without reply.
- The closing preserves a qualification against treating every eunuch as corrupt, noting that a later source will discuss an upright eunuch.
