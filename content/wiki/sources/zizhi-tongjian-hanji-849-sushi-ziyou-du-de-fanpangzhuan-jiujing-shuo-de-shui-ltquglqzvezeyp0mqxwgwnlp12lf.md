# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》849丨苏轼自幼读的《范滂传》，究竟说的谁？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 159 CE [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] field immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-850-huandi-weihe-zhiyi-sha-zhongchen-lokxtceq7vv-giec6iufwwk9fwk7|Hanji 850]]. It opens with a principles, rules, and bottom-line frame from [[MenciusText|《孟子》]] and [[XunziText|《荀子》]], then turns to [[FanPangLateHan|范滂]] as a young Runan talent recommended by [[HuangQiongLateHan|黄琼]] and sent as a Qingzhao envoy whose reputation makes corrupt officials abandon office.

The second half follows [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] recommending five reclusive scholars: [[XuZhiLateHan|徐稚]], [[JiangGongLateHan|姜公]], [[YuanHongLateHan|袁闳]], [[WeiZhuLateHan|韦著]], and [[LiTanLateHan|李昙]]. Their refusal of Huan's gifts and summons makes [[PrincipledOfficeRefusal|原则性拒仕]] the episode's durable contribution: capable scholars are not merely unavailable, but withholding service because court conditions have lost moral credibility.

## Key Claims

- The opening rule-and-bottom-line frame is source-scoped self-cultivation language used to prepare Fan Pang's later official integrity.
- [[HuangQiongLateHan|黄琼]] recognizes [[FanPangLateHan|范滂]] in 159 CE and brings him into office, adding an earlier talent-recognition layer before Fan Pang's later Runan and Party Prohibition roles.
- Fan Pang's Qingzhao-envoy mission to [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]] is presented as principled inspection: he impeaches more than twenty governors, high officials, and powerful dependents while accepting punishment if the charges prove wrong.
- Fan Pang's weed-removal analogy frames anti-corruption enforcement as loyal service rather than private animus.
- [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] recommends five reclusive scholars to Huan, but the emperor's formal gifts do not overcome their refusal to serve.
- [[XuZhiLateHan|徐稚]] combines poverty, farming, repeated office refusal, and careful mourning courtesy; Chen Fan's hanging-couch story marks elite respect for a recluse who still will not enter office.
- [[JiangGongLateHan|姜公]], [[YuanHongLateHan|袁闳]], [[WeiZhuLateHan|韦著]], and [[LiTanLateHan|李昙]] turn filial conduct, family restraint, teaching, and self-cultivation into evidence that refusal is moral judgment, not lack of ability.

## Key Quotes

> "不以规矩，不能成方圆" - the opening rule-and-bottom-line frame from Mencius.

> "忠臣须除奸，才能保证王道清平" - Fan Pang's weed-removal analogy for official cleanup.

> "天下无道则隐，有道则至" - the episode's explanation of why worthy scholars may refuse court summons.
