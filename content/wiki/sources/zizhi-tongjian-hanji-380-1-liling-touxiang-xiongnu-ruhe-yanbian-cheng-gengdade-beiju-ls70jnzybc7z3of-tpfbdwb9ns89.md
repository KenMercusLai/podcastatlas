# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》380-1｜李陵投降匈奴，如何演变成更大的悲剧？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] short episode opens the [[LiLingWesternHan|李陵]] tragedy before the later [[SuWuWesternHan|苏武]] persuasion scene in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-398-ruguo-ni-shi-liling-hui-ti-xiongnu-quanjiang-suwu-ma-lrvkyakuzpnv77uoq4wkrt7vcuzy|Hanji 398]]. It places Li Ling's surrender to [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] inside a late-[[HanWudi|汉武帝]] field of war exhaustion, liquor monopoly finance, imperial ritual touring, drought, frontier fear, and multi-route campaigns under [[LiGuangliWesternHan|李广利]], [[LuBodeWesternHan|路博德]], [[HanShuoWesternHan|韩说]], and [[GongsunAoWesternHan|公孙敖]].

The durable synthesis is that Li Ling is introduced as a tragic rather than flatly treasonous figure. As [[LiGuangHanGeneral|李广]]'s grandson and a fatherless posthumous son, he actively seeks frontier service; after fighting with five thousand infantry against a much larger Xiongnu force near Junji Mountain, betrayal, defeat, capture, and surrender become the first turn in a larger disaster that later includes mistaken blame over [[LiXuWesternHan|李绪]] and clan extermination.

## Key Claims

- Li Ling's surrender is framed as the start of an expanding tragedy, not as a self-contained loyalty failure.
- The episode identifies him as Li Guang's grandson and a posthumous son, making family military reputation part of the pressure around his choice.
- Han Wudi's anti-Xiongnu strategy is already fiscally strained by continuous warfare; the liquor monopoly appears here as a treasury-replenishing measure.
- The same year combines imperial ritual ambition and ineffective fangshi searches with drought, Xiongnu incursions, and severe punishment of a frightened frontier governor.
- The 97 BCE campaign scale is large and fragmented: Li Guangli leads cavalry and infantry from Shuofang, Lu Bode joins him, Han Shuo exits from Wuyuan, and Gongsun Ao attacks from Yanmen.
- The multi-route campaign produces limited results before Li Ling's smaller infantry force becomes the episode's dramatic contrast.
- Later sympathy for Li Ling comes from the combination of lonely battle, surrender under defeat, misattributed blame, and family destruction.

## Key Quotes

> "李陵投敌，归顺了匈奴。" - the episode's blunt statement of the surrender.

> "没有遗臭万年，反而被后人同情。" - the host's reception frame for Li Ling.
