# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》296-1｜卫青挂帅出征，为汉朝吊打匈奴

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]]'s first 元朔六年 departure from Dingxiang against the [[Xiongnu|匈奴]]. [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] supplies a command team combining cavalry leaders, the Xiongnu-origin [[ZhaoXinWesternHan|赵信]], the grassland route knowledge of [[ZhangQianWesternHan|张骞]], and a dedicated crossbow force under [[LiJuWesternHan|李沮]].

The Han army rapidly destroys a rear-guard formation, but Wei Qing does not treat several thousand reported kills as proof that the campaign objective has been met. Because the chanyu's main force remains unlocated, he withdraws to the frontier commanderies, rests the army, and sends scouts before the next operation. The episode's durable contribution is [[LocalVictoryCampaignUncertainty|局部胜利与战役态势不确定性]]: a favorable contact result can increase the need for reconnaissance when it reveals that the decisive enemy force is elsewhere.

## Key Claims

- In February 123 BCE, Wei Qing reportedly leaves Dingxiang for a direct anti-Xiongnu operation along the shortest approach toward the chanyu's eastern pasture area.
- The episode says earlier victories have supplied captured material, frontier experience, and confidence, making the expedition cumulative rather than improvised.
- Han Wudi assigns [[GongsunHeWesternHan|公孙贺]], [[SuJianWesternHan|苏建]], [[LiGuangHanGeneral|李广]], Zhao Xin, Li Ju, and Zhang Qian to roles spanning maneuver, opponent knowledge, route guidance, and missile support.
- Zhao Xin is presented as a former Xiongnu lesser king and chancellor who entered Han service; the host treats his appointment to the front as a way to improve the search for the chanyu's main force.
- Zhang Qian's captivity-era familiarity with grassland geography, pasture, and water is presented as route and sustainment knowledge rather than only diplomatic experience.
- Zhao Xin first meets a Xiongnu rear guard; Gongsun He and Su Jian reportedly envelop it while Li Ju's crossbow troops provide dense fire, ending the engagement quickly with several thousand enemy killed.
- Wei Qing infers from the easy local victory that the chanyu's main body is absent and may be positioned for a counterstroke, so he returns to Dingxiang, Yunzhong, and Yanmen to rest and reconnoiter before moving again.

## Key Quotes

> “别人从胜利当中看到了功名利禄，卫青却从胜利当中闻到了危险的气息。” - the episode's central contrast between visible success and unresolved campaign risk.

> “清醒冷静，是卫青在众多名将当中最大的特点。” - the host's character judgment based on the decision to stop, verify, and prepare again.
