# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》491丨中国外交最强硬的时刻，竟是在西汉？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate aftermath of [[ChenTangWesternHan|陈汤]] and [[GanYanshouWesternHan|甘延寿]] killing [[ZhizhiChanyu|郅支单于]] in 36 BCE. It follows the delivery of Zhizhi Chanyu's head to Chang'an, the commanders' hardline victory memorial, the ten-day public display, court celebration, amnesty, and first reward language before later episodes examine the accusations, pardon, and under-reward dispute.

The durable synthesis is that frontier victory becomes political theater as well as military success. The episode treats the famous "犯我强汉者，虽远必诛" posture as a public deterrence statement, while also preserving Chen Tang's weakness: poverty memory, greed, captured valuables, and paid memorial-writing create later vulnerabilities that enemies can turn into legal and career danger.

## Key Claims

- Chen Tang is presented as brave and strategically capable but politically immature, with real greed and money vulnerability.
- The Zhizhi campaign produces a large military and symbolic result: Zhizhi Chanyu is killed, Han envoy credentials and letters are recovered, captives and surrenders are distributed among Western Regions participants, and the head is sent to Chang'an in 35 BCE.
- Gan Yanshou and Chen Tang's memorial frames [[HuhanyeChanyuWesternHan|呼韩邪单于]] as the submitted northern vassal and Zhizhi Chanyu as the remaining defiant rebel who fled west and refused guilt.
- The episode links Chen Tang's victory memorial to the hardline sentence "犯我强汉者，虽远必诛," making the campaign a model of distance-defying punishment.
- [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] balances ritual objection and deterrence: after [[KuangHengWesternHan|匡衡]] and others object to prolonged head display in spring, Yuan orders a ten-day display and burial.
- Yuan celebrates the victory with suburban and ancestral announcements, amnesty, banquet, and title rewards: Chen Tang becomes Guannei Hou, while Gan Yanshou becomes Yicheng Hou.
- The source then shifts to Yuan's late reign: [[HanChengdi|刘骜 / 后来的汉成帝]] angers Yuan by arriving late and unemotional at 中山哀王刘竟's mourning, while [[ShiDanWesternHan|史丹 / 施丹]] protects the crown prince by taking responsibility for the no-cry instruction.
- Disasters, Yuan's illness, partial restoration of old ancestral shrines and mausoleum parks, Huhanye's renewed court request, and [[WangZhaojun|王昭君]]'s imminent appearance set up the following episodes.

## Key Quotes

> "犯我强汉者，虽远必诛" - the episode's emblem of the Zhizhi victory's hardline diplomatic posture.

> "有勇有谋才是强者思维" - the opening frame for Chen Tang's battlefield ability.
