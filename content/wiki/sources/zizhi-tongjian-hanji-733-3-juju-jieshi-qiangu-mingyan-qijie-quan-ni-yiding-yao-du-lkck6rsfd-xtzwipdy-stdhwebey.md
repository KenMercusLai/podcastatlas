# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》733-3丨句句皆是千古名言！淇姐劝你一定要读！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode steps away from a chronological Han narrative to read the text rendered in the transcript as 《韩遥赋》 and normalized here as [[HanyaoFu|《寒窑赋》]]. The host frames it as a compact lesson on 命, 运, timing, life-course reversal, and conduct inside unequal circumstances.

The source repeatedly uses natural, historical, and social contrasts to argue that ability, virtue, ambition, and effort do not automatically produce outcomes without favorable timing. Its practical conclusion is a restrained ethic: preserve dignity and proportion in adversity, avoid exhausting prosperity when circumstances turn favorable, and keep the work's attribution and metaphysical claims source-scoped.

## Key Claims

- The episode opens with questions about 命, 运, how to live, and how to conduct oneself within social circles, making the installment a classical-text reflection rather than a standard event narration.
- [[HanyaoFu|《寒窑赋》]] is presented as a text where talent, will, virtue, and strength still require favorable timing; the source's central line is that soaring ambition cannot open its own path without 运.
- The source uses historical contrasts involving figures such as [[Confucius|孔子]], [[JiangTaigong|姜太公]], [[YanHui|颜回]], [[ZhangLiang|张良]], [[XiaoHe|萧何]], [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]], [[XiangYu|项羽]], [[LiuBang|刘邦]], and [[HanXin|韩信]] to show that merit and outcome do not map cleanly onto one another.
- The text's low-point ethic emphasizes self-possession: even when clothing is torn or fortune is blocked, one should preserve ritual bearing, inner composure, and one's proper bounds.
- The source's high-point ethic is humility: the narrated ascent from poverty in Luoyang to high court rank is used to say that success is not merely personal ability but a convergence of 时, 运, and 命.
- The closing lesson says prosperity should not be exhausted and poverty should not become self-deception; the host treats this as a cyclical view of human fortune and natural order.

## Key Quotes

> "天有不测风云，人有旦夕祸福" - the episode's opening formula for human and natural uncertainty.

> "人有冲天之志，非运不能自通" - the source's core timing claim.

> "人道我贵，非我之能也" - the poverty-to-high-office contrast attributed to the text's speaker.

> "富贵不可尽用，贫贱不可自欺" - the closing practical admonition.
