# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》826丨乱世的罪与恶，背后是贪婪的人性

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 145 CE layer immediately before the existing [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-827-yi-ju-hua-yinfa-de-diwang-zhisi-lnf6rz1i2vmgsclgiejbh0qevzc2|Hanji 827]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-828-da-jiangjun-liangji-shanquan-feili-de-donghan-jianchen-llqnqijagtmdlgitulddxspncu0e|Hanji 828]] succession sequence. It follows Western Qiang campaign-finance corruption, [[LiangBingLateHan|梁并]]'s trust-based surrender policy, [[TengFuLateHan|滕抚]] and [[FengGunLateHan|冯绲]]'s Xu-Yang anti-bandit campaign, and [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]]'s resentment after [[ZhongHaoLateHan|种暠]] reports [[LiuJunshiLateHan|刘君世]]'s gold-snake bribery.

The durable synthesis is that late-Han disorder is already operating through linked military, local, and court failures before Emperor Zhi's poisoning: campaign money becomes private extraction, anti-bandit success depends on credible commanders and local reward mobilization, and corruption reporting can become personal danger when Liang Ji controls retaliation channels.

## Key Claims

- The Western Qiang war costs more than eighty yi, but generals siphon pay and provisions, bribe officials, and leave soldiers dying needlessly.
- Liang Bing uses trust and grace to induce Li Nan, Hu Nu, and tens of thousands of Qiang households to surrender, making Longyou temporarily peaceful.
- Liang Na seeks commanders for Xu-Yang unrest and appoints Teng Fu, while Zhao Xu assists Feng Gun in the campaign.
- Teng Fu opens rewards and bounties, defeats Ma Mian, Fan Rong, and Zhou Sheng, and lets local actors such as Xie An help finish the campaign.
- Xianbei, Lujiang, Liyang, and Ba Commandery unrest show that 145 CE disorder is not confined to one frontier or one rebel group.
- Zhong Hao reports Liu Junshi's gold patterned snake bribe to Liang Ji, and Liang later uses the failed Ba Commandery suppression to arrest Zhong Hao and Ying Cheng.
- Li Gu's memorial saves Zhong Hao and Ying Cheng from execution, but Liang Ji's resentment extends toward Du Qiao after Du refuses the gold snake and mourning attendance for Liang's daughter.

## Key Quotes

> "白骨相望，堆积旷野" - the episode's image of soldiers destroyed by war-finance corruption.

> "贪婪的人性" - the host's closing frame for the repeated moral source of disorder.

> "莫复尽心" - the warning that punishing failed service indiscriminately makes officials stop taking real risks.
