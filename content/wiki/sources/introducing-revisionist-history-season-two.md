# Introducing Revisionist History Season Two

## Summary

This short [[RevisionistHistory|Revisionist History]] trailer has [[MalcolmGladwell]] describe his attraction to large explanatory ideas and the interview, reading, and library work behind the show's second season. Philosopher [[MarkCohen]] introduces [[Heraclitus]]' river image and the [[ShipOfTheseus|Ship of Theseus]] as a possible framework for stories about McDonald's fries, Los Angeles golf courses, and a dysfunctional friendship.

The trailer makes that connection provisional rather than demonstrated. Gladwell says reasonable people may disagree about whether the stories share one thread, so the source establishes a research question and season tone rather than evidence that identity through change explains every announced case.

## Key Claims

- Gladwell describes a recurring preference for one large idea that might connect otherwise dissimilar subjects.
- He reports interviewing roughly 30 or 40 people, reading several dozen books, and searching the New York University library for historically strange subjects worth revisiting.
- [[MarkCohen]] explains the Heraclitean river image and asks whether a ship remains the same object after its boards are replaced one at a time.
- [[ShipOfTheseus]] supplies a possible season-level question about what is preserved, lost, or transformed through gradual change.
- The trailer previews stories about McDonald's former fries, the history of Los Angeles golf courses, and an intensely dysfunctional friendship.
- Gladwell explicitly leaves the proposed unifying theory open to disagreement.
- Season two was announced to launch on June 15, 2017.

## Key Quotes

> “reasonable minds will probably disagree” - the trailer's qualification of its proposed unifying theory.

> “sometimes the past deserves a second chance” - the program premise used in the closing invitation.
