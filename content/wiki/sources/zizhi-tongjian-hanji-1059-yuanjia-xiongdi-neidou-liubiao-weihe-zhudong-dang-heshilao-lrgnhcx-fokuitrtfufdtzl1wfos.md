# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1059丨袁家兄弟内斗，刘表为何主动当和事佬？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the Jian'an 8 Yuan-family fracture after [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1058-shaonian-sunquan-chuzhang-jiangdong-ruhe-yingdui-caocao-fanan-lgqs4-a9qgf21woeh-ppinw4z9jg|Hanji 1058]]. [[YuanTan|袁谭]] is pressed by [[YuanShang|袁尚]] and sends [[XinPiLateHan|辛毗]] to ask [[CaoCao|曹操]] for help, while [[LiuBiao|刘表]] writes separate letters urging both brothers to stop fighting and unite against Cao. The source reads Liu Biao's mediation as self-preserving delay, then uses the Hanlu dog and Dongguo hare story to explain [[MutualExhaustionThirdPartyGain|mutual exhaustion third-party gain]] before [[XunYou|荀攸]] argues that Cao should exploit the Yuan split before one brother absorbs the other.

## Key Claims
- The opening relationship segment is a source-scoped moral frame: some relationships are built on value extraction, while others remain after status or usefulness changes.
- In 203 CE, after [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s death, Yuan Tan and Yuan Shang's rivalry has become violent enough that Yuan Tan asks Cao Cao, the Yuan family's enemy, for aid.
- Liu Biao first criticizes Yuan Tan for abandoning father-avenging and brotherly duty by turning to Cao Cao, using shame, kinship, and reputation pressure to block outside intervention.
- Liu Biao then tells Yuan Shang to tolerate Yuan Tan, combine different strengths, and join his brother against Cao Cao rather than let internal conflict destroy the Yuan inheritance.
- Liu Biao's mediation is not presented as pure moral concern; the episode says he wants the Yuan brothers to keep Cao Cao occupied and delay Cao's attack on Jingzhou and northern unification.
- The Hanlu dog and Dongguo hare parable from `Zhanguo Ce` becomes the episode's central analogy for [[MutualExhaustionThirdPartyGain|两败俱伤、旁人得利]].
- Cao Cao's staff initially argue for attacking Liu Biao while allowing the Yuan brothers to exhaust each other.
- Xun You gives the counterargument: Liu Biao is defensive and lacks broader ambition, while the Yuan family still holds four provinces and large forces, so letting one brother swallow the other could recreate a hard northern opponent.
- The episode qualifies [[PressureReleaseInfightingStrategy|撤压诱发内斗]]: relaxing pressure can open a rival split, but Cao's side still has to intervene before internal conflict resolves into reconsolidation.

## Key Quotes
> "韩卢犬和东郭兔" - Liu Biao's warning image for destructive rivalry that lets an outsider benefit.

> "价值在的时候人情可暖，价值一丢失人情就显得薄了。" - opening relationship frame, kept source-scoped.
