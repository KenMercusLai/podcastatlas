# 【年终复盘③】用 Kimi 加速 Kimi：RSI前夜，一个AI infra人的10个Aha Moments｜对话赵晨阳

## Summary

This [[ShizilukouCrossing|十字路口Crossing]] year-end conversation has [[Koji]] and [[ZhaoChenyang|赵晨阳]] connect ten practical observations about [[RecursiveSelfImprovement|recursive self-improvement]], evaluation, open-source code governance, cheap intelligence, [[ComputerUseAgent|computer-use agents]], real-time voice systems, and historical organization design. Its strongest technical claim is that RSI is already appearing as a gradual reduction in human participation across data selection, architecture testing, kernel optimization, and model serving, but only where feedback is sufficiently objective, automated, and inexpensive. Its wider synthesis is that cheaper execution increases the value of judgment and accountability: agents can handle tedious digital work, while people still have to choose goals, protect relationships, define rules, grant authority, and own consequences.

## Key Claims

- [[ZhaoChenyang|赵晨阳]] treats RSI as a continuum in which older models contribute to the next generation's data, architecture, kernels, inference engines, and research workflow rather than as a single autonomous “takeoff” event.
- The episode says low-cost, automated, objective evaluation is the main near-term gate on RSI: speech-recognition error rates and kernel correctness or speed are easier to optimize than aesthetics, writing, chip fabrication, or drug experiments.
- Zhao reports using a model under explicit evaluation constraints to improve the performance of an approximately 80-million-parameter TTS model by about 1,700% in two days; this remains a guest-reported result without benchmark artifacts in the supplied file.
- AI lowers code-production cost faster than review and maintenance cost, so open-source projects need explicit merge gates, consistent standards, and maintainers who apply the same rules to themselves.
- The source argues that intelligence becoming cheap may weaken the bargaining value of routine cognitive work and concentrate wealth or decision power; it questions whether income transfers alone preserve meaningful autonomy.
- Computer-use agents become useful when screenshots and accessibility trees let them operate old software without an API, persist through tedious workflows, and return human attention, as illustrated by email negotiation, note export, appointments, and property-management tasks.
- Agent-to-agent exchanges can continue without resolution when neither side has authority to decide or accept responsibility, making human escalation a governance boundary rather than merely a capability gap.
- Interactive voice systems currently span native full-duplex models, turn-based audio models, and cascades joining voice detection, speech recognition, language processing, and speech synthesis; each route makes different latency, modularity, and product tradeoffs.
- Voice input can combine ASR with contextual language-model correction so names and specialist terms are repaired from personal vocabulary and surrounding text, making interface proximity as important as raw model quality.
- The historical-organization section argues that corruption and rule erosion propagate downward when senior actors hold power without responsibility or exempt themselves from the制度 they impose on others.
