# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》353-1｜杨仆如何当上中国首位海军司令？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the campaign deployment between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-352-2-hanwudi-weihe-fengshang-baijun-zhi-jiang-li16pfjb6p0owys5htecscfomhch|Hanji 352-2]]'s mobilization against [[Nanyue|南越]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-356-1-budong-yuren-ni-ping-sha-dang-lingdao-lsaa6j1jskivfhyvvpbpmnorq-jm|Hanji 356-1]]'s retrospective judgment of [[YangPuWesternHan|杨仆]]. It presents [[LuBodeWesternHan|路博德]] and Yang Pu as the two main commanders in a reported five-route, ten-wan-person advance toward Panyu, with Yang Pu leading the tower-ship force from [[YuzhangCommandery|豫章郡]].

The episode also adds the internal-political background it sees behind the war. [[ZhaoTuo|赵佗]] had built Nanyue from former Qin territory and shared power with the locally rooted [[LyuJiaNanyue|吕嘉]] family through office and marriage. Lü Jia's influence among local Yue communities made formal submission more than a royal decision. The source uses Yang Pu's title and force to frame him as China's first “naval commander,” but that superlative and the description of tower ships as ancient Yue inventions remain episode-attributed rather than settled historical classifications.

## Key Claims

- The episode treats the conquest of Nanyue as a major Han Wudi campaign comparable in significance, though not identical in theater, to the better-known northern victories associated with Wei Qing and Huo Qubing.
- Lu Bode is introduced as an experienced former Youbeiping administrator and veteran of a campaign with Huo Qubing, while Yang Pu is presented as a purchased-rank holder who proved capable and became known for harsh action against powerful families.
- Zhao Tuo's settlement joined his kingship to the Lü family's local influence, chancellorship, and marriage ties, giving Lü Jia substantial leverage over whether Nanyue would accept closer Han incorporation.
- [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] sends a reported ten-wan-person force by five routes, including Lu Bode from the Guiyang direction, Yang Pu from Yuzhang, and three routes led by previously surrendered Nanyue figures, with Panyu as the convergence point.
- Yang Pu commands a specially cultivated tower-ship force; the episode describes its large, elevated vessels as capable of both ranged and close combat in coastal waters.
- The label “China's first naval commander” is an interpretive modernization based on Yang Pu's tower-ship title and waterborne command, not a demonstrated equivalence to a modern navy or command structure.

## Key Quotes

> “汉武帝专门为帝国培养的楼船水师。” — the episode's description of the force assigned to Yang Pu.

> “五路大军约定最终在南越都城番禺会师。” — the reported convergence plan for the campaign.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with the existing wiki is adopted. This episode fills the deployment details left open by Hanji 352-2 and precedes Hanji 356-1's account of victory, alleged credit inflation, and renewed service.
- Name normalization: the transcript uses “路伯德”; the wiki routes it to the established received form [[LuBodeWesternHan|路博德]].
- The “first naval commander” title, ten-wan-person total, five-route particulars, places, ranks, service records, private motives, tower-ship origin, and ship capabilities remain episode-attributed pending primary-source and specialist comparison.
- The episode stops before the routes converge and does not itself establish the battle sequence, final conquest, or the later distribution of credit.
