# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》774丨班超接班人如何搞乱西域？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 102 CE Western Regions handoff immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-775-gan-xiang-mingyun-shuo-no-bukuishi-you-tounao-de-nvzhumu-lh0erdh890ooow6dn0wvf1fbzedf|Hanji 775]]. It follows [[BanChaoEasternHan|班超]]'s late request to return from the Western Regions, [[BanZhaoLateHan|班昭]]'s petition to [[EmperorHeOfHan|汉和帝]], Ban Chao's return to [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] and death, and [[RenShangLateHan|任尚]]'s hollow consultation before succeeding him.

The durable synthesis is that frontier succession depends on tacit local judgment as much as formal appointment. Ban Chao tells Ren Shang that Western Regions soldiers and polities need loose, simple, big-picture governance; Ren privately dismisses the advice as commonplace, turning the episode into an early case of [[LightTouchFrontierGovernance|light-touch frontier governance]] ignored before Hanji 779 records the later rebellion and withdrawal.

## Key Claims

- Ban Chao is portrayed as an aging frontier commander who has spent more than thirty years in the Western Regions and repeatedly asks to return before death.
- Ban Chao sends his son [[BanYongLateHan|班勇]] inward with an Anxi tribute mission, making Ban Yong's later Western Regions profile begin with family exposure to the frontier-to-court connection.
- Ban Zhao uses her inner-court teaching access to petition Emperor He, arguing that leaving a man over seventy in command risks both Western Regions disorder and the destruction of long-earned state achievement.
- Emperor He accepts the petition, recalls Ban Chao, and appoints him she sheng xiaowei after his return to Luoyang; Ban Chao dies the next month in 102 CE.
- Ren Shang is formally deferential during the handoff, but Ban Chao's advice is intentionally plain: frontier personnel and Western Regions polities are hard to control, so the new protector should forgive small faults and hold the big picture.
- Ren Shang privately treats the advice as ordinary and expected "extraordinary strategy," which the episode reads as pride, competitiveness, and failure to absorb predecessor experience.
- The host frames Western Regions command as a no-magic-strategy problem: the aim is maintaining equilibrium and not losing the inherited position rather than proving brilliance through novelty.
- The closing Deng Yu descendant teaser shifts the episode toward family virtue and later Deng-line figures, but that material remains setup for the next source.

## Key Quotes

> "但愿生入玉门关" - Ban Chao's return-home wish as presented in the episode.

> "宽简政策" / "总揽大纲即可" - the episode's compact rendering of Ban Chao's governing advice.

> "不战之法" / "不败之法" - the host's summary of the steadiness required in complex frontier command.

## Contradictions
