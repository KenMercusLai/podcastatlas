# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1081丨哀哉！曹操痛失最锋利的谋略之刃

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode links [[GuoJia|郭嘉]]'s death after the [[Wuhuan|乌桓]] campaign with [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s loss of a bold reader of opponents, then contrasts that loss with [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s approaching recruitment of [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]]. It also places [[TianChou|田畴]]'s refusal of enfeoffment beside [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s failure to attack Xu while Cao Cao was in the north, and briefly follows [[LadyWuSunJian|吴夫人]]'s death and [[CuiZhouping|崔州平]]'s friendship with Zhuge Liang.

The episode's strongest causal language remains counterfactual. Its claim that Guo Jia would have detected the Red Cliffs fire attack, linked-ships plan, or even the borrowed east wind is not treated as settled history. The more defensible organizational insight is narrower: an adviser can embody a distinctive judgment function, and losing that person exposes a leadership system unless the function has been distributed or replaced.

## Key Claims

- Guo Jia dies at age thirty-eight during the return from Cao Cao's northern campaign, after helping shape the risk-taking approach that defeated the Wuhuan leadership and secured the northern frontier.
- The episode characterizes Guo Jia's distinctive value as reading hesitation, fear, appetite, and factional weakness before opponents act, extending his existing record of sequencing and pressure-release judgments.
- The claim that Guo Jia's survival would have prevented Cao Cao's [[RedCliffsBattle|赤壁之战]] defeat is an untestable historical counterfactual, not a direct conclusion of the annalistic record.
- Tian Chou refuses a tinghou title and five-hundred-household fief after the Wuhuan campaign, reinforcing the distinction between public service and title-seeking already developed in [[EarnedRewardRefusal|有功辞赏]].
- Liu Biao regrets ignoring Liu Bei's proposal to attack Xu while Cao Cao campaigned in the north; the episode uses the missed window to distinguish recognizing an opportunity from deciding in time.
- Lady Wu dies after entrusting Zhang Zhao with affairs around Sun Quan and is buried with Sun Jian; the burial details remain source-scoped.
- Cui Zhouping is presented as Cui Lie's son, a reclusive scholar, and one of Zhuge Liang's friends alongside [[XuShuLateHan|徐庶]], giving him a historical-network role beyond his literary reception image.
- The closing Xiangyang-Nanyang discussion treats the location dispute over Zhuge Liang's farming years as a contest over spatial memory; the episode favors Xiangyang from its reading of the *Zizhi Tongjian*, while acknowledging the continuing dispute.

## Key Quotes

> "最不缺的就是机会，最难得的是决断的魄力。" - the episode's summary of Liu Biao's missed strategic window.

> "拒绝有时候比接受更需要力量。" - the episode's interpretation of Tian Chou's refusal of reward.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is recorded. The episode's claim that Guo Jia's death caused Cao Cao's later strategic decline and made the tripartite order possible is preserved as a source-scoped counterfactual, not merged into the wiki as verified causation.
- The Red Cliffs narrative is explicitly qualified: no source can establish that Guo Jia would have discovered every deception, predicted a favorable wind, or enabled Cao Cao to eliminate Liu Bei and Sun Quan.
- The Xiangyang-versus-Nanyang conclusion is preserved as the episode's reading of the record rather than treated as resolution of the long-running location dispute.
- Exact dialogue, inner reactions, weather imagery, burial geography, the timing link between Guo Jia's death and Liu Bei's third visit, and the opening 15/85 management statistic remain source-scoped.
