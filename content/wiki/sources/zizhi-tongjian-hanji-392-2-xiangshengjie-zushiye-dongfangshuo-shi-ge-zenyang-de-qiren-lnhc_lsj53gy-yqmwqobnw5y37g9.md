# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》392-2｜相声界祖师爷东方朔，是个怎样的奇人

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode closes the Hanji 14 arc by moving from [[DongfangShuo|东方朔]]'s comic reputation into the posthumous evaluation of [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] and the opening conditions of [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|汉昭帝刘弗陵]]'s reign. The Dongfang Shuo opening uses the "人中长则寿长" story to show [[HumorousRemonstrance|滑稽讽谏]]: he accepts a fangshi's flattering premise, extends it absurdly to Peng Zu, and exposes the claim without direct confrontation.

The episode then contrasts [[BanGuEasternHan|班固]]'s positive account of Han Wudi's Confucian, Taixue, ritual, calendar, music, fengshan, and cultural-institution building with [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s cost-focused judgment that Wudi resembled Qin Shi Huang in luxury, punishment, extraction, expansion, spirit-seeking, and public exhaustion, but avoided Qin's fate because he could still accept upright advice, value talent, punish and reward clearly, repent late, and entrust the heir well. The final section starts the Han Zhao-Huo Guang regency field: the eight-year-old emperor is cared for by [[GaiChanggongzhuWesternHan|鄂邑公主]], [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]], [[JinMidiWesternHan|金日磾]], and [[ShangguanJieWesternHan|上官桀]] control the shangshu center, a seal-keeper refuses to hand over the imperial seal during a scare, and Huo rewards him to display respect for office boundaries.

## Key Claims

- Dongfang Shuo's later comic-ancestor image is tied to a historical-literary pattern of using wit, exaggeration, and absurd extension to puncture flattering or superstitious claims near power.
- The "人中一寸百岁" anecdote works because Dongfang Shuo follows the premise to its grotesque conclusion rather than arguing abstractly against fangshi logic.
- Ban Gu's positive Han Wudi evaluation emphasizes institutional and cultural construction: Confucian classics, Taixue, ritual, calendar, music, fengshan, and dynastic literary brilliance.
- Sima Guang's evaluation is double-sided: Han Wudi produced Qin-like exhaustion but did not produce Qin-like collapse because receptivity, talent use, clear reward-punishment, late repentance, and proper regency entrustment still mattered.
- Han Zhao's accession is framed as a child-emperor transition under elder-sister care and three-regent shangshu control, with Huo Guang quickly becoming the practical center.
- The seal-keeper episode shows Huo Guang building authority by rewarding an official who refuses even Huo's request when it touches the imperial seal.
- The closing notices keep the regime context unsettled: Wudi's burial, amnesty, eastern epidemic, the Jibei king's suicide and kingdom abolition, and Xiongnu raiding all mark the beginning of the new reign.

## Key Quotes

> "我可不是笑陛下，我笑的是彭祖" - Dongfang Shuo's turn from apparent disrespect toward the emperor into comic demolition of the fangshi premise.

> "与秦始皇无异矣" - Sima Guang's sharp comparison of Han Wudi's burdens to Qin-style rule.
