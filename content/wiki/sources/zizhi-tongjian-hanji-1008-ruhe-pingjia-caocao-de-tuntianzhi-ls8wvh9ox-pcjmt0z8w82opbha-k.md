# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1008丨如何评价曹操的屯田制？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode fills the immediate prelude to [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1009-liubei-touben-caocao-xianxie-langru-hukou-llm0ds4krkptjdtb71hbusnpzbdc|Hanji 1009]]. It first explains [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s 196 CE [[TuntianSystem|屯田制]] as a response to late-Han war, famine, and broken agriculture: [[ZaoZhi|枣祗]] proposes it, Cao Cao appoints Zao Zhi and [[RenJun|任峻]], and production around [[Xudu|许都]] becomes the grain base for later campaigns. The second half shows [[LyuBu|吕布]] stopping [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s attack on [[LiuBei|刘备]] through the "辕门射戟" display, a case of [[CredibleForceDisplayDeterrence|可信武力展示威慑]] before Lü Bu's later instability in Hanji 1009.

## Key Claims
- The late-Han war field is food-constrained: troops are raised faster than land can be safely farmed, so soldiers and civilians face severe hunger.
- [[ZaoZhi|枣祗]] proposes屯田 to [[CaoCao|曹操]], and Cao Cao quickly turns the suggestion into offices and execution through Zao Zhi as屯田都尉 and [[RenJun|任峻]] as典农中郎将.
- The source says the first-year Xudu-area屯田 harvest reaches about one million斛, which the host renders as roughly forty thousand metric tons and enough for more than two hundred thousand people for a year.
- The episode treats [[TuntianSystem|屯田制]] as the "关键性的关键" behind Cao Cao's northern rise because it converts displaced people, land, office, and military need into a durable grain base.
- Cao Cao's interest in supply is tied to his reading of [[SunziBingfa|《孙子兵法》]], where the host highlights his attention to roads, cost accounting, and provisions before war.
- [[YuanShu|袁术]] tries to neutralize [[LyuBu|吕布]] with a marriage alliance before sending [[JiLing|纪灵]] and others with roughly thirty thousand troops against [[LiuBei|刘备]].
- Lü Bu decides to help Liu Bei because a Yuan Shu victory would let Yuan Shu connect with [[Taishan|泰山]]-area actors and expose Lü Bu to encirclement.
- At the Pei-area camp meeting, Lü Bu frames Liu Bei as his brother, sets a halberd at the gate, and promises both sides will withdraw if he hits the side branch.
- Lü Bu's successful shot turns a possible battle into a public deterrent bargain; the source reads this as "不战而屈人之兵" and analogizes it source-scopedly to minimal credible deterrence.
- The episode's final judgment is double-edged: Lü Bu can show tactical and political intelligence in a moment, but his unstable character will soon disrupt the balance he has just restored.
