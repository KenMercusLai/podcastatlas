# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》360-2｜汉武帝的疯狂求仙（受骗）路！

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the legitimacy discussion in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-360-1-simaqian-taishigong-chenghao-jing-you-ci-er-lai-lq6ulwhl_jscf6ipkjxev6zvwbrf|Hanji 360-1]]. It contrasts [[SimaQian|司马迁]]'s source-attributed emphasis on popular recognition and virtue with [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s reliance on Heaven, [[FengshanRitualLegitimacy|fengshan]], and the sacred authority of [[Huangdi|黄帝]], while preserving the distinction between the host's political interpretation and independently established authorial intent.

At [[Qiaoshan|桥山]], Wudi asks why Huangdi has a tomb if he ascended as an immortal. [[GongsunQingFangshi|公孙卿]] answers that Huangdi left only clothing to be buried by grieving ministers; Wudi then imagines his own ascent and a future clothing tomb at Dongling before returning to Ganquan to sacrifice to Taiyi. The exchange shows an unverifiable specialist explanation sustaining imperial hope, but the supplied summary does not independently establish deliberate fraud, Huangdi's immortality, or Wudi's complete motive.

## Key Claims

- The episode contrasts two accounts of imperial legitimacy: Sima Qian is associated with popular recognition and virtue, while Han Wudi emphasizes Heaven and sacred authorization.
- It says Huangdi stands at the head of a large ruler genealogy whose evaluative core remains benevolent virtue even when later ritual practice also treats him as an immortal ancestor.
- After fengshan, Han Wudi sacrifices at Huangdi's tomb on Qiaoshan and asks why an immortal who ascended to Heaven would leave a grave.
- Gongsun Qing resolves the tension by saying the tomb contains Huangdi's clothing rather than his body because grieving ministers buried what he left behind.
- Wudi accepts the explanation strongly enough to imagine his own future ascent and instruct that his clothing should then be buried at Dongling.
- Wudi later returns to Ganquan Palace and sacrifices to Taiyi, which the episode treats as evidence that immortality seeking is sustained rather than momentary.
- The closing mention that Huo Qubing's son remains beside Wudi is a preview of the next installment, not a developed account of his life or death.

## Key Quotes

> “既然听说黄帝成仙不死，为什么现在还有坟墓。” - the episode's rendering of Wudi's question at Qiaoshan.

> “群臣也应当在东陵埋葬他的衣冠。” - the consequence Wudi imagines for his own future ascent after hearing Gongsun Qing's explanation.
