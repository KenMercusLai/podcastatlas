# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1044丨关羽千里走单骑，需感谢曹操不杀恩情？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the immediate pre-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]] sequence after [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1042-congming-haishi-wangu-tianfeng-yin-dingzhuang-yuanshao-ruyu-lk5x6if2rouarmgichtynzhl3cgs|Hanji 1042]]. [[YuanShao|袁绍]] reacts angrily after [[YanLiangLateHan|颜良]] is killed, ignores [[JuShou|沮授]]'s cautious Yellow River advice, strips Ju Shou's command authority, and then watches another famous general, [[WenChouLateHan|文丑]], die in [[CaoCao|曹操]] and [[XunYou|荀攸]]'s baiting action at [[YanjinBattleLateHan|延津之战]].

The second half completes the [[GuanYu|关羽]] captive-service arc opened by [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1041-jiemi-lishishang-zhenshi-de-guanyu-jiangcao-luisp0ndk1lfrc87byygn6r8myhx|Hanji 1041]]. After repaying Cao Cao by killing Yan Liang, Guan Yu seals away Cao's gifts, leaves a farewell letter, and returns toward [[LiuBei|刘备]], while Cao Cao refuses his officers' request to pursue him. The episode then adds a short northern-submission note on [[YanRou|阎柔]] and [[XianyuFu|鲜于辅]], plus a Jiangdong cliffhanger in which [[ChenDeng|陈登]] tries to stir [[YanBaihu|严白虎]] remnants behind [[SunCe|孙策]].

## Key Claims
- The opening "anger makes people stupid" frame is used to contrast Yuan Shao's emotionally driven pursuit with Cao Cao's colder battlefield timing.
- Ju Shou advises Yuan Shao to avoid a full southern crossing, first garrison at Yan Ford, and test Guandu with a smaller force, but Yuan Shao treats the warning as disloyal obstruction and transfers Ju Shou's troops to [[GuoTu|郭图]].
- Yuan Shao's crossing turns the pursuit after Yan Liang's death into a high-risk gamble because defeat south of the Yellow River would leave fewer retreat options.
- Cao Cao and Xun You use visible baggage and relaxed horses as bait; once Yuan cavalry disperse to seize goods, Cao launches fewer than six hundred riders and defeats Wen Chou's much larger cavalry group.
- The deaths of Yan Liang and Wen Chou convert Xun Yu's earlier "brave but capturable" assessment into concrete pre-Guandu battlefield damage to Yuan Shao's morale.
- Guan Yu's departure confirms [[CaptiveSubmissionResidualLoyalty|被俘归顺与旧主忠义]]: service under Cao Cao ends after repayment, gift refusal, and return to Liu Bei rather than permanent transfer of loyalty.
- The episode separates the chronicle-source departure from [[RomanceOfTheThreeKingdoms|《三国演义》]] embellishments such as "千里走单骑," "过五关斩六将," and the old-city reunion.
- Yan Rou and Xianyu Fu's move toward Cao Cao shows Yuan Shao losing not only battles but also northern frontier and Youzhou-aligned support.
- Chen Deng's attempt to use Yan Baihu remnants against Sun Ce makes Jiangdong pressure part of the same 200 CE multi-front uncertainty before the next Sun Ce episode.

## Key Quotes
> "不要愤怒，愤怒让我愚蠢." - the episode's emotional-discipline frame for comparing Yuan Shao and Cao Cao.

> "欲成大树，不与草争." - the opening maxim used to warn against low-level retaliatory consumption.
