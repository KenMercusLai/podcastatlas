# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》870丨古代体制内，不干了没有退路

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 165-166 CE [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] field immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-871-cheng-e-yang-shan-weihe-fanbei-wuxian-ruyu-lujkihgvh-cafkh9bfak6-vttpwt|Hanji 871]]. It opens with a source-scoped self-reliance frame around money, knowledge of human nature, emotional steadiness, and health, then turns to [[LiuKuanLateHan|刘宽]]'s humane local-official profile and [[XunShuangLateHan|荀爽]]'s post-eclipse answer criticizing harem scale and palace burden.

The episode's durable historical contribution is the pairing of court extravagance with crisis dependence on capable officials. Huan can promote Liu Kuan and Xun Shuang, draw warning language from an eclipse, and worship Buddha and Laozi with expensive palace ritual, while the same state needs [[ZhangHuanLateHan|张奂]] and [[HuangfuGuiLateHan|皇甫规]] on the frontier so badly that Huangfu Gui's attempted exit through illness and self-impeachment fails.

## Key Claims

- The opening practical lesson is source-scoped: the host says money, emotional steadiness, controllable routine, knowledge reserves, understanding human nature, and bodily health matter more than external "backing."
- [[LiuKuanLateHan|刘宽]] is presented as a humane official: he avoids shouting at subordinates, uses a reed whip to create shame without bodily injury, and teaches elders, youth, and local people through farming, filial piety, sibling affection, and study.
- The 166 CE first-day solar eclipse becomes a court responsibility signal, prompting Huan to ask senior offices, commanderies, and kingdoms to recommend extremely filial people.
- [[XunShuangLateHan|荀爽]] uses the resulting examination to criticize palace women and harem expense, arguing that thousands of women burden innocent commoners and disturb cosmic harmony.
- Xun Shuang proposes releasing women whom the emperor has not visited so they can marry independently, and Huan responds by appointing him langzhong rather than punishing the critique.
- Yuzhou famine is severe enough that the episode reports large-scale death; Huan summons Zhang Huan to become dasinong and appoints Huangfu Gui to replace him as Duliao general.
- Huangfu Gui tries to avoid the appointment by claiming illness and then deliberately crossing his jurisdiction so Bingzhou inspector [[HuFangLateHan|胡方]] might impeach him, but Hu Fang sees the tactic and refuses to help him exit.
- Yellow River clarity, Huan's Buddhist-Laozi ritual at Zhuolong Palace, and luxury ritual objects form a pre-Hanji-873 setting where religious display and omen politics surround court criticism.
- When Xianbei, Southern Xiongnu, Wuhuan, and Qiang groups raid the frontier after hearing Zhang Huan has returned to Luoyang, Huan reappoints Zhang as Protector of the Xiongnu and expands his oversight across You, Bing, and Liang.

## Key Quotes

> "真正能给你撑腰的，是足够的钱和不败的自己" - opening self-reliance frame.

> "用蒲草做成鞭子" - Liu Kuan's shame-without-injury punishment style.

> "想早日脱离官场" - the source's reading of Huangfu Gui's self-impeachment attempt.
