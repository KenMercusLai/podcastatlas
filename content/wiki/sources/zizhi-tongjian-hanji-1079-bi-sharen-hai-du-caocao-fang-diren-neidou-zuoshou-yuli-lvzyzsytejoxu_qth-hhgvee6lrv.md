# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1079丨比杀人还毒！曹操放敌人内斗坐收渔利

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode joins the [[BailangMountainLateHan|白狼山之战]] to the final collapse of [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s sons. [[CaoCao|曹操]] turns a dangerous surprise encounter with [[Wuhuan|乌桓]] into victory through [[ZhangLiao|张辽]]'s attack, then converts battlefield success into surrendered manpower and frontier settlement.

The strategic center comes afterward. Rather than pursue [[YuanShang|袁尚]] and [[YuanXiLateHan|袁熙]] into Liaodong, Cao Cao withdraws because immediate attack would give the refugees and [[GongsunKangLateHan|公孙康]] a common enemy. Once pressure eases, their conflicting security interests prevail: Gongsun Kang kills the Yuan brothers and [[SupuyanLateHan|苏仆延]] and sends their heads to Cao Cao.

## Key Claims

- In Jian'an 12 / 207 CE, Cao Cao's force unexpectedly meets Wuhuan near White Wolf Mountain before its baggage and armor are fully available; he reads the opposing formation as disordered and orders Zhang Liao to lead the attack.
- The victory kills Wuhuan leader [[TadunLateHan|蹋顿]] and produces a large surrender of Hu and Han people; the episode says Cao Cao incorporates Wuhuan cavalry and uses differentiated pacification rather than indiscriminate killing.
- Yuan Shang, Yuan Xi, and Supuyan flee to Gongsun Kang in Liaodong after the Wuhuan defeat.
- Cao Cao refuses proposals for immediate pursuit because attacking Liaodong would bind Gongsun Kang and the Yuan refugees into mutual defense.
- When Cao Cao withdraws, Gongsun Kang's fear of the Yuan brothers and his interest in preserving Liaodong autonomy make killing the refugees safer than sheltering them.
- Gongsun Kang sends the three heads to Cao Cao, ending the Yuan heirs' current northern flight sequence without a Cao-side Liaodong campaign.
- The episode abstracts the case as "急之则相救，缓之则相争": reducing pressure can expose incompatible interests that direct attack would temporarily suppress.

## Key Quotes

> "急之则相救，缓之则相争。" - the episode's compact explanation of why Cao Cao does not pursue into Liaodong.

> "灭六国者六国也，族秦者秦也。" - the opening frame that political destruction often develops through internal failure.
