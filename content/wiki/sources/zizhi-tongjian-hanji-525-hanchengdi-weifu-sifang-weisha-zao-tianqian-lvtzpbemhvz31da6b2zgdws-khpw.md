# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》525丨汉成帝微服私访，为啥遭“天谴”？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Hongjia 1-2 / 20-19 BCE under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]], immediately before the already ingested Hanji 526-533 Chengdi sequence. It follows [[XueXuanWesternHan|薛宣]]'s promotion to Yushi Dafu, [[ZhangYuWesternHan|张瑜 / 张禹]]'s privileged retirement, [[WangYinWesternHan|王音]]'s enfeoffment, Chengdi's hunting and disguised outings under [[ZhangFangWesternHan|张放]]'s household cover, and a pheasant omen that Wang Yin converts into criticism of Chengdi's pleasure-seeking and heirlessness.

The durable synthesis is that omen language gives ministers a safer but still pointed route into questions of ruler conduct, public rumor, succession anxiety, and court silence. Wang Yin does not merely explain a strange bird event; he argues that Chengdi's private outings, weak health, no-son crisis, and flattering court environment threaten the continuity of Gaozu's realm, while the host links imperial heir pressure to modern family pressure around childbirth.

## Key Claims

- In Hongjia 1, Chengdi appoints Xue Xuan Yushi Dafu, visits his mausoleum works, amnesties convicts laboring there, and turns Xinfeng's Xixiang into Cangling county for the imperial tomb.
- Chengdi's pleasures include hunting, cuju, board games, and disguised travel with Qimen attendants and private slaves, sometimes under the claim that he belongs to the Fuping Hou household.
- Zhang Fang's background makes that cover plausible: he descends from Zhang Anshi, is Jingwu Princess's son through Zhang Lin, serves near Chengdi, marries into Empress Xu's family, and enjoys Chengdi's favor.
- Zhang Yu retires from office with high ritual privilege, continuing access, major gifts, and extensive property, making him an example of late Western Han elite security around an imperial teacher.
- Wang Yin is rewarded as Anyang Hou with a Five Marquises-level fief, while the same year also includes Xiongnu succession and hostage diplomacy notices.
- In Hongjia 2, pheasants appear at an archery ceremony, several offices, and Weiyang Palace; Wang Yin reads the omen through the Shang king Wuding precedent as a warning that can become blessing only through corrected conduct.
- Chengdi initially suspects the pheasant anomaly may have been staged, but Wang Yin uses the exchange to criticize flattering courtiers, official silence, Chengdi's outings and illness, and the absence of an heir.

## Key Quotes

> "陛下即位十五年，没有皇子" - Wang Yin's succession-pressure core as summarized by the episode.

> "高祖的天下要托付给谁" - the episode's paraphrased political stakes behind the no-heir warning.
