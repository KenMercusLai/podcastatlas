## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s 215 CE western campaign from internal security and concealed logistics through the difficult approach to Hanzhong and the unexpected collapse of [[ZhangLuFivePecks|张鲁]]'s Yangping defense. Its durable synthesis is that preparation, verified terrain, supply, side-threat control, and rapid exploitation still matter when a battle turns on contingency; afterward, Zhang Lu's preserved storehouses and Cao Cao's favorable treatment make surrender a negotiated political signal rather than simple annihilation.

## Key Claims

- Cao Cao removes remaining anti-Cao influence around [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], gathers men and supplies at Mengjin, and keeps the western objective ambiguous enough to constrain rival inference.
- [[GaoRouLateHan|高柔]] argues that extending deserter liability to parents and siblings would encourage further flight rather than return; the episode says Cao Cao accepts the advice and removes the death penalty for desertion.
- Cao Cao moves through Wudu and attacks Dou Mao before the main Hanzhong advance, treating an unsecured side threat as a risk to the principal operation.
- Reports from surrendering men understate Yangping's defensibility. Cao Cao's own inspection reveals difficult terrain, while casualties and depleted grain push him toward withdrawal.
- During the recall, a forward unit becomes lost at night and enters Zhang Wei's camp. [[XiahouDun|夏侯惇]] verifies the unexpected gain, and Cao Cao converts it into a renewed attack that breaks the defense.
- After Yangping falls, [[YanPuLateHan|阎圃]] advises Zhang Lu to preserve bargaining value rather than submit immediately. Zhang Lu withdraws without burning the government stores and seals them intact as evidence of non-hostile intent.
- Cao Cao responds by protecting the stores, opening a path to submission, and granting offices or titles to Zhang Lu and associated leaders; the episode interprets the treatment as a public incentive for other regional powers to surrender.
