# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》857丨学会共赢，才是真正的赢家

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the Huan-era court field immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-858-weishenme-quan-ni-yiding-yao-huanwei-sikao-lobr-xznqo-ctjpc-ytpstubmahl|Hanji 858]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-859-gudai-haoren-weishenme-nan-dang-haoguan-llkyx18ssvserxncvrtkw7xfduad|Hanji 859]]. It opens with a source-scoped relationship lesson about "win-win" cooperation, then uses [[HuangfuGuiLateHan|皇甫规]]'s accusation, self-defense, eunuch extortion, imprisonment, and eventual release to show why merit and uprightness did not automatically create political safety.

The durable contribution is a missing early case for [[MilitaryRewardEunuchGatekeeping|宦官门槛下的军功赏罚]] and [[PalaceAccessMonopoly|宫廷接触垄断]]. Huangfu Gui can explain the fiscal and military logic of frontier pacification, but [[XuHuangLateHan|徐璜]] and [[ZuoGuanLateHan|左悺]] still use the reward channel as a pressure point; when Huangfu refuses gifts and apology, accusation reaches the ruler more effectively than merit.

## Key Claims

- The opening "共赢" frame argues that durable relationships require investigating the other side's needs, fears, interests, expectations, and future value rather than only asserting one's own values.
- In workplace terms, past contribution alone may not secure support; decision-makers also ask what future value the person brings to the ecosystem they care about.
- Huangfu Gui's anti-eunuch stance and local anti-corruption actions create enemies who accuse him of bribing rebel groups to stage false surrender.
- Huangfu Gui's memorial denies personal enrichment, emphasizes military savings, and reframes any spending on surrender as potentially legitimate low-cost frontier pacification.
- Emperor Huan recalls Huangfu Gui to Luoyang and makes him yilang, but the expected marquisate remains exposed to eunuch-side gatekeeping.
- Xu Huang and Zuo Guan repeatedly send intermediaries around Huangfu Gui's merit review in order to solicit gifts; Huangfu's refusal turns reward processing into renewed slander.
- Huangfu's subordinates pay and apologize to calm the matter, but Huangfu himself refuses; he is jailed, sentenced to hard labor, and later released after petitions by the Three Excellencies and more than three hundred Taixue students including Zhang Feng, plus a general amnesty.
- Guiyang rebel Li Yan, renewed Wuling Man uprising, and Feng Gun's removal show the same court field where local disorder and court resentment make capable officials politically vulnerable.
