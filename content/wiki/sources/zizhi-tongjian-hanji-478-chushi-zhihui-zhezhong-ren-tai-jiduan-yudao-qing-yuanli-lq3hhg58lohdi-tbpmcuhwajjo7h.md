# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》478丨处世智慧：这种人太极端，遇到请远离！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills an earlier Yongguang 1 / 43 BCE personnel-and-character-judgment case under [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]], immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-479-zhichang-qingshang-qianfei-kankan-xihan-jiajuanzhi-de-xiachang-lpsopbj1dtftnsn-kjjeyftst7el|Hanji 479]]. It reads [[YangXingWesternHan|杨兴]], [[ZhugeFengWesternHan|诸葛丰]], [[ZhengPengWesternHan|郑朋]], and [[GongYuWesternHan|贡禹]] as different forms of unprincipled political behavior around [[ZhouKanWesternHan|周堪]], [[ZhangMengWesternHan|张萌]], [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]], and the wider anti-Xiao-Wangzhi court field.

The durable synthesis is that opportunism is not only open factional aggression. It can appear as over-reading the ruler's mood, reversing praise into denunciation, turning private resentment into political accusation, or offering harmless advice while avoiding the decisive problem. The host places ultimate responsibility partly on Yuan's wavering rule: an unsteady superior encourages subordinates to gamble on signals rather than principles.

## Key Claims

- Yang Xing is summoned after abnormal cold and dim sunlight, when Shi Xian and Shi Gao have blamed Zhou Kan and Zhang Meng for disaster omens.
- Yuan appears to want Yang Xing to give Zhou Kan room, but Yang misreads the question as a chance to attack a supposedly fallen official.
- Yang Xing recommends killing Zhou Kan and Zhang Meng, then proposes giving Zhou Kan title and income without real power as a way to preserve Yuan's teacherly reputation while punishing him.
- Zhuge Feng had previously praised Zhou Kan and Zhang Meng, but later denounces them with unverifiable language after his own demotion, so Yuan removes him while still demoting Zhou and Zhang.
- Sima Guang's criticism is that Yuan's split outcome blurs right and wrong: if Zhuge Feng spoke truth, he should not be punished; if he falsely accused them, Zhou Kan and Zhang Meng should not be demoted.
- Zheng Peng moves from failed attachment to Xiao Wangzhi into resentment and then supplies accusations through the Shi Gao outer-relative side against Xiao, Zhou, and Liu Gengsheng.
- Gong Yu's thrift advice is treated as formally correct but evasive because Yuan's urgent problem is trusting slanderers and using corrupt favorites.
- The host's modern lesson is that people without stable principle or self-positioning can become dangerous in organizations because they treat every access point as a personal advancement opportunity.

## Key Quotes

> "讨了人事的便宜，必受天道的亏" - the host's Cai Gen Tan-style summary of Yang Xing's opportunism.

> "小人物若被权力幻觉蒙蔽" - episode summary of Yang Xing's danger when he mistakes proximity for influence.
