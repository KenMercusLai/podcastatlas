# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》957丨他曾雄霸一方，为何懦弱到在厕所自尽？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate pre-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-958-jiemi-shishang-zui-duanming-de-tongchoudikai-lve41q9gyijbop8wk-b7e6shogju|Hanji 958]] late-Han field after [[YuanShao|袁绍]] takes [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]] from [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]]. It follows Han Fu's collapse through [[ZhuHanLateHan|朱瀚]]'s revenge attack, flight to [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] in [[Chenliu|陈留]], and fear-driven suicide, then turns to [[BaoXin|鲍信]] helping [[CaoCao|曹操]] enter [[DongCommandery|东郡]] against [[HeishanArmy|黑山军]] pressure. The final branch shows [[DongZhuo|董卓]] killing [[ZhangWenLateHan|张温]] by wrapping private resentment in celestial-omen language, making the episode a source for [[OmenLaunderedPurge|天象包装式清洗]] and [[PostPowerSafetyCollapse|失势后的安全崩塌]].

## Key Claims

- Yuan Shao consolidates Jizhou by expanding his adviser circle: [[JuShou|沮授]] receives military oversight, while [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]], [[TianFengLateHan|田丰]], [[XuYouLateHan|许攸]], [[FengJiLateHan|逢纪]], and [[XunChenLateHan|荀谌]] become part of the broader personnel field.
- The episode treats Han Fu's fall as a talent-use failure as well as a territorial loss: he had access to capable people but did not convert them into protection or state capacity.
- Zhu Han, a once-slighted subordinate, uses his new position under Yuan Shao to attack Han Fu's household and injure Han Fu's son; Yuan Shao executes Zhu Han the same day, both punishing unauthorized violence and protecting his public image.
- Han Fu's later move to Zhang Miao does not restore safety. A whispered conversation between Yuan Shao's envoy and Zhang Miao becomes enough to make Han Fu believe he is about to be killed.
- Han Fu's suicide is framed as the collapse of judgment after office, land, team, and reciprocal value are gone; the host keeps the judgment harsh, reading him as cowardly and responsible for failing dependents.
- Bao Xin reads Yuan Shao's Jizhou expansion as dangerous and proposes that Cao Cao occupy Dong Commandery, connect west with Zhang Miao, and keep a northern line toward [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] while waiting for Yuan Shao's enemies to exhaust him.
- When [[YuDuLateHan|于毒]], [[BaiRaoLateHan|白绕]], and [[SuiGuLateHan|眭固]] attack Dong Commandery, [[WangGongLateHan|王肱]] cannot hold the field; Cao Cao enters, defeats Bai Rao at [[PuyangLateHan|濮阳]], and is recommended by Yuan Shao as Dong Commandery administrator.
- Cao Cao's Dong Commandery appointment and move to [[DongWuyangLateHan|东武阳]] give him a firmer early political foothold before the later [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-961-neihuang-zhizhan-caocao-chulu-fengmang-ljdlt6kiu8tg0qws-jlyej-yhgxr|Hanji 961]] campaign branch.
- [[ZhangYangLateHan|张杨]] is presented as a mobile survival actor: he moves through Yuan Shao, Southern Xiongnu hostage conditions, Puyang, and finally Dong Zhuo's appointment as Henei administrator.
- Dong Zhuo uses a court astrologer's prediction that a minister will die to accuse Zhang Wen of secret contact with Yuan Shao and have him beaten to death in public.
- The episode's interpretation is that Dong Zhuo turns personal resentment into public inevitability: an omen makes the killing look like Heaven's script rather than private revenge.
- The host also notes a source-critical difference between [[Sanguozhi|《三国志》]] and [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] around Zhang Wen's death, treating Sima Guang's compact version as a way to foreground Dong Zhuo's unreasonable brutality.
