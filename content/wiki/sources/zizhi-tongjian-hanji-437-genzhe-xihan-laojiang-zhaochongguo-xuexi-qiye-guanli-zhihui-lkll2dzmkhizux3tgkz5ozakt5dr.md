# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》437｜跟着西汉老将赵充国，学习企业管理智慧！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the middle of [[ZhaoChongguoWesternHan|赵充国]]'s Qiang campaign between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-436-zhanchang-di-xiaozhitiao-jiemi-zhaochongguo-yu-hanxuandi-de-zhanshu-zhizheng-llab-tn_9wkvigzvisndnmhifppt|Hanji 436]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-439-gongbi-weiqing-huoqubing-zhichang-guanlizhe-bangyang-zhaochongguo-lirqsoujwewu6efd-shb7mypqvu3|Hanji 439]]. It centers on Zhao's slow pursuit of fleeing [[XianlingQiang|先零羌]], his protection of noncombatant settlements and farmland, his release and reassurance of a surrendering chief, and his memorial arguing that屯田, infrastructure, and controlled waiting will solve the crisis more cheaply than imperial pressure for a broad attack.

The durable synthesis is that Zhao's restraint is managerial statecraft, not softness. He avoids forcing cornered enemies into a death fight, preserves trust among detachable Qiang groups, refuses the safer personal path of obeying a bad order, and turns the frontier problem into [[FrontierResourceIntegration|resource integration]] through local food production, bridges, roads, guard posts, and surrender management.

## Key Claims

- Zhao orders a slow pursuit after Xianling Qiang abandon wagons, livestock, families, and children to cross the Huangshui, arguing that cornered fugitives will run if not pressed but may fight desperately if chased too hard.
- The episode presents this as both battlefield prudence and political restraint: Han still gains prisoners, deaths, livestock, and vehicles, but Zhao avoids converting advantage into unnecessary hatred.
- Zhao forbids destroying Qiang settlements or grazing in their fields, making Han trustworthiness visible enough that Qiang groups spread the news and seek permission to return home.
- When a chief personally submits and asks forgiveness, Zhao feeds him, releases him, and has him carry the message that the court will not punish the group if it returns.
- Zhao refuses Zhao Ang's self-protection advice to obey the emperor's attack order, saying such compliance would be disloyal if it harms the state.
- Zhao diagnoses the crisis as partly caused by earlier court failure to accept his grain-storage and personnel advice, especially under-preparation in the Huangzhong area.
- His memorial argues that using intelligence to dissolve the rebellion is easier than forceful suppression, and proposes withdrawing cavalry while keeping 10,281 soldiers at key sites.
- The屯田 proposal includes pre-cut timber, river transport after thaw, repair of villages and posts, irrigation channels, more than seventy bridges west of Huangxia, thirty mu per soldier for spring farming, mounted guards, and grain movement to Jincheng.
- The host translates the case into a management lesson about [[UpwardManagement]], frontline judgment, and letting people who can hear the gunfire guide the use of support.

## Key Quotes

> "穷寇不可迫也" - Zhao's reasoning for slow pursuit of desperate fugitives.

> "失之毫厘，差之千里" - the episode's management and frontier-risk frame for small errors becoming large consequences.

> "让听得见炮火声的人来呼唤炮火、指挥战斗" - the host's Huawei/Ren Zhengfei comparison for frontline authority.
