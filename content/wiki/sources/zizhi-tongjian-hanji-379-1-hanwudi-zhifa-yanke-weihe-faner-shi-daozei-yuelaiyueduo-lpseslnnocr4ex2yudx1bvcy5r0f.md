# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》379-1｜汉武帝执法严苛，为何反而使盗贼越来越多

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] short episode explains why severe punishment failed to suppress banditry under [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]. It describes large eastern groups attacking cities, taking weapons, freeing prisoners, killing officials, and surviving successive campaigns by imperial inspectors and embroidered-uniform envoys.

The durable synthesis is an incentive failure around the source's “审命法.” Frequent war and conscription are presented as the underlying causes of poverty and banditry, while collective death liability for officials who fail to detect or completely capture bandits removes offenders' reason to retreat and gives local governments a reason to conceal incidents. The result is a feedback loop in which punishment addresses symptoms, destroys truthful reporting, and makes the center less able to govern the disorder it is trying to eliminate.

## Key Claims

- In Tianhan 2 / 99 BCE, eastern bandit groups are described as ranging from hundreds to thousands, attacking cities, seizing armory weapons, freeing condemned prisoners, blocking roads, and killing or humiliating senior officials.
- Han Wudi first sends censorial and chancellery officials to supervise suppression, then dispatches Fan Kun, Zhang De, and other embroidered-uniform envoys with symbols of imperial and military authority.
- The campaigns kill large numbers of bandits and people accused of sheltering or feeding them, but surviving groups regroup in difficult terrain.
- The source's “审命法” threatens officials from the two-thousand-stone level down to minor clerks with death if they fail to discover group banditry or cannot capture every offender.
- The episode argues that an all-or-nothing death threat can weaken deterrence for people already facing death, because further resistance no longer worsens the expected penalty.
- Drawing on its account of the *Hanshu* “Treatise on Punishments,” the episode identifies repeated war mobilization and popular poverty as the upstream conditions behind bandit growth.
- Because complete capture is unrealistic, the law gives commandery and county officials a survival incentive to conceal bandit reports, degrading the information needed for central response.

## Key Quotes

> “发觉以后不能全部抓捕……从两千石以下到小吏，主持这件事情的人都得死。” - the episode's explanation of the official-liability rule.

> “郡县上下互相隐瞒，盗贼越来越多。” - the episode's closing account of the reporting backfire.
