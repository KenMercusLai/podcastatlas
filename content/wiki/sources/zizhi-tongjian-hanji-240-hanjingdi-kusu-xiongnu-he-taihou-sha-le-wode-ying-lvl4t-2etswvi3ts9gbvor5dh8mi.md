# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》240｜汉景帝哭诉：匈奴和太后，杀了我的鹰！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode links three [[HanJingdi|汉景帝]]-era family and law crises. Former crown prince [[LiuRongWesternHan|临江王刘荣]] dies by suicide after [[ZhiDuWesternHan|郅都]] imprisons him and [[DouYingWesternHan|窦婴]] secretly supplies writing tools; [[EmpressDowagerDouWesternHan|窦太后]] later uses a [[Xiongnu|匈奴]]-related case against Zhi Du to press for his execution; and [[YuanAngWesternHan|袁盎]] invokes the Song succession disorder to persuade the dowager not to make [[LiuWuLiangKingWesternHan|梁孝王刘武]] heir.

The durable synthesis is a three-way limit on personal rule. A formally legal prosecution can remain politically directed, an official useful to the emperor can become expendable when dynastic grief changes his protection, and affection for a collateral royal cannot safely override an established succession order. The host's claims about Jingdi's hidden direction, Liu Rong's understanding of [[HanWudi|刘彻]]'s future, and moral retribution for Zhi Du remain interpretation rather than established interior motive or complete legal history.

## Key Claims

- In 148 BCE, Liu Rong is summoned over alleged encroachment beyond his palace boundary onto land associated with [[LiuHeng|汉文帝]]'s ancestral temple and is imprisoned by Zhi Du.
- Zhi Du refuses Liu Rong writing tools, while Dou Ying secretly sends a knife and writing tablet; Liu Rong writes a final message and dies by suicide.
- The host interprets the prosecution as part of Jingdi's effort to secure Liu Che's succession, but the episode does not independently establish a direct execution order or Liu Rong's private reasoning.
- Dowager Dou blames Zhi Du for Liu Rong's death; after Zhi is posted to Yanmen and a Xiongnu countermeasure places him in legal jeopardy, Jingdi seeks a pardon but the dowager presses for execution.
- Liu Wu's Seven States service, exceptional ceremony, vehicles, guards, and the advice of Yang Sheng and Gongsun Gui are presented as conditions that turn maternal favor into succession danger.
- Yuan Ang uses the story of Song Xuangong's collateral succession and ensuing five-generation disorder to argue that preserving “small affection” can damage the larger dynastic order.
- The dowager reportedly accepts the warning and sends Liu Wu back to Liang, while the episode opens with Yuan Ang's later assassination but does not narrate the full investigation or killing mechanism.

## Key Quotes

> “难道刘荣就不是忠臣吗？” - the dowager's episode-attributed challenge when Jingdi seeks to spare Zhi Du.

> “小不忍害大义。” - the succession warning attributed to Yuan Ang's remonstrance.
