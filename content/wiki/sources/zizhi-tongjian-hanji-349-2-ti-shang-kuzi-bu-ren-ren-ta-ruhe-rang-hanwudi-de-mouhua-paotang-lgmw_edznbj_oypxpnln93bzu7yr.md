# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》349-2｜提上裤子不认人！他如何让汉武帝的谋划泡汤？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode uses [[ZhaoYingqiNanyue|赵婴齐]]'s concealment of the “Nanyue Wudi” and “Nanyue Wendi” seals to distinguish symbolic submission from effective control. Unlike [[ZhaoTuo|赵佗]] and [[ZhaoHuNanyue|赵胡]], Zhao Yingqi does not openly retain an imperial title or commission a new imperial seal, but he also refuses repeated summons to Chang'an because he fears being subjected to the rules governing interior vassal kings. [[Nanyue|南越]] therefore remains formally subordinate to Han while preserving substantial local autonomy.

The episode then links dynastic succession to [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s longer strategy. Zhao Yingqi asks the Han court to recognize the Han-born [[QueenDowagerJiuNanyue|樛氏]] as queen and their son [[ZhaoXingNanyue|赵兴]] as heir. After Zhao Yingqi dies and Zhao Xing succeeds, the source treats the new queen dowager's position as a potential inside channel for closer incorporation, while warning that later events will not follow the apparent plan cleanly.

## Key Claims

- Zhao Yingqi hides the imperial seals associated with Zhao Tuo and Zhao Hu instead of publicly destroying them or creating one for himself.
- The source reads concealment as partial accommodation: it reduces an open challenge to Han authority without ending Nanyue's inherited internal claims or practical autonomy.
- Under Empress Lü, discriminatory policy and trade or technology restrictions are said to have pushed Zhao Tuo toward the “Nanyue Wudi” title; Emperor Wen later repairs relations and obtains an external return to royal status.
- Zhao Tuo and Zhao Hu are nevertheless presented as continuing imperial-style privilege inside Nanyue, with the two seals serving as evidence of a dual external and internal order.
- Zhao Yingqi asks the Han court to recognize Lady Jiu as queen and Zhao Xing as heir, but repeatedly claims illness rather than appearing at Chang'an.
- His refusal is attributed to fear that direct attendance would let Han apply the institutional constraints used against interior vassal kings.
- After Zhao Yingqi's death, Zhao Xing succeeds and Lady Jiu becomes queen dowager, creating what the host presents as a possible internal advantage for Wudi's incorporation plan.

## Key Quotes

> “名义上不是帝王……实际上依然享受着帝王般的快乐。” - the episode's distinction between Zhao Yingqi's formal rank and effective local rule.

> “成事发大愿，得有耐心。” - the closing lesson attached to Wudi's long strategy and its coming reversals.

## Connections

- [[ZhaoYingqiNanyue|赵婴齐]], [[ZhaoTuo|赵佗]], and [[ZhaoHuNanyue|赵胡]] - three generations whose treatment of imperial titles and seals marks changing levels of open defiance.
- [[Nanyue|南越]] and [[NominalSubmissionLocalAutonomy|名义臣服与地方实权并存]] - polity and governance pattern combining Han-facing submission with internal autonomy.
- [[QueenDowagerJiuNanyue|樛太后]] and [[ZhaoXingNanyue|赵兴]] - queen and heir whose court recognition later places a Han-born mother at the center of Nanyue succession.
- [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] - emperor whose cultivation, recognition, and summons produce symbolic concessions but not Zhao Yingqi's attendance.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-352-1-yi-pao-hong-mie-le-bainian-diguo-lgpuqijq5kyd5ivh8ajlclo8ojex|Hanji 352-1]] - later source showing the queen dowager and Zhao Xing favoring closer Han incorporation before Lü Jia's coup.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. This episode supplies the succession and limited-compliance prequel to Hanji 352-1's later court crisis.
- Name normalization: the supplied summary consistently writes “赵英齐” and “纠氏.” The wiki routes them to the established forms [[ZhaoYingqiNanyue|赵婴齐]] and [[QueenDowagerJiuNanyue|樛氏 / 樛太后]], while preserving the supplied forms as transcription differences rather than silently treating them as separate people.
- The source says Lady Jiu was “sent” by Han Wudi and functions as an inside agent. The existing later source establishes her Han origin and pro-incorporation stance but does not independently prove formal agent status, so that characterization remains episode-attributed.
- The interpretation of seal concealment, Zhao Yingqi's motives, the reported twelve years of imperial cultivation, the balance of Han and Nanyue power, and the precise scope of internal imperial privilege remain source-scoped.
- The source previews that Wudi's plan will fail to proceed smoothly but does not narrate the later banquet confrontation, Lü Jia's coup, or the conquest campaign in full.
