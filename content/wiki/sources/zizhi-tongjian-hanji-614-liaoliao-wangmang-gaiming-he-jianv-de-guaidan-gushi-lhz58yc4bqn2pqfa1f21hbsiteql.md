# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》614丨聊聊王莽改名和嫁女的怪诞故事

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills 2 CE under [[HanPingdi|汉平帝]], immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-615-chengmu-jieshe-gudai-caili-zheme-gao-lkllbe015xkb7wlhkobtastkifdo|Hanji 615]]'s formal marriage, divination, and bride-price sequence. It follows [[WangMang|王莽]] using the "二名非礼" reading of the Spring and Autumn Gongyang tradition to require single-character given names, staging the [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] chanyu's name change as cultural submission, and then trying to place his daughter, later [[EmpressWangPingdi|汉平帝王皇后]], into Pingdi's empress-selection process.

The durable synthesis is that Wang Mang's early ascent already joins classical textual authority, naming order, imperial marriage, and managed public pressure. The single-name decree turns ritual learning into administrative compulsion; the Xiongnu name-change performance makes frontier deference look like acceptance of Han cultural order; and the empress-selection episode shows Wang Mang converting a failed "retreat" tactic into petitions by commoners, scholars, palace gentlemen, and high ministers.

## Key Claims

- Wang Mang grounds the single-name decree in the Gongyang claim that two-character given names are improper, then orders people with two-character names to change to one-character names.
- The episode treats this as an example of Wang Mang converting classical learning into practical state commands.
- Wang Mang has envoys prompt the Xiongnu chanyu to request cultural alignment with Han custom and change his name from "Nangzhiyasi" to "Zhi."
- The host reads the Xiongnu name change as political performance, not merely personal naming.
- Wang Mang frames Pingdi's lack of empress and heirs as a ritual and succession problem, then proposes a regulated selection from elite and classical lineages.
- When the candidate list includes many Wang-family women, Wang Mang fears his daughter may lose and uses self-deprecating memorial language that Wang Zhengjun misunderstands as a request to exclude Wang women.
- Wang Mang recovers by encouraging mass petitions that argue the daughter of Anhan Gong must become empress, turning private dynastic interest into visible public demand.

## Key Quotes

> "二名非礼" - classical phrase used to support the single-name policy.

> "中国不得有二名" - decree formula in the episode's account of the 2 CE naming reform.

> "不亦与众女并采" - Wang Mang's ambiguous self-deprecating line about his daughter and the candidate pool.
