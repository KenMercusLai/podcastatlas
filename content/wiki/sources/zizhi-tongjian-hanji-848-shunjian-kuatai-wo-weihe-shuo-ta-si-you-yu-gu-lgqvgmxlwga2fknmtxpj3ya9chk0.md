# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》848丨瞬间垮台！我为何说他死有余辜？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 159 CE fall of [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]], immediately before the already-ingested Hanji 849-853 post-Liang sequence. It follows [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] reacting after Liang has Deng Meng's mother Xuan assassinated, secretly plotting with five eunuchs, controlling palace access, seizing symbols of command, surrounding Liang's mansion, and forcing Liang plus [[SunShouLateHan|孙寿]] into suicide. The episode's lasting contribution is that Huan's successful purge removes an outer-relative power bloc but also creates the [[FiveMarquisesHuanEra|桓帝五侯]] reward structure that later sources show becoming a new eunuch-favorite problem.

## Key Claims

- Liang Ji's assassination of the mother of Huan's new consort Deng Meng makes the conflict personal and politically intolerable for the emperor.
- Huan's side wins because it controls timing, palace access, the shangshu channel, seals and tallies, armed guards, and the public framing before Liang can respond.
- [[JuYuanLateHan|具瑗]] first neutralizes Zhang Yun inside the palace, then leads guards with Sili xiaowei Zhang Biao to surround Liang's mansion.
- Liang Ji and [[SunShouLateHan|孙寿]] die by suicide on the day Huan's force removes Liang's general-in-chief seal and demotes him.
- The purge destroys more than Liang personally: Liang and Sun relatives, court allies, local officials, former clerks, and guests are arrested, executed, removed, or dismissed.
- Huan turns the confiscated Liang estate into public-facing political benefit through sale proceeds, a half-remission of that year's land tax, and distribution of seized gardens to commoners.
- Huan also erases Liang-family status symbols by demoting the former Liang empress's tomb identity and then restoring Deng Meng's Deng surname.
- The five eunuchs Shan Chao, Xu Huang, Ju Yuan, Zuo Guan, and Tang Heng are rewarded as county marquises, making the anti-Liang coup the founding scene for the Five Marquises.
- Huang Qiong's appointment as taiwei and early impeachment of corrupt local officials create a short-lived expectation that the court may improve after Liang's fall.
- The closing lesson says kindness needs principles, discernment, and bottom lines because greedy or malicious actors exploit boundaryless goodwill.

## Key Quotes

> "瞬间垮台" - title-level description of Liang Ji's collapse.

> "五侯" - the source's label for the eunuchs rewarded after the coup.

> "善良要有原则和底线" - closing moral formulation about bounded kindness.
