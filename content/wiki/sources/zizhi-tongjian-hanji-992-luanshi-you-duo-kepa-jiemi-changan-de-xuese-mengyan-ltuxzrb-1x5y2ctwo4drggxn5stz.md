# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》992丨乱世有多可怕？揭秘长安的血色梦魇

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the late-Han sequence immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-993-shentingling-zhizhan-sunce-dazhan-taishici-lrivpk7col2l7k-s4dqzxxp2kqp1|Hanji 993]]. It pairs [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]'s impoverished court at [[Anyi|安邑]] and the catastrophic collapse of [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] with [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s missed chance to welcome the emperor, then turns to [[SunCe|孙策]] leaving [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s platform and opening his Jiangdong campaign through [[WuJingLateHan|吴景]], [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]], [[DanyangCommandery|丹阳郡]], and [[HengjiangLateHan|横江]]. The main synthesis is that symbolic legitimacy, food security, and early talent/logistics support decide whether disorder becomes opportunity or devastation.

## Key Claims
- In 195 CE, [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] reaches [[Anyi|安邑]] in a rough ox cart, receives offerings from the [[HedongCommandery|河东郡]] governor, and continues rewarding and appointing officials even though the court's material base is nearly gone.
- The episode treats emergency wooden seals, improvised court space, hunger among palace women, and soldiers peering over thorn fences as evidence that Han ritual survives as surface dignity after state capacity has collapsed.
- After the emperor leaves, [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] loses order for more than forty days; strong people scatter, the weak are left behind, and the episode cites "强者四散，羸者相食" as the core image of [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] becoming a disaster zone.
- The source links the Chang'an catastrophe to [[WartimeAgriculturalLaborCollapse|战争抽空农业劳动力]] and [[FamineEntitlementFailure|Famine Entitlement Failure]], while keeping the cannibalism report source-scoped to the episode's historical narrative.
- [[JuShou|沮授]] urges [[YuanShao|袁绍]] to welcome the emperor to Ye, use the emperor's name to command noncompliant lords, and seize the legitimacy window before another actor does.
- [[GuoTu|郭图]] and [[ChunyuQiong|淳于琼]] oppose Ju Shou by warning that hosting the emperor would constrain Yuan Shao: obeying every order would be impractical, while disobeying would look like defying imperial command.
- The host reads Yuan Shao's refusal as a strategic failure: he sees neither the public righteousness nor the practical advantage of the move, leaving the "挟天子以令诸侯" opportunity to [[CaoCao|曹操]].
- [[ZhuZhiLateHan|朱治]] advises [[SunCe|孙策]] to stop remaining under Yuan Shu and instead return toward Jiangdong, where his family network and local opening can become a base.
- Sun Ce asks Yuan Shu for permission to help his uncle [[WuJingLateHan|吴景]] attack [[HengjiangLateHan|横江]], saying he can then recruit in his home region and later support larger campaigns.
- [[YuanShu|袁术]] permits the move because he has repeatedly failed to honor promises to Sun Ce and assumes Sun Ce cannot defeat [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]] at Qu'a or [[WangLangLateHan|王朗]] in Kuaiji.
- By the time Sun Ce reaches [[LiyangLateHan|历阳]], his initially small force has grown to roughly five or six thousand through recruitment along the route.
- [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] provides two thousand troops, fifty boats, funds, grain, and access to the [[DanyangCommandery|丹阳郡]] recruitment channel through [[ZhouShangLateHan|周尚]], turning Sun Ce's Jiangdong start from a nominal mission into a materially viable campaign.
- The capture of [[HengjiangLateHan|横江]] and nearby crossings is presented as the real opening of Sun Ce's Jiangdong base because it gives him access across the Yangtze and breaks a key part of Liu Yao's defensive line.
