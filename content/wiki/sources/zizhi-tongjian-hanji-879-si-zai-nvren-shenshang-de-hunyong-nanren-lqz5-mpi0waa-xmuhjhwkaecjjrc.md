# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》879丨死在女人身上的昏庸男人

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the missing pre-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-880-weihe-zhichang-laoyoutiao-rezhong-yu-chengbing-lnzfgwghrxrpltsmb8godap-nyzw|Hanji 880]] transition from [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]]'s final harem politics to [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s accession. It opens with family-emotion advice, then turns to 167 CE: Huan favors [[TianShengLateHan|田胜]], installs [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦妙]] as empress because Tian lacks elite status, dies childless at thirty-six, and leaves Dou Miao as dowager.

The political center is the first accession choice before the later Dou/Chen crisis. Dou Miao kills Tian Sheng after Huan's death, while [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] and others select the twelve-year-old Liu Hong, later Emperor Ling, as successor. The host treats that selection as a power temptation: a young ruler can be made legitimate through genealogy and escort ritual, but his controllability makes the choice dangerous.

## Key Claims

- The opening frames family emotion as a self-command problem: public gentleness and private cruelty can turn the household into an emotional dumping ground.
- In 167 CE, Emperor Huan strongly favors Tian Sheng and considers making her empress, but her low birth gives eunuch opponents an easy reason to block her.
- Huan installs Dou Miao as empress because of her family status, then continues favoring Tian Sheng, leaving affection and formal rank split between two women.
- Tian Sheng's attempt to deepen Huan's attachment through a small group of palace women is presented as a source-scoped harem-strategy detail, not a full biography.
- Huan dies at Deyang Front Hall on 167-12-28 after twenty-two years as emperor, age thirty-six, and without a son.
- The childless death immediately creates a succession problem that must be solved through collateral Liu-clan lineage rather than a direct heir.
- Dou Miao becomes empress dowager the day after Huan's death, presides over court, and executes Tian Sheng after the favorite loses her patron.
- Dou Wu consults Liu Shu about worthy Liu-clan candidates, reports the choice to Dou Miao, and settles on Liu Hong, the twelve-year-old Liwu tinghou.
- The court sends Liu Shu and [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] with guards and palace personnel to receive Liu Hong, turning the lineage choice into a formal accession procedure.
- The host argues source-scopedly that Dou Wu and Dou Miao prefer Liu Hong partly because his youth makes him easier to control.

## Key Quotes

> "年仅12岁" - the source's controllability marker for Liu Hong.
