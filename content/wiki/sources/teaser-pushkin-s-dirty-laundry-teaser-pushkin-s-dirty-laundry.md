# Teaser: Pushkin’s Dirty Laundry

## Summary

This brief [[RevisionistHistory]] teaser has [[MalcolmGladwell]] revisit an earlier laundry episode by airing internal objections from [[PushkinIndustries]] employees. [[BrendanFrancisNewnam]] defends [[Ecover]] against Gladwell's detergent judgment and argues that labor practices, environmental performance, supply-chain footprint, product performance, and cultural framing all belong in [[EthicalConsumerChoiceEvaluation]]. The exchange is playful and promotional, but its competing claims about Ecover and [[ProcterGamble|Procter & Gamble]] remain unverified within the supplied summary.

## Key Claims

- The teaser previews a monthly bonus episode made available through the renamed Pushkin Plus subscription.
- [[LitalMolad]] questions whether the earlier laundry episode gave [[ProcterGamble|Procter & Gamble]] so much favorable attention that the company should have sponsored it.
- Gladwell responds to the internal criticism by sending producers to interview employees targeted in the original episode.
- [[BrendanFrancisNewnam]] says the original episode singled out his hot-water, bleach, and [[Ecover]] laundry routine and used him as a lightly criticized “straw man.”
- Newnam argues that Ecover's wages, carbon record, buildings, supply-chain footprint, smell, and cleaning performance compare favorably with P&G's; Gladwell counters with a claim about wind-powered P&G detergent factories.
- Newnam argues that the earlier framing reduced Brooklyn residents and environmentally conscious consumers to stereotypes rather than assessing their reasons on the merits.
- Workplace jokes about job security, defamation, and emotional damages turn disagreement into self-critical entertainment while the teaser promotes a paid subscription.
