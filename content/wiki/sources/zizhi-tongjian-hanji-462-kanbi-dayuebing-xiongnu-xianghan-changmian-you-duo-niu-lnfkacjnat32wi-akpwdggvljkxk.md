# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》462｜堪比大阅兵！“匈奴降汉”场面有多牛？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Ganlu 2-3 / 52-51 BCE under [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]], between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-460-60sui-cisha-qinfu-ta-shi-shishang-zui-biaohan-de-heqin-gongzhu-lntrasdevraz8u4k8tlckm3on1jv|Hanji 460]]'s [[HuhanyeChanyuWesternHan|呼韩邪单于]] submission decision and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-463-caicaikan-zai-hanxuandi-yanzhong-shui-shi-zuidade-gongchen-lqblzdg7sdkzln4vxhtnnye92292|Hanji 463]]'s protected-settlement aftermath. It combines domestic notices about royal enfeoffment, poll-tax reduction, [[ZhuyaCommandery|珠崖郡]] unrest, [[YuDingguoWesternHan|于定国]]'s appointment, and [[ZhaoChongguoWesternHan|赵充国]]'s death with the central protocol debate over how to receive Huhanye at Chang'an.

The durable synthesis is that submission is staged through both hierarchy and restraint. [[XiaoWangzhiWesternHan|萧望之]] argues that Han should not simply rank the chanyu below internal kings, because treating him as a high-status guest displays magnanimity while preserving diplomatic flexibility if later Xiongnu rulers stop attending court; [[EmperorXuanOfHan|Xuan]] accepts the plan, then turns Huhanye's audience into a mass spectacle of cavalry, gifts, crowds, ritual deference, and palace display.

## Key Claims

- The episode frames Huhanye's audience as the result of Han strength, Western Regions control, Wusun-Han pressure on Xiongnu, and Xiongnu internal division.
- In Ganlu 2 / 52 BCE, Xuan enfeoffs Liu Xiao as King of Dingtao, issues amnesty, reduces the poll tax by thirty cash, sends Zhang Lu against renewed Zhuya unrest, appoints Yu Dingguo as Yushi Dafu, enfeoffs Liu Yu as King of Dongping, and records Zhao Chongguo's death.
- Zhao Chongguo is presented as retired but still strategically useful: Xuan had honored his retirement, yet still consulted him when major frontier matters arose.
- Huhanye arrives at the Wuyuan frontier with troops and treasures, then comes to Chang'an in Ganlu 3 / 51 BCE to attend Xuan's court.
- Most ministers want to treat the chanyu like an internal vassal king but rank him below the kings; Xiao Wangzhi argues that Xiongnu does not follow Han's calendar and should be treated as a matching state rather than an ordinary subject.
- Xiao's protocol preserves Han's public superiority without overclaiming permanent subjection: Huhanye may call himself subject in audience, but if descendants stop attending later they are not framed as rebel ministers.
- [[XunYue|荀悦]] criticizes Xiao's proposal from a ritual-order perspective, arguing that putting the chanyu above kings disturbs institutional hierarchy unless it is only a temporary expedient.
- Xuan sends Han Chang to receive Huhanye, lines the road with more than two thousand cavalry from seven commanderies, grants lavish gifts, allows Huhanye not to bow at Changping Slope, gathers rulers, kings, marquises, and crowds at Wei Bridge, and hosts him at Jianzhang Palace.

## Key Quotes

> "单于非正朔所加，故称敌国，宜待以不臣之礼。" - Xiao Wangzhi's protocol logic as summarized in the episode.

> "这是一次非常有仪式感的迎接。" - the host's description of the staged reception.
