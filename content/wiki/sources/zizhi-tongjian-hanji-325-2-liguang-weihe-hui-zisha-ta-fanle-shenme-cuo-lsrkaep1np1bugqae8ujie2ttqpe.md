# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》325-2｜李广为何会自杀？他犯了什么错？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode reconstructs [[LiGuangHanGeneral|李广]]'s final campaign. After [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]] reassigns him from the vanguard to an eastern route with right general [[ZhaoShiqiWesternHan|赵食其]], the force lacks a guide, becomes lost in the desert, and misses the main battle against [[Xiongnu|匈奴]]. Li Guang accepts responsibility for his subordinates but kills himself rather than face questioning by junior officials after more than forty years of service.

The episode also presents Li Guang's austere, generous command style: he shares rewards, food, water, and living conditions with his soldiers, helping explain the grief reported after his death. Its stronger claim that the guide may have been removed or killed as part of a political maneuver is explicitly speculative. Sparse evidence, a suspicious absence, and the host's reading of [[SimaQian|司马迁]]'s anger do not establish who caused the route failure.

## Key Claims

- Li Guang and Zhao Shiqi take an eastern route without a guide, become lost, fail to rendezvous with Wei Qing, and miss the main engagement with Xiongnu.
- Because lateness in campaign could bring capital punishment, Wei Qing's headquarters requests an explanation and orders Li Guang's staff to appear for questioning.
- Li Guang says his subordinates are not at fault, accepts personal responsibility, and kills himself rather than submit to questioning by junior clerks in his sixties.
- The episode says Li Guang had fought more than seventy engagements since youth and believed the reassignment away from direct pursuit of the chanyu led to his final failure.
- Li Guang is portrayed as frugal and generous: he distributes rewards, lives like ordinary soldiers, waits for them to drink and eat first, and leaves little family wealth.
- The reported grief of the army and public is used to connect shared hardship with durable commander loyalty.
- The host suspects deliberate removal of the guide and a politically motivated reassignment, but acknowledges that the surviving record is too sparse to establish this theory.
- Zhao Shiqi is tried after the campaign and, according to the episode, redeems the capital sentence with payment before losing office and becoming a commoner.

## Key Quotes

> "部下将领没有罪，是自己迷失道路，愿意亲自到统帅部受审。" - the episode summary's rendering of Li Guang accepting responsibility.

> "自己六十多岁，不能面对那些舞文弄墨的小吏。" - the episode summary's explanation of why Li Guang refuses the expected questioning.
