# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》412-2｜当着群臣和汉宣帝的面抨击汉武帝，夏侯胜有多敢？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the Benshi 2 / 72 BCE court dispute before the later [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-416-xihan-xiahousheng-ruhe-pai-hanxuandi-de-mapi-lvqbs--20kvjimwdwgfciq-n6wee|Hanji 416]] release story. [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] wants sacrificial music for [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] as a public legitimacy statement, but [[XiahouShengWesternHan|夏侯胜]] openly argues that Wudi's conquests cannot erase dead soldiers, exhausted wealth, displacement, famine, and cannibalism.

The durable synthesis is that Xiahou Sheng's courage lies in forcing a full [[ImperialAchievementCostAccounting|imperial achievement cost accounting]] inside a meeting designed to ratify praise. The episode also supplies the missing cause of Xiahou and [[HuangBaWesternHan|黄霸]]'s imprisonment: Huang refuses to join the impeachment, both men are placed in death-row custody, and Xuan quietly preserves their lives while their prison study of [[Shangshu|《尚书》]] turns punishment into a talent-observation interval.

## Key Claims

- Xuan proposes new sacrificial music for Han Wudi in 72 BCE, praising Wudi's benevolence, martial authority, and great merit.
- The court broadly follows the emperor's stated intent, but Xiahou Sheng publicly opposes honoring Wudi with dedicated sacrificial music.
- Xiahou acknowledges Wudi's conquest of the four quarters and territorial expansion, then weighs those achievements against military deaths, fiscal exhaustion, luxury, displacement, insect plague, famine, and cannibalism.
- The episode reads the speech as a direct challenge to Xuan's attempt to use Wudi veneration for succession legitimacy.
- Officials impeach Xiahou as disloyal; Huang Ba is implicated because he does not sign the impeachment memorial.
- Xuan approves their imprisonment but orders that neither prisoner be allowed to die in custody, suggesting punishment and containment rather than immediate execution.
- Huang Ba asks Xiahou to teach him the Shangshu in prison; Xiahou is moved by Huang's "hear the Way in the morning, die in the evening" framing, and the two study for two years.

## Key Quotes

> "朝闻道，夕死可矣" - Huang Ba's argument for continuing study even under death-row uncertainty.
