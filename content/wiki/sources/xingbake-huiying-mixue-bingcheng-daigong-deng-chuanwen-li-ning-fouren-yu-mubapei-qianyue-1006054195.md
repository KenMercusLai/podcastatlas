## Summary
This [[ShengdongZaokafei|声动早咖啡]] episode scans August 2026 business news across consumer brands, AI infrastructure, open models, beverages, autos, semiconductors, music streaming, and local services. Its strongest through-line is company response under pressure: [[Starbucks]] China and [[LiNing|Li-Ning]] deny market rumors, [[Meta]] reasserts open-weight model strategy, [[Spotify]] tries to govern AI artists, and [[Doubao]] tests hotel-order monetization without claiming paid ranking.

## Key Claims
- 36Kr reported that [[ZhangQing|张青]] was working on [[Starbucks]] China's supply chain and cost structure, but Starbucks China responded on 2026-08-12 that the online claims were seriously inaccurate.
- The source title frames the Starbucks item around "Mixue Bingcheng OEM" rumors, while the transcript excerpt grounds the confirmed claim in Starbucks China's denial rather than verified [[MixueBingcheng]] involvement.
- AI compute demand is pulling some technology companies toward data-center hiring and heavier land, power, fiber, GPU, and cooling commitments, extending [[DataCenterPowerBottleneck]] and [[DataCenterCostShifting]].
- [[Meta]] released [[MetaMuseModels|Muse Glimmer]] weights on 2026-08-10 and said Muse Spark 1.2 weights would follow, positioning open weights as a strategic response after Llama 4 disappointment and team changes.
- [[MasterKong|Master Kong / 康师傅]] and [[UniPresidentEnterprises|Uni-President / 统一集团]] show [[BottledBeverageDemandPressure]] as packaged water, juice, and carbonated drinks weaken while sugar-free ready-to-drink tea and ready-to-drink coffee keep growing.
- [[LiNing|Li-Ning]] denied reports that it would sign [[KylianMbappe|Kylian Mbappe / 姆巴佩]], while the episode notes that the rumor was easier to believe because Li-Ning had recently announced a Curry-brand cooperation and Mbappe's [[Nike]] relationship was reportedly in flux.
- July 2026 China passenger-vehicle retail sales were described as sharply weaker, especially for fuel vehicles, while exports grew quickly; the episode treats overseas growth and localization as important offsets to [[ChinaPassengerVehicleDemandPressure]].
- [[Sony]] and [[TSMC]] announced a roughly $4.7 billion image-sensor joint venture expected to begin mass production in 2029, creating a case for [[ImageSensorAssetLightManufacturing]].
- [[Spotify]] said it would label AI-generated artist personas from September and exclude those artists from editorial and algorithmic recommendations, making [[AIPersonaLabeling]] a platform-governance tool rather than only a metadata tag.
- Hotel merchants reported that orders routed from [[Doubao]] through [[Douyin]] would carry a 12% comprehensive fee, while Doubao said life-services currently had no paid promotion and charged only after completed orders; the source frames this as an [[AITravelCommerceTrust]] and [[PlatformIntermediationTax]] problem.
