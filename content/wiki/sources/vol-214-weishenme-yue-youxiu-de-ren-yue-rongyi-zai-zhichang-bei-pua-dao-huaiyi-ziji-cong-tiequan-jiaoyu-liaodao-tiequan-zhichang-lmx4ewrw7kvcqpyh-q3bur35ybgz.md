# VOL.214 为什么越优秀的人，越容易在职场被PUA到怀疑自己？从《铁拳教育》聊到“铁拳职场”

## Summary
This [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode has host [[Atang|阿汤]] and Seoul psychiatrist [[NanJiHe|南基贺]] use [[TrueEducationKDrama|《铁拳教育》/《真教育》]] to move from school violence and "monster parents" into [[WorkplaceBullying|workplace bullying]] and [[PsychologicalBoundaryProtection|psychological boundary protection]]. The discussion treats workplace PUA, humiliation, overwork, and exclusion as problems of [[StructuralPowerImbalance|structural power imbalance]] rather than simple individual weakness. Its practical thesis is that the realistic "iron fist" is not retaliatory violence, but noticing control, separating power-holder judgment from self-worth, preserving evidence, talking with others or clinicians, and using institutional complaint channels where available.

## Key Claims
- [[TrueEducationKDrama|《铁拳教育》/《真教育》]] works as the episode's hook because its revenge fantasy makes authority failure visible, while the speakers repeatedly reject real-world violence or vigilantism as a solution.
- "Monster parent" behavior is framed as [[ParentalProjectionControl|parental projection and control]]: a caregiver may call coercion love when the child, teacher, or school no longer fits the parent's own belief system.
- Parent-teacher conflict and supervisor-worker conflict both show [[StructuralPowerImbalance]] because one side may be able to call, evaluate, punish, or exhaust the other without equal reciprocal risk.
- The episode treats adolescent violence cautiously: antisocial traits are not reduced to one cause, and peer recognition, family neglect, and legal-age awareness may all shape harmful behavior.
- [[WorkplaceBullying]] appears through public humiliation, blame transfer, surveillance, unreasonable extra work, exclusion, and firing pressure rather than only obvious physical violence.
- The AI-task case shows how management enthusiasm for a new technology can become work overload when an employee is asked to learn and redesign systems without proper expertise, staffing, or scope.
- The episode links self-doubt and boss-pleasing to modern work structure: when workers cannot see direct product-of-labor achievement, supervisor approval can become a substitute source of value.
- [[PsychologicalBoundaryProtection]] includes object separation, gray-rock-style low reaction, preserving records or recordings, seeking psychological help, and using complaint channels where available.
- Victims may feel guilt after bullying, including regret over not responding or fear that reporting makes them the aggressor; the source frames expression and complaint as defensive systems rather than counter-abuse.
- Korean workplace complaint examples are treated as source-scoped context, not general legal advice for other jurisdictions.
