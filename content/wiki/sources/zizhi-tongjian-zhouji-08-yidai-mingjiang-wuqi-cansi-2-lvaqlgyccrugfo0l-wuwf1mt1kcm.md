# 《资治通鉴·周纪》08丨一代名将吴起惨死（2）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[WuQi|吴起]]'s fall from [[WeiState|魏国]] by showing him flee to [[ChuState|楚国]], where [[ChuDaoWang|楚悼王]] immediately uses him as a reforming chief minister. Its central synthesis is that Warring States strengthening reforms could quickly increase military power, but by cutting noble stipends, idle offices, and distant royal privileges they also created the elite hatred that made Wu Qi's death politically intelligible.

## Key Claims
- After the marriage-proposal trap in part 1, Wu Qi fears that [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]] may turn on him and escapes from Wei to Chu.
- [[ChuDaoWang|楚悼王]] already knows Wu Qi's ability and appoints him to high office soon after his arrival.
- Wu Qi's Chu reforms clarify law, strip or redirect aristocratic stipends, remove salary-drawing idle officials, cut off distant royal relatives' rank and pay, intensify military training, and oppose reconciliation with [[QinState|秦国]].
- The episode frames these measures as [[WarringStatesReformBacklash|Warring States reform backlash]]: centralization and military strengthening create state gains while concentrating losses among nobles, royal kin, and old officeholders.
- The source says Chu does become stronger under Wu Qi, especially militarily, but the same success makes elite resentment sharper.
- Under [[ZhouAnWang|周安王]]'s fifteenth year, the episode records deaths and accessions in Qin, Zhao, and Han: [[QinHuiGong|秦惠公]] dies and [[QinChugong|秦出公]] succeeds; [[ZhaoWuhou|赵武侯]] dies and [[ZhaoJinghou|赵靖侯]] is installed; [[HanLiehou|韩烈侯]] dies and [[HanWenhou|韩文侯]] succeeds.
- The host uses [[HanFei|韩非]]'s account of Zhao Jinghou to draw out [[LegalistRulerTechnique]]: a ruler's ability to use people and read speech can be treated as politically decisive even when his private conduct is ugly.
- In [[ZhouAnWang|周安王]]'s sixteenth year, the Zhou court formally recognizes [[TianHe|田和]] as a ruler of Qi even though the host notes that the political decision had been agreed earlier and Tian He had already died.
- The source also records a Zhao prince's revolt, flight to Wei, and failed attack on Zhao's newly moved capital, making succession another instability point.

## Key Quotes
> "富國強兵" - the episode's frame for the aim of Wu Qi's Chu reforms.

> "懂得用人、能洞察臣子言語真偽" - the source's summary of the ruler technique drawn from [[HanFei|韩非]].
