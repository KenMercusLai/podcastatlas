## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianwu 4 / 28 CE immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-691-gan-duo-jiangshan-de-weixiao-weihe-zhuding-shu-gei-liuxiu-ljazchpqnxcgav6cmlbm7-d-gv8|Hanji 691]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-692-donghan-kaiguo-dajiang-wangba-de-rensheng-you-duo-baqi-lmqqtsxwemetqqae-qdierska9pl|Hanji 692]]. It centers on [[TianRongYilingEasternHan|田戎]]'s failed turn toward surrender after [[XinChenEasternHan|辛臣]] steals his valuables, defects to [[CenPengEasternHan|岑彭]], and sends a letter that makes the planned submission look unsafe. The wider episode links that trust failure to [[WuHanEasternHan|吴汉]]'s non-assault handling of a local revolt, [[FuZhanEasternHan|傅湛]]'s advice to prioritize core regions before [[PengChongEasternHan|彭宠]], [[GaiYanEasternHan|盖延]]'s failed Lanling rescue, and [[HouBaEasternHan|侯霸]]'s reconstruction of administrative precedent.

## Key Claims

- [[TianRongYilingEasternHan|田戎]] initially wants to submit after hearing of Qin Feng's defeat, but [[XinChenEasternHan|辛臣]]'s stolen treasure, defection, and invitation letter break confidence in the surrender channel.
- Tian Rong's burned-shell divination turns uncertainty into reversal: the unfavorable crack becomes the reason to rebel and join Qin Feng before Cen Peng defeats him.
- [[YanCenEasternHan|延岑 / Yan Cen]] already appears before the later Shu endgame: [[DengYuEasternHan|邓禹]] defeats him at Shunyang, after which he flees to Hanzhong and receives high office from [[GongsunShuEasternHan|公孙述]].
- [[WuHanEasternHan|吴汉]] handles the Gexian revolt as a governance-fault problem, refusing immediate assault, arresting the acting county head, and persuading the five local families to surrender.
- [[FuZhanEasternHan|傅湛]] stops [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] from personally attacking Peng Chong by arguing that closer domestic regions should be secured before a distant Yuyang campaign.
- [[GaiYanEasternHan|盖延]] ignores Liu Xiu's indirect-relief advice to attack Tan, enters Lanling after [[DongXianEasternHan|董宪]] feigns retreat, then fails to save the surrendered Ben Xiu.
- [[HouBaEasternHan|侯霸]] preserves Linhuai through the Wang Mang disorder and later helps Liu Xiu restore governing capacity by collecting scattered institutional documents and usable former laws.
