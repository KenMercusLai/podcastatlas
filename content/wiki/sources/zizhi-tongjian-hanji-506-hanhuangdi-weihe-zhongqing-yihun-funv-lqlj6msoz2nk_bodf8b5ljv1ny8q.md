# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》506丨汉皇帝为何衷情已婚妇女？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianshi 4 / 29 BCE under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]], between the flood-rumor and harem-blame sequence in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-505-xihan-de-huicheng-yaoyan-da-le-shui-de-lian-ljuwccxx_aexasafkpbl-y2rvos5|Hanji 505]] and the Yellow River / Chen Tang sequence in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-507-ta-zhansha-xiongnu-chanyu-weihe-fanbei-xiayu-lhfkej7pqtikx5st73sls-kwo_ze|Hanji 507]]. It starts from meteor falls, the first five-person Shangshu arrangement, [[WangShangWesternHan|王商]] becoming chancellor, and Chengdi's White Tiger Hall questioning of frank speakers while many blame natural anomalies on [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]].

The durable synthesis is that [[GuYongWesternHan|谷永]] turns omen interpretation into factional protection. Rather than attack Wang Feng's power, he argues that Heaven's warning points to harem fertility, urges Chengdi not to monopolize favor, and treats women who can bear sons as politically valuable regardless of prior marriage. The host reads this as a defense of Wang Feng after Wang Feng sends the married sister of one of his concubines into Chengdi's palace, converting a scandal over propriety into an heir-production and dynasty-survival argument.

## Key Claims

- In Jianshi 4 / 29 BCE, stones fall at Bo County and Feilei, Chengdi abolishes a central eunuch office, and the Shangshu post is first fixed at five people.
- Chengdi makes Wang Shang chancellor and later asks recommended frank speakers at White Tiger Hall to explain unusual signs.
- Because state affairs are already entrusted to Wang Feng, many respondents blame the anomalies on Wang Feng's overmighty outer-relative position.
- Gu Yong avoids that target and argues that the realm, borders, feudal lords, and official checks are broadly stable, so blame should not be placed on an innocent person.
- Gu Yong shifts the diagnosis to the harem: Chengdi should not favor only one woman, should spread favor broadly, and should prioritize women who can bear sons regardless of beauty, body, age, or previous marriage.
- The episode says Wang Feng had presented to Chengdi the already-married sister of one of his concubines, provoking official opposition.
- Gu Yong's heir-centered omen argument reframes that propriety dispute as a dynastic survival matter and shields Wang Feng from blame.
- Gu Yong asks Chengdi to show the memorial to trusted ministers; the host reads this as ensuring that Wang Feng, the real intended audience, sees the loyal service.
- Du Qin follows with a similar memorial, Chengdi and Wang Feng are pleased, and Gu Yong is promoted to Guanglu Dafu.
