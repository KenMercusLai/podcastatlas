# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》371-2｜“上帝”属于中国？这下实锤了！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] through the ritual, architectural, calendrical, and frontier events of Taichu 1 / 104 BCE. It begins with the emperor's winter-solstice sacrifice at the Mingtang and the host's claim that “上帝” in this Western Han setting can refer both to a celestial sovereign and to Shun, then places that reading beside earlier imperial sacrifice to the Five Emperors and Wudi's repeated ritual activity.

The second durable branch is the court's creation of the Taichu calendar. [[SimaQian|司马迁]], Gongsun Qing, Husui, Ni Kuan, and classical scholars appear in a state project that adopts the first lunar month as the year's beginning and, in the episode's account, coordinates the solar year, lunar month, intercalation, solar terms, planetary cycles, official titles, pitch standards, and ritual. The closing Xiongnu defection plot supplies the immediate prehistory to [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-373-2-liguangli-shouzhan-dayuanguo-da-de-you-duo-can-ltocao5tfkxnpbxhotnqpg72t9hi|Hanji 373-2]].

## Key Claims

- At the winter solstice in Taichu 1 / 104 BCE, Han Wudi sacrifices to “上帝” at the Mingtang after traveling to Mount Tai.
- The host says Western Han “上帝” can mean the celestial sovereign and can also refer to Shun; this dual reading is retained as an episode claim rather than treated as settled historical semantics.
- Wudi's sacrifice, coastal search for immortals, Houtu worship, Penglai imagery, and construction of Jianzhang Palace show court ritual and immortality seeking operating together rather than as a single undifferentiated belief system.
- The Taichu calendar adopts the first month of the Xia calendar as the beginning of the year and is said to coordinate the tropical year, synodic month, intercalation, twenty-four solar terms, and five-planet cycles.
- The calendar reform is joined to yellow as the dynastic color, five as an auspicious number, renamed offices, pitch standards, and court ritual, making timekeeping part of state order rather than a technical calculation alone.
- The episode calls the Taichu calendar the first complete Chinese calendar with clear written documentation and the most advanced calendar of its time; those superlatives require independent history-of-science verification.
- A Xiongnu Left Grand Commandant secretly offers to kill the chanyu and defect if Han sends support, so Wudi orders [[GongsunAoWesternHan|公孙敖]] to build a surrender-reception city beyond the frontier.
