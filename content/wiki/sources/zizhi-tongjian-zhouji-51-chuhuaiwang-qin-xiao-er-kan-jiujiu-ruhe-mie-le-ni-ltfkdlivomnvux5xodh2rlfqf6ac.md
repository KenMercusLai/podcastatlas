# 《资治通鉴·周纪》51丨 楚怀王：秦小儿看舅舅如何灭了你！

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] deception of [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] from [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-50-zhangyi-shi-zenyang-ba-chuhuaiwang-huyou-que-de-loxg3ylhmskvdm-axtco5y-acege|Zhouji 50]]. It explains why Chu Huaiwang accepted the six-hundred-li [[Shangyu|商於]] promise, then follows his angry attack on [[QinState|秦国]] through [[DanyangBattle|丹阳之战]], the loss of [[Hanzhong|汉中]], the renewed drive through [[Wuguan|武关]] and Shangyu, and defeat near [[LantianBattle|蓝田之战]]. The episode's governing lesson is [[AngerDrivenWarDecision|因怒兴师]]: a ruler who turns humiliation into war can lose both strategy and recovery room.

## Key Claims
- The episode answers the previous episode's question: Chu Huaiwang was experienced, powerful, and not old, but the scale of Zhang Yi's promised six hundred li made the offer hard to resist.
- Chu Huaiwang also misread Qin's credibility because [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] was a great-power ruler and Zhang Yi was Qin's chief minister; he forgot the precedent of [[ShangYang|商鞅]] deceiving [[GongziAng|公子卬 / 公子昂]].
- [[ChenZhenWarringStates|陈枕]]'s warning had little effect because Zhang Yi had already influenced the [[ChuState|楚国]] court through gifts to ministers and favored palace figures.
- The source says Zhang Yi also joined in slandering [[QuYuan|屈原]], pushing a committed anti-Qin voice out of court and making Chen Zhen more isolated.
- In 312 BCE, Chu Huaiwang responds to the six-li reversal by sending Chu troops north from Danyang toward Wuguan instead of containing the loss.
- Qin defeats Chu at [[DanyangBattle|丹阳之战]], kills eighty thousand Chu armored soldiers, captures commander [[QuGai|屈匄]] and many high-ranking nobles, and takes [[Hanzhong|汉中]].
- Chu Huaiwang then mobilizes the whole country again; the episode argues that he should have focused on recovering newly lost Hanzhong, but instead remained fixated on the promised Shangyu land.
- Qin retreats through Wuguan and Shangyu, lures Chu deep toward Xianyang, and counterattacks near [[LantianBattle|蓝田之战]] after Chu has become isolated and supply-stretched.
- [[HanState|韩国]] and [[WeiState|魏国]] exploit the moment by threatening the Chu capital region and moving toward Chu's rear, forcing Chu Huaiwang to panic, withdraw, and cede cities for peace.
- The episode uses [[SunziBingfa|《孙子兵法》]] to frame the result: rulers should not raise armies from anger, and generals should not fight from resentment.
