# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》850丨桓帝为何执意杀忠臣？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 159 CE [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-852-dang-meinv-ruyun-shi-fu-haishi-huo-luml30ytbc-awpvk-7-23ehzbqpx|Hanji 852]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-853-jiemi-hanchao-ban-de-zhaoshi-gu-er-lvsa2pby8iyfevfoobobpik52mij|Hanji 853]]. It opens with a source-scoped psychology frame about self-knowledge and projection, then uses [[WeiHuanLateHan|魏桓]]'s refusal to serve to preview a court where palace luxury, horse-stable scale, powerful-family corruption, and political danger make office unattractive to a clear-eyed outsider.

The durable contribution is [[DirectRemonstranceCriminalization|直谏入罪]]. After removing [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]], Huan rewards the Deng household and eunuch-favorite field instead of repairing the court; [[LiYunLateHan|李云]] criticizes the overlarge rewards and closed feedback channels, [[DuZhongLateHan|杜仲]] voluntarily shares his punishment, [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] and other officials plead for clemency, and [[GuanBaLateHan|管霸]] makes a final mitigation attempt. Huan executes Li Yun and Du Zhong in prison, punishes intercessors, and leaves the eunuch side more arrogant.

## Key Claims

- The opening self-knowledge frame is used to explain why a ruler who cannot accept weakness or criticism may experience loyal correction as personal attack.
- [[WeiHuanLateHan|魏桓]] refuses Huan's summons because he judges that palace women, imperial horses, and powerful-family corruption cannot realistically be reduced or corrected.
- Huan's post-[[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] settlement shifts reward toward the Deng family and eunuch favorites rather than toward institutional repair.
- [[HouLanLateHan|侯览]] gains title through gift-giving and a claimed anti-Liang role, while [[ShanChaoLateHan|单超]] and the [[FiveMarquisesHuanEra|桓帝五侯]] receive enormous food-revenue grants.
- [[LiYunLateHan|李云]] submits an unsealed memorial copied to the Three Excellencies, arguing that overrewarded insiders may demoralize frontier soldiers and that imperial document handling has closed the path for truth.
- Huan treats Li Yun's memorial as an offense, arrests him, and has him interrogated under [[GuanBaLateHan|管霸]], the censorial office, and the commandant of justice.
- [[DuZhongLateHan|杜仲]] turns the case into a public solidarity act by asking to share Li Yun's fate, which further angers Huan.
- [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] argues that Li Yun's words are loyal despite being offensive, but Huan punishes the intercessors and later refuses Guan Ba's effort to reduce the case to ignorant speech.
- Li Yun and Du Zhong die in prison, and the source says eunuchs and imperial intimates become more arrogant afterward.

## Key Quotes

> "活着去、死了被送回来" - Wei Huan's warning about accepting office under Huan.

> "走了一个梁冀，又来了五侯" - the episode's compact judgment on post-Liang power replacement.

> "李云只是乡野书生，杜仲只是小小郡吏" - Guan Ba's attempted mitigation before Huan rejects release.
