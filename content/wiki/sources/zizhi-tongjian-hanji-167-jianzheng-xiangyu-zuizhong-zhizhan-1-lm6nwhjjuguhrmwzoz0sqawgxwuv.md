# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》167｜见证项羽最终之战（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-166-jiran-xiangyu-bujiang-wude-kan-liubang-ruhe-fuchou-ltwwbn9rzeqzzimmxxfihb9-drj1|Hanji 166]] by moving from [[HonggouSettlement|鸿沟议和]] and [[InstrumentalTruceBreach|工具化议和毁约]] into the opening of [[GaixiaBattle|垓下之战]]. [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s pursuit stalls when [[HanXin|韩信]] and [[PengYue|彭越]] do not arrive, so [[ZhangLiang|张良]] explains that the anti-Chu coalition needs explicit territorial payoff before it will risk final commitment. Once Liu Bang promises land, Han Xin, Peng Yue, [[LiuJia|刘贾]], [[ZhouYin|周殷]], and [[YingBu|英布]] help complete the pressure field around [[XiangYu|项羽]], who reaches Gaixia expecting an open-field battle but finds the allied army does not collapse under his first charge.

## Key Claims
- Liu Bang breaks the Honggou settlement and attacks the withdrawing Chu army, but his own force is checked near Yangxia and cannot force the final result alone.
- Han Xin and Peng Yue initially fail to appear because their expected rewards have not been made concrete enough; Zhang Liang treats this as a predictable incentive problem rather than mere disobedience.
- Zhang Liang's advice turns the old [[XiayiPlan|下邑画策]] reward promise into a final mobilization device: give Peng Yue the Suiyang-to-Gucheng area and give Han Xin the Chen-to-sea territory so they fight for their own future holdings.
- The episode makes [[TerritorialRewardMobilization|许地会师式动员]] a separate late-war mechanism: a ruler can turn autonomous allies into active participants by tying battlefield action to land they can expect to control after victory.
- Liu Jia's movement across the Huai River and Zhou Yin's surrender at Shouchun show that the final pressure on Xiang Yu comes from side and rear political-military collapse, not only from Liu Bang's frontal pursuit.
- Zhou Yin's defection is especially important because earlier episodes treated him as part of Xiang Yu's trusted old-Chu merit core.
- Xiang Yu reaches Gaixia with a smaller but still disciplined and highly loyal Chu force, so the episode preserves his battlefield charisma while showing that the strategic field around him has changed.
- The transcript ends before the decisive battle resolution; it stops at Xiang Yu's initial rush against Liu Bang's army and the surprise that the allied forces do not fall into disorder.
