# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》781丨“愚善”，才是人对己最大的恶

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 106-108 CE opening of [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Empress Dowager Deng]]'s regency before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-782-jiemi-donghan-qi-nvzi-jiannan-de-taihou-shengya-lgfljux9aaocptbhxvvifitrgxcy|Hanji 782]]. It covers [[EmperorShangOfHan|Emperor Shang]]'s infant accession and death, Deng's quick installation of [[EmperorAnOfHan|Emperor An]], [[ZhouZhangLateHan|Zhou Zhang]]'s failed anti-regency coup, disaster relief, early Qiang-front reverses, Xianbei hostage-and-trade management, and [[FanZhunLateHan|Fan Zhun]]'s relief memorial.

The durable synthesis is that Deng's regency begins as succession-security governance under compound stress. The host's opening "愚善" frame is carried into the political narrative: Deng combines relief, austerity, education, and refugee reassurance with hard boundaries against coup threats, while the court converts disaster-as-omen pressure into prison review and local-official accountability through [[DisasterOmenCrisisRedirection|disaster omen crisis redirection]].

## Key Claims

- "愚善" is framed as kindness without self-protection, attack capacity, or reality judgment, making the title-level lesson part of the wiki's [[BoundarylessKindnessTrap|boundaryless kindness]] thread.
- After Emperor He dies in 106 CE, Deng bypasses the ill Liu Sheng and enthrones the infant Liu Long as [[EmperorShangOfHan|Emperor Shang]], whose reign lasts only about eight months.
- Deng had already brought Liu Hu into the palace because she expected the infant emperor might not survive; after Emperor Shang dies, she quickly installs Liu Hu as [[EmperorAnOfHan|Emperor An]].
- Zhou Zhang opposes Deng's regency and eunuch-adjacent government assistance by Zheng Zhong and Cai Lun, then plots to close the gates, kill Deng-family relatives and palace assistants, coerce an edict, depose Deng, demote An, and enthrone Liu Sheng.
- Zhou Zhang's plot leaks; he dies by suicide on 106-11-19, implicated actors are punished, and Deng becomes harsher toward return-power remonstrance.
- Deng's early rule combines leniency and severity: elite education, strict Deng-family discipline, disaster austerity, two-meal restraint, relief efforts, and no mercy toward coup enemies.
- On 106-11-20, Deng orders officials and inspectors to reassure rumor-panicked refugees, help those who want to return, and avoid forcing those who do not.
- Early frontier policy is unstable: Deng-family generals and Ren Shang move toward Hanyang, Eastern Qiang defeat Han forces west of Ji county, while Liang Jin later wins at Zhangye and reassures surrendering Qiang leaders near Guzang.
- Xianbei leader Yanliyang receives a royal seal, carriage, horses, settlement near Ningcheng, border trade, and hostels for hostage princes and attendants, showing diplomatic incorporation beside military pressure.
- Fan Zhun's memorial links disasters to relief administration: cut central spending and construction labor, send envoys, relocate the extremely poor, and release public fields to civilians.
- When drought and disaster omens become political attacks on Deng's regency, Deng reviews prison cases, identifies a wronged prisoner, punishes the Luoyang magistrate, and reframes the omen as lower-official harm rather than proof against the regency itself.

## Key Quotes

> "愚善" - the title-level problem: kindness that lacks boundary and judgment.

> "教育或教训" - the host's contrast between learning before reality punishes and learning through painful consequences.

> "杀无赦" - the hard boundary applied to coup enemies in the regency's opening crisis.

> "宽政" - Deng's early order to calm refugees and avoid forced return.

> "天象异常" - the disaster-omen pressure Deng redirects through prison review and local accountability.
