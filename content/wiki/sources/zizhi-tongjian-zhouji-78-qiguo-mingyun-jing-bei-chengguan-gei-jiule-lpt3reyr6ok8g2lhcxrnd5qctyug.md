# 《资治通鉴·周纪》78丨齐国命运竟被城管给救了？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the post-collapse Qi branch after [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-73-lishishang-ziyou-lianai-de-kaichuangzhe-shi-shui-lkumfsfialqgdl6qpb3c-hnz7h9u|Zhouji 73]] by introducing [[TianDan|田单]] at [[JimoWarringStates|即墨]]. [[LeYi|乐毅]] keeps [[YanState|燕国]] from storming the last Qi-held cities, instead using [[OccupationPacificationStrategy|occupation pacification]] to avoid destroying the people-support he needs to govern. The second half contrasts [[YanZhaowang|燕昭王]]'s public trust in Le Yi with [[YanHuiwang|燕惠王]]'s inherited suspicion, which Tian Dan exploits through [[CounterintelligenceRumorWedge|counterintelligence rumor wedge]] tactics and turns into a [[BattlefieldCommanderReplacementRisk|battlefield commander replacement]] disaster for Yan.

## Key Claims
- [[TianDan|田单]] begins the episode as a low-ranking market administrator from [[Linzi|临淄]], not as an established general.
- His instruction to bind cart axles with iron during the flight from Anping preserves his clan and makes [[JimoWarringStates|即墨]] residents see him as practical and militarily alert.
- By this point, most of [[QiState|齐国]] has fallen; [[JuWarringStates|莒城]] and Jimo remain the two surviving Qi-held cities.
- Le Yi's failure to take Ju and Jimo quickly is not presented as simple incompetence. The episode reads his retreat, relief, and non-capture orders as a deliberate attempt to stabilize conquered Qi through people-support.
- Yan Zhaowang rejects slander against Le Yi publicly, punishes the slanderer, honors Le Yi's family, and even frames Le Yi as worthy to rule Qi.
- The source uses Yan Zhaowang's response as a high-trust ruler-commander case: he recognizes the worst-case risk of Le Yi gaining Qi, but chooses visible trust to preserve the larger settlement.
- After Yan Zhaowang dies, Yan Huiwang inherits an older grievance against Le Yi and becomes vulnerable to Tian Dan's rumor campaign.
- Tian Dan's counterintelligence works because it does not invent suspicion from nothing; it inserts a plausible story into a real new-ruler and commander split.
- Replacing Le Yi with [[QiJie|骑劫]] breaks Yan army morale and cohesion, creating the opening for Tian Dan's later counterattack.
- The episode treats the coming fire-ox reversal as conditional: the tactic becomes possible only after Yan has lost Le Yi's prestige, continuity, and occupation strategy.

## Key Quotes
> "城管救齐国命运" - the title's compressed frame for Tian Dan's low-status administrative starting point.

> "阵前换将历来是兵家大忌" - the episode's warning about replacing Le Yi at the front.
