## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode presents the [[RuxukouCampaign217LateHan|217 CE Ruxu confrontation]] as a bounded [[CaoCao|曹操]]–[[SunQuan|孙权]] test that ends with withdrawal, nominal submission, and a temporary relationship rather than decisive conquest. It then follows Sun Quan's appointment of [[ZhouTaiEasternWu|周泰]] at Ruxu: an officers' banquet makes Zhou Tai's scars and past rescue of Sun Quan public, converting private trust into legible command authority for [[ZhuRanEasternWu|朱然]] and [[XuShengEasternWu|徐盛]].

The episode also uses [[JiangQinEasternWu|蒋钦]]'s praise of Xu Sheng despite an earlier grievance to illustrate [[PublicDutyOverPrivateGrievance|public duty over private resentment]]. Its closing branch extends Cao Cao's near-imperial ritual position and records the formal selection of [[CaoPi|曹丕]] as Wei crown prince over [[CaoZhi|曹植]].

## Key Claims

- In 217 CE, Cao Cao moves to Juchao and Sun Quan defends around Ruxu; the episode interprets Cao Cao's caution as a probe of opposition rather than a confident attempt to destroy Jiangdong.
- After an inconclusive encounter, Cao Cao prepares to withdraw and leaves Xiahou Dun over Cao Ren, Zhang Liao, and other forces; Sun Quan sends Xu Xiang to express nominal submission, and Cao Cao accepts a temporary relationship.
- Sun Quan's accommodation is presented as avoiding simultaneous pressure from Cao Cao and Liu Bei after the Sun-Liu relationship has deteriorated; Cao Cao gains a face-preserving exit from a campaign with limited upside.
- Sun Quan appoints Zhou Tai to hold Ruxu over Zhu Ran, Xu Sheng, and other commanders who initially resist the authority of a man presented as lacking comparable pedigree and visible reputation.
- At a banquet, Sun Quan has Zhou Tai explain the scars received while protecting him during the Xuancheng attack and publicly states the debt behind the appointment; the other officers then accept Zhou Tai's command.
- Jiang Qin recommends Xu Sheng as loyal, diligent, forceful, and capable of leading troops despite an earlier conflict in which Xu Sheng detained Jiang Qin's subordinate and sought the man's execution.
- Emperor Xian grants Cao Cao further sovereign-style insignia and travel privileges in 217 CE, which the episode treats as evidence that practical and ritual distance from the throne has nearly disappeared.
- Cao Cao formally names Cao Pi crown prince of Wei, settling the public designation while leaving the wider Cao Pi–Cao Zhi factional struggle as context.
