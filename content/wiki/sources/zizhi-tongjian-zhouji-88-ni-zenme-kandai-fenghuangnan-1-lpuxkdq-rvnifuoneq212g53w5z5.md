# 《资治通鉴·周纪》88丨你怎么看待凤凰男（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s forty-fifth-year, 270 BCE frame after [[EyuBattle|阏与 / 燕宇之战]]. [[WeiRan|魏冉]] recommends [[KeqingZao|客卿灶 / 客卿造]] to attack [[QiState|齐国]] and seize Gang and Shou, but the episode reads the campaign as weak from [[QinState|秦国]]'s strategic view and strong from Wei Ran's private view because it expands his [[Dingtao|陶 / 定陶]] fief.

The episode then introduces [[FanJu|范雎]] before his Qin career. As a poor but ambitious Wei man serving under [[XuJia|须贾 / 徐谷]], Fan Ju is honored by [[QiXiangWang|齐襄王]], accused of leaking secrets, tortured by [[WeiQiWarringStates|魏齐]], rescued through [[ZhengAnping|郑安平]], hidden under the alias 张禄, and finally noticed by [[WangJiQin|王稽 / 王继]] as a talent worth recommending to [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]].

## Key Claims
- Wei Ran's Qi-facing campaign is presented as [[PrivateFiefExpansionThroughStateWar|以国战扩私邑]]: Qin's army is used to take territory that helps Wei Ran's Tao/Dingtao holdings more than Qin's core strategic position.
- The episode uses Wei Ran's conduct to show that Qin's foreign-relative power bloc could threaten royal authority even while Qin was militarily strong.
- [[FanJu|范雎]] is introduced as a mobile Warring States talent whose poverty blocks direct access to the Wei ruler and forces him into subordinate service under [[XuJia|须贾 / 徐谷]].
- [[QiXiangWang|齐襄王]]'s private gift of gold, beef, and wine recognizes Fan Ju's ability but creates a dangerous suspicion problem inside the Wei envoy hierarchy.
- [[XuJia|须贾 / 徐谷]] reports the gift to [[WeiQiWarringStates|魏齐]], and Wei Qi responds with private violence rather than investigation, making Fan Ju's ordeal a case of [[PrivatePunishmentWithoutProcedure|无程序私刑]].
- The host distinguishes Warring States Legalist-style "法制" from modern rule of law: harsh law and statecraft do not guarantee procedure, evidence standards, or protection from elite retaliation.
- Fan Ju's escape, alias 张禄, and discovery by [[WangJiQin|王稽 / 王继]] turn personal disaster in [[WeiState|魏国]] into a Qin talent-recruitment opening.

## Key Quotes
> "法家的'法制'完全不同于现代法制。" - the episode's distinction between Warring States legal technique and modern legal process.

> "范居从此改名换姓，自称张禄。" - the source's turning point from Wei victim to concealed Qin-bound talent.
