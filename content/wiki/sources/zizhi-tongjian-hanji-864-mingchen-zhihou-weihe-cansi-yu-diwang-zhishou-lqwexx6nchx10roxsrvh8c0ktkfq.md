# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》864丨名臣之后为何惨死于帝王之手？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 164-165 CE Huan-era layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-865-zhichang-boss-ye-rongyi-bei-zhezhong-ren-beici-ljfhfevljqnx6zceakjfbw5gwfma|Hanji 865]]. It follows [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] returning to Luoyang after [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]]'s frontier success report, then centers [[KouRongLateHan|寇荣]] as a meritorious-family figure whose anti-eunuch posture, imperial marriage ties, blocked petition route, and risky self-defense memorial end in execution.

The episode's durable synthesis is that late-Han danger runs through both access and language. Kou Rong's petition seeks public review and family preservation, but its historical analogies make the appeal sound like a charge against the ruler, creating [[ImperialPetitionOffenseRisk|申冤上书冒犯风险]]. The second half adds [[ShiBiLateHan|史弼]]'s proposal to process [[LiuKuiBohaiKing|刘亏]] through law before imperial clemency, a missed repair path now tracked as [[LegalJudgmentBeforeImperialGrace|先法度后恩宥]].

## Key Claims

- The opening uses "随和" and "善良" as source-scoped conduct advice: kindness can become self-erasure if a person repeatedly occupies the weak or sacrificial role in unsafe relationships.
- [[KouRongLateHan|寇荣]] is presented as a meritorious-family figure whose distaste for eunuchs and imperial marriage ties make him vulnerable to eunuch and court-favorite resentment.
- Kou Rong's attempt to reach Luoyang and petition is blocked; later amnesties and ransom-permission edicts do not clear the underlying accusation against him.
- His memorial asks for public adjudication and family preservation, but its comparison to [[WuZixu|伍子胥]] and [[JiBu|季布]] makes [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] read the appeal as disrespectful accusation.
- [[ShiBiLateHan|史弼]] proposes that Liu Kui's unlawful conduct be handled publicly under law, then softened by imperial pardon after judgment.
- Huan ignores Shi Bi's proposal, leaving Liu Kui's Bohai problem unresolved before the later [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-903-weishenme-quan-ni-yuanyuan-xiangbao-heshi-le-lokpt93muc8kasvn44vgsjki8bom|Hanji 903]] restoration, brokerage, and purge branch.
- The closing notice on [[HouCanLateHan|侯参]] introduces the corruption case that [[YangBingLateHan|杨秉]] develops in Hanji 865 against Hou Can and [[HouLanLateHan|侯览]].

## Key Quotes

> "不逃必死" - Kou Rong's dilemma as summarized in the source.

> "三公九卿审理自己的冤屈" - the public-review route Kou Rong says he wanted.

> "等判决后再由皇帝发布特赦" - Shi Bi's law-first, grace-second proposal for Liu Kui.
