# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》523丨古代农民起义总失败？没理想or见识短？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Yangshuo 3 / 22 BCE under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]], immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-524-weishenme-youxie-laoban-xihuan-rang-gaoguan-neidou-lsueswk4fqtcytd-m_gziubwmlsz|Hanji 524]]. It centers on [[YingchuanIronOfficeUprising|颍川铁官徒起义]], where [[ShentuShengWesternHan|申屠圣]] and about 180 iron-office convicts seize weapons, kill officials, move through multiple commanderies, gain local support, and are then suppressed by the Han state.

The durable synthesis is that coercive production systems can create rebellion while rebel victory still depends on organization, discipline, legitimacy, and governance capacity. The second half turns to [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]]'s illness and deathbed recommendation of [[WangYinWesternHan|王音]] over [[WangTanWesternHan|王谭]], presenting succession inside a dominant outer-relative bloc as both personnel judgment and self-protective family risk management.

## Key Claims

- The source frames the 22 BCE iron-office uprising as a sign that Western Han crisis had reached state production sites, not only rural tax distress.
- Iron-office laborers include penal convicts, corvee workers, and craftsmen; the episode stresses forced labor, poor subsistence, harsh punishment, and product-quality problems under state iron control.
- [[ShentuShengWesternHan|申屠圣]] leads about 180 iron-office convicts to seize an arsenal, kill the iron official and commandery clerk, leave the city, and move through multiple localities with support from nearby farmers.
- The episode uses the failed uprising to generalize about peasant-rebel weakness: poor organization, weak discipline, limited strategic vision, thin legitimacy, little scholar support, and difficulty turning seizure into governance.
- Li Zicheng and Taiping examples are used as comparative, source-scoped illustrations of how discipline breakdown, external-threat misreading, and leadership loss can prevent rebel movements from becoming stable regimes.
- [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]] rejects Wang Tan and related Wang-family candidates as extravagant and recommends [[WangYinWesternHan|王音]] as cautious, proper, and safer for command.
- The Wang Feng succession scene shows power inversion: [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] asks a dying powerholder to decide the next Da Sima, while Wang Feng uses that choice to reduce posthumous family and reputation risk.
