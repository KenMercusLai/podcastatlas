# 《资治通鉴·周纪》06丨魏文侯选谁当宰相呢（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode fills in the [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]] chancellor-selection question that the adjacent part 2 only uses as a title frame. It centers on [[LiKe|李克 / 李悝]]'s five situational tests for judging people, the contest between [[WeiCheng|魏成]] and [[DiHuang|翟黄]], and the difference between recommending many capable subordinates and attracting people a ruler treats as teachers. The second half turns to [[WuQi|吴起]], using his morally troubling record and military value to complicate the wiki's [[TalentVirtueDistinction|才德之分]] branch with a more role-fit-oriented use of flawed talent.

## Key Claims
- In 389 BCE, [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]] considers two chancellor candidates, [[WeiCheng|魏成]] and [[DiHuang|翟黄]], and asks [[LiKe|李克]] for advice.
- Li Ke first avoids giving a direct name, then offers [[SituationalCharacterTests|five situational character tests]]: observe a person's intimates, associates after wealth, referrals after promotion, boundaries under duress, and refusals under poverty.
- Di Huang's claim rests on referral service: he says he recommended [[WuQi|吴起]] for West River, [[XimenBao|西门豹]] for Ye, [[LeYang|乐羊]] for [[ZhongshanState|中山国]], Li Ke for Zhongshan administration, and [[QuHoufu|屈侯鲋]] as teacher for the ruler's son.
- Li Ke says the decision remains Wei Wenhou's, but infers that Wei Cheng wins because he spends most of his income outside the household and thereby attracts [[ZiXia|子夏]], [[TianZifang|田子方]], and [[DuanGanmu|段干木]], figures Wei Wenhou honors as teachers.
- The episode's personnel lesson is not simply "more recommendations wins"; it argues that [[TalentReferralQuality|referral quality and level]] matter because some people become usable ministers while others raise the ruler's own horizon.
- The source identifies Li Ke with [[LegalistRulerTechnique|early Warring States reform]] and says his legal tradition helped shape later Qin law through [[ShangYang|商鞅]], though this remains a compact source-scoped claim.
- Wu Qi is introduced through the "killing his wife to seek command" story in [[LuState|鲁国]], where [[QiState|齐国]]'s attack creates a loyalty test because his wife is from Qi.
- The episode keeps Wu Qi's reputation problem visible: his military talent is extraordinary, but allegations about ambition, lust, filial conduct, and personal ruthlessness make him hard to trust.
- Li Ke's advice to Wei Wenhou on Wu Qi is a role-fit judgment: despite Wu Qi's defects, his military ability is presented as surpassing [[SimaRangju|司马穰苴]], so Wei can use him for war against [[QinState|秦国]].

## Key Quotes
> "居视其所亲，富视其所与。" - Li Ke's opening character-test pair.

> "达视其所举，穷视其所不为。" - the episode's promotion-and-duress tests.

> "贫视其所不取。" - the poverty-and-temptation test.

> "我愿意终身为你的弟子。" - Di Huang's surrender after Li Ke explains the Wei Cheng comparison.
