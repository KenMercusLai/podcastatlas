# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1073丨壶关之战：曹操的屠城计划害惨了谁？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the [[GaoGanLateHan|高干]] rebellion branch opened by [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1070-jiaochu-shaxue-weimeng-quan-buxia-panjiang-caocao-lqvyfglj3qefhyxyebfvvda8om5o|Hanji 1070]]. At [[HuguanLateHan|壶关]], [[CaoCao|曹操]] threatens to bury everyone after taking the city, turning surrender into certain death; resistance hardens until [[CaoRen|曹仁]] persuades him to withdraw the order, after which the city surrenders. Gao Gan flees, fails to secure Xiongnu aid, and is killed while trying to reach Liu Biao.

The second half follows [[LiangXiLateHan|梁习]]'s settlement of [[BingzhouLateHan|并州]]. Liang first receives local strongmen courteously and recommends some for office, then disperses local young men among expeditionary commands, moves tens of thousands of their relatives to [[YeLateHan|邺城]], and uses force against refusal. The episode presents the result as frontier order, Xiongnu submission, stricter law, farming and sericulture, and the recommendation of displaced scholars, but the combination remains coercive as well as administratively effective.

## Key Claims

- Before campaigning, Cao Cao leaves Cao Pi at Ye and assigns [[CuiYanLateHan|崔琰]] as his tutor, joining rear security to successor formation.
- Cao Cao's “城拔皆坑之” order makes Huguan's soldiers and civilians expect death even after defeat, and months of resistance follow.
- Cao Ren argues that an extermination threat removes the defenders' reason to surrender; Cao Cao withdraws it, and Huguan capitulates.
- Gao Gan flees after the Huguan position collapses, is refused help by the Xiongnu, and is captured and killed by Wang Yan while trying to reach Liu Biao.
- Cao Cao appoints Liang Xi as Bingzhou inspector with military authority in a province marked by frontier pressure, armed strongmen, and weak administration.
- Liang Xi's settlement is sequential: honor and appoint strongmen, draw local youths into military service, disperse those troops, relocate their families to Ye, and suppress refusal.
- The episode credits this policy with making the Xiongnu chanyu and tribal leaders submit, restoring taxation and service, encouraging agriculture and sericulture, and tightening law.
- Liang Xi also recommends refugee scholars including [[ChangLinLateHan|常林]], Yang Jun, Wang Xiang, Xun Wei, and Wang Ling for local office.

## Key Quotes

> “城拔皆坑之。” - Cao Cao's threat that the episode presents as the cause of hardened resistance at Huguan.

> “围城必示之活门。” - the surrender-incentive principle attributed to Cao Ren's remonstrance.
