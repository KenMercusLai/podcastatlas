# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1033丨割据军阀袁术，最终吐血而亡？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode connects an opening lesson on restraint and [[PostSuccessArrogance|得势后的骄傲]] to [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s 199 CE collapse and [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s post-[[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] decision field. It narrates Yuan Shu's luxury amid starving troops, failed refuge with [[ChenJianYuanShuRetainer|陈简]] and [[LeiBoYuanShuRetainer|雷波]], attempted transfer of the imperial title to Yuan Shao, interception by [[LiuBei|刘备]] and [[ZhuLingLateHan|朱灵]], and death at Jiangting. The episode then shifts to Yuan Shao's enlarged northern position, [[GengBaoLateHan|耿包]]'s title-test proposal, [[JuShou|沮授]]'s recovery-and-harassment advice, [[GuoTu|郭图]] and [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]]'s immediate-war counterargument, and [[CuiYanLateHan|崔琰]]'s ignored warning before the [[GuanduCampaign|官渡]] confrontation.

## Key Claims
- The opening uses "low profile, humility, and leaving room" as the moral frame for both Yuan Shu's and Yuan Shao's failure after visible success or advantage.
- After self-coronation, [[YuanShu|袁术]] keeps luxury for his palace circle while soldiers go hungry, making his regime collapse a supply, morale, and legitimacy failure rather than only a battlefield result.
- Yuan Shu's last refuge options fail: Chen Jian and Lei Bo refuse him, his soldiers desert, and his attempt to send the imperial title to [[YuanShao|袁绍]] is blocked when [[CaoCao|曹操]] sends Liu Bei and Zhu Ling to intercept.
- Yuan Shu's remnants do not become a renewed regime. [[YuanYinLateHan|袁胤]] takes Yuan Shu's seals, family, and troops to [[LiuXunLateHan|刘勋]], while [[XuQiuGuanglingLateHan|徐秋]] sends the [[HeirloomImperialSeal|传国玉玺]] back to the court.
- After destroying Gongsun Zan, Yuan Shao controls the northern four-province field but becomes more arrogant toward the court; Geng Bao's imperial-title proposal exposes a temptation Yuan Shao then disowns by killing the proposer.
- Ju Shou argues against immediate attack on [[Xudu|许都]] after the Gongsun Zan war, recommending agricultural recovery, a court-facing report of victory, gradual positioning at [[LiyangYellowRiverLateHan|黎阳]], shipbuilding, arms preparation, and cavalry harassment.
- Guo Tu and Shen Pei reject Ju Shou's caution by stressing Yuan Shao's troop advantage, comparing the anti-Cao campaign to King Wu's attack on Shang Zhou, and calling Ju Shou's plan too conservative.
- Yuan Shao weakens Ju Shou's command authority by splitting his troops among Ju Shou, Guo Tu, and [[ChunyuQiong|淳于琼]], turning strategic disagreement into a command-unity problem before Guandu.
- Cui Yan independently warns that attacking while the emperor is in Xudu will run against public attachment, but Yuan Shao's status, grain, force, and family prestige make him unable to accept the caution.

## Key Quotes
> "事不可使尽，福不可享尽" - opening restraint maxim.

> "过把皇上瘾就死" - host's summary of Yuan Shu's short imperial episode.

> "义兵无敌，骄兵必败" - Ju Shou's distinction between righteous and arrogant warfare.
