# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》356-2｜历史上东越王这样死去多憋屈？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] sequel closes the [[YuShanDongyueKing|东越王余善]] campaign announced in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-356-1-budong-yuren-ni-ping-sha-dang-lingdao-lsaa6j1jskivfhyvvpbpmnorq-jm|Hanji 356-1]]. It first attributes to [[YangPuWesternHan|杨仆]] a proposal to move [[HanguPass|函谷关]] east to Xin'an, reading the new boundary as both a personal status gain and an enlargement of the territory treated as the imperial core.

The second half says Han pressure and internal defection, rather than a simple frontal defeat, end [[DongyueWesternHan|东越]]. [[WuYangYueMarquis|吴阳]] defects with seven hundred followers, while [[JianchengHouAo|建成侯敖]] and [[JuguDongyueKing|繇王居股]] kill Yu Shan and surrender. [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] rewards defectors and campaign participants, then orders the population of the Minyue-Dongyue region moved to the Yangtze-Huai area; the episode presents the resulting emptied territory as a hard post-conquest settlement rather than a routine battlefield consequence.

## Key Claims

- The episode treats Hangu Pass as a military and political boundary separating the protected inner zone from the eastern or outer region.
- Yang Pu's proposed eastward relocation of the pass to Xin'an is presented as aligning his own status interest with Han Wudi's interest in expanding and supervising the imperial core.
- Yu Shan occupies difficult terrain, but Han military pressure makes continued loyalty inside Dongyue increasingly costly.
- Wu Yang's defection and the killing of Yu Shan by Ao and Jugu turn internal fracture into the decisive campaign mechanism.
- Han Wudi rewards surrendering insiders and participating officers, making submission materially legible after victory.
- The final population transfer to the Yangtze-Huai region removes the conquered population from its difficult home terrain and leaves the old territory described as empty.

## Key Quotes

> “把这个关口东移三百里。” — the proposal the episode uses to connect personal title interest with territorial administration.

> “敖和居股联手杀死了余善。” — the reported internal killing that ends Yu Shan's rule.

## Contradictions

- The transcript's “汉武官” is routed to [[HanguPass|函谷关]], and “灵保” to the Lingbao setting; “杨浦” and “洛于善” are normalized to the established [[YangPuWesternHan|杨仆]] and [[YuShanDongyueKing|余善]] identities.
- The transcript forms “眼猴无阳,” “姚王洛居谷,” and “建成侯敖” are cautiously routed to [[WuYangYueMarquis|越衍侯吴阳]], [[JuguDongyueKing|繇王居股]], and [[JianchengHouAo|建成侯敖]]. The exact titles and spellings require primary-text checking.
- Several battlefield names and the sequence of rewarded titles are too corrupted in the supplied transcript to normalize confidently; they are not promoted into separate canonical identities.
- The episode's explanation that Han Wudi had already been waiting for Yang Pu's proposal is an inference about motive, not independently established fact.
- “闽越” and “东越” overlap in the episode's regional narration but are not treated as perfectly interchangeable political labels.
- The claim that the transfer made the region completely empty is retained as the episode's reported outcome, not a demographic measurement.
