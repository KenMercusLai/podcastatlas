# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》981丨三国最刚谋士程昱：一句话让曹操叹服

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate pre-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-982-ba-yi-ba-liubei-ru-xu-beihou-de-liyi-zhi-zheng-ljajdc95xtylhkn5teefz26yyyz1|Hanji 982]] late-Han sequence through [[ChengYu|程昱]]'s intervention with [[CaoCao|曹操]], the [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] succession from [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]] to [[LiuZhang|刘璋]], and [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]]'s final transfer recommendation for [[Xuzhou|徐州]]. It reads [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s request for Cao Cao's family to live at Ye as a [[HostageBackedDependencyTrap|家属质押式依附陷阱]] and treats Cheng Yu's advice as the hinge between fear-driven subordination and continued independent contention. The Yi Province branch shows [[ImportedTroopsLocalEliteCleavage|外来武装与本土豪族裂缝]] through Dongzhou troops and local elite tension, while the Xuzhou branch supplies the first statement of the succession problem that Hanji 982 later deepens through [[LocalEliteProxySelection|地方豪强代理人选择]].

## Key Claims
- The opening boundary lesson is a host interpretation: intimate or dependent relationships can expose weakness, so distance and self-command matter in both personal relations and political alignment.
- In 194 CE, after [[CaoCao|曹操]] loses much of [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] and runs short of grain, [[YuanShao|袁绍]] urges him to send his family to Ye, which the episode treats as a hostage-backed dependency move.
- [[ChengYu|程昱]] argues that Yuan Shao has ambition but insufficient judgment, while Cao Cao still has three cities, more than ten thousand soldiers, and advisers such as [[XunYu|荀彧]] and Cheng Yu himself.
- Cao Cao accepts the warning, thinks of Han Fu's fate, and abandons the idea of submitting to Yuan Shao.
- [[MaTeng|马腾]] and [[HanSui|韩遂]] are described as attacking [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] under a "clear the ruler's side" pretext after a grain-road conflict; the host reads Ma Teng's practical aim as Guanzhong supply control.
- [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]] secretly supports Ma Teng with five thousand Shu troops and has [[LiuFanLateHan|刘范]] and [[LiuDanLateHan|刘诞]] act inside Chang'an, but Li Jue's intelligence network exposes the plan and both sons die.
- After fire damages Mianzhu and Liu Yan dies, senior Yi Province officials including [[ZhaoWeiLateHan|赵韪]] support [[LiuZhang|刘璋]] as the mild successor.
- The episode argues that [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] is structurally divided between Liu Yan's Dongzhou military bloc of Nanyang and Sanfu migrants and the region's older local elite families.
- [[GanNing|甘宁]]'s failed rebellion with Shen Mi and Lou Fa is used as evidence that Liu Zhang's order cannot integrate important local talent; Gan Ning's later move east becomes a source-scoped talent-loss signal.
- The Han court cannot simply impose an outsider on Yi Province and eventually recognizes Liu Zhang as inspector, after which Liu Zhang appoints Zhao Wei to attack [[LiuBiao|刘表]].
- [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]]'s death creates a Xuzhou power vacuum; the episode has him tell [[MiZhu|糜竺]] that only [[LiuBei|刘备]] can protect the province.
- Liu Bei initially hesitates and suggests [[YuanShu|袁术]] because his own force is thin and Xuzhou is surrounded by local and external pressures.
- [[ChenDeng|陈登]] and [[KongRong|孔融]] persuade Liu Bei not to hand Xuzhou to Yuan Shu; Chen Deng promises local troops and political room, while Kong Rong frames refusal of the public opportunity as future regret.
- Liu Bei accepts only after Xuzhou officials, people, money, and arguments converge, making the takeover a dangerous political position rather than a simple act of virtue.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is recorded. Hanji 981 supplies the first Xuzhou succession scene, while Hanji 982 provides the deeper interest explanation and Hanji 986/987 later return to the same transfer as Cao Cao's Xuzhou temptation.
- The source's speculation about Tao Qian's medical condition and the modern relationship-boundary opening are kept as source-scoped interpretation rather than independent historical claims.
