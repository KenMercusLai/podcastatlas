## Summary
In this [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] interview, [[YeGuofu|叶国富]] explains how [[Miniso|名创优品]] joined low-price product discipline, supplier trust, centralized store operations, IP licensing, original-IP experiments, and global stores. The second half applies a related product-and-supply-chain thesis to [[YonghuiSuperstores|永辉超市]]: move from monetizing shelf access toward choosing and owning products, rebuild stores, simplify suppliers, and develop private label. The account is operationally detailed but remains a founder interview whose market forecasts, financial figures, and turnaround expectations require later outcome evidence.

## Key Claims
- [[Miniso|名创优品]] initially paired export-capable suppliers, cash settlement, low prices, rapid assortment turnover, and a headquarters-operated investment-partner model to make store replication easier.
- The company later shifted toward licensed and original character goods, large destination stores, and [[InterestConsumptionRetail|兴趣消费零售]], while keeping different IP shares for different store formats.
- [[BrowseLedPhysicalRetail|逛店驱动的实体零售]] depends on discovery, touch, display, atmosphere, service, and impulse rather than treating the store as a slower version of e-commerce.
- [[LongHorizonIPPortfolioBuilding|长期IP组合孵化]] treats blockbuster creation as partly unpredictable, so the company signs multiple artists, tests products, concentrates behind observed demand, and sometimes limits short-term sales to preserve an IP.
- [[YeGuofu|叶国富]] frames quality consumption and interest consumption as the two large opportunities in Chinese consumer markets, while recognizing that IP success and global scaling remain uncertain.
- [[YonghuiSuperstores|永辉超市]] is presented as a [[ProductLedSupermarketTransformation|产品主导型超市转型]]: rebuild stores, replace fee-driven shelf logic, reduce supplier sprawl, strengthen private label, and adapt package size and freshness to Chinese households.
- Data systems, store visits, customer feedback, weekly product selection, and simplified meeting documents are described as operating infrastructure for both creative retail and supermarket reform.

## Key Quotes
> “线上线下不是零售的本质” — the interview's recurring distinction between channel choice and product or experience quality.

> “卖产品，不卖货架” — Ye Guofu's contrast between product-led supermarkets and fee-led traditional hypermarkets.

> “好逛” — the compact requirement he gives for physical stores that create discovery and unplanned purchase.
