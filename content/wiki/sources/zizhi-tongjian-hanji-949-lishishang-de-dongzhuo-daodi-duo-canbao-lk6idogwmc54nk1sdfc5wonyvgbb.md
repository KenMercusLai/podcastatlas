# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》949丨历史上的董卓到底多残暴？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate pre-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-950-budong-quanli-beihou-de-renxing-bi-bai-wuyi-lu8-u78yqvvwwarsz8dlevvzchxr|Hanji 950]] late-Han field after [[DongZhuo|董卓]] controls [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] and before the open [[AntiDongZhuoCoalition|反董卓联盟]] forms. It pairs Dong Zhuo's civilian terror in Luoyang with a [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] succession crisis around [[YufuluoChanyu|于扶罗]], then turns to Dong Zhuo's xiangguo privileges and his attempt to buy legitimacy through offices and famous scholars. The distinct contribution is the contrast between coercive recruitment and real recognition: [[XunShuangLateHan|荀爽]], [[ChenJiLateHan|陈纪]], and [[HanRongLateHan|韩融]] enter Dong Zhuo's court field, while [[ShentuPanLateHan|申屠蟠]] refuses and becomes the source's clearest case of [[CoercedTalentLegitimacy|胁迫征士合法性]] failing.

## Key Claims

- The opening maxim about not provoking accumulated anger is used as a source-scoped bridge from interpersonal conduct to Dong Zhuo's political danger: private force and public resentment can compound until authority loses usable recognition.
- Around 189 CE, [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] is presented as a city under direct military terror: Dong Zhuo's soldiers burn, loot, seize adult men for military use, abuse women, and make ordinary households experience court control as bodily danger rather than abstract regime change.
- The [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] succession crisis leaves [[YufuluoChanyu|于扶罗]] unable to secure a normal court judgment after [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s death and the Luoyang disorder; his later alliance with [[BaiboArmy|白波军]] fails to produce supplies or a home-base return.
- [[DongZhuo|董卓]] is appointed xiangguo and receives special court privileges such as entering with sword and shoes, but the episode treats those honors as formalized domination rather than stable legitimacy.
- Dong Zhuo's alleged imperial ambitions are presented with source-scope caution: the host says he prepares symbolic imperial items, while also stressing that his real base is thin outside Luoyang and that he controls a hollowed court more than a settled empire.
- Dong Zhuo uses [[ZhouBiLateHan|周毖]], [[WuQiongLateHan|伍琼]], [[ZhengTaiLateHan|郑泰]], and [[HeYongLateHan|何颙]] to clean corrupt officials and recommend respected people, making talent recruitment part of his legitimacy packaging.
- [[XunShuangLateHan|荀爽]]'s rapid rise from summons to the Three Excellencies in 93 days is framed less as normal merit recognition than as Dong Zhuo's rush to borrow Yingchuan scholar prestige.
- [[ChenJiLateHan|陈纪]] and [[HanRongLateHan|韩融]] are named as additional famous men brought into office, reinforcing that Dong Zhuo needs the symbolic capital of scholars even while ruling through fear.
- [[ShentuPanLateHan|申屠蟠]] refuses Dong Zhuo's summons despite pressure from others. The episode presents his silence and noncompliance as a soft but firm rejection that Dong Zhuo cannot easily punish without exposing the coercive nature of the recruitment.
- Shentu Pan's earlier conduct - defending Gou Yu, mourning his parents, studying in seclusion, carrying Wang Ziju's body home, and repeatedly refusing offices - gives the refusal moral weight rather than making it mere evasion.

## Key Quotes

> "众怒之日不可犯，众怨之日不可任" - opening maxim used to frame Dong Zhuo's accumulated political danger.

> "赞拜不名、入朝不趋、剑履上殿" - the special court privileges attached to Dong Zhuo's xiangguo appointment.

> "他只是笑笑不说话" - the source's image of Shentu Pan's refusal to enter Dong Zhuo's service.
