# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》160｜陈平攻心计，比甄嬛传精彩（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode returns from the [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-159-2-zhangliang-shuole-sha-jiuxia-hanchao-lvs9elvrjp-ypz63w4czpuhbsfb|Hanji 159-2]] strategy correction to the [[XingyangStandoff|荥阳相持]] crisis itself. [[LiuBang|刘邦]] seeks peace after [[Aocang|敖仓]] is lost and [[XiangYu|项羽]] presses Xingyang, but [[FanZeng|范增]] blocks the deal and pushes for a final attack. [[ChenPing|陈平]] then proposes a funded [[CounterintelligenceRumorWedge|反间流言楔入]]: exploit Xiang Yu's suspicion and stinginess over rewards, alienate his few trusted ministers and generals, and let Chu's internal trust collapse before Han attacks again.

## Key Claims
- The episode starts from Han year 3, fourth month: Aocang is lost, Liu Bang is surrounded at Xingyang, and he asks to divide the world with Xiang Yu along Xingyang's east-west line.
- [[XiangBo|项伯]] initially persuades Xiang Yu to consider the peace offer, but Fan Zeng rejects it and argues that Liu Bang should be destroyed while he is vulnerable.
- Chen Ping's diagnosis separates courtesy from incentive design. Xiang Yu is respectful and attracts clean, ritual-minded people, but he is reluctant to grant titles and rewards; Liu Bang is arrogant and coarse, yet willing to share rank, money, and delegated authority.
- The source treats reward distribution as a political weapon: talent movement and camp loyalty depend on whether followers expect concrete payoff, not only on whether the ruler behaves politely.
- Chen Ping names Fan Zeng, [[ZhongliMo|钟离眜]], [[LongJuChuGeneral|龙且]], and [[ZhouYin|周殷]] as the small set of upright and reliable Chu-side figures close to Xiang Yu.
- Chen Ping asks Liu Bang for tens of thousands in gold to send agents into Chu and make those loyal figures appear unreliable.
- Liu Bang gives Chen Ping forty thousand gold and does not audit the spending, making Chen Ping's hidden work a ruler-authorized version of [[CovertPoliticalTechnology|阴谋式政治技术]].
- The first named rumor targets Zhongli Mo: agents spread that he has long led armies and earned great merit but has not been made king, so Chu generals want to join Han, destroy the Xiang clan, divide the land, and become kings themselves.
- Xiang Yu does not immediately kill Zhongli Mo, but the source says he no longer trusts him as before.
- The host links the operation's effectiveness to Xiang Yu's already existing weaknesses: suspicion, reluctance to reward, and a camp in which useful people either leave or remain with divided hearts.
- The episode closes by turning toward Fan Zeng's departure and death on the road to Pengcheng, but the available transcript does not fully show the intermediate steps tying Chen Ping's rumor operation to Fan Zeng's exit.

## Key Quotes
> "绝命毒士" - the episode's opening label for Chen Ping's morally uncomfortable strategy.

> "四万金" - the budget Liu Bang gives Chen Ping for the counterintelligence operation.

> "妇人之仁" - the Han Xin-style critique the host uses for Xiang Yu's kindness without effective reward distribution.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is recorded. This source reinforces earlier Han Xin and Hanji 147 criticism that Xiang Yu respects people but fails at reward distribution, while adding the concrete Chen Ping operation that turns that weakness into a rumor wedge.
- Source-scope caution: the transcript jumps from Chen Ping's first rumor against Zhongli Mo to Fan Zeng's departure and death. This page records Fan Zeng's exit as the episode's implied next result, but does not claim the omitted middle causal chain is fully shown in the available text.
