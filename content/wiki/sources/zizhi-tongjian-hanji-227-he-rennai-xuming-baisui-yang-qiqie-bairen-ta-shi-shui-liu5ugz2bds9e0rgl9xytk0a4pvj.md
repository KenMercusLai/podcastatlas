# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》227｜喝人奶续命百岁，养妻妾百人，他是谁？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode joins three pressures in [[LiuHeng|汉文帝刘恒]]'s later reign: crop failure, flood, drought, epidemic, and food shortage prompt an edict of self-questioning; repeated [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] attacks on Yunzhong and Liaodong lead to another heqin settlement; and [[ZhangCang|张苍]] leaves the chancellorship after an allegation that he profited through a recommendation. The episode then expands Zhang Cang's profile through archives, mathematics, calendar and metrology work, gratitude toward [[WangLingHanMinister|王陵]], and sensational late-life longevity anecdotes.

The succession to the chancellorship supplies a second institutional contrast. Liu Heng reportedly considers his capable brother-in-law [[DouGuangguoWesternHan|窦广国]] but declines to appoint him because the public may read the choice as favoritism toward consort kin; he instead appoints [[ShentuJiaWesternHan|申屠嘉]], whose military seniority and refusal of private visitors are presented as signs of probity. The durable addition is therefore not the title's longevity hook alone, but the connection among crisis responsibility, recommendation accountability, and visible restraint in senior appointment.

## Key Claims

- In 162 BCE, Liu Heng responds to poor harvests, floods, drought, epidemic, and grain shortage by asking whether official stipends, commerce, brewing, or livestock consumption contribute to scarcity and ordering senior officials and scholars to submit useful proposals without concealment.
- Repeated Xiongnu attacks reportedly inflict especially heavy losses in Yunzhong and Liaodong, after which Liu Heng sends a letter and restores peace through heqin with the chanyu identified in the transcript as 栾提稽粥.
- Zhang Cang is removed as chancellor after being accused of recommending a中候官 for private profit; the host also treats his earlier water-virtue dispute with [[GongsunChenWesternHan|公孙臣]] as political background rather than the stated charge.
- Zhang Cang's career is presented as spanning Qin archival office, service under [[LiuBang|刘邦]], rescue from execution through Wang Ling's intercession, about fifteen years as chancellor, and later work associated with calendars, weights and measures, and recension of 《九章算术》.
- Zhang Cang continues to honor Wang Ling after attaining high office, visiting Wang Ling's widow during regular leave and bringing food and gifts.
- The episode says the elderly Zhang Cang lived on human milk after losing his teeth, maintained many wives and concubines, and died in 152 BCE at more than one hundred years old; these are source-attributed biographical anecdotes, not medically established explanations of longevity.
- Liu Heng reportedly rejects Dou Guangguo as chancellor despite considering him capable because appointment of the empress's brother could look partial, then appoints Shentu Jia, a veteran described as upright and unwilling to receive private visitors at home.

## Key Quotes

> “只要是对老百姓有利的，都不要有所隐瞒。” - the episode's rendering of Liu Heng's invitation for crisis proposals.

> “天下人会不会认为我偏爱外戚呢？” - the political-appearance concern attributed to Liu Heng when considering Dou Guangguo.

## Connections

- [[LiuHeng|汉文帝刘恒]] - ruler who publicly assumes crisis responsibility, pursues a heqin settlement, and treats perceived kin favoritism as a constraint on choosing a chancellor.
- [[ZhangCang|张苍]] - outgoing chancellor whose dismissal, gratitude, scholarly work, and late-life anecdotes form the episode's center.
- [[ShentuJiaWesternHan|申屠嘉]] - veteran appointed chancellor and characterized through probity and resistance to private access.
- [[DouGuangguoWesternHan|窦广国]] - capable consort relative considered but passed over because the appointment could appear partial.
- [[WangLingHanMinister|王陵]] - minister whose intervention saves Zhang Cang from execution and receives long-term reciprocal loyalty.
- [[ImperialSelfCriticismEdict|帝王自责诏]] - the famine edict adds an early Western Han case where public self-questioning opens a policy-feedback request.
- [[RecommendationAccountabilityTalentSelection|举荐问责式选才]] - Zhang Cang's stated dismissal charge is the abuse-side boundary of recommendation-based appointment.
- [[HanXiongnuHeqinPolicy|汉匈和亲政策]] - another heqin agreement is used as bounded frontier de-escalation after damaging raids.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. Hanji 225-2 supplies the water-versus-earth legitimacy conflict and Zhang Cang's loss of influence; Hanji 227 distinguishes that background from the stated recommendation-for-profit charge attached to his resignation.
- The episode says both Yunzhong and Liaodong suffered more than ten thousand casualties, but the figures, raid chronology, chanyu identification, letter, and terms of renewed heqin require comparison with transmitted histories.
- Zhang Cang's calendar, metrology, and 《九章算术》 contributions, the claim that its algorithms preceded European equivalents by fifteen hundred years, his service length, age, household size, sexual practice, milk diet, and cause of longevity remain source-scoped.
- Liu Heng's private deliberation about Dou Guangguo, Shentu Jia's archery role and campaign record, exact appointment dates and titles, and the claim that no stronger founding-era candidate remained require source comparison.
