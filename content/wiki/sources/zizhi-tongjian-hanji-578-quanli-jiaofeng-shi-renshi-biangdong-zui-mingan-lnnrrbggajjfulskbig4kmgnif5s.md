# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》578丨权力交锋时人事变动最敏感！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianping 2 / 5 BCE under [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]], before the later [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-582-nvren-de-chenhenxin-zui-duo-nanren-ming-ljiuneown6ah1yx5g_uchpb5escp|Hanji 582]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-583-douzhidao-ta-you-wenti-weishenme-hai-tiba-llkef6xxkyf5kufv2j_zcqv3wogs|Hanji 583]] notes. It follows [[ZhuBoWesternHan|朱博]]'s proposal to restore the Yushi Dafu office, Aidi's linked appointment of [[DingMingWesternHan|丁明]] as Da Sima Wei General, and [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]]'s pressure campaign against [[FuXiWesternHan|傅喜]] and [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]].

The durable synthesis is that personnel change is treated as the most sensitive surface of power conflict. A formal institutional restoration can also reorganize hierarchy, succession paths, and factional placement: Zhu Bo makes Yushi Dafu a stepping-stone toward chancellorship, Aidi uses office change to consolidate control and strengthen consort-kin placement, and Kong Guang's removal shows how prior succession advice and dowager conflict leave senior officials exposed.

## Key Claims

- Zhu Bo argues that abolishing the Yushi Dafu office has blurred authority and weakened the route by which capable high officials can rise toward chancellorship.
- Restoring Yushi Dafu is presented as a way to rebuild office sequence: local and commandery officials are tested, promoted to two-thousand-stone status, considered for Yushi Dafu, and eventually made eligible for chancellor.
- Aidi accepts the proposal in summer fourth month, moves Zhu Bo into the Yushi Dafu role, and appoints Ding Ming as Da Sima Wei General with subordinate officials.
- The episode reads the administrative change as a power move: restoring an old office is not neutral when it rearranges real access, status, and factional leverage.
- Fu Taihou's edict removes Fu Xi from imperial access and sends him back to his fief, leaving only Kong Guang from the earlier anti-Fu line.
- Zhu Bo and Fu Taihou-aligned officials attack Kong Guang, and Aidi strips Kong Guang of office and marquis status.
- Kong Guang's vulnerability is traced to earlier opposition to making Liu Xin crown heir and to later conflict with Fu Taihou over title and court-power questions.

## Key Quotes

> "人事变动背后是权力博弈" - the episode's compact reading of the official changes.
