# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》655丨万众归心到民怨沸腾，王莽做错了哪一步？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 18-19 CE late-[[XinDynasty|新朝]] crisis between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-654-jiemi-xihan-yangxiong-de-chuanqi-rensheng-lq3o-13h2in89qwh0dvchczlvrqk|Hanji 654]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-656-zhichang-buhui-shuohua-de-ren-hen-nan-hunchutou-lpn15smz7r1glekugzwtm2tpw-tl|Hanji 656]]. It follows [[FanChongChimei|樊崇]]'s early armed growth in Langya and Taishan, parallel Qing-Xu rebellion by Pang An, Xu Xuan, Xie Lu, Yang Yin, and Diao Zidu, [[WangMang|王莽]]'s failed suppression response, and his attempt to manipulate Xiongnu succession through Xu Bu Dang despite weak mobilization capacity.

The durable synthesis is that Wang Mang's error is an interacting priority failure. As local rebellion and frontier danger expand, he answers with calendar projection, new-era rotation, immortality claims, New Music display, mass conscription, a one-thirtieth levy, official horse burdens, speculative anti-Xiongnu technologists, and dismissal of [[YanYouXinGeneral|严尤]] after Yan You says Shandong rebels should take priority over Xiongnu.

## Key Claims

- [[FanChongChimei|樊崇]]'s group begins in 18 CE as a small Langya/Taishan force and grows rapidly, showing that late-Xin local order is already loose before the later Red Eyebrows naming layer.
- Multiple Qingzhou and Xuzhou rebel groups create a moving disorder field, while official suppression fails to produce military or political control.
- Wang Mang's coercion of Xiongnu-linked figures and plan to install Xu Bu Dang over the Xiongnu creates a frontier provocation without the usable army needed to support it.
- Calendar, era-name, immortality, and New Music performances try to project cosmic and ritual order while the practical crisis worsens.
- Mass conscription, military-criminal recruitment, the one-thirtieth tax, textile transport, and official horse maintenance convert frontier and rebellion crises into broader livelihood pressure.
- Wang Mang's recruitment of spectacular anti-Xiongnu inventors and miracle-workers shows a reputation-first search for talent that tolerates implausible schemes.
- Yan You's warning identifies the central priority error: Xiongnu can wait, but Shandong rebellion is the immediate danger; Wang Mang answers by dismissing him.

## Key Quotes

> "匈奴未能为害也，当忧山东盗贼" - Yan You's priority warning as summarized by the episode.

> "为了博取珍惜人才的名声" - the episode's reading of Wang Mang's implausible anti-Xiongnu talent recruitment.
