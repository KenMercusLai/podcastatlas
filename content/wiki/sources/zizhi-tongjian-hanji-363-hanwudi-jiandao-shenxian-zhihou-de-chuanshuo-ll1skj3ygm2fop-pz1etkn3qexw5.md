# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》363｜汉武帝见到神仙之后的传说

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode juxtaposes two Yuanfeng 2 / 109 BCE branches of [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s rule. In the first, [[GongsunQingFangshi|公孙卿]] reports a divine being at Donglai, receives appointment as 中大夫, and prompts an imperial journey and a large continuing search that produces only a supposed giant footprint rather than a divine meeting.

The second branch returns to the [[YellowRiver|黄河]] breach at Huzi. After recalling the episode's claim that [[TianFenWesternHan|田蚡]] had earlier discouraged repair partly because his own fief benefited, it describes [[GuoChangWesternHan|郭昌]] and Ji Ren mobilizing tens of thousands, Han Wudi inspecting the work, attendants carrying brushwood, the breach being closed, channels being cut, and [[XuanfangPalace|宣房宫]] being built at the site. The durable contrast is between resource-consuming pursuit of unverifiable transcendence and visible state mobilization against public harm.

## Key Claims

- In early 109 BCE, Gongsun Qing reports seeing a divine being at Donglai and says the being seemed willing to meet the emperor.
- Han Wudi goes east, appoints Gongsun Qing 中大夫, waits for the encounter, and sees only what is described as a giant footprint.
- The emperor then dispatches thousands of fangshi to continue searching for immortals and gathering lingzhi while drought affects the realm.
- The episode backfills Yellow River breaches at Suanzao in 168 BCE and Huzi in 132 BCE, and attributes the long delay in repairing Huzi partly to Tian Fen's private interest in protecting his fief.
- In 109 BCE, Guo Chang and Ji Ren mobilize tens of thousands to close the Huzi breach; Han Wudi visits, offers a white horse and jade disk to the river, and orders accompanying officials to carry brushwood.
- After the breach is closed, the court builds Xuanfang Palace at the site and cuts two channels to direct river water northward.
- The episode presents the project as both practical flood control and political theater: it restores security to the Liang-Chu region while displaying the emperor's ability to mobilize officials and protect subjects.

## Key Quotes

> "身富而心贫，知足者身贫而心富。" - the episode's moral frame for Han Wudi's wealth, power, and unsatisfied desire for longer life.

> "神人欲见天子。" - the message attributed to Gongsun Qing that triggers the emperor's journey and reward.
