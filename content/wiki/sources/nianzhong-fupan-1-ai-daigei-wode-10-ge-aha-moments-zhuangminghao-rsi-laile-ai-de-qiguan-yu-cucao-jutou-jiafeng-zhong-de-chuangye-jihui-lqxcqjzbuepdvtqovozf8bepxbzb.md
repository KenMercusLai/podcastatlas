# [年终复盘①]AI带给我的10个Aha Moments｜庄明浩：RSI 来了、AI 的奇观与粗糙、巨头夹缝中的创业机会

## Summary
This [[ShizilukouCrossing|十字路口Crossing]] year-end review has [[Koji]] and [[ZhuangMinghao|庄明昊]] use ten personal “Aha Moments” to connect agent-product convergence, [[Seedance]] and the video-model production loop, early [[RecursiveSelfImprovement|recursive self-improvement]], cyber misuse, small-model diffusion, on-demand personal software, model price-performance competition, and multimodal demonstrations. Its durable synthesis is double-sided: capability is becoming cheaper, more autonomous, and easier to perceive through [[AIModelCapabilitySpectacle|spectacle]], while standardized polish increases the relative value of roughness, unpredictability, and [[HumanImperfectionCreativeValue|human texture]].

## Key Claims
- The early [[OpenCloud]] wave helped converge agent-product questions around persistent execution, local permissions, context, notifications, [[AISkills|skills]], and [[MultiAgentCollaboration|multi-agent collaboration]], even where individual products later cooled.
- [[Seedance]] is presented as a [[ByteDance]] video-model advantage supported by a production-distribution-consumption-advertising loop, although the source leaves open whether specialist video models or broader multimodal/world-model systems win over time.
- An [[Anthropic]] “When AI Builds Itself” visualization is treated as a public marker that AI already participates in parts of AI research and training; the episode correctly stops short of treating local automation as proof of open-ended [[RecursiveSelfImprovement|RSI]].
- The same coding and tool-use ability that accelerates research can compress cyberattack timelines, turning “vibe coding” into a possible “vibe hacking” risk and strengthening the case for continuous automated defense.
- The source reports that smaller models are reaching older frontier capability more quickly, making cost, latency, privacy, and local deployment increasingly important alongside absolute frontier performance.
- [[OnDemandPersonalSoftware|on-demand personal software]] lets users generate narrow, short-lived tools for their own workflows, weakening some lightweight app demand while leaving trust-heavy payments, passwords, security, and privacy work more resistant.
- Price-performance competition is increasingly expressed through a Pareto frontier: models that are both stronger and cheaper can make dominated alternatives hard to justify, while routine usage still spreads across many non-frontier models through routing and task fit.
- [[AIModelCapabilitySpectacle|capability spectacle]] becomes a practical release signal when models turn difficult ideas into dynamic video, 3D, games, or interactive explanations that audiences can evaluate without understanding saturated benchmarks.
- Deliberately rough visual work and uncontrolled recording conditions can feel newly scarce after prolonged exposure to polished synthetic media, reinforcing the value of human presence without proving that roughness is always better.
