# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》457｜汉宣帝心胸狭隘？《史记》功臣杨恽因何而死？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the Yang Yun case under [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]]. [[YangYunWesternHan|杨恽]], remembered here as [[SimaQian|司马迁]]'s grandson and a transmitter of [[Shiji|《史记》]], loses office after the Dai Changle affair, then turns private resentment, banquet display, and a mocking reply to [[SunHuizongWesternHan|孙会宗]] into punishable political evidence.

The durable synthesis is double-edged. The episode treats Yang Yun's death as a failure of self-protection after disgrace: he does not perform fear, withdrawal, or repentance, and 《报孙会宗书》 gives enemies a text to hand upward. It also accepts [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s criticism that Xuan's executions of Zhao Guanghan, Gai Kuangrao, Han Yanshou, and Yang Yun were a serious stain on otherwise strong governance, making the case both a lesson in dangerous speech and a qualification to Xuan's "middle restoration" profile.

## Key Claims

- Yang Yun's historical position is tied to the survival and circulation of Shiji because the episode presents him as Sima Qian's grandson and a figure who helped bring the work into public transmission.
- After losing title and office, Yang Yun remains wealthy but fails to adopt the expected posture of fear, shame, and withdrawal.
- Sun Huizong's letter is a survival warning: a disgraced former official with many guests and open complaints is making himself legible as unrepentant.
- Yang Yun's reply turns grievance into durable text by mocking Sun Huizong and, in the host's reading, indirectly attacking ruler and ministers.
- A solar eclipse enables accusation escalation: an informer frames Yang Yun's luxury and resentment as politically and cosmologically blameworthy.
- Xuan's court extracts the letter, sentences Yang Yun to public waist-cutting, banishes his wife and sons to Jiuquan, and removes or demotes associated figures including Yang Tan, Wei Xuancheng, and Sun Huizong.
- Sima Guang's judgment moves the case beyond Yang Yun's imprudence, arguing that Xuan's killing of several upright or capable officials did not convince public opinion and damaged his otherwise good rule.
- The host turns the story into [[HistoricalReadingAsSurvivalWisdom|读史处世智慧]]: possessing Shiji is not the same as reading history deeply enough to understand power, human nature, and consequence.

## Key Quotes

> "读史使人明智" - the host's closing frame for why the Yang Yun case matters beyond the incident itself.

> "藏之名山，传之其人，通邑大都" - the Shiji transmission frame used to introduce Yang Yun's family position.
