# 《资治通鉴·周纪》42丨给孟尝君提意见的后果

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]] branch by separating two judgments. [[SimaGuang|司马光]] criticizes Mengchang Jun's large-scale [[WarringStatesRetainerPatronage|养士]] when it gathers worthy and unworthy people alike into a private power network, but he praises Mengchang Jun's ability to accept useful correction through the ivory-bed story involving [[GongsunXu|公孙须]] and [[DengTuzhi|邓图直]].

## Key Claims
- The episode stays in the 321 BCE / [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]] forty-eighth-year setting already developed in Zhouji 40-41.
- [[SimaGuang|司马光]] does not reject养士 itself; he defines legitimate patronage as supporting worthy people so they can serve the state and the people.
- In Sima Guang's standard, a true worthy person combines virtue, administrative ability, discernment, long-range judgment, and commitment to仁义.
- The criticism of [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]] is therefore a purpose-and-selection criticism: indiscriminate hospitality to贤愚善恶 turns public resources into private retainer power.
- The episode reads Sima Guang's charge against Mengchang Jun as theft of state wealth, humiliation of the ruler, extraction from the people, and reputation-seeking under the cover of recruiting talent.
- Sima Guang still gives Mengchang Jun credit where the evidence warrants it, so the episode treats the judgment as two-sided rather than a simple denunciation.
- In the [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] story, the Chu king gives Mengchang Jun an expensive ivory bed; [[DengTuzhi|邓图直]], assigned to transport it, fears being ruined if it is damaged.
- Deng Tuzhi asks [[GongsunXu|公孙须]] to prevent the delivery and offers an ancestral sword as repayment, so the advice is useful but not motive-pure.
- Gongsun Xu argues that accepting such a valuable gift in [[ChuState|楚国]] would damage Mengchang Jun's reputation for义 and廉, and would create a diplomatic precedent other states could not comfortably match.
- Mengchang Jun accepts the advice immediately and refuses the ivory bed.
- When Gongsun Xu admits the private bargain, Mengchang Jun does not punish him; he announces that anyone who can promote his good name and block his faults should advise him even if privately bribed.
- The episode's positive lesson is [[ContentOverMotiveRemonstrance|content-over-motive remonstrance]]: useful correction should be tested by whether the claim is right, not only by whether the speaker's private motives are clean.

## Key Quotes
> "圣人养贤以及万民" - Sima Guang's standard for public-purpose patronage.

> "采葑采菲，无以下体" - the classical analogy used to defend taking the usable part of advice.

> "只看意见是否正确，不追究进谏者的私心" - the episode's formulation of Mengchang Jun's advice-taking strength.
