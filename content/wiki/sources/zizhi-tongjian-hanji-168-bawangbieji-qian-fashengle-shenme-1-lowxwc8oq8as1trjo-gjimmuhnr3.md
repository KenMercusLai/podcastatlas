# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》168｜霸王别姬前发生了什么（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the [[GaixiaBattle|垓下之战]] sequence after the battlefield defeat in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-167-jianzheng-xiangyu-zuizhong-zhizhan-2-li14pizmfrzhpg8m5bmxdx8ibw7p|Hanji 167 part 2]]. It shifts from formation and encirclement to the night psychology of defeat: [[FourSidedChuSongs|四面楚歌]] breaks [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s morale, [[GaixiaSong|《垓下歌》]] makes his private despair visible, and [[YuJi|虞姬]]'s suicide turns the final scene into both political collapse and personal tragedy. The episode then follows Xiang Yu's breakout with eight hundred men, [[GuanYing|灌婴]]'s cavalry pursuit, the farmer's misdirection near Yinling, and the host's claim that "Heaven's will" should be read through lost people-support.

## Key Claims
- [[LiQingzhao|李清照]]'s later praise of Xiang Yu frames his ending as a durable cultural memory, not only a failed military result.
- The episode treats [[FourSidedChuSongs|四面楚歌]] as psychological warfare: hearing Chu songs from all sides makes Xiang Yu believe Chu has already been conquered or that many Chu people have joined Han.
- The host argues that [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] weakens the scene by omitting [[GaixiaSong|《垓下歌》]], so she supplements the chronicle with [[Shiji|《史记》]]'s Xiang Yu biography.
- [[GaixiaSong|《垓下歌》]] is read as tragic self-revelation: Xiang Yu still remembers his overwhelming strength, but he now blames timing, the horse's failure, and his inability to save Yu Ji.
- The episode reads the song as militarily costly because a commander's public despair accelerates the army's morale collapse.
- [[YuJi|虞姬]] is presented as the person who understands Xiang Yu's unsolved dilemma: he cannot protect her in a breakout, cannot leave her to Han forces, and cannot say directly that her death is the only clean exit.
- Yu Ji's suicide is interpreted as a reversal of apparent strength and weakness: the battlefield strongman becomes emotionally unable to decide, while the dependent woman acts with hard finality.
- Xiang Yu's breakout with eight hundred men is treated as a further abandonment of the remaining Chu army after Yu Ji's death removes his private obstacle.
- [[GuanYing|灌婴]] leads five thousand cavalry in pursuit after Han forces discover the breakout, reducing Xiang Yu's party to a little over one hundred after the Huai crossing.
- The farmer's false direction near Yinling becomes the episode's people-support lesson: the host reads Xiang Yu's repeated "Heaven will destroy me" language as a sign that lost民心 appears as天意.
