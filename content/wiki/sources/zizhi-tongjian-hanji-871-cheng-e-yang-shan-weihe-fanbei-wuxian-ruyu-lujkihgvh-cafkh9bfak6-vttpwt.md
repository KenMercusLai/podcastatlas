# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》871丨惩恶扬善，为何反被诬陷入狱？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 166 CE social and political substrate before the first Party Prohibition arc in Hanji 872-877. It follows the [[ZhouFuLateHan|周福]] and [[FangZhiLateHan|房植]] rivalry at Ganling, [[FanPangLateHan|范滂]] and [[CenZhiLateHan|岑晊]] as entrusted commandery aides, [[GuoTaiLateHan|郭泰]] and [[JiaBiaoLateHan|贾彪]] as Taixue reputation leaders, and the cases where [[ChengJinLateHan|成瑨]] and [[LiuZhiTaiyuanLateHan|刘质]] punish eunuch-protected offenders but are themselves imprisoned.

The episode's core contribution is that late-Han public opinion and local enforcement are already entangled before formal party accusations. Local moral discipline, Taixue praise-and-blame, and eunuch-protected complaint channels together create the precondition for [[QingyiReputationPressure|清议声誉压力]], [[PartyNetworkTreasonFraming|朋党网络谋反化]], and [[EunuchPatronageViolenceShield|宦官庇护下的地方暴行]].

## Key Claims

- The rivalry between Zhou Fu and Fang Zhi turns teacherly favor, office promotion, and local reputation into organized north-south factional lines at Ganling.
- Fan Pang in Runan and Cen Zhi in Nanyang show how commandery aides can become the real moral-enforcement engines when administrators entrust judgment to them.
- Fan Pang's refusal to appoint Li Song after [[TangHengLateHan|唐衡]]'s recommendation shows local integrity resisting eunuch-backed patronage.
- Taixue's tens of thousands of students, led by Guo Tai and Jia Biao, make praise and blame a public force that even high officials fear.
- Zhang Fan of Wan and Zhao Jin of Taiyuan show the local-damage side of palace-connected privilege: wealth, gifts, harem ties, and eunuch status can convert ordinary prosecution into political risk.
- Cheng Jin and Liu Zhi punish Zhang Fan and Zhao Jin after an amnesty, but Hou Lan-backed complaint channels turn their enforcement into a capital case.
- The Zhang Jian versus Hou Lan branch begins here as a continuation: local enforcement against eunuch abuse becomes both moral action and future retaliation risk.

## Key Quotes

> "天下规矩，房伯武；因师获印，周仲进" - the Ganling reputation rhyme that marks the Zhou Fu / Fang Zhi rivalry.

> "清裁疾恶" - the episode's working description of Fan Pang's official style.

> "天下模楷，李元礼；不畏强御，陈仲举；天下俊秀，王叔茂" - the Taixue praise formula that turns elite reputation into public judgment.
