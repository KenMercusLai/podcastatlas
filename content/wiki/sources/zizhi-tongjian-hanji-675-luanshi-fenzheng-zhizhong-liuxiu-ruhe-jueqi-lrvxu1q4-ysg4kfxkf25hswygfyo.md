# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》675丨乱世纷争之中，刘秀如何崛起？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens the current Eastern Han backfill immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-676-hanguangwudi-liuxiu-jiedao-sharen-shijian-shimo-luebqlivstmkjaqqfdlcukxtnmii|Hanji 676]]. It sets [[WangMang|王莽]]'s failed [[XinDynasty|新朝]] reforms, disaster, [[ChimeiRebellion|赤眉]], and [[LulinRebellion|绿林]] as the collapse field, then enters Jianwu 1 / 25 CE through [[FangWangGengshi|方望]]'s failed use of [[RuziYing|孺子婴 / 刘婴]], [[ChimeiRebellion|Chimei]] expansion, [[GongsunShuEasternHan|公孙述]]'s Chengdu imperial claim, and [[LiuXiu|刘秀]]'s risky northern campaign.

The durable synthesis is that Liu Xiu's rise is not presented as uninterrupted success. The episode foregrounds a serious defeat at Shunshui, [[WuHanEasternHan|吴汉]]'s rumor-control and morale stabilization, the enemy's fear-driven night withdrawal, and [[ChenJunEasternHan|陈俊]]'s [[ScorchedEarthSupplyDenial|坚壁清野式断粮]] plan, which lets Liu Xiu convert a setback into renewed battlefield advantage through discipline, information, and supply control.

## Key Claims

- Wang Mang's archaising and disruptive reform program, compounded by floods, droughts, and social breakdown, sets the background for Chimei, Lulin, and other revolt forces.
- Fang Wang and Gong Lin install Ruzi Ying as an attempted Han restoration figure at Linjing, but Liu Xuan's chancellor Li Song suppresses the group and kills them.
- Ruzi Ying's life is presented as a tragedy of royal identity under disorder: Wang Mang first isolates him as a child, then later actors reuse him as a dynastic banner.
- Deng Yu breaks through Jiguan, defeats a Hedong commandery force, and surrounds Anyi while Chimei forces merge in Hongnong and defeat Liu Xuan's commanders.
- Chimei growth is treated as an organized military escalation: after defeating Su Mao and Li Song, the movement is divided into thirty large camps.
- Gongsun Shu accepts Li Xiong's advice to claim imperial status in Chengdu, establishing Chengjia with the Longxing reign title.
- Liu Xiu's Shunshui defeat nearly kills him, but Wu Han steadies the army during death rumors and Chen Jun's light-cavalry supply-denial plan helps break the retreating enemy.
