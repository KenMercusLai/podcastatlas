# No.227 对话叫兽易小星&粉墨：人人都能用 AI 拍电影了？

## Summary
This [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] episode has [[LiuFei|刘飞]] interviewing [[JiaoshouYiXiaoxing|叫兽易小星]] and [[LiChenFenmo|李晨]] of [[FenmoTeam|粉墨]] about making the AI short [[NvwaZhiSi|《女娲之死》]] for the [[BilibiliAICreationContest|B站 AI 创作大赛]]. The conversation turns [[AIVideoProductionWorkflow]] and [[AIDirectorCoreWorkflow]] into a concrete film-production account: script development, style choice, storyboards, image generation, model-specific video generation, editing, deleting shots, regenerating, sound, color, and human review all remain part of the work.

The durable contribution is a practitioner boundary for AI filmmaking. The guests argue that [[VideoModels]] lower production and trial costs, but they do not remove story, taste, performance judgment, audience understanding, or responsibility. AI may absorb more spectacle, low-quality acting, short-drama, and previsualization work, while human creators remain decisive where comedy timing, subtle performance, style control, and original point of view matter.

## Key Claims
- [[BilibiliAICreationContest|B站 AI 创作大赛]] is presented as a large creator event with a 3 million RMB prize pool, 77 awards, and enough participation to reveal current AI-video workflow strengths and limits.
- [[NvwaZhiSi|《女娲之死》]] used a claymation-like stop-motion style because the material fit the mythic clay/human theme and because the team wanted the result to feel less like a generic AI video.
- [[JiaoshouYiXiaoxing|叫兽易小星]] treats AI as another creator tool in the long movement from film cameras to digital cameras, phones, and short-video platforms: creative rights keep moving downward, but strong creators remain scarce.
- [[LiChenFenmo|李晨]] says AI video first helped him see whether expensive script ideas could become images at all, especially scenes involving snowfields, grasslands, and bodies emerging from water.
- [[FenmoTeam|粉墨]] is using AI short films as a small-team anthology workflow, closer to a series of speculative shorts than to one-off prompt demos.
- The guests argue that current AI video has visual spectacle before it has equally strong story expression; once visual access is democratized, audience attention shifts back to creativity, emotion, and theme.
- [[AIDirectorCoreWorkflow]] appears in the collaboration: 易小星 supplied overall idea and script, while 李晨 broke the text into executable visual plans and generation tasks.
- The episode rejects the idea that AI production workers are merely "抽卡师"; good AI artists still need composition, lighting, scene design, timing, and the ability to tell models what should appear when.
- [[NvwaZhiSi|《女娲之死》]] reportedly took over half a month of script development and about one month of generation, while a comparable ten-minute stop-motion animation might take around two years traditionally.
- AI can move a project from 0 to 80% quickly, but the guests say the jump from 80% to 85% or 90% can require disproportionately more regeneration, editing, and professional finishing.
- AI is useful in preproduction as a research and brainstorming assistant; 易小星 used it to check whether the "humans forget Nüwa" premise had precedents such as Lu Xun's "补天".
- Future live-action film use depends on editability and integration with real footage; 易小星 speculates that if those improve, AI could touch a large share of movie shots within a few years.
- Film workers are described as watchful rather than simply resistant: public AI use can trigger criticism around laziness, craft, plagiarism, copyright, and labor replacement.
- The guests expect earlier pressure on low-quality, repetitive, or low-threshold content, including some short-drama and weak acting work, while top human actors retain a higher performance ceiling.
- Prompt work is becoming easier as models understand natural language better, so the long-term barrier shifts from tool operation to taste, expression, and knowing what one wants.
- AI-generated comedy remains weak in this account because successful comedy depends on face, motion, pause, breath, rhythm, scene chemistry, and fine-grained editability rather than only text jokes.
- The concrete workflow used tools including [[Midjourney]], Banana, [[KlingAI|可灵]], [[Seedance]], [[ChatGPT]], [[Doubao|豆包]], Veo, Higgsfield, and [[Claude|Claude Opus]], selected according to image, motion, action, physical feedback, or brainstorming need.
- The guests warn against blindly buying courses or tool subscriptions: direct practice, English ability, source verification, and cross-checking AI research matter more than anxiety-driven consumption.
- AI can hallucinate facts and lose generation control, so people still need verification, prompt constraints, review, and final responsibility for important work.

## Key Quotes
> "尽量做得不像 AI 作品" - 易小星 on the style aim behind 《女娲之死》.

> "从 0 到 80% 很快" - 李晨 on why final polish can still be expensive.

> "不要报班" - both guests' practical advice to new AI creators.
