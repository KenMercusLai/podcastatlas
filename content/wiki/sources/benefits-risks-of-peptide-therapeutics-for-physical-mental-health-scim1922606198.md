# Benefits & Risks of Peptide Therapeutics for Physical & Mental Health

## Summary
This [[HubermanLab]] solo episode has [[AndrewHuberman]] organize therapeutic peptides into tissue repair, metabolism and growth, longevity, and vitality. Its durable contribution is an evidence-and-risk map: approved drugs and off-label prescriptions differ from animal-data-rich experimental compounds, while pleiotropic signaling, contamination, broad tissue growth, and unknown long-term effects keep peptide use inside qualified medical supervision.

## Key Claims
- [[PeptideEvidenceHierarchy]] separates FDA-approved indications, off-label prescribing, animal evidence, anecdotal human use, and gray- or black-market products rather than treating all peptides as one category.
- [[BPC157ExperimentalRepairPeptide|BPC-157]] has animal-model angiogenesis, nitric-oxide, fibroblast-migration, and wound-healing signals, but the episode identifies essentially no rigorous human clinical evidence for routine repair use.
- [[ThymosinBeta4TissueRepair]] groups thymosin beta-4 and its shortened analogue TB-500 as plausible repair peptides whose controlled human evidence remains limited.
- [[GrowthHormoneSecretagogues]] can raise growth hormone or IGF-1 through GHRH- or ghrelin-related pathways, but compound-specific hunger, anxiety, cortisol, prolactin, receptor-desensitization, glucose, structural-growth, and tumor concerns prevent a simple anti-aging interpretation.
- [[BioregulatorPeptides|Epitalin]] is linked to pineal-peptide, telomere, ocular-aging, and longevity hypotheses without human trials showing lifespan extension.
- [[MelanocortinPeptideVitality]] separates pigmentation-only claims from brain-penetrant mood, libido, appetite, nausea, blood-pressure, and melanoma-related concerns, including the approved but indication-specific PT-141/Vyleesi branch.
- [[KisspeptinReproductiveSignaling]] places kisspeptin upstream of GnRH, LH, FSH, testosterone, and estrogen while treating generalized vitality or libido use as uncertain.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction found. The episode reinforces the existing distinction among mechanism, animal data, anecdote, approval, and controlled human evidence.
- The stated BPC-157 dose patterns, continuous-use warning, growth-hormone decline rate, fasting windows, peptide combinations, epitalin effects, and vitality-peptide outcomes remain source-scoped public education rather than individualized treatment guidance.
- FDA approval for one compound and indication does not validate related analogues, off-label uses, or gray-market preparations.
