# EP276 对话贾樟柯（上）：一个时代走远，一定是因为另一个时代在靠近你

## Summary
This [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] episode has [[JiaZhangke|贾樟柯]] respond to the short-video rediscovery of his older film images, dance scenes, and quoted lines. It frames online remix not only as meme circulation but as [[FilmClipRemixAfterlife]], where ordinary life, labor, farewell, hometown, and drifting youth reactivate [[OrdinaryLifePoetics]] in works such as [[ShanHeGuRen|《山河故人》]], [[JiangHuErNv|《江湖儿女》]], and [[TianZhuDing|《天注定》]]. The episode also sets up [[EraTransitionProximity]]: Jia says an era never simply leaves by itself; it recedes because another era, including AI and new forms of community, is approaching.

## Key Claims
- Jia welcomes short-video clips labeled as scenes he "wanted to shoot" because everyday life already contains poetic images; the viewer's feeling can matter even when the filmed subject had no conscious expressive intention.
- The [[ShanHeGuRen|《山河故人》]] dance ending resonates because the music's freedom collides with aging, staying in the hometown, and a freedom that remains desired but unrealized.
- Jia's creative emotion is tied to personal and collective memory: the disco opening of [[ShanHeGuRen|《山河故人》]] and the departure scene in [[JiangHuErNv|《江湖儿女》]] both show film feeling arising from familiar youth scenes and imagined character fate.
- The quoted line that everyone can only accompany you for one stretch of road is renewed by [[MobilityFarewellGeneration]]: leaving home, moving for education, work, study abroad, or short-term factory jobs makes separation more frequent for younger people.
- Jia treats "young people have no direction" as an ironic, cross-generational claim rather than a simple criticism of today's youth.
- The episode's AI-anxiety answer is not a city-village split. It distinguishes people whose time is mostly spent in virtual platforms from people whose physical lives remain fuller, making [[VirtualPhysicalLifeBalance]] a condition for how technology anxiety is felt.
- Jia's title claim becomes [[EraTransitionProximity]]: globalization, community forms, social management, emotional methods, and AI mark a new civilization approaching while an older era recedes.
- The episode gives younger people a qualified advantage and risk: less historical burden may mean more freedom, but it can also weaken understanding of history, people, and continuity.

## Key Quotes
> "日常生活中本身就有很多诗意" - Jia's answer to short-video images that look like his films.

> "每个人只能陪你走一段路，迟早是要分开的" - the line whose online afterlife the episode connects to youth mobility.

> "一个时代走远，一定是因为另一个时代在靠近你" - the title formulation of era transition.
