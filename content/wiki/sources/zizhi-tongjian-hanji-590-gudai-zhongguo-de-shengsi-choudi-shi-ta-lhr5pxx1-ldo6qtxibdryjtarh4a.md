# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》590丨古代中国的生死仇敌，是它？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the Jianping 4 / 3 BCE [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] debate from [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-589-jiemi-wunao-shengqian-de-kepa-houguo-ln7zzxy9dydebce_b3bomkcr872d|Hanji 589]] by unpacking [[YangXiong|扬雄]]'s memorial on why [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] was the [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]] court's special strategic opponent. Yang argues that a formally submissive chanyu's wish to visit Chang'an should not be rejected as a mere reception expense, because the visit is tied to decades of war, heqin, Western Regions positioning, and fragile diplomatic trust.

The durable synthesis is that prevention is cheaper than repair when a frontier settlement rests on credibility. Yang Xiong treats reception ritual, gift cost, and response wording as part of a larger [[DiplomaticCostRiskAccounting|diplomatic cost-risk accounting]] problem: if Han damages trust before open conflict exists, neither force nor persuasion may easily restore Xiongnu submission.

## Key Claims

- Yang Xiong ranks Xiongnu above other frontier opponents because other enemies had been defeated, territorially incorporated, or made manageable faster, while Xiongnu remained a long-running northern threat.
- The chanyu's requested court visit is presented as the accumulated result of earlier imperial effort, not as an isolated ceremony that can be postponed indefinitely to save money.
- Rejecting or slighting the visit could erase prior Han favors, give Xiongnu grounds to accuse Han of bad faith, and turn submission into renewed suspicion.
- Yang argues that once suspicion hardens, Han would struggle to coerce Xiongnu militarily or persuade it back through softer means.
- The memorial's prevention logic stresses detecting risk before it is visible: avert rupture while the cost is still ceremonial and diplomatic, not military.
- Western Regions policy is interpreted as part of the anti-Xiongnu system: Cheshi control, institutions, and the thirty-six-state order matter because they constrain Xiongnu.
- Han Aidi accepts the remonstrance, recalls the already-returning Xiongnu envoy, agrees to the chanyu's next-year visit, and rewards Yang Xiong.

## Key Quotes

> "明者视于无形，聪者听于无声" - Yang Xiong's prevention-before-crisis maxim in the episode's paraphrased memorial.

> "所有这些手段都是为了牵制匈奴" - the host's summary of Yang Xiong's Western Regions argument.
