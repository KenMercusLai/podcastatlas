# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》444｜“西汉撤回了和亲公主”，匈奴？？？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Shenjue 3 / 59 BCE under [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]]. It centers on [[LiuXiangfuPrincessWesternHan|刘相夫]]: Han prepares to marry her into [[Wusun|乌孙]] to reinforce the [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] containment strategy, but recalls her from Dunhuang after Wengguimi dies and [[NimiWusun|泥靡 / 狂王]] takes the throne.

The durable synthesis is that heqin diplomacy can be canceled as a risk-control move before the bride enters the frontier polity. [[XiaoWangzhiWesternHan|萧望之]]'s caution proves useful once Wusun succession changes the intended groom from Yuanguimi to the Xiongnu-linked Nimi, while the episode's later salary edict frames low official pay as a corruption incentive rather than a purely moral defect.

## Key Claims

- The episode frames the Hexi Corridor and Silk Road as strategic infrastructure for Han access to the Western Regions and for separating Qiang-Xiongnu connections.
- Han-Wusun alliance management matters because Wusun support had already helped weaken Xiongnu in 71 BCE.
- Wengguimi wants Xieyou Princess's son Yuanguimi to inherit and asks Han to send another princess for him, deepening Wusun's break with Xiongnu.
- Xiao Wangzhi warns that Wusun is distant and unstable, but Xuan still creates Liu Xiangfu as a princess and sends her west with gifts.
- When Liu Xiangfu reaches Dunhuang, Wengguimi has died and Nimi has seized the Wusun throne, changing the intended marriage target.
- Continuing the marriage would expose Liu Xiangfu to a ruler tied to a Xiongnu maternal line and would also place her in the same household structure as her aunt [[XieyouPrincessWesternHan|解忧公主]].
- Chang Hui reports the change, goes ahead to challenge Wusun, and the Han court accepts Xiao Wangzhi's renewed advice to recall Liu Xiangfu.
- The episode treats Xuan's order to raise low-level official salaries as an early "high salary to nourish integrity" policy, but it qualifies the mechanism by saying higher pay can restrain corruption only partially.

## Key Quotes

> "嫁到半路又被接回" - the episode's compact frame for Liu Xiangfu's unusual heqin outcome.

> "基层稳则天下安" - the host's governance summary after the low-level salary edict.
