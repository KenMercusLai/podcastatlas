# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》855丨你有多无情，就有多强大

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 160-161 CE Huan-era layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-856-lishi-gaosu-ni-ren-ruo-xinluan-zhenjiao-zi-luan-lo7m-9mz-pvstxtfgavrne4bszks|Hanji 856]]. It opens with a source-scoped boundary-strength lesson, then contrasts [[LiuChongLateHanMinister|刘宠]]'s clean [[KuaijiCommandery|会稽郡]] governance with [[GuoHongLateHan|郭闳]]'s obstruction of [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]] and [[HuHongLateHan|胡闳]]'s failed succession as Protector of the Qiang.

The durable historical contribution is an early [[HuangfuGuiLateHan|皇甫规]] and [[RootCausePacification|查因安抚式治理]] layer. Huangfu Gui argues that the Qiang problem depends less on finding fiercer generals or rereading war books than on clear politics and law-abiding local administrators; his later victory and large surrender are followed by a new [[ShendiQiang|沈氐羌]] crisis that sets up Hanji 856's trapped, epidemic-struck army.

## Key Claims

- The opening argues that strong people are not simply cruel: they may stay outwardly mild while holding firm boundaries when principle or interest is touched.
- In 160 CE, [[LiuChongLateHanMinister|刘宠]] is summoned from his [[KuaijiCommandery|Kuaiji]] governorship to become sikong after reducing burdensome levies and restraining illegal officials.
- Kuaiji elders from the Shanyin/Ruoye area bring farewell coins because Liu Chong's administration reduced official disturbance; he refuses the money and accepts only one coin from each person as a token.
- [[XianlingQiang|先零羌]], [[ShendiQiang|沈氐羌]], and other Qiang groups attack Bingzhou and [[LiangzhouLateHan|Liangzhou]], prompting the court to send [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]] with the Five Camps.
- [[GuoHongLateHan|郭闳]] wants part of the merit and obstructs Duan Jiong's movement, delaying the opportunity and contributing to desertion among Hu soldiers serving far from home.
- Guo Hong shifts blame onto Duan Jiong, after which Duan is recalled, imprisoned, and sent to Left School hard labor before later sources show his restoration.
- [[HuHongLateHan|胡闳]] replaces Duan as Protector of the Qiang but lacks the reputation and planning capacity to contain the Qiang attacks on camps, forts, and commanderies.
- [[HuangfuGuiLateHan|皇甫规]] petitions from Taishan commandery, presenting himself as border-raised, experienced in two Qiang disorders, and useful as an envoy and adviser for the Sanfu region.
- Huangfu Gui's central diagnosis is political: clean administration, lawful commandery governors, and trustworthy rule matter more than searching for brave commanders or studying [[SunziBingfa|Sunzi]] and Wu Qi in isolation.
- [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] appoints Huangfu Gui zhonglangjiang; he defeats Qiang forces, kills more than eight hundred, and receives a mass surrender before a new Shendi Qiang attack on Zhangye and Jiuquan leaves his force cut off and diseased.

## Key Quotes

> "小事糊涂，大事杀伐果断" - opening boundary-strength frame.

> "甄选一枚钱" - Liu Chong's token acceptance after refusing the Kuaiji elders' farewell money.

> "与其费力寻找勇猛将领，不如推行清明政治" - Huangfu Gui's root-cause frontier diagnosis as rendered in the source.
