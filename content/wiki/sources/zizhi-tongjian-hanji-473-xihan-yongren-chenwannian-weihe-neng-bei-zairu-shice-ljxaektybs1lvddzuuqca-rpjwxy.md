# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》473丨西汉庸人陈万年，为何能被载入史册？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Chuyuan 5 / 44 BCE under [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]], between the Zhuya debate and the later Gu Ji frontier mission sequence. It centers [[ChenWannianWesternHan|陈万年]] as a mediocre but successful Yushi Dafu whose advancement depends on calibrated flattery toward powerful figures, then contrasts him with his son [[ChenXianXinOfficial|陈贤]], who is presented as upright, legally skilled, and later willing to withdraw from [[WangMang|王莽]]'s regime.

The durable synthesis is that advancement through flattery can place a weak official in high rank, but it does not become a stable moral inheritance. Chen Wannian reads patron preferences and serves them, while Chen Xian rejects his father's "officialcraft" lesson, defends [[ZhuYunWesternHan|朱云]] against [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]]'s accusation, suffers imprisonment and exile, and later turns integrity into refusal under Wang Mang.

## Key Claims

- Chen Wannian reaches Yushi Dafu not through visible governing achievement but through adaptive flattery and patron service.
- His tactics are preference-specific: he uses money toward wealth-loving Shi Gao and personal sickbed attentiveness toward the respected Bing Ji.
- Bing Ji's deathbed recommendation converts Chen Wannian's deferential care into high office, making patron trust rather than public merit the key advancement channel.
- The father-son instruction scene matters because Chen Wannian's remembered value is negative pedagogy: the "lesson" he tries to transmit is sycophantic survival.
- Chen Xian is presented as rejecting that inheritance through upright enforcement, defense of Zhu Yun, later imprisonment, and eventual reemployment.
- Chen Xian's later refusal to serve Wang Mang connects the early father-son contrast to the already recorded [[PrincipledOfficeRefusal|principled office refusal]] branch.
- [[GongYuWesternHan|贡禹]]'s appointment after Chen Wannian's death supplies a comparison case: moral reputation and repeated remonstrance stand beside, and partly against, Chen Wannian's flattery-driven rise.

## Key Quotes

> "全靠一个谄字" - the host's compact diagnosis of Chen Wannian's advancement.

> "如果遇到陈万年这种父亲，不教还好过教错" - the closing education judgment on value transmission.
