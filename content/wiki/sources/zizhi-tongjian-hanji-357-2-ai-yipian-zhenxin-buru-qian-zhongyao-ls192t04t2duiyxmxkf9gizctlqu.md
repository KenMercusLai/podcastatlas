# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》357-2｜唉，一片真心不如钱重要

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the post-[[ZhangQianWesternHan|张骞]] diplomatic breakdown described in Hanji 357-1. Western Regions states still receive nearby [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] envoys more readily than distant Han missions, while [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s gifts, warehouse displays, and lavish reception advertise wealth without solving the problems of military reach, envoy quality, provisioning, or accountability.

The episode then turns to [[BuShiWesternHan|卜式]]. After promotion to Yushi Dafu, he criticizes salt-and-iron state operations and junshu-pingzhun policies for producing poor compulsory goods, added transport burdens, higher prices, and harm to ordinary people. Wudi removes him from the post under the stated reason that he is not skilled in literary expression and appoints [[NiKuanWesternHan|兒宽]] in his place. The durable synthesis is that visible wealth and fiscal intake are not the same as credible diplomacy or public welfare, while the source's motives, policy mechanics, and causal judgments remain popular-history interpretations.

## Key Claims

- Han victories reduce direct Xiongnu pressure on the empire without eliminating Xiongnu influence over Western Regions routes and states.
- Western Regions rulers reportedly provision Xiongnu envoys because coercion is nearby, while Han envoys must purchase supplies and followers because Han punishment appears distant.
- Wudi's lavish gifts and displays of granaries and treasuries make Han abundance visible but also encourage expectations of material extraction.
- The host attributes diplomatic disorder to limited military projection, long and costly routes, profit-seeking mission participants, weak quality control, and Wudi's preference for scale and display.
- Wudi promotes Bu Shi, a patriotic merchant figure, to Yushi Dafu despite [[SimaQian|司马迁]]'s criticism that Bu's earlier loyalty sought office and reward.
- Bu Shi's criticism of salt-and-iron and junshu-pingzhun implementation focuses on poor iron tools, compelled purchase, transport levies, price increases, and household harm.
- Wudi removes Bu Shi from the Yushi Dafu office under the stated pretext that he is poor at literary expression and appoints Ni Kuan as successor.
- The closing [[ZhuoWenjun|卓文君]] and [[SimaXiangru|司马相如]] preview introduces the next episode's question about love, fidelity, and material interest but does not develop new evidence.

## Key Quotes

> “匈奴骑着马就能打过来” - the episode's compact explanation for why geographic proximity makes Xiongnu pressure more credible.

> “不善文辞” - the stated reason given for removing Bu Shi from the Yushi Dafu post.
