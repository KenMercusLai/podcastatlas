# 《资治通鉴·周纪》96丨毛遂自荐（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues from [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-4-lphaverx7gyxntncak-lb5hsrqa|Zhouji 95 part 4]] into 258 BCE, when [[WangLing|王陵]]'s attack on [[Handan|邯郸]] stalls and [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] tries to force [[BaiQi|白起]] back into command. It argues that Bai Qi's refusal is a strategic judgment about [[QinState|秦国]]'s post-[[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] depletion, Zhao's hardened capital defense, and the rising chance of external rescue, not simple cowardice.

The second half turns from Qin's command problem to [[ZhaoState|赵国]]'s rescue problem. Because [[WeiState|魏国]] and [[QiState|齐国]] are unreliable, [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] sends [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] to seek help from [[ChuState|楚国]], and [[MaoSui|毛遂]]'s voluntary inclusion in that mission becomes the source case for [[MaoSuiSelfRecommendation|毛遂自荐]] and "脱颖而出."

## Key Claims
- The episode treats Bai Qi's refusal to replace Wang Ling as a military calculation rooted in campaign timing, not merely illness or personal fear.
- Handan is harder after Changping because Zhao's capital has become a survival line, and mass death has intensified Zhao's shared anger against Qin.
- Qin's victory at Changping does not remove its own losses; Bai Qi reads Qin as badly depleted in soldiers, population, and food.
- Long-range assaults on another state's fortified capital become weaker as distance, terrain, supply, and defensive concentration compound.
- Bai Qi expects Qin to face danger from two sides: Zhao resistance inside the capital and outside pressure from other states that hate Qin.
- The episode frames Bai Qi as a professional commander with a boundary: he can foresee when a campaign is unwinnable and refuses to protect a ruler's wishful tempo.
- The host reads Bai Qi's refusal through reputation and "value rationality": he would rather risk punishment than take a humiliating, low-probability command.
- Qin Zhaoxiang Wang cannot make Bai Qi accept the post, so [[WangHe|王龁]] replaces Wang Ling instead.
- Zhao's path to survival now depends on external aid, but earlier [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] gestures have not produced reliable Wei or Qi rescue.
- Mao Sui's self-recommendation exposes a retainer-patronage problem: a person may remain invisible in a great household until crisis gives him a reason to demand use.

## Key Quotes
> "远绝河山而争人国都" - Bai Qi's strategic objection to the Handan campaign.

> "毛遂自荐于平原君" - the idiom-source formulation attached to Mao Sui's request.

> "脱颖而出" - the later idiom drawn from Mao Sui's awl-in-a-bag reply.
