# AI 时代，我们到底该学什么？｜对谈于红：三种不会过时的能力

## Summary
This [[ShizilukouCrossing|十字路口Crossing]] conversation with [[YuHong|于红]] asks how education should change when AI makes knowledge retrieval, translation, and routine execution cheaper. Drawing on her move from investing to founding [[TuDongdong|兔咚咚]], Yu Hong argues for reallocating learning time toward [[SocialEmotionalLearning|社会情感学习]], self-knowledge, open learning, responsible choice, and a practiced positive orientation. The episode's strongest synthesis is that AI does not remove the need for foundational study; it raises the value of the person-level capacities that decide what to learn, whom to trust, how to revise a judgment, and how to act.

## Key Claims
- AI-era curriculum choices should be individualized by aptitude, goals, and opportunity cost rather than reduced to whether every child should or should not study mathematics, programming, English, or another subject.
- Language remains foundational because it supports thought, writing, world-understanding, and interaction with AI, while AI may reduce the time needed for some advanced translation or routine knowledge tasks.
- [[SocialEmotionalLearning|SEL]] spans self-management, relationships and conflict resolution, and responsible adaptation to the wider world; emotional stability is only one part of that field.
- [[EmotionAcceptanceSignalAction|情绪接纳—信号—行动顺序]] separates accepting a feeling from endorsing every behavior: emotion is first recognized and interpreted, then action is chosen after enough regulation.
- Critical thinking can begin by separating observation from interpretation, generating alternative explanations, seeking evidence, communicating, and updating the original belief.
- [[ReflectiveChoiceFormation|反思式选择能力]] grows through broad experiments, attention to enjoyment and flow, tolerable mistakes, post-event review, and a future-oriented value hierarchy rather than introspection alone.
- Major irreversible decisions should not be made at the peak of fear, anxiety, or anger; emotional information matters, but a more stable state makes broader reasoning possible.
- Investment learning requires [[IntellectualHonestyFeedbackLoop|智识诚实复盘]]: predictions should be recorded and revisited before distant outcomes make self-justifying stories easy.
- Entrepreneurial learning combines intelligence with openness. Yu Hong's team used “I have a hypothesis” to shift discussion from defending identity toward testing evidence.
- The source's three durable capacities are self-knowledge and choice, openness to learning from others, and an actively practiced positive orientation.

## Key Quotes
> “内心丰盈，积极行动。” — Yu Hong's desired outcome for children's long-term development.

> “我有一个假设。” — a discussion norm intended to make beliefs easier to test and revise.

> “以终为始。” — the episode's future-oriented principle for major choices.
