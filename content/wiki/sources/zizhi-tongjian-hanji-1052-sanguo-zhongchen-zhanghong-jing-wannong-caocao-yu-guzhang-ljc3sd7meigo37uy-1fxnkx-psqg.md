# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1052丨三国重臣张纮，竟玩弄曹操于股掌？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate post-[[SunCe|孙策]] succession layer in 200 CE. [[ZhangHongLateHan|张纮]] restrains [[CaoCao|曹操]] from attacking mourning Jiangdong and turns the moment toward recognition of [[SunQuan|孙权]], while [[LadyWuSunJian|吴夫人]], [[ZhangZhaoLateHan|张昭]], and [[DongXiLateHan|董袭]] make the internal support structure visible. The second half introduces [[LuSu|鲁肃]] as a wealthy, organized, Zhou Yu-recommended adviser whose [[TaShangCeBipartiteStrategy|榻上策二分天下]] proposes holding Jiangdong, taking the Yangtze corridor, and pursuing a two-part天下 before later three-kingdom constraints.

## Key Claims
- Zhang Hong is presented as a Sun-family adviser and court-facing mediator, not as a Cao-side defector.
- Cao Cao's proposed attack during Sun Quan's mourning is rejected through a reputation and enemy-creation argument, making recognition a safer move than opportunistic war.
- Lady Wu, Zhang Hong, Zhang Zhao, and Dong Xi make Sun Quan's under-19 succession look socially and militarily supportable.
- Sun Quan keeps personnel judgment of his own by trusting Zhang Hong despite court-appointment concerns and by valuing Lu Su despite Zhang Zhao's criticism.
- Lu Su's strategic counsel accepts that the Han house cannot be revived and Cao Cao cannot be quickly destroyed, then recommends a Jiangdong-first Yangtze expansion sequence against [[HuangZu|黄祖]] and [[LiuBiao|刘表]].
- The host frames Lu Su's plan as an earlier two-part天下 strategy that later events around Red Cliffs and [[ThreeKingdomsPeriod|三国时期]] qualify toward a three-part order.

## Key Quotes
> "何必呢" - Zhang Hong's practical challenge to Cao Cao's proposed attack during Sun Quan's mourning.

> "汉室不可复兴" - Lu Su's strategic premise for refusing a simple Han-restoration frame.

> "竟玩弄曹操于股掌" - the title's dramatic framing of Zhang Hong's mediation.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is recorded. The source backfills the missing Hanji 1052 layer between the Sun Ce deathbed transition and Hanji 1053's internal consolidation sequence.
- The title's "玩弄曹操" is treated as host framing for strategic mediation, not as proof that Zhang Hong defected to Cao Cao or literally controlled Cao's decisions.
- Lu Su's two-part天下 strategy is preserved as a source-scoped early plan and contrasted with later three-kingdom outcomes rather than treated as a final prediction.
