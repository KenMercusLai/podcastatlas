# 《资治通鉴·周纪》02｜将欲败之 必姑辅之

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode explains the preconditions of the [[BattleOfJinyang|Battle of Jinyang]] through contrasting succession and risk choices. [[ZhiGuo|智果]] warns that [[ZhiYao|智瑶]]'s ability without benevolence will endanger the Zhi house, while [[ZhaoJianzi|赵简子]] tests his sons and has [[YinDuo|尹铎]] turn [[Jinyang|晋阳]] into a low-burden fallback base for [[ZhaoXiangzi|赵无恤]]. When Zhi Yao later humiliates Han and demands land, [[DuanGuiWarringStates|段规]] and [[RenZhangWarringStates|任章]] advise Han and Wei to concede temporarily so his greed, confidence, and enemy-making will expand together.

## Key Claims
- Zhi Guo judges Zhi Yao as highly capable in appearance, skill, speech, and decisiveness but fatally deficient in benevolence; Zhi Shen rejects the warning, and Zhi Guo separates his branch from the clan.
- Zhao Jianzi tests Zhao Bolu and Zhao Wuxu with written instructions; Zhao Wuxu can still recite and produce his tablet three years later, although the host treats the detail as evidence of calculated preparation rather than simple virtue.
- Yin Duo asks whether Jinyang should be treated as a revenue source or a refuge; Zhao Jianzi chooses refuge, after which Yin Duo reduces burdens and strengthens defenses.
- Zhao Jianzi's instruction to flee to Jinyang in a future crisis makes resilience a pre-crisis investment in people, governance, and fortification rather than an improvised wartime response.
- After becoming Zhi leader, Zhi Yao humiliates Han Hu and Duan Gui at a banquet; Zhi Guo warns that visible insult can accumulate hidden retaliation even when the injured side appears weak.
- Duan Gui advises Han Hu to surrender a large city because refusal invites immediate attack, while concession encourages Zhi Yao to repeat the demand elsewhere and manufacture a broader conflict.
- Ren Zhang gives Wei Ju the parallel argument: unjustified gains will make Zhi Yao more arrogant and careless while giving the coerced houses a common interest.
- The title maxim, “将欲败之，必姑辅之,” describes a deliberate concession that helps an aggressor overextend; it is not unconditional appeasement because the conceding side expects later coalition and counterattack opportunities.
- Zhi Yao had used a related gift-first tactic against Qiu You: a large bell induced road construction through mountain terrain, after which Zhi troops used the new route to conquer the state.
- Wei avoids a similar trap after Nan Wenzi interprets unsolicited valuables and horses as a possible precursor to attack and strengthens precautions.

## Key Quotes
> "将欲败之，必姑辅之" - the episode's formula for helping an overconfident opponent deepen the conditions of his own defeat.

> "大人物能在小事上谨慎，才可以避免大的忧患" - the host's rendering of Zhi Guo's warning about small humiliations and hidden resentment.
