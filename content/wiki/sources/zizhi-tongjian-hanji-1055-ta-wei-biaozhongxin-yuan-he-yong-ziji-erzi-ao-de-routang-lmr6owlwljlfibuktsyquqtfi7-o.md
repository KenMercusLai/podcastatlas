# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1055丨他为表忠心，愿喝用自己儿子熬的肉汤？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows Jian'an 6-7 branches in [[YizhouLateHan|益州]], [[Hanzhong|汉中]], and [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s memory politics. [[ChengJiLateHan|程畿]] refuses [[PangXiLateHan|庞羲]]'s self-protective mobilization order, [[ZhangLuFivePecks|张鲁]] consolidates a [[WudoumiDao|五斗米道]] order in Hanzhong, [[YanPuLateHan|阎圃]] warns Zhang Lu not to claim kingship, and Cao Cao sacrifices to [[QiaoXuanLateHan|桥玄]] as an elder who recognized him early. The episode's strongest synthesis is that late-Han autonomy still depends on trust, office-duty language, and restraint over titles.

## Key Claims
- After [[ZhaoWeiLateHan|赵韪]]'s revolt is suppressed, [[PangXiLateHan|庞羲]] fears [[LiuZhang|刘璋]]'s suspicion and tries to have [[ChengJiLateHan|程畿]] gather Cong forces, but Cheng Ji refuses on loyal-duty grounds.
- Cheng Ji's refusal turns personal danger into an office-duty test: suspicion should be answered through explanation and loyal conduct, not through a second-hearted armed move.
- Liu Zhang's suspicion of Pang Xi grows from Pang's force, Baxi/local ties, old favor to the Liu family, and the marriage between Pang's daughter and Liu Zhang's eldest son [[LiuXunYizhouHeirLateHan|刘循]].
- [[ZhangLuFivePecks|张鲁]] governs Hanzhong through a religious-administrative order tied to confession, healing, local "jijiu" officers, and limited submission to the Han court.
- [[YanPuLateHan|阎圃]] treats Zhang Lu's proposed Hanning kingship as unnecessary exposure: Zhang Lu has enough territorial substance to wait, preserve autonomy, or support the emperor without claiming a dangerous title.
- Cao Cao's sacrifice to [[QiaoXuanLateHan|桥玄]] turns Qiao Xuan's early recognition into remembered political debt and reputation lineage.

## Key Quotes
> "谗说殄行，古人所戒." - Cheng Ji frames suspicion as a reason for loyal explanation rather than armed self-protection.

> "乐羊食子，非无父子之恩，大义然也." - Cheng Ji uses the Le Yang precedent to say public duty can override private attachment.
