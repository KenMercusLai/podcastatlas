# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》966丨没有野心，不懂权谋，就别当领导

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate Chang'an crisis before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-967-ta-canyu-mousha-dongzhuo-jingneng-taoguo-sishen-weilie-lv24biyswj9ljdld5htynhauhph3|Hanji 967]]. It moves from a [[JinPingMei|《金瓶梅》]] opening about beauty, capital, and self-protection into the 192 CE collapse after [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s death: [[JiaXu|贾诩]]'s advice sends [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and allied remnant commanders toward [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]], while [[WangYunLateHan|王允]] and [[SongYiLateHan|宋翼]] fail to convert loyalty into crisis judgment. The episode's core contribution is the negative counterpart to Hanji 967's survival craft: public virtue, formal obedience, and righteous language can become fatal when actors misread coercive power, title capture, and the real options still available.

## Key Claims

- The opening treats beauty as a form of capital that can become dangerous without judgment, using Pan Jinlian and Meng Yulou from [[JinPingMei|《金瓶梅》]] as a thematic bridge into political self-protection.
- In 192 CE, [[JiaXu|贾诩]] advises [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and other [[DongZhuo|董卓]] remnant commanders not to disband but to attack [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]], creating the concrete decision point later analyzed as [[ExecutorCharacterAdviceRisk]].
- [[WangYunLateHan|王允]] tries to use [[HuWencaiLateHan|胡文才]] and [[YangZhengxiuLateHan|杨整修]] as Liangzhou influence channels, but his arrogant reception pushes them toward urging Li Jue onward.
- Li Jue's force expands from several thousand into a much larger siege force and combines with [[FanChouLateHan|樊稠]], [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]], and other Dong Zhuo remnants outside Chang'an.
- A mutiny by Shu soldiers inside [[LyuBu|吕布]]'s force opens the city; Lü Bu cannot stop the plunder and escapes with several hundred riders, carrying Dong Zhuo's head.
- Wang Yun refuses Lü Bu's invitation to flee because he will not abandon the young [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], preserving moral loyalty while remaining exposed to the victorious remnant commanders.
- Li Jue and Guo Si use grievance language, office grants, amnesty, and forced appearances to turn captured court form into coercive leverage over Wang Yun and his allies.
- [[HuangWanLateHan|黄琬]], who had joined Wang Yun's anti-Dong-Zhuo planning, is arrested and executed after the remnant commanders gain titles.
- [[WangHongLateHan|王宏]] correctly sees that he and [[SongYiLateHan|宋翼]] remain Wang Yun's outside military shield, while Song Yi treats the imperial summons as unavoidably binding even under captured-court conditions.
- The source's strongest decision lesson is [[RigidObedienceCrisisFailure]]: formal obedience to an edict can become political failure when the edict is issued under enemy coercion and entering the court removes the last deterrent.
- [[ZhaoJianLateHan|赵戬]]'s burial of Wang Yun after others fear collecting the body preserves a smaller loyalty case distinct from Wang Yun's failed crisis management.

## Key Quotes

> "美色如刃." - the host's opening bridge from beauty as capital to danger without judgment.

> "皇帝召命不能抗命." - Song Yi's refusal to treat the summons as a trap under Li-Guo coercion.
