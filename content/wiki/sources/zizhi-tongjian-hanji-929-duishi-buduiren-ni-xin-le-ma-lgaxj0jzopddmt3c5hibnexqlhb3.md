# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》929丨对事不对人，你信了吗？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills 184 CE immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-930-ruhe-pochu-tanxin-he-buganxin-lkyysh5jbgxwjq8ayrfar4gsjdcd|Hanji 930]], moving from [[ZhangXiuFivePecks|张修]]'s early Five-Pecks-style rising through [[HuangfuSongLateHan|皇甫嵩]]'s destruction of the main [[YellowTurbansLateHan|黄巾军]] forces. It then turns to [[LiXieLateHan|李燮]]'s unpopular judgment of [[LiuXuAnpingPrince|安平王刘续]] and to [[GaiXunLateHan|盖勋]], whose choices around [[SuZhengheLateHan|苏正和]] and [[ZuoChangLateHan|左昌]] become the episode's core example of [[PublicDutyOverPrivateGrievance|对事不对人式公义]].

The source's main contribution is a three-part late-Han failure-and-duty pattern: religiously framed mobilization and Yellow Turban remnants outlast battlefield victories, capable commanders can still be punished or undermined by personnel politics, and public office becomes meaningful only when private resentment does not decide public action.

## Key Claims

- [[ZhangXiuFivePecks|张修]] in [[BaCommanderyLateHan|巴郡]] uses a healing practice resembling [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]]'s religious mobilization and receives five pecks of rice from patients' families, producing the "五斗米师" label before his autumn revolt.
- [[HuangfuSongLateHan|皇甫嵩]] defeats the [[YellowTurbansLateHan|黄巾军]] at Cangting and captures the Yellow Turban general Bo Si, while [[DongZhuo|董卓]] fails against Zhang Jiao after replacing [[LuZhiLateHan|卢植]].
- The episode reads Dong Zhuo's failure as an early [[BattlefieldCommanderReplacementRisk|阵前换将风险]] case: a rough replacement commander cannot easily inherit Lu Zhi's relationship with the existing troops.
- [[LiXieLateHan|李燮]] opposes restoring [[LiuXuAnpingPrince|刘续]] because Liu Xu had been captured by Yellow Turbans and failed to uphold his princely role; the court punishes Li Xie before Liu Xu's later execution vindicates the warning.
- The Luoyang saying about "the father unwilling to establish an emperor and the son unwilling to establish a king" links Li Xie to [[LiGuLateHan|李固]], who had once preferred [[LiuSuanLateHan|刘蒜]] over the later Emperor Huan.
- At Guangzong, Huangfu Song studies [[ZhangLiangYellowTurban|张梁]]'s camp, waits for lax discipline, and launches a dawn surprise attack that kills Zhang Liang and destroys a large Yellow Turban force.
- Zhang Jiao has already died of illness, but the court still exhumes and decapitates his corpse; in November Huangfu Song kills [[ZhangBaoYellowTurban|张宝]] at Quyang and breaks the main Yellow Turban uprising.
- The episode states that the main uprising is basically suppressed after Quyang, while remnants continue until [[CaoCao|曹操]] finally settles them in 192 CE.
- Huangfu Song is rewarded as Left Chariot-and-Cavalry General, Jizhou shepherd, and Huaili marquis; the host treats his practice of resting and eating after ordinary soldiers as [[MilitaryMoraleThroughSharedHardship|同甘共苦式士气]].
- In the western branch, [[XianlingQiang|先零羌]] and related groups elect [[BeigongBoyuLateHan|北宫伯玉]] and [[LiWenhouLateHan|李文侯]], kill [[LingZhengLateHan|伶征]], and coerce [[BianZhangLateHan|边章]] and [[HanSui|韩遂]] into managing military affairs.
- [[SuZhengheLateHan|苏正和]] prosecutes corruption in the Wuwei commandery field, but [[LiangHuLateHan|梁鹄]] fears implication and considers killing him; [[GaiXunLateHan|盖勋]] blocks the murder despite disliking Su.
- Gai Xun later refuses Su Zhenghe's personal thanks because his intervention was not private friendship, but a protection of Liang Hu's office and public duty.
- [[ZuoChangLateHan|左昌]] steals military grain and retaliates when Gai Xun warns him, sending Gai Xun into a dangerous forward defense assignment that fails to kill him because Gai wins repeated victories.
- When [[ChenYiJinchengLateHan|金城太守陈懿]] is killed and Zuo Chang is besieged at Ji County, Gai Xun still pushes [[XinZengLateHan|辛曾]] to rescue the superior who had tried to ruin him, invoking [[SimaRangju|司马穰苴]] and military discipline.

## Key Quotes

> "真金不怕火炼" - opening frame for pressure as a test of real quality.

> "人家可不是为了苏正和，而是为了梁史君" - the host's formulation of Gai Xun's refusal to turn public judgment into personal favor.

> "我对苏正和的仇恨跟之前一模一样" - Gai Xun's stance as presented by the host: private dislike remains, but it does not govern official action.
