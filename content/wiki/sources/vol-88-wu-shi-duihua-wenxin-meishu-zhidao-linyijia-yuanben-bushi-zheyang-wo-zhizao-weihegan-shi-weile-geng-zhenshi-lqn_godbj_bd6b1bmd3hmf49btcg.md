# VOL.88无·事｜对话《问心》美术指导：林逸家原本不是这样 我制造违和感是为了更真实

## Summary
This [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode has host [[Atang|阿汤]] interview [[WangJingProductionDesigner|王靖]], production designer of [[AskTheHeartTVDrama|《问心》]], about constructing the fictional Dongli Hospital and using domestic space to express character. Its central synthesis joins [[LayeredInstitutionalRealism]] with [[CharacterSpaceProductionDesign]]: contemporary realism comes not from polished visual uniformity or documentary equivalence, but from historically accumulated buildings, operational patches, crowding, connected movement, and household arrangements chosen for narrative and lived plausibility.

## Key Claims
- Most hospital scenes were built on soundstages in Qingdao because roughly 80 percent of the drama takes place in medical settings and filming during the pandemic could not occupy working hospital resources.
- The fictional hospital combines an older Republican-era building, an expansion associated with the 1980s or 1990s, and a newer modern building; connected stairs, bridges, corridors, offices, wards, and intensive-care spaces create scale and support long-take movement.
- Hospital credibility depends on accumulated use as well as original architecture: formal visual identity and wayfinding coexist with larger accessibility-oriented signs and locally printed A4 notices created by staff.
- Clinicians from several Qingdao hospitals reviewed the completed sets and commented on noticeboards, rosters, equipment carts, floor markings, wayfinding, and intensive-care detail, but this review did not turn the drama into a documentary or clinical teaching resource.
- Lin Yi's compact home assigns the bedroom to the child, places his medically vulnerable brother at the center of the living room, and puts Lin Yi on the balcony where he can watch him; spatial allocation externalizes care, constraint, and mortality awareness.
- Fang Xiaoran's warm home and Zhou Xiaofeng's colder apartment use tone, furniture, and available seating to show family inclusion and exclusion; the two comfortable chairs in Fang's home deliberately leave no established place for Zhou.
- Wang Jing prioritizes functional and biographical plausibility over showroom consistency: ordinary homes acquire mismatched objects over time, and visual harmony can work against character and story.

## Key Quotes
> “美术团队是在设计人物，不是在做装修。” - Wang Jing's distinction between narrative production design and interior decoration.

> “我会选择生活上的合理，舍弃所谓审美上的统一。” - his rule for preferring lived plausibility to visual consistency.
