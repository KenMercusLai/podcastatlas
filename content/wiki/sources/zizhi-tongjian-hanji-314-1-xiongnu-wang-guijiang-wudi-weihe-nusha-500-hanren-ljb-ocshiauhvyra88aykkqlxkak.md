# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》314-1｜匈奴王归降，武帝为何怒杀500汉人

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[HunyeKingWesternHan|浑邪王]]'s surrender to [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] through its domestic reception costs. Wudi seeks vehicles and borrowed horses to receive the surrendered population with credible imperial ceremony, but popular horse concealment leads him to threaten the Chang'an magistrate, while [[JiAnWesternHan|汲黯]] argues that a local official should not die for a burden the court has imposed.

The second dispute concerns more than five hundred Han merchants condemned to death after trading with the surrendered Xiongnu in Chang'an under rules against taking money or selling weapons beyond the frontier. Ji An contrasts literal punitive enforcement with the war deaths, treasury expenditure, civilian labor, and rewards already devoted to the surrender, grounding [[SurrenderReceptionDomesticBurden|归降接纳的内外成本冲突]] in a concrete disagreement over diplomatic credibility, public burden, and contextual justice.

## Key Claims

- Hunye King brings a large surrendered population into Han, and Wudi wants two ten-thousand vehicles for reception and transport.
- After prolonged war leaves the treasury strained, the court seeks horses from the population; some households hide their animals rather than lend them.
- Wudi treats the shortage as a threat to imperial sincerity and prestige and considers executing the Chang'an magistrate, but Ji An offers himself instead and the magistrate survives.
- Ji An argues that a surrendered subordinate should not be honored by exhausting state resources and compelling Han civilians to serve the newcomers.
- The episode says Han rules prohibited selling weapons to foreigners at the frontier or carrying money beyond it; more than five hundred merchants who traded with the surrendered Xiongnu in Chang'an were condemned to death.
- Ji An argues that ordinary merchants could not reasonably understand local Chang'an trade as illicit export beyond the frontier and that rigid application of the rule sacrifices the people the state should protect.
- Wudi does not answer the substance of the second remonstrance, later calls Ji An's words nonsense, and Ji is removed from office after a minor offense and an amnesty.

## Key Quotes

> “知善不举，闻恶无言。” - the episode's formula for why Ji An treats silence in the face of wrongdoing as culpable.

> “护其叶而伤其枝。” - Ji An's attributed image for protecting a narrow rule while harming the state's own people.
