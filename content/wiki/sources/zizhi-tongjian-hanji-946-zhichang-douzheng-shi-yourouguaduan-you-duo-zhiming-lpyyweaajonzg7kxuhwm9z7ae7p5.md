# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》946丨职场斗争时优柔寡断，有多致命？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate pre-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-947-dongzhuo-shoufu-lvbu-kaiqi-chengba-zhilu-lvr-24l6j8f6zvpfcmqgknmgywa1|Hanji 947]] collapse of the late-Han court. It follows [[HeJin|何进]]'s failed attempt to persuade [[EmpressDowagerHe|何太后]] to eliminate the [[TenAttendantsLateHan|十常侍]], [[ZhangRangLateHan|张让]] and [[DuanGuiLateHan|段珪]]'s counterattack, the killing of He Jin, and the uncontrolled retaliation by [[YuanShao|袁绍]], [[YuanShu|袁术]], [[WuKuangLateHan|吴匡]], and others. The distinct contribution is [[ZeroSumConflictHesitationRisk|死斗局优柔寡断风险]]: in a lethal palace struggle, He Jin's delay and hope for a mild settlement let the eunuch side use palace access, forged orders, and faster violence before [[DongZhuo|董卓]] inherits the vacuum.

## Key Claims

- In August 189 CE, He Jin enters the Luoyang palace to ask Empress Dowager He to kill all regular palace attendants, but the source frames his move as late and still softened by hope for a less direct solution.
- Zhang Rang, Duan Gui, and allied eunuchs use palace familiarity and eavesdropping to learn He Jin's intent, then lure him deeper inside under a claimed dowager order.
- [[QuMuLateHan|渠穆]], a Zhang Rang-side follower with prior palace-coup experience from the [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] and [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] suppression, cuts off He Jin's head.
- Zhang Rang and Duan Gui then forge appointment orders for [[FanLingLateHan|樊陵]] as sili xiaowei and [[XuXiangLateHan|许相]] as Henan yin, overturning the appointments He Jin had arranged for Yuan Shao and [[WangYunLateHan|王允]].
- When clerks demand that the great general appear to explain the edict, the eunuch side throws out He Jin's head and claims he was killed for rebellion.
- Wu Kuang, [[ZhangZhangLateHan|张璋]], Yuan Shu, and palace troops attack the gates; Yuan Shu burns the South Palace Qing-suo Gate, making court control visibly collapse.
- The episode applies the same hesitation critique to Yuan Shao, describing him through the traditional "outwardly fierce but timid" judgment while also showing him moving quickly once He Jin is dead.
- Zhang Rang and Duan Gui seize Empress Dowager He, Liu Bian, later [[LiuBianHongnongWang|弘农王]], and Liu Xie, later [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], and flee by palace passageways.
- [[LuZhiLateHan|卢植]] shouts at Duan Gui from below the gallery and makes him release Empress Dowager He, so the queen mother escapes before the imperial boys are carried farther away.
- Yuan Shao and [[YuanWeiLateHan|袁隗]] use a false edict to summon and kill Fan Ling and Xu Xiang, while Yuan Shao and He Miao first capture and kill [[ZhaoZhongLateHan|赵忠]] near Zhuque Gate.
- Wu Kuang blames [[HeMiaoLateHan|何苗]] for He Jin's death, mobilizes soldiers' resentment, and kills him with [[DongMinLateHan|董旻]], showing that anti-eunuch revenge quickly spreads into He-family infighting.
- Yuan Shao closes the North Palace gates and kills eunuchs indiscriminately; the source says more than two thousand people die and some beardless non-eunuchs are killed by mistake.
- Zhang Rang, Duan Gui, Liu Bian, Liu Xie, and a small party flee on foot toward [[Xiaopingjin|小平津]] without the six imperial seals or a normal court escort.
- [[MinGongLateHan|闵贡]] catches up at the Yellow River, kills several eunuchs after rebuking Zhang Rang, and Zhang Rang plus remaining eunuchs jump into the river.
- The source ends by making the double collapse causal for Hanji 947: He Jin and the eunuchs destroy each other, leaving the opening that Dong Zhuo will exploit.
