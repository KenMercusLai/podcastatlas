# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》927丨做生意的最高境界是什么？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 184 CE late-Han crisis immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-928-zhichang-buke-mingshuo-song-dui-li-banshi-geng-shunli-lgup21lsa-u1ykaiyabcko2vhp0o|Hanji 928]]. It opens with [[MerchantComposureDiscipline|商人定气功夫]], the host's source-scoped claim that business requires both gentlemanly trust and hard-headed decisiveness, then turns to [[FuXie|傅燮]]'s warning that [[YellowTurbansLateHan|黄巾军]] disorder has roots inside [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s eunuch-dominated court.

The later branch moves from [[ZhangManchengLateHan|张曼成]]'s [[NanyangCommandery|南阳郡]] campaign and [[QinJieLateHan|秦颉]]'s killing of him to [[JiaCongLateHan|贾从]] in [[JiaozhiCommandery|交趾郡]]. Jia Cong's success creates [[RootCausePacification|查因安抚式治理]]: he investigates why people rebelled, relieves burdens, reassures refugees, removes violent leaders, and appoints clean local officials.

## Key Claims

- The business opening is source-scoped: the host says good business needs a mix of gentlemanly credibility and hard realist judgment, not emotional bondage or naive kindness.
- In 184 CE, Fu Xie submits a memorial while [[ZhuJunLateHan|朱儁]] is attacking Yellow Turban forces; the transcript renders him as "傅解", but this ingest normalizes the actor to [[FuXie|傅燮]] because the role and later Zhao Zhong branch align with the existing page.
- Fu Xie argues that the visible rebellion is not only a local bandit problem. He says disaster begins inside the court because the ruler is too indulgent toward eunuchs and fails to use loyal ministers.
- The memorial frames loyal and wicked officials as unable to coexist, invokes slander examples such as Zeng Shen being falsely accused and "three people make a tiger", and warns that loyal officials may be destroyed like Bai Qi.
- [[ZhaoZhongLateHan|赵忠]] sees the memorial, resents Fu Xie, and later helps prevent him from receiving an enfeoffment despite Yellow Turban merit.
- In [[NanyangCommandery|南阳郡]], Zhang Mancheng kills administrator Chu Gong, occupies the commandery field for more than 100 days, and is killed in June after Qin Jie attacks him.
- Jiaozhi is introduced as a southern commandery in the region of today's northern Vietnam and the Red River valley, with pearls making local extraction especially tempting to previous inspectors.
- The Jiaozhi rebellion arises after inspectors use the post to enrich themselves and then seek transfer; rebels capture the inspector and the Hepu administrator Lai Da and choose a leader styled Zhu Tian General.
- Jia Cong, previously Jing county magistrate and from [[DongCommandery|东郡]], is selected as Jiaozhi inspector after the Three Offices discuss the crisis.
- Jia Cong investigates the causes of unrest, identifies heavy taxation and blocked complaint channels, issues a reassurance proclamation, calls back refugees and hungry people, exempts corvee labor, kills major violent leaders, and installs clean capable acting county heads.
- Within a year Jiaozhi is pacified, and the source presents local praise for clean official conduct as evidence that root-cause repair worked better than indiscriminate suppression.

## Key Quotes

> "一半得是君子，一半得是小人" - the host's compressed business-character frame.

> "情为我所用" - the host's warning against being ruled by emotional attachment.

> "气定则心定，心定则事缘" - the source's business-composure formula.

> "曾参杀人" and "三人成虎" - slander examples Fu Xie invokes to warn that repeated falsehood can become politically lethal.
