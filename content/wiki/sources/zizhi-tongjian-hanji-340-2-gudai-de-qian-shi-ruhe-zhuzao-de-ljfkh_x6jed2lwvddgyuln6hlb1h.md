# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》340-2｜古代的钱是如何铸造的？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode moves from the destruction and rebuilding of [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s dew-collection architecture to court appointments, disaster, and monetary reform. It backfills [[ZhaoZhouWesternHan|赵周]]'s appointment as chancellor, [[ShiQingWesternHan|石庆]]'s appointment as yushi dafu, and [[SangHongyangWesternHan|桑弘羊]]'s rise in the agricultural-finance apparatus.

Its durable contribution is [[CentralizedWuzhuCoinage|五铢钱中央统一铸造]]: the episode says the court ends commandery and kingdom minting, concentrates old coin and copper at the center, divides mint work among the Shanglin Three Offices, and makes weight-matched five-zhu coins the national standard. The claimed fiscal, anti-counterfeit, and long-run stability effects remain the episode's explanation rather than an independently audited monetary history.

## Key Claims

- A fire destroys 柏梁台 and its仙人承露盘, but Wudi continues the [[ImmortalityQuestPolitics|求仙政治]] program by building 建章宫, 神明台, and another承露盘.
- Zhao Zhou is made chancellor after receiving a marquis title; the episode links that eligibility to the remembered loyalty and death of his father 赵夷吾 during the Rebellion of the Seven States.
- Shi Qing is appointed yushi dafu while heavy snow, flooding, and Guandong famine place court staffing beside material distress.
- [[KongJinWesternHan|孔仅]] is promoted to da nong ling and Sang Hongyang to da nong cheng after merchant expertise is recruited into salt-and-iron and fiscal administration.
- The court abolishes local princely and commandery minting, orders old coins destroyed, and requires their copper to be delivered to the center.
- The Shanglin Three Offices divide casting, mould or engraving work, and copper inspection, while the resulting five-zhu coin is presented as matching nominal weight with actual weight.
- The episode attributes reform durability to centralized copper supply, common standards, and a higher technical cost of counterfeiting, then links monetary order to fiscal recovery and further imperial expansion.

## Key Quotes

> “上林三官铸造的五铢钱” - the episode's name for the centrally standardized coin.

> “价格标准和重量标准一致” - its compact explanation of the coin's claimed credibility.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with existing wiki content was found. The source supplies earlier career stages for Zhao Zhou, Shi Qing, and Sang Hongyang before their later pages' events.
- Transcript forms 百良台, 仙人成鹿盘, 赵州, 赵怡武, 孔锦, 桑红阳, 大农城, and 五株钱 are normalized where the referent is clear to 柏梁台, 仙人承露盘, 赵周, 赵夷吾, 孔仅, 桑弘羊, 大农丞, and 五铢钱. The transcript's楚王刘武 is not promoted because the Seven States sequence appears to conflate names.
- The note pairs “元朔三年” with 120 BCE, which would ordinarily correspond to 元狩三年; the exact year and office sequence therefore remain source-scoped.
- The precise functions of the three mint offices, complete elimination of private minting, direct fiscal and macroeconomic effects, “first complete centralization” label, and seven-hundred-year uninterrupted circulation claim are not independently established by this episode.
