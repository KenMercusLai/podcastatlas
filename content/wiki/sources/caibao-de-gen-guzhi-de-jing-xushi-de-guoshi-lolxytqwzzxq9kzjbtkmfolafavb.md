# 财报的根 + 估值的茎 = 叙事的果实

## Summary
This [[Mianji|面基]] episode with [[ZouPeixuan|邹佩轩]] presents [[ChuantouXushi|《穿透叙事》]] as the third part of a financial-report, valuation, and narrative investment trilogy. It reframes "narrative" as a falsifiable, quantifiable market consensus about terminal business value, using [[DCFValuationAsNarrative|DCF as narrative]], [[TerminalValueNarrative]], and reverse valuation to explain why stock returns depend on narrative change rather than raw growth speed. The A-share section adds [[AShareAuctionPricing|A-share auction pricing]] and [[ProsperityInvestingTrap|prosperity-investing traps]]: when short selling is constrained, diffuse expectations and abundant liquidity can let optimistic marginal capital dominate prices.

## Key Claims
- [[ZouPeixuan|邹佩轩]] defines investment narrative as a logical, falsifiable, and quantifiable consensus rather than a loose story.
- [[DCFValuationAsNarrative]] treats future cash-flow discounting as the first-principles frame behind absolute valuation and simplified relative multiples.
- [[TerminalValueNarrative]] argues that terminal profit ceiling and discount rate usually matter more to valuation than the speed of reaching that ceiling.
- Reverse DCF can infer the terminal performance, ceiling, or discount-rate assumptions already implied by the current stock price.
- [[ProsperityInvestingTrap]] appears when investors focus on earnings growth rate, marginal acceleration, or earnings beats while missing a downward revision to terminal space.
- [[AShareAuctionPricing]] explains why A-share stocks with unclear ceilings and dispersed expectations can rise more under abundant liquidity when pessimistic views cannot be expressed through short selling.
- [[CapitalizedResearchProcess]] frames analyst work as a reusable asset: prior case reviews, logic chains, and mistakes should compound instead of being discarded after one report.

## Key Quotes
> "决定股价高度的是叙事" - Zou's compact statement of the level-setting claim.

> "叙事不是故事" - boundary between market consensus and unfalsifiable storytelling.

> "所谓叙事就是终局" - the source's terminal-value reduction.

> "财报是基本功，估值是从财报到叙事的衔接" - trilogy relationship among reports, valuation, and narrative.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction found.
- The source qualifies simple [[EarningsGrowthAcceleration]] or narrow prosperity-investing explanations by arguing that faster earnings growth can coexist with lower valuation if terminal space is revised downward.
- It qualifies efficient-market language by distinguishing long-term weighing around terminal value from short-term voting or auction-like pricing under A-share short-sale constraints.
