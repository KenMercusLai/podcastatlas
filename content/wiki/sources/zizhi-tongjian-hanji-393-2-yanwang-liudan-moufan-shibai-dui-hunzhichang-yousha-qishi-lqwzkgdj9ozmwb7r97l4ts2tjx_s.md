# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》393-2｜燕王刘旦谋反失败，对混职场有啥启示？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues directly from [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-393-1-hanwudi-jiabeng-hou-liang-erzi-shi-zenme-zheng-huangquan-de-loevhdil8uigaqd6izp82tykwx-p|Hanji 393-1]] by following [[LiuDanYanKingWesternHan|燕王刘旦]] from succession grievance into his first exposed rebellion. Liu Dan joins [[LiuZeQiRoyalWesternHan|刘泽]] and Liu Chang, claims false authorization from [[HanWudi|汉武帝]], attacks [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|汉昭帝刘弗陵]]'s legitimacy, gathers weapons and trains forces, and kills Han Yi and fourteen other remonstrators.

The durable synthesis is that desire-capacity mismatch becomes organizational failure when a leader removes correction while widening a secret plan. Liu Ze intends to raise troops from Linzi and kill [[JunBuyiWesternHan|隽不疑]], but Pinghou Liu Cheng informs; Jun arrests Liu Ze's group before the attack and reports the case to Han Zhao and [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]]. Investigation identifies Liu Dan as the principal, yet Han Zhao exempts his elder brother and executes Liu Ze and the other participants. The mercy prevents immediate destruction but does not correct Liu Dan's belief that the throne belongs to him, leaving the grievance that later feeds the Hanji 399-400 conspiracy.

## Key Claims

- Liu Dan conspires with Liu Chang, son of the Zhongshan Ai king, and Liu Ze, grandson of the Qi Xiao king, to rebel against the Han court.
- The group invents an edict in which Han Wudi supposedly authorizes Liu Dan to control his kingdom's appointments and reorganize its troops, turning fabricated legitimacy into a claimed mobilization right.
- Cheng Zhen encourages action by claiming even Yan women would follow Liu Dan; Liu Dan and Liu Ze also circulate documents denying that Han Zhao is Han Wudi's son.
- Liu Ze plans to raise troops at Linzi and kill Qingzhou inspector Jun Buyi, while Liu Dan collects copper and iron for weapons and uses large hunts as military training.
- Liu Dan kills Han Yi and fourteen other men who repeatedly advise him not to rebel, removing an internal correction channel before the plan is tested.
- Pinghou Liu Cheng's report lets Jun Buyi arrest Liu Ze's group before the attack; Jun then reports the rebellion to Han Zhao and Huo Guang.
- Investigation identifies Liu Dan as the principal, but Han Zhao orders that his elder brother not be implicated and has Liu Ze and the other participants executed; Jun Buyi is later appointed Jingzhao Yin.

## Key Quotes

> "能力、位置和欲望不匹配" - the episode's practical frame for Liu Dan's political self-misjudgment.

> "保密工作做得一塌糊涂" - the episode's explanation for why the plan is stopped before Liu Ze can act.
