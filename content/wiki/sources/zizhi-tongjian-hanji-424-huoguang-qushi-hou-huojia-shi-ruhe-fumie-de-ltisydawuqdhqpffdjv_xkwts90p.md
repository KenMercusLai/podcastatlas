# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》424｜霍光去世后，霍家是如何覆灭的？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the event sequence before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-425-huoguang-sihou-canzao-miemen-simaguang-weisha-fenfenbuping-lmz7bdfy8anhkuq24l-f2ulkolbu|Hanji 425]]'s Sima Guang judgment on the [[HuoFamilyPurgeAfterHuoGuang|Huo family purge]]. It follows the Huo household after [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]]'s death: [[HuoXianWesternHan|霍显]]'s private scandal and funerary overreach, palace access, attempted harm to crown prince [[HanYuandi|刘奭]], the Huo plan to use [[ShangguanTaihouWesternHan|上官太后]]'s banquet to kill ministers and depose [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]], and the eventual exposure, suicides, executions, and collective punishment.

The durable synthesis is that Huo-family collapse is not presented as a single unlucky plot. The episode ties it to accumulated court overreach, failure of household discipline, Huo Guang's earlier concealment of Empress Xu's poisoning, Xuan's long fear of overmighty regent power, and the political difference between preventing disaster before it spreads and rewarding those who merely extinguish it afterward.

## Key Claims

- Huo Guang's death removes the stabilizing figure whose merit had protected a household already too close to imperial authority.
- Huo Xian is framed as a central accelerant: adultery with Feng Zidu, expanded funerary display, casual palace access, and plans against Liu Shi all show private desire turning into public-political danger.
- The Huo conspiracy plan targets the chancellor and Xu Guanghan through an empress-dowager banquet, then seeks to depose Xuan and install Huo Yu.
- Xuan's cleanup is swift and massive: Huo Yun, Huo Shan, and Fan Mingyou kill themselves; Huo Yu, Huo Xian, Deng Guanghan, and wider Huo-linked families are punished; Huo Chengjun is deposed.
- Xu Fu's earlier warnings become a prevention-versus-rescue lesson through the "曲突徙薪" story: risk advice before disaster should matter more than firefighting after disaster.
- The "芒刺在背" anecdote explains why Huo Guang's living presence had already made Xuan feel physically unsafe, unlike later attendance by Zhang Anshi.
- Ban Gu's assessment preserves Huo Guang's service while faulting his lack of learning, private desire, and concealment of his wife's murder plot.

## Key Quotes

> "曲突徙薪" - the episode's preventive-risk story for Xu Fu's overlooked warning.

> "芒刺在背" - the phrase used to express Xuan's fear when Huo Guang accompanied him.
