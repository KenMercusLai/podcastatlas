# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》958丨揭秘史上最短命的“同仇敌忾”

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate pre-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-959-ruguo-ni-chuanyue-dao-sanguo-gai-ruhe-cunhuo-lroaihmpnrelmfcugym9zx2h2dhq|Hanji 959]] late-Han field after [[DongZhuo|董卓]] controls the court and local warlords compete under the broken surface of the [[AntiDongZhuoCoalition|反董卓联盟]]. It follows [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] from victory over the [[QingzhouYellowTurbans|青州黄巾军]] through the [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]]-[[YuanShu|袁术]] cavalry dispute, [[GongsunYueLateHan|公孙越]]'s death, and the fast turn from cooperation with [[YuanShao|袁绍]] into northern rivalry. The closing section introduces [[LiuBei|刘备]] as a low-resource Han-clan actor whose reputation, restraint, and social style become early political assets.

## Key Claims

- The episode frames late-Han disorder as a test of ambition and human nature: shared anti-Dong-Zhuo language does not prevent local actors from competing for territory, soldiers, and safety.
- [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] defeats a large [[QingzhouYellowTurbans|青州黄巾军]] force around [[DongguangCountyLateHan|东光县]], captures tens of thousands, and gains enough momentum to project power toward Qingzhou and Xuzhou.
- [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] tries to escape dependence on the Chang'an court by sending [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]]'s son through [[Wuguan|武关]] to request troops, but the request becomes exposed once the envoy reaches [[NanyangCommandery|南阳郡]] and [[YuanShu|袁术]] detains him.
- Yuan Shu's handling of Liu Yu's son is presented as a troop-absorption move, not only loyalist rescue: he wants Liu Yu's cavalry to arrive under an imperial rescue pretext, then become useful to his own position.
- Gongsun Zan warns Liu Yu not to trust Yuan Shu, then separately sends [[GongsunYueLateHan|公孙越]] with cavalry to Yuan Shu and advises Yuan Shu to hold Liu Yu's troops. This double move plants an early grudge between Liu Yu and Gongsun Zan.
- The [[AntiDongZhuoCoalition|反董卓联盟]] begins to break before Dong Zhuo is gone. [[YuanShao|袁绍]] and [[YuanShu|袁术]] compete through offices and proxies while [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] fights Dong Zhuo's forces at the front.
- Yuan Shao's appointment of [[ZhouAngLateHan|周昂]] as Yuzhou inspector leads to Zhou Ang taking Yangcheng from Sun Jian's rear, while Sun Jian is still fighting under an anti-Dong-Zhuo justification.
- Yuan Shu sends Gongsun Yue to assist Sun Jian against Zhou Ang. Gongsun Yue is killed by a stray arrow, and Gongsun Zan treats the death as Yuan Shao's responsibility.
- Gongsun Zan's later campaign against Yuan Shao is not caused only by Gongsun Yue's death. The episode treats the death as a fuse on top of deeper competition over Hebei, offices, and the practical value of Jizhou, Qingzhou, and Yanzhou appointments.
- Yuan Shao's attempt to placate Gongsun Zan by giving [[BohaiCommanderyLateHan|渤海郡]] to [[GongsunFanLateHan|公孙范]] backfires when Gongsun Fan brings the Bohai troops over to Gongsun Zan.
- The source makes [[InterestStructuredAllianceFragility|利益结构式联盟脆弱性]] central: actors can call one another comrades in the anti-Dong-Zhuo cause while simultaneously taking rear bases, detaining allies' messengers, or converting concessions into defections.
- The closing Liu Bei section emphasizes low starting resources, Han-clan identity without much practical inherited power, and behavioral style. His later access to the political table is framed through reputation, restraint, and capacity to avoid unnecessary enemies.

## Key Quotes

> "乱世造英雄" - the opening frame for how disorder reveals political capacity.

> "如果你不在桌子上，那你有可能就在菜单上" - the episode's compact warning about late-Han power competition.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is recorded. The episode backfills the coalition and northern-rivalry prelude before Hanji 959-960 rather than reversing their later survival and Jieqiao accounts.
- Source-scope caution: the host's modern power-table metaphor and Liu Bei management-style generalizations are treated as interpretive framing, not as standalone historical evidence.
- Source-scope caution: the episode presents Gongsun Yue's death as a fuse, while preserving the broader power-struggle explanation as the deeper cause of the Yuan Shao-Gongsun Zan rupture.
