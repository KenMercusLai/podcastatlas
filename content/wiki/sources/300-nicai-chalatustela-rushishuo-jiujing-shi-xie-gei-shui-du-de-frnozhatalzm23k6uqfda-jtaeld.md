It argues that [[FriedrichNietzsche|尼采]]'s widely recognized book is less central in undergraduate philosophy teaching and specialist monographs than several of his more conventionally argumentative works, even as general readers, quotation culture, and wartime circulation made it unusually visible. - The episode says philosophy courses more often assign *The Birth of Tragedy*, *On the Uses and Disadvantages of History for Life*, *Beyond Good and Evil*, and *On the Genealogy of Morality*.
