# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》659丨有些核心人才不要轻易动

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 21 CE late-[[XinDynasty|新朝]] crisis between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-658-erzi-queshi-buyinggai-wangmang-nusha-taizi-ln4tu6ly8eie6jspvrurzn2rjjp|Hanji 658]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-660-2-yi-ji-kan-dong-chimei-jun-de-dansheng-lgqk1gkvh-0cwder6g5-wryi6mce|Hanji 660-2]]. It shows [[WangMang|王莽]] answering revolt, famine, and official misinformation with palace desire, symbolic anti-Han ritual, punishment of truth-tellers, and suspicion toward competent field actors.

The durable synthesis is that Xin collapse worsens when the ruler forbids root-cause reporting and removes the person temporarily making the field governable. [[GongsunLuXinOfficial|公孙禄]] identifies internal policy and personnel failure, [[TianKuangXinOfficial|田邝]] diagnoses county-to-court underreporting and proposes a local defense and food-control plan, but Wang Mang suppresses the former and transfers the latter away from the Qing-Xu crisis field.

## Key Claims

- Wang Mang remains absorbed in omen, immortality, and palace arrangements even as rebellion and famine grow, including Yang Cheng's recommendation to select a new empress and many women for the court.
- His destruction of the [[LiuBang|刘邦]] temple shows the Xin court attacking Han symbolic memory while Han-restoration sentiment is already becoming politically dangerous.
- [[QinFengEasternHan|秦丰]] and [[ChiZhaopingXinRebel|池昭平]] mark the widening late-Xin revolt field before later Chimei and Lulin source notes dominate the sequence.
- [[GongsunLuXinOfficial|公孙禄]] argues that the crisis comes from internal policy failure, bad officials, false astrology, and conflict with Xiongnu rather than from external enemies alone.
- Wang Mang treats root-cause explanations as disloyal: he removes Gongsun Lu from the discussion and later orders officials arrested if they say rebels are driven by hunger and cold.
- [[TianKuangXinOfficial|田邝]] temporarily stabilizes Yiping by unauthorized local mobilization, then wins repeated victories and is appointed to act over Qingzhou and Xuzhou.
- Tian Kuang's memorial identifies systemic failure: county and commandery underreporting, delayed response, incapable commanders, exhausted local officials, and central envoys who disturb civilians more than rebels.
- Wang Mang removes Tian Kuang because his effectiveness and merit become threatening, turning a working field solution into [[KeyOperatorRemovalRisk|key operator removal risk]].

## Key Quotes

> "因饥寒犯法" - the forbidden root-cause explanation that Wang Mang criminalizes.

> "县欺郡，郡欺朝廷" - Tian Kuang's diagnosis of local-to-center underreporting.

> "有些核心人才不要轻易动" - the episode's management lesson drawn from Tian Kuang's removal.
