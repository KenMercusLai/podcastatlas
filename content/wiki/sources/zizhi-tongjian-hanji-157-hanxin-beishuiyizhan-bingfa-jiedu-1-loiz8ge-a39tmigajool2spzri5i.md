# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》157｜韩信“背水一战”兵法解读（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-156-bingxian-hanxin-de-fengshen-zhizhan-2-li2c9d04jpepy4t0mquwjt-bli4|Hanji 156 part 2]] by explaining why [[HanXin|韩信]]'s [[BackwaterBattle|背水一战]] at [[JingxingPass|井陉口]] is hard to imitate. The source accepts Han Xin's own [[SunziBingfa|《孙子兵法》]] explanation that untrained troops can fight harder when placed on death ground, but insists that the real victory depended on prior intelligence and surprise. Its main wiki contribution is [[DeathGroundTacticalPreconditions|死地战术前提]]: forced commitment works only after the commander understands his own army, verifies enemy choices, and creates a second shock that prevents the enemy from exploiting the risk.

## Key Claims
- After the battle, Han officers ask why Han Xin violated ordinary formation rules by putting troops with their backs to water instead of arranging terrain in the expected mountain-water pattern.
- Han Xin answers that he used an existing military principle: because many of his soldiers were newly armed commoners rather than well-trained troops, he had to put them in a situation where scattering would be more dangerous than fighting.
- The episode traces that answer to [[SunziBingfa|《孙子兵法》]]'s "九地" reasoning and treats "陷之死地而后生" as a real morale mechanism, not just a later idiom.
- The source warns that later imitators often copy only the visible backwater formation and miss the hidden prerequisites, so "背水一战" becomes a double-edged tactic.
- Han Xin first uses spies to learn that [[ChenYu|陈馀]] has not accepted [[LiZuoChe|李左车]]'s supply-line plan and that [[JingxingPass|井陉口]] does not contain a Zhao ambush.
- That intelligence matters because [[TransportCorridorSeverance|交通线切割]] would have made the Han position dangerous in a different way: Zhao could have avoided direct battle and trapped Han's stretched supply line.
- Han Xin's other decisive layer is "出奇": the two thousand hidden light cavalry who enter Zhao's empty camp and raise Han flags, turning Zhao's rear into a panic signal.
- The host summarizes Han Xin's method as knowing self and enemy, then using regular deployment and surprise together rather than relying on courage alone.
- The episode speculates that Han Xin did not fully explain the whole mechanism to his officers because difficult tactical judgment cannot be safely reduced to a reusable formula.
- The closing notice says Han Xin accepts Li Zuoche's later advice, sends people to [[YanState|燕国]], and Yan submits as Li Zuoche predicted, showing that Han Xin can convert battlefield victory into wider strategic pressure.

## Key Quotes
> "陷之死地而后生，置之亡地而后存" - the Sunzi principle Han Xin uses to explain the backwater formation.

> "凡战者，以正合，以奇胜" - the source's frame for Han Xin's hidden-cavalry camp seizure.

> "运用之妙，存乎一心" - the source-scoped explanation for why Han Xin may not spell out every layer of the tactic.
