# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》596丨鲍宣劝谏哀帝：有一种爱叫放手

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode fills the late-[[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] sequence between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-595-wangmang-shazi-jielu-quanli-de-canku-lh9nr18cllq1rrl0nz4fzipai4eq|Hanji 595]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-597-taihuangtaihou-wangzhengjun-de-zhaoling-weihe-bei-jushou-lramivd9ej_mnpjmx2kihh2p8cvy|Hanji 597]]. [[BaoXuanWesternHan|鲍宣]] uses eclipses, earthquakes, drought, and public unease to argue that Aidi's initial self-correction has not reached the central cause: extraordinary favor toward [[DongXian|董贤]] has redirected treasury funds, official land, construction labor, and tribute into one household.

The durable synthesis is a hierarchy-aware remonstrance sequence. Bao Xuan first recognizes Aidi's reflection and personnel cleanup, then states the unresolved public danger, and finally translates dismissal, return to the fief, and recovery of imperial goods into protection for Dong Xian: if Aidi truly loves him, withdrawing him from the center is safer than continuing to make him the target of public resentment.

## Key Claims

- Bao Xuan frames the emperor as responsible to Heaven, Earth, and the people, making eclipse, earthquake, and livelihood anxiety evidence of unresolved government failure.
- He acknowledges Aidi's visible corrective acts before escalating the criticism, including avoiding the main hall, soliciting frank advice, and removing some outer relatives or ineffective officials.
- Bao Xuan argues that cloudy, rainless conditions and continuing public resentment show that those initial responses did not reach the underlying problem.
- Dong Xian's favor is described as a public-resource problem: unrestricted reward, treasury expenditure, combined residences, official land, state construction capacity, and tribute all move toward the Dong household.
- Bao Xuan reframes withdrawal as care, arguing that dismissal, return to the fief, and recovery of imperial goods could reduce hatred and protect Dong Xian's family.
- The memorial also proposes a broader repair package: remove Sun Chong and Xifu Gong's fiefs and recall [[HeWuWesternHan|何武]], [[ShiDanWesternHan|施丹]], [[PengXuanWesternHan|彭宣]], and [[FuXiWesternHan|傅喜]].
- The episode says Aidi is impressed and accepts the advice, recalling He Wu and Peng Xuan and appointing Bao Xuan as Sili; it does not establish that the proposed removal of Dong Xian occurred.

## Key Quotes

> "你如果真的怜爱董贤，就应该替他向天地谢罪" - the episode's paraphrase of Bao Xuan's protection-through-withdrawal argument.

> "有一种爱叫放手" - the host's summary of turning dismissal from punishment into protection.
