# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》802丨安帝之死！东汉王朝权力大洗牌！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 125 CE succession setup immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-803-han-shundi-ruhe-zai-xuefengxueyu-zhong-dengshang-huangwei-lqvno7t-gcgxtbs3phuflr4ybe2s|Hanji 803]]. It follows [[EmperorAnOfHan|汉安帝 / Liu Hu]]'s sudden death on tour, [[EmpressDowagerYanLateHan|阎皇后 / 阎太后]] and [[YanXianLateHan|阎显]]'s first concealed-death transition, the choice of [[LiuYiBeixiangHouLateHan|北乡侯刘懿 / Liu Yi]] over [[EmperorShunOfHan|济阴王刘保]], and the Yan camp's first purge against [[GengBaoGrandGeneralLateHan|耿宝]], [[FanFengEunuchLateHan|樊丰]], [[WangShengPalaceActorLateHan|王圣]], and related old networks.

The durable synthesis is that the Shun restoration crisis begins before the eunuch coup: Yan Taihou's side first wins a short-term information and appointment race, but it does so by excluding the stronger heir, concentrating offices in one kin group, and turning former allies into purge targets. That over-concentration creates the threat environment that Hanji 803's remaining eunuch group will exploit.

## Key Claims

- The opening workplace analogy is source-scoped listener advice: durable organizations need interest balance and shared payoff, while direct struggle with a superior usually damages the weaker side.
- In 125 CE, Emperor An becomes ill at [[WanCounty|宛城]], travels onward, and dies near Ye County at age thirty-two, away from the capital.
- Yan Hou, Yan Xian, and Fan Feng conceal the death, continue the appearance of food, medicine, and daily routine, and rush roughly six hundred li back to [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] in four days.
- The concealment gives the Yan side enough time to announce the death from inside the palace, elevate Yan Hou as empress dowager, and give Yan Xian military authority as chariot cavalry general.
- Yan Taihou and Yan Xian choose the young Beixiang Hou Liu Yi as emperor so the Yan family can hold power behind a controllable ruler.
- Liu Bao, the former crown prince and Emperor An's son, is excluded because he had already been demoted to Jiyin Wang; Yan Taihou refuses him entry to mourn at the coffin and leaves him crying outside the hall for three days.
- Yan Xian then turns on old powerholders: Geng Bao's prestige and Grand General office become intolerable after the Yan-backed accession, while Fan Feng, Xie Yun, Zhou Guang, Wang Sheng, and related households are accused, executed, exiled, or demoted.
- The episode reads this as a failed power ecology: the Yan family removes former collaborators and fills key offices with Yan brothers instead of widening the coalition.
- The purge of An-era eunuch and palace networks threatens those not yet destroyed, making a later counter-move more likely.
