# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1092丨富可敌国的娄圭，因一句感慨被曹操赐死？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the succession-and-surrender layer immediately before the civilian retreat in Hanji 1093. After [[LiuBiao|刘表]] dies, [[CaiMao|蔡瑁]] and [[ZhangYunLateHan|张允]] block [[LiuQiLateHan|刘琦]] from seeing his father, support [[LiuCongLateHan|刘琮]], and help place the young successor inside a Jingzhou elite coalition that prefers submission to [[CaoCao|曹操]] over war.

Its distinctive figure is [[LouGuiLateHan|娄圭]]. When Cao-side officers suspect a false surrender, Lou Gui reads Liu Cong's delivery of the imperial tally as costly symbolic evidence and persuades Cao Cao to accept it. The episode then connects Lou Gui's exceptional wealth and weak independent force to [[PowerDominatedWealthInsecurity|power-dominated wealth insecurity]]: in 211 CE, an ambiguous remark reported by [[XiShouLateHan|习授]] is treated as disloyalty and followed by execution. The source does not establish Lou Gui's wealth mechanism, a pre-existing murder plan, or the precise legal form of his death.

## Key Claims
- Cai Mao and Zhang Yun prevent Liu Qi from visiting the dying Liu Biao because renewed father-son contact could disturb the succession arrangement.
- Jingzhou elites support Liu Cong's accession and favor surrender because the young ruler lacks an independent power base and local families fear both military defeat and retaliation after resistance.
- [[FuXunLateHan|傅巽]] argues that reliance on Liu Bei cannot preserve Liu Cong: failure against Cao Cao loses Jingzhou, while success makes Liu Bei too powerful to remain subordinate.
- Lou Gui interprets Liu Cong's voluntary delivery of an imperial tally as evidence that the surrender is genuine, illustrating [[SurrenderTokenCredibility|降服凭证式可信度]].
- The episode portrays Lou Gui as strategically capable and exceptionally wealthy but does not document how he accumulated that wealth.
- Cao Cao's comparison between Lou Gui's money and his own superior power is used by the host to infer political suspicion; that inferred prior intent is not independently established.
- Xi Shou reports Lou Gui's ambiguous remark after seeing Cao Cao and his son traveling in grandeur, and Cao Cao has Lou Gui imprisoned and killed.
- Liu Bei refuses a proposal to seize Liu Cong, mourns at Liu Biao's tomb, and withdraws south; the episode ends by setting up the historical [[ChangbanBattle|长坂坡之战]].

## Key Quotes
> “人生在世，事情要自己做到，你看别人活得好有啥用。” — the episode's rendering of Lou Gui's ambiguous remark before Xi Shou reports him.

> “娄子伯虽然比我有钱，但他势力比不上我。” — the episode's rendering of Cao Cao's wealth-and-power comparison.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is recorded. The source backfills Liu Cong's succession and surrender before Hanji 1093, while Hanji 1095 supplies his post-surrender title and the absorption of Jingzhou officials.
- The source alternates between imprisonment-and-execution language and “赐死”; the wiki records death on Cao Cao's order without treating the precise legal form as settled.
- Lou Gui's wealth source, the degree of his strategic parity with Cao Cao's famous advisers, Cao Cao's supposed earlier intent to kill him, and the private meaning of Lou Gui's final remark remain source-scoped.
- The exact dialogue, dates, sequence of Liu Qi's attempted return, scope of the “seven commanderies,” elite motives, and Liu Biao's alleged deathbed entrustment of Liu Cong to Liu Bei require primary-source verification.
