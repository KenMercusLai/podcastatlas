# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》195｜吕后掌权刘邦长子急忙认妹为“娘”

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode places [[LiuFeiQiKing|刘肥]] in a lethal court-status crisis during [[LiuYing|汉惠帝刘盈]]'s reign: after Liu Ying seats his elder half-brother in the honored position, [[LuZhi|吕雉]] reportedly prepares poisoned wine, Liu Ying's attempt to share the cup exposes the danger, and Liu Fei escapes the banquet. Liu Fei then yields [[ChengyangCommandery|城阳郡]] as a [[TangmuYi|汤沐邑]] for [[LuYuanPrincess|鲁元公主]] and accepts the status reversal of honoring his younger half-sister as queen mother, converting land and kinship rank into a survival bargain.

The second half records [[XiaoHe|萧何]]'s final recommendation of [[CaoShen|曹参]], death, legal-administrative reputation, frugal household strategy, and contested role in [[HanXin|韩信]]'s rise and killing. Its durable synthesis is double-sided: restraint can preserve life or institutional continuity, but the poison scene, private motives, legal authorship, and “成也萧何，败也萧何” moral allocation remain episode-attributed rather than independently established.

## Key Claims

- In Hui year two / 193 BCE, Liu Fei attends a Chang'an banquet where Liu Ying seats him in the honored position because he is the elder brother.
- The episode says Lu Zhi responds by ordering two cups of poisoned wine; Liu Ying's move to drink beside Liu Fei causes her to knock the cup away, after which Liu Fei feigns drunkenness and leaves.
- A Qi official advises Liu Fei to surrender one commandery to Princess Lu Yuan as a bathing estate and honor her as queen mother; Liu Fei gives Chengyang and survives the immediate crisis.
- The episode interprets Liu Fei's low-threat maternal status and willingness to accept humiliation as reasons he outlives other sons of Liu Bang, but intent and causal sufficiency remain inferred.
- Omens, an earthquake, drought, and deaths are narrated around Xiao He's final illness without establishing a causal relationship.
- Xiao He approves Cao Shen as successor, supporting the institutional-continuity branch developed in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-196-jiexi-xiaogui-caosui-da-zhihui-lic6c_-juuvxxrpdwglnlqlgi_8o|Hanji 196]].
- The source credits Xiao He with legal-administrative service, frugality, and loyal caution while also retaining his role in recommending and later helping trap Han Xin.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. The source deepens Liu Fei's profile by adding a court-survival episode before the later political importance of his Qi inheritance.
- The statement that Lu Zhi killed four of Liu Bang's eight sons is the episode's compression and is not used here as a verified count or a claim that all deaths had the same mechanism.
- The poison plan, Liu Ying's awareness, cup handling, Liu Fei's feigned drunkenness, the Qi official's advice, Lu Zhi's motives, the recognition ceremony, and the exact causal path from concession to release remain episode-attributed.
- “Recognizing his sister as mother” is a status shorthand: the source describes honoring Princess Lu Yuan as queen mother, not a biological-family claim.
- The well dragons, earthquake, drought, exact dates, deaths, Xiao He-Cao Shen dialogue, authorship of the Nine Chapters, land and residence details, Liu Bang-Xiao He exchange, and moral interpretation of Xiao He's role in Han Xin's death require comparison with transmitted histories.
