# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》550丨杀敌国太子，吓退几千追兵！他怎么做到的？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Yuanyan 2-3 / 11-10 BCE under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]. It centers on [[DuanHuizongWesternHan|段会宗]]'s Western Regions mission after [[Wusun|乌孙]]'s large and small kunmi order breaks down: [[MozhenjiangWusun|末振将]] kills the large kunmi Cilim, Han installs a new large kunmi, and Duan later kills Mozhenjiang's son [[FanqiuWusun|番丘]] with a small selected party.

The durable synthesis is that late Western Han frontier order depends on calibrated coercion rather than simple occupation. Duan uses stealth, formal accusation, precedent-backed imperial threat, and face-saving explanation to survive encirclement by [[AnlimiWusun|安犁靡]]'s thousands of riders, while [[GuoShunWesternHan|郭舜]]'s later memorial on [[Kangju|康居]] shows the parallel diplomatic dilemma: ritual disrespect may signal unreliability, but the court still chooses engagement to preserve flexibility.

## Key Claims

- [[DuanHuizongWesternHan|段会宗]] becomes the trusted repeat envoy for Wusun disorder because Han direct deployment is costly and slow.
- Wusun's two-kunmi settlement creates continuing instability: [[MozhenjiangWusun|末振将]] is installed as small kunmi, kills the large kunmi Cilim through a feigned defector, and is later replaced by [[AnlimiWusun|安犁靡]].
- Duan's killing of [[FanqiuWusun|番丘]] uses a small-force surprise tactic, not a large punitive army, because a visible army would let the target flee.
- When Anlimi surrounds Duan with thousands of riders, Duan invokes Han's prior executions of a Dayuan king and [[ZhizhiChanyu|郅支单于]] to make retaliation credible.
- Duan also gives Anlimi a face-saving rationale: advance notice would either let Fanqiu escape or force Anlimi to betray kinship openly.
- [[GuoShunWesternHan|郭舜]] argues that Kangju's hostage and tribute are self-interested and disrespectful, but Han chooses continued accommodation because this is Kangju's first prince-hostage mission.
- [[LiuXiang|刘向]] reads the 10 BCE Minshan collapse and river blockage through earlier Zhou disaster precedents, treating landscape disorder as dynastic warning.

## Key Quotes

> "拔下一根牛毛" - Duan's warning that his own death would be a small loss to Han but a grave danger for Wusun.

> "大宛国王和郅支单于的人头曾挂在长安街上" - the remembered-punishment precedent behind Duan's deterrent threat.
