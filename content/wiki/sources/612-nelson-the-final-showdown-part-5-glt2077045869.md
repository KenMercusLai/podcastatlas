# 612. Nelson: The Final Showdown (Part 5)

## Summary

This [[TheRestIsHistory]] episode follows [[HoratioNelson|Horatio Nelson]] from his August 1805 return to Britain through his final separation from [[EmmaHamilton|Emma Hamilton]] and the dawn sighting of the combined French-Spanish fleet on 21 October. It joins private farewell, public celebrity, invasion anxiety, command preparation, and the [[TrafalgarCampaign|Trafalgar campaign]], presenting the “Nelson touch” as a deliberately communicated plan to force an annihilating close action while frigates and beyond-the-horizon positioning encouraged [[PierreCharlesVilleneuve|Villeneuve]] to leave Cadiz.

## Key Claims

- Nelson returned from the Caribbean pursuit worried that he had failed, but public acclaim and senior backing confirmed his position as Britain's leading naval celebrity and commander.
- Merton functioned as Nelson's imagined domestic refuge with Emma and Horatia; the final farewell, prayer, reported exchange of rings, and letters at sea made family attachment and religious fatalism part of the campaign narrative.
- British invasion anxiety in 1805 was strategically intelligible because Napoleon needed only temporary Channel control against a small professional army, even though extensive defenses and mobilization were under way.
- Nelson sought annihilation rather than a limited victory and explained a two-column break into the allied line, accepting approach exposure in order to create a close “pell-mell” battle favorable to British gunnery and delegated captaincy.
- Off Cadiz, Nelson kept the battle fleet beyond the horizon and used frigates as a relay, combining concealment, surveillance, health, supply, drilling, and captain dinners to make aggression operationally possible.
- Villeneuve faced shortage, disease, weak readiness, Napoleon's orders, and the threat of replacement; the source presents his decision to sail as pressured and honor-conscious rather than cowardly.
- Nelson delayed immediate attack on 20 October so the fleet would have a full day to prevent escape, and the episode ends when the allied fleet appears at dawn before the [[BattleOfTrafalgar|Battle of Trafalgar]].

## Key Quotes

> "The country wanted annihilation." - Nelson's formulation of the result he sought, as presented by the episode.

> "I had the huzzahs before. I have their hearts now." - reported reflection after the Portsmouth crowd's farewell.
