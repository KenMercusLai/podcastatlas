# 宏观经济如何传导给我们每个人？

## Summary
This conversation brings [[Mianji|面基]] host [[HouWangMianji|厚望]] and [[InvestmentABC|投资ABC]] host [[AmyInvestmentABC|Amiee]] together with economist [[MiaoYanliang|缪延亮]]. It develops [[GeneralEquilibriumPolicyTransmission|一般均衡式政策传导]] as a method: begin with constraints, then trace how households, firms, markets, and expectations alter an initial shock. It applies that method to [[NeutralRateDrivers|中性利率驱动因素]], household deposits, the dollar cycle, [[TreasurySafeAssetFracture|美债安全资产裂纹]], gold, and [[AIMacroCapitalLaborTransmission|AI 的资本—劳动宏观传导]].

## Key Claims
- [[GeneralEquilibriumPolicyTransmission]] explains why raising a regulated price need not lift aggregate inflation: unchanged household budgets, weaker real-income expectations, lower spending elsewhere, and the paradox of thrift can reverse the local effect.
- Macro theory, the real economy, and policy practice are distinct. Models organize constraints, but policy and market feedback reveal how micro actors actually respond.
- [[NeutralRateDrivers]] groups the neutral rate around potential growth, saving preference, and investment preference; fiscal expansion or stronger demand can move an economy away from a low-rate state.
- Household deposit totals mainly reflect bank credit creation, while the household-versus-corporate split and term-versus-demand composition can reveal risk appetite, opportunity scarcity, and circulation frictions.
- Long investment narratives are fragile because every link adds timing and causal error; fiscal expansion can strengthen rather than weaken a currency if growth and rate effects attract enough capital.
- [[TreasurySafeAssetFracture]] separates imminent default from orderly repricing: higher term premium, missing stable marginal buyers, fiscal dominance risk, and weaker crisis-time negative beta can damage long-bond safety before outright disorder.
- [[AIMacroCapitalLaborTransmission]] treats AI as macro when capex, financing, equity wealth, consumption, trade, labor substitution, prices, and distribution become large enough to alter aggregate outcomes.
- AI can deliver real productivity while its assets remain overvalued; service-sector efficiency may create disinflationary pressure, while entry-level work and income distribution can deteriorate before aggregate employment does.

## Key Quotes
> "AI 即宏观" - the episode's shorthand for AI investment and labor effects becoming aggregate variables.

> "你可以把马牵到河边，但不能强迫马喝水" - boundary on monetary stimulus when households do not want more leverage.

> "一个人的支出是另一个人的收入" - the transmission link behind the paradox-of-thrift example.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction was adopted. The source qualifies any simple view that lower rates automatically create demand, that fiscal expansion automatically weakens a currency, or that productive technology cannot be financially overvalued.
- The source's AI investment share, debt totals, exchange rates, reserve trends, labor observations, and timing claims remain source- and date-scoped rather than independently audited current facts.
- Claims about future service-sector deflation, fiscal dominance, Federal Reserve intervention, AI job displacement, and institutional reform are scenarios rather than established outcomes.
