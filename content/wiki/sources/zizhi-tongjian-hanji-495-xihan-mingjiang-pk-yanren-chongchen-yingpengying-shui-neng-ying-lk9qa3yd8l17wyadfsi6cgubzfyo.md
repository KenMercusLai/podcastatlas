# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》495丨西汉名将PK阉人宠臣，硬碰硬谁能赢？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jingning 1 / 33 BCE under [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]], before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-496-qiong-pa-le-jiemi-tanguan-zouxiang-tanfu-de-xinli-youyin-lnskbcqvjhtpfi4ikq44kpbbfnol|Hanji 496]] and the later [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]] fall sequence. It follows Shi Xian's failed attempt to pull the Feng family into his network, [[FengXunWesternHan|冯勋]]'s private warning to Yuan, and Shi Xian's later blocking of [[FengYewangWesternHan|冯野王]] from becoming Yushi Dafu after [[LiYanshouWesternHan|李延寿]] dies.

The durable synthesis is that Shi Xian can win without openly opposing a qualified rival. He praises Feng Yewang's ability, then reframes the appointment as a reputational danger because Feng is [[FengZhaoyiWesternHan|冯昭仪]]'s brother; by using Yuan's fear of appearing to favor harem kin, Shi Xian blocks a hostile family while appearing public-minded.

## Key Claims

- Shi Xian first tries to approach the Feng family because Feng Zhaoyi is favored by Yuan and the family has useful status.
- Feng Xun secretly warns Yuan that Shi Xian is monopolizing power, but Yuan's favor toward Shi Xian makes the warning backfire and deepens Shi Xian's hostility toward the Feng family.
- After Yushi Dafu Li Yanshou dies, many officials rank Feng Yewang first among possible replacements because of his conduct and ability.
- Shi Xian avoids direct opposition; he first says no one among the Nine Ministers is more suitable than Feng Yewang.
- Shi Xian then argues that appointing Feng Yewang as one of the Three Excellencies could make later observers say Yuan used a favored consort's brother for private reasons.
- Yuan accepts the argument, so [[ZhangTanWesternHan|张谭]] becomes Yushi Dafu and [[ZhaoXinchenWesternHan|召信臣]] becomes Shaofu.
- The episode treats Shi Xian's victory as a speech-and-psychology case: he identifies Yuan's reputational vulnerability and turns a personnel decision into a ruler-image risk.

## Key Quotes

> "后世可能认为皇帝任用后宫亲属、徇私用人" - the episode's summary of Shi Xian's decisive objection to Feng Yewang's appointment.

> "举贤不避亲" - the counter-principle the host says Yuan lacks the courage to own in this case.
