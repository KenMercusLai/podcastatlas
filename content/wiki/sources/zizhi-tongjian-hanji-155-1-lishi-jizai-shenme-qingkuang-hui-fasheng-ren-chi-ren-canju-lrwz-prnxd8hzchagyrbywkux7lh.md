# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》155-1｜历史记载什么情况会发生“人吃人”惨剧

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues from [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-154-diangu-chenping-daosao-lingyou-yinqing-li-c6ezwfpejcvbrpzojg-xv2ge8|Hanji 154]] by briefly recapping the [[ChenPing|陈平]] accusation thread, then using [[HakuinEkaku|白隐慧鹤]] as a moral analogy for bearing false blame. The main Chu-Han sequence follows [[WeiBao|魏豹]]'s renewed break from [[LiuBang|刘邦]], the fall of [[Feiqiu|废丘]] and [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]]'s suicide, and the administrative consolidation of [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] through commanderies. Its strongest contribution is the famine branch: war empties fields of labor, grain prices explode, extreme social order breaks down, and [[RenShiQinMerchant|任氏]]'s grain-hoarding fortune raises the question of how wealth survives in violent scarcity.

## Key Claims
- The episode opens with the classic tactic "声东击西" and defines it as creating a visible direction of attack so the enemy misreads the real target.
- The source frames this tactic as a question for the next [[HanXin|韩信]] operation against [[WeiBao|魏豹]], but the available transcript stops before narrating the full campaign.
- The episode recaps the end of the Chen Ping accusation thread and pairs it with the Hakuin story to emphasize composure under slander or misunderstanding.
- Wei Bao asks Liu Bang to let him return to West Wei to visit his sick mother, then seals Yellow River crossings and submits again to [[XiangYu|项羽]].
- The host records Wei Bao's stated grievance as personal humiliation: he says Liu Bang treats and insults other lords and ministers like slaves.
- Han forces flood Feiqiu after a year-long siege; the defenders surrender, and Zhang Han commits suicide.
- After Feiqiu falls, the Han side fully pacifies the Yong territory and creates [[ZhongdiCommandery|中地郡]], [[BeidiCommandery|北地郡]], and [[LongxiCommandery|陇西郡]].
- Guanzhong suffers a catastrophic famine: the source says rice reaches ten thousand coins per unit and that "人吃人" conditions appear.
- The host explains the famine through [[WartimeAgriculturalLaborCollapse|战争抽空农业劳动力]]: Chu-Han mobilization pulls young labor out of fields, leaving land untilled and food supply broken.
- Liu Bang responds by letting Guanzhong people move toward [[BaShu|蜀郡]] and [[Hanzhong|汉中]] to survive.
- [[RenShiQinMerchant|任氏]]'s grain hoarding in [[Shiji|《史记·货殖列传》]] becomes a case for [[PowerBackedGrainHoarding|权力保护下的乱世囤粮]]: foresight alone may not explain why stores survive when violence and starvation are widespread.

## Key Quotes
> "声东击西" - the tactic used as the episode's opening frame.

> "一壶米竟然卖到了一万钱" - the source's marker for Guanzhong grain-price collapse.

> "我更愿意相信第一种可能" - the host's view that Ren氏 probably had protection rather than merely operating under ordinary order.
