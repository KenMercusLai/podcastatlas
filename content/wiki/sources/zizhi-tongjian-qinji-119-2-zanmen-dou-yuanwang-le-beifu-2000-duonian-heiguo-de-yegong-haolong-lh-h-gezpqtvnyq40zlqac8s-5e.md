# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》119-2｜咱们都冤枉了背负2000多年黑锅的“叶公好龙”

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode expands the [[BaiGongSheng|白公胜]] and [[YeGong|叶公]] comparison introduced in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-1-chaijie-jingke-ciqin-bi-bai-de-mimi-lnonhw9tlsu5dudu8750zvgeaz1p|Qinji 119-1]]. In 479 BCE, [[ZiXiChu|子西]] recalls Bai Gong Sheng into [[ChuState|楚国]] despite Ye Gong's warning that visible 信 and 勇 are dangerous without 仁义; Bai later kills Zi Xi, seizes [[ChuHuiwang|楚惠王]], and is defeated in the [[BaiGongShengRebellion|白公胜之乱]].

The episode's ethical move is to make speech, intention, and action continuous. Bai Gong Sheng's violent speech is treated as a diagnostic sign of disordered motive, while Ye Gong's calm judgment, successful suppression of the rebellion, and later retirement turn him into a positive case of [[RenyiGroundedVirtue|仁义为本的信勇]] and [[PowerExitTrap|功成身退]].

The final section reframes [[YeGongHaolong|叶公好龙]]. The host traces the idiom to [[LiuXiang|刘向]]'s [[XinXu|《新序》]], but treats it as a later moralizing anecdote that damaged Ye Gong's reputation rather than as a reliable biography of Shen Zhuliang.

## Key Claims
- [[YeGong|叶公]] is identified as Shen Zhuliang, courtesy name Zigao, a Chu official associated with Ye county.
- In 479 BCE, [[ZiXiChu|子西]] wants to recall his nephew [[BaiGongSheng|白公胜]] because he sees him as trustworthy, brave, and useful on the Wu-Chu frontier.
- Ye Gong objects that Bai Gong Sheng's 信 must be governed by 仁 and his 勇 must be governed by 义; otherwise the same traits can serve revenge and violence.
- Bai Gong Sheng's demand to attack [[ZhengState|郑国]] for [[TaiziJianChu|太子建]] is first deferred and then reversed when Zi Xi chooses to rescue Zheng against [[JinState|晋国]] pressure.
- Bai Gong Sheng's sharpening-sword threat against Zi Xi is read as a warning sign: language that openly carries killing intent should not be dismissed as blunt personality.
- Bai Gong Sheng defeats a [[WuState|吴国]] attack, uses the presentation of captives to enter the Chu capital armed, kills Zi Xi, and seizes Chu Hui Wang.
- Ye Gong judges Bai Gong Sheng's first success as accidental and then treats Bai's killing of [[GuanXiuChu|管修]] as proof that the rebellion cannot win popular attachment.
- Ye Gong raises troops, suppresses the rebellion, restores Chu Hui Wang, and Bai Gong Sheng flees to the mountains and hangs himself.
- After the crisis, Ye Gong is granted both lingyin and sima authority, then in 475 BCE hands the offices to Gongsun Ning and Gongsun Kuan and withdraws to his fief.
- The episode rejects the "刀子嘴豆腐心" excuse, arguing through [[SpeechAsMoralDiagnostic|言语作为德行信号]] and [[VirtuePracticeInternalization|修养内化]] that intention, speech, and action shape one another.
- The idiom [[YeGongHaolong|叶公好龙]] is treated as a reception problem: [[LiuXiang|刘向]]'s didactic story in [[XinXu|《新序》]] made Ye Gong a hypocrite in common memory, while this episode presents him as a capable and restrained historical actor.
- The closing contrast with [[YanTaiziDan|燕太子丹]] reinforces the source's broader claim that similar elite status can lead to different endings depending on motive, speech, and exit discipline.

## Key Quotes
> "信必须受“仁”的约束，勇必须受“义”的约束" - the episode's Ye Gong standard for trust and courage.

> "刀子嘴豆腐心" - the phrase the host rejects as an excuse for harmful speech.

> "叶公好龙" - the idiom whose common reception the episode reassesses.
