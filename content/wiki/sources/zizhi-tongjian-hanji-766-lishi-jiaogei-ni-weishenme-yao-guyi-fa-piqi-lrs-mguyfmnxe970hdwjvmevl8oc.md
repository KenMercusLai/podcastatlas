# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》766丨历史教给你：为什么要故意发脾气？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 92 CE anti-[[DouXianEasternHan|窦宪]] operation immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-767-congming-ren-dou-shanchang-yanxi-lq3ol7y-jdh7dnnfkmtfdg4ibryq|Hanji 767]]. It follows [[YuanAnEasternHan|袁安]] and [[RenKuiEasternHan|任隗]] resisting Dou Xian's Northern Xiongnu settlement, [[ZhouRongEasternHan|周荣]] answering a Dou-retainer death threat with public boundary-setting, [[DingHongEasternHan|丁鸿]] using a solar eclipse to warn against outer-relative monopoly, and [[EmperorHeOfHan|汉和帝]] secretly coordinating with [[ZhengZhongEasternHan|郑众]] and [[LiuQingQingheKing|清河王刘庆]] before removing the Dou bloc.

The durable synthesis is that the Dou purge is not just one decisive palace move. It is prepared by visible ministerial dissent, strategic anger that makes retaliation costly, omen-coded warning, historical-precedent search, and palace-gate command control; the same success also creates the later hazard that trusted eunuch access becomes politically indispensable.

## Key Claims

- Dou Xian's outer-relative and military prestige lets his household intimidate officials and force even appointed provincial and commandery officers to seek Dou approval before taking office.
- Yuan An and Ren Kui keep opposition to Dou's Northern Xiongnu arrangement alive after Song You backs away, with Zhou Rong drafting Yuan An's anti-Dou memorials.
- Zhou Rong's threatened response is deliberate deterrence: by making a private threat public, he raises the reputational and evidentiary cost of killing him.
- Ding Hong uses the 92 CE solar eclipse and older Lu-clan/Ai-Ping precedents to argue that even close imperial relatives become dangerous when virtue and institutional restraint fail.
- Emperor He cannot safely reach external ministers, so he works through Zheng Zhong and Liu Qing while waiting for Dou Xian and Deng Die to return to Luoyang.
- Liu Qing helps borrow historical material and convey the request for precedents on punishing maternal uncles, making history part of the crisis-planning process.
- The purge succeeds by closing palace gates, mobilizing guards and the northern army, executing the immediate palace conspirators, stripping Dou Xian's general-in-chief seal, and sending the Dou brothers to their fiefs.
- Zhang Pu's defense of Dou Gui adds a narrow mercy layer: he criticizes courtiers' opportunistic reversal and persuades Emperor He that Dou Gui did not share the household's overreach.

## Key Quotes

> "即使因此而死，诚所甘心" - Zhou Rong's posture after the Dou retainer threatens him.

> "不要收葬" - Zhou Rong's instruction that his exposed corpse should warn the court if he is murdered.

> "人常因小事酿成大祸" - host's lesson from Ding Hong's warning about early signs of danger.

> "查历史" - host's frame for Emperor He using precedent to design the anti-Dou solution.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with existing wiki content is recorded. This source backfills the anti-Dou operation that Hanji 767 had previously treated mostly through aftermath.
- Transcript normalization: 丁弘 is treated as [[DingHongEasternHan|丁鸿 / Ding Hong]] because the office, solar-eclipse memorial, and anti-Dou setting match the received He-era figure.
- Narrative tension: the transcript first compresses the Dou brothers as secretly forced to die after reaching their fiefs, then says [[DouGuiEasternHan|窦瑰]] alone survived after Zhang Pu's memorial. The wiki records Dou Gui's survival as the source's resolved case and keeps the compressed death wording source-scoped.
