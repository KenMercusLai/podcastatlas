# VOL.52消化内镜科｜你胃肠功能怎么样？你经历过胃肠镜检查吗？踩过这些误区吗？

## Summary
This [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode features [[LianJingjing|练晶晶]], a digestive-endoscopy clinician at [[ShanghaiEastHospital|上海市东方医院]], on when gastroscopy or colonoscopy is useful, why alternative tests answer narrower questions, and how preparation, anesthesia assessment, biopsy, and follow-up affect the pathway. Its central [[GastrointestinalEndoscopyScreening|screening framework]] is that early gastrointestinal lesions may be silent and that blood tests, tumor markers, fecal occult blood, breath tests, CT, barium studies, capsule endoscopy, and genetic tests cannot simply be treated as interchangeable replacements for direct endoscopy. It also extends [[SedatedGastrointestinalEndoscopy]], [[ColorectalCancerScreening]], and [[EndoscopyPathologyFollowup]].

## Key Claims
- Digestive endoscopy includes gastroscopy, colonoscopy, small-bowel and capsule techniques, duodenoscopy, and endoscopic ultrasonography; the appropriate modality depends on the anatomical and clinical question.
- Early gastrointestinal tumors and polyps may cause no obvious symptoms. Family history, age, bleeding, bowel-habit change, persistent upper-gastrointestinal symptoms, or other risk signals can justify clinician-directed endoscopic assessment rather than waiting for late obstruction or bleeding.
- CT, barium studies, breath tests, tumor markers, fecal occult-blood testing, capsule endoscopy, and genetic tests provide different kinds of evidence. Positive screening signals may route toward endoscopy, while a reassuring result does not necessarily exclude mucosal disease.
- Good bowel preparation is part of examination quality because residual material can conceal small lesions. Fasting and fluid instructions also matter for aspiration safety during anesthesia.
- Painless endoscopy requires pre-anesthesia assessment. Severe cardiopulmonary disease, upper-gastrointestinal obstruction, marked frailty, or an acute respiratory illness may change eligibility or timing, and an escort is needed after sedation.
- Biopsy is a diagnostic step rather than proof of cancer. Endoscopic appearance, tissue pathology, lesion treatment, and risk-specific surveillance complete the interpretation.
- The episode rejects “宿便,” detox, routine colon cleansing, and indiscriminate laxative or enzyme products as clinical health concepts and warns that misuse can injure or discolor the colonic mucosa.
- Cirrhosis can create portal hypertension and esophagogastric varices, making clinician-directed gastroscopy important for assessing severe bleeding risk.
