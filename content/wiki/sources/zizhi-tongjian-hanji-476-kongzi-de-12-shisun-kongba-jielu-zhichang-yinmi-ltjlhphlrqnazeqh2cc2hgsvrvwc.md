# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》476丨孔子的12世孙孔霸，揭露职场隐秘

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Yongguang 1 / 43 BCE under [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]], between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-475-rexue-yushi-xueguangde-gongran-weixie-han-yuandi-lj9b1raz_rjhijw17vxgsp6racuj|Hanji 475]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-477-xihan-shouwei-jianchen-da-huanguan-shi-ruhe-raoluan-chaozheng-de-lq_tjdwttakvkciban4sptyyhuhb|Hanji 477]]. It moves from a modern "立身之本" workplace lesson to [[KongBaWesternHan|孔霸]], a twelfth-generation descendant of [[Confucius|孔子]] and former teacher of Han Yuandi, who accepts honor but repeatedly refuses the chancellorship-level path.

The durable synthesis is that not every promotion is an opportunity. The host treats Kong Ba's refusal less as simple humility than as [[CoreStandingGroundPreservation|立身之本保全]] and [[HighOfficeRefusalAsRiskAvoidance|高位拒受式避险]]: the Kong family's durable resource is Confucian orthodox prestige, while high court office would expose Kong Ba to the unresolved Confucian-Legalist struggle, aristocratic hostility, and [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]]-style eunuch power.

## Key Claims

- In Yongguang 1 / 43 BCE, abnormal cold, crop loss, famine, and mass displacement push senior officials to accept responsibility.
- [[YuDingguoWesternHan|于定国]], [[ShiGaoWesternHan|史高]], and [[XueGuangdeWesternHan|薛广德]] all submit resignations, and Han Yuandi accepts them with honor gifts.
- [[WeiXuanchengWesternHan|韦玄成]] is appointed Yushi Dafu, while [[WangJieWesternHan|王接]] becomes Da Sima and Cheqi Jiangjun.
- Kong Ba had taught Yuan the `Shangshu`; after Yuan's accession he receives Guannei Hou and Jishizhong honors, but refuses higher office by saying his virtue and talent cannot bear such wealth and rank.
- The host interprets the refusal as strategic: Kong Ba's real asset is the Kong family's Confucian lineage legitimacy, not court struggle capacity.
- If Kong Ba became a top minister, he would have to choose between defending Confucian principle against aristocratic Legalist interests or abandoning the very identity that made the Kong family valuable.
- The episode frames eunuch and factional court politics as a danger zone where a teacher of the emperor would be read as an imperial intimate and targeted by rival blocs.
- The opening modern example argues that individuals also need a standing ground: skill, work, and income can preserve resilience when family or employment conditions suddenly change.
