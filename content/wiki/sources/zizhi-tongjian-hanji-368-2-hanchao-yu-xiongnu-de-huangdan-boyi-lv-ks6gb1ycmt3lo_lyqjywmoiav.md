## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-368-1-ewu-zhizhan-beihou-de-tanpan-boyi-lm1l-z62lcewag-x6kxwgyusukh|Hanji 368-1]]'s unresolved Han-Xiongnu negotiation. [[YangXinWesternHan|杨信]] refuses [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] audience protocol and discovers that the promised princely hostage is not an agreed settlement; [[WangWuWesternHan|王乌]] again gains access through ritual accommodation, but the chanyu's new promise to visit Chang'an also proves unreliable.

The durable synthesis is that cultural fluency can secure an audience without securing truthful commitment. The death of a Xiongnu noble in Chang'an, the detention of [[LuChongguoWesternHan|路充国]], renewed raids, and defensive deployment under [[GuoChangWesternHan|郭昌]] and [[ZhaoPonuWesternHan|赵破奴]] turn failed promise verification into a diplomatic and frontier-security crisis.

## Key Claims

- Yang Xin refuses to set aside the Han tally or adopt Xiongnu clothing and face marking, so the chanyu meets him outside rather than admitting him under Xiongnu tent protocol.
- The chanyu rejects Yang Xin's demand for a princely hostage and instead invokes the older heqin bargain of a Han princess, goods, and frontier peace.
- Wang Wu's renewed ritual accommodation produces another apparently favorable message, including a claimed willingness by the chanyu to visit Chang'an and form a fraternal agreement.
- A Xiongnu noble sent to Han dies after becoming ill; Han sends his remains, substantial funeral value, and the high-status envoy Lu Chongguo, whom the chanyu detains.
- The chanyu's assurances are presented as attempts to obtain Han goods rather than implemented commitments to visit Han or send a son as hostage.
- Renewed Xiongnu raids lead Han to appoint Guo Chang as 拔胡将军 and deploy him with Zhao Ponu east of Shuofang.
- The episode's broader judgment is that diplomatic technique matters, but promises require verification and bargaining remains conditioned by credible military capacity.

## Key Quotes

> "汉朝规矩也多了，什么宁死不能放下符节，这是气节。" - the host's framing of Yang Xin's refusal to surrender the imperial tally.

> "我愿意跟汉朝做好兄弟，我亲自去长安住都可以。" - the chanyu's reported promise to Wang Wu, later treated as empty assurance rather than implemented agreement.

> "实力才是谈判桌上最好的语言。" - the episode's concluding interpretation of the defensive deployment and Xiongnu withdrawal.
