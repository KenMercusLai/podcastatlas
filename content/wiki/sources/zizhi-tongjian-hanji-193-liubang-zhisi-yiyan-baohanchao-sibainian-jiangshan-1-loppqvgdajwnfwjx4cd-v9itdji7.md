# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》193｜刘邦之死，遗言保汉朝四百年江山（1）

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode presents [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s death as a transfer-of-power problem. When [[LuZhi|吕雉]] asks who should follow [[XiaoHe|萧何]], Liu Bang names [[CaoShen|曹参]], then pairs [[WangLingHanMinister|王陵]] with [[ChenPing|陈平]], and finally predicts that [[ZhouBo|周勃]] will secure the Liu house; the sequence is treated as a portfolio of complementary roles rather than a simple ranking of talent.

The episode then records Liu Bang's death at [[ChanglePalace|长乐宫]], [[LuWan|卢绾]]'s flight to the [[Xiongnu|匈奴]], and [[LiuYing|刘盈]]'s accession. Its closing branch returns to the dangerous order against [[FanKuai|樊哙]]: suspicion tied to Lu Zhi and [[LiuRuyi|刘如意]] overrides old loyalty, and Liu Bang sends Chen Ping and Zhou Bo to replace and execute the commander.

## Key Claims

- Liu Bang's answer to Lu Zhi orders the post-Xiao He succession as Cao Shen first, Wang Ling with Chen Ping's assistance next, and Zhou Bo as the eventual protector of the Liu house.
- The episode interprets the sequence through role complementarity: Wang Ling supplies firmness, Chen Ping intelligence without sufficient independent force, and Zhou Bo reliability and military weight despite limited eloquence.
- Liu Bang dies at Changle Palace, Liu Ying succeeds him, and Lu Zhi becomes empress dowager, moving the dynasty from founder rule into a young-emperor court.
- Lu Wan's hope of personally explaining himself collapses with Liu Bang's death, after which he enters Xiongnu territory; the episode presents his exile as homesick and insecure rather than triumphant defection.
- Liu Bang's institutional legacy is attributed to delegated specialists: Xiao He for law, [[HanXin|韩信]] for military rules, [[ZhangCang|张苍]] for administrative standards, and [[ShusunTong|叔孙通]] for ritual.
- The final Fan Kuai order shows succession anxiety corroding founding-era trust: Liu Bang accepts an accusation that his old companion may support Lu Zhi's side against Liu Ruyi.
- Chen Ping and Zhou Bo are ordered to remove Fan Kuai from command and execute him in the army; this installment stops before narrating how they handle the order.

## Key Quotes

The supplied episode document is a structured summary rather than a verbatim transcript, so no sentence is promoted as an exact quotation. The central reported judgment is that Zhou Bo, though plain and inarticulate, will be the person who secures the Liu family.
