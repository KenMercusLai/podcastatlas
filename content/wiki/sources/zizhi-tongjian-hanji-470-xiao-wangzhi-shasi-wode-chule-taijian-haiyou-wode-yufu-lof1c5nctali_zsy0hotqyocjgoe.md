# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》470｜萧望之：杀死我的除了太监，还有我的迂腐！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Chuyuan 2 / 47 BCE under [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]], before the later Yongguang conflict around [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]], [[ZhouKanWesternHan|周堪]], and [[LiuXiang|刘向 / 刘更生]]. It gives the fuller death scene for [[XiaoWangzhiWesternHan|萧望之]]: Yuan restores honor and may want to make him chancellor, but Xiao's son [[XiaoJiWesternHan|萧吉]] requests vindication, [[HongGongWesternHan|弘恭]] and Shi Xian turn the case into a punitive lesson, and Xiao commits suicide after a public arrest operation humiliates him.

The durable synthesis is that Xiao Wangzhi's death is not only an evil-favorite story. The episode presents a three-part failure: eunuch favorites understand the ruler and the target's pride, Yuan lacks the strength to protect a teacher once the machinery starts moving, and Xiao's own attachment to reputation makes survival and continued resistance impossible.

## Key Claims

- Yuan re-honors Xiao Wangzhi as Guannei Hou and appears ready to return him to high office, but Xiao's enemies remain near the ruler and inside the information channel.
- Yuan tries to reuse Zhou Kan and Liu Gengsheng, but Hong Gong and Shi Xian downgrade the appointments and later treat Liu Gengsheng's disaster-framed criticism as factional attack.
- Xiao Ji's petition for his father's vindication is politically dangerous because clearing Xiao would also imply that Yuan's earlier handling of Xiao's imprisonment was wrong.
- Hong Gong and Shi Xian exploit Xiao's known pride and concern for moral reputation by presenting temporary arrest as a low-risk lesson rather than a death sentence.
- The arrest operation is staged with a sealed edict, urgent notification, and guards surrounding Xiao's residence, turning legal pressure into public humiliation.
- Xiao's wife urges endurance, but [[ZhuYunWesternHan|朱云]] counsels suicide; the host criticizes Zhu Yun's rigid bookishness and Xiao's inability to preserve life for future struggle.
- Yuan grieves after Xiao's death, blames attendants for killing his teacher, and continues annual sacrifice, but he does not punish Shi Xian or Hong Gong.
- The episode keeps Xiao's reputation complex: he is a major Confucian minister and Qilin Pavilion figure, but the host also recalls earlier harmful conduct toward Zhang Chang, Han Yanshou, and Bing Ji.

## Key Quotes

> "杀死我的除了太监，还有我的迂腐" - the title's compressed judgment on Xiao Wangzhi's death.

> "性格决定命运" - the host's final frame for Xiao Wangzhi and Han Yuandi.
