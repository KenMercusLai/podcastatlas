# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》167｜见证项羽最终之战（2）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the [[GaixiaBattle|垓下之战]] battlefield sequence opened in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-167-jianzheng-xiangyu-zuizhong-zhizhan-1-lm6nwhjjuguhrmwzoz0sqawgxwuv|Hanji 167 part 1]]. The source shifts the focus from [[LiuBang|刘邦]] as political ruler to [[HanXin|韩信]] as coalition commander: Liu Bang's decisive virtue here is that he gives up direct command, while Han Xin uses a three-line deep formation to absorb and exhaust [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s shock attack. Xiang Yu breaks through the first two lines but cannot make the allied army collapse, and by the time he reaches the third line under [[ZhouBo|周勃]] and [[ChaiWu|柴武]], Han Xin's surrounding pressure has turned the battle into Chu's final disaster.

## Key Claims
- Liu Bang's most important correct decision at Gaixia is not a battlefield maneuver but the willingness to hand the allied command to Han Xin after recognizing his own limits in direct large-army field command.
- Han Xin's career reaches its missing peak at Gaixia: after conquering Three Qin, West Wei, Zhao, Yan, and Qi, he finally gets a direct command contest with Xiang Yu.
- The source treats the sixty-ten-thousand-person scale as historically exceptional, pairing Gaixia with large-force turning points such as Changping and Wang Jian's Chu campaign.
- If Xiang Yu won at Gaixia, the source argues he might still retreat south with a core force and use the Yangtze as a defensive line; defeat means even a return to Jiangdong would lack enough troops for a credible defense.
- Han Xin forms three defensive lines: his own thirty-ten-thousand first line with [[KongXi|孔熙]] and [[ChenHe|陈贺]] on the wings, Liu Bang's second line, and the Zhou Bo / Chai Wu third line.
- Xiang Yu's only plausible tactic is to use the Chu army's cohesion and shock force to break the coalition command center quickly, a pattern that recalls his success at the [[PengchengBattle|彭城之战]].
- Han Xin's retreat is controlled rather than panicked; as the center falls back, Kong Xi and Chen He press into the depth and threaten Chu's rear.
- Xiang Yu breaks Han Xin's first line and Liu Bang's second line, but the allied army does not fall into disorder, so the same shock logic that worked at Pengcheng fails at Gaixia.
- The third line holds long enough for the earlier Han Xin and Liu Bang formations to converge again, completing the [[LayeredDepthDefense|纵深分层防御]] and surrounding the exhausted Chu force.
- After the defeat, Xiang Yu retreats into camp with little food and too few troops; the source uses the formula that of the ten-ten-thousand Chu soldiers, eight or nine parts die in battle.
