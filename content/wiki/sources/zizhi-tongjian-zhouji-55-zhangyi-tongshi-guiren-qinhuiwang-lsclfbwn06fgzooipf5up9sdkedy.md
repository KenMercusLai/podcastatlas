# 《资治通鉴·周纪》55丨 张仪痛失贵人秦惠王

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the late [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] branch that [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-54-wo-zun-ni-shi-wang-ni-dang-wo-shi-pi-lvhcz0ban9frlmwshd9vch-q7-ie|Zhouji 54]] left at [[YanState|燕国]] and [[YingSi|秦惠文王]]'s death. Zhang Yi persuades Yan to seek peace with [[QinState|秦国]], then loses his Qin patron under [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] and converts personal danger into a Qin-facing exit plan. The episode also uses a long-route estimate to show that [[ZonghengjiaDiplomacy|纵横家外交]] required stamina, intelligence work, and court-reading, not only clever speech. Its core synthesis is that Zhang Yi's final success is a survival maneuver: he turns [[PatronSuccessionExposure|靠山更替暴露风险]] into [[SelfPreservationAsStateStrategy|以自保包装国策]] and then uses [[YinmouYangmouConversion|阴谋阳谋转换]] to end the Qi-Wei war he helped provoke.

## Key Claims
- Zhang Yi tells the Yan ruler that [[ZhaoState|赵国]] has already entered Qin's orbit and ceded Hejian, so Yan could be attacked by Zhao at Qin's direction if it refuses [[LianhengStrategy|连横]].
- Zhang Yi further claims that Qi and Zhao now behave like Qin's counties and would not dare move troops on their own; Yan accepts the argument, gives Qin five cities near Hengshan, and asks for peace.
- On Zhang Yi's return toward [[Xianyang|咸阳]], he hears that [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] has died and [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] has succeeded, giving Zhang Yi's Qin enemies a chance to attack him.
- The source says the lords respond to the Qin court split by abandoning earlier lianheng commitments and rebuilding [[HezongAlliance|合纵]], showing that Zhang Yi's diplomatic gains depended on court credibility as well as persuasion.
- The host cross-checks the 311 BCE lobbying circuit against [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] and [[Shiji|《史记》]]'s Zhang Yi biography, estimating Zhang Yi's route through Ying, Xinzheng, Linzi, Handan, Ji, and back toward Xianyang at 4,855.4 kilometers.
- The host converts that distance to 9,710.8 li and, at no more than 40 li per day, estimates about 242.7 days of travel, using the calculation to stress the physical and social labor behind zonghengjia work.
- In 310 BCE, Zhang Yi tells Qin Wuwang that [[QiState|齐国]] hates him enough to attack whichever state hosts him, so Qin should send him to [[WeiState|魏国]] and exploit the resulting Qi-Wei war.
- Zhang Yi's pitch promises Qin a larger opportunity: while Qi and Wei are entangled, Qin can attack [[HanState|韩国]], advance into [[SanchuanWarringStates|三川]], and pressure the [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]].
- Qi attacks Wei and [[WeiXiangwang|魏襄王]] becomes afraid, but Zhang Yi sends a subordinate through a Chu diplomatic channel to tell [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]] that the war is exactly what Zhang Yi and Qin Wuwang wanted.
- Qi withdraws once continuing the attack looks like serving Zhang Yi's plan; Zhang Yi then serves as Wei chief minister for a year and dies.
- The host reads the proposed Qin attack on Han as a pretext. Zhang Yi's deeper goal is to leave the dangerous Qin court after his patron's death and find a place where he can end his life safely.
- The episode's final evaluation runs from Zhang Yi's earlier humiliation as a suspected thief, through [[SuQin|苏秦]]'s support and his twenty-two years of Qin service, to his status as a major Warring States strategist, diplomat, and zonghengjia figure.
