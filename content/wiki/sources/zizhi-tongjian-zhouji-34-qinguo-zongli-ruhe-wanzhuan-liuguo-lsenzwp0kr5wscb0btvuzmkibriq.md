# 《资治通鉴·周纪》34丨秦国总理如何玩转六国

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the sequel promised by [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-33-lishi-diyi-shuangwen-nanzhujiao-suqin-lvebjyhvzctjgaevwwoddpvg9nr0|Zhouji 33]]: instead of remaining checked behind [[HanguPass|函谷关]], [[QinState|秦国]] is again active from 332 to 328 BCE. It follows [[GongsunYan|犀首公孙衍]] disrupting the [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] line, Qin's repeated territorial pressure on [[WeiState|魏国]], and [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]'s return-[[PuyangWarringStates|Puyang]] maneuver. The core reading is that Qin's expansion combines force, alliance disruption, hostage signaling, and [[StrategicConcessionDiplomacy|strategic concession diplomacy]], not simple battlefield conquest.

## Key Claims
- In 332 BCE, Qin uses [[GongsunYan|犀首公孙衍]] to draw [[QiState|齐国]] and Wei into an anti-[[ZhaoState|赵国]] arrangement, helping break the still-fragile hezong order.
- [[ZhaoSuhou|赵肃侯]] blames [[SuQin|苏秦]] after the diplomatic failure, and Su Qin leaves Zhao for Yan while saying he will take revenge on Qi.
- Zhao's flooding of the Yellow River dikes forces the Qi-Wei army to withdraw, but the same year Wei still seeks peace with Qin by ceding [[Yinjin|殷晋]].
- Qin keeps pressing Wei after that settlement: it surrounds [[Jiaoyi|焦邑]] and [[QuwoWarringStates|曲沃]] in 330 BCE, receives [[Shaoliang|少梁]] and [[HexiWarringStates|河西]] land, and then takes Fenyin, Pishi, and Jiaoyi in 329 BCE.
- The 329 BCE succession and coup notices widen the map: [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] succeeds [[ChuWeiwang|楚威王]], while [[SongJunYan|宋君偃]] overthrows [[SongTicheng|宋剔成]] and sends him into exile in Qi.
- In 328 BCE, Qin captures [[PuyangWarringStates|蒲阳]] from Wei under Gongzi Hua and Zhang Yi, but Zhang Yi advises [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] to return the city and send a Qin prince as hostage.
- Zhang Yi then turns Qin's apparent generosity into pressure on [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王]], and Wei responds by yielding [[Shangjun|上郡]]'s fifteen counties to Qin.
- The episode treats Zhang Yi's success as "欲擒故纵": a high-risk bet in which a small concession, backed by Qin's stronger position, extracts a much larger territorial gain.
- Zhang Yi's reward is ministerial elevation in Qin, making Zhouji 34 the point where the strategist previewed in earlier [[ZonghengjiaDiplomacy|纵横家外交]] pages becomes a visible Qin decision-maker.
- Qin's later return of Jiaoyi and Quwo becomes the episode's closing problem, suggesting that Qin's strategy is not reducible to keeping every captured place immediately.

## Key Quotes
> "欲擒故纵" - the episode's phrase for Zhang Yi's Puyang-for-Shangjun maneuver.

> "秦国对魏国很宽厚" - Zhang Yi's rhetorical frame for turning Qin's returned territory into Wei's obligation to reciprocate.

> "为什么归还焦邑、曲沃" - the closing question that turns Qin's apparent leniency into the next strategic puzzle.

## Contradictions
- This episode turns the Zhouji 33 warning into a concrete source tension: [[Shiji|《史记》]]'s claim that Qin troops did not dare leave [[HanguPass|函谷关]] for fifteen years after Su Qin's [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] success conflicts with the [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] sequence summarized here, where Qin disrupts alliances, attacks Wei, takes territory, and elevates Zhang Yi within only a few years. The wiki should keep this as a source-tradition conflict rather than flattening either account into a single clean chronology.
