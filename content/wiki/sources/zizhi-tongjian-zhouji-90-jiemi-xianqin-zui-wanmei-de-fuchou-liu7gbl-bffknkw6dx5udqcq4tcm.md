# 《资治通鉴·周纪》90丨揭秘先秦最完美的复仇

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode resolves the setup from [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-89-qinwang-jing-bei-ta-dui-mabaonan-ljugedx8qxhtuzd1ja-xiwxgse19|Zhouji 89]] by showing [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] reclaiming practical authority from [[XuanTaihou|宣太后]], [[WeiRan|魏冉]], and the [[QinFourNoblesCourtPower|秦国四贵权力结构]]. It then turns [[FanJu|范雎]]'s [[YuanjiaoJingongStrategy|远交近攻]] from state strategy into the mechanism of a carefully staged revenge against [[XuJia|须贾]] and [[WeiQiWarringStates|魏齐]].

Fan Ju pressures [[WeiState|魏国]] into sending Xu Jia to Qin, reveals himself from behind the alias 张禄, humiliates Xu Jia in public while sparing his life because of the robe gift, and then demands Wei Qi's head under threat of attacking [[Daliang|大梁]]. The closing shifts the danger to [[ZhaoState|赵国]] because Wei Qi flees to [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] just as [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] dies and [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] succeeds.

## Key Claims
- Qin Zhaoxiang Wang's later ability to remove or marginalize Xuan Taihou and the four nobles suggests he was not wholly powerless before Fan Ju's advice; the larger obstacle was political and familial hesitation.
- Wei Ran's forced move to [[Dingtao|陶 / 定陶]] is treated as deserved court demotion rather than pure injustice, despite [[SimaQian|司马迁]]'s sympathy for his accomplishments.
- Fan Ju's revenge works because personal grievance is embedded in Qin's legitimate pressure on nearby Wei under [[YuanjiaoJingongStrategy|远交近攻]].
- Xu Jia's earlier suspicion and participation in humiliation return as a staged reversal: he misrecognizes Fan Ju as poor, asks him for access to the Qin chancellor, and discovers that the servant-like driver is the chancellor himself.
- Fan Ju spares Xu Jia because the robe gift shows a remaining trace of old feeling, but he still destroys Xu Jia's public dignity before foreign envoys.
- The demand for Wei Qi's head is "public and private" at once: it continues Qin pressure on Wei while serving Fan Ju's personal vengeance.
- Wei Qi's flight to Pingyuanjun turns a Wei internal wrong and Qin diplomatic threat into a Zhao political-asylum problem.
