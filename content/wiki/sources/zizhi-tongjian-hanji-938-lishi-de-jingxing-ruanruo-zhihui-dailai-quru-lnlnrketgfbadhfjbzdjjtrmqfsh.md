# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》938丨历史的警醒！软弱只会带来屈辱

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 187 CE late-Han disorder immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-939-donghan-luanju-de-fengkuang-sanguo-xuqu-lthmvw8ihk96fuez9vamlryzcngy|Hanji 939]]. It follows [[GengBiLateHan|耿鄙]]'s collapsing [[MaTeng|马腾]]-[[HanSui|韩遂]]-[[WangGuoLateHan|王国]] Liangzhou field, [[ZhangWenLateHan|张温]]'s removal and Three Excellencies turnover through [[CuiLieLateHan|崔烈]], [[XuXiangLateHan|许相]], [[DingGongLateHan|丁宫]], and [[CaoSong|曹嵩]], the [[Wuhuan|乌桓]] cavalry supply failure that helps produce [[ZhangChunLateHan|张纯]], [[ZhangJuLateHan|张举]], and [[QiuLijuLateHan|丘力居]]'s Youzhou rebellion, [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]]'s suppression of [[QuXingLateHan|曲星]], and [[ChenShiLateHan|陈寔]]'s moral-authority story.

## Key Claims

- In 187 CE, [[MaTeng|马腾]] appears first as a Liangzhou military officer under [[GengBiLateHan|耿鄙]], then continues into rebellion after Geng Bi's command collapses.
- The episode presents Liangzhou rebellion as more than personal ambition: corrupt frontier governance leaves both Qiang-Hu groups and ordinary locals unable to live, making revolt socially intelligible.
- [[MaTeng|马腾]]'s background is framed as low-resource military ascent: Fufeng origin, claimed descent from Ma Yuan, mixed Qiang lineage through his mother, poverty, woodcutting, and battlefield promotion.
- [[MaTeng|马腾]], [[HanSui|韩遂]], and [[WangGuoLateHan|王国]] form a rebel coalition with Wang Guo as nominal leader, setting up the later Chen Cang failure developed in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-942-yue-youxiu-de-ren-huode-yue-lei-heli-ma-lkwlrcgnt-kxxc2qqz6z6wkg7e5z|Hanji 942]].
- [[ZhangWenLateHan|张温]] is removed as taiwei for ineffective pacification, and the court cycles top offices through [[CuiLieLateHan|崔烈]], [[XuXiangLateHan|许相]], [[DingGongLateHan|丁宫]], and [[CaoSong|曹嵩]] without showing that office churn repairs the crisis.
- [[ZhangWenLateHan|张温]] rejects [[ZhangChunLateHan|张纯]]'s request to command the Wuhuan cavalry and gives command to [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] instead.
- The episode turns the Wuhuan detachment into [[FrontierAuxiliarySupplyFailure|边兵粮饷供应失灵]]: unpaid or unfed auxiliaries desert around Ji, and a failed mobilization asset becomes part of the rebellion environment.
- [[ZhangChunLateHan|张纯]] joins [[ZhangJuLateHan|张举]] and [[QiuLijuLateHan|丘力居]], kills frontier officials, gathers a large force, and helps convert Youzhou disorder into an openly anti-Han challenge.
- [[ZhangJuLateHan|张举]]'s self-coronation and demand that [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] abdicate show [[MingqiLegitimacy|名器合法性]] under humiliation: the Han title still matters, but a local rebel can now publicly claim replacement.
- [[QuXingLateHan|曲星]]'s Changsha rising gives [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] an early office-and-merit branch before his later anti-[[DongZhuo|董卓]] campaigns.
- The court's sale of Guannei marquis status for five million cash extends [[RankSaleDisasterFinance|鬻爵赈灾融资]] into a late-Han crisis-finance and status-monetization case.
- [[ChenShiLateHan|陈寔]] supplies the counter-image to coercive collapse: he reforms a thief through public moral instruction, material aid, and preserved dignity, then dies with extraordinary local mourning.
- The episode closes Hanji 50 / Zizhi Tongjian volume 58 and links the host's long reading project to steady accumulation rather than one-off insight.
