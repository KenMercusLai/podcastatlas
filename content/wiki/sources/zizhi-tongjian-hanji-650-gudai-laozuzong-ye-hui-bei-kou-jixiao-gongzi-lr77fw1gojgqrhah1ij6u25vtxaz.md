# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》650丨古代老祖宗也会被扣绩效工资？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Wang Mang's Tianfeng 2-3 / 15-16 CE crisis before the later 17-22 CE late-[[XinDynasty|新朝]] sequence. It shows [[WangMang|王莽]] guarding power by putting treasuries, grain, and confidential memorial handling under eunuchs and intimates, while constant institutional changes and personal approval requirements turn administration into a bottleneck.

The durable synthesis is that late-Xin breakdown begins before the later rebel and currency crisis notes. Information channels are secret and blocked, petitions sit unanswered, prisons and guards stall, disaster and frontier grain pressure mount, and Wang Mang answers fiscal stress with a fifteen-rank salary system that varies pay by harvest, disaster, and palace supply conditions. The source's "performance pay" analogy is useful only as an interpretation of incentive linkage; the episode's stronger governance claim is that overcomplex salary design under weak administrative capacity leaves officials unpaid and pushes them toward bribery.

## Key Claims

- Wang Mang's fear of losing power leads him to put treasury, money, grain, and confidential memorial handling under eunuchs or close attendants rather than ordinary administrative channels.
- Frequent institutional changes and matters requiring Wang Mang's direct approval create a [[SecrecyDrivenAdministrativeBottleneck|secrecy-driven administrative bottleneck]]: affairs pile up, memorial handlers block information, petitioners cannot return, prisoners wait for amnesty, and capital guards are not rotated for years.
- Fiscal and disaster pressure is already severe in 15-16 CE: frontier armies consume grain, Wuyuan and Dai suffer disaster and banditry, northern Handan floods, Guandong earthquakes, and heavy snow kills bamboo and cypress.
- [[WangYiXinGeneral|王邑 / 王毅]] asks to retire after the earthquake, but Wang Mang refuses and explains the disaster through classical and omen language rather than treating it as a reason for ministerial responsibility.
- Wang Mang temporarily pays top officials only small amounts of cloth, then announces a return to formal salaries once the fiscal emergency eases.
- The fifteen-rank salary system ranges from low-level clerks to the four assistants, and ties pay to harvest quality, local disaster ratios, and palace food supply.
- The host reads the system as an early "performance pay" analogy, but emphasizes the implementation failure: complex calculation means officials still do not receive pay, making abuse of office and bribery more likely.

## Key Quotes

> "古代绩效制度的鼻祖" - the host's modern analogy for linking official pay to harvest, disaster, and supply conditions.

> "一阳九之厄，百六之会" - the disaster-cycle language Wang Mang uses to explain fiscal strain.

> "官员们依旧拿不到俸禄" - the implementation failure that pushes the episode from policy design to corruption risk.
