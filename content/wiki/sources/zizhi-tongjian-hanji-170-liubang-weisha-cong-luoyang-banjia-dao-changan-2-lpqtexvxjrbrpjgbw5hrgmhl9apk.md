# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》170｜刘邦为啥从洛阳搬家到长安（2）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the title promise left open by [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-170-liubang-weisha-cong-luoyang-banjia-dao-changan-1-lpioq8izitba7uhbdybp7i03djdy|Hanji 170 part 1]]. It first contrasts [[DingGong|丁公]] with [[JiBu|季布]], showing how [[LiuBang|刘邦]] used one former enemy as a pardonable loyalty example and the other as a warning against betraying one's office. It then moves from [[Luoyi|洛阳 / 洛邑]] to [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]]: [[LouJing|楼敬]] and [[ZhangLiang|张良]] persuade Liu Bang that a war-made dynasty needs the defensive geography, resources, and transport logic of [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] rather than Zhou-style symbolic centrality.

## Key Claims
- Ding Gong once let Liu Bang escape during the [[PengchengBattle|彭城之战]], but after Xiang Yu's fall Liu Bang executes him publicly for failing to remain loyal to [[XiangYu|项羽]].
- The episode reads Ding Gong's execution as statecraft rather than simple ingratitude: Liu Bang is defining a founding norm that office-bound loyalty matters more than private favor.
- The host also offers a source-scoped suspicion that Ding Gong may have spared Liu Bang for promised private benefit, making his later visit to Liu Bang look like a possible demand for repayment rather than pure old friendship.
- [[LouJing|楼敬]] argues that Liu Bang cannot imitate Zhou's Luoyang choice because Zhou's authority rested on generations of accumulated virtue and a settled feudal order, while Han has just emerged from mass war.
- Lou Jing presents Guanzhong as a secure and fertile base: protected by mountains, the Yellow River, and passes, able to preserve the Qin heartland even if the eastern states rebel.
- Most ministers favor Luoyang because they come from eastern regions and point to Zhou precedent, Chenggao, Xiaoshan, Mianchi, the Yellow River, Yi River, and Luo River as defensive supports.
- Zhang Liang confirms Lou Jing's view from a practical-strategic angle: Luoyang has some terrain but too little hinterland, poorer land, and exposure to attack from several sides.
- Zhang Liang describes Guanzhong as protected by Xiao/Hangu on the left and Long/Shu on the right, with fertile land, Ba-Shu resources to the south, northern pastoral supply, and river transport through the Yellow River and Wei River.
- Liu Bang accepts Zhang Liang's endorsement immediately, travels west the same day, fixes the capital at Chang'an, and rewards Lou Jing as langzhong with the Fengchun Jun title.
- The episode ends by previewing Zhang Liang's post-founding self-protection problem: once the empire is settled, even the most important strategist must think about the danger of being discarded after success.
