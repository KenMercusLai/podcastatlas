# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》402-2｜树敌无数的酷吏杜周，为何最终得以善终？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills a Han Zhao-era transition between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-401-suihong-zhiming-hanzhaodi-tuiwei-rangxian-huoguang-zaile-ll6pyzpjl0mlcfn0g1zjot2wf8s8|Hanji 401]] and the Fu Jiezi Western Regions sequence. It first uses [[DuZhouWesternHan|杜周]] and [[DuYannianWesternHan|杜延年]] to explain how harsh legal service, court survival, and later moderation could coexist in Western Han official life.

The second half turns to northeastern frontier management. [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]] consults [[ZhaoChongguoWesternHan|赵充国]] and [[FanMingyouWesternHan|范明友]] after [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] prepares to attack [[Wuhuan|乌桓]]; when Xiongnu withdraws, Fan follows Huo's fallback instruction and strikes the exhausted Wuhuan instead. The durable synthesis is that Huo converts an otherwise failed interception into a punitive demonstration against a rebellious dependent group and an indirect warning to Xiongnu.

## Key Claims

- Du Yannian's ability to mediate around [[TianQianqiuWesternHan|田千秋]] is traced back to family experience under his father Du Zhou.
- Du Zhou is presented as a Han Wudi-era cruel official tied to [[SimaQian|司马迁]]'s memory of imprisonment, humiliation, torture, and castration after the Li Ling case.
- Du Zhou's legal logic is summarized as ruler-will-as-law: he treats the emperor's intent as the decisive legal standard rather than fixed statutes.
- Du Zhou's survival is explained through political adaptation: although he offended many officials and had serious wealth problems, he later became more mild and retired safely.
- Wuhuan is introduced as a post-Donghu frontier group relocated by Han Wudi near five northeastern commanderies to watch Xiongnu while remaining vulnerable to Xiongnu pressure.
- Zhao Chongguo argues against rescuing Wuhuan from Xiongnu because Wuhuan has recently harmed Han borders and because intervention after "barbarians" fight each other may create needless trouble.
- Huo Guang appoints Fan Mingyou as Duliao general with twenty thousand cavalry, but instructs him not to return empty-handed; after Xiongnu retreats, Fan attacks the exhausted Wuhuan, kills three Wuhuan kings, and claims six thousand-plus heads.

## Key Quotes

> "君主的旨意就是法律" - the episode's summary of Du Zhou's answer to accusations of unfair judging.

> "不要空手而归" - Huo Guang's operational instruction to Fan Mingyou before the campaign.
