# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》169｜盘点刘邦登基前不为人知的故事（2）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-169-pandian-liubang-dengjiqian-buweirenzhi-de-gushi-1-liwkaygjtyafxxpdwzq9pdhuzroi|Hanji 169 part 1]] by moving from [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s accession into the first arrangements of [[WesternHanDynasty|西汉]] rule. It covers [[LuZhi|吕雉]] and [[LiuYing|刘盈]]'s founding positions, southern grants to [[WuRui|吴芮]] and [[WuzhuMinyueKing|无诸 / Wuzhu]], the return of displaced people and demobilized soldiers, the Luoyang south-palace explanation of Han victory through [[SpecialistDelegationLeadership|专才分工式领导力]], and [[HanXin|韩信]]'s difficult conversion from Qi king to Chu king. Its core synthesis is that founding order is not one ceremony but a layered sequence of family legitimation, regional title settlement, social repair, personnel doctrine, and containment of overmighty contributors.

## Key Claims
- Liu Bang builds an accession altar on the north bank of the Sishui and becomes emperor/Han Gaozu; Lu Zhi becomes empress, Liu Ying crown prince, and Liu Bang's mother is posthumously honored as Zhaoling Furen.
- Liu Bang grants the former Hengshan king Wu Rui the new Changsha kingship because Wu Rui's [[Baiyue|百越]] force had helped the anti-Qin campaign.
- The source says the former Yue king named in the transcript as "吴珠" is a [[Goujian|勾践]] descendant scattered after [[ChuState|楚国]] destroyed [[YueState|越国]], and Liu Bang makes him [[Minyue|闽越]] king over Minzhong after he supports Han during the Chu-Han struggle.
- Liu Bang sets the capital at [[Luoyi|洛阳 / 洛邑]] and orders displaced people to return home, recover property and fields, and receive official restraint rather than harassment.
- Veteran policy is tiered by military rank: higher-rank veterans receive county support, while lower-rank veterans and families receive tax and labor exemptions.
- At the South Palace banquet, Liu Bang rejects a reward-sharing-only explanation and says Han won because he could use [[ZhangLiang|张良]], [[XiaoHe|萧何]], and Han Xin in their respective specialist domains.
- The episode reads that statement as Liu Bang praising the three "人中俊杰" while also defining his own unique leadership ability: he can coordinate people whose individual abilities exceed his own.
- Han Xin's move from Qi king to Chu king is treated not as simple home-region reward but as [[HostileTerritoryRoyalContainment|敌意封国安置]], because Chu people may resent him for forcing [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s death.
- Han Xin's reward to the old woman who fed him and appointment of the man who humiliated him as zhongwei are read as more than magnanimity; in the hostile Chu setting, the zhongwei appointment becomes a personal-security move.
- The final preview of an island suicide and followers' suicides is left unidentified by the available transcript.
