# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1005丨孙策注定早逝，是性格决定命运？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode uses [[SunCe|孙策]]'s solo hunting habit after taking [[KuaijiCommandery|会稽郡]] to turn personal style into a leadership-risk problem. [[YuFan|虞翻]] warns him through the "white dragon in fish clothes" and Liu Bang white-snake stories that a leader cannot treat ordinary freedom and high office as compatible security postures. The second half backfills [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s early Xu-court consolidation: attacking [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]], managing [[YuanShao|袁绍]] through a great-general title concession, and beginning the adviser channel through [[XunYu|荀彧]], [[XunYou|荀攸]], and [[GuoJia|郭嘉]].

## Key Claims
- In 196 CE, after defeating [[WangLangLateHan|王朗]], [[SunCe|孙策]] makes himself governor of [[KuaijiCommandery|会稽郡]] and keeps Wang Lang's merit officer [[YuFan|虞翻]] in office.
- Yu Fan's warning is not simply anti-hunting. It argues that rank and mission change acceptable risk: Sun Ce's light clothing, sudden departures, and lack of guard preparation expose both authority and life.
- The episode frames Sun Ce's later death as a consequence of repeated habits and insufficient risk consciousness rather than pure accident.
- After [[CaoCao|曹操]] moves [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] from Luoyang to [[Xudu|许县 / 许都]], [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]]'s failed obstruction breaks their relationship; Cao Cao attacks Yang Feng, who flees south to [[YuanShu|袁术]].
- Emperor Xian's court reproaches [[YuanShao|袁绍]] for failing to assist the emperor, then appoints him taiwei and Marquis of Ye.
- Yuan Shao resents being ranked below Cao Cao's great-general title, so Cao Cao yields the title and accepts sikong plus acting chariots-and-cavalry general as a strategic buffer.
- The adviser section likely contains transcript errors: "徐玉", "徐悠", and "过家" are treated cautiously as [[XunYu|荀彧]], [[XunYou|荀攸]], and [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] based on the office sequence and the surrounding Hanji 1006 branch.
- The episode's two threads share a risk theme: Sun Ce fails to reduce personal exposure, while Cao Cao reduces strategic exposure by conceding visible rank and improving personnel channels.
