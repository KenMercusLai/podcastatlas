# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》381-1｜汉武帝如何成为西汉最长寿的皇帝

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] short episode backfills a 95 BCE Taishi 2 layer for [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]. It uses Wudi's unusually long life and reign to compare Western Han imperial lifespans, count the [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] chanyu he outlasted, and then moves into late-reign administration through [[DuZhouWesternHan|杜周]]'s death, [[BaoShengzhiWesternHan|鲍圣之]]'s appointment, and the construction of the [[BaiquIrrigationProject|白渠]].

The durable synthesis is that Wudi's longevity was politically consequential, not only biographical. Living past six Xiongnu rulers made his reign a long strategic horizon, while the same late reign still depended on harsh legal officials, surveillance appointments, and water-control projects that translated imperial capacity into everyday agricultural benefit.

## Key Claims

- Han Wudi is presented as the longest-lived ruler among the fifteen Western Han emperors from [[LiuBang|刘邦]] to [[HanPingdi|汉平帝]], while the episode says Western Han emperors averaged under thirty-eight years of life.
- The episode says Wudi acceded at sixteen in 141 BCE and lived long enough to outlast six Xiongnu chanyu, though not the seventh, Hulugan Chanyu.
- The Xiongnu succession sequence is used to make Wudi's reign length concrete: Junchen, Yizhixie, Wuwei, Er, Hulihu, and Qiedihou pass before Hulugan succeeds.
- Hulugan Chanyu's accession is presented as a succession compromise after Qiedihou's elder son initially avoids the throne and the younger son is temporarily raised, then yields.
- In 95 BCE, sixty-two-year-old Wudi tours Huizhong, Du Zhou dies, and Bao Shengzhi is appointed Yushi Dafu.
- Du Zhou is summarized as a harsh, opportunistic cruel official whose severe case-handling looked like loyal maintenance of order from Wudi's viewpoint.
- Bai Gong's proposed two-hundred-li canal from Gukou to Liyang draws Jing River water into the Wei region, irrigates more than 4,500 qing, and benefits local farmers in an agricultural society where water is decisive.

## Key Quotes

> "熬死了六位单于" - the episode's hook for making Han Wudi's reign length concrete.

> "农耕时代水非常重要" - the closing lesson attached to Baiqu.
