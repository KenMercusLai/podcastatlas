# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》302-2｜狂给老板泼冷水，伍被有多敢说？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode fills the case-opening stage between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-302-1-xihan-shuzhi-neidou-jieguo-kengdie-ye-kengye-ln6rfk8nzoyeqxduik_edlqqiyug|Hanji 302-1]]'s household conflict and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-303-1-huainanwang-ruhe-bei-jiujiexin-bisi-de-ljtqn-nrxyf8yqx-yowsainkzmjc|Hanji 303-1]]'s proliferating plans. After a letter attributed to Liu Jian exposes [[LiuAn|淮南王刘安]] and crown prince [[LiuQianHuainanCrownPrinceWesternHan|刘迁]], the court sends the matter through the廷尉 to Henan; Liu An concludes that concealment is failing and asks [[WuBeiWesternHan|伍被]] to evaluate armed resistance.

Wu Bei rejects the project through two bounded historical comparisons. [[LiuBiWuKing|吴王刘濞]] had wealth and a larger coalition yet died and destroyed his line, while [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] and [[WuGuang|吴广]] rose under Qin extraction and popular grievance that the episode says do not transfer to Han Wudi's more consolidated order. Even after Liu An answers with passes, armies, and alliances, Wu Bei says he sees only disaster, adds that usable Huainan leaders have already been arrested, and treats troops on paper as no substitute for political support, command talent, or a matched historical setting.

## Key Claims

- A denunciatory letter attributed to Liu Jian brings the alleged plans of Liu An and Liu Qian into formal central investigation through the廷尉 and Henan administration.
- The source says Shen Qing's family grievance against Liu An's father and his access to [[GongsunHong|公孙弘]] help intensify scrutiny, joining private hostility to official procedure.
- Liu An responds to widening exposure by considering armed resistance and asks Wu Bei whether Liu Bi's rising in the Rebellion of the Seven States was right or wrong.
- Wu Bei answers that Liu Bi's wealth and status did not prevent death and lineage destruction, and he urges Liu An not to reproduce that outcome.
- Liu An claims Liu Bi failed through bad operational choices and proposes controlling Chenggao, Huanyuan, Yique, Wuguan, the Three Rivers approaches, Shandong recruitment, and northern allies.
- Wu Bei replies that he sees harm rather than benefit and says Huainan's people capable of commanding others were already removed in an earlier case.
- Liu An then compares his claimed pool of more than one hundred thousand military-age people with Chen Sheng and Wu Guang's small beginning.
- Wu Bei rejects the comparison because Qin's taxation, conscription, corvée, and popular resentment created a response environment unlike the current Han order; he also rates the current central command stronger than Zhang Han and Yang Xiong.

## Key Quotes

> “臣见其祸，未见其福也。” - Wu Bei's compact rejection of Liu An's operational case.

> “大王以陈胜、吴广论之，臣以为过矣。” - the source's formulation of why the Qin-collapse analogy does not transfer.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with the adjacent Huainan notes was found. This episode explains why Liu An resumes planning after the temporary retreat in Hanji 302-1 and before the more elaborate schemes in Hanji 303-1.
- The supplied file is a compact structured summary rather than a transcript or primary-source dossier. The denunciatory letter, routing between officials, Shen Qing's identity and motive, private dialogue, troop counts, place assignments, arrest history, court popularity, and commander comparison remain episode-attributed.
- “刘建” is not normalized to [[LiuJianJiangduKingWesternHan|江都王刘建]]. The source describes a complainant tied to the Huainan household dispute, while the existing canonical Liu Jian is a separate Jiangdu king; the supplied note does not establish that they are the same person.
- The claim that Han Wudi “施行恩惠” and could move the population is preserved as Wu Bei's source-framed argument, not adopted as a complete evaluation of Wudi-era taxation, war burden, or popular welfare.
- Liu An's named officers, routes, forces, alliances, and claimed manpower show how he defended the proposal; they do not establish that those resources were actually available or that his plan was executable.
