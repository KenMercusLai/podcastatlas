# VOL.209 别为“功能性”上头，你以为的“精准养生”其实越补越糟！聊聊代餐粉、电解质等等

## Summary
This [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode has host [[Atang|阿汤]] and guest [[YuWanrouFunctionalFoodGuest|余婉柔]] turn functional foods and supplements into a [[TargetedSupplementNeedAssessment|targeted supplement need-assessment]] problem rather than a consumption ladder. The discussion separates drugs, foods for special medical purposes, special dietary foods, blue-hat health foods, ordinary foods, and market-facing "functional foods" through [[FunctionalFoodRegulatoryIdentity|functional-food regulatory identity]], then applies that frame to protein powder, electrolyte drinks, fish oil, collagen, meal replacements, multivitamins, and viral wellness drinks. Its practical synthesis is that supplements may help in specific disease, life-stage, exercise, deficiency, or diet-gap contexts, but [[FunctionalFoodMarketingClaimSkepticism|functional-food marketing claims]] should be checked against product identity, ingredient lists, nutrition panels, applicable populations, and qualified care.

## Key Claims
- [[FunctionalFoodRegulatoryIdentity]] matters because drugs, foods for special medical purposes, special dietary foods, blue-hat health foods, ordinary foods, and "functional foods" do not carry the same evidence, registration, label, or use-context burden.
- [[TargetedSupplementNeedAssessment]] starts from the person's body state, diet pattern, disease status, life stage, medication use, and actual nutrient gap rather than from price, novelty, naturalness, or stronger-sounding dosage.
- [[FunctionalFoodMarketingClaimSkepticism]] is needed for claims such as "natural statin," medical-grade, nano-scale, high absorption, anti-inflammatory, collagen beauty, white-person Chinese medicine, and one-dose social-media miracles.
- Protein powder, collagen, and [[MealReplacementNutritionBoundary|meal replacements]] cannot be swapped as equivalent protein products because they differ in purpose, nutrient profile, satiety role, and whether they replace a meal or fill a protein gap.
- [[ElectrolyteDrinkUseBoundary]] depends on sweat loss, diarrhea, vomiting, exercise duration, heat, sodium load, blood pressure, kidney function, and sugar-energy content rather than on treating electrolyte water as everyday hydration.
- Fish oil can fill an omega-3 dietary gap for some people, but [[FishOilNaturalStatinBoundary]] rejects turning retail fish oil into a substitute for prescription, indication-based lipid treatment.
- [[NutritionLabelSugarTraps]] extend beyond desserts: meal replacements, energy bars, turmeric-lemon drinks, sleep drinks, and other health-coded products may still carry substantial carbohydrate, sugar, energy density, or sweeteners.
- Older adults, pregnant people, adolescents, exercisers, people with diabetes, people after surgery, and patients with disease states are different use cases; the episode keeps those decisions inside needs assessment and clinician or nutritionist guidance.
