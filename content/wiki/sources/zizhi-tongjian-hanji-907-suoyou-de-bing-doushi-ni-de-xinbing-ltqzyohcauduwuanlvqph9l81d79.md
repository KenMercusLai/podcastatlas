# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》907丨所有的病都是你的心病

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 176-177 CE late-Han layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-908-nijing-chu-qiangzhe-xialu-chu-yongzhe-gudu-chu-zhizhe-lqih1dradhzi0mipxsaiedd52dlj|Hanji 908]]. It opens with a source-scoped self-cultivation claim that many "heart illnesses" come from misreading people, overinvesting in weak relationships, and lacking high-quality solitude, then turns to [[CaoLuanLateHan|曹鸾]]'s failed memorial defending party figures. [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] responds by arresting and killing Cao Luan and expanding party-prohibition liability to students, old subordinates, close kin, and five-degree relatives, making the episode a case of [[PartyProhibitionKinshipExpansion|党锢亲属牵连扩大]], [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|君臣反馈失灵]], and disaster-omen remonstrance through [[NaturalDisasterPoliticalOmen|自然灾害政治征兆]].

## Key Claims

- The opening personal lesson treats "心病" as source-scoped inner confusion: unclear judgment about people can turn ordinary relationships into rumination, resentment, and energy loss.
- The host frames high-quality solitude, historical reading, and political reading as ways to sharpen social judgment rather than as withdrawal from the world.
- In 176 CE, [[CaoLuanLateHan|曹鸾]] memorializes for party figures, arguing that many are elders, moral persons, and talented士大夫 who should be usable by the state.
- Cao Luan links water disasters, drought, epidemic, and broader disorder to the court's unjust treatment of party figures, using the omen logic preserved here as [[NaturalDisasterPoliticalOmen|自然灾害政治征兆]] rather than as a verified causal claim.
- [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] treats the memorial as an attack on imperial authority, orders Cao Luan arrested, and has him brought to [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]], where he dies under interrogation.
- The punishment expands from named party figures to students, followers, old subordinates, fathers, sons, brothers, and eventually relatives within five degrees of mourning, creating [[PartyProhibitionKinshipExpansion|党锢亲属牵连扩大]].
- The episode reads that expansion as a blocked career channel for the士人 world and as a root of later anti-eunuch revenge, which later sources partly repair through [[PartyProhibitionKinshipNarrowing|党锢亲属牵连缩限]] and [[PartyBanCrisisAmnesty|党锢危机赦免]].
- The 176 office notices place [[LiuKuanLateHan|刘宽]], [[YangCiLateHan|杨赐]], and [[YuanWeiLateHan|袁隗]] in the senior-office churn before later omen and remonstrance episodes.
- The episode backfills an early shared-study branch: [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] and a young [[LiuBei|刘备]] study under [[LuZhiLateHan|卢植]] before their later frontier, Youzhou, and Xuzhou roles diverge.
- The 177 material combines general amnesty, locusts, local-official review, [[YangQiuLateHan|阳球]]'s harsh-law case, [[Xianbei|鲜卑]] raids, false Xuanling filial sons, and early literary-artistic appointment favor around [[HongduMenxue|鸿都门学]].

## Key Quotes

> "低质量的社交不如高质量的独处。" — opening personal-cultivation frame.

> "所有的病都是你的心病。" — title and source-scoped interpretive frame.

> "这时候还会有人胆敢给汉灵帝上书吗" — closing question after Cao Luan's punishment.
