# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》371-1｜汉朝第一位魔术师来自非洲？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode uses an embassy from [[ParthianEmpire|安息]] in 105 BCE to connect long-distance diplomacy with entertainment and imperial display. The envoys bring ostrich eggs and a performer described as a “黎轩善眩人”; the host interprets the phrase as a magician from [[Alexandria]], then places this foreign act beside [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s tours, gifts, banquets, warehouses, exotic animals, and 角抵百戏.

The durable synthesis is that performance and material abundance could make state capacity visible to visiting envoys. The episode also preserves a counterpoint: Western Regions polities might attend the Han court while still fearing the nearer [[Xiongnu|匈奴]], so spectacular hospitality did not automatically produce exclusive political allegiance.

## Key Claims

- In 105 BCE Han envoys return from Parthia with Parthian envoys who present ostrich eggs and a “黎轩善眩人.”
- The host glosses 眩人 as an illusionist or magician and identifies 黎轩 with Alexandria, making the anonymous performer an early African entertainer in China in the episode's interpretation.
- Other Western Regions envoys also travel to Chang'an with gifts, giving the court a multi-polity diplomatic audience.
- Han Wudi brings foreign envoys on tours through populous and prosperous places, distributes gifts, hosts banquets, displays stored goods, and stages 角抵百戏 and exotic animals.
- The performance repertoire described by the episode includes juggling balls or swords, plate spinning, narrow-passage feats, suspended balls, fire breathing, pole and rope acts, 鱼龙曼延, and horsemanship.
- Grapes and alfalfa are brought from the Dayuan region and planted near imperial residences, showing that western contact moved crops as well as envoys and performers.
- Western Regions states remain more deferential to nearby Xiongnu envoys despite their Han visits; the closing succession notice moves from Wuwei Chanyu to the young Wushilu Chanyu.
