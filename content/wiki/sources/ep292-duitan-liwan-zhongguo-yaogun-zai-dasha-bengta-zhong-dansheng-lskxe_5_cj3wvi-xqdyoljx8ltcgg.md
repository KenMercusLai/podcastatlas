## Summary
This [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] conversation with music critic [[LiWanMusicCritic|李皖]] uses [[CuiJian|崔健]]'s 1986 performance of 《一无所有》 to interpret forty years of Chinese rock. It argues that [[ChineseRockSocialFormation|Chinese rock formed socially]] through reform-era opening, cultural collision, personal awakening, and local experience rather than by importing a finished genre. [[MusicAccessInfrastructure|Music-access infrastructure]] widened what musicians could hear and make, while [[EraCollectiveResonance|era-level collective resonance]] helps explain both the 1990s peak and why technically stronger contemporary work can receive less mass attention.

## Key Claims
- Chinese rock was not clearly named at its symbolic 1986 beginning; listeners first recognized a new sound, while the category became more public around 1989-1990.
- [[ChineseRockSocialFormation]] joins personal truth, expression of shared historical experience, and emotional force; style alone is not a sufficient definition.
- Radio, flexi discs, copied cassettes, cut-out imports, internet downloads, instruments, studios, and record-company networks formed [[MusicAccessInfrastructure]].
- [[BlackPantherBand|黑豹]], [[TangDynastyBand|唐朝乐队]], [[ZhangChuMusician|张楚]], and [[DouWei|窦唯]] exemplify distinct urban, spiritual, social, and private expressive registers.
- [[MagicStoneRecords|魔岩唱片]] and related production networks acted as resource platforms and informal schools rather than merely distribution labels.
- The 1994-era high point depended on [[EraCollectiveResonance]] among creators, listeners, and social transformation; later artistic improvement does not automatically recreate that force.
- [[SmallVenueCulturalContinuity]] describes the present shift from mass public event to Live House and niche-community creation without equating reduced scale with decline.

## Key Quotes
> "从生活中野生出来的" — 李皖对早期中国摇滚在产业与知识体系不成熟时形成方式的概括。

> "时代之歌" — 个人真实之外，摇滚试图表达同时代人共同经验的标准。

> "万众呼应" — 节目用来对比早期公共高峰与当代分散现场的尺度。
