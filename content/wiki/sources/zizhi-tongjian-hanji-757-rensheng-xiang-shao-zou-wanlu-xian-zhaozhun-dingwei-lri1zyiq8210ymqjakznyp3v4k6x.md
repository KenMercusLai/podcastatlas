## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 86-87 CE [[EmperorZhangOfHan|汉章帝]] layer before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-758-suoyou-gaoqingshang-beihou-doushi-fencungan-lr-6tzffmwdtzidkf3y8ule35qf7|Hanji 758]]. It uses [[GuoGongEasternHanJurist|郭公]] and his legal family training to build a [[CareerPositioningThroughFit|career-positioning-through-fit]] lesson, then turns to [[CaoBaoEasternHan|曹褒]]'s commission to revise Han rites from [[ShusunTong|叔孙通]]'s older `Han Yi`.

The frontier branch follows [[FuYuEasternHan|富裕]] failing to split Qiang and Hu groups, overextending before reinforcements arrive, and dying at Sandou Valley. [[ZhangYuEasternHan|张于]] then kills [[MiWuQiangLeader|迷雾]] and many Qiang leaders after surrender contact, which helps push [[MiTangQiangLeader|弥唐]] into a broader alliance through marriages and hostages. The closing court notices record Yuan An and Ren Kui's promotions, a Qi royal-family accusation case, Zhangdi's era change for auspicious signs, and [[HeChangEasternHan|何畅 / 何敞]] warning that strange signs should not be read only as good omens.

## Key Claims

- The episode's "positioning" frame says effort works only when role choice fits ability, temperament, available resources, and inherited training.
- Guo Gong's lenient legal review is presented as professional fit: he inherits legal learning, serves as tingwei, and finds forty-one heavy-penalty clauses that can be treated more lightly.
- Zhangdi chooses a single responsible ritual compiler because broad scholar consultation might improve coverage but could stall execution through endless disagreement.
- Cao Bao's task is not pure invention; Zhangdi gives him Shusun Tong's twelve `Han Yi` chapters and asks him to update loose early-Han precedent into a more current ritual code.
- The Shusun Tong aside contrasts Sima Guang's moral criticism with Sima Qian's more sympathetic reading of adaptive ritual statecraft.
- Fu Yu's failed divide-and-watch strategy and premature pursuit show frontier action exceeding real control conditions.
- Zhang Yu's banquet killing produces revenge but not settlement: after Mi Wu's death, Mi Tang turns intergroup reconciliation, marriage, and hostages into a stronger rebel coalition.
- He Chang's omen warning treats auspicious signs as politically ambiguous rather than automatically favorable.

## Key Quotes

> "尺有所短，寸有所长" - the episode's compact formula for matching role to strength.

> "路边建房三年不成" - Zhangdi's reason for avoiding open-ended ritual-committee debate.

> "大直若诎，道固委蛇" - Sima Qian's sympathetic frame for Shusun Tong's adaptive conduct.
