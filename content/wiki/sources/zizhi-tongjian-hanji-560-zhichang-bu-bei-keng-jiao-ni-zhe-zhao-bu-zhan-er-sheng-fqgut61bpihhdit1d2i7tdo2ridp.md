# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》560丨职场不被坑，教你这招不战而胜！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills a short interpersonal story about [[ZhaiFangjinWesternHan|翟方进]] and [[HuChangWesternHan|胡长]] under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]. When Hu Chang envies and publicly belittles Zhai's stronger reputation in their shared [[Chunqiu|《春秋》]] teaching role, Zhai does not retaliate; he sends disciples to attend Hu's lectures, ask questions, and record Hu's interpretations.

The durable synthesis is that Zhai's response turns status threat into status recognition. By treating Hu as worth learning from, Zhai satisfies the psychological need behind the attack, making continued hostility harder to sustain and illustrating [[RespectBasedConflictDeescalation|respect-based conflict de-escalation]] rather than direct victory over a rival.

## Key Claims

- Hu Chang's attacks are framed as jealousy over Zhai Fangjin's superior learning and reputation in a shared court teaching setting.
- Zhai refuses the expected reciprocal attack and instead creates public evidence that he respects Hu's scholarship.
- Sending disciples to listen, consult, and record Hu's arguments converts respect from private intention into repeated visible conduct.
- Hu's eventual shame and reversal show that an antagonist's psychological need for recognition can matter more than winning the argument.
- The host generalizes the story into workplace conflict advice: hard confrontation is not the only option when softer tactics can reduce opposition.
- The episode also warns against careless personal judgment, tying conflict prevention to disciplined speech.

## Key Quotes

> "反彈琵琶，攻心為上" - the host's phrase for Zhai Fangjin's indirect conflict strategy.

> "不要張嘴就對一個人亂下評論" - the closing warning against casual personal judgment.
