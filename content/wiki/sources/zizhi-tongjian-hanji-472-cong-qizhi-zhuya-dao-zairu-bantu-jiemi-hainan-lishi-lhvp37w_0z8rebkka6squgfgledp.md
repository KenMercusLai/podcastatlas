# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》472｜从“弃置珠崖”到“再入版图”，揭秘海南历史

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Chuyuan 3-5 / 46-44 BCE under [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]]. It centers Yuan's decision to abolish [[ZhuyaCommandery|珠崖郡]] after repeated local killing and rebellion, then broadens into Yuan-era thrift, court-cost reduction, Confucian education expansion, lighter punishment, and appointments of [[ZhouKanWesternHan|周堪]] and [[ZhangMengWesternHan|张猛 / 张萌]].

The durable synthesis is that Zhuya becomes an early island-frontier case of [[CapacityBoundFrontierCommitment|capacity-bound frontier commitment]]. Yuan chooses famine relief, fiscal restraint, and reduced burdens over prestige-driven punitive expedition, while the later Hepu/Jiaozhi/Ma Yuan notices show that abolished commandery rule did not erase all contact with Hainan but did reduce direct administrative control into looser, more symbolic attachment.

## Key Claims

- In 46 BCE, repeated unrest in Zhuya leads Yuan to ask whether Han should suppress, garrison, or abandon the commandery.
- Yuan argues that Guandong famine, empty stores, and commoner hunger matter more than defending prestige through a distant campaign.
- The abolition order leaves room for people willing to attach to the interior to be settled, while those unwilling are not forced.
- The source distinguishes formal attachment from effective control: after the reduction from two commanderies and sixteen counties to one commandery/one county and then county abolition, Han control over Hainan becomes much thinner.
- Later Eastern Han contact under [[MaYuanEasternHan|马援]] is presented as restored connection and tribute, not full restoration of direct island rule.
- Yuan's same early reign is framed by thrift and burden reduction: cutting palace guards, returning laborers to farming, requiring local officials to reduce costs, and trimming palace consumption.
- The episode also links Yuan to Confucian-facing policies: appointing Zhou Kan and Zhang Meng, honoring Zhou royal descendants, expanding doctoral students, exempting people who understand one classic from taxes, and abolishing more than seventy legal provisions.

## Key Quotes

> "寸寸山河，寸寸金" - the patriotic opening used to introduce the Zhuya question.

> "愿属内地者，处之；不欲，勿强" - the episode's rendering of Yuan's settlement posture toward Zhuya people after abolition.

## Connections

- [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]], [[ZhuyaCommandery|珠崖郡]], and [[CapacityBoundFrontierCommitment|能力约束下的边疆承诺]] - the central case: Yuan narrows direct island commitment because famine, stores, and burden make prestige war too costly.
- [[MaYuanEasternHan|马援]] - later Eastern Han southern campaign context for restored contact with Hainan-area people without restored direct island administration.
- [[ZhouKanWesternHan|周堪]] and [[ZhangMengWesternHan|张猛 / 张萌]] - Yuan-era appointment layer before their later disaster-blame and Shi Xian conflict.
- [[GongYuWesternHan|贡禹]] - adviser associated here with reducing palace and guard burdens.
- [[ConfucianLegalistGovernanceBalance|儒法互补治理]] - adjacent Yuan-era frame because the episode pairs Confucian expansion and lighter punishments with cost-cutting and administrative burden control.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with existing wiki content was found. The episode keeps the exact administrative status of post-abolition Hainan source-scoped: Hepu jurisdiction is presented as a scholarly view, while actual control is characterized as weak and closer to remote nominal attachment.
