# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》830丨东汉李固：我虽死犹荣！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 147-148 CE [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] persecution layer before the later Hanji 832-848 Liang-family sequence. It follows [[LiGuLateHan|李固]] being accused, briefly released after student and public petitions, re-accused, defended by [[WuYouLateHan|吴祐]], and then dying in prison alongside [[DuQiaoLateHan|杜乔]].

The durable contribution is the moral failure of terror. [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] uses prison death, public corpse display, and a mourning ban to intimidate critics, but [[GuoLiangLiGuStudent|郭亮]], [[DongBanNanyangLateHan|董班]], and [[YangKuangDuQiaoRetainer|杨匡]] risk punishment to mourn, guard, and recover the bodies, making [[FuneraryDefianceUnderPoliticalTerror|恐怖政治下的丧葬抗命]] visible before the later Liang extraction, warning, crime-record, and coup notes.

## Key Claims

- In 147 CE, Liang Ji accuses Li Gu and Du Qiao of covertly joining Liu Wen and Liu Wei, but [[EmpressDowagerLiangNaLateHan|梁妠 / 梁太后]] initially refuses to let him arrest Du Qiao.
- Li Gu's student [[WangTiaoLiGuStudent|王调]] and the Henei petitioner [[ZhaoChengLiGuPetitioner|赵成]] help create enough public pressure that Liang Na orders Li Gu released.
- Liang Ji treats Li Gu's release and reputation as a threat, reasserts the accusation, ignores Wu You's defense, and Li Gu dies in prison.
- Li Gu's final letter frames his death as loyal service completed, while accusing Liang-family disorder and senior ministers' passive salary-taking.
- Du Qiao refuses a coerced suicide demand meant to preserve his family and then dies in prison after Liang Ji escalates the case.
- Liang Ji displays Li Gu and Du Qiao's bodies north of Luoyang and bans mourning, but the display draws defiant students, friends, and old subordinates instead of erasing reputation.
- Liang Na pardons Guo Liang, Dong Ban, and Yang Kuang, then permits the bodies to be returned home for burial.
- Yang Kuang, Guo Liang, and Dong Ban withdraw permanently from office after the burial sequence, turning loyal mourning into exit from a corrupted court.
- The closing notice records Southern Xiongnu chanyu succession and Emperor Huan's coming-of-age rite, setting up the question of whether Huan can later recover power from Liang Ji.

## Key Quotes

> "我虽死犹荣，已尽忠义" - Li Gu's final posture as presented by the episode.

> "置生死于度外" - Guo Liang and Dong Ban's response to threats at the displayed body.

> "君不正则奸佞生" - the host's closing diagnosis of the political order.
