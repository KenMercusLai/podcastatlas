## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills 73 CE / Yongping 16 immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-743-chuo-ren-xinwo-de-lishi-shunjian-mingdi-liuzhuang-de-wenqing-meng-lgwtuqtug5bh90qohwiskccx77hy|Hanji 743]]. It follows [[BanChaoEasternHan|班超]] detecting [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|Northern Xiongnu]] envoys at [[ShanshanKingdomLateHan|Shanshan]], striking their camp with thirty-six men, forcing Shanshan into Han allegiance, then repeating the pressure pattern at [[KhotanKingdomLateHan|Khotan]] by killing a Xiongnu-linked shaman.

The durable synthesis is that Ban Chao's Western Regions career begins as shock diplomacy under extreme asymmetry: he reads changed reception as evidence of rival pressure, verifies the risk through bluffing, acts before the host kingdom can surrender him, and turns a tiny envoy party into an intimidation signal. The episode also adds [[LiuYanFulingKingEasternHan|Huaiyang/Fuling King Liu Yan]]'s first prophecy-linked punishment and [[LianFanEasternHan|Lian Fan]]'s paired examples of fire-display deception at Yunzhong and risk-managed night-fire governance in Chengdu.

## Key Claims

- Ban Chao's first Western Regions mission begins when Shanshan receives Han envoys warmly, then turns cold after Northern Xiongnu envoys arrive.
- Ban Chao verifies the hidden Xiongnu presence through a bluff, excludes Guo Xun from planning because secrecy and nerve are decisive, and attacks the Xiongnu camp at night with fire, drums, and ambush positions.
- Shanshan submits after Ban Chao displays the Xiongnu envoys' heads, explains Han authority, and requires the kingdom to cut off relations with Northern Xiongnu and send a prince as hostage.
- [[EmperorMingOfHan|Emperor Ming]] treats Ban Chao's result as evidence enough to keep him in the role, while Ban Chao refuses extra troops because a larger but still insufficient force would slow him and weaken the signal.
- At Khotan, Ban Chao answers Xiongnu-supervised coldness and a shaman's horse demand by summoning and beheading the shaman, punishing the prime minister Si Laibi, and frightening King Guangde into killing remaining Xiongnu envoys and submitting.
- The host reads these actions through [[AdvantageFirstCombatDoctrine|advantage-first combat doctrine]]: "胜兵先胜而后求战" means creating a winnable situation before fighting, not gambling first and hoping for victory.
- Liu Yan's first case shows same-surname royal punishment short of execution, while Lian Fan's later governance shows both military deception through fire display and urban-risk policy that permits night work while requiring household water storage.

## Key Quotes

> "不入虎穴，焉得虎子" - Ban Chao's compressed argument for acting before Shanshan can hand the Han envoys to Xiongnu.

> "胜兵先胜而后求战" - the Sunzi frame the episode applies to Ban Chao's Western Regions action.

> "平生无襦，今五裤" - the popular song frame used to praise Lian Fan's Chengdu governance.
