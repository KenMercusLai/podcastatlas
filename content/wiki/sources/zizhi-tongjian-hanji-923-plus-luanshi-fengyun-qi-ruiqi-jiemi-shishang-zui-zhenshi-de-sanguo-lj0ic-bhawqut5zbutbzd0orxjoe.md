# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》923 plus丨乱世风云起：芮淇揭秘史上最真实的三国

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] plus episode is a short prologue to the show's coming [[ThreeKingdomsPeriod|三国时代]] arc rather than a narrative installment with new events. It defines the arc's reading method: use [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] and historical-source comparison to study human nature, power, and long-running conflict rather than treating the period only through [[RomanceOfTheThreeKingdoms|《三国演义》]] memory. The episode also compares major Chinese classics by what they can train a reader to notice: [[JourneyToTheWest|《西游记》]] as small-team management, [[WaterMargin|《水浒传》]] as large-team management, [[HongLouMeng|《红楼梦》]] as enduring human and cultural texture, and Three Kingdoms as [[ThreeKingdomsStruggleStrategyReading|斗争策略读法]].

## Key Claims

- The episode frames Three Kingdoms as a disorderly world where survival and judgment require understanding conflict, not only admiring heroic scenes.
- Its central reading method is [[ThreeKingdomsStruggleStrategyReading|三国斗争策略读法]]: single-party and multi-party games, internal and external conflict, shifting friend-enemy relations, and long-game positioning matter more than one isolated victory or defeat.
- The source explicitly distinguishes the show's coming Three Kingdoms arc from Luo Guanzhong-style novelized memory in [[RomanceOfTheThreeKingdoms|《三国演义》]], emphasizing a return to historical sources and inferred actor logic.
- [[CaoCao|曹操]] is previewed as both a late-Han powerholder and literary figure, but the episode does not yet add concrete new events to his page.
- [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]], [[GuanYu|关羽]], [[LiuBei|刘备]], Zhao Zilong, [[YuanShao|袁绍]], and Sima Yi are named as expectation-setting figures for the coming arc rather than analyzed in detail.
- The four-classics comparison is instrumental and source-scoped: [[JourneyToTheWest|《西游记》]] is mapped to small-team management, [[WaterMargin|《水浒传》]] to large-team management, [[HongLouMeng|《红楼梦》]] to cultural and interpersonal continuity, and Three Kingdoms to power struggle and strategy.
- The episode's value is programmatic: it supplies the interpretive frame for later Three Kingdoms episodes, especially around human motives, explicit and hidden strategy, and historical truth versus literary reception.

## Key Quotes
