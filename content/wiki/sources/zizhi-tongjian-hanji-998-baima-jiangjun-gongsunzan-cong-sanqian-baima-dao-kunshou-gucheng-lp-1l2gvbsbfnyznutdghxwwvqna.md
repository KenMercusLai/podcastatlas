# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》998丨白马将军公孙瓒：从三千白马到困守孤城

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the northern late-Han branch before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-999-sanguo-luanshi-renxing-you-duome-cuiruo-li8tz30elca7bnipei6dfqdxmmeu|Hanji 999]]. After [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] kills [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] and occupies [[YouzhouLateHan|幽州]], the source presents his failure as a combined loss of virtue, people-support, usable talent, and strategic initiative.

The main narrative follows [[XianyuFu|鲜于辅]], [[YanRou|阎柔]], [[Wuhuan|乌桓]], [[Xianbei|鲜卑]], [[LiuHeLateHan|刘和]], and [[YuanShao|袁绍]]-aligned forces as they attack Gongsun Zan, while commanderies across Youzhou kill his officials and defect. Gongsun Zan then moves to [[YixianLateHan|易县]], builds [[YijingFortress|易京]] with deep trenches and high towers, isolates himself behind iron gates, and stops campaigning; the episode reads this as [[FortressSecurityIllusion|筑城避世安全幻觉]] rather than real security.

## Key Claims
- The episode treats Gongsun Zan's collapse as moral and organizational before it is purely military: killing Liu Yu, retaliating over slights, and persecuting capable士大夫 make his rule politically unsafe.
- Gongsun Zan's disdain for士大夫 pushes him toward merchants, market people, and intimate dependents whose new authority increases popular resentment.
- [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]]'s old staff, especially [[XianyuFu|鲜于辅]], become the revenge-and-restoration nucleus after Liu Yu's death.
- [[YanRou|阎柔]] is selected as Wuhuan sima because of his prestige and uses that position to gather large mixed Hu and Han forces.
- Yan Rou defeats [[ZhouDanLateHan|周丹]], Gongsun Zan's Yuyang taishou, killing Zhou Dan and thousands of his men.
- [[Wuhuan|乌桓]] and [[Xianbei|鲜卑]] cavalry join Xianyu Fu, welcome Liu Yu's son [[LiuHeLateHan|刘和]], and connect with [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s side.
- The anti-Gongsun coalition is described as reaching a nominal 100,000, defeating Gongsun Zan and killing more than 20,000 of his troops.
- After the defeat, Dai, Guangyang, Shanggu, and Youbeiping actors kill Gongsun Zan's appointed officials and join the Liu Yu-aligned coalition, showing that the military setback coincides with local political collapse.
- Gongsun Zan reads a children's song as pointing to Yi County as a refuge, then makes [[YixianLateHan|易县]] his headquarters and builds the heavily trenched [[YijingFortress|易京]] stronghold.
- The episode presents the fortress as a psychological turn from active warlord to trapped hoarder: high towers, iron gates, suspended documents, and shouted commands replace field command and adviser access.
- By cutting off guests, generals, trusted men, warriors, and strategists, Gongsun Zan converts physical defense into [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|君臣反馈失灵]].
- The host contrasts Gongsun Zan's earlier [[BaimaYicong|白马义从]] cavalry image with his later refusal to fight, arguing that acquired assets can make a leader risk-averse and passive.
- The comparison with [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s [[MeiwuFortress|郿坞]] makes the lesson broader: fortress, grain, and walls cannot replace initiative, legitimacy, and people.
- The final historical note records [[YufuluoChanyu|于扶罗]]'s death and [[HuchuquanChanyu|呼厨泉]]'s succession in the [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]], with the group stationed at Pingyang.
- The closing reading of Hanji 53 emphasizes that [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] selects events that bear on state survival, people's lives, and recurring human patterns.

## Key Quotes
> "真正的安全不是躲起来" - the episode's summary of why Gongsun Zan's fortress turn is a false solution.

> "如果你不坐在餐桌上，你很可能就在菜单上" - the host's modern analogy for losing initiative in disorder.
