# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》625丨揭秘人性暗面：为何旁人成功总令我们心生嫉恨？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills a 5 CE [[WangMang|王莽]] court atmosphere immediately before the post-[[HanPingdi|汉平帝]] succession sequence in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-628-ruguo-chuanyue-jiao-ni-ruhe-dang-huangdi-lljocpgp247g7frbrkta39zrtlc|Hanji 628]]. After Wang Yun and other envoys report harmonized customs, fabricated songs, and universal moral transformation, the court rewards praise-making officials and makes nonparticipation politically suspicious.

The central contrast is between [[BanZhiWesternHan|班至]] and [[GongsunHongLangyaWesternHan|公孙洪]]. Both fail to join the auspicious-reporting and praise chorus that benefits Wang Mang's circle; [[ZhenFengXinOfficial|甄丰]] pushes local accusation channels against them, 公孙洪 is killed, and 班至 survives only after [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] distinguishes his offense and he voluntarily lowers himself to a cemetery-guard post. The episode turns this into a survival lesson about [[CoercedPraiseConformityRisk|颂圣不合群风险]] and [[PoliticalSurvivalFirst|安全第一政治生存]] under a polluted power field.

## Key Claims

- In 5 CE, Wang Mang's court presents empire-wide moral transformation through envoy reports, songs, and local praise material.
- The reward of envoys and builders makes praise production politically valuable rather than merely ceremonial.
- Ban Zhi's failure to report auspicious signs and Gongsun Hong's discussion of disasters become grounds for accusation once praise is expected.
- Zhen Feng's use of subordinate and local petition channels shows how a dominant faction can outsource political attack.
- Wang Zhengjun's intervention saves Ban Zhi by separating non-praise from fabricated-disaster accusation, but it does not save Gongsun Hong.
- Ban Zhi's resignation of his chancellor seal and acceptance of a lower cemetery-guard role is framed as survival through visible retreat.
- The host's contemporary lesson is that in a coercive or polluted environment, visible purity can become a risk rather than protection.

## Key Quotes

> "水至清则无鱼，人至清则无友" - the episode's maxim for why excessive visible purity can isolate a person in a dangerous environment.

> "当浑浊成为一种常态，清白就是罪过" - the host's compressed survival lesson from Ban Zhi and Gongsun Hong.

> "到哪座山，唱哪座山的歌" - the closing adaptation rule attached to the episode's practical advice.
