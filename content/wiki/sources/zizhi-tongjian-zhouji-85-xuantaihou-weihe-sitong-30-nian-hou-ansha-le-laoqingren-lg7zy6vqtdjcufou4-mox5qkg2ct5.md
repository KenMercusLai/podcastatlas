# 《资治通鉴·周纪》85｜宣太后为何私通30年后暗杀了老情人？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]]'s 周纪五 frame, moving [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]] into his forty-third year and recapping the late-[[WarringStatesPeriod|战国时期]] world of Qin expansion, alliance switching, and large-scale war. It follows the immediate sequel to [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-84-fangzhi-qinguo-miechu-jing-yong-zhe-zhao-llgh12eistejijsl6yildzkixocm|Zhouji 84]]: [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]] accompanies [[MiHeng|楚顷襄王]]'s crown prince into [[QinState|秦国]] as hostage, while Qin creates [[NanyangCommandery|南阳郡]] and joins Wei and Chu in attacking a weakened [[YanState|燕国]]. The core branch returns to [[XuanTaihou|芈八子 / 宣太后]] and [[YiquState|义渠]]: a decades-long intimate relationship keeps Qin's rear quiet, but when absorption becomes feasible, Xuan Taihou lures the Yiqu king to [[GanquanPalace|甘泉宫]], kills him, and Qin creates [[BeidiCommandery|北地郡]] to secure its northern frontier.

## Key Claims
- 周纪五 covers Zhou Nan Wang's forty-third through fifty-ninth years, the final seventeen years of the Zhouji section, and the episode frames this as the late Warring States phase when Qin's rise becomes the main strategic fact.
- After Huang Xie's previous letter stops a direct anti-Chu campaign, Chu signals alignment by sending the crown prince to Qin as a hostage, with Huang Xie serving as the accompanying left official and future beneficiary of the relationship.
- Qin's new [[NanyangCommandery|南阳郡]] is treated as likely organized around the newly occupied northern Chu region near Wan, today's Nanyang area.
- Qin, Wei, and Chu jointly attack Yan in the same year that [[YanHuiwang|燕惠王]] is killed by [[GongsunCao|公孙操]] and [[YanWuchengwang|燕武成王]] succeeds; the episode reads the war and the succession violence as related even though the chronicle separates the notices.
- [[YanHuiwang|燕惠王]]'s authority had already collapsed after forcing out [[LeYi|乐毅]] and watching [[TianDan|田单]] restore [[QiState|齐国]], leaving Yan open to internal coup and external predation.
- The episode adds an event not emphasized in the main [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] passage: [[XuanTaihou|宣太后]]'s long relationship with the Yiqu king, which the host says is documented only fragmentarily through texts such as [[Shiji|《史记》]] and Hou Han Shu.
- The source cannot decide whether Xuan Taihou and the Yiqu king were primarily lovers, political partners, or both; it therefore treats the relationship as source-limited rather than psychologically certain.
- The host argues that the relationship had clear strategic effect: during roughly thirty years of Qin campaigns against the six states, Yiqu did not raid Qin's rear, which helped [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] consolidate power and maintain outward pressure.
- Once Qin judged the timing mature, Xuan Taihou induced the Yiqu king to come to [[GanquanPalace|甘泉宫]], killed him there, and Qin used the leaderless moment to destroy Yiqu.
- In 271 BCE Qin established [[BeidiCommandery|北地郡]] in the conquered Yiqu region, with Yiqu county as its administrative center according to the episode.
- Together with [[LongxiCommandery|陇西郡]] and [[Shangjun|上郡]], Beidi gave Qin a connected northern defensive line and the basis for wall-building against northern Hu groups.
- The episode says surviving Yiqu people later returned to mobile pastoral life; by Han times some Yiqu descendants submitted, were armed and settled on the frontier against Xiongnu, and a few preserved Yiqu as a compound surname.

## Key Quotes
> "不达目的不罢休" - the host's summary of Qin's ruthless strategic style.

> "以最小的代价换取最大的利益" - the episode's explanation of why killing the Yiqu king before invasion fit Qin's method.

## Contradictions
- No direct contradiction found. The episode closes the open [[YiquState|义渠]] branch from [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-35-lishishang-diyi-qibing-jiaofu-shi-ta-lnxgnu1czkl-ftfb3gq9kzxwnxdk|Zhouji 35]]: earlier Qin had defeated and partially absorbed Yiqu without destroying it; here Qin finally removes the Yiqu king and turns the region into Beidi Commandery.
