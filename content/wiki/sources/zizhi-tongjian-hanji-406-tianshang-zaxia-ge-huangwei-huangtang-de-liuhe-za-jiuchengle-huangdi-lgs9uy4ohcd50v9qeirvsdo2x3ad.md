# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》406｜天上砸下个皇位！荒唐的刘贺咋就成了皇帝？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the accession setup immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-407-zaiwei-jin-27-tian-han-feidi-liuhe-ruhe-bei-huoguang-feichu-lpob_9jkyomzrbeqnbjekqbpjeog|Hanji 407]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-408-feidi-jie-tianhuaban-han-feidi-liuhe-xiagang-la-lgqd0gddiz5plifsvwteaub2emug|Hanji 408]]. It follows [[LiuHeHaihunHou|刘贺]] as a disordered Changyi king who receives strange warnings, then suddenly becomes the heir after Han Zhaodi's death.

The durable synthesis is that Liu He's accession failure begins before he reaches the throne. Wang Ji and the langzhongling understand that the new candidate must mourn visibly, restrain private pleasure, and defer to [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]], but Liu He travels toward Chang'an with concealed women, forced speed, open delight, and only performative crying when warned that failure to grieve could endanger the throne.

## Key Claims

- Liu He's Changyi conduct is framed through repeated ominous signs: a bear visible only to him and blood on his royal seat become warnings that personal disorder has political consequences.
- The langzhongling uses Shijing language and direct kneeling remonstrance to turn strange signs into a moral and political warning.
- When the succession summons arrives in 74 BCE, [[WangJiWesternHan|王吉]] advises Liu He to act like a mourning heir, avoid issuing commands, and rely respectfully on Huo Guang.
- Liu He violates the expected accession posture before arriving in Chang'an by seeking unusual objects, hiding women in a covered carriage, and allowing a subordinate to be killed after the scandal is exposed.
- At Chang'an, Liu He's visible happiness collides with mourning ritual; only after the langzhongling warns that failure to cry could cost him the throne does Liu He perform grief.
- After receiving the imperial seal, Liu He continues promoting Changyi followers and pursuing entertainment during mourning, setting up the remonstrance and deposition sequence in later episodes.

## Key Quotes

> "人不知礼，定有祸" - the host's opening frame for Liu He's failure to understand ritual order.

> "见国都城门而哭" - the mourning rule the langzhongling invokes when Liu He reaches Chang'an.
