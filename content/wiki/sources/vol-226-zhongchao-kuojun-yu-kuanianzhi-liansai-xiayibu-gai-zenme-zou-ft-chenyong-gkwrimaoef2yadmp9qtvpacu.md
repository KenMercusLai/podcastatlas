## Summary
This [[Budongqiu|不懂球]] episode with football reporter [[ChenYongFootballReporter|陈勇 / Chen Yong]] argues that [[ChineseSuperLeague|Chinese Super League]] expansion should be treated as a bundled governance reform rather than a simple increase from 16 to 18 clubs. The discussion links [[ChineseFootballLeagueExpansion]], [[ChineseFootballFinancialDiscipline]], [[ChineseFootballPyramidDesign]], foreign-player rules, and match-calendar design, warning that expansion without stricter wage, tax, debt, admission, and lower-tier competition systems could repeat older structural problems. It is cautious on [[ChineseFootballCalendarPolicy|cross-year calendar reform]], favoring research and better near-term scheduling before a full winter-spanning season shift.

## Key Claims
- The episode says Chinese professional football has moved out of its post-pandemic low, with 2025 framed as the first clear rebound year and the top-flight market recovering enough to make expansion discussion realistic.
- [[ChineseFootballLeagueExpansion]] is presented as necessary but procedurally sensitive: rules should be announced ahead of time, not changed suddenly after clubs have already competed under one promotion-relegation regime.
- The preferred expansion path is [[ChineseSuperLeague]] and [[ChineseLeagueOne|China League One]] both moving toward 18 clubs, with playoff-based transition designs favored over a simple "relegate one, promote three" formula.
- [[ChineseFootballPyramidDesign]] remains incomplete because [[ChineseLeagueTwo|China League Two]] and [[ChineseChampionsLeague|Chinese Champions League]] lack enough stable match supply, home-ground continuity, and club-community links.
- Expansion must be paired with [[ChineseFootballFinancialDiscipline]]: the discussion calls for stricter treatment of new tax arrears, wage arrears, shareholder-loan debt, and club-entry rules.
- The source favors adjusting [[ChineseFootballForeignPlayerPolicy]] from "register five, play five" toward "register six, play five" under unchanged salary constraints, mainly to improve injury tolerance and squad stability rather than restart a spending race.
- [[ChineseFootballCalendarPolicy]] is treated as a long-term research problem: cross-year seasons may be studied, but northeast weather, Xinjiang integration, winter training, CBA and event competition, security coordination, and fairness of repeated away stretches make immediate adoption unattractive.
- The more urgent calendar problem is ordinary season design: dense May fixtures, long June and September interruptions, and repeated concessions to national-team or multi-sport events weaken league rhythm and fan attention.

## Key Quotes
> "扩军不能是单独操作" - the episode's core policy-bundle warning.

> "不能临时改变升降级规则" - the procedural-fairness objection to sudden expansion.

> "现在更紧迫的是把赛历排得更合理" - the near-term alternative to immediate cross-year reform.
