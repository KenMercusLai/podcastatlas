# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》673丨帝王权谋施恩术 ！让下属对你死心塌地

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the Hebei turning point after [[WangLangHebeiClaimant|王郎]]'s defeat and immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-674-ruoguo-wuwaijiao-gengshi-huangdi-liuxuan-you-duo-biequ-lu59astedsljoefnl6yr9d4ilirh|Hanji 674]]. [[GengYanEasternHan|耿弇]] urges [[LiuXiu|刘秀]] not to demobilize or obey [[LiuXuanGengshiEmperor|刘玄]]'s recall, because [[TongmaRebelArmyEasternHan|铜马军]], [[ChimeiRebellion|赤眉]], and other Hebei forces remain too strong for the Gengshi regime to control.

The durable synthesis is that Liu Xiu's autonomous power begins before accession through counsel, coercive mobilization, and surrender incorporation. [[WuHanEasternHan|吴汉]] and Geng Yan force Youzhou cavalry into Liu Xiu's camp, Liu Xiu defeats and absorbs Tongma-linked forces through a [[PersonalRiskTrustSignal|personal-risk trust signal]], and the episode sets up [[XieGongEasternHan|谢躬]]'s later removal by showing Liu Xiu using gifts and envoys to lull his suspicion.

## Key Claims

- Geng Yan reframes the post-Wang-Lang moment as the start of a new power struggle rather than a finished pacification, arguing against demobilization.
- Geng Yan says Liu Xuan lacks capacity to defeat Tongma, Chimei, and other large forces, and that abusive Gengshi generals and royal relatives have alienated the people.
- Liu Xiu refuses Liu Xuan's summons by citing unfinished Hebei pacification, marking the beginning of his estrangement from the Gengshi court.
- Wu Han kills Youzhou governor Miao Zeng after Miao secretly blocks mobilization, while Geng Yan executes Wei Shun and Cai Chong in Shanggu; northern commanderies then send troops.
- Wu Han strengthens Liu Xiu's position not only by bringing cavalry but also by submitting rosters and asking for funding, which signals that the force is not privately held.
- Liu Xiu defeats the Tongma army and allied forces, enfeoffs surrendered leaders, sends them back to their camps, and rides alone through their units to make trust credible.
- The Tongma surrendered soldiers accept Liu Xiu after this trust display, and Liu Xiu's force grows to more than 100,000.
- Liu Xiu later defeats a Chimei branch and other armed groups at Shequan, takes Henei, and receives the Henei taishou Han Xin's submission.
- Xie Gong remains a Gengshi-side danger after Wang Lang; Liu Xiu's envoys and gifts relax him, while Xie Gong's wife warns that this apparent affection is false.

## Key Quotes

> "天下纷争才刚刚开始" - Geng Yan's post-Wang-Lang diagnosis as rendered in the episode.

> "自己单骑巡视各部队" - the episode's compact description of Liu Xiu's trust display among surrendered troops.
