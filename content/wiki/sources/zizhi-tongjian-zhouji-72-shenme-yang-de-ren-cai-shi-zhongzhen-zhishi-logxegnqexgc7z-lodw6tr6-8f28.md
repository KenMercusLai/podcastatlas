# 《资治通鉴·周纪》72丨什么样的人 才是忠贞之士？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the 284 BCE aftermath of [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-70-xihuan-huayang-zuosi-de-qiguo-junzhu-luuyasp5-nhnikdcozzaueosb-md|Zhouji 70]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-71-xunzide-xingbenelun-lnyfxoxauexhwbrwkdevs8m4mr2j|Zhouji 71]] through [[WangZhu|王蠋]], a former [[QiState|齐国]] official and worthy man. [[YanState|燕国]] forces first honor and recruit him, then threaten his city if he refuses; Wang Zhu answers that a loyal minister does not serve two rulers and kills himself to avoid both surrender and harming his locality. The episode then shifts to [[LeYi|乐毅]]'s post-conquest governance of Qi, the Qin-Wei-Han meeting at [[Luoyi|洛邑]], and the flight of [[TianFazhang|田法章]] after [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]]'s death.

## Key Claims
- [[WangZhu|王蠋]] is not presented as a simple recluse: he once served in Qi and withdrew because Qi Min Wang did not accept remonstrance.
- Yan's first approach to Wang Zhu uses honor language: troops are kept away from the surrounding thirty li, and he is offered money and a ten-thousand-household fief.
- The recruitment turns coercive when Yan soldiers threaten to slaughter his city if he will not come.
- Wang Zhu frames the choice through the maxim that a loyal minister does not serve two rulers and a chaste woman does not marry two husbands.
- The host treats Wang Zhu's death as a solution to a [[LoyaltyCollateralDilemma|loyalty-collateral dilemma]]: surrender violates loyalty, refusal may bring punishment on neighbors, and death removes him from the coercive exchange.
- The episode stresses the physical decisiveness of his death method, reading it as a bodily declaration of resolve rather than only an ordinary suicide notice.
- [[LeYi|乐毅]]'s occupation policy combines military control with political repair: discipline troops, forbid plunder, honor recluses, lighten taxes, remove harsh laws, restore older good governance, and absorb Qi elites through titles and fiefs.
- Yan's control of Qi is not only a battlefield result; the episode describes systematic routes, garrisons, and conversion of more than seventy Qi cities into Yan commanderies and counties.
- The meeting of [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]], [[WeiZhaowang|魏昭王]], and [[HanXiwang|韩釐王]] at the Zhou capital keeps [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]] visible as a diplomatic venue even after substantive power has moved to the stronger states.
- [[TianFazhang|田法章]]'s hidden flight after Qi Min Wang's violent death sets up the next survival branch for the broken Qi royal line.

## Key Quotes
> "忠臣不事二君，烈女不嫁二夫" - Wang Zhu's ethical formula for refusing Yan service.

> "与其不义而生，不若死而守义" - the episode's summary of Wang Zhu's final choice.
