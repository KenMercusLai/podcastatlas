# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》645丨必读职场权谋：明哲保身or逆流而上？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills [[WangMang|王莽]]'s early [[XinDynasty|新朝]] crisis around 12 CE, before the later Hanji 647-655 sequence. It centers on [[ChenXianXinOfficial|陈贤]] withdrawing from office after Wang Mang changes Han institutions and kills opponents, then broadens into Yellow River non-repair, Xiongnu hostage execution, hollow rank proliferation, policy churn, and frontier escalation.

The durable synthesis is that principled withdrawal and state misgovernance are paired in the episode. Chen Xian lowers visibility while preserving Han ritual timing and legal texts, whereas Wang Mang turns public disaster, diplomatic trust, rank order, and frontier management into regime risk through private-interest calculation, symbolic overreach, and coercive shortcuts.

## Key Claims

- Chen Xian's retirement is framed as a timing judgment rather than cowardice: after He Wu and Bao Xuan are killed, he reads the situation as no longer serviceable.
- His withdrawal preserves allegiance in low-visibility form: he keeps Han ritual dates and hides Han legal materials instead of publicly contesting Wang Mang.
- Other scholars and officials refusing New Dynasty service make the episode a broader case of [[PrincipledOfficeRefusal|principled office refusal]] under usurpation.
- Wang Mang's Yellow River response exposes private-interest rule: he avoids repairing the breach once the flood no longer threatens the Wang ancestral graves at Yuancheng.
- Killing a Xiongnu hostage in public turns hostage diplomacy from leverage into durable grievance before later Xiongnu crises.
- Large-scale creation of noble ranks and capital/city schemes without measured households or land makes title status detach from material support.
- Xin frontier handling toward Juting and Goguryeo uses demotion, deception, killing, coercive recruitment, and blame shifting in ways that invite rebellion.

## Key Quotes

> "见几而作，不俟终日" - Chen Xian's cited timing principle for withdrawal.

> "明哲保身是明智之选" - the host's interpretation of withdrawal in a dangerous court.

> "祖先不会知道姓王的什么时候祭祀" - Chen Xian's reply explaining why his household keeps Han ritual dates.
