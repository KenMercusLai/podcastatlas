# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》733-1丨历史所遗忘的帝王：治世明君刘庄

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian]] episode backfills the 57-58 CE accession layer for [[EmperorMingOfHan|汉明帝刘庄]], immediately before the sequence already covered from [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-734-huanrong-cheng-daru-yinwei-mingdi-shige-hao-penggen-lhwbxrqocf102or9k0nb6z93gzx9|Hanji 734]] onward. It follows Mingdi from youthful succession and elder assistance through [[DengYuEasternHan|邓禹]], trusted royal collaboration with [[LiuCangDongpingKing|东平王刘苍]], the [[WuLiangEasternHan|吴良]] recommendation, the first [[ShaodangQiang|烧当羌]] crisis, and the Yuanling reporting rite before [[LiuXiu|光武帝]]'s tablet.

The episode also sets up Mingdi's differentiated management of royal kin: deep mourning and frugal burial for [[LiuQiangDonghaiKing|东海恭王刘强]], but spatial containment for [[LiuJingGuanglingKingEasternHan|刘荆]] after renewed conspiratorial hopes. On the frontier and institutional side, [[JiTongEasternHan|祭肜]] uses Xianbei force against Chishan Wuhuan, while Liu Cang proposes rites and music that help make early Mingdi rule legible as settled order.

## Key Claims

- Mingdi's accession rhetoric stresses youth, lack of precedent, and dependence on elder ministers; appointing [[DengYuEasternHan|Deng Yu]] as Taifu and [[LiuCangDongpingKing|Liu Cang]] as piaoji general makes succession look assisted rather than improvised.
- Liu Cang's recommendation of [[WuLiangEasternHan|Wu Liang]] turns royal trust into a talent pipeline, and the appointment as yilang shows Mingdi treating recommendation as state service.
- The [[ShaodangQiang|Shaodang Qiang]] crisis under Dian Liang and Dian Wu exposes immediate frontier pressure on the new reign: Zhang Hong's first response fails, Dou Gu and Ma Wu are sent with a large force, and [[MaWuEasternHan|Ma Wu]] later suppresses the revolt.
- The Yuanling rite makes inherited legitimacy administrative: shangji officials report local grain prices and popular hardship before [[LiuXiu|Guangwu]]'s tablet, as if the founding emperor still audits the realm.
- [[LiuQiangDonghaiKing|Liu Qiang]] and [[LiuJingGuanglingKingEasternHan|Liu Jing]] mark opposite royal-kin cases: one is honored as deferential and mourned, while the other is relocated after astrologer-driven hopes for disorder.
- [[JiTongEasternHan|Ji Tong]] and Xianbei leader Pian He create a northeastern security result against Chishan Wuhuan that allows Han authorities to withdraw border garrisons.
- Liu Cang's rites-and-music proposal ties Mingdi's early reign to sacrificial order, court display, vehicle and dress standards, Guangwu temple music, and bayi dance.

## Key Quotes

> "有政治远见和治国才能" - the host's opening characterization of Mingdi's later reputation.

> "为国举荐贤才是宰相职责" - the episode's framing of Liu Cang's recommendation of Wu Liang.

> "有志者事竟成" - the closing recall of Liu Xiu's praise for [[GengYanEasternHan|耿弇]].
