# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》851丨汉桓帝为何注定遗臭万年？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills a 159 CE [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] layer between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-850-huandi-weihe-zhiyi-sha-zhongchen-lokxtceq7vv-giec6iufwwk9fwk7|Hanji 850]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-852-dang-meinv-ruyun-shi-fu-haishi-huo-luml30ytbc-awpvk-7-23ehzbqpx|Hanji 852]]. [[HuangQiongLateHan|黄琼]]'s illness memorial diagnoses the post-Liang court as a place where eunuchs, corrupt favorites, and loyal officials are no longer morally distinguishable in reward and punishment, and Huan gives no visible correction.

The episode then turns that feedback failure into an enforcement case. [[ShanChaoLateHan|单超]] is elevated while sick, his nephew [[ShanKuangLateHan|单匡]] is exposed for large-scale corruption and witness suppression, and [[YangBingLateHan|杨秉]] plus [[FifthZhongLateHan|第五种]] are punished or exiled while the protected network remains dangerous. [[SunBinFifthZhongRetainerLateHan|孙斌]]'s rescue of Fifth Zhong turns the closing lesson toward political withdrawal and survival under a court where formal office can punish the enforcer.

## Key Claims

- Huang Qiong's memorial reframes Huan's 159 CE court as reward inversion: Liang-family damage remains, eunuchs fill the court, and loyal officials have been killed or humiliated.
- Huan's refusal or inability to respond confirms the episode's judgment that clearing Liang Ji did not produce a healthier governance order.
- Shan Chao's appointment as Chariot-and-Cavalry General while sick shows that eunuch reward and rank inflation continue after Li Yun's warning.
- Duan Jiong's frontier victory still supplies real military capacity, but it sits beside a court that mishandles internal enforcement.
- Shan Kuang's Jiyin corruption and attempted killing of Wei Yu expand the eunuch-protection pattern from violent retainers to investigation and witness control.
- Yang Bing's request to confront Shan Kuang in Luoyang becomes punishable, making enforcement procedure itself dangerous.
- Shusun Wuji's raids create the pretext for Shan Chao to frame Fifth Zhong, while Dong Yuan's position in Shuofang makes exile potentially lethal.
- Sun Bin's rescue of Fifth Zhong gives the episode its survival lesson: in a disordered order, retreat or rescue outside the formal channel may preserve life better than officeholding.

## Key Quotes

> "奸佞与忠良同受恩宠" - Huang Qiong's warning about reward inversion.

> "天下无道则隐，有道则至" - the source's closing withdrawal frame.

> "危机关头见人心" - the source's judgment on Sun Bin's rescue.
