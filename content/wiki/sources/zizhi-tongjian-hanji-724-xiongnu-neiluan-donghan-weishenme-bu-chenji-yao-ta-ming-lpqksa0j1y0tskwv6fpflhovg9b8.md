# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》724丨匈奴内乱，东汉为什么不趁机要它命？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 47-48 CE late [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] frontier layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-725-donghan-mingjiang-jitong-yao-baojiaweiguo-jiu-ba-diren-da-paxia-lq06wntw8k5230zmlncquykara9y|Hanji 725]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-727-gui-xia-chanyu-weihe-baidao-zai-hanshi-mianqian-lmzdh-rrfadzszyetxgg-rou24xe|Hanji 727]]. It joins the [[WulingManLateHan|武陵蛮]] crisis that kills Liu Shang and brings the aging [[MaYuanEasternHan|马援]] back into command with the Xiongnu succession rupture that elevates [[HuhanyeChanyuBi|日逐王比]] as 呼韩邪单于 and begins the [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] / [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|北匈奴]] split.

The durable synthesis is that Guangwu-era frontier policy is shaped by capacity and sequencing, not by a simple choice between softness and attack. [[GengGuoEasternHan|耿国]] argues that accepting Southern Xiongnu submission can create a buffer against [[Xianbei|鲜卑]] and Northern Xiongnu while supporting [[FrontierCommanderyRestoration|边郡恢复治理]], and Liu Xiu does not immediately accept Zhang Gong's request to attack a weakened Xiongnu field.

## Key Claims

- In 47 CE, Wuling Man rebellion escalates after Liu Shang advances too deeply into difficult terrain and is killed with his army.
- Xiongnu succession order breaks when the chanyu Yu kills Zhiya Shi to reserve the heir line for his son, making Bi's southern bloc fear exclusion and danger.
- Bi's secret contact with Han, including a map sent through Guo Heng, marks his break with the northern royal court before open division.
- When the plan to kill Bi leaks during the Longcheng ritual, Bi rapidly gathers the southern eight tribes, and the northern chanyu's cavalry withdraws rather than fight a larger force.
- In 48 CE, the southern eight tribes raise Bi as Huhanye Chanyu, and he asks to become a permanent Han vassal who will resist Northern Xiongnu.
- Geng Guo persuades Liu Xiu to accept the submission because Southern Xiongnu can pressure Xianbei and Northern Xiongnu, set an example for other frontier groups, and help recover border commanderies.
- Ma Yuan's Wuling request shows late-career martial resolve, but his own stated concern is the difficulty of managing powerful young nobles inside the army.
- Zhang Gong treats Xiongnu famine, epidemic, and division as a chance for quick cavalry merit; Liu Xiu's response keeps the decision under review rather than converting weakness into immediate war.

## Key Quotes

> "趁他病要他命" - the host's label for Zhang Gong's attack logic.

> "精神矍铄的老将军" - Liu Xiu's approval after Ma Yuan demonstrates he can still ride and fight.

> "心无谦卑，日伴高人莫助" - the episode's closing self-cultivation admonition.
