# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》467｜官场里讲真话、说实话有多难？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the early [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] and [[GongYuWesternHan|贡禹]] sequence from [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-466-gongyu-tanguan-beihou-wangji-he-gongyu-de-gushi-lo54ac1v8plqft5zxmorlk4-vx0t|Hanji 466]]. It centers [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s criticism that Gong Yu's thrift memorial was true but evasive: palace women, horses, and luxury costs were real problems, yet Yuan's deeper danger was weak judgment under slanderers and corrupt favorites.

The durable synthesis is [[TrueButEvasiveRemonstrance|真实但避重就轻的进谏]]: truthful advice can still fail public duty when it avoids the urgent institutional danger. The host contrasts Gong Yu's selective truth-telling with [[WeiZhengTang|魏征]] and [[FanZhongyan|范仲淹]], treating direct warning as part of an official's responsibility to state, people, and office rather than merely a personal display of courage.

## Key Claims

- In Chuyuan 1 / 48 BCE, Gong Yu advises Yuan to change Han Wudi and Han Xuandi palace precedents by cutting palace women, horses, rare animals, and other consumption.
- Yuan praises and partially accepts the advice, ordering reductions to imperial construction, grain-consuming horses, and meat-eating animals in Shanglin Park.
- Sima Guang still criticizes Gong Yu because loyal ministers should address difficult, urgent problems first rather than only easier, nonurgent ones.
- The episode identifies Yuan's weak decisiveness and the rise of奸佞 as the central danger that Gong Yu avoids.
- The host treats Gong Yu's choice as selective truth-telling: the content is true, but it protects the speaker's reputation while leaving the main problem untouched.
- The episode uses [[WeiZhengTang|Wei Zheng]] and [[FanZhongyan|Fan Zhongyan]] as contrast cases for officials who make dangerous speech part of public duty.
- The closing modern lesson criticizes "为官不为" as looking through a problem without saying it through, or speaking only half-truths while responsibility diffuses.

## Key Quotes

> "知而不言" - the episode's moral charge against officials who recognize danger but avoid naming it.

> "宁鸣而死，不默而生" - Fan Zhongyan's cited phrase used as the direct-speech contrast.
