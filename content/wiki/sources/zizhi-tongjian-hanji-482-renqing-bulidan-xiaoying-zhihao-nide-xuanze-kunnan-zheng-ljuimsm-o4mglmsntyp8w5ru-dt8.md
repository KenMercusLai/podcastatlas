# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》482丨认清“布利丹效应”，治好你的选择困难症！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the [[FengFengshiWesternHan|冯奉世]] Longxi Qiang campaign after [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-481-wei-feng-xihan-fengfengshi-yiju-mie-diguo-libgqzvquegllma9soju57k_jxue|Hanji 481]]: once Han troops assemble in [[LongxiCommandery|陇西郡]], they advance together, defeat the rebels, and leave only defensive/tuntian arrangements after the victory. The host then turns the case into a modern management lesson about [[BuridanIndecisionEffect|布利丹效应]], arguing that leaders must know their goals and bargaining chips, judge incomplete information, and act before opportunity disappears.

The durable synthesis is that decisive action is not only personal courage; it is an organizational responsibility. In this telling, hesitation can waste scarce resources, paralyze teams through too many voices, and make a leader miss the moment when uncertain information still contains usable opportunity.

## Key Claims

- The Han court's later mobilization is partially stood down after the front-line victory, leaving frontier defense and tuntian as the residual policy.
- The episode uses Feng Fengshi's decisiveness as the management contrast to Buridan-style indecision.
- Buridan indecision is explained as failure to clarify both ends and means: the chooser does not know the goal or the available chips.
- For managers, decision failure affects not only the leader but also employees and families exposed to organizational risk.
- Opportunity and information are framed as inversely related: by the time everyone sees the situation clearly, the opportunity has often been competed away.
- The host argues that future commercial competition will make information triage, strategic thinking, and possibly AI-assisted decision support more important.
- The episode closes by treating sustained reading of history as a way to build judgment, breadth, and humane understanding.

## Key Quotes

> "信息越充分，机会越少" - the host's compressed opportunity-information tradeoff.

> "用兵之害，犹豫最大" - military maxim used to connect battlefield delay with managerial indecision.
