# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1154丨曹操如何仅凭一枚锦囊破敌10万？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows the 215 CE [[HefeiBattle215|合肥之战]]. While [[CaoCao|曹操]] campaigns in the west, [[SunQuan|孙权]] attacks [[HefeiLateHan|合肥]] with a reported tenfold-plus advantage; Cao Cao's sealed instructions and [[ZhangLiao|张辽]]'s timing judgment turn eight hundred selected troops into a preemptive shock that damages Wu morale and steadies the defenders before the siege fully forms.

The episode then follows the failed siege, epidemic pressure, and vulnerable Wu withdrawal. Zhang Liao attacks again near Xiaoyao Ford, while [[GuLiLateHan|谷利]] helps Sun Quan clear a broken bridge and escape. The host frames the campaign as both a command case—advance planning, initiative, cohesion, and pursuit—and a source-scoped life lesson about painful experience correcting overconfidence.

## Key Claims
- In 215 CE, Sun Quan attacks Hefei while Cao Cao is campaigning against Zhang Lu; the episode reports roughly one hundred thousand attackers against a seven-thousand-strong garrison.
- Cao Cao leaves delayed sealed instructions with Xue Ti: Zhang Liao and Li Dian are to attack, Yue Jin is to defend the city, and the protecting officer is not to fight.
- Zhang Liao argues that distant relief cannot arrive before the crisis is decided, so the garrison must attack before the Wu force fully concentrates, break its momentum, and stabilize its own morale.
- [[LiDian|李典]] sets aside a poor personal relationship with Zhang Liao and joins the operation rather than let private resentment obstruct the common defense.
- Zhang Liao and Li Dian select eight hundred troops for a dawn assault; the episode says Zhang Liao penetrates toward Sun Quan, breaks out, and then re-enters the encirclement to recover soldiers left behind.
- The initial assault functions as [[PreemptiveMoraleShock|先制士气震慑]]: limited force changes both sides' expectations before the larger siege can exploit the numerical imbalance.
- Sun Quan continues the siege but fails to take the city; low morale, supply consumption, and epidemic disease contribute to withdrawal in the episode's account.
- Zhang Liao exploits the withdrawal gap and attacks near Xiaoyao Ford; Gu Li instructs Sun Quan how to jump a broken bridge, and He Qi's receiving force helps him reach the Wu fleet.
- The host interprets the defeat as a lesson that makes Sun Quan more cautious and makes Zhang Liao famous, but this long-term psychological claim remains episode-level interpretation.

## Key Quotes
> “敌未合，逆击之，折其盛势，以安众心。” - the episode's compressed rationale for attacking before the besieging force fully assembles.

> “个人的恩怨不能耽误国家的大事。” - the host's rendering of Li Dian's decision to cooperate with Zhang Liao.
