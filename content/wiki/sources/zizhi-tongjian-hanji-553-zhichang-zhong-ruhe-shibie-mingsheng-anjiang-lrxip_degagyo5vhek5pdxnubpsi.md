# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》553丨职场中如何识别明升暗降？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Suihe 1 / 8 BCE under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]], between the Chengdi succession decision in Hanji 552 and the Xiongnu land-demand episode in Hanji 554. It begins with Chengdi's honoring of [[KongJiWesternHan|孔吉]] and the Zhou descendant line, then centers on [[HeWuWesternHan|何武]]'s proposal to restore the Three Excellencies so that Da Sima, Da Sikong, and chancellor stand at comparable rank.

The durable synthesis is that office reform can be a power trade, not just a promotion. [[WangGenWesternHan|王根]] receives formal Da Sima recognition and higher-status balance with the other top offices, but loses the Piaoji Jiangjun military command; the episode reads this as "明升暗降," where title, rank, and face rise while the core resource of usable command is removed.

## Key Claims

- Chengdi follows Kuang Heng and Mei Fu's logic that the Confucius family can stand in for Shang-Tang descendants, then elevates Kong Ji and the Zhou successor line to ducal status in 8 BCE.
- He Wu argues that late-dynasty government is too busy for one chancellor of ordinary talent to handle alone, so the old Three Excellencies structure should be restored.
- Chengdi adopts the proposal by giving Wang Gen the Da Sima seal, making He Wu Da Sikong and marquis, and putting Da Sima, Da Sikong, and chancellor on comparable salary/status footing.
- Wang Gen's gain is not simple promotion because the same reform cancels his Piaoji Jiangjun office and removes the military command attached to it.
- The host's workplace analogy treats this as a title-resource swap: a person may receive a higher rank while being asked to hand over clients, troops, decision rights, or other core assets.
- The institutional effect is balancing: shared rank, process participation, and mutual constraint reduce one-person decision power without presenting the move as naked punishment.

## Key Quotes

> "面子有了，里子吃亏了" - the host's summary of Wang Gen's formal gain and practical loss.

> "捧收结合" - the host's phrase for combining visible honoring with quiet power recovery.
