# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》732丨东汉光武帝刘秀驾崩！病死的？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian]] episode backfills the 57 CE death of [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] and the immediate succession of [[EmperorMingOfHan|汉明帝刘庄]], just before the accession layer already covered in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-733-1-lishi-suo-yiwang-de-diwang-zhishi-mingjun-liuzhuang-lor7vhzzpwz1rv9tijksogukjqvr|Hanji 733-1]]. It contrasts the terse [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] death notice with health clues attributed to [[DongguanHanji|《东观汉记》]], treating Liu Xiu's death as a bounded inference problem rather than a confirmed medical diagnosis.

The episode also follows the first accession shock: [[ZhaoXiEasternHan|赵熙]] restores funeral order when the crown prince and princes are unsure of ritual procedure, Liu Zhuang succeeds and honors [[YinLihua|阴丽华]] as empress dowager, and [[LiuJingGuanglingKingEasternHan|刘京 / 刘荆]] sends an anonymous letter trying to pull [[LiuQiangDonghaiKing|刘强]] into rebellion. Liu Qiang reports the letter to Mingdi, while Mingdi keeps the matter private and uses reassurance plus sequencing to contain royal-family risk.

## Key Claims

- [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] gives Liu Xiu's death as a spare court fact: he dies at the Nangong front hall in 57 CE / Zhongyuan 2, aged sixty-two.
- The episode uses [[DongguanHanji|《东观汉记》]] health notices from 41 CE and 44 CE as indirect evidence of earlier dizziness, immobility, cold/wind symptoms, and jaundice-like illness, but it does not settle a death cause.
- Liu Xiu's late image joins overwork, constant state business, and book learning; the source frames "state affairs make me happy" as both admirable ruler diligence and possible health cost.
- The Guangwu evaluation remains balanced around founding by force followed by civilian governance, economic recovery, Confucian learning, and later praise for "Guangwu restoration" order.
- Mingdi's accession begins with ritual uncertainty and court repair: Zhao Xi supplies procedure after Wang Mang-era disruption has weakened inherited ritual memory.
- Liu Jing's anonymous letter to Liu Qiang makes displaced-heir grievance a live succession danger, while Liu Qiang's report and Mingdi's private containment prevent the provocation from becoming open royal conflict.

## Key Quotes

> "手不释卷" - used through the Sun Quan and Lu Meng anecdote to frame Guangwu's learned diligence.

> "治天下我所乐，不为疲也" - Liu Xiu's answer when warned against overwork.
