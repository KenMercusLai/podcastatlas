# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》275｜打破匈奴不败神话，卫青如何做到的？（2）

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode is principally a study of [[HanAnguoWesternHan|韩安国]]'s final decline rather than the detailed [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]] battle analysis suggested by its title. It places Han Anguo between the cautious, balancing statecraft associated with the Wen-Jing period and [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s more aggressive frontier order, then uses a failed demobilization decision to show how a previously useful official can lose political and military fit as objectives, risk tolerance, and reward signals change.

After a captured Xiongnu soldier reportedly persuades Han Anguo that the opposing force has withdrawn, Han asks to release his troops for farming. The [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] return after the force disperses, leaving him with only a small remnant and exposing the defended area to plunder. Wudi then transfers him to [[YoubeipingCommandery|右北平]], while younger commanders such as Wei Qing gain favor through military success; the episode reads Han's illness and death soon afterward as the endpoint of personal misjudgment, political marginalization, and [[PoliticalMilitaryEraFit|political-military era mismatch]].

## Key Claims

- The episode contrasts the stable, conservative political style associated with the Wen-Jing period with Han Wudi's emphasis on initiative, military merit, and sustained action against Xiongnu pressure.
- Han Anguo is presented as capable, steady, calculating, and skilled at balancing interests, while also willing to pursue wealth and attach himself to powerful figures.
- After accepting a prisoner's false report that the Xiongnu had been driven off, Han Anguo reportedly asks to demobilize his force so soldiers can return to agriculture.
- The Xiongnu return soon after demobilization; Han Anguo cannot contest them with the roughly seven hundred men said to remain and watches the raid from camp.
- Wudi responds by transferring Han Anguo to Youbeiping, which the episode interprets as both frontier punishment and political displacement.
- Wei Qing's rising military record supplies the episode's contrast between an ascendant commander and an older official whose cautious operating style no longer matches imperial priorities.
- The episode also places Wudi's talent-recommendation order, [[EmpressWeiZifuWesternHan|卫子夫]]'s elevation, and [[LiuJuCrownPrinceWesternHan|刘据]]'s birth around this transition, but does not develop them into full institutional or biographical accounts.

## Key Quotes

> “死灰复燃。” - the Han Anguo allusion used alongside Chen Ziang to frame reversals of fortune.
