# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》381-2｜怀胎14个月产子，揭秘钩弋夫人的传奇人生

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 94 BCE origin layer behind [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|汉昭帝刘弗陵]]'s later succession. It moves from [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s Taishi 3巡游, 赤雁祥瑞, and immortality-leaning mood to the discovery of [[LadyGouyiWesternHan|钩弋夫人]], Liu Fuling's reported fourteen-month gestation, and the renaming of 钩弋宫门 as "尧母门."

The durable synthesis is that the miraculous-birth story is not only harem legend. The episode treats the "尧母门" gesture as a public political signal: by comparing Liu Fuling's birth to [[YaoShunYuLegend|尧]]'s fourteen-month gestation while [[LiuJuCrownPrinceWesternHan|卫太子刘据]] and [[EmpressWeiZifuWesternHan|卫子夫]] are still in place, Han Wudi creates room for ministers and opportunists to infer a possible heir shift, helping form the interpretive background for the later [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]].

## Key Claims

- In 94 BCE, Han Wudi's巡游, 赤雁 capture, 《赤雁歌》, and sea travel show a late-life receptivity to auspicious signs and immortality-adjacent imagination.
- Lady Gouyi is introduced as a 河间 woman surnamed Zhao, later a 婕妤 living in 钩弋宫, whose entry into the palace is framed through a "奇女" discovery story rather than ordinary selection.
- The episode recounts the legend that she was born with clenched fists and that Han Wudi opened her hand to reveal a hook-like jade shape.
- Liu Fuling is said to have been born after fourteen months of gestation, paralleling the legend of Yao.
- Han Wudi's renaming of the palace gate as "尧母门" is read as a symbolic elevation of Liu Fuling and Lady Gouyi.
- The host treats this gesture as dangerous because Liu Ju is still crown prince; ministers can read the signal as possible favor for a younger alternative heir.
- The episode uses [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s criticism to argue that a ruler's symbolic gestures can become political instructions even without a formal order.
- The source presents the "尧母门" signal as one background condition for later opportunists and the Wugu disaster, not as proof by itself that Han Wudi had already made a settled replacement decision.
