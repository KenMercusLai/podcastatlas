# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1041丨揭秘历史上真实的“关羽降曹”

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the immediate pre-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]] sequence after [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1040-luanshi-yingxiong-caocao-weihe-dudu-kanhao-liubei-lrazzwo4bjspvwmnkx5hoar1iq3x|Hanji 1040]]. [[CaoCao|曹操]] chooses to attack [[LiuBei|刘备]] before facing [[YuanShao|袁绍]], [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] supports the timing judgment, and [[TianFengLateHan|田丰]] sees the chance for Yuan Shao to strike [[Xudu|许都]], but Yuan Shao fails to act.

The second half corrects the familiar [[RomanceOfTheThreeKingdoms|《三国演义》]] "关公约三事" memory. The episode says [[Sanguozhi|《三国志》]] records [[GuanYu|关羽]] being captured, treated generously by Cao Cao, appointed 偏将军, and still intending to return to Liu Bei after first repaying Cao Cao by merit; that makes the case a matter of [[CaptiveSubmissionResidualLoyalty|被俘归顺与旧主忠义]] rather than a historically attested three-condition surrender pact.

## Key Claims
- Cao Cao's pre-Guandu eastward attack is framed as threat sequencing: Liu Bei is currently weaker than Yuan Shao, but his future danger is high enough that leaving him in Xuzhou would create a rear problem.
- Guo Jia's support rests on character and tempo assessment: Yuan Shao is slow and suspicious, while Liu Bei's new base is not yet stable.
- Tian Feng identifies the best Yuan-side opportunity to attack Xudu while Cao Cao is away, but Yuan Shao's private distraction prevents action and confirms the Cao-side estimate of his slowness.
- Cao Cao defeats Liu Bei, captures Liu Bei's family, takes [[Xiapi|下邳]], captures Guan Yu, and also defeats [[ChangXiLateHan|昌豨]] in the East Sea branch.
- The episode treats "关公约三事" as novelized memory rather than a claim supported by official history or wild-history records.
- Guan Yu's actual recorded position is a constrained captive submission: Cao Cao gives him rank and generous treatment, but Guan Yu tells [[ZhangLiao|张辽]] he cannot betray Liu Bei and will leave after repaying Cao Cao's favor with merit.
- The host preserves Guan Yu's loyalty while removing the novel's legalistic three-condition framing; the loyalty appears as residual allegiance and repayment ethics under captivity, not as a formal surrender contract.

## Key Quotes
> "今不击，必为后患." - Cao Cao's threat-sequencing judgment on Liu Bei.

> "吾受刘将军厚恩，誓以共死，不可背之." - Guan Yu's reported loyalty statement as presented in the episode.
