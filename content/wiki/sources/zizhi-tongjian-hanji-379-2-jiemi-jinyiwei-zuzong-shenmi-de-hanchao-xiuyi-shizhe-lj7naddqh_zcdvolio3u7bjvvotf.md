# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》379-2｜揭秘锦衣卫祖宗，神秘的汉朝“绣衣使者”

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] short episode backfills the early [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] special-envoy layer before the later [[XiuyiEnvoyImperialSurveillance|绣衣使者皇权监察]] and Wugu branches. It contrasts [[BaoShengzhiWesternHan|鲍圣之]]'s severe direct envoy authority with [[JunBuyiWesternHan|隽不疑]]'s advice that power should combine威严 and恩惠.

The durable synthesis is that emperor-direct surveillance is not only a coercive title but a governance dilemma. Bao Shengzhi can execute disobedient lower officials while suppressing bandits, yet Jun Buyi's counsel makes moderation part of competent authority. The episode then uses [[WangHeWesternHan|王贺]]'s peaceful handling of Wei Commandery disorder to show the political risk of effective leniency: he saves lives and stabilizes the area, but Wudi dismisses him because the result does not match the emperor's punitive mandate.

## Key Claims

- Bao Shengzhi is appointed as a zhizhi envoy and later remembered as Bao Gongzi, with authority to suppress banditry, inspect commanderies and kingdoms, and punish disobedient officials below the two-thousand-stone rank without prior imperial approval.
- The episode treats this office as an especially concentrated form of Han Wudi's emperor-direct public-order power, broader and harsher than ordinary local administration.
- Jun Buyi, rendered in the transcript as 郡布夷, refuses to remove his sword at Bao's gate and uses ritual self-possession to signal that he is not an ordinary supplicant.
- Jun Buyi advises Bao Shengzhi that excessive hardness breaks and excessive softness abandons government, so severity should be joined to grace and moderation.
- Bao Shengzhi accepts the advice and recommends Jun Buyi, after which Wudi summons him and appoints him Qingzhou inspector.
- Wang He, rendered in the transcript as 王赫, handles Wei Commandery unrest without arrests or executions, releases local officials, and induces voluntary surrender through gratitude and persuasion.
- Han Wudi treats Wang He's non-killing outcome as failure to carry out the emperor's will to establish威 by killing; court pleas spare Wang He's life, but he is dismissed.
- The closing Western Regions notice says Han Wudi enfeoffs the surrendered Xiongnu figure Jiehe / Chengmin as Kailing marquis, has him lead Loulan troops against [[CheshiKingdomWesternHan|车师国]], and sees the force retreat after Xiongnu cavalry rescue Cheshi.

## Key Quotes

> "施威严的时候给予恩惠" - Jun Buyi's balancing advice as the episode presents it.

> "救活上万人" - Wang He's self-account after dismissal for lenient handling.
