# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》173｜韩信逃过胯下之辱 没躲过鸟尽弓藏（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-172-liubang-yangyou-yunmengze-zhiqin-hanxin-lrpkye8y6ibqdqs9pz7artk-6chv|Hanji 172]] into [[HanXin|韩信]]'s life after demotion from Chu king to Huaiyin marquis. Isolated in [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]], Han Xin visits [[FanKuai|樊哙]], receives extreme courtesy, then humiliates him with the line "生乃与哙等为伍." The host argues that the insult may be more than arrogance: because Fan Kuai belongs to [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s Pei/Feng veteran military circle and is tied to [[LuZhi|吕雉]], Han Xin may be publicly cutting distance from a dangerous potential alliance. The episode then opens the larger question of why Han Xin, who once held the leverage to decide the Chu-Han outcome, repeatedly failed to act decisively for his own political survival.

## Key Claims
- After the Yunmeng arrest, Han Xin is no longer a regional king but remains politically dangerous because his military reputation, old relationships, and resentment are still visible.
- Han Xin's isolation is personal and political: [[ZhongliMo|钟离眜]] is dead, [[LiZuoChe|李左车]] has left, [[XiaoHe|萧何]] cannot safely approach him, and Liu Bang has effectively abandoned him.
- Fan Kuai is not merely a crude general mocked by an idiom. The episode stresses his role as Liu Bang's old follower, in-law, Hongmen protector, named marquis, and member of the Pei/Feng military circle.
- Fan Kuai's accidental "大王" address shows how sensitive Han Xin's lost royal status remains in Chang'an.
- "耻与哙伍" records Han Xin's pride and wounded status, but the host resists treating it only as low emotional intelligence.
- A close Han Xin-Fan Kuai relationship could look dangerous to Liu Bang: Han Xin has strategy and prestige, while Fan Kuai has military standing, old-follower legitimacy, and Lu-family ties.
- Han Xin's public disparagement of Fan Kuai can be read as [[PublicDisavowalSelfProtection|公开切割式自保]]: he signals that he is not joining Fan Kuai's faction or the Lu-linked veteran group.
- The episode reopens the earlier [[TripartiteBalanceStrategy|三分天下式制衡]] question by saying Han Xin had at least two strong chances to rebel or detach from Liu Bang, including after conquering Qi.
- Han Xin had once criticized [[XiangYu|项羽]] for "妇人之仁" and indecision over rewards, but the host argues that Han Xin himself failed to decide when his own leverage was greatest.
