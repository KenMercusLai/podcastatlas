# 《资治通鉴·周纪》43丨孟尝君妻子偷情被发现他咋处理？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]] branch by testing his retainer network under two scandals: retainers kill Zhao locals after Mengchang Jun is mocked for being short, and another retainer is discovered having an affair with Mengchang Jun's wife. The episode reads the second story as [[RetainerRiskConversion|门客风险转化]]: Mengchang Jun avoids immediate punishment, later sends the retainer to [[WeyState|卫国]], and the retainer eventually helps prevent a Wey move against [[QiState|齐国]].

## Key Claims
- The episode first returns to [[ZhaoState|赵国]], where [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] receives Mengchang Jun and local people mock Mengchang Jun's short stature.
- The host uses the mockery to explain a Warring States aristocratic body ideal: a noble man was expected to carry martial stature and warrior bearing, so public ridicule could be read as a major dignity injury.
- Mengchang Jun's retainers respond by killing hundreds of people and nearly destroying a county, which the episode treats as intelligible in its historical honor world but morally unacceptable by present-day standards.
- The episode therefore extends [[AristocraticHonorOverLife|贵族名誉高于生命]] from self-destructive dignity sensitivity into other-directed violence.
- In a [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] story, one of Mengchang Jun's retainers has an affair with Mengchang Jun's wife, not a concubine, making the scandal a household-order and succession-legitimacy risk.
- Mengchang Jun initially refuses public punishment, saying attraction and mutual feeling are humanly understandable and telling the informant not to pursue it.
- A year later, he separates the retainer from the household by preparing carriages, money, and goods and sending him to Wey, where the ruler has ties to Mengchang Jun.
- In Wey, the retainer is valued by the ruler; when Wey later considers joining other states to attack Qi, the retainer persuades the ruler to withdraw by invoking old Qi-Wey peace commitments and Mengchang Jun's relationship.
- Qi people praise Mengchang Jun for turning harm into advantage, but the host cautions that the anti-Qi Wey campaign scene may contain narrative embellishment because Wey was weak.
- The episode closes with [[SimaQian|司马迁]]'s later travel note around Mengchang Jun's old place: the local memory of many brave and combative households is attributed to Mengchang Jun's large-scale retainer recruitment.

## Key Quotes
> "三观很正，从来没有绝对标准" - the episode's warning against treating historical value judgments as timeless.

> "因祸为福，转败为功" - the Qi people's praise for Mengchang Jun's handling of the retainer scandal.

> "好勇斗狠的人" - the remembered social residue around Mengchang Jun's former territory.
