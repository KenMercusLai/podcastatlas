# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1143丨古人离婚能有多奇葩？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode connects relationship boundaries, a story about [[Mengzi|孟子]] and his mother, and the 214 CE capture of [[WanChengLujiang|皖城]]. The opening argues that care should not require self-erasure or dependence on outside approval; the household story makes etiquette reciprocal by having Mencius's mother question his unannounced entry before judging his wife's private posture.

The military sequence presents [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]] as recognizing an emerging Cao-side grain base before it matured. He combines supply risk, weak defenses, rising-water transport, falling-water retreat danger, reinforcement time, and Wu morale into a rapid-assault case; [[GanNing|甘宁]] leads the ascent, the city falls before [[ZhangLiao|张辽]] arrives, and Sun Quan appoints Lyu Meng administrator of Lujiang.

## Key Claims

- The opening recommends reducing approval-seeking, screening relationships that repeatedly consume one's agency, and defending interests without treating kindness as limitless obligation.
- Its stronger claim that pity necessarily conceals superiority is presented as the host's interpretation, not as a general psychological rule.
- In the Mencius anecdote, the wife's relaxed posture occurs in a private room, while Mencius enters without warning; his mother therefore redirects judgment from the observed posture to reciprocal respect and the observer's own conduct.
- [[ZhuGuangLateHan|朱光]] is said to cultivate rice and build irrigation around Wan City so Cao's side can support a larger forward force against Jiangdong.
- Lyu Meng argues for attacking before harvest and before the position becomes a mature supply base, making the target's future capacity part of present campaign choice.
- He rejects time-consuming siege earthworks because preparation would strengthen the defenders, invite reinforcements, and risk stranding Wu forces after the water level fell.
- Sun Quan accepts a four-sided rapid assault; Gan Ning leads the ascent, Lyu Meng follows with elite troops and drums the attack, and the city falls before Zhang Liao can relieve it.
- The episode uses the operation as later evidence that directed study widened Lyu Meng's judgment beyond bravery, while preserving accumulated field experience as part of that development.
- The closing quotation “足食，足兵，民信之矣” previews a later discussion of food, force, and public trust rather than fully developing that governance argument here.

## Key Quotes

> “粮食就是兵力。” - the host's summary of why cultivation around Wan City changes military capacity.

> “足食，足兵，民信之矣。” - the closing *Analects* quotation used to preview the next episode.

## Contradictions

- Place-name caution: the supplied episode repeatedly writes “宛城,” but its Lujiang setting, Zhu Guang, Sun Quan, Lyu Meng, Gan Ning, and 214 CE campaign identify the target as [[WanChengLujiang|皖城]], not [[WanCounty|宛县 / 宛城]]. The wiki preserves the transcript wording as a source inconsistency while linking the disambiguated Lujiang page.
- No settled contradiction is adopted. The source extends Hanji 1118's Lyu Meng learning sequence and Hanji 1128-1133's water-and-retreat logic with a later field case.
- Source-scope caution: the Mencius household dialogue, ritual details, exact campaign date, irrigation scale, civilian and force totals, one-day timing, first-ascent sequence, personal motives, and direct causal weight assigned to study require comparison with underlying texts.
- Interpretation caution: the opening's claims about “bad people,” chronically distressed relationships, pity, approval, and inner stability are broad advice rather than evidence that every difficult relationship is exploitative or that dependence and compassion are inherently unhealthy.
