# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》325-1｜卫青如何一战打断匈奴的脊梁？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]]'s arm of the [[MobeiBattleWesternHan|漠北之战]]. Wei Qing orders a two-wing encirclement against the [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] chanyu's main force; after the chanyu escapes northwest with a small mounted party, Wei Qing pursues through the night and attacks the force left behind. The episode presents the leader's disappearance as the decisive morale break: Xiongnu troops lose the will to resist when they learn that the chanyu has fled.

The Han force does not capture the chanyu, but the episode reports nineteen thousand enemy killed or captured, seizure and destruction of supplies at a city associated with Zhao Xin, and more than ten days during which the chanyu is missing from his wider polity. A Right Luli king briefly claims the chanyu title before relinquishing it when the ruler returns. The opening invokes [[LiGuangHanGeneral|李广]] and [[SimaQian|司马迁]]'s “桃李不言，下自成蹊” appraisal as a reputation frame, but leaves Li Guang's final suicide for the sequel.

## Key Claims

- Wei Qing responds to the chanyu's main force with a left-and-right encirclement rather than a simple frontal attack.
- The episode says the armies remain closely matched in the fighting until the chanyu abandons the field with roughly a hundred riders.
- A captive reveals the flight after dark; Wei Qing sends close cavalry in pursuit and drives the main force against the remaining Xiongnu troops.
- The chanyu's disappearance turns a battlefield withdrawal into a command-confidence shock, after which the force left behind disperses.
- The pursuers travel more than two hundred li without catching the chanyu, while the episode reports nineteen thousand Xiongnu killed or captured.
- Wei Qing's army reaches a city associated with Zhao Xin, takes its supplies, rests, and burns material it cannot carry before returning south.
- The chanyu remains out of contact for more than ten days; a Right Luli king briefly assumes the title and gives it up when the chanyu reappears.
- Li Guang's moral reputation and tragic death are introduced as a framing question rather than resolved in this installment.

## Key Quotes

> "桃李不言，下自成蹊。" - the *Shiji* appraisal invoked to frame Li Guang's reputation.

> "单于逃走的一刻，这场战役的胜负实际上已经分晓。" - the episode's interpretation of the command-presence turning point.
