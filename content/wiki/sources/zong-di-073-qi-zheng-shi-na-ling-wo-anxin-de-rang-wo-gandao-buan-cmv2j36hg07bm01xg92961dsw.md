# 总第073期：正是那令我安心的，让我感到不安

## Summary
This [[DuBaoTeleread|读报teleread / 独报]] episode connects aviation-security design, pilot health disclosure, the centenary of the five-day workweek, housework-filled leave, and anxiety during an open-ended employment gap. Its central framework is [[RiskRiskTradeoff|risk-risk trade-off]]: a measure that reduces one danger can increase another because security mechanisms alter incentives and access rather than simply removing risk.

The episode then generalizes that structure through [[SameCauseAttractionAversion|喜恶同因]]. A locked cockpit door, strict health screening, secure employment, a finite holiday, or a valued personality trait may produce both reassurance and discomfort through the same causal chain. In the work-rest branch, it extends [[WorkRestBoundary|工作休假边界]] by distinguishing calendar time from practical control over time and by arguing that a known return point can make temporary freedom feel safer than an indefinite gap.

## Key Claims
- [[RiskRiskTradeoff]] names cases where reducing one risk increases another rather than delivering a costless safety improvement.
- A reinforced cockpit door can deter external intrusion while making intervention harder when danger originates inside the cockpit.
- More demanding pilot-health certification may identify genuine problems while increasing the career cost of disclosure and therefore the incentive to conceal symptoms.
- [[WorkRestBoundary|Work-rest boundaries]] depend not only on formal leave and protection from messages, but also on whether domestic labor, errands, self-improvement pressure, and uncertainty leave usable discretionary time.
- A holiday's known endpoint can constrain freedom while also reassuring the worker that income, structure, and a place to return still exist.
- [[SameCauseAttractionAversion|喜恶同因]] treats some valued benefits and disliked costs as causally coupled: decisiveness can become forcefulness, tenderness can accompany hesitation, and freedom from work can also remove security and structure.
- Recognizing coupled benefits and costs does not eliminate conflict, but it can reduce the feeling that a person, institution, or life choice has unexpectedly betrayed its promise.
