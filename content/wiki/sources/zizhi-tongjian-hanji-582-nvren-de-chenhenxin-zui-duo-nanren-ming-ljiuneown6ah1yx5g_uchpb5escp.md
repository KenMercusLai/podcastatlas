# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》582丨女人的嗔恨心，最夺男人命

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianping 2-3 / 5-4 BCE under [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]], immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-583-douzhidao-ta-you-wenti-weishenme-hai-tiba-llkef6xxkyf5kufv2j_zcqv3wogs|Hanji 583]]. It centers [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]]'s continued pursuit of [[FuXiWesternHan|傅喜]], [[ZhuBoWesternHan|朱博]]'s attempt to execute that will by bundling Fu Xi with [[HeWuWesternHan|何武]], Aidi's discovery of the backchannel, and Zhu Bo's suicide after being summoned for trial.

The durable synthesis is that clever political packaging cannot always hide patron-driven intent. Zhu Bo tries to make a targeted strike look like general accountability, but the emperor reads the political context, Zhao Xuan confesses, and Zhu Bo becomes a negative case of serving a powerful patron's resentment. The episode then contrasts him with [[PingDangWesternHan|平当]], who refuses a deathbed marquisate and becomes a positive case for restraint, reputation, and ending well.

## Key Claims

- Fu Taihou remains angry after Fu Xi has already been dismissed and sent back to his fief, so she seeks a further deprivation of his marquisate.
- Zhu Bo agrees to pursue Fu Taihou's request through Kongxiang Hou despite Zhao Xuan's warning that Aidi has already made a final punishment decision.
- Zhu Bo's risk-control tactic is to bundle Fu Xi with He Wu so the memorial looks like a general principle about dismissed officials retaining rank rather than a targeted Fu-family revenge move.
- Han Aidi recognizes the likely backchannel because he knows Fu Taihou's anger at Fu Xi, interrogates Zhao Xuan, and has Peng Xuan and central officials review the case.
- The outcome is asymmetrical: Zhao Xuan avoids death, Kongxiang Hou loses part of his fief, while Zhu Bo is summoned to Tingwei custody and commits suicide.
- The episode pairs Zhu Bo's failure with Ping Dang's refusal of a formal marquisate while gravely ill, treating the refusal as concern for public office, family reputation, and a good end.
- The Wusun-Xiongnu notice shows Han court authority still mediating frontier hostage diplomacy even while Aidi's inner court is unstable.

## Key Quotes

> "人贵在知足" - the host's conclusion from Ping Dang's refusal of the marquisate.

> "一个人最大的敌人就是自己的贪欲" - the episode's closing moral frame contrasting Ping Dang with Zhu Bo.
