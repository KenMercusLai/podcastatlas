# #402 即将中断的粮食援助：一个中国人在南苏丹看到的饥饿

## Summary
This [[ShengdongJixi|声东击西]] episode has [[XuTao|徐涛]] interview [[WangHongrui|王洪瑞]] about his [[WorldFoodProgramme|WFP]] field visit to [[WedweilRefugeeCamp|维德维尔难民营]] near [[Aweil|阿维尔]] in [[SouthSudan|南苏丹]]. The episode turns food aid cuts from an abstract funding issue into concrete cases: new arrivals waiting for registration, children eating salt, a mother pawning her WFP card, nutrition support limited by measurement thresholds, and school meals keeping children in class. Its main wiki contribution is a linked humanitarian-aid frame connecting [[RefugeeAidRegistrationGap]], [[EmergencyCashAssistance]], [[NutritionTriageThreshold]], [[HumanitarianAidFundingShortfall]], and [[SchoolMealsAsEducationAccess]].

## Key Claims
- [[SouthSudan|South Sudan]] is presented as a compound humanitarian crisis shaped by conflict, weak state capacity, economic stress, climate pressure, disease, and displacement from [[Sudan]].
- Newly arrived refugees can face a [[RefugeeAidRegistrationGap]] because [[UNHCR]] identity checks precede [[WorldFoodProgramme|WFP]] emergency cash and biscuit distribution.
- [[EmergencyCashAssistance]] becomes fragile when cash replaces a fuller food basket and refugees have few work, farming, or government-support alternatives.
- [[NutritionTriageThreshold]] makes severe malnutrition a gate for scarce fortified food, exposing how limited resources force aid systems to prioritize the most visibly acute cases.
- [[HumanitarianAidFundingShortfall]] is the structural background: the source says WFP South Sudan faces a large funding gap and warns that refugee support may be cancelled in October.
- [[SchoolMealsAsEducationAccess]] shows one constructive path: meals can keep hungry children enrolled long enough for schooling to change life chances.
- [[HumanitarianWitnessingBoundary]] captures Wang Hongrui's ethical tension: carrying stories outward may help funding and attention, but it does not feed the person asking for dinner now.
