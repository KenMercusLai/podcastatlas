# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》565丨芮淇和你聊聊小饭局里的大学问

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Suihe 2 / 7 BCE under [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]], after the accession and reform notes in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-562-han-aidi-jiwei-shi-you-duo-biequ-lpv8_ks5b3c0ow_3o9_mvxi9n3xu|Hanji 562]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-564-gudai-xueshu-dalao-bi-xiangxiang-de-gengniu-ljf28ze9-p218vuzjicjcwutvrhl|Hanji 564]], and before the later Dingtao title-conflict sequence. It uses an imperial banquet-seat dispute to show how everyday-looking seating rules can become public declarations of court rank.

The durable synthesis is that [[BanquetSeatingStatusPolitics|宴席座次名分政治]] makes ritual order visible. [[WangMang|王莽]] sees [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]] placed beside [[WangZhengjun|王政君]], removes the seat as improper for a Dingtao consort-side figure, and thereby turns a banquet into a factional rupture: Fu Taihou refuses the feast, Aidi lets Wang Mang retire with honors, [[FuXiWesternHan|傅喜]] is not trusted by Fu Taihou despite kinship, [[ShiDanWesternHan|施丹]] becomes Da Sima, and [[XieGuangWesternHan|谢光]] later helps Aidi move against the Wang family through accusations against [[WangGenWesternHan|王根]] and Wang Kuang.

## Key Claims

- Banquet seating works as a rank signal because where Fu Taihou sits relative to Wang Zhengjun publicly answers whether Dingtao-line status can approach the supreme dowager order.
- Wang Mang's correction is a ritual-order act and a political provocation: he treats Fu Taihou as unqualified to sit alongside Wang Zhengjun, and Fu Taihou reads the change as intolerable humiliation.
- Aidi removes Wang Mang from active office but preserves high honor through gold, an anju carriage, special status, palace access, and regular imperial meals.
- Fu Xi's learning and virtue make him a plausible Wang Mang replacement to the court, but Fu Taihou blocks him because he has refused to cooperate with her outer-relative and title politics.
- He Wu, Tang Lin, and other ministers defend Fu Xi as a loyal and upright inner-court candidate, so Aidi later has room to recall him despite not immediately reversing the initial decision.
- Du Ye's memorial gives Aidi an anti-Wang interpretive file, but Aidi waits until Xie Guang's accusations make the timing more usable before punishing Wang-family figures.
- The episode frames the Wang family as moving from high favor toward decline through a combination of ritual conflict, old misconduct accusations, Aidi's timing, and court-opinion preparation.
