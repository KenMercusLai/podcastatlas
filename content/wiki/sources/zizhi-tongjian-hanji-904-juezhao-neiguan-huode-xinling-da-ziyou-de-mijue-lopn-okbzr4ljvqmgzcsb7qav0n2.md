## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 173-174 CE layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-905-fugui-ruhe-xianzhong-qiu-lklxx5nquka2mtbllxivk0a7b-fu|Hanji 905]]: epidemic, earthquake, eclipse, [[Xianbei|鲜卑]] pressure, amnesties, and rapid Three Excellencies turnover under [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]. It also gives an earlier Cao-family entry point before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-925-tuoxie-bushi-rensong-xuehui-tuoxie-lu-geng-changliang-limafqskgblrwetestyh39w-hzm4|Hanji 925]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-926-zhenzheng-de-qiangzhe-dou-dongde-zaoshi-lssmabzoku4ue28irdmfn0-d-0dc|Hanji 926]]: nineteen-year-old [[CaoCao|曹操]] moves through [[CaoTengLateHan|曹腾]], [[CaoSong|曹嵩]], Xiaolian recommendation, [[QiaoXuanLateHan|桥玄]], [[XuShao|许劭]], and [[SimaFangLateHan|司马防]] toward the Luoyang north commandant post.

The opening practice section recommends early rising, sitting, breath attention, movement, and "觉照内观" as modern self-cultivation. The wiki keeps those practice and body-energy claims source-scoped: they extend [[MeditativeStabilityPractice|定力修行框架]] and adjacent mind-body pages, but do not prove medical or physiological causation.

## Key Claims
- The 173 CE sequence pairs epidemic, Zong Ju's death, amnesty, senior-office replacement, Beihai earthquake, Yang Ci's removal, and a late-year solar eclipse, making disaster and omen notices part of [[CourtOfficeCapture|朝廷开府任官失序]] before later Hongdu, office-sale, and palace-access cases.
- [[TangZhenLateHan|唐珍]]'s appointment as sikong matters because the source identifies him as [[TangHengLateHan|唐衡]]'s younger brother, using eunuch-family connection to explain high-office access.
- The late-173 Xianbei raids into Youzhou and Bingzhou extend the frontier-pressure line before Hanji 905-910.
- The 174 CE amnesty, [[ChenDanLateHan|陈耽]]'s taiwei appointment, Zhongshan kingdom abolition, and Jinan king ritual arrangement keep Emperor Ling's early reign framed by court repair gestures and dynastic-housekeeping moves.
- Cao Cao's first entry is not presented as ability alone: the episode stresses the eunuch-family route through Cao Teng and Cao Song, then the formal Xiaolian/langguan path.
- Qiao Xuan and Xu Shao provide reputation certification before the fuller Hanji 926 reputation-making branch; Xu Shao's appraisal matters because circulation turns judgment into career capital.
- Sima Fang's recommendation for Cao Cao as Luoyang north commandant links reputation to office placement: he reads Cao Cao's bold personality as fitting the capital's difficult enforcement environment.
- Cao Cao is disappointed because he expected a higher office, so the episode makes ambition and status comparison part of his early profile.
- The meditation, abdominal breathing, movement, and "yangqi" claims are recorded as the host's practice teaching, not as independent medical evidence.
