## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the "焚书坑儒" branch by again separating [[QinBookBurningPolicy|焚书]] from the still-unfolding坑儒 incident, then moves into [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]]'s thirty-fifth year, 212 BCE. It frames [[AfangPalace|阿房宫]] as part of a late-Qin construction system that also includes [[QinZhidao|秦直道]], [[Xianyang|咸阳]] expansion, [[QinShiHuangMausoleum|秦始皇陵]], palace networks, forced relocation, and convict labor. The ending returns to [[LuSheng|卢生]] and [[ImmortalityQuestPolitics|求仙政治]], showing how advice about hiding the emperor's whereabouts turns immortal-seeking into [[ImperialItinerarySecrecy|行踪保密恐怖]] and sets up the investigation of allegedly slanderous儒生.

## Key Claims
- The episode treats "焚书" and "坑儒" as separate incidents; this source supplies the political and fangshi prelude to the坑儒 branch but stops before the full punishment sequence.
- In 212 BCE, Qin sends [[MengTian|蒙恬]] to build [[QinZhidao|秦直道]] from Yunyang to Jiuyuan, while the earlier relocation of 120,000 powerful households makes [[Xianyang|咸阳]] crowded enough to support a capital-expansion rationale for [[AfangPalace|阿房宫]].
- Afang Palace is presented as both practical expansion and symbolic architecture: the front hall is enormous, the road and gallery system reaches toward the Qinling, and the Wei River becomes an earthly counterpart to the Milky Way.
- "Afang" is only a temporary project name in the episode because [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]] dies before the complex receives its intended formal name.
- [[QinShiHuangMausoleum|秦始皇陵]] and Afang Palace together mobilize 700,000 punished laborers in the source's account, while stone, timber, palace construction, and forced population movement make the project a case of [[ImperialConstructionOverload|帝国工程过载]].
- Afang Palace becomes unfinished because Qin Shi Huang dies, labor priority shifts toward the Lishan mausoleum, [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] later restarts the palace work, and rebellion news soon interrupts the revived project.
- The episode uses Du Mu's "阿房宫赋" as a political warning: collapse is not caused by the building alone, but by corrupted power damaging the people whose support the regime needs.
- [[LuSheng|卢生]]'s second-stage advice says the emperor should travel unpredictably, conceal his residence, and thereby avoid ghosts while waiting for immortals and deathless medicine.
- Qin Shi Huang accepts this logic deeply enough to call himself "真人" rather than "朕," linking title language, sacred aspiration, and court secrecy.
- The resulting system connects 270 palaces around Xianyang, forbids movement of registered people and furnishings, executes those who leak the emperor's whereabouts, and even kills all those present after a leak about his displeasure at [[LiSi|李斯]]'s large entourage cannot be solved.
- The source's final turn is the [[FangshiAccountabilityTrap|方士问责陷阱]]: once the emperor follows fangshi advice but still receives no immortal or deathless medicine, specialists who promised access to the unseen face lethal political danger.

## Key Quotes
> "焚书和坑儒是两码事" - the episode's repeated boundary between the prior source and this branch.

> "上可以坐万人，下可以建五丈旗" - the reported scale of the Afang Palace front hall.

> "吾慕真人，自谓真人，不称朕" - the source's wording for Qin Shi Huang's adoption of the "真人" posture.
