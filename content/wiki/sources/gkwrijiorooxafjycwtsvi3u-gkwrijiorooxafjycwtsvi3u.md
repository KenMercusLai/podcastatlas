# 低价城际大巴吸引乘客，AI 短剧算力用量引发争议

## Summary
This [[ShengdongZaokafei|声动早咖啡]] roundup connects company performance, AI infrastructure, streaming advertising, vehicle globalization, consumer-brand pricing, and passenger transport. Its most durable contributions are methodological and operational: token volume should not be treated as a direct measure of compute share; open inference software can improve throughput, latency, and serving economics across hardware platforms; and low-cost intercity coaches can compete by moving stops closer to dispersed origins and destinations rather than relying only on fare cuts.

## Key Claims
- The source reports strong quarterly revenue and profit at [[SpaceX]] alongside a year-to-date loss, substantial AI-related revenue, and special committees exploring a possible combination with [[Tesla]]; it also reports a European telecom response to [[Starlink]] in mobile-satellite spectrum.
- [[Netflix]] is described as facing its slowest revenue growth in three years, difficulty acquiring subscribers, a lower full-year advertising forecast, and slower-than-planned rollout of some advertising products.
- [[AITO|问界]] is reported to be targeting a European launch in autumn 2027 with [[Bosch]] cooperation on assisted driving, cockpit, and chassis systems, as [[Seres]] takes greater responsibility for overseas execution.
- The source-named Deepsea organization is said to have open-sourced [[DeepInfer]], an inference framework compatible with [[Nvidia]] and [[Huawei]] Ascend systems, to raise throughput, reduce latency, and lower model-serving cost.
- A reported 55% share of token use attributed to AI short-drama generation was sometimes translated into “half of domestic compute,” but [[ByteDance]] reportedly said only about 10% of its total compute served video-model training and inference. This is a [[TokenComputeMetricMismatch]], not a like-for-like contradiction.
- The episode says video and image generation are heavy token-use categories and argues that [[AIShortDrama]] combines broad demand, high unit cost, a functioning commercial loop, and changing production methods; it also reports that [[Hongguo]] has become China's largest micro-drama platform by users.
- [[Adidas]] is reported to have reached record quarterly operating profit through tariff refunds, lower discounting, and stronger full-price sales, while [[AntaSports|Anta]] deepened family representation in [[AmerSports|Amer Sports]] governance through Ding Shaoxiang's board appointment.
- [[Uniqlo]] is reported to be raising selected Japanese prices because of materials, logistics, and yen pressure while leaving China prices unchanged for now.
- Cheap Pearl River Delta coaches are described as using fares as low as RMB 9.9, free Wi-Fi, and stops near multiple metro stations to compete with rail, cars, ride-hailing, and carpooling through a [[DistributedIntercityCoachModel]].

## Key Quotes
> "Token 消耗比例不能直接等同于算力占比" — the episode's measurement warning in the AI-short-drama segment.

> "竞争力不仅来自低价" — the episode's interpretation of the coach model's distributed-stop advantage.

> "提高处理量、缩短响应时间，并降低 AI 使用成本" — the stated purpose of the DeepInfer release.
