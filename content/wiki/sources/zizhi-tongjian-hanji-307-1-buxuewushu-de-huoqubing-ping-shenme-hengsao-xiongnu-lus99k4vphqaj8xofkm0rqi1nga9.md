# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》307-1｜不学无术的霍去病，凭什么横扫匈奴？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode introduces the [[FirstHexiCampaignWesternHan|第一次河西之战]] in 121 BCE. It places [[HuoQubing|霍去病]] at the head of a reported ten-thousand-cavalry force from Longxi and explains his initial western attack through speed, light equipment, repeated penetration, separation of local groups, and replenishment from the campaign environment rather than a single search for the main [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] army.

The episode's most durable addition is political as well as tactical. Huo is said to offer peripheral groups protection for people and property if they submit, trading some immediate captive credit and plunder for reduced resistance and greater isolation of the core opposing force. The account therefore presents the campaign as a combination of [[MilitaryDoctrineEraFit|mounted operational adaptation]] and conditional incorporation, while its force and loss totals, six-day distance, number of defeated polities, precise route, and proposed pre-campaign consultation with [[ZhangQianWesternHan|张骞]] remain source-scoped.

## Key Claims

- In 121 BCE, Han Wudi sends the newly appointed Piaoqi general Huo Qubing with a force described as ten thousand cavalry from Longxi against Hunye King's western Xiongnu field.
- The episode says Huo crosses the Yellow River, attacks the Suopu group, kills its ruler, and moves through several western groups over six days and more than one thousand li.
- Huo's operational method is described as light cavalry, high tempo, limited delay for plunder or positional consolidation, penetration between groups, and repeated local attacks before a coordinated response can form.
- Rather than seeking only a decisive clash with the main Xiongnu force, Huo is said to divide the western political field and induce peripheral groups to surrender.
- The episode describes campaign replenishment or “以战养战” as part of the method, but does not provide a logistical reconstruction sufficient to establish its exact mechanism or sustainability.
- Huo allegedly promises that surrendering groups will retain their people and property, reducing the burden on the moving Han force while isolating stronger Xiongnu centers.
- The host infers that Huo probably consulted Zhang Qian about Hexi geography and group conditions before departure; the supplied episode provides no direct evidence that such a meeting occurred.

## Key Quotes

> “有的放矢。” - the episode's phrase for acting with a defined target and locally informed plan.

> “不仅是军事天才，而且颇有政治头脑。” - the host's judgment that surrender policy mattered alongside battlefield speed.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. This source supplies the first-Hexi operation before Hanji 308 and 309-1's second-Hexi sequence; the campaigns must not be merged.
- The opening says seven thousand of ten thousand troops were lost, but the compact episode does not explain the accounting basis or reconcile that cost with the later triumphal summary. Both the loss and the victory figures remain episode-attributed.
- “六天上千里,” the force balance, Suopu identification, five defeated “kingdoms,” exact route, casualty and surrender totals, replenishment mechanics, and promises to submitting groups require primary-source and specialist comparison.
- The claim that Huo consulted Zhang Qian before departure is expressly inferential. It is useful as a hypothesis about intelligence preparation, not evidence of a documented meeting.
- “不学无术,” “军事天才,” and “横扫匈奴” are rhetorical or evaluative framing. The source supports a bounded operational account, not ignorance of all military learning or final defeat of Xiongnu.
