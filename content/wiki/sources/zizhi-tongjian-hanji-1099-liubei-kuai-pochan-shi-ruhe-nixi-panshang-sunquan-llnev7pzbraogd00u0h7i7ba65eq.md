# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1099丨刘备快破产时，如何逆袭攀上孙权？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[LuSu|鲁肃]] after [[ChangbanBattle|长坂坡]] as he redirects the defeated [[LiuBei|刘备]] from a claimed refuge with [[WuJuLateHan|吴巨]] toward an alliance with [[SunQuan|孙权]]. Lu Su praises Jiangdong's capacity, discredits the weaker fallback, describes the move as joint enterprise rather than dependency, and invokes his friendship with [[ZhugeJin|诸葛瑾]] to give [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] a personal bridge into the proposed coalition.

The episode reads the resulting change as a sequencing revision to the [[LongzhongPlan|隆中对]]: alliance precedes possession of Jingzhou rather than following it. Its opening five-minute claim that maternal relationships shape adult money behavior is kept source-scoped because the episode provides no evidentiary support and does not connect it to the historical narrative.

## Key Claims
- After the Changban defeat, Lu Su asks Liu Bei where he will go; Liu Bei names Cangwu administrator Wu Ju, which the host interprets as a face-saving answer rather than a strong plan.
- Lu Su presents Sun Quan as the higher-value partner: Jiangdong has territory, troops, supplies, and a concentration of capable people, while Wu Ju is portrayed as weak, remote, and vulnerable to absorption.
- The persuasion avoids the language of surrender or dependence and instead casts the proposed move as equal cooperation in a shared enterprise.
- Liu Bei accepts the proposal after looking to Zhuge Liang, and the episode treats that glance as consultation with the strategist who had already included cooperation with Sun Quan in the Longzhong plan.
- Lu Su's statement that he is Zhuge Jin's friend creates a personal trust bridge between the Sun and Liu camps through Zhuge Liang's elder brother.
- The host argues that alliance-first sequencing improves Liu Bei's survival position because taking Jingzhou before securing Sun Quan could leave him exposed between Cao Cao in the north and Sun Quan in the south.
- The opening psychology segment proposes three maternal patterns—complaint, avoidance, and control—as causes of adult shame, avoidance, or compensatory spending, but these claims remain unverified and disconnected from the main historical branch.

## Key Quotes
> “我是诸葛子瑜的朋友。” — Lu Su's relationship signal to Zhuge Liang as rendered by the episode.

> “共济事业。” — the episode's compact phrase for presenting alliance as cooperation rather than dependency.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is recorded. The source supplies an alliance-opening layer before Hanji 1101's later surrender debate and the Red Cliffs sequence.
- The episode's reconstruction of Liu Bei's inner delight, the meaning of his glance, Lu Su's precise rhetorical intent, and the claim that alliance-first sequencing was unambiguously beneficial remain host interpretation.
- Wu Ju's capacity, the Longzhong plan's exact intended sequence, and the complete diplomatic process require broader evidence; this episode ends after Liu Bei accepts Lu Su's proposal.
- The opening mother-money typology is not treated as established psychological or financial evidence.
