# Hassabis 卸任、Jeff Dean 离开，Google DeepMind 为什么突然大洗牌？｜ S10E28

## Summary
This [[WhatsNextKejiZaozhidao]] episode uses [[DemisHassabis]]' reported move away from day-to-day [[GoogleDeepMind]] operations and [[JeffDean]]'s reported departure to build [[DiscoveryLoop]] as a window into Google's AI organization problem. Guest [[ZhouJiangong]] argues that the reshuffle continues earlier signals from [[DavidSilver]], [[JohnJumper]], and a source-named Transformer author, and that [[Gemini]] pressure, coding-agent commercialization, and the friction between long-horizon research culture and Google-scale product execution explain why the changes were not sudden. The episode adds [[GoogleDeepMindOrganizationalFriction]] while extending [[LanguageModelScalingBet]], [[AIOrganizationDesign]], [[AICommercializationPressure]], [[AIForScience]], and [[CodingAgentAsUniversalActionLayer]].

## Key Claims
- [[DemisHassabis]] is reported as no longer handling daily [[GoogleDeepMind]] operations, becoming DeepMind chair and Alphabet chief scientist; Zhou reads this partly as being "kicked upstairs" while allowing that Hassabis may also prefer a return to research.
- [[JeffDean]] and several long-term Google colleagues are reported to be leaving to found [[DiscoveryLoop]], which Zhou interprets as a smaller-team scientific-discovery bet, likely around healthcare or life sciences but still source-speculative.
- The episode treats earlier exits by [[DavidSilver]], [[JohnJumper]], and a source-named Transformer author as signs that Google DeepMind's post-merger operating model was under strain before the Hassabis and Dean news.
- Coding and agentic AI are framed as an urgent commercial gateway because Anthropic, OpenAI, and Chinese labs made coding assistants a high-value path into broader knowledge work.
- Sergey Brin's return to AI work, the DeepMind-Google Brain merger, and a coding-agent strike team are presented as founder-level and company-level attempts to concentrate execution around [[Gemini]].
- Google's earlier LLM miss is attributed to route judgment, safety and reputation caution, and the search cash-cow problem despite having Transformer, Chinchilla, LaMDA, Sparrow, TPU, data, and product-distribution advantages.
- The episode argues that Google still has major infrastructure, ecosystem, data, and talent advantages, but that [[Gemini]]'s next major generation is strategically important because model quality, coding, and open-weight competition are all tightening.

## Key Quotes
> "往上踢" — Zhou Jiangong's interpretation of Hassabis's reported role change.

> "Coding 是谷歌必须跟进的方向" — Zhou on why coding agents became strategically urgent.

> "不是依赖更多人和更大团队" — source description of the Discovery Loop-style scientific-discovery pattern.
