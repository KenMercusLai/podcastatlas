# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》597丨太皇太后王政君的诏令，为何被拒收？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate prelude to [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-598-gudai-chaotang-de-douzheng-you-duo-canku-luof-1bxomtcsrkcvmuplpugaw4d|Hanji 598]] in the late [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] court. Aidi invokes a supposed [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]] dying instruction and asks [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] to issue an edict giving [[DongXian|董贤]] another two thousand households and supplying fiefs to several already-enfeoffed marquises, but [[WangJiaWesternHan|王嘉]] reseals and returns the edict.

The durable synthesis is that Wang Jia's refusal is a structured protective remonstrance rather than simple disobedience. He argues that titles, salary, and land are Heaven-linked public instruments, not private gifts for a ruler's favorite; he connects excessive Dong Xian favor to empty finances, public resentment, disasters, Aidi's illness, and the lack of an heir, then explains that he returned the edict sealed to resist the policy while containing public backlash.

## Key Claims

- In Yuanshou 1 / 2 BCE after Fu Taihou's death, Han Aidi uses a claimed posthumous instruction to request additional reward for Dong Xian and several marquises.
- Wang Jia returns the dowager-issued edict sealed rather than implementing it or publicly exposing it.
- Wang Jia's memorial frames rank, salary, and land as Heaven-derived public authority that the ruler must distribute with restraint.
- Dong Xian is already wealthy and elevated through Aidi's favor, so additional fief enlargement risks making imperial title and public finance look like a private purse.
- Wang Jia ties public resentment against Dong Xian to disaster-omen language, Aidi's illness, and the unresolved succession problem.
- The episode treats the sealed return as both hard resistance and damage control: Wang Jia refuses the policy while trying to prevent wider public anger at Aidi.
- The closing notes that Aidi becomes more dissatisfied with Wang Jia, setting up Hanji 598's escalation into Tingwei danger.

## Key Quotes

> "爵位、土地、俸禄其实都是属于上天的" - the episode's paraphrase of Wang Jia's public-authority argument.

> "千夫所指，无病而死" - Wang Jia's warning about Dong Xian's public-danger position.
