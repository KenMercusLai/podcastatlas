# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》791丨职场上总有人背后阴你？一招应对

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 120 CE Western Regions debate before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-792-dengkang-renu-taihou-dagou-weishenme-yao-kan-zhuren-lh3xkjneqfd9sqijqo3rgzsa5vwn|Hanji 792]]. After Northern Xiongnu pressure breaks the Han position at Yiwu and threatens Shanshan, [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|和熹邓皇后]] consults [[BanYongLateHan|班勇]] on whether to attack, withdraw, or hold a limited position.

The durable synthesis is twofold. Strategically, Ban Yong rejects both an unaffordable deep expedition and a total Western Regions abandonment, arguing for a low-cost forward post at Shanshan; politically, he answers an impossible guarantee demand by applying the same standard back to ordinary commandery governance. Deng accepts only the deputy-commander and Dunhuang-garrison half, so the episode becomes an early case where a partially adopted frontier strategy loses the forward-positioning mechanism that made it useful.

## Key Claims

- The opening lesson about calm, slow handling of conflict is source-scoped listener advice and prepares Ban Yong's courtroom response.
- In 120 CE, Northern Xiongnu attacks Yiwu, kills the Han long clerk Suo Ban, and pulls Cheshi Qian into pressure against Han-aligned Shanshan.
- Dunhuang administrator Cao Zong asks for five thousand troops to attack Northern Xiongnu and recover the Western Regions, while court officials argue for closing the western passes and cutting off the region.
- Empress Dowager Deng consults Ban Yong because of his Ban-family Western Regions background, local experience, language ability, and reputation for steadiness.
- Ban Yong argues that abandoning the Western Regions would restore a Xiongnu tax base and strategic arm, while immediate major expedition is unrealistic after long Qiang-war spending and treasury exhaustion.
- Ban Yong proposes restoring a Western Regions deputy commandant and sending a long clerk with five hundred troops to Shanshan, using the site to support southern-route states, control northern-route communication, watch Xiongnu, and rely on Dunhuang as rear base.
- The offices are not only military signals: Ban Yong says they should govern Western Regions Hu groups and restrain Han officials from harassing them.
- When Yan Xian, Qiwu Can, and Cui Ju press Ban Yong to guarantee that Xiongnu will never threaten the frontier, Ban Yong answers that he can promise only if inland officials can guarantee their commanderies will never have thieves.
- Mao Zhen's cost objection lets Ban Yong sharpen the accounting: giving the Western Regions to Xiongnu would make future Bingzhou and Liangzhou defense more expensive than a limited forward presence.
- Deng's final decision restores a Western Regions deputy commandant with three hundred soldiers at Dunhuang, but omits Ban Yong's Shanshan forward post and therefore fails to prevent later Northern Xiongnu and Cheshi pressure on Hexi.

## Key Quotes

> "遇事须安详和缓处置" - opening discipline that frames Ban Yong's later response.

> "断其右臂" - Ban Yong's strategic value claim for denying Xiongnu the Western Regions.

> "中原郡县也不能保证永无盗匪" - the analogy that turns an impossible frontier guarantee back onto ordinary officials.
