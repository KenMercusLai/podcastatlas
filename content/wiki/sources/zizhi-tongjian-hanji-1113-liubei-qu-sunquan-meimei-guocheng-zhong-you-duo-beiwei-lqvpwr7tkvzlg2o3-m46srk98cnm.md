# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1113丨刘备娶孙权妹妹，过程中有多卑微？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows the post-[[RedCliffsBattle|赤壁之战]] settlement from [[CaoRen|曹仁]]'s withdrawal from [[JianglingLateHan|江陵]] to [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s small foothold at [[GonganLateHan|公安]]. [[SunQuan|孙权]] needs Liu Bei against [[CaoCao|曹操]] but also fears his expansion, so the episode presents the marriage of [[LadySunLiuBei|孙夫人]] as [[PoliticalMarriageAllianceHedging|政治联姻式同盟对冲]]: a personal tie temporarily reinforces cooperation without removing the underlying territorial conflict.

The second half follows [[JiangGanLateHan|蒋干]]'s failed attempt to persuade [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] away from Sun Quan. Zhou Yu answers by displaying military capacity, material treatment, and ruler-minister trust, making the visit evidence that an embedded political relationship may resist short-term inducement more strongly than a purely transactional tie.

## Key Claims

- Cao Ren eventually withdraws from Jiangling after prolonged resistance; the episode credits the defense with delaying Zhou Yu and buying Cao Cao time to reorganize.
- Liu Bei receives a limited south-bank foothold after Liu Qi's death, uses the Jingzhou governor title, and establishes his base at Gongan while remaining dependent on the Sun-Liu coalition.
- Sun Quan faces two risks at once: Cao Cao remains the larger external threat, while Liu Bei's territorial growth could create a future autonomous rival.
- The episode presents Sun Quan's sister's marriage to Liu Bei as an alliance-preserving choice that makes immediate cooperation more durable but does not settle ownership of Jingzhou.
- Lady Sun is portrayed as intelligent and forceful, surrounded by armed female attendants; Liu Bei's fear inside the household is read as both domestic discomfort and a sign of unequal political dependence.
- The historical text does not preserve Lady Sun's personal name; the episode treats “孙尚香” as a later literary name associated with Yuan drama and keeps “枭姬” as a received epithet rather than a verified personal name.
- Cao Cao sends Jiang Gan, described as Zhou Yu's fellow commandery man, to sound out or persuade Zhou Yu after Red Cliffs.
- Zhou Yu shows Jiang Gan his camp, stores, equipment, and hospitality, then states that trust and treatment under Sun Quan make defection impossible.
- Jiang Gan returns reporting that Zhou Yu's resolve is too high to be changed by speech or material inducement.
- The host's modern “career network” and “startup team” analogies distinguish access from earned capacity and transactional incentives from long-developed political identity.

## Key Quotes

> “每入，衷心常凛凛。” - the episode's rendering of Liu Bei's fear when entering Lady Sun's quarters.

> “外托君臣之义，内结骨肉之恩。” - Zhou Yu's description of his relationship with Sun Quan as rendered by the episode.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is recorded. The source supplies a missing intermediate layer before Hanji 1117-1118's detention, succession, and larger Jingzhou-settlement discussion.
- Chronology and jurisdiction qualification: the assignment of offices, counties, and the Gongan foothold compresses a complicated settlement and should not be read as an uncontested transfer of all Jingzhou.
- Biographical qualification: Lady Sun's age, Liu Bei's age, the armed-attendant count, Fa Zheng's warning, precise household behavior, “孙尚香” name history, “枭姬” usage, and tragic-life interpretation remain source-scoped.
- Causal qualification: the marriage may temporarily reinforce cooperation, but the episode does not prove that it directly caused the later loss of Jingzhou or Guan Yu's death.
- Mission qualification: Jiang Gan's exact mandate, the camp tour, dialogue, material display, and Cao Cao's reaction require comparison with underlying historical texts; the episode's startup-team analogy is interpretive framing.
