# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1091丨诸葛亮点化刘琦，背后暗藏什么战略伏笔？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the missing pre-succession layer before Hanji 1092. [[LiuQiLateHan|刘琦]], exposed inside [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s household and lacking a comparable maternal or military network, traps [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] upstairs to force private advice. Zhuge Liang answers indirectly through the contrast between Shen Sheng, who remains at court and dies, and Chong'er, who survives outside it.

After [[HuangZu|黄祖]] is killed by [[SunQuan|孙权]], Liu Qi seeks the vacant Jiangxia command. The episode treats this move as [[PeripheralCommandExitStrategy|外镇避祸式生存]]: leaving the succession center reduces immediate household danger while giving Liu Qi troops, territory, and strategic relevance. Its further claim that Zhuge Liang already intends Jiangxia as a foothold for [[LiuBei|刘备]] is preserved as host interpretation, although later episodes do show Liu Qi's Jiangxia force and provincial title becoming useful to Liu Bei.

## Key Claims
- The episode presents Liu Qi as the elder son endangered by the pro-[[LiuCongLateHan|刘琮]] household and elite network around [[CaiMao|蔡瑁]] and [[ZhangYunLateHan|张允]].
- Liu Qi removes the ladder after inviting Zhuge Liang upstairs, creating a private setting in which the adviser can no longer avoid answering his survival question.
- Zhuge Liang uses the Shen Sheng-Chong'er contrast rather than direct factional instruction: remaining near a hostile succession center is dangerous, while external distance can preserve life.
- Liu Qi accepts that immediate succession victory is unrealistic and turns toward survival through departure.
- Huang Zu's death creates a vacancy, and Liu Qi persuades Liu Biao to appoint him administrator of [[JiangxiaCommanderyLateHan|江夏郡]].
- Jiangxia converts retreat into capacity because it gives Liu Qi a defensible external post rather than mere exile.
- The host interprets Jiangxia as a later Liu Bei-side strategic hinge that can constrain Liu Cong and support cooperation with Sun Quan.
- The opening management argument favors strong founder control, centralized decisions, selection over development, and leader accountability; those broad claims are not evidenced by the historical case and remain source-scoped.

## Key Quotes
> “申生在内而亡，重耳在外而安。” — the episode's compact historical analogy for Liu Qi's survival choice.

> “顶级猎手往往以猎物的姿态入场。” — the host's interpretation of the hidden-strategy layer.

## Connections
- [[LiuQiLateHan|刘琦]], [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]], and [[PeripheralCommandExitStrategy|外镇避祸式生存]] - indirect counsel turns succession exposure into an external-command strategy.
- [[LiuBiao|刘表]], [[LiuCongLateHan|刘琮]], [[CaiMao|蔡瑁]], and [[ZhangYunLateHan|张允]] - household and elite configuration behind Liu Qi's insecurity.
- [[HuangZu|黄祖]], [[SunQuan|孙权]], and [[JiangxiaCommanderyLateHan|江夏郡]] - death, vacancy, and the command that makes departure possible.
- [[LiuBei|刘备]], [[Jingzhou|荆州]], and [[PostDefeatForceReconstitution|败后兵力重组]] - later episodes show the Jiangxia position contributing troops and political legitimacy after Changban.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is recorded. This source backfills Liu Qi's move to Jiangxia before Hanji 1092's blocked deathbed access, Hanji 1094's Jiangxia reinforcement, and Hanji 1109's use of his provincial title.
- The claim that Zhuge Liang's advice already contains a deliberate Liu Bei plan is the host's retrospective inference, not established intent.
- Liu Cong's maternity, Cai-family marriage ties, Cai Mao's control of the strongest navy, the fifty-five killed local leaders, exact dialogue, and the Shen Sheng-Chong'er analogy's wording require verification against underlying historical texts.
- The opening claims that people cannot be developed and organizations should be highly centralized are broad management opinions, not conclusions established by the historical narrative.
