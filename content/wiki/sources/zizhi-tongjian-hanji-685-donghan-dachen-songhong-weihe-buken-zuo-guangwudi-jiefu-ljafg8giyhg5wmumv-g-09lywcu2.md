# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》685丨东汉大臣宋弘，为何不肯做光武帝姐夫？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills a Jianwu 2 / 26 CE layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-686-liuxiu-fashi-zhi-ai-yinlihua-weihe-li-guoshi-wei-hou-lnqlqgq54srb1zzzgemfwuwujhvc|Hanji 686]] and the later [[PengChongEasternHan|彭宠]] rebellion sequence. It pairs [[SongHongEasternHan|宋弘]]'s court-restraint and marriage-obligation scenes with Peng Chong's first move from disappointed founding ally to armed rebel against [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]].

The durable synthesis is that early Guangwu consolidation is already testing both ethics and coalition management: Song Hong can correct ruler pleasure and refuse a princess marriage without being crushed, while Peng Chong's perceived reward gap, preserved Yuyang resources, conflict with [[ZhuFuEasternHan|朱浮]], and reinforced local suspicion turn an old supporter into a regional threat.

## Key Claims

- In Jianwu 2, Liu Xiu appoints Song Hong as Da Sikong, and Song's recommendation of [[HuanTanEasternHan|桓谭]] creates an early court-feedback scene when Huan's music pleases the emperor too much.
- Song Hong rebukes Huan Tan for winning favor through music, then uses self-blame before Liu Xiu to redirect the issue from entertainment to proper ministerial assistance.
- [[HuyangPrincessEasternHan|湖阳公主]] praises Song Hong's virtue, but Song answers Liu Xiu's marriage test with "贫贱之知不可忘，糟糠之妻不下堂," making existing marital obligation a boundary even under royal interest.
- Peng Chong had materially helped Liu Xiu during the [[WangLangHebeiClaimant|王郎]] campaign but reads later rewards to [[WuHanEasternHan|吴汉]], [[WangLiangEasternHan|王梁]], other high officers, and his own subordinates as proof that his own merit has been undervalued.
- Peng Chong's Yuyang base remains unusually intact after northern disorder, and iron-office resources let him exchange iron for grain and accumulate practical rebel capacity.
- Peng Chong and Zhu Fu clash over Youzhou policy because Zhu Fu recruits local notables and former Wang Mang officials into the state office system while Peng Chong treats expanded offices and grain support as unaffordable during war.
- Liu Xiu's leaked warning, summons, and refusal to call Zhu Fu to Luoyang with Peng Chong deepen Peng Chong's suspicion; his wife and retainers then reinforce refusal rather than submission.
- Peng Chong rebels with more than twenty thousand troops, attacks Ji, and tries to pull [[GengKuangEasternHan|耿况]] into the revolt, but Geng Kuang refuses and kills the envoys.

## Key Quotes

> "贫贱之知不可忘，糟糠之妻不下堂" - Song Hong's answer to Liu Xiu's marriage test.

> "事不谐矣" - Liu Xiu's reply to Princess Huyang after Song Hong's refusal becomes clear.

> "他觉得自己功劳很大" - the episode's framing of Peng Chong's reward grievance.
