# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》812丨古人40岁才能当官？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 132-133 CE [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]] layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-813-gudai-de-gaoguan-jing-ye-hui-bei-ouda-ln-dw3xohnmpavkpu-bdvasvietv|Hanji 813]]. It centers [[ZuoXiongLateHan|左雄]]'s Xiaolian reform: commanderies and kingdoms should recommend candidates aged at least forty, with classical specialists expected to know the classics and administrative clerks expected to draft memorials well, while rare exceptional talents remain exempt.

The second half follows [[LangYiLateHan|郎顗]] being summoned for yin-yang and omen expertise after an imperial tomb-building fire, then using the disaster inquiry to criticize the Three Excellencies' weak responsibility chain and propose repairs around labor, frugality, frontier preparation, delayed lawsuits, and legal simplification. The episode's durable synthesis is that Shun-era reform briefly strengthens scholar-official appointment discipline, but the same strengthened士大夫 field becomes part of the later conflict with eunuch power.

## Key Claims

- [[ZuoXiongLateHan|Zuo Xiong]]'s Xiaolian rule changes recommendation from loose reputation brokerage into an enforceable age, learning, and administrative-skill filter.
- [[HuGuangLateHan|Hu Guang]], Guo Qian, and Shi Chang oppose the rule because exceptional talent, historical precedent, and procedural consultation may be damaged by rigid standards.
- [[EmperorShunOfHan|Emperor Shun]] keeps the rule, and the Xu Shu case shows enforcement: a sub-forty nominee cannot survive Zuo's demand that "exceptional talent" be proved.
- The purge of weak recommendations makes commandery and kingdom officials fear false nomination and produces a cleaner selection environment through the Yongjia years, according to the source.
- [[LangYiLateHan|Lang Yi]] uses disaster and omen language to move from cosmic signs to concrete office accountability: local recommenders are blamed while the Three Excellencies who recommended them are not.
- Lang Yi's seven proposals join relief, frugality, seasonal administration, frontier defense, postponed litigation, and legal simplification into one disaster-remonstrance package.
- The closing Party Prohibition preview links Zuo's selection reform to the rise of the scholar-official group later targeted in conflict with eunuch power.

## Key Quotes

> "大网疏小网密" - Lang Yi's criticism of punishing local recommenders while sparing their higher-level sponsors.

> "奇才可不受年龄限制" - the rule's exception that Xu Shu tries, and fails, to use.

> "删减法令" - Lang Yi's proposal to reduce fine-grained prohibitions and make punishment more avoidable for ordinary people.
