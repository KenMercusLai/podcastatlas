# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》820丨职场警示！小心领导让你背黑锅

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 139-140 CE [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]] layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-821-budong-laoban-xinsi-haixiang-jinsheng-meimen-lmjxr8fdxgw-colrlinmujba74fh|Hanji 821]]. It opens with a workplace warning about not absorbing responsibility for principle or legal risks, then follows [[LiangShangLateHan|梁商]] narrowing the Zhang Kui case, declining an early office for [[LiangBuyiLateHan|梁不疑]], and later recommending a fortified surrender strategy during the [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] revolt.

The historical core contrasts destructive and constructive responsibility. [[ChenGuiDuliaoGeneralLateHan|陈龟]] forces the Southern Xiongnu chanyu and Left Wise King to suicide without court authorization and is later imprisoned and dismissed, while Liang Shang persuades Shun to let [[MaXuLateHan|马续]] hold deep defenses, advertise rewards, and induce surrender instead of chasing small victories. The close sets up Hanji 821 by showing Shun rejecting Liang Shang's warning about [[MaXianLateHan|马贤]] before Ma Xian delays against the Western Qiang.

## Key Claims

- The workplace frame distinguishes small relationship-maintenance responsibility from principle or legal responsibility that should not be absorbed for a superior.
- In the Zhang Kui accusation case, Liang Shang persuades [[EmperorShunOfHan|Shun]] to punish only direct plot participants rather than broaden the case against Cao Teng, Meng Ben, or Liang Shang's circle.
- Liang Shang refuses the Step-Barracks Colonel appointment for [[LiangBuyiLateHan|Liang Buyi]] because his son is too young, and Shun changes the appointment into attendant and carriage-command offices.
- The 140 CE Southern Xiongnu revolt begins with [[WusiSouthernXiongnu|Wusi]] and [[CheNiuChanyu|Che Niu]], survives early defeats by [[MaXuLateHan|Ma Xu]] and [[LiangBingLateHan|Liang Bing]], and then spreads through alliances with Wuhuan, Qiang, and other frontier groups.
- [[ChenGuiDuliaoGeneralLateHan|Chen Gui]] shows the danger of unauthorized responsibility: he coerces an uninvolved chanyu and the Left Wise King into suicide, unsettles potential surrenderers, and is later jailed and removed.
- Liang Shang's counter-strategy combines deep ditches, high walls, grace, trust, reward notices, and patience; the episode presents the surrender of more than 13,000 people under the Right Wise King's camp as evidence that this approach worked.
- Harsh frontier officials Lai Ji and Liu Bing help trigger another Qiang revolt, and Shun's later insistence on using [[MaXianLateHan|Ma Xian]] despite Liang Shang's warning sets up the failure analyzed in Hanji 821.
- [[ZhangDanProtectorOfXiongnu|Zhang Dan]] defeats Che Niu at Mayi and receives Che Niu's surrender, but Wusi continues resistance.
