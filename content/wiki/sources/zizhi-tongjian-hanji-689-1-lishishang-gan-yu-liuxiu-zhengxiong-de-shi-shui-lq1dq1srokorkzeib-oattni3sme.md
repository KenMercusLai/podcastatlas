# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》689-1丨历史上敢与刘秀争雄的是谁？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Jianwu 3 / 27 CE immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-690-jiaoyou-bushen-you-duo-kepa-kankan-tianrong-de-jingli-lpbms8pebzdo2ldiwho1cba81njk|Hanji 690]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-691-gan-duo-jiangshan-de-weixiao-weihe-zhuding-shu-gei-liuxiu-ljazchpqnxcgav6cmlbm7-d-gv8|Hanji 691]]. It links [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]]'s multi-front cleanup through Guanzhong, Nanyang, Lujiang, Suiyang, and northern planning with the first western relationship problem around [[WeiXiaoEasternHan|隗嚣]]: [[LaiXiEasternHan|来歙]] proposes courting Wei Xiao to isolate [[GongsunShuEasternHan|公孙述]], but the host frames Wei Xiao's suspicion and Liu Xiu's high courtesy as an unstable foundation rather than settled submission.

## Key Claims
- Jianwu 3 is still a multi-front consolidation year: Liu Xiu handles Deng Feng, Yan Cen, Qin Feng, Liu Yong, Li Xian, Peng Chong, Zhang Feng, and Zhang Bu-related pressure rather than ruling a settled empire.
- [[FengYiEasternHan|冯异]]'s Guanzhong work combines combat with food and surrender management, making scarcity and local camp realignment part of military consolidation.
- [[WuHanEasternHan|吴汉]]'s Guangle episode shows morale control under visible injury: after falling from his horse and hurting his knee, he steadies the army through public composure and reward before counterattacking.
- [[CenPengEasternHan|岑彭]], [[YanCenEasternHan|延岑]], [[GaiYanEasternHan|盖延]], [[SuMaoEasternHan|苏茂]], and [[ZhouJianEasternHan|周建]] mark the southern/eastern cleanup that runs beside the western strategy.
- [[GengYanEasternHan|耿弇]] appears as a young commander whose request to recruit Shanggu troops and attack Peng Chong, Zhang Feng, Fu Ping, and Zhang Bu shows Liu Xiu's willingness to back ambitious field plans.
- [[LaiXiEasternHan|来歙]] turns the western problem into diplomatic sequencing: if [[WeiXiaoEasternHan|隗嚣]] can be brought toward Luoyang, [[GongsunShuEasternHan|公孙述]] loses a protective outer layer.
- Liu Xiu's high ceremonial treatment of Wei Xiao opens contact but does not resolve the underlying allegiance problem, because Wei Xiao remains suspicious and not yet firmly subordinate.

## Key Quotes
> "任何关系若想留住，就不要把它看得太重" - the host's opening frame for the Liu Xiu-Wei Xiao relationship.

> "疑心很重" - the source's warning about Wei Xiao after surface-level contact with Luoyang.
