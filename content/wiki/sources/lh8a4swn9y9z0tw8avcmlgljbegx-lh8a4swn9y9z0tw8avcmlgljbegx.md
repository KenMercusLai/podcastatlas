## Summary
This [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] interview follows [[ChenMian|陈冕]] from repeated moves and product roles at major Chinese internet companies into founding [[Liblib]] and launching [[Lovart]]. It connects his preference for decision-making authority to an AI-application thesis built around multimodal design, [[AIWorkflowUpstreamPositioning]], rapid iteration, and [[HumanTasteCreativeBoundary|human taste as a changing creative boundary]]. The account is useful as founder testimony, but career details, financial figures, replacement forecasts, and AGI timelines remain source-scoped.

## Key Claims
- [[ChenMian|陈冕]] describes frequent job changes across Tencent, 360, Baidu, Didi, Mobike, MissFresh, and ByteDance as a search for work he could materially direct rather than as a plan for long-term corporate advancement.
- He treats his MissFresh growth work and ByteDance education role as important evidence that he could lead a product or business and see intended outcomes, while also learning that supply-chain-heavy businesses constrained his leverage as a product operator.
- Stable Diffusion, Midjourney, and ChatGPT led him to judge AI as at least a computer-scale opportunity; he resigned in May 2023 because he believed timing mattered more than completing fundraising or team formation first.
- Early financing choices left the company with too many first-round investors and a severe cash shortage even though Chen believed product demand was healthy, separating operating traction from financing discipline.
- [[Liblib]] targeted professional creators and model/material sharing, while [[Lovart]] was positioned as an AI designer for professional design needs that non-designers, clients, and marketing teams could directly use.
- Lovart's infinite canvas, contextual understanding, and directed editing aim to reproduce parts of communication with a human designer; the source's [[AINativeProductDesign|AI-native]] test is whether the experience would exist without AI, not whether every legacy interface element disappears.
- [[AIWorkflowUpstreamPositioning]] argues that startups can originate a workflow or serve new users and demands instead of remaining a replaceable downstream feature inside an incumbent's workflow.
- Chen frames application defensibility as a moving combination of insight, speed, conviction, product execution, compute/API economics, and eventual accumulated barriers rather than an idea that cannot be copied.
- The source says Lovart had just reached positive cash flow at the product revenue-versus-compute-cost level, while company-wide profitability remained distant and continued investment was expected.
- [[HumanTasteCreativeBoundary]] qualifies automation: Chen predicts large displacement of standardized design work while reserving a temporary advantage for frontier taste, new styles, and creative judgment; he also says style protection and long-run AGI risk lack settled answers.

## Key Quotes
> "timing is everything" — Chen's reason for leaving ByteDance before financing and staffing were complete.

> "speed is all you need" — his summary of AI-era operating tempo.

> "大闹一场，然后悄然离去" — the life ideal he associates with making the world more varied before withdrawing.
