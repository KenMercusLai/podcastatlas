# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》348-1｜古人犯错后的焚尸启示录

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode compares three Western Han cruel officials through the question of whether force leaves a path back. [[ZhangTangWesternHan|张汤]] is presented as translating [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s preferences into learned-looking legal judgment; [[ZhaoYuWesternHan|赵禹]] reportedly softens in old age and eventually dies at home; [[YinQiWesternHan|尹齐]] remains clean but so severe that officials stop acting and enemies later try to burn his corpse.

The durable distinction is between integrity, coercive effectiveness, and sustainable rule. Clean finances do not establish proportionate enforcement, and fear may produce visible control while damaging administrative initiative and storing retaliatory hatred. The host uses a *Caigentan*-style warning about standing above immediate entanglement and leaving room in conduct, but the episode is a compact moral-historical commentary rather than a complete legal or biographical study.

## Key Claims

- Zhang Tang recruits classically trained assistants, uses canonical language in difficult cases, attributes discernment to Wudi, and preserves imperial decisions as future adjudicative examples.
- The episode says Zhang makes legal flexibility track the emperor's intention, at times stretching or disregarding stable statutory limits.
- Zhao Yu is described as more severe than other Nine Ministers while serving as shaofu, but as becoming gentler and more accommodating toward colleagues in old age.
- After demotion to chancellor of Yan and later dismissal over a minor matter, Zhao reportedly returns home and dies naturally, giving the episode its positive exit case.
- Yin Qi is portrayed as clean and willing to pursue major cases, yet his severity and killings make officials afraid to act and contribute to his removal.
- After Yin dies while serving as Huainan commandant, enemies from Suiyang allegedly seek to burn his corpse, forcing his family to move it secretly for burial.
- The host's conclusion is not that enforcement itself is wrong, but that action pressed to an extreme can destroy initiative, withdrawal options, and post-office safety.

## Key Quotes

> “立身不高一步立，如尘里振衣，泥中濯足。” - the opening conduct standard used to frame distance from immediate entanglement.

> “处世不退一步处，如飞蛾投烛，羝羊触藩。” - the warning that leaving no room can trap the actor in the consequences of his own method.
