# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》101-1｜这位倒霉君王登基三天即猝死

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-100-2-liuguo-jingran-tongshi-wei-ta-diaosang-ln6-5v-b-ybqmf5ucepusdo3lf68|Qinji 100-2]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-100-1-huangdi-zhong-de-chaochang-daiji-wang-guoshi-le-lt75scavlm8qjn6ulpd-b2kwzwjf|Qinji 100-1]] by moving from [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]]'s death into [[QinXiaowenwang|秦孝文王]]'s formal accession and three-day reign. It records competing explanations for his sudden death, including suspicion of [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]] and illness or exhaustion, but keeps the matter as [[QinXiaowenwangDeathUncertainty|秦孝文王三日而亡疑案]] rather than a settled murder claim.

The episode then returns to [[YanState|燕国]]'s failed eastern-state strategy. After Yan troops take [[LiaochengWarringStates|辽城]], [[YanWangXi|燕王喜]]'s suspicion traps the occupying commander between return, surrender, and death; [[TianDan|田单]] cannot force the city, until [[LuZhonglian|鲁仲连]] shoots a letter into the city and turns the commander's situation into a [[LiaochengCommanderDilemma|辽城守将进退困局]]. The closing discussion uses [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]] and [[ZiShun|子顺]]'s judgment of Lu Zhonglian to frame [[VirtuePracticeInternalization|修养内化]]: repeated outward practice can become stable character, and [[Confucius|孔子]]'s "性相近，习相远" is treated as a more careful human-nature claim than simplified slogans of innate goodness or badness.

## Key Claims
- [[QinXiaowenwang|秦孝文王]] formally accedes in winter tenth month of 250 BCE and dies three days later, making him Qin's shortest-reigning king in this episode's telling.
- [[QinZhuangxiangwang|子楚 / 秦庄襄王]] succeeds him and honors adoptive mother [[HuayangFuren|华阳夫人]] as 华阳太后 and biological mother [[XiaTaihou|夏姬 / 夏太后]] as 夏太后.
- The episode presents [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]] murder suspicion as one possible later explanation, but balances it against physical weakness, long crown-prince pressure, and mourning exhaustion.
- The host explicitly treats the death cause as unrecoverable, so the wiki records it as [[QinXiaowenwangDeathUncertainty|historical uncertainty]].
- [[YanWangXi|燕王喜]]'s suspicion of the commander who captured [[LiaochengWarringStates|辽城]] creates a political trap: the commander cannot advance, return safely, or trust surrender.
- [[TianDan|田单]]'s long siege shows that even a trapped enemy can remain hard to dislodge when the attacker cannot solve the defender's political incentives.
- [[LuZhonglian|鲁仲连]]'s arrow letter reframes the commander's passivity as not loyal, not brave, and not wise, forcing him to compare return to Yan with surrender to Qi.
- The commander's suicide lets Tian Dan recover Liaocheng, making the episode a case where persuasion changes a military stalemate.
- Lu Zhonglian's refusal of reward extends the wiki's earlier [[MoralizedShiIdentity|士的道德化身份]] branch, but the episode also questions whether high-minded conduct can begin as self-conscious performance.
- [[ZiShun|子顺]]'s answer to [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]] argues that if someone keeps "performing" gentlemanly conduct, the practice can eventually become genuine character.
- The episode distinguishes [[Confucius|孔子]]'s "性相近，习相远" from later simplified slogans, stressing formation and habit rather than a blunt verdict that human nature is good or evil.
- It also notes that [[Xunzi|荀子]] did not literally say "人之初，性本恶," keeping later popular formulas separate from source-text precision.

## Key Quotes
> "三天后去世" - the episode's stark description of Qin Xiaowen Wang's formal reign.

> "事实真相如何已经很难查证" - the source's caution on the king's death.

> "鲁仲连射书克辽城" - the compact label for Lu Zhonglian's intervention.

> "性相近，习相远" - the Confucian line used for human plasticity.
