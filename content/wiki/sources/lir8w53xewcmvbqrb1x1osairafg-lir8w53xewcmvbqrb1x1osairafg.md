## Summary
This [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] and [[SixiaoComedy|四孝喜剧]] episode has [[LuoYonghao|罗永浩]], [[CaoGuoStandup|曹国]], [[ShiLifen|史礼芬]], and [[LinJianxinStandup|林简欣]] discuss flaws through baldness, [[ADHDSelfDiagnosisBoundary|ADHD]], compulsive order, lateness, cleanliness, public reviews, online backlash, and live-show preparation. The episode is not clinical education; it uses comedy-panel teasing to turn visible and behavioral imperfections into [[LiveHumanFeeling|活人感]], creative material, and relational pressure. - The closing returns to [[LiveHumanFeeling|活人感]]: a perfect public persona is forgettable, while flaws and sincerity can reduce distance when they do not become an excuse to harm others.
