## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills [[WangMang|王莽]]'s Tianfeng 3 / 16 CE crisis between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-650-gudai-laozuzong-ye-hui-bei-kou-jixiao-gongzi-lr77fw1gojgqrhah1ij6u25vtxaz|Hanji 650]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-652-wangmang-de-waike-shoushu-ltdiqldskazy-cb6igdmrv5huxm9|Hanji 652]]. It opens with [[HetuLuoshu|河图洛书]] as a mythic and cosmological sign system, then shows Xin officials turning a riverbank collapse and watercourse change into a favorable "earth controls water" omen for action against [[Xiongnu|匈奴]].

The durable synthesis is that late-Xin omen politics could convert natural disorder into military confidence while material frontier campaigns were already failing. Wang Mang enjoys the favorable reading and sends [[SongHongEasternHan|宋弘]] and Ren Meng to the frontier; in the southwest, Feng Mao's failed Quting campaign, Lian Dan and Shi Xiong's enlarged force, disease, grain exhaustion, and forced extraction from Yizhou and Liangzhou households make the episode a case of [[AuspiciousOmenPolitics|祥瑞政治]], [[OverextendedFrontierExpedition|边境远征过度]], and war burden transferred to civilians.

## Key Claims

- [[HetuLuoshu|河图洛书]] functions in the episode as cultural-symbol background: a contested but influential sign complex attached to Fuxi, Bagua, yin-yang, and later classical cosmology.
- In 16 CE, a Changpingguan riverbank collapse and watercourse change is interpreted by Xin officials as "earth suppressing water" and therefore as an omen that Xiongnu will be defeated.
- The favorable interpretation serves court flattery and policy activation: Wang Mang accepts congratulations and dispatches Song Hong and Ren Meng to border positions for anti-Xiongnu preparations.
- Fire at Bacheng Gate, a solar eclipse, and amnesty keep the episode inside a wider disaster-and-response political field rather than a neutral natural-history notice.
- Feng Mao's Quting campaign fails after severe epidemic losses and heavy seizure of civilian property; Wang Mang punishes Feng Mao, who dies in prison.
- Lian Dan and Shi Xiong repeat the southwest offensive with a much larger multi-commandery force, but supply exhaustion, hunger, and epidemic deaths turn the campaign into overextension.
- Feng Ying's remonstrance offers a lower-cost alternative: stop the offensive, hold positions, farm locally, and win civilian cooperation through benefits rather than extraction.
- The closing anatomy discussion uses [[HuangdiNeijing|《黄帝内经》]] and Western medical comparison only as setup for Hanji 652's Wang Sunqing dissection case.
