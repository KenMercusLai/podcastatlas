# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》394-1｜古人如何教孩子？看看隽不疑的成长故事

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 86 BCE / Shiyuan 1 layer immediately before the later [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-396-2-hanwudi-zhizi-liuju-wugu-zhi-an-hou-fuhuo-le-lhj6dzjsikkmbbcjlfgnz1bbok-1|Hanji 396-2]] false-claimant crisis. It presents [[JunBuyiWesternHan|隽不疑]] before that palace-gate decision as a Jingzhao Yin whose mother evaluates his work by asking whether he has redressed wrongful cases and saved lives.

The episode's durable synthesis is that family education becomes public ethics when a caregiver's repeated value signal shapes official habit. Jun Buyi's mother does not only praise career advancement; she makes justice, evidence, leniency, and life preservation the emotional standard of success. The second half applies the same family-and-character lens to [[JinMidiWesternHan|金日磾]], whose Xiongnu background, refusal and later acceptance of marquisate, military funeral honors, and careful family continuity are read through low-profile political survival and strict household discipline.

## Key Claims

- Jun Buyi's mother uses daily questions about redressing injustice and saving people to shape his judicial standards.
- The episode treats magistrates and officials with life-and-death authority as especially dependent on benevolence, evidence, and caution because wrongful execution is irreversible.
- Jun Buyi's later reputation as a fair, beloved capital official is presented as an outgrowth of repeated maternal value-setting rather than only innate talent.
- Jin Midi's refusal of Han Wudi's posthumous marquisate order is read as low-profile caution under a young emperor and as sensitivity to his Xiongnu origin.
- Jin Midi's later acceptance of the title on his sickbed is interpreted as a family-continuity choice because the marquisate could pass to his sons.
- Huo Guang's refusal to let both Jin sons receive marquis-level treatment shows a merit-and-institution boundary even in front of Han Zhao's personal favor.
- The episode closes by linking Jin-family continuity to strict maternal discipline, making family education the shared frame across both Jun Buyi and Jin Midi.
