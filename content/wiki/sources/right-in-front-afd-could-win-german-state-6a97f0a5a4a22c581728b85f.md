## Summary
This [[TheIntelligence]] episode covers three practical tests of power and market fit: [[AlternativeForGermany|AfD]]'s chance of winning outright control in [[SaxonyAnhalt|Saxony-Anhalt]], [[Palantir]]'s disputed usefulness in British public services, and the attempt to make premium chocolate culturally legible in [[China]] through [[ChocRevive|Choc Revive]] and peers. The first segment turns AfD from a pressure party into a possible executive actor constrained by [[BrandmauerCordonSanitaire|Brandmauer]] politics, state powers, debt, transfers, and administrative inexperience. The second segment treats Palantir through [[PublicServiceDataPlatformTradeoff]] and [[PublicSectorVendorDependence]]: useful data integration can coexist with lobbying, lock-in, sensitive data, sovereignty, and evidence-quality concerns. The final segment uses Chinese chocolate to show [[ChinesePremiumChocolateLocalization]] as a food-category-building problem, not only a luxury-import story.

## Key Claims
- [[UlrichSiegmund]] is described as the AfD lead candidate in [[SaxonyAnhalt|Saxony-Anhalt]], where the party could potentially win a majority and form a state government without coalition partners.
- A Saxony-Anhalt AfD victory would challenge the [[BrandmauerCordonSanitaire|Brandmauer]] because mainstream parties' refusal to cooperate matters less if AfD wins enough seats alone.
- The episode presents AfD as anti-immigration, opposed to asylum rights, supportive of [[RemigrationPolitics|remigration]], pro-Russian, and hostile to continued German support for [[Ukraine]].
- AfD's state manifesto includes education and memory-politics proposals, but implementation would face state-power limits, about EUR25bn of debt, transfer dependence, and little governing experience.
- German institutions and civil-society actors are already planning containment measures, including parliamentary rule changes and possible exclusion of an AfD interior minister from sensitive security discussions.
- [[Palantir]] is presented as useful for joining fragmented public-service data in [[UnitedKingdom|Britain]], including [[NHS]] workflow tools, armed-forces targeting support, and police evidence review.
- The Palantir segment keeps the practical upside in tension with concerns about health data, lobbying, vendor lock-in, American technology dependence, exaggerated statistics, ICE migration enforcement, and Israel's targeting of Gazans.
- [[GeorgiaBanjo]] argues Britain may lack equivalent domestic alternatives, while examples such as the [[MetropolitanPolice|Metropolitan Police]] blocked deal show procurement legitimacy and open competition still matter.
- [[ChocRevive|Choc Revive]] is trying to make chocolate more Chinese through local flavors, domestic processing and R&D, and rapid store expansion despite low per-capita chocolate consumption and weak consumer confidence.
- [[Nibbo]] and [[SaturnBird|Saturn Bird]] are presented as other signs that China could develop serious domestic chocolate capability, though the episode keeps that outcome uncertain.
