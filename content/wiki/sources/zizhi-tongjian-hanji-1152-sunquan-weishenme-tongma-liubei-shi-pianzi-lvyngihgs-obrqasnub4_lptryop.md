# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1152丨孙权为什么痛骂刘备是骗子？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows the 215 CE rupture in the Sun–Liu settlement after [[LiuBei|刘备]] takes Yizhou. [[SunQuan|孙权]] treats Liu Bei's earlier obstruction of a joint or Wu-led Yizhou campaign and his continued delay over Jingzhou as incompatible with the prior alliance bargain, then sends [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]] against Changsha, Guiyang, and [[LinglingCommanderyLateHan|零陵郡]].

At Lingling, Lyu Meng hides a recall order long enough to create one more operational window and uses Hao Pu's friend to falsely portray Liu Bei and [[GuanYu|关羽]] as unable to relieve the city. [[HaoPuLateHan|郝普]] surrenders, then learns that relief was much closer than claimed. Lyu Meng immediately joins [[LuSu|鲁肃]], whose armed meeting with Guan Yu shows the other side of the same crisis: open bargaining can defend Jiangdong's territorial claim while preserving cooperation against [[CaoCao|曹操]].

## Key Claims

- Sun Quan interprets Liu Bei's earlier refusal to let Sun Yu pass toward Yizhou, followed by Liu Bei's own conquest of the region, as evidence that moral and kinship language had concealed a strategic exclusion.
- After Liu Bei delays returning Jingzhou territory until a future Liangzhou conquest, Sun Quan appoints officials to Changsha, Guiyang, and Lingling; Guan Yu expels them, and Sun Quan answers with force.
- Changsha and Guiyang submit quickly, while Hao Pu holds Lingling as Liu Bei reaches Gong'an and Guan Yu moves toward Yiyang.
- Lyu Meng conceals Sun Quan's recall order, presses the Lingling operation, and uses Deng Xuanzhi to tell Hao Pu that no relief is possible.
- Hao Pu's surrender turns on a false estimate of rescue prospects rather than the city's immediate military collapse, making the case an example of [[FalseReliefDenialSurrender|虚假无援诱降]].
- Lyu Meng's initiative gains Lingling and still raises a command-boundary problem: local information and speed may preserve strategic value, but success does not erase the risk of withholding and disobeying a ruler's order.
- Lu Su seeks a negotiated settlement because Cao Cao remains a stronger common threat, yet his alliance policy does not require Jiangdong to abandon its own territorial interests.
- In the episode's rendering of the armed meeting, Guan Yu claims Liu Bei's campaign contribution deserves territory, while Lu Su answers that Jiangdong supplied the endangered Liu side with a foothold and cannot accept indefinite non-return.

## Key Quotes

> "战争是外交的失败，外交是不流血的战争。" — the host's summary of force and negotiation as alternative instruments.

> "子明病了，须针灸才能治愈。" — the episode's reading of Sun Quan's reported remark as a warning about Lyu Meng's excessive autonomy.
