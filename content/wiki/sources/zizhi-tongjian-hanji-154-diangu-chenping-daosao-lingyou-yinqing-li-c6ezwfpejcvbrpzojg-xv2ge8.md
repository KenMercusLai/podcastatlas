# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》154｜典故“陈平盗嫂”另有隐情？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the unresolved [[ChenPing|陈平]] accusation thread from [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-153-xiayi-huace-canbai-hou-liubang-ruhe-qisi-huisheng-2-ll-rvx07pyw-qg1jofkjro-qfllu|Hanji 153 part 2]]. [[ZhouBo|周勃]], [[GuanYing|灌婴]], and other old generals accuse Chen Ping of "盗嫂" and taking money, but [[WeiWuzhi|魏无知]] and Chen Ping redirect [[LiuBang|刘邦]] from moral scandal toward wartime usefulness. The host treats the received-money charge as explainable and the "盗嫂" charge as likely [[MoralSlanderReframing|political slander]], using [[Shiji|《史记》]] family details and the accusers' weak access to Chen Ping's private history.

## Key Claims
- The episode resolves Hanji 153 part 2's cliffhanger: Liu Bang first blames Wei Wuzhi for recommending Chen Ping, then personally questions Chen Ping about patron switching and taking money.
- Wei Wuzhi defends the recommendation by separating useful talent from personal virtue: Liu Bang needs strategy for the Chu-Han war, not merely high-character figures whose conduct does not change the battlefield.
- The examples of Weisheng and Xiaoji show the source's talent-versus-virtue contrast: admirable moral reputation may still be useless for the immediate political-military problem.
- Chen Ping does not deny every charge. He says he left [[WeiJiu|魏咎]] because advice was unused, left [[XiangYu|项羽]] because outsiders were distrusted, and accepted gifts because a poor newcomer needed daily operating resources.
- Liu Bang accepts the practical answer, apologizes, rewards Chen Ping, and appoints him to supervise generals, making the episode a confirmation of [[VeteranMeritCliqueSupervision|功臣旧将监督]] and [[OutsiderTalentSignaling|厚待外来人才信号]] under pressure.
- The host reads the "盗嫂" charge skeptically because the [[Shiji|《史记》]] family story presents Chen Ping's [[ChenPingSisterInLaw|sister-in-law]] as hostile to him, while [[ChenPingBrother|his elder brother]] supports Chen Ping and expels his wife after her complaint.
- The accusers' information access is weak in the episode's reasoning: Zhou Bo is a Pei County old follower and Guan Ying is from Suiyang, while Chen Ping is from Yangwu/Huyou, so they are unlikely to know a hidden family scandal firsthand.
- The source's final practical lesson is [[MoralSlanderReframing|道德诬陷焦点转移]]: in an unprovable smear, Chen Ping and Wei Wuzhi avoid endless self-proof and return the decision-maker to the real criterion.

## Key Quotes
> "才能是否对刘邦夺天下有用" - the episode's summary of Wei Wuzhi's defense frame.

> "如果不接受馈赠，就无法应付日常开销" - Chen Ping's practical explanation for taking money.

> "盗嫂更像是缺乏证据的政治诬陷" - the source's conclusion about the famous stain.
