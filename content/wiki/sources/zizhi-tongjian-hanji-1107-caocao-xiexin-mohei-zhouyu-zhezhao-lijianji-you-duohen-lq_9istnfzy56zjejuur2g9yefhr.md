# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1107丨曹操写信抹黑周瑜，这招离间计有多狠？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode closes the immediate [[RedCliffsBattle|赤壁之战]] sequence. [[HuangGai|黄盖]]'s feigned surrender lets fire ships reach the Cao fleet, after which [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] presses the attack and [[CaoCao|曹操]] retreats, burns remaining ships and supplies, and leaves [[CaoRen|曹仁]] at [[JianglingLateHan|江陵]] with [[XuHuangCaoGeneral|徐晃]] at 樊城 to slow the victors.

The episode's distinctive contribution is the post-battle information struggle. Cao Cao tells [[SunQuan|孙权]] that disease forced the withdrawal and that he burned the ships himself, minimizing Zhou Yu's role; the host interprets this as [[PostDefeatNarrativeCounterattack|败后叙事反击]] aimed at reputational damage and at sharpening the already real interest tension between Sun Quan, Zhou Yu, and [[LiuBei|刘备]] over [[Jingzhou|荆州]].

## Key Claims
- Huang Gai approaches with ten ships under the cover of surrender, ignites them near Cao Cao's fleet, and the wind carries the fire into ships and the Wulin-side camp.
- Zhou Yu follows the fire attack with selected troops, while Cao-side deaths come from both burning and drowning.
- Cao Cao retreats through the Huarong route and burns ships and supplies that might otherwise become resources for the victors.
- The episode treats Red Cliffs as a major interruption to Cao Cao's rapid unification plan and as a condition for the later tripartite balance.
- Cao Cao leaves Cao Ren at Jiangling and Xu Huang at Fancheng, converting retreat into a layered defensive line against further Sun-Liu expansion.
- Cao Cao's message to Sun Quan attributes the withdrawal to disease and self-burning, reducing Zhou Yu's victory to an undeserved reputation.
- The host reads Cao Cao's appointment language around Zhou Yu and Nanjun as an attempt to channel Wu toward a costly Jiangling campaign.
- The same message can pressure the Sun-Liu alliance because post-victory control of Jingzhou gives Sun Quan, Zhou Yu, and Liu Bei different interests.
- The closing Guo Jia lament and detailed account of Cao Cao's inner grief are literary reconstruction rather than securely established observation.

## Key Quotes
> "军中有瘟疫，主动烧船撤退。" - the episode's paraphrase of Cao Cao's alternative account of the defeat.

> "虚获此名。" - the phrase used to minimize Zhou Yu's battlefield credit.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction found. The episode supplies the retreat and narrative prelude to Hanji 1108-1109's post-Red-Cliffs territorial contest.
- Causal qualification: disease, fire attack, withdrawal, and self-destruction of abandoned materiel need not be mutually exclusive; the source's sharper claim concerns how Cao Cao selects those facts to redistribute credit.
- The “cognitive warfare” label is a modern analogy, not proof of institutional equivalence with modern information operations.
- Cao Cao's certainty illusion, intention to consume Zhou Yu at Jiangling, Sun Quan's inferred suspicion, Guo Jia counterfactual, exact private grief, and the host's imagined tree-side reconstruction remain source-scoped.
