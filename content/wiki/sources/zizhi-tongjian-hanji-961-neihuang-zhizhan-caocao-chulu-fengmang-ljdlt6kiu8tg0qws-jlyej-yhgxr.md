# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》961丨内黄之战：曹操初露锋芒

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the pre-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-962-dongzhuo-yu-lvbu-daodi-weishenme-juelie-lgp3kxfqnhokibf6kpli6p4j8iti|Hanji 962]] late-Han field by showing [[CaoCao|曹操]] before the better-known [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] and imperial-court branches. It first introduces [[ChengYu|程昱]] through his advice to [[LiuDaiLateHan|刘岱]] during the [[YuanShao|袁绍]]-[[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] split, then centers on [[NeihuangBattleLateHan|内黄之战]], where Cao Cao relieves [[DongWuyangLateHan|东武阳]] indirectly by threatening [[YuDuLateHan|于毒]]'s base. The episode's durable contribution is [[IntegratedCommandCapacity|全维度统帅能力]]: Cao Cao's early promise is read as a combination of strategic pressure, tactical adaptation, talent use, and resource integration rather than battlefield aggression alone.

## Key Claims

- The host frames Cao Cao's appeal through resilience: the important trait is not only ambition or cleverness, but his ability to stay active after defeat and disorder.
- Liu Dai's Yanzhou position is caught between Yuan Shao and Gongsun Zan after the anti-Dong-Zhuo alliance field breaks into rivalry.
- Gongsun Zan pressures Liu Dai to hand over Yuan Shao's household and sends [[FanFangLateHan|范方]] as an enforcement channel, showing how alliance help can become coercive leverage.
- Cheng Yu advises Liu Dai not to depend on distant Gongsun Zan and predicts that Yuan Shao is unlikely to harm Liu Dai if Liu Dai stays neutral.
- Liu Dai later wants to appoint Cheng Yu, but Cheng Yu refuses on illness grounds; after Liu Dai dies, [[XunYu|荀彧]] recommends him to Cao Cao.
- The source links Cheng Yu's entry into Cao Cao's camp with his later role in Yanzhou crisis management and preserves the name-change story from Cheng Li to Cheng Yu.
- In 192 CE, Cao Cao is stationed at [[DunqiuLateHan|顿丘]] when Yu Du attacks Dong Wuyang.
- Cao Cao rejects direct relief and marches west into the mountains toward Yu Du's base, reasoning that Yu Du must either return and lift the siege or lose his camp.
- Yu Du does withdraw, and Cao Cao then advances to Neihuang, defeating [[SuiGuLateHan|眭固]] and [[YufuluoChanyu|于扶罗]], the [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] chanyu.
- The battle helps Yuan Shao by easing pressure on his rear and helps Cao Cao consolidate his Dong Commandery position before the later Yanzhou rise.
- The episode treats the maneuver as a late-Han version of [[EncirclingWeiToRescueZhao|围魏救赵]]: the useful target is the point the enemy cannot ignore, not necessarily the besieged place itself.
- The closing interpretation reads Cao Cao as a broad commander: strategic view, tactical variation, adviser use, military theory, and resource conversion work together.
- The host contrasts this source-grounded Cao Cao with the villainized literary image from [[RomanceOfTheThreeKingdoms|《三国演义》]].

## Key Quotes

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is recorded. This source backfills Cao Cao's earlier East Commandery activity and Cheng Yu's pre-Cao judgment role rather than reversing Hanji 964's later Yanzhou succession account.
- Source-scope caution: the broad "full-dimensional commander" praise, Romance contrast, and modern leadership analogies are treated as the host's interpretive frame, not as independent biographical proof.
