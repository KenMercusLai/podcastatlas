# EP277 对话贾樟柯（下）：我没有背叛真实世界，我只是在寻找电影的新可能

## Summary
This [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] episode has [[JiaZhangke|贾樟柯]] explain why experimenting with AI film does not mean abandoning the real world. Starting from [[PingyaoInternationalFilmFestival|平遥国际电影展]]'s AI posters, AI shorts, and "摩登时代" theme, he frames AI as a possible change in film production method rather than only another technical carrier. The central synthesis is [[FilmRealityBeyondRealism]]: cinema can respond to reality through many forms, while AI also raises unsettled questions around [[AIVideoProductionWorkflow]], [[LiveActionFilmUnderAI]], [[CreativeLaborAIBacklash]], likeness rights, copyright, and energy use.

## Key Claims
- Jia rejects the accusation that AI experimentation betrays the real world: realism is one way to respond to the world, but animation, effects, historical stories, and generated images can also carry real-world concerns.
- He distinguishes AI from the shift from film stock to digital. Digital changed the carrier while keeping directors, writers, actors, cameras, sound recorders, art departments, and crews largely legible; AI could alter the production method itself.
- Current AI film remains in a long testing period. The industry has not yet settled who writes, directs, prompts, chooses, performs, owns, or authorizes generated characters.
- Jia sees "抽卡" and instruction-giving roles as transitional signs of AI production, not as proof that a mature AI-film language already exists.
- The episode's AI-aesthetics claim is future-oriented: AI cinema may eventually stop imitating live-action reality and develop its own narrative world, closer to how animation has a distinct relation to live action.
- Industry anxiety is treated as legitimate. Guilds and associations are protecting existing workers, and the episode names copyright, portrait rights, environmental cost, and lawmaking as unresolved boundaries.
- Jia says his own main interest remains live-action work: camera, location, life scene, and captured feeling. AI is a new possibility, not his primary creative home.

## Key Quotes
> "用什么工具拍不重要，拍什么才重要" - the episode's tool-versus-subject boundary.

> "电影需要被重新发明" - Jia's technology-history frame for AI cinema.

> "我没有天赋" - Jia's self-limiting comment on whether AI film will become his main field.
