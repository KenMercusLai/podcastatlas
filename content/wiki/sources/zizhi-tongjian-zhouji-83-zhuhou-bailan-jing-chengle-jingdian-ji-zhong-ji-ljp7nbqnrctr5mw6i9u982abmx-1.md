# 《资治通鉴·周纪》83丨诸侯摆烂竟成了经典计中计？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s forty-first and forty-second years as [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]] tries to escape [[QinState|秦国]] subordination but lacks the strength to make either [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] or opportunistic war work. After [[WeiState|魏国]] and [[ZhaoState|赵国]] attack [[HanState|韩国]] at [[HuayangBattle|华阳之战]], Han uses [[ThreatenedDefectionLeverage|投敌威胁式求援]] to make Qin rescue it, and Qin under [[WeiRan|魏冉]] and [[BaiQi|白起]] crushes the attackers. The closing [[SuDai|苏代]] story turns [[LandForPeaceExhaustionTrap|抱薪救火式割地求和]] into the episode's political lesson: ceding land to buy peace can preserve the current process while steadily destroying the state.

## Key Claims
- In Zhou Nan Wang's forty-first year, Wei Anxi Wang allies with [[QiState|齐国]] in an anti-Qin posture, but both Wei and restored Qi are too weakened to make the alignment a reliable check on Qin.
- Qin reacts through Wei Ran, who attacks Wei again, takes four cities, and inflicts large casualties, deepening Wei's post-accession losses.
- In the next year, Wei joins Zhao to attack Han's Huayang, even though Han had recently helped Wei resist Qin; the episode uses this as a clear case of [[WarringStatesAllianceSwitching|战国同盟转向]].
- Huayang is presented as the northern gate of Han's capital region around Xinzheng, making the campaign a direct threat to Han's survival rather than a minor border raid.
- [[MangMao|芒卯]] commands on the Wei side and appears less as an ambiguous persuader here than as the available Wei military figure in a failing campaign.
- Han's envoy does not merely beg Qin for help; he implies that if Han becomes truly desperate it will surrender to Qin's opponents, turning rescue into Qin's own interest.
- Wei Ran understands the signal and quickly moves with Bai Qi and Qin guest minister Hu Yang, reaching Huayang after an eight-day forced march.
- Qin defeats the Wei army, Mang Mao flees, three Wei generals are captured, and the episode reports 130,000 Wei casualties.
- Bai Qi then fights the Zhao commander Jia Yan, and the source preserves Hu Sanxing's doubt that two full wan of Zhao troops could have willingly drowned in the Yellow River without some unrecorded Qin tactic.
- After the defeat, [[DuanGanzi|段干子]] proposes ceding Nanyang to Qin for peace, but Su Dai warns Wei Anxi Wang that Duan Ganzi's private ambition and Qin's appetite for land point in the same direction.
- Su Dai's "抱薪救火" warning says land-for-peace works like carrying firewood to put out a fire: the fire ends only when the firewood is exhausted.
- Wei Anxi Wang still cedes the Nanyang/Xiuwu-area land, which the episode reads as process inertia and weak leadership rather than a solved strategic problem.
- [[HanXiwang|韩釐王]] dies in the same year, and [[HanHuanhuiwang|韩桓惠王]] succeeds into a situation where Han and Wei are both being pulled into Qin's orbit before the next Qin-Chu campaign.

## Key Quotes
> "如果你不帮我，我就向你的对手投降" - the host's summary of Han's envoy logic.

> "抱薪救火" - Su Dai's metaphor for ceding land to Qin for peace.
