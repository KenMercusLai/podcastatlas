# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》832丨为领导背锅，他是傻还是智？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 148 CE Jianhe second-year field before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-833-tanguan-neng-you-duo-lipu-kanwan-dadieyanjing-lqudlijjyqmcro-wsmrvwkodgt72|Hanji 833]] and later [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-842-shenme-shi-haoguan-ta-wei-baixing-gan-pinming-lj2okcbcvcmrfqrj3d-k1ylqdi2si|Hanji 842]]. It centers [[ChenShiLateHan|陈寔]] absorbing responsibility after [[HouLanLateHan|侯览]] pressures [[GaoLunLateHan|高伦]] into an unfit appointment, then extends Chen's profile through Taiqiu governance, open petition access, and quiet withdrawal after a tax accusation.

The second half adds [[ZhongJinLateHan|钟瑾]] as a prudence case between [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]]'s sharp Mencian right-wrong criticism and [[ZhongHaoYingchuanLateHan|钟皓]]'s self-protection counsel. The closing court notice creates a Liang-family transition layer: [[EmpressDowagerLiangNaLateHan|梁妠 / 梁太后]] returns power to [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] and dies, leaving [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] and the Liang clan exposed before the later honor expansion and coercive household politics.

## Key Claims

- Hou Lan's palace-side request makes local appointment quality politically unsafe because Gao Lun fears the eunuch's power even when the recommended person is unsuitable.
- Chen Shi's intervention is not simple scapegoating: he privately absorbs signature responsibility to preserve Gao Lun's clean reputation under coercive pressure.
- Gao Lun later publicly explains the truth, but Chen Shi continues to treat the fault as his own, turning responsibility into a moral rather than only procedural act.
- Chen Shi's Taiqiu administration is portrayed as quiet, trusting, and open to complaint channels rather than based on visible coercion or blocked petitions.
- Chen Shi's later silence and resignation after a tax accusation reinforce the episode's profile of non-defensive responsibility.
- Zhong Jin's reputation and Zhong Hao's counsel add a prudence branch: public right-wrong clarity can be morally admirable yet politically dangerous in a hostile age.
- Liang Na's return of power and death mark the loss of the Liang clan's dowager anchor before the downstream Liang Ji and Sun Shou power expansion.

## Key Quotes

> "把好的名声给了上司，把过错揽到自己身上" - Gao Lun's public explanation of Chen Shi's conduct.

> "人无是非之心，非人也" - the Mencian right-wrong standard Li Ying invokes against Zhong Jin.

> "当下世界很难分清绝对黑白" - Zhong Hao's prudence frame for self-protection.
