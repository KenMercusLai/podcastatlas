## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate pre-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-764-ta-weile-huoming-jing-jianjie-mie-le-luoma-diguo-lpk0c6muneabhk1oonzyjvawwf4|Hanji 764]] layer around [[DouXianEasternHan|窦宪]], [[BanChaoEasternHan|班超]], and the [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|北匈奴]]. It frames Dou Xian as a violent outer-relative whose Northern Xiongnu campaign begins as a bid to redeem a court crime, while still treating his operational victory as a major step in ending the Han-Xiongnu war cycle. The episode also adds Ban Chao's supply-line defense against the [[Yuezhi|月氏]], the [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] strike on the north, the [[JiluoshanBattleLateHan|稽落山之战]], [[YanranInscription|燕然勒石]], and a qualified prelude to the [[TransEurasianMigrationCascade|欧亚迁徙连锁]] claim later expanded in Hanji 764.

## Key Claims

- Dou Xian is presented as morally and politically dangerous but militarily capable, with the source explicitly separating his character failure from his battlefield achievement.
- Ban Chao defeats a large Yuezhi attack without direct overmatching by denying local grain, waiting for supply exhaustion, and intercepting the request for Qiuci provisions.
- Dou Xian's renewed western and northern pressure begins with the recovery of [[Yiwu|伊吾]] and the shock this creates among Cheshi polities.
- Dou Xian's main Northern Xiongnu campaign is tied to his attempt to escape punishment after killing Liu Chang, [[EmpressDowagerDouHeLateHan|窦太后]]'s lover.
- Southern Xiongnu intervention badly damages the Northern Xiongnu chanyu's camp before Dou Xian presses for final destruction.
- [[GengKuiLateHan|耿夔]] and [[RenShangLateHan|任尚]] defeat Northern Xiongnu forces at Jinwei Mountain in 91 CE, while the larger Dou campaign is remembered through Jiluoshan casualties, mass captures, and Yanran commemoration.
- The episode treats Northern Xiongnu westward movement, Xianbei occupation of the steppe vacuum, Southern Xiongnu/Hu inward migration, and later European upheaval as a chain of historical consequences, but notes that the European-evidence portion is deferred to the next episode.

## Key Quotes

> "宜将剩勇追穷寇" — host's phrase for Dou Xian's decision to keep pursuing after victory.

> "燕然勒石" — the campaign's compressed commemorative formula.

> "近三百年后的欧洲" — the episode's preview of the later European migration narrative.
