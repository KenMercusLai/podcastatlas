# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》150｜陈平为何被称之为成功政治家“样板”？（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens a [[ChenPing|陈平]] biographical branch after [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-149-xichu-bawang-xiangyu-weihe-shijun-2-lpohbb3prsgvokpjipfd7i1ntiwn|Hanji 149 part 2]] teased him as an unusually durable political operator. It backfills his poor Yangwu/Huyou origins, his brother's protection, his marriage into [[ZhangFu|张负]]'s family, and his rise in local standing through friendship, ritual service, and public trust. The episode's central interpretive move is to treat Chen Ping's later political success as rooted not only in clever stratagems but in early [[LocalSocialCapitalAccumulation|乡里社会资本积累]] and [[FairAllocationPoliticalSkill|公平分配式政治能力]].

## Key Claims
- [[ChenPing|陈平]] is introduced as a "successful politician" pattern because the host says he survives repeated patron changes, dangerous office politics, and eventual ascent without losing the practical realism associated with Huang-Lao style governance.
- His poverty is not treated as simple lack of ability: the source emphasizes reading, friendship, social movement, and an elder brother who absorbs the household labor burden so Chen Ping can develop other capacities.
- [[ZhangFu|张负]]'s marriage decision works as a political-investment scene. He notices Chen Ping at funerals, sees both poverty and visitor traffic at Chen Ping's home, and concludes that the young man will not remain poor.
- [[ChenPingWife|陈平妻]] becomes a material and symbolic bridge. The marriage brings Zhang-family funds, a public wedding, and a higher local social tier despite the stigma attached to her repeated widowhood.
- The source uses the marriage to highlight Chen Ping's realistic judgment: he accepts social backing and opportunity without being trapped by superstition or status embarrassment.
- The village sacrifice scene shifts the episode from social climbing to governance capacity. Chen Ping's fair division of sacrificial meat makes him legible as someone who can allocate contested benefits without alienating the community.
- The host treats the meat distribution as a small-scale preview of political skill: fairness, attentiveness to rank and feeling, and concrete execution matter more than abstract cleverness.
- The source's closing turn toward Chen Ping meeting [[LiuBang|刘邦]] is incomplete in the available transcript, so the ingest records the transition as a teaser rather than a finished event.
