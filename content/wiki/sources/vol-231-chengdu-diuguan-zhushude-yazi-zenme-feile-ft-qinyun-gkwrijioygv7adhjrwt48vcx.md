## Summary
This [[Budongqiu|不懂球]] episode with [[QinYunFootballCommentator|秦云]] examines how [[ChengduRongcheng|成都蓉城]] lost a league title after holding a large lead. It attributes the reversal to an interaction among thin squad depth, injuries and suspension, tactical predictability, weak professional management, deteriorating internal cohesion, and inexperienced pressure handling rather than to one match or person. The discussion turns the collapse into a broader case in [[FootballSquadResilience|squad resilience]], [[FootballClubProfessionalGovernance|professional club governance]], [[ChampionshipPressureManagement|championship-pressure management]], and the fragility of a weakened youth pipeline.

## Key Claims
- The episode describes a sharp split between championship-level first-half form and relegation-level second-half results after a lead reported at roughly twelve to fourteen points.
- [[FelipeChengduRongcheng|Felipe]]'s injuries and [[WeiShihao|Wei Shihao]]'s four-match suspension removed distinct attacking functions that similar-profile replacements could not reproduce, exposing a first-team roster built around one preferred lineup.
- Opponents increasingly adapted to a stable tactical model, while the episode judges [[AloisiChengduRongchengCoach|Aloisi]]'s match management and high-pressure adjustment insufficient.
- A selective team meal, conflict around prior coach-management relations, incomplete use of professional advice, and a shortage of experienced football administrators are presented as signs of weak communication and decision translation rather than proven single causes of the slump.
- The final run became a feedback loop among anxious coaches, players, and supporters: a reported 30-million-yuan title bonus could not restore clear execution after collective confidence had deteriorated.
- The episode says the coach was dismissed and the chair and general manager resigned the day after the title was lost, turning sporting failure into an immediate governance crisis.
- Chengdu's economy, football culture, and supporter market remain durable advantages, but an ageing first team, B-team relegation, weak academy linkage, uncertain management reform, and stronger rivals make another title window uncertain.
