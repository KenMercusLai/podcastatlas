## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-1-jiemi-wuguang-bei-miekou-de-lishi-zhenxiang-lk1bkhqkwf9upbbj08pjkn8cstyp|Qinji 129-1]] by showing the [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]] losing commanders after [[TianZang|田臧]] kills [[WuGuang|吴广]], while [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] keeps converting rebel fragmentation into Qin battlefield recovery. It then turns inward to [[LiSi|李斯]] and [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]], where Li Si's fear-driven memorial gives Hu Hai ideological cover for [[DuzeExtractionPolitics|督责式压榨政治]]. The closing story follows [[LiLiangZhaoGeneral|李良]], whose wavering after a [[WangLiQin|王离]] recruitment letter becomes open betrayal when [[ZhaoWuchenSister|武臣姐姐]] drunkenly fails to return his salute, leading to the killing of [[WuChen|武臣]] in [[Handan|邯郸]].

## Key Claims
- [[TianZang|田臧]] leaves Li Gui and other officers to continue the [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]] siege, takes elite troops west, is defeated by [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] at Ao granary, and dies.
- Zhang Han then turns back against Li Gui, defeats him around Yingyang, and continues defeating other Zhang Chu forces such as Deng Shuang and Wu Feng.
- [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] executes the defeated Deng Shuang after retreat, making the [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚]] crisis not only military but also internal-political.
- [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] blames [[LiSi|李斯]] for the spread of rebellion, while accusers also use [[LiYou|李由]]'s failure in Sanchuan as evidence against the Li family.
- Li Si fears losing office and does not resign; instead, his memorial flatters Hu Hai by arguing that a ruler should use inspection, punishment, and pressure so he can enjoy power without serving the people.
- Hu Hai accepts the argument and intensifies incentives for local officials: high extraction and heavy killing become signs of competence, worsening public suffering and further weakening Qin's support base.
- [[LiLiangZhaoGeneral|李良]] is blocked from taking Taiyuan because [[WangLiQin|王离]] and Qin's northern troops control the Jingxing route.
- Wang Li sends Li Liang a letter in Qin Er Shi's name, promising that his earlier service under Zhao will not be punished and that surrender can bring reward.
- Li Liang remains uncertain until [[ZhaoWuchenSister|武臣姐姐]], drunk after a banquet, fails to give him the return ritual he thinks his status requires.
- Li Liang's followers reframe the slight as evidence that [[WuChen|武臣]]'s new Zhao court will not respect them after stabilization, pushing him toward Qin and against Wu Chen.
- Li Liang kills Wu Chen's sister, attacks [[Handan|邯郸]], and kills Wu Chen plus left chancellor Shao Sao, turning a status insult into a restored-Zhao collapse.
- The final Qin Jia and Wu Ping Jun Pan fragment is treated as incomplete because the transcript cuts off before the context is recoverable.
