# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》140｜项羽与刘邦是真友情还是相互利用？（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows the immediate post-Qin rupture between [[LiuBang|刘邦]] and [[XiangYu|项羽]]. Liu Bang enters [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] first, keeps local support through [[YuefaSanzhang|约法三章]] and disciplined occupation, then seals the entry routes against the arriving coalition. Xiang Yu reacts with anger, sends [[YingBu|英布]] and others against the pass, receives [[CaoWushang|曹无伤]]'s warning that Liu Bang intends to rule Guanzhong, and decides before [[HongmenYan|鸿门宴]] to attack Liu Bang the next morning.

## Key Claims
- Liu Bang's earlier arrival in Guanzhong makes him the apparent beneficiary of the [[HuaiwangAgreement|怀王之约]], but it also turns his former ally Xiang Yu into a direct rival.
- Liu Bang's sealing of Guanzhong entry routes is a calculated attempt to keep the region before Xiang Yu and the other lords arrive, not merely a defensive camp measure.
- Xiang Yu's response is repeatedly framed as "great anger": the host criticizes him for reacting like someone whose toy has been taken, without first designing a post-conflict settlement.
- Cao Wushang's leak says Liu Bang wants to become king in Guanzhong, appoint [[ZiyingQin|子婴]] as chancellor, control treasure, and seek confirmation from [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]], making the crisis immediate.
- The episode contrasts Xiang Yu's anger with Liu Bang's capacity to restrain desire after entering Xianyang; [[FanZeng|范增]] sees that restraint as the signal that Liu Bang's ambition is dangerous.
- Fan Zeng is credited with recognizing Liu Bang as a threat but criticized for failing to supply a politically legitimate, nameable reason to attack him.
- The host argues that because Xiang Yu is an aristocratic commander, not a bandit, Fan Zeng should have engineered a public sequence: demand that Liu Bang open the pass, then attack only if he refuses.
- [[XiangBo|项伯]] enters as Xiang Yu's trusted uncle and Xiang-family elder; the available transcript only begins the Xiang Bo, [[ZhangLiang|张良]], and Liu Bang pre-banquet channel and does not yet complete the story.

## Key Quotes
> "项羽没有智慧" - opening judgment framing Xiang Yu's inability to see the situation's essence.

> "说明志向不小" - Fan Zeng's inference from Liu Bang's restraint in Guanzhong.

> "名正言顺、堂堂正正" - the public legitimacy standard the host says Fan Zeng should have handled.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is recorded. The source sharpens, rather than reverses, the existing Liu Bang/Xiang Yu contrast: Liu Bang remains morally mixed, but here his restraint and Guanzhong support become the direct reason Xiang Yu sees him as a rival.
- The episode's criticism of Fan Zeng as "not qualified" is source-scoped. It is compatible with existing pages that credit Fan Zeng's strategic insight, because the critique targets his failure to convert that insight into a legitimate and executable political sequence.
- The available transcript cuts off before the full Xiang Bo-Zhang Liang-Liu Bang interaction, so the wiki records only the pre-banquet setup from this source.
