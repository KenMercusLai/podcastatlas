# Introducing Revisionist History Season Five

## Summary

This short [[RevisionistHistory|Revisionist History]] trailer has [[MalcolmGladwell]] preview season five through a deliberately mixed register: parental joy, moral questions about suffering and responsibility, art museums, collecting, beauty, and comic cultural references. Its playful organizing term is “yuchas,” described as the reverse of “nachas”: joy that parents give their children rather than joy children give their parents.

The preview establishes the season's range but does not develop its arguments. Later episodes supply the evidence behind its fragments: [[dragon-psychology-101-dragon-psychology-101|Dragon Psychology 101]] examines museums, Smaug, and Andy Warhol; [[hamlet-was-wrong-hamlet-was-wrong|Hamlet Was Wrong]] develops the contrast between action and indecision; [[oh-howard-you-idiot-oh-howard-you-idiot|Oh, Howard, You Idiot!]] revisits Howard Hughes; and [[a-memorial-for-the-living-a-memorial-for-the-living|A Memorial for the Living]] asks how remembrance should guide obligations to people who suffer.

## Key Claims

- Season five was announced to premiere on June 18, 2020.
- Gladwell presents “yuchas” as joy flowing from parents to children and uses it as a playful promise that the season will give listeners both pleasure and intellectual stimulation.
- The season is framed as moving between serious inquiry, comic detours, and unexpected connections rather than following a single subject area.
- The preview asks how society should remember both people who suffered and people who caused suffering.
- An excerpt contrasts flawed decisive action with intelligent vacillation, previewing a tension between action, error, and paralysis.
- Art museums, collecting, and beauty are previewed through references to Howard Hughes, Vincent van Gogh, Smaug, and Andy Warhol.
- The trailer announces themes and subjects without supplying enough context to treat its excerpts as completed arguments or findings.
