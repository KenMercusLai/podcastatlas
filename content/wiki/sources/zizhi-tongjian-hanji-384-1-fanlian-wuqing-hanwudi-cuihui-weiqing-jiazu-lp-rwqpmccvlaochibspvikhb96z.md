# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》384-1｜翻脸无情，汉武帝摧毁卫青家族！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] short episode backfills the upstream political-pruning layer of the [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]] before the later open-conflict sequence in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-386-2-diwang-wuqing-huanghou-weizifu-beipo-zisha-lrvdfib62oczcy7loqss4-prkgl|Hanji 386-2]]. It follows the 91 BCE implication of [[GongsunHeWesternHan|公孙贺]] and his son, two princesses, and [[WeiKangWesternHan|卫伉]], while reading those deaths as attacks on the external support network around [[LiuJuCrownPrinceWesternHan|卫太子刘据]] and [[EmpressWeiZifuWesternHan|卫子夫]].

The durable synthesis is that the early Wugu cases are not merely scattered criminal accusations. The episode says officials such as [[DuZhouWesternHan|杜周]] could use curse allegations to remove figures with kinship, office, and military value to Liu Ju, while court actors watching [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s preference for [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|刘弗陵]] and [[LadyGouyiWesternHan|钩弋夫人]] recalculated where succession advantage lay.

## Key Claims

- After [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]]'s death, Han Wudi's earlier grief and honor for him do not protect the Wei household from later succession politics.
- In 91 BCE, Gongsun He and his son are destroyed after accusations involving curse magic against Han Wudi and adultery with Yangshi Princess.
- Zhuoyi Princess, Yangshi Princess, Empress Wei's side, and Wei Kang are implicated in the same Wugu wave.
- Wei Kang matters politically because he inherits Wei Qing's Changping marquisate and is treated by the episode as a military-capable outside support for Wei Zifu and Liu Ju.
- The episode says Wei Qing's other sons, Wei Buyi and Wei Deng, avoid implication partly because they did not participate in court affairs.
- Jiang Chong's hostility to Liu Ju and the possible elevation of Lady Gouyi's son Liu Fuling create incentives for officials to abandon the Wei-Liu Ju side.
- The episode treats the Wugu accusations as a route for weakening Liu Ju before the later command crisis and open fighting.

## Key Quotes

> "灭了卫氏家族" - the episode's compressed frame for the reversal after Wei Qing's death.

> "要先灭掉卫家的势力和太子刘据的外援" - the host's political explanation for why Wei Kang and Gongsun He become targets.
