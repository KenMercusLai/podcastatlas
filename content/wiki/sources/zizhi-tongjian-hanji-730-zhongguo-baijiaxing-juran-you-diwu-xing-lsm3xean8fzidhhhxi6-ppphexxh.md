# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》730丨中国百家姓，居然有“第五”姓？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the late [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-731-jinhu-wanmei-de-liuxiu-zui-da-quedian-shi-ta-lrhwupjmjm-hch3usx26jfxkhoma|Hanji 731]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-732-donghan-guangwudi-liuxiu-jiabeng-bingsi-de-lnsu1le2x-cj75cjslysvbndeuvr|Hanji 732]]. It joins three strands: the death of [[JiaFuEasternHan|贾复]], the early Guangwu-era profile of [[FifthLunEasternHan|第五伦]], and Liu Xiu's decision to perform [[FengshanRitualLegitimacy|封禅]] at [[Taishan|泰山]] after reading chenwei material.

The durable synthesis is that Guangwu's late reign combines practical personnel judgment with prophecy-backed ritual legitimacy. Fifth Lun is introduced before his later Zhangdi-era warnings as a fair market administrator and frank court respondent, while Liu Xiu's fengshan decision shows [[ChenweiPolitics|谶纬政治]] becoming [[ProphecyTriggeredPolicy|预言触发政策]] even for a ruler otherwise described as frugal and restrained.

## Key Claims

- In 54-55 CE, the episode presents repeated omens and disasters, including Ziwei/紫宫 star disturbance, flood, eclipse, and locusts, as the interpretive background for late Guangwu rule.
- [[JiaFuEasternHan|贾复]] dies in 55 CE and is remembered as a brave, undefeated founding commander who kept quiet about his own merits while Liu Xiu recognized them.
- [[FifthLunEasternHan|第五伦]] first appears as a Jingzhao subordinate managing the Chang'an market with fairness, anti-fraud enforcement, and public trust.
- Fifth Lun's wish to speak with Liu Xiu is framed as political confidence rather than empty boasting; when he finally follows the Huaiyang king to court, his answers satisfy Liu Xiu.
- Liu Xiu's questioning of rumors about Fifth Lun shows a personnel test based on candor and composure; Fifth Lun's explanation leads to rapid promotion from county magistrate to Kuaiji administrator.
- In 56 CE, Liu Xiu reads the `Hetu Huichang Fu` prophecy about the "ninth Liu" meeting destiny at Daizong, orders chenwei material checked, and accepts renewed fengshan proposals.
- The episode preserves a tension in Liu Xiu's rule: he dislikes waste and tries to simplify ritual materials, but still completes the Taishan heaven sacrifice, mountain ascent, jade-document sealing, Liangyin earth sacrifice, amnesty, and era change.

## Key Quotes

> "道不同，不相为谋" - Fifth Lun's answer to colleagues who mock his wish to advise the emperor.

> "赤刘之九，会命岱宗" - the prophecy line used to frame Liu Xiu's fengshan decision.

> "可不能随便在人家家里吃饭" - Fifth Lun's famine-and-war explanation for a rumored refusal of hospitality.
