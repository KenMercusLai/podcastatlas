## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the early Dou-family overreach layer before the later anti-[[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]] crisis. Dou Xian is restored as general-in-chief and ranked above the Three Excellencies, while [[DouJingEasternHan|Dou Jing]] and his household abuse people, seize property, humiliate women, and recall frontier cavalry for private protection. [[YuanAnEasternHan|Yuan An]] answers with impeachment and sealed historical-allusive remonstrance, contrasting destructive indulgence of relatives with the restrained example of [[DouGuiEasternHan|Dou Gui]]. The episode also notes [[HeSongEasternHan|He Song]]'s Jinan tutorship, [[LiuYanFulingKingEasternHan|Fuling King Liu Yan]]'s long but politically suspect royal career, floods, amnesty, and a solar eclipse.

## Key Claims

- The Dou-family danger is visible before the later Northern Xiongnu and palace-coup episodes: rank elevation, brotherly household violence, private troop access, and official silence already make the family a practical power center.
- Yuan An's anti-Dou posture begins with concrete legal charges against Dou Jing and the officials who concealed his behavior, not only with later frontier-policy disagreement.
- Yuan An's sealed memorial uses Spring and Autumn precedent to argue that unrestrained favoritism toward relatives harms both the state and the relatives themselves.
- Dou Gui is introduced as an internal Dou-family contrast: scholarly, frugal, restrained, and useful as evidence that anti-Dou judgment need not treat every family member alike.
- He Song's Jinan appointment shows an upright tutor trying to discipline royal arrogance without fully controlling it.
- Fuling King Liu Yan's career illustrates Emperor Ming and Emperor Zhang's repeated preference for limited penalties over execution in same-surname royal misconduct cases.
- The flood, amnesty, and solar-eclipse notices frame political disorder through disaster and omen markers, while leaving fuller interpretation to later episodes.
