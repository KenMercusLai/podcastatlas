# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》162-3｜嘴炮王郦食其，不动一兵拿下70余城

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-162-2-liubang-ruhe-shoushi-wei-gao-quan-zhong-de-xiashu-lqvye2zmqhu3otk0uwu7lakf2prz|Hanji 162-2]] by turning from [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s command recapture and the renewed [[XingyangStandoff|荥阳相持]] toward [[LiShiqi|郦食其]]'s eastern diplomatic mission. It shows Liu Bang using interception and [[PengYue|彭越]]'s supported raids to pull [[XiangYu|项羽]] between fronts, while Li Shiqi reframes [[Aocang|敖仓]], [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]], and [[Chenggao|成皋]] as the food-and-pass system Liu Bang must not abandon. The episode's central branch is Li Shiqi persuading [[TianGuangQiKing|田广]] at [[Linzi|临淄]] to shift [[QiState|齐国]] away from Chu and toward Han, only for [[HanXin|韩信]] to accept [[KuaiChe|蒯彻 / 蒯通]]'s merit argument and secretly attack Qi anyway.

## Key Claims
- After taking over Han Xin's troops, Liu Bang's immediate approach is not another frontal decision battle but interception, route pressure, and support for Peng Yue's harassment of Chu logistics.
- [[LiuJia|刘贾]] and [[LuWan|卢绾]] reinforce Peng Yue, whose capture of Suiyang, Waihuang, and other cities forces Xiang Yu to leave the central front again.
- Xiang Yu leaves [[CaoJiu|曹咎]] to hold Chenggao because of old trust and prior service to the Xiang family, not because the episode presents him as a proven superior field commander.
- Li Shiqi argues that Liu Bang should not give up the Chenggao-Aocang zone: the ruler depends on people, people depend on food, and Aocang's grain makes the front strategically recoverable.
- Li Shiqi proposes Qi as a diplomatic rather than purely military target because Tian-family power, coastline, mountains, rivers, and local strength make quick conquest costly.
- In Linzi, Li Shiqi tells Tian Guang that the world is turning toward Han, because Liu Bang entered Xianyang first, rewards cities and spoils, and can attract capable people, while Xiang Yu broke promises, killed Yi Emperor, and fails at reward allocation.
- The host stresses that some of Li Shiqi's military claims are overstatements, especially treating Aocang and Chenggao control as if already secured, but the argument works because it matches Qi's own need to reassess the Chu-Han balance.
- Qi accepts the proposal, drops the Chu alliance, sends envoys to Liu Bang for reconciliation, and relaxes the defensive posture previously set up against Han Xin.
- Han Xin initially prepares to stop after hearing Li Shiqi has persuaded Qi, but Kuai Che argues that Han Xin has no stop order and risks letting one envoy's speech outrank his own campaign merit.
- Han Xin's secret attack turns a successful diplomatic surrender into danger for Li Shiqi, because Qi can now read the envoy as a deceiver rather than as a real peace channel.

## Key Quotes
> "君王的天是人民，人民的天是粮食" - Li Shiqi's logistics-first warning to Liu Bang in the episode's paraphrase.

> "天下人心归向汉王" - Li Shiqi's opening frame to Tian Guang.

> "三寸不烂之舌说服齐国七十多座城池" - Kuai Che's comparison between Li Shiqi's diplomatic gain and Han Xin's military merit.
