# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》512丨西汉出征夜郎：我不当大哥好多年！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Heping 2 / 27 BCE under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]], before the later Chengdi and Wang-family sequence. It presents [[YelangStateWesternHan|夜郎]], Gouding, and Louwo fighting around [[ZhangkeCommanderyWesternHan|牂柯郡]], while [[YelangKingXingWesternHan|夜郎王兴]] escalates from regional conflict into open provocation against Han authority.

The durable synthesis is that frontier intervention requires more than a simple choice between war and conciliation. The episode uses [[DuQingWesternHan|杜清]]'s advice to [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]] to frame the decision as timing, supply, distance, local geography, and appointment fit: continued mediation may embolden Xing, but a campaign also needs preparation, food, and a capable commandery governor rather than impulsive display.

## Key Claims

- The episode opens from a moral frame about "德," then applies it to Ye Lang King Xing's arrogance and loss of political judgment.
- In 27 BCE, Ye Lang, Gouding, and Louwo fight each other and pull the danger zone toward Zhangke Commandery.
- The Zhangke governor reports that Ye Lang King Xing is the chief source of disorder and asks the court to gather troops for punishment.
- Chengdi's court first hesitates because the region is remote and military action is expensive, then sends Zhang Kuang with a tally to mediate.
- Ye Lang King Xing rejects mediation and has wooden images of Han officials set up as archery targets, turning local conflict into a symbolic challenge to Han authority.
- Du Qing warns Wang Feng that continued conciliation may give Xing time to annex nearby small polities, assemble forces, and attack Zhangke before the center can respond.
- Du Qing recommends quiet war preparation, advance grain provisioning, and selecting a fitted Zhangke governor under the principle that offices need suitable people, not merely available favorites.
