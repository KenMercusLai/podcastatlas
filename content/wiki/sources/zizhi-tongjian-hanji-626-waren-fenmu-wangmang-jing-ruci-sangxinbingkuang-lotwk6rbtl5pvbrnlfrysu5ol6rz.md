# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》626丨挖人坟墓！王莽竟如此丧心病狂？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills 5 CE, immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-627-1-nvxing-biting-dute-shijiao-jingqi-yinyu-beihou-de-lishi-zhihui-lqomjfejmglm9hylkomktx6rtbtt|Hanji 627-1]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-628-ruguo-chuanyue-jiao-ni-ruhe-dang-huangdi-lljocpgp247g7frbrkta39zrtlc|Hanji 628]]. It centers [[WangMang|王莽]]'s posthumous prosecution of [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]] and [[DingJiWesternHan|丁姬]]: he argues that their tombs, seals, ribbons, coffins, garments, and temple arrangements exceed the rank proper to consort-side women from a vassal kingdom, and he pushes [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] from refusal into limited and then fuller approval.

The durable synthesis is that late-Western-Han ritual language could be used not only to honor the dead but also to punish them again. Wang Mang converts above-rank burial and title memory into grounds for [[TombDesecrationPoliticalViolence|陵墓亵渎式政治暴力]], destroys tomb landscapes at mass scale, revokes ritual dignity, restores [[ShiDanWesternHan|施丹]] because he had opposed Fu Taihou earlier, and handles [[MaGongWesternHan|马公]] through a prestige- and relationship-constrained removal that strips high office while preserving marquis status.

## Key Claims

- Wang Mang reopens the Fu Taihou and Ding Ji question in 5 CE by treating burial rank, seals, ribbons, and posthumous titles as unresolved violations of [[LiAsPoliticalOrder|礼制政治秩序]].
- Wang Zhengjun initially refuses to approve grave opening, then accepts a limited compromise, and finally approves stronger demotion measures after Wang Mang continues pressing.
- The removal of seals, replacement of coffins, stripping of jeweled garments, relocation of remains, and leveling of tombs make this a state-directed tomb-desecration case rather than ordinary grave robbery.
- The participation of officials, relatives, students, and other groups turns the punishment of two dead women into a mass political performance.
- The follow-on rewards and punishments show the case functioning as retrospective factional sorting: Shi Dan is restored for earlier opposition to Fu Taihou, while former participants in her title settlement remain exposed.
- Ma Gong's survival shows a constrained purge outcome: personal ties to Wang Mang protect him from death, but not from losing the highest offices.
