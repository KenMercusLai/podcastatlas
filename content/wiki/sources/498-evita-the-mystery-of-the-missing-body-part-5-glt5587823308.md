# 498. Evita: The Mystery of the Missing Body (Part 5)

## Summary

This [[TheRestIsHistory]] episode follows [[EvaPeron|Eva "Evita" Perón]] after her 1952 death: embalming, an unrealized monument, confiscation after the 1955 coup, clandestine burial in Milan, return to [[JuanPeron|Juan Perón]] in Madrid, and final burial in Buenos Aires. It treats her preserved body as a [[PoliticalCorpseRelic]] through which Peronists, anti-Peronist governments, soldiers, priests, radicals, and later regime figures contested legitimacy and memory.

The political chronology connects Perón's weakening and exile to [[IsabelPeron|Isabel Perón]], [[JoseLopezRega|José López Rega]], the 1973 Peronist return, factional violence, and the [[ArgentineDirtyWar]]. Its central synthesis is that [[Peronism]] survived leaders and bans partly because symbols, ritual, grief, and political belonging could outlast formal organizations.

## Key Claims

- Pedro Ara's embalming and the proposed monumental tomb helped turn Evita's body into a quasi-sacred political presence rather than a private remain.
- After the 1955 coup, the new authorities banned Peronist symbols and secretly removed the body because its continued visibility threatened their attempt to erase Peronist legitimacy.
- Evita's concealment did not neutralize her memory: later Peronists and the [[Montoneros]] used her image as a radical and popular symbol.
- Juan Perón's exile network elevated Isabel as messenger and surrogate public presence, while López Rega's proximity to the couple converted household access into state influence.
- Perón's 1973 return exposed an internally divided movement, with left- and right-wing Peronists fighting at Ezeiza and López Rega later associated with the right-wing AAA.
- Evita's return to Argentina and 1976 burial ended the body's physical displacement without resolving the political struggle over what she represented.
- The episode repeatedly distinguishes documented events from rumors, memoir, fiction, and occult stories whose evidentiary status is uncertain.
