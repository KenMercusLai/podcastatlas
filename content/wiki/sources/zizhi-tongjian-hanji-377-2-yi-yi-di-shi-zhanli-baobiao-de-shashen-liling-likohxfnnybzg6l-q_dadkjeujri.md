# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》377-2｜以一敌十！战力爆表的杀神李陵！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] short episode supplies the detailed battlefield sequence behind the isolated infantry defeat previewed in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-380-1-liling-touxiang-xiongnu-ruhe-yanbian-cheng-gengdade-beiju-ls70jnzybc7z3of-tpfbdwb9ns89|Hanji 380-1]]. [[LiLingWesternHan|李陵]] leads five thousand infantry deep into [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] territory, uses carts, bows and crossbows, terrain, injury-based task allocation, and controlled fire to survive repeated attacks by much larger mounted forces, then loses his remaining operational margin as ammunition and equipment run out.

The episode presents internal intelligence failure as the decisive final turn. After military officer [[GuanGanWesternHan|管敢]] defects and reveals that Li Ling has no reinforcements and few arrows, the Xiongnu resume their attack, cut the road, and trap the Han force in a valley. [[HanYannianWesternHan|韩延年]] dies during the night breakout, while Li Ling's later surrender and its effect on [[SimaQian|司马迁]] are left for the following episode.

## Key Claims

- Li Ling combines reconnaissance, mapped terrain reporting, cart defenses, layered infantry weapons, and disciplined volleys before and during the battle.
- Five thousand Han infantry repeatedly inflict heavy losses on a mounted force that grows from about thirty thousand to a reported eighty thousand.
- Mountain, forest, marsh, and valley terrain repeatedly restrict cavalry advantages, although the same terrain later helps the Xiongnu block retreat and roll stones into the trapped force.
- Li Ling preserves combat capacity by assigning wounded soldiers different roles and continuing an organized fighting withdrawal.
- A counterfire that clears nearby reeds protects the Han force from an upwind Xiongnu fire attack.
- Guan Gan's defection reveals the absence of reinforcements, ammunition exhaustion, the forward-command arrangement, and flag identifiers, convincing the Xiongnu to renew an attack they had considered ending.
- Once arrows and carts are exhausted, improvised weapons and a night breakout cannot restore the force's earlier defensive system; Han Yannian dies and Li Ling's defeat becomes unavoidable.

## Key Quotes

> "如果还有数十支箭，哪怕十支，汉军就能逃脱。" - the episode's account of Li Ling identifying ammunition exhaustion as the final operational limit.

> "这是五千步兵对抗八万骑兵的一战。" - the host's summary of the reported force disparity.
