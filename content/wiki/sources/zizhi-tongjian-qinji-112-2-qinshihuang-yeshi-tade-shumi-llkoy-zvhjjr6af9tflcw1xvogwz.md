# 《资治通鉴·秦纪》112-2｜秦始皇也是他的书迷？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode introduces [[HanFei|韩非]]'s political frustration before the fuller Qin-court conflict developed in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-112-1-yaojia-shi-ruhe-ping-yizhangzui-po-siguo-hezong-lthw0fvvinmf6ghxpqut60agsueg|Qinji 112-1]]. It moves from [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]]'s post-[[LuBuwei|吕不韦]] consolidation and renewed Qin-Zhao war into Han Fei as a [[HanState|韩国]] royal who cannot make [[HanAn|韩王安 / Han An]] adopt Legalist reform, so his political voice concentrates in writings such as [[Gufen|《孤愤》]] and [[Wudu|《五蠹》]].

The source's main turn is that writing travels where office fails. Ying Zheng reads Han Fei, becomes an intense admirer, and wants to meet him; when Qin prepares to strike Han first, Han An sends Han Fei to Qin, but that entry into the power center exposes him to [[LiSi|李斯]]'s opposition and to the strategic danger of Qin using admiration without abandoning conquest.

## Key Claims
- After the removal of the Lao Ai and Lu Buwei blocs, Ying Zheng begins exercising more direct Qin power.
- In 234 BCE, [[HuanYiQin|桓齮]] attacks [[ZhaoState|赵国]], kills Zhao general Hu Zhe, and the episode says Qin kills 100,000 people.
- [[LiMu|李牧]] is appointed by Zhao, defeats Huan Yi at the Yi'an and Feixia front, and receives the title 武安君 from Zhao.
- In 233 BCE, Huan Yi attacks Zhao again and takes Yi'an, Pingyang, and Wucheng in the episode's account.
- [[HanState|韩国]] is presented as the weakest of the seven Warring States powers, while Han Fei is a Han royal who watches that weakness deepen.
- Han Fei repeatedly advises Han An to use Legalist methods and reform for self-strengthening, but the ruler does not adopt his proposals.
- Han Fei's resentment centers on rulers who cannot employ worthy and practical people while elevating shallow or useless courtiers.
- The episode names [[Gufen|《孤愤》]], [[Wudu|《五蠹》]], 《内外储说》, 《说林》, and 《说难》 as major Han Fei writings.
- [[ZhengRenMaiLv|郑人买履]] is used to explain Han Fei's anti-dogmatic practical judgment: fixed measures should not replace contact with reality.
- Han Fei's speech difficulty is presented as a practical limit on his political career, making writing his stronger channel.
- Ying Zheng reads 《孤愤》 and 《五蠹》, admires their author intensely, and says he would have no regret if he could meet and befriend him.
- When Qin decides to attack Han first, Han An sends Han Fei as envoy to Qin, where Ying Zheng wants to keep him for Qin service.
- The source uses the Qian Zhongshu "egg" anecdote as a warning that admiring a work does not make meeting the author politically safe.
- Li Si opposes Han Fei's position and tries to move Ying Zheng toward a choice that makes Han Fei give up on preserving Han.
