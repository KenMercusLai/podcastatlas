# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》514丨大小“王”对决，你猜谁能赢？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Heping 3-4 / 26-25 BCE under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]], after the Ye Lang sequence in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-513-yongchuang-duomingdao-hanchen-chenli-zhusha-yelang-guowang-lvfqkcbuzts119gtpzfipzo5wbo|Hanji 513]] and before the later Wang-family court conflicts. It introduces the coming confrontation between [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]] and [[WangShangWesternHan|王商]], then turns to [[LiuXiang|刘向]]'s family background, imperial text collation, and disaster-omen warning about Wang-family outer-relative power.

The durable synthesis is that scholarship, omen language, and hydraulic administration all become court-power diagnostics. Liu Xiang's `Hongfan Wuxing` project uses classics and recorded anomalies to warn Chengdi about Wang Feng's brothers, while the Yellow River breach at Pingyuan shows the state rewarding named engineering managers even as ordinary workers receive corvee-offset credit rather than wages.

## Key Claims

- The host frames Wang Feng and Wang Shang's later conflict as a destructive escalation of resentment, retaliation, and refusal to de-escalate.
- In Heping 3 / 26 BCE, Chu King Liu Xiao visits Chang'an, and Chengdi enfeoffs Liu Xiao's son Liu Xun as Guangqi Hou.
- Earthquake, landslide, river reversal, and eclipse notices create the omen setting for Liu Xiang's intervention.
- Liu Xiang is introduced through his father Liu De's meritorious and learned background, then as an early court scholar-official who becomes Guanglu Dafu.
- Chengdi orders a search for lost books; Chen Nong gathers texts, while Liu Xiang collates classics, masters, and poetry, with other specialists handling military, divination, and medical texts.
- Liu Xiang compiles disaster and anomaly precedents into `Hongfan Wuxing Zhuanlun`, letting Chengdi understand that Wang Feng's brothers and Wang-family power are the political target.
- The Yellow River breaches again at Pingyuan and floods Jinan and Qiancheng; Wang Yanshi and other officials complete repair after six months, while laborers' unpaid work is offset against six months of corvee and frontier-service obligations.
- In Heping 4 / 25 BCE, the Xiongnu chanyu visits Chang'an, Chengdi grants amnesty, Kuang Heng is reduced to commoner for occupying excess land, and Wang Shang becomes chancellor.
