# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1017丨三国隐藏英雄，陈登的传奇往事

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode moves one step before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1018-ta-weihe-rang-caocao-jidan-sunce-weiju-liubei-wanxi-lqccdhrjmero-gt7vsaq2ejuhhw8|Hanji 1018]] by centering [[ChenDeng|陈登]]'s 197 CE meeting with [[CaoCao|曹操]]. Chen Deng labels [[LyuBu|吕布]] as brave but unreliable, and the host turns "轻于去就" into both a late-Han political diagnosis and a modern warning about major life choices. The episode also reads [[ChenGui|陈珪]] and Chen Deng as a father-son team whose short-term balancing, middle-term local base-building, and long-term alignment with Cao Cao preserve family power through Lü Bu's collapse.

## Key Claims
- In 197 CE, [[ChenDeng|陈登]] tells [[CaoCao|曹操]] that [[LyuBu|吕布]] is brave but lacks strategy and changes sides too easily.
- Chen Deng urges early action against Lü Bu, while Cao Cao answers that Lü Bu is difficult to keep for long.
- Cao Cao rewards the Chen father-son channel by promoting [[ChenGui|陈珪]] and appointing Chen Deng as administrator of [[GuanglingCommandery|广陵]].
- Cao Cao privately asks Chen Deng to handle eastern affairs and to contact Lü Bu's subordinates as an inside channel.
- After taking Guangling, Chen Deng secretly trains soldiers, stores grain, and recruits Jiang-Huai local elites, turning an appointment into a strategic base.
- The host defines [[QingYuQuJiu|轻于去就]] as an unstable habit of leaving one person or organization for another when interests shift.
- Lü Bu's repeated side-switching and violent breaks with patrons make him hard to trust even when his military ability is outstanding.
- The episode extends the historical judgment into modern choice-making: marriage, jobs, friends, and investment should not be changed lightly because of short-term frustration.
- In the source's reading, Chen Deng's later defection during Cao Cao's 198 CE attack on Lü Bu completes the Chen family's shift from Lü Bu's orbit into Cao Cao's.
- [[ChenGui|陈珪]] is interpreted as more than a tactical adviser: short term he uses Lü Bu to balance Yuan Shu's pressure, middle term he uses Lü Bu-granted offices to build the family's force, and long term he keeps the family tied to Cao Cao.

## Key Quotes
> "轻于去就" - the compact character judgment the host uses for Lü Bu's unstable attachment and exit pattern.

> "诚难久养" - Cao Cao's reported answer that Lü Bu cannot be maintained safely for long.

> "三思而后行" - the traditional decision maxim the episode extends into a more cautious modern choice ethic.
