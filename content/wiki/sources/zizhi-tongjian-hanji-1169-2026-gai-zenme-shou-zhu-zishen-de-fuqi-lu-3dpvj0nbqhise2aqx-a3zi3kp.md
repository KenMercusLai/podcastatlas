## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode links traditional “fire year” risk language to a practical appeal for restraint, then continues the 219 CE [[FanchengBattle219LateHan|Fancheng campaign]]. [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]] secures sole command for the Wu operation, [[ZhaoYanLateHan|赵俨]] argues for a staged Cao relief, [[DongZhaoLateHan|董昭]] recommends disclosing [[SunQuan|孙权]]'s attack plan to both sides, and [[HuanJieLateHan|桓阶]] persuades [[CaoCao|曹操]] that rear-area support is more useful than personal intervention.

The durable synthesis is about decision structure under pressure. Clear final authority reduces coordination conflict; delay can be active when it gathers force and restores morale; information can be released to alter several actors' incentives at once; and a leader can create confidence through resources and strategic position rather than direct presence. Guan Yu's decision not to withdraw is presented as a [[SunkCostEntrapment|sunk-cost]] reading, but that interpretation remains qualified by his belief in the rear defenses and the value of the Fancheng objective.

## Key Claims

- The opening associates 2026's 丙午 and 2027's 丁未 with “fire” and historical disasters, but the supplied episode does not establish a causal relationship between calendrical labels, earthquakes, floods, or human behavior.
- The practical advice drawn from that framing is to reduce impulsive decisions, speech, conflict, and self-display rather than to treat the traditional forecast as verified prediction.
- Guan Yu's prisoner burden and use of grain in Sun-controlled territory add provisioning pressure and a political pretext to an already deteriorating Cao-Sun-Liu alignment.
- Lyu Meng opposes equal co-command with Sun Jiao by recalling earlier conflict between Zhou Yu and Cheng Pu; Sun Quan gives Lyu Meng final command and places Sun Jiao in support.
- Xu Huang avoids an immediate attack with inexperienced troops, uses pressure and deception, and receives Zhao Yan's advice to move close enough to signal relief while waiting for reinforcements and coordination with Cao Ren.
- Dong Zhao advises Cao Cao to appear to honor Sun Quan's secrecy request while deliberately sending the message into both Fancheng and Guan Yu's camp, improving defender morale and increasing pressure on Guan Yu.
- Guan Yu does not immediately withdraw because he trusts Jiangling and Gong'an and remains close to a valued result; the source interprets this as a possible sunk-cost trap rather than a complete account of his decision.
- Huan Jie argues that Cao Ren is capable and committed, so Cao Cao can contribute more by holding the rear with a large force than by personally entering the front-line danger.
- Cao Cao's withdrawal of Hefei-facing troops is presented as a costly signal that reduces Sun Quan's fear of a Cao attack while Wu moves against Guan Yu.
