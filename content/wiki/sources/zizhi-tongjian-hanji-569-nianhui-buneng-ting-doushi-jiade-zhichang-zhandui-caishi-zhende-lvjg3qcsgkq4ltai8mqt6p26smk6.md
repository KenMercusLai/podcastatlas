# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》569丨“年会不能停”都是假的，职场站队才是真的！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Suihe 2 / 7 BCE immediately after [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-568-hewu-duanjian-gudai-zheqi-panjue-zhide-dangdai-xueyixue-ljd2ojjmsla46no-9m2q802syxlp|Hanji 568]]. It follows [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] dismissing [[FuQianWesternHan|傅迁]], [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]] forcing a reversal, and [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]] with [[ShiDanWesternHan|师丹 / 施丹]] warning that contradictory edicts would confuse the realm and damage imperial credibility.

The durable synthesis is that appointment authority reveals where practical sovereignty sits. Aidi formally controls office but cannot decide who remains beside him; Fu Taihou's intervention protects her relative, teaches Fu Qian that he has a higher patron, and invites other officials to recalculate allegiance. The episode also qualifies the ministers' correction: once their memorial calls Fu Qian part of a faction that should be cleared, defense of consistent command becomes a factional challenge to Fu-family power.

## Key Claims

- Aidi dislikes Fu Qian, removes him from office, and orders him back to his home commandery.
- Fu Taihou angrily pressures Aidi until he reverses the dismissal and keeps Fu Qian in court, where Fu Qian resumes service as shizhong.
- Kong Guang and Shi Dan argue that opposed edicts create public confusion and reduce confidence in the emperor's commands.
- The episode treats Fu Qian's retention as evidence that Aidi lacks effective control over even close-attendant appointments.
- The modern workplace analogy says a protected subordinate will follow the higher patron, while peers infer that the nominal manager cannot enforce personnel judgment.
- The ministers' request to remove a Fu-family “faction” changes the dispute from neutral protection of imperial authority into a struggle over outer-relative power.

## Key Quotes

> "下属背后有更大领导撑腰" - the episode's modern analogy for Fu Qian's protection by Fu Taihou.

> "前后两次诏书内容相反" - the summarized basis of Kong Guang and Shi Dan's credibility warning.
