# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》412-1｜西汉政治明星田延年，为何在巅峰时陨落

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills an earlier [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]] inner-circle case before the later Huo-family collapse arc. It follows [[TianYannianWesternHan|田延年]] from trusted great-general staffer to Hedong taishou and da sinong, then explains why his embezzlement during Han Zhaodi's sudden funeral works became fatal only after he lied directly to Huo Guang.

The durable synthesis is that Tian Yannian's death is not just a corruption case. The episode treats his false denial as a breach of patron trust: Huo Guang might have been able to shield a guilty confidant, but protecting a subordinate who lied to him would damage the information discipline of his whole power system.

## Key Claims

- Tian Yannian's rise depends heavily on Huo Guang's trust: he moves from great-general staff work into intimate longshi service, then to Hedong taishou and da sinong.
- His Hedong record is presented as effective but harsh, with Ban Gu placing him among cruel officials.
- During Han Zhaodi's sudden death and mausoleum preparation, Tian handles scarce funeral materials and confiscates merchant stockpiles by framing them as profiting from an emperor's death.
- The merchants retaliate by gathering evidence that Tian inflated cart-rental costs and pocketed about thirty million cash.
- Huo Guang first appears to seek the truth so he can decide how to protect a trusted subordinate, but Tian's denial changes the case from corruption management into a trust rupture.
- Tian Guangming's indirect probe through [[DuYannianWesternHan|杜延年]] shows that Tian Yannian's fate still depends on Huo Guang's signal.
- Huo Guang's decision to let the case proceed "according to old rules" removes Tian's political protection, after which Tian Yannian kills himself.

## Key Quotes

> "功过不能相抵" - the episode's moral frame for judging Tian Yannian's earlier service and later crime separately.

> "如果霍光连欺骗自己的手下都要搭救，以后就没人会对他说真话" - the host's explanation of why lying to the patron becomes politically fatal.
