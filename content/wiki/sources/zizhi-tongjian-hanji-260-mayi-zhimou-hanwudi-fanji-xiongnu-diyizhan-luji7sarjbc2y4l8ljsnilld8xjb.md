# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》260｜马邑之谋，汉武帝反击匈奴第一战

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode presents the [[MayiAmbushWesternHan|马邑之谋]] as [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s attempt to replace the post-[[BaidengEncirclement|白登之围]] [[HanXiongnuHeqinPolicy|汉匈和亲政策]] with a limited border ambush. [[WangHuiWesternHan|王恢]] argues that recurring raids show accommodation has not produced deterrence, while [[HanAnguoWesternHan|韩安国]] warns that long-distance campaigning, supply strain, fatigue, and missed timing can deliver the army to the enemy instead.

Wang Hui answers by narrowing the proposed operation: [[NieWengyiWesternHan|聂翁壹]] will pose as a disaffected [[Mayi|马邑]] notable, promise the city and its wealth to [[JunchanChanyu|军臣单于]], and draw Xiongnu forces into a prepared encirclement near the frontier rather than force Han troops deep into the steppe. Wudi accepts the plan, reportedly mobilizes three hundred thousand troops, and places the anti-war Han Anguo over the five participating generals. The supplied episode ends after the chanyu commits ten thousand cavalry toward the bait but before the historical outcome, so it establishes design and mobilization rather than the plot's success or failure.

## Key Claims

- The episode treats the Mayi plan as a policy turn made possible by accumulated Han strength and continued dissatisfaction with the limits of heqin.
- Wang Hui argues that recurring frontier killing reflects a deterrence failure and that a successful strike is needed to make Xiongnu forces fear Han retaliation.
- Han Anguo does not reject strategy in the abstract; he objects to unfavorable timing, deep advance, weak supply, and the exhaustion of troops and horses.
- Wang Hui's counterproposal changes the operational problem by bringing the enemy to a prepared border ambush through promised gain rather than pursuing it far into steppe territory.
- Nie Wengyi's feigned defection depends on socially credible evidence: promised access to Mayi, its people and wealth, and displayed heads taken from condemned prisoners.
- Junchan Chanyu reportedly accepts the story and mobilizes ten thousand cavalry after seeing the heads, showing how greed and apparent insider access make the lure credible in the episode.
- Wudi's appointment of Han Anguo over the mobilized force is interpreted as balancing aggressive action with command restraint and court-faction reassurance.

## Key Quotes

> “不战而屈人之兵。” - the maxim the episode uses to frame Han Anguo's preference for favorable conditions over risky advance.

> “把匈奴单于吸引到边境来。” - the operational distinction Wang Hui draws between deep pursuit and a frontier ambush.
