# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》531丨面对造黄谣，她的办法值得你学习！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Hongjia 3 / 18 BCE after [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] deposes [[EmpressXuWesternHan|废后许氏]] and before the later Zhao-sister and Chengdi succession cases. It asks why [[ZhaoFeiyanWesternHan|赵飞燕]] would attack [[BanJieyuWesternHan|班婕妤]] as well as Empress Xu, arguing that Ban Jieyu's seniority, family standing, virtue reputation, and palace-relationship skill made her a potential empress-rank rival.

The durable synthesis is practical and evidentiary. Faced with an accusation of wugu-style cursing against the emperor, Ban Jieyu does not try to prove the negative directly; she answers that the act is irrational under either a believing or unbelieving view of spirits and inconsistent with her values, turning the defense toward motive, character, and plausibility. The host extends this into a modern warning about low-cost slander, reputational injury, evidence collection, authoritative support, and legal recourse.

## Key Claims

- Zhao Feiyan's attack on Ban Jieyu is framed as preventive palace politics: once Empress Xu is removed, Ban Jieyu's qualifications could still obstruct Zhao Feiyan's path.
- Ban Jieyu's practice of recommending other women to Chengdi is read as both court wisdom and possible alliance-building, not merely self-effacement.
- Wugu and curse accusations are especially hard to disprove because the accused can be trapped in proving nonexistence.
- Ban Jieyu's reply works by denying motive and expected benefit: righteous conduct may not guarantee blessing, and unrighteous conduct offers no rational gain whether spirits exist or not.
- The episode connects the historical case to modern slander, online abuse, and workplace accusations, where the accuser's cost can be low while the target's proof and repair costs are high.
- The practical method offered is evidence where possible, motive and value-based rebuttal where direct evidence is impossible, plus authoritative support or legal action when needed.
- The closing advice combines bounded counterattack with disengagement from malicious people: do not create trouble, but answer real harm clearly and keep moving.

## Key Quotes

> "证有不证无" - the host's logic for why invented accusations are hard to disprove.

> "不怕事，但是我从来不惹事" - the host's summary of the desired response posture.
