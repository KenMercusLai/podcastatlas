# 《资治通鉴·周纪》84丨防止秦国灭楚竟用这招？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows the moment after [[HanState|韩国]] and [[WeiState|魏国]] have moved into Qin's orbit and [[BaiQi|白起]] is preparing another campaign against [[ChuState|楚国]]. [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]] writes to [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] and uses [[InterestReframingPersuasion|利益重构式说服]]: he does not ask Qin to pity Chu, but argues that attacking Chu through Han and Wei would expose Qin's rear, strengthen future rivals, and betray Qin's own eastward strategy. Qin accepts the argument, stops Bai Qi's deployment, dismisses Han and Wei troops, and sends Huang Xie back to create a Qin-Chu friendly alliance.

## Key Claims
- After [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-83-zhuhou-bailan-jing-chengle-jingdian-ji-zhong-ji-ljp7nbqnrctr5mw6i9u982abmx-1|Zhouji 83]], [[HanHuanhuiwang|韩桓惠王]] and [[WeiState|魏国]] are positioned to follow Qin against Chu, making the next anti-Chu campaign look imminent.
- Chu's danger is not only military weakness after [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-82-daqin-de-hewuqi-baiqi-laile-lo0w8mifkijhzmx7fdwcimmv7ade|Zhouji 82]]; Qin's challenge is whether it can reach and exploit [[MiHeng|楚顷襄王]]'s new position without depending on untrustworthy corridor states.
- Huang Xie begins by acknowledging Qin's unprecedented strength, then warns that extreme success can generate reversal if Qin keeps extending without guarding against accumulated resentment.
- The letter uses earlier historical warnings: [[Fuchai|吴王夫差]] trusted Yue before [[Goujian|越王勾践]] destroyed Wu, and [[ZhiYao|智瑶]] trusted Han and Wei before they turned against him.
- Huang Xie argues that Han and Wei are not reliable Qin partners because Qin has accumulated deep enmity with their people and elites over several generations.
- If Qin borrows Han-Wei territory to attack Chu, Qin must worry from the first day about whether its return route remains safe; if it avoids that route, the terrain and rivers make concrete gains difficult.
- Huang Xie predicts that [[HanState|韩国]], [[WeiState|魏国]], [[ZhaoState|赵国]], and [[QiState|齐国]] will profit from a Qin-Chu war, with Wei and Qi possibly becoming much stronger while Qin bears the main risk.
- His alternative is not passive peace: Qin should ally with Chu, press Han, station force around Xinzheng, coerce Wei, and use Qin-Han-Wei pressure toward Qi to cut the waist of the anti-Qin world.
- The host summarizes the letter as a four-step structure: warn about overextension, recover Qin's eastward strategic aim, expose the practical route problem, and propose Qin-Chu alliance as the better expansion path.
- The source says the passage in [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] draws from [[Shiji|《史记》]]'s Chunshenjun material, with [[SimaGuang|司马光]] preserving the strategic core while reducing some literary ornament.
- Huang Xie's use of [[Shijing|《诗经》]], Yijing, and allusive precedent shows that classical citation was a working language of elite diplomacy, not just decoration.
- The episode closes [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s 周纪四 frame by showing a near war paused through persuasion, while warning that Qin's military engine has not truly stopped.
