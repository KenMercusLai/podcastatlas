# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》426｜两汉第一清官赵广汉，为何不是好官？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the Yuan康 1 / 65 BCE case of [[ZhaoGuanghanWesternHan|赵广汉]], a celebrated clean and forceful Western Han official whose reputation cannot excuse private retaliation through public power. The source follows Zhao from anti-corruption and anti-haoqiang fame in Yingchuan to the Su Xian arrest, Rong Xu killing, counterattack against [[WeiXiangWesternHan|魏相]], [[XiaoWangzhiWesternHan|萧望之]]'s impeachment, public petitioning, and execution.

The core synthesis is that clean reputation, administrative ability, and popular support are insufficient if an official turns information networks and legal authority into private revenge. The closing contrast with [[YinWengguiWesternHan|尹翁归 / 尹翁规]] suggests that strong local governance still needs procedural restraint, equal treatment, and order rather than personal anger.

## Key Claims

- Zhao Guanghan's early prestige came from attacking corrupt officials and local strongmen in Yingchuan, including the use of anonymous-reporting infrastructure.
- The Su Xian case turns Zhao's clean-official image into a power-boundary problem: a retainer's private wine-market dispute becomes an official arrest.
- Zhao's killing of Rong Xu on suspicion, without adequate proof, escalates the case from retaliatory detention into lethal abuse of authority.
- Zhao's attempt to revive an old case involving Wei Xiang's household is presented as counter-investigation against the chancellor investigating him, not neutral law enforcement.
- Popular mourning and petitioning for Zhao worsen the imperial-security problem because a punished official with mass public support can appear to challenge sovereign authority.
- The episode places Zhao beside Zhang Tang and Gongsun Hong to argue that clean or capable officials can still harm others, retaliate, or lack moral limits.
- Yin Wenggui's final segment offers a contrast: he is also clean, strict, and information-rich, but the source frames his rule as more orderly and less privately retaliatory.

## Key Quotes

> "清正廉洁的官，未必是一个好人。" - the episode's clean-official boundary lesson.

> "仅凭怀疑就杀人，在任何朝代都不行。" - the host's judgment on the Rong Xu killing.
