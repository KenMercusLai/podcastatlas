# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》332-2｜大汉丞相李蔡之死，究竟有没有黑幕

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode links the large Guandong flood and resettlement of Yuanshuo 4 to a court convention in which disaster responsibility could move from the emperor toward the chancellor. It then centers on [[LiCaiWesternHan|李蔡]]'s appropriation and sale of land adjoining an imperial burial-ground grant, followed by removal, imprisonment, and suicide under [[HanWudi|汉武帝]].

The durable synthesis is narrower than the title's suggestion of a hidden plot. The episode supplies a political context in which Li Cai may have failed to perform expected disaster accountability, but the stated case against him is the land transaction; additional corruption, a secret murder order, and the host's reconstruction of his inner fear are not independently established.

## Key Claims

- The episode dates a severe Guandong flood and the resettlement of 725,000 people to Yuanshuo 4 / 119 BCE, while emphasizing the logistical difficulty and continuing shortage faced by the displaced population.
- Within the episode's Heaven-human response model, a major disaster creates responsibility pressure that cannot safely remain on the emperor and may be absorbed by the chancellor through illness, resignation, and ritualized imperial acceptance.
- The host argues that Li Cai did not follow this expected resignation script; this is an interpretation of political convention rather than a separately documented charge in the supplied text.
- After [[GongsunHong|公孙弘]], Li Cai rises from yushi dafu to chancellor, but the episode does not treat promotion as evidence of stronger governing capacity.
- [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] grants the elderly Li Cai roughly twenty mu near Yangling, presented as an honorable future burial arrangement near Emperor Jing's mausoleum.
- Li Cai is said to appropriate three additional qing, sell the land to a wealthy Chang'an buyer for 400,000 cash, and receive removal and imprisonment after the transaction becomes known.
- Li Cai dies by suicide in prison. The episode characterizes this as guilt and fear of legal punishment, while explicitly saying that it has no record of other corruption.

## Key Quotes

> “四十万钱” - the reported proceeds used to frame the disproportion between a relatively bounded gain and Li Cai's fatal outcome.

> “没有记载” - the episode's express limit on allegations of additional corruption.

## Connections

- [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] - grants the burial land and later removes and imprisons Li Cai after the adjoining-land transaction.
- [[GongsunHong|公孙弘]] - predecessor after whom Li Cai moves from yushi dafu to the chancellorship.
- [[LiGuangHanGeneral|李广]] - elder brother used by the host as a moral and reputational contrast with Li Cai.
- [[DisasterOmenCrisisRedirection|灾异压力转移]] - extended with a Western Han resignation-script variant in which a chancellor is expected to absorb disaster responsibility without necessarily being charged for causing the disaster.
