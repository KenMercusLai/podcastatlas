# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》756丨为什么劝你得饶人处且饶人？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 86 CE [[EmperorZhangOfHan|汉章帝]] layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-757-rensheng-xiang-shao-zou-wanlu-xian-zhaozhun-dingwei-lri1zyiq8210ymqjakznyp3v4k6x|Hanji 757]]. It uses "得饶人处且饶人" to connect Zhangdi's ministerial mediation, restitution to [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|Northern Xiongnu]], [[ZhengHongEasternHan|郑洪]]'s final anti-[[DouXianEasternHan|窦宪]] remonstrance, [[FifthLunEasternHan|第五伦]]'s clean self-scrutiny, [[ZhangYuEasternHan|张鱼 / 张于]] releasing [[HaoWuQiangLeader|浩武]], and [[BanChaoEasternHan|班超]] defeating [[JiaoZhongShuleKing|叫中]] in [[ShuleKingdom|疏勒]].

The durable synthesis is that forbearance is not passive softness. The episode presents selective restraint as a governance tool: Emperor Zhang preserves court speech after conflict, buys back captives and livestock to protect frontier credibility, and Zhang Yu releases Haowu when the promised withdrawal is worth more than a captive's death, while Ban Chao's Shule case shows that leniency must still be bounded by threat assessment.

## Key Claims

- Zhangdi treats ministerial disagreement as necessary to major decisions and stops Zheng Hong, Fifth Lun, Wei Biao, and others from turning policy conflict into resignation and factional rupture.
- His Northern Xiongnu decision uses humility and restitution as statecraft: Han pays the [[SouthernXiongnu|Southern Xiongnu]] side for captured people and livestock, returns them to Northern Xiongnu, and preserves the credibility of tribute and submission.
- Zheng Hong repeatedly warns that Dou Xian's power is too great, then is counteraccused after his impeachment of Dou-linked officials leaks to Yang Guang and Dou Xian.
- Zheng Hong's last memorial treats Dou Xian's power as a state-survival problem, making the episode an early anti-Dou warning before the better-developed Hanji 759-767 sequence.
- Fifth Lun's biography turns clean officeholding inward: even an unaccepted horse gift still troubles him because the remembered donor might have affected later recommendation judgment.
- Zhang Yu's release of Haowu shows tactical forbearance on the Qiang frontier: sparing one captured leader produces withdrawal, disbandment, and temporary easing of the frontier situation.
- Ban Chao's killing of Jiaozhong shows the boundary of forbearance: apparent surrender is not accepted blindly when the commander reads it as a trap backed by Kangju troops.
- The closing personal lesson on life positioning is a teaser for Hanji 757 rather than this source's main durable historical contribution.

## Key Quotes

> "不与人争者，常多得利；退一步者，常进百步" - the opening formula for the episode's forbearance frame.

> "如果群臣都沉默不语，并不是朝廷的福气" - Zhangdi's reasoning for preserving open court discussion.

> "是否有私心" - the Fifth Lun question that turns clean conduct into self-examination.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is recorded. The episode backfills Hanji 756 before Hanji 757-758 and adds earlier Zheng Hong, Fifth Lun, Zhang Yu, Hao Wu, and Ban Chao material rather than overturning the existing 88-107 CE Dou, Qiang, and Western Regions sequence.
- Identity caution: the source's 第五轮 is normalized to [[FifthLunEasternHan|第五伦]], 张鱼 is aligned with the existing [[ZhangYuEasternHan|张于 / Zhang Yu]] route, and 元安 is treated as the existing [[YuanAnEasternHan|袁安]] office route because the Taipu-to-Sikong context matches.
