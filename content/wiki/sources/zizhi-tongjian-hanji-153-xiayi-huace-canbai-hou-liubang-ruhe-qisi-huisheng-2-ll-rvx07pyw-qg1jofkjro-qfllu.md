# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》153｜“下邑画策”惨败后刘邦如何起死回生（2）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-153-xiayi-huace-canbai-hou-liubang-ruhe-qisi-huisheng-1-lgvjuxuskyyfmyeeqm9y8aeln-hu|Hanji 153 part 1]] by moving from [[XiayiPlan|下邑画策]] into the first recovery mechanisms after the [[PengchengBattle|彭城之战]]. [[LiuBang|刘邦]] sends [[SuiHe|随何]] toward Jiujiang, falls back to [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]], receives emergency support from [[XiaoHe|萧何]] and [[HanXin|韩信]], and begins [[HanCavalryFormation|汉军骑兵建设]] under [[GuanYing|灌婴]]. The episode closes by shifting from battlefield repair to internal politics, as [[ZhouBo|周勃]] and Guan Ying attack [[ChenPing|陈平]] over character, loyalty, and alleged bribery.

## Key Claims
- Liu Bang's recovery is multi-line rather than single-stroke: diplomacy against [[YingBu|英布]], defensive concentration at [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]], Guanzhong emergency mobilization, Han Xin's blocking action, cavalry-building, and supply-road work all matter.
- Sui He's Jiujiang mission is presented as the first operational step after the Xiayi Plan: Liu Bang needs Ying Bu to rebel or at least hold [[XiangYu|项羽]] for several months.
- [[XingyangStandoff|荥阳相持]] begins when Liu Bang withdraws to Xingyang and scattered troops gather there; the source frames this as the start of a two-year-and-three-month Chu-Han stalemate.
- Xiao He's levy of Guanzhong old, weak, or not-yet-service-registered men shows how severe the manpower crisis is after Pengcheng.
- Han Xin's defeat of Chu pursuers south of Xingyang makes the defensive line viable and complements the earlier claim that Liu Bang needs scalable commanders.
- Liu Bang's cavalry response is explicit learning from defeat: after seeing Xiang Yu's mounted shock at Pengcheng, he builds his own cavalry force around Guanzhong troops, Qin cavalry specialists, and Guan Ying's command.
- [[LiBiQinCavalry|李璧]] and [[LuoJiaQinCavalry|骆甲]] refuse the top cavalry command because their Qin background may not convince Han troops, but they accept deputy roles and recommend a Liu-side trusted commander.
- Guan Ying's first cavalry victory east of Xingyang damages Chu's mounted offensive enough that Xiang Yu's forces can no longer push past Xingyang in the episode's account.
- The road from Xingyang toward the Yellow River ferry and [[Aocang|敖仓]] makes logistics part of the recovery, not a background detail.
- The closing Chen Ping accusations show a second crisis inside recovery: old followers resent an outsider who was promoted quickly and given supervisory authority.

## Key Quotes
> "必須重新配置戰略資源" - the episode overview's recovery frame after Pengcheng.

> "楚漢相持階段...持續兩年零三個月" - the source's framing of the Xingyang phase.

> "劉邦此前主要依靠步兵" - setup for why cavalry-building becomes urgent.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is recorded. This source extends Hanji 153 part 1 by showing initial execution and stabilization after the plan, rather than changing the Xiayi Plan itself.
- Source-scope caution: Guan Ying being "only second to Xiang Yu" as a cavalry tactician is recorded as the host's evaluation, not as an independent ranking.
- Source-scope caution: the claims against Chen Ping are accusations inside a personnel conflict; this episode ends before resolving their truth or Chen Ping's answer.
