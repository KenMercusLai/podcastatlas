# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》428｜从汉宣帝改名，聊聊古代的“避讳”

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills an early [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝 / 刘病已 / 刘询]] layer before the later Hanji 430-465 Xuan branch. It starts from Xuan's Yuankang 2 / 64 BCE edict on judicial fairness and official misconduct, then reads his name change from 刘病已 to 刘询 as a mercy-driven attempt to reduce ordinary people's risk of violating imperial name taboo.

The durable synthesis is that [[ImperialNameTaboo|避讳]] is not only a courtesy custom. In this episode it appears as a writing, speech, office, exam, literary, and punishment regime: Xuan's self-renaming lowers the burden of two common characters, while later dynastic examples show taboo expanding into legally enforced and socially internalized constraints.

## Key Claims

- Xuan's edict criticizes officials who manipulate law, accept bribes, distort statutes, impose extra labor, and decorate stations to flatter traveling superiors.
- Because epidemic conditions affected multiple commanderies and kingdoms, Xuan remits land tax for the most heavily affected areas.
- Xuan says ancient rulers' personal names were not known to commoners, while his own common-name characters could trap people who used them in memorials or ordinary writing.
- The episode infers that people may already have been punished for accidentally using "病" or "已", making the change to 刘询 a practical reduction of taboo risk.
- The host presents this as the first Zizhi Tongjian appearance of the taboo issue and uses it to introduce the broader [[ImperialNameTaboo|避讳]] system.
- Later examples show the taboo field expanding from imperial names into official names, ancestor names, exam eligibility, literary wording, dictionary compilation, and household reading habits.
- The episode contrasts Xuan's burden-reducing act with later periods where taboo became more formalized, more expansive, and sometimes lethal.

## Key Quotes

> "刑罚法律若不公平，百姓就难以生存" - the episode's summary of Xuan's judicial edict.

> "病、已是民间常用字" - the practical problem behind Xuan's name change.

> "本朝帝王或长者的名字不能直接说出来" - the episode's closing definition of taboo practice.
