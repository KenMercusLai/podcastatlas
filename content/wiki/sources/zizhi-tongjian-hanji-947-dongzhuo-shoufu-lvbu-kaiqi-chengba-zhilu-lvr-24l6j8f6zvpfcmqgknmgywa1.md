# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》947丨董卓收服吕布，开启称霸之路

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate pre-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-948-dongzhuo-feidi-quanchen-ruhe-gaibian-huangquan-youxi-ltekg1a-5b5u37aeekjm3dm2m6kk|Hanji 948]] late-Han takeover sequence. It follows [[DongZhuo|董卓]] from his first armed encounter with [[LiuBianHongnongWang|刘辩 / 弘农王]] and Liu Xie, later [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], through [[BaoXin|鲍信]]'s missed preemptive-strike advice to [[YuanShao|袁绍]], Dong Zhuo's staged reinforcement trick in [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]], and [[LyuBu|吕布]]'s killing of [[DingYuanLateHan|丁原]]. The distinct contribution is the conversion of a power vacuum into usable court control: Dong Zhuo manufactures military momentum, absorbs rival troops, takes sikong rank, and coerces [[CaiYong|蔡邕]] into the legitimacy surface later developed in Hanji 948-949.

## Key Claims

- In 189 CE, after the palace disorder following [[HeJin|何进]]'s death, Liu Bian and Liu Xie flee near Luoyang; [[MinGongLateHan|闵贡]] appears in the escape scene before Dong Zhuo reaches the emperor's party.
- Dong Zhuo reads the fire and disorder around the capital as a strategic opening, moves quickly, and brings troops to meet the young ruler rather than waiting for ordinary court command.
- The greeting scene already contains [[ProtectorCaptorAmbiguity|护驾与挟持模糊性]]: Dong Zhuo appears as a rescuer of the emperor, but his soldiers frighten Liu Bian and allow him to ignore officials asking him to withdraw the force.
- Liu Bian's panic and Liu Xie's composed answers lead Dong Zhuo to consider replacing the reigning emperor with Liu Xie, making this episode the motive and first-observation layer before [[CoercedImperialReplacement|胁迫式废立]] in Hanji 948.
- The source says five of six imperial seals are recovered after the emperor returns to the palace, while the [[HeirloomImperialSeal|传国玉玺]] remains missing; the missing seal becomes a symbolic sign of weakened [[MingqiLegitimacy|名器合法性]] before later Sun Jian and Yuan Shu branches.
- Bao Xin warns Yuan Shao that Dong Zhuo's strong troops and obvious ambition make immediate attack necessary while Dong Zhuo has just arrived and his force is tired.
- Yuan Shao hesitates because he had wanted to use Dong Zhuo against the eunuchs and now fears confronting him directly; the source treats this as a lost timing window under [[XingshiqingStrategicAssessment|行势情战略评估]].
- Dong Zhuo starts with only about three thousand infantry and cavalry, but repeatedly cycles soldiers out at night and back into the city by day, creating the appearance of constant Liangzhou reinforcements. This becomes a source case for [[MilitaryPresenceTheater|军力声势表演]].
- Troops formerly attached to He Jin and [[HeMiaoLateHan|何苗]] soon go over to Dong Zhuo, turning psychological intimidation into actual military expansion.
- Lü Bu is introduced as Ding Yuan's trusted subordinate and clerk; Dong Zhuo reads his ambition and uses Ding Yuan's position or payoff as bait, after which Lü Bu kills Ding Yuan and brings his force to Dong Zhuo.
- The episode mentions the [[RomanceOfTheThreeKingdoms|《三国演义》]] version involving Li Su, Red Hare, gold, pearls, and a jade belt, but keeps the historical core to Dong Zhuo's successful recruitment of Lü Bu and Lü Bu's killing of Ding Yuan.
- Dong Zhuo has Liu Hong removed from the sikong office under a rain-related pretext and then takes the office himself, converting military control into formal [[CourtOfficeCapture|朝廷开府任官失序]].
- Cai Yong's backstory explains why his later Dong Zhuo connection is coerced rather than voluntary: after prior exile and renewed slander by Wang Zhi, Dong Zhuo summons him, threatens his whole family when he refuses, and then rapidly promotes him once he appears.
- Dong Zhuo's treatment of Cai Yong links this episode to [[CoercedTalentLegitimacy|胁迫征士合法性]]: he needs scholar prestige, but the recruitment channel is backed by violence and family threat.

## Key Quotes

> "驥驅逐不如駑馬之安步" - opening maxim used to contrast hesitation with steady execution.

> "傳國玉璽不知所終" - the source's symbolic marker of dynastic disorder after the emperor returns.

> "三天內讓他連續升遷三次" - the source's compressed image of Dong Zhuo's urgent borrowing of Cai Yong's prestige.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction with existing wiki pages is recorded. This source fills the missing pre-Hanji-948 layer: Dong Zhuo's first contact with the escaped emperor, Yuan Shao's missed attack window, manufactured troop strength, Lü Bu's defection from Ding Yuan, and Cai Yong's coerced entry.
- Source-scope caution: Dong Zhuo's motive for preferring Liu Xie, Lü Bu's internal ambition, and Bao Xin's "only chance" framing are treated as the episode's interpretation rather than independent access to inner motive.
- Source-scope caution: the Romance of the Three Kingdoms gift package for Lü Bu is recorded only as a literary-memory comparison; the historical claim retained here is Dong Zhuo's inducement, Lü Bu's killing of Ding Yuan, and the resulting transfer of force.
