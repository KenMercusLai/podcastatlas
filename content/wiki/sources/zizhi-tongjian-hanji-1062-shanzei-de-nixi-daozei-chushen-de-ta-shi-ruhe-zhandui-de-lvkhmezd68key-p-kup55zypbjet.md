# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1062丨山贼的逆袭：盗贼出身的他是如何站队的？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the Jian'an 9 [[YuanShao|袁绍]] succession-collapse branch after [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1061-yuanshao-zhizi-he-shafu-chouren-jiecheng-qinjia-lnsnj6ipiyscw-vopnjjgoxpnt5r|Hanji 1061]]. It first turns [[ZhangYanLateHan|张燕]] and the [[HeishanArmy|黑山军]] into a case of mountain-based survival and timed alignment, then shifts back to [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s siege of [[YeLateHan|邺城]], where moat digging, water diversion, isolation, and [[LiFuLateHan|李孚]]'s disguised messenger run define the next campaign stage.

The core synthesis is that value preservation works on both sides of the episode. Zhang Yan remains worth recruiting because his Taihang base is hard to erase and still useful after the Yuan family weakens, while Cao Cao tightens Ye through engineering and information isolation; Li Fu's infiltration briefly breaks that isolation by performing Cao-side authority inside the camp and escaping under the cover of mass surrender.

## Key Claims

- In Jian'an 9, first month, [[ZhangYanLateHan|张燕]] sends envoys to [[CaoCao|曹操]] and offers to help attack the Yuan brothers.
- Cao Cao responds by appointing Zhang Yan as Pingbei general, making Zhang Yan's final alignment a new [[OfficeBasedRebelIncorporation|office-based incorporation]] layer after the earlier Han court grant.
- The episode reads Zhang Yan's rise from Chu Yan / Chu Feiyan and [[ZhangNiujiaoLateHan|张牛角]] successor into Black Mountain leader as a political-symbol and cohesion story, not only a bandit biography.
- Zhang Yan's durable value comes from the [[HeishanArmy|黑山军]]'s mountain base, farming-fighting mix, and ability to survive repeated pressure from [[YuanShao|袁绍]].
- After Yuan Shao dies, [[YuanTan|袁谭]] and [[YuanShang|袁尚]] are distracted by succession conflict, giving Zhang Yan room to absorb scattered troops and choose the side more likely to dominate Hebei.
- The episode says Cao Cao later pairs force and title: he first demonstrates military superiority against Zhang Yan's main force, then converts Zhang Yan's remaining local value into rank and marquis status.
- In the Ye campaign, Cao Cao abandons earth hills and tunnels, opens a long moat, lets it look shallow at first, then deepens it overnight while [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]] fails to disrupt the work.
- Cao Cao diverts Zhang River water into the moat, isolating Ye from outside communication and supply; the source says starvation kills much of the city population.
- Yuan Shang returns with about ten thousand troops and sends [[LiFuLateHan|李孚]] to carry news into the city.
- Li Fu disguises himself as a Cao-side military inspector or `dudu`, uses evening light, headgear, branch props, harsh scolding, and physical intimidation to pass through Cao's camp and reach the Ye wall.
- After delivering Yuan Shang's message to Shen Pei, Li Fu proposes releasing old, weak, sick, and disabled people with white flags; he and his riders blend into that crowd and escape.

## Key Quotes

> "政治投机小能手" - the episode's compact judgment on Zhang Yan's flexible alignment.

> "不容易被消灭" - the value Zhang Yan preserves through the Black Mountain base.

> "要看他怎么出来" - Cao Cao's response after learning Li Fu has entered Ye.
