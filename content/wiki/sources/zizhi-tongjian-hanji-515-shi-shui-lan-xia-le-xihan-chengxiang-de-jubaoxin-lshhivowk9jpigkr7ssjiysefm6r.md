# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》515丨是谁拦下了西汉丞相的举报信？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the Wang-Wang conflict previewed in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-514-da-xiao-wang-duijue-ni-cai-shui-neng-ying-lnlvthkkqbp_zo2lfpp4bqmh5nsu|Hanji 514]]. After a major Langya disaster, chancellor [[WangShangChancellorWesternHan|乐昌侯王商]] tries to impeach Qi commandery governor [[YangRongWesternHan|杨荣]], but [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]] first asks him to stop and then uses his control of the Shangshu memorial route to keep the accusation from [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]].

The durable synthesis is that formal authority to accuse does not guarantee access to the ruler when an interested powerholder controls transmission. Wang Feng's counterattack through [[GengDingWesternHan|耿定]] shifts the dispute from Yang Rong's official responsibility to unverifiable allegations about Wang Shang's private conduct; Chengdi initially resists the weak evidence, yet Wang Feng's continuing pressure ends in Wang Shang's removal and death. The closing Sima Guang passage adds the normative personnel rule that talent amplifies moral direction, while the episode itself shows why moral accusations still require evidence rather than factional convenience.

## Key Claims

- The episode explicitly distinguishes chancellor Wang Shang from the Wang-family marquis of the same name; the chancellor is not Wang Feng's brother.
- Wang Shang seeks to impeach Yang Rong after a large Langya disaster, while Wang Feng asks him to desist because Yang Rong is connected to Wang Feng by marriage.
- Wang Feng controls the Shangshu memorial route and has Wang Shang's accusation intercepted before it reaches Chengdi.
- Wang Feng then directs Geng Ding to accuse Wang Shang of sexual misconduct and possible involvement in a killing connected to Wang Shang's sister.
- The episode says those private allegations cannot be verified; Chengdi initially refuses to treat ambiguous private conduct as sufficient grounds against a senior minister.
- Continued pressure from Wang Feng and an omen-based attack by Zhang Kuang precede Wang Shang's dismissal; Wang Shang becomes ill, vomits blood, and dies three days later.
- Chengdi removes Wang Shang's dependants from palace service but lets his eldest son Wang An inherit the Lechang marquisate.
- The closing personnel discussion restates Sima Guang's fourfold distinction among saint, gentleman, fool, and talented lesser person, warning that ability without virtue magnifies harm.

## Key Quotes

> "才者德之资也，德者才之帅也。" - Sima Guang's talent-and-virtue formulation as presented by the episode.

> "谣言止于智者，信于愚者，起于谋者。" - the host's frame for distinguishing rumor makers, believers, and resistant listeners.

## Contradictions

- Identity correction: the existing wiki had merged chancellor/Lechang Hou Wang Shang with the Chengdu Hou Wang-family outer relative of the same name. This source explicitly says the chancellor was not Wang Feng's brother, so the identities are now split into [[WangShangChancellorWesternHan|乐昌侯王商]] and [[WangShangWangClanWesternHan|成都侯王商]].
- No settled contradiction is adopted about the underlying accusations. Yang Rong's exact responsibility for the Langya disaster, Wang Feng's jealousy, Geng Ding's claims, Zhang Kuang's omen argument, Chengdi's final reason for dismissing Wang Shang, and the medical or political cause of Wang Shang's death remain source-scoped.
