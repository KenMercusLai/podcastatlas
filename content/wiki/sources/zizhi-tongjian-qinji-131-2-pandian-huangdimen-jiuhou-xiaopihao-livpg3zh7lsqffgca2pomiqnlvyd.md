## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode steps outside the narrow late-Qin chronology to ask whether drinking is mainly a source of failure or can also force decisive action. It uses stories around [[CaoCao|曹操]], [[ZhangFei|张飞]], [[LiYuanTang|李渊]], [[GaoYangNorthernQi|高洋]], [[JinPingMei|《金瓶梅》]], [[YangGuifei|杨贵妃]], [[MeiLanfang|梅兰芳]], and [[LiQingzhao|李清照]] to argue that alcohol is not a moral agent by itself. The source's central synthesis is [[AlcoholAsPowerCatalyst|酒作为权力催化剂]]: drink can reveal or intensify desire, courage, grief, humiliation, and brutality, and the damage radius becomes much larger when the drinker holds political power.

## Key Claims
- The host frames alcohol through a neutral-catalyst claim: wine or liquor has no fixed character, but the person and power structure around it make it appear comic, strategic, erotic, violent, or tragic.
- In the [[CaoCao|曹操]] Wancheng story, [[ZhangXiu|张绣]]'s side uses drink to weaken [[DianWei|典韦]]'s guard role and hide his weapon, after which Cao Cao escapes but [[CaoAng|曹昂]] and Cao Anmin die.
- [[ZhangFei|张飞]]'s drunken loss of Xuzhou is used as a shorter "drinking causes trouble" example because it leaves [[LiuBei|刘备]] without a base.
- The [[LiYuanTang|李渊]] Jinyang-palace story is presented as the counterexample: [[PeiJi|裴寂]] and [[LiShimin|李世民]] use a drinking setup, palace women, and the risk of exposure under [[YangGuangSui|杨广]] to push Li Yuan toward rebellion.
- The source warns that this "drink helps accomplish things" story only works because Li Yuan already has unusual capacity and resources; ordinary people placed in the same irreversible situation might simply be executed.
- [[GaoYangNorthernQi|高洋]] is the episode's strongest negative case: the source presents his drunkenness as releasing violence, sexual abuse, filial breakdown, humiliation of ministers, and theatrical cruelty inside an unchecked imperial household.
- The [[JinPingMei|《金瓶梅》]] section treats "酒为色媒人" as a literary mechanism: drinking carries desire, social darkness, and household corruption across the novel rather than functioning as incidental scenery.
- The [[GuifeiZuijiu|《贵妃醉酒》]] branch turns drunkenness toward gendered performance and emotional abandonment, with [[YangGuifei|杨贵妃]] drinking after Tang Minghuang goes elsewhere and later stage versions reshaping the erotic material.
- [[MeiLanfang|梅兰芳]] is credited in the source with removing or softening the more explicit material in older versions of [[GuifeiZuijiu|《贵妃醉酒》]], helping turn it into a classic stage work.
- The [[LiQingzhao|李清照]] segment notes that wine is important across her lyric corpus, but the supplied markdown cuts off before the episode fully develops that final branch.

## Key Quotes
