# 《资治通鉴·周纪》32丨“史上首位联合国秘书长”苏秦

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[SuQin|苏秦]] after [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-31-hezong-lianheng-weiou-qin-lncpgmxrojc0y1meg9a3ul-o9nih|Zhouji 31]] by narrating his tour through [[HanState|韩国]], [[WeiState|魏国]], [[QiState|齐国]], and [[ChuState|楚国]] to complete [[HezongAlliance|合纵]]. The episode highlights his recurring persuasion method: praise a ruler's strength, recast serving Qin as endless territorial loss, then use dignity and face to make submission feel intolerable. It also uses Wei's army list and [[Linzi|临淄]]'s prosperity to show Warring States military and urban change before ending with Su Qin as covenant leader and simultaneous minister of the six eastern states.

## Key Claims
- The source keeps the setting in 333 BCE, Zhou Xian Wang's thirty-sixth year, while continuing the Su Qin branch whose dating the wiki already keeps source-scoped through [[ChronicleChronologyDrift|编年错位]].
- Su Qin's pitch to [[HanXuanhuiwang|韩宣惠王]] frames service to Qin as an impossible bargain: Han has limited land, while Qin's appetite for concessions has no natural stopping point.
- His argument pattern is a case of [[DignityBasedPersuasion|dignity-based persuasion]]: after material threat analysis, he makes acquiescence feel dishonorable through status language and the "chicken mouth / ox rear" proverb.
- The Wei section turns a persuasion speech into military history. Even if the troop numbers are exaggerated, they point toward [[WarringStatesMilitaryTransformation|Warring States military transformation]]: infantry categories dominate, chariot warfare loses centrality, and cavalry is present but not yet decisive.
- The Qi section uses [[Linzi|临淄]] as evidence for [[WarringStatesUrbanProsperity|Warring States urban prosperity]]: dense households, military manpower, entertainment, vehicles, crowds, and confident public life become reasons Qi should not lower itself before Qin.
- Chu receives less detailed treatment, but [[ChuWeiwang|楚威王]]'s acceptance completes the six-state chain and turns hezong from argument into a working diplomatic arrangement.
- Su Qin's title as "从约长" and his simultaneous service as minister to six states are the basis for the episode title's modern comparison to a United Nations secretary-general.
- The closing [[Shiji|《史记》]] material shifts from diplomacy to social reversal: when Su Qin passes through [[Luoyi|洛阳 / 洛邑]], royal reception and family submission dramatize the contrast with the relatives who had mocked him when he was poor.

## Key Quotes
> "宁为鸡口，无为牛后" - the proverb Su Qin uses to push Han from prudence into dignity.

> "韩王从其言" - the compact Zizhi Tongjian result after Su Qin's Han pitch.

> "并相六国" - the phrase behind Su Qin's legendary six-state minister image.
