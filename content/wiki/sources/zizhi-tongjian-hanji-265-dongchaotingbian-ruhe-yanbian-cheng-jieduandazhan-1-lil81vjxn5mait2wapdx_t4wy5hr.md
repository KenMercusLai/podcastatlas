# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》265｜东朝廷辩，如何演变成揭短大战？（1）

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the court-debate prequel to [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-265-dongchaotingbian-ruhe-yanbian-cheng-jieduandazhan-2-lvu23osgv46nn3rbgque1mbhmpgw|Hanji 265 part 2]]. After [[TianFenWesternHan|田蚡]] imprisons [[GuanFuWesternHan|灌夫]] for grave disrespect, [[DouYingWesternHan|窦婴]] defends Guan Fu as a drunken offender being burdened with additional accusations. Tian Fen answers by portraying Dou Ying and Guan Fu's guest networks and court commentary as a possible threat to the two palaces and the political order, moving the case from conduct and punishment into court security.

The resulting audience does not produce a clean collective judgment. [[HanAnguoWesternHan|韩安国]] gives weight both to Guan Fu's military service and to the accusations of local predation; [[JiAnWesternHan|汲黯]] and [[ZhengDangshiWesternHan|郑当时]] initially support Dou Ying but do not sustain a direct answer, and the remaining ministers stay silent. [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] rebukes the court, while [[EmpressDowagerWangWesternHan|王太后]] treats criticism of her brother Tian Fen as a threat to her natal family. Han Anguo later advises Tian Fen that a chancellor should answer attack with resignation and institutional restraint rather than mutual abuse, separating legal uncertainty from the political conduct expected of high office.

## Key Claims

- Dou Ying argues that Guan Fu's immediate offense is drunken speech and that Tian Fen has attached unrelated allegations in order to destroy him.
- Tian Fen reframes the dispute as a security problem by accusing Dou Ying and Guan Fu of gathering powerful guests, criticizing the court, monitoring the eastern and western palaces, and awaiting disorder.
- Han Anguo refuses a simple side-taking answer: he cites Guan Fu's inherited and personal military merit while also acknowledging allegations of violent associates, local oppression, accumulated wealth, and intrusion on royal interests.
- Ji An and Zheng Dangshi are said to favor Dou Ying but retreat from an explicit answer under imperial questioning, while the rest of the court remains silent.
- Empress Dowager Wang directly pressures Han Wudi after hearing the debate, identifying Tian Fen's vulnerability with the standing of her own natal family.
- Han Wudi explains that he convened a court debate because both Dou Ying and Tian Fen were imperial in-laws; an ordinary case could have been resolved directly by edict.
- After court, Han Anguo tells Tian Fen that a chancellor should resign, return his seal, and let the ruler restore him rather than trade personal accusations in public.

## Key Quotes

> “蚡所爱倡优巧匠之属，皆乱法者。” - the episode's rendering of Tian Fen's effort to distinguish his own pleasures from the allegedly political guest networks of Dou Ying and Guan Fu.

> “今日廷论，局趣效辕下驹，吾并斩若属矣！” - Han Wudi's reported rebuke to ministers who discussed the rivals privately but would not judge them openly.
