# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》862丨他因一句话休妻，最终身败名裂

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 164 CE Huan-era reputation and local-governance layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-863-yi-ba-huo-shaochu-de-zhandouli-ljpfmj8pphunruioce64ywjk7yhb|Hanji 863]]. It opens with a source-scoped family-education frame in which parental anxiety, old pain, and future fear are projected onto children, then turns to [[GuoTaiLateHan|郭泰]]'s warning to [[HuangYunLateHan|黄允]] and Huang Yun's reputation collapse after he discards his wife for an imagined Yuan-family marriage.

The second half contrasts empty fame with quiet moral authority. [[HuangYunLateHan|黄允]] and [[JinWenjingLateHan|晋文经]] influence appointment opinion while avoiding office and scrutiny, until [[FuRongLateHan|符融]] warns [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]] that their fame is out of line with proven conduct; [[QiuXiangLateHan|仇香]] then handles [[ChenYuanLateHan|陈元]]'s alleged unfilial conduct through diagnosis, persuasion, and ethical teaching rather than punishment, winning [[WangHuanLateHan|王奂]]'s support and Guo Tai's respect.

## Key Claims

- The opening argues that parents often react to a child's current behavior through projected fear, past wounds, and future anxiety rather than through the child's actual present situation.
- [[GuoTaiLateHan|郭泰]] praises [[HuangYunLateHan|黄允]]'s talent but warns that reputation after forty requires strict self-correction or it will be lost.
- [[HuangYunLateHan|黄允]] hears [[YuanWeiLateHan|袁隗]] praise him as a possible son-in-law, discards his wife, and has hidden misconduct exposed by her in front of assembled relatives.
- [[HuangYunLateHan|黄允]] and [[JinWenjingLateHan|晋文经]] turn fame into selection influence: officials seek their opinions while they refuse office, stay in Luoyang under the pretext of illness, and block ordinary access to themselves.
- [[FuRongLateHan|符融]] warns [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]] that Huang Yun and Jin Wenjing have unproven conduct and empty reputation; after this scrutiny, their public standing fades.
- [[QiuXiangLateHan|仇香]] treats [[ChenYuanLateHan|陈元]]'s alleged unfilial conduct as a failure of education rather than proof of evil nature, persuading mother and son until Chen Yuan becomes known as filial.
- [[WangHuanLateHan|王奂]] recognizes Qiu Xiang's non-punitive transformation as higher than forceful severity and funds his study at [[Taixue|太学]].
- The closing disaster notices record a meteorite in Hu county and hail in [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]], then preview the next episode's military discussion of [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]] and [[DuShangLateHan|度尚 / 杜尚]].

## Key Quotes

> "父母越讨厌孩子某个特点，那个特点越可能在孩子身上被放大" - the opening parenting projection frame.

> "得婿如是，足矣" - Yuan Wei's praise that Huang Yun mistakes for a marriage opportunity.

> "空誉违实" - Fu Rong's warning that reputation and reality have separated.

> "不罚而化" - the source's contrast between punishment and Qiu Xiang's moral transformation.
