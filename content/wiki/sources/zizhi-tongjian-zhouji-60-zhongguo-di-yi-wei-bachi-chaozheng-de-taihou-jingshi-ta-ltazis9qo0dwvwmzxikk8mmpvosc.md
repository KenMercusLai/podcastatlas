# 《资治通鉴·周纪》60丨 中国第一位把持朝政的太后竟是她？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode covers [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s tenth through thirteenth years, 305-302 BCE, after the [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] succession shock. It makes [[XuanTaihou|芈八子 / 宣太后]] and [[WeiRan|魏冉]] the center of early [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] politics: Wei Ran suppresses [[YingZhuang|嬴壮]]'s revolt, Qin's court shifts toward [[ForeignRelativeCourtPolitics|楚系外戚政治]], and Xuan Taihou steers Qin into marriage-backed repair with [[ChuState|楚国]]. The same alignment then flips after [[MiHeng|芈横]] kills a Qin official while serving as hostage, pushing Qin away from Chu and toward a repaired relationship with [[HanState|韩国]] and [[WeiState|魏国]].

## Key Claims
- In 305 BCE, Zhao Wuling Wang's campaign against [[ZhongshanState|中山国]] captures Danchiu, Huayang, Chizhisae, Haocheng, Shiyi, Fenglong, and Dongyuan, forcing Zhongshan to cede four cities for peace.
- [[XuanTaihou|芈八子 / 宣太后]] promotes kin and [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]]'s brothers after Qin Wuwang's death; [[WeiRan|魏冉]] becomes Rang Hou and guards [[Xianyang|咸阳]] as general.
- [[YingZhuang|嬴壮]] and allied princes, officials, and followers revolt, but Wei Ran suppresses them violently, secretly executes [[QinHuiwenhou|惠文后]], and sends Qin Wuwang's wife back to Wei.
- Qin Zhaoxiang Wang is young, so Xuan Taihou holds the court and hands major power to Wei Ran, making early Qin Zhaoxiang Wang politics a case of [[QueenMotherRegency|太后摄政]] and foreign-relative rule.
- Xuan Taihou arranges a Chu bride for Qin Zhaoxiang Wang, and Qin-Chu relations warm while Qin-Han and Qin-Wei relations cool.
- In 304 BCE, Qin Zhaoxiang Wang and [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] meet at [[Huangji|黄棘]], reaffirm the alliance, and Qin returns [[Shangyong|上庸]] to Chu.
- In 303 BCE, Qin attacks Wei and captures [[PubanWarringStates|蒲阪]], [[YangjinWarringStates|阳晋]], and [[FenglingWarringStates|封陵]], then attacks Han and recaptures [[WusuiWarringStates|武遂]].
- Qi, Han, and Wei punish Chu for abandoning the north-south [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] line and moving toward Qin; Chu sends crown prince Mi Heng to Qin as hostage and Qin dispatches [[KeqingTong|客卿通]] to rescue Chu.
- The episode contrasts three Qin diplomatic configurations: Qin Huiwen Wang's Qin-Han-Wei line against Qi-Chu, Qin Wuwang's Yiyang-focused attack on Han, and Xuan Taihou's Qin-Chu marriage alignment against Han-Wei.
- In 302 BCE, Qin Zhaoxiang Wang, [[WeiXiangwang|魏襄王]], and Han crown prince [[HanYing|韩婴]] meet at [[LinjinWarringStates|临晋]], Han Ying visits Xianyang, and Qin returns Puban to Wei.
- Mi Heng kills a Qin official after a private quarrel and escapes back to Chu without apology, rupturing the Qin-Chu alliance and giving Qin a future reason to attack Chu.
- The source's core diplomatic lesson is [[WarringStatesAllianceSwitching|战国同盟转向]]: marriage, hostages, returned land, and battlefield help can stabilize relations briefly, but one violent hostage incident can reverse the whole alignment.

## Key Quotes
> "宣太后把持朝政" - the episode's compact frame for Qin's early Zhaoxiang Wang power structure.

> "秦楚关系迅速升温" - the episode's description of the post-marriage diplomatic turn.

> "秦国外交从联楚攻韩魏转向联韩魏以制楚" - the transcript's summary of the final alliance reversal.
