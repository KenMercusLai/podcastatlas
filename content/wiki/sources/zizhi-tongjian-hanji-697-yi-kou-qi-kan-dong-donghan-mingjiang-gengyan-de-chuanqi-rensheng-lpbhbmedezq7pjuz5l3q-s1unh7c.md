# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》697丨一口气看懂东汉名将耿弇的传奇人生

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the early [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] consolidation sequence immediately before Hanji 698 by finishing the [[ZhangBuEasternHan|张步]] and Qi campaign branch. It follows [[GengYanEasternHan|耿弇]] pursuing Zhang Bu to Pingshou, accepting Zhang Bu's surrender after Liu Xiu pits Zhang Bu and Su Mao against each other, dispersing the remaining army and wagons, and then returning after Qi is pacified.

The episode also gives a compact Geng Yan profile, treating him as an idealized Eastern Han founding commander whose career combines military household background, tactical deception, battlefield composure, and later withdrawal from high command. Its political back half widens the same consolidation frame through [[Taixue|太学]] restoration, Hou Ba's appointment, [[FengYiEasternHan|冯异]] surviving slander through Liu Xiu's trust, [[LuFangEasternHan|卢芳]]'s Xiongnu-backed northern disruption, and [[WeiXiaoEasternHan|隗嚣]] being warned by [[ZhengXingEasternHan|郑兴]] against kingly and imperial-grade overreach.

## Key Claims

- Liu Xiu turns Zhang Bu and Su Mao against each other by promising immediate enfeoffment to whichever one kills the other and surrenders.
- Zhang Bu kills Su Mao and submits at Geng Yan's camp, after which Geng Yan sends Zhang Bu to Liu Xiu and returns to Pingshou to handle the remaining force.
- Geng Yan disperses Zhang Bu's more than 100,000 troops and more than 7,000 wagons, accepts the Wuxiao remnants at Chengyang, and completes the pacification of Qi.
- Liu Xiu combines pardon, an Anqiu marquisate, and retention of Zhang Bu's wife and children in Luoyang, making surrender survivable but controlled.
- The episode profiles Geng Yan as a commander of psychological warfare, strongpoint attacks, encirclement-relief, false-target strikes, avoid-strong/strike-weak tactics, and morale stability after injury.
- Liu Xiu's visit to the rebuilt Taixue and appointment of Hou Ba place postwar military closure beside restoration of court, learning, and ritual order.
- Lu Fang's Xiongnu-backed return and Wei Xiao's attempted kingly/imperial-style posture show alternative poles of authority still threatening Guangwu consolidation.

## Key Quotes

> "每战必胜" - host's evaluative frame for Geng Yan's battlefield record.

> "有志者事竟成" - the episode's concluding Geng Yan life-summary phrase.

> "对实际无益，对名义有害" - Zheng Xing's warning against Wei Xiao's imperial-grade offices.
