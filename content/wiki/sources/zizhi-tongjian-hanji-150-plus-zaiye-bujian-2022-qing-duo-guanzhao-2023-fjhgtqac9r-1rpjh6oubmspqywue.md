# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》150 PLUS｜再也不见2022，请多关照2023

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] PLUS episode steps away from the Chu-Han storyline to offer a short New Year cultural note. It links [[YuanDanFestivalLayering|元旦节日层累]] to the [[YaoShunYuLegend|尧舜禹传说]], [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s calendar standardization, the textual claim about [[JinShu|《晋书》]], and festival customs recorded through [[JingChuSuiShiJi|《荆楚岁时记》]], [[SiminYueling|《四民月令》]], and [[LuYou|陆游]]. Its main wiki value is to show how a calendar term becomes a layered holiday made from rulership myth, official calendar order, food, exorcistic plants, door symbols, poetry, and contemporary blessing.

## Key Claims
- The episode presents the [[YaoShunYuLegend|尧舜禹传说]] as a traditional origin frame for the year's beginning: Yao passes rule to Shun, Shun later passes rule to Yu, and Shun's sacrifice to Heaven and to Yao becomes associated with the opening day of the year.
- It says that from [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] onward, the first day of the first month in the Xia calendar was treated as 元旦, and that this naming convention continued until the end of the Qing.
- It also says that the term "元旦" appears earliest in [[JinShu|《晋书》]], a claim recorded here as source-scoped because the episode does not develop a textual history.
- The episode uses [[JingChuSuiShiJi|《荆楚岁时记》]] to describe early New Year customs in the Jingchu region after formal greetings, including pepper-cypress wine, peach decoction, and eating eggs.
- [[SiminYueling|《四民月令》]] is cited for the early appearance of pepper-cypress wine, with pepper and cypress framed through fragrance, longevity, and protection from illness.
- Peach decoction and peach charms are explained through older beliefs that peach could suppress harmful forces, placing New Year food and doorway practice near [[ChineseFolkReligionLayering]].
- The foods named in the episode include eggs, soup cakes, and New Year cakes; they make the holiday a case for [[FestivalFoodMaterialHistory]] rather than only a calendrical label.
- A [[LuYou|陆游]] poem is used to show the later literary afterlife of peach charms and pepper wine in New Year imagery.
- The episode ends by turning the historical and ritual material back into a 2023 blessing for listeners.

## Key Quotes
> "愿昼吉祥，夜吉祥，昼夜六时，恒吉祥" - the closing blessing that returns the cultural-history note to the listener's New Year.

> "元旦快乐" - the final holiday greeting.
