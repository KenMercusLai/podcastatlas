# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》548丨职场嫉妒心重？芮淇教你化解！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills a Yuanyan-period [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] court episode immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-549-ruguo-youren-ma-bifu-ni-zhidao-sha-yisi-bu-lskfkw_udnitdfzzg1lw8b1ch3si|Hanji 549]]. Chengdi publicly favors [[ZhangYuWesternHan|张瑜 / 张禹]] over [[WangGenWesternHan|王根]] in a land dispute, creating a visible status threat for the Wang-family outer-relative side. When earthquake and eclipse talk is turned into an accusation that outer-relative power caused Heaven's warning, Zhang Yu refuses to exploit the opening; he argues that disaster meanings are hard to know, advises moral self-correction rather than shallow omen certainty, and thereby eases Chengdi's suspicion of the Wang clan while making Wang Gen ashamed of his resentment.

The durable synthesis is that Zhang Yu defuses rivalry by protecting the rival at the rival's moment of exposure. The episode then turns the case into a workplace-jealousy lesson: jealousy grows where another person's advantage touches one's prized self-image, can be exploited by managers or political actors, and can be converted into healthier competition only when individuals and leaders recognize the comparison pressure before it becomes inner depletion or factional conflict.

## Key Claims

- Chengdi's decision to give Zhang Yu the disputed land works as an open political signal, not merely a property ruling, because it tells Wang Gen that Zhang Yu may outrank him in imperial favor.
- Zhang Yu's position is structurally dangerous: Chengdi wants him as a counterweight to Wang-family outer relatives, while Wang Gen now has reason to treat him as a threat.
- Earthquake and eclipse interpretation becomes a court-political weapon when some officials claim the disasters arise from outer-relative domination.
- Zhang Yu avoids a direct attack on Wang Gen by questioning simplistic disaster interpretation and advising Chengdi to answer Heaven with upright conduct.
- The move protects Zhang Yu because it removes the pretext for Wang-family retaliation and turns Wang Gen's resentment into embarrassment.
- The episode frames jealousy as comparison in a valued domain: the feeling is sharpest when someone else exceeds the person in what the person already treats as identity or status.
- Managers and political operators can either inflame jealousy into usable factional energy or turn it toward positive competition and self-awareness.
