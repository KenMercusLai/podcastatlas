# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》541丨他是民族英雄，最后为何因贪入狱？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the Changling punishment sequence between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-540-taihou-de-zangli-lnd6gmr_bk7dv8cbb1zzoagtbwsd|Hanji 540]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-542-zhichang-xinlixue-shengzhi-hou-weihe-geng-rongyi-zao-paiji-lua_jsiusjojpwlwlroehw7jebgm|Hanji 542]]. It centers [[ChenTangWesternHan|陈汤]] being impeached by [[WangShangWesternHan|王商]], spared because of earlier merit, but reduced to commoner status and moved to the frontier.

The durable synthesis is that Changling punishment cannot be read from the formal charge alone. Chen Tang's Guandong elite-relocation proposal served a strong-trunk weak-branch purpose, but it threatened powerful households and their court ties; his reputation for greed made him easier to attack, while [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]'s later reward for [[ChunyuZhangWesternHan|淳于长]] and demotion of [[PingDangWesternHan|平当]] show public reasons and private motives diverging in the same policy field.

## Key Claims

- Wang Shang impeaches Chen Tang for false reporting and disrespectful language toward Chengdi, with court officials adding support for punishment.
- Chengdi remembers Chen Tang's old military merit and spares his life, but demotes him to commoner and sends him to the frontier.
- Chen Tang's Changling work included a proposal to move powerful Guandong households to the mausoleum settlement, read by the host as a revival of early-Han elite relocation and a [[StrongTrunkWeakBranchCentralization|strong-trunk weak-branch]] device.
- The policy threatened Guandong powerful households and their court patrons, creating an interest coalition that wanted both Chen Tang removed and the Changling relocation pressed down.
- The source does not prove a specific corruption amount; it says Chen Tang's known greed and loose self-discipline made corruption suspicion politically usable.
- Chengdi publicly rewards Wang Hong and Chunyu Zhang for proposing to halt Changling palace works, but the host reads Chunyu's reward as repayment for helping [[ZhaoFeiyanWesternHan|赵飞燕]] become empress.
- Ping Dang is demoted after objecting that Chunyu Zhang's proposal does not legally justify enfeoffment, showing the cost of missing the ruler's real reward intention.
