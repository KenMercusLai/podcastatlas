# No.223 当单纯的投放逻辑已经失效，我们如何重新理解种草？

## Summary
This [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] episode has [[LiuFei|刘飞]] interviewing [[LiuXiaoweiMarketing|刘晓微]] about [[Kuaishou]] marketing science, content seeding, brand advertising, performance conversion, and ecommerce linkage. The episode argues that [[ContentSeedingMarketing|内容种草]] should not be reduced to same-session purchase: search, store visits, livestream viewing, comparison, delayed discounts, repeat purchase, and offline transactions can all sit inside the conversion path.

The durable contribution is a practical account of [[BrandPerformanceIntegration|品效合一]] as path construction rather than only attribution proof. [[Kuaishou]]'s R3 seeded-audience frame, search/store/live-room signals, short-video-to-live linkage, and brand-ecommerce traffic weighting all support [[MarketingScienceUserPath|marketing science user-path reconstruction]], while the source keeps causal proof, cross-platform visibility, and platform-reported conversion figures source-scoped.

## Key Claims
- [[ContentSeedingMarketing|内容种草]] includes both immediate impulse purchase and slower mental availability that may convert after search, comparison, livestream explanation, store browsing, discounts, or offline buying.
- [[Kuaishou]]'s R3 seeded-audience category is defined around deeper behavior after content exposure, such as search, livestream visits, and store exploration; the source says R3 may be below 10% of exposed users but can represent 40%-50% of new buyers in some analyses.
- [[BrandPerformanceIntegration|品效合一]] is possible only if platforms and brands build bridges between brand exposure and ecommerce conversion, not if they merely ask whether a branding impression can be proven causal for every buyer.
- Short-term ROI optimization can "pick off" high-intent users but fail to expand demand; seeding is framed as a way to create healthier new demand, repeat purchase, and brand loyalty.
- Rational consumers may need a longer content path rather than a shorter one: content sparks interest, search clarifies terms, livestreams explain details, and stores or product pages reduce purchase concerns.
- Search, store browsing, and non-purchase after entering a shop should not automatically be treated as negative signals; they can indicate interest and later conversion probability.
- [[CreatorTrustAdvertising|creator trust advertising]] and livestream explanation matter because product detail, real use, community sharing, and local supply stories can make goods legible beyond low-price discounting.
- AI can help infer complex user paths from profile and recent behavior, but the source frames this as support for discovering small signals and path breaks, not as proof that every marketing outcome is fully knowable.
- Kuaishou is positioned as a full-domain seeding surface: the episode accepts that users may be seeded on Kuaishou and buy through Tmall, JD.com, offline stores, or other channels.

## Key Quotes
> "不要试图教育用户" - 刘晓微's warning that platforms and brands should restore user paths instead of forcing users into planned funnels.

> "看搜点购" - the episode's compact path formula: watch, search, click/visit when there is a good landing surface, then buy.

> "不要只做证明有效的事情" - the shift from attribution proof toward building the link between brand traffic and ecommerce traffic.
