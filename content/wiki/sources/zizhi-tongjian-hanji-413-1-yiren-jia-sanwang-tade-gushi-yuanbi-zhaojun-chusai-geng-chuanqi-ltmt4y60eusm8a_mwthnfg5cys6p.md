# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》413-1｜一人嫁三王，她的故事远比“昭君出塞”更传奇

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the early [[XieyouPrincessWesternHan|解忧公主]] branch before the later Wusun succession crisis. After Liu Xijun's death, Han Wudi sends Liu Jieyou into [[Wusun|乌孙]], where her marriage to Wengguimi strengthens Han-Wusun alignment against [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] and makes her an important communication channel with Chang'an.

The durable synthesis is that women in heqin networks can be more than symbolic brides. Xieyou's letter helps trigger [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]]'s later rescue response and the Han-Wusun strike coordinated by [[ChangHuiWesternHan|常惠]], while [[FengLiaoWesternHan|冯嫽]] appears as the literate, locally connected partner who will later become an independent diplomatic actor.

## Key Claims

- After Liu Xijun dies, Han Wudi chooses Liu Jieyou from the imperial clan and sends her to Wusun to maintain the alliance.
- Xieyou marries Wengguimi, later known as Fei Wang, and the episode says they have three sons and two daughters.
- Wengguimi's affection for Xieyou strengthens Han-Wusun closeness and sidelines a Xiongnu princess in the Wusun court.
- The neglected Xiongnu princess complains to Xiongnu, helping turn palace rivalry into frontier pressure against Wusun.
- When Xiongnu and Cheshi attack Wusun near the end of Han Zhaodi's reign, Wengguimi asks Xieyou to write to Han for aid.
- Xuan eventually responds by sending Chang Hui to Wusun and launching a five-route anti-Xiongnu campaign in 72 BCE.
- Wusun's interception inflicts heavy losses on Xiongnu and helps produce a longer northern-frontier quiet period.
- Feng Liao enters as Xieyou's attendant, learns local languages and affairs, marries the Wusun right general, and gains the reputation that later missions depend on.
- Xieyou later petitions to return to Han in old age, and Xuan brings her back to Chang'an with material honors.

## Key Quotes

> "为国家稳定牺牲一生的女人" - the host's frame for Xieyou's life in Wusun.

> "一内一外" - the episode's implied division of influence between Xieyou in the Wusun royal household and Feng Liao among ministers and regional actors.
