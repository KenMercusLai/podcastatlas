# 《资治通鉴·周纪》63丨 德高望重孟尝君是伪君子？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s seventeenth year, 298 BCE, by following [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]] after he enters [[QinState|秦国]] as chancellor. [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] comes to suspect his [[QiState|齐国]] loyalties, replaces him with [[LouHuan|楼缓]], imprisons him, and nearly kills him before Mengchangjun escapes through the famous chicken-crow and dog-theft retainer story. The episode then uses [[WangAnshi|王安石]]'s critique to argue that Mengchangjun's reputation for养士 may reveal poor talent discrimination rather than true statecraft talent, before closing with [[ChuState|楚国]] installing a new king and Qin attacking Chu through [[Wuguan|武关]].

## Key Claims
- In 298 BCE, someone warns [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] that [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]] remains a Qi aristocrat even while serving as Qin chancellor, so major decisions may favor [[QiState|齐国]] over [[QinState|秦国]].
- Qin Zhaoxiang Wang accepts the warning, removes Mengchangjun from the chancellorship, appoints [[LouHuan|楼缓]], imprisons Mengchangjun, and prepares to execute him.
- Mengchangjun asks Qin Zhaoxiang Wang's favorite woman for help. She wants his white fox-fur robe, but Mengchangjun has already presented the robe to the Qin king.
- A retainer skilled at theft enters the palace storehouse, steals back the robe, and gives it to the favorite. Her intercession persuades Qin Zhaoxiang Wang to release Mengchangjun.
- Qin Zhaoxiang Wang regrets the release and sends pursuers. Mengchangjun reaches the pass before it opens; a retainer skilled at imitating roosters causes real roosters to crow, letting the party pass through before Qin's pursuers arrive.
- The episode treats the "鸡鸣狗盗" story as both a survival anecdote and a personnel-judgment problem: useful tricks can save a patron without proving that the patron has gathered statecraft-level talent.
- [[WangAnshi|王安石]] criticizes the traditional praise of Mengchangjun's ability to attract士, arguing that a true top-level talent would have changed the Qin situation rather than leaving Mengchangjun dependent on petty skills.
- The host endorses Wang Anshi's critique and argues that Qin Zhaoxiang Wang's admiration could have cooled after seeing Mengchangjun honor chicken-crow and dog-theft retainers as guests.
- The episode's broader leadership lesson is that养士 is not the same as识才: large spending and many guests can produce reputation while failing to distinguish talent rank and moral-political quality.
- Chu sends envoys to Qin announcing that [[MiHeng|芈横 / 楚顷襄王]] has already been made king, which undercuts Qin's attempt to use the detained [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] as leverage.
- Qin responds angrily by attacking Chu through [[Wuguan|武关]], killing fifty thousand troops and taking sixteen cities.
- The episode ends by naming the four famous Warring States retainer patrons: [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]] of Qi, [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]], [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]], and [[Chunshenjun|春申君]], then prepares for Pingyuanjun's next appearance.

## Key Quotes
> "鸡鸣狗盗之徒" - Wang Anshi's critical label for Mengchangjun's retainer circle as presented in the episode.

> "养士不等于识才" - the episode's summary distinction between broad patronage and true talent judgment.
