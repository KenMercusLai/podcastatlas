# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》299-2｜西汉财政大危机，汉武帝如何搞钱？

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode joins the aftermath of [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]]'s anti-[[Xiongnu|匈奴]] campaign to its strategic and fiscal consequences. [[ZhaoXinWesternHan|赵信]] returns to the Xiongnu and recommends moving north of the desert so Han armies must advance deeply and become exhausted, while [[SuJianWesternHan|苏建]] escapes execution through redemption payment, becomes a commoner, and later returns as governor of Dai Commandery.

The central institutional addition is a specific Western Han layer of [[RankSaleDisasterFinance|鬻爵赈灾融资]]. The source says repeated war, rewards, casualties, horses, equipment, clothing, and grain transport exhausted the Grand Agricultural Office, after which [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] allowed rank purchase and penal redemption and created purchasable 武功爵. It reports a lowest price of 170,000 cash and priority for official appointment at the 千夫 level, but the exact grade structure, legal scope, revenue, and appointment effect remain source-scoped.

## Key Claims

- Zhao Xin, described as a former Xiongnu lesser king who had entered Han service, returns to the Xiongnu, receives the title 自次王, marries the chanyu's elder sister, and becomes a strategically valuable adviser because he understands both sides.
- Zhao Xin recommends moving north of the desert, avoiding the Han frontier, drawing Han armies deep into difficult terrain, and attacking after fatigue.
- The episode still treats Han as militarily dominant while Wei Qing and [[HuoQubing|霍去病]] remain active; northward withdrawal is therefore presented as an asymmetric response rather than proof of Xiongnu superiority.
- Su Jian is returned to the capital after defeat, pardoned from death after paying redemption, reduced to commoner status, and later appointed governor of Dai Commandery, where the episode says he dies in office.
- Campaign rewards, soldiers and horses lost, weapons, armor, clothing, and grain transport are combined into one account of war-driven treasury exhaustion.
- The court responds by allowing people to buy rank and offenders to redeem punishments with money, including in the episode's account some theft and corruption offenses.
- Purchasable 武功爵 begins at a reported 170,000 cash, increases by grade, and gives priority for official appointment at the 千夫 level, making emergency finance also a personnel-order risk.

## Key Quotes

> “大司农府库枯竭，已经无法继续供应军需。” - the episode's fiscal turning point.

> “购买武功爵达到‘千夫’的人，可以优先被任命为官吏。” - the claimed bridge from purchased rank to appointment access.
