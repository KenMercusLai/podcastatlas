# MiniMax 创始人闫俊杰×罗永浩！大山并非无法翻越

## Summary

This [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] interview follows [[YanJunjie|严俊杰]] from county-town self-study and mathematics through academic AI, Baidu, SenseTime, and founding [[MiniMax]]. Its central synthesis joins [[MultimodalAGIStrategy]], [[ModelAsProduct]], and [[AINativeRoleConvergence]]: multiple modalities are treated as a route toward AGI, model capability becomes the product core, and fast-changing capability alters team boundaries, internationalization, commercialization, and the meaning of productive work.

## Key Claims

- Yan defines AGI through multimodal input and multimodal output; MiniMax initially developed language, speech, video, and music separately while expecting later integration.
- He argues that scaling remains central across modalities, but compute scarcity forces Chinese teams to improve algorithms, data chains, training efficiency, and infrastructure rather than simply copy U.S. paths.
- MiniMax's founding principles were to serve users directly rather than deliver projects, operate internationally, and remain foundation-driven.
- The model itself is framed as the main product, while applications provide access, feedback, distribution, and monetization; cost and task tradeoffs make a single universal winner unlikely.
- Yan's biography connects early self-study, mathematics, the Chinese Academy of Sciences, Baidu GPU access, SenseTime leadership, OpenAI's game work, and GPT-3 to the decision to found MiniMax before ChatGPT's breakout.
- The DeepSeek breakout triggered internal review: Yan says MiniMax had sometimes lowered standards or made insufficiently firm choices, leading to tighter algorithm-infrastructure integration.
- MiniMax's talent criteria combine intelligence and insight, domain passion, and collaboration; Yan argues that AI-native organizations need complementary profiles rather than one employee template.
- Product, engineering, algorithm, design, sales, operations, and HR boundaries are becoming more permeable as AI lowers prototyping and analysis costs.
- Talkie is described as stronger by usage, while video and API are stronger by revenue; overseas payment is treated as an important commercialization test despite harder competition and a small international team.
- Token demand may grow faster than unit cost falls, but repeatedly consumed generated content can spread production cost across many viewers.
- The source reports large MiniMax video and synthesized-audio volumes and broad audiobook usage, but these figures remain unverified company claims.
- Yan treats open and closed release as tactical choices, not identities; language models had been opened while video and audio remained closed at the time described.
- AI safety issues include voice-cloning controls, all-day recording by AI glasses, model interpretation, human control, regulation, and the eventual distribution of AI-created economic value.
- He expects definable and measurable tasks to approach or exceed the strongest human level, while acknowledging that subjective writing remained below the best humans in the source's late-2025 frame.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. The source's broad multimodal strategy creates a productive tension with later wiki sources that praise concentrated coding and high-value-task focus; the current synthesis treats the later emphasis as execution concentration rather than abandonment of the multimodal thesis.
- The claim that large U.S. resource and valuation gaps translate into only small technical gaps is a founder assessment, not an independently established comparison.
- Predictions about near-term human control, five-to-ten-year safety, non-monopoly, GDP contribution, and top-human task performance remain forecasts rather than settled findings.
