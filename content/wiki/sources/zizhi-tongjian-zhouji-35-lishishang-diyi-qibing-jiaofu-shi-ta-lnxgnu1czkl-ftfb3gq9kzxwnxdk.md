# 《资治通鉴·周纪》35丨历史上第一骑兵教父是他？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]]'s forty-second and forty-third years through three connected problems: [[QinState|秦国]]'s partial victory over [[YiquState|义渠]], Qin's return of [[Jiaoyi|焦邑]] and [[QuwoWarringStates|曲沃]] to [[WeiState|魏国]], and [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]]'s accession after [[ZhaoSuhou|赵肃侯]] dies. The core reading is that apparent softness can be strategic: Qin manages a rear frontier while keeping its eastern ambition alive, [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] uses return-land diplomacy to pull and pressure Wei, and Zhao Wuling Wang's respectful new-ruler gestures may be pre-reform positioning rather than simple conservatism.

## Key Claims
- In Zhou Xian Wang's forty-second year, Qin defeats [[YiquState|义渠]], occupies part of its territory, and incorporates that captured area into Qin's county system.
- Yiqu's ruler submits to Qin, but the episode stresses that this is not Yiqu's final destruction; the Qin-Yiqu relationship remains a long-running frontier problem.
- Qin's strategic center is still the eastern struggle over Wei and Hexi, so its treatment of Yiqu is read through [[QinFrontierBufferManagement|frontier buffer management]] rather than immediate total conquest.
- Qin's return of [[Jiaoyi|焦邑]] and [[QuwoWarringStates|曲沃]] to Wei answers the puzzle left by [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-34-qinguo-zongli-ruhe-wanzhuan-liuguo-lsenzwp0kr5wscb0btvuzmkibriq|Zhouji 34]]: the host reads it as a [[ZhangYiStrategist|Zhang Yi]] policy of half-pulling and half-hitting Wei.
- The return-land move is linked to [[YingSi|秦惠文王]]'s broader diplomatic environment before claiming kingly status, not to Qin suddenly becoming generous in a simple moral sense.
- The episode contrasts [[ShangYang|商鞅]] and Zhang Yi: Shang Yang wins with institutional force and direct extraction, while Zhang Yi wins through flexibility, psychology, and portable diplomatic skill.
- In 326 BCE, [[ZhaoSuhou|赵肃侯]] dies; the episode treats the large funeral attendance by other major states, except Han, as evidence that Zhao remains politically significant.
- [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]] succeeds and immediately creates three broad-learning advisers and left/right fault-correction posts.
- Zhao Wuling Wang also visits [[FeiYi|肥义]], a senior minister from Zhao Suhou's reign, and increases his salary.
- These early actions become [[NewRulerReformSignaling|new-ruler reform signaling]]: they reassure old ministers and advertise humility, but may also prepare political room for the later disruptive reform implied by the episode title.

## Key Quotes
> "半拉半打" - the episode's phrase for Qin's mix of concession and pressure toward Wei.

> "一力降实惠" - the contrast drawn for Shang Yang's more force-centered style.

> "一巧破千斤" - the contrast drawn for Zhang Yi's lighter, more flexible diplomatic method.
