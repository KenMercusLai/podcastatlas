# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》374-1｜汉武帝为何宁愿自损八千，也不愿放过大宛国？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the strategic and frontier prelude to [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-374-2-hanwudi-qingjin-quanli-da-dayuan-zhi-bu-zhi-lmfzkllejz3m0vanmnu7gyxtwbul|Hanji 374-2]]. In 102 BCE it places [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s renewed campaign against [[DayuanWesternHan|大宛]] beside a new Xiongnu chanyu, Han fortification from Wuyuan toward Luqu, garrisons around Juyan, and autumn [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] attacks on Dingxiang, Yunzhong, Jiuquan, and Zhangye.

The central decision follows [[ZhaoPonuWesternHan|赵破奴]]'s capture. Court ministers argue that Han should abandon Dayuan and concentrate on Xiongnu, while Wudi refuses to let an already-declared expedition fail against a smaller polity. The host interprets the war as more than a search for horses: Dayuan's noncooperation obstructs Han efforts to align Western Regions states and pressure Xiongnu from the flank. This strategic and prestige rationale remains source-scoped and does not settle whether the campaign's human and fiscal costs were proportionate.

## Key Claims

- After the death of the child chanyu, Xiongnu elites elevate his uncle, the Right Wise King Houlihu, prompting Han to reinforce the northern and northwestern frontier.
- Xu Ziwei builds walls, forts, and beacon installations from Wuyuan toward Luqu; Wei Kang is stationed nearby, and [[LuBodeWesternHan|路博德]] fortifies and guards Juyan.
- Xiongnu forces raid Dingxiang and Yunzhong, kill several thousand people, defeat senior Han officials, and damage the new defensive works.
- A second Xiongnu force raids Jiuquan and Zhangye and carries off several thousand people before Ren Wen defeats it and recovers the captives.
- The episode uses the stripping of Zhang Chang's marquisate and a retrospective count of surviving meritorious marquis houses to illustrate stricter law and the attrition of inherited noble privilege under Han Wudi.
- After Zhao Ponu is captured, ministers argue for ending the Dayuan expedition so Han can concentrate resources against Xiongnu.
- Wudi instead treats withdrawal as a loss of imperial credibility after Han has publicly committed to defeating a smaller state.
- The host argues that Dayuan mattered because Han sought Western Regions partners able to constrain Xiongnu from another direction, not only because Wudi wanted superior horses.

## Key Quotes

> "大汉帝国既然已经出兵征讨大宛" - the episode's starting point for the credibility argument against withdrawal.

> "从侧翼威胁匈奴" - the host's strategic explanation for seeking Western Regions cooperation.
