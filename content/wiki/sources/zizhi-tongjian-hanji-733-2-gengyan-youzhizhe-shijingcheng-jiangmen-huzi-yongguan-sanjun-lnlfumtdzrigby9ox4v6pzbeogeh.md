# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》733-2丨耿弇：有志者事竟成，将门虎子勇冠三军

## Summary
This episode profiles [[GengYanEasternHan|耿弇 / Geng Yan]] as an Eastern Han founding general whose family military resources become historically decisive only after correct political alignment, tactical competence, and trust management. It follows his choice to join [[LiuXiu|刘秀 / 光武帝]], the securing of Youzhou cavalry, resistance to [[PengChongEasternHan|彭宠]], campaigns around Qinghe, Qi, Linzi, and Zhang Bu, and his later return of high command after merit was complete. The episode also frames Geng Yan as a self-cultivation example through Liu Xiu's praise "有志者事竟成" and the host's lesson that background must be turned into capability.

## Key Claims
- Geng Yan is presented as a fourth-ranked Yuntai Twenty-Eight General, praised by Liu Xiu with "有志者事竟成" and credited by the episode with pacifying 46 commanderies and capturing more than 300 cities.
- Geng Yan's aristocratic and military upbringing matters, but the episode treats family advantage as raw material rather than achievement by itself.
- Geng Yan chooses Liu Xiu after the [[WangLangHebeiClaimant|王郎]] crisis interrupts his attempt to reach the Gengshi regime, and he helps Liu Xiu secure Youzhou cavalry through his family network.
- His suspicion of Gengshi appointees and his father's resistance to Peng Chong mark the Geng family as politically useful because it can distinguish unstable claimants from durable authority.
- The Peng Chong rebellion makes personal ties politically dangerous, so Geng Yan and his family reduce Liu Xiu's possible suspicion by returning to court and sending [[GengGuoEasternHan|耿国]] to serve in Luoyang.
- The Qinghe and Qi campaigns emphasize [[BattlefieldFeintAndLure|feint-and-lure tactics]]: preserving an exit in encirclement, announcing one target while striking another, feigning retreat, and ambushing a retreat path.
- After later service in Longyou and the Weichun campaign, Geng Yan's return of the great-general seal is framed as [[PowerCompletionRetreat|post-merit retreat]] rather than a failure of ambition.

## Key Quotes
> "有志者事竟成" - Liu Xiu's praise as framed by the episode.

> "大将军不应让陛下来陪自己征战" - Geng Yan's reported reason for not waiting passively for imperial arrival.

> "功成之后懂得退让" - the host's concluding judgment on Geng Yan's late-career stance.
