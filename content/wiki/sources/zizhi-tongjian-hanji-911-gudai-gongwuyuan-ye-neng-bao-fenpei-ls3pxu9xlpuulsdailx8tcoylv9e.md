# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》911丨古代公务员也能包分配？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 177-178 CE transition immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-912-mimi-daodi-gai-bugai-shuo-chukou-lhsgipxwc17pahc-r6jl7xdyrs2a|Hanji 912]]. It treats [[HongduMenxue|鸿都门学]] as an appointment and power-allocation problem: a court-sponsored literary/arts channel gives favored students faster office access than [[Taixue|太学]], provoking resentment from Taixue students and士大夫. The episode also adds [[ShiYiguanLateHan|师宜官]], [[LiangHuLateHan|梁鹄]], [[ZhangHaoTaiweiLateHan|张颢]], [[LaiYanLateHan|来艳]], and the omen-and-office prelude that leads into the next secret-remonstrance episode.

## Key Claims
- The episode describes Hongdu Menxue as teaching literary and artistic skills such as fu writing, fiction-like narrative, letters, calligraphy, and painting rather than the Taixue's classical curriculum.
- Hongdu students are presented as recommended by the Three Excellencies, provinces, and commanderies, then favored by eunuch-linked channels for rapid office access after graduation.
- Taixue students and士大夫 oppose Hongdu because office quotas are finite; curriculum conflict becomes [[OfficialSelectionChannelCompetition|选官通道竞争]].
- The host reads Chinese official-selection history as a series of power reallocations, from hereditary office through Han recommendation, Nine-Rank selection, and later imperial examinations.
- The source-scoped interpretation is that [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] uses Hongdu to dilute elite士人 and great-clan control, making Hongdu a political counterweight rather than a mere arts school.
- The episode argues that Hongdu's brief life and political logic complicate flat judgments of Emperor Ling as simply foolish, while the posthumous-name critique remains interpretive rather than settled.
- [[ShiYiguanLateHan|师宜官]] and [[LiangHuLateHan|梁鹄]] show that Hongdu's condemned channel could still gather real artistic talent.
- [[ZhangHaoTaiweiLateHan|张颢]]'s promotion to taiwei is used as an example of eunuch-linked appointment advantage; the podcast renders the name as 张昊, while the chronicle spelling is normalized here as 张颢.
- The gold-seal story around Zhang Hao is treated skeptically as [[AuspiciousOmenPolitics|祥瑞政治]] tied to career ambition rather than as proof of Heaven's favor.
- Earthquake, a hen turning male, [[ChenDanLateHan|陈耽]]'s removal, and [[LaiYanLateHan|来艳]]'s appointment create the omen-and-office setting that [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-912-mimi-daodi-gai-bugai-shuo-chukou-lhsgipxwc17pahc-r6jl7xdyrs2a|Hanji 912]] continues.
