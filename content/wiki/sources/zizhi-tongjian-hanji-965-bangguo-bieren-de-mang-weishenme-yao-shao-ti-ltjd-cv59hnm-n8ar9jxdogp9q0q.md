# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》965丨帮过别人的忙，为什么要少提？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate pre-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-966-meiyou-yexin-budong-quanmou-jiu-bie-dang-lingdao-lldn3hi1gl5v3axvklwakgf6vx3u|Hanji 966]] decision field after [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s death. It begins with [[DiscreetBenefactionDignity|沉默施恩的尊严保护]], arguing that publicized help can turn kindness into shame, pressure, or hidden hierarchy, then turns to [[WangYunLateHan|王允]]'s mishandling of [[LiangzhouWarlordClique|凉州系军阀]] fear. The late-Han core is that canceling amnesty, refusing a [[HuangfuSongLateHan|皇甫嵩]] reassurance plan, and leaving remnant commanders no credible safety route made [[JiaXu|贾诩]]'s attack-Chang'an advice persuasive to [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]].

## Key Claims

- The opening uses [[Daodejing|《道德经》]] and later moral examples to argue that help can lose its ethical force when the giver repeatedly displays it.
- Public benefaction can force recipients to relive dependency, making gratitude feel like a dignity debt rather than a free response.
- The source's practical ethic is [[DiscreetBenefactionDignity|沉默施恩的尊严保护]]: effective help should preserve the recipient's self-respect and not require public repayment.
- After Dong Zhuo's death, [[WangYunLateHan|王允]] first considers pardoning Dong Zhuo's Liangzhou soldiers, then cancels that amnesty because he worries that formally pardoning them would imply guilt and unsettle opinion.
- Wang Yun rejects a proposal to let [[HuangfuSongLateHan|皇甫嵩]] command or settle Dong Zhuo's old troops near Shan County, missing an available reassurance and security arrangement.
- Rumors that Liangzhou people would be killed, plus examples such as Cai Yong's execution, make Dong Zhuo's old generals treat disbanding as loss of self-protection.
- [[LyuBu|吕布]] sends [[LiSuLateHan|李肃]] against [[NiuFuLateHan|牛辅]]; Niu Fu defeats Li Su, Lü Bu kills Li Su, and Niu Fu still panics during a camp night-fright event and is killed while fleeing.
- The camp-panic discussion becomes [[MilitaryCampPanic|军营夜惊群体恐慌]]: morale, fatigue, superstition, environmental stress, rumor, and camp design can determine whether soldiers remain controllable.
- When Li Jue requests pardon after Niu Fu's death, Wang Yun refuses by citing the rule against two amnesties in one year, reinforcing Li Jue's belief that flight or armed action are the only options.
- Jia Xu's advice to attack [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] is presented as survival-first reasoning for desperate commanders, but it also extends [[ExecutorCharacterAdviceRisk]] because success empowers violent remnant commanders.
- The closing reading of Wang Yun treats arrogance after success as a diagnostic condition: power, money, or fame reveals whether a person has durable humility and situation judgment.

## Key Quotes

> "帮过人忙，千万不要说出去." - opening lesson about not turning help into public pressure.

> "君子泰而不骄，小人骄而不泰." - closing humility frame applied to Wang Yun.
