# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》801丨汉安帝为什么狠心废太子？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills 124 CE immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-802-andi-zhisi-donghan-wangchao-quanli-da-xipai-lgvne92e0jjzjnznzfkz7nvztnbj|Hanji 802]]. It moves from [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] succession, [[Xianbei|鲜卑]] pressure, frontier mobilization fatigue, and disaster notices into [[EmperorAnOfHan|汉安帝]]'s decision to depose the ten-year-old crown prince [[EmperorShunOfHan|刘保]] as Jiyin Wang.

The durable synthesis is that the Shun succession crisis begins before Emperor An's death. Palace slander and empress/eunuch interests injure the heir, but the decisive escalation comes when broad ministerial support for Liu Bao is recoded as a ruler-security threat; [[LaiLiLateHan|来历]]'s persistence at Hongdu Gate then shows corrective remonstrance becoming punishable once the emperor treats it as hidden disloyalty.

## Key Claims

- The 124 CE background combines frontier stress, newly attached groups' resentment after repeated mobilization, Xianbei raids, landslide, solar eclipse, earthquake, flood, hail, and [[LongxiCommandery|陇西郡]] administration returning to Didao.
- Wang Sheng, [[FanFengEunuchLateHan|樊丰]], and others first attack Liu Bao's wet nurse Wang Nan and kitchen supervisor Bing Ji, after which Wang Nan and related people are killed and households are exiled.
- Fan Feng fears later retaliation after Liu Bao mourns the loss of Wang Nan and Bing Ji, so he works with [[EmpressDowagerYanLateHan|阎皇后]] to fabricate evidence against the crown prince and his palace officers.
- Emperor An calls senior ministers to discuss deposing Liu Bao; [[GengBaoGrandGeneralLateHan|耿宝]] and others follow the ruler's intent.
- [[LaiLiLateHan|来历]], [[HuanYanLateHan|桓焉]], and [[ZhangHaoRemonstratorLateHan|张皓]] object that a child under fifteen should not bear full legal guilt and that Wang Nan/Bing Ji's plots were not known to Liu Bao.
- Zhang Hao's follow-up memorial invokes [[HanWudi|汉武帝]], Jiang Chong, and the Li Crown Prince disaster as a precedent warning against repeating a father-son political miscarriage.
- On 124-09-07, Emperor An formally deposes Liu Bao from crown prince to Jiyin Wang and houses him near Deyang Hall's west middle tower.
- Lai Li, Tuo Feng, Liu Wei, Xue Hao, Lvqiu Hong, Chen Guang, Zhao Dai, [[ShiYanLateHan|施延]], [[ZhuChangLateHan|朱伥]], and other officials remonstrate at Hongdu Gate that Liu Bao is blameless.
- The episode's interpretive center is [[HeirSupportThreatPerception|储君拥戴威胁感]]: Emperor An starts reading support for a ten-year-old heir as a threat to his own political safety.
- Emperor An's Zhongchangshi edict threatens the remonstrants, accuses them of hidden motives, and makes persistence punishable; Lai Li continues alone and is dismissed along with his brothers, loses fief revenue, and sees his mother Wu'an Princess degraded and barred from palace remonstrance.

## Key Quotes

> "年纪不到十五岁" - age-responsibility principle cited against blaming Liu Bao.

> "太子年仅十岁" - Zhang Hao's warning against harsh judgment.

> "大义灭亲" - edict language justifying the father-son cut.

> "内心却另有私念" - edict language recoding remonstrance as hidden motive.
