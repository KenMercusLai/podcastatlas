# 《资治通鉴·周纪》94丨杀神白起，长平之战中的“草包”名将？（3）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode closes the [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] branch continued from [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-2-lvqctrazn8ddkcnp-lhspzzmzmns|Zhouji 94 part 2]]. After forty-six days of starvation, [[ZhaoKuo|赵括]] divides the trapped [[ZhaoState|赵国]] army for repeated breakout attempts, is killed by Qin arrows during his own assault, and the exhausted Zhao force surrenders to [[BaiQi|白起]].

The episode then turns the surrender into a moral and strategic problem. Bai Qi judges the surrendered troops and Shangdang-linked population as unsafe captives, uses deception to settle them, kills the prisoners, and sends only 240 young soldiers back to Zhao; the host frames this through [[SurrenderedPrisonerSecurityDilemma|杀降安全困境]], [[WarringStatesWarBrutalization|战国战争残酷化]], and [[WarCostTempoDiscipline|战争成本与速决纪律]]. The final analysis returns responsibility to [[FengTing|冯亭]]'s Shangdang transfer, [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]]'s greed for the windfall, Zhao Kuo's paper-war command, and [[LianPo|廉颇]]'s abandoned defensive patience.

## Key Claims
- Zhao Kuo organizes four breakout groups and repeatedly attacks Qin barriers, but the Qin defenses remain firm.
- Zhao Kuo personally leads the fiercest breakout attempt; Qin avoids close combat and uses strong crossbows, killing him with arrows.
- Once the commander dies, the exhausted Zhao army collapses and surrenders to Bai Qi.
- Bai Qi argues that Shangdang had already entered Qin's grasp, that the Shangdang population had gone over to Zhao, and that Zhao soldiers were brave enough to remain dangerous as captives.
- Bai Qi uses deception to reassure the surrendered troops, then kills them; the episode says only 240 young soldiers are spared to report the disaster back to Zhao.
- The source reports Qin's total killing at Changping as 450,000 and treats Zhao's main force as destroyed, while also noting that even a lower exact figure would still imply unprecedented damage to Zhao.
- The host argues that Qin's victory was not cheap: after Changping, [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] sends [[WangLing|王陵]] against [[Handan|邯郸]], but Bai Qi refuses to replace him partly because Qin is already exhausted and hungry.
- The episode invokes [[SunziBingfa|《孙子兵法》]] to stress that war is costly, should be won by nonmilitary means where possible, and should be ended quickly when fighting is unavoidable.
- The episode treats Qin's impulse to press Zhao immediately after Changping as hasty because population and manpower recovery still matter for later unification.
- The host identifies a responsibility chain: Feng Ting's unauthorized Shangdang transfer is the originating cause, Zhao Dan's desire for Shangdang is the ruler-level error, Zhao Kuo's paper-war command is the battlefield failure, and Lian Po's defensive judgment looks stronger in retrospect.

## Key Quotes
> "国虚民饥" - Bai Qi's reason for resisting another hard campaign after Changping.

> "能不打的仗就不要打" - the host's war-cost takeaway.

> "攻则不足，守则有余" - the episode's retrospective judgment on Lian Po's Changping posture.
