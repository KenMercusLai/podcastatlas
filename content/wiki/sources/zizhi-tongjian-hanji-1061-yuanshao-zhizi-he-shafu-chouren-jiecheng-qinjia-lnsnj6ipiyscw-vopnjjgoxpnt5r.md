## Summary
This episode continues the Jian'an 8-9 aftermath of Yuan Shao's death by showing [[YuanTan]] and [[YuanShang]] weakening each other while [[CaoCao]] converts their rivalry into operational leverage around [[YeLateHan]]. It also shifts to [[SunQuan]], whose failed westward pressure on [[HuangZu]] is interrupted by [[Shanyue]] rebellion and then redirected into county-level pacification through commanders and [[HeQiLateHan]].

The source's interpretive frame is that small cleverness can become strategic self-damage: Yuan Tan's secret seal-lure spends credibility with Cao Cao, Yuan Shang keeps prioritizing fraternal war while Ye is exposed, and Xu Huang's surrender-reward advice shows the opposite pattern of reading campaign psychology beyond the immediate battlefield.

## Key Claims
- In Jian'an 8, tenth month, Cao Cao advances to Liyang in support of [[YuanTan]], and Yuan Shang withdraws from Pingyuan toward Ye after hearing that Cao's army has crossed the Yellow River.
- [[LyuKuangLateHan]] and [[GaoXiangLateHan]] defect from Yuan Shang to Cao Cao, but Yuan Tan secretly gives them general seals in an attempt to draw them toward himself.
- Cao Cao learns of Yuan Tan's seal maneuver and reads it as evidence that Yuan Tan is deceptive rather than reliably allied.
- Cao Cao does not immediately punish Yuan Tan; instead, he marries [[CaoZhengLateHan]] to Yuan Tan's daughter to stabilize Yuan Tan temporarily before withdrawing.
- Sun Quan attacks Huang Zu to the west and wins on the water, but he fails to take the city and must withdraw when Shanyue resistance breaks out behind him.
- Sun Quan responds to Shanyue pressure by assigning officers such as [[LuFan]], [[TaishiCi]], [[HuangGai]], [[HanDang]], and [[LyuMeng]] to vulnerable county roles while He Qi suppresses larger Jian'an, Hanxing, and Nanping rebellions.
- He Qi's campaign rebuilds local county seats, extracts more than ten thousand selected troops, and wins promotion, making pacification part of Sun Quan's authority-building.
- In Jian'an 9, Cao Cao crosses the Yellow River, redirects the Qi River into Baigou for grain transport, and surrounds Ye with siege works while Yuan Shang again attacks Yuan Tan at Pingyuan.
- [[SuYouLateHan]] plans to respond from inside Ye for Cao Cao, but the plot is exposed and he flees to Cao's camp.
- After [[HanFanLateHan]] and [[LiangQiLateHan]] surrender their county cities, [[XuHuangCaoGeneral]] advises rewarding them to show other watching counties that submission will be treated well.
