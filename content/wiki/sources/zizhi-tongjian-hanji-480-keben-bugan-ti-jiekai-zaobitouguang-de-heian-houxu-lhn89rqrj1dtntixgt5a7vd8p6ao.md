# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》480丨课本不敢提！揭开“凿壁偷光”的黑暗后续

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Yongguang 2 / 42 BCE under [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]], immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-481-wei-feng-xihan-fengfengshi-yiju-mie-diguo-libgqzvquegllma9soju57k_jxue|Hanji 481]]'s Longxi Qiang force-sizing debate. It starts from the famous "cutting the wall for light" story about [[KuangHengWesternHan|匡衡]], then restores the darker full career: Kuang Heng becomes a learned senior minister whose disaster memorial criticizes amnesty, court conduct, and capital customs, but later falls after excess fief land and family scandal.

The durable synthesis is that learning and office success do not settle character. Kuang Heng's memorial treats social disorder as rooted in elite example, court norms, and failed instruction rather than in punishable acts alone; his later demotion then turns the poor-scholar success story into a restraint warning about power, land, and self-interest.

## Key Claims

- In 42 BCE, Han Yuandi grants amnesty, appoints [[WeiXuanchengWesternHan|韦玄成]] as chancellor and [[ZhengHongWesternHan|郑弘]] as yushi dafu, and then faces eclipse, earthquake, and other disaster pressure.
- Kuang Heng answers Yuan's disaster inquiry by arguing that repeated amnesties cannot reduce crime if education, example, and court conduct remain bad.
- Kuang Heng says the court works like a mold for the realm: if senior officials fight, monopolize, seek reputation, and chase profit, commoners will imitate disorder.
- The memorial treats Chang'an as the hinge of instruction because visitors from commanderies and kingdoms learn from the capital's visible customs.
- Kuang Heng urges Yuan to fear Heaven's warning, reduce extravagance, review institutions, draw near loyal officials, and distance clever or treacherous officials.
- The episode contrasts Kuang Heng's real learning, poor origin, repeated examination struggle, and rise to chancellor with his later acceptance of excess fief land.
- Kuang Heng's son drunkenly kills a person and tries to escape custody; that case helps expose the older land issue, leading [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] to reduce Kuang Heng to commoner status.

## Key Quotes

> "有权不可任性" - the episode's closing moral frame for Kuang Heng's fall.

> "当官不为民作主，不如回家卖红薯" - the closing public-service maxim used to contrast office with private extraction.
