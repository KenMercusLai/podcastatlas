# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》833丨贪官能有多离谱？看完大跌眼镜！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 150 CE Liang-family privilege and private-extraction layer immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-834-lishi-gaosu-ni-xiaoren-weihe-chang-qiqi-lj8z2vfu6lpggumcakwejoi6z9hc|Hanji 834]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-835-donghan-diyi-quanchen-weihe-yao-gui-ta-lpwezt-z-2qloitwd3t4gyxznqbo|Hanji 835]]. It starts from Liang dowager's return of power and death, then follows [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] increasing honors for [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] and [[SunShouLateHan|孙寿]] while Liang's household turns wealth, private parks, household slaves, officials, retainers, and fabricated accusations into coercive power.

The durable contribution is the everyday extraction layer behind later Liang warnings and crime records. [[QinGongLateHan|秦功]] shows private household service becoming official and access power; [[SunFenFufengLateHan|孙奋]] shows forced lending, false enslavement accusation, prison torture, and asset seizure; the rabbit-park deaths and "自卖人" label show Liang's private pleasure and labor demands outranking ordinary lives.

## Key Claims

- In 150 CE, Liang dowager returns power to Emperor Huan and then dies; Huan increases Liang Ji's food-estate base and grants Sun Shou a high-status, revenue-bearing title.
- Sun Shou's income, insignia, and long-princess-like status show that Liang power extends through spouse and household channels, not only through Liang Ji's own title.
- Qin Gong, a favored household slave or steward, reaches the office of Taicang ling, enters Sun Shou's residence freely, and receives pre-office visits from provincial and commandery officials.
- Liang Ji and Sun Shou build luxurious mansions, gardens, ponds, hills, and entertainment spaces that convert urban and local resources into private display.
- Liang's rabbit park near Luoyang makes private property quasi-legal: marked rabbits become death-penalty objects, and a Western Region merchant's mistaken killing leads to more than ten deaths.
- Liang's villa and estate network shelters unlawful actors, while coerced seizure of thousands of good-family children is laundered through the label "自卖人".
- Sun-family relatives and party members use offices and local informants to identify rich households, fabricate charges, torture victims, and extract ransom-like payments.
- Sun Fen's case concentrates the logic: Liang gives a horse, demands a vast loan, retaliates when Sun Fen pays only part, kills Sun Fen and his brother in prison through a false charge against their mother, and seizes the household's wealth.
- Liang's retainers search widely for valuables while seizing women and attacking local officers, showing that his household network can export coercion beyond the capital.
