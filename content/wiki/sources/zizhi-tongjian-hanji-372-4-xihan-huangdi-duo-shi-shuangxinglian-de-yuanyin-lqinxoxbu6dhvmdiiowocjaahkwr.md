# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》372-4｜西汉皇帝多是双性恋的原因？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode moves from [[DengTong|邓通]] under [[LiuHeng|汉文帝刘恒]] to [[HanYanWesternHan|韩嫣]] and [[LiYannianWesternHan|李延年]] under [[HanWudi|汉武帝]], using male favorites to show how personal closeness could become office, wealth, daily access, and political exposure. Deng Tong's confiscation after [[HanJingdi|汉景帝]] succeeds, Han Yan's death after he offends [[LiuFeiJiangduKingWesternHan|江都王刘非]] and the empress dowager, and Li Yannian's rise through performance and [[LadyLiWesternHan|李夫人]] together extend [[CourtIntimacyAsPoliticalResource|court intimacy as a political resource]] and [[PalaceFavoriteProtectionFragility|palace favorite protection fragility]].

The title's claim about bisexual emperors is not treated as a verified diagnosis. The episode often turns terse transmitted phrases such as “与上卧起” into modern lover language, and its genetic explanation is unsupported in the supplied material. The final turn to online sexual “instant gratification” is likewise retained as a contemporary reflection rather than evidence explaining Western Han court relationships.

## Key Claims

- Deng Tong is presented as a low-ranking palace boatman whom Emperor Wen favors after linking him to a dream; imperial minting patronage makes him wealthy, but Jingdi later confiscates the wealth and the episode says Deng Tong dies in poverty.
- The source treats male-favorite relationships as recurrent in Western Han court history, but its move from favorite status or shared sleeping space to modern sexual identity remains interpretive.
- Han Yan's childhood proximity, royal descent, martial skill, office, rewards, and shared daily access with Han Wudi show intimate favor becoming material and political capital.
- Han Yan's use of imperial vehicles humiliates Liu Fei, whose complaint to the empress dowager precedes Han Yan's killing despite Wudi's attempt to protect him.
- Li Yannian rises from an entertainer family and palace service through music, dance, personal favor, and the additional favor shown to his sister Lady Li.
- The phrase “与上卧起” and the comparison with Han Yan support unusually close access, but the episode's claim that Li Yannian simply replaces Han Yan as Wudi's lover is not independently established here.
- The closing discussion argues that networked sexual gratification can reduce patience and invokes sexual impulse as a possible driver of civilization; the episode supplies no research detail sufficient to establish either generalization.

## Contradictions

- No settled factual contradiction was adopted. The Deng Tong outcome is consistent with the existing [[DengTong|邓通]] page, while this episode adds a stronger sexual interpretation than the earlier physiognomy-centered source.
- The episode's repeated use of “恋人,” “男朋友,” and modern identity language goes beyond what its quoted historical phrases alone establish; favorite status, co-sleeping, office, and reward do not by themselves prove a modern sexual orientation category.
- The “遗传基因” explanation is unsupported by evidence in the episode and is retained only as a host speculation.
- Han Yan's body type, the precise sexual content of either relationship, the empress dowager's motive, Li Yannian's early office details, the complete list of Western Han favorites, and the modern instant-gratification claims remain source-scoped pending stronger historical or empirical evidence.
