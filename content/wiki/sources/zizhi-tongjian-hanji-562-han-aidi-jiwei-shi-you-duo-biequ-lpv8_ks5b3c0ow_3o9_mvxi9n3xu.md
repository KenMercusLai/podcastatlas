# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》562丨汉哀帝即位时有多憋屈？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Suihe 2 / 7 BCE at [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]'s accession, before the later Hanji 564-568 and Jianping-era Aidi notes. It follows Aidi's hopeful early austerity and personal attention to government, then shows [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]] moving into the North Palace, using the connected passageway to reach Weiyang Palace, pressing for a higher title, and demanding promotion of favored relatives.

The durable synthesis is that accession does not equal full rule. Aidi begins with public promise, but birth-family filial pressure, residence placement, dowager status competition, title precedent, and consort-kin reward immediately constrain his authority; [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]] sees the danger in Fu Taihou's proximity, [[HeWuWesternHan|何武]] gives the accommodation Aidi wants, [[DongHongWesternHan|董宏]] supplies a Qin precedent for the title demand, and [[WangMang|王莽]] with [[ShiDanWesternHan|施丹]] temporarily block the proposal before Aidi and [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] allow the Dingtao-line elevation to proceed.

## Key Claims

- Han Aidi's reign opens with formal succession acts, amnesty, thrift, reduced expenses, and personal handling of government, creating an early expectation that order might improve.
- The residence question is politically decisive: Kong Guang recommends a separate palace for Fu Taihou because of her forceful temperament and political involvement, while He Wu supports placing her in the North Palace.
- North Palace access gives Fu Taihou daily proximity to Aidi through the Zifang passageway, making title and kinship demands harder for the new emperor to refuse.
- Dong Hong uses the Qin Zhuangxiang precedent of honoring both birth and adoptive mothers to argue that Fu Taihou should become "Di Taihou."
- Wang Mang and Shi Dan oppose Dong Hong's proposal as improper use of Qin precedent and as a threat to the unique dignity of the existing imperial-dowager order.
- Aidi retreats rather than risking an early confrontation with senior officials, but Fu Taihou's coercive anger pushes him to seek Wang Zhengjun's approval and resume the Dingtao-line elevation.
- The resulting rewards to Fu, Ding, and Zhao relatives show filial honor, title order, and outer-relative patronage becoming inseparable at the start of Aidi's reign.

## Key Quotes

> "欲望会让我们迷失方向" - the host's closing frame for Fu Taihou's title pursuit.

> "搬起石头砸自己的脚" - the host's hindsight judgment on letting Fu Taihou live in the North Palace.

> "有治国之心，却不能放手去做" - the episode's implied Aidi predicament after Fu Taihou gains palace access.
