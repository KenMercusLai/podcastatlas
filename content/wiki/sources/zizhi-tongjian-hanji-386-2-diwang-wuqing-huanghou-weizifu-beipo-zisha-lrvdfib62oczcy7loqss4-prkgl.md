# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》386-2｜帝王无情！皇后卫子夫被迫自杀

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] short episode backfills the armed-breakdown layer of the [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]] immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-387-1-taizi-liuju-taowang-hou-can-can-can-lodlsf5wtqtvoejjhe4apec7trku|Hanji 387-1]]. It follows [[LiuJuCrownPrinceWesternHan|卫太子刘据]] from a disputed imperial edict and emergency mobilization into open fighting around Chang'an, while [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] returns from Ganquan, marks his own command tallies, and delegates military control to the chancellor's side.

The episode's durable synthesis is that Liu Ju's failure is not only a battlefield defeat. It is a collapse in command legitimacy: edicts and tallies become contested, cavalry and the North Army do not reliably answer the crown prince, rumor turns his position into "rebellion," and officials who leave room for father-son mitigation are punished. The closing scene adds [[EmpressWeiZifuWesternHan|卫子夫]]'s suicide after Wudi orders her empress seal collected, making the empress-crown-prince household catastrophe explicit before Liu Ju's flight and final death.

## Key Claims

- Liu Ju's immediate dilemma is presented as a false-or-uncertain edict problem like Fusu's predicament: the document may be deadly even before its legitimacy can be verified.
- Both Wudi and Liu Ju compete to mobilize forces through imperial command language, prison releases, tallies, local troops, Hu cavalry, and the North Army.
- Wudi's yellow-tasseled tally distinction turns symbol verification into military control during the crisis.
- The North Army's refusal to respond through Ren An leaves Liu Ju dependent on armed city residents rather than a stable state force.
- The battle near Changle Palace's west gate lasts several days and kills many people, while public acceptance of the "crown prince rebels" frame drains Liu Ju's following.
- Tian Ren's decision to let Liu Ju leave Chang'an and Bao Shengzhi's attempt to slow summary punishment show that some officials still perceive a father-son boundary, but Wudi treats leniency as failure to punish rebellion.
- Wei Zifu's suicide follows Liu Ju's defeat and precedes the physical seizure of her empress seal, turning political defeat into household destruction.

## Key Quotes

> "太子造反" - the rumor frame that turns Liu Ju's followers away as the battle continues.

> "皇后卫子夫自杀" - the episode's closing household-catastrophe marker.
