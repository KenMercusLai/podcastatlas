# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》174｜刘邦为何大封自己的仇人（1）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-173-hanxin-taoguo-kuaxia-zhiru-mei-duoguo-niaojin-gongcang-1-ljowpzxo9u0evorhzbdyi2o0shlx|Hanji 173]] from [[HanXin|韩信]]'s demotion into [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s broader founding settlement. It explains why [[XiaoHe|萧何]] receives the richest merit grant, why [[ZhangLiang|张良]] and [[ChenPing|陈平]] handle reward with restraint, and how Liu Bang replaces or narrows dangerous regional power through Liu-family kingships. The episode ends by moving from reward distribution into the next rebellion question, as Zhang Liang warns that some actors are preparing to rebel even though "the realm is settled."

## Key Claims
- After Han Xin is reduced from Chu king to Huaiyin marquis, Liu Bang can begin formalizing the founding merit order and regional title map.
- Xiao He's large grant as Zan marquis is defended through the "功狗" / "功人" contrast: battlefield fighters pursue the prey, but rear administration and command organization decide whether the hunt works.
- The episode treats Xiao He's logistics, recruitment, and supply work as a founding-level contribution rather than clerical paperwork.
- Zhang Liang's refusal of a thirty-thousand-household grant and acceptance of Liu county makes his post-founding restraint concrete before the later exit pattern.
- Chen Ping's Huyou marquisate is paired with his public credit to [[WeiWuzhi|魏无知]], so Liu Bang rewards both useful strategy and the recommendation channel that surfaced it.
- Liu Bang's Liu-family grants are not simple nepotism in the source. Because his sons are young and his brothers few, he expands the usable royal kin pool to fill important territories.
- Han Xin's former Chu kingdom is split into [[JingKingdomEarlyHan|荆国]] for [[LiuJia|刘贾]] and [[ChuKingdomEarlyHan|楚国]] for [[LiuJiao|刘交]], converting the removed different-surname king's old base into same-surname royal control.
- [[LiuXiDaiKing|刘喜]] receives [[DaiKingdomEarlyHan|代国]], and [[LiuFeiQiKing|刘肥]] receives [[QiKingdomEarlyHan|齐国]], extending Liu-family kingship into the northern and eastern settlement.
- [[HanWangXin|韩王信]] is moved away from the old Han interior to the Taiyuan / Mayi frontier, a defense assignment against [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] that also keeps a capable non-Liu king away from the strategic heartland.
- Zhang Liang's closing warning reframes the episode: reward and enfeoffment solve visible allocation problems while also exposing the next instability that the settlement creates.
