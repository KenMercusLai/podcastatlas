## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s incognito-hunting sequence after [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-252-yiqun-chigua-qunzhong-ruhe-xiansha-hanwudi-lndyhnpi9ygvvrh-h1vnxocs3oap|Hanji 252]]. An innkeeper mistakes the armed party for bandits, while his wife delays violence; Wudi later rewards the household and orders twelve concealed lodging places south of Xuanqu, reducing reliance on ordinary inns without ending the underlying recreational travel.

The episode then pairs two remonstrances. [[DongfangShuo|东方朔]] opposes enlargement of [[ShanglinParkWesternHan|上林苑]] because fertile land, forest and water access, tax revenue, homes, graves, and imperial safety would be sacrificed to hunting space. [[SimaXiangru|司马相如]] warns that personally pursuing boars and bears exposes the ruler to an irreversible accident. Wudi reportedly rewards both speakers but changes neither policy, making [[RewardedUnadoptedRemonstrance|赏谏不纳]] the durable governance distinction between recognizing a capable critic and converting criticism into action.

## Key Claims

- An armed incognito party creates a second authority-recognition failure when an innkeeper interprets Wudi's followers as bandits and prepares local resistance.
- The innkeeper's wife reportedly delays the attack by intoxicating and tying up her husband, after which Wudi rewards her and appoints the husband as a yulin guard.
- Wudi responds to the lodging danger by establishing twelve concealed rest stations, an operational adaptation that protects the traveling ruler without ending incognito hunting.
- Wudi orders Wuqiu Shouwang to survey and price land south of Acheng, east of Zhouzhi, and west of Yichun for enlargement of Shanglin Park, with compensation planned through reported wasteland.
- Dongfang Shuo argues that the enlargement would remove fertile land and livelihood resources, reduce tax revenue, displace homes and graves, and enlarge hunting hazards for the emperor.
- Wudi promotes and rewards Dongfang Shuo but proceeds with the park expansion and population removal.
- Sima Xiangru warns that guards and expert archers may not be able to rescue Wudi during a sudden encounter with a dangerous animal or an ordinary riding accident.
- Wudi praises and rewards Sima Xiangru but continues personally hunting dangerous animals.

## Key Quotes

> “家累千金，坐不垂堂。” - the prudential maxim used in Sima Xiangru's warning that greater responsibility requires less avoidable personal risk.

> “你们说的都对，但我就是不听。” - the episode title's interpretive summary of praise and reward without policy adoption.
