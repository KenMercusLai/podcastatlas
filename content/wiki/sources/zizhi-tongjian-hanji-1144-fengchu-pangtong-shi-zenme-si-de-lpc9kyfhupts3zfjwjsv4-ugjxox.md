# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1144丨凤雏庞统是怎么死的？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s final Yizhou campaign: [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]], [[ZhangFei|张飞]], and [[ZhaoYun|赵云]] advance from Jingzhou, [[YanYanLateHan|严颜]] is captured and treated respectfully, [[PangTong|庞统]] dies by arrows during the long attack on 雒城, and [[MaChao|马超]]'s arrival increases pressure on [[LiuZhang|刘璋]] before the surrender of [[Chengdu|成都]]. Its durable organizational claim is that losing a complementary adviser can redistribute command and judgment functions across an entire political system.

The episode contrasts Zhuge Liang's system-building and stable governance with Pang Tong's faster, higher-risk breakthrough style. It also separates historical record from later literary memory: the familiar “落凤坡” location and the strongest “卧龙、凤雏” formula are treated as later accretions rather than direct statements of [[Sanguozhi|《三国志》]]. The proposed chain from Pang Tong's death to Zhuge Liang leaving Jingzhou, Jingzhou's later loss, and Guan Yu's death remains a plausible strategic interpretation, not settled single-cause history.

## Key Claims

- The episode presents history as a case library for decisions, tradeoffs, and consequences rather than as a source of automatic formulas.
- Zhuge Liang leads Zhang Fei and Zhao Yun west from Jingzhou while [[GuanYu|关羽]] remains behind; after Yan Yan's capture, the invading forces divide and converge toward Chengdu.
- Liu Bei's main force spends roughly a year attacking 雒城, where [[ZhangRenLateHan|张任]] resists and Pang Tong is killed by arrows while directing an assault.
- Pang Tong's death removes a strategist associated with speed, breakthrough, and calculated irregular action; the episode says Liu Bei becomes more dependent on [[FaZheng|法正]] and Zhuge Liang must enter Yizhou.
- Zhuge Liang and Pang Tong are framed as complementary rather than rank-ordered advisers: one emphasizes system, administration, and stable foundations, while the other seeks rapid openings and accepts more political and military risk.
- The “落凤坡” death site and the familiar “卧龙、凤雏，两人得一可安天下” formulation belong to later geographical and literary memory rather than being direct event proof from the historical record.
- After 雒城 falls, Ma Chao's appearance north of Chengdu intensifies psychological pressure; Liu Zhang nevertheless still has troops, provisions, and support before surrendering to avoid further civilian suffering.
- Capturing territory does not complete conquest: the episode closes by making postwar political acceptance and public trust part of durable rule.

## Key Quotes

> “权变之时，固非一道所能定也。” - Pang Tong's reported argument that disorder cannot be governed through one fixed rule.

> “治国之基石”与“开拓之利刃” - the episode's paired characterization of Zhuge Liang and Pang Tong.

- Causal caution: Pang Tong's death may help explain why Zhuge Liang entered Yizhou and Jingzhou's leadership arrangement changed, but it does not by itself establish that the later loss of Jingzhou or Guan Yu's death was inevitable.
