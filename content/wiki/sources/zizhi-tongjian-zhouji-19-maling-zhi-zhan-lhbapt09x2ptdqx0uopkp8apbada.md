# 《资治通鉴·周纪》19丨马陵之战

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode moves from a blank 342 BCE notice into the 341 BCE setup for [[MalingBattle|马陵之战]]. [[WeiState|魏国]] attacks [[HanState|韩国]], Han appeals to [[QiState|齐国]], and [[SunBin|孙膑]] persuades [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] to wait until Han and Wei are both committed before intervening. The episode then shifts from rescue timing to operational design: Qi again threatens [[Daliang|大梁]], Wei recalls [[PangJuan|庞涓]], sends [[PrinceShenWei|太子申]] as commander, and Sun Bin prepares to exploit Wei's contempt for Qi through forced-march exhaustion.

## Key Claims
- 342 BCE is treated as a blank [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] year; the source uses that pause to make 341 BCE and [[MalingBattle|马陵之战]] stand out as an interstate turning point.
- The war begins when [[PangJuan|庞涓]] leads Wei's attack on [[HanState|韩国]], and Han asks [[QiState|齐国]] for help.
- The Qi court debate is not whether to rescue Han, but when. [[ZouJi|成侯邹忌]] argues against rescue, [[TianJi|田忌]] argues for early rescue, and [[SunBin|孙膑]] argues for [[DelayedRescueStatecraft|delayed rescue]].
- Sun Bin's reasoning is that an early rescue would make Qi absorb Wei's full pressure on Han's behalf; waiting lets Qi gain Han's gratitude while meeting an exhausted Wei army.
- [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] accepts the advice, secretly reassures Han, and lets Han keep fighting until it suffers five defeats and gives its fate over to Qi.
- Qi's rescue force names [[TianJi|田忌]], [[TianYing|田婴]], and a third Tian commander in the source transcript; Sun Bin serves as military adviser rather than formal commander.
- The campaign repeats the [[EncirclingWeiToRescueZhao|围魏救赵]] logic from [[GuilingBattle|桂陵之战]]: Qi does not rush to Han's battlefield but attacks toward [[Daliang|大梁]], the place Wei must save.
- Wei responds by recalling Pang Juan from the Han front and raising another army under [[PrinceShenWei|太子申]], which the episode uses to explain [[CrownPrinceCommandRisk|太子挂帅风险]].
- The host connects the crown-prince command problem to a broader power logic: a close heir can be politically threatening because he has both blood legitimacy and possible military networks.
- Sun Bin analyzes Wei morale and psychology, especially Wei soldiers' contempt for Qi soldiers, before designing the next deception.
- The episode links Sun Bin's plan to [[SunziBingfa|《孙子兵法》]]'s warning about long-distance pursuit for profit: speed can break command, supply, and formation.
- The military design is to lure Wei into rapid pursuit, then use [[ForcedMarchOperationalRisk|forced-march exhaustion]] and [[LureEnemyOutOfPosition|诱敌离位]] to let Qi fight rested, fed, and concentrated against a tired enemy.

## Key Quotes
> "早救还是晚救" - the episode's framing of the Qi court debate.

> "五战五败" - Han's condition before Qi's open intervention.

> "百里而趋利者蹶上将，五十里而趋利者军半至" - the [[SunziBingfa|《孙子兵法》]] warning used to frame Sun Bin's operational plan.
