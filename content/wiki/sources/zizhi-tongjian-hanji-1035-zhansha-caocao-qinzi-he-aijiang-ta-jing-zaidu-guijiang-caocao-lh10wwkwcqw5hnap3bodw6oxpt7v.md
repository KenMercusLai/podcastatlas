# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1035丨斩杀曹操亲子和爱将，他竟再度归降曹操？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the immediate pre-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1036-liubiao-weihe-yao-bi-hansong-jian-caocao-lnifeizgoebis2gdovgmzg6dojaz|Hanji 1036]] Guandu setup from [[ZhangXiu|张绣]]'s renewed submission to [[CaoCao|曹操]] into Guanzhong rear policy. [[JiaXu|贾诩]] blocks [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s recruitment bid and advises Zhang Xiu to submit to Cao Cao, judging that Cao Cao's emperor-backed name, weaker position, and need to display magnanimity make pardon useful despite the Wancheng blood debt. The second half follows [[YangFuLateHan|杨阜]]'s Cao/Yuan comparison and [[WeiJiLateHan|魏觊]]'s salt-revenue agricultural recovery plan, making pre-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡]] preparation a matter of defector incorporation, political legitimacy, manpower, grain, and Guanzhong control.

## Key Claims
- Durable upward relationships depend on low-risk asymmetric value, execution, discretion, and clear usefulness; the host uses this modern frame to introduce political patronage and surrender logic.
- Jia Xu rejects Yuan Shao as a patron for Zhang Xiu because Yuan Shao cannot manage kin rivalry and therefore cannot be trusted to tolerate capable outsiders.
- Jia Xu argues that Cao Cao is the better target because he acts under the emperor's name, needs Zhang Xiu more than Yuan Shao does, and can use forgiveness of a former enemy to advertise magnanimity.
- Cao Cao converts the submission into public trust signals: personal reception, banquet hospitality, marriage alliance through Cao Jun and Zhang Xiu's daughter, office for Zhang Xiu, and court recommendation and enfeoffment for Jia Xu.
- Yang Fu frames Cao Cao's likely advantage over Yuan Shao as organizational rather than merely numerical: decisiveness, strict law, strong army, broad talent use, and committed subordinates.
- Wei Ji identifies refugee absorption by Guanzhong generals as a future security risk and proposes restoring salt offices, selling salt, and using the revenue to buy tools and oxen for returning farmers.
- The episode treats Guandu as a power-reconstruction threshold whose outcome depends on pre-battle coalition work, rear governance, grain, and the ability to digest defeated resources.

## Key Quotes
> "袁绍连兄弟关系都处理不好，无法容纳天下英雄豪杰" - Jia Xu's public rejection of Yuan Shao's envoy.

> "曹操奉天子号令天下，名正言顺" - Jia Xu's first reason for choosing Cao Cao over Yuan Shao.

> "曹操势弱，更会重视张绣" - Jia Xu's marginal-value argument for Zhang Xiu's submission.

> "用卖盐收入购买农具、耕牛" - Wei Ji's fiscal-to-agricultural recovery mechanism for Guanzhong.
