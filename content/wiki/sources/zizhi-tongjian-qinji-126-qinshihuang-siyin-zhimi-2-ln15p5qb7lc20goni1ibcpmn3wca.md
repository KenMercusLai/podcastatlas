## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the Qinji 126 death-omen branch by unpacking the "祖龙死" prophecy and Qin Shi Huang's response to it. The host argues that [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]] likely understood "祖龙" as himself, then used divination, migration, and a final tour as an attempt to alter or outrun the foretold death. The episode also brings [[ZhaoZhengShu|《赵正书》]] and a 北河/河北 textual issue into the explanation, keeping the reconstruction source-critical rather than treating Qin Shi Huang's inner motives as certain.

## Key Claims
- The mysterious returned-jade episode from [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-126-qinshihuang-siyin-zhimi-1-lgtlhau6ixqglk1ict1fs0jexjwe|Qinji 126 part 1]] becomes a direct death warning once "祖龙" is read as Qin Shi Huang.
- Qin Shi Huang's public explanation that "祖龙" meant a human ancestor is treated as self-comforting or face-saving; the host argues he likely knew it pointed to the founding emperor himself.
- Because Qin used October as the new year, an autumn warning about death "this year" could make survival until the next year feel like a narrow counterfactual opening.
- The divination result "游徙吉" turns the prophecy into [[OmenCountermeasureMobility|游徙避命]]: movement itself becomes the proposed response to a death omen.
- Qin Shi Huang applies that response in two forms: moving thirty thousand households to the northern frontier around 北河 and 榆中, and then beginning his fifth and final imperial tour.
- The episode treats [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]]'s "河北" as a likely error for [[Shiji|《史记》]]'s "北河"; the difference changes whether the passage reads as migration away from the frontier or settlement toward it.
- [[ZhaoZhengShu|《赵正书》]] is used as corroborating narrative evidence because it describes Qin Shi Huang on his last tour as already aware of a death prophecy and hoping to change fate.
- The source's strongest claim is interpretive: the final tour is not only political inspection or display, but also a fate-altering attempt under death anxiety.

## Key Quotes

> "天命难道真的不能改变吗" - the question the episode draws from the Zhao Zheng Shu frame.
