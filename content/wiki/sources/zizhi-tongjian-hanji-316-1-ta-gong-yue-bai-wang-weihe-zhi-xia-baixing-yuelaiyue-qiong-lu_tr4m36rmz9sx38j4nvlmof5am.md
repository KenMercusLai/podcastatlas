# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》316-1｜他功越百王，为何治下百姓越来越穷

## Summary

This short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode opens the fiscal question developed in Hanji 316-2 by contrasting [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s long record of war with treasury shortage and popular poverty. It treats war as a full resource system—intelligence, mobilization, provisioning, equipment, transport, battlefield loss, and captive settlement—and uses [[DuFu|杜甫]]'s 《百忧集行》 to make household hunger and parental helplessness emotionally legible.

The annalistic half moves to 120 BCE. [[LiuJiJiaodongKingWesternHan|胶东王刘寄]] is implicated after [[LiuAn|淮南王刘安]]'s rebellion case because he knew something of the plot, failed to report it, and allegedly prepared militarily; he dies from fear and illness before naming an heir. The episode says Han Wudi's grief and family attachment then preserve Liu Ji's line by installing his elder son Liu Xian in Jiaodong and making the favored younger son Liu Qing king of Liu'an over former Hengshan territory, adding a posthumous succession variant to [[RoyalKinshipPenaltyMitigation|宗室亲缘减刑]].

## Key Claims

- Han Wudi's reign is presented as being dominated by war, and the episode asks how the court financed the wider military system rather than counting only combat.
- Intelligence, troop movement, provisioning, weapons, transport, losses, and captive settlement make prolonged war a continuous fiscal burden.
- The source links treasury insufficiency to civilian poverty but does not yet specify the fiscal mechanisms developed in Hanji 316-2.
- Du Fu's 《百忧集行》 supplies a literary image of hunger, illness, silent grief, and inability to feed children rather than direct evidence about Western Han households.
- After Liu An's plot is exposed, testimony implicates Liu Ji for non-reporting and alleged military preparation.
- Liu Ji dies before a succession decision or completed adjudication, after which Han Wudi makes Liu Xian king of Jiaodong and Liu Qing king of Liu'an out of grief, kinship, and remembered closeness in the episode's account.
