# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》621丨帝王心术：攻心最重要！

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills 4 CE under [[HanPingdi|汉平帝]], between [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-620-shishang-zui-chuanqi-de-quanmin-daxuan-llijvsut0ldv-ugfa281sllst4gu|Hanji 620]]'s petition-backed "宰衡" reward and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-622-guanyuan-ruhe-zuo-mianzi-gongfu-lutgirh7woafk3nzjza4o-tlmf7j|Hanji 622]]'s face-project governance. It shows [[WangMang|王莽]] consolidating power by managing [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]'s emotional and informational environment: giving money to her close attendants, rewarding her sisters, arranging public outings and charity scenes, visiting sick servants, and letting court praise circulate around her.

The durable synthesis is that Wang Mang's pre-usurpation rise includes an inner-court influence layer, not only formal titles and public petitions. By binding Wang Zhengjun's relatives, servants, local reception scenes, and court speakers into a favorable feedback environment, Wang Mang makes his expanding authority feel personally reassuring and morally admired; [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]]'s frightened withdrawal then shows that senior officials could read the same atmosphere as political danger.

## Key Claims

- The episode uses "grasping people's hearts" as the interpretive frame for Wang Mang's power consolidation.
- In 4 CE, Wang Mang takes ten million cash from the additional thirty-seven million reward package and distributes it to Wang Zhengjun's intimate attendants.
- Wang Mang proposes titles and tax-yielding bathing estates for Wang Zhengjun's elder and younger sisters, extending patronage through her family network.
- Wang Mang arranges repeated seasonal outings for Wang Zhengjun around Chang'an and links those trips to public charity toward widows, orphans, chaste women, and local commoners.
- His personal concern for Wang Zhengjun's servants, including visits when they are sick, turns minor attendants into a favorable influence channel.
- Wang Shun reports that Wang Mang's refusals of land and gold have transformed public morals, comparing the effect to King Wen resolving the Yu-Rui dispute.
- Kong Guang becomes increasingly fearful amid the praise atmosphere and insists on withdrawing from office, after which the court gives him reduced attendance obligations and retirement honors.

## Key Quotes

> "经营中最关键的是把握人心" - the episode's governing frame for Wang Mang's method.

> "小恩养贵人，大恩养仇人" - maxim the host uses to explain small, repeated favors.

> "天下百姓听说安汉公不肯接受千乘大小的封地" - Wang Shun's praise-report premise.
