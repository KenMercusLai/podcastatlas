# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1145丨刘备教你如何应对鄙视自己的人？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s transition from taking [[Chengdu|成都]] to governing a coalition of Jingzhou followers and Yizhou elites. Its central case is [[LiuBa|刘巴]], a prestigious and capable scholar who repeatedly avoids Liu Bei, warns [[LiuZhang|刘璋]] against admitting him, and later treats [[ZhangFei|张飞]] with open contempt, yet is protected and retained after the conquest.

The episode frames Liu Bei's decision as a separation between personal offense and organizational value: anger at Liu Ba's treatment of Zhang Fei does not erase the fiscal expertise needed after wartime rewards empty the treasury. It also offers a practical emotional frame—pause before reacting to contempt and redirect energy toward self-construction—but the psychological claims about shame, inferiority, and aggression remain interpretive rather than clinically established.

## Key Claims

- After entering Chengdu in 214 CE, Liu Bei distributes stored wealth and assigns offices across old followers, military contributors, and local political networks; the episode treats these choices as the opening of a difficult post-conquest integration problem.
- [[MiZhu|糜竺]] receives exceptional rank and honor in recognition of early financial support, marriage ties, and long loyalty, while [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] receives substantive coordinating power.
- Liu Ba is presented as a prominent southern Jingzhou scholar who chooses [[CaoCao|曹操]] when other local elites move toward Liu Bei, later rejects Zhuge Liang's invitation, and eventually reaches Yizhou through Jiaozhi.
- Liu Ba repeatedly warns Liu Zhang that admitting Liu Bei and sending him against Zhang Lu will endanger Yizhou, but those warnings are not adopted.
- During the Chengdu campaign, Liu Bei orders that Liu Ba be protected; after Liu Zhang surrenders, Liu Ba apologizes and enters Liu Bei's regime.
- Zhang Fei visits Liu Ba out of admiration, but Liu Ba reportedly refuses to speak with him and later tells Zhuge Liang that he will not associate with a mere soldier; Liu Bei is angered by the insult.
- [[ZhangZhaoLateHan|张昭]] interprets Liu Ba's conduct as narrowness, while [[SunQuan|孙权]] argues that ingratiating himself with Zhang Fei merely to please Liu Bei would compromise an independent scholar's character.
- Liu Bei nevertheless keeps Liu Ba because post-conquest distributions have produced an urgent fiscal problem and Liu Ba has relevant expertise; the specific monetary solution is deferred to Hanji 1146.
- The episode advises people facing contempt to pause, attend to breathing, avoid immediate emotional capture, and invest energy in building themselves rather than retaliating.

- Psychological caution: the claims that boasting comes from inferiority, contempt from shame, and aggression from inner pain are broad episode interpretations, not diagnoses or universal causal rules.
