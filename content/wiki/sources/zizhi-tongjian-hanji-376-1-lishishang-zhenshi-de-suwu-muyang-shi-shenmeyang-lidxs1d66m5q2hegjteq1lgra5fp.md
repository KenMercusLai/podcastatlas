# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》376-1｜历史上真实的苏武牧羊是什么样？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the opening crisis behind [[SuWuWesternHan|苏武]]'s later Xiongnu exile. A mission sent during a tentative Han-Xiongnu thaw is compromised when deputy envoy [[ZhangShengSuWuMissionWesternHan|张胜]] secretly funds [[YuChangWesternHan|虞常]]'s plan to kill [[WeiLuWesternHan|卫律]]; after the plot fails and Zhang discloses it, Su Wu twice tries to die before coercion can turn the mission into a public humiliation.

The durable synthesis is that the source presents Su Wu's courage first as protection of office identity under collective diplomatic exposure, not yet as the familiar North Sea shepherding scene. His refusal is sharpened by contrast with Zhang Sheng's unauthorized intervention and later surrender, while [[ChangHuiWesternHan|常惠]] first appears as the colleague who stops Su Wu's initial suicide attempt.

## Key Claims

- In 100 BCE, Han Wudi sends Su Wu as chief envoy, Zhang Sheng as deputy, and a mission of more than one hundred people to Xiongnu during a temporary diplomatic thaw.
- The mission initially appears complete, but Zhang Sheng privately agrees to reward Yu Chang for killing Wei Lü and gives him valuables.
- Yu Chang's wider plot fails after a participant informs; he is captured, and Zhang Sheng tells Su Wu only after fearing that interrogation will expose their agreement.
- Su Wu first attempts suicide because he believes dying only after being violated or humiliated would betray the state mission; Zhang Sheng and Chang Hui stop him.
- After Yu Chang implicates Zhang Sheng, the chanyu considers killing all Han envoys and then uses Wei Lü and the threat of collective punishment to press for surrender.
- Su Wu stabs himself rather than yield, survives emergency treatment, and earns the chanyu's respect without ending the coercive pressure.
- Zhang Sheng surrenders when threatened with execution, while Su Wu rejects guilt by association and remains unmoved when Wei Lü raises a sword against him.
- The installment ends before the North Sea exile and therefore explains the origin of detention without completing the later “苏武牧羊” story.

## Key Quotes

> "见犯乃死，重负国。" - Su Wu's reason for seeking death before the mission can be humiliated.

> "屈节辱命，虽生，何面目以归汉！" - Su Wu's refusal to preserve life through surrender.
