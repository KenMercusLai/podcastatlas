# 070 倪匡传奇：姗姗来迟的纪念特辑

## Summary

This [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] memorial episode retells seven or eight stories from [[NiKuang|倪匡]]'s 《倪匡传奇》, moving from restraint techniques, a public execution, and two near-drownings to bedbugs, blood-feeding insects, a folk scorpion remedy, and locust control. Its strongest synthesis extends [[LocalExperienceAsCreativeArchive|local experience as a creative archive]]: witnessed violence, bodily danger, pests, rural knowledge, and unresolved oddities become durable narrative material, while vivid memoir and host discussion do not independently verify physiology, animal cognition, medicine, history, or direct influence on later fiction.

## Key Claims

- The episode presents 《倪匡传奇》 as a comparatively unhurried autobiographical work whose thirty short chapters preserve [[NiKuang|倪匡]]'s mainland experiences from 1951 to 1957.
- A public execution becomes a study in the gap between slogan and embodied violence: an excited crowd falls silent after the shots, and the image of a victim's damaged eye reportedly recurs in Ni Kuang's nightmares and may have informed 《眼睛》.
- Two near-drownings at Tiger Hill and on the Chuoer River are remembered as moments of surprising calm rather than panic; the hosts treat this as personal testimony, not a general law of dying.
- Bedbugs that reportedly bypassed an insecticide ring by dropping from the ceiling, together with mosquitoes, lice, fleas, horseflies, and leeches, turn rural and low-sanitation life into an account of humans competing with other organisms for bodily space.
- A chewed-and-salted “蝎子草” poultice reportedly relieved a scorpion sting, and a later materia-medica reference resembled the treatment without requiring the injured person to chew it; possible efficacy and ritual embellishment therefore remain distinct questions.
- A village leader's choice to let juvenile locusts mature and fly away protected the local crop while potentially transferring harm elsewhere, exposing a conflict between immediate local preservation and wider pest governance.
- The hosts' personal memories and literary associations increase narrative force but also widen the evidence boundary: the Purple Mountain statue, insect behavior, folk medicine, execution history, quoted dialogue, and causal links to fiction remain uncorroborated or source-scoped.

## Key Quotes

> “我会死在苏州。” - Ni Kuang's reported thought while sinking at Tiger Hill.

> “长大了，它们自然就飞走了。” - the team leader's reported rationale for not burning the juvenile locusts.
