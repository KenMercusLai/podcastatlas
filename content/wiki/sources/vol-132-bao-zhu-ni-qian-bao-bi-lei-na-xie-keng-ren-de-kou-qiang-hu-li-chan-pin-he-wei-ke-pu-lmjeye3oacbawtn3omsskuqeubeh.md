# VOL.132保住你钱包！避雷那些坑人的口腔护理产品和伪科普

## Summary
This [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode has oral-health doctor [[BingeOralDoctor|宾哥]] turn China's 2024 National Teeth-Loving Day theme into a practical guide to [[LifecycleOralHealthPrevention]]. It distinguishes mechanical plaque control and clinically indicated care from toothpaste, rinse, whitening, and repair-product marketing through [[OralCareProductTreatmentBoundary]], while placing cosmetic procedures and provider selection inside [[DentalAestheticTreatmentBoundary]] and [[DentalProviderQualificationAssessment]]. The discussion is public education rather than remote diagnosis.

## Key Claims
- [[LifecycleOralHealthPrevention]] joins regular assessment, effective cleaning, sugar-exposure control, childhood prevention and habit review, professional cleaning, and timely restoration of missing teeth across age groups.
- [[OralCareProductTreatmentBoundary]] rejects toothpaste or liquid products marketed as regrowing teeth, permanently repairing established damage, treating Helicobacter pylori infection, or curing inflamed bleeding gums without removing plaque and other causes.
- [[OralMicrobiomePreventiveCare]] depends primarily on effective mechanical plaque disruption; electric brushes, water flossers, and daily rinses are aids whose value depends on technique, fit, indication, and tolerance.
- [[ToothDemineralizationRemineralization]] does not make an established cavity equivalent to laboratory remineralization or justify a consumer product's promise to rebuild a tooth within hours.
- [[DentalAestheticTreatmentBoundary]] separates surface-stain removal, chemical bleaching, veneers, and crowns; sensitivity, tissue damage, irreversible preparation, and professional indication matter more than “celebrity” branding.
- [[OralAirwayDevelopment]] requires cause-specific assessment: pediatric mouth breathing may involve adenoids, septal or turbinate obstruction, habit, and facial development, while adult respiratory obstruction and established skeletal change may need different specialties.
- [[DentalProviderQualificationAssessment]] favors verifiable credentials, appropriate scope, cases, professional participation, and peer recognition over prestigious-sounding titles or public-versus-private labels alone.
