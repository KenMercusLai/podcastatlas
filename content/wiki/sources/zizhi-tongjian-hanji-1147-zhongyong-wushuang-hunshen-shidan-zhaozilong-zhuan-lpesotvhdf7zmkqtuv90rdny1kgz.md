# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1147丨忠勇无双浑身是胆赵子龙传

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode presents [[ZhaoYun|赵云]] as more than a battlefield hero. His reported opposition to distributing Chengdu land and houses to Liu Bei's followers joins civilian restoration to revenue, recruitment, and political stability; his choice of leader, Changban rescue, refusal of [[ZhaoFanLateHan|赵范]]'s marriage proposal, and Hanzhong empty-camp maneuver are used to connect loyalty with value judgment, restraint, and tactical nerve.

The closing branch adds [[HuoJun|霍峻]], whose small garrison reportedly holds Jiameng for nearly a year, rejects Zhang Lu's inducement, and defeats a much larger besieging force. Together the two portraits treat loyalty as active stewardship: preserving civilians, dependants, territory, and entrusted positions rather than merely professing personal attachment.

## Key Claims
- After Liu Bei takes Chengdu, Zhao Yun reportedly opposes dividing local fields and houses among the conquerors and argues for returning them to civilians so that settlement, support, taxation, and recruitment can recover.
- The episode interprets Zhao Yun's early movement from Gongsun Zan to Liu Bei as allegiance grounded in a preference for benevolent government rather than private attachment alone.
- Zhao Yun's Changban rescue is retold as loyalty under battlefield collapse, although the supplied summary marks much of the dramatic staging as unverified.
- After Guiyang's surrender, Zhao Yun refuses Zhao Fan's proposed marriage alliance because coerced submission makes the offer politically risky; Zhao Fan's later flight is presented as confirming the caution.
- At the Han River, Zhao Yun reportedly opens the camp gates and lowers the flags, then attacks the withdrawing Cao force with drums and crossbows; Liu Bei's praise, “一身是胆,” frames courage as judgment rather than rashness.
- The episode attributes some Zhao Yun material to the now-lost *Yun Biezhuan* preserved in Pei Songzhi's annotations, dates Zhao Yun's death to 229, and notes his later posthumous title of Shunping Marquis.
- Huo Jun reportedly refuses Yang Ang's inducement, holds Jiameng with several hundred men against more than ten thousand attackers for nearly a year, and kills Xiang Cun in a counterattack.
- After taking Shu, Liu Bei establishes Zitong Commandery and appoints Huo Jun as administrator; the episode treats the appointment as recognition that the garrison preserved a strategic northern approach.

## Key Quotes
> “国贼是曹操，不是百姓。” - the episode's rendering of Zhao Yun's argument against distributing Chengdu civilian property.

> “小人头可得，城不可得。” - Huo Jun's reported refusal to surrender Jiameng.
