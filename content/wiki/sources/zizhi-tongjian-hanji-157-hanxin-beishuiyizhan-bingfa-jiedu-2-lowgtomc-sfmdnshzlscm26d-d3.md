# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》157｜韩信“背水一战”兵法解读（2）

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-157-hanxin-beishuiyizhan-bingfa-jiedu-1-loiz8ge-a39tmigajool2spzri5i|Hanji 157 part 1]] by moving [[HanXin|韩信]]'s [[BackwaterBattle|背水一战]] from battlefield doctrine into post-victory strategy. It emphasizes that Han Xin won through intelligence and psychological shock, then respected [[LiZuoChe|李左车]] as a usable strategist rather than treating him only as a defeated enemy. Li Zuoche advises rest, pacification, envoy pressure, and sequential persuasion of [[YanState|燕国]] and [[QiState|齐国]], making the episode a source case for [[VictoryDeterrenceDiplomacy|胜势威慑外交]] and an extension of [[PoliticalSurrenderCascade|招降示范级联]].

## Key Claims
- [[HanXin|韩信]]'s victory over [[ZhaoState|赵国]] depends partly on spy-confirmed knowledge that [[ChenYu|陈馀]] rejected [[LiZuoChe|李左车]]'s plan, while Zhao misreads Han's actual force and rear threat.
- The two thousand soldiers who seized the Zhao camp and replaced its flags create a decisive psychological shock, turning the camp itself into an information weapon after the fighting begins.
- Before attacking Zhao, Han Xin orders his army not to kill Li Zuoche and offers a large reward for taking him alive, signaling that the defeated adviser has strategic value.
- Once Li Zuoche is captured, Han Xin releases him, seats him honorably, and asks how to move north against [[YanState|燕国]] and east against [[QiState|齐国]].
- Li Zuoche initially refuses by presenting himself as a defeated general and ruined-state minister, but Han Xin answers with the [[BailiXi|百里奚]] precedent: talent may fail under one ruler and become decisive under another.
- Han Xin admits that if Chen Yu had accepted Li Zuoche's earlier supply-line advice, Han Xin himself might have become Zhao's prisoner.
- Li Zuoche says Han Xin's current advantage is reputation: after crossing the western river, capturing [[ZhaoXie|赵歇]], killing Chen Yu, and taking [[JingxingPass|井陉口]], Han Xin's name frightens the region.
- Li Zuoche also says Han Xin's current weakness is exhaustion. A tired army stuck under Yan walls could lose time and supplies, while Qi would then strengthen its borders.
- The recommended sequence is to rest troops, settle Zhao's people, make a visible northward posture, and send an eloquent envoy to Yan; after Yan submits, a second envoy can use that precedent toward Qi.
- Han Xin adopts the advice, and Yan submits as Li Zuoche predicts, converting battlefield victory into a lower-cost diplomatic result.
- Han Xin reports to [[LiuBang|刘邦]] at [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]] and asks that [[ZhangEr|张耳]] be made Zhao king so the newly won territory can be stabilized.
- [[XiangYu|项羽]] responds by sending cavalry across the Yellow River against the newly arranged Zhao front; Zhang Er and Han Xin keep fighting, take additional cities, and Han Xin later sends troops to support Liu Bang.
- The episode closes by turning toward [[SuiHe|随何]]'s mission to persuade [[YingBu|英布]] to leave Xiang Yu, treating that mission as a major diplomatic success in the Chu-Han war.

## Key Quotes
> "败军之将，不可以言勇；亡国之大夫，不可以图存" - Li Zuoche's initial refusal to advise Han Xin.

> "先声夺人" - the principle used to frame envoy pressure after Han Xin's victory.

> "善于作战的将领不会用自己的弱点去攻击他人的优势" - Li Zuoche's warning against using exhausted troops against fortified cities.

- [[BackwaterBattle|背水一战]], [[DeathGroundTacticalPreconditions|死地战术前提]], and [[BattlefieldInformationControl|战场信息控制]] - this source confirms that intelligence and psychological shock remain central to explaining the victory.

## Contradictions
- No settled contradiction is recorded. The source deepens [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-157-hanxin-beishuiyizhan-bingfa-jiedu-1-loiz8ge-a39tmigajool2spzri5i|Hanji 157 part 1]] by showing that the same intelligence-and-psychology chain matters after the battle as well as during it.
- The transcript form "陈瑜" is normalized to the wiki's canonical [[ChenYu|陈馀]] page.
- Qi's submission remains source-scoped as Li Zuoche's predicted next step; this episode records Yan's submission but does not narrate a completed Qi surrender.
