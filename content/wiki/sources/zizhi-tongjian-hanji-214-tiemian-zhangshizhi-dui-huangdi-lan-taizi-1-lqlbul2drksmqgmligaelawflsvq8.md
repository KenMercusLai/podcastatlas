# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》214｜铁面张释之：怼皇帝拦太子（1）

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode follows [[ZhangShizhiWesternHan|张释之]] from a long-stalled, self-funded palace appointment through [[YuanAngWesternHan|袁盎]]'s recommendation and a series of encounters with [[LiuHeng|汉文帝刘恒]]. Zhang advances by turning historical knowledge into concrete advice, yet his durable profile comes from refusing three tempting shortcuts: equating fluent speech with administrative ability, suspending gate protocol for the crown prince and a favored king, and treating physical tomb strength as a substitute for burial restraint.

The ruler-adviser relationship is reciprocal but not frictionless. Liu Heng proposes the appointments and burial design that Zhang challenges, while also accepting the objections, abandoning the tiger-keeper promotion, allowing the palace-gate accusation to run through formal pardon, and promoting the official who constrained his own family. The episode therefore supports a narrower institutional claim than its modern “向上管理” language: useful remonstrance identifies the decision error and preserves an actionable path for correction.

## Key Claims

- Zhang Shizhi reportedly entered service through a wealth-qualified route, spent more than a decade as a 骑郎 without promotion, and planned to resign before Yuan Ang recommended him as a 谒者.
- Liu Heng asks Zhang to replace broad rhetoric with currently usable proposals; Zhang's discussion of Qin, Chu, and Han then leads to promotion as 谒者仆射 and later advancement toward 廷尉.
- At the imperial animal park, Zhang blocks promotion of a fluent tiger-pen keeper by contrasting verbal polish with the slower speech of respected officials and warning that eloquence can hide administrative failure.
- At Sima Gate, Zhang stops Crown Prince [[HanJingdi|刘启]] and [[LiuWuLiangKingWesternHan|梁王刘武]] for failing to dismount, formally accuses them of disrespect, and waits for a dowager-issued pardon before admitting them.
- Liu Heng's response to the gate incident converts personal-family embarrassment into support for rule-bound enforcement: he apologizes for poor instruction and promotes Zhang rather than punishing him.
- At Baling, Zhang argues that tomb security depends less on stronger physical sealing than on removing desirable grave goods, making restraint a root-cause response to looting risk.
- The host's “向上管理” and hidden-interest-group readings are modern interpretations; the episode does not establish that Zhang deliberately solved an unstated factional problem for Liu Heng.

## Key Quotes

> “周勃和张相如说话迟钝，不像啬夫那样能言善辩。” - the episode's contrast between established judgment and verbal fluency.

> “如果坟墓里没有人们想得到的财宝，即便没有石椁也没有关系。” - the episode's rendering of Zhang's root-cause burial argument.

## Contradictions

- No settled contradiction is adopted. The three central anecdotes are retained as this episode's account pending comparison with transmitted histories.
- The source distinguishes its factual narrative from its speculative reading that the Shanglin personnel decision involved hidden imperial-relative or elite interests; the latter is not treated as established.
- “向上管理” is an interpretive analogy, not a claim that Western Han court service operated like a modern company.
- Zhang's entry route, his brother's name and role, exact offices and promotion sequence, animal-park titles, the Sima Gate procedure, the dowager's identity and wording, Baling construction details, speeches, and chronology remain episode-attributed.
