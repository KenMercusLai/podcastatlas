# 《资治通鉴·周纪》98丨历史上首个老赖登场了！

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode closes the show's 周纪 sequence by following [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]] in 256 BCE, after the [[Handan|邯郸]] relief and [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]]'s first rise in the previous episodes. [[MiWan|楚考烈王]] and [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]] try to turn the residual prestige of the [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]] into another anti-[[QinState|秦国]] coalition, but the weakened king has to borrow from wealthy households to field even a small army.

The effort collapses when promised support fails to materialize, leaving Zhou Nan Wang unable to repay his wartime loans and giving the episode its [[ZhaitaiGaozhu|债台高筑]] idiom story. Qin then forces the [[WesternZhouPolity|西周国]] side of the royal domain to surrender cities and population, Zhou Nan Wang dies at [[Luoyi|洛邑 / 洛阳]], and the chronicle frame moves from 周纪 into the coming Qin-centered sequence.

## Key Claims
- In Zhou Nan Wang's fifty-ninth year, 256 BCE, Qin pressure continues against [[HanState|韩国]] and [[ZhaoState|赵国]], including the seizure of Yangcheng and [[FushuCity|负黍]] from Han.
- [[MiWan|楚考烈王]] wants to exploit Qin's post-[[Handan|邯郸]] setback and accepts [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]]'s idea of calling the lords under the Zhou Son of Heaven's name.
- [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]] is vulnerable to that appeal because he has a title larger than his remaining material power; this creates a [[NominalAuthorityMobilizationTrap|nominal authority mobilization trap]].
- Zhou Nan Wang plans a force far beyond his means, borrows from domestic wealthy households, and promises repayment after victory.
- The anti-Qin coalition is thin in practice: the episode says only Chu and Yan act at first, Chu withdraws when the situation looks poor, and Zhou's small army collapses.
- The source gives [[ZhaitaiGaozhu|债台高筑]] as the story of Zhou Nan Wang hiding on a high palace platform from creditors after the failed expedition.
- [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] responds by forcing Zhou Nan Wang to apologize and surrender thirty-six cities and thirty thousand people; Qin then extinguishes [[WesternZhouPolity|西周国]] as a polity.
- The episode uses the end of Zhou Nan Wang and the closing of 周纪 to shift the narrative center toward Qin, while noting that [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]] is still a young child and the six-state wars are not yet complete.

## Key Quotes
> "天下共主" - the residual title logic used to draw Zhou Nan Wang into the coalition.

> "债台高筑" - the idiom the episode attaches to Zhou Nan Wang's failed war debt.

> "一念闪失" - the host's cautionary frame for late-life overreach.
