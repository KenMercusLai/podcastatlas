# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》610-2丨从一颗夜明珠到宫中神秘防腐法：古代皇帝驾崩后如何下葬？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] short episode backfills the burial-timing question around [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]: why an emperor who died in the sixth month could be buried only in the tenth month at Yiling. It contrasts common three-day or seven-day mourning customs with the longer imperial停灵 cycle, then explains extended imperial burial delay through [[ImperialFuneraryPreservation|帝王停灵防腐]] practices such as washing, wine disinfection, fragrant decoctions, clothing and grave goods, pearls or luminous pearls, aromatics, herbs, charcoal, ash, and sealed coffins.

The durable synthesis is that imperial burial timing combines ritual scale with preservation logistics. The episode treats "入土为安" as only one value inside a larger court procedure: imperial mourning requires time for ceremony, display, and burial preparation, so preservation techniques make the delay materially possible while symbolic grave goods make the body part of a ritual imagination of endurance.

## Key Claims

- The episode uses Han Aidi's sixth-month death and tenth-month burial to ask why imperial interment could be delayed for months.
- Common mourning customs such as three-day停灵 and "头七" are presented as a contrast to imperial burial delay, not as the imperial norm.
- Imperial funerals are described as larger and more procedurally complex than common funerals, with the coffin usually displayed for about three months.
- Long停灵 is made possible by preservation steps: hot-water washing, official body cleansing, liquor disinfection, fragrant bathing, aromatics, herbs, drying agents, and sealing.
- The episode identifies 裸人 or 御人 as officials associated with the bathing rite and cites Jia Gong's Yili commentary for fragrant herbs and wine in major mourning ritual.
- Pearls or luminous pearls are described as grave goods tied to a belief that they could help keep the corpse from decaying, with Cixi mentioned as a later example.
- Charcoal and plant ash are framed as practical coffin-sealing materials that block air and keep the coffin dry.

## Key Quotes

> "皇帝是六月份去世，一直到十月份才下葬" - the episode's starting puzzle.

> "帝皇的尸身在下葬之前是要经过特殊防腐处理的" - the preservation explanation.

> "口含夜明珠" - shorthand for the symbolic grave-good branch.
