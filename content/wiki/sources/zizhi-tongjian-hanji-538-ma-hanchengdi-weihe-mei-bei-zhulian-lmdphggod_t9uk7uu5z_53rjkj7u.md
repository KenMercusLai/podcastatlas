# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》538丨骂汉成帝，为何没被株连

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills Yongshi 2 / 15 BCE under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]], immediately before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-539-hanchengdi-yu-zhangfang-de-qinggan-jiuge-lk_wzo4h-c-xkipffhgikronrk3m|Hanji 539]]'s [[ZhangFangWesternHan|张放]] favorite-removal episode and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-540-taihou-de-zangli-lnd6gmr_bk7dv8cbb1zzoagtbwsd|Hanji 540]]'s funeral-accountability sequence. It centers on [[GuYongWesternHan|谷永]] as Liangzhou刺史, summoned after his capital report to explain repeated unusual natural phenomena and using the inquiry to attack Chengdi's women, drinking, spending, private outings, and self-destructive indulgence.

The durable synthesis is that harsh remonstrance survives here because speech is not judged only by its words. Gu Yong's memorial is severe enough to enrage Chengdi, but [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] and Wang-family senior relatives are presented as the hidden sponsors, [[WangShangWesternHan|王商]] warns Gu Yong to flee, and Chengdi's own tendency to cool down leaves the pursuit bounded rather than lethal.

## Key Claims

- In 15 BCE, Chengdi asks Gu Yong about repeated natural phenomena after Gu Yong finishes his capital reporting duties as Liangzhou刺史.
- Gu Yong uses the omen inquiry to accuse Chengdi of extreme female favor, private outings, drinking, fiscal waste, Changling-related burden, indulgence, and conduct that risks Heaven's displeasure.
- Chengdi is angered enough to order Gu Yong arrested, but the pursuit includes an escape boundary: if Gu Yong reaches Jiaodao, officials are not to continue chasing him.
- The episode says Gu Yong speaks so harshly because Wang Zhengjun and imperial uncles are worried about Chengdi's conduct but cannot comfortably remonstrate in their own names.
- Wang Shang secretly alerts Gu Yong to flee after Chengdi orders the arrest, showing that Gu Yong's courage is backed by palace-family sponsorship.
- Chengdi's lenient or slow-cooling temperament helps explain why the case does not become execution or collective punishment.
- The host frames Chengdi as personally kind enough not to kill a remonstrant yet politically immature: he cannot restrain desire, favorites, relatives, or the court costs of his conduct.

## Key Quotes

> "女宠之极" - the episode's summary of Gu Yong's accusation about Chengdi's consort favoritism.

> "自取灭亡" - the host's rendering of Gu Yong's harsh warning about continuing the same conduct.

> "像小孩子一样不知道如何运用" - the closing judgment on Chengdi's immature use of supreme power.
