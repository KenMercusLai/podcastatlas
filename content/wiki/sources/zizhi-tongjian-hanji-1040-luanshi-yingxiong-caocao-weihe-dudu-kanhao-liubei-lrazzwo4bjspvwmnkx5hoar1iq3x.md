# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》1040丨乱世英雄，曹操为何独独看好刘备？

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode continues the late-Han transition from [[LyuBu|吕布]]'s fall toward the [[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]] field by centering [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s assessment of [[LiuBei|刘备]]. It places the famous "煮酒论英雄" scene inside the [[YidaiZhao|衣带诏]] conspiracy: [[DongChengLateHan|董承]] and Liu Bei are involved in anti-Cao plotting, yet Cao Cao still names Liu Bei and himself as the only true heroes.

The episode argues that Cao Cao values Liu Bei because his future threat is visible beneath current weakness: ambition, resilience, emotional control, and willingness to bend after defeat matter more than immediate troop count. Cao Cao later sends Liu Bei and [[ZhuLingLateHan|朱灵]] to intercept [[YuanShu|袁术]] despite objections from [[ChengYu|程昱]], [[GuoJia|郭嘉]], and [[DongZhaoLateHan|董昭]]; Liu Bei then kills [[CheZhouLateHan|车胄]], retakes [[Xuzhou|徐州]], leaves [[GuanYu|关羽]] at [[Xiapi|下邳]], returns to [[PeiCounty|小沛]], gathers support from [[ChangXiLateHan|昌豨]], and opens contact with [[YuanShao|袁绍]].

## Key Claims
- True hero status is framed as ambition plus strategy plus composure: a person who can remain steady under honor, insult, fear, or anger has more durable political capacity than someone with only visible force.
- The [[YidaiZhao|衣带诏]] plot makes the "煮酒论英雄" scene dangerous for Liu Bei, because he has already joined or discussed a plan against [[CaoCao|曹操]] through [[DongChengLateHan|董承]].
- Liu Bei's dropped chopsticks and thunder explanation become a case of [[CrisisComposureCoverStory|临场定力与借势遮掩]]: visible panic is quickly converted into a plausible harmless reaction.
- Cao Cao's high regard for Liu Bei rests on [[LatentRivalRecognition|潜在强敌识别]] rather than present power; Liu Bei arrives with little force but a reputation for loyalty, toughness, low-posture survival, and recovery after defeat.
- The source contrasts Liu Bei with [[YuanShao|袁绍]], Yuan Shu, [[SunCe|孙策]], [[LiuBiao|刘表]], [[LiuZhang|刘璋]], [[ZhangLuFivePecks|张鲁]], and [[MaTeng|马腾]], saying Cao Cao treats them as less threatening or less complete than Liu Bei.
- Cheng Yu, Guo Jia, and Dong Zhao warn that letting Liu Bei leave with troops is dangerous; Cao Cao's later regret turns the decision into a source case of release-risk after protected shelter.
- Liu Bei validates those warnings by killing Che Zhou, recovering Xuzhou, gaining East Sea support from Chang Xi and other counties, and secretly coordinating with Yuan Shao.
- Cao Cao's first counterattack through [[LiuDaiSikongZhangshi|刘岱]] and [[WangZhongLateHan|王忠]] fails, while the Dong Cheng conspiracy is exposed in early 200 and brings executions of Dong Cheng, [[WangFuDongChengPlot|王服]], [[ZhongJiLateHan|种辑]], and [[WuZilanLateHan|吴子兰]].
