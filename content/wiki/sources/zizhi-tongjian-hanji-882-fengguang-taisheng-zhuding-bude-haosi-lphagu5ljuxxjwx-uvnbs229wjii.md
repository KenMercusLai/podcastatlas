# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》882丨风光太盛，注定不得好死？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the 168 CE accession-reward moment before the [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] / [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] anti-eunuch crisis covered in Hanji 884-887. It shows Dou Wu reaching a dangerous prestige peak after [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s accession, with [[LuZhiLateHan|卢植]] warning him to decline excessive hereditary reward because the succession choice was genealogy and public recommendation, not Dou's sole merit.

The episode then contrasts Dou Wu's unreceptive posture with Chen Fan's refusal of [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦妙 / 窦皇太后]]'s favor-based marquisate. The host uses [[Analects|《论语》]] to frame wealth and rank as legitimate only when obtained in the right way, making the source a new bridge between late-Han title politics, [[RighteousnessOverProfit|义利之辨]], [[MeritBasedRewardPunishment|因功赏罚]], and [[UnearnedRewardRefusal|非道所得不居]].

## Key Claims

- The accession settlement rewards Dou Wu with Wenxi marquis status, his son Dou Ji with Weiyang marquis status, and his nephews Dou Shao and Dou Jing with additional honors, while [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] also receives Changanxiang marquis status.
- Lu Zhi warns Dou Wu that selecting Liu Hong as successor was based on genealogy and public recommendation rather than Dou Wu's unique accomplishment, so receiving excessive reward will damage humility, reputation, and future safety.
- The episode presents Lu Zhi as a disciplined scholar-official whose early conduct under Ma Rong, later withdrawal from official summons, and teaching role before young [[LiuBei|刘备]] all support his authority as a moral adviser.
- Empress Dowager Dou Miao wants to reward Chen Fan because he helped make her empress under [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]], but Chen Fan refuses the title repeatedly.
- Chen Fan argues that land, title, and food revenue must follow public merit or proper royal favor, not a private favor from an empress dowager.
- The host uses the Analects line about wealth and rank obtained by the right path to argue that dignity and legitimacy outrank status, money, or visibility.
- The closing note says [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]] attacks the Qiang again and wins, setting up the next episode's debate over surrender and continued suppression.
