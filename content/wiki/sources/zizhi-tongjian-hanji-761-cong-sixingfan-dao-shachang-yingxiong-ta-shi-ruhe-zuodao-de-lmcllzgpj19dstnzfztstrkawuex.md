## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode backfills the first [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]] Northern Xiongnu campaign layer before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-762-lishi-gaosu-ni-nanren-youquan-neng-you-duo-huangtang-limdo2hfd8epcfkwwsrmzz9no0dj|Hanji 762]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-763-gaixie-lishi-donghan-douxian-po-bei-xiongnu-zhi-zhan-ltlctmb-iprvggoit46-qohm0tb|Hanji 763]]. Court critics oppose the expedition because of imperial mourning, spring farming, logistics, public burden, and the [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|Northern Xiongnu]]'s distance from the frontier, while [[EmpressDowagerDouHeLateHan|Empress Dowager Dou]] ignores the memorials and orders luxury mansions for [[DouDuEasternHan|Dou Du]] and [[DouJingEasternHan|Dou Jing]].

The durable synthesis is that the celebrated Yanran victory begins inside a damaged court feedback system. [[ZhiShouEasternHan|Zhi Shou]] publicly refuses Dou Xian's private request and is pushed from death sentence to exile only after [[HeChangEasternHan|He Chang]]'s rescue memorial, while the later victory at [[JiluoshanBattleLateHan|Jiluoshan]] and [[YanranInscription|Yanran inscription]] strengthens the Dou brothers' military and palace-guard power.

## Key Claims

- The episode frames Dou Xian's campaign through the question of how rich and powerful people can end well, then contrasts that ideal with Dou-family bullying, luxury, and suppression of critics.
- Officials oppose attacking Northern Xiongnu because the court is still in mourning, spring conscription disrupts farming, the enemy has already been pushed far away by Xianbei pressure, and the campaign lacks a direct rebellion justification.
- [[HanLengEasternHan|Han Leng]], [[ZhuHuiHeEraLateHan|Zhu Hui]], and [[LeHuiEasternHan|Le Hui]] submit further objections, but Empress Dowager Dou does not accept them.
- He Chang's memorial joins war restraint to anti-luxury criticism: Northern Xiongnu has not committed a fresh offense, while Dou Du and Dou Jing's mansions burden commoners during fiscal and military strain.
- Zhi Shou makes private pressure public by refusing Dou Xian's letter, sending the messenger to prison, and repeatedly attacking Dou's campaign and mansion-building before the court.
- Dou Xian retaliates by accusing Zhi Shou of buying public land and slandering the court; the dowager favors Dou, and Zhi Shou receives a death sentence.
- He Chang's second memorial argues that correcting great ministers is Zhi Shou's duty and that killing him for "slander" would damage loyal speech; the sentence is reduced to exile, but Zhi Shou kills himself before departure.
- The expedition proceeds in summer: Dou Xian, Geng Bing, the Southern Xiongnu chanyu, and Deng Hong advance by separate routes toward Zhuoye Mountain.
- Dou Xian's force under Yan Pan, Geng Kui, and Geng Tan defeats the Northern Xiongnu chanyu at Jiluoshan, pursues to Siqu Beihai, records large casualties and captures, and receives surrender from many tribes.
- The victory ends with Dou Xian and Geng Bing going more than three thousand li beyond the frontier to Yanran Mountain, where Ban Gu is ordered to inscribe the achievement; afterward the Dou brothers monopolize court power more openly.
