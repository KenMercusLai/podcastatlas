# 21.红与黑：他死于真诚

## Summary
This [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] episode rereads [[Stendhal|斯汤达]]'s [[RedAndBlackNovel|《红与黑》 / The Red and the Black]] through [[JulienSorel|Julien Sorel / 于连]]'s class ambition, emotional volatility, and longing for sincerity. It argues that reducing Julien to a "bad man" or social climber misses the novel's [[PsychologicalRealism|psychological realism]], its [[ClassMobilityPerformance|class mobility performance]], and its portrait of a young person trying to remain human inside [[FrenchRestoration|Restoration France]]'s closed, hypocritical order. The discussion also treats [[MadameDeRenal|Madame de Renal / 雷奈尔夫人]] and [[MathildeDeLaMole|Mathilde de La Mole / 马蒂尔德]] as full subjects rather than moral symbols or plot devices.

## Key Claims
- The episode treats [[RedAndBlackNovel|《红与黑》]] as a novel whose force lies less in plot surprise than in how psychology, social pressure, ambition, shame, and love generate action.
- [[Stendhal|斯汤达]] is presented as a writer formed by the [[NapoleonBonaparte|Napoleonic]] era and later misread or underrecognized by parts of his own literary field, including [[SainteBeuve|Sainte-Beuve / 圣伯夫]].
- [[JulienSorel|Julien]]'s admiration for [[NapoleonBonaparte|Napoleon]] and his turn toward a clerical career show how [[FrenchRestoration|Restoration France]] narrows legitimate routes of upward mobility.
- Julien's religious performance, Paris manners, and calculated humility are not simple hypocrisy; the source reads them as [[ClassMobilityPerformance]] under a society that rewards masks.
- The relationship with [[MadameDeRenal|Madame de Renal]] begins in conquest, pride, and resentment, but becomes the book's most sincere and consoling bond.
- The relationship with [[MathildeDeLaMole|Mathilde]] is a struggle of pride, romance, role-playing, jealousy, and aristocratic imagination rather than a simple seduction plot.
- Julien's shooting of Madame de Renal is condemned as violent and self-destructive, but the episode reads it as the moment when [[SincerityAgainstSocialHypocrisy]] overwhelms calculation and exposes why he cannot become a fully cold opportunist.
- The red/black contrast is not only military uniform versus clerical robe; the episode also reads it as passion versus control, life versus death, blood versus restraint, and even the two women's different emotional worlds.
- The source explicitly resists flattening [[MadameDeRenal|Madame de Renal]] into saintliness and [[MathildeDeLaMole|Mathilde]] into danger; both characters retain agency, contradiction, and [[FemaleSubjectivityInClassicReading]].
- [[WilliamSomersetMaugham|William Somerset Maugham / 毛姆]] is used as a productive critical foil: his dislike of the second half clarifies the tension between strategic self-control and [[PassionAgainstSelfControl|passionate self-disclosure]].

## Key Quotes
> "宁愿死一千次也要飞黄腾达" - the episode's shorthand for Julien's early upward hunger.

> "旧社会把人变成鬼" - the host's social diagnosis of Julien's deformation.

> "在变成鬼的过程中始终不愿放弃当人" - the episode's core defense of Julien's remaining humanity.
