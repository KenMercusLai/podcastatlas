# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》155-2｜转给你脾气大的朋友：魏王叛汉竟是刘邦“爱骂人”

## Summary
This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode completes the [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-155-1-lishi-jizai-shenme-qingkuang-hui-fasheng-ren-chi-ren-canju-lrwz-prnxd8hzchagyrbywkux7lh|Hanji 155-1]] setup by narrating [[HanXin|韩信]]'s campaign against [[WeiBao|魏豹]] after Wei Bao defects from [[LiuBang|刘邦]] back toward [[XiangYu|项羽]]. It frames the defection through status injury as well as power calculation, then shows Han Xin using a visible crossing threat at [[LinjinWarringStates|临晋]] / [[PubanWarringStates|蒲阪]] and a real northern crossing at [[XiayangChuHan|下阳]] to seize [[Anyi|安邑]]. The episode then follows the northern strategic extension toward [[DaiState|代国]], [[ZhaoState|赵国]], [[YanState|燕国]], and [[QiState|齐国]], while noting how Liu Bang both empowers and drains Han Xin's independent army.

## Key Claims
- [[XiaoHe|萧何]] remains the rear-administration anchor after Liu Bang returns to the [[XingyangStandoff|荥阳相持]] front: he manages Guanzhong laws, records, supplies, and reserve manpower while [[LiuYing|刘盈]] stays behind as crown prince.
- Wei Bao explains his refusal to return to Liu Bang through humiliation: Liu Bang is described as arrogant and coarse toward lords and ministers, making Wei Bao's renewed break a status-injury extension of [[PowerBasedAllianceRepricing|实力重估式阵营转向]].
- Liu Bang appoints Han Xin to attack Wei with [[GuanYing|灌婴]] and [[CaoShen|曹参]] assisting; after hearing from [[LiShiqi|郦食其]] that [[BoZhi|柏直]], [[FengJingChuHan|冯敬]], and [[XiangTuoChuHan|项佗]] lead Wei's forces, Liu Bang judges that they cannot match Han Xin and his deputies.
- Han Xin separately asks whether Wei is using [[ZhouShu|周叔]]; when the answer is no, he also treats Wei's command choice as weak.
- Han Xin holds Wei Bao's attention at the expected Yellow River crossing by arranging ships near Linjin and Puban, then moves the real crossing north to Xiayang using wooden boards and jar-floats.
- The hidden crossing lets Han Xin strike Anyi, forcing Wei Bao to abandon the frontal defense and turn back in disorder.
- Cao Shen and Guan Ying convert the crossing into encirclement pressure: Cao Shen takes Anyi and defeats Wei forces, while Guan Ying's cavalry follows across the river and helps chase Wei Bao through further defeats.
- By the ninth month, Wei Bao is captured and sent to Xingyang; Han converts Wei territory into [[HedongCommandery|河东郡]], [[Shangdang|上党郡]], and [[TaiyuanWarringStates|太原郡]], extending [[ConquestCommanderyTransition|灭国后的郡县化过渡]].
- [[ChenYu|陈馀]]'s later discovery that [[ZhangEr|张耳]] is alive turns Liu Bang's earlier substitute-head deception into another trust rupture, pushing Chen Yu and [[ZhaoXie|赵歇]] back toward Chu.
- After pacifying Wei, Han Xin requests thirty thousand troops and proposes a northern plan: take Zhao and Yan, move east against Qi, and then cut Chu's grain route from the north.
- Liu Bang accepts the northern push and sends Zhang Er to assist Han Xin, but repeatedly removes elite soldiers from Han Xin's army for the Xingyang front, limiting Han Xin's ability to execute the full southward grain-line severance.
- Han Xin defeats Dai forces around [[EyuBattle|阏与]], takes [[Jinyang|晋阳]], and sets up Taiyuan commandery; the grain-line mission later shifts toward [[PengYue|彭越]] with support from [[LiuJia|刘贾]] and [[LuWan|卢绾]].

## Key Quotes
> "像骂奴隶一样侮辱人" - the source's phrasing for Wei Bao's complaint about Liu Bang's treatment of lords and ministers.

> "声南击北" - the host's summary of Han Xin's campaign logic against Wei Bao.

> "没有完成最后一步" - the source's judgment that Liu Bang's elite-troop withdrawals prevented Han Xin from finishing the southern grain-line cut.
