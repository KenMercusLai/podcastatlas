# 《资治通鉴·汉纪》388-1｜杀妻杀子之后，汉武帝为何族灭大舅子李广利？

## Summary

This [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode supplies the missing prequel to [[LiGuangliWesternHan|李广利]]'s failed late-[[HanWudi|汉武帝]] expedition. In 90 BCE, Wudi sends Li Guangli, [[ShangqiuChengWesternHan|商丘成]], and [[MaTongWesternHan|马通]] on separate anti-[[Xiongnu|匈奴]] routes after Xiongnu attacks Five Plains and Jiuquan; Li initially wins, but his campaign is overtaken by court politics.

The decisive pressure comes from Li Guangli's succession bargain with [[LiuQuliWesternHan|刘屈氂]]. Because Li's sister [[LadyLiWesternHan|李夫人]] had borne [[LiuBoChangyiKingWesternHan|昌邑王刘髆]], Li and Liu Quli want Liu Bo made crown prince; after Guo Rang reports curse-and-prayer charges, Liu Quli is executed and Li Guangli's wife and children are arrested. Li tries to win enough frontier merit to save them, kills a suspicious army officer, loses military trust, falls into Xiongnu traps, surrenders, and is answered by Wudi's extermination of the Li family.

## Key Claims

- Li Guangli's defeat is presented as a three-way collision of frontier warfare, succession ambition, and family hostage pressure.
- The 90 BCE expedition starts with multi-route Han retaliation for Xiongnu incursions into Five Plains and Jiuquan.
- Li Guangli initially performs well in battle, defeating Xiongnu forces near Fuyang and advancing to Fan Furen City.
- Li Guangli and Liu Quli's shared interest in making Liu Bo crown prince turns the military campaign into a succession-risk case.
- Guo Rang's accusation that Liu Quli's household and Li Guangli prayed for Liu Bo's accession converts private factional hopes into treason and Wugu-adjacent court danger.
- Li Guangli's attempt to deepen the campaign for redemptive merit destabilizes his army and makes ordinary retreat harder.
- The final surrender follows cascading pressures: family arrest, officer suspicion, killing the changshi, loss of military confidence, Xiongnu pursuit, traps, and likely execution if he returned.
