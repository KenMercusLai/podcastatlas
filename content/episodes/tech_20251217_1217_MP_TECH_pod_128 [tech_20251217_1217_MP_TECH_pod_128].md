# Tech Hiring Stabilizes, but Remains Far Below Pre-Pandemic Levels

## 概览

Marketplace Tech examines how the tech job market ended 2025, focusing on an Indeed jobs and hiring trends report and an interview with Indeed senior economist Corey Staley.

The core finding is mixed: tech job postings stopped falling dramatically, but remain well below their February 2020 baseline. Staley says Indeed’s tech job posting index is at 67.2, about 33% below pre-pandemic levels, while health care is about 23% above its pre-pandemic level.

The episode argues that AI is creating demand in some areas, especially AI, machine learning, and data engineering, but this growth has not been broad enough to revive tech hiring overall. Staley also cautions against attributing weak software developer demand only to AI, pointing to pandemic-era overhiring and a broader “low fire” labor market.

## 播客点评/总结

This episode is valuable because it separates tech-sector hype from labor-market reality. Its strongest point is the use of concrete Indeed figures, especially the 67.2 tech posting index and the comparison with health care’s stronger hiring position.

The discussion is especially useful for software developers, students, and early-career tech workers because it explains why the market can feel weak even while AI investment and tech stocks are prominent. It also gives a practical map of where demand is stronger: AI, machine learning, and data engineering.

A limitation is that the conversation relies mainly on Indeed job posting data and does not deeply examine wages, regional differences, company size, or worker outcomes after layoffs. [推测] Listeners looking for career strategy may need additional sources before deciding whether to reskill or shift fields.

[推测] The episode is best suited for listeners who want a concise labor-market read on tech hiring, rather than a technical explanation of AI or a detailed job-search guide.
