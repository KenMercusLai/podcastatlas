# Introducing Revisionist History Season Four

## 概览

Malcolm Gladwell previews the fourth season of *Revisionist History* through a rapid montage of conversations, subjects, and comic exchanges. The trailer moves from tea tasting and music theory to law-school admissions and language pronunciation.

Rather than presenting one unifying theme, Gladwell emphasizes the season’s range: Rome, Jesuits, chess masters, mafiosi, and lawyers all appear among its subjects. He also jokes about the unusual situations podcasting creates for him.

The season is announced for June 20, with listeners invited to subscribe through their preferred podcast platform.

## 播客点评/总结

This trailer’s main strength is its energetic presentation of intellectual variety. Short excerpts involving tea, heavy metal, standardized testing, and pronunciation quickly communicate the show’s willingness to follow unusual questions across unrelated fields.

Gladwell’s self-aware humor gives the preview a playful tone, especially when he admits that the season lacks an obvious theme and highlights the odd situations created by podcasting.

[推测] Because this is a short promotional trailer rather than a full episode, it offers little detail about the arguments or conclusions of the coming stories. It is best suited to existing listeners and newcomers who enjoy wide-ranging historical, cultural, and analytical storytelling.
