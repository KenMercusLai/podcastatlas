# How AI Money Is Shaping U.S. Elections

## 概览

AI has emerged as a major election issue and source of political spending. Industry-linked groups are investing heavily in races that could influence how artificial intelligence and the data centers supporting it are regulated.

The episode contrasts Leading the Future, which is connected to OpenAI and favors less regulation, with Public First Action, which has ties to Anthropic and supports a more pro-regulation position. Their spending has sometimes overshadowed the candidates and local donors involved in individual races.

These groups are borrowing tactics from the cryptocurrency industry: establishing networks of super PACs, supporting or opposing candidates directly, and operating across party lines. Yet the politics of AI are becoming more polarized as data centers—and questions about their power use and costs—gain public attention.

## 分段落总结

[01:05] **AI becomes a major force in election spending**

[事实] AI-focused political groups are spending heavily to shape elections and competing approaches to AI regulation.

[事实] The debate covers both regulation of AI technology and the data centers needed to power it.

[事实] OpenAI president Greg Brockman and his wife withdrew a planned $25 million donation to Leading the Future after an earlier $25 million contribution, with Brockman saying the PAC had become a distraction at OpenAI.

[02:28] **Vague super PAC names obscure industry connections**

[事实] Brendan Glavin of OpenSecrets says names such as “Jobs and Democracy PAC” do not reveal that a group is involved in AI policy unless voters investigate it.

[事实] Leading the Future is connected to OpenAI and takes a more anti-regulation position.

[事实] Public First has connections to Anthropic and promotes a more pro-regulation position.

[03:13] **AI groups turn a New York primary into a regulatory battleground**

[事实] New York congressional candidate Alex Bores had proposed AI-regulation legislation while serving in the state legislature.

[事实] Leading the Future and associated super PACs spent heavily attacking Bores, while Public First supported him.

[事实] Glavin says AI interests came close to overshadowing the candidates themselves in the primary.

[03:55] **Outside spending nationalizes local races**

[事实] In a South Dakota Senate race, AI super PAC spending exceeded the amount contributed by South Dakota residents.

[事实] Glavin says well-funded outside groups can dwarf spending by the candidates themselves.

[事实] This involvement can nationalize a race, reflecting a broader trend in which industries and single-issue groups intervene directly in primary elections.

[05:52] **AI adopts the cryptocurrency industry’s political playbook**

[事实] Cryptocurrency interests established Fairshake and two affiliated super PACs, an approach viewed as successful in the 2024 election cycle.

[事实] AI interests have adopted a substantially similar organizational structure.

[事实] Instead of primarily donating to parties or party-aligned super PACs, these groups identify candidates to support or oppose and spend directly in their races.

[事实] Glavin says political spending techniques are often copied once other industries perceive them as effective.

[07:06] **AI politics span both parties but are becoming polarized**

[事实] AI political organizations use separate PACs to participate in Democratic and Republican races.

[事实] Glavin says AI has retained a bipartisan element, but its politics are becoming more polarized during the general election.

[事实] He observes that the pro-AI position is beginning to lean more Republican than Democratic.

[事实] Data centers have become a central AI-related political issue and are contributing to the weakening of bipartisan alignment.

[08:05] **Industry support can become a political liability**

[事实] Candidates cannot control which outside groups support or oppose them, but they may still be associated with that spending and face questions about it.

[事实] Glavin says politicians may not have anticipated that AI-industry support could become a liability.

[事实] Growing public attention to data centers is increasing that potential liability.

[08:51] **AI ranks among the election cycle’s biggest spenders**

[事实] Glavin identifies AIPAC and related groups, cryptocurrency interests, and AI interests as the three leading outside-spending blocs during the primary season.

[事实] Party-related groups may dominate more spending as the November election approaches.

[事实] AI and cryptocurrency groups are nevertheless expected to remain among the election cycle’s top spenders.

## 播客点评/总结

The episode’s main strength is its concise explanation of how AI companies and their allies are translating technological influence into electoral influence. Concrete examples from New York and South Dakota show how national industry money can reshape individual races.

Its comparison with cryptocurrency spending is especially useful because it places AI super PACs within a broader evolution of campaign strategy: industries are increasingly bypassing traditional party channels and intervening directly for or against candidates.

[推测] The short format leaves limited room to examine donor transparency, the specific content of campaign advertising, or the policy differences between the groups in detail. It is best suited to listeners seeking a clear introduction to the intersection of AI policy, data-center politics, and campaign finance.
