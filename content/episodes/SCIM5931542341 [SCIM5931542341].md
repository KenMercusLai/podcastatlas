# Young Blood, Organ Clocks, and the Biology of Healthspan

## 概览

This episode is a conversation between Andrew Huberman and Dr. Tony Weiss-Corey about aging as a biological process that can be measured, and possibly modified, through circulating factors in blood, cerebrospinal fluid, and other physiological signals. The central thread is that young blood, exercise-conditioned blood, and some fluid-borne proteins can alter brain and organ function in animal models, while human translation remains promising but not yet definitive.

The discussion repeatedly distinguishes lifespan from healthspan. Weiss-Corey emphasizes that no human intervention has been validated to extend lifespan, while exercise and diet have stronger evidence for health-related benefits than supplements or unapproved interventions.

A major theme is measurement: proteins in blood can be used to estimate the biological age of organs and potentially cell types, and organ-specific age gaps may predict future disease risk. The conversation closes by connecting these tools to future precision interventions, especially for neurodegeneration, ALS, Alzheimer's disease, and monogenic diseases.

## 分段落总结

[00:00] **Episode Frame: Young Factors, Brain Aging, and Healthspan**

[事实] The opening teaser says young-organism factors given to an old brain reactivated brain stem cells in mice, reduced inflammation, increased measurable brain activity, and improved memory.
[事实] Huberman introduces Weiss-Corey as a Stanford neurology professor focused on factors that may prevent or reverse organ degeneration and aging.
[事实] The planned topics include young blood, exercise-induced blood factors, nonlinear aging, organ-specific aging, sunlight, fasting, hormones, exercise, and social interaction.

[03:01] **Parabiosis and Young Blood in Mice**

[事实] The modern work referenced uses parabiosis, surgically pairing young and old mice so their circulation exchanges blood.
[事实] Tom Rando used this model to show that young circulation helped old mouse muscle regenerate and look closer to young muscle.
[事实] Weiss-Corey’s group extended the question to the brain, finding reactivated brain stem cells, less inflammation, more neuronal activity, and improved memory in old mice exposed to young factors.
[推测] The mouse findings make blood-borne factors plausible causal candidates, not only biomarkers of aging.

[06:26] **Blood-Borne Factors as Active Signals**

[事实] Weiss-Corey says age-related blood proteins do not only reflect body status; they can influence cell and organ function.
[事实] His group measured thousands of proteins across many individuals and could use protein patterns to estimate approximate age.
[事实] The field is trying to identify which circulating factors could slow aging or keep organs healthier.

[08:44] **Human Translation and Plasma Trials**

[事实] Alkahest tested whether human blood factors could mimic young mouse blood in mouse models and found young human blood had similar effects.
[事实] Clinical work included small trials in Alzheimer’s and Parkinson’s using plasma fractions that had effects in mice.
[事实] A 500-patient blinded placebo-controlled Alzheimer’s plasma-exchange study reported significant benefits.
[事实] Weiss-Corey says the field still needs large clinical studies capable of supporting FDA approval.

[15:36] **Beneficial and Detrimental Blood Factors**

[事实] With age, inflammatory proteins increase; in mice, knocking out or neutralizing some such factors improved cognition.
[事实] Young blood also contains beneficial growth-related factors that may stimulate cells and help maintain stem cells.
[事实] Whole-organism analyses show many cell types change gene expression in response to young blood; stem cells and mitochondria appear to be important targets.
[推测] A future therapy is more likely to require a cocktail or organ-specific combination than one universal molecule.

[20:14] **Blood Banking and Blood Lore**

[事实] Weiss-Corey says people likely do not need to bank their own blood for these studies because pooled young blood still showed beneficial effects.
[事实] On Dracula and blood drinking, he says his group has not fed mice young blood and does not know whether orally consumed factors would survive digestion.
[推测] The historical fascination with blood may reflect recognition that blood is essential to life, but the transcript does not establish a scientific origin for vampire lore.

[23:42] **Organs Age at Different Rates**

[事实] Research in worms and molecular studies in animals indicate organs and cell types can age at different rates.
[事实] In mice, some tissues stay stable for long periods before declining, while others decline from early adulthood or maintain function until late life.
[事实] In humans, blood proteomics can identify proteins originating from the brain, liver, lung, heart, and other organs.
[事实] Organ age gap, the difference between chronological age and estimated organ age, predicts future disease risk in the same organ, such as heart disease, kidney disease, or Alzheimer’s disease.

[28:24] **Vero Compass and Organ-Age Testing**

[事实] Weiss-Corey says he co-founded Vero Biosciences with Paul Coletta to profile organ age and predict or respond to chronic disease risk.
[事实] The Vero Compass combines biological signatures with clinical and wearable data to identify sensitive organs, guide interventions, and retest whether organ age changes.
[事实] Potential interventions discussed include standard medical treatment, lifestyle changes, and exercise tailored to a person’s specific profile.
[推测] The underlying model is iterative prevention: measure risk early, intervene, then check whether the organ-level signal improves.

[30:43] **Precision Trials and Repurposed Treatments**

[事实] Weiss-Corey says complex diseases such as Alzheimer’s likely include different forms and risk profiles.
[事实] He argues drugs may fail when tested across all diagnosed patients, especially late in disease, even if they might help a narrower subgroup.
[事实] Huberman gives the example of David Fajgenbaum and Every Cure using approved drugs and AI to seek treatments for difficult diseases.
[推测] Organ and protein profiling could help match existing or new therapies to patient subgroups more effectively.

[33:05] **NAD, NMN, and Supplement Claims**

[事实] Huberman asks about NAD, NMN, NR, and whether increasing NAD can extend lifespan in humans or models.
[事实] Weiss-Corey says no human intervention has been tested and validated to extend lifespan.
[事实] He notes NMN and related metabolites have shown benefits in animal models, and at least one clinical study showed supplementation can raise blood levels, but not lifespan or clear frailty outcomes.
[事实] He cautions that many supplement products may not contain what their labels claim and recommends third-party testing and attention to stability.

[36:55] **Vitality Versus Longevity**

[事实] Huberman frames puberty, testosterone, estrogen, growth hormone, and IGF-1 as factors that can increase vitality but may not increase lifespan.
[事实] Weiss-Corey connects this to antagonistic pleiotropy: something beneficial when young can be detrimental when old.
[事实] He says blood composition changes in waves, with a first major wave around age 35 to 40 in both men and women.
[事实] He emphasizes that animal lifespan extension studies do not always measure whether extended life is active and vital.

[45:24] **Sunlight, Hormones, and Candidate Molecules**

[事实] Huberman discusses a Swedish sunlight study and argues sunlight may support vitality, though he notes outdoor activity and other variables complicate interpretation.
[事实] The conversation returns to growth hormone and IGF-1 as vitality-promoting factors that may carry lifespan tradeoffs.
[事实] Candidate blood-borne factors named include GDF-11, IGF-1, clusterin/apolipoprotein J, GPLDH, and Closso.
[推测] The discussion treats sunlight and hormones as examples where short-term vitality signals may not automatically translate into longer life.

[47:28] **Exercise-Conditioned Blood**

[事实] Weiss-Corey describes experiments where blood from exercised mice was injected into non-exercised mice and transmitted beneficial brain effects.
[事实] In one version, exercised young blood had stronger effects on old brains than young blood alone.
[事实] His lab identified clusterin, a blood protein also called apolipoprotein J, and showed recombinant clusterin could mimic some exercise effects in mice.
[事实] Saul Villeta’s group identified GPLDH, and later caloric restriction experiments also suggested plasma factors can carry benefits.

[51:21] **Movement and Brain Robustness**

[事实] Huberman discusses the idea that movement may send body-to-brain signals helping preserve brain robustness.
[事实] Weiss-Corey notes there are thousands of studies showing exercise benefits, including cardiovascular and other exercise.
[事实] Weiss-Corey says his own exercise is mainly outdoor running, aiming for two 5-10K runs per week, plus some Pilates in the morning.
[推测] The discussion implies that movement may support brain health through both behavioral and biochemical feedback, but exact mechanisms remain unresolved.

[54:03] **Aging, Injury, and Healing**

[事实] Weiss-Corey says aging of the immune system shifts responses from more specific to more nonspecific, often inflammatory responses.
[事实] He suggests aged wound healing may involve too much inflammation, too little healing response, and extracellular matrix proteins such as collagens that interfere with rapid repair.
[事实] Huberman notes that mouth wounds and wounds in babies can heal with little or no scarring compared with older adults.
[推测] The transcript does not identify a single cause of better oral or early-life wound healing; saliva and blood supply are raised as possibilities, not conclusions.

[56:13] **Clinical Proof and Safety**

[事实] Weiss-Corey says the field should focus on healthspan: preserving organ function and cognition until death, not merely extending years.
[事实] Establishing causation requires animal experiments to isolate factors, followed by blinded, controlled, sufficiently large human clinical trials.
[事实] He says individual molecules such as Closso and inhibitors of detrimental factors may reach human trials, but no single factor is likely to have a miracle effect across all tissues.
[事实] Huberman and Weiss-Corey warn against unproven stem-cell interventions, with Weiss-Corey saying such treatments have not been rigorously tested and should be approached cautiously.

[62:38] **PRP and Exosomes**

[事实] Weiss-Corey says platelet-rich plasma does not contain stem cells but does contain platelets rich in growth factors involved in wound healing.
[事实] He says PRP is often used for sports injuries, usually from a person’s own blood, by concentrating platelets and releasing their factors.
[事实] Exosomes are described as small vesicle packages containing proteins, RNA molecules, lipids, and metabolites, released by cells into blood.
[事实] Weiss-Corey says exosomes may deliver information between cells and may be useful for treatment or diagnostics, but the field is still new.

[65:47] **Environmental Exposures and Food Quality**

[事实] Huberman lists smoking as damaging DNA, increasing inflammation, and shortening life, and asks about cumulative low-dose exposures such as EMFs.
[事实] Weiss-Corey says the same question applies to invented chemicals in food and environmental exposures, including plastics, and that long-term combined effects are uncertain.
[事实] Both discuss avoiding plastics where possible, choosing fresh foods, and the uncertainty around organic versus non-organic produce.
[事实] Huberman mentions endocrine disruptors and crop spraying as concerns associated with rural areas and Parkinson’s disease risk.

[73:16] **Fasting and Caloric Restriction**

[事实] Weiss-Corey says intermittent fasting has no single definition and calls the field messy because mouse findings do not translate cleanly to humans.
[事实] He says there are no clinical studies showing clear fasting benefits in humans, and some monkey studies suggest fasting may be detrimental.
[事实] Animal studies show calorie reduction can affect inflammation, energy metabolism, protein turnover, cellular waste handling, and oxidative damage.
[事实] He has tried Walter Longo’s calorie-reduced, ketogenic-style diet for about five days, and says his regular practice rarely involves intermittent fasting.

[78:14] **Meal Timing and Constant Snacking**

[事实] Huberman describes consuming caffeine, electrolytes, and water until about 10 or 11 a.m. and eating no later than about 7 p.m.
[事实] Weiss-Corey says a roughly 12-hour period without food is a kind of fast and likely triggers metabolic activity different from continuous eating.
[事实] He says eating throughout the whole day is probably the worst pattern because human bodies evolved with regular periods without food.
[推测] The practical message is moderate meal timing and avoiding constant snacking, not a specific fasting prescription.

[79:18] **Sleep, CSF, and Cognitive Resilience**

[事实] Huberman raises sleep and glymphatic clearance, then asks whether Weiss-Corey has studied lymph between young and old animals.
[事实] Weiss-Corey says his group studied cerebrospinal fluid instead; young CSF infused into old mice over about a month improved brain regeneration and cognitive function.
[事实] Oligodendrocytes, cells that wrap neuronal connections, were the strongest target in that CSF experiment.
[事实] In human CSF from about 3,000 individuals, ratios of synaptic proteins strongly predicted cognitive resilience or decline, independent of pathological markers.

[84:31] **Exercise Types and Lac-Phe**

[事实] Huberman cites studies where pole vaulters, gymnasts, high jumpers, and sprinters show substantial longevity advantages compared with age-matched cohorts and other athletes.
[事实] Weiss-Corey describes John Long’s work on metabolites in sprinter dogs, racehorses, and human sprinters.
[事实] Long’s group identified a modified amino acid conjugated to lactate, called lac-phe, that spikes with extreme bursts of muscle activity and shows beneficial effects in animals.
[推测] Different exercise modes may produce different molecular signals, so exercise should not be treated as one uniform intervention.

[89:17] **Choice, Stress, and Hard Effort**

[事实] Huberman describes a rodent study in which voluntary wheel running improved health markers, while forced running produced stress markers, blood pressure increases, and memory deficits.
[事实] He also describes Stanford work on the anterior mid-cingulate cortex, linking stimulation and growth of this region with tenacity, dieting, and overcoming challenges.
[事实] Huberman says he uses short high-intensity air bike intervals and experiences them as unpleasant during the effort but beneficial afterward.
[推测] The segment suggests that voluntary hard effort may matter differently from coerced activity, even when physical work is similar.

[92:43] **Lifestyle Risk and Dementia**

[事实] Weiss-Corey cites work on lifestyle influences on dementia and Alzheimer’s disease.
[事实] Risk factors discussed include poverty, childhood obesity, lower education, smoking, and excessive alcohol use.
[事实] He says optimizing these factors is associated with much lower dementia risk across many studies.
[推测] Some risk factors are individual behaviors, while others are social or environmental conditions that are harder for a person to change alone.

[93:47] **Cognitive Exercise and Use-It-or-Lose-It**

[事实] Weiss-Corey says studies in people who already have cognitive impairment have not shown much benefit from mental exercise interventions.
[事实] He notes that people may differ in whether they enjoy and benefit from activities such as learning a language or instrument.
[事实] Huberman argues that challenging activities, handwriting, reading, and continued use of neural systems may help maintain function.
[推测] The transcript supports mental challenge as plausible and low-risk, but not as a proven intervention for reversing cognitive impairment.

[96:58] **Food, Wine, Social Connection, and Moderation**

[事实] Weiss-Corey says he eats chocolate almost every day, often after lunch with coffee, and connects it with taste and stimulation.
[事实] He says alcohol itself is probably not good for the body, but wine often occurs in a social context such as shared meals.
[事实] He says studies of centenarians often find social connection and community as common features.
[事实] Both speakers distinguish moderate social drinking from excess drinking, with Weiss-Corey emphasizing moderation across food and alcohol.

[100:54] **Breathing, Circulation, and Blood Chemistry**

[事实] Huberman discusses Qigong, Tai Chi, deliberate breathing, and respiratory sinus arrhythmia as ways breathing can alter heart rate and circulation.
[事实] He raises the possibility that aging-related changes in breathing, including mouth breathing, may relate to oxygenation or other physiological changes.
[事实] Weiss-Corey says a direct study could measure blood before and after a breathing intervention to test whether blood chemistry changes.
[推测] Breathwork is presented as a plausible, low-cost research target, but the transcript does not provide mechanistic evidence that it improves organ aging.

[104:08] **Tailored Tools Instead of Generic Advice**

[事实] Weiss-Corey says Vero aims to provide tailored interventions based on which organ shows accelerated aging.
[事实] He gives the example of using studies to determine the exact exercise most likely to benefit an aging heart or brain.
[事实] Huberman argues that the healthspan field needs tools beyond repeating general advice about exercise, sleep, nutrition, social connection, sunlight, and avoiding smoking or excess drinking.
[推测] The segment frames personalized measurement as a way to turn broad health advice into testable, organ-specific action.

[105:00] **Wearables, Light, and Mental Health**

[事实] Weiss-Corey uses a Garmin to measure steps and finds sleep measurements useful.
[事实] Huberman and Weiss-Corey discuss the rise of wearable health tracking and the possibility of measuring daily photon exposure.
[事实] Weiss-Corey says wearing dark glasses all day is bad for the brain, and Huberman says bright days and dark nights are linked to lower susceptibility to mental health conditions.
[事实] Huberman says 10,000-lux artificial light in the morning can help some people when sunlight is insufficient, including for seasonal depression.

[109:53] **Cell-Type Aging and Proteome Mapping**

[事实] Weiss-Corey describes upcoming work extending organ-age models to estimate the age of cell types using thousands of proteins assigned to about 40 cell types.
[事实] In ALS, his group found enrichment of extremely old skeletal muscle cells and cardiomyocytes, and UK Biobank follow-up showed increased future ALS risk in people with these old muscle-cell signatures.
[事实] In Alzheimer’s disease, astrocyte age was a stronger predictor than whole-brain age, especially when combined with a genetic risk factor.
[事实] His current major goal is to build a public map of the human proteome across monogenic diseases, using plasma profiles to learn how the body responds to disruptions of specific genes.

## 播客点评/总结

[推测] The episode’s main value is its careful distinction between exciting biology and proven intervention. It gives listeners a mechanistic view of aging through blood proteins, organ clocks, CSF signals, and exercise-induced molecules without presenting them as ready-made cures.

[推测] Its strongest sections are the explanations of parabiosis, organ-specific aging, and the move from broad health advice toward measurable, individualized intervention. The repeated focus on blinded trials, safety, and healthspan keeps the discussion grounded.

[推测] The main limitation is that many areas remain preclinical, small-trial, or correlative, including young plasma fractions, NAD/NMN, fasting, exosomes, breathing interventions, and single rejuvenation factors. This episode is best suited for listeners interested in neuroscience, longevity research, proteomics, and practical healthspan tradeoffs rather than anyone seeking a simple supplement or anti-aging protocol.
