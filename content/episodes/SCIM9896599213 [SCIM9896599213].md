# Clinical Hypnosis, Self-Hypnosis, and Mind-Body Control

## 概览

This episode features Andrew Huberman’s conversation with Dr. David Spiegel about hypnosis as a focused, clinically useful brain state rather than a stage performance. The discussion frames clinical hypnosis and self-hypnosis as tools for changing attention, body responses, pain, stress, sleep, and emotional processing.

A central conclusion is that hypnosis can increase control rather than remove it. Spiegel repeatedly describes hypnosis as a way to voluntarily enter an altered state, approach difficult sensations or memories, and then reshape the body’s or mind’s response.

The episode moves from definitions and neuroscience into clinical uses: pain control, phobias, trauma, grief, insomnia, pediatric procedures, and group hypnosis. It also covers hypnotizability, the Spiegel eye roll test, EMDR, drug adjuncts, breathing, and resources for finding trained clinical hypnosis providers.

## 分段落总结

[00:00] **Guest and Episode Frame**

[事实] Andrew Huberman introduces Dr. David Spiegel as a Stanford psychiatrist, researcher, and Director of the Stanford Center on Stress and Health.

[事实] The episode focuses on clinical hypnosis, self-hypnosis, mind-body interactions, stress, pain, sleep, trauma, and psychiatric applications.

[事实] Huberman distinguishes clinical hypnosis from stage hypnosis and says the Reveri app was developed from research in Spiegel’s Stanford lab.

[05:23] **What Hypnosis Is**

[事实] Spiegel defines hypnosis as a state of highly focused attention, where experience becomes vivid while broader context is reduced.

[事实] He compares everyday hypnotic-like states to becoming absorbed in a movie, sports event, or other experience so deeply that one is experiencing more than evaluating.

[推测] The episode treats hypnosis less as a mysterious trance and more as an attention state that can be deliberately used.

[07:15] **Clinical Hypnosis Versus Stage Hypnosis**

[事实] Spiegel describes his father’s wartime exposure to hypnosis and its use for acute pain, conversion symptoms, and post-traumatic stress.

[事实] Spiegel became committed to hypnosis after helping a teenage asthma patient relax and breathe more easily during a medical rotation.

[事实] He criticizes stage hypnosis because it selects highly hypnotizable people and may use suggestion in ways that embarrass or harm them.

[事实] Spiegel says self-hypnosis is meant to enhance control over mind and body.

[17:02] **Brain Networks in Hypnosis**

[事实] Spiegel reports fMRI work comparing highly hypnotizable and non-hypnotizable people.

[事实] Entry into hypnosis is associated with reduced activity in the dorsal anterior cingulate cortex, a salience and conflict-detection region.

[事实] Hypnosis also involves increased connectivity between dorsolateral prefrontal cortex and insula, which Spiegel links to mind-body regulation.

[事实] Inverse connectivity between dorsolateral prefrontal cortex and posterior cingulate cortex is linked to doing something without excessive self-reflection.

[22:20] **Attention and ADHD**

[事实] Huberman asks whether hypnosis could help attention because focused attention is central to hypnotic states.

[事实] Spiegel says self-hypnosis can help some people narrow and prepare attention, but he has not directly studied ADHD.

[推测] Spiegel’s comments imply that hypnotizability would likely determine whether self-hypnosis helps a given person with attention problems.

[25:00] **Stress, Sleep, and Problem-Focused Treatment**

[事实] Spiegel describes hypnosis as especially useful for specific, problem-focused treatment.

[事实] For stress, he teaches people to imagine the body floating somewhere safe while viewing the stressor on an imaginary screen.

[事实] For sleep, he advises not looking at the clock after waking and instead using the floating-body and imaginary-screen exercise.

[推测] The technique aims to separate physical arousal from psychological escalation so the person feels at least one controllable element.

[30:01] **Phobias and Imagined Exposure**

[事实] Spiegel says phobias are reinforced when avoidance leaves fear as the only association with the feared object or situation.

[事实] Hypnosis can help create new associations by letting people confront feared situations in imagination while maintaining bodily calm.

[事实] He gives examples involving dog phobia, including gradual re-association of dogs with safety or friendliness.

[34:00] **Mental State Change and Trauma**

[事实] Spiegel calls hypnotic desensitization less systematic than conventional exposure because the key change is the mental state.

[事实] He says mental state change itself can have therapeutic potential.

[事实] In a trauma case, hypnosis helped a woman confront an attempted rape memory and reframe her experience around her own self-protective action.

[事实] Spiegel cites evidence from Israel that adding hypnosis to PTSD treatment improved outcomes.

[40:02] **State-Dependent Memory and Dissociation**

[事实] Spiegel discusses state-dependent memory: being in a similar mental state can improve access to memories formed in that state.

[事实] He says people often dissociate during trauma, describing experiences such as floating above the body or operating on autopilot.

[事实] Hypnosis may help trauma treatment by recreating a related dissociative state in a controlled clinical setting.

[45:00] **Control as the Core Issue**

[事实] Spiegel says the essence of trauma is helplessness rather than fear or pain alone.

[事实] He argues that hypnosis has an undeserved reputation for removing control, when clinical hypnosis is used to increase control.

[事实] Self-hypnosis lets people voluntarily enter and exit a state, which Spiegel contrasts with states that simply overtake a person.

[47:01] **Clinicians, Self-Hypnosis, and Safety**

[事实] Spiegel recommends working with licensed professionals trained in a primary discipline such as medicine, psychology, dentistry, or psychiatry.

[事实] He warns that symptoms such as chest pain should not simply be hypnotically reduced without evaluating possible medical causes.

[事实] His clinical approach often involves one or two sessions to assess hypnotizability, guide an exercise, and teach self-hypnosis.

[事实] The Reveri app includes exercises for pain, stress, focus, insomnia, eating better, and smoking cessation.

[49:01] **Evidence for Pain Relief**

[事实] Spiegel describes a randomized trial in medical procedures where self-hypnosis reduced pain by 80% compared with standard care after about an hour and a half.

[事实] In that trial, the hypnosis group used about half as much opioid medication, had fewer complications, and procedures averaged 17 minutes shorter.

[事实] In women with metastatic breast cancer, weekly support groups with self-hypnosis led to half the pain of controls after a year, with similarly low medication use.

[53:00] **OCD, Intrusive Thoughts, and Superstition**

[事实] Spiegel says hypnosis can sometimes help obsessive thoughts, but highly obsessional people may be less hypnotizable because they evaluate rather than experience.

[事实] He describes OCD checking as a case where evaluation overrides experiential memory.

[事实] Huberman describes breaking a superstition around knocking on wood by preventing the response until it lost meaning.

[事实] Spiegel identifies that approach as response prevention.

[57:01] **Hypnotizability**

[事实] Hypnotizability is described as the capacity to have hypnotic experiences.

[事实] Spiegel says hypnotizability peaks in childhood, especially around ages six to eleven.

[事实] By early adulthood, hypnotizability becomes highly stable; he cites a 25-year test-retest correlation of 0.7.

[事实] About one-third of adults are not hypnotizable, about two-thirds are, and about 15% are extremely hypnotizable.

[62:02] **The Spiegel Eye Roll Test**

[事实] The eye roll test involves looking upward, then closing the eyelids while seeing whether the eyes stay rolled upward.

[事实] More visible sclera while closing the eyes is associated with higher hypnotizability, while the eyes moving down is associated with lower hypnotizability.

[事实] Spiegel links the test to conflicting eye and eyelid control signals and to the role of eyes in consciousness states.

[70:01] **Can Hypnotizability Be Increased?**

[事实] Spiegel says repeated practice may slightly increase measured hypnotizability, but not enough to make it the main goal.

[事实] He says it is more useful to get better at applying one’s existing hypnotic capacity to the problem being treated.

[事实] Low-to-moderately hypnotizable people may benefit from more explanation during treatment.

[72:01] **EMDR and Exposure**

[事实] Huberman asks about EMDR and lateral eye movements in trauma treatment.

[事实] Spiegel says many people improve with EMDR, and the VA uses it, but he believes its benefits are largely exposure-based.

[事实] Spiegel says dismantling studies he has seen do not show the lateral eye movement component adding benefit.

[推测] The discussion suggests EMDR may overlap with hypnosis through focused attention and controlled confrontation of traumatic material.

[79:01] **Triggers, Voluntary Exposure, and Stress Inoculation**

[事实] Spiegel emphasizes that confronting stress or trauma voluntarily is different from being overwhelmed by it involuntarily.

[事实] He criticizes overuse of trigger warnings and argues that people need ways to manage upsetting material rather than avoid all exposure.

[事实] He cites Karen Parker’s primate work on stress inoculation, where brief separation and reunion led to lower later cortisol responses.

[85:00] **Mind-Body Signals and Pain**

[事实] Spiegel says brain and body signals should be treated as tools that help interpret needs, threats, and priorities.

[事实] He distinguishes pain that signals new danger from pain that may reflect healing or recovery.

[事实] He recommends interpreting interpersonal or bodily threats as situations where one can identify some realistic action.

[推测] His model treats mind-body awareness as adaptive when paired with interpretation and action, not as passive immersion in sensation.

[89:02] **Grief and Loss**

[事实] Spiegel describes grief as a natural and necessary stage of life.

[事实] He says rituals such as burials and memorials help make an incomprehensible loss real.

[事实] In grief-related hypnosis, he asks people to face the loss while also identifying what the person left with them.

[事实] In cancer support groups, expressing negative emotion over time was associated with less anxiety and depression in randomized trials.

[92:03] **Children and Medical Procedures**

[事实] Spiegel says children can be highly hypnotizable, though they often need more structure for self-hypnosis.

[事实] Pediatricians and dentists can use hypnotic techniques to help children with shots, blood draws, fear, and pain.

[事实] In a randomized trial involving children undergoing voiding cystourethrograms, self-hypnosis training made imaging easier and shortened procedures by 17 minutes.

[95:00] **Group Hypnosis and Medication Adjuncts**

[事实] Spiegel says he has used hypnosis with groups, including women with metastatic breast cancer.

[事实] He says group hypnosis can work as a shared social experience.

[事实] Spectroscopy work found a relationship between hypnotizability and GABA activity in the anterior cingulate cortex.

[事实] Benzodiazepines may help if anxiety is blocking hypnosis, but may impair hypnosis in less anxious people by reducing focused attention.

[99:01] **Breathing and Performance States**

[事实] Spiegel and Huberman discuss breathing as a bridge between conscious and unconscious control.

[事实] Spiegel uses deep breathing and slow exhalation during induction, and he says longer exhales may support parasympathetic activity.

[事实] He says breathing, vision, bodily state, and directed mental focus are key components of hypnosis.

[事实] He also describes peak athletic, musical, speaking, and work performance as hypnosis-like when people are absorbed rather than self-monitoring.

[102:02] **Resources and Closing**

[事实] Spiegel points listeners to the Reveri app and website.

[事实] He names the Society for Clinical and Experimental Hypnosis and the American Society for Clinical Hypnosis as referral resources.

[事实] He recommends finding someone licensed and trained in a primary professional discipline who also has hypnosis training.

## 播客点评/总结

[推测] The episode’s main value is that it translates hypnosis from a culturally loaded idea into a practical framework for attention, physiology, and therapeutic change. Its strongest sections connect clinical stories with specific brain networks and randomized trial findings.

[推测] The discussion is especially useful for listeners interested in stress, pain, sleep, trauma, phobias, grief, and mind-body tools. It is less useful as a step-by-step self-treatment guide because most techniques are described conceptually rather than delivered as complete protocols.

[推测] A limitation is that some topics, such as ADHD, OCD, EMDR mechanisms, ketamine, and long-term neural rewiring, are treated cautiously or left partly unresolved. That caution is also a strength because the speakers distinguish evidence, clinical experience, and speculation rather than presenting every application as settled.
