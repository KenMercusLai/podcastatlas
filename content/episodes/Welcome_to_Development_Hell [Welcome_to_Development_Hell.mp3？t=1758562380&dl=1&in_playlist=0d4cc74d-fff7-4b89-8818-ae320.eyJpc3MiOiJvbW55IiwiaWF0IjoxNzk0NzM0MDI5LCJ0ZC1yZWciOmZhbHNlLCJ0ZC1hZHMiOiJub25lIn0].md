# Welcome to Development Hell

## 概览

Malcolm Gladwell introduces *Development Hell*, a new *Revisionist History* mini-series about promising film projects that never reached the screen. The idea grew from his own experience pitching a movie across Hollywood, attracting offers and stars, and then watching the project disappear.

The series explores the creative work, emotional investment, and strange circumstances behind abandoned scripts. Gladwell compares Hollywood development to pharmaceutical research, where talented people may spend entire careers on projects that never reach the public.

Across roughly half a dozen episodes, the mini-series will cover Gladwell’s Hollywood adventure, an overly complicated science-fiction story, a chimpanzee biopic, an Elvis musical, celebrity anecdotes, and unexpectedly moving conversations.
