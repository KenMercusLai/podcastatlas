# Power, Propaganda and a Doctor for Kolkata’s Poor

## 概览

This episode examines three very different forms of influence. It begins with Nvidia’s leadership crisis following allegations against founder Jensen Huang, assessing the company’s dependence on him, the challenges facing interim chief executive Colette Kress and the uncertain search for a permanent successor.

The second segment investigates Russia’s campaign to build influence in Africa through cultural centres, media partnerships, journalist training and AI-assisted propaganda. Although the campaign’s effectiveness is difficult to measure, the discussion warns that Russia may benefit from weakening social cohesion in already fragile societies.

The episode closes with a tribute to Jack Prager, the British doctor who devoted decades to treating Kolkata’s poorest residents. His work grew from a pavement clinic into Calcutta Rescue, providing medicine, education, meals and identification documents without regard to religion or caste.

## 分段落总结

[01:28] **Nvidia’s leadership crisis**

[事实] Jensen Huang resigned from Nvidia and was summoned to testify before Congress amid allegations involving personal relationships, espionage and the transfer of strategically important technology.

[事实] The transcript says a newly reported relationship with a direct report made his continued leadership untenable, although it remained unclear whether he resigned voluntarily or under pressure.

[事实] Colette Kress, Nvidia’s chief financial officer, was appointed interim chief executive.

[事实] The company’s board was criticised for giving Huang extensive freedom and for including several directors who had served for more than 15 years.

[03:45] **The difficulty of replacing Jensen Huang**

[事实] Huang reportedly had almost 60 direct reports and was closely involved in manufacturing, product design, sales and public policy.

[事实] Nvidia’s complex products depend on an intricate supply chain involving companies such as TSMC and SK Hynix.

[事实] Huang’s long-standing supplier relationships were described as one of his particular strengths, making a rapid leadership transition difficult.

[推测] Nvidia’s highly founder-centred structure may force the next chief executive to reorganise responsibilities rather than reproduce Huang’s management style.

[05:12] **Investor anxiety and Nvidia’s longer-term outlook**

[事实] Huang represented Nvidia’s principal “key man risk,” and markets had difficulty imagining the company without him.

[事实] Nvidia was described as financially secure, highly profitable and sold out of chips well into the following year, limiting the immediate danger to the business.

[事实] The greater challenge is maintaining the annual growth rates expected by investors from a company valued at nearly $5 trillion.

[事实] Some anticipated growth depended on financing arrangements and partnerships that Huang had been announcing frequently.

[事实] Potential successors mentioned included AMD chief Lisa Su and Arm chief Rene Haas, but no clear favourite had emerged.

[10:00] **Russia’s expanding influence campaign in Africa**

[事实] Vladimir Putin presented Russia as a trusted partner at a summit attended by more than 20 African leaders.

[事实] Since invading Ukraine, Russia has intensified efforts to win support across the Global South, especially in Africa.

[事实] “Russian houses” officially promote Russian culture in a manner resembling the British Council or Institut français.

[事实] At a Russian house in São Tomé and Príncipe, visitors encountered Russian cultural displays and language lessons that could help students pursue university study in Russia.

[13:13] **A broad propaganda infrastructure**

[事实] Russian houses are described as venues for films promoting Russia’s account of the war in Ukraine and the activities of Russian forces in the Sahel.

[事实] They may also host Orthodox Church services; Ukrainian intelligence alleges that some are used to recruit Africans to fight for Russia in Ukraine.

[事实] A Russian-backed news agency forms partnerships with African media organisations, shares content and trains local journalists.

[事实] Investigations by Code for Africa found dozens of fictitious journalists and pseudo-experts producing Kremlin-aligned articles with assistance from AI, sometimes distributing them through genuine African outlets.

[15:19] **Uncertain impact and risks to social cohesion**

[事实] The programme says the effectiveness of Russia’s influence campaign cannot be known with certainty and warns against overlooking the agency of African governments and citizens.

[事实] Attendance by African leaders at a Russian summit does not necessarily indicate acceptance of the Kremlin’s worldview.

[事实] Most Africans do not regularly consume Russian state media or attend events at Russian houses, while African enrolment in Russian universities remains below that in the United States, Britain or China.

[事实] Russia is nevertheless accused of promoting a pro-war worldview and undermining social cohesion because fragile societies are easier to exploit.

[16:42] **Jack Prager’s unexpected call to medicine**

[事实] While working on his farm in west Wales, Jack Prager experienced what he described as words entering his mind and telling him to become a doctor.

[事实] He began medical training at 34 despite having studied politics and economics and lacking scientific qualifications.

[事实] After graduating from the Royal College of Surgeons in Dublin, he became convinced that his mission was to serve poor communities.

[事实] He first worked in refugee camps in Bangladesh but was expelled after accusing the government of connections to an adoption racket.

[18:24] **From a pavement clinic to Calcutta Rescue**

[事实] Prager arrived in Kolkata in 1979, briefly worked with Mother Teresa and then left because he believed practical hygiene and medical measures were being neglected in favour of prayer.

[事实] He began treating patients for free by opening his medical bag on a pavement beneath a flyover.

[事实] By 2000, he was widely known as “Dr Jack” and was estimated to have helped roughly half a million people.

[事实] His services were offered without distinction of caste or religion and expanded to include education, school meals and identity cards for destitute residents.

[事实] After practising for years without an Indian licence, he registered Calcutta Rescue in 1991 and gained lawful status as a resident and doctor.

[20:48] **Frugal service and Prager’s legacy**

[事实] Prager regarded the establishment of West Bengal’s first clinic for HIV patients as his greatest achievement in India.

[事实] He lived simply in one room above a school he had founded, spent very little money and counted Irish whiskey as his sole luxury.

[事实] He left Kolkata at 88 as his strength declined, believing that his work had not substantially reduced the city’s immense needs but that doing something was better than doing nothing.

[事实] Although he publicly denied having a religion, he remained influenced by the instruction to become a doctor and by Jesus’s teaching about serving “the least” of others.

[事实] Jack Prager died at the age of 96.

## 播客点评/总结

[推测] The episode’s strongest quality is its range: a corporate succession crisis, an international propaganda system and an individual life of service are connected by a shared concern with how personal and institutional power operates.

[推测] The Nvidia discussion clearly identifies founder dependence, supply-chain relationships and investor expectations as distinct risks. Its limitation is that many central claims remain allegations or unresolved questions, so the conclusions are necessarily provisional.

[推测] The Africa segment is particularly valuable for showing how influence campaigns combine physical cultural centres, established media organisations, religious institutions and AI-generated identities. It also avoids claiming that African audiences passively accept Russian messaging.

[推测] Prager’s story gives the episode an emotionally grounded conclusion and will appeal to listeners interested in global affairs, technology, media manipulation and humanitarian work. Its concise obituary format, however, leaves limited room for perspectives from Prager’s patients or colleagues in Kolkata.
