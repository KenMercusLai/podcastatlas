# Introducing Revisionist History Season Five

## Overview

This short trailer introduces the fifth season of *Revisionist History*, scheduled to premiere on June 18. The season promises the show’s familiar movement between serious subjects, comic detours, and unexpected cultural connections.

Malcolm Gladwell frames the season around “yuchas,” described as the joy a parent gives to their children—the reverse of “nachas,” the joy children give their parents. Alongside that playful idea, the season will consider how society remembers both those who suffered and those who caused suffering.

The preview also points to stories about art museums, collecting, and beauty, with references to Howard Hughes, Vincent van Gogh, Smaug, and Andy Warhol.

[事实] He describes the coming season as moving from the sublime to the ridiculous, with digressions that carry their own importance.

[推测] “Yuchas” serves as a playful organizing metaphor for a season intended to give listeners both enjoyment and intellectual stimulation.

[01:38] **Remembering suffering and responsibility**

[事实] The trailer says the season will ask how people should remember those who suffered.

[事实] It also asks how people should remember those who brought suffering into the world.

[事实] An excerpted speaker says they would prefer someone unintelligent who acted—even wrongly—to someone who vacillated and did nothing.

[推测] The season may explore the moral tension between decisive action, harmful action, and passive inaction.

[02:03] **Art museums, collecting, and beauty**

[事实] Gladwell says season five contains several stories about art museums, which he characterizes as strange institutions.

[事实] The trailer mentions Howard Hughes, Vincent van Gogh, Smaug the dragon, and Andy Warhol as subjects or reference points.

[事实] A speaker connects Warhol’s statement that everyone must be beautiful or nobody is with a hoarder’s tendency to regard every object as valuable.

[推测] These stories may use museums and hoarding to examine how people assign beauty, importance, and value to objects.

[02:39] **Premiere announcement**

[事实] The trailer repeats that the first episode of season five will be released on June 18.

## 播客点评/总结

The trailer efficiently conveys the range and personality of *Revisionist History*: playful wordplay sits beside questions about suffering, moral responsibility, art, and cultural memory. Its strongest feature is the suggestion that apparently unrelated subjects will be connected through broader ideas about value and remembrance.

Because this is a brief promotional preview, it offers intriguing excerpts rather than developed arguments. The exact relationships among its historical figures, museums, and ethical questions remain unclear.

[推测] The season is likely to appeal to listeners who enjoy revisionist cultural history, moral puzzles, and narrative essays that move freely between humor and serious analysis.
