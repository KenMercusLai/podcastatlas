# Can Better Housing Policy Raise Fertility?

## 概览

The episode examines whether America’s housing crisis is contributing to record-low fertility. Although the U.S. fertility rate is about 1.6 children per woman, surveys suggest that people still consider roughly two children ideal. The central question is therefore not how to persuade unwilling people to have children, but how to remove obstacles facing those who already want families.

Housing affects family formation through affordability, stability, wealth, and space. Homeownership can protect households from rising rents, provide equity, and reinforce long-term commitments. Yet the median U.S. home now costs about five times median household income, compared with roughly two and a half times in 1970.

Economist Ed Glaeser argues that restrictive land-use rules have sharply constrained construction. Austin offers a practical counterexample: permitting reform, smaller minimum lots, greater density, and extensive apartment construction have expanded supply and reduced rents. However, simply building more units may not be enough if most new homes are studios or one-bedroom apartments rather than family-sized housing.

The episode concludes that housing reform could help people realize their desired family size, but fertility is not purely an economic calculation. The policy tools are relatively clear—more construction, faster approvals, family-sized homes, and incentives for local governments—while the political barriers remain formidable.

## 分段落总结

[01:23] **Housing as a Possible Fertility Policy**

[事实] A cited essay argued that unaffordable housing contributes to inequality, financial instability, climate change, obesity, and falling fertility.

[事实] The episode asks whether better housing policy could also improve fertility outcomes, noting that people report wanting more children than they are having.

[事实] Previous evidence discussed by the program linked both working from home and government-backed mortgages with higher fertility.

[03:36] **The Gap Between Desired and Actual Family Size**

[事实] The U.S. total fertility rate is approximately 1.6 children per woman, while surveys indicate an ideal family size of about two children.

[事实] Economist Melissa Kearney says the objective is not to convince people who do not want children, but to help people who do want families arrange their lives accordingly.

[事实] Couples in which both partners work from home at least one day per week have, on average, about one-third of a child more globally and nearly half a child more in the U.S.

[事实] Remote work has increased demand for housing and shifted some demand from city centers toward suburbs and rural areas.

[06:03] **Homeownership, Marriage, and Long-Term Security**

[事实] Demographer Lyman Stone describes joint homeownership as a form of “marriage insurance” because a jointly owned home makes separation more difficult.

[事实] He says people are more likely to marry when homes are more accessible.

[事实] Rising housing costs reduce renters’ available budgets, while homeowners may benefit from higher property values and access to home equity.

[事实] Kearney identifies three housing-related fertility channels: fluctuations in housing costs, wealth generated by housing, and access to mortgages and family-appropriate homes.

[08:41] **Why the Type of Housing Matters**

[事实] Kearney says rents alone do not appear to correlate clearly with fertility and suggests that access to family-friendly housing may matter more.

[事实] American families often associate child-rearing with bedrooms, yards, and single-family homes or townhouses rather than crowded apartments.

[事实] She raises the possibility that America’s historically greater supply of spacious housing helped keep its fertility rate above Europe’s during the 1980s and 1990s.

[推测] The effect of housing on fertility may depend not only on price but also on whether available homes match culturally shaped expectations about raising children.

[10:06] **America’s Growing Housing Shortage**

[事实] The median home cost about 2.5 times median household income in 1970 and about five times median income at the time discussed.

[事实] Ed Glaeser estimates that the U.S. had about 144 million housing units in 2023, up from 131 million in 2010 and 87 million in 1980.

[事实] He calculates that maintaining the 1980–2000 construction growth rate through 2020 would have produced approximately 15 million additional homes.

[事实] Glaeser advises buyers to view a home primarily as the setting for their lives rather than as a speculative financial investment.

[12:40] **Supply Constraints and the “Zoning Tax”**

[事实] Housing supply depends on construction costs, physical land conditions, and a wide range of zoning and land-use restrictions.

[事实] Glaeser defines the “zoning tax” as the gap between the market price of housing and the cost of producing it.

[事实] Research on Manhattan found that around 2000, buying a condominium cost roughly twice as much as adding an additional floor would cost to build.

[事实] Local governments can restrict construction through many overlapping rules, meaning that eliminating only one regulation may not increase supply if the community remains opposed to development.

[16:00] **Restrictions Spread Beyond Coastal Cities**

[事实] Glaeser says cities including Phoenix, Miami, Atlanta, and Dallas have become more restrictive, while Houston remains comparatively effective at supplying abundant housing.

[事实] Phoenix produced about 300,000 units in the 1990s but only 177,000 in the 2010s; Atlanta also experienced a substantial decline.

[事实] Rising prices combined with declining new construction indicate a supply-side constraint rather than falling demand.

[事实] Glaeser acknowledges that directly attributing the decline to individual regulations is statistically difficult, although the evidence can rule out a simple shortage of land.

[19:53] **Competing Explanations and Private-Equity Ownership**

[事实] Glaeser rejects inequality as a complete explanation for the affordability crisis because it does not explain the sharp decline in new housing production.

[事实] He accepts that wealthy buyers can raise prices when supply is limited, but maintains that the missing housing units remain central.

[事实] He views private-equity purchases of surplus homes after the 2008 crisis as broadly beneficial because firms converted unoccupied houses into efficiently managed rentals.

[事实] He considers a nationwide housing monopoly implausible because institutional owners hold only a small share of the overall U.S. housing stock.

[事实] His proposed remedy is to reduce local control over construction or create strong financial incentives for local governments to approve housing.

[24:07] **Austin’s Affordability Strategy**

[事实] Austin Mayor Kirk Watson explicitly connects housing availability with fertility and intergenerational equity.

[事实] Watson argues that Austin must remain affordable if it wants students and other young residents to stay, contribute to the city’s culture, and form families.

[事实] From 2010 to 2019, Austin rents rose by 93 percent, the largest increase among major cities cited in the episode.

[事实] After beginning to overhaul housing policy around 2015, Austin added 120,000 units over the following decade, growing its housing stock at more than three times the national rate.

[29:29] **Streamlining Austin’s Permitting System**

[事实] An external review found that Austin site plans passed through nearly 1,500 steps before approval.

[事实] The city reduced its target for an initial site-plan review from as much as 100 days to about 28 days, while second reviews fell from roughly 50 days to about 14.

[事实] Watson says the former process delayed projects, raised development costs, and undermined the city’s own affordability objectives.

[事实] The old system made it comparatively easier to build large, expensive houses than starter homes or denser housing.

[32:45] **Smaller Lots and Greater Density**

[事实] Austin began allowing three units on lots previously reserved for one single-family home.

[事实] The city reduced its minimum lot size from 5,750 square feet to 1,800 square feet.

[事实] These changes make smaller starter homes and infill development in central neighborhoods more feasible.

[事实] Newly built homes under the reforms are about 44 percent smaller but still have three bedrooms and are significantly more affordable.

[事实] Watson presents these homes as suitable for young households that want to remain in Austin and start families.

[34:43] **Falling Rents but Continuing Family Pressures**

[事实] Austin has experienced both a comparatively low fertility rate and a significant five-year decline in its population of young people.

[事实] Watson acknowledges that childcare costs remain high and says a county initiative is addressing them.

[事实] Extensive apartment construction helped bring Austin’s median rent below the national median in 2024 for the first time in more than a decade.

[推测] Austin’s experience suggests that expanding housing supply can improve affordability relatively quickly, but housing reform alone cannot resolve every barrier to family formation.

[35:52] **Making Reform Politically Replicable**

[事实] Watson believes Austin’s approach can be replicated, but cities need a clear explanation of why reform serves the community.

[事实] He favors incremental experimentation rather than treating housing reform as an all-or-nothing project.

[事实] Watson opposes rigid NIMBY and YIMBY labels because he believes they deepen division and prevent useful ideas from being heard.

[事实] The episode describes YIMBY advocacy as an influence on the broader “abundance” movement, which promotes greater capacity to build housing, transportation, infrastructure, and clean energy.

[39:24] **What a Functioning Housing Market Should Deliver**

[事实] Glaeser defines a well-functioning market as one where middle-income residents can initially rent affordably and later purchase a home with enough bedrooms for children.

[事实] He says public support such as Section 8 vouchers would still be necessary for the most vulnerable households.

[事实] America historically combined abundant land with transportation networks that enabled large-scale suburban development.

[事实] Glaeser argues that regulatory restrictions have manufactured scarcity and transferred advantages to established insiders at the expense of outsiders.

[41:45] **New York’s Delayed Path to Homeownership**

[事实] New York Governor Kathy Hochul says the typical homebuying age has risen from about 30 during her youth to about 40.

[事实] She connects this delay with postponed first and second births and reduced confidence among younger adults that they can attain a starter home.

[事实] New York projects can take up to 56 percent longer than those in peer states, with environmental review adding an average of two years.

[事实] Hochul says regulatory delays add approximately $82,000 per apartment unit in New York City, a cost ultimately passed to renters.

[44:14] **Upzoning’s Promise and Its Limits**

[事实] New York City’s “City of Yes” initiative is intended to enable more than 80,000 additional homes over 15 years.

[事实] Glaeser supports eliminating mandatory parking requirements but questions whether 80,000 units will be sufficient.

[事实] Research discussed in the episode found that ten years after an upzoning, only about 9 percent of newly permitted floor space had been built.

[事实] Glaeser says modest changes from one permitted unit to two often have little impact, while much larger density increases are more likely to change development incentives.

[事实] Existing buildings, lawsuits, and neighborhood opposition can slow construction even after zoning formally permits greater density.

[46:42] **The Shortage of Family-Sized Homes**

[事实] New York City completed 39,000 housing units in the previous year, its highest annual total since 1965, but most were studios or one-bedroom apartments.

[事实] Stone says birth rates are extremely low among residents of studios and one-bedroom homes.

[事实] He argues that fertility-oriented supply policy must include many three-bedroom apartments or other family-sized homes.

[事实] Family-sized homes have experienced particularly strong price growth, indicating high demand.

[推测] Counting total new units without considering bedroom count may overstate how much housing reform helps prospective parents.

[47:46] **Mortgage Policy and Support for Families**

[事实] Federal Housing Administration-backed mortgages introduced during the 1930s increased homeownership and were subsequently associated with more births.

[事实] Stone proposes adjusting FHA eligibility and down-payment assistance by household size, marriage, or number of children.

[事实] Some changes might be possible administratively, while substantial reforms would require congressional action.

[事实] Kearney says federal per-capita spending is about five times greater for people over 65 than for those under 18; including state and local spending reduces the ratio to about two to one.

[事实] She characterizes the imbalance as a solvable policy problem but a difficult political one.

[50:09] **Federal Incentives for More Construction**

[事实] The 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act became law in July after the president allowed the ten-day signature period to expire.

[事实] Its BUILD NOW provision links community-development block grants partly to local housing production, although the legislation contains broad exemptions.

[事实] The law also streamlines environmental reviews, supports prefabricated housing, and creates a pilot program for FHA-backed mortgages below $100,000.

[事实] Glaeser proposes tying more federal transportation funding to housing construction in high-demand areas.

[事实] He also discusses state-level production targets and California’s “builder’s remedy,” which may let developers bypass local zoning when municipalities fail to meet housing obligations.

[53:02] **Housing Cannot Fully Explain Fertility**

[事实] Glaeser says fertility decisions are not based solely on financial calculations and describes parenting as both deeply satisfying and personally costly.

[事实] He notes that raising children demands work, sacrifice, and prioritizing other people.

[事实] Housing scarcity can also reduce geographic mobility because young adults may remain near parents who can provide them with a bedroom.

[事实] Glaeser urges younger people not to accept permanent exclusion from expensive housing markets and to pursue reforms that restore opportunity for outsiders.

[推测] Housing reform may remove an important constraint on parenthood, but it cannot by itself reverse broader cultural and personal changes affecting fertility.

## 播客点评/总结

The episode’s strongest contribution is its integration of fertility research with housing economics. It moves beyond the vague claim that children are expensive and identifies concrete mechanisms: rent exposure, home equity, residential stability, bedroom count, permitting delays, and zoning restrictions.

Austin supplies a valuable real-world case in which permitting and land-use reforms coincided with rapid construction and lower rents. The discussion also avoids treating every new unit as equivalent, emphasizing that studios and one-bedroom apartments may serve housing needs without doing much to accommodate families with multiple children.

A limitation is that much of the episode establishes associations rather than definitive causal effects. Several speakers support greater housing supply, so objections concerning infrastructure, displacement, environmental protection, and the distribution of development receive less attention.

[推测] The episode is especially useful for listeners interested in housing policy, urban economics, demographic decline, and intergenerational inequality. Its central lesson is measured rather than absolute: housing is not “everything,” but affordable, stable, family-sized housing may be one of the most consequential policy levers available to people who want children but feel unable to have them.
