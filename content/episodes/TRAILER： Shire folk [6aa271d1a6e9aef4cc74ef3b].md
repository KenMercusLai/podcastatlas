# Shia Folk Trailer

## 概览

This trailer introduces an investigation into Tommy Robinson’s political influence and the way he frames Britain as a country under threat from radical Islam.

It opens with a scene in central London, where a crowd of 150,000 watches Elon Musk speak with Robinson about a coming civil conflict and the need to “fight back.”

The trailer then shifts to the broader premise: despite being largely excluded from British mainstream media, Robinson has become influential among some powerful figures in America, who treat Britain as a warning in a wider struggle over “the West.”
