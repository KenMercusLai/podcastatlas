# Introducing Revisionist History Season Three

## 概览

This short trailer introduces the third season of Malcolm Gladwell’s *Revisionist History*. Gladwell says the season will focus on mass delusion: situations in which people are so convinced of a false belief that evidence and education cannot easily reach them.

The preview points toward stories involving disputed data, mass hysteria, institutional failures, and individuals harmed by collective misconceptions. It also promises a heroic figure described through comparisons with Joan of Arc, Marie Curie, and Florence Nightingale.

Although the subject matter is serious, the trailer emphasizes the show’s familiar humor. Brief exchanges about an OSS officer traveling with a famous harmonica player, fear of flying, and Gladwell’s condescension suggest a season that combines historical investigation with playful conversation.

## 播客点评/总结

[推测] The trailer’s main strength is its concise presentation of a compelling intellectual problem: why groups reject evidence while remaining unaware of their own distorted assumptions. The selected excerpts create curiosity without revealing the full arguments of the forthcoming episodes.

[推测] Its tonal range is another notable feature. Serious references to poisoned children, rejected data, and mass hysteria are balanced by self-deprecating humor and unusual historical anecdotes, making difficult subjects feel approachable.

[推测] Because this is a promotional trailer rather than a complete episode, it provides few details about the featured cases and offers no sustained evidence for their conclusions. It is best suited to listeners interested in revisionist history, collective psychology, institutional failure, and narrative-driven investigations of accepted beliefs.
