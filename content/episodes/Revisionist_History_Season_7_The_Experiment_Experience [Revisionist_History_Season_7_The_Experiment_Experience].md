# The Experiment Experience: A Preview of Revisionist History Season Seven

## Overview

Malcolm Gladwell previews the seventh season of *Revisionist History*, presenting it as a journey through unconventional questions, overlooked places, and surprising cultural artifacts.

The season is unified by experiments—including thought experiments, natural experiments, “what if” scenarios, and what Gladwell calls “magic wand experiments.” Its central premise is that what people do not know exceeds what they know, so unanswered questions must be tested rather than merely contemplated.

Brief excerpts promise subjects ranging from a 1990s television movie and the original *A Star Is Born* set to scientific hypotheses, an abandoned laboratory, and repeated visits to Ohio.

## 播客点评/总结

This short trailer clearly establishes the season’s strongest idea: uncertainty is not an embarrassment but an invitation to experiment. The lively montage gives scientific inquiry an informal, playful quality while promising an unusually broad range of subjects.

Its greatest appeal lies in the juxtaposition of science, history, popular culture, travel, and eccentric personal anecdotes. The excerpts suggest a season suited to listeners who enjoy curiosity-driven storytelling more than narrowly specialized reporting.

Because this is a promotional preview rather than a complete episode, it offers few substantive findings and leaves most experiments unexplained. [推测] The season’s ultimate value will depend on whether the full episodes turn these intriguing premises into persuasive, well-supported conclusions.
