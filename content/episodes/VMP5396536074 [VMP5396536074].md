# How I Built This Announces an Expanded Release Schedule

## Overview

Guy announces an expanded release schedule for *How I Built This*, including more original interviews, archival episodes, and video content.

For the remainder of 2026, a new episode will arrive every Monday in audio and video, while a favorite archival episode will return each Thursday.

Beginning in the first week of January 2027, the show will publish three times a week: two new founder interviews and a previously unreleased video version of a classic episode.

## Podcast Commentary/Summary

This is a concise programming announcement rather than a conventional interview episode. Its primary value is giving the audience a clear picture of how the show’s publishing cadence and formats will expand through the end of 2026 and into 2027.

The standout development is the increased emphasis on video alongside audio, including new interviews and previously unreleased footage from classic episodes. [Inference] This broader format strategy may appeal especially to existing listeners who want more frequent releases and viewers who prefer YouTube.

Its scope is intentionally limited: the announcement does not identify upcoming founders, specify the exact weekday for each of the two weekly interviews, or provide details about the archival selections. It is most useful for current and prospective *How I Built This* followers who want to track the show’s new schedule.
