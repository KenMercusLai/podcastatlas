# From French School Protests to Campus Justice and Celestial Finance

## 概览

This episode examines three very different forms of uncertainty: political unrest in France, the handling of campus sexual-assault allegations in America, and the influence of astrology on Indian finance.

In France, school protests that began over timetables, teacher shortages and deteriorating facilities have expanded into an expression of wider anxiety about public spending, living costs and the approaching presidential election.

The Cornell Seven case prompts a broader critique of Title IX. The programme argues that shifting federal rules, elaborate campus proceedings and weak oversight have left both complainants and universities struggling with a system whose deterrent effect remains unclear.

The final discussion explores India’s large astrology industry and its influence on investors and businesses. Although some financial professionals treat astrology as an additional decision-making tool, the evidence presented offers little support for its predictive value.

## 分段落总结

[01:30] **French school protests spread nationwide**

[事实] More than a quarter of a million people joined school protests across France, with parents, teachers and union workers supporting pupils who had blocked secondary-school entrances since late September.

[事实] More than 1,000 secondary schools had been blockaded or affected by violence, while police used tear gas against protesters.

[事实] The movement began at one school in Créteil and rapidly spread from the Paris region to cities, small towns and overseas territories.

[事实] By Monday, 5,000 people had been arrested, 85% of them minors; clashes also caused injuries among protesters and police.

[03:49] **Students’ grievances and broader political anxiety**

[事实] Students complained about demanding timetables following changes to the baccalaureate, teacher absences and poorly maintained school buildings.

[事实] Protesters also expressed anger about government budget cuts and concern that Marine Le Pen could lead the next government.

[事实] The discussion connects the protests to uncertainty surrounding the presidential election, France’s 5% budget deficit and pressure from bond markets to reduce public spending.

[推测] The school protests appear to have become a vehicle for wider dissatisfaction with France’s political and economic direction.

[04:43] **The government’s response and risk of further unrest**

[事实] Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu announced that secondary schools would suspend normal classes for half a day so students could raise grievances through school councils and discussions with headteachers.

[事实] Political factions offered competing explanations: the far right accused the far left of encouraging violence, while the far left accused the government of ordering an excessive police response.

[事实] The government feared that discontent would spread beyond schools and become a broader national movement.

[推测] The speaker expected school protests to ease during the half-term closure but warned that anger over living costs and petrol prices would be harder to contain.

[08:41] **The Cornell Seven case**

[事实] Cornell University students rallied in support of a woman identified as Jane Doe, who accused seven men of drugging and raping her at a fraternity party in October 2024.

[事实] The men denied wrongdoing, but screenshots of crude and damaging messages attributed to them circulated widely.

[事实] Jane Doe reported the alleged assault to Cornell University police, and the university opened a formal Title IX investigation.

[事实] No criminal charges were brought, and Jane Doe later sued the men and Cornell, prompting scrutiny of the university, its police department and the county district attorney.

[事实] New York Governor Kathy Hochul appointed state attorney general Letitia James to investigate the case.

[11:04] **How Title IX became a campus justice system**

[事实] Title IX originally focused largely on areas such as sport and admissions but was later used by successive presidential administrations to regulate university responses to sexual assault.

[事实] Because the relevant requirements operate through administrative law, presidential administrations have repeatedly changed their interpretation without requiring Congress to pass new legislation.

[事实] The 2020 rule sought to reduce false or unverified claims by giving both parties extensive procedural protections.

[事实] Universities consequently had to create internal systems involving hearings, cross-examinations and large administrative teams.

[推测] The repeated reinterpretation of Title IX has produced an unstable campus justice framework that changes with the party controlling the White House.

[12:08] **Procedural burdens and declining investigations**

[事实] Jane Doe’s lawsuit says she had to participate in 12 hearings during Cornell’s process.

[事实] In New York in 2024, only 14% of incidents reported to Title IX coordinators proceeded to investigations, a lower proportion than under the rules used during Barack Obama’s presidency.

[事实] A former lawyer for the Department of Education’s Office for Civil Rights said completed investigations into campus sexual violence fell sharply after the 2020 rule.

[推测] The complexity intended to protect due process may also discourage complainants from completing Title IX proceedings.

[13:13] **Punishment, oversight and deterrence**

[事实] Recent versions of Title IX have not prescribed specific punishments; individual institutions determine sanctions according to circumstances, their own standards and state law.

[事实] Cornell said students found responsible for sexual misconduct were expelled or suspended, while two students found responsible for attempted sexual exploitation were assigned courses and essays.

[事实] All but two of the men involved would still be able to receive degrees.

[事实] In 2025, the Office for Civil Rights reached no resolution agreements in school or university sexual-harassment and violence cases despite having more than 1,100 pending complaints.

[事实] A 2025 Cornell survey found that more than one-third of female undergraduates reported experiencing sexual assault during college, an increase from earlier years.

[推测] The programme concludes that Title IX provides uncertain deterrence, limited oversight and heavy administrative burdens without clear evidence that it reduces campus sexual assault.

[15:59] **Astrology’s place in Indian finance**

[事实] A Pew Research Center study found that 44% of Indian adults believe in astrology, while India’s astrology market generates about $7 billion in annual revenue.

[事实] Astrology influences the timing of births, purchases and other life events in India, and some fund managers join WhatsApp groups operated by astrologers.

[事实] These managers may compare astrological advice with conventional financial analysis rather than relying on it exclusively.

[推测] Astrology can function as an additional source of reassurance when investors face decisions under unusually high uncertainty.

[17:55] **How investors and companies use astrological advice**

[事实] One investment-fund manager described astrology as a “fog light” and said he gave it roughly half the weight in his investment decisions.

[事实] He attributed a successful investment in Indian steelmaker Jindal to an astrological association between Mars, red metal and his birth chart.

[事实] Astrologers advise corporate clients on dates for public offerings, deal signings and factory construction.

[事实] Some astrologers also assess executive candidates’ horoscopes and compare them with a company’s perceived character.

[推测] Individual successful trades cannot establish astrology’s predictive power because they may reflect coincidence or selective recall.

[18:57] **Weak evidence but a thriving astrology business**

[事实] Studies of Chinese and Taiwanese investors found that favouring companies associated with the lucky number eight and avoiding those linked to the number four resulted in significant underperformance.

[事实] Online courses claiming to predict stock-market movements through astrology can cost 35,000–45,000 rupees, or as much as $470.

[事实] InstaAstro, a platform connecting traders, investors and astrologers, raised about $12 million from investors in August.

[事实] AstroTalk charges between 14 and 200 rupees per minute for consultations and also sells products such as financial-prosperity bracelets.

[推测] The most reliable financial returns in this market may accrue to businesses selling astrological advice rather than to investors following it.

## 播客点评/总结

The episode’s main strength is its range: it connects street-level reporting, legal analysis and cultural observation without losing sight of the institutions and incentives behind each story. The French segment is especially effective in showing how specific complaints about schools can merge with national political and economic frustration.

The Cornell discussion provides useful statistics and historical context for understanding Title IX’s procedural problems. Its limitation is that the allegations and institutional responses remain contested, while the short format leaves little room to examine competing proposals for reform.

The astrology segment offers a vivid look at how cultural belief interacts with financial decision-making and entrepreneurship. [推测] It will be most valuable to listeners interested in politics, higher education, behavioural finance and the ways uncertainty shapes collective behaviour.
