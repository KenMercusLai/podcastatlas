# Working memory: the surprising decline of dementia

## 概览

This episode of *The Intelligence* leads with a counterintuitive finding: age-adjusted dementia rates have fallen sharply in rich countries over recent decades, even as ageing populations make the total burden feel larger. The discussion separates demographic growth from cohort-level risk, then looks at why prevention, lifestyle, education, and public-health interventions may matter.

The second story examines Pix, Brazil’s instant digital payment system, and why it became a target of President Donald Trump’s tariff regime. The segment argues that Pix has expanded financial inclusion and that accusations of discrimination against American payment firms are weak.

The episode closes with the revival of emo music, using My Chemical Romance’s stadium shows and broader festival demand as evidence that the genre’s appeal has outlasted its mid-2000s peak. The discussion connects the comeback to nostalgia, theatrical performance, and a continuing sense of belonging for fans.

## 分段落总结

[00:42] **Episode lineup**

[事实] Host Jason Palmer introduces the episode as covering dementia, Brazil’s digital payment system Pix, and the return of emo music.
[事实] The first editorial segment focuses on the surprising decline in dementia rates.

[01:16] **Dementia rates are falling in rich countries**

[事实] The episode says dementia inspires particular fear because it threatens memory and identity.
[事实] Jonathan Bousenthal says dementia rates have declined astonishingly over the past 40 years.
[事实] Between 1988 and 2015, the share of older people with dementia fell by 13% per decade across six countries in North America and Europe.
[事实] In the 1980s, about three in ten people in their late 80s had dementia; the rate is now described as roughly one in ten.
[推测] The decline is presented as most evident in richer countries, while poorer countries may bear more of the future dementia burden.

[02:22] **Why dementia still feels like a growing problem**

[事实] Bousenthal says many people assume dementia is increasing because age is the biggest risk factor.
[事实] Dementia risk roughly doubles every five years once people reach their late 60s or 70s.
[事实] Longer lives and the ageing baby-boom generation have led to projections of very large increases in total dementia cases.
[事实] Researchers looking at age-adjusted cohort rates found the decline surprising enough that one researcher delayed publication for two and a half years to recheck the data.

[03:26] **Unclear mechanisms, clearer associations**

[事实] Researchers still do not know the exact mechanisms behind the decline.
[事实] The episode mentions the amyloid hypothesis, involving protein tangles in the brain, but says researchers are unsure whether these are a cause or symptom of dementia.
[事实] Better evidence is emerging about factors associated with higher or lower dementia risk.
[推测] The discussion shifts from searching for one biochemical cause toward identifying practical risks that can be reduced.

[04:07] **Genes matter, but they are not destiny**

[事实] The APOE4 gene is described as strongly associated with dementia risk.
[事实] About 25% of the population has one copy of APOE4 and faces about two to three times higher risk.
[事实] About 2% of the population has two copies, with risk described as about 10 to 15 times higher.
[事实] Many people with APOE4 do not get dementia, and many people without it do get dementia.
[推测] The segment frames genetics as important but insufficient to explain changing dementia rates.

[04:55] **Finland and lifestyle evidence**

[事实] Research from eastern Finland began with efforts to reduce very high rates of heart attacks.
[事实] Finnish public-health work encouraged better diet, more exercise, reduced obesity, and less smoking.
[事实] People who followed the lifestyle guidance had fewer heart attacks and less dementia.
[事实] Later randomized controlled trials in Finland, Sweden, and the UK tested interventions more rigorously.
[推测] The Finnish example suggests that cardiovascular health and brain health are closely linked.

[06:10] **Modifiable risk factors expand beyond smoking and obesity**

[事实] The Lancet Commission on Dementia is described as an authoritative body in the field.
[事实] It estimates that up to 45% of dementia cases could be prevented or postponed through various interventions.
[事实] Medical factors include treating high blood pressure and high cholesterol.
[事实] Untreated hearing loss and untreated depression in middle life are associated with greater dementia risk.
[事实] Better education for children is said to reduce dementia risk later in life.

[07:25] **Shingles vaccine and dementia risk**

[事实] The shingles vaccine is described as a simple and cheap intervention with a large observed effect.
[事实] Natural experiments using vaccine eligibility cutoffs showed about a 20% reduction in dementia risk over the following seven years.
[事实] Some researchers think the protective effect may extend beyond seven years.
[推测] The vaccine example is used to argue that prevention may come from interventions not originally designed for dementia.

[08:16] **Dementia drugs have had limited impact so far**

[事实] Pharmaceutical companies have focused on drugs that target protein tangles in the brains of people with Alzheimer’s.
[事实] The episode says these drugs have had disappointing results, giving only a few months of better cognition at high cost and risk.
[事实] APOE4 carriers are more likely to experience brain swelling or bleeding when taking these drugs.
[事实] GLP-1 drugs for weight loss have not helped people who already have dementia in early studies.
[推测] GLP-1s might still matter if given much earlier, because they address obesity and related risks associated with dementia.

[09:32] **Policy barriers to prevention**

[事实] The episode says public knowledge of risk factors can help people avoid them, similar to reduced smoking.
[事实] Bousenthal argues policymakers should focus on interventions that lower barriers to health improvements.
[事实] He says more money and research should go into promising areas such as the shingles vaccine.
[事实] Cash-strapped governments and health systems are described as focused on immediate crises and struggling to fund prevention.
[推测] The main obstacle is not only scientific uncertainty, but also the political difficulty of funding long-term prevention.

[12:41] **Pix becomes a Brazilian point of pride**

[事实] The episode introduces Pix as a digital payment system launched in Brazil in 2020.
[事实] Brazilians are described as strongly supportive of Pix, with memes and T-shirts showing support.
[事实] Carla Sugirana says Pix has become a source of national pride.
[事实] The segment notes that Pix unexpectedly came under fire from Donald Trump’s tariff regime.

[13:48] **How Pix works and why it spread**

[事实] Pix was created by Brazil’s central bank.
[事实] It lets people send money instantly, 24 hours a day, usually free of charge.
[事实] Users need a phone and a QR code.
[事实] Around 80% of Brazilians use Pix, and it accounts for more than half of all payment transactions in Brazil.
[事实] Pix is described as becoming as normal as cash once was.

[14:26] **Pix and financial inclusion**

[事实] Before Pix, electronic payments often carried fees.
[事实] Many poorer Brazilians relied on cash because digital payments were too expensive.
[事实] Brazil’s central bank estimates that about 70 million people entered the formal financial system after Pix launched.
[推测] The segment treats Pix as a public infrastructure project that helped democratize payments.

[14:59] **The Trump administration’s case against Pix**

[事实] The Trump administration investigated Brazil’s trade practices for a year.
[事实] It concluded that some practices were unfair to American businesses.
[事实] It announced a 25% tariff on a range of Brazilian imports, and Pix was included in the dispute.
[事实] The case against Pix rests on claims that it hurts American payment companies and acts as a protectionist tool.

[15:46] **Why the case against Pix is described as shaky**

[事实] Sugirana says some complaints about Brazil’s trade policies may have merit, but the case against Pix is weak.
[事实] Any licensed financial institution in Brazil, domestic or foreign, can connect to Pix on the same terms.
[事实] The episode says there are no separate rules for domestic and foreign firms.
[事实] Concerns about central-bank governance, data concentration, and single-operator control are described as legitimate but distinct from trade discrimination.

[17:10] **Pix competes more with cash than cards**

[事实] The episode says Pix’s biggest competitor has not been Visa or Mastercard, but cash.
[事实] Credit-card use has risen substantially since Pix was introduced.
[事实] Debit-card use and other electronic payments have also continued to grow.
[推测] The data undercuts the claim that Pix has directly displaced American card networks in Brazil.

[17:39] **Long-term concerns and limits to replication**

[事实] Pix could eventually pressure Visa and Mastercard by lowering merchant payment fees.
[事实] Sugirana says lower prices from competition are not unfair trade practices.
[事实] Pix might worry American card networks if other Latin American countries copied it.
[事实] Brazil’s success depended on a trusted and powerful central bank, mandatory bank participation, and a large cash-reliant domestic market.
[事实] Mexico launched a similar system in 2019, but by 2024 only around 1.6% of Mexicans were using it.

[19:23] **Attacks on Pix may have backfired**

[事实] Sugirana says the attack on Pix has spectacularly backfired.
[事实] Brazil’s president Lula is described as a potential political beneficiary.
[事实] Pix commands broad support across Brazil’s political spectrum because almost everyone uses it.
[事实] Memes defending Pix have spread on social media since Trump’s tariffs were announced.
[推测] The controversy strengthened Pix as a symbol of resistance to perceived foreign interference.

[20:31] **My Chemical Romance and emo’s return**

[事实] The final segment begins with My Chemical Romance, an emo band known from the 2000s that broke up in 2013.
[事实] The band began a world tour marking the 20th anniversary of *The Black Parade*.
[事实] The show is described as theatrical as well as musical.

[21:17] **Political satire on stage**

[事实] My Chemical Romance’s tour staging is described as a political satire about authoritarianism.
[事实] Frontman Gerard Way stayed in character throughout the set.
[事实] He wore a bandleader’s uniform and introduced a fictional ruler on stage.
[推测] The concert’s theatrical framing suggests the band is leaning into spectacle rather than simple nostalgia.

[21:55] **Fans embrace emo performance**

[事实] Fans wore thick black eyeliner, side-swept fringes, fishnets, and studded belts.
[事实] One fan, Nezray, dressed as the mourning bride from one of the band’s music videos and carried roses.
[事实] The segment says fans rose to the band’s call for theatrics.
[推测] The audience’s costumes show that emo’s revival is also a participatory identity culture.

[22:32] **Stadium demand after years away**

[事实] My Chemical Romance filled London’s Wembley Stadium, with 90,000 seats, for three nights in one month.
[事实] The band did this despite not releasing music since 2008.
[事实] The segment says few acts have generated such demand after so long away.
[事实] Oasis and Spice Girls are named as rare comparable examples at Wembley over the past decade.

[23:02] **Emo’s roots and commercial revival**

[事实] Emo is described as short for emotional hardcore.
[事实] It evolved from punk in the 1980s and 1990s.
[事实] The genre is marked by introspective lyrics about difficult topics such as addiction and suicide.
[事实] Other emo bands are now seeing ticket sales above what they achieved during the genre’s mid-2000s peak.
[事实] All Time Low and Jimmy Eat World are given as examples of bands playing major London shows.

[24:05] **Festivals and nostalgia build the revival**

[事实] The When We Were Young festival launched in Las Vegas in 2022 and gathered many major emo acts.
[事实] Vans Warped Tour returned for its 30th anniversary with dates across the U.S., Canada, and Mexico.
[推测] These events show that the revival is not limited to one band, but part of a wider live-music market for emo.

[24:31] **Emo audiences span generations**

[事实] The crowds include millennials, described as “elder emos,” sometimes bringing their children.
[事实] A five-year-old named Iris is mentioned as one of the youngest attendees at the My Chemical Romance concert.
[事实] Gen Z appears highly visible in the crowds, even though many discovered the bands after they stopped touring.
[推测] Emo’s audience now combines original fans, younger listeners, and families.

[25:12] **More than a phase**

[事实] The segment links the revival partly to wider Y2K nostalgia.
[事实] Fans repeatedly told the reporter that emo encouraged people to be their weirdest selves.
[事实] One fan described emo spaces as including people who were neurodivergent, queer, and weird.
[事实] The segment concludes that heavy eyeliner and adolescent angst are “here to stay.”
[推测] The closing argument is that emo endures because it offers belonging as much as musical nostalgia.

## 播客点评/总结

[推测] The episode is valuable because it combines public-health analysis, trade-policy criticism, and cultural reporting without treating any one topic as isolated. The dementia segment is the strongest for practical takeaway, because it distinguishes rising total case numbers from falling age-adjusted risk.

[推测] A highlight is the repeated focus on evidence that complicates simple narratives: dementia may be less inevitable than feared, Pix may not be harming American card firms, and emo’s revival may be more than nostalgia. The episode’s limitation is that several causal links, especially in dementia prevention and future drug effects, remain uncertain within the transcript.

[推测] This episode is best suited for listeners who want concise explanations of current affairs and science with some cultural breadth. Listeners looking for a deep medical guide to dementia, a technical payments-policy paper, or a full music-history documentary would need more detail than this episode provides.
