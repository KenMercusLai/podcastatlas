# The Social Radars Season Two Wrap-Up

## 概览

This episode wraps up season two of *The Social Radars*. The hosts reflect on the season as a strong run of interviews, thank listeners for the large audience response, and note that ratings and reviews have helped the show.

The discussion centers on three especially memorable moments: the timing of the Emmett Shear interview before his brief OpenAI spotlight, Parker Conrad being able to tell his side of the story, and Brian Chesky recounting how Airbnb navigated COVID.

The hosts also confirm that season three is planned, though the timing is not finalized. They tease new interviews, a special guest who is not a YC founder, and more video clips on YouTube.
