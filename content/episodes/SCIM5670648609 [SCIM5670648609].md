# Introducing the Huberman Lab Podcast

## 概览

Andrew Huberman introduces the Huberman Lab Podcast as a show about science and science-based tools for everyday life. He identifies himself as a professor of neurobiology and ophthalmology at Stanford School of Medicine and explains that his recent public teaching and podcast appearances led him to launch this format.

The podcast will differ from many shows by spending an entire month on one broad topic, rather than switching topics or guests every episode. Example topics include motivation and focus, with attention to what is known, what is unknown, practical tools, and barriers.

A central feature is audience participation: listeners are asked to suggest topics and upvote comments so the show can respond to what people want to learn. Huberman also clarifies that “tools” will include behavioral practices, things to avoid, nutrition, supplements, prescription drugs, brain-machine interfaces, and monitoring or intervention devices.
