# Dr. Rhonda Patrick on Exercise, Fasting, Supplements, and Inflammation

## 概览

This episode is a long-form discussion between Andrew Huberman and Dr. Rhonda Patrick about practical health protocols for longevity, cognitive function, metabolic health, and disease prevention. The conversation repeatedly returns to one organizing theme: useful stressors such as exercise, fasting, heat, and some dietary compounds can be beneficial when they are brief, recoverable, and placed in the right daily rhythm.

Patrick emphasizes that exercise is the foundation, not an optional add-on. She describes her own mix of resistance training, high-intensity interval work, running, hiking, and short bursts of activity, while also stressing that even unstructured "exercise snacks" can have large associations with lower mortality risk.

The second major thread is inflammation. The discussion links gut permeability, LPS, visceral fat, sleep loss, meal timing, cortisol dysregulation, and cardiovascular or brain health. Supplements are discussed mainly through a safety-and-evidence lens, with recurring attention to creatine, omega-3s, vitamin D, magnesium, glutamine, NAC, multivitamins, urolithin A, sulforaphane, NR/NMN, L-carnitine, and Alpha GPC.

## 分段落总结

[00:00] **Exercise Snacks and Episode Frame**

[事实] The episode opens with Patrick describing short bursts of unstructured vigorous movement lasting about one to three minutes.

[事实] She says doing three minutes of these bursts three times per day, totaling nine minutes, is associated with 40% lower all-cause mortality, 40% lower cancer-related mortality, and 50% lower cardiovascular-related mortality.

[事实] Huberman introduces Patrick as a biomedical scientist and public health educator and frames the episode around exercise, nutrition, supplementation, sauna, inflammation, visceral fat, arterial plaque, and disease prevention.

[03:39] **Jump Rope, Movement Variety, and Bone Health**

[事实] Patrick says her early athletic background came from competitive jump rope through the San Diego Sandskippers.

[事实] She describes jump rope as cardiovascular exercise with weight-bearing benefits that may support bone density.

[事实] Huberman notes her posture, and they discuss movement beyond standard linear gym patterns, though Patrick says she is not the expert on rope-flow-style cross-body movement.

[07:04] **Exercise as Personal Hygiene**

[事实] Patrick says exercise is a non-negotiable part of her personal hygiene, similar to brushing her teeth.

[事实] Her routine is about five to six hours per week, combining cardiovascular training, resistance training, vigorous intervals, running, and family hikes.

[事实] She says her non-negotiables are cardiorespiratory work and resistance training, with intervals often reaching around 80% of maximum heart rate.

[事实] Her structured workouts include two CrossFit-like sessions with heavy strength work followed by HIIT, plus two longer sessions with friends using rowing, assault bike, skier, and lighter lifting.

[12:30] **Brain Benefits of Vigorous Exercise**

[事实] Patrick says even 10 minutes of vigorous exercise can improve brain function, mood, and executive function.

[事实] She cites studies showing about a 14% improvement in executive function and improved processing speed after vigorous exercise.

[事实] She says higher-intensity exercise increased plasma serotonin in one study, and that increase correlated with better impulse control.

[推测] For Patrick, the cognitive and emotional benefits are a major reason that short workouts still matter on busy or travel-heavy days.

[17:36] **No Phone During Workouts**

[事实] Patrick says she does not bring her phone to workouts, apart from wearing a watch for emergencies.

[事实] She says she prefers real-life social interaction during workouts and sees social media as distracting and generally bad for people's brains.

[事实] Huberman says his own workouts improve when the phone is not allowed in the gym.

[19:16] **Heavy Lifting and Mental Toughness**

[事实] Patrick's strength training centers on multi-joint lifts such as cleans, front squats, back squats, and deadlifts.

[事实] She often works from sets of five or six down to singles, then may add lighter drop sets.

[事实] She describes heavy compound lifting as mentally difficult and says the stress of doing it makes the rest of the day feel easier.

[事实] Huberman connects this to the anterior midcingulate cortex, saying it appears to enlarge when people do things they do not want to do.

[27:12] **Protein Intake and Training Priority**

[事实] Huberman asks about the common target of one gram of protein per pound of body weight.

[事实] Patrick says she generally aims around 1.2 to 1.6 grams per kilogram of body weight, often about 1.3 to 1.4 grams per kilogram.

[事实] She says trying to push protein higher led her to eat more calories and gain weight, so she adjusted downward.

[事实] Patrick says people should become more obsessed with training and less obsessed with protein, while still using protein to complement training.

[31:38] **Patrick's Current Diet Pattern**

[事实] Patrick says her meals are mostly healthy proteins such as homemade turkey burgers, chicken, wild Alaskan salmon, and grass-fed steak.

[事实] She usually pairs protein with greens such as collard greens, kale, or salad.

[事实] She says her portions are smaller now and that she sometimes uses intermittent fasting.

[事实] Huberman says he eats oatmeal, rice, some bread, protein foods, vegetables, and fruit, but feels lousy after some more processed starches.

[33:09] **Postprandial Inflammation and LPS**

[事实] Patrick explains that meals can transiently open gut tight junctions and allow some LPS from gram-negative bacteria to enter circulation.

[事实] She says large meals, ultra-processed foods, refined sugar plus saturated fat, and saturated fat without a fiber matrix can increase this LPS response.

[事实] She describes the postprandial inflammatory response as LPS entering the system and activating the immune system.

[事实] She says healthier whole foods generally create less of this response.

[40:10] **LPS, LDL, and Cardiovascular Risk**

[事实] Patrick says LPS can bind LDL particles through lipid interactions and attach around APO-B.

[事实] She explains that this can interfere with LDL recycling by the liver and contribute to small dense LDL lodging in the arterial wall.

[事实] She says macrophages respond to the LPS signal and can form foam cells, an early step in atherosclerosis.

[事实] She uses this mechanism to connect gut permeability and diet quality with cardiovascular health.

[45:34] **Gut-Brain Inflammation**

[事实] Patrick clarifies that LPS, rather than live bacteria, is the main gut-derived inflammatory signal she is discussing.

[事实] She says LPS can activate immune and glial responses, affect the blood-brain barrier, and contribute to neuroinflammation.

[事实] She frames inflammation as an upstream driver of aging-related diseases such as type 2 diabetes, cancer, and Alzheimer's disease.

[事实] She also notes that emotional stress and inadequate sleep can contribute to inflammation.

[47:34] **Glutamine for Gut and Immune Stress**

[事实] Patrick says evidence for glutamine in humans is limited, but mechanistic and animal evidence suggests it can support gut epithelial energy metabolism.

[事实] She says glutamine is important for immune cell activation, including T-cell activation.

[事实] She discusses endurance-athlete studies where high-dose glutamine was associated with fewer respiratory tract infections.

[事实] Patrick says she takes five grams daily and may increase to 15 or 20 grams in divided doses during exposure, travel, or illness season.

[推测] Her caution around existing colon or liver tumors reflects the broader concern that proliferating cancer cells can use nutrients such as glutamine.

[54:54] **NAC and Antioxidant Timing**

[事实] Huberman says he uses NAC more when traveling, exposed to sick people, or feeling run down.

[事实] Patrick says her concern with daily NAC is that it is a powerful antioxidant and could blunt beneficial oxidative signals from exercise.

[事实] She says short bursts of oxidation from exercise help drive adaptation, while chronic oxidative stress is harmful.

[事实] She warns that too much antioxidant intake can push toward reductive stress, so she prefers NAC situationally rather than as a constant daily intervention.

[61:08] **Stopping Food Three Hours Before Bed**

[事实] Patrick says she tries to stop eating three hours before bed.

[事实] She says eating and digesting activate sympathetic physiology, while sleep should favor parasympathetic rest and recovery.

[事实] She cites a study in which stopping food three hours before bed improved nighttime blood pressure dipping and heart rate lowering.

[事实] She says this cardiovascular reset may translate into lower cardiovascular event risk, and other studies suggest sleep can also improve.

[64:36] **Cortisol as Rhythm, Not Villain**

[事实] Patrick says cortisol is circadian and should peak in the morning, then fall later in the day.

[事实] She says cortisol binds glucocorticoid and mineralocorticoid receptors and can influence a large portion of gene expression.

[事实] She distinguishes beneficial spikes from exercise, intermittent fasting, and possibly cold exposure from chronic slow-drip stress caused by emotional stress or sleep deprivation.

[事实] She says chronic dysregulation can reduce cortisol's anti-inflammatory function.

[68:09] **Fasted Training and Individual Response**

[事实] Huberman says messages about women, fasted training, deliberate cold, and cortisol have often been distorted online.

[事实] Patrick says she listens to how she feels and sometimes eats before training if her body needs it.

[事实] She often trains fasted because she is practicing intermittent fasting and values the cognitive benefits.

[事实] She says fasted training can reduce performance during longer or harder sessions, so the right choice depends on goals, workload, and how the person feels.

[71:02] **Visceral Fat, Menopause, and Metabolic Risk**

[事实] Patrick says intermittent fasting helped her reduce belly fat, which she identifies as visceral fat.

[事实] She explains that visceral fat surrounds organs, acts like a metabolically active endocrine tissue, and secretes hormones and inflammatory factors.

[事实] She says high visceral fat is associated with double the risk of early death and a 44% higher chance of cancer.

[事实] She says estrogen helps direct fat storage subcutaneously, so declining estrogen during perimenopause and menopause can shift fat toward visceral storage.

[73:34] **Measuring and Driving Visceral Fat**

[事实] Patrick says DEXA can directly measure visceral fat, while waist circumference is a useful proxy.

[事实] She gives 35 inches for women and 40 inches for men as waist thresholds associated with higher visceral fat.

[事实] She says about 70% of women over 50 and 50% of men over 52 have high visceral fat.

[事实] She explains that visceral fat releases free fatty acids near the portal vein, contributing to liver fat and insulin resistance.

[事实] She cites a study where five days of excess calories from high saturated fat and high sugar caused young men to gain visceral and liver fat without gaining weight.

[85:41] **Intermittent Fasting and the Metabolic Switch**

[事实] Patrick describes intermittent fasting as both a behavioral tool for reducing calories and a way to trigger a metabolic switch.

[事实] She says it usually takes about 11 to 12 hours, depending on meal size and exercise, to deplete liver glycogen enough to increase fat burning and ketone production.

[事实] She says ketones such as beta-hydroxybutyrate can act as signaling molecules and may support BDNF and GABA.

[事实] Her usual pattern is to sleep around 10 p.m., stop eating around 7 p.m., and take her first meal around 11 a.m. or noon, often with an eating window around 11 to 7.

[事实] She sometimes eats two meals and sometimes three, with a small protein-focused middle meal.

[100:23] **Autophagy and Repair Mode**

[事实] Patrick says the metabolic switch is also a repair mode, not only a fat-burning state.

[事实] She explains that autophagy is always occurring at a basal level but can be increased by fasting and exercise.

[事实] She says liver, brain, and muscle appear more responsive to fasting-induced autophagy than blood cells, which makes human measurement difficult.

[事实] She defines autophagy as a cleanup process for cellular debris such as DNA fragments and misfolded or aggregated proteins.

[104:09] **Circadian Insulin Sensitivity and Late Eating**

[事实] Patrick discusses studies showing that the same meal can create different insulin responses depending on whether it is eaten in the morning, afternoon, or evening.

[事实] She says insulin sensitivity is highest earlier in the day and lower near natural bedtime.

[事实] She explains that rising melatonin before sleep prepares organs such as the pancreas for rest, reducing insulin output.

[事实] She concludes that meals, especially carbohydrate-rich meals, are best avoided in the last three hours before sleep.

[106:28] **Sleep Loss and Exercise as a Buffer**

[事实] Huberman asks whether exercise can offset inflammation and insulin resistance after mild sleep deprivation.

[事实] Patrick says even acute sleep deprivation can cause insulin resistance, but exercise can help negate some of that effect.

[事实] She describes seeing prediabetic-like glucose patterns on a continuous glucose monitor when sleep-deprived as a new parent, and improvement when she resumed HIIT-style spin classes.

[事实] She says exercise increases insulin sensitivity and muscle glucose transport.

[事实] She cites longitudinal data suggesting that people sleeping under seven hours who meet physical activity guidelines can have mortality risk similar to people getting adequate sleep.

[113:13] **VILPA and Exercise Snacks**

[事实] Patrick discusses vigorous intermittent lifestyle physical activity, or VILPA, measured with accelerometers rather than questionnaires.

[事实] Examples include sprinting up stairs, chasing a child or grandchild, playing with a dog, or running to catch a bus.

[事实] She says bursts of one to three minutes can add up meaningfully, even in people who do not identify as exercisers.

[事实] She gives the nine-minute-per-day example as associated with large reductions in all-cause, cancer-related, and cardiovascular-related mortality.

[116:21] **Breaking Up Sedentary Time**

[事实] Patrick says structured exercise snacks can help people who do not naturally get unstructured bursts of movement.

[事实] She cites studies where doing 10 bodyweight squats every 45 minutes over a seven-and-a-half-hour workday regulated blood glucose better than a 30-minute walk.

[事实] She says sedentary time is an independent risk factor and calls sedentary behavior a disease-like problem.

[118:36] **Cardiorespiratory Fitness and Longevity**

[事实] Patrick says low cardiorespiratory fitness predicts mortality as badly as, or worse than, major risks such as smoking, hypertension, or cardiovascular disease.

[事实] She says moving from low to low-normal cardiorespiratory fitness is associated with about two additional years of life expectancy.

[事实] She says moving to high fitness is associated with about five additional years compared with low fitness.

[事实] Huberman cites Steve Magness's view that a practical measure of cardiorespiratory fitness is how fast someone can run a mile.

[125:16] **Creatine Basics and Muscle Performance**

[事实] Huberman says he has taken creatine for decades and remembers older loading protocols.

[事实] Patrick explains that loading protocols were useful in studies because five grams per day can take three to four weeks to saturate muscle creatine stores.

[事实] She says creatine monohydrate is the most researched form.

[事实] She explains that creatine is stored as creatine phosphate and supports cellular energy, especially during training.

[事实] She says creatine does not directly act like an anabolic protein but can increase training volume and thereby support adaptation.

[130:11] **Creatine for Brain Stress**

[事实] Patrick says the brain makes creatine, but the brain is somewhat resistant to supplemental creatine uptake because muscle is greedy for it.

[事实] She says small studies suggest that around 10 grams per day can increase creatine in some brain regions.

[事实] She says creatine appears most relevant for the brain under stress, including sleep deprivation, traumatic brain injury, mild cognitive impairment, and early Alzheimer's disease.

[事实] She discusses a sleep-deprivation study using about 0.35 grams per kilogram body weight and studies using around 20 grams in older adults with cognitive impairment.

[事实] Patrick takes 10 grams daily in divided doses and may take 20 to 25 grams during travel or sleep-deprived situations.

[143:18] **Safety, Cost, and Experimental Use**

[事实] Huberman proposes asking whether a supplement appears safe, whether one wants to be in the experimental or control group, and whether one can afford it.

[事实] Patrick agrees that safety should be the first question.

[事实] They caution that small sample sizes and imperfect studies do not make a topic useless, but claims should be toned down.

[推测] This framework is used throughout the episode to separate low-risk experimentation from unsupported certainty.

[145:20] **Omega-3s, Lovaza, and Inflammation Resolution**

[事实] Huberman says he began taking Lovaza, a prescription omega-3, after a prior conversation with Patrick and saw improvements in his blood profiles.

[事实] Patrick says omega-3s are one of the easiest high-impact interventions for health and inflammation.

[事实] She says about two grams per day can move many people from a low omega-3 index around 4% to a higher index around 8%.

[事实] She says EPA and DHA form resolvins and protectins that help resolve inflammation rather than simply suppressing the immune system.

[事实] She says high omega-3 index is associated with longer life expectancy and lower sudden cardiac death risk.

[150:51] **Omega-3s, Vitamin D, Resistance Training, and Cell Membranes**

[事实] Patrick says randomized controlled trials support omega-3 benefits for cardiovascular outcomes.

[事实] She discusses a Swiss trial in which omega-3 slowed epigenetic aging clocks, while vitamin D and resistance training showed synergy when combined with omega-3.

[事实] She says the three together were associated with 66% lower invasive cancer in that study.

[事实] She explains that DHA and EPA are incorporated into cell membranes, helping membrane fluidity in endothelial cells, neurons, receptors, and transporters.

[事实] Huberman and Patrick both say they take vitamin D and get sunlight, while noting that sunlight does more than support vitamin D production.

[156:51] **Magnesium Forms and Requirements**

[事实] Huberman asks about magnesium L-threonate and magnesium bisglycinate for sleep and cognition.

[事实] Patrick says magnesium L-threonate is thought, based on limited data, to cross the blood-brain barrier more readily.

[事实] She says magnesium glycinate or bisglycinate includes glycine, which she likes for sleep.

[事实] She usually takes magnesium glycinate a couple of hours before bed and may add more magnesium when she has exercised and sweated more.

[事实] She gives daily magnesium requirements of roughly 300 to 350 milligrams for women and 350 to 400 milligrams for men, with athletic needs possibly about 20% higher.

[事实] She says magnesium is needed for DNA repair, energy production, neurotransmission, and activation of vitamin D.

[166:33] **Multivitamins and Covering Nutrient Bases**

[事实] Patrick says she takes a multivitamin to cover nutrient bases and says claims that all multivitamins are useless are wrong.

[事实] She cites three large randomized controlled trials from the COSMOS work in older adults.

[事实] She says older adults taking a multivitamin showed about 2.1 years less global brain aging and about 4.9 years less episodic-memory aging.

[事实] Her core supplement list includes vitamin D, omega-3s, creatine, magnesium, and a multivitamin.

[169:55] **CoQ10 and Urolithin A**

[事实] Patrick says she takes ubiquinol, the reduced form of CoQ10, for mitochondrial health.

[事实] She says urolithin A is formed from ellagitannins found in foods such as pomegranate, raspberries, and walnuts through gut microbiome metabolism.

[事实] She says urolithin A appears to stimulate mitophagy, a mitochondria-specific form of autophagy.

[事实] She describes human data on blood cells and muscle biopsy as emerging, with possible benefits for endurance performance, immune function, and inflammation.

[171:51] **Sulforaphane, Detoxification, and Plastics**

[事实] Patrick says she takes a glucoraphanin-based sulforaphane precursor supplement.

[事实] She explains that glucoraphanin in cruciferous vegetables can be converted into sulforaphane when the plant is crushed or chewed.

[事实] She describes sulforaphane as a strong dietary activator of the NRF2 pathway, which turns on detoxification genes.

[事实] She cites human studies showing broccoli sprout sulforaphane extract increased excretion of air-pollution compounds such as benzene.

[推测] Patrick expects similar detoxification logic may apply to BPA because the same enzyme systems are involved, but she says human BPA data are not yet available.

[178:49] **BPC-157 and Peptide Caution**

[事实] Huberman uses BPC-157 as an example of a compound with many animal studies but almost no strong human data.

[事实] Patrick says her first question is whether something is safe.

[事实] She emphasizes source quality, regulation concerns, and qualified prescribing when people use peptides or similar compounds.

[事实] Huberman says he worries about growth-hormone-pathway stimulation if hidden tumors are present.

[推测] Their discussion frames BPC-157 as a short-term injury-related experiment rather than a daily lifelong protocol.

[182:37] **NR, NMN, and NAD Precursors**

[事实] Patrick says she takes nicotinamide riboside, and Huberman says he takes sublingual NMN.

[事实] Huberman says NMN makes his hair and nails grow faster, which also makes him wonder what else might grow faster.

[事实] Patrick says she is cautiously experimenting with NR for mitochondrial health, fertility-related mechanisms, energy, and recovery.

[事实] She says she is not convinced NR or NMN will directly make her live longer.

[事实] She ranks omega-3s higher than NAD precursors in her supplement priorities.

[186:27] **L-Carnitine, TMAO, and Mitochondria**

[事实] Huberman asks about L-carnitine for mitochondrial effects and fertility-related sperm or egg quality.

[事实] Patrick says Bruce Ames's lab studied L-carnitine with alpha-lipoic acid for mitochondrial health.

[事实] She says her husband takes a supplement containing L-carnitine and alpha-lipoic acid.

[事实] They discuss that oral L-carnitine can be converted to TMAO depending on gut bacteria, and that TMAO can be measured.

[事实] Patrick says she does not have enough data to give an opinion on nattokinase for blood lipid profiles.

[事实] She says she is concerned enough about microplastics to reduce exposure, but less concerned about them than about failing to get adequate nutrients and micronutrients.

[事实] She tries not to drink from plastic water bottles when possible.

[事实] They discuss studies showing glass bottles can contain more microplastic particles than plastic bottles because of lid paint, but that the particles may be larger and therefore less absorbed.

[事实] Another reason is that heating and reheating polyunsaturated fats can accelerate oxidation, and she does not want to consume oxidized lipids.

[事实] Patrick says she has recently paused sauna use but previously did sauna or hot-tub heat exposure about five nights per week.

## 播客点评/总结

[推测] The strongest value of this episode is its mechanism-first style. Rather than presenting health as a checklist, Patrick repeatedly explains why a behavior might work, where the evidence is strong, and where she is experimenting based on safety and plausibility.

[推测] The episode is most useful for listeners who already care about training, metabolic health, and supplements, and who want a framework for prioritizing habits. Exercise, sleep timing, visceral-fat reduction, omega-3 status, magnesium adequacy, and meal timing emerge as higher-priority foundations than most experimental compounds.

[推测] The main limitation is density. Many topics are covered quickly, and some recommendations rely on emerging evidence, small trials, animal data, or Patrick's personal experience. The episode is best treated as a source of hypotheses and decision frameworks, not as individualized medical advice.
