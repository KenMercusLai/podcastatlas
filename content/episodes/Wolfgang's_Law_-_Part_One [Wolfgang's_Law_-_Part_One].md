# Wolfgang’s Law, Part One: How Baltimore Reframed Gun Violence

## 概览

Baltimore Mayor Brandon Scott inherited one of America’s most persistent homicide crises. After two years with little improvement, homicides fell sharply from 2023 onward, raising the episode’s central question: what changed?

The city’s strategy begins with “Wolfgang’s Law”—the finding that serious violence is concentrated among a remarkably small share of the population. In Baltimore’s Western District, analysis identified roughly 700 people across 18 tightly connected groups as the core population associated with gun violence.

Instead of treating crime as a citywide, undifferentiated threat, Baltimore uses weekly meetings to examine individual cases, relationships, grievances, and risks of retaliation. Police work alongside pastors, counselors, and community outreach workers who can intervene through trust and personal influence.

The episode argues that most homicide is expressive rather than calculated: it grows from anger, grief, feuds, and revenge among people who know one another. Preventing violence therefore requires close attention to social networks and credible community figures who can interrupt an escalating conflict.

## 分段落总结

[01:46] **Brandon Scott’s Baltimore upbringing**

[事实] Scott describes growing up in Park Heights amid shootings, drug dealing, overdoses, vacant housing, and the closure of libraries and recreation centers.

[事实] He says police detained and handcuffed him seven times between roughly ages 13 and 22 simply for being Black and outside.

[事实] Scott estimates that more than ten people he knew while growing up have died.

[03:46] **A city defined by persistent violence**

[事实] Baltimore had long ranked alongside St. Louis, Detroit, and New Orleans among the American cities with the highest homicide rates.

[事实] The city experienced a lasting heroin epidemic and had five police commissioners in seven years.

[事实] When Scott took office at the end of 2020, Baltimore had recorded 335 homicides, just below its all-time high.

[05:10] **A public commitment to reduce homicides**

[事实] Scott set a target of reducing homicides by 15 percent each year, despite objections from staff who did not want him to announce a specific number.

[事实] Homicides rose to 337 during his first year and showed no improvement the following year, intensifying public skepticism.

[事实] Baltimore subsequently recorded declines of almost 21 percent in 2023, 23 percent in 2024, and 31 percent in 2025, when the total fell to 133.

[事实] The episode says the city may fall below 100 homicides for the first time since the 1960s.

[06:11] **American violence as a product of choices**

[事实] Malcolm Gladwell introduces the episode as part of a series connected to his book *The American Way of Killing: The Invention of an Epidemic*.

[事实] He argues that America’s exceptional gun violence is not inevitable but results from social choices, historical legacies, legal concepts, and misunderstandings about homicide.

[事实] Baltimore serves as the case study for examining a possible route out of the crisis.

[07:49] **Conditions in the Western District**

[事实] Lieutenant Brian Ford recalls that Penn North once had thousands of people on the streets, with illegal drug activity on nearly every corner.

[事实] Ford says crime near the Western District station became so severe that officers worried about reaching the building safely.

[事实] By the time of the recorded ride-along, the streets were less crowded and police could focus on specific people of concern.

[12:16] **The weekly violence-reduction meeting**

[事实] Baltimore’s homicide-prevention effort is centered in the Western District, where more than one-third of residents live below the poverty line and approximately one-third of the housing is vacant.

[事实] A weekly meeting brings together police officers, pastors, behavioral counselors, outreach workers, and political representatives.

[事实] Participants review recent violence, individual cases, planned notifications, and follow-up services.

[事实] The process grew from a partnership between Baltimore and the University of Pennsylvania’s Crime and Justice Policy Lab.

[14:26] **Identifying the small network driving violence**

[事实] A 2021 analysis found that those involved in shootings were overwhelmingly Black males between 16 and 34 years old.

[事实] Eighty-one percent had previous contact with the criminal justice system, with an average of 12 prior arrests.

[事实] Most shootings were group-related, and victims and shooters commonly knew one another.

[事实] Researchers identified 18 active groups comprising roughly 700 people within a Western District population of about 40,000.

[15:35] **Why 700 is a manageable number**

[事实] Traditional policing would distribute resources across all 40,000 Western District residents, while the analysis narrowed the gun-violence problem to approximately 700 people.

[事实] The episode compares this population to a typical middle school, arguing that 25 well-connected participants could plausibly know something about nearly everyone involved.

[事实] During one case review, participants immediately recognized a homicide victim through teaching and community outreach connections.

[17:30] **Wolfgang’s Law**

[事实] Criminologist Marvin Wolfgang studied 9,945 boys born in Philadelphia in 1945 who remained in the city until age 18.

[事实] He found that 627 boys—about 6 percent of the cohort—committed more than half of all recorded offenses, including all but one homicide and three-quarters of the rapes and robberies.

[事实] Gladwell names this concentration of offending “Wolfgang’s Law.”

[事实] Comparable analyses found about 400 central individuals in a Chicago neighborhood of 50,000 and linked half of New Orleans shootings to one-half of one percent of the city’s population.

[21:38] **Violence concentration beyond the United States**

[事实] Criminologist David Kennedy encountered a similar pattern in a Stockholm community where business owners feared that crime would force them to close.

[事实] Residents initially attributed the problem to 30 young people in a community of 50,000.

[事实] After reconsidering the social network, they concluded that the principal drivers were five local men and one person in Turkey.

[23:07] **From population-wide policing to precision prevention**

[事实] The episode compares personalized cancer vaccines with individualized violence prevention.

[事实] If violence is concentrated among a known network, officials can examine each case rather than treating an entire population as a generalized threat.

[事实] Baltimore’s meetings place individuals and their relationships at the center of prevention efforts.

[推测] The medical analogy suggests that precision and case-specific knowledge may make public-safety interventions both more efficient and less indiscriminate.

[24:32] **Expressive violence and retaliation**

[事实] Criminologists distinguish instrumental violence, used to achieve a calculated objective, from expressive violence arising from strong emotions.

[事实] The episode argues that most homicide is expressive, involving personal grievances, revenge, and conflicts between people who know each other.

[事实] Meeting participants assess who is connected to a shooter or victim, who is grieving, and who may convert that grief into retaliation.

[事实] Social media sometimes explicitly identifies the next intended target, allowing outreach workers to offer protection or relocation.

[28:02] **Immediate intervention through trusted relationships**

[事实] When the meeting identifies a young man at risk of retaliating for a friend’s death, a participant offers to reach him through his father.

[事实] The participant plans to act as a moral voice based on an existing personal relationship.

[事实] Throughout the hour-long meeting, no one uses the word “arrest.”

[事实] Many participants are unarmed community figures whose influence comes from familiarity, trust, and local standing.

[29:56] **Termite and the practice of soft power**

[事实] Termite is a widely known Baltimore outreach worker who mediates disputes and connects senior citizens with young people.

[事实] She and her husband regularly provided food to children who might otherwise go hungry, paying for meals themselves.

[事实] After a man injured a woman during a fight, the woman’s armed nephew began searching for him.

[事实] Termite confirmed at the hospital that the aunt was safe, then approached the nephew and asked him not to retaliate.

[32:18] **Moral standing prevents a killing**

[事实] The young man agreed to abandon retaliation because he remembered that Termite had once bought him his first pair of Jordans, school uniforms, and a coat.

[事实] Termite did not remember helping him because such assistance was routine for her.

[事实] She concludes that she saved a life that night.

[事实] Gladwell presents this kind of trusted conversation as central to Baltimore’s decline from roughly 350 annual homicides toward a possible total below 100.

[推测] The story illustrates how long-term community care can create moral authority that becomes decisive during a brief moment of crisis.

[34:09] **A warning about sustaining success**

[事实] A preview of the following episode describes an intervention associated with an almost 90 percent homicide reduction during its first summer in Minneapolis.

[事实] The speaker says local leaders allowed the program to collapse by the end of that year.

[事实] The preview characterizes this as a recurring pattern of similar interventions producing success and later falling apart.

## 播客点评/总结

The episode’s strongest contribution is its clear explanation of violence concentration. By connecting Wolfgang’s historical research with evidence from Baltimore, Chicago, New Orleans, and Stockholm, it challenges the assumption that serious violence is evenly distributed across dangerous neighborhoods.

Its account of the Baltimore meetings also gives the argument practical substance. The discussion moves beyond statistics to show how police, researchers, clergy, and outreach workers translate network knowledge into timely interventions.

[推测] The episode’s principal limitation is that this first installment emphasizes the strategy’s logic and success stories more than its possible errors, ethical risks, or long-term durability. The closing preview signals that those complications will receive greater attention in the next episode.

[推测] This episode is especially valuable for listeners interested in criminology, urban policy, policing, community violence intervention, and alternatives to broad enforcement-based responses.
