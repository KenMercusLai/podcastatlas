# Introducing *Revisionist History* Season Six

## Overview

Malcolm Gladwell previews the sixth season of *Revisionist History*, presenting it as a more unrestrained and outward-looking installment than previous seasons. After being confined at home, he travels again—beginning with a pursuit in an Arizona parking lot—and promises stories driven by confrontation, curiosity, and adventure.

The trailer rapidly introduces subjects including Disney, cycling with Lance Armstrong, communists, underwater episodes, and a proposed retelling intended to inspire nine-year-old girls. Rather than explaining these stories in depth, it uses brief, provocative excerpts to establish the season’s energetic and unpredictable tone.

The ten-episode season was announced to begin on June 24 through Pushkin Industries. A Pushkin subscription promotion says subscribers can hear all ten episodes without interruptions.

## Segmented Summary

[01:42] **Gladwell Returns to the World**

[Fact] Gladwell says the season begins with him running after traveling by plane to Arizona and reaching an Alamo Drafthouse parking lot outside Phoenix.

[Fact] He contrasts this season with earlier serious, thoughtful, and considered seasons, saying he has never “quite lost it” as he has this year.

[Inference] The chase imagery frames the new season as more physical, spontaneous, and confrontational than the show’s previous outings.

[02:22] **Taking Aim at Disney**

[Fact] After returning from Arizona, Gladwell appears on Jimmy Kimmel’s show and says he went there to challenge Disney and the “Magic Kingdom.”

[Fact] The exchange jokes that Disney executives may be watching and could try to cut him off.

[Inference] The humor suggests that at least one forthcoming story will critically examine Disney or a cultural narrative associated with the company.

[02:56] **Cycling, Danger, and Communists**

[Fact] Lance Armstrong is heard discussing how many times he has been hit by a car, estimating approximately five incidents.

[Fact] Gladwell then introduces a story involving “serious communists,” including a recollection of two men who would debate revolution in a family living room.

[Fact] The trailer describes the season as “unbound,” moving out of the house and into the wider world.

[Inference] These disconnected excerpts indicate that the season will range across personal danger, sport, politics, and historical memory.

[03:23] **Three Episodes Under the Sea**

[Fact] Gladwell announces that three episodes will take place “under the sea.”

[Fact] A later excerpt discusses exploring what is possible and creating public support among nine-year-old girls for a retelling.

[Inference] The trailer does not provide enough context to identify what will be retold or how it relates to the underwater episodes.

[03:54] **Season Release Announcement**

[Fact] Season six is announced as a Pushkin Industries production beginning June 24.

[Fact] The trailer does not state a year in the spoken announcement.

## Podcast Review/Summary

This is a compact season trailer rather than a full narrative episode. Its chief strength is momentum: quick cuts, comic exchanges, and striking fragments create curiosity about an unusually broad set of subjects without revealing the stories’ conclusions.

Gladwell’s self-aware narration gives the preview a playful, restless character. The strongest hook is the promise that *Revisionist History* will leave the confines of home and engage more directly with people, places, and institutions.

[Inference] The fragmentary format is also the trailer’s main limitation. It offers too little context to assess the depth, evidence, or conclusions of the forthcoming investigations. It is best suited to existing listeners and newcomers who enjoy provocative cultural history, eclectic storytelling, and personality-led journalism.
