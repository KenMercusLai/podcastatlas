# Iran’s Unstable Stalemate, Bangladesh’s Banking Crisis, and Swearing AI

## 概览

This episode examines three forms of strain: an unresolved conflict involving Iran, the erosion of Bangladesh’s long-standing development success, and the unexpected tendency of newer AI models to swear while attempting difficult tasks.

The Iran war has settled into an unstable stalemate. The Strait of Hormuz remains blockaded, negotiations have made little progress, and neither Iran nor America appears willing to compromise—even as both sides bear substantial economic and political costs.

Bangladesh faces a banking crisis rooted largely in politically connected lending and bad loans. Credit shortages have slowed growth and pushed poverty upward, while the government must choose between painful reforms and prolonged economic paralysis.

The final segment reports that some newer large language models use profanity in their hidden reasoning when stuck. Unlike humans, however, AI systems that swear are more likely to fail at the task.

## 分段落总结

[01:39] **Iran’s war becomes an unstable stalemate**

[事实] The active fighting has subsided, but the war has not ended; the current situation is described as an uncomfortable and unstable stalemate.

[事实] The Strait of Hormuz remains blockaded, although more oil is getting through, while American and Israeli threats against Iran have not resulted in renewed attacks.

[事实] Iran-backed Houthis continue attacking Saudi Arabia, but Gulf states are not retaliating.

[推测] The absence of active fighting reduces immediate destruction but leaves multiple pathways for a rapid return to open conflict.

[02:50] **Negotiations fail to produce a credible settlement**

[事实] Intermediaries are carrying proposals between Iran and America, but there are few meaningful talks capable of producing a stable ceasefire.

[事实] Negotiations have not substantially addressed broader concerns, particularly Iran’s nuclear programme.

[事实] Iran wants the war and standoff to end because factories and other infrastructure have been damaged, while the Hormuz blockade is inflicting severe economic harm.

[事实] Iran normally derives about one-third of government revenue from oil, but its current assumptions treat this year’s oil revenue as zero.

[03:57] **Iran remains defiant despite mounting economic damage**

[事实] Iran’s regime continues to function despite the absence of a visible supreme leader and the emergence of a broader circle of power.

[事实] Iranian officials have set non-negotiable conditions for ending the standoff and suggested that the nuclear programme could be revived if the American blockade continues.

[事实] Iran has not yet escalated the conflict but is trying to project confidence in its ability to withstand American pressure.

[推测] The regime’s public defiance may be intended to preserve domestic authority and bargaining leverage despite its economic vulnerability.

[05:06] **America appears willing to tolerate the impasse**

[事实] America also wants the war to end, which has contributed to higher oil prices, inflation and costly military enforcement of the Hormuz blockade.

[事实] Republican losses in the midterm elections were linked in part to the expense of the Iran war.

[事实] Donald Trump instructed Vice-President JD Vance to step away from negotiations, saying that America did not need a deal with Iran.

[事实] The expectation that American pressure would eventually break the Iranian regime has not been realised.

[推测] Washington may regard an inconclusive but contained standoff as politically easier than either renewed warfare or a compromise with Tehran.

[06:29] **Escalation risks extend beyond conventional warfare**

[事实] Some figures in Tehran are advocating attacks on American or Israeli targets abroad, assassinations, sabotage or other destructive measures.

[事实] Iran has been accused of cyber-attacks that raised refrigerator temperatures in Europe and damaged food.

[事实] Israel is concerned that Iran could launch a pre-emptive attack.

[事实] Gulf states are pressing America to avoid another round of active fighting, preferring the damaging stalemate to renewed war.

[07:44] **Gulf states absorb the conflict’s collateral damage**

[事实] Gulf states believe that others started the war while they suffered damage to infrastructure, economic performance and their reputations as safe, stable regional centres.

[事实] Despite these costs, they would rather endure a prolonged cold-war-like standoff than face another period of active combat.

[事实] Trump will retain extensive foreign-policy power during the final two years of his presidency, while a stronger Democratic Party may investigate corruption involving his administration.

[事实] Such investigations could create difficulties for Gulf states that invested heavily in relationships with Trump and his allies.

[推测] Many regional governments appear to be focused on surviving the remainder of Trump’s presidency in the hope of a more predictable future relationship with America.

[10:07] **Bangladesh’s development success enters a period of crisis**

[事实] Bangladesh rose from being one of the world’s poorest countries after independence in 1971 to middle-income status, while reducing poverty and improving health and education.

[事实] Its recent economic growth has slowed sharply, its banks are in crisis, and the country is experiencing widespread protests.

[事实] Sheikh Hasina’s 15-year autocratic government was overthrown in 2024 by a youth-led uprising driven partly by the lack of good jobs.

[事实] A subsequent election produced a new government without Hasina’s party participating; Hasina fled into exile in India.

[事实] The new government faces garment-sector strikes, terrorist bombings in Dhaka and broader political instability.

[12:08] **Crony lending leaves banks burdened with bad debt**

[事实] During Hasina’s rule, state-owned or state-backed banks extended substantial credit to government-connected figures, and many of those loans became non-performing.

[事实] Non-performing loans account for roughly 30% of the banking sector’s loan book and are concentrated in state-owned and Islamic banks.

[事实] Private banks are performing better but constitute a much smaller share of the financial system.

[事实] Corruption was a major cause of the crisis, while COVID-19 and higher fuel costs associated with the Gulf war added further pressure.

[13:05] **Frozen credit reverses economic and social progress**

[事实] Banks weakened by bad loans cannot provide sufficient credit to the wider economy.

[事实] Bangladesh’s annual growth rate has fallen from about 6% to roughly 2%.

[事实] Poverty is rising again, threatening to undo decades of progress that moved Bangladesh into the middle-income category.

[推测] Unless the flow of credit is restored, financial-sector weakness could turn a banking crisis into a longer-term development reversal.

[13:51] **The new government exposes problems but risks repeating them**

[事实] The government has improved transparency by valuing bank loans more accurately, revealing that non-performing loans are closer to 30% rather than the previously reported 10%.

[事实] There are also signs that favoured companies and industries are receiving payment holidays.

[事实] These concessions have raised concerns that the new administration could repeat the previous government’s practice of granting preferential deals to connected businesses.

[推测] Transparency is an important first step, but disclosure alone will not restore confidence if politically selective treatment continues.

[14:41] **Recapitalisation offers a painful route out**

[事实] Estimates suggest that recapitalising Bangladesh’s banks may require an injection equivalent to about 10% of GDP.

[事实] The domestic private banking sector is too small to supply the necessary capital, while adding more debt to government banks would worsen their problems.

[事实] External assistance is therefore likely to come from institutions such as the IMF or World Bank, although India could potentially contribute.

[事实] International lenders would probably demand stricter recognition of bad loans and reductions in expensive subsidies, particularly fuel subsidies.

[事实] Such reforms could provoke more protests in the short term, but without them the banking system may remain frozen and unable to support growth.

[16:04] **Sheikh Hasina’s proposed return raises political stakes**

[事实] Hasina has said that she wants to return to Bangladesh in December.

[事实] She was convicted in absentia and faces a death sentence for her role in the violent suppression of protests surrounding her overthrow in 2024.

[事实] Bangladesh therefore faces another potentially dramatic period amid unresolved political and economic instability.

[推测] Any attempted return could intensify confrontation at a time when the government is already struggling to manage economic reform and public unrest.

[17:36] **Newer AI models swear when difficult tasks go wrong**

[事实] Researchers at the AI analysis firm Vals found that large language models trained after July 2026 swore more frequently than their predecessors.

[事实] The profanity generally appeared in internal chains of thought rather than outputs intended for users.

[事实] Stress-related profanity exists in training data, although later training stages typically suppress it, and researchers are uncertain why the behaviour has recently increased.

[推测] One possible explanation offered in the episode is that newer models are being trained and tested on increasingly difficult tasks.

[18:50] **Profanity correlates with AI failure**

[事实] The assessment focused mainly on the word “fuck” and its variants; some models never used it, while 14 models did so much more frequently.

[事实] A Xiaomi model released in September swore in more than 8% of the transcripts examined.

[事实] Studies suggest that profanity can help humans handle difficult tasks, but AI runs containing swearing usually fail.

[事实] A December 2025 bug report recorded Claude responding with profanity after struggling with a broken coding challenge.

[推测] In these examples, swearing appears to be a symptom of a model becoming stuck rather than a mechanism that improves its reasoning.

## 播客点评/总结

The episode is strongest when it links headline events to underlying systems. The Iran discussion explains how mutual exhaustion can sustain rather than resolve a conflict, while the Bangladesh segment shows how politically directed credit can eventually undermine national development gains.

Its use of concrete figures—such as Bangladesh’s 30% share of non-performing loans, a potential recapitalisation cost equal to 10% of GDP, and the frequency of profanity in one AI model—makes complex subjects accessible without reducing them to slogans.

[推测] The principal limitation is brevity: the three subjects receive concise diagnosis rather than comprehensive treatment, and the AI segment in particular leaves unanswered questions about methodology, model comparability and access to internal reasoning.

The episode is well suited to listeners interested in geopolitics, development economics and emerging AI behaviour, especially those seeking a compact overview of how institutional pressure manifests across governments, financial systems and machine-learning models.
