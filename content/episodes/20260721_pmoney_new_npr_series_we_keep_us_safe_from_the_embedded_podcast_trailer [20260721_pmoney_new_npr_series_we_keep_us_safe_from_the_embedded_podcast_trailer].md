# We Keep Us Safe: Trailer

## 概览

Planet Money host Kenny Malone introduces a trailer for a new investigative series from NPR’s Embedded, centered on CHOP, the Capitol Hill Organized Protest in Seattle.

The trailer frames CHOP as a 2020 experiment in creating a police-free community after police abandoned a precinct during anti-police-brutality protests. It describes both the early sense of possibility and the later fear that led some protesters to take on armed security roles.

The central question is how a space built around protecting Black life ended with the death of 16-year-old Antonio Mays Jr. The series says the case remains unsolved six years later and follows reporters Will James and Sydney Brounstone as they revisit rumors, witnesses, and new evidence.

## 分段落总结

[00:00] **Planet Money introduces the Embedded series**

[事实] Kenny Malone tells Planet Money listeners that NPR’s Embedded has a new investigative series coming out.
[事实] Instead of explaining the series directly, he introduces and plays the trailer.

[00:21] **CHOP begins as a police-free protest space**

[事实] The trailer says that in 2020, after a standoff with anti-police-brutality protesters, police abandoned their precinct.
[事实] Protesters occupied about eight city blocks in Seattle and created a space with tents, free food, mutual aid, speeches, and music.
[事实] The space became known as CHOP, the Capitol Hill Organized Protest.
[推测] The trailer presents CHOP’s early days as both a protest and an attempt to model a different kind of community.

[01:12] **Fear and armed self-protection emerge**

[事实] Protesters feared an attack from right-wing activists.
[事实] Some protesters appointed themselves as CHOP security and walked around in tactical gear carrying guns.
[推测] The trailer suggests that the effort to stay safe introduced new risks and confusion inside the protest zone.

[01:33] **The Jeep shooting and Antonio Mays Jr.’s death**

[事实] One night, a Jeep drove into the camp, and protesters believed they were under attack.
[事实] Protesters shot at the Jeep.
[事实] After the shooting ended, one passenger was dead: 16-year-old Antonio Mays Jr.
[事实] The trailer says protesters saw him as a threat that night.

[01:48] **Antonio’s father challenges the threat narrative**

[事实] Antonio’s father says his son was there for the cause.
[事实] He says that if it meant getting his son back, he would not have taught him Black history.
[推测] The father’s comments deepen the tragedy by connecting Antonio’s death to the very ideals that may have brought him there.

[02:14] **The unresolved central question**

[事实] The case remains unsolved six years later.
[事实] The trailer says no one has been able to explain how a place built to protect Black life ended up killing a Black teenager.
[推测] The series appears to investigate not only who was responsible, but how the protest’s goals collided with fear, rumor, and armed action.

[02:25] **Reporters reopen the story**

[事实] Will James and Sydney Brounstone say they spent more than a year investigating what happened that night.
[事实] They describe sifting through rumors, finding new witnesses, and uncovering never-before-seen evidence.
[事实] The trailer includes references to claims about Antonio, witnesses saying they were chased, and evidence involving a long gun.

[03:02] **Series release information**

[事实] The trailer identifies We Keep Us Safe as a new true crime series from NPR’s Embedded podcast, The Seattle Times, and KUOW.
[事实] Listeners are told they can find Embedded wherever they get podcasts or at NPR’s Embedded page.

## 播客点评/总结

[推测] This trailer’s main value is its clear dramatic framing: it turns CHOP from a familiar 2020 protest reference into a focused unresolved case about Antonio Mays Jr.’s death.

[推测] Its strongest element is the tension between CHOP’s stated purpose and the fatal outcome. The trailer uses that contradiction to set up a larger investigation into safety, accountability, and political ideals under pressure.

[推测] Because this is only a trailer, it provides limited detail about the evidence, the witnesses, or the full chronology of the shooting. Listeners looking for conclusions will likely need the full series.

[推测] The series seems best suited for listeners interested in investigative journalism, true crime, protest movements, policing, and the unresolved consequences of the 2020 Seattle protests.
