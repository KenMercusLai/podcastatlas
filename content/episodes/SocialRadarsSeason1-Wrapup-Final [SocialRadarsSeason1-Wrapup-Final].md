# Social Radars Season 1 Wrap-Up: Season 2 Announcement

## 概览

This short wrap-up episode announces that *Social Radars* will return for a second season after an initial run that began with a plan for seven episodes and ended up producing nine.

The hosts explain that they will take roughly a month away to record new interviews, with several promising guests already lined up. They also announce that selected video highlights and useful segments from season one will be released later in the month on YouTube and Twitter.

The episode also reflects on the tone of season one: the hosts felt the interviews became genuine conversations, with guests sharing open stories, backgrounds, and challenges that even the hosts had not known beforehand.
