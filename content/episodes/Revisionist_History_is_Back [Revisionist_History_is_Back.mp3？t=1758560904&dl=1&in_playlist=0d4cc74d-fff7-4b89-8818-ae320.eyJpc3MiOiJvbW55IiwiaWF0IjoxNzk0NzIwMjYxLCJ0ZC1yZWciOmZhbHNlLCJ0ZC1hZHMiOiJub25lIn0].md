# Revisionist History Is Back: A Preview of the Upcoming Season

## 概览

Malcolm Gladwell previews an eclectic new season of *Revisionist History*, promising stories that venture into subjects few podcasts would approach. The lineup moves rapidly between popular culture, policing, food history, animal research, face recognition, and American public health.

The preview highlights investigations of *Paw Patrol*, the death of George Floyd, the closely guarded production method behind Thomas’ English Muffins, the difficulties of studying raccoons, and Robert F. Kennedy Jr. It also introduces Lucy Sullivan as the newest member of the *Revisionist History* team.

## 播客点评/总结

This short preview succeeds by emphasizing the range of the coming season. Its sharp transitions—from children’s television to George Floyd, English muffins, raccoons, face recognition, and public health—present *Revisionist History* as a show interested in finding complexity within both serious events and apparently trivial subjects.

The strongest feature is its mixture of humor, personal storytelling, and investigative ambition. Brief audio excerpts give each announced topic a distinct hook without revealing the eventual argument or conclusion.

Its main limitation is also a consequence of the teaser format: most subjects receive only a sentence or two, so the evidence, framing, and depth of the forthcoming investigations cannot yet be evaluated. [推测] The season may appeal most to listeners who enjoy narrative journalism, revisionist interpretations of familiar stories, and unexpected deep dives into everyday culture.
