# Revisionist History Returns with More Experiments

## Overview

This short promotional episode previews the return of *Revisionist History* on September 15. The trailer teases stories about personal sacrifice, suffering as a motivation to help others, privilege in an Ivy League classroom, and the unexpected consequences of writing a book intended to help people.

It also announces that Pushkin Plus subscribers will be able to access the season’s final three episodes beginning September 22. The podcast preview is framed by advertisements for an Australian caravan and motorhome sale.
