# The Social Radar Season Five Update

## 概览

This short catch-up episode updates listeners on season five of *The Social Radar*. The hosts say the show is launching with a new look and feel, expanding to Substack, and releasing full podcast episodes on YouTube.

They also reflect on two years of episodes, highlighting favorite and notable interviews from the archive, especially Tyler Schultz and the ongoing Ron Conway series.

The hosts explain that season five has taken longer partly because they launched the shorter-form *Founder Mode* series over the summer. They close by asking listeners to rate and review the show on Spotify and Apple Podcasts, and to subscribe to and like the YouTube channel.

This episode is valuable as a concise status update rather than a full thematic conversation. It clearly tells existing listeners what is changing: Substack, YouTube video, a refreshed presentation, and the upcoming season five launch.

[推测] The limitation is that the episode is more promotional than analytical; it does not go deeply into the subjects or guests of season five.
