# Out-numbered: AI’s contentious maths milestone

## 概览

This episode of *The Intelligence* opens with OpenAI’s claimed solution to the Navier-Stokes existence and smoothness problem, one of the Millennium Prize problems. The discussion focuses less on whether a solution matters and more on why mathematicians are uneasy about how the result was produced and presented.

The show then turns to the counterterrorism powers created after 9/11. It argues that America’s post-9/11 legal and security machinery remains largely intact even as the jihadist threat has receded, and that some of those powers are now being adapted for broader political and domestic purposes.

The final segment is an obituary-style tribute to Guo Lanying, the Chinese opera singer honored as a “People’s Artist,” tracing her brutal early training, revolutionary roles, signature song “My Motherland,” and lasting symbolic place in Chinese cultural life.

[事实] The programme previews three subjects: AI and higher mathematics, America’s post-9/11 counterterrorism machinery, and a tribute to a champion of Chinese traditional opera.

[01:39] **OpenAI’s Claimed Mathematical Breakthrough**

[事实] The episode says OpenAI claims to have solved the Navier-Stokes existence and smoothness problem.
[事实] The problem is described as one of the seven Millennium Prize problems, each associated with a million-dollar prize.
[事实] The discussion stresses that, in high-level mathematics, the route to a solution matters as much as the final answer.
[推测] The controversy is framed as a test of whether AI-generated mathematics can be accepted without the usual human-readable trail of insight.

[03:19] **What the Navier-Stokes Problem Is**

[事实] The Navier-Stokes equations describe the motion of fluids, including liquids and gases.
[事实] They are useful in practical settings such as aircraft wings, Formula One cars, blood circulation, and oil moving through pipes.
[事实] Mathematicians are interested not only in using the equations but in understanding their underlying structure and limits.
[事实] The existence and smoothness problem asks whether the equations can break down when describing seemingly well-behaved fluids.

[05:01] **How OpenAI Says the Solution Emerged**

[事实] OpenAI says it began training a new internal model on August 28th.
[事实] The model reportedly surpassed GPT-6 Astra, which the transcript says had been released on September 3rd.
[事实] OpenAI used teams of AI agents that could read the internet, run code, and message one another.
[事实] A group of about 100 agents first resolved a closely related question, after which OpenAI directed a larger swarm toward the Navier-Stokes problem.
[事实] The transcript says around 10,000 agents found a solution after about 88 hours, 2.7 million inter-agent messages, and at least $6.5 million in computing resources.

[06:38] **Why Mathematicians Are Uneasy**

[事实] OpenAI says it began working on the problem after hearing a rumour that Tristan Buckmaster of New York University and Le Van Elgindi, an Anthropic employee, were nearing a solution.
[事实] The transcript says talks between Buckmaster and OpenAI did not appear to go well.
[事实] Buckmaster and Elgindi then published their work early and in unfinished form before OpenAI’s announcement.
[事实] Mathematicians are described as concerned that OpenAI’s paper does not provide the level of detail normally expected about how the solution was reached.
[推测] The dispute is not simply about priority but about whether mathematical credit should attach to an answer, the method, or the insights generated along the way.

[08:39] **What AI Changes About Mathematics**

[事实] The discussion asks whether other Millennium problems and major mathematical questions might now fall to AI systems.
[事实] The guest says the episode raises deep questions about the purpose of mathematics.
[事实] Mathematicians have worked on Navier-Stokes for a long time and generated many insights along the way.
[事实] OpenAI has said it will not claim the one-million-dollar prize.
[推测] The episode suggests that AI may force mathematics to rethink how it values discovery, explanation, and authorship.

[11:32] **Twenty-Five Years After 9/11**

[事实] The episode marks 25 years since two hijacked planes hit the World Trade Center and another hit the Pentagon.
[事实] It says President George W. Bush quickly created a large counterterrorism apparatus after the attacks.
[事实] That apparatus included new war powers, expanded surveillance, looser constraints on agencies such as the CIA, and the creation of the Department of Homeland Security.
[事实] The segment argues that the jihadist threat has mostly disappeared in much of the world while many of the legal powers remain.

[12:26] **The Post-9/11 Legal Architecture**

[事实] Sam Beale says America’s counterterrorism regime initially had overwhelming bipartisan support.
[事实] He distinguishes laws for fighting terrorism abroad from laws for fighting terrorism at home.
[事实] Abroad, the main authority discussed is the 2001 Authorization for the Use of Military Force.
[事实] At home, the segment names the Department of Homeland Security, the Patriot Act, warrantless surveillance powers, and financial statutes targeting terrorist financing.

[事实] The segment says Donald Trump expanded who is treated as a terrorist.
[事实] It gives Latin American narco gangs branded as foreign terrorist organizations as one example.
[事实] The transcript describes a continuing boat-bombing campaign against suspected drug-running vessels as reminiscent of war-on-terror tactics.
[事实] Domestically, it says the administration targeted loose left-wing groups labelled as domestic terrorists.

[15:32] **How the Terrorist Threat Has Changed**

[事实] Beale says the threat from jihadist terrorism has receded.
[事实] He attributes this partly to two decades of wars in the Middle East degrading groups such as al-Qaeda and ISIS.
[事实] The segment notes a rise in lone-wolf attacks by self-radicalized terrorists.

[事实] Democrats are described as alarmed by how the administration has used counterterrorism tools, though reform efforts are called tentative.
[事实] Section 702 of the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act is described as allowing American spies to monitor suspects abroad while also collecting information on Americans.

[事实] Some Democrats want Section 702 reauthorized with stronger safeguards.
[事实] Other members of Congress have called for repeal of the 2001 Authorization for the Use of Military Force.

[17:16] **Generational Pressure for Change**

[事实] Beale says 100 million Americans alive today were not alive during the 9/11 attacks.
[事实] He says a generation has grown up under powerful security tools that curtailed civil liberties and came at great cost.
[事实] A newer generation of lawmakers is beginning to think about how to pare back the system.
[推测] The segment suggests reform may be slow because national-security powers are difficult to relinquish once governments possess them.

[19:08] **Guo Lanying as National Treasure**

[事实] In 2019, China’s government gave Guo Lanying the title of People’s Artist.
[事实] Anne Rowe describes Guo as an opera singer and national treasure.
[事实] Guo’s favorite and signature song was “My Motherland.”

[19:37] **The White-Haired Girl and Revolutionary Opera**

[事实] Guo fell in love at age 15 with the opera *The White-Haired Girl*.
[事实] The opera tells the story of a peasant woman abused by a landlord who tries to make her his concubine.
[事实] The heroine runs away, hides in a cave, and is later rescued by Chinese Communist Party troops.
[事实] Guo felt that singing songs from the opera meant taking part in the revolution.
[事实] She valued the heroine’s role because it allowed her to sing about women’s repression.

[20:57] **A Brutal Path Into Opera**

[事实] Guo’s early life is described as marked by poverty and struggle.
[事实] Her mother once left her in the woods to die because the family could not feed her, but her aunt rescued her.
[事实] At age four, she was sold for five copper coins under contract to an opera master.
[事实] Her training regime was brutal, involving beatings for insubordination or imperfect performance.
[事实] In winter, she had to lie on frozen lakes and vocalize until she melted a hole in the ice.

[22:58] **My Motherland and Guo’s Legacy**

[事实] “My Motherland” became famous from a film about the Korean War.
[事实] In the film, Guo appeared as a soldier hiding in a cave while singing the song.
[事实] The song became a major hit in China.
[事实] Guo said her struggles left her with anger, but that anger could dissolve when audiences roared her songs back to her.
[事实] The segment says Guo Lanying died aged 97.

## 播客点评/总结

The episode’s strongest thread is its interest in old systems suddenly made unstable: mathematics unsettled by AI, counterterrorism law repurposed after the threat environment changed, and revolutionary opera preserved through the life of a singer shaped by hardship and ideology.

The AI segment is especially valuable because it avoids treating the claimed solution as a simple triumph. It highlights the social norms of mathematics: explanation, priority, attribution, and the production of insight all matter.

The counterterrorism segment is sharper as a political and legal warning. It shows how emergency powers built for one kind of threat can remain available for later administrations with very different priorities.

[推测] The episode will suit listeners interested in technology, law, politics, and cultural history, though the fast-moving AI story may require later verification because the transcript presents OpenAI’s claim and the mathematical community’s response before any long process of formal validation is described.
