# Kiku Sharda: From Introvert to Comedy Icon in the Age of AI

## 概览

Kiku Sharda traces his journey from a reserved child in a Marwari business family to one of Indian television’s best-known comic actors. He explains how cinema fascinated him, why his father insisted on an MBA and financial stability, and how *Hatim* finally gave him the recognition and income needed to pursue acting professionally.

The conversation explores his craft: transforming through costume, developing believable backstories, improvising with fellow performers, and balancing a written character with spontaneous celebrity interactions. He credits writers and collaborators while emphasizing that comedy succeeds through atmosphere, timing, chemistry, and human imperfection—not merely through isolated jokes.

The discussion also examines beauty standards, laughter as emotional relief, family support, grief, and the limits of creative freedom. A major closing thread concerns AI’s growing role in film and television. Sharda accepts that artists must learn new tools but maintains that audiences will continue to value real performers, authentic talent, and the human struggle behind art.

[事实] Host Shekhar Natrajan describes the podcast as an attempt to bridge the gap between technological advancement and ordinary people’s preparedness while exploring what makes humans human.

[事实] Kiku says he has never been naturally outgoing and often becomes quiet in crowded rooms or takes time to open up to new people.

[事实] Once he wears a costume and sees the completed appearance in a mirror, he stops seeing himself and begins responding as the character.

[推测] Costume functions for him as a psychological boundary between his private personality and his uninhibited stage persona.

[05:19] **Family origins, Mumbai childhood, and fascination with cinema**

[事实] Kiku grew up in Mumbai as one of three brothers in a Marwari business family; his mother came from Rampur and his father from Jodhpur.

[事实] His father moved to Mumbai because he considered it India’s financial capital and a better place to build a large business.

[事实] Kiku spent his first 20 years in Bandra, loved watching films, copied actors, and became fascinated after seeing stars such as Salman Khan, Jackie Shroff, Shatrughan Sinha, and Sunny Deol at a local club.

[事实] He identifies his mother—a trained Bharatanatyam dancer who became a homemaker—as the likely source of his creative inclination.

[10:59] **MBA, business expectations, and education**

[事实] Kiku’s father expected his sons either to join the family business or start businesses of their own, and insisted that all three complete MBAs.

[事实] Kiku studied commerce and completed a two-year full-time MBA, though he did not ultimately pursue a conventional business career.

[事实] He rejects the idea that the degree was wasted, arguing that education broadens one’s outlook regardless of profession.

[事实] From around age 16, he visited his father’s office to gain practical experience, although finance and accounting never interested him.

[18:00] **Brothers and a close-knit family**

[事实] Kiku’s elder brother understood stocks and the family business from a young age and later settled in New York.

[事实] His younger brother shared his enthusiasm for films; they sometimes discussed a film for longer than its running time, although the brother eventually entered the corporate world and ran a headhunting company.

[事实] After the deaths of their parents, the brothers remained closely connected through regular meetings, family gatherings, and a WhatsApp group.

[推测] The contrast between his brothers’ business careers and his own creative path helped Kiku recognize early that his strengths lay outside finance.

[21:26] **Finding a route into performance**

[事实] Kiku acknowledges that, unlike many aspiring Mumbai actors, he had family support and a fallback plan if acting did not work out.

[事实] While still in school, he trained with choreographer P. L. Raj, learning some Kathak and Bollywood dance, partly because he hoped the relationship would introduce him to film sets and industry professionals.

[事实] In college he befriended people from film families, including Shakti Sagar and Tusshar Kapoor, but those connections did not immediately produce acting work.

[事实] He felt unable to leave his father’s office until he could demonstrate that acting generated real income.

[28:48] **Theatre, events, and the breakthrough with *Hatim***

[事实] Kiku discovered that Hindi theatre in Mumbai offered limited shows and insufficient income compared with the stronger Marathi and Gujarati theatre ecosystems.

[事实] After his MBA, he started an events company that organized pre-wedding and cocktail functions, temporarily placing acting in the background.

[事实] His television breakthrough came with *Hatim*, in which he played Hobo; the role brought money, public recognition, and his father’s approval.

[事实] At approximately 28, he decided that acting could become his full-time profession.

[推测] Recognition mattered almost as much as income because it demonstrated to his business-oriented family that acting was a legitimate and distinctive career.

[36:21] **Learning the craft across theatre, television, and film**

[事实] Kiku says training can provide confidence, but performers also need to learn practical techniques such as hitting marks, finding the light, and adapting to camera restrictions.

[事实] He initially found screen acting restrictive because actors could not simply move wherever they wished while the camera followed.

[事实] He considers theatre an actor’s medium that strengthens line-learning, improvisation, continuity, and the ability to recover when something goes wrong live.

[事实] Film and television require performers to maintain an emotional graph even when scenes are shot out of sequence.

[推测] His account treats acting as a combination of instinct and technical discipline rather than something guaranteed by either talent or formal classes alone.

[42:35] **Body expectations, beauty, and colourism**

[事实] Kiku recalls rejecting a superhero role after being told to lose 25 kilograms in one month.

[事实] He argues that cinema has historically privileged conventional beauty because audiences enjoy attractive people, locations, and frames, although he believes standards have begun to change.

[事实] He agrees that cinema has contributed to pressure surrounding fairness and thinness, while noting that darker skin is viewed more positively today than in the past.

[事实] He says prejudices must be challenged from childhood because families often pass them down through remarks linking fairness with desirability.

[推测] The discussion reveals tension between cinema’s visual appeal and its responsibility not to reinforce damaging social standards.

[48:13] **Creating comic characters and making them believable**

[事实] Kiku credits a team of writers and describes character creation as a mixture of written concepts, performer input, experimentation, and audience response.

[事实] His characters have included Palak, Bachcha Yadav, a foreigner confused by Hindi idioms, inspectors, lawyers, and the constable he played for years in *F.I.R.*

[事实] He draws inspiration from productions and performers including *Mr. Bean*, *Pink Panther*, *Little Britain*, *The Office*, and *Saturday Night Live*.

[事实] He constructs backstories by imagining where a character lives, what family surrounds them, and what they do after leaving the stage.

[推测] For Kiku, a comic character becomes durable when audiences can imagine that person existing beyond the sketch.

[55:43] **Living as Palak and the challenge of character continuity**

[事实] Kiku participated in *Jhalak Dikhhla Jaa* as Palak rather than as himself, meaning the judges addressed and evaluated the fictional character.

[事实] He found the arrangement difficult because he had to receive genuine dance criticism while remaining in character and respecting the judges.

[事实] Three older women once treated him as though Palak were a real young woman and praised her beauty.

[事实] He regarded their reaction as proof that his portrayal had achieved believability.

[58:02] **Parody, contrast, and improvisation on Kapil Sharma’s show**

[事实] Kiku says his Sunny Deol-inspired comedy works through the contrast between his own physique and the actor’s powerful screen image, as well as between Sunny Deol’s soft-spoken personality and explosive performances.

[事实] On Kapil Sharma’s stage, performers may have only 10–20 minutes to establish a character, interact with a celebrity, deliver jokes, and improvise.

[事实] He joined the show several months after it began and says Kapil Sharma, the writers, and the creative team developed its original concept.

[事实] The performances generally continue without pausing or restarting, although editing can remove material that does not work.

[事实] Some characters cannot be reused on other televised platforms, though Kiku may still perform them in non-televised live shows.

[事实] Kiku recounts being taken by Haryana police in 2016 after followers objected to his parody of a religious leader on a program unrelated to Kapil Sharma’s show.

[事实] He travelled to Haryana, appeared in court, obtained bail, and returned to Mumbai by the following morning.

[事实] Television reports suggesting he might remain jailed for two weeks caused serious anxiety for his father and family.

[事实] Members of the film and television community publicly supported him and questioned why a comic actor had been arrested over an unpopular joke.

[事实] Kiku says he is willing to apologize when someone is hurt, but objected to the speed and manner of the process because he received no prior opportunity to respond.

[推测] The episode illustrates the vulnerability of comedians when offence escalates rapidly from audience criticism into legal and personal danger.

[73:01] **Marriage, children, and stability at home**

[事实] Kiku married Priyanka in 2002 through an arranged marriage, and his first major television show followed in 2003.

[事实] He credits her with organizing his life, managing their home, helping raise their two sons, and giving him the calmness needed to work.

[事实] He agrees that a stable marriage can enable greater professional risk rather than merely making a person more cautious.

[推测] Domestic security appears to have given him the emotional freedom to sustain an unpredictable entertainment career.

[75:40] **Laughter as a way of choosing life**

[事实] Kiku describes laughter as one of life’s greatest pleasures and says joking with friends and family can ease misery, provided nobody is deliberately hurt.

[事实] He believes rigid family hierarchies that prevent children from joking with their fathers are becoming outdated.

[事实] He regards laughter as a natural reaction rather than necessarily a conscious attempt to escape sadness.

[事实] He acknowledges that everyone experiences illness, loss, and hardship, regardless of status, and says people can still choose happiness and laughter.

[事实] He especially enjoys children’s humour because they speak without the filters imposed by adult social rules.

[推测] His philosophy does not deny suffering; it presents humour as an active orientation toward life despite suffering.

[86:15] **Global reach and laughter in healthcare**

[事实] Kiku says the show reaches Hindi-speaking audiences around the world and that distribution through Netflix has removed earlier problems with overseas channel access.

[事实] He recalls visiting a hospital that operated a light-entertainment television channel so patients could laugh, smile, and temporarily redirect their attention from illness.

[事实] He does not laugh merely because others are laughing and says he responds only when he personally finds something funny.

[推测] The discussion suggests that comedy’s practical value lies less in curing illness than in offering moments of psychological relief and connection.

[92:10] **Why comedy depends on chemistry, timing, and atmosphere**

[事实] Kiku says improvisation with Kapil Sharma and other performers emerges from conversation and shared situations.

[事实] He argues that telling a joke is easier than creating the atmosphere in which humour naturally develops.

[事实] Comic timing changes depending on the performer, situation, and interaction, making humour difficult to reduce to a fixed formula.

[事实] When hearing a joke, he also thinks about the speaker’s intention, emotional state, and whether the delivery feels genuine.

[推测] These observations imply that comedy is relational: its meaning depends on people reading one another in real time.

[98:02] **AI, creativity, and the future of performers**

[事实] Asked how artists can protect creativity from AI, Kiku says he would still prefer a film or play featuring real performers over work generated entirely by machines.

[事实] He believes even an AI-generated film would lead him to ask about the human creator who conceived the prompts, story, and direction.

[事实] He acknowledges that AI will continue growing and that young filmmakers—including his son, who plans to study film and television at NYU—will need to learn it.

[事实] He notes that roles such as lighting may change when visual effects can be generated computationally.

[事实] He says technology can reproduce images of real places, but it cannot eliminate his desire to experience authentic places and human talent directly.

[推测] His position is adaptive rather than anti-technology: use the tools, but preserve human authorship, presence, and imperfection as sources of meaning.

[事实] After roughly 25 years in the industry, Kiku wants to explore non-comic work, including dark or negative characters.

[事实] He prefers to call himself an actor rather than a comedian because he wants to attempt the full range of acting.

[事实] He found the offer surprising and said he had not yet seen a feature film made entirely through AI.

[事实] He also rejected a dark role involving a guardian secretly drugging a child actor to prevent her from growing up because he could not emotionally accept performing the cruelty.

[事实] The host uses AI to generate a comic character concept: a retired Marwari sari wholesaler who reinvents himself as an AI coach after watching only a few videos.

[事实] Kiku finds the concept interesting and says humans could continue developing and enriching it.

[推测] The exchange demonstrates a collaborative model in which AI supplies a premise while human performers add judgment, contradiction, and lived texture.

[事实] Kiku believes stand-up, television shows, online videos, English comedy, and Hindi comedy can coexist because different demographic groups have different preferences.

[事实] He values the broad reach of Kapil Sharma’s show, which he says can entertain viewers from approximately eight to 80 years old.

[事实] He credits Kapil Sharma with defining contemporary Indian television comedy and changing the field over their 13 years of collaboration.

[事实] He says Mahendra Singh Dhoni could make an excellent guest because he considers him witty and funny, although Dhoni has not appeared on the show.

[推测] Rather than predicting one dominant format, Kiku expects Indian comedy to fragment into multiple durable niches.

[117:22] **Advice on humour, pressure, and overthinking**

[事实] Kiku says people need to be easier on themselves to access their funny side.

[事实] He advises against excessive concern about what might happen 25 or 30 years in the future.

[事实] He sees overthinking and accumulated pressure as barriers to humour.

[推测] This advice reflects his broader belief that comedy requires openness to uncertainty rather than complete control.

[120:00] **Parents, grief, and continuing their legacy**

[事实] Kiku treasures the memory of his mother calling immediately after his television appearances to tell him what she liked and what she did not find funny.

[事实] He says losing one’s parents never becomes easy, regardless of the advice others provide.

[事实] He comforts himself by believing that his parents continue to support and watch over him, especially when positive things happen.

[事实] His message to grieving people is to keep working, continue doing what made their parents proud, and value praise that reflects well on their upbringing.

[推测] Continuing to perform allows him to maintain an emotional relationship with his parents through the values and pride they associated with his work.

[事实] Kiku says the interview has left him thinking about whether artists will still be able to practise their present crafts five years from now.

[事实] He concludes that AI can no longer simply be ignored because it has become part of life.

[事实] His proposed response is to move alongside the technology and consider what new things humans can create with it.

[推测] The episode closes without resolving the threat of AI, but reframes adaptation itself as a creative responsibility.

Its emotional range is equally valuable. The conversation connects career legitimacy, parental approval, marriage, public controversy, laughter, and grief without presenting comedy as an escape from real life. Kiku’s recollections of his parents and the 2016 arrest add vulnerability to an otherwise celebratory career narrative.
